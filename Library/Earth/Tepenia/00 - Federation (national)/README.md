# The Tepenian Federation: national layer

What is shared across the whole Federation rather than belonging to one city: the civic default language (Hawaiian, later Tepenian), national institutions, the identity timeline (Act 1, people are still "X who live in Antarctica"; Act 2, properly Tepenian, solidified by Amundsen Tower's completion about 2688), and the national robot culture. City entries refer here instead of repeating it.
