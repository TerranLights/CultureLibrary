# Sinheung

**What this is:** the Culture Library entry for Sinheung, a location of the Tepenian Federation. Its cultures are synthesized (ULM, then CST, then RWBEM in the Inner Tepenia GDD), not researched from the real world; this folder holds the finished results for other works to draw on.

- **Sources:** the GDD's spec (`Cities/Specs/Mirny subnet/`), its city development passes, `Local_Cultures/` and `Local_Robot_Culture/`.
- **Eras:** Act 1 (2564 to the 2600s: people still "X who live in Antarctica"), Act 2 (late 2600s to 2812: properly Tepenian), post-war (from 2812; the games are set about 10 to 15 years after). Mark each entry with its era.

## Layout
- `Human culture/` and `Robot culture/`: kept apart, as in the GDD. Inside each, one subfolder per sign and one file per concept, mirroring the DCI, once results exist.
