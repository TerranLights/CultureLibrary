# The Tepenian Federation

This establishes regional cultures throughout Tepenia. Additionally, this forms the foundational progenitive basis for neocultures on Mars and the Belt, and then later Jupiter and Saturn.

Those neocultures on Mars (as well as the Belt, and then later Jupiter and Saturn) are to derive their results from this library, rather than from real world research, since the local neocultures of the Tepenian Federation are synthetic and not existing in the real world.

## Appearance in Media

The primary works of media that will draw their data from this sub-library are:
- The [Inner Tepenia](https://github.com/TerranLights/InnerTepeniaGDD) Sci-Fi CRPG
- The [Southern Lights](https://github.com/TerranLights/SouthernLights) WebTV series
