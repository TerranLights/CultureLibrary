# Mars

This establishes regional cultures on Mars. Additionally, this forms the foundational progenitive basis for neocultures across the Belt, and then later Jupiter and Saturn, and then later-still in the Alpha/Proxima Centauri System.

## Appearance in Media

The primary works of media that will draw their data from this sub-library are:
- The [Middle Tepenia](https://github.com/TerranLights/MiddleTepeniaGDD) Sci-Fi CRPG
- The [Cryptograph Helix](https://github.com/TerranLights/TheCryptographHelixDD) novel series
