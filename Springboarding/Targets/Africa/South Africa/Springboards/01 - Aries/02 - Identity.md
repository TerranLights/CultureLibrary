*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **amaMpondo, abaThembu, amaBhaca and amaHlubi in the Eastern Cape:** check whether people introduce themselves first by chiefdom, clan or the wider isiXhosa label.
- **Coloured communities, Griqua and Khoe revival groups (Western and Northern Cape):** see which self-designation each chooses and when one replaces another.
- **Afrikaans speakers of all backgrounds:** separate who claims the word "Afrikaner" from who speaks Afrikaans but rejects it, since the language and the label need not coincide.
- **Vatsonga of Limpopo and Mpumalanga:** look into the Tsonga against Shangaan naming debate as identity work.
- **The Lemba (VaRemba) of Limpopo:** compare their own accounts with those of the VhaVenda neighbors they live among.
- **Portuguese South Africans:** compare Madeiran-born families with those who came from Mozambique and Angola in the 1970s in how each tells its origin.
- **The post-1994 "born free" generation in Gauteng:** test whether it states identity differently from parents, using survey age bands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and for Afrikaner and Coloured identity; mixed for Khoe and San revival; thin for Lemba and Portuguese community self-description.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans and Kaaps for the identity debates of the Cape; isiZulu for izithakazelo; Xitsonga for the naming debate; Portuguese for community newspapers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** apartheid racial categories still structure surveys and pain; Khoe and San identity claims involve land and recognition disputes; genetic ancestry claims need care.
- **Held for review:** identity labels and heritage institutions of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, deferred by rule.
