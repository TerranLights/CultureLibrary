*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bapedi and Vatsonga villages of the Bushbuckridge lowveld:** check when grievance is spoken of as witchcraft and when as plain anger.
- **Soweto and other Gauteng townships:** compare envy and resentment talk across generations, using Ashforth's and newer fieldwork.
- **amaMpondo of the Eastern Cape:** trace collective anger from 1960 to migrant mineworkers at Marikana.
- **Zulu households consulting izangoma in KwaZulu-Natal:** look into whose anger, living or ancestral, a diagnosis blames.
- **Afrikaans-speaking young people since 1989:** examine rebellion music as a record of anger at parents, church and state.
- **Coloured and Black townships of the Western Cape:** compare residents' own explanations of protest anger without assuming either.
- **isiXhosa-speaking parents and teens:** test how anger is disciplined at home against how schools handle it.
- **Women's organizations nationwide:** check how activists frame anger at gender violence, and who hears it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** protest counts and injury figures are plentiful; emotion research is patchier; for Tshivenda and Xitsonga words of anger, expect little beyond dictionaries.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for healing and proverbs; Xitsonga and Sepedi for the lowveld; isiXhosa for Mpondo sources; Afrikaans for music and lyrics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence statistics can stigmatize places; witchcraft accusations led to killings; struggle songs with violent lines have been taken to court (the rulings go to Libra); "people's courts" and mob justice go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** grievance, riot and protest histories involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands are left for the author's review.
