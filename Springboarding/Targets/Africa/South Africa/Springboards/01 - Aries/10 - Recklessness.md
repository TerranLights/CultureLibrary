## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Youth crews in Soweto and the East Rand:** check whether peers call izikhothane and spinning daring, foolish or a craft.
- **Farmworker communities of the Western Cape winelands:** see how the dop system's history figures in local accounts of drinking, as told by residents and researchers.
- **Eastern Cape initiation seasons:** look at who labels unregistered schools reckless, and what families and chiefs each say.
- **Sepedi-speaking families with members in Hillbrow:** compare how risk in the city is described back home in Limpopo.
- **Cape Town surfers, climbers and hikers:** set clubs that teach caution against those who seek big waves and hard routes.
- **Drivers on Free State and Northern Cape long-distance routes:** examine when and where holiday crashes cluster.
- **Vatsonga and Basotho storytellers:** test whether their cautionary tales of rash figures match the isiXhosa ntsomi.
- **Small investors in Gauteng and KwaZulu-Natal suburbs:** trace who was drawn into crypto and pyramid schemes, by their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** road, youth-risk and drug-treatment data are strong; subcultures like spinning and izikhothane are covered mostly by press; Setswana and Sesotho terms for recklessness need dictionary work.
- **Languages needed:** Iscamtho and Tsotsitaal for youth scenes; Afrikaans for winelands and farm sources; isiXhosa for ntsomi and initiation debates; Sepedi for Mpe's setting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** substance use and HIV can stigmatize; initiation deaths are grievous and partly secret; illegal gambling and financial-fraud rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** risk-behavior surveys or case studies singling out South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, withheld for now under the standing rule.
