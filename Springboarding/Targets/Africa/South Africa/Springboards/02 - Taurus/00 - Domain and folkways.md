*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households of KwaZulu-Natal, the Eastern Cape and the Free State:** check where cattle still form the main store of wealth and where cash has replaced them.
- **Batswana and Bapedi in North West and Limpopo:** compare livestock-lending terms (mafisa) and their Nguni counterpart (ukusisa) as ways of holding wealth through others.
- **Nama, Griqua and ‡Khomani communities of the Northern Cape:** keep small-stock herding, land claims and craft income apart when asking what each household treasures.
- **Cape Muslim and Cape Malay families of the Western Cape:** trace how food preparation for Ramadan and Eid, and household heirlooms, are described inside the community.
- **Afrikaner farm and town families:** compare farm inheritance with urban salaried wealth, since the two may diverge sharply.
- **Chinese and Portuguese South Africans of Gauteng:** examine family-shop records as a lens on how a small business holds and passes on wealth.
- **Township youth of Gauteng (Soweto, Tembisa):** test display practices such as izikhothane against the parents' generation in spending surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household surveys, cattle and Cape probate records; mixed for stokvel ethnography; thin for VhaVenda and Vatsonga material culture outside older monographs. The nation's post-war lands await the author's Stage 0, so these Taurus sheets cover the present-day provinces.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu and Sesotho for cattle and wealth terms; Afrikaans and archival Dutch for Cape inventories; Kaaps for household words in Cape Flats sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** enslaved people appear as listed property in Cape records; land and removal histories are raw; wealth by population group is a charged statistic.
- **Held for review:** household goods, foodways and dress of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (parked by rule).
