# South Africa: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Sepedi, Setswana, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, Afrikaans, South African English, Kaaps; \|xam for the national motto; Arabic-Afrikaans for early Cape Muslim texts. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balobedu and Bapedi villages of Limpopo against Gauteng townships:** check whether elders and young adults rank the same priorities when asked in Sepedi or Khelobedu (*check* the name).
- **Zion Christian Church, amaNazaretha (Shembe) and mainline congregations:** compare which conduct each teaches as required, from their own hymnals and sermons.
- **The three Afrikaans Reformed churches (NGK, Hervormde, Gereformeerde):** separate their stated teachings rather than reading one Afrikaner voice.
- **Mosque communities descended from the Cape's exiled and enslaved Muslims:** trace what was taught from Tuan Guru's era to today's madrasah syllabi.
- **amaMpondo of Xolobeni and other mining-affected communities:** examine what each refuses to trade and how they say it.
- **Jewish South Africans and Portuguese Catholic parishes in Johannesburg:** look at how each community's calendar is used to restate priorities.
- **Nama and Griqua congregations of the Northern Cape:** see how church teaching and older Khoe sayings sit together in their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** values surveys and church statements give a strong base; proverbs collections are plentiful for isiZulu and Sesotho but scarce for Tshivenda and Xitsonga; post-1994 "values" talk is heavily political and needs care.
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for Sekese; isiXhosa for iintsomi; Sepedi for *Skeem Saam*; Afrikaans for church and education sources; Arabic-Afrikaans for early Cape Muslim manuscripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred places and graves in mining disputes; "values" is used as a weapon in politics, so sources may be partisan. Law as written is Libra's; its purpose is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** moral and religious teaching of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside here.
