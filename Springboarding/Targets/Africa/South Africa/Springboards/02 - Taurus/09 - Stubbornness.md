# South Africa: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Afrikaans, Kaaps, South African English; Khoekhoegowab for ‡Khomani and Nama claim histories. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Afrikaans families of bittereinder and hensopper descent (Free State, old Transvaal):** check whether the holdout-or-surrender line is still told at home.
- **amaXhosa communities of the Eastern Cape:** see how the cattle-killing is remembered locally, beside Peires's account.
- **Batswana of Mogopa and other North West communities that refused removal:** compare how each tells the decision to stay and its cost.
- **Former Sophiatown residents, Black, Coloured and Chinese:** separate each group's memory of the refusal and the move.
- **amaMpondo and Basotho mineworkers:** trace how strike resolve is spoken of at the mine and at home.
- **Cape Muslim congregations of Claremont and Athlone:** compare how each mosque's commemorations and recorded memories retell Imam Haron's refusal.
- **Former National Party voters and liberation-movement veterans:** check how each speaks of the TRC apologies given or withheld.
- **‡Khomani and Nama claimants of the Northern Cape:** look at how persistence is framed in community histories and claim documents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** histories of resistance and holdouts are very rich; everyday personal stubbornness is thin outside personality research and proverbs.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa for cattle-killing memory; Afrikaans for the war and proverbs; Setswana for Mogopa; Tsotsitaal and isiZulu for Sophiatown; Kaaps for Cape Flats idiom and Cape Muslim recollections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the cattle-killing, Marikana, AIDS deaths and deaths in detention are raw; rival camps claim the holdout figures. Courts, Botha's trial and land-claim law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** episodes of refusing to yield or to move in the past of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; excluded from this sheet.
