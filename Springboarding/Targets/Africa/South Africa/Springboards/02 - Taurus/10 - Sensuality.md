# South Africa: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Tshivenda, Xitsonga, Afrikaans, Kaaps, South African English; Khoekhoegowab for Nama music and dance. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **VhaVenda and Bapedi communities of Limpopo:** check how pleasure in group music and dance is spoken of, using tshikona and kiba as anchors.
- **Young amapiano and gqom crowds in Pretoria, Soweto and Durban:** compare how they and their parents describe the night out and its limits.
- **Afrikaans-speaking congregations and the sokkie circuit:** see where dance and drink are welcomed and where preachers draw a line.
- **Kaapse Klopse troupes, Malay choirs and their critics on the Cape Flats:** follow the New Year festivities through supporters and opponents within the community.
- **Zionist and Cape Muslim families:** look at fasting, abstinence and feast days as the same community's two sides.
- **Farm-worker families of the Boland and wine-tourism visitors:** examine whom the Winelands' pleasures serve and how each side tells it.
- **Portuguese South Africans of Johannesburg:** see which foods and music are offered at public festivals and which are kept for the home table.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** music and dance archives are deep; food writing is strong for Afrikaans and Cape cooking and patchier for other home cuisines; touch, scent and comfort are rarely studied directly.
- **Languages needed:** Tshivenda for Blacking's field sites and tshikona; Sepedi for kiba; Afrikaans and Kaaps for carnival, sokkie and Winelands sources; isiZulu for gqom and impepho; Khoekhoegowab for Nama stap.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** impepho and healing dances are sacred; the dop system and alcohol harm are painful histories; some initiation songs and dances (domba, for instance) are closed to outsiders. Sexuality as such belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** cuisines, music and festivals of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, withheld pending the author's ruling.
