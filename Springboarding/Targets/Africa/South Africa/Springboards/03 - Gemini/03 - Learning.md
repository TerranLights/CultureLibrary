## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xhosa and Basotho families of the Eastern Cape and Free State:** compare what young men are taught at ulwaluko and lebollo with what school teaches, to see how the two settings divide the teaching of adulthood; ask communities what may be described.
- **VhaVenda and Bapedi households of Limpopo:** check how songs, girls' initiation (vhusha, domba; byale) and home chores are used to teach, community by community.
- **Kalahari San and ‡Khomani communities:** test how tracking and plant knowledge are now passed on when fewer people hunt, using the communities' own accounts.
- **Cape Malay and other Cape Muslim families:** compare madrasah, home and public school as three places of learning held side by side.
- **amaNdebele and amaSwati of Mpumalanga:** look at beadwork and painting skills taught at home and whether schools now take part.
- **Afrikaner families in farm districts and in the cities:** compare learning a farm trade by working beside a parent with formal artisan training.
- **Jewish, Portuguese and Chinese South Africans in Johannesburg:** compare community day schools and weekend language classes as settings where heritage learning sits beside the state curriculum.
- **Adults who learned to read late, by province:** use campaign records to compare older rural learners with urban ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for early-grade reading and preschool assessment; mixed for craft and trade learning; thin and partly closed for initiation teaching.
- **Languages needed:** Tshivenda for Blacking's songs and Kha Ri Gude; isiXhosa and Sesotho for initiation terms; Arabic-script Afrikaans for madrasah texts; Setswana for Kuruman print; Khoe and San terms in tracking work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation knowledge is restricted to initiates and must not be pursued beyond what communities share; healer training involves calling and illness; 1976 testimony involves deaths of children.
- **Held for review:** schooling, language-learning and mission-education sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left out of this sheet.
