## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of Red and School descent in the Eastern Cape:** test whether that old division still shapes dress, church and ritual choices, or has faded.
- **Coloured communities of the Cape Flats and of Namaqualand:** compare how people in a city and in rural mission towns name their mixed descent.
- **Griqua communities of Kokstad, Griquatown and Campbell:** check how church and conference records present a dual Khoe and settler heritage.
- **VhaLemba households of Limpopo:** ask how members themselves reconcile two origin accounts, before reading outside studies.
- **Afrikaners of the Free State and former Transvaal:** look at family memories of bittereinder and hensopper ancestors as a split inherited inside families.
- **English-speaking South Africans of KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape:** examine past ties to Britain alongside local belonging, in letters and club records.
- **Portuguese South Africans who came from Mozambique and Angola in 1975:** check how they describe belonging to two African countries and to Portugal.
- **Chinese South Africans:** trace whether shifting official categories placed members of one family in different groups over time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for race classification, Coloured identity and code-switching; mixed for paired symbolism (right and left, senior and junior houses); thin for siSwati and Xitsonga material on twofold worldviews.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa for Red and School terms and Peires's oral sources; Afrikaans for war collaborators and dual-medium debates; Kaaps for Cape Flats self-descriptions; Tshivenda for Lemba sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race classification is painful and its labels are contested; genetic studies of the Lemba raised consent questions; insults aimed at people seen as acting outside their group should be described, not repeated.
- **Held for review:** classification, double-belonging and bilingual-identity research on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, excluded here.
