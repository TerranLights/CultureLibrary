*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Limpopo village families with members in Hillbrow:** trace how news and rumor move between the two places, since the gap tests what gets told and to whom.
- **Bushbuckridge and other lowveld communities of Mpumalanga and Limpopo:** check the step from talk to accusation, and who steps in to stop it.
- **Marico and other North West farm towns:** compare present-day talk in Afrikaans-speaking towns with Bosman's fiction, rather than assume one tradition.
- **Cape Malay and Coloured choir members:** look at which people and events moppies may tease and which are off-limits.
- **Residents of Alexandra and other areas of the 2008 attacks:** gather what rumors circulated before violence, from residents' own accounts.
- **Gauteng readers of the *Daily Sun* and the online public of Black Twitter:** compare print and online gossip audiences by age and province.
- **Jewish and Portuguese South Africans in Johannesburg:** check community social pages and group chats as channels for members' news.
- **isiNdebele- and siSwati-speaking communities of Mpumalanga:** test whether local radio call-ins carry neighborhood news and rumor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tabloids, the 2008 violence and HIV stigma; mixed for everyday gossip settings; thin for gossip words and proverbs in Tshivenda, Xitsonga and isiNdebele.
- **Languages needed:** Sepedi for Mpe's village setting; Xitsonga and Sepedi for Bushbuckridge; Afrikaans for Bosman, *Son* and *7de Laan*; Kaaps for moppies; Sesotho and Setswana for proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** witchcraft accusations have led to killings; anti-foreigner rumor has preceded violence; HIV status is private; slander and defamation as offenses belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** rumor, scandal and community-talk studies about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
