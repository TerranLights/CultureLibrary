## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Khoe and San descendants in the Eastern, Western and Northern Cape:** check how Baartman's return and later reburials are spoken of as acknowledgment, and which groups claim to speak for whom.
- **VhaVenda, Balobedu and amaNdebele royal houses:** check which kingships and queenships are formally acknowledged and how their people respond to being included or left out.
- **Matric candidates in rural Limpopo and the Eastern Cape against Gauteng and Western Cape suburbs:** check how families and schools celebrate a published pass, and what silence follows a missing name.
- **Domestic workers from Limpopo, the Eastern Cape and Mpumalanga:** check memoirs and union records for thanks given and withheld.
- **Afrikaans writers against isiXhosa, isiZulu and Sesotho writers:** check which prizes exist in each language and who sits on the juries.
- **Chinese South Africans:** check community accounts of being left out of both apartheid-era and later classifications.
- **Bapedi and Batswana of Limpopo and North West:** check graduation-day praise singing and feasts as family recognition of a degree.
- **Jewish South Africans:** check community honors and memorial books for how service is marked inside a minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors, TRC records and renaming; mixed for school and workplace recognition; thin for everyday thanking customs in Tshivenda, Xitsonga and siSwati.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu and isiXhosa for praise names and death notices; Afrikaans for prize histories and TRC writing; Sesotho and Setswana for tlotla and graduation customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** TRC testimony concerns named victims and perpetrators; publishing pupils' results raises privacy disputes; recognition of traditional leaders has a legal side that belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** honors, prizes and remembrance practice of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, withheld from this recognition sheet.
