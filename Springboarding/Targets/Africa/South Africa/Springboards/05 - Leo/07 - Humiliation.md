## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Afrikaners of the Free State and former Transvaal:** check family memory of the camps and the school language boards against official commemoration.
- **University workers and students in Bloemfontein, Stellenbosch and Pretoria:** check how residence initiation and the Reitz case reshaped rules on hazing.
- **Coloured farmworkers in the Western Cape, in their own self-designation:** check how former dop-system families speak of it and of employers.
- **Khoe and San descendants:** check reactions to *Miscast* and to museum displays as group-level lowering.
- **isiXhosa speakers in the Eastern Cape:** check where imbongi rebuke and the mocking of the uninitiated (inkwenkwe) fall between teasing and humiliation.
- **Sesotho and Setswana speakers in the Free State and North West:** check teasing and mocking songs at weddings and feasts and where they turn hurtful.
- **Township youth in Gauteng and eThekwini:** check school bullying and online shaming patterns in the youth survey and in press reports.
- **Chinese South Africans:** check oral histories for the slights of classification and how they were absorbed or answered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for apartheid-era degradation, war memory and satire; mixed for bullying and online shaming; thin for teasing traditions in Tshivenda, Xitsonga and siSwati.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans and Dutch for camp memory and school records; isiXhosa for imbongi and initiation talk; Sesotho and Setswana for shame vocabulary; Kaaps for farm testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation schools (ulwaluko and others) are closed to outsiders and involve injury and death; sexual violence used to humiliate, including attacks on lesbians, needs survivor-led sources; footage of the Reitz case shows identifiable people. Revenge belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** histories of segregation slights and their memory among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left off the humiliation sheet.
