## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Batswana of North West and Basotho of the Free State:** check how the kgotla is run today and whether its consensus ideal shows in local meetings, churches and schools.
- **abaThembu and other isiXhosa speakers of the Eastern Cape:** check how the court model in Mandela's account compares with present village practice.
- **isiZulu speakers in KwaZulu-Natal and on the mines:** check the induna as foreman and headman, and what makes workers follow one.
- **Balobedu of Limpopo and women leaders elsewhere:** check how queenship and women's councils compare with men's leadership in the same districts.
- **Griqua communities of the Northern Cape and Free State and Nama of the Richtersveld:** check how kapteins and councils are chosen and contested now.
- **Followers of the Zion Christian Church and of the Shembe church:** check succession disputes and what members say makes a leader legitimate.
- **Afrikaners:** check the dominee, the farmer-leader and the Broederbond's legacy as distinct models of leading.
- **Union members in Gauteng, North West and Mpumalanga:** check shop-steward elections and recall.
- **Cape Muslims and Jewish South Africans:** check how imams and the Chief Rabbi's office lead communities that are minorities in the nation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, church and union leadership; mixed for business and team leadership; thin for women's informal leadership in Tshivenda, Xitsonga and siSwati sources.
- **Languages needed:** Setswana and Sesotho for kgotla records and proverbs; isiZulu for church and induna material; Afrikaans for Broederbond and church sources; Dutch for early Cape records on imams and captains.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** succession disputes in royal houses and churches are live and litigated; the formal powers of traditional leaders belong to Capricorn; colonial writers recast chiefs to suit indirect rule.
- **Held for review:** leaders and organizers from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside on this leadership sheet.
