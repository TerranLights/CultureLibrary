# South Africa: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, isiNdebele, Afrikaans, Kaaps and South African English; |xam (archival) and Khoekhoegowab for signs and herding. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nguni cattle keepers in KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape:** check which hide patterns and horn shapes herders name, and at what age boys learn them.
- **Karoo and Eastern Cape wool farms, Afrikaans- and isiXhosa-speaking shearing teams:** check how classing skill is passed on and who is credited with it.
- **Kimberley sorters and Oudtshoorn feather graders, including Jewish merchant families of the Little Karoo:** check how the sorting eye was rewarded in rank and pay.
- **Cape winelands cellar workers, Coloured in their own self-designation, and newer black winemakers:** check who is credited with the palate and how that has changed since 1994.
- **Cape Muslim and Jewish households:** check how far each relies on certifiers and how far on its own reading of labels and kitchens.
- **Batswana and Bapedi of the North West and Limpopo:** check how hidden causes of misfortune are sought, with care for the accused.
- **isiZulu-speaking young women and men:** check how choosing or accepting a partner (ukuqoma) is described by each side and by elders.
- **Gauteng and Western Cape WhatsApp users across languages:** check how forwarded rumors are tested or passed on.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for taste industries and fraud data; mixed for courtship; thin for Khoe and San sign-reading outside the /Xam archive.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for cattle-hide names and courtship terms; |xam for the presentiment texts; Afrikaans for wool and feather trade records; Setswana for the North West sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accusations of witchcraft after a diviner's verdict have ended in killings; race classification hurt people still living; scam victims, often elderly grant recipients, may not wish to be named. Classification law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** connoisseurship, certification and character-judging sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; the discernment sheet does not use them.
