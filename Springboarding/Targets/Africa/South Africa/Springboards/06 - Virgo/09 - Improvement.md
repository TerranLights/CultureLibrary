*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **amaqaba and amagqobhoka families of the Eastern Cape:** check how each side spoke of the other's road to a better life, and whether the split still shapes talk among grandchildren.
- **Descendants of Natal's amakholwa settlements:** check how schooling and land ownership were told as family advance across generations.
- **Rural communities of Sekhukhuneland, Pondoland and the former Ciskei:** check how residents remember fenced fields and cattle culls sold to them as improvement.
- **Afrikaner families who left farm tenancy for town in the 1920s to 1940s:** check how family stories describe the climb and who helped.
- **Khoe descendants of the Kat River and Griqua communities:** check accounts of farms built up and then lost.
- **N|uu speakers and the ‡Khomani of the Kalahari:** check what renewal means when a community sets its own terms.
- **Cape Flats families removed from District Six and other areas, Coloured in their own self-designation:** check how forced moves broke or reshaped stories of getting ahead.
- **Gauteng's new black middle class:** check how moving to the suburbs is narrated in family, church and social media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for betterment, mission and poor-white history; mixed for post-1994 self-improvement culture; thin for Tshivenda and Xitsonga terms and for renewal led by Khoe and San communities.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa for the Red and School terms; isiZulu for amakholwa letters; Afrikaans for Reddingsdaadbond papers; N|uu for revival materials; Sepedi for Sekhukhuneland memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** imposed "civilizing" and betterment hurt communities that still remember it; skin lightening touches shame; Black Economic Empowerment codes and land reform are law and go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** uplift, schooling-for-advance and self-help sources about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the improvement sheet.
