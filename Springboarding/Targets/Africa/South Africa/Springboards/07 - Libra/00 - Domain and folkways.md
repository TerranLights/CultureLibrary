## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polygynous customary households in rural KwaZulu-Natal and Mpumalanga, against Gauteng civil marriages:** check which regime couples register under and who decides it.
- **Batswana kgotla villages in North West, against Basotho of the eastern Free State:** check how a council hearing opens and who may speak.
- **Afrikaner families of the Free State and Northern Cape:** check opsit courtship, church weddings, and how the republics' treaties are retold.
- **Cape Muslim families of the Bo-Kaap and Cape Flats:** check how a religious marriage stood under state law before and after the 2022 Constitutional Court ruling on Muslim marriages.
- **‡Khomani and Nama of the Northern Cape:** check how the Richtersveld case (2003) wrote a people's customary ownership into a judgment.
- **Jewish South Africans:** check the Beth Din in marriage and religious divorce beside section 5A of the Divorce Act.
- **Chinese South Africans:** check the 2008 High Court ruling on their classification under empowerment law, a case of standing as written.
- **Vatsonga, VhaVenda and amaNdebele families:** check each one's bridewealth terms and negotiators, where Limpopo and Mpumalanga sources are thinner.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, judgments and marriage statistics; mixed on everyday partnerships and rivalries; thin on Khoe and San marriage and redress in their own words. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled (its tracker records no change), so the Libra sheets are written for the nine present provinces. The spirit of the law and trust in courts go to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** VOC Dutch for the Council of Justice; Setswana for kgotla records; isiZulu for the praises; Sesotho for Thaba Bosiu memory; Afrikaans for the republics' conventions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ukuthwala (abduction into marriage) cases in the Eastern Cape; domestic violence; families' private bridewealth sums.
- **Held for review:** marriage-regime, family-forum and partnership-law sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left off this domain sheet.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the latest Stats SA *Marriages and Divorces* release and tabulate the three regimes by province.
2. Search SAFLII for "Recognition of Customary Marriages Act" and read *Bhe* and *Mayelane*.
3. Search the Western Cape Archives catalog (NAAIRS) for "Raad van Justitie".
4. Pull the Reconciliation Barometer reports and question wording from the IJR site.
5. Find Schapera, Kuper and Hunter in a library catalog and read their marriage and hearings chapters first.
