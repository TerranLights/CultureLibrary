## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stokvel members in Soweto and in rural Eastern Cape villages:** check how the order of payouts is drawn, and what is done to a defaulter in each.
- **Rugby followers in Pretoria and Bloemfontein, and in Eastern Cape Black rugby clubs:** check how a selection target is argued as fair or unfair in each setting.
- **‡Khomani and other Khoi and San member communities:** check who received what from the benefit-sharing funds, and which groups said they were left out.
- **Platinum-belt mineworkers (Batswana, Bapedi, amaXhosa and Basotho):** check how the 2012 Marikana wage demand was put as a fair-wage claim in each home language.
- **Coloured farmworkers of the Hex River Valley, in their own self-designation, and Afrikaner farm owners:** check how each side framed the 2012 De Doorns wage strike.
- **Cape Malay families:** check how heirs divide an estate under Islamic shares and how that meets the intestate default.
- **Vatsonga and VhaVenda households:** check which cuts of a slaughtered animal go to whom at a feast, and who may object.
- **Portuguese and Chinese South African family firms in Gauteng:** check how ownership and procurement scoring land on family-held businesses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, sport and labor quarrels; mixed for household division and stokvel rules; thin for Khoe and San sharing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Callaway's tales; Afrikaans for the farm-strike and rugby press; Setswana and isiXhosa for Marikana testimony; Khoekhoegowab for benefit-sharing meetings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race-based redress is a live dispute; Marikana deaths; Indigenous knowledge in benefit-sharing deals may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** shopkeeping, trade-fairness and sport-selection material tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside the fairness sheet.
