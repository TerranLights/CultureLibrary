*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Job seekers in small Free State and North West municipalities:** check how they say a municipal post is won, by open advert or by party branch.
- **Afrikaans and English-speaking advocates at the Pretoria and Johannesburg bars:** check how the cab-rank rule is taught to pupils and when exceptions are argued.
- **amaXhosa and VhaVenda royal houses with claims before the Nhlapo Commission:** check how kin evidence and oral genealogy were read, and how each house answered the result.
- **Khoe descendants of the Kat River valley:** check how Ordinance 50 and the later rebellion are told in local memory.
- **Gauteng motorists and minibus-taxi drivers:** check how "cool drink" requests at roadblocks are described, and whether anyone reports them.
- **Supporters of the Soweto soccer clubs:** check what referee-bias talk after derby matches singles out.
- **Coloured and Cape Muslim residents of Mitchells Plain, in their own self-designations:** check how housing-list order is judged and who is said to skip it.
- **Royal Bafokeng Nation, North West:** check how jobs and bursaries funded by platinum royalties are allotted and challenged.
- **Portuguese and Chinese South African traders:** check how licences and inspections are obtained, and what is said of paid fixers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich since 2016 (capture inquiries, NGO data, judgments); mixed on everyday favoritism; thin on how Khoe and San communities chose arbiters among themselves.
- **Languages needed:** VOC Dutch for landdrost records; Tshivenda and isiXhosa for royal-claims testimony; Afrikaans for "vriendjiepolitiek" in the press; Setswana for Bafokeng sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inquiry findings name living people, some still on trial; whistleblowers' safety; royal succession disputes are active.
- **Held for review:** capture-inquiry chapters and patronage studies centered on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept out of the impartiality sheet.
