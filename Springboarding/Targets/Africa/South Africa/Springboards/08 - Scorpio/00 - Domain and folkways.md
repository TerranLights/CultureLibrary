*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vatsonga and Mapulana villages of Bushbuckridge:** check whether a death is explained one way to a surveillance interviewer and another at the funeral.
- **isiZulu-speaking homesteads against Durban and Johannesburg households:** check when ukubuyisa is held and who leads it once the homestead is far away.
- **amaXhosa of the former Ciskei and Transkei:** compare whether the 1857 famine is remembered as a loss, a lesson or a deception in each district.
- **Cape Muslim families of the Bo-Kaap and Athlone:** check the pace of burial and the round of shrine visits as practice.
- **Afrikaner families of the Free State and Karoo:** check which dead are tended in farm graveyards and which are honored at monuments.
- **‡Khomani and !Xun communities of the Northern Cape:** check who now speaks for the |xam narratives and on what terms they are retold.
- **VhaVenda around Thathe Vondo:** check access to royal graves and sacred forest, as custodians describe it.
- **Jewish, Portuguese and Chinese South Africans:** check the stone-setting, the month's-mind mass and grave-sweeping, each in its own cemetery section.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mortality figures, funeral ethnography and post-1994 memory work; mixed for sex ways outside HIV research; thin for VhaVenda and Batswana death ways in print. The tracker records no change of lands and the author has not yet drawn the post-war borders, so the Scorpio sheets take today's nine provinces as the ground. Wills and succession law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Berglund's ancestor terms; isiXhosa for district memory of 1857; |xam for the death narratives; Afrikaans for monument speeches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some families keep burial and initiation rites closed; ancestral graves on farmland are disputed; a witchcraft accusation can endanger the accused.
- **Held for review:** funeral, cremation and memorial sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept off this domain sheet.
