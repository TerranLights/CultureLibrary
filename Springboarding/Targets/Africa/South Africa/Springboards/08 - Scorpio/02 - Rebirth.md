*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **amaXhosa initiates in rural lodges against those in Gauteng and Cape Town:** check whether a man initiated near a city, or by medical circumcision, is treated as fully remade at home.
- **Bapedi of Sekhukhune and Basotho of QwaQwa:** compare the new name and the new speech forms a returning initiate is given.
- **VhaVenda and Vatsonga girls:** check what standing domba and khomba confer on return.
- **Zion Christian Church members at Moria and Nazareth Baptist members at Ebuhleni:** compare the place of water, white robes and pilgrimage in becoming new.
- **Afrikaner charismatic congregations and Dutch Reformed parishes:** check whether "wedergebore" requires a dated moment of conversion or a lifelong process.
- **Coloured men leaving the Numbers, in their own self-designation:** check how Cape Flats churches and streets receive one, and how long he is watched.
- **!Ora, N|uu and Khoekhoegowab learners in the Northern and Western Cape:** check whether learners speak of reclaiming an identity or adding one.
- **Jewish South Africans returning to observance in Johannesburg:** check how families speak of the newly observant member.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church history and initiation controversy; mixed for addiction recovery; thin for revival-era Afrikaans conversion narratives outside church archives. Forgiveness after crime and amnesty belong partly with Libra and Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa for the lodge and Ntsikana's hymn; Sesotho for Mofolo; Tshivenda for Blacking's song texts; Setswana for Comaroff's Zionist terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** what is said inside initiation lodges is not to be published; initiate deaths are a raw subject; healers' callings are personal.
- **Held for review:** conversion, revival and recovery accounts from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left unconsulted for rebirth.
