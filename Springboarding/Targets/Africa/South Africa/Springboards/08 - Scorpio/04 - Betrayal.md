# South Africa: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Tshivenda, Xitsonga, Afrikaans, South African English and Kaaps; VOC-era Dutch for the first Cape journal; Portuguese for the 1975 arrivals. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former askaris and the families of activists they helped capture:** check how each side names the act and whether any meeting has taken place since.
- **Free State families with a joiner in the family line:** check whether the label is still spoken of at gatherings or kept back.
- **isiZulu speakers and Afrikaners at uMgungundlovu:** compare which betrayal each telling of 1838 stresses.
- **Griqua of Kokstad and Griquatown:** check how the loss of the diamond fields is narrated in family and church memory.
- **amaMpondo rock-drill operators at Marikana:** compare the words used in 2012 for those who stayed with the old union and those who left it.
- **Coloured families of the Cape Flats, in their own self-designation:** check what follows naming someone a gang informer.
- **Jewish South Africans:** check how community bodies spoke of members jailed for struggle activity.
- **Portuguese South Africans who arrived from Mozambique and Angola in 1975:** check whether decolonization is remembered as Lisbon's desertion of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for informers and collaboration (TRC); mixed for infidelity beyond HIV research; thin for Sotho-Tswana and Venda vocabulary of treachery. Treason trials go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Languages needed:** VOC Dutch for the fort journal; Afrikaans for Grundlingh and Brink; isiZulu for Hunter's terms; Tshivenda for *Muvhango*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living informers and their families; HIV status and paternity; naming suspected impimpi can still endanger people.
- **Held for review:** histories of loyalty, defection and broken promises involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not used for betrayal.
