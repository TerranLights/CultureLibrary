# South Africa: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Khilobedu and Tshivenda, Xitsonga, Afrikaans, South African English and Kaaps; !Xun and Khwedam for Platfontein memory. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so; losing control and giving it up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balobedu villages near Modjadjiskloof and Vhembe farmers on irrigation schemes:** compare who is credited with bringing or holding back rain.
- **Rural and urban wives in the SADHS sample:** compare province and settlement splits in each item on a husband's control.
- **Older winelands workers of Paarl and Stellenbosch:** check how the dop and the farmer's hold on their time are remembered.
- **!Xun and Khwe families at Platfontein:** check how army-camp years at Schmidtsdrift are told by veterans and by their children.
- **Afrikaner households of the Free State and Pretoria:** check how the volksmoeder ideal is recalled in church magazines against family memoirs.
- **Gauteng suburbs against Soweto and Alexandra:** check who buys guards and cameras and who relies on street committees and neighbors.
- **Basotho and Batswana families whose men worked the Kimberley compounds:** check what is remembered of searches and fixed hours.
- **Coloured carnival troupes, in their own self-designation, and Chinese South Africans:** check how troupes keep discipline, and how Chinese families recall being kept to certain areas and trades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor control, partner-control survey items and security figures; mixed for parenting; thin for Khilobedu and Tshivenda accounts of rainmaking today. Pass laws and coercive-control statutes as written go to Libra; obedience and legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Khilobedu or Sepedi for rain rites; Sesotho for Kas Maine's world; Afrikaans for the volksmoeder press; !Xun and Khwedam for Platfontein memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner abuse; fetal alcohol damage tied to the dop system; rain-queen rites are not fully open.
- **Held for review:** labor, household and movement-control histories bearing on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back for control.
