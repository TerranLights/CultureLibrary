# South Africa: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Afrikaans, South African English and Kaaps; Cape Dutch for frontier commando records. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; and its alternatives, from payment to forgiveness.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Neighboring sections in Msinga and Tugela Ferry:** check what has ended past feuds: payment, marriage, a chief's ruling or exhaustion.
- **Taxi associations of Gauteng and the KwaZulu-Natal coast:** check who decides on a hit and how members justify it to one another.
- **Bapedi villages of Sekhukhune with Mapogo branches:** check whether members call the beatings punishment, protection or payback.
- **Cape Muslim families of the Bo-Kaap and Coloured families of Hanover Park, in their own self-designation:** compare how 1996 and PAGAD are remembered in each.
- **Afrikaners and isiZulu speakers at Ncome on 16 December:** compare whether the day is spoken of as a vow, a victory, a massacre or reconciliation.
- **Coloured and San-descended families of the Hantam and Roggeveld:** check what family memory keeps of the commando era.
- **Gugulethu residents:** check how the Biehl forgiveness is spoken of by neighbors of the men forgiven.
- **Chinese and Portuguese shopkeepers:** check whether robberies are answered through community networks, private guards or the police.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for vigilantism, taxi killings and war reprisals; mixed for feud settlement; thin for Sotho-Tswana revenge vocabulary and Khoe and San accounts of the frontier raids. Private revenge set against state punishment is Capricorn's; compensation for wrongs as written law is Libra's.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Msinga and Bryant's sources; Sepedi for Mapogo meetings; Cape Dutch for commando reports; Afrikaans for Vow Day speeches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living perpetrators and victims; gang and taxi violence; racial framing of farm attacks and of the commando era.
- **Held for review:** feud, riot and reprisal histories involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not used for revenge.
