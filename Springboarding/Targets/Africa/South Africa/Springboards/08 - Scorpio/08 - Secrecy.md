# South Africa: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Married women of the rural Eastern Cape and KwaZulu-Natal:** check which in-law names and sounds are avoided, and whether younger wives in towns keep the custom.
- **Afrikaner families of Pretoria and Stellenbosch:** check whether a father's Broederbond membership was ever spoken of at home.
- **Coloured families split by reclassification, in their own self-designation:** check how those who stayed speak of relatives who crossed the line.
- **Gay men of the Cape Flats and of Umlazi and KwaMashu:** compare who learns Gayle and isiNgqumo, and from whom.
- **People living with HIV in KwaMashu and Khayelitsha:** check to whom status is told first, and how that changed once treatment was free.
- **‡Khomani, !Xun and Khwe communities:** check which knowledge their councils say may be published.
- **VhaVenda custodians of Lake Fundudzi:** check who answers outsiders' questions and what they decline to answer.
- **Chinese South Africans and English-speaking Freemasons:** check whether papers or birthplaces were kept quiet in the era of restricted entry; check what lodges published of their members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrets, exposés and queer archives; mixed for family secrecy; thin for Tshivenda and Xitsonga terms and for hlonipha outside isiXhosa and isiZulu. State-secrecy laws and privacy statutes are Libra's; the spirit of secrecy and transparency in government is Capricorn's.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans for the Broederbond and *Vrye Weekblad*; isiXhosa for hlonipha; Kaaps and Gayle for Cage; isiZulu for isiNgqumo.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** San, Venda and initiation knowledge is restricted; HIV status; sexual orientation; reclassified families' private histories.
- **Held for review:** family-secrecy, reclassification and disclosure sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, withheld from the secrecy sheet.
