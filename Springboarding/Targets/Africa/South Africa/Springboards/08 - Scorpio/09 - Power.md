*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Residents on Ingonyama Trust land in uMkhanyakude and Zululand:** check how people weigh the inkosi's word over land against the municipality's.
- **Bapedi of Sekhukhune:** check how royal succession quarrels are talked of in villages and in court.
- **Batswana villages in Bojanala and Ngaka Modiri Molema:** check how a leader's seriti is said to grow or shrink.
- **Khoe and San groups seeking recognition:** check which leaders their own members accept, and why.
- **Supporters and critics of Orania and the Solidarity Movement:** compare how each describes the aim of Afrikaner-run institutions.
- **Cape Muslim congregations:** check where an imam's voice ends and the congregation's begins in mosque affairs.
- **Veteran women organizers of Gauteng and the Eastern Cape:** check how they now describe what women's collective power won or lost.
- **Jewish and Chinese South Africans:** check how the bodies that speak for each community choose their leaders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political economy and traditional leadership; mixed for everyday household power; thin for Xitsonga and Tshivenda words for influence in print. Legitimate authority, hierarchy and power ways belong to Capricorn; corruption as written offense to Libra.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for the Shaka testimony; Sepedi for Sekhukhune sources; Sesotho for Mofolo; isiZulu for Shembe hymns; Afrikaans for Orania's own writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** chieftaincy succession disputes; land and the king's trust; the politics of Afrikaner self-determination; Khoe and San recognition claims.
- **Held for review:** patronage, political and economic-power histories tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not cited for power.
