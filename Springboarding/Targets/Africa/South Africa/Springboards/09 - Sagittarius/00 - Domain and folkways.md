## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Limpopo (Bapedi, VhaVenda, Vatsonga, Balobedu):** check how ZCC membership sits beside royal rites such as the Modjadji rain queens', district by district.
- **KwaZulu-Natal isiZulu speakers:** separate amaNazaretha, Catholic (Mariannhill) and Pentecostal congregations.
- **Eastern Cape amaXhosa:** check how far Lovedale and Healdtown schooling shaped later church and intellectual leaders.
- **Western Cape Cape Muslims and Coloured Christians (in their own self-designation):** compare mosque, Moravian and Dutch Reformed Mission congregations within the same towns.
- **Afrikaners of the Free State, North West and the Boland:** separate the three Reformed churches (NG, Hervormde, Gereformeerde) and newer charismatic congregations.
- **Khoe and San of the Northern Cape (‡Khomani, Nama, !Xun and Khwe) and the Griqua:** check who now speaks for older religious traditions and through which church or council.
- **English-speaking South Africans and Gauteng's Jewish, Portuguese and Chinese South Africans:** check which dioceses, synagogues, parishes and temples carry each group's religious life.
- **Batswana and Basotho of North West and the Free State:** compare Setiloane's account with Kuruman and Thaba Nchu mission records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church and mission history and for post-1994 public religion; mixed for AICs, whose records may sit with the churches themselves; thin in print for Tshivenda and Xitsonga philosophical writing. The tracker lists no change of lands and the author has not yet set the post-war borders, so all Sagittarius sheets work from today's nine provinces. Religious-freedom law and the CRL Rights Commission's mandate go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu and Sesotho for AIC hymnals and sermons; Afrikaans for Reformed church archives; |xam and Khoekhoegowab for the oldest narratives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation knowledge and some healers' training are closed to outsiders; San rock art sites can be harmed by publicity; check for disputed leadership successions before citing any one church faction.
- **Held for review:** temple, mosque and festival life of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept off the domain sheet.
