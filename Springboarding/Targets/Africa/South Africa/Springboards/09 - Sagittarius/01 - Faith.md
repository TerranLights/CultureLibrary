*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tshidi Barolong and other Batswana of Mahikeng:** check whether families hold Methodist and Zionist membership together, and how that has shifted since Comaroff's fieldwork.
- **amaXhosa of the Tyhume and Kat River valleys:** check where Ntsikana's hymn is still sung and which congregations claim him.
- **Balobedu around Modjadjiskloof:** check how the royal council and congregants each speak of rain, the queen and God.
- **Coloured congregations of Genadendal, Elim and Mamre:** compare mission-town faith life with that of Cape Town's suburbs.
- **Afrikaners in NG and charismatic congregations:** check whether *geloof* and *bekering* are used the same way in both.
- **Vatsonga of Limpopo and Mpumalanga:** check Swiss Mission heritage against ZCC and ancestral practice in Xitsonga faith words.
- **Portuguese South Africans of Madeiran descent in Gauteng:** check Fátima devotion in their parish calendars.
- **Cape Muslims of the Cape Flats:** check what role the local imam and the older *Tuan* teachers each play in a family's faith.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church-going and prayer measures and for mission-era writing; mixed for AIC faith, which lives in hymns and testimony more than print; thin for faith items in Tshivenda and isiNdebele sources. Secular faith in progress ties to hope (Aquarius).
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Shembe's hymns; Setswana for Mahikeng Zionist testimony; Xitsonga to read Junod's material against speakers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** faith healing claims touch HIV and TB treatment; some church rites are for members only; older ethnographies carry period labels for people.
- **Held for review:** devotional and congregational sources of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not used on this faith sheet.
