# South Africa: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young people without work in Tembisa, Soweto and Khayelitsha:** check where they say purpose comes from when wage work is absent, then hold that beside the NEET and NIDS figures.
- **Platinum-belt mineworkers around Rustenburg and their Eastern Cape home villages:** check whether a hard job's worth is described one way at the shaft and another at home.
- **amaXhosa initiates from rural Eastern Cape and from Cape Town:** check what each says the rite gave them, and what failed rites cost.
- **Afrikaner farming families of the Karoo and Free State:** check how *roeping* to the land is phrased, and what happens to it after a farm is sold.
- **siSwati- and isiZulu-speaking healers in Mpumalanga and KwaZulu-Natal:** check how an illness is retold as *ubizo* in their own life stories.
- **Batswana of the North West:** check which deeds earn praise names now, set against Schapera's collection.
- **Jewish South Africans in Cape Town and Johannesburg:** check which communal bodies (welfare, burial and youth groups) members name when asked what gives life a point.
- **Coloured communities of the Cape Flats, as they name themselves:** check how artists and church youth workers frame purpose against gang recruitment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for life satisfaction and unemployment; few local surveys ask directly about meaning or purpose, so most evidence will be qualitative. Meaning in work ties to Virgo; hope to Aquarius.
- **Languages needed:** Setswana for Schapera's praises; isiZulu for maskanda lyrics; Kaaps for Cape Flats verse; Afrikaans for Van Wyk Louw.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and initiation deaths; initiation teachings are closed to the uninitiated; interviewees' accounts of despair need care.
- **Held for review:** life-writing and purpose narratives from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left out of the meaning sheet.
