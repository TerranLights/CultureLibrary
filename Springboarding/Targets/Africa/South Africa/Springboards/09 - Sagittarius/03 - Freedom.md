# South Africa: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, siSwati, Afrikaans, SA English, Kaaps; |xam for the Bleek and Lloyd testimony. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cape Muslim and Coloured families of Cape Town and the Boland, descendants of the enslaved:** check which days and places they mark for emancipation, and whether family memory links Tweede Nuwe Jaar to it.
- **Khoe descendants of the Kat River valley and the Griqua of Griquatown and Kranshoek:** check how land loss is told as a freedom story.
- **Afrikaners in the old republic heartlands and at Orania (Northern Cape):** check what residents mean by *vryheid* and self-determination, in their own words and in their critics'.
- **People of the former Venda, Transkei, Bophuthatswana and Ciskei:** check how bantustan independence is remembered, district by district.
- **Bapedi of Sekhukhuneland:** check how 1879 and the 1958 revolt are told as fights for freedom.
- **amaNdebele of Mpumalanga:** check memory of the farm labor imposed after 1883 and how it is spoken of now.
- **Women who marched in 1956 and their daughters:** compare accounts across language groups.
- **LGBTQ South Africans in Soweto and Gugulethu:** check how freedom at Pride is weighed against safety at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for struggle memory and Cape slavery; thin for bantustan-era views from residents; risk of one master narrative crowding out others. Freedoms as written in the Bill of Rights go to Libra; their spirit to Capricorn. Autonomy and independence stay in Aries.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa and isiZulu for freedom songs; Afrikaans for republic memory and Orania; |xam for //Kabbo; Sepedi for Sekhukhuneland accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some struggle songs have contested lyrics; Orania and land restitution provoke strong views; queer interviewees may face risk if named.
- **Held for review:** passive-resistance and protest history linked to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not scouted on this freedom sheet.
