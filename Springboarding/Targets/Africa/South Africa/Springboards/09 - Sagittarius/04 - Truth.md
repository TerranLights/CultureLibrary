*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, Afrikaans, SA English. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how it is decided: whom people trust to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, their own experience), and how disputes over facts are settled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Witnesses at TRC hearings in the Eastern Cape and Gauteng, and families who never testified:** check whether the commission's version is accepted as their truth.
- **isiZulu-speaking communities of the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands and south coast:** check how accounts of the 1980s-90s political violence differ between neighboring villages, and which got written down.
- **Bapedi and Kgaga clients of diviners in Limpopo:** check when a reading of the bones outweighs a clinic's diagnosis, and when it doesn't.
- **Afrikaans readers in Stellenbosch and Pretoria:** compare how dissident and mainstream Afrikaans papers were trusted then and are remembered now.
- **Khayelitsha and Lusikisiki households touched by the AIDS treatment fight:** check memory of rival health claims and who was believed.
- **VhaVenda and Vatsonga royal houses:** check how genealogies are recited and how disputes over them are settled.
- **Jewish South Africans in Cape Town and Johannesburg:** check how Holocaust education is used to frame denial of other histories for visiting school classes.
- **Northern Cape Khoe and San heritage groups:** check whose oral account is accepted when origins are disputed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the TRC, misinformation and investigative journalism; mixed for older oral evidence (rich in KwaZulu-Natal, sparse for Tshivenda and Xitsonga); perjury, evidence and courtroom truth go to Libra, the spirit of public inquiry to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Stuart Archive testimony; Sepedi for divination terms; Afrikaans for the dissident press; isiXhosa for TRC hearings in the Eastern Cape.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** TRC testimony holds trauma; KwaZulu-Natal political violence is still raw; diviners' methods may be closed.
- **Held for review:** news, history and commemoration sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside the truth sheet.
