# South Africa: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, SA English, Kaaps. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not the legal sense of a finding of guilt).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **NG Kerk congregations that lost dissident ministers in the 1960s-80s:** check how they now tell of those breaks, and whether the dissidents were later welcomed back.
- **English- and Afrikaans-speaking conscripts' families of the 1980s:** compare how parishes and schools in each language treated sons who refused to serve.
- **Cape Muslims of Claremont and Athlone:** check how Imam Haron's stand is commemorated and taught in madrasahs.
- **amaXhosa around Makhanda (formerly Grahamstown):** check how Nxele is claimed after the town took his name, and by whom.
- **Shack dwellers of eThekwini and Ekurhuleni:** check how members explain holding firm, against the pull of party patronage.
- **Platinum-belt mineworkers near Rustenburg (amaXhosa, Basotho, Vatsonga, Bapedi):** check how strike solidarity and breaking ranks are named in each language.
- **Jehovah's Witness congregations and other small faiths:** check how refusal on religious grounds is remembered inside the community.
- **Black Sash veterans and their daughters:** check whether the silent-stand form carried into later movements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for anti-apartheid conviction and archives; mixed for post-1994 movements; thin for conviction outside politics (religious refusals, food ethics). Treason trials and objection law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); courage itself is Aries.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans for joiner memory, Naudé's sermons and Brink's original; isiXhosa for Nxele and Ntsikana traditions; isiZulu for Abahlali meetings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *impimpi* label led to killings in the 1980s; Marikana and other strike deaths are raw; objectors' families may still be divided.
- **Held for review:** resistance-campaign and principled-refusal sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside the conviction sheet.
