# South Africa: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Basotho of the eastern Free State and QwaQwa:** check famo and lifela lines for how leaving and coming back are judged.
- **Nama of the Richtersveld:** check who still moves stock between seasonal posts, and how the young describe that round against leaving for town.
- **Vatsonga of the Limpopo lowveld:** check how a first trip to Joni is told by the men who made it and by those who stayed.
- **amaXhosa of the former Transkei with families in Cape Town:** check what "home-home" means to children born in the city, and when the December return stops.
- **Afrikaners of the Free State and Gauteng:** check padkos and the holiday drive to the coast as family ritual, and how Trichardt and the Dorslandtrek are retold.
- **Cape Muslims:** check Hajj farewell and welcome customs, and who is honored on return.
- **Chinese South Africans:** check whether family memory separates the 1904-10 mine laborers from the free merchant families who came earlier and later.
- **Portuguese South Africans of Madeiran origin in Gauteng:** check how trips back to the island for village feasts are planned, and which generation still makes them.
- **Hosts and African newcomers in Alexandra and Hillbrow:** check what each side calls the other, and where welcome and suspicion fall.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migrant labor, tourism figures and xenophobia; mixed for pilgrimage outside the big churches; thin for Tshivenda travel songs. Short journeys and commuting stay in Gemini; homesickness in Cancer. South Africans abroad (London, Perth, Auckland) are comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for lifela; Khoekhoegowab for the Nama round; Xitsonga for Joni memory; Afrikaans and Dutch for Trichardt; isiXhosa for the December return.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** xenophobic violence and its slurs; mine-hostel sexual and health histories; migrants without papers who cannot travel freely.
- **Held for review:** arrival, homeland-visit and pilgrimage sources tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept off the travel sheet.
