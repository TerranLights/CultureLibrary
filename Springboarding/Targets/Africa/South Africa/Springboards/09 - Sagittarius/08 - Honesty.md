## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nama and Afrikaans-speaking families of Namaqualand:** check which jackal tales are still told to children and what each teller says they teach.
- **isiZulu storytellers in rural KwaZulu-Natal:** check whether Hlakanyana is presented as a model or a warning.
- **Matric learners and teachers in districts named in the copying cases:** check how each side accounted for what happened, against districts that were not named.
- **Traders and regular buyers at Johannesburg's Bree Street and Mai-Mai markets:** check how fair weight and fair price are vouched for without receipts.
- **VhaVenda depositors in Vhembe:** check how the VBS loss is described, and whom they blame.
- **Cape Muslim and Jewish shoppers:** check what makes them trust or doubt a certification mark.
- **Small business owners in Mpumalanga and North West towns:** check when "tenderpreneur" is used and whether it is spoken with scorn or envy.
- **Afrikaans and English cricket followers:** check how the Cronje case is remembered in each language's press.
