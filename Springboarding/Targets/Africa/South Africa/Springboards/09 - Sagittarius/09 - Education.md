## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern Cape families with Lovedale, Healdtown and Fort Hare generations:** check how the teacher and minister are spoken of now, compared with the 1950s.
- **First-generation students from Limpopo villages at Turfloop and Univen, and at Wits or UCT:** check what their families expect a degree to bring home, and whether the campus changes that.
- **Afrikaans students and parents in Stellenbosch and Pretoria:** compare arguments for Afrikaans-medium study with those of students who felt excluded by it.
- **Coloured students and staff at UWC, in their own self-designation:** check how the campus's segregated founding is narrated by each generation.
- **Batswana alumni of Tiger Kloof (Vryburg) and the Mahikeng campus:** check how a mission education is remembered in North West families.
- **TVET artisans in Free State and Mpumalanga mining towns:** check how a trade certificate is ranked against a degree, by artisans and by their parents.
- **Jewish day-school families in Johannesburg and Cape Town:** check what a community school is said to give beyond marks.
- **Unemployed graduates in Gauteng:** check how they describe the gap between the degree's promise and work.

## 4. Flags
