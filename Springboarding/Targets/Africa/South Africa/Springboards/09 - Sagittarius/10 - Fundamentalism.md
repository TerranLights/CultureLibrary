# South Africa: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** Afrikaans, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Sepedi, SA English, Kaaps. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Members of the NG, Hervormde, Gereformeerde and Afrikaanse Protestantse churches:** check how each congregation states its view of scripture and of the Vow, and where it draws the line with the others.
- **Rural Afrikaners in the North West and Free State who read Siener's prophecies:** check how prophecy is used to explain present events, and who in the community rejects it.
- **Congregants of "prophet" churches in Soshanguve and the Limpopo towns:** check what members say first drew them and what made some leave.
- **Ebuhleni and Ekuphakameni Nazaretha factions in KwaZulu-Natal:** check how each reads Isaiah Shembe's texts to claim authority.
- **The Israelite congregation near Komani (formerly Queenstown):** check how 1921 is commemorated by members and by neighbors.
- **Cape Flats Muslims in Athlone and Rylands:** check how PAGAD is remembered in mosques and by families it affected.
- **Jewish families in Johannesburg's northern suburbs:** check how stricter observance is described by those who took it up and by relatives who did not.
- **Afrikaans and English homeschooling families in Gauteng and the Western Cape:** check whether religion is the reason given, and what curricula they choose.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Afrikaner civil religion and for the 2017 hearings; mixed for Pentecostal and Zionist doctrine, which lives in sermons and church media; thin for measured literalism. The word is often an outsider's charge: record who uses it of whom. Any proposal to regulate churches goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans for the Vow, Siener and APK sources; isiZulu for Nazaretha texts; isiXhosa for Israelite memory; Sepedi for Limpopo congregants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** PAGAD and far-right violence; named pastors facing charges; the Bulhoek dead; ex-members may fear reprisals.
- **Held for review:** strict-observance and revival movements among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not scouted on this fundamentalism sheet.
