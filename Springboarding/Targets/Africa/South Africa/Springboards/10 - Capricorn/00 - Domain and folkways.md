## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ingonyama Trust villages against eThekwini city wards:** check where a resident first takes a request (inkosi, induna or ward councillor), and why.
- **Balobedu of the Modjadji royal house, Limpopo:** check how a queen's rain-making office is installed and obeyed beside male chiefships.
- **VhaVenda under the thovhele and Vatsonga under hosi:** check how the Nhlapo findings on their kingships were received in villages.
- **Griqua and Nama of the Northern Cape:** check how a kaptein is chosen today and what a follower owes the captaincy.
- **Afrikaans Reformed congregations in Free State towns:** check the kerkraad's and the dominee's standing beside the farm patriarch's.
- **Cape Muslims of Bo-Kaap and the Cape Flats:** check how standing passes among imam, sheikh and the Muslim Judicial Council.
- **Soweto and East Rand youth of the 1976-1990 street committees:** check how young comrades claimed command over elders, and how that is remembered.
- **Jewish, Portuguese and Chinese South African family firms:** check who inherits control of a shop and how sons and daughters rank in it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for chieftaincy politics, mining labor and survey data; mixed for urban workplaces; thin on Khoe and San ideas of leadership in their own words. The nation's post-war lands are not yet settled (its tracker records no change), so the Capricorn sheets are written for the nine present provinces.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for the Stuart testimony; isiXhosa for izibongo of chiefs; VOC Dutch for Council of Policy minutes; Sepedi for Sekhukhune hearings; Afrikaans for kerkraad minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation lodges (ulwaluko, lebollo, koma) keep their teaching closed; royal succession quarrels are live and litigated; "baas" and "miesies" carry apartheid's insult and are described, not used.
- **Held for review:** sources on chiefly or elected leadership among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not used on this domain sheet.
