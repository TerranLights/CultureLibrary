## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Married isiXhosa-speaking women in rural Eastern Cape homesteads:** check how hlonipha speech toward in-laws is kept, softened or dropped after a move to town.
- **ZCC congregants across Limpopo and Gauteng:** check whose instruction is followed when the bishop's and a government's guidance differ.
- **Rock drill operators of the Rustenburg platinum belt:** check how strikers in 2012 chose their own leaders over union officials, and what gave those leaders standing.
- **Afrikaners born around 1965 and their parents:** check how each generation speaks of the volk's leaders and the army's call-up.
- **Residents of gang-affected Cape Flats areas, Coloured in their own self-designation:** check whom people call first in trouble (police, neighborhood watch, church, family elder) and why.
- **‡Khomani San of the southern Kalahari:** check who speaks for the community to the state: an elected land body, a traditional leader or the elders.
- **Jewish South Africans:** check how far the Chief Rabbi's word reaches across Orthodox and Progressive congregations.
- **Portuguese South African parishes of Gauteng:** check how a Madeiran or Mozambican-born family weighs the priest's word against the father's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on contested state authority (commissions, police, mines); mixed on household and school obedience; thin on San and Khoe leadership as the peoples now describe it. The permission share is scored at Stage 2 from the law in force, Libra's letter work; this sheet stays with legitimacy and obedience.
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for Mofolo's original; isiXhosa for hlonipha vocabularies; Afrikaans for Voëlvry lyrics and ECC court papers; Setswana and Sepedi for platinum-belt testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** TRC testimony of torture; the Marikana dead and their families; church leaders' disputes in the ZCC; police abuse cases still in court.
- **Held for review:** obedience and leadership material on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept off the authority sheet.
