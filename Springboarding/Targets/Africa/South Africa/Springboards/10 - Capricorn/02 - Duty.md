# South Africa: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, isiNdebele, Afrikaans, South African English and Kaaps; |xam (archival), !Xun and Khwedam; Arabic-Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **First-generation Black professionals in Gauteng:** check how the black-tax share is decided, who receives it, and how parents and children each name the obligation.
- **Grandmothers raising grandchildren in the former Transkei and northern KwaZulu-Natal:** check what they say the children and absent parents owe in return.
- **Former SADF conscripts against MK and APLA veterans:** check how each describes the call to serve, and what each says the state owes them now.
- **Cape Muslim congregations:** check how the mosque organizes ghusl and same-day burial, and who is expected to come.
- **Basotho of the eastern Free State:** check whether lithoko are still composed for returning initiates or miners, and what deeds they name.
- **Vatsonga families in Limpopo with kin across the Mozambique border:** check who decides what an earner in Gauteng sends to relatives in Mozambique, and in what form.
- **!Xun and Khwe at Platfontein:** check how former army service in Namibia is remembered, and what the community says was owed in return.
- **Johannesburg's Jewish families:** check how saying kaddish and keeping a parent's yahrzeit is shared out when siblings live abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migrant remittance and care-burden studies; mixed for civic duty, since juries do not sit and voting is voluntary; thin on Venda and Tsonga duties to the dead in print. What the law requires of citizens goes to Libra; this sheet keeps to obligation as lived and felt.
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for lithoko; |xam via the Bleek and Lloyd translations; Arabic-Afrikaans for the Tuan Guru text; isiXhosa for migrants' home-tie vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ancestral rites performed in the family alone; San veterans' service with apartheid forces; black-tax talk can shame families named in it.
- **Held for review:** caregiving and kin-support writing about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside for the duty sheet.
