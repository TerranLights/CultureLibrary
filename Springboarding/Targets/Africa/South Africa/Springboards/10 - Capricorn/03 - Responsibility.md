*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families negotiating inhlawulo in KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape:** check who carries the payment for a son's act (father, uncles or the son) and who receives it.
- **Signers and critics of the Home for All declaration:** check how each side words a person's share in a past that others ran.
- **Depositors and municipalities in Vhembe after the VBS Mutual Bank collapse (2018):** check whom VhaVenda villagers held answerable: the bank, the councils or the royal house.
- **Families of the Life Esidimeni dead in Gauteng:** check what form of answer they sought (apology, payment, prosecution) and from whom.
- **Uniting Reformed congregations of the Western and Northern Cape, Coloured in their own self-designation:** check how the Belhar Confession is taught as a statement of who answers for division.
- **Griqua and Nama land claimants:** check how a claim names those responsible for the loss of land, and across how many generations.
- **Batswana villages of Bojanala:** check whether a kgosi can be called to account at the kgotla, and how often it happens.
- **Cape Town households on both sides of the suburb and township line during Day Zero:** check who each said should save water first.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in public accountability (commissions, audits, ombud reports); mixed on personal and family answerability; thin on Khoe and San terms for fault. Criminal capacity and liability as written go to Libra.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu and isiXhosa for inhlawulo talks; Afrikaans for Krog's original and the NG Kerk's own confession texts; Tshivenda for VBS depositors' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Life Esidimeni families' grief; named officials not yet tried; pregnancy-damage talks are private family matters.
- **Held for review:** accountability and blame narratives involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside this sheet.
