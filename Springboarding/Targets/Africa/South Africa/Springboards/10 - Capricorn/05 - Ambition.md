# South Africa: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Afrikaans, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, isiNdebele, South African English, Tsotsitaal and Kaaps; Portuguese and Cantonese for family archives of those communities. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **First graduates from Limpopo villages now in Johannesburg:** check how the home village speaks of them, and what they are asked to give back.
- **Izikhothane crews of Soweto and Katlehong and their parents:** check how each generation judges spending as a sign of having made it.
- **Afrikaners raised in the volkskapitalisme years and their grandchildren:** check, in their own words, whom a career that went well was meant to serve: volk, household or self.
- **Madeiran-born Portuguese South Africans of Johannesburg's southern suburbs:** check what parents hoped children would become beyond the greengrocer's or café.
- **Chinese South Africans of Gqeberha and Johannesburg:** check how schooling choices were weighed against the family business, before and after 1994.
- **amaNdebele mural painters of Mpumalanga:** check how a painter's international career is spoken of at home in KwaMhlanga and Siyabuswa.
- **Nama and ‡Khomani youth of the Northern Cape:** check which local careers are held up as success by schools and community trusts.
- **Swenking competitors in KwaZulu-Natal migrant hostels:** check what the judges reward and what winning means back in the village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for middle-class and mobility studies; mixed on rural aspiration; thin on Khoe, San and Venda own-language talk of striving. Rising under preference policies (B-BBEE) touches law: its letter goes to Libra and its purpose to Law (this sign).
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for Moshoeshoe's praises; Sepedi for Tiragalong-style village talk; Afrikaans for Volkskongres papers; Portuguese and Cantonese for community archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** envy, witchcraft accusations against those who rise; tenderpreneur talk naming living people; racial framing of who "deserves" to rise.
- **Held for review:** commercial-rise and schooling-ambition sources tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left off the ambition sheet.
