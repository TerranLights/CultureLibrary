## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Basotho and amaXhosa men of the Free State goldfields:** check how those who won blasting certificates after job reservation ended describe earning them.
- **Coloured workers of the Western Cape, in their own self-designation:** check how people describe being chosen or passed over, first under the labor preference policy and then under employment equity.
- **Teachers in Limpopo and Mpumalanga schools:** check how teachers say a post is won, and what colleagues assume about those who got one.
- **First-generation NSFAS students against graduates from families with old Fort Hare ties:** check which reason each gives for being admitted: marks, money, family or chance.
- **Township rugby clubs of Kwazakhele and Zwide, Gqeberha:** check how players and coaches explain who reaches provincial and national teams.
- **Afrikaans-speaking civil servants who took packages in the 1990s and those who replaced them:** check what each says made a good official, and whether the handover was earned.
- **Batswana graduates of rural North West schools:** check how bursary panels and families weigh marks, need and kin ties in choosing whom to support.
- **Isicathamiya choirs of KwaZulu-Natal and the hostels:** check how all-night competitions are judged and what a win earns a choir.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on redress, appointments and education; mixed on everyday ideas of deserving; thin in print for Xitsonga and Tshivenda. Employment equity and admissions rules as written go to Libra; this sheet keeps to whether reward is felt to follow worth.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for praises and isicathamiya judging; Afrikaans for ARCA party papers and civil-service memoirs; Sesotho for mine testimony; Setswana for bursary talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race-based selection is one of the sharpest public quarrels; named post-selling cases may be unproven; "quota" taunts wound players named in them.
- **Held for review:** admissions, hiring and business-success debates bearing on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not used for merit.
