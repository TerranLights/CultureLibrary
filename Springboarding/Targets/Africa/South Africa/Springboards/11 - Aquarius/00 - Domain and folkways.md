## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **KwaZulu-Natal homesteads under izinduna and Durban's hostel residents:** check how a newcomer is received into a group in each, and who vouches for him.
- **North West villages with a kgotla and the Rustenburg platinum settlements:** check who calls the people together in each and how a pitso is announced.
- **≠Khomani in the Kalahari and !Xun and Khwe at Platfontein, Northern Cape:** check how each land-claim or resettled community names and bounds itself.
- **Coloured townships of the Cape Flats and Namaqualand mission towns (Leliefontein, Steinkopf):** check how church, sports club and troupe divide the week's gatherings.
- **Free State and Northern Cape platteland towns:** check the kerkbasaar, the agricultural show and the rugby club as a town's gatherings, and who attends.
- **VhaVenda, Vatsonga and Bapedi in Limpopo:** check which gathering (the khoro, the ZCC congregation, the funeral) draws the widest circle.
- **Jewish South Africans of Sea Point and Johannesburg, Chinese South Africans of Cyrildene, Madeiran Portuguese of the East Rand:** check which hall, feast or club day draws each together across a year, as a calendar of gathering.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on township ethnography and stokvels; mixed on rural associations; thin on Khoe and San ideas of community in their own words. The nation's post-war lands are still to be settled (the tracker records no change), so the Aquarius sheets are written for the nine present provinces.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for izibongo and clan names in greetings; |xam with Bleek's English glosses; Afrikaans for FAK songbooks and bazaar notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the census's group names carry apartheid classification and some are refused by those named; initiation schools keep their teaching closed; "the community" as a racial label is itself contested.
- **Held for review:** association and gathering sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside from this domain sheet by the standing rule.
