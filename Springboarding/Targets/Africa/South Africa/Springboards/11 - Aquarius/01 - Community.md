*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xolobeni coastal villages and Mthatha town, Eastern Cape:** check how "the community" is defined when a mining license is being decided, and who is left out of it.
- **Batswana wards in North West villages and Mahikeng's suburbs:** check whether ward membership still decides whose funerals and weddings a household attends.
- **≠Khomani San at Andriesvale and the restored farms:** check how the claimant community settled who counted as a member, and how that changed.
- **Genadendal, Mamre and Elim, Western Cape:** check how church membership and the right to live in the town overlap in a mission community.
- **Former Sophiatown and District Six residents and their children:** check how a demolished place is kept alive through reunions, museums and land-restitution claims.
- **Malamulele (Vatsonga) and Vuwani (VhaVenda), Limpopo:** check how each town's municipal-boundary protests of 2014-2016 framed "our community".
- **Free State dorpe and their townships:** check which bodies (church, farmers' union, ward committee, neighborhood watch) cross the old town-and-location line, if any.
- **amaNdebele villages of Mpumalanga and siSwati-speaking areas near Nkomazi:** check how chiefly wards and village committees share the work of a community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the Gauteng surveys and the removal memoirs give plenty; rural wards are patchier; Nama and San accounts of who belongs to a settlement are scarce outside claim files.
- **Languages needed:** Setswana for kgotla records; Afrikaans and Kaaps for mission-town and District Six sources; isiXhosa for Mpondo meetings; Xitsonga and Tshivenda for the Limpopo protest coverage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** neighborhood "justice" by crowds is a painful topic; removals are recent trauma for living people; claimant-community membership is contested and litigated.
- **Held for review:** neighborhood and burial-society studies about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, left out of this community sheet under the standing rule.
