*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm dwellers and landowning families in the Free State and North West:** check how each side narrates the sharing of land, harvest and say over generations.
- **Rural women under amakhosi in KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape:** check how requests for land allocation in a woman's own name are made and answered.
- **Brown and white Afrikaans-speaking congregations in Namakwa, Northern Cape:** check how the separated church history is told on each side of a reunited or still-divided parish.
- **Live-in domestic workers in Cape Town's southern suburbs and their employers:** check whether each side calls the worker "one of the family" or staff, and on what grounds.
- **New Black middle-class earners in Midrand and Soweto's Diepkloof:** check how "Black tax" is discussed as fairness or burden in their own talk.
- **Chinese South Africans of two generations:** check how the apartheid-era "honorary" treatment and later empowerment rulings are spoken of by older and younger members.
- **Khoe and San revival groups:** check how they argue for standing equal to the recognized kingships of Bantu-speaking peoples.
- **VhaVenda and Bapedi families near Mapungubwe:** check how the hill's ranked layout is presented to visitors and to descendants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** heavy on income and wealth statistics; uneven on everyday manners of rank; scant on Khoe herders' own sharing customs outside colonial records.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans and Kaaps for the church split and address forms; isiZulu and isiXhosa for land requests before chiefs; VOC Dutch for the early Cape herders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial classification, hair and skin tests are deep wounds and are described, never used as categories; empowerment and employment-equity statutes belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** redress, classification and mobility studies on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, withheld from this equality sheet by the standing rule.
