*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kliptown and wider Soweto:** check how clauses of the Freedom Charter appear in local murals, posters and street talk now.
- **Orania residents and Afrikaners in Pretoria who reject the town:** check how each states the ideal of self-rule and what it is said to cost.
- **amaNazaretha at Ebuhleni and Ekuphakameni:** check how members describe the holy village as a pattern for living.
- **Qolorha-by-Sea villagers:** check whether descent from believers or unbelievers of 1856-1857 is still named in disputes.
- **Young doctors after a community-service year in the Northern Cape or Eastern Cape:** check how their account of rural service changes in the years after.
- **The struggle generation and the "Born Frees":** check how each words the 1994 ideals and whether they were kept.
- **Khoe and San descendants:** check whether the |xam motto is felt as honor, as token, or both, and by whom.
- **Sesotho and Setswana speakers in Thaba Nchu and Mahikeng:** check what a phrase like molao-motheo is used to name in church and civic talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** ample on founding documents and struggle-era ideals; mixed on lived utopian settlements; thin on idealism among Vatsonga, VhaVenda and amaNdebele in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for the Nazaretha hymnal; Afrikaans for Schoeman and Orania's press; isiXhosa for Qolorha memory; |xam only through Bleek and Lloyd's records, with a linguist.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Orania and the volkstaat idea are politically charged; the 1856-1857 famine is a wound; Shembe church succession is in dispute; the Rivonia trial's legal side belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** writings on ideals and visions of a better order from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside from this idealism sheet under the standing rule.
