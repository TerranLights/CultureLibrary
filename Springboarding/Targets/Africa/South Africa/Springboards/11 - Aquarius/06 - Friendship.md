# South Africa: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern Cape men in Langa and Nyanga hostels:** check whether close friends come from the home village or from work, and what each kind is expected to give.
- **Former conscripts in the Free State and Pretoria:** check how border-war comrades keep up contact and what their reunions are for.
- **Black women professionals in Johannesburg's suburbs:** check how friend circles, stokvels and congregations divide the work of support.
- **Teenagers in Soweto and Mitchells Plain:** check where friendships start (school, street, church, team) and how they are tested.
- **VhaVenda and Vatsonga learners in Thohoyandou and Giyani schools:** check whether close friendships cross the language line, and when.
- **Gay and lesbian South Africans in townships and suburbs:** check how friends stand in at funerals, weddings and illness.
- **Batswana and Basotho in Thaba Nchu:** check whether age-mates from initiation or schoolmates become the closer friends.
- **Jewish, Portuguese and Chinese South African teenagers:** check whether camps, community schools and clubs yield friendships that stay inside the community or cross its line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** generous in fiction and memoir; middling in surveys of contact across groups; slender on friendship terms and customs among Nama, Griqua and San.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans for grensliteratuur; Kaaps for Cape Flats film and speech; isiXhosa for hostel testimony; Sepedi, Xitsonga and Tshivenda to sort friend words from initiation and age-set words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation-mate bonds may be closed; same-sex friendship and sexuality are easily conflated and must be kept apart; conscription memories are divided along political lines.
- **Held for review:** friendship and cross-group contact studies about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept off this friendship sheet by the standing rule.

1. Search a library catalog for *A Bed Called Home* and *Defiant Desire* and list chapters on friends.

5. Search news archives for "Mxit" with "friends" and "teenagers", 2006-2012.
