*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nieu-Bethesda and other Karoo villages:** check how neighbors spoke of Helen Martins in her life and how the village retells her now that she draws visitors.
- **Campuses on the 1989 Voëlvry route (Stellenbosch, Potchefstroom, Bloemfontein):** check how students and university authorities each answered the tour.
- **Izikhothane crews in Soweto and Katlehong:** check how participants, parents and pastors each describe the practice.
- **Cape Flats rappers in Mitchells Plain and Elsies River:** check how schools, churches and Afrikaans literary circles answered Kaaps rap over time.
- **Rastafari communities in Philippi and the Eastern Cape:** check how they deal with neighbors, schools and employers over dreadlocks and dress.
- **Queer and gender-nonconforming people in KwaZulu-Natal and Gauteng townships:** check where acceptance and attack differ by place, using the survey and GALA testimony together.
- **Xitsonga-speaking villages around Mbhokota, Limpopo:** check how Hlungwani's work is spoken of at home and in city galleries.
- **Jewish South Africans in Johannesburg and Cape Town:** check how Orthodox, Reform and secular households each treat a child who leaves observance, as a test of tolerated difference.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** full for Afrikaans dissent, queer archives and youth culture; patchy for rural eccentrics; little on how Khoe, Nama and San communities treat members who break custom.
- **Languages needed:** Afrikaans for Voëlvry and Breytenbach; Kaaps for hip-hop lyrics; isiZulu for trickster tales and township coverage; Xitsonga for Hlungwani's own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against queer people, especially lesbians in townships, is ongoing; slurs are described, not repeated; mental-illness records name real people; objectors' trials belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** dissent and subculture sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, parked outside this nonconformity sheet under the standing rule.
