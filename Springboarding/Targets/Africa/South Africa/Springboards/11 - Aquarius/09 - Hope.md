# South Africa: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, isiNdebele, South African English and Kaaps; N|uu and Khoekhoegowab for language-survival hopes. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fahfee players and runners in Johannesburg and Durban townships:** check how a dream becomes a number, and who keeps and teaches the dream books.
- **Members of new prophetic churches in Limpopo (Mookgophong, Polokwane):** check how believers speak of promises of healing and riches when the promised good does not arrive.
- **Families of the disappeared in the Eastern Cape and KwaZulu-Natal:** check how they describe waiting before and after an exhumation.
- **N|uu learners in Upington and ≠Khomani households:** check what they hope the language will become for their grandchildren.
- **Eastern Cape youth moving to Gauteng and the Western Cape:** check how plans told before leaving compare with the same plans told a few years on.
- **Afrikaans farmers in drought-hit Namakwa and the Karoo:** check how prayer days for rain and "alles sal regkom" are used, and by whom.
- **Cape Muslim households in Bo-Kaap and Athlone:** check how "inshallah" frames plans for children's schooling and work.
- **Basotho, Batswana and Bapedi parents naming a child Tshepo or Kholofelo:** check what parents say the name is meant to carry.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** wide for survey optimism, labor statistics and struggle song; spotty for informal gambling; narrow for San hopes outside the revival and rock-art literature.
- **Languages needed:** isiXhosa for Sontonga's hymn and freedom songs; Afrikaans for Langenhoven and farm-prayer days; Sepedi and Xitsonga for Limpopo church coverage; N|uu, with the community's consent, for revival work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the disappeared and of AIDS deaths are grieving; some rock-art sites are on private or community land with access limits; diviners' practice is guarded and must be described as practitioners allow; sellers of false hope may be in court (Libra).
- **Held for review:** prayer, pilgrimage and migrant-hope sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set apart from this hope sheet by the standing rule.
