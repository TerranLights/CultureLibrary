*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mpondo and Bomvana homesteads of the former Transkei and Gauteng township households:** check who is fed at the door, who is sent on to the clinic or SASSA, and who decides.
- **ACVV branches in Western Cape wine towns and in the Free State:** check whether the poor they aided were sorted by church membership, by color or by need, and how the test changed after 1994.
- **Batswana in North West and Bapedi in Limpopo:** check how proverbs and funeral custom divide the pitiable stranger from the kinsman with a claim.
- **Cape Muslim neighborhoods of Bo-Kaap and Athlone:** check the round of boeka food and Labarang visits, and whether giving crosses into non-Muslim streets.
- **Jewish South Africans and Chinese South Africans in Johannesburg:** check how each community's societies decide who receives help, and how they speak of it in their own records.
- **Home-based HIV carers in KwaZulu-Natal and the Eastern Cape:** check how carers describe their work in isiZulu and isiXhosa, and where mercy shades into exhaustion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on welfare history, HIV care and the TRC; mixed on giving and volunteering surveys; thin on Khoe, San, Venda and Tsonga words for mercy outside Bible translations.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Nyembezi and carers' talk; Setswana for Plaatje's proverbs; Afrikaans for ACVV minutes and Carnegie volumes; isiXhosa for Lusikisiki interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** HIV status and Life Esidimeni families' grief need care; charity images of suffering children are contested as exploitation.
- **Held for review:** compassion here leaves out the charities and almsgiving of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, per the standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the index of Nyembezi's *Zulu Proverbs* for sayings on the poor, the orphan and the stranger.
2. Search Wits Historical Papers for the Black Sash advice-office files and note the case categories.
3. Search Stats SA for the 2010 volunteer module and tabulate helping by province.
4. Open the Carnegie Commission volumes and list how each defines who deserves aid.
5. Search the SASSA site for annual reports and the SRD grant's eligibility rules over time.
