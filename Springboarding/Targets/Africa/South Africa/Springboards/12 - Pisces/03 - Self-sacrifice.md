# South Africa: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gcaleka and Ngqika descendants and amaMfengu families of the Eastern Cape:** check how each line's oral history names the cattle-killing, as offering, folly or betrayal.
- **Free State Afrikaner families of the camps and Black families of the same districts:** check whose dead each commemoration lists, and in which language the grief is spoken.
- **Mpondo and Basotho mineworkers' households in the Eastern Cape and the Free State goldfields:** check whether money sent home is called sacrifice, duty or simply work.
- **Shembe Nazarites at eBuhleni and ZCC members at Moria, Limpopo:** check the fasts, vows and gifts each church asks of members, as each church describes them.
- **Descendants of Chinese mine laborers on the Rand (1904-1910) and Madeiran Portuguese shopkeeping families:** check family letters and memoirs for what parents gave up and what was owed back.
- **Domestic workers from Limpopo and the Eastern Cape in Gauteng suburbs:** check how the grandmothers who raised their children describe that arrangement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war memory, struggle memorials and migrant labor; mixed on church fasting; thin on Khoe and San ideas of giving one's life.
- **Languages needed:** Sesotho for the lithoko; isiXhosa for cattle-killing testimonies in Peires's sources; Afrikaans for Gelofte sermons; isiZulu for Nazarite hymns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr lists are politically contested; Nazarite rites keep parts closed to outsiders; the Mendi and the camps carry descendants' claims.
- **Held for review:** vows, fasts and family sacrifice among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside this sheet for now.
