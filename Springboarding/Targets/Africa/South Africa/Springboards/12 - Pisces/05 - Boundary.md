## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kgaga and other Bapedi villages in Limpopo:** check whether Hammond-Tooke's lines between homestead, fields and bush still order where people may go at night or in mourning.
- **Married amaXhosa women in the former Transkei and in Johannesburg:** check which hlonipha words are kept, dropped or joked about after the move to the city.
- **Sandton security estates and Alexandra, a road apart:** check how each side describes the wall, the boom and the guard, and who may pass.
- **Basotho of the eastern Free State and daily crossers at Maseru Bridge:** check how families split by the border speak of it.
- **≠Khomani in the Kgalagadi park area and Nama in the Richtersveld:** check how each community describes its land's edges against park and mining lines.
- **Chinese South Africans and Cape Malay families under the old race categories:** check how families recount being placed on one side or the other of an official line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on frontiers, removals and the race lines of apartheid (their legal side belongs to Libra and Capricorn); mixed on household and bodily boundaries; thin on Tsonga and Venda boundary terms.
- **Languages needed:** archival Dutch for the *Daghregister*; Sepedi for Kgaga sources; isiXhosa for hlonipha recordings; Afrikaans for grensliteratuur.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** initiation lodges are closed to outsiders and deaths there are a live grief; avoidance speech is personal family practice; old race categories hurt to name.
- **Held for review:** residence, purity and border-crossing sources on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, excluded from the boundary sheet.
