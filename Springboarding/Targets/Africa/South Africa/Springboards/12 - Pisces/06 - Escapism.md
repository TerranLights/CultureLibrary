# South Africa: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** South Africa, region Africa. **Language(s) for searching:** isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho, Setswana, Sepedi, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati, isiNdebele, South African English and Kaaps; Khoekhoegowab and archival Dutch for early plant records. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Cape farm-worker families and Cape Flats households:** check whether drinking is described as wage, refuge or illness in each, and who says so.
- **Pondoland cannabis growers and nyaope users in Tshwane:** check whether the plant and the street mix are spoken of in the same words, and by whom.
- **Nama of Namaqualand and the commercial kanna trade:** check how the plant's use is named by its old users and by its new sellers.
- **Afrikaans fotoverhale readers in the platteland and Tshivenda *Muvhango* viewers in Limpopo:** check what each audience says brings it back week after week.
- **Chinese South African fahfee runners and their players in Soweto and KwaMashu:** check how a dream becomes a number in each telling.
- **North West villagers near Sun City and Gauteng visitors:** check how locals and visitors each describe what the resort was for.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on liquor history and drug-treatment numbers; mixed on gambling and leisure; thin on Khoe and San plant use in their own words. Diaspora sources (South Africans in Perth, London, Auckland) are for comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** archival Dutch for van der Stel; Kaaps for Cape Flats drug slang; Tshivenda for soap audiences; Afrikaans for fotoverhale and Rousseau.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction histories and treatment records are private; the dop system and tik are tied to stigma about Coloured communities, so name variation, never a type; cannabis law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** liquor, leisure and gambling studies on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the escapism sheet.
