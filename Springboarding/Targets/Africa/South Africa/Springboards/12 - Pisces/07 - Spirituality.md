*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nazarites climbing Nhlangakazi and ZCC members at Moria:** check how each church's members describe what the pilgrimage does to a person, and who is allowed which part of it.
- **Basotho of the eastern Free State at Modderpoort:** check where prayer at caves sits beside Catholic, Anglican and AIC membership in the same families.
- **VhaVenda custodians of zwifho near Fundudzi:** check what they choose to share about access, and who is entitled to speak.
- **Cape Muslim kramat visitors and Afrikaans households keeping huisgodsdiens:** check how private devotion is kept at home in each, and by whom.
- **Chinese South African Buddhists at Nan Hua and converts at Ixopo:** check how each describes what meditation is for.
- **Griqua congregations at Kranshoek and other Khoe revival groups:** check how they join Christian worship with reclaimed Khoe identity in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on AICs, Pentecostals and the Dutch Reformed devotional world; mixed on sacred natural sites; thin on San spirituality outside the archive and rock art.
- **Languages needed:** isiZulu for Shembe hymns; Sesotho for Mofolo and Modderpoort; Tshivenda for zwifho custodians; Afrikaans and Dutch for Murray's circle; Kaaps for kramat visitors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred sites in Venda and Khoe and San ceremonies are guarded; church scandals involve living people; belief in ancestors is not to be scored against Christianity.
- **Held for review:** temples, devotions and festivals of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not covered in the spirituality sheet.
