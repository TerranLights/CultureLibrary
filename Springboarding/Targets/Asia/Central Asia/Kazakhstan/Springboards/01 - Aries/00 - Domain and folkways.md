# Kazakhstan: The domain, self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic, plus the Latin alphabet now being phased in), Russian; for minorities Uzbek, Uyghur, Koryo-mar Korean, German, Tatar, Ukrainian, Dungan. **Concept in one line:** what a self is in Kazakhstan's lands, and how it shows and asserts itself: personhood, the body, appearance, naming, and sport as a statement of the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Kazakh-speaking regions (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda)** against **the Russian-speaking north (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar)**: the language a self is lived in may change which categories come first.
- **The three zhuz (Ūly, Orta, Kişi)** and the non-zhuz lines (töre, qoja): lineage as a frame for the self.
- **Almaty and Astana** against **aul and small-town life**: the urban self versus the self placed by kin.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans, Russians, Ukrainians, Tatars, Uzbeks, Uyghurs, Dungan**: each minority's own naming and self-description, set apart from the Kazakh frame.
- **Qandas (formerly oralman) returnees** from Mongolia, China, Uzbekistan: a self formed abroad, now placed inside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ethnography, naming and literature; thinner for psychology of self-construal, where regional studies need searching.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh to reach the oral and ethnographic layers, Russian for Soviet scholarship and most statistics, minority languages for each community's own press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the ethnicity line on documents; clan talk in politics; slurs used between urban Russian-speaking and rural Kazakh-speaking Kazakhs.
- **Scope:** Kazakhstan's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign is written for the core lands of the pre-war republic.
- **Held for review:** none; no naming or self-description pointer had to be set aside.
