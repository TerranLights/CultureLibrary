# Kazakhstan: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh and Russian first; Uzbek, Uyghur, Koryo-mar and standard Korean, German for the minority voices. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders, sons, daughters and the *kelın*:** speaking rights shift with age and marriage, so the household is a unit of comparison in itself.
- **Kazakh-speaking south and west (Shymkent, Kyzylorda, Mangystau, Atyrau)** against **Russian-speaking northern and eastern cities (Kostanay, Petropavl, Oskemen)**: who sets the norms for speaking up in mixed workplaces.
- **Russians and Ukrainians** in the north, and their responses to language-rights claims.
- **Uzbeks of Turkistan region (Sairam, Zhetysai)** and **Uyghurs of Zhetysu**: trade and community leaders as speakers for their groups.
- **Koryo-saram** in Almaty and Ushtobe: how a deported people's children learned when to press a claim and when to stay quiet.
- **Qandas returnees** from Mongolia and China: whether their ways of asking and refusing, learned abroad, are read as polite or pushy by local Kazakhs.
- **Women activists in Almaty** against **rural women**: whether public speaking up looks the same.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for oratory and aitys, thin for measured assertiveness inside the country.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for oratory and aitys texts; Russian for psychology, press and most online debate; German and Korean for those minorities' own archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language-rights confrontations; complaints against officials; abuse testimony on NeMolchi.
