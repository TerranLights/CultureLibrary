## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-speaking and Russian-speaking Kazakhs:** the same ethnic label with different first answers to "who are you?"
- **Ūly, Orta and Kişi zhuz, with the töre and qoja:** whether clan still comes first in introductions, and where.
- **Ural Cossack descendants in Oral and the Russian north:** a settler identity with its own long history on the Ural and Irtysh lines.
- **Uyghurs of Zhetysu:** their identity inside Kazakhstan, kept separate from East Turkestan, which appears only as comparison.
- **Dungan, Koryo-saram, Germans, Chechens, Meskhetian Turks, Poles:** deported or migrant peoples whose identity was made in exile on these lands.
- **Qandas returnees** from Mongolia, China and Karakalpakstan, settled in Mangystau, Turkistan and around Almaty.
- **Astana's newcomers:** a capital filled since 1997 by migrants from every region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; identity is a central theme of post-1991 scholarship and state policy.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh and Russian throughout; German, Korean and Dungan for the community archives; Uyghur for its press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnicity and language in politics; clan identity in appointments; insults between language groups; Uyghur topics touch relations with China.
- **Held for review:** none; no identity source was set aside under the standing rules.
