## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty traders and Astana startup founders** against **aul households in Zhambyl and Kyzylorda**: initiative for markets against initiative for the household and neighbors.
- **Oil towns of Atyrau and Mangystau**: big employers next to small self-started trade.
- **Koryo-saram, Dungan and Uyghur farmers and traders** around Almaty and in Zhambyl region: each with its own history of seasonal farming and market trade.
- **Tatar merchant families** of Semey and Petropavl: a 19th-century trading line.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Zhetysai**: crafts, bazaar trade, and the southern market economy.
- **Qandas returnees**: starting over in a new place, with or without state support.
- **Women founders**, rural and urban, in state microcredit programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and state programs; folklore and history good; data on unasked help at home thin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for business and statistics; Kazakh for folklore, *asar* and rural life; Korean and Dungan for those communities' farming histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civic movements that met state pressure; informal trade sits near tax and customs rules (law to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none; the enterprise and volunteering pointers needed no hold.
