## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households in Mangystau, the Altai and Zhetysu summer pastures** against **wage earners in Astana and Almaty**: different sources of a living to stand on.
- **Northern Russians, Ukrainians and Germans** with their gardens, against **large families in Turkistan and Shymkent**, to test how the benefit debates map onto region rather than assume it.
- **Aral Sea towns of Kyzylorda region**: livelihood lost and regained.
- **Uyghur and Dungan market-garden villages** around Almaty and in Korday district: family enterprise as self-sufficiency.
- **Koryo-saram rice and vegetable farmers** of Kyzylorda and Almaty regions.
- **Qandas returnees** reliant on resettlement support in their first years.
- **Elders and disabled people**: independence as being able to manage, not standing alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state statistics and national-independence history; mixed for household ethnography; thin for emotional independence.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for customs and proverbs; Russian for statistics, benefits and northern life; the Turkic of Shokai's journal (*check* its language and script).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** benefit debates touch ethnicity and region; separatism talk is policed; the 2019 fire and protests. Rules on benefits and statehood go to Libra and Capricorn.
