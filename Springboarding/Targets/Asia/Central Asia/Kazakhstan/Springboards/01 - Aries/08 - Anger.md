# Kazakhstan: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh, Russian; Uzbek, Tatar, Uyghur and Dungan for the communities touched by local clashes; Chechen and Kurdish only through Russian-language sources unless a speaker joins. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oil towns of Mangystau and Atyrau** against **the industrial north (Pavlodar, Ekibastuz, Temirtau)**: two working-class settings with their own grievances and ways of showing them.
- **Men and women**: whether the same outburst is judged differently by gender, and whether the 2024 trial shifted that.
- **Dungan, Chechen, Kurdish and Kazakh villagers** in mixed districts: where neighbor quarrels turned into group conflict.
- **Uyghurs and qandas from Xinjiang**: anger tied to events across the border; East Turkestan and China only as comparison.
- **Russians in the north and east**: anger over language and status since 1991, as they describe it.
- **Children and teenagers**: how tantrums and school fights are handled in Kazakh-, Russian- and Uzbek-language homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for protest and press, thin for everyday display rules and measured emotion.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for curses, emotion words and sermons; Russian for most press, rap and online video; Uzbek and Uyghur for southern communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in 2011 and 2022; domestic-violence cases with named victims; interethnic clashes; Xinjiang topics. Revenge belongs to Scorpio; legal settlement to Libra and Capricorn.
