## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in Almaty and Shymkent** (street racing) against **young horsemen in Turkistan and Zhambyl** (bäige and kökpar): two kinds of daring with different audiences.
- **The mountain south-east** (Ile-Alatau climbers and freeriders) against **the reservoir east** (ice fishermen of Bukhtarma).
- **Pavlodar and Ekibastuz**: industrial work, crypto booms and their risks.
- **Kyzylorda and Karaganda villages** near rocket fall zones.
- **Muslim-heritage groups (Kazakhs, Uzbeks, Uyghurs, Dungan, Tatars)** and **Orthodox, Lutheran and Catholic heritage groups (Russians, Ukrainians, Germans, Poles)**: test drinking and gambling norms by group, practice and region rather than assume them from faith labels.
- **Teenagers in Kazakh-language and Russian-language schools.**
