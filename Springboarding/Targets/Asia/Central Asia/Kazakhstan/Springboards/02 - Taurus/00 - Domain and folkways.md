## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The three zhuz:** Kishi zhuz in the west (oil towns, camel milk, sea and river fishing), Orta zhuz across the center, north and east (mining towns, grain lands), Uly zhuz in the south and southeast (irrigated oases, bazaars); each regional economy may shape what a household keeps.
- **Almaty against Astana:** an older trading city against a planned capital with newer money on show.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north and east:** dacha gardens and home preserving as household provisioning.
- **Uzbeks of Turkistan region and Shymkent:** bazaar trade and the *plov* feast as a spending occasion.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region (Chilik, Uighur district):** market gardening and food trades in the city.
- **Koryo-saram, Dungan, Germans and Tatars:** farming and merchant pasts with other measures of a well-kept house.
- **Qandastar (returnees) from Mongolia and China:** herding households that carried their own goods and habits back.
- Diaspora comparison only: Kazakhs of Bayan-Ölgii (Mongolia) and Ili (Xinjiang).
