# Kazakhstan: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin), Russian; Uzbek, Uyghur, Korean, German, Ukrainian for community material. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding households of the west and center:** movable goods sized for the yurt and the cart, and marks on animals.
- **Settled Uzbek and Uyghur households of the south and Almaty region:** courtyard houses, wall niches and the bride's *сеп*, a different way of storing and showing goods.
- **Deported peoples (Germans, Koryo-saram, Chechens, Poles, Meskhetian Turks):** possession rebuilt from what could be carried.
- **Russians and Ukrainians in northern towns:** Soviet apartments, cellars, sheds and garages as storage.
- **Old Believers of the East:** objects held as sacred, not only as property.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia and China:** what families bring back, and what they leave.
- **Almaty and Astana apartment dwellers against villages.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in ethnography and museum holdings, thin on present-day attachment to everyday objects (no known survey of keepsakes).
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for customs and grammar; Russian for statistics, memoirs and Soviet ethnography; German and Korean for deportee families' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** repression and deportation records; mourning customs differ by family and lineage, ask before describing; the legal side of property belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on herds, dowry or household goods needed holding.
