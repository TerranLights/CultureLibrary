# Kazakhstan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin), Russian; Uzbek, Uyghur, Korean, German and Ukrainian for farming and housing communities. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western oil and gas lands (Atyrau, Mangystau, West Kazakhstan):** state and foreign ownership of the subsoil beside local herders' claims.
- **Northern grain belt (Kostanay, North Kazakhstan, Akmola):** large agricultural firms against family farmers, with Russian, Ukrainian and German farming histories.
- **Southern irrigated oases (Turkistan, Zhambyl, Almaty regions):** water and plot holding among Kazakh, Uzbek, Uyghur and Dungan farmers.
- **Koryo-saram:** leased and kolkhoz land rather than owned land.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers seeking land and housing.
- **Apartment owners against renters in Almaty, Astana and Shymkent.**
- Comparison only: Kazakh herders of Bayan-Ölgii under Mongolian tenure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tenure history and privatization; mixed for present feelings about owning.
- **Languages needed:** Russian dominates archives and Soviet scholarship; Kazakh for custom terms and the land debate; Korean and German for community farming histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1928 confiscations and the famine that followed; the 2016 protests and arrests; disputed elite assets. Property law, customary codes and the Land Code go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; no land, pasture or privatization pointer was set aside.
