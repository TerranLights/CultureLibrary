## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-speaking villages against Russian-speaking cities:** each side may hold a different measure of a person's worth, and labels pass between them.
- **Descendants of *төре* and *қожа* against commoner lineages:** inherited rank in a society without formal estates.
- **Men and women:** *намыс* for men and the daughter-in-law's standing (*келін*) in her husband's family.
- **Russians and Ukrainians after 1991:** a change from majority to minority standing.
- **Deported peoples (Germans, Chechens, Ingush, Koryo-saram, Poles, Karachays):** dignity after collective punishment.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** community worth kept through theater, language and trade.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia, China, Uzbekistan:** standing on arrival.
- **Western, southern and northern regional pride**, voiced in *айтыс* and jokes.
- **Elders (*ақсақал*) against the young in towns.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in classical texts and moral philosophy, thinner in survey psychology.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for Abai, Shakarim and *айтыс*; Russian for psychology and press; German and Korean for deportee voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide; language labels that work as insults; lineage rank can wound; apology videos sometimes follow pressure from authorities or crowds.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on worth, standing or esteem was held back.
