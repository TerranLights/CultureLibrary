## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of Mangystau, Aktobe, Karaganda and Qandastar herding families:** exposure to *жұт* and pasture loss.
- **The Aral shore (Aralsk, Kazalinsk):** livelihood lost with the sea.
- **Semey and Abai region:** health security near the old test site.
- **Flood-hit northern and western towns:** whose losses were covered and whose were not.
- **Oil and mining workers (Atyrau, Zhanaozen, Karaganda, Rudny):** job security tied to large employers.
- **Pensioners in northern towns, many Russian and Ukrainian:** fixed incomes against prices.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** families who crossed in 1962 and later.
- **Chechens, Ingush, Koryo-saram, Germans:** security rebuilt after deportation.
- **Informal and seasonal workers in Almaty and Shymkent, including Uzbek-speaking traders.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, pensions and banking; mixed on how people feel about risk.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh and Russian; Uyghur and Chechen for oral histories; German and Korean for deportee memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine and repression; nuclear-test health effects; the January 2022 unrest that began with fuel prices (handle with care); military and state security are not this concept.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on savings, safety nets or winter stores was set aside.
