# Kazakhstan: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin), Russian; German, Uyghur, Uzbek, Tatar, Korean and Ukrainian for settled and resettled communities. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families who still move between pastures:** continuity as a cycle of movement.
- **Villages where one lineage has lived for generations against Virgin Lands towns built from nothing.**
- **The Ural Cossacks and Old Believers:** long-settled Russian communities.
- **Germans who stayed after the 1990s against relatives who emigrated.**
- **Uyghur and Dungan villages of Almaty and Zhambyl regions:** settled farming communities with long local roots.
- **Koryo-saram:** stability rebuilt twice, after 1937 and after 1991.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers to the *атамекен*.
- **Astana's new residents against old Almaty families.**
- **Oil towns of the west:** boom, strike and turnover.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration and settlement history; mixed on routine and lived continuity.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for zhyrau and *зар заман* verse; Russian for census and political speech; German and Uyghur for community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability" is a loaded political word; Zhanaozen and January 2022 are sensitive; forced settlement and famine.
- **Held for review:** none; no settling, wintering or continuity pointer needed a hold.
