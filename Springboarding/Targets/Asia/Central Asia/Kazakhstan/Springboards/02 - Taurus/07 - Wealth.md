## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The oil west (Atyrau, Aktau, Aksai):** high wages and corporate towns beside herding villages.
- **Almaty and Astana:** concentrated fortunes, finance and display.
- **The rural south (Turkistan, Zhambyl, Kyzylorda):** household size, income gaps with the cities and support sent by city kin, all to measure.
- **Mining towns of the center and east (Karaganda, Zhezkazgan, Oskemen, Ridder):** wealth from one employer.
- **Tatars:** merchant pasts in northern trade towns.
- **Uzbeks of the south:** bazaar trade and feasts.
- **Uyghurs:** restaurant, farming and trade networks in Almaty region.
- **Koryo-saram:** farm earnings turned into urban careers.
- **Germans and Ukrainians of the north:** farm wealth and the effects of emigration.
- **Qandastar:** herd-based wealth carried in and converted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inequality and rankings; mixed on what people count as wealth beyond money.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for epic, folk belief and novels; Russian for business press and statistics; Tatar for merchant history.
