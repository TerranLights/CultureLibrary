## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern villages (Turkistan, Zhambyl):** where abduction and in-law claims are most discussed, to test against data.
- **Northern and city families:** Russian-speaking couples and newer talk of "control" and "abuse".
- **Western lineages:** levirate and kin claims as remembered and as still debated.
- **Uzbek and Uyghur families of the south and southeast:** in-law households and the bride's goods.
- **Dungan and Koryo-saram:** claims of a small community over its members' marriages.
- **Herders against farmers and newcomers:** pasture as turf.
- **Russians, Ukrainians and Germans:** jealousy in songs, film and the Russian-language press.
- **Generations:** grandparents' claims on grandchildren.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong on marriage custom and on partner violence, thin on everyday sharing and stinginess.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for epic, *айтыс* and custom; Russian for surveys and the press; Dungan and Korean for small-community marriage norms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abduction, coercion and partner violence; a high-profile murder trial; the 2020 Masanchi clash, where turf and ethnicity were tangled (handle with care, no blame by group); marriage law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no jealousy or holding-on pointer was set aside.
