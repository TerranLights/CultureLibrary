*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The west (Junior zhuz lands):** where the risings of Syrym and Isatai are local history; memory to compare with the south and north.
- **Kazakh-speaking activists against Russian-speaking city dwellers:** firmness about language from both sides.
- **Russia-German Baptists and Mennonites:** religious refusal under Soviet rule.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** customs kept through exile.
- **Dungan:** language held for generations.
- **Old Believers of the East:** rite kept across centuries.
- **Self-built suburbs of Almaty and Shymkent:** holdouts against demolition.
- **Elders and the young:** who is called *қыңыр* and who *өжет*.

## 4. Flags
