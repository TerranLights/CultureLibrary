- **Uyghurs:** music, dance and food gatherings.
- **Koryo-saram:** a cuisine of spice and fermentation.
- **Russians, Ukrainians and Germans of the north:** the steam bath, dacha gardens, berry and mushroom picking.
- **Tatars:** sweets and tea tables (*чак-чак*).
- **Dungan:** noodle and vegetable dishes of Zhambyl region.
- **Devout and secular households:** fasting and *ауызашар* against secular feasting.
- **Almaty's café scene against village feasts.**
