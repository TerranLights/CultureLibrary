# Kazakhstan: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic; Latin forms *check*) and Russian, with attention to talk that mixes them; Dungan, Uyghur, Koryo-mar and Kazakh Sign Language for the communities below. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what good communication is taken to be and to be for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-dominant and Russian-dominant Kazakhs:** the same family may hold both; a key split for any talk norm.
- **The south against the north:** Shymkent and Turkistan families against Kostanay or Petropavl ones, to see which language fills daily talk in each.
- **Mangystau and Atyrau (west):** regional speech and oratory traditions linked to the Adai and Kishi zhuz lineages.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** a community talking across Uyghur, Russian and Kazakh at once.
- **Dungan villages of Zhambyl region:** a small speech community with its own Cyrillic-written language.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north:** how those who speak only Russian deal with Kazakh-speaking officials and neighbors.
- **Koryo-saram:** how talk changed as Koryo-mar gave way to Russian.
- **Qandastar children:** may act as go-betweens for parents raised in Mongolian- or Chinese-language settings.
- **The Deaf community:** a language community of its own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for oral genres and language choice; thinner for everyday conversation (pauses, gesture), which few have recorded.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for oratory, aitys and speech etiquette; Russian for most sociolinguistics and archives; Dungan and Uyghur for the community studies; sign language for Deaf sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *шала қазақ* and *мәмбет* are insults; aitys that criticize officials touch politics; language status law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on speech, address or eloquence was set aside.
