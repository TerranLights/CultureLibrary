# Kazakhstan: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh and Russian, since state and independent outlets split across both; German, Uyghur and Kazakh in the Arabic-based script (for returnee sources) where noted. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-language audiences of the north and east:** test how far Russian channels (Pervyi Kanal Evraziya *check*) form a separate information space.
- **Kazakh-language audiences of the south and west:** Kazakh sites, Telegram and Instagram channels; compare sources and trust with the north.
- **Semey and the old test-site districts:** a region with its own history of withheld information.
- **Mangystau oil towns:** labor news spread through chats and word of mouth.
- **Uyghurs and qandastar from Xinjiang:** relatives across a closed border make news from there personal.
- **Germans and Koryo-saram:** community papers founded under Soviet rule, now partly online.
- **Dungan, Tatars, Chechens and Uzbeks:** whether community channels (mosque, cultural center, WhatsApp) replace a press.
- **Rural against city:** gaps in broadband and libraries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media and press freedom; thinner for rural word of mouth and folk practice.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh and Russian for media; German, Uyghur and Arabic-script Kazakh for community and returnee material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalists' safety; the 1986 Jeltoqsan, 2011 Zhanaozen and January 2022 events; Xinjiang testimony; laws on false information and media belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no news, press or data pointer was held back under the standing rules.
