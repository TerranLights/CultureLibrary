# Kazakhstan: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (both scripts) and Russian, the two main languages of instruction; Uzbek and Uyghur (school media for those communities), Korean and German (heritage-language classes), Tatar for the old madrasa teachers. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-medium and Russian-medium families:** different school languages, different homework helpers.
- **Herding families of the steppe:** children learning stockwork by doing, often in small mixed-age village schools.
- **The south (Turkistan, Shymkent):** Uzbek-medium schools beside Kazakh ones; master-pupil trades in bazaars.
- **The north and east:** Russian-medium schooling with Kazakh as a second language; Virgin Lands farm families.
- **Semey region:** Abai's home district, where his teaching is a local heritage.
- **Western oil towns:** new trade-training routes alongside older herding life.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** communities that learn an ancestral language as a second one.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** village and city schools teaching in or about their own languages.
- **Qandastar:** children and adults learning a new script and Russian at once.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schooling, policy and ethnopedagogy; thinner for learning by watching in herding and craft families.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for ethnopedagogy, rites and Abai; Russian for archives and assessment reports; Uzbek and Uyghur for school media; Korean and German for heritage classes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** script changes (Arabic, Latin, Cyrillic, Latin again) and the medium-of-instruction question are politically charged; school as institution belongs to Sagittarius (education).
- **Held for review:** none; no school, madrasa or textbook pointer needed holding.
