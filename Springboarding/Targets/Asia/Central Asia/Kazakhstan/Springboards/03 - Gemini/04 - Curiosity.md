## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kyzylorda and the Syr Darya:** Korkyt lore and Baikonur side by side, old and new wonder.
- **The north (Akmola, North Kazakhstan):** home of the Botai finds; curiosity about pre-Kazakh pasts.
- **East (Abai region, East Kazakhstan, the Altai):** kurgans, Shokan's and Abai's legacy, mining-town science.
- **Mangystau:** underground mosques and desert sites that draw explorers and pilgrims.
- **Germans and Chechens:** lineage research driven by deportation and emigration.
- **Koryo-saram:** often asked about their origins by others; the reverse side of curiosity.
- **Uyghurs and qandastar from Xinjiang:** asking about relatives across the border may itself be risky.
- **Urban against aul children:** access to museums, clubs and fast internet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for genealogy, archaeology and folklore, thin for everyday nosiness and children's questions.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for riddles, shezhire and lore; Russian for science institutions; German and Chechen for family lineage records; Uyghur for cross-border inquiry.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan questions touch tribalism debates; excavating graves; DNA testing and clan origin disputes; contact with Xinjiang relatives.
