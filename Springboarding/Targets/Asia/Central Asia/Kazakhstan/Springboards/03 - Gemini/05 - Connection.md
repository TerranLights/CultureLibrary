# Kazakhstan: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh and Russian (networks and favors are talked about in both, often with Russian slang); Uyghur for the *мәшрәп*; Korean and German for the associations of those peoples; Chechen and Mongolian-Kazakh usage where ties cross borders. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakhs of different zhuz and lineages:** whether lineage ties work as networks equally in the south, west and north.
- **Rural migrants in Almaty, Astana and Shymkent:** new city networks built on village and kin links.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north:** dacha, workplace and church ties; many relatives now in Russia.
- **Chechens and Ingush of Karaganda and Pavlodar:** clan (*тайп*) networks formed in exile.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** neighborhood and *мәшрәп* ties, and kin across a closed border.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** associations as networks across several countries.
- **Uzbeks of the south:** *mahalla* neighborhood bonds in Turkistan and Shymkent.
- **Qandastar:** networks in both the old and the new homeland.
- **Elderly people in emptied northern villages:** a case of thinning ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for clan and favor networks; mixed for loneliness and ritual kinship; thin for minority networks outside associations.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for ritual kin and in-law customs; Russian for favor networks and statistics; Uyghur, Korean, German and Chechen for community ties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan and tribalism talk is politically touchy; nepotism and corruption cases belong to Libra or Capricorn; Xinjiang family ties are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none; no networking or ties-between-people pointer was set aside.
