# Kazakhstan: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh in all three of its scripts (Cyrillic, the new Latin alphabet, and the Arabic-based script still used by Kazakhs in China, as comparison), Russian; Korean, German and Dungan for peoples who live between two homelands. **Concept in one line:** how two-ness is lived: two identities, languages, scripts, loyalties or views at once; the world divided into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-speaking and Kazakh-speaking Kazakhs:** the main internal pair; who switches, who is mocked, who chooses one.
- **Families of mixed marriage:** Kazakh-Russian, Kazakh-Tatar, Kazakh-Korean; children with two inheritances.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north and east:** belonging to the country and to a neighboring one at once.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** two homelands, one ancestral and one lived.
- **Dungan and Uyghurs:** faith and speech pulling in different directions; Uyghurs between Kazakhstan and East Turkestan (comparison).
- **Qandastar from China:** arriving with the Arabic-based script, meeting Cyrillic and Latin.
- **Turkistan and the south:** Islam and older practice side by side.
- **Herding families and city dwellers:** whether people with a foot in both the steppe and the flat speak of two selves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language and script; mixed for identity among minorities; thin for paired worldviews in daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh in Cyrillic and Latin, and Arabic-script Kazakh for comparison; Russian for most identity research; Korean, German and Dungan for those communities' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *шала қазақ* and *мәмбет* are insults; loyalty of Russians and other minorities is politically charged; citizenship rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on two-sidedness, two languages or two faces was held.
