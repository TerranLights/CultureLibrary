# Kazakhstan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh and Russian (the old go-between words are Kazakh, the professional ones Russian); Uzbek for migrant labor brokers, Dungan and Chechen for community peacemaking, Tatar for historic intermediaries. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural lineages of the south and west:** test whether elders and kin are the first call in a quarrel.
- **Cities (Almaty, Astana, Shymkent):** professional mediators beside kin and neighbors.
- **Karaganda and Temirtau:** industrial disputes between workers, foreign owners and the state.
- **Dungan villages of Kordai:** the 2020 events and the go-betweens trusted afterward.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** their own elder-led peacemaking in exile communities.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** neighborhood elders and the *мәшрәп* as places where quarrels are eased.
- **Uzbeks of the south and migrant workers:** brokers and *mahalla* elders.
- **Russians, Ukrainians and Germans of the north:** church, union or courts rather than elders, to be tested.
- **Tatars:** historic intermediaries, now a small community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for legend and state bodies; mixed for family and village go-betweens; thin for brokers.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for biy legends and family custom; Russian for professional mediation and press; Uzbek for labor brokers; Dungan and Chechen for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic clashes (Kordai 2020, Malovodnoye 2007); labor unrest; saint veneration disputed by some Muslim authorities; the mediation law, *Жеті жарғы* and biy courts belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no go-between, elder-arbiter or matchmaker pointer was held back.
