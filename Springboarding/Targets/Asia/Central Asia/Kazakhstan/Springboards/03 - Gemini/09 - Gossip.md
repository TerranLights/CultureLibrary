# Kazakhstan: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh and Russian, with the Russian slang of the courtyard and the office; Uzbek and Uyghur for the women's and men's *gap* gatherings of the south and southeast; Dungan for village reputation. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, and rumor, unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aul and small town against city:** how far one family's affairs travel, and how fast.
- **Almaty and Astana:** office gossip, showbiz talk and anonymous pages in two languages.
- **The south (Shymkent, Turkistan):** Uzbek *gap* gatherings beside Kazakh women's and men's circles.
- **Russian-speaking northern towns:** the courtyard bench and the workplace canteen.
- **Western oil towns:** rotation work and talk about who is away and what happens at home.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** neighborhood talk and the *мәшрәп*'s rules on speech.
- **Dungan villages:** reputation in a small community after the 2020 events.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans and Tatars:** dispersed communities whose talk runs through associations and messengers.
- **Young people online:** Instagram and Telegram gossip in place of the bench.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for online shaming, rumor cases and fact-checks; thin for ordinary face-to-face gossip, which few have studied.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for proverbs, verse and religious teaching; Russian for courtyard talk, press and police reports; Uzbek and Uyghur for *gap* gatherings; Dungan for village sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaming of women (*ұят* pages); arrests for "rumors"; defamation and false-information rules belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; no rumor or hearsay pointer needed a hold under the standing rules.
