# Kazakhstan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin), Russian; Uyghur and Uzbek for courtyard houses, German and Ukrainian for northern villages, Korean for Koryo-saram homes. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of Zhetysu and the central steppe:** still moving between seasonal quarters.
- **Soviet-block residents in Karaganda, Temirtau, Pavlodar:** home as an assigned flat, then a privatized one.
- **New-build buyers in Astana and Almaty:** mortgage-bound young families.
- **Southern self-builders (Shymkent, Turkistan):** houses added to over years.
- **Aral and Mangystau shore families:** homes shaped by water loss and oil.
- **Uyghur villages of the Ili valley; Uzbek *mahalla* of Sairam:** inward courtyard homes.
- **Koryo-saram, Volga Germans, Ukrainians, Chechens:** homes rebuilt after deportation or settlement.
- **Russians of Oskemen and Ridder:** long-settled mining-town homes in the east.
- **Qandastar:** returnees housed in new settlements (the homes left abroad are comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on dwellings, housing statistics and yurt layout; mixed on home as feeling; thin on hidden homelessness.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for yurt and threshold vocabulary and folk omens; Russian for housing policy, mortgages and Soviet-era stock; Uyghur and Uzbek for the courtyard house's rooms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced settlement and famine are painful history; evictions and housing protests can be politically touchy; property rules go to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** nothing on dwellings, housing loss or home tours had to be held.
