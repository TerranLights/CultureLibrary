## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty against Astana:** an older southern city against a capital of recent migrants.
- **Rural incomers in big cities:** labels and acceptance one or two generations on.
- **Kazakh-speaking south and west against Russian-speaking north and east:** whose belonging language marks.
- **Qandastar:** returnees from Mongolia (Bayan-Ölgii), China (Ili) and Karakalpakstan, each received differently.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** settler descendants weighing stay or leave.
- **Germans and Koryo-saram:** deported peoples who later built cultural centers.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** southeastern communities with their own schools, press and villages.
- **Meskhetian Turks, Kurds, Azerbaijanis:** smaller deported peoples of the south.
- **Tatars:** long-present urban Muslims, often counted half-inside Kazakh circles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; surveys and press on language and returnees are plentiful, belonging as felt experience is thin outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for insider labels, genealogies and returnee press; Russian for the minority, emigration and city-association material; Ukrainian, German and Korean for each community's own record.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *мамбет* and similar labels are slurs; language and ethnicity disputes are politically charged; citizenship rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on insiders and outsiders called for holding.
