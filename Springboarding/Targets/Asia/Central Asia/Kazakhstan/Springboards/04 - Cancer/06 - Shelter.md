# Kazakhstan: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian; Dungan and Uyghur for those who arrived as refugees in the 1880s, Chechen, Korean and German for the deported peoples who were taken in. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of Betpak-Dala and Mangystau:** hosting travelers far from towns.
- **Almaty and Astana:** most homeless services and most self-built settlements.
- **Western border towns (Oral, Atyrau):** first stop for the 2022 arrivals.
- **Turkistan region:** Arys evacuees; Uzbek neighbors' hosting customs.
- **Dungan and Uyghur villages:** descendants of refugees received in the 1880s.
- **Chechens, Ingush, Koryo-saram, Germans, Meskhetian Turks:** peoples sheltered on arrival in exile.
- **Russian and Ukrainian church communities of the north and east:** parish charity to the unhoused.
- **Qandastar:** returnees who wait for housing in temporary centers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hospitality customs and deportation memory; mixed for disasters; thin for homeless counts, which are sparse and contested.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for hosting customs and tales; Russian for city services, press and memoirs; Dungan, Uyghur, Chechen, Korean and German for the sheltered peoples' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** asylum claims by people fleeing Xinjiang (East Turkestan as comparison only) are politically delicate; homeless people's privacy; asylum law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing; every pointer on hosting travelers, refugees and the unhoused stands.
