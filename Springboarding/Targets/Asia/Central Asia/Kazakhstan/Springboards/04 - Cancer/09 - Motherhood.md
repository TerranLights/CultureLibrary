## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern regions (Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** high birth rates, shrine pilgrimages for children.
- **North and east (Pavlodar, Oskemen):** fewer children, more mothers in full-time work.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau):** large families in oil towns, mothers alone during shifts.
- **Almaty and Astana:** later motherhood, IVF and doulas.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Turkistan:** a mother's blessing and the mother-in-law's household.
- **Tatars:** the *абыстай* tradition of women teaching.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** Orthodox mothers and soldiers' mothers' groups.
- **Koryo-saram:** mothers in small, school-driven families.
- **Uyghur and Dungan villages:** mothers in extended households of the southeast.
- **Single mothers and mothers of many:** the two poles of public debate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on birth figures, payments and pilgrimage; mixed on mothers' own accounts; thin on regret, postpartum illness and infertility.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for songs, shrines and mother terms; Russian for health statistics, protests and forums; Tatar, Uzbek and Ukrainian for those mothers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** maternal deaths and infertility are private; the 2019 protests carry political risk; abortion goes to Aries and family law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on mothers, birth or mothers' movements was put aside.
