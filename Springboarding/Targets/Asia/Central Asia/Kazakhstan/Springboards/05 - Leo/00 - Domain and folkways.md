## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda, Zhambyl):** large *той* and horse-game circuits.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau, Oral):** Junior zhuz song lines and Mangystau's own feast days.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl, Kokshetau):** Russian, Ukrainian and German village leisure, hockey and dacha life.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey):** Abai's home region and Old Believer villages of the Altai foothills.
- **Almaty and Astana:** club, concert and café scenes against the steppe *ауыл*'s horse days.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Turkistan; Uyghurs of Chundzha and Zharkent:** their own wedding music and gatherings.
- **Dungan of Korday district; Koryo-saram of Ushtobe; Tatars in the cities:** small calendars of play kept apart.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia:** eagle-hunting display brought back from Bayan-Ölgii.
