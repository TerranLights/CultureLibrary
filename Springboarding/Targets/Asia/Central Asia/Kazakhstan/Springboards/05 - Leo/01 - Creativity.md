## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West against east-center:** *төкпе* players of Atyrau and Mangystau against *шертпе* of Karaganda, Ulytau and Semey.
- **Kyzylorda and the Syr Darya:** *жырау* schools and felt making along the river.
- **Shymkent and Turkistan:** Uzbek-Kazakh craft bazaars, contemporary art from the south.
- **Almaty and Astana:** academies, galleries and studios against village makers.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay) and east (Oskemen):** Russian, Ukrainian and German crafts; Old Believer painted wood *check*.
- **Uyghurs, Dungan, Koryo-saram:** their own craft and stage lines, each with a homeland reference.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia and China:** felt, saddlery and silver skills kept abroad and brought back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music and applied-art scholarship; mixed for contemporary art; thin for everyday improvising, which lives in video and hearsay.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for craft terms, *күй* legends and makers' interviews; Russian for Soviet art history and the patent office; Uyghur and Ukrainian for those makers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** credit for oral works (whose *күй* is it?) is disputed between regions; copyright belongs to Libra; makers may guard family patterns.
- **Held for review:** none; no maker or craft pointer needed setting aside.
