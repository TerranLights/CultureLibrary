## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-speaking south and west against Russian-speaking north and east:** whose register counts as frank or as rude.
- **Almaty:** galleries, clubs and protest sites; test whether it is the loosest stage.
- **Astana:** a civil-service city; test whether public speech there is more guarded.
- **Western oil towns (Atyrau, Aktau, Zhanaozen):** labor and faith expressed in public.
- **Auyls:** elders close by; test whether *ел не дейді* weighs more in small places.
- **Devout Muslims and secular urbanites:** dress as the dividing line.
- **Germans, Koryo-saram, Uyghurs:** each expressing a self in a second language or a community stage.
- **Ukrainians, Tatars, Dungan:** song circles that keep their own languages in use.
- **Qandastar:** returnees whose Kazakh is often older in style, heard as different by city Kazakhs.
