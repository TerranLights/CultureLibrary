## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan) against north (Petropavl, Kostanay):** regional pride voiced in teasing.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau):** pride in oil wealth and in the Junior zhuz's history.
- **Karaganda and Ekibastuz:** mining-town pride across Kazakh, Russian, German and Korean families.
- **Almaty against Astana:** old capital and new capital, each with its boasts.
- **Kazakh-speaking against Russian-speaking Kazakhs:** language as a matter of pride or of shame.
- **Russians and Cossack descendants of the north and west:** their own settlement histories.
- **Uyghurs, Dungan, Koryo-saram, Uzbeks:** heroes and achievements each community keeps.
- **Germans and Ukrainians:** pride in rebuilding after deportation and settlement.
- **Qandastar:** pride in having kept the language abroad, met by city Kazakhs' views of them.
