## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turkistan and Shymkent:** Uzbek and Kazakh notables, mosque and community honors.
- **Karaganda, Pavlodar, Ekibastuz:** industrial towns with Soviet-style labor honors.
- **Atyrau, Aktau:** oil-company recognition schemes against older local honors.
- **Almaty and Astana:** where state orders, prizes and galas cluster.
- **Auyl elders:** recognition through seating, the sheep's head and the *бата*.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans, Chechens, Ingush:** deported peoples whose ordeal was long unacknowledged.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of the southeast:** their own heroes, partly absent from national lists *check*.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north:** recognition across the 1991 break, Soviet titles kept or lapsed.
- **Qandastar:** credentials and skills set against the recognition the system extends.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state award lists and commemorations; mixed for workplace practice; thin for everyday credit and thanks.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for feast custom, *жырау* verse and obituaries; Russian for decrees, workplace records and northern press; Korean, Uzbek and Uyghur for community honors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine and repression memory are politically charged; awards may track patronage; the January 2022 victims' recognition is disputed; rank by descent goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no honor or credit pointer needed setting aside.
