## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty and Astana:** promenades, cafés and camera networks, the most watched and most watching places.
- **Southern towns (Turkistan, Kentau, Sairam):** close neighborhoods where custom governs who is seen at home.
- **Northern villages (North Kazakhstan, Kostanay):** emptying places and the old who remain.
- **Western oil towns:** workers on shift rotations, absent from home and often from view.
- **Brides and young wives:** the shift from being shown at the wedding to staying out of sight at home *check*.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** small-language media and their audiences.
- **Uzbeks:** citizens of the south against migrants from Uzbekistan in the same bazaars.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** groups present in history but thin on screen *check*.
- **Disabled people and the elderly:** visibility in public space across all groups.
