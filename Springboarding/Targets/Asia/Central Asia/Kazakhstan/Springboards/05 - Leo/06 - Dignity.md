## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karaganda, Ekibastuz, Temirtau:** miners and steelworkers, labor dignity in heavy industry.
- **Southern cities (Shymkent, Sairam, Turkistan):** mahalla help and Uzbek and Kazakh neighborhood life.
- **Almaty and Astana:** shelters, canteens and service workers in big cities.
- **Steppe herding districts:** shepherds hired by absentee owners *check*.
- **Bayanaul and Pavlodar:** the north-east of Mashhur Zhusup and its religious heritage.
- **Disabled and deaf people:** across all ethnic groups.
- **Germans, Russians, Ukrainians:** elders left behind by emigrating children.
- **Uyghurs:** their own neighborhood committees in Almaty region.
- **Qandastar:** dignity in finding housing and work after resettlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for welfare and institutional reports; thin for dignity as felt in daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for religious writing, funeral custom and rural press; Russian for welfare, labor and monitoring reports; Uzbek, Uyghur and German for community aid; sign-language interpreters for deaf people's accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** people in institutions and shelters need consent and anonymity; funeral rites may be private; legal rights go to Libra, compassion to Pisces.
