## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Sairam):** Kazakh and Uzbek feast giving and charitable meals.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau):** oil-company philanthropy against older kin-based giving.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay, Kokshetau):** Orthodox, Catholic and Lutheran parish charity.
- **Almaty and Astana:** online fundraising, corporate funds and restaurant tipping.
- **Auyls across the steppe:** sponsors from the city, mosques and schools built by natives.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** mosque-centered giving in Almaty region and Korday district.
- **Germans and Poles:** Catholic and Lutheran charity networks with links to Europe.
- **Koryo-saram:** community funds and scholarships *check*.
- **Qandastar:** receivers of help, and the terms on which it comes.
