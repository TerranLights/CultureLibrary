## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Senior, Middle and Junior zhuz regions:** whether lineage still shapes who is followed *check*.
- **Western oil region:** foreign managers, local foremen and labor leaders side by side.
- **Southern Kazakh and Uzbek neighborhoods:** mahalla heads and mosque elders.
- **Northern industrial towns:** Soviet-style directors and their successors.
- **Almaty and Astana:** corporate and civil-service leadership cultures.
- **Auyls:** elected akims, elders and the large-herd owners.
- **Uyghurs:** *машрәп* leaders and community elders in Almaty region.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** farm brigades and church or cultural-center leaders.
- **Women across groups:** leaders in schools, NGOs and local government.
