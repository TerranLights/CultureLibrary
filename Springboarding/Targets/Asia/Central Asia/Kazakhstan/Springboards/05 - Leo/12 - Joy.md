## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan):** feast songs, *айт* visits and Uzbek spring nights.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Oral):** feast customs of the Junior zhuz.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay) and east (Oskemen, Ridder):** Orthodox, Lutheran and Catholic calendars.
- **Almaty and Astana:** concerts, comedy and street celebrations.
- **Steppe auyls:** test whether gladness gathers mostly around family feasts and holidays.
- **Devout and secular households:** New Year and music as points of difference.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** wedding dance and song circles *check*.
- **Germans, Ukrainians, Russians:** winter and spring church festivals.
- **Koryo-saram and Tatars:** their own holiday tables in mixed cities.
