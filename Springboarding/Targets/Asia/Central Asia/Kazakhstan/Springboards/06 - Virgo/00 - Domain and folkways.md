## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau, Zhanaozen, Aktobe):** oil-field shift work beside herding.
- **Center (Karaganda, Temirtau, Zhezkazgan, Balkhash):** mining and steel towns raised by camp and deportee labor.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl, Pavlodar, Kokshetau):** grain farming of the Virgin Lands, Russian and Ukrainian villages.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey, Ridder):** metal works and the test-site region's long health shadow.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda, Taraz, Zhetysu):** irrigated farming, bazaars, crafts and the densest *ауыл* life.
- **Germans, Koryo-saram, Chechens and Ingush:** deported peoples whose work history began under compulsion.
- **Uzbeks of the south; Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu and Korday:** market gardening, trade and their own craft lines.
- **Tatars:** urban teachers, merchants and clergy since the nineteenth century.
- **Qandastar (former Oralman) from Mongolia, China and Uzbekistan:** herding skills meeting a wage job market.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet labor history and statistics; mixed for the daily round and the kept house; thin for paid domestic help.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for herding, craft and cleaning words; Russian for archives, statistics and the industrial towns; German for labor-army letters; Korean for Koryo-saram farm history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s famine (*Ашаршылық*) and camp labor; the 2011 Zhanaozen shootings; migrant workers' conditions; illness from the Semipalatinsk test site. Labor law belongs to Libra, the clock and public order to Capricorn. The author has not yet settled the nation's exact lands; the Virgo sheets are written for the core lands of present Kazakhstan.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer in this domain needed setting aside.
