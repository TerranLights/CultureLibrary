# Kazakhstan: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Uzbek, Uyghur, Dungan, Ukrainian and German for the work histories of those communities. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mangystau Adai and incoming oil specialists:** local labor against rotation crews and foreign firms.
- **Karaganda mining families:** Kazakh, German, Ukrainian, Polish and Chechen lines in one shaft.
- **Northern grain belt:** agroholding employees, Ukrainian and Russian village farmers, Mennonite remnants.
- **Ridder, Oskemen and Old Believer villages (east):** metallurgy beside small farming.
- **South Kazakh villages and Shymkent's edges:** smallholders, herders and day laborers.
- **Uzbeks of Maktaaral and Sairam:** cotton, orchards and urban crafts.
- **Dungan of Masanchi and Sortobe; Uyghurs of Chundzha:** vegetable growing and market trade.
- **Kyzylorda fishermen and rice growers, including Koryo-saram descendants.**
- **Almaty and Astana office and IT staff:** career talk against village livelihood.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia and China:** herders seeking wage jobs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official employment data and Soviet industrial history; mixed for informal work; thin for what work means to herders, in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for herding and village talk; Russian for unions, mining and the north; Uzbek and Uyghur for southern and Zhetysu trades; German and Ukrainian for settler letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2011 Zhanaozen killings and later trials; union closures; migrant workers' status; child labor in cotton (*check* period). Labor law and contracts belong to Libra; duty, discipline and merit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on this concept was set aside.
