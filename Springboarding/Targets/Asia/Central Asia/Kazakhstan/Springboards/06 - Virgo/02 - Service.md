## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh households of the south and west:** the *келін* and younger kin serving guests, set against northern city homes.
- **Uzbek *mahalla* of Sairam and Turkistan:** service organized by the quarter.
- **Uyghur communities of Zhetysu:** youth groups that serve at gatherings.
- **Russian Orthodox and Old Believer parishes; Lutheran and Catholic Germans:** church charity and sisterhoods.
- **Koryo-saram:** associations that serve elders and community feasts.
- **Tatar women's and mosque circles in Petropavl and Semey:** a long urban tradition of religious charity.
- **Civil servants of Astana:** a state career that draws from every region.
- **Mangystau and Atyrau volunteers:** disaster response in the oil regions.
- **Migrant domestic workers from Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan:** service given by non-citizens.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and religious discourse on service; mixed for volunteering and customer service; thin for paid household service.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for hospitality, religious and military usage; Russian for the civil service and urban service trades; Uzbek and Uyghur for quarter-level service; Tatar and German for church and mosque charity.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hazing in the army (*дедовщина*, *check* current press); migrant domestic workers' status; historical servitude in the khanates; patronage in state posts. The law of public office belongs to Libra; obedience and hierarchy to Capricorn.
