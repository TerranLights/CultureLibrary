## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding families of the west and the Betpak-Dala:** children's and elders' tasks on the flock.
- **Southern Kazakh households:** grandparents raising the first grandchild, youngest sons keeping the parents.
- **Northern Russian and Ukrainian villages:** garden plots, preserving and the pensioner's role.
- **Uzbek craftsmen of Turkistan and Sairam:** skills handed from master to son.
- **Uyghur and Dungan farming villages of Zhetysu:** family labor on vegetable plots.
- **Koryo-saram families:** practical trades valued through deportation and resettlement.
- **German families who stayed after the 1990s emigration:** elders left behind by children in Germany.
- **Disabled people's groups in Almaty and Astana:** the challenge to worth measured by work.
- **Qandastar:** herding knowledge that may or may not count on the job market.
