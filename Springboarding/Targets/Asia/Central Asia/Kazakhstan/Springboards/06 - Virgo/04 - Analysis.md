## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty and Astana:** universities, labs, think tanks and the data-heavy press.
- **Karaganda, Pavlodar, Oskemen:** engineering and technical schools of the Soviet industrial north and east.
- **Zhezkazgan and Balkhash:** mining towns whose geology made Satpayev's name.
- **Southern villages (Turkistan, Zhambyl):** stone-reading and folk reckoning beside new schools.
- **Western herding districts:** weather and herd knowledge kept by older herders.
- **Russians and Ukrainians of the north:** Soviet technical schooling as family tradition.
- **Koryo-saram and German families:** whether technical careers after deportation ran in families; to test, not assume.
- **Uyghur and Uzbek schools in their own languages:** what analysis is called and how it is taught.
- **Qandastar from China and Mongolia:** schooled in other systems, now in Kazakh classrooms.
