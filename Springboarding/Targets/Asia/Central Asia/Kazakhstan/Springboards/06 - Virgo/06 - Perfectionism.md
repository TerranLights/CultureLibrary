## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turkistan and Sairam:** shrine craft and Uzbek master lines.
- **Almaty and Astana:** exam-driven families, elite schools and the beauty trade.
- **Karaganda, Pavlodar and Oskemen:** Soviet factory quality culture and its heirs.
- **Southern and western Kazakh households:** the guest table and wedding display.
- **Kazakh craftsmen of the steppe districts:** dombra, saddle and felt workshops.
- **Germans in the north:** self-described thoroughness, to be tested against practice.
- **Koryo-saram:** school expectations within the community, a research question only.
- **Uyghur craft families of Zhetysu:** embroidery and woodwork standards.
- **Religious students in madrasas and Quran schools:** recitation standards.
