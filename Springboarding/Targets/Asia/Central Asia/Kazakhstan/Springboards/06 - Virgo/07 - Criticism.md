*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-language press and audiences of the south and west:** satire, verse and complaint in Kazakh.
- **Russian-speaking audiences of Almaty, Karaganda, Kostanay and Pavlodar:** review culture and Russian-language critics.
- **Mangystau:** a region with a record of labor protest and public complaint.
- **Uyghurs of Zhetysu and their press (*Uyghur Avazi* *check*):** Afandi humor and community letters.
- **Uzbeks of Turkistan and Shymkent:** Afandi stories and *mahalla* elders' rebukes.
- **Koryo-saram (*Koryo Ilbo* *check*) and Germans (*Deutsche Allgemeine Zeitung*, Almaty *check*):** minority papers and what they print about the state.
- **Families of different generations:** how the young speak back to elders.
- **Qandastar:** criticized as outsiders and criticizing the reception they got.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary, satirical and Soviet sources; mixed for online criticism; thin for criticism within families outside forums.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for verse and satire; Russian for party archives, reviews and critics; Uyghur and Uzbek for Afandi tales; Korean and German for the minority press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the head of state and officials carries legal risk; the January 2022 unrest; journalists' safety. Defamation and insult law belongs to Libra; authority and obedience to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; every criticism pointer stands.
