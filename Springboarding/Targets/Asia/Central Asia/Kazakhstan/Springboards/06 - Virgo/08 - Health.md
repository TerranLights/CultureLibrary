## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Kazakhstan around Semey:** test-site health legacies and their own clinics.
- **Kyzylorda and the Aral shore:** salt-dust illness and water quality.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau):** oil-industry pollution and health complaints.
- **Southern towns and villages:** healers, shrines and the Saryagash spas.
- **Northern Russian, Ukrainian and German communities:** dacha, *баня* and Soviet sanatorium habits.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** their own medicine and dietary rules.
- **Koryo-saram:** Korean-medicine clinics and ties to Korea for treatment.
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** urban medical families since the Soviet years.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia and China:** healers and remedies brought on return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, Soviet medical history and policy; mixed for folk healing; thin for mental illness and addiction outside the press.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for clinics, statistics and specialists; Kazakh for rites, folk terms and rural talk; Uyghur, Korean and Dungan for minority healing; Uzbek for southern *табиб* practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tuberculosis and HIV stigma; mental illness and suicide; test-site and Aral health damage; healers' own knowledge, to be shared only as they choose. Death and dying belong to Scorpio; care in the family to Cancer.
- **Held for review:** none; no health pointer needed setting aside.
