## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tatars of Petropavl, Semey and Oral:** heirs of the reform-school tradition.
- **Kazakh herders' descendants in the center and the Betpak-Dala:** the memory of forced settling.
- **Southern Kazakhs and Uzbeks of Shymkent:** house extensions and family improvement projects.
- **Almaty and Astana middle classes:** trainings, renovations and self-development books.
- **Karaganda and Pavlodar:** industrial regions remade by canals and plants.
- **Germans and Koryo-saram:** language and culture revival societies.
- **Uyghurs of Zhetysu:** schooling in Uyghur and its own reform history.
- **Mangystau and the oil west:** promises of betterment against lived conditions.
- **Qandastar:** return framed as betterment, and how returnees judge it.
