## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The three zhuz:** Senior (Zhetysu, south), Middle (center, north, east), Junior (west); each remembers its own biy, and genealogy (*шежіре*) governs whom one may marry.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau, Aktobe, Oral):** descent-group ties in matchmaking and quarrels, to test against the cities.
- **South (Turkistan, Shymkent, Taraz, Kyzylorda):** shrine country around Ordabasy; mosque weddings, village elders' councils.
- **North and center (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar, Karaganda):** Russian, Ukrainian and German villages with registry and church weddings.
- **Almaty, Astana:** dating apps, prenuptial contracts and court filings at their densest.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** their own marriage alliances and mosque-led mediation.
- **Koryo-saram:** a wedding feast cycle carried from the Far East.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam:** *mahalla* elders as the first stop in a family quarrel.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** reconciliation between families after a killing, kept in exile since 1944.
- **Tatars; Qandastar (returnees) from Mongolia and China:** town imams' nikah records; customs formed outside the Soviet record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for customary law in imperial and Soviet ethnography and for the written codes; mixed for present wedding, contract and quarrel practice; thin for laboratory fairness games.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for epics, rite songs and biy rulings; Russian for the codes' working texts and imperial records; Uyghur, Uzbek, Korean and German for those communities' marriage records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride kidnapping (*қыз алып қашу*), domestic violence, the 1986 *Желтоқсан* events and the trials after January 2022; genealogy can expose descent-group tensions. The spirit of the law belongs to Capricorn. No scope file exists yet and the author has not settled the nation's exact lands; the Libra sheets are written for the core lands of present Kazakhstan.
- **Held for review:** none for the domain sheet.
