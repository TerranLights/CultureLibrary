## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Mangystau):** bride payments and genealogical checks on a match, compared with the cities.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Taraz):** large two-family alliances through *құдалық* and arranged introductions.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl, Pavlodar):** cohabitation and registry-only unions, set against the south's two-family weddings.
- **Almaty and Astana:** dual-earner couples, dating apps and small-firm partners.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** partners across the Kazakh-Slavic line, and children of such marriages.
- **Koryo-saram:** intermarriage with Kazakhs and Russians against marrying within the community.
- **Germans:** marriages split by emigration to Germany after 1990.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** marrying within the community, as their press frames it.
- **Uzbeks of the south:** family trading partnerships in bazaars.
- **Qandastar:** returnees marrying local Kazakhs, and differences in custom between them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official marriage statistics and for oil joint-venture records; mixed for bride payment and exogamy in practice; thin for unregistered couples.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for customary terms and proverbs; Russian for statistics, company law and business press; Korean and German for community family histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride kidnapping; coercive marriage; same-sex couples have no legal recognition and face hostility, so sources are guarded; the letter of marriage law sits in the domain sheet.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld under the standing rules here.
