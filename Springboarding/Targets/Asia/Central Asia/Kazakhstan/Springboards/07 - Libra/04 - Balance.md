## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North and south:** the regional weighting behind the capital's move and the language balance in schools.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau, Aktobe):** oil revenue sent to the center against what stays, and the Caspian's damaged shore.
- **Kyzylorda and the Aral region:** balance in nature discussed as loss and partial recovery.
- **Center (Karaganda, Ulytau):** single-industry towns out of balance after factories closed.
- **Astana and Almaty:** long hours, commuting and the language of work-life balance.
- **Uyghurs of Zhetysu:** a humoral medical system of their own.
- **Dungan:** food and healing ways from their Chinese-Muslim heritage.
- **Koryo-saram:** diet and health ideas carried from Korea, among the oldest settlers.
- **Russians and Germans of the north:** Soviet sanatorium culture as a route to restoring the body.
- **Qandastar from China and Mongolia:** healing ways learned in other systems.
