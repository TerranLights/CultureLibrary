## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Taraz):** dense kin networks: test whether reproach of *рушылдық* is sharper or milder there.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau, Aktobe):** oil-sector jobs and claims that hiring follows home district.
- **North and center (Pavlodar, Kostanay, Karaganda):** the Soviet vocabulary of *блат* and "telephone law" in Russian-language speech.
- **Astana:** a capital whose civil service gathered staff from every region, and the talk of *землячество* that followed.
- **Russians, Ukrainians and Germans:** perceptions of ethnic preference in state jobs, a delicate subject.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** encounters with police and local officials in Zhetysu villages.
- **Koryo-saram:** business networks and their reputation for trust within the community, to test rather than assume.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Turkistan:** *mahalla* and market leaders as brokers with officials.
- **Tatars:** long-settled urban professionals and their routes into public office.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers without inherited connections facing offices and courts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official anti-corruption material and international indices; mixed for judicial practice; thin and anecdotal for descent-group favoritism.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for the reproach words and comedy; Russian for laws, court rules and most investigative reporting; Uzbek and Uyghur for southern and Zhetysu community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** talk of zhuz or ethnic preference in hiring is touchy and easily slanderous; named corruption cases carry defamation risk; why people trust or distrust courts belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none raised by this concept.
