## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West (Aktau, Atyrau):** the Caspian bargain, oil-company talks and Iran and Russia across the sea.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey, Zaisan):** the China border and the nuclear story, each a diplomatic stage.
- **South (Saryagash, Turkistan, Shymkent):** daily cross-border ties with Uzbekistan, and the Bagys talks.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** the long Russian border and its family and trade links.
- **Astana:** the capital as host of summits, congresses and foreign missions.
- **Germans:** a community negotiating emigration terms and cultural rights between two states.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** neighbors of China whose own ties across the border are delicate.
- **Uzbeks of the south:** a community whose standing rises and falls with relations to Tashkent.
- **Russians of the north:** a population at the center of talks with Moscow.
- **Qandastar from China and Mongolia:** whose return needed agreements between governments.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and treaties; mixed for steppe-era diplomacy outside Russian archives; thin for everyday tact studied as such.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for protocol and proverbs; Russian for archives and treaty commentary; Chinese for Qing and PRC records; English for international-relations work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relations with China touch Kazakhs and Uyghurs detained in Xinjiang (East Turkestan as comparison only); the China border concessions stirred protest; talk about Russia's claims on the north is charged.
- **Held for review:** no diplomacy pointer needed holding back.
