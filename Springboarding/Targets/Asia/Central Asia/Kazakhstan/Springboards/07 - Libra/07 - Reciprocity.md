# Kazakhstan: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and the 2021 Latin script) and Russian; Uzbek, Korean, Tatar and German for those communities' rotating-credit circles, prize collections and neighborly help. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West (Mangystau, Atyrau):** memorial feasts and gift sums where descent-group networks stay dense.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** large weddings, and the sharpest debate over gift inflation.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl):** mixed villages where Kazakh *ерулік* meets Slavic neighborly help.
- **Center (Karaganda, Temirtau):** mining towns whose mutual-aid funds grew up in Soviet workplaces.
- **Almaty and Astana:** apartment neighbors, rotating funds among coworkers, gifts transferred by phone.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam:** *gap* circles and their rules on turns.
- **Koryo-saram:** credit circles and the obligations of the 60th-birthday feast.
- **Tatars and Bashkirs:** Sabantuy giving in town communities.
- **Germans:** neighborly exchange remembered from deportation and kolkhoz years.
- **Ukrainians and Poles of the north:** church-centered giving and returns, as their parishes record them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for customary-law ethnography; mixed for present-day gift practice; thin for statistics on informal help between households.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for customary terms, tales and feast etiquette; Russian for law and mining-town memory; Uzbek, Korean and Tatar for the circles and festivals; German for memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift ledgers are private family papers; where gifts shade into bribes belongs to impartiality and corruption; revenge itself is Scorpio's.
- **Held for review:** none; the gift, loan and raid pointers all stand.
