## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Turkistan, Shymkent):** large families where elders' blessing comes before a marriage, a move or a business.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau):** shift workers weighing a move to the oil cities against staying near kin.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** towns where leaving for Russia or for the capital is a recurring question.
- **Balkhash shore (Ulken):** the village chosen as the plant's site, and its neighbors' stake in a long-deferred decision.
- **Almaty and Astana:** young professionals torn between staying, moving abroad or returning.
- **Germans:** emigration as a family's defining decision since 1990.
- **Koryo-saram:** work migration to Korea in stages.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** staying or leaving after 1991, a question reopened in later crises.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** choices shaped by ties across the Chinese border.
- **Qandastar:** returnees who settle, move on or go back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; divination and elders' counsel sit in ethnography, public deferral in the press, emigration intentions in polls; thin for personal indecisiveness as such.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for divination and proverbs; Russian for policy, polls and press; German and Korean for emigration memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination is disputed by religious authorities; emigration talk can be political; the nuclear vote was contested.
- **Held for review:** nothing about indecision was held back.
