## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** large households where in-laws may press a bride to endure; test against crisis-center records.
- **West (Mangystau, Aktobe):** the 2011 strike and its aftermath, to test how open protest is weighed against caution.
- **Center (Karaganda, Temirtau):** mining towns where silence on safety meets the job market.
- **North (Kostanay, Pavlodar, Petropavl):** Russian-speaking families and the "keep the dirt at home" rule.
- **Almaty and Astana:** activists, lawyers and bloggers breaking silences, and the backlash against them.
- **Uyghurs of Zhetysu:** relatives across the border and the cost of speaking.
- **Qandastar from China:** the same choice, for families split by the Xinjiang camps.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** how the 1944 deportation was spoken of under Soviet rule and after.
- **Germans:** when and to whom labor-army memories were first told.
- **Meskhetian Turks and Dungan:** small communities after local clashes, and the choice to stay quiet.
