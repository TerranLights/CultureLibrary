# Kazakhstan: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and the 2021 Latin script) and Russian; German, Chechen and Korean for deported peoples once labeled enemies; Uzbek and Uyghur for migrants and minorities blamed in local quarrels. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty and Astana against the auyl:** labels each side gives the other, from *мамбет* to *шала*.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan):** migrants from the south blamed in northern and capital cities, and their answer.
- **West (Aktobe, Atyrau):** land and oil grievances aimed outward at China or foreign firms.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay):** Russian-speaking towns where blame runs between "nationalists" and "colonizers".
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** how blame is traded with Kazakh speakers in quarrels over language and history.
- **Germans and Chechens:** branded "fascists" or "bandits" on deportation, and how they tell it.
- **Koryo-saram:** deported in 1937 under a charge of spying (*check*), and how the community answers it.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** sometimes read as "Chinese" in anti-China talk.
- **Uzbeks and Central Asian migrant workers:** blamed in southern and city quarrels.
- **Qandastar:** "oralman" turned into a slur; the 2020 renaming (*check*) and what it answered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet repression and enemy images; mixed for present scapegoating in the press; thin for projection as an everyday psychological idea outside cities.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for labels, rites and tales; Russian for archives, law and most online quarrels; German, Chechen and Korean for deportees' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labels for groups are slurs to be quoted only as data; January 2022 and article 174 cases are politically charged; the evil eye overlaps with healers whom clergy oppose.
- **Held for review:** nothing raised by projection was set aside.
