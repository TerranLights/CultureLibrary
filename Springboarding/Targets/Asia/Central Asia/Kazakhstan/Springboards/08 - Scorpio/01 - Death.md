# Kazakhstan: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and the 2021 Latin script), Russian; Uzbek, Uyghur, Tatar, Korean and German for those communities' words of grief. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how dying, the dead and the meaning of death are understood, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turkistan region and Sairam:** death near the Yasawi mausoleum; pilgrims who ask the dead for help.
- **Kyzylorda and the Aral shore:** deaths blamed on a dying sea; families who left.
- **Semey and Abai region:** test-site deaths and illness; grief mixed with grievance.
- **Mangystau and Atyrau:** oil-town accidents and Zhanaozen's dead; mourning by descent group.
- **Karaganda and Zhezkazgan:** mining deaths (query «шахта авария Караганда») and camp burial grounds.
- **Almaty and Astana:** hospital death and hospice against home death in the village.
- **Russians, Ukrainians, Belarusians of the north:** Orthodox memorial cycle and graveside meals.
- **Germans and Poles of Karaganda, Kostanay, Kokshetau:** Lutheran and Catholic memory of the deportation dead.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** mosque-led burial in Zhetysu and Zhambyl.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia (Bayan-Ölgii):** mourning songs and timing that may differ from local practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1930s famine and camp deaths and for mortality statistics; mixed for grief and hospice; thin for how the dying themselves speak.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for laments, condolence formulas and Yasawi's verse; Russian for medical and Soviet records; Italian and French for famine history; Tatar, German and Uyghur for those communities' mourning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, especially among the young; the famine death toll is disputed; January 2022 deaths are politically charged and some files are sealed. Funeral practice belongs to the domain sheet.
- **Held for review:** none (the grief and dying pointers raise no standing-rule issue).
