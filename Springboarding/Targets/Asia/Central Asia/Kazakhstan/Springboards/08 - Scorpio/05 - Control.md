## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herding auls of the center and west:** fate, weather and the herd; control as a daily wager.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Mangystau):** religious families and schools at odds over dress.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan):** elders' and in-laws' authority in large households.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl):** nuclear-family Russians and Germans, with other household roles.
- **East (Oskemen, Zaisan):** border districts with closer security presence.
- **Almaty and Astana:** camera-heavy streets and the most online life.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Saryagash:** *mahalla* neighbors as watchers.
- **Uyghurs of Zharkent and Almaty:** community gatherings that set and enforce rules.
- **Koryo-saram:** parents' control over schooling and careers, as their own memoirs and studies describe it.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** elders' say over young men's conduct.
- **Qandastar from Xinjiang:** people who have known another state's surveillance and can compare.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state control of information and for Soviet controls on movement; mixed for household control; thin for measured locus of control.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for zhyrau verse, folk custom and talk shows; Russian for NGO and rights reports; Uyghur and Uzbek for community rules; Korean for Koryo-saram family memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and the security services; domestic violence; religious dress. Self-control proper goes to Capricorn.
