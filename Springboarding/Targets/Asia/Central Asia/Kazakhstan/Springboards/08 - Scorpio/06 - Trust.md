## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Auls of the south and west:** trust carried by kin and descent group.
- **Northern towns with Russian, Ukrainian and Belarusian majorities:** whether trust in neighbors and in the state differs from the south, as a question.
- **Almaty and Astana:** app ratings, reviews and banks in place of personal knowledge.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Saryagash:** bazaar credit and family firms.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty region:** trading networks and community lending.
- **Dungan villages of Korday:** trust across groups after the 2020 violence.
- **Koryo-saram:** a dense network across cities.
- **Germans:** congregations of the north; ties kept with emigrated kin.
- **Tatars:** old merchant families of Semey and Petropavl.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** ties inside the community since the deportation, in their own accounts.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers waiting to be trusted by local officials and neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for banking and vaccination data and for observers' reports; mixed for youth and media surveys; thin for everyday lending.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for the *аманат* idea, fables and proverbs; Russian for banking, fraud and survey reports; Korean, Uzbek and Uyghur for community lending.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** courts and police trust belong to Capricorn; elections are political; the 2020 Korday violence remains raw.
- **Held for review:** none, since the lending, banking and survey pointers break no rule.
