## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mangystau and the Adai:** a western descent group with its own memory of raids and redress.
- **Zhetysu and Almaty region:** 1916 memories shared by Kazakhs, Kyrgyz neighbors and settler descendants.
- **Semirechye Cossack descendants and other Russians:** the reprisal story told from the other side.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** feud customs and their settlement in exile; the 2007 Malovodnoe clash (*check*).
- **Kurds and Meskhetian Turks of the south:** village clashes and how they were settled.
- **Dungan of Korday:** reprisal and restraint after 2020.
- **Uyghurs:** hero songs and street-level grievance in Almaty's districts.
- **Koryo-saram:** revenge told in film and memoir rather than custom.
- **Youth gangs of Almaty and Shymkent microdistricts:** codes of payback (query «район на район драки Алматы»).
- **Women online:** exposure and shaming used as revenge on them, and by them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for epic, drama and 1916 memory; mixed for feud settlement among minorities; thin for present-day private revenge.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for epics, Auezov and amends customs; Russian for crime data and 1916 archives; Chechen for feud practice; Uyghur for hero songs; Korean for the film's source.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** image-based abuse (no viewing of material); interethnic clashes; 1916 is a live quarrel between historians. Private vengeance set against state punishment is Capricorn's.
