# Kazakhstan: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and the 2021 Latin script), Russian; Polish, German, Uyghur and Korean for faiths, names and kin ties once kept hidden. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families raising a first grandchild as their own (*бауырына салу*):** a secret of parentage, told or not told later.
- **Kurchatov, Priozersk, Stepnogorsk, Baikonur:** towns built around state secrets.
- **Semey region villages:** what the test years kept from them.
- **South (Turkistan, Sairam):** Sufi lineages and teaching kept for initiates.
- **West (Mangystau):** underground mosques and hidden pilgrim routes, as local guides tell it.
- **Poles and Germans of the north:** faith and language once used only at home.
- **Koryo-saram:** whether Korean given names lived on inside the family beside Russian ones, as a question.
- **Uyghurs:** news of kin across the Chinese border, guarded for safety.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** family matters kept from outsiders, as a question to test.
- **Qandastar:** what they say and do not say about the countries they left.
- **Almaty and Astana:** LGBT people's double lives and online privacy.
