*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of the center and the west:** *жұт* as a lived risk; state help or kin help.
- **Mangystau (Zhanaozen, Aktau):** fuel prices and oil-town strikes as the spark of national crises.
- **Flood oblasts of the north and west:** villages moved, and how they speak of it.
- **Almaty:** earthquake and mudflow risk in a city of two million.
- **Monotowns:** Kentau, Zhanatas, Arkalyk, Temirtau as cases of slow crisis.
- **Germans, Russians, Ukrainians of the north:** the 1990s choice to leave or stay.
- **Ukrainians since 2022:** crisis at home felt in Kazakhstan.
- **Uyghurs:** kin in Xinjiang as a long, unresolved crisis.
- **Dungan of Korday:** the February 2020 violence.
- **Koryo-saram:** a deportation still told as the founding crisis.
- **Qandastar:** arrivals in the hard 1990s, and later.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine, emigration, financial shocks and disasters; mixed for January 2022 (official and independent counts differ); thin for personal crises.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *жұт* memory and the novel; Russian for agency reports and press; French for Ohayon; German and Ukrainian for emigration and war accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** January 2022 casualties and trials; COVID death counts; Xinjiang kin; suicide in personal-crisis records.
- **Held for review:** no entries (disaster, money and helpline pointers clear the standing rules).

## 5. First queries
1. stat.gov.kz: 1989, 1999 and 2009 census tables by ethnicity and oblast; mark the largest drops.

3. Query «жұт болған жылдар» and «джут поголовье падеж» in Kazakh press and archive guides, decade by decade, to list the remembered *жұт* years.
4. Query «паводки 2024 Казахстан итоги МЧС» for the ministry's flood reports by oblast.
5. Library: Ohayon; Elubay; collections of *Ақсақ құлан* legend versions.
