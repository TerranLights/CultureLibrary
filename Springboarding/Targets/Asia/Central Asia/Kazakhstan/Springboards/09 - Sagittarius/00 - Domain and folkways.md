## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Turkistan, Sairam, Shymkent, Kyzylorda, Taraz):** shrine circuits, *қожа* lineages and the densest mosque life.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Mangystau, Oral):** underground-mosque pilgrimage (Beket Ata) beside newer piety currents, to check.
- **North and center (Petropavl, Kostanay, Kokshetau, Karaganda):** Orthodox, Lutheran, Catholic and Baptist congregations in mixed towns.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey, Ridder, Zaisan):** Old Believers of the Rudny Altai; Abai and Shakarim's home country as a philosophy center.
- **Almaty and Astana:** seminaries, the Islamic university, the nonreligious and seekers.
- **Uzbeks and Uyghurs; Dungan of Zhambyl:** *mahalla* mosques and Hui-style worship.
- **Tatars:** heirs of the steppe's imperial-era clergy.
- **Germans, Poles, Ukrainians, Koryo-saram:** deported peoples' churches; Korean Protestant missions after 1991.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** Sufi brotherhoods kept through exile.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia, China and Uzbekistan:** practice formed outside Soviet secularism.
