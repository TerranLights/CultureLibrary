# Kazakhstan: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian; Uyghur, Uzbek, Tatar, Polish, German, Ukrainian and Korean for minority congregations. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven: God, gods, spirits, a sacred order; by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Turkistan, Sairam, Shymkent):** shrine faith and *қожа* families with inherited religious standing.
- **West (Mangystau, Atyrau):** pilgrimage to underground mosques beside a younger scripture-focused piety.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl, Kokshetau):** Kazakhs raised in Russian-speaking towns, where faith may be recent or nominal.
- **East (Semey, Zaisan, Ridder):** Old Believers; Kazakh villages near the former test site.
- **Uyghurs, Uzbeks, Dungan:** mosque-centered neighborhoods with their own imams.
- **Poles, Germans, Ukrainians:** Catholic, Lutheran, Greek Catholic and Mennonite faith shaped by deportation.
- **Koryo-saram:** Protestant converts beside families with no church.
- **Qandastar:** returnees from China and Mongolia whose faith formed outside the Soviet era.
- **Almaty and Astana:** the young devout, the nonreligious and converts between faiths.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islam and for the Catholic memory of exile; mixed for Orthodox and Protestant life; thin for Kazakh converts to Christianity and for unbelief.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for sermons and amulet prayers; Russian for Soviet religion files; Polish and German for the deportees' parish memory; Korean for the new churches' own literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** converts from Islam may face family or local pressure; unregistered groups risk exposure. Religion law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no faith pointer was set aside.
