## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South and Zhetysu:** large families and dense ritual, where a life's point may be read through kin and feasts.
- **West (Aktobe, Atyrau, Mangystau):** oil-boom work against older herding life.
- **North and center (Karaganda, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** mining and grain towns, the closing of Soviet plants.
- **East (Semey, Oskemen):** Abai's and Shakarim's home country, where their teaching is local heritage.
- **Almaty and Astana:** young professionals, the coaching market, purpose talk online.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** exile writing and memory of Soviet purpose.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans, Chechens:** deported peoples whose stories turn on suffering.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** community writing in their own languages.
- **Qandastar:** returnees who came for a homeland; did it meet what they sought?

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and literary sources; mixed for measured meaning; thin for despair outside health records.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *бата*, verse and Abai scholarship; Russian for psychology scales and exile prose; Arabic for al-Farabi's own text; Uyghur, Korean and Ukrainian for those communities' writers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair touch grieving families (the deaths themselves belong to Scorpio); state ideology texts speak for the state, not for people.
