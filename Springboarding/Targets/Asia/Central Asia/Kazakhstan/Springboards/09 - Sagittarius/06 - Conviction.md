## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Mangystau):** headscarf disputes and long labor stands.
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** religious steadfastness; Hong Beom-do's last city.
- **North and center (Karaganda, Kostanay, Astana):** camp martyrs of several churches; language activists in Russian-speaking towns.
- **East (Semey, Oskemen):** the Alash memory; test-site campaigners.
- **Almaty:** eco-activists, feminists and urban defenders.
- **Koryo-saram, Dungan, Ukrainians, Chechens:** each people's own steadfast figures and brotherhoods.
- **Uyghurs:** community advocates; East Turkestan only as comparison.
- **Converts and the born-in, across faiths:** zeal as a convert's mark or not, to check.
