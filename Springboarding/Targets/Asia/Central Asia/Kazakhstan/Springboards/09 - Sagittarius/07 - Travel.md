## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herders of the south and west (Turkistan, Kyzylorda, Mangystau):** seasonal moves still practiced to differing degrees.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl):** Russian and German families shaped by out-migration since 1991; 2022 arrivals from Russia.
- **East (Zaisan, Katon-Karagay):** returnees from China and Mongolia; mountain tourism in the Altai.
- **Zhetysu:** Uyghur traders and the Khorgos corridor.
- **Turkistan region:** Uzbek kin across the border; pilgrims to Yasawi and Arystan Bab.
- **Almaty and Astana:** study abroad, air travel, the travel-blogging young.
- **Koryo-saram and Poles:** journeys "back" to lands most never saw.
- **Tatars:** heirs of caravan merchants, now an urban minority.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers whose own journey defines them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pilgrimage, returnees and heritage routes; mixed for tourism numbers; thin for the lived detail of seasonal moves today.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for farewell songs and pasture practice; Russian for railroad, trade and migration records; Latin for Rubruck; Korean and Polish for the return-migration side; Uyghur and Uzbek for cross-border kin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border crossings and returnees' status touch policy; labor migrants may be undocumented. Migration law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
