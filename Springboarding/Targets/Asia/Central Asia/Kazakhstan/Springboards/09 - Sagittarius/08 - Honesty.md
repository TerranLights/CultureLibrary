## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Taraz):** gift exchange at feasts; bazaar trade with Uzbek and Uyghur sellers.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau):** oil-sector contracts and the honesty of foreign and local firms.
- **North and center (Karaganda, Pavlodar, Petropavl):** Soviet-schooled Russian speakers and their stock of honesty tales.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey):** mining towns; the border trade with Russia.
- **Almaty and Astana:** universities, online shops, public-service centers (ЦОН/ХҚКО).
- **Koryo-saram:** credit circles and market farming.
- **Uyghurs, Dungan, Uzbeks:** trading families and their reputation systems.
- **Germans and Ukrainians:** church-taught honesty in former settler villages.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers meeting unfamiliar offices and their informal fees.
