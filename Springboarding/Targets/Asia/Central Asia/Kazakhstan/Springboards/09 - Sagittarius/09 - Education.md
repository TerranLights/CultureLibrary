## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** large youth cohorts competing for grants; Yasawi University; Uzbek-language schools.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktobe, Aktau):** oil-industry training and company scholarships.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** Russian-language universities and the pull of study in Russia, to measure.
- **East (Oskemen, Semey):** technical and medical schools; Abai's region as a teaching tradition.
- **Almaty and Astana:** the elite universities and branch campuses.
- **Rural against urban:** village schools and the jump to a city university.
- **Russians, Germans, Koryo-saram:** families weighing study in Russia, Germany or Korea.
- **Uyghurs, Uzbeks, Dungan:** minority-language schools and the path beyond them.
- **Qandastar:** returnees' children entering Kazakh-language schools, some from Chinese- or Mongolian-medium schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy, statistics and institutional history; mixed for the standing of the educated; thin for minority-language higher learning.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh and Russian for university histories and the press; Tatar for madrasa records; English for the new universities' documents; Korean and German for partner institutions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kazakh-Turkish lyceums touch a Turkish political dispute; diploma fraud names living people; minority-language school closures are contested. Education law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no education pointer was set aside.
