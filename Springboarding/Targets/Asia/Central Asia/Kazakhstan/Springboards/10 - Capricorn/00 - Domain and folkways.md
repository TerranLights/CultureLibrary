## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Senior Zhuz south and southeast (Almaty region, Zhetysu, Zhambyl, Turkistan, Shymkent):** the strongest *қожа* and saintly-lineage claims to precedence.
- **Junior Zhuz west (Atyrau, Mangystau, Aktobe, Oral):** oil-town wealth against older *ру* standing.
- **Middle Zhuz center, north and east (Karaganda, Kostanay, Pavlodar, Semey):** industrial ranks where the boss may outrank the elder.
- **Astana and Almaty:** the civil service and firms, where merit and connection compete.
- **Russians and Ural or Semirechye Cossack descendants:** an imperial and military line of command.
- **Uzbeks of Sairam and Shymkent:** the *mahalla* and its elders as a tier of rule.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty and Zhetysu; Dungan of Zhambyl:** village elders and imams beside state office.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans, Tatars, Ukrainians:** collective-farm hierarchies of deported and settled peoples.
- **Qandastar from Mongolia, China and Uzbekistan:** elders formed under other states.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal power; mixed for status and reputation; thin for time ways and minorities' own ranks.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for the zhyrau, elders' councils and shaming talk; Russian for Party and election records; Chagatai and Persian for the khans' chronicles; Uzbek, Uyghur and Dungan for the south's local elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticizing the president or officials can carry legal risk; zhuz and clan talk is touchy. The author has yet to settle Kazakhstan's post-war lands and no scope file exists; every Capricorn sheet is written for the core lands of today's republic.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on the domain was set aside under the standing rules.
