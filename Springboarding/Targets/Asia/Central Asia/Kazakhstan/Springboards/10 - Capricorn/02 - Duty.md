*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian; Uyghur, Uzbek, Koryo-mar Korean, German (including Mennonite Plautdietsch usage) and Tatar for those communities' obligations to kin, faith and the dead. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** lineage duties to shrines and saints, and large families with clear roles.
- **West (Mangystau, Atyrau):** memorial feasts and duties to the necropolises of ancestors.
- **North (Kostanay, Akmola):** heirs of the Komsomol "call" to the Virgin Lands, where duty was framed as a state mission.
- **Center (Karaganda, Temirtau):** miners' shift duty and the deportees' descendants.
- **East (Semey, Oskemen):** veterans' and test-site families' claims on what the state owes them.
- **Almaty and Astana:** adult children far from parents, sending money instead of living near them.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** obligations to the dead and to elders in compact villages.
- **Koryo-saram, Germans, Mennonites:** duties kept through exile, sometimes against the state's.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** obligations to the clan (*тейп*) set beside civic duty.
- **Qandastar:** expected duties to kin who stayed in China or Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and religious duty; mixed for filial duty; thin for minorities' obligations and for those who refuse duty.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for epic, shrine and idiom; Russian for war memory and conscription press; Uyghur and Korean for duties to the dead and to parents; German or Plautdietsch for Mennonite memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questioning the Panfilov story has drawn official anger in the region; draft evasion and tax resistance carry penalties, so sources may hide names.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on duty was set aside.
