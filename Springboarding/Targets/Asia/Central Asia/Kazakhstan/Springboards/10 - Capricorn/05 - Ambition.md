## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** large families pooling for one child's study; trading households.
- **West (Atyrau, Aktau):** oil-company careers and the local-content quotas behind them.
- **North (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** young people weighing Russia, Astana or staying.
- **Center (Karaganda, Zhezkazgan, Ulytau region):** mining towns and their exit routes.
- **Almaty:** founders, artists and the most mobile graduates.
- **Astana:** the civil-service and quasi-state ladder.
- **Koryo-saram:** farm honors in the past, business and professions now, from their own histories.
- **Uyghurs and Uzbeks:** trading families and the place of ambition in them.
- **Germans and Russians:** emigration as one form of rising.
- **Qandastar:** newcomers starting low, and their children's hopes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for education and youth aspirations; mixed for business ambition; thin for private feelings of envy and settling.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for tales, satire and aul youth; Russian for business press and northern youth; Korean for the rice-farm histories; English for startup and study-abroad sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founder stories can hide political patrons; women's career barriers touch family conflict.
- **Held for review:** none; ambition's sources raised no standing-rule issue.
