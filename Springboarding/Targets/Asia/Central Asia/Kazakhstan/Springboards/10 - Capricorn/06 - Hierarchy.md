# Kazakhstan: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian; Chagatai and Russian imperial records for the old estates; Uzbek and Uyghur for southern family precedence; Russian for Cossack and Soviet ranks. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Turkistan, Shymkent, Kyzylorda):** *қожа* lineages and seniority among kin most visible.
- **West (Atyrau, Mangystau):** oil-company grades, expatriate managers and local staff.
- **North (Kostanay, Petropavl):** Soviet farm ranks among Russians, Ukrainians and Germans.
- **Center (Karaganda, Temirtau):** industrial grades and the miners' own ladder.
- **Southeast (Almaty region, Zhetysu):** heirs of the Cossack, peasant and herder order.
- **Almaty and Astana:** gated housing, private schools and elite districts.
- **Uzbeks and Uyghurs:** senior-junior precedence in families and trading networks.
- **Dungan:** village leadership and the place of the mosque elder, from community sources.
- **Koryo-saram:** brigade leaders and chairmen as a remembered ladder.
- **Labor migrants from Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan:** the bottom of urban service work, to set beside citizens.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and political elites; mixed for daily rank at table and at home; thin for minorities' internal ranks.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for feast custom and estate terms; Russian for Party, Cossack and urban sources; Chagatai and Persian for chronicles of noble lines; Uzbek for southern family precedence.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** zhuz and clan rank is politically delicate; claims of noble birth can stir resentment; domestic workers may be undocumented.
- **Held for review:** one pointer on rank set aside under the category "South Asian-heritage scholars writing on the nation's lands".
