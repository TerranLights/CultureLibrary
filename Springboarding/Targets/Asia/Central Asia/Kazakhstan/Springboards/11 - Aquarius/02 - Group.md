# Kazakhstan: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Chechen for *teip* life in exile, Uyghur for the *meshrep*, Uzbek for southern work crews. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Junior Zhuz lineages of the west:** where *ру* names still sort oil-town hiring talk.
- **Senior Zhuz of Zhetysu and the south:** the densest lineage settlement, to test how strong *ру* bonds remain.
- **Middle Zhuz of the north and center:** long urban and Russian-speaking life; whether lineage still marks "us."
- **Tore and *қожа* families:** groups outside the three *жүз* by descent claim.
- **Urban professionals in Astana and Almaty:** firm, school and alumni groups that may displace kin.
- **Chechens and Ingush of Karaganda and Pavlodar:** *taip* and Sufi brotherhood in exile.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of the southeast:** village sodalities with their own discipline.
- **Ural and Semirechye Cossack lines, with other Russians of the east:** the *stanitsa* host as a remembered fighting unit, and parishes as its successor.
- **Koryo-saram:** kolkhoz brigades remembered as groups of their own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for clan politics and deportation; mixed for fan and school groups; thin for minority sodalities.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *шежіре*, *ұран* and the clan debate; Russian for Soviet, labor and court records; Chechen, Uyghur and Uzbek for group life inside those peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** talk of *жүз* and *ру* in power is touchy; ethnic clashes and labor closures are policed subjects; families may guard their genealogies.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing about groups was held back under the standing rules.
