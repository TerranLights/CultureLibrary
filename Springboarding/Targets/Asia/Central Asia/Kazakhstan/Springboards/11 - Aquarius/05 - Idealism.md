# Kazakhstan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Arabic for al-Farabi, Uyghur for Almaty's Uyghur writers, Russian also for the Soviet model town and the small faith communities. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Steppe heartland and Ulytau:** places named as the cradle of the ideal past, where state ideals stage their rites.
- **Southern cities (Turkistan, Shymkent):** Islamic ideals of the good life beside national ones.
- **Northern towns of Komsomol and Virgin Lands memory:** Soviet idealism as a living memory among Russians, Ukrainians and Kazakhs.
- **Astana and Almaty youth:** start-up and activist ideals; the cynic's counter-voice.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty:** literary ideals of a people carried from the Soviet period.
- **Germans and Mennonite descendants of the north:** religious ideals of plain living, and their emigration.
- **Baptist and other small faith communities:** chosen ideals against the majority's.
- **Village teachers and doctors in Kyzylorda, Zhambyl and the west:** idealism in a calling, by region.
- **Qandas returnees:** an idealized homeland meeting the real one (Kazakhs abroad only as comparison).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and state programs; mixed for surveys; thin for small communities' own records.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for poetry and proverbs; Russian for Soviet memoirs and film; Arabic and Persian for al-Farabi's texts; Uyghur for Samedi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Turanism was once a charge of treason; small faith groups face pressure; state ideals can be risky to mock.
- **Held for review:** one community pointer was set aside under the standing rules, in the category "South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands".
