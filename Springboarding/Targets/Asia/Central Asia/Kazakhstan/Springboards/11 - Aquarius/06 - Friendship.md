# Kazakhstan: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Uzbek for southern friendship circles, Chechen for guest-friendship, Uyghur, Korean and German for those communities' own words. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aul youth of the south and Zhetysu:** *құрдас* teasing and lifelong age-mate circles in villages.
- **Northern mixed towns (Petropavl, Kostanay, Pavlodar):** school and courtyard friendships across Kazakh, Russian and Ukrainian lines.
- **Oil-shift workers of the west:** crew friendships formed on rotation.
- **University friends in Almaty and Astana:** circles from dormitories and the *Болашақ* cohort abroad.
- **Uzbeks of the south:** the organized *gap* circle.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** *kunak* bonds and their limits with outsiders.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** friendships with the families who sheltered them in 1937 and 1941.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** friendship inside close village communities and outside them.
- **Women and men separately:** whose friendships the sources record, and how much of women's circle life reaches print at all.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official "friendship of peoples"; mixed for folk and literary friendship; thin for everyday adult friendship and women's circles.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *құрдас*, *тамыр* and the epics; Russian for travelers' accounts and urban slang; Uzbek and Chechen for those circles; Uyghur and Korean for minority usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official friendship rites can mask friction; favors through friends shade into corruption.
