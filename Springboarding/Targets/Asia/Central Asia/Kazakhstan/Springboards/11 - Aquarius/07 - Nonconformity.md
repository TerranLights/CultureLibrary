## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty:** the old center of rock, art and the late-Soviet *неформал* scene.
- **Astana:** a planned capital where public conduct is closely watched; test its tolerance against Almaty's.
- **Pavlodar, Karaganda and the north:** rap and art collectives in Russian; German and Russian Protestant dissent.
- **Western oil oblasts:** dress-code fights; check how the press portrays neighborhood norms there, and whether locals agree.
- **Southern cities (Shymkent, Turkistan):** religious nonconformity both toward stricter Islam and away from it.
- **Uzbeks and Uyghurs of the south:** community norms on dress and marriage for daughters and sons.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** Orthodox-majority families meeting Protestant and secular children.
- **Koryo-saram and Tatars:** mixed marriages and urban careers as quiet departures from their elders' ways.
- **Young women in every region:** dress, marriage age and the shaming that follows.
