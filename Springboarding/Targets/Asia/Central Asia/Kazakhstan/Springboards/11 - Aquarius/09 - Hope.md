## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aral region and Kyzylorda:** hope tied to the return of water and fish after ecological loss.
- **Semey and the former test-site districts:** hope for health and redress across generations.
- **Western oil towns:** check how residents weigh oil wealth against their own hopes for work.
- **Southern villages and Turkistan region:** hope taken to shrines and spoken in *бата*.
- **Astana:** a capital built as a promise of the future; test whether residents share it.
- **Russians of the east and north:** the *Беловодье* legacy and hopes split between staying and leaving.
- **Germans and Koryo-saram:** hope after deportation, and the later choice to emigrate or stay.
- **Uyghurs, Dungan and Uzbeks:** hopes for children and community in the southeast and south (Uyghurs of East Turkestan only as comparison).
- **Qandas returnees:** the hope of homecoming, and what met it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for youth surveys and state strategies; mixed for folk blessing and divination; thin for minorities' own hopes.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *бата*, lullabies and *құмалақ*; Russian for the Old Believers and surveys; Uzbek, Tatar, Uyghur, Korean and German for minority voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** adolescent suicide data needs care; divination may be frowned on by clergy; emigration talk can be political.
- **Held for review:** none; no hope pointer had to wait under the standing rules.
