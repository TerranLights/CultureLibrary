*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Koryo-mar Korean, Uyghur, Uzbek and German where a minority speaks of feeling with others in its own words. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kazakh-speaking villages of the south and west:** heeding *көңіл* among elders, in-laws and guests.
- **Russian-speaking northern and eastern cities:** a different emotional vocabulary, closer to Russian psychology.
- **Almaty and Astana's young professionals:** therapy words like "boundaries" and "empathy" borrowed through social media.
- **Mixed Kazakh-Russian, Kazakh-Tatar and Kazakh-Korean families:** whose feelings are read in which language.
- **Koryo-saram:** a deported people's memory, and whether it is shared beyond the community.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** feeling for kin across the Chinese border under watch.
- **Germans and Ukrainians who stayed after the 1990s:** a smaller remnant reading a changed majority.
- **Qandas returnees:** newcomers asking to be understood, and the language they use to ask.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for literature and school materials, thin for measured empathy, and the psychometric studies are small and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for the *көңіл* and *жан* idioms; Russian for psychology and press; Korean and Uyghur for minorities' memories of being understood.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Xinjiang testimonies are politically restricted and their speakers at risk; disability and deportation topics need consent from those who speak.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on feeling with others had to be withheld.
