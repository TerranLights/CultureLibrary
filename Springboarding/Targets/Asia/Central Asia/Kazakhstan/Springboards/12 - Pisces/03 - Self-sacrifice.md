## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The south and Turkistan region:** offerings and *Құрбан айт* shared through dense kin and mosque circles.
- **Industrial Karaganda and Zhezkazgan:** miners' and camp memories of lives used up for the state.
- **Western oil oblasts:** shift workers far from families, and what they say they give up.
- **The northern grain belt and East Kazakhstan:** Russians and Ukrainians, with war and Chernobyl memories in Russian.
- **Germans:** the *Trudarmee* generation and those who stayed after the 1990s exodus.
- **Koryo-saram and Chechens:** deportation-era endurance, told as sacrifice by some and as theft of years by others.
- **Uyghurs of Almaty and Zhetysu:** heroes staged for a community with kin across the border.
- **Qandas returnees:** parents who uprooted the family for a homeland, and children asked to repay it.
