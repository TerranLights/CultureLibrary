## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The south (Shymkent, Turkistan, Kyzylorda):** *насыбай*, tea-house leisure and its own norms on drinking in public.
- **The north and east (Pavlodar, Ust-Kamenogorsk, Petropavl):** Soviet drinking habits and the dacha.
- **Western rotation workers (Atyrau, Mangystau):** *вахта* life, with long absences and its own escapes.
- **Almaty and Astana:** clubs, betting shops, cafes and foreign holidays.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** the dacha and the fishing trip as weekend flight.
- **Koryo-saram:** Korean dramas and churches as different exits from routine.
- **Uyghurs and Uzbeks of the south:** *meshrep* and *gap* gatherings that serve as release.
- **Tatars:** urban leisure in a long-settled minority.
- **Qandas returnees:** whose move was itself an escape, and who may dream of moving on.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drink, drugs and tourism; mixed for gambling; thin for daydreaming, reading and quiet withdrawal.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for narcology, police and gaming; Kazakh for *сері* lore and rural talk; Korean and Turkish for the serials' own fan sites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug data are policed and partial; naming runaways or addicts exposes minors and families; emigration counts are politically touchy.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on escape had to be set aside.
