# Kazakhstan: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Uyghur, German (including Plautdietsch for Mennonites) and Korean for minority words of yielding. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The south around Turkistan:** shrine piety where yielding to God is preached most.
- **Mangystau and the Adai:** the 1870 Mangyshlak rising *check* as a local memory of not yielding.
- **The northern Russian-speaking oblasts:** Orthodox *смирение* and the war's POW memories.
- **Karaganda and Pavlodar:** Germans and Mennonites, with *Gelassenheit* and camp memories.
- **Ushtobe and Kyzylorda:** Koryo-saram accounts of 1937.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** whether the 1944 deportation is told as endurance, yielding or refusal, a question to test.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** fate-talk (*teqdir*) in their own language.
- **Tatars:** a reformist Muslim tradition with its own debates on fate.
- **Qandas returnees:** those who chose to leave rather than submit abroad (China and Mongolia only as comparison).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and religious teaching; mixed for end-of-life care; thin for measured acceptance and for how surrender is felt.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for *тәуекел* and mourning words; Russian for POW, hospice and crisis files; German, Korean and Uyghur for minority accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Turkestan Legion and 1731 are contested in Kazakhstan and Russia alike; baby boxes and end-of-life choices touch the vulnerable; euthanasia's legal status belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on yielding was withheld.
