## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern towns and Kyzylorda:** dense healer and seer networks, often near shrines.
- **Herding districts of the center and Mangystau:** expert reading of weather, grass and animals.
- **East Kazakhstan and Bayan-Ölgii-origin returnees:** eagle hunting and its lore (Mongolia only as comparison).
- **The Russian-speaking north:** Slavic fortune customs beside a scientific, Soviet-trained outlook.
- **Almaty and Astana professionals:** "trust your gut" coaching beside data culture.
- **Koryo-saram:** Korean New Year fortune customs, if they survive *check*.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan:** their own omen words and readers.
- **Tatars and Germans:** minority omen lore, largely unrecorded.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for divination lore and herders' knowledge; mixed for professional judgment; thin for measured intuition.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for seers, stones and herding signs; Russian for medicine, hiring and fraud cases; Ukrainian and Korean for minority fortune customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paid healers and seers can be prosecuted, so some avoid interviews; clients may hide visits from clergy and kin.
- **Held for review:** none; the intuition pointers raised nothing under the standing rules.
