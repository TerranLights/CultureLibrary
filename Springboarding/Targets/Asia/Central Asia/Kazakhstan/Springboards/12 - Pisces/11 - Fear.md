# Kazakhstan: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Kazakhstan, region Central Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Kazakh (Cyrillic and 2021 Latin) and Russian; Uyghur, Korean, German and Chechen for minorities' remembered and present fears. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Almaty and Zhetysu:** quake and mudflow fear in the foothills.
- **The northern oblasts:** fears tied to the Russian border and to who belongs.
- **The east around Semey and Kurchatov:** radiation memory and the ruins of military towns.
- **The steppe center and Mangystau:** winter storms, wolves and herd loss.
- **The south (Shymkent, Turkistan):** night taboos, fright cures and fear of God in everyday talk.
- **Russians and Ukrainians:** Slavic fright cures and their own anxieties of staying or leaving.
- **Koryo-saram and Germans:** family memories of deportation and arrest.
- **Uyghurs and Dungan of Zhetysu:** fear for kin across the border and fear after local clashes.
- **Chechens and Ingush:** fears carried from 1944 into the present.
- **Qandas returnees:** worries about acceptance and work in a new homeland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and hazards; mixed for political and ethnic fears; thin for measured anxiety and fear of the dark.
- **Languages needed:** Kazakh for monsters, cures and sermons; Russian for hazards, polls and Slavic customs; Korean, German and Chechen for memories of repression.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fears about China and Russia are politically charged and often self-censored; repression memories involve living families; mental-health records are private.
- **Held for review:** none; no fear pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
