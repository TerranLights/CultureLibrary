*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Laid-off state-factory cohort of Shenyang, Fushun and Anshan against younger private-sector workers:** different lessons about whether speaking up pays.
- **Women and men in errenzhuan pairings:** the female lead's licensed sharp tongue may differ from what women may say offstage.
- **Chaoxianzu:** Korean speech levels give a separate grammar of who may contradict whom.
- **Khorchin and Harqin Mongols:** festival self-display set against everyday deference to elders.
- **Manchu clan members by generation:** genealogy gatherings fix who speaks for the lineage.
- **Rural Heilongjiang reclamation farms (北大荒) against Dalian's service economy:** state-farm discipline versus customer-facing claims.
- **Russian-heritage families of Ergun:** a bilingual household may switch styles of asking and refusing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in performance and media; mixed in surveys (few regional splits); thin for the Tungusic peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (dialect-aware), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu reading, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest and rights-defense (维权) material is censored and may endanger informants. Stereotypes of a "blunt, combative" Northeasterner are claims to test, never data. Workplace and complaint law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
