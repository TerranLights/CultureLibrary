## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families descended from Shandong migrants against old banner families:** one line's history starts with a choice to leave, the other's with a state posting.
- **Former state-enterprise households against early 个体户:** waiting for placement versus starting a stall.
- **Border-town traders of Suifenhe, Heihe and Manzhouli against inland farmers:** trade openings call different people forward.
- **Chaoxianzu villages:** labor-exchange customs decide who starts a work party.
- **Dalian's foreign-invested port economy against Qiqihar and Jixi:** contrasting chances to start.
- **Hezhe, Oroqen and Hulunbuir Mongol households:** initiative where heritage itself becomes the business.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration history and reform-era economics; mixed for present entrepreneurship by region; thin for the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Russian (border trade), Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** catchphrases such as 等靠要 and "investment does not cross Shanhai Pass" (投资不过山海关) carry a regional stereotype; treat them as claims to test. Business regulation goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
