*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kang-heated village households against centrally heated city flats:** what each family must still supply for itself in winter.
- **Laid-off urban families on relief against those who never held state jobs:** different shame or ease in asking for help.
- **Xiajia-type villages in the Harbin plain:** fast change in when sons and daughters set up their own homes.
- **Chaoxianzu villages living on remittances:** self-reliance redefined as a migrant's duty.
- **Shenehen Buryats and Hulunbuir herders:** self-provisioning on pasture, against Khorchin farming villages.
- **Hezhe fishing families and descendants of the Trekhrechye Cossacks:** small communities whose old self-sufficiency broke.
- **Elders who winter in Hainan:** comparison only; a choice of independence in old age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for village ethnography and welfare studies; mixed for living-arrangement data by province; thin for Hezhe and Buryat households.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Russian, Manchu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** separatism, the East Mongolian autonomous movement of 1946 and Manchukuo are politically sensitive; welfare recipients are a stigmatized group to approach with care. Welfare rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
