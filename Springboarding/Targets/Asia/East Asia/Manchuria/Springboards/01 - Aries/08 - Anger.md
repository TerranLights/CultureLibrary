# Manchuria: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin; Korean; Manchu (lexical sources); Mongolian; Russian. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women across generations:** who may shout, who must hold it in, and what happens to held-in anger.
- **Laid-off and privatized-plant workers of Tonghua, Liaoyang and Fushun:** grievance aimed at managers and the state.
- **Chaoxianzu:** a Korean vocabulary of suppressed anger and illness.
- **Households that consult spirit mediums:** anger explained as affliction rather than character.
- **Sports crowds in Shenyang, Dalian and Yanji:** sanctioned public anger and its limits.
- **Oroqen and Evenki elders:** memory of clan feuds and how they were ended.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in language and media; mixed for health and survey data; thin for minority-specific emotion research.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (dialect-aware), Korean, Manchu and Mongolian for lexical work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor violence and protest are censored topics; domestic violence needs ethical care with informants. A "hot-tempered Northeasterner" image circulates in media and must be tested, never assumed. Grievance and justice as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
