## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Winter-sport cities (Qiqihar, Harbin, Jilin) against Dalian's sea-port sport culture:** different sports set the local idea of winning.
- **Daur villages of Morin Dawa:** a small people's game turned into a route to national rank.
- **Chaoxianzu families:** school competition and festival contests side by side.
- **Hulunbuir herders:** rivalry through horses, not persons.
- **Plant workers raised in emulation campaigns against younger private-sector staff:** state-organized against market rivalry.
- **Revived Manchu sport clubs:** a contest used to show a heritage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and exams; mixed for envy and status rivalry; thin for Tungusic traditional contests.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu reading.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam cheating and sports doping cases may be sensitive; claims of a group's "competitiveness" must come from data, never reputation.
- **Held for review:** none.
