# Manchuria: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin; Korean; Manchu (tale texts); Mongolian; Russian (the Amur gold fields). **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young urban men against rural men of the same age:** cars, drink and nights out against field and forest risks.
- **Border-trade and smuggling towns on the Amur, Ussuri and Tumen:** money-making risk where the law is thin.
- **Changchun households of the 1980s:** a speculative fever among ordinary savers.
- **Chaoxianzu families of the migration era:** high-stakes bets on work abroad.
- **Hunting families of the Oroqen and Evenki:** rashness as a breach of forest rules.
- **Gold-panning settlements of the Mohe area, past and present:** the frontier gamble.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health and accident statistics; mixed for history (bandit sources are partisan); thin for minority risk-taking.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (dialect-aware), Korean, Russian, Manchu reading, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol harms among small Indigenous peoples are a stigmatizing subject; handle with their own organizations' framing. The "heavy-drinking, rash Northeasterner" image is a stereotype to test, not data. Criminal law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
