# Manchuria: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the post-war nation Manchuria, East Asia, whose lands were part of pre-war China. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with northern colloquial), Manchu, Korean, Mongolian (Daur and Khorchin contexts), Russian. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Han families on the Songnen plain:** sons and daughters-in-law contesting the parents' house and plot.
- **Rust-belt families moved by redevelopment:** one flat, several heirs.
- **Manchu clans:** whether genealogies and shrine objects are kept from other branches of the clan.
- **Daur villages near Qiqihar:** what kin are expected to share and what may be held back.
- **Hezhe and Oroqen:** fishing places and hunting grounds and whether they are shared or guarded.
- **Yanbian Korean couples apart for years:** jealousy and trust at a distance.
- **Gorlos and Dörbet Mongol herders:** pasture boundaries after contracting.
- **Urban young couples in Harbin, Shenyang, Changchun and Dalian:** phone-checking and location-sharing as new forms of claim.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good on family property disputes and on spousal control surveys at national level; thin for Indigenous peoples and for regional jealousy studies.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu for older word senses; Korean; Mongolian; Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence data may be partial; the history dispute with Korea over Goguryeo is politically charged on both sides; family quarrels in the news involve private people, so use only what is public.
- **Held for review:** none.
