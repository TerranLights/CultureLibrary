# Manchuria: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** post-war Manchuria, an East Asian nation formed from lands in pre-war China. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with Northeastern comfort and food words), Korean, Manchu, Mongolian, Russian (Harbin food and bath heritage); Nanai (Hezhe) food terms where recorded. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Harbin households with Russian-era tastes against Shenyang and Changchun:** two city food histories to keep apart.
- **Yanbian Koreans:** heat, spice and fermentation in a separate cuisine; bath and sauna customs to compare.
- **Gorlos and Dörbet Mongols:** dairy, mutton and the lake fishing rites.
- **Hezhe (Nanai) on the Amur and Ussuri:** raw and roasted fish dishes and fish-skin textures as their own sensory world.
- **Manchu families who keep household rites:** sacred foods, incense and drums.
- **Hui communities:** halal cooking and the pleasures their dietary rules allow.
- **Coastal Dalian:** seafood and sea air against the interior's winter stores.
- **Rural villages versus city bathhouse culture:** comfort at home on the kang against comfort bought outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, festivals and drink; good in travel media and folklore; mixed for touch and personal-space norms.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian; Russian; Manchu for ritual vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Manchu shamanic rites are family-held and filmed only by permission; dog meat in some Korean-Chinese dishes is contested; bathhouses are sometimes linked in the press to sex work, which is Scorpio's concern and should not color this concept's research.
