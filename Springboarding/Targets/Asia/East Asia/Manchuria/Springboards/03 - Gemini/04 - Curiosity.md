# Manchuria: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia; scouted as its own culture-nation, outside the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Japanese for early explorers and ethnographers. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Harbin townspeople against the tourists they host:** curiosity and the questions people ask may differ when one side is the spectacle.
- **Mongol herding households:** greetings that ask for news of the road may make asking a courtesy rather than prying; to check.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhen:** rules on naming the dead and on sharing hunting or ritual knowledge may close some questions to outsiders.
- **Manchu clans keeping genealogies (家谱) and ritual books:** what outsiders may look at and what stays within the clan.
- **Yanbian Korean villages:** visitors' questions in Korean and in Mandarin, and which ones are thought rude in each.
- **City children of the science-museum generation against farm children:** where questions get answers, and who gives them.
- **Diasporas, for comparison only:** heritage tourists from Japan and Korea visiting old family sites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good for museums, tourism and media; thin for everyday norms of asking, which need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for Qing exploration records; Japanese for Mamiya and Torii.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genetic research on small peoples; colonial anthropology; clan ritual books; ghost and spirit beliefs studied as superstition in some literature.
- **Held for review:** none.
