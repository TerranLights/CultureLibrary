# Manchuria: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia; a separate culture-nation, not a member of the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu, Russian. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of Shandong settlers against those of Hebei and Henan settlers:** home-province ties may still decide whom people trust first.
- **Manchu clans:** clan and banner memory as a network beyond the village.
- **Yanbian Korean households:** ties stretched across a national border by work abroad, set beside ties at home.
- **Horqin and Hulunbuir Mongols:** distance between herding camps and the role of kin in seasonal moves.
- **Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhen after settlement:** how resettled hunters and fishers kept or lost ties to camps and rivers, as they tell it.
- **State-enterprise workers and their children:** workplace ties that once carried housing, schooling and marriage.
- **Hui communities:** mosque networks linking families across towns.
- **Rural elders whose children have left:** contact by phone and visit at the New Year, and isolation in between.
- **Diasporas, for comparison only:** Korean Chinese in Seoul; Northeasterners in the Sinian coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for guanxi scholarship (often from other regions, to be checked against Northeastern cases); mixed for loneliness data; thin for Tungusic peoples' ties after settlement.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for clan records; Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** connection shades into corruption (cases belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here); clan genealogies are family property; family separation across borders.
- **Held for review:** none.
