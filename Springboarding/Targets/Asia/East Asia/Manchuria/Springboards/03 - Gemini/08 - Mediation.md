# Manchuria: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia; its own culture-nation, not a part of the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Manchu, Korean, Mongolian; Latin and Russian for frontier diplomacy. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu clans:** lineage heads as insiders who keep quarrels inside the clan.
- **Hui communities:** religious authority as the first third party, to set beside secular mediators.
- **Mongol herding areas:** elders and monasteries in grazing disputes across long distances.
- **Oroqen and Evenki:** clan councils rooted in hunting life, and what replaced them after settlement.
- **Yanbian Korean families:** commercial marriage brokers and church elders as two kinds of go-between.
- **City neighborhoods of the old work units:** committee mediators who know every family, against newer gated estates.
- **Farm villages:** the respected neighbor or matchmaker called in, and who counts as respected.
- **Diasporas, for comparison only:** Korean Chinese brides in South Korea, and the brokers they deal with from there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing and Republican-era material and for Korean-language broker studies; mixed for everyday mediation; thin for Tungusic clan councils today.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin (classical Chinese for Qing rites); Manchu; Korean; Mongolian; Latin and Russian for the Nerchinsk talks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state mediation committees and petitions are legal and political matters for Libra and Capricorn, not this sheet; spirit-medium practice is sometimes suppressed; brokered marriages can involve exploitation; clan affairs are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
