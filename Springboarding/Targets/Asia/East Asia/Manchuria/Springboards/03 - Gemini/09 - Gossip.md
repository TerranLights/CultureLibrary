# Manchuria: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia; a culture-nation scouted on its own, outside the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu, Russian; Japanese for occupation-era rumor files. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns against Shenyang, Dalian and Harbin:** whether city anonymity weakens the sanction of being pointed at.
- **Forestry and mining company towns:** one workplace, one housing estate, so talk at work and at home overlap.
- **Korean Chinese villages emptied by migration:** who talks about whom when many working-age people are abroad.
- **Mongol herding areas:** distance between households and the role of gatherings (fairs, naadam) in passing news of people.
- **Manchu clans:** what is said inside the clan and what is kept from outsiders.
- **Hui neighborhoods:** a close community with its own mosque, where reputation may bear on marriage and trade.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhen settlements:** very small communities where everyone is related; how talk works there, as members describe it.
- **State farms that took in sent-down youth:** newcomers as the object of local talk, and local people as theirs.
- **Diasporas, for comparison only:** Harbin Russian émigrés' memoirs of the city's talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good for media, forums and village sociology; thin for minority-language gossip and its genres.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for Qing dialogues; Russian; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rumor is policed online, and "spreading rumors" can bring penalties (a Libra matter); occupation-era files; gendered words for gossip need care, as the claim that women gossip more is a stereotype to test.
- **Held for review:** none.
