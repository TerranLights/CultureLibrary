# Manchuria: Domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and its Northeastern Mandarin varieties (with Jiao-Liao Mandarin on the Liaodong coast); Korean (Yanbian); Mongolian (Horqin and other eastern Mongol varieties, traditional script); Manchu and Xibe (written heritage, revival); Daur, Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhe (Nanai); Russian and Japanese for older sources. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what is home, and who is family (household forms, building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han Northeasterners by migrant origin:** Shandong-descended families of Liaodong and the Songhua plain against Hebei-descended families of western Liaoning; different routes, dialects and family memories.
- **Rust-belt worker families** (Shenyang's Tiexi, Fushun, Anshan): homes set by work-unit housing.
- **State-farm and oil-field families** (the Great Northern Wilderness farms, Daqing): households formed by settlement programs.
- **Manchu families** of banner-descended villages and the Manchu autonomous counties: clan rites in the house.
- **Korean-Chinese** of Yanbian and scattered Heilongjiang villages: a different house plan and kin vocabulary.
- **Mongols** of Horqin, Fuxin and Dorbod: herding-to-farming change in the dwelling.
- **Xibe, Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhe:** each with its own kin and dwelling terms.
- **Hui, the Russian-descended villages of the Ergun area, the Fuyu Kirgiz:** small, with distinct households.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Korean-Chinese in South Korea, Northeasterners in Beijing and Hainan, the Xibe of Qapqal in Xinjiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han and Manchu households (census, registers, gazetteers, ethnography); mixed for Korean-Chinese and Mongols; thin and scattered for the small hunting and fishing peoples.
- **Lands:** Manchuria's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker lists only China as the pre-war land); this sign's sheets are written for the core Northeast.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese first; Korean, Mongolian, Japanese; Manchu for the rite texts and genealogies; Russian for Harbin and border villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan rites and shamanic household practice belong to their families; ask before using. Manchukuo-era sources carry colonial framing. Household registration rules belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
