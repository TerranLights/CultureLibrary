# Manchuria: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Northeastern Mandarin kin vocabulary), Manchu (genealogies, kin terms), Korean, Mongolian, Xibe; Daur, Evenki and Oroqen clan terms via Chinese ethnography. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shandong- and Hebei-descended lineages** with written genealogies against families whose papers were lost or never kept.
- **Banner-descended families** (Manchu, Mongol and Han-martial banners): ancestry recorded by the banner, then hidden or reclaimed.
- **Korean-Chinese:** peninsular lineage books and different kin terms.
- **Mongols of Horqin and Fuxin:** clan names among farming Mongols.
- **Daur, Oroqen, Evenki, Hezhe:** clan and lineage groups with their own names.
- **Xibe of Shenyang and Fuyu:** a cord as the family record.
- **Hui:** marriage within the community and mosque-centered kin.
- **Urban single-child families of the planned-birth era:** few siblings, cousins as near-siblings.
- **State-farm families formed by sent-down youth:** kin far away, neighbors as kin.
- **Mixed Russian-Chinese families near Ergun.**
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Korean-Chinese families split between Yanbian and South Korea; Xibe of Qapqal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han and Manchu genealogies and urban families; mixed for Korean-Chinese and Mongols; thin and outsider-written for the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Manchu (classical reading); Korean; Mongolian; some Japanese for colonial surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan records and rites belong to the families who keep them; genealogies may not be shown to outsiders. Banner ancestry and ethnic re-registration can be personal. Marriage law goes to Libra and Capricorn; siblings to Gemini.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Search 《中国家谱总目》 for Manchu clans with a Liaoning or Jilin place of compilation.

4. Search Korean-language sources for 조선족 족보 to locate lineage books.
5. Read the migration-strategy chapters of *Swallows and Settlers*.
