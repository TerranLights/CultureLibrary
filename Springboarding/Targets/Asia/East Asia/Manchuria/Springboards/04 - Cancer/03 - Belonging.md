# Manchuria: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu; Russian (Harbin); Japanese (Manchukuo-era official material); Hezhe, Evenki and Oroqen through their own performers and Chinese-language studies. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banner-descended Manchus against Manchus who re-registered later:** inherited against chosen membership.
- **Descendants of North China migrants:** belonging to the Northeast and to an ancestral county at once.
- **Korean-Chinese:** pulled between Yanbian, the state and South Korea.
- **Horqin, Fuxin and Dorbod Mongols:** belonging across provincial lines.
- **Daur:** their separate classification from Mongols is a question in itself.
- **Hezhe, Oroqen, Evenki:** very small peoples where recognition and performance carry belonging.
- **Xibe of Shenyang:** a home community with most of its kin far west.
- **Rust-belt worker families:** belonging to a work unit that closed.
- **Harbin old-city residents and border-town traders:** cities shaped by Russians and others.
- **Hui:** belonging through the mosque community.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Harbin Russians abroad; Korean-Chinese in Seoul.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Manchu and Korean-Chinese identity scholarship; mixed for regional Northeastern identity (much of it online and in popular culture); thin for Daur, Hezhe and Oroqen self-accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Japanese, Russian; Manchu for Qing sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic identity, minority-language schooling and Korean-Chinese ties abroad are politically sensitive; protect informants. Membership by registration touches Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Group and community sides of belonging overlap with Aquarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
