# Manchuria: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with Northeastern child-rearing words); Korean (Yanbian schools and families); Mongolian; Manchu (Qing instruction texts); Hezhe, Oroqen and Evenki practice through ethnography. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grandparent-raised children of migrating parents:** nurture shifts to the older generation.
- **Urban single-child households:** concentrated attention, studied apart from farm families.
- **Korean-Chinese families:** schooling in Korean and long parental absence abroad.
- **Manchu villages** where the hanging cradle and child rites were kept.
- **Mongol herding-farming families:** children learning animals.
- **Oroqen and Evenki:** hunting and reindeer skills passed down beside state schooling.
- **Hezhe fishing families:** river skills taught to children.
- **Factory districts:** the master-apprentice line as a second upbringing.
- **Farm and ginseng-growing families:** tending crops and roots over years.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Northeastern parents raising children in Beijing or abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban families and surveys; mixed for Korean-Chinese and Mongols; thin and outsider-written for Hezhe, Oroqen and Evenki.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for Qing texts; Russian for some Tungus ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-protection cases and minority schooling are sensitive; protect children's identities. Discipline belongs to Capricorn; learning to Gemini and Sagittarius; play to Leo.
