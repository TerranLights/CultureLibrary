# Manchuria: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (including folk-religion vocabulary); Manchu (rite and tale manuscripts); Korean; Mongolian; Oroqen and Evenki spirit names through Chinese-language studies. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households that keep 保家仙 against those that do not:** a spirit guard beside locks and police.
- **Manchu families keeping child-protection rites.**
- **Korean-Chinese:** talismans, church groups and families split by migration.
- **Oroqen and Evenki:** guns, forest spirits and the hunting ban.
- **Hezhe and other river villages:** flood and border.
- **Forest towns of the Greater Hinggan:** fire as the defining danger.
- **Rust-belt cities:** gangs, layoffs and protection rackets.
- **Border cities** (Heihe, Hunchun, Dandong): guarding a frontier.
- **Mongol herding villages:** wolves, snow disasters and livestock.
- **Families of missing children:** protection after its failure.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Northeastern migrants protecting kin by remittances from afar.
