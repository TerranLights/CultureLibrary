# Manchuria: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu; Russian and English-language sources for Harbin's refugee past; Japanese for the 1945-1946 camps. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of post-station households** and of migrant shack-dwellers: shelter as family origin.
- **Harbin:** a city with layers of refugees; compare old residents with new arrivals.
- **Korean-Chinese border villages** of Yanbian: proximity to people seeking refuge.
- **Mongol herding families:** shelter for animals and people in winter disasters.
- **Hezhe and river villages:** flood evacuation.
- **Rust-belt cities:** people living rough after layoffs, and the relief stations.
- **Forest and border towns:** remote shelter on long winter roads.
- **Buddhist, Christian and Muslim communities:** religious duties to lodge strangers.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Harbin Russian and Jewish families abroad; Japanese families of former settlers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Harbin's refugee past and official relief; mixed for disaster shelter; thin for violence shelters and people living rough, where official figures may mislead.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Russian; Japanese; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for Qing station records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** North Korean refugees and those who help them face serious risk; never record identifying details. Settler-era camp memoirs carry colonial framing. Asylum and eviction rules belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
