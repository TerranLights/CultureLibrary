# Manchuria: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese; Manchu (filial texts); Korean; Mongolian (healing traditions); Daur through translated ethnography; English for missionary and epidemic records. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rust-belt retirees:** care once tied to the work unit, and children who may live elsewhere.
- **Rural elders whose children migrated:** care at a distance and neighbors stepping in.
- **Korean-Chinese elders:** village associations and children working abroad.
- **Mongol families of Horqin:** a named healing tradition beside state clinics.
- **Daur, Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhe:** shaman-healers and elders, and the move to hospitals.
- **Manchu families:** filial teaching in Qing texts against present practice.
- **Pilot cities against other cities:** paid long-term care against family care alone.
- **Hui:** mosque and community help for the sick and the old.
- **Christian congregations:** church visiting of the sick, going back to mission hospitals.
- **Men who care** for a wife or parent: a group the data may hide.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Korean-Chinese women working as hospital carers in South Korea; Northeastern women working as home carers in other Chinese cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elder-care policy and surveys; mixed for folk healing; thin for small peoples' care practice outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Mongolian; Korean; Manchu for the Qing texts; English for missionary and epidemic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing ceremonies and shaman practice belong to their communities; care-home abuse cases are sensitive; protect carers' privacy. Health and service work leans to Virgo; duty to Capricorn; compassion to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
