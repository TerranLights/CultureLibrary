# Manchuria: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese; Japanese (war orphans and settlers); Korean (families split by work abroad); Manchu and Xibe (pre-modern departures, folktales); Mongolian (orphan tales). **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Korean-Chinese families of Yanbian:** long parental absence abroad.
- **Rural Han villages of migrant parents:** children and elders left with each other.
- **Laid-off worker households of the rust belt:** being dropped by the work unit.
- **Coal and timber towns** (Fuxin, Hegang, Yichun and others): whole places declared spent.
- **Families tied to the Japanese war orphans:** adoptive kin, orphans who left, those who stayed.
- **Xibe of Shenyang:** a community defined by kin who left long ago.
- **Manchu and Mongol storytelling communities:** the orphan in folktale.
- **Hezhe, Oroqen and Evenki:** villages thinned by moves to towns; elders left on old ground.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** children from Northeastern orphanages adopted abroad; Japanese orphans resettled in Japan; Xibe of Qapqal.

## 4. Flags
