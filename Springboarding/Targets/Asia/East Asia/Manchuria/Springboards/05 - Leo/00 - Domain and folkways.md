# Manchuria: The domain: creativity and self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia (a culture-nation of its own, separate from the Sinian Federation). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Northeastern Mandarin (东北话); Korean; Mongolian; Manchu; Daur, Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhen terms where they exist; Russian for Harbin's émigré past. **Concept in one line:** how people shine, play and take pleasure: creativity, play, pleasure, romance, children as joy, celebration, fame, gambling and sport.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han Northeasterners:** the Liaodong coast and Dalian (Shandong roots), the Shenyang–Anshan–Fushun industrial belt, central Jilin's farm plain, Heilongjiang's frontier and state farms (北大荒).
- **Manchus:** urban bannermen's descendants against rural autonomous counties (Xinbin, Kuandian) and Sanjiazi (三家子), a village where Manchu is still spoken.
- **Koreans:** Yanbian against scattered Heilongjiang villages and city neighborhoods.
- **Mongols:** Khorchin, Barga and Buryat of Hulunbuir, Dörbet of Heilongjiang.
- **Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhen; Xibe of Shenyang; Hui; Russian-descended families of Ergun:** each presents itself differently.
- **State-enterprise workers against farmers and private traders.**
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Northeastern winter residents in Hainan; Korean-Chinese in South Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for errenzhuan, festivals and sport; mixed for measured values; thin and mediated for the smallest peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with dialect), Korean, Mongolian (traditional script), Manchu, Russian, Japanese.
- **Scope:** Manchuria's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign is written for the core lands of the historic Northeast, with the Hulunbuir and Khorchin steppe where a pointer reaches it.
- **Sensitive:** 满洲 evokes Manchukuo; Chinese sources say 东北. Read Japanese colonial surveys critically; shaman rites may be clan-restricted; Korean heritage listings have drawn cross-border disputes. National comedy fixed a loud, joking "Northeasterner" image: study it, never adopt it.
- **Held for review:** none.
