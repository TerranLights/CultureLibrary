# Manchuria: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (standard and Northeastern), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhen craft vocabularies through Chinese-language heritage records. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu paper-cutters and embroiderers in Liaoning's Manchu counties** against urban artists who use Manchu motifs: inheritance against reinvention.
- **Hezhen, Oroqen and Evenki makers,** whose crafts moved from use to sale; separate each people.
- **Korean writers and artists of Yanbian,** working in Korean inside a Chinese-language market.
- **Mongol craftspeople in Hulunbuir and Khorchin** (felt, silver, leather), whose forms differ from both.
- **Rust-belt workers** in Tiexi, Anshan and Fushun, where improvising came from scarcity.
- **Port and border cities** (Dalian, Harbin, Dandong), open to Russian, Japanese and Korean influence.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Harbin émigré artists and musicians abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage crafts and film history; mixed for measures; thin for everyday tinkering, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu, Japanese for the colonial film era.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heritage listing fixes one "authentic" form and names official inheritors (传承人), which can silence other makers; tourism sells minority designs with little return to their owners; the Manchukuo studio past is politically charged; Hongshan finds feed origin narratives and must be read critically.
- **Held for review:** none.
