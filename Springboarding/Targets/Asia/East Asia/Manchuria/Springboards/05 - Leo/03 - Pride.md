# Manchuria: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (standard and Northeastern), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu, Hezhen through Chinese transcription. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

English keywords: industrial pride Northeast China; rust belt identity; regional discrimination against Northeasterners; Manchu identity after 1911; Chaoxianzu collective pride; Yanbian football; Hezhen song; Harbin city image.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-industry workers and their children,** whose pride in work met mass layoffs.
- **Beidahuang state-farm families and sent-down youth,** with pride in reclaimed land.
- **Manchus,** from concealment after 1911 to present revival; urban and rural differ.
- **Koreans of Yanbian,** check whether schooling, sport or language anchors group pride, and for whom.
- **Mongols of Khorchin and Hulunbuir,** whose pride may attach to herding and ancestry.
- **Hezhen, Oroqen, Evenki, Daur:** small peoples whose public image is often made by others.
- **City rivalries:** Shenyang against Dalian, Harbin against Changchun, *check*.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Northeastern migrants facing regional prejudice in southern cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state-made models of pride and for city identity; mixed for ordinary people's own accounts; thin for the small peoples' views.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** model-worker and model-soldier cults are state propaganda and must be read as such; Beidahuang pride sits on the displacement of earlier peoples; regional-prejudice debates are heated online; Manchu ethnic pride can be read politically.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

4. Search 乌苏里船歌 著作权 案 for the authorship dispute.
5. Read Rhoads's chapters on the Republic years and note named Manchu figures to follow.
