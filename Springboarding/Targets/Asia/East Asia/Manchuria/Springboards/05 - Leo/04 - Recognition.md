# Manchuria: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Japanese and Russian for communities whose recognition is in question. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Peoples whose standing depends on classification:** Manchus, Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhen, Xibe, Russians of Ergun, and the very small Kyrgyz group of Fuyu, *check*.
- **Koreans,** whose resistance fighters and elders are honored in their own ways.
- **Mongols,** with their own titles for wrestlers and singers.
- **Industrial workers:** model workers against the unnamed majority; laid-off workers whose service went unacknowledged.
- **Women,** whose credit in war, farm and factory may differ from men's.
- **Families tied to the Japanese era:** foster parents of Japanese orphans; former Manchukuo employees.
- **Harbin's vanished Jewish and Russian communities,** and how present residents speak of them.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** returned Japanese orphans in Japan; Harbin Jews in Israel.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors and memorials; mixed for everyday credit at work; thin for how small peoples judge the state's recognition of them.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, Manchu, Japanese, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory is politically controlled; the Fangzheng dispute and Korean resistance figures linked to North Korea are delicate; ethnic classification is a state matter, not a people's own judgment; model-worker lists are propaganda as well as data.
- **Held for review:** none.
