# Manchuria: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern; Jiaoliao on the Liaodong coast), Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu, Mongolian (traditional script), Japanese and Russian for pre-1945 sources; Hezhe, Oroqen, Evenki, Daur and Xibe through linguists' work. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service, with daily routine and health: what is good work and a well-kept life?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han of migrant descent:** Shandong-origin against Hebei- and Henan-origin families; the Jiaoliao-speaking Liaodong coast against the interior; home-region work customs may have traveled.
- **Industrial towns by sector:** steel (Anshan, Benxi), machinery (Tiexi, Changchun), oil (Daqing), coal (Fushun, Fuxin, Hegang, Jixi), forestry (Yichun, Greater Khingan); each work unit declined or survived differently.
- **State farms:** Beidahuang reclamation farms and former sent-down youth, distinct from villages.
- **Manchu communities:** autonomous counties (Xinbin, Kuandian, Huanren, Yitong among them), Sanjiazi village, Ula.
- **Chaoxianzu:** Yanbian, Changbai, Shenyang's Xita, Heilongjiang rice villages; paddy farming and later labor migration.
- **Mongols:** Dörbet, Qian Gorlos, Fuxin and Harqin Left Wing communities; herding and farming mixes.
- **Hezhe, Oroqen, Evenki, Daur:** fishing, hunting and reindeer or horse economies, each with its own seasonal round.
- **Xibe, Hui, the Kirgiz of Fuyu, Russian-heritage Harbin:** small groups with their own trades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for industrial work and health statistics; mixed for daily routine (all-country surveys may not break out these provinces); thin for the Tungusic peoples outside folklore.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists the pre-war lands only as "China" and no scope file exists yet, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the core lands of the former Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang, and whether eastern Inner Mongolia's leagues belong is open.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Japanese (Mantetsu, Manchukuo); Russian (Harbin); Manchu (Qing archives); Korean (Chaoxianzu press).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor under Manchukuo; Cultural Revolution episodes; layoff protests; contested Goguryeo and Balhae heritage; shamanic practitioners' knowledge, only as they share it.
- **Held for review:** none.
