# Manchuria: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern and Jiaoliao speech), Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu, Mongolian; Japanese and Russian for the railway and colonial decades. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oilfield and coal-town families** (Daqing, Liaohe; Fushun, Hegang, Jixi, Qitaihe): campaign-era model labor against later depletion and closure.
- **Forestry-bureau towns** (Yichun, Greater Khingan): loggers whose trade ended with the logging stop, set beside miners for contrast.
- **Former state-farm workers and their children:** a planned agrarian workforce.
- **Han villages** of the Songnen and Liao plains: smallholder grain farming and its slack season.
- **Chaoxianzu villages:** paddy farming, then heavy outmigration of working-age adults (overseas work as comparison only).
- **Hezhe fishers and Oroqen former hunters:** two subsistence trades narrowed by state policy, each in its own way.
- **Border-trade towns** (Suifenhe, Heihe, Hunchun, Dandong): shuttle traders and market work across borders.
- **Dalian and the coast:** port work and foreign-invested factories; a separate labor market.
- **Graduates choosing the civil-service exams (考公) against private firms:** generational divergence in what counts as a good job.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state industry, model workers and statistics; mixed for informal and household work; thin for Tungusic peoples' own words for work.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Japanese (colonial labor bureaus); Russian (railway); Manchu for 17th-century records; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colonial forced labor and prisoner labor; Cultural Revolution labor reform; layoff protests (legal aspects go to Libra and Capricorn); the Oroqen hunting ban, told by the Oroqen themselves.
- **Held for review:** none.
