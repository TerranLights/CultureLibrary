# Manchuria: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu (for the banner and court record), Mongolian; Japanese for Manchukuo mobilization. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or a god), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fushun and Anshan civic life:** two cities that host living service models; compare with cities that do not.
- **Military and veteran families,** many around former Shenyang Military Region bases.
- **Tourism workers by season:** Harbin's winter festival, Dalian's summer coast, Changbai Mountain resorts; whose service is seasonal and migrant.
- **Village households** trading help at weddings, funerals and pig-killing feasts.
- **Manchu families with banner or bondservant memory,** as they tell it.
- **Chaoxianzu villages,** where elders now serve elders.
- **Hui mosque communities** in Shenyang and Harbin: religious charity and service.
- **Mongol and Tungusic families** with their own hosting and sharing rules.
- **Women in paid domestic service,** local and migrant (work elsewhere as comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state models and campaigns; mixed for paid service work; thin for small peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu and classical Chinese for Qing material; Japanese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory (Korean War, Manchukuo); hereditary servitude; volunteer statistics as official claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
