# Manchuria: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with Northeastern colloquial forms), Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu, Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Laid-off men and women of the 1990s,** now pensioners: whether the loss of a post is remembered differently by gender.
- **Retirees in large cities** (senior universities, square-dance teams, volunteer patrols) against rural elders still farming.
- **Chaoxianzu grandparents** raising grandchildren in villages emptied by labor migration.
- **Oroqen and Hezhe craftspeople,** whose useful objects became heritage goods.
- **Horqin, Dörbet and Gorlos Mongols** for land-use history.
- **Disabled workers** in welfare factories (福利厂) against the open labor market.
- **Rural households** that keep everything for reuse, against new urban consumers.
- **Exile and frontier descendants** in Ningguta's region (present-day Ning'an), for local memory of exiles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; elder surveys and fiction are rich, while direct words for usefulness need careful collection.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Qing accounts; Korean; Manchu and Mongolian for terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "useless" as a label on elders and disabled people; land called "waste" when taken from herders.
- **Held for review:** none.
