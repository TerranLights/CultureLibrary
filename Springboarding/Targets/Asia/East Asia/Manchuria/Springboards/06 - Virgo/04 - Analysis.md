## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Laboratory and engineering towns** (Dalian's science park, Changchun's institutes, Harbin's universities): professional analysts.
- **State-enterprise accountants and planners** whose reckoning skills were built for the planned economy.
- **Village elders who kept collective accounts,** against younger farmers using apps.
- **Korean-medium school graduates** for bilingual analytic vocabulary.
- **Hunters and fishers** (Oroqen, Evenki, Hezhe) for field inference outside schooling.
- **Mongol herders** in Dörbet and Gorlos areas for their own reading of land and weather.
- **Manchu scholars and heritage learners** rebuilding the language's technical words.
- **Dalian's business community,** long tied to Japanese firms, for workplace analysis styles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; science institutions and exams are well documented; folk reasoning and hunters' knowledge are thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Japanese for Mantetsu; Korean; Mongolian and Tungusic terms via linguists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputes over official statistics; colonial research made for control; hunters' knowledge shared only with consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
