# Manchuria: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern colloquial especially), Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu, Mongolian, and the Daur, Hezhe and Oroqen terms that appear in folklore collections. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ginseng, jade and fur traders** for professional appraisal, and their customers.
- **Forest-edge villagers who gather mushrooms and herbs (采山)** against town buyers who rely on markets.
- **Older city residents targeted by health-product sellers** and their adult children who intervene.
- **Mongol herders** judging livestock, against Han farmers judging seed and soil.
- **Daur, Hezhe and Oroqen storytellers** for their own models of the wise person.
- **Chaoxianzu families** with members recruited for overseas work (abroad as comparison only).
- **Harbin's and Dalian's collectors** of foreign-era objects.
- **Young urban internet users** against older rural residents for rumor and news judgment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; market and consumer records are plentiful, folk models of judgment sit in folklore volumes, and personal taste is thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Manchu and Mongolian for the mergen family of words; Tungusic forms through folklorists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fraud victims' shame; official rumor control can shade into censorship; regional comedy can harden into stereotype, so name variation.
- **Held for review:** none.
