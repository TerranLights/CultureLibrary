# Manchuria: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Chaoxianzu Korean, Manchu, Mongolian; Hezhe and Oroqen craft terms via heritage listings. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aerospace, automotive and machine-tool workers** in Shenyang, Changchun and Harbin, held to tolerances measured in microns.
- **Laid-off former top-grade workers** whose exacting skills lost their workplace.
- **Ice sculptors and winter athletes,** for performance judged against a perfect mark.
- **Hezhe, Oroqen and Manchu craftspeople,** each with distinct material standards.
- **Chaoxianzu performers and families,** for performance and household standards.
- **High-school students in elite urban schools** against rural county schools.
- **Small-business owners in Dalian** supplying export buyers with strict quality specifications.
- **Older and younger generations** for "make do" against "it must be right".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on industrial quality and model workers; mixed on psychology; thin on household standards.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; classical Chinese for Qing standards; craft terms in Hezhe and Manchu via heritage files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and mental illness, treated with care; military-industrial sites restrict access; makers' techniques shared only as they choose.
- **Held for review:** none.
