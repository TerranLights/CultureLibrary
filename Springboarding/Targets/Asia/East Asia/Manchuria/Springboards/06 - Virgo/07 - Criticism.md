# Manchuria: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern colloquial included), Chaoxianzu Korean, Mongolian, Manchu. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Generations who lived through the Cultural Revolution** against those raised after it, on public and self-criticism.
- **State-enterprise and party employees** with formal self-criticism routines, against private-sector workers.
- **Errenzhuan performers and rural audiences** for teasing as correction.
- **Urban literary circles** in Shenyang, Changchun and Harbin.
- **Chaoxianzu forum users** who criticize from inside a minority community.
- **Mongol performers** of praise-and-mockery verse.
- **Manchu heritage communities** looking back on bannermen's self-satire.
- **Families,** where parents' 数落 and children's replies vary by class and town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; official and media forms are well recorded, private criticism is not.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu and classical Chinese for zidishu; Korean; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution violence; criticism of officials and censorship; layoff protests (legal side to Libra and Capricorn); regional mockery that can harden into stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
