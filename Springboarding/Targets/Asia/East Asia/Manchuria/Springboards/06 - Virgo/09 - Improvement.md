# Manchuria: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Chaoxianzu Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Russian for Soviet-aid plant histories. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planners and officials** who wrote the revitalization programs, against residents of the towns they targeted.
- **Shop-floor innovators and model workers** in older plants, against younger hires in restructured firms.
- **Former loggers and their families** in forestry towns after the felling stopped.
- **Mining families** moved out of shantytowns into new blocks.
- **Oroqen and Hezhe** who lived through settlement programs, and their children.
- **Mongol herders** under grazing bans.
- **Chaoxianzu villagers** rebuilding with migrant earnings.
- **Manchu descendants of resettled bannermen** in Shuangcheng and Lalin.
- **Rural young people** who pursue exams, against those who build online businesses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state programs and land projects; mixed for personal self-improvement; thin for minority peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Russian for Soviet-aid histories; Manchu and classical Chinese for Qing resettlement; Korean; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** coercive "reform" of people (labor camps; Cultural Revolution); settlement and grazing policies; displacement by redevelopment; land law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
