# Manchuria: the domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia; pre-war lands carried from China (written for the northeastern core: Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin Chinese (Northeastern Mandarin), Manchu, Korean (Yanbian), Mongolian (Khorchin, Barga, Buryat), Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhen; Japanese and Russian for the archives. **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are: partnership, marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance and justice, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of Shandong and Hebei migrants** (闯关东): customs carried from home counties may differ from long-settled families'.
- **Manchu families** in Liaoning's Manchu autonomous counties (Xinbin, Kuandian, Fengcheng), Sanjiazi village (Fuyu) and Aihui: banner-era clan and marriage rules may survive unevenly.
- **Koreans of Yanbian and Changbai:** own wedding rites, mutual-aid associations and labor migration abroad.
- **Mongols** of Tongliao (Khorchin), Hulunbuir and the Mongol autonomous counties of Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang: herding partnerships and alliance marriages to separate from farming ones.
- **Daur, Evenki (including Aoluguya reindeer herders), Oroqen and Hezhen:** small peoples whose clan, hunting-share and marriage rules each need their own unit, told in their words.
- **Xibe of Shenyang, Hui, the Fuyu Kyrgyz, the Russians of Ergun:** small communities whose marriage and dispute ways may diverge.
- **Industrial cities against farm counties** (Shenyang, Fushun, Anshan, Daqing against the Songnen and Sanjiang plains): the work unit and its collapse reshaped partnership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Mandarin for marriage, gifts and courts; mixed for Korean and Mongolian; thin for the four small peoples. Manchu archives are large but need trained readers.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; written Manchu; Korean; Mongolian (traditional script); Japanese (colonial surveys); Russian and Latin (treaties).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Manchukuo years and the Japanese occupation; competing Korean and Chinese histories of Jiandao/Gando; trafficking of brides across the border; court records and ethnic-policy material whose access has narrowed. **Scope:** Manchuria's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core northeastern provinces.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search China Judgments Online for 彩礼 返还 (bride-price refund) limited to courts in Liaoning, Jilin and Heilongjiang; note the rule cited in each ruling.
2. Search a library catalog for 旗民分治 盛京 and for "Heitu dang" to see what Liaoning Provincial Archives has published.
3. Read the Xiajia chapters of *The Flow of Gifts* to build a gift-ledger template for later village work.
4. Pull marriage and divorce registration tables from the three provincial yearbooks for the latest year, by prefecture.
5. Search CNKI for 伊玛堪 婚姻 and 达斡尔 乌钦 to find published transcriptions.
