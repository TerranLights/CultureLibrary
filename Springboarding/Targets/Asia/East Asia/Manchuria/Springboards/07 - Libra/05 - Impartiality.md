# Manchuria: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; classical Chinese and Jurchen-era sources for the old examinations. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship (the letter of the law only, where law enters).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of state-enterprise workers** in Anshan, Daqing and Fushun: jobs once passed from parent to child by rule, then opened to competition.
- **Villages that hold open-nomination elections against those that do not:** impartial selection as local practice.
- **People who registered as Manchu after 1980:** group preference and how neighbors judge it.
- **Yanbian Korean cadres and teachers:** office-holding under autonomy rules, and the pull of family abroad.
- **Mongol banners with hereditary noble houses in memory:** birth-based office as a living reference.
- **Private business owners against state-sector employees:** who expects fair treatment from offices, and who expects to need a contact.
- **Hezhen, Oroqen and Daur officials in very small communities:** where everyone is kin, recusal is a real puzzle.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for anticorruption and exam material in Mandarin; mixed for village-election studies; thin for Mongolian and Korean sources on connections.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Manchu; Jurchen script only through Chinese records; Korean; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases involve living officials; ethnic preference and identity re-registration are delicate; scandal coverage is censored unevenly.
- **Held for review:** none.
