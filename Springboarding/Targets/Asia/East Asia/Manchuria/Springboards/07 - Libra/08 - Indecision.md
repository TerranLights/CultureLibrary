# Manchuria: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Recent graduates in Shenyang, Changchun and Harbin:** whether to take a state post, a private job, or leave for the south; delay itself is the object.
- **Rust-belt families with parents in their fifties:** deciding whether to follow children elsewhere or stay near old work-unit housing.
- **Young rural couples:** marriage and childbearing timing under parents' pressure.
- **Hulunbuir and Khorchin herders:** seasonal moves and herd sales decided with elders and signs, not alone.
- **Yanbian Korean households:** whether to go abroad to work and when to come back, often decided across borders.
- **Clients of mediums and fortune-tellers in towns and villages:** who hands a choice to a ritual specialist, and for which decisions.
- **Daur and Manchu households with clan traditions of consultation:** whether a family council still decides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on fertility, migration and graduate choices; thin on indecision as a named trait; folk-religion material is scattered and partly hidden.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian; Manchu for the council records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** spirit mediums work at the edge of legality and are often unwilling to be recorded; population decline is politically touchy.
- **Held for review:** none.
