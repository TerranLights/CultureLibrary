# Manchuria: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Japanese for the occupation years. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older villagers of the Hulan and Songhua basins:** settling quarrels quietly may be framed differently by generation; Xiao Hong's places offer a test case.
- **Korean women in Yanbian and in cross-border marriages:** silence about hardship may be shaped by migration and legal status.
- **Small-business owners in cities hit by protection rackets:** paying to keep the peace as an economic calculation.
- **Laid-off workers who accepted settlements against those who protested** (Liaoyang, Daqing): two answers to the same grievance.
- **Manchu families after 1911 and after 1949:** keeping heads down about identity as a form of peace.
- **Mongol herders in grazing disputes with mining firms:** when yielding was chosen and when it was not.
- **Families of victims of the occupation:** the long silence before official memory changed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the 1931 decision and the Manchukuo years; mixed on family silence (court and women's-federation data); thin for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Japanese; Korean; Mongolian; classical Chinese for the Song–Liao–Jin treaties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence, protection rackets and labor protest; collaboration in the Manchukuo years carries strong judgments; survivors' privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
