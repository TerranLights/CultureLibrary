# Manchuria: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern), Manchu, Korean, Mongolian, Russian, Japanese. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu families:** cast as the outsider at the fall of the Qing; how that memory shapes their own blaming of others.
- **Mongols of Hulunbuir and the former Jirim league:** named enemies within during the purge years; testimony is uneven by family.
- **Yanbian Koreans:** placed between Han neighbors, North Korea and South Korea; each mirror differs.
- **Russians of Ergun and the last Harbin Russians:** both targets and holders of images across the border.
- **Long-settled families against later migrants from Shandong and Hebei:** "inside the pass" labels may run both ways.
- **Border-town residents (Suifenhe, Heihe, Dandong):** blame for smuggling, crime or disease lands on the cross-border other.
- **Rural and urban northeasterners:** the rustic image used inside the region itself.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical scapegoating (1900, 1911, 1931, the purge years); mixed for folk beliefs; thin for survey measures of blame inside the region.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Manchu; Korean; Mongolian; Russian; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution purges and ethnic persecution are restricted topics in China; slurs and regional bashing need careful handling; living victims' testimony.
- **Held for review:** none.
