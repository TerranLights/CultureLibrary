# Manchuria: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin (Dongbeihua), Manchu, Korean (Yanbian), Mongolian (Khorchin and other eastern dialects), Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhe (Nanai), Xibe; Japanese and Russian for colonial-era and Harbin sources. **Concept in one line:** how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden: death ways, sex ways, inheritance, shared resources and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han Northeasterners by migrant root:** the Liaodong peninsula vs. later settlers in Heilongjiang; funerals and household division may follow the home province.
- **Manchus:** urban banner descendants vs. Sanjiazi (Fuyu) and other rural villages where clan rites lasted.
- **Korean Chinese (Chaoxianzu):** Yanbian and Changbai; Confucian and Protestant death rites side by side.
- **Mongols:** Khorchin, Fuxin, Harqin Left Wing, Qian Gorlos and Dorbod counties; Buddhist and shamanic layers.
- **Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhe:** small peoples whose own burial, shaman and clan customs need separate treatment.
- **Xibe of Shenyang, Hui of the cities, Harbin's former Russian and Jewish communities:** separate funeral rules and cemeteries.
- **Comparison only (diaspora):** Xibe of Qapqal in Xinjiang; Korean Chinese living in South Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese for Han village life and the Qing; mixed for Manchu (much is archival); thin for Hezhe, Oroqen and Evenki.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu (script and Möllendorff romanization); Korean; Mongolian (traditional script); Japanese and Russian for colonial and Harbin archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shamanic rites whose keepers limit what outsiders hear; human remains in museums; occupation-era mass graves and Unit 731; spirit-medium practice kept from officials. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled (the tracker lists only "China"); this sign is written for the three northeastern provinces as the core.
- **Held for review:** none.
