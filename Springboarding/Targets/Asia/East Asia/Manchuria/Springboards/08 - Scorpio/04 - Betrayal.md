# Manchuria: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin, Manchu, Korean, Mongolian; Japanese for settler memory; classical Chinese for Qing compilations. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu families:** to test whether suspicion after 1911 and 1945 shapes how they speak of traitors and of loyalty.
- **Han descendants of wartime guerrillas and of Manchukuo staff:** two family memories of who sold out whom.
- **Korean Chinese of Yanbian:** marriages and families strained by work migration to South Korea; the language of broken trust in Korean.
- **Mongols of Khorchin lineages:** old alliances with the Qing court, to test whether "defector" is a live word in their histories.
- **Former state-factory workers of Shenyang, Fushun and Anshan:** the factory as a betrayer, not a spouse or friend.
- **Comparison only (diaspora):** Japanese settlers' families in Japan, and Harbin Russians abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime and political betrayal; mixed for marriage and friendship (mostly fiction and surveys); thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Manchu; Japanese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collaboration under Manchukuo still stigmatizes families; Zhang Xueliang's record is politically charged; infidelity data is self-reported and private.
- **Held for review:** none.
