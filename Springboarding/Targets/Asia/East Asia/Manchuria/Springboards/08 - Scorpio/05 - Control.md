## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu banner descendants:** inherited memory of tightly registered lives, against village Manchus outside the garrisons.
- **Oroqen and Evenki:** state control over hunting, guns and forest use against their own customs of the hunt.
- **State-farm families of Beidahuang and Sanjiang:** control of land, labor and schedules by the farm.
- **Korean Chinese of Yanbian:** how life on a border shapes felt control, as a question; household decision-making in Korean families.
- **Factory-town residents of Anshan, Fushun and Daqing:** work-unit control over housing, marriage and daily time.
- **Rural Han households of Jilin and Heilongjiang:** who holds the purse, and spirit mediums as an alternative route to steering events.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and colonial control; mixed for control inside couples (survey-based); thin for small peoples' own accounts.
