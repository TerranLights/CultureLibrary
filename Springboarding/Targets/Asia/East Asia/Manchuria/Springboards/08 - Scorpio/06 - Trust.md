# Manchuria: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin, Manchu, Korean, Mongolian, Oroqen, Evenki. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed (loyalty is researched here for now).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han settlers' descendants by origin (Shandong vs. Hebei lineages):** whether migrant networks set a narrower or wider radius of trust.
- **Manchus and Khorchin Mongols:** old sworn-friend (anda) and oath traditions to test against current speech.
- **Korean Chinese:** savings groups and church networks as trust circles, strained by migration.
- **Urban factory districts vs. villages:** trust in the work unit's promises against kin-based reliance.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhe:** sharing rules of the hunt and the river, and trust in outside agencies.
- **Livestream sellers and buyers across the region:** new trust built in public online talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey trust measures and scandals; mixed for everyday ritual kinship; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu (for archives); Korean; Mongolian; Japanese for orphan memoirs.
