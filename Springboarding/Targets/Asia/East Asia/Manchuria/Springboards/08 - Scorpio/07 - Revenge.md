# Manchuria: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin, Manchu, Korean, Mongolian, Hezhe, Oroqen; Japanese for settler accounts. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu clan memory:** whether Nurhaci's grievance story is told as clan heritage or only as dynastic history.
- **Han villages of the land-reform heartland:** descendants of the "turned over" and of those struck down, each with a different memory of payback.
- **Korean Chinese:** *han* as a word for unresolved grievance, and the An Jung-geun story in Korean-language schooling.
- **Hezhe and Oroqen:** vengeance in hero tales against everyday custom for ending quarrels.
- **Khorchin and other eastern Mongols:** whether a chronicle's revenge theme reaches present speech.
- **Families of the Japanese occupation's victims and of left-behind Japanese:** two sides of one cycle of reprisal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public and political revenge (war, revolution); mixed for personal grudges (mostly fiction and folklore); thin for small peoples outside epic texts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese and Manchu for Nurhaci; Korean; Mongolian; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform violence; anti-Japanese feeling; violent crime cases. Private vengeance set against the state's punishment goes to Capricorn; trial rules to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
