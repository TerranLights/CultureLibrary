# Manchuria: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeastern Mandarin (including trade and outlaw argots), Manchu, Korean, Mongolian, Daur, Evenki; Japanese and Russian for colonial and espionage records. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden, privacy included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchus:** hidden identity after 1911 and its reversal in the 1980s, against clans that guard shaman books from outsiders.
- **Korean Chinese of Yanbian:** Korean as a private language in mixed settings, and whether border matters go unspoken, as a question.
- **Ginseng diggers, loggers and hunters of the Changbai and Khingan ranges:** occupational secrets and cover words.
- **Daur and Evenki:** shamanic knowledge whose keepers decide what may be told.
- **State-factory families:** the dossier as a secret held about them, not by them.
- **Spirit mediums and their clients in Han villages:** secrecy about practice that officials discourage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime secrecy and outlaw lore; mixed for family and personal secrets; thin and restricted for clan and shamanic knowledge.
