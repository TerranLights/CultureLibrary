## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu banner lineages vs. commoner Manchus:** whether noble descent still carries weight in clan life.
- **Khorchin and other eastern Mongol nobility's descendants:** inherited rank after the princes' rule ended.
- **Korean Chinese of Yanbian:** autonomy in name and in practice, and who speaks for the prefecture.
- **State-enterprise towns (Daqing, Anshan) vs. private-business cities (Dalian, Shenyang's new districts):** two routes to influence.
- **Village Han in Jilin and Heilongjiang:** clan size, officials and elections as competing sources of sway.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhe:** how much say over their own lands, rivers and forests they report, in their own voices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court, warlord and colonial power; mixed for present-day power (press is cautious); thin for small peoples' internal leadership.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Manchu; Mongolian; Japanese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bo Xilai and organized-crime cases are politically sensitive; questions on officials may draw guarded answers. Legitimate authority and power ways go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
