# Manchuria: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Northeastern Mandarin colloquial and standard), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu; Oroqen and Evenki where recorded. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former state-enterprise families** of Shenyang, Fushun, Anshan, Hegang and Daqing versus private-sector and service workers: meaning tied to the work unit or to family and money.
- **Sent-down youth** who stayed in Heilongjiang versus those who returned to Harbin, Shanghai or Beijing.
- **Farm counties** of the Songnen and Sanjiang plains versus forest towns facing logging bans.
- **Yanbian Koreans** with relatives working in South Korea: purpose measured by remittances, or by the family left at home.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhe** after resettlement; **Manchu** families in revival networks.
- **Mongols** of the eastern steppe and **Hui** of the cities: religious and herding sources of purpose.
- **Young people leaving the region** versus those who stay, a theme in local fiction and online talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; film, fiction and memoir are rich, while survey data on meaning itself is thin at the provincial level.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Mongolian, some Manchu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair data are restricted or uneven; layoffs and labor unrest touch politically guarded history; sent-down-youth memory is contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
