# Manchuria: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, Korean, Manchu (Qing-era status terms), Mongolian, Russian (Harbin émigré sources), Japanese (colonial-era sources). **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of bannermen** versus families with bondservant or civilian roots: inherited status and its memory.
- **Han migrant lineages** who broke the closure versus later arrivals who came under state plans.
- **Yanbian Koreans:** 1945 as 해방, and later debates about movement to South Korea.
- **Harbin's former Russian and Jewish residents,** as past layers (diaspora accounts as comparison only).
- **Mongol herders** of the eastern steppe and **Evenki, Oroqen, Daur and Hezhe** hunters and fishers: freedom of land and movement.
- **Border-trade towns** (Heihe, Suifenhe, Manzhouli, Dandong) versus interior farm counties.
- **Dalian and the old Kwantung Leased Territory:** port openness and a different colonial past.
- **Hui** communities in the cities: freedom of worship as practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; history and memory sites are rich, while open talk of present-day freedom is thin and guarded.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Manchu, Russian, Japanese, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor-camp history, Cultural Revolution memory, border crossings from North Korea and religious practice outside registered bodies are restricted subjects; legal rights belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
