# Manchuria: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Northeastern colloquial included), Korean, Manchu, Mongolian, Russian and Japanese for contested histories. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older readers of party papers** versus younger people on Douyin and Kuaishou: whose account counts as true.
- **Yanbian Koreans** following both Chinese and South Korean news: two national histories of the same land.
- **Manchu revival circles** versus mainstream accounts of Manchu origins and the Qing.
- **Families of Japanese war orphans and returnees,** whose memories sit between two national stories.
- **Harbin,** where Russian, Jewish and Chinese accounts of the city's founding differ.
- **Small peoples** (Oroqen, Evenki, Daur, Hezhe) whose oral records meet written state history.
- **Mongols** of the eastern banners and **Hui** communities, with their own histories of the 20th century.
- **Rural elders** who remember the occupation and land reform, as oral-history sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; contested-history material is abundant, while independent data on trust in sources is thin and filtered.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Korean first; Japanese and Russian for rival histories; Manchu and Mongolian for older records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Goguryeo-Bohai dispute and occupation memory are politically charged across three countries; disaster disclosure, land-reform violence and the Cultural Revolution are guarded; archives may restrict access.
- **Held for review:** none.
