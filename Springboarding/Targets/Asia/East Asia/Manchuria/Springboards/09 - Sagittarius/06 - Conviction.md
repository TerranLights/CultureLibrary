# Manchuria: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with Northeastern colloquial), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu descendants of bannermen** and **descendants of Han bannermen:** inherited questions of whose loyalty was firm.
- **Yanbian Koreans:** independence-fighter heroes and later political campaigns that struck their own community.
- **Northeastern Han** in old industrial cities versus farm counties: workplace protest versus village quarrels.
- **Mongols of Khorchin** with uprising memory versus **Fuxin and Harqin** Mongols with monastic traditions.
- **Small peoples** (Hezhe, Oroqen, Evenki, Daur): their own heroes, if any are told.
- **Hui** communities: religious firmness through campaigns against religion.
- **Party cadres** taught martyr models versus private citizens who keep their views to themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; heroic and martyr material is abundant and official, while private conviction and dissent are thinly documented.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Japanese (An Jung-geun materials), Mongolian, Manchu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor protests, Cultural Revolution victims and present-day dissent are restricted subjects; An Jung-geun is honored and condemned by different nations; petitioning touches state control. Law itself goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
