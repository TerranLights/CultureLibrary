## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old state-enterprise cities** versus newer private-business hubs like Dalian: different ideas of fair dealing at work.
- **Farm villages** where gifts and connections settle matters versus city offices with formal procedures.
- **Livestream sellers and their audiences** across rural Heilongjiang and Jilin: a new arena of trust.
- **Yanbian Koreans:** honesty within the community versus with brokers and employers abroad.
- **Hui** traders and customers, with halal trust at stake.
- **Mongol herders** dealing with traders; **Hezhe, Oroqen and Evenki** dealing with tourism operators who sell their culture.
- **Generations:** those schooled in the planned economy's moral campaigns versus market-era youth, who meet different temptations.

## 4. Flags
