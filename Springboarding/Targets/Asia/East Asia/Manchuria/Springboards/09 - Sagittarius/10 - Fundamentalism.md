# Manchuria: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (including Chinese Muslim terms from Arabic and Persian), Korean, Manchu, Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hui congregations** of different factions in each city, and their changing balance over generations.
- **Yanbian Korean Protestants:** registered churches, house churches and those linked to South Korean missions.
- **Han Chinese "national learning" circles** and Wang Fengyi followers versus mainstream families who reject them.
- **Manchu purists** of language and rite versus those who mix freely with Han practice.
- **Mongol monastic communities** restoring rules versus lay Mongols with little practice.
- **Party orthodoxy** in older industrial cities, where the term's fit to secular doctrine is itself contested.
- **Evenki, Oroqen and Hezhe:** whether strict conversion movements have reached them at all (likely thin).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin and sensitive; most material is official, critical or from outside the movements.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Korean, Arabic and Persian loan terms in Chinese Muslim usage, Manchu, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the state bans several religious movements, one founded in Changchun; banned groups stay a flag here, not a pointer; Muslim faction debates and foreign missions are policed; members of strict groups may be at risk if named. Research needs consent and care.
- **Held for review:** none.
