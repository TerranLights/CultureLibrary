# Manchuria: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia (pre-war lands from China; core: Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang and the adjoining eastern steppe). **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Standard and Northeast Mandarin), Manchu, Korean, Mongolian, Russian, Japanese (colonial-era records). **Concept in one line:** the domain question, who commands, who obeys and what is owed, across career, status, reputation, and the folkways of age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han Northeasterners by origin and place:** descendants of *chuang Guandong* migrants; the Liaodong peninsula (Dalian, Jiaoliao Mandarin) against Harbin and the north, since migrant history and colonial rule differ.
- **Manchu communities:** autonomous counties of eastern Liaoning and Jilin, and the Sanjiazi area near Qiqihar, for banner and clan memory.
- **Chaoxianzu Koreans** of Yanbian and Changbai, with their own schools, press and elders' bodies.
- **Mongols:** Horqin and Hulunbuir (Barga, Buryat), shaped by banner princes and pastoral life.
- **Daur, Evenki (including Aoluguya reindeer herders), Oroqen, Hezhe, Xibe:** small peoples with clan offices of their own.
- **Ergun Russians and Harbin's Russian legacy; Hui of Shenyang.**
- **Settings:** factory cities, Beidahuang state farms, Greater Khingan forestry towns, border-trade towns (Suifenhe, Heihe, Hunchun).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Qing banners, Manchukuo and the 1990s rustbelt; mixed for the reform era; thin for the small Tungusic peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for nearly everything; Manchu for Qing-era authority in its own words; Japanese for colonial administration; Korean for Yanbian community life; Mongolian (traditional script) for banner and steppe governance; Russian for Harbin and Ergun.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Manchukuo collaboration and its memory; the Goguryeo and Balhae dispute over the region's past; ethnic classification; Japanese orphans left behind in 1945; shamans' clan knowledge, used only as clans share it. Manchuria is a separate culture-nation from the Sinian Federation in this setting. Its exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets write for the core Northeast lands.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 东北 + 规矩, 东北 + 单位制 and 八旗 + 责任.
