# Manchuria: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (official and colloquial); Japanese for wartime responsibility; Manchu for Qing liability; Korean, Mongolian and Russian in their communities. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Forest-industry towns of the Greater Khingan:** life after the 1987 fire and the logging bans, when responsibility for the forest shifted.
- **Liaoning factory cities** (Shenyang, Anshan, Fushun): enterprise, director and worker answering for collapse.
- **Beidahuang state-farm households** against old peasant villages: responsibility assigned by contract or by custom.
- **Oroqen and Evenki hunters** turned rangers or herders.
- **Chaoxianzu Koreans** in Yanbian, with their own school system.
- **Japanese-era memory communities:** families near Fushun, Pingfang (Harbin) and other wartime sites.
- **Mongol herders of Hulunbuir:** grazing responsibilities under household pasture contracts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and scandals, where official reports name the liable; mixed for everyday and family responsibility; thin for the small peoples' own accounts of stewardship.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for investigation reports, online blame talk and textbooks; Japanese for confessions and war-responsibility debates; Manchu for Qing case files; Korean for Yanbian school materials; Mongolian for herders' pasture duties; Russian for the Amur-side view of the 2005 spill.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war-crimes sites and confessions are heavily curated; disaster reports may omit political blame; victims' families deserve care in any approach.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 大兴安岭 火灾 责任 追究 and 松花江 事件 问责.

5. Read Shuang Xuetao's novella, then search reviews for 罪 and 责任 to see how readers framed its guilt.
