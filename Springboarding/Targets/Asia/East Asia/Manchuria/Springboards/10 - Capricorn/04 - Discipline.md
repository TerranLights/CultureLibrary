# Manchuria: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Northeast and Standard Chinese; Manchu for banner training; Korean for Yanbian schooling; Mongolian for monastic life; Japanese for colonial academies; Russian. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu clans** that keep a shaman line against those that do not.
- **Factory and mining cities** of Liaoning: industrial time-discipline and its collapse.
- **Military-founded state farms** of Heilongjiang against civilian villages.
- **Winter-sport towns** (Qiqihar, Qitaihe, Harbin, Jilin) with their training schools.
- **Chaoxianzu Koreans:** study discipline and parental pressure in Korean-medium schooling.
- **Mongols of Hulunbuir and Horqin:** monastic and herding discipline.
- **Oroqen, Evenki, Hezhe:** hunting and fishing training passed by elders.
- **Ergun Russians and the Orthodox and Old Believer heritage** of Trekhreche, for fasting disciplines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on banner and socialist-era discipline campaigns; mixed on parenting (forums, little survey data at province level); thin for the small peoples except in ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for parenting debates, sport and factory records; Manchu for banner edicts and clan rites; Japanese for the colonial military academy; Korean for Yanbian schools and the academy's Korean alumni; Mongolian for monastic rule; Russian for Old Believer fasting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor-camp histories are politically restricted; shaman training is clan knowledge, used only as clans share it; child punishment needs care with any personal testimony.
- **Held for review:** none.
