# Manchuria: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (legal, colloquial and bandit argot); Manchu and Mongolian for Qing-era plural law; Japanese for colonial courts; Korean; Russian for Harbin's foreign courts. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. This sheet takes the spirit; the letter is Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mongol herders** of Hulunbuir and Horqin: a separate Qing code, then pasture contracts.
- **Oroqen, Evenki, Hezhe:** customary hunting and fishing rules against state game law.
- **Chaoxianzu Koreans:** use of Korean in courts and mediation in Yanbian.
- **Harbin and Dalian:** cities once under foreign courts (Russian, Japanese) against the interior.
- **Rustbelt workers and petitioners:** law approached from below after the layoffs.
- **Private entrepreneurs** in the three provinces: law as a business risk.
- **Forest and border towns:** where custom, poaching and smuggling blur.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing plural law, banditry and high-profile trials; mixed for everyday legal feeling; thin for customary law outside studies written in Chinese.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for court decisions, petitions and bandit argot; Manchu for Qing legal terms and banner cases; Mongolian for the steppe code and herders' customs; Japanese for Manchukuo law; Korean for Yanbian mediation; Russian for Harbin's courts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criminal cases name living people; petitioning and rights defense are politically sensitive; customary law is the peoples' own, used only as they share it. The permission and correctional-control shares are scored at Stage 2 with Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Search CNKI for 东北 流人 and 宁古塔 流放 to map the exile literature.

4. Search CNKI for 鄂伦春 习惯法 and 呼伦贝尔 草场纠纷 调解.
