# Manchuria: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese and its Northeast dialect words for neat and messy; Manchu for ritual and spatial order; Japanese for colonial "public peace"; Korean, Mongolian and Russian for village life. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manchu villages of eastern Liaoning and Jilin:** household and ritual order, house layout, clan rites.
- **Han villages settled from Shandong and Hebei:** village compacts and seasonal rhythms.
- **Planned cities** (Changchun, Harbin, Dalian) against unplanned mining and forest towns.
- **Chaoxianzu Korean villages:** festival order and household customs.
- **Mongol and Daur herding communities:** order of camp and pasture.
- **Evenki and Oroqen:** spirits, taboos and balance with the forest, as they describe them.
- **Ergun Russian villages:** households shaped by Orthodox and Siberian ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and colonial order (plans, campaigns, reports); mixed for public feelings of safety; thin for cosmic order among small peoples outside ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for procuratorate reports, lockdown posts and village compacts; Manchu for the Nisan tale and Qing closure edicts; Japanese for the collective-hamlet and city-planning records; Korean for Yanbian village rules; Mongolian for pastoral order; Russian for Harbin's railway town.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability maintenance" and protest are politically sensitive; collective hamlets and lockdowns touch living grief; shamanic cosmology is shared only as communities allow; regional crime images should be tested, not repeated.
- **Held for review:** none.
