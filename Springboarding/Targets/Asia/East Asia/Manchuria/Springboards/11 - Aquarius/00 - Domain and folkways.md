# Manchuria: Community and ideals, domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Northeastern; Jiao-Liao on the Liaodong coast); Korean; Mongolian; Manchu (heritage, archival); Daur, Evenki, Oroqen, Hezhen, Xibe; Japanese and Russian for archives. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for (friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals), with the folkways of settlement, migration, association and hospitality.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han Northeasterners by origin:** Shandong-descended Liaodong (its own dialect area); Hebei-descended Liaoxi; the northern Heilongjiang frontier, settled last.
- **Manchu:** banner-descended urban families against Manchu farming villages (the Sanjiazi village area of Fuyu, Heilongjiang *check*).
- **Koreans of Yanbian** and of scattered rice villages: their own village and association forms.
- **Mongols:** Khorchin and Fuxin farmers and herders; Dorbod; Barga and Buryat of Hulunbuir if the steppe margin is included.
- **Hui, Xibe, Daur, Evenki (including the reindeer Evenki), Oroqen, Hezhen, Fuyu Kirgiz, Russians of the Ergun area:** small peoples with distinct kin-group and settlement forms.
- **Company towns:** Daqing (oil), Anshan (steel) and the Beidahuang state farms, where the state built the community.
- **Port cities:** Dalian and Yingkou, with treaty-port and leased-territory pasts.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Northeasterners wintering in Hainan; Korean Chinese in South Korea; Harbin Russians abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han towns, banner history and colonial surveys; mixed for Koreans and Mongols (better in their own languages); thin, mostly state ethnography, for Oroqen, Evenki, Hezhen and Daur.
- **Lands and status:** Manchuria is a separate culture-nation in this setting, not part of the Sinian Federation. Its exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core Northeast (Liaoning, Jilin, Heilongjiang), with the eastern steppe margin noted where it matters.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin for most of the domain; Classical Chinese and Manchu for Qing gatherings and clan records; Korean for Yanbian village life; Mongolian (traditional script) for Khorchin gatherings; Japanese for the colonial village surveys; Russian for Harbin's émigré associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Manchukuo years; Korean-Chinese border history; clan and shaman rites kept within families.
- **Held for review:** none.
