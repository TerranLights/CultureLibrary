# Manchuria: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese and Manchu (Qing reforms); Japanese (war-criminal re-education testimony); Korean; Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-firm workers** in Shenyang, Anshan, Fushun and Harbin, against the private traders and entrepreneurs who rose in the same years.
- **Liaoning,** the first to pilot many reforms, against Jilin and Heilongjiang.
- **Farm households** under the contract system, against state-farm employees under farm restructuring.
- **Oroqen, Evenki and Hezhen,** targets of settlement, hunting and fishing reforms, in their own accounts.
- **Mongol herders** under grazing bans, if the steppe margin is included.
- **Koreans,** whose schools and villages were reshaped by language policy and outmigration.
- **Banner descendants,** whose last privileges were removed by late-Qing and Republican reforms.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Northeastern workers who left for southern factories, as an exit from reform at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for industrial reform and policy; mixed for workers' own voices (good in labor ethnography); thin for small peoples' accounts of reforms done to them.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese and Manchu for Qing script and administrative reforms; Japanese for the Fushun returnees' testimony; Korean for Yanbian school and village debates; Mongolian for herders' views of grazing reforms; Mandarin for policy and labor literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "remolding" in labor camps (the Masanjia and Xingkaihu camps *check*) and political campaigns; layoff protests; forced resettlement. Legal reforms as such belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
