# Manchuria: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin Chinese (Northeast Mandarin), Korean, Mongolian, Manchu (historical), Japanese (for the war-orphan record). **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Han of Shenyang and Harbin against village Han:** talk-based closeness (唠嗑) may work differently in apartment blocks and in villages.
- **The laid-off generation against their children:** whether parents' hardships are understood across the 1990s divide.
- **Joseonjok of Yanbian:** nunchi as a named skill, and families split between China and South Korea.
- **Manchu and Han-army banner descendants:** to see whether any separate idiom of fellow-feeling survives.
- **Horqin and Hulunbuir Mongols:** reading the needs of guests and kin in herding life.
- **Families tied to the Japanese war orphans (Fangzheng, Harbin, Mudanjiang):** empathy across a war line.
- **Russians of Ergun:** mixed families bridging two ways of reading feeling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Psychology studies exist but rarely name the region; media and literature are rich; ethnography of everyday empathy is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for scales, news and fiction; Manchu to read Qing renderings of the classics; Korean for nunchi and Yanbian broadcasting; Japanese for the war orphans' records and memoirs; Mongolian for herding-family talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sympathy toward Japanese settlers is politically charged; hospital violence cases involve grieving families; hotline data are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 共情 or 同理心 with 东北, 沈阳, 长春, 哈尔滨 to list regional psychology studies.

3. Find the Manchu *Mencius* and look up the passage on fellow-feeling (恻隐) and the reciprocity passages.
4. Sample 300 Douban comments on 《漫长的季节》 and note words of identification.
5. Search 中国医科大学 共情 医学生 for medical-empathy studies.
