# Manchuria: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Manchuria, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin Chinese (Northeast Mandarin), Korean, Manchu (Qing), Russian and Japanese (bandit-era and Manchukuo records), Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** what counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how those who commit it are seen. Law itself is researched only under Libra (its letter) and Capricorn (its spirit); this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rust-belt cities (Shenyang, Fushun, Harbin) against farm villages:** how the layoff years and their wrongdoing are remembered may differ.
- **Joseonjok:** a people tagged with a criminal stereotype abroad, and their own views of wrongdoing.
- **Descendants of Ningguta exiles and Qing convict settlers:** whether a family past as wrongdoers is told or hidden.
- **Former labor-camp inmates and state-farm workers (Beidahuang):** the convict stigma from the inside.
- **Horqin and Hulunbuir Mongols:** livestock theft and pasture wrongs as herders define them.
- **Border towns (Heihe, Suifenhe, Hunchun):** smuggling, tolerated or condemned.
- **Hui communities:** mosque-centered sanction and reconciliation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historic banditry, crime fiction and big trials; thin for public victimization data (no public national victimization survey is known to the scout; *check*); official crime numbers serve the state's purposes.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for exile writing, folklore and press; Russian for the khunkhuzy records; Japanese for Manchukuo police files; Korean for the Yanbian stereotype debate; Manchu for Qing terms of guilt; Mongolian for herding wrongs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Unit 731 and Manchukuo atrocities; official corruption; human trafficking across the North Korean border; labor camps (Masanjia in Liaoning among them) and political prisoners; campaign confessions. Law itself goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Wu Zhaoqian's Ningguta poems and Fang Gongqian's account for words of guilt and exile.
2. Search library catalogs for 曹保明 胡子 and list his bandit volumes.
3. Search JACAR for 匪賊 討伐 満洲 for police and army labeling of "bandits".
4. Search press archives for 慕马案 and 刘涌案 for public commentary.
5. Search Korean press for 황해 조선족 반발 for the Joseonjok response.
