# Bashu: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Nuosu Yi, Tibetan, Hmong-Mien, Tujia. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu against Chongqing:** reputations of teasing wit against blunt loudness, to be tested, not assumed.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan plain:** a defiant street idiom (不服周) against the official polish of the provincial capital.
- **Yunnan and Guizhou Han:** settler-descended towns, where deference to old garrison families may shape who speaks first.
- **Liangshan Nuosu:** public oratory as a male art; compare clan elders, young men and women.
- **Kham Tibetans of Garzê:** epic singers' formulaic self-announcement, against monastic teaching on pride.
- **Miao and Dong of southeast Guizhou:** antiphonal singing, where a reply must answer a challenge.
- **Tujia brides; migrant workers demanding unpaid wages (讨薪); children talking back:** the less powerful and when they may speak.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and media; thin in survey data split by province; minority speaking norms mostly in ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Sichuanese and Wuhan speech (much is dialect-only and oral), Nuosu Yi, Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning (上访) and collective complaints are politically sensitive and partly a legal matter (letter of the law to Libra, legal culture to Capricorn); wage-arrears protests may be underreported.
- **Held for review:** none.
