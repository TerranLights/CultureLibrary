# Bashu: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Sichuanese and other Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Na (Mosuo), Miao (Hmu), Dong (Kam). **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan youth against rural youth:** who gets a say in study, work and marriage, and when.
- **Families of migrant workers:** parents far away, grandparents in charge, children deciding alone.
- **Na (Mosuo) of Lugu Lake:** households led by a senior woman; compare Yunnan and Sichuan sides.
- **Miao and Dong courtship customs** (游方, 行歌坐月): youth choosing partners in sanctioned spaces.
- **Liangshan Nuosu:** clan-arranged marriage and bride-price, and young people's push against them.
- **Tibetan herders and farmers of Garzê and Ngawa;** monastic life as one chosen path.
- **Women, elders and disabled people:** who decides for them at home and in hospital.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han family decision-making (surveys, May Fourth literature); mixed for minorities; collective self-rule mostly in Chinese folk studies.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Sichuanese for media; Na, Hmu, Kam and Nuosu terms for courtship and self-government.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political autonomy in Tibetan areas is a restricted topic; love-suicide, bride-price and early marriage are sensitive; marriage and autonomy statutes go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
