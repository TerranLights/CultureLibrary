## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubei's Chu heritage** against **Sichuan's Shu and Ba heritage:** whose origin story is told first in museums.
- **Settler-descended Han:** families whose stories start with a migration, not with the land.
- **Peoples with their own year start:** Qiang, Yi (and Hani's October year), Dai, Miao, Tibetans of Kham and Amdo.
- **Day-choosing traditions:** Han almanac users, Shui script readers, Nuosu *bimo*.
- **People forced to restart:** Three Gorges relocatees, Wenchuan survivors, Tibetan and Yi herders moved into new settlements.
- **Laid-off state workers** of Wuhan and Chongqing in the 1990s, and later migrant workers returning home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals, origins and excavated texts; mixed for personal fresh starts, best found in media, memoirs and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for slips and gazetteers; Mandarin; Qiang, Tai, Wa, Hmu and Shui for rites and myths.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation and earthquake rebuilding are politically sensitive; Shui and *shibi* knowledge may be held by specialists; museum accounts of origins are official.
- **Held for review:** none.
