*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing and eastern Sichuan:** wartime capital memory and the Ba sacrifice tale.
- **The Chengdu plain:** the Sichuan army's men and the Paoge code, against a city image of ease.
- **Hubei:** Chu war hymns, Wuchang's revolutionaries, Wuhan's 1938 defense.
- **Yunnan's border counties** (Tengchong, Dehong), where the 1940s war was fought on home ground.
- **Liangshan Nuosu, Kham Tibetans and Lisu:** epic heroes and ordeal rites with their own meanings.
- **Tujia and Miao** of the Wuling mountains: soldier traditions under the old chieftains.
- **Women's endurance against men's daring:** compare who is called brave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in war, revolution and disaster material, much of it official; mixed for everyday and moral courage; thin in surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Sichuanese slang; Nuosu Yi and Tibetan for epics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr narratives are state-controlled and legally protected from "slander" (letter of the law to Libra); whistleblowing in the 2020 Wuhan outbreak is highly sensitive; Tibetan protest acts are restricted and are not a source for courage findings here.
- **Held for review:** none.
