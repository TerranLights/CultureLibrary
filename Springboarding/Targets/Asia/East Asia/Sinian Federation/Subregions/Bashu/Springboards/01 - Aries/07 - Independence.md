# Bashu: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Nuosu Yi, Tibetan, Bai, Hui usage, Hmong. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu-plain farm households and mountain households:** different chances of living from one's own land.
- **Urban single children** of Wuhan, Chengdu and Chongqing: leaving home and paying their own way.
- **Labor-exporting counties** of Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou: remittance-dependent families.
- **Liangshan Nuosu and Guizhou Miao villages** under poverty relief and relocation.
- **Yunnan Hui and Bai traders:** independence through commerce and networks.
- **Kham and Amdo herders:** self-provisioning against reliance on caterpillar-fungus income.
- **"Empty-nest" elders, disabled people and widowed women:** on whom each may depend.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for living arrangements, migration and provincial political history; mixed for everyday self-provisioning; thin for emotional independence.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Nuosu Yi, Tibetan for minority accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** separatism and Tibetan or Yi political independence are restricted topics; poverty-relief claims are official and need village voices; Hui history after 1873 is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
