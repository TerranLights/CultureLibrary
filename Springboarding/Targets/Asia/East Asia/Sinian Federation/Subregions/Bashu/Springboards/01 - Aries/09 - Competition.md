## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubei:** an exam-heavy province with famous cram schools.
- **Chengdu against Chongqing:** city ranking, football and business rivalry since the 1997 split.
- **Guizhou's Miao and Dong villages:** contests between villages, often for food and honor rather than money.
- **Kham Tibetans and Liangshan Nuosu:** horse races, wrestling and beauty contests at festivals.
- **Tujia of the Wuling mountains:** a founding contest myth and later martial traditions.
- **Bai of Dali; Dai of Xishuangbanna** (boat races at the water festival).
- **Urban youth:** e-sports, rap battles and online ranking.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, festivals and sport; mixed for attitudes to competition by province; envy and its beliefs mostly in folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Kam and Hmu for festival terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling at mahjong and bullfights is policed; beauty contests among minorities are tied to tourism and state display; gaokao fairness and "exam migrants" are politically touchy.
- **Held for review:** none.
