## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men of Chongqing and Wuhan:** motorcycles, e-bikes and wild swimming in big river cities.
- **Liangshan Nuosu youth:** migration, drugs and the passage to manhood.
- **Guizhou's banquet and liquor towns:** drinking as duty, not thrill.
- **Yunnan's mushroom foragers and eaters:** risk taken at the table.
- **Kham and Amdo:** local guides and herders against outside backpackers on the same peaks.
- **Coal-mining communities:** risk imposed by work, set apart from risk sought.
- **Urban investors and online youth:** speculation and stunts for views.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in public-health and traffic statistics and in media; mixed for attitudes; minority meanings mostly in a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan slang; Nuosu Yi for Liangshan material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drugs and HIV in Liangshan, mine accidents and cross-border gambling on the Yunnan frontier are sensitive and partly censored; legal penalties for drink-driving and gambling go to Libra, legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
