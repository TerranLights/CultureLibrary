# Bashu (巴蜀): The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (普通话), Southwestern Mandarin (西南官话) and Classical Chinese; Nuosu (Yi), Tibetan, Qiang, Hmu and Hmong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Bouyei, Tujia, Bai, Naxi, Hani, Dai. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how it is held (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth), with the food, dress and wealth folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain** against **Chongqing and the Three Gorges counties**: one basin, two urban styles of spending.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan plain** (Hubei): Chu heritage and Hankou's river commerce.
- **Southern Sichuan salt and liquor towns** (Zigong, Yibin, Luzhou): local fortunes.
- **Nuosu (Yi)** of Liangshan and Chuxiong; **Tibetans** of Garzê and Ngawa; **Qiang** and **Gyalrong**: herding, silver and marriage goods.
- **Miao and Dong** of Qiandongnan; **Bouyei and Shui** of south Guizhou; **Tunpu** of Anshun (Ming-garrison Han with their own dress).
- **Tujia** of Enshi and southeast Chongqing; **Bai** of Dali; **Naxi** and **Mosuo**; **Hani**; **Dai**; **Hui** of Yunnan.
- **Hakka** enclaves such as Luodai (洛带), from the Qing resettlement.
- Comparison only: Yunnanese communities in northern Thailand and Myanmar; Hmong abroad.
