# Bashu (巴蜀): Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Classical and Qing documentary Chinese (contracts); Tibetan, Nuosu, Naxi/Mosuo, Kam and Hmu for minority tenure. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain** tenancy history against **hill Sichuan** and the **Three Gorges** counties: land markets to separate.
- **Jianghan plain** (Hubei): diked lake polders (垸) held by groups.
- **Miao and Dong** timber households of the Qingshui valley: written shares in forests.
- **Zigong salt** families and investors: shares in wells.
- **Mosuo** of Lugu Lake against **Naxi** of Lijiang: house and inheritance systems to separate.
- **Nuosu** clans of Liangshan; **Tibetan** herders and monasteries of Garzê and Ngawa; **Qiang** villages.
- **Hani** and **Yi** villages with sacred groves; **Bai** lineages of Dali.
- **Urban owners against migrant renters** in Wuhan, Chengdu and Chongqing; **Tujia** stilt-house villages of Enshi.
- Comparison only: Hmong and Yunnanese abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; large published contract collections (Qingshui, Zigong, Ba County), plus surveys and census tables; thin on non-Han concepts of owning in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Qing documentary Chinese and its contract formulas; Tibetan and Nuosu for herders' and clans' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land requisition (征地) protests; pasture policy in Tibetan areas; Liangshan's old ranked order and its official framing; land-reform violence. Property law is Libra's (letter) and Capricorn's (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
