# Bashu (巴蜀): Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin (Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming, Guiyang speech); Classical Chinese; Nuosu, Tibetan and Hmu for minority voices. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu** against **Chongqing**: public styles of pride and teasing, to be compared.
- **Wuhan** and Hubei's **exam-belt** counties (Huanggang): worth by schooling.
- **Laid-off state-factory workers** of Wuhan, Chongqing and Panzhihua.
- **Porters, out-migrants and left-behind children** from rural Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou.
- **Nuosu** of Liangshan: clan (家支) standing and young men's worth.
- **Miao** of Qiandongnan and **Mosuo** of Lugu Lake: peoples under tourists' eyes.
- **Tibetans** of Garzê and Ngawa; **Tujia**; **Hui** of Yunnan.
- **Elders** and **people disabled in the 2008 earthquake**.
- Comparison only: Sichuanese workers in other member states.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese psychology (self-esteem studies of students and migrants); mixed in ethnography; thin in minorities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Southwestern Mandarin for tone and insult; Nuosu, Hmu and Tibetan for minority voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and HIV among Liangshan youth; ethnic representation; suicide and mental-health data; earthquake school collapses. Recognition and honor go to Leo; status and reputation to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
