# Bashu (巴蜀): Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Nuosu (Yi script), Tibetan, Dai, Naxi and Hmong (Pollard script) for religious and teaching texts. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain** against **Chongqing** and **Hubei's** Jingzhou and Wuhan: Han regional moral vocabularies to separate.
- **Daoist and Buddhist** lay communities (Qingcheng, Emei, Wudang).
- **Hui** of Yunnan (Weishan, Shadian, Kunming); **Dai** of Xishuangbanna and Dehong.
- **Tibetans** of Garzê and Ngawa; **Nuosu** of Liangshan with bimo and suni ritualists.
- **Naxi** (Dongba), **Bai** (benzhu), **Mosuo**.
- **Christian A-Hmao, Lisu and Miao** communities against non-Christian neighbors.
- **Generations**: those raised before and after reform; urban youth against rural elders.
- Comparison only: Hui and Yunnanese communities in Myanmar and Thailand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; national surveys have small provincial samples; the official values discourse is loud and can crowd out lived values in searches; religious and minority texts exist but need specialist readers.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Nuosu, Tibetan, Dai and Pollard-script Hmong; Arabic and Persian terms in Hui texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion (unregistered churches, Tibetan monasteries, disputes over mosque rebuilding in Yunnan); bimo ritual knowledge, shared at the priests' discretion. Village covenants and customary codes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
