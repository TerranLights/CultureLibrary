## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain** irrigated farmers against drought-prone **east Sichuan and Chongqing hills**.
- **Hubei flood-plain** households on the Jianghan dikes and Wuhan's lake districts.
- **Longmen Shan earthquake belt** (Wenchuan, Beichuan Qiang, Lushan) and **Yunnan's quake zones** (Ludian, Zhaotong).
- **Tibetan herders** of Garzê and Ngawa; **Nuosu** households relying on clan (家支) aid.
- **Dong and Miao** wooden villages of Qiandongnan; **Hani** terrace villages.
- **State-sector retirees** against **gig workers and delivery riders** in Chengdu and Wuhan.
- **Out-migrants without local social insurance** and the **elders and children** left at home.
- Comparison only: Sichuanese workers in other member states' factories.
