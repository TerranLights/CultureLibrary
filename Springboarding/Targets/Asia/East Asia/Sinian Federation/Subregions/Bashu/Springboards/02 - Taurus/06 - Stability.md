# Bashu (巴蜀): Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin (with Wuhan and Chengdu old-city vocabulary); Hani and Nuosu for genealogies; Tibetan for herding families. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Chengdu families** against the city's fast-growing newcomer population.
- **Third Front families** of Panzhihua, Mianyang, Shiyan and Liupanshui: settled by order.
- **Hankou lane residents** in Wuhan; **Chongqing** old districts.
- **Tunpu** of Anshun; **Hakka** of Luodai: Han groups with distinct origins.
- **Hani** of Honghe and **Nuosu** of Liangshan: genealogy as continuity.
- **Tibetan herders** moving into fixed homes; **Mosuo** households.
- **Three Gorges resettlers** and **out-migrants** who left home towns for work.
- **Hui** villages of Yunnan with long-settled mosque communities.
- Comparison only: Hakka and Yunnanese communities abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration and marriage statistics and Third Front history; mixed for old-city life (memoirs, photographs); thin for minority genealogies in print.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Wuhan and Chengdu speech; Hani, Nuosu and Tibetan for oral genealogies and herders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** parts of Third Front records remain closed; demolition disputes; herder settlement policy; political "stability maintenance" (维稳) belongs to Capricorn, change to Aries, home and roots to Cancer.
