# Bashu (巴蜀): Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Tibetan, Bai, Hmu and Nuosu for courtship and marriage songs. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; national surveys on couples are summarized more than released; ethnography of courtship is rich for minorities but often an outsider's; regional jealousy talk is easiest to find online.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Bai, Hmu, Nuosu and Tibetan for song and marriage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and named victims; poison-keeping accusations that stigmatize families; bride-price controversies; minority marriage forms open to sensational framing. Domestic-violence law goes to Libra; control and trust to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
