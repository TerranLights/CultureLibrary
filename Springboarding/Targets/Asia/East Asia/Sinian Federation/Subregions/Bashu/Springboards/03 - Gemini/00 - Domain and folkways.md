# Bashu: Domain and folkways (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (lands: Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin (西南官话) in its local forms; Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu (Liangshan Yi); Hmong/Miao varieties; Dai, Bai, Naxi, Tujia, Dong, Buyei and other minority languages. **Concept in one line:** the domain of communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech ways, learning ways, neighbors, short journeys).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain (Shu) and Chongqing with eastern Sichuan (Ba):** the old pair inside the state's own name; speech, pace and public life to be checked separately.
- **Minjiang-dialect belt (Leshan, Zigong, Yibin):** a distinct Southwestern Mandarin subgroup.
- **Hubei:** Wuhan; the Jianghan plain; eastern Hubei (Jianghuai speech); southeastern Hubei (Gan speech); Enshi (Tujia and Miao).
- **Yunnan and Guizhou Han:** Kunming, Tengchong and Dali Han; Guizhou Han, including the Tunpu (屯堡) of Anshun.
- **Han minorities of origin:** Hakka of Luodai and other Sichuan pockets; "Old Huguang" (老湖广话) speech islands.
- **Non-Han peoples:** Yi (Nuosu of Liangshan; Sani, Axi and Lolopo in Yunnan), Tibetans (Kham in Garzê, Ngawa, Diqing), Qiang, Tujia, Miao/Hmong (Hmub, Chuanqiandian Hmong, A-Hmao), Dong, Buyei, Shui, Gelao, Bai, Naxi and Mosuo, Dai, Hani, Lisu, Lahu, Wa, Jingpo, Zhuang of Wenshan, Hui.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Sichuanese migrant workers in coastal cities; Yunnanese communities in Myanmar and northern Thailand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese for Han speech and schooling; mixed for the larger minorities; thin in English for Guizhou and small Yunnan peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu; Hmong/Miao varieties; Dai scripts; others by unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority-language schooling in Tibetan and Yi areas is politically sensitive; some archives are closed or slow; ethnic classification is contested for some groups. Language law and education law go to Libra or Capricorn. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the five provinces in the tracker.
- **Held for review:** none.
