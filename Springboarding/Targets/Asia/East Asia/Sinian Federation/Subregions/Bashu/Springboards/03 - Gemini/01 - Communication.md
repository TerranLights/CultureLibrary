# Bashu: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming and Guiyang speech; Tibetan (Kham, Amdo); Nuosu; Hmong/Miao; Lisu; Zhuang; Chinese Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the local ideas of what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing:** popular talk sets their speech against each other; check speech rate, tone and directness with recordings, not reputation.
- **Minjiang subgroup (Leshan, Zigong, Yibin):** older sound system; whether local speech carries status at home and in town.
- **Wuhan; eastern Hubei (Jianghuai); southeastern Hubei (Gan):** three speech worlds inside one province.
- **Kunming and Guiyang; the Tunpu of Anshun:** Southwestern Mandarin with distinct histories; Tunpu speech and its garrison origins.
- **Hakka and "Old Huguang" pockets in Sichuan:** home language against the regional lingua franca.
- **Nuosu Yi:** kenre and formal speech at gatherings, separate from Yunnan Yi groups.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans:** honorific use, and Chinese at school and market.
- **A-Hmao, other Miao, and Lisu:** script histories and church literacy.
- **Deaf signers:** a language community of their own.
- **Generations:** older dialect-dominant speakers against children schooled in Standard Chinese.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han dialects in Chinese; mixed for Yi and Tibetan; thin for smaller peoples' talk norms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu; Hmong/Miao; Lisu; reading of dialect transcription.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** script choice and minority-language use in schools and media are political; missionary sources carry a conversion agenda. The national language law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
