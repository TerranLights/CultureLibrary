# Bashu: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese for older records; Tibetan; Nuosu; Shui; Hmong/Miao. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how people gather, pass on, trust, withhold and use it: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing readers:** two metro press markets with rival outlets; test whether trust differs.
- **Wuhan:** residents with a recent crisis of information; separate pre-2020 and post-2020 sources.
- **Rural Guizhou and Yunnan mountain villages:** loudspeaker, market day and phone, not print.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans (Garzê, Ngawa, Diqing):** Tibetan-language media, monastery knowledge and pastoral word of mouth.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** oral lineage knowledge beside state media.
- **Shui, Naxi and other peoples with ritual scripts:** knowledge held by specialists.
- **Qiang and Tibetans of the 2008 quake zone:** disaster information as its own case.
- **Dai and other border peoples of Xishuangbanna and Dehong:** media from across the border.
- **Age groups:** television and loudspeaker against short-video apps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in state and commercial Chinese media; thin and hard to reach for independent or minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu; Shui and Miao for local work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship and internal reporting; the Wenchuan school collapses and the 2020 Wuhan outbreak are sensitive, and some archives are closed; Tibetan media are tightly controlled; ritual manuscripts may be restricted by their keepers. Information law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
