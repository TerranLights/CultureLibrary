## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan families:** tutoring and enrichment against learning in the family trade.
- **Rural sending areas (northeast Sichuan, Guizhou, eastern Chongqing):** grandparent-raised children; learning by helping with fields and younger siblings.
- **Nuosu Yi (Liangshan):** home language, preschool Chinese, and bimo learning.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans:** monastic learning against state schools.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna:** temple learning for boys; ask about girls separately.
- **Dong of Qiandongnan:** singing in age groups as a teaching form.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** mosque schooling.
- **Craft and trade lines:** Zigong salt families, Shu brocade and embroidery workshops, Sichuan cooks and their masters.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Yunnanese Chinese schools in northern Thailand and Myanmar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese for Han schooling and for heritage crafts; mixed for minority home learning; thin in English except for Dai, Naxi and Tibetan cases.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Nuosu; Tibetan; Dong; Dai; Arabic terms for Hui schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan monastic learning and language-of-instruction policy are sensitive; Larung Gar has faced demolitions and access limits; cases of harm to left-behind children are sensitive; school-system law goes to Libra or Capricorn; formal schooling and universities go to Sagittarius.
