# Bashu: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Naxi; Hmong/Miao. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan families:** museum and science-center use, and how parents answer children's questions.
- **Rural Guizhou and Yunnan:** whether wonder is met in schools, markets or elders' stories; FAST's neighbors as a case.
- **Guanghan residents and Sanxingdui:** local pride against outside fascination.
- **Hubei forest communities of Shennongjia:** tellers of the wild-man stories.
- **Naxi of Lijiang:** partners and subjects of foreign collectors.
- **Miao and Buyei of Guizhou:** long the objects of others' curiosity; their own view.
- **Kham Tibetans of Diqing and Garzê:** sacred limits on climbing and digging.
- **Generations:** short-video learners against those raised on radio and almanacs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for museums, science surveys and tourism; thin for how families treat children's questions in minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Naxi; Miao; English for collectors' archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred sites and Tibetan religious limits; the displacement around FAST; Qing albums and collectors' records carry outsiders' prejudice; the Yelang idiom is a stereotype about Guizhou and belongs in research only as a stereotype to examine.
- **Held for review:** none.
