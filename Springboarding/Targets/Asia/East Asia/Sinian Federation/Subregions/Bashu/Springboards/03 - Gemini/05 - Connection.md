*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local Southwestern Mandarin; Nuosu; Tibetan; Hmong/Miao; Dong; Zhuang; Bai. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu-plain farmsteads and Chongqing hill towns:** dispersed against clustered settlement; market-day ties.
- **Huguang-descended families and older settler lines:** origin claims as a tie.
- **Hakka of Luodai and other Sichuan pockets:** their own associations and links to other Hakka.
- **Jianghan-plain lineages of Hubei:** lineage halls and genealogies.
- **Nuosu Yi:** clan ties as obligation and protection.
- **Miao, Dong, Zhuang and Bai:** festival meetings and song as tie-making.
- **Tibetan herders of Garzê and Ngawa:** ties across long distances and through monasteries.
- **Migrant workers and their left-behind families:** ties kept by phone, money and the yearly return.
- **Urban elders living alone:** isolation in Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Sichuan and Guizhou hometown associations in coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han guanxi, migration and genealogies; mixed for minority festival ties; network data for the five provinces specifically may be thin.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Nuosu; Miao; Dong; Zhuang; Bai; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ties to officials and the cost of guanxi are sensitive topics; clan networks among the Yi touch on drug and policing stories that stigmatize; genealogies may be private family property. Friendship and community belong to Aquarius; belonging to Cancer.
- **Held for review:** none.
