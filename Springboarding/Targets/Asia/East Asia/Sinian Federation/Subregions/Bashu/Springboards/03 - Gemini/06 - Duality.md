# Bashu: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Naxi; Nuosu; Lahu; Tujia. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing:** the pair inside the state's name; rivalry or partnership.
- **Daoist temple communities (Qingcheng, Heming, Wudang):** pairs as doctrine and practice.
- **Tujia of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** two recorded identities in one life.
- **Lahu of Lancang:** paired-gender ideas.
- **Naxi of Lijiang:** opposed pairs in Dongba texts.
- **Nuosu Yi:** the black-white division as a social pair, now changing.
- **Gyalrong Tibetans of Ngawa:** between Tibetan and Han worlds.
- **Bon and Buddhist Tibetans:** two traditions side by side.
- **Hani of Mojiang:** twins in public life.
- **Migrant families:** a village house and a city life; two-home lives.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Yunnanese with two homelands in northern Thailand and Myanmar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Daoism and Han art; mixed for minority dualisms; thin for twin beliefs beyond a few peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Naxi (via translation); Nuosu; Lahu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Yi black-white strata are a sensitive hierarchy and must be researched with Nuosu voices; Tibetan religion is politically sensitive; ethnic re-registration touches state policy; Dongba ritual texts may carry rules on use.
- **Held for review:** none.
