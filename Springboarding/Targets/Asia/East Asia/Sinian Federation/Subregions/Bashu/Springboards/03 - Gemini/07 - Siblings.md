# Bashu: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Classical Chinese; Na (Mosuo); Hmong/Miao; Tujia; Tibetan; Bai. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and local ideas of the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han gentry and working-class families:** Chengdu's old houses against Hankou and Chongqing riverside poor.
- **Urban only-child generation and rural multi-child families:** Chengdu, Wuhan and Chongqing against the Sichuan and Guizhou countryside.
- **Migrant households:** older siblings minding younger ones while parents work away.
- **Na (Mosuo) of Lugu Lake:** sibling-centered households.
- **Miao and Dong:** sibling words for age cohorts; origin myths.
- **Tujia of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** sibling roles at marriage.
- **Bai of Dali:** household division and seniority.
- **Nuosu Yi:** brothers within clan obligation.
- **Kham Tibetans:** households where brothers share a marriage in some areas *check*.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Sichuanese siblings working in coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han family literature and surveys; mixed for minority kinship; thin for twins.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Na; Miao; Tujia (largely lost; Chinese sources); Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-control policy and its enforcement; brother-sister origin myths and Tibetan marriage forms can be exoticized, so use the peoples' own accounts; Na family life is often sensationalized in tourism. Inheritance law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
