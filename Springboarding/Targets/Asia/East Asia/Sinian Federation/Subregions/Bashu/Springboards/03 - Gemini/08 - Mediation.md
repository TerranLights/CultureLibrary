# Bashu: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local Southwestern Mandarin; Classical Chinese and Qing archival Chinese; Nuosu; Miao (Hmub); Dong (Kam); Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain and Chongqing:** teahouse settlements and Paoge leaders before 1949; residents' committees now.
- **Hankou and Hubei river towns:** guilds and merchant brokers.
- **Tujia of Enshi and Guizhou's former chieftain lands:** the memory of chieftains as brokers.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** degu speakers and clan go-betweens.
- **Miao and Dong of Qiandongnan:** village elders, reasoning speeches and pacts.
- **Kham Tibetans of Garzê and Diqing:** religious and elder mediators.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** imams and mosque elders.
- **Rural migrants in cities:** who speaks for them in quarrels with employers and landlords.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese archives and local memoirs for Han areas; mixed for Miao, Dong and Yi; thin and sensitive for Tibetan areas.
- **Languages needed:** Standard, Classical and Qing documentary Chinese; Nuosu; Hmub; Kam; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "people's mediation" (人民调解) is a legal institution, and customary settlements such as Tibetan life-price payments touch law: send the legal side to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit). Paoge history touches organized crime; Hui and Tibetan cases touch ethnic and religious tension.
- **Held for review:** none.
