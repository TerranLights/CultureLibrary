# Bashu: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Nuosu; Hmong/Miao; Yunnan Yi varieties. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu teahouse regulars and Chongqing hill-lane neighbors:** two styles of public talk to test, not assume.
- **Wuhan street neighborhoods:** summer-night and breakfast-street talk.
- **County towns and villages against capitals:** where reputation is felt more sharply, per residents' own accounts.
- **Women's and men's talk:** test the claim that one sex gossips more; look at markets, mahjong tables and teahouses alike.
- **Nuosu and Lolopo Yi:** proverbs and talk about the dead.
- **Miao and Dong villages:** gossip in close song-sharing communities.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** talk in close religious communities.
- **Migrant workers:** home-village news by group chat.
- **Online young people:** regional teasing and rumor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chengdu teahouses and regional fiction; mixed for online gossip (deleted posts); thin for minority villages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and the local speech of Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan; Nuosu; Miao.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "rumor" is a legal category in China, and the Li Wenliang case and the Weng'an incident are sensitive; deleted online material limits research; slander law goes to Libra or Capricorn. Regional stereotypes (九头鸟, 地域黑) are named here only as objects of study.
- **Held for review:** none.
