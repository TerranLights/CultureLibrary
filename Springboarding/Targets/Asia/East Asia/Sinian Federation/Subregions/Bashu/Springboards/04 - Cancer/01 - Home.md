# Bashu: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou varieties); Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmongic; Qiang; Bai; Naxi; Tai Lue. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing city dwellers:** apartment owners against renters and those in 城中村 (urban villages).
- **Sichuan Basin farm households:** self-built two- and three-story houses against older timber-frame homes.
- **Three Gorges resettlers:** people moved uphill or out of the province, whose home was flooded.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan Plain:** lane-house neighborhoods and lakeside villages.
- **Tujia and Miao of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** the stilt house as household and as heritage.
- **Qiang and Gyalrong Tibetans:** stone houses rebuilt after the 2008 earthquake.
- **Khams and Amdo Tibetans:** village houses against herders' tents and winter homes.
- **Nuosu Yi:** the hearth-centered house, and new state-built villages.
- **Guizhou Dong, Buyei and Shui:** drum-tower villages and stone-slab houses.
- **Yunnan Bai, Naxi, Hani, Dai:** courtyard, mushroom-roof and stilt houses.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Sichuanese workers in Guangdong dormitories, whose "home" stays elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for city housing and demolition (news, surveys, film); mixed for village and minority homes, where heritage and tourism material may stand in for daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmongic and Kam for Guizhou; local dialect terms for the house.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and resettlement are politically touchy; tourism villages may present a staged home; property and tenure rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 家 意义 OR 家的观念 with 四川 OR 重庆 OR 西南, then for 三峡移民 家园 认同.
2. Watch *Last Train Home* and *Still Life* and log every scene of leaving, returning or losing a house.
3. Pull CHFS tables on homeownership and number of homes owned for the five provinces.
4. Search 上梁 仪式 with each province's name to map building rites.
5. Search Lijiang and Xijiang studies for residents' own words about home after tourism.
