## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Earthquake belt families:** Wenchuan, Beichuan, Ya'an, Luding and Jiuzhaigou, where protection means buildings and drills.
- **Chengdu Plain and Chongqing:** old brotherhood networks against today's police and neighborhood committees.
- **Wuhan and Hubei:** flood defense along the Yangtze and lakes; the 2020 lockdown as protection.
- **Qiang:** rooftop shrines and stone watchtowers.
- **Nuosu Yi:** clan solidarity and bimo rites as two layers of protection.
- **Khams and Amdo Tibetans:** protector deities, cords and guard dogs.
- **Naxi and Mosuo:** Dongba and daba ritual guardians.
- **Guizhou Miao, Dong, Buyei, Tunpu:** silver amulets, door masks and village gates.
- **Border Yunnan (Dai, Wa, Jingpo, Lisu):** fears tied to the drug trade and cross-border traffic.
- **Men as protectors:** what fathers and brothers are expected to do, to separate from mothers' protection.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Sichuanese workers on the coast relying on 老乡 networks for safety.
