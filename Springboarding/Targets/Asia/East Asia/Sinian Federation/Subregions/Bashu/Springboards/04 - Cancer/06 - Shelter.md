# Bashu: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan for herders' dwellings; Nuosu Yi; Burmese and Kokang Chinese for border refugees. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Earthquake-zone households:** Wenchuan, Beichuan, Ya'an and Luding, who lived through tents and prefabs.
- **Three river cities:** Chongqing, Wuhan and Yibin, where floods and heat drive people to shelter.
- **Urban poor and migrants:** urban-village renters in Chengdu, Chongqing, Kunming and Guiyang.
- **Relocated mountain households:** Nuosu Yi of Liangshan; Miao, Buyei and Shui in Guizhou.
- **Tibetan herders:** black tents, winter houses and settlement villages.
- **Tunpu and Gelao villagers:** stone villages as places of refuge in local memory, to check.
- **Border Yunnan (Dai, Wa, Jingpo, Kokang Chinese):** refuge across the line in both directions.
- **People without a roof:** street sleepers, those in aid stations, and those sent back to home counties.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Bashu migrants in coastal dormitories and worksite sheds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disaster and relocation programs (official reports, studies); thin and sensitive for homelessness and refugees.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Burmese for cross-border material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation, Tibetan herder settlement, the 2020 epidemic, homelessness and border refugees are politically sensitive; detention and vagrancy law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
