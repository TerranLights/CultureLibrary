# Bashu: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and other Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan (medicine and household care); Nuosu Yi; Hmongic for Miao healing. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages emptied by migration:** Sichuan Basin, Chongqing's outer counties and northeast Guizhou, where elders care for each other or alone.
- **Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan families:** hired aides and pilot insurance against kin care.
- **Hubei's Jianghan Plain:** farm families with strong local elder-care rules to check.
- **Nuosu Yi:** clan help in sickness and the crisis years of drugs and AIDS.
- **Khams and Amdo Tibetans:** monastery, Tibetan doctor and household as carers.
- **Miao, Dong and Buyei:** village healers and herbal remedies.
- **Bai, Naxi and Mosuo:** in Mosuo households, which kin care for the old.
- **Tujia and Qiang:** mountain villages with long distances to clinics.
- **Women and men as carers:** whether sons, daughters or daughters-in-law carry the load, by area.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Bashu domestic workers in coastal homes, caring at a distance for their own families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elder care (CHARLS, CLHLS, policy pilots); mixed for folk healing and minority care; thin for carers' own voices outside online posts.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmongic for healing terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and AIDS in Liangshan, the 2020 Wuhan events and Tibetan monastic life are sensitive; the legal duty to support parents goes to Libra; filial authority goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
