# Bashu: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Nuosu Yi and Tibetan for minority areas; English for overseas adoptee material (comparison only). **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Labor-export counties of Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou:** children raised by grandparents, and parents judged for leaving.
- **Northwest Hubei:** where the legend of leaving the old is tied to real caves.
- **Wuhan and Chongqing orphanages:** the infants left during birth-limit years.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** orphans of the drug and AIDS years, and clan responses.
- **Tibetans of Ganzi and Aba:** monastery and kin as fallbacks for children without parents.
- **Miao, Buyei, Dong and Shui villages:** hollow villages and parents working in the coastal provinces.
- **Third Front towns:** workers and families left when factories closed.
- **Earthquake survivors:** children orphaned in 2008 and the towns given up.
- **Mothers who leave and fathers who leave:** separate the judgment of each.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** adoptees raised abroad who search for birth families in Bashu.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for left-behind children (counts, studies, press); mixed for elder abandonment and spouses who leave; thin and painful for infant abandonment.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; English for adoptee material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-limit history, child deaths, trafficking, drugs and AIDS in Liangshan, and Third Front secrecy are all sensitive; abandonment as a crime goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
