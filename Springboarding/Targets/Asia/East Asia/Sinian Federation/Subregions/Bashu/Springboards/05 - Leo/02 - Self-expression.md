# Bashu: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Nuosu Yi, Tujia, Lisu and Tibetan for song and dress traditions. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing youth scenes:** two dialects and two music scenes; test whether their styles of speaking out differ.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan plain:** a speech and protest tradition of their own; punk as one strand.
- **Rural farming villages in all five provinces:** where 含蓄 and family pressure may weigh most; Yu Xiuhua as a test case.
- **Tujia and Miao of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** ritual laments and song duels as sanctioned channels.
- **Nuosu Yi (Liangshan) and Yunnan Yi branches; Khams Tibetans:** dress, headwear and song as display.
- **Lisu and other Christian communities of Nujiang; Hui of Yunnan:** expression shaped by faith rules.
- **Older women (square dancing) against young creators online:** generations in the same public space.
- **LGBTQ people in Chengdu and Kunming:** visible in some settings; see flags.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music, dialect comedy and online life; mixed for minority song (folklore collections, some staged); thin for private feeling and what families leave unsaid.
- **Languages needed:** Sichuanese and Wuhan speech for comedy and rap; Tujia and Nuosu for laments and songs; Lisu for church material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political expression, religious expression and LGBTQ visibility are restricted and censored; online posts vanish, so archive early; Wenchuan-earthquake grief belongs partly here and partly under Dignity.
- **Held for review:** none.
