# Bashu: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (including classical Chinese for honors records); Sichuanese for forms of address; the languages of groups seeking official recognition (Chuanqing speech, Gejia, Mosuo/Na). **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han urban workers in Wuhan, Chongqing and Chengdu:** the honor-board and model-worker tradition, and its weakening after layoffs.
- **Third Front families (Panzhihua, Shiyan, Liupanshui, Chongqing):** people brought in from elsewhere whose work may feel unsung.
- **Unclassified and contested groups: Chuanqing, Gejia, Mosuo:** recognition as a people, not as a person.
- **Yunnan's many classified peoples (Dai, Bai, Hani, Lisu, Wa, Jingpo and others):** recognized, but under names and boundaries set from outside.
- **Nuosu Yi:** standing through clan and elders, against state titles.
- **Theater and craft lineages (Sichuan opera troupes, Han opera in Wuhan):** recognition through awards and masters.
- **Rural teachers and village doctors in Guizhou and Liangshan:** frequent subjects of media honor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors (registers, press, yearbooks); mixed for ethnic recognition (scholarship exists, policy is sensitive); thin for everyday credit at home and work.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Nuosu and other minority languages for how a people names itself.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification and requests for recognition touch state policy; Tibetan Buddhist recognition of reincarnate teachers is a restricted political issue and best left to a specialist; wartime redress is diplomatic.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Search provincial government sites for 四川省劳动模范 名单 and 云南省劳动模范 名单 and compare who is honored.
5. Search CNKI for 三线建设 口述史 and 三线人 认同 for the unsung-labor strand.
