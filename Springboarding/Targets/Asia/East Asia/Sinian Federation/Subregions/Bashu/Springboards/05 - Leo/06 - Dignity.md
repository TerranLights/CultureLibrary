# Bashu: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech for daily usage; Qiang, Nuosu Yi, A-Hmao (Miao) and Tibetan for disaster, slavery-history, mission and burial sources. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Earthquake survivors of Wenchuan, Beichuan and Lushan, many of them Qiang and Tibetan:** dignity after disaster and relocation.
- **Laid-off state-factory workers in Chongqing, Wuhan, Panzhihua and Guizhou's Third Front towns:** dignity tied to work units.
- **Rural old people and allowance households in Guizhou, Yunnan and the Wuling mountains:** receiving help in view of neighbors.
- **Nuosu Yi, including descendants of formerly enslaved strata:** dignity across an old hierarchy.
- **A-Hmao (Large Flowery Miao) of northwest Guizhou and northeast Yunnan:** schooling and faith as remembered ways out of contempt.
- **Khams and Amdo Tibetans:** care of the dead and of the dying under Buddhist teaching.
- **People with disabilities, including those disabled in 2008:** public bearing and access.
- **Returned migrant workers:** how city treatment is told back home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on disaster, labor and welfare (press, policy, scholarship); mixed for minority accounts; thin for how dignity is spoken of at home.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Qiang and Tibetan for disaster areas; Nuosu for strata history; A-Hmao for Shimenkan memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school collapses in 2008 and the naming of dead students are censored; sky burial is closed to cameras by the communities; Nuosu strata history is painful; deaths in Wuhan in early 2020 belong here only with great care and are heavily censored.
- **Held for review:** none.
