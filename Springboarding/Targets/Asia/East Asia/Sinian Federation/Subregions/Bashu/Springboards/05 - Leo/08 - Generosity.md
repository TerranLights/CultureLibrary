# Bashu: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and Guizhou speech for banquet and treating words; Dai, Tibetan, Hani, Kam, Bai and Nuosu Yi for offering and feast customs. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing urban middle class and new rich:** treating and banqueting as standing.
- **Rural Han villages of the Sichuan basin and Guizhou:** gift registers and banquet obligations.
- **Hani of Honghe and Kam of Qiandongnan:** communal feasts where giving is shared by households.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna and Dehong:** religious giving to temples and monks.
- **Khams Tibetans:** monastery patronage and kin support.
- **Bai of Dali:** a formal hospitality sequence.
- **Nuosu Yi:** host's honor through slaughter and feast.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** zakat and mosque giving on their own calendar.
- **Migrant workers and their home villages:** money sent home and hometown donations, as giving within the family and back to the place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for banquets, gift registers and post-2008 philanthropy; mixed for minority feasting (tourism-shaped); thin for anonymous or private giving.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and Guizhou speech; Dai, Hani, Kam, Tibetan and Nuosu for feast and offering customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gifts to officials shade into bribery (anti-corruption rules, Libra and Capricorn); monastery giving in Garzê is politically watched; charity scandals of the 2010s left distrust that may color interviews.
- **Held for review:** none.
