*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (classical Chinese for chieftain and official records); Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Kam, Hmub, Nuosu Yi, Hani, Tujia and Tibetan for customary leaders. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kam of Guizhou and western Hubei:** elders' councils and alliances.
- **Hmub of Qiandongnan; A-Hmao of the northwest:** rotating festival leaders, church elders.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** speakers, ritual specialists and clan heads, each a different kind of leader.
- **Hani and Yi of Honghe and Chuxiong:** village ritual leaders tied to the land.
- **Tujia of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** chieftain history and today's leaders.
- **Khams Tibetans:** lay headmen and monastic teachers (see flags).
- **Han villages of the Sichuan basin and Guizhou:** village heads, Party secretaries and lineage elders.
- **Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan firms:** family founders against professional managers.
- **Women leaders:** few in office; test where they lead informally.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, state models and firm cases; mixed for minority customary leaders (good ethnography, little data); thin for leadership inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Kam, Hmub, Nuosu, Hani and Tujia for customary leaders; Tibetan for Khams.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leadership in Tibetan monasteries and the choice of religious teachers are restricted political topics; Party leadership is narrated through official memorials; formal authority and command belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
