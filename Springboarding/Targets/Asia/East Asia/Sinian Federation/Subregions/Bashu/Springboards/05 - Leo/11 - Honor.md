# Bashu: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Chongqing speech (including old brotherhood slang); Nuosu Yi, Sani Yi, Qiang, Tujia and Tibetan for epic, feud and funeral traditions. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu-plain Han and Chongqing river towns:** brotherhood honor in the countryside against the wharves.
- **Hubei, Jingzhou and Yichang:** the loyalty cult tied to the Three Kingdoms.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** clan honor, feud and settlement as a separate system.
- **Sani and other Yunnan Yi:** honor as told in women's narrative poems.
- **Khams Tibetans and Qiang of Ngawa:** epic honor, warrior and ancestor.
- **Tujia of Changyang and Enshi:** honor owed to the dead.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** honor bound to faith and community memory (see flags).
- **Martial-arts students and veterans' families:** codes taught and lived.
- **Women in all groups:** family honor and sexual reputation as a question to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, epic and literature; mixed for living codes; thin for how honor is defended today outside official honors.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Sichuanese brotherhood slang; Nuosu, Tibetan and Qiang for epics; Sani Yi for Ashima in its own form.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** brotherhood and gang codes touch organized-crime campaigns in Chongqing; Tibetan epic performance can be politically watched; the mid-19th-century Hui uprising in Yunnan (Du Wenxiu) and its memory are sensitive; feud settlement rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
