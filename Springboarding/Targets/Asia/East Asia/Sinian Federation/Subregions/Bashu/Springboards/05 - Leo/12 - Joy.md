# Bashu: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Tibetan, Hmub (Miao), Bai and Jingpo for festival joy. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and the western Sichuan basin:** contentment words (安逸, 巴适) and a city brand of happiness; test against data.
- **Chongqing:** its own night-life and river-city brand; test whether joy is named and shown differently from Chengdu.
- **Wuhan and Hubei:** spring crowds and recovery after 2020.
- **Hmub of Qiandongnan:** New Year and drum festivals as collective joy.
- **Bai of Dali; Jingpo of Dehong; Dai:** festival joy with song and dance.
- **Khams Tibetans of Kangding and Garzê:** summer festivals and horse races.
- **Rural elders:** joyful funerals and late-life contentment.
- **Young urban workers:** joy against long hours and the "lying flat" (躺平) mood.
- **People with depression:** the edge of the concept.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and city happiness branding; mixed for survey happiness by province; thin for private, quiet joy.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Hmub, Bai, Jingpo and Tibetan for festivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wuhan's 2020 experience colors spring rituals; Tibetan festivals have been limited in some years; "happiest city" rankings are partly marketing.
- **Held for review:** none.
