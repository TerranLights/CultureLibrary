# Bashu: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Hubei's Jianghuai and Gan varieties; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Hmong/Miao, Dong, Hani and Dai for community sources. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Zigong, Neijiang and Yibin salt and distilling towns: the lands' longest industrial lineage.
- Chongqing and Wuhan river ports, where carrying and dock work defined the poor man's job.
- Third Front towns (Shiyan, Panzhihua, Liupanshui): workforces moved in from elsewhere, with their own work culture.
- Labor-sending counties of east Sichuan, northern Guizhou and Enshi, where work means working away.
- Gejiu and Dongchuan miners, Han and Hui, against Hani and Yi terrace farmers nearby.
- Kham and Amdo herders with seasonal fungus harvests; Dai and Akha tea and rubber workers in Xishuangbanna.
- Dong and Miao rice farmers of Qiandongnan, whose labor exchange differs from Han wage farming.
- Nuosu Yi youth in coastal factories, as a mobile working population.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migrant labor and state industry; mixed for minority subsistence work; folk work lore sits in heritage files and ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for nearly all records; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi and Hani for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine disasters in Guizhou and Yunnan; child-labor cases involving Liangshan youth; worker unrest reporting; defense-plant history. Labor contracts and labor law go to Libra; duty and discipline to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
