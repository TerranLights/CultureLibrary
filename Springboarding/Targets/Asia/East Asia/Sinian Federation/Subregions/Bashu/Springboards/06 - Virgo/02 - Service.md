# Bashu: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese; Sichuanese and the other Southwestern Mandarin varieties; Tibetan, Dai (Tai Lue, Tai Nüa), Bai, Lisu and Hani for community service roles. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu teahouse and street-service trades against Chongqing's hot-pot and port service economy.
- Civil-service exam takers in Wuhan, Chengdu and Kunming, where public office is a career goal.
- Dai and Blang Theravada villages, where service is given to the temple.
- Kham and Amdo Tibetan households sponsoring and serving monasteries.
- Bai and Naxi hosts in the tourist economy of Dali and Lijiang, where serving guests became a trade.
- Lisu, A-Hmao and Jingpo Christian congregations, with lay offices.
- Hani and Yi villages with rotating ritual offices.
- Women from rural Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou in domestic service elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state slogans, volunteering and the hospitality industry; mixed for minority religious service; thin for domestic workers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan and Dai for monastic and temple sources; Lisu for church records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic service in Tibetan areas (Larung Gar and similar sites); church activity in Yunnan; the political use of "serve the people". Hierarchy and duty go to Capricorn; obligations owed by contract to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
