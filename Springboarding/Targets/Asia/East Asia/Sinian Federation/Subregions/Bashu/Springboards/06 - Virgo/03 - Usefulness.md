# Bashu: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing, Wuhan and Yunnan speech for the everyday insults and praise; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Naxi/Mosuo, Dong and Miao for elders' and children's roles. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Grandparents in labor-sending villages of east Sichuan, Guizhou and Enshi, raising grandchildren.
- Urban retirees of Chengdu and Wuhan, whose days center on teahouse, park and mahjong.
- Laid-off state-factory workers of Chongqing, Wuhan, Panzhihua and Shiyan.
- Mosuo households around Lugu Lake, with roles set by the matrilineal house.
- Nuosu Yi and Kham Tibetan families where children herd and fetch from an early age.
- Dong and Miao elders recognized as song and craft keepers.
- Disabled people in rural and urban settings.
- Scrap collectors and street repairers, many of them migrants from the poorer counties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elders and left-behind families (survey-heavy); mixed for minority household roles; thin for direct talk of being a burden.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Naxi/Na for Mosuo sources; Nuosu Yi and Tibetan for village life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder suicide and neglect in rural areas; the 寄死窑 legend, which can be taken as a slur on a place; disability; laid-off workers' protests. Pensions as legal entitlements go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
