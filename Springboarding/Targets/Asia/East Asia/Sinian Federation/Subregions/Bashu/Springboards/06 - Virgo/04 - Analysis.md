# Bashu: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin for everyday talk of reckoning; Tibetan for monastic scholastics; Nuosu Yi, Naxi and Shui for ritual reckoning traditions. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Rice-farming lowlands (Chengdu plain, Jianghan plain) against wheat and mixed farming in northern Hubei, a split the thinking-style studies use.
- Tech and university hubs (Chengdu high-tech zone, Wuhan's Optics Valley, Guiyang) against mountain counties.
- Exam-driven schooling in Huanggang and other Hubei counties.
- Zigong salt and Chongqing merchant families, with long accounting traditions.
- Tibetan monastic scholars, Naxi Dongba, Shui and Nuosu bimo specialists, each with their own reckoning.
- Third Front engineers' families and research towns (Mianyang).
- Hui traders of Yunnan and their own record-keeping networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science institutions and exam culture; mixed for traditional reckoning, where texts survive but readers are few; the main international school tests may not have sampled these provinces (check).
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Tibetan; Shui, Naxi and Nuosu specialists' readings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mianyang's defense research; Wuhan's virology institute; Tibetan monastic education; ritual texts held by specialists. Court records in the 巴县档案 are read here only as record keeping; their law content goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
