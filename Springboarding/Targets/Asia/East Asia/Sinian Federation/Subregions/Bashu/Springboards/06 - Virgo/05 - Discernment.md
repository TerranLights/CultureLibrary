*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Yunnan speech for market and street savvy; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi and Naxi for ritual ways of deciding; trade slang of tea, jade and antiques. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Yi and Han foragers of central Yunnan, who judge mushrooms for a living, against city buyers who rely on markets.
- Jade traders in Ruili and Tengchong, among them Han, Dai and Jingpo, where reading stone is a profession.
- Tea-mountain peoples (Hani, Blang, Jinuo, Dai, Lahu) and the Ya'an growers of Mengding, each with their own grading words.
- Distillery towns of Renhuai, Yibin and Luzhou, where palates are trained and certified.
- Tibetan households who seek a lama's divination before choices; Nuosu families who consult bimo; Naxi who consult Dongba.
- Chan and Pure Land monastics of Chengdu (Wenshu, Baoguang) and Emei, for spiritual discernment.
- Urban parents in Chengdu and Wuhan choosing matches; rural elders targeted by fraud.
