# Bashu: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese; Sichuanese for the everyday "make do" and "fussy" vocabulary; Tibetan for sacred painting and printing; Miao, Dong and Bai craft terms. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Hubei's bronze-heritage cities (Suizhou, Jingzhou), where exact casting is a civic symbol.
- Chengdu brocade and embroidery workers, many of them women, under strict craft standards.
- Kham thangka painters and Derge printers, whose exactness is tied to sacred text and image.
- Miao silversmiths and embroiderers, Dong carpenters and Bai carvers, each with their own measures of finished work.
- Distillery towns of Renhuai, Yibin and Luzhou, where process is repeated by rule.
- Sichuan and Chongqing chefs, judged on knife and heat control.
- Students under exam pressure in Hubei and Chongqing.
- Everyday speech in Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan, to see where 将就 and 讲究 each carry praise or blame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts and heritage (official, often promotional); mixed for psychology of perfectionism; thin for makers' own words on mistakes.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan for painting and printing; Hmub (Eastern Miao), Dong and Bai for craft terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic workshops in Tibetan areas; student suicide and mental illness; trade secrets of crafts and distilleries. Discipline and order go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
