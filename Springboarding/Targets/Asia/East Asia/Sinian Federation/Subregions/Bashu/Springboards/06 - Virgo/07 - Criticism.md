# Bashu: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** classical and modern Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech for scolding, teasing and satire; Nuosu Yi for verbal contest; Bai and Miao for antiphonal song. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu's storytelling and teahouse audiences, where mockery is a stage art.
- Wuhan, where bluntness is a common self-image: a claim to test, not adopt.
- Chongqing and Wuhan Cultural Revolution generations, who lived through public criticism as punishment.
- Medical and academic workers in Wuhan after 2020.
- Nuosu Yi speakers of 克智, and Bai and Miao singers whose criticism rides on song.
- Tibetan communities of Garzê and Aba, where sources on criticizing authority will be scarce and filtered.
- Online reviewers and posters, young and urban.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and literary criticism and for online complaint; thin for private family criticism; heavily filtered for political criticism.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Nuosu Yi; Bai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship of 2020 Wuhan material; Cultural Revolution violence; criticism of authority in Tibetan and Yi areas; deleted posts that survive only in archives. Defamation and speech law go to Libra; authority and obedience to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
