# Bashu: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin for illness talk; Tibetan for Sowa Rigpa; Nuosu Yi, Hmong/Miao, Dai and Naxi for healing practices. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu and Chongqing households, for everyday talk of heat, dampness and diet.
- Qichun and other Hubei herb-trading towns, where classical pharmacy is local heritage.
- Kham and Amdo Tibetans using Sowa Rigpa alongside hospitals.
- Nuosu Yi families who use bimo and suni rites with clinics; Yunnan Yi communities studied by Mueggler.
- Miao herbalists of Guizhou, Dai healers of Xishuangbanna, Naxi Dongba healing rites, Hmong ritual healers of Wenshan.
- Hui communities of Yunnan (Shadian, Weishan), to separate religious purity practice from other hygiene norms.
- Returned migrant miners with occupational disease; border towns such as Ruili.
- Qiang and Tibetan survivors of the 2008 earthquake.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for biomedicine, surveys and classical pharmacy; mixed for minority medicine (much is official codification); thin for healers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmong; Dai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** HIV, addiction and mental illness; Wuhan 2020; occupational disease claims; ritual healing knowledge held by specialists; the plague's ethnic framing. Medical law and malpractice go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
