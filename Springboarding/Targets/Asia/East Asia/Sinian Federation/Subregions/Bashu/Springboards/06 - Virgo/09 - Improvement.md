# Bashu: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands from Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Hmong (A-Hmao) and Nuosu Yi for the peoples most often targeted by "improvement" programs; Tibetan for development talk in Kham and Amdo. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Former chieftain domains (Tujia of western Hubei and Chongqing, Bouyei, Miao, the Yi of Shuixi), where improvement arrived as imposed rule.
- A-Hmao of Weining, with their own memory of mission-era schooling.
- Nuosu Yi of Liangshan, where customs-reform and relocation campaigns were run.
- Three Gorges resettlers and Guizhou relocated households, comparing their own verdicts.
- Wuhan, with its self-strengthening heritage and university-city striving.
- Daoist and martial-arts practitioners of Wudang, Qingcheng and Emei.
- Chengdu's urban consumers of body and beauty improvement.
- Kham and Amdo Tibetan herders under settlement programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state programs and their critics; mixed for affected peoples' own accounts; thin for private self-help habits outside cities.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Nuosu Yi; A-Hmao; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation and settlement of herders and minorities; the political charge of 素质 and 改造; Three Gorges criticism; mission history. Land rights and compensation law go to Libra; state authority to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
