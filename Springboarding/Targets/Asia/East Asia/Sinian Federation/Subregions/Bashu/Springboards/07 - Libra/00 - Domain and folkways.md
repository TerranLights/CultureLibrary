# Bashu: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands carried: Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; traditional for pre-1950 sources); Southwestern Mandarin varieties (Chengdu-Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming, Guiyang); Nuosu Yi, Kham and Amdo Tibetan, Qiang, Hmu and A-Hmao Miao, Kam (Dong), Tujia, Bai, Naxi and Na (Mosuo), Dai, Bouyei, Hani, Jingpo. **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are: marriage, contracts, sworn bonds, open enemies, settling quarrels, civility, the roles of the sexes, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain Han against Chongqing and the eastern hills:** two urban cultures, separate units since 1997.
- **Hubei (Jianghan Plain, Wuhan, the eastern Jianghuai-speaking counties):** the old Chu lands and a river trading city, to separate from the Sichuan basin.
- **Yunnan and Guizhou Han, among them the Tunpu (屯堡) villages of Anshun, of Ming garrison descent:** frontier Han wedding and lineage forms.
- **Hakka and "Huguang" migrant enclaves in Sichuan (Luodai):** carried-in marriage and lineage customs.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan and the Yi of Yunnan and Guizhou:** clan-based alliance and mediation, varying by region.
- **Kham, Amdo and Gyalrong Tibetans (Ganzi, Aba, Diqing):** herding, farming and monastic communities settle quarrels differently *check*.
- **Miao and Dong of southeast Guizhou:** compact-based village law.
- **Tujia (Enshi, Wuling):** wedding and lineage forms.
- **Bai, Naxi and Na of northwest Yunnan:** three neighbors with very different marriage forms.
- **Dai, Hani, Jingpo, Wa, Lisu and the Hui:** Theravada, highland and Islamic traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Sichuan and Hubei; mixed for the larger minorities; thin for the smaller peoples. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the five provinces the tracker lists.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Nuosu Yi and Tibetan for primary custom; Dong, Miao and Dai for oral law.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan and Hui topics and the 1950s "democratic reforms" in Liangshan and Kham are politically sensitive; customary knowledge held by bimo priests and elders is theirs to share.
- **Held for review:** none.
