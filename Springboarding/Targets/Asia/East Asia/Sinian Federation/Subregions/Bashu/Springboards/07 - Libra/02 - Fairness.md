# Bashu: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese, Kunming and Guiyang varieties; Hmong (Wenshan), Hani, Nuosu Yi, Kham Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain farmers against Chongqing city-dwellers:** different stakes in land and hukou reform.
- **Three Gorges relocatees (Fengjie, Wushan, Yunyang, Zigui):** a population defined by a compensation question.
- **Hani and Yi of the Ailao mountains:** water and land shared under customary rules.
- **Miao and Dong of Qiandongnan:** village leagues and collective feasts.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** clan shares at feasts and funerals.
- **Hakka of Luodai and Huguang-descended lineages:** inheritance customs carried in from elsewhere.
- **Hubei (Wuhan and the Jianghan Plain):** known for high-pressure exam schools such as Huanggang's *check*, a reference point for the gaokao debate.
- **Tibetans of Ganzi and Aba:** pasture allocation after the household-contract reforms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for attitude surveys and policy; mixed for customary sharing rules; thin for any experimental (ultimatum or dictator game) work in these lands, so far as the agent knows.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Hani and Nuosu Yi for field terms; Tibetan for pasture rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic bonus points and relocation compensation are contested; land seizures and petitions around them are politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
