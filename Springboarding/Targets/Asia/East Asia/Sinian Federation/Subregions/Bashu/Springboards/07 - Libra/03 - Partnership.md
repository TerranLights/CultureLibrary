*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan varieties; Na (Mosuo), Jinghpaw (Jingpo), Kam, Kham Tibetan; Chinese as used by the Hui. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zigong and the salt towns:** shareholding partnerships with a long paper trail.
- **Chengdu and Chongqing as cities:** paired, rival and now formally partnered.
- **Wuhan:** industrial and commercial partnership; fiction of ordinary marriages.
- **Na (Mosuo) of Lugu Lake, against their Pumi, Yi and Naxi neighbors:** a visiting union beside married households.
- **Miao and Dong of southeast Guizhou:** song-based courtship and sworn friendship.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** marriage as a written religious contract.
- **Jingpo of Dehong:** lineage alliance through marriage.
- **Han migrant lineages (Huguang, Hakka of Luodai):** native-place bonds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han business history and marriage statistics; mixed for sworn friendship and godparent ties; thin for some smaller peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Na and Jinghpaw for field terms; Arabic loanwords in Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mosuo partnership has been sensationalized by tourism, so the Na's own accounts should come first; Hui religious practice is a sensitive area.
- **Held for review:** none.
