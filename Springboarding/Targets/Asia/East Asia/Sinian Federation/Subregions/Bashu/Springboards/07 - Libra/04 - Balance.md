# Bashu: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Kham Tibetan, Naxi, Dai (Tai Lue), Nuosu Yi. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others, and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu against Chongqing and Wuhan:** a leisure-city image against faster industrial cities.
- **Daoist communities of Qingcheng and Wudang:** balance as religious discipline.
- **Kham and Gyalrong Tibetans:** a separate medical system of balance.
- **Naxi of Lijiang and Dai of Xishuangbanna:** two nature-centered traditions, one scriptural, one Buddhist and village-based.
- **Yangtze river communities (Yichang, Wanzhou, Fuling):** dams and fishing bans as lived trade-offs.
- **Yi of Chuxiong and Liangshan:** cosmology claims and ritual.
- **Rural migrants working in the cities:** whose hours give a contrast to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical, medical and water-engineering sources; mixed for time use and ecological practice; thin for the Yi calendar claims.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Tibetan; Naxi (Dongba pictographs, through specialists); Dai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Dongba and bimo ritual knowledge belongs to its keepers; Tibetan medicine is entangled with monastic institutions.
- **Held for review:** none.
