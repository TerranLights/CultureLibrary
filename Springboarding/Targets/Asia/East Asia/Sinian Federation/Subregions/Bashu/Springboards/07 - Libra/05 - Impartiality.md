# Bashu: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Kham and Amdo Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Hmu Miao; Chinese as used by the Hui. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing urban middle class:** allocation by lottery and exam.
- **Lineage villages of Hubei and eastern Sichuan:** kin power and village office.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** clan loyalty against a mediator's standing.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans:** religious arbiters between herding groups.
- **Miao and Dong of Guizhou:** elders' councils in small kin-dense villages.
- **Hui of Yunnan (Kunming, Weishan, Shadian):** religious mediation.
- **Yunnan border peoples (Dai, Jingpo, Wa):** cross-border kin networks and local office.
- **Tujia of Enshi:** whether descent from the old chieftain (tusi) families still carries weight in local office *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical stories, statutes and press scandals; mixed for customary arbiters; thin for survey data cut by province.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Tibetan and Nuosu Yi for arbiters' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases are politically framed; Tibetan and Hui religious authority is a sensitive area.
- **Held for review:** none.
