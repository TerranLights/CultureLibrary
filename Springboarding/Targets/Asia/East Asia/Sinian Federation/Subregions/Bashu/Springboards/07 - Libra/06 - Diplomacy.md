## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing and Wuhan:** wartime capital and treaty port, each with a diplomatic history to compare.
- **Dali Bai and the Nanzhao-Dali heritage:** a former kingdom with its own treaty record.
- **Shuixi Yi (northwest Guizhou) and Liangshan Yi:** lords who negotiated with the court, against communities long outside direct rule *check*.
- **Gyalrong and Kham Tibetan chieftaincies:** a buffer zone's inter-chief diplomacy.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna and Dehong:** princedoms between two courts.
- **Jingpo, Wa and Lisu of the border:** kin spread across the Myanmar line.
- **Dong and Miao villages of Guizhou:** inter-village hosting customs.
- **Tujia of Enshi and southeast Chongqing:** the Tangya chieftaincy's descendants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and modern state diplomacy; mixed for chieftain-era bargaining; thin for everyday politeness research in local dialects and minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Dai and Burmese for border and chieftaincy sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Myanmar border, Tibetan chieftaincy history and the 1945-1949 civil war are politically sensitive; the conquest legends read differently among Yi and Dai.
- **Held for review:** none.
