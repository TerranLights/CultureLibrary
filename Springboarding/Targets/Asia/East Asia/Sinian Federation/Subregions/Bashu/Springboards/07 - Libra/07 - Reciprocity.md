# Bashu: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Guizhou varieties; Kham Tibetan, Dai (Tai Lue), Bai; Chinese as used by the Hui. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Han of the Sichuan basin and Chongqing:** banquet and gift-money circuits.
- **Rural Guizhou (Han, Miao, Bouyei, Gelao):** where official rules now cap the circuit.
- **Hubei's Jianghan Plain villages:** gift lists among lineage villages.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna:** reciprocity routed through the temple.
- **Bai of Dali:** host-guest rites.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** exchanges across a religious line.
- **Kham Tibetans of Ganzi:** scarves, hospitality and long-distance trade ties.
- **Wenchuan and Beichuan (Qiang, Tibetan and Han):** recipients of paired aid.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gift economies in Han villages and for policy; mixed for minority labor exchange; thin for Hui and Tibetan exchange customs in Chinese-language scholarship.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan, Dai and Bai for field terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "gratitude" campaigns after Wenchuan are politically framed; family gift registers are private documents needing consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
