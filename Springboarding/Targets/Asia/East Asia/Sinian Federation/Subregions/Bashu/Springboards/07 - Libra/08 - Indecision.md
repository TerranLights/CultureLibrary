*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young urban adults in Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan and Kunming:** exam, career and marriage choices with family input.
- **Rural Han households:** how far day-choosing and temple lots enter wedding and building plans.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** which decisions are referred to bimo divination, and by whom.
- **Shui of Sandu:** a written day-choosing tradition.
- **Kham Tibetans:** whether and which choices are taken to lamas for divination.
- **Wuhan Buddhist lay community:** temple chance-practices.
- **Three Gorges counties (Yichang, Wanzhou, Fengjie):** people who lived through a decision deferred for decades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for classical and policy history and for urban psychology; thin for how divination is used to settle choices today.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Nuosu Yi and Shui script through specialists; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some divination is officially discouraged as "superstition", so people may under-report it; bimo and Shui knowledge belongs to its keepers; the Three Gorges vote is politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
