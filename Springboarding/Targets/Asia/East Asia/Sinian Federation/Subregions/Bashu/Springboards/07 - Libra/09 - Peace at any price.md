# Bashu: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan varieties; Kham and Gyalrong Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu's old gentry households and today's families:** the novel's household against present-day talk of "boundaries".
- **Wuhan and the Hubei plain:** a novelist's account of family silence; the "Mr. Fine-Fine" story's home ground.
- **Kham and Gyalrong Tibetans:** settlement by payment between families, where it is practiced *check*.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** feud endings brokered between clans, to set against the Tibetan case.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** community choices after past violence (the Shadian memory), as Hui sources tell them.
- **Rural women in migrant-sending counties:** whether violence at home is reported where many adults work away.
- **Chongqing's school-age youth:** bullying and bystanders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and classical history; mixed for mediation and domestic-violence statistics; thin for blood-price practice outside specialist studies.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Tibetan for settlement customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wang Jingwei, land-reform violence, Shadian, Tibetan customary settlement and the Lhamo case are all politically or personally sensitive; victims' names and families need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
