# Bashu: The domain, death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (working lands: Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script); Southwestern Mandarin varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin and Gan in eastern Hubei; Yi, Tibetan, Hmong-Mien, Tai, Bai, Naxi, Kam-Sui and Tujia languages. **Concept in one line:** how a people faces death, power and what is hidden: its death ways, sex ways, inheritance, shared resources and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu-plain (Shu) Han against Chongqing and eastern Sichuan (Ba) Han: plain against river-port and mountain life.
- Hubei Han: the Wuhan and Jianghan plain; eastern Hubei (Jianghuai speech) and the southeast (Gan speech) differ from the Southwestern Mandarin core.
- Descendants of the Qing "Huguang fills Sichuan" migrations and Hakka towns near Chengdu (Luodai): lineage and funeral forms to separate.
- Yunnan and Guizhou Han, including Anshun's Tunpu (Ming garrison descendants).
- Hui Muslims of Yunnan: burial and inheritance follow Islamic practice.
- Tibetans of Garzê, Ngawa and Diqing; the Qiang; the Gyalrong.
- Nuosu Yi of Liangshan against Yunnan's Yi groups (Sani, Lolop'o and others).
- Na (Mosuo), Naxi, Bai, Dai, Hani, Lisu, Lahu, Wa, Jingpo.
- Miao (eastern Guizhou Hmub and the Western Hmong of the three-province borderland), Dong, Bouyei, Shui, Gelao, Yao, Zhuang (Wenshan).
- Tujia of western Hubei and southeastern Chongqing.
- Christians among the Lisu and A-Hmao; Buddhist and Daoist lay networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han gazetteers, Qing archives, the Yi, Naxi and Na; mixed for the Miao, Dong and Tujia; thin for smaller border peoples. Bashu's exact lands are still to be settled (the five provinces are a working list); these sheets are written for the core lands. Federation-wide surveys need provincial breakdowns.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and the local Mandarin varieties; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; Naxi; Hmong-Mien; Tai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan areas; the 1975 Shadian events; the Great Leap famine; Cultural Revolution violence; the 2008 school collapses; COVID-19 in Wuhan. Some Dongba and bimo rites are for initiates; Na walking marriage is exoticized in tourism.
- **Held for review:** none.
