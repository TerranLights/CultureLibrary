# Bashu: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Nuosu and other Yi languages; Tibetan; Western Hmong and Hmub; Naxi; Tai. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead and what death means, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu-plain Han, where Daoist priests "open the road" (开路) for the dead, against Chongqing and eastern Sichuan wakes: two Han funeral idioms to separate.
- Hubei Han of the Jianghan plain against the eastern and southeastern dialect zones: different mourning songs (孝歌).
- Tujia of Enshi and Changyang: a dance at the bier sets grief in a different key.
- Hui Muslims: swift burial, no coffin, mosque-led rites.
- Nuosu Yi (cremation customary *check*) against Yunnan Yi groups that bury.
- Tibetans and Gyalrong; the Qiang, whose shibi priests lead funerals.
- Western Hmong and eastern Hmub Miao: different chants, different festivals of the dead.
- Naxi and Na; Bai; Dai (Theravada practice); Dong; Bouyei (Mo scriptures).
- Christians among the Lisu and A-Hmao; unregistered and registered congregations differ.
- Families of the 2008 earthquake dead and of the famine years: grief shaped by mass death.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han ritual, Chu and Han archaeology and Yi texts; thinner for Dong, Bouyei and border peoples; hospice data new and patchy.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Nuosu Yi (Yi script); Tibetan; Hmong; classical Chinese for Chu and Han texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine deaths, Cultural Revolution deaths, Tibetan self-immolations in Ngawa, the 2008 school collapses, COVID-19 deaths in Wuhan; sky-burial photography is resented; some bimo and Dongba rites are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Search Bilibili for 坐夜 四川 and 跳丧 恩施 for current funeral footage.
5. Search the Chongqing and Hubei provincial yearbooks for suicide and accidental-death tables.
