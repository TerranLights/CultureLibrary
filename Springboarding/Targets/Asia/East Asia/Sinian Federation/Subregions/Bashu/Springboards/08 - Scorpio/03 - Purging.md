# Bashu: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Tai Lü; Tujia and Hmub ritual vocabularies (mostly published in Chinese). **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Rural Han of Sichuan and Chongqing with their ritual masters (端公): exorcism by hired specialist.
- Tujia with their own priests (梯玛) and Nuo vows: cleansing tied to lineage altars.
- Nuosu Yi: bimo rites against defilement and curses; clan-level cleansing.
- Hui of Yunnan: ritual washing and halal food as daily purity.
- Tibetans and the Pumi: smoke offerings and mountain-god rites.
- Dai: Theravada merit, water and temple washing.
- Miao and Dong: where gu accusations fall, and the women who carry them.
- Urban Chengdu and Wuhan wellness culture: detox, dampness and diet talk among the young.
- Party members and officials: the bureaucratic purge as its own world.
- Chongqing families caught on both sides of the 2009-2011 campaign.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Daoist, Nuo and Yi ritual and for anti-corruption bulletins; mixed for folk medicine; thin and hard to reach for political purges.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and classical Chinese; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; Tai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Party purges, the Cultural Revolution in Yunnan and the Chongqing campaign; gu accusations harm living women; some exorcism rites are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
