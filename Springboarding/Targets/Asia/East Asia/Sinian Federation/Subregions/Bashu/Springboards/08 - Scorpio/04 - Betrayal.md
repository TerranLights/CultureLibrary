## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu and Chongqing city dwellers: talk of 出轨 and 小三 in a fast-changing marriage market.
- Rural Sichuan and Guizhou couples split by migrant work: long separations and suspicion.
- Paoge descendants and the older street code against "letting your brothers down".
- Party families who remember denouncing kin in the Mao years.
- Bai of Dali and Yi of Chuxiong: rival tellings of the same treachery legend.
- Nuosu Yi clans: betrayal across clan lines and how it starts feuds.
- Na (Mosuo): a different frame for fidelity.
- Tibetans of Garzê, where fraternal polyandry has been practiced *check*: what counts as faithlessness.
- Tujia and Ba descendants who claim Linjun as ancestor.
- Hui of Yunnan, whose memories include promises broken in the Qing wars.
