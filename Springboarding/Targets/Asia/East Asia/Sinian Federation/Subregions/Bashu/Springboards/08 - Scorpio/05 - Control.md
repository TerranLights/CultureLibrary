# Bashu: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin (Chengdu speech especially); Na (Mosuo) and Nuosu Yi; Qiang; Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chengdu households, where the 耙耳朵 husband is a running joke, against Chongqing households: compare who holds the purse.
- Hubei's Jianghan plain and its flood-prone villages: control of water as daily life.
- Rural Guizhou and Yunnan families with grandparents raising children while parents work far away.
- Na (Mosuo): household control by women, a sharp internal contrast.
- Nuosu Yi under bride-price and funeral campaigns.
- Qiang of Beichuan and Wenchuan, heirs to the Yu legend.
- Tibetan monasteries under state management committees: control from outside, a sensitive case.
- Dai and Hani rice farmers who share water by custom.
- Hui communities with mosque-centered self-management.
- Young urban professionals in Chengdu and Wuhan: parental control and "chicken-blood" parenting (鸡娃).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water control, family novels and urban history; mixed for household-decision data; thin and sensitive for surveillance and grid management.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Na; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; Qiang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance, epidemic controls, monastery management, campaigns that reshape minority customs.
- **Held for review:** none.
