# Bashu: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin (Chongqing and Chengdu speech); Wuhan speech; Nuosu Yi; Kam (Dong); Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Chongqing's river-port and porter culture, where 耿直 is a badge, against Chengdu's teahouse sociability: two styles of earning trust.
- Wuhan's merchant migrants and their native-place ties.
- Zigong salt families and other old merchant towns.
- Rural Sichuan and Hubei villages, where kin and fictive kin set the circle of trust.
- Nuosu Yi clans: oaths between clans and with the state.
- Dong and Miao villages of eastern Guizhou with sworn leagues.
- Hui of Yunnan: trade networks along caravan routes.
- Tibetans in Garzê and Ngawa: trust in monasteries against trust in officials.
- Christians, Buddhists and Daoists as communities of trust.
- Migrant workers far from home who must rely on bosses and fellow villagers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history, classical stories and national surveys; mixed for minority oath traditions; thin for current distrust between peoples, which is sensitive.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and classical Chinese; Nuosu Yi; Kam.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Weng'an case; the 2020 Wuhan events; trust between Han and Tibetans or Yi; Paoge memory touches organized crime.
- **Held for review:** none.
