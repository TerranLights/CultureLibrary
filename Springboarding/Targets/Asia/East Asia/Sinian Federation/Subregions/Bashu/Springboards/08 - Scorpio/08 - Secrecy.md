# Bashu: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin, including Paoge argot; Sui (Shui) ritual vocabulary; Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy included.
