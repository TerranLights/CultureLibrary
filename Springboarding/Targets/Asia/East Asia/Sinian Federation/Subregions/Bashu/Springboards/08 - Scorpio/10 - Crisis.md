# Bashu: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Wuhan speech; Nuosu Yi; Qiang; Tai Lü. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Qiang, Tibetans and Han of the Longmen Shan fault: repeated quakes and relocation.
- Yi and Han of Ludian and other Yunnan quake zones.
- Hubei's Yangtze and Han river plains, where floods recur.
- Chongqing's older residents with wartime bombing memories.
- Sichuan basin farming families whose elders lived through the famine.
- Wuhan residents, medical staff and the bereaved after 2020.
- Hui of Shadian and southern Yunnan.
- Liangshan Nuosu and Xishuangbanna Dai families hit by drugs and AIDS.
- Former sent-down youth in Yunnan and their home cities.
- Laid-off industrial workers of Chongqing, Wuhan and Third Front towns.
