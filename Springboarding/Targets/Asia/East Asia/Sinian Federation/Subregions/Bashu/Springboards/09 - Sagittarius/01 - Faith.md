# Bashu: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan, Dai (Tai Lü), Nuosu Yi, Bai and Kam (Dong) terms; Hui Islamic vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han lay worshippers** at temples in Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan against **small-town and village practice** in eastern Sichuan and the Hubei hills.
- **Registered and unregistered Protestants** in the big cities, and **old Catholic villages** in Sichuan, Guizhou and Dêqên (Cizhong).
- **Hui** of Yunnan against Hubei's smaller Hui communities.
- **Tibetan monastics and lay pilgrims** in Garzê, Ngawa and Dêqên.
- **Dai** (Theravada), **Bai** (*benzhu*), **Dong** (Sama), **Nuosu Yi** (bimox), **Lisu** and **A-Hmao** (mission-era Protestant histories): each keeps faith through different specialists.
- **Generations and party members:** declared, hidden and secular faith (in the nation, in science).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Many site studies and ethnographies; few reliable counts of the faithful by province.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese (inscriptions); Tibetan, Nuosu Yi and Lisu for scripture and hymn sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** declaring faith can carry costs for party members, teachers and students; Tibetan, Hui and house-church topics are sensitive, so protect informants. COVID-19 Wuhan materials are censored and contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
