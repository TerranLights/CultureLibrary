# Bashu: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Naxi, Tibetan and Nuosu Yi sources; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu's leisure culture against Chongqing and Wuhan's work-and-trade cities:** contentment, striving and their local vocabularies.
- **Migrant-sending counties** (eastern Sichuan, northern Chongqing, Bijie) against the cities they work in: meaning at a distance from family.
- **Third Front towns** (Panzhihua, Shiyan, Liupanshui): sacrifice and its aftermath across generations.
- **Naxi, Tibetan, Nuosu Yi and Hui communities:** each has its own written or oral account of the good life and the next world.
- **Generations:** reform-era strivers against "lying flat" youth; retirees in CHARLS samples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature and history are rich; well-being data exist but rarely ask directly about meaning, and provincial samples are small.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Sichuanese for oral and video sources; Naxi, Tibetan and Nuosu Yi for the minority corpora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide (the Naxi tradition; rural suicide), depression and "lying flat" are sensitive topics; online posts are often deleted, so archive them early.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Read 天问 and 前赤壁赋 in annotated editions and list the questions of meaning each raises.
