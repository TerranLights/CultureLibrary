# Bashu: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi and Hmub (Qiandong Miao) sources. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Chongqing intellectuals** against **Wuhan's** revolutionary and student traditions.
- **Migrants** from rural Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou: movement, registration and return.
- **Nuosu Yi:** former caste ranks and how each tells its "liberation."
- **Tibetans of Kham and Amdo:** freedom tied to monasteries, land and self-rule.
- **Qiandongnan Miao:** uprising memory.
- **Hui and Christians:** freedom of worship as each group frames it.
- **Han newcomers in Dali and Lijiang** against **Bai and Naxi** residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History and commemoration are rich; present-day attitudes are hard to survey honestly, and self-censorship shapes answers.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan and Nuosu Yi for minority accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan self-rule, house churches, Hui mosques, Li Wenliang and 1989 in Chengdu are censored and risky for informants. Rights and statutes go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
