# Bashu: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Sichuanese and other Southwestern Mandarin; classical Tibetan; Nuosu Yi and Hmub (Qiandong Miao) oral genres. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban news readers** in Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan and Kunming against **rural households** relying on WeChat groups and village loudspeakers.
- **Tibetan monastic scholars** in Garzê and Ngawa, for whom truth is a matter of trained debate.
- **Nuosu Yi** oath and ordeal customs; **Miao** and **Hani** singers as keepers of history.
- **Tujia and other chieftaincy descendants** in Enshi and northern Guizhou, with family histories to set beside official ones.
- **Hui** communities and their religious teachers.
- **Earthquake-zone parents** (Beichuan, Dujiangyan) and **Wuhan residents after 2020**: groups with their own account of a disputed event.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Classical and Tibetan philosophical sources are rich; surveys of trust in news and science are national, with thin provincial detail.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; classical Tibetan; Nuosu Yi and Hmub for oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wenchuan school deaths, COVID-19 origins, the Cultural Revolution in Chongqing and Tibetan history are censored and risky; protect informants and archive web pages early.
- **Held for review:** none.
