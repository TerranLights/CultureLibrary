# Bashu: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi, Tujia, Hani and Dai terms (mostly via Chinese renderings). **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Han** in Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan, from party members to temple-goers, and **rural Han** in eastern Sichuan and the Hubei hills.
- **Tujia** of Enshi and southeastern Chongqing, who claim Ba ancestry.
- **Tibetan Buddhists and Bön followers**, and **Nuosu Yi**, with distinct beliefs about souls and spirits.
- **Dai** and **Hani** spirit beliefs alongside or apart from Buddhism.
- **Miao**, the subject of others' beliefs (gu), as distinct from their own.
- **Hui** and **Christians:** creeds held against the folk-belief majority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore collections and ethnography; survey data on belief are national with thin provincial samples, and atheism is the official norm, so answers may be guarded.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; local Mandarin for tales and online channels.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" and "cult" labels can carry penalties; gu accusations stigmatize Miao women; Bön and Tibetan belief topics are monitored.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Find the Sichuan, Hubei, Yunnan and Guizhou volumes of 中国民间故事集成 and 谚语集成 in a library catalog.

3. Search 丰都 鬼城 信仰 and 九歌 楚巫 for underworld and Chu-belief studies.
4. Collect provincial 反邪教 exhibition materials to see which beliefs are labeled "cult."
5. Search 蛊 苗族 污名 for studies of the accusation and its effects.
