## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing and eastern Sichuan** against **the Chengdu plain**: the 耿直 self-image against a reputation for ease, to be measured, not assumed.
- **Wuhan**: a revolutionary and student city with its own martyr sites.
- **Tibetans of Ngawa and Garzê**: vows, monastic discipline and protest.
- **Hui** of Yunnan, **Catholics** of Guizhou and Sichuan, **Protestants** in house churches: faith held under pressure.
- **Honghu and the Jianghan lake country**, with its revolutionary memory; **Nu, Lisu and Tibetan villagers** in dam disputes.
- **Party members** taught 理想信念 against citizens who keep quiet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for commemorated martyrs and heroes; thin for living people's own stands, which are often unsafe to state.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan; Chongqing and Chengdu speech for oral and online sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** self-immolation, Shadian, house churches and protests are censored and risky to research in the field; nationalism topics can draw online harassment.
- **Held for review:** none.
