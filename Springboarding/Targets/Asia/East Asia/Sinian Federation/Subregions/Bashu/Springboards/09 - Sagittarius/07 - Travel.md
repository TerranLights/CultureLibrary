# Bashu: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin (incl. Yunnan and Wuhan usage); Tibetan; Hui and Miao usage. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Migrant-sending counties** (eastern Sichuan, northern Chongqing, Bijie, Enshi) and their returnees.
- **Three Gorges resettlers** in new towns and in other provinces.
- **Tibetan herders of Garzê and Ngawa**: seasonal moves and pilgrimage circuits, as they describe them.
- **Hui** of Yunnan: Hajj and trade networks.
- **Western Yunnan border towns** (Tengchong, Ruili): trade travel abroad.
- **Miao, Dong, Naxi, Mosuo and Bai villages** that host mass tourism, against villages that do not.
- **Urban Han** in Chengdu, Wuhan and Kunming as tourists themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Travel writing, migration statistics and tourism studies are plentiful; minority hosts' own views are thinner.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan; English and French for foreign travelers' accounts; local Mandarin for oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Three Gorges resettlement, left-behind children and Tibetan pilgrim travel are sensitive; foreign researchers' access to Garzê and Ngawa may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
