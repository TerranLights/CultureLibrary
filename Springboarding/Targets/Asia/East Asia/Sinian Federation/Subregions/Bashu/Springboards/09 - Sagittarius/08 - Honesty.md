## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old merchant towns** (Zigong, Hankou) against **new e-commerce sellers** in Chengdu and Wuhan.
- **Tourist destinations** (Lijiang, Dali, Jiuzhaigou, Emei, Enshi) where hosts and visitors meet as strangers.
- **Border traders** in Ruili and Tengchong: Han, Dai, Jingpo and Burmese dealers.
- **Tibetan collectors and Han buyers** in Garzê's fungus trade.
- **Hui food traders** and their community oversight.
- **Exam towns and schools:** parents, teachers and students in high-stakes testing.
- **Rural markets** with periodic fairs against city supermarkets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature, press and complaint platforms are rich; behavioral measures are scarce at the provincial level.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Tibetan; Burmese for some border sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases and exam protests are sensitive; blacklists name real people, so cite patterns, not names; tourism scams can stigmatize whole places, so report what each side says.
- **Held for review:** none.
