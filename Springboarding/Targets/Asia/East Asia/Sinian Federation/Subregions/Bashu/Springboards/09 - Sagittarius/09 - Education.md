## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubei's exam-prep centers** (Huanggang, Wuhan) against **Chengdu and Chongqing** school cultures (to be measured, not assumed).
- **Mountain counties** in western Guizhou, Liangshan, Zhaotong and Enshi: boarding schools, substitute teachers, distance.
- **Tibetan students** in Garzê and Ngawa: monastic learning, state schools and vocational routes.
- **Nuosu Yi:** bilingual streams; **Dong and Miao:** heritage in school.
- **Hui:** mosque education beside state schooling.
- **Girls** in poor counties; **first-generation university students** in the provincial capitals.
- **Third Front and university towns** (Mianyang's research city; Shiyan) with their own schooling histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. University histories, yearbooks and education research are abundant; minority families' own views are thinner.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan and Nuosu Yi for bilingual-education sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan-language schooling, monastic education and Hui religious schooling are politically sensitive; school-closure and boarding-school debates touch child welfare.
- **Held for review:** none.
