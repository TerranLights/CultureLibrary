# Bashu: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Hui Islamic vocabulary (Chinese, Arabic and Persian loans); Tibetan; Lisu for church sources. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hui currents** in Yunnan (Gedimu, Yihewani, Salafiyya) and in Hubei.
- **Khampa lay herders** and the monastic reformers who preach to them.
- **Lisu, A-Hmao and other minority Protestant churches** with mission-era rules.
- **Han house churches and registered churches** in Chengdu, Wuhan and Kunming, and the groups each calls heretical.
- **Confucian-revival families** choosing classics schools.
- **Older residents** who lived through the Cultural Revolution in Chongqing and Wuhan.
- **Daoist and folk-religion villages**, often on the receiving end of purity campaigns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Historical and ethnographic sources exist; current groups are mostly visible only through state anti-cult sources and exile media, both partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Arabic and Persian loanwords in Hui usage; Tibetan; Lisu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** extremely sensitive: 邪教 cases (including Falun Gong and Eastern Lightning), Hui sect politics and Tibetan reform campaigns carry legal risk for informants; the word "fundamentalist" is itself a charge, so report who uses it of whom.
- **Held for review:** none.
