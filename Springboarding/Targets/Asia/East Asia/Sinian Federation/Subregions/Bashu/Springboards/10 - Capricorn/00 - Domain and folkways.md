# Bashu: Domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (working lands: Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin (Chengdu-Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming, Guiyang speech); Nuosu Yi, Khams and Amdo Tibetan, Hmu and other Miao languages, Kam (Dong), Bai, Naxi, Tai Lü. **Concept in one line:** authority and duty: who commands, who obeys, and what is owed (with career, status, reputation, and the age, time, rank, order and power ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu plain (Shu) vs. Chongqing and eastern Sichuan (Ba):** the old twin of the name, with distinct urban registers.
- **Hubei:** Wuhan's wharf and industrial city; the Jianghan plain's Chu heritage; Jianghuai- and Gan-speaking east and southeast.
- **Yunnan and Guizhou Han:** Kunming and Guiyang; the Tunpu (屯堡) garrison descendants of Anshun.
- **Hakka enclaves of Sichuan** (Luodai, Dongshan): a separate settler lineage pattern.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan; other Yi (Lolopo, Sani):** strata and clans vs. village offices.
- **Tibetans of Kham (Garzê, Dêqên) and Amdo (Ngawa); Gyalrong; Qiang:** chiefdoms, monasteries, ritual specialists.
- **Tujia (Enshi, southeast Chongqing), Miao, Dong, Bouyei, Shui, Gelao:** elders, compacts, former chieftaincies.
- **Bai, Naxi, Mosuo, Dai, Hani, Jingpo, Wa, Lisu; Hui of Yunnan:** very different routes to command.
- Diaspora only as comparison: Yunnanese communities of northern Thailand and Myanmar; Hmong abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Han regional cultures in Chinese; mixed for minorities, where much comes from 1950s state classification surveys and later state framing. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled (the provinces above are a working list).
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (incl. classical), Sichuanese and Wuhan idiom; Nuosu Yi, Tibetan; Miao, Dong and Tai sources mostly through Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan areas (monastic authority, protests); the 2008 Wenchuan school collapses; Chongqing's 2009-12 campaigns; Wuhan 2020; poverty and drug framings of Liangshan; some ritual knowledge (Shui masters, bimo, dongba) may be held by specialists.
- **Held for review:** none.
