**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Sichuan-Chongqing, Wuhan and Guiyang speech; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmu, Tujia and Hui terms through Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Labor-exporting villages in Sichuan, Chongqing and Guizhou vs. the provincial capitals:** duty carried at a distance, through remittances and grandparents raising children.
- **Third Front settler communities (Panzhihua, Shiyan, Liupanshui):** work-unit duty, often among families from other provinces.
- **Jianghan plain and east Hubei:** Chu-era texts and lineage halls; check whether lineage obligation runs stronger than in migrant-settled Sichuan.
- **Nuosu clans:** obligation organized by clan rather than village.
- **Hmu and Dong villages:** ancestral feasts and rotating hosts.
- **Tujia:** communal labor and former chieftain levies.
- **Khampa and Gyalrong Tibetans:** monastic and pilgrimage duties.
- **Hui:** religious obligations inside a Han-majority province.
- Diaspora only as comparison: Hmong families abroad, for clan duty kept across borders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for filial duty and wartime service in Chinese; mixed for minority obligations, often recorded as "custom" by outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for the slips; Nuosu for funeral scriptures; Tibetan for pilgrimage guides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime and Third Front memory is curated by the state; bimo texts may be family-held; left-behind children is a charged public topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
