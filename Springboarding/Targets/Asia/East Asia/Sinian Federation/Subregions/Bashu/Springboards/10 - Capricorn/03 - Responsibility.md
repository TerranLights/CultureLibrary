## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu-plain farmers vs. Three Gorges resettled families (Wanzhou, Fengjie, Yichang):** chosen vs. imposed reassignment of responsibility for land and livelihood.
- **Wuhan's urban public:** a city with its own on-air accountability rituals.
- **Earthquake belt (Wenchuan, Beichuan, Lushan, Ludian):** Qiang, Tibetan and Han communities who lived the blame debates.
- **Guizhou's labor-exporting counties:** responsibility split between absent parents, grandparents and schools.
- **Nuosu clans and Khampa groups:** collective liability.
- **Hani, Yi and Dai rice-farming villages:** shared water duties.
- **Zigong and other salt and mining towns:** enterprise liability with long local roots.
- **Guiyang's Yangming revival:** a state- and scholar-led heritage of answerability.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official accountability and the rural reforms; mixed for minority customary liability; sensitive episodes are documented mostly outside mainland channels.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; classical Chinese for Wang Yangming; Tibetan and Nuosu for customary terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wenchuan school collapses and those who investigated them; Bijie and left-behind children; customary compensation is officially discouraged, so informants may be guarded. Ages of criminal responsibility belong to the Law sheet.
- **Held for review:** none.
