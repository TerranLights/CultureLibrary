## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-school towns (Huanggang, county high schools in Sichuan and Guizhou) vs. Chengdu's reputation for ease:** test the leisure image against school regimes.
- **Factory districts (Chengdu and Chongqing electronics, Wuhan auto plants, Shiyan):** workplace discipline among migrants.
- **Han Buddhist and Daoist centers (Emei, Xindu, Qingcheng, Wudang):** precepts and martial practice.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna and Dehong:** temple novitiate.
- **Khampa and Gyalrong Tibetans:** monastic rule.
- **Hui of Yunnan (Najiaying, Shadian, Weishan):** fasting and scripture study.
- **Nuosu:** bimo apprenticeship.
- **Miao, Dong and Bouyei:** check how children are corrected in village life, from their own accounts.
- **Military families and veterans' communities in Kunming and Chongqing.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schooling and work; mixed on home correction (surveys exist; local nuance thin); religious discipline is documented mostly by its own institutions and by outside ethnographers.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan; Tai Lü for temple texts; Arabic and Persian terms in Hui study.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic life in Tibetan areas (expulsions and limits on monk numbers); Hui religious schooling under recent restrictions; corporal punishment of children; factory suicides.
- **Held for review:** none.
