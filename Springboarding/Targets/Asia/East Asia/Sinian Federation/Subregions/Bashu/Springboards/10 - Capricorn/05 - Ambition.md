# Bashu: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Sichuan-Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Tengchong and other Yunnan Han speech; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-driven county towns vs. Chengdu's leisure image:** test whether 安逸 talk masks fierce striving.
- **Chongqing's port and industrial migrants vs. Wuhan's graduate-heavy city:** different ladders.
- **Labor-exporting rural Guizhou, Sichuan and Chongqing:** ambition through migration.
- **Liangshan Nuosu:** migration, risk and manhood, with women's paths studied separately.
- **Khampa herders:** new fungus wealth vs. monastic renunciation.
- **Tengchong and other Yunnan border towns:** trade abroad as the ladder.
- **Hui traders of Yunnan:** caravan and market heritage.
- **Dong, Miao and Tujia villages turned to tourism:** ambition through heritage enterprise.
- **Women across all groups:** how ambition is named for women vs. men.
- Diaspora only as comparison: Yunnanese communities in Myanmar and Thailand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han exam and enterprise ambition; mixed for minorities, where much writing is outsiders' or poverty-relief framing.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Su and Sima); Nuosu and Tibetan for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and HIV in Liangshan studies; official "poverty alleviation" narratives; elite political careers. Avoid casting any group as more or less striving.
- **Held for review:** none.
