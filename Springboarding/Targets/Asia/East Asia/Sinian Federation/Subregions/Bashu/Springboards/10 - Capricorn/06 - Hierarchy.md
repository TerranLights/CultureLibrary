*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Sichuanese, Kunming and Guiyang forms of address; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; Tai Lü; Naxi. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing (a direct-controlled municipality since 1997) vs. its rural districts:** rank between city and countryside inside one unit.
- **Chengdu and Wuhan vs. prefecture cities:** the rank of places and the people tied to them.
- **Nuosu of Liangshan:** strata with memory of the 1956 reform, felt differently by descendants of each stratum.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna and Dehong:** a former court order.
- **Naxi of Lijiang:** chiefly lineage vs. commoners; Mosuo houses studied apart.
- **Khampa and Gyalrong Tibetans:** chief, lama and commoner.
- **Bai of Dali; Hani terrace villages; Miao and Dong villages:** check whether rank runs by age, lineage or wealth, from each group's own accounts.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** clergy and lay leaders.
- **Rural migrants in Chengdu, Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming:** registration rank in daily life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the state's own orderings (classification, hukou, ranks of posts); mixed for minority systems, recorded mainly through reform-era surveys and outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for genealogies and Zeng inscriptions; Tai Lü; Nuosu; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from Liangshan strata remains touchy in marriage and local politics; ethnic classification disputes (Mosuo, Gejia and others who sought separate status); Tibetan religious ranks.
- **Held for review:** none.
