## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu, Wuhan and Chongqing's elite high schools vs. county schools:** who reaches the top universities.
- **Rural Yunnan and Guizhou pupils on livestreamed classes:** shared teaching, unequal starts.
- **Tibetan students in the "9+3" stream and in monastic colleges:** two ladders of merit.
- **Nuosu and other Yi students:** bonus points and their critics, from inside and outside the group.
- **Dong and Miao villages:** skill contests and earned titles.
- **Craft towns (Zigong, Jianshui, Shu embroidery workshops):** master titles from the state vs. guild memory.
- **Civil servants and state-enterprise staff:** seniority vs. merit inside the post system.
- **Hubei's eastern exam belt (Huanggang) vs. its western mountains (Enshi, Shennongjia).**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams and admissions; mixed on hiring by connections (talk is everywhere; data thin); minority-merit debates are politically framed.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for exam history); Tibetan for monastic degrees.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority bonus points and quotas; Tibetan monastic education limits; cadre selection and corruption cases.
- **Held for review:** none.
