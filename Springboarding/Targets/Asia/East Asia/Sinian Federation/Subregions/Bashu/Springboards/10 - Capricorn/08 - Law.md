*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing and Chengdu urban litigants vs. rural petitioners:** court vs. petition as routes.
- **Liangshan Nuosu:** clan mediators and customary compensation alongside state courts.
- **Hmu and Dong of southeast Guizhou:** recited village law and contract culture.
- **Khampa and Amdowa Tibetans:** secular, religious and state law together; monastery-led mediation.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** religious norms in family matters.
- **Tujia and Qiang mountain counties:** check whether elder mediation persists.
- **Wuhan's legal academy and urban middle class:** a different relation to courts.
- **Business communities (Chengdu tech, Chongqing manufacturing):** contracts vs. connections.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing practice (large county archives) and for current court records; mixed for customary orders; sensitive cases are better documented outside mainland channels.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu for mediators' speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Chongqing campaign and its aftermath; rights lawyers; Tibetan monastic law; customary compensation officially discouraged; court databases have had cases removed.
- **Held for review:** none.
