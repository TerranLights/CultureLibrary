## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu-plain market towns vs. upland Ba districts:** lodge order vs. other local power.
- **Wuhan:** a river-port and industrial city with its own memory of uprisings and lockdown.
- **Anshun Tunpu villages:** a garrison order kept apart from neighbors.
- **Miao and Dong of Guizhou:** histories of revolt and resettlement.
- **Khampa Tibetans:** epic, monastery and state as rival orderers.
- **Naxi and Bai of northwest Yunnan:** ritual orders of nature and village.
- **Nuosu and Chuxiong Yi; Dai of Xishuangbanna:** festival time as release.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** memory of the 1870s suppression and the 1975 Shadian incident.
- **Rice-farming vs. herding peoples:** test the rice-wheat claim against highland pastoralists.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical disorder (rebellions, lodges, devastation) and for urban policy; mixed for minority cosmologies, documented largely by specialists and outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Qing accounts); Tibetan for Gesar; Naxi ritual specialists' readings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** stability maintenance in Tibetan areas; the Shadian incident; Wuhan 2020; Dongba and bimo ritual knowledge; rice-wheat findings are contested and must not become a stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
