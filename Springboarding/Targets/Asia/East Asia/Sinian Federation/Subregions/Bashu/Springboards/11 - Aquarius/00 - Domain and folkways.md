# Bashu: Domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin; Southwestern Mandarin (西南官话: Chengdu-Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming, Guiyang forms); Gan and Jianghuai in eastern Hubei; Sichuan Hakka; Tibetan, Nuosu Yi, Hmub (Miao), Kam (Dong), Bai, Naxi, Tai Lü, Qiang, Tujia, Buyi, Hani. **Concept in one line:** community and ideals: what binds people into groups, and what they hope for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain (川西):** the Sichuanese core, distinct from river-port Chongqing.
- **Chongqing city and its counties:** dock and porter traditions; southeastern Tujia and Miao counties apart.
- **Jianghan Plain and Wuhan (Hubei):** a Jingchu identity separate from Ba-Shu; eastern Hubei's Gan and Jianghuai speakers apart again.
- **Enshi and western Hubei; Wuling hills:** Tujia and Miao communities across the Hubei-Chongqing line.
- **Liangshan and Panxi:** Nuosu Yi clans; Third Front migrants.
- **Garzê, Ngawa and Diqing:** Kham and Amdo Tibetan, Gyalrong and Qiang highland societies.
- **Southeastern and southern Guizhou:** Hmub (Miao), Kam (Dong), Shui, Buyi village societies with their own assemblies.
- **Anshun Tunbao (屯堡):** Han descended from Ming garrisons, a tightly kept enclave.
- **Dali, Lijiang, Lugu Lake:** Bai, Naxi and Mosuo, with separate patterns of household and village.
- **Southern and western Yunnan borderlands:** Dai, Hani, Wa, Jingpo, Lisu, Lahu, Derung; Hui Muslim towns.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai, Dongshan):** a migrant-descended speech island.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Yunnanese in northern Thailand and Myanmar; Hmong abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan; mixed for Yunnan and Guizhou peoples (strong ethnography, uneven native-language writing); thin for the small far-western peoples.
- **Lands:** the tracker's five provinces are a working list; the member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Sichuanese dialect dictionaries; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmub and Kam romanized writing; Western-language ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan areas, Liangshan "democratic reform" history and religious associations need careful handling; some village rites are closed to outsiders.
- **Law:** rules of association and registration belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
