# Bashu: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Southwestern Mandarin (Sichuanese, Chongqing, Wuhan, Kunming, Guiyang speech); Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hmub; Kam; Bai; Tujia; Hui usage of Arabic-Persian loans. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life, recognize one another as members and owe one another something, and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu old courtyards (院落) against new gated compounds (小区):** two kinds of neighborliness in one city.
- **Chongqing hillside districts:** stairs, porters and stilt houses, shaped by terrain.
- **Hankou and the Wuhan triad:** lane life, river trade, the lockdown experience.
- **Rural Sichuan and Chongqing market-town catchments:** village against market town as the unit of belonging.
- **Third Front compounds (Panzhihua, Shiyan, Liupanshui):** work units as whole communities of transplanted easterners.
- **Three Gorges resettlers:** moved villagers inside host towns.
- **Kam and Hmub villages of southeastern Guizhou:** drum tower and drum society as centers.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** clan-based rather than place-based ties; resettled villages.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans (Garzê, Ngawa, Diqing):** pastoral camps against farming villages; monastery and village.
- **Hui Muslims of Yunnan and Hubei:** mosque congregations.
- **Bai, Naxi, Hani, Dai villages of Yunnan:** distinct village institutions and feasts.
- **Tujia of Enshi and southeastern Chongqing:** hill villages, labor exchange.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chengdu and Wuhan (history, governance studies); mixed for rural Guizhou and Yunnan (ethnography strong, numbers weak); thin for Tibetan pastoral areas.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; dialect dictionaries; Tibetan; Hmub, Kam and Nuosu sources where they exist.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2020 Wuhan lockdown accounts; Shadian's history; resettlement critiques; Tibetan community studies face access limits.
- **Law:** residents' committee statutes go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
