# Bashu: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), one of the Sinian Federation's member states, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Sichuanese and other Southwestern Mandarin speech; secret-society argot; Nuosu Yi; Hmub; Kam; Tibetan (Kham, Amdo, Gyalrong). **Concept in one line:** a set of people seen as a unit; how the culture weighs the group against the person, draws "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain Han:** the Paoge legacy, lodges, guilds.
- **Chongqing river and dock workers:** crews, porters, trackers; group forms tied to work.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan Plain:** factory and port groups; the 1967 factions.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** clan as the core unit; caste lines inside it (see Equality).
- **Hmub (Miao) and Kam (Dong) of Guizhou:** village and multi-village compacts.
- **Tujia and Gelao areas:** former chieftain-led domains.
- **Gyalrong and Kham Tibetans:** former chieftaincies, monastery affiliations, pastoral bands.
- **Qiang:** village-based groups in the upper Min valley.
- **Tunbao Han of Anshun:** a garrison descent group holding itself apart.
- **Third Front transplants:** easterners in work-unit towns.
- **Hui Muslims:** trade and kin networks linking Yunnan and Sichuan towns, apart from Han groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Paoge and Republican Sichuan; good on Yi clans; mixed on Miao and Dong institutions (much in Chinese ethnography only); thin on Tibetan group forms in Kham.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Qing memorials; secret-society argot; Nuosu Yi and Hmub sources; Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution factional violence; secret societies' modern links to crime; Yi clan feuds and the 1950s reforms; Tibetan local histories.
- **Law:** written rules for associations and gangs go to Libra; their spirit to Capricorn.
