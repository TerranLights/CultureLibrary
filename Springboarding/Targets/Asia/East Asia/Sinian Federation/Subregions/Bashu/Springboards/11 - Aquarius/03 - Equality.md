# Bashu: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese (Song histories); Sichuanese and other southwestern speech; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan; Tai Lü; Hmub. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan urban hukou holders against rural migrants:** a likely everyday dividing line, to be tested.
- **Chongqing's 2010 hukou conversion:** a test of whether formal change shifted standing.
- **Nuosu Yi strata in Liangshan:** a ranked order with its own words and memories.
- **Kham and Gyalrong Tibetans:** lords, monasteries and taxpayer households, before and after.
- **Tai Lü of Xishuangbanna:** a former court society with ranked nobles.
- **Hmub and Kam villages:** assembly-governed villages, a contrast case to test.
- **Women in each group:** Sichuanese spousal jokes, Liangshan bride-price debates, Miao and Dong customs.
- **Third Front families:** imported status inside local societies.
- **Small peoples of the Nujiang and borderland (Derung, Nu, Lisu, Wa):** standing within a ranked list of nationalities.

## 4. Flags
