# Bashu: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; late-Qing and Republican written Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; Hui Muslim usage. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wuhan's late-Qing elite and students:** reform from a governor's office, then revolt.
- **Chengdu city:** police and street reform against teahouse and street users.
- **Beibei and the Chongqing hinterland:** private and missionary-style reform experiments.
- **Rural Sichuan cadres and farmers (Guanghan, Qionglai):** decollectivization and land reform in practice.
- **Nuosu Yi of Liangshan:** reforms done to them in the 1950s and custom reforms now; also Yi reformers.
- **Hmub, Kam and Buyi of Guizhou:** the long shadow of 改土归流.
- **Tujia and Gelao of former chieftain lands:** the same Qing reform, northern side.
- **Tibetan monastic communities:** reform imposed and reform proposed.
- **Hui Muslims:** reform inside the faith.
- **Third Front workers:** reform as layoffs and conversion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late-Qing Hubei and Republican Sichuan; rich but politically shaped for post-1978 reform; thin for minority peoples' own accounts of reform.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Qing documentary Chinese; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; English scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Chongqing model; 1950s "democratic reform" in Yi and Tibetan areas; Tibetan monastic rules.
- **Law:** reforms of statute and courts go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); here only the idea and practice of reform.
- **Held for review:** none.
