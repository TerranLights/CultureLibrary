*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese (Chu verse, Tang poetry, Ming philosophy, Daoist scripture); Tibetan (Kham epic); Nuosu Yi; English-language scholarship. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubei's Chu heritage:** Qu Yuan's region; test how far his figure shapes local talk of principle.
- **Chengdu literary circles:** Du Fu and Ba Jin as two kinds of ideals.
- **Guizhou Confucian and Yangming scholars:** a frontier province claiming a philosophy.
- **Kunming wartime intellectuals and their heirs:** Lianda's memory.
- **Kham Tibetans of Garzê and Diqing:** epic and monastic ideals in their own terms.
- **Nuosu Yi:** instruction texts and bimo teachings.
- **Chongqing:** revolutionary martyr culture as civic education.
- **Daoist communities of Qingcheng and Heming mountains:** religious ideals of a well-ordered life.
- **Young volunteers from Wuhan and Chengdu universities:** service idealism.
- **Naxi and Lisu of Diqing:** living inside a branded utopia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical figures and Republican writers; mixed for minority ideals (texts exist, translations few); good for present online talk.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Tibetan; Nuosu Yi; English scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wen Yiduo's death and *Red Crag* are politically framed; Tibetan religious texts; some Daoist and bimo rites closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
