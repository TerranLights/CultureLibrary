# Bashu: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan speech; Zhuang (Wenshan); Kam; Hmub; Nuosu Yi; Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain Han:** friendships formed around card rooms, tea and food, to compare across classes and ages.
- **Chongqing:** men's loyalty as told in film and song, set against everyday practice.
- **Wuhan and the Jianghan Plain:** the Boya legend's home; dock and campus friendships.
- **Rural Guizhou and Yunnan Han:** formal same-age friendships (老庚).
- **Zhuang and Yao of Wenshan:** ritual friendship (老同).
- **Kam (Dong) villages:** friendship between villages, not only persons.
- **Nuosu Yi:** drinking circles, sworn ties across clans.
- **Tibetans of Garzê and Ngawa:** host-guest and trading friendships along caravan routes.
- **Hui traders of Yunnan:** friendships across faith lines in market towns.
- **Women's friendships:** 闺蜜 culture in cities against village sisterhoods (Miao Sisters' Meal).
- **Older adults:** square-dance and card-room friends.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and present online talk; mixed for formal folk friendships (scattered county studies); thin for Tibetan and small borderland peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; dialect knowledge (the 耍朋友 trap); Zhuang, Kam, Nuosu and Tibetan terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** little; friendship-city lists are diplomatic framing; some village rites closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
