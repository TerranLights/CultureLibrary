# Bashu: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; late-Ming literary Chinese; Sichuanese, Chongqing and Wuhan slang; Naxi; Nuosu Yi; youth-subculture slang and English loans. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubei literati country (Macheng, Gong'an):** a home of late-Ming dissent, set against Wuhan's present scenes.
- **Chengdu youth and artists:** indie music, rap, LGBT life.
- **Chongqing youth:** dialect rap, a separate scene from Chengdu.
- **Wuhan students and workers:** punk and student circles.
- **Rural Guizhou and Yunnan migrant youth:** shamate and their villages.
- **Dali's urban dropouts and the Bai and Han locals:** two views of the same choice.
- **Naxi of Lijiang:** a minority tradition of refusing marriage rules.
- **Nuosu Yi youth in Liangshan and in cities:** pressures from clan and from the city.
- **Kham Tibetans:** monks and lay people who break expected roles (to approach with care).
- **Daoist hermits and eccentrics of Qingcheng and Wudang:** licensed oddity in religion.
