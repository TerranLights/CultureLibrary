# Bashu: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Southwestern Mandarin; Tibetan; Hmong; Tai Lue (Dai); Nuosu. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu and Wuhan urban middle class:** platform giving and volunteering against older kin-based help.
- **Hill-county Sichuan and Chongqing villages:** help through lineage, neighbors and village funeral teams.
- **Garzê and Ngawa Tibetans:** merit, life release and monastery-led relief.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna:** Theravada merit-making at temples.
- **Christian A-Hmao, Lisu and Nu villages:** church-centered relief with a missionary past.
- **Hui of Yunnan** (Shadian, Weishan, Kunming): mosque-based alms and zakat.
- **Families of migrant workers:** usually the recipients of pity; how they speak of it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for earthquake relief and online charity; thin and scattered for minority mutual aid; Qing archives rich but slow to use.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with dialect), Tibetan, Hmong, Tai Lue; Qing documentary Chinese for the archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Larung Gar's status; independent charities limited after 2016 registration rules; earthquake school-collapse debates; stories of the Bijie children concern minors.
- **Held for review:** none.
