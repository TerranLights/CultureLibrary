# Bashu: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Sichuanese; Hmu (Qiandongnan Miao); Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing:** two layers of local sacrifice (Ba Manzi and Red Crag) in one city's school and tourist life.
- **Western Hubei (Zigui, Yichang):** the Qu Yuan cult at its source.
- **Chengdu Plain:** Shu-Han loyalty and gentry-family self-denial (Ba Jin country).
- **Third Front cities** (Panzhihua, Shiyan, Guizhou plants): sacrifice framed as service to the state.
- **Migrant-sending counties** (eastern Sichuan, Guizhou, Zhaotong): parents' sacrifice through absence.
- **Miao of Qiandongnan:** a resistance memory separate from the state's.
- **Tibetans of Ngawa and Garzê:** monastic and lay ideas of giving up the self (see Flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and classical models; thin for how ordinary people judge sacrifice as a burden; minority memory mostly in local Chinese publications.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers, modern Chinese, Sichuanese, Tibetan, Hmu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the self-immolations in Ngawa and nearby since 2009 are highly sensitive: official and exile sources conflict, and researchers must handle them with care and never as spectacle; 2020 Wuhan medical deaths; Third Front and famine-era costs.
- **Held for review:** none.
