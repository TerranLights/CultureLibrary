# Bashu: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** classical and modern Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Tibetan; Nuosu; Naxi; Tujia (through Chinese). **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Three Gorges resettled people** (Wanzhou, Fengjie, Yunyang, Badong): towns ended by water.
- **Sichuan Hakka** against the **Tunpu Han of Anshun**: blending in against holding apart.
- **Tujia of Changyang and Badong:** dance at death; a language near loss.
- **Nuosu of Liangshan:** cremation and the journey to the ancestors.
- **Kham Tibetans of Garzê:** sky burial and phowa as lived practice.
- **Naxi of Lijiang:** love-suicide memory; an old town remade by tourism.
- **Former state-firm workers** in Wuhan, Chongqing and Third Front towns.
- **Ersu, Shixing and other small Sichuan peoples:** identity counted under larger groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for classical texts, the reservoir and funeral rites; thin for felt experience of merging, trance or dementia in minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese, modern Chinese, Tibetan, Nuosu, Naxi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "sinicization" as a term is political; resettlement grievances; sky burial is closed to onlookers in places and should not be studied as spectacle; love-suicide material touches suicide.
- **Held for review:** none.
