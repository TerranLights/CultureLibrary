# Bashu: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Sichuanese and Chongqing speech; Tibetan; Nuosu; Tai Nüa and Jingpo (Dehong border); Mosuo/Na (through Chinese). **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Chengdu residents and Manchu descendants:** a remembered walled quarter inside the city.
- **Chongqing's hillside neighborhoods:** cramped, stacked living where privacy is negotiated differently.
- **Dai, Jingpo and Wa of the Yunnan borderlands:** kin on both sides of a state line.
- **Nuosu of Liangshan:** clan and rank boundaries, a sensitive area of study.
- **Mosuo/Na of Lugu Lake:** a different household line, now pressed by tourism.
- **Tusi-descended Tujia and Bouyei areas:** the old native-rule frontier in memory.
- **Migrants with rural hukou in the cities:** living on the wrong side of a paper line.
- **Tibetans of Diqing and Garzê:** sacred-mountain boundaries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for frontier history and housing policy; mixed for personal boundaries (mostly online talk and national surveys without regional splits).
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for tusi records, modern Chinese and dialect, Tibetan, Nuosu, border Tai languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nuosu rank categories (黑彝/白彝) are sensitive and tied to official "slave society" narratives; the Wuhan lockdown; border closures and fences in Yunnan; tourists at Mosuo homes.
- **Held for review:** none.
