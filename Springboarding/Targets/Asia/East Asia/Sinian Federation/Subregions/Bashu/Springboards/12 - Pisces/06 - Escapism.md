*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu's teahouse and mahjong world:** leisure praised as local character; test against working hours and debt.
- **Chongqing's night city:** hotpot, river-view bars and "internet-famous" sights.
- **Wuhan:** a working industrial city's different leisure, by the lakes and the river.
- **City migrants who moved to Dali and Lijiang:** escape as a life plan.
- **Nuosu young men of Liangshan:** migration and drugs as a risky exit.
- **Dai of Xishuangbanna:** the Water-Splashing Festival 泼水节 as release, now also a tourist product.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans:** barley beer and pilgrimage; their homeland sold as others' escape.
- **Left-behind teenagers in Guizhou and Sichuan counties:** games and screens where parents are absent.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drugs, drink and tourism; mixed for everyday daydreaming and fiction reading; lottery data good at province level only.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese with dialect, Nuosu, Tibetan, Tai Lue.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug policy and compulsory treatment; the Golden Triangle border trade; Shangri-La's naming politics; "lying flat" was censored in places.
- **Held for review:** none.
