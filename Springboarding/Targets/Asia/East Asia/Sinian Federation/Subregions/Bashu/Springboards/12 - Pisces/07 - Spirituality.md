## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young city temple-goers in Chengdu, Chongqing and Wuhan:** a recent fashion for incense and temple visits; test what they seek.
- **Daoist mountain communities** (Qingcheng, Heming, Wudang): monastic and lay practice side by side.
- **Kham and Amdo Tibetans:** monastery-centered devotion with pilgrim circuits.
- **Bai of Dali:** layered village cults.
- **Lisu, Nu and Jingpo Christians:** a church faith with its own hymn tradition.
- **Hui of Yunnan:** Chinese-language Islamic learning.
- **Dai Theravada villages:** merit and monastic boys.
- **Qiang and Hmong:** specialist-led ritual, rebuilt or pressed.
- **Protestant city congregations** (Chengdu, Wuhan): registered and unregistered (see Flags).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Daoism, Emei and Tibetan Buddhism; mixed for lay inner life; thin for unregistered groups.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (Daoist canon), Tibetan, Lisu (Fraser script), Hmong, Tai Lue, Bai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches (the Chengdu Early Rain Covenant Church case, 2018) and Tibetan monasteries are politically sensitive; Qiang and Hmong ritual knowledge may be restricted by its keepers; do not treat the federation's religious policy as the region's spirituality.
- **Held for review:** none.
