# Bashu: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** classical and modern Chinese; Sichuanese; Sui (through Chinese); Nuosu; Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu TCM practitioners and patients:** expert feel against laboratory tests.
- **Former qigong practitioners** in Sichuan cities: memory of the 1980s claims and their collapse.
- **Sui of Sandu:** a written specialist tradition for reading days.
- **Nuosu of Liangshan:** priest-read signs of hidden causes.
- **Tibetans of Garzê:** decisions referred to lamas' divination.
- **Rural women mediums** in eastern Sichuan and northern Guizhou.
- **Yunnan tea traders and Sichuan chefs:** commercial and craft expertise.
- **Earthquake-belt residents** (Longmenshan, Ludian, Lushan): reading animals and weather.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for qigong history, TCM and divination texts; mixed for everyday hunches; psychology data limited to student samples.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (slips, Chan texts), modern Chinese, Sichuanese, Tibetan, Nuosu; Sui manuscripts need specialists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** qigong history leads into politically sensitive ground; earthquake-forecast rumors were punished; some divination manuscripts are held privately by families.
- **Held for review:** none.
