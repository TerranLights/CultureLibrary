## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chengdu Plain villages and market towns:** brotherhood memory, between honor and crime.
- **Chongqing:** a city marked by a campaign against gangs and by its legendary killers.
- **Wuhan:** a transport hub's underworld in film and memory.
- **Yunnan borderlands** (Dehong, Lincang, Xishuangbanna, Wenshan): drugs, fraud and trafficking near the line.
- **Nuosu of Liangshan:** often labeled from outside; their own idea of wrong and repair (cite Capricorn for customary order).
- **Guizhou and Yunnan source counties for trafficking:** victims' families as voices.
- **Migrant workers:** both victims of crime and targets of suspicion in cities elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for brotherhood history, film and campaigns; thin for unofficial views; surveys broad, not local.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Sichuanese and Wuhan speech, Nuosu, Yunnan border languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Chongqing campaign and its leaders; Liao Yiwu's work is banned in the federation; drug-war and trafficking cases; stereotypes of Nuosu and rural Sichuanese as thieves must be recorded as stereotypes, never as findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
