# Bashu: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bashu (巴蜀), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Sichuan, Chongqing, Hubei, Yunnan, Guizhou). **Language(s) for searching:** classical and modern Chinese; Sichuanese and Wuhan speech; Hmong; Tai Lue (Dai); Lolopo and Nuosu Yi; Tibetan. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chongqing:** fear of bombing in living memory; shelters still part of the city.
- **Longmenshan quake belt** (Wenchuan, Beichuan, Mianzhu, Dujiangyan): fear of the ground itself.
- **Wuhan and Hubei:** fear of contagion and of being feared.
- **Dai villages of Xishuangbanna:** witch-spirit suspicion within the village.
- **Miao of Qiandongnan and Xiangxi borders:** the people most named in gu accusations.
- **Lolopo and Nuosu Yi:** ghosts tied to clan, land and past violence.
- **Tibetans of Garzê and Diqing:** fear of offending mountain and water spirits.
- **Shennongjia and Three Gorges residents:** wilderness legends and a dam overhead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, war memory and ghost lore; mixed for everyday anxiety; little regional survey data.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (slips, calendars), modern Chinese and dialect, Hmong, Tai Lue, Yi languages, Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gu and pipa gui accusations harmed real people and must be studied as accusations; Wuhan 2020; dam-safety rumors; the federation's limits on ghost content in film and TV shape what media exist.
- **Held for review:** none.
