**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands per the tracker header: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard written Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan, northwest highlands); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan); Wu 吴语 (Shangrao area); Classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the domain of self and identity: what a self is here, and how it asserts itself (personhood, body, appearance, naming).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking heartland:** Nanchang and the Chang-Du area, Yichun, Ji'an/Luling, Fuzhou/Linchuan, Yingtan and Yiyang; each frames a different local self.
- **Hakka of Gannan** (Ganzhou counties) and the **northwest highland Hakka** (Tonggu, Xiushui, Wanzai, Yifeng, descendants of Qing-era "shack people" 棚民 *check*): separate origin stories and lineage forms.
- **She 畲 communities** (ethnic townships in Guixi and Qianshan *check*): a recognized minority whose speech is Hakka-type; identity by registration against by custom.
- **Huizhou-speaking Wuyuan and Fuliang**: Huizhou culture inside Jiangxi.
- **Wu-speaking Shangrao, Guangfeng, Yushan** and **Southern Min enclaves in the northeast** *check*.
- **Mandarin islands:** Jiujiang (Jianghuai) and Ganzhou city (Southwestern Mandarin).
- **Jingdezhen**: a migrant city of potters' guilds.
- **Poyang Lake fishing communities**; returning migrant workers.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: descendants of the Ming-Qing "Jiangxi fills Huguang" 江西填湖广 migration; today's Jiangxi workers in Guangdong and Zhejiang.

The federation's other member states serve only as contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for genealogies, gazetteers and literati writing; mixed for provincial survey data; thin for She and Southern Min enclaves.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese, Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for oral material; some Huizhou and Wu.
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists Jiangxi as the member state's one province; that is a working list the author still confirms in Stage 0.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary heritage is presented through official narratives; ethnic classification of the She; genealogies may hold living persons' data.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the 中国家谱总目 catalog for Jiangxi counties (南昌, 吉安, 抚州, 赣州) and list genealogies with 字辈 poems.
2. Read the customs 风俗 sections of two contrasting county gazetteers, one Gan (e.g. 吉安县志) and one Hakka (e.g. 兴国县志), for birth and naming rites.
3. Search CNKI 知网 for "江西老表" 身份认同 and "客家 认同 赣南".
4. Locate a Nanchang dialect dictionary (e.g. the 南昌方言词典 volume in the 现代汉语方言大词典 series *check*) and note person words and pronouns.
5. Check the supplementary data of Talhelm et al. (2014) for Jiangxi participants.
