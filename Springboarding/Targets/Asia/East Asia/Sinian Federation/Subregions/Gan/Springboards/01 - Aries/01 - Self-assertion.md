# Gan: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, Sinian Federation member state (Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard written Chinese; Gan (Nanchang, Ji'an, Fuzhou varieties); Hakka (Gannan); Classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking north and center** (Nanchang, Yichun, Fuzhou, Ji'an): compare how requests and refusals sound across Gan subgroups.
- **Hakka of Gannan** and **northwest highland Hakka**: test the 硬颈 frame against local practice.
- **She communities** (Guixi, Qianshan): minority voice toward township officials and Han neighbors.
- **Women by generation and setting:** daughters-in-law in rural lineages against urban professional women in Nanchang.
- **Workers and bosses:** Pingxiang miners' heirs, Jingdezhen workshop artisans and apprentices, factory workers in Nanchang.
- **Returning migrant workers** from Guangdong and Zhejiang: changed norms of speaking up.
- **Students and teachers** in exam-focused high schools.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi workers in coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classical exemplars and media disputes; mixed for complaint statistics; thin for minority and dialect discourse data.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Gan and Hakka for recorded speech and song; Classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Lushan Conference, petitions to officials and labor disputes touch politics; consumer and mediation material may name private persons. Formal disputes in courts and the old "litigious Jiangxi" 健讼 theme belong to Libra, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
