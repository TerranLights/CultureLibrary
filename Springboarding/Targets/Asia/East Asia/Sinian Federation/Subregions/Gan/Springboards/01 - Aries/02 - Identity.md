## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan subregions:** Nanchang (capital, red "Hero City" image), Luling/Ji'an and Linchuan/Fuzhou (literati heritage), Yichun and Pingxiang (mining and western border), Yingtan and Shangrao (Daoist and trade towns): each has its own heritage label.
- **Hakka:** Gannan core counties, and the northwest highland Hakka whose ancestors moved back from Guangdong and Fujian; origin stories differ.
- **She:** small, registered, Hakka-speaking; ethnic identity by state category against daily life.
- **Wuyuan and Fuliang:** Huizhou heritage under Jiangxi administration.
- **Shangrao Wu speakers** and **Min-speaking enclaves** in the northeast *check*.
- **Jiujiang**: Yangtze river port, Mandarin-speaking, Lushan resort past.
- **Religious identities:** Zhengyi Daoist families of Longhushan; Buddhists of the Lushan and Yunju temples; Catholic and Protestant congregations; small Hui communities in Nanchang and Jiujiang *check*.
- **Generations:** migrant workers born after 1990 against their parents.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Huguang and Sichuan lineages tracing Jiangxi roots; overseas Hakka.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and Hakka studies; mixed for Gan regional identity surveys; thin for She, Min and Hui communities in Jiangxi.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews and recordings; Classical Chinese for genealogies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification, religious registration and red heritage are official topics; Hakka origin theories are disputed; genealogies hold family data.
- **Held for review:** none.
