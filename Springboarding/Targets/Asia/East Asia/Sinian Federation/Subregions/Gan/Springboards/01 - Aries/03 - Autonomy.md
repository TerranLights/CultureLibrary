## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan lineage villages** of Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun against **Nanchang** and **Jiujiang** city households: kin control against urban choice.
- **Hakka of Gannan** and **northwest highland Hakka**: women's roles and lineage authority.
- **She communities** in Guixi and Qianshan: collective self-rule as a recognized minority.
- **Huizhou-speaking Wuyuan**: merchant-lineage family rules.
- **Religious communities:** Daoist Longhushan, Buddhist Lushan and Yunju temples, Christian congregations; their room to govern themselves.
- **Left-behind children and elders** against migrant parents.
- **Young women and men** facing marriage pressure, by county and education.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi migrants living away from kin control in coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts and lineage rules; mixed for surveys with Jiangxi breakdowns; thin and sensitive for birth-planning experience.
