## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan heartland traders** of Fuzhou, Ji'an and Nanchang (old 江右 networks) against **Wuyuan's Huizhou-style merchants**.
- **Zhangshu and Nancheng** pharmacists: a craft-trade niche.
- **Jingdezhen** old kiln families, state-factory workers after restructuring, and new incomers.
- **Gannan Hakka** orange growers and market towns (Xunwu, Ruijin, Xingguo).
- **Northwest highland** and **She** villages: small-scale forestry and tea enterprise.
- **Industrial Nanchang, Pingxiang and Xinyu:** state-firm workers against private founders.
- **Women** in rural e-commerce and market stalls; **returning migrants** against those who never left.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi-founded shops and guildhalls in other provinces; today's Jiangxi entrepreneurs in Guangdong and Zhejiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and economic statistics; mixed for volunteering; thin for She and highland enterprise.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Classical Chinese for gazetteers and merchant manuals; Gan and Hakka for interviews; French for d'Entrecolles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** firm collapses and debts (e.g. large agricultural groups) can be sensitive; official success stories need cross-checking.
- **Held for review:** none.
