## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan north and center** (Nanchang, Yichun, Fuzhou, Ji'an): lineage founding stories and New Year cycles.
- **Gannan Hakka**: lantern rites, founding-ancestor halls, siting tradition in Xingguo.
- **Northwest highland Hakka**: lineages whose stories begin with a return migration.
- **She** villages: their own founding and ancestor tales, set against Han lineage forms.
- **Poyang Lake** fishers and resettled villagers; **reservoir resettlers** (e.g. Zhelin reservoir, Yongxiu *check*).
- **Industrial cities** (Pingxiang, Jingdezhen, Xinyu): starting over after closures.
- **Daoist and Buddhist** communities: ordination and tonsure as new beginnings.
- **Wuyuan** (Huizhou customs) and **Shangrao Wu** areas: different New Year usages.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: lineages elsewhere whose 始迁祖 left from Jiangxi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for archaeology, genealogy and customs records; mixed for modern fresh-start experience; thin for She founding stories.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for rites and oral stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary founding narratives are official; resettlement and layoffs can be contentious; siting practice may be framed officially as superstition.
- **Held for review:** none.
