## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Luling/Ji'an** with its loyalist memory, against **Shangrao and Yiyang** (Xie Fangde, Fang Zhimin): two local hero traditions.
- **Gannan Hakka** and the **former Soviet counties** (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu): revolutionary sacrifice told at village level.
- **Poyang Lake** and **Gan River** boat and fishing people: courage at water.
- **Miners** of Pingxiang, Dayu and Dexing: courage at work.
- **She** communities: their own heroes and how they enter official lists.
- **Women:** martyrs and veterans against everyday endurance.
- **Young men** in rural counties: dares, enlistment, physical tests.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi veterans and martyrs honored elsewhere.
