## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan lineage villages** (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun): household division, elder support, farming-and-study ideals.
- **Wuyuan** (Huizhou contract tradition) as a documented contrast.
- **Gannan Hakka**: highland self-provisioning; Soviet-era memories of self-reliance.
- **She** villages: subsistence forestry and tea; dependence on state programs.
- **Poyang Lake** fishers: livelihoods tied to fishing bans and compensation.
- **Industrial towns** (Pingxiang, Jingdezhen, Xinyu): state-firm dependence against self-employment.
- **Elders left behind, women migrants, disabled people:** differing meanings of standing alone.
- **Buddhist and Daoist communities:** monastic self-support.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi workers abroad and in coastal cities sending money home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for contracts, migration statistics and elder-care research; mixed for household division today; thin for She and fishers.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for contracts and codes; Standard written Chinese; Gan and Hakka for village interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Ruijin regime and any talk of political independence are sensitive; fishing bans and resettlement compensation can be contentious; elders' and disabled people's data need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
