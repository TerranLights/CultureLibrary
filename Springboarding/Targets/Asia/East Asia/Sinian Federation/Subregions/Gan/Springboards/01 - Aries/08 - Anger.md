# Gan: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, Sinian Federation member state (Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard written Chinese; Gan; Hakka; Classical Chinese (medical and family texts). **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking areas** (Nanchang, Yichun, Fuzhou, Ji'an): vocabulary and quarrel styles by subgroup.
- **Gannan Hakka** and **northwest highland Hakka**: their own words; memories of Hakka-native conflict.
- **She** communities: relations with Han neighbors, and grievances voiced or held back.
- **Wuyuan** and **Shangrao Wu** areas: different dialect cursing and family-instruction traditions.
- **Men and women; elders and young people;** in-laws in rural households.
- **Urban drivers, hospital patients, online users:** modern anger settings.
- **Poyang Lake** fishers under fishing bans: grievance over livelihoods.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi migrant workers' grievances in coastal factories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for archival quarrel cases and dialect dictionaries; mixed for surveys; thin for She and for anger in minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for speech and sketches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective protest, online incidents and medical disputes are sensitive; domestic-violence data need care. Courts and dispute law go to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
