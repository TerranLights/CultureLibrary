## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou/Linchuan:** long exam memory, today's exam-focused schools.
- **Nanchang** against **Ganzhou, Jiujiang and Shangrao:** city rivalry for status.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage and village rivalry; dragon dances and lantern contests.
- **Jingdezhen:** craft competition among family workshops, state-trained masters and newcomers.
- **She** villages: how they enter county contests and quotas.
- **Wuyuan** (Huizhou merchant and exam tradition).
- **Rural households** in gift and wedding rivalry against **urban families** in school and housing rivalry.
- **Young people** using "involution" talk against parents' exam ideals.
- Diaspora, as comparison only: Jiangxi students and workers measured against coastal peers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam history and education statistics; mixed for sports and business rankings; thin for minority and village contests outside official records.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for exam records; Standard written Chinese; Gan and Hakka for proverbs and interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** publicizing top exam scorers has been restricted; gambling at mahjong is policed; rankings may be promotional.
- **Held for review:** none.
