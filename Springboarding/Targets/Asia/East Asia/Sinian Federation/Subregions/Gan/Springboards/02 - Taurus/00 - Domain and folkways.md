# Gan: The domain, possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (its lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script, with traditional forms for pre-1950s print); Gan 赣语 varieties; Hakka 客家话 of southern and northwestern Jiangxi; Hui 徽语 and Wu 吴语 varieties of the northeast; the speech of She 畲 communities. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth), with the folkways of food, dress and wealth.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang 南昌 and the Gan River plain:** city consumption against the surrounding rice villages.
- **Ji'an 吉安 (Luling 庐陵) and Fuzhou 抚州 (Linchuan 临川):** scholar-lineage areas; worth may be framed through learning and land.
- **Jingdezhen 景德镇 and Fuliang 浮梁:** a craft economy where the product itself is value.
- **Poyang Lake shore and Jiujiang 九江:** fishing, river trade and flood losses.
- **Gannan 赣南 Hakka counties (Ganzhou, Longnan, Xunwu, Ningdu, Xingguo):** walled houses, periodic markets, mining; overlaps the federation's Hakka member state, so keep the comparison explicit.
- **Northwest hill Hakka (Tonggu 铜鼓, Xiushui 修水, Wanzai):** later migrants with their own holdings history.
- **Wuyuan 婺源 and the northeast (Shangrao 上饶, Yushan, Guangfeng):** Huizhou and Wu cultural ties, tea and timber trade.
- **She 畲 communities (e.g. Yanshan 铅山 and Guixi 贵溪 townships):** a non-Han people with distinct dress and exchange customs *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for porcelain, tombs, gazetteers and statistics; mixed for everyday household practice; thin for She and small dialect communities.
- **Lands:** written for Jiangxi, the tracker header's working list; the member state's exact lands are still to be confirmed by the author.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese for gazetteers, Gan and Hakka for fieldwork and folk sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resource conflicts (rare earths, relocated fishers) and the 1930s land upheavals; handle with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
