# Gan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical and documentary Chinese (deeds, genealogies); Gan and Hakka for village usage. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka lineage-trust counties:** shares in ancestral trusts may outweigh household plots.
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou scholar lineages:** study fields 学田 and charitable estates as a separate kind of holding.
- **Northwest hill settlers (Tonggu, Wanzai, Xiushui):** later arrivals whose tenure was long disputed with earlier residents.
- **Poyang Lake fishers:** water and fishing grounds as property.
- **Jingdezhen craft groups:** ownership of kilns and trades set by native place.
- **Wuyuan and the northeast:** a dense deed culture and present heritage limits on selling houses.
- **Nanchang flat owners and their owners' committees 业主委员会:** urban, bought ownership.
- **Former Soviet-area villages (Ruijin, Xingguo, Jinggangshan):** memory of redistribution.
- **She communities:** possible differences in village and hill holdings *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and tenure history; mixed for present attitudes.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese for deeds and genealogies; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land revolution and later campaigns; demolition and compensation disputes; some lineages restrict who may read their genealogies.
- **Held for review:** none. Law: what property law says goes to Libra; what legal culture is for goes to Capricorn.
