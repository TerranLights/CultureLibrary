# Gan: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for epitaphs and essays; Gan and Hakka for everyday talk of pride and shame. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou scholar lineages:** worth framed by degrees and learning; check whether that still carries weight.
- **Gannan Hakka communities:** hall plaques, couplets and lineage pride may give a separate pattern.
- **Nanchang urban youth and graduates:** worth through jobs and housing in a provincial capital.
- **Migrant-worker families and left-behind children:** worth earned far away and judged at home.
- **Jingdezhen artisans:** worth tied to titles and a craft's name.
- **Elders in hollowed-out villages:** worth after the young have gone.
- **Women in high-payment marriage markets and the men who must pay:** worth set in the marriage bargain; check across counties, not as one rule.
- **She communities:** standing as a small recognized minority *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial elites and present psychology studies; mixed for ordinary adults; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Gan and Hakka for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and mental illness; marriage payments; online regional insults.
- **Held for review:** none.
