## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou scholar lineages:** academy and examination teaching left a dense written record of ranked duties.
- **Gannan Hakka:** language and lineage may be ranked as sacred in their own way.
- **Nanchang and the Xishan area:** the Xu Xun cult and Jingming teaching.
- **Yingtan and Longhushan:** a priestly Daoist community.
- **Former Soviet counties (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu 于都, Jinggangshan):** official revolutionary virtues sit beside older lineage teaching.
- **Wuyuan and the northeast:** merchant families who joined trade and learning.
- **Christian and Buddhist communities:** competing accounts of what may not be traded.
- **Urban young adults in Nanchang:** generational change, to test rather than assume.
- **She communities:** their own teachings and ancestor stories *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and official sources; mixed for survey data at provincial level; thin for minority teaching.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese, Standard Chinese, Gan and Hakka for proverb meaning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious groups; political teaching; private family instructions.
- **Held for review:** none. Law: what codes say goes to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
