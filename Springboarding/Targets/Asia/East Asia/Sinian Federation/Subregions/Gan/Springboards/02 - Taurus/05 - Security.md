*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka compound villages:** security historically built into the house itself.
- **Poyang Lake polder villages and Jiujiang riverside towns:** floods, not people, as the main danger.
- **Northwest hill counties (Tonggu, Xiushui, Wanzai):** heirs of settlers once without secure tenure.
- **State-sector households in Nanchang:** salaried posts and pensions against private and gig work.
- **Mining towns (Pingxiang coal, Dexing copper, Dayu 大余 tungsten):** security tied to a single employer and its decline.
- **Migrant workers and their villages:** insurance split between the coast and home.
- **Left-behind elders:** security without resident children.
- **Jingdezhen independent potters:** craft income without an employer.
- **She villages:** small hill communities with their own protective rites *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for floods, official coverage figures and heritage houses; mixed for felt insecurity; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese for statistics; classical Chinese for granary and defense records; Gan and Hakka for rites and elders' interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collapsed savings groups and fraud; official polls of public safety; some ritual specialists keep texts private.
- **Held for review:** none.
