## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old single-lineage villages (Le'an, Ji'an, Wuyuan):** continuity of place and name across centuries.
- **Gannan Hakka:** a history told as migration that then rooted.
- **Poyang Lake shore:** settlement shaped by changing water and flood moves.
- **Nanchang work-unit families against private-sector workers:** lifetime posts against churn.
- **Jingdezhen old potter families and the newcomer "Jingdezhen drifters" 景漂:** continuity of craft carried by outsiders.
- **Pingxiang and other mining towns:** stability tied to one industry.
- **Outmigrants and returnees:** villages emptied and partly refilled.
- **She communities:** continuity under pressure of a small minority *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage villages, migration history and census numbers; mixed for felt stability; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for genealogies and siting manuals; Standard Chinese for census and memoirs; Gan and Hakka for talk of home and leaving.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation; geomancy is sometimes discouraged officially; mining-town layoffs.
- **Held for review:** none.
