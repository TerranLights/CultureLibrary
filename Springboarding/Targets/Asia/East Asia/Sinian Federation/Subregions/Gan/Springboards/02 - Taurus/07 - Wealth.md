# Gan: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for merchant, official and literary sources; Gan and Hakka for folk tales and village talk. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old merchant hometowns (Zhangshu, Nancheng, Fuzhou, Ji'an):** trade wealth with deep roots.
- **Jingdezhen:** wealth from one product, and the gap between kiln owners, dealers and workers.
- **Nanchang entrepreneurs and salaried middle class:** new wealth and its display.
- **Gannan Hakka and other remittance villages:** wealth earned away and shown in houses.
- **Former Soviet and mountain counties:** long counted as poor; the meaning of "rich" there.
- **Mining districts (Dexing copper, Gannan rare earths and tungsten):** extraction wealth and who keeps it.
- **Wuyuan and the Huizhou-linked northeast:** merchant-scholar families and present tourism income.
- **Poyang fishing households:** wealth reckoned in boats and catch, now ended by the ban.
- **She communities:** silver ornaments and other forms of stored wealth *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and official poverty data; mixed for present attitudes; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for inventories, merchant records and poems; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka to hear how folk tales name the rich.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the land revolution's violence against "tyrants"; corruption cases; private fortunes.
- **Held for review:** none. Left-right measure: see Ownership.
