## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage villages with a feud history (Ji'an, Gannan):** group guarding of hills, graves and water.
- **Northwest native and settler communities (Wanzai, Yifeng, Tonggu):** guarding of access, not only of land.
- **Jingdezhen craft families:** knowledge kept from outsiders and from daughters.
- **Left-behind families:** grandparents and parents competing over a child's loyalty.
- **Urban young couples in Nanchang:** dating norms, phone checking, new terms.
- **Rural women and married daughters:** claims on family property after marriage.
- **Gannan Hakka communities:** songs and customs of courtship and fidelity.
- **Poyang fishing villages:** guarded waters among neighboring villages.
- **She communities:** courtship songs and marriage customs of their own *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for historical disputes and televised mediation; thin for private jealousy.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, documentary Chinese, Gan and Hakka.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; minors; family names in case records and on television; lineage feuds may be recent.
- **Held for review:** none. Law: what the codes say goes to Libra.
