## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling) lineages:** heirs of a famed loyalist; check whether that model shapes local speech.
- **Ningdu and other Gannan Hakka counties:** loyalist history plus "hard-neck" self-description.
- **Former guerrilla areas (Dayu, Xinfeng 信丰, Yudu):** persistence told as revolutionary virtue.
- **Yiyang and Shangrao:** a separate revolutionary hero and a distinct opera tradition.
- **Nanchang and county-town households facing demolition:** holding out against developers and officials.
- **Mountain elders slated for relocation:** refusing to move in old age.
- **Jingdezhen craftsmen:** refusing to change method.
- **She communities:** whether their tales and customs of yielding differ *check*.
