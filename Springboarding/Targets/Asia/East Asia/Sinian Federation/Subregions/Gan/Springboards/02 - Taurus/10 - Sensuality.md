## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang:** snack streets, foot-bath parlors and evening leisure in the capital.
- **Pingxiang 萍乡 and the western counties:** how chili heat is spoken of at the table.
- **Gannan Hakka:** stuffed, preserved and rice-based dishes, mountain songs and opera.
- **Poyang Lake and Jiujiang:** lake fish, wild greens and the sights of the lake.
- **Wuyuan, Fuliang and Lushan tea country:** tea tasting and landscape tourism.
- **Fuzhou (Linchuan):** the Tang Xianzu heritage and local opera.
- **Jingdezhen:** pleasure in fine objects; potters' and collectors' ways of judging them.
- **Buddhist and Daoist practitioners:** vegetarian days and restraint as the other pole.
- **She communities:** festival foods such as black rice 乌饭 for the third day of the third month *check*.
