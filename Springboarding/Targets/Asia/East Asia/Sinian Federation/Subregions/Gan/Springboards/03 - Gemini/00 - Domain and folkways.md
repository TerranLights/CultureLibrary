*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); pre-war lands carried: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua, simplified script; traditional script for pre-1950 print); Gan 赣语 and its local forms; Hakka 客家话 (south and northwest); Huizhou 徽语, Wu 吴语 and Mandarin varieties in border counties; She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home: speech ways, learning ways, neighbors and short journeys.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Nanchang and the Chang-Du Gan core vs. Yichun-Pingxiang (Yi-Liu), Ji'an/Luling (Ji-Cha), Fuzhou/Linchuan (Fu-Guang) and Yingtan-Yiyang (Ying-Yi): speech and schooling traditions differ by subgroup *check*.
- Gannan Hakka (Ganzhou, Ruijin, Xingguo, Ningdu, Longnan walled-house villages): a separate speech world.
- Northwest Hakka settlers (Xiushui, Tonggu, Wanzai) living among Gan speakers.
- Wuyuan, Fuliang and Dexing (Huizhou speech) and Guangfeng-Yushan (Wu speech) on the eastern edge.
- Jiujiang and Ganzhou city (Mandarin varieties) vs. their hinterlands.
- She communities (e.g. Guixi, Yanshan townships *check*).
- Jingdezhen's craft town; Poyang Lake fishing and boat communities.
- Returned migrant workers and left-behind children (留守儿童).
- Federation-wide: the other member states only as contrast. Diaspora (Jiangxi people in Guangdong, Zhejiang, Fujian): comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dialect geography, academies and gazetteers; mixed for everyday talk and neighbor customs; thin for She speech and lake communities. Gan's lands are the province of Jiangxi, a working list the author still confirms in Stage 0.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua (simplified and traditional script), classical Chinese, Gan, Hakka; some French for Sagart.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary-era sources (Jiangxi Soviet, Anyuan) are framed by official historiography; family records may be private; left-behind children are a sensitive topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
