## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Fuzhou/Linchuan and Ji'an, with deep exam histories, vs. Gan counties remembered for farming or trade.
- Jingdezhen's craft families and migrant apprentices: learning by hand, inside a guild and kin system.
- Gannan Hakka lineages and their charitable schools vs. Gan-area lineages.
- Wuyuan, once part of Huizhou, with its own merchant-scholar tradition.
- Pingxiang's mining towns: night schools and workplace learning.
- State-farm and forestry communities shaped by the work-study university.
- Poyang Lake fishing families: skills learned on the boat.
- Rural counties with many left-behind children vs. Nanchang's urban schools.
- She children moving between home speech and school Putonghua. Migrants' children schooled in coastal cities, as comparison only: what changes when Jiangxi children learn elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam history and academies; mixed for modern schooling statistics by county; thin for everyday learning by pitching in, She children and fishers.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua, classical Chinese, Gan and Hakka; French for d'Entrecolles in the original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution-era schooling and sent-down youth in Jiangxi are politically sensitive; studies of left-behind children involve minors.
- **Held for review:** none.
