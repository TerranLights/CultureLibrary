# Gan: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Putonghua; classical Chinese for Song notebooks and travel diaries; Gan and Hakka for folk tales and everyday prying talk; Huizhou speech in Wuyuan; She speech. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Nanchang museum-goers and science-center families vs. villages far from any museum: who meets organized curiosity.
- Poyang Lake fishers and farmers vs. birders who come to watch the same water.
- Jingdezhen collectors and dealers, whose curiosity is also a trade.
- Gannan Hakka villages and their customs for questioning strangers (lineage, surname, origin) *check*.
- Wuyuan and other tourist villages: residents looked at by visitors, and what they ask back.
- Lushan's resort history: locals beside a foreign colony.
- She communities, often the object of outside study: how they meet researchers' questions.
- Elders with folk-tale warnings vs. children raised on science books and short videos. A neighboring member state's museum culture may serve as an outside yardstick, nothing more.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Song notebooks, travel writing and archaeology news; mixed for science-interest surveys (provincial at best); thin for everyday prying customs and minority views.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua, classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for tales and talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** research on She and other minorities raises consent questions; questions about family, marriage and pay touch private matters; tomb excavation can be locally contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
