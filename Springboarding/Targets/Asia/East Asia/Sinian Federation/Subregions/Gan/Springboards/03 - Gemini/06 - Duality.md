## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gan "native" lineages vs. Hakka settler lineages in the same counties of the northwest and south: two registrations, two speech forms.
- Gannan Hakka who also speak Gan or Mandarin to outsiders.
- Wuyuan residents, whose county has belonged to two provinces.
- She who hold or held both Han and She identities.
- Longhushan's household Daoist priests, lay and priestly at once.
- Linchuan, home ground of the dream plays, as a theater-going public.
- Migrant workers with a city self and a village self.
- Nanchang youth who switch between Gan and Putonghua.
- Former Jiangxi Soviet counties, where revolutionary and lineage memory sit side by side *check*. Jiangxi people living in other provinces, as comparison only: a second, outside identity added to the home one.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chan, Daoist and dramatic texts; mixed for native-settler history; thin for present-day experience of double identity among She and migrants.
