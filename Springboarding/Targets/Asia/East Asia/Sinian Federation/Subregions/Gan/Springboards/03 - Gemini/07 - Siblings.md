## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gan lineage villages with strict generation names vs. Nanchang families of the one-child era: siblings as a named rank vs. siblings as a rarity.
- Gannan Hakka households, where division and sworn brotherhood may follow their own customs.
- She families (common surnames 蓝, 雷, 钟 *check*): how siblings and cousins are counted.
- Wuyuan's merchant families, with rich division documents.
- Jingdezhen workshops run by brothers, where the kiln and the family are one.
- Poyang Lake boat families: siblings growing up on the water.
- Rural migrant households where an elder sister raises younger ones.
- Longhushan's hereditary Daoist line, where succession may pass among brothers and nephews *check*. Brothers and sisters who migrated together to coastal cities, as comparison only: how sibling chains work away from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household division, genealogies and Song family writings; mixed for present-day care-sharing; thin for twin lore and She kin terms.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua, classical Chinese (deeds, family rules), Gan and Hakka kin vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-planning history and sex ratios are sensitive; inheritance disputes are private and can be bitter; secret-society history is officially framed. Inheritance as law belongs in Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
