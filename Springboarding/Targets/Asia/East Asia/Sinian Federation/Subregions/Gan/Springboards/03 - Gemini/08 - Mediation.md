# Gan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Putonghua; classical Chinese for Ming compacts, deeds and Qing feud records; Gan and Hakka for the words of everyday peacemaking; Huizhou speech in Wuyuan; She speech. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). Law as such is not scouted here (Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gan lineage villages, where elders may settle quarrels in the ancestral hall.
- Gannan Hakka counties, heirs to Wang Yangming's compact and to a history of hill feuds.
- She communities and their Han neighbors: who steps in when a dispute crosses the line between them.
- Poyang Lake fishing villages, with disputes over water rather than land.
- Jingdezhen and Zhangshu traders, where brokers and guilds stood between buyers and sellers.
- Nanchang's housing estates, where residents' committees may take the elder's place.
- Migrant workers dealing with contractors far from home.
- Families who take quarrels to television vs. those who keep them inside. Disputes among Jiangxi workers far from home, as comparison only: who goes between when the elders are absent.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ming compacts, deeds and Qing feuds; mixed for present-day lineage settlement; thin for She practice and temple intercession.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua, classical Chinese, Gan and Hakka.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official mediation is part of the state's control system; lineage feuds and wage disputes are sensitive; televised quarrels expose private lives. Law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
