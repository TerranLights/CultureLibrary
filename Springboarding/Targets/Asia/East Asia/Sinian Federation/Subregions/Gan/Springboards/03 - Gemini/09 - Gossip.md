## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Small Gan market towns vs. Nanchang's apartment blocks: dense talk vs. relative anonymity.
- Gannan Hakka walled-house villages (Longnan, Quannan), where many households share one building: who knows whose business.
- She villages, as both talkers and the subject of Han talk.
- Old lineage villages with chastity arches (e.g. Liukeng in Le'an *check*) vs. newer settlements.
- Jingdezhen's artist and dealer circles, where reputation is a trade asset.
- Poyang Lake boat communities, close in space and often apart from land villages.
- Women's and men's gossip settings (washing places, mahjong rooms, tea stalls), described, not assumed.
- Migrant workers judged at home by what neighbors say of their absence; their own online talk from the coast is comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteer biographies and Song notebooks; mixed for village gossip studies; thin for She and lake communities.
- **Languages needed:** Putonghua, classical Chinese, Gan and Hakka; online slang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rumor is policed online; talk about women's reputations and unmarried men touches private shame; forum posts name real people. Defamation as law belongs in Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
