*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; its lands are Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话; Huizhou, Wu, Min and Mandarin varieties at the edges. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: what is home, and who is family (household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking core by subgroup:** Nanchang and the Changdu area; Yichun (Yi-Liu); Ji'an/Luling (Ji-Cha); Fuzhou/Linchuan (Fu-Guang); Yingtan (Ying-Yi): lineage density and house types are likely to differ.
- **Hakka of Gannan** and the **northwest Hakka settler counties** (Tonggu, Xiushui, Wanzai *check*): different house forms and a history of native-settler (土客) tension.
- **She communities** (Zhangping 樟坪 in Guixi, Taiyuan 太源 in Yanshan *check*): the main non-Han people; thin sources.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou culture** and the **Wu-speaking northeast** (Guangfeng, Yushan): ties outward to Anhui and Zhejiang.
- **Mandarin islands** (Jiujiang; Ganzhou city) and **Min-speaking pockets** in the northeast *check*.
- **Poyang Lake fishing communities**, **Jingdezhen**'s migrant-origin kiln trades, **urban Nanchang** against rural counties, and **migrant-worker households** split across provinces.
- Diaspora (Jiangxi workers in Guangdong and Zhejiang; Hakka abroad who trace roots to Gannan) for comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage, genealogy, gazetteers and house types; mixed for living households; thin for She, Min-speaking and fishing communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for terms and songs; Huizhou and Wu for border counties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-planning enforcement, sex-ratio and infanticide history, the 2018 funeral reform in Ji'an *check*, left-behind children; private genealogies may be shown only to lineage members. Family law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Jiangxi; a working list the author confirms in Stage 0.
- **Held for review:** none.
