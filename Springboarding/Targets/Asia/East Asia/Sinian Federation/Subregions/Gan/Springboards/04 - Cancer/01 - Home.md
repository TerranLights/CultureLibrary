# Gan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); lands: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话; Huizhou and Wu varieties in the northeast. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Nanchang apartment owners** (old city against new districts such as Honggutan *check*) and **rural self-builders** in Gan-speaking counties: ownership and house pride may run on different terms.
- **Gannan Hakka families** who left enclosed houses for new single-family houses: what "home" meant before and after.
- **Lake-shore and fishing households** around Poyang: homes moved by flood control.
- **Wuyuan residents** (Huizhou culture) living inside a heritage site.
- **She villages** of Guixi and Yanshan *check*: whether house and home words differ.
- **Migrant workers' households:** the house built at home and the dormitory or rented room elsewhere.
- **Jingdezhen newcomers** against old kiln families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and old houses; mixed on what home means to people; thin for She and lake communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for home words and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced demolition and resettlement; disputes over compensation; online posts may be removed. Property law goes to Libra.
