# Gan: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); lands: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话; She, Huizhou and Wu forms at the margins. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage-dense Gan villages** of Ji'an and Fuzhou against **urban Nanchang** nuclear households: how far kin duties reach.
- **Poyang Lake kin groups**, whose ties run through water rights.
- **Gannan Hakka** and the **northwest settler Hakka** (Tonggu, Xiushui *check*): separate lineages and halls, and a history of native-settler marriage limits.
- **She families** (the Lan, Lei and Zhong surnames *check*) in Guixi and Yanshan: distinct marriage and naming customs to check.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou lineages**, with their dense written records.
- **Split migrant families**, grandparents raising grandchildren while parents work out of province.
- **Christian families** in Jiangxi *check*: church teaching on family set beside lineage rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineages, genealogies and kin terms; thinner for everyday family life in surveys at provincial level; thin for She.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka kin terms; genealogy conventions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lineage feuds and violent disputes; birth-planning history; genealogies often closed to outsiders and women's lines recorded unevenly. Marriage, adoption and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
