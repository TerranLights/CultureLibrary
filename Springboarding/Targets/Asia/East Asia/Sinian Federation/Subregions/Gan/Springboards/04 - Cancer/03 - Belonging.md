## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking natives** of the core prefectures, and the old native-settler line in the **highland counties** of the west and northwest.
- **Hakka of Gannan**, where Hakka is the majority, against **Hakka minorities** elsewhere in the province.
- **She communities**: membership by descent, registration and practice.
- **Wuyuan**, administered from Jiangxi but tied to the old Huizhou world: which way belonging runs.
- **Wu-speaking Guangfeng and Yushan**: ties toward Zhejiang.
- **Jingdezhen** and **Pingxiang** (Anyuan): towns built by incomers and workers.
- **Registered locals against unregistered newcomers** in Nanchang.
- **Returnees** who came back from coastal factories.

## 4. Flags
