## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Nanchang parents** against **rural grandparent caregivers** in Gan-speaking counties.
- **Gannan Hakka families**, whose writing on schooling and girls' work may differ from Gan counties.
- **Lineage villages** of Ji'an and Fuzhou with a long exam history against **lake and mountain communities** with less of one.
- **She families**: language at home against Standard Chinese at school.
- **Jingdezhen craft families**, where nurture means training in a trade.
- **Boarding-school children** in rural counties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pre-modern schooling and on left-behind children; mixed on everyday child-rearing; thin on She and minority-language homes.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for rhymes and child words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** left-behind children and child welfare; birth-planning history; children's images online. Discipline and authority go to Capricorn; schooling as learning to Gemini and Sagittarius.
