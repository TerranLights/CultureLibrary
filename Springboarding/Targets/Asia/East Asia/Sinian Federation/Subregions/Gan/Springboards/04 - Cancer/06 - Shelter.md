*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); lands: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lakeside and river households** displaced by floods against **upland villages** sheltering kin.
- **Gannan Hakka communities**, whose origin story is itself one of refuge.
- **Lushan and other monastic centers**, with their own rules for guests.
- **Mountain-road towns** on old trade routes (Dayu, the Meiling pass) with inn and pavilion traditions.
- **Urban homeless people** in Nanchang, Jiujiang and Ganzhou, and **returning migrant workers** between jobs.
- **She communities** in the uplands: hospitality and lodging customs to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and pre-modern relief; mixed for present-day homelessness (official framing dominates); thin for shelters from violence at home.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Buddhist monastic vocabulary; Gan and Hakka for lodging customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention under the pre-2003 custody-and-repatriation system and its history; disaster-response criticism may be censored; homeless people's privacy. Asylum and housing law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
