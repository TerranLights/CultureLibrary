# Gan: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); lands: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Gan-speaking counties** with many absent adult children against **Nanchang** with care homes and paid aides.
- **Gannan Hakka villages**: care norms between sons, daughters and daughters-in-law.
- **Lake and mountain communities** far from clinics.
- **She households**: care and healing customs to check.
- **Childless and widowed elders** under state support.
- **Christian and Buddhist lay groups** that visit the sick *check*.
- **Care workers from Jiangxi** in other provinces (comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on elder care and rural programs; mixed on healing traditions; thin on She practice and on paid care in small towns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; medical vocabulary; Gan and Hakka care words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder neglect; conditions in care homes; official praise framing; health data on individuals. Inheritance and duties of support in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
