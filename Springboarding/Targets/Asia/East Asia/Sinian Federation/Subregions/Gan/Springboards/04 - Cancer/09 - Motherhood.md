*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (East Asia); lands: Jiangxi 江西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture (the mother of the nation, the holy mother, Mother Earth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking urban mothers** in Nanchang against **rural mothers** in Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun counties.
- **Gannan Hakka mothers**, with their own lineage rites and field-work traditions.
- **She mothers** in Guixi and Yanshan *check*: birth, naming and language at home.
- **Wuyuan** and the **Wu-speaking northeast**: confinement and in-law customs that may lean toward Anhui and Zhejiang.
- **Migrant mothers** working away from children, and **grandmothers** who mother in their place.
- **Fishing families** on Poyang Lake: thin data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical mother lore, health figures and birth-planning history; mixed for mothers' own voices; thin for She and lake communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for address terms and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-planning enforcement (forced abortion and sterilization history); sex-ratio questions; maternal deaths; official framing of model mothers. Marriage and reproductive law go to Libra; women's roles more broadly to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
