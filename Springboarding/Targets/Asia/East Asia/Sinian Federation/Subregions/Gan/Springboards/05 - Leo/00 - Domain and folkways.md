## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking north and center** (Nanchang, Yichun, Pingxiang, Xinyu): the urban, industrial core against rice villages.
- **Luling (Ji'an) and Linchuan (Fuzhou):** old literary and examination cultures with strong claims about talent.
- **Gannan Hakka** (Ganzhou counties: Ruijin, Xingguo, Ningdu, Yudu, Longnan): walled houses, mountain songs, tea-picking opera.
- **Northwest Hakka "huaiyuan" settlers** (Tonggu, Xiushui, Yifeng): separate from Gannan Hakka by origin.
- **Northeast** (Shangrao Wu speakers; Wuyuan Huizhou-culture villages; Jingdezhen's ceramic workers and incoming young artists).
- **Mandarin-speaking cities** (Jiujiang, Ganzhou city): river-port and garrison histories.
- **She 畲 communities** (Guixi, Yanshan, Yongfeng, Ji'an, Le'an): their own songs and festivals, as they share them.
- **Poyang Lake fishing communities and mining towns** (Anyuan, Dexing): occupational leisure cultures.
- **Labor migrants and left-behind families:** much leisure is now spent elsewhere or online.
- Federation-wide comparison only briefly; the Jiangxi merchant guildhalls (万寿宫) and Hakka communities abroad are for comparison, not as targets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for opera, ceramics, masked dance and classical literature; mixed for leisure statistics (often national, not provincial); thin for She communities and for northwest Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (classical Chinese for pre-modern texts); Gan, Hakka, Wu and Huizhou dialect knowledge for fieldwork and song texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** red-tourism and revolutionary sites are politically curated; religious festivals operate under state regulation; gambling beyond the state lotteries is illegal, so data are indirect. Gan's lands are listed as Jiangxi; this is a working list the author still confirms.
- **Held for review:** none.
