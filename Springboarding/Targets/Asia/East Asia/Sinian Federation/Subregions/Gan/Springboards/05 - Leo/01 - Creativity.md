## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jingdezhen's lineage workshops against incoming jingpiao artists:** old trade families and newcomers make things for different markets and reasons.
- **Gan-speaking literati heartlands** (Linchuan, Luling, Xiushui): a written tradition of poetic and scholarly creativity.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou-culture villages:** carving and building styles shared with southern Anhui, not with the Gan core.
- **Gannan Hakka:** walled-house building, bamboo and woodwork, song-making; compare by county.
- **She communities:** weaving, embroidery and song, as the communities share them; very little is published.
- **Industrial makers** (Nanchang aviation, Ganzhou rare-earth and tungsten towns): engineering invention against art.
- **Urban creative-industry workers against rural craftspeople** making for tourism.

## 4. Flags
