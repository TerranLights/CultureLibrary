*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Gan (Nanchang and other city varieties), Gannan Hakka, Shangrao Wu, Wuyuan Huizhou and She speech, since much expression happens in local speech and song. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking cities against villages:** Nanchang's urban youth culture against rice-growing counties.
- **Gannan Hakka:** a strong singing tradition that may license open expression; separate Xingguo, Ningdu, Yudu and Longnan.
- **She communities:** antiphonal singing as their own expressive form, as they share it.
- **Linchuan and Luling literati heritage:** written expression and its prestige.
- **Wuyuan and Shangrao:** Huizhou- and Wu-culture borderlands with different song and opera traditions.
- **Generations:** elders shaped by the collective era against young people online; returned migrant workers bring city styles home.
- **Women and men:** who may lament, sing love songs or argue in public.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drama, philosophy and folk song; mixed for modern psychology (national samples); thin for She speech and northwest Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for songs and videos; English for Western drama scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political expression is restricted and censored online; data on it will be indirect and research must not expose individuals. Religious and gender expression also sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
