# Gan: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Gan, Hakka, Wu, Huizhou and She varieties for local sayings and nicknames. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling) and Fuzhou (Linchuan):** pride rooted in scholars and exam success; compare their rivalry.
- **Nanchang:** the capital's civic and revolutionary titles against its residents' everyday pride.
- **Gannan Hakka counties:** Hakka identity pride, which may sit apart from or above Jiangxi provincial identity.
- **Jingdezhen:** a craft city with a world-famous name; trade families against newcomers.
- **Shangrao and Wuyuan:** borderlands whose people may identify with Zhejiang or Huizhou as much as with Jiangxi.
- **Jiujiang and Lushan:** river port and resort with a history of foreign presence.
- **She communities:** minority pride and state ethnic categories.
- **Rural migrants abroad in coastal cities:** pride in sending money home against being looked down on.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and literati pride; mixed for everyday pride; thin for She and northwest Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hakka and Gan for local sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary pride is official doctrine, so criticism is muted in the record; avoid treating regional jokes as findings about a people; national pride questions are politically loaded.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 伶官传序 (ctext.org, 新五代史) and collect modern commentary on 骄傲 and 忧劳兴国.
2. Search CNKI 知网 for 江西人 地域认同 and 庐陵文化 认同 for studies of provincial and city identity.
3. Search 万寿宫 江西会馆 for lists of guildhalls and studies of native-place pride.
4. Search Baidu Tieba and Zhihu for 江西 存在感 and 江西老表 to sample self-images (as data on talk, not facts).
5. Search 客家摇篮 赣州 for city branding and the Hakka conference record.
