## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou's examination lineages:** a long record of formal honors.
- **Jingdezhen:** a title-driven craft economy; masters against unnamed workers.
- **Gannan Hakka and the old revolutionary base counties:** recognition through martyrdom and service; compare Xingguo, Ruijin and Yudu.
- **She communities:** recognition as a people; compare townships in Guixi, Yanshan, Yongfeng and Ji'an.
- **Hakka in northwest Jiangxi:** a group less often recognized in provincial narratives.
- **Women:** honored in old gazetteers mainly for chastity; compare with modern honors.
- **Migrant workers:** often recognized as remitters at home, little recognized where they work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial honors, lineage records and official modern honors; thin for everyday credit and praise.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers and genealogies; Standard Chinese; Hakka and She oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr, rehabilitation and political-honor records can be restricted; ethnic-identification files sit in archives with limited access; genealogies are family property.
- **Held for review:** none.
