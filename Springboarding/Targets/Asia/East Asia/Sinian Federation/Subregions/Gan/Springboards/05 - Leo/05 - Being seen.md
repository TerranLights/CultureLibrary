# Gan: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for greetings, nicknames and child-protection customs; She and Huizhou varieties for community witnessing. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Left-behind families in Gan and Hakka counties** against families that moved together to the cities.
- **Nanchang's urban public** against market towns where everyone is known by sight.
- **Women across Gan, Hakka and Huizhou-culture areas:** different histories of public visibility.
- **She communities:** visibility inside provincial narratives and museums.
- **Wuyuan and other tourist villages:** residents who live under visitors' cameras.
- **Poyang Lake fishers resettled under the fishing ban:** a way of life fading from view.
- **Elders living alone** in hollowed-out villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for left-behind studies and census; mixed for online attention; thin for She and for everyday greeting norms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for greetings and folk practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance policy and data are restricted; studies of left-behind children involve minors, so use published research, not identifiable cases; tourism and livestream images involve residents' consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
