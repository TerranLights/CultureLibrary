# Gan: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for forms of address, funeral speech and labor talk; She for community usage. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mining and industrial towns** (Anyuan/Pingxiang, Dexing copper, Ganzhou tungsten): labor dignity and its memory.
- **Gan-speaking rice villages:** elders, funeral customs and the reform campaigns.
- **Gannan Hakka:** distinct burial and care customs.
- **She communities:** dignity of a people's name and recognition.
- **Women across generations:** from child-bride histories to migrant factory work.
- **Left-behind elders** against those living with children in cities.
- **Disabled people** in urban and rural Jiangxi.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor history and official programs; mixed for elder and end-of-life care; thin for She and for everyday courtesy norms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for funeral and kinship speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral-reform enforcement, poverty-labeling and labor disputes are politically sensitive; personal accounts may be censored; avoid identifying individuals in cases of degradation.
- **Held for review:** none.
