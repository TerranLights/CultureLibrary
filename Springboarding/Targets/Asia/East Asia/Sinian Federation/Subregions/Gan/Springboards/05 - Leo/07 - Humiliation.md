## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former Soviet base counties** (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu, Ji'an's Futian area): layered memories of political humiliation.
- **Jinggangshan uplands:** native and guest settlers, with old mutual contempt.
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages** of Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun: face and feud.
- **Gannan Hakka:** song contests and teasing with their own rules of winning and losing.
- **She communities:** a history of being named and treated as lesser.
- **Left-behind and migrant children:** bullying both at home and in host cities.
- **Women:** wedding pranks and gossip fall unevenly on brides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political campaigns (but filtered) and for wedding-prank and bullying policy; mixed for everyday face; thin for She and for Hakka song contests' rules.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for insults and teasing; reading Qing archival script.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution and Soviet-era purges are restricted topics, with archives often closed; victims' families may still be affected; treat regional mockery as data on talk, not as a description of anyone.
- **Held for review:** none.
