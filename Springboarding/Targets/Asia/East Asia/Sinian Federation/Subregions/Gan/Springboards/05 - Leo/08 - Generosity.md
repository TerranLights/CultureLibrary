## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages** (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun): lineage charity and banquet obligations.
- **Northern Jiangxi around Jiujiang and De'an:** the old communal-lineage ideal and Lushan's religious giving.
- **Gannan Hakka:** hospitality customs and remittances from Hakka migrants.
- **Wuyuan and Shangrao:** Huizhou- and Wu-culture villages with their own pavilion and charity records.
- **She communities:** feasting and mutual help, as they share them.
- **Migrant households:** remittances and gifts sent home.
- **Urban Nanchang donors and online givers** against village giving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and gazetteer charity; mixed for modern giving (national surveys, some provincial reports); thin for She and for informal help.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for steles and genealogies; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for gift vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity is regulated by the state and religious giving is supervised; donor lists in genealogies are family property; gift-giving to officials shades into bribery (leave the law to Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
