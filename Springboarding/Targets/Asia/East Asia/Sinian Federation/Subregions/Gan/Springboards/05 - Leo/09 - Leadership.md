# Gan: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for compacts, monastic rules and lineage records; Gan and Hakka for village leadership talk; She for community heads. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages:** heads chosen by seniority, wealth, learning or ability; compare Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun.
- **Gannan Hakka:** walled-house lineages, compact traditions and guerrilla-era memory.
- **Monastic and Daoist communities** (Lushan, Fengxin, Longhushan): religious leadership.
- **Jingdezhen's workshop heads** and kiln masters: leadership in a craft economy.
- **She communities:** their own heads and elders, as they describe them.
- **Women:** household heads in migrant-sending villages where men are away.
- **Urban firms and state-owned enterprises** in Nanchang against rural leadership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern compacts, lineages and official cadre training; mixed for informal village leadership; thin for She and for workplace leadership in private firms.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party leadership is political and official accounts dominate; criticism of local leaders is risky for informants; the formal right to command belongs to Capricorn, and law is not researched here.
- **Held for review:** none.
