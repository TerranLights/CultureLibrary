*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang and county towns:** screen play, malls and school pressure.
- **Gan-speaking villages:** traditional games and left-behind children.
- **Gannan Hakka:** dialect rhymes, festival dragons and lanterns.
- **Ji'an:** the Xie Jin wit tradition and local riddle culture.
- **She communities:** games and sports of their own, as they share them.
- **Poyang Lake communities:** water-based play and fishing.
- **Older adults:** mahjong rooms, cards and square dancing against younger generations online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk rhymes and tales; mixed for time use (national surveys); thin for She games and for dialect names of games.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for rhymes and game names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mahjong and cards with stakes shade into illegal gambling; minors' gaming is regulated; studies of children need consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
