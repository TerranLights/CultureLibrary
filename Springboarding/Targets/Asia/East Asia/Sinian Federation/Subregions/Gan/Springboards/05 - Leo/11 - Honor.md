# Gan: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for loyalist writings, lineage instructions and arches; Gan, Hakka and Huizhou varieties for reputation talk; She for origin narratives. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling):** the loyalist and literati model of honor.
- **Pingxiang and the Anyuan mining area:** brotherhood codes among laborers.
- **Wuyuan:** Huizhou merchant ideals of trust.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage mottos and walled-house defense; compare with Gan lineages.
- **She communities:** honor rooted in origin narratives, as they share them.
- **Women and men:** chastity and reputation against courage and loyalty; test the split, do not assume it.
- **Migrant and left-behind families:** honor sent home as money and success.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loyalist literature and lineage texts; mixed for women's honor (historical studies are national); thin for She narratives and for modern insult responses.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and She for oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related violence against women must be studied through survivors' and community voices; secret-society history is politically colored; She origin legends are sacred to the community and were used against them; law is not researched here.
- **Held for review:** none.
