# Gan: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for poetry; Gan and Hakka for everyday words of gladness and for festival songs; Wu, Huizhou and She varieties for local celebrations. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking north and center:** temple fairs, banquets and city festivals.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lanterns, suona and song; compare Shicheng, Yudu and Ningdu.
- **Wuyuan and Shangrao:** Huizhou- and Wu-culture celebrations.
- **She communities:** their own festivals of song, as they share them.
- **Urban young people:** concerts, nightlife and online fun against rural elders.
- **Religious communities:** Daoist, Buddhist and Christian forms of joy.
- **Left-behind elders and children:** joy at reunion during the New Year return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and music; mixed for well-being surveys (national designs); thin for She and for small-county customs.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for songs and festival speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious festivals are regulated; fireworks bans are contested; mental-health data are limited and sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
