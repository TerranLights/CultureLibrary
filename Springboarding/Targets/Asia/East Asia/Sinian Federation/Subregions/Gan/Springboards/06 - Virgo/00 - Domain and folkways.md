# Gan: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (its lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan, northwest hills); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan); Wu 吴语 (Shangrao border); Mandarin islands (Jiujiang, Ganzhou city); She 畲 speech; Classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, work and service: what good work and a well-kept life are taken to be, across work, service, the daily round and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Gan-speaking core, by subgroup:** Nanchang and the Changjing 昌靖 area (the capital's urban working day against its rice counties); Yichun and the Yiliu 宜浏 area; Ji'an/Luling 庐陵 (how learning and work were ranked in an old scholar-official region); Fuzhou/Linchuan 临川 (medicine and printing trades); Yingtan and Yiyang.
- **Jingdezhen 景德镇:** a one-craft city whose workers came from many counties; keep it apart from its hinterland.
- **Poyang Lake 鄱阳湖 and Gan River 赣江 households:** fishing and boat work with its own seasonal round.
- **Pingxiang 萍乡:** coal and fireworks work; a Gan variety near Xiang.
- **Gannan Hakka (Ganzhou, Ningdu, Shicheng, Xunwu, Longnan):** lineage villages and hill farming; Ganzhou city itself is a Mandarin-speaking island.
- **Northwest hill Hakka (Xiushui, Tonggu, Wanzai):** descendants of Qing hill settlers 棚民.
- **Wuyuan 婺源 (Huizhou speech) and the Shangrao Wu-speaking counties:** border cultures with ties east.
- **She 畲 townships (for example Guixi and Yanshan, *check*):** a small minority whose work customs may be under-recorded.
- **Out-migrant workers and the households left behind:** working lives spent largely outside the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for Jingdezhen, revolutionary history and statistics; thin for dialect-level daily life and the She.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan, Hakka, Huizhou and Wu for oral material; a She word list.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary and Cultural Revolution-era labor history (camps, cadre schools) is curated and partly closed; mining pollution may be restricted.
- **Scope:** the tracker carries Jiangxi only; the member state's exact lands are still to be confirmed by the author in Stage 0.
- **Held for review:** none.
