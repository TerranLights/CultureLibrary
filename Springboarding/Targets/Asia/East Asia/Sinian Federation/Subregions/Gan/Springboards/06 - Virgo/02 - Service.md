## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka lineages:** rotating lineage offices and service to the ancestral hall; compare across counties.
- **Old Soviet counties (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu, Ningdu):** where state memory of service is strongest; families' own accounts may differ.
- **Longhushan and Yingtan:** priestly families whose service is hereditary.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban service economy and the old mission cities.
- **Lushan, Wuyuan and Sanqingshan:** tourism communities serving outsiders.
- **Jingdezhen:** craft service owed to the court, then to state enterprise, now to the market.
- **She townships:** hospitality and mutual-help customs to be recorded on their own terms.
- **Poyang Lake villages:** mutual help in flood seasons.
- **Jiangxi domestic and service workers in other provinces:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on official and revolutionary service, thin on everyday hospitality and domestic service.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Red Army recruitment (扩红) and martyr counts are politically framed; read with care. Religious service at temples and churches may be under-reported.
- **Held for review:** none.
