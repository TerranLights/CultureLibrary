# Gan: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Gan 赣, the Jiangxi 江西 member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan; Hakka; Classical Chinese; She speech where recorded. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Gan-speaking counties with heavy out-migration:** left-behind elders and grandchildren; their roles may differ sharply from urban retirees'.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang urban retirees:** pensions, senior universities, square-dance groups.
- **Gannan Hakka lineages:** elders' standing in lineage halls; check whether age or usefulness grants it.
- **Poyang Lake households:** land and water whose "use" changed by policy.
- **Jingdezhen:** a craft city where old skills became useless and then heritage.
- **People with disabilities:** separate urban and rural experience.
- **She townships and Wuyuan's Huizhou villages:** customs for elders and helpers recorded apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: strong surveys on elders and migration; folk material needs digging in the collections.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for sayings; Classical Chinese for Li Gou and Wang Zhen.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child brides raised as household labor 童养媳 and child labor in older records; elder suicide in rural areas, to be handled with great care; disability terms that are now slurs (残废).
- **Held for review:** none.
