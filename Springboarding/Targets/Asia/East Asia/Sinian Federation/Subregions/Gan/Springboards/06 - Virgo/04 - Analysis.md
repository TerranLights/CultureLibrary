## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou/Linchuan and Jinxi:** home of Lu Jiuyuan's learning and of modern exam schools; two different models of the clever mind.
- **Ji'an/Luling:** a scholar region with its own heroes of evidence and argument.
- **Nanchang:** universities, the statistics bureau, industry engineers.
- **Gannan Hakka (Xingguo, Ningdu, Ganzhou):** fengshui masters and their clients beside modern planners.
- **Jingdezhen:** craft judgment against laboratory ceramics science.
- **Wuyuan:** Zhu Xi's ancestral county, Huizhou-speaking; how his legacy is used.
- **Rural and older people against urban graduates:** experience against schooling, as each describes it.
- **She communities:** their own ways of reckoning and reading weather, unrecorded so far.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Neo-Confucian debate and exam culture; thin for everyday reasoning in dialect.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (essential), Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for sayings and riddles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** statistical falsification is touchy; fengshui is officially "superstition" in some contexts, so practitioners may speak guardedly.
- **Held for review:** none.
