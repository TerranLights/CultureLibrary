## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangshu and Nancheng drug traders:** a commercial eye trained over generations.
- **Jingdezhen workshops and dealers:** the copyist's skill against the collector's eye.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou speech):** inkstone and tea connoisseurship in a merchant tradition.
- **Lushan and Xiushui tea areas:** palate expertise among growers.
- **Gannan Hakka:** proverbs and elders' counsel on judging strangers; check variation between counties.
- **Nanchang's urban young:** online reviews and anti-fraud messaging.
- **Rural elders:** most targeted by scams and health-product sellers (a research question).
- **Temple and Daoist communities (Yifeng, Yihuang, Longhushan):** spiritual testing.
- **She communities:** their own ways of judging matches and goods, unrecorded so far.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on goods and craft judgment; thin on how people judge each other day to day.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (Chan records, Hong Mai, Wang Yangming), Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and Huizhou for sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fraud victims underreport; fate calculation and some folk practice are officially frowned on; antique copying touches law (for law, see Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
