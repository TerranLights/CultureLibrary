# Gan: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation; lands: Jiangxi 江西; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan; Hakka; Huizhou (Wuyuan). **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jingdezhen imperial against folk kilns 民窑:** two standards of perfection side by side for centuries.
- **Jingdezhen locals against incoming artists 景漂:** inherited standards against new ones.
- **Wuyuan carvers and tea makers:** Huizhou craft standards.
- **Wanzai and Fenyi ramie weavers:** a home craft, often women's, with its own measures.
- **Nanfeng, Pingxiang and Wanzai Nuo troupes:** ritual exactness, troupe by troupe.
- **Students in Linchuan, Ji'an and Nanchang exam schools:** academic pressure (a research question, never assumed).
- **Nanchang's aviation and auto workers:** industrial tolerance for error (*check* plants).
- **Gannan Hakka and She makers:** weaving, bamboo and house-building standards, unrecorded so far.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for porcelain (archaeology, history, ethnography); thin for everyday perfectionism and its distress.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (Tang Ying, Song poetry), Standard Chinese; French for d'Entrecolles' originals; Gan and Hakka for workshop talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and mental health are sensitive; handle with care and through published studies only. Ritual knowledge of Nuo troupes is shared only as performers choose.
- **Held for review:** none.
