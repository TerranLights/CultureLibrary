# Gan: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (the Jiangxi 江西 lands of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan; Hakka. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an/Luling and Fuzhou/Linchuan:** home ground of famous critics in letters and statecraft; compare with the area's role in the 1930 purges.
- **Old Soviet counties (Ruijin, Ningdu, Xingguo, Yudu):** revolutionary practice of criticism, then and in memory.
- **Gannan Hakka singers:** mocking duels as licensed criticism.
- **Nanchang:** media, mediation television and urban complaint culture.
- **Lushan and Poyang Lake (Liyuzhou):** places where national criticism campaigns landed on local ground; locals' and outsiders' accounts differ.
- **Jingdezhen, Wuyuan, Lushan tourism workers:** criticism from visitors.
- **She communities and Huizhou-speaking Wuyuan:** their own rules on who may correct whom, unrecorded so far.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on elite and political criticism; thin on everyday family and workplace criticism.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for songs and daily speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Futian purges, the Lushan Conference and the Cultural Revolution are politically sensitive; archives are partly closed and memoirs may be self-censored. Political dissent today is a restricted subject; keep to published sources.
- **Held for review:** none.
