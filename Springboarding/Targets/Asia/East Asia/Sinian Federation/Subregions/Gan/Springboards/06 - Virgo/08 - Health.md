## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou, Nancheng and Nanfeng:** the heartland of learned medicine and drug processing.
- **Zhangshu:** a market town of medicine traders and Daoist herb lore.
- **Poyang Lake and Gan River lowlands:** schistosomiasis zones and the water-borne illness the campaigns fought.
- **Gannan Hakka counties:** fright healing, postpartum rules, mining harms.
- **Pingxiang and Shangli:** mining and fireworks work and its injuries.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** hospital medicine and urban health habits.
- **Lushan and Yichun:** health resorts and wellness trade.
- **She communities:** their own herb knowledge, shared only as healers choose.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou speech):** Huizhou medical lineages across the old border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical medicine and public-health campaigns; mixed for present-day folk healing.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (medical texts), Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and She for healers' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rare-earth pollution and mining health are sensitive and partly restricted; some folk healing is labeled superstition, so healers may speak guardedly; healers' knowledge is shared on their terms only.
- **Held for review:** none.
