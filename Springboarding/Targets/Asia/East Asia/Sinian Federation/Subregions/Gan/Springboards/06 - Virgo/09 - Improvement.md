# Gan: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, whose lands are Jiangxi 江西, within the Sinian Federation; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan; Hakka; She speech. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an/Luling and Fuzhou/Linchuan:** old exam regions where betterment ran through study.
- **Gannan Hakka lineages:** school-fields and later orchard development; compare counties.
- **Jinggangshan, Ruijin and other old Soviet counties:** improvement as state narrative.
- **Wuyuan villages:** tourism makeovers in a Huizhou-speaking county.
- **Jingdezhen and Nanchang:** urban renewal and its displaced.
- **Poyang Lake households:** land and water "improved" and then reversed by policy.
- **Gan and Hakka speakers of different generations:** language change felt as gain or loss.
- **She townships:** development received from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official development and exam history; thinner for residents' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (academy rules, gazetteers), Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation and demolition disputes are sensitive; poverty statistics are politically framed; thought reform (改造) belongs with its history and archives, which are partly closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
