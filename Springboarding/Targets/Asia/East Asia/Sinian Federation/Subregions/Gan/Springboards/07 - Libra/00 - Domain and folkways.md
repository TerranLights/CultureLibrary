# Gan: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua, simplified and traditional characters); Gan 赣语 and its subgroups; Hakka 客家话 (south and northwest); Huizhou, Wu and Mandarin varieties at the edges; She 畲 community speech. **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what is a fair bargain and a good partnership: marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance and justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking subregions:** Nanchang and the Poyang plain (Chang-Du group); Yichun and Pingxiang (Yi-Liu); Ji'an/Luling (Ji-Cha); Fuzhou/Linchuan (Fu-Guang); Yingtan and Yiyang (Ying-Yi). Separate them: lineage density, trade ties and dialect differ.
- **Hakka of Gannan** (Ruijin, Xingguo, Ningdu, Xunwu, Longnan and others) and the **northwestern Hakka settlers** of Tonggu, Xiushui and Wanzai: separate histories of settlement and of conflict with earlier residents.
- **She 畲 communities** (for example Guixi and Yanshan townships *check*): a minority with its own marriage and kin rules.
- **Edge speech areas:** Wuyuan (Huizhou speech); Shangrao, Yushan and Guangfeng (Wu); Jiujiang (Jiang-Huai Mandarin); Ganzhou city (a Southwestern Mandarin island).
- **Jingdezhen** porcelain workers of mixed origin; **Poyang Lake** fishing households; Christian, Daoist (Longhushan) and Buddhist communities.
- The federation as a whole, briefly, and Jiangxi-descended communities elsewhere, as comparison only.
