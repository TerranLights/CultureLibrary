# Gan: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (northeast); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an/Luling and Fuzhou/Linchuan:** old literati centers that produced judges and essays on punishment; compare their written ideals with county practice.
- **Nanchang and the Poyang plain:** the provincial courts and the petition offices; urban against lakeside villages.
- **Gannan Hakka counties:** dense lineage sanctions and frontier pacification history.
- **Northwestern Hakka settlers** (Wanzai, Tonggu, Xiushui): settler-native conflicts that reached Qing courts.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou speakers:** a contract-and-lawsuit document culture of its own.
- **Jingdezhen:** guild rules punishing those who broke trade custom.
- **Poyang Lake fishing households:** quarrels over fishing waters.
- **She communities:** their own customary sanctions, as they share them.
- The federation's courts as a whole, briefly; Jiangxi-born migrants elsewhere, as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Song to Qing judicial texts and present court reports; mixed for village practice; thin for the She.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for judgments and memorials; modern Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary-era purges, petitioning and recent judgments are politically sensitive; online access may change.
- **Held for review:** none.
