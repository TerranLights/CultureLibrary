*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语 (Nanchang, Yichun, Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yingtan varieties); Hakka 客家话; Huizhou, Wu and Mandarin varieties at the edges; She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou lineage villages:** heavy use of written division contracts and ritual shares.
- **Gannan Hakka:** shares inside enclosed-house (围屋) settlements and by lineage branch.
- **Wuyuan:** Huizhou document culture with dense lot-drawing records.
- **Poyang Lake fishers and Jiujiang's lakeside:** shares of water rather than land, and the fishing ban.
- **Jingdezhen:** shares of craft work among migrant trade groups.
- **She townships:** distribution rules inside a small minority, as they share them.
- **Urban Nanchang against rural counties:** queues, bill-splitting and exam competition in the city.
- The federation's exam and land rules, briefly, as the common frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for division contracts, land reform and exam history; mixed for present attitudes at the provincial level; thin for She and fishers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese for contracts; modern Chinese; Gan and Hakka for village terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land redistribution, land loss and resettlement compensation are sensitive; married-out daughters' cases touch on gender disputes.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Search library catalogs for "江西 分家书" and "婺源 阄书", and pull two or three printed collections.

3. Read the 兴国调查 sections on land division and note the principles named.
4. Search "江西 外嫁女 集体经济 分红 判决" for court cases on village shares.
5. Check the CGSS documentation for Jiangxi sample counts and its fairness items.
