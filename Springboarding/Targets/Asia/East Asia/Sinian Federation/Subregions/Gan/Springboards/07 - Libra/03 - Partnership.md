**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan, northwest); Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (Shangrao area); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Linchuan/Fuzhou and Ji'an:** literati families with written marriage alliances and partner networks.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang cities:** dating, matchmaking corners and late marriage in urban life.
- **Gannan Hakka counties:** child-bride history, sworn kin, and the song traditions of courtship.
- **Northwestern Hakka settlers:** marriage across the settler-native line and its history.
- **She townships:** uxorilocal marriage and song-duel courtship.
- **Wuyuan:** written marriage and business contracts in the Huizhou manner.
- **Jingdezhen:** business partnerships across groups of different origin.
- **Migrant couples** from rural Yichun, Shangrao and Ganzhou: partnership at a distance.
- The federation's marriage law, briefly, as the shared frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for merchant, porcelain and literary sources; mixed for present couples by county; thin for She and for fishers' crew partnerships.
- **Languages needed:** modern Chinese; classical and documentary Chinese for contracts; Hakka for song texts; Gan for kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child marriage, bride-buying and trafficked brides are sensitive; party-alliance history carries an official frame.
- **Held for review:** none.
