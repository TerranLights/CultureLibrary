# Gan: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech in Wuyuan; Wu in the northeast; Mandarin around Jiujiang; She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang and the Poyang plain:** flood history, lake policy and city work hours.
- **Fuzhou region (Nancheng, Nanfeng, Linchuan):** a medical tradition and a living Nuo rite.
- **Zhangshu and Yichun:** herb trade and the cooling-warming remedy economy.
- **Gannan Hakka (Xingguo, Ganzhou):** the siting tradition and enclosed-house design.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou speakers:** village siting and tea-garden landscape.
- **Lushan and Jiujiang:** academy teaching and Mandarin-speaking lakeside towns.
- **Poyang Lake fishers and farmers:** livelihoods that rise and fall with water levels.
- **She communities:** their own herbal knowledge, as they share it.
- The federation's water and ecology policies, briefly, as the outer frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Neo-Confucian, medical, siting and lake-ecology sources; mixed for daily work-rest data by province; thin for She herbal practice.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for medical and siting texts; modern Chinese; Gan and Hakka for remedies and sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** siting practice has been labeled superstition in official settings; healers' own knowledge may be held privately and shared only with consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
