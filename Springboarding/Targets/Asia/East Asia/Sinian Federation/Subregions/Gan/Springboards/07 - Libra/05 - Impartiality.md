## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou:** heavy exam success, so a long stake in fair selection and in faction debates.
- **Nanchang:** the provincial exam hall, today's provincial offices and courts.
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages of Yichun and Shangrao:** village elections and lineage voting.
- **Gannan Hakka:** elders judging kin inside large lineages.
- **Fenyi and Xinyu:** the local memory of Yan Song, to be compared with the national picture.
- **Yingtan and Guixi:** the hereditary Daoist office at Longhushan.
- **She townships:** small communities where every judge knows every party.
- **Wuyuan:** a contract culture where the middleman's standing was written down.
- Federation-wide civil-service rules, mentioned briefly, to set against how the province applies them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for exam, avoidance and corruption sources; mixed for village practice; thin for She communities.
