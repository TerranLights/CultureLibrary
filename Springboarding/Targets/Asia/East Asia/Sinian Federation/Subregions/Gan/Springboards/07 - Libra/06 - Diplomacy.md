# Gan: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for envoy records; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (Shangrao area); Jiang-Huai Mandarin (Jiujiang); She 畲 speech; English and French for treaty-port records. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiujiang and Lushan:** treaty-port and summer-resort history, foreign contact and Mandarin speech.
- **Nanchang:** provincial seat where local and foreign authorities met, then and now.
- **Ji'an and Poyang:** homes of noted envoys; how local memory treats them.
- **Gannan Hakka and She upland communities:** on the receiving end of pacification terms.
- **Wuyuan:** a population that negotiated its own provincial placement.
- **Pingxiang/Anyuan:** labor negotiation in a mining town.
- **Jingdezhen:** gift-making for outside powers.
- **Shangrao's Wu-speaking counties:** ties that face Zhejiang and Fujian.
- The federation's foreign relations, briefly, as a layer above the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for envoy records, treaty-port archives and Republican mediation; mixed for everyday tact; thin for She accounts of pacification.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; modern Chinese; English and French for consular and mission archives; Gan and Hakka for household diplomacy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** missionary incidents and frontier campaigns carry contested blame; the She were labeled bandits in Ming records, so read those sources critically and seek the community's own account.
- **Held for review:** none.
