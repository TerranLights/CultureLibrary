*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语 (all subgroups); Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (Shangrao, Yushan, Guangfeng); Mandarin (Jiujiang); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Gan-speaking counties of Yichun, Ji'an and Fuzhou:** dense gift ledgers and labor exchange in rice villages.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage feasts and the obligations they create.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban gift-money customs, and rising amounts.
- **Poyang Lake communities:** help in floods and between fishing households.
- **Shangrao's Wu-speaking counties and Wuyuan:** exchange customs that face Zhejiang and Huizhou.
- **She townships:** their own exchange customs, as they share them.
- **Migrant-sending households:** reciprocity stretched across provinces.
- Jiangxi-origin associations in coastal cities, as a comparison only.
- The federation's welfare and pension rules, briefly, as the state's side of the exchange.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for gift-economy scholarship and village practice; mixed for Jiangxi-specific surveys; thin for She exchange customs and for rotating-credit records.
- **Languages needed:** modern Chinese; classical Chinese for granaries and inscriptions; Gan and Hakka for interviews and ledgers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift ledgers are private family papers; rotating-credit collapses can involve fraud cases; anti-banquet campaigns are policy-sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
