*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (northeast); Jiang-Huai Mandarin (Jiujiang, Pengze); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** city families with exam choices and career options.
- **Pengze and the Jiujiang riverside:** a community that lived with an undecided project.
- **Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun lineage villages:** decisions taken jointly in ancestral halls.
- **Gannan Hakka:** temple and lineage consultation before major choices.
- **Yingtan and Guixi:** Longhushan's ritual specialists as advisers on choices.
- **She communities:** who decides for a household or village, as they share it.
- **Migrant-sending counties** (Shangrao, Ganzhou, Ji'an): decisions made at a distance by phone.
- The federation's planning procedures, briefly, as the outer limit on local decisions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for classical and religious texts and for exam-season material; mixed for village deliberation; thin for direct measures of indecisiveness in the province.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for poetry and Chan records; modern Chinese; Gan and Hakka for sayings and interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination and fate calculation are sometimes officially labeled superstition, so practitioners may be reluctant to talk; the nuclear project is a policy-sensitive topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
