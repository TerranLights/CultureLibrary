# Gan: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (Shangrao area); Mandarin (Jiujiang, Ganzhou city); She 畲 speech; Japanese for occupation-era records. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an/Luling and Yiyang:** homes of famous refusers; how local memory uses them.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** occupation-era collaboration and its later judgment.
- **Lushan:** a mountain that hosted both the 1937 turn and the 1959 silence.
- **Gannan Hakka and the old base-area counties:** memory of revolution and of purges kept quiet.
- **Lineage villages in Yichun, Fuzhou and Shangrao:** settlement customs after feuds.
- **Women, migrant workers and minors:** groups most often asked to give way; compare by place.
- **She townships:** their own peacemaking customs, as they share them.
- The federation's mediation policy, briefly, because it shapes how local disputes end.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Song loyalist texts, Republican and occupation history; mixed for household conflict; thin for She sources.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; modern Chinese; Japanese for occupation records; Gan and Hakka for village interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collaboration, the 1959 conference, violence at home and protection rackets are all sensitive; family members of those named may be living.
- **Held for review:** none.
