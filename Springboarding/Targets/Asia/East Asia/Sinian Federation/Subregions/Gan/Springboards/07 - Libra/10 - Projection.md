# Gan: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (pre-war Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; She 畲 speech; Huizhou speech (Wuyuan); Wu (northeast); Mandarin (Jiujiang). **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northwestern Hakka settlers** (Wanzai, Tonggu, Xiushui) and Gan-speaking natives: each side's image of the other.
- **She communities:** labeled from outside for centuries; their own account comes first.
- **Gannan Hakka:** a group once treated as newcomers, now a majority in the south.
- **Nanchang:** missionary-era rumors and present urban panics.
- **Yujiang and the Poyang lakeside:** disease campaigns and the blame they carried.
- **Base-area and other counties** with heavy Cultural Revolution violence, against quieter ones.
- **Wuyuan and Jiujiang:** speakers of other varieties who may see Gan people as outsiders, and the reverse.
- Images of Jiangxi held elsewhere in the federation, briefly, as the outside mirror.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Qing settler disputes, missionary riots and campaign history; mixed for folk healing; thin for the She community's own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; modern Chinese; English and French for mission sources; Gan, Hakka and She speech for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution violence, ethnic labels and the She's history are sensitive; folk healing may be shared only with consent; regional slurs must be recorded as claims, never adopted.
- **Held for review:** none.
