# Gan: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands carried: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语 varieties; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan); Wu 吴语 (Shangrao), Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan) and Mandarin (Jiujiang, Ganzhou city) pockets; She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how a people faces death, power and what is hidden, with inheritance, shared resources, secrets and sex ways as its traditional areas.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern plain and Poyang Lake (Nanchang, Jiujiang):** fishing and dike communities living off shared waters.
- **Ji'an / Luling 庐陵:** dense lineages and a scholar-official past; rich genealogies.
- **Fuzhou / Linchuan and Nanfeng:** Nuo villages; literary heritage.
- **Yichun, Pingxiang, Xinyu:** western Gan subgroups; the Anyuan mining past.
- **Gannan Hakka (Ganzhou, Ruijin, Ningdu, Longnan):** secondary burial, walled houses 围屋.
- **Shangrao (Wu) and Wuyuan (Huizhou culture):** Huizhou-style deeds and distinct dialects.
- **Jingdezhen:** a kiln-guild town of migrants.
- **She communities** (Yanshan 铅山, Guixi 贵溪, others *check*): own ancestor lore and rites.
- **Christians, Buddhists, Daoist ritual households; migrant workers' families:** separate liturgies; money and death handled at a distance.
