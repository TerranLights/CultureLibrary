# Gan: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Gan 赣语 (Nanchang, Yichun, Ji'an, Fuzhou varieties); Gannan Hakka; Shangrao Wu and Wuyuan Huizhou speech; She. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead and what death means, grief and mourning included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang and other cities against villages:** cremation, hospitals and funeral parlors against home death and burial.
- **Gan-speaking lineage areas (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun):** single burial in lineage hills.
- **Gannan Hakka:** secondary burial changes when a death is "finished."
- **Poyang Lake fishing families:** drowning deaths and death on the water.
- **She communities in Yanshan and Guixi *check*:** their own mourning rites.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou culture) and Shangrao (Wu speakers):** distinct funeral documents and dialect words.
- **Lushan and Ji'an Buddhist lay devotees; Catholics and Protestants in Jiujiang and Nanchang:** different liturgies of dying.
- **Elders in villages emptied by migration:** who sits with the dying when children work in Guangdong or Zhejiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical writing and revolutionary martyrdom; mixed for vital statistics below province level; thin for suicide and hospice detail.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka dictionaries for euphemisms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data are partly withheld; deaths in political campaigns (1930s purges, the famine years) are restricted topics; funeral rites of She and Hakka families are theirs to describe.
- **Elsewhere:** laws on cremation go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
