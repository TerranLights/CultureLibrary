## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lushan and Jiujiang Buddhist circles:** Pure Land rebirth as practiced, against Chan awakening around Yichun and Jing'an.
- **Daoist centers (Xishan, Gezaoshan 閤皂山, Longhushan):** ritual transformation and salvation rites for the dead.
- **Christian villages in the north and east *check*:** conversion as a break with lineage rites.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage birth rites and the region's state-led revival as a former Soviet area.
- **Poyang Lake resettled villages:** new life after flood.
- **Jingdezhen potters:** fire and remaking as a craft idiom.
- **She communities:** their own ancestor stories of origin and return, as they tell them.
- **Young migrants who return from coastal cities:** starting over at home.
