- **Gan lineage heartlands (Ji'an, Fuzhou):** expulsion and shaming inside lineages.
- **Gannan Hakka and rare-earth counties:** land and water purging as a live issue.
- **Lakeside counties (Yujiang, Poyang, Yugan):** schistosomiasis memory.
- **Former Soviet counties (Ruijin, Ningdu, Xingguo, Yongfeng):** purge memory inside revolutionary families.
