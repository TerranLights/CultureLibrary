## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage areas (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun):** loyalty owed to lineage against loyalty owed to a spouse.
- **Xinyu and Fenyi:** a county that lives with a famous "traitor" son.
- **Pingxiang and the former Soviet counties of Gannan:** revolutionary loyalty, defectors and their families.
- **Gannan Hakka:** song traditions of love and broken faith; walled-house communities.
- **Shangrao (Wu speakers) and Yanshan:** loyalist literature and the wartime camp.
- **Migrant-sending villages:** marriages strained by distance.
- **Nanchang:** urban divorce and online exposure culture.
- **She communities:** their own rules on marriage and loyalty, as they describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dynastic and revolutionary loyalty; mixed for marriage; thin for business betrayal outside merchant studies.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for songs and insults.
