## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka:** walled-house life and the Ming legacy of policing a frontier.
- **Poyang Lake fishers and dike farmers:** control of water as daily work and dispute.
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages (Ji'an, Fuzhou):** elders' control inside lineages.
- **Jingdezhen potters:** craft control of fire.
- **Linchuan and other exam-school towns:** control of youth for exams.
- **Nanchang urban households:** two-earner couples and money control.
- **She communities:** village governance in She townships, as they describe it.
- **Mining towns (Pingxiang, Dexing):** company and union control of labor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ming social-control texts and water works; mixed for household control; thin and sensitive for state control in recent decades.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; dialect for household idioms.

- **Law:** state regulation of births and policing goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
