## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage areas (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun):** trust anchored in lineage estates and managers.
- **Jiujiang and De'an:** a lineage that claims a famous model of communal trust.
- **Poyang Lake communities:** migration-origin memory and fishing partnerships.
- **Gannan Hakka:** sworn kin and walled-house solidarity.
- **Jingdezhen:** a market town of migrants and craft reputation.
- **Wuyuan and Shangrao:** Huizhou-style contracts and middlemen.
- **She communities:** trust between She and Han neighbors, as each side describes it.
- **Returned migrants and urban Nanchang:** online ratings and app-based trust against acquaintance trust.
