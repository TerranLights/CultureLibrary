## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan lineage heartlands (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun):** feud memory between neighboring lineages.
- **Gannan Hakka and native Gan speakers in border counties (Wanzai, Tonggu, Xiushui):** settler and native grudges.
- **Former Soviet counties (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu, Ningdu):** revolutionary and counter-revolutionary reprisals in family memory.
- **Poyang Lake fishers:** fights over waters and catches.
- **Mining districts (Pingxiang, Dexing):** labor grievances and retaliation.
- **She communities:** their own ways of settling wrongs, as they describe them.
- **Urban Nanchang youth:** online revenge and shaming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing feuds and revolutionary narrative; mixed for folk tales; thin for present-day revenge outside press stories.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for memorials; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for oral material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reprisals after 1934 and land-reform killings are politically sensitive; feud memory can reopen old quarrels; press crime stories carry stigma for places.
- **Law:** punishment of revenge crimes and mediation procedure go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
