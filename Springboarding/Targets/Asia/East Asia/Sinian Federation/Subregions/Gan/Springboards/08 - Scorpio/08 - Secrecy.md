## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka and the Fujian border counties:** sworn-society history and mountain hiding places.
- **Former Soviet counties (Ruijin, Yudu, Ningdu):** revolutionary secrecy and its memory.
- **Longhushan and Gezaoshan Daoist families:** restricted ritual knowledge.
- **She communities:** their own rules on what may be told.
- **Jingdezhen potters and Zhangshu pharmacists:** craft and trade secrets.
- **Gan lineage villages (Ji'an, Fuzhou):** genealogy silences and gossip in close communities.
- **Nanchang and other cities:** privacy as a modern claim against family and neighbors.
- **Families of adoptees and child brides:** origins kept quiet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revolutionary secrecy and Qing secret societies; mixed for craft secrets; thin for everyday privacy at province level.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and She for argots and rites.
