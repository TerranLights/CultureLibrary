## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou scholar-official lineages:** pre-modern power through examinations.
- **Nanchang:** provincial capital, seat of party and state power.
- **Jingdezhen:** imperial and later state power over a craft town.
- **Longhushan Daoist families:** hereditary sacred power.
- **Western highlands (Jinggangshan, Yongxin, Ninggang):** bandit chiefs, lineages and party power.
- **Gannan Hakka counties (Xingguo, Ruijin, Yudu):** military careers and Soviet-era power.
- **Pingxiang and Anyuan:** workers' power and its legend.
- **Poyang Lake counties:** strongmen over water and sand.
- **She communities:** their place in township government, as they describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Song–Ming elites and revolutionary history; mixed for present-day local power (official and press only); thin for informal power inside families.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for local terms of power.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** present-day party power, the killing of Yuan Wencai and Wang Zuo, and "black and evil" cases are sensitive; official lists show only formal power.
- **Law:** legitimate authority and obedience go to Capricorn; election rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
