## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Poyang Lake and Yangtze counties (Jiujiang, Poyang, Yugan, Duchang):** flood and drought as recurring crises.
- **Gannan Hakka:** migration and war as founding crises; mountain refuges.
- **Former Soviet counties (Yudu, Ruijin, Ningdu):** the 1934 collapse and its aftermath.
- **Nanchang:** wartime battle and urban emergency services.
- **Industrial and mining towns (Fengcheng, Pingxiang, Dexing):** workplace disasters.
- **Fireworks counties (Wanzai, Shangli 上栗 *check*):** risk built into a home industry.
- **Migrant-sending villages:** crises arriving from coastal economies.
- **She communities:** how small mountain communities meet disaster, as they describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for floods and pre-modern calamity records; mixed for industrial disasters; thin and restricted for the famine years and for personal crisis.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers; Standard Chinese; dialect for oral flood and migration memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine deaths, wartime germ warfare and accident blame are sensitive; casualty figures vary by source (do not settle them here).
- **Law:** emergency law and accident liability go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
