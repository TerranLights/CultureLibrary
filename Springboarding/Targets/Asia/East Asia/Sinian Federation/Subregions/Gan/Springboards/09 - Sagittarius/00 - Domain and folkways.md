# Gan: The domain, belief and philosophy (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for pre-modern texts; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan, northwest hills); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan); Wu 吴语 (Shangrao-Yushan-Guangfeng); Jianghuai Mandarin (Jiujiang) and Southwestern Mandarin (Ganzhou city). **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: religion, philosophy, higher learning and long journeys; what is true, and where meaning is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang and the Gan-Fu plain:** the Xu Xun cult and Hongzhou Chan memory.
- **Ji'an (Luling):** literati lineages and academy culture.
- **Fuzhou (Linchuan) and Nanfeng:** Lu Jiuyuan's home ground; nuo ritual.
- **Yingtan and Guixi:** Longhushan's priesthood and its villages.
- **Shangrao, Yushan, Guangfeng:** Wu-speaking border; the Sanqingshan 三清山 Daoist landscape.
- **Wuyuan:** Huizhou culture and speech.
- **Jingdezhen:** a craft town of migrant workers with guild cults.
- **Jiujiang and Lushan:** treaty-port and mission history.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage ritual and geomancy.
- **Northwest hill Hakka (Tonggu, Xiushui):** later settlers among Gan neighbors.
- **She 畲 townships (Guixi, Yanshan):** distinct ancestor rites *check*.
- **Poyang Lake fishing communities:** water deities.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi merchant halls elsewhere in the federation; Jiangxi labor migrants in Guangdong and Zhejiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists one province, Jiangxi, as a working list the author still confirms in Stage 0; this sign's sheets are written for those lands.
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern religion and philosophy; mixed for present-day belief (few surveys break out one province reliably); thin for She and Poyang Lake communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan, Hakka, Huizhou and Wu for fieldwork; Japanese for much Daoist and Chan scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered groups and the 邪教 ("heterodox teaching") label are politically sensitive; some Daoist ritual texts stay within priestly families.
- **Held for review:** none.
