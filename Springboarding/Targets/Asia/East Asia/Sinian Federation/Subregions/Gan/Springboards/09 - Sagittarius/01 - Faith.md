# Gan: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou and Wu varieties in the northeast; She 畲 community speech. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang-area villages around Xishan:** a faith centered on one patron saint and his miracles.
- **Lushan and Jiujiang:** Pure Land laity beside the oldest Protestant communities.
- **Longhushan and Guixi:** households whose livelihood is ritual service; faith as hereditary craft.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage-run offering rites; whether faith is held by the family or the person.
- **Fuzhou and Nanfeng:** exorcist troupes; faith expressed through performance.
- **Jingdezhen:** guild and kiln deities; faith tied to the dangers of a trade.
- **Rural Protestants (provincewide):** conversion stories and their reasons, as converts tell them.
- **Catholic villages:** inherited faith across generations.
- **She communities:** ancestor-hero faith beside Han neighbors.
- **Party members and cadres:** official political faith and its private reception.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi migrants in coastal cities who join or leave congregations there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhist and Daoist institutions; mixed for popular practice (scattered in CNKI articles and local gazetteers); thin and sensitive for unregistered Christians.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for village terms; French for the Jesuit letters; Japanese for Daoist studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered congregations and their members must not be identifiable; Daoist register texts may be restricted to the ordained.
