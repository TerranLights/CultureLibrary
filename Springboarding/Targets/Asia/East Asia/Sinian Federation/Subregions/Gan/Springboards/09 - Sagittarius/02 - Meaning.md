# Gan: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou speech in Wuyuan. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou literati villages:** legacy measured in degrees and books; compare how heirs today speak of it.
- **Gannan Hakka:** founding-ancestor stories and fortified houses 围屋; meaning anchored in migration and descent.
- **Wuyuan:** Huizhou merchant families; meaning tied to trade abroad and return home.
- **Jingdezhen:** old kiln families against newcomer artists; inherited trade versus chosen calling.
- **Nanchang and Ganzhou cities:** salaried and service workers; where office culture sets the terms.
- **Rural migrant-sending counties (e.g. Yudu, Poyang, Duchang):** meaning split across two places.
- **Lushan and Longhushan religious communities:** monastic and priestly vocations.
- **She communities:** ancestor-hero lineage as a frame for a person's place.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi workers in Guangdong and Zhejiang factories, whose findings apply back home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and lineage sources; mixed for present-day attitudes; thin and sensitive for despair and suicide.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and personal family registers need care; living people named in local studies.
- **Held for review:** none.
