## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Soviet counties (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu, Ningdu):** where "liberation" is local memory, not only a textbook word.
- **Jinggangshan highlands:** the base area; freedom bound to revolutionary sacrifice.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban professionals; personal freedoms in work and leisure.
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou lineage villages:** the pull between lineage duty and individual choice.
- **Hakka of Gannan and the northwest hills:** settler histories at the edge of state registration.
- **She communities:** past and present standing relative to state categories.
- **Jingdezhen:** independent studio artists beside factory and state-firm workers.
- **Women and men separately in each area:** marriage and mobility differ by sex; a question to research.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi migrant workers in coastal cities, living under another city's registration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official liberation history; mixed for pre-modern and religious sources; thin and filtered for present-day political views.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews; English for diaspora and overseas scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political freedom, the Lushan Conference and religious freedom are sensitive; protect informants. Statements of rights in law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
