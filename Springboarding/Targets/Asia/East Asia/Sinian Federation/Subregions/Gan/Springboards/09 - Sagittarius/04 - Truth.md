*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Jianghuai and Southwestern Mandarin in Jiujiang and Ganzhou city. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou (Jinxi) and the Lu school heartland:** where "truth from the mind" has local pride; compare with Zhu Xi's academy at Lushan.
- **Ji'an:** the Jiangyou Wang school and the Futian memory; two very different contested truths.
- **Gannan Soviet counties:** official revolutionary history beside family memories.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban readers of provincial and national news.
- **Fuzhou's Yihuang and other rural counties:** where land disputes test official accounts.
- **Jingdezhen:** a market where telling a genuine antique from a copy is daily business.
- **Hakka and She communities:** genealogy and oral origin stories as records held true by their keepers.
- **Religious communities (Daoist, Buddhist, Christian):** revealed or transmitted truth beside the official view.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi migrants elsewhere who get local news through family chat groups.

## 4. Flags
