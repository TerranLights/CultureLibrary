## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Poyang Lake shore counties (Duchang, Poyang, Yugan):** water beliefs tied to fishing and shipping risk.
- **Jingdezhen:** guild beliefs among kiln workers, and how studio artists treat them.
- **Gannan Hakka:** household taboos and geomantic beliefs bound to lineage.
- **She townships:** an origin belief distinct from their Han neighbors'.
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou lineage villages:** ancestor beliefs and Neo-Confucian rejection of some popular cults.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban skepticism and urban temple-going side by side.
- **Christian villages and congregations:** creeds learned in catechism and Bible study.
- **Party members and state employees:** a stated atheism and what is kept at home.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi workers in coastal cities, who may carry or drop village beliefs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore collections and gazetteers; mixed in surveys (belief items exist, provincial samples small); thin for She beliefs in Jiangxi.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for tales and taboos; French for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "superstition" label can stigmatize informants; She sacred scrolls may not be shown to outsiders; belief questions to party members are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
