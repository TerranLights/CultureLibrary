## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling):** a self-image built on integrity; compare with its everyday politics now.
- **Shangrao and Yiyang:** two exemplars seven centuries apart (Xie Fangde and Fang Zhimin).
- **Ningdu and the Gannan hills:** Ming loyalists and later Soviet martyrs in the same landscape.
- **Gannan Hakka:** the "stiff-necked" self-description; test against what people actually say.
- **Pingxiang and Anyuan:** a miners' tradition of the strike.
- **Nanchang:** where official commemoration of conviction is staged for the whole province.
- **Christian and Buddhist communities:** religious steadfastness under past campaigns.
- **She communities:** their histories of holding to their own rites.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi workers in coastal factories, where labor disputes test resolve.
