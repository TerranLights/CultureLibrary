## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wuyuan:** a Huizhou merchant tradition with a written record of trust and credit.
- **Zhangshu and Nancheng:** medicine-trade towns where reputation was the product.
- **Jingdezhen:** a market where copying is a craft and buyers must judge authenticity.
- **Gannan orange country and Nanfeng:** growers facing counterfeit origin claims.
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou lineage villages:** lineage rules and elders as keepers of a person's word.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang:** urban consumers and service trades, where reviews decide reputations.
- **Hakka and She villages:** smaller markets where everyone knows the seller.
- **Officials and state employees:** the honesty standard set in campaigns, compared with public talk about it.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi traders in other provinces, whose dealings carry the home reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and official campaigns; mixed for everyday honesty; thin for minority communities.
