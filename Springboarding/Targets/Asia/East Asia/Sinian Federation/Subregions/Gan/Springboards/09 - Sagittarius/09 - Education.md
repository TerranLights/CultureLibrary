## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling):** a historic exam elite; compare its present schooling outcomes with the claim.
- **Fuzhou and Linchuan:** scholarly epithets and present exam-school fame.
- **Wuyuan:** Huizhou lineage schooling and Zhu Xi's memory.
- **Nanchang:** the province's universities and the urban middle class.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lineage-funded study; rural boarding schools now.
- **Jinggangshan and Ruijin:** revolutionary schooling and today's political-education visits.
- **Jingdezhen:** craft training and a ceramics university side by side.
- **Labor-sending counties:** children schooled while parents work away.
- **She communities:** minority education policy in practice.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi students at universities in other provinces, and migrant children schooled outside Jiangxi.

## 4. Flags
