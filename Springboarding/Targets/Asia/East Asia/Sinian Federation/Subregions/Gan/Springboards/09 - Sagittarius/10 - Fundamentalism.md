## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an lineage villages:** Neo-Confucian ritual purism written into lineage rules; did it hold against popular cults?
- **Fuzhou and Jinxi:** the Lu school, once called heterodox by orthodox Zhu followers.
- **Longhushan:** a priesthood that certifies who is a proper Daoist; a boundary, not a literalism.
- **Fengxin and Yifeng monasteries:** strict monastic codes and their revival.
- **Rural Protestant networks:** registered churches beside groups with end-times or exclusive teachings.
- **Catholic communities:** registered and unregistered, with different lines of authority.
- **Counties fought over in the Taiping war:** memory of iconoclasm.
- **Urban classics-reading parents (Nanchang):** revival as an education choice.
- **She communities:** rites pressed by Han, Christian and state orthodoxies in turn.
- **Comparison only:** Jiangxi migrants who join strict churches in coastal cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern orthodoxy and heterodoxy; mixed for present-day strict religion (scholarship is mostly national); state sources dominate on banned groups.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for village interviews.
