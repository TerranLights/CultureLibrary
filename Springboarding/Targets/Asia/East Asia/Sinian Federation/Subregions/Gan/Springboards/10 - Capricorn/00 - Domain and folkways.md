# Gan: Domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands carried: Jiangxi 江西 (working list, for the author to confirm). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua, simplified and traditional characters, Classical Chinese for older texts); Gan 赣语 dialects; Hakka 客家话 of the south and the western highlands; Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan, Fuliang), Wu 吴语 (Guangfeng, Yushan) and Jiang-Huai Mandarin (Jiujiang) at the edges; She 畲 community speech. **Concept in one line:** the domain of authority and duty: who commands, who obeys, and what is owed; with career, status, reputation, and the age, time, rank, order and power ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking north and center** (Nanchang, Yichun, Xinyu, Pingxiang): the capital and the industrial west.
- **Luling 庐陵, Ji'an**, and **Linchuan 临川, Fuzhou**: two literati heartlands with separate local histories of office and learning.
- **Gannan 赣南 Hakka**, and the **Hakka of the western highlands** (Jinggangshan, Tonggu, Xiushui): lineage and walled-house societies, and settler-native tension.
- **Northeast:** Huizhou-speaking Wuyuan, Wu-speaking Guangfeng and Yushan, and Jingdezhen's craft hierarchies.
- **Poyang Lake** fishing communities: work and order unlike the rice villages.
- **She communities** (e.g. Guixi, Yanshan *check*): an ethnic minority with its own offices of ritual.
- **Religious hierarchies:** Zhengyi Daoists, Chan monasteries, Christian communities.
- Comparison only: Jiangxi people living outside the state; findings for Gan apply to them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for the pre-modern elite and the revolutionary period; thinner for women's, minorities' and workers' own voices; Jiangxi survey subsamples may be small.
- **Languages needed:** Modern and Classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka; some Huizhou and Wu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary history is politically framed in official sources; some genealogies are private; She ritual items are studied only as the community allows.
- **Held for review:** none.
- **Lands:** the exact lands of Gan are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for Jiangxi as the working list.
