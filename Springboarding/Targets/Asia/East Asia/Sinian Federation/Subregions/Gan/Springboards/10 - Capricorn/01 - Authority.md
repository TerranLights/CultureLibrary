*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Putonghua and Classical Chinese; Gan dialects; Hakka; Huizhou and Wu at the northeastern edge; She speech. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking plains villages** of Nanchang, Yichun and Ji'an, where single-surname lineages are strong: lineage heads against state cadres.
- **Gannan Hakka counties** (e.g. Xunwu, Xingguo, Ruijin): walled-house lineages and the revolutionary base's later claims.
- **Highland settlers and natives** (Jinggangshan, Wanzai, Tonggu): authority split by who arrived first.
- **Jingdezhen**: craftsmen under court, then factory, then market authority.
- **Longhushan and Daoist households** of Yingtan and Guixi: inherited religious standing.
- **She communities**: ritual offices and elders against township officials.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou)** and **Wu-speaking Shangrao**: lineage compacts on another dialect's terms.
- **Nanchang city and its professionals**: workplace and expert authority, and generational change.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for officials' own writings and revolutionary history; mixed for village life; thin for open survey items on obedience at the Jiangxi level.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and modern Chinese; Gan and Hakka for village fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about resisting state and party authority are sensitive to ask and to answer inside the federation; sources on 1959 and on revolutionary violence vary by publisher; She ritual knowledge only as the community shares it.
- **Measures:** the permission share and correctional-control share are collected at Stage 2, for the federation and, where possible, this member state.
- **Held for review:** none.
