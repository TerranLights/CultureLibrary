## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou literati heartlands**: where loyalist and filial exemplars are most celebrated.
- **Gannan Hakka**: lineage estates, grave rites, and the revolutionary families' claims of sacrifice.
- **Out-migrating counties** (e.g. the hill counties of Ganzhou, Shangrao and Yichun): duty carried from afar by working children.
- **Nanchang and other cities**: smaller families, pensions, and paid care.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou lineages**: duties written in hall rules under another dialect tradition.
- **She communities**: ancestor duties in their own forms.
- **Christian families** (Catholic and Protestant): duty to God set beside duty to lineage rites.
- **Women** across all groups: daughters, daughters-in-law and widows carry different duties, to research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exemplary duty in the elite record; rich in surveys for elder care; thinner for those who refused or failed a duty.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for loyalist and gazetteer texts; Hakka for songs and rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary sacrifice is a state narrative with little room for dissent; chastity records touch on coercion of women.
- **Held for review:** none.
