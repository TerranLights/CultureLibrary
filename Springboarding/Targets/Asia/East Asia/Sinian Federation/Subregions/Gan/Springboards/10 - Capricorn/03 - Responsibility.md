## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Poyang Lake shore and Gan River plains**: flood-prone communities where responsibility for dikes and fishing grounds is shared or disputed.
- **Gannan Hakka hill counties**: lineage liability, and mining's effects on land.
- **Out-migrant households** across the state: grandparents and children carrying the household load.
- **Industrial towns** (Pingxiang, Xinyu, Fengcheng, Dexing): workplace safety and blame.
- **Jingdezhen**: answerability for craft quality, from court to market.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou heritage** and **Wu-speaking Shangrao**: contract and guarantor customs.
- **She communities**: who answers for a member's wrong, as they describe it.
- **Women**: the household's mental load and who carries it, to research rather than assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and policy; mixed for everyday blame and apology; thin for minority customs.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Song texts and gazetteers; Huizhou document conventions; Gan and Hakka for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official accident and disaster findings are framed by the state; independent reporting on mining and floods may be limited.
- **Held for review:** none.
