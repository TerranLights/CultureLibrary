## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chan and Daoist religious communities** (Yunju Shan, Baizhang Shan, Xishan, Longhushan): disciplines of the body and mind.
- **Lushan and Ji'an academy towns**: the classical school model and its modern heirs.
- **Linchuan and other exam-focused counties**: study discipline under heavy competition.
- **Gannan Hakka families**: lineage rules on child-rearing, compared with Gan-speaking plains families.
- **Jingdezhen workshops**: apprentice training in a craft.
- **Factory and mining towns** (Pingxiang, Xinyu, Dexing): work discipline.
- **She communities**: the training of ritual specialists, as the community shares it.
- **Generations**: grandparents raising left-behind children against parents returning from the cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious, scholarly and state-campaign discipline; mixed for household practice; thin for minority and women's accounts.
