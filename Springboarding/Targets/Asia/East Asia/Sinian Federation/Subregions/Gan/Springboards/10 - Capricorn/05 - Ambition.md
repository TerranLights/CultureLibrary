*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Linchuan (Fuzhou) and Luling (Ji'an)**: long histories of sons sent toward office, and of skepticism about it.
- **Merchant districts** (Zhangshu, Hekou, Nanchang's old guild quarters): ambition through trade rather than office.
- **Out-migrating hill counties** (Ganzhou, Shangrao, Yichun, Ji'an uplands): migrant work as the main road up.
- **Gannan Hakka**: lineage hopes for sons, and later the pull of Guangdong's factories.
- **Jingdezhen**: craft ambition, old porcelain families against newcomers.
- **Nanchang's professional class**: civil service, state firms and private business compared.
- **She and other small communities**: what rising means where land and language set people apart.
- **Women**: daughters' ambitions and the limits placed on them, to research without assuming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite and merchant history; good for migration surveys; thin for personal accounts of failed or refused ambition.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese and Ming drama language; Gan and Hakka for village interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "lying flat" discourse is policed online; local-government ambition in failed firms is a politically touchy topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
