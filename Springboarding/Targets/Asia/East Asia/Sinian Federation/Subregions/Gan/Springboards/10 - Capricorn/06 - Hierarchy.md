## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking single-lineage villages** (e.g. around Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun): big and small surnames side by side.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou villages**: the bond-servant legacy and its memory.
- **Hakka and settler hills** (Gannan, Jinggangshan, Wanzai, Tonggu, Xiushui): native-settler ranking.
- **Former landlord and poor-peasant families**: the afterlife of class labels across generations.
- **Jingdezhen**: rank among trades and among home counties.
- **She communities**: ethnic rank in the past and now.
- **Officials and ordinary residents in Nanchang**: grade and access in the state system.
- **Women and men**, elder and younger: household rank, to research case by case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and land-reform history; mixed for present-day class talk; thin for the voices of servile lineages and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese and document conventions; Huizhou and Hakka for local terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class labels, landlord families and land reform are sensitive; descendants of bond-servant lineages may not want the label raised; She historical slurs are recorded only to describe, never adopted.
- **Held for review:** none.
