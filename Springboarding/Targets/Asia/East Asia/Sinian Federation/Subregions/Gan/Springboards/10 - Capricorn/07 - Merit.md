## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling) and Fuzhou (Linchuan)**: counties with long claims of exam success, to compare with less successful ones.
- **Northeast Huizhou-linked Wuyuan**: lineage-funded study and merchant money.
- **Settler and Hakka districts** (Wanzai, the western highlands, Gannan): access to the exams and to posts.
- **Xingguo and the old Soviet counties**: merit claimed through revolutionary service.
- **Jingdezhen**: craft titles against family workshops.
- **Urban professionals and county-town youth**: civil-service exams and connections today.
- **She and other small communities**: preferential policies and how they are viewed.
- **Daughters and sons**: unequal access to schooling in the past, to research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for the examination era (lists, databases, monographs); good for modern exam statistics; thin for honest accounts of connections.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for exam records; Putonghua for modern data; Hakka for settler histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption in hiring and exams is sensitive; minority bonus points in the gaokao draw resentment that should be described, not echoed.
- **Held for review:** none.
