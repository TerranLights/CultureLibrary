## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages** of Ji'an, Fuzhou and Yichun: lineage rules, mediation and the courts.
- **Gannan Hakka**: walled-house communities, settler disputes over land and quotas, and the revolutionary legal legacy.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou heritage)**: a deep contract culture with its own written conventions.
- **Poyang Lake fishers**: water rights and fishing bans where state rules meet custom.
- **Jingdezhen**: guild rules and the courts in a craft town.
- **She communities**: customary order and state law.
- **Nanchang and the cities**: courts, lawyers and petitioning in an urban setting.
- **Christians and Daoist households**: religious rules beside civil ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Song to Qing legal sources and inscriptions; mixed for present practice; court databases have been thinned.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese and legal-document conventions; Gan and Hakka for dispute fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning, corruption and legal activism are sensitive; the "fond of lawsuits" label is an outsiders' stereotype to examine, never to adopt; the letter of the law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
