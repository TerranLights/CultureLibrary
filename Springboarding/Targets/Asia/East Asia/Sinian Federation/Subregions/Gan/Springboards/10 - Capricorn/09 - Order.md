## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan Hakka and the old frontier counties**: walled villages, bandit history, fengshui traditions.
- **Gan River and Poyang Lake communities**: flood, fishing and the order of water.
- **Nanchang and other cities**: grid governance, campaigns, and generational quarrels over public space.
- **Northeast villages** (Wuyuan, Wu-speaking Shangrao): Huizhou-style village planning and New Year rites.
- **Western highlands** (Pingxiang, Jinggangshan): mining, revolution and order imposed afterward.
- **Jingdezhen**: a crowded craft town's own order of trades and streets.
- **She communities**: their own settlement layout and rites.
- **Daoist and Buddhist communities**: ritual order in the cosmos and the monastery.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern local order and the 1930s; mixed for present-day safety statistics; thin for open data on unrest.
