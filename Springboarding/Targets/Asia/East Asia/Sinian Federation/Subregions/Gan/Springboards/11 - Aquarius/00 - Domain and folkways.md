# Gan: Domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; pre-war lands carried: Jiangxi 江西 (the working list, still to be confirmed by the author). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (Putonghua) and written Chinese; Gan 赣语 (Chang-Du 昌都, Yi-Liu 宜浏, Ji-Cha 吉茶, Fu-Guang 抚广 and Ying-Yi 鹰弋 subgroups *check*); Hakka 客家话 (Gannan and the northwest highlands); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan, Fuliang), Wu 吴语 (Shangrao, Guangfeng, Yushan) and the Mandarin of Jiujiang and Ganzhou city. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups, and what they hope for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking north and center** (Nanchang, Yichun, Ji'an/Luling, Fuzhou/Linchuan, Yingtan): the lineage heartland; compare lineage-centered and town-centered life.
- **Hakka of Gannan and the northwest highlands** (Ganzhou counties; Tonggu, Xiushui, Wanzai *check*): walled-compound settlement and a separate migration story.
- **Jingdezhen and the Fuliang area:** a manufacturing town whose groups formed by trade and origin, not village.
- **Wuyuan and the Huizhou-speaking northeast:** belonged to Huizhou until the 20th century *check*; a different regional "we".
- **Shangrao's Wu- and Min-speaking pockets:** border communities with ties eastward.
- **Poyang Lake fishing and boat communities:** water-based settlement, apart from the farm village.
- **She 畲 communities** (Guixi, Yanshan *check*): an officially recognized minority with its own group rites.
- **Jiujiang and the Yangtze riverfront:** Mandarin-speaking port culture shaped by trade.
- **Out-migrants and returnees** (Jiangxi workers in Guangdong and Zhejiang): what "home community" means at a distance; comparison only. The federation-wide picture is a separate frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage, migration, revolution and religion; mixed for present-day associations; thin for border-dialect and She communities. The exact lands are still to be settled: the tracker header's single province is the working list until the author confirms Gan's territory in Stage 0.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese for sources; Gan and Hakka for field terms; Huizhou and Wu for border counties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary history is officially curated; lineage revival and religion can be sensitive.
