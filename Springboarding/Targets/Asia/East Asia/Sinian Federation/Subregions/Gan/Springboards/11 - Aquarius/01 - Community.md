# Gan: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese (modern and classical); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话; Huizhou 徽语 and Wu 吴语 in the northeast; She 畲 community speech (largely Hakka-like *check*). **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage villages of the Gan heartland** (Ji'an, Fuzhou, Yichun): community and kin overlap; check where they part.
- **Gannan Hakka:** temple-fair and compound-based community with contributions by household.
- **Jingdezhen:** a manufacturing town where trade and workshop, not birth, set membership.
- **Poyang Lake fishers and boat households:** a community on water, with rights to grounds rather than land.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang city neighborhoods:** the state's 社区 against older street and work-unit ties.
- **Wuyuan and the Huizhou-speaking northeast:** halls and village compacts with a different regional history.
- **She townships:** a minority community amid Han villages.
- **Hollowed villages and returnees:** what community means when most adults work in Guangdong or Zhejiang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage villages and rituals; mixed for present-day neighborhoods; thin for She and the Wu- and Min-speaking border pockets.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for local terms and oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lineage revival and temple associations can be politically touchy; "grid" governance touches surveillance; flood records may be officially framed.
- **Held for review:** none.
