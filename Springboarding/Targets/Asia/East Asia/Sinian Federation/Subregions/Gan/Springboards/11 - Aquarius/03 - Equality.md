# Gan: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese (classical for the imperial record); Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (Gannan); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan documents); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage villages of the Gan heartland:** status between strong and weak branches and surnames; check how equality among members coexisted with rank by generation.
- **Gannan Hakka and former Soviet counties:** redistribution was most intense here; compare memory with practice.
- **Wuyuan and the Huizhou-speaking northeast:** a history of hereditary servant status distinct from the rest.
- **Jingdezhen:** pay and status between master potters and laborers.
- **Women across subregions:** Gan, Hakka and Huizhou areas differ in women's field work and in inheritance; compare.
- **She communities:** minority standing set against Han neighbors.
- **Urban Nanchang against rural counties:** the urban-rural gap and household registration.
- **Migrant workers from Jiangxi:** treated as outsiders in coastal cities; comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land revolution and imperial exams; mixed for gender and status orders; thin for She and border-dialect communities. Legal equality (laws against discrimination, the letter of land law) belongs to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Huizhou-area document conventions for Wuyuan deeds.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class labels, land reform violence and poverty statistics are politically framed; some archival files are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
