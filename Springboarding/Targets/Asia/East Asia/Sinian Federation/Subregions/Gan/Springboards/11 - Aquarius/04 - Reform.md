# Gan: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese, with classical Chinese for Song and Ming statecraft; Gan 赣语 and Hakka 客家话 for oral and village sources; Huizhou 徽语 for Wuyuan. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou/Linchuan:** home of Wang Anshi and Tang Xianzu; check how local memory treats a famous reformer.
- **Gannan:** target of Wang Yangming's compacts and later of Soviet reforms; reform repeatedly imposed from outside.
- **Nanchang:** provincial capital where Nationalist and later campaigns began; urban reform against rural reception.
- **Hill and forest villages** (Luoxiao and Wuyi foothills): forest and land reforms.
- **Wuyuan:** heritage tourism has changed village life; compare villagers' and planners' views.
- **Christian communities** (Lichuan and other counties): reform by missions and churches.
- **She communities:** customs targeted by campaigns against "backward" practice; their own account first.
- **Women:** bride-price and marriage-custom reforms touch them directly; check by subregion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Song and Ming statecraft and for 20th-century campaigns; mixed for present-day reforms of custom; thin for She and border pockets. The legal texts of any reform belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); here only the idea and practice of reform.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Wang Anshi and Wang Yangming; Mandarin; Gan and Hakka for villagers' views.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2018 funeral reform and its coffin seizures drew national criticism; campaign histories are officially framed; Cultural Revolution-era "reforms" may be closed in archives.
- **Held for review:** none.
