# Gan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese; classical Chinese for Song and Ming academies and loyalist writing; Gan 赣语 and Hakka 客家话 for oral sources and songs; Shanghainese (Wu) for the memoirs of Shanghai volunteers and sent-down youth who came to Jiangxi. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an/Luling:** loyalist and academy traditions centered on moral ideals; check how strongly local identity draws on them.
- **Fuzhou/Linchuan and Jinxi:** home of Lu Jiuyuan and Tang Xianzu; ideals of mind and of feeling.
- **Lushan and Jiujiang:** academy, monastery and summer-resort town; ideals of learning, religion and leisure overlap.
- **Former Soviet counties** (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu): revolutionary ideals as everyday heritage; compare older and younger residents.
- **Gongqingcheng and other volunteer-founded settlements:** an idealism imported from Shanghai.
- **Gannan Hakka villages:** ideals in lineage teaching against those of the revolution.
- **Christian and Buddhist communities:** religious ideals of service and renunciation.
- **Young urban professionals in Nanchang:** online irony about 情怀 and 初心.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and revolutionary ideals; mixed for present-day personal idealism; thin for She, Wu-speaking and Huizhou-speaking communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Gan and Hakka for songs and interviews; Wu for Shanghai-origin memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary ideals are an official doctrine; critical memoirs of the sent-down years may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
