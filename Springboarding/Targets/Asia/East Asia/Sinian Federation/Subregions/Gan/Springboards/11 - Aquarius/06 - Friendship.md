**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese (classical for Song and Ming letters and essays); Gan 赣语 (Nanchang, Yichun, Ji'an, Fuzhou speech); Hakka 客家话 (Gannan songs and address terms); Huizhou 徽语 (Wuyuan); She 畲 speech. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan heartland villages:** where lineage is strong; check how much room friends have beside kin.
- **Gannan Hakka:** song traditions and fictive-kin customs that may shape friendship differently.
- **Scholars and officials of Ji'an and Fuzhou (historic):** literary friendship and its political risk.
- **Jingdezhen artisans and the "Jingdezhen drifters" 景漂:** friendships formed by craft and by arrival from elsewhere.
- **Pingxiang and other mining towns:** workmates' bonds.
- **Women's friendships:** sworn sisterhood and same-age friends across subregions, little studied.
- **Students and young migrants:** classmates' groups and factory friends far from home; the diaspora for comparison only.
- **She communities:** song exchange and visiting customs with neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literati friendship; mixed for village favor-exchange; thin for women's friendships and for She and border-dialect communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Gan and Hakka address forms and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sworn brotherhoods overlap with secret-society history; 关系 and cronyism touch corruption.
- **Held for review:** none.
