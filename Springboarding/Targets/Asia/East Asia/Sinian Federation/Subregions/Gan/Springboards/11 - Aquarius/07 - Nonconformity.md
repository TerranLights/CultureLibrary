# Gan: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣 (Jiangxi 江西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and written Chinese (classical for gazetteers, sect cases and painting inscriptions); Gan 赣语 (insults and nicknames for the odd one out); Hakka 客家话 (Gannan sect and village sources); Huizhou 徽语 and Wu 吴语 in the northeast. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanchang:** provincial capital with student and online subcultures; compare with county towns.
- **Jingdezhen:** a city partly made of incomers who chose a different life.
- **Gannan Hakka:** sect history and strong lineage norms; check how dissent was handled.
- **Ji'an and Fuzhou literati tradition:** historic tolerance of the "wild scholar" type, to be tested.
- **Lushan and religious sites:** monks, nuns and recluses who left ordinary life.
- **Unmarried men and women in rural counties:** nonconformity imposed by circumstance rather than chosen.
- **Christian and sectarian communities:** belief outside the officially approved forms.
- **She communities:** customs that differ from Han neighbors, and whether they are treated as difference or deviance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historic eccentrics and sect cases; mixed for youth culture; thin for LGBTQ life and present-day religious dissent.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Gan and Hakka for nicknames, insults and oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religion, "heterodox" labels, LGBTQ organizing and political dissent are sensitive; protect informants. Cultural Revolution rebel factions in Jiangxi are a further, restricted topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
