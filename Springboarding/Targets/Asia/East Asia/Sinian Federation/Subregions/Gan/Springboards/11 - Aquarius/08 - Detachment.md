## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lushan and Jiujiang:** monasteries, a retreat town and Tao Yuanming's homeland; a long local tradition of withdrawal to test.
- **Chan monastery counties** (Jing'an, Yifeng, Fengxin): religious detachment as a local institution.
- **Gan heartland villages:** lineage life with little room for solitude; check funeral and wedding display rules.
- **Gannan Hakka:** lament and song traditions set against reserve.
- **Wuyuan:** urban incomers seeking retreat beside long-settled villagers.
- **Nanchang's young professionals:** online "lying flat" and "Buddha-style" talk.
- **Villages emptied by migration:** detachment imposed on elders and children.
- **She communities:** mourning and singing customs that may differ from Han neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious and literary withdrawal; mixed for everyday display rules; thin for She, border-dialect and urban emotional norms. Impartiality as fairness belongs to Libra.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese and Buddhist and Daoist terms; Mandarin; Gan and Hakka for laments and interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "lying flat" has been officially criticized; elderly isolation and suicide are sensitive topics; monastic life is regulated.
- **Held for review:** none.
