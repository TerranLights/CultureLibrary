# Gan: Domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands per the tracker header: Jiangxi 江西, a working list the author still confirms), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script; traditional and classical Chinese for older sources); Gan 赣语 and its local varieties; Hakka 客家话 of the south; the Wu, Huizhou and Mandarin varieties of the border areas. **Concept in one line:** the hidden and transcendence: how a culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with magic ways (luck, harm, hidden forces) as its folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking heartland** (Nanchang, Yichun, Ji'an/Luling, Fuzhou/Linchuan, Yingtan, Poyang Lake and Gan River counties): the base case, itself varied by prefecture.
- **Hakka of Gannan** and the western hills (Ganzhou counties, Tonggu, Xiushui uplands): own burial, soul-calling and geomancy customs.
- **She 畲 communities** (She ethnic townships, e.g. in Guixi and Qianshan, *check*): ancestor rites and initiation lore as they share them.
- **Wuyuan and the Huizhou-speaking northeast:** a Huizhou religious world inside Jiangxi.
- **Wu-speaking Shangrao area** and Mandarin-speaking Jiujiang and Ganzhou city: border cultures that may diverge.
- **Jingdezhen:** a craft city with its own kiln gods and migrant workforce.
- **Christians, Buddhists, Daoists and the non-religious:** separate them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Daoism, Chan Buddhism and nuo; mixed for present-day belief (thin provincial samples); thin for county-level distress.
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists Jiangxi; the member state's exact lands are still to be settled, and this sheet is written for Jiangxi.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for interviews and dialect terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" is a loaded official label; unregistered practitioners may be guarded; psychiatric records are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
