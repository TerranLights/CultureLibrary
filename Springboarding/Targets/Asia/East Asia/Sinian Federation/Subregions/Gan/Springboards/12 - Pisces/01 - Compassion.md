# Gan: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 in the south; classical Chinese for older charity records. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages** of Ji'an and Fuzhou: help organized through kin estates.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** mutual aid among migrants' descendants in hill counties.
- **She 畲 communities:** customs of helping as they describe them.
- **Poyang Lake fishing households:** both givers and receivers of relief after the fishing ban.
- **Nanchang and Jiujiang city residents** against the labor-export counties whose elders and children stay behind.
- **Buddhist, Daoist, Catholic and Protestant circles:** separate charity traditions.
- **Jingdezhen's migrant artisans:** help outside kin networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical charity and religious teaching; mixed for present-day giving (national surveys, thin provincial samples).
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan for street usage of pity words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** officially registered charity and religious charity are treated differently; mission history is politically framed and must be read against Chinese sources.
- **Held for review:** none.
