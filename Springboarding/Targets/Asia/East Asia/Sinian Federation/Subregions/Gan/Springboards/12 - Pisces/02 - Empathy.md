**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Gan 赣语 (Nanchang and county varieties); Hakka 客家话; classical Chinese for poetry and philosophy. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking heartland** counties: the base case, varied by prefecture.
- **Hakka and native groups of the western and southern hills** (Jinggangshan, Gannan, Tonggu): an old line across which empathy was tested.
- **She 畲 communities** with Han neighbors: intermarriage and mutual understanding as they describe it.
- **Families split by migrant labor** (parents in coastal cities, children and elders at home) against settled urban families.
- **Northeastern Wuyuan and Wu-speaking Shangrao:** different speech and kin customs.
- **Medical and social-work professionals** against the families they serve.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: a large psychology literature on children, thin data on adults and on minorities; rich classical texts.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Gan and Hakka for laments and forum idiom.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hakka-native conflict and the sent-down years are politically framed; studies of left-behind children can label families; television mediation turns private pain into entertainment.
- **Held for review:** none.
