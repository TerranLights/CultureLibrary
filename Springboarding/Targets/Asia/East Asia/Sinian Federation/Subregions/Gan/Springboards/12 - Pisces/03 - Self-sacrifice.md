# Gan: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for loyalist writing and gazetteer biographies; Gan 赣语 and Hakka 客家话 for family speech and songs. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ji'an (Luling) and Fuzhou literati lineages:** where loyalist and filial models were written down.
- **Gannan Hakka counties** (Xingguo, Ruijin, Yudu): the revolutionary-martyr landscape, and Hakka women's burdens as they tell them.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou culture):** chastity arches and merchant families' long separations.
- **Jingdezhen potters and kiln workers:** a craft community's sacrifice stories.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own accounts of giving for kin and village.
- **Labor-export households** across the province: parents away, grandparents raising children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loyalist, gazetteer and revolutionary sources; mixed for present-day family sacrifice (survey data, thin by county).
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for oral family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr narratives are state-curated; chastity and flesh-cutting records reflect coercion of women and must be read with that in mind; organ-donation history in China is a contested topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
