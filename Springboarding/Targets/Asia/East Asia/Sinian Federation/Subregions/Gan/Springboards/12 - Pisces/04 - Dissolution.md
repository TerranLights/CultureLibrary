# Gan: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for Chan records; Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话, the She communities' speech and Huizhou speech of Wuyuan (for language-shift questions). **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking heartland:** Chan temples' home ground; dialect shift in cities.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** secondary burial and their own sense of a kept identity.
- **She 畲 communities:** language and identity loss, in their own words.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou-heritage people:** a culture placed inside Jiangxi.
- **Poyang Lake towns:** drowned-town legends, declining ports.
- **Labor-export villages** against Nanchang and Jiujiang.
- **Buddhist and Daoist practitioners** against secular residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chan texts and funerary policy; mixed for dissolution of communities; thin for trance and ecstatic practice today.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and She speech for shift studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burial reform and village mergers are politically charged; spirit possession is officially discouraged; minority identity loss is a delicate subject to raise.
- **Held for review:** none.
