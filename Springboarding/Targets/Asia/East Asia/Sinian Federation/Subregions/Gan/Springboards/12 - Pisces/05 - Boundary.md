# Gan: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for genealogies and lake documents; Gan 赣语, Hakka 客家话 and Huizhou speech for village usage. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages** of the plain: kin lines and village edges.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** walled houses and a long native-newcomer line.
- **Wuyuan (Huizhou heritage):** a county once inside another province.
- **She 畲 communities:** ethnic boundaries as they draw them.
- **Poyang Lake fishing villages:** water boundaries.
- **Urban compounds** of Nanchang and Ganzhou against villages: privacy and neighbor lines.
- **Generations:** parents and grown children on privacy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical boundaries (genealogies, lake documents, gazetteers); mixed for personal boundaries today, mainly online and national.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and Huizhou speech for village terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** provincial and ethnic boundaries are political topics; native-Hakka history can reopen old grievances; postpartum and purity customs touch women's health and should be studied with consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
