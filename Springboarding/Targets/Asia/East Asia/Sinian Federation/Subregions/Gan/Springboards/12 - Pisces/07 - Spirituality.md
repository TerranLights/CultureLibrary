*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking plains:** lineage halls, Buddhist and Daoist temples, river gods.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** earth gods, ancestral halls, Hakka-specific deities.
- **She 畲 communities:** ancestor lore and initiation, as they share it.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou heritage:** Neo-Confucian lineage rites against folk temples.
- **Christian communities** (northern cities, rural congregations): registered and unregistered.
- **Monastics and lay followers** on Lushan and Yunjushan against occasional visitors.
- **Young urban "Buddha-style" visitors** against elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhist and Daoist history; mixed for present practice (official counts miss home and village practice); thin for She rites.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Japanese for Akizuki; Gan, Hakka and She speech for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious activity is politically sensitive; She initiation knowledge may be restricted to initiates and should be studied only as shared; Christian house churches face pressure.
- **Held for review:** none.
