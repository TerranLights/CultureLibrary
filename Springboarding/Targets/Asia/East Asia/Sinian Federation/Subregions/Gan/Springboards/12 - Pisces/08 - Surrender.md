# Gan: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for monastic codes, Ming memorials and gazetteers; Gan 赣语 and Hakka 客家话 for sayings of resignation. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking heartland:** lake villages that gave up land; city families facing hospice choices.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** memory of holdouts and of surrender in the base-area years.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own memory of yielding and resisting in the hill wars.
- **Monastics** under a rule against lay followers who "let go" in daily life.
- **Wuyuan and Wu-speaking Shangrao:** different county histories of conquest.
- **Older generations** shaped by campaigns against younger ones who talk of "lying flat."

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and monastic history; mixed for attitudes to fate (national surveys); thin for end-of-life choices by county.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Lushan Conference, base-area defections and Ming-Qing "turncoats" are politically sensitive; resettlement may be a grievance for those moved.
- **Held for review:** none.
