*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for Neo-Confucian, Chan and geomancy texts; Gan 赣语 and Hakka 客家话 for omen sayings and craft talk. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou (Linchuan) and Ji'an literati heartland:** home of mind-learning; Gan-speaking.
- **Gannan Hakka counties:** geomancy by eye; hill farmers' signs.
- **Jingdezhen's craft workers:** trained intuition passed by apprenticeship.
- **Poyang Lake fishing villages:** weather and water knowledge.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own knowledge of hills and herbs, as shared.
- **Northeastern Wuyuan and Shangrao:** Huizhou and Wu heritage counties near the Goose Lake site.
- **Urban professionals** in Nanchang against rural elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for philosophy and Chan; mixed for craft and medical intuition (oral, needs fieldwork); thin for survey measures by province beyond the rice-theory work.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka for craft and omen talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fate-calculation and divination are discouraged and sometimes policed; practitioners may refuse to talk on record.
- **Held for review:** none.
