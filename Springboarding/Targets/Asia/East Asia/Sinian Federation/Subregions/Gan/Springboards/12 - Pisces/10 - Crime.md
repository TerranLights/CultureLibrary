## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking lineage villages:** kin sanctions and feud memories.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** once labeled bandits by officials; their own accounts first.
- **She 畲 communities:** the 畲贼 label and how She writers answer it.
- **Cities** (Nanchang, Jiujiang) against rural counties: different crimes in the news.
- **Wuyuan and Shangrao border counties:** smuggling and cross-province crime lore.
- **Released prisoners and their families:** stigma in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for moral ledgers and official writing on bandits; mixed for present-day views (press, not surveys); thin for victims' and offenders' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Gan and Hakka for insults and gossip.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic "bandit" labels need care; corruption and fraud reporting is politically managed; statistics on crime by county are restricted; any law question goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
