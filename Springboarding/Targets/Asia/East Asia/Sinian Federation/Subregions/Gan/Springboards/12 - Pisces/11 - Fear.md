# Gan: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Gan 赣, member state of the Sinian Federation (Jiangxi 江西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical and vernacular Chinese for Ming stories and gazetteers; Gan 赣语; Hakka 客家话 (where 惊 is the everyday verb "to fear" *check*); Huizhou speech of Wuyuan. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gan-speaking plains and lake counties:** floods, the river dragon, boatmen's taboos.
- **Hakka of Gannan:** fright illness, soul-calling, hill dangers.
- **Wuyuan's Huizhou heritage:** the Wutong cult's home ground.
- **Western nuo counties** (Pingxiang, Wanzai): fear handled by masked ritual.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own protective lore, as they share it.
- **Shangrao-area counties:** wartime germ-warfare memory.
- **Children and elders:** who is frightened, who soothes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for floods, epidemics and demonic cults in history; mixed for fright illness (scattered studies); thin for present anxiety data by province.
- **Languages needed:** classical, vernacular and Standard Chinese; Gan, Hakka and Huizhou speech for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** germ-warfare testimony and campaign histories are politically framed; healers who treat fright may be called "superstitious" and avoid publicity.
- **Held for review:** none.
