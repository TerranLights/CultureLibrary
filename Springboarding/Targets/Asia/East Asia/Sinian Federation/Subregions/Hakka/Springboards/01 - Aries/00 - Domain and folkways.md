# Hakka: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话; Meixian reading as reference, plus Tingzhou, Gannan, Huiyang and Sichuan varieties), Standard Chinese (Mandarin), Cantonese for the Hong Kong and Pearl River material, English. **Concept in one line:** the domain question: what is a self here, and how does it assert itself (the self, the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative, and the naming ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and its neighbors** (Xingning, Wuhua, Pingyuan, Jiaoling): the reference dialect; does its self-image speak for the rest?
- **Dabu, Fengshun and the semi-Hakka 半山客 of Jiexi:** living against Teochew neighbors; mixed naming and dress.
- **Huiyang, Longgang (Shenzhen) and Hong Kong's New Territories Hakka:** urbanized, beside Cantonese Punti villages.
- **Western Fujian** (Changting, Ninghua, Yongding, Liancheng, Shanghang): tulou lineages and strongly divergent dialects.
- **Southern Jiangxi** (Ganzhou, Ningdu, Ruijin, Yudu, Xingguo): "old" versus "new" Hakka settlers, with Gan speakers nearby.
- **Northern Guangdong** (Shaoguan, Heyuan): mountain Hakka on the Cantonese frontier.
- **Guangxi** (Bobai, Luchuan, Hezhou), **Sichuan** (Dongshan near Chengdu, "土廣東話" speakers), **Hunan** (Liuyang area), **Hainan:** outlying settlements where Hakka-ness may persist mainly in speech or genealogy.
- **She people 畲族:** intertwined with Hakka origins and speech; separate their own account.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew, Gan; each defines Hakka difference from its side.
- **Christian Hakka** (Basel Mission and Catholic villages): baptismal names beside lineage names.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka and the overseas diaspora, for how Hakka self-naming and identity work where the heartland is far away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in identity-history scholarship and genealogies; thin in psychology, since surveys carry no Hakka category.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (several varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese, German for Basel records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin debates (northern descent against southern fusion with She and other peoples) carry pride and political weight; self-description discourse (硬頸, "hard-necked") easily turns into a stereotype, so treat it as a source to examine, not a description. The set of communities in this sub-unit is a working list, still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** none.
