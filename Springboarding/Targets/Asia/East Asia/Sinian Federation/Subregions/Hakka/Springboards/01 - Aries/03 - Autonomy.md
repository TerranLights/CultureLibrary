## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women of emigrant households in Meizhou and Dabu:** long-absent husbands left them to run farms and homes.
- **Tulou residents of Yongding and Nanjing counties:** life in a single shared building leaves little private choice; test this.
- **Gannan upland settlers:** history of living beyond close supervision.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** village self-rule bodies inside a city.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** self-run migrant institutions away from the heartland.
- **Catholic and Protestant Hakka villages:** faith choices that set them apart from lineage ritual.
- **Younger urban Hakka** in Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Xiamen: choices made far from elders.
- **She neighbors:** their own household and marriage rules; do not merge.
- **Cantonese and Teochew neighbors:** different marriage and residence customs.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka villages; overseas sojourner households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on lineage and marriage history, thin on present personal choice.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** childhood betrothal and forced marriage are painful family histories; revolutionary-base material is politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
