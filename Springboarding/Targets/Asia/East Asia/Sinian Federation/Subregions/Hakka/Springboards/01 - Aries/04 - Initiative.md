## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Dabu emigrant districts:** long traditions of leaving to trade; test against stay-at-home counties.
- **Wuhua and Xingning:** builders and labor migrants; craft-based ventures.
- **Western Fujian (Yongding, Shanghang):** tobacco and printing trades historically; lineage-backed ventures.
- **Gannan:** upland farmers and later revolutionary mobilization; initiative from poverty.
- **Hong Kong Hakka:** quarrying, building and small industry in a colonial port.
- **Shenzhen's Longgang and Bao'an Hakka:** villagers turned landlords and founders in a boom city.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** settlers who once started over; whether that is remembered.
- **Women:** market trade and farming while men were away; women's own ventures are often unrecorded.
- **Teochew and Cantonese neighbors:** known for their own merchant networks; compare, do not rank.
- **Comparison only:** Southeast Asian Hakka merchants; Taiwan Hakka small business.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on emigration and donors, thin on everyday unasked help and on women's ventures.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, Cantonese, classical Chinese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** success stories are curated by associations; donor lists honor some and silence others. Avoid "industrious Hakka" clichés; test them.
- **Held for review:** none.
