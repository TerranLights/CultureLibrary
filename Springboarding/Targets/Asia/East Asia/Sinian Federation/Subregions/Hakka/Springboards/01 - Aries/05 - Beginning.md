*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ninghua and Changting:** keepers of the "first arrival" legend; how they tell it differently from Meizhou.
- **Meizhou weilongwu villages:** founding rites built into house form.
- **Heyuan reservoir resettlers:** starting over under state orders.
- **Returnee farm communities:** emigrants who came back to begin anew.
- **Guangxi, Hainan and Sichuan Hakka:** settlements that began as relocations; their founding memories.
- **Hong Kong relocated villages:** old lineages restarted in new towns.
- **Christian Hakka:** baptism as a second beginning beside birth rites.
- **She people:** their own origin story and birth customs.
- **Cantonese neighbors:** the Zhuji Lane 珠璣巷 migration story as a rival origin to compare.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka arrival stories; overseas arrival narratives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on origins and migration, mixed on birth customs, thin on personal second chances.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese, German, English; She terms if available.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reservoir and land relocations are painful and political; origin legends carry pride and dispute.
- **Held for review:** none.
