## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huiyang and Longgang Hakka:** martial lineages and wartime guerrilla villages; physical courage on display.
- **Gannan (Yudu, Ruijin, Xingguo):** revolutionary-era losses; official and family memories may differ.
- **Meizhou counties:** scholar-official exemplars; moral courage over physical.
- **Longnan and western Fujian fortified communities:** collective defense rather than lone heroes.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** colonial and wartime layers; village memorials.
- **Women:** endurance in labor and widowhood, praised in gazetteers; separate praise from lived account.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** frontier settlers; local tales of defending new settlements.
- **She communities:** their own hero tales; do not fold into Hakka lore.
- **Cantonese neighbors:** the same feuds and wars told with opposite heroes.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka resistance memory (1895); overseas Hakka war heroes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and revolutionary heroism, mixed for martial arts, thin for everyday moral courage.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Hakka, Cantonese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary heroism is state-curated; feud and wartime memories remain divisive; gazetteer "exemplary women" honored suffering; martial-arts lineage claims are often disputed. "Warlike Hakka" is a stereotype to test.
- **Held for review:** none.
