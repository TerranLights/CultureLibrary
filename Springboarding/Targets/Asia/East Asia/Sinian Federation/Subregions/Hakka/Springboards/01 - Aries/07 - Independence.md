## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Remittance-dependent districts of Meixian and Dabu** against counties with little emigration: two economies of self-support.
- **Western Fujian craft villages** (Sibao, Liancheng paper makers): independence through trade networks.
- **Tulou communities:** self-supply as a building, but interdependence within it.
- **Gannan hill farmers:** subsistence and poverty-alleviation programs; aid and its stigma.
- **Women and widows:** often the household's sole support; whether that counts as independence in local talk.
- **Left-behind elders and children:** dependence in hollowed villages.
- **Hong Kong Hakka villagers:** from farming to wage and rent incomes.
- **Sichuan, Guangxi and Hainan Hakka:** small minorities; mutual help among their own or with neighbors.
- **She neighbors and Cantonese, Teochew lowlanders:** different land and trade bases.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka rural households; overseas Hakka remitters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on remittances and lineage welfare, thin on emotional independence and present attitudes to help.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, Hakka, classical Chinese, Cantonese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty-alleviation data are politically framed; "frugal, self-reliant Hakka" is a stock self-portrait to test against records.
- **Held for review:** none.
