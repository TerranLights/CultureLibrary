# Hakka: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese, French (missionary dictionary), English. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Fujian lineages:** histories of inter-lineage feuds; how they ended.
- **Hakka-Teochew border villages** (Fengshun, Jiexi): grievances along a language line.
- **Pearl River delta Hakka** (Chixi area): bitterness left from the feuds of the 1850s-60s.
- **Gannan:** land-reform grievance meetings; old and new settler tensions.
- **Meizhou town and village families:** urban and rural display of temper.
- **Women:** scolding and cursing as women's speech in folk portrayals; separate portrayal from practice.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** anger aimed at authorities over land.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** a minority's anger kept quiet or spoken in the home tongue.
- **She neighbors; Cantonese and Hokkien speakers:** each has its own anger words and taboos.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka; overseas Hakka families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for feuds and political grievance, thin for private emotion and everyday display.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties (anger words differ by county), Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform and feud memories; protest coverage; "hot-tempered" ethnic labels are stereotypes to test, not describe.
- **Held for review:** none.
