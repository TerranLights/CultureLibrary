## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Xingning:** reputations for learning; test whether the label matches results.
- **Wuhua:** a football reputation; sport as a route to rank.
- **Changting and Ninghua lineages:** exam and hall rivalry in western Fujian.
- **Gannan:** poorer hill counties; competition for limited school places.
- **Hong Kong Hakka:** rivalry with Punti lineages in festivals and land.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** contest for standing among other migrant guilds.
- **Women:** shut out of the exams in the past; their own contests (song, craft, schooling now).
- **Returnees and stay-at-homes:** envy and display in emigrant villages.
- **Cantonese and Teochew neighbors:** commercial and sporting rivalries with Hakka towns.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka sport and exam culture; overseas Hakka clan contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for examinations and football, mixed for lineage rivalry, thin for envy and everyday contest.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese (for examination and lineage records), Hakka, Cantonese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "scholarly Hakka" self-labels are promotional; check against data. Exam pressure touches student mental health.
- **Held for review:** none.
