## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ganzhou mining counties:** risk taken in dangerous digging; work against rashness.
- **Fengshun and Hong Kong's Tai Hang:** fire festivals where daring is sanctioned.
- **Emigrant districts of Meixian and Dabu:** going abroad on borrowed money as gamble or duty.
- **Hill counties of western Fujian and Gannan:** banditry and feuding past; check local memory.
- **Young men and young women:** risk roles differ; do not assume only young men.
- **Christian and Buddhist Hakka:** temperance teaching and vows against gambling and drink.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** festivals and drinking customs set beside their neighbors'.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** urban youth risk against village elders' warnings.
- **She, Cantonese and Teochew neighbors:** different festival risks and drinking customs.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka festivals; overseas gamblers and speculators.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for festivals and folk warnings, thin for private risk behavior; no survey records Hakka identity.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (for oral accounts of mine and festival risk), Standard Chinese, Cantonese, classical Chinese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mining accidents and illegal mining are politically touchy; addiction carries family shame. Do not let one district's notoriety stand for all Hakka.
- **Held for review:** none.
