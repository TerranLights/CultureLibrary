# Hakka: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) in its Meixian, Tingzhou, Gannan and Hailu varieties; Standard Chinese; Cantonese for the New Territories. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tulou and weilongwu households** against single-family flats in Meizhou or Ganzhou city: shared storage against private storage.
- **Remittance villages** (Meixian, Dabu, Yongding) against non-emigrant villages: imported goods and the things sent home.
- **Hakka Christians** against temple-going households: tablets, altars and their objects.
- **She 畲 communities:** ancestral scrolls and staffs held as lineage objects *check*.
- **New Territories walled villages:** lineage halls and colonial-era records kept beside the objects.
- **Sichuan (Luodai) and Guangxi settlements:** what migrants carried from Guangdong two or three centuries ago.
- **Women and men:** dowry and a wife's own goods against the husband's household.
- Taiwan's Hakka: comparison only, for material on household property in Meinong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; wedding and reburial customs are well documented, everyday belongings and lending much less.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (more than one variety) for particles and object words; Standard Chinese; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reburial and graves; goods seized in land reform and genealogies and tablets destroyed in the 1960s; theft of tulou fittings for sale *check*. Claims on property go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no belongings pointer was set aside.
