# Hakka: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hakka (客家) communities across the Sinian Federation, a sub-unit with no lands of its own; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese for tenure records and scholarship; Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) for spoken claims and sayings; Cantonese and English for the New Territories. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Fujian and Gannan:** split field rights and forest tenure, where hill land matters more than paddy.
- **Meizhou remittance villages:** houses built with money from abroad, taken and returned.
- **New Territories villages:** colonial land register and indigenous-villager status shape every claim, unlike the mainland.
- **Tulou and weilongwu branches:** shares in one building against separate houses.
- **Sichuan, Guangxi and Hunan settlers:** land cleared or bought on arrival, against inherited heartland fields.
- **Hakka in Hokkien or Teochew counties:** smaller holdings beside larger lineages *check*.
- **She 畲 villages:** hill and forest rights beside Hakka neighbors.
- **Women:** whether daughters held any share of lineage land.
- Taiwan comparison only: Myron Cohen's work on family estates in Meinong (*House United, House Divided*, 1976 *check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage estates, contracts and the New Territories; mixed for present attitudes; thin for the outlying settlements.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese for deeds; Standard Chinese; Hakka for sayings and interviews; English and Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform, collectivization and the Punti–Hakka wars; New Territories village land claims. The letter of property and land law goes to Libra; its spirit to Capricorn. The government-spending figure for the left-right axis is a federation-level number, read beside Hakka sources, not of them.
- **Held for review:** none among the tenure pointers.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 《尋烏調查》 and list every kind of owner it names (landlord, lineage trust, temple, shop).
2. CNKI: 一田二主 汀州 and 田皮 田骨 贛南; note the period and county of each study.
3. CNKI: 客家 蒸嘗 and 客家 族產; separate Meizhou, western Fujian and Gannan.
4. Check the Hong Kong Public Records Office catalog for Block Crown Lease schedules of Hakka villages in Sai Kung and Tsuen Wan.
5. Search 武平 林改 for field studies of villagers' views.
