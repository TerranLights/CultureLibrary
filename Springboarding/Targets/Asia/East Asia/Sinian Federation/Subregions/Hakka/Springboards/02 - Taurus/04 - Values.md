# Hakka: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hakka (客家) people wherever they live in the Sinian Federation; a sub-unit defined by people, not territory; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka (客家話 / 客家话: Meixian, Wuhua, Tingzhou, Ningdu and other varieties); classical Chinese for lineage texts; Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Chinese | 耕讀傳家; 晴耕雨讀 | farming and study passed down; farm in fair weather, read in rain | common on hall plaques |
| Chinese | 楹聯 / 楹联 | paired couplets on hall pillars and doors | a compact record of stated ideals |
| Hakka | 做人 tsò-ngìn | conducting oneself, being a proper person | Hakka 人 ngìn *check* |
| Hakka | 抵得 tí-tet | worth it | Hakka usage *check* |
| Chinese | 客家 諺語 / 谚语 | Hakka proverbs | collections by county |
| Chinese | 移風易俗 / 移风易俗 | "change habits, reform customs" | official campaigns on weddings and funerals |
| Chinese | 風水林 / 风水林 | geomantic grove behind a village | Hong Kong: fung shui wood |
| Hakka | 月光光 | "Bright moonlight", a children's rhyme | many local versions |

English keywords: Hakka family instructions; Hakka proverbs; ancestral hall couplets; fung shui woods Hong Kong; Hakka Christians values; Basel Mission Hakka.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Christian Hakka** (Basel-founded churches in Meizhou, Wuhua and the New Territories; Catholic villages) against households built around the ancestral hall.
- **Gannan's former Soviet-base counties** (Ruijin, Xingguo, Yudu) where official revolutionary values are strongest in public space.
- **Meizhou emigrant villages:** overseas money and its effect on what is held worth spending on.
- **New Territories villagers,** where colonial rule left lineage halls and temples less disturbed *check*.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka,** whose values grew beside Sichuanese and Zhuang neighbors.
- **She 畲 communities:** a separate ancestral tradition alongside shared customs.
- **Elders in villages and the young in Shenzhen and Guangzhou factories** *check*: whether they rank the same things.
- Taiwan's Hakka and overseas clan associations: comparison only, for which lineage instructions survived abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in texts (genealogies, proverbs, couplets); thin in measured values specific to Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for genealogies and steles; Standard Chinese; Hakka for rhymes, songs and interviews; German and English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official heritage narratives and revolutionary history; religious practice under state rules; avoid any single "Hakka spirit" list as a finding. Rules of conduct as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; every values pointer stands.

## 5. First queries
1. Find two or three printed Hakka genealogies from different prefectures (梅州, 汀州, 贛州) and copy out the headings of their 家訓.
2. Pull the Hakka-county sections of the Guangdong volume of 《中國諺語集成》 and tag each proverb by theme.
3. CNKI: 客家 祠堂 楹聯; compare inscriptions by prefecture.
4. Search the mission21 archive catalog for Hakka-language catechisms and station reports.
5. Check CGSS documentation for any dialect or home-language variable.
