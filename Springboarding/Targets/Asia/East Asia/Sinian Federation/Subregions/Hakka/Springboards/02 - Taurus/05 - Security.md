# Hakka: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hakka (客家) communities in the Sinian Federation (heartland at the Guangdong-Fujian-Jiangxi junction and outlying settlements), a non-territorial sub-unit; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) in several varieties; Cantonese for Hong Kong villages. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

English keywords: Hakka fortified houses; Gannan weiwu; rotating credit Fujian; Hakka village defense; remittance dependence Meizhou; rural pension Jiangxi.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan border counties** (Longnan, Anyuan, Dingnan) with fortified houses, against **Yongding and Nanjing tulou** villages: two answers to danger.
- **Meizhou emigrant households,** where security came from abroad, against non-emigrant hill villages.
- **New Territories villagers** with Hong Kong pensions and village land, against mainland villagers on rural pensions.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi settlers,** whose families moved to find safety and land.
- **Hakka in Hokkien or Teochew counties,** where a small group may have relied more on alliance *check*.
- **She 畲 villages** in the same hills.
- **Young migrant workers** in Shenzhen or Dongguan and the village land they keep as a fallback *check*.
- Overseas Hakka: comparison only, as the senders on whom heartland households once relied.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on fortified buildings and migration; mixed on savings and insurance; thin on Hakka-specific measures of worry or want.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hakka for songs and interviews; classical Chinese for granary records; Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the famine of 1959-1961; Republican banditry and armed feuds; land reform; disaster losses. Public insurance rules as law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** no security pointer held back.
