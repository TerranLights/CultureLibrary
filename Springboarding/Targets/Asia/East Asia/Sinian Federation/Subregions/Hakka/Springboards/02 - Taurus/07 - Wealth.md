# Hakka: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) and their home communities in the Sinian Federation, a sub-unit drawn by people rather than borders; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka (客家話 / 客家话), Meixian, Dabu, Yongding, Gannan and Hailu forms; Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou and Dabu emigrant villages,** where wealth came from Southeast Asia, against **Gannan and western Fujian hill counties,** where it came from local trades (paper, tobacco, books, timber) *check*.
- **Gannan orchard households** that gained in the last thirty years, against those that left for factory work.
- **New Territories Hakka villagers,** whose wealth is tied to Hong Kong land values; a different source of riches from any mainland group.
- **Hakka in Shenzhen, Huizhou and Dongguan,** where villages turned into city districts *check*.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** merchant guilds and farm wealth in settler country.
- **She 畲 villages** in the same counties, often poorer in official figures *check*.
- **Lineage halls against single households:** wealth held in common, or in private.
- Diaspora tycoons (Aw Boon Haw and others) and Taiwan's Hakka: comparison only, as givers to and models for the home counties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on merchant fortunes, mansions and philanthropy; mixed on present incomes; thin on how ordinary Hakka talk about the rich.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hakka for folktales and interviews; classical Chinese for Qing records; English and Malay sources only for diaspora comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform and class labels (地主, 富農) still matter to families; New Territories village wealth is politically charged; tax and inheritance law go to Libra. The left-right spending measure is read under Ownership.
- **Held for review:** none of the wealth pointers.
