# Hakka: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hakka (客家) households, lineages and villages in the Sinian Federation, treated as a people rather than a territory; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka (客家話 / 客家话), including song language; Cantonese for New Territories villages. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties** (Meixian, Dabu, Xingning), where long absences of husbands shaped jealousy and guarding, against counties with little emigration.
- **Western Guangdong and Guangxi Hakka,** descendants of the feud zone, where turf memories may run deeper *check*.
- **Lineage-strong villages** in western Fujian and Gannan, against mixed-surname towns.
- **New Territories villages,** where lineage claims and outsiders' rights remain sharp (the legal side goes to Libra).
- **Hakka Christians:** church teaching on marriage beside lineage custom.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own marriage customs, often contrasted with Hakka ones *check*.
- **Younger couples in Shenzhen and Guangzhou** against village elders: whether jealousy still reads as love.
- Taiwan's Hakka: comparison only, for studies of mothers-in-law and household control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historic women's roles and feuds; mixed on present-day couples; thin on everyday sharing and turf.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hakka for songs, drama and interviews; classical Chinese for gazetteers and verses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child marriage, domestic violence and feuds are painful family histories; avoid portraying Hakka men or women as one type. Inheritance rights of daughters (including the 1994 New Territories dispute) and family-violence law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing held on jealousy or turf.
