# Hakka: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hakka (客家) kitchens, festivals and homes across the Sinian Federation; a sub-unit defined by its people, not a territory; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) for food, bathing and music words; Standard Chinese; Cantonese for New Territories feasts and Sichuanese for Luodai. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou and the Dongjiang** (salt-baked chicken, preserved greens, braised pork) against **western Fujian** (dried and cured foods) and **Gannan** (heat and smoke) *check*.
- **Hailu and Jiexi Hakka,** where pounded tea is a daily or guest custom, against areas where it is rare *check*.
- **New Territories walled villages:** the basin feast and Hong Kong's restaurant trade.
- **Sichuan (Luodai) and Guangxi Hakka:** tastes changed by long residence among other cuisines.
- **Hakka among Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew neighbors:** which dishes cross over and which mark difference.
- **She 畲 communities:** their own teas, rice wines and songs beside Hakka ones *check*.
- **Christian households and vegetarian vow-keepers,** who set limits on drink or meat.
- Taiwan's Hakka cuisine and overseas Hakka restaurants: comparison only, for which flavors traveled and how they changed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on food, tea and music; mixed on bathing and touch; thin on private comfort, rest and how pleasure itself is judged.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for dish names and sensory words (which vary by county); Standard Chinese; Cantonese and Sichuanese for the two outlying food cultures.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heritage marketing simplifies foods into a single "Hakka cuisine"; avoid treating thrift or hardiness as a fixed taste. Sex itself belongs to Scorpio; health claims of herbal foods to Virgo.
- **Held for review:** no sensory pointer set aside.
