# Hakka: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese (both scripts), Hakka varieties for oral and online material, Cantonese and English for Hong Kong records. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage elders and young migrant workers:** different sources of news (hall notices and kin against phones and work groups).
- **Ruijin, Xingguo and other former base counties in Gannan and western Fujian:** a distinct history of state information work.
- **Meizhou households with overseas kin:** news arrived from abroad by letter and now by messaging; compare with households without such ties.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** villages under British, then Hong Kong, record-keeping; English and Cantonese media.
- **Christian Hakka congregations:** church bulletins and mission networks as a channel separate from lineage and temple.
- **Sichuan, Hunan and Guangxi Hakka:** small communities that depend more on majority-language media.
- **Hainan Hakka:** thin data; check what exists before assuming any channel.
- **She villages:** their own records and their place in Hakka-area gazetteers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for genealogies and gazetteers, thin for present-day Hakka-specific news habits, since national surveys rarely separate Hakka from neighbors.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern written Chinese; Hakka for broadcast and oral material; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** media, rumor and censorship are restricted topics in the federation; 1930s base-area history is politically charged; genealogies contain living people's personal data.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the genealogy catalog for Hakka-county surnames (梅縣, 永定, 寧化, 興國) and note holdings.
2. Find the Ruijin newspaper reprint set and list issues aimed at village readers.
3. CNKI: 鄉村 微信群 謠言, then filter for Meizhou, Ganzhou and Longyan.
4. Check the CGSS codebook for county and dialect variables.
5. Search the Hong Kong archive's online catalog for New Territories Hakka village files.
