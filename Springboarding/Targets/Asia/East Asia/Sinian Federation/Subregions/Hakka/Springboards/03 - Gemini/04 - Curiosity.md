# Hakka: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese (both scripts), Hakka varieties for sayings and riddles, Cantonese for Hong Kong, English for 19th-century outside accounts. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiaoling and Meixian scholars' families:** written records of local inquiry to set beside villages with none.
- **Western Fujian tulou villages:** the communities most exposed to visitor curiosity since heritage listing.
- **Gannan (Xingguo, Ningdu):** the siting-master tradition and a different religious landscape.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** empty and revived villages; hikers and heritage groups as outsiders.
- **Sichuan (Luodai) and Hunan (Liuyang):** Hakka as the object of a majority's curiosity.
- **Guangxi (Bobai, Luchuan) and Hainan:** smaller communities with little outside attention; check whether any study exists.
- **Returned-migrant families:** curiosity shaped by overseas kin.
- **She villages:** their own taboos and tale traditions, not folded into Hakka ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good for origin debates, tales and tourism; thin for everyday questioning norms and children's questions.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for riddles, songs and taboo words; classical Chinese for Qing notes; English for mission writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin and genetics research touches ethnic identity and claims of descent; treat with care. Tourism studies may concern residents' privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
