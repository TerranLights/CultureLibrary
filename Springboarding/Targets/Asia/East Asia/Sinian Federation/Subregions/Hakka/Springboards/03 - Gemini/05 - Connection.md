# Hakka: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese (both scripts), Hakka varieties for kin and ritual terms, Cantonese for Hong Kong associations, English for migration scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou lineages with large overseas branches:** distance ties through gifts and visits.
- **Western Fujian tulou households:** shared-building life, now thinning.
- **Gannan villages:** lineage and temple ties with Gan-speaking neighbors.
- **Western Pearl River Delta (Taishan, Enping, Chixi area):** the aftermath of the Punti-Hakka wars.
- **Hakka migrants in Shenzhen, Dongguan and Guangzhou:** new urban networks.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** associations, village councils and emigrant households.
- **Sichuan (Luodai), Guangxi (Bobai, Hezhou), Hunan (Liuyang), Hainan:** communities whose ties run to majority neighbors as well as to other Hakka.
- **She villages:** ritual and marriage links with Hakka, studied separately.
- **Elders, women and young migrants:** different networks and different risks of isolation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for associations and lineages; mixed for ritual kinship; thin for loneliness and for the smaller communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Hakka for kin and ritual terms; Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Punti-Hakka wars still touch local feelings; networks of favor (關係) can slide into corruption topics; elder isolation is personal.
- **Held for review:** none.
