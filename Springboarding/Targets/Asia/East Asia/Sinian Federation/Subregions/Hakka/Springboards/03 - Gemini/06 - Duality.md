# Hakka: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese (both scripts), Hakka varieties, Cantonese and Teochew for border communities, English. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian heartland:** the most fully Hakka-speaking setting; a baseline for two-ness elsewhere.
- **Eastern Guangdong edge (Jiexi, Fengshun, Raoping, Huilai):** Hakka-Teochew bilingual villages.
- **Western Pearl River Delta and Huizhou:** Cantonese-speaking families of Hakka descent.
- **Gannan:** a Hakka label taken up beside a Gan one.
- **Western Fujian and Zhangzhou border (Zhao'an, Pinghe, Nanjing):** Hakka and Hokkien in one county.
- **Guangxi, Sichuan, Hunan and Hainan:** two or more languages daily.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** Hakka, Cantonese and English; Christian and non-Christian villages.
- **She villages:** a separate people whose relation to Hakka is itself the duality question.
- **Returned migrants:** homeland and abroad within one household.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for identity debates and burial customs; thin for twins and everyday "two-faced" sanctions.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Cantonese, Teochew and Hokkien for border zones; classical Chinese for Qing essays.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whether the Hakka are "pure" Han or mixed with She and others is sensitive for many Hakka and for She communities; avoid taking a side. Secondary burial involves the dead and family graves.
- **Held for review:** none.
