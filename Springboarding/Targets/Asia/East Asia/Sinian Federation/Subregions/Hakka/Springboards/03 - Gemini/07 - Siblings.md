# Hakka: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties (kin terms differ by district), written Chinese including classical documents, Cantonese for Hong Kong, English. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Dabu lineages:** dense genealogies and branch trusts; strong records of brothers' shares.
- **Western Fujian tulou families:** one building shared by many brothers' households.
- **Gannan villages:** larger rural families and later family-planning histories.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** division under colonial land rules; brothers split by emigration.
- **Guangxi (Bobai, Luchuan) and Hainan:** less studied; sibling terms may follow neighbors.
- **Sichuan (Luodai) and Hunan (Liuyang):** settler families; check whether kin terms shifted toward the majority.
- **Daughters and sons:** sisters' places in division, rites and care, studied separately from brothers'.
- **Christian families:** rites of the eldest son changed or kept.
- **She villages:** their own kin terms and division customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for division deeds, genealogies and kin terms; mixed for sisters' roles; thin for sibling feelings and rivalry in Hakka-specific sources.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for deeds; Hakka kin terms by district; Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family planning and adopted daughters-in-law are painful topics; division deeds may involve living families' property; inheritance rules as law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
