# Hakka: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese (classical and modern, both scripts), Hakka varieties, Cantonese for Hong Kong, English for mission records. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian lineages:** strong elder and hall-based settlement; check how far it survives.
- **Gannan:** the compact tradition and lineage feuds with Gan-speaking neighbors.
- **Western Fujian (Changting, Wuping, Yongding):** deity cults and tulou households settling shared-building quarrels.
- **Western Pearl River Delta:** Punti-Hakka war memories; broker roles across the old line.
- **Eastern Guangdong border (Jiexi, Fengshun):** bilingual brokers between Hakka and Teochew.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** village representatives and associations between villages and government.
- **Sichuan (Luodai), Guangxi (Guiping, Bobai), Hunan (Liuyang), Hainan:** migrants' lodges and go-betweens with majority neighbors.
- **Women:** matchmakers and kin-keepers, studied apart from elders.
- **She villages:** their own peacemakers, and the go-betweens between She and Hakka.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage rules, wedding customs and the Punti-Hakka feuds; mixed for present-day settlement; thin for women's peacemaking and for the smaller communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Hakka varieties; Cantonese for Hong Kong; English for mission sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state mediation committees, contracts with middlemen and court procedure are law: Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit). Feud history can still divide neighbors; medium practice may be under-reported.
- **Held for review:** none.
