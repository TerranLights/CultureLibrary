# Hakka: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties (gossip vocabulary is colloquial and local), written Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong, Sichuanese for Chengdu-area sources. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian villages:** dense lineage settlements where reputation is shared property.
- **Western Fujian tulou:** many households under one roof; check whether talk works differently there.
- **Gannan:** stage comedy and mountain songs as public outlets for talk.
- **Eastern Guangdong border:** talk that crosses Hakka and Teochew markets.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** small villages, emigrant families and a Cantonese gossip press.
- **Sichuan (Luodai):** a Hakka town inside a teahouse culture.
- **Guangxi (Hezhou, Bobai), Hunan (Liuyang) and Hainan:** little known; begin with nicknames and songs.
- **Women and men:** who is said to gossip and whose talk gets another name; a claim to test, not adopt.
- **Returned migrants and those who stayed:** opposite ends of village talk.
- **She villages:** separate reputation customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for moral texts and chastity records; thin for everyday gossip, which is oral and rarely recorded.
- **Languages needed:** colloquial Hakka; Cantonese; some Sichuanese for Luodai; classical Chinese for gazetteers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reputations of living people and women's sexual reputations; rumor rebuttal is tied to state control of speech; defamation as law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
