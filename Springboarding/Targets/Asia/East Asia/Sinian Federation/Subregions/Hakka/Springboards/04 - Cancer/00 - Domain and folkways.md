# Hakka: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; written for the Hakka people and their communities, not a territory. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) in its Meixian, western Fujian (Tingzhou), southern Jiangxi, Huiyang, Hailu and other varieties; written Chinese (traditional and simplified); Cantonese for Hong Kong sources. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what is home, and who is family (household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, building ways, family ways, child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou (Jiaying) Hakka:** the self-described center; weilongwu and emigrant households.
- **Western Fujian (Yongding, Shanghang, Changting, Liancheng, Ninghua):** tulou living and the Shibi ancestral-site tradition.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ganzhou counties):** walled houses and lineage halls; Hakka living next to Gan speakers.
- **Northern and eastern Guangdong (Shaoguan, Heyuan, Huizhou, Hailu Hakka of Shanwei):** dialect and house variants away from Meizhou.
- **Guangxi, Hunan, Sichuan and Hainan Hakka:** later migrants; in Sichuan the "土廣東" whose speech and houses changed among non-Hakka neighbors.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** walled villages, recognized indigenous villagers, and Christian (崇真會) families.
- **Semi-Hakka of the Chaozhou hills (半山客, *check*) and the She people (畲族):** where household customs blur with Teochew and She ways.
- **Neighbors for contrast:** Cantonese, Hokkien, Teochew and Gan households, so no Hakka trait is claimed for what the region shares. Taiwan's Hakka and overseas communities: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for dwellings, lineages and genealogies; mixed for present-day household forms, because mainland censuses do not record the Hakka as a group and figures must come from heartland counties as a proxy. The list of Hakka communities is still a working list for the author to settle.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (several distant varieties), classical and modern written Chinese, Cantonese; German for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin debates (Central Plains descent against local mixing) are identity-laden; genealogies may be private lineage property.
- **Held for review:** none at the domain level.

## 5. First queries
1. CNKI: 客家 AND (家庭 OR 宗族 OR 民居), then filter by 梅州, 龍岩, 贛州.
2. Library catalog: 客家傳統社會叢書, listing every volume's county.
3. Digitized gazetteers: 嘉應州志 風俗 and 方言 sections.
4. FamilySearch catalog: 梅縣, 永定 or 寧化 genealogies with 家訓.
5. Hong Kong census tables: usual language = Hakka, by district council.
