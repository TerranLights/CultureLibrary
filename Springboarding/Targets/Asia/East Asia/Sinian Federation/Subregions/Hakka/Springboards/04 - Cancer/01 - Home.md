# Hakka: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian and other varieties), written Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hakka | 屋 (vuk); 屋下 (vuk-ha) | house; home | the same morpheme does both jobs; *check* dialect spread |
| Hakka | 老屋 (lo-vuk), 祖屋 | old house; ancestral house | the house a family "comes from" |
| Hakka | 轉老屋 | go back to the old house | festival homecoming phrasing; *check* |
| Written Chinese | 化胎, 禾坪, 半月池 | mound behind, threshing yard before, pond in front of a weilongwu | parts of the home as named places |
| Written Chinese | 上樑, 入伙 / 進火 | raising the ridge beam; moving in | house-raising and housewarming rites; *check* Hakka usage |
| Written Chinese | 灶君, 神龕, 堂號 | stove god, household shrine, hall name over the door | what marks a house as a home |
| Written Chinese | 僑房, 番樓 | emigrants' houses, "foreign-style" houses | home built with money sent back |
| Written Chinese | 空心村 | "hollow village" | homes emptied by out-migration |
| Cantonese | 圍村 (wai4 cyun1) | walled village | Hong Kong heritage and press term |

English keywords: Hakka dwelling, weilongwu layout, tulou room allocation, Gannan fortified house, Hakka homecoming, emigrant house, hollow village Meizhou, New Territories walled village.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou weilongwu households:** the half-moon pond, hall and mound as one home; strong emigrant link.
- **Yongding and Shanghang tulou dwellers:** home as one bay in a lineage ring; compare with Hokkien-built tulou in Nanjing and Hua'an counties so the form is not taken as Hakka alone.
- **Ganzhou weiwu families:** square fortified homes; Hakka living beside Gan speakers in southern Jiangxi.
- **Shenzhen and Huizhou Hakka:** old villages swallowed by city growth, with former homes rented to migrants (城中村).
- **New Territories Hakka:** walled villages, later village houses (丁屋; its legal side belongs to Libra) and emigrant owners abroad.
- **Sichuan, Guangxi and Hunan Hakka:** later settlers; home as a remade origin; Guangxi walled houses around Hezhou (*check*).
- **She (畲族) hill villages near Hakka ones:** neighboring house forms and how the two describe their homes.
- **Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew neighbors:** courtyard and row houses as a contrast. Taiwan's Hakka 伙房 compounds: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on architecture and heritage; thinner on what home feels like to the people inside, which needs oral history and literature.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for house-part and rite words that standard Chinese renders loosely; written Chinese for surveys; Cantonese for Hong Kong heritage files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation of villages, and heritage tourism that moves residents out, are touchy in local media.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here.
