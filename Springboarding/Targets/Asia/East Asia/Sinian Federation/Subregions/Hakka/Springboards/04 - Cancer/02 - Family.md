# Hakka: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian, Tingzhou, Gannan, Huiyang and Hailu varieties), classical and modern written Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou lineages:** large hall-centered lineages with many emigrant branches abroad, so "family" spans oceans.
- **Western Fujian (Yongding, Shanghang, Liancheng, Ninghua):** tulou-based lineages; Sibao printing lineages as a case of kin as business.
- **Southern Jiangxi:** lineage villages beside Gan-speaking ones; tests how far kin forms differ across a language line.
- **New Territories Hakka:** lineage trusts under British-era rules, and families divided between Hong Kong and abroad.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** lineages founded by later migrants; genealogies that record the move.
- **Hakka Christians (Basel-mission and Catholic villages):** kin ties redrawn by conversion.
- **She (畲族) neighbors:** a few surnames, intermarriage and shared villages; where kin customs cross the ethnic line.
- **Cantonese and Hokkien lineages:** the better-studied cases that Hakka ones are often read through. Taiwan's Hakka kin studies: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and genealogy, mixed for present-day household and kin contact because surveys do not code the Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for genealogies, Hakka for kin terms and their uses, modern Chinese for scholarship, Cantonese for New Territories village voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies can be lineage-owned and shown only to members; adopted and in-marrying members may be recorded in hidden ways.
- **Held for review:** a Hakka diaspora community in South Asia and its ethnography of family firms, set aside under the standing rule (category only).
