# Hakka: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka in several varieties, modern and classical written Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong and the Punti side of old conflicts). **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou Hakka:** the self-styled "world Hakka capital" (世界客都); belonging asserted from the center.
- **Western Fujian (Ninghua, Changting):** ancestral-site claims; Ninghua's Shibi and Changting's "Hakka capital" title compete with Meizhou.
- **Southern Jiangxi:** the "cradle" claim (客家搖籃); Hakka living beside Gan speakers, where the line between groups is blurrier.
- **Pearl River delta and Hong Kong Hakka:** memory of the Punti-Hakka wars; recognized indigenous villagers against later arrivals.
- **Semi-Hakka of the Chaozhou and Jieyang hills:** two-sided belonging, Hakka at home and Teochew in the market (*check*).
- **Sichuan, Guangxi, Hunan and Hainan Hakka:** belonging kept or lost far from the heartland; Hakka who no longer speak it.
- **She (畲族):** a minority people with Hakka-like speech; where ethnic categories cut across shared village life.
- **Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew neighbors:** each with its own view of the Hakka. Taiwan's Hakka and overseas Hakka associations: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on identity debates; thin on measured feelings of belonging, since no survey isolates the Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Qing and Republican identity texts; Hakka for in-group speech and song; Cantonese for the delta's side of the story.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin and "purity" debates, including genetic studies, are charged; ethnic classification of the She and Hakka touches state categories.
- **Held for review:** no item set aside for this concept.
