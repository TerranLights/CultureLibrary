# Hakka: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (deity, charm and rite names differ by county), modern and classical written Chinese, Cantonese for New Territories villages. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jiangxi weiwu villages:** the most fortress-like Hakka homes; frontier banditry in the Gannan hills.
- **Western Fujian tulou lineages:** a whole lineage behind one wall; the Dingguang and 公王 guardians.
- **Meizhou emigrant villages:** protection bought from abroad (towers, walls, hired guards).
- **Pearl River delta and New Territories Hakka:** walls and militia shaped by the Punti-Hakka wars; fengshui groves still guarded.
- **Huiyang and Bao'an Hakka:** wartime protection and guerrilla memory.
- **Guangxi Hakka:** the God Worshippers' setting; Hakka beside Zhuang and Cantonese settlers.
- **Jieyang and Fengshun hill communities:** Three Mountain Kings worship shared with Teochew neighbors.
- **She (畲族):** their own guardian rites in villages close to Hakka ones.
- **Cantonese, Hokkien and Gan neighbors:** contrast in guardian gods and fortification. Taiwan's Hakka 伯公 and 義民 cults: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on fortification and guardian cults; thin on present-day family protection, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for charm, deity and rite names; classical Chinese for militia and gazetteer records; Cantonese for New Territories sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some rites and charms are held by ritual specialists; the Taiping and wartime histories are politically framed. State protection, policing and self-defense rules belong to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld for this sheet.
