# Hakka: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (hut, roof and lodging words), modern written Chinese for welfare and relocation records, classical Chinese for charity institutions, Cantonese for Hong Kong resettlement files. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan and western Fujian hill dwellers:** shed-people origins and paper-making huts; shelter at its most makeshift.
- **Meizhou town households:** charity halls and pavilions funded by emigrants and merchants.
- **Shaoguan and northern Guangdong:** wartime hosts to refugees from the delta.
- **Bao'an, Huiyang and Shenzhen Hakka:** refuge across the Hong Kong border; later, old village houses rented as cheap shelter to migrant workers.
- **New Territories Hakka:** villages relocated by reservoirs; resettlement housing in new towns.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** shelter in migrant guild halls during the resettlement of Sichuan.
- **Hainan and Guangxi Hakka:** typhoon and flood exposure; *check* local shelter customs.
- **She (畲族):** hill hamlets moved in poverty relocation alongside Hakka ones.
- **Cantonese and Hokkien neighbors:** their charitable halls (善堂) for contrast. Taiwan's Hakka 茶亭 tradition: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on institutions and relocation programs; thin on how present-day Hakka families take in kin or strangers.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for hut and lodging terms; classical Chinese for charity steles and gazetteers; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong government files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border flight and forced relocation are politically framed; welfare-station data can be partial.
- **Held for review:** nothing to hold.
