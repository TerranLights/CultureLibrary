# Hakka: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (song and lament vocabulary above all), modern written Chinese, classical Chinese for Qing records, Cantonese and English for Hong Kong emigration files. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

| Written Chinese | 僑眷, 留守婦女 | dependents of emigrants; women left behind | present-day and historical |
| Written Chinese | 溺女, 棄嬰 | drowning of girls; abandoned infants | Qing prohibitions and charity records |
| Written Chinese | 孤魂, 無主墳 | lonely ghosts; graves without kin | the dead no one tends |

English keywords: Hakka emigrant wives, songs of going abroad, deng lang mei, infant abandonment Qing Guangdong, Red Army children left in Gannan, emptied New Territories villages, hungry ghosts Hakka.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou, Dapu and Xingning emigrant counties:** generations of men abroad; wives, parents and children waiting.
- **Western Fujian (Yongding, Shanghang):** Southeast Asian emigration and Red Army history in the same villages.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ruijin, Yudu, Xingguo):** children and families left by the 1934 retreat; labor migration now.
- **New Territories Hakka:** villages left to the old or to no one; families split between Hong Kong and Europe.
- **Shenzhen and Huizhou:** villages rebuilt by the city, where some families left farming and kin behind.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** where the heartland itself was left generations ago, and how the leaving is retold.
- **She (畲族) and Teochew neighbors:** for testing whether a girl-waiting or girl-giving custom is Hakka or regional.
- **Cantonese emigrant districts:** the closest parallel in scale. Taiwan's Hakka and the Southeast Asian Hakka who did the leaving: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration and song; thin and indirect for infant abandonment and for elders left alone, which come through prohibitions, charity records and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for songs and laments; classical Chinese for Qing bans and steles; English and Malay or Indonesian archives only if the leaver's side is traced (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** infanticide, girl-giving and the Red Army orphans are painful and politically framed; adoptees' records are private.
- **Held for review:** no pointer withheld.
