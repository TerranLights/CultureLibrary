# Hakka: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (mother and in-law terms differ between Meixian, Hailu, Huiyang and Gannan speech), modern written Chinese, classical Chinese for exemplary-women biographies, Cantonese for Hong Kong collections. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou emigrant counties:** mothers who headed households while husbands were abroad.
- **Western Fujian tulou villages:** many mothers under one roof; mother-in-law and daughter-in-law in close quarters; Lüshan birth rites.
- **Southern Jiangxi:** Hakka mothers beside Gan-speaking ones; out-migrant mothers who leave children with grandmothers.
- **Hailu and Huiyang Hakka:** different mother terms and wedding laments.
- **New Territories Hakka:** working mothers in fields and on building sites; church mothers' groups.
- **Shenzhen urban Hakka:** smaller families, city hospitals, paid confinement nannies (月嫂).
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** postpartum and birth customs far from the heartland; whether they held.
- **She (畲族):** a distinct mother-figure tradition in shared hills.
- **Cantonese, Teochew and Hokkien neighbors:** confinement foods and birth goddesses overlap, so contrast is needed. Taiwan's Hakka mothers: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteer ideals and customs; mixed for present-day mothers' lives, which need oral history and census proxies.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for kin, confinement and lament words; classical Chinese for 列女 biographies and arch inscriptions; Cantonese for Hong Kong oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** widow-chastity ideals, girl-giving and son preference are painful topics; avoid one portrait of "the Hakka mother," since roles vary by place, class and era. Marriage and gender roles are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** nothing held under the standing rules.
