# Hakka: Domain and folkways (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) in its Meixian, Huiyang, Tingzhou (western Fujian), Gannan (southern Jiangxi), Guangxi and Sichuan forms; Standard Chinese for most scholarship; Cantonese for Hong Kong's New Territories material. **Concept in one line:** the domain of creativity and self-expression: how people shine, play and take pleasure (traditionally creativity, play, pleasure, romance, children).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou core (Meixian, Xingning, Wuhua, Dabu, Pingyuan, Jiaoling):** the "standard" Hakka forms; test whether its genres are taken as the whole.
- **Eastern and northern Guangdong (Huizhou, Heyuan, Shaoguan, Shenzhen's Longgang):** Hakka living beside Cantonese; the contrast in entertainments.
- **Western Fujian (Changting, Ninghua, Liancheng, Shanghang, Yongding):** tulou life, procession festivals and the Sibao printing district.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ningdu, Xingguo, Yudu, Ruijin, Longnan):** tea-picking opera and the songs of the 1930s soviet base.
- **Guangxi (Bobai, Luchuan, Hezhou, Guiping), Sichuan (Luodai, Dongshan), Hunan (Liuyang, Rucheng), Hainan:** outlying settlements with their own adaptations.
- **New Territories Hong Kong (Sai Kung, Tai Po, Sha Tau Kok, Tsuen Wan):** village Hakka under a different modern history.
- **Semi-Hakka (半山客) on the Chaoshan edge, and the She people:** bilingual borders.
- **Women and men; villagers and urban migrants:** separate rather than merge.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka; Southeast Asian and other overseas Hakka.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for songs, opera and festivals (heritage listing drove much documentation); thin for everyday leisure and pleasure, since surveys rarely tag respondents as Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** Standard written Chinese; Hakka (for song texts and interviews, with dialect dictionaries); Cantonese for Hong Kong; She speech at the borders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the community list in the tracker header is a working list still to be settled by the author. Heritage-promotion writing tends to praise; read it as promotion.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search ihchina.cn for 客家 and list every Hakka item, by county and category (music, dance, opera, custom).
2. Pull the 风俗 chapters of the Jiaying and Tingzhou gazetteers for festival and entertainment entries.
3. Find which *Traditional Hakka Society* volumes cover Meizhou, western Fujian and Gannan, and index their festival chapters.
4. Search CNKI for 客家 民俗 娱乐 and 客家 节庆 to gather recent county studies.
5. Check the Hong Kong inventory for New Territories Hakka items.
