# Hakka: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for New Territories sources. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Fujian printers and builders (Liancheng, Yongding, Changting):** lineage-owned trades; test how credit passes in a family workshop.
- **Meizhou song and painting circles:** improvisers against fixed repertoires; the town's modern artists.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ganzhou, Shicheng, Xingguo):** opera writers and lantern makers serving the festival year.
- **Guangxi (Bobai, Hezhou):** craft turned industry.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai, Dongshan):** crafts reshaped by two centuries among Sichuanese neighbors.
- **New Territories villages:** women's handwork under urban pressure and revival.
- **She people in shared districts:** distinct patterns and dress-making, often merged with Hakka in displays.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka craft revival; overseas Hakka makers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on architecture, printing and heritage crafts, thin on everyday improvising and personal hobbies.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese for heritage files; Hakka for song texts and makers' own terms; English for the tulou dossier and Brokaw.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** credit disputes between villages over heritage listings; Hakka and Hokkien claims to tulou overlap and need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
