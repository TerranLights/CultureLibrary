# Hakka: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong associations. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rival "centers":** Meizhou, Changting, Ninghua and the Gannan counties each claim pride of place; separate their accounts.
- **Lineages:** pride is often lineage-scale before it is Hakka-scale; compare surnames within a village.
- **Hakka Christians (Basel Mission congregations) and temple-going villagers:** different grounds for pride.
- **Returnee and remittance families in Meixian and Dabu:** pride tied to overseas success, kept as comparison.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** long self-named "Guangdong people"; whether pride attaches to Hakka or to Guangdong origin.
- **Hong Kong Hakka associations against New Territories villagers:** organized pride and village pride.
- **Women:** pride in labor and endurance, told separately from men's lineage pride.
- **She people:** neighbors whose own pride narratives are often absorbed into Hakka ones.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka; Southeast Asian Hakka associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on group and lineage pride (associations, gazetteers, promotion); thin on personal pride and arrogance in daily speech.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese; Hakka for sayings and nicknames; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin debates carry ethnic-status stakes; "famous Hakka" lists include disputed claims.
- **Held for review:** none.
