# Hakka: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for New Territories festival records. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Degree-holding lineages against non-degree lineages:** recognition built into some villages and absent from others.
- **Women:** honored through chastity arches and labor stories; often unnamed in genealogies.
- **Overseas-linked families in Meixian and Dabu:** recognized through donor walls, compared with stay-at-home families.
- **Gannan and western Fujian revolutionary districts:** recognized as collectives by the state.
- **She people:** recognized as a separate people by the state, unlike the Hakka; the two sides of the same act.
- **New Territories villagers:** recognition through festival boards and village status.
- **Sichuan, Hunan and Guangxi Hakka:** whether heartland institutions recognize them at all.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka, recognized by a dedicated government council; overseas Hakka honored by home cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal and inscribed recognition (gazetteers, stones, boards); thin for everyday praise and credit.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern written Chinese; Hakka for thanking and address forms; Cantonese for Hong Kong boards.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification is politically charged; genealogies may be held privately by lineages. Legal status questions go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
