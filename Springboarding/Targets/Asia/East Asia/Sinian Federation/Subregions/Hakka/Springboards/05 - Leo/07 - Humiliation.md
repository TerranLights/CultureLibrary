# Hakka: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard and classical written Chinese; Cantonese for Punti-side records and Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hakka of the western Pearl River Delta (Taishan, Kaiping, Heshan, Chixi):** heirs of the feud defeat, set apart from heartland Hakka.
- **Meizhou and Gannan heartland:** rarely a minority locally; humiliation there is mostly internal or political.
- **Hakka in Cantonese-majority cities and Hong Kong:** everyday language shame.
- **Guangxi Hakka near Jintian:** the Taiping legacy.
- **Women within lineages:** separate exposure to shaming (widows, child brides, divorced women).
- **Sichuan Hakka:** mocked or merged under Sichuanese majority; compare.
- **She people:** themselves slighted, sometimes by Hakka.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka under Hokkien majority; overseas Hakka in mixed communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective humiliation (feuds, textbook affairs) and its organized replies; thin on everyday teasing and personal shame.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers and memorials; modern Chinese; Hakka for insults and song duels; Cantonese for Punti-side and Hong Kong material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs written with animal radicals appear in Qing texts; quote nothing, cite only. Political-campaign memories are restricted in parts of the record.
- **Held for review:** none.
