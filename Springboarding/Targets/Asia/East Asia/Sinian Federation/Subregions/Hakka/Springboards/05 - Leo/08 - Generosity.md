# Hakka: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong giving customs. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Remittance-receiving households (Meixian, Dabu, Xingning) against farming households without overseas kin:** who can give, and who is expected to.
- **Heartland villages against Sichuan migrants:** giving through lineage against giving through guild halls.
- **Gannan and western Fujian mountain villages:** pavilion and bridge giving along trade routes.
- **Women and men:** who hosts, who cooks, who pays, who gives the red packet.
- **Hong Kong New Territories villagers:** gift norms shared with Cantonese neighbors.
- **Christian Hakka congregations:** church giving beside lineage giving.
- **She communities:** labor exchange with Hakka neighbors.
- *Comparison only:* overseas Hakka donors as givers; Taiwan's Hakka temple sponsorship.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on philanthropy, remittances and inscribed public works; thinner on everyday treating and its tensions.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese (classical for steles); Hakka for gift vocabulary; Cantonese for Hong Kong; English for Oxfeld.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift-giving at official occasions can shade into bribery debates; leave the legal side to Libra or Capricorn. Remittance letters are family papers.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Oxfeld 2010 for its chapters on debt, gratitude and reciprocity; note the local terms she records.
