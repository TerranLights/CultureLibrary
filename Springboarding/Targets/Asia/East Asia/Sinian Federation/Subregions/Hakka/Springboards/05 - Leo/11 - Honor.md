# Hakka: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; classical and Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong oath and feud material. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Feud-prone border districts (western Fujian, southern Jiangxi) against calmer interior villages:** where honor was defended by force.
- **Gannan revolutionary counties:** collective honor of the dead, officially framed.
- **Women:** reputation guarded through marriage and widowhood; separate sources needed.
- **Sworn-brotherhood and society members against lineage-bound men:** two codes of loyalty.
- **New Territories villages:** dispute settlement under different institutions.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** codes reshaped among other peoples.
- **She people:** their own codes of oath and reputation beside Hakka neighbors.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka feud histories; overseas Hakka brotherhoods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; feuds, loyalist legends and memorials are documented; everyday face and promise-keeping are thin in writing.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers and feud records; modern Chinese; Hakka for reputation vocabulary; Cantonese for Hong Kong; English for Hu, Lamley and Murray.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence justified by honor, especially against women, must be researched in survivors' words; martyr narratives are officially managed. Legal aspects of feuds go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
