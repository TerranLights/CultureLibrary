# Hakka: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家话) and its dialects; Standard written Chinese; Cantonese for New Territories village feasts. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Fujian procession towns against Meizhou lantern towns:** different shapes of festival joy.
- **Christian Hakka:** joy in hymn and church feasts, set beside temple festival crowds.
- **Women and men:** who cooks the festive food, who drinks and dances, who teases at weddings.
- **Families with new sons, new daughters, or none:** whose joy is publicly marked.
- **Migrant workers returning for New Year:** joy of reunion against the rest of the year.
- **New Territories villages:** basin feasts and festivals in a city setting.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** joy shaped by neighbors' calendars.
- **She people:** their own festival songs beside Hakka ones.
- *Comparison only:* Taiwan's Hakka festivals; overseas Hakka New Year gatherings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on festival joy through heritage listings; thin on private contentment and on how joy is spoken of day to day.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for joy words and storytelling; written Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong; romanized Hakka for mission hymnals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wedding teasing can shade into harassment; ask with care. Survey data cannot isolate Hakka respondents.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the heritage dossiers for 罗坊走古事, 姑田游大龙, 兴宁花灯 and 于都唢呐.

3. Search for 客家 婚俗 闹洞房 to collect wedding-joy customs by county.
4. Locate romanized Hakka hymnals in mission archive catalogs.
5. Sample Douyin for 客家 过年 videos and note the districts shown.
