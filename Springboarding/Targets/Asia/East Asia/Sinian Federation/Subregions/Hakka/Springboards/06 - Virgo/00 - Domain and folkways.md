# Hakka: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话; Meixian speech as the reference variety, plus the western Fujian and southern Jiangxi varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what good work and a well-kept life are, with the daily round, the kept house, chores, craft, rest and health as its traditional areas.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Meizhou counties (梅縣, 興寧, 五華, 大埔, 蕉嶺, 平遠): the reference heartland and its emigrant villages.
- Western Fujian (長汀, 上杭, 永定, 連城, 寧化, 武平): tulou living, river trade and printing towns.
- Southern Jiangxi (寧都, 興國, 于都, 瑞金, 尋烏, 龍南): many there call themselves 本地人; hill farming and walled houses (圍屋).
- Huizhou, Heyuan and eastern Shenzhen; northern Guangdong (韶關): Hakka life beside Cantonese towns.
- Guangxi (博白, 陸川, 賀州), Sichuan (Chengdu's 東山, 洛帶), Hunan border counties, Hainan, Hong Kong's New Territories: later migrations, different neighbors.
- The She people (畲族), whose speech is close to Hakka; Cantonese, Hokkien, Teochew and Gan neighbors, to keep the Hakka material distinct.
- Taiwan's Hakka and the overseas diaspora (mining, shopkeeping and trade work abroad): comparison only, for how work and service shift away from the heartland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese ethnography and heritage listings; thin in statistics, because Hakka is not a census category. County proxies (Meizhou's counties) will be needed and are imperfect.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, Hakka (several varieties), Cantonese, Japanese; German and French for mission sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** contested origin narratives; the Punti-Hakka feuds; the community list in the tracker header is a working list still to be settled by the author.
- **Held for review:** none.
