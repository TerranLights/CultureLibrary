# Hakka: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian as reference; Minxi, Gannan, Huiyang, Sichuan and Hong Kong varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

English keywords: Hakka children's chores; Hakka elders' roles; Hakka left-behind villages; Hakka proverbs idleness; Hakka women capability; Hakka household tools reuse.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Meizhou's emigrant villages: usefulness tied to remittances and to those who stay behind.
- Hill villages of southern Jiangxi and western Fujian with fewer emigrants: usefulness tied to field and forest labor.
- Huizhou and eastern Shenzhen: Hakka villages swallowed by factories, where land and rent replace field work.
- Sichuan's Dongshan Hakka: farm villages near a metropolis; elders' roles in tourist towns such as 洛帶.
- Guangxi's Bobai and Luchuan: large farming Hakka populations away from the heartland.
- Hong Kong's New Territories: emptied villages, returnees and the value of land and village rights.
- The She people: overlapping customs of household labor that need separating from Hakka ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Lexical and folk material is rich; data on elders and disability exists only by place.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties, Standard Chinese, Cantonese; English for missionary dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child brides and adopted daughters-in-law; disability and old age; women's labor framed as a single ethnic trait.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Search CNKI for 梅州 留守老人 and 客家 老人會.
