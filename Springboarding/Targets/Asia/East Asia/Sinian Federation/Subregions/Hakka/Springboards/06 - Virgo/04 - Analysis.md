## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Southern Jiangxi (Xingguo, Ningdu, Yudu): home of the Gannan geomantic school and of the Soviet-era surveys.
- Meizhou and Dabu: a long examination record and an early modern-school network.
- Western Fujian's merchant lineages (Sibao, Yongding): ledger and contract culture.
- Huizhou and eastern Shenzhen Hakka: urban, technical schooling beside Cantonese neighbors.
- Hong Kong Hakka villages: colonial schooling and accounting in English.
- Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka: whether almanac and geomantic practices traveled with migrants.
- Teochew and Hokkien merchant neighbors, whose bookkeeping is the nearest comparison; the She, for shared ritual reckoning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for geomancy, examinations and the Soviet-era surveys; thin for everyday reasoning, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (gazetteers, examination lists, geomantic manuals), Standard Chinese, Hakka, Cantonese, Japanese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** geomantic and divinatory practice sits in a gray zone of official tolerance; practitioners may be wary of outsiders. Origin debates turn on contested readings of genealogies.
- **Held for review:** none.
