# Hakka: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian, Xingguo, Changting, Huiyang and Hong Kong speech), Standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Southern Jiangxi: song contests and the "old"/"new" settler distinction sharpen questions of judgment and belonging.
- Meizhou: tea, weilongwu siting and a dense genealogy culture.
- Western Fujian (Ninghua's Shibi, the claimed point of origin): where ancestral claims meet.
- Huizhou, Heyuan and eastern Shenzhen: Hakka judgment in mixed Cantonese markets.
- Hong Kong's New Territories: competitive folk performance and village status.
- Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka: whether markets and matchmaking kept heartland forms.
- The She, the Teochew and the Cantonese, against whom Hakka identity is judged and told apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Song, ritual and identity scholarship are rich; everyday market and personal judgment is thin and needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (song texts need local readers), Standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity boundaries between Hakka and She, and between Hakka and Punti, touch old conflicts; divination is officially tolerated only in part; scams aimed at elders.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 興國山歌 對唱 and 客家山歌 鬥歌 and list studies of contests.

4. Search CNKI and county folklore volumes for 客家 婚俗 相親.
