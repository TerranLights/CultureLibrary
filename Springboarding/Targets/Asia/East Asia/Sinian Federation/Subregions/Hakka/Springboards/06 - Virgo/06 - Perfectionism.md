# Hakka: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong crafts). **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Western Fujian (Yongding, the Hakka edge of Nanjing county 南靖, Liancheng): earthen building, woodblock carving and paper, three exacting crafts in one area.
- Meizhou (Meixian, Dabu): weilongwu carpentry, ceramics, Han opera and eight-tone music.
- Southern Jiangxi (Longnan, Dingnan, Quannan): walled houses with different building standards.
- Hong Kong's New Territories: women's weaving and heritage restoration under a different administration.
- Sichuan's Luodai: guild halls rebuilt by Hakka migrants, whose craft standards may differ.
- Guangxi's Hakka: whether builders kept heartland forms or took Zhuang and Cantonese ones.
- Cantonese and Teochew neighbors, whose fine carving and ritual exactness are the nearest comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts and heritage listings; thin for personal perfectionism, which needs psychology studies with place-identified samples.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese (manuals), Hakka (craft vocabulary), Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** academic pressure and student mental health; heritage listings can present one "correct" form and hide variation.
- **Held for review:** none.
