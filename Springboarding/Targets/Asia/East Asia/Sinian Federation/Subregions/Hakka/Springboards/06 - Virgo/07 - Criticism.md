# Hakka: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (song texts and village speech), Standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese, English for colonial-era records. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Meizhou: the densest written record of Hakka self-defense against outside criticism, and of satirical song.
- Southern Jiangxi and western Fujian: the old Soviet areas, where campaign-era denunciation left the deepest marks.
- The Pearl River Delta's Hakka (Chixi, Taishan, Heshan): the feud zone, where mutual reproach with Cantonese neighbors was sharpest.
- Guangxi's Thistle Mountain area: Taiping origins and dissent.
- Hong Kong's New Territories: Christian Hakka villages and an open press, where criticism has different outlets.
- Sichuan's Dongshan Hakka: a minority among Sichuanese speakers, with its own ways of answering outsiders.
- The Cantonese (Punti) and the She, whose views of the Hakka and whose quarrels with them need separating.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organized responses to outside criticism and for song; thin for everyday family criticism, which needs ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (songs and sketches), Standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese, German and English for mission and colonial sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** insulting historical names for Hakka in old texts; the feuds; campaign-era violence; online criticism of officials may be deleted.
- **Held for review:** none.
