# Hakka: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, Cantonese; English and Dutch for Hong Kong villagers' emigration. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Southern Jiangxi and western Fujian: improvement framed by the state as repayment to old revolutionary bases.
- Meizhou: improvement through emigration, remittances and schooling.
- Changting: improvement of land after severe erosion.
- Huizhou and eastern Shenzhen: Hakka villages remade by industry and rent, the fastest change of all.
- Sichuan's Dongshan and Guangxi's Bobai: betterment through heritage tourism or farming in later-settled areas.
- Hong Kong's New Territories: betterment through emigration and land development.
- The She, the Cantonese and the Teochew, who were often the object or the rival of Hakka betterment narratives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy, schools and heritage development; thin for personal self-improvement, which needs fieldwork and online material.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese, Hakka, Cantonese, English; Dutch for Hong Kong Hakka in the Netherlands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official revitalization narratives tied to revolutionary history; displacement by development and tourism; contested ecological effects of hill settlement.
- **Held for review:** none.
