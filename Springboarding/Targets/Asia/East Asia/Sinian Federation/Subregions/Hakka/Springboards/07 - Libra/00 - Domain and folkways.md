# Hakka: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家) people and communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话; Meixian, Tingzhou, Gannan and other varieties), Mandarin, Cantonese, Literary Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what is a fair bargain and a good partnership? (partnership, marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance, justice, and the letter of the law).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou core** (Meixian, Jiaoling, Pingyuan) against **Xingning and Wuhua**: other subdialects, possibly other marriage and quarrel customs.
- **Dabu and Fengshun, and the 半山客 ("half-mountain Hakka") on the Teochew border**: partnership and rivalry with Chaoshan neighbors.
- **Western Fujian**: Changting and Ninghua (Shibi) against **Yongding**, whose round houses sit beside Hokkien-built ones.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Gannan)**: older and later Hakka layers next to Gan speakers.
- **Dongjiang Hakka** (Huizhou, Heyuan, Shaoguan) and urbanized **Shenzhen–Dongguan** Hakka.
- **Western Pearl River Delta Hakka**: the feud zone with Cantonese Punti.
- **Hong Kong's New Territories**: villages under a distinct written land law.
- **Guangxi** (Bobai, Luchuan, Hezhou), **Sichuan** (Luodai), **Hunan** and **Hainan**: settlers among Zhuang, Huguang migrants and Min speakers.
- **The She (畲) people**, close Hakka-speaking neighbors in the same hills.
- Christian and non-Christian Hakka, for marriage rites.
- Taiwan's Hakka and the overseas kongsi tradition only as comparison, for organized partnership outside the heartland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage papers, marriage customs and Hong Kong land law; mixed for feuds (sources lean to one side); thin for surveys that isolate Hakka respondents.
- **Communities:** the tracker's community list is a working list for the author to confirm; the sheets of this sign are written for the heartland and the listed communities.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (several varieties), Mandarin, Cantonese, Literary Chinese; English and German for mission and colonial papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** memory of the Hakka-Punti wars; disputed origin claims; male-line rights in the New Territories; privately held lineage papers.
- **Held for review:** none.
