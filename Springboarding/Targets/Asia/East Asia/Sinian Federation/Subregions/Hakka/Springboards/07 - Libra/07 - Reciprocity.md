# Hakka: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Mandarin, Literary Chinese for lineage and temple records, Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties (Meixian, Dabu, Jiaoling, Yongding)**: gifts flow between the hometown and those abroad, so reciprocity has a long-distance form.
- **Gannan counties** (emigration levels to check): if fewer people left, exchange may stay among neighbors and kin, a useful contrast.
- **Western Fujian round-house communities**: many households under one roof, where help and gifts are visible to all.
- **Hong Kong New Territories**: village feasts and lineage trusts under a cash economy and colonial law.
- **Shenzhen and Huizhou**: factory wages and city life changing the size and timing of gifts.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai)** and **Guangxi Hakka**: exchange with non-Hakka neighbors, and how the rules mix.
- **She communities**: gift and labor exchange with Hakka villages nearby.
- **Christian Hakka**: church giving beside customary gift books.
- Overseas Hakka donors only as comparison from the giving side; the heartland's receiving side is the subject here.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigrant donations and gift customs; mixed for household accounts (private); survey data exist but Hakka respondents must be found by county.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (rite names and gift terms vary by county); Mandarin; Literary Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift money can shade into bribery and is officially policed; private gift books need family consent; religious rites were suppressed for decades and may be reported cautiously.
- **Held for review:** none.
