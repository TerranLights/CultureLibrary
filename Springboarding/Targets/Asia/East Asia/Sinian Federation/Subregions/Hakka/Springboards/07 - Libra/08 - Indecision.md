# Hakka: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Mandarin, Literary Chinese for lineage and temple texts, Cantonese and English for Hong Kong policy records. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties of Meizhou and Yongding**: the choice to leave was common enough to have its own song genre, so indecision has a migration form here.
- **Western Fujian temple towns (Wuping, Changting)**: temples whose lots may be a main way of settling doubt (to check).
- **Gannan villages**: home ground of day-choosing and siting specialists, so a decision may be split between family and expert.
- **Hong Kong New Territories**: public decisions on village land that have stayed open for decades.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan Hakka**: urban choices (jobs, housing, marriage) made with fewer lineage elders involved.
- **Sichuan and Hunan Hakka**: small communities where a family may weigh whether to keep Hakka ways at all.
- **Hakka Christians**: prayer and pastors in place of temple lots.
- Taiwan's Hakka only as comparison, for a modern consultation culture around heritage and language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for indecision as such; most leads are indirect (divination, ritual timing, songs, stalled collective decisions). Survey measures that isolate Hakka respondents are unlikely.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for songs and opera; Literary Chinese for lot verses and lineage records; Mandarin; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination may be reported cautiously in official sources; heritage demolition disputes involve compensation quarrels.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 客家 籤詩 and 客家 擇日 to find studies of decision rituals.
2. Search 過番歌 客家 for collected texts and recordings.
3. Search the Legislative Council archive for "Small House Policy review" and list the dates it was raised.
4. Search 一事一議 with 梅州 or 贛州 for village case studies.
5. Read the 公議 sections of two heartland genealogies and note how long matters took.
