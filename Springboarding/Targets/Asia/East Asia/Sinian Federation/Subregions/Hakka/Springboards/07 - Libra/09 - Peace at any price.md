# Hakka: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Mandarin, Literary Chinese for family instructions and Qing memorials, Cantonese and English for Hong Kong debates. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Pearl River Delta Hakka and the Chixi enclave**: the community whose war ended in a settlement that moved many of its people.
- **Hainan and Guangxi Hakka**: descendants of those moved, with their own memory of the price paid.
- **Meizhou emigrant households**: wives who kept homes running for absent husbands, a setting for long endurance.
- **Hong Kong New Territories**: women who challenged village custom in the 1990s, and the elders who opposed them.
- **Gannan and western Fujian revolutionary counties**: the revolutionary record of the 1930s offers a contrast to accommodation.
- **Guangxi Hakka near the Taiping homeland**: rebellion rather than giving way.
- **Urban Shenzhen and Dongguan workers**: workplace silence or protest away from lineage pressure.
- **She communities**: how a smaller neighbor kept peace with Hakka majorities in shared hills.
- **Christian Hakka**: church teachings on forgiveness and their use in family quarrels.
- Taiwan's Hakka only as comparison, for a later public debate on Hakka women's roles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; norms of endurance are well documented in family instructions and songs, but what was kept silent is by nature hard to find. Legal and mediation data are official and may favor settled outcomes.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for songs and oral accounts; Literary Chinese for instructions and memorials; Mandarin; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and abuse; women's testimonies; land-reform and campaign memories; the Hakka-Punti war's rival memories. Interviews need consent and care.
- **Held for review:** none.
