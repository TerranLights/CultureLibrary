# Hakka: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, Mandarin, Cantonese (for how neighbors name and picture the Hakka), Literary Chinese for Qing records, English for missionary and textbook sources. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Pearl River Delta Hakka**: enemy images may be sharpest here (to test), formed in open war with Cantonese neighbors.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka**: pictured daily in Cantonese media, so outside images can be traced in real time.
- **Meizhou and Xingning scholars**: among those who answered outsiders' images with a counter-narrative in the 1920s–30s (*check* who led it).
- **Western Fujian and Gannan**: Hakka next to She and Gan neighbors, with different "others" from the Cantonese.
- **Sichuan Hakka**: labeled by other migrant groups with a name tied to Guangdong.
- **Guangxi Hakka**: neighbors of the Zhuang, and tied by Qing records to the Taiping rebels.
- **Hainan Hakka**: a smaller community among Min speakers and Li, with its own outsider images.
- **Hakka Christians**: once the target of blame in mission cases.
- The overseas diaspora only as comparison, for how Hakka images shift where Hakka are a minority among non-Chinese.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the textbook disputes, the war, and the origin debate; mixed for sorcery and misfortune beliefs; thin for direct measures of how Hakka now picture neighbors.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka; Cantonese (the main outside voice); Mandarin; Literary Chinese; English for missionary and textbook sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic slurs between Hakka and neighbors must be quoted only as recorded terms; origin claims are identity-charged; sorcery accusations named real women and should not be repeated as fact.
- **Held for review:** none.
