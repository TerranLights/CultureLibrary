# Hakka: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话), Meixian, Huiyang, Tingzhou and Gannan varieties; Standard Chinese; Cantonese and Hokkien for neighbors' sources. **Concept in one line:** how a people faces death, power and what is hidden: death and rebirth, sex, shared resources, inheritance and secrets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou core (Meixian, Xingning, Wuhua, Dabu, Pingyuan, Jiaoling):** the "standard" Hakka of most literature; keep it from speaking for the rest.
- **Huiyang-speaking Hakka (Huizhou, Heyuan, Shenzhen's Longgang) and Hailu Hakka (Haifeng, Lufeng):** urbanized or beside Cantonese and Teochew neighbors.
- **Western Fujian (Changting, Ninghua, Yongding, Shanghang) and southern Jiangxi (Ganzhou, Ningdu, Ruijin, Longnan):** walled-house lineages beside Hokkien and Gan speakers.
- **Guangxi, Hunan, Sichuan (Luodai), Hainan, Shaoguan:** migrant pockets; what survives away from the heartland.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka; Christian against temple households; the She 畲族 of the same hills** (a distinct people, not to be merged).
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka and the overseas diaspora, for how reburial and ancestral rites are carried on away from the home villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funerals, secondary burial, lineages and song; thin for money in marriage and sex ways, since general Chinese surveys rarely separate Hakka speakers. The community list is a working list for the author to confirm, so which pockets count is still open.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hakka for songs, laments and interviews; Cantonese for Hong Kong; some German for the Basel archive.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exhumation is handled by kin and specialists; grave and lineage disputes can be live quarrels; mid-20th-century violence in these hills is sensitive on the mainland.
- **Held for review:** none.
