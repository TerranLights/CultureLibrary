# Hakka: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話); Standard Chinese for scholarship, party records and news; Cantonese for Hong Kong village rites. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jiangxi and western Fujian soviet counties (Ruijin, Ningdu, Yudu, Changting, Shanghang):** where purge memory runs deepest.
- **Ganzhou mining counties (Longnan, Dingnan, Xunwu):** cleanup of poisoned land as a lived issue.
- **Shanghang and the Ting River valley:** a single river disaster to trace through its villages.
- **Meizhou cadre families:** possible memory of Guangdong's removals of local officials; check before assuming.
- **Hong Kong New Territories villages:** periodic jiao cleansing, kept under different politics.
- **Guangxi Hakka near Guiping:** memory of Taiping image-smashing.
- **Christian Hakka:** how they handle images, tablets and exorcism differently from temple households.
- **Neighbors:** Gan-speaking "native" villages in the Jiangxi highlands, the other side of the guest-native split; Cantonese villages that share the jiao; the She.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka jiao and diaspora temple cleansing rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual cleansing and for the political purges; mixed for bodily cleansing; thin for shunning inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and party-history idiom; classical Chinese for genealogies; Hakka for healers' words; Cantonese for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930–31 purges, land reform and later campaigns are politically controlled topics; pollution reporting has been censored; healers may not share their formulas.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. FamilySearch catalog: Hakka county genealogies from Meixian and Changting; read their 族规 sections.
2. CNKI: 中央苏区 肃反, then 闽西 肃社党, then 客家 打醮.
3. Read Averill's chapters on the 1930 purges and follow his sources.
4. Open web: 紫金矿业 汀江 2010, then 赣州 稀土 生态修复.
5. Library catalog: Faure, then the eastern New Territories jiao reports he cites.
