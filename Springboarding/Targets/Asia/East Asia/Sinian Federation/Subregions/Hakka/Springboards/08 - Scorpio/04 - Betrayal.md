# Hakka: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話) and Standard Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong and East River sources. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangxi Hakka around Guiping and Jintian:** the Taiping homeland, where the movement's betrayals are local memory.
- **Huiyang and Shenzhen's eastern districts:** wartime guerrilla and collaborator memory.
- **Shaoguan and northern Guangdong:** defection memory from the soviet years.
- **Haifeng and Lufeng:** the Hailu Hakka beside Hoklo-speaking neighbors; check which side of local revolutionary betrayals each group sits on.
- **Emigrant counties (Meixian, Dabu, Xingning, Mei River villages):** left-behind wives and broken promises across the sea.
- **Southern Jiangxi and western Fujian:** land-reform and soviet-era accusations among kin.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** colonial takeover and collaboration in village memory.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** whether keeping Hakka speech among Sichuanese speakers is framed as loyalty, and dropping it as betrayal.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka, and diaspora remittance families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on political and military betrayal; mixed on marital betrayal (songs and opera rich, surveys area-only); thin on betrayal in business.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hakka for songs and idioms; Cantonese for Hong Kong and East River sources; English for colonial records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traitor" labels from party history are still official; land-reform accusations involve living families; affairs and child-bride customs touch private family pain.
- **Held for review:** none.
