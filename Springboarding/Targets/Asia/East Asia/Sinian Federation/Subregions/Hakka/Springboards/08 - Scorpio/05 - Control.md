# Hakka: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話) and Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for geomantic and stele texts. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jiangxi (Xingguo, Ganzhou city):** home of a geomantic lineage; control of fortune as a profession.
- **Western Fujian tulou lineages (Yongding, Nanjing 南靖 county):** control built into defensive housing and lineage rule.
- **Meizhou emigrant counties:** households run by women for long stretches; check how control was shared or contested.
- **Huizhou and Shenzhen Hakka:** fast urbanization and new forms of state and workplace control.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** village control of land under colonial and later rules.
- **Guangxi, Sichuan, Hunan and Hainan pockets:** control negotiated as minorities among other-speaking neighbors.
- **Christian Hakka:** providence and prayer in place of day-choosing and geomancy.
- **The She and Gan-speaking neighbors:** different ritual tools for the same hills and weather.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka almanac and geomancy practice; diaspora associations' control over members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on geomancy, day-choosing and village rules; mixed on control inside couples (national surveys, no Hakka split); thin on personal locus of control.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for steles and geomantic texts; Standard Chinese; Hakka for proverbs and household talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family planning enforcement and grid surveillance are politically sensitive; domestic violence is private and under-reported; geomancers may guard their methods.
- **Held for review:** none.
