# Hakka: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話); Standard Chinese; classical documentary Chinese for old contracts; Sichuanese and Cantonese where Hakka pockets sit among those speakers. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou emigrant counties (Meixian, Dabu, Songkou area):** trust stretched across the sea through couriers and letters.
- **Western Fujian tulou lineages:** trust inside a shared fortress house against trust in the outside market.
- **Southern Jiangxi:** official narratives of trust between villagers and the revolution, against family memory.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai and other Chengdu-area settlements):** trust organized through guild halls among a minority.
- **Huizhou and Shenzhen Hakka:** fast commercial growth; trust moving from kin to markets and platforms.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** villagers' trust in colonial and later land rules and in their own representatives.
- **Guangxi and Hainan pockets:** Hakka minority among Zhuang, Cantonese or Hainanese speakers; trust across speech lines.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew traders the Hakka deal with; the She in shared hills.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka rotating clubs and diaspora remittance houses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for remittance trust and contracts; mixed for rotating credit (scattered news and local studies); thin for generalized trust measured among Hakka speakers.
- **Languages needed:** documentary Chinese for contracts; Standard Chinese; Hakka for proverbs and interviews; English for Hong Kong colonial files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rotating-club collapses and informal lending can be legally sensitive; official narratives of trust in the Party dominate public sources.
- **Held for review:** none.
