# Hakka: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話) and Standard Chinese; Cantonese for the western Pearl River Delta feud literature and Hong Kong; classical Chinese for Qing memorials and gazetteers. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

English keywords: Punti-Hakka war; Hakka feuds; lineage feuding Fujian Guangdong; Chixi; Taiping anti-Manchu proclamations; wronged ghost tales; feud mediation lineage elders.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chixi and other Hakka enclaves in Taishan, Enping and Kaiping:** pockets inside Cantonese country shaped by the 1850s–60s war.
- **Guangxi Hakka (Guiping, Bobai, Luchuan):** feuds with "native" villages and the Taiping aftermath.
- **Fujian–Guangdong border counties (Yongding, Pinghe, Dabu, Raoping):** feuds along the line between Hakka and Hokkien or Teochew speech.
- **Southern Jiangxi hill settlers:** long frictions between incoming and settled lineages.
- **Hailu Hakka (Haifeng, Lufeng):** living beside Hoklo-speaking majorities.
- **Meizhou core:** check whether feud memory is weaker where Hakka form the whole population.
- **Sichuan and Hunan Hakka:** settlers among other migrant groups; check for feud records.
- **Hong Kong villages:** disputes held inside a court-based order.
- **The She people:** their own place in the hill conflicts, studied on their own terms.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka–Hokkien feud history and diaspora clashes (for example in Borneo's kongsi).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing-era feuds and the Punti–Hakka war; mixed for modern village clashes; thin for personal and household revenge.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for memorials and gazetteers; Standard Chinese; Cantonese for the delta; Hakka for tales and insults.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feud memory can still divide neighboring villages; violence of the 1960s (including in Guangxi) is a closed subject on the mainland; present-day clashes are lightly reported.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: Liu Ping's monograph and the 赤溪县志; read the account of how the war ended.
2. Find Lamley's essay in *Violence in China* and list the counties he names in the Hakka borderlands.
3. CNKI: 土客械斗, then 客家 械斗 闽西, then 客家 宗族 调解.
4. Folk-tale volumes for Meixian, Changting and Ningdu: index search for 冤 and 报应.
5. Open web: 械斗 和约 碑 for inscriptions in Hakka counties.
