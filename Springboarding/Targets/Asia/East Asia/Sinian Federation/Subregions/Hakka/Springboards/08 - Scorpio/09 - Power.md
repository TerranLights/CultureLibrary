# Hakka: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities (non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話) and Standard Chinese; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong village politics; classical Chinese for examination lists and gazetteers. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou core (Meixian, Dabu, Jiaoling):** examination success, overseas money and high office; test whether that is the pattern elsewhere.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Xunwu, Ruijin, Ningdu):** the place where revolutionary power displaced lineage and landlord power.
- **Western Fujian tulou lineages:** power held inside a single fortress house.
- **Hong Kong New Territories:** village representatives and the Heung Yee Kuk, with Cantonese "Punti" lineages as rivals and partners.
- **Jiexi and Hepo, where Hakka meet Teochew speakers:** temple power and the Three Mountain Kings.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** minority settlers under brotherhood and warlord power.
- **Guangxi Hakka:** a minority that once rose against the state; how power is held now.
- **Huizhou and Shenzhen:** new economic power in fast-growing cities.
- **The She:** a neighbor people with a different place in local hierarchies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lineage and revolutionary power; mixed on present-day networks (official biographies only, sensitive); thin on power inside households.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Hakka for songs and interviews; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elite networks and elite family power are sensitive on the mainland; Hong Kong village rights are politically charged; avoid treating "Hakka networks" as a fact before it is tested.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Report from Xunwu* and list the kinds of power holders it names.
2. Gazetteer database: 嘉应州志 选举志 and 汀州府志 选举志, counting degree holders by county.

4. CNKI: 宗族 村委会 选举 梅州, then 三山国王 理事会.
5. Open web: 田家炳 中学 and 曾宪梓 捐赠 梅州, noting what donors are given in return.
