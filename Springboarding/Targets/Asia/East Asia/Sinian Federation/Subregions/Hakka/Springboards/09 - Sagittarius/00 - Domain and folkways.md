# Hakka: The domain: belief and philosophy (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话) (Meixian, Tingzhou, Gannan, Huiyang forms); Standard Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** belief and philosophy: what is true, where meaning is found, and the religious, freedom and learning ways that carry those answers.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou (Meixian speech core) vs. western Fujian (Tingzhou, Yongding tulou country) vs. Gannan:** three cores whose cults and lineage forms need separate files.
- **Huizhou, Shenzhen and Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** long mission contact plus village *jiao* (醮) festivals; separate Christian and non-Christian villages.
- **Guangxi Hakka (Guiping, Bobai, Luchuan):** the Taiping homeland; check how its memory differs from Guangdong's.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai near Chengdu, the "土廣東"):** Qing-era settlers; guild halls (會館) as shrine and civic center.
- **Hunan and Hainan Hakka:** scattered; separate before merging.
- **The She people (畲族):** non-Han hill neighbors, many speaking a Hakka-like variety; do not file their rites as Hakka.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese, Hokkien, Teochew and Gan communities around each core, for contrast in cults.
- **Taiwan's Hakka and the overseas diaspora (comparison only):** their temples to the same Three Mountain Kings and the Yimin (義民) dead show how the cults travel when the home sites are absent.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Meizhou, Minxi and the New Territories; mixed for Gannan; thin for Hunan and Hainan. Census and religion surveys do not name the Hakka, so counts need proxies.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional), Hakka varieties and church romanization, Cantonese for Hong Kong; German and English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** registered and unregistered churches; "feudal superstition" (封建迷信) labels on folk ritual; contested origin claims. Taiping memory is politically loaded.
- **Scope:** the community list in the tracker header is a working list still to be confirmed by the author; this sign's sheets are written for the communities it names.
- **Held for review:** none.
