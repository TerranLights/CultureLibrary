# Hakka: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian as reference variety; Changting, Xingguo, Bobai and Sichuan forms); Standard Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou walled-house lineages vs. Minxi tulou communities:** two house forms that may frame family purpose differently.
- **Gannan, especially the former Soviet counties:** meaning tied to revolutionary memory; check how villagers and museums differ.
- **Guangxi Hakka:** Taiping and later histories; whether a cause still frames local identity.
- **Sichuan Hakka ("土廣東"):** two to three centuries away from the heartland; whether ancestral purpose weakened or was rebuilt.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** postwar emigration to Europe (*check*) and emptied villages; meaning sought abroad and at home.
- **Women and men separately:** lineage sources speak for men; songs and memoirs keep women's accounts.
- **Neighbors (Cantonese, Hokkien, Gan):** whether proverbs and songs on hardship are shared regional stock or distinct.
- **Overseas diaspora (comparison only):** their memoirs of leaving answer the same question from the far end of the 過番 journey.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in songs, genealogies and literature; thin in measured well-being, since surveys do not code Hakka identity.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties for song lyrics (often written with local characters); Standard Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair are under-reported; revolutionary memory is officially curated; girl-bride and adoption histories touch living families.
- **Held for review:** none.
