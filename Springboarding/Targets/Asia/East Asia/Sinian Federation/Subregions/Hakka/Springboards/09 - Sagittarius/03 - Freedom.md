# Hakka: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties; Standard Chinese; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong's New Territories; Qing documentary Chinese for archives. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangxi and Guangdong (Taiping) Hakka:** freedom as revolt; check whether descendants claim or avoid it.
- **Hakka of the old feud zone (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping):** displacement and the coolie trade; freedom lost by force.
- **Gannan and Minxi soviet counties:** "liberation" as a state story; compare official and family accounts.
- **New Territories villages:** colonial-era resistance and later rights claims; a different state frame from the mainland.
- **Women in each core:** bound feet, girl-brides, unbound labor; freedom differs by sex and by era.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** settlers who moved under a state resettlement policy; movement as both freedom and order.
- **Cantonese (Punti) neighbors:** the other side of the feuds; their records frame the same events differently.
- **Taiwan's Hakka (comparison only):** the 1988 "give back my mother tongue" march (還我母語) frames language as a freedom; contrast with how the heartland discusses Hakka in schools and broadcasting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Taiping and the feuds; mixed on women's bodily freedom; thin and guarded on present-day political freedom.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese for Qing archives; Standard Chinese; Hakka varieties for songs and oral memory; English for colonial records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current civil liberties in mainland China and Hong Kong; revolutionary memory is officially curated; coolie-trade descendants may not wish to be identified. Law on marriage, land and village rights goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
