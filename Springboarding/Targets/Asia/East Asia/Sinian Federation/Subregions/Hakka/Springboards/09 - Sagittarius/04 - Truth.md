# Hakka: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka varieties (Meixian, Changting, Ningdu, Huiyang); English and German for nineteenth-century mission ethnology. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou vs. Minxi vs. Gannan scholars:** each heartland's universities argue origins differently; separate the local schools of thought.
- **Lineage elders and genealogy compilers vs. academic historians:** two authorities on the same past.
- **Hong Kong New Territories Hakka:** genealogies kept under colonial rule, and lineage claims tied to land; check how proof is handled.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** descent stories may point to the heartland rather than the north; which origin counts as true there.
- **The She (畲族):** their own origin account and their place in the native-southern thesis; keep their voice separate.
- **Cantonese and Hokkien neighbors:** past published claims about the Hakka, and the replies they drew.
- **Overseas Hakka associations (comparison only):** the World Hakka Conference circuit spreads the northern-descent story; useful to see how a contested account is affirmed far from the evidence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the origins debate; mixed on everyday truth practice (oaths, divination, rumor); thin on surveys that separate Hakka views of science or news.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hakka varieties for oath formulas and legends; English and German for missionary ethnology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent claims touch identity and pride; genetic results can be misused; official media is the main news source and is curated; living lineages may resist critique of their genealogies.
- **Held for review:** none.
