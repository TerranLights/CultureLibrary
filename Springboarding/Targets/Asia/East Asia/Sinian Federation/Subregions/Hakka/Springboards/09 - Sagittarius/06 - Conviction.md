# Hakka: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hakka varieties (Jiaoling and Huiyang forms matter here); classical Chinese for gazetteer biographies; Cantonese for New Territories material. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou (Jiaoling, Meixian) exemplars vs. Huiyang exemplars:** two homelands of famous resisters; compare how each town builds memory.
- **Gannan and Minxi soviet counties:** conviction counted in martyr rolls; separate official praise from family memory.
- **Women and men:** chastity arches and widow biographies hold one model; women revolutionaries and church members hold others.
- **Hakka Christians:** conviction set against lineage duty; Protestant and Catholic cases may differ.
- **New Territories villagers:** stands taken through institutions under colonial and later rule.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** whether the heartland's hero cycles are told there, or local ones replace them.
- **Cantonese and Teochew neighbors:** shared Song-loyalist legends (the last Song court fled along this coast); check which parts are told as Hakka.
- **Taiwan's Hakka (comparison only):** Qiu Fengjia belongs to both shores; comparing the two memories shows how one man's stand is read by two societies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exemplars and gazetteer biographies; mixed on ordinary people's stands; thin and risky for present-day protest.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for biographies; Standard Chinese; Hakka for proverbs and oral memory; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution persecution, religious refusal and current protest are politically sensitive; anonymize living informants. Law on dissent belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
