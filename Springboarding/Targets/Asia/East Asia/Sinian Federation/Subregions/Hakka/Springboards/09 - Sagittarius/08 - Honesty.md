# Hakka: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties (market and kinship talk differs by county); Standard Chinese; Cantonese for Hong Kong; inscriptional classical Chinese for steles. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Market towns vs. upland villages:** fair dealing among strangers at the market against trust among kin in the village.
- **Meizhou, Minxi and Gannan:** each core's steles and proverbs may name different offenses.
- **Men and women:** check who ran household marketing and rotating credit in each area; women's own view of cheating may differ from lineage rules.
- **New Territories Hakka:** honesty judged under a different public-integrity regime; compare survey answers with the mainland.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** guild-hall merchants among non-Hakka neighbors; trust across group lines.
- **Cantonese and Teochew traders:** often the other party at market; their tales may cast the Hakka as cheats or as dupes, and vice versa.
- **Overseas diaspora (comparison only):** clan and rotating-credit networks abroad show how the same honesty mechanisms work far from village sanctions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich in folk tales and steles; patchy in surveys, which rarely separate Hakka respondents; present-day material is shaped by state campaigns.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties for proverbs and broadcasts; Standard and inscriptional classical Chinese; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and social-credit listings are politically and legally sensitive; law on fraud and credit goes to Libra or Capricorn. Avoid casting any group as dishonest.
- **Held for review:** none.
