# Hakka: Education (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for exam records and academy histories; Hakka varieties (school-campaign and family speech); Cantonese and English for Hong Kong schools. **Concept in one line:** formal and higher learning (schools, universities, degrees, scholarship and lifelong study) and the standing of the educated person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou vs. Gannan vs. Minxi:** exam lists and school-building histories may differ sharply; compare county by county, not by core alone.
- **Emigrant districts (Meixian, Dabu, Jiaoling) vs. inland counties:** overseas money may have reshaped schooling; check the timing.
- **Christian and non-Christian families:** mission schools offered a different curriculum and a different path to standing.
- **Boys and girls:** check whom lineage trusts paid for; girls' schooling has its own history and sources.
- **New Territories Hakka:** colonial English-language routes to standing, unlike the mainland.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** whether migrant communities kept heartland schooling customs (flagpoles, study trusts).
- **Cantonese and Hokkien neighbors:** shared exam culture; claims of Hakka distinctness need the same county-level data for neighbors.
- **Taiwan's Hakka (comparison only):** their own Hakka-medium schooling programs show one way language education can be organized, useful against the heartland's campaigns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam history and modern school histories; mixed on attainment, since statistics are by place not by group; thin on Hunan and Hainan Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (exam lists, academy records); Standard Chinese; Hakka for classroom campaigns; Cantonese and English for Hong Kong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hakka language teaching touches national language policy; school-level data on minors is restricted. Learning in early childhood belongs to Gemini.
- **Held for review:** none.
