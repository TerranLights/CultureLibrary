# Hakka: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; nineteenth-century literary Chinese for Taiping texts; Hakka varieties (Guangxi and Huadu speech for Taiping memory); English for mission records. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangxi Hakka (Guiping, Xiangzhou):** the Taiping heartland; descendants and neighbors may recall the movement as faith, rebellion or terror.
- **Huadu (Hong's home county) and Meizhou:** where the founder came from vs. where most Hakka live; whether the movement is claimed or kept at a distance.
- **Christian Hakka by church:** Basel-descended Lutherans, Catholics, and independent strict churches; literalism varies by tradition.
- **Folk-religion villages:** as targets of iconoclasm (Taiping, Nationalist, Maoist); their memory of one-truth campaigns.
- **Buddhist lay ritualists and monastic Buddhism:** a purity dispute inside one religion.
- **The She (畲族) and neighboring non-Han communities:** whose shrines were called idolatrous by more than one movement.
- **Cantonese (Punti) neighbors:** recorded the Taiping and the feuds from the other side; their accounts color the Hakka image.
- **Taiwan's Hakka (comparison only):** a setting with no official heterodoxy list, so strict Hakka-speaking congregations there may describe themselves in terms the heartland's records lack *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Taiping; mixed on twentieth-century campaigns; thin and guarded on present-day strict groups.
- **Languages needed:** nineteenth-century literary Chinese; Standard Chinese; Hakka for oral memory; English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "邪教" labels carry legal penalties; never identify members or leavers. Applying "fundamentalism" to the Taiping, to lineage rules or to state campaigns is contested; keep the label as a question. Law on banned groups goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
