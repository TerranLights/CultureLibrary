# Hakka: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka, standard and classical Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong). **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Jiaying counties against hill counties in Gannan and Minxi:** where exam claims are strongest, and where they are thinner, to test the reputation.
- **Lineages with and without degree holders:** whether merit stayed in a few branches.
- **Hong Kong NT Hakka:** colonial English schooling and civil-service exams as a different merit system.
- **Shenzhen and Huizhou urban Hakka:** private firms and connections against exams.
- **Sichuan and Guangxi Hakka:** whether exam-and-study ideals travelled with the settlers.
- **Women:** the late entry of daughters into schooling and its rewards.
- **Taiwan (comparison only):** flagpole stones at Qing-era Hakka halls in Taiwan, to compare how degrees were displayed outside the homeland.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hakka graduates in Malaysia competing under ethnic quotas, a sharp contrast between merit and assigned places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams and lineages; mixed for present-day hiring; local exam-success claims are often promotional and need checking against indexes.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for exam lists and inscriptions; Mandarin; Hakka for interviews; German and English for mission reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** connections and corruption in state hiring; officials discourage publicity for top gaokao scorers.
- **Held for review:** none.
