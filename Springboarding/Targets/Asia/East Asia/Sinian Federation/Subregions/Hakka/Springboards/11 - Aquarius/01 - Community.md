# Hakka: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話; Meixian, Changting, Ningdu, Huiyang varieties), written Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong villages), English, German (mission records). **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest or cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Fujian tulou villages (Yongding, and its border with 南靖 county):** one building can hold the whole community; compare with dispersed hamlets.
- **Meizhou enclosure-house villages:** the community as a lineage house with emigrant members abroad.
- **Gannan walled-house villages (Longnan, Dingnan):** fortified communities shaped by banditry and border feuds.
- **Shenzhen's Longgang and Huizhou suburbs:** urbanized Hakka villages where newcomers outnumber members.
- **Sichuan's Dongshan and Guangxi's Bobai:** communities that hold together as speech islands.
- **Hong Kong's New Territories:** emptied villages, Christian villages, and village revival.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese and Hokkien villages nearby, to see what is shared regional practice and what is Hakka; the She 畲族 in mixed villages.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka and diaspora towns, where Hakka "community" is also an organized minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for village and lineage ethnography and gazetteers; mixed for present-day neighborhood life; survey data rarely separates Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese; Meixian and other Hakka varieties for interviews and songs; Cantonese for Hong Kong; German and English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform and the collectives broke many communities; village demolition and relocation are touchy; lineage feuds; compacts and village rules touch law, which belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 南贛鄉約 in 王陽明全集 and note what duties it lays on neighbors in southern Jiangxi.
2. Read *Report from Xunwu* chapters on the county town, listing every association and temple named.
3. CNKI: 客家 AND (圍龍屋 OR 土樓) AND (社區 OR 公共空間), sorted by citation.
4. Search the Basel Mission archive catalog for Hakka-district congregations and photo series.
5. Search news archives for 荔枝窩 復村 and 深圳 客家 城中村 to trace present-day loss and revival.
