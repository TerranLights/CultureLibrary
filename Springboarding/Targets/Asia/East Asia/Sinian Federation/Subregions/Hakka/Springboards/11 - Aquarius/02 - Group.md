# Hakka: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話, several varieties), written Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong and Guangxi contact zones, English. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" and "them" divide, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taishan, Enping and Heshan Hakka enclaves:** heirs of the war zone, where the Hakka-Punti line was drawn hardest.
- **Meizhou core:** a Hakka majority, where the decisive group may be the lineage or the county, not "Hakka".
- **Western Fujian lineages (Changting, Shanghang, Liancheng):** single-surname villages and lineage rivalry among Hakka themselves.
- **Gannan:** groups shaped by the 1930s revolution and its local splits.
- **Guangxi and Sichuan:** Hakka as one migrant group among many; different out-groups.
- **Hong Kong's New Territories:** Hakka and Punti villages in alliances (約) and in rivalry.
- **The She 畲族:** a neighbor whose boundary with Hakka is official yet blurred in speech and custom.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka beside Hoklo majorities, and diaspora Hakka associations, where the group line follows speech again in a new setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineages, feuds and native-place halls; mixed for present-day group life; thin for any measure of Hakka conformity or loyalty.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese, classical for gazetteers and genealogies; Hakka varieties; Cantonese for Hong Kong and Taishan; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Punti-Hakka killings and their memory; ethnic-classification claims about She ancestry; lineage feuds still alive in some villages; collective punishment and lineage rules touch law (Libra, Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Liu Ping's account of the war beside the 赤溪縣志 for how each side named the other.
2. Search CNKI for 客家 AND (嘗會 OR 公嘗) to find studies of lineage trusts by county.
3. Pull a dozen Hakka 族譜 from FamilySearch across Meizhou, Tingzhou and Gannan and compare their membership and expulsion clauses.
4. Read Cohen (1968) and the She survey volumes together, to see two ways the boundary was drawn.
5. Search fan forums of 梅州客家 for 客家人 and 本地人 to see present-day us/them talk.
