# Hakka: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話), written Chinese (including Qing and Taiping documents), Cantonese for Hong Kong, English. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangxi's Guiping and Thistle Mountain area:** low-status Hakka migrants whose movement wrote the Taiping leveling program.
- **Gannan Soviet counties (Ruijin, Xingguo, Ningdu):** the land-redistribution experience and its memory.
- **Meizhou emigrant villages against villages without emigrants:** remittances as a new inequality inside the group.
- **Western Fujian:** tenancy and lineage land in tulou villages; who held the shares.
- **Sichuan and Hunan Hakka:** settlers who arrived later and may have held lower standing than natives.
- **Hong Kong's New Territories:** a live dispute over women's shares in village land.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese, Hokkien and Teochew women, many with bound feet in the past; She women; the comparison tests what was Hakka and what was class.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Meinong, where Cohen's study sets a baseline for family division, and diaspora Hakka, where emigrant wealth was made.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land, lineage and Taiping documents; mixed for gender; survey data on attitudes to equality cannot isolate Hakka.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern written Chinese; Hakka for songs and interviews; Cantonese; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform violence and class labels; the anti-localism purge; minor marriage and 等郎妹; ownership claims on Taiping and revolutionary history; legal rules on land and inheritance belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
