# Hakka: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話), written Chinese (late Qing and Republican texts included), English, German (Basel Mission). **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou:** the late Qing reformers, mission schools and emigrant funding of new schools.
- **Jiaoling and the Teochew border:** reform led by a returnee from Taiwan in a mixed Hakka and Teochew zone.
- **Gannan:** two reform programs from above in one decade, the Soviet's and the New Gannan's.
- **Guangxi's Taiping heartland:** reform proposals born inside a rebellion.
- **Shenzhen's Longgang and Bao'an:** villages remade by market reform, now inside a megacity.
- **Western Fujian:** tulou heritage listing and tourism as a reform of village life.
- **Sichuan's Dongshan:** cultural revival by a community long cut off from the heartland.
- **Hong Kong:** colonial reform imposed on villages, and village bodies resisting it.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese and Teochew reformers of the same era, to see which programs were Hakka-led and which shared.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka language movement, a reform from below; diaspora Hakka who funded schools at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late Qing reformers and Republican programs; mixed for present-day cultural policy; thin for how ordinary Hakka judged reforms, so oral history matters.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern written Chinese; Hakka (and the old Pha̍k-fa-sṳ romanization); English; German for Basel records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral reform and reburial; the Cultural Revolution's attacks on lineage halls and temples; the language policy debate; any reform law itself goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
