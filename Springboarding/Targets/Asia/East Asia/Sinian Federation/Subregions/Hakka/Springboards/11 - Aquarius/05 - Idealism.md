# Hakka: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話), written Chinese (including Taiping and Soviet-era texts), English (missionary and scholarly), German. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangxi's Guiping and Jintian:** where a Hakka-led utopia began; how local memory treats it now.
- **Huaxian and Guangzhou's northern edge:** the Taiping founder's home district, with its own claims and silences.
- **Gannan Soviet counties:** ideals of the 1930s and the memory of their cost.
- **Meizhou:** a city that brands itself the "world Hakka capital"; civic idealism and its critics.
- **Western Fujian's Ninghua and Changting:** an idealized place of origin turned into a pilgrimage site.
- **Christian Hakka villages (Meizhou, Wuhua, Hong Kong):** faith ideals beside lineage ideals.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** writers who carried an ideal of origin far from the heartland.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese and Hokkien reformers and revolutionaries, to check whether ideals said to be Hakka were in fact shared.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka songwriters of rural ideals, and diaspora Hakka who funded revolutions at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Taiping and the revolution; mixed for everyday ideals; thin for any measure of Hakka idealism as such.
- **Languages needed:** written Chinese, classical and modern; Hakka for songs; English and German for mission sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Taiping death toll and its memory; revolutionary history is officially framed; claims that great figures were Hakka are contested; religious practice may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Hamberg (1854), then Spence's chapters on the Guangxi years, noting what the vision promised.

4. Search for 興國山歌 紅色歌謠 anthologies and recordings.
5. Photograph or search survey reports of 堂號 and 楹聯 in two counties, one in Meizhou and one in Gannan.
