# Hakka: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka people and their communities, a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話; Meixian, Gannan, Tingzhou and Guangxi varieties), written Chinese, Cantonese (Hong Kong), Sichuanese Mandarin for the Chengdu area, English. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

English keywords: Hakka sworn brotherhood; same-year friendship south China; fictive kinship godparents Guangdong; Hakka hospitality tea; gift registers rural China; Chengdu teahouse friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan and western Fujian:** the areas where named same-year friendships may be strongest; test county by county.
- **Meizhou:** visiting and hospitality customs, and friendships kept with emigrants abroad.
- **Guangxi Hakka:** friendship pacts across the Hakka-Zhuang line.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** friendship in teahouses shared with Sichuanese speakers; is a separate Hakka circle kept?
- **Shenzhen and Huizhou:** native villagers and millions of migrant newcomers.
- **Hong Kong's New Territories:** village friendships split by emigration to Britain and elsewhere.
- **The She 畲族:** song-duet customs close to Hakka ones; test what is shared.
- **Neighbors:** Cantonese and Hokkien sworn brotherhoods, to see which forms are regional and which Hakka.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka, where hospitality foods were reworked for visitors, and diaspora Hakka associations, where fellow speakers become friends far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: ritual friendship and gift exchange are studied for south China in general, less for Hakka in particular; men's and women's friendship barely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties (friendship words differ by county); written Chinese; Cantonese; Sichuanese; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sworn brotherhoods shade into secret societies and gangs; 人情 favors shade into corruption; private gift registers need consent.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries

4. Search Hong Kong Memory for New Territories Hakka oral histories that mention childhood friends.
5. Search CFPS documentation for 人情 items and the sample counties in Guangdong, Fujian and Jiangxi.
