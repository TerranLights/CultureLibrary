# Hakka: Domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), a non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia; communities centered on the Guangdong-Fujian-Jiangxi hills. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (客家話 / 客家话; Meixian speech as the usual reference form, plus Tingzhou, Gannan, Huiyang and other varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong's New Territories. **Concept in one line:** how a culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with its magic ways (healing, protection, divination, hidden forces).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou (Meixian, Xingning, Dabu, Wuhua):** the best-documented core; set its funeral liturgy against the other areas.
- **Western Fujian (Longyan, Yongding, Changting, Ninghua):** tulou lineages and Tingzhou cults (定光古佛) that differ from Meizhou's.
- **Southern Jiangxi (Ganzhou counties):** fengshui-master villages and a Gan-language edge where Hakka and Gan practice blend.
- **Guangxi Hakka (Bobai, Luchuan, Hezhou):** later settlers among Zhuang and Cantonese neighbors.
- **Sichuan "Tuguangdong" Hakka (Luodai near Chengdu):** Qing migrants whose rites may have kept or shed the old forms.
- **Hunan and Hainan pockets:** small; check how much is distinct.
- **New Territories Hakka:** Christian and other villages side by side.
- **She people (畲族):** many speak Hakka-like speech; separate their own rites rather than fold them in.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka, whose 義民 cult turns the war dead into protectors, a test of whether mainland communities have a parallel; Southeast Asian Hakka for transplanted temples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Ritual ethnography is rich for Meizhou and western Fujian; official statistics do not separate the Hakka from other Han, so measures come from county data and Hong Kong.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (several varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese; German for Basel Mission files; classical Chinese for gazetteers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mediums and some rites are labeled "feudal superstition" (封建迷信) officially, so informants may be guarded; mental illness carries stigma.
- **Coverage:** the community list in the tracker header is a working list for the author to confirm, so the sub-unit's exact coverage is still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on the domain was set aside.
