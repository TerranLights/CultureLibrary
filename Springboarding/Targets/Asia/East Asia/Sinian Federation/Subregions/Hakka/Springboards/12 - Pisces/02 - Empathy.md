# Hakka: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian as reference; Tingzhou, Gannan, Huiyang, Guangxi and Sichuan varieties), Standard Chinese, Cantonese for Hong Kong. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Xingning:** the song centers; check whether answer-singing survives as a living skill or only on stage.
- **Gannan (Xingguo, Ruijin, Yudu):** its own song style and opera; Gan-speaking neighbors make a cross-dialect contrast.
- **Western Fujian (Changting, Liancheng, Shanghang):** puppetry and tulou living, where many families share one courtyard and must read each other daily.
- **Taishan, Enping and the old Hakka-Punti front:** the place to test empathy across a group line with a history of killing.
- **Guangxi and Sichuan Hakka:** minorities among Cantonese, Zhuang and Sichuanese speakers; how outsiders' feelings are read across languages.
- **New Territories Hakka:** shift to Cantonese; does the empathy vocabulary change with the language?
- **She people:** shared song forms; separate their own answer-song tradition.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka-language television drama, as a check on how a state-funded medium invites viewers into Hakka characters' feelings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to thin. Song and theater sources are rich; direct measures of empathy among Hakka speakers are likely few.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties (song lyrics are dense in local words), Standard Chinese, Cantonese; classical Chinese for Qing poetry.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Hakka-Punti wars still divide some lineages; handle descendants' memory with care.
- **Held for review:** none; no empathy pointer was set aside.
