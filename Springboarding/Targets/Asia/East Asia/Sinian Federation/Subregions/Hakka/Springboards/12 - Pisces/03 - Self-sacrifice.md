# Hakka: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (Meixian, Xingning, Tingzhou, Gannan and other varieties), Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese for the New Territories. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Dabu:** emigrant districts; sacrifice of wives and parents left behind.
- **Xingning and Wuhua:** later, internal labor migration to the Pearl River delta; a newer form of leaving to provide.
- **Gannan and western Fujian (Ruijin, Xingguo, Changting, Shanghang):** former Soviet counties, where state martyr talk and family memory may diverge.
- **Guangxi Hakka (Guiping, Bobai):** Taiping memory and later settlers.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai and other Chengdu-plain towns):** migrant-founder stories from the Qing resettlement.
- **Hunan and Hainan pockets:** test whether women's labor and vow customs differ.
- **New Territories Hakka:** women's wage labor in Hong Kong's twentieth-century economy.
- **She people:** separate their women's work and vow customs.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hakka cult of the 義民 (militia dead of the 1780s), whose yearly rites honor men who died defending villages; ask whether any mainland lineage keeps a like cult.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official honors and revolutionary history; mixed for women's own accounts; thin for measures.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka (songs, memoir, interviews), Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary martyrdom is politically controlled ground; chastity and widowhood are painful family topics; Taiping memory is contested.
- **Held for review:** none; no self-sacrifice pointer was set aside.
