# Hakka: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka (all varieties, including endangered outlier speech in Sichuan, Hunan, Guangxi and Hainan), Standard Chinese, Cantonese and Teochew for shift zones. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meizhou core:** the strongest hold on the language; the baseline for measuring shift elsewhere.
- **Chaoshan border (Fengshun, Jiexi, Raoping):** the "half-mountain Hakka" zone where identity and speech blur into Teochew.
- **Southern Jiangxi:** Hakka-Gan transition; check where speakers draw the line.
- **Western Fujian:** emptied tulou and lineage branches dispersed to cities.
- **Sichuan (Chengdu plain):** the furthest island; likely the most shift.
- **Guangxi (Hezhou, Bobai) and Hunan pockets:** multilingual zones where Hakka may be one of three or four tongues a person uses.
- **Hainan pocket:** check whether the community still speaks Hakka.
- **New Territories:** shift to Cantonese and English, and village depopulation.
- **She people:** assimilation in the other direction.
- **Comparison only:** Myron Cohen's study of household division in a Taiwan Hakka village, *House United, House Divided* (1976), a model for asking how mainland households come apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on language shift and origin debates; mixed on trance; thin on measured ego-loss experience.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka varieties, Standard Chinese, Cantonese, Teochew, Sichuanese for the island communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the origin debate touches pride and the She people's standing; mediums and trance draw official suspicion; dementia carries family shame.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on dissolution was set aside.
