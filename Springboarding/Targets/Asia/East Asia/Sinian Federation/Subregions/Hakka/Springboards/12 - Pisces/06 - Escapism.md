# Hakka: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties (Meixian, Huiyang, Tingzhou, Gannan, outlier speech in Guangxi and Sichuan), Standard Chinese, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Meixian and Meizhou city:** football, tea resorts and urban leisure.
- **Xingning, Wuhua and Gannan sender counties:** migrant parents and left-behind children; escape both by leaving and by screens.
- **Yongding and Nanjing county:** tulou tourism, where the home became others' getaway.
- **Huiyang, Boluo and the Huizhou Hakka:** urbanizing fast near Shenzhen; Luofu Mountain's retreat lore.
- **Guangxi Hakka (Bobai, Luchuan):** check drinking and gambling customs among Cantonese and Zhuang neighbors.
- **Sichuan Hakka (Luodai):** an old town sold as weekend escape for Chengdu.
- **Hunan and Hainan pockets:** check whether any distinct customs survive.
- **New Territories Hakka:** legal betting, emigration and city leisure.
- **She people:** their own drink and festival customs, kept separate.
- **Comparison only:** Southeast Asian Hakka mining towns of the 1800s, where opium and gambling were part of the labor system; a contrast for what "escape" meant to men who left the hills.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Opium history and left-behind-children studies are rich; Hakka-specific drinking or gambling data are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, Cantonese; English for diaspora comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use, gambling and the underground lottery are crimes in the mainland (crime itself is concept 10); left-behind children are a delicate family topic.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on escapism was set aside.
