# Hakka: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese (geomantic and almanac texts), Cantonese (New Territories). **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan (Xingguo, Ningdu, Yudu):** the home ground of landform geomancy; professional eyes for land.
- **Western Fujian (Sibao, Changting, Liancheng):** printed aids to divination, and their readers.
- **Meizhou:** date-selection shops, temple lots and an urban clientele.
- **Huiyang and Heyuan:** villages pulled toward Shenzhen's economy; check whether almanac use holds.
- **Guangxi and Sichuan Hakka:** settler farmers in new land; which weather and land lore traveled with them.
- **Hunan and Hainan pockets:** check for distinct omens or healers.
- **New Territories Hakka:** fengshui claims as a public matter.
- **She people:** their own healers and hunting lore, separate from Hakka.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan Hakka farming almanacs and Hakka-language weather sayings, which have been published in Taiwan and could show which mainland sayings survived a crossing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on geomancy and printed almanacs; mixed on healers; thin on measured intuition among Hakka speakers.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka for omen and weather sayings; Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for geomantic classics and almanacs; Cantonese for New Territories fengshui cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** geomancy and divination are officially "superstition" in some settings; practitioners may guard methods; all such material is a record of practice, never evidence.
- **Held for review:** none; no intuition pointer was set aside.
