# Hakka: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** the Hakka (客家), non-territorial sub-unit of the Sinian Federation, region Asia / East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Hakka varieties, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese (Ming-Qing official writing), Cantonese (New Territories). **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched only under Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes the idea of the wrong and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gannan:** the "bandit country" of Ming writing; compare the record with local memory.
- **Western Fujian (Tingzhou, Longyan):** salt routes and tulou built against raiders.
- **Meizhou (Xingning, Wuhua):** lineage feuds and emigrant money; whose wrongs drew shame.
- **Old Punti front (Taishan, Chixi):** violence each side still names differently.
- **Guangxi Hakka:** Taiping-era rebels called bandits by the state; check how descendants speak.
- **Sichuan Hakka:** settler communities and the 哥老會 brotherhoods around them; *check* any Hakka role.
- **Hunan and Hainan pockets:** check for distinct views.
- **New Territories Hakka:** smuggling across the border and triad presence.
- **She people:** labeled "畲賊" in Ming texts; their own account of that name.
- **Comparison only:** the Larut Wars in 1860s-70s Malaya, where a largely Hakka society fought a rival group; colonial records cast both as criminal gangs, a contrast for how Hakka violence abroad was named.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing and Ming official views; mixed for village and present-day views; thin for measured victimization outside Hong Kong.
- **Languages needed:** Hakka, Standard Chinese, classical Chinese, Cantonese, English for colonial records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state violence of the 1950s-70s, current fraud stigma and trafficking are restricted or painful ground; "bandit" labels on the She and hill peoples are slurs from outside.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on crime was set aside.
