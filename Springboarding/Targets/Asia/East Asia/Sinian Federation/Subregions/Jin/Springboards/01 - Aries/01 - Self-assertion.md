## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan / Hedong** (Linfen, Yuncheng): Fenhe Mandarin speech; check whether refusal and request forms differ from Jin-speaking areas.
- **Taiyuan and the Jinzhong basin**: merchant-town etiquette of bargaining and customer talk, against city service culture now.
- **Datong and Jinbei**: frontier and mining towns; compare workplace talk to superiors in state coal enterprises.
- **Shangdang** (Changzhi, Jincheng): base-area speaking norms (meetings, "speak bitterness") left in local memory.
- **Lüliang and the Yellow River counties**: song as a sanctioned outlet when speech is not.
- **Hui communities**: claims made as a minority (halal food, mosque affairs) to neighbors and offices.
- **Catholic villages**: whether a parish gives laypeople a voice that village politics does not.
- **Migrant miners** from other provinces: claims made as outsiders.
- Women against men, juniors against elders, in-laws, and urban youth against village parents.
