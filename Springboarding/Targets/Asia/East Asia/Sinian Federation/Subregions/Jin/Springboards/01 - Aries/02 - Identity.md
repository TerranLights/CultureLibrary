## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan / Hedong**: Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers who may tie identity more to Yao, Shun and Guan Yu than to "Jin speech".
- **Jinzhong basin**: merchant-heritage towns; test whether 晋商 identity is felt outside them.
- **Jinbei frontier** (Datong, Shuozhou, Youyu): wall, pass and coal; Northern Wei and Mongol-contact history.
- **Shangdang**: an "old revolutionary base" 老区 identity in Changzhi and Jincheng.
- **Lüliang and Hequ**: identity shaped by poverty and migration north.
- **Hui Muslims**: religion and *minzu* as identity markers.
- **Catholic villages**: faith communities with long lineages of their own.
- **Wutai Shan**: monastics, Han and Tibetan Buddhist, for whom the mountain is the identity.
- **Migrant workers and their children** in coal towns; returnees from Inner Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage, archaeology, merchants and dialect; mixed for minorities; thin for surveys of regional identity.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Jin and Fenhe dialects; Classical Chinese; Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification; registered against unregistered Catholics; outside nicknames used as slurs; contested heritage claims between provinces.
- **Held for review:** none.
