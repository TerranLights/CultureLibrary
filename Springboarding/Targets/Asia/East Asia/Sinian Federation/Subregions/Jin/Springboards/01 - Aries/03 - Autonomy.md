## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: the old firm and family hierarchy and how much of it survives in family businesses.
- **Lüliang and Jinbei villages**: high bride prices and migrant work, which change who decides.
- **Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: a farming south with its own Mandarin; compare household division and marriage choice.
- **Shangdang**: villages under strong collective-era organization; what self-rule means to their people now.
- **Coal-company towns**: work hours and housing set by the enterprise.
- **Hui families**: marriage within the faith and the mosque community's say.
- **Catholic parishes**: church guidance on marriage and children against state rules.
- **Wutai Shan monastics**: autonomy chosen through renunciation.
- **Women and men, elders and the young**, rural and Taiyuan urban households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in national surveys with Shanxi samples and in political history; thin in direct ethnography of choice in families.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; dialect for village interviews; Classical Chinese for Jie Zitui sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-policy enforcement and its harms; village elections and Party control; religious life outside registration; leave the legal side to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
