## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns** (Pingyao, Qi, Taigu, Jiexiu): business founding as family tradition; compare old firms with today's small businesses.
- **Hedong (Yuncheng, Linfen)**: farming and fruit growers' cooperatives; legends of founders.
- **Xiyang and the eastern hills**: the Dazhai legacy of collective initiative.
- **Coal regions** (Datong, Shuozhou, Lüliang, Jincheng): private mine starts, then consolidation.
- **Shangdang**: mutual-aid traditions from the base areas.
- **Taiyuan**: urban startups and state-firm workplaces.
- **Hui traders and restaurant owners**: small businesses in a minority network.
- **Catholic parishes**: church-run charity and who starts it.
- **Returnees from Inner Mongolia, Beijing and elsewhere**: initiative carried home.
