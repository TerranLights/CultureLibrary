## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: the founding-legend south; origins weigh heavily in local self-telling.
- **Jinzhong and Taiyuan**: New Year fire customs and the Tang founding at the capital.
- **Jinbei (Datong, Shuozhou)**: frontier towns refounded many times; coal-city restarts.
- **Lüliang and Jinxi**: cave-dwelling rites and relocated villages.
- **Shangdang**: mountain counties with their own New Year and temple-fair calendar.
- **Hui families**: Islamic New Year and birth rites (naming by an imam) against Han practice.
- **Catholic villages**: baptism as a person's start; the Christian year against the lunar one.
- **Wutai Shan**: ordination as a second birth.
- **Migrants and returnees**: starting over away and coming back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals, legends and archaeology; mixed for rites of passage today; thin for personal fresh starts outside fiction and film.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; dialect for rite names; Classical Chinese for histories and steles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced or pressured relocation; claims that pit counties against each other over the same legend.
- **Held for review:** none.
