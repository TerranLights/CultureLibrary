## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei frontier** (Daixian, Shuozhou, Yanmen): the Yang family legend and old garrison towns.
- **Hedong (Yuncheng)**: Guan Yu's home district; righteousness rather than battle.
- **Lüliang and Wenshui**: militia and martyr memory.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Wuxiang)**: Eighth Route Army memory.
- **Jinzhong basin**: martial arts and escort agencies; merchant-town courage.
- **Coal towns**: miners' daily danger and rescue teams.
- **Catholic communities**: martyr memory from 1900.
- **Hui communities**: their own accounts of wartime and community defense; *check* what exists.
- **Women**: endurance and wartime roles against men's battle stories; young and old.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in war memory, opera and official awards; mixed in miners' accounts; thin in surveys of courage.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; dialect for oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nationalist against Communist war memory; whistleblowing and forced-labor cases; 1900 killings; mining deaths.
- **Held for review:** none.
