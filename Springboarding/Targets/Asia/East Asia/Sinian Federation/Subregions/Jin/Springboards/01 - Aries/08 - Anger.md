**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin 晋语 dialects (emotion words differ sharply from Putonghua); Fenhe 汾河 Zhongyuan Mandarin; Classical Chinese and medical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei villages** (around Datong): the world of Cao Naiqian's stories; harsh-climate poverty.
- **Shangdang** (Lucheng, Changzhi): land-reform memory of organized anger.
- **Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: opera of grievance; temple retribution.
- **Taiyuan**: urban workplace and traffic anger; state-firm work units.
- **Coal towns**: drink, danger and fights; anger at owners after accidents.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: business restraint as a heritage ideal.
- **Hui communities**: religious teaching on controlling anger; disputes with neighbors over food and space, *check* with care.
- **Catholic parishes and Wutai Shan**: two religious languages of anger.
- **Women and men**; children and elders; in-laws.
