## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: business rivalry with a code of trust; Pingyao, Qi and Taigu against each other.
- **Hedong and the Fen valley**: water-sharing villages; drumming contests.
- **Xinzhou and Jinbei**: wrestling, frontier games and coal wealth.
- **Taiyuan**: gaokao pressure and urban job markets.
- **Shangdang**: opera rivalry; mountain villages with fewer school places.
- **Coal towns**: display contests among new rich families; miners' team quotas.
- **Hui communities**: business competition in food and trade; how the mosque frames envy, *check*.
- **Catholic villages**: church festivals and rivalry between parishes, *check*.
- **Boys and girls**: school and sport competition by gender.
