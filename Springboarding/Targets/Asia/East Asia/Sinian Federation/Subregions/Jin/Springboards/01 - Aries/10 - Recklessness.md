## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal regions** (Datong, Lüliang, Linfen, Jincheng): risk for money underground and on the road.
- **Fenyang and Xinghuacun**: liquor-making towns where drink is heritage.
- **Lüliang towns**: private lending booms and busts.
- **Jinxi Yellow River counties** (Ji county, Hequ): river crossings, falls and boatmen's risk.
- **Taiyuan**: urban youth, cars and nightlife.
- **Taihang counties** (Changzhi, Jincheng): cliff roads and hikers.
- **Hui communities**: abstinence from alcohol against the banquet norm; *check* how this shapes social risk.
- **Catholic parishes and Wutai Shan**: religious teaching against gambling and drink.
- **Young men** against women and elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in accident and disaster records; mixed in health surveillance; thin in direct studies of thrill-seeking.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for poems and famine texts; dialect for slang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine deaths and cover-ups; illegal lending; gambling arrests; disputed heritage claims (the Xinghuacun site is claimed by more than one place).
- **Held for review:** none.
