# Jin 晋: Possessions and value, domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; the Jin 晋语 dialect group (Bingzhou 并州, Lüliang 吕梁, Shangdang 上党, Wutai 五台, Da-Bao 大包 subgroups); Fenhe 汾河 Zhongyuan Mandarin in the south; Classical Chinese; Japanese; Hui religious vocabulary; some Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held: possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth, and the food, dress and wealth ways around them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei 晋北 (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou):** Great Wall frontier, oats, coal, the Zouxikou 走西口 out-migration.
- **Jinzhong basin (Taiyuan, Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** old merchant and banking towns; compounds as stored wealth.
- **Lüliang and Jinxi (Lin county, Qikou 碛口):** river trade, cave dwellings, poverty programs.
- **Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng):** Taihang uplands, iron and anthracite, fortified gentry villages.
- **Jinnan and Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng):** wheat, cotton, salt, a different dialect family.
- **State-enterprise workers in Taiyuan and the coal cities** against farm households.
- **Hui Muslims; Manchu descendants of the Taiyuan garrison; small northern Mongol communities *(check)*.**
- **Catholic and Protestant villages; the monastic estates of Wutai Shan 五台山 (Han and Tibetan Buddhist).**
- *Comparison only:* Shanxi guild halls and Zouxikou descendants elsewhere; the Sinian Federation as a whole, briefly.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history, coal and food; mixed for household belongings and money talk, found mostly in surveys and media.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin and Fenhe dialect dictionaries; Japanese; some Mongolian and Hui vocabulary.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands (Shanxi, as listed) are a working list the author still confirms in Stage 0.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine accidents, land seizures and corruption cases are sensitive and partly censored; regional nicknames about Shanxi people and money are stereotypes to test, never to adopt.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Shanxi part of 《中国地方志民俗资料汇编·华北卷》 and tabulate food, dress and market-day entries by county.
2. Run CFPS or CHFS asset and consumption tables with the Shanxi province code, urban and rural apart.
3. Search 《山西统计年鉴》 for 居民人均消费支出 by category, 2000-2020.
4. Read the Wei feng and Tang feng with a standard commentary; list every good named.
5. Search CNKI 中国知网 for 山西 民间饮食 and 山西 服饰 民俗.
