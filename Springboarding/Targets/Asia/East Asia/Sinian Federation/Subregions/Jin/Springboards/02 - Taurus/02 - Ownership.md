## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shangdang and the Taihang base areas (Changzhi, Lucheng, Pingshun):** early land reform and model collectives; the deepest written record of revolutionary tenure.
- **Lüliang and the Jin-Sui area (Xing county, Lin county):** a harsher land-reform history; separate it from Shangdang.
- **Jinzhong merchant families:** owners of compounds and firms who lost them; their descendants' claims.
- **Jinbei and Lüliang coal counties:** private mine owners, village collectives and state groups as rival owners.
- **Jinnan wheat plains (Linfen, Yuncheng):** larger farm plots and land transfers to agribusiness.
- **Taiyuan and coal-city work-unit households:** flats bought from employers in the 1990s housing reform.
- **Hui communities:** waqf and community property.
- **Buddhist monasteries of Wutai Shan and Catholic dioceses:** religious land and its return or loss.
- **Villages relocated under poverty programs:** house plots exchanged for flats.

## 4. Flags
