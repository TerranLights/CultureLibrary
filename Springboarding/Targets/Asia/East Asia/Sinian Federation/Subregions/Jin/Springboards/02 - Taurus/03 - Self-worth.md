## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Miners and steelworkers in Datong, Yangquan and Taiyuan:** worth tied to a hard, paid job; compare before and after closures.
- **Rural women in the Taihang and Lüliang uplands:** equal-pay history and new care work as two tests of worth earned by labor.
- **Children left behind in Jinbei and Lüliang villages:** self-esteem studies exist for them.
- **Merchant-descendant families in Jinzhong:** inherited honor carried by houses and plaques.
- **Zouxikou households in Hequ and Baode:** worth proved by returning with money, or lost by not returning.
- **Jinnan opera-going villages:** face and rank as staged and discussed.
- **Hui residents:** standing as a religious minority in Han-majority towns.
- **Wutai Shan monastics:** humility as a taught ideal, beside the lay donors who seek merit.
- **Dialect speakers in Putonghua settings:** whether a Shanxi accent is heard as a mark of lesser worth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich in classical and opera material, growing in psychology studies; thin on adult self-worth outside surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; opera libretto language; dialect for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, mine-town violence and humiliation by officials are sensitive; stereotypes of the "coal boss" are media images to test, not findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
