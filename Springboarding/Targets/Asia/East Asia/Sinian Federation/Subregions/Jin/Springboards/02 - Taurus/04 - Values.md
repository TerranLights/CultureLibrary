*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** a written commercial code (firm rules, Guan Yu worship) to compare with farm villages.
- **Jinnan and Hedong (Yuncheng, Linfen):** the Sima Guang, Pei and Guan Yu legacies; a different dialect and older literati record.
- **Shangdang and Taihang villages:** wartime and collective-era teaching layered over older village compacts.
- **Lüliang and Jinbei:** frontier and migrant households; values of those who left and those who stayed.
- **Hui communities:** Islamic teaching inside a Han majority.
- **Catholic villages around Taiyuan and in the south:** a centuries-old Christian code beside Confucian family rules.
- **Daoist and Buddhist clergy and lay devotees (Yongle Palace, Wutai Shan):** renunciation as a ranked good.
- **Young urban Taiyuan against rural elders:** generational ranking of family, money and freedom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classical, inscription and merchant sources; mixed for present-day survey data at provincial level.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for inscriptions and family instructions; Standard Chinese; dialect for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official "core socialist values" campaigns shape public answers; religion is a regulated topic; online "what Shanxi people are like" threads are stereotype material, to be read as opinion only. Law questions go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read 《训俭示康》 in full and list the goods it ranks above and below each other.
2. Search the Xia, Wenxi and Pingyao volumes of 《三晋石刻大全》 for 乡约 and 禁约 (prohibition steles).
3. Ask the China Piaohao Museum for a transcript of the Rishengchang 号规.
4. Read Gillin's chapters on Yan's moral campaigns; then search 洗心社 讲词 for the texts.
5. Pull WVS China child-qualities items and check whether a Shanxi code exists.
