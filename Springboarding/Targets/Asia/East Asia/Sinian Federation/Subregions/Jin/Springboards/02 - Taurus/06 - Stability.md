# Jin 晋: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Jin 晋语 and Fenhe 汾河 dialects; Errentai 二人台 and opera language for stage sources; Hui community vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan villages around Hongtong and Linfen:** strongest root-memory sites and oldest stages.
- **Jinzhong walled towns (Pingyao, Qixian):** preserved fabric with changing residents.
- **Lüliang cave-dwelling villages:** empty villages beside relocated new towns.
- **Jinbei coal and frontier cities (Datong, Shuozhou):** rebuilding and resource cycles.
- **Coal towns at the end of their seams (Xiaoyi, Huozhou):** single-industry stability ended.
- **Work-unit families in Taiyuan and Changzhi steel and mining plants:** stability by employer.
- **Hui congregations in old mosque quarters (Changzhi, Datong):** continuity organized around a mosque.
- **Zouxikou border counties:** a long tradition of seasonal and permanent leaving.
- *Comparison only:* families elsewhere who trace roots to the Hongtong tree.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on migration memory, heritage and resource cycles; mixed on everyday routine, which needs fieldwork and time-use data.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; dialect and folk-opera language.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 维稳 (stability maintenance) is a policing term and is out of scope here; demolitions and forced relocation are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
