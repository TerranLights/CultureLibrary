# Jin 晋: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Jin 晋语 and Fenhe 汾河 dialects; Mongolian and Russian for the steppe and tea-trade records; English and Japanese for scholarship. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties (Qixian, Taigu, Pingyao, Yuci):** old commercial wealth and its memory industry.
- **Hedong salt and southern wheat lands (Yuncheng, Linfen):** wealth from a state-regulated resource and from farming.
- **Coal prefectures (Datong, Shuozhou, Lüliang, Jincheng, Yangquan):** sudden new fortunes beside miners' wages.
- **Taihang and Lüliang poverty counties:** the low end of wealth and the programs aimed at it.
- **Taiyuan's urban middle class:** wealth in flats and salaries.
- **Zouxikou border counties:** wealth earned beyond the Wall and sent home.
- **Hui traders and restaurateurs:** their own trade networks.
- **Buddhist and Daoist temples:** donation economies (Wutai Shan, Yongle Palace).
- *Comparison only:* Shanxi firms' branches across China and in Mongolia and Russia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and coal; mixed for present wealth, since asset data are rarely broken down below province.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Mongolian and Russian for the steppe trade; Japanese for older merchant studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** coal-era corruption, officials' family wealth, and stereotypes about rich Shanxi people; treat media images as views to test. Tax and inheritance law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
