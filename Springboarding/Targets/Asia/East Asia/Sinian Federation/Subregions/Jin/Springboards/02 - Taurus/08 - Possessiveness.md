## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan irrigation villages (Hongtong, Zhaocheng, Jiexiu):** the densest record of guarding water shares.
- **Jinzhong merchant households:** multiple wives, heirs and the house as a closed world.
- **Taihang villages (Zuoquan, Wuxiang):** the source of the free-marriage stories and the blossom tunes.
- **Jinbei and Lüliang poor villages:** exchange marriage and bride scarcity as a cause of guarding.
- **Coal counties:** village and firm turf over mineral land.
- **Urban Taiyuan couples:** new forms (phone checks, location sharing) and the language of control.
- **Hui families:** marriage within the community as the community explains it.
- **Monastic communities at Wutai Shan:** a teaching against clinging, for contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on water disputes and literature; partial on present couple dynamics, which depend on national surveys with provincial reports.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; dialect lyrics for folk songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and trafficked brides are sensitive and under-reported; "stingy Shanxi people" jokes are a stereotype to study, not a pointer; disputes that reach courts go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
