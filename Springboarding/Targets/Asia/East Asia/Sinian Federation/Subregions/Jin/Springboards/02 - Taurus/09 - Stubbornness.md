## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taihang uplands (Changzhi, Jincheng, Zuoquan):** Yu Gong and wartime holdout stories; a mountain frame.
- **Lüliang villages:** guerrilla legends and the 1940s land conflicts.
- **Jinnan and Hedong:** the literati record of unbending officials (Sima Guang) and old site claims.
- **Jinbei sandy counties (Youyu, Pianguan):** persistence against the land itself.
- **Jinzhong old towns:** preservationists against demolition, and residents caught between.
- **Coal-town workers:** firmness in wage and safety disputes (cases need careful sourcing).
- **Hui and Catholic communities:** holding to faith through hard decades, as they tell it.
- **Generations in Taiyuan:** elders "set in their ways" against the young, in family sketches online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classical, opera and state-hero material; thin on everyday stubbornness outside family comedy and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; dialect for sketches and oral stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning and protest standoffs are sensitive; official hero narratives are campaigns to be read as such; "stubborn Shanxi" jokes are a stereotype to test.
- **Held for review:** none.
