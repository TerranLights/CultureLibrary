# Jin 晋: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialect food and craft names; Fenhe 汾河 Zhongyuan Mandarin; Classical Chinese and Yuan drama language; Hui dietary vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei (Datong, Xinzhou):** oats, potatoes and mutton; a cold-country table and the hot springs.
- **Jinzhong (Taiyuan, Pingyao, Qingxu):** vinegar, Pingyao beef, banquet foods of the merchant towns.
- **Jinnan and Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng, Wenxi):** wheat breads, dough flowers, drums; a different dialect for the same foods.
- **Lüliang (Fenyang, Lin county):** liquor country and cave-dwelling comfort.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** upland foods and the Shangdang opera's sound.
- **Hui communities:** halal beef and mutton dishes and the rules around them.
- **Buddhist and Daoist clergy:** vegetarian temple food and limits on pleasure.
- **Urban young people in Taiyuan:** milk tea, delivery apps and food video against family cooking.
