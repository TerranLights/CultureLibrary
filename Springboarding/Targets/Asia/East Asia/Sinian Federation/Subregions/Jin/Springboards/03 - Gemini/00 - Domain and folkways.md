# Jin: Domain and folkways (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation (the lands of pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua) in simplified characters; the Jin 晋语 dialect group (Bingzhou 并州片, Lüliang 吕梁片, Shangdang 上党片, Wutai 五台片 and Da-Bao 大包片 subgroups); the Fenhe 汾河片 subgroup of Zhongyuan Mandarin 中原官话 in the south; small Hui, Manchu and Mongol communities; Chinese Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech ways, learning ways, siblings and neighbors, short journeys).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North 晋北 (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou):** Da-Bao and Wutai speech; old frontier; the "going out west" 走西口 migration.
- **Taiyuan basin and Jinzhong 晋中 (Taiyuan, Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** Bingzhou speech; old merchant towns; the capital's mixed migrant population.
- **West, Lüliang 吕梁 hills and the Yellow River:** Lüliang speech; remote hill counties.
- **Southeast, Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng):** its own Jin subgroup, ritual and storytelling traditions, coal towns.
- **South 晋南 (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin rather than Jin; a separate opera and folk repertory.
- **Mining and industrial towns (Yangquan, Datong, Jincheng):** in-migrants from many provinces; work-unit neighborhoods.
- **Hui communities** (Taiyuan, Datong, Changzhi), **descendants of Taiyuan's Manchu banner garrison**, **Mongols** near the northern border, and **Wutai Shan's Han and Tibetan Buddhist monastic communities:** each with its own speech settings.
- **Catholic villages** (for example around Taiyuan and Changzhi) and **Deaf signers:** separate networks of talk and learning.
- The Sinian Federation as a whole: a comparison frame only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dialectology and folk literature; mixed for everyday neighbor and sibling life; thin in English beyond a handful of village studies.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese reading; classical Chinese for pre-modern sources; ear training in Jin subgroups for recordings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective-era archives may need permission; religious minorities' records (Catholic, Hui, Tibetan Buddhist) to be approached through the communities' own institutions. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author in Stage 0; the sheets are written for Shanxi as the core.
- **Held for review:** none.
