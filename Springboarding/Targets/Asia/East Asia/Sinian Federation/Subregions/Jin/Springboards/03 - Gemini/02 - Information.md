# Jin: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for gazetteers and steles; Jin and southern Mandarin speech for oral news terms; Tibetan and Mongolian for Wutai Shan's printed holdings. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal districts (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi, Jincheng, Lüliang mines):** accident and pollution news, where official and local accounts may differ.
- **Remote Lüliang and Taihang hill villages:** the gap in broadband and newspapers; reliance on loudspeakers, markets and returning workers.
- **Taiyuan's university and office population:** heavy online news use; compare with older rural readers.
- **Southern counties around Linfen and Yuncheng:** the old printing region, and a separate network of market towns.
- **Catholic villages:** parish channels of news.
- **Hui communities:** mosque announcements and community networks.
- **Wutai Shan's Tibetan and Mongol monks and pilgrims:** information in other languages and scripts.
- **Returned migrant workers and families of the "going out west" migrants:** news carried home by people, not media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official press and gazetteers; mixed for rumor and word of mouth; thin and sensitive for concealed accidents and disasters.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tibetan and Mongolian for monastery holdings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship and press control shape every modern source; disaster and accident records may be closed; personal dossiers 档案 are not open.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Edgerton-Tarpley's *Tears from Iron* for its sources on how famine news traveled, and list them.
2. Search 晋绥日报 影印 and 抗战日报 兴县 for reprints and digitized runs.
3. Search the Shanxi Library catalog for 山西村政汇编 and 地方文献 newspapers.
4. Search CNKI for 山西 煤矿 瞒报 and 黑砖窑 网络 舆论 for studies of hidden and surfaced news.
5. Download the CGSS documentation and check the Shanxi sample size and media items.
