## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Merchant towns of Jinzhong (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Jiexiu):** the shop-apprentice route beside the classical one.
- **Southern counties (Yuncheng, Linfen):** old scholar lineages and family instructions.
- **Lüliang and Taihang hill counties:** consolidated boarding schools and long journeys to school.
- **Coal towns:** mine technical schools and work-unit schooling.
- **Taiyuan:** the university city.
- **Hui families:** mosque schooling beside state school.
- **Catholic villages:** parish teaching and catechism.
- **Wutai Shan's monks and nuns:** monastic study.
- **Deaf and disabled children:** special schools.
- **Children of migrant workers, left behind or brought to cities:** learning without parents at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history of schools and classical texts; mixed for family and apprenticeship learning; good modern data for enrollment but little on learning at home.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin vocabulary for crafts; the Hui religious register.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** campaign-era materials (Dazhai) carry political framing; religious schooling and minors are sensitive topics.
- **Held for review:** none.
