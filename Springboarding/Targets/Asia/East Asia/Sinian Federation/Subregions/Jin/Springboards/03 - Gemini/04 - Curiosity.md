# Jin: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Jin and southern Mandarin words for prying and wondering. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiyuan and the university districts:** museums, science center, research institutes; compare with towns that have none.
- **Wutai Shan, and the temple counties of Jinzhong and the south (Pingyao, Hongtong, Gaoping, Jincheng):** hosts of outside curiosity; how locals see the visitors.
- **Xiangfen, Ruicheng and other southern archaeology counties:** origins as local interest.
- **Lüliang and Luya hills:** nature-reserve areas; the explorer's and birder's Shanxi.
- **Yangquan and other coal towns:** industrial science, and a science-fiction author's home ground.
- **Datong:** grottoes and frontier sites as a second tourist magnet.
- **Older villagers and young urban visitors:** the "why" of the young set against village norms on questions.
- **Hui and Catholic communities:** whether outsiders' questions about faith are welcome; check, do not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage, archaeology and tourism; mixed for popular science; thin for everyday norms of asking and prying.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; English and German for outsiders' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the launch center and coal research are security matters; asking about income, family planning or politics may be unwelcome in fieldwork.
- **Held for review:** none.
