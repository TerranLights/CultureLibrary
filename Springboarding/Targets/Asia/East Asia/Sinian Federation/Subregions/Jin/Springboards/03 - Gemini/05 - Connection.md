# Jin: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for clan, guild and stele texts; Jin and southern Mandarin kin and gift terms; the Hui community's own terms for mosque networks. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hequ, Baode, Pianguan and the other northwest Yellow River counties:** heartland of "going out west"; families split across the border with Inner Mongolia.
- **Hongtong and the Linfen basin:** keepers of the ancestral-root story that ties outsiders to them.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** native-place business networks.
- **Yuncheng (Guan Yu's home):** sworn-brotherhood cult; southern Mandarin speech area.
- **Coal-mining districts:** work-unit ties and their unraveling.
- **Taiyuan:** the city of in-migrants from every county, where 老乡 ties may matter in new ways.
- **Hollowed-out hill villages of Lüliang and Taihang:** empty-nest elders; who stays connected and how.
- **Hui families:** ties across provinces.
- **Catholic villages:** ties through parish and religious kin.
- **Diaspora** (Shanxi merchant guild halls elsewhere; Inner Mongolian descendants of migrants): comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration legends, merchant networks and guild halls; mixed for present-day gift exchange and loneliness; good quantitative data on elders.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin terms for gift and kin customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "back-door" ties border on corruption and on law (point to Libra and Capricorn); gift registers are private family papers.
- **Held for review:** none.
