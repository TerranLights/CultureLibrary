# Jin: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Jin dialect forms (for two-way word formation); Tibetan for Wutai Shan's Tibetan Buddhist side; the Hui register. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jin-speaking north and center against the Mandarin-speaking south:** two speech worlds in one state; check whether people see themselves as one or two.
- **Taiyuan's young professionals:** switching between Standard Chinese and home speech.
- **Village elders who speak only local varieties:** the other end of the bidialectal scale.
- **Wutai Shan's Han and Tibetan Buddhist communities:** two traditions on one mountain.
- **Hui families:** two belongings in daily life.
- **Catholic villages:** two religious frames, with a long local history.
- **Old merchant families of Jinzhong:** scholar and trader roles.
- **Border counties near Inner Mongolia:** families with members on both sides.
- **Children raised by grandparents while parents work away:** two homes, two sets of rules.
