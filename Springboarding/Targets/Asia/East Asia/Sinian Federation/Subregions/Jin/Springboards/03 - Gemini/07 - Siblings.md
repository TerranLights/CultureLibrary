# Jin: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for genealogies and early texts; Jin and southern Mandarin kin terms (which differ county to county). **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yicheng county against neighboring one-child counties:** the clearest internal contrast in sibling numbers.
- **Rural Lüliang and Taihang hill villages:** larger families longer; older siblings as caregivers when parents migrate.
- **Taiyuan and other cities:** the only-child generation of work-unit families.
- **Jinzhong merchant lineages:** brothers as business partners.
- **Southern counties with strong lineages (Yuncheng, Linfen):** genealogies and generation names.
- **Coal-town families:** brothers in the same mine; deaths in accidents and the care of a lost brother's family.
- **Hui families:** different family-size policies applied in some periods *check*; sibling obligations framed by religious teaching.
- **Catholic villages:** large families and religious "brothers and sisters".
- **Daughters and sons:** separate the duties assigned to each rather than assuming one pattern.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for demography and genealogy; mixed for lived sibling relations; thin for adult siblings' feelings and quarrels.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-planning enforcement is a painful subject; family-division papers are private; inheritance as law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
