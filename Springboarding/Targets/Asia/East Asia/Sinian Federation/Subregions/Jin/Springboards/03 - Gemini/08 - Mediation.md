# Jin: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for steles, contracts and chronicles; Jin and southern Mandarin terms for go-betweens; the Hui community's terms for an imam's role. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly the go-between (broker, interpreter, intercessor). Courts and the law as written go to Libra; the spirit of law to Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Irrigated basin villages (Taiyuan basin, Linfen basin, Jiexiu):** a long record of water quarrels and the brokers who ended them.
- **Dry hill villages of Lüliang and the Taihang:** check whether disputes went to kin, temple heads or officials.
- **Merchant towns of Jinzhong:** guild settlement among firms.
- **Coal districts:** accident settlements; trade-union and company roles.
- **Southern counties (Yuncheng, Linfen):** different lineage strength, which may change who mediates.
- **Hui communities:** imams and elders as go-betweens.
- **Catholic villages:** priests as mediators and intercessors.
- **Wutai Shan's monasteries:** abbots between pilgrims, villagers and officials.
- **Women as kin-keepers and matchmakers:** a gendered role to check, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical water disputes and steles; mixed for present-day informal mediation; thin for family mediation inside households.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for steles and contracts; Standard Chinese; local terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** private settlements after deaths are sensitive and may be suppressed; legal mediation belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
