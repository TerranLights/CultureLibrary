# Jin: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (pre-war Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for poetry and moral texts; Jin and southern Mandarin words for tale-bearing and idle talk; current online slang. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor, unconfirmed reports passed from person to person, is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small hill villages of Lüliang and the Taihang:** dense, inescapable talk; check whether reputation works differently from the basin towns.
- **Basin market towns (Jinzhong, Linfen, Yuncheng):** markets and fairs as news-and-gossip exchanges.
- **Taiyuan and Datong:** online gossip, work-unit talk and anonymous city life.
- **Coal-town work units:** shift-crew talk and the reach of rumor after accidents.
- **Hui and Catholic communities:** close-knit networks where reputation is shared inside a faith group; check, do not assume.
- **Wutai Shan's monastic communities:** codes of speech for monks and nuns.
- **Women's and men's settings:** compare the well, the kitchen, the bathhouse and the card table rather than assuming one gender gossips.
- **Migrant workers returning home:** news of who prospered or failed away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in fiction and drama; mixed for history of rumor; thin for measured, present-day everyday gossip.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect hearing for opera and recordings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** online rumor is policed by law; campaign-era denunciations are painful and partly closed; individuals' reputations must stay unnamed in research notes.
- **Held for review:** none.
