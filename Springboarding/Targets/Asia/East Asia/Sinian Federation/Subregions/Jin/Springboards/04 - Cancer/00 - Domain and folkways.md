**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation (its lands: the pre-war province of Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script, with traditional forms for pre-1950s sources); the Jin 晋语 dialect group (Bingzhou 并州, Lüliang 吕梁, Shangdang 上党, Wutai 五台 and Da-Bao 大包 subgroups *check*); Zhongyuan Mandarin 中原官话 (Fenhe 汾河 varieties) in the south. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what is home, and who is family? Household forms, roots, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong 晋中 basin (Taiyuan, Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** merchant compounds and Jin-dialect core; the household as a business base.
- **Jinnan 晋南 / Hedong 河东 (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers, older farming plains, ancestral-roots sites; separate from the Jin-speaking north.
- **Jindongnan 晋东南 / Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** Taihang upland villages, fortified settlements, a distinct dialect subgroup.
- **Lüliang 吕梁 mountains and Yellow River counties (Linxian 临县, Liulin 柳林, Xingxian 兴县 *check*):** cave-house villages, out-migration.
- **Jinbei 晋北 (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou, the frontier counties Hequ, Pianguan, Youyu):** frontier history, migration north, contact with Mongol herders.
- **Mining towns (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi, Jincheng):** company housing and miners' households.
- **Hui 回族 communities (Taiyuan, Datong, Changzhi *check*):** mosque-centered family life.
- **Catholic villages:** distinct marriage and family networks.
- **Small Mongol and Manchu populations *check*:** comparison only.
- Diaspora (comparison only): descendants of Shanxi migrants in Inner Mongolia's Hetao and Hohhot, and old Shanxi merchant guild halls 山西会馆 elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteers, census tables and merchant-compound heritage; mixed for current everyday family life outside Taiyuan; thin in English beyond a few village studies.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional); Jin dialect for terms and oral sources; Zhongyuan Mandarin varieties in the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform and collective-era family histories; household registration records; Catholic communities' history. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for Shanxi as the core. The Sinian Federation is a wider frame only.
- **Held for review:** none.
