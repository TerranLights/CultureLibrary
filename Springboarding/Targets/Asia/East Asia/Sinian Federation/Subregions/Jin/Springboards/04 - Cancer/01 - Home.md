# Jin 晋: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 varieties across the center and north; Zhongyuan Mandarin 中原官话 in the Linfen-Yuncheng south. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cave-house districts (Lüliang, Linxian, the Yellow River counties, parts of Jinnan):** home dug into or built from loess; strong out-migration.
- **Jinzhong walled towns and merchant compounds (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Lingshi):** courtyard homes, heritage tourism, residents moved out.
- **Taiyuan apartment blocks and urban villages 城中村:** commercial housing vs. old work-unit flats.
- **Coal towns (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi, Jincheng, Xiaoyi):** subsidence, relocation, company housing.
- **Jinnan plains (Yuncheng, Linfen):** brick courtyard farmhouses, older settled villages; different dialect words for house parts.
- **Taihang uplands (Zuoquan, Wuxiang, Pingshun):** stone houses and terraced villages.
- **Northern frontier counties (Hequ, Pianguan, Youyu):** homes left behind by men who went north.
- **Hui households:** home practices around halal kitchens and the mosque quarter.
- Diaspora (comparison only): Shanxi-origin families in Inner Mongolia who still call Shanxi 老家.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on architecture and heritage; mixed on what residents say about their own homes; thin in English beyond architecture.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for house-part words and oral history; southern Zhongyuan Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation disputes; mining-damage compensation. Property rights belong to Taurus, and any law on housing belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
