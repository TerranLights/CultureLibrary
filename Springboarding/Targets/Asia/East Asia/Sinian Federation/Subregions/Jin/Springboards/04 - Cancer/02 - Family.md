## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** family as firm; branches and managers drawn from kin.
- **Jinnan / Hedong (Yuncheng, Linfen, Wenxi, Hongdong):** old named lineages, ancestral-roots tourism, Zhongyuan Mandarin kin terms.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** distinct kin terms; lineage halls in fortified villages.
- **Lüliang and Yellow River counties:** labor migration leaving grandparents and children together.
- **Jinbei and Wutai area:** frontier households, ties to kin who moved north.
- **Coal-mining families:** shift work, accidents and widowhood reshaping households.
- **Urban Taiyuan:** smaller families, one-child cohorts now caring for parents.
- **Hui families;** **Catholic families** (closed marriage networks to check); **small Mongol and Manchu households *check*.**
- Diaspora (comparison only): Shanxi-surname associations and descendants of Ming-era Hongdong migrants elsewhere.
