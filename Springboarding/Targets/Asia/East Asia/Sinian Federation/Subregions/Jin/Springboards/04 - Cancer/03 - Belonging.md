# Jin 晋: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 (the speech that marks many Shanxi insiders); Zhongyuan Mandarin in the south; Hui community usage. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jin speakers vs. Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers of the south:** different speech, different roots stories; does "Shanxi person" mean the same in both?
- **Hongdong, Linfen and Yuncheng:** roots-heritage hosts; belonging as something offered to visitors.
- **Datong and the Jinbei frontier:** Xianbei, Mongol and garrison history; a border identity.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** strong village-festival traditions, separate dialect.
- **Lüliang villages emptied by migration:** belonging to a place one no longer lives in.
- **Coal towns with workers from other provinces:** outsiders who stayed for decades.
- **Taiyuan as the provincial magnet:** newcomers from the counties.
- **Hui, Catholic and Buddhist (Wutaishan) communities:** faith-based belonging alongside place.
- Diaspora (comparison only): Shanxi-descended communities in Inner Mongolia's Hetao and those claiming Hongdong origin elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for roots heritage and dialect material; mixed for felt belonging (survey cuts at provincial level may be small); English thin.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect; classical Chinese for poetry and gazetteers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** household registration 户口 and residence permits (the rules belong to Libra or Capricorn; only the felt side here); ethnic and religious identity questions. Regional teasing can slide into stereotype: record variation, not one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
