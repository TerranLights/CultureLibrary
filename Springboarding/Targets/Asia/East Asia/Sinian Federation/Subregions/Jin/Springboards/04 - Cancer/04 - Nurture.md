*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 varieties (child-talk and nursery words differ by county); southern Zhongyuan Mandarin; Hui usage in mosque schooling. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lüliang and Jinxi sending counties:** grandparent rearing, boarding schools, out-migrant parents.
- **Jinzhong towns:** merchant-family traditions of training heirs; heavy tutoring now.
- **Jinnan (Yuncheng, Linfen):** ritual-bread and birth customs; Zhongyuan Mandarin nursery talk.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** cloth-tiger making, village childhood rites.
- **Jinbei herding and frontier counties (Youyu, Pianguan, Shuozhou):** children's work with livestock; lambs and kids as children's charges.
- **Coal-town families:** fathers on shifts, mothers as sole daily carers; accident orphans.
- **Taiyuan middle-class families:** kindergartens, enrichment classes, smaller families.
- **Hui and Catholic families:** religious upbringing through mosque and parish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk objects and customs; mixed for current practice outside national panels; English thin.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for nursery words and rhymes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's data; studies of left-behind children can frame rural families as deficient: read the families' own accounts first. Discipline and parents' authority belong to Capricorn; play to Leo; formal schooling to Gemini and Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
