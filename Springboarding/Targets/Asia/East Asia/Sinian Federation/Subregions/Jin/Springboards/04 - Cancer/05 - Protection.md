## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jincheng and Yangcheng (Qinhe valley):** fortified lineage villages; protection organized by kin.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** walled compounds, escort firms, protection of wealth.
- **Jinbei frontier (Datong, Shuozhou, Dai county, Youyu):** garrison heritage, Great Wall villages.
- **Jinnan (Yuncheng, Linfen):** Guan Yu worship; temple protection rites.
- **Taihang uplands (Wuxiang, Zuoquan, Licheng):** wartime self-defense memory.
- **Coal-mining families:** danger at work; widows and rescue crews.
- **Yu county and Cangshan area:** the orphan-protection story as local identity.
- **Hui communities:** mosque-based mutual protection; **Catholic villages:** memory of 1900 violence and of walled parishes *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fortifications, legends and temple cults; mixed for present feelings of safety; English thin.
