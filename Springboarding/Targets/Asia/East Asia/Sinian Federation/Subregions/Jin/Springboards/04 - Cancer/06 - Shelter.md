## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Linfen and Hongdong (Jinnan basin):** earthquake history; rebuilding traditions.
- **Datong and Yanggao (Jinbei):** cold-climate disaster shelter; frontier inns.
- **Wutai and Fanshi:** monastery hospitality; pilgrims from Mongol and Tibetan Buddhist communities.
- **Lüliang and the Yellow River counties:** cave homes as shelter; ferry-town inns at Qikou.
- **Jinzhong (Pingyao, Jiexiu, Qixian):** 2021 flood damage; heritage houses at risk.
- **Taiyuan:** relief stations, public rental housing, migrant workers in shared rooms.
- **Coal towns:** dormitories and temporary housing after subsidence.
- **Catholic parishes and mission compounds *check*:** historical refuges in famine and war.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and imperial relief; mixed for present homelessness, where official figures are sparse; English thin except missionary and famine sources.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** returns of vagrants; domestic-violence cases; wartime and 1900 histories. Hospitality to guests sits on no sign's list: note it, do not research it here.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search gazetteer databases for 养济院 and 栖流所 across Shanxi counties and tabulate them.

3. Search press archives for 山西 2021 暴雨 安置 and list temporary sites by county.
4. Search CNKI for 五台山 挂单 and 朝台 to find studies of pilgrim lodging.
5. Look for the Taiyuan relief station's annual reports or press releases.
