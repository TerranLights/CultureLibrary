# Jin 晋: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 varieties; southern Zhongyuan Mandarin; classical Chinese for odes, inscriptions and filial biographies. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lüliang and Linfen mountain counties:** elders left in emptied villages; women leaving to care for strangers.
- **Coal regions (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi, Jincheng, Xiaoyi):** long illness from mining; widows' care work.
- **Jinnan (Yuncheng, Linfen):** old filial-model traditions and tomb art.
- **Jinzhong (Taigu, Qixian, Pingyao):** pharmacy and mission-hospital history; merchant families' care of dependents.
- **Taiyuan:** care homes, hired aides, one-child cohorts caring for two sets of parents.
- **Jinbei and Wutai:** Buddhist temples' care roles; cold-season isolation of elders.
- **Hui families:** care obligations framed by mosque teaching; **Catholic families:** parish and sisters' care *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for filial texts and official models; mixed for real caregiving hours; thin on paid care in small towns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral interviews with elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** health records; elder neglect; miners' disease and compensation (claims belong to Libra). Duty and parents' authority belong to Capricorn; health systems to Virgo. Official "filial model" material is promotion: read it beside families' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** none.
