*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 varieties (northwest Shanxi song and drama texts); southern Zhongyuan Mandarin; classical Chinese for odes, stelae and legends. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northwest frontier counties (Hequ, Baode, Pianguan):** the historical home of men leaving and women waiting.
- **Lüliang and Taihang mountain villages:** hollowing out; elders and empty houses.
- **Jinnan famine belt (Linfen, Yuncheng, Pingyang areas):** the deepest 1870s losses; stelae and family memory.
- **Coal towns after closure (Huozhou, Xiaoyi, parts of Datong) *check*:** workers' families left by industry.
- **Fenyang and Jinzhong towns:** emigration abroad and to big cities; divorce and remarriage.
- **Taiyuan:** welfare institutes, care homes, the urban empty nest.
- **Catholic communities:** memory of mission orphanages and the conflict around them.
- Diaspora (comparison only): Shanxi-descended families in Inner Mongolia who were the ones who left.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine history and song; mixed for present-day abandonment (official figures are partial); English rich only for the famine.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; northwest Jin dialect for songs and drama.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child abandonment and disability; international adoption; 1870s and 1890s anti-missionary rumors; mining layoffs. Desertion as a legal matter belongs to Libra; inheritance to Scorpio. Use survivors' and families' accounts first.
- **Held for review:** none.
