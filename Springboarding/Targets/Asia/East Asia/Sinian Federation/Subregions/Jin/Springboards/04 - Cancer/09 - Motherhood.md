## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong (Taiyuan, Jiexiu, Pingyao):** temple mother cults; merchant-family women managing households while men traveled.
- **Jinnan (Yuncheng, Linfen, Wanrong, Yicheng):** Houtu worship; the two-child pilot; Zhongyuan Mandarin mother terms.
- **Shangdang and Taihang (Pingshun, Changzhi, Jincheng):** collective-era working mothers; child-giving temple fairs.
- **Lüliang and Jinxi:** mothers raising children alone while husbands migrate; grandmothers as mothers.
- **Jinbei (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou):** Northern Wei heritage; colder-climate postpartum practice.
- **Coal-town mothers:** shift-work husbands; miners' widows.
- **Taiyuan professional mothers:** later births, one or two children, paid help.
- **Hui mothers and Catholic mothers:** religious framings of motherhood.
