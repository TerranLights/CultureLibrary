## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong 大同 and Jinbei**: Buddhist sculpture, copper crafts, Liao and Northern Wei layers; makers working between steppe and Han styles.
- **Wutai 五台**: monastic craft for Han and Tibetan Buddhist temples.
- **Jinzhong 晋中**: lacquer, merchant-compound carving, banking invention.
- **Hedong 河东 (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: printing, mural painting, drum music; Central Plains Mandarin speech.
- **Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng)**: Taihang village crafts; ironworking and glazed tiles (*check*).
- **Lüliang 吕梁** and the western loess: women's paper-cut and embroidery; cave-dwelling (窑洞) building.
- **Industrial towns** (Yangquan, Datong coal): engineers, mine and plant workers as improvisers.
- **Hui communities**: mosque decoration and Arabic-script calligraphy in Chinese settings.
- **Gender**: needlework and paper-cut credited to women, carving and building to men; worth separating.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi craftsmen in merchant guild halls 会馆 outside the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for architecture, sculpture and heritage crafts; mixed for modern arts; thin for everyday improvising and minority craft.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect dictionaries for craft and wit vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** looted and displaced sculpture (Tianlongshan 天龙山 heads abroad and recent returns) is a live heritage dispute; treat with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
