## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northwest Yellow River counties** (Hequ, Baode 保德, Pianguan 偏关): a song culture shaped by men leaving for the steppe.
- **Taihang uplands** (Zuoquan, Heshun, Shangdang): blossom tunes and Shangdang opera; revolutionary-era song reworkings.
- **Jinbei / Datong**: shawm bands, Beilu opera, Yanbei dialect humor.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: family codes of reserve; test whether these still shape talk.
- **Taiyuan**: urban youth style, campus life, online creators.
- **Hedong (Linfen, Yuncheng)**: Puju, drum troupes; southern speech.
- **Hui**: dress and faith expression.
- **Catholic villages**: testimony and hymn singing as expression.
- **Sexual minorities**: largely private expression; see flags.
- **Gender and generation**: older women's lament singing; young women's online self-presentation.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi-descended communities in Inner Mongolia who share errentai.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for song and opera; mixed for everyday speech norms; thin and guarded for political and sexual expression.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for lyrics and videos; classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political speech, religious dress (especially for Hui) and LGBTQ expression are under legal and social constraint; interview data needs consent and anonymity. "Reserved northerners" is a stereotype to test.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Locate the Shanxi folk-song anthology volume and index its love songs and laments by county.
2. Search CNKI for "傅山 宁拙毋巧" and "元好问 论诗 真" for the pre-modern aesthetic of genuine expression.
3. Search Douyin for "山西方言" and sample twenty creators by city.
4. Read Stephen Jones on Shanxi shawm bands for who voices feeling at funerals and weddings.
5. Search the Education Department site for "中小学生 仪容仪表".
