## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: plaque culture and merchant honors.
- **Taihang and Shangdang villages** (Xiyang, Pingshun, Wuxiang): model villages and model workers, the most honored and the most politicized.
- **Coal and steel workforces** (Datong, Yangquan, Taiyuan): collective honors against unsung, dangerous work.
- **Jiexiu and Mianshan**: a town built around a story of unrewarded loyalty.
- **Rural women**: from chastity arches to labor models; whose work counts.
- **Yu county and other wartime-affected villages**: recognition of harm.
- **Hui communities**: clerical titles and community honors.
- **Opera professionals**: national awards against local fame.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi natives honored elsewhere (hometown associations of Shanxi people in other provinces and abroad).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official honors and model-worker lists; mixed for everyday credit and praise; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jingtang terms for Hui titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime sexual violence survivors and their families require great care and consent; Dazhai and Cultural Revolution-era honors are politically charged; chastity commendations tie recognition to constraint on women.
- **Held for review:** none.
