# Jin 晋: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia; lands: Shanxi 山西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (Buddhist records); Jin 晋语 dialect for words of looking and showing; Japanese for one pilgrim's diary; Hui Jingtang 经堂语 and Arabic script on signage. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women in merchant towns** (past and present): inner-quarter seclusion in compounds; today's guides and performers there.
- **Rural elderly in the Lüliang and Xinzhou uplands**: left behind, rarely seen.
- **Miners and their families** (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng): unseen work.
- **Hui communities**: visible dress and signage under scrutiny.
- **Wutai pilgrims and clergy**: Han, Tibetan Buddhist and Mongol devotees watching and being watched on a holy mountain.
- **Taiyuan's urban middle class and migrants**: city squares and anonymity.
- **Young live-streamers**: chosen, paid visibility.
- **Children**: twelve-year rites and school display.
- **People with disabilities**: query separately; often out of public view.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi migrants in big eastern cities, and how they are seen there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Wutai and architecture; mixed for daily gaze and greeting norms; thin for the elderly, disabled and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Japanese (or translation) for Ennin; dialect for looking words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and minority visibility are politically sensitive; photography of rural people raises consent issues; mine-disaster reporting is restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
