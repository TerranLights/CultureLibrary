## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shangdang and Taihang villages** (Lucheng, Wuxiang, Zuoquan): land-reform memory and its idioms of standing.
- **Lüliang and the western loess**: long poverty, out-migration, aid programs.
- **Hongtong and the Linfen basin**: the kiln case; also ancestral pride, an apt contrast.
- **Mining towns** (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng): dangerous work, compensation, closures.
- **The rural old**: care at home against new care homes.
- **People with disabilities**: blind musicians and storytellers, and others less visible.
- **Women**: famine-era sales of wives and daughters, ghost marriage, and today's claims of respect.
- **Hui communities**: burial and dietary rules as dignity.
- **Catholic communities**: survival under past persecution (handle carefully).
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi migrant workers in other provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine, land reform and literature; mixed for current welfare and care; thin for minorities and disability.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect for address and kin terms; Jingtang terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor, ghost marriage, famine cannibalism and land-reform violence are painful and partly censored; mining deaths are politically sensitive; quotation of victims needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
