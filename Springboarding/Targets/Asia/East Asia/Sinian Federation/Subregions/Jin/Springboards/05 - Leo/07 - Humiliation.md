*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia; lands: Shanxi 山西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Jin 晋语 and southern Mandarin insult and teasing vocabulary; Hui Jingtang 经堂语 where Han–Hui relations come up. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hongdong and the Linfen basin**: a county carrying a famous slur.
- **Jinzhong (Qixian, Taigu, Pingyao)**: yangge comedy and merchant-town propriety.
- **Fenyang and Lüliang**: small-town street life as filmed and lived.
- **Taihang and Shangdang villages**: campaign-era shaming in memory.
- **Datong and Jinbei**: frontier history, wartime occupation, mining labor discipline.
- **Taiyuan**: the 1900 killings and the university; urban online shaming.
- **Hui communities**: insults tied to diet and faith; handle carefully.
- **Catholic villages**: memory of persecution in 1900 and later.
- **Women and young men**: wedding pranks fall on brides and bridesmaids; bullying falls differently on boys and girls (to test).
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi migrants mocked in other provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for opera, classical tales and war memory; mixed for everyday teasing; thin and censored for political campaigns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect for insults and comic plays.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution and land-reform shaming, the 1900 killings, Han–Hui friction and bullying cases all need care; survivors' accounts should not be quoted beyond a few words.
- **Held for review:** none.
