## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: lineage giving, guild halls, relief works.
- **Wutai and Xinzhou**: monastic patronage and pilgrim giving.
- **Southern Shanxi** (Linfen, Yuncheng): temple and stage donor steles, Guandi temple offerings.
- **Shangdang and Taihang villages**: village festival funding and labor contributions.
- **Coal-rich counties**: large private donations and the debate over showy giving (test, do not assume).
- **Lüliang and poorer uplands**: receivers of aid; poverty-alleviation pairings.
- **Hui communities**: obligatory and voluntary alms.
- **Catholic parishes**: church charity.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi merchant guild halls 山陕会馆 in other provinces; Shanxi-ancestry hometown associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inscriptions, gazetteers and merchant history; mixed for present-day giving; thin for informal giving and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect for treating words; Jingtang for Hui practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine relief and missionary aid are politically framed; charity scandals and coerced "donations" exist; stereotypes of coal-rich donors need testing, not repeating.
- **Held for review:** none.
