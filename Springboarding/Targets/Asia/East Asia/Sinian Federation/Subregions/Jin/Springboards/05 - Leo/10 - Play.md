## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong and Yingxian**: shuahai'er, frontier festival games.
- **Northwest Yellow River counties** (Hequ, Baode): lantern mazes and New Year play.
- **Jiexiu and Jinzhong**: Cold Food lore; school activity programs.
- **Southern Shanxi** (Linfen, Yuncheng, Changzhi): tomb-art heritage, cloth toys, drum games.
- **Mining towns**: shift workers' card and chess games; company clubs.
- **Taiyuan**: urban parks, malls, game cafés, esports audiences.
- **Rural elderly and left-behind children**: who plays with whom when the middle generation is gone.
- **Hui communities**: games and festival play under religious rules on gambling (to test, not assume).
- **Girls and boys**: games sorted by gender in rhyme collections.
- **Comparison only:** the shared northwest game repertoire in Shaanxi and Inner Mongolia, kept apart from Shanxi's own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festival and opera play; mixed for children's games (scattered in folklore books); thin for current adult play outside mahjong and cards.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect for game names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling games shade into illegal betting; minors' gaming is tightly regulated; studies of left-behind children need care with privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
