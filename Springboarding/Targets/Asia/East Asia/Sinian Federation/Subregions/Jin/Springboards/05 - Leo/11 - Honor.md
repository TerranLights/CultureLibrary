# Jin 晋: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia; lands: Shanxi 山西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical and bronze-age Chinese (covenant texts); Jin 晋语 and southern Mandarin terms for reputation and sworn ties; Hui Jingtang 经堂语 vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedong (Yuncheng, Xiezhou)**: Guan Yu's homeland; loyalty cult at its source.
- **Yu county and the eastern hills**: Orphan of Zhao lore; also wartime harm (see Recognition) that bears on family honor.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns**: honor as credit; escort codes.
- **Daixian and Yanmen**: frontier-general legends of loyalty.
- **Datong**: 江湖 codes in a coal and rust-belt city.
- **Wenshui and the Taihang base areas**: revolutionary honor and martyr cults.
- **Rural women and men**: whose conduct carries the family name (to test).
- **Hui communities**: purity and trust.
- **Catholic communities**: martyr memories of 1900, honored by the Church.
- **Comparison only:** Guandi temples and guild halls built by Shanxi merchants in other provinces.
