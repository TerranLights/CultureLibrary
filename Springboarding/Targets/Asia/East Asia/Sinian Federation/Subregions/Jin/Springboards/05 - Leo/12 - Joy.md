## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taihang uplands** (Zuoquan, Heshun): dance, love-legend festivals.
- **Southern Shanxi** (Linfen, Yuncheng, Xinjiang county): drumming, temple fairs, Central Plains speech.
- **Yellow River and Lüliang towns** (Qikou, Hequ): river work songs, mountain songs.
- **Taiyuan**: urban comedy, parks, festivals; Catholic villages on its edge.
- **Datong and Jinbei**: singers of the north, frontier festivals.
- **Southeast villages** (Long Bow): how births and weddings are greeted, across generations.
- **Hui communities**: their own festival calendar.
- **Elders and the young**: long-life funerals against nightlife and online celebration.
- **Men and women**: who may show delight at births, weddings and in public (to test).
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi-descended communities in Inner Mongolia who share mountain songs and errentai joy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festival, music and folk dance; mixed for survey measures; thin for private joy and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and archaic Chinese; dialect for lyrics and comedy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** son preference around births is sensitive; staged "happiness" in official media needs critical reading; religious festivals of Catholics and Hui fall under state oversight.
- **Held for review:** none.
