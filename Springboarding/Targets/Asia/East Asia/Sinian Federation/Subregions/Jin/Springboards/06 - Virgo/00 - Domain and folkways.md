## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns** (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Jiexiu): commercial work, apprentices, shares.
- **Taiyuan:** state industry (steel, machinery, the old arsenal).
- **Yanbei 雁北 (Datong, Shuozhou):** coal and a frontier past of Xianbei, Khitan and Mongol contact.
- **Northwest Xinzhou** (Hequ, Baode, Pianguan): out-migration for seasonal work.
- **Wutai Shan's monasteries**, Chinese and Tibetan Buddhist: a separate round of work and service.
- **Lüliang mountain counties** (Lin county 临县, Xing county): cave-dwelling farming and labor export.
- **Yangquan:** an eastern mining-and-power belt.
- **Shangdang** (Changzhi, Jincheng): Taihang farming, anthracite, old iron working, wartime base areas.
- **Hedong and Pingyang** (Yuncheng, Linfen): wheat, cotton, the salt lake, a different Mandarin.
- **Hui** (Taiyuan, Datong, Changzhi): halal trades, mosque-centered service.
- **Catholic villages** (Dong'ergou 洞儿沟 near Taiyuan): parish rhythms of work and rest.
- **Smaller groups:** Manchu descendants of the Taiyuan garrison, Mongol families, migrant miners from other provinces.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi merchant guild halls (晋商会馆) elsewhere and the *zouxikou* settlements in Inner Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchants, coal and the collective era; mixed for the daily round and health; thin for Hui, Manchu and Mongol work customs.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Shanxi only, as a working list the author still confirms; this sign's sheets are written for Shanxi's lands.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialects and southern Mandarin for terms and songs; classical Chinese; Japanese; Hui Arabic and Persian loanwords.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine disasters; the 2007 Hongtong 洪洞 brick-kiln forced-labor case; wartime forced labor in Datong's mines; the Cultural Revolution; religious communities. Labor law belongs to Libra.
