## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal belts** (Datong, Shuozhou, Yangquan, Jincheng, Gujiao): mine work, risk and pay set work's meaning apart from farming districts.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** commercial apprenticeship rather than field labor as the respected path.
- **Taihang uplands** (Zuoquan, Xiyang, Pingshun, Wuxiang): terraced farming, wartime mutual-aid teams, model villages.
- **Lüliang and the northwest:** labor export, past (*zouxikou*) and present (care work, city construction).
- **Southern plains** (Yuncheng, Linfen): grain and cotton farming, salt workers of the Xiezhou 解州 lake, a separate seasonal round.
- **Taiyuan's state-enterprise workers:** the "work unit" life against private and informal work.
- **Hui traders and butchers:** halal trades as a niche with its own norms of clean earning.
- **Wutai Shan monastics:** labor inside a religious discipline.
- **Women and men:** the field, the home and the mine were gendered differently.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi shopkeepers and miners in Inner Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for coal and the collective era; mixed for ordinary private-sector work today; thin for Hui and Mongol work customs.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine deaths and cover-ups; wartime forced labor; the brick-kiln case; slurs for miners. Labor law and courts are Libra's.
