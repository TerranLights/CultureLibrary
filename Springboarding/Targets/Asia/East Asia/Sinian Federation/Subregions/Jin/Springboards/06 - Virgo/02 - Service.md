## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** service as lifelong loyalty to a firm, rewarded by shares.
- **Yuncheng and the Hedong south:** Guan Yu's home ground, where loyal service has its main shrine.
- **Jiexiu and Mianshan:** the Jie Zitui tradition, an unpaid-service ideal tied to one place.
- **Taihang and Lüliang base areas** (Wuxiang, Zuoquan, Xing county): civilian service to an army, remembered as revolutionary.
- **Yanbei frontier** (Daixian, Yanmen, Datong): soldiering and garrison service on the old border.
- **Wutai Shan:** Chinese and Tibetan Buddhist monastic service and pilgrim care.
- **Hui congregations:** mosque offices and community welfare.
- **Catholic villages:** service through parish, sisters and lay leaders.
- **Taiyuan's public employees and the tourist towns' hospitality workers:** salaried service against service as a trade.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi guild halls (山陕会馆) elsewhere, where Guan Yu was served by merchants far from home.
