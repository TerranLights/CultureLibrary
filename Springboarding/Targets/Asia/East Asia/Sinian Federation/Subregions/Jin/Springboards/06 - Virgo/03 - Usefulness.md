## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mine towns after closure** (Datong, Yangquan, Huozhou): usefulness tied to a single industry.
- **Lüliang and northwest Xinzhou villages:** households where the young are away and elders hold the farm.
- **Jinzhong merchant families:** the old merchant reckoning of sons' usefulness in trade against study.
- **Southern plains** (Yuncheng, Linfen): the Sima Guang and Liu Zongyuan home ground, with strong school traditions.
- **Taiyuan's state-enterprise retirees:** a pension class whose role ended with the work unit.
- **Disabled people and blind musicians:** roles carved out by the excluded.
- **Women:** handiness as a measure of a bride; care of elders as unpaid usefulness.
- **Hui families:** family trades (butchery, restaurants) where children's help is expected.
- **Wutai Shan's monks:** a life judged by other measures than output.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for aging and labor statistics, thin for how people speak of usefulness in dialect.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin and southern dialects; classical Chinese; Mongolian, Tibetan and Manchu only for small groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability and the old; layoffs; suicide among elders, to be handled with great care.
- **Held for review:** none.
