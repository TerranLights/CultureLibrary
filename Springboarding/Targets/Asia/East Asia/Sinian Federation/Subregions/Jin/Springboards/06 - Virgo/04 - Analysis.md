## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** reckoning as a trade skill, taught to apprentices with the abacus.
- **Taiyuan's universities and institutes:** analysis as a profession.
- **Coal and power towns** (Yangquan, Jincheng, Datong): engineers and safety inspectors against miners' rule-of-thumb.
- **Southern scholar counties** (Yuncheng, Linfen, Anze): a classical learning tradition.
- **Village farmers across the plateau:** weather and soil judged by experience; set beside the almanac (通書) and day-selection.
- **Wutai Shan:** monastic reasoning, Chinese and Tibetan.
- **Hui merchants:** their own trade reckoning and religious learning in mosque schools.
- **Generations:** gaokao-trained young people against elders who trust experience.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant accounting and architecture surveys; thin for everyday reasoning styles; one national psychology study at most.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect for everyday thinking words; Japanese for some merchant and architecture studies; Tibetan for monastic debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine-accident reports can be incomplete; Yan Xishan's statistics served his politics.
- **Held for review:** none.
