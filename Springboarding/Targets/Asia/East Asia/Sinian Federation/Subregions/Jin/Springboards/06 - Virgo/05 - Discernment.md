## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong banking towns:** discernment as an institutional system for credit and hiring.
- **North Shanxi livestock country** (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou): judging animals at fairs.
- **Fenyang and Qingxu:** taste discernment in liquor and vinegar trades.
- **Wutai Shan:** wisdom as a religious goal, with Chinese and Tibetan Buddhist forms.
- **Hui households:** a daily rule of telling clean from unclean.
- **Catholic families:** conscience and vocation in a minority church.
- **Village elders and matchmakers:** judging people for marriage.
- **City young people:** online rumor and scam savvy against older relatives' targeting by scams.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi bank branches across the old empire, where trust was judged far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant credit and halal practice; mixed for everyday judgment of people; thin for the livestock-broker trade, now mostly in memory.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect; Hui Arabic-derived terms; Tibetan.
