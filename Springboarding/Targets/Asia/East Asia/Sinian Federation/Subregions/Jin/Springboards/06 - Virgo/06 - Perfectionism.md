# Jin: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialects; southern Central Plains Mandarin; classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong temple and merchant-house craftsmen** (Pingyao, Lingshi, Jiexiu): ornament and lacquer done to a patron's demand.
- **The southern opera belt** (Puzhou 蒲州, Linfen, Yuncheng): stage technique drilled to exactness.
- **Taiyuan's state steelmakers and engineers:** precision as industrial policy.
- **Northern Yanbei** (Yingxian, Datong's Yungang 云冈): heritage sites where restoration standards are argued.
- **Noodle-makers across the province:** a daily, domestic form of exactness, often women's.
- **Students in county and city schools:** exam perfectionism with different stakes.
- **Hui households:** ritual exactness in washing and food.
- **Wutai Shan monastics:** exact liturgy and recitation.
- **Village "good enough" farming** in the Lüliang uplands: the counterweight of making do.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage craft and architecture; mixed for opera training; thin for psychology of perfectionism with Shanxi samples.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect, with its false friends; Persian-derived Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student distress and suicide, to be handled with great care; official craftsman campaigns are propaganda as well as data.
- **Held for review:** none.
