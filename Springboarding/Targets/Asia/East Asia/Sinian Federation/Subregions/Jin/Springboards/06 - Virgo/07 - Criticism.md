# Jin: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialects; southern Central Plains Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hui sectarian vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jin-state heartland** (Linfen, Yuncheng, Houma): the classical remonstrance tradition claimed locally.
- **Taihang villages** (Changzhi, Jincheng, Zuoquan): wartime satire and later struggle meetings.
- **Lüliang festival culture:** improvised verse that teases and reproves in public.
- **Taiyuan's city comedy and press:** professional and media criticism.
- **Coal counties:** criticism of mine owners and officials, often silenced.
- **Hui congregations:** criticism across religious currents.
- **Catholic parishes:** church discipline and confession.
- **Older and younger generations:** memories of struggle meetings against online complaint culture.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi natives elsewhere who answer outside jokes about the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and literary sources; mixed for folk satire; thin and sensitive for political criticism.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for comic forms; Hui Arabic-derived terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution memory; corruption cases; press control; online censorship of complaints. Defamation and press rules as law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
