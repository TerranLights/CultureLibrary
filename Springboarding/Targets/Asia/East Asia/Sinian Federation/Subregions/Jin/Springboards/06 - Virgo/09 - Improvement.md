## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Fen River valley** (Hejin, Yuncheng): the classical self-cultivation and exam tradition.
- **Taihang terraces** (Xiyang, Pingshun): land remade by collective labor.
- **Northern sandy counties** (Youyu, Zuoyun): afforestation as civic improvement.
- **Datong city:** old-town reconstruction and displaced residents.
- **Pingyao:** heritage restoration and tourism.
- **Lüliang relocation villages:** improvement brought by the state.
- **Coal towns in transition:** workers retraining or leaving.
- **Hui families:** moral and educational betterment within the community.
- **Catholic villages:** church schooling and its long role in advancement.
- **Diaspora, for comparison only:** Shanxi migrants who "got ahead" in Inner Mongolia and Beijing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state campaigns and urban renewal; mixed for self-improvement as people practice it; thin for minority views.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Jin dialect for village memory; Hui Arabic-derived terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Dazhai's falsified figures; forced relocation and demolition; campaigns to "change customs". Property and relocation law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
