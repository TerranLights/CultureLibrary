**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Shanxi 山西), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (traditional script for pre-modern titles); the Jin 晋语 dialect subgroups (Bingzhou 并州片, Lüliang 吕梁片, Shangdang 上党片, Wutai 五台片, Da-Bao 大包片); Fen River 汾河片 Zhongyuan Mandarin in the south; Hui Islamic vocabulary; small Mongol and Manchu heritage communities. **Concept in one line:** the domain of partnership and justice: what counts as a fair bargain and a good partnership, with marriage ways, gender ways and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jin-dialect north and center against the Mandarin-speaking south:** Yanbei 雁北 (Datong, Shuozhou), Jinzhong 晋中 and Taiyuan against Hedong 河东 (Yuncheng, Linfen); wedding steps and words differ.
- **Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng):** its own dialect, opera and temple-festival traditions.
- **Lüliang and Xinzhou mountain counties:** poorer uplands where bride price and bachelorhood press hardest.
- **Taiyuan and the big cities against villages.**
- **Hui communities** (Taiyuan, Changzhi, Datong): marriage within the faith.
- **Catholic villages:** church marriage law beside the state's.
- **Former merchant families of Jinzhong:** households run around absent men.
- **Coal towns** (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi): migrant miners and mining-camp families.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi merchant communities elsewhere and their guild halls.
