## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedong 河东 (Yuncheng, Linfen):** home of the Gaoyao, Liu Zongyuan and Su San traditions; Mandarin-speaking south.
- **Jinzhong 晋中 and Taiyuan:** yamen museums and merchant towns, where justice ran through guilds as well as courts.
- **Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng):** land-reform village justice; separate opera repertoire.
- **Yanbei 雁北 and the coal belt:** mining accidents, labor and compensation cases.
- **Hui communities:** mosque elders and ahong 阿訇 as judges of quarrels.
- **Catholic villages:** memories of the Boxer killings of 1900.
- **Victims' and petitioners' families:** those who sought redress for wartime harms, kiln labor or mine deaths.
- **Comparison only:** Jin merchant guild courts in other cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and Yuan-drama sources, temple art and recent scandals; mixed for court statistics by prefecture.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Japanese for the redress suits.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime sexual violence; forced labor; anti-corruption cases; court data whose public access was cut back.
- **Held for review:** none.
