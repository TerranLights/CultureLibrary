# Jin: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, the Shanxi 山西 member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialect forms (Taiyuan, Datong, Changzhi, Lüliang); Fen River Mandarin of Yuncheng and Linfen; Classical Chinese for steles and dynastic histories. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upstream and downstream villages** on the Jin, Huo and Fen water systems: water fairness was argued place by place.
- **Collective-era model villages** (Dazhai, Xigou) against ordinary brigades.
- **Coal-rich and coal-poor counties:** windfalls and their sharing.
- **Women and men in farm and factory work.**
- **Owners, managers and apprentices** in old merchant firms.
- **Hui traders and butchers:** market dealing within and across faith lines.
- **Villages founded by Henan migrants** after the 1940s famines (*check*): newcomers' access to shares.
- **Urban and rural household registration.**
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi merchant houses elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water steles and collective-era archives; mixed for present-day attitudes by prefecture (survey samples may be small).
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective-era files and coal compensation records may need permission.
- **Held for review:** none.
