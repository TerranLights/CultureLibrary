## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns** (Qixian, Taigu, Pingyao, Yuci): firms built on owner-manager partnership.
- **Hedong:** the Guan Yu cult's home ground.
- **Yanbei and the Great Wall counties:** migrant men's partnerships in Inner Mongolia; marriages strained by absence.
- **Shangdang and the Taihang base areas:** mutual-aid teams and early cooperatives.
- **Coal-mining crews:** work partnerships under danger.
- **Hui families:** marriage and trade partnerships inside the faith.
- **Catholic villages:** church-sanctioned marriage and confraternities.
- **Urban professionals in Taiyuan:** dual-earner couples.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi firms and guilds in Mongolia, Kyakhta and other Chinese cities.
