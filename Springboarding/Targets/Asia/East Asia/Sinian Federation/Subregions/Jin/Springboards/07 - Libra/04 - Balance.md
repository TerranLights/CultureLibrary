## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal belt** (Datong, Shuozhou, Yangquan, Changzhi, Jincheng): shift work and land damage.
- **Loess uplands of Lüliang and Xinzhou:** erosion, poverty and the sex-ratio question.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** ledgers and built symmetry.
- **Taiyuan:** urban workers, office hours, TCM clinics.
- **Hedong farmland:** irrigated plain with a different work rhythm.
- **Buddhist communities of Wutai and Daoist sites** (Hengshan, Longshan): teachings of the middle path.
- **Hui households:** food rules beside folk hot-cold ideas.
- **Rural migrants in cities:** families split between two places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medicine, architecture and environmental policy; thin for what ordinary people mean by balance.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral health lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sex-ratio data touch on sex-selective abortion; pollution data have been disputed.
- **Held for review:** none.
