## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedong:** home of Xue Xuan and of many classical Jin stories.
- **Jinzhong merchant families:** firm rules on kin and hiring.
- **Lüliang:** Yu Chenglong's home and a center of the 2014 cases.
- **Jincheng and Yangcheng:** the Chen family's official lineage.
- **Village leadership in the northwest** (Hequ, Baode): election experiments.
- **Coal towns:** licensing and the place of personal ties.
- **Hui communities:** imams hired from outside.
- **Catholic parishes:** priests appointed by the diocese, not the village.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi native-place networks in Beijing.
