# Jin: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, whose lands are Shanxi 山西 (a Sinian Federation member state), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for the long frontier record; Jin 晋语 dialects for everyday tact; Mongolian, Japanese and English for the foreign side of exchanges. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong and the Great Wall counties:** frontier trade and talks with steppe peoples over two thousand years.
- **Wutai and Xinzhou:** monastic hospitality to Mongol and Tibetan Buddhists.
- **Hedong:** the classical Jin court and the Pei and Wang lineages of officials.
- **Jinzhong:** the merchants' own negotiating culture with Mongol and Russian partners (comparison for its trade abroad).
- **Taigu and Fenyang:** missionary and educational links with the United States.
- **Hui communities:** links with Muslim communities elsewhere.
- **Catholic communities:** dealings through foreign missionaries, before and after 1949.
- **Local officials and business hosts:** banquet diplomacy today.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and Ming frontier diplomacy and for Wutai; mixed for present-day everyday tact.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Mongolian and Tibetan for frontier and Wutai sources; Japanese and English for exchange records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan Buddhist ties; wartime collaboration; current foreign-affairs files.
- **Held for review:** none.
