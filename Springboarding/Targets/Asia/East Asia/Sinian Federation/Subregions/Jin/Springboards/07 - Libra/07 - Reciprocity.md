*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialects (gift and visiting words vary by county); Fen River Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Hui Islamic vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong (Jiexiu, Qixian, Taigu):** Cold Food rites and merchant credit.
- **Hedong (Wenxi, Hongdong):** gift buns and the visiting-kin festival.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** temple festivals with rotating duties; the hereditary musician households 乐户 who served them (a former debased status group).
- **Taihang and Lüliang base-area counties:** exchange labor and mutual aid.
- **Yu county and Xinzhou:** the Zhao orphan cult.
- **Hui communities:** alms and festival sharing.
- **Catholic villages:** parish charity and godparent duties.
- **Older and younger generations:** support across a lifetime.
- **Comparison only:** guild halls and native-place associations elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical tales, festivals and gift-food heritage; mixed for present-day gift amounts by county.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for gift vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift registers are private family papers; gifts to officials shade into bribery.
- **Held for review:** none.
