## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Hedong and Lüliang:** almanac-led choices of dates and matches, as a question.
- **Taiyuan and Datong office workers:** personal choice among many options.
- **Coal towns after mine closures:** families deciding whether to stay or go.
- **Young migrants and the parents who stay behind:** who decides a child's future.
- **Hui families:** consultation with religious teachers before marriage and travel.
- **Buddhist and Daoist laypeople at Wutai and Jinci:** divination at temples.
- **Officials:** decisions passed upward.
- **Comparison only:** Shanxi migrants in Beijing and Inner Mongolia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin as a named concept; rich only in indirect sources (divination practice, migration intentions, history). A thin result here is honest.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination can be classed as "feudal superstition" in official sources; frame questions neutrally.
- **Held for review:** none.
