# Jin: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, the Sinian Federation member state on the lands of Shanxi 山西, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialects; Fen River Mandarin; Classical Chinese (histories, opera texts); Hui vocabulary of reconciliation. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal towns and mining families:** silence and payment after accidents.
- **Jinzhong merchant families:** forbearance as household and business policy.
- **Datong and the north:** frontier memory of ceding land for peace.
- **Rural daughters-in-law and older women:** who is expected to give way at home, as a question.
- **Petitioners and their villages.**
- **Hui communities:** reconciliation customs of their own.
- **Buddhist monastics and laypeople at Wutai.**
- **Catholic families:** confession and parish mediation.
- **Younger urban women in Taiyuan:** a generation that may name domestic violence differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; court and mediation numbers exist, but silence is by nature hard to record.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for interviews and local TV.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence, mine deaths, petitioning and stability maintenance; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for "打金枝 晋剧 不痴不聋" and read a published script.
2. Search CNKI and news archives for "山西 矿难 瞒报 私了".
3. Search China Judgments Online for Shanxi courts and "人身安全保护令".
4. Read the Five Dynasties histories on Shi Jingtang, then later judgments of him (Sima Guang's comments in the Zizhi tongjian).
5. Find Shanxi justice department reports with people's mediation case counts.
