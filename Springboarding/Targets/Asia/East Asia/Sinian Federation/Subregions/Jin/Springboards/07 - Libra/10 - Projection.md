# Jin: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a Sinian Federation member state covering Shanxi 山西, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin 晋语 dialects (spirit and blame vocabulary is local); Fen River Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic villages and their neighbors:** a long history of mutual blame.
- **Hui communities and Han neighbors.**
- **Coal towns and farming counties:** each with images of the other.
- **Former base-area villages in Lüliang and Taihang:** wartime and revolutionary enemy images.
- **Taiyuan and the rural counties.**
- **Migrants from Henan and Hebei and long-settled families.**
- **Families of former merchants and of revolutionaries:** class labels that stuck.
- **Comparison only:** how Shanxi migrants are seen in other provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich historical case material (Boxer era, Cave Gully, Cultural Revolution); thin for everyday projection, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for spirit and blame words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious and ethnic tension; regional slurs; Cultural Revolution violence; recorded only as claims, never as findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
