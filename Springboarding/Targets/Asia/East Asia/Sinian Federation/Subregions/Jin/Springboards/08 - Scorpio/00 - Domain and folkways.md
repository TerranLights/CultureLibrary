# Jin (晋): The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Shanxi 山西 lands), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua); the Jin 晋语 dialect group (Bingzhou 并州, Lüliang 吕梁, Shangdang 上党, Wutai 五台 and Da-Bao 大包 subgroups); Zhongyuan Mandarin of the Fen River 汾河片 in the south; Hui religious vocabulary; some Mongolian and Tibetan at Wutaishan. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and rebirth, sex, shared resources, inheritance and secrets: how death, power and what is hidden are faced.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North (Datong, Shuozhou; Da-Bao Jin):** frontier coal country with Mongol contact.
- **Xinzhou and Wutai:** a Buddhist mountain economy with Tibetan and Mongol ties.
- **Taiyuan basin and Jinzhong (Bingzhou Jin):** merchant counties (Qixian, Taigu, Pingyao, Jiexiu, Lingshi) where inheritance met business shares.
- **West (Lüliang subgroup, Yellow River loess counties):** upland villages; check older funeral and marriage forms.
- **Southeast (Shangdang: Changzhi, Jincheng):** sacrificial festivals and Taihang mountain villages.
- **South (Linfen, Yuncheng; Fen River Mandarin):** Yao, Shun and Guan Yu sites; old stage and temple culture.
- **Hui communities; Catholic villages; Protestant congregations; folk ritual specialists; Wutai monastics:** each holds its own death and transformation rites.
- **Coal-mining towns and migrant miners from other member states.**
- **Comparison only (diaspora):** 走西口 descendants in Inner Mongolia; Shanxi merchant guild halls elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteers, stelae, merchant history and funeral music; mixed for sex ways and money in marriage; thin in minority languages.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Shanxi only, a working list the author still confirms; these sheets are written for Shanxi's lands. The Sinian Federation as a whole is named only for context.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Jin-dialect dictionaries; Hui Arabic- and Persian-derived terms; some Mongolian and Tibetan.
