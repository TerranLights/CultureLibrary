## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North (Yanggao, Datong) against south (Yuncheng, Linfen):** different ritual specialists and mourning lengths to test, not assume.
- **Rural burial against urban cremation (Taiyuan):** funeral reform reached towns first.
- **Hui Muslims:** burial speed, no coffin, and a separate afterlife teaching.
- **Catholic villages and Protestant congregations:** martyr memory and Christian burial.
- **Wutai's Buddhist monastics and Mongol and Tibetan pilgrims:** rebirth-oriented rites for the dead.
- **Coal-mining families and migrant miners:** sudden death at work and compensation.
- **Families of revolutionary martyrs:** official honor and private grief.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical ritual, epitaphs and archaeology; mixed for present grief; thin for suicide by county.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Jin dialect; some Tibetan and Mongolian.
