# Jin (晋): Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi lands), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialects; classical and religious Chinese; Hui, Tibetan and Mongolian religious terms. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, revival, and the return of the dead.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic villages (Taiyuan plain, Changzhi, Yuncheng):** faith inherited by birth, set against Protestant churches built on adult conversion.
- **Buddhist monastics and lay devotees at Wutai; Tibetan and Mongol communities there:** rebirth as doctrine, not metaphor.
- **Daoist and folk-sectarian traditions in the south and center:** karma tales and awakening.
- **Hui Muslims and those who marry in.**
- **Coal cities in transition (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng, Lüliang towns):** economic rebirth told from above and below.
- **Heritage towns against hollowed-out villages:** who gets a second life.
- **People leaving drug use, prison or the sex trade.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for missionary and Daoist sources and official transition plans; thin for personal recovery accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern, classical, religious); Japanese for the returnees' memoirs; Tibetan and Mongolian for Wutai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious groups; compulsory drug treatment; thought reform; reincarnation recognition is politically controlled.
- **Held for review:** none.
