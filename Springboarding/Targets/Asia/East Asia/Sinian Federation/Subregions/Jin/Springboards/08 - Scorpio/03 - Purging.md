## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old revolutionary base villages (Taihang, Shangdang, Lüliang) against areas Yan Xishan held to 1949 (Taiyuan basin):** two histories of who purged whom.
- **Officials and coal-boss families:** the campaign's targets, and their kin.
- **South (Quwo, Linfen):** exorcist ritual still performed; compare with northern bonfire customs.
- **Hui Muslims:** ritual purity on a separate scheme.
- **Buddhists at Wutai; Daoist and folk ritual specialists:** confession and rites for the dead.
- **Families of those purged in the Cultural Revolution:** memory against silence.
- **Polluted industrial towns against rural counties.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for campaign history and religious art; mixed for folk purification; personal accounts of purges are guarded.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); Tibetan for Wutai rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution and land-reform violence; current anti-corruption cases; party archives closed. Discipline and punishment as law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
