## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old resistance base villages (Taihang, Lüliang) against towns the Japanese held:** opposite memories of who collaborated.
- **The north (Daixian, Shuozhou, Datong):** frontier legend of betrayed generals.
- **Merchant counties of Jinzhong:** business defection and the partner who walked away.
- **Labor-migrant households, past (走西口) and present:** the spouse left behind.
- **Catholics:** tension between registered and unregistered communities over who betrayed whom.
- **Cadre and officials' families:** denunciation in campaigns.
- **Hui Muslims:** marriage outside the faith and leaving it, as the community judges them.
- **Opera audiences in the south (Puju) against the center (Jinju):** different repertoires of betrayal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical, opera and war-era sources; thin and guarded for present infidelity and informing.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern, classical); Japanese for occupation and soldiers' accounts; some Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collaboration charges still stain families; church divisions; denunciation memories. Treason as law belongs to Libra.
