# Jin (晋): Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi lands), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialects (with Mongolian loanwords in the north); classical Chinese; Hui community terms. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yan Xishan's core counties (Wutai, Dingxiang, Taiyuan basin) against communist base areas (Taihang, Lüliang):** two models of village control before 1949.
- **Merchant families of Jinzhong:** firms and lineages that ruled employees and heirs at a distance.
- **Southern lineage villages (Wenxi, Yuncheng):** family instructions as control.
- **Couples in coal towns and in migrant-worker households:** who 当家 when one partner is away.
- **Hui congregations:** mosque committees and their reach.
- **Northern Mongol-contact counties:** land enclosure and herding control.
- **Urban Taiyuan against rural counties:** surveillance, schooling and parenting pressure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Yan-era and water records; mixed for household control; thin for coercive control as people describe it.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern and classical); some Mongolian for northern place names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; surveillance; Yan-era and party archives. Legal controls belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
