## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** firm-based trust and guarantors; compare with farming villages nearby.
- **Coal-boom towns (Lüliang, Datong, Jincheng):** trust between acquaintances under fast money.
- **South (Yuncheng, Linfen):** the Guan Yu cult at home, and the oath tradition.
- **Hui Muslims:** trust networks in food and trade.
- **Catholic and Protestant congregations:** trust among believers and toward the state.
- **Rural elders and urban young people:** trust in doctors, vaccines, apps and strangers.
- **Comparison only (diaspora):** Shanxi merchant guild halls in other cities.
