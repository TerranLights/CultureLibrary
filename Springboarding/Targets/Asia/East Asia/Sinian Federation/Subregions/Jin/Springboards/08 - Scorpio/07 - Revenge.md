## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yu county and the Taihang east:** Orphan of Zhao cult beside wartime memory of occupation.
- **Taiyuan basin (Jinci, Chiqiao):** classical revenge placed in local legend.
- **Hongtong, Zhaocheng and other canal villages:** feud over shared water.
- **Lüliang and the northwest (old Jin-Sui area):** land-reform reckoning and its memory.
- **Coal villages:** grievance against officials and bosses.
- **Hui Muslims:** asking pardon as a practice; check for separate feud norms.
- **Buddhist and Daoist devotees:** teaching against the cycle.
- **Women survivors of wartime violence and their families.**
