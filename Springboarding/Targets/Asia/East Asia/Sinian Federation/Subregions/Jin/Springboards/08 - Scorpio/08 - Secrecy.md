## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** commercial secrecy and hidden wealth.
- **Coal counties (Hongtong, Huozhou, Lüliang):** concealment of deaths and labor abuse.
- **Taihang counties (Zuoquan, Wuxiang):** hidden courtship stories and wartime clandestine networks.
- **Northern villages (Yanbei, Datong):** open secrets in small places.
- **Speakers of Jin argots against Mandarin-dominant cities.**
- **Wutai monastics:** teachings given only to the initiated.
- **Catholic communities:** some worship outside registration.
- **Families shaped by hidden births and adoptions.**
- **Hui Muslims:** whether disputes and family matters are kept inside the congregation, as a question to test.
