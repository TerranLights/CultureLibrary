## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South (Yuncheng, Linfen):** the core of the 1876–79 famine and of historic earthquakes.
- **North (Datong, Yanggao, Shuozhou):** frontier drought, the 1989 earthquake, coal-town downturn.
- **Coal counties in the center and west (Xiangning, Lüliang, Gujiao):** mine disasters and subsidence.
- **Old war zones (Xinkou, Yangquan, Taihang):** the war as family memory.
- **Hui congregations:** relief through their own giving.
- **Catholic and Protestant communities:** missionary-era famine relief and later memory.
- **Laid-off state-enterprise workers against private miners and migrant laborers.**
- **Comparison only (diaspora):** famine migrants who settled beyond the Great Wall (走西口).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1870s famine, earthquakes and war; mixed for recent disasters; thin and guarded for the 1959–61 famine and for personal crisis.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (modern, classical); English for missionary and relief records; some Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Great Famine; mine-disaster death tolls; mental-health records. Emergency law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
