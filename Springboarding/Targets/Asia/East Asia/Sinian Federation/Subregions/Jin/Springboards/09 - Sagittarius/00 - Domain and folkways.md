# Jin 晋: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; its lands are the pre-war province of Shanxi 山西. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (simplified script); the Jin 晋语 dialect group (Bingzhou 并州, Lüliang 吕梁, Shangdang 上党, Wutai 五台 and Da-Bao 大包 subgroups); Zhongyuan Mandarin Fen-He 汾河 dialects of the south; Literary Chinese for older sources. **Concept in one line:** the domain of belief and philosophy (religion, philosophy, higher learning, long journeys), asking what is true and where meaning is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei 晋北 (Datong, Shuozhou):** frontier along the Great Wall, Northern Wei and Liao-Jin heritage, cave temples.
- **Wutai mountain district:** a monastic economy with Chinese-rite (青庙) and Tibetan-rite (黄庙) houses side by side.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Jiexiu):** merchant patronage of temples and early mission stations.
- **Taiyuan:** state, universities, the Catholic cathedral, Hui mosques.
- **Lüliang uplands and the Yellow River counties (Hequ, Baode, Pianguan):** poorer, migration-shaped, wartime base areas.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** its own dialect subgroup and the sai festival tradition.
- **Jinnan (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speech, sites tied to Yao, Shun, Yu and Guan Yu.
- **Hui, Manchu and Mongol residents; Catholic villages and Protestant congregations; lehu descendants; Tibetan and Mongol clergy at Wutai.** Separate each: they hold different religious calendars and specialists.
- **Comparison only:** Zou Xikou descendants in Inner Mongolia and Jin merchant guild communities elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temples, epigraphy and ritual ethnography; thin and filtered for present-day affiliation counts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Literary Chinese, Jin dialect fieldwork, Tibetan for Wutai; Italian and Latin for Franciscan mission records *check*; Japanese for wartime surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered Catholic and Protestant groups; banned movements; the 1900 Boxer killings in Taiyuan and their contested memory; interviewee safety. The member state's lands (Shanxi) are a working list the author still confirms.
- **Held for review:** none.
