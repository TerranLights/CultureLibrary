# Jin 晋: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi lands), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin dialects and southern Fen-He Mandarin for spoken usage; Literary Chinese; Tibetan for the Wutai monasteries; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic villages of the Taiyuan plain and the Changzhi and Yuncheng dioceses:** faith held as a hereditary community identity; compare open and unregistered communities.
- **Protestants of Linfen and Taigu:** converts' lines from missionary work; a different idea of personal conversion.
- **Wutai pilgrims and resident clergy, Chinese-rite and Tibetan-rite:** faith shown by vows and climbs, by monks and by visitors.
- **Jinnan villages around Guandi and Houtu shrines:** efficacy-centered devotion to local gods.
- **Hui of Changzhi, Taiyuan and Datong:** daily prayer and fasting within a Han majority.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** faith bound to trade, guild halls and lineage halls.
- **Lüliang and Taihang former base areas:** the secular faith of the revolution and its fading.
- **Young urban Taiyuan residents:** the faith question asked by people with little religious upbringing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for missions and temple cults, thin and politically filtered for current practice.
