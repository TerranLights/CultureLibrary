# Jin 晋: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, the Shanxi lands of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin dialect speech (where 甚 shèn serves as "what"); southern Fen-He Mandarin; Literary Chinese for family instructions and famine records. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal-town families in Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi and Jincheng:** meaning tied to a single industry's boom and decline.
- **Jinzhong merchant lineages and their descendants:** legacy carried by houses, firms and genealogies.
- **Lüliang and Yellow River upland villages:** meaning under poverty and out-migration.
- **Jinnan farming counties (Linfen, Yuncheng):** famine memory and the agricultural year.
- **Wutai monastics and lay devotees:** vocation outside the family line.
- **Hui families:** an afterlife frame and congregational remembrance.
- **Catholic villagers:** suffering read through a Christian story.
- **Young Taiyuan graduates and county-town youth who left:** purpose sought in cities beyond the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong in literature, film and famine history; no direct survey item on meaning for the province.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Jin dialect for interviews, Literary Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data are sensitive and patchy; Great Leap famine memory is restricted; mine accidents and closures.
- **Held for review:** none.
