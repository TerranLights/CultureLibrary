# Jin 晋: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin and southern Mandarin dialects for oral history; Literary Chinese and Qing archival language for status emancipation; Tibetan for Buddhist "liberation"; Japanese for occupation-era records. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shangdang villages (Changzhi, Lucheng):** land-reform "turning over" as the founding meaning of liberation.
- **Taihang former base-area counties (Wuxiang, Zuoquan, Licheng):** wartime mobilization and marriage reform.
- **Taiyuan:** a city "liberated" after a long siege; two competing memories.
- **Lehu descendants and other formerly debased groups:** release from status and what remained of the stigma.
- **Women of north Shanxi (Datong, Yuxian):** footbinding's end and wartime captivity; their own testimony.
- **Coal-region migrant miners:** freedom of movement and exit.
- **Catholic, Protestant and Hui communities; Tibetan-rite monks at Wutai:** freedom of worship as each experiences it.
- **Wutai lay Buddhists:** release as inner, not political, freedom.

## 4. Flags
