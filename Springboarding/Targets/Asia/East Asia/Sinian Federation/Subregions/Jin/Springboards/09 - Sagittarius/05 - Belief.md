# Jin 晋: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (the Shanxi lands), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin dialects and southern Mandarin for folk-belief vocabulary; Literary Chinese for dynastic treatises; Tibetan for Wutai; Hui religious terms. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and the warrant people want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong and the north (Jinbei):** belief shaped by Northern Wei, Liao and Jin monuments and frontier mixing.
- **Hunyuan and Hengshan:** Daoist sacred-mountain belief and three-teachings temples.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** siting, almanac and lineage observance in compound houses.
- **Lüliang villages:** folk belief and spirit mediums under thin state presence.
- **Shangdang villages:** festival gods and village-wide observance.
- **Jinnan farm counties:** rain gods, Houtu and harvest omens.
- **Hui congregations:** articles of faith learned in mosque schools.
- **Catholic villages:** doctrine taught through catechism and seminary.
- **Tibetan-rite monks and Mongol pilgrims at Wutai:** recognized rebirth.
- **Party members and cadres:** political belief as a duty of office.
- **Students and young urban adults:** belief and doubt shaped by schooling and the internet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on folk belief (folk-literature collections, temples, steles); thin on present-day private belief, which survey respondents may under-report.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Literary Chinese, Jin dialect, Tibetan, Arabic and Persian loanwords in Hui speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" is a state label, so informants may hide practice; mediums can face pressure; reincarnation recognition is politically controlled.
- **Held for review:** none.
