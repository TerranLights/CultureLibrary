## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiexiu and Jinzhong:** a local cult of principled refusal kept in the calendar.
- **Yuxian and the north-central hills:** the Orphan of Zhao shrine; also wartime survivors.
- **Wenshui and the Lüliang edge:** revolutionary martyrdom taught to schoolchildren.
- **Taiyuan:** two memories of 1949, Communist and Nationalist.
- **Pingshun and the Taihang villages:** model loyalty to the collective line.
- **Catholic communities of the Taiyuan diocese:** martyr memory.
- **Hui congregations:** holding to religious practice in a Han majority.
- **Petitioners and their families:** persistence outside official channels.
- **Comparison only:** descendants of Nationalist officials in Taiwan and the Japanese veterans' families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exemplars, memorials and classical stories; thin for measured conviction today.
