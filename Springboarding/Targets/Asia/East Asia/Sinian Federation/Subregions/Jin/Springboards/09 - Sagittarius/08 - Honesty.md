*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi lands), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin dialect and southern Mandarin for market and village speech; Literary and documentary Chinese for merchant-house rules; Hui usage for halal trade. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant towns (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Yuci):** a tradition of written firm rules and reputation.
- **Qingxu and the vinegar trade:** honesty in a branded local product.
- **Coal counties:** contracts, safety records and payments in a cash-heavy industry.
- **Taiyuan's officials and offices:** clean government campaigns.
- **Lishi and Lüliang:** the home district of the upright-official exemplar.
- **Village accountants and cadres:** collective-era and present-day accounts.
- **Hui traders:** halal trust within and beyond the congregation.
- **Antique dealers and tourist-trade sellers:** fakes, reproductions and their labeling.
- **Students:** honesty in exams (see Education).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich merchant documents and official corruption reporting; little measured everyday honesty.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, documentary and Literary Chinese, Jin dialect.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases are politically framed; avoid any claim that a people is honest or dishonest; credit blacklists affect named people. Law and courts belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
