*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Jin 晋, a member state of the Sinian Federation on Shanxi's lands, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Jin dialects for oral histories of schooling; Literary Chinese for academies and examination records; English for missionary-founded schools; Tibetan and Hui terms for religious schooling. **Concept in one line:** formal and higher learning (schools, universities, degrees, scholarship and lifelong study) and the standing of the educated person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan (Linfen, Yuncheng):** old examination counties and the Wang Tong memory.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** learning paired with trade; missionary and early modern schools.
- **Taiyuan:** universities and the provincial gatekeepers of exams.
- **Lüliang and Taihang uplands:** literacy drives, school mergers, boarding pupils.
- **Coal cities (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi):** technical and mining colleges; whether coal wealth bought schooling.
- **Hui families:** mosque schooling beside state schools.
- **Monks and nuns at Wutai:** a separate ladder of learning.
- **First-generation students from county towns:** leaving the province for study.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; examination records, school histories, yearbooks and diaries.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Literary Chinese, English for missionary records, Tibetan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Boxer-indemnity origins; the Cultural Revolution's closure of universities; religious schooling of minors is restricted; exam-fraud cases.
- **Held for review:** none.
