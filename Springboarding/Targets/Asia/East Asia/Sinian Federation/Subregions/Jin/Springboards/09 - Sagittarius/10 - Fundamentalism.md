# Jin 晋: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Literary Chinese for Northern Wei, Daoist and Qing sources; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loanwords); Jin dialect for oral history. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong (Pingcheng):** the Northern Wei purification and its stone legacy.
- **Taiyuan and the Jinzhong mission counties:** 1900 and its two memories.
- **Hui congregations of Taiyuan, Datong and Changzhi:** old-teaching and reform currents, each in its own words.
- **Catholic communities, registered and not:** loyalty to Rome and to older forms of worship.
- **Protestant house churches:** literal reading and separation from "the world".
- **Wutai's strict monastics:** observance against commerce.
- **North Shanxi villages with sectarian pasts:** the old teachings and their suppression.
- **Former Red Guards and cadres:** secular dogmatism as they remember it.
