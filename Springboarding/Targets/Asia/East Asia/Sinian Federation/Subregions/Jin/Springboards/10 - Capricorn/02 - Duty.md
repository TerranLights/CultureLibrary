# Jin: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), one member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (oath texts, plays, family instructions); Jin dialects for folk song and oral sources; Zhongyuan Mandarin in the south; Hui Islamic vocabulary; some Mongolian for the northern border. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei and Hequ-Baode:** a long history of men leaving to work beyond the passes; whom the duty to provide fell on.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** apprentices away for years; duties to the firm against duties at home.
- **Shangdang:** rotating temple-society service and model collective villages.
- **Lüliang:** wartime mobilization and martyrs; duty as the state later narrates it.
- **Jinnan:** the Guan Yu cult's home and older scholar families' instructions.
- **Hui Muslims:** religious duties beside family and civic ones.
- **Catholic villages:** duties to church and parish beside lineage duties.
- **Rural elders left behind** (留守老人) and their migrant children in cities.
- **Women:** who carries care for parents-in-law, to research by region and generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in legends, oaths and opera; good survey data on elder care; collective-era records exist but are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for oath texts and plays; Jin dialect for songs and oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr cults are state-curated; conscription records closed; religious duties need care.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read a translation or study of the Houma covenant texts for oath formulas and penalties.

4. Filter CHARLS for Shanxi households on care and money given to parents.
5. Search CNKI for 赛社 社首 轮值 山西 on rotating village service.
