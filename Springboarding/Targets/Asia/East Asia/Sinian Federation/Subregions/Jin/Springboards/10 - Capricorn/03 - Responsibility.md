## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mining districts** (Lüliang, Linfen, Datong, Jincheng): accountability after accidents; owners, managers and local officials as separate groups.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** an old business order of liability and guarantors; whether family firms recall it.
- **Xiyang and the Dazhai area:** the model brigade's home, set against counties that decollectivized early.
- **Southern Hedong:** the home of exemplary officials; how local memory frames them.
- **Rural households with migrant parents:** children and grandparents carrying the load.
- **Migrant miners from other provinces:** who answers for workers far from their kin.
- **Women and men:** who answers for household money, children and parents-in-law, by region and generation.
- **Officials and ordinary residents:** how each speaks of blame for pollution.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official accountability after disasters; mixed on households; the household responsibility system's local history needs digging.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral history in mining villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mine deaths, forced labor and dismissals of officials; families of victims need care; some reports withdrawn.
- **Held for review:** none.
