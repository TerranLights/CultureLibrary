## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** firm and family rules for apprentices; a martial-arts heartland.
- **Hedong (Yuncheng, Hejin):** scholarly self-cultivation traditions and exam-oriented schools.
- **Wutai and Xinzhou:** monastic discipline, Han and Tibetan lines, monks and nuns.
- **Coal districts** (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng, Lüliang): safety drill, shift work and fines.
- **Xiyang and other model-labor villages:** the memory of collective work discipline.
- **Opera communities:** troupe life and its training ways, by genre (Jinju, Puju, Beilu, Shangdang).
- **Hui Muslims:** fasting and prayer disciplines.
- **Urban and rural parents:** how children are corrected, and how this has shifted with one-child and later families.
- **County boarding students** set against city day-school students.
