## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong** (Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu, Yuci): a merchant past and its revival campaigns; family ambition through trade.
- **Hedong** (Yuncheng, Linfen): examination families and officials; ambition through study.
- **Coal-boom counties** (Lüliang, Linfen, Datong, Jincheng): sudden private wealth and its displays.
- **Fenyang and Datong youth:** small-city ambition and leaving, as film and fiction frame it.
- **Poor upland counties** of Lüliang and the Taihang: schooling and migration as ladders.
- **Xiyang and model villages:** the politics of rising through the collective.
- **Women:** how a woman's rise is spoken of, from the empress of Wenshui to today's graduates.
- **Hui trading families** *check*: business ambition inside a minority community.
- **Generations:** reform-era strivers against young people who speak of lying flat.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Shanxi merchant houses once in Baotou, Hohhot and Kyakhta.
