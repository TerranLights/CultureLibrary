## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Datong and the northern garrison towns:** hereditary military households, princely seat and later mining towns.
- **Jinzhong compound counties:** masters, wives, concubines, servants and shop hands.
- **Hedong lineages:** written genealogies and generation names; ancestral halls.
- **State work units** (steel, coal, rail): cadre and worker grades.
- **Coal-boom counties:** private owners, managers and migrant miners.
- **Rural and urban residents:** rank by household registration.
- **Wutai Shan:** Han and Tibetan-Mongolian monastic ranks.
- **Hui congregations:** religious leaders, elders and members.
- **Manchu descendants in Taiyuan** *check*: memory of banner rank.
- **Women:** rank by birth order, marriage and the generation of in-laws.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in archaeology, temple art, merchant history and statistics; genealogies plentiful but scattered; danwei records partly closed.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for address forms; Tibetan, Mongolian and Manchu for Qing religious and banner sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class labels from land reform (地主, 富农) still matter to families; hukou and migrant workers' status.
- **Held for review:** none.
