# Jin: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), within the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for Jin and Wei texts and examination records; Jin dialects for oral accounts in firms, troupes and villages; Zhongyuan Mandarin in the Fen valley south; Hui mosque-school vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedong counties** (Yuncheng, Linfen): many old examination graduates; schooling as the proof of worth.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** an old system of shares for performance; whether family firms today copy it.
- **Lüliang and coal districts:** cases of bought office; how residents judge appointments.
- **State firms** in Taiyuan, Datong, Yangquan: seniority against performance pay.
- **Rural students** under quota policies, set against city students.
- **Opera troupes and craft workshops:** merit judged by masters, juries and the state.
- **Hui mosque students:** religious learning and its ranks.
- **Women:** equal pay for equal work claims in collective-era villages, and today's hiring.
- **Young exam-takers** for state jobs, against those who choose private work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for examination history and the merchant firms; mixed for survey data; corruption cases well reported but partial.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for interviews in firms and troupes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bought office and exam fraud; named officials; families of the accused.
- **Held for review:** none.
