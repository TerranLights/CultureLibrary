# Jin: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for Zhou-era and imperial sources; Jin dialects for opera lyrics and village testimony; Zhongyuan Mandarin in Hongdong, Linfen and Yuncheng; Hui Islamic terms for religious law. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law; the letter goes to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Water-sharing villages** of the Fen valley (Hongdong, Zhaocheng, Jinci): old customary rules, still remembered.
- **Coal districts** (Lüliang, Linfen, Datong): where state rules on mining were most openly bent.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** contracts, guild arbitration and reputation as older private order.
- **Villages hit by the anti-gang campaign:** local strongmen against the state.
- **Hui families:** religious and state family law.
- **Wutai Shan monasteries and Catholic parishes:** religious rule beside state rule.
- **Urban residents** who go to court, against villagers who settle privately; a split to test, not assume.
- **Migrant workers** owed wages: law as a tool or as a closed door.
- **Women** in divorce and inheritance disputes, by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern legal thought and water customs; mixed for court practice since 2021; strong press record on mining.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese legal register; Jin dialect for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detentions, petitioners, anti-gang cases and religious regulation; court data being withdrawn; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none. Libra handles the statutes as written.
