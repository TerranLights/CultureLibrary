# Jin: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for histories, temple inscriptions and almanacs; Jin dialects for festival and household vocabulary; Zhongyuan Mandarin around Linfen and Yuncheng; Hui usage for neighborhood life around mosques. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong and Qinshui fortified-village areas:** a memory of self-defense and village walls.
- **Taiyuan and other prefectural cities:** grid management and civilized-city campaigns.
- **Linfen and the south:** festival drumming and crowd traditions; Zhongyuan Mandarin speech.
- **Datong and the north:** frontier walls and rebuilt city walls; ordered space as display.
- **Coal and subsidence districts:** land and towns in disorder; resettlement.
- **Pingyao:** a heritage town ordered for tourism.
- **Catholic villages** with memories of 1900.
- **Hui neighborhoods:** daily order around mosques and halal food.
- **Upland Lüliang villages** emptied by migration.
- **Wutai and Hengshan:** religious accounts of cosmic order.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical history, temples, festivals and architecture; thin and sensitive for recent unrest.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for festival and household words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Cultural Revolution, lockdowns and stability work; protest is hard to research; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
