# Jin: Community and ideals, domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (the lands of Shanxi 山西), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (Putonghua 普通话; simplified script, traditional for pre-1950s print); the Jin 晋语 dialects of the center and north (Bingzhou 并州片, Lüliang 吕梁片, Shangdang 上党片, Wutai 五台片, Da-Bao 大包片 *check* the subgroup names); Zhongyuan Mandarin of the lower Fen valley (Fenhe 汾河片) in the south; Classical Chinese for steles and gazetteers; the Islamic vocabulary of Hui speech. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what they hope for: friends, groups, hopes and humanitarian ideals.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinbei 晋北 (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou):** old frontier garrisons and horse markets with Mongol neighbors; the Da-Bao and Wutai dialect zones.
- **Jinzhong 晋中 (Taiyuan basin, Pingyao, Qixian, Taigu):** merchant towns and compound-lineages; the Bingzhou dialect heartland.
- **Lüliang mountains and the Yellow River bank (Lishi, Hequ, Linxian):** poor upland cave villages and the main Zouxikou outflow.
- **Shangdang 上党 (Changzhi, Jincheng):** the saishe ritual zone and the Taihang revolutionary base.
- **Jinnan 晋南 (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Mandarin-speaking south, oldest farming plain, the Big Locust Tree story.
- **Hui communities (Taiyuan, Datong, Changzhi *check*):** mosque-centered groups with their own giving and gathering.
- **Catholic and Protestant villages:** congregations as a whole social world.
- **Coal-mining towns and state compounds (Datong, Yangquan):** work units as communities.
- **Small Mongol and Manchu populations:** *check* the census counts.

## 4. Flags
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists Shanxi alone, a working list the author still confirms in Stage 0; this sign's sheets are written for Shanxi's lands.
- **Data:** rich for ritual, merchant and collective-era history; mixed for present-day associational life; thin for minorities.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious groups, political protest and some collective-era archives are restricted; protect interviewees.
