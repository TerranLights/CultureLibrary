*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin; Jin 晋语 dialects (center and north); Fenhe Zhongyuan Mandarin (Linfen, Yuncheng); Classical Chinese for steles; Hui mosque vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Irrigation villages of the Fen valley (Hongdong, Jiexiu, Taiyuan's Jinci springs):** communities defined by a shared water source.
- **Mountain villages of Lüliang and the Taihang:** small, scattered, now emptied or relocated; community under depopulation.
- **Coal-town and work-unit communities (Datong, Yangquan, Changzhi):** membership through the mine rather than the lineage.
- **Hui congregations:** a community bounded by faith and food inside Han towns.
- **Catholic villages around Taiyuan and in the south:** parish and village as one body.
- **Heritage towns (Pingyao, Qixian):** old residents against tourists and incomers.
- **Jinnan plain villages:** larger, older settlements in the Mandarin-speaking south.
- **New migrant neighborhoods in Taiyuan:** rural incomers without a village around them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for irrigation and ritual communities, collective-era villages and literary portraits; mixed for urban neighborhoods; thin for Hui and Christian communities in Chinese-language scholarship.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese (steles), dialect for oral interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation is politically framed; Christian and Hui community life touches regulated religion; land subsidence tied to mining companies.
- **Held for review:** none.
