# Jin: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin; Jin 晋语 (Bingzhou, Lüliang, Shangdang, Wutai, Da-Bao subgroups); Fenhe Mandarin in the south; Classical and Qing business Chinese for merchant house rules; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how the person is weighed against the group, where the line between "us" and "them" falls, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinzhong merchant counties (Qixian, Taigu, Pingyao, Jiexiu):** business groups built on kin, native place and shares.
- **Old base-area counties (Wuxiang, Lüliang, Wutai):** militia and party cells as the remembered model group.
- **Mining towns:** crews where the group carries shared risk.
- **Shangdang villages:** ritual roles passed among households.
- **Linfen and Yuncheng drum villages:** the performing team as village emblem.
- **Hui congregations:** divisions between schools of Islam inside one minority.
- **Catholic villages:** a religious in-group with long boundaries against neighbors.
- **Taiyuan's university students:** native-place associations 老乡会 as a first group away from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant firms and wartime groups; mixed for present-day voluntary groups; thin for gangs and informal crews.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Qing business and Classical Chinese, Jin dialect for oral work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution factionalism, unregistered religious groups, organized crime and coal-business networks; anonymize.
- **Held for review:** none.
