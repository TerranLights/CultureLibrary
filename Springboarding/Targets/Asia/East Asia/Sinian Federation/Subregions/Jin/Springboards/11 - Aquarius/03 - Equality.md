## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shangdang villages (Changzhi, Pingshun, Xiyang):** the land-reform and collective models of equality.
- **Coal counties (Datong, Shuozhou, Lüliang's Liulin and Xiaoyi):** sharp new wealth beside farm poverty.
- **Jinzhong merchant towns:** old merchant wealth and its inheritors; rank between master and staff.
- **Mountain Lüliang and Taihang villages:** the poorest; equality of opportunity seen from the bottom.
- **Jinnan plain:** larger landholding villages; land-reform memories may differ.
- **Women in high bride-price counties:** equality between sexes judged through marriage payments.
- **Hui residents:** equal citizenship and minority standing; *check* census counts.
- **Rural migrants in Taiyuan:** household registration (hukou) as a line of unequal access.
