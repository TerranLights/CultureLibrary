## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinnan (Linfen, Yuncheng):** the land of the sage-king legends and of Hedong Confucian teachers.
- **Old Taihang and Lüliang base areas:** revolutionary ideals kept in museums and family memory.
- **Jinzhong merchant counties:** an ethic of trustworthy trade, and the question of how ideal it was.
- **Taigu and Fenzhou mission-school graduates:** an imported, Christian or liberal idealism.
- **Reform-era county towns like Fenyang:** the young who left for cities and how they speak of ideals.
- **Rural teachers and village officials in poor counties:** idealism in daily work.
- **Hui believers:** ideals of a righteous community of faith.
- **People who made or lost money in the coal boom:** how fast wealth and its loss are talked about beside ideals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and classical ideals; mixed for ordinary people's ideals; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, English for Oberlin archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary martyr stories are protected by official memory; criticism of them is restricted. Conviction and fundamentalism belong to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
