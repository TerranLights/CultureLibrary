# Jin: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin; Jin 晋语 subgroups (folk-song and everyday address terms differ between the north, center and southeast); Fenhe Mandarin in the south; Classical Chinese (the *Classic of Poetry*, Tang verse); Hui speech with Persian and Arabic loanwords. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yuncheng and Linfen (Hedong):** Guan Yu's home ground and the center of his cult; test how sworn loyalty is spoken of there.
- **Northwest Jin (Hequ, Baode, Pianguan):** a song culture where friend and sweetheart share words.
- **Taihang counties (Zuoquan, Wuxiang):** wartime comradeship and kaihua song culture.
- **Jinzhong county towns (Fenyang, Pingyao):** reform-era male friendship groups on film.
- **Coal and factory towns:** workmates as friends; shared danger.
- **Hui neighbors:** friendship with dietary and religious boundaries.
- **Women's friendships in villages:** less documented; sewing circles, temple-going companions *check*.
- **Students and young migrants in Taiyuan:** friends made away from home, and online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and ritual friendship; mixed for everyday adult friendship; thin for women's friendship and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Jin dialect for songs and sketches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sworn brotherhoods can shade into gangs; drinking pressure and favors among friends touch on corruption. Betrayal goes to Scorpio, reciprocity to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
