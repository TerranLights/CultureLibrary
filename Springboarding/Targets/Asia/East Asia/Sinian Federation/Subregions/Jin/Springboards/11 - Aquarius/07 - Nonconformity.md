## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiyuan literati and loyalists (Fu Shan's circle):** dissent through dress, style and refusal of office.
- **Hedong (Hejin, Yuncheng):** an older line of drinker-eccentrics and recluse poets.
- **Catholic villages near Taiyuan and in the south:** a faith minority with a memory of persecution.
- **Followers of folk sects:** religious difference outside both state and mainstream temples.
- **Festival performers in Jinzhong and the north:** licensed transgression at New Year.
- **Lüliang and Taihang villages:** where outsiders (sent-down youth, cadres) met local norms.
- **Taiyuan's young people:** music scenes, online subcultures, gender and sexual minorities.
- **Women who refused binding or arranged marriage:** nonconformity by sex, in two directions over a century.
- **Hui residents:** headscarves, caps and food rules that set them apart in Han towns, and younger Hui who depart from their own elders' norms.
