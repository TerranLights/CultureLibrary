## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wutai Shan and Hengshan religious communities:** detachment as a full way of life, Chinese-rite and Tibetan-rite side by side.
- **Jiexiu and Mianshan:** the festival town of the withdrawn courtier.
- **Southern Shanxi (Yongji, Ruicheng, Linfen):** recluse legends and Daoist renunciation sites.
- **Xinzhou literati memory:** loyalist withdrawal after dynastic collapse.
- **Hollowed villages of Lüliang and Xinzhou:** distance forced by out-migration, not chosen.
- **Taiyuan's young adults:** chosen disengagement from competition online.
- **Mining families:** composure under constant risk.
- **Hui families:** different norms for grief and for reliance on God.
