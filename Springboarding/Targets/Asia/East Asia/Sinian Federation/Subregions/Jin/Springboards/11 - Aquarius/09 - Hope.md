## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiyuan basin temple-goers (Jinci, Jiaocheng):** vow-and-repay hope, Buddhist and popular.
- **Dry northern counties (Youyu, Datong, Shuozhou):** hope tied to rain, trees and land reclamation.
- **Datong residents:** a coal city's hopes for renewal, and those moved out for it.
- **Yangquan and other power and coal towns:** engineers' futures, from plant floor to science fiction.
- **Southern plain (Linfen, Yuncheng):** New Year print and harvest-wish traditions; old farming hopes.
- **Mountain families of Lüliang and the Taihang:** hope pinned on schooling and on children who leave.
- **Hui families:** supplication and hope within Islam, beside Han neighbors' temple vows.
- **Pure Land devotees and monks:** hope for the next life rather than this one.
