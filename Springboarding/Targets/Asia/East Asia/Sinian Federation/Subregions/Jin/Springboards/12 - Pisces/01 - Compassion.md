**Target:** Jin 晋 (lands: Shanxi 山西), one member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect terms (central, northern, Lüliang and Shangdang areas) and southern Shanxi Zhongyuan Mandarin; Hui Muslim and Catholic religious vocabulary; Classical Chinese for steles and gazetteers. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Shanxi merchant towns (Pingyao, Qi, Taigu, Jiexiu):** merchant philanthropy and its legends; compare with ordinary villagers' giving.
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers and famine memory in the Fen valley.
- **Lüliang hills and the Yellow River counties:** long poverty and out-migration (走西口); compassion received as well as given.
- **Northern frontier districts (Datong, Shuozhou, Xinzhou):** Buddhist charity around Wutaishan; steppe-edge history.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** temple-centered village communities; relief through shrine associations.
- **Hui Muslims:** almsgiving through mosques; **Catholic villages:** parish charity and the works of mercy; **Buddhist lay associations**.
- **Coal-mining families:** mutual help after accidents; **migrant workers** in mining towns as recipients of pity or suspicion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1870s famine and Qing local charity; mixed for present-day giving; national survey figures with thin provincial breakdowns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for gazetteers and steles; dialect knowledge for oral accounts of begging and help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine memory includes cannibalism accounts (handle with care); the brick-kiln case and mine disasters touch official responsibility; religious charity may be reported cautiously. Charity regulation belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
