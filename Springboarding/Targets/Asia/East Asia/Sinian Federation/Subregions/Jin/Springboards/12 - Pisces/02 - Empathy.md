**Target:** Jin 晋, Sinian Federation member state covering Shanxi 山西, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, including newer psychology vocabulary; Jin dialect colloquial speech (Bingzhou, Wutai, Da-Bao, Lüliang and Shangdang subgroups); southern Shanxi Zhongyuan Mandarin; Classical Chinese for the ethical canon. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taiyuan and Jinzhong cities against rural Lüliang and Xinzhou counties:** psychology vocabulary (共情) against older idiom (将心比心).
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speech and the Puju theater audience.
- **Shangdang (Changzhi, Jincheng):** funeral music and village ritual as group feeling.
- **Northern Shanxi (Datong, Shuozhou):** Da-Bao Jin speakers; frontier and coal towns.
- **Hui Muslims and Han neighbors:** empathy across group lines in mixed districts.
- **Catholic villages:** confession and pastoral listening as a separate practice.
- **Left-behind children and elders** in out-migration counties; **coal-mine families** facing loss; **young urban professionals** using EQ language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; psychology studies exist but are often small student samples; cultural material (opera, funeral customs) is plentiful but must be read for empathy rather than assumed to show it.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Jin dialect for oral and online material; Classical Chinese for the ethical canon.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** doctor-patient violence is a charged topic; funeral customs vary by family and should not be generalized.
- **Held for review:** none.
