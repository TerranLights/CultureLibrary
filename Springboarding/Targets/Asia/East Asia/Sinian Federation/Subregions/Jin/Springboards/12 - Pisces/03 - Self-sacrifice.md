# Jin: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋 (Shanxi 山西), a Sinian Federation member state in the East Asia region. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (the Jin state chronicles, gazetteers, memorial arches); Jin dialect speech of the central basin, the Lüliang and Taihang uplands and the north; southern Zhongyuan Mandarin; Hui and Catholic devotional usage. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiexiu and Jinzhong:** the Jie Zitui cult as a local identity; merchant households with long-absent men.
- **Taihang southeast (Changzhi, Jincheng, Wuxiang, Zuoquan):** revolutionary base memory and war sacrifice.
- **Xiyang and Dazhai:** the collective-era model and families' memories of its cost.
- **Northern Shanxi (Datong, Shuozhou, Youyu):** frontier hardship, afforestation and coal towns.
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers; Guan Yu's loyalty cult as another frame.
- **Women and men separately:** chaste-widow honors and mothers' self-denial against soldiers' and miners' risk.
- **Catholics** (martyr memory), **Hui** (fasting), **Buddhists** (body-giving traditions), **mining families**, **left-behind grandparents** raising grandchildren.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and classical exemplars; mixed for everyday family sacrifice; many sources are propaganda or hagiography and need reading against each other.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for oral history; Latin and Italian for some Catholic martyr records *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary martyrs are officially protected from "defamation," so critical sources may be scarce; 1900 killings and wartime memory are charged; chaste-widow suicides must be handled carefully.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Jie Zitui passages in *Zuozhuan* and *Shiji*, then search 介休 寒食节 非遗 for the present festival.
2. Search digitized Shanxi gazetteers for 割股 and 节妇 and count entries by county.
3. Search 刘胡兰 纪念馆 and 文水 for exhibition texts and school-visit materials.
4. Search CNKI for 大寨 精神 回忆 and 铁姑娘 口述 for oral histories.
5. Check the CHARLS documentation for province codes and caregiving modules.
