# Jin: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, whose lands are Shanxi 山西, within the Sinian Federation (East Asia region). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for Ming frontier records and deeds; Jin dialect speech, whose own boundary with southern Zhongyuan Mandarin is a subject here; Hui Chinese usage around purity. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern frontier counties (Datong, Shuozhou, Youyu, Pianguan):** life along the wall; mixed Han-Mongol history.
- **Central basin merchant towns (Pingyao, Qi, Taigu, Lingshi):** walled cities and courtyard compounds.
- **Southeastern Qinshui-valley and Shangdang villages:** castle-villages and the tight village boundary.
- **Loess cave-dwelling areas (Lüliang, Linfen hills):** yards and cliff homes with different public-private lines.
- **Jin speakers against Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers** across the Linfen basin.
- **Hui and Han** neighbors; **Catholic villages** with their own marriage circles; **migrants from other provinces** in coal towns; **urban gated-compound residents** against villagers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for walls, houses and frontier history; mixed for interpersonal privacy and family boundaries (mostly national survey data and online talk).
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for local sayings about neighbors and outsiders; Mongolian for frontier-side sources *check*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Han-Hui lines can be politically sensitive; family-conflict forums are sometimes censored. Property law belongs to Libra; deeds here are read for how edges were drawn.
- **Held for review:** none.
