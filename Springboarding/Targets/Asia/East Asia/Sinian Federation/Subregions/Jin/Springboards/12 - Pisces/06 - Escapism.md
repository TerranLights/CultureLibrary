## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal cities (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng, Xiaoyi):** boom-and-bust wealth, nightlife and drinking among miners and owners.
- **Taiyuan:** urban youth, white-collar workers, gaming and short trips.
- **Fenyang and the Fen valley:** liquor-making towns where drinking is also an industry.
- **Lüliang and northwest uplands:** festive 闹红火 traditions, out-migration as escape.
- **Southern Shanxi (Linfen, Yuncheng):** Zhongyuan Mandarin areas; opera and temple-fair leisure.
- **Hui Muslims** (abstention from alcohol as a contrast); **Buddhist and Daoist retreatants** at Wutaishan and Hengshan; **students and young migrants**; **older men's teahouse and card-table circles**; **women's escapes** (dance squares 广场舞, short-video apps) to study separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for drink culture, opium history and media; thin for provincial-level drug and gambling statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and internet slang; Jin dialect for festival vocabulary; Classical Chinese for poetry and Republican documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and gambling are illegal and data are partial; internet-addiction camps involved reported abuse; coal-boss coverage can slide into stereotype, so name variation. Drug enforcement belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
