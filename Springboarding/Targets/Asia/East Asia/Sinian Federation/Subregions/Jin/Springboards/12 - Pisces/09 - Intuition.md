## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central merchant towns (Pingyao, Qi, Taigu):** the banker's eye for people as a trained trade skill.
- **Coal regions (Datong, Yangquan, Jincheng):** miners' sense of danger; engineers' instruments against the old hands.
- **Loess farming counties (Lüliang, Xinzhou, Linfen hills):** weather and crop judgment by sign.
- **Southern Shanxi (Yuncheng, Hejin, Linfen):** Zhongyuan Mandarin speakers; the Hedong scholarly tradition.
- **Taihang uplands (Zuoquan, Wuxiang):** blind musicians and itinerant seers.
- **Hui Muslims** (attitudes to fortune-telling within Islam), **Buddhist practitioners** (insight teaching), **TCM practitioners** against hospital doctors; **women and men** (claims about "women's intuition"); **older and younger** residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in folklore, merchant history and dialect proverbs; thin in measured studies at provincial scale.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Jin dialect for proverbs and miners' speech; merchant jargon.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling and face-reading are officially discouraged as "superstition"; earthquake prediction claims can be politically sensitive after disasters.
- **Held for review:** none.
