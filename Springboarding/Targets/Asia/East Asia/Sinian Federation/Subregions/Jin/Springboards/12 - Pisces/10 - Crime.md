# Jin: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Jin 晋, the member state of the Sinian Federation that carries Shanxi 山西; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, including official and slang terms; Classical Chinese for case literature and temple steles; Jin dialect insults and names for wrongdoers; southern Zhongyuan Mandarin and opera lyrics. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched under Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes the idea of the wrong and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coal regions (Datong, Lüliang, Linfen, Jincheng):** illegal mines, coal-field bullies and official collusion.
- **Southern Shanxi (Yuncheng, Linfen, Hongtong):** Zhongyuan Mandarin areas, tomb-rich land, the Su San story.
- **Central merchant towns (Pingyao, Qi, Taigu):** the bandit as the trade's enemy; merchant honesty codes.
- **Taiyuan:** urban crime talk, corruption news, Judge Dee as a city figure.
- **Lüliang and northwest uplands:** poverty, out-migration and old campaigns against idlers.
- **Hui and Han neighbors** (who is suspected of what, a stereotype to record and test); **migrant miners** from other provinces; **rural women** in bride-trafficking cases; **ex-offenders and their families**; **officials and ordinary residents** judging the same wrongs differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in opera, fiction, temple imagery and news; thin for victimization surveys at provincial level; crime statistics are partial and political.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and slang; Classical Chinese for case literature; Jin dialect for local insults.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and protest topics are censored; ethnic suspicion must be recorded only as stereotype; ex-offender identities must be protected. All law, courts and punishment as written belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
