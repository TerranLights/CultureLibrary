**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; provinces Fujian and Hainan (a working list the author still confirms). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (traditional forms for older print); Southern Min (闽南话, Hokkien); Eastern Min (福州话, Fuzhou); Puxian Min (莆仙话); Northern Min (闽北话); Hainanese (海南话); Hlai (Li); She speech; Lingao (Be); Tsat (Utsul). **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it asserts itself: the self, the body, appearance, beginnings and initiative, with naming as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan core (Xiamen, Quanzhou, Zhangzhou):** three city speeches and customs, often merged; separate them before generalizing about a Hokkien self.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong (Fuzhou, Fuqing, Changle, Ningde):** a different Min language and its own emigrant identity.
- **Puxian (Putian, Xianyou):** a language island between the two larger groups.
- **Inland Minbei and Minzhong (Nanping, Jian'ou, Sanming):** upland; tests coast-based assumptions.
- **Hui'an and Xunpu women:** dress that marks a local self strongly; read as a case, not a type.
- **She of Ningde and Luoyuan:** separate ethnic self-name and origin story.
- **Hui lineages of Jinjiang (Chendai) and Hui'an (Baiqi):** identity by descent.
- **Boat-dwelling communities on the Min River and at Sanya:** long kept outside landed lineages.
- **Hainanese Han (Wenchang, Qionghai, Haikou, Danzhou):** heavy emigration and several local Han speeches.
- **Li (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu and Sai groups), Hainan Miao, Lingao, Cun, and the Utsul of Sanya:** distinct languages and selves on one island.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** a separate sub-unit; name only where they meet Min communities.
- **Comparison only:** the Southeast Asian diaspora; Taiwan (separate target); Chaoshan and Leizhou (in Yue).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou lineages; mixed for Hainan Han; thin in English for Li, Lingao, Cun and Utsul. **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this run writes for Fujian and Hainan.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; the Min languages for oral material; Hlai; German and Japanese for early Hainan ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Li facial tattoos are carried by elderly women (consent before any image work); the She dog-ancestor legend was once used as an insult; older names for boat people are pejorative; ethnic classification of the Cun and Hainan Miao is contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
