## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen's urban middle class:** the PX case makes it the obvious test of collective, civic speaking up.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang family-firm owners:** bargaining and claiming a share of the market in a private-enterprise heartland.
- **Putian villages:** ritual precedence in processions is a claim to standing, distinct from talk.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** separate the Eastern Min register of quarreling and talking back.
- **Hainanese Han men at tea houses and Hainanese Han women at markets:** split by gender who airs claims where.
- **Li communities in the central highlands:** older headman-led settling of claims set against today's village committees.
- **She villages of Ningde:** challenge-and-answer singing as a bounded form of assertion.
- **Young migrants in factory towns (Jinjiang, Shishi, Haikou):** newcomers asserting themselves against local employers and landlords.
- **Comparison only:** the Hokkien diaspora's clan associations (who speaks for the clan); Taiwan's Hokkien media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical exemplars and opera; mixed for everyday talk (needs fieldwork); thin for Li and She speech norms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for Hai Rui; early vernacular Southern Min for the Lychee Mirror; Fuzhou and Hainanese dialects for recorded talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rights defense (维权) and protest are politically sensitive; protect informants; any legal side of complaints goes to Libra (letter of the law) or Capricorn (spirit).
