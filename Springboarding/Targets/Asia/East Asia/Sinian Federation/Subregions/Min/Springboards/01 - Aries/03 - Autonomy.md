## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hui'an and Chongwu coastal townships:** a marriage custom that gives women a long stretch of separate living; ask whether it widened or narrowed their choices.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou countryside:** where minor marriage and vegetarian halls are recorded; the two ends of women's choice.
- **Fuzhou, Fuqing and Changle:** emigration decided by households; the young person's say in it.
- **Putian:** strong temple and lineage organization; compare how much a village decides for its members.
- **Hainanese Han villages around Wenchang:** an emigrant district; where men were long abroad, ask who made the household's choices.
- **Li of Qiongzhong, Baisha, Ledong and Baoting:** personal courtship freedoms and collective autonomy both in question.
- **Hainan Miao:** a minority inside Li autonomous counties; nested autonomy.
- **She of Ningde:** ethnic townships as a small, separate test.
- **Urban young professionals in Xiamen and Haikou:** living alone, choosing late marriage or none.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's minor-marriage scholarship; Zhejiang's She autonomous county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Chinese for marriage history; mixed for present-day personal choice (survey splits by province are uncertain); thin in English for Li and She self-government.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min for women's oral histories; Hlai; She speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reported suicides among young Hui'an women in past decades (handle with care); family estrangement; regional ethnic autonomy is a political subject; any legal rule on marriage or self-government goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
