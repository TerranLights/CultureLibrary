## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** family-workshop founding at its densest; test the "self-starter" image against firm records.
- **Chendai Hui township:** does minority status shape who starts what?
- **Putian:** a single home region behind several nationwide trade networks (hospitals, gold, timber); a case of initiative and its excess.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** initiative expressed by leaving; compare founding at home with founding abroad.
- **Xiamen:** an early special economic zone and the Tan Kah Kee legacy; state-led and donor-led starts side by side.
- **Inland Sanming and Nanping:** government and forestry reforms, not private trade; a different kind of first mover.
- **Hainan incomers since 1988:** starters from outside; boom-and-bust ventures.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai returnees:** schools, arcades and roads built from remittances.
- **Li and Miao villages of central Hainan:** brocade and rural-tourism ventures; who leads them, and whether outsiders do.
- **Women in Hui'an and Putian:** check reports of women's collective works and businesses.
- **Comparison only:** Hokkien, Fuzhou and Henghua founders in Southeast Asia and New York.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for enterprise and official reform (Chinese press and economics); mixed for everyday, civic initiative; thin for minority villages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min and Puxian for business oral history; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Jinjiang experience" (晋江经验) is now part of national political messaging, so separate it from the economic record; the Putian hospital scandals; collapsed credit clubs left victims.
- **Held for review:** none.
