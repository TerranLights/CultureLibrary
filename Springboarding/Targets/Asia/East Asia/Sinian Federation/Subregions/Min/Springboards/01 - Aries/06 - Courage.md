## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou gentry and student families:** courage written as letters and principled death (Lin Juemin, the 1884 sailors).
- **Zhangzhou and Dongshan:** loyalist scholar-courage remembered locally.
- **Fuqing and the coast:** defense against raiders, remembered through shrines and food.
- **Quanzhou and Yongchun martial schools:** physical courage trained in lineages of teachers.
- **Women in coastal Fujian:** honored endurance in old records against women's own accounts.
- **Putian temple communities:** ritual ordeals as public courage.
- **Hainanese Han of Qionghai and Wenchang:** revolutionary and wartime memory, including women fighters.
- **Li of Baisha and the central highlands:** uprising memory told by the Li themselves.
- **Hainan fishing communities and boat people:** everyday danger at sea.
- **She of Ningde:** check for local accounts of resistance or endurance before folding them into Han narratives.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's own Koxinga memory; Southeast Asian Hokkien wartime resistance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martyrs and famous men; mixed for women and minorities; rich official record that needs reading against local memory.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou for local memory; Hlai for Baisha accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary and martyr stories are part of state education, so treat them as both history and messaging; Koxinga is claimed differently across the Strait; widow suicide is a painful subject; war memory involves Japanese occupation of Hainan.
- **Held for review:** none.
