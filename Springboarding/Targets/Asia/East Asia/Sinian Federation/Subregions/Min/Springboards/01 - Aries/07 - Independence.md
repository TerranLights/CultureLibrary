## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang and Shishi family firms:** being one's own boss as a goal; compare owners with employees.
- **Fuqing and Changle households:** independence of the emigrant set against dependence of those left behind.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai:** remittance households across generations; did dependence shrink as remittances fell?
- **Inland Yongtai and Hua'an:** fortified self-sufficiency in the past; today's depopulated villages.
- **Hui'an women:** earning without men's help; ask how they describe it.
- **Xiamen and Haikou young adults:** living alone against 啃老 on current housing costs.
- **Li hill farmers:** self-provisioning changed by forest and land policy.
- **Fuzhou and Sanya boat people:** independence afloat, dependence ashore.
- **Northern retirees in Sanya:** outsiders living independently among locals; a contrast, not a local norm.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** separate sub-unit; their earthen fortresses only as a comparison with Minnan ones.
- **Comparison only:** self-made founders in the Hokkien and Hainanese diaspora.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for remittance and lineage history; mixed for present-day personal independence; thin for Li and boat-people self-accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou for oral history; Hainanese; Hlai; the Sanya boat people's speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional breakaways (the Min state, the Fujian Incident) are politically sensitive in any present-day framing; Taiwan independence is out of scope and sensitive; welfare dependence carries stigma for informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
