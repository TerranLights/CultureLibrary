# Min (闽): Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Fujian and Hainan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou, Putian and Zhangzhou countryside:** where the feud sources above are set; ask how grievances between villages are handled now.
- **Fuzhou literati heritage:** restraint of anger as a gentleman's ideal, against everyday Fuzhou speech.
- **Inland Minbei:** Zhu Xi's teaching ground; test whether self-cultivation ideas live on outside texts.
- **Jinjiang and Xiamen factory towns:** workplace anger among migrant workers from other provinces.
- **Hainanese Han of Haikou and the east coast:** compare temper talk at tea houses and markets with that of Fujian.
- **Li villages of the central highlands:** anger tied to misfortune and sorcery talk.
- **Hui and She minorities in Fujian:** grievances held as minorities; ask how and to whom they are voiced.
- **Women in coastal Fujian:** women's anger in songs and laments, set against men's public displays.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Zhang–Quan feud history; Southeast Asian Hokkien clan fights.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for feuds and classical teaching; mixed for everyday display rules (needs fieldwork); thin for Li in English.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min, Fuzhou and Puxian for curses and oral memory; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations among the Li can harm living people, so anonymize; strikes and protests are politically sensitive; domestic violence (legal side to Libra); feud memory can still divide villages.
- **Held for review:** none.
