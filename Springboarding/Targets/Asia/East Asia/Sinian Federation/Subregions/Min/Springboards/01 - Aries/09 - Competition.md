## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian and Xianyou:** a reputed exam record and today's tutoring and test pressure; compare the old contest with the new.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** sportswear-brand rivalry and lineage prestige in building.
- **Xiamen:** a citywide game of chance and rank at Mid-Autumn; ask how workplaces use it.
- **Anxi and Wuyishan tea villages:** competition through taste, with real money at stake.
- **Fuzhou and Min River towns:** dragon-boat rivalries between neighborhoods.
- **Fuqing and Putian families:** wedding and bride-price rivalry.
- **Haikou and Sanya:** pageants, tourism rankings and exam migration from outside.
- **Li and Miao of central Hainan:** traditional contests as staged for sports meets against as played at home.
- **She of Ningde:** a smaller group's games.
- **Comparison only:** Hokkien and Hainanese clan rivalries in Southeast Asia; Taiwan's temple-procession contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, tea and boat races; mixed for workplace and family rivalry; thin for Li and She contests.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min for game terms and song lyrics; Fuzhou for boat-race lore; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling in the dice game and in races raises legal questions (to Libra); exam fraud cases; pageants and appearance raise gender questions; the song's slogan use can be stereotyping.
- **Held for review:** none.
