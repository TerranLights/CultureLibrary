## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changle and Fuqing:** emigration by smuggling; ask whether home villages saw it as courage, recklessness or duty.
- **Coastal Zhangzhou and Quanzhou:** piracy and feud history against present-day speed and gambling.
- **Rural Fujian and Hainan villages:** betting crazes; compare which villages caught them and which resisted.
- **Minnan temple communities:** ritual wounding treated as devotion, not rashness.
- **Hainanese villages with festival piercing:** a separate ritual of daring.
- **Young men on motorbikes in Haikou, Danzhou and small Fujian towns:** a likely risk group; test with crash data rather than assume.
- **Hainan incomers and investors since 1988:** speculation and its fallout.
- **Li and Miao communities:** be wary of outside stereotypes of drinking and risk; seek their own accounts.
- **Surfers, divers and outdoor clubs in Sanya and Xiamen:** chosen risk as leisure.
- **Comparison only:** Fuzhounese in New York; Taiwan's procession mediums.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for piracy, smuggling and lottery crazes; mixed for traffic and youth risk at province level; thin for minority views.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou for oral accounts; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human smuggling involves living victims and families; gambling and lottery cases touch criminal law (to Libra); avoid stereotypes of minority drinking; ritual wounding should not be framed as madness.
- **Held for review:** none.
