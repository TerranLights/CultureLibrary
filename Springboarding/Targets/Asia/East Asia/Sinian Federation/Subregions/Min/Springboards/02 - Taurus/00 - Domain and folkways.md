**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified, traditional for older print); Southern Min (Minnan/Hokkien, Pe̍h-ōe-jī spelling), Eastern Min (Fuzhounese, Foochow Romanized), Puxian Min, Northern and Central Min, Hainanese; minority languages Hlai (Li), the She people's speech, Kim Mun (Hainan Miao), Lingao (Be) and Tsat (Utsat). **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (money, resources, food, dress and wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan heartland (Xiamen, Quanzhou, Zhangzhou):** trade wealth, emigrant money and brand manufacturing.
- **Fuzhou and Eastern Min (Fuqing, Changle, Ningde area):** later emigration, with its own remittance-built houses.
- **Puxian (Putian, Xianyou):** a separate dialect area known for gold jewelry and furniture trades.
- **Northern and Central Min (Nanping, Wuyishan, Sanming, Shaxian):** inland tea, timber and food-stall economies.
- **She communities (Fu'an, Xiapu and other Ningde counties):** their own dress, silver and festival foods.
- **Hui lineages of Quanzhou (Chendai, Baiqi):** Muslim-descended clans; food rules vary by family.
- **Coastal and boat communities (Hui'an, Pingtan, Dongshan, Danjia boat people in Fujian and Hainan):** fishing, sea-farming and shoreline holdings.
- **Hainanese Han (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai) against the Danzhou- and Lingao-speaking west, and state-farm newcomers:** different emigrant ties and crops.
- **Li (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Jiamao branches), Hainan Miao, Utsat of Sanya:** wealth reckoned in cloth, land, cattle or trade.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** a separate sub-unit; named only where they share markets or tulou with Min speakers.
