# Min: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Fujian and Hainan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min (Hokkien), Eastern Min (Fuzhounese), Puxian Min, Hainanese; Hlai (Li), She speech, Kim Mun, Lingao, Tsat. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou-Xiamen-Zhangzhou Minnan:** dense paper-offering trades and emigrant keepsakes; compare city flats with old red-brick houses.
- **Fuzhou, Changle and Fuqing:** goods sent home from abroad, and new houses filled but often empty.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** jewelry and hardwood trades where possessions double as stock.
- **Inland Minbei (Jianyang, Wuyishan):** tea wares and Jian bowls as both tools and collectibles.
- **Sanming and Shaxian:** households that left for snack-shop work and stored their goods behind.
- **She of Ningde:** silver ornaments and dress kept through the generations.
- **Hui'an coast and Quanzhou Hui lineages:** what women hold apart from husbands' households; Muslim-descended grave customs.
- **Hainanese Han of Wenchang and Qionghai:** returnees' trunks and objects from Southeast Asia.
- **Li by branch, Hainan Miao, Utsat:** cloth, jars and tools; what goes with the dead.
- **Danjia in both provinces:** boat-held possessions, and moving ashore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on paper offerings, collectibles and emigrant objects; thin on everyday Li, Lingao and Tsat belongings and on hoarding.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhounese for oral terms; Hlai for Li object names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** objects taken from Li villages by collectors; mediums' practice; huanghuali poaching and illegal logging.
- **Held for review:** none.
