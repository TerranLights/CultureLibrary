*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangzhou and Quanzhou plains:** dense contract records and layered field rights; old lineage feuds over land.
- **Fuzhou basin and Fuqing:** lineage trusts and emigrant houses whose owners live abroad.
- **Putian plain:** irrigation-system and temple holdings shared across villages.
- **Northern and Central Min uplands (Nanping, Sanming, Yong'an):** forests and the tenure reform; the contract finds.
- **She villages of Ningde:** hillside land beside Han neighbors; who held what after resettlement.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** lineage halls and graves held as a descent group.
- **Coastal Danjia and Hui'an fishing towns:** sea-farming plots and boats.
- **Li and Hainan Miao uplands:** hemu groups, state farms and tourist zones on the same land.
- **Hainanese Han coast and Haikou-Sanya property buyers:** home ownership by outsiders against local families.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** only where tulou or forest tenure crosses into Min areas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Ming-Qing contracts and lineage estates; mixed on present-day attitudes; thin on Li and Lingao tenure in Li voices.
