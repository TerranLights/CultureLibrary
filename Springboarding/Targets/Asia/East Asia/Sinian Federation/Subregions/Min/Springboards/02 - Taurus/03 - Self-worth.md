# Min: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Fujian and Hainan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min, Eastern Min, Puxian Min, Hainanese; She speech, Hlai (Li). **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Putian scholar lineages:** worth counted in degrees, halls and genealogies.
- **Jinjiang, Shishi and Fuqing:** worth counted in firms built and money sent home.
- **Fuzhou city:** old official families against new migrant-worker neighborhoods.
- **Minbei and Minzhong uplands:** farm households whose young left; worth of the ones who stayed.
- **She communities:** a people's own account of descent against outside ranking.
- **Danjia in Fujian and Hainan:** a people once held low by law; change after settling ashore.
- **Hui'an women:** worth by labor, dress and the long stay in the natal home.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** pride in foreign ancestors, and how it is shown.
- **Hainanese locals against 1988 newcomers and later wintering retirees:** who counts as a real islander.
- **Li by branch and Hainan Miao:** worth under tourism's gaze; tattoo, cloth and language.
- **Migrant workers in Xiamen and Jinjiang factories:** worth far from their home provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lineage honor and emigrant prestige; mixed on psychology surveys; thin on Lingao, Tsat and Hainan Miao voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhounese for speech; Hlai and She speech for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for Danjia and other groups (record, never repeat); Li tattoo as spectacle; religion in Fuzhou and Putian; mental health and suicide data.
- **Held for review:** none.
