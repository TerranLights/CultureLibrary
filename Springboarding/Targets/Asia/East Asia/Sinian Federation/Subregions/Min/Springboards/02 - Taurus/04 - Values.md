## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minbei scholar towns (Wuyishan, Jianyang, Nanping):** the Neo-Confucian legacy at its source.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** the Three-in-One teaching and dense temple alliances.
- **Quanzhou:** many faiths over centuries; Buddhist, Daoist, Hui and Christian sites side by side.
- **Xiamen and Zhangzhou:** emigrant-founded schools and mottos; port-city change.
- **Fuzhou and Fuqing:** strong Protestant and folk-temple life in the same towns.
- **She villages:** song as moral teaching; ancestor rites unlike Han neighbors'.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** which practices were held as non-negotiable.
- **Hainanese Han and the west (Danzhou, Lingao):** exile-official traditions; Lady Xian rites.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** tales and ritual specialists' teaching, as they give it.
- **Young urban workers against village elders:** generation as a likely fault line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on written Neo-Confucian and lineage material; mixed on surveys at provincial level; thin on Li, Miao and Tsat moral teaching in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for lineage books; Puxian and Hokkien for opera and proverbs; Hlai for Li tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion (Protestant house churches, Hui practice, popular sects); official values campaigns sit beside local ones and should be kept apart.
- **Held for review:** none.
