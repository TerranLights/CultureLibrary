## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuqing, Changle and Lianjiang:** security sought through migration, often costly and risky.
- **Mingxi and other Sanming counties:** inland migration to Europe; left-behind elders.
- **Putian, Ningde and Quanzhou counties:** rotating credit groups and their failures.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi factory towns:** migrant workers without local residence rights against settled locals.
- **Coastal fishers (Dongshan, Pingtan, Lianjiang, Tanmen, Danjia of Sanya and Lingshui):** typhoon and sea risk.
- **Central Fujian uplands (Yong'an, Datian, Youxi):** fortified-house memory; mountain farming.
- **She of Ningde:** hill farming and later resettlement; social-insurance reach.
- **Hainanese Han coast:** typhoons, emigrant remittances, pensions of returnees.
- **Li and Hainan Miao uplands:** rubber, tourism wages and the hunger memory in their own accounts.
- **Wintering northern retirees in Sanya and Haikou:** imported pensions shaping a local economy.
