## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old lineage villages of the Quanzhou and Putian plains:** genealogies revised on a cycle; halls still in use.
- **Xiamen and Fuzhou cities:** heavy in-migration and redevelopment; stable cores, moving edges.
- **Emigrant counties (Changle, Fuqing, Mingxi):** houses kept for people who rarely return.
- **Minbei tea and timber towns:** seasonal rhythm against market swings.
- **Anxi and Dehua:** craft and tea households handed on in one family.
- **She villages:** resettled settlements against older hill sites.
- **Danjia of the Min River and Hainan:** a recent move from water to land.
- **Hainanese Han (Wenchang, Qionghai):** emigrant families and their kept houses.
- **Li and Hainan Miao in the park zone:** relocation and continuity.
- **Haikou and Sanya newcomers:** a population that changes with the seasons.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on genealogies, census migration and dialect recordings; mixed on residents' own views of redevelopment; thin on Li, Miao and Danjia voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese for genealogies; Hokkien, Fuzhounese and Puxian; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation, demolition and resettlement; the Gushi claim is a matter of pride and should be handled as such.
- **Held for review:** none.
