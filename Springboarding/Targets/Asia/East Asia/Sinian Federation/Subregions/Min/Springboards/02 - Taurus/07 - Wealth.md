## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang, Shishi and Xiamen:** manufacturing fortunes from family workshops.
- **Fuzhou, Fuqing and Changle:** emigrant money, and the Southeast Asian tycoons from Fuqing.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** gold, furniture and private-clinic fortunes.
- **Ningde:** battery-industry wealth beside older fishing and tea economies.
- **Minbei tea counties (Wuyishan, Fuding, Anxi):** tea prices and tea fortunes.
- **Sanming and Shaxian:** modest wealth from food stalls far from home.
- **She villages:** silver as family savings; the gap with Han neighbors.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** shoe and garment firms in Chendai.
- **Hainanese of Wenchang and Qionghai:** emigrant wealth and return.
- **Haikou and Sanya:** property and tourism wealth, much of it held by outsiders.
- **Li, Hainan Miao and Utsat:** different reckonings of wealth; Utsat trade and fishing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on merchant history and modern business; mixed on attitudes to the rich; thin on Li, Miao and Utsat ideas of wealth in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhounese and Puxian for business talk; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** smuggling cases (the Xiamen Yuanhua case of the late 1990s); counterfeit trades; private-hospital networks; emigrant smuggling routes.
- **Held for review:** none.
