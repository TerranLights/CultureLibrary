## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangzhou and Quanzhou lineage villages:** the feud history and its memory today.
- **Putian plain:** water shares and high bride prices.
- **Hui'an coast:** a marriage custom that delays a husband's claim.
- **Fuzhou and Fuqing:** marriages kept across long absences abroad.
- **Craft towns (Dehua, Shoushan, Anxi):** guarded skills and family secrets.
- **She villages:** marriage customs unlike the Han; ties between bride's and groom's families.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** marrying in or out of the lineage.
- **Danjia and fishing towns:** sea turf.
- **Hainanese locals against wintering newcomers:** who owns the beach and the market.
- **Li by branch and Hainan Miao:** courtship freedom and its limits, as their songs and elders tell it.
- **Migrant-worker couples in Xiamen and Jinjiang:** control and trust when living apart.
