## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen and Kinmen-facing coast:** Ming-loyalist memory and the long military front.
- **Zhangzhou and Quanzhou villages:** feuds and elders' mediation.
- **Fuzhou:** the Lin Zexu legacy; official-family pride.
- **Putian:** temple alliances that settle or prolong quarrels.
- **Minbei tea towns:** craft conservatism against new methods.
- **She of Ningde and the old Zhangzhou She areas:** resistance memory and its retelling.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** a separate sub-unit; their "hard neck" self-image only as comparison.
- **Hainanese of Haikou and Qiongshan:** Hai Rui as local hero.
- **Li of Baisha, Qiongzhong and Wuzhishan:** uprising memory.
- **Lingao and Danzhou speakers:** keeping their own languages beside Hainanese and Standard Chinese.
- **Urban professionals against village elders:** whether giving way is read as wisdom or weakness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official heroes and resistance histories; mixed on everyday stubbornness; thin on Li, She and Lingao self-descriptions.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for memorials; Hokkien and Fuzhounese for proverbs and opera; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary histories are officially framed, so local memory should be sought beside them; demolition disputes; cross-Strait history.
- **Held for review:** none.
