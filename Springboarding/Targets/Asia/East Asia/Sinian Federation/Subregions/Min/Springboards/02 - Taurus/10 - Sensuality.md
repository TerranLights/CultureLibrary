## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Xiamen:** Nanyin, tea and snacks; pleasure in long-established forms.
- **Zhangzhou:** flower growing and Xiang opera; scent and song.
- **Fuzhou:** hot springs, jasmine and a rich banquet cuisine.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** the Song tea legacy; tonic and festival foods.
- **Minbei tea country:** taste as a trade skill.
- **She villages:** black-rice festival foods and antiphonal song.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** food rules kept, relaxed or dropped, family by family.
- **Hainanese of Haikou and Wenchang:** tea houses, chicken rice and coconut in a hot climate.
- **Danzhou and Lingao speakers:** their own songs and drinks.
- **Li by branch and Hainan Miao:** rice wine, courting customs and textiles to the touch.
- **Utsat of Sanya and the Danjia:** sea food, song and Muslim food rules among the Utsat.
- **Wintering retirees and tourists:** pleasures brought in from outside, and how locals see them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on tea, food and music; mixed on bathing and scent; thin on touch norms and on Li, Miao and Lingao sensual life in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Song texts; Hokkien and Fuzhounese for food and song; Hlai and Danzhou speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Li courting customs have been exoticized for tourism; sexuality belongs to Scorpio; betel and alcohol health data.
