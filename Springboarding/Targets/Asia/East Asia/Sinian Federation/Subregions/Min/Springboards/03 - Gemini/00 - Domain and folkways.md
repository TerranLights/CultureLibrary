# Min: Domain and folkways (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation (Fujian and Hainan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Minnan (Hokkien); Fuzhou (Eastern Min); Puxian; inland Min; Hainanese; Hlai (Li), Kim Mun, Tsat, Be, She speech. **Concept in one line:** the domain of communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (siblings and neighbors, short journeys, early learning, speech ways and learning ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan heartland** (Xiamen, Quanzhou, Zhangzhou; the Quanzhou–Zhangzhou accent divide) and **Longyan Minnan** inland: rival prestige accents.
- **Eastern Min**: the Fuzhou "Ten Towns" (including Fuqing, Changle, Pingtan) against **Ningde, Fu'an and Fuding** to the north.
- **Puxian** (Putian, Xianyou): a distinct speech and opera world between the two larger blocs.
- **Inland Min** (Jian'ou, Nanping, Sanming, Yong'an, Shaowu): less studied.
- **Hainanese** coastal Han (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai, Wanning) against **Danzhou, Lingao (Be), Cun and Mai** speech communities in the west and south.
- **Li (Hlai)**, with their named branches (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai, *check*), and **Hainan Miao** (Kim Mun speakers).
- **She** of Mindong; **Hui of Quanzhou** (Chendai, Baiqi); **Utsat of Sanya**; **Danjia boat communities** (Min River, Sanya, Lingshui); the **Qinjiang Manchu** village (Changle); the **Nanping Mandarin enclave**; **returned overseas Chinese farms** (Xinglong and others); **internal migrant workers** in Xiamen and Jinjiang.
- **Hakka** of western Fujian, briefly (a separate sub-unit): only in towns shared with Min speakers (Longyan, Zhangping).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou; mixed for Puxian and Hainanese; thin for inland Min, Hainan's speech islands and Li branches. **Scope:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled; Fujian and Hainan are a working list the author confirms in Stage 0.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; the Min varieties; Japanese (wartime Hainan surveys); English, Dutch and German; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cross-Strait framing (Kinmen and Matsu belong to the Taiwan target; Taiwan, Chaoshan, Leizhou and the Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua diasporas are comparison only); ethnic classification disputes (Utsat, She); old slur names for Danjia; dialect-policy politics; South China Sea material from Hainan fishing records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
