*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Port cities** (Quanzhou, Xiamen, Fuzhou), with centuries of foreign contact, against **inland Min** counties of Nanping and Sanming: openness to the strange may differ.
- **Fuzhou's late-Qing literati families** against **Minnan merchant families**: book curiosity against trade curiosity.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai**, emigrant and space-launch districts of Hainan.
- **Li and Hainan Miao**, where ritual knowledge held by specialists may be closed to outside askers.
- **Utsat** of Sanya and **Quanzhou Hui**, often studied by outsiders: how they handle curious visitors.
- **Hui'an women**, a frequent target of tourists' and photographers' curiosity.
- **Danjia boat communities**, long gazed at from shore.
- **Children and elders** in the same household: the "why" stage and how it is answered.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**, briefly: a separate sub-unit; their tulou draw curious visitors too.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern inquiry and natural history; mixed for museums and science outreach; thin for folk attitudes to prying, especially in Hainan.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Minnan, Fuzhou and Hainanese for everyday words; Hlai for Li tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Li ritual knowledge may be restricted; tourist curiosity about Hui'an women, Danjia and Li villages raises consent questions; PISA's China samples did not include Fujian or Hainan, so national test data cannot stand in (*check*).
- **Held for review:** none.
