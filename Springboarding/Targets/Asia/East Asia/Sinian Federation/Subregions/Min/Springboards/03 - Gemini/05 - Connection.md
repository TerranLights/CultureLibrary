# Min: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties** (Changle, Fuqing, Jinjiang, Shishi, Wenchang, Qionghai) against **non-emigrant upland counties** of Nanping, Sanming and central Hainan: far ties against near ties.
- **Putian**: temple alliances and home-town business networks in one place.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou lineage villages**, and their links to Taiwan branches (comparison only).
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages** (Ding of Chendai, Guo of Baiqi): Muslim descent held through Han-style lineage ties.
- **Utsat**, with links to Muslim communities abroad.
- **Li hemu groups** and **Hainan Miao** villages, with their own ways of binding households.
- **She** of Mindong, whose kin ties may cross the Han–She line.
- **Danjia boat communities**: ties on the water and the shore's distance from them.
- **Hui'an women**, keeping strong ties to the natal home after marriage.
- **Internal migrants and returnee farms**: new networks without old lineages.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**, briefly: a separate sub-unit with its own clan networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage, temple and emigrant ties; mixed for business networks; thin for Li, Miao and Danjia ties and for loneliness measures.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese (genealogies, temple stelae); Minnan, Fuzhou and Puxian for fieldwork; Hlai; English for Western scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies may list living people; the Putian hospital network and smuggling of emigrants are contentious; Taiwan, Southeast Asian and American branches are comparison only; cross-Strait temple ties are politicized.
- **Held for review:** none.
