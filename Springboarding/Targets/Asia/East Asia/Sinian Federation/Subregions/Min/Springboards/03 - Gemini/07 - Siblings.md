# Min: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), Sinian Federation member state (Fujian, Hainan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Minnan; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; She speech. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lineage villages of Quanzhou, Zhangzhou and Putian**, where brothers' branches share ancestral estates, against **upland Min** villages with thinner lineages.
- **Emigrant counties** (Changle, Fuqing, Wenchang): brothers split between home and abroad.
- **Urban only-child cohorts** in Xiamen and Fuzhou against **rural families** with several children.
- **Li**, whose kin terms and household division may follow other rules; **Hainan Miao** likewise.
- **She** of Mindong, with their own naming and in-marriage customs.
- **Hui'an women**, whose age-mate sisterhoods may rival kin ties.
- **Danjia boat families**, where siblings may work one boat together.
- **Tanmen fishing families**, whose cult of brothers ties to the sea.
- **Migrant families**, with siblings left behind or brought along.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**, briefly (a separate sub-unit): sibling customs only where Hakka and Minnan families marry each other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household division, lineage shares and sworn brotherhood; mixed for adoption; thin for Li, Miao and She sibling norms and for Hainan generally.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and Standard Chinese (deeds, gazetteers); Minnan and Fuzhou kin terms; Hlai; She speech; English and French (Baptandier wrote first in French, *check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-limit history and sex-ratio questions are delicate; adoption of girls and boys touches living families; Taiwan's Hokkien material is comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none.
