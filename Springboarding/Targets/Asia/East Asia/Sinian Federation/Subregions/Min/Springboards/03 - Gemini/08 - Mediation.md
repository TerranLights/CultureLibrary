## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Fujian lineage villages** (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou), with a history of feuds, against **Fuzhou-area** and **Puxian** villages: different brokers may step in.
- **Anxi tea villages**: lineage, cooperative and party roles mixed.
- **Port merchant families** of Xiamen and Fuzhou, heirs to broker trades.
- **Li villages** with headmen and hemu elders; **Hainan Miao** settlements with their own heads.
- **She** of Mindong: mediation inside She lineages and across the Han line.
- **Utsat** and **Quanzhou Hui**: religious leaders as go-betweens.
- **Danjia boat communities**, who may lack shore-based brokers.
- **Hui'an coastal villages**, where women's groups may settle matters among themselves.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**, briefly (a separate sub-unit): disputes across the Min–Hakka border in the Longyan and Zhangzhou uplands, and who brokered them.
