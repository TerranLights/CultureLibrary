## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small inland towns** of Nanping, Sanming and Ningde (everyone known) against **Xiamen**, with its many newcomers.
- **Emigrant villages** in Jinjiang, Fuqing and Wenchang, where absent men and waiting wives drew talk.
- **Hui'an women**, often the subject of outside talk as well as village talk.
- **Danjia boat communities**, long gossiped about from the shore.
- **Li youth** (youth houses) and **Hainan Miao** villages, with their own rules for open and hidden talk.
- **Utsat** and **Quanzhou Hui**: small communities where reputation travels fast.
- **She** of Mindong: talk inside She villages and between She and Han.
- **Migrant workers** in factory dormitories.
- **Older and younger** residents: temple square against group chat.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**, briefly (a separate sub-unit): talk between Hakka and Min neighbors in shared market towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for online rumor and its control; mixed for village talk places; thin for Li, Miao, Utsat and Danjia gossip norms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Minnan, Fuzhou, Puxian and Hainanese for sayings and fieldwork; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "rumor" is a legal and political category (the letter of the law: Libra; its spirit: Capricorn); the Xiamen PX case is politically delicate; gossip about women's sexual conduct and about Danjia needs care; group chats need consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
