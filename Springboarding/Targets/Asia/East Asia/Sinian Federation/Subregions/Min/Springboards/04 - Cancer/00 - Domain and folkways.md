**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; its lands are Fujian and Hainan (a working list the author still confirms). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (traditional characters for pre-1950s print); Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Northern and Central Min, Hainanese; Hlai (Li), She, Danzhou, Lingao (Ongbe), Utsul (Tsat). **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what is home, and who is family?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou, Xiamen):** emigrant counties (Jinjiang, Shishi, Nan'an) apart from inland Anxi and Yongchun.
- **Eastern Min (Fuzhou, Fuqing, Changle) and northern Mindong (Ningde, Fu'an, Xiapu):** emigrant coast against uplands.
- **Puxian (Putian, Xianyou):** its own speech and temple alliances.
- **Northern and Central Min (Nanping, Jian'ou, Sanming, Yong'an, Shaowu):** uplands with fortified earthen manors; least studied.
- **Hui'an coast (Chongwu, Dazuo):** distinct marriage residence.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka** of the Min River and Ningde bays, and of Sanya and Lingshui: homes on water.
- **The She** (Ningde, Luoyuan, Zhangpu): own kin and origin traditions.
- **Hui of Quanzhou** (Chendai Ding, Baiqi Guo): Muslim descent kept inside Han-style lineages.
- **Hainanese Han** (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai), apart from **Danzhou**, **Lingao** and **Mai/Junjiahua** speakers.
- **The Li** and their branches (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai *check*), the **Hainan Miao**, the **Utsul** of Sanya, the **Cun** speakers.
- **Hainan state-farm settlers, returned overseas Chinese** and seasonal northern retirees.
- Hakka in western Fujian: name briefly; a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Southern Min lineages, houses and emigration; mixed for Fuzhou and Puxian; thin for Northern and Central Min, the She, Utsul, Cun and Lingao speakers. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for Fujian and Hainan as the core.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou romanizations; classical Chinese for genealogies and gazetteers; Hlai; German and Japanese for pre-1949 Hainan ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification of the Li, She and Utsul; old slurs for boat dwellers; the Sinian Federation's household registration as a frame of whose home counts.
- **Held for review:** none.
