# Min: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian and Hainanese; Hlai (Li), Danzhou, Lingao, Utsul. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties (Jinjiang, Shishi, Fuqing, Changle, Wenchang):** homes built with money from abroad, often left empty.
- **Inland Southern Min and upland Min (Anxi, Yongchun, Nanping, Sanming):** farm homes and fortified manors, outmigration to the coast.
- **Xiamen and Fuzhou city dwellers:** flats, urban villages (Zengcuo'an, now a tourist quarter *check*) and relocation.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka** of the Min River, Ningde and Sanya's rafts: home afloat, then ashore.
- **Hui'an coast:** stone houses and a distinct pattern of where a wife lives.
- **Northern Hainan farmers** (volcanic-stone villages) against **Hainanese of the east coast**.
- **The Li** in the central highlands, including villages moved down from the hills.
- **The Utsul of Sanya** (Huixin and Huihui villages *check*): homes around their mosques.
- **She villages** of Mindong: upland home and origin stories.
- **Northern retirees and new migrants** in Sanya and Haikou: home elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on emigrant houses and heritage; mixed on rented and urban homes; thin on Li, Utsul and upland Min home meanings.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; classical Chinese for Su Shi and gazetteers; Malay and English for Nanyang-side letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation; old slurs for boat dwellers; resettlement of highland villages.
- **Held for review:** none.
