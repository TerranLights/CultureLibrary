## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min plains (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou):** big single-surname villages and dense halls, to test against the textbook model.
- **Fuzhou and its hinterland:** lineages built by claims and settlement.
- **Puxian:** kin bound into temple and irrigation alliances.
- **Inland and upland Min (Minbei, Minzhong):** smaller, poorer lineages, much less studied.
- **Hui'an coast:** marriage and residence that set wives and husbands' kin apart.
- **Hui of Quanzhou:** Muslim descent inside Han-style lineages.
- **The She:** four surnames and a shared origin story, with intermarriage rules to check.
- **The Li branches:** the 合亩 form and village kin; each branch may differ.
- **Hainanese Han of Wenchang and Qionghai:** lineages stretched by emigration.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** kin on boats, often outside the lineage halls.
- **The Utsul** and **Hainan Miao:** own kin and marriage ways.
- **Jinjiang's migrant factory workers:** families split across provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Southern Min and Puxian lineages; mixed for Hainanese Han; thin for upland Min, the She's own voice, the Li 合亩 after the 1950s, the Utsul.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Puxian; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies that leave out daughters and adoptees; minor marriage; ethnic classification; some lineage halls and scrolls are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
