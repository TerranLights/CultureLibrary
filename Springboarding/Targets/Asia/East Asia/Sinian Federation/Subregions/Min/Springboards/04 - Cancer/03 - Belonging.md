# Min: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Hainanese; Hlai (Li), She, Utsul (Tsat). **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old residents and migrant workers** in Jinjiang, Shishi, Xiamen and Fuzhou: the biggest insider-newcomer line today.
- **Minnan against Fuzhou against Puxian:** belonging to a speech area inside one province.
- **Returned overseas Chinese** on farms in Wanning and around Xiamen: a status of their own.
- **Native Hainanese against 1988 newcomers, state-farm families and northern retirees.**
- **The Li** beside state farms and resorts; their branches may differ.
- **The Utsul of Sanya:** a small Muslim people with its own language.
- **The She:** belonging kept through song, surnames and origin scrolls.
- **Hui'an and Xunpu women:** belonging shown in dress.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** a people long kept outside village life.
- **Hui of Quanzhou:** belonging to two ways of tracing descent at once.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on migrants and returnees; mixed on Minnan identity; thin on the Utsul, She and Tanka in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; Utsul; Indonesian and Vietnamese for returnee memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification; slurs for boat dwellers; land disputes between state farms and Li villages; regional labels (Putian) that can stigmatize; household registration rules belong to Libra, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
