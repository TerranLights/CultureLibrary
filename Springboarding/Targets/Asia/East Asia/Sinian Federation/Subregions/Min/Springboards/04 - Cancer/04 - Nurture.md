## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant counties (Fuqing, Changle, Jinjiang, Wenchang):** grandparents raising children whose parents are abroad.
- **Inland Fujian and highland Hainan:** left-behind children of internal migrants.
- **Xiamen and Fuzhou urban middle class:** intensive schooling and enrichment.
- **Migrant workers' families in Jinjiang and Shishi:** children who come along or stay behind.
- **The Li:** youth houses, weaving taught girl to girl; branches may differ.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** children raised on boats and then ashore.
- **The She:** language and song carried, or not, into the next generation.
- **The Utsul:** children taught their language and faith beside Chinese schooling.
- **Puxian and Hui'an families:** coastal fishing households with absent fathers to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on left-behind children and schooling; mixed on Hokkien home practice; thin on Li, She, Utsul and Tanka child-rearing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; She; English for transnational studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's privacy; exoticizing accounts of Li youth houses; discipline belongs partly to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
