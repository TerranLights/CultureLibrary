## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Puxian and Southern Min coast:** sea goddess cult and boat rites; fishing families at risk.
- **Xiamen-Zhangzhou coast:** the pestilence-boat ceremony as community protection.
- **Central and Northern Min uplands:** fortified manors and a history of banditry.
- **Hui'an and Chongwu:** fortress memory and fishing households.
- **Hainan's east coast and Tanmen:** fishermen's own guardian deities, far-sea voyages.
- **The Li:** their own ritual specialists; branches to separate.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** protection afloat, outside the village temples.
- **Urban Xiamen, Fuzhou and Haikou:** bars, cameras, hotlines.
- **The She and the Utsul:** own protective traditions to check (the Utsul's are Islamic).

## 4. Flags
