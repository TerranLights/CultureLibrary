## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Emigrant towns (Fuqing, Changle, Jinjiang, Wenchang):** elders cared for at a distance or by hired help.
- **Inland upland villages (Nanping, Sanming, Longyan):** aging villages emptied by migration.
- **Xiamen and Fuzhou:** hospitals, paid carers, small families.
- **Southern Min temple communities:** healer deities and herb stalls beside clinics.
- **Hainanese Han villages:** very old residents and their families.
- **The Li:** healers, bone-setting, village care; branches differ.
- **The She:** herbal knowledge held in families.
- **The Utsul:** care of the sick and the dead through their mosques.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** care of the old once ashore.
- **Northern retirees in Sanya and Haikou:** cared for by whom, far from children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on elder care surveys and mission hospitals; mixed on village and temple care; thin on Li, She and Utsul healing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; She; English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family secrets of herbal knowledge; elder neglect; health data privacy; parents' maintenance duties under the law belong to Libra.
