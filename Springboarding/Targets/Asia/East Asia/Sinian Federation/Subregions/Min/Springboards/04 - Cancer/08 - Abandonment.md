*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou-area emigrant towns (Changle, Fuqing, Lianjiang, Pingtan):** recent emigration, smuggling debts, empty houses.
- **Southern Min emigrant counties (Jinjiang, Nan'an, Anxi):** older Nanyang emigration and its songs.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai, Hainan:** Hainanese wives and parents left by emigrants.
- **Coastal villages split in 1949**, as in Hui'an and Pingtan: waiting across the strait.
- **Inland Min villages:** emptied by internal migration; elders alone.
- **Infants left in the past and present:** Qing foundling records against recent welfare institutes.
- **The Li and the She:** outmigration from highland villages, to check.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** families broken by loss at sea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on emigration and its songs; mixed on infant abandonment; thin on elders left alone and on minority villages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; English for diaspora and adoption sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** infanticide; trafficking of children; adoptees' privacy; smuggling; cross-strait politics. The legal duties of support belong to Libra.
