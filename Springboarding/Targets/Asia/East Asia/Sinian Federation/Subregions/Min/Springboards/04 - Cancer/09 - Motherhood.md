## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mindong (Fuzhou, Gutian, Ningde):** the childbirth goddess's heartland.
- **Southern Min (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou, Xiamen):** flower rites, mother-in-law ties, son pressure.
- **Puxian:** coastal mothers with husbands at sea.
- **Hui'an coast:** when a wife comes to live with her husband's family, tied to motherhood.
- **Hainanese Han (Wenchang, Haikou):** their own mother goddess; mothers of emigrant families.
- **The Li:** births in highland villages; branches differ.
- **The She:** an ancestral mother at the heart of origin.
- **The Utsul:** Muslim mothers in Sanya.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka:** births and mothering afloat.
- **Migrant mothers** working in Xiamen and Jinjiang, children elsewhere.
