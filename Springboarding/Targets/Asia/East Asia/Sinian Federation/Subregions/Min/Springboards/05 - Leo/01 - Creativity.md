## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dehua and Yongchun (inland Quanzhou):** porcelain town with a named-master culture.
- **Hui'an coast:** stone carving as a mass trade and export industry, against Shoushan's connoisseur market.
- **Fuzhou:** lacquer, cork painting and stone carving with family lineages.
- **Putian:** gilt woodcarving and the sneaker-copy economy, a sharp test of originality.
- **Jianyang and Nanping (Minbei):** Jian ware revival, new makers claiming old forms.
- **Zhangzhou:** prints and glove-puppet carving.
- **She of Ningde and Fuding:** ribbon weaving and silver work; designs tied to family.
- **Hlai subgroups (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Jiamao) *check*:** each weaves its own patterns; compare, never merge.
- **Kim Mun Miao and Lingao speakers:** small, thinly documented making traditions.
- **Xiamen and Haikou:** design firms, games and startups beside the crafts.
- Hakka tulou builders of western Fujian: named only; their craft belongs to the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han crafts with state master titles; mixed for invention and design; thin for Hlai, Kim Mun and She makers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min for workshop speech; Fuzhou speech for lacquer and stone workshops.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** counterfeiting is a legal and reputational topic, so treat Putian with care and leave the law to Libra; Li patterns may carry lineage meaning, so ask makers before publishing designs.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's puppet and print workshops; Chaoshan woodcarving; diaspora craft guilds.
- **Held for review:** none.
