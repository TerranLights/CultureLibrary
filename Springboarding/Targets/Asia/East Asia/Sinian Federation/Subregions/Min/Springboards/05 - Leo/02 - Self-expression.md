## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou fisherfolk women (Xunpu) and Hui'an women:** two coastal dress traditions, read differently by insiders and visitors.
- **Zhangzhou and Xiamen Southern Min:** opera and song cultures that overlap with Taiwan's.
- **Fuzhou literati families:** letter-writing and essay traditions.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** Puxian opera as another outlet; check its repertoire separately.
- **She of Mindong:** song duels and costume as markers.
- **Hlai elders and youth:** a generation gap in body marking, dress and language.
- **Lingao and Danzhou speakers:** non-Min or mixed speech communities in north and west Hainan.
- **Tanka of Sanya and the Minjiang:** a history of exclusion shaping what could be said.
- **Hui of Sanya (Utsat speakers):** religious dress as expression.
- **Students and migrant workers in Xiamen and Haikou:** online style and code-switching.
- Hakka mountain-song singers in Longyan: a contrast only; the Hakka are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
