## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou Southern Min:** old trade-city pride against agricultural-county pride.
- **Xiamen:** newer, special-economic-zone pride.
- **Fuzhou:** provincial capital and literati names (Lin Zexu, Yan Fu).
- **Putian and Xianyou:** Mazu's home and business networks abroad.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** inland towns, often left out of the coastal story.
- **Hui of Quanzhou:** ancestral pride with a foreign origin.
- **She:** a legend that is both a source of pride and a target.
- **Hainanese towns (Wenchang, Qionghai, Haikou):** emigrant and military pride.
- **Hlai and Kim Mun Miao:** minority pride under state heritage branding.
- **Tanka:** pride rebuilt after past contempt.
- **Lingao and Danzhou speakers:** small speech communities and their claims.
- Hakka of Longyan and Sanming: their ancestral-origin pride is studied in the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and examination pride; mixed for modern civic and sports pride; thin for She, Li and Tanka self-accounts outside state media.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese for gazetteers and genealogies; Southern Min and Fuzhou for sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Panhu legend has been used to mock the She; national-pride survey items are politically framed; Taiwan ties around 《爱拼才会赢》 are comparison only.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan; Chaoshan; Singapore and Penang Hokkien clan associations.
- **Held for review:** none.
