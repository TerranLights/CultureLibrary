# Min: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min emigrant villages (Jinjiang, Shishi, Nan'an):** visibility through houses and donations.
- **Fuqing and Changle (Fuzhou):** high emigration, children raised by grandparents.
- **Xiamen and Gulangyu residents:** living inside a tourist spectacle.
- **Putian:** procession roles and temple display.
- **She brides and singers:** moments of being watched at weddings.
- **Hlai and Cun speakers:** visibility in state counts and media.
- **Hainanese elders (Chengmai, Wenchang):** longevity put on show.
- **Sanya's migrant workers and winter residents:** different kinds of presence in one city.
- **Tanka of Sanya and the Minjiang:** long overlooked on the water's edge.
- **Hui of Sanya:** a visible religious minority in a resort city.
- Hakka villages of western Fujian: a neighboring case of emigrant display, left to the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteer records and emigrant houses; mixed for modern public space; thin for Cun speakers and Tanka self-accounts.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (gazetteers, Zhu Xi); Standard Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou speech for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public surveillance is out of scope for scouting and politically sensitive; children left behind are a private family matter, so name no families; census ethnicity items are political.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan; diaspora communities in New York (Fuzhou), Singapore and Manila.
- **Held for review:** none.
