## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** respectful address forms of their own; literati ideals of bearing.
- **Southern Min villages and factory towns:** dignity through work and through funerals.
- **Putian:** large temple economies that fund care of the dead.
- **Tanka (Minjiang and Sanya):** housing and respect after long contempt.
- **She of Ningde:** poverty narratives told about them by others.
- **Hlai and Kim Mun Miao:** farm labor, settlers and land.
- **Returned overseas Chinese on Hainan and Fujian farms:** dignity of refugees from Southeast Asia.
- **Hainanese elders:** longevity, care and children abroad.
- **Hui of Quanzhou and Sanya:** burial customs that differ from Han practice.
- **Migrant workers from other provinces:** a large, often unseen population in Jinjiang and Haikou.
- Hakka of western Fujian and Hainan: burial and elder-care customs to set aside for the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for state programs and gazetteers, thin for people's own words on respect, especially in Hlai, She and Tanka communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou speech for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty showcases are politically framed; burial reform touches grief and conflict; disability and end-of-life topics need care in interviews.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's hospice and burial customs; Chaoshan charitable halls; diaspora burial grounds in Southeast Asia.
- **Held for review:** none.
