## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou lineage villages:** feud histories driven by insult.
- **Fuzhou emigrant villages (Changle, Fuqing, Lianjiang):** face, debt and failure abroad.
- **Mawei and Fuzhou:** defeat memorialized.
- **Tanka of the Minjiang and Sanya:** stigma by name.
- **Hlai subgroups:** colonial-era labels and present answers.
- **She of Mindong:** outsiders' mocking names to check.
- **Hainanese survivors of the occupation and their families.**
- **Lingao, Danzhou and Cun speakers:** mocked speech, a test of language shame.
- **Pupils in county and city schools:** bullying patterns.
- **Migrant workers and the rural poor:** humiliations of dependence and labor.
- Hakka-Min feud histories in western Fujian: note where they cross Min villages; the Hakka themselves are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; history of feuds and war is documented, everyday humiliation less so; thin for Tanka and Hlai voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for Qing sources; Southern Min and Fuzhou for slurs and sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime sexual violence; 1966-1976 struggle sessions; ethnic slurs (record as terms, never repeat as labels); online shaming of private persons, so name none.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan; Fuzhou communities in New York; Chaoshan feuding.
- **Held for review:** none.
