## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min emigrant counties (Jinjiang, Nan'an, Anxi):** donor halls and school gifts.
- **Fuzhou emigrant villages (Changle, Fuqing):** newer emigration, temple and house building.
- **Putian:** temple patronage and business networks.
- **Northern Fujian (Wuyishan, Jian'ou):** the granary tradition and hill villages.
- **Hainanese of Wenchang and Qionghai:** emigrant gifts from Southeast Asia.
- **Hlai of Wuzhishan and Qiongzhong:** shared labor, compare subgroups.
- **Kim Mun Miao:** small mountain communities, likely thin data.
- **She of Ningde:** shared work and wedding gifts.
- **Hui of Quanzhou and Sanya:** alms customs of their own.
- **Urban middle classes in Xiamen and Haikou:** formal charity and online giving.
- Hakka emigrant donors of Yongding and Shanghang: compare briefly; they belong to the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigrant philanthropy and qiaopi; mixed for festival feasting; thin for Hlai, Kim Mun and She sharing customs.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for granary and gazetteer sources; Southern Min and Hainanese for letters and oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** emigrant gifts carry politics of patriotism; wedding-gift pressure is a family matter; name no private donors.
- **Comparison only:** Chaoshan qiaopi, Singapore and Malaysia clan associations, Fuzhou communities abroad.
- **Held for review:** none.
