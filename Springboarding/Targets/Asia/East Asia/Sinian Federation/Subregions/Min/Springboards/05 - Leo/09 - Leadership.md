## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou plain lineages:** strong lineage management.
- **Southern Min villages:** rotating temple leadership and business bosses.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** ritual alliances with their own leaders.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** inland villages, less studied.
- **She of Mindong:** clan elders and village roles.
- **Hlai subgroups:** elders, headmen and shared-farm heads.
- **Kim Mun Miao:** small hill communities with priest-leaders *check*.
- **Hainanese coastal villages:** festival organizers and emigrant patrons.
- **Tanka crews:** leadership on boats.
- **Hui of Quanzhou and Sanya:** mosque leaders and lineage elders.
- **Xiamen and Haikou firms:** managerial leadership in new industries.
- Hakka lineage leaders of western Fujian: a neighbor for contrast; studied in the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineages and merchants; mixed for village politics; thin for Hlai, Kim Mun and Tanka leadership in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min for temple roles; Fuzhou speech for lineage practice; Hlai terms through Chinese studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party leadership and elections are politically sensitive; the formal right to command belongs to Capricorn; revolutionary histories carry official framing.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan temple committees; Singapore and Malaysia clan-association leaders; Chaoshan.
- **Held for review:** none.
