## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen and Quanzhou Southern Min:** riddles, comic dialogue, puppetry.
- **Zhangzhou:** glove puppets and children's games.
- **Fuzhou:** mahjong, storytelling halls and teahouse play.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** Lantern-season games and processions.
- **Northern Fujian (Jian'ou, Wuyishan):** tea contests past and present.
- **She of Ningde:** song games and sports, thin data.
- **Hlai and Kim Mun Miao:** dance-games and traditional sports.
- **Hainanese of Haikou and Wenchang:** teahouse leisure, dragon boats.
- **Sanya's resort economy:** imported adult play, golf and theme parks.
- **Children in cities against children in emigrant villages:** time and space to play.
- Hakka children's games in Longyan: mention only where they meet Southern Min play; separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for performance and festival games; mixed for children's everyday play; thin for She, Hlai and Kim Mun play outside staged events.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min for rhymes and comic dialogue; Fuzhou speech for mahjong terms; Hainanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** underground lotteries are illegal and linked to crime; name no players; staged minority games may not match community practice.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's glove puppetry and children's rhymes; Chaoshan games; Singapore Hokkien rhymes.
- **Held for review:** none.
