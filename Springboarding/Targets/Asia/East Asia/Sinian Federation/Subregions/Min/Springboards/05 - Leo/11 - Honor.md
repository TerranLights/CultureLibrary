# Min: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou lineages:** feud alliances, sworn brotherhoods, chaste-widow honors.
- **Fuzhou literati:** official integrity and examination honors.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** temple and lineage rites.
- **Dongshan and the Zhangzhou coast:** Guandi loyalty and fisher honor.
- **Hainanese of Qiongshan, Wenchang and Qionghai:** Hai Rui's legacy and emigrant volunteers.
- **Hlai subgroups:** oaths and reconciliation.
- **She of Mindong:** clan honor and naming.
- **Kim Mun Miao:** thin data.
- **Hui of Quanzhou and Sanya:** honor in religious and lineage life.
- **Women of Hui'an and Xunpu:** whose honor was defined for them, and their own accounts.
- Hakka of the Fujian uplands: their lineage codes are researched in the separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gazetteers, officials and secret societies; mixed for living codes; thin for Hlai and She accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Southern Min and Fuzhou speech for sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** chastity suicides and honor tied to women's bodies; Shi Lang and Taiwan politics; secret-society history; martial memorials are state-framed.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan (Shi Lang and Koxinga memory), Singapore and Malaysia secret-society histories, Chaoshan.
- **Held for review:** none.
