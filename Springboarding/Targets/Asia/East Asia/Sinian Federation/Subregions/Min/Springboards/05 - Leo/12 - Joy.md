## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen and Gulangyu:** music and leisure culture.
- **Fuzhou and Changle:** literary joy and emigrant-village celebrations.
- **Putian and Meizhou:** festival seasons and pilgrimage.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou:** funeral bands and temple fairs.
- **Northern and Central Fujian:** quieter hill-town festivals, little studied.
- **Hainanese of Haikou, Qiongshan and Wenchang:** bayin and flower festivals.
- **Danzhou:** Su Shi's memory and Danzhou song.
- **Hlai subgroups and Kim Mun Miao:** drinking songs and festival joy.
- **She of Mindong:** song gatherings.
- **Tanka of Sanya:** weddings on the water.
- **Christians and Hui Muslims:** joy in their own religious calendars.
- Hakka festivals of western Fujian: compare only where they share a valley with Min speakers; separate Hakka sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and literary figures; mixed for measured happiness; thin for Hlai, She, Kim Mun and Tanka joy in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Su Shi; Standard Chinese; Southern Min, Puxian and Hainanese for festival speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** church life is sensitive; minority festivals are often staged for tourists; city rankings are promotional.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's funeral bands and temple festivals; Hainanese and Hokkien Singapore; Chaoshan festivals.
- **Held for review:** none.
