# Min: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified characters); Southern Min (Hokkien, in Pe̍h-ōe-jī), Eastern Min (Fuzhou), Puxian, Northern and Central Min, Hainanese; She, Hlai (Li), Kim Mun, Be (Lingao), Tsat (Utsat). **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what is good work and a well-kept life (daily round, kept house, chores, craft, rest, work ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan coast** (Xiamen, Quanzhou, Zhangzhou) against **inland Minnan** (Anxi, Yongchun, Dehua): factory towns against tea and porcelain uplands.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong**, with **Fuqing and Changle** emigrant villages apart: remittance-shaped work.
- **Puxian** (Putian, Xianyou): its own speech, temple networks and trade specializations.
- **Minbei and Minzhong** (Nanping, Jian'ou, Wuyishan, Sanming): under-studied mountain tea, timber and paper work.
- **Hui'an women's communities**: a distinct gendered division of heavy labor.
- **She** of Ningde, Fu'an and Xiapu: tea hills and their own songs.
- **Boat-dwelling Tanka** (Min River, Xiamen, Sanya): water work and a history of exclusion.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages** (Chendai Ding, Baiqi Guo): Muslim-descended clans in the shoe trade.
- **Hainanese-speaking Han** (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai) against **Danzhou, Lingao (Be) and Cun speakers** of the west.
- **Li** (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Jiamao subgroups), **Hainan Miao** and **Utsat** of Sanya: separate work and healing ways.
- **Returned overseas Chinese and state-farm workers** on Hainan (Xinglong): imported work cultures.
- **Hakka of western Fujian**: name briefly; they belong to their own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou (gazetteers, missionary sources, diaspora studies); mixed for Hainan's Han; thin for Minbei, Minzhong, She, Be, Tsat and Cun. Min's exact lands are still to be settled: Fujian and Hainan are a working list the author will confirm.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Japanese (wartime Hainan surveys); English, French and German (missionary sources).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Taiwan Strait and South China Sea matters; undocumented emigration from Changle and Fuqing; old surveys that call Li customs "backward"; unregistered religion. Comparison only: Taiwan, Kinmen and Matsu; Chaoshan and Leizhou (Guangdong); the Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua diasporas. Law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
