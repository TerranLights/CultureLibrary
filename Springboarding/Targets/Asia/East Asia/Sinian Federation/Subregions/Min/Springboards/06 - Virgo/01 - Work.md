## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang and Shishi factory owners and their inland migrant workforce:** same shop floor, two relations to work.
- **Fuqing and Changle emigrant villages:** work abroad as the expected path; contrast with **Putian's** trade networks (jewelry, timber, private clinics) run from home.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's heavy labor against men's seafaring absence.
- **Anxi and Wuyishan tea households:** seasonal crunch work, family labor.
- **Minbei and Minzhong (Sanming, Mingxi):** forestry, paper and late industry; a separate emigrant stream.
- **Tanka and other fishing communities** (Min River, Pingtan, Dongshan, Sanya): sea work and its risks.
- **She of Ningde:** hill farming and tea.
- **Li, Hainan Miao, Be and Utsat:** communal and household labor outside the Han pattern; Utsat fishing and trading at Sanya.
- **Hainan state-farm families and returnee settlements:** collective work memory.
- **Hainan's newer residents** (1988 arrivals, seasonal retirees from the north): work cultures brought in.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on emigration, factories and tea; mixed on fishing; thin on She, Be, Utsat and Hainan Miao work in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Su Shi and Song Yingxing; Hokkien and Fuzhou for interviews and songs; Hainanese and Hlai for Hainan field work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor disputes and NGO sources; undocumented emigration; Production and Construction Corps memories; old surveys' language about minority "idleness". The Southeast Asian Fuqing, Henghua and Hainanese trades abroad are comparison only. Labor law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
