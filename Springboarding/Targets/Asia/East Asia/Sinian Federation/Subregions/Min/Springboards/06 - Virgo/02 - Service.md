## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou ward temples and Putian village alliances:** service by rotation, festival by festival.
- **Xiamen and Sanya resort workforces,** many from inland provinces, against local owners.
- **Fuzhou official-heritage culture** (Lin Zexu, the Three Lanes and Seven Alleys) against **Minnan merchant philanthropy** (Tan Kah Kee).
- **Returnee families and qiaoxiang villages** (Jinjiang, Fuqing, Wenchang): service to the hometown from abroad.
- **Coastal frontline villages:** militia and military families.
- **Hui'an and other women's networks:** care and temple work done by women.
- **She of Ningde:** wedding and feast service customs, by local account.
- **Li, Hainan Miao and Utsat:** service owed to elders and village heads, as they describe it.
- **Hainan's seasonal northern retirees:** a large population that is served.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on temples, philanthropy and model officials; mixed on domestic service; thin on minority service customs.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for temple and village work; Puxian for Putian alliances; Hainanese and Hlai for Hainan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military and militia service on the Strait; bonded maidservants (婢) in the past; registered and unregistered religious charity; model-official campaigns as political messaging. Overseas Hainanese domestic and coffee-shop service, and Southeast Asian Hokkien clan associations, are comparison only. Conscription law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
