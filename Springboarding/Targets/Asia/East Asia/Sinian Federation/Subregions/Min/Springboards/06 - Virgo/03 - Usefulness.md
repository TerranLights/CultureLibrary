## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuqing and Changle emigrant villages:** grandparents' usefulness tied to emigrants' children and remittances.
- **Minnan coastal towns** where son preference shows in records, against **Hainan's Han and Li** households.
- **Hui'an women:** a reputation for heavy useful labor, to be heard in their own accounts.
- **Tanka and fishing families:** children's help on boats.
- **She of Ningde:** plant knowledge and craft as roles for elders.
- **Li, Hainan Miao, Utsat and Be:** whose knowledge is "useful" and whose language is called less so.
- **Xiamen and Haikou professionals** against **inland Minbei villages** losing working-age people.
- **Disabled residents and their families.**
- **Seasonal northern retirees in Sanya:** a borrowed population of elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elders and migration; mixed for disability; thin for minority knowledge of useful plants and tools.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Fuzhou for family talk; Hainanese, Hlai and She for field work; classical Chinese for Zhu Xi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sex ratios and the "money-losing" slur touch abortion and abandonment of girls; disability stigma; minority languages under pressure. Southeast Asian Hokkien families and Taiwan's Hokkien sayings are comparison only. Pensions and care law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
