## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minbei** (Wuyishan, Jianyang, Jian'ou): the Zhu Xi heritage and its academies.
- **Fuzhou's scholar-official families** and **Putian-Xianyou** with their examination record: book learning as a path.
- **Minnan merchant towns** (Quanzhou, Xiamen, Jinjiang): reckoning for trade.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** links to Arabic and Persian maritime knowledge, as they tell it.
- **Fishing communities of Qionghai and Wenchang:** sea knowledge kept by practice.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** their own ways of reading weather, soil and season.
- **She of Ningde:** farming knowledge and its testing.
- **Scientific enclaves** (Sanya seed labs, the Wenchang launch site, Xiamen's tech firms) against nearby farm villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Song and modern intellectual history; mixed on everyday reckoning; thin on minority knowledge systems in their own voices. PISA's Chinese samples have not covered these provinces (*check*), so school data must come from national panels.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Hokkien and Hainanese for practitioners; English for psychology literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the route books are cited in South China Sea claims; treat as navigation records only. Divination practice is described in practitioners' terms, without judging it. Taiwanese and Singaporean riddle and almanac traditions are comparison only. Courts and evidence law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
