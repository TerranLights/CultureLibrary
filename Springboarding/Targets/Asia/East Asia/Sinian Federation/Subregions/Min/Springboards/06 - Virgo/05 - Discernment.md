## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tea districts:** Anxi, Wuyishan, Fuding (white tea) and Fuzhou (jasmine), each with its own marks of quality.
- **Fuzhou stone carvers and collectors** against buyers from elsewhere.
- **Putian traders,** whose city name is bound up with fakes in national talk; hear them in their own words.
- **Minnan market towns:** bargaining and goods-judging at the stall.
- **Hainan agarwood and rosewood dealers** and the **Li and Miao foragers** who supply them.
- **Quanzhou's Buddhist lay circles** and **temple-going households:** spiritual counsel.
- **Elders in emigrant villages:** frequent targets of remittance and phone scams.
- **She, Utsat and Tanka:** judging outsiders and traders from the margin.
