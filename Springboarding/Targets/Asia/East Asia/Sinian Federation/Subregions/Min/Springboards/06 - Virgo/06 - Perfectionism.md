*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), East Asia: the Sinian Federation member state carrying Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Hokkien; Fuzhou (for lacquer and stone workshops); Puxian (for Xianyou furniture makers); Hainanese; Hlai and She for textile makers. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou workshop crafts** (lacquer, Shoushan stone) against **Putian and Xianyou** furniture and carving.
- **Hui'an and Chongwu stoneworkers** and **Dehua potters**: inherited lines and newcomers.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou performers** (puppetry, Liyuan, Nanyin amateurs).
- **Factory workforces** at Fuyao, CATL and Jinjiang plants, against owners and engineers.
- **Li weavers** by subgroup (Run, Meifu, Ha, Qi, Jiamao), and **She** silversmiths and embroiderers.
- **Ritual specialists:** Daoist masters, temple elders and lineage ritual heads.
- **Students** in elite city schools against vocational schools and emigrant-village schools.
- **Hainanese-Han coconut carvers and cooks** against Li and Miao makers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on crafts and heritage files; mixed on industry; thin on clinical perfectionism by province and on minority makers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for Quanzhou and Zhangzhou performers; Fuzhou for lacquer and stone; Puxian for Xianyou; Hlai and She for textiles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student mental health and suicide (handle with care; no figures as findings); workplace pressure; heritage-bearer titles as status and money. Taiwan's puppetry and Chaoshan's crafts are comparison only. Product-standards law goes to Libra.
