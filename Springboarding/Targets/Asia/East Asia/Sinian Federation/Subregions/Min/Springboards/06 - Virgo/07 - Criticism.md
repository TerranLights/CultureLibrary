## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haikou and Hainanese Han** with the Hai Rui heritage against **Hainan's minorities**.
- **Quanzhou:** Li Zhi, Gaojia clowns and old ward rivalries.
- **Xiamen:** educated urban critics, comic dialogue and protest history.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** storytelling satire and scholar-official reproach.
- **Zhangzhou:** Lin Yutang's indirect humor and its local reception.
- **Li, She and Hainan Miao:** song duels and teasing.
- **Factory floors** with Taiwanese, Hong Kong or local bosses and inland workers.
- **Generations:** elders who lived through struggle sessions; young online "roasters".
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** named only for Gutian; their own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on classical remonstrance and famous critics; mixed online (deleted posts); thin on family scolding and minority teasing.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Hokkien and Fuzhou for performance; Hlai and She for songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political dissent, protest and censorship; Cultural Revolution memory; deleted online material. Taiwan's satire and the diaspora press are comparison only. Defamation and petition law go to Libra.
