## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan coast:** god-centered healing (Baosheng Dadi), herbal stalls, hospitals side by side.
- **Mindong (Fuzhou, Gutian, Ningde):** childbirth cults and Fuzhou dietary habits.
- **Minzhong (Sanming):** a reform model in a mountain city.
- **Putian:** private-hospital entrepreneurs, to be heard in their own words, not through a byword.
- **Yongchun and other boxing towns:** bone-setters.
- **She:** herbal medicine and healers.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** forest remedies; malaria history in the highlands.
- **Utsat and Quanzhou Hui:** halal diet and health customs, as they describe them.
- **Tanka and fishing families:** working life on water and access to care.
- **Factory migrants in Jinjiang and Xiamen:** occupational health.
- **Medical tourists and northern retirees in Hainan.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on temples, hospitals and statistics; mixed on folk healers; thin on Li, She, Be and Utsat health in their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for temples and stalls; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai and She; classical Chinese for gazetteers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' restricted knowledge (ask before recording); the Putian hospital network; disease stigma; pricing disputes. Southeast Asian Hokkien temple healing and Taiwan's Baosheng temples are comparison only. Health-insurance law goes to Libra.
