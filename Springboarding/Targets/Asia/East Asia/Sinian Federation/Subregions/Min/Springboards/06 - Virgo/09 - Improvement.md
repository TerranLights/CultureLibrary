## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qiaoxiang** (emigrant home districts: Jinjiang, Shishi, Fuqing, Changle, Wenchang): betterment through departure and return money.
- **Ningde mountain counties and She villages:** objects of state poverty campaigns.
- **Tanka** of the Min River and Sanya: resettled ashore.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** housing, land and custom "improved" by others; their own renewal efforts.
- **Xiamen and Haikou:** special-zone cities built on the promise of progress.
- **Minbei and Minzhong uplands:** depopulation and late development.
- **Hainan returnee farms and 1988 arrivals:** improvement sought by moving.
- **Utsat and Quanzhou Hui:** community rebuilding on their own terms.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** separate sub-unit; mention only for shared relocation programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on development policy and emigrant houses; mixed on resettlement; thin on the voices of those resettled.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Qiu Jun and the sweet-potato texts; Hokkien and Fuzhou for emigrant families; She, Hlai and Tanka speech for resettlement accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political texts and campaigns; forced or pressured relocation; customs suppressed as "backward"; language policy. Overseas returnees' philanthropy belongs in its home places; diaspora betterment abroad is comparison only. Land and planning law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
