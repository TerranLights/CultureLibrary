## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan:** the Quanzhou, Xiamen and Zhangzhou coast against the Anxi and Dehua hills; the Hui'an coast for its residence rule.
- **Mindong:** Fuzhou against the emigrant towns of Fuqing and Changle, and Ningde to the north.
- **Puxian:** Putian and Xianyou, a speech and ritual area of its own.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** Jian'ou, Nanping; Sanming, Yong'an, Shaxian; least written-about.
- **She:** Ningde, Fu'an, Xiapu, Luoyuan; own marriage songs and ancestor rites.
- **Hui-descended lineages of Quanzhou** (Chendai Ding, Baiqi Guo): marriage across a religious line.
- **Boat-dwelling families** (连家船民 on the Min River; 疍家 of Sanya): long kept apart in marriage.
- **Manchu garrison descendants** at Qinjiang, Changle.
- **Hainan:** Hainanese-speaking Han (Wenchang, Qionghai, Haikou); the Li and their subgroups (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Jiamao); the Miao of Hainan; Lingao and Danzhou speakers; the Utsul Muslims of Sanya; returned overseas Chinese on the overseas-Chinese farms; migrants and seasonal retirees in Sanya.
- **Hakka of western Fujian** (Yongding, Changting): brief only; a separate sub-unit.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan (separate target); Chaoshan and Leizhou (in the Yue member state); Southeast Asian Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou; mixed for Hainanese Han; thin for Minbei, Minzhong, the She, the Utsul and Lingao speakers. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for Fujian and Hainan as a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Hainanese; Hlai, She and Tsat with native speakers; Japanese (wartime Hainan ethnography); German; English.
