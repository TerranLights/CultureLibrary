# Min (闽): Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Fujian and Hainan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Hainanese; Hlai (Li), She. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou Minnan:** a long record of lineage feuds and private settlements beside the magistrate's court.
- **Fuzhou and the Mindong coast:** the provincial capital's courts against the emigrant towns, where justice could be pursued from abroad.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** a dense temple culture whose stage and ritual answer wrongs in their own way.
- **Northern Fujian (Jianyang, Jian'ou):** the home ground of Song Ci and of Zhu Xi's learning.
- **Sanming and Yong'an:** inland valleys with few studies of how disputes reached court.
- **She villages of Ningde:** their own household and ancestral rules.
- **Hui-descended Ding and Guo lineages:** whether religion shaped the rules they kept.
- **Hainan Li, by subgroup:** compensation and headmen in the interior, unequal in record.
- **Hainanese Han of Wenchang and Qionghai:** the Hai Rui heritage and emigrant families.
- **Utsul of Sanya; Miao of Hainan:** the smallest records; ask the communities first.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** brief, a separate sub-unit.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan (a separate target, with its rich Qing court archives); Chaoshan; Hokkien and Hainanese clan courts of Southeast Asia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern Fujian judgments and Qing archives; mixed for present-day courts; thin for the Li, She, Utsul and Miao in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese, Mandarin; Hokkien and Hainanese for oral history; Hlai and She with native speakers; Japanese for the Tokyo lawsuits.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wrongful convictions and political-campaign redress are sensitive in public; survivors of wartime violence and their families; lineage punishments that families may prefer not to publicize.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the 《名公书判清明集》 for decisions by Fujian officials and list the kinds of wrongs they judged.

4. Search news archives for "念斌案" and "陈满案" and collect the local coverage at each stage.
5. Visit or search the publications of the Quanzhou city-god temple for its underworld-court imagery.
