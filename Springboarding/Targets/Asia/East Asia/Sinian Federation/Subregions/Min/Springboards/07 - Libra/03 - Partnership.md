## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian plain:** the densest village alliances through shared temples.
- **Xiamen and Quanzhou:** ship and trade partnerships of the old port cities.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** family and multi-household firms in the 1980s and after.
- **Changle, Fuqing and Lianjiang:** couples and households split by emigration.
- **Ningde mountain She:** marriages where either side's home may be the couple's.
- **Hui'an and Chongwu coast:** women's sisterhood groups and marriage residence.
- **Northern Fujian (Wuyi, Nanping):** tea-firm partnerships; barely studied.
- **Hui-descended lineages of Jinjiang:** marriage partners chosen across religious lines.
- **Northern Hainan (Haikou, Qiongshan, Wenchang):** Lady Xian's cult and emigrant families.
- **Li and Miao of central Hainan:** courting and marriage as each people describes them.
- **Returnees on Hainan and Fujian overseas-Chinese farms:** mixed marriages and new work bonds.
- **Danjia and Lingao communities:** marriage kept within the group or not.
- **Hakka of Yongding and Shanghang (a separate sub-unit):** noted only where Min and Hakka families intermarry or trade.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan; Chaoshan merchant partnerships; Fuzhou and Hokkien partnerships overseas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and village alliances in coastal Fujian; good for emigrant families; thin for She and Li partnership in their own words, and for Minbei and Minzhong.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou and Puxian for fieldwork; Hainanese; Hlai and She with native speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cross-strait marriages; undocumented emigration from the Fuzhou area; the history of Hui'an sisterhood groups includes deaths that families may not wish discussed.
- **Held for review:** none.
