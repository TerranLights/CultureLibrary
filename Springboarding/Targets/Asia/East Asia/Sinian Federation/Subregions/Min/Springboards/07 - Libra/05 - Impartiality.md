## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian and Xianyou:** a long examination tradition, and a stage culture that debates duty against kin.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** strong lineages and "big surname, small surname" villages, where neutral rules meet bloc loyalty.
- **Xiamen:** a Special Economic Zone and the city of the largest favoritism case.
- **Fuzhou:** the provincial seat, examination hall and the Lin Zexu legacy.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** emigrant families with privileged access through overseas ties.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** small inland towns where everyone knows the official; little studied.
- **She of Ningde:** bonus points and elders' authority.
- **Hui-descended lineages:** official minority status and the debates around it.
- **Hainan Li and Miao:** headmen, autonomous-county offices and bonus points.
- **Hainanese Han of Haikou:** the early Special Economic Zone and its scandals.
- **Hainan state-farm communities:** a separate administrative world with its own rules.
- **Hakka of Changting and Ninghua (a separate sub-unit):** only where shared exam and posting rules cross the dialect line.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's examination and civil-service traditions; Chaoshan networks; overseas Hokkien and Hainanese associations' rules on office.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for examinations and official scandals; mixed for village politics; thin for how Li and She communities judge their own.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for examination and administrative records; Mandarin; Hokkien and Puxian for oral and stage sources; Japanese; Hlai and She with native speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases with living subjects; anticorruption politics; minority bonus points are debated publicly.
- **Held for review:** none.
