## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou villages:** the Pudu round and dense gift registers.
- **Xiamen and Jimei:** emigrant philanthropy and its public memory.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** remittance-funded giving and its obligations at home.
- **Putian:** temple donor lists and vow plays.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's networks of help.
- **Minbei and Minzhong villages:** exchange labor on rice and tea farms; little studied.
- **She of Ningde and Fu'an:** mutual help at weddings and funerals.
- **Hui-descended lineages:** gift customs at Muslim-derived and Han occasions.
- **Hainanese Han of Wenchang, Qionghai and Ding'an:** village-god feasts and emigrant donations.
- **Li and Miao:** labor exchange and feasts, as each people describes them.
- **Danjia fishers:** help between boats; little recorded.
- **Hakka neighbors in Longyan and Sanming (a separate sub-unit):** only where gift exchange crosses into Min villages.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Pudu; Chaoshan gift customs; Southeast Asian Hokkien and Hainanese associations and their mutual-aid funds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temple giving and emigrant philanthropy; mixed for present-day household exchange; thin for Li, She and Danjia exchange in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for inscriptions; Mandarin; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian and Hainanese for registers, songs and interviews; Hlai and She with native speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gift registers are private family records; rotating-credit failures can involve living debtors; seventh-month rites are sacred to practitioners.
- **Held for review:** none.
