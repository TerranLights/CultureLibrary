## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Xiamen:** home of the day-choosing almanac and busy temple lot booths.
- **Zhangzhou and Anxi:** village temples where moon blocks settle farm and business choices.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** spirit mediums and processions where gods are asked to decide.
- **Fuzhou:** different temple customs and a different language for hesitation.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** mountain villages; few records of how choices are made.
- **She of Ningde:** what their elders and rites do with a hard choice.
- **Hui-descended lineages:** whether temple consulting is shared or avoided.
- **Hainanese Han:** island temples and the village-god cults.
- **Li and Miao:** readings by bones and eggs, and the elders who interpret them.
- **Utsul of Sanya:** Muslim practice, which may refuse such consulting; ask first.
- **Xiamen's young professionals:** a city cohort where decision talk may follow national trends.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where they share temples and lot sets with Min neighbors.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's temples and moon-block customs; Chaoshan practice; Hokkien temples overseas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on temple consulting and day-choosing; mixed on everyday indecision; thin on Li and She practice in their own words, and on psychological measures by province.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Puxian for temple speech; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai and She with native speakers; Classical Chinese for lot verses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** consulting practices are sacred to practitioners and sometimes officially discouraged; underground lotteries are illegal; Muslim communities may object to being grouped with temple practice.
- **Held for review:** none.
