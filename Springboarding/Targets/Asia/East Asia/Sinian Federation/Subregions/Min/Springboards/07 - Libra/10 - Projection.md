## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen, Zhangzhou and Quanzhou coast:** plague-boat rites and old rivalry between Zhangzhou and Quanzhou speakers.
- **Quanzhou's Hui-descended lineages:** heirs of a port where foreigners were once blamed and killed.
- **Gutian and Mindong:** the missionary cases and converts' descendants.
- **Putian:** a city now carrying a national stereotype.
- **Fuzhou, Fuqing and Changle:** stereotypes about emigrants, held at home and elsewhere.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** whether mountain counties are the butt of coastal jokes.
- **She of Ningde and Fu'an:** a people whose sacred story was turned against them.
- **Boat-dwelling families of the Min River and Sanya:** long the despised "other".
- **Li of Hainan:** curse accusations inside the community, and outsiders' images of the Li.
- **Utsul of Sanya:** a small Muslim community among Han and Li neighbors.
- **Hainanese Han and northern migrants:** who blames whom in Sanya.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for the images Min speakers and Hakka hold of each other.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's plague-boat rites and Zhangzhou-Quanzhou feuds; Chaoshan; Hokkien and Hainanese communities in Southeast Asia, often the target of local blame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the plague-boat rite, missionary cases and the Danjia; mixed for present-day regional stereotypes; thin for Li curse accusations and She responses in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for gazetteers; Mandarin; Hokkien for ritual speech; Fuzhou and Hainanese; Hlai and She with native speakers; English, French and other mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** curse accusations can still harm living people; the Panhu story is sacred to the She and was used to insult them; stereotypes of Putian and Fuzhou people must be recorded as claims, never as findings; wartime collaboration trials.
- **Held for review:** none.
