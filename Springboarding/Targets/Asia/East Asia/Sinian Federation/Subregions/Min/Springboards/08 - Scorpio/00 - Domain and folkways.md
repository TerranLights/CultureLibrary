**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan (working list). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Putonghua); Southern Min (Hokkien, Pe̍h-ōe-jī spelling); Eastern Min (Fuzhou, Foochow Romanized); Puxian (Hinghwa); Northern and Central Min; Hainanese; She; Hlai (Li); Kim Mun; Lingao (Be); Tsat (Utsul). **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how people face death, change, shared and inherited resources, sex, power and what is hidden.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min** of Xiamen, Quanzhou and Zhangzhou: the largest group; Quanzhou and Zhangzhou customs differ and should be split.
- **Eastern Min** of Fuzhou, Fuqing, Changle and Ningde: separate language and ritual calendar.
- **Puxian (Xinghua)** of Putian and Xianyou: dense temple and ritual-alliance life.
- **Northern and Central Min** of Nanping, Jian'ou and Sanming: inland, less studied, with a Neo-Confucian heritage.
- **Hui'an coast** women and their marriage residence customs: a distinct sex and household pattern to test.
- **She** of eastern Fujian: their own ancestor scroll, initiation and funeral rites.
- **Hui (Muslim) lineages** of Quanzhou (Chendai, Baiqi): Islamic and Han ancestral practice side by side.
- **Tanka** boat people of the Min River and Hainan's coasts: historically excluded from land and lineage.
- **Hainanese** of Wenchang, Qionghai and Haikou: emigrant households, ancestral halls kept by remittances.
- **Li (Hlai)** subgroups (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai) in Hainan's interior; **Kim Mun ("Miao")**; **Lingao, Danzhou and Cun** speakers; **Utsul** of Sanya.
- **Hakka** of western Fujian (Longyan, Sanming): named only; they are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou (centuries of lineage and ritual documents, Xiamen University scholarship); mixed for Puxian and Minbei; thin and mostly Mandarin-filtered for the Li, Lingao and Utsul.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Fuzhou (with romanizations); classical Chinese for gazetteers and deeds; Hlai for Li fieldwork.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (Fujian and Hainan are a working list; the status of Kinmen, Matsu and Hainan's offshore claims is open).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral reform and cremation campaigns, Cultural Revolution deaths, and family secrets; law on inheritance and marriage goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan (separate target), Chaoshan and Leizhou (Yue member state), Southeast Asian Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua diasporas.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (historic port material), set aside under the standing rule.
