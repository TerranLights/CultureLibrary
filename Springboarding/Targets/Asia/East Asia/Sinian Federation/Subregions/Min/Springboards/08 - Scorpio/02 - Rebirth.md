## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian–Xianyou:** dense temple revival, the Three-in-One, and a strong Protestant presence in one small area.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** cradle of indigenous Protestant movements and of older Catholic villages.
- **Quanzhou and Xiamen Buddhists:** large monasteries and lay devotees speaking of rebirth.
- **Zhangzhou and the southwest coast:** coming-of-age and temple revival patterns to set against Quanzhou.
- **She:** initiation rites with new names.
- **Li of the interior:** craft and ritual revival led from inside, set against tourism-led revival.
- **Hui of Quanzhou:** lineages that returned to an Islamic identity in the 1980s and 1990s, as they describe it.
- **Returned Overseas Chinese:** new lives started in Fujian and Hainan after leaving Southeast Asia.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** named only; separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temple revival and Daoist ritual (Dean and Zheng); mixed for Christianity (state, church and overseas sources differ sharply); thin for Li, Lingao and She inner accounts.
