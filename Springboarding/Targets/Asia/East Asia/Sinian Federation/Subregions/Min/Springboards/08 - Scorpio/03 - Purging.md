## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen–Zhangzhou coast:** ship-sending rites and plague-lord temples.
- **Fuzhou and its suburbs:** Five Emperors cult and its own boat-sending.
- **Putian–Xianyou:** fire-walking and temple processions on a crowded calendar.
- **Quanzhou and Hui'an:** soul-calling, herbal cures and lineage expulsions to compare.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** inland temples, less studied.
- **Hainanese coast:** Hai Rui's legacy and island purge history.
- **Li, Kim Mun and Lingao speakers:** healing and cleansing by their own ritual specialists.
- **Utsul and Quanzhou Hui:** Islamic purity and fasting.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** named only (the Ting River spill site).
