## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou city:** long memory of the Pu Shougeng episode; Han and Hui accounts to set side by side.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** Shi Lang's home ground and emigrant households.
- **Zhangzhou and Zhao'an coast:** Ming smuggling, Zheng-era loyalties, Xiang opera.
- **Fuzhou and Fuqing:** Min opera, the 1933 revolt, emigrant marriages.
- **Putian:** temple alliances where switching allegiance is a known issue.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai (Hainan):** emigrant husbands and wives left at home.
- **Hui lineages (Chendai, Baiqi):** how they handle the Pu Shougeng story about themselves.
- **Li and She:** betrayal motifs in their own oral tales.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for alliances broken along the Min–Hakka edge in the Zhangzhou and Longyan uplands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Zheng era and Ming coastal history; mixed for wartime collaboration; thin for everyday infidelity data by province.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Song and Ming sources; Hokkien, Fuzhou and Hainanese for opera and slang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 汉奸 labels and collaboration; cross-Strait defections; Cultural Revolution denunciations; the Pu Shougeng story may be used against Hui communities, so handle with care.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Koxinga and Shi Lang memory; diaspora second households in Southeast Asia; Chaoshan migrant marriages.
- **Held for review:** none.
