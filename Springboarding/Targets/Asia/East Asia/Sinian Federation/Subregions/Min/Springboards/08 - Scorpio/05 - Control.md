## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen:** special economic zone and city credit scheme; former frontline.
- **Fuzhou:** provincial capital, Manchu garrison legacy, its own credit scheme.
- **Quanzhou–Jinjiang:** private-enterprise towns where family firms and lineages steer members.
- **Putian:** temple alliances that coordinate village life.
- **Minbei and Minzhong uplands:** dams, forests and resettled villages.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's work and household authority, to set beside other coasts.
- **Tanka:** boat dwellers moved ashore under later campaigns.
- **Hainan towns and the free trade port's new arrivals:** a border regime and fast in-migration.
- **Li uplands:** park rules and forest access.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where upland resettlement and forest rules reach Hakka and Min villages alike.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state campaigns and on Ming–Qing control (gazetteers, Szonyi); mixed on household control; thin on Li and Tanka accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien for almanac and temple terms; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance, birth control enforcement, resettlement; credit schemes may change quickly.
- **Comparison only:** Kinmen's military-era control (Szonyi, *Cold War Island*, *check*); Taiwan's temple oracles; Chaoshan family firms.
- **Held for review:** none.
