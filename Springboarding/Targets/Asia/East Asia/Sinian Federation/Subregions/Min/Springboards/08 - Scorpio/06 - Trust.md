## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou–Jinjiang–Shishi:** kin and emigrant capital in family firms.
- **Putian:** a hometown whose trade networks carry both trust and scandal.
- **Fuzhou, Changle and Fuqing:** migration chains built on kin credit and smugglers.
- **Xiamen:** remittance-house history and a younger, mobile population.
- **Zhangzhou countryside:** savings clubs and village temples as places of oath.
- **Minbei uplands:** tea and timber trades, less studied.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai:** remittance families and coffee-shop regulars.
- **Li villages:** their own record-keeping and mutual aid.
- **She of Ningde:** trust between She and Han neighbors.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where Min and Hakka traders give each other credit in shared market towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for qiaopi and migration networks; mixed for survey trust by province (small samples); thin for Li and She accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Fuzhou for letters and slang; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human smuggling; scam and fraud cases; state "trust" lists; trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law (Capricorn).
