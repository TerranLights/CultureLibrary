## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou coast and Hui'an:** the classic feud zone with named alliances.
- **Zhangzhou and its Hakka border:** revenge across dialect and lineage lines.
- **Putian:** temple alliances that both caused and settled disputes.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** factional and urban conflict, less known for lineage feuds.
- **Minbei uplands:** few studies; check whether feuding was rare or unrecorded.
- **Hainanese plains:** land-reform settling of accounts and wartime reprisals.
- **Li of the interior:** feuds between villages and their settlement.
- **She and Tanka:** small groups often on the losing end of disputes.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for their side of the cross-dialect feuds their Min neighbors remember.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing feuds and the Hainan revolutionary story; mixed for the Cultural Revolution; thin for Li and She practice.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien and Puxian for opera; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** factional violence and land-reform killings; wartime reprisals; present-day village conflicts. Private vengeance against the state's punishment is the spirit of the law (Capricorn).
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Zhangzhou–Quanzhou feuds; Chaoshan lineage feuds (Yue member state); diaspora clan wars in nineteenth-century Malaya and Singapore.
- **Held for review:** none.
