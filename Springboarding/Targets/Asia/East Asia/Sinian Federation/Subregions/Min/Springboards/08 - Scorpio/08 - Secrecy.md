# Min: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangzhou and the southwest:** brotherhood origins and frontier secrecy.
- **Xiamen:** port rebellions, frontline broadcasting, smuggling exposure.
- **Quanzhou–Jinjiang:** Manichaean and Muslim histories kept partly hidden.
- **Ningde, Fu'an and Xiapu:** Catholic lineages and hidden manuscripts.
- **Putian:** ritual masters and closed manuals.
- **Fuzhou:** argots and the Fuzhou-language underground of trade.
- **Hainanese villages:** revolutionary secrecy and emigrant families' unspoken stories.
- **Li:** hidden-harm accusations and restricted ritual knowledge.
- **She:** ritual knowledge held within initiated men.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where sworn brotherhoods recruited across the Min–Hakka line.

## 4. Flags
