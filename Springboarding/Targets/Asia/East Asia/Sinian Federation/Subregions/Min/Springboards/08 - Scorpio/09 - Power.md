## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou:** the old royal and provincial seat; officials and lineages.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** merchant power, then and now.
- **Zhangzhou:** founder-god legitimacy and southwestern lineages.
- **Putian:** temple elites and nationally known business networks.
- **Xiamen:** special economic zone power and its scandals.
- **Minbei:** Neo-Confucian gentry heritage, quieter political profile.
- **Haikou and Hainan's special zones:** fast-moving power tied to island privileges.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai:** emigrant wealth and famous families.
- **Li and Kim Mun uplands:** headmen, cadres and park authorities.
- **She:** a minority's charter claims.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (Gutian, Changting):** named only; the party's revolutionary sites lie there.
