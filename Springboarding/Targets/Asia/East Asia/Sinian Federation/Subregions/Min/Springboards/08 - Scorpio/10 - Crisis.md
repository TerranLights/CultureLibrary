## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Minnan (Zhangpu to Jinjiang):** evacuation memory, typhoons, the shelling years.
- **Fuzhou and the Min River:** floods and urban response.
- **Pingtan and Hui'an:** fishing villages and losses at sea.
- **Putian:** temple-led relief after disasters, to check.
- **Minbei and Minzhong:** mountain floods and landslides.
- **Haikou and the northeast coast:** earthquake memory and the property crash.
- **Wenchang:** typhoon front line, emigrant help in hard times.
- **Returned Overseas Chinese:** crisis abroad as founding story.
- **Li uplands:** wartime and famine crises, from their own accounts.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where mountain floods and relief work crossed the Longyan–Zhangzhou line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for natural disasters and the Qing evacuation; mixed for famine and wartime (muted local records); thin for Li crisis memory.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for gazetteers; Hokkien and Hainanese for oral histories; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine deaths; wartime atrocities; the shelling years; pandemic control.
- **Comparison only:** Kinmen's side of 1958; Taiwan's typhoon culture; Southeast Asian Hokkien memories of the 1930s depression.
- **Held for review:** none.
