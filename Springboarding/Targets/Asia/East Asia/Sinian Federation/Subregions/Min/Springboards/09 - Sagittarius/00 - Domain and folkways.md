## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan** (Xiamen, Quanzhou, Zhangzhou): dense temple networks and old port faiths; split the three.
- **Eastern Min** (Fuzhou, Fuqing, Changle, Ningde): church centers beside Chen Jinggu's cult.
- **Puxian** (Putian, Xianyou): Mazu's homeland and the Three-in-One teaching.
- **Northern Min** (Nanping, Jianyang, Wuyishan): Zhu Xi's academies and printing towns, the scholarly core.
- **Central Min** (Sanming, Yong'an, Shaxian): least studied for belief.
- **Hainanese** (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai, Wanning): Lady Xian and emigrant-funded temples.
- **Western Hainan** (Danzhou, Lingao, Cun speakers): Su Dongpo's exile legacy; separate languages.
- **Li (Hlai)** subgroups (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai): ancestor and spirit practice, under-documented.
- **Kim Mun** ("Miao") of Hainan: Daoist-style ordination traditions.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: the one Muslim community native to these lands.
- **She** of Mindong: their own ancestor legend and ritual.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages** (Chendai, Baiqi): Muslim descent, Han ancestral halls.
- **Tanka** boat people: sea gods and shore shrines.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where their mountain temples share gods with Min villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan, Puxian and Fuzhou religion and for Neo-Confucian philosophy; thin and Mandarin-filtered for the Li, Lingao and Utsul.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou and Puxian with their romanizations; Hlai for fieldwork.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (Fujian and Hainan are a working list; Kinmen, Matsu and Hainan's offshore claims are open).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state control of religion; unregistered churches; groups labeled heterodox; Islam on Hainan; South China Sea claims in sailing manuals. Law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan (separate target); Chaoshan and Leizhou (Yue member state); Southeast Asian Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua diasporas.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (Song–Yuan port monuments), set aside under the standing rule.
