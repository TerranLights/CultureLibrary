# Min: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai; She; Tsat. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Puxian** of Putian and Meizhou: faith organized through Mazu's root temple and pilgrim traffic.
- **Minnan** (Zhangzhou, Quanzhou, Xiamen): healer gods and many vow cycles; Anxi's tea villages separately.
- **Eastern Min women** (Fuzhou, Gutian, Ningde): Chen Jinggu's childbirth faith.
- **Mindong Catholics** (Fu'an, Ningde): an old village church faith.
- **Fuzhou and Putian Protestants**: revivalist and denominational faith, urban and village.
- **Minbei** (Jianyang, Wuyishan): Buddhist and Daoist mountains in a Neo-Confucian heartland.
- **Hainanese fishermen** (Tanmen, Wenchang): sea faith tied to long voyages.
- **Li (Hlai)**: ancestor and spirit practice with its own specialists.
- **Kim Mun** of Hainan: Daoist-style ordinations as a faith commitment.
- **Utsul** of Sanya and **Quanzhou Hui** lineages: Muslim faith, kept in very different ways.
- **She** of Mindong: ancestor faith around their origin legend.
- **Tanka** boat people: shrines afloat and on shore.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where their Dingguang Buddha 定光古佛 draws Min worshipers too.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Mazu, Chen Jinggu and Puxian village faith; mixed for Christian communities; thin for Li, Kim Mun and Utsul faith.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien, Fuzhou and Puxian for temple and church speech; Arabic loanwords for the Hui; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches and Mindong Catholic divisions; Islam on Hainan; surveys may undercount faith that people do not call "religion". Do not treat medium possession as pathology.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Mazu pilgrimages; Southeast Asian Hokkien and Hainanese temples.
- **Held for review:** none.
