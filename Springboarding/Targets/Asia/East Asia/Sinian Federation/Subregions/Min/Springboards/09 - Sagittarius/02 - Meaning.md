# Min: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; She; Hlai. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changle and Fuqing** (Eastern Min): life plans shaped around going abroad.
- **Quanzhou and Xiamen** (Minnan): private business and amateur music as competing sources of purpose.
- **Zhangzhou countryside** (Pinghe, Zhangpu): farming valleys that send children to cities; Lin Yutang's home.
- **Putian** (Puxian): Three-in-One congregations and temple service as purpose.
- **Minbei and Central Min** (Nanping, Sanming): out-migration and aging villages.
- **Hainanese** of Wenchang and Qionghai: emigrant families and the homes they built with remittances.
- **New and seasonal arrivals on Hainan**: northern retirees and free-trade-port workers.
- **Li (Hlai)**: songs and rites that state a good life in their own terms.
- **She** of Mindong: song as the frame for a life.
- **Kim Mun** and **Utsul**: small communities whose meaning-making is barely documented.
- **Tanka** fishing families: sea life, resettlement ashore and what was lost.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where tulou families' sense of a life's point meets Minnan neighbors in Nanjing and Pinghe counties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; literature and Minnan song are rich, survey data are national with small provincial samples, minority sources thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for song and speech; Fuzhou for Changle; She and Hlai through song collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and depression data are patchy and politically handled; "lying flat" talk is sometimes censored; emigration from Changle includes irregular routes. Do not treat one writer as speaking for the region.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's Hokkien song industry; Fuzhounese in New York; Southeast Asian Hokkien and Hainanese families.
- **Held for review:** none.
