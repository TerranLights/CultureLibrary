# Min: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangzhou and Quanzhou coast** (Minnan): long histories of sailing against bans and of smuggling.
- **Xiamen**: coolie-port memory and a special economic zone's freedoms.
- **Fuzhou** (Eastern Min): reform-era translators and revolutionaries.
- **Putian and Xianyou** (Puxian): emigrants who left for freedom of trade.
- **Hui'an coast women**: marriage customs that bear on a woman's freedom.
- **Minbei and Central Min** uplands: farther from the sea, where freedom of movement meant road and river.
- **Qionghai and Wenchang** (Hainanese): the Red Detachment story and emigrant routes.
- **Li (Hlai)**: courtship freedom and the uprising memory.
- **She** of Mindong: resettlement from the mountains and how it changed movement.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: religious-practice freedom as a minority.
- **Tanka** boat people: long barred from settling ashore.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for the 1930s Soviet base area at their edge, which Min histories of liberation also cite.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for maritime bans, the coolie trade and revolutionary memory; mixed for marriage freedom; thin for minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political speech, press freedom and religious freedom are controlled topics; surveys on them may be refused or self-censored; Kinmen and Taiwan-strait material is politically charged. Law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's own freedom narratives; Southeast Asian Hokkien memories of indenture.
- **Held for review:** none.
