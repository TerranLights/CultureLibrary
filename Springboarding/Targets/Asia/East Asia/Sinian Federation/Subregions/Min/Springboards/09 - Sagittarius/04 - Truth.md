*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; She; Hlai. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minbei** (Jianyang, Wuyishan): academy towns where Neo-Confucian truth was taught and printed.
- **Fuzhou** (Eastern Min): translators and scientists; the city's own science institutes.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou** (Minnan): oath rites and temple lots as ways to settle a fact.
- **Xiamen**: storytellers who blend history and invention.
- **Putian** (Puxian): ritual specialists whose texts carry truth claims about the gods.
- **Central Min** (Sanming): county histories with sparse study.
- **Hainanese** of Haikou and Wenchang: newspaper readers, space-base neighbors, emigrant letters as eyewitness.
- **Li (Hlai)**: origin stories told in Hlai and retold in Mandarin.
- **She** of Ningde: sung genealogy as a record of the past.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: their own account of where they came from.
- **Kim Mun** of Hainan: Daoist-style scriptures held as authoritative.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where their genealogies and Min genealogies tell different stories about the same migrations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Neo-Confucian sources and official local history; mixed for oath and divination practice; thin for minority accounts outside state ethnology.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Mandarin; Hokkien for speech and storytelling; She and Hlai through published songs and myths.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official history and censorship, rumor laws and online speech; Cultural Revolution memory; South China Sea claims. Courts and evidence rules go to Libra.
