## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen and Quanzhou** (Minnan): ghost-month rites and almanac use in dense cities.
- **Zhangzhou** villages (Minnan): geomancy over graves and houses.
- **Fuzhou** (Eastern Min): plague-god belief and its processions.
- **Putian** (Puxian): many gods in one village alliance, with different warrants.
- **Minbei and Central Min**: hill villages with their own spirit beliefs.
- **Hainanese** of Haikou, Wenchang and Qionghai: island gods and taboos.
- **Western Hainan** (Danzhou, Lingao): mixed languages and beliefs.
- **Li (Hlai)** subgroups: ghost and taboo categories that differ from Han ones.
- **Kim Mun** of Hainan: ancestor belief and Daoist-style texts.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: Islamic belief in a mostly non-Muslim island.
- **She** of Mindong: ancestor beliefs and taboos.
- **Protestants and Catholics** across Fujian: confessional belief beside village gods.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where Hakka and Min villages in the Zhangzhou uplands share geomancers and grave-siting beliefs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou folk belief; mixed for survey data (small provincial samples); thin for Li and Kim Mun belief beyond state ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Fuzhou; classical Chinese for almanacs; Hlai and Kim Mun for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "superstition" label is political; belief surveys may undercount; heterodox-group labels go to fundamentalism. Do not frame minority beliefs as primitive.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwan's almanac and ghost-month practice; Southeast Asian Hokkien beliefs.
- **Held for review:** none.
