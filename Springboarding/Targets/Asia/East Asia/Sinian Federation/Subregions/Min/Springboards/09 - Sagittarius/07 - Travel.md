## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang** (Minnan): long emigration to the Philippines and Southeast Asia, and a famous port past.
- **Xiamen** (Minnan): the main departure port and today's tourism.
- **Zhangzhou** (Minnan): emigration to Southeast Asia from farming counties.
- **Changle, Fuqing and Lianjiang** (Eastern Min): routes to North America, Europe and Japan.
- **Putian** (Puxian): Henghua emigrants with their own trades and routes.
- **Minbei** (Wuyishan): mountain tourism and tea pilgrims.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai** (Hainanese): South Seas emigration and returnee villages.
- **Returned overseas Chinese** on Hainan and in Fujian: arrival as a homecoming to a place they never knew.
- **Li (Hlai)**: journeys for work to the coast and cities.
- **Tanka** fishing families: life on the water as long movement.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: origin accounts that, in their own telling, involve a sea crossing (*check*).
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where their migration stories run through Min ports on the way out.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration and maritime history; mixed for tourism attitudes; thin for minority journeys.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian and Hainanese for songs and letters; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** irregular migration from the Fuzhou coast; Kinmen and Taiwan-strait crossings; South China Sea fishing voyages.
- **Comparison only:** Southeast Asian Hokkien, Hainanese, Fuzhou and Henghua communities; Fuzhounese in New York; Taiwan; Chaoshan emigration (Yue member state).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (medieval port visitors), set aside under the standing rule.
