## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang** (Minnan): private manufacturing and the brand-versus-fake question.
- **Xiamen** (Minnan): an official integrity showcase city.
- **Zhangzhou** (Minnan): Chen Chun's home, where classical honesty terms were glossed.
- **Putian** (Puxian): a region whose name became a media label; separate press framing from local accounts.
- **Fuzhou and Fuqing** (Eastern Min): emigrant credit and business word-keeping.
- **Minbei and Central Min**: smaller market towns and their norms of fair dealing.
- **Sanya and Haikou** (Hainan): honesty toward tourists.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai**: returnee families and trust in remittance dealings.
- **Li (Hlai)**: promises without writing.
- **She** of Mindong: tea and farm trade with Han neighbors.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: whether religious teaching on fair trade shapes market dealing, a question for the community itself.
- **Tanka** fishing families: fish trading and price-setting at the harbor.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only where Longyan's market towns trade with Minnan buyers on their own terms of fair weight.
