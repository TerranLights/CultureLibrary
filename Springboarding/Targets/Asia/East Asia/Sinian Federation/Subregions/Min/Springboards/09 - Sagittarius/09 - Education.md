## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minbei** (Jianyang, Wuyishan): academy and printing towns, the old center of learning.
- **Fuzhou** (Eastern Min): the arsenal school, missionary colleges and examination families.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang** (Minnan): emigrant-funded schools and business families' choices between school and trade.
- **Xiamen** (Minnan): a university city built by a donor.
- **Zhangzhou** (Minnan): rural counties' routes into higher education.
- **Putian** (Puxian): an examination reputation as local histories present it; test it against present-day data.
- **Central Min** (Sanming): mountain counties and boarding schools.
- **Haikou** (Hainanese): the island's academy and university.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai**: emigrant-built schools.
- **Li (Hlai)**: bilingual schooling and access to university.
- **She** of Mindong: minority-school provision and language.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: religious and state schooling side by side.
- **Tanka** fishing families: schooling for children of the water.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for the Changting and Liancheng printing and study traditions that fed the same examination system as Min students (Sibao 四堡 printing, *check*).
