*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Tsat; Arabic loanwords in Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou** (Eastern Min): birthplace of the Local Church movement; urban Protestant revivalism.
- **Mindong** (Fu'an, Ningde): Catholic communities whose history runs through the Dominicans' stand in the Rites dispute.
- **Putian** (Puxian): Protestant congregations beside Three-in-One halls.
- **Quanzhou** (Minnan): Li Zhi's city; Hui lineages and Islamic revival.
- **Xiamen and Zhangzhou** (Minnan): officials' purification campaigns and rival ritual lineages.
- **Minbei** (Jianyang, Wuyishan): Zhu Xi's orthodoxy and today's Confucian revival.
- **Central Min** (Sanming): little studied; check for strict sects in mountain counties.
- **Hainanese** of Haikou and Wenchang: Protestant and Catholic churches with emigrant links.
- **Utsul** of Sanya: a small Muslim community where practice is a public question.
- **Li (Hlai)**: missionary and state pressure on Li ritual, and Li responses.
- **She** of Mindong: ancestral ritual under church and state pressure.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (a separate sub-unit):** only for Basel Mission congregations near the Fujian–Guangdong border, a different strict-Protestant lineage that Min churches met.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong historical sources for orthodoxy, missions and the Rites Controversy; present-day material on churches, Islam and banned groups is restricted and partisan.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou church language; Latin and Spanish for Dominican sources; Arabic for Hui religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state labels of heterodoxy; detained believers; Utsul dress and mosque rules; researchers and informants at risk. Use "fundamentalist" only where the community or a named scholar does; do not apply it to temple religion by default. Law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Comparison only:** Local Church communities in Taiwan and the diaspora; Southeast Asian Hokkien churches.
- **Held for review:** none.
