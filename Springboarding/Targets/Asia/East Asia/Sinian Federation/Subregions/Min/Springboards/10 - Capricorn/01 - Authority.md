# Min: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a Sinian Federation member state (Fujian, Hainan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai; She. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou villages:** lineage heads, temple committees and elected cadres side by side; whose word ends a dispute.
- **Xiamen:** a special economic zone city where firm, Party and migrant-worker hierarchies may outweigh lineage.
- **Fuzhou and the Mindong coast:** official families and emigrant villages (Fuqing, Changle) where money from abroad may shift who is heard.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** god-cult alliances and medium sessions as rival seats of command.
- **Northern Fujian uplands:** smaller lineages; query whether state and Party authority meet less resistance.
- **Hainanese Han coast (Wenchang, Qionghai):** emigrant-family patriarchs and the revolutionary-base memory.
- **Li of central Hainan (Wuzhishan, Qiongzhong, Baisha):** headmen and dong territories replaced by autonomous-county officials; the 1943 Baisha uprising.
- **She of Ningde:** branch heads and ritual masters beside village cadres.
- **Utsat of Sanya and Quanzhou's Hui lineages:** imams and mosque committees as a separate authority.
- **Boat people (Min River, Sanya):** authority on boats and in fishing teams, outside landed lineages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lineage and temple authority; mixed survey coverage because province samples are small; thin for Li, Utsat and boat people in their own words. Permission-share and correctional figures for the member state must be built from national law plus provincial rules.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien for medium sessions and village talk; German for Stübel; Hlai through Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions on obeying the Party, on police and on protest are restricted in fieldwork and surveys; the Feng Baiju "localism" case touches Party history; medium practice may be unregistered religion.
- **Held for review:** none.
