# Min: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), member state of the Sinian Federation (Fujian, Hainan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai and She through Chinese sources. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang emigrant villages:** duty paid in remittances and in building schools and ancestral halls back home.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** duty tied to debts for passage abroad and to temples that vouch for the traveler.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's labor duties and marriage-residence customs set against the Han lineage norm.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** rotating ritual duty for village alliances.
- **Inland Fujian (Sanming, Nanping):** query whether duty to parents meets more out-migration to the coast than abroad.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai (Hainan):** wives and parents left behind by emigrants to Southeast Asia.
- **Li of Hainan:** obligations of kin and work group outside Han filial frameworks.
- **She of Ningde:** duties taken on through initiation and lineage ritual.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** religious duties of prayer, fasting and alms in a Muslim minority.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** worth one comparison: query how upkeep and defense duties were divided among households sharing one walled house.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and ritual duty and for emigrant letters; mixed for surveys at province level; thin for Li, She and Utsat duties in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for rituals and genealogies; Hokkien for chapbooks and temple practice; Fuzhou for emigrant villages; Hainanese for Wenchang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** illegal emigration and smuggling debts; militia and military service on the Taiwan Strait; some initiation knowledge is kept within the community.
- **Held for review:** none.
