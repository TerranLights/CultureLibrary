## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian and Xianyou:** shared upkeep of irrigation and temples set beside a footwear trade that tests producer answerability.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** dense private industry where accidents and fraud raise who answers, owner or official.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** grandparents responsible for children of emigrants.
- **Xiamen:** a city of migrants from inland Fujian and beyond; query whether neighbor answerability weakens.
- **Inland Fujian (Sanming, Nanping, Longyan's non-Hakka counties):** left-behind elders and children.
- **Hainanese coast:** typhoon exposure and fishing crews where the captain answers for the boat.
- **Li of the central uplands:** forest stewardship and resettlement.
- **She of Ningde:** query any She village compacts and mutual-aid rules.
- **Danzhou and Lingao speakers:** separate local institutions on Hainan's west coast; query local mutual-surety customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official accountability notices and disasters; mixed for attitude surveys at province level; thin for minority stewardship in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese for state records; classical Chinese for compacts and baojia; Hokkien and Hainanese for everyday blame talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accountability inside the Party is reported only in part; land resettlement is politically delicate; counterfeit trades are criminal matters.
- **Held for review:** none.
