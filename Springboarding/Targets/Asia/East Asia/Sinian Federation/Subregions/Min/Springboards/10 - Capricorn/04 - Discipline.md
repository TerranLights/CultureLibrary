## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou:** monastery, martial-arts and opera discipline side by side in one city.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** Three-in-One meditation and Puxian opera apprenticeship.
- **Fuzhou:** large monasteries and dragon-boat crews of the Min River.
- **Northern Fujian (Wuyishan, Jian'ou):** tea-craft training and the academy tradition.
- **Xiamen:** seminaries and a modern school system with heavy exam preparation.
- **Hainanese Han (Qionghai, Wenchang):** revolutionary drill memory beside school and family discipline in emigrant towns.
- **Li:** body marking and work-group discipline before collectivization.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** fasting and prayer discipline in a small Muslim community.
- **Boat people of Sanya and the Min River:** discipline of crews at sea.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** one comparison: query whether village martial training for defense of walled settlements differs from Songjiang troupe drill.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for monastic, martial and craft discipline; mixed for parenting data at province level; thin for Li and Utsat accounts in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien for opera and martial-arts lineages; classical Chinese for Song and Ming texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corporal punishment of children is a delicate survey topic; Li tattooing involves elderly women's own bodies and should be approached through their accounts; some martial and religious training keeps parts private.
- **Held for review:** none.
