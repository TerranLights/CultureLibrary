# Min: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a Sinian Federation member state made up of Fujian and Hainan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou (for Fuqing and Changle); Puxian; Hainanese; Li and She through Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** firm founders and garment and shoe workshops; becoming a boss as the local route up.
- **Putian:** an examination past beside national trading networks in gold, timber and medicine.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** going abroad, legally or not, as the main ladder.
- **Xiamen:** university and port city attracting graduates from inland.
- **Inland Fujian (Sanming, Nanping):** query whether the coast is the goal and staying is the exception.
- **Haikou and Sanya:** newcomers from other provinces chasing the free-trade-port economy, beside native Hainanese.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai:** emigrant and military careers.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** schooling and state jobs as routes, and the costs of leaving the village.
- **She of Ningde:** query She graduates and officials and what their communities expect of them.
- **Quanzhou Hui lineages:** merchant ambition with Muslim ancestry kept in lineage memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and emigration; mixed for survey items at province level; thin for minority aspirations in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien for songs and small-firm talk; Fuzhou for Changle and Fuqing; Hainanese for Wenchang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** irregular emigration and smuggling; tycoons' political ties; minority preferences in exams are debated; diaspora and Taiwan material is comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none.
