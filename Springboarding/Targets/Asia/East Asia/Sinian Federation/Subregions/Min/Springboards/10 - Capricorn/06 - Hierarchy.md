## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian plain:** lineages ranked by branch and by degree history; temples in alliance hierarchies.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou:** palace-style houses and banquet order as public rank display.
- **Fuzhou and the Min River:** boat people and the landed city, historically and after resettlement.
- **Emigrant counties (Jinjiang, Fuqing, Wenchang):** returned emigrants and families with kin abroad, raised and lowered by changing state labels.
- **Xiamen:** hukou holders against migrant workers; cross-strait firms.
- **Northern Fujian:** smaller lineages; query whether official rank matters more than lineage rank.
- **Haikou and Sanya:** natives, mainland newcomers and seasonal residents.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** state ranking of peoples, past and present; the Miao's own account of how they came to the island's uplands.
- **She of Ningde:** a minority set among Han lineages; query terms used of and by She.
- **Utsat of Sanya and the Tanka:** small coastal groups at the edges of the island's ranks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and built rank; mixed for survey data; thin for boat people and Utsat in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Qing notes and genealogies; Hokkien for banquets and opera; Fuzhou for boat-people history; Hainanese for Sanya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for boat people and old ethnic labels must be described, never used; land-reform labels remain painful family history; hukou and migrant ranking touch current policy.
- **Held for review:** none.
