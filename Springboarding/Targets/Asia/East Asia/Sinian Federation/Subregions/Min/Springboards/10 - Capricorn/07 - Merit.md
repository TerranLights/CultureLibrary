## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou:** a city that memorializes examination and naval-school success; query whether old merit families still carry weight.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** high pre-modern degree counts beside today's networks of kin-run businesses, where hiring by trust may compete with hiring by test.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** the self-made factory founder as the local model of desert.
- **Anxi and Wuyishan:** craft merit judged in contests.
- **Xiamen:** university admissions and migrant professionals.
- **Hainanese Han:** query how the island remembers its few nationally known degree holders; gaokao rules shaped by in-migration.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** sung skill and state minority preferences as two different routes to esteem.
- **She of Ningde:** song duels and schooling.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** query religious learning as a route to esteem in a small community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for examinations, honors and business lore; mixed for attitude surveys broken down by province; thin for minority measures of merit in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for examination records; Fuzhou for pinghua; Hokkien for tea and business talk; Li and Miao song texts usually reach outsiders in Chinese translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recruitment fraud and nepotism cases are reported selectively; minority exam preferences are argued over; some business memoirs are promotional.
- **Held for review:** none.
