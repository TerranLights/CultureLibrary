# Min: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), Sinian Federation member state (Fujian and Hainan), region East Asia. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law: what it is for, and how people relate to it. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Li, She and Tsat through Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou:** a history of lineage feuds and temple oaths; query how far kin and gods still sit beside the courts.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** query the 1980s markets in smuggled and copied goods and the later move to registered brands.
- **Xiamen:** an economic zone that writes its own regulations and the city of the Yuanhua case.
- **Fuqing and Changle:** emigration through smugglers; law seen from both ends of the route.
- **Putian:** temple alliances and village mediation.
- **Northern Fujian hill counties:** contracts, forest prohibitions and village covenants.
- **Hainanese Han coast:** the island of exile and later of special-zone experiments.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** autonomous-county rules beside customary settlement.
- **She of Ningde:** forest and lineage prohibitions, query their survival.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** Islamic norms and state law in daily life.
- **Hakka of western Fujian:** one comparison: query the written defense and feud rules of walled settlements beside Southern Min lineage feuds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern contracts, judgments and inscriptions; mixed for present attitudes, since surveys on courts are limited; thin for Li, Miao and Utsat legal orders in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical and documentary Chinese (contract language); Hokkien for oaths and temple practice; Fuzhou for smuggling-village fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political trials, petitioning and the court-database restrictions; smuggling cases involve living people; Party discipline sits beside the law and is reported in part. The letter of the law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
