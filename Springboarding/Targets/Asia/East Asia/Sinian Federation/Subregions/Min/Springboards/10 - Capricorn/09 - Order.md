## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen:** repeated civilized-city campaigns set beside its 2007 protest and its Pudu history.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou coast:** pestilence boats and ghost feasts as the order of the unseen.
- **Fuzhou and Putian:** large processions as licensed disorder, and the rules around them.
- **Northern and central Fujian uplands:** fengshui groves and village siting.
- **Gulangyu:** a small island shaped by foreign-run order, now a heritage site.
- **Haikou and Sanya:** rapid growth, tourism and a new customs boundary.
- **Hainanese villages (Wenchang, Ding'an):** query village siting and ancestral-hall order.
- **Li of Wuzhishan and Baoting:** taboos and spirit rules as the frame of order.
- **She of Ningde:** query She rules on settlement and ritual purity.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** mosque-centered daily rhythm as a separate order.
- **Boat people of the Min River and Sanya:** order on water, outside the village grid.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual and festival material; mixed for official safety surveys, which are state-reported; thin for minority cosmologies in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien for ritual texts and festival speech; classical Chinese for Qing lectures; English for Gulangyu council records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest, stability maintenance and policing are restricted topics; some ritual knowledge is kept by specialists; the customs boundary is new and its effects are still unfolding.
- **Held for review:** none.
