# Min: Domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script); Southern Min (Hokkien, Pe̍h-ōe-jī); Eastern Min (Fuzhou, Foochow Romanized); Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai; She speech; Tsat. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups, and what they hope for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan heartland (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou, Xiamen):** dense lineages and temple wards; Quanzhou and Zhangzhou differ.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong (Ningde, Fu'an, Xiapu):** a different Min language and a long Christian presence alongside lineages.
- **Puxian (Putian, Xianyou):** the densest village ritual alliances; the Mazu cradle at Meizhou.
- **Minbei and Minzhong (Nanping, Jian'ou, Wuyishan, Sanming, Yong'an):** inland hill settlement, thinner study.
- **She (Ningde, Fu'an, Luoyuan):** a non-Han people with its own ancestral legend and song gatherings.
- **Hui-descended lineages (Chendai Ding, Baiqi Guo):** Han-speaking families with Muslim ancestry.
- **Danjia boat people (Mindong, Sanya):** long excluded from land-based groups.
- **Hainanese Han (Haikou, Wenchang, Qionghai, Wanning):** emigrant hometowns and village festivals.
- **Danzhou, Lingao (Be) and Jun-speech communities:** western Hainan's distinct speech groups.
- **Li (Hlai) subgroups (Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu, Sai), Hainan Miao (Kim Mun speakers), Utsat (Sanya Hui, Tsat):** separate kin and territorial systems.
- **State-farm settlers and post-1988 incomers to Haikou and Sanya:** communities without old roots.
- **Hakka of Longyan and western Fujian:** a separate sub-unit; compare only for the shared tulou settlement form.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Puxian lineage and temple life; mixed for Fuzhou; thin in English for Hainan's minorities.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled. Kinmen and Matsu (administered from Taiwan before the war) and the South China Sea islands under Hainan's former Sansha city are for the author to place; Taiwan, Chaoshan and Leizhou are comparison only, as is the Southeast Asian diaspora.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou for oral material; Japanese for colonial-era Hainan surveys; German for Stübel.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest material and unregistered churches; ethnic classification (the Be and the Utsat are counted under other groups); Danjia stigma; Mao-era campaigns.
- **Held for review:** none.
