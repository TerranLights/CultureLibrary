## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan lineage villages vs. Xiamen and Quanzhou urban 社区:** a community one is born into against one assigned by residence.
- **Fuqing and Changle emigrant villages:** communities with many members abroad; what absent members still owe.
- **Puxian villages:** membership marked by the temple ward and its festival levies.
- **Minbei and Minzhong hill villages:** aging and emptying communities.
- **She villages of Mindong:** a small people living beside Han neighbors; how the two communities overlap.
- **Danjia (Mindong coast, Sanya harbor):** a community remade by moving ashore.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai villages:** houses kept for relatives abroad.
- **Li villages of the central highlands:** relocation and new housing.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** a compact community of two villages around their mosques.
- **Sanya's seasonal incomers and Hainan's state-farm settlements:** communities of strangers, or of the work unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Minnan and Fuzhou village studies; mixed for city neighborhoods; thin for Li, Utsat and Danjia community life outside heritage and relocation coverage.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou for interviews; Hlai and Tsat for those communities' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation programs are officially praised and the residents' views may be harder to find; residents' committees also do surveillance work, so ask how "community" is used.
- **Held for review:** none.
