**Target:** Min (闽), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Fujian, Hainan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Puxian; Hainanese; Hlai; Tsat. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit; how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" and "them" are drawn, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou people:** old feuding blocs whose speech still marks the line.
- **Big- and small-surname lineages:** who joins whom in a quarrel.
- **Putian plain villages:** sect and ritual membership crossing kin lines.
- **Fu'an Catholic and Mindong Protestant lineages:** faith groups inside kin society.
- **Chendai Ding and Baiqi Guo:** groups whose label changed in living memory.
- **She:** a classified minority whose members live mixed among Han.
- **Li subgroups:** Ha, Qi, Run, Meifu and Sai as distinct units, not one bloc.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** a small group with clear outer lines.
- **Fuqing and Changle emigrant networks; Putian business blocs:** groups bound by migration debt or trade.
- **State-farm workers and their descendants:** the work unit as group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Qing feuds, sects and lineage blocs; mixed on present-day groups; thin on Li group forms in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou for troupes and crews; Hlai and Tsat.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** smuggling, hospital scandals, secret societies and unregistered churches; ethnic-classification disputes; avoid treating any surname group or county as one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
