## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Li of the Wuzhishan highlands vs. lowland Li:** the shared-harvest unit against private plots.
- **Danjia:** a people long ranked below landed villagers; whether that ranking survives in talk.
- **Hui'an coastal women:** a distinct marriage pattern and dress; how equality is discussed among them.
- **She:** a minority once looked down on by neighbors; what changed with classification.
- **Overseas-Chinese families (侨眷) in Quanzhou, Jinjiang and Wenchang:** wealth from abroad set against village neighbors.
- **Migrant workers vs. locals in Xiamen, Jinjiang and Haikou:** registration as a line of standing.
- **Coastal cities vs. Minbei and central Hainan uplands:** regional inequality.
- **Utsat and Hainan Miao:** small minorities' standing under autonomy policies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ming-Qing land tenure and for official gender data; mixed for minority standing; thin for the Danjia's own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien and Hainanese for oral sources; Hlai for Li terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform violence, minority policy and hukou are politically delicate; heritage framing of the Red Detachment is official; treat slurs for the Danjia and She as data, never as usage.
- **Held for review:** none.
