## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou:** the old reformers' city; how its schools and families keep that memory.
- **Xiamen and Jinjiang:** market reform's showcases, seen from owners, workers and migrants.
- **Putian and Xianyou:** home of Song reform ministers; compare old memory with present business reputations.
- **Sanming and Minbei forest counties:** sites of rural and health reform from inside the province.
- **Hui'an and other coastal Minnan towns:** targets of custom-reform campaigns.
- **Li and Miao of central Hainan:** reforms imposed on them, and their own changes since.
- **Haikou's 1988 incomers and old Hainanese families:** the island's reform seen from newcomers and natives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Song, Qing and Republican reformers; rich official material on post-1978 reform, thinner independent voices.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou for oral history; Hlai for Li accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official reform narratives linked to national leaders' years in Fujian; corruption cases; minority-area reforms of the 1950s; separate praise from evidence.
- **Held for review:** none.
