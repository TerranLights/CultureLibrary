*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fuzhou literati and Christian families:** martyr memory and mission-school ideals in one city.
- **Jimei and Xiamen returnee families:** education as the ideal a fortune serves.
- **Minqing and Gutian Methodist villages:** the home ground of a utopian founder.
- **Zhangzhou and Dongshan:** loyalist memory.
- **Qionghai, Wanning and Ding'an:** families of the island's long-isolated revolutionaries.
- **Hainan state-farm settlers and former sent-down youth:** idealism imported from other provinces.
- **Li communities:** the gap between tourism's ideal image and Li accounts.
- **Wenchang and Quanzhou emigrant families:** volunteers of 1939 and their memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martyrs, philanthropists and mission education; mixed for present-day mission work; thin for idealism in rural and minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Hokkien for song booklets and oral history; Hlai for Li voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary memory is officially curated; sent-down-youth memoirs vary in what can be published; avoid outsiders' romantic images of the Li.
- **Held for review:** none.
