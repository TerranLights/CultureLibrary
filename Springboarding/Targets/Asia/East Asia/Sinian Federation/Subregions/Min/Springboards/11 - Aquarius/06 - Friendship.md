## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan tea-table circles vs. Minbei rock-tea circles:** two tea cultures, two settings for friendship.
- **Fuzhou literati:** a written record of friendship in letters and prefaces.
- **Hui'an women's sister-companion groups:** close peer ties within a distinctive marriage pattern.
- **Danzhou singers and Lingao fishing-song communities:** friendship carried in song.
- **Li of the central highlands:** youth houses and festival gatherings.
- **She of Mindong:** sung hospitality to friends and guests.
- **Men's sworn brotherhoods in Minnan and Hainan:** ritual bonds that can shade into gang ties.
- **Migrant workers and incomers in Xiamen, Jinjiang and Sanya:** friendships formed away from home.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (optional):** their own sworn-brotherhood and song-meeting forms, to separate from the Minnan ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literati friendship and tea culture; mixed for ritual friendship; thin for Li, She and Danjia friendship in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Hokkien, Danzhou speech and Hlai for song and oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 契兄弟 and same-sex bonds need care, without projecting modern labels; sworn brotherhood and gangs overlap; courtship customs among the Li are often exoticized by outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
