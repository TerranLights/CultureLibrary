## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou:** a dissenter's home city and a port of many faiths; tolerance tested in stone and text.
- **Haikou and Qiongshan:** the memory of an incorruptible official who defied his ruler.
- **Xiamen:** poets, avant-garde artists and students; a city that markets itself as relaxed.
- **Fuzhou and Mindong:** cradle of independent church movements.
- **Minnan opera audiences in Zhangzhou and Quanzhou:** sympathy for lovers who break the rules, to test.
- **Li elders and young people:** a body norm abandoned within a lifetime.
- **Factory youth in Jinjiang and Xiamen:** styles of difference among migrants.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** a collective, not individual, difference of faith and speech.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Li Zhi, Hai Rui and the Minnan opera tradition; mixed for present-day art and online culture; thin for minority and working-class nonconformity in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Hokkien for opera and folk usage; Hlai for Li accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches, online dissent and some poets' records are restricted or deleted; sexual and gender nonconformity needs careful sourcing; Li tattooing is often exoticized.
- **Held for review:** none.
