## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minnan lay vegetarian women and monastic communities:** renunciation as a chosen life path.
- **Wuyishan and Minbei hill retreats:** a long tradition of withdrawal from office and society.
- **Fuqing monastic heritage vs. Fuqing emigrant families:** detachment chosen by monks, distance imposed by migration.
- **Minnan and Puxian village funerals vs. urban Xiamen funerals:** required wailing against composure.
- **Li communities:** their own grief and composure norms.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** Muslim burial customs with their own rules for showing grief.
- **Young urban workers in Xiamen and Fuzhou:** online talk of stepping back.
- **Hainanese of Haikou and Wenchang:** the tourism image of a relaxed island tested against working life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhist and Daoist detachment and for funeral custom; mixed for everyday reserve; thin for minority emotional norms.
