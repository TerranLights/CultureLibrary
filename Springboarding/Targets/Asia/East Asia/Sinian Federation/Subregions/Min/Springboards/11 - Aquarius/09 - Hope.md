## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changle and Fuqing emigrant villages:** hope placed abroad, at high cost.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** older overseas wealth and newer factory fortunes.
- **Xiamen urban households:** festival luck games as everyday hope.
- **Haikou's 1988 incomers vs. old Hainanese families:** frontier hope and its aftermath.
- **Wenchang:** emigrant hometown turned launch-site town.
- **Li upland villages:** harvest rites, and poverty-alleviation targets.
- **She villages and former Danjia boat families of Mindong:** hope as narrated by the state against their own words.
- **Hui'an and Pingtan fishing families:** hopes tied to the sea and the season.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration hopes and temple practice; mixed for measured optimism by province; thin for Li and She hopes in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Hokkien for songs and temple texts; Fuzhou for emigrant interviews; Hlai and She speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** irregular migration and debt; official poverty-alleviation narratives tied to national leaders; cross-Strait songs are politically framed; hopes for sons (lanterns for male heirs) need care with gender.
- **Held for review:** none.
