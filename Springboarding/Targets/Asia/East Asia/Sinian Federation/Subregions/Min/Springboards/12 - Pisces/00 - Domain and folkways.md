# Min: The hidden and transcendence, with magic ways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Min (闽), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Southern Min (Hokkien), Eastern Min (Fuzhou), Puxian, Northern and Central Min, Hainanese; Hlai (Li), Lingao, Danzhou speech, Kim Mun (Hainan Miao), Utsat, She speech. **Concept in one line:** how a culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, plus its magic ways (healing, protection, divination, hidden forces).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Min (Quanzhou, Zhangzhou, Xiamen):** temple and medium networks to separate by prefecture.
- **Puxian (Putian, Xianyou):** the Three-in-One teaching 三一教 and Mazu's home island.
- **Eastern Min (Fuzhou, Fuqing, Changle, Ningde):** plague-god and Chen Jinggu cults; emigration history beside grief and longing.
- **Northern and Central Min (Nanping, Wuyishan, Sanming):** upland monasteries; Zhu Xi's homeland.
- **She (Ningde, Fu'an, Xiapu):** ancestor scrolls and initiation rites of their own.
- **Hui of Quanzhou (Chendai, Baiqi):** lineages tracing Muslim descent.
- **Danjia 疍家 boat people (Mindong coast; Sanya, Lingshui):** sea deities and water-borne death customs.
- **Hainanese Han, Danzhou and Lingao speakers:** village feast days and the Lady Xian 冼夫人 cult.
- **Li, Hainan Miao, Utsat Muslims of Sanya:** separate soul and healing systems.
- **Returned overseas Chinese 归侨 farms and northern retirees in Sanya:** imported devotions and escape migration.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** only where tulou villages share Min temples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for southern Min and Puxian religion; mixed for Fuzhou; thin and state-filtered for Li, Lingao and Utsat. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; Fujian and Hainan are a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese, Hokkien (POJ church texts), Fuzhou (Bàng-uâ-cê), Hainanese, Hlai; Dutch and French for early sinology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "feudal superstition" 封建迷信 labels shape official sources; mediums and some sects may be unregistered; sorcery accusations touch living families; psychiatric records are private.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (pre-modern Quanzhou remains).
