## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang, Shishi, Nan'an (emigrant home districts):** remittance-funded schools, temples and clinics, to set against districts without them.
- **Xiamen and Quanzhou cities against their villages:** urban foundations and volunteers beside village Pudu feasts.
- **Fuzhou, Changle, Fuqing:** elder-honoring porridge rites; recent emigrant money in temples and relief.
- **Putian:** Mazu as rescuer at sea; Three-in-One halls that run charity.
- **Mountain counties of Nanping, Sanming and the Ningde coast:** long targets of state poverty relief; how the helped speak of it.
- **She villages:** mutual-help customs, and how state aid to minority villages is received.
- **Danjia boat people:** a historically excluded status to check; whether mercy crossed that line.
- **Hainanese Han coast (Tanmen, Wenchang):** cults of drowned seafarers.
- **Li and Hainan Miao uplands:** kin-based care beside state aid.
- **Refugee farms and Sanya's seasonal retirees:** the received and the newly arrived as givers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temple charity and emigrant giving; mixed for state relief (official tone); thin for Li, Miao and Utsat practice.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien for opera and shrine terms; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** infanticide records; Danjia stigma; refugee histories touch foreign policy; release rites are sometimes banned locally.
- **Held for review:** none.
