## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou:** Liyuan opera and Nanyin audiences, a place to study identification with sung characters.
- **Xiamen:** a newer counseling market and a mixed population of locals and incomers.
- **Fuzhou and Changle:** a fading city dialect; can the young still hear the feelings of elders who speak it?
- **Putian and Xianyou:** Puxian opera's tragic repertoire.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's distinct dress and marriage customs, long read through outsiders' eyes.
- **She (Ningde, Fu'an):** bridal laments, a voiced feeling in a minority language.
- **Danjia (Mindong coast; Sanya, Lingshui):** song exchange as listening.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi migrant workers:** outsiders inside a Hokkien-speaking workforce.
- **Hainanese Han (Haikou, Wenchang):** Qiong opera 琼剧 audiences.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** reading and being read in tourism.
- **Sanya's northern retirees:** friction and fellow-feeling with locals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: psychology studies are many but rarely split by subregion; folklore of laments is rich for Minnan, thin for Li.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien (incl. Ming stage language); Fuzhou; She; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** doctor-patient violence; migrant workers' grievances; tourism portrayals of minorities.
