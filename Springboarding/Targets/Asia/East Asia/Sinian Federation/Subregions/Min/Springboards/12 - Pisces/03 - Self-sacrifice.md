## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jinjiang, Nan'an, Anxi, Yongchun:** qiaopi families; wives left for decades.
- **Changle, Fuqing, Lianjiang:** debt-financed emigration; grandparents raising children sent home.
- **Hui'an coast:** women's labor and long natal residence after marriage.
- **Putian and Meizhou island:** Mazu's maiden-saint model.
- **Fuzhou city:** revolutionary martyrs and their houses as shrines.
- **Zhangzhou:** Ming loyalist memory.
- **Xiamen's islands:** front-line civilians of 1958.
- **Wenchang and Qionghai:** emigrant households and revolutionary memorials side by side.
- **Li of Baisha and Qiongzhong:** who is named among the dead.
- **Danjia fishing families:** losses at sea and what families give up for boats.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** Changting and Shanghang's Red Army martyr counts, as a contrast to coastal emigrant sacrifice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for qiaopi, chastity records and revolutionary memory; mixed for present-day caregiving; thin for Li and Danjia voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien and Fuzhou for letters and songs; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** widow suicide and self-injury; smuggled emigration (蛇头) and debts; revolutionary history is official doctrine; organ donation in China is a contested topic internationally.
- **Held for review:** none.
