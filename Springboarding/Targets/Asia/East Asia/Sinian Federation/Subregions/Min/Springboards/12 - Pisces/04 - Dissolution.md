## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Putian and Xianyou:** palanquin and lantern processions, to study how far walkers feel absorbed into the crowd.
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou:** medium trance and boat burnings.
- **Fuzhou and Fuqing:** night-feast rites; the city dialect's standing against Standard Chinese, to measure.
- **Northern Min mountains (Nanping, Wuyishan, Ningde uplands):** Chan and Daoist withdrawal; villages thinned by out-migration (scale to check).
- **Chendai Hui:** identity dissolved, then reclaimed.
- **She (Ningde, Fu'an):** the direction and pace of their language shift, to check.
- **Danjia:** a boat world ended by settlement.
- **Lingao, Danzhou and Cun speakers:** small Hainan languages under pressure.
- **Li and Hainan Miao:** relocation and language shift among the young.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** a small Muslim speech community.
- **Haikou's Hainanese villages:** trance at feast days.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** on the Pinghe and Nanjing borders, where Min and Hakka speech blend in one village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chan texts and Putian ritual; mixed for language loss (good audio, few speaker accounts); thin for Danjia and Utsat.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (Chan, Daoist texts); Standard Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Hainanese; She, Lingao, Utsat, Hlai.
