## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hua'an, Nanjing, Pinghe:** Hokkien tulou living.
- **Youxi, Datian, Yong'an:** upland forts and Central Min speech.
- **Quanzhou's old feud belts (Hui'an, Nan'an, Jinjiang):** lineage and alliance lines.
- **Fuzhou city:** walled courtyard houses and their degrees of access.
- **Xiamen and Pingtan:** the strait as a daily line.
- **Hui'an women:** dress worn as a marker of place.
- **Danjia:** the water-shore status line.
- **She:** marriage across the She-Han line.
- **Li (central and southern Hainan):** territorial units, tattoos, and the old frontier.
- **Utsat of Sanya:** religious and dietary lines beside Han neighbors.
- **Sanya's incomers and gated estates:** newcomer enclaves on an island now behind a customs line.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** the same tulou form held by another group, for a test of whether walls mean the same thing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tulou, feuds and frontier history; mixed for personal privacy (national surveys, little local split); thin for Danjia and Utsat lines.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (gazetteers, edicts); Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the cross-strait line is political; Li frontier sources carry imperial contempt; customs rules belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
