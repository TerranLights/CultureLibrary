## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiamen and Gulangyu:** a city sold as a retreat; locals' leisure against visitors'.
- **Quanzhou and Jinjiang:** Nanyin clubs as evening refuge; factory owners and workers in their spare hours.
- **Anxi and Wuyishan:** tea-growing places where the tea table is also work.
- **Fuzhou:** old yellow-wine drinking 老酒 and a younger gaming workforce.
- **Zhangzhou:** puppet and opera audiences in villages.
- **Putian:** evening entertainment against a dense festival calendar (to check).
- **Haikou:** measured drinking data.
- **Wanning, Lingshui, Sanya:** betel chewing; incoming retirees.
- **Li uplands (Qiongzhong, Wuzhishan, Baisha):** rice wine in ritual and in daily life.
- **Lingao:** a real county turned into a fiction others escape into.
- **Danjia fishing ports:** time ashore between trips (to check).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for opium history and tourism; mixed for drinking (one Hainan cohort); thin for Li and Danjia voices; drug data are official and partial.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and compulsory treatment; illegal gambling; drinking stereotypes about minorities (avoid; let communities speak); youth gaming limits are policy.
- **Held for review:** none.
