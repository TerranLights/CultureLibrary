## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou:** the 1276 surrender told by the city itself.
- **Jinjiang:** Shi Lang's home, where 1683 is a local story.
- **Zhangzhou:** Huang Daozhou's refusal to yield, as the opposite memory.
- **Xiamen and Gulangyu:** occupation, surrender in 1945, and its markers.
- **Fuzhou:** deathbed customs against hospital dying.
- **Putian:** ordination and lay vows.
- **Danjia and fishing villages (Mindong, Sanya, Lingshui):** yielding to the sea.
- **Hainanese Han (Wenchang, Qionghai):** bone reburial; 1950 memories.
- **Li (Baisha, Qiongzhong, Wuzhishan):** "submission" in imperial records against Li accounts.
- **Sanya's northern retirees:** dying far from home and kin graves.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** secondary burial in jars is reported there too (to check); compare when and why the bones are let go.
