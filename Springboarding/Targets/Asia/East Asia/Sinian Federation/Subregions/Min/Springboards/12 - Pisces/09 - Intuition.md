## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xianyou and Putian:** dream-seeking sites, and how far the custom reaches beyond them (to check).
- **Quanzhou and Xiamen:** temple slip interpreters, face-readers and herb stalls in dense cities.
- **Jinjiang and Shishi:** entrepreneurs' instinct as a local business story, to set against firm data.
- **Anxi and Wuyishan:** tea makers' trained senses.
- **Fuzhou:** medical schools where pulse-reading meets laboratory medicine.
- **Tanmen and Qionghai:** captains' sea sense written in route books.
- **Danjia fishing families:** reading water and weather.
- **Li (Qiongzhong, Baisha, Ledong):** divination before action; hunters' knowledge.
- **She (Ningde, Fu'an):** plant knowledge held by healers.
- **Hakka of western Fujian (separate sub-unit):** feng shui masters there trained in a different school of landform reading, useful to contrast with Minnan practitioners (to check).

## 4. Flags
