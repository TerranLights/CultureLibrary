**Target:** Min (闽), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia; working lands Fujian and Hainan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Hokkien, Fuzhou, Puxian, Hainanese; English for diaspora-side reporting (comparison only). **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changle, Fuqing, Lianjiang:** smuggled emigration; the snakehead as a village figure.
- **Anxi:** a fraud label set against its tea economy.
- **Putian:** fakes and private-hospital networks as debated wrongs.
- **Xiamen:** the Yuanhua case and officials' wrongs.
- **Shishi and Jinjiang:** how the grey markets of the 1980s are remembered now.
- **Zhangpu and Yunxiao:** sworn-brotherhood origins.
- **Tong'an and the Xiamen coast:** pirate memory.
- **Jianyang:** Song Ci honored at home; what the memorial teaches about hidden wrongs.
- **Danzhou:** a Hainan fraud label.
- **Haikou elites:** the 1980s car scandal.
- **Danjia:** imperial stigma of the boat people.
- **Li:** rebellions recorded as crimes by the state, against Li memory.

## 4. Flags
