## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quanzhou and Zhangzhou:** seismic memory and ghost-month observance in villages and cities.
- **Fuzhou:** plague-god processions as epidemic fear made public.
- **Putian and Xiamen:** recent outbreak fear and lockdown memory.
- **Xiamen's coast:** air-raid sirens and the strait.
- **Mindong, Hui'an and other fishing coasts:** sea taboos.
- **Danjia:** life on the water and its own avoidances.
- **Haikou and Qiongshan:** drowned-village legends.
- **Wenchang and the east Hainan coast:** typhoon dread on an exposed shore.
- **Li uplands:** the "miasma" of imperial writing against Li ideas of danger in the forest.
- **She villages:** fright cures and bogeys in a minority language (to check).
- **Northern migrants in Sanya and Haikou:** fears of a new climate (heat, storms, insects).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and historical disasters; mixed for anxiety data (national surveys, little regional split); thin for Danjia and Li voices.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (gazetteers); Standard Chinese; Hokkien; Fuzhou; Hainanese; Hlai; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war fear on the strait is political; pandemic controls; fright cures may be dismissed as superstition; occupation-era atrocities need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
