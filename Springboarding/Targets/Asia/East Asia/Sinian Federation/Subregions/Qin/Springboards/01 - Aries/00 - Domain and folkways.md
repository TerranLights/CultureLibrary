## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han** (Xi'an, Baoji, Weinan): the self inside the lineage.
- **Shaanbei** (Yan'an, Yulin): Jin-speaking loess country, its own song and revolutionary memory.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): Southwestern Mandarin, Qing settler descent.
- **Longdong, Tianshui, Lanzhou, the Hexi Corridor:** garrison and settler histories.
- **Hui:** south Ningxia, the Yinchuan plain, Linxia, Xining; Sufi orders and the Yihewani shape naming.
- **Dongxiang, Bao'an, Salar, Tu:** small peoples with their own origin stories.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (farmers and herders; Rebkong, Golok, Labrang, Tianzhu); **Kham Tibetans of Yushu** separately.
- **Mongols** of Haixi and Henan County; **Yugur** of Sunan; **Kazakhs** of Aksay; **Wutun** speakers.
- **Han settlers** in Qinghai and Gansu since the 1950s.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** the Dungan of Central Asia.
