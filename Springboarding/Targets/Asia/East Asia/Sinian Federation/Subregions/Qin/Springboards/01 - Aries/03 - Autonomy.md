## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong lineage villages** against **Xi'an's university and tech workers**: family say over marriage and work may differ sharply.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong**: bride price and labor migration are the places to test a young person's room to choose.
- **Shaannan**: closer to Sichuan and Hubei family patterns, worth testing separately.
- **Hexi Corridor oil, nickel and space towns**: state employers once ruled housing and schooling.
- **Hui of Linxia and south Ningxia**: test how religious order, gender and intermarriage rules bear on choice, for women and men apart.
- **Amdo herders against monastics**: vows and resettlement are two different limits on choice.
- **Tu and Tibetan communities once under tusi or monastic rule**, against those under direct county rule.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols**: pastoral households, clan elders and marriage customs.
- **Salar, Bao'an, Dongxiang, Yugur**: each has its own autonomous county; what that unit decides for them.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Hui and Tibetan communities abroad, for how far choices widen away from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han marriage and migration; mixed for the Hui; mixed for Amdo (much is film and ethnography); thin for Kazakhs and Yugur.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective autonomy of Tibetans and Hui, birth policy for minorities, resettlement and religious governance are politically sensitive; claims of self-rule must be read through each group's own voices; bride abduction must not be generalized to a people.
- **Held for review:** none.
