## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong market towns with a merchant past** (Jingyang, Sanyuan) against **farming villages**: does an old trading tradition still shape who starts a business?
- **Shaanbei**: oil, coal and gas money since the 2000s against older labor-exchange habits.
- **Shaannan**: tea and mountain crops; small-scale enterprise worth testing apart.
- **Hexi Corridor state towns** (Jiayuguan, Jinchang, Yumen): initiative inside large employers.
- **Hui and Salar of Hualong, Xunhua and Linxia**: kin-financed shops and long-distance trade.
- **Dongxiang and Bao'an**: transport, trading and craft roles may differ from Hui neighbors.
- **Amdo herders**: seasonal harvest trade against settled-town shopkeeping; **Rebkong artisans** apart.
- **Ningxia's Yinchuan plain** (irrigated farming, wine) against the **south Ningxia hills** (out-migration).
- **Kazakhs and Mongols**: herd management and new tourism ventures.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Hui restaurant owners and traders outside the region, for how founding works away from home.
