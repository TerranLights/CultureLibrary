## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong**: Zhou and Qin founding stories sit in its own landscape; test whether locals tell them as "our" beginning.
- **Shaanbei**: cave-dwelling founding rites and the Yan'an era as a second founding story.
- **Shaannan**: new-year and birth customs close to Sichuan and Hubei, worth separating.
- **Hexi Corridor**: towns that date their start to Han garrisons or to PRC industry.
- **South Ningxia migrants** against **Yinchuan plain** residents: those who started over and those who received them.
- **Hui**: origin legends, a new year set by the Islamic calendar, and conversion as a new start.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: farming-area and herding-area Losar dates may differ *check*; monastery rites open the year.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs**: their own spring and winter new years.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur**: how each times and opens its year; little is known in print.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Dungan new-year and birth customs, as a view of older Shaanxi and Gansu forms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han new-year and birth customs and archaeology; mixed for Hui and Tibetan rites; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic for Hui calendar terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement stories are often state-produced and need migrants' own accounts; monastery festivals can be restricted to visitors; conversion through marriage touches sensitive Hui-Han relations.
- **Held for review:** none.
