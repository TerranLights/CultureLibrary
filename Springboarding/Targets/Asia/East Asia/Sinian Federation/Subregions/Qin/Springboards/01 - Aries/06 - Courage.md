# Qin: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan (and the Golok dialect for epic); Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong**: a heritage of Qin and Han soldiers in museums; test how ordinary families use it, if at all.
- **Shaanbei**: revolutionary heroes, coal-mine risk and martial drumming in one region.
- **Shaannan**: mountain trades and floods; courage of a different, quieter kind.
- **Hexi Corridor**: frontier poetry, oil-field endurance and the Western Route Army's memory.
- **Hui of Ningxia and Linxia**: martyr memory and martial-arts lineages; sensitive and internal.
- **Golok and other Amdo herders**: Gesar epic and horse culture; **Amdo monks**: courage framed by vows and nonviolence.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: their own hero tales, mostly unrecorded in print.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols**: batyr and baatar traditions, wrestling and horse racing.
- **Women across groups**: endurance and protection of kin may be judged by different words than men's daring.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Dungan hero songs and war memories in Kyrgyzstan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han classical and revolutionary sources; mixed for Gesar (much in Tibetan); thin for small peoples and women's courage.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary and military memory is state-curated; Hui martyrdom and Tibetan heroism toward the state are politically sensitive; some Gesar performance is tied to religious vocation and bards' own experience, to be approached with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
