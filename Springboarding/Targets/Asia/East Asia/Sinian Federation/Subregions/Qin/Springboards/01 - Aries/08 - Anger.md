*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with classical medical vocabulary and local quarrel speech); Amdo Tibetan (religious and medical terms); Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong** and **Shaanbei** speech: quarrel styles and swearing differ by dialect; test how outsiders hear them.
- **Shaannan**: closer to Sichuan speech; compare quarrel and mediation habits.
- **Longdong and Lanzhou**: shadow-play audiences and city crowds as two outlets.
- **Hui of Ningxia and Linxia**: religious teaching and mosque mediation as cooling channels.
- **Amdo herders (Golok)** against **Amdo farming villages (Rebkong)**: feud traditions and Buddhist patience may weigh differently.
- **Monastics**: anger trained by text and vow.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: village dispute handling with little in print.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols**: clan elders as mediators.
- **Women and men**: who may show anger in public and at home.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Dungan memory of the 1860s violence and its anger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical medicine and Han psychology; mixed for Tibetan and Hui mediation; thin for small peoples and women's anger outside surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Chinese dialects; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective anger at authorities (lockdowns, protests, the 1860s and later conflicts) is censored and politically sensitive; Tibetan feud cases may involve deaths and living families; domestic violence data needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
