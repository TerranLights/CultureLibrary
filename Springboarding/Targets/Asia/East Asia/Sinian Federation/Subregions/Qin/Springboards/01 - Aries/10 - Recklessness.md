# Qin: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, with Guanzhong and Shaanbei slang; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Mongolian and Kazakh for drinking and riding lore. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong**: the 冷娃 figure is regional; test whether it praises or warns, and in which generation.
- **Shaanbei**: coal-boom wealth and speculative lending as financial recklessness.
- **Shaannan and the Qinling**: mountain trails and rivers as stages of risk for city hikers.
- **Lanzhou and the Hexi Corridor**: Yellow River swimming, desert driving and long-haul trucking.
- **Hui of Linxia and Ningxia**: religious limits on drinking and gambling, against young men's lives in the cities.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders**: horse and motorcycle riding, and the gold and caterpillar-fungus harvests on high ground.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols**: riding games and drinking customs.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur**: little in print; ask local researchers.
- **Han migrants to Qinghai** in the 1990s gold rush, as a group apart.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** northwest migrant workers in eastern cities, whose risks at work and on the road are studied there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good for traffic, lottery and finance; thin for minority perspectives; much is news and anecdote.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and dialect slang; Amdo Tibetan; some Arabic; Mongolian and Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and trafficking must not be pinned on any people without solid data; alcohol bans in Hui areas became politically charged; traffic statistics are partly restricted; poaching and gold-rush history involve conflict between groups.
- **Held for review:** none.
