## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong vs. Shaanbei vs. Shaannan:** wheat plain, Loess uplands and rice-growing Qinba valleys keep different household budgets.
- **Hexi Corridor oases vs. Longdong and Longzhong (Dingxi):** irrigated towns against dry-farmed hills; water itself is the scarce good.
- **Northern Ningxia plain vs. Xihaigu:** canal-fed farms against a long-poor upland; compare what spending looks like in each.
- **Hui (by mosque order: Gedimu, Ikhwan, Sufi *menhuan*):** halal rules, alms and trade shape what goods mean; orders differ.
- **Amdo Tibetan farmers vs. nomads; Kham Tibetans of Yushu:** herds against fields, monastery giving.
- **Tu (Huzhu, Minhe):** embroidery and wedding exchange; **Salar (Xunhua) and Dongxiang/Bao'an (Linxia):** Muslim Mongolic and Turkic peoples with trading histories.
- **Mongols (Haixi, Henan county, Subei), Kazakhs (Aksay), Yugur (Sunan):** livestock wealth in small populations.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou urban middle classes vs. Third Front and oil towns (Yumen, Jinchang, Golmud):** consumer city against company town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Guanzhong and for Hui Xi'an; mixed for Gansu and Ningxia; thin and mostly in Chinese for Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur.
