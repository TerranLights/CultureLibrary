# Qin: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with Guanzhong, Lanyin and Shaanbei Jin dialect forms; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong farming villages:** heirloom furniture and paper offerings to the dead; a settled household's long memory of things.
- **Shaanbei cave-dwelling households:** what is kept in a *yaodong* and its storage niches, under a scarce budget.
- **Amdo Tibetan nomads vs. resettled herders in Sanjiangyuan towns:** what moved with the tent, what was left behind at resettlement.
- **Hui in Linxia, Xi'an and Xihaigu:** a Muslim's things at death; trade goods kept at home.
- **Bao'an, Dongxiang and Salar:** carried crafts (knives, embroidery) and gift objects in small communities.
- **Tu of Huzhu and Minhe:** dowry and embroidered goods, made and kept by women.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Kazakhs of Aksay:** saddles, felt and household goods in herding life.
- **Migrant workers from Gansu and Ningxia:** what fits in the bag carried to a city job.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou collectors and dealers:** possession as a hobby and an investment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Tang objects and Han Guanzhong; mixed for Hui; thin and mostly secondhand for Tu, Bao'an, Yugur and Kazakh possessions.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, some Arabic for Hui terms; Mongolian and Kazakh for small groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relics and ritual objects in monasteries and mosques; trade in Tibetan antiques and looted grave goods; resettlement losses. Ask before photographing ritual objects.
- **Held for review:** none.
