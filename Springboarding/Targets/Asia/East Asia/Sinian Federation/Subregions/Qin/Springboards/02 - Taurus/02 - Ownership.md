# Qin: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and local dialect forms; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Mongolian, Kazakh, Tu, Salar. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on. Property law as written goes to Libra; its spirit to Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong owner-cultivators vs. Shaanbei hill farmers:** the "few landlords" debate and the Border Region redistribution give two separate tenure memories.
- **Shaannan (Hanzhong, Ankang):** Qing-era settler land from the Hubei and Hunan migrations *check*; ownership born of clearing hills.
- **Hexi oases:** land worth nothing without water rights; compare how both are claimed.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders (Golog, Huangnan, Gannan) vs. Amdo farmers:** pasture contracted to households against fields held by family.
- **Labrang and Kumbum lay communities:** a monastery as owner and patron.
- **Hui of Linxia and Xihaigu:** mosque endowments and family trading capital.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan county, Kazakhs of Aksay:** pasture boundaries drawn among small herding peoples.
- **Married-out daughters in villages (出嫁女):** who loses land rights at marriage, unit by unit.
- **State-farm and Third Front households:** homes and land held by an enterprise, not a family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land reform and Guanzhong history; mixed for Hui endowments; sensitive and uneven for Tibetan pasture and monastic holdings.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for bronzes and *Shiji*), Amdo Tibetan, some Arabic for Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement and grassland fencing, demolition disputes, monastery and mosque property under current campaigns; give informants anonymity. Property law itself is Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
