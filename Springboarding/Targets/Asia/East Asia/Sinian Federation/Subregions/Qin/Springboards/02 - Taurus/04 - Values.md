# Qin: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loans); Mongolian, Kazakh, Tu, Salar. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong gentry heritage (Lantian, Mei county, Hancheng):** compacts, Neo-Confucian schools and carved mottos give a written record unusual elsewhere in Qin.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong:** revolutionary Border Region values laid over village custom.
- **Hui of the Gedimu, Ikhwan and Sufi orders:** different rankings of ritual, reform and loyalty to a saint's lineage.
- **Salar and Dongxiang:** Muslim peoples with their own languages; compare with Hui.
- **Amdo Tibetan farmers, nomads and monastics:** Buddhist ethics lived in three settings.
- **Tu:** Buddhist, folk and Daoist layers in one people's ideals.
- **Mongols and Kazakhs:** custom-based ideals of small herding peoples.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou young urbanites:** online debates where new and old values clash.
- **Christian villages in Guanzhong (Catholic communities near Gaoling and Sanyuan *check*):** a minority value system with deep local roots.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Guanzhong classical sources; mixed for Hui (much is in Chinese and Arabic); thin and outsider-heavy for Tu, Salar, Bao'an, Yugur.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian for scripture-hall texts, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious values under current "sinicization" campaigns for mosques and monasteries; official values campaigns make survey answers guarded. Record variation; never one voice per people.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Lü compact's four headings and list the virtues each covers.
2. Search CNKI for 经堂教育 胡登洲 and for 门宦 价值 甘肃.

5. Find a bilingual edition of Sakya Pandita's sayings and note the verses on wealth and virtue.
