# Qin: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Shaanxi and Gansu dialect words; classical Chinese for Han slips; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Longzhong and Dingxi dry farmers:** water and grain storage as the first line of security.
- **Guanzhong grain farmers:** famine memory against today's pensions and land.
- **Shaanbei coal counties (Shenmu, Fugu):** local welfare built on resource money, and what happens when it shrinks.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetan herders (Golog, Yushu, Gannan):** snow, disease and price swings; kin herds as insurance.
- **Hui of Xihaigu and Linxia:** mosque charity beside state aid.
- **Tu and Salar villages:** small-community mutual aid on the Huangshui and Yellow River.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay, Mongols of Haixi:** herding risk in small populations.
- **State-enterprise retirees in Lanzhou, Baoji and Yumen:** pensions from a shrinking industrial base.
- **Rural elderly with migrant children:** "raise sons against old age" when the sons have gone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on welfare statistics and disaster studies; mixed on famine memory; thin and guarded on Tibetan and Hui self-help networks.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Han slips and Dunhuang), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic for Hui terms, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1959-61 famine and labor camps (Jiabiangou, Tongwei); security campaigns in Tibetan and Hui areas; mosque and monastery funds. Crime and policing sit outside this concept's research scope.
- **Held for review:** none.
