## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong lineage villages:** families on the same land for centuries, with genealogies to show it.
- **Shaannan mountain villages:** recent mass relocation down to valley towns.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong cave-dwelling villages:** abandoned *yaodong* and new houses; who stayed.
- **Third Front and oilfield towns (Baoji, Hanzhong, Yumen, Golmud):** stability built by the state, then withdrawn.
- **Amdo Tibetan nomads vs. settled herders:** two opposite meanings of a steady life.
- **Hui Sufi orders vs. Ikhwan communities:** continuity through hereditary leaders against through scripture reform.
- **Tu, Salar and Bao'an villages:** small peoples whose villages are their main continuity.
- **Mongols and Kazakhs who arrived in the 1930s and earlier:** groups whose current home is itself the result of a move.
- **Young civil-service aspirants in Xi'an and Lanzhou:** a generation's search for a fixed post.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Third Front and resettlement; mixed on lineage continuity; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Fangmatan), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian terms for Sufi orders, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability maintenance" (维稳) is a political term with a policing sense; resettlement and incarnation lines are sensitive. Keep the cultural sense apart from that political one.
- **Held for review:** none.
