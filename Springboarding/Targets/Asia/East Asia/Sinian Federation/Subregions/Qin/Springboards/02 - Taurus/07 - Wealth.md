# Qin: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese, Northwest dialect words; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong merchant towns (Jingyang, Sanyuan, Hancheng):** old trading fortunes and family estates.
- **Shaanbei coal and gas counties:** new money arriving within one generation.
- **Dingxi, Longzhong and Xihaigu:** the long-poor areas that define the other end.
- **Hui traders of Linxia, Xi'an and Wuzhong:** wealth tied to trade networks and religious giving.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders (Golog, Gannan) and Rebkong artists:** herds, jewelry and art sales as different kinds of wealth.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Kazakhs of Aksay:** livestock counts and their meaning in small peoples.
- **Tu, Salar and Bao'an:** craft (embroidery, knives) and transport trades.
- **Xi'an's tech and tourism earners:** a city fortune unlike the land-based ones.
- **Married-out daughters and widows:** who holds family wealth by gender, unit by unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history, coal and poverty programs; mixed for Hui trade; thin and secondhand for Tibetan and Kazakh household wealth.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Arabic for Hui terms, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastery and mosque finances; anti-corruption cases around coal wealth; family wealth is private; ask before naming families.
- **Held for review:** none.
