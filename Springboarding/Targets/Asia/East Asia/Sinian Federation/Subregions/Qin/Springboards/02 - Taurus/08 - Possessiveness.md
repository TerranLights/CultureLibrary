*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Northwest dialect words; classical Chinese for Tang tales; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Kazakh, Mongolian, Tu, Salar. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han families:** jealousy and parental control framed through lineage and marriage negotiation.
- **Hexi oasis villages:** turf over water shares, past and present.
- **Amdo Tibetan herding villages:** boundary guarding over pasture, and Buddhist teaching against clinging.
- **Hui of Hualong, Linxia and Ningxia:** sermons on envy, and the noodle-name rivalry with Lanzhou.
- **Salar of Xunhua and Tu of Huzhu:** wedding songs that stage a family's hold on a daughter.
- **Bao'an knife-makers and Rebkong artists:** guarding craft knowledge within families.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay and Mongols of Haixi:** pasture claims between small herding groups.
- **University students in Xi'an and Lanzhou:** dating norms of jealousy and phone-checking.
- **Older parents in Xi'an park matchmaking corners:** control over an adult child's choice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology and online talk; mixed for wedding ethnography; thin and sensitive for pasture and water disputes.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Tang tales), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic for Hui terms, Kazakh, Mongolian, Salar and Tu glossaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and partner control; inter-ethnic boundary disputes; anonymity for informants. Laws on protection orders go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
