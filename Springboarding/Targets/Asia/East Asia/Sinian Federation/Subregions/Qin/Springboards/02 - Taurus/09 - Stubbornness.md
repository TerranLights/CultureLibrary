## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong:** the place of the "cold lad" image; test it against local voices, women's and men's.
- **Shaanbei:** Border Region memories of endurance; compare with Guanzhong's.
- **Gansu dry-land villages (Wuwei, Dingxi):** tenacity against desert and drought.
- **Hui Sufi orders vs. Ikhwan reformers:** loyalty to a lineage against reform; each calls the other stubborn.
- **Golog and Yushu herders:** feud and mediation; when holding out is honor.
- **Salar and Dongxiang:** small Muslim peoples with their own stories of not yielding.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay:** a people formed by refusing to stay put in the 1930s and 40s; their own telling.
- **Young urban viewers in Xi'an and Lanzhou:** the generation re-reading Wang Baochuan.
- **Livestock brokers and bazaar traders:** firmness as a trade skill, not a trait.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Han literary and opera sources; mixed on Hui and Tibetan; thin on small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for *Hanshu*), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic for Hui terms, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi persecution history, Tibetan feuds and current protest; resistance to resettlement. Regional temperament labels are stereotypes to test, never findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
