*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Shaanxi and Gansu dialect words; classical Chinese for Tang poetry; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Tu, Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong noodle culture:** wheat, chili oil and vinegar; eating as hearty pleasure.
- **Shaanbei:** drums, song and the warm kang in a cold upland.
- **Shaannan:** rice, tea and a wetter, greener sensory world.
- **Hexi Corridor and Longdong:** fragrance pouches, dry-land foods, oasis fruit.
- **Hui of Linxia, Xi'an and Ningxia:** sweet tea, halal feasts and Tangping care; abstention from alcohol.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an:** hua'er singers; feasting customs of small peoples.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** butter, smoke, incense, herbal baths, and open-air love songs.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Kazakhs of Aksay:** mutton, milk drinks and felt-tent warmth.
- **Ningxia wine-country newcomers and Xi'an food tourists:** pleasure as a market.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on food, song and Tang poetry; mixed on bathing and touch; thin on everyday comfort among small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, hua'er dialect lyrics (Hezhou speech), Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** love songs that some communities keep out of the village and away from elders; religious rules on alcohol and dress. Respect those limits in fieldwork.
- **Held for review:** none.
