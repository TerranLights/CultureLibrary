# Qin (秦): Domain and folkways, communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and the regional Mandarin groups (Central Plains, Lanyin, Southwestern), Jin; Amdo Tibetan (Khams in Yushu); Hui usage with Arabic and Persian loans; the Mongolic (Tu, Santa, Bonan, Eastern Yugur, Oirat) and Turkic (Salar, Western Yugur, Kazakh) languages. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home: speech ways, learning ways, neighbors and short journeys.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong** (Wei River plain): old capital region, densest records.
- **Shaanbei** (Yulin, Yan'an): Jin speech and loess villages; the Yan'an campaigns left a separate archive.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): Southwestern Mandarin, migrant-settled hills.
- **Longdong and Tianshui; Lanzhou and Longzhong; the Hexi Corridor**: three Gansu Han zones with different dialects.
- **Ningxia plain against Xihaigu**: irrigated north and southern mountains, with relocated villages.
- **The Hehuang valley** (Xining, Minhe, Ledu): where Chinese, Tibetan and Mongolic speech meet, including mixed varieties such as Wutun and Gangou (*check*).
- **Hui**: Xi'an, Linxia, Tongxin and Xining, split further by Sufi order and Yihewani.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: farmers, nomads (Golog, Gannan, Hainan), and Khams speakers in Yushu.
- **Tu** (Huzhu and Minhe varieties), **Salar** (Xunhua), **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**, **Yugur** (Turkic and Mongolic speakers in Sunan), **Mongols** (Oirat in Haixi, Tibetan-speaking Mongols in Henan county *check*, Subei) and **Kazakh** (Aksay).
- **Third Front and university migrants** in Lanzhou, Baiyin, Jinchang and Xi'an, with speech from elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Chinese-language material in Guanzhong and Shaanbei; mixed for the Hexi Corridor and Ningxia; thin and scattered for Tu, Bao'an, Yugur and Kazakh. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the four core provinces.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and modern), Amdo Tibetan, and at least reading help for the Mongolic and Turkic languages; Arabic and Persian for Hui teaching texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan-medium schooling, monastic education and Hui mosque teaching have been politically charged in recent decades; treat sources and informants with care. Some monastic teachings are for initiates only.
- **Held for review:** none.
