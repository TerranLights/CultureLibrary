# Qin (秦): Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin, a Sinian Federation member state (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, with Shaanbei and Guanzhong colloquial; Hui usage with Persian loans; Amdo Tibetan; Monguor (Tu); Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han**: gift registers and lineage ties in dense plain villages.
- **Shaanbei**: temple associations and ritual kinship as the links of a dispersed loess population.
- **Shaannan**: Qing-era migrant settlers whose ties ran back to Hubei, Hunan and Sichuan (*check*).
- **Gansu and Ningxia Han**: heavy out-migration; home-place networks against the empty village.
- **Relocated Xihaigu families**: new neighbors, old ties stretched by distance.
- **Third Front city dwellers**: work-unit ties among people from other provinces.
- **Hui**: mosque community, Sufi order and kin business networks; Hualong and Linxia cases differ.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: camp, tribe and monastery ties; nomads against farmers.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an**: village festivals and Muslim and Buddhist networks that cross group lines.
- **Haixi Mongols, Aksay Kazakhs and Yugur**: small peoples whose ties reach other provinces.
- **Diaspora, comparison only**: Shaanxi native-place guild halls elsewhere in the federation, and the Dungan of Central Asia, descended from Shaanxi and Gansu Hui (*check*); this member state's findings apply to them, not the reverse.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on guanxi in general and on Han villages; mixed on Hui networks; thin on minority festivals and loneliness outside the elder surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Kazakh; Persian loans for Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** networks across the borders of Xinjiang and Tibetan areas are watched; guanxi research touches corruption; relocation is a policy subject with official framing.
- **Held for review:** none.
