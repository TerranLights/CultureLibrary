# Qin (秦): Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin, a Sinian Federation member state (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (modern and classical); Hui usage; Amdo Tibetan; Wutun and other mixed varieties; Yugur (Turkic and Mongolic); Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** how two-ness is lived with: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han**: classical pairs in temple, burial and opera; the folk sense of face and lining.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han**: yinyang specialists and temple rites; Fuxi and Nüwa claims.
- **Hui of Xi'an, Ningxia and Linxia**: Muslim and Chinese at once; quarters, villages and orders differ.
- **Tibetan-speaking Hui** in Hualong (*check*): a further layer of two-ness.
- **Wutun people**: classified one way, living another.
- **Henan Mongols**: a Mongol identity held in Tibetan speech.
- **Yugur**: two languages inside one people.
- **Tu of Huzhu and Minhe**: Mongolic speech with Tibetan Buddhist and Chinese folk practice.
- **Rebgong villages**: Buddhist, Bön and medium traditions side by side.
- **Amdo Tibetans broadly**: Tibetan and Chinese schooling and work selves.
- **Third Front families and their children**: two home places.
- **Salar and Dongxiang**: Turkic or Mongolic speech, Muslim practice, Chinese neighbors; how many sides are claimed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical paired thought; mixed for Hui dual identity (several good ethnographies); thin and scattered for mixed groups such as Wutun, Henan Mongols and Tibetan-speaking Hui.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with classical), Amdo Tibetan, Yugur and Mongolian help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official ethnic classification is politically fixed and contesting it is sensitive; dual loyalty is a charged subject for Hui and Tibetans; spirit-medium rites may limit what outsiders may record.
- **Held for review:** none.
