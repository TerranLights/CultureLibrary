# Qin (秦): Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin, a Sinian Federation member state (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, with village colloquial; Hui usage with Arabic and Persian terms; Amdo Tibetan; Monguor (Tu); Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). Formal mediation law and courts: Libra; the spirit of legal culture: Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong and Longdong Han**: lineage elders and the Yan'an-era model of talking-out.
- **Shaanbei Han**: matchmakers and temple committees as go-betweens.
- **Urban Han in Xi'an and Lanzhou**: offices and work units in place of elders.
- **Hui of Linxia and Ningxia**: imam, mosque elders and Sufi shaykhs; Yihewani communities without saints' tombs differ.
- **Salar and Dongxiang**: Muslim go-betweens in small peoples; overlap with Hui orders.
- **Golog and Gannan nomads**: lamas and respected men between feuding groups.
- **Amdo farmers**: village heads and monasteries; may differ from nomads.
- **Tu**: spirit mediums and lamas as go-betweens.
- **Haixi Mongols and Aksay Kazakhs**: steppe roles of the eloquent elder.
- **Hui and Tibetan trade partners**: mediation across a group line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good material on Golog feuding and Sufi orders; thin on everyday Han and Hui go-betweens outside Chinese rural sociology.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian terms for Sufi sources, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi tombs, reincarnate lamas and feuding are politically sensitive; compensation and court matters belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
