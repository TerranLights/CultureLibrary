# Qin (秦): Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin, a Sinian Federation member state (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (modern and classical), Guanzhong and Gansu colloquial; Hui usage with Arabic terms; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor, unconfirmed reports passed from person to person, is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong villages**: dense lineage villages with daily outdoor gatherings where talk is shared.
- **Shaanbei**: dispersed cave-dwelling hamlets; songs as a channel for what cannot be said openly.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou**: online gossip, local media and urban anonymity.
- **Third Front compounds**: closed communities where work and home overlap.
- **Hexi Corridor oasis villages**: small, distant places where everyone is known.
- **Relocated Ningxia villages**: new neighbors who do not yet know one another's histories.
- **Hui mosque communities**: religious teaching against backbiting beside close neighborhood life.
- **Amdo nomads and farmers**: camp life against village life; rites against harmful talk.
- **Tu, Salar and Dongxiang villages**: small peoples where reputation travels across kin lines.
- **Haixi Mongols and Aksay Kazakhs**: steppe sayings and small populations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good material on official rumor control and online exposure; thin on everyday gossip outside a few village studies and fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with classical), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic for Hui sources, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "rumor" is also an official charge, so sources on rumor carry state framing; gossip about real people in fieldwork needs consent and anonymity.
- **Held for review:** none.
