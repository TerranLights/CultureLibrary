# Qin: The domain, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (lands: Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and the regional Mandarin and Jin dialects; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolic Tu (Monguor), Dongxiang, Bao'an and Eastern Yugur; Turkic Salar and Western Yugur; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** what is home, and who is family: household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, the house's walls and rooms, building, family and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong** (Xi'an, Baoji, Weinan): Wei-plain courtyard houses and lineage halls, to set against the other regions.
- **Shaanbei** (Yulin, Yan'an; Jin-speaking): cave-dwelling households and the departure songs of 走西口.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): settlers from Hubei, Hunan and Sichuan, a different house tradition.
- **Longdong and Tianshui; central Gansu; the Hexi Corridor**: sunken courtyards, Lanzhou's city families, oasis farmsteads.
- **Ningxia plain against Xihaigu**: irrigated villages against resettled hill families.
- **Hehuang valley** (Xining, Haidong, Linxia): walled farmsteads shared across Han, Hui, Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetans** (Gannan, Huangnan, Golog, Yushu): farming houses against herders' tents and winter quarters.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs and Yugur**: pastoral households in Haixi, Henan, Subei, Aksai and Sunan.
- **City against village; migrant workers' split families** (留守 households).
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** the Dungan of Central Asia, who descend from Shaanxi and Gansu Hui.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Shaanxi and the census; mixed for Gansu and Ningxia; thinner for Tu, Salar, Bao'an, Yugur and Kazakh households. Qin's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet uses the working list.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with dialect help for Guanzhong and Jin forms), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian; Turkic and Mongolic minority languages for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement of herders and hill villagers; Tibetan and Hui family religion under state limits; family-planning history; boarding schools for Tibetan children. Mosque and monastery family records may not be open.
- **Held for review:** none.
