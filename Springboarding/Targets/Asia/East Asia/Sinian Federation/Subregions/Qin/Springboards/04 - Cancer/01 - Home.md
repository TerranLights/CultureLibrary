# Qin: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with Guanzhong, Lanyin and Jin dialect forms; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an and Yugur for depth. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an against the provincial towns:** high-rise compounds next to urban villages and a historic Hui quarter.
- **Shaanbei cave villages:** whether cave homes are left, kept or rebuilt.
- **Shaannan hill farms** (Ankang, Shangluo): hill households and the relocation programs.
- **Hexi oases and Lanzhou:** desert-edge farmsteads against a riverside industrial city.
- **Ningxia's irrigated plain and Xihaigu:** whose home was moved, and where to.
- **Hui neighborhoods and Hehuang villages:** whether prayer and ablution shape the house plan.
- **Amdo farmers, Amdo herders, Yushu (Kham) Tibetans:** the house, the tent and the winter quarters as three kinds of home.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an:** small peoples whose house forms overlap; separate them by courtyard and decoration.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs and Yugur:** felt-tent and pasture homes against settled housing.
- **Migrant workers:** dormitory and rented rooms in the cities against the house back in the village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Han urban housing and real estate; mixed on rural cave and courtyard homes; thin and politically shaped on pastoral resettlement.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian and Kazakh for pastoral homes; local dialect knowledge for folk terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation and demolition are contested; Tibetan resettlement and Hui neighborhood renewal are politically charged; disaster rebuilding reports may be one-sided.
- **Held for review:** none.
