# Qin: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and dialect kin terms (Guanzhong, Shaanbei Jin, Lanyin Mandarin, Xining); Amdo Tibetan; Salar; Kazakh; Mongolian; Tu, Dongxiang, Bao'an and Yugur for depth. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** printed genealogies and lineage halls, a written kin tradition to set against oral ones.
- **Shaanbei Jin speakers:** distinct kin terms, a test of how dialect divides relatives.
- **Shaannan settler families:** check genealogies for origins in Hubei, Hunan and Sichuan.
- **Hexi and central Gansu Han:** frontier settler lines; ask where each family says it came from.
- **Hui:** how family, mosque community and Sufi order overlap, compared in Linxia, Ningxia and Xi'an.
- **Amdo Tibetan farmers and herders; Yushu Tibetans:** bone lineages, tent groups and tribes compared.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Tu:** small peoples whose descent units each need their own term.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs and Yugur:** clan names and remembered genealogies of pastoral peoples.
- **Urban one-child families and migrant households:** recent family shapes set beside the old.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Central Asian Dungan, whose kin terms can be set beside Qin's dialects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han genealogies and survey data; mixed for Hui orders; thin in English for Salar, Bao'an, Dongxiang and Yugur kin systems.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for genealogies and Zhang Zai), Amdo Tibetan, Salar, Kazakh, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family planning history; Hui Sufi order disputes; Tibetan tribal structures under state control; the Ma warlord legacy. Some genealogies are private to the lineage.
- **Held for review:** none.
