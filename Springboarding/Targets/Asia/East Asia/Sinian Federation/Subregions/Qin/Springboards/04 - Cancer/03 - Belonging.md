# Qin: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Shaanxi, Gansu and Ningxia dialect words for insiders and outsiders; Amdo Tibetan; the self-names and terms of the Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur, Mongols and Kazakhs; the Hui community's own Chinese and Arabic-Persian loan vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong "old Shaanxi" against newcomers to Xi'an:** a city of students and migrants around an old core.
- **Shaanbei and Shaannan:** Jin-speaking north and Southwestern Mandarin south may not call themselves the same kind of Shaanxi people.
- **Lanzhou's industrial transplants:** Third Front and Northeast families against old Gansu residents.
- **Hexi Corridor towns:** oil and mining towns such as Yumen and Jinchang, where most families came from elsewhere *check*.
- **Ningxia Hui and Ningxia Han:** a province named for one people, lived by two.
- **Xi'an, Linxia and Xining Hui:** three Hui communities with different histories.
- **Amdo, Yushu and Tianzhu Tibetans:** regional Tibetan identities and their monasteries.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Wutun:** peoples whose recognized borders may not match their own.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur:** belonging carried by pastoral clans across provincial lines.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** Dungan in Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan, who left Qin's lands in the 1870s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Han urban identity and migrant integration; mixed on Hui; thin and sensitive on Tibetan and small-minority belonging.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Kazakh; Salar and Tu for self-description.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic and religious identity under state "unity" policy; Tibetan and Hui self-expression may be censored; hukou is legal (letter of the law is Libra's, its spirit Capricorn's).
- **Held for review:** none.
