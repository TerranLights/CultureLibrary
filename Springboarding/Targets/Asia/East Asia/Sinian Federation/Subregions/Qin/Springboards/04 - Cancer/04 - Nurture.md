# Qin: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and regional child-rearing vocabulary; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh and Mongolian for pastoral childhood rites; Hui religious vocabulary; Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an through their own scholars. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an urban middle class:** how far tutoring and expert feeding advice reach into upbringing.
- **Rural Shaanxi and Gansu villages:** who raises the children when parents leave for work.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong:** cave-village childhoods and their crafts and rhymes.
- **Shaannan mountain counties:** where outside parenting programs have been tested.
- **Hui families (Xi'an, Linxia, Ningxia):** religious naming and mosque schooling beside state school.
- **Dongxiang and Bao'an:** small communities; ask their own teachers and elders how a child is raised well.
- **Amdo Tibetan farmers and herders:** home, school and monastery as three places of nurture.
- **Tu and Salar:** rites of the first months and years, little documented in English.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols:** cradle, first steps and first haircut rites.
- **Third Front and urban danwei children:** what work-unit nurseries left behind in family memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rural early childhood thanks to development programs (outsiders' framing); mixed on folk practice; thin on Tu, Salar and Yugur nurture.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh, Mongolian; Arabic and Persian loan terms for Hui schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding schools for Tibetan children; limits on minors' religious education; development programs that describe rural parents as deficient.
- **Held for review:** none.
