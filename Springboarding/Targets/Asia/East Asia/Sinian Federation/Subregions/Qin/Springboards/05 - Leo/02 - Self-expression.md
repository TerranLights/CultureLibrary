# Qin: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with Guanzhong, Shaanbei (Jin) and Hehuang speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Tu, Salar and Yugur through specialists. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong city-dwellers and villagers:** loud opera and street talk; a regional saying on Guanzhong bluntness (生冷蹭倔) to test, not adopt.
- **Shaanbei:** song as the outlet for feeling; Yan'an's double legacy as folk heartland and party birthplace.
- **Shaannan:** speech and song closer to Sichuan; compare reserve and frankness with Guanzhong.
- **Hexi and Lanzhou:** city migrants and old oasis families.
- **Hui (Ningxia, Linxia), Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** expression through faith and dress; views differ by school and generation.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (Rebkong, Golog, Gannan) and **Yushu**: modern literature, love songs, monastic restraint.
- **Tu:** song and dress at festivals.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur:** pastoral song traditions; language use against Mandarin schooling.
- **Generations:** the post-1990 and post-2000 urban young against their parents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich song collections; thin, guarded survey data on speaking freely.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese with dialect skill; Tibetan for literature and song; Mongolian and Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political expression in Tibetan areas of Qinghai and Gansu is highly sensitive (protests, detentions); Muslim dress and mosque design are under state pressure; respondents may self-censor, and researchers must protect them.
- **Held for review:** none.
