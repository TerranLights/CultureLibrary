# Qin: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with Shaanbei and Hehuang speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui greetings from Arabic. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou young urbanites:** online display and city branding.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong migrant-sending villages:** left-behind children and elders.
- **Shaannan's mountain counties:** small, scattered settlements, likely less seen in media.
- **Hexi farm towns:** tourist gaze in heritage zones against residents.
- **Hui (Ningxia, Linxia):** dress and greeting that make faith visible; women's presence in religious space.
- **Dongxiang, Bao'an, Salar:** small peoples, often absent from national media.
- **Tibetan-speaking Muslims and other in-between groups:** fit poorly in census categories.
- **Amdo and Yushu Tibetans:** heavily watched and selectively shown.
- **Tu, Yugur, Mongols, Kazakhs:** shown in tourism as costume; daily life less visible.
- **Disabled and elderly people living alone.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Census and survey data exist; visibility as a felt need is thin and mostly in Chinese-language fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic terms in Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance in Tibetan and Muslim areas is a sensitive and dangerous topic for informants; minority counts are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
