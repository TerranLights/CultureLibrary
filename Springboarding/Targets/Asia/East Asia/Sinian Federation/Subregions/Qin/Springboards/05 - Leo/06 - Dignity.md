# Qin: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; northwest dialects; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic terms in Hui religious teaching. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaanbei and Longdong farmers and miners:** test the work-centered dignity of Lu Yao's novels against lived accounts.
- **Guanzhong:** funerals and family standing in dense, old villages.
- **Shaannan:** mountain poverty and outward migration.
- **Xihaigu and resettled Ningxia villagers:** moved by the state; dignity in resettlement.
- **Urban migrants in Xi'an and Lanzhou:** service and scrap workers.
- **Hui, Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** dignity framed by Islamic teaching and halal practice.
- **Amdo and Yushu Tibetans:** treatment of the dead and of the living under political pressure.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur:** herders settled off grassland.
- **Elders left in villages; disabled people.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Literature and policy documents are rich; direct surveys on dignity are few.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; some Arabic for Hui teaching; Mongolian and Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan protests in the 2010s, camp history and resettlement are sensitive; legal remedies belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the 《西铭》 in an annotated edition and list its terms for shared human worth.

4. Check CLDS and CLHLS documentation for province codes and respect or fair-treatment items.
5. Search for 回族 丧葬 洗埋 宁夏 ethnographies.
