*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Lanzhou colloquial speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong villages:** test how far news of lost face travels in dense kin networks.
- **Shaanbei:** teasing songs and storytelling as public ridicule.
- **Shaannan:** wedding and festival customs nearer Sichuan's.
- **Gansu and Qinghai Han:** targets of online regional jokes; replies to them.
- **Hui, Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** insults to faith as group humiliation; community responses.
- **Amdo and Yushu Tibetans:** monastic debate; political campaigns that demand denunciation.
- **Tu, Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur:** teasing and mocking songs in small, close communities.
- **Generations who lived through the 1960s–70s campaigns.**
- **Schoolchildren and women in households:** bullying and domestic humiliation.
