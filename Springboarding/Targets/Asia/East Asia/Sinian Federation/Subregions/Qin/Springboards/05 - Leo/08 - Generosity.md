# Qin: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Guanzhong speech; Hui religious terms from Persian and Arabic; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong villages:** gift ledgers and village banquets; the Lü compact's own district.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong:** poor, dry counties; giving among kin under scarcity.
- **Shaannan:** compare kin giving with the north.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou middle class:** online and organized charity.
- **Hui of Linxia, Xining and Ningxia:** alms, mosque endowments, merchant giving.
- **Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** smaller Muslim communities with their own feast customs.
- **Amdo Tibetans and Tu:** offerings to monasteries and lamas.
- **Kazakhs, Mongols, Yugur:** guest rights in herding life.
- **Third Front and work-unit families:** collective welfare and its end.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gift exchange and religious giving in Chinese fieldwork; charity statistics exist; little in English on Salar, Bao'an or Yugur giving.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Persian and Arabic terms in Hui use; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious giving to mosques and monasteries is regulated and watched; foreign-funded charity is restricted; bride price belongs to Libra (marriage).
- **Held for review:** none.
