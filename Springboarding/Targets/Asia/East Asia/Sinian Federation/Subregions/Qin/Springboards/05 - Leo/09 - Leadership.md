*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Shaanbei speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui mosque vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong:** lineage memory and state-centered leadership near the old capital.
- **Shaanbei:** revolutionary leaders and folk troupe leaders.
- **Shaannan:** mountain villages with small, scattered leadership.
- **Longdong and Hexi:** collective-era leaders and today's village worthies.
- **Hui of Linxia and Ningxia:** imams, Sufi masters, mosque boards and merchant leaders.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** community heads within small Muslim peoples.
- **Tu:** the native-chieftain past and present village roles.
- **Amdo and Yushu Tibetans:** tribal chiefs, abbots and lamas.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur:** elders and pastoral headmen.
- **Women:** leaders in families, mosques and villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history of chiefs and warlords; mixed for present informal leaders; national surveys only.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic and Persian terms for Hui leaders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious leaders' standing in Tibetan and Muslim areas is politically sensitive; party leaders' biographies are official texts; formal authority and command belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
