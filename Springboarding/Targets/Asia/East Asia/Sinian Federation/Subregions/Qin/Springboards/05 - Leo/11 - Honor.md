# Qin: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Guanzhong speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui and Salar religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong:** a regional story of stubborn bravery to test against lived accounts.
- **Shaanbei:** revolutionary honor and folk-song heroes.
- **Shaannan:** a southern frontier with different martial memory.
- **Hexi and Longdong:** war memory and frontier garrisons.
- **Hui of Xihaigu, Linxia and Ningxia:** Sufi martyrdom, family reputation, mosque standing.
- **Salar of Xunhua and Dongxiang:** history of uprisings and its present memory.
- **Bao'an:** knife-making and its link, if any, to men's honor.
- **Amdo, Golog and Yushu Tibetans:** feud and blood-price customs.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols:** lineage honor and elders' settlement.
- **Tu and Yugur:** smaller communities' codes.
- **Women in each group:** honor as men describe it against honor as women describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. History and ethnography are rich; survey measures of honor are almost absent.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Chinese; Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic and Persian for Sufi texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Muslim uprisings and Tibetan customary justice are sensitive; claims about "violent" peoples are stereotypes to avoid; revenge goes to Scorpio, legal codes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
