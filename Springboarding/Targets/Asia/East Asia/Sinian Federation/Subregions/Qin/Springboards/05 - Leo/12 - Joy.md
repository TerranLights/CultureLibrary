# Qin: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Shaanbei speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui festival terms. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaanbei:** loud drum and yangge joy; joyful funerals.
- **Guanzhong:** city lantern festivals and village temple fairs.
- **Shaannan:** southern festival forms, compare with the north.
- **Lanzhou and the Hexi towns:** drumming and city celebrations.
- **Hui of Ningxia and Linxia:** festival joy and the debates over music in it; differences by school.
- **Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** smaller Muslim festival customs.
- **Tu:** Nadun and Luröl.
- **Amdo and Yushu Tibetans:** monastery festivals, Losar, summer gatherings.
- **Kazakhs and Mongols:** Nauryz and summer festivals.
- **Yugur:** their own seasonal gatherings, little documented.
- **Older and younger generations:** calm contentment against excited fun, to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and heritage performance; well-being surveys exist but are national; little in English on Tu, Salar or Yugur festivals.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and Classical Chinese; Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Arabic festival terms in Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan religious festivals face limits on size and on participation by officials and students; Muslim festivals likewise; heritage staging may replace lived celebration.
- **Held for review:** none.
