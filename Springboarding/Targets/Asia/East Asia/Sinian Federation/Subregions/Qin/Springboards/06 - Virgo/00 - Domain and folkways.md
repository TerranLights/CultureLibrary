**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and the local Mandarin (Guanzhong, Lanyin) and Jin (Shaanbei) speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Salar, Monguor (Tu), Dongxiang (Santa), Bonan, Western and Eastern Yugur, Kazakh; Hui religious vocabulary from Arabic and Persian. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work, service, the daily round and health: what counts as good work and a well-kept life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong** (Xi'an, Wei valley): wheat plain and old court culture; the dominant Han voice, not the only one.
- **Shaanbei** (Yan'an, Yulin): Jin-speaking loess farming, coal and gas, revolutionary work lore.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): rice and tea south of the Qinling, with Sichuan and Hubei ties.
- **Longdong and Longzhong** (Qingyang, Pingliang, Tianshui, Dingxi): dry farming, out-migration.
- **Hexi Corridor** (Wuwei, Zhangye, Jiuquan, Dunhuang): oasis irrigation, mining and state farms.
- **Ningxia**: the irrigated Yellow River plain vs. the dry south (Xihaigu), with resettlement villages.
- **Hehuang** (Xining, Haidong): mixed farming of Han, Hui, Tu and Tibetans.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders and farmers** (Hainan, Huangnan, Golog, Gannan, Tianzhu); **Yushu** with its own Kham ties.
- **Hui**, **Salar** (Xunhua), **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an** (Jishishan): Muslim peoples with distinct trades and purity rules.
- **Tu (Monguor)** (Huzhu, Minhe), **Yugur** (Sunan), **Mongols** (Henan, Haixi, Subei), **Kazakhs** (Aksay): herding and mixed livelihoods.
- Urban vs. rural, state vs. private, migrant vs. settled workers cut across all of these.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Guanzhong, Shaanbei and the Yan'an era; mixed for the Hexi Corridor and Ningxia; thin in English for Salar, Bonan, Yugur and Kazakh work customs.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (including dialect lexicons), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Turkic (Salar, Yugur, Kazakh), Mongolic (Monguor, Dongxiang, Bonan); Arabic and Persian loans for Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement (生态移民), labor programs and religious practice in Tibetan and Muslim areas are politically sensitive; fieldwork access may be limited. Labor law goes to Libra and Capricorn. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author.
- **Held for review:** none.
