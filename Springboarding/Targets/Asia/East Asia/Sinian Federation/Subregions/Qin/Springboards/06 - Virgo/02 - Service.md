# Qin: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northwestern colloquial; Amdo Tibetan; Hui mosque vocabulary of Arabic and Persian origin; Mongolian; Kazakh; Salar and Monguor where sources exist. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong villages:** wedding and funeral help as an owed, reciprocal service between households.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou service economies:** restaurant, hotel and tourism workers, many from rural Shaanxi and Gansu.
- **Shaanbei:** the Yan'an memory of serving the people, and how it is taught and staged.
- **Hui (Ningxia, Linxia, Xining):** mosque service, Sufi-order loyalty, the different structures of the Gedimu, Sufi orders and Yihewani.
- **Dongxiang and Salar:** shrine and mosque service in smaller Muslim communities with their own languages.
- **Amdo Tibetans (Gannan, Huangnan, Hainan, Golog):** monastic offices and lay patronage; family members given to monasteries.
- **Tu (Huzhu, Minhe):** village festival roles and the Tibetan Buddhist ties of Monguor communities.
- **Mongols and Kazakhs of Haixi, Subei and Aksay:** guest service and hospitality in herding camps.
- **Frontier-support settlers and their descendants** in Ningxia and Qinghai, whose families came to serve.
- **Women:** domestic and care work at home and in cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state rhetoric and Chinese-language rural studies; mixed for monastic and Sufi service (much is internal and oral); thin for Kazakh and Mongol hospitality roles.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic and Persian terms in Hui usage; Monguor, Mongolian, Kazakh for small communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic life and Sufi orders are politically sensitive and may be closed to outsiders; secret military service records are restricted; military service and obedience belong to Capricorn, civil-service rules to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
