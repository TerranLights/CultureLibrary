# Qin: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northwestern dialect words; classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Monguor, Salar and Dongxiang where sources exist. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose: for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong farm families:** sons as old-age security and the pressure on a son to be of use.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong migrant-sending villages:** grandparents raising children, elders who feel useful or set aside.
- **Lanzhou, Baiyin, Tongchuan and other industrial towns:** workers whose usefulness was tied to a state work unit that closed.
- **Shaannan:** mountain households with forestry and tea work; household roles for the old.
- **Hexi Corridor oasis farmers:** water and land shortages that set what a pair of hands is worth.
- **Hui and Salar families:** sons and daughters in trade, and religious teaching on beneficial knowledge.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders:** the yak economy and the elder's place in the tent; monks as useful to the community.
- **Tu and Yugur:** mixed farming and herding households with small populations, where each person's role is visible.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan, Kazakhs of Aksay:** herd work by age and sex.
- **Disabled people and their families:** across all groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chinese-language aging and migration studies; mixed for disability; thin for minority-language words on usefulness and burden.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese and classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian, Kazakh; Monguor, Salar, Bonan and Dongxiang dictionaries for small-people terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder suicide and family neglect are sensitive and underreported; disability data is official and may be incomplete; resettlement of herders bears on herders' sense of usefulness and is politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
