## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an and Guanzhong:** old-capital pride in taste and antiques; a city of experts and of copyists.
- **Shaanbei:** song courtship and village judgment of character; coal-boom money and new consumer taste.
- **Shaannan:** tea country, with its own tasting habits distinct from Fu tea.
- **Longdong and Longnan:** markets and rural fairs where buyers judge goods and animals.
- **Hexi Corridor:** horse and jade traditions; oasis trade.
- **Hui (Ningxia, Linxia, Xining):** halal judgment, trading savvy, choice of spouse within or across Sufi orders and schools.
- **Dongxiang, Salar and Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples with distinct trading and marriage practices.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** lama counsel and divination beside lay judgment; judging yaks, horses and cordyceps.
- **Tu, Yugur, Mongols, Kazakhs:** horse and livestock judgment at festivals; elders' counsel.
- **Older vs. younger urban residents:** fraud targets and new online savvy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts on judging people and for Han connoisseurship; mixed for halal and Tibetan practice; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Lanyin colloquial; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic loanwords in Hui Chinese; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** halal certification and Muslim food rules are politically sensitive; antiques trade touches on tomb robbing; lama divination and Sufi loyalties are religious matters under state scrutiny; prejudice between peoples must not be passed off as "judgment".
- **Held for review:** none.
