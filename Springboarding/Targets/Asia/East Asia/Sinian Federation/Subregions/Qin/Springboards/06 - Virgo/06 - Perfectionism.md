*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese and Shaanxi colloquial; Amdo and literary Tibetan; Arabic (Qur'anic recitation terms) as used by Hui and Salar; Monguor; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong scholarly families:** the Guan school legacy of exacting self-cultivation and its echoes in village codes.
- **Xi'an's performers and calligraphers:** training by drill and model-copying; standards of the old capital.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong households:** festival breads, paper-cuts and the judging of a woman's handiwork.
- **Students in poor counties (Huining, Xihaigu):** exam pressure as a family project.
- **Aerospace, aviation and nuclear workers** in Xi'an, Lanzhou and the Hexi Corridor: industrial zero-defect culture.
- **Amdo monks and painters:** ritual exactness, iconometry and impermanent art.
- **Hui and Salar:** exact recitation and ritual practice; differences between schools of Islam in strictness.
- **Tu women of Huzhu:** embroidery standards; **Yugur, Mongol and Kazakh** women's felt and costume crafts.
- **Generations:** older craft standards against younger "good enough" and "lying flat" talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and Han craft material and for student psychology studies; mixed for monastic art; thin for Mongol and Kazakh craft standards.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Tibetan (iconometry texts are technical); Arabic for recitation rules; Monguor and Kazakh for craft terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and exam stress are sensitive and underreported; religious recitation events are under state oversight; aerospace and nuclear quality records are restricted; Qin penalties for faulty work belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
