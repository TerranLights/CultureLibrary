## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an's educated classes:** a long literati line of remonstrance and literary criticism; also the home of state-run critique.
- **Shaanbei:** Yan'an's legacy of Party self-criticism meetings, and folk storytellers' satire.
- **Guanzhong villages:** indirect criticism through rhymes, opera and gossip; face in tight lineages.
- **Gansu (Longdong, Hexi):** the memory of Jiabiangou and how families speak, or keep silent, about it.
- **Ningxia:** Hui communities' debates between reformist and Sufi currents.
- **Linxia and Dongxiang:** where Yihewani reform began; internal religious criticism.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** writers and bloggers who criticize custom; monastic correction; very different public limits.
- **Tu, Salar, Bao'an, Yugur, Mongols, Kazakhs:** teasing and correction within small communities; elders' license to scold.
- **Workplaces:** state enterprises vs. private firms in feedback style; age and rank in who may criticize.
