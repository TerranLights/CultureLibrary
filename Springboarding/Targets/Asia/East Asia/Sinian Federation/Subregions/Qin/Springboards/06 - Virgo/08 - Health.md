*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical medical Chinese; Amdo and literary Tibetan (medical texts); Hui medical vocabulary with Arabic and Persian roots; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou:** hospital medicine, Chinese medicine clinics and a large health-product market.
- **Guanzhong and Shaanbei villages:** temple healing, fright illness and village doctors; Kashin-Beck and fluorosis areas.
- **Shaannan:** humid hill country with its own herbs and endemic problems.
- **Longdong, Longzhong and Longnan:** herb-growing districts and poor rural services.
- **Hexi Corridor:** desert oases; mining and industrial health.
- **Ningxia Hui:** Hui medicine, washing customs, halal diet as health.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples with high-altitude and remote-valley health issues.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders and farmers:** Sowa Rigpa, monastery clinics, zoonoses, altitude.
- **Tu, Yugur, Mongols, Kazakhs:** herding health practices; Mongolian medicine in Haixi.
- **Women:** postpartum confinement (坐月子), as each group keeps it; elders' chronic illness in migrant-sending villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for epidemiology and Chinese medicine; mixed for Tibetan and Hui medicine (much promotional material); thin for Mongol and Kazakh healing in Qinghai and Gansu.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese medical terms; literary Tibetan; Hui terms; Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** temple healing and spirit mediums are officially "superstition" and may be hidden; healing rites of Tibetans and Muslims are under religious oversight; COVID crisis records are censored; health law and insurance rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
