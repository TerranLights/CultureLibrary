**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Guanzhong, Shaanbei, Lanyin and Zhongyuan varieties), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Kazakh; Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an and Yugur through their dictionaries. **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are: marriage, contracts, open enemies, dispute settlement, civility, gender ways, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han** (Xi'an, Baoji, Weinan): clan and compact traditions; densest records.
- **Shaanbei Han** (Yan'an, Yulin): the Border Region's legal experiments and their marriage cases.
- **Shaannan Han** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): marriage and clan ways closer to the Sichuan basin and the Han River.
- **Hexi Corridor and Longdong Han** (Wuwei, Zhangye; Qingyang, Pingliang): oasis against loess farming bargains.
- **Hehuang Han** (Xining, Haidong): long mixing with Tibetan, Tu and Hui neighbors.
- **Hui**: Ningxia plain, Xihaigu, Linxia and Xi'an's Muslim quarter as separate cases; sect matters.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (Gannan, Tianzhu, Qinghai): farmers against herders; monastery and tribe.
- **Tu (Monguor), Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: small peoples with their own wedding rites and language.
- **Yugur** (Sunan), **Mongols** (Henan county, Haixi, Subei), **Kazakhs** (Aksay), **Baima** (Wen county): distinct herding and marriage customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Guanzhong, the Border Region and Dunhuang-era texts; mixed for Hui and Amdo Tibetans; thin for Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur and Aksay Kazakhs. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the four provinces listed.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Kazakh; Hui Arabic-Persian loanwords; dictionaries for the small peoples' languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sect conflict among Hui, nineteenth-century uprisings, Tibetan monastic authority, petitioning and court access are politically sensitive; court-judgment databases have been reduced since about 2021. The law cited is pre-war PRC law, a baseline for what the post-war member state carries.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. On the national laws database, pull the Civil Code's marriage-and-family and contract books, then search "变通规定 婚姻" with each autonomous prefecture's name.
2. In the 2020 census provincial volumes for Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia and Qinghai, find marital status by nationality.
3. CNKI: "彩礼 甘肃 OR 宁夏 OR 青海" and "草山纠纷 调解", sorted by citations.
4. Library: the Tang Code with commentary (Chinese edition and an English translation), then the Zhouyuan inscriptions on land exchange.
5. Gather hua'er recordings from the Lianhuashan fair; list courtship and rivalry themes.
