## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** canal-turn fairness and a long record of estate division.
- **Shaanbei Han:** splitting customs and the "ordinary world" picture of unequal chances.
- **Shaannan Han:** out-migration from Shangluo and Ankang, and the migrant's view of fair pay.
- **Hexi oasis farmers:** water turns as the main fairness question.
- **Ningxia, Linxia and Xi'an Hui:** Islamic inheritance against civil equal shares; trader bargaining.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders:** division of pasture, households against groups.
- **Aksay Kazakhs and Haixi Mongols:** shares by role at inheritance.
- **Tu, Salar, Bao'an, Dongxiang villages:** small farming communities with their own division customs.
- **Exam takers in Qinghai and Ningxia:** locals against arrivals over quota seats.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Dunhuang and water history; mixed for inheritance practice; thin for herders' division and the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for steles and division deeds; regional Mandarin; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic bonus points and exam migration are contested; grassland policy touches resettlement of herders; Islamic inheritance against state law needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.
