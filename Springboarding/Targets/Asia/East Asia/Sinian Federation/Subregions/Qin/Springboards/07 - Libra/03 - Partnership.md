# Qin: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Guanzhong, Shaanbei, Hehuang usage); Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui Arabic-Persian usage. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** merchant partnerships and clan-arranged marriage.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han:** the marriage-reform cases and exchange marriage.
- **Shaannan Han:** marriage customs nearer the Sichuan and Hubei borderlands.
- **Ningxia Hui (plain and Xihaigu), Linxia Hui, Xi'an Hui:** nikah and intermarriage rules; migrants moved to Minning.
- **Tu of Huzhu and Minhe; Salar of Xunhua:** listed wedding rites, very different from each other.
- **Dongxiang and Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples with their own languages.
- **Sunan Yugur:** a distinct union form.
- **Amdo Tibetan farmers and herders:** alliance between households; monastic celibacy beside it.
- **Henan county and Haixi Mongols; Aksay Kazakhs:** in-law alliances among herders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for marriage reform and Dunhuang; mixed for Hui and Amdo; thin for Yugur, Bao'an and Aksay Kazakhs; business partnership is thin outside merchant history.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui loanwords.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exchange marriage, bride trafficking into poor counties and Hui-Han intermarriage are sensitive; the Yugur union form is an ethnographic topic that can be exoticized; read it in Yugur scholars' own work first.
- **Held for review:** none.
