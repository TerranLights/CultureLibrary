## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Guanxue, Daoist sites and Sun Simiao's medicine.
- **Shaanbei Han:** paper-cut symmetry and the three-thirds rule.
- **Shaannan Han:** a wetter, rice-and-tea economy with different food balances.
- **Hexi oasis farmers:** water balance at the desert edge.
- **Ningxia and Linxia Hui:** Hui medicine and its humoral roots.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** Tibetan medicine, the Middle Way, and pasture limits; herders against farmers.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan county; Aksay Kazakhs; Sunan Yugur:** herders under grass-livestock limits.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** village medicine and diet, to be found.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, Tibetan medicine and ecological policy; mixed for folk health; thin for small peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan (medical and philosophical); Arabic and Persian medical vocabulary; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** herder resettlement under ecological programs is contested; Tibetan monastic texts may carry teaching restrictions.
- **Held for review:** none.
