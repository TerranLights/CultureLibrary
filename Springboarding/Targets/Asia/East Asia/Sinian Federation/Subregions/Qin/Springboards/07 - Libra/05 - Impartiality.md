# Qin: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Legalist inheritance and the magistrate satire on the Xi'an stage.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han:** village ties and mass-line judging; how a neutral judge is pictured there.
- **Hexi and Hehuang Han:** frontier administration among several peoples.
- **Hui of Ningxia, Linxia and Xi'an:** the state's even hand toward Muslims, and the ahong's position across sects.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** lamas as outside arbiters; herders against farmers.
- **Aksay Kazakhs:** the customary judge.
- **Henan county and Haixi Mongols; Sunan Yugur:** small herding communities where everyone is kin; who can be neutral there.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** village elders and clergy as arbiters; data to be found.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts and law; mixed for anticorruption cases; thin for customary arbiters among the small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases, the state's treatment of Muslims and Tibetans, and religious mediation outside state courts are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
