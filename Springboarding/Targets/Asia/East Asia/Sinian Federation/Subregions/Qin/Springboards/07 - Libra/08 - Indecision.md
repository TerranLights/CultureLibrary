## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** temple lots and urban marriage markets; the oldest written divination.
- **Shaanbei Han:** the *Life* story and Baiyunshan lots; out-migration choices.
- **Shaannan Han:** migration toward the Yangtze cities; separate temple traditions.
- **Longdong and Hexi Han:** village collective decisions in poorer counties.
- **Hui of Ningxia, Linxia and Xi'an:** guidance prayer and the ahong.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** *mo* asked of lamas; farmers against herders.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** village clergy and elders; data thin.
- **Aksay Kazakhs, Qinghai Mongols, Sunan Yugur:** herders' divination aids.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the written cooling-off and return rules and for literature; mixed for temple and Hui practice; thin for herders' divination.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (oracle bones and *Zhouyi*); Amdo Tibetan; Arabic loanwords; Kazakh; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination outside registered religious sites is officially "feudal superstition"; some lamas' *mo* is private consultation. Approach both through published work first.
- **Held for review:** none.
