*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Hui usage of Arabic terms; Kazakh; Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** court-avoidance norms with a long written record.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han:** village mediation and silence about home violence.
- **Shaannan Han:** to be checked; migrant families split by distance.
- **Hexi Han:** the Jiabiangou memory sits in their region.
- **Ningxia, Linxia and Xi'an Hui:** the 1872 surrender's memory; patience as religious duty.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** patience teaching beside revenge and blood-price traditions; herders against farmers.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small communities where keeping peace with larger neighbors matters; data thin.
- **Aksay Kazakhs, Qinghai Mongols, Sunan Yugur:** elders' quiet settlements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical debate and written law; mixed for domestic violence; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic loanwords; Kazakh; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor camps, the Hui uprisings, Tibetan grievances and "stability maintenance" (维稳) are highly sensitive; some works are hard to obtain inside the federation. Domestic-violence fieldwork needs survivor-safe methods.
- **Held for review:** none.
