# Qin: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Hehuang dialect terms; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian; Tu through its dictionaries. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** classical texts and the capital's sorcery case; urban media blame.
- **Shaanbei Han:** campaign-era scapegoating in old base areas.
- **Shaannan Han:** to be checked; images of Sichuan and Hubei neighbors.
- **Hexi and Longdong Han:** targets of online regional jokes.
- **Hehuang Han, Tu and Tibetans:** spirit-keeping accusations and exorcism rites.
- **Ningxia, Linxia and Xi'an Hui:** images of Muslims held by others, and their answers.
- **Amdo Tibetans (Labrang, Rebkong, Golog):** *mi kha*, *glud*, and town relations with Hui and Han.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples seen through larger groups' images.
- **Aksay Kazakhs, Haixi Mongols, Sunan Yugur:** evil-eye beliefs among herders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts and folk belief; mixed for ethnic images; thin for herders' beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Hehuang dialect; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian; Tu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accusations of spirit-keeping can harm real families; never name them. Ethnic images, the 1989 protests and Cultural Revolution records are sensitive; some files are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
