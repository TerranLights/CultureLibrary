# Qin: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Guanzhong, Lanyin and Jin dialects), Amdo Tibetan, Tu, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Salar, Yugur, Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** how a people faces death, power and what is hidden; with Fischer's death ways and sex ways, inheritance and shared resources.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Wei River lineage villages; the setting of most written sources.
- **Shaanbei Han (Jin-speaking):** cave villages with their own ritual specialists and funeral music.
- **Shaannan Han:** Hanzhong, Ankang and Shangluo, with Ba-Shu and Chu links in funeral singing.
- **Longdong and Longzhong Han:** Qingyang to Dingxi; dry farming and famine memory.
- **Hexi Corridor Han:** settler-descended oasis towns, Wuwei to Dunhuang.
- **Hehuang Han:** mixed-language frontier towns around Xining and Linxia.
- **Hui:** Ningxia's south (Xihaigu), Yinchuan, Linxia, Xi'an's old quarter; Sufi orders, Gedimu, Yihewani and the Xidaotang of Lintan bury and remember differently.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** farmers of Rebgong and Hualong, herders of Golog and Gannan; Yushu's Khampa for contrast.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur:** small peoples with their own kin, burial and marriage rules.
- **Mongols** (Haixi, Henan, Subei) and **Kazakhs** (Aksay): herders with their own death and inheritance ways.
- **Urban migrants and Third Front families** (Xi'an, Lanzhou, Baoji): village rites reworked in cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Guanzhong Han, Dunhuang manuscripts and Hui ethnography; mixed for Amdo; thin for Bao'an, Yugur and Aksay Kazakhs.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and Dunhuang vernacular too), Amdo Tibetan, Hui Arabic and Persian loanwords; minority tongues for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hui and Tibetan religious life; famine and campaign deaths; sky-burial sites (no outsiders' cameras); the inter-ethnic violence of the 1860s and 1920s. **Lands:** the pre-war provinces listed are a working list; Qin's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** none.
