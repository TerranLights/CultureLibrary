# Qin: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (Guanzhong, Shaanbei Jin and Gansu-Ningxia dialects), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, and the Hui community's Arabic and Persian loanwords; Tu, Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an for fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead and what death means, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** lineage villages; test how strictly mourning grades and ritual weeping are kept.
- **Shaanbei Han:** cave villages with their own specialists to set the hour and place of a burial.
- **Shaannan Han:** where the sung wake is recorded; links south to Sichuan and Hubei.
- **Longdong and Longzhong Han:** famine dead of the 1920s and 1960s in family memory.
- **Hexi Han:** precious-scroll recitation and oasis graveyards.
- **Hui, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** burial under Islamic rules, carried out differently by order, people and village.
- **Amdo farming and herding Tibetans:** monastery-led rites, and sky burial against earth burial.
- **Tu, Yugur, Mongols:** Buddhist peoples who are not Tibetan, each with their own disposal of the dead.
- **Urban Xi'an and Lanzhou:** hospital deaths and funeral homes against home deaths in villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han ritual, Tang epitaphs and Hui burial; mixed for Amdo; thin for Yugur, Bao'an and Mongols.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for epitaphs), Amdo Tibetan, Hui loanwords; minority tongues for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine and labor-camp deaths; Tibetan self-immolations in Rebgong and Gannan; sky-burial sites; Hui grave relocations.
- **Held for review:** none.
