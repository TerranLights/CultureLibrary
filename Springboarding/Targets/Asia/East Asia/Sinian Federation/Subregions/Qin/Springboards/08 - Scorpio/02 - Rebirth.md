# Qin: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Hui Islamic vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loans), Tu and other minority tongues for fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Catholic and Protestant converts beside lineage villages; Daoist sites near Xi'an.
- **Shaanbei Han:** the leaving-the-village story told in its own fiction and in migration.
- **Longdong and Xihaigu people (Han and Hui):** the resettled, and those who stayed behind.
- **Hui:** compare what renewal of faith means in Gedimu, Sufi and Yihewani congregations.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples with their own histories of conversion.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** incarnation lines, monastic vows and lay renewal rites.
- **Tu and Yugur:** language revival and Buddhist practice outside Tibetan identity.
- **Longnan and Yushu townspeople:** rebuilt towns.
- **Urban recovering users:** Lanzhou and Xining; state facilities against family and mosque routes to recovery.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Daoist and Sufi history and literature; mixed for recovery; thin and guarded for incarnation.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for slips and Daoist texts), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian terms in Hui use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of incarnate lamas is politically controlled; resettlement and labor-camp "remaking" are contested; compulsory rehabilitation.
- **Held for review:** none.
