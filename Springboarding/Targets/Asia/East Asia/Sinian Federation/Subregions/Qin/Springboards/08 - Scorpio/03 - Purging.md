# Qin: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with classical Chinese), Amdo Tibetan, Hui ritual vocabulary; Salar, Dongxiang and Tu for fieldwork. **Concept in one line** (proposed): getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** a long history of state purges near the capital, against village fumigation and medicine-temple fairs.
- **Shaanbei Han:** Yan'an's rectification memory beside year-turn rites of riddance.
- **Hexi and Hehuang Han:** masked exorcisms and frontier temple life.
- **Hui of Xi'an, Linxia and Ningxia:** ritual purity in daily life; expulsion memory of the 1860s.
- **Salar and Dongxiang:** how ritual purity is kept in villages with their own languages and orders.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** smoke offerings, year-end casting out, medicinal bathing.
- **Tu:** Buddhist and village exorcism between Tibetan and Han forms.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan:** check fire and smoke cleansing among herders, apart from Tibetan forms.
- **Lanzhou and Yinchuan residents:** industrial pollution and cleanup.
