# Qin: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with classical Chinese and Qinqiang stage language), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian; Hui, Salar and Tu speech for fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** compare opera audiences with lineage elders on how a spouse's abandonment is judged.
- **Shaanbei Han:** revolutionary-base memory of comrades accused of treachery.
- **Longdong and Hexi Han:** migration and separated couples; how desertion is told.
- **Hui:** memory of 1870s leaders who surrendered; ties between orders.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small, close communities; test how informing is handled where everyone knows everyone.
- **Amdo and Khampa Tibetans:** epic treachery and loyalty to monastery and chief.
- **Tu and Mongols:** loyalties split between Tibetan, Han and their own leaders.
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou urban middle class:** affairs, divorce and consumer disputes in a media setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history, opera and fiction; mixed for infidelity data; thin for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and stage language), Amdo and Golog Tibetan, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party-history cases and Cultural Revolution informing; the 1870s war; Tibetan political loyalty. Law on treason goes to Libra or Capricorn.
