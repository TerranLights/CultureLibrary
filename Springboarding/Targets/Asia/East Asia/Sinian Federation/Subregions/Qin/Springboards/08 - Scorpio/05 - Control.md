# Qin: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (dialects of Guanzhong, Shaanbei and Gansu-Ningxia; classical Chinese), Amdo Tibetan, Hui Islamic terms, Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** water-management history; test how far lineage elders still steer households.
- **Shaanbei Han:** temple committees and rain rites as village self-management.
- **Longdong and Longzhong Han:** dry-land fatalism to test, and bride-price pressure.
- **Hexi Han:** oasis water quotas and desert edges.
- **Ningxia Hui:** the mosque's part in community order; women's mosques and schools, where they exist.
- **Linxia Hui, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** strict and less strict families compared, by order and village.
- **Salar:** marriage arrangements in Xunhua villages.
- **Amdo herders and farmers:** grassland policy against monastic and family control.
- **Tu and Mongols:** herding and farming households under different land regimes.
- **Third Front and urban families:** work-unit control and its loosening.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water history, development projects and women's-status surveys; mixed for family control; guarded for monasteries and mosques.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Legalist texts), Amdo Tibetan, Hui terms; Salar and Dongxiang for village work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state control of religion and of Tibetan herders; surveillance; partner violence. Law on control goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
