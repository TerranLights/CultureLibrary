# Qin: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (with classical Chinese for compacts and deeds), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian, Hui Islamic vocabulary; Salar and Dongxiang for market fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is included here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** compact tradition and merchant credit to test against present-day trust surveys.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han:** village credit and fictive kin in poor dry-land counties.
- **Hexi Han:** oasis towns on old trade routes; trust among settlers from many places.
- **Hui traders of Linxia, Xi'an and Ningxia:** long-distance commerce and halal trust.
- **Dongxiang, Bao'an, Salar:** small peoples trading in shared markets; whom each relies on, and for what.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders:** oaths, monastery mediators, and dealing with Hui and Han traders.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan:** sworn-brother bonds and herding partnerships.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay:** a small community at the edge of the corridor.
- **Urban Xi'an and Lanzhou:** trust in brands, platforms and hospitals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and national surveys; mixed for frontier brokers; thin for Kazakh and Bao'an communities.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and deed language), Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian; Arabic terms in Hui use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inter-ethnic trust is a guarded topic; food-safety scandals. Trust in police and courts is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
