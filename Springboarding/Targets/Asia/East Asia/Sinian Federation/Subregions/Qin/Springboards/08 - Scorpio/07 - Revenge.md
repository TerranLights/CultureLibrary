# Qin: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (classical Chinese for the debates; Qinqiang stage language), Amdo and Golog Tibetan, Hui vocabulary, Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** classical filial-revenge ideal and lineage fights in Wei River villages.
- **Shaannan Han:** the setting of the 2018 case; hill-county neighbor disputes.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong Han:** test whether old grudges pass through families in poor villages.
- **Hui of Ningxia and Linxia:** the 1860s and 1920s reprisals in memory; forgiveness asked before death.
- **Dongxiang, Salar, Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples; how they stood in, or stayed out of, larger groups' reprisals.
- **Golog and other Amdo herders:** feud, mediators and blood-price.
- **Amdo farmers and Tu:** farming villages, to set against herders' feuds; local tellings of Milarepa.
- **Mongols and Kazakhs:** herders with their own compensation customs.
- **Urban netizens of Xi'an and Lanzhou:** online exposure as payback.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical debate and opera; mixed for Amdo feud; thin for Kazakh and Mongol practice in Qin.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese, Amdo and Golog Tibetan, Kazakh, Mongolian; Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inter-ethnic reprisals; Tibetan customary compensation (politically contested); recent revenge killings. Private vengeance against state punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
