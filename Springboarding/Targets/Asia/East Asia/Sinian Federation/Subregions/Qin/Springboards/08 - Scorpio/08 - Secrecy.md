# Qin: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Chinese (including trade argots), Amdo Tibetan, Hui Sufi vocabulary, Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** craft and opera families with guarded skills; Catholic villages under pressure.
- **Shaanbei Han:** brotherhood networks and the old revolutionary base's habits of secrecy, to test.
- **Hexi Han:** heirs of the Dunhuang past; oasis towns near once-secret defense sites.
- **Hui:** compare transmission inside Sufi lineages with open Gedimu and Yihewani congregations; family businesses and their recipes.
- **Dongxiang and Salar:** Sufi members who speak their own languages; test what is shown to outsiders and what is not.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** restricted religious knowledge; monks and lay people under watch.
- **Haiyan herders (Tibetan and Mongol):** moved for the nuclear base.
- **Tu and Yugur:** check how much of their own practice is recorded at all, and by whom.
- **Families touched by trafficking:** hidden adoptions in Weinan and beyond.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Dunhuang and Sufi history; mixed for secret societies; thin and restricted for defense sites and esoteric Tibetan practice.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (trade argots, classical for old texts), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian Sufi terms, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tibetan tantric content is restricted knowledge: research its existence, not its content. Underground churches, Sufi orders, nuclear sites and trafficking victims all need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
