# Qin: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** the old capital region; test office, connections and lineage as paths to power.
- **Shaanbei Han:** revolutionary prestige and new coal money in the same counties.
- **Hexi Han:** company towns and state farms.
- **Ningxia and Linxia Hui:** hereditary Sufi leadership against reformist congregations; warlord memory.
- **Dongxiang and Salar:** village leaders and religious heads in small peoples.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** monastic estates, lay chiefs and their successors since the 1950s.
- **Tu:** former native chiefdoms of Huzhu and Minhe.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan:** banner nobility and herders now.
- **Women across groups:** household, religious and public power, compared.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical history, Labrang and the Shaanbei base; mixed for Mongol and Tu chiefdoms; thin and guarded on current elites.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Mongolian; Hui Arabic and Persian terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** today's political networks, monastic leadership, Sufi succession and warlord memory are all guarded. Legitimate authority and power ways are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
