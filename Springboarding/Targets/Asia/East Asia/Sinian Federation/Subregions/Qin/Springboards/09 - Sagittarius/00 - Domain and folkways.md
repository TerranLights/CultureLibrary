## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han** (Xi'an, Baoji, Weinan): Guanxue heritage, Buddhist ancestral temples, Daoist Louguan Tai; urban secularity.
- **Shaanbei Han** (Yulin, Yan'an): dragon-king and village temple religion, revolutionary memory side by side.
- **Shaannan Han** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): Sichuan- and Hubei-linked folk practice; ask whether it groups with Guanzhong at all.
- **Longdong and Tianshui Han**: Fuxi temple, Xiwangmu shrine at Jingchuan, Kongtong Shan Daoism.
- **Hexi Corridor**: Dunhuang and Zhangye, Buddhist grotto heritage and migrant-settler towns.
- **Hui**, split by order (Gedimu, Khufiyya, Jahriyya, Qadiriyya, Yihewani, Salafiyya) and by place (Linxia, Xihaigu in southern Ningxia, Yinchuan plain, Xining).
- **Salar** (Xunhua), **Dongxiang** and **Bao'an** (Linxia, Jishishan): Muslim peoples with their own languages and origin stories.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (Gannan, Huangnan/Rebkong, Golog, Haibei, Tianzhu) and **Kham Tibetans** of Yushu: Gelug, Nyingma and Bön communities apart.
- **Tu (Monguor)** (Huzhu, Minhe, Datong): Buddhist monasteries plus village rites like Nadun.
- **Mongols** (Henan, Haixi, Subei), **Yugur** (Sunan), **Kazakhs** (Aksay): pastoral religion along the Qilian range.
- **Han Christians** (Catholic villages of Guanzhong; Protestant congregations) and unaffiliated urbanites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hui Islam, Amdo Buddhism and Dunhuang; mixed for Han folk religion; thin for Yugur, Bao'an and Kazakh communities.
- **Lands:** the state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; the sheets cover the four provinces listed in the tracker.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (including classical), Amdo Tibetan, some Arabic and Persian vocabulary, Mongolic and Turkic languages for depth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion policy (registration, limits on minors' religious study, mosque architecture changes, monastery management) is politically sensitive; protect informants. Some lineage and esoteric teachings are restricted by their own holders.
- **Held for review:** none.
