# Qin: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Northwest Mandarin; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic, Persian loans); Salar, Dongxiang, Tu, Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jahriyya Hui** of Xihaigu (Xiji, Haiyuan, Guyuan) against **Gedimu** and **Yihewani** Hui of the Yinchuan plain and Xining: different relations to saints and tombs.
- **Khufiyya and Qadiriyya** Hui of Linxia, with their tomb shrines.
- **Salar**, **Dongxiang** and **Bao'an**: Muslim peoples whose faith is tied to separate origin stories.
- **Amdo Tibetans** of Labrang and Rebkong (Gelug, Nyingma lay tantrists) against **Golog** herders and **Yushu** Khampas.
- **Tu** of Huzhu and Minhe: Buddhist and village-deity practice side by side.
- **Haixi and Henan Mongols**; **Sunan Yugur**; **Aksay Kazakhs**: smaller pastoral faiths.
- **Guanzhong Catholics**; **Han Protestants** in cities and Longdong villages.
- **Shaanbei** temple-goers; **Shaannan** villages with Sichuan-style rites.
- **Unaffiliated urban Han** in Xi'an, Lanzhou, Yinchuan, Xining, whose faith may sit in the nation, science or family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hui and Amdo Buddhist faith; mixed for Han Christians and folk vows; thin for Yugur and Kazakh communities.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic and Persian for Hui texts; Salar and Dongxiang for oral testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches, Sufi lineages under pressure, Tibetan monastic devotion to exiled teachers, and minors' religious instruction are all politically sensitive; anonymize. Some initiations are closed by the holders themselves.
- **Held for review:** none.
