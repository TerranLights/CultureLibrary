# Qin: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Guanzhong and Shaanbei dialect wording, Lan-Yin Mandarin; Amdo Tibetan; Hui Islamic vocabulary; Mongolian and Kazakh for pastoral areas. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong clan villages**: lineage, ancestral halls and opera as meaning sources.
- **Shaanbei** loess villages and coal towns: hardship literature and labor migration.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): southern funeral songs and mountain villages emptied by migration.
- **Third Front families** in Baoji, Hanzhong, Tianshui, Lanzhou: inherited duty and later disappointment.
- **Hexi and Longdong** farmers facing water loss and outmigration.
- **Hui** of Xihaigu and Linxia: meaning set within religious duty and the next life; separate by order.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: smaller Muslim peoples whose trade and family paths differ.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: monastic and lay; herders against resettled town dwellers.
- **Tu, Mongol, Yugur, Kazakh** herders on the Qilian and Haixi grasslands.
- **Urban youth** in Xi'an, Lanzhou, Yinchuan, Xining, where 躺平 talk circulates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and Han-side scholarship; mixed in survey data (few provincial meaning items); thin for Yugur, Kazakh and Bao'an voices.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (including classical and dialect), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian terms for Hui sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data are restricted and politically handled; resettlement and religious frames of meaning touch policy; anonymize.
- **Held for review:** none.
