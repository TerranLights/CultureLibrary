# Qin: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Shaanbei, Guanzhong and Hehuang dialect wording; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal. Law on rights goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaanbei**: border-region memory, *xintianyou*, migration songs; a home-grown liberation story.
- **Guanzhong**: older gentry and clan order against reformers' freedoms; urban Xi'an youth.
- **Shaannan**: mountain villages with a different revolutionary history; check separately.
- **Hui of Guanzhong descent** (many now in Ningxia and Gansu after the 1870s) and **Hui of Xihaigu and Linxia**: memories of the Tongzhi era differ by place and order.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: trading peoples whose mobility and limits need their own accounts.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders** (Golog, Maqu, Haibei) against **farming and town Tibetans** (Rebkong, Xining).
- **Kazakhs of Aksay** and **Mongols of Subei, Haixi and Henan**: mobility and pastoral freedom.
- **Yugur** of Sunan and **Tu** of Huzhu: small groups with distinct histories of rule.
- **Migrant workers** limited by household registration; **urban middle class** seeking release through travel or retreat.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the official revolution and the Shaanbei folk-song record; mixed for Hui memory; thin and constrained for Tibetan and Kazakh accounts inside the lands.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with dialect), Amdo Tibetan, Kazakh, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political freedom, 1958 in Amdo, the Tongzhi era, and minority self-rule are highly sensitive; informants need full protection, and much material circulates only in exile or private hands. No law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
