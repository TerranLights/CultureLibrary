## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong** literati heritage (Sima Qian, Guanxue) and Xi'an's universities.
- **Shaanbei**: home of the 实事求是 slogan and of archaeological claims at Shimao.
- **Longdong and Hexi** villages, where rumor and official word meet; the Jiabiangou region.
- **Hui** by order: scriptural truth taught through scripture halls (Yihewani, Salafiyya) against saint-centered knowledge (Sufi menhuan).
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Tu**: languages that mark evidence in grammar; how testimony is judged.
- **Amdo Tibetan monastics** (debate tradition) against **lay herders** (bards, oracles) in Golog and Yushu.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur**: oral history and genealogy as records.
- **Urban professionals** and **students** relying on platforms and official media; **migrant workers** relying on family chat groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical historiography, Buddhist epistemology and the Gesar tradition; mixed for media trust; thin for minority-language rumor studies.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese, Standard Chinese, Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian for Hui texts; linguistics for evidential grammars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine and camp history, official rumor control and minority accounts of the past are highly sensitive; some testimony circulates only abroad; protect sources.
- **Held for review:** none.
