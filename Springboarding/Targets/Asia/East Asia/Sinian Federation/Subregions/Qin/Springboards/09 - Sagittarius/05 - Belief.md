# Qin: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Hehuang, Guanzhong and Shaanbei dialect wording; Amdo Tibetan; Tu (Mangghuer, Monguor); Hui Islamic vocabulary; Mongolian, Kazakh, Western Yugur. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant people want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han**: village gods, ancestral rites and *shehuo*; urban skeptics in Xi'an.
- **Shaanbei Han**: geomancers, temple gods, belief in dragon-king rain powers.
- **Shaannan Han**: Ba-Shu style rites; check separately.
- **Longdong and Hexi Han**: farming beliefs about rain and hail; settler mixes along the corridor.
- **Hehuang Han** of Xining, Huangzhong and Ledu, who share mediums and festivals with Tu and Tibetan neighbors.
- **Hui** by order: Yihewani and Salafiyya critics of tomb visits against Sufi orders that keep them.
- **Salar**, **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**: Muslim creeds held alongside local custom and origin legends.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: monastic doctrine against village deity cults; **Bön** villages of Rebkong.
- **Tu**: Buddhist and village deity belief.
- **Mongols of Haixi and Henan**, **Yugur**, **Kazakhs of Aksay**.
- **Party members and cadres**: political belief as a stated duty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore archives and Hehuang festivals; mixed for Han village belief; thin for Yugur, Kazakh and Mongol belief inside these lands.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese with dialect, classical Chinese for gazetteers, Amdo Tibetan, Tu, Arabic and Persian terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "superstition" label carries state weight; mediumship is sometimes policed; some deity rites and Bön practices are closed to outsiders; minority religious teaching to minors is restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
