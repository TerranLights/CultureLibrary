## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han**: the scholar-refuser tradition; urban professionals for whom principle may be private.
- **Shaanbei Han**: revolutionary model figures, local memories of rectification.
- **Longdong and Tianshui**: famine-era memory and the *Spark* group.
- **Hexi** desert-edge villages: resolve in land and water work.
- **Hui**: Jahriyya martyr memory against Yihewani reformers' conviction; Xihaigu and Linxia apart.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: smaller Muslim peoples; ask who they hold up as steadfast.
- **Amdo Tibetan** writers and monks; **Yushu** Khampas and the Hoh Xil patrols.
- **Tu**, **Mongols**, **Yugur**, **Kazakhs**: their own exemplars of holding firm, still to be found.
- **Party cadres** and **model workers**: conviction as a public duty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official model figures and Hui order histories; mixed for dissent (scattered, often abroad); thin for Tu, Mongol, Yugur and Kazakh exemplars.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and modern), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dissent, famine history, Tibetan writers and Sufi martyr rites are politically sensitive; some Jahriyya commemorations are closed to outsiders; protect informants. Avoid turning dialect self-descriptions into a regional stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none.
