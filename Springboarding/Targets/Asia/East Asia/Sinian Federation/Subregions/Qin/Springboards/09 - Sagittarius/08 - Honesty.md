# Qin: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with Guanzhong, Shaanbei and Lan-Yin dialect wording; classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui trade vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han**: old merchant towns and Xi'an's service economy.
- **Shaanbei**: coal and energy money, with its own talk of graft and trust.
- **Shaannan**: smaller trading towns tied to Sichuan and Hubei.
- **Hexi and Longdong** farm districts: market-fair trading and outmigration.
- **Hui** traders and brokers of Linxia and Ningxia; halal businesses.
- **Salar**, **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**: trade-minded peoples; check each group's own sayings on fair dealing.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: herders selling to outside dealers; monastery economies.
- **Tu**, **Mongols**, **Yugur**, **Kazakhs**: pastoral exchange and the Kazakh *adal* sense.
- **Officials and state employees**, against **private traders** and **migrant workers**: who is expected to be candid with whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history and official integrity campaigns; mixed for everyday trade ethics; thin for Mongol, Yugur and Kazakh sources.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and dialect), Amdo Tibetan, Kazakh, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases and the credit system are politically sensitive; avoid any reading of a people as honest or dishonest; minority traders are often stereotyped in Han press, so read with care. No law research here.
- **Held for review:** none.
