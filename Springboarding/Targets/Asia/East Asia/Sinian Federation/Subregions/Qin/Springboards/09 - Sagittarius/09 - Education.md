## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an** and **Lanzhou** university towns: degree holders, faculty families, students from elsewhere.
- **Guanzhong villages**: the farming-and-study ideal; clan scholarships.
- **Shaanbei**: coal wealth and schooling choices; boarding-school concentration.
- **Shaannan**: mountain schools and outmigration of students.
- **Longdong** (Huining, Tongwei, Dingxi): schooling as exit from dry farming.
- **Hexi** oasis towns.
- **Hui** of Xihaigu and Linxia: mosque schooling beside state schools; girls' schooling.
- **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**, **Salar**: language and schooling gaps to check, by group.
- **Amdo Tibetans**: monastic, state Tibetan-medium and Chinese-medium routes.
- **Tu** of Huzhu, **Mongols** of Henan and Haixi, **Kazakhs** of Aksay, **Yugur** of Sunan: small-language schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and universities; mixed for mosque and monastic schooling; thin for Yugur, Bao'an and Kazakh schooling.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical for Dunhuang and academy sources), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian for scripture-hall texts, Mongolian, Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority-language schooling, religious instruction of minors and monastic enrollment limits are politically sensitive; student protests over language schooling have occurred in Amdo; protect students and teachers.
- **Held for review:** none.
