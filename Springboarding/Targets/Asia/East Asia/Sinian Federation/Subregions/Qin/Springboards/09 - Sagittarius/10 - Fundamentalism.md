# Qin: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Hui religious Chinese; Arabic and Persian terms; Amdo Tibetan; Dongxiang and Salar for village-level debate. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gedimu** Hui (oldest pattern, local customs) against **Yihewani** and **Salafiyya** reformers; separate Linxia, Xining, the Yinchuan plain and Xihaigu.
- **Sufi orders** (Khufiyya, Jahriyya, Qadiriyya, Kubrawiyya): tomb shrines as the line reformers dispute.
- **Xidaotang** of Lintan: a different answer to modern change.
- **Salar**, **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**: home ground of reform founders; see how strictness and ethnicity interact.
- **Amdo Tibetan** Gelug monasteries (Labrang, Kumbum) against **Nyingma** and **Bön** communities of Rebkong; herders touched by lay vow movements in **Golog**.
- **Han Christians**, registered and unregistered, and **new religious groups** under the official label.
- **Han folk sectarian** heritage in Shaanbei and Guanzhong.
- **Party ideologues** and **neo-Maoist** circles, where the term is disputed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hui order histories and official framing; mixed for Tibetan sectarian debate; thin and biased for small Christian-derived groups.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, Arabic and Persian terms, Amdo Tibetan; Dongxiang and Salar for local preaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** this is the most sensitive concept in the sign for Qin. "Extremism" and "cult" labels carry state penalties; never treat a label as a finding; protect every informant; much material is censored or held abroad. Only a rare edge of any faith turns violent; do not frame whole peoples or orders by it.
- **Held for review:** none.
