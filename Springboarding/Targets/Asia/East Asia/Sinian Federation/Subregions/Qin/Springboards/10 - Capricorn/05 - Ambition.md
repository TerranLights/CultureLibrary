# Qin: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Northwest dialect phrases; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui, Salar and Dongxiang voices mostly through Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an's professionals and graduates:** tech, aerospace and state-job ambition in a big inland city.
- **Guanzhong farm families:** a long tradition of exams and trade; compare with the uplands.
- **Shaanbei:** the "leave the land" story of Lu Yao's country, now joined by coal wealth in Yulin and Shenmu.
- **Shaannan's Shangluo, Ankang and Hanzhong:** migrant labor to cities as the main ladder.
- **Longzhong, Longdong and Xihaigu:** poorest counties, where school and resettlement are the routes out.
- **Hexi oasis towns:** smaller cities whose young people leave for Lanzhou or the east.
- **Hui of Linxia, Hualong and Ningxia:** trade and restaurant enterprise, shaped by order and kin networks.
- **Salar and Dongxiang:** trade and transport niches; separate from Hui.
- **Amdo Tibetan herders and townspeople:** monastic careers, caterpillar-fungus income and state jobs as rival paths.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs and Yugur:** small pastoral peoples whose young must choose between herding and town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han rural-to-urban striving and exam culture; mixed for Hui enterprise; thin for Kazakh, Yugur and Mongol aspirations.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian and Kazakh for small-community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty-alleviation and resettlement stories are official showcases, so separate the state narrative from residents' own accounts; monastic careers are regulated.
- **Held for review:** none.
