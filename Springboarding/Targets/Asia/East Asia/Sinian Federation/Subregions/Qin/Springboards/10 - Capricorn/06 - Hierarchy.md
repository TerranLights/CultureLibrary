*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Amdo Tibetan (including its honorific register); Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui religious titles. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an and the Guanzhong plain:** ancient capitals, dense bureaucracy and lineages; rank tied to office.
- **Shaanbei:** a landlord past remade by land reform; compare the old order and today's coal-boom rich.
- **Shaannan:** settler counties with shallower lineages; does rank rest more on money or office?
- **Gansu's Hexi oases and Longzhong uplands:** urban-rural gaps and state-farm rank lines.
- **Ningxia's north and south:** the irrigated plain against Xihaigu, a gap of wealth and status.
- **Hui:** hierarchy inside each Sufi order against the flatter mosque-centered Yihewani; compare, do not merge.
- **Salar and Dongxiang:** clan seniority and religious offices in small Muslim peoples.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** monastic, tribal and lay ranks; Rebkong, Labrang and pastoral Golog may differ.
- **Tu:** a remembered chieftain-and-subject order beside Buddhist ranks.
- **Mongols of Henan and Haixi:** noble and commoner lines of the banner era.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay:** the white-bone and black-bone distinction, its memory and present use.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ancient and imperial rank in Guanzhong; mixed for Sufi and Amdo hierarchy; thin for Kazakh and Mongol rank in these lands.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Amdo and classical Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class labels from land reform still carry weight in families; hukou and official privilege are sensitive topics; succession inside Sufi orders and reincarnate lineages is politically regulated, and some order matters are kept from outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
