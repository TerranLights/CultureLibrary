# Qin: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northwest Mandarin speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Salar; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong plain villages** against **Xi'an apartment compounds**: the old compact and clan hall against the gated 小区.
- **Shaanbei hill villages** (Yulin, Yan'an): scattered cave dwellings and exchanged labor; heavy out-migration.
- **Shaannan** mountain villages (Ankang, Shangluo): resettled from slopes to new towns.
- **Ningxia Hui villages** and **relocation towns** (Minning, Hongsibu): faith community and new-built community together.
- **Linxia Hui** congregations: community defined by mosque and Sufi order.
- **Amdo farming villages** against **Amdo and Golog herders**: fixed village ritual against moving camps; and the **Yushu Kham** area after 2010.
- **Tu villages** (Huzhu, Minhe) and **Salar villages** (Xunhua): small peoples whose community ties run through kin groups.
- **Danwei towns** (Third Front, oil and mining): community made by the employer.
- *Comparison only:* Dungan villages in Central Asia, for a Hui community cut off from Qin since the 1870s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han villages and Hui quarters; mixed for Amdo; thin for Kazakh, Mongol and Yugur camps.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Salar terms (usually given in Chinese transcription); Arabic and Persian loans.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relocation and urban-renewal disputes; mosque and monastery communities under state management; ask before recording religious community life.
- **Held for review:** none.
