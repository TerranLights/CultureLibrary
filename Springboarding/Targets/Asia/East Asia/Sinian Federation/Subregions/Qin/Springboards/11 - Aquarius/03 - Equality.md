*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (classical and modern) and local Mandarin speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui religious terms. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an and Lanzhou urban residents** against **Longzhong and Xihaigu farmers**: the widest gap in income and schooling.
- **Shaanbei**: Border Region egalitarian experiments, then oil and coal wealth unevenly shared.
- **Guanzhong women** and **Hui women** (Ningxia, Linxia): different routes to and limits on equality between the sexes.
- **Amdo herders and farmers** before and after 1958: chieftain rank, monastic rank and state class labels.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: small peoples' standing beside Han and Hui in mixed counties.
- **Third Front migrants** and **local villagers**: two classes of residents in the same town.
- **Gaokao migrants** in Qinghai and Ningxia: outsiders using the rules meant for local equality.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** income data rich at province level, thinner by people; oral history rich for Shaanxi; mixed for Amdo; thin for Kazakh, Mongol and Yugur.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for registers; modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian and Kazakh for the small herding peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class labels and campaigns; the 1958 "democratic reform" in Amdo; ethnic preferential policies; household registration rules. Equality as written law goes to Libra; fairness to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
