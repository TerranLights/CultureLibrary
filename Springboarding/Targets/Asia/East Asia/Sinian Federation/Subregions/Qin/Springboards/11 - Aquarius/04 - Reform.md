# Qin: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui and Dongxiang religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong elites and engineers**: Republican-era reformers working through opera, irrigation and schools.
- **Shaanbei**: the Border Region's reforms, then Grain-for-Green and energy development.
- **Ikhwan Hui** against **Sufi-order Hui** and the **Xidaotang**: reform's supporters and targets inside one faith.
- **Dongxiang** (birthplace of the Ikhwan's founder): a small people at the center of a reform movement.
- **Amdo herders** (Golog, Sanjiangyuan) against **Amdo farmers**: resettlement and fencing hit them differently.
- **Kazakhs** (Aksay) and **Mongols** (Haixi): grazing reforms on other herding peoples.
- **State-firm workers** in Lanzhou, Baiyin, Baoji and Jinchang: reform's losers by their own account.
- **Xihaigu villagers** moved by the anti-poverty programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical reform and recent state programs; mixed for Hui reform (much is internal and partisan); thin for Kazakh and Mongol views.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan for herders' own words; Arabic and Persian for Hui religious debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1958 reforms in Amdo, thought reform and labor reform, sectarian splits, resettlement disputes. Legal reform goes to Libra (text) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
