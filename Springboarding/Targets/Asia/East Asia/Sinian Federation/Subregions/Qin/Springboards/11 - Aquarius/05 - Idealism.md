*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong scholars and teachers**: the Guanxue ideal of service, and its use in schools.
- **Shaanbei**: the revolutionary ideal on its home ground, and what villagers say of it.
- **Jahriyya, Xidaotang and Ikhwan Hui**: three distinct ideals of a faithful community.
- **Amdo monastics and lay Tibetans** (Kumbum, Labrang, Rebkong): the ideal of the one who serves all beings, against everyday practice.
- **Third Front and Base 221 families**: inherited ideals of national service, and their children's verdicts.
- **Dunhuang Academy staff and Hexi oasis towns**: a heritage calling inside a hard place.
- **Young volunteers from eastern provinces** in Qinghai and Gansu, against local hosts' views of them.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur**: what the ideal herder or ideal person is; little known, to be asked.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official ideals and Han classics; mixed for Hui (partisan sources); thin for Mongol, Kazakh and Yugur ideals.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic and Persian for Sufi texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Jahriyya history, the Panchen Lama's petition, and Base 221's displaced herders are sensitive; official "spirit" campaigns should be read as messages, not as evidence of what people hold.
- **Held for review:** none.
