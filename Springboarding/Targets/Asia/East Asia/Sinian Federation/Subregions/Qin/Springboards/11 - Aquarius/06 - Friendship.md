# Qin: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, classical Chinese and northwest speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui vocabulary with Persian loans. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong men** and **Guanzhong women**: work and drinking friendships against neighbor and kin friendships; a question to test.
- **Shaanbei**: old bonds of song and labor; friendships made with sent-down youth.
- **Lanzhou and Hexi**: drinking-table customs as a place where friendship is made.
- **Hui** (Ningxia, Linxia, Xi'an): friendships inside the congregation and across the faith line.
- **Amdo farmers and herders**: companions in herding and pilgrimage; young men's groups.
- **Tu and Salar**: friendship among small peoples who share villages with Hui, Han and Tibetans.
- **Mongols and Kazakhs**: sworn and age-mate bonds among herders.
- **Third Front and danwei families**: friendships made by shared work and transfer.
- *Comparison only:* Dungan in Central Asia, for friendship terms kept from Shaanxi speech.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and Han literary sources; mixed for Hui; thin for Kazakh, Mongol, Yugur and Tu friendship.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; modern Chinese and local speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Persian loans in Hui speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendships across Han, Hui and Tibetan lines can touch on tension; some Gesar recitation is held by bards who decide what is shared.
- **Held for review:** none.
