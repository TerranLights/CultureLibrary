*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an** (students, artists, rock musicians) against **Guanzhong villages**: room for difference in a big city and in a close village.
- **Shaanbei**: the 1942 dissenters, and today's rural youth who leave.
- **Lanzhou and Tianshui**: student dissent in 1960 and the music scene since the 1990s.
- **Hexi** (Jiabiangou, Jiuquan): the place where nonconformists were sent.
- **Hui and Salar** orders once labeled "new" or heterodox, against those called orthodox.
- **Amdo monastics and lay intellectuals** (Rebkong, Labrang): iconoclasts and holy madmen.
- **Tu, Dongxiang, Yugur, Mongol, Kazakh** youth: pressure to fit Han norms and their own; little studied.
- **Gender and sexual minorities** in the cities: varied acceptance; little public data.
- *Comparison only:* Dungan in Central Asia, kept apart from both Qin and their new neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han literary dissent and 1957-1960; mixed for Hui and Tibetan religious dissent; thin for gender nonconformity and for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic and Persian for order texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor camps, 1960 dissent, religious "heterodoxy", Tibetan dissent and gender minorities; some films circulate unofficially; protect informants.
