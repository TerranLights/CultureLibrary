## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhongnan hermits** and **Guanzhong villagers**: reclusion as ideal against the cost of a family member withdrawing.
- **Daoist** and **Buddhist** clergy in Shaanxi and Gansu (Louguan, Chongyang Palace, Kongtong Shan): different reasons for distance.
- **Amdo monastics and lay Tibetans**: renunciation as a calling, and its place in lay life.
- **Hui Sufi orders**, against **Ikhwan Hui**, who may view seclusion practices differently.
- **Left-behind elders** in Longzhong and Shaanbei: isolation as hardship, not choice.
- **Mongol and Kazakh herders**: long solitary herding; little written.
- **Young urban workers** in Xi'an and Lanzhou: "lying flat" and its critics.
