# Qin: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northwest speech; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loans). **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Longzhong and Xihaigu farmers**: hope pinned on exams, migration and relocation.
- **Guanzhong and Xi'an**: city futures, housing and jobs; a different horizon of hope.
- **Shaanbei**: new energy wealth for some, migration for others.
- **Hexi oasis villages**: folk scroll readings and water scarcity.
- **Hui** (Ningxia, Linxia) and **Salar**, **Dongxiang**, **Bao'an**: hope for this life and the hereafter as their preachers and families speak of it.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (Rebkong, Golog, Gannan): hopes for a new generation, for language and for a good year.
- **Kham Tibetans of Yushu**: rebuilding after 2010.
- **Mongols, Kazakhs, Yugur**: herders' hopes under grazing limits; little written.
- *Comparison only:* the Dungan of Central Asia, for hopes kept from a lost homeland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for rural Gansu schooling (GSCF) and Han fiction; mixed for Hui and Amdo Tibetan voices; thin for Kazakh, Mongol and Yugur.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Amdo Tibetan (modern literary); Arabic and Persian loans in Hui speech; Mongolian; Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hopes for Tibetan language and future, and Dhondup Gyal's death, are sensitive; Hui religious preaching online is often removed; poverty programs carry official framing.
