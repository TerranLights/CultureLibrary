# Qin: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and regional varieties (Central Plains and Lanyin Mandarin, Jin in Shaanbei, Southwestern Mandarin in Shaannan); Amdo and Kham Tibetan; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loans, Xiaoerjing); Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an, Yugur, Oirat Mongolian, Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (folkway: magic ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han** (Xi'an, Baoji, Weinan): temple and opera core.
- **Shaanbei** (Yulin, Yan'an): Jin-speaking, its own dragon-king cults.
- **Shaannan** (Hanzhong, Ankang, Shangluo): ritual masters closer to Sichuan's.
- **Longdong and Longzhong** (Qingyang, Pingliang, Tianshui, Dingxi): dry-farming loess villages.
- **Hexi Corridor** (Wuwei to Dunhuang): oasis towns, the precious-scroll recitations.
- **Ningxia**: Yellow River plain against the poorer Xihaigu south.
- **Hui by order** (Gedimu, Yihewani, Xidaotang, Sufi menhuan): saints' tombs accepted or rejected.
- **Amdo Tibetans**, farming and pastoral (Golok, Gannan); **Kham Tibetans** of Yushu.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an**: Hehuang peoples with mixed ritual worlds.
- **Yugur** (Sunan), **Qinghai Mongols** (Haixi, Henan), **Aksay Kazakhs**.
- **Guanzhong Catholic villages**; **city dwellers and migrants** in Lanzhou, Xining, Yinchuan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han Shaanxi and Dunhuang; mixed for Hui; thin for Tibetan areas and small peoples. Qin's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the four provinces of the working list.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with classical), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian for Hui terms; Tu, Salar, Mongolian help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion is state-regulated; Tibetan and Hui religious life, labor camps and famine are political topics; Sufi and esoteric Tibetan teachings are for initiates; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
