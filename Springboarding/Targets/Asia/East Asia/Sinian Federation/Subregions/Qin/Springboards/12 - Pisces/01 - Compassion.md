## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Buddhist and folk merit-making; community-compact heritage.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong villages:** poorer loess areas, mostly recipients of aid; giving within kin.
- **Shaannan:** wetter, more Sichuan-like village customs of helping.
- **Hexi Corridor towns:** precious-scroll tales of rewarded kindness; migrant labor.
- **Hui:** Gedimu, Yihewani and Sufi mosque communities, Xidaotang's communal model.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetans:** monastery-centered giving; herders' care for animals.
- **Tu, Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small Hehuang peoples; almsgiving as Muslim or Buddhist, by people.
- **Qinghai Mongols, Aksay Kazakhs, Yugur:** steppe customs toward the traveler and the destitute.
- **Xihaigu and other aid-receiving counties** against provincial capitals: who is pitied, who pities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Tang charity and Sun Simiao; mixed on modern giving by province; thin for small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with classical), Amdo Tibetan, Arabic and Persian loans in Hui speech; Mongolian and Kazakh.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious charity is regulated, and mosque or monastery relief may be under-reported; poverty-relief media is official messaging; avoid portraying aid-receiving peoples as one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
