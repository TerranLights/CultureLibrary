*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, with regional speech of Guanzhong, Shaanbei, Shaannan, Lanzhou, Hexi and Hehuang; Hui religious vocabulary; Amdo Tibetan; Kazakh; Mongolian; Tu and Salar for the Hehuang song tradition. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** the Guanxue scholarly line next to plain village speech; whether directness and empathy are paired or opposed.
- **Shaanbei:** song traditions of separation from migration north.
- **Shaannan:** wake singing and a southern register of feeling.
- **Hexi and Longdong:** blind singers and shadow-puppet audiences.
- **Hehuang song peoples** (Hui, Dongxiang, Salar, Tu, Bao'an, Han): shared hua'er, different religions and homes.
- **Amdo Tibetan monastics and lay herders:** trained practice against everyday attunement.
- **Kazakhs of Aksay, Qinghai Mongols, Yugur:** condolence customs and pastoral hospitality.
- **Doctors, nurses and teachers** in the provincial capitals: empathy as professional skill.
- **Migrant workers and left-behind families:** whose feelings travel by phone.
- **Autistic people and their families:** their own accounts against outsiders' labels; search "孤独症 家长 西安".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on folk song and the Guanzhong school; thin on measured empathy by province; most psychology samples are students.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (with classical), Amdo Tibetan, Kazakh; dialect skill for songs and humor.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cross-ethnic empathy is a political theme in official "ethnic unity" (民族团结) campaigns, so separate campaign texts from lived accounts; monastic teachings may be restricted; doctor-patient violence is a charged topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
