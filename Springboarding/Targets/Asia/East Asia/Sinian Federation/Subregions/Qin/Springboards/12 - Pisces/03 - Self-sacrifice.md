*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Guanzhong, Shaanbei and Gansu speech; Hui religious vocabulary; Amdo and Kham Tibetan; Salar, Dongxiang and Bao'an through Chinese sources. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** gazetteer filial and chastity traditions, relic devotion.
- **Shaanbei:** coal work and the Border Region's revolutionary memory.
- **Shaannan and Longdong:** poorer hill farms; out-migration for children.
- **Hexi Corridor:** precious-scroll recitation; Western Route Army memory.
- **Hui by order:** Jahriyya martyr veneration against Yihewani reformers who reject shrine cults.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** the Feast of Sacrifice in their own villages.
- **Amdo farmers and herders; Kham Tibetans of Yushu:** pilgrimage and funerary giving.
- **Tu and Mongols:** offerings to local deities and how they are framed.
- **Women and men:** who is praised for self-denial, in the arches and now.
- **Soldiers' and miners' families.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Tang devotion and gazetteers; mixed on Hui martyr memory; thin on modern sacrifice data below the province.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic loans in Hui speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Tibetan self-immolations of 2011-2012 in Rebgong, Labrang and Machu are highly sensitive; Hui martyr history touches Qing-era violence; revolutionary martyrdom is official doctrine; Sufi shrine teachings are for members.
- **Held for review:** none.
