# Qin: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Shaanbei speech; Hui Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Qinghai Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** walled courtyards and inside-the-passes identity.
- **Shaanbei:** cave dwellings, Great Wall frontier memory, links to Inner Mongolia.
- **Shaannan:** open hill villages, different house forms.
- **Hexi Corridor:** pass towns and oases between farm and steppe.
- **Hui:** neighborhoods around mosques; food and marriage lines with Han.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** Muslim peoples with their own lines toward Hui and Han.
- **Amdo Tibetans:** pastoral fences, monastery precincts, gender in sacred space.
- **Tu:** villages mixing Buddhist, folk and Han ways at their edges.
- **Qinghai Mongols, Aksay Kazakhs, Yugur:** dwelling order and pasture markers.
- **Urban newcomers** in Xi'an, Lanzhou, Yinchuan and Xining: hukou and privacy in apartments.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on frontier history; mixed on purity lines; thin on personal space and privacy measured by province.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian and Kazakh for dwelling and pasture terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inter-ethnic boundaries and halal rules are politically charged (recent campaigns on halal labels and mosque architecture); grazing fences tie to state policy; retreat practices are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
