## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xi'an's urban middle class:** nightlife, road trips, the "lying flat" talk.
- **Shaanbei coal towns** (Shenmu, Fugu): boom and bust; what leisure followed, as residents tell it.
- **Guanzhong and Longdong farmers:** slack-season entertainment and card play.
- **Hexi and Lanzhou:** drinking-game culture and the Yellow River city.
- **Hui:** leisure under a religious ban on alcohol (tea, food streets), and how strictly it is kept by place and order.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** Muslim norms in small communities.
- **Tu:** barley-liquor hospitality.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetans:** barley beer, and the monastery-led pledges.
- **Qinghai Mongols and Aksay Kazakhs:** pastoral drinks and summer festivals.
- **Recluses and monastics:** withdrawal honored as practice.
- **Migrant workers and the young:** screens and gaming as escape.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on hermits and Tang leisure; mixed on modern drinking; thin and politicized on drugs.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Tu and Mongolian for drink customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and trafficking are framed by ethnicity in some media (avoid any people-wide labels); compulsory rehab is a closed system; the Border Region opium trade is a sensitive history.
- **Held for review:** none.

5. Read the national drug report and its provincial counterparts for the four provinces.
