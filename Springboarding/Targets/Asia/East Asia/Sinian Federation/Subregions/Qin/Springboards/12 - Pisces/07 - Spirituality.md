## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Pure Land and esoteric temples, goddess shrines, Catholic villages.
- **Shaanbei:** mountain temple fairs and village deities.
- **Shaannan:** Daoist and ritual-master traditions shared with Sichuan.
- **Longdong and Tianshui:** Kongtong and Fuxi sites.
- **Hexi:** cave temples and lay Buddhists.
- **Hui:** scripture-hall lineages, Sufi orders, reformist mosques.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** Muslim practice in their own languages.
- **Amdo Tibetans** (Golok, Rebgong, Gannan) and **Kham Tibetans** (Yushu): mountain and lake deities.
- **Tu, Yugur, Qinghai Mongols, Aksay Kazakhs:** mixed Buddhist, sky and ancestor layers.
- **Urban seekers** in Xi'an and Lanzhou: meditation camps, "body-mind-spirit" circles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on sacred mountains and temples; mixed on lay practice; thin on non-religious seekers.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Arabic and Persian loans; Mongolian, Kazakh, Yugur; Japanese for pilgrim accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious groups and banned movements are high risk to discuss; Tibetan and Hui practice under state campaigns; closed initiations stay closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
