## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** Zhou-homeland heritage, the antique trade, medical colleges.
- **Shaanbei:** blind fortune-tellers and village divination.
- **Shaannan:** mountain farmers; ritual masters' readings.
- **Longdong and Tianshui:** wheat farmers; trigram-origin lore.
- **Hexi:** oasis farmers reading water and wind.
- **Hui:** Sufi guidance against reformist rejection of it.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** village elders' knowledge.
- **Amdo herders and Kham Tibetans:** lama divination; reading the land.
- **Tu and Yugur:** mixed Buddhist and older readings of signs.
- **Aksay Kazakhs and Qinghai Mongols:** pastoral divination and weather sense.
- **Doctors, nurses, traders and investors** in the provincial capitals: gut against data at work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ancient divination and medicine; mixed on folk practice; thin on measured intuition by province.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese (oracle and medical texts), Amdo Tibetan, Kazakh; dialect skill for proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is officially discouraged as "feudal superstition", so practitioners may not speak freely; Sufi guidance is internal to orders; the antique trade touches tomb looting.
- **Held for review:** none.
