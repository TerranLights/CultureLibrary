# Qin: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Guanzhong and Shaanbei speech; Hui Chinese; Amdo and Kham Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** what people count as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how they see those who commit it. Law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn, not here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** knife-men lore, opera justice, tomb-looting debates.
- **Shaanbei:** bandit and revolutionary labels; coal-boom wrongdoing as residents tell it.
- **Shaannan:** Qinba mountain bandit lore on a three-province border.
- **Longdong and Hexi:** frontier robbers in legend; Baiyin's industrial towns.
- **Hui:** congregational shaming and forgiveness, varying by order and place.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** village elders' handling of wrongs.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetans:** wrongs against land, animals and kin; monastic mediators.
- **Tu, Yugur, Qinghai Mongols, Aksay Kazakhs:** herders' views of theft of livestock.
- **Former prisoners and their families:** stigma from inside.
- **Urban youth** in Xi'an and Lanzhou: thugs, scams and online shaming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on historical outlaws and opera; mixed on modern attitudes; thin on victimization by province.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for the Han histories, modern Chinese and dialect for lore, Amdo Tibetan, and the Arabic-derived moral words of Hui speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political labeling as crime (labor camps) is a sensitive history; drug crime is often tied to particular peoples in media, which must be recorded as a claim and never adopted; customary compensation for killing (赔命价) in Tibetan areas belongs to Capricorn's research, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
