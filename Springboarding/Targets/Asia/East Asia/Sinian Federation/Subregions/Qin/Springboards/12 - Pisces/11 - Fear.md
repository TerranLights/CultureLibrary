# Qin: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Qin (秦), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shaanxi, Gansu, Ningxia, Qinghai). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Guanzhong, Shaanbei and Hexi speech; Hui Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian; Kazakh. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways people name, show, manage and use it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanzhong Han:** earthquake and famine memory; opera's ghosts.
- **Shaanbei and Longdong loess villages:** wolves, cave collapse, drought.
- **Shaannan and the Qinling:** wild animals, floods, mountain spirits.
- **Hexi Corridor:** sandstorms and the desert's advance.
- **Ningxia's south (Xihaigu):** the 1920 quake and poverty.
- **Hui:** fear of God and of hellfire in sermons; inherited fear from the 1860s-70s war.
- **Salar, Dongxiang, Bao'an:** small Muslim peoples near the 2023 quake zone.
- **Amdo and Kham Tibetans:** fierce spirits, the Yushu quake, snow disasters.
- **Tu and Yugur:** mixed spirit worlds.
- **Qinghai Mongols and Aksay Kazakhs:** the evil eye, winter storms, wolves.
- **Xi'an's city dwellers:** lockdown memory and urban anxieties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on earthquakes and opera; mixed on folk fright and bogeymen; thin and politicized on famine and lockdown.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Standard Chinese; Amdo Tibetan; Mongolian and Kazakh; dialect skill for children's frighteners.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Great Famine, the 1860s-70s war, the nuclear base and the 2021 lockdown are politically sensitive; Tibetan fierce-deity practice may be restricted; protect survivors who speak.
- **Held for review:** none.
