*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Shanghainese and the other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin (Nanjing, Hefei); She speech. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older Shanghai-born residents and young migrants in Shanghai offices**: the dialect and the workplace may reward different kinds of voice.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou traders against Suzhou and Hangzhou civil-service families**: bargaining talk against restraint expected of officials.
- **Nanjing (Jianghuai Mandarin) against the Wu south of Jiangsu**: two speech norms within one provincial unit.
- **Northern Anhui and northern Jiangsu**: Mandarin speakers often met as migrants in the south; does their voice get heard or labeled?
- **Women and men in each of these**: who may raise their voice at table, at market, at meetings.
- **She communities**: song duels as a sanctioned frame for claims.
- **Hui Muslim neighborhoods in Nanjing and Shanghai**: claims made through mosque and community.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Shanghainese in Hong Kong; Wenzhou traders in Italy and France.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai; mixed for Zhejiang and Jiangsu; thin for the She and northern Anhui.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties for media and talk; She for songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 维权 has a political sense beyond consumer complaints; petitions to authorities and rights-defense litigation are sensitive, and anything legal goes to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
