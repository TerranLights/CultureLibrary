## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Only children of Shanghai and southern Jiangsu against children of large rural families in northern Anhui**: family size changes who decides.
- **Women from Wuwei, Fuyang and other Anhui sending counties against urban women of Hangzhou and Nanjing**: migration as a route to choice against choice at home.
- **Huizhou lineages, past and present**: lineage control over marriage and property.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu family firms**: autonomy of sons and daughters inside the family business.
- **She of Jingning and Lishui**: collective autonomy as a recognized minority.
- **Hui Muslim families**: marriage choice within a faith community.
- **Elderly people in Shanghai and in emptied rural counties**: who decides on care and housing.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Wenzhou families in Europe deciding across borders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai and Jiangnan history; mixed for medical and household data; thin for She decision-making.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese for oral sources; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective autonomy (autonomous counties, self-government) is politically sensitive; reproductive autonomy and birth-policy history need care; legal rules on marriage, hukou and consent go to Libra or Capricorn.
