## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou (household firms) against southern Jiangsu (collective township firms)**: two models of starting enterprise.
- **Yiwu, Yongkang and Jinhua inland traders**: hill-county peddling traditions.
- **Ningbo and Zhoushan seafaring families**: shipping and overseas ventures.
- **Huizhou (past merchant homeland) against today's southern Anhui**.
- **Northern Anhui (Fengyang, Fuyang) and northern Jiangsu (Suqian, Lianyungang)**: rural initiative under scarcity, and migration as the first step.
- **Shanghai and Hangzhou tech founders against state-firm employees**.
- **Women founders (Taobao shop owners, small-factory bosses)**: a gendered split to check.
- **Hui Muslim food businesses in Nanjing and Hefei; She tea and farm cooperatives in Lishui**.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Wenzhou and Qingtian traders in Europe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (economic statistics, business histories, ethnography), weighted to Zhejiang and southern Jiangsu; thin for She and Hui enterprise.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Wenzhou Wu for oral histories (hard even for other Wu speakers).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founding myths are often promotional; Xiaogang's story is official memory; private-lending and illegal fund-raising cases overlap with law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
