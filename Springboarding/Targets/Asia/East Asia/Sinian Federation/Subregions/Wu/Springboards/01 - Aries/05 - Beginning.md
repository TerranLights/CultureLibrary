*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Suzhou and Shanghai Wu; Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin; She speech. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Hangzhou (old-calendar cities) against Shanghai (a city whose story starts with a port)**: different "first chapters."
- **Northern Anhui and northern Jiangsu**: famine migration as forced new beginning, and its songs.
- **Returned sent-down youth and their children in Shanghai**: a generation's restart.
- **Coastal Ningbo and Zhoushan fishing communities**: the opening of the fishing season (开渔节, *check*).
- **She of Lishui and Jingning**: seasonal openings on their own festival calendar.
- **Hui Muslims of Nanjing and Anhui**: a year opened and closed by Ramadan and Eid, beside the lunar calendar.
- **Buddhist and Daoist communities (Putuoshan, Jiuhuashan, Maoshan)**: ordination and lay vows as new beginnings.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: 1949 Shanghai émigrés who restarted in Hong Kong and Taiwan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for calendar customs and archaeology; mixed for personal restarts (divorce, release from prison, recovery); thin for She seasonal rites.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for *Guoyu* and Qing customs books; Standard Chinese; Wu for oral accounts; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the sent-down years and the 1949 departures touch political memory; origin claims in museums are part of national narrative and should be read as presentation.
- **Held for review:** none.
