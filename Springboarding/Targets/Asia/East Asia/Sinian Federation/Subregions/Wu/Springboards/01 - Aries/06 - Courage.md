## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Wuxi literati traditions against Taizhou and Yiwu soldiering traditions**: moral against physical courage.
- **Shaoxing (Qiu Jin, Xu Xilin) and Anqing, where Xu Xilin's 1907 rising took place**: revolutionary courage across two provincial units.
- **Shanghai's 1937 memory against Nanjing's war memory**: defense against massacre in public remembrance.
- **Coastal fishing communities (Zhoushan, Shitang, Lianyungang)**: occupational danger.
- **Northern Anhui and Huai River villages**: flood years and endurance.
- **Women in each tradition**: whose endurance is counted as courage.
- **She communities**: the ancestor epic as their own frame.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Ningbo seafarers abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memorials and pre-modern heroes; mixed for present-day civilian bravery; thin for She and fishing-community oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Zhoushan and Shaoxing Wu; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory (Nanjing 1937, Shanghai 1932 and 1937) is politically managed and emotionally heavy; the 1645 resistances concern Han-Manchu conflict; recent civic courage (whistleblowing, the 2022 Shanghai lockdown) is sensitive and may be censored.
- **Held for review:** none.
