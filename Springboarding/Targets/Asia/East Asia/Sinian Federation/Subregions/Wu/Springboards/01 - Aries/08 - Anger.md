## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai lane (弄堂) households against high-rise estates**: crowding and quarreling.
- **Suzhou and Hangzhou against Huai'an, Xuzhou and Huaibei**: Wu speech and Mandarin north; check whether display differs or only the stereotype does.
- **Shaoxing village theater audiences**: anger carried by ritual figures.
- **Women and men within each region**: who may show anger, and in front of whom.
- **Migrant workers in Shanghai and Suzhou factories**: grievance at work and its outlets.
- **Hui communities of Nanjing and Anhui**: anger in relation to faith and to slights.
- **She communities**: song and ritual as possible outlets.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Wenzhou traders in Europe and conflicts with host societies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and literature; mixed for clinical and survey data; thin for She and for village-level display.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese, Shaoxing and Suzhou Wu for opera and oral sources; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protests, rebellions and present-day collective anger are politically sensitive; domestic violence data need care; mediation shades into law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
