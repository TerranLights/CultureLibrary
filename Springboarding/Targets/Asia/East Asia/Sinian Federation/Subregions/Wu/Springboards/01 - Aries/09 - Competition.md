## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiangsu and Anhui against Shanghai and Zhejiang**: exam pressure by place; compare cohort sizes and cut-offs rather than reputations.
- **Southern Jiangsu, central Jiangsu and northern Jiangsu cities**: the 苏超 rivalries.
- **Jinhua and Yiwu villages**: lineage rivalry in folk contests.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou small firms**: price competition among neighbors in one trade.
- **Rural northern Anhui pupils against Hefei's key schools**: unequal starting lines.
- **Girls and boys**: who is pushed in which contest.
- **She and Hui communities**: their own sports and contests, and their place in provincial minority games.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Wenzhou wholesale traders competing in European markets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams (pre-modern and modern) and sport; mixed for folk contests; thin for She traditional sport.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for rosters and treatises; Standard Chinese; Wu for cricket-market and village oral sources; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling on cricket and other contests is illegal; doping cases in Zhejiang swimming are sensitive; the 2021 tutoring rules touch policy (legal side to Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
