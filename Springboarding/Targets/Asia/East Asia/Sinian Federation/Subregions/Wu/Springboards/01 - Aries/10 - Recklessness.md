## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hangzhou and Haining tide-country against inland Anhui**: water as the stage of daring.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou traders against Shanghai salaried workers**: financial risk as livelihood against risk as gamble.
- **Young male e-bike couriers in Shanghai and Hangzhou**: risk driven by delivery times rather than thrill.
- **Northern Jiangsu (Suqian, Xuzhou) and northern Anhui**: hero lore of daring commanders.
- **Hikers in Huangshan, Tianmushan and the Dabie Mountains**: urban backpackers against local guides.
- **Hui Muslim communities**: abstention from alcohol as a contrast in drinking risk.
- **She hill villages**: risk in forest and mountain work.
- **Diaspora (comparison only)**: Wenzhou investors abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for financial episodes and press stories; mixed for health and injury data by province; thin for She and village practice.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Shanghainese and Wenzhou Wu for oral and online sources; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** P2P losses led to protests and are partly censored; gambling is illegal; drunk-driving and lending cases are also legal matters (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
