# Wu: Domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (lands: Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Written Chinese (Mandarin); Classical Chinese; Wu varieties (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Hangzhou, Ningbo, Wenzhou); Hui (Huizhou) and Jianghuai Mandarin; She. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how is it held? Folkways: food, dress and wealth ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taihu Wu heartland** (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou, Huzhou, Jiaxing): the silk and rice economy that produced the old object manuals.
- **Shanghai city against its suburbs and Chongming island:** treaty-port consumer culture beside farm and island households.
- **Ningbo-Shaoxing-Zhoushan:** merchant and banking families abroad, dowry display, fishing islands.
- **Southern Zhejiang** (Wenzhou, Taizhou; Southern Min speakers in Cangnan): family firms and private credit.
- **Interior Zhejiang** (Jinhua, Quzhou, Lishui): hill farming, ham and celadon towns.
- **Huizhou** (Hui-dialect southern Anhui): merchant lineages with deep written records.
- **Jianghuai Mandarin belt** (Nanjing, Yangzhou, Huai'an, Hefei) and **Zhongyuan Mandarin north** (Xuzhou, Huaibei, Fuyang): different household economies from the delta's.
- **Anqing** (Gan-speaking southwestern Anhui), **the She**, **Hui Muslims** (Nanjing, Hefei), **Subei and Anhui migrants in Shanghai.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai and the Jiangnan towns (centuries of print, contracts, surveys); mixed for interior Zhejiang; thin for northern Anhui, the She and the fishing islands. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled (the four listed provinces are a working list); this sheet is written for them as the core.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese for the Ming-Qing sources; Wu, Hui and Jianghuai dialect dictionaries for spoken terms; She for She sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform and collectivization; the Great Leap famine in Anhui; demolition and relocation disputes; private genealogies and family contracts held by living families.
- **Held for review:** none.
