# Wu: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese (contracts, registers); Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; Hui; She. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on. The letter of property law belongs to Libra; this sheet points only to customs, tenure and attitudes.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jiangsu collective villages** (Wuxi, Suzhou, Jiangyin): the village as owner of firms and land.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou family firms:** private ownership early, and private credit beside it.
- **Huizhou:** lineage halls as landholders; the history of bonded tenant-servants 佃仆.
- **Shanghai public-housing tenants against commodity-flat owners;** relocated households.
- **Northern and central Anhui grain farmers:** contracted plots now often leased out to large farms 土地流转.
- **Lake and sea fishers** (Taihu, Hongze Lake, Zhoushan): waters closed by fishing bans; who owned the catch.
- **She villages in Lishui:** collective forest land and its later division into household shares *check*.
- **Married-out daughters 外嫁女:** disputes over village shares.
- **Hui Muslim congregations:** mosque property 寺产 held in common.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (centuries of contracts and registers; heavy modern scholarship); thinner for She and fishing communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese and contract script; Wu and Hui dialect terms for tenure.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform and the classing of landlords; demolition and relocation; collectivization; the bonded tenant-servants. Property statutes and court practice go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
