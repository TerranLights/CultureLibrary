## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai families of "upper corner" and "lower corner" districts:** address as a mark of standing.
- **Subei-origin Shanghainese:** an inherited low ranking, and what descendants say of it.
- **Anhui and northern Jiangsu migrants:** worth judged by "quality" and residence points.
- **Old exam-heritage families of Suzhou, Changzhou and Shaoxing:** worth from learning and office.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou self-made owners:** worth from success in trade with little schooling.
- **Huizhou women, past and present:** honor granted by arches; later lives.
- **Laid-off mill workers and the rural elderly left behind 留守老人:** worth after losing a role.
- **The She:** a people's standing after centuries of outside naming.
- **Hui Muslims:** standing as a minority in Nanjing and Hefei.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich; heavy scholarship on face and on exams, thinner on worth in the She and in rural Anhui.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese slang and its dictionaries; Jianghuai dialect for Subei voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional prejudice (Subei, Anhui, "country people"); suicide among students; layoffs; chastity honors; avoid repeating slurs beyond naming them as objects of study.
- **Held for review:** none.
