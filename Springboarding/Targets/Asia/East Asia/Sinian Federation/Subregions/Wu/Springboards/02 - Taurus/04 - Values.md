# Wu: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou Christians and Wenzhou temple patrons:** two religious frames for the same trading economy.
- **Huizhou lineages:** a written merchant ethic inside the Confucian order.
- **Shanghai generations:** treaty-port elders, planned-economy parents, internet-era children.
- **Southern Jiangsu collective villages:** the group before the household, as officials state it; to be tested.
- **Ningbo-Shaoxing families:** learning and trade ranked together.
- **Northern Anhui and Xuzhou:** family customs of the Huai plain, to be studied apart from the delta's.
- **Hui Muslims of Nanjing and Hefei;** **Buddhist lay circles** of Putuoshan and Jiuhuashan.
- **The She:** what She songs and village rules name as right conduct.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the classical and lineage sides; survey data mostly national, with thin province samples; little written for the She.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Wenzhou and Shanghainese for local sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official values campaigns and dissent from them; church-state conflict in Zhejiang; surveys on politics. The letter of the law goes to Libra; its spirit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
