## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai urban retirees against suburban town-insurance holders:** different nets inside one city.
- **Land-lost farmers** around Shanghai, Hangzhou, Suzhou and Hefei: land traded for pensions and flats.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou households:** savings placed in private credit circles.
- **Huai River flood-plain villages** (Fuyang, Lu'an, Huai'an): flood as a known yearly risk.
- **Island and coastal fishers** (Zhoushan, Wenling, Cangnan): sea, storm and fishing-ban risk.
- **Migrants without local hukou:** thinner coverage, money sent home as insurance.
- **Left-behind elderly in rural Anhui:** sons and daughters far away.
- **She villages moved downhill** under Zhejiang's resettlement programs 下山移民 *check*.
- **Hui Muslim congregations:** mosque relief for poor members.
