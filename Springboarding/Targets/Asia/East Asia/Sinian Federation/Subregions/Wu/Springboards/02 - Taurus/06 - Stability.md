## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Shanghai lane families against relocated households** in outer districts.
- **Huizhou and Quzhou single-lineage villages:** surname, hall and land unchanged for centuries.
- **Water-town residents** (Zhouzhuang, Wuzhen, Tongli): continuity performed for tourists.
- **Anhui and northern Jiangsu out-migrants:** high mobility, return at the New Year.
- **New industrial towns** (Kunshan, Suzhou Industrial Park, Hefei's new districts): mostly newcomers.
- **Grand Canal and Taihu boat families** settled on shore.
- **She lineages and Hui Muslim congregations:** minority continuity in place.
- **Civil servants and state-firm staff against private-sector and gig workers.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage, lineages and Shanghai housing; census mobility data good; thin for boat families and the She.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Suzhou dialect for memory and nostalgia sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability maintenance" 维稳 as political control is out of scope here (order belongs to Capricorn); demolition disputes; lockdown disruptions.
- **Held for review:** none.
