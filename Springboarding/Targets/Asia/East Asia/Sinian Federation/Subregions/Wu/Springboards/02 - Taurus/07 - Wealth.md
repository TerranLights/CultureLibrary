## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai old money against new money:** pre-1949 families against fortunes made in shares and flats.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou private fortunes:** wealth from small trades grown large.
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing merchant lineages:** banking and shipping wealth over generations.
- **Yangzhou:** the memory of salt wealth in a city now less rich.
- **Southern against northern Jiangsu:** the sharpest wealth gap inside one province.
- **Northern Anhui and the Dabie mountains:** recent exit from official poverty.
- **Hangzhou tech staff:** paper wealth from shares.
- **Zhoushan boat owners and crews:** wealth from the sea.
- **The She** (tea and hill farming) and **Hui Muslims** (trade and food businesses).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchants and the modern rich; mixed for poverty in Anhui; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Wenzhou and Shanghainese slang for money and the rich.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hidden fortunes and corruption; the expropriation of capitalists after 1949; criminal wealth; rich lists are estimates.
- **Held for review:** none.
