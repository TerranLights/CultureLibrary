# Wu: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin; Hui; She. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai one-child families:** mothers and only sons; in-laws and new couples.
- **Patrilocal rural households in northern Anhui and Jiangsu:** a bride moving into her husband's family.
- **Craft lineages** (Jingxian paper, Shanlian brushes, Longjing tea villages): knowledge kept in the family.
- **Lake and sea fishers:** guarded grounds and secret spots.
- **Migrant couples living apart:** jealousy at a distance.
- **Wenzhou family firms:** control kept by founders and sons.
- **Hui Muslims and the She:** rules on marrying out, as each community explains them.
- **Buddhist lay circles:** teachings against clinging.
