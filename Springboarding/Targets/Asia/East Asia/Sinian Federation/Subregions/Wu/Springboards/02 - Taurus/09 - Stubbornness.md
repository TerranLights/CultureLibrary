## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaoxing and Taizhou:** self-told lore of hardness and unbending scholars, to be tested against data.
- **Jiangyin and Jiading:** towns whose identity rests on a remembered refusal.
- **Villagers at the urban edge** (Wenling, Hangzhou, Hefei suburbs): holdouts against demolition.
- **Shanghai workers by native place:** different strike habits among trades and origins.
- **Northern against southern Jiangsu and Anhui:** wheat and rice areas, for conformity tests.
- **Huizhou emigrant families:** persistence as a family story.
- **Elderly city dwellers:** refusal of digital routines.
- **Wenzhou Protestants:** firmness of worship under pressure.
- **Hui Muslims:** keeping food and prayer rules in Han workplaces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much on heroic refusal in history, less on everyday stubbornness; regional "hardness" claims are lore to test, not findings.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shaoxing, Taizhou and Shanghainese dialect for sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resistance to the state (1645, demolitions, church protests); war memory and apology; holdout cases can touch court rulings (law to Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
