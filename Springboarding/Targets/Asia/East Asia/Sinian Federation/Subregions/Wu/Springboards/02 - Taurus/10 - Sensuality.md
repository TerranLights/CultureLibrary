**Target:** Wu (吴), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese, Suzhou and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin (Yangzhou, Nanjing); Hui; She. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Wuxi:** sweet cooking, garden culture and soft Wu singing.
- **Shanghai:** local cooking beside cafés, dance halls and imported pleasures.
- **Yangzhou and Huai'an:** Huaiyang cuisine, morning tea, baths.
- **Hangzhou, Shaoxing and Ningbo:** rice wine, fermented and sea foods.
- **Huizhou:** mountain foods, fermentation, tea.
- **Northern Anhui and Xuzhou:** wheat and mutton, stronger seasoning.
- **The She:** festival foods and song.
- **Hui Muslims of Nanjing and Hefei:** halal beef and duck dishes.
- **Buddhist lay vegetarians** (Hangzhou, Putuoshan, Jiuhuashan): pleasures given up.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; a large body of classical and modern food and leisure writing; thin for the She and island communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Written Chinese; Classical Chinese; Suzhou and Shanghainese for song and slang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** courtesan history (the Qinhuai pleasure quarter); dance-hall bans; sexuality belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
