# Wu: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin; Huizhou Chinese; She; Hui mosque register. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media; and what is held to be good communication and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-line Shanghainese and "new Shanghainese" (新上海人):** dialect choice in shops, offices and families marks belonging; compare who switches and when.
- **Subei-origin Shanghai families:** a documented accent hierarchy inside the city (see Emily Honig's work *check*), so being heard as "from the north" carries social weight.
- **Wenzhou and other southern Wu speakers:** their speech is hard for northern Wu listeners, so cross-dialect talk inside the member state is itself a case.
- **Nanjing and Hefei Mandarin speakers:** a Mandarin base changes the Putonghua-versus-dialect contest.
- **Huizhou villages:** isolated valleys and a strong written-letter tradition from migrant merchants.
- **Hui Muslims:** a religious register layered on local speech.
- **She of Jingning and Lishui:** bilingual in She and local Chinese; sung exchange as talk.
- **Deaf communities in the four provinces:** a sign variety that does not follow the spoken dialect map.
- **Generations:** grandparents raised before Putonghua schooling against children raised in it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai and Suzhou (literature, dialect studies, comedy); mixed for Hangzhou and Ningbo; thin for She, Deaf and Hui communication.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; listening skill in Shanghainese and Suzhou Wu; classical Chinese for letter manuals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dialect-preservation campaigns can be politically touchy; online speech is filtered, so absence in posts is not absence in talk. Free-speech questions belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
