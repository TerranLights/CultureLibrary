# Wu: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for Song to Qing notebooks and travel diaries; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties for everyday words of nosiness and crowd-watching. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's commercial city:** curiosity sold as entertainment and novelty since the treaty-port era; compare with towns where it was not.
- **Lower Yangzi literati towns** (Suzhou, Shaoxing, Hangzhou): a written tradition of connoisseurship and strange tales; the literate minority's curiosity, not the whole town's.
- **Hefei's science district:** a research city built in the planned era; what draws local youth to science there.
- **Huangshan and the Huizhou hills:** places long visited by the curious; how residents see visitors.
- **Qiantang and coastal Zhejiang:** spectacle customs tied to tide and sea.
- **She villages of Jingning:** now marketed to tourists; curiosity directed *at* a people, and how they set limits on it.
- **Hui communities:** questions outsiders ask about faith and food, and the replies people give.
- **Older and younger residents:** what each may ask of the other (age, income, marriage plans) and where the line falls.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literati notebooks and Shanghai entertainment history; mixed for present-day science interest; thin for She and Hui views on asking.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for the notebook tradition; Shanghainese for street words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** curiosity about politics and state matters is limited; research on onlooker behavior can touch on public incidents that are censored. The She and other minorities should be heard on visitors' curiosity in their own words.
- **Held for review:** none.
