## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing people in Shanghai:** the classic native-place networks; compare their ties with those of native Shanghai residents.
- **Huizhou lineages:** kin-based trust used for trade over long distances.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou entrepreneurs:** credit and business by personal guarantee; a different weight on ties than in state-sector cities.
- **Subei migrants in Shanghai:** historically shut out of some networks, so they built their own.
- **Yiwu's foreign traders:** Arab, African and Central Asian residents with their own mosques and markets.
- **Hui communities:** mosque-based ties across towns, and ties to Hui elsewhere in the federation.
- **She lineages:** a small set of surnames and song gatherings linking scattered villages.
- **Elderly living alone** in old Shanghai lanes, and **young single professionals** in new towers: two kinds of isolation to set side by side.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Qingtian and Wenzhou people in Europe, and Shanghai families in Hong Kong, as a contrast with ties at home.

## 4. Flags
