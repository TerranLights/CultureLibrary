# Wu: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Shanghainese and Hangzhou Wu for broadcast and street usage; classical Chinese for deeds, stories and anecdotes; Hui mosque terms. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai lane neighborhoods:** a named folk role (the "old uncle") and a TV format grew from it; compare with new towers where neighbors may not know one another.
- **Huizhou and Tongcheng:** lineage halls and gentry anecdotes; peacemaking tied to status and restraint.
- **Zhuji and other Zhejiang counties:** the source of a state model; how villagers describe it in their own words.
- **Wenzhou entrepreneurs:** business quarrels kept inside chambers and kin networks.
- **Hui communities:** imams and elders as arbiters.
- **She villages:** elders and lineage heads; query 畲族 族长 调解 (thin).
- **Families where grandparents speak only Wu or Huizhou speech:** children in the middle.
- **Fishing ports** (Zhoushan, Shenjiamen): disputes over boats and catch; boat-owner associations as third parties *check*.
