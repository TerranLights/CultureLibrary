# Wu: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and the other Wu varieties; Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Hui Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lilong residents and their children now in high-rise compounds:** two very different home forms within one family's memory.
- **Owners and long-term renters** in Shanghai, Hangzhou, Nanjing and Suzhou: ownership is tied to schooling and marriage.
- **Newcomers to Shanghai** (新上海人) and migrant workers in dormitories or shared flats.
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu homes** whose working-age members live in the cities.
- **Wenzhou and Qingtian villages** with houses built from earnings abroad (diaspora itself as comparison only).
- **Huizhou village households** living in old merchant houses, some now heritage.
- **Water-town and boat-dwelling families** around Taihu.
- **She mountain villages** in Lishui and southern Wenzhou.
- **Hui Muslim neighborhoods**, e.g. Nanjing's old quarter around 七家湾 *check*.
- **Zhoushan islanders** and **Huai River floodplain villages**: homes shaped by sea and flood.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai (literature, film, scholarship, housing data); mixed for Jiangsu and Zhejiang towns; thin for She homes and boat families.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese; Wenzhou Wu; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation disputes, petitions and evictions; residents may be cautious. Property law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
