## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huizhou** and **Ningbo-Shaoxing:** strong written lineages; compare register-making today.
- **Shanghai urban families:** the only-child generation and their parents' role in marriage.
- **Southern Jiangsu villages** (Suzhou, Wuxi): uxorilocal marriage worth tracing locally.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** family firms and kin credit; emigrant kin abroad as comparison only.
- **Northern Jiangsu and Huaibei:** Mandarin kin terms and village kin networks, to set beside the Wu south.
- **She:** lineage through the ancestral scroll and the Panhu origin.
- **Hui Muslims:** marriage within the community, mosque-centered families.
- **Catholic families** of Shanghai and the Jiangnan countryside: godparents, church marriage.
- **Same-sex couples and chosen families** in Shanghai and Hangzhou (sensitive).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (genealogies, family codes, surveys, drama); thinner for She and Hui family ways in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese, Ningbo and Wenzhou Wu; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex families; the birth-limit years; some lineages restrict their registers to members. Marriage, adoption and inheritance law go to Libra; authority within the family to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
