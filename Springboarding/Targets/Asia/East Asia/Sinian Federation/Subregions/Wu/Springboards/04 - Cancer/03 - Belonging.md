## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Shanghainese and new Shanghainese:** formal registration against felt acceptance.
- **Subei-origin Shanghai families:** a long history under an outsider label.
- **Wu against Yue in Zhejiang** (Shaoxing and Ningbo as old Yue lands): two regional pasts in one member state.
- **Jianghuai and Huaibei people in Wu cities:** Mandarin speakers in Wu-speaking places.
- **Huizhou people:** a strong home-region identity carried by merchants.
- **Wenzhou people:** a separate Wu variety and home-town networks to check; emigrant Wenzhounese abroad only as comparison.
- **Anhui migrants in Shanghai** (nannies, builders, vendors).
- **She, Hui Muslims, Catholics and Protestants:** belonging through people and faith.
- **Sent-down youth who came back** to Shanghai, and their children born elsewhere.
- **Foreign residents** in Shanghai and Yiwu, for contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Shanghai (history, migrant surveys, press); mixed on smaller cities; thin on She and Hui in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese; Wenzhou and Ningbo Wu; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional prejudice (Subei, Anhui, "country people"); ethnic and religious belonging; registration rules (any legal reading goes to Libra). Name variation, never one Shanghai voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
