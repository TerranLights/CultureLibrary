## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai and Hangzhou middle-class families:** tutoring, school districts and the 2021 change.
- **Grandparent-raised children** in cities and villages: two generations' methods side by side.
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu** families with parents working away.
- **Southern Jiangsu villages** and **Huizhou**: older teaching traditions in lineage and school.
- **Silk districts** (Huzhou, Jiaxing, Tongxiang): nurture of young people and of silkworms.
- **Wenzhou family-business households:** children raised around the firm.
- **She families:** teaching children through song.
- **Hui families:** religious teaching of children alongside school.
- **Children of migrant workers** in Wu's cities, in migrant schools.
- **Kindergartens** in Anji against those in central Shanghai.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cities (surveys, PISA, education research); mixed for rural Anhui; thin for She and Hui child-rearing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** education policy and religious teaching of minors are sensitive; discipline and corporal punishment go to Capricorn; schooling as learning to Gemini and Sagittarius.
