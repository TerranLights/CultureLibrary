## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Zhejiang** (Taizhou, Ningbo, Zhoushan): sea raiders in the past, typhoons and fishing danger today.
- **Hangzhou and Haining:** the river's tide as a threat to be held back.
- **Huai River and Huaibei villages:** flood defense, and villages put at risk to protect others.
- **Shanghai high-rise residents and lane-house residents:** gated compounds against open lanes.
- **Nanjing:** wartime memory of protection, and of its failure, as a public subject.
- **Huizhou villages:** protection built into siting and walls.
- **Women and children at home:** whose safety is guarded by whom.
- **She communities:** ritual protection through initiation.
- **Hui Muslim communities:** protecting a minority community's life and food.
- **Migrant workers** in factory dormitories: who looks after them.
- **The elderly living alone** in old Shanghai neighborhoods.
