*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (gazetteers, charity records); Shanghainese; Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin (Huai valley); Hui Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huai floodplain villagers** (northern Anhui, northern Jiangsu): shelter from water as a recurring need.
- **Coastal and island families** (Zhoushan, Taizhou, Wenzhou): typhoon shelter for homes and boats.
- **Migrant workers** in crowded rentals, dormitories and worker sheds on building sites.
- **People sleeping rough** in Shanghai, Nanjing and Hangzhou.
- **Descendants of Shanghai's shantytown families**, and the regions they came from.
- **Women and children leaving violence at home:** who takes them in (kin, federation, shelter).
- **Huizhou and Zhejiang hill villages:** old traveler shelters on mountain roads.
- **She hill communities:** shelter on remote farmland.
- **Buddhist monasteries and pilgrims** at Jiuhuashan and Putuoshan.
- **Refugee memory groups:** Shanghai's Jewish refugee history; Nanjing's war refugees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war refugees and flood history; mixed for present-day homelessness (official framing dominates); thin for She and for rough sleepers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese; Huai-valley Mandarin; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homelessness and relief stations; forced removals; war memory; flood-storage decisions that put villages at risk. The legal side goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
