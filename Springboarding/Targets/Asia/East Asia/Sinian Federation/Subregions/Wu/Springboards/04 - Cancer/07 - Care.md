# Wu: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (lineage charity, medical texts); Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin; Hui Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's old inner-city residents:** elders living alone or with an aged spouse in old housing.
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu** elders whose children work elsewhere.
- **Anhui and Subei women working as carers** for families in Shanghai, Nanjing and Hangzhou.
- **Southern Jiangsu and Zhejiang villages** where collective welfare may shape care (Huaxi in Jiangyin as one case *check*).
- **Huizhou:** lineage welfare in the past, to set against village and state care now.
- **She communities:** herbal care and kin care in hill villages.
- **Hui Muslims:** community care through the mosque.
- **Catholic and Protestant communities:** church-run care.
- **Families of disabled children and adults**, and those who care for them.
- **Men who care** for a wife or parent.
