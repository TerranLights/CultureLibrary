## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu:** parents leaving children to work in the cities.
- **Shanghai families of sent-down youth:** children born and left in other provinces, and returns.
- **Shanghai's elderly living alone**, and their children abroad or across the city.
- **Island and mountain villages** in Zhejiang and southern Anhui emptied by moves to towns.
- **Women put away or widowed** in the old lineage societies of Shaoxing and Huizhou (pre-modern).
- **Foundlings and adoptees** raised in Wu orphanages; those adopted abroad as comparison only.
- **She and Hui communities:** how orphans and the left are taken in.
- **Fengyang and Huaibei:** a song tradition of leaving home.
- **Migrant workers' children** in Wu's cities, separated from grandparents back home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history (charities, literature) and for left-behind children; thin for She and Hui; adoption records restricted.
