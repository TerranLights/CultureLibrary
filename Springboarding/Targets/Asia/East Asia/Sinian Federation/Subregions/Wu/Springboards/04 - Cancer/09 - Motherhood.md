## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai mothers of only children** and those raising a second or third child.
- **Mothers-in-law and daughters-in-law** in Shanghai, Nanjing and Hangzhou, including marriages across regions.
- **Rural Anhui mothers** working away from their children, and the grandmothers raising them.
- **Huizhou and Shaoxing:** widowhood and mothers' standing in lineage society (historic).
- **Eastern Zhejiang (Ningbo, Taizhou):** where the rented-wife practice was written about.
- **Silk and water-town women** who earned while mothering.
- **She mothers:** an ancestral mother in their own origin story.
- **Hui Muslim mothers** teaching faith at home.
- **Catholic and Protestant mothers:** Marian devotion and church groups.
- **Single and unmarried mothers** in the cities (sensitive).

## 4. Flags
