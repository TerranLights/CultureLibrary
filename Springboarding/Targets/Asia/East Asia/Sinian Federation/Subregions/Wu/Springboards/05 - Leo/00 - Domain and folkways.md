## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai:** old-city families against post-1990 newcomers; Republican entertainment heritage against today's.
- **Taihu Wu heartland** (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou, Jiaxing, Huzhou, Hangzhou): Kunqu, Pingtan, gardens; the classic elite leisure model.
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing:** Yue opera's home ground, village opera, merchant wedding display.
- **Southern Wu** (Wenzhou, Taizhou, Jinhua, Lishui, Quzhou): their own opera forms and speech, hard for northern Wu speakers to follow.
- **Subei and Jianghuai Mandarin Jiangsu** (Nanjing, Yangzhou, Huai'an, Yancheng) and **Xuzhou** (Zhongyuan Mandarin): the north-south line inside Jiangsu.
- **Anhui:** the Huai plains (Fuyang, Bozhou, Bengbu: flower-drum lantern dances), central Hefei (Lu opera), Huizhou (Hui speech, lineage culture), Anqing (Gan speech, Huangmei opera).
- **She people** (Lishui, Jingning, Wenzhou hills): song festivals and courtship singing.
- **Hui Muslims** (Nanjing, Hefei and other cities): their own festival calendar and limits on pleasure.
- **Zhoushan islanders and fishing communities;** **migrant workers** from inland; **foreign residents** in Shanghai.
