# Wu: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Shanghainese and other Wu varieties, Jianghuai Mandarin, Huizhou speech. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai natives and newcomers:** pride in the city against resentment of it, both ways.
- **Ningbo, Shaoxing and Wenzhou merchants:** home-town business pride, at home and in Shanghai.
- **Subei and northern Anhui people:** pride rebuilt against stigma.
- **Nanjing:** pride in the "capital of six dynasties" and the Republican capital; Suzhou's pride in gardens and scholars.
- **Huizhou:** lineage and merchant pride, the name fight.
- **Hefei:** a fast-growing city's newer pride in science and industry.
- **She people;** **Hui Muslims** (Nanjing's claim on Zheng He's memory, *check*); **LGBT communities.**
- **Workers in old trades** (silk, porcelain, shipbuilding): pride in craft against job loss.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai identity and merchant groups; mixed for intercity rivalry (mostly online); thin for She and rural Anhui.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Ningbo Wu; Huizhou speech for village fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional put-downs (Subei, "country people", Anhui) can wound informants; LGBT Pride; ethnic pride is framed by state policy.
- **Held for review:** none.
