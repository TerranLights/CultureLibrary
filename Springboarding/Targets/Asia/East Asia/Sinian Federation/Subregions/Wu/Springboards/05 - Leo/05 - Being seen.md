# Wu: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Shanghainese and other Wu varieties, Hui Muslim usage, She speech. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai lane-house residents against gated-compound dwellers:** neighbor watching against anonymity.
- **Hangzhou:** the most watched city by its own tech; also a livestream hub where being seen is a job.
- **Suzhou and Wuxi garden towns:** old reticence about showing wealth; check against today.
- **Anhui and northern Jiangsu migrants in the delta cities:** counted, portrayed or overlooked.
- **Wenzhou and Qingtian families:** visibility abroad and discretion at home (diaspora as comparison only).
- **Women:** courtesans, film stars, contest entrants, square dancers: changing rules for being looked at.
- **Hui Muslims:** veiling and dress as visible faith; **She people:** costume at festivals, everyday dress otherwise.
- **The elderly living alone** in old neighborhoods: who notices them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai's visual culture and tabloids; mixed for surveillance (official and critical sources differ); thin for She and rural customs.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese for lane-life interviews; English secondary works.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and health-code data are politically sensitive; domestic workers and sex workers need careful consent; ridicule campaigns can expose private people.
- **Held for review:** none.
