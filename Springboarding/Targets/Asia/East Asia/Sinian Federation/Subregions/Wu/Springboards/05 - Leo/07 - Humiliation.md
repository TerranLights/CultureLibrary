## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai natives and northern Jiangsu or Anhui migrants:** jokes, accents and put-downs; who is the butt.
- **Jiangyin, Jiading, Yangzhou:** towns with memories of forced submission in 1645.
- **Nanjing:** the city most tied to national humiliation in public memory.
- **Shaoxing:** Lu Xun's home ground; local readings of Ah Q.
- **She people:** slurs from Han neighbors, and how the She answer them.
- **Hui Muslims:** insults over food and religion, a delicate topic.
- **Families of those struggled against in 1966 to 1976,** by city.
- **Schoolchildren and young workers:** bullying, hazing in factories and the military.
- **Women:** jilting, betrayal and gossip; how blame falls differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national humiliation and face theory; mixed for school bullying; thin for everyday local teasing outside Shanghai and for minority experience.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese for comedy and teasing; English secondary works.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Cultural Revolution; ethnic slurs; national-humiliation themes can stir strong feeling; survivors of public shaming may not wish to be found.
- **Held for review:** none.
