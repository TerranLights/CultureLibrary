## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Jiangsu (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou):** collective village leaders and township enterprises.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** private bosses, clan networks, consultative experiments.
- **Huizhou:** lineage elders and merchant managers.
- **Northern Anhui (Fengyang, Fuyang) and northern Jiangsu:** founders' legends, rural reform from below, military recruitment.
- **Shanghai:** gang bosses, industrialists, Party leaders, corporate founders across a century.
- **Hefei:** Huai Army memory; today's science-city administrators.
- **Women:** the Soong sisters, Yue opera troupe heads, village women's directors (妇女主任).
- **Hui Muslims:** imams and elders; **She people:** village and ritual leaders.
- **Lineage and religious leaders** (Buddhist abbots, Protestant pastors in Wenzhou).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite and historical leaders and reform models; mixed for everyday workplace leadership; thin for She and Hui leaders.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Mandarin; Wenzhou and Taizhou Wu for local governance fieldwork; English for comparative studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current political leadership; Chiang Kai-shek's memory in Fenghua is politically delicate; church leaders in Zhejiang; formal authority belongs to Capricorn, law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
