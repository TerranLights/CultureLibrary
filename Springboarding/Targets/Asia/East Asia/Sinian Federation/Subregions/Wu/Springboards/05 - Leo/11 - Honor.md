*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Shanghainese, Huizhou speech, She speech. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huizhou lineages:** honor through arches, halls and women's chastity; merchant word-keeping.
- **Suzhou and Wuxi:** scholar-official integrity; commoners' honor in the 1626 riot.
- **Yangzhou, Jiangyin and Jiading:** loyalist memory of 1645.
- **Shaoxing and Ningbo:** revolutionary martyrs; old merchant credit.
- **Shanghai:** trade honor, gang codes, and modern reputation.
- **Northern Jiangsu and northern Anhui:** military recruitment, sworn brotherhood, rural feuds (*check*).
- **Women:** chaste widows honored, revolutionaries honored, and today's gendered gossip.
- **She people:** origin honor; **Hui Muslims:** religious integrity and community name.
- **Veterans and soldiers' families.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loyalist and lineage honor (arches, gazetteers, biographies); mixed for merchant honor; thin for everyday honor among young people and for She views.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Mandarin; Huizhou speech and Shanghainese for interviews; English and Japanese secondary works.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor tied to women's chastity and the harm it did; martyr status is state-defined; the Southern Anhui Incident is contested between parties' histories; slurs against the She.
- **Held for review:** none.
