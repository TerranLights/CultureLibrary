# Wu: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Shanghainese and Suzhou Wu, Anqing and Huai River speech, She speech. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Hangzhou:** calm, garden and lake delights; Kunqu and teahouse music.
- **Shanghai:** comic theater, dance halls past and present, city festivals.
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing:** wedding display and village opera.
- **Anqing and western Anhui:** Huangmei opera, Gan-speaking counties.
- **Huai River plains (Bengbu, Fengtai, Fuyang):** loud, physical festival joy.
- **Huizhou:** lantern and lineage festivals.
- **Wenzhou:** Protestant worship joy against temple festivals.
- **Hui Muslims:** Eid gatherings; **She people:** song gatherings and weddings.
- **Elderly park-goers and young clubbers:** joy by generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical literature, opera and arts; mixed for survey happiness (provincial samples are small); thin for She and rural festivals.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Mandarin; Shanghainese and Suzhou Wu for comedy and teahouse fieldwork; Anqing speech for Huangmei settings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official "happiness" rankings are partly publicity; religious joy (Wenzhou churches) is sensitive; festival noise disputes.
- **Held for review:** none.
