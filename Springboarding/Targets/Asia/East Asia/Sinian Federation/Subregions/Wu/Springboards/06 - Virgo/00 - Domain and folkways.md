# Wu: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia: Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Classical for older sources); Wu varieties (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Ningbo, Wenzhou); Jianghuai and Central Plains Mandarin; Huizhou dialects; Gan in southwest Anhui; She (Shehua). **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: work, service, the daily round, the kept house and health, and how they are grouped.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taihu Wu core** (Shanghai, Suzhou, Hangzhou, Jiaxing, Huzhou): rice, silk and the oldest factory workforce.
- **Ningbo, Shaoxing, Zhoushan**: merchant emigration and island fishing seasons.
- **Wenzhou, Taizhou, Jinhua**: family workshops and Yiwu trading, in their own Wu varieties.
- **Jianghuai Jiangsu** (Yangzhou, Yancheng, Huai'an): salt yards, service trades, the Subei stigma.
- **Xuzhou and Huaibei**: wheat north of the Huai; flood and famine memory.
- **Central and southwest Anhui**: Jianghuai and Gan speech; a large source of migrant and domestic workers.
- **Huizhou**: merchant lineages, mountain tea, tenant-servants.
- **She people** of the Zhejiang hills; **Hui Muslims** of Nanjing and Anhui (halal trades, washing rules); **boat and fishing peoples** (the "nine-surname fishers" 九姓渔民, *check*); **Southern Min enclaves** of southern Wenzhou.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai and the Taihu core (Chinese, English, Japanese); mixed for Jianghuai and Huaibei; thin for the She and boat peoples. The unit's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet uses the four provinces listed. The federation is a frame only.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Wu varieties for oral sources; Japanese for Jiangnan social history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** duomin and tenant-servant descendants may carry stigma; "Subei" can be a slur; Anhui famine and labor-camp archives are partly closed; labor protest is censored online.
- **Held for review:** none.
