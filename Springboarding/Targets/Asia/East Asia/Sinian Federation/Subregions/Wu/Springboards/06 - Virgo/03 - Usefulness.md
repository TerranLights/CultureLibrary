## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai retirees**: life after retirement and the search for a role (grandparenting, volunteering, square dancing).
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu family firms**: children's help in the business as normal work.
- **Suzhou and Hangzhou literati heritage**: the refined "useless" object as status.
- **Huizhou**: lineage schools and the scholar against the merchant; whose learning counts.
- **North Jiangsu and Anhui migrants in delta cities**: scrap collecting and odd jobs, and how others rate them.
- **Rural Anhui left-behind elders**: the elders who mind grandchildren while parents work away.
- **She communities**: roles kept by elders in song and ritual.
- **Hui Muslim communities**: mosque-based elder roles and trades.
- **Disabled residents**: whether city programs or family carry them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statecraft thought and education history; good for elder surveys; thin for She and Hui elder roles; moderate for disability.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the thinkers; Standard Chinese; Wu varieties for everyday insults and praise; English for Clunas and survey documentation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder suicide and the left-behind elderly; disability stigma; scrap collectors' legal status; Cultural Revolution attacks on "useless" intellectuals.
- **Held for review:** none.
