## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Changzhou literati heirs**: a text-critical tradition carried into modern scholarship.
- **Huizhou**: the evidential school of Dai Zhen beside merchants' arithmetic.
- **Wenzhou**: a mathematics reputation beside a workshop economy.
- **Shanghai professionals**: accountants, engineers, financiers and the city's reputation for calculation (to test, not assume).
- **Hefei**: a newer science city in a mostly farming province.
- **Rural Huaibei and north Jiangsu**: farm reckoning and almanac knowledge beside school science.
- **She communities**: their own ways of reading weather and land, as they share them.
- **Hui Muslim communities**: mosque education and its arithmetic for trade and calendars.
