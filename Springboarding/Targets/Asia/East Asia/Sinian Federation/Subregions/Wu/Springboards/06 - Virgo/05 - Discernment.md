*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Suzhou Wu (much of the vocabulary of shrewdness is Wu); Huizhou dialects; She. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou**: connoisseurship of gardens, food, ballad singing, and the forgery trade beside it.
- **Shanghai**: market savvy, matchmaking by criteria, and an anti-fraud apparatus.
- **Hangzhou and the West Lake tea villages**: palates trained on tea grades.
- **Huizhou**: appraisers and pawnbrokers; judging goods for a living.
- **Shaoxing and Yuyao**: the Wang Yangming heritage of moral judgment.
- **Subei and Anhui migrants**: the newcomer as the supposed mark, and how they judge city people in turn.
- **Wenzhou**: trust within kin networks for credit (the informal lending crisis of 2011, *check*).
- **She communities**: song contests as a test of persons.
- **Hui Muslims**: halal discernment of food and trust in vendors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for connoisseurship and literati taste; rich for present-day scams and matchmaking; thin for She and rural judgment practices.
- **Languages needed:** Wu varieties (much of the vocabulary is Wu); Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; English and Japanese for art-history scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "rumor" is a political category, and fact-check sites carry the state's view; regional stereotypes of the "shrewd Shanghainese" and the "naive outsider" must be tested, not repeated; scam victims underreport.
- **Held for review:** none.
