# Wu: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (craft and exam sources); Shanghainese, Suzhou and Ningbo Wu; Huizhou dialects; She. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou crafts and gardens**: polish that conceals effort.
- **Shaoxing**: calligraphy heritage and its exacting copying.
- **Yixing and Longquan potters**: kiln losses and the rejection of flawed pieces.
- **Shanghai factory and white-collar workers**: planned-economy quality pride against present-day speed.
- **Ningbo Red Gang tailors**: exact fit for Western clothing.
- **Jiangsu and Anhui exam towns**: drilling for scores; family pressure.
- **Huizhou paper and ink makers**: long processes, low tolerance for flaws.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu small goods makers**: speed and price against finish.
- **She weavers**: their own standards for a finished sash.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts and exams; growing for psychology; thin for She crafts beyond heritage listings.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for treatises; Standard Chinese; Wu varieties for craftspeople's own terms; English for perfectionism research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and mental illness; the gendered word 作; heritage listings flatten variation; the cheap-goods image of some towns is a stereotype to test.
- **Held for review:** none.
