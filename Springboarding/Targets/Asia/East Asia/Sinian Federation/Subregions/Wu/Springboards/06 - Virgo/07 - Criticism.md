## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wuxi and Suzhou literati heritage**: remonstrance and the costs of speaking out.
- **Shaoxing**: the Lu Xun essay tradition and its local pride.
- **Shanghai lanes and comic stages**: quarrels and satire in Wu speech.
- **Shanghai and Hangzhou white-collar firms**: formal feedback, ranking and anonymous complaint.
- **Fengyang and Huaibei Anhui**: song as complaint, from migrants and the poor.
- **Huizhou lineages**: reprimand in the ancestral hall under lineage rules.
- **Nanjing**: an old capital; test whether mockery of officials takes its own form there.
- **She and Hui communities**: correction inside a minority group, kept from outsiders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for literary and political criticism; rich online; thin for minority practices.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Wu for comic theater and quarrels; English and Japanese for Ming-Qing history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political criticism is heavily censored; campaign-era records and some memoirs are closed; whistleblowers and critics may be at risk, so sources should not be exposed; satire of regional groups can be demeaning.
- **Held for review:** none.
