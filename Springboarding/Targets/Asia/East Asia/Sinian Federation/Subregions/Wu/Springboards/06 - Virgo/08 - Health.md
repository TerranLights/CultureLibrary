## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Changzhou**: rival medical lineages and pharmacy traditions.
- **Huizhou**: Xin'an doctors, mountain herbs, merchant patronage of medicine.
- **Shanghai**: hospital medicine, public health and the lockdown memory.
- **Jiaxing, Qingpu and lake villages**: the snail-fever campaign in living memory.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou**: private clinics and folk remedies in a self-employed economy (to test).
- **Huaibei and north Jiangsu**: wheat-region diets and health gaps against the delta.
- **Zhoushan islanders**: fishermen's illnesses and island access to care.
- **She communities**: their own healers and herbal knowledge.
- **Hui Muslims**: halal rules, washing customs and mosque-linked clinics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for Chinese medicine and public health; rich in surveys; thin for She and island folk healing.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for medical texts; Standard Chinese; Wu varieties for healing terms and oral histories; English for historiography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2022 lockdown, famine-era health and some epidemic records are restricted or censored; mental illness and HIV carry stigma; healers' knowledge is shared only as they choose.
- **Held for review:** none.
