# Wu: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (morality books, statecraft); Shanghainese and Wenzhou Wu; Jianghuai Mandarin (Hefei); She. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai lane residents**: renewal as gain or loss of home.
- **Shanghai sent-down generation**: improvement imposed on youth, recounted decades later.
- **Chun'an and the Xin'an River resettled**: a dam's benefits against a drowned county.
- **Anji and Zhejiang model villages**: rural renovation and ecological branding.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu traders**: self-made rising and family capital.
- **Hefei and Anqing**: Self-Strengthening memory and Anhui's later development push.
- **Rural Anhui and north Jiangsu**: migration and remittances as the road to a better house.
- **Jiashan and Jiaxing**: the home ground of the merit-ledger tradition.
- **She communities**: renewal of language and song against assimilation.
- **Hui Muslims of Nanjing and Anhui**: mosque schooling and community uplift on their own terms (*check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban renewal, reform history and mobility surveys; mixed for self-help; thin for She revitalization.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Wu varieties for residents' accounts; English and French for scholarship on sent-down youth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced relocation, sent-down youth and thought reform are politically sensitive; evicted residents' petitions are restricted; minority "development" can echo assimilation.
- **Held for review:** none.
