*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese, modern and classical; Shanghainese, Suzhou and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin (Yangzhou, Hefei); Huizhou Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai and Hangzhou office workers:** long hours and the language of burnout.
- **Kunshan and Suzhou factory workers, many from Anhui and Subei:** shift work far from family.
- **Yangzhou and Suzhou:** old leisure traditions of tea, baths, gardens and storytelling.
- **Huizhou villages:** balance with the land through siting and water.
- **Taihu and Grand Canal towns:** water quality and the fishery.
- **Zhoushan and Taizhou fishers:** the sea's limits.
- **She communities in Lishui and Jingning:** their own medicine and its terms for the body.
- **Hui Muslims:** halal diet and the Ramadan rhythm of fasting and feasting.
- **Northern Anhui farmers:** flood and drought along the Huai, and how a farm year is kept in balance.
- **Richer Shanghai and Sunan against poorer northern Anhui:** the balance of revenue among the four lands and with the federation (fiscal yearbooks, *check*).
- **Older and younger generations:** tonics and solar terms against wellness apps and "lying flat".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medicine, gardens, cuisine and ecology; mixed for work-life data by province; thin for She medicine in published form.
- **Languages needed:** modern and Classical Chinese; Wu dialect reading for everyday terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor protest and "lying flat" have drawn official criticism; environmental crises can be sensitive locally; She healers' knowledge may be restricted and should be gathered only as they share it.
- **Held for review:** none.
