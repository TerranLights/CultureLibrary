## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hefei:** home of the Bao Zheng sites; how far residents, not only officials, invoke him.
- **Suzhou and Songjiang gentry descendants:** the history of the powerful facing an upright governor.
- **Shanghai:** impersonal platforms, lotteries and exams as the official answer to connections.
- **Zhejiang counties:** the "run at most once" reform from the citizen's side.
- **Wenzhou and Rui'an villages:** lineage voting blocs.
- **Huizhou:** lineage loyalty with an exam-and-office ideal.
- **Subei and northern Anhui towns:** small places where everyone knows the official.
- **She and Hui communities:** preferential cadre and exam policies (partiality by design) and how members see them.
- **Locals and migrants:** "locals first" hiring and access to services.
- **Private firms and state enterprises:** family firms in Wenzhou and Yiwu against rule-bound hiring.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and official reform; mixed for everyday use of connections; thin for minority viewpoints.
- **Languages needed:** modern and Classical Chinese; Wu dialect reading for local speech; English for political science on village elections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases, the 海瑞罢官 affair and village elections are politically sensitive; use published records only. Regional slurs about "clannishness" are stereotypes to study, not findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
