## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai young professionals:** delayed marriage, house purchase and career choices.
- **Exam families in the four lands:** different subject-choice systems put the decision at different ages.
- **Rural Anhui and Subei families:** whether elders, parents or the young decide on migration and marriage.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu traders:** fast business decisions as a reputation to test, not assume.
- **Xuzhou and Suqian:** local memory of Xiang Yu's hesitation and Liu Bang's resolve.
- **Temple-goers in Hangzhou, Nanjing and Putuoshan:** lots and almanacs as decision aids.
- **Christian communities in Wenzhou:** prayer and pastors' counsel in personal decisions.
- **She and Hui communities:** whose counsel is sought (elders, imams) before a choice, as they describe it.
- **Older people and their children:** end-of-life and care decisions left to the family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, history and exam policy; mixed for everyday decision-making; thin for minority practice.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the histories; modern Chinese; Wu dialect reading for local expressions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** residents' objections to public projects are sensitive; clinical material requires ethics approval; divination practice may be discussed reluctantly.
