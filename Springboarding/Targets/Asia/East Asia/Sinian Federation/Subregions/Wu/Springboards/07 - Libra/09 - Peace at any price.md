# Wu: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese, modern and classical; Shanghainese, Hangzhou, Wenzhou and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin (Tongcheng, Yangzhou, Nanjing); Huizhou Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tongcheng and Anqing:** official use of the lane story, and how residents tell it.
- **Hangzhou:** the city of the Yue Fei shrine and the memory of the Southern Song peace.
- **Nanjing and Yangzi delta towns:** wartime accommodation and its judgment.
- **Jiangyin and Jiading:** how resistance is remembered locally.
- **Shanghai families:** dialect TV mediation and quarrels in crowded housing.
- **Rural Anhui and Subei women:** silence about violence and the reach of hotlines.
- **Office workers in Shanghai, Suzhou and Hangzhou:** voice and silence at work.
- **She and Hui communities:** keeping peace with Han neighbors and officials; what is left unsaid, as they describe it.
- **Wenzhou Protestants:** church teaching on forbearance and on speaking out.
- **Older and younger generations:** "let it go" against newer language of boundaries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, literature and proverbs; mixed for domestic violence and workplace silence; thin for minority voices.
