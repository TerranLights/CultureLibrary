# Wu: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** written Chinese, modern and classical; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties (where many labels for outsiders live); Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin of Subei and Anhui; Huizhou Chinese; She. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Shanghai families and Subei-descended Shanghainese:** the historic insider-outsider line inside the city.
- **Shanghainese and newcomers (新上海人, 外地人):** current labels in both directions.
- **Jiangsu's cities:** Nanjing, Su-Xi-Chang and Xuzhou as one another's mirrors.
- **Wenzhou:** a group named in national press labels, and how Wenzhou people answer them.
- **Anhui migrants in delta cities:** whether and how they are made targets, and their own images of the cities.
- **Deqing and other Zhejiang towns of the 1768 scare:** local memory of a panic.
- **Rural and urban people:** 乡下人 as a label, and rural images of city people.
- **She and Hui communities:** whether rumors have attached to them, as they report it.
- **Christians in Wenzhou and Buddhists at the region's temples:** confession and repentance as practiced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and literature; mixed for current othering (online data is large but unstable); thin for minorities' accounts of being blamed.
- **Languages needed:** modern and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese for labels and humor; English for much of the scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution violence, religious and ethnic targets, and regional slurs are sensitive. Record every slur and stereotype as a claim made by someone, never as a finding. Some archives are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Honig's chapters on the label 江北人, then search CNKI for 苏北人 上海 歧视 for Chinese-language studies.
