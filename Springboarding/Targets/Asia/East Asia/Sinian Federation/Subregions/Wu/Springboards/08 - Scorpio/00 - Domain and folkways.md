# Wu: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; traditional for older print); the Wu varieties (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Ningbo, Hangzhou, Wenzhou and others); Jianghuai and Central Plains Mandarin; the Huizhou dialects; She (Shanha) speech; the Hui Muslims' religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how a culture faces death, power and what is hidden, with its death ways, sex ways, inheritance and shared resources.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's core against Pudong and the suburban villages (本地人):** funeral homes against village burial and ancestor rites.
- **The Lake Tai lands (Suzhou, Wuxi, Changzhou, Jiaxing, Huzhou):** the silk-and-rice heartland where lineage estates and village credit were first studied.
- **Ningbo, Shaoxing, Zhoushan:** merchant migrants who shipped their dead home; island fishing families.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** hillside tombs, private finance and large Christian congregations.
- **Huizhou (southern Anhui):** the deed archives make inheritance traceable household by household.
- **Jianghuai Mandarin lands (Nanjing, Yangzhou, Hefei) and the Central Plains Mandarin north (Xuzhou, Huaibei, Fuyang):** other speech, other rites; not to be folded into "Jiangnan".
- **She people (Lishui, Jingning):** their own funeral and ancestral rites.
- **Hui Muslims (Nanjing, Hefei, Shouxian, Hangzhou):** a mosque-centered funeral tradition to trace on its own.
- **Subei-origin families in Shanghai and newer migrants from inland provinces.**
- **Diaspora (Shanghainese in Hong Kong and Taiwan; Wenzhou and Qingtian people in Europe):** comparison only; inheritance and remittances across borders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai and Huizhou (archives, histories, surveys); mixed for the Lake Tai and Ningbo-Shaoxing lands; thinner for She, Hui and northern Anhui.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sheet is written for the four listed provinces.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese in both scripts; classical Chinese and Huizhou deed handwriting; Wu varieties for oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grave clearance and cremation drives; sexuality and LGBT topics are politically constrained; property disputes involve living families.
- **Held for review:** none.
