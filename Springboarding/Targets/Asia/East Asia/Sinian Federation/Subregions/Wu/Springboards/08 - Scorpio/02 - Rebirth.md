# Wu: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Buddhist and Daoist classical vocabulary; Wu varieties (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Hangzhou, Wenzhou); Hui Muslim terms; She. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery and redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiuhuashan pilgrims and southern Anhui villagers:** rebirth tied to a mountain and its bodhisattva.
- **Lay Pure Land circles in Suzhou, Hangzhou and Shanghai:** rebirth as a deathbed goal, not a second chance in this life.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou Protestants, and older Catholic villages near Shanghai:** conversion as a break with family rites.
- **Hui Muslims:** repentance without reincarnation.
- **She people:** an initiation that renames.
- **Shanghai's laid-off workers and returning sent-down youth:** starting over in the same city after decades away or out of work.
- **Residents moved out of demolished lanes:** the reborn city as their loss.
- **Former prisoners and people in recovery:** whether a new life is believed.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; Wenzhou congregations abroad as a check on conversion stories at home.
