## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou and the southern coast:** home of the oldest betrayal plots on the stage.
- **Hangzhou:** the Yue Fei cult keeps a single treachery in public view.
- **Changshu, Taicang, Suzhou:** literati whose choices at the Ming fall are still judged.
- **Nanjing and Shanghai under occupation:** collaboration and resistance in the same streets.
- **Shanghai families of sent-down youth:** desertion of spouses and children as a generational wound.
- **Anhui's migrant-sending villages:** marriages split by distance.
- **Suzhou-speaking audiences of pingtan:** betrayal of kin as a stock plot.
- **Hui Muslims and She people:** loyalty inside small communities; little known, to check.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; how Shanghainese who left in 1949 tell who betrayed whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drama, fiction and wartime history; mixed for present-day infidelity (surveys few, media plenty); thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Suzhou Wu for pingtan; Shanghainese for TV and oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traitor" labels in party history are politically fixed; wartime collaboration is touchy for families; infidelity programs show identifiable people.
