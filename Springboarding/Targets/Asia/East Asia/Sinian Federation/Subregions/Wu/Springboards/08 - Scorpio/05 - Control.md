*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huizhou villages:** lineage and stone arches as instruments over women and juniors.
- **Pujiang and other Zhejiang communal lineages:** household rules for a whole kin group.
- **Shanghai parents and their adult children:** marriage and schooling as the field of control.
- **Hangzhou and Zhuji:** places that exported models of public control.
- **Migrants without Shanghai hukou:** the controlled side of the residence rules.
- **Chun'an and other reservoir resettlers:** control of nature borne by one group.
- **Hui Muslims:** consent and permission inside the family and mosque.
- **Northern Anhui and Xuzhou households:** control between spouses, without the Shanghai stereotypes.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; parental control as Wu emigrant families abroad describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and digital control in Shanghai and Hangzhou; mixed for family control (surveys and fiction); thin for rural northern Anhui and the minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese for family and TV sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance, health codes and hukou are politically sensitive; the jokey image of the "henpecked Shanghai husband" (妻管严) is a stereotype to test, not a pointer.
- **Held for review:** none.
