## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** private lending among kin and neighbors, and its collapses.
- **Ningbo merchants and their descendants:** trust carried along native-place lines.
- **Huizhou lineages:** kin credit in the merchant trade.
- **Fengyang and other famine-scarred Anhui villages:** trust forged by shared danger.
- **Shanghai lane neighbors against high-rise residents:** trust among people who once shared kitchens.
- **Shanghai locals and inland migrants:** who is believed, hired and rented to.
- **Hui Muslims:** trust in food and in co-religionist traders.
- **Online shoppers across the four provinces:** platform scores in place of acquaintance.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; whether Wenzhou and Qingtian traders in Europe lend along the same ties, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant and financial history and for surveys; mixed for everyday trust between strangers; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Wenzhou and Ningbo speech for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in government and the press is politically sensitive; crime cases involve named families; trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
