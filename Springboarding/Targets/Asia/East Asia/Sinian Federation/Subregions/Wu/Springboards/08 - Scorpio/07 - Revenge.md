## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou and Shaoxing:** the two old capitals whose foundation stories are revenge stories.
- **Huaibei and Xuzhou:** a plain long described through feud and self-defense; check, do not assume.
- **Southern Zhejiang lineages:** feuds recorded in gazetteers and settled by elders.
- **Tongcheng:** both the avenging daughter and the yielding lane, from one county.
- **Shanghai's wartime population:** assassination and reprisal in a divided city.
- **Yangzhou storytelling audiences:** revenge as a long, savored performance.
- **She villages:** disputes with Han neighbors and their settlement.
- **Young online readers:** revenge as a fantasy genre.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; Jin Yong's Hong Kong readers as a check on how revenge plots travel.
