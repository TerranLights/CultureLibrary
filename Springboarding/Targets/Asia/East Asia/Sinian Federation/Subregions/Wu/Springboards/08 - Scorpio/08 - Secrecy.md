## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing lineages:** knowledge held as a family's possession.
- **Suzhou and Lake Tai merchants:** commercial secrecy written into script and ledgers.
- **Shanghai's lane dwellers against flat dwellers:** privacy in shared space and in sealed apartments.
- **Former underground and intelligence families in Shanghai and Jiangshan:** secrets kept for a cause, and the cost.
- **She people:** ritual objects not shown to outsiders.
- **LGBT people in Shanghai, Hangzhou and Nanjing against smaller towns:** whether a hidden life is easier in a big city.
- **Families of patients with grave illness:** secrecy as protection.
- **Bozhou and the Huaibei north:** legends of hidden tombs and tunnels.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; families abroad who kept political pasts from kin at home.
