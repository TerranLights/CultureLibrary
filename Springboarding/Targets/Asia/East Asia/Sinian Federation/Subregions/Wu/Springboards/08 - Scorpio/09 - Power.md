## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanjing:** a capital many times over, with power written into tombs and walls.
- **Hangzhou and the Lake Tai cities:** power through wealth, scholarship and exam success.
- **Shaoxing:** a reputation for advisers rather than rulers.
- **Shanghai:** foreign, gangster, party and corporate power layered in one city.
- **Huaibei and Xuzhou:** dynastic founders and rebels from the northern plain.
- **Quzhou and the interior of Zhejiang:** inherited lineage standing far from courts.
- **Hui mosque communities:** religious standing and its limits.
- **She lineages:** leadership inside a minority's villages.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; Ningbo and Shanghai tycoons in Hong Kong as power carried abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial and Republican history and for Shanghai; mixed for present-day local power; thin for minority leadership.
