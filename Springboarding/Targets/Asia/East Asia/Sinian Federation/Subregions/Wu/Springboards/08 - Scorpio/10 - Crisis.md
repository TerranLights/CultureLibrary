*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huai River plain (Fuyang, Funan, Bengbu) and Fengyang:** floods and famine as recurring crises.
- **Yangzhou and Jiading:** towns whose memory is marked by the 1645 massacres.
- **Shanghai residents:** war, epidemic and lockdown, each met in a dense city.
- **Haining and the Qiantang estuary:** living beside a dangerous tide.
- **Wuxi and the Lake Tai shore:** water crises tied to industry.
- **Coastal Zhejiang (Taizhou, Wenzhou, Ningbo):** typhoon seasons and evacuation.
- **Hui Muslim communities:** religious response in hard times.
- **Young adults in the big cities:** personal crises, overwork and breakdown.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only; emigrant aid sent home after floods and typhoons.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, flood and epidemic history and for Shanghai; mixed for present-day personal crises; thin for minority experience of disasters.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Huaibei speech for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the famine, the 2022 lockdown and disaster inquiries are politically sensitive; online records are often removed.
- **Held for review:** none.
