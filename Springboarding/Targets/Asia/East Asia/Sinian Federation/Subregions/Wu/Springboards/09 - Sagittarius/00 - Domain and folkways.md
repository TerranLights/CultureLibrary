## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's core vs. outer districts** (Songjiang, Qingpu, Chongming): port city against rural Wu parishes and temples.
- **Southern vs. northern Jiangsu** (Suzhou-Wuxi-Changzhou against Yangzhou-Huai'an-Xuzhou): Wu against Mandarin speech; prejudice against "Subei people" shapes whose voice is recorded.
- **Nanjing:** Six Dynasties Buddhism, Ming capital, Taiping capital, Hui center.
- **Zhejiang's zones:** Hangzhou-Jiaxing-Huzhou; Ningbo-Shaoxing; Wenzhou-Taizhou (dense churches and temples); the Jinhua-Lishui hills; the Zhoushan islands.
- **Anhui:** Huizhou (lineage, merchants); the Anqing-Hefei belt; Huaibei (Bozhou, Fuyang), a North China Plain culture.
- **Peoples:** the She; Hui Muslims (Nanjing, Yangzhou, the Huai valley); garrison Manchu descendants *check*; minority migrants; foreign residents of Shanghai and Yiwu.
- **Faith groups:** Buddhist, Daoist, temple networks, Catholic, Protestant (registered or not), Muslim, unaffiliated.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Wenzhou and Huizhou; thinner for Huaibei, the Zhejiang hills and the She. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled (the four provinces in the tracker header are a working list); these sheets are written for them as the core.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Wu varieties for oral material (Wenzhou speech separately); Hui Chinese; She; mosque vocabulary.
