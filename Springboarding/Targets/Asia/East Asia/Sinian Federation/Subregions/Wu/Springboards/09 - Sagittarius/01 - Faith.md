# Wu: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Wu Chinese varieties; Jianghuai Mandarin; Hui Chinese; She; Hui Muslim usage; Classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elderly lay chanting women in Wu towns vs. young urban Buddhists** in Shanghai and Hangzhou who meet through temples' classes and apps: faith by habit against faith chosen.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou Protestants vs. rural Anhui Protestants** (Huaibei and the Jianghuai plain): merchant congregations against farming ones; the size of the Anhui flock is a question *check*.
- **Shanghai's old Catholic families** (Xujiahui, Songjiang, Pudong villages) vs. new converts: inherited against chosen faith.
- **Taihu and Grand Canal boat people and Zhoushan fishers:** gods of water and weather.
- **Huizhou lineages:** faith tied to ancestor halls rather than temples.
- **Hui Muslims** in Nanjing, Yangzhou and the Huai valley; **the She** of Jingning and Lishui: faith in their own terms.
- **Subei and Anhui migrants in Shanghai:** do they carry home-village gods or join city congregations?
- **The unaffiliated:** where faith in science, nation or market sits instead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhism, Catholicism and Wenzhou Protestantism; thin and sensitive for unregistered groups; thin for the She and for Huaibei folk practice.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Wu varieties (Wenzhou separately) for oral testimony; She; Hui mosque vocabulary.
