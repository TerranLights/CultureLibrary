# Wu: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Wu Chinese varieties (Shanghainese above all for city speech); Jianghuai Mandarin; Classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai office workers vs. Hangzhou tech workers:** finance and trade against platform firms; where each says the point of work lies.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu family-business owners:** meaning through the firm and the family it carries.
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu villages:** the left-behind generation; meaning when the children are gone.
- **Factory towns of southern Jiangsu** (Kunshan, Suzhou industrial parks) and their migrant workers from other provinces.
- **Huizhou and Shaoxing lineage villages:** legacy and ancestor halls.
- **Shanghai retirees, including returned sent-down youth:** lost years and late meaning.
- **Buddhist and Christian believers vs. the unaffiliated:** is meaning given or made?
- **The She and Hui Muslims:** meaning tied to their own ancestors and faith, in their own words.
- **Zhoushan fishing families:** a trade in decline and what replaces it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai's literature, media and clinics; mixed for surveys (meaning items are rare; well-being and depression stand in); thin for the She and for Huaibei villages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese for city speech and *Blossoms*; Wenzhou speech for oral histories there.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data are sparse and handled carefully; "lying flat" talk has been censored at times; sent-down-youth memories touch politics.
- **Held for review:** none.
