# Wu: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Wu Chinese varieties; Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin (Anhui, northern Jiangsu). **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai hukou holders vs. migrants** from Anhui, Subei and beyond: who can stay, school children and move freely.
- **Wenzhou, Taizhou and Yiwu traders:** freedom as running one's own business.
- **Anhui farmers:** freedom as land and leaving the village; Xiaogang as a local memory.
- **Women across generations:** footbinding's end, free choice in marriage, and today's single urban women in Shanghai.
- **Students and scholars** at Fudan, Nanjing University and Zhejiang University: academic freedom (see also education).
- **Registered and unregistered Christians in Wenzhou; Hui Muslims; the She:** freedom of worship and of custom.
- **Descendants of hereditary "mean" groups** (Shaoxing, Huizhou): whether the memory survives.
- **Shanghai's foreign residents:** a different set of permitted freedoms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Republican Shanghai (press, archives, memoirs); thin and sensitive for after 1949; surveys rarely allow province breakdowns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Wenzhou speech for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speech, press and protest after 1949; the 2022 lockdown; worship. Researchers inside the federation may face risk; anonymize informants. Laws on speech, residence and religion go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
