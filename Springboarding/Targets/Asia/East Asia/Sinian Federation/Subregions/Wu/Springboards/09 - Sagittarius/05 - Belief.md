## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's young professionals vs. their grandparents:** half-ironic temple visits against inherited observances.
- **Huizhou lineage villages vs. Wenzhou Christian villages:** siting and ancestor beliefs against beliefs that reject them.
- **Shaoxing and Ningbo** (ghost lore, Mulian theater) vs. **Huaibei** (northern plain customs).
- **Party members and state employees:** required atheism beside family practice; whether the two meet.
- **Hui Muslims:** belief in one God and the afterlife, and taboos shared or not with Han neighbors.
- **The She:** their ancestor beliefs, told by the She; outsiders' versions of the Panhu story have been used to mock them.
- **Zhoushan and Taihu boat people:** water taboos and omens.
- **Scientists and engineers** in Hefei and Shanghai: belief and warrant in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern texts and folklore collections; mixed for surveys (questions on ghosts or fate are sensitive and rarely asked by province); thin for the She.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Wu varieties (Shanghainese, Shaoxing) for taboo and luck vocabulary; Hui Chinese; She.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" is an official label; party members' beliefs; the She origin story. Rules for registering sites go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
