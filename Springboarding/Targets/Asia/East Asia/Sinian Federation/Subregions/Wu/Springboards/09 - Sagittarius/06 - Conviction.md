## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaoxing and Ningbo (Zhedong):** a long line of loyalists, scholars and revolutionaries; is the local pride in it shared by all classes?
- **Suzhou and Shanghai:** the commercial, "sensible" self-image of Wu cities, against their own dissidents.
- **Yangzhou and Nanjing:** cities whose memory centers on sieges and massacres.
- **Huizhou lineages:** steadfastness as women's chastity and men's filial duty.
- **Wenzhou Christians** who resisted cross removals; **Hui Muslims** holding to observance in mixed towns.
- **Rural Anhui petitioners and protesters:** conviction over land, wages and pollution.
- **Students and young professionals:** do they speak of "principles" or of "being sensible"?
- **The She:** steadfastness in their own customs, told by the She.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern loyalists and Republican revolutionaries; thin and suppressed for post-1949 dissent; protest data come from outside groups and are incomplete.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for loyalist writing; Standard Chinese; Wu varieties for oral history; English for diaspora-published dissident records (comparison only).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Lin Zhao and other dissidents; protest counts; religious resisters. Researchers and informants inside the federation may face risk. Criminal convictions belong to Libra.
