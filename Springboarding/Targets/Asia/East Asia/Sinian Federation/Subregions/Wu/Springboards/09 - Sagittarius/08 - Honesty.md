## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu traders vs. Shanghai's corporate world:** trust by kin and handshake against contracts and audits.
- **Huizhou and Ningbo merchant traditions:** two old business ethics, and how each lineage remembers them.
- **Shanghai natives vs. newcomers:** stereotypes of the "shrewd" Shanghainese and of "cunning" outsiders, to be tested, not repeated.
- **Rural Anhui and Subei villages:** honesty in face-to-face communities and in remittance families.
- **Students:** exam and thesis cheating at Wu's universities and in the high-school examination factories.
- **Hui Muslim food sellers:** honesty in halal labeling.
- **The She:** honesty norms in their own communities, as the She state them.
- **Platform sellers on Taobao (Hangzhou):** fake reviews and the trust systems built against them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business history, consumer complaints and platform credit; mixed for everyday honesty (few behavioral studies by city); thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Shanghainese for city slang and comic theater; Wenzhou speech for lending networks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** social-credit systems and blacklists; corruption cases; regional stereotypes. The legal side (fraud law, debtor lists, credit regulations) goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
