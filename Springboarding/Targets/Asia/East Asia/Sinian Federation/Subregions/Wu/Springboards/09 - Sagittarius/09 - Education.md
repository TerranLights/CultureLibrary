## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suzhou, Changzhou and Wuxi:** old exam-success counties; does that pride shape parenting now?
- **Shanghai's middle class vs. migrant families:** school places, district housing, and who may take the exams where.
- **Rural Anhui and northern Jiangsu:** boarding high schools and the gaokao as escape.
- **Huizhou and Tongcheng:** lineage and county traditions of learning.
- **Vocational-track students** in Jiangsu and Zhejiang factory towns: respect for the trades.
- **Returned sent-down youth who sat the 1977 exams:** education interrupted and regained.
- **Hui Muslims:** mosque schooling beside state schooling.
- **The She:** She language and culture in Jingning schools, as the She describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich (statistics, PISA, exam history, university archives); thinner for migrant children's own accounts, vocational students and the She.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; English for mission-college archives; Wu varieties and She for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant children's access; ideological content of curricula; academic freedom. Education law and admissions rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
