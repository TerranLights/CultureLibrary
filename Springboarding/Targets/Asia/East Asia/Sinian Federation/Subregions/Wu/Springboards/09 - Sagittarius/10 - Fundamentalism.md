# Wu: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Wu Chinese varieties; Hui Muslim usage (Arabic and Persian loanwords); English for mission and diaspora-published sources. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Registered Protestant congregations vs. restorationist and house-church networks** in Wenzhou, Taizhou and rural Anhui: literalism and separation from "the world".
- **Gedimu and reformist Hui congregations** in Nanjing, Yangzhou, Hefei and the Huai valley.
- **Huizhou lineages:** Neo-Confucian orthodoxy as lived rule, and its local critics.
- **Strict monastic and lay Buddhist groups** vs. temple-going majority.
- **Older Shanghainese who lived through 1966-1976:** political certainty as memory.
- **Urban parents choosing classics academies** vs. mainstream schools.
- **Hanfu and other revival communities:** purists against casual members.
- **The She:** whether outsiders' campaigns against "superstition" or missions touched their rites, as the She tell it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good history for the Taiping and Shanghai radicals; thin and polarized for present-day strict groups (sources are either state or advocacy); no survey scale of fundamentalism known by province.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Hui Muslim usage; Wu varieties for oral history; English for mission and diaspora-published sources (comparison only).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 邪教 label and banned groups; Islamic "extremism" campaigns; informants at real risk. Do not adopt either the state's or the advocates' labels as findings. Laws on banned groups go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
