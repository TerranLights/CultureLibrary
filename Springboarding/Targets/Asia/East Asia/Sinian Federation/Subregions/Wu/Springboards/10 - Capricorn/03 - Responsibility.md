## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fengyang and the Huaibei plain:** responsibility framed by land contracts and floods.
- **Suzhou and Wuxi:** lineage welfare and, today, environmental accountability around Taihu.
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** personal answerability for business and debt.
- **Shanghai:** official accountability after urban disasters.
- **Huizhou:** guarantors and lineage surety.
- **She villages:** group responsibility for hills and forests.
- **Hui Muslims:** responsibility to the congregation's poor (religious alms).
- **Migrant workers:** answering for families far away.
- **Zhoushan fishing crews:** a skipper answers for a boat and its crew.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classics, Huizhou documents and reform history; mixed for attitudes; thin for She and island communities.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Wu dialect for Shanghai speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster accountability (the 2011 rail crash, the 2010 fire) is sensitive, and reports may be hard to find; Xiaogang's story is official memory, so seek villagers' accounts too.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the 正始 entry of 《日知录》 and note who is said to answer for the realm.

4. In 《徽州千年契约文书》, sample contracts for the 中人 and 保人 clauses.
5. Search CSS releases for "政府责任" items by province.
