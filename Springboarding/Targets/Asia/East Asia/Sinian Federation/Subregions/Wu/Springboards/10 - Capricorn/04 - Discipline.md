## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai and Hangzhou middle-class families:** test-driven study discipline at its most intense.
- **Southern Jiangsu monastic centers (Zhenjiang, Yangzhou, Changzhou):** a strong tradition of monastic rule.
- **Wenzhou and Yiwu small-business households:** work discipline organized by the family.
- **Anhui and northern Jiangsu villages:** households where parents work away; who disciplines left-behind children?
- **Hui Muslims:** fasting and prayer routines.
- **She communities:** their own training practices.
- **Factory belts (Suzhou, Kunshan, Ningbo):** shop-floor discipline for migrant workers.
- **Military and police families** around Nanjing.
