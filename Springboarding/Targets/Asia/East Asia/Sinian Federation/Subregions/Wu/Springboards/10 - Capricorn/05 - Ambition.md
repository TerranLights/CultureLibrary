## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenzhou and Taizhou:** family-business venturing.
- **Ningbo and Shaoxing:** merchant and banking networks reaching into Shanghai.
- **Shanghai:** salaried professionals and the tension between climbing and lying flat.
- **Huizhou:** historic merchant lineages that put trade wealth into schooling.
- **Northern Jiangsu and Huaibei:** sending regions; ambition as leaving.
- **Hui Muslim newcomers in Yiwu:** language and trade as a ladder.
- **She communities:** enterprise in tea and tourism; standing within and beyond the county.
- **Women migrants** from Anhui's domestic-work counties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for merchant history, fiction and youth discourse; mixed for surveys by province; thin for She and Hui newcomers.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for drama and Song debates; Wu dialect (Shanghai, Wenzhou) for 《繁花》 and business talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lying-flat discourse is censored at times; Douban posts may vanish (use archives); wealth scandals and fled debtors involve real people.
- **Held for review:** none.
