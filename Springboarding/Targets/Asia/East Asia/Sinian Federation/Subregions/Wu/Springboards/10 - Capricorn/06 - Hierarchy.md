## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Shanghainese against Subei-origin families and newer migrants:** origin as rank inside one city.
- **Shaoxing and Ningbo:** the memory of outcast households.
- **River and boat communities** (Jiande, Tonglu, Zhoushan): rank by way of life.
- **Huizhou:** master and servant lineages in the same villages.
- **Manchu descendants** of the Hangzhou and Nanjing garrisons.
- **She communities:** ranked by Han neighbors in the past, by their own lineage order within.
- **Hui Muslims:** a minority's place in city ranks, and offices within the mosque.
- **Provincial capitals against county towns:** the ranking of places.
- **State-sector employees against private-sector workers:** "inside the system" as a rank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai's social history and Huizhou documents; mixed for present-day stratification by province; thin for outcast and boat groups in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for status law; Wu dialect for Shanghai and Shaoxing usage; Standard Chinese for statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descendants of outcast and servant groups may not wish to be named; slurs (江北人, 阿乡) must be described, not used; hukou inequality is politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
