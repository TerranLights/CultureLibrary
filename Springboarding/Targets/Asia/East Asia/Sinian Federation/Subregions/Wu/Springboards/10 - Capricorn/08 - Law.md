## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaoxing and Ningbo:** a tradition of legal advisers and its local memory.
- **Shanghai:** a former treaty port with layered foreign and Chinese courts, and today's specialized courts.
- **Taizhou and Wenzhou:** litigation records and informal finance at the edge of legality.
- **Hefei and Huaibei:** the popular ideal of the upright judge.
- **Huizhou:** lineage rules and contracts working beside the yamen.
- **She communities:** customary rules.
- **Hui Muslims:** religious law and mediation.
- **Migrant workers:** law as a tool or a trap far from home.
- **Online entrepreneurs in Hangzhou:** e-commerce disputes and platform rules as everyday law.
