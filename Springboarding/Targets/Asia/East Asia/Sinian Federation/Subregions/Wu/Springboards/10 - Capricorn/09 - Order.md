## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhuji and rural Zhejiang:** village-level settlement of disputes as the model.
- **Shanghai:** layered memories of concession police, gangs, work units and residents' committees.
- **Huaibei and northern Anhui:** a history of insecurity, self-defense and banditry.
- **Southern Jiangsu canal towns:** planned and gardened order.
- **Hangzhou and Suzhou:** data-driven order and scoring.
- **She villages:** compacts and lineage rules.
- **Hui Muslim neighborhoods:** mosque-centered order and relations with police.
- **Migrant enclaves and factory dormitories:** order kept by employers and landlords.
- **Fishing and island communities (Zhoushan):** order at sea and in port.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Republican Shanghai and official models (Fengqiao, grids); mixed for perceived safety; thin for She and migrant enclaves in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Wu dialect (Shanghai TV and residents' talk); Classical and Daoist liturgical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2022 lockdown, stability maintenance and surveillance are highly sensitive; posts were deleted (use archives); protect informants.
