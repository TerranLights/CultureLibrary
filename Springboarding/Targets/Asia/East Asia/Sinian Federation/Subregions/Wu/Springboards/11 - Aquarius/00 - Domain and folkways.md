# Wu: Community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), a member state of the Sinian Federation (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; traditional for older print); Wu Chinese (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Ningbo, Wenzhou and others); Jianghuai Mandarin (Nanjing, Hefei); Central Plains Mandarin (Xuzhou, Huaibei); Hui Chinese (Huizhou); She speech. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups, and what they hope for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shanghai's lane neighborhoods against Pudong's towers and the "new Shanghainese" (新上海人): settlement and who meets whom.
- Southern Jiangsu (Suzhou, Wuxi) against northern Jiangsu (Yangzhou, Yancheng, Huai'an, Xuzhou): the sharpest insider-outsider line.
- Northern Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Shaoxing, Ningbo) against southern Zhejiang (Wenzhou, Taizhou, Lishui): different kinship and enterprise patterns.
- Huizhou lineage villages in southern Anhui against the Huaibei plain, with its Central Plains speech and famine migration.
- The She of the Lishui and Wenzhou hills; Hui Muslims of Nanjing, Hefei, Bozhou and Shanghai (mosque-centered community).
- Fishing people of Zhoushan and Lake Tai: boat-based settlement.
- Emigrants from Wenzhou and Qingtian in Europe: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai, Hangzhou, Suzhou and Nanjing; mixed for northern Jiangsu and Anhui; thin for the She and the boat peoples. Wu's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; the sheets are written for Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu and Anhui. Federation-wide surveys are national samples, so provincial splits need care.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Wu dialects for oral sources; Japanese for scholarship on Jiangnan guilds and charity; Hui usage's Arabic and Persian loans.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** association registration, religious congregations (Wenzhou's 2014-2016 cross removals), the 2022 Shanghai lockdown, protests and Shanghai's Cultural Revolution are politically sensitive; online posts may vanish. Law on associations belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
