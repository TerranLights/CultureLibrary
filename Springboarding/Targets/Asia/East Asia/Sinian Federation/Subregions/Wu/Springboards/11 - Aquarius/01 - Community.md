# Wu: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Wu Chinese (Shanghainese, Suzhou, Ningbo, Shaoxing, Wenzhou); Jianghuai and Central Plains Mandarin; Hui Chinese; She speech. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shanghai lane-house residents against owners in gated estates (小区): neighbors known against neighbors unknown.
- Sunan collective villages against Wenzhou and Taizhou family-firm towns: whose welfare is shared, and how.
- Huizhou lineage villages (Xidi, Hongcun) against the Huaibei plain's villages, with their history of flight from flood and famine.
- Zhoushan fishing villages and Lake Tai fishing families.
- She villages of Jingning and Cangnan; Hui mosque communities of Nanjing, Hefei and Shanghai.
- Migrant workers in urban villages (城中村) of Suzhou, Hangzhou and Shanghai's outskirts: members or passers-through.
- Wenzhou and Qingtian emigrant hometowns, whose remittances build temples and roads; the emigrants abroad are comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Jiangnan charity history and Shanghai lane life; mixed for present-day neighborhood surveys (provincial samples); thin for She and boat communities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Japanese (Fuma); Shanghainese for oral history and farce.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2022 lockdown, grid management (网格化) and forced demolitions are sensitive; posts vanish. Residents' committees as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
