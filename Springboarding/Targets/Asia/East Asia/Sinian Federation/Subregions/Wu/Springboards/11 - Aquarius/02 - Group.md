# Wu: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu (吴), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Wu Chinese (Shanghainese, Ningbo, Wenzhou); Jianghuai Mandarin; Central Plains Mandarin (Huaibei, Xuzhou); Hui Chinese; She speech. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Ningbo, Shaoxing and Huizhou merchant networks: different kinds of origin group.
- Wenzhou and Taizhou lineage towns, with strong kin units, against Shanghai's nuclear households.
- The Huaibei wheat plain against Jiangnan's rice country, along the Huai River line.
- Shanghai natives against Subei migrants and newer arrivals: in-groups inside one city.
- The She, organized by surname and clan; Hui Muslims, organized around mosques.
- Factory workers by origin and trade in Shanghai, Wuxi and Nantong's textile mills.
- Jiuxing boat families and other hereditary out-groups of the rivers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Republican Shanghai (gangs, guilds, strikes); mixed for present-day group attitudes; thin for She and boat-people views in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Wenzhounese for oral sources; English for much of the labor scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution factions, gangs, lineage violence and religious groups are sensitive; the Panhu legend must be read in She voices first. Rules on parties and associations go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
