## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shanghai hukou holders against migrants without it: the plainest citizen-and-outsider line.
- Sunan and Hangzhou Bay against Subei, Huaibei and western Anhui: rich and poor regions.
- Women and men across the lineage country of southern Zhejiang and the cities, where son preference may differ.
- Descendants of the duomin and the river boat people: old status barriers.
- The She and the Hui: equal treatment set against group-specific policies, as each describes its own standing.
- Domestic workers and their employers; tech workers and their firms: rank inside the home and the office.
- Old Shanghai "upper corner" and "lower corner" districts: standing ranked by address.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income and regional gaps (yearbooks, CHIP); mixed for attitudes; thin and scattered for hereditary low-status groups.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Shanghainese and Shaoxing dialect for oral accounts; Japanese for part of the scholarship on Ming bondservants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hukou protests, ethnic policy and labor disputes are sensitive; descendants of low-status groups may not wish to be named. Equal treatment in law goes to Libra; rank and merit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
