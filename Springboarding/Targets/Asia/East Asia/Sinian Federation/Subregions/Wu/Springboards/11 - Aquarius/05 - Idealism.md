## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Yuyao-Shaoxing tradition of conscience-based action against the Suzhou literati's ideal of refined retreat: two scholarly models of the good life.
- Shanghai's New Culture and anarchist circles against Anhui's revolutionary bases in the Dabie Mountains: different idealisms of the early 1900s.
- The sent-down generation against the post-1990 generation: whether ideals were spent or never given.
- Hui scholars of Nanjing: ideals of right living written across two traditions.
- Wenzhou's Christians: religious ideals of a better society, in their own words.
- Hangzhou's tech founders and their staff: missions as stated and as lived.

## 4. Flags
