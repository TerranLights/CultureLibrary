## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shanghai's lane-born friendships (同学, 邻居) against white-collar newcomers who build friendships by activity: what a friend owes in each.
- Women's sworn sisterhoods in mill towns against men's sworn brotherhoods in merchant and gang settings: different uses of the formal bond.
- Yangzhou and Subei's daily bathhouse and teahouse rounds against Suzhou's pingtan teahouse regulars: how often friends must meet, and where.
- Wenzhou partners in business: where friendship and money meet.
- The She, whose song exchange ties friend and guest; Hui Muslims, whose friendships center on the mosque.
- Older women in square-dance groups against young people in online buddy groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and literary friendship; mixed for present-day surveys (few ask about friends directly); thin for minority friendship customs.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Mandarin; Shanghainese and Yangzhou speech for oral accounts; Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendship as connection (关系) shades into corruption, which belongs to Libra and Capricorn; same-sex friendship and romance in the Liang-Zhu story are read in contested ways.
- **Held for review:** none.
