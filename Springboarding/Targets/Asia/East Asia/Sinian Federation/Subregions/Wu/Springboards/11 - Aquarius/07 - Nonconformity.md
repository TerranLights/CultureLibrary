## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Shanghai, whose image as an open city is a claim to test, against the Huaibei plain and inland Anhui: does tightness rise away from the delta?
- Suzhou and Yangzhou's tradition of the eccentric artist against Shaoxing's tradition of the defiant critic.
- Wenzhou's Christians and house churches against Hangzhou's Buddhist and Daoist mainstream.
- Shanghai's queer communities against small-town Zhejiang and Jiangsu: how the cost of being openly different changes with place.
- Hui Muslims and the She: a group's own difference from the Han majority, and nonconformity within each.
- Young fans and musicians in Hangzhou and Shanghai against their parents' generation.
