## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Suzhou and Hangzhou's tradition of the cultured recluse against Huizhou's merchant lineages, whose men spent years far from home: withdrawal by choice set against distance for trade, to test which kind is praised.
- Shanghai's dense, anonymous neighborhoods against Zhejiang and Anhui villages where everyone is known: everyday reserve with strangers.
- Monastics and lay retreatants on Jiuhua, Putuo and Tiantai against the urban young who "lie flat": two kinds of letting go.
- Elders living alone in Shanghai against those in multi-generation rural homes.
- She and Hui mourning customs: how much grief is shown and by whom, in each community's own account.
- Professionals trained to keep distance (doctors, journalists) in Shanghai and Nanjing, and how their codes are taught.
