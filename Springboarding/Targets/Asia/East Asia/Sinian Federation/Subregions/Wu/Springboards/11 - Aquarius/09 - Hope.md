## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Families in poor Anhui counties (Jinzhai, Lu'an) against Shanghai families: hope placed in schooling and the exam, at different stakes.
- Huaibei farmers with a history of floods against delta towns: whether hope rests on the state, on luck or on leaving.
- Wenzhou's Christians, Ningbo's Maitreya devotees and Hangzhou's temple-goers: three religious hopes.
- Emigrant counties (Qingtian, Wenzhou) against non-emigrant ones: hope pinned on relatives abroad.
- The She and the Hui: each people's own hopes for its language, faith and young.
- Young workers in Hangzhou's tech firms against older laid-off workers: hope for the future set against hopes already spent.
