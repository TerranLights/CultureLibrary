*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huizhou lineages against Taihu gentry:** arch-building for widows was densest in Huizhou; compare the two.
- **Dabie Mountains and Jinzhai 金寨 (Lu'an):** an old revolutionary base with many dead; family memory against state honor.
- **Huai flood-plain villagers:** sacrifice asked of a place, not a person.
- **Parents of only children in Shanghai and Hangzhou:** the exam bargain in its urban form.
- **Migrant parents who left children in Anhui villages:** sacrifice by absence.
- **Tech workers:** sacrifice for the firm and its critics.
- **Buddhist monastics and lay devotees:** self-denial as practice.
- **Hui Muslims:** fasting and martyr language of their own.
- **Zhoushan fishing families:** men lost at sea and the widows who stayed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pre-modern martyrs, chaste women and state martyrs; mixed for exam families; thin for flood-storage villagers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for arches, gazetteers and histories; Standard Chinese; Jianghuai Mandarin for Anhui fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state martyr lists are curated; flood-storage compensation is a touchy topic; wartime and famine sacrifice are politically framed.
- **Held for review:** none.
