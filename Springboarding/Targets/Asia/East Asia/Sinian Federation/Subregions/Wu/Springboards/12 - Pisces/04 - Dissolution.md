## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Shanghai lilong residents moved to outer districts:** a neighborhood self taken apart.
- **Chun'an relocatees and their descendants:** a county dispersed, some to other provinces.
- **Young Shanghai and Hangzhou people who no longer speak Wu:** language shift within one family.
- **Hangzhou and Nanjing families of Manchu descent:** an identity that faded.
- **The She:** language shift toward Chinese and what is kept.
- **Hui Muslims:** quick earth burial against cremation drives; compare how each body is returned.
- **Villages around Anqing (southwest Anhui) under the 2014 burial reform:** a dispute over how the body should end *check*.
- **Chan and Pure Land practitioners:** self-loss sought in practice.
- **Club and festival crowds in Shanghai:** ecstatic merging, urban and young.
- **Dementia families:** "losing" a parent while the parent lives.
