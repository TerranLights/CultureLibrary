## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai natives and Subei descendants:** an old internal line, still heard in slurs and jokes.
- **Registered residents and newcomers in Shanghai and Hangzhou:** belonging measured by papers.
- **Huizhou lineage villages against Taihu water towns:** closed walls against canal-front houses; compare how each guards the household.
- **Southern and northern Jiangsu (苏南, 苏北):** the Yangtze as an inner border.
- **Wenzhou and the rest of Zhejiang:** a separate speech and a strong local network.
- **The She in their mountain villages:** the line between She and Han neighbors, as the She draw it.
- **Hui Muslims:** food and marriage lines.
- **Old concession districts and the old walled Chinese city:** colonial lines still visible in the street plan.
- **Young adults and their parents:** marriage pressure, phones and money.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai's history and online talk; mixed for Huizhou documents (abundant but hard to read); thin for She and village lines today.
