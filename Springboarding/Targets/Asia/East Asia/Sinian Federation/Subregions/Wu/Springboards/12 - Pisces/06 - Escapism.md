## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai white-collar workers and Anhui or Subei migrant laborers:** escapes priced by class (a weekend guesthouse against a cheap game room).
- **Elders in Shanghai, Suzhou and Hangzhou lanes:** mahjong, opera clubs and teahouses as daily relief.
- **Young players and readers:** games, online novels and murder-mystery clubs.
- **Women players of romance games:** a market built for them in Shanghai.
- **Rural northern Jiangsu and Anhui:** New Year gambling and drinking, as locals describe them.
- **Zhoushan and Ningbo fishing crews:** drink and long seasons at sea.
- **Hui Muslims:** abstention from alcohol, and other escapes.
- **Literati recluses against today's 逃离 urbanites:** compare the old ideal of withdrawal with the new rural move.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Shanghai's history and the digital trades; mixed for drink and gambling; thin for drug use (official numbers only).
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for recluse and pleasure-quarter texts; Standard Chinese; Wu speech for teahouse and mahjong talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and gambling are policed (as law, see Libra); 躺平 has been criticized in state media.
- **Held for review:** none.
