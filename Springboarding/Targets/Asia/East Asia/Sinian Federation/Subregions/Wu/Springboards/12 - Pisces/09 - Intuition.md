## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rice-growing south and wheat-growing north of the member state:** the test case for thinking-style research.
- **Huizhou geomancers and lineage elders:** reading land for houses and graves.
- **Chinese-medicine lineages of Suzhou and Changzhou against hospital doctors:** feel against protocol.
- **Shanghai retail investors and professional traders:** hunches against models.
- **Longjing and Anji tea growers and graders:** the senses trained by work.
- **Zhoushan fishermen:** reading weather and sea.
- **She healers:** a minority's own diagnostic knowledge.
- **Wenzhou entrepreneurs:** how they describe sensing an opening, in their own accounts; variation, not a type.
- **Chan and Neo-Confucian lineages:** sudden insight as a religious and moral ideal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for philosophy and medicine; mixed for divination (often hidden under 迷信); thin for She healing and fishermen's lore.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Wu speech for market and street terms; Huizhou speech for geomancers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is discouraged officially; some healers keep methods within families, respected and not pursued.
- **Held for review:** none.
