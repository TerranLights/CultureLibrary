*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu, Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese and other Wu varieties; Jianghuai and Zhongyuan Mandarin (Huaibei). **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shanghai's old underworld against today's white-collar fraud in Hangzhou and Wenzhou:** whose wrongs are condemned and whose are admired.
- **Huaibei and northern Jiangsu:** a long story of banditry and smuggling, as local histories tell it.
- **Coastal Zhejiang and Zhoushan:** smuggling and piracy in local memory.
- **Migrants in the big cities:** who gets blamed when something is stolen; record the claim, never adopt it.
- **Elderly targets of phone fraud:** the most feared crime of the 2020s.
- **Yiwu and Taobao traders:** what counts as wrong in a counterfeit economy.
- **Hefei and its Judge Bao cult:** a city that treats a judge as a local god.
- **Hui Muslims and other minorities in the cities:** how they are pictured in crime talk, and how they speak of wrongdoing inside their own communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Republican Shanghai; mixed for today (official crime figures are sparse); thin for village views.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese for slang; English for Settlement records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime statistics and court reporting are controlled; groups labeled 邪教 and political offenses are off limits and not pursued; what law says goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
