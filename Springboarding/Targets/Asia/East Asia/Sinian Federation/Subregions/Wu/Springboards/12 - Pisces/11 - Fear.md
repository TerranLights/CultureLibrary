# Wu (吴): Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Wu, Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Shanghai, Zhejiang, Jiangsu, Anhui). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Shanghainese, Wenzhou, Ningbo and Zhoushan speech; Jianghuai Mandarin; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Zhejiang and the Qiantang estuary:** typhoon and tide fears.
- **Huai flood-plain villages:** fear of water from the north side of the member state.
- **Zhoushan fishing families:** taboos and the lost-at-sea.
- **Wenzhou temple communities:** plague and demon processions, as they hold them now.
- **Shanghai elders and Shanghai youth:** fraud against social anxiety and exam fear.
- **Neighbors of the Qinshan plant:** living beside a feared technology.
- **Hui Muslims, Catholics and Protestants:** fear of God, hell and judgment in each tradition's own words.
- **Rural Anhui and Jiangsu families:** fright and soul-loss in children, and who is called to cure it.
- **The She:** night, mountain and spirit dangers, as She storytellers share them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and legend; mixed for surveys (official safety surveys exist, independent ones are few); thin for the She and fishing taboos in print.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Wu speech (Zhoushan, Wenzhou) for taboo words and tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2022 lockdown is censored; rumors (谣言) are punished, so fear talk online is guarded; public-safety surveys are political tools, to read critically.
- **Held for review:** none.
