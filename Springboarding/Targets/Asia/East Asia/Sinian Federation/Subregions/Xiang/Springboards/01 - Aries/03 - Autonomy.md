## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha young professionals:** rented rooms, dating apps and distance from parents.
- **Rural Old Xiang counties (Shuangfeng, Xinhua, Lianyuan):** lineage pressure on marriage and residence.
- **Out-migrant families in Shaoyang, Yongzhou and Huaihua:** parents far away; grandparents in charge of children.
- **Women of Jiangyong and nearby Yao villages:** sworn sisterhood and late moving to the husband's home as room for choice.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan:** chieftain memory and the autonomous prefecture.
- **Miao of Huayuan, Fenghuang and Chengbu:** courtship by choice at markets against family arrangement.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** village assemblies and the 款 tradition.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** an autonomous county; own rites of adulthood.
- **Hakka lineages of Rucheng and Guidong:** strong clan halls; compare lineage control with Xiang villages.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese working in Guangdong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1919 marriage debates and for left-behind-children research; mixed for minority self-rule; thin for measured personal autonomy by county.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tujia, Miao and Dong for village self-rule memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide in the Zhao case; family-planning enforcement and bodily autonomy; current limits on regional and ethnic self-rule.
- **Law:** autonomy law, marriage law and constitutions go to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
