## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shaodong and the Baoqing merchant counties:** trade abroad started through kin; compare with farming counties next door.
- **Xinhua:** a single-trade network of firms; initiative spread by fellow-villagers.
- **Pingjiang and Liuyang (Gan-speaking):** household workshops (snacks, fireworks) grown into industries.
- **Changsha:** media and tech founders; office cultures that reward or punish acting unasked.
- **Lianyuan and Loudi:** heavy-industry founders from mining towns.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia villages:** official programs that judge initiative from outside; labor exchange from inside.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** villagers who fund and build bridges and drum towers together.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** collective work and new tourism businesses.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese shopkeepers in Guangdong and beyond.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for firms, model figures and campaigns; mixed for village collective work; thin for measured everyday initiative at work.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and Gan listening for interviews; Tujia, Miao and Kam for work songs and donor customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** model-figure campaigns (Lei Feng) and poverty programs are official narratives; read with villagers' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** none.
