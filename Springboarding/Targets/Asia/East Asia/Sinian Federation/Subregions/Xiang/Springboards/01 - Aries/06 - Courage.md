**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang (Changsha, Old Xiang of Xiangxiang and Shuangfeng, home of many nineteenth-century soldiers); Southwestern Mandarin of Xiangxi; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Yao. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng and Lianyuan:** the recruiting ground of the Xiang Army; family memory of service.
- **Changsha and Hengyang:** cities that endured sieges; civic war memory.
- **Xinhua and Anhua (Meishan):** hunters' rites and a run of revolutionary pamphleteers and women soldiers.
- **Mining districts (Lengshuijiang, Loudi, Chenzhou):** courage as endurance in dangerous work.
- **Dongting Lake counties:** flood-fighting and fishermen's tales.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao:** blade-ladder feats, bandit lore, and memory of Qing campaigns.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Rucheng:** initiation ordeals.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** lore of village defense; compare with Miao frontier memory.
- **Women across regions:** whose endurance is called courage and whose is not.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese veterans who settled across the Taiwan Strait.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military history and memorials; mixed for minority ordeals; thin for measured attitudes to everyday moral courage.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Chu Ci and Qing histories; Tujia, Miao and Mien ritual language.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civil-war memory (whose dead are honored); suicide in martyr narratives; bandit-suppression campaigns; some initiation details may be restricted to initiates.
- **Held for review:** none.
