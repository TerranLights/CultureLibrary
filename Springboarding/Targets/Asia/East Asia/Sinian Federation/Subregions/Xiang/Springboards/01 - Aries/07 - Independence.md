# Xiang: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for village and household talk; Hakka for the lineage counties of the southeast; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong, Yao. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha young adults:** renting alone against living with parents; the 啃老 charge.
- **Out-migrant households of Shaoyang, Yongzhou and Huaihua:** independence earned far away; dependence at home on remittances.
- **Elders left in villages:** how they speak of relying on themselves.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong:** strong lineage estates as a safety net.
- **Xinhua terrace villages:** self-supply in farming and water.
- **Taoyuan and Changde:** a county named for the classical image of living apart; Hui families, whose mosque and kin networks may offer other forms of support.
- **Tujia and Miao of Xiangxi:** subsistence foods and the shift to wage work.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** village self-supply of timber and rice.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Longhui:** histories of moving to farm new hills.
- **Disabled people:** independence as a claim on services, not a refusal of them.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese migrants' remittance networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census, welfare and migration data; mixed for household-division documents (scattered); thin for measured attitudes to help-seeking by region.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang and Hakka for interviews; Tujia, Miao, Kam and Mien for subsistence vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** welfare stigma; ethnic subsistence practices framed by poverty programs; disability terms.
- **Law:** property and inheritance law belong to Libra; secession and provincial status to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
