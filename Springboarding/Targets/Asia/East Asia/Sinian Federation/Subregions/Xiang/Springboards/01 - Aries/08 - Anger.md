**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Changsha Xiang (rich in emotion and cursing words), Old Xiang, Gan, Southwestern Mandarin; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong, Yao. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** a dense city with its own dialect; check whether residents read loud speech as anger at all.
- **Old Xiang villages (Shuangfeng, Xiangxiang, Shaoyang):** lineage grudges and gentry ideals of restraint.
- **Dongting polder villages (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** anger over water and land.
- **Southern counties (Chenzhou, Yongzhou):** histories of feuds between lineages and between Hakka and older settlers.
- **Xiangxi Miao:** memory of uprisings and suppression; collective grievance.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan:** wedding laments that let anger be sung; compare with daily restraint.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** elders' dispute-settling customs.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** whether reconciliation customs exist after quarrels, and who leads them.
- **Women and men:** who may shout, who may sulk; anger at home.
- **Young urban people:** rap, online outrage, gaming chats.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese workers in coastal factories and their labor disputes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history of feuds and revolts; mixed for clinical studies; thin for measured display rules by subregion.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Changsha Xiang for slang; minority languages for emotion vocabulary.
