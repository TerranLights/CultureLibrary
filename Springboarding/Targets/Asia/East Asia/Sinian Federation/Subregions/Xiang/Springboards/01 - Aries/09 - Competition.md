## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha school families:** the sharpest exam rivalry; compare with county towns.
- **County high schools of Hengyang, Shaoyang and Huaihua:** striving to reach the provincial elite schools.
- **Yuanling and the Yuan River towns:** neighborhood and clan rivalry in dragon-boat racing.
- **Miluo and Yueyang:** racing framed by commemoration.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** village contests in bullfighting and music.
- **Miao of Huayuan and Fenghuang:** drumming and singing contests.
- **Tujia of Longshan and Yongshun:** sport and weightlifting traditions to check, and festival games.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** festival contests at the King Pan gathering, to check.
- **Card and tile players across regions:** different local rules as small identities.
- **Changsha, Zhuzhou and Xiangtan:** a city cluster that cooperates and competes.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese students sitting exams elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, media contests and festivals; mixed for village contests; thin for measured attitudes to competition by region.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Kam and Miao for festival terms; Xiang for card-game talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling; animal welfare in bullfights; the publicizing of top exam scorers has been restricted at times.
- **Held for review:** none.
