## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Liuyang and Liling:** fireworks work where danger is a living; compare workers' and owners' talk.
- **Zhangjiajie:** daredevilry staged for tourists; what locals make of it.
- **Mining districts (Lengshuijiang, Chenzhou, Loudi):** risk taken for wages.
- **Changsha nightlife:** drinking, driving and street racing among young men and women.
- **Jishou and Fenghuang:** families caught in the fund-raising collapse.
- **Rural southern and western villages:** underground betting waves.
- **Dongting and Yuan River communities:** water risk; boatmen and children who swim.
- **Tujia and Miao hosts:** drinking customs as tests between host and guest.
- **Dong and Yao villages:** whether bullfights, hunts or initiations carry their own lines between daring and rashness.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Hunanese migrants in risky construction and mining jobs elsewhere.
