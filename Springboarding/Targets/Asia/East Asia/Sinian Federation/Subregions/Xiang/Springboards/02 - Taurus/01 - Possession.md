# Xiang (湘): Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and Old Xiang dialects; Gan and Southwestern Mandarin varieties of Hunan; Tujia, Western Hunan Miao, Dong and Yao languages (mostly reached through Chinese-language sources). **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha apartment households**: small flats, resale apps, decluttering talk; set against village homes with storage lofts.
- **Dongting lake and river households** (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde): boat dwellers and fishers who moved ashore after the 2020 fishing ban; what a boat-home held.
- **Old Xiang heartland** (Loudi, Shaoyang, Shuangfeng): lineage villages with ancestral halls, where shared things sit beside private ones.
- **Tujia communities** (Yongshun, Longshan, Sangzhi, Zhangjiajie): dowry and wedding goods in song.
- **Miao communities** (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Chengbu): silver as kept property.
- **Dong communities** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): drum-tower villages and communal objects.
- **Yao and Nüshu-area villages** (Jiangyong, Jianghua): women's written keepsakes and burial customs.
- **Waxiang speakers** (Yuanling, Luxi): a little-studied group; check whether separate customs are recorded.
- **Hui families of Changde and Shaoyang**: Islamic custom for a dead person's goods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for excavated and Han literary sources, thin for present-day minority households outside ICH framing.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang dialect dictionaries; minority sources mostly in Chinese translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral customs and the dead's belongings; Nüshu as a heritage industry (authenticity disputes); hoarding among the elderly as a family matter.
- **Held for review:** none.
