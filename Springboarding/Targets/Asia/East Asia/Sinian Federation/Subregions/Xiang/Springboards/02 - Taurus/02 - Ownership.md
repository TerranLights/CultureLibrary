# Xiang (湘): Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese (deeds, genealogies, charters); Xiang dialect place and land words; Mien and other Yao speech; Tujia, Miao and Dong terms reached through Chinese-language ethnography. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Xiang lineage villages** (Shuangfeng, Xiangxiang, Shaoyang): hall estates and lineage control of land sales.
- **Dongting polder settlers** (Yuanjiang, Nanxian, Huarong): land made from the lake, with no ancient title.
- **Yao mountain lineages** (Jianghua, Jiangyong, Longhui's Huayao): claims by charter and by clearing.
- **Former tusi domains** (Yongshun, Longshan, Baojing): chieftain land turned into registered household land.
- **Dong and Miao forest villages** (Tongdao, Huitong, Jingzhou, Chengbu): forests held by village and by household.
- **Changsha flat owners and migrant renters**: owners' committees against tenants with short leases.
- **Bai of Sangzhi**: settler origin stories and their claim to land.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang**: mosque property held for the community.
- **Hunanese outside the member state**: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and lineage sources; mixed for minority tenure, often described by outsiders; contested for land reform.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Yao charters may mix Chinese with Yao readings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform and its violence; land seizures and demolitions today; lineage disputes. The letter of property law goes to Libra; the spirit of legal culture goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
