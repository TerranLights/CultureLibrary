# Xiang (湘): Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (Song to Qing philosophy, academy plaques); Tujia, Miao and Dong oral texts in Chinese transcription and translation; Xiang dialect sayings. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Scholar-heritage centers** (Changsha, Xiangtan, Hengyang): academies, statesmen's homes, a strong written tradition; test how far it reaches ordinary households.
- **Old Xiang lineage villages** (Shuangfeng, Lianyuan, Xinhua): family instructions in genealogies; compare stated and lived rules.
- **Young urban professionals of Changsha**: consumer and career priorities against parents' generation.
- **Buddhist and Daoist pilgrims; Christian congregations in Changsha and Changde**: religious rankings of value.
- **Tujia** (Longshan, Yongshun): values carried by ritual specialists' songs.
- **Miao** (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Chengbu): oral lore and village rules.
- **Dong** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): kuan alliances' precepts.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong**: ancestral-king festival 盘王节 and its teaching.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang**: Islamic ethics in trade and family.
- **Hunanese elsewhere**: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite philosophy and official messaging; mixed for survey data (national samples, small provincial cells); minority oral texts mostly in edited Chinese versions.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tujia and Miao terms where editions give them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official slogans and model figures are state messaging; read them as such. Religious practice is politically sensitive. Provincial "spirit" talk invites stereotype; record variation.
- **Held for review:** none.
