## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dongting flood-plain households** (Huarong, Yuanjiang, Anxiang): the yearly flood watch.
- **Former lake fishers**: security lost by policy, not by disaster.
- **Xiangxi mountain counties** (Huayuan, Baojing, Guzhang): long histories of banditry, poverty programs and fundraising scandals.
- **Dong villages** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): fire as the village's great danger.
- **Changsha pensioners against migrant workers**: full urban social insurance against little or none.
- **Rural elderly in Old Xiang counties**: family as insurance when sons work far away.
- **Southern Hunan** (Chenzhou, Yongzhou): ice-storm memory; Yao and Hakka uplands with their own storage habits.
- **Miao of the old frontier**: the Southern Great Wall from the other side.
- **Hui of Changde**: mosque and community support.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, floods and modern disasters; mixed for minority practice; thin for local accounts of 1950s campaigns.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fundraising collapses and unrest; food-safety scandals; banditry suppression campaigns; frontier violence against the Miao. Public order as policing belongs to other signs; keep to livelihood and protection.
