**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for genealogies and inscriptions; Xiang, Gan and Hakka speech for settlement lore; Yao, Dong and Tujia village life through Chinese-language fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old lineage villages** (Yueyang's Zhangguying, Shuangfeng, Xiangxiang): continuity of place and name.
- **Migrant-sending counties** (Shaoyang, Loudi, Yongzhou): stability kept at home while the working generation lives elsewhere.
- **Industrial towns of Zhuzhou, Xiangtan and Lianyuan**: lifetime jobs, then layoffs.
- **Changsha newcomers and renters**: a fast-changing city.
- **Dongting polder settlers**: land only a few generations old, and floods that move whole villages.
- **Dong villages** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): drum towers and rebuilding after fire.
- **Tujia of the former chieftain domains**: long political continuity, then sudden change with direct rule in the 1720s.
- **Yao** (mountain-crossing and lowland groups): mobility against settlement inside one people.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong**: later arrivals with their own origin stories.
- **Hui of Changde**: Ming-era settlers and their mosques.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration statistics and lineage records; mixed for minority village continuity; factory histories are official.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Yao and Dong terms through Chinese sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political stability (维稳) is a state security term; keep to everyday continuity. Demolition and relocation are sensitive. Genealogical origin claims are often legendary; test them.
- **Held for review:** none.
