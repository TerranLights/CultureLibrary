*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for coinage, merchant and gazetteer records; Changde Southwestern Mandarin for the flower-drum repertoire; Miao and Tujia practice through Chinese-language ethnography. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's new rich** (machinery, media, tea-drink and food brands): fortunes made since the 1990s.
- **Rice farmers of the Dongting plain**: the old granary wealth of the province against today's farm incomes.
- **Mining towns** (Lengshuijiang, Changning, Huayuan's manganese district): wealth from the ground and its costs.
- **Craft towns of the east** (Liuyang fireworks, Liling porcelain): workshop fortunes.
- **Shaoyang and Xinhua traders**: a long trading tradition, now in small business across China.
- **Xiangxi and Wuling poverty counties**: poverty and its official end.
- **Miao of Huayuan and Fenghuang**: wealth shown through feasts.
- **Dong and Yao villages**: rice, timber and tea as wealth.
- **Hui families of Changde**: beef and rice-noodle trades.
- **Remittance households in sending counties**: wealth earned far away and spent at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official poverty and income data and for rich lists; mixed for merchant history; thin for minority reckonings of wealth outside ICH and older surveys.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty statistics and showcase villages are official messaging; land-reform labels of "rich"; mining pollution. The government-spending ownership measure sits under Ownership.
