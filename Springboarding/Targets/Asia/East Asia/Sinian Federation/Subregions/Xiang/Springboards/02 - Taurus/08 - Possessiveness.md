*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang dialect speech for stinginess and jealousy; classical Chinese for lineage feuds in gazetteers; Miao, Dong and Tujia courtship and sharing customs through Chinese-language fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dongting fishers and lake communities**: claims over waters, before and after the fishing ban.
- **Old Xiang lineage villages**: hills, graves and water guarded by lineage.
- **Meishan hunting villages** (Xinhua, Anhua): sharing rules that limit keeping.
- **Young urban couples in Changsha**: phones, location sharing, talk of control (PUA).
- **Rural households with absent migrant spouses**: jealousy across distance.
- **Miao and Dong courtship cultures**: whether claims on partners begin earlier or later; check each community's own account.
- **Tujia families**: bride's departure songs and kin attachment.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong**: lineage halls and land guarding.
- **Hui of Changde**: family and marriage norms within the faith.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; family-violence and psychology data exist but are national in design; gazetteer feuds are rich; minority courtship is often romanticized in tourism.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang dialect for envy and stinginess words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence; lineage violence; ethnic courtship customs exoticized by outsiders; the letter of family and property law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
