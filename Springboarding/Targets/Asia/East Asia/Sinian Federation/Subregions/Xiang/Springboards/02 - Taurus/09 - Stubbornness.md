# Xiang (湘): Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and Old Xiang dialect words for obstinacy and grit; classical Chinese for Han-era and Qing texts; Miao and Tujia history mostly through Chinese and English scholarship. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Xiang heartland** (Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng, Lianyuan): the Xiang Army's recruiting ground; source of much of the "mule" image.
- **Changsha**: urban professionals; nail-house conflicts; whether the label is used with pride or irony.
- **Gan-speaking east** (Liuyang, Pingjiang): reformers and revolutionaries with separate local memory.
- **Miao of the old frontier** (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Jishou): resistance remembered on both sides.
- **Tujia** (Yongshun, Baojing, Longshan): soldier traditions and chieftain-era memory.
- **Dong** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): consensus customs that may handle refusal differently.
- **Women**: who is called 倔 and who 有主见 (has her own mind).
- **Elders in villages**: refusing relocation, new farming or new technology.
- **Hui of Changde**: religious steadfastness under pressure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for biography and military history; thin for everyday measured stubbornness; regional personality studies are uneven.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Changsha dialect lexicography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Hunanese are stubborn" is a stock stereotype; record variation and test it, never assume it. Purges, demolition holdouts and frontier wars are politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Platt's introduction and chapter on the making of Hunanese identity, and list the texts he cites.
2. Look up 霸蛮 and 犟 in the Changsha dialect dictionary and note register and examples.
3. Search CNKI for 湖南 区域人格 大五 for measured comparisons.
4. Search news archives for 长沙 钉子户 and code how each holdout is described.
5. Search CNKI for 侗款 议事 for accounts of decision-making and dissent.
