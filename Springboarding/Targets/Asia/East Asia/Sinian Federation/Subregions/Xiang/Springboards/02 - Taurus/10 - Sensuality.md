## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha**: night snacks, song halls, foot baths; pleasure as public city life.
- **Xiangtan**: areca-nut chewing as a local habit.
- **Changde**: rice-noodle mornings, with Hui beef noodle shops.
- **Dongting lake towns** (Yueyang, Yiyang): fish dishes and lake views.
- **Eastern and central tea country** (Taojiang, Anhua, Xiangyin): drinks served to guests.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong**: hot springs and their own cooking.
- **Tujia and Bai of Sangzhi and Zhangjiajie**: song and dance as pleasure.
- **Dong and Miao of the southwest and west**: sour, smoked and glutinous foods.
- **Hui families**: halal food and abstaining from alcohol.
- **Buddhist pilgrims and vegetarians at Nanyue**: restraint of the senses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, media and trade; mixed for touch and bodily comfort; thin for minority accounts of pleasure apart from tourist framing.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Changsha and Changde dialect food words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** areca-nut health harms; massage trades linked in the press to sex work; sex itself belongs to Scorpio. Avoid exoticizing minority foods and songs.
