**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Putonghua) and the Xiang 湘语 varieties; also Gan, Southwestern Mandarin, Hakka, southern Hunan Tuhua 土话, Waxiang 瓦乡话; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Kam (Dong) and Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** the domain of communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech ways, learning ways, neighbors, short journeys).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huxiang Han of the Changsha–Zhuzhou–Xiangtan core:** New Xiang speech; the hub of schools and media.
- **Old Xiang areas (Loudi, Shaoyang, Shuangfeng):** another dialect subgroup, rural market networks.
- **Hengyang (Hengzhou subgroup):** a second urban pole.
- **Eastern Gan speakers (Liuyang, Pingjiang, Liling, Chaling *check*):** Han neighbors with another Chinese language.
- **Hakka pockets (Rucheng, Guidong *check*):** migrant-lineage speech.
- **Southwestern Mandarin northwest (Changde, Zhangjiajie, Huaihua):** different everyday speech.
- **Southern Tuhua areas (Yongzhou, Chenzhou):** poorly classified varieties; home of Nüshu.
- **Waxiang speakers (Yuanling, Luxi, Guzhang, Chenxi):** a small, unclassified variety.
- **Tujia of Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** compare language-keeping villages with shifted ones.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Jishou) vs. Chengbu and Suining Miao:** different varieties and histories.
- **Dong of Tongdao, Xinhuang, Zhijiang, Jingzhou:** assembly and song traditions.
- **Yao of Jianghua vs. Huayao 花瑶 of Longhui and Xupu:** separate Yao groups.
- **Bai of Sangzhi, Hui of Changde, and the Uyghur-descended families of Taoyuan *check*:** small communities with their own histories of arrival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han dialectology, Huxiang intellectual history and the Nüshu case; mixed for Tujia and Miao everyday speech; thin for Waxiang, Bai and the Taoyuan Uyghur-descended families. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sheet is written for Hunan.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional for older works); reading knowledge of Xiang dialect writing; Tujia, Miao, Kam and Mien materials often appear in IPA or in pinyin-based scripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority-language status and ethnic classification are politically sensitive in the Sinian Federation; early ethnographies use outsider categories. Nüshu is now a heritage brand; separate living practice from tourism displays.
- **Held for review:** none.
