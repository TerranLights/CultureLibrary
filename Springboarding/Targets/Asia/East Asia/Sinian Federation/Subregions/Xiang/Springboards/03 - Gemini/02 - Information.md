# Xiang: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for early records; Xiang dialect terms; Xiangxi Miao and Tujia for knowledge keepers' vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how people gather, pass on, trust, withhold and use it: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Chang-Zhu-Tan cities:** dense media and digital access; the provincial broadcasters sit here.
- **Mountain counties of the Wuling 武陵 and Xuefeng 雪峰 ranges:** signal gaps, distance from newsstands, reliance on word of mouth.
- **Dongting Lake 洞庭湖 communities (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde shores):** weather and water-level information as daily need; flood-season warnings.
- **Southern Hunan (Chenzhou, Yongzhou):** news ties with Guangdong through migration.
- **Xiangxi Miao villages:** specialists' recited knowledge beside state media.
- **Tujia villages of Yongshun, Longshan and Sangzhi:** the tima's knowledge and its present standing.
- **Dong villages of Tongdao:** drum-tower notices and village announcements.
- **Elders against students:** forwarded WeChat posts against video platforms; a generation gap in sources.
- **Migrant workers and their left-behind families:** information carried by phone between city and village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for early records (excavated documents) and the reform-era press; mixed for present-day news use at province level (much survey data is national); thin for minority-language media.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese and paleography for the slips; English for customs reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship, unofficial publications and Cultural Revolution records are politically sensitive in the Sinian Federation; archives may be closed. Ritual specialists' knowledge may be restricted by the communities themselves; ask before recording.
- **Held for review:** none.
