# Xiang: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and southern Tuhua terms for kin and sworn ties; Kam (Dong), Miao and Tujia terms for visiting and ritual kinship. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha professionals:** classmate and workplace networks against kin ties.
- **Lineage villages of central Hunan (Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng, Liuyang):** dense genealogy-based ties.
- **Migrant-sending counties (Xinhua, Shaodong, Youxian *check*):** chain ties that run out of the county and back.
- **Southern Hunan (Jiangyong, Daoxian):** women's sworn ties in the Nüshu tradition.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** group visiting between villages.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia:** godparent customs, market-day meetings and clan ties.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Huayao of Longhui:** marriage circles and ritual kin.
- **Hui of Changde and the Taoyuan Uyghur-descended families *check*:** ties held through mosque and lineage.
- **Rural empty-nest elders:** isolation against the reach of phone and WeChat.
- **Hakka communities of the southeast:** lineage halls and links across the Jiangxi and Guangdong borders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineages and Xiang Army networks; mixed for present-day gift exchange (village studies, not provincial data); thin for minority ritual kinship outside folklore files.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for genealogies; dialect and Tuhua for kin and sworn-tie terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies may be private family property; ask before copying. Discussions of 关系 can touch on corruption; keep to social ties. Home-county trade networks are comparison only for the post-war target.
- **Held for review:** none.
