# Xiang: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; southern Hunan Tuhua and Xiangnan Mandarin (two-variety speakers); Tujia, Xiangxi Miao and Kam (Dong) for mask and identity terms. **Concept in one line:** how people live with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha bilinguals in dialect and Putonghua:** the everyday home/public split.
- **Southern Hunan Tuhua speakers (Yongzhou, Chenzhou):** two Chinese varieties by setting.
- **Xiangxi Miao on both sides of the old frontier wall:** a historic line between "inside" and "outside" communities.
- **Tujia families of Yongshun, Longshan and Sangzhi:** re-registered identities and the chieftain/state memory.
- **Dong of Xinhuang:** Nuo masks and Dong–Han two-language life.
- **Bai of Sangzhi:** a small group with a registered identity and a Chinese-speaking present.
- **Hui of Changde:** Muslim and Hunanese at once.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** mountain and plains Yao, often named as a pair *check*.
- **Rural–urban migrants:** village registration and city lives.
- **Children of inter-ethnic marriages:** one registered box for two heritages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical philosophy and Qing frontier history; mixed for present-day identity (much sensitive, much in policy literature); thin for Bai and Hui two-identity life in Hunan.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Tuhua material in IPA; Miao and Kam terms for masks and rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification and registration changes are politically sensitive in the Sinian Federation. Qing labels such as 生苗 and 熟苗 are outsider categories; never use them as descriptions. Masked rites may hold restricted parts.
- **Held for review:** none.
