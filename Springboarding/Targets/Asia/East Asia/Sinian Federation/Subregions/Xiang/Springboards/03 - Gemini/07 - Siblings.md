*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (letters, genealogies); Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin kin terms; Tujia, Miao, Kam (Dong) and Mien (Yao) kin vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Hunan Han lineages (Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng, Xiangtan):** eldest-brother authority and lineage ranking.
- **Changsha only-child generation:** families with one child, and cousins as near-siblings.
- **Northwest Mandarin-speaking areas (Changde, Zhangjiajie):** different kin terms for the youngest.
- **Hakka of the southeast:** large lineage halls and brothers' shares.
- **Tujia of Xiangxi:** bridal laments addressed to siblings.
- **Xiangxi Miao:** sibling ancestors in myth; mother's-brother rights in marriage.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Jingzhou:** cross-cousin marriage customs and their decline.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** sibling flood myths and lineage naming.
- **Jiangyong women:** chosen sisters.
- **Migrant families:** elder siblings who left to work and paid for younger ones' schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lineage and elite letters; mixed for present-day sibling care (village studies); thin for twin customs and for Bai and Hui families.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect kin terms; IPA sources for Miao, Kam and Mien kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-planning history is politically sensitive in the Sinian Federation; origin myths may be sacred to the peoples who tell them. Household division can touch on law: keep to custom here and send law questions to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
