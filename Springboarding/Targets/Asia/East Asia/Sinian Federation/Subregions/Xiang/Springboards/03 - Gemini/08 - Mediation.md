# Xiang: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (lineage rules, contracts); Xiang dialect terms for wrangling and peacemaking; Miao, Kam (Dong) and Tujia terms for elders and reasoners. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). Statutes and court mediation belong to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Hunan lineage villages (Xiangxiang, Liuyang, Hengshan):** lineage elders and ancestral halls as the first stop.
- **Changsha neighborhoods:** residents' committees, property managers and television mediators.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Jishou):** the 理老 tradition and its present standing.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Jingzhou:** village elders and alliance customs.
- **Tujia of Yongshun, Longshan and Sangzhi:** village go-betweens after the chieftain era.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Huayao of Longhui:** elder councils and marriage go-betweens.
- **Hui of Changde:** the mosque's role between families *check*.
- **Dongting Lake fishing and farming communities:** disputes over water and fishing grounds.
- **Hakka villages of the southeast:** lineage-based settlement across the provincial line.
- **Migrant workers and their families:** brokers between village and city.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Miao and Dong customary practice in ethnography; mixed for Han lineage practice (rules survive, practice less recorded); thin for Hui and Bai communities and for urban informal go-betweens.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Miao and Kam terms (in Chinese transcription, sometimes IPA); dialect for everyday wrangling words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** this sheet covers custom only. State mediation statutes, courts and contracts as law belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Ethnic customary practice is sometimes framed by officials as "backward"; read such framing critically.
- **Held for review:** none.
