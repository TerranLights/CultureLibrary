# Xiang 湘: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang 湘语 and its subgroups, Gan, Southwestern Mandarin, Hakka, Tuhua 土话, Waxiang 瓦乡话; Northern Tujia, Xiangxi Miao (Qo Xiong), Kam (Dong), Mien and other Yao languages. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, home and family: what home is, and who counts as family, with its building, family and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huxiang 湖湘 heartland (Changsha, Xiangtan, Zhuzhou, Yiyang):** urban households and the gentry-letter tradition of family instruction.
- **Lou-Shao hill country (Loudi, Shaoyang, the Meishan 梅山 area):** Old Xiang speech and dense lineage villages.
- **Dongting Lake plain (Yueyang, Changde):** polder settlement and flood-shaped households.
- **Eastern Gan- and Hakka-speaking counties (Liuyang 浏阳, Pingjiang, Rucheng 汝城, Yanling):** different lineage origins and house forms.
- **South (Hengyang, Chenzhou, Yongzhou):** Tuhua speakers; Jiangyong 江永 and its women's script.
- **Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** Tujia and Miao households; Bai of Sangzhi 桑植; Waxiang speakers of Yuanling, Luxi and Chenxi.
- **Huaihua and Shaoyang uplands:** Dong (Tongdao, Xinhuang, Zhijiang), Miao of Chengbu, Jingzhou and Mayang.
- **Jianghua 江华 and the south:** Yao households.
- **Hui (Changde, Shaoyang) and the Uyghur families of Taoyuan 桃源:** Muslim households inside Hunan.
- Hunanese migrants elsewhere: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Hunan only, a working list the author still confirms; there is no scope file yet, so this set of sheets is written for Hunan's lands.
- **Data:** rich for Han lineages, gazetteers and census; mixed for Tujia, Dong and Yao in their own words; thin for Waxiang and Tuhua communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Changsha and Lou-Shao Xiang; Tujia, Qo Xiong Miao, Kam and Mien for community terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-planning history; left-behind children; ethnic classification disputes; family altar and ancestral rites, shared only as families choose.
- **Held for review:** none.
