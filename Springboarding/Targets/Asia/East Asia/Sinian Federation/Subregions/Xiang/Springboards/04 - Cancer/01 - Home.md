# Xiang 湘: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang varieties (Changsha, Loudi, Shaoyang, Hengyang); Gan and Hakka of the eastern counties; Southwestern Mandarin; Waxiang; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Kam and Mien. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Chang-Zhu-Tan 长株潭 cities:** flat ownership, demolition and the housing market.
- **Lou-Shao and Hengyang villages:** the old house 老屋 left behind by migrants, and the new house built with their wages.
- **Dongting Lake polders and fishing families:** homes shaped by water and floods.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao:** stilt houses and hearth platforms; relocation from mountain villages.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** wooden villages, fire risk and rebuilding.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Nanling 南岭 hills:** upland homes and resettlement downhill.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Yanling:** a separate house tradition and story of arrival.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** homes around the mosque quarter.
- Hunanese migrants in Guangdong and elsewhere: comparison only, as the people who say "back home".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban housing statistics and literature; mixed for village homes; thin for boat families and Waxiang speakers.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang dialect words for house and home; Tujia, Miao and Kam for hearth and house terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced demolition and relocation; poverty-relief resettlement; wartime loss.
- **Held for review:** none.
