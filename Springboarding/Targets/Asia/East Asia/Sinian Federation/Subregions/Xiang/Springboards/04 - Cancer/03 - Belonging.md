# Xiang 湘: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and other Xiang varieties (where accent marks insiders); Gan, Hakka, Southwestern Mandarin, Tuhua and Waxiang; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Kam, Mien. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha natives and newcomers:** dialect and accent as the test of who is local.
- **Lou-Shao and Hengyang hill counties:** strong home-village loyalty among people who work away.
- **Eastern Gan and Hakka counties:** belonging to Hunan through a different speech.
- **Tujia of Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** belonging redrawn by classification in the 1950s and 1980s.
- **Xiangxi Miao:** festival gatherings and the Miao-Han border in local memory.
- **Waxiang speakers of Yuanling, Luxi and Chenxi:** a group whose category is still argued.
- **Dong, Yao and Bai communities:** belonging to village leagues, clan charters and distant origin places.
- **Hui and the Uyghurs of Taoyuan:** belonging to a faith and a province together.
- **Migrants into Changsha from other provinces:** the newcomer's view.
- Hunanese migrants and students outside Hunan: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for provincial-identity history and migrant surveys; mixed for minority self-accounts; thin for Waxiang and Tuhua communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and other Xiang dialects; Tujia, Miao, Kam, Mien for self-names and festival terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification and recognition claims; Hui and Uyghur identity; provincial self-rule history; household registration as a line of exclusion.
- **Held for review:** none.
