# Xiang 湘: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, the Sinian Federation member state covering Hunan 湖南, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (medical and family texts); Xiang dialects for child-rearing words and nursery rhymes; Gan, Hakka and Southwestern Mandarin areas; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Kam and Yao song and custom terms. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and other cities:** small families, paid childcare and the education race.
- **Out-migration counties of Shaoyang, Hengyang, Yongzhou and Huaihua:** grandparents as the main nurturers.
- **Dongting Lake farming villages:** children's work and family farming as teaching.
- **Tujia and Miao of Xiangxi:** carriers, songs and birth customs distinct from Han practice.
- **Dong of the southwest:** children taught songs and village duties in a song-centered culture.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** early rites and upbringing in upland villages.
- **Hakka and Gan counties:** separate birth customs and lullaby traditions.
- **Hui families:** religious upbringing alongside state schooling.
- Hunanese parents raising children in coastal cities: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for left-behind and grandparent care, school pressure and public health; mixed for minority customs; thin for upbringing in Waxiang and Tuhua communities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang dialect words for children and rhymes; Tujia, Miao and Kam for song texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child welfare in left-behind families; the one-child era; corporal discipline; children's images online.
- **Held for review:** none.
