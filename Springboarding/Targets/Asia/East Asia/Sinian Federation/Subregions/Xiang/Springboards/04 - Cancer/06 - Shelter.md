*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Hunan 湖南 as a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (gazetteers, charity records); Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin place and building words; Tujia, Kam, Miao and Mien building and travel terms. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dongting Lake plain:** shelter shaped by floods, evacuation and resettlement.
- **Changsha and the Chang-Zhu-Tan cities:** rough sleepers, migrant housing and public rental flats.
- **Tujia of Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** stilt houses on steep slopes.
- **Miao of Huayuan, Fenghuang and Mayang:** rebuilt villages and relocation housing.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** wooden villages, covered bridges and fire risk.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Nanling hills:** the move from shifting shelter to fixed villages.
- **Southern Hunan along the old roads to Guangdong:** roadside pavilions and travelers' lodging.
- **Hengshan pilgrimage country:** lodging for pilgrims.
- **Waxiang communities of Yuanling and Luxi:** river-valley houses, little studied.
- Hunanese migrants in coastal dormitories: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for floods, wartime relief and state resettlement; mixed for charity history; thin for rough sleeping and for minority shelter words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Kam, Tujia, Miao and Mien for building and shelter terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced relocation and demolition; the old detention-and-return system for vagrants 收容遣送 (abolished 2003); wartime atrocities.
- **Held for review:** none.
