# Xiang 湘: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese (gazetteer biographies); Xiang dialect words for tending the sick and old; Gan, Hakka and Southwestern Mandarin areas; Miao and Tujia healing vocabularies; Kam and Mien mutual-help terms. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and other cities:** paid aides, elder homes and hospital companion care.
- **Out-migration villages of Shaoyang, Loudi, Hengyang and Yongzhou:** old people caring for each other and for grandchildren.
- **Dongting Lake farming communities:** neighbors' help in flood and illness.
- **Tujia and Miao of Xiangxi:** healers and family care alongside state clinics.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** village mutual help as a form of care.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** upland villages far from hospitals.
- **Hakka and Gan counties:** how far lineage networks still share the care of the old.
- **Hui families:** washing and burying the dead within the faith community.
- **Bai of Sangzhi:** care customs of a small, distinct people.
- Hunanese care workers in other provinces: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for aging surveys and civil-affairs statistics; mixed for healers and funeral care; thin for minority-language terms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang dialects; Miao and Tujia for healing terms; Kam and Mien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder neglect; healers' knowledge kept within families or lineages; care-home quality; funeral rites shared only with permission.
- **Held for review:** none.
