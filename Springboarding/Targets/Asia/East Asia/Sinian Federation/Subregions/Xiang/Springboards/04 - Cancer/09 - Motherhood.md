# Xiang 湘: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; the Nüshu 女书 script and the Tuhua it records (Jiangyong); Xiang dialect words for mother; Gan, Hakka and Southwestern Mandarin; Tujia, Miao, Kam and Yao song terms. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiangyong and the Xiao River 潇水 valley:** a women's script, sworn sisterhood and bridal books.
- **Changsha and other cities:** mothers in paid work, small families and online mothering.
- **Out-migration counties:** mothers who migrate, and grandmothers who mother in their place.
- **Tujia of Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** bridal laments and the mother's part at a daughter's wedding.
- **Miao of Huayuan, Fenghuang and Chengbu:** birth customs and mothers' embroidery for children.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** song culture and mothers' roles in it.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** childbirth and naming customs in upland villages.
- **Hakka and Gan counties:** mothers' place in lineages with separate customs.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** motherhood within Islamic teaching.
- Hunanese migrant mothers in coastal cities: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Nüshu, women's surveys and health statistics; mixed for bridal and birth customs; thin for Waxiang and Tuhua-speaking mothers outside Jiangyong.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Nüshu script and the local Tuhua; Xiang dialects; Tujia, Miao, Kam and Mien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-planning history and forced procedures; maternal death; postpartum illness; Nüshu's commercialization; songs that families may not wish recorded.
- **Held for review:** none.
