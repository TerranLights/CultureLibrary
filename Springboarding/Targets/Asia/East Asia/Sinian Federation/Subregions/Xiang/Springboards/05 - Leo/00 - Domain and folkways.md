## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Chang-Zhu-Tan 长株潭 cities:** media, nightlife and consumer leisure concentrate here.
- **Lou-Shao 娄邵 (Loudi, Shaoyang, Xinhua):** Old Xiang speech and the Meishan 梅山 ritual zone; festivity tied to local cults.
- **Dongting Lake plain (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** dragon boats, lake fisheries and Southwestern Mandarin in Changde.
- **Eastern Gan-speaking counties (Liuyang, Liling, Pingjiang):** fireworks and porcelain as celebratory trades.
- **Xiangnan 湘南 (Chenzhou, Yongzhou):** Tuhua varieties and Guangdong-facing tastes.
- **Tujia of the northwest (Longshan, Yongshun, Baojing, Sangzhi):** the hand-waving dance and Maogusi 毛古斯 performance.
- **Miao of Xiangxi and Chengbu:** market-day courtship and festival games.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** grand song, drum towers and village feasts.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong; the Huayao 花瑶 of Longhui:** Panwang festival and women's crafts.
- **Smaller communities:** Bai of Sangzhi, Hui of Changde and Shaoyang, Uyghurs of Taoyuan 桃源, Waxiang 瓦乡 speakers of Yuanling, Luxi and Guzhang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Changsha media and Han festivals; mixed for minority festivals (much is tourism-shaped); thin for Waxiang and Taoyuan Uyghur leisure.
