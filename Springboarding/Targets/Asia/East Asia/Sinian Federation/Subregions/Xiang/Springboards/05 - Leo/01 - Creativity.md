# Xiang: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and Lou-Shao Xiang; Gan of the eastern counties; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong and Yao craft vocabularies. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** media, animation and design firms; creativity as industry and as city brand.
- **Xiangtan:** Qi Baishi's home and a carpentry heritage; craft and fine art meet.
- **Liling and Liuyang (Gan-speaking east):** porcelain and fireworks; invention inside factory trades.
- **Shaoyang and Longhui:** carving and print workshops, and the Huayao 花瑶 women's cross-stitch.
- **Xinhua and Anhua (Meishan zone):** carved ritual figures made by village specialists.
- **Tujia of Longshan and Yongshun:** brocade and family motifs; who may weave which pattern.
- **Miao of Fenghuang, Huayuan and Chengbu:** silver and embroidery under tourist demand.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** builders and brocade; creativity in shared structures.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Nüshu 女书 women of Jiangyong:** needlework and writing as women's making.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for porcelain, embroidery and Qi Baishi; mixed for minority crafts (heritage files exist, independent study thinner); thin for everyday improvising.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Xiang dialect for workshop talk; Tujia, Miao and Dong for craft terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heritage listing can freeze a craft and decide who is a "transmitter"; reported copying of Miao designs by outside brands; forgery in the art market.
- **Held for review:** none.
