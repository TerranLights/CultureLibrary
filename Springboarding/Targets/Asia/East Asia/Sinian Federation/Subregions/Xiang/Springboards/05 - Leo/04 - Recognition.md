*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state within the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (official and classical registers); Xiang dialects for everyday credit and praise; Tujia, Miao, Waxiang and Yao for the communities whose status is at issue. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old academic counties (Changsha, Hengyang, Xiangtan, Xiangxiang):** long lists of degree holders; recognition as a family inheritance.
- **Zhuzhou's industrial workforce:** model-worker titles and factory honor boards.
- **Tujia of the northwest:** recognized as a people only after the 1950s studies.
- **Miao of Xiangxi:** heroes and gods claimed by both Miao and Han, and the state's choice among them.
- **Waxiang speakers:** official category and self-understanding may differ.
- **Taoyuan Uyghurs and Hui:** recognition of a distant origin inside a Han province.
- **Pingdi Yao 平地瑶 vs. mountain Yao of Jianghua:** recognition within one registered people that speaks different languages.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong:** a distinct Han group with no separate official status.
- **Families of Nationalist-era soldiers:** honor delayed and partial.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official honors and examination lists; mixed for ethnic recognition (much is in Chinese-language ethnology); thin for Waxiang and Taoyuan.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Waxiang, Tujia and Miao for self-descriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic registration is politically managed; veteran recognition touches Civil War memory; school ranking publicity is restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
