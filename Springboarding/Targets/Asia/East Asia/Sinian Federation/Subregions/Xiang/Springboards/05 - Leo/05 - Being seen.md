# Xiang: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and internet slang; Changsha Xiang; Xiangnan Tuhua and Southwestern Mandarin; Miao, Dong and Tujia for village public life; Nüshu for women's writing. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's young and online:** attention sought on purpose through check-ins and livestreams.
- **Rural Lou-Shao and Hengyang villages with out-migration:** elders and children left at home, seldom seen by their own kin.
- **Xiangnan Tuhua speakers:** their speech invisible in many classifications; how they are seen in the city.
- **Miao and Tujia of Fenghuang and Jishou:** watched by tourists; staging and refusing the gaze.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** visibility governed by drum-tower seating and choir roles.
- **Jiangyong women:** a script that hid writing from men, now on display.
- **Yao of Jianghua and the Huayao of Longhui:** festival dress that makes groups stand out to visitors.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** visible markers of faith in Han towns.
- **Disabled people and the very old:** often outside the frame of city branding.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media and tourism; mixed for village public life; thin for Tuhua speakers and the institutionalized.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and online slang; Changsha Xiang; Miao, Dong and Tujia for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gu accusations stigmatize Miao women and must be handled as rumor history; talent-show regulation and surveillance are politically sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
