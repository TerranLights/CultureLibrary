# Xiang: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** classical and Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang terms of address; Lou-Shao Xiang for mining and rural counties; Yao, Miao and Tujia for the dignity of peoples. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's old neighborhoods:** forms of address to elders, and the move into care homes.
- **Mining counties of Lou-Shao and Leiyang:** dignity tied to dangerous work and its illnesses.
- **Xiangtan and Zhuzhou factory families:** lost work identity after restructuring.
- **Daoxian and the Yongzhou area:** home of Zhou Dunyi; moral exemplars in local teaching.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong:** a people's name and image reformed in living memory.
- **Miao and Tujia of the Wuling Mountains:** poverty programs and how recipients describe them.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** elders' place in drum-tower life.
- **Hui of Changde:** burial customs that differ from cremation drives.
- **Unmarried and widowed women in rural counties:** the old Zhao Wuzhen debate continued in new forms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts and medical history; mixed for workers and the old; thin for minority self-accounts of dignity.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Xiang dialects for oral accounts; Miao, Dong and Yao for elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor petitions and funeral-reform conflicts are sensitive; poverty programs are politically framed, so look for independent accounts.
- **Held for review:** none.
