# Xiang: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, including the political vocabulary of the twentieth century; Changsha Xiang for teasing and insult; Southwestern Mandarin of West Hunan; outsiders' Chinese terms for the Miao, read critically. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Han counties of the 1927 peasant movement (Xiangtan, Xiangxiang, Hengshan, Liling):** where public shaming was first used as a political tool.
- **Daoxian and southern Yongzhou:** violent humiliation in 1967 and its long silence.
- **Changsha:** wartime destruction by fire and modern online shaming.
- **Changde and Zhijiang:** grievance and vindication sharing a war memory.
- **Miao of Fenghuang and Huayuan:** the frontier wall and Qing labels; how Miao memory answers them.
- **Tujia of Yongshun:** the town in *Hibiscus Town* and its life as a film set.
- **Xinhua and the Meishan zone:** Chen Tianhua's home; local pride in a protest death.
- **Left-behind pupils in boarding schools:** a group especially exposed to bullying.
- **Women in rural families:** humiliation inside marriage, as surveys and memoirs report it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political and war history; mixed for everyday shaming; thin for minority voices on frontier humiliation.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Changsha Xiang for teasing; Miao and Tujia for counter-memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1967 Daoxian killings and Cultural Revolution humiliation are politically restricted topics; outsiders' labels for the Miao and gu rumors are demeaning; handle survivor testimony with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
