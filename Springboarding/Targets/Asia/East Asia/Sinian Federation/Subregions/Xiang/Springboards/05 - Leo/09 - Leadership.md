# Xiang: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** classical and Standard Chinese (military and political registers); Xiang for village and family heads; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao for chiefs, elders and ritual leaders. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiangxiang, Xiangtan and Shuangfeng:** gentry-military leadership in family memory.
- **Changsha's school and student circles:** leaders formed by mentors and peers.
- **Tujia of Yongshun, Longshan and Baojing:** hereditary chiefs in the past; ritual leaders now.
- **Miao of Huayuan and Fenghuang:** rebel leaders and elders; model-village politics today.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** alliance leaders and elders; consensus forms.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** ritual masters who lead initiations and festivals.
- **Lianyuan and the Lou-Shao industrial belt:** founder-entrepreneurs and their firms.
- **Women in rural cooperatives and village committees:** leadership outside the usual male lines.
- **Lineage villages around Yueyang and Hengyang:** clan councils working beside the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing and Republican elite leadership; mixed for village leaders today; thin for women and minority ritual leaders outside heritage files.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao for ritual and village terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Party leadership and leader cults are politically sensitive; Dong alliance rules belong to Libra and Capricorn; ritual knowledge may be restricted by its keepers.
- **Held for review:** none.
