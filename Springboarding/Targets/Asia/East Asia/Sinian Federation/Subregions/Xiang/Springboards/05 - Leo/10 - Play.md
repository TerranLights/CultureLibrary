*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state in the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and Lou-Shao Xiang; Gan (Liuyang, Liling) and Hakka (Rucheng) for festival games; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao for ritual play and contests. **Concept in one line:** activity done for its own sake, set apart from ordinary life by rules or make-believe: games, sport as play, pretending, joking, toying.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** commercial adult play (song halls, escape rooms, mystery games) and pressured childhoods.
- **Lou-Shao and the Meishan zone:** martial arts and ritual drama as play.
- **Gan-speaking east (Liuyang, Liling):** dialect words for play and festival fireworks.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Guidong:** the incense dragon and new-year games.
- **Dongting Lake towns:** boat racing and lake-side games.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan:** Maogusi and the hand-waving dance as group play.
- **Miao of Xiangxi:** feats of daring and festival contests.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** buffalo fighting and firecracker grabbing between villages.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** drum dancing and festival games.
- **Left-behind children in rural boarding schools:** play with few adults and few resources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festival performances and commercial play; mixed for children's lore; thin for everyday adult play in villages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang for rhymes and banter; Gan and Hakka for festival terms; Tujia, Miao and Dong for ritual play.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some ritual drama is not meant for outside eyes; animal fighting draws welfare criticism; adult entertainment venues have had police attention.
- **Held for review:** none.
