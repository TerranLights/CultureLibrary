# Xiang: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (pre-war Hunan), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** classical and Standard Chinese (gazetteer, military and lineage registers); Xiang dialects for face and reputation talk; Tujia and Miao for oaths and feast-hosting. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiangxiang and Xiangtan:** Xiang Army families and their stake in how the army is remembered.
- **Changsha and the Yuelu hills:** official honor for revolutionary dead.
- **Hengyang and Mount Heng:** war memorials and pilgrimage honor.
- **Miluo and the Dongting Lake towns:** honoring Qu Yuan through races and rites.
- **Tujia of Yongshun:** the bronze pillar as a mark of standing between peoples.
- **Miao of Xiangxi:** honor won through feast-hosting and kept through lineage.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** alliance oaths and the good name of a village (rules themselves belong to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Women of Han lineage villages:** chastity honors in the past; reputation and marriage talk today.
- **Hui of Changde:** religious honor codes beside lineage norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and gazetteer honor; mixed for brotherhoods and lineage sanctions; thin for minority honor in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Xiang for proverbs; Tujia and Miao for oaths and feasts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nationalist war dead and revolutionary heroes carry political weight; chastity records describe coerced suicide and must be handled as harm, not virtue; brotherhood history touches organized crime.
- **Held for review:** none.
