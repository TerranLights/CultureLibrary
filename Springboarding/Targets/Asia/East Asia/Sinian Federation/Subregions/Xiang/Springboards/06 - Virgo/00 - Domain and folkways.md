## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha–Zhuzhou–Xiangtan core:** urban office, factory and service work; New Xiang (长益片) speech.
- **Dongting plain (Yueyang, Changde, Yiyang):** polder rice and lake fishing set different seasons; boat-dwelling fishers resettled ashore.
- **Central hills, Lou–Shao and Meishan (Xinhua, Anhua, Lianyuan):** Old Xiang speech; hunting, tea-porting and mining pasts; out-migrant trades.
- **Hengyang and Mount Heng:** pilgrimage service work.
- **Southern Hunan (Chenzhou, Yongzhou):** Tuhua and Southwestern Mandarin speakers, Hakka of Rucheng, Jiangyong women, Yao of Jianghua; mining districts.
- **Western Hunan (Xiangxi):** Tujia (Longshan, Yongshun, Baojing), Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Jishou), Waxiang speakers (Yuanling, Luxi, Guzhang); own farming and craft rounds.
- **Zhangjiajie and Huaihua:** Tujia and Bai of Sangzhi; Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang; Miao of Mayang and Jingzhou; timber and tourism work.
- **Gan-speaking east (Liuyang, Liling, Pingjiang, Chaling):** fireworks and porcelain trades.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang; the Uyghur community of Taoyuan:** rest days and food rules of Muslim communities.
- **Migrant households:** work far away, care left at home. Hunanese in Guangdong only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Han core and Changsha; mixed for Xiangxi and Huaihua; thin for Tuhua, Waxiang and Hunan Bai.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang dialect forms; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Mien for songs and oral lore; Nüshu.
- **Lands:** the tracker header lists one province, Hunan, as a working list the author still confirms; these sheets are written for Hunan's lands. Federation-wide surveys need their Hunan subsample.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Qing frontier records and some Republican ethnographies use derogatory exonyms; reform-through-labor and collectivization-era labor are sensitive; archive access is restricted. Labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
