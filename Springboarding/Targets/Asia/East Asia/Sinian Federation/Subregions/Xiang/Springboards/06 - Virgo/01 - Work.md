# Xiang: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang, Gan and Southwestern Mandarin local forms; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao and Dong for oral lore. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planned-industry cities (Zhuzhou, Xiangtan, Loudi):** state-sector work and 1990s layoffs set a different work biography than private Changsha.
- **Changsha's private and media sectors:** construction machinery, television and food chains bring long-hour office and service work.
- **Mining districts (Lengshuijiang, Chenzhou, Changning, Leiyang):** dangerous work and its illnesses.
- **Dongting lakeshore (Yueyang, Yuanjiang, Hanshou):** where full-time lake fishing has been wound down, the end of a work life can be studied.
- **Meishan hills (Xinhua, Anhua):** native-place trades taken across China.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao villages:** terraced farming, labor exchange, craft, tourism work in Fenghuang and Furong.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** carpentry and timber; communal building work.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Hakka of Rucheng:** upland farming in the far south.
- **Hui traders of Changde and Shaoyang:** trades tied to halal food.
- **Left-behind households:** farm work done by grandparents and children while parents work outside. Guangdong-based Hunanese workers serve only as an outside comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on industry and migration; mixed on minority farm work; thin on Tuhua- and Waxiang-speaking areas.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang, Gan and Southwestern Mandarin forms; Tujia, Miao and Dong for work songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** occupational lung disease among Hunan miners and migrants (pneumoconiosis petitions), mine accidents, fireworks-workshop explosions and wage protests are sensitive; reform-through-labor is restricted. Labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
