# Xiang: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (Classical Chinese for exam and statecraft texts); Changsha and other Xiang speech for forms of address; Tujia, Miao and Dong for hosting customs. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's hospitality and food trade:** chain-store service as a brand; whether tipping exists there at all is an open question, and reviews may carry what tips carry elsewhere.
- **Yueyang and Xiangxiang–Xiangtan:** places tied to scholar-official and soldiering histories; how memorials frame service.
- **Wangcheng and Lei Feng sites:** the state's model of service, in his home district.
- **Han villages of the Lou–Shao hills and Dongting plain:** service owed at weddings and funerals.
- **Tujia and Miao villages of Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** hosting at the gate and at feasts, now also sold to tourists.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** village-wide hosting of visiting villages.
- **Mount Heng and its pilgrims:** service for religious merit.
- **Rural women from southern and western Hunan:** domestic service in cities, inside and outside Hunan.
- **Veterans and their families:** military service as a family tradition in some counties *check*.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** mosque-based mutual service and halal hosting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official volunteering and civil service; mixed on village mutual help; thin on domestic workers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; local speech for forms of address; Tujia, Miao and Dong for hosting songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** volunteer and Lei Feng data are campaign-driven, read as official presentations; military records restricted; the Hunan Army's suppression campaigns are contested history. Law on public office goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
