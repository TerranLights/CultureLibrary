## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha retirees and state-sector pensioners (Zhuzhou, Xiangtan):** pensions change what being useful after work means.
- **Farming elders of the Lou–Shao and Hengyang hills:** how long a person stays useful on the land when the young have left is a question for these counties.
- **Children in left-behind households (Shaoyang, Huaihua, Xiangxi):** the age and kind of tasks they take on.
- **Dongting lakeshore:** land and water reclassified as useful or as waste.
- **Tujia and Miao communities:** ritual specialists and singers whose usefulness is in what they know.
- **Dong carpenters and Yao of Jianghua:** skills that keep or lose a market.
- **Yiyang and Taojiang bamboo workers:** everyday crafts against factory goods.
- **Disabled people across the province:** which jobs are offered to them, and on whose terms.
- **Gan-speaking eastern counties (Liuyang, Liling):** fireworks and porcelain as a county's useful trade; Hunanese elsewhere only for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on elders and left-behind families; mixed on disability; thin on minority ideas of a person's worth.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; local speech; Tujia, Miao and Dong for elders' roles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** left-behind children, disability and old-age poverty are sensitive and need care with people's dignity; land reclamation touched relocated communities.
- **Held for review:** none.
