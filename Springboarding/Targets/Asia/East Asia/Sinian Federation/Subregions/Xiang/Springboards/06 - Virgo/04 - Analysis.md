## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's school and university world:** dense olympiad training, engineering and research institutes.
- **Zhuzhou and Xiangtan engineers:** factory quality control and engineering reasoning in rail and steel work.
- **Mining districts (Chenzhou, Lengshuijiang):** ore assay and survey as local professions.
- **Rural rice counties of the Dongting plain and central hills:** a test case for claims about rice-farming thought styles.
- **Miao and Dong singers of Xiangxi and Huaihua:** reasoning tested in song rather than on paper.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** ritual manuscripts and counting in ceremonial texts *check*.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** trade reckoning and mosque education.
- **Gan-speaking Liuyang:** the reformers' mathematics school and its legacy.
- **Older and younger generations:** abacus against calculator and app.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schools, exams and excavated texts; mixed on everyday reckoning; thin on minority reasoning forms outside song.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for slips and Qing scholarship; Standard Chinese; Miao, Dong and Yao for song texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defense research in Changsha (supercomputing at the national defense university) is restricted, so leave it out; thinking-style studies by province can slide into stereotype, so read as contested; "investigation" as a political method belongs with official history.
- **Held for review:** none.
