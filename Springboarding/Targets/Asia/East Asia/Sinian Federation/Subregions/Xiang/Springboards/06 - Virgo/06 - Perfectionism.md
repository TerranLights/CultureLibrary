# Xiang: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for calligraphy and craft writing; Xiang dialect words for fussy and sloppy work; Dong, Miao and Tujia for carpentry and silver-craft terms. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha students and parents:** the provincial capital's elite schools and tutoring market as the first place to measure exam pressure.
- **Xiang embroidery workers (Changsha, Shaping):** stitch-level standards set by a craft lineage.
- **Liling and Liuyang (Gan-speaking east):** porcelain and fireworks, where a flaw ruins a piece or causes harm.
- **Zhuzhou rail and aviation-engine workers:** industrial quality systems.
- **Dong carpenters of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** how exactness is taught where builders may work without drawings, as they describe it.
- **Miao silversmiths of Fenghuang and Huayuan:** whether tourist buyers have shifted the standard a piece must meet.
- **Ritual specialists in the Yuan River valley:** exactness in rite.
- **Xiangtan:** a painter-carpenter's hometown as a test of how the craft-to-art story is told.
- **Rural schools of Huaihua and Yongzhou:** whether the pressure differs from Changsha.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on calligraphy, crafts and student pressure; mixed on industry; thin on minority ideas of a flawless rite or object.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Dong, Miao and Tujia for craft terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and eating disorders need care; fireworks-workshop accidents are sensitive; "craftsman spirit" is a state campaign, read as such.
