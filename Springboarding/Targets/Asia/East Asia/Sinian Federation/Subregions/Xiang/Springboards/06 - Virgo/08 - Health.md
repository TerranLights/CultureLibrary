*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese for Han manuscripts and Qing medical books; local speech for illness talk; Miao, Tujia, Dong and Yao for healing knowledge, as healers share it. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** top hospitals, private clinics and wellness consumption.
- **Dongting lakeshore (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** a disease ecology of snails and floods.
- **Xiangtan and Changsha betel-nut chewers:** a habit linked to place and trade.
- **Mining and migrant-sending counties (Leiyang, Lengshuijiang, Lianyuan):** occupational lung disease.
- **Miao of Huayuan, Fenghuang and Jishou:** their own medicine and its official version.
- **Tujia of Longshan, Yongshun and Baojing:** herbal and ritual healing.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** bathing and plant remedies in the far west and south.
- **Yuan valley Han and Waxiang speakers:** talisman and incantation healers.
- **Elderly rural Han:** the barefoot-doctor generation; whether its trust leans to herbs, to injections, or to both.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** halal diet and health.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on epidemiology and TCM; mixed on ethnic medicine (official versions dominate); thin on healers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Miao, Tujia, Dong and Yao for plant and illness names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' knowledge may be restricted by the communities; the old gu poison (蛊) accusation against Miao women is a slur to study as stigma only; mental illness, betel-nut harms and pneumoconiosis are sensitive; health law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
