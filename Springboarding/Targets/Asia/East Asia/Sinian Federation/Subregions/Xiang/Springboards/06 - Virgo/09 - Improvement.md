## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiangxiang–Shuangfeng (Zeng Guofan's home area):** how the hometown presents his self-cultivation to visitors and schools.
- **Changsha's young professionals:** night schools, certificates and online striving.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan:** imperial reform as remembered history, and language renewal now.
- **Miao of Huayuan and Fenghuang:** frontier "pacification" in the past, poverty-alleviation showcases today.
- **Zhangjiajie's Tujia and Bai:** relocation for parks and tourism.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** heritage listing as a form of upgrading.
- **Rural migrant-sending counties (Shaoyang, Loudi):** whether new houses and children's schooling serve as the yardstick of betterment.
- **Zhuzhou and Xiangtan old industrial districts:** urban renewal and laid-off workers' retraining.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** resettlement from high mountains to lower ground *check*.
- **Hakka of Rucheng:** whether migration histories shape striving stories, to be found.
