# Xiang: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Xiang dialects; Xiangxi Miao, Tujia, Dong and Mien terms where known. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Chang-Zhu-Tan cities:** where courts, media and petition traffic concentrate.
- **Hengyang and Xiangnan villages:** the base of the rural rights-defense studies.
- **Yongzhou, especially Daoxian and Jianghua:** historical-justice questions after 1967; Yao communities alongside.
- **Xiangxi Miao counties (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Jishou, Baojing):** the frontier where custom and imperial law met.
- **Tujia counties (Yongshun, Longshan, Sangzhi):** justice under and after chieftain rule.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** village covenant sanctions alongside state courts.
- **Dongting polder towns (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** water and land conflicts that reach courts.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** community mediation through mosque networks, to test *check*.
- **Lou-Shao and Meishan areas:** oath and spirit-judgment practices, to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing archives and modern official reports; mixed for minority practice; thin and guarded on political justice.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Qing archival style; Xiang dialects for oral testimony; Miao and Tujia for customary terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Daoxian killings, the Cultural Revolution in general, petitioning and wrongful convictions are politically sensitive; access inside the federation may be limited, and interviewees may be at risk.
- **Held for review:** none.
