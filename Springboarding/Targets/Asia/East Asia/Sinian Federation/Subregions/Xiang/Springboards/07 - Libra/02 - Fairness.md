**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang (Changsha and Loudi-Shaoyang varieties); Southwestern Mandarin of western Hunan; classical Chinese; Yao, Dong and Tujia terms where known. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha white-collar workers:** bill-splitting and pay norms likely to differ from the countryside's.
- **Shaodong and Lianyuan 涟源 trading towns:** small-merchant networks where fair price and credit are daily matters *check*.
- **Dongting Lake rice villages:** water turns and collective shares.
- **Hengyang and Chenzhou mining areas:** wage fairness and compensation.
- **Huayuan, Jishou and Fenghuang Miao villages:** need-based targeting and its fallout.
- **Tujia counties of Zhangjiajie and Yongshun:** tourism income shares between villages and companies.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** customary divisions of labor and catch.
- **Hui of Changde:** trade and halal-market price customs.
- **Gan-speaking Liuyang and Liling:** fireworks and porcelain workshops' piece-rate pay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gaokao, consumer and wage material; mixed for household division; thin for minority distribution rules outside ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin; classical Chinese for the Chu Ci and Qing reports; Miao, Yao and Tujia for customary shares.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admissions cheating and poverty targeting involve officials; married-out women's claims can be politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
