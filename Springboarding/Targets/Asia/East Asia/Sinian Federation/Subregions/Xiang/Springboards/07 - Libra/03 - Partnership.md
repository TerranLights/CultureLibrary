*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang (New and Old); Southwestern Mandarin; Gan of eastern Hunan; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao terms where known. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha urban professionals:** later marriage, dual-career couples and co-founders.
- **Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng and Loudi gentry heartland:** old lineage alliances by marriage.
- **Xiangtan and Yiyang trading towns:** merchant partnerships and family firms.
- **Dongting fishing communities:** crews and husband-and-wife boats.
- **Xupu and Chenxi in the Yuan valley:** Chenxu Xiang speakers with Tujia and Miao neighbors *check*.
- **Tujia of Yongshun, Longshan and Sangzhi:** bridal lament and family-to-family marriage.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Chengbu Miao:** courtship fairs and freer choice of partners, to test.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** courtship singing and sworn friendship.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong:** uxorilocal unions and sworn friends.
- **Gan- and Hakka-speaking eastern counties (Pingjiang, Liuyang, Rucheng):** separate lineage marriage webs.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** marriage within and outside the community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gentry letters, early twentieth-century debates and statistics; mixed for minority marriage forms; thin for business partnerships outside large firms.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang dialects for oral history; Tujia and Miao for laments and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political figures' marriages carry official narratives; minority courtship practices are often packaged for tourists, so separate staged from lived.
- **Held for review:** none.
