# Xiang: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese; Xiang dialects; Tujia, Miao and Dong terms for healing and harmony where known. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others, and the steadiness that results in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** night economy and office overwork side by side.
- **Zhuzhou-Xiangtan-Hengyang industrial belt:** pollution and restoration on the Xiang River.
- **Dongting Lake villages (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** farming against the lake's needs.
- **Loudi and Shaoyang hill counties:** Old Xiang speakers; home-remedy and food customs to test.
- **Yongzhou (Daoxian, Ningyuan):** Zhou Dunyi's home ground and Xiangnan Tuhua speakers.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia:** minority medicine and ideas of bodily balance.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** architecture and village siting.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** herbal-bath traditions *check*.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and eastern counties:** their own food and remedy customs.
- **Hui of Changde:** halal diet and its own balance of foods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medicine, ecology and food; mixed for minority healing systems; thin on checks and balances in government, since provincial bodies sit inside a single-party system.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang dialects for folk-remedy talk; Miao and Tujia for healing terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pollution data (heavy metals, cadmium in rice) has been contested; minority medicine is often promoted for tourism and trade.
- **Held for review:** none.
