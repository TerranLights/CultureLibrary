## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** dialect humor, banquet culture and the province's external relations.
- **Xiangyin and Xiangxiang:** home ground of the nineteenth-century envoys and their families.
- **Yueyang and the Dongting ports:** river trade with outsiders; treaty-port era contacts *check*.
- **Fenghuang, Jishou and Huayuan:** the old Miao frontier, where pacification and talks alternated.
- **Tujia of Yongshun, Baojing and Longshan:** chieftains' bargaining with the court.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Huitong:** inter-village visiting and welcome rites.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong:** relations between hill communities and lowland governments.
- **Bai of Sangzhi:** a small community's dealings with larger neighbors.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Gan speakers of Pingjiang:** in-migrant communities negotiating place.
- **Hunanese communities abroad:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for nineteenth-century envoys and modern trade diplomacy; mixed for everyday tact; thin for minority inter-village diplomacy outside Guizhou-centered studies.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Qing Chinese; Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang for humor and tact; Miao and Dong for welcome formulas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** frontier pacification and 1949 events carry official narratives; anti-foreign episodes are framed in contested ways.
- **Held for review:** none.
