*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang (Changsha, Loudi-Shaoyang); Southwestern Mandarin of the west; Hakka of Rucheng; classical Chinese; Miao, Tujia, Dong and Yao terms where known. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind (gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm): the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and Zhuzhou cities:** cash gifts and banquets in restaurants.
- **Loudi, Shaoyang and Xinhua villages:** home feasting 做酒 and gift ledgers.
- **Dongting plain villages:** labor exchange in rice farming.
- **Hengyang and Nanyue area:** pilgrims and vow economies.
- **Labor-export counties of Xiangnan and Xiangxi:** remittances and left-behind elders.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Zhangjiajie:** house-raising mutual aid.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Baojing):** sacrifice feasts between kin.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** inter-village hospitality and festival exchanges.
- **Hakka of Rucheng and Gan speakers of Liuyang:** pounded tea and lineage gift networks.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** religious giving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gift-burden studies and official campaigns; mixed for minority feasting; thin for rotating credit in the present.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and Hakka for local gift words; classical Chinese for slips; Miao and Tujia for feast terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** favor exchange shades into bribery in official settings; some ritual feasts were once banned as "superstition".
- **Held for review:** none.
