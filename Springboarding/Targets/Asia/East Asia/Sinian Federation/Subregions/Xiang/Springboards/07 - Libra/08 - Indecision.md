# Xiang: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for everyday assent and delay; classical Chinese (Chu texts); Miao, Tujia and Dong terms for ritual specialists where known. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** office life, dating and career choices among the young.
- **Xiangyin and Xiangxiang:** home ground of nineteenth-century statesmen remembered for resolve or caution.
- **Xinhua and Anhua (Meishan):** ritual specialists consulted for choices.
- **Miluo and Pingjiang villages:** Han Shaogong's setting; Xiang and Gan speech.
- **Hengyang and Nanyue:** temple divination traffic.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia counties:** ritual consultation before major choices.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** village assemblies and their pace.
- **Hui of Changde:** imams consulted on choices *check*.
- **Hakka of Rucheng:** lineage elders' say in household decisions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, policy and media; mixed for everyday decision-making; thin for minority ritual consultation outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for Chu texts; Standard Chinese; Xiang and Gan for interviews; Miao and Tujia for ritual terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination was long labeled "superstition" and may be under-reported; the "Hunanese character" literature leans on stereotype and must be read as self-image.
- **Held for review:** none.
