# Xiang: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for everyday sayings; classical Chinese (Chu texts); Dong, Miao and Tujia terms where known. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** courts, therapists and the press, where silences are most often broken in public.
- **Mining and industrial towns (Lengshuijiang, Loudi, Chenzhou, Zhuzhou):** compensation in exchange for silence.
- **Liuyang and Pingjiang:** Gan-speaking lineage areas; pollution and fireworks-factory disputes.
- **Shaoyang and Wugang:** pollution cases and labor migrants' families.
- **Dong of Xinhuang and Tongdao:** small-county silences and how they broke.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia:** relations with officials and the majority, and what goes unsaid.
- **Waxiang speakers of Yuanling, Luxi and Guzhang:** identity kept quiet or pressed.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Hakka of Rucheng:** hill and in-migrant communities' accommodation with neighbors.
- **Hui of Changde:** a small religious minority's relations with the majority *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in news of cover-ups once exposed; thin for silences that stayed unbroken; surveys exist but rarely at province level.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang and Gan dialects for village and family interviews; Dong and Miao where needed.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability maintenance", petitioning, cover-ups and the 1959 events are politically sensitive; domestic-violence and bullying interviews need care for survivors.
- **Held for review:** none.
