# Xiang: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for local labels and folk healing; Qing administrative Chinese; Miao and Tujia terms where known. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** online talk, media and the province's self-image.
- **Shaoyang and Loudi:** Old Xiang areas that carry intra-provincial nicknames.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Jishou):** the target of gu and "raw Miao" images; their own accounts.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Zhangjiajie:** the "bandit" and "mystery" image, now sold to tourists.
- **Waxiang speakers of Yuanling and Luxi:** corpse-driving lore told about the Yuan River valley.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** less studied for accusation lore; a gap to check.
- **Dongting Lake villages:** blame for floods, voles and fishing collapse.
- **Yueyang and border counties near Hubei:** pandemic-era blame between neighbors.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** a minority's experience of being othered *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing frontier writing and online regional bashing; mixed for folk healing and accusation lore; thin for first-person accounts of people accused of gu.
- **Languages needed:** Qing Chinese; Standard Chinese; Xiang dialects for nicknames and healing words; Miao and Tujia for their own counter-accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gu and "raw Miao" labels are stigmatizing; campaign-era enemy labels are politically sensitive; quote labels only as recorded, never as fact.
- **Held for review:** none.
