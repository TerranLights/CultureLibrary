# Xiang: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional script); Xiang 湘语 (New Xiang around Changsha and Xiangtan, Old Xiang around Shaoyang and Loudi); Southwestern Mandarin of the west and south; Gan along the Jiangxi border; Hakka pockets; Tuhua 土话 of the far south; Waxiang 瓦乡话 of western Hunan; Tujia, Xong (Western Hunan Miao), Kam (Dong) and Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, how a culture faces death, power and what is hidden, with its folkways for death, sex, shared resources, inheritance and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha–Zhuzhou–Xiangtan core (Huxiang 湖湘):** urban funerals at state funeral homes; the media center.
- **Old Xiang heartland (Shaoyang, Loudi, Xiangxiang):** the old dialect area; lineage and Meishan 梅山 ritual traditions *check*.
- **Dongting Lake plain (Yueyang, Changde, Yiyang):** polder communities, flood memory, shared water.
- **Western Hunan (Xiangxi 湘西, Zhangjiajie, Huaihua):** Tujia and Miao, Waxiang speakers, Nuo 傩 ritual.
- **Southwestern Hunan (Tongdao, Jingzhou, Xinhuang):** Dong villages with drum towers and their own rites.
- **Southern Hunan (Chenzhou, Yongzhou, Jiangyong, Jianghua):** Yao, Tuhua speakers, Hakka pockets, Nüshu.
- **Eastern border (Liuyang, Pingjiang, Liling):** Gan and Hakka speech, a separate migration history.
- **Hui, Bai and Uyghur communities (Changde and Shaoyang Hui; Sangzhi Bai; Taoyuan's Fengshu 枫树 Uyghur):** Islamic burial and inheritance for the Muslim groups; Bai lineage histories *check*.
- Hunanese migrants in Guangdong and abroad: comparison only, for how funeral and inheritance customs travel or lapse away from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funerals, lineage records and Han-period archaeology; mixed for inheritance practice; thin and sensitive for sex ways outside survey data.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for fieldwork; Tujia, Xong, Kam and Mien terms need native consultants.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Hunan only, as a working list the author still confirms; this sign's sheets are written for Hunan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** funeral reform and grave clearance are politically charged; famine and political deaths are restricted topics; minority ritual knowledge belongs to its keepers.
- **Held for review:** none.
