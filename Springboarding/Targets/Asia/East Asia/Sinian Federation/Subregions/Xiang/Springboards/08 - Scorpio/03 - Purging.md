# Xiang: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin of Xiangxi and Huaihua; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha urban households:** detox, decluttering, discipline-campaign news as everyday talk.
- **Old Xiang counties:** soul-calling, household exorcists, lineage expulsion.
- **Yuan River valley (Yuanling, Luxi, Chenxi):** Nuo and dragon-boat rites; Waxiang speakers.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Fenghuang, Huayuan):** accusation lore that fell on Miao women; separate cleansing rites.
- **Xinhuang and Tongdao Dong:** their own Nuo form.
- **Dongting Lake districts:** the disease campaign and its memory.
- **Industrial towns (Zhuzhou, Lengshuijiang, Chenzhou mining areas):** pollution cleanup and blame.
- **Southern Hunan (Daoxian, Jianghua):** a mass killing remembered locally; Yao neighbors.
- **Hui Muslims:** ablution and dietary purity rules.
- Hunanese communities outside the province: comparison only, for which cleansing rites they keep or drop.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Nuo, environmental programs and anti-corruption notices; mixed for folk healing; thin and guarded for accusation lore and political purges.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for the calendar text; Southwestern Mandarin and Waxiang in the Yuan valley; Kam, Xong and Mien for minority rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Daoxian, the soviet purges and land-reform violence are restricted; gu accusations stigmatize real women and Miao communities, so handle as accusation, never as fact.
- **Held for review:** none.
