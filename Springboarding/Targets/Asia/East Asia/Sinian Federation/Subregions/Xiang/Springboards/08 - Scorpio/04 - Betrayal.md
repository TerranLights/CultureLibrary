## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and Xiangtan:** the revolutionary history of informers and splits; urban divorce.
- **Ningxiang, Xiangtan and Shaoshan:** home counties of leaders accused of betrayal and of their accusers; memorial politics.
- **Hengyang and the southern Xiang valley:** Wu Sangui's memory; Qi opera audiences.
- **Yueyang and the Dongting towns:** wartime occupation and its collaborators.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Suining:** compacts and oaths as the guard against betrayal.
- **Miao and Tujia of Xiangxi:** oath and ordeal customs; frontier history of shifting loyalties under the tusi and the Qing.
- **Yao of southern Hunan:** sworn ritual brotherhood and its breaking *check*.
- **Gan- and Hakka-speaking east (Liuyang, Pingjiang):** soviet-era loyalties and defections.
- Hunanese abroad after 1949: comparison only, since "traitor" labels were also pinned on those who left.
