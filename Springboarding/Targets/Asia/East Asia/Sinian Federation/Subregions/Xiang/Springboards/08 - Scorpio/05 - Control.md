*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha middle class:** school pressure, tracking apps, two-purse households.
- **Xiangxiang, Shuangfeng and Loudi gentry heartland:** patriarchal household letters and lineage discipline.
- **Xiangtan:** betel nut economy and its habit.
- **Tujia of Yongshun, Longshan and Sangzhi:** memory of chieftain rule and its end; bridal laments.
- **Miao of Fenghuang, Huayuan and Jishou:** frontier wall and garrison history.
- **Reservoir-resettled communities (Anhua, Yuanling):** lives reorganized by water control.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Yao of Jianghua:** village self-rule against state rule *check*.
- **Gan and Hakka east:** separate lineage and household customs.
- **Migrant-worker families of the south (Shaoyang, Yongzhou):** control over children left with grandparents.
- Hunanese migrant workers in coastal cities: comparison only, for parental control exercised from afar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for frontier history and household-decision surveys; mixed for addiction and gambling; thin for coercive control as a named idea in minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Qing documentary Chinese; Xiang for household speech; Tujia and Xong for laments and memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state surveillance and grid management are guarded; resettlement disputes can be politically delicate; domestic-violence casework is confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.
