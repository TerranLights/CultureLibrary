# Xiang: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and other cities:** stranger trust through platforms and brands.
- **Old Xiang villages (Hengshan, Hengyang, Shaoyang):** cadre trust, lineage trust.
- **Yuan River trading towns (Hongjiang, Yuanling):** merchant credit networks.
- **Jishou and Xiangxi:** the fundraising crisis; Tujia and Miao sworn-friend customs.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Jingzhou:** age-mate bonds and village compacts *check*.
- **Jiangyong women:** sworn sisterhood.
- **Dongting rice districts:** trust in the rice crop's safety.
- **Gan and Hakka east:** separate merchant and lineage networks.
- **Hui traders:** halal trust networks *check*.
- Hunanese native-place associations 同乡会 elsewhere: comparison only, for trust built on a shared home county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and contract documents; mixed for sworn-bond customs; thin for minority trust concepts in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Qin script for Liye; Kam and Tujia for bond terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Jishou unrest and food-safety cases are politically delicate; trust in the state is a guarded survey topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
