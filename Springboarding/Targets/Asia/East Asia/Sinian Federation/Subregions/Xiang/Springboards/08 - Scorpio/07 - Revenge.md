# Xiang: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong, and the alternatives to doing so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Xiang valley:** proverb culture of cold revenge; online shaming.
- **Hengyang, Shaoyang and Loudi:** lineage feud records; temple justice.
- **Changde and the lake districts:** war memory and redress.
- **Huaihua (Zhijiang):** peace framing of a war site.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Fenghuang, Huayuan):** uprising memory, bandit history.
- **Tujia of Yongshun and Sangzhi:** clan conflict and chieftain-era memory *check*.
- **Yao of Jianghua and Lanshan:** the 1832 rising and its aftermath.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** compact-based settlement in place of revenge *check*.
- **Gan and Hakka communities of the east:** separate lineage-feud histories.
- Hunanese abroad: comparison only, for how war grievances are voiced away from the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and Qing uprisings; mixed for lineage feuds; thin for everyday grudges outside media.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Qing documentary Chinese; Xong and Mien for oral memory; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for village testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform violence is restricted; uprisings are framed politically; war memory with Japan is charged. Private vengeance set against the state's punishment is the spirit of the law, for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
