# Xiang: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** revolutionary-era secret plots; urban privacy online.
- **Liuyang, Pingjiang and Liling:** Gan and Hakka speakers; the 1906 rising's secret networks.
- **Xinhua, Anhua and Lianyuan (Meishan area):** ritual secrecy in Old Xiang country.
- **Yuan River valley (Yuanling, Luxi):** talisman lore; Waxiang speakers.
- **Xiangxi Miao:** Badai knowledge.
- **Tujia of Longshan and Baojing:** Tima knowledge.
- **Jiangyong and Daoxian:** women's writing; Tuhua and Yao neighbors.
- **Huaihua and the west's former Third Front towns:** secret industry.
- **Hengyang and Shaoyang adoptee-origin areas:** family secrets across borders.
- Hunan-born adoptees abroad: comparison only, for the secrets their searches bring to light at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for secret societies and revolutionary history; mixed for ritual-knowledge studies; thin and private for family secrets.
- **Languages needed:** classical and Qing documentary Chinese; Xong and Tujia for ritual terms; Xiang for village speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual specialists' restricted knowledge must be researched only as far as its keepers allow; state secrecy and dossiers are off limits; adoption and hidden-birth cases involve living people.
- **Held for review:** none.
