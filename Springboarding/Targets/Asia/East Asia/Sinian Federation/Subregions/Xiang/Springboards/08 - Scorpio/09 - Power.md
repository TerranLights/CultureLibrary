# Xiang: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语; Southwestern Mandarin; Gan, Hakka, Tuhua and Waxiang varieties; Tujia, Xong (Miao), Kam (Dong), Iu Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha:** provincial government, corporations, Hunan's media power.
- **Xiangxiang, Xiangtan and Shaoshan:** Xiang Army gentry and revolutionary leaders; memorial politics.
- **Hengyang:** Wang Fuzhi's home ground; southern Xiang valley elites.
- **Yueyang:** lineage villages; lake-region power.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao:** warlords, Miao land politics, elder rule.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Huitong:** drum-tower assemblies.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** King Pan and ritual authority.
- **Gan and Hakka east:** soviet-era power shifts.
- **Hui communities:** mosque leadership *check*.
- Hunanese officials and entrepreneurs serving elsewhere: comparison only, for networks of influence that run back to the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gentry, revolutionary and political history; mixed for corporate power; thin for village power seen from inside minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Xiang for village speech; Kam, Xong and Mien for elder and ritual titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current leadership, factional networks and Mao's legacy are guarded; land politics in Xiangxi is delicate. Legitimate authority as such is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
