## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dongting Lake counties (Huarong, Yuanjiang, Nanxian, Anxiang):** flood as a recurring crisis.
- **Chenzhou and the southern mountains:** the 2008 ice storm; mining hazards.
- **Hengyang and Changsha:** wartime sieges and their memory.
- **Zhuzhou, Xiangtan and Hengyang industrial districts:** layoffs and economic crisis.
- **Liuyang and Liling (Gan-speaking east):** fireworks hazard.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Huitong:** fire in wooden villages.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao:** drought, landslides, remoteness from help *check*.
- **Yao and Tuhua-speaking south:** mountain hazards and relief access *check*.
- **Migrant-sending counties (Shaoyang, Yongzhou):** crises at a distance, when earners are away.
- Hunanese workers stranded away from home (as in the 2008 storm): comparison only, for crises felt from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for floods, war and recent disasters; mixed for economic crises; thin and restricted for famine years and personal crises.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers; Xiang dialects for oral history; Kam, Xong and Mien for local disaster lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the famine of 1959–1961 and blame for dike failures are politically delicate; mental-health records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.
