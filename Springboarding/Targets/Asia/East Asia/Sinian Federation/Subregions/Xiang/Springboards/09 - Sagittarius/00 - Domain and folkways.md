## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Huxiang core** (Changsha, Zhuzhou, Xiangtan): the academies' and reformers' region.
- **Lou-Shao hills** (Loudi, Shaoyang, Xinhua, Anhua; Old Xiang): the Meishan zone.
- **Hengyang and Nanyue:** the sacred peak's hinterland.
- **Dongting plain** (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde): river cults, mission stations, Hui congregations *check*.
- **Gan-speaking east** (Liuyang, Liling, Pingjiang, Chaling): Jiangxi-derived lineages.
- **Xiangnan** (Yongzhou, Chenzhou; Tuhua; Hakka in Rucheng and Guidong): Shun's tomb at Jiuyi Shan *check*.
- **Xiangxi Tujia** (Yongshun, Longshan, Sangzhi) and **Miao** (Fenghuang, Huayuan): separate specialists and languages.
- **Dong** of Tongdao, Xinhuang, Jingzhou: Sa Sui and the Feishan 飞山 cult *check*.
- **Smaller communities:** Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong; Bai of Sangzhi; Waxiang speakers of Yuanling and Luxi; the Uyghur-descended Jian 翦 lineage of Taoyuan *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical philosophy; mixed for current practice (surveys rarely cut by province); thin for minority ritual outside ethnographies.
- **Lands:** this member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author (working list: Hunan). The federation enters only where national surveys need a Hunan cut.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese alongside the modern language; Xiang dialect dictionaries; Tujia, Miao, Kam and Mien (mostly in Chinese transcription).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mao veneration and groups labeled 邪教 are politically sensitive; some Badai and Tima texts are for initiates; 蛊 poison beliefs have stigmatized Miao women. Any legal question (registration rules, religious-affairs regulations) belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
