# Xiang: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha and Lou-Shao Xiang; Southwestern Mandarin of the northwest; the Tujia, Miao, Kam and Mien languages of the west and south. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and Zhuzhou temple-goers** against **Lou-Shao villages** served by ritual masters (师公).
- **Hengyang counties** feeding the Nanyue pilgrimage: organized lay groups.
- **Dongting fishers and river boatmen** (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde): water deities and boat rites.
- **Tujia** of Yongshun, Longshan, Baojing and Sangzhi; **Miao** of Fenghuang, Huayuan and Mayang: different specialists and objects of trust.
- **Dong** of Tongdao, Xinhuang and Jingzhou; **Yao** of Jianghua and Jiangyong; **Bai** of Sangzhi.
- **Hui** of Changde and Shaoyang; the Taoyuan Uyghur-descended community.
- **Catholics** (Hengyang, Changsha) and **Protestants** (registered and house churches; Yiyang's former Lutheran field *check*).
- **Hakka** of Rucheng and Guidong; **Waxiang** speakers of Yuanling, the old Chenzhou ritual center.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage-listed rites; mixed for registered faiths; thin for house churches and private devotion.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Tujia and Miao ritual vocabulary (Chinese transcriptions); Hui Arabic-Persian loanwords; Xiang dialect for vow formulas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered Christian groups; groups the state labels 邪教; Mao veneration; initiates-only Badai and Tima texts. Venue-registration rules are a Libra or Capricorn matter, not this sheet's.
- **Held for review:** none.
