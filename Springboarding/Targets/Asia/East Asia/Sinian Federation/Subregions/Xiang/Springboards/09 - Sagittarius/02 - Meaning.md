# Xiang: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and classical Chinese; Changsha Xiang for everyday speech; Gan and Southwestern Mandarin varieties; Xiangxi Miao and Tujia for oral epics. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's young professionals** (media, tech, Mango TV) against **migrant-sending counties** (Shaoyang, Loudi, Yongzhou, Huaihua): different scripts for a worthwhile life.
- **Left-behind elders** in villages: meaning when children are away.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia:** origin epics and ancestor obligations, each in its own language.
- **Dong** of Tongdao and Xinhuang; **Yao** of Jianghua: village and lineage roles.
- **Gan-speaking lineages** of Liuyang and Liling, and **Hakka** of Rucheng: genealogy and hall culture.
- **Dongting fishing families** and the Yuan River's former boatmen: lost trades.
- **Retired state workers** of Zhuzhou and Xiangtan industry: meaning after the factory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and literary sources; mixed for psychology (many student samples, few others); thin for minority-language voices on purpose.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, both classical and modern; Xiang dialect for funeral songs; Miao and Tujia for epics (Chinese transcriptions first).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data are sensitive and partly unpublished; 躺平 discourse has been censored at times; mourning songs may be performed only at real wakes.
- **Held for review:** none.
