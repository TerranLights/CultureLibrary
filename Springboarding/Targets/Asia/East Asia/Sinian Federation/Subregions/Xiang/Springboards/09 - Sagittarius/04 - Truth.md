*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (Chu and Song-Ming texts); Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for oral oath formulas; Tujia and Xiangxi Miao for dispute customs. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's university and press world** against **rural Lou-Shao and Xiangnan counties**: lab, newspaper and school versus elders, temple lots and kin networks.
- **Changde:** a city whose history was confirmed through a foreign court.
- **Dao county and Yongzhou:** silence and testimony about 1967.
- **Xiangxi Miao** (Fenghuang, Huayuan) and **Tujia** (Longshan, Yongshun): oath customs and village arbitration.
- **Dong** of Tongdao: song as the record of village history *check*.
- **Yao** of Jianghua: charters and genealogies as evidence of origin.
- **Gan-speaking** Liuyang and Pingjiang and **Hakka** Rucheng: genealogy claims of origin.
- **Waxiang** speakers: disputed classification of their own language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for excavated texts and classical epistemology; mixed for media studies; thin and blocked for politically disputed events.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese and Chu script specialists for excavated texts; modern Chinese; Japanese for the Changde court record; Miao and Tujia for oath formulas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Dao county 1967, censorship and rumor labeling are politically sensitive; the Zidanku manuscript's ownership is disputed. Courts and law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
