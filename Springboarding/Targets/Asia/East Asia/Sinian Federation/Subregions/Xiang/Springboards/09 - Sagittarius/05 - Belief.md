*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang (Changsha, Lou-Shao) and Southwestern Mandarin (Changde, Xiangxi, Huaihua) for folk-belief vocabulary; Waxiang for the Yuan River valley; Tujia and Xiangxi Miao. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and the warrant wanted for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's young urban residents** (玄学, apps) against **rural Lou-Shao elders** (soul-calling, fengshui).
- **Yuan River valley** (Yuanling, Chenxi, Luxi; Waxiang speakers): talismans and corpse-driving lore.
- **Xiangxi Miao** (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Jishou): Panhu and Badai beliefs, and the stigma of 蛊.
- **Tujia** (Yongshun, Longshan, Sangzhi, Cili): the white-tiger split.
- **Dong** (Tongdao, Xinhuang): Sa and village guardians; **Yao** (Jianghua, Ningyuan): Panhu and King Pan.
- **Bai** of Sangzhi: beliefs carried from Yunnan, as they tell it *check*.
- **Hui** of Changde: belief within a minority faith among folk-religion neighbors.
- **Gan-speaking east and Hakka Rucheng:** ancestor and grave beliefs tied to lineage halls.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk-literature collections and ethnography; mixed for surveys; thin for how belief is held (certainty, irony) as against what is believed.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Southwestern Mandarin and Waxiang for western lore; Miao and Tujia terms in Chinese transcription.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 蛊 beliefs have harmed real women; "superstition" is an official label, so note who applies it; some rites are for initiates only. Anti-superstition regulations are for Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
