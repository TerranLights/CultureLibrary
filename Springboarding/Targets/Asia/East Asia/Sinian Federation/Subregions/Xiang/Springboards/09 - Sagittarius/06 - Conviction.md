# Xiang: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical and late-Qing Chinese for loyalist and reformer texts; Xiang dialects (the slogans of "Hunan spirit" are often given in Changsha forms); Mien and Miao for minority risings, mostly via Chinese records. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on; the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's reformer and student milieu** against **Hengyang's loyalist memory**: two kinds of holding fast.
- **Xiangtan and Xiangxiang** (Zeng Guofan, Peng Dehuai): army and dissent.
- **Lou-Shao counties** (Xinhua, Shaoyang): pamphleteers, Xiang Army recruiting grounds.
- **Gan-speaking Liuyang and Pingjiang:** reform and revolutionary bases.
- **Yao** of Jianghua and Ningyuan; **Miao** of Fenghuang and Huayuan; **Tujia** of Yongshun: minority resolve in state records and in their own.
- **Dong** of Tongdao and Jingzhou: village solidarity customs.
- **Catholic communities** of Hengyang; **Hui** of Changde.
- **Present-day urban volunteers** against **village elders**: civic versus lineage loyalty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martyrs and reformers (much of it hagiographic); mixed for opponents and dissenters; thin for minority and religious steadfastness outside state records.
- **Languages needed:** Qing-era and modern Chinese; Italian and Latin for Franciscan records; Mien and Miao oral sources in transcription.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Peng Dehuai, the 1959 dissent and the canonization of 2000 are politically sensitive; "Hunanese stubbornness" is a stereotype to examine, never to repeat as fact. Trials and sentences of dissenters are a Libra or Capricorn topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
