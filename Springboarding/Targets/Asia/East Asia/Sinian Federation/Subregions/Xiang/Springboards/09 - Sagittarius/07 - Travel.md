# Xiang: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese for travel diaries; Southwestern Mandarin and Waxiang of the Yuan River ports; Hakka; Mien (Yao) charters and Miao and Dong migration songs, mostly in Chinese transcription. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha and the Xiang River cities:** students, envoys and officials who traveled abroad, and the gentry who judged them.
- **Yuan River towns** (Yuanling, Chenxi, Hongjiang, Luxi; Waxiang speakers): boatmen, rafters, river merchants.
- **Labor-exporting counties** (Shaoyang, Loudi, Yongzhou, Hengyang): the yearly journey south and home.
- **Zhangjiajie and Fenghuang hosts** (Tujia and Miao): tourism seen from the receiving end.
- **Yao** of Jiangyong and Jianghua: homeland legends and charters; **Dong** of Tongdao: migration epics and guest customs.
- **Hakka** of Rucheng, Guidong and Liuyang's hills: a people named for arriving.
- **Dongting lake people** of Yueyang and Yiyang: lake travel and flood displacement.
- **Hui** of Changde and Shaoyang: links to distant co-religionists; the Hajj *check*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical travel writing and modern tourism statistics; mixed for migrant labor (good numbers, fewer voices); thin for minority migration accounts outside collections.
- **Languages needed:** Ming and late-Qing classical prose plus modern Chinese; English and French for envoys' and students' contexts; Waxiang and Southwestern Mandarin for river lore; Mien, Miao and Kam song texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hukou and migrant conditions are politically sensitive; tourism disputes may be censored; some Yao charters are family-held. Diaspora pointers stay comparison only. Migration and residence rules are left to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
