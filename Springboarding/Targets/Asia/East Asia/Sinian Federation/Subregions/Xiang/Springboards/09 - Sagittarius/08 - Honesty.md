*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Song-Ming classical Chinese; Changsha Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for words of lying and cheating; Tujia and Miao market and dispute vocabulary through Chinese ethnography. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's commercial and online economy** against **county towns** where face-to-face reputation governs dealing.
- **Exam-heavy families** in Shaoyang, Hengyang and Yongzhou: honesty under gaokao pressure.
- **Tourist hosts** in Fenghuang (Miao, Tujia, Han) and Zhangjiajie (Tujia, Bai): dealing with strangers.
- **Xiangxi market peoples:** Miao and Tujia traders and Han merchants at shared markets.
- **Dong** of Tongdao and **Yao** of Jianghua: village norms of keeping one's word.
- **Rice-growing counties** of the Xiang valley: food honesty after 2013.
- **Hui** butchers and restaurateurs of Changde: honest labeling within a religious rule.
- **Gan-speaking east and Hakka** areas: merchant and lineage reputations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical teaching and official campaigns; mixed for scandal reporting; thin for everyday honesty norms (few surveys at province level).
- **Languages needed:** Song-era classical Chinese and present-day Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for lying and cheating vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and food-safety reporting are censored at times; the credit system is political and its legal side goes to Libra. Avoid repeating stereotypes of "honest" or "cunning" places; name variation instead.
