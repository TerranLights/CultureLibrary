## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's name-school families** against **rural Lou-Shao and Xiangnan counties** of left-behind children.
- **Hengyang and Yueyang:** older academy and lineage-school traditions outside the capital.
- **Xiangxi Miao** (Huayuan, Fenghuang) and **Tujia** (Longshan, Baojing): bilingual schooling, language loss.
- **Dong** pupils of Xinhuang and Zhijiang, **Yao** pupils of Jianghua: minority boarding schools and quota places.
- **Bai** of Sangzhi; **Hui** of Changde (mosque-based Arabic study as a second schooling *check*).
- **Gan-speaking** Liuyang and Liling; **Hakka** of Rucheng and Guidong: lineage scholarship halls.
- **Waxiang** and **Tuhua** speakers: dialect speakers in standard-language classrooms.
- **First-generation university students** from labor-exporting counties.
