# Xiang: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; late-Qing classical Chinese for orthodoxy polemics; English for mission archives; Hui religious vocabulary (Arabic and Persian loans); internet slang. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's late-Qing gentry** against **its reformers**: orthodoxy defended and orthodoxy attacked in the same city.
- **Xiang Army counties** (Xiangxiang, Xiangtan): the defense of the teaching in war.
- **Changsha's Cultural Revolution factions** against **rural counties** where purity campaigns turned on temples and lineages.
- **Protestant congregations** shaped by the Hunan Bible Institute's legacy, against **Catholic** communities of Hengyang.
- **Buddhist and Daoist clergy** (Nanyue, Weishan): institutional defense, not literalism.
- **Hui** of Changde and Shaoyang: internal reform debates.
- **Uyghur-descended community** of Taoyuan: a small faith community under national security policy *check*.
- **Miao, Tujia, Dong and Yao:** usually on the receiving end of purity campaigns against "superstition" rather than authors of them; check their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late-Qing polemics and mission archives; mixed for the Cultural Revolution; thin and one-sided for present-day groups labeled 邪教 (state sources dominate).
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; English for the Biola papers; Arabic and Persian terms for Hui debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** groups labeled 邪教, Islam and Uyghur-related topics, and the Cultural Revolution are politically sensitive; apply the word "fundamentalist" only as the sources do, never as a label for a people. Anti-cult statutes and their enforcement go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
