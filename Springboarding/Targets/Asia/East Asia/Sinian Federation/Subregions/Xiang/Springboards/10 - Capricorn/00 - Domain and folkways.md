# Xiang: Domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified script for modern sources, traditional for pre-modern ones); Xiang 湘语 (Changsha and Shuangfeng forms); Southwestern Mandarin of Changde, Zhangjiajie and Huaihua; Gan and Hakka of eastern Hunan; Tuhua 土话 and Waxiang 瓦乡话; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong (Kam) and Mien. **Concept in one line:** authority and duty: who commands, who obeys and what is owed, across career, status, reputation, age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha-Zhuzhou-Xiangtan (Changzhutan) cities:** seat of provincial office and of the large firms, where careers and status goods are most visible.
- **Xiangzhong (Loudi, Shaoyang, Shuangfeng):** Old Xiang speech and the old recruiting ground of the Xiang Army; a different history of rising through service.
- **Dongting Lake plain (Yueyang, Changde, Yiyang):** lake and dike communities where power over water decides much.
- **Xiangxi Tujia and Miao, Zhangjiajie Tujia, Sangzhi Bai:** former chieftain lands with their own offices and ritual heads.
- **Huaihua Dong and Miao (Tongdao, Xinhuang, Jingzhou), Jianghua and Longhui Yao:** village councils and ritual ranks of their own.
- **Xiangnan (Hengyang, Chenzhou, Yongzhou), with Tuhua, Hakka and Jiangyong Nüshu communities:** a southern border culture with separate speech and customs.
- **Hui and Uyghur-descended communities of Changde (Taoyuan):** religious leadership as a further source of standing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Han urban and elite history (gazetteers, Xiang Army sources, Mao-era materials); mixed for minority lands, where much is in ethnographies and heritage reports rather than the communities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Xiang dialect for field terms; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Mien for minority terms, through local scholars.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be confirmed by the author at Stage 0; this sheet is written for Hunan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Party-era archives are partly closed; political authority is a guarded topic in interviews; minority ritual offices are shared only as each community chooses.
- **Held for review:** none.
