# Xiang: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; classical Chinese for imperial and earlier texts); Xiang dialect terms; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong and Mien terms gathered through local scholars. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiangzhong lineage villages (Shuangfeng, Xiangxiang, Lianyuan):** dense clans, where a lineage head's word and the state's met head on.
- **Former chieftain lands (Yongshun, Longshan, Baojing, Sangzhi):** Tujia and Bai communities with a remembered hereditary authority.
- **Dong villages of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** elder councils working alongside village committees.
- **Yao of Jianghua, Ningyuan and the Longhui Huayao:** authority grounded in charters and ordained ritual rank.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** the imam's and mosque committee's standing beside civil offices.
- **Changsha's younger urban workers and students:** the most exposed to new pressures on a boss's or parent's say.
- **Party cadres and villagers in the same county:** office against informal standing, inside one place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gentry, reform-era and Party sources; thin for the minority communities' own accounts of their offices.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Mien through interpreters; Changsha speech for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about political authority and Party power are guarded; Cultural Revolution sources are partly restricted; ritual offices are discussed only as the communities allow.
