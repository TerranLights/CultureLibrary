# Xiang: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; classical for the Chu songs and Qing letters); Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin folk terms; Tujia, Miao, Dong and Mien terms, and Nüshu script, through specialists. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tujia families of Yongshun and Longshan:** brides' laments make a daughter's duties audible in a way Han weddings may not.
- **Jiangyong and Daoxian women's networks:** sworn sisters and the Nüshu tradition record duty from women's side.
- **Labor-sending counties (Shaoyang, Yongzhou, Huaihua):** filial and parental duty performed by remittance and absence.
- **Miao and Dong hill villages (Huayuan, Tongdao):** exchanged labor and shared building and harvest duties.
- **Hui of Changde and Shaoyang:** religious duties beside lineage ones.
- **Military and veteran families of the Xiangzhong counties:** service as a family expectation, to test against the old saying.
- **Hunanese workers in Guangdong (diaspora, comparison only):** how duty home is kept across the province line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gentry letters and state models; mixed for women's and minority voices, which survive in song, script and ethnography rather than prose.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Nüshu readers; Tujia and Miao speakers; local Xiang varieties for funeral and wedding terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state model figures are politically charged; left-behind family studies concern children; laments and mourning songs are performed in private settings.
- **Held for review:** none.
