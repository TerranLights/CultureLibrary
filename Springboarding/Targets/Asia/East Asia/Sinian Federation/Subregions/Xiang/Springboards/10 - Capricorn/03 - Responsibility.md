# Xiang: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; classical for slips and Song prose); Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin speech for blame idioms; Dong, Miao and Tujia terms gathered in the field. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dongting Lake dike villages (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde):** answerability for a shared flood wall, with a long record of breaches.
- **Mining towns of Lianyuan, Lengshuijiang and Leiyang:** blame in a high-risk trade, among owners, officials and miners.
- **Dong and Miao wooden villages of Tongdao and Huitong:** shared fire risk sets a different weight on one household's carelessness.
- **Tourism towns (Fenghuang, Zhangjiajie):** answerability to visitors and investors after rapid building.
- **Cadres sent to poor villages:** office-holders made answerable for other people's livelihoods.
- **Eldest daughters in labor-sending families:** the household load carried while parents work away, to research, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official inquiries and media cases; thin for how ordinary households talk about blame, which needs fieldwork and local-speech searches.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin idiom; Dong and Miao for village terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster and corruption cases involve named people and Party discipline; some inquiry reports are not public in full.
- **Held for review:** none.
