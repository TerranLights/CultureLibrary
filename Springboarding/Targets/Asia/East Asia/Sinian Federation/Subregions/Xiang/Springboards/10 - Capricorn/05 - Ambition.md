# Xiang: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified); Changsha and Xiangzhong speech for striving idioms; Tujia and Miao terms in tourism and migrant communities, through local scholars. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xinhua, Shaodong and Lianyuan:** merchant and founder counties, where rising often runs through trade and kin networks.
- **Changsha's young professionals:** a likely center of talk about over-competition and lying flat, to test.
- **Civil-service aspirants:** ambition aimed at office, with its own rituals of exams and patrons.
- **Miao and Tujia hosts in Xiangxi and Zhangjiajie:** rising through heritage tourism, inside villages that also judge it.
- **Women across generations:** from Xiang Jingyu's era to contestants on Mango TV.
- **Hunanese workers in Guangdong (diaspora, comparison only):** how ambition abroad feeds the house built at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in media and literature; mixed in surveys; thin on how minority villagers describe their own aims.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Xiang speech for sayings like bàmán; Miao and Tujia for tourism-village fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the officialdom theme touches on corruption; Mao-related material is politically framed; talent-show voting was itself a political debate.
- **Held for review:** none.
