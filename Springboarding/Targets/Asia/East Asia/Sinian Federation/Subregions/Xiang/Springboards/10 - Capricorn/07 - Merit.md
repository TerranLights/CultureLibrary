## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's top-school families against county-town and rural students:** whether the same exam feels like the same race.
- **Tujia, Miao, Dong and Yao students with bonus points:** merit argued from both sides of one policy.
- **Craft lineages (Shaping embroiderers, Liling potters, Liuyang firework makers):** skill judged by masters and by state titles.
- **Tujia brocade weavers (a craft associated with women, to check):** earned mastery in a household craft now given heritage status.
- **Civil-service candidates and serving cadres:** exams set against seniority and patrons.
- **Descendants of Xiang Army families in Xiangxiang and Shuangfeng:** a remembered path of rising through war rather than exams.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam and official records; mixed for crafts and song; minority views of bonus points need careful sampling.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for exam records; Tujia, Dong and Miao for skill titles; Standard Chinese for policy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic bonus points and Cultural Revolution admissions are politically charged; individual exam scores are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
