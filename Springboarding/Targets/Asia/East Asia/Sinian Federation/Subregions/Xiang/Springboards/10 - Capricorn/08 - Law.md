# Xiang: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; classical for Han silk texts and Qing precedents); Xiang speech for folk legal phrases; Dong and Miao terms for customary law, through local specialists. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. This sheet takes the spirit of the law only; the letter belongs to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang:** a written-and-recited community law that sits beside state law.
- **Miao of Huayuan and Fenghuang:** arbiters and payment for wrongs, a different idea of what settles a case.
- **Yao of Jianghua:** check whether stone-tablet compacts like those reported among Yao elsewhere exist here.
- **Rural petitioners of Hengyang and Yongzhou against Changsha litigants:** two routes to redress, and two views of the courts.
- **Hui of Changde:** religious rules on marriage and inheritance set beside civil law.
- **Lawyers and officials trained in Xiangtan and Changsha:** the profession's own account of what the law is for.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite and official texts; mixed for customary law; legal consciousness among ordinary people needs surveys and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for silk texts and Qing precedents; Dong and Miao for customary law; Standard Chinese for courts and media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning, labor re-education and corruption are politically sensitive; customary law is shared as communities choose; court-level data belong to Libra's letter-of-the-law work.
- **Held for review:** none.
