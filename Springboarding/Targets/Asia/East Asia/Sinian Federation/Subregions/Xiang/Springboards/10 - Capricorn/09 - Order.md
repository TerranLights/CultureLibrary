*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified; classical for Song and Qing frontier records); Changsha speech for street-life words; Dong, Tujia and Miao terms for ritual order, through local scholars. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's night-life districts and their neighbors:** bustle welcomed by some, reported as nuisance by others.
- **Xiangxi Miao and Tujia counties once called bandit country:** order told by the state and by local families.
- **Daoxian and other southern Hunan counties:** memories of 1967 that may shape how order is spoken of.
- **Dongting Lake fishing families:** order imposed on the lake, from the side of those it moved ashore.
- **Dong of Xinhuang and Nuo performers of Yuanling:** order of the cosmos and the house kept by ritual.
- **Meishan uplands (Xinhua, Anhua):** a region long described by outsiders as outside order.
- **Jishou residents:** a minority-prefecture city's experience of unrest and crackdown.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and elite accounts; thin for the views of those cast as disorderly (Miao in rebellion, bandits, 1967 victims, protesters).
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Miao, Tujia and Dong for frontier and ritual order; Changsha speech for street terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution killings, protest events and sweep-black cases are politically sensitive and partly censored; Nuo rites are shared as performers choose; frontier records use offensive terms for peoples.
- **Held for review:** none.
