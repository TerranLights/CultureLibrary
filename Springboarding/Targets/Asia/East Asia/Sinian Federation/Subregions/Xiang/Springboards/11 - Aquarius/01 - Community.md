# Xiang: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语 varieties; Southwestern Mandarin of the northwest and southwest; Gan of the east; Tuhua and Waxiang; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Dong, Mien. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest, cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha old city (Pozijie 坡子街, Taiping Street 太平街) against new high-rise estates:** street neighborhoods against gated compounds.
- **Zhuzhou and Xiangtan work-unit districts:** company-town communities and their aftermath.
- **Dongting polder villages (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde, Huarong, Nanxian):** communities bound by dikes and floods.
- **Central Hunan lineage hamlets (Loudi, Shaoyang, Xiangxiang):** 屋场-type settlement, *check*.
- **Gan-speaking eastern counties and the Hakka of Rucheng and Yanling:** different settlement histories at the Jiangxi border.
- **Southern Hunan Tuhua villages (Yongzhou, Chenzhou):** single-lineage villages with their own speech.
- **Tujia (Yongshun, Longshan, Baojing, Sangzhi), Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Chengbu), Dong (Tongdao, Xinhuang, Zhijiang), Yao (Jianghua), Bai (Sangzhi):** village forms, markets and festivals.
- **Waxiang speakers along the Yuan River:** a community defined by speech.
- **Hui congregations of Changde and Shaoyang:** mosque communities, *check*.
- **Comparison only:** Hunanese migrant-worker enclaves in Guangdong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and literary sources, mixed for lived neighborhood life, thin for minority village life outside heritage and tourism writing.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang, Gan and Southwestern Mandarin for oral sources; Dong and Miao for village terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** residents' committees are partly state organs, and grid management 网格化 blurs community with surveillance; flood relocations and demolitions are contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
