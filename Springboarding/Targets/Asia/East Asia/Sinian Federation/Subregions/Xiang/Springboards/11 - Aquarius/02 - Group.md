# Xiang: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), one member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Classical Chinese; Xiang 湘语 (Changsha-type New Xiang, Loudi-Shaoyang Old Xiang); Gan of Liuyang and Liling; Southwestern Mandarin of Changde and Huaihua; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Mien and Kam terms. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit; how the person is weighed against the group, where "us" ends and "them" begins, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Xiang Army home counties (Xiangxiang, Xiangtan, Shuangfeng, Loudi):** kin- and county-based military groups, a founding model of group loyalty.
- **Liuyang, Liling and Pingjiang (Gan-speaking):** sworn-society and uprising history at the Jiangxi border.
- **Fenghuang, Huayuan, Jishou (Xiangxi Miao):** the frontier-wall line; outsider labels imposed from outside.
- **Yongshun, Longshan, Baojing (Tujia):** former *tusi* domains and clan branches.
- **Jianghua and Jiangyong (Yao):** charter-based group identity.
- **Daoxian and southern Yongzhou:** the memory of 1967 killings.
- **Shuikoushan and Hengyang mining towns:** crews and unions.
- **Changsha's young urban public:** fandoms and online groups.
- **Dong of Tongdao and Bai of Sangzhi:** group forms other than the Han lineage.
- **Comparison only:** Hunanese native-place associations elsewhere in the federation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military, rebel and revolutionary groups; mixed for everyday group life; thin for minority clan structures outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Xiang and Gan for oral history; Mien, Tujia and Xiangxi Miao for self-names and group terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1967 killings and Cultural Revolution factions are restricted topics; sources may be removed. Labels such as 生苗 are official slurs and stay in quotation. Secret-society history is mostly told by the state that suppressed it. Province-level collectivism scores invite stereotype; test them, never assume them.
- **Held for review:** none.
