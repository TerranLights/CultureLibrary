# Xiang: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), a Sinian Federation member state in East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; late-Qing documentary and Classical Chinese; Changsha and Old Xiang speech for oral history; Southwestern Mandarin of Xiangxi and Huaihua; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao and Mien for frontier reforms. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Changsha's reform gentry against its conservative gentry:** the 1898 split ran through one city.
- **Shaoyang, Anhua and Xiangyin:** home counties of statecraft officials; how each honors its reformer.
- **Fenghuang and the old Miao frontier:** reforms imposed by garrison and school; Miao and Han memory side by side.
- **Yongshun and Longshan Tujia:** the end of native chiefs in 1728 and its retelling.
- **Dongting polders (Yueyang, Yiyang, Anxiang, Nanxian):** households moved by lake restoration.
- **Zhuzhou Qingshuitang, Hengyang and Chenzhou mining belts:** reform after pollution.
- **Jianghua and Jiangyong Yao:** mountain resettlement and poverty programs, *check*.
- **Dong counties of Huaihua (Tongdao, Xinhuang):** campaigns to change customs set against heritage protection.
- **Comparison only:** reform movements in the federation's other member states.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1840-1925 elite reform; mixed for recent reforms (official sources dominate); thin for reforms as minority communities experienced them.
- **Languages needed:** late-Qing documentary Chinese; Standard Chinese; Xiang and Southwestern Mandarin for oral history; Miao, Tujia and Mien for frontier memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reform against revolution is an officially narrated story; pollution, resettlement and 1920s federalism are touchy. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn: this sheet points to movements and people, not statutes.
- **Held for review:** none.
