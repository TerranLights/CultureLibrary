# Xiang: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), East Asia; a member state of the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese, including the Chu song style of the *Chu ci*; Changsha and other Xiang speech; Tuhua of southern Yongzhou; Mien and Xiangxi Miao for oral epics and legends. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Miluo and Yueyang (Dongting's east shore):** places that keep the Qu Yuan and Fan Zhongyan ideals in festival and school.
- **Hengyang and Xinhua:** home ground of a loyalist philosopher and a revolutionary martyr, for older and newer ideals of principle.
- **Daoxian, Jiangyong and Jianghua:** a Song thinker's home and the Yao's lost-homeland legend in the same corner.
- **Xiangxi Miao (Huayuan, Fenghuang, Jishou):** oral tradition as a separate source of ideals.
- **Changsha's urban youth, past and present:** Red Guards, sent-down youth, today's volunteers.
- **Shaoyang hill counties (Suining, Chengbu):** Miao and Han villages that receive idealists from outside.
- **Tujia of Zhangjiajie and Yongshun:** ideals in Tujia ritual song, to be found.
- **Dong of Tongdao:** village song traditions, for ideals of the good village.
- **Comparison only:** Hunanese idealists abroad (students in Japan, 1900s).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and revolutionary idealism; mixed for present-day volunteering; thin for minority ideals beyond collected epics.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese and *Chu ci* diction; Standard Chinese; Xiang for oral history; Mien and Xiangxi Miao for legend and epic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyr stories are officially curated; Red Guard idealism and its violence remain restricted topics; some memoirs are published only outside the federation. Yao and Miao ritual texts may hold knowledge that keepers restrict.
- **Held for review:** none.
