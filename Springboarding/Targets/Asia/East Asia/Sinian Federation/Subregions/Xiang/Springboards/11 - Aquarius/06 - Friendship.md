# Xiang: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia, part of the Sinian Federation. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Tang-era and later Classical Chinese; Changsha, Xiangtan and Loudi speech; Southwestern Mandarin of Changde and Huaihua; Jiangyong Tuhua and Nüshu; Kam (Dong), Mien (Yao), Xiangxi Miao. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiangyong and Daoxian women:** *laotong* and bonded sisters, and what replaced them.
- **Dong, Yao and Miao villages of Tongdao, Jianghua and Chengbu:** *laogeng* ties within and across peoples.
- **Changsha's young workers and students:** night-market and online friendship.
- **Xiangtan and Changsha men's circles:** cards, mahjong and betel nut as the frame of men's friendship.
- **Changde and Yongzhou:** the Tang exiles' towns, and how they keep their poets' friendship.
- **Rural elders in labor-sending counties (Shaoyang, Loudi, Yongzhou):** friends as care when children are away.
- **Tujia of Sangzhi and Zhangjiajie; Hui of Changde:** friendship across faith and people, to be found.
- **Hakka of Rucheng:** border-county friendships with Jiangxi and Guangdong neighbors.
- **Comparison only:** friendship among Hunanese migrant workers in Guangdong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and elite friendship; mixed for women's ritual friendship (strong Nüshu literature, little elsewhere); thin for everyday men's friendship and minority ritual friendship.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and Standard Chinese; Xiang for oral sources; Nüshu reading and Jiangyong Tuhua; Kam, Mien and Xiangxi Miao for ritual-friendship terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *guanxi* among officials touches corruption (law goes to Libra and Capricorn); betel nut is a health topic; Nüshu is marketed as heritage, so separate staged from lived accounts. Men's and women's friendship vary by county and generation: no single voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
