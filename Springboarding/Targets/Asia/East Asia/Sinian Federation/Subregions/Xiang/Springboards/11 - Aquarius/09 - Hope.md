# Xiang: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘 (Hunan 湖南), in East Asia; a Sinian Federation member state. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese and Chu-era script; Xiang speech of Changsha, Xiangtan and Hengyang; Southwestern Mandarin of Huaihua and Xiangxi; Mien (Yao), Xiangxi Miao, Kam (Dong) and Tujia for vow rites. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hengyang and the Nanyue pilgrim catchment:** vow-making Han villagers from many counties.
- **Yuanling, Xinhuang and Huaihua:** Nuo vow rites among Han, Dong and Tujia, county by county.
- **Jianghua and Jiangyong Yao:** vow repayment to King Pan.
- **Huayuan and Fenghuang Miao:** buffalo offerings and household vows.
- **Tongdao Dong:** village protection hopes at the Sa altar.
- **Xiangtan and Shaoshan:** political pilgrimage and folk petition at one site.
- **Labor-sending counties (Shaoyang, Yongzhou, Loudi):** hope carried by migrants and their remittances.
- **Changsha families:** exam hopes and medical crowdfunding.
- **Chenzhou:** a city's memory of the 2008 storm.
- **Changsha Christians and Changde Hui:** hope in scripture-based faiths, *check* access.
- **Comparison only:** Hunanese migrants' hopes as told in Guangdong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual hope (well-studied vow rites) and migration data; mixed for everyday hope; thin for numbers on hope at provincial level.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Chu-script studies for the silk manuscript; Xiang for oral sources; Mien, Xiangxi Miao, Kam and Tujia for vow-rite terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** folk veneration of Mao and unregistered religion are touchy; Nuo, Yao and Miao ritual texts may be restricted to initiates. Crowdfunding pages carry personal data: cite patterns, not persons. Despair and suicide data are restricted and sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
