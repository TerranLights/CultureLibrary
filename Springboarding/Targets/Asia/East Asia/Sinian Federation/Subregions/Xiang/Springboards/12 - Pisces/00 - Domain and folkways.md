# Xiang: The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, a member state of the Sinian Federation (lands: Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Xiang 湘语 (New Xiang of Changsha, Old Xiang of Loudi and Shaoyang, and the Chenxu, Hengzhou and Yongquan subgroups); Gan, Southwestern Mandarin, Hakka, Tuhua 土话, Waxiang 瓦乡话; Tujia, Xiangxi Miao, Kam (Dong), Mien (Yao). **Concept in one line:** how a culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape; the folkway is magic ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Changsha-Zhuzhou-Xiangtan: medical and secular idioms of distress against the countryside's.
- The Meishan zone (Xinhua, Anhua, Lengshuijiang, Shaoyang): the densest household ritual-master tradition.
- Tujia (Yongshun, Longshan, Sangzhi) and Miao (Fenghuang, Huayuan, Chengbu): separate specialists and languages.
- Dong of Tongdao, Xinhuang and Jingzhou: village deity cults and masked plays.
- Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong: Mountain Yao and Pingdi Yao 平地瑶 differ in rites.
- Dongting Lake towns (Yueyang, Yiyang, Changde): water spirits and flood memory.
- Waxiang speakers of Yuanling and Luxi; Hui and Uyghur of Taoyuan; Bai of Sangzhi: small groups, own sacred boundaries.
- Gan east (Liuyang, Pingjiang) and Hakka south (Rucheng, Guidong): dialect borders that may track ritual ones.
- Hunanese migrants in the Pearl River Delta cities: comparison only, to see which rites for the unseen travel with them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, archaeology and Xiangxi ethnography; mixed for central Hunan ritual; thin for province-level numbers.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang dialects; Tujia, Miao, Kam, Mien; French for the EFEO work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gu-sorcery accusations once fell on Miao women: a history of accusation, never a people's belief. Folk ritual is often filed as "feudal superstition" 封建迷信, which shapes what gets published; ritual masters may keep texts private; broadcast rules limit supernatural content.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Hunan as a working list the author still confirms; the exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Held for review:** none.
