**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang; the Tuhua of the far south (Jiangyong, the home of Nüshu); Tujia and Kam (Dong) song vocabularies; Xiangxi Miao. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Women of Jiangyong and neighboring Daoxian 道县: a written women's channel for shared feeling, set against men's speech in the same villages.
- Tujia families of Xiangxi: whether bridal lament still teaches shared weeping or has become a stage art.
- Dong villages of Tongdao and Xinhuang: song as group attunement; compare with Miao song exchanges in Huayuan.
- Changsha youth and urban professionals: therapy vocabulary (共情, 情商) beside older idioms.
- Doctors and nurses in Changsha's large hospitals, and the families who clash with them.
- Gan-speaking Liuyang and Pingjiang, and the Hakka hill towns: whether everyday reading of others differs across dialect lines.
- Jiangyong and Xiangxi women married out of the province: comparison only, for whether their channels of shared feeling survive the move.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Nüshu and minority song; mixed for psychology studies (many student samples); thin for everyday empathy outside schools and hospitals.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; the Jiangyong Tuhua in which Nüshu is read; Tujia, Kam and Miao for song texts; Changsha Xiang for everyday idiom.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** medical disputes are reported under tight control; Nüshu has become a tourism brand, so heritage versions may differ from transmitters' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** none.
