# Xiang: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese; Xiang (Changsha, Lou-Shao, and the Chenxu subgroup of the Yuan valley); Waxiang; Xiangxi Miao and Tujia for uprising and ancestor lore. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Xiang valley gentry tradition (Xiangtan, Xiangxiang, Liuyang): the loudest line of soldier-scholar sacrifice; test whether others share it.
- Yuan valley towns (Xupu, Chenxi, Yuanling): Mulian opera and Waxiang communities, where filial sacrifice is performed.
- Miao of Fenghuang, Huayuan and Songtao borderlands: a memory of uprising that may not match state martyrdom.
- Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan: the chieftain era and ancestor heroes as a separate frame.
- Dongting Lake counties (Huarong, Yuanjiang, Xiangyin): flood labor and dike sacrifice.
- Migrant-sending Shaoyang, Loudi and Yongzhou: parents gone for years, seen from the children's side.
- Women in every region: who carried the gazetteer honor and the household's self-denial.
- Hunanese communities elsewhere: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and literary martyrdom; mixed for war memory; thin for household sacrifice.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese for gazetteers and the *Shiji*; Standard Chinese; Miao and Tujia for uprising memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** revolutionary martyrs are protected by law against "defamation"; Nationalist war dead and 1795 Miao rebels are politically charged; chaste-widow suicides need careful handling.
- **Held for review:** none.
