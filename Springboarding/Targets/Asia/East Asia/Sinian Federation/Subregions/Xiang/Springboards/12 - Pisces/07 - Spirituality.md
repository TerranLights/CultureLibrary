## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Pilgrim villages around Hengshan (Hengyang, Hengshan, Xiangtan counties): mountain devotion by walking.
- The Meishan heartland (Xinhua, Anhua): household ritual and hunting cults.
- Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang; Tujia of Yongshun and Longshan; Miao of Fenghuang and Huayuan; Yao of Jianghua and Jiangyong: four separate village sacred systems.
- Hui and Uyghur of Taoyuan's Fengshu township; Christian congregations of Changsha and Yueyang.
- Urban Changsha: qigong, meditation and "Buddha-style" talk among the young.
- Shaoshan and Xiangtan: revolutionary sites as places of reverence.
- Hunanese pilgrims who return from other provinces for Nanyue: comparison only, for the pull of the home mountain.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Nanyue and Daoist history; mixed for minority rites (much is heritage-framed); thin for private, non-institutional practice.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Kam, Tujia, Miao and Mien for village rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some ritual knowledge is held by masters and not for outsiders; unregistered religion is under pressure; Mao devotion is politically charged; the line between faith (Sagittarius) and practice must be kept.
- **Held for review:** none.
