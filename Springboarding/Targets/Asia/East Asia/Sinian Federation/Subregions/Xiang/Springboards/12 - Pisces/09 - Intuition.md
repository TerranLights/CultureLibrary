# Xiang: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; classical Chinese (Song Neo-Confucian debate, Chu and Han manuscripts); Xiang and the Gan and Southwestern Mandarin of the edges, for proverbs and folk practice; Tujia, Miao and Kam for divination terms. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Changsha's scholar-official tradition (Yuelu, Huxiang school): learned debate on insight.
- Practitioners of Chinese medicine in Changsha and county hospitals: expert feel as a profession.
- Meishan villages (Xinhua, Anhua): geomancers and ritual masters who read land and illness.
- Miao, Tujia and Dong villages: separate divination arts; compare who may practice.
- Dongting fishermen and Xiang valley rice farmers: reading weather and water by long experience.
- Older rural women: spirit-medium and soul-asking practice, where it survives.
- Hunanese ritual masters and practitioners of Chinese medicine working outside the province: comparison only, for how a trained eye fares among strangers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Neo-Confucian debate and medicine; mixed for folk divination (much classed as "superstition"); thin for everyday hunches outside psychology papers.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Xiang for proverbs; Miao, Tujia and Kam for divination.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination and mediumship are discouraged, so practitioners may avoid researchers; almanacs and lots are records of practice, never sources of fact.
- **Held for review:** none.
