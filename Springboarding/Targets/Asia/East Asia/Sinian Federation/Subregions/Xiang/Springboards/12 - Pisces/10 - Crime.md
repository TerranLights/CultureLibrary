# Xiang: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang street slang; Southwestern Mandarin of the Xiangxi towns where the bandit lore is told; Qin script and documentary Chinese for the Liye slips; Miao and Tujia for community ideas of wrongdoing. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Western Hunan (Xiangxi, Huaihua, Zhangjiajie): the bandit past as shame, legend and tourist draw.
- Changsha: the urban hoodlum, gang and corruption stories of a big city.
- Daoxian and Yongzhou: a local memory of mass killing and silence.
- Hengyang and other prefectural cities: officials' wrongdoing in public view.
- Miao and Tujia villages: community ways of naming guilt and repairing harm.
- Dong of Xinhuang: a county where one case drew national anger.
- Migrant-sending counties: wrongs suffered far from home and abductions.
- Hunanese migrants caught up in crime reporting elsewhere: comparison only, for how the provincial label of wrongdoer is applied from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for banditry history and famous cases; mixed for safety polls (official framing); thin for victims' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Qin script for Liye; Changsha slang; Miao and Tujia for village practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Daoxian killings and other campaign-era violence are restricted topics; trafficking and corruption reporting is censored; the "bandit country" image of western Hunan is a stereotype to record, never adopt. Law and courts go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
