# Xiang: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Xiang 湘, Sinian Federation member state (Hunan 湖南), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Changsha Xiang (fright words, child-scaring talk); Lou-Shao Xiang and the Gan of the east for soul-loss cures; classical Chinese for Song tales and gazetteer disaster entries; Kam and Miao for shape-shifter and ghost lore. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Changsha families: fright cures and child-scaring next to clinic and phone-based anxiety talk.
- Lou-Shao and Meishan villages: soul-loss cures by ritual masters.
- Dongting Lake counties: flood dread as yearly rhythm.
- Changde: germ-warfare memory in families.
- Miao of Xiangxi, and Dong of Tongdao and Xinhuang: their own feared beings and bogey tales; the memory of being feared as "poisoners".
- Southern hill counties (Chenzhou, Yongzhou): tiger lore and older wild-animal fear.
- Young people in the universities: social anxiety and exam dread.
- Hunanese families scattered by work: comparison only, for fears carried about children and parents left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk tales, disaster history and war memory; mixed for anxiety surveys; thin for private fears.
- **Languages needed:** classical Chinese; Standard Chinese; Changsha and Lou-Shao Xiang; Kam and Miao.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poison and sorcery accusations stigmatized Miao women; germ-warfare memory carries diplomatic weight; horror and ghost content is limited in licensed media; anxiety data are partly unpublished.
- **Held for review:** none.
