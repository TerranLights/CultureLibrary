# Yue (粤): The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (this sheet covers Yue's own lands and peoples, not the whole federation), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese (traditional script in Hong Kong and Macau, simplified elsewhere), Standard Written Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese (Macau), English (Hong Kong). **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: what a self is here, and how it asserts itself (the self, the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative; naming ways; sport ways as a runner-up).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangfu Cantonese of the Pearl River Delta** (Guangzhou, Foshan, Shunde): the core Yue self others define themselves against.
- **Siyi (四邑) and Taishanese areas:** a home-county self tied to long emigration.
- **Hong Kong urban Cantonese against New Territories lineage villagers (Punti, Weitou):** an urban civic label against lineage membership.
- **Danjia (疍家, Tanka) boat people and Hoklo fishers:** a self long named by others.
- **Macanese (土生葡人) and Macau's Portuguese residents:** mixed-ancestry identity.
- **Chaoshan (Teochew) and Leizhou Min speakers in eastern and western Guangdong:** non-Cantonese Han selves inside Yue.
- **Guangxi Baihua (Cantonese) cities, Pinghua speakers and the Guiliu Mandarin north:** three Han language worlds in one province.
- **Zhuang (northern and southern), Yao (Mien, Pai Yao of Liannan, Hongyao of Longsheng), Miao, Dong, Mulao, Maonan, Jing (Dongxing), and Guangdong's She:** separate self-names and personhood rites.
- **Migrant workers in Shenzhen and Dongguan; domestic workers in Hong Kong and Macau:** selves formed away from home.
- **Hakka communities (optional):** in Hong Kong the "Punti" self was long defined against the Hakka "guest" label, so the pair shows identity as a relation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau; mixed for Guangdong; thin and mostly in Chinese for Guangxi's minorities. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** this sheet is written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Standard Zhuang and Sawndip, Teochew, Portuguese, English; Yao and Dong for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hong Kong identity polling is politically charged; mainland ethnic categories are state-assigned; some Zhuang and Yao ritual texts belong to their specialists.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong's lands (category only, set aside under the standing rule).
