# Yue (粤): Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and Foshan Cantonese against Hong Kong Cantonese:** the same language with different public stages for speaking up (phone-ins and forums in one, state-run hotlines in the other).
- **Macau's Chinese majority and its Macanese:** satire and associations as separate routes to a voice.
- **Migrant workers in Shenzhen, Dongguan and Foshan:** claims made from outside the local language and household registry.
- **Hailufeng and Chaoshan Min speakers:** village-level collective claims (Wukan) and lineage-based voice.
- **Zhuang, Dong and Yao song-fair communities:** assertion inside sung exchange, where wit is the weapon.
- **Danjia boat communities:** whose claims ashore were long unheard; check their own accounts.
- **New Territories lineage villagers:** claims made as a lineage rather than as persons.
- **Domestic workers in Hong Kong and Macau:** the asymmetry of saying no to an employer who is also a landlord.
- **Generations:** students and young workers against parents raised in the 1960s and 1970s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (corpora, phone-ins, forums); good for Guangdong labor studies; thin for Guangxi minorities outside song collections.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (spoken and written, including forum slang), Standard Written Chinese, Zhuang, Hailufeng Min, Portuguese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest material from 2011 (Wukan) and 2019 (Hong Kong) is politically sensitive and some archives have been withdrawn; labor activists may be at risk if named. Rights and labor law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** any pointer on speaking up among South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong, set aside as a category only.

## 5. First queries

2. Search HKCanCor for refusals (唔得, 唔要, 唔使) and code how each is softened or hardened.
