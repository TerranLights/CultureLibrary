## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shunde, Panyu and Zhongshan silk districts:** marriage resistance and delayed transfer give women's autonomy a local history.
- **Hong Kong young adults in subdivided flats or parental homes:** autonomy limited by housing.
- **Guangzhou families against Hong Kong families:** different patient-consent and schooling choices.
- **Migrant factory workers in Dongguan and Shenzhen:** autonomy lost to dormitory rules.
- **Dong villages of Sanjiang and Yao villages of Jinxiu:** collective self-rule traditions.
- **Zhuang villages:** check how marriage choice worked at song fairs, where young people met partners themselves.
- **Teochew families in Chaoshan:** lineage authority over life choices; check against delta patterns.
- **Macanese families:** Catholic and Chinese pulls on the same choice.
- **Domestic workers living in employers' homes:** the narrowest personal room in the four lands.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka women's long record of field labor gave them a different say in household choices; check against Cantonese delta women.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (surveys, bioethics, NGOs); good historical work on the delta; thin for minority self-rule outside Chinese ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Dong, Portuguese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the political autonomy of Hong Kong and Macau, and of the Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, is politically sensitive and its legal side goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); labor NGO material may be withdrawn.
- **Held for review:** family decision-making among South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong, as a category only.
