*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shunde and Foshan township entrepreneurs:** collective rural enterprise turned private.
- **Shenzhen's migrant founders:** initiative by newcomers ("come and you are a Shenzhener" is the city's slogan).
- **Hong Kong's Shanghainese industrialists and local Cantonese small firms:** two kinds of founding.
- **Teochew merchants of Chaoshan:** initiative carried through kin and native-place networks.
- **Siyi emigrant families:** initiative as leaving.
- **Macau's association leaders:** initiative as organizing.
- **Wuzhou and Yulin Cantonese traders in Guangxi:** river and market trade.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** cross-border trade with Vietnam (Vietnamese material is comparison only).
- **Zhuang and Yao villages of Longsheng's terraces:** village-run tourism ventures.
- **Women in delta family firms:** check who gets credit for starting things.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on business founding and reform history; thin on everyday initiative (helping unasked) outside Hong Kong surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Teochew, Zhuang, Portuguese, English; Classical and early modern Chinese for Canton system records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civic groups in Hong Kong have closed since 2020 and their records may be scattered; reform-era official history is curated. Business law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** business founding by South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong (named only as a category).
