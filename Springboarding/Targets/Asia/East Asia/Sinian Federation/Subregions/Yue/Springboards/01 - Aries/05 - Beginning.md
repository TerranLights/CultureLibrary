*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and delta Cantonese:** flower markets and 開年 as the Lunar New Year's first acts.
- **Hong Kong's waves of arrivals** (1949, 1962, 1970s, post-1997): arrival as the founding story.
- **Shenzhen's migrants:** a city where almost everyone is a newcomer.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** 出花園 as a rite of beginning not found in the delta.
- **Western Guangdong (Maoming, Zhanjiang, Leizhou):** 年例 timing against the delta's calendar.
- **Zhuang:** Buluotuo and 三月三 as their own beginnings.
- **Yao (Mien, Pai Yao, Hongyao):** initiation as a second birth.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** an arrival story kept in festival.
- **Macanese:** baptism and the Catholic year against the Lunar year.
- **Danjia boat families:** launching a new boat; check their rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and birth customs (heritage lists, gazetteers); good for Hong Kong arrival stories; thin for starting over after failure outside Hong Kong statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Zhuang and Sawndip, Mien, Portuguese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border-escape history is sensitive in the mainland; some Yao initiation knowledge is restricted to initiates.
- **Held for review:** arrival and new-start stories of South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong, kept here as a category only.
