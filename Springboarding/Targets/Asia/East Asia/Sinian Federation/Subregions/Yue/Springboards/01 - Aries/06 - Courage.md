## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Foshan and Hong Kong martial-arts lineages:** courage as a trained, coded virtue.
- **Guangzhou's revolution memory against Hong Kong's wartime memory:** two official stories of the brave.
- **Zhuang communities of the Youjiang and Zuojiang valleys:** their own heroes (Wa Shi, Nong Zhigao).
- **Guangxi Han soldiers:** the Black Flags and the later Guangxi armies' reputation for toughness.
- **Danjia and Hoklo fishers:** sea courage and its rites.
- **Macanese:** 1622 as a founding act of collective valor.
- **Migrant workers in construction and factories:** endurance as daily courage.
- **Journalists and doctors in Guangzhou:** moral courage in reporting and in epidemics.
- **Women:** check whether women's endurance in silk work and in households is spoken of as courage.
- **Hakka (optional):** the Hakka 硬頸 ("stiff neck") idea of unbending endurance offers a neighboring model of courage to set beside Cantonese 捱.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martial-arts culture, memorials and war history; mixed for minority heroes; thin for everyday moral courage outside journalism.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war and revolution memory is state-curated; Nong Zhigao is claimed differently in Vietnam (comparison only); recent Hong Kong protest courage is politically sensitive; journalists may still be at risk.
- **Held for review:** courage pointers touching South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong (category only, under the standing rule).
