# Yue (粤): Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Taishanese, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shunde and Panyu silk-district women:** the delta's historical model of economic independence.
- **Hong Kong young adults:** independence delayed by housing (上車).
- **Hong Kong and Macau elders living alone:** self-reliance at the end of life.
- **Siyi remittance households:** independence of the emigrant, dependence of the home.
- **Zhuang and Yao mountain villages in Hechi and Baise:** subsistence farming against relocation and aid.
- **Migrant workers in the delta:** self-support that also funds families elsewhere.
- **New Territories villagers:** land and inherited houses as a base for not depending on others.
- **Danjia families moved ashore:** from owning a boat to renting a flat.
- **Macau residents:** the handout as a test of self-reliance talk.
- **Domestic workers:** whose independence rests on an employer's home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong welfare and housing; good history on the delta; mixed for Guangxi, where most material is state reporting.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Taishanese, Zhuang, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** any talk of regional or territorial independence for Hong Kong, Macau or Guangdong is politically sensitive and in places illegal; that legal side goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Relocation reporting is official and may omit dissent.
- **Held for review:** self-support and welfare pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong, set aside as a category.
