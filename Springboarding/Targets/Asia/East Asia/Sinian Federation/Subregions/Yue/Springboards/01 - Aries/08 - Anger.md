# Yue (粤): Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** ritual outlets (打小人), profanity and protest.
- **Guangzhou Cantonese:** the same language, different rules for public anger.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Hailufeng Min:** a long history of lineage feuding.
- **Macau's Chinese and Macanese:** 1966 remembered from two sides.
- **Migrant workers in Zengcheng, Dongguan and Shenzhen:** anger as outsiders.
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong villages:** elder mediation in place of courts.
- **Women:** domestic-violence data and the "angry woman" stereotype online; name variation, never one voice.
- **Youth against elders:** angry youth (憤青) online against older restraint.
- **Danjia fishing communities:** check how quarrels at sea and in harbors are handled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (welfare data, NGOs, music, protest archives); mixed for Guangdong (feud history strong, present data thin); thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (including slang and profanity), Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Zhuang, Portuguese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1967, 1966 and 2011 unrest are politically sensitive; domestic-violence material needs care; any legal side of riots or violence goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** anger and conflict pointers involving South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong (a category only).
