## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong families:** a single public exam and a large tutoring market.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** a rivalry between two cities over rank and money.
- **Delta villages and lineages:** dragon-boat rivalry as old inter-village competition.
- **Cheung Chau islanders:** a local contest turned into a show.
- **Dong and Miao villages of Sanjiang and Rongshui:** team scrambles and bullfights.
- **Zhuang youth at festivals:** courtship games.
- **Macau:** motor sport and the betting economy.
- **Teochew merchants:** cooperation inside the network, rivalry outside it.
- **Migrant workers:** piece rates and production quotas as daily competition.
- **Girls against boys:** check how exam and sport rivalry is told for each.
