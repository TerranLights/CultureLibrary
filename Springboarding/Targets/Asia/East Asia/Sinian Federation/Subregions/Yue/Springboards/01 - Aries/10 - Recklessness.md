## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Macau residents and casino workers:** living beside the betting economy without being its players.
- **Hong Kong youth:** drugs, street racing and gangster-film culture; name variation, not one type.
- **Delta speculators in shares and property:** financial risk as a respected skill or a vice.
- **Rural Guangdong and Guangxi villages:** underground lotteries and road risk.
- **Danjia and coastal fishers:** sea risk as livelihood, apart from thrill-seeking.
- **Hikers and urban explorers in Hong Kong:** chosen risk on hills and rooftops.
- **Migrant workers:** risk imposed by work, not chosen.
- **Zhuang and Yao villages:** check local words for rashness and how elders warn young men.
