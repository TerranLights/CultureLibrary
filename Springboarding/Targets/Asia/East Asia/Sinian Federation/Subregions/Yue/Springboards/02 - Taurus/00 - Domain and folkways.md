# Yue (粤): The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples only), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese (traditional characters in Hong Kong and Macau, simplified in Guangdong and Guangxi), Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how it is held (possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth; food, dress and wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangfu delta Cantonese** (Guangzhou, Foshan, Shunde): silk, fish-pond and trade money as the core case.
- **Hong Kong urban renters and owners against New Territories indigenous villagers:** bought flats against inherited lineage land (the Small House Policy itself is Libra's).
- **Macau's Chinese majority and its Macanese:** gaming-era wealth beside an older Portuguese household world.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** merchant networks and tea ways distinct from the delta.
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui):** households long funded from abroad; what remittance money bought.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** a Min-speaking periphery of the province.
- **Guangxi Baihua cities (Nanning, Wuzhou, Yulin) and the Pinghua countryside:** Cantonese trade towns against older farming Han.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** land, cloth, silver and feasts as their own kinds of wealth.
- **Danjia boat families:** possessions held afloat, and the land they lacked.
- **Migrant and domestic workers:** wages sent elsewhere; few things kept on hand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau; good for Guangdong; thin, mostly in Chinese, for Guangxi's minorities. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** this sheet and the rest of the Taurus set are written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese with slang, written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property questions are charged in Hong Kong's housing debate; fine-scale mainland asset data are restricted; some lineage land disputes are live.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
