# Yue (粤): Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English (and Tagalog and Indonesian for Hong Kong's domestic workers). **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese youth:** worth tied to exam results, flats and jobs; the 廢青 label and its answer.
- **Guangzhou and delta Cantonese against northern newcomers:** local pride and the slurs that rank outsiders; check terms with care.
- **Migrant workers in Dongguan and Shenzhen:** worth sought through self-improvement and money sent home.
- **Hong Kong and Macau domestic workers:** their own measures of worth, apart from their employers'.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** worth through a family firm and lineage standing; check, not assume.
- **Danjia descendants:** a group once ranked low by law.
- **Zhuang:** language and rural origin as felt sources of worth or shame.
- **Yao, Miao and Dong:** worth in tourism and in their own rites; compare villages with and without tourists.
- **Macanese:** a small group's standing after the end of Portuguese rule.
- **Elderly poor in Hong Kong:** worth after work and family support end.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (schools, mental-health surveys, NGOs); mixed for Guangdong migrants; thin for Guangxi's minorities except tourism and language studies.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese with slang, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Portuguese, English, Tagalog, Indonesian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data need careful handling; regional slurs should be studied, not repeated; tourist villages raise consent questions about photographs.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Hong Kong's lands (category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Read Ho (1976), then search WiseNews and Hong Kong forum archives for 爭啖氣 and 揸頸就命 in use.
2. Request APASO's self-concept scales and the Mental Morbidity Survey's published tables.
3. Search CNKI for 壮语 语言态度 and 黄洛瑶寨 旅游 女性.
4. Read Constable (1997) and Chang (2008) side by side for workers' own words on worth.
