## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong across camps and generations:** the same "core values" phrase used by opposed sides; never one voice.
- **Guangzhou and delta Cantonese:** commerce and the family firm, checked against stated Confucian ranking.
- **Siyi and other emigrant counties:** values brought back by returning emigrants and their money.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** charitable-hall merit and lineage duty.
- **Western Guangdong and Leizhou:** temple and festival life as the main carriers of shared values.
- **Guangxi Han (Baihua, Pinghua, Guiliu Mandarin speakers):** three Han communities to separate.
- **Zhuang:** sung moral teaching and village custom.
- **Yao, Miao, Dong, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** each group's own teachings; do not merge them.
- **Macau Catholics, Macanese and Hong Kong Christians:** church schools as values teachers to non-Christians too.
- **Migrant workers:** values carried from home villages into the factory city.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (surveys, curricula, press); good for classical Lingnan writing; mixed for Guangdong today; thin for Guangxi's minorities outside published folk texts.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese; Standard Written and Classical Chinese (family instructions, Lingnan philosophy); Zhuang for the moral songs; Teochew; Portuguese for Macau's Catholic archives; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** values talk in Hong Kong and the mainland is politically charged since 2012 and 2019; some surveys stopped or changed hands. Law and its letter or spirit go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
