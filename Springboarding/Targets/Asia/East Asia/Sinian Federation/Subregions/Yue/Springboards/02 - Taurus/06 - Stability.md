## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong old-district residents (Sham Shui Po, Wan Chai, Yau Ma Tei):** long tenure in a fast-rebuilt city.
- **New Territories lineage villages:** centuries on one site, and continuity by descent.
- **Guangzhou Xiguan and Dongshan neighborhoods:** two older quarters with different histories of change.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan:** cities of newcomers where almost no one has deep roots.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** village calendars kept by emigrants returning yearly.
- **Western Guangdong (Maoming, Zhanjiang):** rural continuity under heavy out-migration.
- **Guangxi Zhuang villages:** drums, song fairs and fields kept across generations.
- **Yao of Bama and the Dayaoshan; Miao and Dong of the north:** hill continuity and tourism's change.
- **Macau's old Chinese and Macanese families:** two kinds of long-settled family in one small city.
- **Danjia families moved ashore:** stability as something newly acquired.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (heritage campaigns, monetary history, press); good for Guangzhou; mixed for Guangxi, mostly heritage and tourism studies.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (heritage campaigns, song lyrics), Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese (temple steles), Zhuang, Teochew (festival studies), Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability" 穩定 is also an official political watchword in the mainland, so separate its political use from everyday continuity; Hong Kong's 1997 promises are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
