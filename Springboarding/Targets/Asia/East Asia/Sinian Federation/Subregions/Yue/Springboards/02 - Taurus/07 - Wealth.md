## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong tycoon families and the grassroots:** the sharpest gap to test; name variation within each.
- **Guangzhou and Shunde-Foshan manufacturing families:** township factory fortunes from the 1980s on.
- **Shenzhen tech and finance wealth:** new money of migrants from all over the federation.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** merchant networks that link Shantou, Hong Kong and Southeast Asia.
- **Siyi and Taishan:** remittance wealth and its houses; diaspora material stays comparison only.
- **Macau:** gaming wealth beside public-housing waiting lists.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** poorer farm and fishing counties.
- **Guangxi Baihua trading towns against Zhuang, Yao and Miao villages:** market wealth against farm and forest livelihoods.
- **Urban-village landlords and their tenants:** rentier wealth inside one building.
- **Danjia and Jing fishing families:** the catch, the boat and the fish farm as wealth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau (official and NGO data, press); rich for Guangdong's big firms; thin for minority household wealth in Guangxi.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Taishanese, Zhuang, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tycoon and property coverage is politically charged; mainland wealth data on officials are restricted; developer failures involve ongoing litigation.
- **Held for review:** none.
