## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong urban residents:** holdouts against redevelopment, and the line between principle and price.
- **New Territories villagers:** refusal to sell land, and the pressure from developers and kin.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen urban-village households:** the classic nail houses.
- **Xinhui and the western delta:** local pride in the Yamen last stand.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** check whether "stubborn" is a self-description or an outsiders' label.
- **Guangxi Zhuang and Yao:** long histories of not submitting, told from inside and outside.
- **Macau's Portuguese and Macanese:** staying on after 1999.
- **Migrant workers:** strikes and holdouts over unpaid wages.
- **Elders across the four lands:** refusal of new payment, food or housing, and how families handle it.
- **Hakka (optional):** 硬頸精神 ("stiff-neck spirit") is a self-description many Hakka claim, so compare how the same Cantonese word is used of oneself and of others.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (press, campaigns, oral history); good for Guangdong history; thin for Guangxi outside histories of uprisings, mostly told by the state.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese with idioms, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2014 and 2019 protests, land holdouts and ethnic uprisings are politically sensitive; some archives may be restricted or removed.
- **Held for review:** none.
