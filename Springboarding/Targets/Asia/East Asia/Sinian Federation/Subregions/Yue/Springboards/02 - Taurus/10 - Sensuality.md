## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and delta Cantonese:** the best-documented food culture; test the usual claims about texture and freshness.
- **Hong Kong:** comfort in a dense city (spas, cafés, air conditioning), where space is scarce.
- **Shunde:** a food town within the delta, often named as a culinary center; check the claim.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** tea, braised goose and cold seafood as a separate taste world.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** coastal and sugar-cane tastes; less studied.
- **Siyi and Taishan:** village dishes kept by emigrants; diaspora versions are comparison only.
- **Guangxi Baihua cities (Nanning, Wuzhou):** rice noodles and night markets.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Miao, Dong:** sticky rice, oil tea, sour foods, herbal baths, song.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** fish sauce and monochord music.
- **Macanese:** a creole kitchen and Catholic feast days.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food (heritage dossiers, food writing, diet surveys); good for sound and gardens; thin for touch norms, which will need fieldwork and comparative psychology.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Zhuang, Iu Mien, Vietnamese for the Jing, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paid massage is linked in press to sex work, so separate the two carefully; Yao herbal knowledge belongs to its holders. Sex itself is Scorpio's.
