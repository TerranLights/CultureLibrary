# Yue (粤): The domain: communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese and the other Yue varieties (Taishanese among them), Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang and Sawndip, Teochew and Leizhou Min, Portuguese, English, Hong Kong Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech ways, languages, neighbors, short journeys, early learning).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou (Xiguan) and Hong Kong Cantonese:** two prestige forms of one speech, each with its own schools and media.
- **Macau:** Cantonese street talk beside Portuguese official life and Macanese households.
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui):** a Yue speech apart from the delta standard; which variety a child learns first.
- **Western Guangdong Gao-Yang and Guangxi Goulou Yue (Yulin, Beiliu):** Yue varieties far from the delta standard.
- **Guangxi Baihua towns and Pinghua villages:** two Han speech worlds; Pinghua as an old layer under Cantonese.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou Min speakers:** Min speech inside Guangdong, with their own rime books and operas.
- **Zhuang (northern and southern), Yao (Mien, Bunu, Lakkja), Dong, Miao, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** home tongues beside Putonghua schooling; song as a medium.
- **She of the Fenghuang mountains (Chaozhou):** which language a small people's villages use today.
- **Danjia boat families:** song and speech of the water.
- **Deaf communities:** Hong Kong Sign Language and mainland signing.
- **Putonghua-speaking migrants in Shenzhen and Dongguan:** cities whose daily tongue is not Cantonese.
- **Hakka (optional):** New Territories and eastern Guangdong Hakka villages learning Cantonese as a second tongue; a separate sub-unit, contrast only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong, Macau and Guangdong dialectology; mixed for mainland daily talk; thin, mostly in Chinese, for Guangxi's minority languages. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** the Gemini set is written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (spoken and written), Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang (romanized and Sawndip), Teochew, Portuguese, English; Hong Kong Sign Language for the Deaf.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cantonese in schools and broadcasting is a live political question; so is minority-language schooling. Law on language goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
