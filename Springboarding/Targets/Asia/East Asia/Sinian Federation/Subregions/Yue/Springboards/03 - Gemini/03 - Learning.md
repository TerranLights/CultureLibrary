## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong middle-class families:** learning planned from toddlerhood; set against working-class families with less time to coach.
- **Guangzhou and delta family firms:** children learning by working in shops, restaurants and workshops.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant families:** children learning far from grandparents, or left with them.
- **Siyi:** children taught English or overseas ways for an expected emigration.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** craft and merchant skills learned under a master or a relative.
- **Western Guangdong and Leizhou:** opera troupes and festival roles as schools for village youth.
- **Zhuang:** Zhuang learned at home, Putonghua at school; which skills each language carries.
- **Dong of Sanjiang:** songs learned in age groups from song-teachers.
- **Yao:** ritual learning from priests, open only to some; separate from school learning.
- **Macau:** Portuguese-medium learners and the Chinese-medium majority.
- **Danjia families:** children historically kept out of schools, learning the boat's work instead.
- **Deaf children:** learning in sign or in speech, and the arguments between the two.
