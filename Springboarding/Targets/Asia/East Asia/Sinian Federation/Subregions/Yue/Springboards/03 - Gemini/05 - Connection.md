*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** families stretched across the boundary; elders living alone in public housing.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** business ties and banquet favors; the line between a favor and a bribe.
- **Shenzhen:** a city of newcomers without lineage halls; networks built from workplaces and home-province 老乡 groups.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** native-place ties carried into Hong Kong and beyond.
- **Siyi:** villages kept in touch with distant emigrants; the village as a node of a far-flung network.
- **Western Guangdong (Maoming, Zhanjiang):** festival visits that pull scattered kin home.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** Baihua trading networks along the West River.
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong:** sworn same-age friendships and village visits as chosen ties.
- **Danjia boat families:** ties built along the water, not around land.
- **Macau:** Macanese and Chinese networks side by side.
- **Migrant workers:** home-place groups as safety nets in factory towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (elder services, press); good for guanxi scholarship; thin for minority chosen-kin ties outside Chinese-language ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Mien, Dong, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** connections shade into corruption and organized crime; triad material calls for care. Rules on bribery and favoritism go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
