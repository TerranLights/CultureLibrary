## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** Chinese and British pasts; double identity as a public question.
- **Macau Macanese:** Portuguese and Chinese ancestry in one family line.
- **Macau Chinese majority:** a Chinese town with a Portuguese public face.
- **Guangzhou:** old port culture mixing Chinese and foreign; Cantonese and Putonghua lives.
- **Shenzhen:** daily crossers who live in one system and work or study in the other.
- **Siyi:** villages where returned emigrants built houses in two styles.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** Min speakers inside a Cantonese-led province.
- **Zhuang:** Zhuang at home, Han in public, for some families; others not; variation to map.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** two languages and two states in one community.
- **Pinghua and Baihua speakers:** two Han speech worlds in one Guangxi town.
- **Danjia:** shore and water lives, and the move ashore.
- **Hakka (optional):** New Territories Hakka who speak Hakka in the village and Cantonese in town; a separate sub-unit, contrast only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau; good for historical compradors and Eurasians; thin for Zhuang and Jing two-ness outside Chinese-language studies.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Portuguese, English, Zhuang, Vietnamese for the Jing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dual identity and dual loyalty in Hong Kong and Macau are politically charged; "one country, two systems" belongs as law to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
