## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** professional mediators and public conciliation against neighbors, clans and churches who still step in.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** people's mediation, labor disputes and the fate of worker-advice groups.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan factories:** migrant workers and the brokers, recruiters and labor agents between them and employers.
- **Macau:** a small city where associations and the government sit close; Macau as broker abroad.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** village elders and lineage halls as mediators.
- **Siyi and western Guangdong:** temple committees and festival bodies as neutral ground.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** market quarrels and their go-betweens.
- **Zhuang villages:** respected households and ritual specialists as settlers of quarrels; to separate from Miao and Dong forms.
- **Miao of Rongshui:** elders who reason disputes out.
- **Danjia:** boat-community heads between fishers and shore authorities.
- **Newly arrived families:** children and social workers standing between them and offices.
- **Hakka (optional):** New Territories Hakka village heads between villagers and the government; a separate sub-unit, contrast only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau institutions; good for mainland people's mediation statistics; mixed for informal and minority go-betweens.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (with underworld slang), Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Miao, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor organizing in Guangdong is politically sensitive and some groups were shut; triad brokering needs care. Court-annexed mediation and its rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
