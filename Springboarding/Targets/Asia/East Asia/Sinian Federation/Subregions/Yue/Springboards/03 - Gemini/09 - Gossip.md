*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; and rumor, unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** a large tabloid and paparazzi trade beside forums and group chats; how celebrity talk and neighbor talk differ.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** online 吃瓜 and old-street gossip in arcade neighborhoods.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan dormitories:** talk among workers far from their home villages, and its reach back home.
- **Macau:** a small city where many people know each other; Macanese and Chinese circles.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** lineage reputation and the reach of village talk into emigrant families.
- **Siyi:** "Gold Mountain wives" left behind and the talk that watched them (diaspora tie as comparison only).
- **Western Guangdong and Leizhou:** festival crowds as places where news and rumor spread.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** market-day talk in Baihua and Pinghua areas.
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong:** sung public criticism as a check on conduct.
- **Danjia:** shore people's talk about boat people, and boat people's own talk.
- **Domestic workers and building watchmen:** listening trades that hear much and repeat some.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong media and online talk; good for village ethnography of Guangdong; thin for minority gossip outside song collections.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (with slang), Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** privacy of real persons named in scandals; 2019 rumors are politically charged on all sides; mainland "rumor" is also a legal category. Defamation and rumor law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
