# Yue (粤): The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese and the other Yue varieties (Siyi, Gao-Yang, Goulou), Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Iu Mien and the other Yao tongues, Teochew and Leizhou Min, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what home is and who counts as family: household forms, roots, house-building ways, kin ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta lineage villages (Nanhai, Panyu, Shunde, Dongguan) against Guangzhou's old city:** ancestral-hall village household against lane house.
- **Hong Kong estates and New Territories indigenous villages (Punti, Weitou):** who is "the family" in a flat and in a village house.
- **Macau's Chinese, Macanese and Portuguese households:** three family forms in one small city.
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui) emigrant families:** households with members abroad for generations.
- **Western Guangdong (Gao-Yang, Leizhou Min) and Chaoshan Teochew:** house forms and household size away from the delta.
- **Guangxi Baihua towns and Pinghua villages:** two Han household worlds in Guangxi.
- **Zhuang (northern and southern), Yao (Mien, Bunu, Lakkja), Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** stilt houses, kin ways and child-rearing outside the Han model.
- **Danjia boat families; Hui families of Guangzhou and Guangxi:** home as a boat; a household around a mosque.
- **Hakka (optional):** eastern Guangdong and New Territories Hakka houses are a separate sub-unit; contrast only where one village holds both.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau household figures and delta lineage ethnography; mixed for mainland cities; thin, mostly in Chinese, for Guangxi's smaller peoples. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** the Cancer set is written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese (spoken and written), Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Mien, Teochew, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family planning, household registration and inheritance touch law (Libra and Capricorn); some lineage rites and Yao family scrolls are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
