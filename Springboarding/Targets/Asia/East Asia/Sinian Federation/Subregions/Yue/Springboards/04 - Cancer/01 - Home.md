*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Dong (Kam), Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong public-rental tenants and private flat owners:** home as a waiting-list allocation against home as a mortgage.
- **New Territories indigenous villagers:** the village house as a right of descent, set against newcomers who rent village houses.
- **Guangzhou old-city families and urban-village tenants:** one city's long-settled homes and its migrants' rented rooms.
- **Macau patio residents and Macanese families:** a shared courtyard home against a Portuguese-style townhouse.
- **Siyi emigrant families:** homes built with money from abroad and often left empty.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou households:** house forms unlike the Cantonese delta.
- **Zhuang, Dong and Miao stilt-house villages; Yao moved down from the mountains (Jinxiu, Bama):** home after resettlement against the inherited house.
- **Jing of the Three Islands (Dongxing); Danjia moved ashore:** home by and on the sea.
- **Hakka (optional):** New Territories walled houses kept as museums (Sam Tung Uk 三棟屋) show a home form beside Punti villages; contrast only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau housing and heritage records, and for Guangzhou architecture; thin for minority homes after resettlement.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang and Dong (for the resettlement files and stilt-house villages), Teochew, Portuguese (for Macau housing records), English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation are political subjects on the mainland and in Hong Kong; the Small House Policy is contested. Housing law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
