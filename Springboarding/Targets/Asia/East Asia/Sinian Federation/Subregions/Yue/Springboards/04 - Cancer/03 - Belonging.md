# Yue (粤): Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong-born and new arrivals from the mainland:** a line drawn by accent, papers and years.
- **New Territories indigenous villagers and everyone else:** belonging fixed by descent from pre-1898 villages.
- **Macau's Macanese, Portuguese, Chinese residents and mainland workers:** four ways of being "of" Macau.
- **Guangzhou natives and outside workers:** an old city with a newcomer majority in some districts.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan:** cities where most people came from elsewhere.
- **Guangxi's Cantonese-speaking towns and its Guiliu Mandarin north:** two Han ways of being from Guangxi.
- **Zhuang (Bouxcuengh) and the Yao groups:** belonging to a people, a valley or a song style.
- **Hui of Guangzhou and Guilin:** a faith community long settled among Cantonese.
- **Danjia:** a group long held outside the land-based community.
- **Southeast Asian domestic workers:** residents kept outside permanent belonging.
- **Hakka (optional):** the Siyi clan wars made Hakka and Punti into opposing labels; that history belongs to this concept, though the Hakka are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau identity writing; mixed for mainland migrant integration; thin for minority belonging outside Chinese sources.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English; Tagalog and Indonesian for domestic workers' own media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hong Kong identity, localism and new-arrival policy are politically charged; ethnic labels on the mainland are official categories. Residence and citizenship law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
