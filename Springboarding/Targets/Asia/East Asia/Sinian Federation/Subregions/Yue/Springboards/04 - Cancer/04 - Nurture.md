# Yue (粤): Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong middle-class families and public-housing families:** helpers, tutors and enrichment against parents' own time.
- **Cross-boundary grandparents:** children raised partly in Shenzhen and partly in Hong Kong.
- **Guangzhou single-child families of the 1980s-2010s:** nurture centered on one child.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant parents:** nurture by phone, with grandparents in home villages.
- **Delta villages and western Guangdong (Gao-Yang, Leizhou):** grandmother-led rearing and local rhymes.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** large-family rearing and its own children's songs.
- **Zhuang villages:** the mother's kin in early infancy, and the Zhuang language at home.
- **Yao (Mien, Bunu) and Dong:** raising children through song and work in mountain villages.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** children taught the sea and the monochord.
- **Macanese and Portuguese families of Macau:** amas (nannies) and the bilingual home.
- **Hui of Guangzhou and Guangxi:** religious teaching of the young beside state school.
- **Danjia:** children raised on boats, then ashore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong parenting studies and education policy; mixed for mainland cities; thin for minority child-rearing outside Chinese-language ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English; Tagalog and Indonesian for helpers' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical discipline borders on child protection (see Protection); schooling-language policy is political; discipline as authority goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
