# Yue (粤): Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Vietnamese (for the Hong Kong camps), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Danjia and Hoklo boat households:** typhoon shelters and stilt houses as the roof.
- **Refugees of 1949-1962 and their children:** hillside huts, resettlement blocks, then estates.
- **Rennie's Mill residents:** a political refuge with its own schools and loyalties.
- **Vietnamese camp residents:** shelter under detention, a Vietnamese-language record (Vietnam itself is a separate target).
- **Street sleepers and cage-home tenants in Sham Shui Po and Yau Tsim Mong:** the city's least sheltered.
- **Macau:** typhoon refuge and its small, dense housing.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen rough sleepers:** migrants under bridges and the relief-station system.
- **Guangxi flood and landslide areas (Hechi, Baise):** shelter after disaster in mountain villages.
- **Dong and Zhuang villages:** covered bridges, drum towers and stilt houses as shelter for strangers and stock.
- **Women fleeing violence in Guangdong and Guangxi:** refuges little used or unknown.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong coasts:** typhoon landfalls and village refuges.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong squatting, resettlement and refugee camps (English and Chinese archives); mixed for mainland relief stations; thin for minority practices of lodging strangers.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Teochew, Vietnamese, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention of boat people, forced clearances and the treatment of rough sleepers are contested; asylum law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
