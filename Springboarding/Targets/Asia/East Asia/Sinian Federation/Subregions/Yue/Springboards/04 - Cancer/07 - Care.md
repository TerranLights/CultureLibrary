# Yue (粤): Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong families with live-in helpers:** elder care handed to a paid migrant worker in the home.
- **Hong Kong elders in residential homes:** how much care moves into institutions, against care at home.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen families:** long-term care insurance and paid ward carers against family care.
- **Rural Guangxi (Hechi, Baise, Guilin's hills):** the old caring for the old.
- **Siyi and Zhongshan elders with children overseas:** care at a distance, paid by remittance.
- **Macau:** a small state that funds elder care directly; Macanese and Chinese families compared.
- **Zhuang households:** Zhuang medicine at home and in the hospital.
- **Yao, Dong and Miao villages:** village healers and herbalists beside clinics.
- **Chaoshan Teochew families:** which son, daughter or daughter-in-law is expected to care, a question to check.
- **Hui families:** care of the dying and washing of the dead by the community.
- **Danjia and fishing families:** care of the old on boats and after moving ashore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau elder-care policy and for CHARLS; mixed for mainland care markets; thin for minority healers and village care.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Portuguese, English; Tagalog and Indonesian for helpers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in care homes and helpers' working conditions are sensitive; some healers' knowledge is restricted. Health and labor law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
