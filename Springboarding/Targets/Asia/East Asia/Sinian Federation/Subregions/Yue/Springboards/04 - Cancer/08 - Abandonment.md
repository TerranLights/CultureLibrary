# Yue (粤): Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese (including Taishanese), Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui) wives and children:** generations of waiting for men abroad.
- **Chaoshan Teochew families:** the same pattern under another name and in another language.
- **Hong Kong wives and Shenzhen second households:** abandonment across a boundary in the 1990s.
- **Guangzhou and the delta cities:** infants left at institutions, and which infants.
- **Hong Kong babies of the 1950s-1960s:** infants left in the refugee years, some adopted abroad.
- **Hong Kong elders living alone:** families who move away or emigrate and leave a parent behind.
- **Guangxi's emptied villages (Hechi, Baise, Hezhou):** who stays when working-age people leave.
- **Zhuang and Yao families:** absence of parents in minority villages, in their own languages.
- **Macau:** foundlings in church charity records and the city's small welfare system.
- **Danjia and Leizhou fishing families:** men lost at sea, and how widows and orphans were placed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong charities, Siyi emigration archives and Qing charity studies; mixed for mainland infant abandonment (press, then little); thin for minority experience.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese and Taishanese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Teochew, Zhuang, Portuguese, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abandoned and disabled children, adoptees' records and second households involve living people; adoption files are closed. Family and adoption law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
