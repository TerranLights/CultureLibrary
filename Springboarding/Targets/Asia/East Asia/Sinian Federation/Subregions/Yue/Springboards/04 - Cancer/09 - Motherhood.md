# Yue (粤): Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Vietnamese (as the Jing speak it), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong working mothers with live-in helpers:** mothering shared with a paid worker.
- **Mainland mothers who gave birth in Hong Kong:** children with a right of abode their mothers do not have.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen 宝妈:** online-era motherhood in the delta cities.
- **Siyi and Zhongshan emigrant households:** mothers who ran the household as its head for decades.
- **Shunde and Panyu silk districts:** the history of women who stayed unmarried, as a contrast to the mother role.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** mothers in large families, to be compared, not assumed.
- **Zhuang villages:** the Flower Grandmother and the mother's kin around a birth.
- **Yao and Dong:** birth and early mothering in mountain villages, and which elders teach the young.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** mothering in a Vietnamese-speaking fishing people.
- **Macanese and Chinese mothers of Macau:** Catholic and Chinese ideals side by side.
- **Southeast Asian domestic workers:** mothers raising other people's children while their own grow up abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong statistics, film and literature; mixed for mainland mothers (press, platforms); thin for minority motherhood outside Chinese ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Zhuang, Vietnamese, Portuguese, English; Tagalog and Indonesian for helpers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** births to mainland parents, family planning and maternity rights are political; the history of birth cults under campaigns against superstition is sensitive. Maternity law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
