## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Foshan and Shunde craft towns against Guangzhou:** kiln and workshop traditions against a city art scene.
- **Hong Kong's film, design and advertising trades:** commercial invention under deadline.
- **Shenzhen's migrant designers and painters:** a young city built by newcomers; copy and invention side by side.
- **Macau's Macanese cooks and Chinese artisans:** fusion cooking against inherited Chinese crafts.
- **Chaoshan workshops:** family-run crafts in a Min-speaking area with its own guild memory.
- **Zhuang troupes and singers:** composing in Zhuang for stage and song.
- **Dong carpenters (Sanjiang), Baiku Yao (Nandan), Maonan (Huanjiang):** making held inside a people, with its own rules on change.
- **Qinzhou and Beihai coast:** a revived craft and a pearl trade on the Guangxi shore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Hong Kong creative industries and on listed crafts; thinner on how makers themselves speak of originality, which needs interviews in Cantonese, Zhuang and Kam.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for makers and press; Classical Chinese for the Song treatise; Zhuang for opera scripts; Kam for carpenters' terms; Portuguese for Macau gastronomy files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Yao designs carry ritual meaning; copying and copyright disputes touch law (Libra); art after 2019 may be self-censored.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
