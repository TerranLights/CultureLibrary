*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta lineages against the cities:** recognition through the lineage hall against recognition through the state, the firm and the media.
- **Hong Kong's civil and business elites:** an honors system with colonial roots and a post-1997 shape.
- **Macau's Chinese and Macanese notables:** two communities in one honors list.
- **Guangdong factory and service workers:** model-worker titles against unsung migrant labor.
- **Chaoshan:** merchant and lineage honors, and recognition through home-town donations.
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong singers:** peer-given song titles against state heritage listing.
- **Jing, Maonan and Mulao:** small recognized peoples, and how their recognition came.
- **Film and food workers:** stunt crews and street cooks against named stars and chefs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors (gazettes, lists); mixed for informal credit; thin for small peoples' own titles.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for press notices and stunt-crew interviews; Classical Chinese for plaques and examination records; Zhuang for song-title talk; Portuguese for Macau's Boletim Oficial.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic-classification files may be closed; honors since 2020 carry political weight; obituary notices name private families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
