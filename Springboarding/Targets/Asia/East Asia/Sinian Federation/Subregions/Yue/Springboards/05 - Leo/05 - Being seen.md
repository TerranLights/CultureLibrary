# Yue (粤): Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and the mainland cities:** different camera, mask and media regimes for being watched and shown.
- **Macau's Portuguese-speaking residents:** a small community with its own broadcaster.
- **Guangzhou's African and Middle Eastern traders:** highly visible newcomers.
- **Zhuang villages near tourist routes against those far from them:** a people seen as spectacle in one place and hardly at all in another.
- **Yao and Dong of Longsheng and Sanjiang:** daily life under the tourist's camera.
- **Chaoshan towns:** parade seasons that put certain youths before everyone.
- **Danjia and former boat families:** visibility once tied to stigma.
- **Cleaners, guards and night workers in all four lands:** the workers nobody looks at.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Hong Kong surveillance and media debates; mixed on tourism ethnography; thin on how Zhuang and Yao villagers judge being shown.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for workers' and disabled people's own accounts; Classical Chinese for the tribute album; Zhuang for signage studies; Teochew for parade talk; Portuguese for TDM.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance is a security subject on the mainland; foreigners' treatment in Guangzhou was contentious in 2020; old albums use slurs for minority peoples.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
