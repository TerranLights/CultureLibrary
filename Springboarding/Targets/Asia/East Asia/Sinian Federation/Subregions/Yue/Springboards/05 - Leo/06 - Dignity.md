# Yue (粤): Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Iu Mien, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong port and building workers:** union language of dignity in a free-market city.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan factory workers:** dignity under dormitory regimes.
- **The old in Hong Kong and Macau:** care, end of life and being spoken to.
- **Zhuang and Yao villagers in poverty programs:** aid as help or as loss of face.
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong as peoples:** the written names once used for them.
- **Disabled people in all four lands:** different access regimes.
- **Sex workers and other stigmatized groups:** respect sought from outsiders.
- **Macau's Macanese and Chinese elders:** two care traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong labor and care research; mixed for the mainland's factories; thin for small peoples' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for strikers' and patients' words; Classical Chinese for the ritual texts; Zhuang and Mien for village accounts; Portuguese for Macau care standards.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor organizing on the mainland is risky; end-of-life and sex-work topics touch law (Libra and Capricorn); informants need privacy.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
