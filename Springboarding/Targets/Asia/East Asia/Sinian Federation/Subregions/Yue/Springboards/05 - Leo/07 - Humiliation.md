# Yue (粤): Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English (and Indonesian for one case). **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong against the mainland cities:** different limits on state and online shaming.
- **Delta lineages:** old shaming punishments and how villages remember them.
- **Dongguan and Humen:** where national humiliation is taught at the very site.
- **Sex workers, domestic workers and migrants:** groups most exposed to degradation.
- **Guangxi villages after the Cultural Revolution:** degradation remembered locally, often in silence.
- **Zhuang and Yao speakers mocked for accent or dress:** being laughed at as minority people.
- **Macau's Macanese:** humiliation in a colonial order that has since reversed.
- **Football and other fans:** defeat as public loss of face.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the Hong Kong press; mixed for mainland cases (censored or local); thin for minority peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for forums, slurs and apologies; Classical Chinese for Qing punishment records; Zhuang for village memories; Indonesian for the 2014 worker's own statements; Portuguese for Macau.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution violence is restricted on the mainland; victims' names in shaming cases need care; slurs are quoted only as search terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
