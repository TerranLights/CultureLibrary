## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta lineages against city charities:** giving through the lineage against giving through merchant charities.
- **Hong Kong and Macau:** large Chinese charities with merchant founders; different tax and tipping customs.
- **Shenzhen's migrants:** giving to strangers in a city where few have kin.
- **Siyi and Chaoshan emigrant counties:** donations from abroad, and the expectations they create.
- **Guangxi Zhuang and Han villages:** gift money that binds households to each other.
- **Dong, Yao and Miao of the northern hills:** shared feasts and guest customs.
- **Hui of Guangzhou and Guangxi:** almsgiving by religious rule.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka donors such as Tin Ka Ping 田家炳 funded schools across these lands; their giving differs in origin from Cantonese merchants', and belongs to the Hakka sub-unit otherwise.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau charities and for donor steles; mixed for mainland online giving; thin for village gift customs among small peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for etiquette and press; Classical Chinese for stele texts; Kam for feast customs; Teochew for Chaoshan donors; Portuguese for Macau records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity law and taxes are for Libra; crowdfunding fraud cases name patients; religious giving needs the community's consent.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
