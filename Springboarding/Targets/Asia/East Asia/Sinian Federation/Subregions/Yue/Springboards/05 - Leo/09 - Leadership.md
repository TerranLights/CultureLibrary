## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta lineages:** elders and estate managers against elected village heads.
- **Hong Kong firms and civil service:** family owners, professional managers and a colonial legacy of taipans.
- **Shenzhen's tech companies:** founder-led firms with high-pressure leadership talk.
- **Macau's Chinese community associations:** brokers between government and residents.
- **Qingyuan and northern Guangdong villages:** council reforms led by local worthies.
- **Zhuang and Yao villages:** ritual specialists and elders as leaders.
- **Teochew merchant networks:** lineage and business leadership tied to home towns.
- **Women in all four lands:** formal posts against informal leading in families and associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for management research and Hong Kong statistics; mixed for village leadership on the mainland; thin for Zhuang and Yao leaders' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for prefects and business talk; Classical Chinese for Ming sources on Yuan Chonghuan; Zhuang for ritual specialists; Portuguese for Ho Yin in Macau records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party leadership and student-leader movements are politically sensitive; formal authority belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
