## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong estates against Guangzhou lanes:** different spaces for children's free play.
- **Macau:** Portuguese and Chinese school recess customs, and a city whose adult play is gaming.
- **Shenzhen's migrant families:** children with little space and grandparents far away.
- **Chaoshan:** puppetry and festival games outside the Cantonese world.
- **Siyi and western Guangdong villages:** rural play and seasonal festival games.
- **Zhuang villages:** pole-beating, song play and courtship games.
- **Yao, Dong and Miao of the northern hills:** top spinning and contests at festivals.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** sea-shore play in a fishing people.
- **Older men in bird gardens and parks:** adult play that is rarely studied.
