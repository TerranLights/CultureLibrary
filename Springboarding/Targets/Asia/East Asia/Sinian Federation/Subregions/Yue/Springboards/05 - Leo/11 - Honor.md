# Yue (粤): Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta lineages:** family honor through degrees, arches and ancestral halls.
- **Hong Kong's police, triads and kung-fu schools:** codes of loyalty that mirror each other.
- **Macau:** a Portuguese military and Catholic honor tradition beside Chinese merchant codes.
- **Chaoshan:** lineage standing and business word-keeping in Teochew towns.
- **Guangxi border counties:** soldiers' honor after the 1979 war.
- **Zhuang and Yao villages:** honor given to guests and kept by sworn ties.
- **Dong and Miao of the northern hills:** village compacts and the standing they confer.
- **Women in all four lands:** honor once tied to chastity, now argued over.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loyalist history and Hong Kong popular culture; mixed for honor codes as people live them; thin for minority peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for opera, film and police and triad lore; Classical Chinese for Ming loyalist records and arch inscriptions; Zhuang for toasting songs; Teochew for Chaoshan business talk; Portuguese for Macau's military history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** triad lore must be handled with care; the 1979 war and veterans' protests are sensitive on the mainland; violence in families justified by honor is a question to research, not to assume.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
