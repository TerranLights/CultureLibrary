*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written and Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Bunu, Kam, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and Guangzhou:** different public celebrations and rules on fireworks and crowds.
- **Macau's fishmongers, Macanese and Portuguese residents:** a trade festival, Catholic feasts and a Lusophone calendar.
- **Chaoshan:** Lantern Festival wit and long god-parade seasons.
- **Siyi and western Guangdong villages:** village festivals funded from abroad and at home.
- **Zhuang of the Hongshui River:** the frog festival's mix of grief and joy.
- **Bunu Yao against Mien Yao:** two Yao peoples with different festivals of thanks.
- **Dong of Sanjiang and Rongshui:** their own New Year.
- **The old and the young in Hong Kong:** what each reports as joy, in happiness surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong happiness surveys and festivals; mixed for mainland city rankings; thin for what joy means in Zhuang, Bunu and Kam words.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for comedies and wake customs; Classical Chinese for Qing verse; Zhuang, Bunu and Kam for festival explanations; Teochew for riddles; Portuguese for Macau feasts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the flower boats involved coerced women; some festival rites are for members only; drinking at festivals needs care in framing.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
