# Yue (粤): The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese and the other Yue varieties (Taishanese, Gao-Yang, Goulou, Guangxi Baihua), Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew and Leizhou Min, Kam (Dong), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: work, service, the daily round, the kept house, craft, rest and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta core (Guangzhou, Foshan, Shunde) against Shenzhen and Dongguan:** an old trading city's day against migrant cities' factory and tech shifts.
- **Macau's Chinese, Macanese and imported workers:** civil-service, gaming and household jobs split by origin.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** households run on remittances earned abroad; **Hong Kong** office hours against Macau's casino shifts.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou Min speakers:** dense farming, home piecework and trading families outside the Cantonese world.
- **Western Guangdong and Guangxi Baihua and Pinghua areas:** farm and market-town work beyond the delta.
- **Zhuang (northern and southern), Yao (Mien, Bunu, Lakkja), Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** their own farming, forest, fishing and craft calendars and splits of work by sex.
- **Guangxi's out-migrant workers:** the people who staff the delta's factories and go home once a year.
- **Danjia boat families and the Hui:** fishing and boat work; a day shaped by prayer times.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka and Cantonese villages in the New Territories split farm and wage work differently; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau labor statistics; mixed for mainland cities (strong on factories, weak on households); thin, mostly in Chinese, for Guangxi's smaller peoples. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** the Virgo set is written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list, which the author still confirms.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for the street words of work and rest; Classical Chinese for the gazetteers; Zhuang for the farming songs; Teochew for Chaoshan home work; Portuguese for Macau's statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mainland labor organizing is sensitive and its sources come and go; working-hours law goes to Libra and Capricorn; some Yao and Zhuang healing rites are for initiates.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
