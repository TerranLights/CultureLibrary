# Yue (粤): Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Iu Mien, Kam (Dong), Jing (Vietnamese), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant workers against Guangzhou's settled workforce:** dormitory labor against jobs held near home.
- **Hong Kong:** a factory past and an office and service present; two union camps to read side by side.
- **Macau residents against imported workers:** who deals at the tables and who builds, cleans and cooks.
- **Liuzhou, Nanning and other Guangxi cities:** state industry and its layoffs.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** family trading firms and dense farming, with their own sayings on effort.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** farm and fishing work far from the delta's factories.
- **Zhuang, Yao (Mien, Bunu, Lakkja), Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan:** swidden, terraces and rice-fish fields; each with its own work calendar.
- **Jing of Dongxing and Danjia fishers:** sea work, tides and boat crews.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka women's field labor in mixed villages of eastern Guangdong may be named for contrast with Cantonese women's factory work; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on factory labor (a large English and Chinese literature) and on Hong Kong unions; mixed for Guangxi industry; thin for the smaller peoples' work outside tourism and heritage writing.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for work slang and film; simplified Chinese for CLDS and dagong writing; Zhuang, Mien, Kam and Jing for subsistence terms; Portuguese for Macau shift work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor activism is policed on the mainland and the confederation dissolved in 2021; labor law and wage law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
