## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong civil servants and Macau's Macanese former officials:** two colonial public services and what serving the public meant in each.
- **Guangzhou's Manchu descendants:** a people once defined by service to the throne.
- **Front-line workers in Hong Kong, Macau and Guangzhou:** three service cultures, from casino floor to teahouse.
- **Guangxi cadres and village officials:** state-framed service in poor counties.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** festival rotas and charitable halls as community service outside the state.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** rotating village duties and ritual service, each in its own form.
- **Guangxi domestic workers in delta homes (query 广西 家政 保姆 广州):** paid household service across a provincial line.
- **Hakka (optional):** where New Territories Hakka and Punti villages sent recruits to the Military Service Corps, a check on whether village origin shaped who served; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (trade bodies, unions, government); mixed for Guangdong (official volunteer data, thin independent studies); thin for village ritual rotas outside Chaoshan.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for front-line speech and address forms; simplified Chinese for official exemplars; Teochew and Zhuang for festival duties; Portuguese for Macau's public service.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civil-service oaths and military service touch politics since 2020; the legal side of service work goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
