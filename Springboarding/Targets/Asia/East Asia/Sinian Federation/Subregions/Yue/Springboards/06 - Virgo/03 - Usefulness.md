- **Hong Kong young adults against their elders:** the 廢青 label runs along a generation line.
- **Guangzhou and Foshan retirees against Shenzhen's young migrant city:** where elders still count and where they are few.
- **Macau's retirees and Macanese families:** a small welfare city where elders' roles may differ by community.
- **Liuzhou and other Guangxi industrial towns:** usefulness tied to the factory and its products.

- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing villages:** children's and elders' tasks in farming and ritual, each separately.
- **Disabled people across the four lands:** which work is open to them in each system.
