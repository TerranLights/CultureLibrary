## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's professional class (accountants, auditors, analysts):** analysis as a career and a public institution.
- **Shenzhen's engineers and genomics workers:** analysis as industry, under mainland rules.
- **Guangzhou's old scholarly families and universities:** the line from Qing textual study to today's campuses.
- **Macau:** a small university sector building its own science.
- **Chaoshan Teochew traders:** reckoning and records inside family firms.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** reading weather, seasons and sea in each people's terms.
- **Racegoers and small investors:** amateur statistics in betting halls and brokers' offices.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka merchants' ledgers kept in the same eastern Guangdong towns as Teochew ones could test whether bookkeeping followed trade or language; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong institutions and media; mixed for Guangdong (strong on science policy, thin on everyday reasoning); thin for Guangxi's peoples, where most writing is official ethnology.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Chen Li and Li Mingche; Cantonese for racing and stock talk; simplified Chinese for science and the incinerator dispute; Teochew for ledgers; Zhuang for season terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the gene-editing case and environmental protests are sensitive on the mainland; geomantic lore must be reported as practice, not endorsed.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
