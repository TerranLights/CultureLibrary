## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta collectors and dealers (Guangzhou, Foshan, Hong Kong):** an old trade in judging antiques, tea and jade.
- **Hong Kong and Macau shoppers against mainland visitors:** who is cast as the expert buyer and who as the mark in tourist streets.
- **Macau's Catholics and Macanese:** discernment in Jesuit and parish terms.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** tea tasting and trade judgment within a Teochew-speaking world.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** ritual ways of reading signs and choosing, each with its own specialists.
- **Older people in Guangxi's and Guangdong's countryside:** often addressed by police scam campaigns; check who is actually defrauded against case data.
- **Migrants from other provinces:** on the receiving end of origin-based screening.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka tea villages that supply Fangcun buyers may be named to see how growers and urban tasters judge the same leaf; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong taste writing and Jesuit history; mixed for mainland scam data (police figures, little independent study); thin for minority sign-reading outside ethnology.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for painting catalogs; Latin and Portuguese for Jesuit records; Cantonese for market and food talk; Zhuang for ritual terms; Teochew for tea and trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** almanac and sign-reading must be reported as practice, not endorsed; regional bias is sensitive; fraud law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
