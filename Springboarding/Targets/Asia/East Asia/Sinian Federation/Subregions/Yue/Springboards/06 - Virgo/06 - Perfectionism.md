# Yue (粤): Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Miao, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and Foshan craft families against Shenzhen factories:** hand perfection against zero-defect production.
- **Hong Kong students and parents:** self-demand and exam pressure in one school system.
- **Macau:** Portuguese public craft standards beside Chinese ones.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** a reputation for careful farming and food craft to test, not assume.
- **Zhuang, Miao, Yao, Dong, Mulao, Maonan:** each craft's own standard (tuning, embroidery, carving), stated by makers.
- **Cooks and chefs across the four lands:** where Cantonese cooking sets fixed rules and where cooks improvise.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka and Cantonese dim sum chefs train in the same kitchens, a check on whether exact rules follow the kitchen or the cook's background; otherwise its own sub-unit.
