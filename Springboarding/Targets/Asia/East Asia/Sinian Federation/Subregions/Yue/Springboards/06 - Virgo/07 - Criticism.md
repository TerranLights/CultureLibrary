## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong media and public bodies:** satire and complaint before and after 2020, compared.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** criticism through official channels (hotlines, approved reporting) and online reviews.
- **Macau's Chinese and Portuguese-language publics:** two presses and two sets of limits.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** stage mockery and family reproach in Teochew.
- **Dong, Zhuang, Yao, Miao villages:** public reproach written into village rules.
- **Guangxi county towns:** how far complaint reaches officials away from the delta.
- **Parents and schoolchildren across the four lands:** scolding and self-criticism as children meet them.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka villages in western Guangxi adopt the same posted compacts as Zhuang neighbors, a check on whether reproach rules follow the people or the county; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (archives of satire, complaints and press); mixed for Guangdong (official complaint data, online reviews); thin for minority village rules outside case studies.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese and Classical Chinese for satirical verse; simplified Chinese for posters and hotlines; Teochew for opera; Kam and Zhuang for village compacts; Portuguese for Macau's press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political criticism is sensitive across the four lands and some archives are no longer online; the 1974 poster's authors faced punishment (to check); press and speech law go to Libra and Capricorn.
