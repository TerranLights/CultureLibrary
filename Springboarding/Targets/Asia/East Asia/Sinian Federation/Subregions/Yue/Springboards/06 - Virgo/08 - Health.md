*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Kam (Dong), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and Macau against Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** public systems, private clinics and cross-border patients.
- **Delta households and western Guangdong towns:** home remedies, tonic foods and ideas of heat and damp, compared.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** fresh-herb healing and family nursing in Teochew terms.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** healers, herb markets and causes of illness, each named separately.
- **Guangxi mountain counties:** distance from care and screening programs for inherited disease.
- **Elders and the mentally ill:** two groups whose care brings stigma and family duty into view.
- **Macanese families:** health customs drawn from Portuguese and Chinese homes.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka herbal soups and tonics sold beside Cantonese ones in delta markets could test whether health foods sort by people or by place; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong surveys and colonial medical history; mixed for Guangdong (strong clinical literature, weak on lay belief); mixed for Guangxi (much codified minority medicine, little patient voice).
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for miasma treatises; Cantonese for stigma idioms; Zhuang for medical terms and herb names; Teochew for herb stalls; Portuguese for Macau's health records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness, leprosy and inherited disease carry stigma; some healers' knowledge is held within families or initiates; wildlife and medical law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
