# Yue (粤): Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang (and Sawndip), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong workers and professionals:** night school, course subsidies and the language of "adding value".
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrants:** striving and "comeback" stories far from home.
- **Delta lineage villages:** dike and polder improvement as lineage business.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** land improvement by mass campaign.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** the special zone's promise, read against Teochew striving words.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** improvement brought from outside (scripts, customs, museums) and their own aims.
- **Macau's Chinese, Macanese and newcomers:** a city remade by reclamation and casinos.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka villages drawn into Guangxi's ecomuseum and custom-reform drives beside Zhuang ones could show whether outside "improvement" picks villages by people or by county; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong education and housing records; mixed for mainland campaigns (official accounts dominate); thin for minority views of outside improvement.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for dike records; Cantonese for self-improvement talk; simplified Chinese for campaigns; Zhuang for script debates; Teochew for striving words; Portuguese for Macau planning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** custom-reform and relocation campaigns are politically sensitive; mass-campaign histories can be hard to obtain; planning law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
