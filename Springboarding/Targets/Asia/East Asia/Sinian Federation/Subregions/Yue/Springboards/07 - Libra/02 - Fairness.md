## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong, Macau, Guangdong, Guangxi:** four different wage floors and anti-discrimination regimes as written.
- **Delta cities against Guangdong's outer counties:** the tiered minimum wage writes the gap into law.
- **Migrant workers from Guangxi in Guangdong:** fairness at work seen from the out-migrants' side.
- **New Territories lineages:** shares in a trust for men of the line, set against women's claims.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** exam bonus rules applied to some peoples and not others; their own sharing customs.
- **Danjia fishing families:** shares on a boat.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** lineage trusts and their own share rules.
- **Hakka (optional):** where the minority-bonus question arises for Hakka students, who count as Han and get none, a check on how the rule draws its line; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
