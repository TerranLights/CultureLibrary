# Yue (粤): Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Kam (Dong), Iu Mien, Vietnamese (for cross-border unions), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong couples under common law against Macau couples under the civil code:** different marital property regimes and different standing for unmarried partners.
- **Same-sex couples in Hong Kong, Macau and Guangzhou:** three legal answers to one question.
- **Siyi and other emigrant counties:** partners in firms and marriages split across oceans.
- **Chaoshan Teochew traders:** partnerships among kin and townsmen, a separate commercial world.
- **Border counties of Guangxi (Dongxing, Pingxiang, Jingxi):** cross-border unions and trading partners.
- **Danjia fishing families:** the couple as a working crew.
- **Dong, Yao, Zhuang, Miao, Jing:** courting customs, residence after marriage, and who heads the house.
- **Macanese:** partnerships of Portuguese and Chinese families in law and trade.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka women's field labor made the wife a full working partner in a way Punti households did not, a check on equality inside the couple; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and Hong Kong case law; mixed for business partnership history (strong archives, scattered); thin for minority courtship outside Chinese-language ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for 拍檔 and couple talk; Classical Chinese for shop contracts; English for Hong Kong statutes and the 2023 ruling; Portuguese for Macau's union rules; Vietnamese for cross-border wives' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex partnership is politically charged in all four lands; unregistered cross-border wives may lack papers and avoid interviews.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
