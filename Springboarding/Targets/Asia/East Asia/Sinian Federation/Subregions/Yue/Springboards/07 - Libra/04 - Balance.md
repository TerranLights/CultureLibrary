*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and Macau against the two provinces:** two Basic Laws that write a balance of powers, against provincial government under the Party.
- **Guangxi's autonomous region and its autonomous counties (Jinxiu Yao, Longsheng, Sanjiang Dong) *check*:** where the center-region balance is written and where it is left open.
- **Yao, Zhuang, Dong, Miao, Jing:** their own healing systems and teachings on keeping the body and land even.
- **Delta households against western Guangdong and Guangxi villages:** soups and seasonal foods differ with climate and means.
- **Hong Kong and Macau shift workers:** casino, hospital and logistics hours against office hours.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** a separate cuisine and tea custom with its own balancing talk (gongfu tea).
- **Hong Kong's northern villages and new towns:** conservation and development argued street by street.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka villages in the Northern Metropolis zone weigh land sale against farming differently from Punti lineages, a test of who bears the cost of the plan; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional and labor texts and Hong Kong press; mixed for food and medical customs (abundant popular writing, little careful study); thin and in Chinese for Yao medicine.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for He Mengyao; English and Chinese for the two Basic Laws; Portuguese for Macau's labor law; Mien and Zhuang for healers' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the balance of powers in Hong Kong and Macau is politically charged; some Yao healing knowledge is passed only to initiates.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
