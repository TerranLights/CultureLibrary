# Yue (粤): Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Iu Mien, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong, Macau and the mainland provinces:** three written regimes for recusal, bribery and judicial independence.
- **Hong Kong before and after 1974:** the same city with and without an anticorruption commission.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen residents against those outside the delta:** allocation by lottery and quota for plates, schools and household registration.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Cantonese lineage villages:** lineage weight in village voting.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** customary elders against state cadres, and ethnic quotas in office.
- **Macanese and Portuguese-trained jurists in Macau:** questions of who may judge whom.
- **Family firms in Hong Kong and the delta:** kin preference as policy.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka and Punti candidates competed for quotas in the Qing provincial examination, which officials adjusted for settler status *check*, a test of written fairness between communities; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong statutes, judicial guides and the 1970s record; mixed for the mainland (codes open, practice opaque); thin for Yao and Zhuang customary judging.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for examination and avoidance rules; English and Chinese for Hong Kong's ordinances and guides; Portuguese and Chinese for Macau's judicial law; Mien for elders' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** judicial independence and national-security judges; ethnic quotas; living officials named in corruption cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
