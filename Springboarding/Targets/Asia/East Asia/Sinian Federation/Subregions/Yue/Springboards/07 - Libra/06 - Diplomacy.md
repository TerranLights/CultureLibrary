# Yue (粤): Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Portuguese, English, Vietnamese and Jing (for the Guangxi border), Standard Zhuang, Teochew. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and Macau officials against Guangdong and Guangxi officials:** offices abroad and treaty texts on one side, provincial foreign-affairs offices on the other.
- **Macanese:** a community built on going between languages.
- **Guangxi border peoples (Zhuang, Jing, Yao) and Dongxing, Pingxiang and Napo traders:** diplomacy with kin across a state border.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** lineage elders and native-place associations as negotiators.
- **Delta business families:** banquet and tea-table negotiation.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** native-place and clan associations that dealt with foreign governments for emigrants.
- **Danjia and other water people:** go-betweens in harbor and fishing quarrels.
- **Hakka (optional):** where colonial officials used Hakka and Punti village heads as separate go-betweens in the 1899 New Territories takeover, a test of how intermediaries were chosen; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaties, colonial records and Macau's archives; mixed for everyday tact (popular writing, few studies); thin for border peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the *Shiji* and Qing foreign-affairs records; English and Portuguese for treaties and colonial archives; Cantonese for tact words; Vietnamese and Jing for the border.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Joint Declarations' standing after 2020; the 1979 war and the border; the standing of Hong Kong's offices abroad is a live political question.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
