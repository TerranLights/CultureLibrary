## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's legislature and planning system against Guangdong's and Guangxi's government:** decisions slowed by procedure on one side, pushed by pilot schemes on the other.
- **Hong Kong families in 1989-1997 and since 2020:** two generations facing leave or stay.
- **Temple-goers in Hong Kong, Guangzhou and Chaoshan:** private and collective choices put to gods.
- **Guangxi villages (Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Maonan, Mulao):** village assemblies and customary councils, and how long they deliberate.
- **Macau's Chinese and Macanese:** consultation culture under a small government.
- **Young adults in Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Hong Kong:** marriage, children and housing decisions put off.
- **Siyi emigrant families:** the old choice of leaving, weighed over generations.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka villages in the New Territories split over whether to sell land for new towns, a check on how lineage councils reach or fail to reach a decision; otherwise its own sub-unit.
