# Yue (粤): Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong and Guangzhou:** each city's labels for the other and for outsiders from further north.
- **Guangdong residents and Guangxi migrants:** the images each holds of the other.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou people:** images of them held by Cantonese, and theirs of Cantonese.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Miao, Dong:** accusations of poison magic inside villages, and the older northern images placed on all southern peoples.
- **Danjia boat people:** a people long placed outside respectable society by land people.
- **Macau's Chinese, Macanese and Portuguese:** images traded within a small city.
- **New immigrants in Hong Kong and in Shenzhen:** blamed for costs and crowding.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Punti and Hakka each described the other as the aggressor in the 1855-1867 feud, a direct case of mirrored blame; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong press, film and court records; mixed for regional stereotyping (much online talk, little careful study); thin and sensitive for witchcraft accusations in Guangxi.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the gu sources; Cantonese for labels and blame talk; English for Hunter and the 2013 ruling; Portuguese for Macau confession and press; Zhuang for village accusations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2019 labels; ethnic incitement law; accused women and their families must not be exposed; regional slurs are to be recorded as data, not repeated as judgment.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
