## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta Cantonese households:** pomelo-leaf washing, damp-expelling soups, the household as the first site of cleansing.
- **Hong Kong's political camps:** 割席 and disqualifications read from each side.
- **Hailufeng and Chaoshan Min speakers:** Boshe and Guiyu as two kinds of "rotten village" story.
- **Coastal lineages and Danjia boat people of western Guangdong:** the memory of the Clearance and later sea bans.
- **Guangxi Baihua and Pinghua towns:** cadre purges and drug routes on the border.
- **Zhuang, Maonan, Yao, Dong, Miao, Jing:** their own rites against sickness and spirits, and how state campaigns treated those rites.
- **Macau's Catholics:** confession and Lenten fasting beside Chinese cleansing customs.
- **Hakka (optional):** the 1957 anti-localism purge struck cadres from east Guangdong guerrilla bases that included Hakka counties *check*, so their memoirs belong beside the Cantonese ones.
