# Yue (粤): Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Taishanese, Standard Zhuang, Vietnamese (for returnee voices), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's pro-government and opposition camps:** opposite lists of who betrayed whom.
- **Guangzhou and delta villages:** Cultural Revolution denunciations and their aftermath among neighbors.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Siyi Taishanese emigrant families:** households split across oceans, with their own words for the absent husband.
- **Macau's Chinese and Portuguese-language publics:** one scandal read in two presses.
- **Returnee Chinese from Vietnam on Guangxi farms:** betrayal by a former ally, told from inside Yue.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao:** courtship songs and village rules on the unfaithful.
- **Triad-linked and police worlds:** codes against informing, and those who broke them.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka county gazetteers of east and north Guangdong *check* may judge the Ming-Qing turncoats differently from delta writers, a useful cross-check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong press, courts and TV; mixed for the Cultural Revolution (memoirs, partly closed archives); thin for minority voices on betrayal.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the Ming-Qing turncoats; Cantonese for slang and lyrics; Teochew and Taishanese for emigrant households; Vietnamese for returnee memoirs; Portuguese for Macau's press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informing since 2020 and the "traitor" labels in Hong Kong are live political subjects; Cultural Revolution denunciations involve living families; affair-related sources involve private persons. Treason as law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
