## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta Cantonese lineages:** old feuds remembered in genealogies and court stories.
- **Hong Kong's street and gang worlds:** payback codes, and the 2019 crowd violence read from both camps.
- **Macau before and after 1999:** gang reprisals and their end, as told by the press in two languages.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Hailufeng Min speakers:** lineage feuding in living memory, to check against the delta.
- **Guangxi Baihua and Pinghua towns:** reprisals of the war years and the Cultural Revolution.
- **Yao, Zhuang, Miao, Dong:** feud and peacemaking customs, from the peoples' own records.
- **Christian and Buddhist congregations:** teaching on forgiveness and on leaving revenge to Heaven.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau press and film; mixed for Guangdong court stories; thin and Chinese-mediated for minority feuding.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and early modern Chinese for the Liang case and the novel; Cantonese for slang and legends; Portuguese for Macau's 1990s reporting; Yao and Zhuang for feud terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1967 and 2019 violence are political; revenge images involve victims and must not be reproduced; gang sources need care. Private revenge against state punishment is the spirit of the law, under Capricorn.
