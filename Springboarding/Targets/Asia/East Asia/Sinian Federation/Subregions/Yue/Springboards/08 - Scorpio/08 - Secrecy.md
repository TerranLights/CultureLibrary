*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's open city:** privacy law, online confessions, coming out in public.
- **Guangzhou's churches and house churches:** faith partly hidden, partly visible.
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui) emigrant villages:** false names kept across generations.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou Min speakers:** insiders' money networks and the boundary between "our own people" and outsiders.
- **Foshan and Guangzhou martial-arts and craft lineages:** knowledge held back by rule.
- **Macau's Chinese and Portuguese-speaking residents:** the seal of confession and data protection in two legal languages.
- **Yao (Iu Mien, Bunu), Zhuang, Dong, Miao:** ritual knowledge with its own keepers and limits.
- **Hakka (optional):** paper-son papers were also prepared in some Hakka emigrant villages *check*, so comparing their coaching notes with Taishan's shows whether the method of concealment was shared.
