## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's tycoon families, civil service and Beijing-linked bodies:** wealth, office and hidden backing as three kinds of power.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** provincial rulers against the national center, from warlords to reform officials.
- **Hailufeng and Chaoshan Min speakers:** peasant power in 1927 and lineage power later.
- **Macau's traditional associations and Macanese:** a civic system run through associations.
- **Guangxi Baihua and Pinghua towns:** the Guangxi cliques and their officer corps.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan:** Lu Rongting's legacy, village strongmen and local headmen.
- **Danjia boat people:** once at the bottom of the land order; Henry Fok's rise is one story among many.
