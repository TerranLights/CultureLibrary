# Yue (粤): Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation; Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau, with their peoples; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong professionals and grassroots workers:** the Lion Rock story told from two incomes.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant workers:** meaning tied to sending money home, or lost on the line.
- **Guangzhou old-city families:** shopkeeping and lineage as the point, against the young who lie flat.
- **Siyi villages:** lives spent abroad to build a house at home; meaning measured by the return.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** trade, clan and charity as the told sources of a full life.
- **Guangxi Baihua towns:** slower economies; what young people leave for and why.
- **Zhuang and Dong singers:** meaning carried in song cycles across a life.
- **Yao mountain villages:** a man's ritual name and standing earned through rites as a life's point, in their own account.
- **Macau casino workers:** shift work in gaming and what workers say it is for.
- **Elders in public housing:** life review in old age, often alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (psychology, suicide research, film); good for delta factory life; thin for Guangxi minorities, mostly song collections.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for song lyrics and youth slang; Classical Chinese for Zhan Ruoshui; Zhuang for Liao song texts; Teochew for family life accounts; Portuguese for Macanese memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and worker deaths; worker-poets' writing faced censorship; "lying flat" talk is policed on mainland platforms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
