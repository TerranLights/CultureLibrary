# Yue (粤): Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau) and its peoples; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Iu Mien, Teochew, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong readers by generation:** print, television and social feeds as different sources of what is true.
- **Guangdong family chat groups:** elders and the young correcting, or not correcting, each other.
- **Macau's Portuguese and Chinese readers:** two presses, two sets of facts.
- **Guangzhou medical circles and herbal-tea sellers:** two authorities on bodily truth.
- **Siyi emigrant families:** news from overseas relatives as a rival source.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** clan elders and temple boards as the settlers of village disputes over fact.
- **Zhuang:** the state's classification beside villages' own accounts of who they are.
- **Yao and Dong:** letter-songs, genealogies and songs as accounts of origin, beside state histories.
- **Danjia and Leizhou fishers:** oral memory where paper is missing.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka genealogies claim northern origin, so the truth of descent stories is argued among Hakka scholars on their own terms, apart from Cantonese claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong media trust; mixed for Guangdong platforms (CNKI, press); thin for Yao songs and fishing communities' oral records.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for rumor talk, oaths and the wiki; Classical Chinese for gazetteers; Iu Mien for letter-songs; Zhuang for village accounts; Portuguese for Macau's news.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** press freedom and history curricula are politically charged in Hong Kong; ethnic classification remains a delicate topic in Guangxi. Courtroom oaths and evidence rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
