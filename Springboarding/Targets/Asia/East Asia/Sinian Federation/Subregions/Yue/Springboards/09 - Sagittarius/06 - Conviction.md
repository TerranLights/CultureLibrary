## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong students of the 1970s and of later decades:** first causes in different eras, and what became of their stands.
- **Hong Kong church schools and congregations:** conviction tested where church, school and state meet.
- **Guangzhou merchants and students:** the old boycott town; whether conviction is now voiced through spending.
- **Delta villages (Panyu, Lufeng):** holding out over land and village office.
- **Hailufeng Min speakers of Lufeng and Haifeng:** their own record of village stands, apart from the Cantonese delta.
- **Guangxi Han and Zhuang students:** wartime volunteers and the memory of them.
- **Buddhist and sectarian vegetarians:** quiet lifelong vows rather than public stands.
- **Macau residents:** stands taken rarely, and why the 2014 one broke the pattern.
- **Catholics on the mainland and in Macau:** loyalty to Rome or to the registered church, as each side tells it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong movements and press; mixed for Guangdong village disputes (press, NGO reports); thin for Guangxi minorities' stands outside state histories.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for press vocabulary and opera; Classical Chinese for loyalist poems; Hailufeng Min for village testimony; Portuguese for Macau's press; Zhuang for war memoirs from Zhuang counties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** village protests, Catholic loyalties and Hong Kong's recent movements involve living people at risk; protest law goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
