# Yue (粤): Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation: Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau and their peoples; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese (with Siyi Taishanese), Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Iu Mien, Standard Zhuang, Vietnamese (for camp records), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping, Enping, Xinhui):** generations of men abroad; the village as a place to return to.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** leaving for Siam and the South Seas, by sea and on credit.
- **Hong Kong families:** emigration waves, return, and the "astronaut" parent.
- **Macau residents:** a city that lives by receiving visitors more than by leaving.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** a city of arrivals; the Spring Festival exodus.
- **Zhuang and Guangxi Han:** seasonal travel to delta factories and back.
- **Yao (Iu Mien):** migration through the hills as their own history, and the lost homeland.
- **Jing of the Beibu Gulf:** sea journeys and the border with Vietnam.
- **Danjia boat families:** a life on the water, always traveling within the delta.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka emigrants built their own overseas routes from Meizhou, so their leaving songs and remittance houses form a separate strand to compare with Siyi's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration history, Hong Kong and Macau tourism; mixed for Guangxi labor travel; thin for Yao and Jing journeys outside legends and heritage files.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese and Taishanese for emigrants' songs and letters; Classical Chinese for Song port notes; Teochew for leaving songs; Iu Mien for homeland legends; Vietnamese and English for camp records; Portuguese for Macau's port history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent emigration from Hong Kong is politically charged; camp records name living refugees; diaspora findings serve only as comparison.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
