## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong retail and tourist trade:** shops serving residents and shops serving tour groups.
- **Macau:** a visitor economy where shop trust is certified.
- **Shenzhen electronics traders:** the copy, the fake and the clever product, as they define each.
- **Foshan and Guangzhou guild heirs:** old trade rules beside today's market.
- **Chaoshan Teochew merchants:** word-of-honor credit across distance.
- **Siyi families:** honesty in remittances and in accounts kept for absent kin.
- **Guangxi market towns:** periodic markets 圩日 where Zhuang, Yao and Han trade face to face.
- **Students in Hong Kong and Guangzhou universities:** what counts as cheating under each system.
- **Jing border traders of Dongxing:** cross-border trade and what is declared.
