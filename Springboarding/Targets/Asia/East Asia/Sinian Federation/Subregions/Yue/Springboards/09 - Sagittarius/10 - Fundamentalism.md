# Yue (粤): Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation, written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau and their peoples; region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Arabic (for mosque teaching), Latin and Portuguese (Macau's church records), English. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong evangelical and mainline churches:** inerrancy and separation against social engagement; the line drawn within each denomination.
- **Macau Catholics:** a church shaped by the rites dispute, now beside Chinese temple life.
- **Guangzhou's Hui:** scripture-first reform and older custom inside one minority.
- **Mainland registered and unregistered Protestants in Guangdong:** who calls whom "orthodox" or "cult".
- **Classics-recitation families:** a non-religious return to founding texts, and the schools that resist it.
- **Guangxi counties of the late 1960s:** political certainty and its violence, read through survivors and records.
- **Zhuang and Yao ritual masters:** strict exactness in reciting texts; whether that is orthodoxy or craft, in their own terms.
- **Teochew and Cantonese vegetarian halls:** lay sects with strict rules of diet and celibacy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong churches (seminary, advocacy, press); mixed for Guangdong (official cult lists, press); thin and closed for Guangzhou's mosques and for Cultural Revolution records.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for church and street words; Classical Chinese for Ming-Qing orthodoxy campaigns; Arabic and Chinese for mosque teaching; Latin and Portuguese for the rites dispute; English for seminary statements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 邪教 label carries state force; Cultural Revolution killings are restricted; leavers and members may be at risk if named. Describe groups as members and scholars do; the word "fundamentalist" is a charge, not a finding.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
