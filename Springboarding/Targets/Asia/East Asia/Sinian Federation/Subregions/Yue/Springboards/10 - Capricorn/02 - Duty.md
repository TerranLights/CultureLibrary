## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** officials' duties redrawn by oaths; no conscription; families buying care they cannot give.
- **Macau:** a small civil service under oath; family duty in a gaming economy of shift work.
- **Shunde and the delta:** sworn spinsters' bonds to natal families against married women's duties to in-laws.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** duties carried out from abroad, by money and by return.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** eldest-son duties and ancestral rites that call emigrants home.
- **Leizhou Min speakers:** village rites with their own rotas of obligation.
- **Guangxi Han:** settler families keeping grave and hall duties in mixed counties.
- **Zhuang:** the 《传扬歌》 code and secondary burial.
- **Maonan, Mulao, Yao, Dong, Jing:** vows, ancestor rites and village work owed by each household.
- **Guangxi migrant workers in the delta:** duty to parents and children at home met from far away.
- **Foreign domestic workers from Southeast Asia:** caring for Hong Kong families while supporting their own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong official codes and elder-care studies; mixed for Guangdong; thin and ritual-centered for Maonan and Zhuang duties outside heritage files.
- **Languages needed:** Zhuang for the 《传扬歌》 text; Classical Chinese for grave-guarding records; Cantonese for sworn spinsters' oral histories; Teochew for eldest-son duties; Portuguese for Macau's oath texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** officials' oaths after 2021 are politically charged; some vow rites are performed only for the family that owes them. Law goes to Capricorn's Law sheet (spirit) or Libra (letter).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
