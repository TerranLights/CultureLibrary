## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** ministers under an accountability system; a public that judges resignations closely.
- **Macau:** a small administration watched by its own ombudsman.
- **Guangdong cities:** mainland accountability campaigns that name and discipline officials after disasters.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant parents:** who answers for children left in home villages or alone in rented rooms.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** family firms where debts and names pass between generations.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** relatives at home answering for land and graves of those abroad.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** farm and fishing villages with their own compacts *check*.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** mining counties where industry and rivers meet.
- **Zhuang:** downstream villages of polluted rivers, and their forest rules.
- **Yao, Dong, Miao, Maonan, Jing:** village compacts on hills, fields and fishing grounds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong inquiries and law; mixed for mainland accountability (official reports open, independent checks thin); thin for village stewardship outside Chinese-language field notes.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the Canton bonds; Cantonese for blame slang; Teochew for family-firm debt talk; Zhuang for forest compacts; Portuguese for Macau ombudsman reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster inquiries on the mainland are political; care-home abuse involves victims who cannot speak for themselves. Law goes to Capricorn's Law sheet (spirit) or Libra (letter).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
