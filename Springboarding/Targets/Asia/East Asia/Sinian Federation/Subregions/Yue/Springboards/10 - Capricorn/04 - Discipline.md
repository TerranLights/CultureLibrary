## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** disciplined services in transition; schools that ended caning.
- **Macau:** its own security-forces academy and Portuguese-founded schools.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** martial-arts halls with long teaching lines; factory regimes for migrants.
- **Northern Guangdong (Shaoguan):** monastic discipline in a mountain district.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** opera and dance troupes that train children hard.
- **Guangxi Han cities (Nanning, Liuzhou, Guilin):** private camps and strict schools sold to anxious parents.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Jing:** village upbringing and its own corrections, to be heard from the communities.
- **Young migrants from Guangxi in Guangdong factories:** discipline met at work, far from family.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka and Cantonese martial-arts lineages (Southern Mantis and Wing Chun) train in the same towns, a test of whether a hall's discipline follows its lineage or its people; otherwise its own sub-unit.
