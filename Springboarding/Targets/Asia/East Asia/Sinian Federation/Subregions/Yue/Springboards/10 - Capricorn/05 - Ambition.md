*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** generations with very different ladders; property as the measure of having risen.
- **Macau:** careers shaped by one dominant industry.
- **Shenzhen and the delta:** migrants who came to rise, and what the cities offer or withhold.
- **Guangzhou's old families:** merchant ambition that once bought rank.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** ambition long pointed abroad, and the houses built at home to show it.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** founders' stories and the networks behind them.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** lower-income counties whose young people leave for the delta.
- **Guangxi Han cities:** students and workers choosing between home and Guangdong.
- **Zhuang and Yao:** first-generation students from mountain villages, in their own words.
- **Dong, Miao, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** whether ambition means leaving or building at home, case by case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong surveys and policy; mixed for Guangdong (city policy open, aspiration data scattered); thin for Guangxi minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Qing title purchases; Cantonese for envy and promotion slang; Teochew for founders' talk of striving; Zhuang and Yao for students' family stories; Portuguese for Macau talent policy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** youth discontent in Hong Kong since 2019; resentment of the rich on the mainland; minors in dream-job polls.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
