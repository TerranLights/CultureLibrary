*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** early banding, open recruitment exams and talent visas; debates over who deserves a flat or a post.
- **Macau:** a small civil service moving from personal hiring to open exams.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** exam ladders for local students against points and talent rules for newcomers.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** schools and scholarships funded from abroad, rewarding local students *check*.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** university places and scholarships given by Teochew donors.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** students from low-cutoff counties competing with the delta.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** civil-service posts as the main ladder in poorer counties.
- **Zhuang:** "capable persons" against lineage elders in village leadership.
- **Yao, Dong, Miao, Maonan, Mulao, Jing:** how merit is judged in song, ritual craft and village work, each in its own terms.
- **Hakka (optional):** where Hakka lineage genealogies in northern and eastern Guangdong list examination degrees, a check on whether exam merit was recorded and honored the same way in Cantonese lineages nearby; otherwise its own sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong exams, allocation and visas; mixed for mainland exams (cutoffs and ratios open, hiring by connections hard to see); thin for minority villages' own ideas of merit.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Tang biographies; Cantonese for "connections" slang and school talk; Teochew for donors' scholarship stories; Zhuang for village leadership; Portuguese for Macau recruitment notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** connections in public hiring are a political subject on the mainland; minors in school sorting. Bonus points for minority candidates were already scouted under Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
