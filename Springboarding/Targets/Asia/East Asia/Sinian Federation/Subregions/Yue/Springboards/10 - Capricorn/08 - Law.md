# Yue (粤): Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation (its own lands and peoples), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Teochew and Hailufeng Min, Dong and Miao (through Chinese sources), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line (spirit of the law only):** how people relate to the binding rules of their community: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong:** a common-law system whose purpose people argue over openly, before and after 2020.
- **Macau:** Portuguese-derived law in Chinese hands; a small bar and its own debates.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** mainland "rule according to law" beside petitioning and personal ties.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and the Hailufeng area:** lineage settlement and village solidarity set against outside law.
- **Siyi emigrant counties:** family disputes carried across borders and legal systems.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** county courts and state legal-education drives.
- **Zhuang:** customary rules beside state law; legal campaigns in the Zhuang language.
- **Dong (Sanjiang, Longsheng) and Miao (Rongshui):** alliance covenants and stone covenants, as the communities describe them.
- **Yao, Maonan, Jing:** their own village rules and keepers, each to be heard in its own terms.
- **Danjia fishers:** sea customs that work as law at the harbor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong legal culture and debate; mixed for the mainland (campaign material plentiful, independent data thin); thin for Dong and Miao covenants beyond heritage and field reports.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Qing litigation; Cantonese for proverbs and courtroom talk; Teochew for lineage mediation; Dong and Miao for covenant texts; Portuguese for Macau's legal localization.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civil disobedience prosecutions and the 1999 interpretation are politically charged; petitioners on the mainland face pressure. Statutes, codes and cases as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
