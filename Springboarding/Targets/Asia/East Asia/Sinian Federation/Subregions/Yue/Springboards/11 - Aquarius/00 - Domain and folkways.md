# Yue (粤): The domain: community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation, written for its own lands and peoples (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese (Guangzhou-Hong Kong standard, Siyi, Guangxi Baihua), Standard Written Chinese in both scripts, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Leizhou Min, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for: friends, associations, humanitarian ideals and the future, with settlement, migration, hospitality and neighboring as the sign's social ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong public-estate tenants and New Territories villagers:** a resettled city against lineage villages.
- **Macau's Chinese residents and Macanese:** two old communities joined through associations.
- **Guangzhou old-city residents and urban-village migrants:** one city, two settlement patterns.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan:** newcomer cities with few native ties.
- **Siyi and Zhongshan emigrant districts:** villages that hold members who live abroad.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou Min speakers:** Min villages inside Guangdong, each with its own gods and halls.
- **Guangxi Han (Baihua, Pinghua, Guiliu):** market towns among non-Han villages.
- **Zhuang, Yao, Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing, Yi, Gelao:** each people's village and gathering forms, separate.
- **Danjia (boat people) and Hui:** a group long kept apart, and a faith community.
- **Hakka (optional):** their round and square fortified houses in northern and eastern Guangdong are a different settlement pattern from Cantonese villages; the Hakka are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong and Macau associations; mixed for Guangdong cities; thin for Guangxi minorities outside Chinese-language fieldwork. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** this sign is written for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau as a working list the author still confirms.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for neighbor talk; Classical Chinese for altar and compact texts; Teochew and Leizhou Min for their villages; Zhuang for place names; Portuguese for Macau's registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hong Kong civil society since 2020 (groups disbanded or deregistered); official "ethnic unity" material is one-sided. Rules on registering associations go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
