# Yue (粤): Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), member state of the Sinian Federation; its own lands (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau) and peoples, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Pinghua, Standard Zhuang, Yao (Chinese renderings), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit: how the group is weighed against the person, how "us" is set against "them", and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and Dongguan river villages:** dragon-boat alliances as group lines.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** lineage camps in the past, merchant bands abroad (diaspora as comparison only).
- **Hong Kong students and young workers:** hall loyalty, estates and street groups.
- **Hong Kong families across the 2019 divide:** a political line through kin.
- **Macanese:** a group kept through schools and associations.
- **Guangxi Han (Pinghua, Baihua, Guiliu):** separate group names inside the Han label.
- **Zhuang branches:** many self-names under one category.
- **Baiku Yao and other Yao groups (Pan, Bunu, Lajia):** each with its own group forms.
- **Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing:** separate, not one "minority" block.
- **Leizhou Min villages:** lineage and village loyalties in the far west of Guangdong.
- **Hakka (optional):** the Hakka were themselves one side of the region's best-known group feuds, so their own accounts of those camps belong here; the Hakka are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong social science and press; mixed for Guangdong village alliances (Chinese-language fieldwork); thin for small Guangxi peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for slang of joining and betraying; Classical Chinese for feud and guild records; Teochew for merchant bands; Pinghua and Zhuang for self-names; Portuguese for Macanese associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political camps in Hong Kong since 2019; gang research involves minors; ethnic classification is an official process. Group punishment as law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
