# Yue (粤): Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a Sinian Federation member state of Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Yao (Chinese renderings), Portuguese, English, Tagalog and Indonesian (for domestic workers' groups). **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where the answers differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's richest and poorest districts:** equality of outcome measured street by street.
- **Pearl River Delta against eastern, western and northern Guangdong:** a provincial gap.
- **Chaoshan Teochew families:** daughters and sons, separately from delta Cantonese.
- **Zhuang villages:** inheritance and women's standing, to compare with Han neighbors.
- **Yao, Miao and Maonan mountain counties:** peoples in the poorest counties.
- **Guangxi Han (Pinghua, Baihua, Guiliu):** town and village ranks among Han themselves.
- **Macau residents and non-resident workers:** two classes of worker in one city.
- **Hong Kong foreign domestic workers:** live-in workers outside settlement.
- **Mainland migrants in Guangdong cities:** equality of schooling and services without local registration.
- **Danjia fishing families:** a group once ranked below farmers.
- **Gay and lesbian Cantonese speakers:** family equality debates in Guangzhou and Hong Kong.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong income and gender data; mixed for Guangdong; thin and official in tone for Guangxi minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for address terms; Classical Chinese for Taiping texts; Teochew for family surveys; Zhuang for customary terms; Portuguese, Tagalog and Indonesian for workers' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-reform history; sexual-minority organizing on the mainland; worker complaints. Equality laws, anti-discrimination ordinances and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
