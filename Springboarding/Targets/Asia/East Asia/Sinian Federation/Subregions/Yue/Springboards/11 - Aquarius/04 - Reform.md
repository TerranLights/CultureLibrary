# Yue (粤): Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤) in the Sinian Federation: Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau, with their peoples, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese in both scripts, Classical and late-Qing Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Yao (Chinese renderings), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nanhai, Xinhui and Xiangshan (Zhongshan):** home districts of the late-Qing reformers and revolutionaries.
- **Guangzhou:** municipal reform in the 1920s and market reform after 1978.
- **Hong Kong:** colonial reform from above, then electoral reform argued in public.
- **Macau:** administrative and gaming reforms on a small scale.
- **Shenzhen against Shantou:** two zones, two outcomes.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** the 1930s provincial program.
- **Zhuang villages:** self-government from below at Hezhai.
- **Yao of Dayaoshan and other Yao groups:** reforms carried into the mountains.
- **Dong, Miao and Jing communities:** each met state reform differently.
- **Danjia fishing families:** shore-settlement programs, as reform done to a group.
- **Cantonese opera circles:** reform as art.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late-Qing reformers and Hong Kong policy; mixed for Guangdong's reform era (official histories lead); thin for Yao and Zhuang voices on 1950s reforms.
- **Languages needed:** late-Qing Chinese for reform tracts; Cantonese for opera scripts and consultations; Zhuang and Yao for local memory of reforms; Portuguese for Macau's administrative records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hong Kong electoral reform and the protests around it; 1950s reforms in minority areas; NGO rules since 2017. Legal reforms as text go to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
