# Yue (粤): Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), one member state of the Sinian Federation, covering Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau and their peoples, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Leizhou Min, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong students of the 1970s and of the 2010s:** two generations of youthful idealism.
- **Shenzhen's reform-era youth:** ideals beside "make money first".
- **Guangzhou's cultural circles:** a merchant city's view of dreamers.
- **Macau's Portuguese and Macanese writers:** a literary idealism of their own.
- **Leizhou and western Guangdong:** a land of exiles remembered for principle.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** merchant ideals at home, and their overseas form only as comparison.
- **Zhuang and Yao of the poor counties:** receivers of volunteers and programs, with their own views.
- **Guangxi Han towns (Guilin, Liuzhou, Wuzhou):** missions, tourism and the staging of ideals.
- **Dong and Miao villages:** cast as idylls by tourism, to compare with their own accounts.
- **Catholic and Protestant communities in Guangdong and Hong Kong:** callings to serve.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong literature, film and student history; mixed for mainland youth (official framing); thin for minority voices on how they are idealized.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for film, song and student writing; Classical Chinese for shrine records; Leizhou Min for local memory of the exiles; Portuguese for Macau's literary idealism; English for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** films and books withdrawn from circulation in Hong Kong since 2020; missionary records carry outsiders' bias.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
