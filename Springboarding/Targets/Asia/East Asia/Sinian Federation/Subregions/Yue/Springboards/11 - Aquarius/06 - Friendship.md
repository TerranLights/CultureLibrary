# Yue (粤): Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state; Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau and the peoples of those lands, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (simplified and traditional), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Yao and Dong (Chinese renderings), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guangzhou and Foshan elders:** park and teahouse circles in later life.
- **Hong Kong men and women:** friendship patterns to be tested by sex.
- **Hong Kong school-leavers:** whether old-student groups last.
- **Macanese:** a small community where friends and kin overlap.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrants:** friends made by home county and dormitory.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** friendship circles and favors among merchants, set apart from Cantonese ones.
- **Siyi emigrant families:** friendships kept across oceans (diaspora as comparison only).
- **Zhuang, Yao and Dong:** sworn friendship and its local forms, district by district.
- **Miao, Mulao, Maonan and Jing:** friendship customs not yet mapped.
- **Guangxi Han (Pinghua, Baihua, Guiliu):** friendships across Han and non-Han lines in market towns.
- **Hakka (optional):** whether sworn same-age bonds are made across Hakka and Zhuang villages in mixed Guangxi counties is a question for this concept; the Hakka are a separate sub-unit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong surveys and Cantonese poetry; mixed for Guangdong migrants; thin for minority friendship outside Chinese-language fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for poetry societies and lineage rules; Cantonese for slang, songs and opera circles; Zhuang, Yao and Dong for sworn-friend terms (the Zhuang word for "friend" to be taken from a Zhuang-Chinese dictionary); Portuguese for Macanese circles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collusion cases name living people; friendship research on minors needs consent.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
