# Yue (粤): Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation, for the peoples of Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), late-Qing literary Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong youth subcultures:** MK style, punk, graffiti and their audiences.
- **Hong Kong film and opera audiences:** licensed rule-breaking on screen and stage.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen artists and dropouts:** alternative ways of life in mainland cities.
- **Macau's independent artists and Macanese writers:** a small scene with its own tolerance.
- **Chaoshan Teochew youth:** lineage pressure as the norm departed from.
- **Siyi and Zhongshan:** returnees from abroad with new dress and habits, and how villages received them (overseas forms only as comparison).
- **Zhuang villages:** trickster tales and village norms, district by district.
- **Yao, Dong, Miao and Jing young people:** leaving or remaking customs in their own ways.
- **Guangxi Han tourist towns (Yangshuo, Guilin):** norms loosened around visitors.
- **Never-married men and women:** standing in Cantonese, Teochew and minority families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong culture and media studies; mixed for mainland subcultures; thin for minority youth.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for slang, punk lyrics and comedy; late-Qing Chinese for Su Manshu and his critics; Teochew for family pressure; Zhuang for tale collections; Portuguese for Macau's arts press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest-linked music and art in Hong Kong since 2020; gender and sexual nonconformity on the mainland; Chaoshan family research touches private conflicts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
