# Yue (粤): Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state: the lands of Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau and the peoples living there, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (traditional and simplified), Classical and Buddhist Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms count as calm or objectivity, others as coldness or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong's high-rise residents:** distance among neighbors in a dense city.
- **Hong Kong young people:** withdrawal as hidden youth against public engagement.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen white-collar workers:** reserve at work and at home.
- **Macau's casino workforce:** composure as a job.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** monastic and lay calm in a lineage society.
- **Buddhist, Daoist and Catholic practitioners:** detachment as discipline, separately by tradition.
- **Zhuang villages:** mourning and restraint, district by district.
- **Yao, Dong and Miao communities:** their own forms of mourning and calm, not grouped together.
- **Guangxi Han (Pinghua, Baihua, Guiliu):** reserve among townspeople.
- **Elders living alone:** in Hong Kong estates and Guangdong villages emptied by migration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong psychology and social work; mixed for Guangdong; thin for minority emotional styles.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for everyday blame and praise words; Buddhist and Classical Chinese for Chan and hermit texts; Teochew for temple life; Zhuang for funeral terms; Portuguese for Macau's church and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental-health and withdrawal research involves vulnerable people; political apathy is a charged label in Hong Kong after 2019.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
