# Yue (粤): Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), the Sinian Federation's member state for Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong and Macau and their peoples, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese in both scripts, Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang and old Zhuang script (Sawndip), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong families who emigrated, and those who stayed:** two kinds of hope from one city.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan newcomers:** hope as a wager on a new city.
- **Guangzhou and Foshan households:** New Year wish customs, old and new.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** sayings on luck and fate, and hopes placed in sons.
- **Siyi emigrant districts:** hopes sent home from abroad (diaspora as comparison only).
- **Danjia fishing families:** hope at sea.
- **Zhuang villages:** ritual requests and farming festivals, district by district.
- **Yao, Dong, Miao, Maonan and Mulao:** each people's own rites for a good year, separately.
- **Guangxi Han towns:** hopes for children who leave for the delta.
- **Macau residents and gamblers:** a city built on betting, and its residents' own hopes for the future.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong surveys and emigration studies; mixed for Guangdong youth; thin for minority ritual hopes outside Chinese translations.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for wish phrases and betting talk; Classical Chinese for print captions; Teochew for proverbs; Zhuang and its old script for the Mo scriptures; Portuguese for Macau's vows to saints.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** emigration after 2020 is politically charged; hopelessness and gambling research involves vulnerable people; some Zhuang ritual texts belong to ritualists and may be restricted by them.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
