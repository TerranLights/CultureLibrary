# Yue (粤): The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), a member state of the Sinian Federation, written for its own lands and peoples (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang and Sawndip, Iu Mien, Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden self, madness, sleep, confinement, spirits, transcendence and magic ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** dense housing, hospital psychiatry, a ghost-story market.
- **Macau's Chinese residents and Macanese:** temple and Catholic layers over the same streets.
- **Delta and western Guangdong Cantonese (Foshan, Zhaoqing, Maoming):** village healers beside the cities.
- **Siyi (Taishan, Kaiping):** emigrant villages praying for absent members.
- **Northern Guangdong (Shaoguan, Qingyuan):** mountain monasteries and polluted mining villages.
- **Chaoshan Teochew and Leizhou Min speakers:** their own exorcisms and seventh-month customs.
- **Guangxi Han (Baihua, Pinghua, Guiliu):** town temples in Wuzhou, Guilin and Liuzhou.
- **Zhuang of Baise and Hechi:** mediums and Mo masters, with Sawndip texts.
- **Yao (Iu Mien, Bunu, the Pai Yao of Liannan):** Taoist-derived rites for spirits and the dead.
- **Dong, Miao, Mulao, Maonan, Jing, She, Shui, Yi, Gelao:** each people's healers, mostly seen through Chinese fieldwork *check*.
- **Danjia boat people:** water spirits, long recorded by outsiders.
