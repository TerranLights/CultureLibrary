# Yue (粤): Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang (through Zhuang-Chinese dictionaries), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** a counseling and social-work vocabulary drawn from English.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** mainland psychology terms and hotlines; a city of migrants reading strangers.
- **Macau's Chinese residents and Macanese:** daily life across a language line, where reading the other is a working skill.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** how a tight lineage world trains children to read elders.
- **Northern Guangdong and western Guangxi mining districts (Shaoguan, Hechi):** disasters that tested public feeling.
- **Zhuang and Yao singers:** song exchange as a way of answering another's sorrow.
- **Blind and Deaf communities in Hong Kong and Guangzhou:** on the receiving end of "empathy" simulations, with their own views.
- **Mainland newcomers and Southeast Asian domestic workers in Hong Kong:** groups whose feelings the majority is asked to imagine.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong counseling and medical education; mixed for Guangdong; thin for Zhuang and other Guangxi peoples, where song collections are the main route.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for idioms and crisis-line work; Standard Chinese for the competing translations; Classical Chinese for the reciprocity texts; Zhuang for song texts; Portuguese for Macau.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crisis-line records are confidential; mine-disaster reporting remains touchy; simulations can offend the groups they imitate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
