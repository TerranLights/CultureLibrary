## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** film melodrama of parents' sacrifice; uniformed-service honors.
- **Guangzhou and the delta:** state martyr culture beside family duty.
- **Hailufeng (Shanwei) and Chaoshan Min speakers:** revolutionary martyrdom in Haifeng; the Teochew household ideal under strain.
- **Western Guangdong (Zhanjiang, Maoming, Wuchuan):** vow rites with pain in village festivals.
- **Guangxi border counties (Chongzuo, Fangchenggang, Baise):** families of the 1979-era war dead.
- **Zhuang and Jing border villages:** militia service and losses, in their own accounts *check*.
- **Macau's Catholic orders and Macanese families:** vowed lives and Catholic ideals of self-denial.
- **Hakka (optional):** in Haifeng and Lufeng the Hakka and Min speakers lived side by side during the peasant movement, so its memory is shared across the two.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong film and press; mixed for Guangdong (state martyr histories lead); thin and sensitive for Guangxi's war dead.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for Wen Tianxiang and the old sanctions; Cantonese for film and family talk; Hailufeng and Leizhou Min for village memory and vow rites; Portuguese for the Macau sisters' records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1979 war and later border fighting are touchy; hunger strikes link to political cases; donor families are private people. Military duty as law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
