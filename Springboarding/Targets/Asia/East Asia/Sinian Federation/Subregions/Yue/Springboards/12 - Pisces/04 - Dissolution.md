## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** dementia, sea burial, the end of factory work, a crowd culture around pop idols.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen:** a migrant city where newcomers blend in or stay apart.
- **Macau's Macanese and Portuguese-speaking residents:** a community thinned since 1999.
- **She of Huizhou, Shanwei and Guangzhou's Zengcheng:** a language near its end.
- **Zhuang towns in Nanning, Baise and Liuzhou:** the pull of Standard Chinese and Cantonese on Zhuang speakers.
- **Yao, Dong and Miao villages emptied by migration (Hezhou, Sanjiang, Rongshui):** villages that come apart as the young leave.
- **Danjia fisherfolk:** a water-borne way of life ending ashore.
- **Hakka (optional):** many abandoned New Territories villages, such as Lai Chi Wo 荔枝窩, are Hakka, the clearest case of a village dissolving and being restored as heritage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong statistics, press and psychology; mixed for Guangdong; thin for She and other small peoples outside linguistic surveys.
