# Yue (粤): Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Teochew, Standard Zhuang, Jing (a Vietnamese variety), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** extreme density, thin walls, strong privacy talk.
- **New Territories villagers and border residents (Sha Tau Kok, Ta Kwu Ling):** decades inside a closed zone.
- **Macau residents and daily crossers to Zhuhai:** a city whose edge is a gate.
- **Guangzhou and Shenzhen compound dwellers:** gated living as the norm.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** lineage-village lines between insiders and outsiders.
- **Yao subgroups of the Dayaoshan (Jinxiu):** lines among Yao groups themselves.
- **Zhuang and Jing border villages (Napo, Dongxing):** kin and trade across a national line.
- **Danjia fisherfolk:** a group once kept off the land and out of marriage with farmers.
- **Mainland newcomers in Hong Kong:** marked as outsiders in daily talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong press, statistics and planning; mixed for Guangdong cities; thin for border villages in Guangxi outside Chinese-language fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for the pass and its travelers; Cantonese for neighbor and privacy talk; Zhuang and Jing for border villages; Portuguese for Macau's gate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** border zones may limit fieldwork; doxxing material names private people; harassment survivors are living people. Border and privacy law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
