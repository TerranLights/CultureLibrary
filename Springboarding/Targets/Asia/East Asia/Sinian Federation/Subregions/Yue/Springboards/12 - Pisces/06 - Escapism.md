## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** travel abroad, fandom, staycations, the drug register.
- **Macau residents and casino workers:** living inside the escape others come for.
- **Shenzhen and Dongguan migrant workers:** net cafés and dropping out in factory towns.
- **Guangzhou and Foshan:** weekend escapes to mountains, hot springs and old towns.
- **Siyi and Zhongshan emigrant families:** a tradition of leaving that shaped shopping and leisure.
- **Guangxi Han and Zhuang towns (Nanning, Liuzhou, Baise):** internet cafés and addiction camps; local drinking customs.
- **Yao and Dong villages (Jinxiu, Sanjiang):** places others escape to as tourists, with their own view of visitors.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** tea as an everyday pause, and family pressure on those who withdraw.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong (drug register, tourism, press); mixed for Guangdong (press and ethnography of migrant youth); thin for Guangxi minorities' own escapes.
- **Languages needed:** Portuguese and Chinese for Macau's gaming history; Cantonese for leisure slang; Standard Chinese for Shenzhen and Nanning cases; Zhuang for drinking words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug users, runaways and camp victims are living people or their families; addiction-camp coverage is touchy. Gambling and drug law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
