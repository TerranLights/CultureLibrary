# Yue (粤): Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Yue (粤), Sinian Federation member state (Guangdong, Guangxi, Hong Kong, Macau), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Cantonese, Standard Written Chinese (both scripts), Classical Chinese, Standard Zhuang, Japanese (for 1941-1945 records), Portuguese, English. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hong Kong Cantonese:** memory of 1941, end-of-life debates, a crowded city that forces letting go of things.
- **Guangzhou:** the 1938 retreat and the 1945 ceremony in the same city.
- **Macau:** neutrality in the war, then its own handovers.
- **Chaoshan Teochew:** village deity vows where one yields to a god's decision *check*.
- **Zhuang and Yao villages:** sayings and rites of yielding, recorded mainly in Chinese.
- **Jing of Dongxing:** fishing life and sea danger, where acceptance of the sea's will is a theme to check.
- **Christians in Hong Kong and Guangzhou:** surrender to God as a stated goal.
- **Hakka (optional):** Hakka village militias fought the British takeover of the New Territories in 1899 alongside Cantonese lineages, a comparison of who yielded and who held out.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hong Kong war history and end-of-life care; mixed for Guangdong's wartime memory; thin for Guangxi peoples' own words on yielding.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese for 1850s verse; English, Japanese and Chinese for 1941-1945; Cantonese for acceptance talk; Zhuang for sayings; Portuguese for Macau.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime defeat is told differently by each side; the 2019 reuse of "Be water" is political; patients and pet owners are private people. End-of-life law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category only, under the standing rule.
