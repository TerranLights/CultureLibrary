**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands: Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and the local Mandarin groups (Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao, Zhongyuan Mandarin), Jin Chinese (north Henan, northwest Hebei); Manchu and Mongolian heritage terms; Hui Islamic vocabulary; Korean. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it asserts itself: personhood, body, appearance, naming ways, sport as a statement of the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijingers and banner descendants** against **migrants (北漂)**: inherited against self-made urban selves.
- **Tianjin**: port and concession history, own dialect.
- **Hebei**: central plain; Cangzhou (Hui villages); the north (Jin speech; Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties: Weichang, Fengning, Kuancheng).
- **Shandong**: Jiaodong peninsula (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai); Qufu-Zoucheng; the southwest (Heze, Jining); Yimeng hills (Linyi).
- **Henan**: east (Kaifeng), west (Luoyang), north (Anyang, Jin speech), south (Nanyang, Xinyang).
- **Peoples:** Hui, Manchu, Mongols, Chaoxianzu and Korean residents, Kaifeng Jewish descendants.
- **Faith groups:** Hebei Catholic villages, Henan Protestants, Buddhist and Daoist clergy.
- **Out-migrants:** Shandong-Hebei descendants in the Northeast (闯关东); Shandong-origin Chinese in Korea.
