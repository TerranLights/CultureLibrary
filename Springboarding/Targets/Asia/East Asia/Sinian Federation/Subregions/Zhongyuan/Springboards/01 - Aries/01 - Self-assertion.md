*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Beijing, Tianjin, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan Mandarin slang; Jin Chinese; Korean; Mongolian and Hui vocabulary for minority settings. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing natives and Tianjin natives:** two sharp-tongued urban speech traditions to separate rather than merge, against migrants who code-switch at work.
- **Shandong host culture** (banquet seating, toasting) in Jinan and Linyi against **Jiaodong port cities** with foreign-firm workplaces.
- **Henan villages** that send many migrant workers: how claims are pressed at home against at a distant employer.
- **North Hebei Manchu and Mongol counties** and **Hui neighborhoods** (Niujie, Cangzhou, Zhengzhou's Hui quarter): claims made as a minority.
- **Women and men; elders and the young:** talking back to elders and in-laws is where assertion is most contested.
- **Korean residents** in Wangjing (Beijing) and Qingdao: a second politeness system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on face and politeness in general; mixed for province-specific studies; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, with dialect glossaries for Beijing and Tianjin slang; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning and rights defense (维权) are politically sensitive; the legal side goes to Libra; regional speech stereotypes must be named as variation, not as one voice.
- **Held for review:** none.
