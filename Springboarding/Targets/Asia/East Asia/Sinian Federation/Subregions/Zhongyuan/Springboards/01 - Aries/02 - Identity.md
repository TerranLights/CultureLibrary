# Zhongyuan: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and the regional Mandarin and Jin varieties; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; the Hui community's Arabic and Persian loan vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qi (east and Jiaodong) against Lu (Qufu, Jining) in Shandong:** an internal identity split named in its own scholarship.
- **Henan's "Central Plains" claim** against its migrants elsewhere, who meet the stigma.
- **Hebei (Yan-Zhao) against Beijing and Tianjin,** which it surrounds; the new **Xiong'an** area as an identity in the making.
- **Old Beijing and Tianjin natives** against residents without local hukou.
- **Manchu and Mongol counties** of north Hebei; **Hui** of Beijing, Cangzhou, Henan and Jinan; **Korean** residents; **Kaifeng Jewish descendants**.
- **Christian villages** in Hebei and Henan: a faith identity beside a place identity.
- **Out-migrants and descendants:** the Northeast's Shandong families; Shandong-origin Chinese in Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Han regional discourse and on Hui and Manchu history; mixed on present-day minority self-identification; thin on Kaifeng Jewish descendants.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; Korean; Japanese for older scholarship; Manchu for archival work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification and religious identity; Kaifeng Jewish recognition; hukou; regional stigma (name it as a phenomenon, never as a trait of a people).
- **Held for review:** none.
