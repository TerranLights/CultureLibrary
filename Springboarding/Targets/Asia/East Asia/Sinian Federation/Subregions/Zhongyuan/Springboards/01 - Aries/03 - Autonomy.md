# Zhongyuan: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and regional Mandarin; Korean; Mongolian and Manchu for north Hebei; the Hui community's Islamic vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin graduates and renters** against **county-town and village youth** in Henan and Hebei, where marriage and career are more often family matters.
- **Shandong lineage villages** (Qufu, Jining) against **Jiaodong** and **Qingdao** households with long histories of out-migration and foreign trade.
- **Rural women** in labor-exporting Henan counties against **urban women** with their own incomes.
- **Hui autonomous counties** (Dachang, Mengcun) and **Manchu or Mongol counties** (north Hebei): collective self-rule as lived.
- **Catholic and Protestant congregations:** self-governing faith groups under supervision.
- **Gig workers and migrants** in Beijing: autonomy promised by platforms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on family and marriage in North China; mixed on present-day province-level attitudes; thin on minority counties' internal practice.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; English for the North China ethnographies; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and grid management (网格化管理); religion and church self-government; ethnic autonomy; birth-planning history. Law (marriage law, autonomy statutes) goes to Libra; legal culture to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
