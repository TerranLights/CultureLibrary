## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's tech founders** against **Shandong and Hebei county-town traders** (Cao county, Baigou): very different paths into business.
- **Henan out-migrants** who start businesses elsewhere against those who return home to start one.
- **Tianjin's state-firm workforce** against its rural industrial villages.
- **Collective-model villages** (Nanjie, Red Flag Canal area) against **private-model** ones.
- **Hui traders and halal food entrepreneurs** (Niujie, Zhengzhou, Jinan): a long trading tradition to separate.
- **Korean-run firms** in Qingdao and Wangjing: workplace hierarchy under a second business culture.
- **Women founders** on e-commerce and livestream platforms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and model-village histories; mixed for everyday unasked help at home; thin for minority entrepreneurship.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; English and Korean for firm studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** model campaigns are official narratives to read critically; resident-informant networks and disaster-response criticism are sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
