## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Henan's origin-myth heartland** (Zhoukou, Biyang, Xinzheng) against **Beijing**, which dates its beginnings as a capital.
- **Four print centers** (Yangliuqing, Zhuxianzhen, Yangjiabu, Wuqiang): four local styles of opening the year.
- **Laid-off state workers** (Tianjin, Luoyang, Pingdingshan) against **resettled villagers** (Xichuan): starting over by job and by place.
- **Xiong'an's three counties** (Xiongxian, Rongcheng, Anxin) as a new start imposed from outside.
- **Manchu, Mongol, Hui and Korean** communities, with their own year-starts and first rites.
- **Converts** (Christian, Muslim through marriage, Buddhist refuge-takers): rebirth as a beginning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and myth sites; mixed for personal fresh starts; thin for minority first rites outside festivals.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian for heritage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement and layoffs (official and lived accounts differ); religious conversion; myth sites used in state heritage promotion should be read critically.
- **Held for review:** none.
