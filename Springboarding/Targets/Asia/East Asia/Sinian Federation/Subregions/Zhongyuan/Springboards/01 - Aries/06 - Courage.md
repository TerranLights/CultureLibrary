*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** classical and Standard Chinese; northern colloquial; Manchu and Mongolian for bravery titles; Hui Islamic usage; Korean. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Martial-arts towns** (Dengfeng, Wenxian, Cangzhou, Laiyang) against **cities** where courage is talked about in moral or civic terms.
- **Hui martial lineages** (Cangzhou, Guan county) as their own tradition.
- **Yimeng (Linyi) and Baiyangdian** revolutionary memory against **Beijing and Tianjin** official commemoration.
- **Mining districts** (Tangshan, Pingdingshan, Jiaozuo) and **Bohai fishing towns**: dangerous work.
- **Women's courage** (Mulan, Mu Guiying, Yimeng) against men's (Water Margin, wuxia).
- **Manchu and Mongol heritage** in north Hebei: older martial and valor titles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, martial arts and war memory; mixed for everyday moral courage; thin for minority traditions outside martial arts.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Japanese for war-period studies; Manchu for Qing titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official martyr narratives are protected and disputes over them carry legal risk; the Boxers, the 1942 famine and whistleblowing are sensitive; read martial-arts lineage claims critically.
- **Held for review:** none.
