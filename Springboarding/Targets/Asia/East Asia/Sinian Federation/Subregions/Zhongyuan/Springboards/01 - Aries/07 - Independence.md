## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Labor-exporting Henan counties** (Zhoukou, Zhumadian, Shangqiu, Xinyang) against **Beijing and Tianjin**, where those earners arrive.
- **Shandong coastal households** (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai) with denser welfare and jobs against **inland southwest Shandong** (Heze, Liaocheng).
- **Hebei villages around Beijing** commuting to the capital against **Bashang** herding and farming areas.
- **Hui communities** with mosque-centered support.
- **Older women** (often caregivers) against **young graduates**; urban **disabled** people against rural ones.
- **Korean residents** in Qingdao, often expatriate households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on migration, elder support and welfare; mixed on attitudes to asking for help; thin on minority self-provisioning.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese; English for much of the welfare scholarship; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** welfare and poverty data are politically framed; migrant and left-behind statistics vary by source; avoid treating labor-exporting provinces as one voice. Secession is not a live theme for these lands; any political-independence material is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
