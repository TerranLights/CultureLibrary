# Zhongyuan: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and classical Chinese (including medical classics); northern colloquial; Korean; Manchu and Mongolian; Hui Islamic usage. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin street speech** (two separate urban registers to study apart) against **village quarrels** in Henan and Hebei.
- **Shandong lineage villages** (disputes framed by kinship, to check) against **new urban estates** (disputes between strangers).
- **Women and men:** anger and domestic violence, and who may show anger to in-laws.
- **Hui communities** (religious teaching on patience); **Korean residents** (hwabyeong as a named syndrome).
- **Migrant workers** in Beijing (anger at wage arrears) against **civil servants and state-firm staff**.
- **Older people** remembering campaigns of mobilized anger against **younger people** online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in medicine, opera and literature; mixed for psychology with regional samples; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Korean; Arabic for Hui sermon terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest and collective anger (including 1989 in Beijing and later nationalist protests) are highly sensitive; mobilization campaigns, medical violence and domestic violence need care. Justice and revenge go to Libra and Scorpio; law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
