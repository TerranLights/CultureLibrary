## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-heavy provinces** (Henan, Shandong, Hebei) against **Beijing and Tianjin**, where quotas and schooling differ.
- **County exam towns** (Hengshui) against **urban key schools**.
- **Craft-contest towns** (Weifang kites, Wuqiao acrobats, Ningyang crickets) as local identities built on contest.
- **Rural families** in bride-price rivalry against **urban families** in school rivalry.
- **Mongol and Manchu heritage** in north Hebei: Naadam-style games and archery revival.
- **Korean residents**: school rivalry in a second exam system.
- **Women and men** in sport, exams and work; **older and younger** generations on 内卷.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, sport and media; mixed for envy and everyday rivalry; thin for minority contests.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Mongolian and Manchu for heritage terms; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exam quotas and regional fairness are contentious; never state a province's rivalry level from reputation; gambling on contests is illegal (law goes to Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
