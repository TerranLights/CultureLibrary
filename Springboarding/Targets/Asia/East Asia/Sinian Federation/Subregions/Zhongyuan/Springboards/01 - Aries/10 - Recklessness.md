## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in county towns** (e-bikes, drinking, fights) against **urban youth** (stunts online, extreme sports).
- **Hui communities**, where alcohol is religiously barred: risk in other domains to separate.
- **Banquet hosts and guests** across Shandong, Hebei and Henan: drinking customs vary by county and occupation; compare, do not generalize.
- **Beijing outdoor clubs** against **rural mountain villages** that rescue them.
- **Small investors** (older urban women, retirees) against **young crypto traders**.
- **Industrial workers** in chemical and port zones (Tianjin Binhai, Cangzhou) where others' recklessness is the risk.
- **Korean residents** and **Mongol** heritage areas as separate cases.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, health and accident data; mixed for attitudes; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese, including internet slang; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Tianjin explosion, financial collapses (including the 2022 Henan rural bank case) and disaster accountability are sensitive; drunk driving, gambling and fireworks rules are law matters for Libra. 鲁莽 shares a character with Shandong's short name (鲁): treat any pun as a stereotype to avoid.
- **Held for review:** none.
