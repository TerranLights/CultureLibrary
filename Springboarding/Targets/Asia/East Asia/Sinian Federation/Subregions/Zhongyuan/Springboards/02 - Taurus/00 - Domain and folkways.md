# Zhongyuan: The domain, possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (simplified; traditional for older print); Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan Mandarin, Jin in northern Henan and northwest Hebei; Manchu and Mongolian (heritage); Hui religious vocabulary; Korean. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held: possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth, and the food, dress and wealth ways around them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing households against newer residents:** banner-descended and courtyard families against migrants and professionals; to test whether what is kept differs.
- **Tianjin:** a former treaty port with concessions and merchant families; a commercial counterpart to Beijing's court city.
- **Hebei (Yan-Zhao):** the plains around Baoding and Shijiazhuang, Tangshan's industry, Xiong'an's new town; plus Chengde's Manchu counties and Weichang's Manchu-Mongol county.
- **Shandong:** coastal Jiaodong (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai) against inland Lu around Jinan and Qufu, and the Yimeng hills; sea trade against farming.
- **Henan:** the Yellow River plain, Luoyang and Kaifeng's heritage, and the southern and eastern counties that send many workers away.
- **Hui Muslims** in all five lands; **Manchu** and **Mongol** communities in Hebei; **Korean** residents; **Hebei Catholic villages**; **Henan Protestants**; rural against urban **household registration (户口)**; the **diaspora** (Shandong settlers in the Northeast; Shandong-origin Chinese in Korea).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing and the provincial capitals; mixed for rural counties; thin in minority languages. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet uses the tracker's working list.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Japanese (Mantetsu); some Manchu and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land requisition and demolition (拆迁); frozen rural bank deposits in Henan (2022); prejudice against people from Henan as an object of study, never a stance; church property; survey microdata needs registration.
