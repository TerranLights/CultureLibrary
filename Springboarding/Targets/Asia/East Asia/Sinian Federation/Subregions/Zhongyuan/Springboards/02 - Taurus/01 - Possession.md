# Zhongyuan: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, with Beijing and Tianjin colloquial forms and the Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan dialects; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing courtyard families and banner descendants:** objects carried from the Qing city; how much they keep after demolition and moving.
- **Tianjin:** concession-era homes and the secondhand trade in foreign goods; a port's different flow of objects.
- **Hebei villages and Xiong'an:** households moved off their land, and what they took.
- **Shandong coast and interior:** fishing households' gear against inland farm tools and dowry chests.
- **Henan:** families of long-absent migrant workers; what is left at home and what travels.
- **Hui:** Islamic burial customs, set beside Han grave goods and paper offerings.
- **Manchu and Mongol households in northern Hebei:** clan objects held in common.
- **Korean residents and Christians of Hebei and Henan:** devotional objects and keepsakes from elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite and archival households and for urban surveys; mixed for village life after 1980; thin for minority households.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Manchu and Korean for small parts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** confiscation of goods in campaigns (land reform, the Cultural Revolution's house searches) is still touchy; demolition losses; temple and church objects seized and returned.
- **Held for review:** none.
