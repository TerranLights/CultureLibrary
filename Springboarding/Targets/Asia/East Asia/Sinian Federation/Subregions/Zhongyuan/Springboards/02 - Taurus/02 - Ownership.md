# Zhongyuan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with the region's colloquial forms); classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui religious usage. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing title holders against renters without local registration:** whether the 房本 marks belonging in the city.
- **Old courtyard owners and their heirs:** restitution, shared courtyards (大杂院) and split claims to one house.
- **Edge-of-city villagers around Beijing and Tianjin:** house plots, "small-title" housing and demolition compensation.
- **Tianjin's former concession districts:** foreign-held land, then state, then private flats.
- **Hebei plains and Xiong'an:** farmland transfer; a new town where property dealing was frozen *(check)*.
- **Shandong coast:** sea-area and pond rights in fishing and aquaculture villages against inland grain farmers.
- **Henan:** Yellow River floodplain (滩区) households moved by the state; collective villages such as Nanjie.
- **Hui mosque communities; Manchu and Mongol counties of northern Hebei** (pasture contracting in Weichang); **Catholic parishes of Hebei** (church land).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history (Mantetsu-based studies, deeds, archives) and for national surveys; mixed for present attitudes at the province level.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Japanese for the Mantetsu material behind Huang and Duara.
- **Law:** what property law says goes to Libra; legal culture and obedience to Capricorn. This sheet covers lived claims only.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced demolition, land seizure and petitioning; church property; the federation's ownership of land is a political subject.
- **Held for review:** none.
