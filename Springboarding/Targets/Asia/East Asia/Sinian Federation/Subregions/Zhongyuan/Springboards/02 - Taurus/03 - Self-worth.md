# Zhongyuan: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and the Beijing, Tianjin, Shandong and Henan colloquial registers; classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Born Beijingers against "Beijing drifters" (北漂):** whether local registration and accent carry worth.
- **Tianjin's crosstalk audiences and neighborhoods:** check how teasing (损) is taken, as a bond or as a wound, and by whom.
- **Hebei's exam belt and the commuter ring around Beijing (环京):** worth by score and by distance from the capital.
- **Shandong:** the pull of civil-service posts (考公, 编制) as proof of a son's worth, to check; coast against interior.
- **People from Henan living elsewhere in the federation:** how they answer slurs, against how home-county people speak of their worth.
- **Men expected to provide a house and bride price** in rural Hebei, Shandong and Henan, against women's routes to worth.
- **Hui** of Niujie and Henan; **Manchu** banner descendants; **left-behind elders**; **rural women**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for face scholarship and survey data; mixed for minority voices; thin for men's own accounts of low worth.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, classical Chinese; Manchu for banner sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional prejudice (study it, never repeat it); suicide; migrant workers' organizing; the Cultural Revolution's public humiliations.
- **Held for review:** none.
