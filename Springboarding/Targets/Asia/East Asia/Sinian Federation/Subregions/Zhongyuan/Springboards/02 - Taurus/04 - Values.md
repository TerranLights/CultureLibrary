# Zhongyuan: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (official, colloquial and classical registers); Hui religious vocabulary; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qufu, Zou and the Confucian heritage zone:** values staged as heritage and tourism, beside villagers' own talk.
- **Beijing's political center against its old hutong neighborhoods:** official slogans and the 老理儿 of older residents.
- **Tianjin's merchant and port families:** commerce and trust beside court-centered values.
- **Hebei:** the Yan-Zhao self-image and its martial towns (Cangzhou) against Xiong'an's planned city.
- **Henan:** model-official counties; rural Protestant congregations; women's mosques.
- **Hui, Catholic, Buddhist and Daoist communities;** Manchu banner descendants; Korean residents.
- **Generations** (those raised before and after 1978, and the post-2000 cohort); Party members and non-members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for texts and official campaigns; mixed for surveys at the province level; uneven for religious minorities.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Arabic and Persian loans for Hui teaching.
- **Law:** none here; law-related values go to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveys on political values are restricted; house churches and unregistered Catholic communities; official exemplars should be read beside local voices.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Read 论语·里仁 and 墨子·节用 on ctext.org; list each one's ranking.
