# Zhongyuan: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and its northern colloquial forms; classical Chinese; Korean; Manchu; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin residents with full local insurance against migrants on thinner cover:** the gap registration makes.
- **Former state-factory families in Tianjin, Tangshan and Shijiazhuang:** the end of lifetime employment.
- **Hebei's flood-storage zones (蓄滞洪区), such as Zhuozhou in 2023:** land kept for deliberate flooding; whose security is traded for whose.
- **Shandong fishing towns:** the summer fishing ban and typhoon seasons against inland farm cycles.
- **Henan:** families with famine and flood memories; the floodplain; Zhengzhou's 2021 flood.
- **Left-behind elders without children nearby;** rural pensioners against urban ones.
- **Hui mosque networks; Korean residents; Catholic and Protestant mutual aid.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine and relief history and for insurance statistics; mixed for present feelings of security by province.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Korean for one community.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine deaths of 1959-1961 (Henan's Xinyang case) are politically sensitive; the 2021 flood toll and the 2022 bank freeze; public-security surveillance as a form of "security".
- **Held for review:** none.
