*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (standard, colloquial, classical); Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing families who stayed through demolition against those moved to outer districts:** what continuity survives relocation.
- **Tianjin's long-resident port families against newer residents of Binhai:** an old city beside a built-from-scratch one.
- **Hebei villages around Xiong'an and businesses moved out of Beijing:** stability lost by planning decisions.
- **Shandong's settled inland lineages against families with relatives in the Northeast and Korea:** staying and leaving inside one kin group.
- **Henan's migrant-sending counties:** households split between the home village and distant work cities; what stays fixed while members are away.
- **Banner descendants; Hui of Niujie and Henan; Korean residents;** couples on either side of the divorce figures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lineages, migration history and official statistics; mixed for how people feel about change at the county level.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Japanese for Manchukuo-era migration records, if followed.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 维稳 and petitioning; forced relocation for dams, floods and new towns; demolition.
- **Held for review:** none.
