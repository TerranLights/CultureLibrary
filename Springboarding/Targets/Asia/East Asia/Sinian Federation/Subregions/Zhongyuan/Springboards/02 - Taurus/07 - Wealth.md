## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's old courtyard families, new tech and finance money, and outer-district villagers:** three kinds of wealth in one city.
- **Tianjin's merchant memory against the port-industrial Binhai economy.**
- **Hebei:** steel fortunes of Tangshan and Handan against the poor hill counties of the Taihang.
- **Shandong:** coastal Qingdao and Yantai against the southwest (Heze, Linyi).
- **Henan:** Zhengzhou's food and logistics firms against out-migrant villages, where bride-price levels are to be checked.
- **Hui traders** (Sangpo, Niujie, Cangzhou); **Manchu** families of the vanished banner system; **Korean** business residents.
- **Party-linked against private wealth**, to be handled with care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys, rich lists and history; mixed for how ordinary people talk about the rich.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** officials' and Party-linked wealth; anti-corruption cases; land-reform violence against landlords; "common prosperity" debates.
- **Held for review:** none.
