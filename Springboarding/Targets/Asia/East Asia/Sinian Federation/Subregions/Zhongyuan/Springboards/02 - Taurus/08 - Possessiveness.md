# Zhongyuan: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, including online slang and the Shandong vernacular of older fiction; classical Chinese; Korean, Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Beijing and Tianjin couples:** phone and location checking as a new form of watching.
- **Rural Shandong and Henan households:** mother, son and daughter-in-law under one roof; check who claims the son.
- **Parents at Beijing's marriage corners:** claims over grown children.
- **Martial-arts and craft lineages of Henan and Hebei:** guarded knowledge, opened or kept.
- **Young fans in the capital's entertainment economy:** possessive devotion to stars.
- **Hui families:** marriage within the community, asked as a question of how families explain it, never assumed.
- **Korean residents** and **Mongol households of Weichang:** jealousy words and sharing customs in other languages.
- **Generations:** whether jealousy is read as love or control, by age.

## 4. Flags
