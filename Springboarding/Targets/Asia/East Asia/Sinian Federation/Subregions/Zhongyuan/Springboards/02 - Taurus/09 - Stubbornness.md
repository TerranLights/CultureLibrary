## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** petitioners from other provinces who come to the capital, against Beijing natives who meet them.
- **Tianjin and Hebei mining and steel towns:** long labor standoffs and their memory.
- **Henan:** home ground claimed for the Foolish Old Man; check whether people there use the tale with pride or with irony.
- **Shandong:** the Wu Xun story's home ground; the Boxers' Shandong beginnings and the Red Spear societies of Shandong and Henan as cases of collective refusal *(check)*.
- **Hebei's Catholic villages:** communities that kept a faith through bans.
- **Hui villages of Cangzhou and Xian county:** remembered resistance.
- **Elders labeled 老顽固 against young 杠精:** stubbornness by generation.
- **Korean** and **Mongol** residents: their own words and stories for holding firm.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in idiom, legend and drama; thin in measured data at the province level.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Yuan drama language.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning, nail houses, unregistered churches and strikes are politically sensitive; read official praise of tenacity beside the cases it leaves out.
- **Held for review:** none.
