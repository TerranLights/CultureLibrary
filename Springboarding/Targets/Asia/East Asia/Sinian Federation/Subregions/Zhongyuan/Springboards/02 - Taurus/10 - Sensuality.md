# Zhongyuan: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and the Henan, Shandong and Beijing dialect words for comfort and taste; classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui food vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure. (Sex itself goes to Scorpio.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing hobbyists** (birds, crickets, pigeons, tea) **against younger urban spa and café goers:** two kinds of leisure in one city.
- **Tianjin:** breakfast stalls and evening comic theaters as sensory routines to compare with Beijing's.
- **Hebei:** the Chengde mountains and Bashang grasslands; check where Mongol, Manchu and Han foodways meet.
- **Shandong:** coastal seafood and beer against inland pancakes and scallions.
- **Henan:** noodles, soups and night markets; check how taste is tied to home.
- **Hui:** halal rules on what the body may take in; festival foods.
- **Monastics, abstainers and older square dancers:** pleasure bounded, and pleasure claimed in public.
- **Korean** and **Mongol** residents: other cuisines, baths and music.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food culture and memoir; mixed for touch and personal space; thin in time-use data at the province level.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin with dialect forms; classical Chinese for the Zheng and Wei songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "sensual" overlaps with Scorpio's sex and with vice campaigns (bathhouses and massage parlors); alcohol is restricted in Hui practice.
- **Held for review:** none.
