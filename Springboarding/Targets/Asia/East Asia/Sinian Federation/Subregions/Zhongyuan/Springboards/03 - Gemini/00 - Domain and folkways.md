# Zhongyuan: The domain: communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (普通话; traditional script for classical texts); Beijing, Tianjin, Jilu, Jiao-Liao and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Jin (northwest Hebei, north Henan); Hui religious vocabulary; Manchu and Mongolian (heritage); Korean; Chinese Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** how people talk, learn and connect close to home: speech ways, learning ways, neighbors and short journeys.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing old city against its outer districts:** hutong speech and courtyard life against rural districts and new estates.
- **Tianjin:** its own dialect island and comic-performance tradition, distinct from surrounding Hebei.
- **Hebei, south and north:** Jilu Mandarin plains against the Jin-speaking northwest (Zhangjiakou) and the Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties around Chengde.
- **Shandong, west against Jiaodong peninsula:** Jilu against Jiao-Liao speech; the Confucian heartland (Qufu, Zoucheng) as its own case.
- **Henan, north of the Yellow River against the central and southern plains:** Jin pockets against Zhongyuan Mandarin.
- **Hui communities:** Beijing Niujie, Cangzhou (Mengcun), Kaifeng and Zhoukou mosques and their schools.
- **Manchu and Mongol heritage communities** (Chengde area; Beijing banner descendants).
- **Internal migrants** in Beijing and Tianjin, **Korean communities** in Beijing, and **Deaf signers**.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing and for classical learning; mixed for rural Henan and Hebei counties; thin for Manchu and Mongol heritage speech.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); dialect literacy; Japanese for the Mantetsu surveys; Manchu for Qing archives.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled; write for the five listed units until the author confirms them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional prejudice and dialect mockery (never reproduce the stereotypes); minority residents in Beijing; censorship shapes what talk is recorded.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Language Resources Protection Project platform for one county in each of the five units, starting with Beijing Dongcheng, Tianjin Heping, Zhangjiakou, Yantai and Kaifeng.
2. Pull the 2020 census provincial yearbooks for literacy, schooling and ethnicity in the five units.
3. Read Fei Xiaotong's chapters on literacy and association, then Gamble on Ding County and Yang on Taitou.
4. Search CNKI for 北京话 社会语言学, 天津方言, 胶辽官话 and 中原官话 for the dialect sociolinguistics of each area.
5. Search 地方志 风俗 with each prefecture name (for example 保定 地方志 风俗) for county-level customs of visiting and speech.
