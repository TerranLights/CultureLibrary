## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing old-city families against new arrivals:** check whether address forms and banter differ by length of residence.
- **Tianjin speakers:** a center of staged comic dialogue; compare everyday and performed speech.
- **Shandong banquets** (seat order, toasting speech) against **Henan village chats** at the gate: two settings with set speaking orders.
- **Jin-speaking northwest Hebei and north Henan:** check whether speakers switch to Putonghua with outsiders.
- **Hui congregations,** men's and women's mosques: sermon language and Arabic loans.
- **Deaf signers** in the five units: Beijing against Shandong signing.
- **Officials and petitioners:** official register against plain speech (handle with care).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing speech and classical rhetoric; mixed for rural Henan and Hebei; thin for Deaf and Hui women's speech outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and classical Chinese; dialect reading; English for older outsider sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning (信访) and complaint channels touch on politics; speech online is filtered, so online corpora are shaped by deletion.
- **Held for review:** none.
