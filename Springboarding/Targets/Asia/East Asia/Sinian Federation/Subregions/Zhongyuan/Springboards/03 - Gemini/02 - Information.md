# Zhongyuan: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); local dialects for broadcast and folk sources; Manchu (Qing archives); Japanese (occupation-era reports); Korean (Beijing community media). **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how people gather, pass on, trust, withhold and use it: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's central-institution workers against Beijing's outer districts:** proximity to official information may change what people trust; test it.
- **Rural Henan and Hebei short-video users** (Kuaishou, Douyin) against **city newspaper readers:** different channels, different checks.
- **Older residents:** how they vet claims, given press reports of health-product and investment scams aimed at them.
- **Shandong coastal cities** (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai) with ties to Korea and Japan against the inland west.
- **Hui communities:** mosque announcements and Hui media as a parallel channel.
- **Migrant workers** in Beijing and Tianjin: news from home through village WeChat groups.
- **Manchu heritage communities** (Chengde): records held in Manchu and who can read them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, press history and archives; mixed for rural media habits; thin for trust and rumor at county level.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Manchu for Qing archives; Japanese for occupation-era survey material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship, the dossier system, internal-reference reports and petition records are politically sensitive; some archives are closed. Privacy-law questions belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 谣言传播 农村 河南 and 信息获取 老年人 河北 for local studies of rumor and older people's sources.
