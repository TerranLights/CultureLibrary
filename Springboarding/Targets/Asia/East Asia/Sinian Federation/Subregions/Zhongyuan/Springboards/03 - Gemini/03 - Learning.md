## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's top school districts against its outer districts and migrant families:** compare access to tutoring and selective schools.
- **Hebei county high schools** on the Hengshui model against Tianjin's city schools.
- **Shandong's Confucian heartland** (Qufu, Zoucheng) against coastal Qingdao and Yantai, where heritage education and trade-oriented learning may differ.
- **Henan villages with many left-behind children:** grandparents as teachers.
- **Hui families:** scripture-hall students and women's Quranic classes alongside state school.
- **Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties** around Chengde: heritage-language classes, if any.
- **Apprentice trades:** quyi performers, chefs, martial-arts students.
- **Deaf pupils:** sign-based against oral teaching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, Beijing schooling and the classical tradition; mixed for village family learning; thin for Manchu and Mongol heritage schooling.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; some Arabic and Persian terms for Hui learning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant-school closures, minority-language schooling and the Wu Xun campaign are political subjects; exam-pressure deaths are a sensitive press topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
