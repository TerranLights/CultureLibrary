# Zhongyuan: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Beijing, Tianjin, Shandong and Henan dialect words for prying and marveling; Korean; French and English for early museum and fossil-hunting records. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haidian's research-campus households** against **rural Hebei** families: compare how children's questions are answered when science institutions are next door.
- **Henan's archaeology-rich districts** (Luoyang, Anyang, Kaifeng): public archaeology alongside illicit digging.
- **Tianjin's former concession districts:** museums and foreign collecting as a local legacy.
- **Shandong** in two settings: the classical heartland (Qufu) and the Jiaodong ports.
- **Hui and Manchu residents:** the questions outsiders ask them about food, dress and faith, and how they answer.
- **Generations at Spring Festival:** elders' questions about marriage, salary and children, and the young's reaction.
- **Girls and boys** in school science.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history, museums and popular science; mixed for everyday nosiness; thin for county-level measures beyond the science-literacy survey.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; French for Licent's museum; English for Zhoukoudian history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tomb robbing and antiquities trafficking; some topics closed to public inquiry; onlooker videos of accidents and violence.
- **Held for review:** none.
