# Zhongyuan: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Beijing and Tianjin colloquial; Shandong and Henan dialect usage; Hui community vocabulary; Korean (Beijing's Korean communities and Shandong-origin Chinese in Korea). **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing work-unit compounds and their successors:** test whether ties weakened as compounds gave way to commercial estates.
- **Tianjin job seekers:** strong ties against institutional channels, following Bian's design.
- **Shandong villages with Manchuria and Korea migration histories:** long-distance ties.
- **Henan sending counties** (Gushi, Zhoukou): migrant chains to Beijing and the south; left-behind households.
- **Hui congregations** (Niujie, Cangzhou, Kaifeng): mosque-centered networks and marriage ties between Hui villages.
- **Manchu banner descendants** in Beijing and Chengde: ties based on banner origin, if any remain.
- **Older people living alone** in city and village.
- **Korean residents** of Beijing's Wangjing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for guanxi, native-place ties and migration; mixed for loneliness; thin for Manchu and Mongol networks.
- **Languages needed:** Standard Chinese; Korean for the Korea-based diaspora; English for much guanxi scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and nepotism are political topics; root-seeking events carry state messaging; genealogy access depends on lineages.
- **Held for review:** none.
