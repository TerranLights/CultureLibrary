*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); local kin terms in Beijing, Shandong and Henan dialects; Manchu and Mongolian kin terms (heritage); Korean. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin one-child cohorts** against **rural Henan and Shandong** families of the same age: different sibling worlds at the same date.
- **Hui families,** under birth rules that differed from Han rules in some places (check which), and with Islamic inheritance norms in view.
- **Manchu and Mongol families** in the Chengde counties.
- **Jiaodong peninsula** against **southwest Shandong**: test whether household-division customs differ.
- **Daughters-only households** and sisters who support brothers.
- **Migrant siblings:** chain migration from Henan and Hebei villages to Beijing.
- **Twins** and the families recruited by the Qingdao registry.
- **Sworn and lineage "siblings":** martial schools, stage-art lineages.

## 4. Flags
