# Zhongyuan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Beijing, Shandong and Henan colloquial; Hui religious vocabulary; Korean. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing residents' committees and property managers** against **village elders** in Henan and Hebei: compare which third parties are trusted.
- **Tianjin's television mediation** against face-to-face practice.
- **Shandong lineage villages** (strong genealogies) against villages without strong lineages.
- **Hui communities:** imams and mosque committees as go-betweens.
- **Hebei and Shandong Catholic villages:** priests and the Virgin as intercessors.
- **Mount Tai and Henan temple pilgrims:** divine intercession.
- **Migrant renters** dealing with agents in Beijing.
- **Parents brokering adult children's marriages** across classes and regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, village history and urban neighborhood governance; mixed for present-day informal mediation; thin for Manchu and Mongol practice.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official "people's mediation" and petition handling are statutory and political: their legal side goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); Hui-Han disputes are a sensitive topic.
- **Held for review:** none.
