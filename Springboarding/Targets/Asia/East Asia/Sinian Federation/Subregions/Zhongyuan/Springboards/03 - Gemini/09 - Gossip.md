# Zhongyuan: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan), region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); northern colloquial (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan); Hui religious vocabulary; Korean. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing hutong residents against new high-rise estates:** test whether neighbors who cannot see each other still talk about each other.
- **Henan and Hebei villages:** the village entrance (村口) as the watching post; who sits there, men or women, and when.
- **Tianjin and Shandong men's banquet talk:** gossip that is not called gossip.
- **Hui congregations:** backbiting as a religious fault, and talk about who keeps or slips from practice.
- **Online fan groups** (饭圈) in Beijing's entertainment world.
- **Migrants and Henan people** as targets of group rumor.
- **Teenagers in boarding schools** (Hebei's large county schools): rumor and cyberbullying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical sayings, fiction and online scandal; mixed for village talk; thin for Manchu, Mongol and Korean community gossip.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; northern dialect fluency for village talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "spreading rumors" (造谣) is also a criminal and political charge; its legal side goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Informant networks and regional stereotypes are sensitive; never repeat slurs as findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
