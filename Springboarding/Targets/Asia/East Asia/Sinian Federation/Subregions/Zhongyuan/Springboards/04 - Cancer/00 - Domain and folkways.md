# Zhongyuan: Home and family, domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands carried: Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified characters; Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Jin dialects in parts of Henan and Hebei); Manchu; Mongolian; Korean; Hui Islamic vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the domain question: what is home, and who is family? Household forms, roots, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-city Beijingers (bannermen descendants included) against post-1980s newcomers:** household form may track arrival date.
- **Tianjin:** a treaty-port, merchant city with its own house forms.
- **Hebei's central plain, Jidong (Tangshan area) and the northern mountain counties** (Zhangjiakou, Chengde): plains villages against Jin-speaking and Manchu or Mongol districts.
- **Shandong: the Jiaodong peninsula** (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai; Jiaoliao Mandarin, sea migration), **the Confucian southwest** (Qufu, Jining, Heze) **and the Yimeng hills** (Linyi).
- **Henan: west (Luoyang, loess caves), east (Kaifeng, Shangqiu), north (Anyang, Jiaozuo, Jin-speaking pockets) and the south** (Nanyang, Xinyang, near the Huai and closer to central China).
- **Hui communities** (Beijing's Niujie, Hebei's Mengcun and Dachang counties, Kaifeng, Zhengzhou): a separate home-and-family frame.
- **Manchu and Mongol households** in Hebei's autonomous counties; **Korean-Chinese** households in Beijing and Qingdao.
- **Christian households** (Hebei Catholic villages; Henan Protestants) and **split migrant households** between village and city.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han households (census, surveys, gazetteers); thinner for Manchu, Mongol and Korean-Chinese ones.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin (simplified and traditional characters, classical Chinese for pre-modern texts); Japanese for the 1940s village surveys; Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources; dialect knowledge for kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** household registration and family-planning history are politically sensitive; some archives restrict foreigners. Registration rules and family law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Scope:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled; the sheets of this sign are written for the five units listed.
- **Held for review:** none.
