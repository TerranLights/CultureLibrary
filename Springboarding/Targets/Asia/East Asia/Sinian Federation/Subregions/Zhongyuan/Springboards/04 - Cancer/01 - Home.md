# Zhongyuan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and its northern dialects; Mongolian, Korean and Hui usage for minority homes. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hutong residents moved to outer ring roads, and those who stayed:** the same city, two kinds of home loss or keeping.
- **Beijing and Tianjin renters (北漂, 津漂) against registered owners:** home without the paperwork.
- **Commuter towns in Hebei around Beijing** (Yanjiao, Gu'an): the home bought where one does not work.
- **Jiaodong coastal villages** (seaweed-thatch houses, families with relatives in the Northeast) against **inland Shandong** villages.
- **West Henan loess households** in caves and sunken courtyards against **east Henan plains** brick houses.
- **Yellow River floodplain (滩区) households** in Henan and Shandong, resettled from flood land: home as something the river and the state both move.
- **Hui families** whose home is set around a mosque district; **Mongols in Weichang** with a pastoral idea of home land; **Korean-Chinese** in Wangjing (Beijing) and Qingdao.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing; mixed for home as feeling (memoir, fiction, oral history needed); thin for minority homes.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Song-era texts; Mongolian and Korean for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition, relocation and village merging are contested topics; residents' complaints may be censored online. Property rights and residence registration as rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
