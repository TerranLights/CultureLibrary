# Zhongyuan: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin with its northern kin vocabularies; Manchu and Mongolian for clan terms; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Kong lineage of Qufu and other well-organized Shandong lineages** against **ordinary North China villages** with thinner lineage institutions: the range of "family" on paper.
- **Jiaodong families with branches in the Northeast** and abroad (Korea): family held together across distance.
- **Urban one-child-generation families in Beijing and Tianjin** against **Henan and Hebei villages** with sibling sets: different kin webs.
- **Manchu families** with clan names and banner pasts; **Mongol families** in Weichang.
- **Hui families**, where kin and faith community overlap.
- **Catholic families in Hebei** whose godparenthood and marriage rules come from the Church.
- **Families with uxorilocal sons-in-law** and **families built by adoption**: where blood is not the line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han lineage and household history; rich survey data; thinner and more scattered for Manchu, Mongol and Hui kin.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for genealogies and family instructions; Manchu for clan records; Japanese for some wartime surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-planning history and son preference are sensitive; genealogies may hold personal data of living people. Marriage, adoption and inheritance as rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
