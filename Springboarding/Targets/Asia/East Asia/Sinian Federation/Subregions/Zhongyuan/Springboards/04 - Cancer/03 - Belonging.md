# Zhongyuan: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and local dialects (dialect itself marks insiders); Hui Islamic vocabulary; Manchu; Korean. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijingers against non-registered residents:** belonging by accent and history against belonging by papers.
- **Tianjin natives**, with their own dialect and humor, against Beijing: two adjacent cities with separate insider codes.
- **Henan migrants** in other cities: whether and where they are labeled outsiders is a question to test.
- **Hui neighborhoods** (Niujie; Hui villages near Beijing; Kaifeng; Cangzhou) where belonging is set by the mosque congregation.
- **Manchu descendants** in Beijing and in Hebei's Manchu counties: banner belonging and its afterlife.
- **Jiaodong families with kin in Korea and the Northeast:** belonging stretched across borders.
- **Kaifeng's Jewish descendants** and other very small groups, whose recognition is unsettled.
- **Rural villagers** in Hebei and Shandong, where belonging follows surname and lane.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migrants and native-place history; mixed for felt belonging in villages; thin and sensitive for the smallest groups.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, with dialect knowledge; Korean for the diaspora in Korea; Manchu for banner-era records; some Arabic and Persian loanwords for Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic and religious identity of Hui, Manchu and Kaifeng Jewish communities is politically sensitive; regional discrimination is a live grievance. Residence registration rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
