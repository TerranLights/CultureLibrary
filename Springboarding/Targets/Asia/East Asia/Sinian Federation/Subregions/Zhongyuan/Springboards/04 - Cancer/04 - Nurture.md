*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern dialect words for children and child-minding; Korean; Hui religious-education vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's Haidian district families** with tutoring-heavy routines against **Hebei county-town families** sending teenagers to exam boarding schools: two ways of handing nurture to schooling.
- **Rural Henan and Hebei grandparents** raising children whose parents are away: nurture shifted a generation up.
- **Qufu and Zoucheng heritage-education programs** (classics classes for children): whether old texts shape present nurture.
- **Hui families** with mosque schooling; **Korean-Chinese families** with bilingual schooling.
- **Manchu families** in Hebei's autonomous counties, if language classes for children exist there (*check*).
- **Christian households** in Henan and Hebei, where Sunday schooling of children is a sensitive matter.
- **Tianjin and Qingdao port-city families** against the inland plain: commercial cities with their own child-raising media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schooling and feeding surveys and for intensive parenting; mixed for village grandparents' methods; thin for Manchu and Korean-Chinese nurture.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for 《礼记》 and Qing fiction; Korean; dialect vocabulary for local forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious education of children (Hui and Christian) is politically sensitive; tutoring curbs are a state policy topic. Discipline and parents' authority are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
