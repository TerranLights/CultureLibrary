# Zhongyuan: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with classical Chinese for famine and relief records); Korean; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Henan famine and flood districts** (the 1938 floodplain, Zhumadian, Weihui): family memory of fleeing and being taken in.
- **Hebei flood-storage counties** (Zhuozhou, Bazhou and others): sheltering as a cost borne for neighbors.
- **Tangshan and Beijing households** with earthquake-shack memories.
- **Jiaodong families** whose forebears fled hunger to kin in the Northeast: shelter found through relatives far away.
- **Beijing and Tianjin migrant tenants** in basements and urban villages; **people sleeping in public spaces.**
- **Women fleeing violence** in villages against cities: whether any shelter exists nearby.
- **Hebei Catholic communities**, whose memory includes the church as refuge; **Hui communities**, if mosques lodge travelers (*check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine, flood and relief history; mixed for official homelessness figures; thin for women's shelters and for street sleepers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Qing relief records; English and French for mission records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1942 famine, the 1975 dam failure, the 2017 clearances and flood diversion decisions are sensitive and partly censored. The former custody-and-repatriation system and asylum law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
