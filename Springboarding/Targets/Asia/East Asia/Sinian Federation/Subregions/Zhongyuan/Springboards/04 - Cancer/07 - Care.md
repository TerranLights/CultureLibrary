# Zhongyuan: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern colloquial care words; classical Chinese for the filial canon; Korean; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin pensioners** with care stations and hired help against **Henan and Hebei village elders** whose children work far away: two care systems.
- **Beijing retirees in Hebei elder homes** (towns near the capital, *check*): care bought across a provincial line.
- **Henan and Hebei women working as carers** in Beijing homes, whose own families are cared for by others.
- **Shandong families near Qufu and Zoucheng,** where filial-piety programs are promoted: whether local campaigns change care at home.
- **Hui families** with mosque-linked care; **Catholic families** served by church charities in Hebei.
- **Families caring for disabled members** in villages against cities.
- **Korean-Chinese families** in Qingdao and Beijing, with kin working in Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elder-care surveys; mixed for paid domestic care; thin for folk healing and for minority care practices.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Korean for Korean-Chinese sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious charities and spirit mediums operate under restriction; elder neglect and carers' burnout are under-reported. Provincial rules on caregiving leave go to Libra; filial obedience and authority go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
