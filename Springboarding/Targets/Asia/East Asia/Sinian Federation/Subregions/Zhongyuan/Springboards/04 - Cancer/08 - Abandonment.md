# Zhongyuan: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with the vocabulary of migration and welfare); classical Chinese for charity records; Korean; French and English for mission archives. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Henan and Hebei sending villages** with left-behind children, wives and elders, against **urban Beijing families** whose elders are left by children who moved abroad or across the city.
- **Tangshan families** marked by the 1976 earthquake's orphans and split families.
- **Henan villages hit by the blood-sale epidemic**, where whole generations of parents died.
- **Tianjin**, whose 1870 orphanage crisis is part of local memory.
- **Christian communities in Hebei and Henan** that ran or run orphanages; **Hui communities**, if orphan care falls to the mosque (*check*).
- **Former welfare-institute children**, including those adopted abroad who return to search for birth families.
- **Korean-Chinese children** with parents working in Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for left-behind populations and for Qing charity history; mixed for infant abandonment (official counts are sparse); thin and contested for the Henan epidemic.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for charity records; French and English for mission archives; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** infant abandonment under birth planning, the Henan blood-sale epidemic and bereaved-parent petitioning are politically sensitive and partly censored. The crime of abandonment and duties of support go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
