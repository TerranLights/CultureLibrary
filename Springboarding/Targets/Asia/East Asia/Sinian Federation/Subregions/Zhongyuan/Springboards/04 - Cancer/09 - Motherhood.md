*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (with northern rural forms of address); classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean mother words; Hui women's religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zoucheng and Qufu:** places that present a classical mother as heritage.
- **The Yimeng hills (Linyi):** a revolutionary maternal icon set beside village mothers' own accounts.
- **Huaiyang and other Henan pilgrimage sites:** motherhood sought through temple vows.
- **Beijing and Tianjin professional mothers** with postpartum centers and hired help, against **rural Hebei and Henan mothers** with mothers-in-law as helpers.
- **Hui women in Henan's women's mosques:** religious teaching as part of mothering.
- **Manchu families in Beijing and Hebei,** and **Mongol families in Weichang:** different kin roles around the mother.
- **Korean-Chinese mothers** in Qingdao and Beijing, some working abroad.
- **Mothers raising children alone**, whose registration and support may differ (*check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical models, birth statistics and online talk; mixed for pilgrimage practice; thin for minority mothers.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean for minority sources; Arabic and Persian loanwords for Hui women's religious life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth-planning history, single motherhood and women's religious leadership are politically sensitive. Birth policy and maternity rules go to Libra; a mother's authority goes to Capricorn.
