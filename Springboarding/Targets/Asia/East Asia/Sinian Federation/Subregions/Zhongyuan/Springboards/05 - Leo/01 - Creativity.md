*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for craft and literary theory; Manchu and Mongolian for craft words in the north; Korean. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's professional art world** (academies, districts, studios) against **craft villages and counties** across Hebei, Shandong and Henan: two different meanings of "creative".
- **Tianjin craft families** (clay figures, prints) against Beijing's institutions: inherited name against academic credential.
- **Hebei craft counties** (Quyang stone carving; Hengshui inside-painted bottles; Wuqiang prints): specialized county economies.
- **Shandong:** Weifang-Gaomi folk arts, Zibo's Boshan glass and ceramics, Qufu's scholarly arts.
- **Henan:** Yuzhou and Ruzhou ceramics, Luoyang's Tang tricolor reproduction workshops, Kaifeng's Song-revival crafts: copying an old style as skill.
- **Manchu counties of Chengde** and **Hui calligraphers and cooks:** creativity within an ethnic or religious tradition.
- **Migrant artists** in Beijing (北漂) against home-province makers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heritage crafts and Beijing's art scene; mixed for everyday improvising; thin for minority makers outside heritage lists.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Manchu for a few craft terms; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** contemporary art faces censorship and closures, so archives and exhibitions may be incomplete; who owns a craft name or design can be disputed (point to Taurus for ownership; any legal side belongs to Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
