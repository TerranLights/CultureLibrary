*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijingers** (capital pride, imperial heritage) against **Tianjiners** (port-city pride, a rivalry with Beijing).
- **Hebei:** a province around the capital, with its own Yan-Zhao self-image; compare Baoding, Shijiazhuang, Tangshan, Zhangjiakou and Chengde.
- **Shandong:** the Confucian heartland against Jiaodong's maritime cities and the Heze-Jining area with its martial self-image.
- **Henan:** "cradle of civilization" pride beside the experience of being looked down on elsewhere; Luoyang, Kaifeng and Anyang each claim a capital past.
- **Manchu communities** in Chengde and Beijing, and **Mongol communities** of Weichang and the Bashang: group pride as small peoples inside a Han member state.
- **Hui:** pride in faith, cuisine and martial-arts lines (Cangzhou, Mengcun).
- **Korean communities:** pride split between two homelands.
- **Diasporas:** Shandong descendants in the northeast and Korea; Henan migrants who hide or assert their origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state-promoted pride and heritage; mixed for personal pride and modesty norms; thin for minority pride outside official festivals.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian only for small heritage material; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national pride is politically charged and survey answers may be guarded; regional rivalries can slide into prejudice, so name variation, never a provincial character.
- **Held for review:** none.
