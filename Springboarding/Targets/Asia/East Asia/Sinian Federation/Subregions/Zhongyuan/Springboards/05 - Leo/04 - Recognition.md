# Zhongyuan: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for examination and plaque records; Manchu and Mongolian for small heritage material; Korean. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's institutions** (academies, ministries, state honors) against **county and village recognition** in Hebei, Shandong and Henan.
- **High-pressure exam provinces** (Henan, Hebei, Shandong) against Beijing and Tianjin, where university access differs.
- **Professions with old title cultures:** opera performers, craftsmen with master titles, teachers.
- **Women:** whose work at home and in fields is often unthanked; compare across rural and urban settings.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities** of northern Hebei: recognition as a people.
- **Hui communities:** recognition within the mosque (imams, benefactors) against state honors.
- **Migrant workers** in Beijing and Tianjin, whose labor is visible but seldom credited.
- **Korean communities:** foreign credentials and professional titles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, honors lists and opera stardom; mixed for workplace praise; thin for everyday thanks in families.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for steles and plaques; Manchu only for older banner records; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rehabilitations and the recognition of past wrongs are politically sensitive and records may be closed; ethnic classification is sensitive; any legal side of titles or apologies belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
