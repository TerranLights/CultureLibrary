# Zhongyuan: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, including online slang; Classical Chinese for older visual and textual records; Korean; Hui and Uyghur community terms where relevant. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's center** (heavily watched, highly visible) against **outer districts and villages-in-the-city**, where migrants live.
- **Tianjin and Qingdao:** port cities with public bathing, promenades and seafront display.
- **Rural Henan, Hebei and western Shandong:** left-behind elders and children; village weddings and fairs as times of being seen.
- **Women of different generations:** square dancers against young urban women online.
- **Hui Muslims:** visibility of dress, signage and mosques; **Uyghur and other Muslim residents of Beijing**, more closely watched.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities** of northern Hebei, made visible mainly at festivals.
- **Korean communities** in Beijing and Shandong: visible as foreigners or as citizens, depending on who is looking.
- **Migrant workers:** counted, or missed, in statistics and on screen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for online visibility and urban public space; mixed for elders and left-behind children; thin for minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, including fast-changing internet slang; Classical Chinese for older images and texts; Korean; Uyghur only if Beijing's Uyghur residents become a unit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and the treatment of Muslim minorities are highly sensitive; informants and online posters must be protected; some sources may be blocked or deleted.
- **Held for review:** none.
