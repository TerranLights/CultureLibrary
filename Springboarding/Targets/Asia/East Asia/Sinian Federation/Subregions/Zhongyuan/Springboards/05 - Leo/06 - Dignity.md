# Zhongyuan: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; Korean; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing courtesy** (您, 讲究) against **the speech of the city's service economy:** who is addressed with respect.
- **Migrant workers and domestic workers** from Henan, Hebei and Shandong villages, working in Beijing and Tianjin: how they are addressed and housed.
- **Rural Henan:** regional prejudice, past epidemics and burial reform as three different tests of dignity.
- **Shandong villages:** an older literature on respect and decorum to compare with present life.
- **Hebei towns around the capital:** dignity of commuters and relocated villagers.
- **Hui Muslims:** dignity tied to halal purity and to how outsiders speak of the faith.
- **Elders:** urban empty-nesters against rural elders with migrant children.
- **Disabled people,** whose own groups frame dignity differently from official bodies.
- **Korean communities:** dignity and face in a second language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Strong literature on migrant labor and elder care; thin for everyday courtesy and for minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Henan blood-selling epidemic, burial campaigns, ethnic insult and migrant activism are sensitive; Picun's center has faced closures; protect informants. Rights claims as law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
