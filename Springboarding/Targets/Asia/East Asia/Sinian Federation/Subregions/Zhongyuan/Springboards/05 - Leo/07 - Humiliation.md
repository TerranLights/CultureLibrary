# Zhongyuan: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; Korean; English and Japanese for foreign records of the treaty-port and war years. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** imperial defeat sites, 1966 memories and school bullying in one city.
- **Tianjin:** a former concession city; humiliation turned into tourism.
- **Shandong:** foreign leaseholds (Qingdao, Weihai) and the Jinan incident; the *Water Margin* country around Liangshan.
- **Hebei:** Chengde's imperial rites; villages relocated for new cities and reservoirs.
- **Henan:** famine memory and regional jokes aimed at Henanese.
- **Men and women,** village and city: what counts as humiliation for each (the married-in son-in-law; a woman's reputation).
- **Hui Muslims:** insults to faith and food as a distinct kind of humiliation.
- **Manchu descendants:** blamed in some narratives for the dynasty's defeats.
- **Korean communities,** where the wartime past can make insult cut two ways; **migrant workers** in the cities, mocked for rural speech and dress.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national humiliation and for online shaming; mixed for school bullying; thin for private family humiliations.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; English and Japanese for treaty-port and wartime records; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution records, famine histories and ethnic insult are sensitive and partly blocked; national-humiliation material is official messaging and must be read beside private accounts; no claims about national character. Punishment as law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
