# Zhongyuan: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; the Arabic- and Persian-derived giving vocabulary of the Hui; Korean. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages in Henan, Hebei and western Shandong:** heavy gift-money systems and banquets.
- **Beijing and Tianjin offices:** gift money among colleagues; going Dutch among the young.
- **Jiaodong** (Qingdao, Yantai, Weihai): fishing communities' sharing of catch and feasting.
- **Hui Muslims:** mosque-based giving with its own calendar and recipients.
- **Catholic villages of Hebei** and **Protestant congregations in Henan:** church giving beside village gift systems.
- **Buddhist and Taoist temple donors** (Shaolin, Baiyun Guan, Taishan): merit lists on steles.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities** in northern Hebei: feasting customs to compare.
- **Migrant workers:** remittances home and gift money owed in two places.
- **Korean communities:** gift customs carried from Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gift exchange and banquets; mixed for formal philanthropy (data concentrated in Beijing); thin for minority giving outside the Hui.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Hui religious vocabulary; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the line between gift and bribe is politically sensitive (any legal side belongs to Libra); religious charities operate under limits; disaster-aid stories can be censored.
- **Held for review:** none.
