## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's official and corporate worlds** against **village leadership** in Hebei, Shandong and Henan.
- **Tianjin's industrial villages and suburbs:** strongman leaders and collective firms.
- **Shandong:** the Kong lineage and Confucian heartland; western Shandong's martial and sectarian leaders; Qingdao's corporate founders.
- **Henan:** model-cadre memory (Lankao, Linzhou); collective villages; women's mosques.
- **Hebei:** revolutionary sites; Catholic villages where priests and lay leaders lead.
- **Hui communities:** imams, mosque committees and women ahong.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities** of northern Hebei: clan and banner offices in memory.
- **Women:** leaders in families, villages and faith groups against formal posts.
- **Korean communities:** leadership in associations and churches.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for model leaders, firms and village history; mixed for everyday leadership in families and workplaces; thin for minority leadership outside the Hui.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Japanese for the wartime village surveys; Manchu for banner records; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party leadership and elections are sensitive; model-leader stories are official and need local counter-accounts; authority, hierarchy and law are Capricorn's or Libra's, so keep this sheet to willing following.
- **Held for review:** none.
