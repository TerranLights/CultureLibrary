## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing pastimes** (birds, crickets, pigeons, kites) against **new urban play** (escape rooms, esports, board-game cafés).
- **Tianjin:** its own mahjong and comic-play culture.
- **Hebei:** shadow-puppet villages of the east; the Bashang grasslands, with horse games; villages around the capital.
- **Shandong:** kite and cricket counties (Weifang, Ningjin, Ningyang); card games; Jiaodong sea play.
- **Henan:** temple-fair play and toys; the Jin-speaking north; martial play around Shaolin and Chenjiagou.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities:** games kept or revived as ethnic markers.
- **Hui communities:** play within halal and mosque life; martial-arts schools.
- **Children in cities against left-behind children in villages:** very different time and places to play.
- **Korean communities:** games carried from Korea.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traditional pastimes, kites and the game industry; mixed for children's everyday play; thin for minority play outside staged meets.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin with dialect knowledge; Classical Chinese; English for missionary-era collections; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling in card, mahjong and cricket play is legally sensitive; limits on minors' gaming are rules, so their legal side belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
