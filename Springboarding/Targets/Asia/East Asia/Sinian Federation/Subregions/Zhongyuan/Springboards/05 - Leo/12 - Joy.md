# Zhongyuan: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and regional speech (Beijing, Tianjin, Shandong, Henan); Classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian for heritage terms; Korean; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin audiences** (comedy, opera, temple fairs) against **village festivals** in Shandong, Henan and Hebei.
- **Jiaodong:** beer festivals and seafront leisure; fishing-village New Year.
- **Henan:** storytelling fairs, flower festivals and suona bands; the Jin-speaking north to separate.
- **Hebei:** eastern shadow-puppet country and the Bashang plateau; villages near the capital.
- **Hui Muslims:** Eid and family visiting.
- **Christians:** Hebei Catholic villages at Christmas; Henan Protestants and their hymns.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities:** festival revivals in Chengde's counties and Weichang.
- **Older and younger generations:** park singing and dancing against concerts and online celebration.
- **Korean communities,** with festival days of their own; **migrant workers,** whose joy gathers around the yearly trip home for the New Year.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals, comedy and happiness surveys; mixed for private and spiritual joy; thin for minority celebrations other than Hui festivals.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and regional speech; Classical Chinese; Hui religious vocabulary; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** house-church worship is sensitive and its sources may be blocked; city happiness rankings are promotional and must be read with care; festival restrictions can be political.
- **Held for review:** none.
