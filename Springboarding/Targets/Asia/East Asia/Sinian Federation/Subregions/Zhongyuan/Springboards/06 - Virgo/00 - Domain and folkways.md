# Zhongyuan: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (普通话) and its northern varieties (Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao, Zhongyuan Mandarin; Jin in northwest Hebei and north Henan); Classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui Muslim vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service, daily routine and health: what is good work and a well-kept life?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** state offices, the Zhongguancun tech sector, old hutong trades.
- **Tianjin:** port and treaty-port industry; its own dialect.
- **Hebei:** the ring around the two cities; the Bashang plateau (Zhangjiakou, Chengde) with Jin speech and herding margins; Tangshan heavy industry.
- **Shandong:** Jiaodong peninsula fishing and seafaring against inland western Shandong around Qufu.
- **Henan:** the plain's farming heartland and a large migrant-sending province; Jin-speaking north; the Nanyang basin.
- **Hui Muslims** in all five lands, whose purity and prayer rules shape the day.
- **Manchu** counties of northern Hebei and Beijing banner descendants; **Mongols** of Weichang; Beijing's **Koreans**.
- **Catholic villages** of central Hebei, with their own feast and rest calendar.
- **Migrant workers** from Henan and Hebei in the two cities, against settled residents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, gazetteers and Republican-era sociology; thin for Manchu and Mongolian sources on everyday work.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin with dialect awareness; Classical Chinese; Japanese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor unrest and the former labor camps (劳动教养) are sensitive, and any legal side goes to Libra or Capricorn; microdata access is restricted; regional prejudice against Henan people (地域黑) needs care. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author.
- **Neighboring folkways:** the clock and public order belong to Capricorn; only the household side is here.
- **Held for review:** none.
