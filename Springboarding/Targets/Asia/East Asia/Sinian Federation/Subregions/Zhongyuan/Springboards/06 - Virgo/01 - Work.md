# Zhongyuan: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and its northern varieties; Classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhongguancun programmers** against **state-unit staff** in Beijing: overtime culture against the secure post.
- **Port and Binhai New Area factory workers** in Tianjin.
- **Steel and coal workers** of Tangshan and Handan, Hebei, through closures.
- **Greenhouse vegetable growers** of Shouguang and the **Jiaodong fishing families**, Shandong: two farm and sea work rhythms.
- **Henan sending counties** (Zhoukou, Zhumadian, Shangqiu) against **Zhengzhou's electronics workforce**.
- **Left-behind women and elders** (留守) who take over farm work when men migrate.
- **Hui** family trades and **Manchu banner descendants**: occupational histories to test, not assume.
- **Bashang herders** and Weichang Mongols, whose work year differs from the plain's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; national statistics, labor history and fiction are deep; minority-language work vocabulary is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Japanese for occupation-era surveys; Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** factory unrest (e.g. Zhengzhou electronics plants), labor NGOs and strike reporting are restricted topics; labor law and 996 rulings go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
