# Zhongyuan: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural grandparents** in Henan and Hebei raising left-behind grandchildren, against **Beijing and Tianjin retirees** with pensions.
- **Scrap and recycling workers** in Beijing (often from Henan counties: to test), whose trade turns waste to use.
- **Disabled workers**, including blind masseurs, against those outside work.
- **Manchu banner descendants**, for the history of the "useless" label.
- **Women** whose housework and farm work are not counted as work.
- **Hui** households; **Mongol herders** of Weichang; Beijing **Koreans**: their own measures of a contributing member.
- **Jiaodong fishing families** against **plain farmers**: different chores for children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; surveys and classical texts are rich; elders' own voices on being a burden are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rural elder suicide linked to feeling a burden is a grave topic; read only published studies and handle with care; disability and old-age welfare law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
