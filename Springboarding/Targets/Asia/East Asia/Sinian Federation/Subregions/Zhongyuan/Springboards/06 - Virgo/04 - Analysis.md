## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haidian's universities and research institutes** against **county schools of the Henan and Shandong plains**: who is trained to analyze, and how.
- **Exam-drill schools** like Hengshui against **Olympiad-track schools** in Beijing: two school cultures of reasoning.
- **Tianjin's engineering and port-logistics workforce.**
- **Village farmers** deciding by long experience, against **agronomists and extension officers**.
- **Hui communities**, where crescent-moon sighting against calculated calendars is debated (*check*).
- **Catholic communities** of Hebei, heirs to the Jesuit science link.
- **Mongol and Manchu** communities, with their own reckoning traditions in their languages.
- **Generations:** abacus-trained elders against spreadsheet- and app-trained young people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; classical texts, education data and science-policy material are deep; minority-language sources are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese above all; Mandarin; Manchu for Qing records; Arabic and Persian for Hui calendar texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official statistics and data falsification are sensitive; academic misconduct cases too; statistical law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
