## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing antique and tea connoisseurs** against **ordinary market shoppers**: taste as learned status.
- **Tianjin audiences and crosstalk fans**, with their own reputation as judges.
- **Elders in all five lands**, the target of health and telecom scams.
- **Henan people** as the target of regional prejudice, and how they answer it.
- **Hui consumers and certifiers** deciding what counts as halal.
- **Rural matchmakers** (媒人) and bride-price negotiations in Henan and Shandong villages against urban parents' matchmaking corners.
- **Livestock traders** of Hebei and Henan markets.
- **Manchu, Mongol and Korean** communities' own words for shrewdness and naivety.
