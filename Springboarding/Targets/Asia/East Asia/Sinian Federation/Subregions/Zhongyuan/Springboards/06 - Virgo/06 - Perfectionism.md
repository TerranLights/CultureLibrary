## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing court-craft and conservation workshops** against **mass-production factories** in Tianjin and Zhengzhou.
- **Henan and Hebei kiln towns** (Ruzhou, Yuzhou, Quyang), where perfection is a local trade identity.
- **Shandong manufacturers** in Qingdao and Yantai, with a zero-defect business story.
- **Opera performers and calligraphers**, trained by copying a master exactly.
- **Students** in high-pressure exam provinces against Beijing's international-school pupils.
- **Rural households** where 凑合 is a necessity, not a choice: test, not assume.
- **Hui** and **Manchu** craft traditions (Hui halal food preparation standards; Manchu court textiles).
- **Mongol** and **Korean** communities' own standards in their crafts.
