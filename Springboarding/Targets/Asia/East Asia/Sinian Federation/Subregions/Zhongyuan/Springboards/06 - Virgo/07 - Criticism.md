## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing officials and state employees**, who meet formal "criticism and self-criticism", against **private-sector staff** and their performance reviews.
- **Tianjin and Beijing street talk**, where needling and banter are a verbal art; how outsiders hear it.
- **Shandong families and lineages** where correcting elders is constrained by filial norms.
- **Henan villagers**: satire of officials in local opera against everyday restraint (test, not assume).
- **Young online reviewers** against older readers of letters pages and call-in radio.
- **Hui congregations**, where religious disputes can carry public criticism (handle with care).
- **Catholic parishes** of Hebei, with confession and fraternal correction.
- **Manchu, Mongol and Korean** communities: their own teasing and admonishing forms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classics, satire and online reviews; political criticism is heavily filtered and partly deleted.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin with dialect; Classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political dissent, the Cultural Revolution, petitioning and censorship are restricted topics; protect any living informant; law on speech goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
