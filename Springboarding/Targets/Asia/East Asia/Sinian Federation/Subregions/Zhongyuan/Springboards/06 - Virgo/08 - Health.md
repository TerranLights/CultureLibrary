## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's top hospitals** against **rural Henan and Hebei clinics**: where people go for serious illness and how far they travel.
- **Industrial Hebei and Tianjin** neighborhoods living with air pollution.
- **Henan's former plasma-selling villages**: survivors' health and stigma.
- **Chinese-medicine families and lineages** (Nanyang, Luoyang, Anguo) against biomedical households.
- **Hui** households, with Hui medicine and halal diet rules.
- **Mongol** communities of Weichang and Bashang, with Mongolian medicine (蒙医) (*check* local presence).
- **Manchu** households with older healing customs; Beijing **Koreans** and their tonic traditions.
- **Elders** in park exercise groups against **young office workers** buying supplements and gym time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; surveys, medical classics and public-health records are deep; minority healing traditions are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Arabic and Persian for Hui medicine; Manchu and Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Henan AIDS crisis, SARS, COVID lockdowns and pollution data are politically sensitive; health records are private; health law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
