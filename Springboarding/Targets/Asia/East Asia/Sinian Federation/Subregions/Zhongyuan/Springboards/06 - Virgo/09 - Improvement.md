# Zhongyuan: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui reformist writing. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haidian and Chaoyang parents** against **county-town families in Henan and Shandong** for whom schooling is the way out of farming.
- **Residents moved by renewal**: Beijing hutong households; Sanmenxia and reservoir resettlers; merged Shandong villages.
- **Collective model villages** in Henan against neighboring ordinary villages.
- **Coastal Shandong and Tianjin** industrial towns against **inland plain counties**.
- **Hui communities** with their own reform and education tradition.
- **Manchu** and **Mongol** communities renewing their languages; Beijing's **Korean** community and its schools.
- **Older generations** shaped by the "remolding" campaigns against **young self-improvers** using apps and courses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; classics, policy material and reportage are deep; minority-led renewal is thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian; Arabic for Hui religious schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced relocation, demolition, "low-end population" evictions and past thought-reform campaigns are sensitive; property and relocation law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
