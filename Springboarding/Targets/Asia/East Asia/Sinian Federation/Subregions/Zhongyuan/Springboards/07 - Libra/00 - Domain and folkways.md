# Zhongyuan: Domain and folkways (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin in simplified characters; the regional Mandarins (Beijing, Jilu, Jiao-Liao, Zhongyuan Mandarin) and Jin of northern Henan and northwestern Hebei; Hui mosque vocabulary; Manchu and Mongolian (heritage); Korean. **Concept in one line:** partnership and justice: what a fair bargain and a good partnership are, across marriage, contracts, rivals, dispute settlement, manners, gender ways and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing hutong and banner-descended families** against **state and army compounds (大院)**: two urban codes in one city.
- **Tianjin**, a port with a foreign-concession past and its own speech.
- **Hebei:** Jidong (Tangshan), the central plain (Baoding, Cangzhou), the south (Handan), Jin-speaking Zhangjiakou, the Chengde hills with Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties.
- **Shandong:** Jiaodong (Qingdao, Yantai), the southwest (Qufu, Heze), Jinan and the river plain, the Yimeng hills (Linyi).
- **Henan:** the Jin-speaking north (Anyang, Xinxiang), the east (Kaifeng, Shangqiu), the west (Luoyang), the south (Nanyang), Zhengzhou.
- **Hui** of Niujie, Cangzhou, Mengcun and Dachang counties, Kaifeng and Zhoukou; **Manchu**; **Mongols** of Weichang; **Korean** residents of Wangjing and Qingdao; **Catholic villages** of central Hebei; **Protestants** in Henan; descendants of **Kaifeng's Jews**.
- Rural and urban, migrant and local, generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing and Shandong (classics, archives, ethnography); mixed for Henan and Hebei counties; thin for Mongols, Manchu speech and Kaifeng's Jewish descendants. Zhongyuan's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; the sheets are written for the five units in the tracker header.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin (including classical Chinese for pre-modern texts); Japanese for the Mantetsu surveys; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean for the smaller peoples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** court databases and archives have been narrowed since about 2021; petitioners, rights lawyers, house churches and Hui mosque policy are politically sensitive; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
