# Zhongyuan: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for early texts); Beijing, Tianjin, Jilu, Jiao-Liao and Henan speech; Mongolian and Korean for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin residents** against **Henan, Shandong and Hebei exam candidates**: one quota system read from both sides.
- **Hukou holders** against **migrants** in Beijing and Tianjin, on who earns schooling and city services.
- **Rural Hebei and Henan villages** (land, family division) against **Qingdao, Jinan and Zhengzhou** salaried workers.
- **Sons and daughters** in family division, by region and generation.
- **Hui** traders and butchers (Niujie, Cangzhou, Kaifeng): fair dealing in the halal trade, as they describe it.
- **Manchu and Mongol** autonomous counties under ethnic policy (exam bonus points and their critics).
- **Korean** residents (Wangjing, Qingdao) in a Chinese labor market.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exam and hukou fairness (public debate is loud); mixed on village division customs; thin on minority views.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Mongolian and Korean for small groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional quotas and ethnic bonus points provoke anger; inequality data can be sensitive; wage-arrears protests are watched.
- **Held for review:** none.
