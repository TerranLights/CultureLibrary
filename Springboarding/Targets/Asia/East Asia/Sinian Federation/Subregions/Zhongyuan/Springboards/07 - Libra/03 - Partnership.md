# Zhongyuan: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for tales); northern colloquial and regional speech of Shandong and Henan; Hui, Manchu, Mongolian and Korean usage for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin professionals** against **rural Henan, Hebei and Shandong** couples: bride price, housing and matchmaking differ.
- **Jiaodong** fishing and trade towns, against the **inland Shandong plain**.
- **Henan's south** (Nanyang, Xinyang) against its **Jin-speaking north**.
- **Hui** couples (imam-led nikah alongside registration), **Manchu** families and **Mongols** of Weichang, each with its own rules on whom one may marry.
- **Korean** residents and Chaoxianzu migrants (Wangjing, Qingdao, Yantai): cross-border marriages and partnerships with Korean firms.
- **Catholic** couples in Hebei villages, married in church and at the registry.
- **Migrant couples** split between a city job and a home village; **same-sex couples** in Beijing, without legal recognition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban Beijing marriage and matchmaking; mixed for village customs (county gazetteers help); thin for minority partnerships.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Korean; Mongolian and Manchu for heritage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT organizing is constrained; bride-price and marriage-migration stories can touch on trafficking; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** none.
