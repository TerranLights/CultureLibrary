# Zhongyuan: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for philosophy and medicine); regional speech; Mongolian and Korean for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing and Tianjin office and tech workers** against **farm households** of Henan and the Shandong plain.
- **Hebei towns ringing Beijing** (Langfang, Baoding, Xiong'an) against the capital they serve.
- **Practitioners of Chinese medicine and taiji** (Chenjiagou, Nanyang) against **biomedical** framings.
- **Daoist communities** (Laoshan, Qingdao; White Cloud Temple, Beijing), as they teach it.
- **Hui** households, where halal diet and mosque teaching shape what counts as moderation.
- **Mongols** of Weichang and **Korean** residents, with their own food and medicine traditions.
- **Resettled reservoir villagers** of Xichuan against receiving cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical texts, medicine and city form; mixed for everyday practice; thin for minority framings.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Mongolian and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 躺平 and labor-hours debates have been censored at times; resettlement accounts can be sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
