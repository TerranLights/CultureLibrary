# Zhongyuan: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for exam and philosophy texts); Beijing and regional speech; Korean and Mongolian for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing** central officials and the public that watches them, against **county towns** of Hebei, Shandong and Henan, where officials and residents share kin and school ties.
- **State-sector employees** against **private-sector** workers and migrants without connections.
- **Exam-heavy provinces** (Shandong, Henan, Hebei) against **Beijing and Tianjin**.
- **Hui** communities with imams and mosque boards chosen inside the community, as they describe it.
- **Manchu and Mongol** autonomous counties, where ethnic quotas and preferences apply.
- **Korean** residents in a market that runs on Chinese ties.
- **Fans** of Beijing Guoan, Tianjin, Shandong Taishan and Henan clubs, on referees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams and on publicized privilege cases; mixed on ordinary favoritism; thin on minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases are politically framed; interviews on connections and bribes need care and anonymity.
- **Held for review:** none.
