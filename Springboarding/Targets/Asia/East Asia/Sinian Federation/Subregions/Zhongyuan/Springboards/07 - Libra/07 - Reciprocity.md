# Zhongyuan: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for the *Odes* and *Rites*); northern colloquial; Hui mosque vocabulary; Korean. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages** of Hebei, Shandong and Henan (gift ledgers, labor exchange) against **Beijing and Tianjin** offices, where favors pass through work units and firms.
- **Jiaodong** fishing communities, with shared boats and catches, against inland grain farmers.
- **Southwestern Shandong** around Qufu, with its ritual heritage, against **Zhengzhou's** migrant newcomers.
- **Hui** communities (Niujie, Cangzhou, Kaifeng, Zhoukou): alms, mosque help and Eid exchanges, as they describe them.
- **Manchu** families of Chengde and banner-descended Beijingers: old visiting customs.
- **Korean** residents: rotating clubs and exchange among Chaoxianzu migrants.
- **Generations:** elders who keep ledgers against young people who complain of gift debt.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (renqing is a major research theme; Kipnis and Yang are local); mixed for Hui and Korean groups; thin for Mongols.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Korean; Arabic and Persian loanwords in Hui usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** favor exchange shades into bribery, so officials may not speak freely; gift ledgers are private family papers and need consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
