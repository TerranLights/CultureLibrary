# Zhongyuan: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese and oracle-bone script for the oldest records); Beijing and Henan colloquial; Mongolian and Korean for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-heavy Henan, Shandong and Hebei families** against **Beijing and Tianjin** families with more options and later deadlines.
- **Northern Henan** around Anyang and Tangyin, which claims the divination heritage, against **Zhengzhou's** new professionals.
- **Temple-goers** (Beijing, Kaifeng, Mount Tai) against secular urban youth.
- **Village decision-makers** (Dengzhou and other Henan counties) against **urban residents' committees**.
- **Hui** families who ask an imam's view, as they describe it; **Catholic** families of central Hebei who seek a priest's counsel.
- **Korean** residents weighing whether to stay or go home, and **Mongols** of Weichang, with their own ways of seeking counsel.
- **Women and men** in household decisions, as a variation to test, not a premise.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for divination history and classical idioms; mixed for everyday decision-making; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, classical Chinese, oracle-bone paleography (via specialists); Korean, Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is officially discouraged and practitioners may avoid notice; divorce statistics are not always published by province.
- **Held for review:** none.
