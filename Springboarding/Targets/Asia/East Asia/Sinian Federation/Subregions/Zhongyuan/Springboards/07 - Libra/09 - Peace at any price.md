# Zhongyuan: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for histories); northern colloquial; Manchu and Mongolian (Qing records); Korean. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Shandong, Henan and Hebei** families, with lineage and village pressure, against **Beijing and Tianjin** households with more access to courts and counselors.
- **Western Shandong** (the Zhang halls, Shouzhang area) against **Jiaodong**.
- **Workers in Tianjin and Zhengzhou factories** against **Beijing professionals**.
- **Hui** communities, where mosque elders may settle quarrels, as they describe it.
- **Catholic** and **Protestant** congregations of Hebei and Henan, with teachings on forgiveness.
- **Manchu and Mongol** counties and their memory of marriage alliances.
- **Korean** residents, who may keep a low profile as foreigners or a minority (a question).
- **Women** and **men** in family quarrels, as a variation to test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical appeasement and harmony texts; mixed for family and workplace silence; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Manchu and Mongolian for Qing marriage records; Japanese for 1930s sources; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability maintenance" (维稳), intercepted petitions and women's-rights groups are politically sensitive; domestic-violence informants need protection.
- **Held for review:** none.
