# Zhongyuan: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (simplified; classical Chinese for idiom sources and anecdote collections); regional sayings; Korean. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing, Tianjin and Baoding** as subjects and authors of each other's labels.
- **Henan migrants** in Beijing and Tianjin, and **Henan at home**, on the stigma.
- **Western Shandong and Zhili** Boxer areas against **Catholic villages** of central Hebei, on old rumors and blame.
- **Hui** communities of Cangzhou, Niujie and Kaifeng, on being blamed and on how they speak of others, as they describe it.
- **Manchu** descendants in Beijing, after anti-Manchu blame in 1911 and after.
- **Korean** residents, on the labels Chinese media pin on them and theirs for others; **Mongols** of Weichang, on frontier stereotypes.
- **Rural** households, where fox and weasel spirits may be blamed, against **urban** therapy users.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical idioms, spirit lore and Boxer-era rumor; mixed for present-day projection; thin for minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and classical Chinese; Korean; French and English for missionary records of 1870 and 1900, read critically.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "foreign forces" rhetoric, Cultural Revolution blame and pandemic blame are politically sensitive; regional and ethnic labels hurt, so quote them only as data.
- **Held for review:** none.
