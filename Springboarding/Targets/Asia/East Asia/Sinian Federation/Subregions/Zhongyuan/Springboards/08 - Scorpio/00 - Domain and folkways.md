## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing families and Banner descendants against newcomers (北漂):** funeral and inheritance customs may differ between rooted city families and migrants who keep home-village ways.
- **Tianjin:** a port and former concession city whose funeral trades (paper goods, musicians) may set it apart from Beijing.
- **Hebei's plain (Jizhong) against its north (Chengde, Zhangjiakou):** the north holds Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties with their own death and kin customs.
- **Shandong's southwest (Qufu, Jining), center (Zibo, Weifang) and Jiaodong peninsula:** old Lu, old Qi and the maritime east may differ on lineage strength and inheritance.
- **Henan's east plain, west (Luoyang), north of the Yellow River (Jin speech) and south (Nanyang, Xinyang):** each may handle burial land and household division differently.
- **Hui communities** (Niujie in Beijing, Cangzhou and Dachang in Hebei, Jinan, Kaifeng, Zhengzhou): burial timing, grave forms and inheritance shares under Islamic rules, as each community applies them.
- **Catholic villages in Hebei and Protestant congregations in Henan:** church funerals and cemeteries, a separate track.
- **Korean communities and Kaifeng Jewish descendants:** ancestral rites and openness about origin, tested apart.
