## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **City hospital deaths against rural home deaths:** where one dies may change what a good death means.
- **The very old against the young:** a death at ninety may be mourned unlike a child's or a young adult's.
- **Henan's AIDS-affected villages (for example in Shangcai county):** death in bulk, and the mourning shaped by stigma.
- **Hui Muslims:** death framed by Islamic teaching on judgment, with its own words and timing.
- **Catholics in Hebei and Protestants in Henan:** resurrection and prayer for the dead against ancestor offerings.
- **Lay Buddhists and Daoist congregations (Beijing, Kaifeng, Shandong temples):** rebirth and merit for the dead.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities in north Hebei:** older traditions about the dead to check against the Han majority.
- **Party members and state employees:** secular memorial meetings and state honors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, archaeology and official statistics; mixed for grief and belief; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; French for Chavannes; Hui religious vocabulary; Manchu and Korean for small groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in political campaigns, famines and the AIDS epidemic are guarded topics; ghost-marriage cases involve crime against the dead; suicide data are partial.
- **Held for review:** none.
