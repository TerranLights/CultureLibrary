*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese, regional Mandarin; Hui religious vocabulary; Korean; Manchu for revival work. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Henan and Shandong Protestants against Hebei Catholics:** sudden new birth and testimony against sacramental, lifelong change.
- **Lay Buddhists and Daoist devotees:** rebirth across lives and self-cultivation in this one, two different models.
- **Hui Muslims:** repentance and return to practice, and converts by marriage, as a separate frame.
- **Laid-off state workers in Tianjin, Tangshan and Henan cities:** economic reinvention after the 1990s.
- **People leaving prison or drug treatment:** whether a second start is accepted by kin and employers.
- **Manchu communities in Beijing and north Hebei, and Kaifeng Jewish descendants:** revival of language and identity, on their own terms.
- **Rural migrants in Beijing:** the move to the capital as a remade life.
- **Survivors of the Tangshan earthquake:** personal and civic rebirth told apart.
