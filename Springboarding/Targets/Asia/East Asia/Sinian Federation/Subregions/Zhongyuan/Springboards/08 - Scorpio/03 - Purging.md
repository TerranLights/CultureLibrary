# Zhongyuan: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese, Classical Chinese (medical and ritual texts), Beijing and other northern speech; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line** (proposed): getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hui communities:** ablution and fasting rules give purity a set form; city and rural Hui may differ.
- **The Cultural Revolution generation in Beijing schools:** memories of purging as perpetrators, victims or onlookers.
- **Officials and state employees:** discipline campaigns as a working hazard, against how ordinary people watch them.
- **Migrants in Beijing's outer districts:** on the receiving end of city clearances.
- **Villagers in Hebei under the clean-air drive:** cleansing imposed from above.
- **Temple goers and spirit-medium clients in Hebei, Tianjin and the Beijing outskirts:** evil driven out of bodies and homes.
- **Christians in Hebei and Henan:** confession and repentance, and the memory of the Boxer killings.
- **Users of traditional medicine across classes:** "heat" and "toxins" as everyday ideas, to test by age and education.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medicine, campaigns and folklore; mixed for emotional release; thin for minority purity customs outside the Hui.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Hui religious vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution killings, anti-corruption cases and the 2017 clearances are guarded; online sources are often removed.
- **Held for review:** none.
