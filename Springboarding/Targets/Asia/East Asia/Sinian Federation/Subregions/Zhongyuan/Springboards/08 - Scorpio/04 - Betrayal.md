## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing and Tianjin families:** memories of occupation and of who collaborated may still shape talk of "traitors" in old neighborhoods.
- **Tianjin's former concession districts:** compradors and foreign employers, and the charge of serving outsiders.
- **Families split in the Cultural Revolution:** children who denounced parents, colleagues who informed.
- **Henan and Hebei opera audiences in the countryside:** whether the faithless-husband plot still speaks to rural marriage and migration.
- **Wives and husbands separated by migrant work:** left-behind spouses and the suspicion of affairs.
- **Young rural graduates drawn into pyramid schemes:** recruited by friends and kin.
- **Christians in Hebei and Shandong:** the Boxer-era charge that converts had gone over to foreigners, and how churches remember it.
- **Korean Chinese in Beijing and Qingdao:** loyalty questions between two homelands.
