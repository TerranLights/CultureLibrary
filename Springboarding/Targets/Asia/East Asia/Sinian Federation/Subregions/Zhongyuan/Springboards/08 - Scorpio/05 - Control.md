*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's educated parents against Hebei county-town families:** different stakes in controlling children's schooling (Haidian against Hengshui).
- **Villagers along the Yellow River in Henan and Shandong:** flood and water control as lived experience across generations.
- **Rural women in Henan and Hebei against urban women in Beijing and Tianjin:** who holds the household purse and the final say.
- **Migrants without a Beijing household registration:** control by residence rules over schooling and work.
- **Manchu descendants in Beijing and north Hebei:** family memories of Banner restrictions.
- **Hui neighborhoods:** mosque committees and community norms as their own form of control.
- **Shandong and Hebei banquet hosts:** drinking customs where control over guests is a courtesy, a test to check rather than assume.
- **Registered and unregistered religious groups:** the state's control of worship felt differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state control, education and water history; mixed for couples; thin for minority groups.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Manchu for Banner archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance, social credit and religious regulation are guarded topics; hotline records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.
