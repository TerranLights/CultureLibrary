## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old hutong neighbors in Beijing against high-rise newcomers:** long acquaintance against anonymous towers.
- **Shandong and Henan villages:** trust carried by kin, sworn brothers and gift exchange, to set against city ways.
- **Henan migrants in Beijing and Tianjin:** the prejudice they report and the networks of fellow-villagers they rely on.
- **Hui traders and buyers:** halal trust networks around Niujie, Cangzhou and Zhengzhou.
- **Korean Chinese businesses in Wangjing and Qingdao:** trust inside a minority trade network.
- **Parents of young children:** food and vaccine trust, sharply tested in Hebei and Shandong.
- **Retirees:** targets of investment and health-product scams.
- **Christian congregations:** church networks as circles of trust outside kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (surveys, scandals, ethnography); mixed below province level; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Korean; Hui trade vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protests by depositors and investors are guarded; trust in police and courts is Capricorn's (the spirit of the law); regional prejudice must be handled as a subject, not repeated.
- **Held for review:** none.
