## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cangzhou and other Hebei martial counties:** honor and redress taught in lineages, against the plains towns around them.
- **Old Tianjin neighborhoods:** street-tough codes in memory and in local fiction.
- **Southwest Shandong (Heze, Liangshan):** outlaw legends, and whether they color how people speak of getting even.
- **Henan martial villages (Shaolin, Chenjiagou):** revenge in teaching stories against the discipline those schools stress.
- **Hui and Han neighbors in Henan:** reprisal after quarrels, and the mosques' and elders' role in calming them.
- **Women avengers in story and fact (Shi Jianqiao):** whether revenge is judged differently by gender.
- **Petitioners from the countryside:** grievance turned toward the state.
- **Young viewers of revenge dramas:** fantasy payback as entertainment, against older audiences.
