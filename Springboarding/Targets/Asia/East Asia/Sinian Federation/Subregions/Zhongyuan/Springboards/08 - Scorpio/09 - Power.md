## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central officials and compound families in Beijing against ordinary Beijing residents and migrants:** the capital's two experiences of power.
- **Hebei counties around Beijing and Xiong'an:** how places that serve the capital speak of its pull on them.
- **Tianjin's merchant and retired-official milieu:** wealth and former office as power.
- **Shandong's Confucian heartland (Qufu, Zou) against its coastal cities:** lineage and rank against commerce; the reported pull of public-service careers, to test.
- **Henan villages with strong party secretaries or lineage bosses:** rural power close up.
- **Hui communities:** mosque committees and imams as community power, beside the state.
- **Manchu and Mongol communities in north Hebei:** memories of Qing power and of its loss.
- **Women in rural and urban households:** the gap between formal equality and who decides.
