## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Residents of the Yellow River floodplain in Henan and Shandong:** generations of flood as an expected crisis.
- **Survivors and families in Zhumadian and Xinyang:** disasters long kept quiet, and what breaking the silence means locally.
- **Tangshan:** a city whose identity rests on one disaster and its rebuilding.
- **Shandong and Hebei families with ancestors who went to the northeast:** leaving as a family strategy.
- **Zhengzhou and Tianjin residents:** recent urban disasters and online mutual aid.
- **Villagers in Hebei's flood detention areas against Beijing residents:** whose safety comes first.
- **Factory and migrant workers in Zhengzhou and Beijing:** crisis under lockdown far from home.
- **Hui communities and church congregations in Henan:** mosques and churches as relief centers in floods, to be checked case by case.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, famines and wars; mixed for personal crises; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; English for wartime reporting; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Great Famine, the 1975 dam failures, death tolls and lockdown protests are guarded topics; some reports have been removed online.
- **Held for review:** none.
