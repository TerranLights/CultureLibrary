# Zhongyuan: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional); Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Jin speech of northwest Henan and southwest Hebei; Hui religious vocabulary; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean. **Concept in one line:** belief and philosophy: what is true, and where is meaning found (religion, philosophy, higher learning, long journeys; religious ways and freedom ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** banner legacy; seat of national religious bodies (Baiyun Guan, Yonghegong, Fayuan Si, Niujie).
- **Tianjin:** treaty port; Mazu at Tianhou Gong; the 1870 church conflict.
- **Hebei:** Catholic villages (Baoding, Xianxian), sectarian history, Chan sites at Zhengding and Zhaozhou.
- **Shandong:** the Qufu–Zoucheng heartland against the Jiaodong peninsula (Laoshan Daoism, Penglai); Boxer origins in the northwest.
- **Henan:** Kaifeng, Luoyang (White Horse Temple), Songshan (Shaolin), the Huaiyang fair; the Jin-speaking north against the south facing old Chu.
- **Hui:** Niujie, Mengcun and Dachang counties, Zhengzhou, Kaifeng; Gedimu, Yihewani and newer currents apart.
- **Manchu and Mongol counties:** Fengning, Kuancheng, Weichang; Chengde's Tibetan Buddhist temples.
- **Smaller groups:** Kaifeng Jewish descendants; Korean residents; house-church networks; internal migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical philosophy, Beijing and Mount Tai; mixed for village practice; thin for unregistered groups. The member state's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet uses the working list.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Japanese (Mantetsu surveys); some Manchu and Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches, groups labeled 邪教, Hui Islam and Tibetan Buddhism under policy; protect informants; weigh surveys as possible undercounts.
- **Held for review:** none.

5. Search ctext.org for 天, 命 and 道 in the *Analects*, *Mencius*, *Mozi* and *Zhuangzi* to set the classical baseline.
