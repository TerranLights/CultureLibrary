# Zhongyuan: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; North China Mandarin speech; Hui mosque vocabulary; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean for smaller communities. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hebei Catholic lineages:** faith inherited over centuries, a case of keeping faith through crisis.
- **Henan Protestants:** many first-generation converts; how faith is found, and through healing.
- **Hui Muslims:** Gedimu and Yihewani mosques may teach faith and practice differently.
- **Beijing lay Buddhists and Baiyun Guan Daoists:** urban, partly middle-class; faith chosen rather than inherited.
- **Temple-goers at Taishan, Huaiyang and Xunxian:** faith measured by efficacy and vows, not membership.
- **Party members and cadres:** official atheism; where faith in the nation or in progress stands in.
- **Manchu families of Chengde and Beijing:** household spirit rites and their memory.
- **Weichang Mongols:** Tibetan Buddhist devotion in the Bashang uplands.
- **Korean residents and migrant workers:** faith carried into Beijing and Tianjin from elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic Hebei and for pilgrimage history; mixed for Protestants (many unregistered); thin for household practice and for faith lost.
- **Languages needed:** modern and Ming-Qing vernacular Chinese for the scriptures; Arabic and Persian loans in Hui speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered Catholics and house churches; any group labeled 邪教; Hui mosque reform under current policy; party members' private belief. Anonymize informants.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library of China and CNKI for 宝卷 山东 and 宝卷 河北 to list surviving scriptures by county.
2. Read Madsen and Lian Xi to set Hebei Catholic and Henan Protestant faith side by side.
3. Query CNKI for 妙峰山 香会 and 泰山 碧霞元君 信仰 to follow vow-and-repayment studies to the present.
4. Pull WVS China items on God's importance and institutional confidence; check whether results split by province.
5. Search 中国穆斯林 issues for Niujie, Cangzhou and Zhengzhou to find Hui writers on faith in their own words.
