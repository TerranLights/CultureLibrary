# Zhongyuan: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia, covering Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong and Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese with northern colloquial forms; Hui religious terms; Mongolian and Korean for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Henan and Hebei elders:** meaning tied to children who have left; a test of family as the source.
- **Migrant workers from Henan and Shandong:** work as sacrifice for home; does the point lie elsewhere?
- **Beijing and Tianjin graduates:** striving, 躺平, and the elite-school version of emptiness.
- **Retired industrial workers in Tianjin and Hebei steel towns:** lost work roles; meaning after the plant.
- **Jiaodong fishing and coastal towns:** seafaring livelihoods; possibly different answers than inland farm counties.
- **Hui Muslims:** the hereafter as an explicit frame for this life.
- **Henan and Hebei Christians:** calling and suffering read through scripture.
- **Party veterans and cadres:** service to a cause as the stated source; compare generations.
- **Mongol herding families of Bashang:** land and livelihood as a candidate source.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and online talk; mixed in surveys (life satisfaction more often asked than meaning); thin for minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** modern Chinese including internet slang; classical Chinese for Song prose; Hui loanwords.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and despair (handle with care); youth disaffection is politically touchy; protect online posters' identities.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the 1980 Pan Xiao issues of 中国青年 and the letters that followed.
2. Search CNKI for 人生意义感 with each province name to find local studies using meaning scales.
3. Pull CHARLS depression and satisfaction items by province.
4. Query 空心病 and 躺平 on Weibo and Douban, dated, to trace the vocabulary.
5. Read Feng Youlan's four realms and Alitto's Liang interviews together as a twentieth-century baseline.
