# Zhongyuan: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia: Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (modern and classical); Mongolian for Bashang herders; Manchu and Korean for smaller groups. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing university faculty and students:** heirs to the May Fourth claims; academic freedom as lived and as remembered.
- **Old Beijingers and Beijing's migrants:** the hukou line splits who may stay and settle freely.
- **Tianjin:** a city whose history is framed by concessions; sovereignty as the freedom in question.
- **Rural Shandong and Henan villagers:** liberation stories of land and debt; freedom from want.
- **Women of different generations:** marriage choice, mobility and work.
- **Catholics, Protestants and Hui Muslims:** freedom of worship in their own terms.
- **Bashang Mongol and Han herders:** freedom of movement on grassland.
- **Liangshan and western Shandong martial-arts towns:** the outlaw and knight-errant image of freedom.
- **Online urban youth:** freedom as lifestyle and consumption.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for May Fourth and academic freedom; restricted inside the country for 1978-1979 and 1989 (use archives and diaspora collections); thin for herders and minorities.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Mongolian for herders; some English and Japanese for scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1989 and dissent; ethnic and religious freedom; internet censorship. Protect informants; anything about the constitution or rights as written belongs to Libra.
