## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing scientists and academics:** professional standards of proof; Tsinghua, Peking University, the Academy of Sciences.
- **State media and party press:** the official account as a source of truth in its own right.
- **Xinyang and other famine-hit Henan counties:** family memory set against the written record.
- **Hui Muslims:** God named as "the True Lord"; revealed truth alongside state truth.
- **Protestants and Catholics in Henan and Hebei:** scripture as the final word.
- **Retirees in WeChat groups and urban young on Bilibili:** generational split in rumor and correction.
- **Tianjin and Beijing crosstalk audiences:** a comic tradition that tests lies.
- **Rural Shandong and Hebei villages:** elders and genealogies (族谱) as keepers of the record.
- **Mongol and Manchu communities:** their own histories set against national narratives.
