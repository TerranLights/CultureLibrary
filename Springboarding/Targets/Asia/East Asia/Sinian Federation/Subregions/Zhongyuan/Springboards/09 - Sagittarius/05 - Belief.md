*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state (East Asia) made up of Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong and Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Qing literary Chinese; Hui Islamic terms; Manchu for banner rites; Mongolian and Korean. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and the warrant people want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural elders in Hebei and Henan:** animal spirits, mediums and household taboos; belief without a creed.
- **Urban educated professionals:** agnostic or atheist positions and what they accept as warrant.
- **Party members:** a formal atheist commitment; private practice may differ.
- **Hui Muslims:** a defined creed (six articles); compare Gedimu and Yihewani teaching.
- **Manchu families in Chengde, Fengning and Beijing:** banner household rites, revived or remembered.
- **Christians in Henan and Hebei:** doctrine taught in study groups; tension with village 讲究.
- **Jiaodong fishing towns:** sea taboos set against inland farm beliefs.
- **Young online users:** 玄学 and luck lore treated as play or as belief.
- **Kaifeng Jewish descendants:** belonging and belief may come apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Qing literature and pre-1949 folklore; mixed for present-day folk belief (often filed as 迷信 and under-reported); thin for minority creeds.
- **Languages needed:** classical and modern Chinese; Manchu for the 1747 code; Arabic-derived Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labeling a belief 迷信 or 邪教 carries consequences; mediums may avoid researchers; Hui teaching is under policy pressure.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read selected tales from Pu Songling and Ji Yun side by side, noting where each states doubt.

4. Pull Ding County temple tables from Gamble and compare with the county's modern 县志.
