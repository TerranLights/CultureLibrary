## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zou–Lu (Qufu, Zoucheng):** Mencian 气节 as heritage; how locals and tourists hear it.
- **Henan's Tangyin, Lankao and Linzhou:** loyalist and socialist exemplars side by side.
- **Old base areas (Yimeng, Taihang edge, Xibaipo):** conviction credited to whole villages.
- **Beijing intellectuals:** May Fourth and later dissenters; principle against career.
- **Hui communities of Cangzhou and Xianxian:** faith and national loyalty held together.
- **Hebei Catholics:** registered and unregistered paths as two kinds of steadfastness.
- **Petitioners from all five provinces:** persistence without a movement behind it.
- **Tianjin merchants and Beijing professionals:** pragmatic bending may be the norm to test.
- **Party cadres:** conviction as a trained, examined quality.
