*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing old-brand shopkeepers and their customers:** honesty as inherited reputation.
- **Tianjin market traders:** bargaining culture and where a fair price ends.
- **Henan migrants:** targets of a regional stereotype and often victims of wage fraud; both sides of the honesty story.
- **Shandong:** Confucian-heritage branding and the Rongcheng scoring pilot; does the scored city differ?
- **Hebei market towns (Baigou bags, Anguo herbs):** counterfeit and quality disputes in trading hubs.
- **Hui butchers and restaurateurs:** halal status as a religious guarantee of honest goods.
- **Students and parents:** exam cheating where the stakes are highest.
- **Officials:** honesty in office as the public judges it.
- **Mongol herders selling livestock:** trust in cross-ethnic markets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in news, consumer reporting and experiments; mixed in surveys (trust more often asked than honesty); thin for minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** modern Chinese with slang; classical Chinese for Han Fei and Sima Qian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional stereotypes (Henan) must be researched as stereotypes, not findings; credit scoring and corruption probes are sensitive. Bribery and fraud as legal offenses go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
