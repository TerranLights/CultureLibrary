## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** home of the elite universities; admission advantages and the capital's academic class.
- **Tianjin:** Nankai and Tianjin University; an older engineering and commerce emphasis.
- **Hebei:** the Hengshui model, and the Ding County legacy of mass literacy.
- **Shandong:** Confucian-heritage branding and very large exam cohorts.
- **Henan:** among the largest cohorts and few top universities; a sharp test of access.
- **Rural first-generation students:** the 小镇做题家 experience across all five provinces.
- **Vocational students:** status of 职业教育 against the university path.
- **Hui scripture-hall students:** religious learning beside state schooling.
- **Manchu, Mongol and Korean students:** language programs and admission policies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, statistics and institutional history; mixed for student experience; thin for religious and minority schooling.
- **Languages needed:** modern and classical Chinese; Arabic and Persian for scripture-hall texts; Manchu for Qing banner exams.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** admission quotas and minority bonus points are contested; campus politics and academic freedom (see Freedom); religious schooling of minors is restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
