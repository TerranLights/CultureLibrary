# Zhongyuan: Domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands: Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese (simplified and traditional script); Beijing, Tianjin, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Jin (晋语) in western Hebei and northern Henan; Hui Islamic vocabulary; Manchu and Mongolian for northern Hebei. **Concept in one line:** authority and duty: who commands, who obeys and what is owed, with career, status, reputation, age, time, rank, order and power as its folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing** families and banner descendants against **new Beijingers** (北漂 drifters, graduates): inherited courtesies versus career authority.
- **Tianjin** port city against its rural districts (Baodi, Jizhou, Jinghai): merchant and guild authority versus village office.
- **Hebei** plains (Baoding, Shijiazhuang, Hengshui) against the north (Chengde, Zhangjiakou) and **Xiong'an**, a planned city.
- **Shandong**: old Qi (east, sea trade, Jiaodong) against Lu (southwest: Qufu, Jining, Heze).
- **Henan**: Heluo (Luoyang, Zhengzhou), Kaifeng, the Jin-speaking north, the south (Nanyang, Xinyang).
- **Hui** (Henan; Hebei's Mengcun and Dachang; Shandong; Beijing); **Manchu** and **Mongol** counties of northern Hebei; descendants of Kaifeng's historic Jewish community; **Christian** villages in Hebei and Henan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the classical and imperial record and for Beijing; mixed for village and minority sources; thin for recent survey data by province.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the five provinces listed.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Japanese for occupation-era surveys; Manchu and Mongolian; Arabic and Persian loanwords in Hui sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petitioning, protest, unregistered religion and 1989; some archives closed. Online stereotypes of Henan people are common: record them as claims, never findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
