# Zhongyuan: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Beijing, Tianjin, Jilu and Zhongyuan Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Hui usage; Mongolian and Manchu for northern Hebei. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing compound children** (大院子弟, from ministry and army compounds) against **hutong** families and **migrant** workers: authority as inheritance, neighborhood standing or employer control.
- **Model villages** (Nanjie, the former Daqiuzhuang) against ordinary Henan and Tianjin villages: whether exceptional leaders shape expectations.
- **Lu** Shandong (Qufu, Zoucheng) with its Confucian sites against **Jiaodong** port cities (Qingdao, Yantai), where foreign concessions and trade left other models.
- **Hui** congregations, where imams are hired by lay boards, against Han lineage villages.
- **Catholic** villages of central Hebei: bishops recognized by Rome, by the state, or by both.
- **Generations**: those raised before 1978 against those born after 1995, online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical and Qing sources, workplace psychology and Beijing; mixed for village studies; thin and filtered for open attitudes to Party authority.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Mongolian and Manchu for Chengde materials; Japanese for older Sinology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveys on political authority face censorship and self-censorship; unregistered churches and 1989 are restricted topics. The permission share and correctional-control share are scored from the law in force: point to Libra; this sheet covers authority's spirit.
- **Held for review:** none.
