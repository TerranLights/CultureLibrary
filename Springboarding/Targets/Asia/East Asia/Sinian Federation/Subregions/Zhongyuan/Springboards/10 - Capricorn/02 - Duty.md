# Zhongyuan: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shandong and Henan rural usage; Hui religious vocabulary; Manchu and Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Migrant-sending Henan counties** (Zhoukou, Zhumadian, Shangqiu, Xinyang) with left-behind elders, against **Zhengzhou** and **Beijing** households: duty by remittance or by presence.
- **Beijing only children** of the 1980s who now care for four parents (the 4-2-1 family) against large rural families in Heze or Handan.
- **Lu** Shandong with restored lineage halls and genealogies, against **Tianjin** port families with fewer lineage ties.
- **Hui** families, whose named religious duties sit beside filial ones, against Han households.
- **Banner descendants** in Beijing and northern Hebei; **Mongols** of Weichang.
- **Military families** around Beijing and Shandong's coastal bases; **Christian** families who rank duty to God above lineage rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for filial duty (classical and survey); mixed for civic duty; thin for minority duty concepts in Chinese-language scholarship.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Manchu for banner archives; Arabic and Persian terms in Hui texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burial reform, religious duties of unregistered groups, and conscription data; model exemplars come through state channels and need village-level counterweights.
- **Held for review:** none.
