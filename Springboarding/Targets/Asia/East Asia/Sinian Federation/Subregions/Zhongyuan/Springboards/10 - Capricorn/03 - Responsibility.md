## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tianjin Binhai**, **Shijiazhuang** and **Zhengzhou**: three cities with recent public disasters and investigations; compare how residents and officials spoke of blame.
- **Sending counties** in eastern and southern Henan, and Hebei's Handan and Xingtai, where older children mind siblings, against Beijing's single-child families.
- **Township cadres** in Shandong's Zouping or Henan counties against **ministry staff** in Beijing: answerability by targets or by protocol.
- **Factory and mining towns** (Tangshan steel, Pingdingshan coal) where workplace accidents shape talk of fault.
- **Hui** congregations, where repentance has its own vocabulary; **Christian** villages, with sin and confession.
- **Older and younger generations** online, where 甩锅 humor is common.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official accountability and recent disasters; mixed for family and child responsibility; thin for minority perspectives.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; internet slang; Hui terms of Arabic and Persian origin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the blood-plasma epidemic, smog, the 2021 flood death counts and industrial disasters are censored topics in places; victims' families have faced pressure. Criminal-liability ages and liability law are letter-of-law matters: point to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
