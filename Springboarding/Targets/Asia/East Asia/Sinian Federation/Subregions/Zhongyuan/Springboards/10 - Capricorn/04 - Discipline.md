# Zhongyuan: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northern colloquial usage; Classical Chinese; martial-arts and opera jargon; Hui usage; Mongolian for Weichang. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, religious bodies, the state) or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hengshui** and other exam-factory towns in Hebei, Henan and Shandong against **Beijing's** well-funded schools: whether harsh schedules are chosen or forced by competition.
- **Dengfeng**, **Chenjiagou**, **Cangzhou** and **Wuqiao**: martial and acrobatic training traditions with different lineages and markets.
- **Daoist** and **Buddhist** monastics (White Cloud Temple; Shaolin) against lay practitioners.
- **Factory workers** in Zhengzhou and Tianjin against office staff in Beijing.
- **Hui** families with fasting and prayer routines; **Christian** families with their own rules.
- **Weichang Mongols** and **Manchu** banner descendants, where riding, archery or wrestling were trained, *check* current practice.
- **Urban "gentle parenting"** circles against rural households.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schools, martial arts and opera; mixed for family discipline; thin for minority training traditions.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; craft jargon; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corporal punishment, harsh camps and factory deaths are sensitive; compulsory "re-education" sites lie outside this sheet's lands but surface in searches; handle with care. Prison discipline belongs with Law and Order.
