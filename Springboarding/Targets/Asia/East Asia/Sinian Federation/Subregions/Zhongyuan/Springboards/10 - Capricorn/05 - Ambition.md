# Zhongyuan: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and Beijing colloquial; Classical Chinese; 2020s online slang; Manchu-heritage and Hui sources in Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing natives** (including Manchu banner descendants, whose forebears were barred from trade) against **北漂 newcomers** and graduates: ambition with or without a Beijing hukou.
- **Shandong** and **Henan** exam counties against **Tianjin** and **Qingdao** business families: rising by office versus by trade.
- **Return migrants** in Henan and Hebei villages who built houses with city wages, against those who stayed in Zhengzhou or Beijing.
- **Hui** traders and restaurateurs (Dachang, Mengcun, Niujie) against Han small-business owners.
- **Women** in each group: whose ambition is praised, and whose questioned.
- **Post-95 "lying flat"** youth against parents raised under reform-era striving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, migration and fiction; mixed for opinion polls (many are commercial or state-run); thin for minority ambition paths.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; online slang; Japanese for Manchuria migration sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "lying flat" content was censored in 2021; regional prejudice about Henan migrants is common online, so record it as a claim. Rich lists are estimates.
- **Held for review:** none.
