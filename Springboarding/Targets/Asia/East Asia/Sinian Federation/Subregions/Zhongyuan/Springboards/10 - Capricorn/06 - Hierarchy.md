# Zhongyuan: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Shandong and Beijing banquet and address customs; Hui parish terms; Manchu and Mongolian rank words. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing hukou holders** against **non-hukou residents**, and compound families against hutong families.
- **Municipalities** (Beijing, Tianjin) against **sub-provincial** (Jinan, Qingdao) and ordinary prefectures in Hebei and Henan: rank of places felt by residents.
- **Qufu** and **Zoucheng**, where descent from Confucius or Mencius carries standing, against non-lineage towns.
- **Rural Shandong** and **Hebei** counties with strict seating customs against Tianjin and Qingdao.
- **Banner descendants** (Manchu, Mongol, Han banner) in Beijing and Chengde; **Weichang Mongols**.
- **Hui** parishes and their internal ranks.
- **Henan migrants** across the country against Henan residents at home: rank by origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical rank, hukou and Beijing; mixed for rural etiquette; thin for minority ranking systems today.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Manchu; Mongolian; French for Tianjin concession history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hukou and inequality figures are politically charged; regional prejudice is a live harm, so record it as data about prejudice, never as description of a people.
- **Held for review:** none.
