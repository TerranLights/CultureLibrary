# Zhongyuan: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and online slang; Classical Chinese; Hui religious-school terms; Manchu for banner examinations. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing** and **Tianjin** students (admission rates reported as higher, to check) against **Henan**, **Shandong** and **Hebei** candidates: merit judged under unequal quotas.
- **County-town graduates** (小镇做题家) against urban children with tutoring and connections.
- **Civil-service candidates** in Shandong against private-sector workers in Qingdao or Tianjin: merit by test or by market.
- **Lineage towns** like Qufu, where descent still confers honors, against ordinary counties.
- **Banner descendants**, whose ancestors had reserved routes, against Han families.
- **Hui** students in mosque schools against those in state schools.
- **Folk performers** and martial artists judged by public contest or by lineage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial exams and gaokao; mixed for hiring and nepotism (often anecdotal); thin for minority paths.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Manchu for banner exam records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** officials' family privilege and hiring scandals are censored at times; regional quota debates are heated. Keep online class taunts as data about talk, not about people.
- **Held for review:** none.
