# Zhongyuan: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese; Classical Chinese; Tianjin and North China street speech; Hui religious-law terms; Manchu and Mongolian legal words. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. This sheet takes the spirit of the law; its letter is Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing** residents, near the courts and ministries, against **petitioners** arriving from Henan, Hebei and Shandong counties.
- **Kaifeng**, where the Judge Bao tradition is local, against cities with other legal memories (Tianjin's concessions with foreign courts).
- **Villages** with posted compacts and strong lineages (southwest Shandong, northern Henan) against suburban Beijing communities.
- **Tianjin** old-city families with stories of street codes, against new residents of Binhai.
- **Hui** communities, where imams and elders mediate under religious norms.
- **Lawyers and judges** against people who avoid courts; younger, legally trained Beijingers against older relatives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for imperial legal culture and famous cases; mixed for survey data on trust in courts; thin for minority legal orders.
