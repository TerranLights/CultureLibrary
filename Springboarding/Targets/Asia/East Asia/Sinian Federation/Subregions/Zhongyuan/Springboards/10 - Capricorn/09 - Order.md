*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing** inner districts, with the axis, towers and patrols, against **suburban** and **migrant** districts (Daxing, Changping).
- **Tianjin** concession quarters against its old Chinese city and Binhai.
- **Henan** flood and famine counties (the 1938 floodplain; Xinyang) against Zhengzhou and Luoyang.
- **Tangshan** survivors' families against Hebei towns without such a break.
- **Rongcheng** and other scored cities against cities without such pilots.
- **Hui** neighborhoods (Niujie, Kaifeng's Shunhe district) with purity rules of their own.
- **Temple-fair and pilgrimage** groups, where festive disorder has set limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for planning, disasters and policing campaigns; mixed for perceived-safety data; thin for minority ideas of order.
- **Languages needed:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Beijing and Tianjin colloquial; Mongolian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1989, the 2017 removals, the 1975 Zhumadian dam collapse, crime data and surveillance are censored in part; famine and flood death tolls are contested. Present every count as a claim with its source.
- **Held for review:** none.
