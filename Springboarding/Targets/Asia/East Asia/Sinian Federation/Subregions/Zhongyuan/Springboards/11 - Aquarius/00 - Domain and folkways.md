# Zhongyuan: The domain, community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; lands carried: Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Mandarin (普通话) and Classical Chinese; Beijing, Jilu, Jiaoliao and Central Plains Mandarin; Jin in northern Henan and northwestern Hebei *check*; Hui usage; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what do they hope for (friends, groups, hopes, humanitarian ideals), with the folkways of settlement, association, hospitality and neighboring.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing:** hutong-born old Beijingers; children of the state and army compounds (大院); recent migrants (北漂).
- **Tianjin:** the old walled city against the former concessions.
- **Hebei:** the Bashang plateau and Chengde (Manchu and Mongol counties) against the central plain (Baoding, Hengshui) and the coast (Tangshan, Qinhuangdao); Cangzhou's Hui towns.
- **Shandong:** Qi (coastal, Linzi) against Lu (Qufu, Zoucheng); the Jiaodong peninsula; the southwest (Heze, Jining); the Yellow River delta's oilfield towns.
- **Henan:** Heluo (Luoyang), Kaifeng, the Nanyang basin, the Jin-speaking north of the river, and Xinyang in the far south.
- **Peoples and faiths:** Hui (Niujie, Mengcun, Dachang, Kaifeng); Manchu and Mongol counties of northern Hebei; Korean residents; Catholic villages in Hebei; Protestant congregations in Henan; Kaifeng's Jewish-descended families.
- **Diasporas:** descendants of the Shandong and Hebei migrants who went to the Northeast (闯关东); Shandong-origin Chinese in Korea; Henan workers in other provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing and for Hebei and Shandong villages (a century of surveys); thinner for southern Henan, the minority counties and groups outside the state's categories. The member state's lands are a working list the author still confirms; these sheets are written for the five provinces listed.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin and Classical Chinese; Japanese (Mantetsu); English; some Manchu and Mongolian for heritage sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered religious groups, independent NGOs, protest and dissent, and Uyghur and Tibetan neighborhoods in Beijing; informants may face risk. Rules on registering associations belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
