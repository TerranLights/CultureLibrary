# Zhongyuan: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin and its northern varieties; Classical Chinese; Hui usage; Korean; Manchu for heritage terms. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (place, faith, trade, origin, interest, cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Housing forms in the cities:** hutong courtyards; work-unit compounds; new gated estates (小区); urban villages (城中村) where migrants rent; commuter towns across the Hebei line (Yanjiao).
- **Villages:** Jiaodong lineage villages against the mixed-surname villages of the North China Plain; Henan's densely farmed plains against the Taihang and Funiu hill villages.
- **Hui 坊 communities:** Niujie (Beijing), Mengcun and Dachang (Hebei), Kaifeng and Zhengzhou (Henan), Jinan.
- **Catholic villages** of central Hebei, whose parish life runs beside the village's.
- **Manchu and Mongol villages** of Chengde and Weichang.
- **Korean residents** in Wangjing and Qingdao, as newer communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing and Hebei village studies; mixed for Shandong; thin for Henan below the county seat and for minority villages.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, regional speech for interviews; Korean for Qingdao and Wangjing community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolition and relocation; disaster death tolls and deleted posts; residents' committees' surveillance role; unregistered congregations.
- **Held for review:** none.
