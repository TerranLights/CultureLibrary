# Zhongyuan: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (northern varieties), Classical and vernacular-novel Chinese; Manchu terms for the banners; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; how the culture weighs group against person, draws "us" against "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banner descendants and Han** in Beijing and Chengde: a hereditary group against a civilian one.
- **Compound children and hutong children** in Beijing, often rival groups in memoirs.
- **Western Shandong and Henan villages** with histories of armed self-defense groups, against coastal Jiaodong with its emigrant and trading networks.
- **Tianjin dockside and old-city workers**, whose guild and gang histories are their own.
- **Hui congregations** in Cangzhou, Kaifeng and Beijing, and **Catholic villages** in central Hebei: faith groups that also mark kin and neighbors.
- **Migrant 老乡 groups** in Beijing (by province and county of origin).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history (banners, Boxers, Red Guards); mixed for present-day groups; thin for measured group attitudes broken down by province.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical and vernacular-novel Chinese; Manchu for banner records; Japanese for some wartime studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution factional violence; unregistered churches; Falun Gong and other banned groups; 汉奸 as an accusation; the rice-wheat study must not be read as a verdict on any people.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Liangshan oath and ranking chapters of 水浒传, then Esherick, to set the pre-modern and modern band side by side.

3. Search 北京国安 球迷 协会 历史 and 泰山 球迷 for supporters' histories.
4. Search 红枪会 河南 史料 for county-level sources on the Red Spears.
