# Zhongyuan: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; Hui usage; Manchu-Han terms of the Qing; Korean for Korean residents. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing hukou holders and non-holders**; Tianjin's natives and its migrants.
- **City and countryside:** Zhengzhou, Jinan and Shijiazhuang against farming counties; the Hebei counties ringing Beijing (described as a poverty belt in some reports *check*).
- **Gaokao candidates** from crowded Henan and Shandong against those from Beijing and Tianjin.
- **Manchu and Han** in Beijing and the Chengde counties; minority bonus points (加分) and their critics.
- **Hui women** in Kaifeng and Zhengzhou, and Hui elsewhere in the five provinces.
- **Domestic workers and rural women** who migrate to Beijing, and the women's groups that serve them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income and hukou gaps; mixed on attitudes by province; thin on minorities' own views.
