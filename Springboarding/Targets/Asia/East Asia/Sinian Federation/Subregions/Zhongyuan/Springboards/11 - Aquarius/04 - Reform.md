# Zhongyuan: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for imperial reform debates; Hui usage for mosque reform movements. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformers' home grounds:** Dingxian (Hebei) and Zouping (Shandong), worth setting against nearby counties never touched by the movements.
- **Factory cities:** Tianjin, Shijiazhuang and Luoyang workers laid off in the 1990s (下岗) against the managers who led the changes.
- **Villages under customs reform:** Zhoukou and other Henan counties; Shandong and Hebei villages with wedding and funeral rules.
- **Xiong'an and Baiyangdian** residents, against Beijing planners.
- **Hui congregations**, split between older and reformist traditions.
- **Beijing intellectuals and students**, the usual authors of reform programs, against those the programs reached.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on imperial and Republican reform; mixed on the reform era; thin on villagers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; English; Japanese for some Republican-era studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Democracy Wall and 1989; grave flattening; demolition and resettlement; thought and labor reform. Reform of laws as written goes to Libra; what law is for to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
