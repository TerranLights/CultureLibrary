# Zhongyuan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese; the political vocabulary of the revolutionary and reform eras; Hui usage; Korean. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elite Beijing students** (Peking University, Tsinghua) against students at provincial universities in Zhengzhou, Jinan and Shijiazhuang.
- **Henan collective villages** (Nanjiecun) against the market villages around them.
- **Qufu and Zoucheng**, where a Confucian revival (academies and study halls) presents its own ideals.
- **Generations:** the May Fourth heirs, the sent-down generation, the 1980s generation, and today's students.
- **Faith communities** (Henan Protestants, Hebei Catholics, Hui) whose ideals run beside or against official ones.
- **Old industrial workers** in Tianjin and Luoyang who speak of collective-era ideals.
