# Zhongyuan: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin with Beijing and Shandong colloquial terms; Classical Chinese; Mongolian, Manchu and Korean for minority residents. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing hutong men** of the older street culture against compound children and new middle-class arrivals.
- **Shandong banquet culture** in Jinan and Weifang against Jiaodong and the southwest.
- **Henan villagers and Henan migrants** in Beijing and Zhengzhou, where friendship and 老乡 ties overlap.
- **Women's friendship** (闺蜜) and men's (哥们儿), as a question, not an assumption.
- **Hui and Han** friends, where food rules shape who eats at whose table.
- **Mongol and Manchu** residents of the northern Hebei counties; Korean residents in Qingdao and Wangjing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classical sources and in guanxi studies; thinner on women's friendship and on friendship outside business and drinking.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Beijing and Shandong colloquial; Classical Chinese; Mongolian, Manchu and Korean for minority terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** favors among friends that shade into corruption; heavy drinking; same-sex closeness misread.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the 管晏 and 廉蔺 biographies on ctext.org; note which idioms grew from them.
2. CNKI: 友谊 男性 北京 访谈 and 闺蜜 关系 研究 for interview studies.
3. Search 山东 酒桌 规矩 主陪 副陪 for etiquette guides and videos.
4. Search 搭子 北京 交友 on Douban and Xiaohongshu.
5. Read Kipnis's chapters on 感情, then Mayfair Yang, to set village and city side by side.
