## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Beijing's art, music and gay scenes** against the provincial capitals (Zhengzhou, Jinan, Shijiazhuang) and county towns.
- **Tianjin's storytelling and crosstalk world**, with its own tradition of licensed oddity.
- **Henan and Shandong villages**, where the pressure on people who do not marry, or have no son, is a question to test against the cities.
- **Generations:** the 1980s rock youth, the 2000s 非主流, and today's online subcultures.
- **Hui youth** balancing faith and city life; **Korean** youth in Wangjing and Qingdao.
- **Women and men**, as separate cases of who may be eccentric.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Beijing's scenes and for the classical eccentric; thin for provincial towns and for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin (including slang), Classical Chinese, English for much scene scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBTQ organizing (several Beijing groups closed in recent years *check*); dissident art and poetry; religious groups labeled heterodox (邪教); people in psychiatric care.
- **Held for review:** none.
