# Zhongyuan: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese (Daoist, Chan and literati texts); net slang; Hui usage; Korean. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms prized (calm, objectivity, equanimity), others condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Daoist and Chan communities** (White Cloud Temple, Laoshan, Bailin, Shaolin) against lay practitioners in the cities.
- **Beijing's elderly living alone** in old hutongs and new towers, against Henan and Shandong villages emptied by migration (left-behind elders, 留守老人).
- **Young urban workers** in Beijing and Tianjin who speak of lying flat, against provincial peers.
- **Professions** that require composure (doctors at Beijing's big hospitals; police; journalists).
- **Hui, Catholic and Protestant** communities, each with its own teaching on grief and letting go.
- **Korean residents** of Wangjing and Qingdao.
