# Zhongyuan: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), Sinian Federation member state, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin with northern colloquial words; Classical Chinese; the language of sectarian scriptures; Hui usage; Korean. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sending and receiving places:** Henan and Shandong villages that send workers out, against Beijing and Tianjin, where migrants hope to stay.
- **Jiaodong and western Shandong**, with different histories of emigration to the Northeast and abroad.
- **Henan villages hit by the 1990s blood-selling epidemic**, as a distinct case (handle with care).
- **Exam families** in Hebei, Henan and Shandong against Beijing families with more routes open.
- **Faith communities:** Henan Protestants, Hebei Catholics, Hui congregations, and temple-goers at Taishan and Huaiyang, each with its own object of hope.
- **Korean residents** in Qingdao and Wangjing; the elderly left in villages.

## 4. Flags
