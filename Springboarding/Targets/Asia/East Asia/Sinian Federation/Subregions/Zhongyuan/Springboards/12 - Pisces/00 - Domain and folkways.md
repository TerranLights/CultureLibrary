# Zhongyuan: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia; working lands Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan. **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; regional Mandarins and Jin; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui religious vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (the hidden and the unconscious, confinement and institutions, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence), with magic ways as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Beijing families, including Manchu banner descendants,** against the capital's newcomers: inherited customs against migrant lives.
- **Tianjin,** a port city with its own dialect and goddess cult (Tianhou): a coastal pattern distinct from Beijing.
- **Hebei's central plain, the Jin-speaking Bashang plateau and the Chengde Manchu and Mongol autonomous counties:** three ecologies of the unseen.
- **Hebei's Catholic villages** (Baoding, Hengshui, Xingtai areas): a centuries-old Christian layer in a folk-religious landscape.
- **Shandong's west (Qufu, Jining, Heze), Jiaodong peninsula (Yantai, Weihai, Qingdao) and Yimeng (Linyi):** Confucian heartland, Daoist coast and old revolutionary base.
- **Henan north of the Yellow River (Jin speakers), the Zhengzhou-Kaifeng-Luoyang core, the east (Zhoukou, Shangqiu) and the south (Nanyang, Xinyang):** separate temple-fair and healing cultures.
- **Hui Muslims** (Niujie and Changying in Beijing, Dachang and Mengcun in Hebei, Cangzhou, Jinan, Zhengzhou, Zhoukou); **Henan's Protestant networks; Kaifeng's Jewish-descended families; Korean Chinese** in Beijing and Qingdao.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the pre-modern and Republican eras; mixed for today, since belief and mental-health surveys rarely publish province-level results.
- **Lands:** the member state's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this set is written for the five working provinces above.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and modern Chinese, the regional Mandarins and Jin for fieldwork, Japanese (Mantetsu), Manchu and Mongolian for minority archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** groups labeled *xiejiao* 邪教 (heterodox teachings), including movements that arose in Henan, house churches, petitioners and coercive psychiatric detention; protect informants. Search collision: pinyin "Zhongyuan" also matches 中元节, the ghost festival; always search in characters.
- **Held for review:** none.
