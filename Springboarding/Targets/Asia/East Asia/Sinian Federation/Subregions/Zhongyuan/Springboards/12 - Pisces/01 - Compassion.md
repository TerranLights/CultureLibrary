# Zhongyuan: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese, regional Mandarins; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui usage. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Qufu and Zoucheng against the Jiaodong cities:** the classical home of Mencian pity beside fast-commercial coastal cities.
- **Henan's famine-memory counties** (1876-79, 1942, and the Great Leap years in Xinyang): where remembered hunger may shape how relief is given and refused.
- **Tangshan survivors and their children:** compassion received after 1976, and how it is repaid.
- **Hebei Catholic parishes:** organized mercy with its own institutions.
- **Hui neighborhoods:** almsgiving with a separate religious grammar.
- **Beijing's migrant workers and the city's settled residents:** who is pitied, who helped, and across what line.
- **Buddhist lay circles** around Zhengding and Zhao county temples: merit-making against secular volunteering.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famine relief and Republican charity; mixed for modern giving, since national reports rarely split by province; fieldwork needed for village almsgiving.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese, modern Chinese, Japanese for some charity histories, Manchu for banner relief records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1942 and Great Leap famines; independent charities and church charities face registration limits; crowdfunding fraud cases. Mercy toward offenders belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** translated scriptures of South Asian origin (category only); Chinese-authored sources are used instead.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Mencius* 2A6 and the *Mozi* "Jian'ai" chapters side by side, then search CNKI for 恻隐之心 民间 for modern readings.
2. Search 粥厂 北京 民国 and 红卍字会 济南 for Republican relief records.
3. Read Will and Edgerton-Tarpley for Zhili and Henan; follow their archive citations.
4. Search 进德公益 年报 and 松堂关怀医院 for present-day organized mercy.
5. Search 水滴筹 河南 / 山东 for crowdfunding appeals and their wording.
