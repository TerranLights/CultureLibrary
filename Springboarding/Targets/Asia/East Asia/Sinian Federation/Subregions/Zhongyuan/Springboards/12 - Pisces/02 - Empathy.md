# Zhongyuan: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Beijing and Tianjin colloquial speech; Korean and Manchu for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Native Beijingers and newcomers:** a local speech code of noticing others' needs, against migrants learning it.
- **Tianjin's crosstalk and teasing culture:** joking as a test of reading other people, distinct from Beijing.
- **Shandong banquet hosts and guests:** seating and toasting that require reading rank and feeling; urban and rural forms.
- **Rural Henan and Hebei left-behind elders and children:** who is understood across long separation.
- **Patients traveling to Beijing hospitals and the hospital staff:** empathy across fatigue and class.
- **Hui and Han neighbors:** empathy across religious lines (Niujie, Cangzhou, Zhoukou).
- **Korean Chinese:** their own word for reading the room, in Wangjing and Qingdao.
- **Men and women in each province:** who may show feeling openly, compared rather than assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; lab and scale studies exist but mostly use student samples from Beijing universities; rural and minority samples are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and colloquial), Korean for Chaoxianzu sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cultural Revolution memoirs and doctor-patient violence are sensitive topics; protect informants in hospital fieldwork.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search CNKI for 共情 量表 中文版 and 人际反应指数 to find Chinese scale norms and their sample sites.
2. Search Han Shihui's publications on empathy and in-group bias, and list sample locations.
3. Read the *Hongloumeng* flower-burial chapter with the Zhiyan zhai notes; search 红楼梦 读者 哭 for reader-response studies.
4. Search 叙事医学 北京大学 and 医患关系 共情 for clinical training and its critics.
5. Watch three episodes each of *Xinli fangtan* and *Aiqing baowei zhan* and note the phrases used for "seeing it from their side".
