# Zhongyuan: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Hui usage; Mongolian and Korean for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hebei's Yan-Zhao heroic memory, Shandong's filial-and-loyal tradition and Henan's loyal-general and model-official cults:** three regional repertoires of who is praised for dying or wearing out.
- **Old base areas** (Yimeng in Linyi; the Taihang slopes of Henan and Hebei; the central-Hebei tunnel villages) against cities: wartime sacrifice remembered locally.
- **Hebei Catholics:** a parallel martyr calendar.
- **Hui communities:** their own fallen and their fasting month.
- **Women across generations:** from chastity-arch honor to today's mothers and daughters-in-law, by county.
- **Migrant parents and left-behind children in Henan and Shandong:** sacrifice by absence.
- **Henan's former plasma-donation villages:** bodily sacrifice under poverty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official martyrs and pre-modern exemplars; mixed for ordinary family sacrifice; caregiving surveys allow provincial cuts.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (*Shiji*, gazetteers), modern Chinese, Arabic-derived Hui terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the honor of state martyrs is protected and disputes over them can be prosecuted; Henan's AIDS villages; organ donation and its past; Boxer-era violence remembered differently by Catholics and others.
- **Held for review:** none.
