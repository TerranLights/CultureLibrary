# Zhongyuan: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Hui usage; Korean, Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Residents of crowded shared courtyards (大杂院) and of gated tower compounds (小区):** two very different ways of living beside neighbors.
- **Native Beijingers and Tianjiners against migrants from Hebei, Henan and Shandong:** who counts as local.
- **Hebei commuters across the Beijing line:** a boundary crossed twice a day.
- **Hui and Han neighbors:** food, marriage and burial lines (Niujie, Cangzhou, Mengcun, Zhengzhou).
- **Shandong and Hebei families with relatives who went "outside the pass":** a remembered crossing.
- **Former concession neighborhoods** (Tianjin, Qingdao, Weihai): a colonial line now heritage.
- **Korean Chinese in Wangjing and Qingdao:** their own word for crossing a line.
- **Rural multi-generation households in Henan against nuclear city households:** what parents may decide for grown children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for architecture and historical borders; mixed for personal and family boundaries, where online talk and fieldwork will carry most of the load.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese (classical and colloquial), Korean, some Japanese for concession-era studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** residence registration and checkpoints touch policy (anything on law goes to Libra and Capricorn); Hui purity lines need careful, community-led framing.
- **Held for review:** none.
