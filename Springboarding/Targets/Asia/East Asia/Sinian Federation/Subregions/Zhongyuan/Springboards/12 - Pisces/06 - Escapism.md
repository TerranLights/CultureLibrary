# Zhongyuan: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Beijing and Tianjin colloquial speech; internet slang. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old-Beijing pastime keepers** (birds, crickets, pigeons) and **young Beijing office workers** who game or stream: two generations of absorption.
- **Tianjin's comedy audiences:** a city famous for its stages.
- **Shandong banquet drinkers and Hui neighbors under Islam's ban on alcohol:** intoxication against a religious rule, with practice on both sides to be checked, not assumed.
- **Rural men's card games and drinking in Henan and Hebei, and rural women's square dancing:** who escapes where.
- **Migrants drifting to Beijing and those leaving it for home:** escape in both directions.
- **Students in Hebei's exam schools:** escape under the heaviest pressure.
- **Jiaodong and Beidaihe resort towns against inland counties:** escape as a place to go.
