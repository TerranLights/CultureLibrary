# Zhongyuan: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Manchu, Mongolian, Korean; Hui religious vocabulary; Japanese for some temple scholarship. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (institutions of faith belong to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taishan pilgrims and Qufu's Confucian ritual revival:** goddess devotion and ancestral rites, both in Shandong.
- **Henan temple-fair devotees and Henan house-church Protestants:** two rural spiritualities, sometimes in the same villages.
- **Hebei Catholic villages:** Marian devotion and pilgrimage.
- **Young Beijing professionals at temples:** a new, practical seeking.
- **Lay meditators around Hebei Chan monasteries:** contemplative practice by urban youth.
- **Hui Muslims:** prayer, fasting and women's mosques.
- **Manchu and Mongol descendants near Chengde:** older shamanic and Tibetan-Buddhist ties.
- **Korean Chinese congregations:** Protestant networks in Beijing and Qingdao.
- **Non-believers, including many Party members:** a secular baseline to measure against, not to assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pilgrimage history and Catholic and sectarian studies; mixed for today because religion surveys are sensitive and underreport; temple fairs are well documented by folklorists.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and modern Chinese; Manchu; Tibetan and Mongolian for Chengde temple texts; Japanese for some sectarian scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered churches, banned groups, Catholic divisions, and the Shaolin abbacy's controversies; Hui religious life is a politically sensitive topic. Protect informants and keep fieldwork notes private.
- **Held for review:** translated scriptures of South Asian origin (category only); only Chinese-authored Buddhist sources are named above.
