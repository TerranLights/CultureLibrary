# Zhongyuan: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; market and trade jargon; Korean, Manchu and Mongolian for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chinese-medicine doctors and biomedical doctors in Beijing and Zhengzhou hospitals:** two schools of clinical judgment.
- **Antique dealers and livestock brokers:** trained eyes in urban and rural trade.
- **Rural women in Henan and Shandong who read omens and dreams, and young urban professionals:** who trusts which signs.
- **Residents of Hebei's earthquake belt** (Xingtai, Tangshan, Qinglong): disaster foresight in memory.
- **Fengshui practitioners and the families who hire them:** paid intuition about space.
- **Zhongguancun engineers:** data over gut.
- **Korean Chinese:** their own words for hunches.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for classical divination and medicine; mixed for present-day trust in hunches; trade-jargon studies exist but are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Classical Chinese (oracle inscriptions need specialists), modern Chinese, trade jargon, Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paid fortune-telling is officially discouraged and practitioners may avoid researchers; the earthquake-warning stories are politically framed.
- **Held for review:** none.
