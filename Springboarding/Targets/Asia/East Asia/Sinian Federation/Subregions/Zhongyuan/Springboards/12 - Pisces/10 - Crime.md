# Zhongyuan: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard and Classical Chinese; Tianjin and Beijing colloquial; Hui usage; Manchu and Mongolian. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. The law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Liangshan and Heze, with their outlaw-hero lore, and Qufu-Zoucheng's Confucian self-image:** two Shandong pictures of the wrongdoer.
- **Tianjin's old street-tough tradition:** urban rough honor, distinct from Beijing.
- **Henanese at home and Henanese migrants elsewhere:** the experience of being stereotyped.
- **Hebei families harmed by the Sanlu scandal:** victims of corporate wrongdoing.
- **Families of abducted children in Shandong and Henan:** the wrong that unites searchers.
- **Families of the wrongly convicted (Hebei, Henan):** the innocent as criminal.
- **Migrants in Beijing and Tianjin, blamed for crime in local talk:** a claim to test against data.
- **Hui communities:** their own words for sin, and any stereotyping they face.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and history; likely thin for victimization (check whether any regular public victimization survey covers these provinces); crime statistics may be published at a high level only.
- **Languages needed:** Classical and modern Chinese; Tianjin colloquial; Japanese for some banditry studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wrongful convictions, petitioning, the Henan bank protests and anti-corruption cases are politically sensitive; regional and ethnic stereotypes must be recorded only as claims. Law and punishment as written go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
