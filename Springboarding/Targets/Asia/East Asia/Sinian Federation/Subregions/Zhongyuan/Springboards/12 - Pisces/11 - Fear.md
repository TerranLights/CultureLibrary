# Zhongyuan: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Zhongyuan (中原), a Han member state of the Sinian Federation, region East Asia (Beijing, Tianjin, Hebei, Shandong, Henan). **Language(s) for searching:** Standard Chinese and northern colloquial speech; Classical Chinese for legends; Hui usage; Manchu, Mongolian and Korean. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural grandparents who call back frightened souls and urban parents who take a child to a pediatrician:** two treatments of fright.
- **Old Beijing and Tianjin families with child-snatcher lore, and Henan families with the Mahu bogey:** regional fear figures.
- **Hebei's earthquake belt (Tangshan, Xingtai):** inherited fear of the ground.
- **Zhengzhou residents after 2021 and Beijing residents who lived through SARS:** urban disaster memories.
- **Hebei tunnel-war villages:** wartime fear turned into heroic memory.
- **Fishing communities of the Jiaodong coast and Tianjin:** sea dangers and their protectors.
- **Hui Muslims and Christians:** reverent fear of God as a separate register.
- **Young workers and students against elders:** social and economic anxiety against older fears.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and disaster reportage; mixed for anxiety (one strong provincial survey for Shandong; little for the others).
- **Languages needed:** Standard and colloquial Chinese, Classical Chinese, Korean, some Manchu.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2021 Zhengzhou flood toll, SARS-era reporting and COVID-19 lockdowns are politically sensitive; ghost content in film and television faces approval limits.
- **Held for review:** none.
