# Taiwan: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, the Indigenous languages (Atayal and Bunun first for formal speech). **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo north and south:** Taipei office culture against Tainan and Kaohsiung family firms, for who may speak first.
- **Hakka:** whether the 硬頸 self-label shows in everyday assertion or only in public memory.
- **Waishengren families:** military-village upbringing and deference to rank.
- **Indigenous peoples with different speaking orders:** Amis age sets (juniors and seniors), Paiwan and Rukai chiefly houses against commoners, Bunun and Tsou men's ritual speech, Tao community meetings on Orchid Island.
- **Marriage migrants and migrant workers:** speaking up to in-laws, brokers and agencies; the government's 1955 hotline as a record.
- **Gender and age:** daughters-in-law, young workers, LGBTQ people at Pride.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese Americans in US workplaces.
