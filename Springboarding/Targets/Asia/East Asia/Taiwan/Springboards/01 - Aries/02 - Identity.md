# Taiwan: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, the Indigenous languages; Japanese for 1895–1945 registers; Classical Chinese for Qing writing. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo,** including Hoklo-ized Hakka families who have lost the language but keep the lineage.
- **Hakka,** northern belt against southern Liudui, and county by county language loss.
- **Waishengren** across three generations, from the 1949 arrivals to grandchildren raised in Taipei.
- **The 16 recognized Indigenous peoples,** and urban Indigenous residents who grew up away from home villages.
- **Pingpu reclaimants:** Siraya around Tainan, Kaxabu and Pazeh around Puli, Makatao in Pingtung.
- **New immigrants and 新二代:** Vietnamese, Indonesian, Filipino and Thai mothers and their children.
- **Kinmen, Matsu and Penghu:** island identities with separate wartime histories.
- **Generations** before and after the 1987 end of martial law.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese Americans; Taiwanese in Japan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for national identity; rich for Hakka and Indigenous counts; thin and contested for Pingpu.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese, Classical Chinese; Vietnamese and Indonesian for new-immigrant media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national identity divides along party lines, so read surveys from more than one house; some group labels are slurs; status and recognition rules go to Libra; heritage to Capricorn, belonging to Cancer.
- **Held for review:** none.
