## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo patrilineal families,** city and countryside: inheritance, coresidence and marriage choice.
- **Hakka lineages,** whose ancestral halls and estates may bind choice differently.
- **Waishengren families,** many without extended kin nearby after 1949.
- **Amis,** with matrilineal and uxorilocal traditions, for whose voice decides in a household.
- **Paiwan and Rukai,** where the first-born of either sex inherits the house, and chiefly houses shape village choices.
- **Atayal, Truku and Seediq** (Gaga or Gaya norms), **Tao** (collective claims over the island), **Bunun** (large joint households).
- **Marriage migrants** and **live-in migrant caregivers,** whose time and movement others often set.
- **LGBTQ young adults** and **people with disabilities** seeking their own say.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese American "parachute kids" sent abroad alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family surveys and youth panels; mixed for medical decision-making; thin for village councils outside a few well-known communities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese, the Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "self-determination" and "autonomy" also name contested national and Indigenous political claims; keep political sovereignty for Capricorn and the law for Libra. Family conflict and LGBTQ accounts need care with privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
