# Taiwan: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi (business and workshop talk), Hakka, Amis and other Indigenous languages for communal work; Classical Chinese for Qing land deeds. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Taiwan's workshop belts** (Taichung machine tools, Changhua hardware and socks) against **Hsinchu Science Park** engineers: two routes to founding.
- **Hoklo family firms** in Tainan and Kaohsiung, and family-business succession.
- **Hakka farming and tea districts** (Miaoli, Hsinchu, Meinong) and their cooperatives.
- **Waishengren families:** check how civil-service and military careers bore on starting businesses.
- **Indigenous communities:** Amis age sets, Paiwan and Rukai chiefly direction of work, community enterprises in Atayal and Truku villages, urban Indigenous construction crews.
- **New immigrants** opening Southeast Asian shops and eateries.
- **Returning young farmers (青農, 返鄉青年)** in rural counties.
- **Penghu fishing households,** for seasonal starts.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese founders in Silicon Valley and their associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and volunteering; mixed for community projects; thin for Indigenous communal work outside media reports.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Amis and other Indigenous languages, Classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founder myths are often polished by companies, so pair them with workers' accounts; rotating clubs sometimes collapsed in fraud. Work ethic goes to Virgo, ambition to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the latest 中小企業白皮書 and find its tables on new firms by owner sex and age.
2. Search Airiti and the thesis library for 標會 創業 and 黑手 頭家.
3. Search the Institute of Taiwan History's deed database for 合約 開墾 股份.
4. Browse join.gov.tw for proposals that reached the threshold, by year.
5. Search TITV for 換工 and malapaliw.
