*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo settler lineages** in the southwest (early Han settlement areas) against northern and eastern later-settled areas.
- **Hakka:** 新丁 rites in Miaoli and Hsinchu against southern Liudui practice.
- **Waishengren and Dachen evacuees:** a 1949–55 beginning rather than a Qing one.
- **Indigenous peoples:** Amis ilisin and age-set promotions, Puyuma 少年猴祭, Bunun, Tsou and Atayal origin places in the mountains, Tao season openings, Paiwan cycles; Siraya revival of the Dutch-era record.
- **New immigrants:** first years in Taiwan and their orientation classes.
- **People starting over:** ex-prisoners, people in recovery, the divorced.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese emigrants who returned after decades abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for New Year, birth and settler lore; rich but colonial-framed for Dutch and Japanese records; mixed for Indigenous origin stories in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese, Dutch, Classical Chinese, the Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some origin stories and ceremonies are restricted to members; "who was here first" is politically charged; founding heroes are contested. Rebirth goes to Scorpio, tradition to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
