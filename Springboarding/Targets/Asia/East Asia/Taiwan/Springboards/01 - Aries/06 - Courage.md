*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Seediq, Truku and Atayal:** tattoo, rainbow bridge and resistance memories, with village-by-village differences after 1930.
- **Tsou, Paiwan, Rukai, Bunun, Amis and Puyuma:** warrior honors and men's houses, each with its own signs of valor.
- **Hakka:** the 義民 tradition in the north against southern Liudui militia memory.
- **Hoklo temple communities:** spirit mediums and procession troupes.
- **Waishengren veterans and Kinmen civilians:** wartime courage with a different memory of 1949–58.
- **Families of 228 and White Terror victims:** accounts of moral courage that reached print only after 1987; compare by decade of telling.
- **Women:** endurance, testimony and the question of whose bravery is named.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** overseas Taiwanese activists once barred from returning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political memory and war; mixed for civilian bravery; thin for Indigenous warrior honors outside a few peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese, Seediq, Tsou and other Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting history, restricted ritual knowledge, suicide and self-immolation, sexual violence in wartime, and party-coded memory; handle each with care. Honor goes to Leo, duty to Capricorn, fear to Pisces.
