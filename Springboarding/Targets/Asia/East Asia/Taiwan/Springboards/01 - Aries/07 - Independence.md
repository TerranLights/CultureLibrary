## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo and Hakka multi-generation households** (伙房 courtyard homes in Hakka districts) against nuclear households in New Taipei and Taichung.
- **Waishengren veterans,** including residents of veterans' homes (榮民之家).
- **Indigenous peoples:** Amis and Tao self-provisioning; Bunun and Atayal hunting; urban Indigenous workers far from home villages.
- **Elders living alone (獨居老人)** in rural townships of Chiayi, Yunlin and Pingtung.
- **Disabled people** pressing for independent living.
- **Marriage migrants** and the households that depend on their labor.
- **Young adults** priced out of housing in Taipei.
- **Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu:** island households and their supply links to the main island.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** exiled activists abroad before 1990.
