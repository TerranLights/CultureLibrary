## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taipei families and élite high schools** against rural and eastern counties with fewer school places.
- **Hoklo temple towns** with procession rivalries (Tainan, Changhua, Chiayi).
- **Hakka districts** with Yimin-festival contests (Hsinchu, Taoyuan).
- **Indigenous peoples** in baseball, track and weightlifting (Amis, Bunun, Paiwan, Puyuma), and their own festival contests.
- **Waishengren families** and the civil-service examination path.
- **Students from new-immigrant families** in a contest built for Mandarin speakers.
- **Fishing villages and Yilan river communities** with boat races.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese American students in US admissions.
