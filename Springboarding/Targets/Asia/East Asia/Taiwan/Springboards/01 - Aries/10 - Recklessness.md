*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in central and southern counties** with car and scooter cultures, against Taipei's transit-dependent youth.
- **Hoklo temple towns** with fire and firework rites (Yanshui, Taitung, Toucheng, Hengchun).
- **Hakka districts:** northern Ghost Month customs against Pingtung's.
- **Indigenous peoples:** Tao and Amis fishermen and Bunun and Atayal hunters, where daily danger is a livelihood, not a thrill; separate that from recklessness. Drinking stereotypes about Indigenous people are a claim to test, not a starting point.
- **Waishengren veterans** and Kinmen islanders, for wartime risk memories.
- **Migrant workers** in construction and fishing, and how far their risk is set by employers.
- **Day traders and retail investors** across age groups.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese youth abroad in street-racing scenes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, gambling history and festivals; mixed for drugs; thin for Indigenous-language words of rashness, which need the online dictionaries.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Indigenous languages, Classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class and ethnic stereotypes cling to drinking and gangs; deaths and drug cases involve private people. Gambling and drug law go to Libra; escape and fear to Pisces.
