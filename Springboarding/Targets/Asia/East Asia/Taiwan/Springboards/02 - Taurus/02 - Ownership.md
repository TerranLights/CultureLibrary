# Taiwan: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; classical Chinese, Japanese and Dutch for older records. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on. Land and property law as written goes to Libra; what law is for goes to Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Plains Indigenous descendants** (Siraya, Taivoan, Makatao, Kaxabu): claims lost through Qing contracts, now being reasserted.
- **Mountain peoples:** Atayal, Truku and Seediq hunting grounds under *gaga/gaya*; Bunun and Tsou family and clan lands; Paiwan and Rukai chiefs' territories and tribute.
- **Amis and Kavalan of the east coast:** coastal land against resort development.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** fishing grounds, taro fields and the island as a whole.
- **Hakka of Liudui and of Taoyuan-Hsinchu-Miaoli:** ancestral associations holding shared land.
- **Hokkien landlord and tenant families:** opposite memories of land reform.
- **Mainlander families and former military villages:** housing given by the state, then rebuilt or sold.
- **Young urban renters in Taipei and New Taipei:** the generation shut out of owning.
- **Penghu villagers:** shares in stone weirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deeds, statistics and protests; thinner for each Indigenous people's own tenure rules in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; classical Chinese for Qing deeds; Japanese for colonial surveys; Siraya (romanized) for specialists; Formosan languages for territory terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** territory mapping is politically live and some sites (burial grounds, sacred places) may be restricted by communities. Keep legal readings for Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
