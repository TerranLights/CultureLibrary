# Taiwan: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Vietnamese, Tagalog and Thai for migrant workers' own accounts. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Civil servants, teachers and military retirees** (the "iron rice bowl") against private-sector and dispatch (派遣) workers.
- **Farmers and fishers:** crop and boat insurance, farmers' associations (農會) and fishermen's associations (漁會) as safety nets; Yilan's Nanfang'ao and the Penghu fleets.
- **Indigenous peoples:** millet and taro stores, hunting and sharing as the peoples describe them; urban Indigenous construction workers' job insecurity; the Tao's season-bound fishing.
- **Hakka farm towns** and **Hokkien market vendors:** different uses of savings clubs and land as insurance.
- **Earthquake belts:** Hualien and Taitung on the east, the Taichung-Nantou area of 1999, Tainan of 2016.
- **Migrant workers and caregivers** from Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand, and from the archipelagos to the south.
- **Elders living alone** and families using long-term care (長照2.0).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Social insurance, disaster and food data are open; informal security (savings clubs, kin) is thinner.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for savings-club talk; migrant workers' languages for their own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national defense and cross-Strait threat are a separate, charged field; keep household security in view and handle defense sources as context only. Migrant workers who left employers ("missing" workers) face legal risk; protect identities.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the NHI and Labor Insurance yearly statistics; list groups outside each scheme.

5. Visit or write to TIWA for published migrant testimonies.
