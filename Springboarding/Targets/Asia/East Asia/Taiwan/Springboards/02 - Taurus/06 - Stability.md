*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Hokkien towns** (Lukang, Tainan, Dadaocheng) with families on one street for generations, against **new towns** of Taoyuan and Hsinchu built around industrial parks.
- **Hakka farm villages** with courtyard houses kept by one branch, against Hakka who moved east to Hualien and Taitung.
- **Mainlander families:** a generation that lived "temporarily" and the generations born after.
- **Indigenous peoples moved by the state:** villages shifted down the mountains in the colonial and postwar eras; Morakot resettlement of Paiwan and Rukai; Tao; Atayal near reservoirs *check*.
- **Urban Indigenous families** of the Taipei area who move between city and home village.
- **Military and civil-service families** with steady posts, against small-business families.
- **Penghu and the outlying islands:** out-migration and the empty houses left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Registration and labor data are fine-grained; ceremonies and relocation stories need community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for house and almanac terms; Hakka; Paiwan, Rukai, Saisiyat and Tsou for ceremonial cycles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** parts of Saisiyat and Tsou ceremonies are closed to outsiders; resettlement is a raw subject for Morakot survivors.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Buy a current 農民曆; list its sections on moving house and opening business.
5. Search local government sites for 百年老店 listings in Tainan, Lukang and Taipei.
