# Taiwan: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old landed families** against **post-1960s industrial fortunes** and **semiconductor-era wealth** around Hsinchu Science Park.
- **Small-firm families** of central Taiwan (Changhua, Taichung) against salaried Taipei households.
- **Hakka** farm and tea families (Beipu, Emei) against Hokkien merchant towns.
- **Paiwan and Rukai** ranked houses (wealth as tribute and heirloom) against **Amis**, **Bunun** and **Tao** ways of reckoning and sharing.
- **Mainlander families:** wealth lost in 1949, and the state-linked careers that followed.
- **Rural east coast (Hualien, Taitung)** against the western corridor.
- **New immigrants:** remittances and small shops.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tax, survey and business sources; the peoples' own measures of wealth need their own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for boom-era sayings and temple talk; Japanese for colonial firms; Paiwan and Tao for wealth terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** village-level income data invite stigma of places; rich lists are estimates; the sacred-pig contest draws animal-welfare protest; ranked-house matters belong to the houses.
- **Held for review:** none.
