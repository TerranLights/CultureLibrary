## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hakka of Liudui and Meinong** against **northern Hakka** of Taoyuan-Hsinchu-Miaoli: does "hard neck" mean the same to both?
- **Seediq, Truku and Atayal:** memories of 1930 and of resistance to the colonial "pacification" campaigns.
- **Amis:** the Morotai holdout and other soldiers' stories.
- **Hokkien fishing districts** (Gongliao, the northeast coast) and **farming villages** (Miaoli's Dapu, Changhua) in long fights over land and plants.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** decades of refusal over the island's waste site.
- **Unionized flight crews and laid-off factory workers** against employers.
- **Democracy-movement generations** (1979 Kaohsiung Incident, 1990 Wild Lily, 2014 Sunflower) and their opponents.
- **Mainlander veterans** who held to the hope of return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in protest history and news; personal and family stubbornness needs interviews and fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Hakka for the key folk words; Japanese for 1930 and wartime records; Seediq and Amis accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wushe, 228 and martial-law memories are painful and contested; self-immolation and hunger strikes need care; whether a protest was "principle" or "obstruction" depends on who speaks, so record both.
- **Held for review:** none.
