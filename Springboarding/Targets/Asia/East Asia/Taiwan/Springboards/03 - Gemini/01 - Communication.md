# Taiwan: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages, Taiwan Sign Language; Japanese for colonial-era speech. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the local standards of what good communication is and is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Japanese-educated elders against younger cohorts:** the oldest speakers learned talk norms under a different school system.
- **Hokkien and Hakka households:** politeness formulas and the use of indirect speech may differ by home language.
- **Atayal communities in Yilan:** a contact language reshaped everyday talk there.
- **Bunun, Paiwan and Tsou:** respect forms toward elders and chiefs vary by people; Paiwan noble houses add a rank dimension to address.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** island isolation and a strong oral tradition make a distinct case.
- **Mainlander families:** home dialects and military-school speech habits.
- **The Deaf community:** a full communication system outside spoken norms.
- **Vietnamese and Filipino spouses and workers from Java:** how meaning is negotiated across languages in homes and workplaces; children as language brokers.
- **Taipei professionals against small-town southern speakers:** register and directness as urban or rural markers, to be tested.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Mandarin pragmatics; mixed for Hokkien and Hakka; thin for most Indigenous peoples beyond word lists.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, Atayal and other Indigenous languages, Taiwan Sign Language (with a Deaf researcher or interpreter).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school punishments for home speech; martial-law limits on dialect broadcasting; Indigenous oral knowledge as each people shares it.
- **Law:** speech regulation and broadcasting rules belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
