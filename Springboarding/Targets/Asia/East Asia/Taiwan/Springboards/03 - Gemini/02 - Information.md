## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders on LINE against young people on PTT, Dcard and Instagram:** different channels, likely different trust.
- **Rural townships in Yunlin, Chiayi and Pingtung against Taipei and Hsinchu:** loudspeakers and temples against broadband and tech jobs.
- **Mountain Indigenous townships:** connectivity gaps, and knowledge held by elders and clans as each people describes it.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** a long dispute over information about the nuclear-waste site there.
- **Hakka and Hokkien audiences:** minority-language news services against the Mandarin mainstream.
- **New immigrants and migrant workers:** news in Vietnamese, Thai, Tagalog and the languages of Java, often through their own pages and radio programs.
- **Survivors of martial-law repression and their families:** records long withheld, and opened later.
- **Tech workers in the Hsinchu Science Park:** an information-industry milieu.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; Taiwan is a heavily studied case for disinformation, which may crowd out ordinary questions of trust and the local spread of news.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, classical Chinese, Indigenous languages, Southeast Asian languages for migrant media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political security and cross-strait matters; martial-law files touching living families; knowledge some Indigenous peoples restrict by clan, gender or rank; research with Indigenous peoples requires consultation (letter to Libra).
- **Law:** information-access, privacy and broadcasting law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
