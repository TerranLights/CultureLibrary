## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amis age-set communities:** graded age groups as a candidate learning path outside school, to be described as each community does.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** boat-building and fishing knowledge taught on the shore and at sea.
- **Bunun and other mountain peoples:** hunting and land knowledge, as each people chooses to describe it.
- **Rural Hakka townships such as Meinong:** whether local study traditions differ from nearby Hoklo towns.
- **Mainlander military-village children:** learning shaped by displacement and state schooling.
- **Rural Yunlin, Chiayi and Taitung families against Taipei and Hsinchu:** unequal access to cram schools and enrichment.
- **Children of new immigrant mothers:** home languages from Vietnam and other countries, and school support programs for them.
- **Deaf children:** learning through Taiwan Sign Language or through spoken-language schooling.
- **Penghu islanders:** fishing households and the move to Taiwan's cities for schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam pressure and cram schools; thinner for learning by watching and pitching in, and for Indigenous ways of learning.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, classical Chinese, Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Japanese-era 蕃童教育所 (schools for Indigenous children) and their legacy; corporal punishment in schools (a past practice, now banned); some Indigenous training knowledge is restricted by gender or age set.
- **Boundary:** the school system itself goes to education in Sagittarius; laws on schooling to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
