*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Amis and their home villages on the east coast:** ties stretched by migration and renewed at festivals.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** ties around noble houses and their followers, a structure to set beside Han kin networks.
- **Hakka of Hsinchu and Taoyuan:** village alliances through shared ritual hosting.
- **Hoklo towns along Mazu procession routes:** temple alliances as an intertown network.
- **Mainlander veterans and military-village families:** ties to lost home provinces and to fellow soldiers.
- **Penghu islanders who moved to Kaohsiung:** hometown bonds after migration.
- **Filipino, Vietnamese and Thai workers and spouses, and workers from Java:** networks built across borders and around churches and mosques.
- **Elders living alone in rural townships:** contact thinning as the young move to cities.
- **Taishang business families (comparison):** ties kept across the strait for work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for guanxi theory and elder surveys; mixed for ordinary friendship-making; thin for Indigenous ties beyond ceremony descriptions.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Amis, Paiwan, classical Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, Thai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** vote-buying and patronage networks (letter to Libra); some ceremonies restrict outsiders; elders' isolation is a welfare subject.
- **Boundary:** friendship and community go to Aquarius; belonging to Cancer.
