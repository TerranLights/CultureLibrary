*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka (including the Zhao'an variety), Indigenous languages; Japanese and classical Chinese for colonial and Qing records. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and the treatment of people who show two faces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhao'an Hakka in Yunlin and other Hoklo-speaking Hakka:** a heritage carried under another language.
- **Siraya, Taivoan and Makatao descendants in the south:** reclaimed identities beside registered ones.
- **Mainlander second and third generations:** belonging to a home province never seen and to the island at once.
- **The Japanese-schooled oldest generation:** two school languages and two state loyalties in one life.
- **Indigenous people in cities:** a Han-style name at work and a people's own name at home, by personal choice.
- **Children of Vietnamese, Thai and Filipino mothers:** two home languages and two sets of grandparents.
- **Christian Indigenous communities:** church practice beside ancestral rites, to be described as each community does.
- **Taishang families who live on both sides of the strait (comparison):** two places of work and residence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national identity surveys, which may drown quieter forms of two-ness (Hakka under Hoklo, Plains identities, folk-religion pairs).
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka (Zhao'an), Japanese, classical Chinese, Siraya revival materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national identity is politically charged and must be handled as variation, never a single answer; Qing and colonial ethnic labels are slurs in places and are cited only as historical terms; Plains Indigenous recognition is contested.
- **Law:** nationality, recognition and naming rules to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
