# Taiwan: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Paiwan, Amis and other Indigenous languages; Japanese and classical Chinese for household registers and division deeds. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and the local understanding of the sibling bond: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paiwan, with Rukai beside them:** succession to the house by birth order, and how far sex enters it in each people, to set beside Han eldest-son norms.
- **Amis:** household and kin roles that differ from Han patrilines, to be described as Amis communities do.
- **Meinong and Miaoli Hakka:** joint households and the timing of division.
- **Hoklo farm families in Changhua and Yunlin:** division of shrinking plots among brothers.
- **Taipei and New Taipei apartment families:** one or two children, and adult siblings in different cities.
- **Mainlander families:** siblings left on the mainland in 1949 and met again decades later.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** kin roles in boat groups and fishing.
- **New immigrant families:** siblings in two countries, and older children who translate for a parent.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Han family structure from classic ethnography; mixed for current adult sibling relations; thin for most Indigenous peoples beyond older kinship surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Paiwan, Amis, Tao, Japanese, classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** adoption of future daughters-in-law and its effects on living people; household registers hold personal data and have restricted access; sisters' inheritance is a live family conflict.
- **Law:** inheritance and adoption rules to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. NTU Library or Academia Sinica: search 鬮書 and 鬮分 in digitized Taiwanese old documents.
2. SRDA: PSFD variables on 兄弟姊妹 往來 and 奉養父母.
3. Library catalog: Cohen 1976 and Wolf 1968, and later restudies of the same communities.
4. Ministry of Finance: 遺產稅 by heir's sex.
5. National Central Library periodicals: 排灣族 長嗣 家屋 and 手足關係 臺灣.
