# Taiwan: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Atayal, Seediq, Paiwan and other Indigenous languages; classical Chinese and Japanese for Qing and colonial records; Southeast Asian languages for broker disputes. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Atayal, Seediq and Truku:** whether ancestral norms and kin groups, rather than one chief, carry the settling of quarrels; to test against other peoples.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** hereditary nobles as possible arbiters.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** disputes in a small island society, settled by whom.
- **Hoklo towns with strong temples (Lukang, Beigang, Tainan):** temple committees and gods as go-betweens.
- **Hakka villages in Taoyuan, Hsinchu and Miaoli:** memories of feuds and the brokers of peace between groups.
- **Urban high-rise residents:** building management committees instead of borough chiefs.
- **Migrant workers and their brokers:** a paid go-between who may favor one side.
- **New immigrant families:** children and NGOs as go-betweens with institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal mediation counts; mixed for everyday go-betweens; thin for Indigenous practice today, as against older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Atayal, Seediq, Truku, Paiwan, classical Chinese, Japanese, Vietnamese and Tagalog.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputes in archives may name living families; broker abuses are a labor-rights issue; Indigenous dispute customs are each people's to describe.
- **Law:** the letter of mediation and family-court rules goes to Libra; the spirit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
