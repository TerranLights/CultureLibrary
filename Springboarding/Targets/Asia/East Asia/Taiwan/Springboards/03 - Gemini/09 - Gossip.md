# Taiwan: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; Japanese and classical Chinese for colonial and Qing records. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small Hoklo farm towns in Changhua and Pingtung against Taipei apartment blocks:** the weight of neighbors' talk by community size.
- **Hakka villages in Miaoli:** kin networks and the reach of reputation.
- **Small Indigenous villages (for example Bunun or Tsou settlements):** gossip and shame in tight communities, as residents describe them.
- **Tao on Orchid Island:** island-scale reputation, as residents describe how talk travels.
- **Mainlander military villages before redevelopment:** shared alleys and the talk in them.
- **Migrant workers' dormitories and church groups:** rumor about employers and brokers as warning and protection.
- **New immigrant wives:** talk about them in husbands' villages, and their own networks of talk.
- **Entertainment-industry and political circles in Taipei:** gossip as a professional currency.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tabloid and online forms; thin for everyday face-to-face gossip outside literature and old ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Bunun and Tsou for village work, Japanese, classical Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** named people in tabloid and forum material; cyberbullying of minors; defamation questions (letter to Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
