# Taiwan: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; Vietnamese and Tagalog for newer residents. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taipei renters and young workers:** home as a rented room (雅房, 套房, rooftop add-ons) far from the family home.
- **Central and southern townships (Changhua, Yunlin, Chiayi, Pingtung):** the 透天厝 family house and the home young adults leave for the north.
- **Hakka of the Miaoli hills and of Meinong:** the old compound as a returning point.
- **Mainland-origin families:** a home kept in memory across the strait, and the juancun homes later demolished.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** island home against state housing schemes.
- **Amis and other urban Indigenous families:** a village home on the east coast and a self-built home near the cities.
- **Morakot-relocated Rukai, Paiwan and Bunun communities:** home moved from the mountains.
- **Marriage migrants and live-in caregivers:** home as a household they enter, not one they founded.
- **Kinmen and Matsu islanders:** home places shaped by emigration and the garrison decades, to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and protest history; mixed for what "home" feels like; Indigenous accounts should come from the peoples' own writers and organizations.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese for songs and sayings; Hakka; Indigenous languages; Vietnamese and Tagalog for migrant forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eviction cases are live disputes; Morakot and 921 involve grief; tenancy and land rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Ministry of the Interior's housing statistics for 「住宅狀況調查」 and pull tenure and satisfaction tables.
2. Search newspaper archives (United Daily News, Liberty Times) for 「迫遷」 with 「家」 and 「回憶」, 2010-2025.
3. Search library catalogs for 夏曼·藍波安 and for 「永久屋」 with 原住民.
4. Search 「黃昏的故鄉」 and 「思鄉 台語歌」 for song histories.
5. Search PTT's rental and home-buying boards for 「租屋」 and 「買房 結婚」 to collect everyday wording.
