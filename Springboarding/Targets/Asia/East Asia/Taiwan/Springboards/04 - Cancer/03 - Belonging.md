# Taiwan: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages, Vietnamese and other Southeast Asian languages of newer residents. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo and Hakka:** the two island-born Han groups; compare their language histories and their own accounts of standing.
- **Mainland-origin families, first and later generations:** belonging that shifted across generations.
- **The sixteen recognized Indigenous peoples, each apart:** their self-names and the gap between legal status and felt belonging.
- **Unrecognized and Pingpu peoples:** Siraya, Makatao, Taivoan and others still seeking recognition.
- **Second-generation children of new immigrants (新二代):** two homelands, one school system.
- **Migrant workers:** long stays framed as workers rather than settlers, to check.
- **North and south, city and countryside:** the Taipei label wars.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** islands closer to the mainland coast than to Taiwan.
- **Returning overseas Taiwanese and long-term foreign residents.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich, and politically charged. Identity surveys are abundant; felt belonging in daily life is thinner and needs ethnography and memoirs.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka, Indigenous languages, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Thai and the archipelago languages of migrant workers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national identity is a live political question across the strait; ethnic labels are contested and must be presented as labels; recognition and citizenship rules go to Libra and Capricorn; group membership is each Indigenous people's own to define.
- **Held for review:** none.
