# Taiwan: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; classical Chinese for primers and family instructions. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban professional parents in greater Taipei and Hsinchu's science-park towns:** a place to test the enrichment and cram-school track.
- **Working-class and rural families in the south and east:** check how far grandparents carry daily care while parents work away.
- **Hakka families:** sayings such as 晴耕雨讀 (farm in fair weather, read in rain) as a lead, to check in the families' own words.
- **Indigenous peoples, separately:** Amis age-grades, Bunun infant rites, Tao child-naming, Paiwan and Rukai house upbringing; urban Indigenous families raising children away from their villages.
- **New-immigrant mothers:** Vietnamese and other Southeast Asian mothers raising Taiwanese children, and the schools' and in-laws' expectations.
- **Mainland-origin families:** upbringing in the juancun generation.
- **Religious communities:** Buddhist (Tzu Chi, Fo Guang Shan), Christian (Presbyterian, Catholic) and folk-temple families, each with its own teaching on raising children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Strong child panels, a busy parenting media, deep debate over schooling pressure. Indigenous child-rearing is documented in older ethnography; seek the peoples' own revival and education materials.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese, Hakka, Indigenous languages, classical Chinese; Vietnamese for mothers' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corporal punishment and child-protection cases; children's data in cohort studies; school and child-protection law go to Libra and Capricorn; some rites for infants may not be for outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
