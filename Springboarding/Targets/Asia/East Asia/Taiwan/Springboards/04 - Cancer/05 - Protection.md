## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern coastal towns (Tainan, Donggang, Penghu):** sea-facing guardian rites and emblems to compare with inland practice.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** frontline garrison history and wind-lion guardians; civil defense with a different memory.
- **Big-city households in Taipei and Kaohsiung:** locks, building guards and civil-defense drills.
- **Rural township households:** temple guardians, 土地公 shrines and ward patrols.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** protection against nuclear waste and spirits, in their own words.
- **Truku, Seediq and Atayal:** hunting, territory and defense of land.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** protective roles of chiefly houses, to check.
- **Women and children as named protected groups:** shelter and hotline users.
- **Migrant caregivers and marriage migrants:** who protects them inside the households they serve.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state protection and disaster preparedness; folk protection is rich but scattered across temple, folklore and local sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese (rites and amulet wording), Hakka, Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cross-strait defense is a live security topic; child-abuse and violence cases; some Indigenous protective rites and taboos are not for outsiders; protection orders and criminal law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
