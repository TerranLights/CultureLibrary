# Taiwan: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; classical Chinese and Japanese for older relief records; Southeast Asian languages for migrant workers. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wanhua and Taipei Main Station street communities:** the best-known urban street community; its age and sex mix to check.
- **Kaohsiung and Tainan:** southern heat and a different service network.
- **Women and children leaving violence:** compare NGO-run, church-run and public shelters.
- **Migrant workers:** shelters for those who leave employers; Filipino, Vietnamese, Thai and archipelago workers apart.
- **Hong Kong arrivals:** refuge for the persecuted, newly tested.
- **Disaster-hit communities:** Nantou and Taichung (921), southern mountain villages (Morakot), each with its own temporary-housing memory.
- **Mountain Indigenous peoples (Atayal, Bunun, Tsou):** hunting cabins and field huts as shelter in the mountains.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** dwelling forms and their seasonal uses, read in Tao accounts.
- **Temple pilgrims:** lodging given on the road.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Official homeless counts and NGO reports exist; shelter for migrant workers and Hong Kong arrivals is thinner and uneven; pre-modern relief is in gazetteers and colonial records.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese, classical Chinese, Japanese; Tagalog and Vietnamese for migrant workers' accounts; Tao for Lanyu dwellings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter residents' privacy, above all in violence shelters (locations are secret); migrant workers' legal exposure; asylum and residence rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
