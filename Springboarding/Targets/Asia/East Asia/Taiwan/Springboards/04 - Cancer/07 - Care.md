# Taiwan: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; Vietnamese, Tagalog and the archipelago languages of migrant care workers. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban middle-class families:** a place to compare hired live-in aides, day centers and kin care, and which child manages it.
- **Rural Hoklo and Hakka households:** care by the daughter-in-law, an expectation to check against present practice.
- **Mainland-origin veterans (榮民):** older veterans, some without kin on the island, to check; the Veterans Affairs Council's homes 榮民之家 as a separate care setting.
- **Indigenous villages on the east coast and in the mountains:** elders cared for by kin, health stations and church networks.
- **Migrant caregivers from the Philippines, Vietnam and the archipelago:** caring for other families while their own are cared for back home.
- **Marriage-migrant women:** how often they become the household's main carer, to check.
- **Buddhist, Christian and folk-religious communities:** different ideas of care as merit, service or duty.
- **Offshore islands (Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu, Lanyu):** distance from hospitals and specialist care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Strong aging panels, official care statistics and an active caregivers' movement; migrant carers' own accounts are scattered across NGOs and forums.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese (elders), Hakka, Indigenous languages, Tagalog, Vietnamese and the archipelago workers' language.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** elder abuse, caregiver suicides and murder-suicides in the news; migrant workers' working conditions; labor and care-insurance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the latest 老人狀況調查 report and locate the tables on 主要照顧者.
2. Search the Family Caregivers Association's site for its annual reports and 「照顧者 心聲」.
3. Search the Ministry of Labor statistics site for 「外籍看護工」 by county and nationality.
4. Search the Council of Indigenous Peoples' site for 「文化健康站」 and a list of stations by township.
5. Search library catalogs for "Lan Global Cinderellas" and for 楊力州 「被遺忘的時光」.
