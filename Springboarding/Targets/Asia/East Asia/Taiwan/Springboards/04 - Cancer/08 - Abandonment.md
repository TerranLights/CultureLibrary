# Taiwan: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders in rural Yunlin, Chiayi and the east coast:** test how far the move of the young to the cities has left elders alone.
- **Mainland-origin veterans and their two families:** some with a family left on each side of the strait.
- **Tao of Lanyu and other Indigenous villages:** elders at home while the young work on the main island, to check in their own accounts.
- **Children of new-immigrant mothers:** after divorce, a mother deported or returned, a child left with the father's kin, to check.
- **Migrant workers:** their own children left in the Philippines, Vietnam and the archipelago.
- **Gay and transgender youth:** cast out by family, and their chosen families.
- **Hoklo and Hakka folk practice:** compare how each tends the kinless dead.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** villages thinned by emigration and the garrison drawdown, to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Elder-isolation statistics and NGO reports are good; abandonment inside families is likely under-reported; folk practice around the kinless dead is rich in folklore journals.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese (shrines and songs), Hakka, Indigenous languages; Tagalog and Vietnamese for migrant families left abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abandoned infants and adoptees' privacy; deportation-related family separation; solitary deaths in the news; desertion, support and adoption law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
