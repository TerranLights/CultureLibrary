# Taiwan: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages (Atayal, Amis and others), Vietnamese and Thai for migrant media. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Martial-law generations against post-1987 ones:** the sharpest split for political and linguistic expression.
- **Hokkien speakers:** a language once kept off the airwaves; check how this shaped expression in it.
- **Hakka communities:** sung exchanges and Hakka-language media (Hakka TV) as expressive outlets.
- **Post-1949 mainlander families:** veterans' village memoirs and their own silences.
- **Indigenous peoples:** body marking (Atayal, Seediq, Truku), song (Amis, Puyuma, Bunun), and Tao voices from Orchid Island; each to be asked separately.
- **Pingpu revival groups:** expression of an identity long hidden.
- **LGBTQ Taiwanese:** Taipei against Kaohsiung and smaller towns.
- **Southeast Asian migrant workers and new immigrants:** their own media and gatherings.
- **North and south:** Taipei office culture against southern street politics and campaign rallies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on censorship history, pop music and protest; mixed on family-level emotional display; thin on Pingpu and migrant voices.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien and Hakka; Indigenous terms; Vietnamese, Thai and Tagalog for migrant media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** facial-tattoo motifs and their meaning, as elders share them; coming-out stories; political persecution histories. Free-speech law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
