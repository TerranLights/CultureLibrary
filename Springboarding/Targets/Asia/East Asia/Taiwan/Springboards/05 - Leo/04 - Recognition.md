*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hokkien towns with old lineage halls (Lukang, Tainan, Hsinchu):** degree plaques and donor steles as inherited credit.
- **Hakka villages:** recognition of Hakka contribution through the Hakka Affairs Council and Yimin temple honors.
- **Post-1949 mainlander families:** military honors and service medals as the main recognition channel.
- **Recognized Indigenous peoples:** state honors against community-given standing (Amis age-grades; Paiwan and Rukai rank).
- **Peoples awaiting recognition:** Siraya, Taivoan, Makatao and other Pingpu groups.
- **Southeast Asian migrant workers and new immigrants:** acknowledged or invisible labor.
- **Science-park firms against small family businesses:** bonuses and awards against personal thanks.
- **Schoolchildren and parents:** the exam-driven recognition system as its own world.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on formal awards and school honors; mixed on workplace credit; thin on how recognition is given inside Indigenous communities without outside mediation.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Classical Chinese for steles and plaques; Hokkien and Hakka for everyday praise words; Indigenous terms for passage ceremonies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of peoples is politically charged; Pingpu claims are ongoing. The legal side of recognition belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
