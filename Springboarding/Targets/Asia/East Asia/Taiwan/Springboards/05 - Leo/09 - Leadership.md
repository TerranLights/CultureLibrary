*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hokkien townships:** temple committees, faction bosses and village chiefs; north and south to be checked separately.
- **Hakka villages:** lineage and clan-association leadership in Miaoli, Hsinchu and Liudui.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** hereditary ranked houses.
- **Amis and Puyuma:** age-grade leadership.
- **Atayal, Truku, Seediq and Bunun:** leaders by ability and consensus, as each people describes it.
- **Tao of Orchid Island:** village and fishing-group leadership, as the Tao share it.
- **Post-1949 mainlanders and veterans:** military and party hierarchies.
- **Firms:** family-owned small businesses against science-park multinationals.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** lineage-village leadership on the islands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on management research and elections; mixed on Indigenous leadership (older outsider ethnography, newer community work); thin on informal neighborhood and work-crew leaders.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka; Paiwan, Atayal, Puyuma, Bunun terms; Japanese for colonial records on headmen (頭目).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leader cults remain politically divisive; local politics touches organized crime. Formal authority and the right to command go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
