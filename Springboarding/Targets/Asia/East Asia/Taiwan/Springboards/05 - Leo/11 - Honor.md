# Taiwan: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages (Atayal, Seediq, Truku and others), Classical Chinese, Japanese. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hokkien lineage towns (Lukang, Tainan, Hsinchu):** arches, genealogies and family name.
- **Hakka communities:** the Yimin (義民) dead honored for defending the settlements; Hakka lineage halls.
- **Post-1949 mainlander and military families:** service honor, the five beliefs, veterans' memory.
- **Atayal, Seediq and Truku:** Gaga and Gaya codes.
- **Paiwan, Rukai and Puyuma:** rank, deeds and the honoring of chiefs and hunters.
- **Families of Japanese-era soldiers:** honor and loyalty that later regimes judged differently.
- **Urban working-class men:** street and gang codes, sworn brotherhood.
- **Women:** reputations and family honor across generations; a pattern to test, not assume.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** lineage honor in single-surname villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on face research, Qing honors and military history; mixed on gang and street codes; thin on how Indigenous peoples' codes work today, outside revival settings.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka; Classical Chinese; Japanese; Atayal, Seediq and Truku terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting history behind some Indigenous honors; colonial-war service; organized crime. Defamation law goes to Libra; duty goes to Capricorn.
