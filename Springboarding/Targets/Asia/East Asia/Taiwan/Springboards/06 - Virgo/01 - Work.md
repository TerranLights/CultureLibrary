# Taiwan: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese for 1895 to 1945. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Export-zone and factory women of the 1960s to 1980s** against their daughters in offices: the generational change in what a job meant.
- **Science-park engineers (Hsinchu, Tainan, Taichung)** against small-firm and family-shop owners: salaried overwork against owner-operated work.
- **Hoklo rice and fruit farmers of the south (Chiayi, Tainan, Pingtung)** against northern Hakka tea farmers: different crop calendars and songs.
- **Tao of Lanyu and coastal Amis:** subsistence fishing and communal labor beside wage work in Taiwan's cities.
- **Indigenous workers in construction and high-rise steel work**, a labor path in Taipei and beyond, to separate by people (Amis, Paiwan, Atayal).
- **Retired soldiers (榮民):** road building, farms and state firms.
- **Migrant workers by nationality and sector:** home caregivers, factory crews and distant-water fishing crews; comparison only.
- **Penghu and Kinmen:** island fishing and emigration for work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for statistics, labor history and literature; mixed for Indigenous wage labor; thin for Pingpu peoples' work histories.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, the Indigenous languages, and the migrants' languages for their writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor under colonial rule; abuse of distant-water fishing crews; workplace deaths; martial-law suppression of unions. Labor law (hours rules, the 2016 rest-day reform) goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. In the Ministry of Labor portal, pull hours and injury series by industry and county, and search 過勞 for its overwork pages.
2. Read the prize-winning entries of the migrants' literature award for any year, in the Chinese translations.
3. Search Airiti for 黑手變頭家 and 謝國雄 to follow the work-to-ownership literature.
4. Search the Hokkien song booklets for 勸世 and 貧惰 at the National Taiwan University library catalog.
5. Search PTT Tech_Job for 責任制 and 爆肝 to see current language.
