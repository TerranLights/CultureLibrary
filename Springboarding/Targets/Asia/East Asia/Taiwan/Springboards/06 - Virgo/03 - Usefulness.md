*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural grandparents raising grandchildren** (central and southern counties, Indigenous townships) against urban retirees: different expected roles in old age.
- **Indigenous elders:** separate Tao, Bunun, Atayal and Amis, where language and land knowledge make elders central teachers in different ways.
- **Hoklo farm families** against **Hakka families of the Liudui area:** sons' and daughters' expected help on farm and in shop.
- **Mainlander veterans who lived alone** in veterans' homes (榮民之家): old age without kin nearby.
- **Disabled adults and their families:** separate those in sheltered workshops from those at home.
- **Young urban workers and graduates:** the "strawberry" label and its rejection.
- **Migrant caregivers** who keep frail elders at home: comparison of who is useful to whom in a household.
- **Penghu and Matsu:** aging islands where the young leave for work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for aging and disability (strong surveys and care programs); mixed for youth uselessness talk; thin for Pingpu peoples and the outer islands.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese for land and school records, the Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life choices (the patient-autonomy rules go to Libra); elder suicide data, to be handled with care; disability labels; land taken as "unused".
- **Held for review:** none.
