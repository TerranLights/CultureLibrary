*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese and classical Chinese for older records. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Science-park engineers (Hsinchu, Tainan) and their families** against **farm and fishing households**: whose decisions run on measurement and whose on long experience.
- **Temple-goers who decide by divination** against secular professionals: ways of knowing side by side, often in one person.
- **Tao, Bunun and Atayal** knowledge-holders: Indigenous systems of observing land, sea and weather, each separately.
- **Kinmen:** a strong local memory of imperial examination success, *check*.
- **Hakka and Hoklo farm towns** against Taipei: schooling and cram-school density.
- **Mainlander academic and military families:** ties to the scholars who moved with Academia Sinica.
- **Civic-tech volunteers** against older television audiences: different trust in data and pundits.
- **New immigrant mothers:** their children's math schooling and their own schooling abroad, as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for education, science policy and civic data; mixed for everyday reasoning styles; thin and sensitive for Indigenous knowledge systems.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, classical Chinese, Japanese, Hokkien, Hakka, Tao, Bunun, Atayal.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous knowledge belongs to its holders; some is not for outsiders. Thinking-style studies risk stereotype and are read as claims, not findings.
- **Held for review:** none.
