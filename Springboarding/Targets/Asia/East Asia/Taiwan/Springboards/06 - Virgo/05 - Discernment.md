## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo tea growers of Lugu** against **Hakka tea growers of Beipu and Emei:** two regional schools of tasting and grading.
- **Older adults** against **students and young workers:** different scams, different channels (phone, LINE groups, dating apps), different trusted advisers.
- **Atayal, Seediq and Truku:** sign-reading and omen traditions, to separate by people; **Amis and Paiwan** for elders' counsel in choosing partners and leaders.
- **Buddhist lay followers, folk-religion temple-goers and Christians:** different routes to spiritual discernment (right view, divination and counsel, prayer).
- **Taipei collectors and dealers** against market buyers in country towns.
- **Kinmen:** distilling and liquor-tasting culture around its sorghum spirit.
- **New immigrants:** how newcomers learn to judge goods, prices and people; comparison only.
- **Mainlander families** with ties to the palace-collection and literati world.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for fraud, disinformation and tea; mixed for everyday people-judging; thin for Indigenous sign-reading outside the northern peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, classical Chinese, Hokkien, Hakka, Atayal, Seediq, Truku.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some omen and dream knowledge may be restricted to its holders; scam victims' shame; avoid any national stereotype about scams. Fraud law goes to Libra.
