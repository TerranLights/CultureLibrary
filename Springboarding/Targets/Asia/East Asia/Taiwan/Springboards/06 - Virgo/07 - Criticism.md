## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **People who grew up under martial law** against those raised after 1987: what could be said aloud and where.
- **Political camps:** criticism of leaders across party lines, without assuming any group's style.
- **Amis, Atayal, Paiwan and Tao communities:** separate correction customs, from age-set discipline to public reprimand by elders.
- **Presbyterian congregations** (many Hoklo and Indigenous) against Buddhist and folk-religion groups: criticism of the state against inward self-examination.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** under military administration until the early 1990s, a different history of what could be said.
- **Workplaces:** family firms, Japanese-influenced firms and tech companies, with their review meetings (檢討會).
- **PTT and Dcard users** against older LINE-group audiences: different norms of public criticism.
- **New immigrants and migrant workers:** how they voice complaints, and how they are criticized; comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for political dissent, media and online criticism; mixed for workplace feedback; thin for Indigenous correction customs and for Hakka-specific sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, classical Chinese, Hokkien, Hakka, Amis, Atayal.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** White Terror victims and families; online shaming linked to suicide (handle with great care); current party politics. Speech and defamation law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
