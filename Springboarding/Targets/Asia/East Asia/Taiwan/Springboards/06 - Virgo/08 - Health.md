*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo temple-goers** who use 收驚 and medicine slips against **secular urban patients:** different routes through illness, often used together.
- **Hakka herbal traditions** in Miaoli and Meinong: to separate from Hoklo herb-shop practice.
- **Indigenous peoples by people:** Amis, Paiwan, Bunun, Atayal, Truku and Tao each have their own healers, causes of illness and clinic access.
- **Pingpu revival communities (Siraya):** ritual specialists and their present role.
- **Lanyu and the outer islands (Penghu, Kinmen, Matsu):** distance from hospitals.
- **Mainlander veterans:** the veterans' hospital system (榮總).
- **Presbyterian and Catholic mission hospitals' patients**, especially in the east.
- **Migrant workers and new immigrant mothers:** access to care and postpartum customs from their own countries, as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich (strong statistics, a long medical-anthropology record); mixed for folk healing today; thin for Pingpu healing and for smaller Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, English (missionary records), Amis, Paiwan, Tao, Bunun.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous healing rites and healers' knowledge are shared only as communities allow; mental illness, suicide and HIV stigma; nuclear-waste harm. Medical law and patient-rights law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
