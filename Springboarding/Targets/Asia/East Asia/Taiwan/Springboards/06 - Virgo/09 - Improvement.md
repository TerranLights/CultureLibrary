# Taiwan: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese for assimilation-era records; classical Chinese for self-cultivation texts. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo, Hakka and Mainlander families:** mobility through schooling, business or state jobs; separate by town and class, never assumed.
- **Indigenous peoples:** Atayal, Amis, Paiwan, Tao and Thao (whose lakeside land was flooded for the Sun Moon Lake power project) each faced imposed change and lead their own renewal.
- **Pingpu peoples:** recognition campaigns as a form of renewal.
- **Residents displaced by renewal:** Losheng's patients, Treasure Hill's veteran settlers, residents of older Taipei blocks.
- **Farmers of the Chianan plain** against **eastern farmers:** different histories of irrigation and land policy.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** change after military administration ended.
- **New immigrant spouses and their children:** schooling and literacy programs, as comparison.
- **Young self-optimizers online** against those who reject "striving".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for development history, mobility surveys and assimilation policy; mixed for everyday self-help; thin for Pingpu peoples and the outer islands.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, classical Chinese, the Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colonial and postwar assimilation and language punishment; displacement by renewal; memory of Japanese rule is politically charged. Land and language law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
