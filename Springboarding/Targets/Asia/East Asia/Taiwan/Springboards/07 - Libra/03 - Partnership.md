*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan languages; Vietnamese and Bahasa Indonesia for marriage-migrant sources. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amis and Puyuma:** house-centered residence in which husbands may join the wife's house; partnership seen from the other side of the usual Han default.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** marriage as alliance between ranked houses; which partner's rank passes on.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** the boat crew and the household as working partnerships.
- **Hoklo and Hakka uxorilocal marriage (招贅):** a minority form with its own terms for the husband and children.
- **Cross-border couples:** Vietnamese, mainland Chinese and Indonesian spouses, and the different residence rules they face.
- **Same-sex couples:** married since 2019, with later changes for couples with foreign partners.
- **Family-firm couples:** small manufacturers in Changhua, Taichung and Tainan.
- **Veterans who came in 1949:** many married late; a cohort to check, not to assume.
- **Households split across the strait or the ocean (business families, couples living apart):** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, survey and family-firm studies; mixed for Indigenous marriage alliances (largely older ethnography); thin for older Pingpu marriage forms.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Hakka for songs and family speech; Amis, Puyuma, Paiwan, Rukai, Tao; Vietnamese and Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex couples' privacy; cross-border marriage brokerage carries stigma; rank in Paiwan and Rukai houses is a matter for those houses.
- **Held for review:** none.
