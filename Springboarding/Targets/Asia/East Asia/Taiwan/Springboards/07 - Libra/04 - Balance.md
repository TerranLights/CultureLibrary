*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tao of Lanyu:** the yearly order of fishing and rest, kept apart from main-island practice.
- **Bunun:** the millet calendar and polyphonic singing as sources on harmony.
- **Atayal, Seediq and Truku hunters:** limits on hunting, set against state wildlife rules.
- **Hoklo and Hakka households:** winter-tonic and cooling-food customs may differ by family and region; check, never assume.
- **Hsinchu Science Park engineers and service workers:** the hours debate looks different from each side.
- **Farmers and fishers:** a year paced by seasons and the almanac's solar terms.
- **Children of marriage migrants:** living between two languages.
- **Party camps (blue, green, white) and the unaligned:** balance in government.
- **Taiwanese abroad:** comparison only, for tonic-food and rest customs kept in another climate and work culture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor hours, politics and health regulation; mixed for folk medicine (many studies, scattered); thin for Indigenous ecological rules outside community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for herb shops and tonic talk; Hakka; Tao and Bunun.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tao and Bunun ritual calendars hold restricted parts; media-license rulings are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
