# Taiwan: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan languages; Classical Chinese for Qing administration. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** hereditary houses and elected officials side by side; what impartial leadership means in each order.
- **Amis:** age-set leaders and their duties to all members of a grade.
- **Atayal and Seediq:** how leaders are chosen under *gaga* and *gaya*, and whether standing is earned or inherited, as each people describes it.
- **County political factions:** north, central and southern counties, and Hakka townships, to see how local patronage differs.
- **Small-island communities (Penghu, Lanyu, Green Island):** recusal is hardest where everyone is related.
- **Civil servants and teachers:** formal neutrality rules apply to them first.
- **Family business groups:** succession and hiring of kin.
- **Temple committees:** leadership chosen by lot or by blocks.
- **Overseas compatriot networks:** comparison only, for how connections work far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, election procedure and corruption surveys; mixed for the everyday use of connections (mostly scholarship and journalism); thin for customary leadership as each Indigenous people describes it.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for local politics and temple life; Hakka; Paiwan, Rukai and Amis for leadership terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** named corruption cases involve living people; Paiwan and Rukai house rank is the houses' own matter.
- **Held for review:** none.
