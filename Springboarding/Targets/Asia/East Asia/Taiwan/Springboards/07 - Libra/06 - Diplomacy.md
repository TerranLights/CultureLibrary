# Taiwan: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan languages; Dutch, English and Japanese for older diplomatic records. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paiwan, including the Seqalu chiefdoms of the Hengchun peninsula:** the main Indigenous actors in the 1867 and 1874 negotiations.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** relations with the state over the nuclear-waste site on their island.
- **Amis and other east-coast peoples:** who takes part in Austronesian exchanges with Pacific partners, and on whose terms.
- **Hoklo and Hakka:** nineteenth-century feuds and the settlements made between them; differences in household etiquette to check.
- **Mainlander families with kin across the strait:** family diplomacy after visits resumed in 1987.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** daily dealings with the facing coast since the 2001 "mini three links".
- **Brokers and migrant workers:** negotiation across languages, often through intermediaries.
- **Children of marriage migrants:** language brokering for parents.
- **Overseas compatriot associations (僑團):** comparison only, as informal channels beside the state's diplomacy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and cross-strait talks; rich for nineteenth-century encounters (Dutch, Qing, Japanese and American records); mixed for everyday tact (linguistics and etiquette writing); thin for Indigenous diplomatic protocol in its own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for temple protocol; Hakka; Paiwan and Amis; Dutch, Japanese and English for archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cross-strait and sovereignty wording is contested; descendants of the 1867 and 1874 parties may object to outside retellings.
- **Held for review:** none.
