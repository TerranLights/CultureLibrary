# Taiwan: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (Traditional), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan languages; Classical Chinese and Japanese for older images of others. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo, Hakka and Mainlander families:** images each holds of the others, shaped by 1947 and by language politics; collect as images, never as truths.
- **Indigenous peoples and Han neighbors:** stereotypes in both directions, and Indigenous writers' answers to them.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** misfortune explained through *anito*, and the nuclear-waste site as a present-day grievance.
- **Donggang, Xigang and other royal-lord festival towns:** expulsion rites in full.
- **Marriage migrants and migrant workers:** frequent targets of blame in media.
- **Online partisan camps:** mutual accusations of paid trolling.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** images of the facing coast held by people who live within sight of it.
- **Pentecostal and charismatic churches:** talk of demons and spiritual enemies.
- **Taiwanese communities abroad:** comparison only, for the images of home-island groups they carry.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martial-law archives, disinformation research and media studies; mixed for folk explanations of misfortune; thin for measured projection inside couples.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for temple and folk-healing practice; Hakka; Tao; Classical Chinese for Qing texts; Japanese for colonial images.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martial-law files name informers and victims; ethnic images can wound and must be quoted only as recorded claims; Tao spiritual concepts belong to the Tao.
- **Held for review:** none.
