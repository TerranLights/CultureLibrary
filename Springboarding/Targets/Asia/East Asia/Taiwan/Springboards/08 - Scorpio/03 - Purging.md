*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southwestern coastal Hoklo towns (Pingtung, Tainan, Chiayi):** plague-boat rites are strongest there.
- **Changhua and Lukang:** exorcism after violent deaths.
- **Hakka of Liudui and the northwest:** cleansing herbs, bathing customs and avoidance after funerals, compared with Hoklo.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** purification under hereditary ritual specialists.
- **Tao of Lanyu:** avoidance and cleansing around the dead and around the nuclear-waste site on their island.
- **Christian Indigenous congregations:** where older cleansing rites were set aside or reworked.
- **Families of White Terror victims and Mainlander officials' families:** the same purge remembered from opposite sides.
- **Industrial towns (Taoyuan, Kaohsiung):** pollution and cleanup as working-class experience.
- **Online generations:** Dcard and PTT users against older users of LINE groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on temple rites, transitional justice and pollution cases; mixed on household and bodily purging; thin on Indigenous cleansing beyond older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for rites; Japanese for colonial policy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** transitional justice is politically divisive; present both readings; exorcism after suicide involves grieving families; Indigenous rites may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
