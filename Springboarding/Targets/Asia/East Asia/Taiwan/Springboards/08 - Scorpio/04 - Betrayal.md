# Taiwan: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese for the colonial period. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Benshengren (本省人) and Mainlander families:** the same 1947 and 1950s events carry opposite charges of treachery.
- **Hoklo and Hakka in the southwest and northwest:** the loyalist-rebel memory of the 1780s still shapes temple rites.
- **Seediq groups (Tgdaya, Toda, Truku):** whose ancestors turned on whom in 1930-1931 is a living matter.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** frontline islands where defection ran both ways.
- **Military-village families:** loyalty to the party-state and its unraveling.
- **Tech and factory workers:** loyalty to firms and the poaching debate.
- **Women and men:** the double standard on infidelity as a question to test.
- **Southeast Asian spouses:** suspicion of betrayal in arranged cross-border marriages.
- **Comparison only:** overseas Taiwanese activists once watched by informers on campuses abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on political betrayal, defection and infidelity in media; mixed on everyday betrayal; thin on Indigenous concepts of broken custom outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for slang and dramas; Japanese for colonial records; Seediq and Atayal terms through community dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informers' names in released files; Wushe memories among descendants; abuse accounts need care; cross-Strait framing is politically charged.
