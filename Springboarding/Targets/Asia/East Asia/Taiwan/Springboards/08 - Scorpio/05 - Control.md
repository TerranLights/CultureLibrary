# Taiwan: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo and Hakka parents:** family authority and daughters' freedom, to test rather than assume.
- **Mainlander military-village families:** discipline shaped by army life.
- **Mountain Indigenous peoples (Bunun, Atayal, Truku, Seediq):** control of hunting grounds, guns and movement, colonial and modern.
- **Amis and other matrilineal or bilateral peoples:** who controls the household when inheritance runs through women.
- **Southeast Asian caregivers and factory workers:** control by employers and brokers.
- **Marriage migrants from Vietnam and Indonesia:** control inside the household and by in-laws.
- **Students in the exam track:** control of time by schools and cram schools.
- **Farmers on irrigated plains (Chianan, Changhua, Yilan):** water schedules set by others.
- **Comparison only:** "parachute kids" sent abroad from Taiwan, a case of control loosened at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on family control (indigenous psychology), martial-law censorship and migrant-worker conditions; mixed on fate and day-selection; thin on Indigenous concepts of self-restraint.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for fate vocabulary; Japanese for colonial records; Vietnamese, Indonesian and Tagalog for workers' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence accounts; martial-law files naming people; Indigenous hunting disputes.
- **Law:** protection orders, stalking and hunting law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
