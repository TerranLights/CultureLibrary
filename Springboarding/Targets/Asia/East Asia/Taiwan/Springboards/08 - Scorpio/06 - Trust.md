# Taiwan: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Taipei and rural townships:** strangers against acquaintances as the default for credit and help.
- **Hoklo small-business families:** trust inside family firms and the guarantor problem.
- **Hakka corporate lineages:** pooled funds under elected managers.
- **Mainlander military villages:** trust built in closed, mutually known communities.
- **Tao of Lanyu and Amis of the east coast:** cooperative labor and shared catch.
- **Atayal and Truku communities:** gaga and gaya as rules that make dealings trustworthy, as the peoples describe them.
- **Elderly LINE users:** the most targeted by rumors and scams.
- **Southeast Asian workers and spouses:** trust in brokers, employers and their own community networks.
- **Partisan camps:** trust in institutions split along political lines.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwanese American businesses relying on credit among fellow migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on institutional and generalized trust (several surveys), food-safety and fraud; mixed on particular trust inside families; thin on Indigenous concepts outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for proverbs and village credit; Hakka for lineage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in institutions is partisan; present both camps; fraud victims' accounts need care.
- **Law:** trust in police and courts is the spirit of law, under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
