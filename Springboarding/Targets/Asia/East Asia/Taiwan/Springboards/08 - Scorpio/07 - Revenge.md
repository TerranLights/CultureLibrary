*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zhangzhou-descended and Quanzhou-descended Hoklo:** old feud lines that may survive in temple rivalries.
- **Hakka of Liudui:** militias and memory of clashes with neighbors.
- **Seediq, Atayal, Truku, Paiwan, Bunun and Tsou:** past headhunting differed in meaning, rules and endings; each people's own account comes first.
- **Saisiyat:** a rite built on wrong and appeasement.
- **Urban gang neighborhoods (Wanhua, Sanchong, parts of Kaohsiung):** codes of payback in 角頭 culture.
- **Buddhist and I-Kuan Tao communities:** teaching on karma as a replacement for revenge.
- **Christian congregations:** forgiveness teaching, including among Indigenous churches.
- **Women and men:** whose revenge is told as madness and whose as honor.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwanese American communities in California, where the 1984 killing took place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich in folklore, opera, film and serial drama; mixed on everyday petty revenge; sensitive and uneven on headhunting history.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for opera, oaths and ghost lore; Japanese for colonial headhunting records; Indigenous languages for the peoples' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting must be framed through the peoples' own scholars and elders; paSta'ay has taboos for visitors; the 1980 murders touch a living family.
- **Law:** private vengeance set against state punishment is Capricorn's.
