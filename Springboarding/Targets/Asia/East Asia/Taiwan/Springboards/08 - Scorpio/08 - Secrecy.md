# Taiwan: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese for household registers. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is a facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo and Hakka lineages:** adoption, minor marriage and what children were told.
- **Mainlander families:** wives and children left on the mainland, and second families formed in Taiwan.
- **White Terror families:** silence kept to protect children, and when it broke.
- **Tongzhi communities in Taipei, Kaohsiung and smaller towns:** the closet differs by place and generation.
- **Paiwan and Rukai noble houses, Tsou and Saisiyat ritual groups:** knowledge restricted by house or role.
- **I-Kuan Tao families and other once-banned groups:** worship hidden for decades.
- **Temple troupes and their neighborhoods:** in-group knowledge and its edges.
- **Undocumented migrant workers ("runaway" workers):** hiding as survival.
- **Comparison only:** overseas Taiwanese dissidents of the blacklist era.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on political secrecy, tabloid culture and tongzhi history; mixed on family secrets (mostly memoir and fiction); thin and rightly closed on restricted Indigenous knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for family talk; Japanese for registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted Indigenous knowledge is recorded only as the peoples allow; household registers touch living families; undocumented workers must not be exposed.
- **Law:** privacy and archives law go to Libra.
