# Taiwan: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages; Japanese for scholarship on Taiwanese politics. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Counties with long faction rivalries** (e.g., Changhua, Taichung, Kaohsiung, Pingtung): local power divided differently in each.
- **Hoklo temple towns:** power through temple boards.
- **Hakka townships in Taoyuan, Hsinchu, Miaoli and Liudui:** their place between larger groups at the ballot box, as a question.
- **Mainlander officials' and veterans' communities:** the core of the old party-state and its loss of power.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** hereditary nobles and commoners.
- **Tsou:** ritual authority in the men's house.
- **Amis and Tao:** whether age grades or kin-based crews spread power differently, to be tested.
- **Kinmen and Matsu residents:** decades of army rule.
- **Women in politics:** reserved seats for women and what they change about who holds power.
- **Factory towns near conglomerate sites:** one employer's weight.
- **Comparison only:** Taiwanese business networks abroad and their ties to home politics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on factions, elections, movements and conglomerates; mixed on hidden power (gangs, temples); uneven on Indigenous chieftainship by people.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for local politics; Japanese for some scholarship; Paiwan and Tsou for terms of rank.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** party politics is divisive; present each camp's account; claims about gang ties name living people; chiefly privileges are a live matter inside communities.
- **Law:** authority and the spirit of the law are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.
