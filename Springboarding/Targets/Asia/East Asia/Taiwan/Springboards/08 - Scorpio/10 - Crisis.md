# Taiwan: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Taiwan towns hit in 1999** (Nantou, Taichung's mountain districts): earthquake memory.
- **Southern mountain villages** (Siraya and Taivoan-descended Xiaolin, Rukai and Paiwan of Pingtung): relocation as crisis.
- **East coast Amis and Truku communities:** earthquakes and isolation from the capital.
- **Kinmen and Matsu:** generations raised under bombardment.
- **Southern hog-farming and fishing towns:** livelihood crises.
- **Young urban debtors:** consumer credit and its collapse.
- **Families who emigrated after 1971 and 1979:** crisis met by leaving.
- **Migrant workers:** whether warnings and relief reach them in their own languages.
- **Hakka and Hoklo temple communities:** religious explanations and processions after disasters, to be compared.
- **Comparison only:** diaspora fundraising networks after 1999 and 2009.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on disasters, preparedness and diplomatic crises; mixed on household and personal crises; thinner on Indigenous accounts outside Morakot.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for oral histories; Japanese for 1935 records; Rukai, Paiwan and Amis for affected villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims' families and memorial disputes; cross-Strait war preparation is politically charged; crisis-line data is aggregated only.
- **Held for review:** none.
