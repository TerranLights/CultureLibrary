# Taiwan: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages, Japanese (colonial records). **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo temple devotees** in the Mazu and Wang Ye networks of the southwest, against northern city temples (Longshan, Xiahai City God) for urban devotion.
- **Hakka villages** of the north and of Liudui in Pingtung, for Yimin and earth-god devotion bound to lineage.
- **Indigenous peoples, people by people:** Truku and Atayal Presbyterians, Paiwan and Rukai Catholics, Amis of several churches, Tao on Orchid Island; affiliation differs by village, so it is to be measured.
- **Pingpu peoples** reviving older devotion (Siraya ancestral vessels; Kavalan rites).
- **Han Christians,** separated so that their handling of ancestral rites can be studied directly.
- **Mainlander families,** for Guanyin devotion, Yiguandao and the sects brought in 1949.
- **Filipino Catholic and Muslim Southeast Asian migrant workers,** with their own congregations.
- **Generations,** young and old, in cities and in the countryside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temple devotion, Buddhist movements and church history; mixed for Indigenous faith (many accounts by missionaries); measured data exists but uses categories that blur folk religion with Buddhism and Daoism.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese for vows and temple speech; Hakka; Truku, Paiwan, Amis and other Formosan languages; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions of conversion and ancestral rites touch family duty; Indigenous ceremonies only as the peoples share them; abuse or money scandals in religious bodies need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
