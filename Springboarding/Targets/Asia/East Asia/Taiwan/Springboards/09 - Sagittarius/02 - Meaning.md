# Taiwan: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young urban workers** in Taipei and the Hsinchu science parks against those in Tainan, Chiayi or Yilan, for work and pay as sources or drains of meaning.
- **Farmers and fishing families** (Yunlin, Pingtung, Penghu) whose work ties a life to place.
- **Tao, Bunun, Seediq and Amis** voices, each read separately, for meaning bound to sea, mountains and ancestral norms.
- **Hakka towns** such as Meinong and Beipu.
- **Veterans and mainlander elders (榮民)** whose lives were set by 1949.
- **Religious and secular Taiwanese,** separated, to test whether their sources of meaning differ.
- **Southeast Asian spouses and migrant workers,** whose own writing (the migrant literature award) speaks to purpose abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in essay, fiction, film and Buddhist publishing; measured data thinner (well-being indexes rather than meaning scales).
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese for songs and sayings; Hakka; Tao, Bunun and Seediq for their own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and despair need careful handling; the spirit-bridge belief belongs to the Seediq and Truku to explain.
- **Held for review:** none.
