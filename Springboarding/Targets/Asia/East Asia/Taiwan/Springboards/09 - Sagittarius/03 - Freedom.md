# Taiwan: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages, Japanese (1895-1945 sources). **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of political prisoners** and 228 victims, against those who served the old order, to compare how the unfree years are remembered.
- **Mainlander, Hoklo and Hakka families,** separated, to test whether their stories of 1947, 1949 and martial law differ.
- **Indigenous peoples,** one by one, to read freedom framed through land, name and self-rule: the Seediq (Wushe), Tao (Orchid Island), Atayal and others.
- **Generations:** those who lived under martial law against those born after 1987.
- **Christians, Buddhists and Yiguandao members,** each with its own history of restriction.
- **LGBTQ Taiwanese,** for the freedom to live openly.
- **Migrant workers,** for freedom of movement and of changing employer.
- **Outlying islands (Kinmen, Matsu),** whose military administration ended later (1992 *check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; memory institutions, oral histories and surveys abound; the main risk is partisan framing, so read across camps.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese for slogans and songs; Hakka; Seediq and other Formosan languages; Japanese for the colonial movements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims' files and families' privacy; the independence and unification question is charged; legal questions on rights go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
