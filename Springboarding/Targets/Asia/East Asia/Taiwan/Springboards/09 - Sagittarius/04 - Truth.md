# Taiwan: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Formosan Indigenous languages, Japanese (colonial press). **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older adults in LINE family groups** against young users of PTT, Dcard and Instagram, for different routes by which claims arrive.
- **Supporters of the major parties,** separated, since their news diets and trusted outlets may differ.
- **Indigenous peoples,** each with its own oral record (Atayal lmuhuw, Paiwan and Rukai noble genealogies, Tao stories), weighed against written history.
- **Temple communities** that take revealed messages (spirit-writing, mediums) as a source of truth, compared with secular households.
- **Scientists and engineers** in the science parks and research institutes.
- **Hakka and mainlander communities,** whose memories of 1947 and 1949 may not match.
- **Kinmen and Matsu residents,** close to the mainland coast and its broadcasts.
- **Southeast Asian migrants,** who follow news in their own languages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for misinformation research and historical-truth debates; mixed for folk ways of judging truth.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese for rumor and speech; Hakka; Atayal and other Formosan languages; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** information operations from across the strait are a security topic; 228 and White Terror files involve victims' privacy; some lmuhuw may be sung only by recognized singers. Legal truth (courts, evidence) goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
