## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo southwest** (Tainan, Kaohsiung, Pingtung) against **northern cities,** for temple density and everyday taboo.
- **Hakka districts,** with earth-god and Three Mountain Kings cults.
- **Bunun, Tao, Atayal and Paiwan,** each with its own taboo system; separate them rather than speak of "Indigenous belief".
- **Indigenous Christians and traditionalists** within the same people.
- **Mainlander families** and their sects, with their own folk beliefs from many provinces.
- **Kinmen, Matsu and Penghu,** for island protective beliefs.
- **Engineers and scientists,** for the meeting of science with folk practice (for example rites at a factory or lab opening, a question to research).
- **Southeast Asian residents,** for imported belief and practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief and its colonial-era records; surveys exist; Indigenous taboo systems recorded mostly by outsiders, so the peoples' own accounts are needed.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Taiwanese (likely the main language of temple and taboo talk); Hakka; Bunun, Tao and other Formosan languages; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" is a judgment, not a neutral category; some Indigenous taboos are not for outsiders. Divination and fortune-telling count here only as belief, not as a classifier of people.
- **Held for review:** none.
