## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Veterans of the democracy movement** against **KMT veterans and their families,** who may hold equally firm but opposite commitments.
- **Hakka towns** (Meinong, Miaoli, Hsinchu), for long local campaigns and 1895 memory.
- **Tao, Tsou, Atayal and Amis,** each with its own movements over land, waste and names.
- **Christian groups:** Presbyterians, Jehovah's Witnesses and others whose stands set them against the state at times.
- **Buddhist and lay-sect members,** for conviction kept through vows rather than protest.
- **Farmers and fishing villages** in Yunlin, Changhua and Miaoli.
- **Students,** by generation (1990, 2014 and after).
- **Workers and unions,** including migrant workers' shelters and advocates.
