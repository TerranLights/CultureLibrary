## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small traders and night-market vendors** against **large firms,** for honesty in different kinds of trade.
- **Rural and urban** communities, for informal finance and the honor system.
- **Older and younger generations,** for exposure to phone and online scams.
- **Atayal, Truku and other Indigenous peoples,** each with its own norms on theft and truthfulness, taught in its own terms.
- **Hakka and Hoklo** folk narrative, to compare their trickster and liar tales.
- **Students and academics,** for exam and research honesty.
- **Temple communities** that use sacred oaths, against secular families.
- **Southeast Asian workers and their advocates,** whose dealings with labor brokers raise honesty questions from the other side.
