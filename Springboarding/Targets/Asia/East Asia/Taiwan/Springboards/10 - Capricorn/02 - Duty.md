# Taiwan: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eldest sons, daughters-in-law and married daughters:** the old patrilineal roles divided ritual and care duties sharply; how much remains is the question.
- **Hoklo and Hakka:** different calendars for the dead (掛紙 against Qingming) and different temple obligations.
- **Mainlander families:** households whose ancestral graves lay out of reach after 1949; how duty to the dead was rebuilt.
- **Amis, Puyuma and other age-set peoples:** duties set by grade, not only by family.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** obligations between commoner and chiefly houses, *check* their present form.
- **Tao:** household and boat-crew duties tied to the fishing season.
- **Christian and secular households:** families that do not keep ancestral rites, and how other duties replace them.
- **Migrant caregivers and new-immigrant spouses:** people hired or married into duties of care.
- **Conscripts and alternative-service men:** duty to the state as lived, by cohort length of service.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese emigrant families caring for parents across the Pacific.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich; good family-survey data and a large Mandarin literature on filial duty; thinner for Indigenous obligations, which communities may describe differently.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien and Hakka for ritual terms; Indigenous languages for age-set and exchange duties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family disputes over inheritance and care; migrant caregivers' working conditions; some festival rites restricted to members.
- **Held for review:** none.
