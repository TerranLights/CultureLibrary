# Taiwan: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tech-park engineers against small-firm owners:** salaried "responsibility" in Hsinchu firms and the owner's total liability in family shops.
- **Hoklo temple-goers and Buddhist lay groups:** answering to gods and karma, and how that sits beside answering to people.
- **Christian households:** confession and personal sin as a separate frame of answering, *check*.
- **Mainlanders and descendants of 1947 victims:** the same history, weighed from two sides of blame.
- **Bunun, Atayal and Truku hunters:** stewardship responsibilities in their own terms.
- **Tao (Orchid Island):** collective responsibility for the island's waters and the dispute over the waste site.
- **Hakka households:** whether eldest-son and eldest-daughter responsibilities differ from Hoklo ones, *check*.
- **Women in the "sandwich" years:** caregiving and household management between children and parents.
- **Migrant workers:** who answers when a contract worker is hurt (employer, broker, state).
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese emigrant parents and the expectations placed on their children abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for public accountability and disasters (official reports, heavy press coverage); mixed for household responsibility; thin for Indigenous terms, which need community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for morality tracts and temple settings; Bunun and other Indigenous languages for stewardship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blame in 228 and White Terror cases names real families; disaster victims and defendants; a mass-killing drama touches living cases.
- **Held for review:** none.
