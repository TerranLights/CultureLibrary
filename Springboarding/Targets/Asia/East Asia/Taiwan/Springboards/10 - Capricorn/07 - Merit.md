*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taipei's 大安 and other advantaged districts against rural and eastern counties:** the core of the admissions debate.
- **Indigenous students and the Stars Program's rural quota:** two separate tools for unequal starting points.
- **Hakka and Hoklo political families:** dynasties in Miaoli, Hsinchu and the southwest, to be compared.
- **Mainlander military and civil-service families:** an older system of rank that critics called privilege.
- **Atayal, Truku and Sediq elders:** deed-based standing within living memory.
- **Paiwan and Rukai:** inherited rank alongside earned honors.
- **Tech-park employees against small-firm and public-sector workers:** merit pay and seniority pay compared.
- **New immigrants' children:** whether school results open doors equally.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese students abroad, judged by foreign admissions systems.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich; admissions and exam data are detailed and the debate is heavily studied; thinner for workplace merit outside big firms.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin; Hokkien for slang of connections; Indigenous languages for earned-standing customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** facial-tattoo meanings and images belong to the peoples concerned; political-family claims need care to avoid naming without evidence.
- **Held for review:** none.
