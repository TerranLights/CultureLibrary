## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taipei against older towns such as Tainan and Lukang:** rule-dense transit and public space against older street life, *check*.
- **Temple communities:** festival seasons that bend everyday order.
- **Hakka villages:** settlement layouts around lineage halls and their own festival order, to be tested.
- **Mainlander juancun:** villages built on a military plan, many now demolished.
- **Tao (Orchid Island):** order set by the flying-fish calendar and its taboos.
- **Paiwan, Rukai and Bunun after Morakot:** relocated villages and their new layouts.
- **Amis and other coastal peoples:** village order through age sets, as the peoples describe it.
- **Night-market vendors and scooter commuters:** everyday negotiation with street rules.
- **Former gang members and juvenile offenders:** a rival code of order, as they describe it.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** Taiwanese emigrants' comments on streets, queues and safety in their new countries, as a mirror for home norms.
