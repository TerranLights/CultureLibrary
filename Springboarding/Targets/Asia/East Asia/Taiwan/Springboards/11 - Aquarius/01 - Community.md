# Taiwan: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Indigenous languages (Amis, Atayal, Paiwan, Tao and others). **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old market towns (Lukang, Daxi, Tainan's old city) against new towns and Taipei high-rises,** where building management committees (管委會) stand in for neighbors.
- **Hakka villages:** Meinong in the south and the Miaoli hills in the north, separately.
- **Juancun residents and their descendants:** a community dispersed by redevelopment.
- **Indigenous peoples by village:** Tao on Lanyu, Bunun in the central mountains, Paiwan in Pingtung, Amis both on the east coast and in city riverside settlements, Kavalan and Sakizaya in Hualien.
- **New immigrant spouses in rural townships:** joining a village through marriage.
- **Penghu and other outlying islands:** island community and out-migration.
- **Folk-temple, Buddhist and Christian congregations,** each with its own idea of members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for neighborhood policy and disaster rebuilding; mixed for everyday felt community; thinner for migrant workers' communities.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese for colonial records, the village languages of each Indigenous people.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Morakot relocation and urban Indigenous evictions remain contested; let residents speak first. Qing-era communal feuds (分類械鬥) between origin groups are a sensitive history; read without assigning blame to today's groups. Law on Indigenous land belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
