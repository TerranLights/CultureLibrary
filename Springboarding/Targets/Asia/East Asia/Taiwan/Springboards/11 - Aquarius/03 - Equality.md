# Taiwan: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Paiwan and other Indigenous languages; Vietnamese, Indonesian and Tagalog for migrant voices. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Indigenous peoples:** ranked-house Paiwan and Rukai against Amis and Puyuma age-grade orders, and against Bunun, Tsou and Truku; plains peoples such as Siraya seeking recognition.
- **Hakka against Hoklo:** a minority-language community's equality claims.
- **Mainlanders:** distinguish elite families from poor retired soldiers (榮民 / 老兵); the two need separate sources.
- **Women and men,** by generation; and gay, lesbian and transgender people.
- **New immigrant spouses** (mainly from Vietnam, Indonesia and mainland China) and **migrant workers** (factory and home caregivers, separately).
- **Taipei against the south and the east coast:** regional gaps in pay and services.
- **Disabled people:** their own organizations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income, gender and ethnic statistics; mixed for lived class and manners; thin for migrant caregivers inside homes.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Japanese (colonial schooling), Paiwan, Amis; Vietnamese, Indonesian, Tagalog and Thai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Qing and colonial terms for Indigenous peoples are slurs; cite as history, never as labels. Provincial-origin grievance remains politically charged; give each side its own sources. Rights in law (marriage, labor, Indigenous status) belong to Libra; only the movements and their claims are here.
- **Held for review:** none.
