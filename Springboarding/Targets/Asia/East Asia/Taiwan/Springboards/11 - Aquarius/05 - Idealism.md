# Taiwan: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Atayal and Seediq; Classical Chinese for older texts. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Generations:** the folk-song generation of the 1970s, the student movements of 1990 and 2014, and those who later call ideals naive.
- **Buddhist movements:** Tzu Chi (Hualien), Fo Guang Shan (Kaohsiung), Dharma Drum (New Taipei); distinct visions of service.
- **Christians:** the Presbyterian Church (worship in Taigi, Hakka and Indigenous languages *check*) against Mandarin-language evangelical churches.
- **Indigenous peoples:** Atayal (Smangus), Seediq, Truku; their ideals of right living in their own terms, set apart from outsiders' romantic images.
- **Mainlander elders** raised on the state's ideals of return; their children.
- **Rural returnees and Hakka farming villages:** ideals tested by land and money.
- **New immigrants:** ideals carried from Vietnam, Indonesia or the Philippines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, religion and movements; mixed for measured values; thin for migrant workers' ideals.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Taigi (church and song), Hakka, Atayal, Seediq.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state ideals of the martial-law era remain divisive; avoid treating any one as "the nation's". Religious movements have loyal members and sharp critics; use both. Outsiders' romantic images of Indigenous peoples are a stereotype to name, not adopt.
- **Held for review:** none.
