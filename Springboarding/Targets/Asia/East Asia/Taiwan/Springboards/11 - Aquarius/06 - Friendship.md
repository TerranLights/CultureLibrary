# Taiwan: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Classical Chinese; Amis and Puyuma for age-mate bonds; Tagalog and Indonesian for migrant workers' circles. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women:** conscript cohorts and drinking circles against 姊妹淘; and friendships that cross the line between them.
- **Generations:** elders who made friends through temple and village, against students on Dcard.
- **Mainlander veterans and their old comrades (老戰友),** far from their own kin.
- **Indigenous peoples:** Amis and Puyuma age-mates; Tao fishing-crew companions; Atayal and Bunun hunting partners (*check*); each described in its own terms.
- **Hakka and Hoklo villages** against Taipei, where newcomers from the south make friends.
- **Gay and lesbian Taiwanese:** friends as chosen family.
- **Migrant workers and new immigrant spouses:** friendship as the main tie away from kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classics, film and song; mixed for measured friendship (few direct survey items); thin for Indigenous terms for friend.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Taigi (drinking and sworn-brother talk), Hakka, Amis, Puyuma, Tao; Tagalog and Indonesian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sworn brotherhood overlaps with gang culture (see Group); keep the two apart. Cross-strait and diplomatic "friendship" is politically framed; read official texts as public acts.
- **Held for review:** none.
