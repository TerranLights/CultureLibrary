# Taiwan: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin, Taigi, Hakka, Classical Chinese; Indigenous languages for gender and life-path terms. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taipei against southern and rural towns,** where the room for difference may differ.
- **Class:** temple-troupe "8+9" youth (labeled from outside) against middle-class indie scenes.
- **Gay, lesbian and transgender Taiwanese,** by generation (martial-law era against post-2019).
- **Religious minorities:** Jehovah's Witnesses, Yiguandao (一貫道, once banned), new religious groups.
- **Indigenous people:** young Indigenous musicians and artists; gender-diverse Indigenous people in their own terms; ask each people's view of those who differ.
- **Hakka and Hoklo youth;** juancun youth of the 1960s-80s.
- **Otaku and cosplay fans; alternative-school families; rural returnees choosing unusual lives.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, film, music and queer history; mixed for measured tolerance; thin for Indigenous gender diversity and for migrant workers' subcultures.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Classical Chinese, Taigi (street slang), Hakka, Indigenous languages; Japanese for colonial-era dress and hair rules.
