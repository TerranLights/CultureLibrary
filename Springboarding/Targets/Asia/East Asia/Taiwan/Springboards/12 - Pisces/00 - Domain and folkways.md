# Taiwan: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô romanization), Hakka (Sixian and Hailu), the Indigenous Formosan languages, Japanese (for colonial-era sources). **Concept in one line:** the hidden and transcendence: how suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape are met (folkway: magic ways; runner-up: order ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hoklo south against north:** Tainan, Kaohsiung and Pingtung temple density and plague-god rites set against Taipei and Keelung, where the Ghost Festival is the larger public rite.
- **Hakka districts** (Taoyuan, Hsinchu and Miaoli; the Liudui settlements of Kaohsiung and Pingtung): the Yimin cult of the righteous dead and their own healing words.
- **Families who came after 1949** and the veterans' villages (眷村): a different set of gods, burial customs and silences.
- **Indigenous peoples, separately:** mountain Atayal, Seediq and Truku; east-coast Amis, Puyuma and Kavalan; southern Paiwan and Rukai; Bunun and Tsou of the central range; Saisiyat; Tao on Orchid Island; Thao at Sun Moon Lake.
- **Plains Indigenous communities** (Siraya and others), whose rites survive in Tainan's villages.
- **Christian minorities** (Presbyterian, Catholic, True Jesus Church), strong in some Indigenous townships.
- **Penghu and the outlying islands;** newer residents, including marriage migrants and migrant workers from Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hoklo folk religion and for mental-health statistics; mixed for Hakka; uneven by Indigenous people. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (for example the outlying island groups); this pass writes for the main island and its near islands.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, and the Indigenous languages through their communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous rites restrict who may speak of them (the Saisiyat ritual of the ta'ay; Tao rules on naming the dead); political-prison testimony; suicide reporting follows media guidelines.
- **Held for review:** none.
