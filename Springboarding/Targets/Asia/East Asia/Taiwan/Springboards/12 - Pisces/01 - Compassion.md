# Taiwan: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, the Indigenous Formosan languages. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tzu Chi members and other Buddhist laity** against **folk-temple congregations,** whose charity runs through festivals, rice and the hungry dead.
- **Presbyterian and Catholic communities,** including Indigenous parishes, whose charity runs through hospitals, kindergartens and church relief.
- **Indigenous peoples,** each on its own: the Bunun and the Amis, whose sharing of game and harvest differs; the Tao of Orchid Island, receiving outside aid; urban Indigenous migrants in New Taipei and Taoyuan.
- **Hakka districts,** with their own temple and lineage charities.
- **The givers and the given-to:** the unhoused of Wanhua and Taipei Main Station; families of the disabled; marriage migrants and migrant workers from Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand, as recipients and as givers in their own churches and mosques.
- **City and countryside;** generations (giving through the temple, through an app, or through crowdfunding).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organized Buddhist and Christian charity; mixed for folk-temple and household giving; thin for Indigenous words and practices in print.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka; Indigenous terms only through community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity scandals and donation controversies are contested; photographs of the poor and sick; unhoused people's consent in media.
- **Held for review:** none.
