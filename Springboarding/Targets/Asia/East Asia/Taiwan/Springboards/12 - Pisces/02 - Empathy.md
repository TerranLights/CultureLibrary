# Taiwan: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, the Indigenous Formosan languages; the home languages of migrant workers for their own writing. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Generations:** people schooled after the curriculum reforms against those schooled under martial law; the Japanese-educated eldest generation.
- **Hoklo, Hakka and post-1949 families,** whose household speech for feelings may differ; check, do not assume.
- **The sixteen recognized Indigenous peoples** and the plains peoples, as speakers in their own right and as the ones asked to be understood; compare the Tao, the Atayal and the Amis separately.
- **Employers and live-in caregivers** from Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand: empathy inside one household across language and class.
- **Service work:** nurses, teachers, call-center and convenience-store staff, and the "customer from hell" (奧客) as the test of their empathy.
- **Political camps:** empathy across party and national-identity lines, where rhetoric about the other side can turn harsh.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology and media; mixed for household and regional practice; thin for Indigenous terms in print.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Indigenous languages; Vietnamese, Tagalog and Thai for migrants' own writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness and violence (the drama's subject); migrant workers' privacy; transitional-justice testimony. Restorative justice belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
