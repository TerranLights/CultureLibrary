# Taiwan: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, Seediq, Tsou and other Indigenous Formosan languages, Japanese. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hakka communities,** with the Yimin dead at the center of their public memory.
- **The Tsou,** for whom the Wu Feng story was an injury, not an example.
- **Seediq and Truku,** whose memory of 1930 is their own, and differs from Atayal and other neighbors.
- **Amis, Paiwan and other peoples who sent men to the Pacific war,** compared one by one.
- **Families who came after 1949** and veterans (榮民) separated from kin across the strait.
- **Hoklo daughters, adopted daughters and wives,** across generations of factory and household work.
- **Democracy activists and their families;** Christians whose churches honor their own dead.
- **Live-in caregivers from Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand,** whose sacrifice for families at home happens inside Taiwanese homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and political commemoration and for gender scholarship; mixed for Indigenous memory in print.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, Seediq, Tsou and other Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** self-immolation and suicide (follow reporting guidelines); the Musha deaths; Tsou views on Wu Feng; caregiver homicides. Military law and service law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
