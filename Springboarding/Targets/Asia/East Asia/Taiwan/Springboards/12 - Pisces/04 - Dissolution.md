# Taiwan: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, the Indigenous Formosan languages (including the most endangered), classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (working definition proposed; the author decides).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Temple communities** with active mediums and troupes (Tainan, Chiayi, Kaohsiung, Pingtung) against **Buddhist meditation communities** and **charismatic Christian congregations,** each with its own idea of losing the self.
- **The most endangered Indigenous language communities** (Hla'alua, Kanakanavu, Thao, Saisiyat, Kavalan) against larger ones (Amis, Atayal, Paiwan).
- **Plains Indigenous descendants** (Siraya, Taivoan, Makatao, Pazeh and others), whose identity is being reclaimed.
- **Hakka families** where the language has faded in one generation, against the Liudui settlements.
- **Military-village families** (眷村) and their children after demolition.
- **Young festival-goers** and **elderly people with dementia,** at the two ends of the life course.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and for religious ethnography; mixed for personal accounts of trance; thin in print for some Indigenous rites of joining and leaving.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, classical Chinese, and the endangered languages through their own communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** possession and mental illness must not be conflated; cult cases involve living people; disaster dead of Xiaolin; drug use (law aspects to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
