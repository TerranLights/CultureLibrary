# Taiwan: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, Atayal, Tao and other Indigenous Formosan languages, Japanese, classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Hoklo villages** with active five-camp shrines and procession territories, against **northern cities** where the apartment gate is the main line.
- **Hakka districts:** northern Hakka against Liudui, with the earth god at field edges.
- **Indigenous peoples with live territory claims,** taken one by one: Atayal, Truku and Seediq in the north; Paiwan and Rukai in the south; the Tao on Orchid Island; the Amis on the east coast.
- **Plains Indigenous communities** on the old frontier line.
- **Post-1949 military villages,** once walled communities of their own.
- **Live-in caregivers and their employers,** where household lines are hardest to keep.
- **Kinmen and Matsu,** if they fall inside the nation's lands, where the line with the mainland runs past the shore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temple territory, frontier history and family-conflict talk; mixed for household privacy measures.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, classical Chinese, and Indigenous languages for territory names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous territory knowledge can be restricted to the community; sexual-harassment cases name living people; cross-strait security topics. Land and privacy law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
