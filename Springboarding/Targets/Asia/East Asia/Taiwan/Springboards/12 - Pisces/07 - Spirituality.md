## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Folk-temple worshippers,** south and north; coastal fishing communities against inland farming towns.
- **Buddhist laity** of the large orders against **sectarian practitioners** (Yiguandao, spirit-writing halls).
- **Christians:** Hoklo Presbyterians; Catholic and Presbyterian Indigenous parishes; the True Jesus Church; charismatic city churches.
- **Hakka communities,** with their own sacred calendar and gods (三山國王, 伯公).
- **Indigenous peoples,** each on its own, where ancestral ceremonies and church life sit side by side: Paiwan, Bunun, Amis, Atayal, Tao, Saisiyat, Tsou, Puyuma.
- **Plains Indigenous villages** reviving ancestral rites (Siraya Alid worship).
- **Urban young adults** in the body-mind-spirit scene; **migrant Muslims and Catholics** keeping their faith in Taiwan.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for popular religion, Buddhism and Christianity; mixed for New Age; uneven for Indigenous practice, much of which is not for outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, classical Chinese, Dutch, Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed Indigenous ceremonies (respected, not researched); sectarian groups once persecuted; religious-fraud cases. Faith and institutions belong to Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none.
