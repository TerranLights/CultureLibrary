# Taiwan: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, Bunun, Truku and other Indigenous Formosan languages, Japanese, Dutch. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher; release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Indigenous peoples, each with its own memory of the colonial wars:** Paiwan of the Mudan area (1874), Truku (1914), Seediq (1930), Bunun (holdouts into the 1930s).
- **The Japanese-educated eldest generation** against **post-1949 families,** whose 1945 and 1949 memories differ.
- **Hoklo and Hakka families,** where 認命 may be spoken differently by women and men; check, do not assume.
- **Buddhist, folk-religion and Christian households** on letting go at a deathbed.
- **Families of White Terror victims,** for whom surrender to the state is a living memory.
- **Fishing and farming communities** facing typhoons and floods, deciding whether to leave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hospice and religious material; mixed for colonial surrender records (outsiders' frame); thin for Indigenous terms in print.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, Japanese, Dutch, classical Chinese, and Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** assisted death and suicide; political-case files of living families; Indigenous war memories. End-of-life law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
