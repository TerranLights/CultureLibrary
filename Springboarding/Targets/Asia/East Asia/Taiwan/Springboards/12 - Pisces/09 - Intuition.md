*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Temple and fortune-telling clients,** urban and rural, against **engineers and managers** in data-driven industries.
- **The eldest and the youngest generations,** where the 鐵齒 skeptic and the believer may switch sides.
- **Atayal, Truku, Seediq and Bunun hunters,** whose omen practices differ; **the Tao,** for sea knowledge; **Amis fishers** of the east coast.
- **Hakka and Hoklo households,** for dream reading and house omens.
- **Christians,** for prayerful discernment, and **Buddhists,** for meditation insight.
- **Chinese-medicine doctors** against **biomedical doctors,** on trusting trained senses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for divination and Chinese medicine; mixed for cognitive-style research with Taiwanese samples; thin in print for Indigenous sign-reading.
- **Languages needed:** Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka, classical Chinese, Japanese, and Indigenous languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some hunting rules and omen knowledge belong to the communities; fortune-telling fraud cases. Divination is studied as a practice people use, never as a source.
- **Held for review:** none.
