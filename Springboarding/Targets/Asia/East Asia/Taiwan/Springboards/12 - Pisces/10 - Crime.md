# Taiwan: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Taiwan, region East Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Mandarin (traditional characters), Taiwanese Hokkien (Tâi-lô), Hakka, Atayal, Bunun and other Indigenous Formosan languages, Japanese, classical Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn, not here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Temple-troupe youth and their neighborhoods,** against the urban middle class who use the 8+9 label.
- **Families of victims** and **families of offenders,** both of whom carry shame.
- **Post-1949 military villages,** linked in gang histories to the rise of some large gangs; a link to record, never generalize.
- **Indigenous peoples,** each with its own norms of wrong and repair: Atayal and Truku gaga, Bunun samu, Tao rules about the dead.
- **Hakka and Hoklo towns,** for local turf gangs and temple politics.
- **Migrant workers labeled "lost contact" (失聯移工)** after leaving abusive jobs: how the label makes them wrongdoers.
- **Older people** as fraud targets; **young couriers** recruited into scam rings.

## 4. Flags
