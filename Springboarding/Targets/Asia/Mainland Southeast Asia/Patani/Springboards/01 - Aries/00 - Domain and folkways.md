# Patani: Domain and folkways (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for *kitab*. **Concept in one line:** self and identity: the self, the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative and naming; what a self is and how it shows itself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Pattani villages and the interior rice, rubber and orchard villages of Yala and Narathiwat:** check whether birth and naming rites differ with livelihood.
- **Pattani town, Yala city, Narathiwat town and the border town of Sungai Kolok:** check how Thai-schooled town families combine names and dress.
- **The Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla (Chana, Thepha, Na Thawi, Saba Yoi) and Satun:** check whether "Malay" and "Muslim" separate where Thai is the home language.
- **Sam-Sam communities:** check how a Thai-speaking Muslim identity is named and recorded.
- **Buddhist Thai residents of the region:** check their own rites that make a new self, such as ordination at local temples.
- **Thai Chinese of Pattani town, Yala and Betong (including the Piyamit villages):** check naming across Chinese, Thai and Malay.
- **The Maniq (Mani) and Kensiw of the Hala-Bala forest, Than To and the Banthat range:** check personhood and naming only through their own representatives and the anthropology centre.
- **The Urak Lawoi of the Satun islands:** check whether their self-naming is recorded apart from the Malay majority.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan, Thailand's national norms, and the diaspora (workers in Malaysia, scholar families in Mecca, Bangkok Muslims of Patani descent).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: political identity since 2004 is heavily studied; everyday self, body and naming are thinner and sit in Thai theses.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the rest of the sign are written for the core lands of Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat and the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi reading for chronicles and *kitab*; Thai for census, theses and press; Patani Malay in Thai script for current teaching materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** self-labels are part of the southern conflict; research on them needs consent and care, and the Maniq's own wishes govern any record of them.
- **Held for review:** writings on South Asian-heritage Muslim families in the region's towns and on how they name themselves (category only).
