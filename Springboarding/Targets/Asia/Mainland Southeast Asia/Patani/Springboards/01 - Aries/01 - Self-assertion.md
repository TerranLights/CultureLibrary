# Patani: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for *adab* texts. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pondok students and students of Thai government schools and universities:** check which settings reward a student who speaks up.
- **Market women and mosque committees:** check who may speak, ask and refuse in each setting, and whether it changes by district.
- **Thepha and Saba Yoi in Songkhla and the core three provinces:** check whether request and refusal formulas follow Thai or Malay patterns in mixed districts.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and the Sam-Sam:** check which language's politeness registers govern their disputes.
- **Thai Chinese merchants of Yala and Betong:** check how claims are pressed through associations rather than individuals.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers in mixed districts:** check their own forms of standing one's ground in temple and village meetings.
- **The Maniq and Kensiw:** check who speaks to officials for a band, and whether outsiders' records reflect that at all.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's Malay speech norms; Patani workers in Malaysia, who negotiate as migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for everyday assertion, rich for political claims; the gap needs recorded speech, not only conflict studies.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay by ear for *dikir hulu* and market talk; Thai for complaint records; Jawi and Arabic for *adab* manuals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public claim-making in the region is tied to the conflict; protect speakers in any recording; the legal frame belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** any pointer on speaking-up norms within South Asian-heritage Muslim congregations or a South Asian-founded preaching movement in the region (category only).
