# Patani: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Chinese for town associations. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

English keywords: Patani Malay identity, Malay nationalism southern Thailand, Thainess and the south, Patani historiography, Thai Chinese Betong identity, Muslim Buddhist boundaries Songkhla.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families carrying old titles (Tengku, Tuan, Nik, Wan) and families without them:** check whether introductions open with lineage in one group and with village or work in the other.
- **Saiburi and the Pattani coast against Yala's interior and Betong:** check whether "who are you" is answered by place, trade or religion.
- **Chana, Thepha and the other Malay-speaking Songkhla districts:** check whether people there use *oghe Nayu* or Thai labels for themselves.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and the Sam-Sam:** check how a Muslim identity without daily Malay is named and taught.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Pattani and Narathiwat:** check which local temples and saints anchor their sense of who they are.
- **Thai Chinese of Betong, Yala and Pattani, and the Piyamit settlers:** check which of several possible labels each generation chooses.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check their own names for themselves against those in Thai records.
- **Converts and mixed Malay-Thai or Malay-Chinese families:** check how a changed or double identity is recorded.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantanese identity across the border; Bangkok Muslims of Patani descent; Patani families in Mecca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political and historical identity, uneven for small communities; the Maniq, Urak Lawoi and Piyamit settlers need fieldwork-based sources.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for Syukri's original and *salasilah*; Thai for surveys and curricula; Chinese for Betong association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity labels are contested in the conflict; historical narratives are disputed; never present one group's narrative as the region's.
- **Held for review:** studies of identity among South Asian-heritage traders' descendants in the region's towns (category only).
