# Patani: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for creed texts. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for a person and for a group.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pondok-educated and Thai-schooled young adults:** check who expects to choose a spouse and a career, and who expects family to decide.
- **Town households of Yala city and Pattani against farming villages of Narathiwat's interior:** check age at leaving home and who holds the household purse.
- **Women and men within the same villages:** check which personal choices each may make without asking.
- **Buddhist Thai families in Pattani and Narathiwat:** check how their decisions to stay, move or send children away are made.
- **Thai Chinese families of Betong and Yala:** check how far children's choices are tied to the family business.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Songkhla's Malay-speaking districts:** check whether religious committees there hold the same say over personal life as in the core provinces.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check collective control of land and movement, through their own voices.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's family decision-making; young Patani workers in Malaysia, whose distance changes who decides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; collective self-rule and religious schooling are well studied, while family decision-making sits in scattered Thai theses.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for creed texts; Thai for health and policy records; Patani Malay for interviews about marriage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective autonomy is a live political question tied to the conflict; health and marriage data involve minors and consent.
- **Held for review:** material on choice and family authority among South Asian-heritage Muslim households in the region (category only).
