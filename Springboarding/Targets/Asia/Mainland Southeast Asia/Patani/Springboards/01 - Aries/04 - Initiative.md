*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Chinese for foundation records. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Market women of Pattani, Yala and Sungai Kolok and men in boat crews and rubber tapping:** check who is expected to start a trade, and what counts as overstepping for each.
- **Coastal fishing villages and inland rice villages:** check who calls a communal work party and how labor exchange is organized.
- **Pondok founders' families and families without religious standing:** check whether starting an institution needs lineage or learning.
- **Thai Chinese of Yala, Pattani and Betong:** check how foundations and clan associations organize volunteering and first response.
- **Buddhist Thai residents:** check whether temples or village committees take the lead in their communities.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Songkhla's Malay districts:** check whether cooperatives and community enterprises follow the core provinces' pattern.
- **The Urak Lawoi:** check who leads a fishing trip or a community task.
- **Comparison only:** Patani-run tom yam restaurants in Malaysia, a migrant route into business; Kelantan's market traders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** business and cooperative data are reachable; everyday initiative (helping unasked) is thin and needs ethnography or interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for registries and surveys; Patani Malay for folktales and market talk; Jawi and Malay for *ulama* biographies; Chinese for foundation records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** first responders and volunteers work in a conflict zone; any personal details need care.
- **Held for review:** studies of new shops and ventures started by families of South Asian heritage in the region's towns (category only).
