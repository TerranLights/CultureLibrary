# Patani: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for rite formulas; Chinese for town festivals. **Concept in one line:** how starting is treated: origins, firsts, new years, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal communities and inland rice villages:** check whether the year's working start is set by monsoon and fishing or by planting.
- **Households that still consult a *bomoh* or a *ketika* manual and households that do not:** check who decides when to begin a house or a marriage.
- **Buddhist Thai residents of Tak Bai, Nong Chik and Saiburi:** check which new-year and founding rites their temples keep.
- **Thai Chinese of Betong, Yala and Pattani town:** check how their calendar of beginnings runs beside the Hijri and Thai ones.
- **Converts, returnees and people in recovery:** check how each kind of new start is named and received in villages and towns.
- **The Urak Lawoi of Satun's islands and the Maniq of the Banthat range:** check their own rites for a new season or a new camp, through their own accounts.
- **The Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun:** check whether founding legends tie them to the old polity or to other origins.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's new-year and house rites; Patani families who started over in Malaysia or Mecca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rites are documented in Thai folklore theses, while starting over after conflict or addiction is newer and scattered across reports.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Malay for *ketika* texts; Thai for health and rehabilitation studies; Urak Lawoi terms through its speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** returnee, widow and rehabilitation material involves the conflict and private lives; some *bomoh* knowledge is guarded and shared only by consent.
- **Held for review:** accounts of new-year and birth rites among South Asian-heritage Muslim families in the towns (category only).
