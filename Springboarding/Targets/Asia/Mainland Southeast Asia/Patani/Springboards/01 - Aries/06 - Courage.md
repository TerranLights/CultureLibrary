# Patani: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for devotional texts; Hokkien and Teochew terms for shrine rites. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men in *silat* circles and men outside them:** check what each counts as brave and as foolish.
- **Women of coastal and farming households:** check whether their endurance is named as courage, and in which words.
- **Malay and Buddhist Thai audiences of shadow theater:** check which hero types each repertoire praises.
- **Teachers, health workers and officials posted from outside the region:** check how they and local people describe staying in danger.
- **The Thai Chinese of Pattani town and Yala:** check what shrine rites ask of a devotee's body.
- **Fishers of Pattani Bay, Urak Lawoi divers and Maniq hunters:** check work-based courage on each group's own terms.
- **Satun and Songkhla's Malay districts:** check whether conflict-era hero narratives circulate there at all.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's *silat* and shadow-theater traditions; diaspora retellings of Patani heroes in Malaysian Malay publishing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and conflict, mixed for everyday and work courage; trauma research is growing but clinical.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for puppeteers' and *silat* masters' talk; Southern Thai for *nang talung*; Jawi for the devotional *kitab*; Thai for state records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heroes, martyrs and enemies differ by narrator in the conflict; present every narrative as one side's; *silat* and *keris* knowledge may be restricted to initiates.
- **Held for review:** courage narratives within South Asian-heritage communities of the region (category only).
