*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber smallholders and orchard owners in the hills against wage workers on the coast:** check what owning land changes in talk of standing on one's own.
- **Households with members working in Malaysia and households without:** check how pride and dependence are spoken of when income comes from abroad.
- **Elders in Narathiwat villages and in Yala city:** check who is expected to care for them, and what living alone means.
- **Buddhist Thai villages:** check reliance on temple, state and kin, beside Malay villages' reliance on mosque and *zakat*.
- **Thai Chinese of Betong and Yala:** check how clan associations and family firms carry members through hard times.
- **Satun and Songkhla's Malay districts:** check whether the region's independence question is present in their histories at all.
- **The Kensiw and Maniq:** check what self-provisioning in the forest means today, in their own terms.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's rural economy; Patani families settled in Malaysia and their ties of support.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the political question and on rubber, mixed on household self-reliance, thin on elders and disability in the region.
- **Languages needed:** English for colonial files and policy reports; Thai for welfare and survey data; Patani Malay for proverbs and village talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the independence question is the core of the conflict; cite each party's own documents and never present one as the region's view; protect informants.
- **Held for review:** studies of community welfare in the region run by a South Asian-founded Islamic movement (category only).
