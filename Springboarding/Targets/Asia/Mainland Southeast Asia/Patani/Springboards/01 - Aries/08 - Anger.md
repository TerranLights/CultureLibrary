# Patani: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for ethical texts. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed and cooled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men and women in the same village:** check which displays of anger each may show, and before whom.
- **Children in *tadika* and in Thai primary schools:** check whether the rules for anger differ between the two settings.
- **Families who use mosque reconciliation and families who go to officials:** check which disputes go where.
- **Buddhist Thai households of Pattani and Narathiwat:** check whether *chai yen* teaching runs beside or against local Malay norms.
- **Thai Chinese families of Yala and Betong:** check how anger is handled in family firms and associations.
- **Young men in town and young men in rubber villages:** check the outlets each has (sport, music, the motorbike).
- **The Maniq:** check how a band handles quarrels, through the band's own accounts.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's healing performances and emotion words; Thai national ideals of a cool heart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; emotion words and healing are documented in Malay and Thai research, while anger at home is under-reported.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for emotion talk and healing sessions; Jawi for ethical texts; Thai for health and school data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective anger is entangled with the conflict; never attribute anger to a community; domestic-violence data need strict privacy; some healing knowledge is guarded.
- **Held for review:** anger and mediation practices in South Asian-heritage congregations of the region (category only).
