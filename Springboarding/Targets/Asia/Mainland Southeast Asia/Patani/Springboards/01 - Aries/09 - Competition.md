# Patani: Competition (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Dutch for trading-company records. **Concept in one line:** striving against others for a prize, a rank or a result.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dove breeders' clubs in Yala and village bird-keepers:** check how prestige is shared between owner, bird and village.
- **Coastal boat crews of Narathiwat and Pattani:** check whether village rivalry carries into other parts of life.
- **Students of private Islamic schools and of Thai government schools:** check which contests (recitation, exams, sport) each values and prepares for.
- **Buddhist Thai audiences and performers in Songkhla's border districts:** check how Nora contests set rules for winning gracefully.
- **Thai Chinese associations in Betong and Yala:** check how they compete and cooperate in festivals and trade.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether their contest forms follow Kedah, Songkhla or the core provinces.
- **The Urak Lawoi and the Maniq:** check whether any contest forms exist among them and how they are named, through their own accounts.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's top-spinning, kite and bird contests; national Thai sports culture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for contest forms in folklore research and the press; thin for envy and everyday rivalry, which need ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch and early modern English for trading records; Patani Malay for contest talk; Southern Thai for Nora; Chinese for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** animal contests involve betting and animal-welfare debate; envy talk can touch sorcery accusations; handle both without judging a community.
- **Held for review:** contests and trade rivalry involving South Asian-heritage merchants of the region's towns (category only).
