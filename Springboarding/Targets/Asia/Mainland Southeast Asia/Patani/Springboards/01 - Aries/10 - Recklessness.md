## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in towns and young men in rubber villages:** check which risks each group's peers admire and which they mock.
- **Families with a member in rehabilitation and families without:** check how intoxication is named (sin, illness, foolishness) in each.
- **Fishing households of Pattani Bay and Saiburi:** check how putting to sea in bad weather is judged.
- **Buddhist Thai residents:** check how amulets and protective rites enter their talk of danger.
- **Thai Chinese households of Yala and Pattani town:** check attitudes to games of chance and to insurance.
- **Satun and Songkhla's Malay districts:** check whether youth-risk patterns follow the core provinces or Hat Yai.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check how forest and sea work risks are named, in their own words.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's festive firecracker customs; Patani workers in Malaysia facing work hazards abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in public-health statistics; thin on what risk means to people, which needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for health and traffic data; Patani Malay for folktales and youth talk; Arabic and Jawi for teaching on *gharar* and *tawakal*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** invulnerability knowledge has been linked in some accounts to the conflict; present it through each tradition's own sources only; drug and gambling data involve minors and stigma.
- **Held for review:** harm-reduction or preaching work on youth risk by a South Asian-founded Islamic movement in the region (category only).
