# Patani: Domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for teaching on wealth; Chinese for the towns' records. **Concept in one line:** possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (money, resources, saving and display, land, food, dress and wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fishing coast of Pattani Bay, Saiburi and Tak Bai against hill rubber and fruit villages:** check what each household keeps in reserve and spends on first.
- **Pattani town, Yala city and Sungai Kolok against farming villages:** check whether cars, shop goods and gold rank differently as signs of means.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Nong Chik, Khok Pho and Tak Bai:** check their giving to temples and ancestors as a system of its own.
- **Thai Chinese shopkeeping families of Anoru road, Yala and Betong:** check what a trading family holds as capital, heirloom and gift.
- **Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla (Chana, Thepha, Saba Yoi) and Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether feast and dress customs follow the core provinces or nearby Hat Yai.
- **Sam-Sam communities:** check which feast and food words they use, Malay or Thai.
- **The Urak Lawoi of the Satun islands and the Maniq and Kensiw of the forests:** check how goods, catches and forest products are shared, through each people's own accounts.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's household economy; Thai national consumer culture; Patani households in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: trade history and household surveys are reachable; everyday worth (what a family would save first) needs interviews.
- **Lands:** the post-war nation's exact lands are not yet fixed; this Taurus series is written for the core lands (Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat, and the Malay-speaking parts of Songkhla and Satun).
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for teaching on wealth; Thai for surveys and craft studies; Dutch, English and Japanese-source translations for the port era.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** income and debt data touch poverty in a conflict zone; describe districts, never rank a people; inheritance rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** writing on cloth and spice merchants of South Asian heritage in the region's port towns (category only).
