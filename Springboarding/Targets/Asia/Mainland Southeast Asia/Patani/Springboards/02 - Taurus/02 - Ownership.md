# Patani: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for teaching on stewardship. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of the old raja and noble houses against ordinary farming families:** check what each lost or kept after the old states ended, and how each tells it.
- **Hill orchard and rubber owners of Bannang Sata, Kabang and Sri Sakhon against landless tappers and laborers:** check how a claim is described from each side of a share agreement.
- **Peat-swamp villages around Pru Toh Daeng and rice villages of the Saiburi plain:** check how shared and private uses of wet land are told apart.
- **Buddhist Thai villages and their temples:** check how temple land, family plots and state land are distinguished in daily speech.
- **Thai Chinese associations of Yala and Betong:** check collective holding by association against family ownership of shophouses.
- **Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla, Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and the Sam-Sam:** check whether land is described with Malay or Thai terms, and which papers families keep.
- **The Maniq of the Banthat range and the Urak Lawoi:** check how each names its own places and claims, through its own spokespeople.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's land customs; Thai national land policy; Patani families buying land in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: land statistics and archives are reachable; how people justify a claim needs interviews and local-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for land records and archives; Jawi for *kitab* and old deeds; Chinese for association records; Patani Malay for oral claims to land.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes with parks and the state are live grievances; record each party's own words; land law and inheritance shares belong to Libra, the purpose of law to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** studies of shophouse and trading-property holdings by South Asian-heritage families of the region's towns (category only).
