# Patani: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for religious ethics; Chinese for the towns' schools. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of traditional *pondok* and of reform-minded Islamic colleges:** check which conduct each ranks first when duties collide.
- **Narathiwat's interior villages (Cho-airong, Rueso) and Sungai Kolok's border town:** check whether trade and travel change what is held fitting.
- **Parents and children of the same households:** check which priorities each names, and where they part.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers in Saiburi and Yaring:** check how temple teaching and civil-service life rank duties.
- **Thai Chinese of Yala and Betong, and the Piyamit communities:** check how family, school and a past political movement shape stated priorities.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and the Malay-speaking villages of Chana:** check whether *adat* is named or replaced by Thai terms.
- **The Sam-Sam and the Urak Lawoi:** check whose teachings each draws on, through their own speakers.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's Islamic governance debates; Thailand's state values campaigns; Patani scholars trained in Mecca and Cairo.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: sermons, curricula and surveys exist; how people rank priorities in daily choices needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for *kitab* and sermons; Patani Malay for *pantun* and radio; Thai for curricula and surveys; Chinese for school records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates over religion and entertainment are tied to the conflict and to reform movements; never present one current's position as the region's; law and its purpose belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** moral teaching by a South Asian-founded Islamic movement active in the region (category only).
