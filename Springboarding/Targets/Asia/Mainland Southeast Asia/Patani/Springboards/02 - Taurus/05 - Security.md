# Patani: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for prayers of protection; Chinese for association funds. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber tappers and orchard laborers against teachers and other salaried civil servants:** check which kind of job households call secure, and whether it is sought for children.
- **Flood-prone villages of the Kolok and Bang Nara basins against upland villages of Yala:** check what each stores or moves when the monsoon comes.
- **Small-scale fishers of Pattani Bay and Panare:** check how bad seasons are carried (credit from middlemen, kin, the mosque).
- **Women heading households in Rangae and Raman:** check where they turn first for help, and what they keep in reserve.
- **Buddhist Thai villages that are a minority in their district:** check how they describe keeping safe and whether some families left.
- **Thai Chinese shopkeepers of Sungai Kolok and Yala:** check how they protect stock and savings, and which institutions they trust.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Songkhla's Malay districts outside the main conflict area:** check how talk of security differs where daily violence was rarer.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check food and housing security in their own terms.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's rural welfare; Thai national social protection; Patani workers in Malaysia without legal status.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on incidents and welfare coverage; thin on what security feels like and how households plan, which needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for welfare and disaster records; Patani Malay for prayer feasts and household talk; Arabic and Jawi for protective prayers; Chinese for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state security and the conflict dominate the word "security" in Thai; keep this concept on livelihood and person; emergency rules and their aims belong to Libra and Capricorn; protect anyone who speaks about threats.
- **Held for review:** mutual-aid funds of South Asian-heritage Muslim families in the region's towns (category only).
