# Patani: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older readers of Jawi and younger readers of Rumi and Thai:** check which script each insists on in religion, letters and signs.
- **Teachers and families of unregistered *pondok* and of registered Islamic schools:** check how each describes its decision and what it cost.
- **Villagers of Thepha and Chana:** check whether their refusals are framed as land, livelihood, religion or all three, in their own documents.
- **Buddhist Thai families who stayed in mixed villages and those who moved to towns:** check how each explains the decision.
- **Chinese shrine committees of Pattani town:** check how a legend of refusal is told at the festival and in print.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and Malay speakers of Pattani:** check which customs each kept and which it let go.
- **The Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe and the Maniq:** check how holding to land or forest is described in their own words.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's *kaum tua* debates; Malaysian Jawi revival campaigns; diaspora scholars in Mecca who kept Patani teaching lines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: policy history and protests are documented; everyday obstinacy and apology need ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the concordance; Jawi for school and *kitab* material; Thai for policy and protest records; Chinese for shrine booklets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective refusal is easily read into the conflict; never call a community stubborn; let each group's own documents speak; the legend touches a mosque tied to violent events.
- **Held for review:** disputes within a South Asian-founded Islamic movement's congregations in the region (category only).
