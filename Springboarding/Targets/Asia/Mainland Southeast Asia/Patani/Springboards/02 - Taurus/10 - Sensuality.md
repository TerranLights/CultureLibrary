## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women cooking for feasts and men at village tea shops:** check which pleasures of food and talk each describes, and where each takes them.
- **Young *nasyid* fans and elders who grew up with *rebana* and *rong ngeng*:** check where each draws the line between enjoyable and improper sound.
- **Coastal households of Pattani Bay and hill households of Betong and Than To:** check which foods and seasons each counts as a treat.
- **Buddhist Thai residents of Yala city and Tak Bai:** check their temple-fair foods, massage and music on their own terms.
- **Thai Chinese of Betong:** check how food, hot springs and festivals are offered to themselves and to visitors.
- **Malay-speaking villages of Saba Yoi and Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check whether dishes and scents follow Patani or Southern Thai usage.
- **The Sam-Sam:** check which festive foods and music they share with Malay or with Thai neighbors.
- **The Maniq of the forest and the Urak Lawoi of the islands:** check which tastes, sounds and comforts each names, in its own words.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's cuisine and spa trade; national Thai food culture; Patani cooks in Malaysia's restaurants.
