# Patani: Domain and folkways (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; the towns' Chinese varieties; Urak Lawoi and Maniq languages. **Concept in one line:** communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (speech ways, learning ways, siblings and neighbors, short journeys).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older villagers schooled only in *pondok* and grandchildren schooled in Thai:** check which language each uses to explain a hard thing to the other.
- **Pattani Bay's coast and the hill districts of Bannang Sata and Sukhirin:** check how far a day's errands reach and where news is swapped.
- **Border families of Sungai Kolok and Tak Bai:** check how a crossing every week changes who counts as a neighbor.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers of Saiburi and Nong Chik:** check how temple schools and Southern Thai speech carry learning in their villages.
- **Hokkien-descended families of Pattani town and Guangxi-descended families of Betong:** check which Chinese variety survives at home and where Thai takes over.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims, the Sam-Sam and Malay-speaking Chana:** check what is taught in Malay, in Thai and in Arabic in each.
- **The Urak Lawoi and the Maniq:** check how children learn their elders' languages, through each people's own teachers and representatives.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's dialect and *pondok*; Thai national schooling; Patani speakers raising children in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: the *pondok* and language policy are well studied; everyday talk, neighbor ties and short trips need recordings.
- **Lands:** no settled list of the post-war nation's lands yet exists; the Gemini series is written for Pattani, Yala, Narathiwat and the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for the court letters; Arabic and Jawi for *kitab* teaching; Thai for theses, surveys and state material; Patani Malay by ear for recordings; Urak Lawoi and Maniq through native speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language choice is part of the southern conflict's story; record speakers with consent and never treat a language as a marker of loyalty.
- **Held for review:** itinerant preaching tours of a South Asian-founded movement as a channel of village-to-village talk (category only).
