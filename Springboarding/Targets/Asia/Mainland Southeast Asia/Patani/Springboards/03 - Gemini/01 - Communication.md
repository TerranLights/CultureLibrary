# Patani: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for sermon terms. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the standards for doing it well.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders who write letters in Jawi and grandchildren who send Thai-script voice notes:** check what each considers too blunt or too cold to put in writing.
- **Malay-speaking villagers of Raman and Cho-airong in a Thai hospital:** check who speaks for the patient and how much the patient says.
- **Buddhist Thai and Malay neighbors of mixed sub-districts in Yarang and Mae Lan:** check which language carries small talk across the fence, and when it switches.
- **Shopkeepers of Yala's market streets:** check how a single counter conversation moves among Thai, Malay and Chinese.
- **Deaf children of Malay homes and their families:** check how signs, gestures and lip-reading in two spoken languages fit together.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun's La-ngu and Malay-speaking Chana:** check whether Malay politeness formulas survive in Thai-language talk.
- **The Maniq and Thai officials or visitors:** check, through the Maniq's own spokespeople, who carries a band's words outward.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantanese conversational norms; Patani migrant workers' talk with Malaysian employers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: oratory and texts are reachable; recorded everyday conversation in Patani Malay is barely collected.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay by ear for *ceramah* and greetings; Jawi for letters and *adab* manuals; Thai and Southern Thai for theses and dictionaries; Thai Sign Language for Deaf research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recordings of public talks may touch the conflict; strip names before reuse; clinic communication involves patients' privacy.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here; the talk sources named above raised no case under the standing rules.
