# Patani: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for religious notices; Chinese for association notices. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages that rely on the mosque loudspeaker and town wards that rely on LINE groups:** check where each first hears of a death or a road closure.
- **Coastal fishers of Yaring and inland tappers of Rueso:** check which forecasts and price reports each trusts to plan the day.
- **Households whose news comes in Patani Malay and those that follow Thai television:** check how the same event is told on each channel.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers of Khok Pho and Tak Bai:** check whether temple, village headman or radio is their first source.
- **Chinese association members in Yala and Betong:** check how much of their news crosses the border from Malaysian Chinese media.
- **Sam-Sam and Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check whether religious notices reach them in Thai, Malay or both.
- **The Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe and the Maniq:** check, through their own spokespeople, how outsiders' data about them is gathered and used.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's religious-notice system; national Thai media habits; Patani workers in Malaysia getting home news by phone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: print history and national media surveys are reachable; local news flow and rumor need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for manuscripts and moon notices; Thai for theses, forecasts and archives; Patani Malay for loudspeaker notices; Chinese for association material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** information and surveillance are entangled with the conflict; never name sources of rumors; the Maniq's forest knowledge is theirs to release; data-protection rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** no news or record source for this concept needed setting aside.
