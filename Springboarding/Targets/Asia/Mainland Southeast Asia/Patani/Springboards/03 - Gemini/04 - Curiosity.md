# Patani: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for the sky-reckoning texts; Chinese for family genealogies. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pupils of selective science schools and pupils of *pondok*:** check which questions each kind of school invites, and which it postpones.
- **Village women at the well or market and men at the mosque gate:** check where asking about a neighbor's affairs is ordinary and where it is resented.
- **Yala's young town dwellers and Sukhirin's hill villagers:** check how far each will travel just to see a new place.
- **Buddhist Thai teachers and monks writing local history:** check what drives them to record and what they leave unasked.
- **Chinese families of Betong tracing roots in China:** check how far the search goes and who in the family leads it.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Pattani's Malay speakers:** check whether the same prohibitions on asking and looking hold in both.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own representatives, what visitors may ask, photograph or enter.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's taboo lore; Thai national science-promotion campaigns; Patani students abroad meeting new subjects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: prohibitions and schooling are documented; everyday curiosity and nosiness need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for *falak* texts; early modern Dutch and English for the voyage; Thai for theses and forums; Chinese for genealogies; Patani Malay for greeting talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** curiosity about the conflict can endanger informants; tourist curiosity can harm the Maniq; some *pantang* knowledge is private.
- **Held for review:** for this concept no pointer on wonder or prying was put aside.
