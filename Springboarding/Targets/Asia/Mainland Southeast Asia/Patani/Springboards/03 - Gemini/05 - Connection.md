# Patani: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for pilgrimage terms; Chinese for hometown ties. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and being connected or cut off.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families with a pilgrim broker or a Mecca-based relative and families without:** check which doors such a tie opens at home.
- **Village households that keep a gift-money book and town households that give by bank transfer:** check how each tracks what it owes.
- **Older parents of Yaring and Saiburi with children in Malaysia:** check who calls, how often, and who visits at harvest.
- **Buddhist families of Mae Lan and Muslim families with *kloe* ties to them:** check whether these bonds are still made by a new generation.
- **Betong's Guangxi-descended families and Pattani town's older Hokkien families:** check which keeps the stronger link to a place in China.
- **Hill villages of Sukhirin without steady signal and lowland towns:** check how being out of reach changes who one can rely on.
- **The Urak Lawoi and the Maniq:** check, through their own representatives, which ties outsiders have helped or cut.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's kin networks; the Patani community in Mecca; Patani workers' associations in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
