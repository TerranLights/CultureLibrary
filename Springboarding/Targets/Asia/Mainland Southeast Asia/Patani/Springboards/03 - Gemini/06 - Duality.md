# Patani: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for the paired terms of Sufi teaching; Chinese for naming. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once, dividing the world into pairs, and how two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **University students in Pattani and village youth who stayed home:** check whether the pairing of Malay and Thai is felt as tension, ease or neither.
- **Intermarried Buddhist-Muslim households of Songkhla's border districts:** check which festivals and rites each household keeps, and how the children describe themselves.
- **Buddhist Thai families who speak Patani Malay and those who do not:** check what speaking the majority's language changes in their standing.
- **Thai-Chinese families of Yala and Betong:** check how Chinese and Thai names, schools and shrines are kept side by side.
- **The Sam-Sam of Satun and Songkhla:** check, in their own words, whether they see themselves as between or as a whole of their own.
- **Shadow-play audiences in Pattani villages:** check how the disguised clown is read by young and old viewers.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check how each holds its own name beside a state-issued one, through its own representatives.
- **Comparison only:** the Thai-speaking Buddhist villages of Kelantan; Patani-origin families holding Malaysian papers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: identity is among the most studied topics in the region, but mostly in political terms; paired worldviews and two-faced talk need *kitab* reading and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for Sufi texts; Patani Malay for the shadow play; Thai for theses and press; Chinese for family naming.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loyalty and identity are at the heart of the conflict; never describe any community as divided or two-faced; cross-border citizenship questions go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the classical source epic behind the shadow play's main story cycle, which is South Asian in origin (category only).
