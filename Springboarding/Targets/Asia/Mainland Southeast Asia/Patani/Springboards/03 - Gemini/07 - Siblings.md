# Patani: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for milk kinship; Hokkien and Cantonese kin terms. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households where parents work in Malaysia and households where they stay:** check how much of the parenting falls to an elder sister or brother.
- **Families that keep birth-order names and families that use given names:** check whether the eldest's authority follows the name.
- **Malay village families in Narathiwat's interior and town families in Pattani:** check who among the siblings stays to care for the parents.
- **Buddhist Thai siblings of Saiburi and Khok Pho:** check how brothers and sisters divide the rites for a dead parent.
- **Thai-Chinese siblings of Betong:** check which one keeps the family shop and the ancestral tablets.
- **Sam-Sam families with Buddhist kin:** check how siblings of different religions keep in touch.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own speakers, who is counted as brother or sister.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's birth-order naming; sibling chains of Patani workers in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: kin terms and care studies exist in Thai theses; sibling rivalry, closeness and quarrels need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for kin terms; Jawi and Arabic for milk kinship; Thai for theses and memorial books; Chinese for family records; Maniq and Urak Lawoi through their speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** orphaned children of the conflict need the strictest care; shares between brothers and sisters in an estate go to Libra; never generalize the place of daughters.
- **Held for review:** nothing on brothers and sisters was set aside here.
