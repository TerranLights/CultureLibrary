# Patani: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for religious terms of arbitration and intercession; Chinese for trade brokers. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and the go-between more broadly (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who send a respected relative to ask for a bride and families who approach directly:** check what the go-between's absence says to the other side.
- **Smallholders selling to a village middleman and those selling to cooperatives:** check how each speaks of the one in the middle.
- **Teachers of the older *pondok* lines and reform-minded teachers:** check how each explains whether anyone may stand between a believer and God.
- **Buddhist Thai and Malay village leaders of Mae Lan and Nong Chik:** check who steps between them when trouble starts.
- **Chinese families of Yala and Betong:** check whether their association or a single elder carries their quarrels to officials.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Saba Yoi's Malay speakers:** check whether *imam*, headman or schoolteacher is called first.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own accounts, who speaks between them and outsiders.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's marriage go-betweens; Malaysia's state role as facilitator; Patani labor brokers working from Malaysia.
