# Patani: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic for the religious terms; Hokkien and Cantonese for town talk. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men at village shelters and women at the feast kitchen:** check what each setting allows to be said about whom.
- **Small villages of Raman and Rangae and the wards of Yala city:** check how quickly talk of a broken engagement spreads in each.
- **Students on campus confession pages and their parents' generation:** check what each counts as harmless talk and as slander.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers of Tak Bai:** check where talk about neighbors happens around the temple calendar.
- **Chinese shopkeepers of Betong's market streets:** check how news of a family business travels among neighbors.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and the Sam-Sam:** check whether Malay religious terms for backbiting are used, or Thai ones.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own accounts, how outsiders' talk about them reaches them.
- **Comparison only:** Kedah and Kelantan village studies; Malaysian celebrity culture; Patani workers' talk in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin: gossip is rarely studied directly in the region; religious teaching and media are reachable, everyday talk needs long fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for the teaching texts; Patani Malay by ear for village talk; Thai for theses and online pages; Southern Thai for *phleng bok*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** talk about named people can harm them; record patterns, never repeat a rumor; sorcery accusations can lead to danger; slander as an offense goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** for gossip, nothing was withheld under the standing rules.
