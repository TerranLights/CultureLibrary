# Patani: Domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; the towns' Chinese varieties; Urak Lawoi and Maniq. **Concept in one line:** what home is and who counts as family (household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, building, family and child-rearing ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rice villages of the Saiburi plain with several related houses on one family plot, and single shophouses in Yala city:** check how near kin live and who eats from one kitchen.
- **Households whose working-age members spend most of the year in Malaysia:** check who heads the house meanwhile and who is still counted as living there.
- **Buddhist Thai households of Nong Chik and Tak Bai:** check their household forms and house rites on their own terms, apart from the Malay majority.
- **Chinese households of Pattani town's old quarter and Betong's hill town:** check whether three generations still share one building, and which floor each uses.
- **Malay-speaking families of Chana and Thepha and Thai-speaking Muslim families of La-ngu:** check whether house and kin words come from Malay, Thai or both.
- **Sam-Sam households:** check which kin terms, Malay or Thai, name their household.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check, through their own representatives, what each counts as one household.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's household studies; Thailand's national family statistics; Patani-descended households in Bangkok's old Malay communities and in Mecca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: household counts come from registers and surveys, while what people call home and family needs Patani Malay interviews and the few village ethnographies.
- **Lands:** Patani's post-war lands are not yet fixed, so the Cancer series addresses its core: Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat with the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay by ear for kin and house words; Jawi and Arabic for the guest *kitab*; Thai for registers; Chinese script for altar tablets; French for the excavations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** house searches and arrests in the conflict touch the home directly; never describe a household type as typical of a people; family and inheritance rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing about household forms or house ways was set aside here.
