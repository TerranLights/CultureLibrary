# Patani: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families whose *pondok* passes to a son-in-law or nephew, and families with no such school:** check how far a family line and a teaching line overlap.
- **Boat crews of Yaring made of kin and orchard families of Bannang Sata sharing one garden:** check how far shared work decides who counts as close family.
- **Converts to Islam who marry into Malay families:** check which kin they keep from before and which they join.
- **Buddhist Thai families of Saiburi and Nong Chik:** check how wide a family reaches at funerals and ordinations.
- **Chinese families in Yala's surname associations and Betong's later arrivals:** check whether a surname tie is treated as family.
- **Malay speakers of Na Thawi and Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check whether *besan* or the Thai idiom names their in-law ties.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own speakers, who is owed food, help and mourning.
- **Comparison only:** Langkawi and Kelantan kinship studies; Thai national family policy; Patani families split between Thai and Malaysian homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: kinship appears in theses and state family indexes; what kin owe each other in practice needs household interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for in-law and grandparent terms; Jawi for flyleaf notes and *silaturrahim* texts; Thai for theses and center reports; Chinese for association rolls.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin marriage and adoption are private; never present a marriage pattern as a people's custom; family law and inheritance go to Libra, parental authority to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no kin or family source here fell under the standing rules.
