# Patani: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households that follow a grandmother's first-food rules and households that follow the clinic's booklet:** check where the advice clashes and who settles it.
- **Fathers who feed and bathe infants and fathers who do not, in Rangae and in Yala city:** check how each describes a father's share of early nurture.
- **Working mothers of Pattani town and mothers of Bacho's rice villages:** check what decides breast or bottle: work hours, advice or supply.
- **Buddhist Thai families of Saiburi:** check which infant rites and *khwan* teachings shape their early care.
- **Chinese families of Betong:** check how the month-old feast and later tutoring frame a child's first years.
- **Malay speakers of Thepha and Thai-speaking Muslims of La-ngu:** check whether nurture proverbs and cradle customs survive in Thai.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own mothers and spokespeople, how infants are carried and fed in forest camps and on boats.
- **Comparison only:** Terengganu and Kelantan studies of infant feeding; Malaysian children's media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: health indicators are rich; how families talk about raising children is thin and needs interviews with mothers, fathers and grandmothers.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for al-Fatani's treatise; Thai for health data and theses; Patani Malay for grandmothers' advice; Chinese for month-feast customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-nutrition figures can stigmatize a region; give them by district; some folk healing is guarded knowledge; children's data need consent; discipline and authority belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing about feeding or raising children was put aside here.
