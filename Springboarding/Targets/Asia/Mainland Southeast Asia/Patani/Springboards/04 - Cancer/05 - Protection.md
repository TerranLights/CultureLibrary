# Patani: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mothers in confinement in rice villages of Nong Chik and new mothers in Yala's flats:** check which guards (objects, kept-away visitors, prayers) are kept in each.
- **Sons and daughters of the same families:** check who is escorted, who does the escorting, and from what age.
- **Villages with a security-volunteer team and villages without:** check how residents themselves describe who keeps watch at night.
- **Panare fishing villages that set up a protected zone and neighbors that did not:** check who decided, and who keeps watch over it.
- **Buddhist Thai households of Yaring and Mae Lan:** check which threads, amulets and monks' rites guard their homes.
- **Chinese shophouse families of Yala and Betong:** check which door guardians and shrine gods they keep, and for what.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and the Sam-Sam:** check whether Malay charms, Thai amulets or neither are used.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own accounts, what protects a forest camp or a boat at sea.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's charm traditions; Thai national child-safety campaigns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: security data is plentiful but shaped by the conflict; family and spiritual protection needs fieldwork and manuscripts.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for charms and plaques; Thai for theses and agency reports; Chinese for shrine banners; Patani Malay for village guardian lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protection here is bound up with armed security and detention; never characterize volunteers, detainees' families or any group; charm knowledge may be guarded or stigmatized; child-protection and detention law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** no guarding or charm source was held back for this concept.
