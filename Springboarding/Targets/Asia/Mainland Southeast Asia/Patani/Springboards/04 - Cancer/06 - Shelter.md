# Patani: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |

| Malay | *berteduh*; *tempat berteduh* | to take cover; a place of cover | from *teduh*, shade |

| Chinese | 收容 (*shourong*) | to take in, shelter | association and charity usage |

English keywords: evacuation centers Narathiwat floods, homelessness southern Thailand, Maniq shelters, displacement Deep South, children's shelters Pattani.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who went to relatives during the Kolok floods and families who went to school-gym centers:** check what decided each choice and how long each stay lasted.
- **Tappers who spend nights in garden huts and those who go home:** check whether the hut is spoken of as a home, a refuge or neither.
- **Children lodging with relatives in Pattani town for school:** check how hosts and guests each describe the arrangement and its limits.
- **People without a roof in Yala and Sungai Kolok:** check, through their own accounts, where they sleep and who helps.
- **Buddhist Thai families who stayed in temple halls or moved in with kin in town:** check which they call refuge, and which displacement.
- **Members of Yala's Chinese association halls:** check whether the halls open to non-members in a disaster.
- **Satun's island villages and the Urak Lawoi:** check where they take cover in storms and whose doors open to them.
- **The Maniq:** check, through their representatives, what moving a shelter means and who decides.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's flood-relief centers; Patani lodging in Mecca and in Malaysian towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: state shelter figures can be requested; everyday taking-in of kin and strangers is unrecorded and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Malay for refugee histories; Arabic for Mecca *wakaf* deeds; Thai for agency records and press; Patani Malay and Southern Thai for hut and lodging words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement in the conflict is disputed; people in shelters, above all children, must stay anonymous; never publish Maniq camp locations; asylum and shelter law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** overnight stays of a South Asian-founded preaching movement's traveling groups in village mosques (category only).
