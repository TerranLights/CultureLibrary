# Patani: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sons who nurse a bedridden parent themselves and families that leave it to daughters or daughters-in-law:** check how each explains who should do the work, by district.
- **Families with a trained carer from the long-term care program and families relying only on kin:** check what outside help changes inside the house.
- **Patients of village bone-setters and of the hospital wards in Yala and Narathiwat:** check how families decide between them, and who pays.
- **Families caring for a disabled member in Rangae and in Yala city:** check who carries the daily work, and what support reaches them.
- **Buddhist Thai elders of Khok Pho whose children have moved away:** check who looks after them, and what the temple does.
- **Chinese families of Betong:** check whether filial teaching passes on in Chinese or in Thai, and who cares in practice.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and Malay speakers of Chana:** check whether visiting-the-sick customs follow Malay or Thai forms.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their representatives, how the sick are cared for in camp or village, and how clinics reach them.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan hospitals used by border families; Thailand's national long-term care system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: health and disability data are rich; the daily work of family carers, and who does it, needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for *Bughyat al-Tullab*; Classical Chinese for the exemplars; Thai for program records and theses; Patani Malay for carers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** illness, disability and mental illness are private; never present any community as neglectful; chained patients' cases need the strictest anonymity; end-of-life choices belong to Scorpio, health rights to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the care sources named here raised nothing under the standing rules.
