# Patani: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wives whose husbands stopped sending money from Malaysia and wives whose husbands came back:** check what each calls being left, and where each turns first.
- **Orphans in mosque-run homes and orphans taken in by kin:** check how each child is spoken of, and what each is expected to give back.
- **Single mothers of Sungai Kolok and of Cho-airong's villages:** check who helps, and what is said about the father who left.
- **Buddhist Thai families of Saiburi:** check which rites they keep for kin who died with no one to make merit for them.
- **Chinese associations of Yala and Pattani town:** check who counts as a "lonely soul", and why the rites are kept.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and the Sam-Sam:** check whether tales of the leaving mother are told, and in which language.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their representatives, how members who leave a band or an island are spoken of.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's tellings of the same tales; Patani workers who stayed on in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed: state care and press data exist; the experience of being left needs careful interviews and the region's tales and songs.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for tales and songs; Thai for state data, theses and press; Chinese for rite records; Arabic for sermons on the orphan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** an accusation of abandonment can harm named people; children in care stay anonymous; never present migration as a people's desertion of their families; disappearance cases tied to the conflict need the families' consent.
- **Held for review:** for abandonment, nothing was put aside under the standing rules.
