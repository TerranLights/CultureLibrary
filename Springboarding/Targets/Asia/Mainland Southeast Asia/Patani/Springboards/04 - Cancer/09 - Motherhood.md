# Patani: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mothers in Pattani town's offices and mothers tapping rubber in Bannang Sata's hills:** check how each fits mothering around the working day.
- **Women raising a sister's or a brother's children:** check what the child calls her and what she may decide.
- **Mothers under twenty and mothers in their late thirties:** check how kin and clinics treat each.
- **Buddhist Thai mothers of Tak Bai:** check how Mother's Day and merit-making for mothers are kept in their homes.
- **Chinese mothers of Yala:** check which goddess they turn to, and what they ask for.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and Malay speakers of Saba Yoi:** check whether mothers are honored in Malay sayings, Thai ones, or both.
- **The Maniq and the Urak Lawoi:** check, through their own women, how birth and mothering are done and who helps.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's mother-in-law lore; Patani mothers working in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: birth and maternal-health data are good; how mothers describe their role, pride and strain needs interviews with mothers themselves.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for tales and songs; Thai for health data, awards and theses; Chinese for shrine books; Arabic for the sayings on mothers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** adolescent births and maternal deaths can stigmatize; give figures by district; never present one model of mother as the region's; marriage age and reproductive law go to Libra, sexuality to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none of the mother-related sources here needed holding back.
