# Patani: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*: Jawi, Thai script, Rumi); standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pondok-trained calligraphers and art-school graduates of Pattani:** check how each side talks about learning a form and departing from it.
- **Batik and weaving groups of women and carving or boat trades of men:** check who is named as the maker of a design in each.
- **Saiburi's and Panare's craft villages and Yala's town workshops:** check whether rural makers sell under their own names or a workshop's.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of La-ngu and Satun's Sam-Sam:** check which craft words and models they take from Malay or from Thai sources.
- **Chinese ritual makers and Buddhist temple artisans:** check how much a new design is welcomed or resisted in ritual objects.
- **The Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check, with their elders, who builds the ritual boat for their rite and whether its design may change.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's craft centers; Bangkok's art market buying works from the region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: crafts are well listed by the state and in theses; makers' own sense of what counts as new and fitting is thin and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for workshop talk and verse; Jawi and Arabic for *taswir* and *bid'ah* teaching; Thai for registers and theses; Chinese for paper-craft shops.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** where figurative art is questioned on religious grounds, record makers' own accounts and do not set one camp against another; Maniq and Urak Lawoi craft knowledge is theirs to share or withhold.
- **Held for review:** a South Asian source epic behind part of the local shadow-play repertoire (category only), set aside from the pointers on variation within tradition.
