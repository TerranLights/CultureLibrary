# Patani: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and the Southern Thai dialect; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women students of Pattani's campuses and young women in the villages of Rueso and Bacho:** check how dress, makeup and posting differ, and who comments on them.
- **Young rappers and older *dikir* singers:** check what each form allows a singer to say about himself.
- **Yala city and Narathiwat's border town of Sungai Kolok:** check whether expression in dress and nightlife differs where trade and travel are heavier.
- **Malay-speaking families of Na Thawi and Saba Yoi and Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check in which language each finds it easier to say how they feel.
- **Buddhist Thai residents speaking the Southern Thai dialect:** check how far they speak openly in mixed workplaces and at home.
- **Chinese of Betong and the Piyamit villages:** check which settings invite or discourage open talk, in Chinese, Thai or Malay.
- **The Urak Lawoi and the Maniq:** check whether their own songs and dances are where feeling is voiced, with their consent.
- **Comparison only:** young people's styles across the Kelantan border; the Bangkok campuses where many of the region's students live for years.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for everyday self-expression; theses and online material give a start, and lived norms need careful interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay by ear for songs and online talk; Thai and Southern Thai for theses and Buddhist voices; Jawi for the *kitab* on the tongue; Chinese for Betong's clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speech, songs and posts can carry risk under the region's security rules (the legal frame is Capricorn's and Libra's); never quote or identify a speaker without consent, and never sort a community by how outspoken it is.
- **Held for review:** the beard, head-covering and dress guidance spread through the region's mosques by a South Asian-founded missionary movement (category only), kept out of the dress pointers.
