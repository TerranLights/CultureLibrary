# Patani: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic for scripture commentary; English for international rights reports. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villagers of Bannang Sata and Rangae, where security operations have been heavy, and townspeople of Pattani:** check how treatment by officials is described in each.
- **Rubber tappers and fishing crews, and civil servants of Yala city:** check what each counts as decent work and decent treatment.
- **Old people living with children and those living alone in Narathiwat's coastal villages:** check who washes, feeds and speaks for them, and what they say about it.
- **Older Malay monolinguals of Saba Yoi and bilingual younger relatives who interpret for them:** check how being served through an interpreter is felt.
- **Buddhist Thai residents:** check how they speak of being treated by neighbors and officials since 2004.
- **Chinese families of Betong and Yala:** check how the foundations' burials for the poor are spoken of by givers and receivers.
- **Urak Lawoi families on Ko Lipe and Maniq bands near Than To:** check what each names as an affront, and who is entitled to say so for them.
- **Comparison only:** Patani workers in Malaysian restaurants; descendants of Patani captives in Bangkok's Malay communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rights reports cover detention and searches in depth; dignity in ordinary work, poverty and old age is thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for *maruah* in daily talk; Arabic and Jawi for *tafsir* and funeral manuals; Thai for theses and hospital material; English for Amnesty and UN reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture and detention allegations are contested; cite sources and their dates, and record no detainee's name without consent.
- **Held for review:** no pointer on courtesy, work or death with dignity had to be held back.
