# Patani: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and the Southern Thai dialect; Arabic; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages near the sites of the 2004 incidents (Saiburi, Tak Bai) and districts further away:** check whether the memory is carried differently by place and by generation.
- **Pupils of Thai state schools and pupils of private Islamic schools:** check which kinds of teasing or correction each meets, and from whom.
- **Young men working away in Malaysia and those who stayed:** check what each speaks of as a loss of face, and before whom.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of La-ngu and Malay speakers of Chana:** check whether teasing about accent or faith runs in both directions.
- **Buddhist Thai and Chinese families of the towns:** check how losing face in business or a family scandal is handled and hidden.
- **The Sam-Sam:** check whether they have been mocked as neither one thing nor the other, in their own telling.
- **The Maniq:** check, only through their representatives, which names and treatments they experience as contempt.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's satirical stage traditions; how Malaysian and Thai media mock each other's border peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: collective wounds are written about at length; everyday teasing, shame and saving face need interviews and school studies.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for *malu*, *aib* and folk tales; Arabic for the verses on ridicule; Thai for theses, campus and police material; Southern Thai for *nora* clowning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2004 incidents and the display of suspects involve the dead and their families; quote no victim or relative without consent; never present one community as the humiliators of another.
- **Held for review:** every source found on teasing, exposure and lost face could stay in; none needed setting aside.
