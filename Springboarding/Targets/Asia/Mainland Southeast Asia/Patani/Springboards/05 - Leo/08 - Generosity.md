# Patani: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Japanese and classical Chinese for the court's gift letters; Hokkien for the towns. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mosque congregations of prosperous town quarters and of poor fishing villages:** check who sponsors meals and *qurban*, and who only receives.
- **Families with members working in Malaysia and those without:** check whether remitting households are expected to give more at feasts.
- **Hill orchard villages of Than To and Bannang Sata, and paddy villages near Yaring:** check whether neighbors still give a day's labor at harvest or a house move.
- **Mosques of Satun town, where Thai is the sermon language, and of Thepha, where Malay is heard:** check how appeals for *sedekah* are worded and answered in each.
- **Buddhist villagers alongside Muslim neighbors:** check whether gifts cross the line at the other's festivals, and how they are received.
- **Chinese shopkeepers of Sungai Kolok and Betong:** check how giving to shrines, schools and the poor is split.
- **Maniq bands and Ko Lipe's Urak Lawoi:** check, as their spokespeople tell it, whether donated goods from outsiders were wanted and on whose terms.
- **Comparison only:** fund-raising for mosques across the Kelantan border; aid sent home by Patani associations working in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: organized religious giving leaves records; everyday open-handedness, stinginess and the debt of *budi* are spoken more than written.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for *budi* and village giving talk; Jawi for the *hikayat*; Japanese and classical Chinese for the court letters; Thai for theses and press; Hokkien for the Chinese towns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity money in the region has been suspected of funding violence; do not repeat such claims unsourced, and do not tie any charity to a side.
- **Held for review:** charitable and preaching networks founded in South Asia that collect or distribute in the region (category only), kept out of the organized-giving pointers.
