*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages led by a *tok guru*'s family and villages where elected officials hold sway:** check whose word settles a village matter, and how followers show it.
- **Fishing coast of Panare and Yaring and the orchard interior of Yala:** check whether leaders at sea and in the gardens are chosen the same way.
- **Women's groups of Narathiwat town and those of inland Rueso:** check where women lead openly and where they lead through others.
- **Mosque officials of Satun's Thai-speaking parishes and of Sam-Sam villages:** check whether an imam there is expected to preach, mediate and organize as a Pattani imam is.
- **Buddhist Thai villages:** check whether abbots, headmen or schoolteachers are followed first.
- **Chinese associations of Yala, Betong and Pattani town:** check how elected presidents and rotating festival heads share leadership.
- **The Urak Lawoi's ritual elders (*to' mo'*, โต๊ะหมอ) and the senior men and women of Maniq bands:** check, as each people tells it, whether leading a rite and leading a camp's move fall to the same person.
- **Comparison only:** party leadership in Kelantan's state government; scholars of Patani birth who led circles of students in Mecca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: elections and religious offices leave records; why people follow a given leader needs interviews and village studies.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for village talk of leaders; Arabic and Jawi for *imamah* rules; Thai for election and ministry data; Hokkien for shrine customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaders in the region have been targeted in the violence, and some movements' leadership is secret; point only to public roles and published accounts, and make no claim about who leads any armed group. Authority and obedience belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** how a South Asian-founded missionary movement picks the heads of its traveling groups in the region (category only); not used as a pointer.
