# Patani: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; classical and standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Hakka. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of the old Tengku, Nik and Wan lines and families of ordinary farmers in Raman:** check whether honor is spoken of as inherited, earned or both.
- **Young men of Tak Bai's border villages and of Yala city:** check how an insult is answered, and who steps in to stop a fight.
- **Mothers and daughters in Pattani's university district and in Saiburi's villages:** check who is said to carry the family's name, and whether that is changing.
- **Chana's Malay speakers and La-ngu's Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether a slighted man there speaks of his *maruah* and *nama*, or of Thai *kiat* and *sia chue*.
- **Buddhist farmers of the Khok Pho area:** check which oaths and debts of gratitude bind them, and to whom.
- **Chinese traders of Yala's market and Betong's shops:** check how *xinyong* is built and lost, and what happens to one who defaults.
- **Urak Lawoi boat crews and Maniq hunters:** check, only as they choose to tell it, whether a broken promise or a failed share costs a person standing, and how it is mended.
- **Comparison only:** the Tuah-Jebat argument as taught in Kelantan; honor among Patani crews and cooks in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin: honor is often invoked but rarely studied as such for the region; Thai theses and interviews are the main way in.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay and Jawi for the epic and *kitab*; Patani Malay for *maruah* and *nama* in talk; Thai and Southern Thai for Buddhist oaths and theses; Hokkien and Hakka for funeral ranks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family honor and women's conduct must be studied in women's own words; violence over honor, if found, is reported only from sources, never presumed; revenge belongs to Scorpio and the legal rules to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the oath, credit and good-name pointers raised no standing-rule issue, so none is held.
