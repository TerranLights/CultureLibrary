# Patani: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese; Urak Lawoi; the Maniq languages. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what good work and a well-kept life are taken to be (the daily and yearly round, the kept house and body, chores, craft, rest, health).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pattani Bay and the Saiburi coast against the rubber hills of Bannang Sata, Rueso and Sukhirin:** check whether tide and monsoon set the coastal day as pre-dawn tapping sets the inland one.
- **Pattani, Yala and Sungai Kolok shopkeepers, many of them Thai Chinese:** check how long the shop day runs and how Lunar New Year closures sit beside Hari Raya.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Nong Chik, Mayo and Tak Bai:** check whether Buddhist holy days (วันพระ) still mark a pause in the week.
- **Malay-speaking Chana, Thepha and Saba Yoi, and Satun's Malay-speaking pockets:** check how work and health words shift where Southern Thai is the market tongue.
- **Sam-Sam households of Satun and Songkhla:** check whether their daily round follows the Malay or the Thai village pattern.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check how fishing time has given way to tourist-season time.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's working week; Thailand's national calendar; Patani workers' days in Malaysian restaurants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: surveys give hours and rates; a good working day in local words needs Patani Malay interviews and *kitab* reading.
- **Lands:** until the author draws Patani's post-war borders, the Virgo set aims at Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat plus the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for village work talk; Jawi and Arabic for the prayer manuals; Thai for surveys and fisheries data; Chinese dialects for the shop towns; Maniq and Urak Lawoi through interpreters the communities choose.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the conflict has struck people at work (tappers, teachers, market sellers); link no group to it and keep victims' details out.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage trading families in the region's towns (category only), set aside from the shop-routine pointers.
