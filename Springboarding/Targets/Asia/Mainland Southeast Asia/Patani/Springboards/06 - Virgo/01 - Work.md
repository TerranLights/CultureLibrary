# Patani: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien; Burmese and Khmer for port crews. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber smallholders and the hired tappers who split latex with them:** check who in each pair is spoken of as "working" and who as "owning".
- **Households with sons or daughters in Malaysian kitchens and households without:** check whether a stint abroad counts as a proper start to working life.
- **Malay and Buddhist Thai government employees (teachers, clerks, health staff):** check how state salaried work is ranked against trade and farming in each community.
- **Trawler owners of Pattani port and the foreign crews they hire:** check how each side describes the work, through separate interviews.
- **Women in *budu*, *keropok* and market trade along the Saiburi and Bang Nara rivers:** check whether this income is called "work" or "helping".
- **Chinese family firms of Yala and Sungai Kolok:** check how sons, daughters and in-laws divide the shop's labor and its pay.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's informal economy across the Golok; Bangkok's Malay Muslim workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for job counts and wages from Thai agencies; thin for how a working life is judged, which needs Patani Malay life histories.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for migrant workers' and tappers' talk; Arabic and Jawi for the earning chapters; Thai for agency data; Burmese and Khmer, through interpreters, for port crews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reports of forced labor on fishing boats and of undocumented work in Malaysia; protect workers' identities and do not tie abuses to any community.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside for work; the job and labor sources named here raised no issue under the standing rules.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the Informal Employment Survey tables for Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat for the last five rounds.
2. Read Fraser's *Fishermen of South Thailand* chapters on crews and shares, and list every work term he records.
3. Search ThaiLIS for "แรงงาน ต้มยำ มาเลเซีย" and "ร้านต้มยำ ปัตตานี".
4. Search Malay manuscript catalogs for Jawi works with "kasb" or "mencari rezeki" in the title.
5. Ask the Pattani fishing port's labor coordination center for its yearly crew registration counts.
