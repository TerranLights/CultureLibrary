*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- ***Pondok* pupils and pupils of state or Islamic private schools:** check how much personal service to a teacher each setting expects, and whether it is named as such.
- **Town mosques of Pattani and Yala against village *surau* in Rangae and Kapho:** check whether upkeep is a paid post, a rota or goodwill.
- **Malay and Buddhist Thai state employees:** check whether "serving the state" carries the same words and the same pride in each language.
- **Women's and men's funeral teams:** check how the gendered division of this service is arranged and who trains newcomers.
- **Chinese temple families of Pattani town and Betong:** check how rotating offices are allotted and what they cost the holder.
- **Buddhist villages around Tak Bai's temples:** check who now serves the monks where young men have left.
- **Comparison only:** Patani families who served pilgrims in Mecca; Kelantan's mosque administration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: state service is counted; religious and neighborly service lives in practice and in *kitab* teaching.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for mosque and funeral roles; Arabic and Jawi for the student's manual; Thai for civil-service and conscription data; Hokkien for shrine records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** village security volunteering and conscription are tied to the conflict; record what the schemes ask and how people describe them, labeling no one.
- **Held for review:** a South Asian-founded Islamic preaching movement active in the region (category only), set aside from the volunteer and religious-service pointers.
