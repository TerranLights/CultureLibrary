## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elders in coastal Panare and Saiburi and in the orchard villages of Than To:** check which tasks each set of elders keeps once heavy work ends.
- **People with disabilities, including those hurt in the violence:** check, with their own groups, which roles families and mosques open or close to them.
- ***Pondok* graduates and graduates of technical colleges:** check which kind of knowledge each circle calls useful, and whether the two judge each other.
- **Chinese parents choosing schools in Yala and Betong:** check how Mandarin, Hokkien and Thai are weighed as useful to the next generation.
- **Sam-Sam families of Satun:** check whether Malay is still counted a useful language or only a family one.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check which boat and sea skills keep their worth now that tourism pays more.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's vocational schools; Bangkok's view of the south's degrees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: training and disability figures exist; the felt sense of being useful or a burden is thin in print and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for idioms of help and burden; Arabic and Jawi for *kitab* and sermons; Thai for vocational and disability data; Mandarin and Hokkien for the Chinese schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Maniq plant knowledge may be restricted; use only what the community agrees to share. Disability from the violence calls for consent and care.
- **Held for review:** no usefulness pointer was set aside; the reuse, training and *kitab* sources above stand under the standing rules.
