## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of *pondok* logic and of university science faculties:** check what each accepts as proof, and where the two meet in public debate.
- **Malay market women and Chinese shopkeepers in the same Pattani market:** check how each keeps accounts and checks a sum.
- **Rubber tappers of Bannang Sata and the buying-station owners:** check how a latex test is read and argued over from both sides.
- **Users and critics of *ketika* reckoning in Narathiwat villages:** check how each side argues its case, quoting both in their words.
- **Buddhist Thai farmers near Khok Pho and Malay farmers of the same plain:** check which extension advice and records each relies on.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's *pondok* curricula; Bangkok research centers' work on the south.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: datasets and research centers exist; how ordinary people weigh evidence against experience is thin in print.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for the logic primers; Patani Malay and Jawi for glosses and almanac booklets; Thai for statistics and surveys; Hokkien for shop records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conflict data are disputed by many parties; give each dataset's own method and take no side. Inheritance reckoning belongs to law and goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** no analysis pointer was held back; none of the logic, data or measuring sources raised a standing-rule issue.
