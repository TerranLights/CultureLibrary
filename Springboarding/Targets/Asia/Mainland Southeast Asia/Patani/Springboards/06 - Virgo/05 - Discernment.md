# Patani: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- ***Pondok* teachers and university-trained preachers in Yala:** check whether they draw the line between *syubhah* and the clearly lawful in the same places.
- **Users of dream booklets and *firasat* and the teachers who reject them:** check how each side argues, recording both in their own words.
- **Fish buyers at Pattani port and housewives at the Narathiwat morning market:** check how each judges a catch and whom each trusts to grade it.
- **Kris collectors of Raman and Malay families who keep inherited blades:** check how a blade's worth is judged by the trade and by the family.
- **Chinese shrine users in Pattani town and Buddhist temple-goers of Nong Chik:** check how a sign from the gods or a monk's advice is weighed against one's own judgment.
- **Sam-Sam communities of Satun and Songkhla:** check whether Malay or Southern Thai proverbs carry their warnings about strangers.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's *firasat* booklets; national Thai fact-checking.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin in print for everyday judgment; popular booklets and practice are rich but need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for hadith commentaries and dream booklets; Patani Malay for proverbs and market talk; Thai for fact-checks and fraud data; Hokkien for shrine practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dreams, *firasat* and shrine signs are disputed in religious debate, and Sufi teaching may be given only to members; record what is offered and label no group. Rumor in the conflict can endanger people; never repeat a rumor's content.
- **Held for review:** none; the judging, appraisal and fact-checking sources above needed no setting aside.
