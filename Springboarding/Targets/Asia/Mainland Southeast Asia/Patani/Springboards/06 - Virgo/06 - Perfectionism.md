*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Children in *tajwid* classes and the same children in state classrooms:** check where an error is corrected at once and where it is let pass.
- **Town pupils in Pattani and Yala chasing medical places and village pupils in Sukhirin and Si Sakhon:** check how far exam pressure reaches and how families speak of failing.
- **Woodcarvers on old-mosque restoration and builders of new concrete mosques:** check which standards each holds and who inspects them.
- **Buddhist lay candidates for Dhamma exams in Tak Bai:** check how exactness in Buddhist learning is kept with few teachers nearby.
- **Teochew and Hokkien ritual-craft families of Yala:** check what makes a funeral offering "wrong", in their terms.
- **Sam-Sam families of Satun preparing for Hari Raya:** check whether house and feast standards follow Malay or Thai neighbors.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's *tajwid* schools; Thailand's national exam culture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for perfectionism as a trait; rich for exact practice in recitation, crafts and exams, which need observation.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for *was-was* chapters; Patani Malay for classroom and wedding talk; Thai for exam and grading documents; Teochew and Hokkien for ritual crafts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** scrupulosity and exam distress touch on mental health; seek consent, and do not present any community as prone to it.
- **Held for review:** none for perfectionism; the recitation, exam and craft sources passed the standing rules without question.
