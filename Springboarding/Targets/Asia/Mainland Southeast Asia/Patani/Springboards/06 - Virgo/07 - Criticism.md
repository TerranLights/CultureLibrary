# Patani: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young people at *malam muhasabah* and their parents' generation:** check whether self-reproach is taught the same way now as in older *pondok* practice.
- **Reformist and traditionalist mosque circles in Yala and Pattani:** check how each criticizes the other in print and in sermons, quoting both.
- **Students of Prince of Songkla University, Pattani, and Fatoni University:** check how open campus criticism is in each, and in which language.
- **Chinese business owners of Sungai Kolok and Malay shopkeepers of the same street:** check where each takes a complaint: to the council, to an association, or to no one.
- **Buddhist villagers of Mae Lan and Muslim neighbors:** check how each complains about security measures, and to whom.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check how the community voices complaints about resorts and visitors.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's religious press; Bangkok media's criticism of the south.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: official complaints and press data exist; everyday reproach, teasing and self-criticism need Patani Malay fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for comedy and family talk; Jawi and Arabic for the polemical and counsel literature; Thai for press, complaint and rights records; Hokkien for shop and association talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public criticism of the state can carry risk; protect authors' identities, quote no one who did not publish, and characterize no student group or movement. Law-related complaint rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing for criticism was set aside; no satire, press or polemical source needed holding back under the standing rules.
