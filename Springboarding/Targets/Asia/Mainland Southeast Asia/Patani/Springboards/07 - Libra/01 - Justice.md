# Patani: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien of the towns; Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of people detained under the special laws, and families of officials, teachers and volunteers killed:** check what each asks of courts and remedy schemes, keeping their records apart and drawing no conclusion about any side.
- **Satun, where the emergency decree has not applied, and the Songkhla districts where it was lifted at different dates:** check how the written procedure for an arrest differs by place.
- **Thai Chinese traders of Betong and Sungai Kolok:** check whether a cheated trader turns first to the police, the court or an association's own arbitration.
- **Villages whose teachers trained in Mecca or Cairo and those whose teachers trained in Thai universities:** check whose ruling on a wrong a village follows when the two differ.
- **The Maniq:** check whether wrongs within a band are handled apart from state law, only as far as their representatives choose to share.
- **Beyond Patani, as foils:** Kelantan's Syariah Criminal Code (II) Enactment 1993 and its status in force *check*; the Thai Criminal Code as applied in Bangkok.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on statutes, inquests and remedy rules; mixed on court outcomes; thin on village-level judgment of wrongs, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for statutes, judgments and remedy criteria; Jawi Malay and Arabic for the fatwa literature; Patani Malay to hear how wrongs are named at home.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention, deaths in custody and prosecutions are live grievances on more than one side; cite records only, name no private person, and characterize no community or movement. Trust in courts and what law is for belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the security-law, fatwa and land-case pointers stand under the standing rules.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the three security statutes and tabulate their detention periods and liability clauses.
