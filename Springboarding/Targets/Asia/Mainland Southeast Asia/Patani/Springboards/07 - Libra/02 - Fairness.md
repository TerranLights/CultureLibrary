# Patani: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Hakka; the Maniq languages. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Landholding families and hired tappers in Rangae and Rueso:** check whether the split each side calls fair has shifted with rubber prices.
- **Boat owners and crews of Panare and Tak Bai:** check how a bad season's loss is shared out.
- **Thai Chinese shops of Yala's market streets and the Malay morning markets:** check where the price is fixed and where it is bargained, and what each side calls a fair price.
- **Students entering Pattani and Yala universities by quota and by open exam:** check what each group says about how places are given.
- **Sam-Sam and Malay-speaking villages of Satun:** check whether inheritance among siblings runs by the 1946 Act or by family agreement.
- **The Maniq:** check how a hunt or a forest harvest is divided, only through what their representatives choose to share.
- **Kelantan and Kedah, for contrast only:** their minimum wages and market inspections across the border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: statutes and wage notices are open; share arrangements on boats and holdings sit in practice, not in print.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for tapping and boat talk; Thai for wage, measure and quota rules; Jawi and Arabic for zakat categories; Hokkien for the shop towns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** complaints against officials can expose complainants; quote no individual, and cite quota debates from published sources only.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld on fair shares; the measure, wage and tale pointers met the standing rules.
