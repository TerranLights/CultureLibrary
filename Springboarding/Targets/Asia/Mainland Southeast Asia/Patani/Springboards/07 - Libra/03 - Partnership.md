# Patani: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese of Betong and the towns. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Couples married under the 1946 Act and Buddhist Thai couples of Mueang Narathiwat married at the district office:** check how the paperwork of marriage and of parting differs.
- **Buddhist-Muslim couples, and Sam-Sam families of Songkhla's Malay districts:** check which partner converts, which rite is held, and which register records it.
- **Pondok-teaching families of Yaring and Saiburi and trading families of Pattani town:** check how far marriages there join households for learning or for trade.
- **Yala's Chinese firms and Malay rubber dealers:** check how each writes or does not write a partnership down.
- **The Urak Lawoi of the Satun islands:** check how a boat partnership and a marriage are made, in their own words where they share them.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's Islamic Family Law Enactment; Thai civil marriage elsewhere; Patani couples who settled in Kuala Lumpur.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on statutes and registers; mixed on the couple's own view, which needs women's and men's interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for the marriage manuals and the *nikah* forms; Thai for business registers; Chinese dialects for firm histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child marriage, polygamy and unregistered cross-border unions touch private lives; use aggregate records and published cases only.
- **Held for review:** for partnership, no marriage, firm or alliance source here needed setting aside.
