# Patani: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Cantonese (Betong); Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Heirs settling estates by *faraid* and families that divide land equally by agreement in Raman and Kabang:** check where the written shares are followed and where they are traded off.
- **Malay healers of inland Rueso and Chinese medicine halls of Betong:** check whether "hot" and "cool" sort the same foods in both systems.
- **Buddhist Thai villages around Mueang Yala:** check how the middle-path teaching is used in daily advice.
- **Fishers of the Pattani Bay sand spit and farmers near the Narathiwat peat swamp:** check what each names as the land or sea going out of balance.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether *wasatiyyah* sermons reach them in Thai and with the same examples.
- **The Maniq of the Hala-Bala forest edge:** check how forest balance is spoken of, through their chosen representatives.
- **Outside the region, as contrast:** Kelantan's own hot-cold healing; national Thai budget lines for other regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: statutes and budgets are open; bodily and ecological balance in local terms needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for the inheritance texts; Patani Malay for healers' terms; Thai for the administrative acts; Cantonese and Hokkien for the herbal shops.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the civil-military balance and local-autonomy proposals are politically charged; cite texts, not people. Healers' knowledge may be guarded; record only what they offer.
- **Held for review:** no balance pointer had to be withheld; inheritance, healing and budget sources met the standing rules.
