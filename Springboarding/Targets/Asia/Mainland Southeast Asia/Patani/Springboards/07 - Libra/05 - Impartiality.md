# Patani: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Teochew of the towns. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages where one family has held the headman's post for decades and those with contested elections, in Raman and Cho-airong:** check what residents say an even-handed headman would do differently.
- **Thai Chinese business circles of Yala and Betong:** check how far *guanxi* and association membership steer contracts and credit, as association records show.
- **Muslim and Buddhist parties in the same Pattani court:** check whether interpreters, judges and lawyers are drawn from both communities, and what the parties report.
- **Pondok-trained and university-trained religious teachers:** check which examiners each trusts, and why.
- **Malay-speaking villages of Saba Yoi and Thepha:** check whether Songkhla's officials are seen as further from local kin ties than those of the three core provinces.
- **The Urak Lawoi:** check how disputes with resort owners are judged when officials and investors know each other, from records they share.
- **Kelantan, as an outside foil only:** its Syariah court appointments and recusal rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rules and procurement data; mixed on recusal practice; thin on how favoritism is talked about locally.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for the codes, NACC and procurement records; Jawi and Arabic for the *qadi* chapters; Patani Malay to hear words like *orang dalam* in use; Hokkien for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming officials in favoritism cases can harm them or their sources; work from published rulings only. The peace-dialogue item stays at the level of public statements.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on judges, contests or elections had to be put aside here.
