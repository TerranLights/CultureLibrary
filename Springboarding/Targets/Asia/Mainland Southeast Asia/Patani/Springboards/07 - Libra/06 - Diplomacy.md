# Patani: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; classical Chinese, early modern Dutch, English and Japanese for the port's records. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delegations of *ulama* and village elders in Pattani and Narathiwat meeting provincial officials:** check who speaks first, in which language, and how a request is framed.
- **Thai Chinese associations of Yala and Narathiwat towns:** check how they keep working ties with both the state and Malay neighbors, as their own histories record it.
- **Buddhist Thai residents of Mueang Pattani:** check how they describe the etiquette of visiting Muslim neighbors and hosting them.
- **Women's networks in the region:** check what part their members have taken in local and national talks, from their own reports.
- **The Maniq:** check how their dealings with forest officials and lowland villages are arranged, as their representatives tell it.
- **Contrasts beyond Patani:** Kelantan's protocol with Bangkok in the tribute era; Patani students' associations in Malaysia and Egypt, and what part they take in representing the region abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 17th-century court and the present talks; thin for everyday tact, which needs speech recordings.
- **Languages needed:** Malay (Jawi) for the chronicle; classical Chinese, early modern Dutch and Japanese for the port records; Thai and Malay for the peace documents; Patani Malay for speech studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the peace dialogue is ongoing and its parties are named in law and in the press; point to documents only and characterize no party.
- **Held for review:** trade and envoy contacts between the old port and South Asian coastal states (category only), set aside from the court-diplomacy pointers.
