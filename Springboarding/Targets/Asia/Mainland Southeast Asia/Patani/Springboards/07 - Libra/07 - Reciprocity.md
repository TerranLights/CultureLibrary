# Patani: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Teochew; the Maniq languages. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mosque-centered villages of Yarang and Mayo and new housing estates around Mueang Yala:** check whether a *khairat* fund or a savings scheme carries the mutual help in each.
- **Rice villages of Bacho and Tak Bai and rubber villages of Than To:** check whether help is returned in labor, in cash, or not tracked.
- **Thai Chinese families of Yala's market and Buddhist Thai families of Khok Pho:** check which occasions oblige a return gift and how soon.
- **Households with members working in Malaysia and households without:** check what money sent home buys in standing and obligations.
- **The Maniq and the lowland villages they trade with:** check what each side says it gives and gets, through representatives the Maniq choose.
- **Elsewhere, for contrast only:** *gotong-royong* programs in Kelantan; Patani workers' rotating savings among themselves in Malaysian cities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: cooperatives, trade and transfers are counted; everyday return of favors needs interviews and household diaries.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for the gift law; Patani Malay for exchange-labor talk; Thai for the statutes and surveys; Hokkien or Teochew for the Chinese foundations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** negative reciprocity in the conflict (retaliation) is not scouted here; revenge belongs to Scorpio, and any case is cited from records only. Household debts are private; use aggregates.
- **Held for review:** nothing on gifts or mutual aid was set aside; the *kitab*, cooperative and border-pass pointers passed the standing rules.
