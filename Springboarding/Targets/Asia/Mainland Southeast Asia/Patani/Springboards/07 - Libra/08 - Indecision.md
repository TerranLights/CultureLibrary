# Patani: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic; Hokkien and Teochew (shrine usage). **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families who set a wedding day by *ketika* and those who choose by school holidays, in Yarang and Mueang Narathiwat:** check who in each family settles the date.
- **Young people of Sungai Kolok weighing work in Malaysia and those of Pattani town weighing university:** check how long the choice takes and whom they consult.
- **Thai Chinese worshippers at the Lim Ko Niao shrine and Buddhist Thai at Wat Chang Hai:** check whether drawing fortune sticks settles a choice or only reopens it.
- **Villagers near the Chana estate site and those in Chana town:** check how each side describes the long delay in deciding.
- **Maniq bands of the Banthat range:** check how a move of camp is decided and by whom, as their representatives describe it.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether *istikharah* is taught there in Thai or in Malay, and in what words.
- **Comparison only:** Kelantan's state moon-sighting decisions; Patani families in Mecca choosing whether to return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin for personal wavering; mixed for collective delay (minutes, hearings); rich for law on deciding in advance.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for *ketika* manuscripts and prayer manuals; Thai for the statutes and court cases; Hokkien or Teochew for fortune-stick verses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' readings and family health choices are private; record only what people offer. The Chana dispute has named parties; use public records.
- **Held for review:** none; the reckoning, prayer, lottery and hearing pointers all stand under the standing rules.

## 5. First queries
1. Find a *Taj al-Muluk* edition and list each kind of decision its *ketika* tables cover.
2. Search Thai press for "ดูเดือน จุฬาราชมนตรี ปัตตานี ไม่ตรงกัน" for years when announcements differed.
3. Search the Administrative Court database for "ล่าช้าเกินสมควร" with ปัตตานี, ยะลา or นราธิวาส.
4. Ask a Pattani hospital ethics office for annual counts of section 12 directives.
5. Read the Chana industrial estate hearing records and list each postponement with its date.
