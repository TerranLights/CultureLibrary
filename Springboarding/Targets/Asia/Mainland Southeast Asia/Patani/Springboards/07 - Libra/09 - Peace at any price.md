# Patani: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Hokkien of the towns; Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wives seeking divorce before Islamic committees in Pattani and in Satun:** check how many reconciliation rounds each committee requires and who attends them.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Yaring and Sai Buri and Malay villages beside them:** check what each says is left unsaid between neighbors, without drawing conclusions for either.
- **Thai Chinese shopkeepers of Sungai Kolok and Tak Bai:** check what accommodations to curfews and security rules they describe making to keep trading, from published sources only.
- **People who entered the section 21 route and families who chose a trial:** check what the written terms offered each, from court and program records.
- **The Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check what they say they gave up in land disputes to avoid confrontation, in their own words where shared.
- **Elsewhere, as contrast only:** Kelantan's family-court reconciliation rules; peacebuilding programs in Mindanao.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: statutes and surveys are open; silence, by its nature, leaves thin records and needs careful interviewing.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for the *sulh* chapter; Thai for the criminal code, ISA and program rules; Patani Malay for wives' and villagers' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** amnesty-like programs, family violence and silence under threat are highly sensitive; never identify participants, and characterize no community, movement or security body. What law is for, and obedience to it, belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing put aside on settlement and silence; the *sulh*, statute and survey pointers met the standing rules.
