*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai script and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic; Hokkien of the towns; the Maniq languages. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buddhist Thai residents and Malay Muslims of the same districts in Yala and Pattani:** check which labels each meets from the other and from Bangkok, and the context in which each label is used.
- **Thai Chinese families of Betong:** check how they describe being cast by others, and whom they in turn name, from their own published histories.
- **Villages where *main teri* is still held and villages where it no longer is:** check where misfortune is now traced instead.
- **Students in Pattani's universities and older villagers of Kapho and Thung Yang Daeng:** check whether conspiracy accounts of local violence differ by generation.
- **The Maniq:** check which outsiders' names for them they reject, such as *Sakai* (ซาไก), and what they say those names carry.
- **Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims and the Sam-Sam:** check whether they are grouped with the core provinces in national blame or set apart.
- **Across the border, as contrast only:** Kelantan's reporting of the region; Bangkok national-press framing of "the south".
