# Patani: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), written in Jawi, Thai letters and Latin; standard Malay; Thai and its southern dialect; Arabic for the *kitab*; the towns' Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese; Urak Lawoi; Maniq (Mani). **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how death, the hidden and power are faced, with the folkways of dying and mourning, sex and courtship, inheritance and pooled goods.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Malay villages (Yaring, Saiburi, Tak Bai) and rubber-hill ones (Bannang Sata, Rueso, Sukhirin):** check whether burial, memorial feasts and estate division shift when kin are scattered by work.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Nong Chik and Khok Pho:** check how cremation, merit for the dead and inheritance run beside a Muslim majority.
- **Thai Chinese families of Yala, Pattani town and Betong:** check which of the funeral, ancestor and estate customs carried from the dialect homelands are still kept, and by which associations.
- **Sam-Sam of Satun and Songkhla:** check where mixed descent shows in the rites a family uses to bury and to divide goods.
- **Maniq bands of the Banthat and Hala-Bala forests and the Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check death, burial and shared-resource customs only in their own representatives' accounts.
- **Outside Patani, for contrast:** Kelantan's funeral and *faraid* practice; Buddhist funerals elsewhere in Southern Thailand; Patani families who bury their dead in Malaysia or Mecca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on vital and incident counts; mixed on rites (theses, religious print); thin on sex ways and on the forest and sea peoples.
- **Lands:** with the post-war borders still open, this note stands once for the whole Scorpio set: it writes for the three provinces and the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for the eschatology and funeral *kitab*; Thai for health data and theses; Chinese for the towns' funeral trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conflict deaths and their graves; healers' secret knowledge, which keepers may withhold; sexuality. Cite records, seek consent, characterize no community or party.
- **Held for review:** burial grounds and death rites kept apart by a South Asian-heritage congregation in a market town of the region (category only), left out of the funeral pointers.
