# Patani: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai and Latin letters; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic terms of repentance and resurrection; Chinese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending (conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, the dead returning).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chinese wives and husbands who entered Islam to marry into Malay families in Yala and Pattani town:** check how the new name, dress and kin ties are taken on, and what is kept from before.
- **Sam-Sam lineages in Satun's Khuan Kalong and Songkhla's Chana:** check stories of a family line moving between temple and mosque across generations.
- **Young men leaving drugs in coastal Panare and in Rangae's rubber villages:** check whether the mosque, the clinic or the family leads the return.
- **Buddhist Thai of Pattani's and Yala's towns:** check whether rebirth in a coming life, merit and retreat come up in their talk of starting over.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe and Maniq groups now in fixed villages:** check, through their own spokespeople, what renewal rites persist and how settlement is framed.
- **For contrast:** Kelantan's state-run *saudara baru* programs; addiction treatment in Hat Yai.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on addiction treatment and on creed texts; thin on reincarnation talk in the towns and on the forest and island peoples' renewal rites.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for *taubat* and resurrection articles; Patani Malay to hear testimony of a changed life; Thai for clinic and prison records; Chinese for temple talk of rebirth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the return of former fighters touches the conflict; cite program and press sources only and say nothing of any group. Conversion stories can expose families; ask consent.
- **Held for review:** guidance on becoming a "new" Muslim spread by a South Asian-founded preaching movement in village mosques (category only), set apart from the *taubat* pointers.

## 5. First queries
1. In *'Aqidat al-Najin*, mark the articles on the grave, the rising and the gathering, and note the Jawi terms used for each.
2. Ask Thanyarak Pattani Hospital for its yearly admissions and readmissions by province.
3. Search ThaiLIS for "การกลับไปเสพซ้ำ ปัตตานี" and "การบำบัด ยาเสพติด นราธิวาส" and sort by method.
4. Ask *pondok* teachers whether *mandi Safar* is still held anywhere near Saiburi or Tak Bai, and where it was last done.
5. Search YouTube for "haji mabrur ceramah Patani" and log what each teacher says the pilgrim returns to.
