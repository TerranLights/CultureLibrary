# Patani: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), in Jawi and in Thai and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic moral terms; Chinese of the market towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village officials of Rueso, Cho-airong and Bacho and the people they serve:** check, from published reports only, how the label of informer is applied and resisted.
- **First wives in Narathiwat's border towns and households where a husband married again across the border:** check what is known, told or hidden, and by whom.
- **Rubber tappers of Yala's hills and the market-town buyers:** check which words are used for cheating in a sale, on both sides of the scale.
- **Buddhist Thai and Muslim neighbors in Panare and Mayo:** check how each side recalls friendships that ended or held.
- **Chinese partners in Pattani town and Betong shops:** check how a partner who leaves with the trade is spoken of in Chinese and in Thai.
- **Maniq bands and Urak Lawoi families:** check, only as their spokespeople put it, which promises made by outsiders over land, aid or schooling are counted as broken.
- **For contrast:** Kelantan's *derhaka* lore and its own court-and-ruler tales.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich in press on marriage across the border and on politics; thin and dangerous on informing; thin on infidelity outside online confessions.
- **Languages needed:** Portuguese or a translation for Mendes Pinto; Patani Malay for dramas and confessions; Thai for press, surveys and theses; standard Malay for the Malaysian side of cross-border marriage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming anyone as an informer can endanger lives; use only published, anonymized sources and never characterize a group, movement or community. Infidelity cases need privacy.
- **Held for review:** the betrayal sources chosen (travel narrative, theses, fiction, press) cleared the standing rules, so this sheet holds none back.
