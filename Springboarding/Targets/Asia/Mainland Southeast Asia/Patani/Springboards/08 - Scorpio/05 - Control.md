# Patani: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), spelled in Jawi, in Thai letters and in Latin; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic creed terms; the towns' Chinese; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control as discipline is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's autonomy).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Students of Islamic private schools and of state schools in Yala and Pattani:** check whether locus-of-control scores and talk of *takdir* differ by schooling.
- **Rice villages of the Pattani plain under the irrigation scheme and upland orchards outside it:** check how much farmers say they can steer the season.
- **Wives in town households and wives on hill rubber farms:** check who sets limits on movement, money and phones, and whether wives name it as control.
- **Chinese shopkeepers of Betong and Yala:** check which steps (the almanac, a shrine visit, insurance) they take to steer an uncertain venture.
- **Buddhist Thai villagers of Mae Lan and Khok Pho:** check how guns, guards and routines figure in their sense of safety, from published interviews.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe; Maniq of the Banthat range:** check, in their spokespeople's own terms, who controls access to the sea and the forest they use.
- **For contrast:** Kelantan's *latah* cases; locus-of-control studies from Bangkok.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good on dams, licenses and scale studies; thin on coercive control and on the fate-and-effort balance outside sermons.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic terms inside the Jawi attribute primers; spoken Patani Malay to follow talks on striving and fate; Thai for irrigation, license and psychology records; Cantonese to read the almanac's day tables.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the state's security controls (curfews, checkpoints, detention) belong to Libra and Capricorn as law; here, cite only how people describe living with them, and never characterize any community or group.
- **Held for review:** the steering-and-restraint sources listed (primers, scales, dams, almanacs) needed no setting aside.
