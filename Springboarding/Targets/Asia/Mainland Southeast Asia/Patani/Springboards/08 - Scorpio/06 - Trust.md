# Patani: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households near hospitals in Mueang Pattani and those in hill villages of Raman far from them:** check whom each turns to first when ill, and what reasons are given.
- **Teochew and Hokkien traders of Yala and Betong:** check whether credit between Chinese shops runs on kin, dialect, or written guarantees.
- **Tak Bai and Sungai Kolok households sending sons to Malaysian kitchens:** check which carrier, changer or bank gets the wages home, and on whose word.
- **Buddhist Thai and Malay Muslim residents of the same subdistrict:** check, through surveys and published interviews, how each rates the subdistrict office and the mosque or temple.
- **Sam-Sam families of the Satun border:** check whether kin across the line are trusted with money and children differently from neighbors at home.
- **Maniq bands; Urak Lawoi households:** let their chosen spokespeople say which traders, officials or researchers came to be relied on, and by what steps.
- **For contrast:** generalized-trust items in Thailand's national samples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: fair on finance and health; thin on trust between neighbors outside survey items; trust in the security forces and courts belongs to Capricorn.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for *ceramah* and scam notices; Jawi for *akhlak* chapters; Thai for theses and bank surveys; Teochew or Hokkien for Chinese trade credit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions of whom people trust in the conflict can expose them; use published, anonymized data only, and characterize no group. Money carriers may work outside the law; record practice, not names.
- **Held for review:** informal money-sending networks run by a South Asian-heritage minority of the border towns (category only); omitted from the carrier and changer pointer.
