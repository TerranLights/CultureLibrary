# Patani: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-letter and Latin forms; standard Malay; Thai; Qur'anic Arabic of the pardon verses; Cantonese and Hokkien of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families bereaved by killings in Bannang Sata and Rangae and families bereaved by road deaths there:** check whether talk of *dendam*, forgiveness or fate differs by the kind of death.
- **Young men in Yala's and Sungai Kolok's town gangs and those in *pondok*:** check how each answers an insult, from published studies and their own accounts.
- **Contenders in subdistrict elections and their kin:** check, through press reports, how a local defeat is answered, avenged or let go.
- **Cantonese families of Betong and Hokkien families of Pattani town:** check whether symbolic revenge rites are known, kept or mocked.
- **Buddhist Thai villages of Sai Buri and Thung Yang Daeng:** check whether the angry-dead lore shapes how a violent death is followed up.
- **Urak Lawoi and Maniq:** check, as their own speakers choose to tell it, how quarrels with outsiders are answered or left.
- **Outside the nation, as a foil:** how Kelantan viewers discuss the same *drama dendam* online; grudge killings in Hat Yai's crime press.
