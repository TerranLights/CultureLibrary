# Patani: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*: Jawi, Thai and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Thai; Arabic terms of blessing and sanctity; Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how who holds it, how it is held and how it is used are judged (legitimate authority is Capricorn's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old noble lineages (Tengku, Nik, Wan) and families risen through trade or office:** check how each describes the roots of its standing.
- **Villages of Yarang and Yaring around famous *keramat* graves and villages without one:** check whether a saint's power changes who leads locally.
- **Chinese business families of Yala and Betong:** check how power runs through the chamber of commerce, clan associations and shrines.
- **Buddhist Thai officials posted from elsewhere and local-born officials:** check how each names the real power in a district.
- **Women leaders in subdistrict councils and in civic networks:** check which kinds of influence they say they hold.
- **Maniq and Urak Lawoi:** check, as their spokespeople set out, who speaks for the group to outside powers and how that role is filled.
- **For contrast:** provincial *chao pho* in other Thai regions; Kelantan's royal and party power.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on elections and business boards; thin on hidden power and on spiritual power beyond site lists.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Malay for *daulat* and *keramat* talk; Patani Malay for sayings and satire; Thai for state lists and theses; Hakka, Hokkien or Cantonese for shrine mediums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** power in the conflict (armed groups, security agencies) must be cited, never judged; naming "influential persons" can harm them. The law side of authority is Capricorn's and Libra's.
- **Held for review:** village religious leadership by graduates of South Asian seminaries (category only), left out of the *keramat* and dynasty pointers.
