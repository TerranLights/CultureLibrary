# Patani: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), in Jawi, in Thai letters and in Latin; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; the Arabic of creed and worship; Hokkien and Hakka of the shrine committees; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the domain of belief and philosophy: what is held true and where meaning is sought, with religious ways (faiths, sacred space, clergy, calendar), freedom ways, higher learning and long journeys.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Town mosques of Mueang Pattani, Yala and Narathiwat against hill-district *surau* (Bannang Sata, Si Sakhon, Kabang):** check how preachers and teachers are chosen, to map religion below the province.
- **Traditionalist and reformist congregations (the *Khana Kau* and *Khana Mai* of Thai writing, *check*):** compare where each places religious authority, in their own publications, without ranking either.
- **Buddhist Thai of Nong Chik, Mae Lan, Yaring's temple villages and Yala town:** check temple life, monks' numbers and lay ritual district by district.
- **Betong, Yala and Sungai Kolok Chinese families:** check, town by town, which shrine, temple or church each belongs to.
- **Sam-Sam and Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and Songkhla's Chana, Thepha and Saba Yoi:** check where prayer language and religious teachers differ from Malay-speaking congregations.
- **Maniq of the Banthat and Hala-Bala forests; Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check religious life only through accounts their communities approve, including the conversions urged on them.
- **For contrast, outside Patani:** Kelantan's state religious council and *pondok*; Patani families settled in Mecca and Kuala Lumpur.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islam and its texts; mixed for Buddhist and Chinese religion in the region; thin for Christian, Maniq and Urak Lawoi religious life.
- **Lands:** no settled lands are listed yet (the row carries pre-war Thailand); for that reason, stated once, Sagittarius scouts the three provinces plus Malay-speaking Songkhla and Satun.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi to read what the *Tok Guru* teach; Thai for the census and temple counts; Chinese characters for Betong's shrine booklets; community interpreters for Urak Lawoi and Maniq.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion is bound up with the southern conflict; no community, movement or group is characterized here. Maniq and Urak Lawoi sacred knowledge may be restricted; ask first.
- **Held for review:** touring lay preachers sent out by a South Asian-founded missionary body, a real presence on the faith map (category only), kept off the landscape rows.
