# Patani: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*, in Jawi, Thai-script and romanized forms); standard Malay; Thai; Mandarin and Hakka for Betong's settlement villages; Maniq; Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, hold a faith and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal (deciding for oneself and not depending on others stay in Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women traders of Pattani's town markets and young women of Bacho villages:** check the freedoms of work, dress and travel each describes, in published studies and interviews.
- **Young people in *pondok* dormitories and in Yala's state secondary schools:** compare which limits on speech, dress and outings each names, and who is said to impose them.
- **Former guerrillas and their children in Betong's Piyamit villages:** check whether "liberation" still names anything for the second generation.
- **Buddhist Thai of Mae Lan and Nong Chik:** check how curfews, escorts and fear of travel shape their movement, as they tell it.
- **Households holding papers on both sides of the Kolok river:** check whether dual identity widens or narrows what they may do, by their own account.
- **Maniq of Satun's Manang district:** check, with their spokespeople, the change between forest movement and registered settlement.
- **For contrast:** religious-freedom arrangements across the line in Kelantan; Patani workers' freedoms of movement in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich in rights and press reports; thin in what residents themselves name as freedom; collective freedom (*merdeka*) is deeply contested.
- **Languages needed:** to read testimony on fear and release, Patani Malay; to follow the hijab and SIM-card disputes, Thai; to read the ex-guerrillas' memoirs, Mandarin and some Hakka.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the independence question, disappearances and surveillance. Cite records, protect informants, and characterize no movement, party or community. The special security laws are Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none; on liberty, bondage and movement nothing had to be set apart.
