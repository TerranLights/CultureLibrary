## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of Pattani's *pondok* and of the Pattani university campus:** check what each cites to settle a disputed point: a graded hadith, a *kitab*, a textbook or a scientist.
- **Families of those killed at Krue Se and Tak Bai, and the officials of those years:** check how each account of the same days was recorded and kept, without weighing one above the other.
- **Mueang Yala's Buddhist Thai residents:** compare the sources they name for the first hour after a blast with those named a week later.
- **Fishers of the Narathiwat coast at Bang Nara:** check whether the forecast, the radio or the elders' signs decide whether a boat goes out.
- **Chinese shopkeepers of Sungai Kolok:** check whether Thai, Malaysian or Chinese-language news is trusted first on border matters.
- **Kensiw of Ban Sakai in Than To and the Maniq of the Banthat range:** check whose account of their past and their names appears in print, and what they say of it.
- **For contrast:** Kelantan's religious-council rulings; Patani students in Cairo and Medina, who meet other standards of proof.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on contested history and incident counts; thin on how ordinary people check claims; the conflict makes every fact a possible dispute.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic grading terms and Jawi glosses for the *pondok* method; Malaysian Malay for the cross-border press; Thai for the incident tallies and theses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Krue Se and Tak Bai accounts, incident attribution and the land's name are all live disputes. Cite each side's record; characterize none.
- **Held for review:** truth is scouted here without any pointer kept back; the method texts, tallies and newsrooms all clear the rules.
