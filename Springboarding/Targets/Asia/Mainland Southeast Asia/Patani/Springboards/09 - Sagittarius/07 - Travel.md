# Patani: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), whose travel words appear in Jawi, in Thai spelling and in Rumi; standard Malay as used in Kelantan and Kuala Lumpur; Thai; the Arabic of the pilgrimage; Chinese of the temple tours; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the road, the foreign and the traveler are regarded; short trips and commuting stay in Gemini.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pilgrim families of Saiburi and Bacho and households that have never sent anyone to Mecca:** check how the journey is saved for, delayed or given up.
- **Graduates back from Cairo and those back from Malaysian universities:** check what each says the time abroad gave and took away.
- **Buddhist Thai of Yala town and of Khok Pho:** check where they travel for merit, holidays and family, and whether the conflict changed the routes.
- **Chinese families of Pattani town who host the festival's visitors:** check how guests from abroad are received and lodged.
- **Urak Lawoi boat families of Ko Lipe:** check, with their consent, which voyages are remembered and which are still made.
- **Maniq of the Banthat range:** ask their chosen representatives which far journeys (to markets, clinics, kin) are made now, and why.
- **For contrast, outside Patani:** Kelantanese pilgrims and their brokers; Patani workers who travel the Malaysian peninsula for jobs (their own route and story, kept as comparison).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the pilgrimage (history, commerce, rite); mixed on tourism (official counts, advisories); thin on Buddhist, Chinese and sea-people travel from the region.
- **Languages needed:** the Arabic formulas and Patani Malay talk of the send-off; Thai to read *nirat* verse and the tour records; Chinese for the festival pilgrims' groups; English for the advisories and Tagliacozzo.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** travel advisories and exile journeys touch the conflict; record them, characterize no one. Forced journeys and flight belong to the history sources, not to this concept's leisure pointers.
- **Held for review:** long preaching journeys, between villages and abroad, organized by a revival movement founded in South Asia (category only); kept out of the pilgrimage and lodging rows.
