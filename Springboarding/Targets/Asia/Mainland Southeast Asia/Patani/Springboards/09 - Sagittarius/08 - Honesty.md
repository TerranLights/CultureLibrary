## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber sellers of Bannang Sata and the buying stations they deal with:** check how each side says it judges whether the other is dealing straight.
- **Gold-shop owners of Yala's Chinese quarter and their Malay customers:** check which signs of honest dealing (receipts, posted prices, years of custom) each side names.
- **Students of the Pattani campus and of Fatoni University:** check whether cheating is described differently in religious and secular settings.
- **Savings-group members in a Buddhist village of Khok Pho and a Muslim one of Nong Chik:** check how each group words its rule on honesty and handles a defaulting member.
- **Market traders of Tak Bai and Sungai Kolok who sell to Malaysian visitors:** check what they say is fair to tell, or not tell, a buyer from across the border.
- **Ko Lipe's Urak Lawoi fishing crews:** compare what they say a fair *tauke* does at the scale with what they say an unfair one does.
- **For contrast:** Kelantan's markets and their weights inspectors; Patani shop workers in Malaysia and the honesty demanded of them.
