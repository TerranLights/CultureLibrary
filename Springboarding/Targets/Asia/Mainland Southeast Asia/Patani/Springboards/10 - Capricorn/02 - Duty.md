# Patani: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; *kitab* Arabic; Hokkien and Hakka; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households with a son or daughter working in Malaysian restaurants, against those whose children stayed:** check how duties to parents are carried out from afar.
- **Eldest children against youngest in Malay families:** check who is expected to stay near the parents and why, as families explain it.
- **Buddhist families of Khok Pho and Sai Buri's temple villages:** check what sons and daughters owe parents in ritual and in care.
- **Shophouse families of Betong and Yala:** check who keeps the ancestral altar after a father dies, and who takes over his association dues.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and of Saba Yoi:** check whether state duties (the draft, territorial defense training) sit differently with them.
- **Urak Lawoi elders of Ko Lipe:** check what is owed to the ancestors at their own rites, as they choose to tell it.
- **Maniq camps:** check, through a trusted intermediary, what a band member must share or do for others.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** religious and state obligations are well documented; filial care partly studied; almost nothing in print on Maniq or Urak Lawoi obligations.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi to read the primer's lists of what is owed, with Arabic for its terms; Thai for the head-tax files and the officials' pledge; Patani Malay to hear obligation named at home.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conscription, the territorial defense program and teachers' posts are entangled with security; no group's sense of duty is to be judged, only recorded from sources.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing owed to God, parents, the dead or the Thai state had to be kept off this sheet.
