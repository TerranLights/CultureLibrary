## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber-tapping families of Bannang Sata and Rangae, against trading families of Pattani town:** check whether failure is put down to effort, prices or God's will.
- **Families of young people in juvenile centers, against their village committees:** check who is said to answer for a youth's wrong: the youth, the parents or the village.
- **Mosque congregations in Sai Buri and Yaring:** check how an *imam* is called to account, by vote, by talk or by the provincial committee.
- **Buddhist families of Mae Lan and Khok Pho:** check how fault is assigned after a loss, in their own words.
- **Shop owners of Sungai Kolok's market streets and Betong:** check who is expected to cover a partner's unpaid debt.
- **Sam-Sam households of Satun:** check whether a child is taught to be answerable in the same terms in Thai and in Malay.
- **Forest Maniq and island Urak Lawoi:** check whether harm to forest or reef is laid on a person, a group or outsiders, as each community chooses to say.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on official accountability, thin on how villagers assign blame, which needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for *daman* and *baligh* chapters, Arabic for their terms; Thai for the ITA scores and juvenile data; Patani Malay to record who is said to be at fault.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who is to blame for the conflict's deaths is itself contested ground; record each source's attribution without adopting it or characterizing any community or movement. The statutory age of criminal responsibility is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; no fault, liability or age-of-answerability pointer was set aside.
