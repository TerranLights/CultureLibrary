# Patani: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; *kitab* Arabic; Chinese of Yala and Betong; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, religious bodies, the state) or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boarding *pondok* pupils against day pupils of state schools in Mueang Yala:** check who sets the waking hour and what a broken rule costs.
- **Girls' and boys' sections of the same Islamic school:** check whether rules, punishments and enforcers differ.
- **Fishing households of Panare and Tak Bai against rubber-tapping households of Raman:** check how children are trained into pre-dawn work.
- **Buddhist boys who spend a term at Pattani and Yala temples:** check how temple training is described by the boys and by their parents.
- **Betong's Chinese, including families of the former guerrilla villages:** check whether training is spoken of in Chinese, Thai or both, and by whom.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check whether the Thai beating proverb or Malay sayings are used when correcting children.
- **Maniq parents:** check, with consent, how children learn forest skills and whether correction is part of it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on school and religious discipline; mixed on household discipline; thin on Maniq and Urak Lawoi child-rearing.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi to follow al-Palimbani's hours of the day; Thai for MICS tables and school rules; Chinese or English for the guerrilla memoir; Patani Malay for dusk warnings told to children.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corporal punishment, youth camps and army programs are disputed; report methods, not judgments. Camps run by security agencies involve detained or monitored youths; their records may be closed.
- **Held for review:** the classical Buddhist scripture behind the monks' code of discipline, kept off the source rows; temple discipline is reached here only through local practice.
