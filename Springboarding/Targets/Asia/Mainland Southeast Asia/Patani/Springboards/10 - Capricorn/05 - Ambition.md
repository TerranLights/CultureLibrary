## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sons and daughters of rubber smallholders in Bannang Sata and Si Sakhon:** check whether the hoped-for path is a state post, trade, religious study or work in Malaysia.
- **Graduates of private Islamic schools against graduates of state schools in Pattani town:** check which careers each is steered toward, and by whom.
- **Women traders of the Pattani and Narathiwat markets:** check how they speak of expanding a business and who must approve.
- **Betong's Chinese business families:** check whether ambition is put in terms of the family, the town or the child.
- **Buddhist households of Nong Chik and Yaring:** check whether children's hopes are tied to staying in the region or leaving it.
- **Saba Yoi and Thepha villages within reach of Hat Yai:** check whether the nearby city changes what young people aim for.
- **Ko Lipe's sea people and the Banthat range's Maniq:** check which futures parents name for a child who finishes school, and which they fear.
