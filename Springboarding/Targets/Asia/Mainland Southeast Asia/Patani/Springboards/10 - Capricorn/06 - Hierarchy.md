# Patani: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; *kitab* Arabic; Hakka, Cantonese and Hokkien of Betong and Yala; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of Tengku, Nik and Wan descent in Yaring and Sai Buri, against commoner families of the same villages:** check where precedence is still shown and where it has faded.
- ***Pondok*-trained *babo* against university-trained *ustaz*:** check which kind of learning ranks higher in a village, and in a city mosque.
- **Buddhist officials born in the region against those posted from Bangkok:** check how each is ranked by colleagues and residents.
- **Betong's dialect associations side by side:** check whether being Hakka, Cantonese or Hokkien still decides who may marry whom and who chairs a board.
- **Muslims whose home tongue is Thai, in Satun (the Sam-Sam) and in Chana:** check which rung Malay-speaking neighbors assign them, and which they claim.
- **Migrant crews at Pattani's fishing port:** check how employers and neighbors rank them, from employers' records and workers' groups.
- **Forest and sea peoples (Maniq, Urak Lawoi):** check how lowland neighbors rank them, and how they answer that ranking.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on official ranks (state, mosque, temple), thin on class and color as lived.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi to read the guardians' order; Thai for monks' and officials' rank lists; Chinese for the order of banners; Patani Malay for how titles are said aloud.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic and religious ranking is the conflict's sore point; record each source's terms and rank no group. Questions on noble descent can touch old claims to rule.
- **Held for review:** two categories: South Asian-heritage Muslim trading families of the towns, and the leadership grades of a South Asian-founded preaching movement, both kept out of the rows and comparisons.
