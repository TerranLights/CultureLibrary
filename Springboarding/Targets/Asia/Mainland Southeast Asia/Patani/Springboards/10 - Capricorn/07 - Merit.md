# Patani: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; *kitab* Arabic; Chinese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Graduates of the Pattani campus against graduates of Middle Eastern universities:** check which credential wins a teaching or mosque post, and in whose eyes.
- **Officials' families in Yala town against farming families in Bacho and Cho-airong:** check how each explains who gets a government post.
- **Buddhist families in Pattani's temple villages:** check whether success is put down to *bun*, schooling or a patron.
- **Chinese associations of Betong and Pattani town:** check whether board posts follow donations, seniority or election.
- **Satun's Muslim families who speak Thai at home:** check how they rate state exams against religious credentials when a child applies for work.
- **Boat crews of Panare and Tak Bai:** check whether the skipper's share is defended by skill or by ownership.
- **Maniq camps:** check how a successful hunter's share is decided, only as band members choose to explain it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** exam results, quotas and scholarship lists are easy to find; talk of who deserved what is mostly unrecorded.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the counsel book's chapters on officials; Thai for hiring notices and theses; Malay of Malaysia for the *meritokrasi* debate; Patani Malay for how *kabel* and *usaha* are used.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional quotas, religious-school hiring and patronage are touchy; record claims with their sources and attach no trait to any group.
- **Held for review:** none; quotas, patrons, prizes and religious reward could all be scouted as written.
