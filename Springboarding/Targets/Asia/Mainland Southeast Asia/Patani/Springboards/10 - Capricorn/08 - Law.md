# Patani: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic of *fiqh*; Chinese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Here the spirit only; what the law says, and courts as written, are Libra's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- ***Pondok*-educated villagers against university-educated professionals in Pattani town:** check which of the state's law, *syariat* or village rules each names first for a family quarrel.
- **Families who have dealt with the special security laws against those who have not:** check how each speaks of the purpose of law, keeping their accounts apart and drawing no conclusion about any side.
- **Temple-going residents of Yala's old town:** check whose law they say the state's statutes are, and what they say those statutes are for.
- **Chinese traders of Sungai Kolok and Betong:** check when they would use a lawyer, a connection or their association.
- **Satun, where the emergency decree has not applied:** check whether trust in police and courts reads differently from the three provinces.
- **Maniq and Urak Lawoi spokespeople:** check how each describes the state's park and forest rules, and what they ask of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on Islamic legal thought and on surveys of trust; thin on legal consciousness at village level.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi to follow al-Fatani's answers; Thai for trust surveys and road counts; Patani Malay to hear *undang-undang* and *hukum* told apart in talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the special laws, detention and punishment are live grievances; cite sources, take no side, characterize no community or movement. Statutes, the 1946 Act on Islamic law in the four provinces and court procedure are Libra's letter, not scouted here.
- **Held for review:** none; the *fatawa*, village rules, forum choices and compliance counts all stand as written.
