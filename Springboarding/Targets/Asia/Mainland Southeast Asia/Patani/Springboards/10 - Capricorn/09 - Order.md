# Patani: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai letters and Rumi; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic of sermons; Chinese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Market traders of Yala's and Pattani's town centers against rubber tappers who work before dawn in Than To and Bannang Sata:** check at what hours and places each says order holds or fails.
- **Women and men of the same villages:** check whether safety and orderly conduct are spoken of differently, as each describes them.
- **Buddhist villages near Nong Chik and Mae Lan:** check what keeps or threatens order in their own accounts, without ranking them against neighbors.
- **Betong's Chinese merchants:** check how the town's orderly image is made, kept and advertised, and by whom.
- **Satun's Malay- and Thai-speaking Muslims:** check whether the absence of the emergency decree changes how order is talked about.
- **Island and forest minorities (Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe, Banthat Maniq):** check whether a village or camp "in good order" has its own word, and who keeps it so.
- **Kelantan's Rantau Panjang and Pengkalan Kubor, as a cross-border foil:** check how the opposite bank speaks of order at the river.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on violence and policing, thin on household order and on order as a cosmic idea.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for police data, curfews and theses; Jawi for prayer-row teaching; Chinese for Betong's civic pride; Patani Malay for how *aman* and *kacau* are used day to day.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** order in the region is fought over; security incidents must be cited from sources, with no community, movement or group characterized; nightlife and drug material involves people who can be harmed if named.
- **Held for review:** none; neither the prayer-row teaching nor the curfew, crime or zoning pointers needed holding back.
