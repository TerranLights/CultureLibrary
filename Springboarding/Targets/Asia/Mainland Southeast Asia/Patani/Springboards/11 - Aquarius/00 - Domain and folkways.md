# Patani: Domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*), in Jawi and in Thai-script and Latin renderings; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; *kitab* Arabic; the towns' Chinese varieties; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups and what they hope for, with the social ways of settlement, migration, association and hospitality.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fishing hamlets around the Pattani bay against tapping villages up the Sai Buri and Bang Nara rivers:** check whether feasts and joint work draw kin only or the whole mosque parish.
- **Older Buddhist settlements (Khok Pho, Nong Chik) against families who came with the settlement schemes:** check how each tells its arrival and names its neighbors.
- **Chinese households of Pattani town, Yala and Betong:** check which halls and charities a family joins, and whether dialect lines limit membership.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Chana and Thepha, and the Sam-Sam of Satun:** check how a congregation forms where Malay is not spoken at home.
- **Urak Lawoi of the Adang islands and Maniq of the Banthat hills:** ask, through spokespeople they pick, which gatherings outsiders should not attend or record.
- **As foils, never as Patani's core:** Kelantan's mosque parishes; hostel circles of Patani workers in Kuala Lumpur.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; ethnography and association records cover the towns and the old coast; thin for interior villages since 2004, Maniq, and women's own circles.
- **Scope of lands:** Patani's post-war territory awaits the author's ruling (the tracker carries only pre-war Thailand); the eleven Aquarius sheets therefore all take the three provinces plus the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun, and say so only here.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the founding chronicle; Thai for registers, curricula and settlement theses; Chinese characters for hall anniversary books; Patani Malay by ear for visiting customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** in the conflict, "community" and "people" are political words, membership lists can expose people, and Dusun Nyior is contested memory. Cite sources; characterize no community, movement or group.
- **Held for review:** congregational outreach circles of a revival network founded in South Asia (category only), left out of the associational pointers.
