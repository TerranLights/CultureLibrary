# Patani: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic of congregational teaching; Chinese of the clan halls; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit: how the person is weighed against the group, where "us" and "them" are drawn, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Voters of Rueso and Rangae against voters of Mueang Yala and Betong:** check whether bloc voting shows in both, and around which candidates.
- **Fishers of Pattani Bay against tappers of the Yala interior:** check which acts of the group (strike, petition, pooled sale) each has tried, and who stood out.
- **Thai Buddhist hamlets with armed volunteer units, and Muslim villages with their own:** check what membership demands and who refuses, from cited studies.
- **Clan halls of Pattani town and the Hakka associations of Betong:** check whether surname or dialect decides membership.
- **Urak Lawoi crews of Ko Lipe and Maniq bands:** check how a working party is formed, as their spokespeople describe it, not as outsiders label it.
- **Across the border, as a foil:** party branches in Kelantan villages; the *tomyam* restaurant crews Patani men form in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; election data and rights reports are rich; little on how members experience group pressure, and nearly nothing on Maniq groups.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the congregation chapters; Thai for factional history, election returns and theses; Malay for chorus lyrics and supporters' chants; Chinese for clan registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective blame and armed units sit at the heart of the conflict; use documented cases, name no suspects, and characterize no community, movement or group. Youth drug groups: anonymize.
