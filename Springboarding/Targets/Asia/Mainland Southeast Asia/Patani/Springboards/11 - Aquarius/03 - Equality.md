# Patani: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai; the Arabic of Qur'an commentary; Chinese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Descendants of the old noble lines in Yaring and Sai Buri against families without titles:** check whether titles change seating, address or marriage choices today.
- **Buddhist families of Yala town and Muslim families of the same streets:** check which unequal treatments each side reports, using surveys and published interviews only.
- **Daughters and sons in rubber-tapping households of Rueso:** check who gets schooling, land and paid work, as households report it.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun's coast and Malay-speaking villagers of Narathiwat:** check whether the language of home changes access to state jobs and schools.
- **Chinese families of Betong and Sungai Kolok:** check how they describe their standing as citizens across three generations.
- **Maniq of the Banthat range and Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe:** check, with their advocates and spokespeople, where equal treatment as citizens is claimed or missing.
- **Malaysia's Malay-first policies, for contrast only:** what Patani families working in Kelantan or Selangor report about being treated as foreigners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** poverty, income-spread and land figures are plentiful; felt discrimination is surveyed unevenly; the manners of rank and the Maniq's standing are barely documented.
- **Languages needed:** reading Jawi with some Arabic is the key to the commentary and sermons; Thai opens the poverty, land and census series; only spoken Patani Malay shows address and titles in use; Chinese helps with town histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of discrimination are charged in the conflict; cite surveys and documented cases, and characterize no community, movement or group. Inheritance shares and anti-discrimination rules are law: they go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none; the equal-worth sources chosen are Jawi, Thai, Chinese and local, and none needed setting aside.
