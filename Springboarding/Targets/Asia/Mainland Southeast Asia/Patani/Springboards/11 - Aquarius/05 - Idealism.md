# Patani: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai; Arabic of Sufi and Qur'anic phrases; Chinese of the moral-uplift halls; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Readers of Malay popular history in Pattani town and pupils of Thai-curriculum schools in Yala:** check which golden age each is taught, and by whom.
- **Abbots and lay Buddhists who stayed in Narathiwat's temples and those who moved away:** check which ideals the memorial books and their own words give for each choice.
- **Teachers from Bangkok or the northeast posted to Rueso and local-born teachers in the same schools:** check how each names the ideal of the calling.
- **Halal food makers of Yala and Pattani's small processors:** check whether "wholesome" means more than the certificate to them.
- **Moral-uplift halls of Betong and Sungai Kolok:** check what their founding charters list as the good life, and whether younger members still cite them.
- **Maniq spokespeople and Urak Lawoi elders:** check how each describes a right way of living, apart from outsiders' images.
- **Kelantan's "Serambi Mekah" self-image, for contrast only:** what a neighboring state's ideal of an Islamic polity looks like in its own slogans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** slogans, sermons and memorial books are plentiful; first-person talk about giving up an ideal for a wage or a family is scarce in print.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for the *insan kamil* texts and Qur'anic slogans; Malay for popular history, *sajak* and *nasyid*; Thai for cremation volumes, teachers' memoirs and campus speeches; Chinese for association charters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** an ideal past or homeland can be read as a political claim; record who invokes it and where, and characterize no community, movement or group. Memorial books concern the dead and their families.
- **Held for review:** none; the ideal-past, ideal-person and model-village pointers all stand without screening.

## 5. First queries
1. Ask a Pattani Jawi bookseller which *kitab* on the *insan kamil* are taught, and buy the most used one.
2. Search Thai news archives and Facebook for "บัลดะตุน ฏ็อยยิบะตุน" and the Malay form, and log speaker, place and date.
3. Read the chapters on the four queens in Ahmad Fathy al-Fatani's history and list the words used to praise their age.
4. Find three cremation volumes for abbots of Narathiwat or Pattani temples in a university library and copy their tables of contents.
5. Watch the last two commencement addresses at Fatoni University and PSU Pattani and note each ideal named.
