# Patani: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rappers and band members of Pattani town against young men of Raman's villages:** check where each finds room to dress, sing or act differently, and who stops them.
- **Mak Yong troupe members and *pondok* teachers of the same district:** check how each speaks of the performance and of the performers.
- **Chinese devotees who walk as mediums in Pattani and Yala:** check how their families describe the role, and whether it marks the person all year.
- **Buddhist and Muslim *kathoey* of Hat Yai's edges and Narathiwat:** check, through published interviews, how families and congregations respond.
- **Sam-Sam young people of Satun's coast:** check whether nonconformity is measured against Malay, Thai or mixed norms.
- **Lipe's Urak Lawoi teenagers working in resorts, and Maniq who chose village life:** ask how their own elders judge the break, in words they agree to have recorded.
- **Kelantan, as contrast only:** its long ban on Mak Yong performances (*check* dates) and the debates it raised.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed; theses and online music are reachable; religious dissent and gender nonconformity are under-studied and often unsafe to research openly.
- **Languages needed:** an ear for Baso Yawi slang (rap) and old stage Malay (Mak Yong); Jawi and Arabic for censure notices; Thai for theses and dress disputes; Hokkien for the mediums' helpers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaving Islam, religious dissent and gender identity can endanger informants; use published or anonymized sources only. Characterize no community, movement or group; record slurs only as data.
- **Held for review:** a religious minority movement of South Asian origin that is named in warnings against deviant teaching (category only); kept out of the dissent pointers.
