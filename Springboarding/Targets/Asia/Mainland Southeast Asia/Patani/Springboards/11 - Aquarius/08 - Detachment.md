# Patani: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic of the Sufi manuals; Hokkien and Hakka of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; prized in some forms (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and condemned in others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men who spend the last ten nights in Yala's central mosque and their wives at home:** check how each describes the absence and the return.
- **Nurses of Narathiwat's district hospitals and *bomoh* healers of the same villages:** check how each keeps distance from a patient's suffering, and what each says of the other.
- **Buddhist widows of Tak Bai and Muslim widows of Rangae:** check which mourning restraint each was taught, through their own published accounts.
- **Chinese elders alone in Betong and Malay elders in Bacho whose children work in Malaysia:** check whether solitude is described as rest, burden or shame.
- **Reporters from Bangkok outlets and local Malay-speaking reporters:** check how each defines neutral coverage of an attack.
- **Maniq camps of the Banthat range and Urak Lawoi households of Ko Bulon:** check, in words their spokespeople approve, whether moving on after a death or quarrel is told as calm or as grief.
- **Kelantan, for contrast:** *iktikaf* and retreat customs in its larger mosques.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; clinical surveys and aging data are solid; chosen equanimity, as opposed to numbness, is little studied; Maniq material comes only from outside comparison.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the *'uzlah* chapter; Thai for clinical surveys, the aging survey and Komin; Patani Malay for proverbs and memes; Hokkien and Hakka for elders' associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** do not read silence after violence as indifference; fear, grief and trauma are separate questions. Cite clinical sources and characterize no community, movement or group. Funerals: ask before recording.
- **Held for review:** nothing was set aside while choosing the withdrawal, mourning and composure leads.
