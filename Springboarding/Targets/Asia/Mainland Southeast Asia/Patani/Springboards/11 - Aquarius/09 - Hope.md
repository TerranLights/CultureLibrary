# Patani: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*) in Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings; standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Arabic of prayer and Sufi ethics; Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese of the towns; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families awaiting news of a disappeared son in Narathiwat and families of victims of attacks in Yala:** check what each hopes for from officials and from God, using only published testimony.
- **Restaurant workers home from Malaysia in Tak Bai and tappers who stayed in Sukhirin:** check whether a better life is placed abroad, at home or in the children.
- **Buddhist temple committees in Pattani and Chinese shrine committees in Betong:** check what each community's rituals ask for, year by year.
- **Hajj savers in Yaring's villages and their urban relatives in Hat Yai:** check how long each waits, and how the wait is spoken of.
- **Sea-people elders on Ko Lipe and Maniq parents near Pa Bon:** ask, via the speakers they nominate, what each expects the next generation to have.
- **Kelantan, as comparison:** its pilgrimage savings fund and its migrant returnees' hopes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich; prayer gatherings, migration theses, clinical hope scales and confidence indexes are reachable; private despair and children's hopes are thinner.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for the *khauf*-and-*raja'* chapters; Patani Malay for *ceramah* and *doa*; Thai for the dialogue press, theses and indexes; Chinese for wish slips and shrine notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hopes about the conflict's end are political; cite statements and characterize no community, movement or group. Families of the disappeared and of victims need consent and protection.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the prayer, pilgrimage, migration and waiting leads for hope all come from the region's own people and records.
