# Patani: Domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin renderings); standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; *kitab* Arabic; the towns' Chinese varieties; Urak Lawoi; Maniq. **Concept in one line:** the domain of the hidden and transcendence: how suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape are met; its folkways are the magic ways (healing, charms, divination).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fishing villages of Panare and Yaring against rubber villages of Rueso:** check which sea or forest spirits are named, and which healers are called.
- **Traditional *pondok* circles and reform-minded circles in Yala:** check where each draws the line between cure by recitation and the *bomoh*'s craft.
- **Buddhist Thai of Khok Pho and of the Thai-speaking villages of Tak Bai:** check which rites call on ancestral teachers, monks or local guardian spirits when misfortune comes.
- **Chinese households of Yala and Betong:** check which troubles go to a shrine medium and which to a doctor.
- **Muslim households of La-ngu and Khuan Don, Sam-Sam among them:** check whether healing words, charms and spirit names come in Malay, in Thai or in both.
- **Maniq bands of the Hala-Bala and Banthat forests:** ask, only through the people they choose, what may be recorded about their healing and their unseen beings.
- **For contrast only:** healing rites in Kelantan and Terengganu.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for magic ways and healers; mixed for mental health (national data, few local studies); thin for sleep and dream-telling.
- **Scope of lands:** the tracker gives only pre-war Thailand and no settled territory, so all twelve Pisces sheets take the three provinces with the Malay-speaking districts of Songkhla and Satun as the core lands, and say so only here.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi to read the chronicle's cure and written charms; Patani Malay by ear for the healers' sung invocations; Thai for hospital and prison series; Chinese for shrine notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' formulas and sorcery suspicions are private; detention is charged, and its legal side, like the order ways, goes to Libra and Capricorn. Characterize no community, movement or group.
- **Held for review:** a single category: trance and cure accounts kept by Muslim households of South Asian heritage in the market towns.
