# Patani: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Thai; Arabic loanwords of almsgiving; Chinese of the town shrines. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bay villages of Pattani's coast against hill hamlets of Bannang Sata and Kabang:** check whether the mosque or kin leads help for a stricken family.
- **Widows of the unrest in Rangae and Cho-airong:** check, from their own accounts, which help came from the state, the mosque or an NGO, and which felt like pity.
- **Buddhist households around the temples of Mae Lan and Nong Chik:** check whether almsgiving to the poor runs through the temple or the household.
- **Chinese families of Betong and Yala:** check whom the shrine kitchens and benevolent gifts reach beyond their own members.
- **Malay-speaking villages of Saba Yoi and Thepha:** check whether *zakat* and *sedekah* move in Malay or Thai forms where the two worlds meet.
- **Maniq bands:** check how outside aid is offered, received and named, through spokespeople they choose.
- **For contrast only:** Kelantan's state-run *zakat* body; Patani workers in Malaysia sending money home after a death or an attack.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on programs and charities (reports, press); mixed on almsgiving custom (theses, videos); thin on mercy to enemies and on how pity is felt by those pitied.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the alms chapters; Thai for remedy-program reports and the volunteer survey; Patani Malay for *nasyid* and village talk of *kesian*; Chinese for shrine-kitchen notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as a victim is political; widows and orphans need consent and anonymity. Mercy to offenders and pardons are law: Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). Characterize no community, movement or group.
- **Held for review:** the orphan, widow and alms pointers raised no case under the standing rules, so mercy has none set aside.
