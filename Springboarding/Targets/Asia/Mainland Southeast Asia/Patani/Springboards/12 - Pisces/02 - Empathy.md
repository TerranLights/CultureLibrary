# Patani: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Thai and Southern Thai; Chinese; Maniq (Kensiw) through chosen interpreters. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Teachers born in the provinces against teachers posted from Bangkok or the Northeast:** check which language and cues each relies on to read a pupil's distress.
- **Mixed hamlets of Khok Pho and Sai Buri:** check what Buddhist and Muslim neighbors say they understand, or fail to grasp, about each other's mourning and fasting.
- **Women and men in the same Narathiwat households:** check who is expected to put another's feeling into words at a quarrel or a death, as researchers have recorded it.
- **Chinese shopkeepers of Sungai Kolok and Betong serving Malaysian and local customers:** check how they switch language and manner to read buyers.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun:** check whether they are cast as go-betweens who can see both sides, and what they say of the role.
- **Kensiw and other Maniq bands of Than To and the Banthat range:** check, through their chosen spokespeople, whether outsiders' guesses at their feelings are welcome.
- **For contrast only:** a Kelantan village with Thai-speaking Buddhists among Malay neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; dialogue programs and surveys are documented; everyday reading of others' minds is barely studied, and the EQ data may not break down below province.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay to hear *jaga hati* in use; Thai for dialogue reports, screening data and fiction; Jawi for the neighbor chapters; Malay subtitles for the film's reception.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dialogue participants may be at risk if named; cite published notes only, and characterize no community, movement or group. Youth confession pages need anonymizing.
- **Held for review:** this sheet sets no perspective-taking or dialogue source aside under the standing rules.
