# Patani: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Thai; classical Arabic terms of devotion; Hokkien of the shrines. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wives and daughters in Rangae and Si Sakhon whose men cook in Malaysia:** check who gives up school, rest or wages to keep the household, in their own accounts.
- **Locally recruited rangers and soldiers rotated in from other regions:** check how each speaks of the risk they carry and what their families expect in return.
- **Monks of the remaining temples in Rueso and Sai Buri:** check what staying costs them, from published interviews.
- **Mediums and fire-walkers of the Pattani town shrines:** check what bodily ordeals they accept and how they explain the choice.
- **Pilgrim families of Yaring and Nong Chik:** check who sold what for a parent's Hajj.
- **Maniq bands of the Hala-Bala forest:** check, through spokespeople they choose, how sharing and risk for the band are described.
- **For contrast only:** Kelantan *nasyid* recordings on mothers; Patani cooks in Malaysia telling of their own years away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on deaths on duty and remembrance; mixed on fasting and vows; thin on caregivers' own accounts and Maniq ways of giving.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for the fasting and vow chapters; Thai for memorials, rangers' records and migration theses; Hokkien for the shrine legend's own telling; Patani Malay for songs of the mother.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *syahid* and พลีชีพ are weapons in the conflict's war of words; cite who uses them and never adopt either side's frame. Characterize no community, movement or group. Duties owed by law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; martyrdom texts, vow manuals and mothers' songs all clear the rules, so sacrifice keeps every pointer.
