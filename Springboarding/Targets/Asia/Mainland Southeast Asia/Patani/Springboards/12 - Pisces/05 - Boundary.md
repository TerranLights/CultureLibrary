# Patani: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Thai; the Arabic of purity law in the *kitab*; Chinese of the shophouse towns; Maniq through chosen interpreters. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households of Sungai Kolok, Tak Bai and Betong against inland villages of Kabang and Krong Pinang:** check how daily crossing changes where people say "our" land ends.
- **Buddhist and Muslim neighbors in Mae Lan and Panare:** check which meals, utensils, animals and rooms are shared, and who decides.
- **University women in Pattani town and women of hill villages in Si Sakhon:** check what each reports about covering, touch and being alone with men outside the family.
- **Chinese families of Yala and Pattani town:** check which family matters stay inside the clan or dialect circle, and what is told to Malay or Thai neighbors.
- **Muslims of Mueang Satun who speak Thai with Buddhist kin:** check whether purity and food lines bend where language and family are shared.
- **Maniq living at the sanctuary's edge in Waeng and Sukhirin:** check, through spokespeople they choose, which lines they draw with villagers, rangers and visitors.
- **For contrast only:** Rantau Panjang and Pengkalan Kubor on the Kelantan bank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the national border and security lines; mixed on purity and modesty (theses); thin on private life and on how family members keep things from each other.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for the purity chapters; Thai for handbooks, crossing data and theses; Patani Malay to hear *tepi kain* talk; Malay of Kelantan for the far bank.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** checkpoint and zoning records touch security and can expose people; modesty is personal. The legal side of borders, papers and dress rules belongs to Libra and Capricorn. Characterize no community, movement or group.
- **Held for review:** for lines of body, house, faith and border, every pointer clears the rules; none is held.
