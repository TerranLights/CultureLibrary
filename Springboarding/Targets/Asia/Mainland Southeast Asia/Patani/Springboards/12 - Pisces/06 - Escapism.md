# Patani: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay and Indonesian (for the dramas and novels people read and watch); Thai and Southern Thai; Chinese of the towns. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men of Rueso and Bacho against students in Pattani town:** check which escapes each is reported to use (kratom brew, games, football, travel), and how studies sampled them.
- **Women at home in Rangae and Cho-airong:** check what their own breaks look like (dramas, phone time, visits), from studies that asked them.
- **Buddhist families of Mueang Yala and Khok Pho:** check whether temple fairs, drinking and trips to Hat Yai serve as release, and how often since 2004.
- **Chinese families of Betong:** check which weekend pastimes they keep, and how the Malaysian visitor trade enters their streets.
- **Thai-speaking Muslims of La-ngu:** check whether the Thai-language media they watch differs from Malay-speaking homes.
- **Urak Lawoi families of Ko Lipe:** check, through their leaders, how the island's visitor trade reaches their own evenings.
- **For contrast only:** Kelantanese visitors in Sungai Kolok; young Patani men who leave for Malaysia to get away from trouble at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on drugs and drinking (control-board and survey data); mixed on media and leisure; thin on women's escapes and on daydream and fantasy as such.
- **Languages needed:** Patani Malay for sermons and youth slang; standard Malay and Indonesian for the dramas and novels; Thai for drug and survey data; Chinese for Betong's leisure clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use carries arrest risk and stigma; anonymize, and cite data, not individuals. Drug and alcohol law belongs to Libra and Capricorn. Characterize no community, movement or group; nightlife pointers must not become claims about any people.
- **Held for review:** none; drug data, sermons, dramas and fairs were all usable without setting anything aside.
