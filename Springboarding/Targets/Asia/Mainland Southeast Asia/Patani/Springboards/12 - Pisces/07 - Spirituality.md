# Patani: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; devotional Arabic; Thai; Chinese of the shrines; Maniq and Urak Lawoi through their own speakers. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (the experience and practice; faith as institution is Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old town quarters of Pattani, fishing villages of Panare and hill hamlets of Than To:** check which devotions (grave visits, *maulid*, night prayers) each keeps, and how often.
- **Traditionalist congregations and reform-minded ones in Yala:** check what each counts as proper devotion and what as innovation, from their own teachers' talks.
- **Women's study circles in Narathiwat villages:** check which litanies and reminders women share among themselves, and who leads them.
- **Buddhists of Khok Pho and Mueang Narathiwat:** check whether pilgrimage and retreat rose or fell after 2004, and why they say so.
- **Chinese families of Betong and Yala:** check which generation fills the vegetarian halls, and whether Thai or Chinese is chanted.
- **Urak Lawoi elders and Maniq band heads:** learn first from them what of their sacred life may be studied at all.
- **For contrast only:** Kelantan's *maulid* processions; Patani students in Mecca and Cairo and the devotions they bring home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on devotional practice (ethnography, video, sermons); mixed on Buddhist and Chinese practice in the region; thin on Maniq and Urak Lawoi, rightly so where ceremonies are closed.
- **Languages needed:** devotional Arabic and Jawi for *berzanji* and litanies; Patani Malay to follow *tazkirah* and grave-side prayer; Thai for nursing theses and temple notices; Hokkien for shrine announcements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputes over *maulid*, grave visits and sea rites divide congregations; report each side's own words and characterize no community, movement or group. Closed ceremonies stay closed.
- **Held for review:** devotional and grave-visit circles of South Asian-heritage Muslims in the market towns, a category set aside from this sheet.
