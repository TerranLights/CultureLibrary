# Patani: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; the Arabic of creed and Sufi practice; Thai; Chinese of the towns. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Disciples of the old *tarekat* lineages and members of reform-minded study circles:** check whether a pledge to a guide is sought, and what each says yielding to God requires.
- **Families of Raman and Bannang Sata who lost members to violence, and families who lost them to illness:** check whether *redha* is voiced in the same words for each.
- **Former fighters who surrendered in the 1980s, and their villages:** check, from published testimony only, how the surrender is told now.
- **Buddhist households of Khok Pho and Sai Buri:** check which teachings of acceptance they hear at funerals, and from whom.
- **Hokkien and Hakka households of Yala and Betong:** check which release rites by fire are kept in full and which are cut short.
- **Maniq of the Banthat range:** check, through spokespeople they choose, how a band speaks of loss and what it leaves behind.
- **For contrast only:** Kelantan's *tarekat* pledges; Patani-born residents of Malaysia whose relatives surrendered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on programs and palliative studies; mixed on Sufi pledges; thin on everyday yielding in quarrels (see Libra for *mengalah*) and on how surrender in conflict is remembered.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi for lineage records and creed; Patani Malay for proverbs and condolence wording; Thai for program histories, nursing theses and cremation volumes; Chinese for funeral ritual names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surrender programs and their returnees are live security matters; use published sources, name no one, and characterize no community, movement or group. Terms offered to those who surrender are law, for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** pledges, endowments and deathbed acceptance raised no case; nothing on yielding is held back.
