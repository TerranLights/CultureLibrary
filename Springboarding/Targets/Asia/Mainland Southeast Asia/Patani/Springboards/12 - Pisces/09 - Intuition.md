# Patani: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay; Arabic of Sufi psychology; Thai and Southern Thai; Hokkien and Hakka of the towns; Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Older healers of Rueso and Ra-ngae, and *ruqyah* practitioners in Yala town:** check whether hidden knowledge is "seen" by a gift, read from signs, or refused as forbidden.
- **Nurses trained in Hat Yai and those from local villages working in Narathiwat wards:** check whose hunches are trusted, and how language shapes what a patient's family conveys.
- **Tappers of Bannang Sata and fishers of the Sai Buri coast:** check how each reads weather and danger, and how the young learn it.
- **Buddhist families of Mueang Pattani:** check whether dreams and monks' insight guide decisions about moving, building or marrying.
- **Chinese families of Betong:** check when a master is called and when the family's own feel is enough.
- **Urak Lawoi of Ko Lipe and Ko Adang:** check, with their elders, which sea knowledge may be written down at all.
- **For contrast only:** omen lore of Kelantan and Terengganu as recorded in Malaysian folklore studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; omen literature and healer practice are reachable; studies of expert and parental intuition are scattered across nursing and health theses; very thin on Maniq and Urak Lawoi sea and forest sense.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi to read twitch-omen texts; Patani Malay for *tilik* sessions and dream talk; Thai for nursing theses, patrol accounts and monk biographies; Hokkien or Hakka to follow a master's reading.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination is disputed within the faith, so record each side's own words; premonition talk around attacks touches security. Characterize no community, movement or group.
- **Held for review:** the omen texts, healers' readings and trained-sense pointers raised no case; the hunch keeps all thirteen.
