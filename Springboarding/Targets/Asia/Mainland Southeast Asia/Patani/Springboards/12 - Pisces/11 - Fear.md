# Patani: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Patani, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Patani Malay (*Baso Yawi*; Jawi, Thai-script and Latin spellings); standard Malay and Indonesian (for horror media); Thai; Chinese of the towns; Maniq through chosen interpreters. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rubber tappers who work before dawn in Bannang Sata and Cho-airong:** check how they speak of the dark hours and which precautions they keep.
- **Buddhist teachers and monks in Mueang Narathiwat and Sai Buri:** check, from published interviews, what they fear on the road and at the temple.
- **Mothers of young men in Rangae and Tak Bai:** check what they fear for their sons (arrest, attack, drugs) in their own testimony.
- **Chinese shop families of Yala and Betong:** check how fear has changed opening hours, children's schooling and plans to move.
- **Malay-speaking villages of Chana and Saba Yoi:** check whether fear of violence is named as sharply where the conflict has touched less.
- **Maniq bands of the Banthat range:** check, through spokespeople, which dangers (thunder, outsiders, illness) they choose to talk about.
- **For contrast only:** fear-figure lore in Kelantan; Patani families in Malaysia on what made them stay away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on violence-linked fear (studies, rights reports, surveys); mixed on fright illness and bogeymen; thin on fear as pleasure and on Maniq accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for the eschatology text; Patani Malay for sermons, bogeyman names and fright talk; Thai for PTSD, stress and victimization studies; Hokkien for soul-calling rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fear in the conflict attaches to every side; cite documented sources, protect informants, and characterize no community, movement or group. Special security powers and arms rules are law: Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none for dread; eschatology, rumor and soul-calling sources all pass.

5. Log the 2024 opening hours of thirty shops in central Yala and compare them with older accounts.
