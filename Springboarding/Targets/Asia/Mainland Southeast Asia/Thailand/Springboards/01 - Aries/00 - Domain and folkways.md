# Thailand: The domain, self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Northern Thai (Kham Mueang); Isan (Lao of Thailand); Southern Thai (Pak Tai); Karen, Hmong, Mon, Northern Khmer and other minority languages. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it asserts itself: the self, the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative, with naming ways as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** standard speech and court forms; urban naming as one case, not the default.
- **Lanna (khon mueang):** own script, lineage spirits (ผีปู่ย่า), life-extension rites.
- **Lao-speaking Isan:** own rites, music and migrant experience.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy (Surin, Buriram, Sisaket):** own naming and khwan rites.
- **Phu Thai, Tai Yai, Tai Lue, Lao Song:** dress that marks the person.
- **Southern Thai Buddhists and Muslims** (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla, Satun): own speech, mixed faiths.
- **Thai Chinese:** dual surnames and clan associations.
- **Karen, Hmong, Mien, Akha, Lahu, Lisu, Lua:** distinct naming; uneven citizenship.
- **Mon:** own script and temples.
- **Moken, Urak Lawoi, Moklen and the Maniq:** sea and forest peoples, mostly outsider-described.
- **Patani** (comparison only; a separate culture-nation, marking the edge of the national self-image the domain looks at). **Thai communities abroad** (comparison only, for which markers of self are kept or dropped away from home).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Central Thai, Lanna and Isan; mixed for the south; thin for the sea peoples and the Maniq. Thailand's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Central Thai for state records and scholarship; Tham Lanna and Tham Isan scripts for manuscripts; Karen and Hmong for naming ceremonies told from inside.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the monarchy (legal limits, self-censorship); ID status of stateless people; colorism; Kayan villages shown to tourists.
- **Held for review:** scholarship on Thai personhood and spirit cults by South Asian-heritage scholars; classical religious texts in their original learned languages (category only).
