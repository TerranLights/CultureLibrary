# Thailand: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Southern Thai; Hmong and Karen for village speech. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok office workers vs. Central Plains farmers:** different stages (meeting room, village assembly) for claiming a voice.
- **Isan:** repartee song gives a public channel for claims that everyday speech may not.
- **Lanna:** sor exchanges; how Thai media portray northern speech, checked against northern speakers' own accounts.
- **Southern Thai speakers:** media images of southern speech, checked province by province (Songkhla, Trang, Nakhon Si Thammarat).
- **Thai Chinese merchants:** bargaining and business talk as a setting of its own.
- **Hmong and Karen villages:** council speech by elders and clan heads.
- **Women and kathoey:** who is called "kla" or "kao rao" for the same act.
- **Students vs. teachers:** the classroom as the first lesson in when to speak.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation, useful to contrast how speaking up before state officials is framed in Malay-language media). **Thai workers abroad** (comparison only, e.g. how they report speaking up to foreign bosses).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much on Central Thai politeness, little on regional or minority speech in disputes.
- **Languages needed:** Thai to read ThaiJO studies; Isan and Kham Mueang to follow song lyrics; Southern Thai for puppet-theater jokes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speaking up to the monarchy is legally restricted; the 2020 protest material carries legal risk for named people.
- **Held for review:** sermon traditions on humility drawn from texts in their original learned languages (category only).
