# Thailand: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang and Isan for lineage-spirit rules; Karen and Hmong for village self-rule; Moken for sea-nomad life. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok middle class vs. rural Central Plains:** coresidence and parental say over marriage may differ by class.
- **Lanna and Isan:** how far lineage-spirit rules (phit phi) give kin a say over courtship, and how that has changed.
- **Isan migrant daughters and sons:** remittance duty against personal choice.
- **Southern Thai Muslims of Satun and Songkhla:** religious guidance on marriage and dress, set beside Buddhist neighbors.
- **Thai Chinese families:** inheritance and family firms; who chooses a career.
- **Karen, Hmong, Lisu, Akha:** village and clan self-rule meeting state forest and citizenship rules.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** mobility and resettlement.
- **Mon and Khmer-speaking Thais:** temple and village councils.
- **Monks, nuns (แม่ชี) and kathoey:** lives chosen outside family plans.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation, with its own autonomy debates). **Thai emigrants** (comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on coresidence and family obligation; mixed on bodily autonomy; testimony-heavy and partisan on land rights.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for surveys and forums; Kham Mueang and Isan for spirit-rule interviews; Sgaw Karen for land testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forest-eviction cases involve disappearances and prosecutions; abortion and gender are contested; any regional self-rule talk is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** monastic discipline texts in their original learned languages that set limits on a monk's own choices (category only).
