## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Plains:** state calendar, royal plowing, Bangkok founding as one model of origin.
- **Lanna:** its own new year and pillar shrine; the Lua as an earlier people.
- **Isan:** gourd origin and fifth-month new year.
- **Mon of Phra Pradaeng and Koh Kret:** a separate Songkran with its own customs.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** their own new-year and house rites in Surin and Buriram.
- **Thai Chinese:** arrival stories and the lunar new year.
- **Hmong, Mien, Lahu, Karen:** new years set by their own calendars.
- **Southern Thai:** rites of boat launching and first fishing trips on the Gulf and Andaman coasts.
- **Moken:** a boat-launching and spirit-pole festival on the Surin Islands *check*.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation, to contrast its own calendar of first acts, such as Malay-Muslim naming and new-house customs). **Thai migrants starting over abroad** (comparison only, for which opening rites travel with them).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on festivals and founding rites; mixed on birth and rehab; thin on Moken and Lua beginnings in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for almanacs and state notices; Kham Mueang and Lua for the Doi Kham rite; Hmong for new-year songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** commentary on royal rites is legally constrained; first-people claims can bear on land; addiction records are private.
- **Held for review:** classical cosmogony texts in their original learned languages used in Thai temple teaching (category only).
