# Thailand: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Southern Thai; Kuy for elephant lore; Hmong; Phuket Hokkien for the festival. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Plains:** check how far the written record leans to royal and national war heroes.
- **Isan:** boxers, migrant laborers and mo lam heroes.
- **Lanna:** heroes of Lanna's wars with Burma, less told in Bangkok.
- **South:** Phuket heroines, southern boxers and fishermen.
- **Thai Chinese:** festival ordeals and merit by rescue.
- **Kuy and Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin:** elephant capture and its rites.
- **Hmong and other highland peoples:** wartime memories the state frames differently.
- **Moken:** diving and free-diving at sea, and how they speak of it.
- **Women:** heroines celebrated vs. everyday endurance not named as courage.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation, with its own heroes of resistance). **Thai veterans abroad** (comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national heroes; mixed for regional ones; thin for minority voices.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and old verse for court epic; Isan for lam phuen; Kuy and Hmong for oral memory; Hokkien terms for Phuket mediums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal heroes are protected from critique; Thao Suranari's story is contested; wartime and insurgency memory; the 1990 suicide.
- **Held for review:** epic and religious sources of hero tales in their original learned languages (category only).
