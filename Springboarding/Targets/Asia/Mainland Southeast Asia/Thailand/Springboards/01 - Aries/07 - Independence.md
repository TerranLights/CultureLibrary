# Thailand: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Southern Thai; Mlabri and Maniq for forager life; Moken. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok salaried class:** independence through jobs and condos; dependence on family for child care.
- **Isan:** remittance-dependent villages; earners as providers.
- **Lanna:** craft and orchard households; elders' self-provision.
- **Southern Thai:** smallholders and fishers, and the Songkhla savings-group model.
- **Thai Chinese:** family-firm dependence across generations.
- **Mlabri and Maniq:** foragers under settlement.
- **Moken:** sea foraging vs. tourism and aid after 2004.
- **Karen and Hmong:** subsistence farming within state forest rules.
- **Monks and nuns (แม่ชี):** do nuns, with weaker alms support, speak of self-support differently?
- **Disabled people and elders:** care at home vs. independent living.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation with its own independence claims). **Thai workers abroad** (comparison only; their remittances, not their lives, fall inside).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income, remittances and elders; mixed on self-reliance discourse; thin on foragers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for NESDC plans and NSO tables; Mlabri for forest knowledge; Isan for migrant song lyrics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sufficiency economy is a royal philosophy, so open critique is constrained; forager settlement; Patani separatism is a separate, sensitive topic.
- **Held for review:** sermon sources on self-reliance in their original learned languages (category only).
