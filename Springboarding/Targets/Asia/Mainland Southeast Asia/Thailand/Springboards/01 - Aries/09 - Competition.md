## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** exam and tutoring pressure, corporate rivalry.
- **Isan:** village-against-village rocket contests; provincial football.
- **Lanna:** boat racing in Nan; craft contests in Chiang Mai fairs.
- **South:** bull arenas and cockpits; check whether Buddhist and Muslim southerners share them.
- **Thai Chinese:** business rivalry and clan-association contests.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais (Buriram, Surin):** football and elephant shows as rivalry.
- **Hmong, Lahu, Akha:** New Year games.
- **Karen:** whether community norms discourage open contest, to check in Karen sources.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** contests in sea festivals, little recorded.
- **Students vs. elders:** a generation's view of exams.
- **Patani** (comparison only; separate culture-nation; its dove-singing contests are a ready contrast *check*). **Thai boxers and students abroad** (comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams and sports; mixed on animal contests (often under-documented because of gambling); thin on minority games.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for exam data and media; Isan for the rocket festival verse; Southern Thai for arena talk; Hmong for New Year games.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling on animal contests and football is illegal or regulated; animal welfare; provincial politics behind clubs.
- **Held for review:** none.
