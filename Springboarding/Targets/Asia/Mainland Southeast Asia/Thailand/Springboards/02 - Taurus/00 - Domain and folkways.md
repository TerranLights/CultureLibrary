# Thailand: The domain, possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang (Northern Thai); Isan; Southern Thai (Pak Tai); Thai Chinese usage of Teochew origin; Hmong, Karen, Mon, Northern Khmer, Moken and other minority languages. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how it is held: possessions, money, resources, worth, with food, dress and wealth ways as the folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** malls, cards and condos against rice-farming households of Suphan Buri.
- **Lanna:** weaving and lacquerware as family goods; water-sharing customs; northern food.
- **Isan:** sticky rice, pa daek and the remittance economy of migrants' households.
- **Southern Thai Buddhists and Muslims** (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla, Satun, Krabi): rubber and fishing wealth; halal and Buddhist food rules side by side.
- **Thai Chinese, and Phuket's Baba families:** shophouse trade, gold, ang pao, Peranakan wedding jewelry, family-firm inheritance.
- **Hmong, Akha, Mien, Lahu, Lisu:** silver and embroidered cloth as stored wealth.
- **Karen and Lua:** rotational rice fields and seed stores.
- **Mon and Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin and Buriram:** silk and temple giving.
- **Moken, Moklen and Urak Lawoi:** sea resources, boats and few fixed goods.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for Islamic giving and the Malay marriage gift). **Thai communities abroad** (compare only for remittances and goods sent home).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on money, debt and consumption; rich on food and textiles; thin on household goods of the sea peoples and forest Maniq. The author has not yet fixed Thailand's post-war borders; these sheets assume the core Thai lands.
- **Languages needed:** Central Thai for surveys and finance; Isan and Kham Mueang for market and food talk; Hmong and Akha for silver and dress meanings; Teochew for Chinese shop records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal wealth and the Crown Property Bureau (legal limits on comment); informal lending and debt-collection violence; land-title disputes.
- **Held for review:** scripture-language texts on giving and monastic property (category only).
