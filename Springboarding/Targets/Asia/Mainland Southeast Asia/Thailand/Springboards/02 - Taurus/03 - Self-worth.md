# Thailand: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan for regional pride and slight; Southern Thai for honor talk; Hmong, Akha and Lisu for the experience of being labeled; Thai Chinese family idiom. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok office workers vs. Central Plains farmers:** degree and job title set against land and household standing.
- **Isan migrants in the capital:** whether accent and skin tone are used against them, and the pride movements that answer.
- **Lanna:** whether a royal past and an own script feed personal standing.
- **Southern Thais of Phatthalung, Trang and Songkhla:** nakleng honor; Buddhist and Muslim Thai-speakers side by side.
- **Thai Chinese:** worth earned through commerce and filial support.
- **Hmong, Akha, Lahu, Lisu and Karen:** labels from outside against self-names.
- **Mon and Khmer-speaking Thais:** worth tied to sounding Central Thai or not.
- **Kathoey and gay men:** where visibility and respect part ways, in their own accounts.
- **Moken:** "sea gypsy" as an outside name.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for how Malay Muslim dignity is framed against the Thai state). **Thai workers abroad** (compare only for how migrant labor in Taiwan, Korea or Israel affects standing back home).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on face and on mental-health figures; mixed on lived dignity among minorities; thin on Moken self-description.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for psychology and media; Isan for village film and talk; Hmong and Akha for the labeling studies' own informants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide; colorism; gender identity; sharp limits on any comparison involving the monarchy's honor.
- **Held for review:** scripture-language treatises on merit and rebirth used as Thai self-worth sources (category only).
