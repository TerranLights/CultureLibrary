# Thailand: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok salaried workers vs. informal vendors and motorcycle-taxi riders:** formal insurance against none.
- **Central Plains farmers:** flood risk and crop-price swings.
- **Isan households:** granaries, migrant remittances and land pledged for loans.
- **Lanna villages:** irrigation failure and northern forest fires.
- **Southern Thai rubber and fishing households** (Trang, Surat Thani, Satun): price and storm exposure.
- **Thai Chinese family firms:** gold, property and kin lending.
- **Akha, Lahu, Hmong and Karen:** highland livelihoods and citizenship-linked access to programs.
- **Mon and Karen near the Myanmar border:** cross-border work and paperwork.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea risk and resettlement.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for how armed unrest reshapes the meaning of safety). **Thai workers abroad** (compare only for remittances as household insurance).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state programs and disaster research; mixed for informal protection; thin for Moken own-language terms.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for program data; Tham script for Lanna protective texts; Southern Thai for growers' talk; Akha for Doi Tung beneficiaries' own voice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "national security" as a political term; royally sponsored projects (careful framing); statelessness and benefit access.
- **Held for review:** protective chant texts in the liturgical language of Thai temples (category only).
