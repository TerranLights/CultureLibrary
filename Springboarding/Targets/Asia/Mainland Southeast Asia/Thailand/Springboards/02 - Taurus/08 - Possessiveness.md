# Thailand: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan for stinginess words and foraging spots; Southern Thai for coastal fishing turf; Hmong for marriage customs; Thai Chinese family idiom. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok young couples vs. provincial Central Plains couples:** phone checking and location sharing against older chaperone customs.
- **Isan:** husbands working away; what spouses and in-laws watch for.
- **Lanna:** whether lineage-spirit rules on touching shape guarding of daughters.
- **Southern Thai fishing villages** (Trang, Satun, Songkhla, Nakhon Si Thammarat): guarding near-shore waters; Muslim and Buddhist marriage customs.
- **Thai Chinese:** parents' say over a daughter's marriage and control of the family firm.
- **Hmong and Mien:** bride-taking and clan claims over wives and children.
- **Karen and Akha:** courtship customs and village rules on outsiders.
- **Mon of Samut Sakhon and Ratchaburi; Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin:** who controls the marital house when a couple lives with the bride's kin.
- **Motorcycle-taxi riders, vendors and fishers:** turf as livelihood.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for how polygyny under Islamic family custom is discussed beside Thai minor-wife practice). **Thai wives abroad** (compare only for jealousy and control in marriages to foreigners, as told back home).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on partner violence and gender history; mixed on everyday guarding of things and places; thin on minority courtship in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for NGO counts and columns; Isan for village testimony; Hmong for marriage customs told by Hmong speakers; Southern Thai for fisherfolk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner killings; marriage by capture (consent questions); minor wives and children of second families.
- **Held for review:** scriptural sermons in a classical learned tongue on clinging and craving (category only).
