# Thailand: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang for northern monastic dissent; Isan for millenarian and mine-protest memory; Southern Thai for coastal protest; Northern Khmer for the border-temple dispute; Karen for holdout testimony. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok office culture vs. village councils of the Central Plains:** how far one person may block a decision.
- **Isan:** millenarian memory and mine and dam protests.
- **Lanna:** monks and teachers who defied Bangkok, and their followers.
- **Southern Thais of Krabi, Songkhla and Nakhon Si Thammarat:** coastal holdouts; a reputation for directness to test, not assume.
- **Thai Chinese:** founders who keep control of a firm; family quarrels over change.
- **Karen of Kaeng Krachan:** elders' own accounts of staying on ancestral land.
- **Hmong and Akha:** relocation and crop-eradication episodes, as each people tells them.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy of Sisaket and Surin:** border feeling during the temple dispute.
- **Mon:** upkeep of language and temples under assimilation pressure, in Mon accounts.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for how long armed refusal is framed differently from Thai protest holdouts). **Thai activists in exile** (compare only for how holding out from abroad is judged at home).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on protest movements and labor history; mixed on everyday obstinacy; thin on minority words for stubbornness.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for protest records; Kham Mueang for Khruba Siwichai's northern following; Isan for Phu Mi Bun memory; Northern Khmer for border villagers; Sgaw Karen for Kaeng Krachan elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** activists' hunger strikes tied to royal-insult prosecutions (legal limits); border nationalism; monks in conflict with church authority.
- **Held for review:** sermons in the classical scriptural tongue on attachment and views (category only).
