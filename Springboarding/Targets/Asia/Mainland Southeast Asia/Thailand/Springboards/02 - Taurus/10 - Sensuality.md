# Thailand: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan for taste words and dance stages; Kham Mueang for feasts and fermented tea; Southern Thai for breakfast and coffee-shop culture; Northern Khmer for kantrum; Hmong for taste talk; Hokkien and Teochew-derived food names. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok cafés, spas and malls vs. Central Plains farm households:** bought comfort against everyday pleasures of the field and canal.
- **Isan:** heat, chili and dance at temple fairs.
- **Lanna:** cool-season comfort, miang and slow feasts.
- **Southern Thais of Trang, Phuket and Satun:** coffee shops and seafood; Muslim and Buddhist food rules.
- **Thai Chinese:** fasting festivals and banquet pleasures in the same year.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** kantrum, silk and local dishes.
- **Hmong, Akha and Lahu:** highland foods and New Year feasts.
- **Mon:** khao chae and festival sweets, and Mon accounts of where they come from.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea foods and the feel of life on boats and beaches.
- **Patani** (a separate culture-nation; compare only for halal food pleasures and Malay dress textures beside southern Thai ones). **Thai restaurant owners abroad** (compare only for which tastes are changed for foreign diners and which are kept).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on food and massage; rich in court verse; mixed on touch norms; thin on sea peoples' sensory life.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for court verse and reviews; Isan and Kham Mueang for regional taste words; Northern Khmer for kantrum lyrics; Hokkien for Trang shop names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the sex industry (sex itself goes to Scorpio); massage-parlor stigma; alcohol advertising limits.
- **Held for review:** monastic discipline texts in their scriptural language on the five sense pleasures (category only).
