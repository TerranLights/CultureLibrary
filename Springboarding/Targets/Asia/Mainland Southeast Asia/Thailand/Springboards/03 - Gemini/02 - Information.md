# Thailand: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang and Tham Lanna script for manuscripts; Isan for community radio; Southern Thai and Moken for sea knowledge; Hmong, Karen and other highland languages for knowledge-ownership work. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok readers vs. Central Plains farmers:** online feeds against village broadcast and market talk.
- **Isan listeners:** community and partisan radio as a rival to Bangkok television.
- **Lanna temples and scholars:** a manuscript culture with its own archive of knowledge.
- **Andaman coast (Phang Nga, Krabi, Satun):** official warnings beside fishers' and sea peoples' knowledge.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** oral knowledge and their wariness of being recorded.
- **Karen, Hmong, Lisu, Akha:** forest knowledge, and who may hold the record of it.
- **Thai Chinese:** clan-association bulletins and Chinese-language dailies as a separate news stream.
- **Older Thais on LINE:** forwarded health claims and chain messages.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for news passed through Malay-language and mosque networks where state reports are doubted, and for Isranews' coverage of the conflict zone. **Thai channels run from abroad:** compare only for overseas Thai-language commentators whose news reaches home despite blocking.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on press history and media surveys; mixed on rumor and LINE studies; thin on how minorities keep their own knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Thai throughout; Tham Lanna script for manuscripts; Isan for radio; Moken, Karen and Hmong for oral knowledge (with consent).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blocked sites and news about the monarchy (the access law itself belongs to Libra); restricted highland and sea-people knowledge, shared only as the peoples allow.
- **Held for review:** none; the manuscript and press pointers above are all Thai and Lanna material.
