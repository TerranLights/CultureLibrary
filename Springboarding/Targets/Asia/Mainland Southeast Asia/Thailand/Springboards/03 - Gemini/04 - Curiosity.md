# Thailand: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang for taboo and forbidden places; Isan; Southern Thai and Moken on the Andaman coast; Kayan, Karen and Hmong for highland villages visited by tourists; Teochew and Mandarin for ancestral-village searches. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore; and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok science-center families vs. Central Plains villages:** organized wonder against questions answered by elders and monks.
- **Isan:** fossil finds, village museums and the curiosity of migrants returning.
- **Lanna:** khuet and forbidden places beside a strong astronomy and university scene.
- **Southern coast** (Phuket, Krabi, Phang Nga): tourism's gaze and locals' own questions about visitors.
- **Moken, Moklen and Urak Lawoi:** the looked-at as well as the looking.
- **Kayan, Karen, Akha, Hmong:** villages visited, photographed and questioned.
- **Thai Chinese:** genealogy and roots searches as a late form of asking.
- **Ghost-media audiences across classes:** curiosity about the uncanny is not one group's.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for where an outsider's questions read as surveillance, in villages under long security watch. **Second-generation Thais abroad:** compare only for "roots" trips to a parent's village and what they ask there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on science outreach and ghost media; mixed on everyday nosiness; thin on how each minority rules which questions an outsider may ask.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Kham Mueang for khuet; Kayan and Karen for tourism voices; Moken with an interpreter; Teochew for clan genealogy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic tourism and consent; display of human remains; Moken and Karen restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none beyond the cosmology text's own sources, which are not pointed to here.
