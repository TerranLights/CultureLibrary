# Thailand: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan and Lao for the Isan-Thai pairing; Kham Mueang; Northern Khmer for kantruem; Southern Thai and the Samsam's mixed speech; Teochew for Thai Chinese self-naming. **Concept in one line:** how two-ness is lived: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** office persona and family persona; English and Thai in one sentence.
- **Isan:** Lao by speech, Thai by citizenship, and how each is claimed.
- **Lanna:** khon mueang and Thai; Kham Mueang revival beside Central Thai schooling.
- **Southern Thai:** Buddhists and Muslims, and the Samsam between.
- **Thai Chinese:** Chinese name and Thai surname, shrine and temple.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** a third language under two state labels.
- **Hmong and Karen:** Christian and animist kin in one village; shamans who travel between worlds.
- **Mon of Phra Pradaeng and Ko Kret:** a Mon Songkran inside Thai Songkran.
- **Kathoey and other gender-diverse Thais:** categories beyond two.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for the paired labels "Thai Muslim" and "Malay Muslim," and lives split between Thai state school and religious school. **Thai-heritage children abroad:** compare only for those raised between two languages, and luk khrueng born overseas who move to Thailand.
