# Thailand: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan and Kham Mueang for kin terms and inheritance; Southern Thai for Nora lineages; Teochew-derived Thai Chinese address terms; Hmong; Pwo and S'gaw Karen; Moken. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** smaller families; eldest siblings as second parents in Bangkok's working class.
- **Isan:** who among the siblings stays to care for parents, and elder siblings sending money from the city.
- **Lanna:** whether house and spirit group still follow the daughters' line.
- **Southern Thai Buddhists and Muslims:** Nora lineages; Islamic inheritance shares in Satun and Songkhla families.
- **Thai Chinese:** brothers in the family firm, hia and che as titles, sons favored or not.
- **Hmong and Mien:** clan brothers and sisters married out.
- **Karen and Lua:** siblings in swidden work and village ritual.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** kin flotillas and island households.
- **Mon and Khmer-speaking Thais:** kin terms in a second language beside Thai.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for Malay kin terms for siblings and Islamic inheritance shares, against Buddhist northern daughter-heirs. **Thai families abroad:** compare only for older siblings who migrate first and sponsor the younger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kinship terms and fertility decline; mixed on inheritance in practice; thin on twins and sibling violence.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Isan and Kham Mueang for kin terms; Teochew; Hmong; Karen; Moken through a trusted interpreter.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inheritance quarrels (law to Libra); family violence; children in skipped-generation homes.
- **Held for review:** none; the sibling tales chosen are Thai, Lanna and Isan versions.
