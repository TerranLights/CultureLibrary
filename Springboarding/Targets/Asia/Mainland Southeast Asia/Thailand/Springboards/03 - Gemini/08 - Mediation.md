# Thailand: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan for marriage go-betweens; Kham Mueang for spirit mediums and weir chiefs; Southern Thai and Malay religious terms in Satun; Hmong and S'gaw Karen for village elders; Burmese for migrant-labor brokers. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok offices vs. Central Plains villages:** HR and lawyers against headmen, kamnan and abbots.
- **Isan:** wedding go-betweens and village elders.
- **Lanna:** weir chiefs and spirit mediums, two kinds of go-between.
- **Southern Thai Muslims of Satun and Songkhla:** imams and Islamic committees.
- **Thai Chinese:** association heads and shrine committees as business arbiters.
- **Hmong and Mien:** clan elders across village lines.
- **Karen:** ritual heads and forest-use quarrels with park officials.
- **Migrant workers:** brokers, NGOs and employers in a three-way bind.
- **Political camps:** reconciliation bodies and who each side accepts as neutral.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for peace dialogues with a foreign facilitator, a third party of a kind Thailand's other quarrels rarely see. **Thai temples abroad:** compare only for abbots called on to settle quarrels inside overseas Thai communities.
