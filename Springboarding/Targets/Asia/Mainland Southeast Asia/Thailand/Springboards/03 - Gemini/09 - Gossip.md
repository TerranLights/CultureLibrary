# Thailand: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai, including online slang; Isan for spirit-accusation rumors; Kham Mueang for old sayings; Southern Thai for tea-shop talk; Hmong, Akha and Karen with local help; Thai Chinese usage in Bangkok's old quarters. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok condo dwellers vs. Central Plains villagers:** chat-group gossip against talk at the temple fair and the shop.
- **Isan:** spirit accusations and the gossip around migrant wives and husbands.
- **Lanna:** old sayings and spirit-group rules as checks on talk.
- **Southern Thai men and women:** tea shops for one, markets and kitchens for the other (to test, not assume).
- **Thai Chinese of Yaowarat and Phuket:** family-firm and marriage talk inside dialect groups.
- **Hmong, Akha, Lahu:** small-village reputation and courtship talk.
- **Karen:** gossip and church or spirit discipline in mixed villages.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** island communities and outsiders' rumors about them.
- **Celebrity and social-media publics:** the national gossip arena.
- **Patani** (separate culture-nation): compare only for rumor during insurgency, where a word about an informer can be deadly. **Thai communities abroad:** compare only for gossip about who prospered and who failed overseas, carried home through village networks.

## 4. Flags
