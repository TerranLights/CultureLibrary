# Thailand: The domain, home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Thai Chinese (Teochew) usage; Hmong, Karen, Mon, Northern Khmer, Kuy; Moken. **Concept in one line:** home and family, with building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways as the folkways: what home is, and who counts as family.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lanna:** related houses on one compound; the kalae-gabled house.
- **Isan:** married daughters living beside their parents; grandparents raising children of migrant parents.
- **Bangkok and its ring:** condos, townhouses and housing estates beside canal-side timber houses.
- **The Pak Tai south** (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla, Satun, Phuket, Krabi): Buddhist, Muslim and mixed-faith households.
- **Western border hills:** Karen, Lua and Mon households.
- **Surin, Buriram and Sisaket:** Khmer-speaking Thai and Kuy families.
- **Thai Chinese:** ancestral tablets, clan surnames, shophouse homes.
- **Hmong, Mien, Akha, Lahu, Lisu:** clan-based highland households.
- **Lao Song and Phuan villages** of the Central Plains: house types carried from older homelands.
- **Moken, Moklen, Urak Lawoi:** boat and shore homes.
- **Patani, for contrast only** (its own culture-nation): Malay Muslim house forms and Islamic family courts, the nearest different frame for what a household is. **Thai households overseas, for contrast only:** which Thai house ways survive in a rented flat abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on census tables and architecture; mixed on lived household practice; thin on sea peoples and unregistered highland households. Thailand's post-war lands are not yet settled by the author; so the Cancer sheets cover the present heartland, and this note is not repeated in them.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Kham Mueang and Tham Lanna script for the treatises; Isan kin vocabulary; Hmong ritual terms; Teochew clan records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family days tied to the monarchy (comment is legally limited); family law goes to Libra and Capricorn; some kin-spirit rites are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none; the classical house and family material named here is Thai and Lanna.
