*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai and its regional kin vocabularies (Isan, Kham Mueang, Pak Tai); Teochew and Hakka for Thai Chinese lineages; Mon; S'gaw Karen; Iu Mien; Tai Dam (Lao Song). **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Isan rice villages:** married daughters near parents; kin cooperation in fields and merit-making.
- **The Lanna valleys:** kin groups linked to house spirits, and how far that link still holds.
- **Central Plains and Bangkok:** small families, in-laws in condos, the reach of kin in a big city.
- **Pak Tai Buddhists** (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Phatthalung) and **Southern Thai Muslims** (Satun, Songkhla, Krabi): ancestor merit beside Islamic family custom.
- **Thai Chinese:** lineages, surname groups and graves; Teochew, Hakka, Hokkien and Hainanese lines.
- **Mon and Lao Song:** minority ancestor rites in the Central Plains.
- **S'gaw and Pwo Karen:** kin lines drawn by feasts.
- **Iu Mien and Hmong:** clan, surname and adoption.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin:** Saen Don Ta (แซนโฎนตา), the ancestors' feast in Khmer.
- **Urak Lawoi and Moken:** island kin groups.
- **Patani, held apart** (its own culture-nation): Malay Muslim kin terms and Islamic inheritance shares, useful only to show where Satun's Thai-speaking Muslims follow or depart from them. **Thai families split by migration, held apart:** children left with kin while parents work in Korea or Taiwan, a test of who counts as family at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kin terms and ancestor festivals; mixed on in-law relations; thin on Mien and Karen kin lines outside older ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; regional kin vocabularies; Teochew for lineage books; Mon and Karen for ancestor rites; Mien terms through specialists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kin-line spirit rites may be closed to outsiders; family law and inheritance go to Libra and Scorpio; adoption records involve minors.
- **Held for review:** none; ancestor rites named here are local Thai, Tai, Mon, Karen, Mien and Chinese forms.
