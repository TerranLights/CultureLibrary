# Thailand: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai, including the old manual and charm vocabulary; Isan and Kham Mueang for village guardian spirits; Hmong; Moken; Pak Tai for southern rescue and watch customs. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Isan:** guardian groves, ta laeo, and men's protective objects.
- **Lanna:** river and forest defenders; town-pillar and city spirits.
- **Central Plains and Bangkok:** guards in housing estates, gun ownership, child-safety campaigns.
- **Pak Tai, Buddhist and Muslim** (Phatthalung, Trang, Satun, Krabi): protective rites of rubber and fishing villages.
- **Thai Chinese:** household gods, door charms and temple protector deities.
- **Hmong and Mien:** soul protection by strings and shamans.
- **Karen of Tak and Mae Hong Son:** forest protected by custom against park law.
- **Mon and Khmer-speaking Thais:** protective tattoos and spirit rites in their own languages.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** ancestral posts and boat ceremonies.
- **Akha and Lahu:** village gates and spirit protection.
- **Patani, outside the core** (separate culture-nation): ranger posts, village guard units and checkpoints where protection is armed and daily, the strongest contrast to Pak Tai villages to the north. **Thai migrants overseas, outside the core:** amulets carried by workers abroad as their personal protection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on amulets and state volunteer bodies; mixed on household protection; thin on Moken and Mien protective rites in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and old Thai script for manuals; Kham Mueang and Isan; Hmong; Moken via interpreters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Village Scouts' role in the 1976 violence; protection of the monarchy is legally fenced; some protective formulas are secret to their teachers; policing and law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the scripture-language originals of the guarding chants monks recite when blessing a new house (category only).
