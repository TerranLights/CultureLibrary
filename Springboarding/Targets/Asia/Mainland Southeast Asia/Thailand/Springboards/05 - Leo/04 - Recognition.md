*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** degrees, titles and the business press as channels of standing.
- **Lanna:** khruba monks raised by their followers; recognition from below.
- **Isan:** mo lam teachers and singers named as National Artists, against the many who are not.
- **Pak Tai, outside Patani:** nora masters and their lineages; Satun's Muslim leaders recognized in Thai state forms.
- **Central Plains villages:** headmen's awards and temple donor lists.
- **Thai Chinese:** shrine boards, clan associations and philanthropy as standing.
- **Karen, Hmong, Akha, Lahu:** the push to be recognized as Indigenous peoples at all.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** recognition of sea peoples' claims and names.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** whose heritage the state honors in Surin and Buriram.
- **Mon:** monks and teachers known across the border.
- **Patani, a counter-example only** (separate culture-nation): Islamic religious scholars (tok guru) who are recognized by communities while the state gives them no title, beside the samanasak system. **Thai professionals abroad, a counter-example only:** foreign degrees and credentials, and how they are weighed on return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on honors lists, awards and the Gazette; mixed on everyday praise; thin on minority forms of recognition in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, including the old title system; Kham Mueang for khruba lore; Isan for mo lam lineages; Teochew for donor boards; Karen, Hmong and Akha for Indigenous claims.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honors and degrees conferred by the royal family are hard to discuss openly; monastic rank disputes can be political; the word "Indigenous" is contested by the state.
- **Held for review:** none; the titles and honors named are Thai.

## 5. First queries
1. List the National Artists by year, field and region from the Department of Cultural Promotion site.
2. Search the Government Gazette for samanasak lists for one recent year and tally them by region.

4. Search ThaiLIS for "ครูบา" and list the studies of northern holy men by province.
5. Search for "สภาชนเผ่าพื้นเมือง 9 สิงหาคม" and record which peoples take part and what they ask.
