## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** online shaming, police parades and campus hazing.
- **Isan:** jokes and slurs aimed at Isan people, to be documented, and the comic answers in mo lam.
- **Lanna:** school and temple discipline; northern speech mocked or prized.
- **Pak Tai, outside Patani:** puppet clowns who mock the powerful; Satun's Muslims and how ridicule crosses religion.
- **Central Plains:** cafe comedy and the hell gardens of Suphan Buri and Chonburi.
- **Hmong and other highland peoples:** slurs and televised drug arrests as exposure, to be documented.
- **Thai Chinese:** the 1914 pamphlet and older family memories of insult.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin and Sisaket:** border quarrels and being called "Khmer" as an insult.
- **Kathoey and gay Thais:** draft days and school bullying.
- **Burmese and Lao migrant workers** (inside Thailand, not citizens): jokes and treatment at work.
- **Patani, set beside only** (separate culture-nation): the Tak Bai detentions (2004), where arrested men were stacked in army trucks; how that day is remembered sets off how other regions recall their defeats. **Thais abroad, set beside only:** Thai migrants shamed in foreign news for illegal work, and the answer at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on news cases and school surveys; mixed on humiliation inside families; thin on minority accounts of slurs in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai slang and police jargon; Isan for jokes and mo lam answers; Hmong for slur experiences; Teochew for older Thai Chinese memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shaming of minors and suspects; reprinting slurs; any humiliation of royalty in print is a crime, so satire is limited; hazing victims' families.
