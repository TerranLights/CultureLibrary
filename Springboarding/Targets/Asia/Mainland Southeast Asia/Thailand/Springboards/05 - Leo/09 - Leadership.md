*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Plains:** provincial bosses and party machines (Suphan Buri, Chonburi).
- **Lanna:** pho luang headmen and founder legends.
- **Isan:** headmen beside spirit-shrine keepers; Red Shirt village organizers.
- **Pak Tai, outside Patani:** nai hua of rubber estates and fishing fleets; Satun's imams beside Buddhist headmen.
- **The east** (Chanthaburi, Rayong, Trat): Taksin's memory and Chinese merchant leaders.
- **The west:** Karen and Mon village elders dealing with parks and the army.
- **Thai Chinese:** thao kae firms and the handover to children.
- **Hmong, Akha, Lahu:** clan heads, founder-priests and elected headmen.
- **Moken:** leadership of boat groups, to be described from inside.
- **Women leaders:** headmen, health volunteers and politicians.
- **Patani, a reference case only** (separate culture-nation): ulama and village imams who lead without state office, a contrast to Satun's imams within Thai administration. **Thais abroad, a reference case only:** temple committees and Thai associations abroad, where leaders are chosen without any state role.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on national politics and bureaucracy; mixed on village and workplace leadership; thin on highland and sea peoples' own forms of leading.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, including 1940s propaganda; Kham Mueang for northern headmen; Isan for spirit-shrine offices; Teochew for family firms; Akha and Hmong with interpreters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the monarchy cannot be discussed freely; army leadership and coups; the authority side belongs to Capricorn and law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the ruler's virtues are named only in their Thai form.
