## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** retirement at sixty from office jobs and the search for a role after it.
- **Lanna north:** ex-monk ritual leaders and craft villages that keep old hands at work.
- **Isan:** grandparents keeping households while the middle generation works far away.
- **Pak Tai south:** elderly smallholders who keep tapping rubber and fishing into old age.
- **Western Karen and Lua villages:** elders as keepers of forest and seed knowledge.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** village healers and elephant men whose skills now have a tourist use.
- **Thai Chinese:** children and grandparents helping behind the shop counter.
- **Hmong, Mien and Akha:** ritual specialists whose skills a community still needs.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea knowledge valued by outsiders only in disaster.
- **People with disabilities:** a group cutting across every region; ask them directly.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): whether Malay Muslim homes frame the worth of a frail elder through religious duty of care rather than output (to check). **Thai elders abroad, for contrast:** grandparents flown out to mind grandchildren for emigrant children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on aging and elder care; mixed on disability; thin on how minority elders judge their own use.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for surveys and forums; Kham Mueang and Isan for temple roles; Hmong for funeral specialists; Moken to hear the sea lore first-hand.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal philosophy limits open critique of Sufficiency Economy; disability, burden and suicide talk needs care.
- **Held for review:** none; the elder-care, recycling and folk-knowledge pointers are all Thai or about Thailand.
