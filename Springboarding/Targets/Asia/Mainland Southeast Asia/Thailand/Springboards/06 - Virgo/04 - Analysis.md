## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** elite schools, tutoring, think tanks; where test scores concentrate.
- **Central Plains provinces:** farm cooperatives keeping books under official guidance.
- **Lanna north:** university science in Chiang Mai beside manuscript reckoning in temples.
- **Isan:** rocket-making know-how and forest-monastery contemplation, two native modes of taking things apart.
- **Pak Tai south:** fishers' reckoning of tides and weather; rubber prices watched daily.
- **Thai Chinese:** shop arithmetic and the abacus; family business reasoning.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** schooling gaps in Thai-medium classrooms that test scores may hide.
- **Karen, Hmong and other highland peoples:** farm trials and observation outside the school's terms.
- **Moken:** reading sea signs; ask how they explain the method themselves.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): Islamic school reasoning in pondok study, a separate tradition of argument from texts. **Thai students abroad, for contrast:** scholarship students in science who return and judge the home system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on tests and curricula; mixed on everyday reckoning; thin on minority and village ways of reasoning, which need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Thai for curricula and news; Isan for rocket makers; Teochew for old shop ledgers; Hmong and Karen for farm observation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the GT200 affair touches the army; royal science history limits critique; children's test data need care.
- **Held for review:** none; the eclipse reckoning is listed as a Thai court event, not as any calendar text.
