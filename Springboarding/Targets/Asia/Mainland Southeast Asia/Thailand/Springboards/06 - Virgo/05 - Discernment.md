## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** amulet, gold and food connoisseurs; the most scam reports.
- **Central Plains:** fortune-tellers and temple fairs where buyers judge charms and monks.
- **Lanna north:** silk, silver and lacquer judged by northern dealers; tea and coffee growers.
- **Isan and Khmer-speaking lower Isan:** rice smell and grade as a regional skill.
- **Pak Tai south:** contest birds and fighting cattle judged by trained eyes and ears.
- **Thai Muslims of Satun and Songkhla:** halal judgment in shared markets.
- **Thai Chinese:** gold purity, face-reading, and trust in business partners.
- **Karen and Lua:** seed choice and forest signs.
- **Hmong and Mien:** judging silver and embroidery at markets and New Year.
- **Moken:** judging weather and sea; how they test outsiders who arrive.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): bulbul contests also flourish there under different judges and religious frames, a check on what is southern rather than Thai. **Thai emigrants, for contrast:** romance and job scams aimed at Thais seeking work overseas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on scams, amulets and food guides; mixed on folk ways of judging people; thin on minorities' own criteria.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, including old samut khoi script; Southern Thai for contest judges; Teochew-derived jargon of gold and face-reading; Karen and Hmong at markets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monk scandals touch the Sangha's authority; the state's role in declaring news false is politically disputed; fraud victims may be ashamed.
- **Held for review:** none; the treatises on marks are listed as Thai manuscripts.
