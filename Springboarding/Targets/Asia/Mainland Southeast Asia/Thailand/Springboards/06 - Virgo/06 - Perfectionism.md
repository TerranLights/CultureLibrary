## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** elite schools, tutoring and corporate quality drives.
- **Central Plains court heritage:** the Fine Arts Department's craftsmen and dance schools.
- **Lanna north:** silver, lacquer and woodcarving with masters who judge apprentices.
- **Isan:** mat and silk weavers working to buyers' standards; mo lam singers judged by audiences.
- **Pak Tai south:** nora lineages and shadow-puppet masters with strict apprenticeships.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy of Surin:** gold-brocade silk as the region's most exacting craft.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket Baba:** ritual exactness at weddings and ancestor rites.
- **Hmong, Mien and Akha:** needlework and silver as women's and men's measured skill.
- **Karen and Mon of the west:** backstrap weaving and temple carving with their own standards.
- **Moken:** boat-building judged by sea-worthiness rather than finish; ask them.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): Quran recitation contests judged on exact pronunciation, a standard of correctness outside Thai court arts. **Thai students abroad, for contrast:** scholarship holders under the pressure of family and state expectations far from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on court arts and craft revival; mixed on workplace quality; thin on personal perfectionism and its distress outside clinical studies.
- **Languages needed:** Thai craft and dance jargon; Kham Mueang for Wualai masters; Northern Khmer for Surin weavers; Southern Thai for nora teachers; Hmong for needlework terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal ceremonies and royal craft patronage limit comment; student suicide data need great care; some nora and khon rites are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none; the craft and dance pointers are Thai institutions.
