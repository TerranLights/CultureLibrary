# Thailand: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Southern Thai (for shadow-puppet clowns); Isan; Kham Mueang; Sgaw Karen; Teochew-inflected Thai of Botan's era. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** press, film critics and online review culture; office feedback norms.
- **Central Plains provinces:** village headmen and temple committees; complaint routes to the district.
- **Lanna north:** *so* singers' jibes and northern speech's own softeners.
- **Isan:** *lam* verse duels and political talk on community radio.
- **Pak Tai south:** nang talung clowns, said to speak sharply about power; check, do not assume.
- **Thai Chinese:** shop-family scolding and the Chinese-Thai critic's voice in Botan.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** *kantruem* singers' comic verses; who may mock whom.
- **Karen:** admonition through sung verse; elders' authority to correct.
- **Hmong, Mien and Lahu:** clan elders' councils where faults are named.
- **Moken:** how they answer outside critics of their way of life.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): criticism of the state there is tied to security cases and Malay-language media, a far narrower channel. **Thai voices abroad, for contrast:** exiled critics who speak from overseas because they cannot at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on media and satire; mixed on workplace and family criticism; thin on minority forms of rebuke.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Southern Thai for puppet clowns; Isan and Kham Mueang for verse duels; Sgaw Karen for hta.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the monarchy carries heavy legal risk (the law goes to Libra and Capricorn); critics in exile; online pile-ons on private people.
- **Held for review:** none; every pointer above comes from Thai-language or Thailand-based sources.
