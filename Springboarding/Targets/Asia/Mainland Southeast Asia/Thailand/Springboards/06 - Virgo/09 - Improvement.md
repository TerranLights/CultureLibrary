*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok elites:** *siwilai* and its heirs in schooling abroad and urban planning.
- **Central Plains farm villages:** credit, irrigation and machinery as improvement and as debt.
- **Lanna north:** royal and state projects among highlanders; Chiang Mai's craft upgrading.
- **Isan:** the region most often named as needing development; its own answers through migration and marriage.
- **Pak Tai south:** rubber and tourism booms changing coastal villages.
- **Karen of the western forests:** conservation rules as "improvement" set against rotational farms.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** silk upgrading for markets; elephants moved from forest to tourism.
- **Thai Chinese:** rags-to-riches family stories and the stress on children's schooling.
- **Hmong, Akha, Lahu, Mien:** crop substitution and citizenship as paths up.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** settled villages, schools and tourism imposed or chosen.
- **Patani, for contrast** (its own culture-nation): state development programs offered as answers to unrest, and how Malay Muslims receive them. **Thais who went abroad, for contrast:** returnees whose overseas wages built the bigger house back home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state development and its critics; mixed on personal self-improvement; thin on minorities' own words for betterment.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, including Sukhothai-era prose; Isan for village responses; Sgaw Karen, Hmong and Akha for highland voices; Moken for island programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal projects limit open critique; evictions and forest cases are in the courts; colorism and cross-border marriage need care.
- **Held for review:** none; the merit cosmology listed is a Sukhothai work written in Thai, and every development pointer is Thai state or scholarly material.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the *Traiphum*'s sections on the human realm; list what raises or lowers a being.
2. Read Thongchai (2000); list the measures of *siwilai* he cites, then search Thai newspapers of the 1900s-1930s for "ศิวิไลซ์".
3. Compare the stated aims of the 1st, 8th and 13th development plans.
4. Search ThaiLIS for "อีสานเขียว" and "โครงการหลวง ชาติพันธุ์"; sort studies by people and stance.
5. Pull SE-ED and Naiin self-development bestseller lists for five years and tag each title by theme.
