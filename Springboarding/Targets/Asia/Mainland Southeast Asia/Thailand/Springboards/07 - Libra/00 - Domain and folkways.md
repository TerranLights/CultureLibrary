## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** registered marriage and written contracts at their densest.
- **Lanna north:** its own law-text lineage, husbands moving into the wife's house, spirit fines.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** sworn friendship and two wedding traditions side by side.
- **Pak Tai (Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla, Satun, Krabi):** bonded-friend ties and Satun's Islamic family law.
- **Western valleys:** Karen, Mon and Tai Yai (Shan) of Mae Hong Son, each with its own marriage order.
- **Surin, Buriram and Sisaket (Khmer-speaking Thais, Kuy):** wedding rites, and a border-temple dispute kept as an enemy story.
- **Thai Chinese and the Phuket Baba:** kongsi firms and Chinese betrothal gifts.
- **Highland peoples (Hmong, Mien, Akha, Lahu, Lua):** clan exogamy and elders' courts beside the state.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** partnership across boats and islands, barely written down.
- **Yuan (Vietnamese Thais) of Nakhon Phanom and Udon:** long watched by the state as possible enemies.
- **Patani, comparison only (a separate culture-nation):** Islamic family law beside emergency decrees, a mirror for Satun's practice. **Thai spouses abroad, comparison only:** households where two countries' marriage rules meet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on legal history and Bangkok family law; mixed on regional weddings; thin on Maniq, Mlabri and sea peoples. No scope file exists and post-war Thailand's lands are not yet settled, so the Libra sheets are written for the present core lands; the other sheets do not repeat this.
- **Languages needed:** old legal Thai for the 1805 code; Tham-script Tai Yuan for Mangraisat; French for Lingat; Malay and Arabic for Satun; Hmong and Teochew for bride-price and kongsi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Article 112 limits comment on judgments given in the king's name; same-sex marriage is new and still argued; trafficking for marriage.
- **Held for review:** studies tying the old Siamese code to its claimed sources in the canonical scripture language, as a category.
