*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** courts, prisons and political trials at their densest.
- **Lanna and Nan (Tai Yuan, Tai Lue):** khuet taboos and spirit fines as a second answer to wrongs.
- **Isan (Lao Isan, Phu Tai):** stories of peasants wronged by officials; count its provinces' share of political defendants.
- **Pak Tai:** shadow-play satire of officials; Satun's Islamic judges in family cases.
- **Karen of Kaeng Krachan and the western forests:** land, eviction and a disappearance as a test of justice for a people.
- **Hmong, Lahu and Akha villages:** the 2003 drug war's killings as an unresolved question there.
- **Thai Chinese:** oaths sworn before shrine gods as proof of innocence.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi of Phuket and Krabi:** beach-land quarrels with developers in court.
- **Migrant workers from Myanmar:** defendants who meet Thai courts through interpreters.
- **Patani, comparison only:** the Tak Bai deaths of 2004, whose case ran out under the limitation period in 2024; how a time limit closed a door. **Thai dissidents in exile, comparison only:** how people charged at home speak of justice from outside its reach.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on courts, political trials and impunity; mixed on spirit fines and taboos; thin on how the Mlabri and sea peoples answer a wrong.
- **Languages needed:** Thai legal register; Tham-script Tai Yuan and Tai Lue; Sgaw Karen for Bang Kloy testimony; Burmese for migrant defendants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Article 112 prosecutions; 1976 and 2010; the 2003 drug-war killings; enforced disappearances.
- **Held for review:** treatises on the righteous king written in the canonical scripture language, as a category.
