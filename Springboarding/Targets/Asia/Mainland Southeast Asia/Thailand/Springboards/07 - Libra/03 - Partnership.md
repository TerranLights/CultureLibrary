*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** the first same-sex registrations of January 2025, and how far they spread beyond the capital.
- **Lanna:** a husband joining his wife's spirit line, set against Central Thai patterns.
- **Isan:** Western husbands and the money owed to the bride's parents; twin-town ties across the river.
- **Phu Tai of Kalasin, Sakon Nakhon and Mukdahan:** their wedding order next to Lao Isan neighbors'.
- **Pak Tai:** Buddhist-Muslim couples in Songkhla and Satun, and which rite and law each follows.
- **Thai Chinese:** sons and sons-in-law as partners in one firm; the tea ceremony that joins two families.
- **Hmong and Mien:** whether a wife moves into the husband's clan, as ethnographies report.
- **Mlabri:** a lopsided work bond with farming neighbors.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** boat families moving as a flotilla.
- **Patani, comparison only:** Islamic family law there lets a man take more than one wife, while the code allows one registered spouse: two letters of what a lawful couple is. **Thai couples running restaurants abroad, comparison only:** how husband and wife split work and pay when the business is the household.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on marriage figures and Sino-Thai business; mixed on regional and minority weddings; thin on Mlabri and Moken partnership in their own words.
