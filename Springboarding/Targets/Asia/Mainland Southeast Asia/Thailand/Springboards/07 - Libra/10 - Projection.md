## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** textbook and film enemies; the capital's names for upcountry people.
- **Lanna:** phi ka families held apart by neighbors.
- **Isan:** phi pop expulsions; Lao speech mocked in Bangkok comedy.
- **Pak Tai:** sorcery-poison accusations; "Sakai" as an outsider's label for the Maniq.
- **Thai Chinese:** cast as the foil in 1914; trace how later writing recast them.
- **Vietnamese Thais of Isan:** accused as communists in the 1970s.
- **Hmong, Karen and other highland peoples:** blamed for drugs and forest loss.
- **Myanmar, Cambodian and Lao migrants:** blamed in outbreaks and crime news.
- **Political camps:** each calls the other the hypocrite.
- **Patani, comparison only:** unrest blamed on "outside hands" across the border rather than on grievances at home; see how Satun is spoken of beside it. **Thai women married abroad, comparison only:** the "mia farang" image at home, placed on the nation's own emigrants.
