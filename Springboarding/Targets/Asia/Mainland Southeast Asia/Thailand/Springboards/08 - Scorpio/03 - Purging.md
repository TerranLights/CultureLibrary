# Thailand: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan and Phu Tai; Northern Khmer; Mon; Phuket Hokkien; Urak Lawoi. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and its suburbs:** coffin rites, detox clinics, and the press that names the "dirty."
- **Lanna north:** khuet and the old fault-clearing at new year.
- **Phu Tai of Kalasin, Sakon Nakhon and Mukdahan, with Lao Isan neighbors:** the yao medium who draws illness out.
- **Northern Khmer speakers:** mamuat healing, set against how Kuy households undo a spirit's anger.
- **Mon of the lower Chao Phraya:** lineage taboos and their public repair.
- **Phuket's Baba-Peranakan and Hokkien Thais; Trang's Sino-Thais:** the nine-day abstinence.
- **Andaman coast (Urak Lawoi, Moken):** misfortune sent off by sea.
- **Hmong, Lahu and Akha of the north:** opium detoxes, village cleansings, and the spirit gate rebuilt each year.
- **Yuan of Isan:** the Vietnamese repatriation drives of the 1950s and 1960s as an expulsion.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for blacklists and sweeps aimed at whole Malay villages, beside the 2003 drug lists that fell on Thai ones. **Undocumented Thai workers in Korea, nicknamed *phi noi* (ผีน้อย):** compare only for being rounded up and sent back by a host state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rites and festivals; mixed on political purges, where sources are partisan; thin on how the purged speak.
- **Languages needed:** Hokkien and Teochew for festival liturgy; Mon and Northern Khmer for healers; Lanna Tham script for khuet texts; Urak Lawoi for the boat rite.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug-war killings; 1976; monks under discipline; post-coup detentions; mediums' trance knowledge is not always open.
- **Held for review:** the monastic code's original-language articles on expulsion offenses (category only).
