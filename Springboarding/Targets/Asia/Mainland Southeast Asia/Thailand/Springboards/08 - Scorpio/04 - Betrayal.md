# Thailand: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai (and Thai Khorat); Isan and Lao; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Hmong; Vietnamese; Teochew. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which loyalty was owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok court tradition:** chronicles and epics that judge disloyal queens and brothers.
- **Korat (Thai Khorat) against Lao Isan:** opposite memories of the same 1820s war.
- **Lanna:** a kingdom whose rulers changed overlords, and the chronicles that judge it.
- **Southern Thai provinces:** the Red Drum (ถังแดง) killings of suspected communists in Phatthalung, a village-level charge of disloyalty.
- **Thai Chinese:** King Vajiravudh's *The Jews of the Orient* (1914) and later school closures, a loyalty test set for a whole community.
- **Catholic Thais and the Yuan of Isan:** both treated as suspect in wartime.
- **Hmong and Mien:** allies left in camps after 1975.
- **Karen and Tai Yai border villages:** caught between armed groups in Myanmar and the Thai army; informers on both sides.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for the charge of being a state informer, which there can cost a life, beside how informing is judged elsewhere. **Thai political exiles in Laos and Europe:** compare only for being branded *chang chat* from home.
