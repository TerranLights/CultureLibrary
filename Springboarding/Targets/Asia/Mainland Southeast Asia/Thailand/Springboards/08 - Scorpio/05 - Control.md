## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok office and school life:** uniforms, hair rules, workplace hierarchy.
- **Isan villages and Lao Isan farmers:** resettlement, dams and land titles.
- **Lanna women's spirit lineages:** ritual control over courtship and household.
- **Sgaw and Pwo Karen of the western hills:** fire and fallow under forest law.
- **Hmong, Lahu and Akha:** highland crop-replacement and settlement programs.
- **Sino-Thai shop households:** the proprietor-father (thao kae) steering sons' careers and daughters' marriages.
- **Southern Thai fishers and rubber smallholders:** trawler zones and price controls.
- **Maniq of the Banthat range and Mlabri of Nan:** state programs of settlement.
- **Andaman sea peoples (Moken, Moklen, Urak Lawoi):** rules on where boats may anchor and who may cut timber.
- **Tai Yai and Lua of Mae Hong Son:** border checkpoints and status cards.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for curfews, checkpoints and DNA sampling of young men under emergency rule, a harder edge of state control than elsewhere. **Thai workers sent abroad through brokers:** compare only for passports held by employers and debts that bind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state control and protest; mixed on control inside couples; thin on forager and sea-people views.
