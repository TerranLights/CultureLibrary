# Thailand: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Hmong; Mon; Vietnamese; Phuket Hokkien; Moken. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here too, and trust in police and courts under Capricorn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok's middle class:** high use of banks and apps, and high exposure to scams.
- **Isan villages:** canvassers, money and the long memory of promises.
- **Lanna:** charismatic monks whose word moves labor and money.
- **Pak Tai towns and Phuket:** Baba and Hokkien networks across the Malay Peninsula.
- **Hmong, Mien and Lisu:** trust bounded by clan and lineage.
- **Karen and Lua:** village elders as guarantors toward the state and forest officers.
- **Mon and Tai Yai:** temples vouching across a border.
- **Yuan of Isan:** an inward-built circle under surveillance.
- **Moken:** wariness of outsiders, as their own advocates describe it.
- **Thai Muslims of Bangkok and Satun:** mosque-based saving and credit.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for distrust of state agencies after unsolved killings, where even health workers are doubted. **Thai students and nurses in the US and UK:** compare only for which Thai institutions they trust from a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on institutional confidence and scams; mixed on everyday credit; thin on how foragers and sea peoples speak of trust.
- **Languages needed:** Ayutthaya verse Thai for the oath poem; Hmong clan terms; Mon for temple networks; Vietnamese for Yuan associations; French for Ivanoff.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in the monarchy cannot be polled openly; monk scandals; scam victims' names.
- **Held for review:** none; the Ayutthaya loyalty oath is pointed to in its Thai verse alone.
