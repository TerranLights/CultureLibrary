# Thailand: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Hmong; Thai renderings of Chinese martial fiction; Pak Tai. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong, with forgiveness, payment and letting go as the alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok viewers and writers:** the revenge serial as an industry.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** flood-myth vengeance and trickster payback in one oral tradition.
- **Lanna villages:** whether a grudge goes to a spirit medium, a headman or a monk, as a question.
- **Southern Thai provinces outside Patani:** shadow-play stories and their audiences.
- **Sino-Thai readers:** martial-fiction feuds; clan honor in old Yaowarat.
- **Hmong, Mien and Lahu:** elders' payments in place of retaliation.
- **Karen and Tai Yai of the borderlands:** reprisals between armed groups, as refugees recall them.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** who settles a quarrel before it turns to payback, studied on their own terms.
- **Families of the 2010 dead:** grievance kept alive by memorial and court.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for reprisal killings after Krue Se and Tak Bai in 2004, where revenge runs between armed groups and state forces. **Thai women who married abroad:** compare only for revenge stories told on Thai-language video channels about foreign husbands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in drama, film and sermon; mixed on actual feuds; thin on highland and sea-people practice.
- **Languages needed:** Isan Tham script for *Phadaeng*; Southern Thai for the shadow play; Hmong for mediation; Chinese for the source novels behind the translations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations harm families; 2010 remains politically raw; image-abuse victims; paid killings.
- **Held for review:** sermon proof-texts on how hatred ends, quoted from the canon's classical tongue (category only).
