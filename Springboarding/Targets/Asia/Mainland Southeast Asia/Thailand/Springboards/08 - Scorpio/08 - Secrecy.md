# Thailand: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan in Tham script; Pak Tai; Kuy; Iu Mien (with its ritual Chinese). **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** palace discretion, minor wives, and online privacy habits.
- **Central Plains temples:** tattoo and amulet lineages with vows of silence.
- **Lanna:** village experts who keep Tham-script manuals.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** palm-leaf lore held by elders and monks.
- **Southern Thai provinces:** Phatthalung and Nakhon Si Thammarat lineages of protective knowledge.
- **Kuy of Surin:** hunt speech and rope spirits.
- **Iu Mien and Hmong:** priests' books and soul-chants taught only to apprentices.
- **Akha and Lahu:** long genealogies memorized and recited rather than written down.
- **Teochew and Hokkien Thais:** the old angyi lodges, and the open clan halls that took their place.
- **Tai Yai and Lua:** knowledge kept from outsiders in border villages, as they describe it.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for networks kept hidden because exposure can mean arrest or death. **Thai LGBT people who moved abroad:** compare only for lives open in a new country and still closed at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on magic lineages and political silence; mixed on family secrets; thin where knowledge is restricted on purpose.
- **Languages needed:** Tham-script Isan and Lanna; Kuy; ritual Chinese and Iu Mien for Mien texts; Thai of the 1950s court for Kukrit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some lore is taught only to initiates and should not be published; the palace; Article 112; HIV status; outing.
- **Held for review:** incantations whose wording comes from the canon's classical tongue, set aside as a group (category only).
