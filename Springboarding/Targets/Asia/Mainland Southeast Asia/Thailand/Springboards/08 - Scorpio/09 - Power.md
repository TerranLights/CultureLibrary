# Thailand: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai, including the royal vocabulary; Isan; Pak Tai; Tai Lue; Tai Yai; Akha; Teochew. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how it is judged who holds it, how it is held and how it is used; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** palace, army, bureaucracy and tycoons at the center.
- **Eastern seaboard (Chonburi, Rayong):** godfather dynasties tied to industry and ports.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** voters turned force in national politics; monks of great potency.
- **Lanna:** old princely houses, khruba monks and new provincial money.
- **Southern Thai provinces:** party loyalty and the brokers who keep it.
- **Sino-Thai business families:** chao sua and their links to generals.
- **Tai Yai, Akha and Lahu of the border:** warlords, militias and spirit authority.
- **Tai Lue of Nan and Phayao:** a migrant people's own ritual lords.
- **Karen and Hmong:** village power under forest and border agencies.
- **Lua of Mae Hong Son:** first-people claims inside Lanna lore.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for power split between imams, armed groups and the military, three poles seldom in one place elsewhere. **Thai businesspeople in Cambodia and Laos:** compare only for Thai economic power wielded as foreigners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on national politics and money; mixed on local power, which hides behind euphemism; thin on highland and sea-people authority.
- **Languages needed:** court Thai for *Yuan Phai*; rachasap; Tai Lue and Tai Yai; Akha; Teochew for tycoon biographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anything on the monarchy (Article 112); military business; named godfathers; drug-trade history.
- **Held for review:** the ten royal virtues as stated in their original scriptural wording, which court speeches cite (category only).
