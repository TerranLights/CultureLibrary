# Thailand: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Southern Thai (for storm memory); Kham Mueang; Isan; Hmong; Phuket Hokkien; Teochew; Burmese for the old captives' testimony. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** the 1997 crash and the 2011 flood, unequally shared between city and fringe.
- **Isan:** drought, labor migration and rain rites.
- **Lanna:** quakes, the Mekong's decline and the burning season.
- **Gulf coast of the south (Chumphon, Nakhon Si Thammarat):** storm memories and rebuilding.
- **Andaman coast (Phang Nga, Phuket, Krabi):** the tsunami, and Phuket's tin collapse before it.
- **Sino-Thai families:** bad-year rites and the 1997 losses.
- **Hmong of the Phetchabun and Phitsanulok hills:** war years remembered beside the state's version.
- **Karen and Mon of the west:** floods and crossings at the Burmese border.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** rebuilt villages and land disputes after 2004.
- **Lao Isan and Phu Tai migrants in Bangkok:** COVID-19 job losses and the trip home.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** compare only for crisis that never ends, under emergency rule since 2005, beside one-time disasters elsewhere. **Thai farmworkers in Israel:** compare only for the October 2023 attacks and the evacuations home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on disasters and the 1997 crash; mixed on how families cope; thin on highland and sea-people crisis memory.
- **Languages needed:** Burmese and old Thai for the captives' testimony; Southern Thai for storm memory; Hmong for the war years; Hokkien and Teochew for tin-era sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1992 and the dead; the counterinsurgency years; suicide-line data; tsunami victims' families.
- **Held for review:** none; the crisis pointers are all the nation's own records and studies of them.
