# Thailand: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Tai Yai; Hmong; Vietnamese; Satun Malay. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** vows to Phra Phrom, Phra Phikhanet and Kuan Im, beside temple merit.
- **Lanna north:** faith in khruba holy men; mediums of princely spirits; a Protestant minority since the 1860s.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** forest-monk devotion and sky-spirit healing side by side, to test whether people sort them at all.
- **Southern Thai provinces (Songkhla, Phatthalung, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Satun):** the cult of Luang Pu Thuat; Thai-speaking Muslims' mosque life.
- **Sino-Thai shrine-goers and Phuket's Baba families:** vows paid at shrine festivals; Kuan Im vegetarian vows.
- **Yuan of Isan and the Catholics of Tha Rae and Songkhon:** faith remembered through martyrs and wartime suspicion.
- **Tai Yai and Tai Lue of the north:** devotion carried across the Shan and Sipsong Panna borders.
- **Karen, Hmong, Akha and Lahu:** conversion, and households split between church and spirit altar.
- **Mon riverside villages (Ko Kret, Phra Pradaeng) and Sangkhlaburi:** devotion led by Mon-speaking abbots.
- **Northern Khmer of Surin and Sisaket:** the rueam mamuat (เรือมมะม็วด) trance rite for healing, beside temple devotion.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for iman taught in pondok schools in a Malay-language frame, against Satun's Thai-language Islam. **Thai migrants in Taiwan and Israel:** comparison only, for amulets and vows carried abroad and paid back on return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Buddhist devotion and shrine vows; mixed on conversion and church life; thin on loss of faith, which few write about.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan for medium rites; Tai Yai for Khruba Bunchum's followers; Hmong and Akha for conversion talk; Vietnamese for Yuan parish records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** faith in the monarchy is a guarded subject; conversion stirs family and village conflict; mediums' initiation can be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** the classical-language scriptural passages Thai sermons cite when they rank kinds of faith, and Islamic preaching movements of South Asian origin active in Thai mosques (categories only).
