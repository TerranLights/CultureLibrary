## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok students and young voters:** liberty claimed in speech, dress and protest symbols.
- **Lanna north:** memory of a kingdom absorbed by Siam; the Phaya Phap rising of 1889.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** holy-man risings of 1901-02 and the communist forest years after 1965, each a claimed freedom.
- **Southern Thai of Phatthalung, Trang and Nakhon Si Thammarat:** a separate insurgent forest memory of the 1970s.
- **Thai Chinese:** limits on Chinese schools and names under the 1930s-1950s nationalist governments.
- **Yuan of Isan:** decades of confined movement before citizenship.
- **Karen, Akha, Lahu, Hmong and Tai Yai:** papers, checkpoints and permits as the daily face of unfreedom.
- **Yong, Tai Lue and Tai Khuen of the north; Lao Song of Phetchaburi:** descendants of war captives.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** seasonal sea routes that now cross marine-park lines.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for a merdeka idiom of Malay self-rule set against the Thai story of national independence. **Thai political exiles in Laos, France and Japan since 2014:** comparison only, for freedom sought by leaving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on 1932, 1973, 1976 and 2020; mixed on slavery's social memory; thin on uplanders' and sea peoples' own words for freedom.
- **Languages needed:** court and archival Thai of the 1870s-1900s for abolition; Kham Mueang chronicles in Tham script; Hmong; Chinese for the Thai Chinese press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speech touching the monarchy, and exiles' disappearances, carry legal and personal risk; what the law permits belongs to Libra, the spirit of control to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural passages on release from craving that monks preach from in the original classical language (category only).
