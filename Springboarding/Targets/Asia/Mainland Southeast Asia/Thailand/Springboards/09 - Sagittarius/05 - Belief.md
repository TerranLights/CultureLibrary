## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Plains villages:** monk and magic side by side, as Terwiel's village and later restudies show it.
- **Lanna north:** site legends that tie each hill and relic to the Buddha's visit.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** rain myths addressed to Phaya Thaen; the naga of the Mekong.
- **Southern Thai (Phatthalung, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Songkhla):** Nora teacher spirits and local holy women.
- **Sino-Thai streets of Yaowarat, Nakhon Sawan and Phuket's Baba quarter:** earth gods and household altars beside temple Buddhism.
- **Yuan of Isan:** ancestor and Mahayana belief inside Catholic and Buddhist families.
- **Tai Yai and Tai Lue:** belief carried in their own manuscripts.
- **Karen, Akha, Lahu and Mien:** each people's way set against church teaching, with converts and traditionalists in one village.
- **Lua and Mlabri:** spirit beliefs with very little in print.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for doctrine taught in Jawi texts, beside the Thai-script lessons of Satun. **Thai Buddhist women married in Europe:** comparison only, for beliefs kept, hidden or argued in mixed homes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on popular practice and legends; mixed on Christian and Muslim belief in Thai; thin on disbelief and on Lua and Mlabri.
- **Languages needed:** Tham script for Lanna legends; Tai Yai script for lik long; Akha via community writers; Jawi for comparison material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** open disbelief can draw social cost; some Akha and Mien ritual knowledge is held by specialists only.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural discourse on testing teachings before accepting them, quoted often by Thai reformers, in its classical original (category only).
