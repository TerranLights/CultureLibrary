## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok students and the urban middle class:** conviction in waves (1973, 1992, 2006-2014, 2020), with sides reversing.
- **Lanna north:** monks and villagers defending watershed forests; the memory of Khruba Siwichai's defiance.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** village holdouts against mines and dams; students who join them.
- **Southern Thai (Krabi, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Satun):** coastal campaigns across the Buddhist-Muslim line.
- **Thai Chinese:** sworn-brother loyalty as a model in associations and business.
- **Karen of Kaeng Krachan and Omkoi:** refusal to move, framed as duty to ancestors.
- **Hmong of Phetchabun and Nan:** memory of fighting on either side of the 1960s-80s conflict.
- **Mon of Sangkhlaburi and Tai Yai of Mae Hong Son:** abbots who stay put through border crises and resettlement.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for conviction framed by Islam and Malay identity, where the state reads steadfastness as insurgency. **Thai workers abroad who organize:** comparison only, for convictions acted on outside Thai law and Thai pressure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on student and environmental movements; mixed on religious conviction; thin on Hmong and Mon accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Ayutthaya-era verse Thai for the Si Prat material; Isan for village campaign records; Sgaw Karen for Bang Kloi elders; Chinese for association records; Hmong for veterans' memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hunger strikes by detainees and protests touching the monarchy carry legal risk (Libra and Capricorn hold the legal side); Karen activists have been harmed.
- **Held for review:** passages on unwavering resolve quoted from the classical canon in its original tongue (category only).
