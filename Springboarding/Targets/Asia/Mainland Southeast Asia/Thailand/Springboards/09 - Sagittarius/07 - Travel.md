# Thailand: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok's middle class:** foreign trips as status; Japan and Korea as favorite destinations.
- **Lanna north:** relic pilgrimages by birth year; the Mae Hong Son loop's visitors.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** decades of work journeys to Bangkok and abroad; That Phanom pilgrims.
- **Southern Thai beyond the Patani provinces:** Hajj savings in Satun and Songkhla; the tenth-month trip home to Nakhon Si Thammarat.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket's Baba-Peranakan:** visits to Chinese home villages; old sea links to Penang.
- **Tai Lue, Lao Song and other resettled Tai:** journeys to former homelands their ancestors left as captives.
- **Border Mon and Tai Yai (Kanchanaburi, Mae Hong Son, Chiang Rai):** holy places and kin on the far side of a frontier that opens and shuts.
- **Karen, Hmong and Akha:** long trips limited by district permits for those without citizenship.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin and Sisaket:** visits to kin and temples across the Cambodian line, cut whenever border tension flares.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** the dry-season boat round between Andaman islands, now hemmed in by park rules.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for journeys to Malaysia and to Mecca that pass outside Thai channels. **Thai migrants in Los Angeles and Taiwan:** comparison only, for the trip home as a yearly test of standing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** official counts are plentiful for tourists and workers abroad; pilgrimage is studied unevenly; Tai Lue and Moken journeys are scarcely told in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** classical Thai for nirat; Kham Mueang for relic lore; Tai Lue and Mon for return-journey stories; Teochew for elders' homeland talk; Hmong for New Year visiting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** undocumented workers' routes and brokers; border closures and stateless travel.
- **Held for review:** pilgrimage circuits to the holy sites of the Buddha's life outside Southeast Asia, sold by Thai tour firms (category only).
