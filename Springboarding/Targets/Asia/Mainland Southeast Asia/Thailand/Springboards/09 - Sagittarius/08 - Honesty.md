## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok:** exam culture, tutoring and the cheating scandals that follow it; anti-graft protests by the middle class.
- **Lanna north:** mild speech norms that may favor the soft untruth; compare with plain talk in village meetings.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** labor brokers who cheat migrants; village rotating-savings groups that run on trust.
- **Southern Thai (Phatthalung, Trang, Nakhon Si Thammarat):** clowns and Nora jesters licensed to tell truths.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket's Baba traders:** a merchant's word, gold weights and posted prices.
- **Hmong and Yunnanese (Haw) Chinese of the hills:** market dealings where outsiders' scales and promises are doubted.
- **Karen and Lua:** small-village life where a lie is quickly known.
- **Phu Tai and Yuan of Isan:** tight communities whose shops and stalls live on a name for fair dealing.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for honesty taught as religious duty in tadika lessons, against Thai integrity slogans. **Thai workers in Korea:** comparison only, for brokers' lies and visa overstays seen from both sides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on corruption perception and exam cheating; mixed on everyday white lies; thin on minorities' own ideas of candor.
- **Languages needed:** court verse for *Phali Son Nong*; Pak Tai for nang talung jokes; Hmong and Yunnanese Mandarin for hill markets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming corrupt officials risks defamation suits; anti-graft law and courts belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the precept on false speech as taught from its classical-language source (category only).
