# Thailand: The domain, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Teochew; Sgaw Karen; Northern Khmer. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys and what is owed: career, status, reputation, authority and responsibility, with age, time, rank, order and power ways as the folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** seat of ministries, palace and academies.
- **Lanna north:** princely houses whose command passed to Bangkok around 1900.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** Phu Tai towns founded by resettled war captives under Bangkok-named governors.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Nora and shadow-play lineages; Satun's and Songkhla's mixed villages.
- **Thai Chinese and the Phuket Baba-Peranakan:** the thao kae and clan associations beside granted Siamese titles.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy of Surin, Sisaket and Buriram:** lay ritual masters; Kuy elephant masters as a separate rank line.
- **Karen, Hmong, Mien, Lahu and Akha:** clan and village offices beside the Interior Ministry's headmen.
- **Mon and Tai Yai of the west:** Mon nobles in old court service; Mae Hong Son's Tai Yai princely past.
- **Lua, Mlabri, Maniq and Moken:** little formal office: the low end of rank.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, where imam and pondok teacher, not abbot, head village respect.
- **Thai migrants in Taiwan, Israel and South Korea:** comparison only, for Thai workers under foreign bosses and brokers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on court, state and Sangha; mixed on firms and hill peoples. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; every Capricorn sheet is written for today's core lands, and this note appears only here.
- **Languages needed:** Thai with its royal register; Tham script for Lanna and Isan palm leaves; Teochew and Hokkien for association records; Sgaw Karen for village offices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the monarchy heads this domain and is legally guarded. Some Nora and Kuy lineages keep initiatory knowledge closed.
- **Held for review:** the classical-language sources behind the ten royal virtues (category only); South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
