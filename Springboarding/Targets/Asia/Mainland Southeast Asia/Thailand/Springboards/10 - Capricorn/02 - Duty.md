# Thailand: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Pak Tai; Northern Khmer; Teochew; Sgaw Karen; Hmong. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** office workers with parents upcountry, often the money side of family duty.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** a migrant-sending region; check how daughters' and sons' money duties are each described.
- **Lanna north:** lineage spirits that bind duty to women's lines.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** tenth-month duties to ancestors; zakat for Muslim Thais of Satun and Songkhla.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket Baba-Peranakan:** tomb festivals, ancestor tablets and the eldest son's role.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** ancestor offerings in the tenth month, beside Kuy obligations to elephant spirits.
- **Tai Yai and Mon:** ordination festivals as parent-repaying duty in their own scripts and dress.
- **Karen, Hmong and Mien:** forest ties, funeral chants and clan duties to the dead.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan of Isan):** a community long registered as aliens, whose duties to a homeland across the Mekong need their own accounts.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for religious duties (prayer, fasting, zakat) that set the community's calendar instead of the merit year.
- **Isan women in Denmark and Germany:** comparison only, for the duty to send money home when the sender is a wife abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on filial duty and ancestor rites; mixed on civic duty; thin on hill peoples' duties in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Northern Khmer for Surin rites; Tai Yai for ordination songs; Teochew for clan halls; Sgaw Karen and Hmong for forest and funeral duties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conscript abuse and draft avoidance; duty to the throne is legally protected speech. Hmong funeral chants are specialist knowledge.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural discourse on a householder's duties often cited in Thai sermons (category only).
