# Thailand: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Kuy and Northern Khmer; Hmong; Moken. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** where flood and office blame are fought out in the national press.
- **Lanna north, with Hmong, Karen, Lahu and Akha uplands:** the haze dispute sets valley, hill and agribusiness against one another.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** the youngest daughter's charge of house and parents; check how sons' charge differs.
- **Phu Tai of Kalasin and Sakon Nakhon:** whether their own household customs place the charge differently from Lao Isan neighbors.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani, with the Moken and Urak Lawoi:** tsunami coasts where warning and rebuilding raised the blame question.
- **Thai Chinese:** the eldest son and the family firm's succession as a charge across generations.
- **Kuy and Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin:** elephant keeping as a household charge with public stakes.
- **Lua and Tai Lue of Nan:** village forest rules and who answers when they are broken.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for blame for violence passed between the state and armed groups, with civilians caught between.
- **Thai workers in Israel and South Korea:** comparison only, for who answers when a migrant is hurt or dies abroad: broker, employer, family or the Labor Ministry.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official accountability and disaster blame; mixed on households; thin on sea nomads and hill peoples in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Kham Mueang for Songkran pardon rites; Isan for household customs; Kuy and Northern Khmer at Ta Klang; Hmong and Karen for the haze dispute; Moken and Urak Lawoi with community consent.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blame for haze and deforestation lands hard on hill peoples, so weigh their own accounts first; the role of the throne in disasters is guarded.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural source text behind the Phra Malai story (category only).
