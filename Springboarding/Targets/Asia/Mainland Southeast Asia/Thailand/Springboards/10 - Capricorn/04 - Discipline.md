# Thailand: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Hokkien; Hmong; Akha; Sgaw Karen. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, churches, the state) or by oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** elite schools, cadet academies and mass meditation halls; test whether they set the national template.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** forest-monk austerity; check how muay camps take in village boys.
- **Lanna north:** martial dance and drum teachers, and Christian schools of long standing in Chiang Mai.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** the Suan Mokkh model of silence, and Ramadan fasting among Satun and Songkhla Muslims.
- **Phuket Baba-Peranakan and Thai Chinese:** abstinence and mortification during the ninth-month festival.
- **Hmong, Akha and Karen:** children shaped in hostels run by temples, churches or the state, far from home.
- **Tai Yai and Mon:** novice training in their own scripts; check differences in monastic routine.
- **Mlabri and Maniq:** forest peoples where outside schools first brought timetables; ask their own view.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for pondok schooling, where Qur'an study and the daily prayers set the pupil's routine.
- **Thai boxers in gyms abroad:** comparison only, for how the muay camp's training order is carried to foreign students.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schools, monks and boxing; mixed on families; thin on hill and forest peoples' own child-training.
- **Languages needed:** Thai with military and campus slang; Isan for camp talk; Hokkien for festival rules; Hmong, Akha and Sgaw Karen for hostel memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse of children, cadets and conscripts; former hostel pupils may not wish to be named. Medium ordeals have their own restrictions.
- **Held for review:** the classical-language monastic rulebook itself (category only).
