# Thailand: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Pak Tai; Teochew; Hmong. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them; in Capricorn only the spirit (what law is for, obedience, discretion, legal culture). The letter of the law is in Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** courts, protest prosecutions and the "clearing" of traffic and petty cases.
- **Lanna north:** khuet custom, spirit fines and community forests set against national park law.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** hit khong and village mediation before any police report.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** a remembered history of state killings, and Muslim Thais who take family matters to imams.
- **Thai Chinese:** clan associations and the memory of secret societies as private order.
- **Hmong and Karen:** clan or village settlement beside forest law; count how often they appear in forest cases.
- **Lua, Tai Lue and Tai Yai:** border-zone villages where papers decide which law applies.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** marine-park rules over customary fishing and burial grounds.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan of Isan):** decades under alien registration and movement limits; check their memories of policing.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for emergency decrees and martial law as the standing frame, and Islamic family courts beside them.
- **Thai workers in Taiwan and South Korea:** comparison only, for how undocumented Thais abroad (the "little ghosts", ผีน้อย) weigh foreign law against earnings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on courts, impunity and political law; mixed on daily compliance; thin on minority custom in its own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai including legal register; Tham script for Lanna custom texts; Isan; Teochew for clan records; Hmong and Sgaw Karen for village settlement; Malay for Muslim family matters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lèse-majesté and sedition cases carry real risk for researchers and sources; anonymize interviewees. Some Lanna and Karen custom is ritually restricted.
- **Held for review:** the classical-language legal treatises behind the old royal law (category only).
