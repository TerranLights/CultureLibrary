# Thailand: The domain, community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Pak Tai; Teochew; Sgaw Karen; Mlabri. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for: friends, groups, hopes and humanitarian ideals, with settlement, migration, association and affiliation as the folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** canal-side and condo neighborhoods, where neighbors may never meet.
- **Lanna north:** village heart-posts and the Lua's place in city founding rites.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** soul-calling for returning migrants; how labor migration reshapes who stays.
- **Phu Tai of Sakon Nakhon:** towns of resettled communities, with their own welcome rites.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** market towns where Buddhist and Muslim Thai-speakers share one *lat*.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket's Baba-Peranakan:** dialect halls and shrine committees as the first bond of newcomers.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan) of Nakhon Phanom and Udon Thani:** mid-century arrivals and their own associations, to check.
- **Karen, Hmong and Lahu:** clan ties that cross the border, and New Year gatherings.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** village-wide ancestral and elephant-village gatherings in Surin and Sisaket.
- **Mlabri, Maniq and Moken:** small bands and boat groups, where "we" may mean a few households.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, on how the mosque congregation, not the temple, sets the circle of neighbors.
- **Thais in Los Angeles, Sydney and Berlin:** comparison only, on which temple and food networks bind migrants abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the village-community debate and civil society; mixed for hill and sea peoples; thin for newcomers' guides in Thai. Thailand's post-war lands are not yet fixed; these Aquarius sheets take the present core lands, and the hedge is stated only on this sheet.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan for most sources; Teochew and Hokkien for association halls; Sgaw Karen, Hmong and Mlabri for the peoples' own names for "us".
- **Sensitive or restricted:** humanitarian bodies under royal patronage are written about cautiously; refugee testimony needs consent and care.
- **Held for review:** canonical-language teachings on concord within the monastic order, as background to *samakkhi* (category only); South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
