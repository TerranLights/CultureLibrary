# Thailand: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Pak Tai; Northern Khmer; Hmong; Moken. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** settlements under eviction against gated estates (หมู่บ้านจัดสรร).
- **Lanna north:** villages bound by weirs and guardian spirits.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** merit gatherings kept up while working-age members live in Bangkok.
- **Tai Lue of Nan and Phayao:** villages that keep the name of their Sipsongpanna origin.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Songkhla Lake fishing villages; mixed Buddhist and Muslim hamlets of Satun.
- **Karen of Lamphun and Tak:** faith-built settlements around a revered monk.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** homecoming rites; Kuy elephant villages around Ta Klang.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** boat flotillas and shore villages after the tsunami.
- **Thai Chinese:** shrine committees that run a neighborhood's festival.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for a village counted around its mosque and *pondok*.
- **Thai temples abroad (e.g. Wat Thai of Los Angeles):** comparison only, as the hub of a dispersed migrant community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich, in Thai, on the community-culture debate and on Bangkok slums; mixed on Lanna village rites; thin on Thai Chinese neighborhoods as communities.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Tham script for Lanna and Isan custom texts; Northern Khmer for festival songs; Moken and Urak Lawoi for oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** evictions and land rights are live disputes (law aspects to Libra); some Karen and Moken communities are stateless or lack land titles.
- **Held for review:** monastic-canon passages on the order's own common life, as a model for lay community (category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Read Chatthip's English edition and note every claim about the self-reliant village to test.
2. Search ThaiLIS for "เหมืองฝาย" and "แก่ฝาย", then for "ผีเสื้อบ้าน", by province.
3. Download one province's กชช. 2ค tables and list the community groups per village.
4. Search CODI's site for Baan Mankong case reports from Khlong Toei and one Isan city.
5. Search news archives for "อูรักลาโว้ย ราไวย์" and "มอแกน สึนามิ" for post-2004 and land-dispute coverage.
