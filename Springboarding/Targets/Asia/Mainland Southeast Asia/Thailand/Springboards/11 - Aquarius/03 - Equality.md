# Thailand: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Mien; Tai Yai. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok elites and the Central Plains:** old titled families and new money, set against the city's informal workers.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** the most populous region; how far the *phrai* frame was taken up there is a question to test.
- **Lanna north:** *khon mueang* lowlanders and the hill peoples around them.
- **Mien, Hmong, Karen, Lahu, Akha and Lisu:** the "hill tribe" label, citizenship and land.
- **Lua and Mlabri:** Lua standing today set against their role in Lanna founding rites; Mlabri sharing in small bands.
- **Tai Yai (Shan) in Mae Hong Son and Chiang Mai:** citizens and newer non-citizen workers side by side.
- **Thai Chinese and Thai Vietnamese:** paths from outsider to equal citizen, at different speeds.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Thai-speaking Muslims of Satun and Songkhla as a test of religious equality.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea peoples whose papers and land claims test equal citizenship.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for claims of equal standing made as a people rather than as individuals.
- **Thai women in Europe:** comparison only, for how marriage migration changes standing at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income inequality and the red-shirt frame; mixed on gender and sexual minorities; thin for Mlabri and Moken, beyond NGO reports.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and its rank-marked pronouns; Isan; Mien and Tai Yai for highland and migrant voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** any comparison that touches the monarchy is legally risky; citizenship, marriage equality and discrimination law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); slurs are studied, never repeated.
- **Held for review:** monastic teaching texts in their original learned language on whether birth fixes worth (category only).
