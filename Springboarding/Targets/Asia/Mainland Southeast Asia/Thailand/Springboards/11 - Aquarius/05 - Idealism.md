# Thailand: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan (Lao script in older texts); Kham Mueang; Vietnamese; Hmong. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** university idealism of 1973, and its fate in later careers.
- **Lanna north:** the holy-man ideal around which builders' movements formed.
- **Isan, Lao Isan and Phu Tai:** epic heroes on temple walls; Phu Phan villages that joined the armed left.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan) of Nakhon Phanom, Sakon Nakhon and Udon:** revolutionary networks and their memory today.
- **Hmong of Phu Hin Rong Kla and Nan:** highland recruits to the left, and how they now tell it.
- **Karen of Mae Hong Son and Tak:** an ideal of forest stewardship; test whose ideal it is, Karen elders' or outside advocates'.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Suan Mokkh at Chaiya and the ideals it spread.
- **Thai Chinese:** Chinese-school leftism in the 1940s and 1950s, *check*.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for ideals of a just Islamic order that run apart from Thai programs.
- **Thai Buddhist meditation centers in Europe and North America:** comparison only, for which Thai ideals travel and how they are recast for foreigners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on student and leftist idealism and on engaged Buddhism; mixed on northern holy men; thin on Hmong and Phu Tai accounts of the forest years.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Isan and Lao script for epic texts; Vietnamese for Thai Vietnamese memoirs; Hmong for oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 6 October 1976 and the armed-left years still divide families; some ideals named here are tied to the monarchy and must be written about with care.
- **Held for review:** classical-language sources of the bodhisattva ideal that stand behind some Thai heroic tales (category only).
