# Thailand: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Pak Tai; Kham Mueang; Northern Khmer; Hokkien; Hmong; Tai Yai. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** class-year friends from school and university, kept for life.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** *siao* sworn friendship, and friends who travel to Bangkok work together.
- **Lanna north:** *puean* groups at temple fairs; friendship among Tai Lue and Tai Yai villagers and their lowland neighbors.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** *kloe* ties that cross the Buddhist-Muslim line in Songkhla and Satun.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais, Kuy and Lao in Surin and Sisaket:** villages where friends may speak three languages between them.
- **Thai Chinese:** sworn brotherhood and business partners; the *Sam Kok* model in practice.
- **Hmong and Karen:** youths in town schools making friends outside the clan.
- **Mon of Sangkhlaburi:** friendships with Karen and Thai neighbors across a lake town, *check*.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan) of Nakhon Phanom:** friendships with Thai-Lao families, read against the Cold War suspicion of the community, *check*.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for how friendship with Buddhist neighbors fared after 2004.
- **Thai students in Japan and Australia:** comparison only, for whether friend groups abroad stay all Thai.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in film, song and proverbs; mixed on ritual friendship; thin in measured data on numbers of close friends.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and its pronoun registers; Isan and Pak Tai for *siao* and *kloe*; Northern Khmer and Kuy for Surin's multilingual villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patronage among friends shades into corruption claims; boys'-love fandom and men's friendship touch on sexual identity, to be handled with care.
- **Held for review:** classical maxim collections in their original learned language behind *Lokaniti* (category only).
