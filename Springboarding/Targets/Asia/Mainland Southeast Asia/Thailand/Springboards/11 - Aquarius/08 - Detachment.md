# Thailand: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Hokkien (Phuket); Vietnamese; Moken. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** office and condominium distance; meditation as a weekend practice.
- **Lanna north:** hermit legends and forest retreats in the hills around Chiang Mai.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** the forest-monk heartland; *bo pen yang* in daily talk.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Suan Mokkh at Chaiya and the southern lay following of its retreats.
- **Phuket's Baba-Peranakan and the Thai Chinese:** the vegetarian fast as yearly withdrawal from meat, sex and quarrels.
- **Thai Vietnamese (Yuan):** Mahayana renunciation in Annam Nikai temples.
- **Mae chi and women renunciants:** detachment without full monastic status.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea peoples' relation to place and goods.
- **Karen and Lua of the hills:** spirit-based ideas of loss set beside Buddhist letting go in mixed villages, *check*.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for Malay Islamic *sabar* (patience) and *tawakal* (reliance on God) set beside *ploi wang*.
- **Thai monasteries in Britain and the United States (e.g. Amaravati, Abhayagiri):** comparison only, for how forest teaching on letting go is retold to Western lay people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Buddhist renunciation and meditation; mixed on everyday display rules; thin on bystander research in Thai.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and its Pali-derived religious register; Isan for forest-monk talks; Hokkien for Phuket festival texts; Vietnamese for Annam Nikai liturgy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monastic scandals and the state's control of the Sangha color the sources; elders left alone is a painful topic for families.
- **Held for review:** canonical discourses in their original learned language on non-attachment, as cited by Thai teachers (category only).
