# Thailand: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Pak Tai; Teochew; Northern Khmer; Hmong. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** lottery and shrine hopes in the city; middle-class hopes for children's schooling.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** holy-man movements in the past; migrant hopes in song today.
- **Phu Tai of Sakon Nakhon and Kalasin:** soul-calling and village rites for a returning member's luck, *check*.
- **Lanna north:** fate-extending rites for persons and rivers; Tai Lue weavers' hopes in a revived craft market.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** the Jatukham boom; fishing towns' hopes after the 2004 tsunami.
- **Thai Chinese and Phuket's Baba-Peranakan:** New Year luck and business hopes; *heng* as a word of the shop.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy of Surin and Sisaket:** offerings to the dead as a family's request for a good year, *check*.
- **Karen of the Thai-Myanmar border:** camp residents' hopes of resettlement or return.
- **Hmong:** hopes tied to the clan's future and to kin abroad.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for hopes framed as peace talks and as reward in the afterlife, apart from Thai hopes of a coming age.
- **Thai workers in South Korea:** comparison only, for the debt and hope carried by undocumented migrants and their families at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lottery, amulet and shrine practice and on surveys of expectations; mixed on Lanna and Khmer rites; thin on Hmong and Karen voices.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Isan for holy-man narratives; Kham Mueang and Tham script for *suep chata* texts; Teochew for temple materials; Northern Khmer and Hmong for village voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hopes placed in royal figures are guarded by law and custom; suicide data require care in use; underground lottery and amulet trading touch crime and law (to Libra).
- **Held for review:** canonical-language sources of the future-Buddha prophecy that Thai *Phra Malai* texts draw on (category only).
