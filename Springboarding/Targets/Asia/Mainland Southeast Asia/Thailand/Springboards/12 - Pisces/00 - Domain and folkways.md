# Thailand: The domain, the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Pak Tai; Northern Khmer; Lua (Lawa); White and Green Hmong. **Concept in one line:** how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden self, madness, sleep and dreams, confinement, spirits, transcendence, with magic ways as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** amulet markets and Mae Nak's shrine beside the first asylum's city.
- **Lanna north:** lineage-spirit dances and Lua guardian rites that predate the city temples.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** sky-spirit healing and the village *phi pu ta* shrine.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** Nora teacher-spirits and shared rites at the coast.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy of Surin and Sisaket:** healing dances and the elephant-spirit rites of the Kuy.
- **Hmong, Karen and Lahu:** shamans and soul-calling beside mission and state clinics.
- **Mlabri and Maniq:** forest peoples whose own spirit words are barely recorded.
- **Thai Chinese:** spirit-medium shrines and the Phuket vegetarian procession.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for where Islamic healers and the *bomoh* draw the line that Buddhist Thai healers draw elsewhere.
- **Thais abroad (for example in Los Angeles or Stockholm):** comparison only, for which hidden-world practices travel with migrants and which stay home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spirits, amulets and films; mixed for mental health; thin for Mlabri, Maniq and Moken inner life. Thailand's post-war lands are not yet settled (no scope file exists); these Pisces sheets take the present core lands, and the note is given here only.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, Kham Mueang, Isan and Pak Tai for ritual terms; Northern Khmer, Kuy, Lua and Hmong for healers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' knowledge may be guarded; asylum records carry patient privacy; royal patronage shapes what is printed about temples and hospitals.
- **Held for review:** the liturgical-language chant texts that monks use against spirits (category only).
