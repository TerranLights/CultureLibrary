# Thailand: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Tai Lue; Northern Khmer; White Hmong; Thai Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thai | ความเห็นอกเห็นใจ (khwam hen ok hen chai) | "seeing the chest, seeing the heart": sympathy, empathy | the usual translation of "empathy" in psychology papers, *check* |
| Thai | เอาใจเขามาใส่ใจเรา (ao chai khao ma sai chai rao) | "put their heart into ours" | the classroom maxim of perspective-taking |
| Thai | เกรงใจ (kreng chai) | reluctance to impose, anticipating another's discomfort | here as reading another's state before acting |
| Thai | รู้ใจ (ru chai) | to know someone's heart without being told | praised in friends and spouses |
| Thai | อิน (in) | to be "into" a character, absorbed | loanword slang for audience identification |
| Thai | ซึ้ง (sueng) | deeply moved | what viewers say of a touching scene |
| Isan; Kham Mueang | ฮู้ใจ (hu chai) | knowing another's heart | regional sound of *ru chai*, *check* |
| Northern Khmer | จรียง (chariang) | sung repartee of Surin and Sisaket | singers answer each other's feelings, *check* |
| White Hmong | paj ntaub | "flower cloth", embroidered story cloths | telling one's own life to outsiders |
| Thai Sign Language | ภาษามือไทย (phasa mue thai) | Thai Sign Language | Deaf community's own channel for being understood |

English keywords: empathy Thailand, Thai perspective-taking, Thai medical students empathy, Thai crisis line, attitudes to migrant workers Thailand, audience identification Thai drama.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** service-sector empathy as trained work, and middle-class views of migrants.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** the songs that ask the city to feel with the migrant.
- **Lanna north and the Tai Lue of Nan:** mural traditions that look kindly on common people.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** how reading a guest's mood is taught in tourist towns like Krabi and Phuket.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** sung repartee as a test of reading the other.
- **Hmong and Karen:** being understood by outsiders through cloths, songs and testimony.
- **Deaf Thais:** empathy across a language line inside the nation.
- **Shan and other migrant workers:** whose feelings the surveys show as counted or not.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for what national surveys say about feeling for southern Malay Muslims across the religious line.
- **Thai students abroad and Thai spouses in Europe:** comparison only, for their accounts of moving between a reading-the-room culture and one of saying things outright.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: many translated scales, fewer studies on Thai words for feeling-with; thin for minority sources.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan; Northern Khmer for the sung forms; Hmong and Thai Sign Language for the groups' own testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** juvenile case material is closed; migrant-worker attitudes touch ethnic prejudice and must be quoted with care.
- **Held for review:** none; the classroom maxims and murals named are Thai and Tai Lue.
