# Thailand: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Tai Yai (Shan); Phuket Hokkien; Vietnamese (of the Yuan of Isan); Kham Mueang. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Plains:** royal and village sacrifice in the state's history lessons.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** daughters and sons who send wages home; the weight of that duty by sex.
- **Lanna north:** ordination as repayment, and body donation in Chiang Mai's medical school.
- **Tai Yai of Mae Hong Son:** novice festivals as a family's offering.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** island heroines and coastal rescuers.
- **Phuket Baba-Peranakan and Thai Chinese:** mediums who suffer for the shrine's community.
- **Thai Vietnamese of Nakhon Phanom:** sacrifice for a cause across the river.
- **Karen and Mon of the western border:** families divided by flight; what elders say they gave up.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for how deaths in the southern conflict are called martyrdom by some and loss by others.
- **Thai workers in Israel, Taiwan and South Korea:** comparison only, for how years abroad are spoken of as a gift to the family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heroes and festivals; mixed for caregiving and remittances; thin for minorities' own words for giving oneself.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan; Shan for Poy Sang Long; Hokkien and Baba speech for Phuket shrines; Vietnamese for the Yuan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal heroines and political hunger strikes are touchy topics; self-harm framed as protest needs care.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural-language birth stories of a self-giving hero that Thai sermons retell (category only).

5. Search Phuket shrine records and newspapers for "ม้าทรง" with "จุ้ยตุ่ย".
