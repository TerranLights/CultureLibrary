# Thailand: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Mon; Nyah Kur; Chong; Teochew; Mlabri. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** mass-meditation crowds and urban divorce.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** all-night *mo lam* abandon at temple fairs.
- **Lanna north:** meditation reform and lost manuscript traditions.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** island party culture and the locals around it.
- **Thai Chinese of Yaowarat and the provinces:** assimilation and the fading of Teochew forms.
- **Mon and Karen of Sangkhlaburi:** a drowned town and a moved temple.
- **Nyah Kur of Chaiyaphum and Chong of Chanthaburi:** languages near their end.
- **Mlabri and Moken:** ways of life dissolving under settlement.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for whether Malay holds or gives way to Thai in a separate culture's schools and pondok.
- **Thai-American second generation:** comparison only, for how far the Thai language fades within a single generation abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for meditation and festivals; mixed for language loss; thin for dementia in Thai voices and for forager peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan; Mon, Nyah Kur, Chong and Mlabri for speakers' own accounts; Teochew for opera.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Dhammakaya is a contested movement; resettled peoples' losses need consent and care.
