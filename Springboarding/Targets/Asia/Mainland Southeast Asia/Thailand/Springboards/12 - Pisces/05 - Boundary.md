# Thailand: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Isan; Akha; White Hmong; Kuy; Phuket Hokkien. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** gated estates, condos and the rise of "privacy" talk.
- **Lanna north:** lineage-spirit rules on touch and courtship.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** *khalam* taboos and border life along the Mekong.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** shophouse towns where Buddhist and Muslim neighbors share streets; check how food and kitchen lines are drawn.
- **Phuket Baba-Peranakan:** the five-foot way as a half-shared space.
- **Akha and Hmong:** spirit gates and closed-door taboo days.
- **Kuy and Khmer-speaking Thais of Surin:** sacred objects and who may touch them.
- **Karen and Tai Yai at Mae Sot and Mae Hong Son:** lives lived across the national line.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for where the lines between the sexes and between halal and non-halal food are drawn in a separate culture next door.
- **Thai wives of European men:** comparison only, for their accounts of colliding notions of family closeness and privacy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for body etiquette and spirit taboos; mixed for privacy and personal space; thin for Kuy and Akha in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Kham Mueang; Akha, Hmong and Kuy for spirit lines; Hokkien and Malay loanwords for Phuket's streetscape.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Akha and Kuy objects are not to be touched or photographed; border disputes with Cambodia are a live political topic.
- **Held for review:** the monastic code's original-language rules on monks and women (category only).
