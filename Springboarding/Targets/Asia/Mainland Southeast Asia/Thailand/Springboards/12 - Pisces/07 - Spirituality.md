# Thailand: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Kham Mueang; Tai Yai; Phu Tai; Pak Tai; Northern Khmer; Lao Isan. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the institution.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** the urban shrine rush and lay retreats.
- **Lanna north:** *khruba* followings and life-extension rites for rivers and cities.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** village guardian groves and Catholic martyr shrines on the Mekong.
- **Phu Tai:** healing song beside the temple.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** the stupa-wrapping crowds and coastal shrines.
- **Eastern Thais of Chanthaburi:** a mountain pilgrimage made at night.
- **Khmer-speaking Thais and Kuy:** ancient sanctuaries and village guardians.
- **Tai Yai and Tai Lue:** temple festivals of the north's Tai peoples.
- **Karen and Hmong:** Christian converts, spirit keepers and those who move between them.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for visits to the tombs of holy men (*keramat*, *check*), which sit outside Thailand's shrine culture.
- **Thai temples in the American West and Europe:** comparison only, for whether Western members practice meditation without the shrine and vow side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buddhist practice and shrines; mixed for minorities; thin for spirituality outside both temple and church, where only social media speaks.
- **Languages needed:** Thai, Kham Mueang and Shan; Phu Tai and Isan; Northern Khmer and Kuy; English for the foreign-retreat literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some rites are not to be filmed; monastic controversies and nuns' ordination are contested topics.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural-language meditation manuals behind Thai retreat practice (category only).
