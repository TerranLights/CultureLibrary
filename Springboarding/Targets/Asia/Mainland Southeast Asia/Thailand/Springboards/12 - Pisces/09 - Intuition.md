# Thailand: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Teochew; Moken; Sgaw Karen; Lahu; Isan; Pak Tai. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** fortune-teller rows, lottery hunches and the court's harvest forecast.
- **Lanna north:** manuscript traditions of signs, and hill peoples' augury close by.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** rain and harvest omens in farming villages.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** fishermen's sea sense along both coasts.
- **Moken and Urak Lawoi:** sea knowledge proved in 2004.
- **Thai Chinese:** face reading and fengshui in business choices.
- **Karen, Lahu and Lisu:** bone augury as a deciding practice.
- **Kuy and Khmer-speaking Thais:** knowledge of elephants built over generations.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for the *bomoh* and Islamic dream guidance as a neighbor's ways of knowing without proof.
- **Thai fortune-tellers working in Los Angeles and Sydney:** comparison only, for whether readers abroad change what signs they read for migrant clients.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for omens and fortune trades; mixed for expert intuition; thin for minorities' own words for hunches.
- **Languages needed:** Thai; Teochew loanwords; Moken and Urak Lawoi; Karen, Lahu and Lisu; Pak Tai for fishers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monks' claims of special knowledge are a disciplinary matter in the Sangha; some augury is private to the household.
- **Held for review:** the original-language meditation texts that define higher knowledge (category only).
