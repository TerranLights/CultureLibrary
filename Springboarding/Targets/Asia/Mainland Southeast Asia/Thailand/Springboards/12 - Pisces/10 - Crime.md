# Thailand: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Thailand, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Central Thai; Isan; Kham Mueang; Teochew; Sgaw Karen; Hmong; Moken. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law as written and legal culture go to the Libra and Capricorn sheets; this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bangkok and the Central Plains:** crime pages, reenactments and true-crime television.
- **Eastern seaboard (Chon Buri, Rayong):** the *chao pho* as a local institution.
- **Isan and Lao Isan:** migrants cast as thieves in the city, and village views of theft at home.
- **Lanna north and the hill peoples:** Karen, Hmong, Akha and Lahu cast as offenders by lowland media.
- **Southern Thai outside Patani:** the Si Ui case and what Nakhon Si Thammarat villagers said of him.
- **Thai Chinese:** the long shadow of the secret-society image.
- **Tai Yai and Shan migrants:** border towns and the drug-courier label.
- **Moken:** sea foragers whose fishing inside national parks has been treated as a crime.
- **Patani (separate culture-nation):** comparison only, for how the same acts are named crime, rebellion or martyrdom across the line of the southern conflict.
- **Thai migrants without papers abroad (for example in South Korea):** comparison only, for how being "illegal" is felt by people who see themselves as honest workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime news and corruption surveys; mixed for public views of wrongdoers; thin for minority ideas of wrongdoing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Thai and Isan; Teochew for the secret-society era; Karen, Hmong and Moken for communities labeled as offenders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2003 drug war, disappearances and royal-defamation cases are dangerous ground; never name private suspects; written law is the Libra sheet's, legal culture the Capricorn sheet's.
- **Held for review:** none; the moral imagery named is from Thai temples, not from any text in a learned language.
