# Vietnam: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm for older texts); Cham, Khmer, Tày-Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Mường, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar; Hoa varieties; French for colonial sources. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it asserts itself, across the body, appearance, beginnings, initiative, naming ways and sport.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kinh of the Red River Delta, the central coast (Huế; Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh) and the Mekong Delta and Saigon:** naming, xưng hô and lineage practice may differ by region.
- **Hoa** (Cantonese, Teochew, Hokkien, Hakka; the older Minh Hương): a second naming and lineage system.
- **Khmer Krom:** temple-centered life stages.
- **Cham Ahier, Cham Awal and the Cham Muslims of An Giang:** three religious frames for one people.
- **Northern uplands:** Tày, Nùng, Black and White Thái, Mường, Hmong, Dao, Hà Nhì, Lô Lô; rites that give adult names and marks.
- **Central Highlands and Trường Sơn:** Ê Đê and Jarai (matrilineal), Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Mnông, K'Ho, Cơ Tu, Bru-Vân Kiều, Raglai.
- **Faith communities:** Catholics, Buddhists, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo, highland Protestants.
- Overseas Vietnamese (Little Saigon, Paris, Berlin, Sydney), for comparison only: which names, dress and marks of the self are kept or changed on arrival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh literature and lineage; mixed for minorities (much is state ethnography, read critically); thin in English for Cham and highland personhood.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm for lineage books; French; minority languages for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic classification is political; some minority rites and names are private; lineage books are family property. Civil naming rules are law: point to Libra. Vietnam's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; these sheets write for the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and the classical-language inscriptions and scriptures of South Asian origin in some Cham and Khmer Krom religious life, as a category only.
