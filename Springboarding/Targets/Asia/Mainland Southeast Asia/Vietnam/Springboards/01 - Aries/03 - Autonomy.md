# Vietnam: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Nôm for older didactic verse); Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Thái and Tày for marriage and household choice; Cham for matrilineal households; French for colonial-era family writing. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ê Đê, Jarai, Raglai and Cham Ahier households:** matrilineal descent changes whose choice counts.
- **Hmong and Dao families in the northern uplands:** clan say over marriage; courtship markets such as Khâu Vai in Hà Giang *check*.
- **Tày, Nùng and Thái:** peoples who once lived under the autonomous zones.
- **Kinh in the Red River Delta against the Mekong Delta:** household authority and the age of leaving home may differ.
- **Rural-to-urban migrants:** autonomy won by leaving the village and lost to factory rules.
- **Catholic parishes:** the priest's and parish's say over marriage and family life.
- **Women traders and household business owners:** control over money and time.
- **Older people living with married children:** whose house, whose choices.
- **Hoa families:** family-firm control over children's careers.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: how much say parents keep over marriage and careers when the family lives abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family and marriage (Vietnamese family studies are large); mixed for medical choice; thin and politically shaped for collective autonomy of minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Nôm for *Truyện Kiều* and didactic verse; Hmong, Ê Đê and Jarai for fieldwork; French for older ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bride capture, highland self-rule movements and religious bodies outside state recognition are sensitive; reproductive decisions are private. Rules on marriage age, consent and population policy are law: point to Libra; what obedience to authority is for goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
