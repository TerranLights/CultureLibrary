# Vietnam: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán for 19th-century memorials); Cantonese and Teochew for Hoa business history; Hmong, Dao, Cham and Ê Đê for local economic life; French for colonial-era enterprise. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

English keywords: Vietnamese entrepreneurship; Đổi Mới fence-breaking; household contracting Vietnam; volunteerism Vietnam youth; employee initiative Vietnam; Hoa entrepreneurs Chợ Lớn.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Saigon against Hà Nội business circles:** founding stories and appetite for private ventures may differ.
- **Hoa merchant families:** guild and kin support behind starting a firm.
- **Cham Muslim traders of An Giang:** mobile trade as a livelihood of their own.
- **Hmong and Dao in Lào Cai and Hà Giang:** borderland trade and homestay tourism.
- **Kinh migrants and Ê Đê in Đắk Lắk's coffee zones:** who starts farms and on whose land.
- **Red River farmers of Vĩnh Phúc and neighboring provinces:** the history of local experiments.
- **Khmer Krom in Sóc Trăng and Trà Vinh:** temple-centered collective work against private ventures.
- **State-sector against private-sector employees:** acting unasked rewarded or punished.
- **Women in household businesses:** the large small-trade sector.
- **Former Youth Volunteer Force members:** a generation shaped by volunteering first.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: how small family ventures are started in another economy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and reform history; mixed for workplace culture; thin for minority enterprise beyond a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for 19th-century memorials; Cantonese and Teochew for Hoa sources; Hmong for borderland trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** private civic initiative outside state bodies, independent charity drives and land disputes in the highlands are sensitive. Business registration and charity rules are law: point to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Kerkvliet (2005) and Fforde and de Vylder (1996) for initiative from below in the reform story.

5. Read *Frontier Livelihoods* and Taylor (2007) for minority enterprise.
