*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kinh of the north, the center and the south:** Tết firsts and day-choosing customs may differ in detail and weight.
- **Hoa in Chợ Lớn:** Lunar New Year and Lantern Festival customs at Thiên Hậu and congregational temples.
- **Khmer Krom:** a new year in April, opened at the temple.
- **Cham Ahier, Cham Awal and Cham Muslims:** three calendars and three sets of opening rites in one people.
- **Tày and Nùng:** farming-year openings.
- **Mường:** creation sung in *mo* ritual at funerals and rites.
- **Hmong and Dao:** clan New Year feasts; Dao rites that start a man's ritual life.
- **Bahnar, Jarai, Ê Đê, Xơ Đăng:** new-rice and newborn rites.
- **Catholics:** baptism and the Christian year as another calendar of beginnings.
- **Families resettled after 1975 and migrants to the Central Highlands:** starting over on new land.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: refugee arrival stories and Tết kept abroad as another kind of beginning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Tết and Kinh customs; mixed for minority new-year and birth rites (state festival literature dominates); thin for starting over after failure.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for edicts and temple texts; Khmer, Cham, Hmong and highland languages for the rites themselves.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** post-1975 resettlement and re-education are sensitive; some birth and calendar rites are family or clergy matters; calendrical divination is popular but officially discouraged at times.
- **Held for review:** the classical-language calendrical and ritual texts of South Asian origin used in some Cham and Khmer Krom year rites, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Chiếu dời đô* and search for school textbooks that teach it, to see how a founding is taught.
2. Search "xông đất" with "tuổi" (birth year) and "xem ngày khai trương" for how first acts are timed.
3. Read Toan Ánh's Tết volume beside one Khmer Krom and one Cham festival account.
4. Search "lễ thổi tai" and "cúng mụ" for when a newborn becomes a person.
5. Search memoir collections for "vùng kinh tế mới" for starting over.
