# Vietnam: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán for exhortations and annals); Ê Đê and other Central Highlands languages for epic heroes; Hmong for uprising histories; Cham for Cham heroic verse; French and English for war-era archives. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern and southern veterans' families:** two war memories; whose courage is public and whose is private.
- **Women of the Mekong Delta and the north:** "long-haired army" protest against front-line service.
- **Fishing communities of Lý Sơn and the central coast:** courage at sea as a trade.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar and Mnông:** epic heroes and village defense.
- **Hmong:** remembered revolts.
- **Tày and Nùng of Cao Bằng and Lạng Sơn:** border-war memory since 1979.
- **Cham:** heroic figures of Champa such as Po Rome, told in Cham verse.
- **Khmer Krom:** courage stories told at the temple.
- **Catholics:** martyr traditions of their own church.
- **Young urban Vietnamese:** civilian bravery stories online.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: boat-people and veterans' memoirs honor courage that state honors lists leave out.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war heroism and Kinh epics; mixed for civilian bravery; thin for minority and southern-republic perspectives in state sources.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for annals and exhortations; Ê Đê and Hmong; French and English for war archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory is political (the southern side, the 1979 border war, the 1968 massacres); dissidents' moral courage is sensitive; some families keep war records private.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Hịch tướng sĩ* and trace where it is quoted in school textbooks and military writing.
2. Search "dũng cảm cứu người" in the press for civilian courage stories by province.
3. Read Malarney (2002) and Kwon (2008) together for whose courage is honored.
4. Search "Đam San" with "anh hùng" and the Pa Chay uprising for minority heroes.
5. Search "lính Hoàng Sa" "Lý Sơn" for courage at sea.
