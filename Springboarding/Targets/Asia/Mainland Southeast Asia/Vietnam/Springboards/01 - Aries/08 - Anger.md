*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán for medical and Buddhist texts); Khmer for Khmer Krom temple teaching; Cantonese for Hoa families; Hmong, Thái and highland languages for emotion and illness terms in fieldwork. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kinh of the Red River Delta, the central coast and the south:** folk reputations for temper differ by region in jokes and sayings; test them, never adopt them.
- **Men and women:** who may show anger openly, and who must sulk or hold it in.
- **Children and elders:** whose anger is excused.
- **Hoa families:** anger and face in family firms.
- **Khmer Krom:** temple teaching on restraint.
- **Cham Awal and Ahier:** clergy and elders as calmers of disputes.
- **Hmong and Dao:** clan mediation of feuds.
- **Thái and Mường villages:** how quarrels are settled locally.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Bahnar:** village elders' role in cooling conflict.
- **Buddhist lay practitioners and Catholics:** two religious vocabularies for anger.
- **Factory workers and farmers in land disputes:** collective anger.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: anger over the war's outcome as voiced in exile media and community protests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic violence and child discipline (surveys); mixed for display rules; thin for minority emotion terms.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for medical texts; Khmer, Cantonese and minority languages for fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest and land disputes are political; domestic violence data need care; mental-health records are private. Mediation and violence as rules are law: point to Libra.
