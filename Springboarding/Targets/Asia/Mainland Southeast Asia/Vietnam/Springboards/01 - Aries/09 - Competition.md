## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Exam-proud provinces (e.g. Nam Định, Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh) against others:** local reputations for laureates are a claim to test.
- **Hà Nội and Saigon parents:** tutoring markets and school-entrance contests.
- **Red River Delta villages:** wrestling, tug-of-war and festival contests.
- **Hải Phòng's Đồ Sơn:** a coastal animal contest.
- **Khmer Krom of Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh and Bạc Liêu:** temple-team racing.
- **Hmong, Thái, Tày and Mường:** courting games (*pov pob*, *ném còn*) where rivalry meets romance.
- **Mnông and Ê Đê of Đắk Lắk:** elephant culture and its contests.
- **Cham:** whether contests feature in Cham festivals at all; ask before assuming.
- **Hoa traders:** business rivalry inside congregations.
- **Young esports players against their parents:** two generations' views of play and winning.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: diaspora pageants and student contests as a second stage for rivalry.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams and education; mixed for sport and festival contests (much is tourism writing); thin for minority views of competition.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for stelae and laureate lists; Khmer, Hmong and Thái for festival games.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** animal fights draw welfare debate and gambling concerns; exam cheating scandals are politically touchy (e.g. the 2018 score-tampering cases). Competition rules for business and fairness are law: point to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
