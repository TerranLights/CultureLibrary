## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young urban men in Hà Nội and Saigon:** street racing, drinking and showing off on motorbikes.
- **Mekong Delta Kinh and Khmer Krom:** cockfighting and betting as village pastimes.
- **Hoa:** gambling customs in congregations and at festivals.
- **Thái and Hmong of the northwest:** opium's history and today's drug risks, told by the communities themselves.
- **Highland and central-coast miners:** risk taken for income.
- **Fishermen of the central coast:** sea risk as livelihood, not thrill.
- **Retail investors and land speculators:** money risk across classes.
- **Domestic adventure travelers:** daring as leisure.
- **Cham Awal and Cham Muslims:** whether alcohol and gambling customs differ; ask, do not assume.
- **Women:** recklessness in women is likely judged differently; check.
- Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only: how relatives abroad judge the risks taken at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road safety and health risk; mixed for gambling and speculation (largely illegal or press-based); thin for minorities' own risk vocabularies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for early miscellanies; Cantonese; Thái and Hmong for drug and opium histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use, compulsory rehabilitation and illegal gambling are sensitive; opium history in the uplands touches minority stigma. Helmet, drink-driving and gambling rules are law: point to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
