# Vietnam: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Khmer, Hmong, Cantonese (Hoa), Thái, Jarai, Bahnar and Ê Đê for minority material. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta lineages:** altar objects and heirlooms kept across generations in long-settled houses; the deepest baseline for keeping.
- **Mekong delta Kinh:** newer settlement and river mobility; test whether fewer inherited objects follow.
- **HCMC and industrial-zone migrants:** rented rooms with few things; pawn and secondhand use at its densest.
- **Jarai and Bahnar:** grave goods, tomb statues and the release of the dead's belongings.
- **Ê Đê and Mnông:** jars and gongs held by the household; who in a matrilineal longhouse keeps them.
- **Hmong and Dao:** silver and embroidery carried on the body rather than stored.
- **Thái (Black and White Thái):** woven blankets and cloth prepared for marriage.
- **Cham Ahier/Balamon and Awal/Bani:** custody of goods through the female line; priests' ritual objects.
- **Khmer Krom:** belongings given to the pagoda; monks' minimal kit.
- **Hoa:** shop stock and assembly-hall property held for a whole community.
- **Coastal fishers:** boats and nets as the household's main things; painted boat eyes as marks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh rites and folklore; mixed for everyday belongings; thin for smaller highland peoples outside Vietnamese ethnology.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; French for colonial-era ethnography of highland objects; Jarai, Bahnar and Ê Đê for ritual terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** goods confiscated after 1975 and losses of refugees; sacred highland objects (gongs, tomb statues) sold to collectors, whose communities may restrict talk of them. Ownership as a recognized claim is sheet 02; property law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
