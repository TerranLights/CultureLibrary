# Vietnam: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (chữ Hán roots for the formal words); Hmong, Khmer, Cantonese (Hoa), Thái and Cham for minority material. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is thought to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta Kinh:** the scholar ideal and village precedence; worth through learning and examination.
- **Huế and old mandarin families:** standing from rank and lineage, now without office.
- **Mekong delta Kinh:** a looser village hierarchy; test whether standing is granted differently.
- **HCMC youth:** online comparison, brands and the "other people's child" pressure.
- **Factory workers in industrial zones (Bình Dương, Bắc Ninh):** young rural migrants judged by wage and remittance.
- **Hmong of Lào Cai and Hà Giang:** schooling in a second language and minority stereotypes.
- **Thái and Tày old elite lineages:** standing from former chiefly families.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Bahnar:** standing in matrilineal clans and village elders' councils.
- **Khmer Krom:** novice monkhood as a route to standing for young men.
- **Cham:** memory of Champa and a minority's pride in its script and temples.
- **Hoa:** standing rebuilt after the late-1970s exodus and the years after.
- **Veterans and families affected by dioxin:** worth after service and harm.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: good youth and child surveys; little adult self-esteem data; rich classical and literary sources.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Nôm for *Truyện Kiều* in the original; Hmong and Khmer for minority voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and depression; stereotypes of minorities; the 1979 exodus of the Hoa; war legacy and disability. Honors as law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the Young Lives Vietnam questionnaires; list every self-esteem, pride and shame item by round.
2. Search *Tạp chí Tâm lý học* for "lòng tự trọng" and "tự ti" + "học sinh".
3. Read the SAVY 2 report chapters on self-image and mental health.
4. Search a Vietnamese library catalog for "thể diện" + "văn hóa" and "sĩ diện" + "người Việt".
5. Read *Bài ca ngất ngưởng* beside Nguyễn Văn Huyên's pages on village rank.
