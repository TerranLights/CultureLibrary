# Vietnam: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta lineages:** ancestral halls and the communal house; where classical virtues are most explicitly kept.
- **Young urban adults (Hanoi, HCMC, Đà Nẵng):** post-1986 generations; test for a shift toward self-expression.
- **Huế Buddhists:** lay youth associations and pagoda networks as a second moral school.
- **Hòa Hảo (An Giang) and Cao Đài (Tây Ninh):** delta religions with their own ranked precepts.
- **Catholics (Bùi Chu, Phát Diệm, Đồng Nai's 1954 settlers):** church teaching beside or against state teaching.
- **Khmer Krom:** codes taught in their own script at the pagoda.
- **Cham Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani and Cham Muslims:** three religious authorities inside one people.
- **Mường:** oral chant as the carrier of order.
- **Hmong:** clan elders and Protestant pastors offering rival guides.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai:** matrilineal precedence and highland churches.
- **Hoa:** assembly halls and merchant ethics across Chợ Lớn and Hội An.
- **Party members and the non-religious:** where official and private rankings meet or part.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: two decades of surveys, a large normative literature, school texts; minority teachings mixed.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Nôm for *Gia huấn ca* and Trúc Lâm texts; Khmer; Mường.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official "traditional values" writing reads like findings; treat it as a source to test. Unrecognized religious groups; political values. Moral law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
