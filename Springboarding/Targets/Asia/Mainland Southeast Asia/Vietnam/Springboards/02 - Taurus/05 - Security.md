# Vietnam: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta smallholders:** tiny plots; kin and lineage as the first line against want.
- **North Central coast households:** storms and long migration; remittances from children as insurance.
- **Central coast fishers (Quảng Ngãi, Lý Sơn):** offshore danger and boats as the whole livelihood.
- **Mekong delta farmers:** floods, salinity and debt on inputs.
- **Migrant workers in HCMC and industrial zones:** no local registration, rented rooms, the 2021 lockdown.
- **State employees against informal workers:** a secure post against day work.
- **Hmong, Dao and Hà Nhì of the far north:** lean months and landslides.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** coffee and pepper price swings; land loss.
- **Khmer Krom:** the pagoda as a safety net in hard years.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận:** a dry province; drought and kin help along the female line.
- **Hoa:** clan and assembly-hall mutual aid.
- **Elderly without children nearby:** who steps in when the old-age saying fails.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social insurance, poverty and disasters; thin on fear of crime (no regular victim survey known) and on feelings of security.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for granary records; minority languages for upland coping.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1945 famine's causes; the 1980s shortages; national security as a topic (out of scope here); land loss. Social-insurance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
