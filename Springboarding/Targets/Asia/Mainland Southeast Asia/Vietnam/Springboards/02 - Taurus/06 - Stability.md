*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages:** the bamboo-hedged village and centuries-old lineages; the strongest case for staying put.
- **Hanoi Old Quarter families:** street trades and shop signs held across generations.
- **Northerners who moved south (1954, after 1975):** lineages split from their graves and halls.
- **New Economic Zone settlers in the highlands:** Kinh families planted in new places.
- **Mekong delta canal villages:** two to three centuries of settlement, mobile along waterways.
- **HCMC boarding-house migrants:** high turnover and the unsettled pole.
- **Thái chiefdoms:** chronicled continuity of the mường.
- **Hmong and Dao:** histories of moving fields, then fixed settlement.
- **Jarai, Bahnar and Ê Đê:** villages once moved on a farming cycle, now fixed or resettled.
- **Cham:** priests keeping the ritual year unchanged.
- **Khmer Krom:** the pagoda as the fixed point of a hamlet.
- **Boat-dwelling communities (Hạ Long, the Mekong):** water-based life moved ashore.
- **Hoa family firms in Chợ Lớn:** continuity broken and rebuilt.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in Hán-Nôm records, migration surveys and ethnography; thin on emotional stability and routine as people describe them.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán; Thái script; Akhar Thrah; Jarai; French for colonial ethnography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "ổn định chính trị" (political stability) is official discourse, not a topic for this sheet; post-1975 resettlement; highland relocations. Registration law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
