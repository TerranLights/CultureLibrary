## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanoi:** seasonal street foods, lotus-scented tea and the old connoisseur's pastimes.
- **Huế:** small, refined court dishes and royal music; a separate standard of taste.
- **HCMC:** night markets, café life and drinking sessions; Chợ Lớn's Hoa kitchens.
- **Mekong delta:** hammocks, river fruit and music circles; leisure built into the day.
- **Central coast (Quảng Nam, Đà Nẵng):** fish sauce, heat and noodle dishes of their own.
- **Thái:** circle dance, sticky rice and river bathing.
- **Red Dao:** herbal baths and the comfort of heat.
- **Hmong:** the Khâu Vai market (Hà Giang) and courtship songs; beware outsiders' romanticizing.
- **Ê Đê and Mnông:** jar wine drunk through tubes, gong music and shared feasts.
- **Cham:** temple music at Kate; the Bani Ramưwan observance as a season of restraint.
- **Khmer Krom:** festival foods and Khmer theater.
- **Buddhist vegetarians and Catholics in Lent:** pleasures given up, and when.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, music and literature; thin on touch, scent and bodily pleasure as people describe them.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Nôm and chữ Hán for classical texts; French for colonial Huế; Thái, Dao and Khmer for festival terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** censorship of sexual content; massage and karaoke venues tied to sex work; minority festivals staged for tourists; alcohol harm. Sex itself is Scorpio's.
- **Held for review:** none.
