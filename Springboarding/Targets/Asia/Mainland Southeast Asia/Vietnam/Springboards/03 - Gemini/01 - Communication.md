# Vietnam: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Ê Đê, Bahnar and Hoa Chinese for community sources; French for the colonial record. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and what good communication is taken to be and to be for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern, central and southern Kinh:** particles (ạ, dạ, nhé, nha) and address shift by region; test claims about directness rather than assume them.
- **Generations in the cities:** older address formulas against youth "teencode" and messaging styles.
- **Khmer Krom households:** speech switches between Khmer at home and Vietnamese in offices.
- **Cham communities:** who speaks Cham, Vietnamese or a religious language in which setting.
- **Hmong and Dao villages:** sung and instrumental speech beside everyday talk.
- **Highlands longhouse communities (Ê Đê, Jarai):** recitation as public talk; elders' speaking order.
- **Hoa families:** Cantonese, Teochew and Vietnamese by setting and age.
- **Deaf people and interpreters:** communication rights in schools and courts (law aspects: Libra).
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese families, where children broker between languages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh pragmatics and literature; mixed for minority speech; thin for measured conversation (turn length, pauses).
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for pre-modern texts; French; Khmer, Hmong, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** freedom of expression and online speech are sensitive and partly a legal matter (letter of the law: Libra; legal culture: Capricorn). Some recitations and funeral music belong to ritual specialists; approach through the communities.
- **Held for review:** none.
