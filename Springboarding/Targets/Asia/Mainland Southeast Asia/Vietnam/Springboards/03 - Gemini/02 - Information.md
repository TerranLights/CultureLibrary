# Vietnam: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese for court records; French for colonial archives; Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar and Hmong for community media. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City readers:** different newspapers, different online habits; compare where each turns first.
- **Rural communes:** loudspeakers, notice boards and commune meetings as main channels.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda announcements, Khmer broadcasts and cross-border media.
- **Cham communities:** manuscript keepers and religious leaders as sources of knowledge.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** village elders as news carriers; minority-language print and radio.
- **Northern upland markets (Hmong, Dao, Tày):** market day as the week's news exchange.
- **Hoa:** Chinese-language press and association bulletins.
- **Age groups:** television against social media and video platforms.
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese media, which often frame home news differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official records and press history; mixed for audience trust; thin for informal rumor networks.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese; French; Khmer, Cham, Hmong and Highlands languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** press and online speech are tightly regulated, and writing on them is sensitive (statutes: Libra; legal culture: Capricorn). Outside indices are not neutral; read them beside Vietnamese sources. Cham and upland ritual knowledge may be restricted to specialists.
- **Held for review:** none.
