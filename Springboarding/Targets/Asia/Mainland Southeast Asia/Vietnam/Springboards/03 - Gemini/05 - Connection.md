# Vietnam: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for lineage and village records; Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê and Mnông for kin networks; Hoa Chinese varieties for congregations. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta lineage villages:** how far registers and village alliances carry ties beyond the household.
- **Mekong delta settler communities:** compare whether more recent settlement means looser lineage reach and more weight on neighbors and patrons.
- **Rural-to-urban migrants:** province-of-origin networks as a city lifeline.
- **Hmong clans:** ties that cross provinces and the border with China and Laos.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai longhouses:** compare networks reckoned through the mother's line.
- **Cham clans:** ties across the Ahier and Awal/Bani line within shared clans.
- **Khmer Krom pagoda communities:** the temple as the hub of village ties.
- **Hoa congregations:** dialect and origin as the basis of ties.
- **Older people living alone:** the isolated, a group surveys can find.
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese families linked by remittances and visits home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for village ethnography and remittances; mixed for networks in cities; thin for loneliness and for minority kin networks outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm; French for Condominas and colonial ethnography; Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** connections for jobs and permits touch on corruption (legal culture: Capricorn); Hmong cross-border ties and Central Highlands networks are politically sensitive; gift ledgers are private family papers.
- **Held for review:** none.
