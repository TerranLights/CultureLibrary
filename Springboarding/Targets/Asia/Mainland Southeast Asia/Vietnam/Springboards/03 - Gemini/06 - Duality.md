# Vietnam: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for myth collections and poetry; French for Jarai and Cham ethnography; Khmer, Cham, Jarai; Hoa Chinese varieties. **Concept in one line:** how people live with two-ness: two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; the world divided into pairs; and how those who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former North and former South:** different twentieth-century histories under one state; compare how people narrate two pasts.
- **Hanoi and Saigon:** the paired-city rivalry in jokes, food and media.
- **Hoa and Minh Hương:** two ways of being Chinese-derived in Vietnam.
- **Khmer Krom:** Khmer identity and Vietnamese citizenship together.
- **Cham Ahier and Awal/Bani:** a people that names itself in two halves.
- **Jarai and neighboring Highlands peoples:** ritual pairs and dual offices.
- **Amerasians and other mixed families:** two parentages in daily life.
- **Urban youth:** two languages in one sentence.
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese, whose two-country lives mirror these debates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for myth and theory; mixed for lived biculturalism; thin for any measure of tolerance of contradiction in Vietnam.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm; French; Khmer, Cham, Jarai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the North–South division and the war's sides remain sensitive; Amerasians and Hoa identity carry painful histories; Jarai ritual offices and Cham clerical knowledge are not open to all.
- **Held for review:** none.
