# Vietnam: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Nôm for verse tales; Tày, Nùng, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê and Jarai for ritual and village go-betweens. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages:** matchmakers, lineage heads and ward teams as layered go-betweens.
- **Southern Kinh communities:** compare neighbors, patrons and temple committees as peacemakers.
- **Đạo Mẫu devotees:** mediums as spiritual brokers; compare northern temple centers with southern ones.
- **Tày, Nùng and Thái:** then masters as intermediaries with the sky realm.
- **Mường:** chanters as guides between the living and the dead.
- **Hmong:** marriage between clans negotiated through go-betweens.
- **Central Highlands (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Xơ Đăng):** elders between village and state.
- **Khmer Krom and Cham:** temple and clerical figures as settlers of disputes.
- **Catholic parishes:** priests and intercession.
- **Industrial-zone workers:** go-betweens between workers and employers.
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese associations as go-betweens with host institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual go-betweens and matchmaking customs; mixed for village mediation in practice; thin for brokers and for labor mediation outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Nôm; Tày, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mediation rules and customary dispute settlement are law (letter: Libra; legal culture: Capricorn); religious mediumship and spirit writing have been restricted at times; Highlands elders' roles are politically sensitive. Some ritual texts and chants are held by specialists.
- **Held for review:** none.
