# Vietnam: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (including southern slang and online usage); Hán-Nôm for anecdote collections; Khmer, Hmong, Cham and Hoa Chinese varieties for community talk. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed on) is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern villages:** compare the village gate, well and communal house as places where reputations are made.
- **Market women in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi:** talk as a trading tool and a source of labels.
- **Apartment blocks and alleys (hẻm, ngõ):** neighbor talk in dense city housing.
- **Students and young adults online:** call-outs and confession pages.
- **Hmong and Dao villages:** small communities where talk bears on marriage.
- **Khmer Krom pagoda communities:** what monks and elders discourage, and what spreads anyway.
- **Cham Ahier, Awal/Bani and Islamic villages:** compare talk across religious lines in neighboring villages.
- **Hoa congregations:** reputation inside a minority with its own press.
- **Officials and public figures:** where talk about them stops (sensitive).
- *Comparison only:* overseas Vietnamese communities, where rumor about home circulates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, sayings and online media; mixed for ethnography; thin for any measure of everyday gossip.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, including slang and teencode; Hán-Nôm; Khmer, Hmong, Cham, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rumor about officials and politics can bring penalties (statute: Libra; legal culture: Capricorn); call-out pages name private people, so cite with care; gendered labels for gossips must be treated as claims to test.
- **Held for review:** none.
