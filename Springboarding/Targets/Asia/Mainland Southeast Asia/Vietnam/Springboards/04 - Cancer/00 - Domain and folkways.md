# Vietnam: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Nôm for pre-modern texts), French and English for scholarship, and minority languages (Tày-Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Cham, Khmer, Cantonese). **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what is home and who is family, across household forms, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh** (the lineage-and-village model most written about); **Thanh-Nghệ-Tĩnh** (own kin terms and house forms); **Huế and the central coast** (garden houses, court rites); **Mekong Delta and Saigon** (newer settlement, fewer lineage halls).
- **Mường**, whose house and kin forms are often set beside the Kinh.
- **Tày, Nùng and Black and White Thái**: stilt houses, patrilineal clans, own scripts.
- **Hmong and Dao**: clan-based households in the far north.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai, Raglai, Chu Ru**: matrilineal descent, residence with the wife's kin.
- **Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Mnong, Cơ Ho**: communal house beside family dwellings.
- **Cham Ahier (Balamon), Awal (Bani) and Muslim Cham of An Giang**: kin rules differ by community.
- **Khmer Krom** (hamlets grouped around the pagoda); **Hoa** of Chợ Lớn, split by speech group; **Catholic parishes** (church rites at birth, marriage and death); **Cao Đài and Hòa Hảo** households (home altars of their own form); **urban migrant renters** in rented rooms (nhà trọ), far from the home village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Kinh; mixed for larger minorities; thin for small northern groups (Hà Nhì, Lô Lô, Si La), where state ethnology dominates.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; French (colonial ethnography); English; Hán-Nôm; minority languages for the peoples' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Central Highlands land, religion and resettlement are politically sensitive; the 54-group list is a state classification; some genealogies stay within the lineage. Marriage and family law go to Libra and Capricorn. Vietnam's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; this sheet is written for its core lands.
- **Held for review:** none.
