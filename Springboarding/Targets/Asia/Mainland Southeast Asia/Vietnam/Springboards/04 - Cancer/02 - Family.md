# Vietnam: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Ê Đê, Jarai, Hmong, Tày, Khmer, Cham and Chinese varieties for minority kin systems; French and English for scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern lineage villages**, where halls and printed genealogies have been rebuilt since the 1990s.
- **Mekong Delta Kinh**, settled later than the north, so lineage organization there needs its own study.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai, Raglai, Chu Ru**: kin and clan run through the mother's line.
- **Cham Ahier and Awal**, whose matrilineal customs differ from those of Muslim Cham in An Giang.
- **Hmong and Dao**: exogamous patrilineal clans with ritual duties among clan brothers.
- **Tày, Nùng, Thái and Mường**: patrilineal kin with in-marrying sons-in-law in some cases.
- **Bahnar and Xơ Đăng**: kin reckoned through both sides.
- **Khmer Krom**: kin ties built around the pagoda.
- **Hoa**: surname and native-place ties beyond the household.
- **Catholics**: godparents as an added layer of kin.
- **Families split by 1975 and the boat exodus**: kin on both sides of the ocean (diaspora for comparison only).
- **Urban same-sex couples and chosen families**, a separate case for who may be counted as kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Kinh (demography, literature, family studies); mixed for Highland matrilineal peoples (much of it French, older); thin for small northern groups.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; French; English; Hán-Nôm for old genealogies; Thái script, Ê Đê and Jarai for epics and oral sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families split by war and 1975 (re-education camps, exile) remain charged; some lineage genealogies are private; same-sex families are a sensitive topic. Inheritance belongs to Scorpio, siblings to Gemini, marriage and family law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Luong (1990) on kin terms, then search Vietnamese linguistics journals for "từ xưng hô thân tộc" (kin address terms).
2. Search the VNAS reports for "sống cùng con" (living with children).
3. Search library catalogs for "Jörai" or "Jarai" with "parenté" or "matrilinéaire".
4. Search Vietnamese newspapers for "ban liên lạc dòng họ" (lineage councils) and "xây nhà thờ họ".
5. Search the Facebook and Zalo group directories for "mẹ chồng nàng dâu" and note the largest groups.
