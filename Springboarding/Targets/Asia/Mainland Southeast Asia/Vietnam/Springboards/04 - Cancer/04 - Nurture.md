# Vietnam: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for old primers; Hmong, Jarai, Khmer and Cham for mother-tongue schooling; English for development studies. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern rural families**, the setting of Rydstrøm's study, as a baseline to test elsewhere.
- **Urban middle-class parents in Hanoi and Saigon**, to see how imported parenting styles are taken up or refused.
- **Grandparents raising children left behind** in the Red River and Mekong deltas while parents work in cities.
- **Hmong and Dao families in Lào Cai and Hà Giang**, for children's farm and household work in upland settings.
- **Jarai and Ê Đê households**, to see what part the mother's kin play in raising a child.
- **Khmer Krom boys schooled at the pagoda**, for the temple as a second place of upbringing.
- **Cham families**, teaching a script of their own beside Vietnamese.
- **Hoa families**, with Chinese-language classes after school.
- **Catholic parishes**, where catechism shapes the young.
- **Children in social protection centers and orphanages**, nurtured by institutions instead of kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on nutrition and schooling; mixed on daily upbringing, which rests on a few ethnographies; thin for minorities in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for old primers; English; Hmong, Jarai, Khmer and Cham for mother-tongue materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical punishment of children is debated; school political education is a state matter; minority-language schooling touches national policy. Learning belongs to Gemini, play to Leo, discipline to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
