# Vietnam: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm (classical Chinese) for dynastic relief records; Khmer and Highland languages for community refuges; English and French for disaster and colonial records. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central coast fishing communes**, for shelter against typhoons and floods.
- **Mekong households on canals**, for shelter on water and in the flood season.
- **Upland Hmong, Dao and Thái villages**, where houses given under state programs meet local building ways.
- **Bahnar and Xơ Đăng villages**, for the communal house as shelter for guests.
- **Khmer Krom hamlets**, for the pagoda as a refuge in hard times.
- **Street children and elderly street people in Saigon and Hanoi**, the people most visible without a roof in the cities.
- **Women and children fleeing violence**, for shelter from one's own household.
- **Migrant factory workers in Bình Dương and Bắc Ninh**, for whom the rented room is the only roof.
- **Former war zones (Quảng Trị, Củ Chi)**, where families lived in shelters for years.
- **Catholic resettlers of 1954**, for the move from camp to village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; state housing campaigns and war shelters are well reported; homelessness counts are thin and figures on round-ups are hard to find.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm or its translations; English and French; Highland languages and Khmer for community refuges.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the handling of street people and of shelters for trafficking survivors is sensitive; boat people and refugee camps after 1975 remain charged. Asylum and housing law belong to Libra; homelessness as a human failure to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** none.
