# Vietnam: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for pre-modern medical and moral texts; Hmong, Khmer and Jarai for healers; English and Japanese for migrant care work. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural households in the north and center**, to see how an elder's care is split among sons, daughters and daughters-in-law.
- **Urban middle-class families who hire help**, for the shift from kin care to paid care.
- **Families in the Mekong Delta with migrant children**, for care given at a distance.
- **Hmong households in the far north**, for care of the sick through clan and healer.
- **Jarai and Ê Đê households**, for who looks after the old when descent runs through women.
- **Khmer Krom families**, for the pagoda and its monks as carers.
- **Cham communities**, for how care of elders and the sick differs between Ahier and Awal villages.
- **Families of people disabled by Agent Orange** in Quảng Trị, Đồng Nai and elsewhere, for care that lasts a whole life.
- **Catholic parishes and nuns' orders**, for church-run homes as an alternative to kin care.
- **Domestic workers from Thanh Hóa and Nam Định in Hanoi**, carers far from their own families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; filial piety and elder-care policy are well studied for the Kinh; unpaid care time and minority care are thinly measured.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán-Nôm for old medical and moral texts; English; Japanese for programs abroad; Hmong, Khmer and Jarai for healers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Agent Orange is a charged political subject; some healing rites are restricted to their practitioners; elder neglect is a private shame and hard to study. Health as a domain belongs to Virgo, compassion to Pisces, duty to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** canonical Buddhist scriptures of South Asian origin on repaying parents (category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Read Shohet (2021), then search Vietnamese journals for "chăm sóc người cao tuổi" with "gia đình".
2. Search the HelpAge site for "Intergenerational Self-Help Club Vietnam" evaluations.
3. Search VnExpress and Tuổi Trẻ for "nuôi bệnh" and "cơm từ thiện bệnh viện".
4. Search Hán-Nôm catalogs and translations for Lê Hữu Trác's "Y huấn cách ngôn" (his rules for physicians).
5. Search the press for "điều dưỡng sang Nhật" (caregivers going to Japan).
