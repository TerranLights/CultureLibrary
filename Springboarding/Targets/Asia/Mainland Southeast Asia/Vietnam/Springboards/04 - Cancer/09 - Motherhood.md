# Vietnam: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with regional words for "mother"); Cham, Khmer, Hmong, Ê Đê and Tà Ôi for minority mothering; English for demographic and medical anthropology. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a public figure (mother of the nation, holy mothers, the land as mother).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Kinh villagers**, for the Mother Goddess cult as lived devotion.
- **Mekong Delta pilgrims to Bà Chúa Xứ**, for a mother figure tied to trade and luck.
- **Cham Ahier**, for the mother of the land and descent through mothers.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai women**, for mothering inside matrilineal longhouses.
- **Tà Ôi and other Trường Sơn peoples**, for mothers carrying children while working.
- **Hmong women**, for mothering among the husband's clan.
- **Khmer Krom mothers**, for postpartum customs, compared with those across the Cambodian border.
- **Mothers of the war dead**, for the line between mothers the state honored and those it did not.
- **Women from Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh working abroad**, mothering from a distance.
- **Single mothers in towns and cities**, for the newer debates on raising a child alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; motherhood is heavily written about in literature, religion, demography and state honors; thinner for minority mothers in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; English; French for older ethnography; Cham, Khmer, Hmong and Ê Đê for minority accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Heroic Mother honor is politically framed, and who falls inside or outside it is itself a question to research; sex-selective abortion and single motherhood are sensitive; some spirit-medium rites are guarded by their practitioners. Fatherhood is covered under the domain sheet; birth and sexuality belong to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** the scriptural story behind the Vu Lan festival, of South Asian origin (category only).

## 5. First queries
1. Read Ngô Đức Thịnh (1996), then search the UNESCO file on the Mother Goddesses for its community lists.
2. Search the statistics office for the latest population change survey tables on "tỷ số giới tính khi sinh".
3. Search library catalogs for "postpartum" with "Vietnam" and "Khmer" or "Hmong".
4. Search the press for "Bà mẹ Việt Nam anh hùng" with province names to see how mothers are profiled.
5. Search Facebook for "mẹ bỉm sữa" with "ở cữ" and note the most argued confinement rules.
