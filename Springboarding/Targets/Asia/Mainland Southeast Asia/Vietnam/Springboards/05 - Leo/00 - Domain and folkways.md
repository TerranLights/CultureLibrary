# Vietnam: The domain: creativity and self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt with chữ Hán; chữ Nôm for pre-modern verse); Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Tày-Nùng, Thái, Mường, Dao, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar; Cantonese and Teochew (Hoa); French for colonial sources. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: how people shine, play and take pleasure (creativity, play, pleasure, romance, children), with sport ways as its folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh villages:** the densest communal-house festival calendar and quan họ singing.
- **Hà Nội and Hồ Chí Minh City:** two leisure economies with different nightlife histories.
- **Huế:** court music and royal pastimes as a separate register.
- **Xứ Nghệ and the central coast:** ví giặm, bài chòi fairs, martial-arts contests.
- **Mekong Delta Kinh:** đờn ca tài tử and cải lương as everyday amusement.
- **Khmer Krom:** boat races and pagoda-centered festivity.
- **Cham (Ahier, Awal, and Cham Muslims of An Giang):** separate festival calendars.
- **Central Highlands peoples (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, M'nông, Xơ Đăng, Cơ Ho):** gong feasts, epic recitation.
- **Northern uplands (Thái, Hmong, Tày-Nùng, Mường, Dao, Hà Nhì):** New Year games, courtship gatherings.
- **Hoa:** lion dance, clan-hall festivals.
- **Religious communities and generations:** Catholics, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo, Đạo Mẫu; pre-1975 South, war generation, Gen Z.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh festivals and urban leisure; mixed for minorities (mostly Vietnamese-language state ethnography); thin on private pleasure outside press and fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with Hán-Nôm, French, and the minority languages above.
- **Territory:** Vietnam's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign's sheets are written for its core lands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** performance licensing and censorship; past restrictions on pre-1975 southern music; animal contests; unlicensed gambling is illegal; Central Highlands and Hmong Protestant communities; Cham and Khmer Krom politics. Legal questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** one pointer, as a category: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (scriptural sources behind some Cham and Khmer Krom performance).
