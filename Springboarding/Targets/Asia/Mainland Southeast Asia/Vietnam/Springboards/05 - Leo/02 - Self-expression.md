# Vietnam: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt; Nôm for older verse), Hmong, Cham, Khmer, Tày; French and English for scholarship. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern delta Kinh women:** the "thân em" folk-verse tradition as a first-person outlet.
- **Đạo Mẫu mediums and their followers:** possession rituals as licensed display of other identities.
- **Hà Nội and Saigon youth:** two street-style scenes and two slangs of self-presentation.
- **Families of the former South:** a memory of songs and speech that were once restricted.
- **Catholic parishes:** confession and testimony as structured self-disclosure.
- **Hmong:** grievance and longing carried in sung poetry rather than talk.
- **Cham intellectuals:** writing to keep a Cham voice inside Vietnamese letters.
- **Khmer Krom:** expression through Khmer-language theater and pagoda performance.
- **Tày-Nùng Then singers:** a ritual art now also performed on stage.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** gong dances in tourist shows set against village rites.
- **LGBTQ people and lô tô troupes:** visibility in cities against silence at home.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** variety shows and blogs made beyond home-country licensing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, ritual performance and youth media; mixed for survey data on emotion; thin for minorities' everyday self-presentation.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (address-term pragmatics matter), Hmong, Cham, Khmer, Tày.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political speech online and in print is closely watched (prosecutions of bloggers are on record *check*); the legal side goes to Libra and Capricorn. Coming out and gender expression may expose informants; religious expression among Central Highlands and Hmong Protestants is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** none.
