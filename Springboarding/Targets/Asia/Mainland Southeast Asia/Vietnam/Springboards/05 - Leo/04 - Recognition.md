# Vietnam: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt and chữ Hán for steles and royal decrees), Dao, Khmer, Hmong, Cham; Chinese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta lineages:** laureate ancestors kept alive in genealogies and on altars.
- **Revolutionary families in the North and Center:** standing drawn from resistance titles and medals.
- **Families of the former southern regime:** largely absent from state honors.
- **Hà Nội academics:** the professor and doctorate titles as status.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City business circles:** rankings and "under 30" lists.
- **Minority tradition bearers (Then, gong, Mo):** state artisan titles set against standing within their own communities.
- **Dao:** ritual rank conferred by ordination.
- **Central Highlands villages (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar):** an elder's standing granted by the village, not by a certificate.
- **Khmer Krom:** monastic ranks and the lay achar as recognized roles.
- **Cham:** heritage nominations as a route to recognition of their festivals and crafts.
- **Hoa:** donor names inscribed in assembly halls and temples.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** their own community awards and halls of fame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors, examinations and school awards (lists are published); mixed for informal recognition at work and home; thin for minority communities' own honors.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese and Hán (steles, decrees), Dao, Khmer, Cham, Hmong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the war dead of the former southern army are outside martyr recognition; ethnic classification is disputed by some groups; land-reform victims; title applications can involve payment (allegations, *check*). Legal criteria for honors are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
