# Vietnam: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt and Nôm for older texts), Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Ê Đê, Jarai; English for NGO and scholarly reports. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households on the poor list:** the official label against the wish to stand on one's own.
- **Workers in the Bình Dương and Đồng Nai industrial zones:** treatment by managers as a source of strikes.
- **Patients in crowded provincial and central hospitals:** dignity of the body in care.
- **Elders:** family care at home against care homes, and how each is judged.
- **Families affected by dioxin:** living with disability under charity's gaze.
- **Mường and Hmong:** funeral recitations as the measure of a fitting death.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** outside labels of "backwardness" set against their own self-description.
- **People held in compulsory treatment centers:** the sharpest test of treatment by the state.
- **Catholics:** a church vocabulary of human dignity.
- **Khmer Krom and Cham:** dignity tied to keeping one's language and religion.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** refugee-camp memoirs on dignity lost and regained.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and press; mixed for surveys on respect; thin for minorities' own terms for dignity.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with Nôm; Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Central Highlands languages; English for NGO reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention centers and human rights are sensitive topics (Capricorn for the spirit, Libra for the letter, including the legal protection of "danh dự, nhân phẩm"); Hmong funeral chants are ritual knowledge to approach through practitioners.
- **Held for review:** none.
