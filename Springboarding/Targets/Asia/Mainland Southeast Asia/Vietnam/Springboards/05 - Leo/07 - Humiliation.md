# Vietnam: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt; Nôm for chèo and older verse), Khmer, Hmong, Jarai, Bahnar; Chinese (Hoa); French and English for history. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Schoolchildren:** corrected before the class or the flag assembly.
- **Party members and civil servants:** criticism sessions as a routine of the job.
- **Families classed as landlords in 1953–56:** denunciation remembered across generations.
- **Former southern officers and their families:** camps and the loss of standing after 1975.
- **Women in village lore and theater:** disgrace tied to chastity and gossip.
- **Urban borrowers:** debt exposure by loan sharks.
- **Celebrities and influencers:** online pile-ons and staged apologies.
- **Northern border provinces:** a war memory tied to national humiliation.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** slurs and labels from colonial and later times.
- **Upland pupils at lowland boarding schools:** teasing over accent and dress, to check.
- **Hoa families:** the late-1970s losses remembered as disgrace, to check.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** camp memoirs published abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, theater and press; mixed in surveys; thin and politically limited for land reform and the camps.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with Nôm; Khmer, Hmong, Central Highlands languages; Chinese; French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform, re-education camps, the 1979 war and the Hoa departures are sensitive; some key books are banned at home; survivors and their families need care. Punishment and its legal side go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
