# Vietnam: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt), Khmer, Cham (with Arabic religious terms among Cham Muslims), M'nông, Hmong; Cantonese and Teochew (Hoa); French for colonial ethnography. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern delta villages:** gift ledgers at weddings and funerals, and the duty to return in kind.
- **Saigon streets:** free water, food and rice machines set out for strangers.
- **Buddhist laity:** giving as merit, through pagodas.
- **Catholic parishes:** church-run aid (Caritas) set apart from state drives.
- **Hòa Hảo communities:** bridges and clinics built as religious duty.
- **M'nông and other highland peoples:** the feast-giver's standing.
- **Cham Muslims:** almsgiving rules from Islam.
- **Khmer Krom:** offerings that sustain the pagoda and its school.
- **Hoa:** clan-hall clinics and funeral help for members.
- **Celebrities and influencers:** relief drives that are also performances.
- **City migrants and home villages:** money sent back home, and the obligations it carries.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** remittances and relief drives from abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in press and in disaster-relief records; mixed in surveys; thin for minorities' own giving customs outside older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Khmer; Cham and Arabic terms; M'nông and other highland languages; Chinese dialects; French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independent and religious charity is regulated, and fundraising disputes have reached the courts (Libra for the letter); official campaigns against "wasteful" highland sacrifice feasts are reported (*check*); donor-list scrutiny touches privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
