# Vietnam: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt and chữ Hán for chronicles), Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Thái, Hmong, Mường, Khmer, Cham; Chinese (Hoa); French for colonial ethnography. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta Kinh villages:** lineage heads beside the Party secretary and commune chair.
- **Black and White Thái of the northwest:** memories of hereditary lords.
- **Hmong:** clan heads who settle disputes across villages.
- **Jarai and Bahnar:** ritual masters and elders who lead by knowledge.
- **Ê Đê:** matrilineal houses where women hold the house and men speak for the village, to check.
- **Mường:** the legacy of the lang noble lines.
- **Khmer Krom:** head monks and lay achar who lead pagoda communities.
- **Cham:** clergy councils as the face of each religious community.
- **Hoa:** the old dialect congregations and their heads.
- **Catholic parishes:** priests as community organizers in the delta and the Southeast.
- **Southern business founders:** self-made leadership in firms.
- **Women:** a long line of women war leaders in memory against present numbers in office.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** elected heads of community associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official leadership ideals and village studies; mixed for minority informal leaders; thin for leadership in firms beyond business press.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese and chữ Hán; Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Thái (and its old script), Hmong, Mường, Khmer, Cham; French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the cult of the national founder is not open to criticism at home; leaders among Central Highlands and Hmong Protestant groups and some Cham and Khmer Krom religious figures are watched. Formal authority and command are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Dournes's *Pötao* and list the Jarai terms for leading and following.
2. Search *Tạp chí Dân tộc học* for "già làng", "trưởng dòng họ" and "người có uy tín".
3. Pull PAPI provincial scores and their leadership-related items.
4. Search the national library for "Quắm tố mướn" editions.
5. Read *Sửa đổi lối làm việc* and the youth handbooks side by side for the traits they ask of leaders.
