*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern delta children:** chanted rhyme games kept in villages.
- **Central coast (Bình Định, Quảng Nam, Phú Yên):** bài chòi as adult public play.
- **Mekong fairs:** lô tô troupes and their audiences.
- **Red River puppet villages:** guilds that guarded their tricks.
- **Hmong, Tày and Thái youth:** ball games bound up with New Year courting.
- **Khmer Krom:** team racing at pagoda festivals.
- **City children in Hà Nội and HCMC:** little open space; paid play centers.
- **Teenagers and young men:** online games, cafés and esports.
- **Older men:** sidewalk chess and songbird clubs as daily play.
- **Hoa:** mahjong and lion-dance troupes.
- **Central Highlands children:** games recorded in village ethnographies, to check.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** Tết fairs run by student groups abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk games, festivals and heritage files; mixed for adult play and hobbies; thin for minority children's play.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with Nôm; Hmong, Tày, Thái, Khmer; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling outside licensed outlets is illegal, so Tết play and lô tô sit near a line; animal contests (cockfights, buffalo fights) draw welfare criticism; regulation of online games belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
