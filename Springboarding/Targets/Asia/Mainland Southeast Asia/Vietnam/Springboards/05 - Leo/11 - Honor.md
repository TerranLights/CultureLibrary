# Vietnam: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán-Việt with chữ Hán; chữ Nôm for verse novels), M'nông, Hmong, Khmer, Cham; Chinese (Hoa); French for colonial-era village studies. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta villages:** precedence at the communal house and the feasts that bought it.
- **Mekong Kinh:** a righteous-hero ideal fed by *Lục Vân Tiên* recitation.
- **Bình Định and the central coast:** martial schools and their oaths.
- **Northern families of the war dead:** honors certified by the state.
- **Families of former southern soldiers:** the dead long kept outside official honors.
- **Women:** marital and sexual honor, and the public fights it can start.
- **Kinh and Hoa merchants:** a trader's word as standing.
- **Hoa associations:** sworn loyalty among members.
- **M'nông, Ê Đê and other highland peoples:** warrior honor in epics.
- **Hmong:** a clan's name to keep.
- **Khmer Krom:** a son's time as a novice as a mark of family honor, to check.
- **Cham:** lineage honor in a matrilineal setting, to check.
- **Football fans:** match-fixing as national disgrace.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** veterans' groups abroad and their own memorials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classical literature, village ethnography and state commemoration; mixed for current family honor; thin for minorities' honor codes outside epics.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with Hán and Nôm; M'nông, Hmong, Khmer, Cham; Chinese; French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor of the former southern army's dead; violence defended as honor; women's sexual reputation. The legal protection of "danh dự" (defamation, insult) belongs to Libra; duty belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
