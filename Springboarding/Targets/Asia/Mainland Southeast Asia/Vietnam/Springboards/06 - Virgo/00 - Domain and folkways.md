# Vietnam: Domain and folkways, work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm for older material); French; Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Tày-Nùng, Thái, Mường, the Highlands languages; Cantonese and Teochew (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** the domain of work, service, the daily round and health: what counts as good work and a well-kept life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta Kinh villages:** dense rice work and the craft-village belt around Hanoi.
- **North-central coast (Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh, Thanh Hóa):** poor soils and a long habit of leaving to find work.
- **Huế and the central coast:** former court service; fishing and lagoon work.
- **Mekong delta:** a frontier of land clearing and river trade.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City and the industrial provinces (Bình Dương, Đồng Nai, Bắc Ninh):** factory shifts, internal migrants.
- **Central Highlands peoples (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Mnông, K'ho, Sedang):** swidden rounds beside coffee and rubber.
- **Northern upland peoples (Tày, Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Dao, Hà Nhì):** terraces, periodic markets, own seasonal calendars.
- **Mường:** near the Kinh in language, separate ritual specialists.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani, Muslim Cham of An Giang):** their own religious calendars of work and rest.
- **Khmer Krom:** a village round set by the pagoda.
- **Hoa:** trade guilds and Chợ Lớn shops.
- **Catholic parishes (Phát Diệm, Bùi Chu), Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo:** religious rules of work and rest.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Kinh and for labor statistics; mixed for the Khmer Krom and Hoa; thin and mostly outsider-written for many upland peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; French; classical Chinese and Nôm; Khmer; Cham (Akhar Thrah); Hmong; Tày-Nùng; Thái; the Highlands languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independent labor organizing, land disputes and Highlands religious minorities are politically sensitive; healing and ritual knowledge (Tày *then*, Mường *mo*, Cham priestly lineages) only on the practitioners' terms.
- **Scope:** Vietnam's exact lands are still to be settled by the author. This set is written for its core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. On the GSO site, search "Điều tra lao động việc làm" and "Khảo sát mức sống dân cư"; list waves with hours worked and health spending.
2. Pull the WVS Vietnam files and tabulate the "work is important" item by region and wave.
3. In the Hán-Nôm Institute catalog, search "lịch" (almanac) and "gia huấn" (household instruction).
4. Read Gourou and Condominas side by side for a delta-against-Highlands outline of the work round.
5. Search "hò giã gạo" and "ví phường vải" in the Vietnamese Institute for Musicology (Viện Âm nhạc *check*) collections to find recorded work songs.
