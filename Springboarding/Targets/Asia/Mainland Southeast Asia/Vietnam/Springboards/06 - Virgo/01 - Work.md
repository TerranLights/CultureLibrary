# Vietnam: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; French (colonial labor); classical Chinese and Nôm; Khmer; Hmong; Thái; Ê Đê and Jarai; Cantonese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta craft villages:** household workshops beside rice fields; whether craft counts as better work than farming.
- **Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** provinces sending many workers abroad; work as debt, remittance and absence.
- **Industrial zones of Bắc Ninh, Thái Nguyên, Bình Dương and Đồng Nai:** young migrant factory workers and the overtime question.
- **Hanoi and Hồ Chí Minh City:** office workers (*dân văn phòng*) against street vendors and motorbike drivers.
- **Mekong delta:** hired harvest crews, aquaculture and seasonal moves to the city.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai:** matrilineal households dividing farm tasks; wage work on coffee land.
- **Hmong and Dao of Hà Giang and Lào Cai:** terrace farming against new tourism jobs.
- **Thái of Sơn La and Điện Biên:** paddy work and home weaving.
- **Khmer Krom:** seasonal labor migration out of Sóc Trăng and Trà Vinh.
- **Cham:** weaving at Mỹ Nghiệp and pottery at Bàu Trúc (Ninh Thuận); trading by the Muslim Cham of An Giang.
- **Hoa:** family firms where kin are the workforce.
- **State employees (*công chức*) against the private sector:** security of tenure and its reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor statistics and Kinh villages; thinner for upland groups' own accounts of work.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, French, classical Chinese, Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Thái, Highlands languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wildcat strikes, independent labor groups, and labor in compulsory drug-rehabilitation centers are politically sensitive. Labor law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the *Báo Lao Động* archive for "đình công" (strike) and "tăng ca" by year and province.
2. Pull the 2019 census occupation tables, cross-tabulated by ethnic group.
3. Read Kerkvliet, then Lương, for the northern village sequence from collectives to markets.
4. Search the DOLAB site for "số lao động đi làm việc ở nước ngoài theo tỉnh" (workers abroad by province).
5. In Nguyễn Đổng Chi, index the tales whose plots turn on laziness or diligence.
