# Vietnam: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese; French; Thái; Ê Đê; Cham; Khmer; Cantonese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern rural Kinh families:** the son's place in keeping ancestral rites against daughters' household help.
- **Migrant-sending provinces (Thanh Hóa, Nghệ An, the Mekong delta):** grandparents as the household's child-minders.
- **Urban middle class in Hanoi and Hồ Chí Minh City:** children kept at study rather than chores.
- **Families affected by Agent Orange (Quảng Trị, Đồng Nai, Thái Bình):** disability, dependence and income.
- **Veterans and retired state staff:** finding a role after leaving work.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai:** contribution within matrilineal households.
- **Thái, Tày and Mường elders:** script and ritual knowledge as an asset.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận and Bình Thuận:** who keeps the Akhar Thrah script alive.
- **Khmer Krom:** elders who organize pagoda festivals.
- **Mekong delta trading families:** children working market stalls and boats.
- **Hoa:** retirees in clan and dialect associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on elders and children in surveys; thin on disabled people's own voices and on upland groups.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, classical Chinese, French, Thái, Ê Đê, Cham, Khmer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Agent Orange and disability stigma; suicide among older people; family conflict over dependence. Handle with care and with families' consent.
- **Held for review:** none.
