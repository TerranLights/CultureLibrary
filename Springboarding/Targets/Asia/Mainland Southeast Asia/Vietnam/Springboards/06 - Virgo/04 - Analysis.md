## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanoi and the northern delta:** the old examination tradition and the gifted schools.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City and the south:** commercial reckoning in trade and tech.
- **Provinces said to be a "land of learning" (*đất học*), such as Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** a reputation to test, not to assume.
- **Mekong delta farmers:** experience and trial beside extension-service advice.
- **Hmong and Dao terrace farmers:** reading weather and slope.
- **Ê Đê and Mnông:** choosing and rotating swidden plots.
- **Cham Ahier priests:** reckoning the ritual calendar.
- **Khmer Krom pupils:** learning mathematics in a second language.
- **Hoa merchant families:** bookkeeping and abacus training in family shops.
- **Lottery players and fortune-tellers' clients:** where numbers and omens meet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on schooling and contests; mixed on everyday reasoning; thin on minority knowledge systems in their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, classical Chinese, French, Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official statistics and critical policy analysis can be politically sensitive; Cham priestly calendrical knowledge is held by lineage.
- **Held for review:** none.
