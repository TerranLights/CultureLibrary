# Vietnam: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese (examination papers); Cham; Hmong; Thái and Mường; Khmer; Cantonese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Master artisans of the craft villages (Bát Tràng, Đồng Kỵ, Vạn Phúc):** exacting standards passed down in families.
- **Huế:** court-ritual exactness and the presentation of royal cuisine.
- **Gifted-school families in Hanoi, Hải Phòng and Hồ Chí Minh City:** exam pressure.
- **Mekong delta:** test the common claim of a looser southern style instead of assuming it.
- **Workers in Japanese and Korean factories:** foreign quality rules.
- **The beauty industry and pageant contestants:** standards of the body.
- **Cham potters of Bàu Trúc:** women's standards of form without the wheel.
- **Hmong weavers of Lùng Tám:** cloth and indigo standards.
- **Thái and Mường weavers:** brocade patterns that must be exact.
- **Khmer Krom boat-racing crews (*Ok Om Bok*, Sóc Trăng):** precision as a team.
- **Hoa restaurateurs of Chợ Lớn:** standards of a dish carried across generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on schooling and crafts in Vietnamese; thin on perfectionism as a measured trait.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, classical Chinese, Cham, Hmong, Thái, Khmer, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and cosmetic-surgery harm; criticism of *bệnh thành tích* can touch official performance figures.
- **Held for review:** none.
