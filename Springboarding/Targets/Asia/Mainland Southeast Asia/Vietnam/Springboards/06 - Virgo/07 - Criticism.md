# Vietnam: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Nôm (pre-modern satire); French (colonial press); Khmer; Tày-Nùng; Cantonese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State offices and Party cells:** criticism in set formats and at set times.
- **Private and foreign-invested firms:** feedback norms imported with management systems.
- **Patrilocal northern households:** a mother-in-law's correction of a new bride.
- **Pupils and teachers:** whether and how a student may question a teacher.
- **Young social-media users:** pile-ons and call-outs.
- **Northern and southern speech:** claims of directness or indirectness, to test.
- **Catholic parishes:** reproof from the pulpit and by parish councils.
- **Khmer Krom:** theater as a venue for mockery.
- **Tày and Nùng:** reproach and teasing carried in song.
- **Writers, artists and journalists:** professional critique under editorial limits.
- **Hoa family firms:** how the older generation corrects heirs in the business.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on literature, satire and official practice; mixed on everyday feedback; thin on minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Nôm, French, Khmer, Tày-Nùng, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political criticism and dissent are tightly limited; sources on bloggers and jailed critics need careful handling and must not expose individuals. Rules on speech go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
