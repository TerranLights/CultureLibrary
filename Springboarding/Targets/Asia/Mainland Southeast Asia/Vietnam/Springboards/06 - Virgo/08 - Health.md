# Vietnam: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese and Nôm (medical texts); French (colonial medicine); Tày-Nùng and Thái; Hmong and Dao; Khmer; Ê Đê and Jarai; Cantonese (Hoa). **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages:** commune health stations beside home herbal gardens.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City:** private hospitals and pharmacy self-medication.
- **Hanoi's older residents:** the morning-exercise culture at the lakes.
- **Mekong delta:** water-borne and mosquito-borne illness and river hygiene.
- **Tày, Nùng and Thái:** *then* and *mo* specialists beside the clinic.
- **Red Dao and Hmong of Lào Cai and Hà Giang:** herbal and shamanic healing.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Bahnar:** village healers and distance to hospitals.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda medicine and monks as healers.
- **Cham Ahier and Bani villages:** birth and postpartum customs.
- **Hoa:** Chinese-medicine practitioners and shops.
- **Factory workers in industrial zones:** occupational illness and episodes of mass fainting reported in the press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official surveys and traditional medicine; mixed on folk illness; thin on minority healing in its own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, classical Chinese, Nôm, French, Tày-Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Dao, Khmer, Highlands languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness, HIV and drug use are stigmatized; some healing rites are restricted to initiates; spirit healing can draw official disapproval. Health law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
