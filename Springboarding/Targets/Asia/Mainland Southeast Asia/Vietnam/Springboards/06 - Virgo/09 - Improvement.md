## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upland peoples in poverty programs (Hmong, Dao, Bru-Vân Kiều, Cơ Tu):** improvement done to them, and their own aims.
- **Kinh settlers of the New Economic Zones in the Central Highlands:** "developers" of land others already used.
- **Former Thủ Thiêm residents:** displaced by a renewal project.
- **Mekong delta farmers:** dikes, triple cropping and their later doubts.
- **Students and parents in the tutoring economy:** betterment bought by the hour.
- **Rural young people who leave for the cities:** migration as the route to a better life.
- **Highlands and Hmong Protestant converts:** conversion described by some as renewal (sensitive).
- **Cham teachers of Akhar Thrah:** reviving a script.
- **Khmer Krom monks and teachers:** language renewal through the pagoda.
- **Hoa entrepreneurs:** rebuilding family firms after Đổi Mới.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state programs and reform history; mixed on personal self-improvement; thin on how upland peoples judge imposed "development".
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, classical Chinese, French, Cham, Khmer, Hmong, Highlands languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resettlement, land seizures, religious conversion and the late-1970s measures against the Hoa are politically sensitive; "civilized" and "backward" labels need critical reading.
- **Held for review:** none.
