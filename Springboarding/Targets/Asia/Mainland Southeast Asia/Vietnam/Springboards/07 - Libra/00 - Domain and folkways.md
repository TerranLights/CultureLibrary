# Vietnam: Domain and folkways (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; older material in chữ Hán and chữ Nôm), French for colonial records, and minority languages (Tày, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Hoa). **Concept in one line:** partnership and justice: what a fair bargain and a good partnership are, with marriage and gender ways, contracts, rivals, settling quarrels and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages:** home of the hương ước, so village rules are best documented here.
- **Thanh Hóa, Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh:** lineage custom to test apart from the delta.
- **Huế and the old court region:** where the Nguyễn code and court rites touched daily life most.
- **Southeast and Mekong delta Kinh:** newer settlement, different kin terms and wedding forms.
- **Tày, Nùng and Thái uplands:** bride service and their own wedding rites.
- **Hmong and Dao:** clan rules on whom one may marry.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, M'nông:** recited customary law; several are matrilineal.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani, Muslim Cham of An Giang):** three communities with different marriage and inheritance rules.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda-centered community life and their own wedding order.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn and the delta:** clan and native-place associations as bonds between equals.
- **Catholic communities (Phát Diệm, Bùi Chu, Hố Nai):** church marriage beside the civil one.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only; the nation's findings apply to them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh marriage and village custom; mixed for upland peoples; thin for small northern groups.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; these Libra sheets are written for Vietnam's core lands. Provincial series break at the 2025 merger of provinces *check*.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm before 1900; French for colonial records; minority languages for customary law.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes, religious groups and political trials are sensitive; violence in marriage needs care; some upland ritual knowledge belongs to its keepers.
- **Held for review:** liturgical and scriptural texts of South Asian origin kept by Khmer Krom pagodas and Ahier/Balamon priests (a category, set aside under the standing rule).
