# Vietnam: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with Hán-Việt and native words side by side), plus Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai, Hmong and Hoa (Cantonese, Teochew) for local terms. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former members of northern cooperatives:** memories of work points as a test case for equal against earned shares.
- **Mekong delta smallholders:** how land was redivided after decollectivization, and whose claims were heard.
- **Ethnic-minority and remote-district students:** recipients of priority points, against urban students who resent them.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai matrilineal families:** inheritance through daughters as a different baseline for sibling fairness, to test.
- **Kinh patrilineal families of the north:** the eldest son's share for ancestral worship set against equal division.
- **Market traders of Đồng Xuân and Bến Thành:** fair price and fair weight as daily practice.
- **Hoa merchants of Chợ Lớn:** fixed against haggled prices and fair dealing among partners.
- **Khmer Krom farmers of Sóc Trăng and Trà Vinh:** land shares after reform compared with Kinh neighbors.
- **Factory workers from the north and center in southern industrial zones:** pay fairness felt by migrants.
- **Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only:** remittances shared among siblings at home as a test of fair division; the nation's findings cover them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams, inheritance law and pay; mixed on everyday turn-taking and queuing; thin on experimental fairness games in Vietnam (search before assuming any exist).
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hoa varieties for Chợ Lớn trade; Khmer, Ê Đê and Jarai for land and inheritance custom.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and land allocation are sensitive topics; priority points for minorities are a live dispute, to be described from every side.
- **Held for review:** none.
