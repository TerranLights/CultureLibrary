# Vietnam: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Literary Chinese for envoy records and court letters; French and English for twentieth-century negotiations; Jarai, Cham, Hmong and Khmer for go-betweens and older relations between peoples. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanoi civil servants and Party cadres:** official tact and the colloquial "ngoại giao" of gifts and visits.
- **Ho Chi Minh City business people:** dealing with partners and foreign firms, to test against Hanoi.
- **Huế and the central coast:** old court manners and formal speech, a candidate unit for politeness.
- **Border provinces facing China (Lạng Sơn, Cao Bằng, Quảng Ninh):** daily cross-border trade as small-scale diplomacy, with Tày and Nùng traders.
- **Hmong of the high north:** go-betweens in marriage and clan disputes.
- **Jarai and Bahnar of the Central Highlands:** older relations with lowland courts through ritual figures and gifts.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận and Bình Thuận:** their kingdom's dealings with Đại Việt, remembered in their own manuscripts.
- **Khmer Krom:** relations with both the Vietnamese state and Cambodia, carried by pagoda leaders.
- **Hoa of Hà Tiên and Chợ Lớn:** a history of brokering between Siam, Cambodia and the Nguyễn.
- **Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only:** consular relations with a divided diaspora as a case of the state's diplomacy; the nation's findings cover them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and dynastic diplomacy; mixed for everyday tact; thin for upland and Cham diplomacy outside French and Cham sources.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Literary Chinese, French, English; Cham (Akhar Thrah manuscripts), Jarai and Hmong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relations with China and the sea disputes are tightly managed in public speech; diaspora relations are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library of Vietnam catalog for "Quân trung từ mệnh tập" and pick a modern annotated edition.
2. Search the Communist Party's online newspaper for "ngoại giao cây tre" and collect the 2021 speech text.
3. Read Kelley's chapters on how envoys described their missions.
4. Search Vietnamese and Cham sources for "Huyền Trân Chế Mân" and set the two accounts side by side.
5. Search VOZ for "ngoại giao công sở" and sample threads from several years.
