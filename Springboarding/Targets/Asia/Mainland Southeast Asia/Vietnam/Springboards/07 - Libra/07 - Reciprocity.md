# Vietnam: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (northern, central and southern names for the same customs differ), Khmer, Hmong, Tày-Thái languages and Chinese varieties for local exchange customs. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages:** dense ceremonial gift ledgers and feast obligations.
- **Southern Kinh traders and households:** hụi as a daily tool, and debts owed to temple goddesses.
- **Kinh Catholic parishes:** church-run mutual aid and what members owe the parish.
- **Families of war dead and veterans:** a state-run account of what the nation owes in return.
- **Tày, Thái and Mường farmers:** labor exchange at transplanting and house-building.
- **Hmong households:** exchange within clans, and between converts and non-converts.
- **Khmer Krom villages:** labor exchange and gifts to the pagoda as obligation and merit.
- **Cham Ahier/Balamon and Awal/Bani:** offerings to ancestors at Kate and Ramưwan and what is asked in return.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** red-envelope customs and mutual-aid associations.
- **Migrant workers in the southern industrial zones:** money sent home as the return owed to parents.
- **Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only:** gift-bearing visits home, checked against household duties in the nation; the nation's findings cover them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gifts, offerings and remittances; mixed for labor exchange (older ethnography); thin for experimental measures of reciprocity in Vietnam.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (regional terms), Khmer, Hmong, Tày-Thái, Cham, Cantonese and Teochew.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gifts to officials touch on corruption; hụi collapses end in criminal cases; family debts can be private and shaming.
- **Held for review:** none.
