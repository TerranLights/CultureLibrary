# Vietnam: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese, chữ Hán for classical tales, and upland languages for accusations of harmful spirits. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Thái, Tày and Dao villages of the northwest:** spirit accusations and who becomes their target.
- **Hmong converts and non-converts:** blame for misfortune running both ways across a religious line.
- **Central coast and Central Highlands communities:** ma lai lore and the neighbors it names.
- **Kinh beliefs about Khmer and Cham charms (bùa ngải):** a lowland image of minority neighbors' power, recorded as belief to test.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn and the north:** memory of being blamed in 1978-1979.
- **Families from the former South:** post-1975 labels and the blame they carried.
- **Northern and southern Kinh:** regional jokes and slurs that each side directs at the other.
- **Migrants from poorer provinces in the big cities:** whether city talk blames them for disorder, to test.
- **Families with disabled children in sprayed areas:** sin, poison or neighbors as explanations.
- **Overseas Vietnamese, for comparison only:** mutual enemy images between the diaspora and the state; the nation's findings cover them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in idiom, literature and official speech; mixed for spirit accusations; thin for psychological research on projection.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; chữ Hán for classical tales; Thái, Tày, Dao, Hmong, Jarai and Bahnar for accusation terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform, the 1978-1979 Hoa exodus and post-1975 labels are sensitive; people accused of being ma gà are vulnerable, so never repeat their names.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search VnExpress and Tuổi Trẻ for "bị nghi là ma gà" and "đuổi khỏi bản".
2. Read "Chuyện người con gái Nam Xương" in a modern edition, with the school commentary.
3. Search the *Journal of Vietnamese Studies* site for "land reform" and "Nguyễn Thị Năm".
4. Search the Communist Party's online newspaper for "thế lực thù địch" and count uses by year.
5. Look up "suy bụng ta ra bụng người" and related entries in Nguyễn Lân's dictionary.
