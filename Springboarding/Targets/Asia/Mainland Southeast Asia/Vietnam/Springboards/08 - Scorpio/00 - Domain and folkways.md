## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh:** reburial (cải táng) and lineage halls (nhà thờ họ); how bones tie death to inheritance.
- **Central Kinh (Huế, Quảng Nam, Quảng Ngãi):** court ritual and heavy war loss in one band.
- **Mekong Delta and Hồ Chí Minh City Kinh:** a frontier society; whether pooling and inheritance customs differ from the north's.
- **Central Highlands peoples (Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Mnông, Cơ Ho):** descent lines (matrilineal among Jarai and Ê Đê, as they describe it) decide who inherits and who mourns.
- **Northern upland peoples (Tày, Nùng, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Dao):** each has its own funeral specialist and chant.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani, Cham Muslims of An Giang):** three communities in one people, each handling the body its own way.
- **Khmer Krom (Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh):** pagoda-centered funerals; a separate calendar of the dead.
- **Hoa (Chợ Lớn, Hội An):** congregations (bang, hội quán) that ran cemeteries and mutual aid.
- **Catholics, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo, upland Protestants:** each reworked ancestor rites.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** what diaspora practice kept or changed.
