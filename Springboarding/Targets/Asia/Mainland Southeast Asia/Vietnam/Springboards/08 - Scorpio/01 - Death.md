# Vietnam: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese, with chữ Nôm for the classical texts; Hmong, Khmer, Cham. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead and what death means, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families of northern and southern war dead:** martyr status and state cemeteries against unmarked graves; grief under unequal recognition.
- **Massacre villages of Quảng Nam and Quảng Ngãi:** collective death days and shrines to the unclaimed.
- **Urban middle class in Hà Nội and Hồ Chí Minh City:** hospital deaths and crematoria against home deaths in the countryside.
- **Cham Ahier and Bani:** cremation and burial in one people; how each names a finished death.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda cremation and ashes kept in pagoda stupas, a different place for the dead.
- **Hmong, Dao and other northern upland peoples:** a dead soul guided home by chant; whether conversion to Protestantism changes that.
- **Jarai, Bahnar, Ê Đê and neighbors:** mourning that ends at the grave-abandonment feast, after which the dead are let go.
- **Catholic communities:** prayers for the dead and All Souls beside the death anniversary.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** remains sent home or kept abroad, and the dead of the sea crossing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war dead, ancestors and classical texts; mixed for palliative care and diagnosis disclosure; thin and under-counted for suicide.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with chữ Nôm for Nguyễn Du and Nguyễn Dữ; Cham, Khmer and Hmong for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the former Republic's dead; the boat exodus; massacres; Cham and Hmong funeral knowledge, shared as keepers allow; suicide. End-of-life law: Libra.
