# Vietnam: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (religious vocabulary in chữ Hán); Hmong, Dao, Khmer, Cham. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending (conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Kinh Buddhists and the Trúc Lâm revival:** renunciation as a model of starting over.
- **Mekong Delta lay movements (Hòa Hảo; other southern sects):** renewal tied to the end of an age, not to one life.
- **Cao Đài followers (Tây Ninh and beyond):** spiritual progress across lives as a ladder.
- **Catholic communities (Phát Diệm, Bùi Chu, Hố Nai):** resurrection and adult baptism; the convert's place in a Kinh family.
- **Hmong converts in Hà Giang, Lào Cai, Điện Biên:** conversion as a break with funeral and soul rites; families split by it.
- **Central Highlands Protestants (Ê Đê, Jarai):** conversion entangled with land and politics; handle with care.
- **Dao and Khmer Krom:** remaking through initiation and ordination rather than conversion.
- **Cham communities:** script and ritual revival as collective rather than personal rebirth.
- **Released prisoners, former drug users, former re-education detainees:** whose fresh start is believed.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** the refugee's "new life" story set against those who stayed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religion and Đổi Mới; mixed for recovery and reintegration; thin and contested for re-education and compulsory rehab.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese with chữ Hán for Buddhist terms; Hmong, Khmer, Cham.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** camps and compulsory centers; Highlands Protestantism; Dao and Hmong rites, as keepers allow. Amnesty law: Libra.
- **Held for review:** rebirth teachings read from South Asian classical-language scriptures in Khmer Krom pagodas (category only).
