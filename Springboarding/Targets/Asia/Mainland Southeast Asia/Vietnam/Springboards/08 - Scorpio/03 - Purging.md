# Vietnam: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern villages that lived through land reform:** memory of being purged, or of purging neighbors; the rectification's afterlife.
- **Party and state officials:** discipline campaigns as career risk; how the purged describe themselves.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn and the north:** the community most marked by expulsion.
- **Urban middle class:** detox, fasting and decluttering as new wellness against older folk cleansing.
- **Rural and older Kinh:** coining, cupping and steaming as household medicine.
- **Dao and other northern upland healers:** herbal bathing in its own idiom, and its sale to visitors.
- **Cham Ahier and Bani:** separate cleansing calendars; who performs them.
- **Khmer Krom:** washing of Buddha images and elders at New Year.
- **Buddhist laity and clergy:** paid bad-year and spirit rites, and reformers who attack them.
- **Families near Agent Orange and Formosa sites:** pollution felt in the body and the sea.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** exiles of 1975 and 1978–79 as the purged, telling it from outside.
