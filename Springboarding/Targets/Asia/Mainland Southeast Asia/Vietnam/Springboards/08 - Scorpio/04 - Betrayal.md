# Vietnam: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese, plus classical Hán and Nôm for the chronicles; Thái and Khmer for minority literatures. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern and southern families divided in 1954 and 1975:** each side's word for the other ("Việt gian", "ngụy", "Việt Cộng nằm vùng"); a traitor depends on who tells it.
- **Former southern officials, soldiers and their children:** what it meant to carry a "traitor" background in state records.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** wartime alliances with outside powers, and charges of disloyalty since; tread carefully.
- **Hoa:** suspected of divided loyalty in 1978–79; their own account.
- **Urban women and men:** whether the man, the mistress or the wife bears the blame in an affair.
- **Thái, Tày and other northern peoples:** fidelity and arranged marriage in their own verse.
- **Khmer Krom:** betrayal stories in Khmer-language theater beside Kinh versions.
- **Hụi members in market towns:** small-circle betrayal of pooled money.
- **Party members who left (defectors, dissidents):** how leaving is named from inside and outside.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** exile politics, where "traitor" charges also fly.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, history and television; mixed for infidelity measures; thin and guarded for informers and defectors after 1975.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; classical Hán and Nôm for chronicles; Thái and Khmer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** post-1975 labels; Central Highlands loyalties; the Hoa exodus; dissidents; private affairs. Treason and adultery law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
