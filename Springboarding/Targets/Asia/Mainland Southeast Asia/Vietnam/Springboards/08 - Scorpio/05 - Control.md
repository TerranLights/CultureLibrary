## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villagers:** dense village bodies and dyke duty; control spread across many neighbors.
- **Mekong Delta households:** looser villages and canal settlement; whether control sits more in the household.
- **Urban migrants without local registration:** living partly outside the registry's reach.
- **Industrial-zone workers (Bắc Ninh, Bình Dương, Đồng Nai):** time and body regulated by the factory.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai longhouses:** household authority held through women's lines, as they describe it.
- **Hmong clans:** clan elders' say over marriage, and young people who refuse.
- **Thái and Mường resettled by dams:** control of land and river taken from above.
- **Women who hold the household purse, and men mocked as "sợ vợ":** where money control sits in practice.
- **Spirit mediums and their followers:** control given up in possession, and gained through ritual.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** parents' control of children raised abroad, set against home.
