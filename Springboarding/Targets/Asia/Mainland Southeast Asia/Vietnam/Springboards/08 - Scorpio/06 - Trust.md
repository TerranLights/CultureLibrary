# Vietnam: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Cantonese and Teochew for Hoa trade networks; Khmer, Ê Đê and Hmong for community ties. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed (loyalty is researched here for now).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villagers:** dense kin and lineage; trust measured by reciprocity across generations.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City traders and small firms:** trade on credit with people met through networks.
- **Hoa merchants:** congregation and dialect ties as guarantees.
- **New urban migrants:** whom they rely on without kin nearby (landlords, hometown associations, đồng hương).
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and other Highlands peoples:** sworn kinship and village oaths against outside institutions.
- **Hmong:** clan surname as a passport to strangers' trust; check how far it reaches.
- **Khmer Krom:** the pagoda as the community's trusted center.
- **Older savers and the young online:** gold under the bed against apps and banks.
- **Southern families after 1975:** trust in state institutions shaped by that history; ask, don't assume.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** remittances sent through trusted couriers rather than banks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys (PAPI, PCI, Asian Barometer) and business studies; mixed for everyday trust; thin for Highlands and Hmong trust networks in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Cantonese and Teochew for Hoa records; Khmer, Ê Đê, Hmong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in the Party and security forces is a guarded topic in surveys; bank-fraud trials. Trust in police and courts goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
