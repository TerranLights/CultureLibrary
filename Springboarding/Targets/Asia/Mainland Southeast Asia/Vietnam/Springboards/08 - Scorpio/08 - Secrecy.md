# Vietnam: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hán for court records; Cham (akhar thrah), Nôm Tày and Nôm Dao for ritual books. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kinh families of the Red River Delta:** lineage reputation and the pressure to "shut the door" on trouble.
- **Southern Kinh in Mekong Delta sects:** histories of worship and organizing in hiding.
- **Hoa:** sworn societies and congregation records; what outsiders were never told.
- **Craft villages (Bát Tràng, Vạn Phúc and others):** method kept inside the family line.
- **Cham Ahier priests and Bani clergy:** manuscripts and rites held back by right.
- **Dao, Tày and Hmong ritual masters:** knowledge passed only to initiates.
- **Former wartime agents and sleepers on both sides:** double lives judged long after.
- **LGBT Vietnamese in cities and in the provinces:** coming out against staying hidden.
- **Children born outside marriage and their mothers:** who knows and who is kept from knowing.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** family secrets split across borders since 1975.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history and war memory; mixed for privacy attitudes; thin and protected for ritual knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Hán; Cham akhar thrah; Nôm Tày; Nôm Dao; French for colonial police files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cham, Dao, Tày and Hmong ritual knowledge (record only what keepers choose to share, never its content); archive access; state secrecy; private sexual life. State-secrets and data law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

4. Search news for "xét nghiệm ADN huyết thống", "lộ lọt dữ liệu" and "come out".
5. Contact Cham cultural bodies in Ninh Thuận only through published channels, asking what may be studied.
