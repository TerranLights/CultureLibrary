# Vietnam: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; classical Hán for chronicles; Jarai, Thái, Hmong and Cham; French for colonial-era studies. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it and how it is used (legitimate authority goes to Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern village elites:** ranked seating and old titles; how office in the village translated into power.
- **Huế and the former court families:** memory of dynastic power after its end.
- **Southern landholders and their heirs:** power from land in the delta before 1975, and what replaced it.
- **Jarai, Ê Đê and Bahnar:** village elders (già làng) and ritual masters; power without a ruler.
- **Thái, Hmong and Tày lords' descendants:** upland chiefly lines under several states.
- **Cham:** royal memory, priestly status and present-day standing.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda abbots as community leaders beside appointed officials.
- **Party, state and military families:** the "con ông cháu cha" question seen from inside.
- **New business groups:** money as power, and its ties to office.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** remittances and investors as outside power.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and political science; mixed for village and upland power in people's own words; thin for elite networks.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; classical Hán; Jarai, Thái, Hmong, Cham; French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Party leadership and factions; business-state ties; upland chiefly lines and land. Corruption as law goes to Libra; authority, hierarchy and power ways to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Thượng kinh ký sự* and Woodside for the pre-modern baseline.
2. Search Vietnamese journals for "ngôi thứ" and "quyền lực làng xã".
3. Read Kerkvliet and Gainsborough, then press archives for "lợi ích nhóm" and "ô dù".
4. Search library catalogs for Dournes and *Quắm tố mướn*.
5. Search news for "Bà Chúa Kho vay vốn" and "Bà Chúa Xứ" (yearly coverage).
