## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villages:** the famine's heartland, where 1945 memory is densest.
- **Central coast (Quảng Bình to Quảng Ngãi):** yearly typhoons and floods as recurring crisis.
- **Mekong Delta farmers, Kinh and Khmer Krom:** floods as routine against salinity as new threat.
- **Tày, Nùng and Hmong of the northern mountains:** landslides and flash floods; the 1979 border war.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận and Bình Thuận:** drought as the recurring hazard, with its own rites.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City migrants and workers:** the 2021 lockdown and the exodus home.
- **Hanoians who lived through the subsidy era:** whether that hardship is recalled with bitterness or fondness.
- **Young urban people:** personal crisis, depression and help-seeking in new words.
- **Officials and relief donors:** who is judged for the response.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** remittances and relief sent home in disasters.

## 4. Flags
