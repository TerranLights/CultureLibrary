# Vietnam: Domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm for older sources); Tày-Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Cantonese (Hoa); French for colonial sources. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, belief and philosophy: what is true, and where meaning is found (religion, philosophy, higher learning, long journeys), with religious ways and freedom ways as its folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh villages:** đình and goddess cults, the baseline the state's categories grew from.
- **Huế:** royal ritual sites and court Buddhism, a separate center of authority.
- **Mekong Delta:** home of Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo and smaller 19th-century movements.
- **Northern uplands (Tày, Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Dao, Mường):** own ritual specialists, scripts and conversion histories.
- **Central Highlands (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Mnông, Sê Đăng):** spirit traditions layered with missions.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận, Bình Thuận and An Giang:** three religious communities in one people.
- **Khmer Krom (Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh):** the pagoda as village center.
- **Hoa:** temple associations by dialect group.
- **Catholic settlements:** Bùi Chu, Phát Diệm and the 1954 resettlement at Hố Nai.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Kinh majority and organized religions; mixed for minority faiths; thin and filtered for unrecognized groups.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; classical Chinese and Nôm; French; the minority languages above.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** unregistered groups, Highland Protestantism and Khmer Krom monastic politics touch state security; some Cham and Highland ritual knowledge belongs to specialists.
