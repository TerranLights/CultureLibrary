# Vietnam: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese for minority congregations; French and Latin for mission archives. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Catholic dioceses (Bùi Chu, Phát Diệm) and Hố Nai:** parish-centered faith, kept through 1954 and after.
- **Hmong Protestants of Lào Cai, Hà Giang and Điện Biên:** recent conversion against household spirit practice.
- **Highland Christians (Ê Đê, Jarai Protestants; Bahnar Catholics of Kon Tum):** missionary faith in local languages.
- **Cao Đài of Tây Ninh and Hòa Hảo of An Giang:** two 20th-century faiths with their own scriptures.
- **Cham Bani and Cham Muslims:** two ways of keeping one inherited faith, inland and in the delta.
- **Khmer Krom pagoda communities:** trust shown through merit-making and gifts to monks, a different grammar of faith.
- **Hoa temple congregations:** trust placed in particular deities' power, such as Thiên Hậu for those who work the sea.
- **Urban youth of Hanoi and HCMC:** the switching and lapsing that surveys may record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholics and mediumship; mixed for Protestants and Cao Đài; thin and guarded for unregistered house churches and Highland faith.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Khmer, Cham; Latin and French for mission archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conversion in the uplands and Highlands is politically watched; mediums' and Cham priests' knowledge may be kept to initiates.
- **Held for review:** none.
