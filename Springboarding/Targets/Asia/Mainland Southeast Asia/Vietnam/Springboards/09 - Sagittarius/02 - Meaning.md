# Vietnam: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Nôm for classical verse); Ê Đê, Mường, Hmong, Khmer, Cham for oral and minority sources. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern lineage villages:** purpose tied to ancestral halls (nhà thờ họ) and continuing the line.
- **War-generation veterans, north and south:** meaning made in war, and its loss afterward, on both sides.
- **Families of the former southern republic and re-education camp survivors:** life-purpose broken and rebuilt.
- **Young urban professionals in Hanoi, Đà Nẵng and HCMC:** career, consumption and "finding oneself".
- **Catholic families sending sons and daughters to seminaries and convents:** vocation as a family's meaning.
- **Highland peoples with epic traditions (Ê Đê, Mnông, Bahnar):** purpose in sung narrative.
- **Khmer Krom young men in temporary monkhood:** a set life stage for reflection.
- **Hoa merchant families of Chợ Lớn:** meaning carried in business continuity and lineage halls.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and song; mixed in surveys (few direct meaning-in-life items); thin for minority voices outside the epics.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, chữ Nôm for the classical texts; Ê Đê, Mường, Cham, Khmer, Hmong.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory from the southern side, re-education camps and suicide data are touchy; some Highland epics are performed only in ritual settings.
- **Held for review:** none.
