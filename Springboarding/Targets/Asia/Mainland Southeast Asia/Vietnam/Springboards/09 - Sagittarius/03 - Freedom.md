# Vietnam: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (chữ Hán for pre-modern texts); French for the colonial press; Thái, Hmong, Jarai, Ê Đê, Khmer, Cham for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villages:** local self-rule ("lệ làng") as an old, small-scale freedom against the court.
- **Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** the 1930 to 1931 soviets make liberation a local memory, not only a national one.
- **Former Saigon intellectuals and press families:** a southern civic-liberty tradition from before 1975.
- **Central Highland peoples (Jarai, Ê Đê, Mnông):** autonomy claims and their suppression.
- **Thái and Hmong of the northwest:** memory of the autonomous region; Hmong messianic hopes, as Hmong tell them.
- **Catholic parishes in disputes over church land:** freedom of worship as a local fight.
- **Hoa who stayed after 1978 to 1979:** freedom of movement and belonging after the exodus.
- **Internal labor migrants in industrial zones:** movement freedom tied to registration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the official national narrative and literature; mixed for surveys; thin and risky for dissent, minority autonomy and religious freedom.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, French, chữ Hán; Thái, Hmong, Jarai, Ê Đê, Cham, Khmer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dissent, Highland and Khmer Krom autonomy, religious freedom and the southern memory of 1975 are politically sensitive; protect informants. Rights as written law and the household-registration rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
