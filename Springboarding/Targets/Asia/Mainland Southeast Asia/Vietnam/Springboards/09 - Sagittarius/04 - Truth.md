# Vietnam: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (chữ Hán for chronicles and Neo-Confucian texts); French for Trần Đức Thảo; Thái (old Thái script), Hmong, Khmer for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern state-press readers and Party members:** truth set by official organs and theory journals.
- **Southern urban readers raised on pre-1975 press memory:** different trust in printed sources.
- **Huế and Thừa Thiên:** a city where 1968 is remembered in more than one version.
- **Thiền practitioners and lay Buddhists:** truth as realization rather than proof.
- **Thái of Sơn La and Điện Biên:** their own written chronicles as a source of historical truth.
- **Hmong clans:** oral genealogy as record.
- **Khmer Krom of Trà Vinh and Sóc Trăng:** to test whether abbots and achar (lay ritual leaders) are asked to settle disputed facts, and how far Vietnamese-language media reach them.
- **Rural elders and village oath customs:** sworn truth before the village spirit.
- **Young social-media users:** truth sorted through feeds, fact-checks and rumor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official doctrine, chronicles and press history; mixed for surveys; thin for minority epistemologies.
