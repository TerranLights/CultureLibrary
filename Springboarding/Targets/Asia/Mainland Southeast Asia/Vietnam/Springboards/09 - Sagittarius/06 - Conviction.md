## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Scholar lineages of Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** to test whether family histories there honor ancestors who refused office on principle.
- **Delta people raised on Lục Vân Tiên recitation:** steadfastness learned through sung verse.
- **War volunteers and their families in the north:** conviction tied to sacrifice and martyr status.
- **Southern families who resisted or defected:** turncoat and loyalist judged by different sides.
- **Huế Buddhists of the 1960s movement:** conviction carried by monks and lay associations.
- **Catholic communities with detained clergy in their history:** holding firm as a parish story.
- **Khmer Krom monks:** conviction expressed through monastic collective action.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai Christians:** steadfastness under pressure to give up a faith.
- **Cham Awal priests (Po Acar) of Ninh Thuận:** the vows and abstentions of the Ramưwan month as a lived test of firmness *check*.
