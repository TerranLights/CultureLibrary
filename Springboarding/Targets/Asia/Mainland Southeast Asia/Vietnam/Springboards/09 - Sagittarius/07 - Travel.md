# Vietnam: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (chữ Hán for envoy writing and gazetteers); Dao, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese and Teochew (Hoa); French for colonial travel records. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh labor-sending villages:** leaving as family strategy and risk.
- **Mekong Delta migrants to the Southeast's factories:** internal migration on a large scale.
- **Northern delta pilgrims:** the spring journey to temples as a yearly round.
- **Young urban Kinh motorbike travelers:** the road as adventure and self-making.
- **Dao and Hmong of the northern mountains:** ancestral migration accounts and cross-border kin ties.
- **Hoa of Hội An and Chợ Lớn:** arrival histories kept in assembly halls.
- **Cham Muslims of An Giang:** pilgrimage abroad and ties to Muslim communities overseas, to be traced.
- **Khmer Krom monks:** study journeys across the border, and how they are received on return (to trace).
- **Hospitality hosts (homestay villages in Sa Pa, Mai Châu):** the guest seen from the host's side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism statistics and classical travel texts; mixed for labor migration; thin for minority travel in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, chữ Hán, French; Dao, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese, Teochew.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** irregular migration and trafficking, Khmer Krom cross-border monastic study and the 1978 to 1979 Hoa exodus are sensitive. Diaspora (overseas Vietnamese) sources for comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none.
