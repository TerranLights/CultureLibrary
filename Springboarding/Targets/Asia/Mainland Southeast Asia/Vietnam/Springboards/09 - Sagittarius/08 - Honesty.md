# Vietnam: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Khmer, Hmong, Cantonese and Teochew (Hoa) for minority and market sources; English for international indices. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural north-central households:** moral readers and folktales as the honesty curriculum.
- **Southern delta storytellers:** to test whether the tall tale is heard as play rather than as deceit.
- **Provinces with different PAPI bribe reports:** where informal payments are reported more or less often.
- **Students and teachers in high-pressure exam provinces:** cheating as pressure, shame or routine.
- **Hoa traders of Chợ Lớn:** reputation within dialect networks.
- **Khmer Krom villages:** trickster tales and pagoda teaching on lying.
- **Hmong and Dao market sellers in upland towns:** honesty in mixed-ethnic markets and toward tourists.
- **Tourist-town residents (Sa Pa, Hội An):** how locals talk about pricing for outsiders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys and folklore; mixed for everyday white lies; thin for minority norms in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Khmer, Hmong, Dao, Cantonese, Teochew.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases tied to named officials are sensitive. Bribery, fraud and exam rules as law go to Libra and Capricorn. Do not read any index as one voice for the nation.
- **Held for review:** none.
