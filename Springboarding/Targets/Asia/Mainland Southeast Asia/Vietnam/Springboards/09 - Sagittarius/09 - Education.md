## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** an exam-going reputation to test against today's scores and migration.
- **Red River Delta laureate villages:** whether study is treated there as an asset a lineage inherits and must keep up.
- **Huế:** the last imperial exam center and a university town.
- **HCMC and the southeast:** private and international schools, and a market view of degrees.
- **Mekong Delta provinces:** attainment gaps against the national average, as data may show.
- **Khmer Krom of Trà Vinh and Sóc Trăng:** pagoda and state schooling side by side.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận:** a script taught by community committees.
- **Highland peoples (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar):** boarding schools and the language of instruction.
- **Hmong and Tày-Nùng of the north:** mother-tongue literacy and the distance to schools.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** Chinese-language centers alongside state schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam history, official statistics and PISA; mixed for higher-education quality; thin for minority-language schooling in the minorities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, chữ Hán, French, Russian; Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Tày-Nùng, Mandarin, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the closing of southern religious universities in 1975, ideological curricula and minority-language policy are sensitive. Early schooling and learning itself belong to Gemini.
- **Held for review:** none.
