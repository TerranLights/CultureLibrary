# Vietnam: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (chữ Hán for court edicts); Hmong, Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Cham, Khmer for minority movements; French and English for scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hmong of Hà Giang, Cao Bằng and Điện Biên:** restorationist and messianic currents, as members and the state each describe them.
- **Jarai and Ê Đê Protestants of Gia Lai and Đắk Lắk:** whether conversion is framed as a strict break with spirit practice, and how political suspicion shapes that.
- **Bahnar and Jarai Catholics of Kon Tum:** splits from mission authority.
- **Cham of Ninh Thuận and An Giang:** where Bani practice meets reformist Islam.
- **Khmer Krom monks:** disputes over who keeps the correct monastic line.
- **Hòa Hảo and Cao Đài followers in the delta:** registered against dissident branches claiming purity.
- **Party and security institutions in the capitals:** doctrinal certainty as a secular comparison.
- **Urban seekers in HCMC and Hanoi:** new groups that draw charges of control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much material comes from the state or from exile advocacy groups, each with an agenda; members' own voices are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, chữ Hán, Hmong, Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, Cham, Khmer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** high. Unregistered groups, Highland churches and Khmer Krom monastic politics are security matters; members may face pressure. Use "fundamentalist", "cult" and "heterodox" only as the labels sources apply. Bans as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** pointers on Islamic reform movements of South Asian origin among Cham Muslims, held as a category.
