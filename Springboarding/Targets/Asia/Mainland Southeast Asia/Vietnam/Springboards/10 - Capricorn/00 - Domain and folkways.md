## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh villages:** the examination state's old core, where communal-house (*đình*) seating showed village rank.
- **Huế and the central coast:** the last imperial capital; court protocol survives in rites and family memory.
- **Mekong Delta and Hồ Chí Minh City:** frontier settlement, commerce, and the southern republic's institutions before 1975.
- **Northern uplands (Thái, Mường, Tày, Nùng, Hmong, Dao):** hereditary lords, clan heads and ritual masters beside state offices.
- **Central Highlands (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, M'Nông, Cơ Ho, Xơ Đăng):** village elders and matrilineal houses meeting state cadres.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani, Sunni Muslim Cham of An Giang):** separate clergy ranks and calendars.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda-centered authority in Sóc Trăng and Trà Vinh.
- **Hoa:** congregation and clan associations in Chợ Lớn and elsewhere.
- **Catholics, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo, Highland Protestants:** religious hierarchies that sit beside the state's.
- **Generations:** war-era cohorts against those born after Đổi Mới (1986).
- **Overseas Vietnamese** (California, France, Australia, Germany, Czech Republic): comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Kinh majority and the state; mixed for the northern uplands; thin for the Highlands and Cham outside ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese and chữ Nôm for court and village records; French; Khmer, Cham (Akhar Thrah script), Thái, Hmong and Highland languages for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** open criticism of the Party and leaders carries legal risk; Highland and religious-minority topics are politically sensitive; respect restricted ritual knowledge of Highland and Cham clergy.
- **Scope:** the tracker records no settled lands for Vietnam yet; the exact lands are still to be settled by the author, so this sign is written for Vietnam's core lands.
- **Held for review:** none.
