# Vietnam: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for court chronicles; French for colonial ethnography; Thái, Mường, Jarai, Ê Đê, Hmong, Khmer and Cham for community sources. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh:** village notables once spoke for the commune; compare who speaks for it now that cadres are appointed.
- **Mekong Delta:** prophetic and sect authority of Hòa Hảo and Cao Đài, set against the reach of court and state, to check.
- **Central Highlands (Jarai, Ê Đê, Bahnar, M'Nông):** elders and ritual kings beside the state's appointed "persons of standing".
- **Thái and Mường:** hereditary lords until the 1950s; whether their descendants still hold weight.
- **Hmong:** clan heads against pastors where Protestant conversion has spread.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani):** clergy whose standing comes from ritual rank, not the state.
- **Khmer Krom:** monks and lay officiants (*achar*) as the community's voice to officials.
- **Catholic parishes:** priests and bishops as an authority that can face local officials over land.
- **Southern families with pre-1975 Republic ties:** memories of a different source of rule.
- **Urban young people online:** whose word they accept (influencers, experts, parents).
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only; whose word carries weight once the Party-state's reach falls away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official doctrine and court history; mixed for minorities; thin for honest polling, since questions about obeying the authorities are answered under political constraint.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese; French; Thái, Mường, Jarai, Ê Đê, Hmong, Khmer and Cham.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticizing leaders can be prosecuted; Highland and Protestant authority is politically charged; ritual kings' and clergy rites may be restricted to insiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
