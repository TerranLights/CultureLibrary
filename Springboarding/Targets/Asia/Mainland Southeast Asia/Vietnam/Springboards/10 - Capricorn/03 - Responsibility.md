# Vietnam: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for court records; Khmer, Bahnar, Xơ Đăng and other Highland languages for community sources; English for development and governance reports. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State and Party cadres:** answerable to the collective and to superiors at once; compare formal blame with who in fact steps down.
- **Private-sector managers in Hồ Chí Minh City and Hà Nội:** company accountability against older office norms.
- **Red River Delta villages:** households answering for each other through lineage and hamlet ties.
- **Mekong Delta households:** frontier families and how blame is shared when crops or migration fail.
- **Fishing communities of the central coast:** where corporate pollution put responsibility in public dispute.
- **Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Jarai and Ê Đê:** compensation customs that may make the kin group answer.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda teaching on cause and effect as a frame for personal blame.
- **Hoa merchant families:** answerability for debts and partners in clan and congregation networks.
- **Young people:** "adulting" talk on social media against parents' expectations.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only; who is blamed when a family plan abroad fails.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official discipline and press coverage of scandals; mixed for everyday household responsibility; thin for Highland compensation customs today.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for court records; Khmer; Bahnar, Xơ Đăng and Jarai; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pollution and corruption cases touch protests and arrests; blame for Party decisions is publicly assigned only in set forms.
- **Held for review:** none.
