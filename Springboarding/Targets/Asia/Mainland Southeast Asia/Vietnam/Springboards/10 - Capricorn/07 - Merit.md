# Vietnam: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for examination records and stelae; Khmer for Khmer Krom usage; English for education studies. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta "laureate villages":** places proud of exam history, where study as the route up may weigh most, to test.
- **Hồ Chí Minh City and the southeast:** business success as a rival measure of worth to degrees.
- **Families with "good" and "bad" political backgrounds:** how *lý lịch* has helped or blocked careers since 1954 and in the South after 1975.
- **Tày and Nùng:** long schooled in Vietnamese, with a strong presence in cadre ranks, to check.
- **Hmong, Dao and Highland peoples:** priority points and nominated places as help or stigma.
- **Khmer Krom:** pagoda learning in Khmer against state degrees.
- **Cham:** Akhar Thrah scholarship within the community, beside university paths.
- **Hoa:** family firms where trust and kin may outweigh credentials.
- **Women:** honors, degrees and promotion rates.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only; how foreign degrees are weighed against domestic ones on return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for examinations old and new and for press on nepotism; mixed for hiring practice; thin for minority views of priority policies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for stelae and registers; Khmer; Cham; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming officials' relatives in posts carries legal risk; exam-fraud cases involve provincial officials; political-background files are not public.
- **Held for review:** none.
