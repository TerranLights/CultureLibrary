# Vietnam: Law, the spirit of the law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese for the Lê code and old village covenants; Ê Đê, M'Nông, Hmong and Khmer for customary orders; English for legal-culture scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit; the letter goes to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villages:** covenant and custom, and the proverb that custom outranks royal law, tested against practice now.
- **Mekong Delta and Hồ Chí Minh City:** legal memory of the pre-1975 republic, and business use of courts and contracts.
- **Ê Đê and M'Nông:** verse customary law and the elders who recite it.
- **Jarai and Bahnar:** village compensation customs beside state courts.
- **Hmong and Dao:** clan mediation and its reach across the border.
- **Khmer Krom:** monks and pagoda committees as mediators.
- **Cham communities:** clergy and community councils on marriage and inheritance.
- **Catholic parishes:** church teaching on conscience in land disputes with officials.
- **Lawyers and the urban middle class:** a newer, rights-minded use of courts.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** comparison only; attitudes to police and courts formed under another legal system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for doctrine, legal history and press; mixed for trust surveys; thin for customary orders today and for candid views of courts.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Classical Chinese, Ê Đê, M'Nông, Jarai, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land disputes, Highland unrest and critiques of the courts carry legal risk; some customary law belongs to elders and is shared on their terms.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the Lê code's preface and family chapters for stated purposes.
2. Search the Hán-Nôm Institute catalog for "hương ước" with a delta province name.
3. Find *Luật tục Ê Đê* in a library and note its terms for wrong and repair.
4. Download the Justice Index reports and the GCB Asia 2020 Vietnam tables.
5. Search "tổ hòa giải ở cơ sở" with "kết quả" for mediation counts by province.
