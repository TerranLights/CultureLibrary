# Vietnam: Domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; Hán and Nôm for older texts); French for colonial sources; Khmer, Cham, Chinese dialects, Tày, Thái, Hmong, Mường and Central Highlands languages for minority sources. **Concept in one line:** community and ideals; what binds people into groups, and what they hope for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta Kinh villages:** compare against the communal-house and covenant model most studies start from.
- **North Central coast (Thanh Hóa, Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh):** test whether the Delta model fits.
- **Huế and the Central coast:** compare village groupings where Kinh, Hoa and Cham histories meet.
- **Mekong Delta and Đông Nam Bộ:** compare canal-side hamlets and their association forms.
- **Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City:** collective housing blocks, alleys and new estates as gathering settings.
- **Hoa:** compare congregations of each dialect group.
- **Cham:** Ahier (Balamon) and Awal (Bani) villages in Ninh Thuận and Bình Thuận; Muslim Cham of An Giang.
- **Khmer Krom:** compare hamlets and their temple committees in Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh and An Giang.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Mnông, Cơ Ho; compare each village house and elder.
- **Northern uplands:** Tày, Nùng, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Dao; compare valley and highland village forms.
- **Religious communities:** Catholic parish villages (Phát Diệm, Bùi Chu, the 1954 migrants' parishes in Đồng Nai), Cao Đài (Tây Ninh), Hòa Hảo (An Giang).
- **Resettled communities:** 1954 migrants, post-1975 New Economic Zones, upland sedentarization villages; compare how planned settlement formed groups.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare hometown and mutual-aid associations rebuilt abroad (Little Saigon, Paris, Berlin) with the home forms; the nation's findings apply to them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh villages and state campaigns; mixed for the Mekong Delta; thin and mostly outsider-written for upland and highland peoples' own accounts. The exact lands of post-war Vietnam are still to be settled by the author; this sheet is written for Vietnam's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese and Nôm; French; Khmer, Cham, Chinese dialects and the upland and highland languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independent associations and religious registration; highland land and religion conflicts; the Hoa departures of 1978-1979; some lineage and clan rites are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
