# Vietnam: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with Hán and Nôm for older village texts); Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Tày, Bahnar, Ê Đê and Cantonese for minority communities; French for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villages:** compare full members and settled outsiders, as covenants and novels record them.
- **Mekong Delta hamlets:** compare how newcomers become neighbors along canals, against Northern membership rules.
- **Hanoi collective blocks and Ho Chi Minh City alleys (hẻm):** compare apartment and alley neighbor life.
- **Peri-urban edges (Hóc Môn, Bình Dương, outer Hanoi):** compare community where migrants and old villagers meet.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** compare neighborhood associations with Kinh ward units.
- **Cham (Ninh Thuận, Bình Thuận; An Giang):** compare village and faith community among Ahier, Awal and Muslim Cham.
- **Khmer Krom (Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh, Kiên Giang):** compare the hamlet and its temple as a place community.
- **Central Highlands (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Xơ Đăng, Mnông):** compare the village house and gong occasions as community ties.
- **Northern uplands (Tày, Thái, Mường, Hmong, Dao):** compare valley villages, clan settlements and resettled villages.
- **Catholic parishes (Phát Diệm, Bùi Chu, Hố Nai):** compare the parish as a whole-life community.
- **Cao Đài and Hòa Hảo:** compare faith communities with their own halls and charity.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare parish and neighborhood community in Little Saigon, Paris 13e and around Berlin's Đồng Xuân Center, setting membership by choice against membership by birth into a village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh villages and cities; mixed for Hoa and Cham; thin and outsider-weighted for upland and highland peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Classical Chinese; French; Khmer, Cham, Cantonese, Hmong and Highlands languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** relief work outside official channels; religious community registration; resettlement and land conflicts in the Highlands and Northwest.
- **Held for review:** none.
