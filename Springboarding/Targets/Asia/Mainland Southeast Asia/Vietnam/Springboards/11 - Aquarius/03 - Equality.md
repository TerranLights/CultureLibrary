# Vietnam: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Nôm and Hán for pre-modern texts); Khmer, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai and Cham for minority voices; French and English for outside scholarship. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River Delta villages:** compare equal communal-field shares with ranked seating at the communal house.
- **Mekong Delta:** compare memories of landlord and tenant with present address forms between employers and workers.
- **Families with pre-1975 Southern backgrounds:** compare how background dossiers bore on schooling and jobs.
- **Rural migrants in Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City and Bình Dương:** compare access to schools and services with and without household registration.
- **Women by region:** compare daughters' standing in Northern, Central and Southern Kinh families.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Cham:** compare inheritance and household standing as each people describes its own kinship.
- **Khmer Krom:** compare the boarding-school and nominated-place routes with ordinary schooling.
- **Hmong, Dao and other upland peoples:** compare survey gaps and how each people speaks of them.
- **Hoa:** compare standing in work and schooling before 1975, after 1978-1979, and after Đổi Mới.
- **Gay, lesbian and transgender people:** compare urban and provincial acceptance.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare address and pronoun use in families in California and France, to see which rank markers last and which level out outside the home country.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income and ethnic gaps (official surveys); rich on gender in the press; mixed on class background; thin on minority peoples' own words on equality.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Nôm and Classical Chinese; French; Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Ê Đê and Jarai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land reform, class labels and background dossiers; the treatment of Hoa after 1975; ethnic and religious complaints in the Highlands. Law on equality belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none.
