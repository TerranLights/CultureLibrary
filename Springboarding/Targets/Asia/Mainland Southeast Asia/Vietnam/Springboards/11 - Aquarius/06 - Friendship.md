# Vietnam: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Nôm for older poems and plays); Tày, Hmong, Khmer, Cham and Cantonese for minority forms of friendship; English for youth surveys. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bắc Ninh and Bắc Giang Quan họ villages:** compare ritual singer friendship with everyday friendship.
- **Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City:** compare dropping in, treating and splitting the bill among friends.
- **Central coast (Huế, Đà Nẵng, Quảng Nam):** compare friend groups at the café and at home.
- **Men and women:** compare drinking circles, coffee circles and women's friend groups across regions.
- **Veterans:** compare wartime comrade friendships with present ties.
- **Tày and Nùng:** compare sworn friendship and its duties to the families.
- **Hmong and Dao:** compare friendships across clan lines.
- **Khmer Krom:** compare friendships formed in temple novitiate with school friendships.
- **Cham:** compare friendships across Ahier, Awal and Muslim Cham lines.
- **Hoa:** compare sworn siblinghood with ordinary friendship.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** compare friendships with Kinh newcomers and within the village.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare how friendships with people at home are kept across distance and on return visits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and school texts; mixed in surveys; thin on minority forms of ritual friendship outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Nôm; Tày, Nùng, Hmong, Khmer, Cham and Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heavy drinking as a health topic; friendship as a channel for favors in public office (Libra and Capricorn take the legal side).
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Nguyễn Khuyến's two poems with annotations; list what a friend is owed in each.
2. Read the Quan họ UNESCO dossier; search Bắc Ninh papers for "kết chạ" and "kết bạn quan họ".
3. Search ethnology journals for "kết lạo" and "kết nghĩa" among Tày, Nùng and Thái.
4. Download the SAVY reports; extract the peer and friendship items by region and ethnicity.
5. Search VnExpress and Tuổi Trẻ for "văn hóa nhậu" and "tình bạn khác giới"; sample reader comments.
