# Vietnam: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Nôm and Hán for older verse); Hmong, Khmer, Thái and Cham for minority settings; French and English for scholarship and overseas writing. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City youth:** compare scenes, slang and dress tolerated in each city.
- **Huế and provincial towns:** compare what dress and conduct draw comment against the two large cities.
- **Rural Red River Delta villages:** compare treatment of the unmarried, the childless and the "lập dị".
- **Writers and artists:** compare Northern and Southern avant-gardes before and after 1975.
- **Mother Goddess mediums:** compare the room given to gender crossing in ritual with daily life.
- **Gay, lesbian and transgender people:** compare family responses in cities and provinces.
- **Hmong and Dao:** compare how converts and other departures from clan custom are treated.
- **Cham:** compare how Ahier, Awal and Muslim Cham communities treat members who depart from their practice.
- **Khmer Krom:** compare how young men who do and do not spend time as temple novices are spoken of.
- **Central Highlands peoples:** compare responses to new religious movements in villages.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare which styles and ways of life count as departures in families abroad, against the same choices at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and literary dissent; mixed for youth scenes (mostly press and online); thin for surveys on tolerance of the unconventional.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Nôm and Classical Chinese; Hmong, Thái, Cham and Khmer; French and English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political dissent and dissident writers; unregistered religious groups; LGBT topics in provincial settings; some mediumship rites are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read "Bài ca ngất ngưởng" and a Tản Đà selection with commentary; list what each claims the right to refuse.
2. Read Kim Ninh's chapters on Nhân Văn–Giai Phẩm; collect the writers' own texts named there.
3. Search the National Library and overseas archives for *Sáng Tạo* and Bùi Giáng's books.
4. Read Fjelstad and Nguyễn Thị Hiền; list the gender-crossing roles named and the places studied.
5. Search VnExpress and Thanh Niên for "nhuộm tóc học sinh" and "hình xăm" rule debates.
