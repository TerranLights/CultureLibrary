# Vietnam: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Nôm and Classical Chinese for older tales and prophecy texts); Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê and Mnông for minority new-year and harvest rites; English for international surveys. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nghệ An and Hà Tĩnh:** compare the hopes households place on labor migration.
- **Mekong Delta pilgrims (Châu Đốc, Bà Đen mountain in Tây Ninh):** compare vows to goddesses for trade and health.
- **Hòa Hảo and Cao Đài:** compare expectations of a coming age in each faith.
- **Catholics (La Vang in Quảng Trị; Phát Diệm):** compare Marian pilgrimage petitions.
- **Families of the missing war dead:** compare search, waiting and the turn to psychics.
- **Northern families with 1945 famine memory:** compare hopes passed on from scarcity.
- **Young Lives cohorts by region and ethnicity:** compare children's aspirations.
- **Hmong:** compare the Gầu Tào vow cycle with Kinh temple petitions.
- **Ê Đê and Mnông:** compare water-source and new-rice rites for a good year.
- **Khmer Krom:** compare new-year (Chôl Chnăm Thmây) wishes with Kinh Tết wishes.
- **Cham:** compare the Rija Nagar new-year rite with Katê petitions.
- **Hoa traders:** compare opening-day and new-year rites for business luck.
- **Overseas Vietnamese:** compare the hopes of remittance senders abroad with those of the families who receive the money at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for popular religion, lotteries and youth aspirations; mixed for prophecy (editions vary and are contested); thin for minority hope rites beyond heritage files.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese; Nôm and Classical Chinese; Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê and Mnông; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prophecy texts with political readings; the Hòa Hảo movement's history; irregular migration and trafficking; some shrine and rite practice is private.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Download Young Lives Vietnam reports; extract aspiration items by region and ethnicity.

4. Search VnExpress and Tuổi Trẻ for "xuất khẩu lao động đổi đời" and "tìm mộ liệt sĩ ngoại cảm".
5. Search provincial heritage sites for "Gầu Tào", "lễ cúng bến nước" and "Rija Nagar".
