# Vietnam: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces domain and folkways): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ; chữ Hán and chữ Nôm for older texts), French, Cham, Khmer, Tày-Nùng, Thái, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** how a people meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (the hidden, confinement, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence); folkway: magic ways, with order ways as runner-up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta Kinh:** Mother Goddess shrines and city fortune-telling markets.
- **Huế and the central coast:** roadside ghost shrines (miếu) and household cults for the war dead.
- **Mekong delta Kinh:** Bà Chúa Xứ pilgrimage, Cao Đài, Hòa Hảo and the Thất Sơn healers' hills.
- **Cham (Ahier/Balamon, Awal/Bani, Cham Muslims):** which clergy handle spirits; the Rija and Katê cycles.
- **Khmer Krom (Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh):** temple protection, achar and kru roles, neak ta shrines.
- **Northern uplands (Tày, Nùng, Thái, Mường, Dao, Hmong):** then, mo, tào and txiv neeb practitioners.
- **Central Highlands (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Mnông, Xơ Đăng, Cơ Ho):** Yang rites, grave-abandonment feasts, Protestant conversion.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** assembly-hall temples and the paper-offering trade.
- **Catholic parishes (Phát Diệm, Hố Nai, La Vang):** saints' intercession set against neighbors' spirit cults.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** which domain practices (mediums, altars, fortune-tellers) are carried abroad and which are left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh popular religion and war memory; mixed for minorities (often written from outside); thin for psychiatric and belief surveys.
- **Territory:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign's sheets are written for Vietnam's core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (modern and Hán-Nôm), French, Cham, Khmer, upland languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "superstition" label and past bans on mediumship; re-education camps, unregistered churches and highland Protestants; bùa ngải accusations aimed at Khmer, Cham or upland neighbors are records of fear, never facts about those peoples; some clergy-held ritual knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** classical scriptures from South Asian-heritage traditions bearing on Cham and Khmer Krom practice.
