# Vietnam: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (Quốc ngữ, with chữ Hán for older terms), Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Tày, highland languages, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta and Mekong delta Kinh villages:** how neighbors collect for a funeral or a sick household, and who keeps the list.
- **Catholic parishes against Kinh pagoda networks:** bác ái works and parish care groups set beside pagoda merit-making and orphan care.
- **Khmer Krom temples:** the direction of giving, lay to monks and temple to poor.
- **Cham Awal/Bani and Cham Muslims (Ninh Thuận, An Giang):** Ramưwan offerings and zakat practice, side by side.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Bahnar villages:** which kin group takes in an orphan, and what is shared at feasts with the poor.
- **Hmong and Dao clans:** help kept inside the clan against help to non-kin.
- **Hoa benevolent associations (Chợ Lớn):** free clinics and burial help, beside Kinh charities.
- **Families living with Agent Orange effects and disability:** how they describe being pitied against being supported.
- **Rural migrants in Hanoi and HCMC (lottery-ticket sellers, street vendors):** which givers stop for them.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** how flood-relief money sent home is collected, framed and accounted for.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kinh charity and press; mixed for religious charity; thin for minority terms and survey measures.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Cantonese; Hán for older texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity outside state channels (unregistered religious groups; the 2020-2021 celebrity flood-relief disputes) is touchy; amnesty law goes to Libra or Capricorn; study "poverty porn" in appeals, never repeat it.
- **Held for review:** scriptural texts in South Asian-heritage languages used in Khmer Krom and Cham teaching on giving.
