# Vietnam: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán for older registers), Khmer, Cham, Thái, upland languages, French. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families with members honored as liệt sĩ (Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh, Quảng Trị):** what they say of a life given, against the state's words.
- **Families of the former southern army's dead:** how and where their sacrifice is remembered (Biên Hòa military cemetery).
- **Labor-export villages (Nghệ An, Thái Bình):** what migrants and the families left behind say is given up.
- **Eldest daughters and daughters-in-law in Kinh households:** who leaves school or work for care.
- **Catholic parishes:** devotion to the martyrs, set beside family sacrifice talk.
- **Monastics and lay Buddhists:** giving up the self as vowed practice and as protest.
- **Khmer Krom families:** the son's ordination as a gift back to parents.
- **Cham Awal/Bani and Cham Muslims:** clergy fasting for others against individual fasting.
- **Central Highlands peoples (Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar):** what is offered at buffalo feasts, and the state's limits on them.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** how parents' sacrifice is told to children born abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war sacrifice and filial piety; mixed on care work (thin time-use data); thin for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (modern and Hán-Nôm), Khmer, Cham, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the honor given to one side's dead and not the other's; the 1963 Buddhist crisis and later protest deaths; disputes over official hero stories; labor migration deaths; animal sacrifice in the highlands is regulated and contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
