# Vietnam: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán for older terms), Thái, Hmong, Tày-Nùng, Khmer, Cham, Ê Đê, Jarai, Bahnar, Cantonese, French (colonial maps). **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River delta villages and Mekong canal settlements:** how the edge of a village is marked and crossed.
- **Hanoi tube-house alleys and HCMC hẻm against new gated estates:** who may walk in, and when.
- **Three-generation Kinh households:** the lines between a wife and her husband's parents.
- **Thái and Mường villages:** taboo signs at house and village gates, and who must obey them.
- **Hmong and Dao along the northern border:** kin, trade and marriage across the national line.
- **Ê Đê, Jarai and Bahnar:** village land, sacred forest and grave forest as distinct spaces.
- **Cham Ahier, Awal and Cham Muslims:** food and marriage lines among the three.
- **Khmer Krom near the Cambodian border:** temple grounds and cross-border kin.
- **Migrants without local registration in HCMC and Bình Dương:** what the hộ khẩu line meant in daily life.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** the edges of a dialect group's association, school and temple.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** how "chuyện nhà" is kept private or opened up under another country's norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the village and the state's categories; mixed for privacy and touch; thin for measured personal space.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, French, Thái, Hmong, Khmer, Cham, highland languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the China border (the 1979 war, marker disputes) and maritime borders are state-guarded topics; ethnic classification and highland land are sensitive; village conventions (hương ước) and privacy statutes belong to Libra or Capricorn; some sacred forests are closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none.
