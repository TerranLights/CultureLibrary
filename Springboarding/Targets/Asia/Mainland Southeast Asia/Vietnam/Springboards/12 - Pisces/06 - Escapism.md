# Vietnam: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with Hán-Nôm for older poetry), Hmong, Thái, Khmer, highland languages, Cantonese, French. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men's drinking circles in the north and in the south:** who drinks with whom, on what day, and who may refuse.
- **Urban youth in Hanoi and HCMC:** games, roaming and "healing" trips as their escapes.
- **Women of different ages:** romance novels, Korean dramas and pagoda outings as permitted escapes.
- **Ê Đê, Mnông and Bahnar:** jar wine (rượu cần) at rites against drinking outside them.
- **Hmong and Thái of the northwest:** memories of opium and current drug-treatment experience.
- **Mekong and Khmer Krom communities:** cockfighting and lottery as weekly leisure.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** audiences for Cantonese film and martial-arts serials beside Kinh readers.
- **Factory workers in Bình Dương and Bắc Ninh:** after-shift leisure and lottery tickets.
- **Former detainees of drug centers:** their accounts of escape, confinement and return.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** trips home for Tết as an escape from life abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on drinking and media; mixed on gambling (much is illegal); thin and guarded on drug use.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, French, Hmong, Thái, Khmer, highland languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** compulsory drug detention; flight from the country by boat after 1975 (vượt biên) is a political topic; drink-driving and gambling statutes belong to Libra or Capricorn; avoid tying opium or drink to any one people.
- **Held for review:** none.
