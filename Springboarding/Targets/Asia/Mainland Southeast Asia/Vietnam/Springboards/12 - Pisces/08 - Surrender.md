# Vietnam: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán for proverbs and older verse), Khmer, Cham, Hmong, Ê Đê, Jarai, French. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic parishes:** "phó thác" and "xin vâng" at sickbeds and funerals.
- **Lay Buddhists in Hanoi and Saigon:** who uses "buông bỏ" and "tùy duyên", and about what.
- **Families of the former southern army:** how 1975 is told as defeat, surrender or survival.
- **Families of northern veterans:** how the enemy's surrender at Điện Biên Phủ is retold.
- **Women in the violence surveys, by region:** staying, leaving and the words they use.
- **Cham Ahier and Cham Awal:** cremation and burial as two ways of releasing the dead.
- **Khmer Krom:** cremation and the temple's ash stupas.
- **Hmong:** the funeral chant's instructions to the departing soul.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai Protestants:** yielding to God in testimony, against the old spirit rites.
- **Rural elders and urban hospital patients:** where each expects to die, and who decides when to stop treatment.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** how emigrants describe accepting that they will not return to live.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich in proverbs, verse and religious language, thin in measured attitudes; "fatalism" is an outsiders' label to record, never adopt.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (with Hán-Nôm), Cham, Khmer, Hmong, highland languages, French.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1975 and the re-education camps are politically sensitive; domestic violence data involve survivors' safety; adoption and infant abandonment cases involve children; end-of-life law and the terms of surrender in treaties belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** funeral recitations in South Asian-heritage languages used by Cham and Khmer Krom clergy.
