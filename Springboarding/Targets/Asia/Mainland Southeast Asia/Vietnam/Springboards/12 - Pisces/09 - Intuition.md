# Vietnam: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán and Nôm for prophecy and medical texts), Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Jarai, Bahnar, Ê Đê, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Red River and Mekong rice farmers:** which sky and insect signs each reads for the weather.
- **Central coast fishing villages:** sea signs and the whale-spirit shrines (lăng Ông) in decisions to sail.
- **Hanoi shopkeepers and Hoa merchants of Chợ Lớn:** date-choosing and spatial readings before opening a business.
- **Hmong households:** horn and bone divination, and who among the young still asks for it.
- **Jarai, Bahnar and Ê Đê swidden farmers:** dreams and bird calls before choosing a field.
- **Khmer Krom villages:** kru teay and achar consulted on timing.
- **Cham Ahier priests and households:** whether and how the sakawi is used to time rites and travel.
- **Families searching for war dead:** psychic hunches against records and DNA.
- **Nurses and doctors in provincial and city hospitals:** clinical judgment against protocol.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** readings and almanac use carried on by phone and app across borders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on omens and divination as practice; mixed on psychics (much is press); thin on measured intuition.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (with Hán-Nôm), Hmong, Khmer, Cham, highland languages, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** psychic searches for remains touch grieving families and state agencies; the official "superstition" label colors sources; the line between hunch and prejudice (judging people by region or ethnicity) must be studied, not repeated; divination is a record of practice, never evidence.
- **Held for review:** none.
