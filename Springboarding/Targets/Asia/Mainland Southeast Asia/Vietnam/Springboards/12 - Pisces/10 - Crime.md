# Vietnam: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán for older terms), Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese, French. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched only under Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet cites that work rather than repeating it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hải Phòng and Hanoi underworld lore against Saigon's:** how each city tells its gangsters.
- **Mekong farming villages:** which wrongs (cattle theft, gambling) are shrugged off and which condemned.
- **Northern border provinces (Lào Cai, Hà Giang, Lạng Sơn):** trafficking as told by returnees and their families.
- **Hmong communities:** how "kéo vợ" (marriage by capture) is described by Hmong speakers and by the press.
- **Ê Đê and Jarai villages:** village sanctions for wrongs, beside the state's view (law itself under Libra).
- **Khmer Krom and Cham:** how crime news portrays them, against their own accounts.
- **Hoa of Chợ Lớn:** memories of the 1950s gambling and Bình Xuyên era.
- **Officials and business elites:** how corruption trials are received inside and outside their circles.
- **Former prisoners and their families:** what "hoàn lương" asks of them.
- **Older and younger scam victims:** who is blamed when money is lost online.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** how home press and families speak of emigrants convicted or trafficked abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in fiction, reportage and press; mixed in surveys; official crime statistics are partial and police-sourced.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, French (colonial records), Hmong, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political offenses and dissidents are a guarded topic; corruption trials are political; drug hubs and trafficking can stigmatize named villages and peoples, so record such labels, never adopt them; law, courts and penalties belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read "Chí Phèo" and *Bỉ vỏ* in annotated editions; list the words for wrongdoers.
2. Search the *An ninh Thế giới* archive for "giang hồ" and "hoàn lương" by decade.
3. Pull PAPI provincial tables on corruption and safety.
4. Search Google Scholar for "Vietnam" with "crime", "social evils" and "trafficking".
5. Search VnExpress for "án oan" (wrongful conviction) and "lừa đảo qua mạng".
