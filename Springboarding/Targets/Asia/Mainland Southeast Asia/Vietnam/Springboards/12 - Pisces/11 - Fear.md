# Vietnam: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Vietnam, region Mainland Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Vietnamese (with chữ Hán and Nôm for older tales), Hmong, Thái, Tày, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central coast flood provinces (Quảng Bình to Quảng Ngãi):** how typhoon fear is spoken of and prepared for each season.
- **Quảng Trị farmers:** daily caution about ordnance in the fields.
- **Mekong delta parents:** fear of drowning and the stories told to keep children from the water.
- **Families with Agent Orange exposure:** fears carried into pregnancy and marriage.
- **HCMC residents:** street-theft fear and volunteer patrols.
- **Thái and Tày villages:** sorcery accusations and what follows them.
- **Hmong households:** fright as soul loss and who is called to cure it.
- **Khmer Krom villages:** the fear and respect shown at neak ta shrines.
- **Cham Ahier and Awal:** whom families call when a child falls ill from fright.
- **Catholic and Buddhist families:** hell and judgment as each teaches children to fear them.
- **Upper-secondary students:** exam fear before the national graduation and entrance exams.
- **Overseas Vietnamese (comparison only):** which ghost stories and audio tales scare listeners abroad, set beside those told at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ghosts and war haunting; mixed on disaster and crime fear; thin but growing on measured anxiety.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese (with Hán-Nôm), Hmong, Thái, Tày, Khmer, Cham, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can endanger real families, so never name them; fear of the state and of political trouble is hard to research openly; ordnance and Agent Orange touch living victims.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Lan Trì kiến văn lục* in a modern edition and list the frightening beings.
2. Pull the V-NAMHS report and the Gallup Global Safety Report rows for Vietnam.
3. Search Project RENEW reports for risk-education materials.
4. Search *Dân tộc học* and Google Scholar for "ma gà", "ma lai" and "soul loss Hmong".
5. Search VnExpress for "hiệp sĩ đường phố" and "F0 kỳ thị" (stigma).
