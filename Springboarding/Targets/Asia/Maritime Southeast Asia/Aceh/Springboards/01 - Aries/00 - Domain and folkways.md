# Aceh: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi script), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, the Simeulue languages (Devayan, Sigulai), Kluet, Singkil; Malay/Indonesian where apt; Arabic loanwords. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it shows itself: the person, the body, appearance, naming, beginnings and initiative.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how rebuilt post-tsunami neighborhoods and old *gampong* name where a person belongs.
- **Pidie:** check Siegel's field area against later work, since one district has long stood for the whole in English scholarship.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen, the Pasai area):** check how the Samudra Pasai past enters local self-description.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno/Daya):** check local histories of the old Daya realm as a separate identity marker.
- **Gayo highlands (Takengon, Bener Meriah, Gayo Lues):** check teknonyms and *belah* (clan section) membership as parts of a person.
- **Alas of Southeast Aceh (Kutacane):** check whether the *merga* clan name leads a self-introduction.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check clan names and the Singkil language next to the Abdurrauf heritage.
- **Aneuk Jamee (Tapaktuan, Susoh, Labuhan Haji):** check how a people named "guests" tells its own origin.
- **Tamiang:** check the old Tamiang court heritage against the Acehnese label.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check Devayan, Sigulai and Haloban labels as separate selves.
- **Kluet, highland Javanese settlers, Chinese Acehnese of Peunayong:** check which census label each uses.
- **Comparison only:** the Acehnese in Malaysia (Yan, Kedah) and in Scandinavia; Indonesian national identity-card categories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich colonial and English ethnography for Aceh Besar, Pidie and the Gayo; thin for Simeulue, Kluet, Singkil, Alas and Tamiang.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker carries "Indonesia" as pre-war land); these sheets are written for the core lands of the former province of Aceh.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Indonesian; Dutch for colonial sources; Arabic for court and Sufi texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity was contested in the GAM conflict (1976-2005); tsunami losses; dress rules under sharia (law questions go to Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** court-era writings on the self and kingship by South Asian-born scholars at the sultanate, and South Asian-heritage merchant families' own identity labels.
