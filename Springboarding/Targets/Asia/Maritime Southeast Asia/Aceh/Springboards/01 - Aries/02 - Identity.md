# Aceh: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages, Singkil, Kluet; Indonesian for modern sources; English and Dutch for scholarship. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how migrants from the districts describe themselves once in the capital (by district, by *gampong*, or as Acehnese).
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check whether the long trading migration (*meudagang*) gives a district-based identity abroad.
- **North coast around Lhokseumawe:** check how the Pasai heritage and the gas-industry years each shaped local self-labels.
- **West and southwest coast:** check the ABAS campaign and Aneuk Jamee labels against the Acehnese one.
- **Gayo highlands:** check ALA campaign material, and Gayo Lues against Central Aceh Gayo.
- **Alas:** check whether *merga* or Alas or Acehnese is given first.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check labels where Singkil, Pakpak-related and Acehnese speakers mix.
- **Kluet:** check whether this small group appears as its own unit in surveys.
- **Tamiang:** check Malay identity against Acehnese, near the border with North Sumatra.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check whether islanders answer "who are you" with an island, a language (Devayan, Sigulai, Haloban) or "Aceh", and whether *smong* stories enter that answer.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check the generations born in Aceh.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese in Malaysia and Scandinavia; Indonesian national identity as the frame Aceh argues with.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the political side of Acehnese identity (conflict studies in English); thin for Kluet, Singkil and Simeulue self-names.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for oral self-labels; Indonesian for the province campaigns; English for diaspora studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity questions touch the GAM conflict, the highland split campaigns and the militias of the conflict years; interviewees may still be at risk.
- **Held for review:** the identity histories of South Asian-heritage merchant communities in Aceh's port towns.
