# Aceh: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian for economic data and reconstruction records; Dutch and English for the pepper-trade history. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pidie:** check traders' migration and shop-keeping networks, the setting most often studied for Acehnese enterprise.
- **Banda Aceh:** check the start-up and creative-economy scene after the reconstruction years.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check small business formation after the gas industry wound down.
- **West and southwest coast:** check the old pepper clearings and the post-tsunami rebuilding of Calang and Meulaboh.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee cooperatives and who in a family starts a new garden.
- **Alas:** check farmers' groups in the Alas valley.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check oil-palm smallholders against company plantations.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check the trading towns of Tapaktuan and Susoh.
- **Tamiang:** check plantation workers and smallholders near the border.
- **Simeulue:** check island ventures against mainland ones.
- **Women's groups and men's networks:** check who starts savings groups and who starts shops.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese shopkeepers in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good history of the pepper frontier and the reconstruction; business data exist at district level; folk vocabulary for initiative is thin in English.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *seuneubok* and *mawah* terms; Indonesian for economic data; Dutch for colonial pepper records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reconstruction funds and contracts were contested; some ventures were tied to conflict-era networks.
- **Held for review:** credit and shop networks of South Asian-heritage traders in the coastal towns.
