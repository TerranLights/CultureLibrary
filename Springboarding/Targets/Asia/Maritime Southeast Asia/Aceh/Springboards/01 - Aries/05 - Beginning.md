# Aceh: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian for reconstruction and survey material; Arabic religious terms; English for archaeology. **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether the year's turning is felt at 1 Muharram, January 1 or the tsunami anniversary, and in which neighborhoods.
- **Pidie:** check whether *keuneunong* is still read for planting, and by whom.
- **North coast, the Pasai area:** check how the Pasai conversion story is told as a beginning at local sites.
- **West coast (Calang, Meulaboh):** check resettlement towns for new rites of starting over.
- **Gayo highlands:** check birth rites and the start of the coffee harvest; Gayo calendar words likely differ.
- **Alas:** check origin stories of the first Alas settlements in the valley.
- **Singkil:** check how the area's scholarly heritage frames the start of religious life there.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check its arrival story against Minangkabau records (comparison only for the latter).
- **Tamiang:** check Malay new-house and wedding firsts.
- **Simeulue:** check how the island's conversion and its 2004 survival are told as beginnings.
- **Former combatants, widows, the displaced:** check who was given a new start and who was not.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese exiles who returned from Scandinavia and Malaysia after 2005.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for post-2004 starting over (surveys, archives, NGO reports); mixed for farming and birth rites; thin for Alas and Simeulue origin stories in print.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for rite names; Indonesian for reconstruction and reintegration reports; English for archaeology and STAR.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the tsunami's dead and missing; reintegration funds were disputed; land given to the displaced is still argued over.
- **Held for review:** the origin and founding chronicles of the sultanate written by South Asian-born court scholars.
