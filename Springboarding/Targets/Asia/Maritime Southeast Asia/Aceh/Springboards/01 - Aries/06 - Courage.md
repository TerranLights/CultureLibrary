# Aceh: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian; Dutch for the colonial war; Arabic religious terms. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aceh Besar:** check how the war's heroes are honored at local graves and in village memory.
- **Pidie:** check the *Hikayat Prang Sabi* tradition at its supposed home.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Aceh Utara):** check conflict-era stories of civilians who protected others.
- **West coast (Meulaboh):** check how Teuku Umar is remembered against the national story.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the memory of 1904 and of the conflict-era militias; courage may be told by opposing sides.
- **Alas:** check the 1904 villages' accounts against Kempees.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check local resistance stories; likely thin in print.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check southwestern fighters against the northern canon.
- **Tamiang:** check whether its border position gave different heroes.
- **Simeulue:** check courage stories from 2004, separate from the *smong* warning itself.
- **Women:** check widows, fighters and rescuers as three different kinds of courage.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national memory of Acehnese heroes; Dutch veterans' memoirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war courage (Dutch, Indonesian and English histories); thin for moral and everyday courage; women's courage well documented for the famous few only.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for colonial sources; Acehnese and Jawi for the *hikayat*; Indonesian for modern histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyrdom vocabulary is political; conflict-era courage is claimed by opposing sides; 1904 involves mass killing of civilians.
- **Held for review:** none set aside for courage under the standing rules.

5. Search Indonesian repositories for "Teuku Cut Ali" and "pahlawan Aceh Selatan".
