# Aceh: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian for welfare and fiscal data; English for the sultanate's foreign ties, Ottoman Turkish for its archive side. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others: self-reliance and self-sufficiency, for persons, households and peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how civil servants' and traders' households differ in leaning on salaries, kin and aid.
- **Pidie:** check whether trading households count as self-reliant when kin at home carry part of the load during journeys.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Aceh Utara):** check the GAM heartland's argument for political independence against everyday dependence on jobs and transfers.
- **West coast:** check post-tsunami aid dependence in Calang and Meulaboh against rebuilt self-reliance.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee households' independence from buyers, and how highland views of the independence movement were recorded.
- **Alas valley:** check forest-edge households and the Leuser conservation zone.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check smallholders' reliance on plantation companies.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check fishing and nutmeg households' self-provisioning.
- **Tamiang:** check plantation workers' dependence on estates.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check island self-sufficiency and the supply boats.
- **Former combatants and widows:** check who became self-supporting and who still relies on reintegration funds.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese who work in Malaysia and send money home; Indonesian national welfare programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political independence; moderate for welfare and aid data; thin for household self-provisioning outside the coast.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for fiscal and welfare data; Acehnese for household terms; English for the Ottoman ties and GAM studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** independence is the conflict's core issue and still sensitive under Indonesian rule; the law on special autonomy belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** sultanate-era trade-credit records tied to South Asian-heritage merchant houses, for the household self-reliance question.
