*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian for health and violence data; Arabic religious terms; English for medical anthropology. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check the needs-assessment districts against their own ethnographies, since one survey may stand for many places.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Aceh Utara):** check how anger at the gas industry and at the security forces was voiced, and in what words.
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check urban outlets (football, coffee-shop argument, protest) against village ones.
- **West coast:** check anger after the tsunami at slow aid and land disputes.
- **Gayo highlands:** check conflict-era anger between groups that took opposite sides; sources may come from both.
- **Alas and Southeast Aceh:** check feud and quarrel-settling words in Alas.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how anger between religious communities was handled in recorded disputes; sensitive.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check local reconciliation words.
- **Tamiang:** check quarrels on the plantation frontier.
- **Simeulue:** check island dispute-cooling; likely thin.
- **Men and women; young and old:** check display rules taught to boys and girls.
- **Comparison only:** exiles' anger in diaspora writing; Indonesian national violence statistics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collective and post-conflict anger (health surveys, conflict studies); thin for everyday temper and the smaller peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for emotion words; Indonesian for health and violence reports; English for medical anthropology.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture and massacre sites; survivors' testimony; inter-religious tension in Singkil; domestic violence data involve victims.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer for anger was set aside under the standing rules.
