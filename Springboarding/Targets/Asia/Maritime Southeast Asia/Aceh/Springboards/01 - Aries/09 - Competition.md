## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pidie:** check *geudeu-geudeu* and *seudati* together, since one district holds both a body contest and a song contest.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check *rapa'i pasee* rivalries between villages.
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check football and school rivalry in the capital, and bull contests in Aceh Besar.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check sea and boat contests; data likely thin.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *pacu kude* and *didong jalu*, and whether Gayo Lues and Central Aceh compete in different ways.
- **Alas:** check games and races in Kutacane.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check local contests of the swamp and river villages.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check song and dance contests in the southwestern towns.
- **Tamiang:** check Malay contests such as verse exchange and games.
- **Simeulue:** check island games and their place in district fairs.
- **Women and girls:** check which contests (recitation, dance, sport) are open to them.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national sports week and national exam averages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Performance contests are well filmed; written study of rules and judging is thinner; animal contests are rarely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for verse contests; French for Beaulieu; Indonesian for sport records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** betting on contests falls under sharia rules (law to Libra or Capricorn) and is likely missing from records; animal contests draw welfare objections; check which performance contests have been closed to women, and when.
- **Held for review:** contests and trade rivalry involving South Asian-heritage merchant guilds in the sultanate's port.
