# Aceh: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue languages; Indonesian for psychology and welfare studies; Arabic loanwords from religious teaching. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is said to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how civil-service rank and university degrees enter talk of a man's worth, against land and kin in the villages.
- **Pidie:** check returning traders: whether money sent or brought home is spoken of as proof of worth.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Aceh Utara):** check former combatants' standing after demobilization, district by district.
- **West coast:** check how aid-receiving households in Calang and Meulaboh describe their own standing after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *kemel* and the ranking of *belah* sections; worth may be argued differently in Gayo Lues and Takengon.
- **Alas:** check whether a *merga*'s standing passes to its members.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how the Abdurrauf heritage is used to claim local standing.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check whether a man's standing in Tapaktuan or Susoh is tied to his mother's lineage or to his own earnings.
- **Tamiang:** check former court families' titles and how they are used now.
- **Simeulue:** check whether islanders report being looked down on from the mainland, and in what words; likely thin.
- **Women, widows and orphans:** check self-worth studies by group rather than one average.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese students and workers abroad; Indonesian national happiness averages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Psychology theses and surveys exist in Indonesian; folk words for worth are known but little analyzed; Gayo is better covered than Alas, Singkil or Simeulue.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay to read Abdurrauf; Indonesian for the self-esteem theses; Acehnese and Gayo to hear *malèe* and *kemel* in use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public punishment under sharia shames its targets (law to Libra or Capricorn); widows, orphans and former combatants are vulnerable informants; aristocratic titles recall 1946 killings.
- **Held for review:** writings on the abasement and worth of the soul by South Asian-born scholars at the Aceh court.
