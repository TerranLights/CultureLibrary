# Aceh: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil and Simeulue languages; Malay, Indonesian and Arabic. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a people holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether urban professionals and *dayah* families rank piety, learning and income in the same order.
- **Pidie:** check how long trading absences are weighed against duties at home in local sayings.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check how the gas-industry decades are judged in talk of what was gained and lost.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check post-2004 religious revival talk against reconstruction-era consumer change.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the published Gayo value list against what coffee-growing families actually prioritize.
- **Alas:** check whether *adat Alas* is written down at all, and by whom.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check value talk in a district with Muslim and Christian villages; sensitive.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check the Minang formula on custom and religion as used in Tapaktuan (comparison only for the Minang side).
- **Tamiang:** check the Malay custom formula in local ceremonies.
- **Simeulue:** check island statements of custom and the place of the *smong* story in teaching.
- **Generations:** check post-peace youth against conflict-era parents, without assuming the direction of change.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesian national WVS figures; Acehnese associations abroad and what they teach their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Normative statements (council publications, fatwas, curricula) are plentiful; measured data for Aceh alone are thin; stated against lived values will need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Jawi for *Taj al-Salatin*; Acehnese for *hadih maja* and the war poem; Indonesian for MAA, MPU and curricula.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious values are policed by law in Aceh (letter to Libra, spirit to Capricorn); Singkil's interfaith tensions; outsiders' "values" talk can read as criticism of sharia.
- **Held for review:** treatises that set orthodox creed above rival Sufi teaching, by South Asian-born scholars who served the sultans.
