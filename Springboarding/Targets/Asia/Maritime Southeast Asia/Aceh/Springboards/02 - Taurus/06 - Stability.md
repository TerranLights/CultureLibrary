# Aceh: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Simeulue and Singkil languages; Javanese for settler villages; Indonesian for census and price data. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check the share of residents born elsewhere, and whether old families of the city's *gampông* speak of being outnumbered after 2004.
- **Pidie:** check the rhythm of men leaving and returning, and whether it is counted as instability or as a stable pattern.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Aceh Utara):** check boom-and-bust from the gas fields as a break in continuity.
- **West coast (Calang, Lamno, Meulaboh):** check villages rebuilt on the same ground against those moved inland.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *belah* continuity against coffee-boom newcomers.
- **Alas:** check whether founding lineages still hold the oldest *kute*.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check river villages that moved after floods, and how often.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check whether Tapaktuan's old families describe the town as unchanged or transformed.
- **Tamiang and transmigration villages:** check returnees against those who stayed away after the conflict.
- **Simeulue:** check out-migration of the young from the island; data likely thin.
- **The old and the young:** check whether elders and post-peace youth name the same things as lost or kept.
- **Comparison only:** long-settled Acehnese villages in Kedah, as a mirror for staying away; Indonesia-wide migration rates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Census, price and displacement data are good; studies of routine and attachment to place are thin outside Banda Aceh and the Gayo highlands.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for the *ranto* poems and *keuneunong*; Indonesian for census and price data; Javanese for settler interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement of Javanese settlers was violent; mass graves are sacred sites; divorce data touch courts (law to Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** the continuity of South Asian-heritage neighborhoods in the old port, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Drewes's edition of the *Hikayat Ranto* and list its words for home, leaving and return.
2. Download the Census 2020 lifetime-migration table and sort Aceh's districts by share born elsewhere.
3. Search for "keuneunong perubahan iklim" and "kalender musim Aceh" in theses and journals.
4. Download BPS CPI series for the four Aceh cities and note the largest jumps.
5. Search IDMC and journal indexes for "Aceh transmigrants displaced return".
