# Aceh: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil and Simeulue languages; Indonesian for surveys and psychology theses. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, people included, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether students and young couples report phone-checking and location-sharing as normal, and how counselors respond.
- **Pidie:** check how long absences of traveling husbands are handled, and what both spouses say about trust.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check how *lhok* fishers react when boats from other provinces enter, and who speaks for them.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check remarriage after 2004 and quarrels over a dead spouse's goods.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *sumang* and the *depik* trap sites as two kinds of guarding.
- **Alas:** check betrothal and elopement customs and how families respond.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check whether villages along the Singkil river system bar outsiders from their fish ponds and oxbows.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check whether a mother's family claims the couple's household (comparison only for Minang patterns).
- **Tamiang:** check what a broken engagement costs each family there, and who keeps the token.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check islanders' claims on reefs and lobster grounds against visiting boats.
- **Men and women:** check jealousy and control as each side reports it, rather than one voice.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia-wide SPHPN figures; Acehnese couples abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Jealousy and control are studied mainly through violence surveys and NGO casework; folk words and tales are scattered; fishing-ground guarding is better documented.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *hikayat* and songs; Gayo for lake-fishing terms; Indonesian for surveys and theses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence and controlling partners involve victims; love magic is stigmatized, so informants may deny it; sharia rules on men and women in public, polygamy and guardianship are law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** nothing; the jealousy, envy, charm and fishing-ground leads above needed no exclusion.
