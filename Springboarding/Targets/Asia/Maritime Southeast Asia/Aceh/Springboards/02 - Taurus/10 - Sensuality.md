## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether coffee-shop evenings, beach outings at Lhoknga and Ulee Lheue, and *ie bu peudah* are described as the city's pleasures, and by whom.
- **Pidie:** check sweets and snack traditions and the feast foods families travel home for.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check the frame-drum and dance clubs as pleasure, not only as contest.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check *kopi khop* and fish dishes, and whether coffee-shop custom differs from the north.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee taste, cool air and lake fish as highland pleasures in Gayo words.
- **Alas:** check Alas feast dishes and songs; almost unrecorded in print.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check whether sago and freshwater fish dishes are named as treats or as hardship food.
- **Aneuk Jamee and South Aceh:** check *rendang*-style dishes and nutmeg sweets as a separate cuisine (comparison only for Minang dishes).
- **Tamiang:** check Malay dishes and sung *pantun* as pleasure on the eastern border.
- **Simeulue:** check island cooking and *nandong* evenings as rest and enjoyment.
- **Women and men:** check which pleasures are public for each (the coffee shop, the beach, singing) and how this differs by district.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese restaurants and coffee shops in Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Food, coffee and music are well covered in Indonesian heritage and tourism writing; touch, rest and bodily comfort are barely studied; Alas, Singkil and Simeulue tastes are thin.
- **Languages needed:** English for Kartomi's music study; Indonesian for cupping and patchouli research; spoken Acehnese and Gayo to hear *mangat* and its kin used at the table.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public music, dance, bathing and dress are regulated under *qanun* (letter to Libra, spirit to Capricorn); sexuality belongs to Scorpio; questions on pleasure may embarrass informants, so ask about food and music first.
- **Held for review:** spice dishes, breads and sweets that came with South Asian-heritage families, as a category only.
