# Aceh: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian for press and records; Dutch for colonial reports. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how archives, press and the provincial government cluster here, and which districts this leaves without local news.
- **Pidie:** check whether returning traders and migrants are a main source of outside news in Pidie villages.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how news of the gas field and the conflict was gathered by people living beside both.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check which warnings were known before 2004 and what is taught now.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee-price news and how growers learn of the market.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check who holds knowledge of the forest edge (rangers, villagers, NGOs) and how it is shared.
- **Singkil:** check news flow in a district of rivers and swamps, and in its mixed-religion villages.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check how verse-borne warning sits beside sirens and phones.
- **Aneuk Jamee, Kluet and Tamiang:** check which local-language media, if any, exist.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check which news sources each relies on, given their own languages.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese in Europe running diaspora news sites; Indonesia's national press as the yardstick for coverage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Disaster-warning and press-freedom material is rich; folk channels of news and the old court's record-keeping are thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for colonial intelligence; Indonesian for press and archive work; Devayan for *nandong*; Acehnese for village notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conflict-era records may name informants and victims; colonial files hold intelligence on families still living; information rights and *qanun* on records are law (Libra or Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** on the court's record-keeping, the chronicles compiled by South Asian-born scholars, held back unnamed.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Voorhoeve catalog for genres (chronicle, letter, almanac, *hikayat*) and count items by type.
2. Search Delpher for "Koloniaal Verslag Atjeh" and note the yearly report sections on Aceh.
3. Search journals for "evacuation Banda Aceh 11 April 2012" and "sirene tsunami Aceh".
4. Search repositories for "smong nandong" and "hoaks WhatsApp Aceh".
5. Ask PDIA and the provincial archive office for their catalogs.
