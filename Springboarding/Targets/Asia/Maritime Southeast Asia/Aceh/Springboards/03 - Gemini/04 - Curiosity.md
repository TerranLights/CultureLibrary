*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check which sites draw curious visitors (museums, tsunami relics, the observatory) and who visits them.
- **Pidie:** check whether traders' children ask about distant markets and places first through kin abroad.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how the gas industry's engineering drew young people toward technical study.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lhong):** check how residents answer outsiders who come to study the coast's past waves.
- **Gayo highlands:** check local interest in the cave finds and in who owns the story they tell.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check the relationship between Alas guides and visiting scientists in Ketambe.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check curiosity about the Abdurrauf heritage and about neighbors of another religion.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check how islanders treat outsiders' questions since 2004 drew researchers to the island's warning verse.
- **Aneuk Jamee, Kluet and Tamiang:** check whether there are tales that forbid looking or opening, alongside the Gayo legend.
- **Chinese Acehnese and Javanese settlers:** check what questions neighbors ask them, and which they would not answer.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesian national science-competition averages; Acehnese abroad as people newly asked about Aceh.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Science and tourism leave records; children's questions, staring and prying norms are mostly unrecorded and will need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for *falak* and *tib* texts; Gayo for the legend in its own words; Indonesian for theses and tourism studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tsunami relics are also sites of loss; neighbors' reports to the sharia police make prying a legal matter (law to Libra or Capricorn); foreign research needs permits and has caused resentment.
- **Held for review:** books of marvels and nature that circulated through South Asian-born men at court; listed as a type, not by title.
