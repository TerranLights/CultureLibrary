# Aceh: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic loans for religious ties. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how relocated tsunami survivors rebuilt ties with neighbors they did not choose.
- **Pidie:** check how families keep ties with kin who trade far from home, and for how long such absences run.
- **North coast (Bireuen, Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how ex-combatant networks shape who gets work and contracts.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check villages that lost most of their people and how new ties were knit.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *sinte* obligations against the pull of coffee work and migration to Takengon.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check ties of Alas families with Karo and Gayo neighbors.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check kin ties that cross the religious line and the provincial border with Pakpak lands.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check how often islanders can reach kin on the mainland and by what means.
- **Aneuk Jamee, Kluet and Tamiang:** check marriage ties across these groups and with Acehnese families.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check ties kept with home regions and with neighbors after the conflict years.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese associations elsewhere in Indonesia (Taman Iskandar Muda among them), in Malaysia and in Scandinavia, for ties kept across distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Social-capital and migration statistics exist; the felt sense of connection and loneliness is barely measured for Aceh alone.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for visiting and wedding terms; Gayo for *sinte*; Indonesian for surveys and press; Turkish for any Ottoman-side record of the tie.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patronage after the peace touches living officials; orphans' and families' tracing records are confidential; adoption and guardianship rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** trade and marriage ties between Aceh's port families and South Asian-heritage merchant lineages, as a category only.
