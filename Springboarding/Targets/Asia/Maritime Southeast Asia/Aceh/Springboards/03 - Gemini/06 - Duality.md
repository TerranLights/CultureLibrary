# Aceh: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic and Jawi Malay for Sufi texts. **Concept in one line:** how two-ness is lived with: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how civil servants switch between Indonesian state language and Acehnese at home, and where each is expected.
- **Pidie:** check how the aristocrat-cleric split of the 1940s is remembered in family histories.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Pasai area):** check local claims on Shamsuddin as-Sumatrani as a son of Pasai.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check villages where former informers and former fighters live side by side.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *edet* and *ukum* in Gayo talk, and Gayo-Acehnese double identity in Takengon.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check Alas households that span Muslim and Christian kin.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how the Abdurrauf heritage frames the one-and-two question locally.
- **Simeulue:** check Devayan-Sigulai bilingual homes.
- **Tamiang and Aneuk Jamee:** check each group's place between an Acehnese and a Malay or Minangkabau heritage.
- **Kluet, Chinese Acehnese and Javanese settlers:** check which second identity each keeps and where it shows.
- **Comparison only:** dual citizens among Acehnese abroad, for two passports held at once; the Indonesian state identity as the partner term in any two-identity question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. The Sufi debate is well edited and studied; everyday paired concepts, twins and code-switching have scattered thesis coverage only.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay for the Sufi texts; Acehnese for sayings and conflict testimony; Gayo for *edet* and *ukum*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the informer label can still endanger people; the unity-of-being debate is still doctrinally charged for some clerics; custom and religious law as rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the polemics of South Asian-born scholars at the Aceh court against the unity-of-being teachers, as a category only.
