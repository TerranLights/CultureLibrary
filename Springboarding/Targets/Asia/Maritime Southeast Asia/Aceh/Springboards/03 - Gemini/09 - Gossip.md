# Aceh: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic terms from religious teaching on speech. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether city anonymity weakens village talk or moves it into online groups.
- **Pidie:** check how news of migrant traders' lives abroad returns to home villages.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check conflict-era rumors and how they are retold now.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check talk in relocation settlements where neighbors were strangers.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether mockery of named people is allowed inside a *didong* bout that would not pass outside it.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check talk between Alas, Gayo and Karo neighbors, and who is talked about.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check rumor across religious lines.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check talk in small island villages where everyone is kin.
- **Aneuk Jamee, Kluet and Tamiang:** check sung or spoken teasing forms in each group.
- **Chinese Acehnese and Javanese settlers:** check how they are spoken of, and what they say of being spoken of.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese diaspora groups online abroad; national gossip television as the Indonesian yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Online shaming and rumor in crises are studied; ordinary village gossip is mostly unrecorded and must be heard in fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** spoken Acehnese and Gayo to hear gossip itself; Jawi Malay and Arabic for the counsels; Indonesian for media studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gossip can trigger sharia raids and public punishment; online defamation is prosecuted (law to Libra or Capricorn); informants may fear being talked about for talking.
- **Held for review:** talk and slurs that targeted merchant families of South Asian heritage, kept as a category for later review.
