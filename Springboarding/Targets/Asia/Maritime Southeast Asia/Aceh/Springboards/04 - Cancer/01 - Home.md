# Aceh: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether "home" names the city house or the village of origin for families who moved to the capital.
- **Pidie:** check which house traders working elsewhere call home, the one in Pidie or the one where they trade.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check homes lost or abandoned in the conflict's worst districts, and who returned.
- **West coast (Calang, Meulaboh, Lamno):** check how families judge a rebuilt house on a new site against the lost one by the shore.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how conflict displacement in Bener Meriah and Central Aceh changed which house families call home.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check how Alas homes and Gayo Lues homes differ in layout and in who may enter.
- **Singkil and Pulau Banyak:** check flood-prone river homes and island homes, and the Haloban terms for them.
- **Simeulue:** check how the island's own words for house and village compare with the mainland's.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South and Southwest Aceh):** check whether the Jamee home village is placed in Aceh or in the Minangkabau past.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay homes on both sides of the old provincial line.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check whether "back home" means Java, China or Aceh, by generation.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese villages in Kedah, Malaysia, for a home rebuilt abroad; Indonesian housing programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Post-tsunami housing is heavily documented; conflict-era home loss is patchy; house forms of the smaller peoples rest on one or two inventories.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for room names and songs; Gayo and Alas for house words; Indonesian for tenure data and theses; English for Dampier and Jauhola.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burned homes involve named perpetrators and victims; returning to land after displacement can still be disputed; house inheritance and tenancy rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the houses and street names that South Asian-heritage traders left in the capital's old port, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Find the Depdikbud house inventory for Aceh and list every room name and dwelling type by people.
2. Read Dampier's Aceh chapter on houses against the 1980s inventory.

4. Pull the Census 2020 tenure table for Aceh by district.
5. Search rights-report and press archives for "rumah dibakar Aceh 2003".
