# Aceh: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Haloban and the Simeulue languages; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for lineage terms. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether descendants of titled families keep genealogies and kin gatherings that other families do not.
- **Pidie:** check how the 1946 violence is remembered inside the families concerned, on each side.
- **North coast (Bireuen, Lhokseumawe):** check how marriages across former conflict lines are arranged and received by both families.
- **West coast (Calang, Meulaboh):** check which uncles, aunts and grandparents took in survivors after 2004, and on what kin reasoning.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how clan reckoning sets who must attend and host a *kerje*.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check Alas clan names in daily address beside neighbors who use none.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how one clan surname runs across Muslim and Christian branches of a family.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check Devayan and Haloban kin reckoning against both mainland Acehnese and nearby Nias patterns.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether Jamee families keep mother's-line ties and how Kluet families trace descent.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check kin address shared with Malay families across the line in Langkat.
- **Sayyid families and Chinese Acehnese:** check their written genealogies as two family records unlike the oral reckoning around them.
- **Comparison only:** kin networks stretched to Penang, Kuala Lumpur and Sweden since the 1990s; the national family-development score.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Royal, titled and sayyid lineages are written down; ordinary families' kin reckoning lives in speech; the smaller peoples are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for royal genealogies; Acehnese for in-law words and mourning recitation; Gayo for *kerje*; Indonesian for BKKBN and BPS series.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1946 killings still divide descendants; descent claims of sayyid status are contested; inheritance, guardianship and adoption as rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** mixed-descent lineages formed by marriage between Acehnese and South Asian-heritage families, as a category only.
