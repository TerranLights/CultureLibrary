# Aceh: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Javanese for settler families; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how long a family from another district lives in the capital before neighbors stop calling it newcomers.
- **Pidie:** check how traders' families who live away are received when they come home for feasts.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check whether workers who came for the gas and fertilizer plants were ever treated as locals.
- **West coast (Nagan Raya, Meulaboh):** check the Syattariyah community and its neighbors, each on their own terms.
- **Gayo highlands:** check where Gayo and Javanese-descent villages meet, and which events bring both in.
- **Southeast Aceh:** check how Alas, Gayo, Karo and Batak residents name who is "from here".
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check belonging for Pakpak-related Singkil families beside Acehnese and Jamee newcomers.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check how islanders and mainland civil servants posted there mark each other.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check the Kluet valley's own term for outsiders against the Jamee name.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether Tamiang Malay families are counted as Acehnese, Malay or both, by themselves and by others.
- **Converts and Chinese Acehnese:** check acceptance as told by converts and by Chinese Acehnese families of long standing.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese communities in Malaysia and Scandinavia, for belonging held at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Measured belonging for Aceh alone is scarce; minority and newcomer studies are scattered theses; most evidence will come from fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for newcomer and guest words; Gayo for highland insider terms; Javanese for settler associations; Jawi Malay for Hamzah.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** belonging of religious minorities and converts touches policed rules (letter to Libra, spirit to Capricorn); returned exiles and settlers driven out in the conflict are at risk; questions about the Nagan Raya order's practice may be locally sensitive (*check*).
- **Held for review:** how South Asian-heritage residents of the port were counted as insiders or outsiders over time, as a category only.
