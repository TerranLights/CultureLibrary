# Aceh: The domain: creativity and self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch and French for outside accounts. **Concept in one line:** how people shine, play and take pleasure: the arts and making, play and sport, pleasure, courtship, children as joy, festival and display.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how art studios (*sanggar*), stages and beachfront evenings sort leisure into staged and casual.
- **Pidie:** check the long Maulid feasts and *idang* trays as a season of display.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check what the gas-boom decades added to entertainment and what closed after.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check which performing troupes regrouped after 2004 and which genres were lost with their players.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *didong* clubs against *saman* teams, which may draw different members.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas wedding and harvest arts; print sources are few.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check *dampeng* and feast music in towns with Pakpak-speaking and Christian neighbors.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check *nandong* evenings and island games as their own repertoire, not a mainland echo.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check which arts came with Jamee settlers and which are held to be Kluet.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay sung verse and dance on the eastern border.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check which of their performance forms are still staged.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese students' dance groups in Jakarta and Malaysia; national arts-participation averages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the performing arts; thin for pleasure, courtship and private leisure as lived; Alas, Kluet and Singkil arts barely in print.
- **Lands:** "Indonesia" is the only pre-war land the tracker names, so which districts the post-war nation holds is not yet settled; the Leo sheets are written for the old province's lands.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for performance genres and their lyrics; Dutch and French for the colonial and early-modern accounts; Jawi Malay for the court *hikayat*; Indonesian for surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** limits on public performance, music and mixed audiences, and on gambling, go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); performers' standing can be contested, so ask rather than assume.
- **Held for review:** court festival and pleasure literature written at the sultanate by South Asian-born scholars, as a category only.
