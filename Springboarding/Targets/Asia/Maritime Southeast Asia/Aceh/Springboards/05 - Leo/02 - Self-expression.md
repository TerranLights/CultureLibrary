# Aceh: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for modesty and speech terms. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how campus, café and online settings let students voice what they would not say at home.
- **Pidie:** check whether men home from trading elsewhere bring new styles of talk and dress, and how they are received.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check how people speak now of conflict years when speech carried danger.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check grief expressed in song, prayer and memorials after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *sebuku* and *didong* as two outlets, one private grief and one public comment.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas songs of feeling and how much of them survives.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check expression of faith in mixed-faith villages, and what is said or left unsaid.
- **Simeulue:** check *nandong* singers and who on the island may sing what.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check Jamee sung verse beside Kluet songs, and which language each uses for feeling.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether Malay *pantun* evenings still carry courtship and teasing.
- **Women and men, young and old:** check who may raise a voice, sing or dress boldly in public, district by district.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese students and workers in Kuala Lumpur and Scandinavia, where dress and speech may shift.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Performance and dress are studied; private expression, letters and diaries are thin; online material is plentiful but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese to follow song lyrics and comment verse; Gayo for *sebuku*; Devayan for *nandong*; Indonesian for youth and media studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dress, music and gatherings are regulated, and the punk case touched public order; those rules belong to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); speakers may still be cautious about conflict-era views.
- **Held for review:** devotional songs and public mourning processions of South Asian-heritage communities in the port, as a category only.
