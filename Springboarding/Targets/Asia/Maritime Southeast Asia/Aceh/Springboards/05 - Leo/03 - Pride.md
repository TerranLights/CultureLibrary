# Aceh: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for the vocabulary of arrogance. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check pride in the sultanate's capital and its mosque against pride in the rebuilt city.
- **Pidie:** check how families speak of members who succeeded as traders elsewhere, and what they bring home to show.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check pride tied to the old Pasai kingdom and the region's place in the conflict.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check pride in Teuku Umar's homeland and in recovery after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check pride in coffee, *saman* and the lake against the province-split campaign.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check pride in clan, river and valley, set apart from Gayo and coastal labels.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check pride in the scholar from Singkil whom the province honors, and how locals claim him.
- **Simeulue:** check whether the island's *smong* lore is retold as an achievement of its own.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether Jamee and Kluet speakers are proud of a separate origin or of a shared Acehnese one.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay sultanate memory and how it sits beside Acehnese symbols.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese Acehnese:** check what each group's festivals and associations hold up as theirs.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese associations abroad (Malaysia, Denmark, Norway) and their festival displays; Indonesian national-pride survey items.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Group pride and symbols are well covered in press and politics; personal pride and arrogance in everyday terms are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for Syamsuddin's texts; Acehnese for the *Malem Dagang* epic and sayings; Gayo for highland identity talk; Indonesian for curricula and surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the flag, separatist memory and province-split campaigns are politically charged; pride and its rivals among peoples should be heard from each side.
- **Held for review:** the place of South Asian-heritage families in Aceh's port pride and heritage displays, as a category only.

1. Search manuscript catalogs and theses for Syamsuddin as-Sumatrani's texts on *kibr* and *'ujb*.
