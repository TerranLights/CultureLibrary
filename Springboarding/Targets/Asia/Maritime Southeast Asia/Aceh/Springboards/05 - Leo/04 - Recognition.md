# Aceh: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for scholarly licences. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how old noble titles and new academic titles compete on invitations and seating.
- **Pidie:** check how survivors of the Rumoh Geudong era rate the recognition they have received.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check the standing of senior dayah leaders and how disciples name them.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check who was publicly thanked, and who was left out, in reconstruction ceremonies.
- **Gayo highlands:** check teknonyms and clan titles, and how highland figures sought provincial or national honors.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas clan offices and the honors given at weddings.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how the province's honors reach Singkil's scholars and village heads.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island elders who kept *smong* lore have been honored, and by whom.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether Jamee and Kluet titles of address differ from coastal Acehnese ones.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay royal titles from the old Tamiang courts, and whether they are still used.
- **Women:** check which honors go to women (heroines, teachers, midwives) and which are closed to them.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesian state decorations; recognition given to Acehnese returnees from Malaysia and Scandinavia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Formal honors and titles leave records; everyday credit and praise are thin; conflict acknowledgment is documented but contested.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for *sarakata* and seal legends; Arabic for licences; Gayo for teknonyms; Indonesian for decrees, commission reports and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims' testimony and ex-combatant status are delicate; titles linked to the 1946 upheaval may still divide families; how the law defines victims goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** titles and honors the sultanate bestowed on South Asian-born scholars and merchants, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Gallop's Aceh seal entries and list the titles and grant formulas named on them.
2. Search the Museum Aceh and manuscript catalogs for "sarakata" and note recipients by district.
3. Pull the KKR Aceh public reports and list what testimony says should be recognized.
4. Search repositories for "gelar Teuku Cut", "ijazah sanad dayah" and "peusijuek haji".
