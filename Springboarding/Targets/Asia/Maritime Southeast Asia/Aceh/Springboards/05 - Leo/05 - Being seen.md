# Aceh: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Singkil, Devayan, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for gaze and sincerity terms; Dutch for colonial photo records. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how the city's young people present themselves on social media against what parents allow in the street.
- **Pidie:** check families of the disappeared and how they keep their missing visible.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check who was noticed, and who was not, in the industrial enclave against the villages around it.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check how villages feel about being shown in disaster photographs and films.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the audience's role in *saman* and *didong*, where being watched is part of the art.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether Alas people feel counted under the labels of others.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how swamp and river villages appear, or fail to appear, in provincial plans.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check islanders' sense of distance from the capital's attention.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether census and school records name them or fold them into larger labels.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether Tamiang Malay is shown in provincial culture weeks and on what terms.
- **People with disabilities and elders living alone:** check where they appear in village life and where they do not.
- **Comparison only:** foreign aid agencies' images of Aceh; Acehnese seen as newcomers in Malaysian towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Census, missing-person and media records exist; the felt need to be noticed is rarely studied; Alas, Kluet and Singkil voices are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for the *Adat Aceh* manuscript; Dutch for photo captions; Acehnese for evil-eye and wedding terms; Indonesian for census, rights and media studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the missing and the dead; photographs of the defeated and the dead; women's visibility is regulated, and those rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** how the old port's South Asian-heritage residents appear or are missing in heritage displays, as a category only.
