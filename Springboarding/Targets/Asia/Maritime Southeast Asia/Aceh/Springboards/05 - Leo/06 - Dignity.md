# Aceh: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for theological terms; English for rights reports. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how residents of the elder home and the barracks describe being treated by officials.
- **Pidie:** check how families of detainees from the military-operations era speak of what was taken from them.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check dignity at work for men left jobless when the gas plant wound down.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check survivors' views of handouts against rebuilding by their own hands.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *mukemel* as the highland frame, and how it shapes asking for help.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how Alas custom marks respect for elders and the dead.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check respect between neighbors of different faiths at funerals and feasts.
- **Simeulue:** check how island custom names offenses against a person's standing.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check address forms in Jamee and Kluet speech, and whether they mirror Acehnese levels.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay notions of *maruah* on the plantations near the border.
- **Plantation laborers and Javanese settlers:** check how settlers speak of respect from established residents.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese domestic and construction workers in Malaysia; Indonesia-wide discrimination surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rights reports and relief studies are plentiful in English and Indonesian; Acehnese-language ideas of worth are thin and need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for address and self-respect terms; Arabic and Jawi Malay for Kuta Karang; English for rights and relief reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture, detention and mass graves; aid recipients' pride; punishments in public and their effect on dignity go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** court-era ethical treatises on rank and human worth written by South Asian-born scholars in Aceh, as a category only.
