# Aceh: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Singkil, Devayan, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for consultation terms; English for political studies. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check the pull of provincial politicians against neighborhood figures in the capital.
- **Pidie:** check whether memory of the district's own scholars and rebels shapes whom young men follow today.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check *dayah* leaders in Samalanga and their reach into politics.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Woyla):** check memory of women war leaders and who leads villages rebuilt after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the *reje* against elected officials, and how the highland province campaign chose its spokesmen.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan heads beside elected heads in Kutacane.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check leaders who speak for mixed-faith villages.
- **Simeulue:** check island leaders and their ties to the mainland parties.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether South Aceh villages follow custom heads of their own or Acehnese-speaking ones.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check plantation, union and Malay custom leaders on the border.
- **Former fighters and their widows:** check who among them is followed now, and in what.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese community heads in Kuala Lumpur and Scandinavia; Indonesian national leadership surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for rulers, rebels and scholars; thinner for everyday and women's leadership; Alas, Singkil and Simeulue leaders barely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for sultanate records; Acehnese for sayings and war verse; Gayo for *reje* and *petue*; English for Khan, Hasan di Tiro and election studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** GAM leadership and conflict loyalties remain charged; the formal powers of office (authority) belong to Capricorn, and law to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the influence of South Asian-born scholars on the sultanahs' court and its leadership quarrels, as a category only.
