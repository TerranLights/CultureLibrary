*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how screens, parks and malls have changed where city children play.
- **Pidie:** check kite season and whether it still draws adults as well as boys.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check what children played in the industrial housing estates against nearby villages.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check the play programs set up for children after 2004 and what stayed when the aid ended.
- **Gayo highlands:** check children's role at the horse races and what highland children play away from them.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas children's games and riddles in the valley villages.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check river and swamp play, and games shared across faith lines.
- **Simeulue and Pulau Banyak:** check sea play and island games against mainland repertoires.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether South Aceh games come with Jamee or Kluet names, and which children know them.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay games and rhymes on the North Sumatra border.
- **Girls and boys:** check which games and spaces each may use, and from what age play is expected to stop.
- **Comparison only:** play in Acehnese families in Malaysia; national studies of children's screen time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Folk games are recorded as heritage; adult play, joking and online play are less studied; Alas, Kluet and Singkil games are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for riddles and game names; Gayo for highland games; Devayan for island games; Indonesian for gaming and child-protection studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling and some games are restricted, and those rules go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); child jockeys raise child-protection questions.
- **Held for review:** board and card games that came with South Asian-heritage traders to the port, as a category only.

## 5. First queries

2. Read press coverage of the 2019 MPU ruling and of young players' replies.

4. Search heritage-office booklets for games in Alas, Singkil and Simeulue.
