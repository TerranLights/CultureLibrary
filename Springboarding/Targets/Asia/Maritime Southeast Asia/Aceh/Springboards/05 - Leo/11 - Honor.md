# Aceh: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for honor and martyrdom terms; Dutch for colonial reports. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how urban families weigh a good name against new ideas of individual dignity.
- **Pidie:** check traders' sense of a kept word and what happens to one who breaks it.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check how former fighters and their families speak of honor in war and peace.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check the honor given Teuku Umar at home, and whether local views differ from school texts.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *sumang* and clan standing against coastal ideas of family name.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan honor and how insults between clans are settled.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how honor is kept between Muslim and Christian families who intermarry or trade.
- **Simeulue:** check island custom on insult, apology and keeping one's word.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check whether family reputation rules follow Jamee or Kluet custom, and where they diverge.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay ideas of *maruah* and good name on the plantation frontier.
- **Women and men:** check what each is expected to guard, without assuming the answer.
- **Comparison only:** honor talk among Acehnese abroad; the colonial and Jakarta portraits of Acehnese, read as outsiders' claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war honor and martyrdom; thinner for everyday good name and kept word; Alas, Singkil and Simeulue codes thin.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for war verse and sayings; Dutch for Kern; Gayo for *sumang*; Indonesian for gender and trade theses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family control of women's conduct; violence justified by honor; adat settlement of insult and injury and the rules on morality go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); outsiders' "fanatic" labels must not be adopted.
- **Held for review:** court-era writings on the honor of kings and subjects by South Asian-born scholars in Aceh, as a category only.
