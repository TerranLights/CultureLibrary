# Aceh: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Singkil, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for devotional joy and gratitude. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how the capital's young people find gladness in cafés, beaches and concerts, and how elders see it.
- **Pidie:** check whether returning traders' homecomings at the festivals are described as the year's high point.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check peace-anniversary gatherings in former conflict strongholds.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang, Lamno):** check the balance of mourning and celebration in late December.
- **Gayo highlands:** check weddings with *guel* and the coffee-harvest mood as two highland sources of joy.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas wedding entertainments and harvest days.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check *dampeng* and whether Christian festivals are shared in by neighbors.
- **Simeulue:** check island festivities and the songs sung at them.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet:** check which festive songs South Aceh villages sing, and in which language.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay merrymaking at weddings near the border.
- **Women and men:** check who may sing, dance or laugh aloud in which gatherings.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesian national happiness rankings; Acehnese communities abroad celebrating the festivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Happiness surveys and festival press exist; Acehnese words for joy and the felt experience are thin; Alas, Kluet and Tamiang celebrations barely printed.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay for Sufi remembrance texts; Acehnese for Maulid songs and *hikayat* humor; Gayo for *guel* song; Indonesian for surveys and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** limits on music, mixed dancing and New Year festivities go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); joy near grief after 2004 is delicate.
- **Held for review:** festivals and devotional processions kept by South Asian-heritage families in the old port, as a category only.
