# Aceh: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for religious texts; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: work, service, the daily and yearly round, the kept house and body, health, craft and rest.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how office hours and coffee-shop mornings divide the day for civil servants against traders.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check the weight of trading journeys against farming in household work plans.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen, North Aceh):** check how the gas and fertilizer plants changed who worked for wages, and what followed their decline.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh, Nagan Raya):** check fishing, rubber and the plantation economy, and how reconstruction jobs reshaped work after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands (Central Aceh, Bener Meriah, Gayo Lues):** check how the coffee year sets the highland round, and whether Gayo Lues differs from the lake district.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether rice and cacao work follows its own calendar and offices.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check nutmeg and coastal work in Tapaktuan and the Kluet valley.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check swamp, river and oil-palm work, and the place of Pakpak-speaking neighbors.
- **Simeulue:** check island cloves, buffalo and fishing as a separate work year.
- **Aceh Tamiang and Langsa:** check estate labor on the eastern plantations.
- **Javanese transmigrant villages:** check settler work customs carried into Central Aceh and Nagan Raya.
- **Comparison only:** routines of work, rest and clinic visits among Acehnese settled in Malaysia; Indonesia-wide labor and health averages as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich: surveys and colonial ethnography cover work and health; routine, rest and the kept house are thin outside theses.
- **Lands:** the post-war nation's territory awaits the author's Stage 0 (the tracker lists "Indonesia" alone as pre-war land); the work, health and service sheets of this sign assume the former province's districts.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for livelihood and season words; Gayo for highland farm terms; Dutch for the two colonial monographs; Jawi Malay for the court compilation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rules on work hours and dress at work go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit); conflict-era livelihoods and levies are delicate.
- **Held for review:** port labor and shopkeeping by South Asian-heritage families in the old harbor, listed only as a category.
