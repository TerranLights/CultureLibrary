## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how graduates weigh public posts, trade and NGO work, and how that changed after the reconstruction agencies left.
- **Pidie:** check the shopkeeping and trading tradition that sends men to other towns, and who keeps the fields meanwhile.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check memories of jobs at the gas plant and who was hired or passed over.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check beach-seine crews and plantation wage work as two different work worlds.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee harvest labor, and how work is shared between spouses and hired pickers.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check rice and cacao work, and whether Alas villages keep work-exchange customs of their own.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check nutmeg and coastal work, and whether Jamee households split labor differently from Acehnese ones.
- **Singkil:** check river transport, swamp gardens and palm-oil wage labor.
- **Simeulue:** check how clove, coconut and sea work rotate through the island year.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check estate labor and smallholder rubber on the eastern edge.
- **Women:** check the work women do in markets, gardens and crafts, and how surveys count it.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese contract workers in Malaysia; national labor-force averages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor statistics and cash crops; mixed for attitudes to work and idleness; thin for Alas, Kluet and Singkil work customs.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for the trade names and sayings on effort; Gayo for coffee-farm words; Dutch for de Houtman; Indonesian for labor tables and theses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** jobs tied to the gas enclave and to conflict-era levies are charged; labor rules and contracts go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** credit and hiring among South Asian-heritage traders in the old ports, as a category only.
