# Aceh: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil, Tamiang Malay, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how public-counter service and ministry work are judged, using the Ombudsman's district figures.
- **Pidie:** check which households still keep dayah pupils or relatives as live-in helpers, and on what terms.
- **North coast (Bireuen, Lhokseumawe):** check the service large *dayah* expect from pupils, and how alumni describe it later.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check who served as first responders in 2004 and whether their roles were later formalized.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how the four *sarak opat* offices share village service against the coastal *keuchik*-and-*teungku* pair.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan duties at feasts, and who serves whom by lineage.
- **Singkil:** check how Abdurrauf's home district tells his story of service, and what local shrine custodians do.
- **Simeulue:** check how island teachers and midwives are recruited and kept.
- **Aneuk Jamee:** check whether feast service and guest order follow Jamee customs or Acehnese ones.
- **Former combatants:** check how service to the independence struggle is spoken of, and by whom it is contested.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesian national civil-service norms; Acehnese domestic workers in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Offices and public-service complaints are documented; everyday service at feasts and in homes sits mostly in theses and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for van Langen; Arabic and Jawi Malay for Abdurrauf's manual; Gayo for *sarak opat* terms; Acehnese for hosting formulas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pupil service in *dayah* can shade into exploitation claims; service in the armed movement is politically charged; rules on public servants' duties go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** service to the sultans by South Asian-born court scholars and officials, as a category only.
