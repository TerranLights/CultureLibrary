# Aceh: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how urban parents pick schooling and languages for practical payoff against heritage.
- **Pidie:** check how families judge sons who stay home against sons who trade elsewhere.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe):** check how polytechnic graduates fare once industry jobs shrank.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Calang):** check how orphaned grandchildren and the elders who raise them divide household tasks.
- **Gayo Lues and Central Aceh:** check which forest and garden plants are named as useful and which as weeds.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check the work expected of elders and the young at rice planting.
- **Kluet:** check plant knowledge at the Leuser margin and who keeps it.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check river and swamp skills, and whether young people still learn them.
- **Simeulue:** check how buffalo and coconut skills pass between generations.
- **People with disabilities:** check whether the conflict and the 2004 tsunami changed how disability and work are handled.
- **Comparison only:** national elderly and disability statistics; Acehnese migrants' remittances from Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Elderly and disability statistics exist; "being useful" as a lived feeling is thin and mostly in theses; plant and animal knowledge is rich but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for Zarnuji and the Tanoh Abee handbooks; Acehnese for the dead-weight sayings; Kluet and Gayo for plant names; Indonesian for statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability, widowhood and orphanhood need care; some plant knowledge may be held by healers who do not share it freely.
- **Held for review:** practical manuals copied at the court by South Asian-born scholars, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Tanoh Abee catalog for handbooks on farming, building and healing, and list their subjects.

4. Find which *dayah* teach *Ta'lim al-Muta'allim* and at what level.
