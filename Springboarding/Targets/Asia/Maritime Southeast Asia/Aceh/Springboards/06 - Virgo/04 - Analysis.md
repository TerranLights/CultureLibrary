*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether the observatory's calculations and the scholars' sightings agree, and how the capital announces the result.
- **Pidie:** check how traders reckon prices and credit, and how they teach it to apprentices.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh):** check how large *dayah* teach logic and disputation, and whether their methods differ from the modern Islamic schools.
- **West coast (Nagan Raya):** check communities that set fasting dates by their own reckoning, and how they explain it.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how coffee growers measure quality and yield, and whom they trust for figures.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check rice measures and harvest counts in the valley.
- **Singkil:** check whether Abdurrauf's home area keeps its own tradition of scholarly method.
- **Simeulue:** check how fishers read sea and sky before sailing, and how they test it.
- **Aceh Tamiang and Langsa:** check gold and grain measures in Malay-speaking markets.
- **Schools:** check how public schools, Islamic schools and *dayah* differ on assessment scores.
- **Comparison only:** national assessment averages; Acehnese researchers abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. School and audit data are rich; native reckoning and riddles are thinly documented; *falak* manuscripts exist but are hard to reach.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for logic primers and *falak*; Jawi Malay for Kuta Karang's manuscripts; Dutch for Snouck's advisory reports; Acehnese for riddles and measures.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the fasting-date question can split communities; colonial analyses served conquest and must be read critically; any legal reasoning goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** works of reckoning and theology written at the court by South Asian-born scholars, listed as a category only.

3. Search repositories for "keuneunong menghitung", "mantiq dayah Aceh" and "hiem Aceh".
