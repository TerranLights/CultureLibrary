# Aceh: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how townspeople test news and rumor online, against older word-of-mouth checks.
- **Pidie:** check how the *seulangke*'s inquiry runs here, how long it lasts and who is consulted.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check which investment schemes reached the industrial towns, and who was taken in.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check how tsunami warnings and false alarms are judged in places that lost most in 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how coffee buyers and growers tell grades apart, and how marriage-form choices are weighed.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how Alas families vet a match and whether a go-between role exists.
- **Singkil:** check how Abdurrauf's teaching on judging others is taught in his home district today.
- **Simeulue:** check how islanders judged the 2004 sea signs, and whether the same test is applied to new warnings.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how Malay-speaking families judge gold and cloth in the eastern markets.
- **Religious minorities and new movements:** check how small groups labeled deviant describe the label, carefully and with consent.
- **Comparison only:** national fact-checking networks; Indonesia's halal agency as a frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Fatwas, certification and scam reports are documented; personal discernment (matches, character, taste) is thin and lives in theses and oral accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay for Abdurrauf and dream manuals; Gayo for marriage-form terms; Acehnese for the go-between's questions; Indonesian for press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "deviant sect" labels have led to harm, so tread carefully; matchmaking inquiries touch family honor; legal sanctions go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the court's tests for sorting sound from heretical teaching, as set by South Asian-born scholars in the 17th century; category only.
