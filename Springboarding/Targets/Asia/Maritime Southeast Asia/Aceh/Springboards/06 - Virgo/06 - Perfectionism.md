## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how students rate their own and their parents' standards on perfectionism scales.
- **Pidie:** check how *meukeutop* hat makers and embroiderers judge finished work and flawed pieces.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh):** check how large memorization *dayah* treat a pupil's errors.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Calang):** check how survivors judged the quality of the rebuilt houses, and whom they held responsible.
- **Gayo Lues:** check how *Saman* leaders correct a mistake in front of an audience, and whether they repeat the line.
- **Central Aceh and Bener Meriah:** check how embroidery cooperatives grade pieces for market.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether wedding rites must follow an exact order, and what is done after a slip.
- **Singkil:** check how recitation teachers in Abdurrauf's home district are themselves certified.
- **Simeulue:** check how boat builders and net makers judge sound work.
- **Aneuk Jamee (South Aceh):** check how Jamee seated dances are rehearsed and judged.
- **Comparison only:** national MTQ rules; Indonesian SNI quality standards.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Recitation and dance precision are well documented; perfectionism as distress has a few theses; household standards are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for *tajwid* rules and the Sufi ideal; Jawi Malay for the Sumatran treatises; Gayo for *Saman* teaching words; Indonesian for theses and contest rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** scrupulosity and student distress touch on mental health, so keep identities private; reconstruction blame is politically charged; legal standards go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Sufi treatises by South Asian scholars that shaped Sumatran teaching on the perfect human, as a category only.
