# Aceh: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how students, columnists and online critics address the provincial government, and which topics they avoid.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how scholars of the large *dayah* deliver counsel to officials, and whether it is ever public.
- **Pidie:** check how families on both sides of the 1946 upheaval speak of the scholars' criticism of the chiefs.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how local reporters covered abuses during the emergency years, and what they left out.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check whether reconstruction complaints went through village heads, press or protest.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *didong jalu* as a licensed space for mockery, and when clubs crossed the line.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether Alas verse forms carry teasing or reproach, and who may use them.
- **Singkil:** check how disputes over places of worship were voiced by each side, with great care.
- **Simeulue:** check how islanders press complaints on a distant district capital.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay *pantun* teasing at weddings as gentle correction.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese exiles who criticized from Sweden; national press-freedom rankings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, protest and conflict-era criticism; mixed for satire; thin for criticism inside families and workplaces.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *hikayat* rebuke and storytelling; Gayo for *didong jalu* verses; Arabic for the counsel frame; Indonesian for press and union reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalists and critics have faced threats; the 1946 upheaval still divides families; criticism of religious rules and its prosecution goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** public rebukes and the burning of rival teachers' books urged at court by South Asian-born scholars, kept as a category only.
