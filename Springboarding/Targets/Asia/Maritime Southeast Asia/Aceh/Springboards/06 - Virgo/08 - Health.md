# Aceh: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Kluet, Aneuk Jamee, Singkil, Tamiang Malay, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for prophetic medicine and healing verses; Dutch for colonial medical records. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how the main referral hospital, private clinics and recitation healers share patients, and how people move between them.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check how the conflict's distress shows up in clinic visits and in healers' rooms.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check health claims tied to the industrial zone and how residents voice them.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check how the rebuilt clinics are used by villages that lost their health workers in 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check highland healers and remedies, and how coffee farm work and cold climate enter talk of illness.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas birth customs and village healers around Kutacane.
- **South Aceh (Kluet, Aneuk Jamee):** check forest remedies and who still prepares them.
- **Singkil:** check how swamp and river villages reach care, and which illnesses are named locally.
- **Simeulue:** check referral journeys to the mainland and island remedies used meanwhile.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay healers and estate clinics on the plantations.
- **Comparison only:** national health averages; Acehnese patients who travel to Penang or Kuala Lumpur for treatment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, programs and conflict mental health; mixed for healing practice; thin for Alas, Kluet and Singkil healing.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for spirit and vow terms; Arabic and Jawi Malay for medical manuscripts and recitation; Gayo and Alas for highland healers; Dutch for colonial medical files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mental illness and shackling, healers' secret formulas, and childbirth deaths need care and consent; rules on medicines and drugs go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** remedies and healers from South Asian-heritage families in the old port, as a category only.

1. Download the latest *Profil Kesehatan Aceh* and note health workers and facilities by district.
2. Read the IOM psychosocial assessment and Samuels on post-tsunami recovery.
3. Search repositories for "kemitraan bidan dukun Aceh", "pengobatan rajah" and "kaoy nazar".
