# Aceh: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Singkil, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Devayan; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch for colonial schooling and policy. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how scholarship returnees and NGO veterans speak of improving the province, and whom they credit.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how *dayah* adding general subjects describe the change to parents.
- **Pidie:** check how *ikhtiyar* and *takdir* are weighed in households that send members away to trade.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how post-industrial towns frame their next "development" after the gas era.
- **West coast (Lhoknga, Aceh Jaya):** check how families altered their aid houses, and what those changes say about a better home.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the language-revitalization effort and whether highlanders see the proposed new province as betterment.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether the Ladia Galaska road and district plans are seen locally as gain or loss.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how the district splits of 1999 and 2007 are judged as improvement.
- **Simeulue:** check how islanders weigh development money against keeping island ways.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how oil-palm expansion is talked of as progress or damage.
- **Comparison only:** national development rhetoric; Acehnese scholarship graduates who stayed abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for reconstruction and development spending; mixed for religious self-improvement (Kloos and theses); thin for self-help markets and the smaller peoples' own ideas of betterment.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay for the Syattariyah manuals; Dutch for colonial school records; Gayo for revitalization materials; Indonesian for budget and donor reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** province-splitting proposals for the highlands and the west are politically charged; "improvement" of others through religious policy touches enforcement, which goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** reformist teaching at the court by South Asian-born scholars on correcting popular belief, held as a category only.
