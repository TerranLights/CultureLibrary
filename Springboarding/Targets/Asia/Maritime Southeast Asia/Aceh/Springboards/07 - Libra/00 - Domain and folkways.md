# Aceh: The domain: partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for jurisprudence; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** what a fair bargain and a good partnership are, across bonds, contracts, enemies, disputes, civility, marriage and gender, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check where a neighbor quarrel goes first (village head, sharia court, police), city against hinterland.
- **Pidie:** check how gold payments and a daughter's house set marriage terms where men travel to trade.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check written land deals and compensation disputes around the gas fields.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check fair-share rules in fishing and in post-2004 aid.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how the two marriage forms and the village's four offices divide rights between in-laws.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan rules on marriage partners and on who answers for a kinsman's wrong.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South Aceh):** check marriage residence and inheritance around Tapaktuan and the Kluet valley.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check the old Tamiang realm's titles and written Malay customary rules.
- **Singkil and Pulau Banyak:** check quarrel rules between Muslim and Christian villages, and among Haloban speakers.
- **Simeulue:** check island elders and marriage payments in Devayan and Sigulai areas.
- **Javanese settlers and Chinese-Acehnese traders:** check which written provisions apply to non-Muslims under the *qanun*.
- **Comparison only:** how Acehnese in Malaysia and Scandinavia register marriages; Indonesian family law as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, judgments and the peace process; mixed for marriage and gender; thin for the small peoples' own rules.
- **Territory not yet fixed:** the tracker gives "Indonesia" as the sole pre-war land, so post-war Aceh's borders still await the author's ruling; the Libra pointers follow the twenty-three districts and cities of the former province.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for sayings and rites; Gayo for kin terms; Jawi Malay for the court manual; Dutch for colonial reports; Indonesian for statutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** caning and criminal judgments involve living people; conflict-era testimony is traumatic; the spirit of law (legitimacy, obedience) belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** jurisprudence and judgeship manuals written at the Aceh court by South Asian-born scholars, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the UUPA, the Helsinki text and Qanun 9/2008 and 10/2008, and list every partnership, marriage and dispute provision.
2. Search the decision directory for Mahkamah Syar'iyah Takengon, Kutacane and Sinabang marriage cases.
3. Read Hadi and the *Mir'at al-Tullab* sections on judging and sales.
4. Search "adat perkawinan" with each people's name, then read the KKR Aceh hearing reports.
