# Aceh: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for penal jurisprudence; English for rights reports. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check where and how often public caning is staged, against outlying districts.
- **Pidie:** check what the Rumoh Geudong memorial and victims' files ask of the state.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check the Simpang KKA victims' written demands and court records.
- **South Aceh and the Kluet valley:** check the Jambo Keupok case and how village hearings are written up there.
- **Gayo highlands (Bener Meriah, Central Aceh):** check conflict-era killings between rebel and militia sides, and the highland words for compensation.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether a wrong is answered between clans or between households.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how the code's clause on non-Muslims is applied to Christian residents.
- **West coast fishing villages:** check written fines of the sea-custom courts under the *Panglima Laot*.
- **Simeulue:** check whether village rules there provide expulsion from the village, and in what words.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay customary fines near the border with North Sumatra's jurisdiction.
- **Women complainants:** check how rape and harassment cases are filed under the *qanun* against the national code.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's new criminal code (in force from 2026) as the national yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, rights reports and conflict cases; mixed for village hearings; thin for Alas, Simeulue and Singkil.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for the *qanun* and judgments; Arabic for penal categories; Acehnese for compensation sayings; English for rights reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** caning and conflict-era files name living victims and accused; obedience to and trust in the courts go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** penal rulings and heresy trials urged at the 17th-century court by South Asian-born scholars, as a category only.

3. Search "pedoman peradilan adat" and "diyat sayam" in Aceh university repositories.
4. Read the Komnas HAM summaries on Simpang KKA, Rumoh Geudong and Jambo Keupok.
