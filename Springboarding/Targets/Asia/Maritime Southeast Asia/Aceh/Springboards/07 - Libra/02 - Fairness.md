# Aceh: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for inheritance shares. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pidie and Aceh Besar:** check how a daughter's house gift is weighed against her brothers' shares in estate quarrels.
- **Gayo highlands and Southeast Aceh (ALA area):** check the written arguments of the campaign for a separate province about the share of funds.
- **West and southwest coast (ABAS area):** check the same claims from Meulaboh to Tapaktuan, and how they differ from the highland case.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how shares of gas revenue were argued in the 1970s to 1990s.
- **Fishing ports (Lampulo, Idi Rayeuk, Kuala Bubon):** check catch-share rules port by port.
- **Tsunami-hit west coast (Aceh Jaya, Calang):** check complaints about house and boat allocation.
- **Former combatants and civilian victims:** check how each group's claims on reintegration money were set out.
- **Alas villages:** check how clan land is divided among sons and daughters.
- **Simeulue:** check clove and coconut harvest shares between owners and workers.
- **Tamiang and Langsa estates:** check written wage and bonus rules on oil-palm plantations.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese laborers' pay disputes in Malaysia; Indonesia's national compilation of Islamic law as the inheritance yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inheritance cases and aid disputes; mixed for share contracts; thin for children's fairness and for island and Alas customs.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for court rulings and budgets; Jawi Malay and Arabic for the inheritance manuals; Acehnese for share-contract terms; Gayo for highland estates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** estate files name families; reintegration money is politically charged; the ALA and ABAS campaigns are contested.
- **Held for review:** goods shared out among South Asian-heritage merchant households in the port, as a category only.
