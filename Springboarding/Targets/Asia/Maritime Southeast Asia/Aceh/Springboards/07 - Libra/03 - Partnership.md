# Aceh: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for contract forms; Ottoman Turkish and English for the sultanate's alliances. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pidie:** check whether risk on a trading journey rests on a business partner or on the household left behind.
- **Banda Aceh:** check how couples hold accounts and loans under the all-sharia banking rule.
- **Gayo highlands:** check husband-and-wife farming of coffee and who signs cooperative membership.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how clan-to-clan marriages are written up as alliances between *merga*.
- **Aneuk Jamee villages (South and Southwest Aceh):** check where newlyweds live, and with whose kin.
- **Simeulue:** check the roles of *nandong* singers at island weddings, and fishing partnerships between households.
- **Singkil and Pulau Banyak:** check what the written rules allow for marriages across Muslim and Christian families.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay marriage alliances that reach across the border into Langkat.
- **Conflict and tsunami widows (west coast, Pidie, North Aceh):** check the paperwork of remarriage when a spouse's death was never certified.
- **Former rebel leaders and the central government:** check the joint bodies the 2005 text created and how each side's role was written.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese households in Kedah and Selangor (Malaysia), for marriages contracted abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for banking and aid partnerships; mixed for marriage and divorce; thin for alliances between clans and for island partnerships.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for statutes and judgments; Acehnese and Jawi for the romance and the proposal terms; Ottoman Turkish and English for the alliance papers; Gayo for highland marriage forms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the polygamy draft is politically charged; divorce files name couples; conflict widows' cases involve the missing.
- **Held for review:** written partnership deeds among South Asian-heritage traders in the sultanate's harbor (a category, unnamed).
