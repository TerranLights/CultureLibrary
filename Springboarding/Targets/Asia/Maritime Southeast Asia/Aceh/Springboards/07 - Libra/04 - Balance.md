# Aceh: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic and Jawi Malay for Sufi and medical texts. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how the written roles of governor, legislature, Wali Nanggroe and scholars' council are argued in the capital's press.
- **Pidie and the north coast:** check how far local parties outpoll national ones, district by district, across five elections.
- **Gayo Lues:** check who may sit in a *saman* line and how unison is taught.
- **Central Aceh (Lake Laut Tawar):** check lake-fishing limits and who enforces them.
- **Simeulue:** check how *smong* verse is taught, and to whom.
- **Southeast Aceh and the Leuser edge:** check forest-use rules where villages border the national park.
- **West and southwest coast:** check whether Aneuk Jamee and Acehnese mothers follow different postpartum warming rules.
- **Singkil swamps:** check rules for the Rawa Singkil reserve against plantation claims.
- **Traditional *dayah* and modern schools:** check how each curriculum orders law and the Sufi path.
- **Women in farming households:** check the double load of field and home in district labor and time data.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national checks between legislature, president and constitutional court, as a yardstick for Aceh's own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elections and statutes; mixed for folk medicine and dance; thin for words of balance in Alas, Tamiang and the island languages.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for Hamzah and the remedy books; Acehnese for hot-cool and ritual terms; Gayo for *saman* lyrics; Devayan for *smong* verse.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sufi teachings of union remain a disputed subject; healers may keep remedies private; reckoning of auspicious days is a living practice, to be asked about with care.
- **Held for review:** rulings that weighed outward law against mystic teaching, issued at court by South Asian-born scholars (category, no titles).
