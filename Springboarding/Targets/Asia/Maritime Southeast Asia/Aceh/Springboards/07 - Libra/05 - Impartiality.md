# Aceh: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Jawi Malay and Arabic for court and juristic texts. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check which provincial agencies draw the most favoritism complaints, and from whom.
- **Pidie, Bireuen and North Aceh:** check village-fund prosecutions and village-election disputes over kin on committees.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how *reje* elections and the four village seats are filled where few lineages dominate.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether a judge from a party's own clan may hear a *kute* case.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how permit decisions affecting Christian villages are written up and appealed.
- **Simeulue:** check hiring complaints in a small island civil service.
- **Aceh Tamiang and Langsa:** check land-office and plantation-permit decisions brought to the Ombudsman.
- **Firms tied to former commanders:** check how audits and courts recorded contract awards after 2005.
- **Javanese settler villages:** check equal treatment in land certificates and aid lists.
- **Women on village councils:** check how women are seated, or not, in written procedure.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national civil-service neutrality rules and the KPK's national case mix, as the yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption cases and watchdog reports; mixed for election supervision; thin for kinship rules in Alas and island hearings.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for judgments, audits and watchdog reports; classical Malay in Jawi for the mirror for princes; Acehnese for sayings on just and oppressive rulers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations against named living people; former combatants' business ties; how far residents trust officials goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** favor shown at the sultanate to South Asian-born advisers and judges over local scholars, as a category only.
