*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how much young couples, against parents, settle a match and a wedding date.
- **Aceh Besar and Pidie:** check whether a village meeting may close without full agreement, and how often items are tabled.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how long land decisions for the industrial zone stayed pending.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Lhoknga, Lamno):** check relocation choices after 2004 and who in the household made them.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether the "full agreement" pillar requires everyone, and what a holdout does.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check which clan elders are consulted before a marriage or land choice.
- **Simeulue:** check how quickly islanders choose to climb when the ground shakes, in post-2004 evacuation studies.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how long building-permit decisions for houses of worship stayed open.
- **Students in Banda Aceh and Lhokseumawe:** check career and study choices and the share left to parents.
- **Families of *dayah* graduates and of state-school graduates:** check whom each turns to before a hard choice.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national budget and regulation deadlocks as a yardstick for the provincial ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for budget and flag deadlocks; moderate for disaster decisions; thin for personal indecision and for Alas and island practice.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for statutes, budgets and theses; Jawi Malay for the reckoning booklets; Acehnese for the war verse; Gayo for village-pillar terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the flag dispute is politically charged; reckoning good days is a living practice some clerics reject; forced-marriage cases involve minors.
- **Held for review:** nothing here; the deadlock, delay and deferral leads above raised no case under the standing rules.
