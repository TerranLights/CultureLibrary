# Aceh: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for repentance and accusation terms; Dutch for colonial reports. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how punks, foreign workers and religious rivals are named in the city's sermons and press.
- **Pidie and North Aceh:** check the enemy images each side put out during the conflict, and which ones outlived it.
- **Lhokseumawe:** check how police statements and local press framed the 2018 raids.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Calang):** check the child-trafficking and conversion rumors after 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the names highlanders and coastal Acehnese use for each other.
- **Javanese settlers in Bener Meriah and Central Aceh:** check how they were named as outsiders in the conflict years.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how Christian neighbors appear in rumor and in official statements.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island explanations of the 1907 and 2004 waves blame sin, fate or outsiders.
- **Chinese-Acehnese of Peunayong:** check how they appear in press coverage during crises and price rises.
- **Women charged under morality rules:** check whom reports blame in *khalwat* cases, the woman or the man.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia's national anticommunist imagery as the yardstick for Aceh's 1965 record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for conflict, 1965 and post-tsunami material; moderate for religious disputes; thin for folk blame in Alas, Singkil and the island languages.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for the colonial inquiry; Indonesian for army statements and press; Jawi Malay for Hamzah's verse; Gayo for highland names of coastal people.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual minorities, punks and accused "deviant" groups face real danger; 1965 and conflict records name perpetrators and victims; sorcery accusations can incite violence.
- **Held for review:** how residents and students reacted in 2023 to refugee boats from camps in South Asia (a category, not a pointer).
