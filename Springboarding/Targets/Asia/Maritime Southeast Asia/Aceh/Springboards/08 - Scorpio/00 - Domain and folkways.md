# Aceh: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for funeral and inheritance texts; Dutch for colonial-era records. **Concept in one line:** how death, a person's change of state and what is kept out of sight are faced, across death ways, inheritance, shared resources, sex ways, secrets and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how the tsunami changed burial and memorial practice in the capital against village custom inland.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check inheritance where a daughter's house is settled at marriage, and how the 2016 earthquake dead were mourned.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh, Bireuen):** check conflict graves and memory in the gas-field districts.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Meulaboh):** check how fishing villages with heavy losses rebuilt cemeteries and settled inheritance claims.
- **Gayo highlands:** check reformist and traditionalist arguments over feasts for the dead, recorded in Takengon.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan-based funeral duties and inheritance in the Kutacane valley.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South Aceh):** check whether property customs there follow a different line of descent.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay funeral customs and the old realm's burial grounds.
- **Singkil:** check burial practice in Christian villages and around Abdurrauf's family lands.
- **Simeulue:** check how *smong* verse sits beside funeral custom on the island.
- **Chinese-Acehnese of Peunayong and Javanese settlers:** check their own burial grounds and rites.
- **Comparison only:** Muslim funeral practice elsewhere in Indonesia as a yardstick; Acehnese buried abroad in Malaysia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the tsunami and conflict; mixed for everyday funerals and inheritance; thin for sex ways and for the Alas, Kluet and island peoples.
- **Lands:** the tracker names only "Indonesia" as the carried land, so post-war Aceh's borders remain the author's call; this sign's sheets follow the former province's districts and islands.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for funeral and spirit words; Jawi Malay and Arabic for Hamzah's verse and inheritance texts; Devayan for island memory; Dutch for colonial reports; Indonesian for statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mass graves, the missing and identifying remains; sex ways fall under provincial statute (letter in Libra, spirit in Capricorn).
