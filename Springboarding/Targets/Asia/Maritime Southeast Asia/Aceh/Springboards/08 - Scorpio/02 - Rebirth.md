# Aceh: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for Sufi and creed terms. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending, from conversion, recovery and redemption to renewal after disaster, cultural revival and the return of the dead.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check which rebuilt quarters residents call new, and which they call lost.
- **Aceh Besar (Lampuuk, Lhoknga):** check how villages rebuilt by foreign donors describe their own renewal.
- **Southwest coast (Labuhan Haji, Abdya):** check who enters Naqsyabandi seclusion and whether it is a once-in-a-life turn.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe):** check how former gas workers and former fighters each speak of starting over.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check how former combatants rejoin village life and whether elders accept them.
- **Gayo highlands and Gayo Lues:** check how the *Saman* revival is led and how youths join it.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether youth rites of passage are kept or revived.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check *tepung tawar* at a new house, a return from pilgrimage or a recovery.
- **Chinese-Acehnese converts:** check how a family receives a member who converts.
- **Singkil and Pulau Banyak:** check how island villages rebuilt after the 2004 and 2005 quakes.
- **Comparison only:** returnees from the Malaysian and Scandinavian diaspora after 2005; Indonesia's national rehab framework.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for reconstruction and reintegration; mixed for Sufi practice; thin for Alas and Kluet rites, and for the experience of converts.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for Abdurrauf's treatises; Gayo for the revival and the tale; Acehnese for the didactic verse; Indonesian for agency reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** do not question individuals about leaving Islam; Sufi orders may keep initiation teaching to members; people in drug rehab carry legal exposure (its letter is Libra's, its spirit Capricorn's).
- **Held for review:** conversion stories in the sultanate's founding legends that credit preachers from South Asia, as a category only.
