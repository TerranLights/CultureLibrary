# Aceh: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for purity law and exorcism terms. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check who leads public cleansing actions in the city (police, Wilayatul Hisbah, ulama) and how residents respond.
- **Aceh Besar:** check whether *Rabu Abeh* bathing at the shore was taken up again after 2004.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check how families of the old chiefs and of the 1946 revolutionaries each tell the purge.
- **North coast and East Aceh:** check how the 1965-1966 killings and the expulsion of 1966 are remembered locally.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Meulaboh):** check how mining villages talk about mercury and blame.
- **Gayo highlands and Gayo Lues:** check purification practice in coffee villages and their debates over local rites.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check cleansing rites before weddings or after a death.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South Aceh):** check whether a bath before the fasting month is kept, and under what name.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check the Malay lime-bath and who leads it.
- **Simeulue:** check rites for driving off sickness or spirits on the island.
- **Chinese-Acehnese:** check family memory of 1966 and how return to the province was negotiated.
- **Comparison only:** national anti-corruption cases elsewhere in Indonesia; Malaysian Acehnese memories of 1965.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political purges and corruption cases; mixed for exorcism and seasonal rites; thin for Alas, Kluet and island practice.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese to follow rite names and their arguments; Jawi Malay and Arabic for purity manuals and *ruqyah* texts; Indonesian for fatwas and court reporting; French and Dutch for the early-modern accounts behind Reid's essay.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors and perpetrators of 1965-1966 may both be alive; followers of banned teachings face pressure; the punishment of moral offenses as statute is Libra's and as purpose Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** the 17th-century purge of followers of the unity-of-being teaching, as argued at court by its South Asian-born instigator; a category only.
