# Aceh: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for creed terms on God's decree. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so; self-control as discipline is Capricorn's, freedom from control is Aries's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how residents, especially young women, describe street supervision and curfews, and how this differs by neighborhood.
- **Lhokseumawe and North Aceh:** check how district conduct rules were received, and memories of military checkpoints along the highway.
- **Pidie:** check who controls household gold and land when husbands migrate for trade.
- **Bireuen:** check how cafés and markets adjusted to local rules on mixed company.
- **West coast and Nagan Raya:** check control of peat land and fire between villagers and plantation companies.
- **Gayo highlands:** check who controls coffee income within the household, and village oversight of youth.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas) and the Leuser fringe:** check village control of forest and wildlife against park rules.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South Aceh):** check how household authority is divided where descent customs may differ.
- **Simeulue:** check how the island's preparedness habits are taught to children.
- **Singkil:** check how Christian residents experience provincial conduct rules.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese students abroad on living outside village supervision; Indonesian national parenting and disaster data as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military control and religious policing; mixed for household and parenting control; thin for Alas, Kluet and island practice.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for the court compilation and creed primers; Arabic for *kasb* and decree; Indonesian for rights and NGO data; Acehnese for proverbs on fate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** people under religious and military supervision may not speak freely; partner-violence data needs consent; what conduct rules say is Libra's, and their legitimacy Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** port rules applied to South Asian-heritage merchants under the sultanate's harbor master, as a category only.
