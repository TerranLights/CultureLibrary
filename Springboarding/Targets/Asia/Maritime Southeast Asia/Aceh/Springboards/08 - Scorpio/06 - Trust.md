# Aceh: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether city residents trust strangers, neighbors and banks differently from village kin.
- **Pidie:** check trust between traders who migrate and the kin who mind their shops and lands.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, Bireuen):** check trust between villagers and former fighters who now hold contracts and posts.
- **West coast fishing villages:** check how crews and *toke bangku* judge each other's reliability.
- **Gayo highlands:** check growers' trust in cooperatives against private buyers.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether clan ties set who may be trusted with land or money.
- **Aneuk Jamee and Kluet (South Aceh):** check trust between speakers of the two languages in shared markets.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check trust across the provincial border with North Sumatra's Malay towns.
- **Simeulue:** check whether elders' warnings carry more weight with young people there than official alerts.
- **Singkil:** check trust between Muslim and Christian villages after church closures.
- **Javanese settlers:** check how returning settler families rebuilt trust with neighbors after displacement.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese traders in Malaysia and their credit networks; national BPS figures as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for post-conflict surveys and banking; mixed for village governance; thin for Alas, Kluet and island trust, and for women's savings circles.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the royal letters; Indonesian for BPS and donor surveys; Acehnese to hear *keude kupi* talk and fishing-credit terms; Gayo for cooperative meetings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** distrust of the state and of former fighters can be risky to voice; trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn's spirit of the law.
- **Held for review:** safe-conduct letters the sultanate granted to ships from South Asian ports, as a category only.
