# Aceh: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for Sufi terms of the hidden; Dutch for colonial intelligence. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how urban families guard health and marriage secrets in a city where neighbors and officials both watch.
- **Pidie:** check what villages still decline to say about the conflict years, and to whom they do speak.
- **North Aceh and Lhokseumawe:** check how the gas industry's security zone kept information from residents.
- **West coast and Aceh Jaya:** check how *pawang laot* hand on sea knowledge and what stays within a lineage.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether highland healers and Sufi teachers restrict their lore, and in what words.
- **Southeast Aceh and the Leuser fringe (Alas, Kluet hunters):** check forest and game knowledge held back from outsiders and park staff.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check which Malay taboos are passed on without reasons given, and by whom.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island specialists keep knowledge that mainland Acehnese do not share.
- **Singkil:** check how Christian congregations decide what to make public about their worship.
- **Women of the former women's wing:** check what they now disclose and to whom.
- **Comparison only:** exile networks in Sweden and their archives; Indonesian national secrecy laws as a yardstick (the letter to Libra).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial and conflict secrecy; mixed for Sufi and *pawang* lore; thin for Gayo, Alas and island specialists, and deliberately so where knowledge is restricted.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for the adviser's memoranda; Jawi Malay and Arabic for Hamzah's treatises; Acehnese for *pawang* and charm terms; Indonesian for rights findings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** do not retell charm, invulnerability or initiation content, even if offered; HIV status, sexuality and conflict roles can expose people to harm; the law on state secrets is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** initiation chains of Sufi orders in Aceh that pass through teachers from South Asia, as a category only.
