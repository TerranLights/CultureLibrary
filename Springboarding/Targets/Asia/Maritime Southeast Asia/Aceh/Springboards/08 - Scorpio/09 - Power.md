# Aceh: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic for sovereignty and sanctity terms; Dutch for colonial administration. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how it is judged who holds it, how it is held and how it is used; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how the old court quarter, the governor's office and the *Wali Nanggroe* palace each stage power for visitors.
- **Pidie:** check where *uleebalang* descendants, ulama families and former commanders each hold sway today.
- **North Aceh and Lhokseumawe:** check how the former rebels' party and contractors relate to village heads.
- **West and southwest coast (Meulaboh, Labuhan Haji):** check the political weight of large *dayah* and their leaders.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how highland parties and leaders position themselves against coastal parties.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how clan seniority and elected office coexist.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how descendants of the old Malay realm are treated in local politics.
- **Simeulue and Singkil:** check how outlying districts bargain with the provincial capital for funds.
- **Women in politics:** check how women candidates and officials gain and keep office.
- **Javanese settler districts:** check who represents settlers in district councils.
- **Comparison only:** exiled leaders' networks in Sweden before 2005; Indonesian national parties' branches in Aceh as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court history, the conflict and elections; mixed for spiritual power; thin for Alas, Tamiang and Simeulue realms.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the court chronicle; Indonesian for audits and election records; Acehnese for *keuramat* and title usage; Gayo for the Linge histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticizing powerful former commanders or ulama can carry risk for informants; legitimacy and obedience are Capricorn's, and the written powers of offices are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** the rise and sudden fall of a South Asian-born scholar's faction at the 17th-century court, as a category only.
