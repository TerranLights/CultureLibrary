## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether the university town and the old dayah belt teach the same account of where knowledge comes from.
- **Pidie:** check how the old quarrel between scholars and chiefs is retold by each side's descendants.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh, Bireuen):** check how the Pasai past is used in teaching near the old kingdom's site.
- **West coast (Nagan Raya, Aceh Jaya):** check the Syattariyah community of Nagan Raya against neighbors on the provincial religious calendar.
- **Gayo highlands:** check where speakers place *edet* beside religion when they explain how life should run.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how Muslim and Christian villages each tell the arrival of their faith.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check how far the Labuhan Haji dayah's teaching line reaches along that coast.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether Malay teaching lines there face North Sumatra rather than Banda Aceh.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how claims on Hamzah and Abdurrauf shape local religious pride.
- **Simeulue:** check founding-teacher tales on the island against the mainland's.
- **Chinese-Acehnese and Javanese settlers:** check their temples, churches and mosques as separate worlds.
- **Comparison only:** national religiosity surveys as a yardstick; Acehnese in Malaysia and Scandinavia for how belief travels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islamic scholarship and post-2004 change; mixed for freedom ways, which the conflict literature treats mostly as politics; thin for Alas, Kluet, Singkil and island belief.
- **Lands:** with only "Indonesia" given as the carried pre-war land, the post-war nation's map is not yet drawn; the belief-and-philosophy sheets take the old province's districts and islands as their working core.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for chronicle and dayah texts; Acehnese for village rites; Gayo for highland explanations of custom; Dutch for colonial religious reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority worship, "deviant sect" labels and conversion; religious enforcement belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** the strands of the Pasai conversion story and of later court learning that pass through South Asia, as a category only.
