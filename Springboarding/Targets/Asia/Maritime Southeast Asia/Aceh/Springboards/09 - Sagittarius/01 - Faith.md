*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether students and civil servants practice faith through study circles more than through village rites.
- **Aceh Besar and Pidie:** check vow-making and *ratéb* groups in dayah villages against town mosques.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh, Lhokseumawe):** check how the big dayah of Samalanga draws pilgrims of learning from across the province.
- **Nagan Raya and the west coast:** check the Seunagan calendar against neighboring villages' observance.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how Takengon's reform-minded and custom-minded households differ in what worship must include.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how Muslim and Christian Alas families keep separate observance within shared clans.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check Naqshbandi-Khalidi retreat-keepers against other villagers in fasting months.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay recitation traditions against those of Acehnese-speaking newcomers.
- **Singkil:** check how Pakpak-speaking Christians and Singkil Muslims each describe faith passed from parents.
- **Simeulue:** check how island congregations hold to faith with few resident scholars.
- **Chinese-Acehnese:** check Buddhist and Christian practice in Peunayong families.
- **Comparison only:** national piety indexes; Acehnese in Malaysia who keep home-district recitation groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islamic devotion and the post-tsunami years; mixed for measured piety; thin for Christian and Buddhist minorities and for island practice.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for Syamsuddin and the *syair*; Acehnese for vow and recitation terms; Gayo for Bowen's field material; Pakpak-related speech for Singkil churches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conversion and apostasy are charged subjects; minority believers may not speak freely; rules on dress and worship go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** devotional texts and saints' cults that reached Aceh through South Asian intermediaries, kept only as a category.
