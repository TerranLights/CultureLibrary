# Aceh: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether students and office workers name career and family where dayah graduates name worship as the point of life.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how families of the drowned coastal villages speak of purpose two decades on.
- **Pidie:** check how merchants who spend years away say their trading gives life a point.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how the end of the gas boom changed what young men say they live for.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check fishing families' sense of purpose after rebuilding.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how Gayo poets and farmers each answer what a life is for.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check purpose in Alas oral verse beside Christian and Muslim teaching in the valley.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check whether Kluet and Aneuk Jamee advice verse differ on a good life.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check *pantun* advice on life's aim against Acehnese sayings on the same theme.
- **Singkil:** check what young people say keeps them in the district or sends them away.
- **Simeulue:** check meaning in island life where elders' songs carry advice.
- **Comparison only:** whether Acehnese laborers in Malaysia describe their years abroad as giving life a point; Indonesia-wide youth data only to scale Aceh's figures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for religious teaching and post-disaster studies; thin for measured meaning below the province level and for Kluet, Alas and island voices.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for Hamzah's prose; Acehnese for sayings and sermons; Gayo for L.K. Ara's verse; Tamiang Malay for *pantun*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, drug use and ex-combatants' despair; take care with names and with families.
- **Held for review:** for the why-we-exist question, 17th-century answers given in Aceh by mystics and their critics born in South Asia (category; no titles).
