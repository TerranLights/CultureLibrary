# Aceh: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian; Dutch. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal (self-rule as personal autonomy and independence stays in Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check campus debate on speech against what students say they can say at home.
- **Pidie and Aceh Besar:** check whether former prisoners and their kin count release as freedom regained in full or only in part.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how gas-field villages recall the checkpoints that fenced movement.
- **West coast fishing towns:** check crews' stories of detention abroad and coming home.
- **Gayo highlands:** check Rimba Raya memory beside highland groups that opposed the independence movement.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check what the separate-province drive meant to Alas villages as freedom from the coast.
- **South Aceh and Southwest Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check how ABAS supporters phrased self-rule.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how traders remember conflict-era limits on daily crossings to Langkat's markets.
- **Singkil:** check how Christians and Muslims each describe the freedom to build and use places of worship.
- **Simeulue:** check whether islanders felt the conflict's limits on movement as mainlanders did.
- **Chinese-Acehnese and Javanese settlers:** check what each lost and regained in freedom of residence during and after the conflict.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese asylum communities in Sweden and Denmark, for freedom as exiles define it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, conflict and rights monitoring; mixed for religious freedom; thin for pre-modern bondage and for Alas, Kluet and island views.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for bondage reports; Acehnese for songs and family memories; Gayo for Rimba Raya oral history; Indonesian for indexes and NGO reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** former detainees, informants and banned songs; independence talk is still delicate; dress, curfew and speech statutes go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** bondsmen and traders from South Asian ports in the sultanate's records of the free and the bound, as a category only.
