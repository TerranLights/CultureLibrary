*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether campus scientists and dayah teachers are asked different kinds of questions by the same families.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how the dayah villages near the capital rank a teacher's word against an official's.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check what residents believed during the 2016 aftershock rumors, and from whom.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check which conflict histories villages hold as true against the official record.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check how coastal towns judge warnings from the agency against warnings from the mosque.
- **Gayo highlands:** check Gayo storytellers' versions of origins against school history.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Alas clan histories against church and mosque accounts of the valley's past.
- **South Aceh (Kluet, Aneuk Jamee):** check origin tales of the two peoples for points where they contradict.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check what the seminar's host district holds about where its faith came from.
- **Singkil:** check Singkil claims about its scholars against outside histories.
- **Simeulue:** check how island elders' memory of the wave is now treated in schools.
- **Comparison only:** national fact-checking outlets, to see how Aceh rumors are handled from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scripture study, colonial records and disaster science; mixed for news trust; thin for the told histories of Alas, Kluet and Singkil.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for the commentary and Sufi texts; Dutch for Snouck's advice; Acehnese for sayings and Wikipedia; Gayo for *kekeberen*; Devayan for island testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputed conflict histories; accusing someone of *fitnah*; truth-testing oaths in disputes belong to Libra, and trust in courts to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** theories that place the source of Aceh's Islam in South Asia, and the 1980 seminar's treatment of them, as a category only.
