*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian; Dutch; Chinese for temple materials. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true, about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and the warrant wanted for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how campus students rank dayah creed teaching beside what their universities teach.
- **Aceh Besar:** check *meurukon* troupes for the beliefs they rehearse and who coaches them.
- **Pidie:** check how reform-minded and dayah-minded families sort customs into belief and superstition.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh):** check whether creed teaching in large dayah differs from village *meunasah* lessons.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Nagan Raya):** check omen-reading among fishers against what preachers there call superstition.
- **Gayo highlands:** check what remains of the spirit beliefs Snouck recorded, and how Gayo explain them now.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how the old beliefs are remembered differently in Muslim and Christian households.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check whether the two peoples keep different omen lore.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay belief customs and their names against Acehnese ones.
- **Singkil:** check how Pakpak Christians and Singkil Muslims speak of a shared older layer.
- **Simeulue:** check belief in holy graves against official preaching on the island.
- **Chinese-Acehnese:** check which beliefs older and younger Peunayong people say they keep, and which they let go.
- **Comparison only:** youth-attitude results for other provinces, read only to place Aceh's answers on creed and tolerance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for creed texts and deviance rulings; mixed for youth surveys; thin for older Alas, Pakpak and island beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for creed treatises; Acehnese for *meurukon* and creed verse; Gayo and Dutch for the highland baseline; Chinese for temple notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** people accused of heresy, hidden doubters and converts are at risk if named; blasphemy and *aqidah* statutes go to Libra, their purpose to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** treatises at the Aceh court that judged the unity-of-being belief, by South Asian-born authors, and South Asian-founded preaching movements active in Aceh, both as categories only.
