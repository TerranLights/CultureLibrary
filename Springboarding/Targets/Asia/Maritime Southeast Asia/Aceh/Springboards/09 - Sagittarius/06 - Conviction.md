# Aceh: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (a finding of guilt is not this concept).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how activists in the capital explain why they keep vigils that bring no result.
- **Aceh Besar and Pidie:** check how families who followed Daud Beureueh judge his refusal to compromise.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh):** check whether dayah ulama who stayed neutral were praised or doubted by villagers.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check how coastal voters treat politicians who switch party.
- **South Aceh (Meukek and nearby, Aneuk Jamee and Kluet):** check whether the cleric of the *hikayat* is still invoked there.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how Gayo who opposed the independence movement describe their own stand.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how forest defenders and logging interests each describe principle.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how a Malay sworn pledge is kept or broken in local politics.
- **Singkil:** check how Christians who stayed and those who left after 2015 explain their choices.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island politics produces the same talk of party loyalty as the mainland.
- **Women teachers and preachers:** check positions women scholars hold that differ from male colleagues.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia-wide boycott calls of 2023, to see what in Aceh's response was local.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the conflict and its leaders; mixed for present-day activism; thin for Alas, Kluet, Tamiang and island convictions.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for the *hikayat* and sayings; Indonesian for the biography, press and election data; Gayo for highland accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed holdouts, loyalty pledges under martial law and minority worship; naming individuals may endanger them. Conscience before the law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** for steadfastness under pressure, South Asian-founded preaching movements whose members are active in Aceh, as a category only.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the *Hikayat Teungku di Meukek* in Drewes and note its words for a stand.
2. Read El Ibrahimy's chapters on 1953 and on the surrender talks.
3. Search the press for "ikrar setia Aceh 2003", "Aksi Kamisan Aceh" and "jemaat Singkil tenda".
4. Download KIP Aceh candidate lists for two elections and mark party switches.
