## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check how students and families describe study in Cairo, Turkey or Java as a departure.
- **Pidie:** check which trade routes Pidie migrants follow and how often they return.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check whether shrine visits to Malikussaleh draw visitors from other districts.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check which coastal families sent members to Malaysia after the conflict and the wave.
- **Gayo highlands:** check visitors at Lake Laut Tawar and how highland hosts receive coastal and foreign guests.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check Ketambe guides' views of foreign trekkers.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check what stories of their forebears' arrival by sea the Aneuk Jamee tell.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check daily travel across to North Sumatra for trade and school.
- **Singkil and the Banyak islands:** check island visitors and what the boats bring.
- **Simeulue:** check how islanders hosting surfers manage dress and prayer times.
- **Javanese settlers:** check journeys of flight during the conflict and of return afterward.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese in Yan (Kedah) and Kuala Lumpur, and in Scandinavia, for how leaving and staying are judged abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the hajj, conflict displacement and tourism promotion; mixed for labor migration; thin for Alas, Kluet and island travel customs.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for the 1602 embassy and Snouck's Mecca study; Acehnese for sayings and home-going terms; Arabic for hajj texts; Indonesian for BPS and agency data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** undocumented workers in Malaysia; displaced families; tourist dress and conduct rules belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** the sultanate's sea links to ports in South Asia, and refugee landings from camps there, both as categories only.
