*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how civil servants and shopkeepers describe unofficial fees in their own dealings.
- **Aceh Besar:** check whether honesty canteens in village schools fared differently from city ones.
- **Pidie:** check how traders' families judge a sharp deal against a dishonest one.
- **North coast (Bireuen, Lhokseumawe):** check gold-shop practice in the big market towns.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Nagan Raya):** check vote-buying reports in coastal districts against the capital's.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how growers, collectors and exporters each define honest coffee.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check honest dealing in the Kutacane market between Alas and newcomer traders.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check nutmeg and patchouli trade for disputes over weight and quality.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check smallholders' complaints about mills' scales.
- **Singkil:** check the same scale disputes among Singkil and Pakpak-speaking growers.
- **Simeulue:** check how clove and lobster buyers are trusted to weigh fairly on the island.
- **Comparison only:** BPS's anti-corruption index for other provinces, to place Aceh's scores.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption cases and election reports; mixed for market honesty; thin for children's teaching outside the dayah and for Alas, Kluet and island trade.
- **Languages needed:** early-modern English for Lancaster; Acehnese for tales and readers; Arabic for the ethics primer; Gayo for growers' talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming people accused of fraud or bribe-taking; prosecutions and penalties belong to Libra, and how far rules are obeyed to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** for honest trade at the old port, merchants from South Asian ports whom European visitors judged, as a category only.
