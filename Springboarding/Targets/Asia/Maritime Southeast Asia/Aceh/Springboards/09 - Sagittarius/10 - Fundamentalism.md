# Aceh: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against change; whether the word fits at all is itself disputed, so the sheet asks who uses it of whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check which neighborhoods host Salafi study circles and which oppose them, and why each says so.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how villagers near the Jantho camp now tell the 2010 events.
- **Pidie:** check how the reformist movement of the 1930s-50s is remembered by families on each side.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh):** check how the large dayah define the limits of acceptable reform.
- **West coast (Nagan Raya, Meulaboh):** check whether Sufi communities there have been called deviant, and by whom.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *kaum muda* and *kaum tua* villages for differences in ritual today.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether reform movements recruit among Alas Muslims and how Christian neighbors see it.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check Naqshbandi communities' answers to charges of innovation.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether reform currents there follow Medan's organizations more than Banda Aceh's.
- **Singkil:** check how the 2015 church dispute is explained by Muslim groups involved and by church members.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island preaching has seen the same quarrels over pure practice.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia-wide studies of Salafism and of hardline groups, to separate local from national currents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for reformist history and policy reports; mixed for present-day movements, whose own words must be sought; thin for Alas, Kluet and island currents.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay and Arabic for Kuta Karang and reformist texts; Indonesian for reports and press; Gayo for the Takengon dispute.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labels such as "Wahabi", "radical" or "deviant" can harm the labeled; name no private person; never treat Aceh or any group as one voice. Statutes and their enforcement belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** reform and preaching movements founded in South Asia and active in Aceh, and the 17th-century court purge urged by a South Asian-born scholar, as categories only.
