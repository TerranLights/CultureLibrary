# Aceh: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan, Sigulai and Leukon (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys and what is owed, across career, status and reputation, with the ways of age, time, rank, order and power as folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check whether office-based authority in the capital and the old *mukim* chiefs inland draw on different claims.
- **Pidie:** check how descendants of the 1946 upheaval's two sides now speak of birth and learning as grounds to lead.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check how the gas-industry years reshaped careers and who counted as a big man.
- **West coast (Meulaboh, Aceh Jaya):** check whether rebuilding after 2004 raised new figures of standing beside the old ones.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the village estates against the district bureaucracy as two ladders of rank.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check seniority within and between clans as a separate axis of authority.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check whether the mother's brother still carries any weight in Aneuk Jamee families.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether titled Malay elders keep a voice next to elected village heads.
- **Singkil and Simeulue:** check how far provincial titles reach island and riverine village offices.
- **Javanese settlers in the interior:** check whether their own forms of address and deference persist or give way.
- **Comparison only:** the Acehnese settlement at Yan in Kedah, for how elders' authority travels abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Chiefs and scholars are well covered; age and time ways barely appear outside theses; Simeulue, Singkil and Alas offices are thin.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists no settled lands and gives "Indonesia" alone as the pre-war land, so the shape of post-war Aceh is still to be fixed; this sign's sheets read the former province, Sabang to Simeulue, as their core.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Gayo for titles and age words; Dutch for the Gayo monograph; Jawi Malay for old office names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descendants of chiefs killed in 1946 and of conflict-era officials; law in this sign is taken only as spirit (letter in Libra).
- **Held for review:** court offices and titles held by men of South Asian birth at the sultanate, as a category only.
