# Aceh: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Portuguese for one early siege. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

English keywords: Aceh youth aspirations; Aceh scholarship corruption; Aceh women candidates; Acehnese envy and success; Iskandar Muda Malacca campaign.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how civil-service, medicine and academic careers rank against trade among the capital's young.
- **Pidie:** check how families rank trade against office as a way up, and whether parents and children rank them alike.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check which jobs sons of plant workers now chase, set beside their fathers' company careers.
- **West coast (Meulaboh):** check how reconstruction jobs after 2004 raised and then dropped expectations.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether coffee wealth turned into schooling for children or into land and houses.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether Alas students head for Banda Aceh or for Medan, and what each says about ambition.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check in which words Aneuk Jamee and Kluet families speak of a child leaving to seek a living, and how the two differ.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how Abdurrauf's story is used to urge local pupils upward.
- **Simeulue:** check how island youths weigh leaving against staying in fishing and clove work.
- **Women:** check how a daughter's aim for a career is spoken of next to her marriage prospects.
- **Comparison only:** Pidie-born traders in Medan and Jakarta, and Acehnese workers in Malaysia, for ambition pursued outside Aceh.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Education and election numbers are open; aspiration and envy are only in theses; Alas, Kluet and island data are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Portuguese and Malay for the siege; Arabic and Jawi Malay for Abdurrauf's travels; Acehnese for the envy and success words; Indonesian for youth indices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** envy talk can slide into sorcery accusations; ex-combatant fortunes touch on conflict grievances; the scholarship case names people.
- **Held for review:** careers at the 17th-century court of scholars born in South Asia, a category only.
