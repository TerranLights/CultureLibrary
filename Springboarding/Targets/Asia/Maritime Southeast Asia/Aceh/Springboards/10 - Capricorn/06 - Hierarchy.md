# Aceh: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; early-modern English and Dutch for visitors' accounts. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether official echelon now outranks descent at weddings and public seating.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how much of the old *sagoe* and *mukim* ordering is still named when land or seats are shared out.
- **Pidie:** check how families on each side of 1946 now intermarry, and what is said when they do.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check former combatants' rank order in villages against that of officials and scholars.
- **West and south coasts:** check how residents describe their region's place in provincial spending and attention.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether *belah* and *kejurun* descent still matters in choosing village heads.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check founder-clan precedence against newcomer households in the same *kute*.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether royal descent carries any present standing.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how ethnic and religious lines map onto who owns land and who works it.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island descent groups carry ranks that mainland Acehnese titles do not reach.
- **Chinese-Acehnese:** check how merchants' wealth sits with their minority status in the capital's order.
- **Comparison only:** national poverty and inequality rankings, to place Aceh among provinces.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Pre-modern and conflict hierarchies are well studied; present-day class, color and descent ranking sit in scattered theses; Simeulue and Tamiang are thin.
- **Languages needed:** early-modern English for Davis; Jawi Malay for court rank words; Acehnese for titles and sayings; Gayo for highland lineages; Indonesian for administrative data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1946 killings still divide families; sayyid marriage rules and color ranking touch living people; ethnic ranking in Singkil is a live grievance.
- **Held for review:** court ranks, titles and merchant quarters tied to people of South Asian heritage, as a category only.
