# Aceh: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether merit-index scores for provincial offices match what staff say about how promotions happen.
- **Aceh Besar:** check whether the elite boarding school draws its pupils from across the province or from nearby districts.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check how *dayah* standing and state degrees are weighed against each other when a village picks its *teungku*.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check the dispute over ex-combatants given posts, from both sides.
- **West coast (Meulaboh):** check whether the post-2004 boom in NGO jobs rewarded English and skills over family ties.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether coffee prizes and certification reward small farmers or the buyers who enter them.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether Alas and Gayo pupils use the merit routes into provincial schools at the same rate.
- **Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam:** check whether minority students reach the scholarship routes, and through whom.
- **Simeulue:** check how island pupils compete for mainland places with fewer schools behind them.
- **Women:** check whether women's religious learning earns them titles comparable to men's.
- **Comparison only:** national studies of clientelism in Indonesian hiring, as a yardstick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Merit indexes, exam data and admission routes are published; how people judge desert sits in theses and press; Simeulue and Alas are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for licenses and chains of teachers; Jawi Malay for older title usage; Acehnese for the sayings; Indonesian for indexes and admission notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims of bought posts can be defamatory; former combatants' appointments are touchy; hiring rules as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** scholarly chains at the court that run through teachers of South Asian birth, held as a category.
