# Aceh: The domain: community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai (Simeulue); Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch for colonial ethnography. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for (friends, groups, hopes, ideals toward humanity), with settling, gathering, hosting and joining as folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check coffee shops, campus groups and NGOs against *meunasah* life as rival gathering centers.
- **Pidie:** check how traders living away keep funding home-village feasts.
- **North coast (Bireuen to Lhokseumawe):** check which village associations outlived the conflict and which ex-combatant networks replaced.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Meulaboh):** check how relocated villages rebuilt "our village" on new sites.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *saman*, *didong* and coffee cooperatives as three ways of forming a group.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether clan or village sets the larger "we" at feasts and in disputes.
- **Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how Muslim and Christian villages meet, or avoid meeting, at markets and gatherings.
- **Simeulue:** check *nandong* evenings as both pastime and the way survival lore is passed on (Devayan needed).
- **Aceh Tamiang and the Aneuk Jamee and Kluet south:** check which hospitality and joining customs follow Malay or Minangkabau-derived lines.
- **Javanese settler villages inland:** check whether their associations sit inside or apart from the *gampong*.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese associations in Malaysia and Scandinavia, for gatherings rebuilt abroad; national social-capital figures, to place Aceh.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Village halls, feasts and post-tsunami recovery are well studied; friendship, dissent and future hopes sit in theses and press; Simeulue, Singkil and Tamiang are thin.
- **Lands:** no unit list exists yet and the tracker carries only "Indonesia" as pre-war land, so where the post-war nation's borders fall is still open; the Aquarius sheets treat the former province's mainland districts and islands as the core.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for pronouns and feasts; Gayo for performance words; Dutch for Snouck; Indonesian for statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informers and killings split villages in the conflict; relocated villages carry grief; law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** port neighborhoods and associations of South Asian-heritage residents as part of the old capital's mixed community, as a category only.
