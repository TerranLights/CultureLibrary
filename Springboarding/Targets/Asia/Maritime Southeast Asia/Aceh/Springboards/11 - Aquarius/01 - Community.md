# Aceh: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether new housing estates built after 2004 have formed village-style bonds or remain address lists.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how *mukim* federations still matter for shared land, mosques and water among member villages.
- **Pidie:** check how emigrant traders stay members of a home village they rarely live in.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe):** check company housing of the gas years against older fishing villages as two kinds of place community.
- **West and southwest coasts:** check relocated villages for who was resettled together and who was split up.
- **Gayo highlands:** check cooperative membership against *belah* membership as overlapping claims on a grower.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether a Christian minority in Kutacane-area villages takes part in Muslim neighbors' funeral and feast help.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how riverine villages define the community after the 2015 crisis.
- **Simeulue:** check whether the island's small villages pool labor differently from mainland ones.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how Malay villages and plantation workers' settlements share, or do not share, mosque and feast life.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese settlements in Malaysia, for what migrants carry of village duty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tsunami recovery and village governance; mixed for faith and work communities; thin for loneliness and for Simeulue, Tamiang and Alas.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for funeral and council terms; Gayo for cooperative and clan talk; Indonesian for Podes and program reports; English for the recovery volumes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grief in relocated villages; neighbors who informed on neighbors in the conflict; the Singkil houses of worship remain a live dispute; questions about written rules on worship go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** whether the capital's old harbor wards with South Asian-heritage households counted as one *gampong* with their neighbors (category; nothing named).
