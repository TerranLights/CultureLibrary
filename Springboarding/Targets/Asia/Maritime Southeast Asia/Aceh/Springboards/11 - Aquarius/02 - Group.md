# Aceh: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aceh Besar:** check whether the old *sukèe* names still sort people at weddings or in village offices.
- **Pidie:** check how the 1946 rivalry between chiefs' and scholars' camps still shapes which group a family stands with.
- **North and east coasts (Bireuen to Aceh Timur):** check ex-combatant networks as a group with its own claims on jobs and contracts.
- **West coast:** check whether fishing crews or villages are the unit that acts together in sea disputes.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the clash between *belah* loyalty and the urban parties of Takengon.
- **Bener Meriah and Central Aceh's settler villages:** check how Javanese settlers were pressed into anti-separatist militias and how they now speak of it.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check clan blocs in district elections.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check how faith groups closed ranks in 2015, and who crossed the line to help.
- **Simeulue:** check how island identity and mainland party politics pull on voters.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese exile circles in Sweden and Denmark, for how a movement's leaders kept a group abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the movement and party politics; mixed for kin groups; thin for crews, fans and girls' troupes, and for Simeulue and Tamiang.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *cuak* and *sukèe*; Jawi-script Acehnese for the epic; Indonesian for truth-commission testimony; Swedish only if the exile circles are followed.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming informers can still bring revenge; collective punishment touches living victims and perpetrators; crowd raids touch privacy and are a legal question for Libra.
- **Held for review:** guilds and factions of South Asian-heritage traders in the sultanate's port quarrels, as a category only.
