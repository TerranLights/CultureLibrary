# Aceh: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Jawi Malay for older texts. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check whether the capital's professional women report equal treatment at work that village women do not.
- **Aceh Besar and Pidie:** check how *peunulang* and family property talk differ between farming and trading families.
- **North and east coasts:** check how conflict victims and ex-fighters each describe their share of peace dividends.
- **West coast:** check tsunami survivors and those spared for claims of uneven aid within the same district.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the inheritance arguments Bowen describes against what younger Gayo women now say.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether Christian and Muslim households describe equal standing in local office.
- **Singkil and Subulussalam:** check how Pakpak-related and coastal speakers each describe access to land and jobs.
- **Simeulue:** check island residents' talk of being equal or lesser provincial citizens.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check plantation laborers' and smallholders' accounts of pay and land.
- **Disabled survivors and widows:** check whose access and benefits differ by where they live.
- **Comparison only:** Indonesia-wide gender-gap and Gini series, so Aceh's gaps can be read against other provinces' rather than alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Gender and post-conflict unevenness are well covered; speech levels, *peunulang* and minority accounts sit in scattered theses; Simeulue and Tamiang are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for pronoun levels and gifting terms; Gayo for inheritance talk; Jawi Malay for the *Tarjuman*; Indonesian for indexes and peace-process studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inheritance and spousal equality touch religious debate, and written inheritance rules belong to Libra; conflict compensation still stirs anger.
- **Held for review:** the standing of South Asian-heritage residents as equal townsfolk in the old capital, as a category only.
