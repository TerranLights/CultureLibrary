# Aceh: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; French for Lombard. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how former student activists of 1999 now describe their ideals of that year.
- **Pidie:** check the memory of Hasan di Tiro's home ground against his movement's later course.
- **Bireuen and North Aceh:** check rank-and-file ex-fighters' talk of betrayal against commanders' talk of realism.
- **Nagan Raya:** check how the Syattariyah community explains its own way against outside criticism.
- **West coast:** check whether rebuilding after 2004 is recalled as an ideal realized or a promise broken.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the Linge origin and *didong* poetry as sources of a separate highland ideal.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check which ideals of the good life the valley's clan histories hold up.
- **Singkil:** check how Abdurrauf is invoked as a model scholar.
- **Simeulue and Aceh Tamiang:** check whether the coastal golden-age story is shared or seen as someone else's.
- **Teachers, health workers and imams in remote areas:** check how each speaks of service against pay.
- **Comparison only:** the exile leadership in Sweden, for how an ideal was kept alive from abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political ideals and the golden-age story; mixed for religious community ideals; thin for personal idealism, callings and Alas, Simeulue and Tamiang material.
- **Languages needed:** French for Lombard; Acehnese and Jawi for the didactic verse; Gayo for *didong*; English for the 1976 declaration and Sjamsuddin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** separatist texts remain politically charged; the Nagan Raya community has faced outside pressure; ex-fighters' grievances are live.
- **Held for review:** court-era writings on the perfect human and the ideal ruler by South Asian-born scholars, as a category only.
