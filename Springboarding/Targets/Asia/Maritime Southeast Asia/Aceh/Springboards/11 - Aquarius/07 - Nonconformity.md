# Aceh: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic; Dutch for colonial psychiatry and police files. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how campus and café youth negotiate dress and music limits against older residents' expectations.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how *dayah* communities judge Salafi-leaning mosque groups, and the reverse.
- **North Aceh and Lhokseumawe:** check how salon workers and their customers describe life after the 2018 raids.
- **West coast (Aceh Barat, Nagan Raya):** check the memory of wandering scholars and holy eccentrics.
- **Gayo highlands:** check whether highland youth face the same scrutiny as coastal youth, or a different kind.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check what counts as odd conduct where Christian and Muslim villages live side by side.
- **Singkil:** check whether converts and mixed families are treated as outsiders.
- **Simeulue:** check how distance from the capital changes what is noticed and corrected.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check whether Malay border towns see more tolerance of difference than the core.
- **People with mental illness and their families:** check which families seek clinics, healers or confinement.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese students abroad, for how they describe returning with changed ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Raids, fatwas and youth panics are in the press; eccentrics, unmarried adults and the island districts are thin; academic work on dissent inside the faith is growing.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *pungo* and folk tales; Dutch for colonial psychiatry; Indonesian for fatwas and press; Gayo for highland youth talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trans people, punks, religious minorities and the mentally ill can be harmed by exposure; never publish names; penalties and enforcement go to Libra and Capricorn.
