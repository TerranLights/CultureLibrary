# Aceh: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan and Sigulai; Malay/Indonesian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how young graduates describe their prospects, and whether they hope to stay or leave.
- **Aceh Besar and the west coast:** check families still waiting for word of the missing from 2004.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check how families of the conflict's disappeared frame hope, and whom they still petition.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe):** check hopes for jobs after the gas fields, and what residents expect from new industry.
- **Gayo highlands:** check coffee prices and harvests as the axis of household hope.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check farmers' hopes tied to roads, rice and the forest margin.
- **Singkil:** check flood-prone river villages' hopes for relief and resettlement.
- **Simeulue:** check how island youth weigh hopes on the island against hopes on the mainland.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check plantation families' hopes for land and wages.
- **Fishing and farming households:** check how the *keuneunong* is still used to set hopes for a season.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese workers' communities in Malaysia, for hopes held from abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Post-tsunami and post-conflict hopes are well recorded; religious hope is taught but little studied; despair, island hopes and seasonal reckoning are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for *kaoy* and *peusijuek* formulas; Arabic for *rajāʾ* teaching; Gayo for coffee talk; Indonesian for press and indexes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the missing and the disappeared; suicide is stigmatized and underreported; contact through trusted local groups only.
- **Held for review:** hopes and vows kept by South Asian-heritage families of the old port at local shrines, as a category only.
