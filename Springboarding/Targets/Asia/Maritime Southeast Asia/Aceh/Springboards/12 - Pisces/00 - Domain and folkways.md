*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check museums, mass graves and counseling as routes for tsunami grief, against rites in nearby *gampong*.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check how detention sites shape talk of hidden suffering, and the 2016 earthquake as a second calamity.
- **North coast (Bireuen to Lhokseumawe):** check gas-field towns' trauma records against farming villages inland.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh, Nagan Raya):** check how the handful of survivors in all-but-erased settlements name, count and mourn the missing.
- **South and Southwest Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check Tuan Tapa lore and local healers against Acehnese practice nearby.
- **Gayo highlands:** check lake and cave lore, and whether Gayo healers keep a spirit vocabulary of their own.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check which explanations of misfortune Muslim and Christian households in Kutacane give a researcher.
- **Singkil, Subulussalam and the Banyak Islands:** check healers along the rivers and the sea stories told in Haloban.
- **Simeulue:** check night customs and sea tales in Devayan and Sigulai.
- **Aceh Tamiang and Langsa:** check Malay charm traditions against the Acehnese coast north of them.
- **Comparison only:** national mental-health figures; Acehnese in Malaysia, for healing ties kept with home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Trauma, disaster memory and post-2004 care are well documented; spirit lore and healing sit in theses and folklore series; Alas, Kluet, Singkil and island material is thin.
- **Lands:** the tracker header names only "Indonesia" as the pre-war land carried, which is broad; Aceh's post-war extent is not yet fixed, so these Pisces sheets are written for the old province's districts, from Sabang and Simeulue to the Alas valley and Singkil.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for the *Tajul Muluk* and household manuscripts; Acehnese and Gayo for spirit names spoken in the field.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention sites and testimony touch living perpetrators and survivors; healers may guard inherited formulas; possession is a charged religious topic; law on sorcery goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a Sufi doctrine of grades of being taken from a South Asian-born author's treatise, which shaped court mysticism in Aceh; kept as a category only.
