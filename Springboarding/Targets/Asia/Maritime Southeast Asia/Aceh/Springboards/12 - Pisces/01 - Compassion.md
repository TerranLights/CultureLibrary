# Aceh: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Tamiang Malay, Aneuk Jamee, Devayan; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how mosque-door giving, NGO offices and city clearances of beggars coexist.
- **Pidie:** check whether migrant traders' alms sent home follow kin lines or village need.
- **North coast (North Aceh, Lhokseumawe, East Aceh):** check the fishing villages that pulled refugees from the sea against those that later turned boats away.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Meulaboh):** check where tsunami orphans went: kin, *dayah* or institutions.
- **Gayo highlands:** check how highland villages took in coastal survivors in 2004, and how the Javanese families who fled the conflict years were or were not helped.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check church relief and mosque alms side by side in one valley.
- **Aceh Singkil:** check who carries food and boats to whom when the river floods mixed Muslim and Christian villages.
- **Simeulue:** check how islanders relieve one another when outside aid is slow to arrive.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check flood relief and Malay alms customs against the Acehnese north coast.
- **Chinese-Acehnese of Peunayong:** check temple and clan charity, and how it reached Muslim neighbors.
- **Comparison only:** national zakat agency figures; Acehnese associations in Malaysia raising money for home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alms agencies and post-2004 relief; mixed for health policy; thin for mercy toward enemies and for animal welfare.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese for the pity words that sort the deserving; Jawi Malay for Abdurrauf's counsels and the *Taj al-Salatin*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** orphans' and widows' records hold private data; refugee arrivals are a heated local issue; pardons and amnesty in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the refugees' own community and the camps in a South Asian country many sailed from, kept as a category only.

1. Read the *Taj al-Salatin* chapters on rulers and subjects; search "Mawaizul Badi'ah Abdurrauf".

3. Search "Jaminan Kesehatan Aceh evaluasi" and "panti asuhan Aceh pasca tsunami".
4. Search press archives for "nelayan Aceh selamatkan pengungsi 2015" and "pengemis musiman Ramadan Banda Aceh".
