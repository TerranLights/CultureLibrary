# Aceh: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Singkil, Devayan; Malay; Arabic; Indonesian; Japanese for the disaster-exchange material. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check counseling rooms, campus psychology and media workshops as places where empathy is taught as a skill.
- **Aceh Besar:** check how houses of mourning in villages near the capital receive strangers who come to sit with them.
- **Pidie:** check whether conflict victims' families describe being understood by officials, NGOs or neighbors, and by whom not.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh, Lhokseumawe):** check whether ex-combatants' memoirs and soldiers' accounts make any room for the other side's losses.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, Meulaboh):** check survivors who later traveled to other disaster zones and how they speak of those victims.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check how listeners react to sad sung tales in the Aneuk Jamee idiom against Acehnese hikayat.
- **Gayo highlands:** check *pepongoten* as a skill some women hold, and who now learns it.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check how mixed-faith clans console one another across a funeral of the other faith.
- **Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam:** check dialogue efforts after 2015, and whose fear each side is asked to imagine.
- **Simeulue:** check whether island elders' stories of the 1907 wave are told to make listeners feel the victims or to teach survival.
- **Javanese settler families in the interior:** check how they narrate their own displacement and that of Acehnese neighbors.
- **Comparison only:** cross-national empathy surveys that include Indonesia; Japanese disaster-zone groups linked to Aceh.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Trauma counseling and testimony archives are rich after 2004; measured empathy exists only in student theses; lament forms in Gayo, Alas and island languages are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Gayo for sung weeping; Aneuk Jamee speech for violin tales; Japanese for the Tohoku side of the exchanges.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivor and victim testimony is personal and painful; church-burning accounts remain raw; law on the 2015 events goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing; every lament, testimony and counseling pointer here comes from Aceh's own peoples and programs.
