# Aceh: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Devayan; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian; Dutch for war records. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check which sacrifices the city's monuments and cemeteries display, and which they leave out.
- **Aceh Besar and Pidie:** check how *dayah* families speak of sons sent away to study for years.
- **North Aceh and Lhokseumawe:** check Cut Meutia's memory in Pirak against school texts, and the anniversaries of fighters buried locally.
- **Bireuen:** check whether village tellings of the 1946 volunteers survive beside the official histories.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check whom fishing villages name as having died while saving others in 2004.
- **Gayo highlands:** check what Gayo verse and family histories say of those who left to fight in 1946 and after.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check which clan dead are named as givers of their lives, and in which language.
- **South Aceh and Southwest Aceh:** check how Aneuk Jamee families talk about sons who went to work abroad to keep the household.
- **Simeulue and Singkil:** check who is honored locally for giving their lives, since provincial histories seldom reach them.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check how the feast of sacrifice is shared in Malay villages against Acehnese ones nearby.
- **Comparison only:** Acehnese workers in Malaysia, on what they send home; national hero lists, to set beside local martyrs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war heroes and martyrdom poetry; mixed for family caregiving and remittances; thin for minority peoples' heroes and for island stories.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Jawi Malay to read the war poem against Hasjmy's Indonesian; Dutch for casualty lists; Gayo for highland verse.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martyrdom talk touches living ex-combatants and the state's own dead; caregivers' accounts are private; law on rebellion and amnesty goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** empty for this concept: the martyrs, caregivers and givers named here are all Aceh's own.

1. Read Hasjmy's study of the *Hikayat Prang Sabi* and list the sacrifices the poem names.

4. Search press archives for "kisah penyelamat tsunami Aceh meninggal" and "ziarah makam syuhada milad".
