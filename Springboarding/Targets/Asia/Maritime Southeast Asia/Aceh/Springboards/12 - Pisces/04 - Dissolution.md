# Aceh: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Aneuk Jamee, Tamiang Malay, Kluet, Singkil, Devayan, Sigulai, Leukon and Haloban; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh and Aceh Besar:** check which erased coastal villages kept their names on new sites and which vanished from maps.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh, Lhokseumawe):** check how former fighters describe the day their units disbanded.
- **Nagan Raya:** check Syattariyah remembrance nights for talk of the self lost in God.
- **South Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check whether Kluet speakers are shifting into Aneuk Jamee or Indonesian speech.
- **Gayo Lues and Central Aceh:** check Saman and *didong* performers' words for being carried by the group.
- **Bener Meriah:** check whether children of Javanese settlers now count themselves Gayo, Javanese or both.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check marriage across clans and faiths as a test of which bond dissolves into which.
- **Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam:** check pilgrims at Oboh, and the Singkil language's place beside Pakpak and Acehnese.
- **Simeulue and the Banyak Islands:** check whether the smallest speech communities there are being absorbed into Devayan, Indonesian or neither.
- **Aceh Tamiang:** check Malay identity under Acehnese and national pressure.
- **Comparison only:** national divorce and language-vitality figures; Acehnese descendants in Malaysia, for the pace of assimilation abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sufi texts and language counts; mixed for performance trance; thin for dementia, households broken and remade, and minority assimilation.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Jawi Malay for the treatises; Devayan, Sigulai, Leukon and Haloban for vitality fieldwork; Acehnese for what *dabôh* players say.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** teaching self-union with God is still disputed and can bring "deviant sect" charges; trance practices may be guarded; divorce rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the 17th-century court campaign against the unity-of-being school led by a South Asian-born scholar, and his polemics against it, kept as a category only.
