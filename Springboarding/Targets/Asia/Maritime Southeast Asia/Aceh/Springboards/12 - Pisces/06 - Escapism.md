*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check cafés, gaming spots and the beach as three different getaways for young people.
- **Aceh Besar and Sabang:** check how local visitors use island and beach resorts against outside tourists.
- **Pidie and Bireuen:** check whether going to the *rantau* is told as escape, duty or adventure.
- **North coast and East Aceh:** check rehab and street-use reports in port towns against inland villages.
- **Langsa and Aceh Tamiang:** check weekend trips to Medan, so close by road, and how they are spoken of.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the highland side of lake tourism: hosts, prices and complaints.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check whether river and forest outings serve as escape, and in which language they are talked of.
- **West and Southwest coasts:** check coffee-shop nights in fishing towns after a day at sea.
- **Simeulue and the Banyak Islands:** check whether islanders leave for the mainland to get away, as the mainland comes to them for the same reason.
- **Women across districts:** check which getaways are open to women and girls, and which are only talked about.
- **Comparison only:** national drug and gaming surveys; Acehnese workers in Malaysia, on leisure away from home.

## 4. Flags
