# Aceh: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Aneuk Jamee, Kluet, Devayan; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how hospital, campus and market settings rank a hunch against a test result.
- **Aceh Besar:** check goldsmiths' and buyers' talk about trusting the eye in the old gold trade.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check how the 2016 earthquake revived stories of animal signs and warnings felt beforehand.
- **North coast (Lhokseumawe, North Aceh):** check boat captains' trust in their own reading of the sea against forecasts.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check premonition stories told in villages that lost the most in 2004.
- **South Aceh and Southwest Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check tiger handlers' standing and the words Kluet speakers use for their skill.
- **Gayo highlands:** check cuppers and farmers on judging cherries by eye and taste against lab grades.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check forest guides at Ketambe and Alas words for reading the forest.
- **Simeulue:** check whether running from the sea in 2004 is told as learned lore or as a felt alarm, in Devayan and Sigulai.
- **Aceh Singkil and the Banyak Islands:** check river and reef fishermen's sense of danger.
- **Comparison only:** national studies of clinical judgment; Acehnese fishers working Malaysian boats, on reading foreign waters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Local ecological knowledge and disaster warnings are studied; expert sensory judgment and clinical intuition are thin; minority-language terms are mostly missing.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for the *syair* and physiognomy texts; Devayan and Sigulai for the islanders' accounts; Gayo for coffee-trade speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gut suspicion of informers in the conflict years led to killings, so hunches about people are a painful subject; handlers may guard their methods; sorcery rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** this sheet sets nothing aside; its seers, handlers and omen texts are all local.
