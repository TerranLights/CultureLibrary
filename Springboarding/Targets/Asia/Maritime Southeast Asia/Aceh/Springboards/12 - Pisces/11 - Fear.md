# Aceh: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Aceh, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Acehnese (Latin and Jawi), Gayo, Alas, Singkil, Aneuk Jamee, Devayan, Sigulai; Jawi Malay; Arabic; Indonesian. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banda Aceh:** check how women, students and newcomers describe danger in a city rebuilt after 2004.
- **Aceh Besar:** check whether families who moved inland after the tsunami still keep children from the beach.
- **Pidie and Pidie Jaya:** check fear after the 2016 quake and the Tangse flood, and memories of the conflict's night raids.
- **North coast (Bireuen, North Aceh, Lhokseumawe):** check how villages that lived with unknown gunmen speak of night, knocks at the door and strangers.
- **West coast (Aceh Jaya, West Aceh):** check slow fear of erosion against memories of sudden loss.
- **South and Southwest Aceh (Aneuk Jamee, Kluet):** check which night beings Tapaktuan and Kluet grandmothers name when calling children home.
- **Gayo highlands:** check the 2013 quake and the fear words Gayo speakers use for it.
- **Southeast Aceh (Alas):** check fear of flash floods in the Alas river valley and how warnings travel.
- **Aceh Singkil and Subulussalam:** check river fears and what each faith community says it fears from the other.
- **Simeulue:** check how island children are taught to fear the sea's withdrawal, in Devayan and Sigulai.
- **Comparison only:** national surveys of feeling safe; Acehnese refugees of the conflict years in Malaysia, on the fear that made them leave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Disaster and conflict fear is well recorded in theses and reports; fear figures, dusk customs and everyday worry are thin, and Alas, Singkil and island words are missing.
- **Languages needed:** Acehnese and Jawi Malay for the grave poems and fear words; Devayan and Sigulai for island teaching; Gayo for the 2013 quake.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the disappeared may still fear speaking; child trauma needs ethical care; security and policing rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no fear pointer was withheld.
