## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Baduy (Kanekes) of Banten**: self-sufficiency as an ancestral rule, not a choice.
- **Sedulur Sikep (Samin) of Blora and Pati**: refusal of dependence on the state.
- **Javanese families**: the pull to stay together set against leaving to earn.
- **Minangkabau and Batak young men**: leaving home to earn, but on different kin rules.
- **Moluccans and other sago eaters**: food self-sufficiency outside rice.
- **Dayak swidden farmers**: forest provisioning against plantation wage work.
- **Urban middle class**: the sandwich burden and new financial-independence talk.
- **Elders**: living with children, alone, or in a *panti jompo*.
- **Disabled people**: independence through support, not alone.
- **Regional movements in Sulawesi and Maluku**: collective independence claims.
- **Migrant workers' home villages**: comparison only for the workers themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy and statistics, mixed for household ethnography, thin for small communities outside Java.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese; Sundanese for Baduy material; Dutch for Samin and RMS sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** RMS and regional rebellions remain politically charged; Baduy restrict visits and recording in the inner villages; poverty labeling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
