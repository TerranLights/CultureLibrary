## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese**: the *rukun* ideal set against school and job ranking.
- **Balinese**: cockfight rivalry between villages and kin groups.
- **Madurese**: bull racing as family and village prestige.
- **Minangkabau and Riau Malay**: festival races tied to harvest and the river.
- **Mandar and Bugis sailors**: boat races and seafaring skill.
- **Toraja**: rivalry in funeral sacrifice and display.
- **Bima and Sumbawa**: horse racing and child jockeys.
- **Santri communities**: recitation contests and pesantren rankings.
- **Kudus and other badminton towns**: sport clubs that recruit children.
- **Urban middle class**: exams, *bimbel* and *gengsi*.
- **Chinese-Indonesian schools and families**: academic striving, from data, not stereotype.
- **Indonesian students abroad**: comparison only, for rankings met in foreign schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, exams and festivals; mixed for envy and rivalry in daily life; thin for small eastern-island contests.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese; Madurese; Balinese; Arabic for MTQ; Dutch for court spectacles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cockfighting and gambling are illegal but widespread; child jockeys; animal welfare in bull races and buffalo fights; sorcery accusations and killings.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
