## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Surabaya youth and *bonek* supporters**: daring as a group badge.
- **Urban motorbike youth** (Jakarta, Bandung, Makassar): street racing and night riding.
- **Rural drivers and holiday travelers**: overloading and fatigue during *mudik*.
- **Lamalera whalers and Bajau divers**: risk as work and tradition, not thrill.
- **Batak, Minahasan and Balinese drinking settings**: palm wine and spirits in ritual and leisure.
- **Hikers and climbers on Java, Sumatra and Lombok volcanoes**: adventure tourism against warnings.
- **Online traders and gamblers**: young men and women chasing quick money.
- **Pontianak Malay and Dayak festival-goers**: river cannon contests.
- **Pious households**: *tawakal* and the line between trust and carelessness.
- **Women**: whose risk-taking is judged differently.
- **Workers on irregular routes to Malaysia**: comparison only, for the risk of leaving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, drugs and gambling statistics; mixed for youth thrill cultures; thin for ethnography outside a few cases (Lamalera, Bajau).
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Suroboyoan; Lamaholot and Sama-Bajau for local terms; Arabic for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug offenses carry the death penalty (law to Libra); gambling and alcohol are religiously contested; Lamalera whaling is debated over conservation; deaths of poor workers framed as "reckless".
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
