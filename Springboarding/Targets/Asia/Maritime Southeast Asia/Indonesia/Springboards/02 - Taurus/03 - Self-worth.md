# Indonesia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Bugis-Makassar, Lampung, Balinese and Minangkabau; Dutch for Kartini's letters. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese aristocratic (*priyayi*) families against common people (*wong cilik*): speech levels place worth in every sentence.
- Madurese of Madura and the East Java "horseshoe" (*Tapal Kuda*): honor as named by men there, with women's views sought separately.
- Bugis and Makassar: *siri'* in marriage, migration and trade.
- Lampung Pepadun and Saibatin groups: two forms of the honor code.
- Balinese: caste titles beside personal achievement.
- Minangkabau: worth shown by success away from home.
- Toba Batak: honor (*hasangapon*) in an often-cited triad of life goals *check*, tested against how people actually speak.
- Chinese Indonesians: citizenship papers and name changes as attacks on worth.
- Women migrant workers returning from abroad: stigma and pride.
- People with mental illness: shackling (*pasung*) and what families say about it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich ethnography for Java, Bali and Bugis lands; thin survey data on self-esteem outside students.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese (speech levels); Madurese, Bugis and Lampung terms; Dutch for Kartini's originals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *carok* and honor violence; shackling; colorism and anti-Chinese discrimination. Avoid reading any one group as "touchy" or "proud": name variation.
- **Held for review:** none.
