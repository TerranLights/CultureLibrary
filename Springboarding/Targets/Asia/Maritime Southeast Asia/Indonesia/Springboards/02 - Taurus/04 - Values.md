## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Javanese pious-Muslim, nominal-Muslim and aristocratic streams (*santri*, *abangan*, *priyayi*): an old scheme to test, not assume.
- Nahdlatul Ulama and Muhammadiyah members: two Muslim traditions with different emphases on custom.
- Protestant Batak, Minahasans and Ambonese; Catholics of Flores and Toraja: church teaching beside local custom.
- Balinese Hindus: *Tri Hita Karana* in temple life and in tourism.
- Followers of Indigenous faiths (*penghayat kepercayaan*, Kaharingan, Sunda Wiwitan): values outside the official religions.
- Riau and Jambi Malays: Malay counsel literature as a living reference.
- Bugis-Makassar and Madurese: regional formulas against national ones.
- Confucian and Buddhist Chinese Indonesians: filial devotion and business ethics.
- Urban young adults against their parents: values under social media and city life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: national surveys, curricula and regional maxims are plentiful; risk of taking official formulas as lived practice.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Malay (Jawi) for classic texts; Old Sundanese; regional languages for maxims.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion, blasphemy and Pancasila are touchy topics; regional slogans can be used politically. Values written into law: Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
