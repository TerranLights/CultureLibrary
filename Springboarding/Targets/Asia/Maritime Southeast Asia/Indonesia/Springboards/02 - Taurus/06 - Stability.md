# Indonesia: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese court towns against north-coast port cities: canon and continuity against trade and churn.
- Minangkabau, Bugis and Banjar: leaving home to work, and what returning means, compared across the three.
- Balinese: a campaign for steadiness beside mass tourism.
- Kampung Naga and similar villages: change limited by custom, as residents explain it.
- Transmigrant settlers from Java in Lampung, Kalimantan and Sulawesi, and their host peoples: uprooting and new roots.
- Betawi of Jakarta: a home city changed around them.
- Ambon and the Maluku islands: continuity broken by the 1999-2002 conflict and rebuilt after.
- Chinese Indonesian family firms: continuity across ruptures (1965-1967, 1998).
- App-based drivers and contract workers: work without steady terms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national statistics and New Order history; mixed for lived routine; prophecy literature is plentiful but its dating and authorship are disputed.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese for prophecy and almanacs; Balinese; Dutch for colonial sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** New Order memory (1965-1966 killings), Maluku and Poso conflicts, transmigration disputes. Political order itself: Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
