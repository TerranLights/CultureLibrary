## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese and Betawi families: seclusion before the wedding and its fading.
- Bugis and Makassar: how the bride's kin respond to elopement, and how reconciliation is reached.
- Sasak of Lombok and Balinese: bride-taking and elopement customs, as each community explains them.
- Madurese: jealousy over wives in the honor-fight literature, with care over stereotype.
- Minangkabau: the in-marrying husband's place in a matrilineal household, and who guards whom.
- Toba Batak: clan rules on who may marry whom.
- Fishing communities on Java's north coast and in Sulawesi: disputes with outside boats over grounds.
- Urban students and young workers: phone checking and the *bucin* label.
- Informal parking attendants and their bosses: claims over public space.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: violence surveys and news are rich; jealousy as an everyday norm is thinly studied outside psychology theses.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian (slang shifts fast); Javanese, Bugis, Sasak and Balinese for marriage customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** intimate-partner violence, honor-related harm and coerced marriage; treat elopement customs without exoticizing. Marriage and violence law: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
