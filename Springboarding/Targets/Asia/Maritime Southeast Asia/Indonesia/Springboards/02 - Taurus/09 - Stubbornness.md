## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Sedulur Sikep (Samin) of Blora, Pati and Kudus: refusal through non-cooperation, as they describe it.
- Javanese families where compliance (*manut*) is the ideal, against the blunt Werkudara model.
- Makassar and Bugis coastal communities: seafarers' sayings of not turning back.
- Ngaju and other Dayak peoples: a motto of not retreating, and land disputes with companies.
- Madurese and Batak: both often stereotyped as hard; test the label against how they speak of themselves.
- Ambonese and other Maluku communities: Pattimura and heroes of resistance in local memory.
- Villagers in mine and factory disputes (Kendeng, Wadas): holdouts named "stubborn" by one side and "steadfast" by the other.
- University students: protest traditions from 1966, 1998 and 2019.
- Older and younger generations: elders "set in their ways" against youth who refuse elders' rules.
