## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese court tradition (refined taste, *rasa*) against north-coast and East Java street food and music.
- Sundanese: *lalapan* (raw greens) and *jaipongan* dance as sources to examine.
- Balinese: the flowers and incense of daily offerings, and spa tourism beside them.
- Minangkabau: cooking and communal feasts, and local debates on propriety in dance.
- Banjar: river-market food and taste words.
- Toba Batak: palm-wine stalls (*lapo tuak*) and song.
- Minahasans: harvest-thanks feasts under Christian dietary rules, a contrast with Muslim neighbors.
- Osing of Banyuwangi: *gandrung* dance as a regional pride.
- Maluku: spice history, sago and palm liquor.
- Chinese Indonesians: Peranakan kitchens, incense and Cap Go Meh.
- Devout urban middle class: halal, modesty and the *hijrah* trend reshaping leisure.
