## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese**, split at least into the court heartland (Yogyakarta, Surakarta), East Java's plainer *arek* speech, and Banyumasan *ngapak*: speech-level use differs sharply.
- **Sundanese**, **Madurese**, **Betawi** (Jakarta's older population) and **Balinese**: each has its own register system or its own reputation for frankness to test.
- **Minangkabau**: verbal art and the custom of *merantau* (leaving home to learn and work).
- **Batak peoples** (Toba, Karo, Simalungun, Angkola-Mandailing): wedding oratory and clan-based address.
- **Bugis, Makassarese, Mandar and Toraja** in South Sulawesi; **Minahasa** in the north.
- **Dayak peoples** and **Banjar** in Kalimantan; **Malay** communities of Riau, Jambi and Palembang.
- **Eastern islands**: Sumba, Rote, Flores (Manggarai, Ngada), the Atoni of West Timor, Ambon and Kei in Maluku, where parallel ritual speech matters.
- **Chinese-Indonesians** (Peranakan and *totok*), **Arab-Indonesians**, **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo)**.
- **Small peoples**: Baduy, Tengger, Sama-Bajau sea people, Orang Rimba.
- **Deaf communities** and **urban Jakarta youth** (slang, code-mixing).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java, Bali, Minangkabau and pesantren learning; mixed for Sulawesi and Kalimantan; thin and older for many eastern-island and small-forest peoples.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Aceh and the Papuan provinces are separate targets in this setting and are left out here.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Dutch above all; Javanese and Sundanese for manuscripts and regional print; Arabic and Pegon for pesantren texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some ritual speech and lontar knowledge is restricted to specialists; the 1965-66 killings and anti-Chinese measures shaped who could teach and print what; Deaf language politics (SIBI against BISINDO). Press and education law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
