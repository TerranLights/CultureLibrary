# Indonesia: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Minangkabau, Toba Batak, Buginese, Balinese, Madurese; Dutch; Chinese-Malay and Hokkien for Chinese-Indonesian sources. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the ideas held about what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Javanese** of Yogyakarta and Surakarta against **East Javanese** (Surabaya *arek* speech) and **Banyumasan** speakers: the same language with very different expectations of plainness.
- **Batak** and **Madurese** speakers, often described as frank: test that reputation against recorded talk instead of repeating it.
- **Minangkabau**: an explicit adat theory of register (*kato nan ampek*).
- **Sundanese**: refined speech and its own speech levels.
- **Bugis and Makassarese**: speech shaped by honor (*siri'*), where a careless word can carry weight.
- **Sumba, Rote and Flores** peoples: ritual couplet speech held by specialists.
- **Chinese-Indonesians**: trade talk, Chinese-Malay and Hokkien loans, and decades when Chinese print was banned.
- **Deaf communities**, and **Jakarta youth** mixing Indonesian and English online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Javanese politeness and speech levels; mixed for Sumatra and Sulawesi; thin for Kalimantan's Dayak peoples and most of Maluku.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Javanese; Dutch for colonial-era etiquette writing; Minangkabau and Batak for adat texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual speech in Sumba and elsewhere may be held by specialists and not open to recording; claims of a whole people's "indirectness" are a stereotype to test. Speech crimes and online defamation law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
