# Indonesia: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese, Makassarese and Buginese; Dutch for VOC and colonial records; Chinese for the Chinese-Indonesian press; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese villages** under the New Order's information groups, against **urban Jakarta and Surabaya** readers of the independent press: how far the state's channels reached.
- **Balinese** with their own-language news sections and *banjar* announcements; **Sundanese** with their own regional press.
- **Bugis and Makassarese**, heirs to a strong written chronicle and diary tradition.
- **Chinese-Indonesians**, whose own-language press was closed for three decades.
- **Muhammadiyah and NU communities**, which trust different calendar authorities.
- **Coastal and island peoples** in Maluku, Nusa Tenggara and Sulawesi, where disaster warnings and phone signal are uneven.
- **Dayak** and other **adat communities** in Kalimantan and Sulawesi, mapping their own lands.
- **Generations**: television-era elders against smartphone-era youth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the press and media history of Java; growing for online hoaxes; thin for eastern islands and for information sources in regional languages.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Dutch; Old Javanese (in translation) and Makassarese for pre-modern records; Chinese for the community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** information about 1965-66 remains contested and partly closed; adat knowledge may be restricted by its holders; online material on religion and ethnicity can be inflammatory. Press, information and defamation law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: Hill (1994), Kitley (2000), Cummings (2007); then search "Kelompencapir" plus "Orde Baru" in university repositories.
2. cekfakta.com and Mafindo's site: browse hoax categories (health, religion, disaster, election) by year.
3. BPS site: "Statistik Telekomunikasi Indonesia", latest edition, provincial tables.
4. Search "sidang isbat" plus "perbedaan awal Ramadan" for reporting on competing calendar announcements.
5. BRWA site: registered customary territories by province.
