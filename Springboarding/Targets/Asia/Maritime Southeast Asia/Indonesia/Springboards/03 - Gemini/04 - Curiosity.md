# Indonesia: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Banjar, Balinese, Toraja; Ambonese Malay; Dutch for colonial natural history; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** court-centered families, where esoteric knowledge is guarded, against **urban middle-class** families keen on science contests.
- **Sundanese** and **Banjar**, whose *pamali* lore sets limits on children's looking and asking.
- **Balinese** and **Torajans**, curious about and watched by outsiders through tourism.
- **Ambonese** and other **Malukans**, with a long record of natural knowledge drawn into colonial science.
- **Baduy**, **Tengger** and other peoples who close parts of their life to visitors.
- **Minahasa** and **Batak** Christian communities with mission schooling and Bible study.
- **Chinese-Indonesians**: trade-network curiosity about distant places.
- **Children** in big cities against children in remote islands with few museums.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Natural-history and tourism sources are rich; studies of everyday nosiness and children's questions are thin and mostly in theses.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Dutch; Javanese for mystical teaching; regional languages for taboo lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some esoteric and adat knowledge is closed to outsiders by its keepers and must not be pried into; colonial specimen and object collecting raises repatriation questions (objects in Dutch museums returned from 2020 on *check*).
- **Held for review:** none.
