# Indonesia: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Minangkabau, Ngaju Dayak, Toraja, Buginese; Old Javanese (in translation); Dutch for Eurasian and colonial sources; Chinese for Peranakan sources; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once, dividing the world into pairs, and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balinese**: an explicit doctrine of paired forces.
- **Javanese**: inner and outer self; Islamic and Javanese layers; refined and coarse.
- **Minangkabau**: matrilineal adat beside Islam.
- **Ngaju** and other **Dayak** peoples; **Toraja**; **Sumba, Rote and Flores** peoples: paired cosmologies and paired speech.
- **Bugis**: more than two genders, a check on any binary model.
- **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo)** and **Peranakan Chinese**: double belonging, and suspicion of it.
- **Hadrami Arab-Indonesians**: Arab descent and Indonesian nationhood.
- **Interfaith families** (Muslim-Christian in Maluku, North Sumatra and Java): two faiths in one household.
- **Bilingual urban youth** switching between Indonesian, regional language and English.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Balinese and eastern Indonesian paired thought and for Javanese religion; mixed for bicultural identity; thin for measured bilingual stress or double lives.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Dutch; Old Javanese in translation; Balinese and regional languages for ritual terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender diversity is politically charged and some *bissu* rites are restricted; Chinese and Eurasian loyalty questions recall violence (1965-66, 1998, the 1945-46 *bersiap* period); interfaith marriage is contested. Dual citizenship and interfaith marriage law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian works bearing on the Hindu-Buddhist heritage (category only).
