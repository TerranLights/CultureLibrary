# Indonesia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Minangkabau, Balinese, Toba Batak, Ngaju Dayak, Ambonese Malay; Dutch for colonial records; Chinese for the *kapitan* system; Arabic for religious terms. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** villages and pesantren communities: the *kiai* and village head as go-betweens.
- **Minangkabau**: a formal three-part adat council.
- **Balinese**: ward and customary-village assemblies.
- **Malukans** (Ambon, Seram, Lease islands): inter-village *pela* alliances in a Muslim-Christian setting.
- **Central Sulawesi** (Poso): a state-brokered settlement.
- **Dayak peoples** and **Madurese** settlers in Kalimantan: adat peace after violence.
- **Batak**: speech specialists at kin events.
- **Chinese-Indonesians**: community officers and associations as brokers.
- **Traditionalist and reformist Muslims**: for and against intercession.
- **Urban residents** dealing with fixers to get papers through offices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for post-1998 conflict and peacebuilding; mixed for adat dispute settlement; thin for everyday family and neighbor mediation.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Minangkabau and Balinese for adat terms; Dutch for colonial broker records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** communal-conflict areas (Maluku, Poso, Kalimantan) are painful and contested; peace accords are part of the Aceh conflict too, but Aceh is a separate target. Court-annexed, labor and other legal mediation belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
