# Indonesia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian (including Jakarta slang); Javanese, Betawi, Balinese, Buginese, Minangkabau, Osing (Banyuwangi); Arabic for religious terms; Chinese for Chinese-Indonesian sources. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Jakarta** (Betawi and newcomers): the home of *ngerumpi* and infotainment.
- **East Javanese** and **Osing** in Banyuwangi: rumor and sorcery accusations.
- **Central Javanese** villages: talk at the night-watch post and the market.
- **Balinese**: gossip inside the *banjar*, and formal shunning.
- **Minangkabau**: what the village and the clan will say.
- **Bugis and Makassarese**: gossip touching family honor.
- **North Maluku** and **central Maluku**: rumor in conflict.
- **Chinese-Indonesians**: targets of rumor in riots.
- **Pious Muslim** communities teaching against *ghibah*, against secular celebrity media audiences.
- **Men and women**: whose talk is called gossip, tested and not assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for celebrity media and for rumor in violence; mixed for everyday village gossip; thin for measured gossip in daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Jakarta slang; Javanese and Osing for sorcery-rumor sources; Arabic for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations, May 1998, and Maluku conflict are traumatic; naming living accused persons or online targets must be avoided; customary ostracism is contested. Defamation and online-speech law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
