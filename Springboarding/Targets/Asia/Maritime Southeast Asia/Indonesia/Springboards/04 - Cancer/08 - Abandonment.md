# Indonesia: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Minangkabau, Batak Toba, Sasak, Madurese. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of migrant-sending districts (Wonosobo, Cilacap, Indramayu, Ponorogo):** absence for work against desertion, as families judge it.
- **Sundanese of Cianjur and Sukabumi:** another major sending region, with its own words for the waiting family.
- **Sasak of East Lombok:** men's long absences in Malaysia; wives and children at home.
- **Flores, Sumba and West Timor:** migration over irregular routes and children left with grandparents.
- **Madurese:** men working abroad and in other islands for years.
- **Minangkabau:** the son who leaves (*merantau*) and the legend that warns against forgetting the mother.
- **Batak:** urban migrants and parents left in the home village.
- **Bugis seafarers:** long voyages and families ashore.
- **Indonesian Chinese and urban middle class:** elder homes as a choice, not desertion, to test.
- **Families of people with mental illness, in every region:** confinement as a form of keeping and leaving at once.
- **Diaspora, comparison only:** the migrant parent's side of the separation, seen from Malaysia, Hong Kong and the Gulf.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good welfare and NGO data on institutions and shackling; rich folklore; thin on deserting fathers (no child-support data source *check*).
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese for songs and legends; regional languages in sending districts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *pasung*, infant abandonment and children born outside marriage carry deep shame; protect identities. Divorce, child-support and adoption law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

5. Indonesian folktale collections: "Malin Kundang" and "Jaka Tarub" versions, noting the moral each version draws.
