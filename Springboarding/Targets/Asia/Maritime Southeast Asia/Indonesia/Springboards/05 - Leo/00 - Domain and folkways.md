## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court cities (Yogyakarta, Surakarta) against East Java's *arek* culture (Surabaya, Malang):** refined restraint against loud public fun, to test.
- **Sundanese:** *jaipongan* and *sisingaan*, a performance world apart from Java's.
- **Balinese:** temple festivals where art is offering, tying pleasure to ritual duty.
- **Madurese, at home and as migrants:** bull racing (*karapan sapi*) and the display of success.
- **Minangkabau:** *randai*, *silek* and oratory, framed by matrilineal houses and the *rantau*.
- **Batak peoples (Toba, Karo, Mandailing):** church choirs, *gondang*, and Karo youth festivals (*guro-guro aron*).
- **Bugis, Makassar and Toraja:** weddings, funerals (*rambu solo'*) and feasts as public rank.
- **The eastern islands (Sasak, Sumbawa, Sumba, Flores, Timor, Rote, Maluku, Minahasa):** ritual contests such as *pasola*, *caci* and *peresean*; the least covered.
- **Dayak peoples and the Mentawai:** festivals and body decoration under mission and state pressure.
- **Chinese-Indonesians (Tionghoa), Arab-Indonesians and Betawi:** Cap Go Meh, *gambus*, *lenong*; Tionghoa public celebration was restricted 1967-2000.
- **Santri and secular urban milieus:** where pleasure is permitted or suspect.
- **Migrant workers abroad (Hong Kong, Taiwan):** comparison only; how success is shown on return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java and Bali (ethnography, recordings, state inventories); mixed for Sumatra and Sulawesi; thin for Nusa Tenggara and Maluku outside heritage tourism.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Dutch, Javanese (Old Javanese for older texts), Balinese, Sundanese, Bugis; Jawi and Lontara scripts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling is illegal, so cockfighting and *togel* data are hidden; dance and music meet piety debates; LGBT expression is policed; 1965-66 touches performers (Lekra, *ludruk*); some ritual knowledge (Kanekes/Baduy, Tengger) belongs to insiders.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. Aceh and the Papuan provinces are separate targets in this setting and are left out here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian works, scholars and South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only).
