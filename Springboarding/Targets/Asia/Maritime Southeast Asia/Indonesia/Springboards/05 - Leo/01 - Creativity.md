# Indonesia: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (with Old Javanese for kakawin), Balinese, Sundanese, Minangkabau; Dutch for colonial art writing. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court artists against coastal makers:** *abdi dalem* craft under the kraton set beside freer *pasisir* workshops.
- **Peranakan Chinese of Lasem and Pekalongan:** hybrid batik and the question of whose tradition a mixed motif belongs to.
- **Balinese village painters (Batuan, Ubud, Penestanan):** styles named by village rather than by person, then sold to tourists.
- **Toraja carvers and Sumba, Flores and Timor weavers:** named motifs tied to rank and house, so invention meets inheritance.
- **Dayak carvers and beadworkers:** motif rights in longhouse societies and their revival for festivals.
- **Minangkabau:** Pandai Sikek songket and *rumah gadang* carpentry, craft guarded by village.
- **Jepara and Madurese woodcarvers:** an export industry against heirloom work.
- **Urban creative milieus (Bandung, Yogyakarta, Jakarta):** design, independent music and collectives as a new path.
- **Pesantren communities:** calligraphy and Qur'an-calligraphy contests (MKQ *check*), the forms of art pious makers favor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for modern painting, batik and Bali; mixed for crafts; thin for makers' own accounts in Nusa Tenggara and Maluku.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese and Old Javanese, Balinese, Dutch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Lekra artists were killed or jailed in 1965-66; some textiles and carvings are sacred or rank-bound; copyright and design-ownership law belong to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian works and scholars on the shared epic sources of Old Javanese poetry (category only).
