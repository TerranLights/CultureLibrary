# Indonesia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian (including youth slang); Javanese (ngoko and krama), Sundanese, Balinese, Minangkabau, Batak Toba, Banjar, Makassarese; Dutch for colonial-era letters. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese *priyayi* against East Java's *arek* milieus:** court-refined indirection against the blunt speech of Surabaya and Malang; a contrast to test.
- **Minangkabau:** speech registers (*kato nan ampek*) and oratory, where saying things sideways is a mark of skill.
- **Batak, Bugis and Makassar:** reputations for directness or for *siri'*-bound reserve, to be checked against their own speakers, not assumed.
- **Balinese, north and south:** whether Wikan's North Bali findings hold in Gianyar or Denpasar.
- **Banjar of South Kalimantan:** *madihin* as public, joking speech.
- **Mentawai and Dayak peoples:** body marking as identity, under decades of discouragement.
- **Pious urban Muslim women and men:** dress and *hijrah* style as self-presentation.
- **Christian regions (Minahasa, Batak churches, Flores):** testimony and choir singing as approved outlets.
- **Tionghoa after 1998:** Chinese names, script and Imlek shown in public again.
- **LGBT and *waria* communities:** expression under rising pressure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java and Bali; online data abundant but noisy; thin for eastern islands' own talk about feeling.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian slang, Javanese with its speech levels, Balinese, Minangkabau; Dutch for colonial letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT expression and dance controversies carry real risk for informants; anonymize online material; speech and pornography law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
