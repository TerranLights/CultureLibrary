## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese courts and their *abdi dalem*:** titles granted for service or patronage.
- **Minangkabau men:** a title at marriage as the mark of adulthood.
- **Balinese *triwangsa* and commoners (*jaba*):** birth-rank titles against achievement.
- **Bugis and Makassar nobility:** *Andi* and *Karaeng* in modern office.
- **Batak clans:** recognition by granting a *marga* to outsiders.
- **Sumba and Toraja:** standing acknowledged through exchanges and feasts.
- **Adat communities (Kasepuhan, Kanekes, Dayak groups, Orang Rimba):** seeking recognition from the state.
- **Followers of local faiths (*penghayat kepercayaan*):** recognition on identity cards.
- **Tionghoa and Khonghucu followers:** identity recognized after 1998.
- **Honorary teachers (*guru honorer*) and other unsung workers:** praise without secure pay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors and court titles; mixed for adat titles; thin for small peoples' own ways of naming merit.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Old Javanese (inscriptions), Javanese, Minangkabau, Balinese, Batak Toba.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** caste titles in Bali are contested; hero designations can be fought over (1965, regional rebellions); rights-violation victims' groups need careful approach; recognition law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
