# Indonesia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian (with online slang); Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese, Minangkabau; Arabic terms from Islamic teaching. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court towns:** inherited rules of audience and display, now set beside mall and screen culture.
- **Balinese:** ritual spectacle where visibility is owed to gods and village.
- **Minangkabau and Balinese families:** set rites for first showing a baby.
- **Pious Muslims:** teaching against *riya'* beside the rise of celebrity preachers.
- **Urban creators in Jakarta, Bandung and Surabaya:** visibility as income.
- **Women performers (*ronggeng*, *gandrung* of the Osing, *dangdut* singers):** being looked at as work and as risk.
- **Nusa Tenggara and Maluku peoples:** thin screen presence and the stereotypes that fill the gap.
- **Small peoples in broad census categories (Orang Rimba, Bajau, Tengger):** counted or not.
- **Domestic workers and app drivers:** labor no one looks at.
- **Tionghoa:** visibility as danger in 1998 and as display at Imlek since.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java, media and online life; mixed for rites; thin for how small peoples talk about being witnessed.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and its slang, Javanese, Balinese, Minangkabau; Arabic religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charm and healer practices can be stigmatized; 1998 violence against Tionghoa women needs care; surveillance law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
