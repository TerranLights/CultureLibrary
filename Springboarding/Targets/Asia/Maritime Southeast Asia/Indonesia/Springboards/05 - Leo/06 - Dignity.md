# Indonesia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Minangkabau, Riau Malay, Toraja; Arabic terms from Islamic teaching; Dutch for colonial writing. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese:** dignity through speech and dress, by the proverb *ajining diri saka lathi, ajining raga saka busana*; test across classes.
- **Minangkabau:** tact (*raso jo pareso*) as the guard of others' dignity.
- **Riau and coastal Malays:** *marwah* as a word used for persons and for the Malay people.
- **Toraja:** the dignity of the dead and the cost of honoring them.
- **Orang Rimba and other forest peoples:** names imposed by outsiders and their own preferred names.
- **Tionghoa:** dignity bound up in what the group is called.
- **Migrant-worker regions (East and Central Java, Lombok, NTT):** dignity of work abroad and on return.
- **Disabled people and their families:** self-naming and access.
- **Elders in cities and in villages:** care at home against care homes.
- **Street children and the urban poor:** dignity on the street and in aid queues.
