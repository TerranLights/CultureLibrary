# Indonesia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian (with online slang); Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Balinese, Bugis; Japanese and Dutch for occupation and colonial records. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Madurese, on Madura and in East Java and Kalimantan:** insult and answer in their own words, against outsiders' stereotypes.
- **Bugis and Makassar:** *ripakasiri'* when a family's daughter elopes (*silariang*); how families respond.
- **Balinese:** everyday *lek* and the national memory of the *puputan*.
- **Javanese:** *wirang* beside comic forms (Srimulat, *ludruk*); where lowering turns into shared laughter, to test.
- **Sundanese:** whether allusive teasing (*sisindiran*) works as a buffer against open insult.
- **Students at uniformed academies and boarding schools:** hazing as a rite.
- **Unmarried couples, LGBT people and suspected thieves:** targets of crowd shaming.
- **Survivors of the occupation and their families:** humiliation spoken late or not at all.
- **Tionghoa:** collective humiliation in 1998 and its memory.
- **Debtors and the urban poor:** shaming tied to money.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press cases and Java; mixed for surveys; thin for eastern islands' terms for shame.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Madurese, Balinese, Bugis; Dutch and Japanese for wartime records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual violence, crowd violence, the 2001 Sampit conflict and 1998 need trauma-aware handling; defamation and honor-crime law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
