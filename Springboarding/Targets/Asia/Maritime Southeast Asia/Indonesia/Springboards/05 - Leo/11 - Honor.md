# Indonesia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Bugis and Makassarese (with Lontara texts), Riau Malay (with Jawi), Javanese, Batak Toba, Ambonese Malay, Madurese, Kodi (Sumba). **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bugis and Makassar:** *siri'* in marriage, migration and politics; urban and village readings may differ.
- **Malays of Riau:** honor framed around the ruler and the betrayal of him.
- **Javanese *priyayi* families:** a good name across generations, and the duty to hide a parent's faults.
- **Batak:** honor owed by kin position more than by person.
- **Christian and Muslim villages of Maluku:** honor of the oath that binds them.
- **Kodi and other Sumba peoples:** honor of houses kept through feud and exchange.
- **Madurese:** defending a name, and the religious voices that argue for restraint.
- **Dayak peoples:** honor once tied to warfare, now remembered and reworked; to approach with care.
- **Military families and veterans:** the codified soldier's honor.
- **Young women and men under family-honor norms:** their own accounts of reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for South Sulawesi and Java; mixed for Batak, Malay and Maluku; thin for the smaller eastern peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Bugis, Makassarese (Lontara script), Malay (Jawi), Javanese, Batak Toba.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor and violence against women, elopement and feuds need consent-based fieldwork; the 1999-2002 Maluku conflict is still raw; honor-crime and defamation law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian works on the source epics of the *Serat Tripama*'s figures (category only).
