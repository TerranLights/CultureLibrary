# Indonesia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Minangkabau, Buginese, Balinese, Batak Toba; Hokkien for Chinese Indonesian trade; Arabic for religious terms; Dutch for colonial labor records. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese farm villages against industrial belts (Bekasi, Karawang, Gresik):** where factory shifts replaced the farm round.
- **Kudus and Malang kretek towns:** women's piece-rate work as a local identity.
- **Sundanese tea and vegetable uplands:** estate wage work beside smallholding.
- **Madurese:** migrant work (satay selling, scrap trading, salt) as a work style carried across Java.
- **Minangkabau:** restaurant and trade careers built away from home.
- **Bugis of Bulukumba (Tana Beru, Bira):** shipbuilding crews and their skill hierarchy.
- **Bangka-Belitung:** tin mining, from Hakka coolie labor to small unlicensed pits.
- **South Kalimantan's Banjar:** diamond and gold panning around Martapura.
- **Dayak swidden and rubber-tapping households:** work timed by forest and river.
- **NTT and NTB (Sasak, Flores, Timor):** heavy sending of workers abroad.
- **Chinese Indonesian shopkeeping families:** family labor inside the *toko*.
- **Sedulur Sikep and Orang Rimba:** two refusals of mainstream work, one farming, one forest.
- **Bajau sea people:** fishing and diving as livelihood.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and Java's labor history; mixed for outer-island ethnography; thin for Indigenous forest and sea peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Dutch, Javanese; regional languages for local work words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labor unions and the 1965 to 1966 purge; forced-labor memory (*romusha*); undocumented migrant work. Labor law and wage rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
