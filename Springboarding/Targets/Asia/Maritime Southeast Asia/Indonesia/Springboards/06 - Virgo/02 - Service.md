*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (court and village registers), Balinese, Batak Toba, Minangkabau, Sundanese, Toraja-Sa'dan, Kambera (East Sumba); Arabic for pesantren usage; Dutch and Old Javanese for older sources. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of the court cities against the pasisir:** palace service as a living institution against mercantile towns without it.
- **Balinese:** service owed in kind to temple and hamlet, and its clash with tourism shifts.
- **Toba Batak:** serving one's wife-givers as kin duty, not as a lowly role.
- **Minangkabau:** the in-marrying man's duties at his wife's family's feasts.
- **Toraja and Sumba (Kodi, Kambera):** inherited service strata and their present-day echoes.
- **Pesantren communities in East Java and Madura:** students serving a kiai.
- **Christian communities (Minahasa, Toba, NTT Catholics):** service as church office (*majelis*, deacons).
- **Chinese Indonesians:** temple committees and funeral societies run by volunteers.
- **Baduy (Kanekes):** service framed as an adat obligation to the ruler.
- **Rural women from Central and West Java, Lampung and NTT:** the main supply of household helpers, against urban employers.
- **Bali and Lombok tourism workers:** paid hospitality beside customary service.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java's court culture and pesantren; mixed for Bali; thin for eastern hierarchies, where much is oral.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese speech levels (krama inggil for court service); Balinese; Batak Toba; Dutch for colonial servant records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descent from bonded or servant strata in Sumba, Toraja and Bugis lands; abuse of domestic workers; criticism of a kiai's or a sayyid's authority. Labor rules for helpers and civil servants go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
