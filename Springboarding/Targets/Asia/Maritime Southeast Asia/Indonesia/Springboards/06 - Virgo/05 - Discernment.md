*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court and village families:** marriage judgment by *bibit, bebet, bobot*, and veiled speech that must be read.
- **Minangkabau:** adat sayings that pair feeling with checking, and family councils that weigh a match.
- **Balinese:** consulting priests or healers on a choice, beside personal judgment.
- **Toba Batak:** clan checks before a marriage is allowed.
- **Bugis and Makassar:** family assessment of suitors and the stakes of a wrong match.
- **Devout urban youth in the *hijrah* trend:** *ta'aruf* and prayer as the road to a choice.
- **Christian Minahasa and NTT Catholics:** discerning a vocation to ministry or religious life.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** temple consultation and family vetting.
- **Older rural people and first-time smartphone users:** test whether SMS and app scams single them out, and how they judge senders.
- **Collectors' circles in Central and East Java:** connoisseurship of keris, birds and batik.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong for scams and hoaxes (state and press data); good for Javanese texts; thin for most outer-island traditions of judging people.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese (court registers for older texts), Minangkabau, Balinese, Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blasphemy cases and religious authority; descent-based marriage screening; mystical claims around keris. Fraud prosecution goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
