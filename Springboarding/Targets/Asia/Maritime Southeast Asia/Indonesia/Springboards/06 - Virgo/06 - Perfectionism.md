# Indonesia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (court arts vocabulary), Balinese, Sundanese, Minangkabau; Arabic for Qur'an-recitation and Sufi terms; Old Javanese for older texts. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court arts of Yogyakarta and Surakarta:** fixed canonical forms in dance, puppetry, dress and batik.
- **Javanese coastal batik towns (Pekalongan, Lasem):** market batik outside court rules; test whether variation counts for more than canon there.
- **Balinese:** ritual exactness in offerings and temple festivals beside village improvisation.
- **Minangkabau songket weavers (Pandai Sikek):** exacting weaving inside a matrilineal craft line.
- **Sumba and Flores ikat weavers:** long dyeing cycles and the judging of finished cloth.
- **Toraja carvers:** set motifs on ancestral houses.
- **Pesantren and Qur'an schools:** recitation exactness as piety.
- **Chinese Indonesian family firms:** shop reputations for consistent quality.
- **High-pressure schools in Jakarta, Surabaya and Medan:** academic perfection and its costs.
- **Market traders in copies:** the people who make "good enough" a livelihood.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court arts and craft; mixed for psychology; thin for outer-island craft standards in the makers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Balinese technical vocabularies; Arabic for recitation and Sufi terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heresy charges around Javanese mysticism; suicide and mental illness; ritual knowledge some priests and makers may not share. Product-standard law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
