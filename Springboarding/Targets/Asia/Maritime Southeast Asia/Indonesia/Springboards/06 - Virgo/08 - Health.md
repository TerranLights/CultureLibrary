# Indonesia: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese, Buginese, Batak Toba, Mentawai, Dayak languages (Benuaq, Ngaju), Uab Meto (Timor); Arabic for Islamic healing; Hokkien for Chinese medicine; Dutch for colonial medical records. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese:** herbal drink sellers, scraping and massage as home care beside the puskesmas.
- **Sundanese:** traditional midwives in a province with its own village-health history.
- **Balinese:** written healing manuscripts and *balian* who read them.
- **Toba Batak:** the *datu* tradition after mass Christian conversion.
- **Bugis and Makassar:** the *sanro* as healer and birth helper.
- **Mentawai:** healers working inside their own account of illness; check how resettlement changed access to them.
- **Benuaq and other Dayak peoples:** healing rites as community gatherings.
- **Timor and other NTT peoples:** after-birth heat practices and distant clinics.
- **Tengger:** their own priest-healers in the Bromo highlands.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** *sinshe* and medicine shops.
- **Santri and urban pious middle class:** *ruqyah* and cupping as Islamic choices.
- **Charismatic Christian congregations (Minahasa, Maluku, cities):** prayer healing.
- **Urban middle class:** private hospitals and treatment abroad in Penang or Singapore.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys and Java and Bali; mixed for Sulawesi and Sumatra; thin for Mentawai, Dayak and NTT healing in the healers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Balinese for manuscripts; Batak and Buginese scripts for older texts; Dutch for colonial medical reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations, which have led to killings; shackling; healers' secret knowledge, shared only as they choose. Health-insurance and practice-licensing law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
