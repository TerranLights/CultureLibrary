# Indonesia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Buginese, Minangkabau, Belitung Malay; Arabic for religious reform terms; Dutch for colonial "uplift" policy. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese:** test how acceptance of one's lot (*nrima*) and 20th-century progress talk sit side by side.
- **Sundanese:** a named four-part ideal of the formed person.
- **Bugis:** a well-known motto tying grace to unceasing work.
- **Minangkabau:** learning from the world, and schooling as family investment.
- **Belitung and Bangka Malays:** schooling and mobility as tin mining shrank (Andrea Hirata's *Laskar Pelangi*, 2005, is the popular entry *check*).
- **Balinese farmers:** Green Revolution packages arriving in subak lands.
- **Transmigrant settlers in Lampung, Kalimantan and Sulawesi:** state-sponsored betterment, and the host peoples' view of it.
- **Peoples labeled "remote" (Orang Rimba, Halmahera's forest peoples, Baduy):** improvement offered, refused or reshaped.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** which paths to betterment families chose under New Order restrictions, and after 1998.
- **Jakarta and Surabaya kampung residents:** upgrading against eviction.
- **Urban *hijrah* youth:** self-reform through piety.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state development and nationalist history; mixed for self-help and religious reform; thin for Indigenous answers to development, in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Dutch, Javanese; Buginese for *pappaseng*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** transmigration conflicts; evictions; the "backward" label on adat peoples; New Order violence behind *pembangunan*. Land and agrarian law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
