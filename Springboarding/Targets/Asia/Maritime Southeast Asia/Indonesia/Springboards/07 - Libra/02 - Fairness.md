## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Heirs under different inheritance systems: Minangkabau ancestral property held through women; Batak and Balinese lines through sons; Muslim *faraid* shares; civil-code heirs among Chinese-Indonesian and Christian families.
- Javanese and Sundanese sharecroppers and landowners; Balinese *subak* members upstream and downstream.
- Fishing crews: Lamalera whalers, Bugis and Mandar sailors, Madurese and Bajau (Sama) sea people.
- Factory workers in Bekasi, Karawang and Batam, whose wage floors differ by district.
- Urban middle-class parents facing school zoning; contract and *honorer* civil servants on unequal terms.
- Chinese-Indonesian traders and *kongsi* descendants in West Kalimantan and Bangka.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, wages and experiments at Lamalera; mixed for everyday splitting and queuing customs, which need fieldwork or social-media sampling.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Minangkabau for proverbs; Mandarin for the Chinese press; Lamaholot for Lamalera.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2023-24 election rulings are partisan; inheritance gaps by sex touch religious law.
- **Held for review:** none.
