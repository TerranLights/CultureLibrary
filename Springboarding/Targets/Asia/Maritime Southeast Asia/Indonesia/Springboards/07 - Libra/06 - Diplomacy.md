## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese of Yogyakarta and Surakarta, with their speech levels, against Javanese of the north coast and East Java, whose speech differs.
- Sundanese and Balinese, each with their own register systems.
- Minangkabau, where ceremonial oratory carries negotiation between lineages.
- Batak and Madurese communities, often described as more direct; test that claim rather than assume it.
- Betawi of Jakarta; Bugis of South Sulawesi with their deliberative gatherings.
- Muslim and Christian leaders in Maluku and Central Sulawesi after the peace accords.
- Chinese-Indonesian community leaders as long-standing go-betweens.
- Career diplomats (Kemlu) and labor mediators as professional cultures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foreign policy and Javanese politeness; mixed for regional oratory; thin for go-between customs in the east.
