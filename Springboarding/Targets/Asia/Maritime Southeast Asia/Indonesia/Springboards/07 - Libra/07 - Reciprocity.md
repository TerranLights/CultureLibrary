*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Minahasan languages, Toraja (Sa'dan), Bugis, Kodi and other Sumbanese languages; Malay pantun forms; Hokkien and Mandarin; Old Javanese for inscriptions; Dutch. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Rural Javanese, with notebooks of feast gifts, against Jakarta's salaried middle class.
- Balinese *banjar* members, where labor is owed to the hamlet.
- Toraja highlanders, whose funeral gifts are tallied in public; Sumbanese houses tied by long exchange debts.
- Minahasa and other Christian-majority communities of North Sulawesi.
- Bugis and Makassarese, with their triad of mutual regard.
- Migrant-sending villages of Lombok, Sumbawa, Flores and East Java.
- Chinese-Indonesian clan and funeral associations; Arab-Indonesian *haul* gatherings.
- Voters in districts studied for patronage, by region.

## 4. Flags
