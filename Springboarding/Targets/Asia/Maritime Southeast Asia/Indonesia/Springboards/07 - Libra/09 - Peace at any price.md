# Indonesia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Ambonese Malay, Sundanese, Madurese; Dutch for colonial-era sources. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Javanese urban *kampung* and court-city families, whose didactic texts praise yielding (to test in practice), against Madurese and Bugis honor traditions (to test likewise).
- Balinese *banjar* members, where sanctions enforce agreement.
- Religious minorities: Ahmadiyah, Shia, native-faith followers, Christian congregations denied permits.
- Chinese-Indonesians, who adjusted names and customs under the New Order.
- 1965 survivors' families and perpetrators' communities in North Sumatra, Central and East Java, and Bali.
- Muslim and Christian communities of Ambon and Poso after the conflicts.
- Women in pesantren, campuses and workplaces; small traders paying for "security".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, minority cases and Java; mixed for family conflict avoidance; thin for eastern islands.
