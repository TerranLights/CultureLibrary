# Indonesia: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (including Osing), Sundanese, Balinese, Makassarese and Bugis, Ambonese Malay; Arabic devotional terms; Dutch for colonial records; Old Javanese and Sundanese manuscript texts. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Osing and other East Javanese communities after the 1998 sorcerer killings.
- Balinese, with *leak* beliefs and the Calon Arang tradition.
- Sundanese and Javanese, for the images each keeps of the other.
- Dayak and Madurese communities in Central and West Kalimantan, for enemy images after Sampit and Sambas.
- Muslim and Christian villages of Maluku and Poso; Makassarese and Bugis with *doti* and *parakang* beliefs.
- Chinese-Indonesians, often cast as the outside "other" in crises.
- Families of 1965 detainees; Ahmadiyah and LGBT Indonesians, targets of later moral panics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1965, 1998 and communal conflicts; mixed for witchcraft belief; thin for everyday couple-level projection, which needs psychology studies or fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese (Osing), Balinese, Makassarese; Dutch for 1740 sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can endanger the accused; ethnic-violence material; survivors of 1965 and 1998. Report each group's images of others as claims, never as findings. Revenge belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
