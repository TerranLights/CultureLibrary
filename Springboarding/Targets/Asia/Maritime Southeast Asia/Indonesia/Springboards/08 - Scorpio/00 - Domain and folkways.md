# Indonesia: Domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Balinese, Minangkabau, Batak (Toba, Karo), Buginese, Makassarese, Banjar, Sasak, Sa'dan Toraja, Ngaju and other Dayak languages, Manado and Ambonese Malay, Hokkien; Dutch for colonial records; Arabic and Old Javanese (Kawi) for older texts. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how a people faces death, power and what is hidden, with its folkways of death, sex, shared resources and inheritance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of the Mataram heartland against the East Java coast:** court-derived death ways against pesantren piety.
- **Sundanese:** their own taboo vocabulary (*pamali*); not to be folded into "Java".
- **Madurese:** honor and inheritance customs unlike their Javanese neighbors'.
- **Balinese Hindus:** cremation, caste rank and temple-run water.
- **Minangkabau:** matrilineal property inside a Muslim society.
- **Batak Toba and Karo:** patrilineal clans and reburial monuments.
- **Sa'dan Toraja:** funeral feasts that drive the local economy.
- **Bugis and Makassarese:** honor (*siri'*) and their own gender categories.
- **Dayak peoples by river system:** secondary burial; Kaharingan and Christian layers.
- **Sumba, Flores, West Timor; Minahasa; the Moluccas; Sasak:** Marapu megaliths, Christian death ways, village alliances, Wetu Telu.
- **Chinese Indonesians, Arab Indonesians (Hadhrami descent), Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo):** distinct grave, marriage and family-money ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the ethnography of death; mixed for survey data on money and sex; thin for regional detail on secrecy. Death registration is incomplete outside cities.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese and Dutch for archives; regional languages for rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1965 is politically charged; LGBT contacts need care; some mortuary and initiatory knowledge (Marapu priests, Kanekes Baduy, Toraja *to minaa*) is kept by its holders. Law on inheritance and sex belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; written for the core islands. Aceh and western New Guinea are separate targets.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a possible unit for death and family customs (category only).
