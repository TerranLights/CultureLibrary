# Indonesia: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Buginese, Nias, Mentawai, Sundanese; Arabic for Islamic terms; Old Javanese (Kawi) for older texts. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and the return of the dead.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balinese:** whether rebirth is reckoned inside the family line.
- **Tengger and other Hindu Javanese:** conversion as refuge after 1965.
- **Karo Batak:** a wave of church joining in the late 1960s, with its own story.
- **Urban middle-class Muslims of Jakarta and Bandung:** *hijrah* as a style of being new.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** name change, Pentecostal growth and Muslim conversion in one community.
- **Bugis *bissu*:** initiation as sacred rebirth; check how piety movements bear on it.
- **Nias and Mentawai:** ordeal and tattoo as becoming adult.
- **Yogyakarta and Klaten quake survivors; Merapi villages:** rebuilding against moving.
- **Former militants in Lamongan and Poso (Central Sulawesi):** reform watched by neighbors.
- **Migrant domestic workers returning from Hong Kong:** comparison only; their new faith or style tests what home accepts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for conversion and ritual ethnography; mixed for recovery; thin for measured belief in reincarnation or in whether people change.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Balinese, Buginese; Arabic for Islamic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious conversion is politically touchy and leaving Islam may be hard to discuss openly; *bissu* rites belong to their holders; deradicalization data sits with security agencies. Rules on religious registration go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own teachings on rebirth (category only).
