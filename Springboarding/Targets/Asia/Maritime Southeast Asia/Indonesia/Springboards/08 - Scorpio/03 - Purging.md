# Indonesia: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Osing, Banjar, Buginese; Hokkien and Indonesian usage among Chinese Indonesians; Arabic for Islamic purity terms; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of Yogyakarta and Surakarta:** court-centered *ruwatan* forms.
- **Osing of Banyuwangi:** the 1998 sorcerer killings as a local purge.
- **Balinese:** purification organized by temple and *banjar* (neighborhood council).
- **Banjar and Malay coastal Muslims:** Safar bathing to ward off misfortune.
- **Bugis and Makassarese:** the *mappacci* night and other pre-wedding cleansing.
- **Cina Benteng of Tangerang:** Peh Cun bathing in the Cisadane.
- **Muhammadiyah and Salafi circles against Nahdlatul Ulama:** what each wants purged from practice as innovation (*bid'ah*).
- **Families of 1965 political prisoners:** those certified "unclean" by the state.
- ***Waria*, sex workers and street vendors:** groups most often cleared from public space.
- **Political exiles stranded abroad after 1965:** comparison only; the purge reached them through lost passports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual ethnography and 1965 history; mixed for urban clearance campaigns; thin for bodily cleansing theories outside Java.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Balinese; Arabic for fiqh; Dutch for VOC and colonial records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1965 and the 1998 sorcerer killings remain raw for families; *waria* and sex workers face real danger from exposure; some Balinese and Javanese rites are led by priests who may restrict recording. Law on raids belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own cleansing festivals (category only).
