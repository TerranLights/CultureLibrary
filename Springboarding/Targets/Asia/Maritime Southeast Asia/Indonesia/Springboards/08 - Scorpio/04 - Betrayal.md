# Indonesia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Minangkabau, Buginese and Makassarese, Ambonese Malay; Old Malay and Old Javanese for inscriptions and chronicles; Dutch for colonial and revolution-era records; Arabic for Islamic terms. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese wayang audiences:** whether Kumbakarna or Wibisana is admired.
- **Bugis of Bone against Makassarese of Gowa:** opposite verdicts on Arung Palakka.
- **Moluccans (Ambon and Lease islands):** KNIL service and the 1950 separatist republic as loyalty contests.
- **Minangkabau:** the Malin Kundang tale set beside the reality of men who migrate (*merantau*).
- **Chinese Indonesians:** repeatedly cast as disloyal; how they tell it themselves.
- **Families of 1965 detainees:** branded traitors for decades.
- **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo):** caught between sides in 1945-1950.
- **Madurese:** betrayal of honor as a trigger for feuds (see Revenge).
- **Urban middle-class couples:** affairs exposed on social media.
- **Moluccan community in the Netherlands:** comparison only; their account of promises broken by the Dutch.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state narratives and legend; mixed for infidelity (stigmatized, under-reported); thin for measured attitudes on informing.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Buginese and Makassarese; Dutch for 1945-1950; Old Malay for inscriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1965 label "traitor" is still used against families; abuse in religious institutions involves victims who need protection; treason and adultery law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own stories of loyalty (category only).
