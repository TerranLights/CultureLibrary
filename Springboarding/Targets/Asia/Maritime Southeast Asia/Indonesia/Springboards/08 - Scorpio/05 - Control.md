## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of the court cities against the north-coast towns:** an ideal of restraint, and how far it holds outside court circles.
- **Batak and Javanese in popular talk:** often set against each other as loud and restrained; test that contrast rather than repeat it.
- **Balinese:** trance as controlled loss of control inside temple ritual.
- **Ponorogo and East Java *reog* troupes:** frenzy, *warok* strongmen and their followers.
- **Minangkabau households:** whether control of property and of public affairs splits by gender as often described.
- **Rural women in Java and Bali in the family-planning era:** the main targets of reproductive control.
- **Pesantren pupils:** discipline under a *kiai*.
- **Chinese-Indonesian family firms:** control held by a founder and passed on.
- **Gig workers in Jakarta, Surabaya and Medan:** app control and resistance.
- **Indonesian domestic workers in Hong Kong and the Gulf:** comparison only; live-in work shows employer control at its tightest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the New Order state and Javanese ethics; mixed for couples' control (survey items exist, under-reporting likely); thin for measured locus of control.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese (including the courtly register); Balinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence survey data needs protection; trance rites may restrict recording; censorship and family-planning coercion are politically sensitive. Discipline and obedience as such belong to Capricorn; law on censorship goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own household and trance practices (category only).
