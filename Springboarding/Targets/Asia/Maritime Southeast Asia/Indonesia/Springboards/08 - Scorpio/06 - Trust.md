## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moluccan Christians and Muslims:** village alliances as trust across faiths.
- **Dayak credit-union members:** how trust is scaled up from longhouse to cooperative.
- **Bugis traders and sailors:** patron-follower loyalty at sea and in trade.
- **Minangkabau migrants:** shared-profit trade networks far from home.
- **Chinese-Indonesian traders:** whether credit rests on name and family ties, and how outside suspicion bears on them.
- **Javanese villagers:** shop credit, *arisan*, and the neighborhood as guarantor.
- **Madurese:** oaths and honor as guarantees.
- **Urban online shoppers and sellers:** platforms in place of personal knowledge.
- **Poso (Central Sulawesi) after its conflict:** trust between faiths rebuilt or not.
- **Indonesian workers in Malaysia sending money home by informal couriers:** comparison only; trust where banks are out of reach.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for polling on institutions; mixed for interpersonal trust by region; thin for loyalty as a measured idea.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Ambonese Malay, regional languages; Old Javanese for inscriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fraud victims may be ashamed to speak; inter-faith trust in Maluku and Poso touches recent killings; some pela oaths are held as sacred by the villages. Trust in police and courts goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own trade-trust networks (category only).
