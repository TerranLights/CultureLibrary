# Indonesia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (with its speech levels), Balinese, Sundanese, Madurese, Buginese and Makassarese, Sasak, Minangkabau, Sumbanese; Arabic for Islamic terms; Old Javanese and Dutch for older records. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how who holds it, how it is held and how it is used are judged.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of the court cities against the north coast and East Java's "horseshoe":** court ideas of power against merchant and *kiai* power.
- **Balinese:** palace (*puri*) houses, title groups and the temple network.
- **Bugis and Makassarese:** nobles descended from a founding ruler, and the authority of traders.
- **Sasak (Lombok):** the *menak* nobility and *tuan guru* religious leaders.
- **Minangkabau:** lineage heads, women elders and religious scholars as three seats of power.
- **Batak Toba and Karo:** whether clan relations (*dalihan na tolu*) or chiefs carry power.
- **Banten and Madura:** strongmen beside *kiai*, and family dynasties in provincial office.
- **Sumba:** noble houses and the descendants of former bondsmen.
- **Chinese-Indonesian tycoons:** how economic weight does or does not turn into political power; ties to military patrons.
- **Toraja:** rank confirmed through feasts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elites, Java and Bali; mixed for outer-island local power; measures of power distance are country-level only.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese (all speech levels), Balinese, Buginese; Dutch for colonial reports on rulers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** legacy hierarchies on Sumba and in Bali touch descent-based status; military power after 1965 is politically sensitive; *jawara* and street-organization research carries risk. Legitimate authority and hierarchy belong to Capricorn; corruption law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own business and temple leadership (category only).
