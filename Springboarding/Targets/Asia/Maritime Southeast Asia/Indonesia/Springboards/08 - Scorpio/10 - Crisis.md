# Indonesia: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages on Merapi's slopes:** spiritual guardianship against official evacuation.
- **Sidoarjo residents:** a slow crisis with a company in the middle.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** the 1998 riots as their crisis within the national one.
- **Sasak of Lombok and Kaili of Central Sulawesi:** the 2018 quakes and their local rites of recovery.
- **Balinese tourism workers:** crises that empty the island (2002 and 2005 bombings, COVID-19).
- **Banten and Lampung coasts:** Krakatau in 1883 and its 2018 tsunami.
- **Sumbawa:** Tambora's eruption (1815) in local memory.
- **East Nusa Tenggara:** drought, hunger seasons and Cyclone Seroja (2021).
- **Jakarta's riverbank poor:** floods and eviction as recurring crises.
- **Migrant workers sent home from Malaysia in 1998 and 2020:** comparison only; crisis at home and abroad at once.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and the 1997-98 crisis; mixed for epidemics before 1950; thin for personal crises (stigma; little measurement).
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, regional languages of affected areas; Malay in Jawi script for older poems; Dutch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** May 1998 sexual violence survivors need protection; *pasung* involves people unable to consent to research; blame framing ("azab") can wound victims. Emergency law and powers go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for their own experience of the 1998 crisis (category only).
