## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** north-coast Islam against the Yogyakarta and Surakarta courts' mystical literature.
- **Sundanese**, with Baduy and Cigugur faith communities apart; **Madurese**, whose kiai networks are their own system.
- **Balinese** and the **Tengger** of East Java: two Hindu-heritage communities, two histories.
- **Sasak** of Lombok (Wetu Telu beside orthodox Islam); **Minangkabau** (matrilineal custom against Islamic reform; home of merantau).
- **Batak**: Toba (mostly Protestant, with Parmalim) against Mandailing (mostly Muslim).
- **Bugis and Makassar** against **Toraja** (Christian and Aluk Todolo).
- **Minahasa**, **Ambon and Lease** (Christian and Muslim villages), **Ternate and Tidore** (sultanate Islam).
- **Flores, Sumba and Timor** (Catholic, Protestant, Marapu); **Dayak** peoples (Kaharingan) against Muslim **Banjar**.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, **Arab-Indonesians** (Hadhrami families), **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo)**.
- Diaspora, comparison only: Suriname's Javanese, to test which domain traits travel with migrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organized religion; mixed for philosophy; thin, and often outsider-mediated, for local faiths and small islands.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese (with Krama), Arabic, Dutch; Old Javanese; Buginese, Balinese, Batak.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interreligious violence (Maluku, Poso), religion in the 1965-1966 killings, minority sects' standing. Blasphemy, religion-registration and education law: Libra or Capricorn. Some Kaharingan, Marapu and Baduy knowledge is restricted; follow each community's published accounts.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the core lands. Aceh and West Papua are separate targets in this setting and are not written here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and Islamic movements founded in South Asia, as categories.
