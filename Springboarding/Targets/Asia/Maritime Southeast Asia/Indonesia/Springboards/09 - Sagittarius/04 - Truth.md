*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** court culture, where chronicles mix record and prophecy, against reformist Muslims who set scripture as the measure.
- **Minangkabau**, where truth sits at the top of a stated chain of consensus.
- **Bugis**, with a long habit of written court diaries and lontaraq records as evidence.
- **Batak**, whose genealogies (*tarombo*) are kept as true records and argued over.
- **Balinese**, where clan *babad* settle, or unsettle, claims to rank.
- **Madurese** and **Bantenese**, where the shroud oath is reported.
- **Osing** of Banyuwangi, near the center of the 1998 sorcery-rumor killings.
- **North Sumatra** (Medan region), where 1965 perpetrators' accounts were filmed.
- **Ambon** and **Poso** (Central Sulawesi), where rumor by text message fed conflict around 2000.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, for whom the truth of the May 1998 violence remains disputed.
- **Urban and rural** users with different access to news and fact-checks.

## 4. Flags
