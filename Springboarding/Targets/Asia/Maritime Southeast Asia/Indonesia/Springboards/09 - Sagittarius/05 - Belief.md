*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese; Sundanese; Balinese; Batak Toba; North Maluku and Nusa Tenggara local languages for witchcraft terms; Hokkien for temple practice. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** kejawen households that consult primbon, against reformist families who reject such reckoning.
- **Sundanese**, and the **Baduy** whose taboos (*pikukuh*) are especially strict, as they describe them.
- **Madurese**, where amulets from kiai and fear of sorcery both run strong (to be tested).
- **Balinese**, whose belief is partly written in lontar and partly lived through balian.
- **Batak**, where old spirit belief survives under Christianity in specific forms.
- **North Maluku** (Halmahera) and **Nusa Tenggara**, where suanggi belief matters to social life.
- **Kalimantan Dayak**, where Kaharingan belief is organized under the state's Hindu category.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, whose temple and geomancy practice runs beside Christianity or Buddhism.
- **Urban middle-class** audiences of horror media, where belief and play blur.
- **Nonbelievers**, a small and hidden group whose stated reasons need their own channel.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief and horror media; mixed for measured belief (few public items beyond God and morality); thin for disbelief.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese for primbon; Balinese and Batak for manuscripts; eastern Malay varieties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations and killings; atheists' safety; Baduy and Kaharingan restricted knowledge (use only what communities publish). Blasphemy and registration law: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
