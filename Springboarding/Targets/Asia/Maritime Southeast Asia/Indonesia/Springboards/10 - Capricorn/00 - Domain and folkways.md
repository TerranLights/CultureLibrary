## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese heartland (Yogyakarta, Surakarta) against the north coast and East Java:** court-centered rank and speech against port and city settings.
- **Sundanese and Bantenese:** the menak gentry; Banten's jawara strongmen.
- **Minangkabau:** matrilineal titles held through the mother's line; authority split three ways.
- **Toba, Karo and Mandailing Batak:** clan (marga) obligations and different Christian and Muslim settings.
- **Bugis and Makassar:** ranked nobility and siri' in a migrant, seafaring frame.
- **Balinese:** title groups and village-temple duties.
- **Dayak peoples of Kalimantan:** ranked Kenyah and Kayan against flatter Iban and Meratus groups.
- **Madurese, Sasak, Toraja, Sumba, Moluccan and Minahasa communities:** own chiefs, elders or nobility.
- **Chinese Indonesians and Arab Indonesians:** their own rank orders (the sayyid question among Hadhrami families).
- **Migrant workers abroad and Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo):** comparison only, for duty to kin kept from afar.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java, Bali and the Minangkabau; mixed for Sulawesi and Batak lands; thin for many eastern islands and Kalimantan interior groups. **The nation's exact lands are still to be settled;** this sheet is written for the core lands.
