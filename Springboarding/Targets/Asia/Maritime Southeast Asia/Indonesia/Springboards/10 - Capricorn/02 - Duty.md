# Indonesia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian, Javanese, Balinese, Toba Batak, Minangkabau, Minahasan Malay, Hokkien-influenced Chinese-Indonesian usage, Arabic religious terms, Dutch. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minangkabau:** the mother's brother carries duties to his sister's children; changing with merantau.
- **Toba, Karo and Mandailing Batak:** feast and funeral obligations by clan role.
- **Balinese:** ngayah and village levies as heavy, specific duties.
- **Javanese court milieu and abdi dalem families:** service to the kraton.
- **Toraja:** funeral obligations and the debts in buffalo and pigs they create.
- **Minahasa and other Christian communities of North Sulawesi and Maluku:** mapalus and church duties.
- **Sasak, Sumbawa and Flores villages:** migrant-sending areas where remittance duty is concentrated.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** filial and ancestral duties at home altars and clan temples.
- **Urban middle class in Jakarta and Surabaya:** the "generasi sandwich" debate.
- **Migrant workers abroad (Malaysia, Gulf, Hong Kong, Taiwan):** comparison only, for duty performed at a distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on remittances, zakat and ritual duties; mixed on care of the elderly; thin on how duty is felt.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese and Batak for rites; Arabic for fiqh on obligation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exploitation of migrant workers; forced "kerja bakti" under the New Order; Balinese and Toraja funeral rites are sacred; ask before recording.
- **Law:** duties set by statute (marriage law, taxes) are Libra's; this sheet takes their spirit (Law, concept 8).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
