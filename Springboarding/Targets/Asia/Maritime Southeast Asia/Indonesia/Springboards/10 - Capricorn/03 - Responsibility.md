*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese priyayi and urban professionals:** the graceful stepping-aside (legawa) ideal against public pressure to resign; to test.
- **Minangkabau:** the mamak's answerability for his kin set against reformist stress on personal effort.
- **Balinese:** collective sanctions on households through the banjar.
- **Dayak communities of Central and West Kalimantan:** adat fines paid by kin groups.
- **Toraja and Batak:** debts that pass to heirs at funerals.
- **Santri families (NU and Muhammadiyah):** different stresses on takdir and ikhtiar; to research.
- **East Java's Sidoarjo residents:** a living case of disputed blame.
- **Chinese-Indonesian business families:** family liability for firms and debts.
- **Women in microcredit groups:** collective liability in daily life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official accountability (audits, KPK, accident reports); mixed on adat sanctions; thin on how blame is felt in families.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Balinese and Dayak terms; Arabic for fiqh; Dutch for colonial-era adat records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1965 killings and later rights cases; victims' privacy in disasters; adat sanctions are internal community matters.
- **Law:** the age of criminal responsibility and liability rules are Libra's letter; here only their spirit, see Law (concept 8).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
