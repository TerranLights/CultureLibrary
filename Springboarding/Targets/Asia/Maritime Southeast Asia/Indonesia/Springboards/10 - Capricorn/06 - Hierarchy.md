# Indonesia: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Balinese and Sasak (all with speech levels); Buginese and Makassarese; Toraja; Sumbanese languages; Kayan and Kenyah; Arabic for Hadhrami sources; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yogyakarta and Surakarta court towns against Banyumas (ngapak speakers):** heavy level-marking against a reputedly plainer speech area; to test.
- **Sundanese ménak families and ordinary villagers.**
- **Balinese triwangsa and jaba households:** marriage across title groups.
- **Bugis and Makassar nobility (Andi titles) and free commoners.**
- **Toraja strata after the end of slavery and the rise of new wealth.**
- **Sumba and Flores (Ngada, Manggarai):** legacies of servitude.
- **Kayan and Kenyah against Iban and Meratus Dayak:** ranked and less ranked.
- **Minangkabau:** matrilineal titles against Islamic and state ranks.
- **Hadhrami Arab families:** sayyid and non-sayyid lines.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** peranakan and totok distinctions, and old officer families (Kapitan, Majoor).
- **Women:** the rank-gender relation, especially in Minangkabau and Bali.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java, Bali and South Sulawesi; mixed for eastern islands; thin on present-day attitudes to inherited rank.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian plus the speech-level languages; Dutch colonial records; Arabic for Hadhrami sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** descendants of slaves in Sumba, Toraja and Flores; Balinese marriage across title groups; colorism; anti-Chinese prejudice.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
