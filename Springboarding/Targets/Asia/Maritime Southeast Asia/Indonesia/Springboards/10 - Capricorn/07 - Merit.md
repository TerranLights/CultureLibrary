*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Civil-service families in Java and outer-island provincial capitals:** the PNS career as earned or arranged.
- **Nias:** merit by feasting, set against inherited rank.
- **Lampung Pepadun and Saibatin:** earned titles against inherited ones.
- **Minangkabau:** elected penghulu titles within matrilines.
- **Balinese and Javanese aristocratic families:** inherited rank under pressure from schooling.
- **Pesantren families:** the kyai's son (gus) succeeding by birth, against scholars who rise by learning.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** merit in business against barriers to state careers in past eras.
- **Migrants from eastern Nusa Tenggara and Maluku:** how distance from Jakarta's networks shapes access to posts; to research.
- **Students abroad on LPDP and other scholarships:** comparison only, for merit judged by foreign institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on civil-service and school admissions; mixed on private-sector hiring; thin on feasts of merit today.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Old Javanese for charters; Nias and Lampung terms; Dutch for colonial ethnographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption allegations about named people; the Suharto hero dispute; "titipan" claims are often unproven.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
