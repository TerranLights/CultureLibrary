# Indonesia: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Dutch; Arabic; Balinese, Minangkabau, Buginese, Ambonese Malay, Kei and Dayak terms; Riau Malay. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it. Spirit only; the letter belongs to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minangkabau:** the adat-and-Islam settlement and nagari courts.
- **Balinese:** desa adat rules running beside state law.
- **Bugis-Makassar:** pangadereng and siri' as the frame for wrongs.
- **Maluku (Ambon, Kei):** customary compacts in reconciliation.
- **Dayak of Central Kalimantan:** the damang and adat councils.
- **Toba Batak women and men:** forum choice in inheritance.
- **Javanese santri and abangan milieus:** use of religious courts; to research.
- **Samin communities:** law kept at a distance on principle.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** colonial legal-status categories and their afterlife.
- **Penghayat kepercayaan (followers of local faiths):** recognition struggles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in legal anthropology; mixed on everyday legal consciousness; thin for small eastern societies.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Dutch, Arabic, regional terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** impunity for 1965 to 1966; blasphemy cases; adat land conflicts; some adat proceedings are closed to outsiders.
- **Letter and spirit:** the wording of any statute or rule named here goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
