# Indonesia: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (including court literature); Balinese; Old Malay for Srivijaya inscriptions; Uab Meto (Atoni) and Toraja terms; Arabic; Chinese; Dutch and Japanese for occupation-era records. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese court towns:** order as cosmology and as speech etiquette.
- **Balinese villages:** temple and subak order beside state order.
- **Atoni, Rotenese and other Timor and Nusa Tenggara peoples:** house and ritual order.
- **Toraja:** ritual order of the ancestors under church and tourism.
- **Maluku and Central Sulawesi (Poso):** order lost and rebuilt after communal conflict.
- **Madurese:** honor fights (carok) and the order they claim to restore; handle with care.
- **Jakarta's Betawi and migrant neighborhoods:** preman, ormas and RT order.
- **West Kalimantan and Central Kalimantan:** 1997 and 2001 Dayak-Madurese violence and later peace pacts.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** targets of the 1998 riots; how they speak of public safety since.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on violence and policing since 1998; rich on Javanese and Balinese cosmology; thin on everyday household order outside Java.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Balinese; Old Malay; Japanese for occupation sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1965 to 1966 killings, Petrus, May 1998 sexual violence, Poso, Maluku and Sampit; survivors first, no graphic detail; some ritual knowledge (Toraja, Bali) is restricted.
- **Law:** public-order statutes are Libra's letter; their purpose is in Law (concept 8).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands (category only).
