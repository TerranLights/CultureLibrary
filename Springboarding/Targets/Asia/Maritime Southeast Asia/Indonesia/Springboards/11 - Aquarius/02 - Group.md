# Indonesia: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Madurese, Minangkabau, Batak Toba, Buginese, Balinese; Hokkien for Chinese clan bodies; Dutch for colonial ethnography. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit; how the person is weighed against the group, where "us" and "them" are drawn, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Santri and abangan Javanese:** a religious-cultural line that once ordered parties and marriages.
- **Javanese and the outer islands:** resentment of Java-centered rule as an "us and them".
- **Pribumi and Chinese Indonesians:** a boundary drawn by the state and by riots (1998), and crossed in daily life.
- **Minangkabau moieties and Batak clans:** groups by descent with their own ways of deciding.
- **Bugis and Makassar:** honor held collectively by kin.
- **Balinese descent groups (*soroh*):** rank and temple membership as group.
- **Madurese and Dayak in Kalimantan; Christians and Muslims in Maluku and Poso:** places where group lines turned violent.
- **Football supporters, student groups and youth organizations:** chosen groups, mostly of young men.
- **Arab Indonesians:** descent-based subgroups within one minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on conflict and politics; thinner on everyday groups outside Java. Census ethnicity data exist only for some rounds.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Dutch; regional languages for clan and moiety terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1965-1966 killings; the May 1998 riots against Chinese Indonesians; Maluku, Poso and Sampit; football deaths. Treat survivors' accounts first and with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
