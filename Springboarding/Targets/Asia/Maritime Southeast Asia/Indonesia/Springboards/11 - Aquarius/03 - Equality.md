# Indonesia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese (its speech levels are central here), Balinese, Minangkabau, Buginese, Sumbanese; Arabic for Hadhrami debates; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese of the old court towns and of East Java:** speech levels and *priyayi* rank in Yogyakarta and Surakarta against the *arek* speech of Surabaya and Malang, where register use may differ.
- **Minangkabau:** the two customary traditions (one ranked, one leveling, by their own accounts) and matrilineal standing.
- **Balinese:** commoners and the three high ranks.
- **Bugis, Makassar, Sumbanese, Toraja:** noble, free and slave-descended ranks in living memory.
- **Arab Indonesians:** *sayyid* and non-*sayyid*.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** equal citizenship against decades of separate rules (Libra or Capricorn for the law itself).
- **Women and men; urban and rural; Java and the eastern provinces:** gaps the statistics office measures.
- **Sexual and gender minorities:** contested claims to equal standing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on income and on Javanese language; good on Bali and on women's movements; thin on rank in the eastern islands outside a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Dutch; Arabic for Hadhrami sources; Buginese for chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slave descent in eastern islands; anti-Chinese violence; LGBT issues under current pressure; skin color.
- **Held for review:** none.
