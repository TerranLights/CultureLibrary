# Indonesia: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese; Minangkabau; Balinese; Arabic for Islamic reform terms; Dutch for the colonial reform era. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muhammadiyah and Nahdlatul Ulama circles:** modernist and traditionalist circles, which may set different reform agendas; check by region.
- **Minangkabau:** a long record of reform quarrels between custom and Islam.
- **Javanese *kejawen* adherents and official faiths:** pressure to reform private practice into recognized religion.
- **Balinese:** reformed, standardized Hinduism against village variety; *ajeg Bali* as resistance to change.
- **Peoples of indigenous faiths (Kaharingan Dayak, Marapu on Sumba, Toraja *Aluk To Dolo*):** people reformed by others, in their own accounts.
- **Students and urban middle classes:** central to the 1966 and 1998 movements in most accounts; check whose voices those accounts leave out.
- **Farmers and fishers in land and coastal conflicts:** reform from below.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** the 1900s THHK school movement as their own reform.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Islamic modernism and on 1998; mixed on Bali; thin on reform as eastern-island peoples tell it.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Dutch, Arabic; Minangkabau and Javanese for local sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1960s land actions and the killings after them; disappearances of 1997-1998 activists; pressure on liberal Islamic voices.
- **Held for review:** none.
