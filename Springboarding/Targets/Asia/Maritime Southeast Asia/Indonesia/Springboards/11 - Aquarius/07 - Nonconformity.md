## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sedulur Sikep (Samin) in Central and East Java:** refusal as a way of life over generations.
- **Followers of Sunda Wiwitan, Kaharingan, Marapu and other ancestral faiths:** religious difference long pressed to conform to recognized faiths.
- **Bugis communities in South Sulawesi:** named categories for gender difference, as Bugis sources and Davies describe them; check how far they reach beyond ritual life today.
- **Javanese performance traditions (*ludruk*, *lengger*, *reog*):** licensed cross-gender roles.
- **Urban punks, metalheads and indie scenes in Jakarta, Bandung, Yogyakarta and Bali:** chosen subcultures, mostly young men.
- **Groups labeled *sesat* (Ahmadiyya, Gafatar):** difference treated as a threat, with expulsions.
- **Artists in Yogyakarta:** the town's reputation for tolerating eccentrics, to be tested.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** pressure to assimilate in names and worship under the New Order.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Java and Bali music scenes and on Bugis gender; mixed on the Samin; thin on everyday eccentrics in the eastern islands.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian slang, Javanese, Buginese; Dutch for colonial reports on the Samin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT people face raids and local bylaws; *aliran sesat* accusations put people at risk; *bissu* rites may be restricted to insiders. The legal side of blasphemy and recognition is for Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
