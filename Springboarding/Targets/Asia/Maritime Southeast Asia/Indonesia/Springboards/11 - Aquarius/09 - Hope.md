*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese villagers in Banten and Central Java:** awaited-deliverer movements as hope in crisis.
- **Santri and pilgrims:** hope through prayer, vows and saints' tombs.
- **Catholic and Protestant minorities (Flores, Minahasa, Batak, Java):** Marian shrines and church hope.
- **Balinese:** blessings and offerings for well-being; and Bali's tourism-dependent hope after 2002, 2005 and 2020.
- **Chinese Indonesians:** temple lots and hope after decades of restriction.
- **Migrant-sending districts (East Nusa Tenggara, West Nusa Tenggara, East Java):** hope placed in work abroad.
- **Victims of disaster and state violence:** hope as waiting.
- **Young urban people:** hope and doubt about 2045, jobs and housing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on millenarian movements and official visions; good on consumer expectations; thin on hope as people in the eastern islands voice it.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Javanese for prophetic and court texts; Old Malay for inscriptions; Hokkien for temple practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the disappeared; suicide (follow safe-reporting guidance); gambling is illegal, so *togel* research touches crime.
- **Held for review:** none.
