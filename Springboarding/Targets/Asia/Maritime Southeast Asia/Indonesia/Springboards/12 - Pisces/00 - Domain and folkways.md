# Indonesia: The hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese, Minangkabau, Batak, Buginese; Pegon (Arabic-script) pesantren terms; Hokkien; Dutch. **Concept in one line:** the hidden and transcendence: how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with magic ways (healing, charms, divination, sorcery) as the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese**, split three ways: court cities (Yogyakarta, Surakarta) with *kebatinan* circles; the East Java pesantren belt; the Osing of Banyuwangi.
- **Sundanese**, and the Baduy (Kanekes) and Sunda Wiwitan followers, who keep their own rules on the hidden.
- **Balinese** Hindus, and the Sasak of Lombok (Wetu Telu practice against orthodox Islam).
- **Minangkabau**: Sufi orders and reformist Islam differ on charms and healing.
- **Batak** peoples: Toba Protestants, Muslim Mandailing, Karo with spirit-medium rites, Parmalim followers.
- **Toraja**, **Bugis-Makassar** (the *bissu* ritual specialists) and **Minahasa** Christians in Sulawesi.
- **Dayak** peoples of Kalimantan (Kaharingan practice) beside Muslim **Banjar**.
- **Mentawai**, **Nias**, **Sumba** (Marapu), **Flores** Catholics and **Maluku** (Ambon, Halmahera): outer-island spirit and healing traditions.
- **Chinese Indonesians** (temple ghost rites), **Hadhrami Arab descendants** (saint veneration), **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo)** for colonial-era fear and confinement.
- Migrant workers abroad: comparison only, for how charms and healers travel with them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java and Bali; mixed for Sumatra and Sulawesi; thin for many eastern peoples. Health data rarely asks about the unseen.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for its core lands. Aceh and West Papua are separate targets and are not covered here.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese (with *krama*), Balinese, Sundanese, Dutch, Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations have led to killings; the 1965-66 violence; people kept in *pasung*; followers of *kepercayaan* faiths under pressure; restricted ritual knowledge of Marapu, Kaharingan, Baduy and Mentawai healers, used only as each community shares it. Law on sorcery and religious deviance: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands, and South Asian works and scholars on the archipelago's religious history.
