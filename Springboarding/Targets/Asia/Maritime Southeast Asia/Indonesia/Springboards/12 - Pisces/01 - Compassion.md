# Indonesia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Balinese, Minahasan Malay; Arabic for zakat and alms terms; Mandarin and Hokkien for Chinese Indonesian charity. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Santri Muslims through NU and Muhammadiyah** (LAZISNU, Lazismu): two ways of organizing alms, one rooted in pesantren and kiai, one in schools and hospitals.
- **Javanese court and village ideals of *welas asih***, to compare with the Sundanese *silih asih* triad in West Java.
- **Balinese banjar** (ward associations): help to a family in crisis given as a ward duty rather than as a personal gift.
- **Minahasa** with *mapalus* labor sharing and Protestant church diaconate; **Batak** congregations (HKBP) and their deacon work.
- **Flores and wider NTT Catholics**, where parishes and religious orders run much of the care for the sick and poor.
- **Chinese Indonesians**: temple charity, funeral associations and Buddhist foundations, and memories of 1998 that shape whom they help and how publicly.
- **Maluku** Christians and Muslims, where *pela* bonds across faith shaped aid after the 1999-2002 conflict.
- **Urban middle class online donors** against **rural mutual help** in Java and Sumatra.
- Indonesians abroad: comparison only, to see whether remittances are spoken of as compassion or as duty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islamic giving and disaster relief; mixed for everyday pity and its tone; thin for animal compassion outside Bali and Java.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Sundanese, Arabic for fiqh of alms, Mandarin for Tzu Chi material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** aid tied to proselytizing is a live accusation between faiths; post-conflict aid in Maluku and Central Sulawesi; mercy toward offenders (sentence remissions) is law and goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: Fauzia, *Faith and the State*; then search Google Scholar for "filantropi Islam Indonesia" and "Hilman Latief".
2. BAZNAS site: download *Outlook Zakat Indonesia* and note the categories of recipients (*mustahik*).
3. BPS site: search "Statistik Modal Sosial" and list the helping and giving items.
4. Kompas archive: "Dompet Kemanusiaan" with "gempa Yogyakarta 2006" and "Palu 2018".
5. Search "Talang Tuwo prasasti terjemahan" for a translated edition.
