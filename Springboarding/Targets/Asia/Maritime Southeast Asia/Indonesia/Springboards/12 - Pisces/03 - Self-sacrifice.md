# Indonesia: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Balinese, Toba Batak, Minangkabau; Arabic for martyrdom and offering terms; Portuguese-derived Catholic terms of Flores; Mandarin for filial duty. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Balinese**, for whom *puputan* is a living public memory.
- **Javanese priyayi** heirs of the *Serat Tripama* ideal, against **santri** Javanese who frame sacrifice through *kurban* and service to the kiai.
- **Toba Batak** families who sell land to school their children, and **Minangkabau** men whose duties run to their sisters' children.
- **Toraja**, where funeral spending tests family devotion.
- **Flores and Larantuka Catholics**, and **Minahasa** and **Ambon** Protestants, for Christian forms of offering.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, where filial duty and the family business ask for different sacrifices.
- **Migrant-sending districts** (NTT, East Lombok, Indramayu and Cilacap) against wealthier regions.
- **Military and police families** (TNI, Polri) and their own rites for the fallen.
- Indonesians working abroad: comparison only, to set their own accounts of what they gave up beside the "foreign-exchange hero" label used at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for heroes, war memory and migrant labor; mixed for mothers' and caregivers' sacrifice; thin for minority faiths' self-denial practices.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese (including Old Javanese for kakawin), Balinese, Arabic, Dutch for colonial accounts of *puputan*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** militant martyrdom claims (Bali bombings 2002 and 2005); state hero choices are political (1965, Papua, Aceh, the latter two being separate targets); migrant abuse cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesia (a category only; nothing named).

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Ministry of Social Affairs site for "daftar pahlawan nasional" and sort by province and year.
2. Library: an Indonesian edition of *Serat Tripama*, then "Kumbakarna gugur" lakon recordings.
3. BP2MI data portal: "data penempatan PMI per provinsi"; pair with Bank Indonesia remittance tables.
4. Search "puputan Badung 1906 sumber Belanda" for Dutch and Balinese accounts side by side.
5. Google Scholar: "pengorbanan ibu" with "budaya Jawa" OR "Batak" for studies of parents' sacrifice.
