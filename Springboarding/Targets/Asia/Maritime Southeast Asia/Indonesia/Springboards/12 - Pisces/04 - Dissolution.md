# Indonesia: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kejawen Javanese** of Yogyakarta and Surakarta, for whom union teaching is old and esoteric.
- **Sufi-order santri** (NU-linked orders) against **reformist Muslims** (Muhammadiyah, Persis, Salafi circles), who reject union talk.
- **Balinese** temple communities, where trance is part of rites, and the **Osing** and **Cirebon** coast with their own trance dances.
- **Ponorogo** with *reog* and its *warok* figures.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, split between those who blended in after 1966 and those who kept or revived Chinese names and rites after 1998.
- **Betawi** of Jakarta, a people formed from many mixing into one.
- **Transmigrant descendants** in Sumatra and Kalimantan, whose Javanese identity has blended with local ones.
- **Maluku and North Sulawesi** speakers of fading languages.
- **Dutch-Indonesian Eurasians (Indo)**, whose community largely left after independence: a group coming apart.
- Indonesians settled abroad: comparison only, for how quickly language and rites fade outside the archipelago.
