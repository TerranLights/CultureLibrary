*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kejawen and *kepercayaan* followers** in Central and East Java, practicing outside mosque or church.
- **Urban santri** in Jakarta, Bandung and Surabaya (Sufi study circles, *hijrah* youth) against **village pesantren** practice.
- **Minangkabau**, with old Sufi orders at Ulakan and modernist critics.
- **Balinese** of North and South Bali, and **Tengger** highlanders.
- **Sundanese** Sunda Wiwitan at Cigugur and the Baduy.
- **Toba Batak** Parmalim beside Batak Protestant congregations.
- **Sumba** Marapu, **Toraja** Aluk To Dolo and **Central Kalimantan** Kaharingan practitioners, each as they share it.
- **Javanese Catholics** with inculturated shrines, and **Flores** Catholics with older Portuguese-era forms.
- **Chinese Indonesians** in temples, Buddhist centers and charismatic churches.
- Indonesians living abroad: comparison only, for online *kajian* and pilgrimages home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Java, Bali and Sufi revival; mixed for indigenous faiths (much is closed or oral); thin for measured spiritual experience.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Old Javanese, Balinese, Sundanese, Arabic.
