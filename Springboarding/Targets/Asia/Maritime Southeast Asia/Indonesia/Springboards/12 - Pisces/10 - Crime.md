# Indonesia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and street slang; Javanese, Betawi Malay, Madurese, Minangkabau, Buginese and Makassarese; Dutch for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** what counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how those who commit it are seen. Statute and courts belong to Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes crime as a cultural idea.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Betawi** and **Banten**, where the *jago* and *jawara* stand between bandit and protector.
- **Madurese**, at home and in East Java and Kalimantan, on *carok* and honor.
- **Bugis and Makassar**, where *siri'* can justify violence; check local critiques.
- **Yogyakarta** youth and the *klitih* panic, against the city's image of calm.
- **Minangkabau**, where shame falls on the matrilineal clan.
- **Balinese**, with village (*desa adat*) sanctions and the *pecalang* guards.
- **Toba Batak** and **Medan**, with their own strongman and organization networks.
- **Maluku** and **Poso**, where communal killings blurred crime and war.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, as frequent targets and as suspects in public talk of corruption.
- **Urban poor** in Jakarta and Surabaya, most exposed to raids and to the label "criminal".
- Indonesians imprisoned abroad or working in risky trades: comparison only, for how the home press judges them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption, banditry history and media; mixed for victimization (heavy underreporting); thin for how wrongdoers re-enter communities.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Madurese, Betawi, Dutch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Petrus and 1965 killings; sorcery-related mob killings; May 1998 sexual violence. Statute, courts, drug law and law on sorcery or deviance go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Indonesian lands (category only).
