# Indonesia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Indonesia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian; Javanese, Sundanese, Minangkabau, Toba Batak, Makassarese, Balinese; Malay in Jawi (Arabic) script for 19th-century poems; Arabic for fear-of-God terms. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Javanese** villages and cities, for dusk spirits, 1965 memory and Merapi.
- **Sundanese**, with *sandekala* and the *kolong wewe* tales.
- **Minangkabau** and **South Sumatra** forest-edge villages, for tigers and *palasik*.
- **Toba Batak**, for *begu ganjang* accusations.
- **Bugis and Makassar**, for *parakang* stories.
- **Balinese**, for *leak* and for the 1965 killings in Bali.
- **Chinese Indonesians**, for fear of being targeted, from 1965 to 1998.
- **Maluku** and **Poso** communities after communal war.
- **Palu, Lombok and Merapi** disaster survivors, for quake, tsunami and eruption fear.
- **Dayak** and **Madurese** in Kalimantan, after the 1999-2001 conflicts.
- **Families of former political prisoners** (Buru Island exiles), on fear kept silent.
- Indonesians abroad: comparison only, for how migrant workers describe fear of employers and deportation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1965, disasters and horror media; mixed for everyday anxiety; thin for measured fear outside adolescents.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian, Javanese, Sundanese, Minangkabau, Batak, Makassarese, Balinese; Jawi script for 19th-century poems.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1965 remains politically charged; sorcery accusations endanger the accused; disaster grief. Law on sorcery and on hoaxes goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities settled in Indonesia (category only).
