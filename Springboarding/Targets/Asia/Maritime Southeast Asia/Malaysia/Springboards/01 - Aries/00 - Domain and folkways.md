*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English, Mandarin and the Southern Chinese varieties (Hokkien, Cantonese, Hakka, Foochow), Iban, Kadazandusun, Bidayuh, Orang Asli languages (Semai, Temiar), Baba Malay. **Concept in one line:** the self and identity: what a self is here, and how it asserts itself (the body, appearance, beginnings, naming).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West-coast Malays** (Selangor, Perak, Johor, Melaka), many of Javanese, Bugis or Banjar descent: sub-ancestries inside "Malay".
- **Negeri Sembilan** (*adat perpatih*): the clan (*suku*) as a frame for the person.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: own pronouns, court heritage, healing arts.
- **Kedah and Perlis**: a separate royal origin story (*Hikayat Merong Mahawangsa*) behind self-naming.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Melanau, Orang Ulu** (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit, Penan): longhouse, village and forest settings.
- **Kadazandusun, Rungus, Murut, Bajau, Suluk** (Sabah): separate naming and dress.
- **Orang Asli**: Semang (Batek), Senoi (Semai, Temiar), Proto-Malay (Temuan, Jakun).
- **Malaysian Chinese by speech group** (clan and generation names); **Peranakan Chinese** and **Kristang Eurasians** (blended dress and names); **Malaysian Siamese** (Buddhist rites of passage).
- Comparison only: Patani Malays (a separate culture-nation) and Malaysians abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malays and Chinese; mixed for Sabah and Sarawak; thin and dated for Orang Asli voices. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet and the rest of the sign are written for the core lands (Peninsula, Sabah, Sarawak).
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for court texts on the self; Iban and Kadazandusun for Borneo person-terms; Chinese for generation names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race, religion and royalty (the "3R") are touchy; Orang Asli and Borneo spirit names may be restricted. The legal definition of "Malay" belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** domain pointers on the self among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
