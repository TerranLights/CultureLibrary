# Malaysia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi, plus Jawi for older print), Mandarin, Cantonese and Hokkien, Malaysian English, Iban, Semai. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: *dikir barat* retorts and the market women of Kota Bharu as possible spaces for blunt speech, to test rather than assume.
- **Negeri Sembilan**: clan chiefs (*buapak*, *lembaga*) speak for kin; who may speak in one's own name.
- **Royal courts and commoners** in all sultanate states: court speech levels set who may address whom.
- **Iban longhouses** in Sarawak against **Bidayuh villages**: compare speaking order at gatherings.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut communities** in Sabah: village headmen, the *Huguan Siou* title, and assertion of a people's voice.
- **Semai and Batek** (Orang Asli): refusing and requesting where coercion is avoided.
- **Malaysian Chinese guilds and clan associations**: collective petitioning versus individual speech.
- **Urban middle class** in the Klang Valley and Penang online, against rural speakers.
- **Women** in factories, religious debate and markets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich on Malay politeness and on organized protest; thin on everyday refusals among Borneo and Orang Asli peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Malay pragmatics needs native-speaker recordings; Kelantan Malay dialect for *dikir barat* texts; Chinese for petitions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of rulers and of religion touches sedition rules (the law itself belongs to Libra and Capricorn); protest coverage is polarized.
- **Held for review:** studies of speaking up and complaint within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
