# Malaysia: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of Javanese, Bugis, Minangkabau, Banjar and Mandailing descent**: how origin identities persist or fade under "Malay".
- **Kelantanese and Terengganu people**: a state and dialect identity to test against the national label.
- **Sabahans and Sarawakians**: Borneo identity set against "Malaya".
- **Kadazan, Dusun, Rungus and Murut**: a contested shared name.
- **Bajau**: west-coast Kota Belud communities against the Sama-Bajau of Semporna.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Orang Ulu and Melanau**: when "Dayak" or the specific name is chosen; Muslim against Christian Melanau.
- **Orang Asli subgroups**: self-names against the official three-way grouping.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by speech group; **Peranakan** against **sinkeh**; **Sino-native** families in Sabah.
- **Kristang** and **Malaysian Siamese**: small communities with a strong self-name.
- **Converts** (*mualaf*) of every origin.
- Comparison only: Patani Malays, a separate culture-nation across the Thai border, as a check on shared-dialect identity; Malaysians abroad, whose labels shift overseas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; identity is among the most studied topics in Malaysian social science. Thinner for Murut, Bajau and Orang Ulu self-naming.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for clan and association records; Kadazandusun and Iban for self-names; Jawi for older Malay periodicals on Malayness.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic categories tie to state benefits; conversion and religious identity are charged; Sabah citizenship disputes are political. Legal definitions belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** identity studies centered on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
