*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan**: clan land passing through women under *adat perpatih*; test what this does to women's choices.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: stricter state rules on public religious conduct; how far they reach into private choice.
- **Klang Valley young adults** against **FELDA settlement families** in Pahang and Johor: rent, distance and parental control.
- **Sabah and Sarawak as collectives**: regional self-rule claims against the federal center.
- **Iban *bilik* families**: household decisions inside the longhouse; **Kelabit** and **Penan**: highland and forest self-rule.
- **Batek, Semai and Chewong**: refusal of command at the personal level.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut villages**: elders' authority over young adults' marriage and land.
- **Malaysian Chinese families**: filial expectations by speech group and by religion (Buddhist, Taoist, Christian).
- **Peranakan households**: older matriarchal patterns, to check.
- **Elders and the disabled**: who decides care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good survey data on family decisions; strong Orang Asli ethnography; thin on Borneo youth.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic loan terms in Jawi for the free-will debate; Chinese for school-movement records; Penan statements often come via translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaving Islam, dress choices and sexuality are legally and socially charged; regional autonomy is political. Legal questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** autonomy and family-choice studies about South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
