# Malaysia: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin, Hokkien, Hakka and Cantonese, Malaysian English, Iban, Sama-Bajau. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan market women** (Kota Bharu): trading as a woman's undertaking, to compare with west-coast markets.
- **FELDA settler families**: state-launched lives against self-started ones in the next generation.
- **Malaysian Chinese speech groups** (Hokkien, Hakka, Cantonese, Teochew, Foochow): different trade niches and founding stories.
- **Malay civil servants and Malay small traders**: test, without stereotype, where unasked action is rewarded.
- **Iban, Kelabit and Bidayuh** young people: journeying against staying.
- **Sama-Bajau and west-coast Bajau**: sea crews against horse-and-cattle villages.
- **Rungus and Kadazandusun** longhouses and villages: communal work parties.
- **Orang Asli villages**: dependence on agency programs against self-started projects.
- **Religious founders** of *pondok* and *tahfiz* schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. Strong enterprise statistics and policy writing; thin on helping unasked in households.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for association histories and towkay lore; Iban for *bejalai* songs and accounts; Malay business press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "lazy native" stereotypes and ethnic business quotas are charged; avoid any one group as the model.
- **Held for review:** enterprise and trading histories of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Alatas (1977), chapters on colonial judgments of effort, and note every term used.
2. Download GEM Malaysia reports and SSM yearly new-company counts by state.
3. Search the national library catalog for *ringan tulang*, *daya usaha* and *Pelanduk Jenaka*.
4. Search the *Borneo Research Bulletin* and *Sarawak Museum Journal* for "bejalai".
5. Search Malaysian Chinese newspapers for 白手起家 plus 头家.
