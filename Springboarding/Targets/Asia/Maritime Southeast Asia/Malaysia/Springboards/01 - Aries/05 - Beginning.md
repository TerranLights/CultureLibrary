*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malacca, Johor and Perak courts** against **Kedah**: separate royal origin stories.
- **Negeri Sembilan**: Minangkabau settlers' founding of the *luak* districts.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: older seaside opening rites, some now discouraged.
- **FELDA families**: a state-made start in the 1950s-60s.
- **Chinese New Village residents**: resettlement in the 1950s as a forced beginning.
- **Foochow in Sibu; Hakka in Sabah**: migrant founding legends.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Melanau**: farm-year and sea-season calendars.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau**: origin places and harvest-time firsts.
- **Orang Ulu and Penan**: dam relocation and settlement as new starts.
- **Batek and Temuan**: creation stories and displacement by dams and plantations.
- **Converts** taking a new name.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on rites of birth, new year and founding; thin on starting over after failure (bankruptcy, divorce) outside statistics.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for *ketika* manuscripts; Chinese for almanac and association records; Kadazandusun and Melanau for origin narratives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Malay opening rites are discouraged by religious authorities; resettlement and the Emergency are painful histories; origin legends may have restricted versions.
- **Held for review:** birth, new-year and arrival rites of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
