## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Perak, Pahang, Kelantan and Terengganu**: each keeps its own anti-colonial hero; compare how they are ranked.
- **Rural Malay families with army and police service**: soldiering as a path to honored courage.
- **Iban** and **Sarawak Malays**: warrior heritage, trackers, resistance to Brooke rule.
- **Murut** and **Bajau-Suluk** in Sabah: inland and coastal resistance memories.
- **Semai** and other **Senoi**: military service set against a reputation for nonviolence.
- **Malaysian Chinese**: lion dance, martial-arts schools and wartime memories that differ by politics.
- **Kadazandusun**: wartime and resistance memories in Sabah (e.g. under Japanese occupation), to check.
- **Women**: Tun Fatimah, Kelantan queen legends and modern heroines.
- **Silat lineages** by state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official heroes and military history; mixed for oral warrior traditions; thin for moral courage (whistleblowing) outside news.
- **Languages needed:** Iban for warrior chants; Malay court texts in Jawi; Chinese for troupe and wartime memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting history; communist insurgency and the Emergency; *ilmu kebal* treated as deviant by religious authorities; contested heroes divide communities.
- **Held for review:** wartime and gallantry records of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
