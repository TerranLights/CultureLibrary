## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sabah and Sarawak**: talk of leaving the federation, against loyalty to it.
- **Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu**: a past of tribute to Siam that frames later self-rule.
- **Penang and Melaka**: former Straits Settlements under direct British rule; a different independence story.
- **Rural Malay smallholders and FELDA families**: land, aid and debt.
- **Klang Valley young renters**: how long they stay in the parental home.
- **Iban and Kelabit rice households**: food from one's own fields.
- **Penan and Batek foragers**: forest self-provisioning against settlement programs.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut villages**: *tagal* and shared resources.
- **Malaysian Chinese** family firms and rotating credit.
- **Elders and disabled people** across groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Good household and welfare data; strong ethnography of Borneo and Orang Asli subsistence; thin on emotional independence and help-seeking.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for welfare and zakat records; Chinese for mutual-aid societies; Penan and Kelabit terms via ethnographers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secession talk can draw sedition charges; special-privilege debates are charged; land claims are legal (Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** self-help and mutual-aid bodies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
