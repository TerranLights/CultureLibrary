# Malaysia: Competition (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Hokkien, Mandarin and Cantonese, Malaysian English, Iban, Sama-Bajau. **Concept in one line:** striving against others for a prize, a rank or a result.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: kites, tops and a football derby; village-level contest traditions.
- **Johor and Selangor**: club money and rivalry in modern football.
- **Selective-school Malay students** and **Chinese-school families**: different exam races.
- **Malaysian Chinese speech groups**: drum, lion-dance and clan-association contests.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Sarawak Malay** regatta crews.
- **Sama-Bajau of Semporna** and west-coast Bajau horsemen.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut**: harvest-festival games and contests, to check.
- **Orang Asli**: play forms that may avoid ranking.
- **Women athletes** (squash, badminton, diving) and public expectations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and exams; mixed for traditional games; thin for envy and losing.
- **Languages needed:** Hokkien for *kiasu* talk; Malay for festival rules; Iban for regatta lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic quotas in university entry and business are sore topics; match-fixing and gambling scandals.
- **Held for review:** sports and school-contest records of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
