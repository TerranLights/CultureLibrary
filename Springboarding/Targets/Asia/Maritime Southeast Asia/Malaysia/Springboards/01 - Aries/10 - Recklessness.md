## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in towns across the Peninsula**: racing culture from Johor Bahru to Kota Bharu.
- **Kedah, Perlis and Kelantan**: *ketum* use and gambling bans set northern norms apart.
- **Non-Muslim families with legal gambling access**: how relatives judge a gambler, without assuming a group trait.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Kadazandusun** festival-goers: where drinking turns from custom to danger.
- **Sabah east-coast fishers**: risk taken for a catch.
- **Kadazandusun and Dusun communities near Kinabalu**: risk defined by sacred rules.
- **Batek, Temiar and Semai**: rash acts against spirits and animals.
- **Investors across classes**: scheme victims and the shame after losses.
- **Women and girls**: risk judged by a different standard, to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on road safety and drugs; mixed on gambling (moral and legal framing); thin on sexual risk and private daring.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese and Hokkien for gambling and risk idioms; Kadazandusun for Kinabalu taboos; Malay for police and campaign texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drugs carry harsh penalties; gambling is forbidden to Muslims, so research must be careful; Kinabalu rites may be restricted. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** risk, drinking and gambling studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
