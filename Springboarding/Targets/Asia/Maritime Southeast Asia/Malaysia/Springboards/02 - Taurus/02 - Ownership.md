*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Hokkien and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Orang Asli languages (Temiar, Semai). **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on; here also the left-right axis (government spending as a share of output).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan** (*adat perpatih*, ancestral land through women) against **Kedah, Perlis and Perak** rice villages (*adat temenggung*), where shares follow other lines.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**, where the weight of reserve-land rules on everyday buying and selling is a question to check, against the mixed land markets of **Selangor, Penang and Johor**.
- **Malaysian Chinese** clan and trade associations (Hokkien, Teochew, Hakka, Cantonese), and Sarawak's **Foochow** of Sibu, who built land and timber holdings.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Melanau** under longhouse and village tenure; **Orang Ulu** (Kayan, Kenyah) with aristocratic rights in old custom; **Penan** with forest-use claims.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Rungus** of Sabah; **Bajau Laut** with sea-based holdings.
- **Orang Asli** (Temiar, Semai, Jakun, Temuan): ancestral territories with weak paper title.
- **FELDA settlers**, a state-made class of smallholders, and the urban **strata** owners of high-rise Klang Valley.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on land politics and corporate ownership; mixed on what ordinary people count as "theirs"; Indigenous claims are well documented in advocacy writing, which must be read as advocacy.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for titles and settlement records; English for colonial land files; Iban and Temiar tenure words as their speakers use them; Chinese for clan property ledgers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Malay reserve land and Bumiputera ownership quotas fall under the "3R" sensitivities; Indigenous land cases are live disputes. The statutes and court rulings belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** pointers on property holding and trade by South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the latest *Economic Outlook* and note total public spending; set the figures aside for Stage 2.

5. Contact PACOS Trust for its community land maps in Sabah.
