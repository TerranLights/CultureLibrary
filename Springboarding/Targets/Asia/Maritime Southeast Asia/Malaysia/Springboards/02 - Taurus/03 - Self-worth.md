# Malaysia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Cantonese and Hokkien, Iban, Kayan and Kenyah (Orang Ulu), Kadazandusun. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is held to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantanese** and **Terengganu** people, with their own speech forms, against the **west-coast** urban middle class: regional standing as a lead.
- **Sabahans** and **Sarawakians** as Borneo peoples, whose sense of standing toward the peninsula is a separate lead.
- **Orang Ulu** (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit), with inherited ranks to study, against the **Iban** and **Bidayuh**, whose rank customs differ.
- **Kadazandusun** and **Murut**; the **Bajau** of Sabah, including stateless Bajau Laut.
- **Orang Asli**, long named by outsiders' labels.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by schooling (Chinese-medium against national schools) and the **Peranakan**, whose standing between communities is its own question.
- Men against women; *orang kampung* (villagers) against *orang bandar* (city people).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective and communal worth (essays, politics, education movements); mixed on personal self-esteem (psychology studies in Malay and English journals); thin for Orang Asli and stateless groups in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Malay (including Jawi for the court texts and early reformist press), Chinese for the school movement, Iban and Kayan rank and honor words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bumiputera status and communal worth sit inside the "3R" topics; slurs for Orang Asli and Borneo peoples must be described, never quoted; stateless people in Sabah are a protected subject; suicide data need care.
- **Held for review:** pointers on dignity and standing among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
