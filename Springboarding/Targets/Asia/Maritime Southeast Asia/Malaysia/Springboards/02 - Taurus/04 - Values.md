# Malaysia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Iban, Kadazandusun, Chewong and other Orang Asli languages through the ethnographies. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muslim Malays** schooled in *Pendidikan Islam* against non-Muslim pupils in *Pendidikan Moral*: two official value lists inside one system.
- **Kelantan** (PAS-governed since 1990) and **Terengganu** (PAS-led for several terms) *check*, against **Selangor, Penang and Melaka**.
- **Negeri Sembilan**'s *adat perpatih* sayings against the custom of the other Malay states.
- **Christian peoples of Borneo:** Iban, Bidayuh, Kadazandusun, Lun Bawang and Kelabit (with the Borneo Evangelical Church), set against those who keep the older rites.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by religion (Buddhist, Taoist, Christian, none) and school stream; the **Peranakan**.
- **Orang Asli** peoples (Chewong, Semai, Temiar, Jakun); the **Bajau** and **Suluk** of Sabah's east coast.
- Generations: urban youth against rural elders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official and religious value statements; mixed for survey data below the national level (small samples for Sabah, Sarawak and minorities); thin for Orang Asli in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Malay (Jawi for *Taj al-Salatin* and religious tracts on *adab*), Chinese for clan mottos and school charters, Iban for the *ensera*, Kadazandusun for Kaamatan liturgy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religion is among the "3R" topics; apostasy and interfaith questions are restricted; some Kadazandusun and Iban rites are kept by ritual specialists. Questions of law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** pointers on the moral teaching and religious instruction of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
