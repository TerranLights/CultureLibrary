# Malaysia: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin and Hakka, Iban, Kayan and Kenyah, Bajau (Sama), Kristang. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Malays** of Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu who stayed in the home village, against urban migrants in the Klang Valley who *balik kampung* each year.
- **Negeri Sembilan** Minangkabau-descended villages, whose *merantau* custom is a lead to check, against other Malay states.
- **Chinese New Village** families (Perak, Selangor, Johor), resettled in the 1950s, against older Penang and Melaka families; **Sarawak Foochow** and **Sabah Hakka** settler towns.
- **Iban** longhouses, whose history of moves is a lead, against **Bidayuh** hill villages and **Melanau** river settlements.
- **Orang Ulu** resettled by dams; **Bajau Laut** moving from boats to stilt villages; **Orang Asli** in regroupment schemes.
- **Kristang** Eurasians and the **Peranakan**, small communities centered on one city each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on resettlement and displacement (Emergency, dams, heritage cities); mixed on everyday routine; political "stability" talk is plentiful but partisan, so read both sides.
- **Languages needed:** Malay (Jawi for *Misa Melayu* manuscripts), Chinese for New Village histories and shop records, Iban and Kayan for resettlement testimony, Sama-Bajau for Semporna voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Emergency and New Villages remain politically charged; May 1969 is a subject handled with care in public; dam resettlement is a live dispute.
- **Held for review:** pointers on settlement history and plantation-estate life among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
