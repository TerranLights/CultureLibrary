*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Hokkien and Cantonese, Iban, Penan and Semai (through ethnographies), Malaysian forum slang. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay Muslim** households, monogamous and polygamous, where co-wife relations are their own subject; **Kelantan** and **Terengganu** against the **Klang Valley**.
- **Malaysian Chinese** families, where sons, daughters and in-laws may contest the family shop and house.
- **Iban** and **Bidayuh** longhouse and village courtship: how rival suitors are handled is a lead.
- **Orang Asli** (Semai, Chewong, Batek) and **Penan**, whose recorded sharing rules are a contrast case to test.
- **East-coast inshore fishers** and **Bajau** fishers of Sabah, guarding grounds against trawlers and outsiders.
- **Urban youth** with phone tracking and location sharing; **Peranakan** families with heirlooms held by one branch.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on controlling relationships (NGO reports, helplines) and on sharing ethics among forest peoples (ethnographies); mixed on everyday jealousy; thin on Sabah peoples' terms for possessiveness.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for helpline and court-news language (Jawi only for older religious tracts on *bakhil*), Chinese for forum threads and songs, Penan and Semai terms via the ethnographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** polygamy and Islamic family matters are sensitive and partly under religious authority; domestic violence data is guarded for victims' safety; secret societies remain a policing subject. The family law itself goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on jealousy, marriage and family control among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
