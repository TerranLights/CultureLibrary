# Malaysia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English, Mandarin and Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Sama-Bajau, Melanau, Semai. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Peninsular Malays:** *silaturahim*, *balik kampung* and open houses; Negeri Sembilan clans as a separate kin order.
- **Kelantan and Kedah Malays:** ties that run across the Thai border; Patani as comparison only.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by dialect group: clan and hometown associations, and *guanxi* in business.
- **Peranakan Chinese:** ties that bridge Malay and Chinese networks.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Melanau and Orang Ulu** (Kelabit, Kenyah, Penan): longhouse, river and highland ties.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Rungus** in Sabah; **Sama-Bajau** on land and **Bajau Laut** at sea.
- **Orang Asli** (Semai, Temuan, Jakun): small-group sharing ties and ties to forest land.
- **Older people living alone** in cities, and **young people who moved from Borneo** to the peninsula for work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Chinese associations and political patronage; mixed on Malay festival ties; thin on loneliness measures by people and region.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for *silaturahim* and *kabel* talk; Chinese and Hokkien for association records; Iban and Kadazandusun for festival practice; Sama-Bajau for sea-village kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patronage and cronyism are politically charged; ties across ethnic and religious lines can be touchy; stateless Bajau Laut ties are tangled up with immigration controls (for law, see Libra).
- **Held for review:** kin and association networks of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
