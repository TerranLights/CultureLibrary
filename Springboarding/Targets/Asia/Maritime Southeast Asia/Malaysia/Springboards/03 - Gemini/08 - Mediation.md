*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Semai. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Peninsular Malays:** imams, *penghulu*, village heads and family elders as go-betweens; east and west coasts may differ.
- **Negeri Sembilan's *adat perpatih* communities:** clan office-holders (*buapak*, *lembaga*) who speak between families.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by dialect group: clan and town-hall arbitration; temple committees.
- **Peranakan Chinese:** go-betweens in marriage across Malay and Chinese customs.
- **Iban, Bidayuh** and **Orang Ulu** longhouses: headmen and *penghulu*.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut, Rungus** and **Sama-Bajau** in Sabah: village heads, Native Chiefs and *sogit*.
- **Orang Asli** (Semai, Batek, Temiar): the Semai *becharaa'* and how other groups handle quarrels.
- **Siamese Malaysians** of Kedah and Kelantan: temple and monk roles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Chinese associations and Semai conflict studies; mixed on Malay village go-betweens; thin on Sabah and Sarawak practice outside customs councils.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Jawi for chronicles and *adat* sayings; Chinese for association minutes; Iban and Kadazandusun for headmen's practice; Semai terms through Robarchek and Dentan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** customary courts, religious courts and the Mediation Act are law, for Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); intercession debates divide religious groups; family quarrels are private.
- **Held for review:** go-betweens and dispute settlement among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
