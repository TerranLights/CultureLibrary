*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rice villages of Kedah and Perlis** against **coastal towns of Kelantan and Terengganu:** whether class and kin bear on who talks about whom.
- **FELDA settlers:** reputation among neighbors who arrived as strangers.
- **Chinese New Villages** and urban Malaysian Chinese neighborhoods by dialect group.
- **Peranakan Chinese** of Malacca and Penang: tight family networks and household talk.
- **Iban** and **Bidayuh** longhouse or village communities; **Melanau** coastal towns.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut** and **Sama-Bajau** villages in Sabah.
- **Orang Asli** (Batek, Semai, Temiar): small groups with their own speech rules.
- **Young people online** against older people in neighborhood and family groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kedah village studies and online rumor; mixed for celebrity media; thin for Borneo and Orang Asli gossip outside older ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Malay slang (*bawang*) and religious Arabic loans; Cantonese and Mandarin for Chinese forums; Iban for longhouse talk; Batek terms through Endicott.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1969 riots remain sensitive; rumor about race, religion and royalty can bring legal trouble (for the laws, see Libra); naming gossip targets is out of bounds.
- **Held for review:** rumor and gossip material involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
