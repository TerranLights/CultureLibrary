# Malaysia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, plus Kelantanese and Sarawak Malay), Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu vs. the west-coast states:** east-coast house types (twelve-post and five-ridge roofs) and kitchen-centered layouts differ from Perak's *rumah kutai* or Melaka's tiled stairs.
- **Negeri Sembilan:** where houses pass through the female line, "whose home" has a separate answer.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Melanau, Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit:** gallery-and-apartment longhouses against the Melanau tall house and the Bidayuh village.
- **Kadazandusun, Rungus, Murut, Bajau:** village house, longhouse and stilt house in Sabah.
- **Chinese terrace, shophouse and New Village households; Peranakan Chinese; Kristang of the Melaka Portuguese Settlement:** urban and colonial-era homes.
- **Orang Asli (Temiar, Semai, Jakun, Mah Meri, Batek):** forest, resettled and peri-urban dwellings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malay architecture and housing statistics; mixed for Borneo longhouse life today; thin for Orang Asli homes in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for house-building manuscripts; Chinese for clan-jetty and shophouse community records; Iban and Kadazandusun for longhouse and village self-descriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eviction, resettlement and Kampung Baru redevelopment are politically charged; *kampung air* residents' legal status is sensitive. Tenure law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** house forms and home customs of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: "Housekeeping among Malay Peasants" and "Tajul Muluk", then compare an early household record with a pre-modern building text.
2. PRPM: look up *rumah*, *kampung halaman* and *teratak*, and list every proverb with *rumah* in it.
3. OpenDOSM: "Household Income and Basic Amenities" for tenure and house type by state.
4. KALAM, UTM: search "measured drawings" for one house each from Kelantan, Negeri Sembilan and Perak.
5. COAC site and newspaper archives: "pengumpulan semula Orang Asli" and "Kampung Baru pembangunan semula".
