*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; Negeri Sembilan Malay), Mandarin with Hokkien, Cantonese, Hakka and Foochow, Iban, Bidayuh, Kadazandusun, Papia Kristang, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan (*adat perpatih*) vs. the bilateral Malay states (Kedah, Perak, Johor, Selangor):** two answers to which line a family runs through.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** whether women's market trade changes who heads an east-coast household is a question to check.
- **Chinese dialect groups (Hokkien, Hakka, Cantonese, Teochew, Hainanese, Foochow) and Peranakan Chinese:** lineage organizations differ, and Peranakan families draw on more than one set of customs.
- **Iban vs. Kayan and Kenyah:** a *bilik* among equals against households in ranked houses.
- **Sabah's Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau, and Sino-Kadazan mixed families:** intermarriage makes kin cross ethnic lines.
- **Orang Asli (Batek, Semai, Temuan, Mah Meri) and the Kristang:** small peoples whose kin systems are easily lost in national averages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malay and Chinese kinship and official family statistics; mixed for Bidayuh and Sabah peoples; thin and old for several Orang Asli groups.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the Kedah and Melaka chronicles; traditional-script Chinese for genealogy books; Iban for *tusut*; Kadazandusun for its kin terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interfaith marriage and conversion within families are sensitive; genealogies may be private family or clan property. Marriage and adoption law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** family and kin organizations of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. PRPM (DBP): look up *besan*, *biras*, *ipar* and *mentua* for each word's exact range.
2. LPPKN site: "Indeks Kesejahteraan Keluarga" for the index items and any state breakdown.
3. Chinese-language search: "族谱 马来西亚 宗亲会" for digitized genealogies and association anniversary volumes.
4. Tun Jugah Foundation catalog: "tusut" and Iban genealogy recordings.
5. Library catalog: Stivens, Geddes, Endicott and Rousseau, one family system per book, to set side by side.
