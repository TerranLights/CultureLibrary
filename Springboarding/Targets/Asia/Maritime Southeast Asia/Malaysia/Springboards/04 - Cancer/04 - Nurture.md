# Malaysia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, with Arabic religious loanwords), Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Semai and other Orang Asli languages (through ethnographers' glossaries), Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Kelantan and Terengganu Malays vs. urban west-coast Malays:** religious schooling and kin care may weigh differently in each setting.
- **Chinese-Malaysian families by schooling stream (Chinese-medium or national) and dialect:** different routes of formation.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut of interior Sabah:** Williams's Dusun as a baseline to recheck.
- **Iban and Kelabit:** farm-household nurture and the Kelabit feeding idiom.
- **Semai, Temiar and Batek:** child-rearing that ethnographers have long set against the national mainstream.
- **Peranakan Chinese and Kristang families:** small creole communities whose ways of raising children need their own entry, not a footnote to Malay or Chinese patterns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official child-health surveys and Malay-language parenting studies; mixed for Chinese-language material; thin and dated for Sabah and Orang Asli upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** Malay with its Arabic religious terms; Chinese for the press debate; Iban and Kadazandusun glossaries; Semai and Temiar only through ethnographers' word lists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Orang Asli schooling deaths and conversion in schools are sensitive; corporal punishment at home and school is contested. Discipline as authority goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** child-rearing material on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
