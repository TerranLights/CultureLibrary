# Malaysia: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, including the vocabulary of charms and healing), Mandarin and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Temiar through ethnographers' glossaries, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** east-coast healing performances and their contested public standing, against west-coast urban practice.
- **Iban, Murut and Kayan:** warrior and longhouse-defense pasts, now held in oral history and festivals.
- **Kadazandusun of Penampang, Tambunan and Ranau:** *bobohizan* traditions and where they now stand beside the churches and Islam.
- **Temiar, Semai and Mah Meri:** spirit-guardian protection among Orang Asli.
- **Penan and other Orang Ulu:** land defense as protection of home.
- **Chinese New Village families and urban gated-estate households:** state-made against bought protection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial and ethnographic records of charms and for child-protection statistics; mixed for living healers; thin for Penan and Murut voices in print.
- **Languages needed:** Malay healers' and charm vocabulary (some Arabic, some archaic); Kadazandusun ritual language; Cantonese for temple godchild rites; Iban for oral war histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healers' formulas and *rinait* may be secret or restricted; amulets and *Main Puteri* are contested under Islamic authorities; domestic violence and police matters are sensitive. Law on violence and child protection goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** protective rites and amulets of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
