# Malaysia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; manuscript Malay for healing texts), Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Iban, Melanau, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay families in Kelantan and Terengganu vs. the Klang Valley:** whether co-residence with aged parents differs between east-coast villages and the capital is a question for the data.
- **Chinese-Malaysian households by dialect and religion (Buddhist, Taoist, Christian):** clan homes, temple charities and church homes as alternatives to kin.
- **Iban and Melanau:** ritual healing alongside hospital care.
- **Kadazandusun and Lun Bawang Christian communities:** church-based visiting and care networks.
- **Orang Asli (Temuan, Semai, Jakun):** access to care and the Gombak hospital's role.
- **Households with migrant domestic workers vs. those without:** a class line in who does the caring.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ageing and formal care; mixed on family carers' own experience; thin on Melanau and other small peoples' healing care.
- **Languages needed:** manuscript Malay in Jawi for *Kitab Tib*; Chinese for temple and clan charity records; Iban for *manang* chants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant domestic workers' treatment is sensitive; healing chants may be restricted to practitioners; elder neglect cases involve privacy. Medical care law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** care institutions and healers of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
