# Malaysia: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; classical Malay for *penglipur lara* tales), Mandarin, Iban, Kelabit, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay families of the peninsula:** whether the *anak derhaka* tales shape how grown children who leave are judged, and how far into urban families, is open.
- **Chinese-Malaysian families:** adoption-out in hard times, and elders left in homes, in Chinese-language sources.
- **Peranakan Chinese:** memoirs give inside accounts of departure within creole families.
- **Iban, Kelabit and other Sarawak longhouse peoples:** rural emptying and elders who stay.
- **Sabah's undocumented and stateless families:** children left behind after deportations, a separate and sensitive case.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** whether women left without support appear more often in east-coast reports is a question to check.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in the press and folk tradition; mixed in official figures (baby-dumping counts vary by agency); thin for Borneo peoples' own words on emptied villages.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the *penglipur lara* texts; Mandarin for Chinese-press cases; Iban and Kelabit for longhouse accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** births outside marriage, adoption across religions and deportation of parents are sensitive; individual cases involve minors. Legitimacy, adoption and maintenance law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** cases and institutions involving South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
