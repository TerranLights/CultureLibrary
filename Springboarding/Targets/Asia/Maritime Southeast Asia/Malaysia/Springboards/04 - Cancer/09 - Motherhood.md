# Malaysia: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; Terengganu and Kelantan dialects for midwifery terms), Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Terengganu and Kelantan rural mothers vs. Klang Valley professional mothers:** village midwifery against hospital birth and paid confinement services.
- **Negeri Sembilan's *adat perpatih* families:** where clan and property are reckoned through women, a mother's standing is its own question.
- **Chinese-Malaysian mothers by dialect group:** the confinement month in Hakka, Cantonese and Hokkien households.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Kadazandusun mothers:** how Christian and older ritual practice around birth relate, congregation by congregation.
- **Orang Asli mothers (Temuan, Semai, Jakun):** clinic access and their own birth customs.
- **Single mothers, stepmothers and milk mothers across groups:** motherhood outside the two-parent model.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Malay midwifery and maternal health; mixed on Chinese confinement (mostly press and commerce); thin on Orang Asli and Sabah mothers in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Malay midwifery and religious vocabulary; Chinese for confinement-month discussion; Kadazandusun for the Kaamatan narrative; Iban for birth ritual.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth outside marriage, infertility and maternal death are sensitive; some Kadazandusun and Iban ritual texts are restricted to practitioners. Maternity-leave and custody law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** birth customs and mothers' associations of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
