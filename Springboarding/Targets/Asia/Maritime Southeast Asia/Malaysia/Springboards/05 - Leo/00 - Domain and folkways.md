## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West-coast Malay states vs. Kelantan and Terengganu:** state religious governance shapes which public entertainments are staged, so the line between pleasure and restraint may run between states.
- **Negeri Sembilan (Adat Perpatih):** matrilineal custom changes who hosts weddings and feasts.
- **Malaysian Chinese by dialect group and city** (Penang Hokkien, Kuala Lumpur Cantonese, Sibu Foochow, Sabah Hakka): festival styles and gambling ways differ.
- **Peranakan Chinese and Kristang Eurasians in Melaka:** small creole communities with their own festive calendars.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Orang Ulu and Melanau in Sarawak:** *Gawai*, Kaul and longhouse feasting run on different calendars and hosts.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut, Rungus and Bajau in Sabah:** harvest, horsemanship and sea festivals differ.
- **Orang Asli peoples:** forest and coastal groups (Temiar, Semai, Mah Meri, Batek) have distinct music and play.

## 4. Flags
