# Malaysia: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, plus classical Malay), Mandarin and Malaysian Chinese varieties, Iban, Kadazandusun and Rungus, Temiar and other Orang Asli languages, English. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu craft villages** (songket, batik, carving, kites) vs. **Klang Valley and Penang studios and design firms:** inherited forms versus market-driven novelty.
- **Iban weavers by river region** (Saribas, Baleh): design repertoires and rights may be held differently.
- **Orang Ulu carvers and beadworkers** (Kenyah, Kayan, Kelabit): rank may govern who uses which motif.
- **Rungus, Kadazandusun and Kota Belud Bajau makers:** beadwork and weaving as district marks.
- **Temiar, Mah Meri and Jah Hut:** dream, ritual and tourist economies each shape new work differently.
- **Malaysian Chinese writers and temple craftsmen;** **Peranakan beadwork and embroidery** in Melaka and Penang.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts and state-sponsored arts; mixed for literary theory; thin for everyday improvising and kitchen invention.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay for the vocabulary of craft; Iban for weaving names; Mandarin for Mahua debates; Temiar song terms through Roseman's glosses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some *pua kumbu* designs are held to carry ritual danger; dream songs may be private; ownership quarrels with neighbors over songs, batik and songket are politically heated (the 2007 *Rasa Sayang* dispute with Jakarta); figurative imagery and Islamic guidance is a live debate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
