# Malaysia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; Kelantan Malay for trance theater), Mandarin and Cantonese, Baba Malay, Iban, Kadazandusun, Chewong and other Orang Asli languages, English. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** *Main Puteri* and *Mak Yong* channel feeling through performance under state limits on staging; compare with the Klang Valley's concert and club scene.
- **Chinese-educated vs. English-educated Malaysian Chinese:** different media, idioms and models of saying one's mind.
- **Peranakan Chinese of Melaka and Penang:** a sung, Malay-language channel shared with Malay neighbors.
- **Iban and Kadazandusun:** sung verse at festivals may permit what daily talk does not.
- **Chewong, Semai and Temiar:** ethnographies record emotion rules of their own; keep them separate from Malay ones.
- **Christians of Sabah and Sarawak vs. Peninsular Muslims:** dress and public-behavior expectations diverge.
- **Young urban women and men:** dress, hair and online voice differ by gender.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on controversies, censorship and online speech; mixed on art forms; thin on ordinary household reticence.
- **Languages needed:** Kelantan Malay for the trance and theater texts; Mandarin and Cantonese for satire and its comment threads; Iban and Kadazandusun for sung verse.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** speech on race, religion and royalty is closely policed and risky to quote; gender and sexual expression is legally and religiously fraught (law belongs to Libra and Capricorn); trance sessions are private healing.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
