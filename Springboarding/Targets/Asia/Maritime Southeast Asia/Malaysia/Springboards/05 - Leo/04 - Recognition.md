## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The nine royal states** (Johor, Kedah, Pahang, Perak, Selangor, Kelantan, Terengganu, Perlis, Negeri Sembilan) vs. **Melaka, Penang, Sabah and Sarawak** under governors: who may confer titles differs.
- **Negeri Sembilan (Adat Perpatih):** titles flow through clan lineages, not only the ruler.
- **Malaysian Chinese business and clan leaders:** state titles as community standing.
- **Kelabit and other Orang Ulu peoples:** feasting and naming.
- **Iban longhouses:** renown earned abroad, recognized at home.
- **Orang Asli peoples:** recognition as distinct peoples, not only as individuals.
- **Schoolchildren and teachers:** exam-based recognition is a national ritual.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors (gazetted lists, press); mixed for workplaces; thin for everyday thanks and credit inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Malay, including the royal register, for honors; Mandarin for Chinese-press readings of titles; Kelabit and Iban for naming and renown terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** comment on rulers' honors can touch royalty limits; title-buying cases involve criminal charges (Libra); Indigenous recognition overlaps land disputes (Libra).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
