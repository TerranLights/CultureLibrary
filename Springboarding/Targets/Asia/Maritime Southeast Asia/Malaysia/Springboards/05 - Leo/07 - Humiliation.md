# Malaysia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi; Negeri Sembilan and Kelantan usage), Penang Hokkien, Malaysian Cantonese, Mandarin, Iban, Kadazandusun, English. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan's matrilineal villages:** shame and gender may be arranged differently than under Adat Temenggung.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** religious and political rivalry has its own forms of public denunciation.
- **Malaysian Chinese business families:** face in commerce and debt.
- **Iban longhouses and Kadazandusun villages:** ridicule tales and customary amends.
- **Orang Asli children in state schools:** humiliation across a cultural gap.
- **Urban youth online:** viral shaming follows its own rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the vocabulary of *malu* and for school bullying data; mixed for Borneo; thin for humiliation inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Malay with Jawi for *aib* in religious teaching; Hokkien and Cantonese for face talk in commerce; Kadazandusun for *sogit*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1969 riots are one of the most sensitive topics in the country; religious raids and caning are legal matters for Libra and Capricorn; individual bullying and shaming cases involve minors and grieving families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
