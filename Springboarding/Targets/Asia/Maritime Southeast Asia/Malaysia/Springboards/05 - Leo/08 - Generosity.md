## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu vs. Selangor and the Federal Territories:** zakat practice and giving to religious schools may differ in scale and channel.
- **Malaysian Chinese by dialect group:** red-packet sums and temple giving may vary between Cantonese, Hokkien, Hakka and Foochow communities.
- **Peranakan Chinese:** feasting customs distinct from newer Chinese communities.
- **Iban and Kadazandusun villages:** labor exchange and longhouse feasting.
- **Kayan and Kenyah aristocracies:** giving tied to rank.
- **Batek and Semai:** sharing norms of forest peoples.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for zakat and formal charity; mixed for festive giving; thin and old (ethnographic) for Orang Asli sharing.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic-derived Malay terms in Jawi for alms; Cantonese and Hokkien slang for stinginess and red packets; Iban and Kadazandusun for labor exchange.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** zakat is a religious obligation tied to state law (Libra); conversion-linked aid in Borneo is a sore point; ethnographies of Orang Asli sharing are decades old and should be checked against current community voices.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
