*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan (Adat Perpatih) vs. the Adat Temenggung states:** clan-chosen chiefs against rulers' appointees.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** religious scholars as political leaders.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** Kapitan history, towkay patronage, New Village and association leaders.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Orang Ulu longhouses:** headmen by persuasion, with rank among Kayan and Kenyah.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau in Sabah:** native chiefs and the *Huguan Siou*.
- **Batek, Semai and Temuan:** egalitarian leadership against appointed headmen.
- **Kristang Eurasians of Melaka:** a community head in a small minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national politics and corporate surveys; mixed for village and longhouse leadership; thin for women's informal leadership.
- **Languages needed:** Negeri Sembilan Malay for customary sayings; Mandarin for association leadership; Iban and Kadazandusun for headman roles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** formal authority and royalty belong to Capricorn; criticism of rulers is restricted; Orang Asli headman disputes touch land claims (Libra).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
