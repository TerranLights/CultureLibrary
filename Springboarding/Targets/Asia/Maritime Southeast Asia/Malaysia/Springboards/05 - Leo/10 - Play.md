## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu kite and top players** vs. **Klang Valley children in malls and online:** village craft-games against screen play.
- **Melanau, Murut and Bajau festivals:** play bound into ritual and display.
- **Malaysian Chinese families:** festival games and mahjong across generations.
- **Kristang Eurasians:** a festive calendar of their own.
- **Orang Asli communities:** hunting skills turned into contests.
- **Urban boys and girls:** gendered toys and games to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traditional games (heritage bodies love them) and esports news; mixed for everyday children's free play; thin for adult play outside sport.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for game names and rhymes; Hokkien and Cantonese for family games; Melanau, Murut and Kristang for festival play terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling and cockfighting overlap with play and are legally restricted (Libra); some festival play is ritual and not for outsiders to join.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
