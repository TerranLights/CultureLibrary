# Malaysia: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin and the Chinese varieties (Hokkien, Cantonese, Hakka, Foochow, Hainanese), Iban, Kadazandusun, Bidayuh, Bajau (Sama), Orang Asli languages. **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what good work and a well-kept life are, across work, service, the daily round, the kept house and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West-coast urban states** (Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru): factory and commuting time against the kampung round.
- **East-coast Kelantan and Terengganu:** monsoon fishing, women's market trade and a Friday rest day.
- **Kedah and Perlis rice plains:** the double-cropped padi year as a work calendar.
- **Negeri Sembilan:** *adat perpatih* households divide work and property differently.
- **Sabah peoples** (Kadazandusun, Murut, Bajau, Rungus): wet rice, upland hunting and sea work in one state.
- **Sarawak peoples** (Iban, Bidayuh, Melanau, Orang Ulu): longhouse labor, sago and river trade.
- **Orang Asli** (Semai, Temiar, Batek, Jakun and others): foraging, swidden and wage work side by side.
- **Malaysian Chinese by dialect group** (Hakka, Hokkien, Foochow of Sibu, Hainanese): trades may track dialect lines.
- **Peranakan Chinese** (Melaka, Penang), **Kristang** Eurasians of Melaka and **Malaysian Siamese** of Kedah and Kelantan: small communities whose household rounds may differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Peninsular Malays and urban Chinese; mixed for Sabah and Sarawak; thin for Orang Asli.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for court texts and religious print; Chinese for guild records; Iban and Kadazandusun for rice-year rites.
- **Territory:** the tracker records no change to the pre-war lands and leaves the territory to be settled; this set is written for the core lands (Peninsula, Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bumiputera policy and migrant labor are charged topics; some rice-spirit rites are restricted to practitioners.
- **Held for review:** pointers on the daily round, work and kept house of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
