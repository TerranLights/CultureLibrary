*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kedah and Perlis padi villages:** mechanization changed who is hired and who is idle.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** women's market trade and monsoon fishing set household roles apart from the west coast.
- **Klang Valley and Penang factory districts:** shift work and migrant labor from other states.
- **Hakka, Cantonese and Hokkien Chinese:** mining, trade and shopkeeping followed dialect lines in the colonial era, worth separating.
- **Foochow of Sibu:** a single settlement story of pioneer farming.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Orang Ulu:** longhouse farming against urban wage work in Kuching and Miri.
- **Melanau and Bajau Laut:** sago and the sea as distinct subsistence trades.
- **Batek, Semai and other Orang Asli:** foraging and swidden next to plantation wage labor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for factory and rice-village studies; thin for Kadazandusun and Murut work terms in print.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for proverbs and press; Chinese for clan and guild records; Iban and Kadazandusun for village work words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant labor conditions and Bumiputera hiring are charged; labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** labor-history and livelihood pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
