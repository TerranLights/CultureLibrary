## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sultanate states with strong courts** (Kelantan, Terengganu, Johor, Perak): court speech and royal service as living practice.
- **Negeri Sembilan:** elected *Undang* chiefs under *adat perpatih* may frame service to leaders differently from the sultanates.
- **Javanese-descended Malays of Johor and Selangor:** the *rewang* feast tradition may differ from other Malay feast labor.
- **Hainanese and Cantonese Chinese:** cooks, stewards and *majie* in colonial and Peranakan households.
- **Iban:** a strong thread of military service to separate from longhouse helping.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut:** village heads and native chiefs in Sabah's interior.
- **Orang Asli:** the *batin* role sits between community and state agency.
- **Malaysian Siamese of Kedah and Kelantan:** temple service in Buddhist villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on royal service and civil service; mixed on volunteering; thin on Bornean community service in print.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay and Jawi for the court texts; Cantonese for *majie* oral histories; Iban for military memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal institutions are protected topics; migrant domestic workers' treatment is contested; loyalty and treason (*derhaka*) go to Capricorn, statutes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on service roles, temple volunteering and household service among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
