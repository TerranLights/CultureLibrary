## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Malay households** in Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu: children's labor on farms and boats against urban schooling.
- **Klang Valley middle-class families:** grandparents as child-minders while both parents commute.
- **Kadazandusun and Rungus of Sabah:** rice-origin stories tie giving and feeding to a sacred figure.
- **Iban and Bidayuh:** skills such as weaving or farm knowledge may carry rank inside the longhouse.
- **Penan and other Orang Ulu:** forest-use rules on what is kept for later.
- **Mah Meri and Semai:** Orang Asli ways of sorting the useful from the harmful.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** family businesses employ children and elders; hawker families especially.
- **People registered as OKU:** a group the state counts separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in survey form for elders and families; thin for Bornean concepts of usefulness.
- **Languages needed:** Malay idioms need a proverb dictionary; Cantonese and Hokkien slang for insults; Penan and Mah Meri terms through ethnographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability, aging and suicide data need care; some Kaamatan rites are restricted to *bobohizan* priestesses.
- **Held for review:** pointers on elders, family help and reuse among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
