## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan, Terengganu and Kedah *pondok* graduates:** logic learned through religious texts.
- **Klang Valley and Penang technical workforce:** engineering and data work in the electronics and services sectors.
- **Chinese independent-school graduates:** a separate mathematics tradition and abacus culture.
- **Sabah and Sarawak students:** lower test coverage and different schooling histories, worth separating in any score.
- **Iban:** riddling and augury as older modes of working things out, to compare with schooled reasoning.
- **Bajau and other sea peoples of Sabah:** navigational reckoning.
- **Temiar, Semai and Jahai:** Orang Asli classification systems; often filtered through outsider theories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school assessment and religious-science history; thin for Bornean and Orang Asli reasoning practices.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic terms for *falak* and *mantiq*; Chinese for independent-school sources; Iban for riddles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the UEC and 1MDB are political; the Senoi dream claim is a known distortion; *ketika* reckoning is contested by religious authorities.
- **Held for review:** pointers on schooling, learned traditions and reckoning among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
