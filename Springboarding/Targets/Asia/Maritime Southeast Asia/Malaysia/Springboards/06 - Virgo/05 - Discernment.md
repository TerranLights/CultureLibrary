## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kedah and the sultanate courts:** the royal register as a place to study the line between *halus* and *kasar*.
- **Muslim and non-Muslim shoppers:** halal discernment applies differently, and mixed households negotiate it.
- **Older urban users in the Klang Valley:** often named in scam reports; separate by age and language (a research question).
- **Malaysian Chinese:** *识货* shopping talk and almanac date-choosing.
- **Peranakan of Melaka and Penang:** collectors' taste as a family inheritance.
- **Iban:** omen-reading as a mode of deciding.
- **Murut and Kadazandusun:** whether jar valuation enters bride-wealth and inheritance (*check*).
- **Jahai, Batek and other Orang Asli:** plant knowledge as discernment of the forest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for halal, scams and crafts; mixed for Bornean heirloom valuation; thin for Orang Asli in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Malay royal register; Chinese for almanac use; Iban for omen terms; Murut dialects for jar lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** halal disputes touch religion; omen and almanac practices are disputed by religious authorities; certain Orang Asli plant knowledge is guarded; scam victims underreport.
- **Held for review:** pointers on taste, almanac-style date choosing and food rules among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
