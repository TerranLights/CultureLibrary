*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English, Mandarin, Hokkien, Cantonese, Iban, Rungus, Bajau, Jakun and Semelai. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Malay and Chinese families in the Klang Valley and Penang:** exam pressure and tuition, separated by school stream.
- **Chinese independent-school and national-type-school students:** different grading regimes may produce different pressure.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu craft villages:** songket, boat building and Mak Yong as master-to-pupil standards.
- **Peranakan of Melaka and Penang:** needlework and household display.
- **Bajau of Semporna and Rungus of Kudat:** judged decoration and beadwork in Sabah.
- **Iban:** tattoo motifs with set forms.
- **Jakun and Semelai:** weaving traditions under tourist demand.
- **Factory and engineering workers in Penang and Johor:** imported quality systems.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exam pressure and industry; mixed for crafts; thin for Orang Asli makers.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Hokkien slang (*cincai*, *kiasu*); Chinese press; Bajau and Rungus for makers' own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student mental health and suicide need care; Mak Yong's religious status in Kelantan is contested; some Iban motifs are restricted by rank.
- **Held for review:** pointers on exam pressure and fine crafts among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
