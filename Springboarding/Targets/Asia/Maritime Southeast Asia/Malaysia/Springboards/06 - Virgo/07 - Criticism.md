## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu:** whether shadow-play and *dikir barat* work as channels for public teasing (*check*).
- **Negeri Sembilan:** matrilineal households may route reproach through different kin.
- **Melaka Malays and Peranakan:** the shared *dondang sayang* form.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** Chinese-language press and satire on separate media channels.
- **Iban and Bidayuh:** comic tales and longhouse meetings as settings for correction.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut:** sung exchanges and village gatherings.
- **Semai and other Orang Asli:** dispute assemblies as a structured setting.
- **Civil servants and private-sector workers:** formal appraisal against workplace hints.
- **Young online users:** pile-on culture and its own vocabulary.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political criticism and linguistic politeness; mixed for performance forms; thin for Bornean verse.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for *seloka*, *pantun* and Kelantanese dialect; Cantonese and Mandarin for satire; Iban and Kadazandusun for oral forms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race, religion and royalty are restricted topics in public speech; criticism of rulers carries legal risk (statutes to Libra, its purpose to Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** pointers on satire, press and verbal correction among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
