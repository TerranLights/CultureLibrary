## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Malays of Kedah and Kelantan:** land schemes, irrigation and the NEP as lived change.
- **Urban middle-class Malays:** self-help seminars and the *hijrah* genre.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** family-business mobility and Chinese-language self-improvement media.
- **Peranakan Chinese:** an older story of advancement through English schooling (*check*).
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Kelabit (Orang Ulu):** dam resettlement against schooling success stories.
- **Iban:** longhouse communities between land development schemes and urban migration.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau:** state foundation scholarships and coastal livelihood programs.
- **Orang Asli:** regroupment and development imposed from outside.
- **Kristang of Melaka:** a small community facing waterfront projects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national development policy; mixed for self-help culture; thin for Bornean peoples in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Malay (including 1970s policy prose); Jawi for *Al-Imam*; Chinese for business memoirs and columns; Orang Ulu languages for resettlement testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bumiputera policy, resettlement and land rights are politically charged; land law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on mobility, self-help and resettlement among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
