# Malaysia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin and Cantonese, Iban and Kadazandusun for village and native-court offices, Semai and Temuan for Orang Asli headmen. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban professionals against kampung (village) networks:** who uses *kabel*, and for what.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu under long opposition rule:** village offices and grants when the state and federal parties differ.
- **Sabah and Sarawak:** native chiefs and *Penghulu* appointed by state governments; kin ties in small districts.
- **Orang Asli villages:** headmen confirmed by a federal department.
- **Malaysian Chinese business networks and clan bodies:** *guanxi* in trade and loans.
- **Civil servants and teachers:** the public service's make-up by community and the favoritism complaints raised about it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on corruption cases, election reform and court rulings; mixed on everyday favoritism; thin on native-court and Orang Asli headmen in practice.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for MACC and village records; Chinese for business-network talk; Iban and Kadazandusun for native-office disputes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cases involving royalty or sitting politicians; claims of ethnic favoritism in hiring touch Article 153 and the Sedition Act.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and their own complaints about access to offices and schools, kept for later.
