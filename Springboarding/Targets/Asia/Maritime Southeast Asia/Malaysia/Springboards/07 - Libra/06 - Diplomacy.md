# Malaysia: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, including palace language), English, Mandarin, Cantonese and Hakka, Tausug and Bajau for the Sulu question, Iban and Kayan, Kadazandusun. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Palace and court circles:** the most formal register and its protocols.
- **Kelantan, Terengganu and Kedah:** border states with an old memory of tribute to Siam.
- **Sabah's Kadazandusun, Bajau and Suluk:** the 1963 bargain and the Sulu question pull differently.
- **Sarawak's Iban and Orang Ulu:** peacemaking traditions and longhouse protocol for guests.
- **Malaysian Chinese associations:** negotiation through chambers, clan halls and party.
- **Orang Asli:** petitions and delegations, with networks like JOAS.
- **Corporate Kuala Lumpur against kampung life:** office indirection and village *musyawarah*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and classical letters; mixed for everyday tact; thin for Borneo peacemaking in the peoples' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and palace Malay for letters; classical Chinese for Ming records; Tausug and Malay for the Sulu deed; Iban and Kayan for peacemaking accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sulu claim is under litigation and inflames feeling in Sabah; talks with the Communist Party remain a charged memory.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, along with their own envoys and community negotiators, kept apart.
