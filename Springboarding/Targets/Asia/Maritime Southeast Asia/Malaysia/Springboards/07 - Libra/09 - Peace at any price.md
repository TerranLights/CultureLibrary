# Malaysia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Manglish, Cantonese, Hokkien and Mandarin, Penan and Semai (through interpreters), Kadazandusun for native-court settlements. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay and Malaysian Chinese communities after 1969:** what each was told not to say, and how memory of the riots is kept.
- **Women in Kelantan and Terengganu against Klang Valley women:** reporting abuse under different state religious and social settings.
- **Penan and other Orang Ulu in logging areas:** the price of silence in remote places.
- **Semai and other Orang Asli:** nonviolence as a way of life against avoidance under pressure.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau:** customary payment as peacemaking.
- **Chinese small traders in Penang, Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru:** extortion and the choice to pay.
- **Young online Malaysians:** speaking out against the 3R limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, riots and NGO reports; mixed for family silence, which surveys undercount; thin for Borneo customary settlements.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Chinese for the 1969 literature; Penan and Kadazandusun through interpreters; Cantonese for extortion reporting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** May 13 and the 3R topics are policed by law; abuse survivors' accounts need consent and care; Penan testimony involves vulnerable minors.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own experience of keeping quiet under pressure, set apart.
