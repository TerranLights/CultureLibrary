# Malaysia: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu Malays:** the strongest heritage of seances and the strongest campaigns against them.
- **West-coast urban Malays:** *ruqyah*, detox and wellness markets in place of the *bomoh*.
- **Kadazandusun of the Kinabalu districts:** a sacred mountain cleansed after a breach.
- **Melanau of Mukah and Dalat:** a coastal cleansing kept by Muslim, Christian and older-faith Melanau together.
- **Temiar and Semai:** healing that removes illness through song, under pressure from missions and state programs.
- **Malaysian Chinese of Penang, Ampang and Klang:** temple purification, ghost appeasement and household cleaning before the year turns.
- **Iban:** the *manang*'s healing rites and church deliverance prayer as rival cleanings.
- **Undocumented Filipino and Indonesian migrants in Sabah:** periodic deportation drives, the purge of a population (comparison with Sabah's own peoples).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malay spirit healing and for political detentions; mixed for Borneo rites (festival material abundant, ritual meaning less so); thin for how ordinary people use *ruqyah* and detox products.
- **Languages needed:** Kelantan and Terengganu dialect for seance texts; Jawi for *kitab tib*; Hokkien for temple practice; Kadazandusun and Melanau for rite names and chants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "deviant teaching" labels carry legal penalties; detention-without-trial history is politically charged; seance and priestess knowledge is held by practitioners; deportation data is restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own purification festivals and fasting rites, set aside for the author.
