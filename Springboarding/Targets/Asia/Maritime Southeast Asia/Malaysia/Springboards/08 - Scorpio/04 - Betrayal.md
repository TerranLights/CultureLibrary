# Malaysia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Sabah Malay, Sarawak Malay, Iban, Kadazandusun, Semai. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause one owed loyalty to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of the old sultanate states:** *derhaka* to ruler against betrayal of a friend, as the Tuah-Jebat quarrel frames it.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu voters:** long PAS-UMNO rivalry, where switching sides carries religious charge.
- **Sabah's peoples (Kadazandusun, Bajau, Murut):** the 1994 defections and MA63 grievances shape talk of betrayal by the center.
- **Sarawak's Malays, Iban and Chinese:** cession memory, and loyalty to the state against the federation.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** 汉奸 charges inside party politics; wartime collaborators; triad codes against informers.
- **Former communists and the families of the Emergency's informers:** betrayal on both sides of an armed conflict.
- **Orang Asli (Semai, Temiar, Jakun):** wartime and Emergency service, and later disputes over land promises.
- **Peranakan and Kristang families:** small communities where marriage out could be read as desertion of the group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court literature and political betrayal; mixed for wartime and Emergency collaboration (archives exist, memory is guarded); thin for infidelity as measured behavior.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for the *hikayat* manuscripts; Cantonese and Hokkien slang for informer talk; Sabah and Sarawak Malay for regional political speech; Iban for oral tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticizing rulers can bring sedition charges, which bears on any *derhaka* research; communist history remains guarded; Borneo secession talk is sensitive. Treason as law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own material on loyalty and betrayal, set aside for the author.
