# Malaysia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English and its slang, Mandarin, Hokkien, Cantonese, Iban, Kayan and Kenyah, Semang (Menraq) languages. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Kelantan and Terengganu Malays:** *takdir* and *tawakal* in farming and fishing talk, beside west-coast professional Malays' planning language.
- **FELDA settler families:** life shaped by a scheme, a distinct generational experience.
- **Malaysian Chinese of the New Villages:** a memory of fences and curfews, and a commercial culture of managing luck.
- **Iban and Bidayuh farmers:** omen-reading before work, alongside church practice.
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Ukit of the Balui:** a river taken under control by others.
- **Semang (Menraq), Semai and Temuan:** movement and settlement directed by the state's Orang Asli department.
- **Sabah's Bajau and Kadazandusun:** compare with Sarawak for state control of land and fishing grounds.
- **Urban Klang Valley households:** tracking apps, tuition schedules and hired help as present-day control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state control (resettlement, censorship, land schemes); mixed for household control (NGO material, little survey data); thin for Orang Asli and Orang Ulu accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for almanac and *ketika* manuscripts; Hokkien and Cantonese for New Village memory and feng-shui talk; Iban for augury; Kayan and Kenyah for Bakun testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention and censorship history is politically charged; domestic-violence records are confidential; Orang Asli land disputes are in the courts; Iban augury and *pawang* knowledge belong to practitioners. Statutes on control belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own material on control at home and at work, set aside for the author.
