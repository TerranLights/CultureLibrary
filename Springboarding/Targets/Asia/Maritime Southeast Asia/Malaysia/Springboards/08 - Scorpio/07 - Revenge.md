# Malaysia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Kelantan Malay, Malaysian English, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Hakka, Iban, Kadazandusun, Murut, Kayan and Kenyah. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; and the alternatives to it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of Pahang and Perak:** memory of anti-colonial avengers, kept in songs, graves and film.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu villagers:** feared covert payback through sorcery, and religious campaigns against it.
- **Iban:** the old raid-and-reprisal pattern, the 1924 peace and how it is told in longhouses now.
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Kelabit:** the other side of the Kapit peace, with their own memory of raids.
- **Murut and Kadazandusun:** headhunting past, the Rundum rising, and the Monsopiad legend.
- **Malaysian Chinese of Perak and Penang:** society feuds of the tin era, and later gang retaliation.
- **Semai and other Orang Asli:** published claims of nonviolence to test against accounts of Semai soldiers in the Emergency.
- **Online generation in the Klang Valley:** viral shaming as payback.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial-era feuds, *amok* and secret societies; mixed for Borneo raiding (colonial records heavy, community accounts thinner); thin for everyday grudges and covert revenge as people report them.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for court and colonial-era Malay sources; Cantonese, Hokkien and Hakka for society and gang history; Iban, Kayan and Murut for raid memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** heads kept in longhouses are sacred heritage; sorcery accusations can lead to violence; recent gang feuds are active police matters. Private vengeance set against the state's punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own material on feuds and reprisal, set aside for the author.
