# Malaysia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Kelantan Malay, Malaysian English and its slang, Mandarin, Cantonese, Hokkien, Baba Malay, Iban, Kadazandusun. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays in close kampung life:** *tutup aib* as duty, beside gossip circles and the exposure of *tangkap basah* raids.
- **Kelantan performers and healers:** *wayang kulit* and *main peteri* masters who hold knowledge under oath.
- **Silat lineages across the Peninsula:** teachings guarded by oath and licence.
- **Malaysian Chinese:** the secret-society past, family shame kept in the house, and dialect switched to keep children out.
- **Peranakan families of Malacca and Penang:** recipes and household knowledge passed down only to some.
- **Iban and Kadazandusun ritual specialists:** chants that belong to their keepers.
- **Orang Asli communities:** what may be shared with researchers and officials, and what may not.
- **LGBT Malaysians, urban and rural:** the closet under religious and state policing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for secret societies, 1MDB and press freedom; mixed for silat and ritual knowledge (insider accounts exist but are guarded); thin for family secrets and privacy as people experience them.
- **Languages needed:** Kelantan Malay for performers' talk; Cantonese and Hokkien for society argots; Baba Malay for Peranakan household sources; Iban and Kadazandusun for ritual chants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** chants, silat inner teachings and society oaths are restricted and are not to be retold; LGBT and adoption research needs consent and anonymity; official secrets and naming rules are Libra's for the law itself.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their own secret-society and family-secret material, set aside for the author.
