# Malaysia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin, Iban, Kadazandusun, Murut, Penan and Temiar, English. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay nationalists**: the negotiated, royal-led stream (UMNO 1946) against the radical left.
- **Chinese Malaysians** of the New Villages, for freedom of movement lost and regained.
- **Sabahans and Sarawakians**, for autonomy framed against the Peninsula.
- **Iban**, through Rentap and the Brooke wars; **Murut**, through Antanum's rising.
- **Kadazandusun** and **Bajau** of Sabah, for Mat Salleh's memory and its claimants.
- **Penan**, **Kelabit** and **Temiar**, for land as freedom.
- **Kelantan** and **Pahang** Malays, for Tok Janggut and Mat Kilau.
- **Urban students and graduates**, for Baling (1974), *Reformasi* (1998) and Bersih.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political history and NGO records; mixed for everyday talk of personal freedom; thin for Orang Asli and Borneo peoples' own words for it.
- **Languages needed:** Malay; Mandarin for New Village and leftist sources; Iban and Murut for oral memory of resistance; English for colonial files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the communist insurgency, detention without trial, royalty and religion are touchy topics; some publications may be restricted; interviewees may self-censor; law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
