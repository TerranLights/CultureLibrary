## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay readers of Malay-language press** against **Chinese-press readers**: two news worlds.
- **Kelantan**, where PAS and UMNO media tell rival truths.
- **Penang and Melaka Chinese** temple-goers, for divine confirmation of facts.
- **Iban**, for omens and dreams as sources of knowledge.
- **Kadazandusun** villages, for rival origin versions.
- **Semai** and **Temiar**, for dream-knowledge.
- **Sabahans and Sarawakians**, for histories of 1963 told against the Peninsular textbook.
- **Peranakan Chinese**, for family histories that fit neither main narrative.
- **Urban young people**, by platform (WhatsApp, TikTok).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media and political disputes; mixed for religious epistemology (much in Jawi *kitab* and lectures); thin for Orang Asli and smaller Borneo peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Malay, with Arabic for Sufi terms; Mandarin and the dialects for temple practice; Iban and Kadazandusun for oral knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** May 13, the Emergency, royal matters and religion are touchy; some records stay classified; "fake news" carries legal risk (law goes to Libra).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
