*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin and Cantonese, Penang Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Sama-Bajau, Melanau, Batek, English. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Terengganu and Kelantan Malays**, where reform-minded teaching meets older spirit belief most openly.
- **Perlis**, for the sharpest official line against *khurafat*.
- **Factory and dormitory workers** in Selangor and Penang, for belief under industrial life.
- **Penang Hokkien** and **Kuala Lumpur Cantonese**, for taboo and luck customs.
- **Peranakan Chinese**, for blended belief.
- **Iban** and **Melanau**, for spirit belief kept or dropped after conversion.
- **Kadazandusun**, between rice-spirit rites and church teaching.
- **Batek** and **Mah Meri**, for Orang Asli worlds of beings.
- **Bajau Laut** against land-based **Bajau**, for belief and mobility.
- **Urban nonbelievers** and freethinkers, a small and quiet group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official doctrine and Malay folk belief; mixed for Chinese belief (spread across press, temples and commerce); thin for smaller Borneo and Orang Asli groups.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Jawi for manuscripts; Mandarin, Cantonese and Hokkien for taboo lore; Iban, Melanau and Kadazandusun for spirit vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "deviant" belief can bring official action; public atheism draws hostility; some ritual knowledge (healing chants) is held by specialists; belief in sorcery can lead to accusations against named people.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
