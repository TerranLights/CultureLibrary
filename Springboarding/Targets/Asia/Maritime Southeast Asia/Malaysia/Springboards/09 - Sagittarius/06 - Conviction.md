## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negeri Sembilan** *adat perpatih* communities, where custom itself is the thing held fast.
- **Kelantan** PAS supporters, for religious-political steadfastness, against **Johor** and **Selangor** UMNO loyalists.
- **Pahang** east-coast townspeople, for environmental stands.
- **Chinese Malaysian** educationists, for decades-long campaigns.
- **Kenyah, Kayan and Penan**, for dam resistance; **Bidayuh**, for resettlement.
- **Dusun** of Papar and the interior, for land-based resolve.
- **Kristang**, for a small minority's persistence.
- **Sabah** voters, after repeated defections, for views on switching sides.
- **Malay celebrities and fans**, for the *hijrah* turn.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political defections and the Hang Tuah debate; mixed for environmental and Indigenous campaigns (mostly NGO and news sources); thin for private, non-political conviction.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Negeri Sembilan dialect; Mandarin for the Chinese education campaigns; Orang Ulu and Dusun languages for community statements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest leaders have faced charges; dam and land disputes can involve ongoing cases (law to Libra and Capricorn); the *hijrah* trend touches personal faith and should not be caricatured.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
