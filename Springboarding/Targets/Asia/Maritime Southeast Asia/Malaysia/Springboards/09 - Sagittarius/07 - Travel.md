*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu** Malays, with long hajj and Mecca-study links, against **Klang Valley** professionals who travel for work and leisure.
- **Negeri Sembilan** Malays, for the *merantau* heritage.
- **Chinese Malaysians** by origin county and dialect (Hokkien, Hakka, Cantonese, Teochew, Foochow), for migration memory and study abroad.
- **Iban**, for *bejalai*.
- **Sama-Bajau** and **Suluk** of eastern Sabah, for sea mobility and cross-border kin.
- **Kadazandusun**, as hosts on a sacred mountain.
- **Penan** and **Batek**, for nomadic and seasonal movement.
- **Malaysians abroad** (Singapore, Taiwan, Australia, the UK), as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hajj, tourism and emigration statistics; mixed for Chinese migration (Chinese-language association records); thin for Orang Asli and Bajau mobility outside a few studies.
- **Languages needed:** Malay and Jawi for pilgrim records; Mandarin and the varieties for association books; Iban; Sama-Bajau; English for colonial shipping files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** stateless and undocumented people in Sabah are a sensitive topic; migrant-worker abuse; air disasters remain painful; sacred-site behavior can be a grievance.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
