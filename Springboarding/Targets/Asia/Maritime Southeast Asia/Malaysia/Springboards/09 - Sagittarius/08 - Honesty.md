## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay** communities, where *budi* and indirect speech can pull against bluntness; compare **Kelantanese** and **Johor** speech styles.
- **Chinese Malaysian traders** by dialect group (Hokkien, Hakka, Teochew), for business good faith.
- **Peranakan Chinese** of Melaka, for family-firm trust.
- **Sabahans** after a large graft scandal, against **Sarawakians**.
- **Dusun** and **Bajau** traders at the *tamu*.
- **Orang Asli** (Temuan, Jakun, Semelai), as sellers facing middlemen.
- **Iban** longhouse communities, for honesty under *adat* (law itself to Libra).
- **Students and parents** under exam pressure.
