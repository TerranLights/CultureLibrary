# Malaysia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Arabic religious vocabulary, Mandarin, Kenyah, Kadazandusun, English. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**, where PAS governs, against **Penang** and **Selangor**.
- **Perlis**, for state-sponsored literalism, against **Kedah's** Sufi-inclined traditions.
- **Urban professional Malays** formed by campus *dakwah*, against rural pondok traditionalists.
- **Chinese Malaysians** in sectarian halls and evangelical churches.
- **Kenyah** and **Kayan**, for the Bungan restoration.
- **Kadazandusun** and **Murut**, for memory of conversion pressure.
- **Iban** and **Lun Bawang** evangelicals, for revivalist strictness.
- **Orang Asli** communities targeted by competing missions.
