# Malaysia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Hokkien and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Melanau, Kristang, Chewong and other Orang Asli languages. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malay Muslims across states**: whether *fardu kifayah* is felt as a village burden or left to the state.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu villages**: duties of the *surau* and the funeral rites kept by neighbors.
- **Negeri Sembilan's *Adat Perpatih***: duties pass through the mother's clan.
- **Iban, Bidayuh and Melanau**: longhouse, village and coastal rites that bind households.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut**: duties to rice, harvest and the bobohizan's rites.
- **Orang Asli peoples** (Chewong, Semai, Temiar): sharing claims instead of ranked duties.
- **Malaysian Chinese, by variety and religion**: filial duty under Buddhist, Taoist and Christian teaching; the Peranakan's mixed rites.
- **Kristang and Malaysian Siamese**: small communities where saint's feasts or temple duties fall on few families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malay loyalty literature and religious teaching; mixed for Chinese filial practice; thin for Murut and most Orang Asli groups.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for older *kitab* on religious duty; Iban for longhouse obligations; Mandarin and Cantonese for clan records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loyalty to the rulers is a legally guarded theme; national service and Biro Tatanegara courses are politically contested; some bobohizan rites are restricted.
- **Held for review:** filial and communal duties of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Patani and Brunei loyalty traditions only as comparison.
