*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Federal civil servants and state administrations**: where *amanah* is invoked, and where blame passes upward.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu under different ruling parties**: whether accountability is framed as religious or administrative.
- **Kedah and Perlis rice families**: the farmer's load in a bad season and its attribution.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau of Sabah**: compensation customs set beside the courts.
- **Iban and Bidayuh**: longhouse versus village answerability under customary rules.
- **Penan and other Orang Ulu peoples**: stewardship duties tested against logging.
- **Semai, Temiar and Jakun**: how small Orang Asli groups handle harm without courts.
- **Malaysian Chinese business families and the Peranakan**: owner responsibility for workers and kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public-sector accountability; mixed for customary compensation; thin for the Bajau and Penan in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for audit and religious sources; Kadazandusun and Iban for native-court practice; Mandarin for guild histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1MDB is still before the courts; native-court records may be closed; Penan land disputes are ongoing.
- **Held for review:** answerability inside South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Brunei and Riau sources serve only as comparison.
