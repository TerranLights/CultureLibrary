*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Temiar. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, churches, the state) or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **National schools, Chinese vernacular and independent schools, and religious schools**: three disciplinary regimes in one system.
- **East-coast *pondok* and *tahfiz* students against urban west-coast pupils**: religious against secular routines.
- **Factory workers in Penang, Selangor and Johor**: industrial time-discipline at work.
- **Iban and Orang Ulu youth**: training of young men in longhouse life versus schooling away from home.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau families**: corrections at home and the rice-cycle round of tasks.
- **Temiar, Semai and Batek**: Orang Asli child-rearing set against boarding schools run by the state.
- **Silat, lion dance and sports clubs across groups**: chosen disciplines outside school.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school rules and religious practice; mixed for factory and civil-service discipline; thin for Murut and Bajau child-rearing.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for school circulars; Mandarin for independent-school rules; Iban and Kadazandusun for home training.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** caning and abuse in *tahfiz* schools have made headlines; bobohizan chants are restricted knowledge; BTN is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** discipline regimes within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Singapore's school discipline is comparison only.
