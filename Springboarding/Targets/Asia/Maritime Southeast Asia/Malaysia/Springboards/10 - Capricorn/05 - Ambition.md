# Malaysia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), English, Mandarin, Hokkien and Foochow, Iban, Kadazandusun, Jahai and other Orang Asli languages. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Malay families in Kedah, Kelantan and Pahang**: FELDA and scholarships as routes upward.
- **Negeri Sembilan Malays**: *merantau* as a customary push to leave and return better off.
- **Malaysian Chinese varieties**: Hokkien traders, Hakka miners, Foochow pioneers and Cantonese artisans tell different stories of rising.
- **Peranakan Chinese of Melaka and Penang**: older wealth weighing ambition against keeping standing.
- **Iban men and women**: *bejalai* against new wage work and university.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut and Bajau**: education and civil-service ambitions in Sabah.
- **Orang Asli peoples**: aspiration shaped by resettlement and schooling rather than chosen alone.
- **Urban professionals in the Klang Valley, Penang and Johor Bahru**: migration abroad versus staying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state planning and Malay debate; mixed for Chinese-language material; thin for the Bajau, Murut and most Orang Asli voices.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for policy and essays; Mandarin and Hokkien for family striving; Iban for *bejalai*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnic quotas and "lazy native" claims stay charged; treat national-character writing as a subject of study, never as fact.
- **Held for review:** striving and mobility among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Malaysians abroad appear only for comparison.
