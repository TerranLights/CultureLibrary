## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Perak, Johor, Selangor and Kedah courts**: royal descendant lines and titled chiefs ranked differently in each.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu**: *Tengku*, *Nik* and *Wan* lines beside rural *rakyat*.
- **Negeri Sembilan**: clan ranks set by the mother's line under *Adat Perpatih*.
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Kelabit**: inherited strata versus rank won by feasting.
- **Iban and Bidayuh**: ethnographers disagree on how ranked the longhouse is; set their claims beside Iban and Bidayuh writers.
- **Bajau, Suluk and Kadazandusun of Sabah**: titled leaders, native chiefs and village heads.
- **Temuan, Jakun and Semai**: formal Proto-Malay offices against Senoi peoples with fewer ranks.
- **Malaysian Chinese and the Peranakan**: towkay, clan elder and generational seniority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court and civil-service rank; mixed for Chinese clan precedence; thin for Bajau and Orang Asli offices outside reports.
- **Languages needed:** palace Malay; Mandarin for clan rules; Kayan and Kenyah for the Borneo strata.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** royal rank and *Bumiputera* status are legally guarded topics; colonial descriptions of "slaves" among Borneo peoples must be read beside the peoples' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** rank inside South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Brunei's court ranks only as comparison.
