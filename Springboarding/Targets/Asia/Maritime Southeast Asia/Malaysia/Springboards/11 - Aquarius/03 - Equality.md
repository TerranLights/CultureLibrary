# Malaysia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi, including court Malay), Mandarin, Malaysian English, Kayan, Batek, Iban. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is the question.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kedah and Perlis rice villages, rich and poor households**: equality argued inside one Malay village.
- **Batek, Semai and other Orang Asli**: small forest societies against the state's ranked order.
- **Kayan and Kenyah against Iban**: ranked and less ranked Bornean peoples side by side.
- **Kelantan's market women** (Kota Bharu): the east-coast question of women's standing in trade.
- **Sabah and Sarawak against the Peninsula**: claims of "equal partners" in the federation (the legal side belongs to Libra).
- **Urban B40 households in Klang Valley flats** against rural poor: two kinds of being left behind.
- **Chinese-school graduates**: equality of schooling and certificates.
- **Stateless people in Sabah** (Bajau Laut, children of migrants): those outside the citizen frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income statistics and policy debate; mixed for gender; thin for Orang Asli and stateless people's own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** court Malay for the honors and address system; Chinese for education claims; Kayan and Batek terms only through ethnographers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bumiputera policy and the Malay special position are guarded topics; questioning the rulers is restricted; constitutional equality and discrimination law belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
