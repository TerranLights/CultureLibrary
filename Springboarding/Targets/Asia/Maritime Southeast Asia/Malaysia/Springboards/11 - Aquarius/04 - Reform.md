# Malaysia: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi, and Jawi for the reformist press before about 1960), Arabic loan terms of Islamic renewal, Mandarin, Malaysian English, Kayan and Kenyah for Bornean religious renewal. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu** against the **west-coast states**: Islamist reform at state level against developmental reform from Putrajaya.
- **Kaum Muda towns** (Penang, Malacca, Singapore links) against **pondok-centered villages** of Kedah and Kelantan.
- **The urban Malay middle class of 1998**: who joined Reformasi and who stayed home.
- **Negeri Sembilan adat perpatih families**: disputes over reforming matrilineal property under Islamic and civil rules (the legal side to Libra).
- **Chinese educationists and associations**: reform demands made through *huatuan*.
- **Sabah's Kadazandusun leaders** and **Sarawak's Dayak parties** of the 1980s: reform as claims of Borneo peoples.
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Kelabit**: religious renewal from within.
- **Orang Asli**: on the receiving end of state reform.
- **Students and first-time voters**: Undi18 and campus movements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious reform and Reformasi; good press archives in Malay and Chinese; thin for Borneo movements outside the Sarawak and Sabah journals.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi reading for *al-Imam* and *Pengasuh*; Arabic terms of Islamic renewal; Chinese for the 1999 appeals; Kayan and Kenyah via ethnographers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Reformasi involves living politicians and trials; Islamic reform movements later banned (Darul Arqam); electoral and constitutional changes are law, so Libra and Capricorn take the legal side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
