# Malaysia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Malaysian English slang, Mandarin, Hokkien and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Kayan and Kenyah. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pupils of national, Chinese-medium and religious schools**: whether friendship crosses school streams.
- **Malay men's circles at the *warung* and surau** against **Chinese men's kopitiam regulars**: places and hours of men's friendship.
- **Kelantan and Terengganu youth**: friendship in dialect, and its migration to the Klang Valley.
- **Iban youths on *bejalai*** (journeying away to work): friendships made far from the longhouse.
- **Kayan, Kenyah and Iban**: alliances and friendships between peoples once at war.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut villages**: age-mates and drinking companions at *tapai* gatherings, to test.
- **Orang Asli children in boarding schools**: friendships across peoples and with Malay pupils.
- **Peranakan Chinese**: friendship networks once built through clubs and social societies.
- **Malaysian Chinese sworn brotherhoods**: formal friendship, and its shading into secret-society ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in popular film, comics and survey summaries; mixed for ethnographic studies of adult friendship; thin for Bornean ritual friendship outside colonial records.
- **Languages needed:** Malaysian English slang for online sources; Cantonese and Hokkien for older Chinese friendship talk; Iban, Kayan and Kadazandusun terms via dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** close friendship between men can be read through Malaysia's guarded sexuality debates, so use care; cronyism ties friendship to corruption cases, where Libra and Capricorn take the legal side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
