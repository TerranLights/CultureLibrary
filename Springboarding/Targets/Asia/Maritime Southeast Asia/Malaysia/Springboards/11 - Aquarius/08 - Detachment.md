# Malaysia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi, and Jawi for Sufi texts), Arabic terms of Islamic ethics, Mandarin and Hokkien, Thai for Kelantan's Siamese temples, Iban, Chewong and Semai terms through ethnographers. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms are prized (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and others condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pondok and *tariqah* communities of Kelantan and Kedah**: renunciation as a chosen path.
- **Klang Valley condominium dwellers**: anonymity and "not knowing the neighbors" as a complaint or a comfort.
- **Negeri Sembilan Malays**: the reason-and-passion talk as studied there.
- **Malaysian Chinese Buddhists and Taoists**: letting go in temple practice, and temporary abstinence in festivals.
- **Kelantan's Siamese**: a Theravada minority with monastic retreat.
- **Chewong, Semai and Temiar**: rules on feeling in small Orang Asli societies.
- **Iban**: formal ending of grief, and the *bejalai* journey as detachment from home.
- **Kadazandusun and Murut Christians and animists**: mourning customs to contrast with Iban practice.
- **Young urban voters**: political apathy as stance or as resignation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Islamic and Buddhist teaching on non-attachment; mixed for emotion ethnography (mostly from the 1980s and 1990s); thin for present-day measures of reserve.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi and Arabic for Sufi texts; Hokkien for everyday "can't be bothered" talk; Thai for Siamese temple material; Iban for mourning rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some *tariqah* have been ruled deviant by religious authorities; mental-health topics carry stigma; voters' disengagement is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
