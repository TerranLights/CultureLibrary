# Malaysia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Arabic formulas in Malay use, Mandarin, Hokkien and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Kelabit, Temiar terms through ethnographers, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Terengganu migrants in the Klang Valley**: hope pinned on work in the capital and on returning home.
- **Urban low-income families in PPR flats**: hope and despair under financial strain.
- **Malaysian Chinese small traders**: luck, vows and number games beside planning.
- **Iban youths on *bejalai***: the journey out as a wager on fortune.
- **Kadazandusun farmers**: harvest hope in ritual and in church.
- **Kelabit highlanders**: religious hope after revival.
- **Temiar and other Orang Asli**: hope for healing through dream and song.
- **Sabah's east-coast Bajau and migrant communities**: hope for papers and settlement.
- **Graduates considering leaving the country**: hope at home or abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national campaigns, politics and surveys; mixed for ritual hope (much of it colonial-era); thin for Sabah's migrants and Orang Asli voices.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic formulas for everyday Muslim hope; Hokkien and Cantonese for luck talk; Iban and Kadazandusun for ritual texts; Kelabit for revival memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** number games are closed to Muslims, so luck data split along religious lines (the legal side to Libra); suicide data are sensitive; some Iban and Kadazandusun ritual texts are restricted to specialists.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
