*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi); Malaysian Chinese varieties (Hokkien, Cantonese, Hakka, Mandarin); Iban; Kadazandusun; Bajau; Orang Asli languages; English. **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with its magic ways (healing, charms, divination) and the places where people are confined or set apart.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West-coast Malays** (Selangor, Perak, Malacca) against **east-coast Malays** (Kelantan, Terengganu), where healing performance survived longer; **Negeri Sembilan** for its Minangkabau-rooted *adat perpatih*; **Kedah and Perlis** for the northern court tradition.
- **Malaysian Chinese by dialect** (Penang Hokkien, Klang Valley and Ipoh Cantonese, Sabah Hakka, Sibu Foochow) and **Peranakan Chinese** of Malacca and Penang, whose household rites blend.
- **Iban, Bidayuh, Melanau** (the Kaul sea rites of Mukah) and **Orang Ulu** (Kayan, Kenyah, Kelabit, with Christian revival history).
- **Kadazandusun, Murut, Rungus** and **Bajau** (Semporna's sea Bajau) in Sabah.
- **Orang Asli**: Temiar, Semai, Chewong, Batek, Temuan, Jakun.
- **Malaysian Siamese** of Kedah and Kelantan; **Kristang** Eurasians of Malacca.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for peninsular Malay and Chinese practice; mixed for Sarawak and Sabah; thin and partly restricted for Orang Asli. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the core lands (the peninsula, Sabah, Sarawak, Labuan).
- **Languages needed:** Malay in Jawi for manuscripts and *kitab*; Hokkien and Cantonese for temple speech; Iban and Kadazandusun for ritual chant; Temiar and Semai for healing song.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state religious departments regulate *bomoh* practice and heterodox groups; some ritual knowledge is held by priestesses or shamans; possession outbreaks involve schoolchildren; legal sides go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
