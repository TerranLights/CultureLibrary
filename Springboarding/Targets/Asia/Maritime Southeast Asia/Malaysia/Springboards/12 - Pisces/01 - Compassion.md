# Malaysia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin and the southern Chinese varieties, Iban, Kadazandusun, English. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of the Klang Valley** against **Kelantan and Terengganu**, where flood relief and *surau* networks are the first line of help.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by setting: Penang and Malacca temple charity against Klang Valley secular NGOs; **Peranakan Chinese** households.
- **Iban longhouses** and **Bidayuh villages** in Sarawak, where help moves along kin and longhouse lines; **Melanau** of the coast.
- **Kadazandusun**, **Murut** and **Bajau** in Sabah, with the stateless sea Bajau as a test of who is owed pity.
- **Orang Asli** groups (Batek, Semai, Temuan) on sharing and the care of the sick.
- **Christian** congregations of Sabah and Sarawak, with their own relief arms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on zakat, welfare and NGOs; thin on Borneo languages' terms for pity.
- **Languages needed:** Malay of zakat and sermon prose, Mandarin for Tzu Chi and clan records, Iban for oral texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** zakat eligibility and converts; statelessness in Sabah; mercy to offenders through royal pardons is a legal matter, left to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category, for their own charity and almsgiving customs.
