# Malaysia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin and Cantonese, Iban, Kadazandusun, Semai, English. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Urban Malays** in mixed Klang Valley workplaces against **rural Kelantan and Terengganu** Malays, where indirect speech and *hati*-idioms may weigh differently.
- **Malaysian Chinese** schooled in Chinese-medium schools against those schooled in Malay or English; **Peranakan Chinese**, who live between idioms.
- **Iban** and **Bidayuh**, whose longhouse and village life share grief collectively; **Melanau** and **Orang Ulu** for comparison within Sarawak.
- **Kadazandusun**, **Murut** and **Bajau** in Sabah, a state where intermarriage is common.
- **Semai**, **Chewong** and **Temiar** for the *punen* complex.
- **Malaysians working in Singapore** (diaspora, comparison only) on being read by others.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong on Malay emotion words and on psychometrics, weak on Borneo-language terms.
- **Languages needed:** Malay idiom and *pantun* (wordplay matters), Mandarin for the press, Iban for laments, Semai for *punen*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic feeling is politically charged; laments may be restricted to funerals; minors in school-contagion cases.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their roles in cross-community films and festive ads are set aside with them.
