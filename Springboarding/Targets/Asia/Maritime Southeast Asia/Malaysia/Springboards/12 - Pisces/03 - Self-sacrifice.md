# Malaysia: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Mandarin and Hokkien, Iban, Kadazandusun, Murut, English. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Peninsular Malays** of the west coast against **Kelantan and Terengganu**, where religious self-denial and royal loyalty may be weighed differently; **Negeri Sembilan** for *merantau*, leaving home to provide.
- **Malaysian Chinese**: Chinese-educated against English-educated families on sacrifice for schooling; **Peranakan** households.
- **Iban** (warrior tradition), **Bidayuh** and **Orang Ulu** in Sarawak.
- **Kadazandusun** (Huminodun), **Murut** (Antanum) and **Bajau and Suluk** (the 1943 revolt) in Sabah.
- **Orang Asli** who served (Senoi Praaq) against those displaced by the same war.
- **Malaysians commuting to Singapore** (diaspora, comparison only) as earners for families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and religious sacrifice; mixed for caregiving and Borneo resistance figures.
- **Languages needed:** classical Malay of the *hikayat*; Mandarin for the schools movement; Kadazandusun for Huminodun tellings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** communist fighters of the Emergency are excluded from national honors and remain contested; Rosli Dhobi's killing of a governor; martyrdom language in politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (war dead, plantation labor and penitential vows are not scouted here).
