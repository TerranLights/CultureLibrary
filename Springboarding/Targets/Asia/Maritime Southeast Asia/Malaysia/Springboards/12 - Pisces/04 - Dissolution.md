# Malaysia: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and Jawi), Hokkien and Mandarin, Javanese as spoken in Johor, Iban, Bajau, Kristang, English. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the self losing its edges in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kelantan and Kedah** *tarekat* milieux against urban Klang Valley Muslims wary of ecstatic practice.
- **Johor's Javanese-descended Malays** (trance dance) against Malays of Malacca and Selangor.
- **Malaysian Chinese** temple devotees against churchgoing and secular Chinese; **Peranakan** and **Kristang** communities whose languages are fading.
- **Bajau Laut** of Semporna against land-based **Bajau** of the west coast of Sabah; **Kadazandusun** and **Murut** in mixed marriages.
- **Iban**, **Bidayuh** and **Melanau** (how far conversion merged Muslim Melanau into Malay identity is a question to test).
- **Orang Asli** facing *masuk Melayu* pressure and resettlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on heterodox-sect cases and divorce counts; mixed on trance; thin on Borneo rites of ending.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi Malay for Sufi texts, Hokkien for temple trance talk, Johor Javanese, Kristang and Baba Malay, Bajau Sama.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** apostasy and "deviant teaching" are policed; secession talk in Sabah and Sarawak; the 1969 riots; syariah-court divorce (as law, it belongs to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside here with their trance and fire-walking rites.
