# Malaysia: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ethnic categories and housing; mixed on personal space and touch; thin on Borneo terms for privacy.
- **Languages needed:** Malay for purity and etiquette, Cantonese for prying talk, Iban for longhouse space, Kelabit and Lun Bawang for the border highlands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ethnicity and religion lines are policed by law and public anger; close proximity between unmarried couples (*khalwat*) and land rights are legal questions for Libra and Capricorn; the Sulu claim and eastern Sabah security.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, along with their own purity and threshold customs.
