## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Klang Valley** urban youth against **Kelantan and Terengganu**, where public entertainment is restricted.
- **Northern Malays** of Kedah and Perlis (*ketum*) against the southern peninsula.
- **Malaysian Chinese** by class and dialect on gambling and drinking; **Peranakan** women's card circles.
- **Iban** and **Bidayuh** on *tuak*, and **Orang Ulu** on *bejalai*-like journeys.
- **Kadazandusun** and **Murut** on festival drink, set against church temperance teaching.
- **Orang Asli** groups whose history includes flight into the forest.
- **Cross-Causeway workers** who come home at weekends (abroad, so a comparison case only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on drugs, gambling and youth panics; mixed on alcohol by region; thin on Borneo drinking from the communities' own side.
- **Languages needed:** Malay of the moral press, Cantonese and Hokkien for gambling talk, Baba Malay, Iban and Kadazandusun for festival speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use carries heavy penalties; alcohol and gambling are forbidden to Muslims, so reporting differs by community; minors in the *bohsia* coverage; penalties themselves are Libra and Capricorn material.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, including their drinking and leisure customs.
