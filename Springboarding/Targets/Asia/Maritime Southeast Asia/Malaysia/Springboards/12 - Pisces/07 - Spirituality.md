*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pondok-taught Muslims** of the northern and east-coast states against professionals in Putrajaya and Shah Alam whose devotion runs through study circles (*usrah*) and phone apps.
- **Negeri Sembilan** villages against **Johor** and **Malacca** on spirit practice beside Islam.
- **Malaysian Chinese** Taoist and folk temple-goers, Buddhists and Christians; **Peranakan** households with mixed altars.
- **Malaysian Siamese** of Kelantan and Kedah.
- **Iban** keepers of *miring* against Iban Christians; **Bidayuh**; **Kenyah**, **Kayan** and **Kelabit** of the uplands.
- **Kadazandusun**, **Rungus** and **Murut** in Sabah; Bajau Muslims of the east coast.
- **Temiar**, **Semai** and **Jahai**, alongside Orang Asli who have converted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Malay Islamic practice and survey items; mixed for Chinese folk practice; thin and partly closed for Borneo and Orang Asli ritual.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for *kitab*, Hokkien for shrine speech, Kelantan Siamese Thai, Iban and Kenyah for chant, Temiar for dream songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Muslim participation in non-Islamic rites is policed by state religious authorities; conversion among Orang Asli is contested; closed ceremonies are respected, not researched.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, with their shrines and pilgrim festivals.
