# Malaysia: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay in both scripts, Mandarin, Cantonese and Hokkien, Malaysian English, Iban, Kadazandusun and Murut, Temiar and Semai, English. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays** of Kelantan, after the 2014 flood, against urban Malays of the west coast in the language of *reda* and complaint.
- **Malaysian Chinese** Buddhists, Taoists and Christians on letting go at death; families of former communist fighters.
- **Iban** following omens against Iban Christians; **Bidayuh** and **Melanau**.
- **Kadazandusun, Murut** and other peoples of Sabah's interior (Keningau, Tambunan) on what was yielded in 1963.
- **Temiar** and **Semai** resettled under regroupment.
- **Bajau** of Sabah's east coast after the 2013 incursion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war and religious acceptance; mixed on palliative care; thin on Borneo terms for yielding.
- **Languages needed:** Jawi for creed manuals, Mandarin for Buddhist teaching, Iban for augury, Kadazandusun and Murut for interior memory, Temiar and Semai.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the communist insurgency; Sabah and Sarawak grievances over the 1963 terms; end-of-life choices touch religious and legal limits (the legal side is for Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their deathbed and vow customs wait with them.
