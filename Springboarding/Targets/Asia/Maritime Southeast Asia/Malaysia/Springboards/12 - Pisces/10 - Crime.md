# Malaysia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi, with Jawi for older sources), Cantonese, Hokkien and Mandarin, Iban, Kadazandusun, Iranun, Semai, English. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. (The law itself is researched under Libra and Capricorn.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of Pahang and Kelantan**, where rebel heroes and religious sin weigh heavily, against **Klang Valley** Malays shaped by tabloid crime news.
- **Malaysian Chinese** of Perak and Penang (secret-society history) against middle-class Chinese of Petaling Jaya; **Peranakan** families.
- **Iban** (headhunting memory) set beside **Bidayuh** villages near Kuching and upriver **Orang Ulu** longhouses.
- **Kadazandusun** and **Murut** (*sogit*) against **Iranun, Bajau and Suluk** of Sabah's coasts, often named in fear talk.
- **Semai** and other **Orang Asli** settling wrongs by moot.
- **Undocumented migrants**, as a group others talk about, heard in their own voice where possible.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in tabloid and official counts; mixed on perception surveys; thin on Borneo ideas of wrong outside customary courts.
- **Languages needed:** tabloid Malay, Cantonese street slang, Iban, Kadazandusun for *sogit*, Iranun.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race and migrant blame; royal-insult and sedition limits on speech; the 1MDB affair is politically charged; criminal and customary law themselves belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; any crime-image pointer about them is set aside unscouted.
