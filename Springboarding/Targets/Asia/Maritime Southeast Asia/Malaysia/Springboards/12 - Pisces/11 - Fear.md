# Malaysia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Malaysia, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Malay (Rumi and, for the court chronicles, Jawi), Cantonese and Hokkien, Iban, Batek and Jahai, Kadazandusun, Malaysian English. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malays of Kedah and Perlis**, home of the Raja Bersiong lore, against **Johor** and the Klang Valley on ghost fear and the fear of God.
- **Malaysian Chinese** Cantonese and Hokkien families on fright rites and ghost-month caution; **Peranakan** grandmothers' warnings.
- **Iban** in longhouses against Iban in Kuching on spirit fear; **Bidayuh** and **Melanau** for comparison.
- **Sabah east-coast residents** (Bajau, Suluk, Chinese of Sandakan and Semporna) on kidnapping fear; **Kadazandusun** of the interior on *rogon* and mountain spirits.
- **Batek** and **Jahai** on thunder; **Temuan** near Kuala Lumpur.
- **Christian** and **Muslim** communities in the 2010 dispute.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ghosts, horror and disasters; mixed on anxiety measures; thin on Borneo and Orang Asli fear outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** colloquial Malay for horror speech, Cantonese for temple rites, Iban for spirit names, Batek and Jahai for thunder taboos.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** May 13 and interreligious incidents are tightly controlled topics; fright cures may be closed to outsiders; the punishment side of fear (policing, penalties) goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; fright cures and ghost lore from these communities are left out here.
