## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland Christian households** (Tagalog, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Bikol, Waray): extended family and godparent ties; compare towns and Metro Manila.
- **Muslim households in Lanao, Maguindanao and Sulu:** marriage arranged between families in some lineages; check each people separately.
- **Cordillera peoples:** village councils and elders against individual choice.
- **Mangyan peoples (Buid, Hanunuo, Iraya) and Palawan peoples:** small-scale societies with their own norms of personal self-rule.
- **Lumad communities:** their own schools and councils, and pressure from armed groups on both sides.
- **Chinese Filipino families:** family firms, inheritance and marriage within the community.
- **Families of overseas workers:** decisions made from abroad.
- **Gender and age:** daughters against sons; the eldest child; elders in care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in demographic surveys (NDHS, YAFS, Cebu cohort); mixed in ethnography; thin for Moro and small Indigenous groups.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano; Cordillera and Mindanao languages for collective self-rule.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** contraception, divorce and gender identity are live religious and political disputes; Lumad schools and activists have faced "red-tagging" (being labeled communist), so name no person; law on autonomy and ancestral domains belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
