## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog, Cebuano and Hiligaynon lowlands:** the Catholic year (Santo Niño, Holy Week, fiesta) as a set of starts; compare provinces.
- **Ifugao, Kankanaey and other Cordillera peoples:** agricultural calendars with their own openings.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** sea-season openings.
- **Muslim peoples (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug, Sama):** the Hijri calendar and Islamic rites of first things.
- **Panay peoples (Sulod or Panay Bukidnon) and Lumad groups:** origin epics and first-planting rites.
- **Waray of Leyte and Samar:** starting over after Haiyan.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** the lunar year.
- **Overseas workers' families, former rebels, returnees from prison:** the people most often told to start over.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for myths, folk customs and Catholic records; mixed for agricultural calendars; thin for Lumad and Sama openings.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Spanish, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon and Kinaray-a; Ifugao, Ivatan and Maranao for the regional rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some rice and fishing rites are restricted to the community; the 1521 framing divides people; Marawi and rebel reintegration are tied to conflict; divorce and annulment are legal matters for Libra.
