*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog Metro Manila:** traffic, tabloids, protest; against provincial towns.
- **Visayan peoples:** Cebuano, Hiligaynon and Waray terms and joking norms differ; test reputations, never assume them.
- **Ilocano and Bikol:** separate anger vocabularies and church histories.
- **Bugkalot of Aurora and Nueva Vizcaya:** *liget* as a named theory of anger.
- **Teduray and other Lumad peoples:** restraint norms and their own justice ways.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug, Iranun:** honor, feud and settlement.
- **Cordillera peoples:** older feud and peace-pact traditions.
- **Men and women; children; drinking men's groups:** who may show anger and where.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in classic ethnography (Bugkalot, Teduray) and on Mindanao feuds; mixed for lowland everyday anger; thin for current measured data on emotion.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Maranao and Maguindanaon for *rido*; Bugkalot and Teduray mostly through translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *amok* and *juramentado* carry colonial and anti-Moro baggage; treat them as labels to study, not explanations. Feuds involve living families. Domestic violence data needs care. Barangay conciliation, peace pacts and feud settlement as legal systems belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
