## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila universities and their alumni:** organized rivalry; against provincial schools.
- **Visayan towns:** Cebuano and Hiligaynon festivals, regattas and arnis clubs.
- **Ilocano and Bulacan towns:** board-exam and farm-contest pride.
- **Cordillera peoples:** rank won by feasting and generosity.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug:** rivalry between families and clans; Maranao *maratabat* in contests for rank.
- **General Santos and Davao:** boxing and migrant towns.
- **Mangyan, Palawan peoples, Aeta:** small-scale societies to check for cooperative rather than competitive games.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** school ranking and business rivalry.
- **Women and *bakla*:** pageants, including gay beauty contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sports, pageants and media; mixed for regional festival contests; thin for Indigenous games and feasting today.
