## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila commuters and riders:** motorcycles, jeepneys, tricycles; against rural roads in Mindanao and the Visayas.
- **Coastal fishers in Palawan, the Visayas and Sulu (Sama and Tausug):** sea risk as livelihood.
- **Mining communities:** Kankanaey and Ibaloi in Benguet; migrants at Mount Diwata.
- **Volcano and typhoon belts:** Batangas (Taal), Albay (Mayon, Bikol speakers), Eastern Visayas (Waray).
- **Students and fraternity members:** initiation and drinking.
- **Maranao and other Moro storytelling:** trickster daring.
- **Young men against women and elders:** who is expected to take risks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in health and crash statistics; mixed in ethnography; thin on how people themselves draw the line between brave and reckless.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English for health data; Cebuano, Hiligaynon and Ilocano in the field; Maranao for *Pilandok*; Benguet languages for mining.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the drug war and its killings; hazing deaths with grieving families; HIV status; poor people's dangerous work should not be labeled reckless before they are heard. Drug, gambling and traffic law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
