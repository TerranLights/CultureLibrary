*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog Catholic lowlands:** the base of most "Filipino values" literature; compare, don't generalize.
- **Iglesia ni Cristo, evangelical and Aglipayan congregations:** different church teaching inside the Christian majority.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon Visayans:** values literature in their own languages.
- **Ilocano and Kapampangan:** separate proverb stocks and conduct traditions.
- **Cordillera peoples (Kankanaey, Bontok, Ifugao):** *inayan* and village elders' teaching.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug:** Islamic teaching and *adat*.
- **Mangyan of Mindoro:** *ambahan* verse as a carrier of values.
- **Lumad of Mindanao:** their own elders' teaching, often in mission or school settings.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** Confucian-influenced schooling beside Catholic schools.
- **Generations and classes:** Manila's middle class, rural farmers, and the young online.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lowland "Filipino values" claims and school curricula; mixed for Moro and Cordillera peoples; thin for small Lumad and Mangyan groups beyond a few collections.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Spanish; Cebuano, Ilocano, Maranao, Tausug, Kankanaey; Hanunó'o script for the *ambahan*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Filipino values" lists are often used as stereotypes, for praise or blame; read them as claims. Vote buying touches election law (Libra); *adat* as customary law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
