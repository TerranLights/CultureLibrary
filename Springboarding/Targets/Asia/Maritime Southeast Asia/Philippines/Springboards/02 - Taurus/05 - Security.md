# Philippines: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino and English; Cebuano and Hiligaynon (typhoon belt and Mindanao); Ilocano; Ivatan; Ifugao; Kapampangan and Sambal (Pinatubo area); Maguindanaon and Maranao (conflict areas); Lan-nang. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila formal workers against informal vendors and drivers:** covered against uncovered.
- **Eastern Visayas (Waray) and Bicol:** the typhoon front line; repeated rebuilding.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** island isolation, storms, and supply by sea and air.
- **Ifugao and other Cordillera rice farmers:** granaries, terraces and kin as security.
- **Aeta of Zambales, Pampanga and Tarlac:** displacement after Pinatubo.
- **Mangyan of Mindoro:** swidden farming as risk-spreading.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Lumad of Mindanao:** insecurity from conflict and displacement.
- **Sama and Tausug fishers:** sea-based livelihoods and the off-season.
- **Overseas workers' households:** remittance-funded houses, schooling and insurance.
- **Chinese-Filipino families:** family firms and kin networks as security, and fear of kidnapping in past decades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys and disaster studies; mixed for informal insurance; thin for how Lumad and Moro households speak of security in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English; Waray, Bikol, Cebuano, Hiligaynon; Ilocano, Ivatan, Ifugao; Maranao, Maguindanaon.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed conflict, displacement and kidnapping; the anti-drug campaign and fear of police; demolitions. Labor and social-insurance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
