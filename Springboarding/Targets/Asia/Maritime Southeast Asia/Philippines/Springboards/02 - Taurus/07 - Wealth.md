## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Manila and Spanish-mestizo families:** land and corporate wealth since the 19th century.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** trading and retail fortunes, and how others talk about them.
- **Negros and Panay sugar families (Hiligaynon):** hacienda wealth against cane workers.
- **Cebu's commercial families:** a port-city elite with its own history.
- **Ilocano and Kapampangan provincial elites:** political clans and land.
- **Ifugao and Ibaloi:** feast rank, cattle and gold as upland wealth.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** gold, brass, pearls and marriage payments in Moro rank.
- **Lumad and Aeta:** wealth reckoned in other terms, often outside the money economy.
- **Overseas workers' families:** new houses and rising consumption.
- **The urban poor:** lotteries, charismatic faith and the hope of *asenso*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on elites and poverty statistics; mixed on attitudes to wealth; thin on how Lumad and Aeta reckon wealth in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English; Hiligaynon, Cebuano, Ilocano; Ibaloi, Ifugao; Maranao; classical Chinese and Lan-nang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Marcos-era wealth and its politics; anti-Chinese resentment; kidnapping of wealthy families; illegal gambling and its links to officials. Law on these goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
