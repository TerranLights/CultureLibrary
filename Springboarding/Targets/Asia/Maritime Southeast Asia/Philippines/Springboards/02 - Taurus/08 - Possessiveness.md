*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino and English (Taglish dominates dating talk); Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon (Kinaray-a for Panay's interior); Maranao and Tausug; Palawan languages; Lan-nang. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila young adults:** app dating, location sharing, new talk of "toxic" partners.
- **Provincial Tagalog and Bicol towns:** older courtship forms (*harana*, bride service) and family guarding of daughters.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon Visayans:** their own jealousy vocabulary and song traditions.
- **Panay's Sulod and Kinaray-a speakers:** epic and upland marriage customs.
- **Ilocano:** separate jealousy terms and long-distance marriages from labor migration.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** polygyny, family honor and the guarding of women.
- **Batak, Palaw'an and Tagbanwa of Palawan:** sharing duties and small-group life.
- **Fishing towns:** guarding municipal waters against outsiders.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** parental control of marriage choice.
- **Overseas workers' families:** jealousy and control across long separations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spousal-control survey items and drama; mixed for sharing and territoriality; thin for minority peoples' terms.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a, Ilocano; Maranao, Tausug; Palawan languages; Lan-nang.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against women, including killings by jealous partners; polygyny; ethnic slurs in marriage choice; fishing conflicts can turn violent. Family and anti-violence law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
