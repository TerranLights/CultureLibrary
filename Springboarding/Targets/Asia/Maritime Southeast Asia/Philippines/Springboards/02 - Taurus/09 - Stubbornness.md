## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boholanos:** the long revolt as local memory and pride.
- **Kalinga, Bontok and other Cordillera peoples:** refusal of conquest, then of dams and mines.
- **Tausug and Maranao:** long armed refusal of outside rule, read differently by each side.
- **Tagalog and Ilocano lowlanders:** everyday *matigas ang ulo* in families and schools.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon Visayans:** their own words for hardheadedness, to compare in use.
- **Lubang Islanders:** neighbors of a famous holdout.
- **Metro Manila informal settlers:** holdouts against demolition.
- **Labor unions:** long strikes as collective firmness.
- **Generations:** older and younger Filipinos on apologizing and changing one's mind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for resistance history; mixed for everyday stubbornness (mostly parenting and folklore); thin for minority languages' terms.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English; Cebuano and Boholano; Ilocano, Kalinga, Bontok; Tausug, Maranao; Spanish and Japanese archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** killings of land defenders; Moro conflict history; demolitions; strikes that ended in violence. "Hardheaded" labels have been used against whole peoples; record who applies them. Law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
