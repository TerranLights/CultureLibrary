# Philippines: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Tausug, Philippine Hokkien; Spanish for colonial parish records. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Kapampangan towns:** large godparent networks at baptisms and weddings; count, never assume.
- **Ilocano and Visayan migrant-sending provinces:** households held together by remittances and calls.
- **Cordillera peoples:** kin and village ties renewed through feasts and peace pacts.
- **Tausug, Maranao and Maguindanaon:** clan and descent ties (*tarsila*), and alliance through marriage.
- **Lumad communities:** kin networks across displaced settlements.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** clan and surname associations and business ties.
- **Mindanao settler towns** (from Luzon and the Visayas): new ties built far from home provinces.
- **Elders living alone and urban poor in Metro Manila:** where ties fail; isolation rather than connection.
- **Overseas Filipinos,** as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ritual kinship, patronage and remittances; mixed on loneliness; thin on minority peoples' ways of keeping ties.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano; Spanish and Latin for parish records; Malay and Arabic script for some *tarsila*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** political-clan research touches living families and violence; keep it documented and neutral. Remittance and migration data can expose households.
- **Held for review:** none.
