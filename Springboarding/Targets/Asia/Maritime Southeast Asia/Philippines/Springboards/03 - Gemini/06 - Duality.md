# Philippines: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog Catholic heartland:** *loob*/*labas* and Passion piety.
- **Bicol and the Visayas:** saints beside spirit mediums; ritual specialists in both worlds.
- **Chinese Filipinos and Chinese mestizo families:** two languages and two school systems; older mestizo lines that merged.
- **Muslim Filipinos** (Maguindanaon, Maranao, Tausug): Moro and Filipino identities, sometimes at odds.
- **Cordillera peoples:** Christian and Indigenous rites held together or kept apart.
- **Spanish-Filipino mestizo families** and **Chavacano speakers:** a Spanish-tinged identity against the national mainstream.
- **Amerasians** around former bases.
- ***Bakla*** and other gender-variant Filipinos, in their own words.
- **Return migrants,** as comparison only: life between two countries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on hybrid identity in literature and history; mixed on everyday two-facedness; thin on Lumad and Negrito paired worldviews.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Spanish; Bikol and Cebuano for village sources; Chinese for community writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender-crossing and Amerasian topics touch living people; follow their own terms. Treat claims of a "Filipino split personality" or "damaged culture" as contested positions, not descriptions.
- **Held for review:** none.
