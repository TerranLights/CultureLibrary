# Philippines: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Maranao, Ifugao, Philippine Hokkien; Spanish for parish and early dictionary sources. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog households,** urban and provincial: the *kuya*/*ate* system and the use of *Diko*-type titles, which varies by family.
- **Ilocanos:** *manong*/*manang* address, and the place of siblings in land and emigration.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon families:** different kin words and Visayan family sizes.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug:** whether and how brothers answer for a sister's honor (Maranao *maratabat*), to be asked, not assumed; siblings in clan politics.
- **Ifugao and other Cordillera peoples:** eldest-child customs in land and fields.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** Hokkien titles and siblings in family firms.
- **Large rural families** against **small Metro Manila families:** the change in sibling numbers.
- **Households with a member abroad:** the sibling who works for the others (overseas siblings as comparison only).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on kin terms, family size and migration; mixed on sibling feeling and conflict; thin for Lumad and Negrito kinship in recent sources.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Cebuano, Ilocano; Hokkien for Chinese Filipino terms; Spanish for early dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hazing, honor violence and family estrangement involve living people. Inheritance among siblings belongs to Libra for its letter of the law; point there.
- **Held for review:** none.
