*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila natives against provincial newcomers:** *promdi* labels and the weekend *uwian* show belonging split across two places.
- **Cebuano and other Visayan speakers:** a regional "we" defined partly against Manila and Tagalog.
- **Ilocanos:** settler-migrants across northern Luzon and Mindanao; belonging carried by language.
- **Kapampangans:** a strong *kabalen* identity in Central Luzon.
- **Cordillera peoples:** "Igorot" as an umbrella some embrace and some refuse; village identity beneath it.
- **Moro peoples (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug, Sama, Yakan, Iranun):** belonging to a Muslim nation and to separate peoples at once.
- **Lumad peoples:** Indigenous belonging to ancestral domains against settler towns.
- **Ayta and Agta:** small peoples often treated as outsiders in their own lands.
- **Chinese Filipinos and mixed (*mestizo*) families:** formal citizenship against felt acceptance.
- **Settlers in Mindanao from Luzon and the Visayas:** two or three generations in, still "from" elsewhere.
- **LGBTQ Filipinos, returning OFWs:** acceptance at home after long absence or exclusion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on national and Moro identity and on psychology; mixed on regional belonging (mostly in regional-language media); thin on small Indigenous peoples' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Kapampangan; Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug for Moro sources; Hokkien for Chinese Filipino community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Moro and Lumad identity is tied to armed conflict and land disputes; ethnic slurs and regional jokes need care; who a people counts as its own is for that people to state.
- **Held for review:** none.
