## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug families:** kin defense and family standing (*maratabat*) in a setting of feuds and armed groups.
- **Kalinga, Bontok and other Cordillera peoples:** village defense and peace-pact traditions (pact terms go to Libra).
- **Lumad communities in Bukidnon, Davao and Surigao:** defense of land against outside pressures, in their own accounts.
- **Metro Manila gated subdivisions against informal settlements:** private guards against the *tanod* and neighbors.
- **Eastern Visayas and Bicol:** households that face typhoons every year.
- **Ivatan and other small-island communities:** isolation shapes who can be called for help.
- **Rural lowland farm families:** reliance on kin, the *tanod* and the saint rather than police.
- **Women and children in OFW-split households:** who stands guard when a parent is abroad.
- **Chinese Filipino families:** kidnapping fears of past decades and their responses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on disaster protection, child protection and policing; mixed on folk protection (much in folklore studies and in person); thin and sensitive on armed kin defense in Mindanao.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clan feuds, armed groups and vigilantism are live and dangerous topics; abuse cases involve minors; some protective rites and amulet knowledge are kept by specialists. Laws on violence, guns and child protection go to Libra; ideas of authority and order to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
