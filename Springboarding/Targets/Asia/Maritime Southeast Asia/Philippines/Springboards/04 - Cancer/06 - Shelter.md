*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waray and Bicol communities of the typhoon belt:** repeated evacuation and rebuilding.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** stone houses and older refuges built for wind.
- **Sama Dilaut of Tawi-Tawi, Zamboanga and Davao:** former boat dwellers; some living on city shores and streets.
- **Maranao of Marawi and Lanao:** families in transitory sites since 2017.
- **Lumad communities of Davao, Bukidnon and Surigao:** *bakwit* evacuations from conflict areas.
- **Metro Manila informal settlers:** shanties, relocation sites outside the city, and *nakikitira* lodging with kin.
- **Cordillera towns:** landslide refuges and highland building.
- **Street families and children in Manila, Cebu and Davao:** life with little or no cover.
- **Mayon and Taal volcano communities:** evacuation as a recurring part of life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on disaster shelter (humanitarian and government reports) and informal settlement; mixed on everyday lodging with kin; thin on Lumad and Sama accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Waray, Bikol; Maranao and Sama for Mindanao displacement; Ivatan for Batanes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "squatter" and some street terms are slurs; Lumad evacuees and Sama street families face harassment; government sweeps of street dwellers are contested. Housing and land law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
