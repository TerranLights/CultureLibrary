## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Central Luzon mothers:** the media's default mother; test the "light of the home" script against lives.
- **Bikolanos:** a region whose central devotion is to the Virgin as *Ina*.
- **Panay (Kinaray-a and Hiligaynon speakers):** mother figures in old epics against lowland Catholic ones.
- **Ilocanos and Cebuanos:** many mothers working abroad; childcare handed to grandmothers.
- **Sama and Tausug of Tawi-Tawi and Sulu:** Islamic family life, polygyny where practiced, and local midwives.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** mothers in noble houses and in displaced families.
- **Cordillera peoples:** mothers' work in fields and terraces.
- **Agta and Ayta:** mothering among foragers.
- **Teen mothers in poor urban areas:** the survey's main concern.
- **Single mothers and grandmothers raising children:** motherhood without a husband or without the birth mother.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich on maternal health, overseas mothers and religious imagery; mixed on everyday mothering in minority groups; thin on mothers' own words outside Manila media.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano, Bikol; Sama and Tausug for the south; Kinaray-a for Panay epics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** teen pregnancy, births outside marriage and the abortion ban are charged topics (law goes to Libra); polygyny among some Muslim families needs care; mothers of the disappeared face danger.
- **Held for review:** none.
