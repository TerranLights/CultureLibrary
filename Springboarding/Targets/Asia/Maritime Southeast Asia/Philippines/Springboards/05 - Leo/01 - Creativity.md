## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **T'boli and other South Cotabato weavers:** dream-given designs make authorship a question.
- **Maranao of Lanao and the brassworkers of Tugaya:** *okir* as a closed grammar, a test of variation against novelty.
- **Yakan of Basilan:** geometric weaving, a separate design vocabulary from the Maranao.
- **Ifugao, Kalinga and Bontoc:** carving and weaving bound to ritual, then drawn into the tourist market.
- **Hanunuo and Buhid Mangyan:** making in verse and script on bamboo.
- **Ilocano weavers of *inabel*:** household looms facing machine-made cloth.
- **Paete, Angono and San Fernando (Pampanga):** towns that brand themselves on carving, painting and giant lanterns.
- **Metro Manila agencies, studios and startups:** creativity as a paid profession.
- **Cebu furniture, guitar and fashion makers:** export design outside the capital.
- **Chinese Filipino businesses:** creativity framed as enterprise and product.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state awards, literature and film; mixed for crafts (often in tourism material); thin for measured invention.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Kapampangan; Maranao and T'boli terms with interpreters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dreamed and ritual designs that weavers may keep to themselves; sacred carvings sold as décor; fashion use of Indigenous textiles. The rules on Indigenous designs as property go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
