## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Metro Manila:** "national" pride often voiced from the capital.
- **Cebuano speakers:** a long rivalry with Manila and the claim to be first city.
- **Ilonggo and other Western Visayans:** pride words of their own, such as *bugal*.
- **Ilocanos:** an epic tradition and their own literary guild.
- **Waray of Samar and Leyte:** pride voiced in local song and in rebuilding after Typhoon Haiyan (2013).
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug:** sultanate pride and the name *Moro* reclaimed.
- **Cordillera peoples:** pride tied to resisting colonial rule, as their own histories tell it.
- **Lumad peoples:** pride asserted against displacement and against outside labels.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** family firms and clan halls.
- **Bakla and LGBTQ communities:** pride as a movement against stigma.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national pride, colonial mentality and media wins; mixed for regional pride; thin for personal arrogance norms.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Arabic religious terms for Moro sermons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colorism; Moro and Indigenous claims tied to armed conflict and land; questions of which region is "more" Filipino.
- **Held for review:** none.
