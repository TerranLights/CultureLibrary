# Philippines: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Sama and Tausug for the Sulu seas; gay lingo; Spanish and Italian for the earliest European accounts. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sama Dilaut and other sea Sama:** undocumented and often seen only as beggars in cities.
- **Ayta, Agta, Ati and Mamanwa:** seen mostly through outsiders' festivals and photographs.
- **Lumad evacuees:** visibility sought for safety and risky at the same time.
- **Moro women in Marawi, Cotabato and Jolo:** public life on their own terms.
- **Domestic workers from the Visayas and Mindanao in Manila homes:** present every day, seldom noticed.
- **Deaf people and other persons with disability:** a visible ID set against unseen daily barriers.
- **Chinese Filipino families:** a low profile around kidnapping fears, a claim to check.
- **Bakla and trans women:** high visibility in comedy and pageants, less in other fields.
- **Rural elders and children left behind by overseas workers:** absent parents, watched-over children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media, census and life-course rites; mixed for folk beliefs; thin for minority self-accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon; Sama and Tausug with interpreters; Spanish and Italian for Pigafetta.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** enforced disappearances; surveillance and house-to-house drug-war operations; red-tagging of Lumad schools; statelessness. Civil-registration law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
