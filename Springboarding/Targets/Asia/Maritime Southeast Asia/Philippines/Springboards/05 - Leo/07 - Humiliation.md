## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tausug of Sulu:** *sipug* and the answer it demands.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** *maratabat*; an affront to one person can involve a whole clan.
- **Tagalog and Cebuano urban youth:** teasing, nicknames and online shaming.
- **Ilocano and Hiligaynon communities:** shame words of their own, to see whether the *hiya* model fits them.
- **Cordillera peoples:** the 1904 exhibit and later tourist gazes as group humiliation.
- **University and academy fraternities:** hazing as a separate institution.
- **The urban poor and arrested suspects:** shaming used by authorities.
- **Women in households:** partner abuse that includes humiliation.
- **Chinese Filipino families:** face and its loss, and anti-Chinese slurs.
