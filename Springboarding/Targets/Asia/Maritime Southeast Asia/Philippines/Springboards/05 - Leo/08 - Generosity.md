## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ifugao, Bontoc and Kalinga:** feast-giving and rank, unlike lowland parish sponsorship.
- **Tagalog lowlanders:** the communities where most *utang na loob* studies were done.
- **Cebuano, Boholano and Hiligaynon towns:** godparent cash gifts and fiesta hosting.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug:** zakat, *kanduli* feasts and giving tied to clan.
- **Lumad communities:** sharing of the hunt and harvest, as each people practices it.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** red envelopes, clan associations and school building.
- **Overseas workers' home towns:** remittances, boxes and the pressure to give.
- **Urban poor neighborhoods:** pooling, *abuloy* at wakes and community pantries.
- **Wealthy families and corporations:** foundations and named buildings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for remittances, reciprocity studies and NGOs; mixed for Moro giving; thin for Lumad sharing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano and Hiligaynon for Visayan gift terms; Ifugao terms; Maranao and Arabic for religious giving; Hokkien.
