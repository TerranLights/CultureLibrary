## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Southern Tagalog provinces:** dynasty politics near the capital.
- **Cebu:** a separate political center with its own families.
- **Ilocos:** leadership loyalties that hold across generations, a pattern to test.
- **Bangsamoro:** sultanate titles beside former rebel commanders now in government.
- **Bontoc, Sagada and other Cordillera villages:** elders' councils against elected officials.
- **Manobo, Subanen and other Lumad:** leaders made by deeds and mediation.
- **Chinese Filipino business families:** *thâu-ke* leadership in firms and associations.
- **Women barangay captains:** local leadership outside dynasties.
- **Youth in the Sangguniang Kabataan:** a school for future officials.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national and provincial politics; mixed for workplaces; thin for Lumad and Cordillera leadership in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** English and Filipino for politics; Cebuano and Ilocano for provinces; Tausug, Maranao, Bontoc and Manobo terms; Hokkien for business.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election violence; vote buying; armed groups and their leaders; killings of Lumad leaders. The right to command and the purpose of office go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
