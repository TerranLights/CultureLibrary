## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila street children:** street games, malls and *piso net* shops as play spaces.
- **Rural Visayan and Bicol children:** seasonal toys and fiesta games.
- **Ifugao farming villages:** play folded into the rice cycle.
- **Maranao of Lanao:** *sipa* and other games tied to the lake and the mosque calendar.
- **Tausug and Sama of Sulu and Tawi-Tawi:** sea play and boat races.
- **Cebu:** eskrima clubs as an adult form of play.
- **Chinese Filipino households:** mahjong as family and club play.
- **Older adults:** bingo, cards and mahjong in barangay halls.
- **Young gamers nationwide:** a play culture that crosses regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for basketball, esports and game lists; mixed for regional names and rules; thin for adult play outside gambling.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Waray, Ilocano; Maranao, Tausug and Sama for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child gamers and gaming disorder; gambling at wakes; children working instead of playing. Gaming regulation goes to Libra.
