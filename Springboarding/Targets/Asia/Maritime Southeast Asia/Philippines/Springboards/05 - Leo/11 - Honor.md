# Philippines: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano; Maranao and Maguindanaon; Ifugao, Kalinga and Bugkalot; Philippine Hokkien; Spanish for colonial and military sources. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** *maratabat*, clan rank and how an affront is handled.
- **Tausug:** a separate honor vocabulary in Sulu.
- **Bugkalot (Ilongot):** honor and grief in their own terms, tied to a headhunting past.
- **Kalinga:** pact-holders who manage affronts between villages.
- **Ifugao:** lineage honor and feast rank.
- **Lowland Catholic Tagalog and Visayan families:** daughters' *puri* and the family name.
- **Cebuano and Ilocano speakers:** words of their own (*dungog*, *dayaw*) to set beside Tagalog *dangal*.
- **Military and police academies:** written honor codes.
- **Chinese Filipino merchants:** honor as creditworthiness.
- **War veterans and families:** collective honor and its recognition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for *maratabat*, Ilongot and military material; mixed for lowland family honor; thin for honor among Lumad peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano; Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug; Kalinga and Bugkalot terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** past headhunting; clan feuds; control of women in the name of honor; the Marcos burial. Peace-pact law goes to Libra; duty and obedience to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
