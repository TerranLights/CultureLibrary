## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland Catholic Tagalog and Bikol towns:** a joy season around Christmas and Peñafrancia.
- **Cebu:** Sinulog joins devotion and spectacle.
- **Davao and its Lumad neighbors:** thanksgiving after harvest.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** gongs, weddings and the two Eids.
- **Tausug and Sama:** dance and boat display at celebrations.
- **Ilocano and Pangasinan communities:** joy words of their own to set beside Tagalog.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** the Lunar New Year as the peak of the year.
- **Young fans:** delight in screen couples and idols.
- **Evangelical and Pentecostal congregations:** loud joy in worship, unlike Catholic forms.
- **Families at Undas:** gladness and mourning on the same day.
