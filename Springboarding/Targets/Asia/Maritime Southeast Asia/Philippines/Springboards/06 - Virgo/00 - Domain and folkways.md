# Philippines: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino (Tagalog), English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Bikol, Waray, Kapampangan, Pangasinan; Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug; Cordillera, Lumad and Mangyan languages; Philippine Hokkien; Spanish for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service (work, service, daily routine, health): what good work and a well-kept life are.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog heartland and Metro Manila:** the documented core; urban office, call-center and informal work.
- **Central Luzon (Kapampangan, Pangasinan):** rice and sugar tenancy and its labor history.
- **Ilocano coast and the Cordillera (Ifugao, Kankanaey, Bontoc, Kalinga, Ibaloi):** terrace farming and its seasonal round.
- **Bikol and Eastern Visayas (Waray):** coconut, abaca and fishing work; typhoon recovery as routine.
- **Western Visayas (Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a):** the Negros sugar hacienda and seasonal cane cutters (*sacadas*).
- **Cebuano Visayas and Mindanao settler towns:** migration-built work ways.
- **Moro peoples (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug, Yakan, Sama Dilaut):** Islamic rest days, trade, sea work.
- **Lumad (Manobo, T'boli, B'laan, Subanen, Higaonon), Mangyan, Palawan peoples, Ayta and Agta, Ivatan:** swidden, foraging and island rounds.
- **Chinese Filipinos; Chavacano-speaking Zamboanga.**
- **Overseas Filipino workers:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Manila and the lowland Christian majority; mixed for the Visayas; thin and older for many Lumad, Negrito and Mangyan peoples. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the core islands.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon; Spanish for archives; Maranao and Tausug for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous chant and healing knowledge may be restricted; ask the community. The colonial charge of "indolence" is a claim to study, not a finding. Mindanao fieldwork has security limits. Labor law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
