# Philippines: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Tausug, Sama, Philippine Hokkien; Spanish for the colonial record. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila's wage and informal workers:** vendors, drivers, office and call-center staff; separate formal from informal.
- **Negros and Panay (Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a):** hacienda and *sacada* labor.
- **Ilocos and the Cordillera:** smallholder and terrace labor; separate Ilocano from Ifugao, Kankanaey and Bontoc ways.
- **Central Luzon tenant farmers (Kapampangan, Tagalog):** tenancy and land-reform history shape the meaning of farm work.
- **Bikol and Samar-Leyte:** abaca, coconut and fishing households.
- **Sulu and Tawi-Tawi (Tausug, Sama, Sama Dilaut):** sea work, trade and boat building.
- **Lanao and Maguindanao:** Maranao brass and trade; Maguindanaon farming.
- **Mangyan, Palawan peoples, Lumad of Bukidnon and Davao, Ayta:** swidden, forest-product and day-labor work.
- **Chinese Filipino family firms:** shop and wholesale work.
- **Overseas workers:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wage work and unions in Luzon; mixed for regional farm labor; thin for Lumad and Negrito work as the peoples describe it.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Hiligaynon, Cebuano, Ilocano; Spanish for colonial labor records; Tausug and Sama for the south.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child labor, hacienda violence and union killings are live and painful topics; the "lazy native" label is a colonial claim to examine, not repeat. Labor law and the labor code go to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
