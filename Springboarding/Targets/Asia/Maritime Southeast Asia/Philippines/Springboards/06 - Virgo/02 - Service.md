# Philippines: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Kapampangan, Ilocano, Tausug and Maranao (with Arabic loanwords), Spanish for colonial sources. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila middle-class households and their live-in workers:** service seen from both sides.
- **Eastern Visayas, Bikol and other source provinces:** where many household workers come from; a research question, not a premise.
- **Call-center cities (Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Bacolod, Clark):** customer service as a career.
- **Kapampangan and Tagalog towns with old *principalía* families:** inherited patterns of house service.
- **Cordillera peoples:** exchanged labor and duties to the village.
- **Tausug, Maranao and Maguindanaon:** service to a *datu* and to a mosque; separate royal lineages from commoners.
- **Lumad of Bukidnon and Davao; Mangyan; Ayta:** service to elders and the community as each people describes it.
- **Church service:** Catholic, Iglesia ni Cristo, evangelical and Aglipayan bodies.
- **Seafarers and overseas care workers:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on domestic, call-center and overseas service; mixed on volunteering; thin on Moro and Lumad ideas of service in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Waray, Hiligaynon; Spanish for friar reports; Tausug and Maranao.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse of household workers and child domestic work; trafficking into service. Household-worker law and civil-service law go to Libra (the letter) and Capricorn (the spirit). *Datu* lineages can be politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
