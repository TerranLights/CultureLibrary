# Philippines: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ilocano villages:** where the Tarong record allows a then-and-now look at children's help.
- **Metro Manila's informal settlements:** salvage, vending and *raket* as ways to be of use.
- **Cebu and Davao:** fast-growing cities with their own reuse and repair trades.
- **Batanes (Ivatan):** island scarcity and practical design.
- **South Cotabato (T'boli, B'laan) and Bukidnon (Higaonon, Talaandig):** craft and forest knowledge as usefulness.
- **Sulu and Tawi-Tawi (Tausug, Sama):** boat repair and sea skills.
- **Cordillera peoples:** elders' practical knowledge of terraces and forest.
- **Older people and disabled people across regions:** separate rural extended households from urban ones.
- **Families with members abroad:** the home side of remittance roles; overseas members as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good survey data on aging and disability; strong colonial botany; thin on how minorities talk about a person's usefulness.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Ilocano, Cebuano; Spanish for Morga and Blanco; local plant names in many languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labels such as *palamunin* and *tambay* wound; the 2018 drive touched on policing (law goes to Libra and Capricorn). Plant knowledge held by healers may be restricted; ask.
- **Held for review:** none.
