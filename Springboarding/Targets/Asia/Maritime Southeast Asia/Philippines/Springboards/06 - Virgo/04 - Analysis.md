## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila and university towns (Diliman, Los Baños, Cebu, Iloilo):** the science and survey establishment.
- **Subanen of the Zamboanga Peninsula:** the best-documented native diagnostic system.
- **Hanunóo and Buhid Mangyan of Mindoro:** folk classification and their own scripts.
- **Ilocano and Cordillera farmers:** terrace and water reckoning as field analysis.
- **Sama Dilaut, Tausug and Yakan:** sea and trade reckoning.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** madrasa and public-school learning side by side.
- **Chinese Filipino schools and shops:** bookkeeping and trade reckoning; compare without a stereotype.
- **Bikol and Eastern Visayas:** typhoon-warning use and trust.
- **Lumad of Davao and Bukidnon:** community schools and their science teaching.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for test scores and colonial statistics; mixed for ethnoscience (older studies, few peoples); thin for how reasoning is valued in daily talk outside Manila.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano; Spanish for colonial surveys; Subanen and Hanunóo terms through the ethnographies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some sea and healing knowledge is held within families; colonial census categories carry racial language, quote only as history. Lumad schools have been caught up in conflict.
- **Held for review:** none.
