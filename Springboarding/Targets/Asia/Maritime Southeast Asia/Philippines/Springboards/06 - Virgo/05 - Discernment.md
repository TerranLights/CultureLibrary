# Philippines: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Maranao, Tausug, Ifugao; Spanish for Jesuit chronicles. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Metro Manila:** media literacy, scams and online judgment.
- **Baguio and the Cordillera (Ibaloi, Kankanaey, Ifugao):** market trust and ritual reading of signs.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** *akkal*, religious counsel and *maratabat* as frames for judging others.
- **Cebuano Visayas and northern Mindanao:** investment schemes and how local trust networks spread them.
- **Ilocano and Pangasinan:** matchmaking and sponsor choice in tightly kin-linked towns.
- **Hiligaynon and Kapampangan:** regional food judgment and the authority of the home cook.
- **Siquijor and Bohol:** healers' diagnosis, and how others judge the healers.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** choosing partners and business associates across a community line.
- **Lumad and Mangyan:** elders' counsel in choices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong on disinformation and food writing; good older ethnography of markets and ritual kinship; thin on minority ways of judging truth.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Cebuano; Maranao and Tausug; Ifugao for ritual terms; Spanish for Chirino.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ritual readings may be closed to outsiders; disinformation research touches party politics; colorism is painful; investment-scam victims may not want contact.
- **Held for review:** none.
