# Philippines: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Bikol, Tausug, Maranao, Sama, T'boli; Arabic terms in Moro religious life. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila's elite schools and review centers:** grades, honors and board exams.
- **Ilocos:** loom work and exam culture; separate the two.
- **Laguna (Paete, Lumban) and Pampanga (San Fernando lanterns, Betis carving):** town crafts with their own masters.
- **Bikol:** beauty contests and church festivals.
- **Tausug, Sama and Yakan:** weaving and mat standards; Qur'an recitation.
- **Maranao:** *okir*, brass and the torogan house.
- **T'boli and Bagobo:** dream-patterned and ikat weaving as each people explains it.
- **Cordillera peoples:** carving and weaving rules within each village.
- **LGBTQ pageant circles:** a distinct field of the perfected look.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pageants, exams and honored crafts; mixed on psychology; thin on what makers say a flaw means in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Ilocano, Tausug, Maranao, T'boli; Arabic for religious terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** student suicide and anxiety need care; some weaving designs come from dreams and may be restricted to their makers. Pageant circles include marginalized people; do not exoticize.
- **Held for review:** none.
