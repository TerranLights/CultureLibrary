## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila's middle class and informal settlements:** aspiration and relocation, side by side.
- **Ilocos and other high-emigration towns:** remittance-built improvement.
- **Kalinga, Bontoc and Ifugao:** resistance to imposed development, and their own renewal of terraces and weaving.
- **Central Luzon:** land reform as promised betterment.
- **Cebu and Davao:** fast-growth cities with their own boosterism.
- **Negros and Panay:** hacienda towns and the hope of leaving them.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug:** post-war rebuilding of Marawi (after 2017) and Bangsamoro self-government.
- **Lumad of Bukidnon and Davao; Mangyan; Ayta after the Pinatubo eruption (1991):** displacement and resettlement.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** generational mobility through family firms and schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on development policy, surveys and reform writing; mixed on self-help; thin on how minorities define improvement in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Ilocano, Cebuano; Kalinga, Bontoc and Maranao for affected communities; Spanish for the reform era.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** martial-law history and the Chico River killings are politically charged; Marawi rebuilding and Mindanao land settlement are live disputes; *ningas-kugon* is a stereotype label to test, not adopt.
- **Held for review:** none.
