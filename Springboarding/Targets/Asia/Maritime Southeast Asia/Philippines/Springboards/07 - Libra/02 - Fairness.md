# Philippines: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** English and Filipino, with Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon and Waray for market and farm talk; Moro languages for Islamic shares; Hokkien for Chinese Filipino trade. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rice villages of Central Luzon and Laguna:** harvest shares and tenant-landlord splits under land reform.
- **Visayan fishing communities (Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray):** crew shares of the catch and boat-owner splits; ask by fishery.
- **Ilocano farm families:** dividing small inherited plots among many heirs.
- **Moro families (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug):** Islamic inheritance shares against Civil Code shares, often within one province.
- **Chinese Filipino family firms:** who inherits the business, and how daughters and sons are treated.
- **Agta, Aeta and Batak:** sharing rules of small foraging groups.
- **Sama-Bajau:** sea-based livelihoods with their own divisions of catch; little written.
- **Metro Manila commuters and office workers:** queues, transit lines and bill-splitting habits of the city.
- **Households receiving remittances:** what a fair share between siblings abroad and at home looks like.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, wages and elections; mixed for harvest and fishing shares (strong older studies, thinner recent ones); thin for sea peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano and Hiligaynon for Visayan fisheries; Ilocano; Moro languages and Arabic legal terms; Hokkien for family firms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election-fraud claims are partisan; relief-list complaints name local officials; gambling studies touch illegal games such as *jueteng*.
- **Held for review:** none.
