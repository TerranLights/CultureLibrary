## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila and big-city couples:** live-in unions, dating apps, dual earners; where "partner" language spreads first.
- **Central Luzon farm families:** the *kasama* bond between tenant and owner, a partnership of unequals.
- **Visayan courtship (Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray):** serenade and visiting customs with their own words.
- **Bontoc and other Cordillera peoples:** the *olog* (girls' dormitory) and trial courtship, documented by older ethnography.
- **Moro peoples:** arranged marriages that ally families; polygyny permitted under Muslim personal law.
- **Lumad peoples (T'boli, Manobo, B'laan):** bride-wealth and alliance marriages between kin groups.
- **Mangyan peoples:** courtship in verse.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** partners in trade, and the marriage choices of older and younger generations.
- **Same-sex couples:** partnerships outside the law's recognition.
- **Couples split by migration:** partnership kept across years apart; diaspora accounts as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lowland marriage and youth surveys; mixed for cooperatives and firms; thin for many Lumad and sea peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English and Taglish; Cebuano and Hiligaynon; Moro languages; Hokkien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** infidelity and live-in status carry stigma for some; mixed marriages with foreigners touch trafficking cases; same-sex unions are politically contested.
- **Held for review:** none.
