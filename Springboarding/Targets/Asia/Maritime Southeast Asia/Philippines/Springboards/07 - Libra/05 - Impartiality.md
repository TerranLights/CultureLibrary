*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provinces held by long-ruling families:** where the official and the relative are often one person; compare provinces with and without dynasties.
- **The national civil service and Metro Manila offices:** merit exams and fixers side by side.
- **Public-school teachers:** neutrality demanded of them every election, and the pressures on them.
- **Moro peoples (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug):** lineage-based titles and customary order beside elected office.
- **Cordillera villages:** small communities where every judge is someone's kin; how elders keep distance.
- **Lumad peoples:** recognition of leaders through the state, and disputes over who truly speaks for a community.
- **Chinese Filipino business networks:** *koan-hē*, chambers and clan associations.
- **Religious groups that endorse candidates as blocs:** the line between a member's loyalty and a voter's choice.
- **Barangay officials:** the smallest office, closest to kin and neighbors.
