# Philippines: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino above all (the exchange vocabulary is dense); Bikol, Cebuano and Ilocano for labor and gift customs; Cordillera languages for feasts; Maranao and Maguindanaon for Islamic giving; Hokkien for favor networks; English for law and finance data. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog provinces:** the home of the classic *utang na loob* studies; test their reach elsewhere.
- **Bikol:** exchange with spirits and saints, as studied in one community.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon areas:** exchange labor and Santo Niño devotion with their own words.
- **Ilocano towns:** labor returned in turn; remittance-built houses.
- **Bulacan:** vow dances at Obando.
- **Cordillera peoples:** feasts that build rank, and labor exchange in the terraces.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** Islamic alms and thanksgiving feasts beside customary gifts.
- **Lumad peoples:** gifts between kin groups at marriages and settlements.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** red envelopes, favor networks and clan aid.
- **Urban poor neighborhoods:** savings clubs, wakes and informal lending.
- **Households of workers abroad:** obligations carried in remittances; overseas accounts as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich, among the deepest topics in Philippine social science; mixed for Moro and Lumad exchange; strong finance data for remittances.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Bikol, Cebuano, Ilocano; Cordillera languages; Maranao and Maguindanaon; Hokkien; Spanish for early accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** vote buying is a crime; funeral gambling may be illegal; some feast objects are sacred and held by communities.
- **Held for review:** an informal "five-six" lending pointer tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
