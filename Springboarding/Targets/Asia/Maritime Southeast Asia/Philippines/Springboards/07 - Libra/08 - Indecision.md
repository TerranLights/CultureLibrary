## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog speakers:** the home of *bahala na* talk; check whether other regions use their own phrases.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon speakers:** their words for doubt and leaving it to God.
- **Ilocano families:** decisions about who goes abroad to work, made by the household.
- **Cordillera peoples:** consensus in elders' councils and omen-taking that can delay a choice.
- **Moro peoples:** guidance prayer and elders' counsel before marriages and settlements.
- **Lumad councils:** consensus as a form of decision; how long they may take.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** temple divination and family councils in business choices.
- **Politicians:** party switching and the coalitions it builds.
- **Senior high school students:** first career choices under parents' influence.
- **Families of workers abroad:** the long choice of whether to return; overseas accounts as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; *bahala na* has a large literature, party switching some counts, family decision-making scattered studies; thin for Lumad and Moro councils.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Cordillera languages; Arabic terms; Hokkien; Spanish for chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** do not frame *bahala na* as a national trait; some omen rites are restricted to practitioners; party-switching claims name living officials.
- **Held for review:** none.
