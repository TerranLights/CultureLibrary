# Philippines: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino for blame and folk-belief words; Cebuano and Hiligaynon for sorcery terms tied to particular islands; Kapampangan for Lenten penance; Hokkien and Filipino for mutual ethnic labels; Spanish for colonial records and folklore; English for security law. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila:** media capital where regional and ethnic images are made and spread.
- **Capiz and Siquijor:** places others tie to creatures and sorcery; their own accounts first.
- **Visayan migrants in Manila:** cast in media as maids and rustics.
- **Moro peoples:** targets of centuries of enemy images, and holders of their own images of settlers.
- **Cordillera peoples:** "Igorot" as an outsider's label they have contested and reclaimed.
- **Lumad and Aeta peoples:** othering by skin and lowland prejudice; red-tagging of their schools.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** labels traded both ways (*intsik*, *huan-á*).
- **Kapampangan penitents:** the inward rite as a counter-case.
- **People who use drugs and their families:** the recent "folk devil" and its consequences.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and colonial history; mixed for media images; thin for measured prejudice between groups.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Kapampangan; Hokkien; Spanish; English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accusations of sorcery have led to violence against real people; red-tagging endangers those named; regional labels must be treated as labels others apply, never as findings; avoid making "crab mentality" a national trait.
- **Held for review:** none.
