## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Central Luzon lowlands:** the debated *bahala na* and the "neighbors" as control; the most studied.
- **Ilocano:** farmer-run irrigation societies; household money managed across long labor migration.
- **Ifugao and other Cordillera terrace peoples:** control of water and fields by household and community.
- **Cebuano and Davao-area Visayans:** a city held up nationally as a model of strict order; ground to compare consent and fear.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** family control over marriage and movement, as they describe it, beside Islamic teaching on decree.
- **Tausug and Sama in Sulu:** control of sea routes and trade; outsiders' control as a grievance.
- **Urban poor in Metro Manila:** curfews, checkpoints and barangay watch.
- **Families of overseas workers:** control of remittances across distance.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** family-firm control and parents' control over children's marriages, as a question.
