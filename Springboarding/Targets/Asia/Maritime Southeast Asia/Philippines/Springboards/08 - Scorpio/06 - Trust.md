## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog lowlands:** the classic texts on obligation and familiarity were written here first.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon Visayans:** separate trust words; Visayan market and church networks.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** small, isolated islands famed for honesty stores; a case to test, not assume.
- **Buid and other Mangyan:** wide sharing that avoids personal debt.
- **Tausug and Maranao:** trust inside kin groups and clans, and toward the state.
- **Mindanao settlers and Lumad (for example Manobo, B'laan):** trust across groups after land loss and migration.
- **Market vendors and sari-sari store keepers:** credit on trust at street level.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** credit among merchants; whether colonial-era expulsions and registration drives still bear on trust in the state, as a question.
- **Urban poor and overseas workers' families:** scams, loan sharks and the reach of trusted kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey trust and institutional ratings; mixed for interpersonal credit practice; thin for Lumad and Sama trust in their own words.
