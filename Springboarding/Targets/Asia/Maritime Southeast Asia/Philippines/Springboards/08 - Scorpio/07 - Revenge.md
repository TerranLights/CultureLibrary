# Philippines: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug; Kalinga, Ilongot (Bugkalot), Teduray; Chinese; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong; and its alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maranao:** how Maranao writers and elders themselves link *maratabat* to feud and to peace-making; the epic as a source.
- **Maguindanaon:** whether feuds here turn on land and local office more than elsewhere, as a question.
- **Tausug:** the praised avenger in ballads and the colonial *juramentado* image, to be pulled apart.
- **Kalinga:** pact-holders who manage disputes between villages.
- **Ilongot (Bugkalot):** a well-known outside account of anger and taking heads; their own present-day voice needed.
- **Teduray:** a contrast case of settling wrongs by talk.
- **Davao and other Mindanao cities:** vigilante killing and public approval of it, as a question.
- **Visayan rural communities:** sorcery and divine retribution as hidden revenge.
- **Tagalog urban working class:** slum revenge in film; *resbak* among youth gangs and fraternities.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** kidnapping-for-ransom waves and the community's answers, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Mindanao feuds and for film; mixed for urban retaliation; thin for women's revenge and for revenge inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug; Filipino, Cebuano; Kalinga; Spanish for colonial military records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** active feuds (names can endanger people); extrajudicial killings; headhunting history, which peoples want told on their terms. Private vengeance against state punishment is Capricorn's; pact and settlement law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
