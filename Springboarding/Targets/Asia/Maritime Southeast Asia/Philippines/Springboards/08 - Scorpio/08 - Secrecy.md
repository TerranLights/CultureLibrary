## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog lowlands:** the confession, the *pasyon* and the Katipunan; the most written-about case.
- **Mangyan (Hanunuo, Buhid):** a script and verse form used for private messages.
- **Waray of Samar:** legends of hidden cities and enchanted beings.
- **Cebuano and Bohol Visayans:** healers' murmured prayers and the secrecy around sorcery.
- **Cordillera peoples:** ritual chants kept by specialists (for example Ifugao *mumbaki*), not for outsiders.
- **T'boli and other Lumad weavers:** design knowledge held by persons and families.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** local Islamic teaching on the hidden (*sirr*), and which family matters stay closed to outsiders, both as questions.
- **University and military fraternities:** initiation silence.
- **Families of the disappeared:** secrecy imposed by the state.
- **LGBT Filipinos:** the closet and coming out, in Manila and in provinces.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** clan associations and family business kept within the group, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial and revolutionary history and for literary study; mixed for family secrets; thin by design for restricted Indigenous knowledge.
