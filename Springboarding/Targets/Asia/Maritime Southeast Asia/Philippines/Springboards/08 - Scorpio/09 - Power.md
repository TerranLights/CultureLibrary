## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Central Luzon:** landlord and peasant histories; the uprising of 1986 in Manila.
- **Ilocano and Pangasinense:** strong family machines and long-held offices, as a question.
- **Cebuano:** a separate political center with its own bosses and newspapers.
- **Hiligaynon of Negros and Panay:** sugar estates, planter power and labor.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug:** sultanates, datu lines and clan power within the Bangsamoro.
- **Ifugao, Kalinga and other Cordillera peoples:** rank through feasts and wealth; councils of elders.
- **Lumad peoples:** datu-led councils and their dealings with plantation and mining interests.
- **Babaylan revival groups:** spiritual power claimed by women and gender-variant ritualists.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** large business groups and their tie to political power, as a question.
- **Urban poor organizers:** power through numbers and community organizing.
