## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waray of Eastern Visayas:** the storm-surge crisis of 2013 and life on a typhoon coast.
- **Kapampangan and Aeta of the Pinatubo area:** one eruption, very different losses and recoveries.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** typhoon-adapted houses and island self-reliance, as a question.
- **Maranao of Lanao:** war, displacement and rebuilding after Marawi.
- **Bicolanos of Albay:** life beside an active volcano (Mayon), with repeated evacuations.
- **Metro Manila urban poor:** floods, demolitions and lockdowns as recurrent crises.
- **Cebuano and Boholano:** earthquake and typhoon in the same year (2013).
- **Lumad of Mindanao:** displacement by armed conflict, often called a crisis by others.
- **Overseas workers' families (comparison only):** crisis abroad and its echo at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, hunger and economic crises; mixed for personal and mental-health crises; thin for Lumad and Sama accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Waray, Cebuano, Kapampangan, Bikol; Maranao; Spanish for colonial chronicles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' trauma; aid politics; Marawi's contested rebuilding. Emergency law and declarations go to Libra.
