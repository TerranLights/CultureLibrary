## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Manila's urban poor and the Quiapo devotees:** vows and endurance in a crowded city.
- **Cebu and Bohol:** faith organized around the Santo Niño and its feast.
- **Bicol (Naga):** a regional Marian devotion with its own river procession.
- **Pampanga and Bulacan:** Lenten penitents and Passion plays.
- **Iglesia ni Cristo members:** faith defined by membership and doctrine, apart from Catholic devotion.
- **Evangelical and charismatic Christians (El Shaddai, born-again churches):** personal conversion and testimony.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** Islamic faith in towns shaped by war and displacement.
- **Tausug and Sama of Sulu and Tawi-Tawi:** a maritime Islam with saints' tombs and older sea rites.
- **Ifugao and Bontoc:** ancestral rites kept beside Christian membership.
- **Aeta and Ati:** small peoples whose faith is often described by missionaries rather than by themselves.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** temple and church in the same family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic and charismatic devotion; mixed for Islam; thin for many Indigenous peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Cebuano and Bikol for devotees' talk; Spanish for colonial texts; Arabic, Maranao and Tausug for Islamic faith; Hokkien for temples.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous rites are not for outsiders; penitents' vows are personal, so approach with consent; faith-and-money controversies around some charismatic leaders need care.
- **Held for review:** none.
