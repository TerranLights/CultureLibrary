# Philippines: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bohol:** the Dagohoy memory as a local story of holding out.
- **Pampanga:** vows kept through Lenten self-punishment, as a test of resolve.
- **Negros:** clergy and sugar workers in a long labor struggle.
- **Bukidnon farmers:** land claims pressed on foot.
- **Antique and other Western Visayan provinces:** a local martyr of the 1986 election (Evelio Javier) as civic model.
- **Maranao:** *maratabat* as a separate frame from Christian conscience.
- **Cordillera communities:** collective refusal of projects on ancestral land.
- **Iglesia ni Cristo members:** unity of the body set against individual choice.
- **Evangelical converts:** a personal turning held against family religion.
- **Professional politicians and their staff:** party switching and the *balimbing* charge.
- Diaspora: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on activists, martyrs and protest; mixed on everyday conviction; thin on how Lumad and small island peoples talk about holding firm.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano for Bohol and Bukidnon; Hiligaynon for Negros; Maranao for *maratabat*; Spanish for 17th-18th-century records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed movements and red-tagging; *maratabat* is sometimes tied to clan feuds (*rido*) and must not be reduced to violence; penitents' vows are personal.
- **Held for review:** none.
