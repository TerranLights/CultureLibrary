## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ilocos and the Cagayan Valley:** a long history of labor migration, from Hawaii's plantations to today.
- **Batangas and Pampanga:** seafarers' and caregivers' towns with their own return customs.
- **Metro Manila:** the arrival point of provincial migrants and the airport departure lounge.
- **Eastern Visayas (Samar, Leyte):** typhoon displacement and outmigration.
- **Bicol (Naga):** a regional pilgrimage that draws people home each September.
- **Sama Dilaut of Tawi-Tawi and Sulu:** a sea-based life that crosses modern borders.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** hajj, and trade routes to Sabah and the Middle East.
- **Mindanao settlers from Luzon and the Visayas:** the 20th-century move south and its memory.
- **Ati and Aeta:** small peoples whose seasonal movement is often misread as homelessness.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** ancestral ties to Fujian and family travel back.
- Diaspora (Gulf, Hong Kong, North America): comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for overseas migration and remittances; mixed for pilgrimage; thin for Indigenous seasonal rounds.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Ilocano and Cebuano for migrant towns; Sama and Tausug for sea mobility; Arabic for hajj; classical Chinese and Spanish for early trade.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trafficking and undocumented status; conflict displacement in Mindanao; stereotyping of Sama as "sea gypsies".
- **Held for review:** none.
