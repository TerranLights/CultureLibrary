## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Batanes (Ivatan):** a small island society known for honor-system trade.
- **Metro Manila commuters and drivers:** daily contact with *kotong* and fixers.
- **Public schoolteachers:** they staff the polls, so election honesty runs through them.
- **Bontoc, Sagada and Kankanaey communities:** *inayan* as a restraint on wrongdoing.
- **Maranao:** trickster lore and *amana* teaching side by side.
- **Cebuano-speaking Visayas:** Juan Pusong tales and a different market culture.
- **Chinese Filipino traders:** credit on one's word in family firms.
- **Market vendors and buyers:** weights, scales and bargaining.
- **Families of would-be overseas workers:** frequent targets of recruitment fraud.
- Diaspora: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on corruption and petty bribery; mixed on everyday candor; thin on Indigenous honesty norms beyond the Cordillera and Batanes.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Cebuano and Ilocano; Ivatan and Kankanaey for local terms; Spanish for confession manuals; Arabic and Maranao for Islamic teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption talk easily turns into a national stereotype; name variation and frame indirection as a communication style, not as lying.
- **Held for review:** none.
