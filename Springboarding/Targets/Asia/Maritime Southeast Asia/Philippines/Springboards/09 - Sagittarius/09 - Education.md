## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila private-university families:** elite colleges and their networks.
- **State-university students (UP and regional campuses):** scholarship by exam and public subsidy.
- **Rural public-school families in Eastern Visayas and Bicol:** distance, cost and dropout.
- **Ilocos and Pangasinan nursing and maritime graduates:** schooling chosen for work abroad.
- **Dumaguete and Iloilo:** university towns shaped by Protestant colleges.
- **Baguio and the Cordillera:** a regional school center drawing highland students.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon:** *pangadian*, madrasah and state school together.
- **Mangyan and Lumad communities:** Indigenous-led schools under different pressures.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** trilingual schooling and its fading or revival.
- **Adults in the Alternative Learning System:** schooling resumed late.
- Diaspora (students abroad): comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on enrollment, exams and policy; mixed on Islamic schooling; thin on Indigenous learning in its own terms.
- **Languages needed:** English and Filipino; Spanish for colonial schools; Arabic and Maranao for madrasah sources; Hokkien and Mandarin for Chinese schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Lumad community schools were closed amid red-tagging; fraternity hazing deaths; avoid ranking peoples by test scores.
- **Held for review:** none.
