## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kalinga, Bontoc and Tinggian (Itneg) communities with peace pacts:** answerability shared by village or kin, set against individual blame.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon clans:** feud-settlement records on who pays for a wrong.
- **Eastern Visayas (Waray) and the Bicol volcanic belt:** recurring disasters keep the "who answers?" question open.
- **Marinduque, Benguet and Caraga mining areas:** corporate and state answerability for land and water.
- **Ilocano villages:** the Tarong record as a baseline for children's tasks, to revisit rather than generalize.
- **Families of overseas workers:** older children left to run households; responsibility handed down early.
- **Metro Manila officials and the urban press:** where national accountability fights play out.
- **Chinese Filipino business families:** succession and who answers for the firm.
- **Catholic and Muslim teaching:** confession and penance beside *amanah* and the Day of Judgment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on official accountability and disasters; mixed on household and child responsibility; thin for Lumad and Mangyan practice beyond peace-pact studies.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Ilocano, Cebuano, Waray; Maranao, Tausug and Arabic; Spanish for colonial reviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *rido* cases involve living families and ongoing violence; graft cases may still be in court; child offenders' identities are protected by law.
- **Held for review:** none.
