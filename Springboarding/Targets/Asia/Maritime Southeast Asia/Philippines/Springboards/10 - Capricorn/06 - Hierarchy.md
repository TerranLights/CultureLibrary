## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Negros and Panay sugar districts (Hiligaynon, Kinaray-a):** planter and worker ranks, with seasonal cane cutters (*sacada*).
- **Makati, Forbes Park and other gated "villages":** the national elite's spatial separation.
- **Metro Manila informal settlements:** the bottom of the urban ladder, in residents' own terms.
- **Tagalog and Bulacan towns:** the classic lowland alliance studies, to update.
- **Ifugao and other Cordillera communities:** wealth ranks shown through feasts and heirlooms.
- **Tausug of Sulu and Maranao of Lanao:** titled lineages and royal houses.
- **Sama Dilaut (Bajau):** how land-based neighbors place them is a question to research, with their own view first.
- **Aeta and Agta:** where outsiders put them and where they put themselves; sensitive.
- **Chinese Filipinos and Chinese mestizo families:** a separate commercial rank order and its history.
- **Cebu and Davao elites:** regional hierarchies not ruled from Manila.
- **Overseas workers' families:** remittances reshuffling village rank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on class, colorism and elites; mixed on rank among Muslim and Cordillera peoples; thin for Aeta, Agta and Sama views of their own standing.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Hiligaynon and Cebuano; Tausug and Sama; Ifugao; Spanish for colonial registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for the poor, for Aeta and for Sama should be described, never used; household abuse of domestic workers; heirloom objects may be sacred to their owners.
- **Held for review:** none.
