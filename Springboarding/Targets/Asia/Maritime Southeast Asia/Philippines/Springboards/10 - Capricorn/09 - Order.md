*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Marikina against Quiapo and Divisoria:** cleared sidewalks against dense market streets, inside one metropolis.
- **Baguio:** a planned hill city now crowded past its plan.
- **Kalibo, Cebu and Iloilo:** festival towns where revelry and its policing can be compared.
- **Ifugao and other terrace communities:** landscape order bound to a ritual calendar.
- **Aeta of Zambales, Tarlac and Pampanga:** life before and after resettlement.
- **Maranao of Lanao:** the *Darangen* order, and who decides how Marawi is rebuilt after the 2017 siege.
- **Sama-Bajau of Tawi-Tawi and Zamboanga:** stilt villages and boats that land zoning does not fit.
- **Waste pickers of Payatas and Tondo:** people who live from what others discard as disorder.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** *hong-súi* siting of shops, homes and graves.
- **Christian lowland towns:** the plaza, church and town hall as the old frame of order.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on planning, festivals, disasters and policing; mixed on household order; thin for Lumad, Mangyan and Sama ideas of cosmic order beyond older collections.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon; Maranao and Arabic; Ifugao; Spanish for colonial plans.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Marawi's rebuilding and displacement are live disputes; demolitions of informal homes; quarantine arrests; *Darangen* chanting belongs to its keepers.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Jocano's *Outline* for creation accounts that describe the world set in order.
2. Search the NCCA and UNESCO sites for *Darangen* documentation and its chanters.
3. Read *SPARK v. Quezon City* in the Supreme Court e-library, then search local news for "videoke" with "ordinance".
4. Find Burnham's 1905 report and Reed's *Colonial Manila*, and compare the two plans of order.
5. Search news archives for "Traslacion" with "crowd" and "marshals", year by year.
