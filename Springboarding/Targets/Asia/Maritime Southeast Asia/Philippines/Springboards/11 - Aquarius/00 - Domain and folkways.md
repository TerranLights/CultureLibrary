## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila and the Tagalog provinces:** dense urban barangays, informal settlements and gated subdivisions side by side test what neighboring means.
- **Ilocano lands and the Cagayan Valley:** long out-migration makes the home village (*ili*) and hometown associations a separate thread.
- **Cebuano-speaking Visayas and settler Mindanao:** frontier towns built by migrants show community being made, not inherited.
- **Hiligaynon Panay and Negros:** sugar-estate settlements shape who gathers with whom by class.
- **Bikol and Waray Eastern Visayas:** the typhoon belt, where disaster relief is a recurrent community event.
- **Cordillera peoples (Ifugao, Bontoc, Kankanaey, Ibaloi, Kalinga, Isneg, Tingguian):** ward councils, labor exchange and peace pacts are their own group forms.
- **Moro peoples (Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug, Sama, Yakan, Iranun):** clan, sultanate and congregation give a different "we".
- **Lumad peoples (Manobo groups, Subanen, T'boli, Blaan, Higaonon, Teduray, Mandaya), Mangyan of Mindoro, Palawan peoples, Aeta and Agta:** small-scale communities whose ties are least documented.
- **Kapampangan, Pangasinan, Ivatan and Chavacano speakers; Chinese Filipinos:** distinct language and association traditions.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** overseas workers' hometown clubs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lowland Christian groups and Manila; mixed for Moro communities; thin for many Lumad and Mangyan peoples. The 1960s values literature is contested; read it with its critics.
- **Lands:** the tracker records no change of lands; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled by the author.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Spanish; at least Cebuano, Ilocano and Hiligaynon; Jawi for Moro manuscripts; Hokkien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Indigenous chants and rites are restricted to their communities; work through the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples' consent process. Community organizers have faced red-tagging; the Marawi siege (2017) and the Bangsamoro transition need care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
