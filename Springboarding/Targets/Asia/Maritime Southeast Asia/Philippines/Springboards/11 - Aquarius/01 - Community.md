*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Informal settlements of Metro Manila and Cebu:** membership is claimed under threat of eviction.
- **Gated subdivisions and condominium towers (Makati, Taguig, Alabang, Cebu IT Park):** community sold as an amenity, with homeowners' associations.
- **Poblacion against remote sitio:** center and edge inside one municipality.
- **Fishing villages of the Visayas and Bicol coasts:** the boat crew and the shore settlement as a work community.
- **Mining and plantation towns (Benguet mines, Davao banana estates, Negros haciendas):** company and estate communities.
- **Cordillera wards and terraced villages:** membership tied to land and inherited fields.
- **Lake Lanao and Maguindanao river settlements; Tausug and Sama islands; Sama Dilaut boat-dwellers:** community on water and along clan lines.
- **Lumad ancestral-domain villages in Bukidnon, Davao and Caraga:** community bound to a mapped territory.
- **Mangyan settlements of Mindoro:** small upland communities with their own written tradition.
- **Iglesia ni Cristo, evangelical and Aglipayan congregations:** faith communities that cut across place.
- **Chinese Filipinos; Japanese-descended families of Davao:** community by origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on urban poor organizing and disasters; mixed on fiestas and parishes; thin on Mangyan and Sama Dilaut communities.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English and Spanish; Cebuano and Hiligaynon for Mindanao settler towns; Tausug and Maranao for Moro areas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolitions, displaced Lumad schools and Marawi's evacuees are live disputes; organizers have been red-tagged. The barangay's courts and the local government statute belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the PIDS site for "KALAHI-CIDSS" and "social capital"; note any barangay-level membership measures.
2. Read Phelan's chapters on resettlement, then Bankoff on community after disaster.
3. Search the *Philippine Sociological Review* archive for "sense of community" and "urban poor".
4. Search the PSA site for the 2018 National Migration Survey report and its residence tables.
5. Query the Inquirer and SunStar archives for "relocation site" plus "community" to find present-day resettled communities.
