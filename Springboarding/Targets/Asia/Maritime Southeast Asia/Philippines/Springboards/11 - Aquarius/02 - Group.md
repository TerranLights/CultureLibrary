*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino, English, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon; Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug; Kalinga and other Cordillera languages; Spanish (colonial records). **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provincial political families (Ilocos, Maguindanao, Cebu, Negros):** the kin group operating as a party.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon clans:** feuds where the whole clan carries the blame.
- **Kalinga, Bontoc and Ifugao villages:** pacts bind one village to another as units.
- **University fraternities and sororities; Philippine Military Academy classes:** oaths, hazing and lifelong brotherhood.
- **Urban youth gangs and jail gangs in Manila and Cebu:** groups with their own rules and punishments.
- **Union workers in export zones (Cavite, Laguna, Mactan) and plantations (Davao, Negros):** collective action under pressure.
- **Iglesia ni Cristo, El Shaddai and evangelical congregations:** test whether a congregation acts and votes as one.
- **Regional voting blocs ("Solid North", Visayan and Mindanao voters):** region as a political "we".
- **Tausug and Sama communities in Sulu and Tawi-Tawi:** old ranking between peoples tests "us" and "them".
- **Chinese Filipino surname associations:** groups by lineage.
- **Overseas worker groups (comparison only).**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite politics and fraternities; mixed for unions and sects; thin for Lumad and Mangyan group forms.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, English, Spanish; Maranao and Maguindanaon for feud records; Cebuano for Mindanao politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed fronts, terror listings and red-tagging; drug-war killings; hazing deaths; clan feuds with ongoing violence. Hazing and election statutes belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
