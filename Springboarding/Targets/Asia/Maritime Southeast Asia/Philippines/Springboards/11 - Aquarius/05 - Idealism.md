## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-university students (UP campuses in Diliman, Baguio, Visayas, Mindanao):** the scholar-for-the-nation ideal and the activist path.
- **Graduates of Jesuit and other Catholic schools in Manila, Cebu and Davao:** service years as idealism with a church frame.
- **Rizalista and Mount Banahaw communities (Quezon, Laguna):** utopian living within the nation.
- **Moro youth in BARMM:** one ideal, carried through both armed and peaceful forms.
- **Cordillera activists:** an ideal of land and life defended against projects.
- **Families of overseas workers:** sacrifice for kin as an ideal, weighed against one's own aims.
- **Rural poor against urban professionals:** whether idealism reads as a luxury, to be tested.
- **Catholic, evangelical and Iglesia ni Cristo members:** different pictures of the ideal person.
- **Chinese Filipino families and their schools:** Confucian ideals of conduct alongside Filipino ones.
- **Lumad communities and the volunteers who taught in their schools:** outside idealism seen from inside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revolutionary-era texts and heroes; mixed for present-day volunteering; thin for Lumad and Mangyan ideals of right living in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Spanish, English; Hiligaynon and Kinaray-a for the Panay epic; Maranao and Maguindanaon for Moro texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hero cults and contested heroes (Bonifacio against Aguinaldo); armed movements and red-tagging; some epic chanting is restricted to its performers. The law side of autonomy belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
