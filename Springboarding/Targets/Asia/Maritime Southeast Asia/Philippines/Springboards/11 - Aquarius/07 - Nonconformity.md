## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Quezon City and Cubao music and art scenes:** the capital's subcultures and their venues.
- **Baguio:** a mountain city that draws artists and independent filmmakers.
- **Bohol:** a province whose identity includes a long refusal of colonial rule.
- **Cavite:** outlaw legends told as local pride.
- **Tausug and Sama communities of Sulu and Tawi-Tawi:** gender nonconformity under Islamic norms.
- **Aglipayan, Rizalista and Iglesia ni Cristo members:** departures from the Catholic majority.
- **Converts to Islam (*balik-Islam*) in Christian families:** religious change inside a household.
- **Rural against urban bakla and tomboy lives:** where difference is tolerated and where it is policed.
- **Surf towns of Siargao and La Union:** people who choose unusual lives and livelihoods.
- **Cordillera peoples:** a refusal once labeled deviance by outsiders, told by the peoples themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in film, fiction and history; mixed on youth scenes; thin in surveys that measure tolerance for difference.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and online slang; Cebuano (Bohol); Tausug; Spanish for colonial records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** LGBT and mental-health respondents; converts; student activists who were red-tagged; outlaw lore that touches living families. Statutes on mental health and discrimination belong to Libra and Capricorn.
