# Philippines: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino/Tagalog and English; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Bugkalot (Ilongot) and Hanunoo Mangyan for two well-documented smaller traditions; Filipino Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog-speaking Manila and southern Luzon:** where most Filipino psychology was written; check whether its terms travel.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon speakers:** different emotion words may carve feeling differently.
- **Ilocano north:** a third major vocabulary of feeling to compare.
- **Bugkalot (Ilongot), Nueva Vizcaya and Quirino:** an emotion vocabulary already described in ethnography.
- **Mangyan peoples of Mindoro:** verse as the vehicle for reading another's mind.
- **Muslim and Christian neighbors in Zamboanga, Cotabato and Lanao:** empathy across group lines with a history of conflict.
- **Deaf Filipinos:** a sign-language community with its own norms of attention.
- **Call-center workers in Metro Manila and Cebu:** the professional script against the home register.
- **Men and women:** whether men's open feeling is mocked or praised, by region and class.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Filipino psychology; mixed for scales and measures; thin for most minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Spanish for early dictionaries; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Filipino Sign Language for Deaf sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mangyan verse belongs to living communities; conflict-affected Mindanao.
- **Held for review:** none.
