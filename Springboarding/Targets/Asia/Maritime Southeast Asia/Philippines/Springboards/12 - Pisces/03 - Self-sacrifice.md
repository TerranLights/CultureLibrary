## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kapampangan and Tagalog Holy Week towns:** bodily penance is concentrated in some places, not national.
- **Bikol (Naga):** a separate devotional complex around the Peñafrancia feast.
- **Ilocano and Visayan sending provinces:** regions that send many overseas workers may speak of the provider's sacrifice differently.
- **Tausug and other Bangsamoro peoples:** martyrdom and the fight for kin carry their own religious and historical frames.
- **Cordillera peoples:** service to the community, as each people describes it, outside the Passion frame.
- **Eldest daughters and sons; mothers and fathers:** who is expected to give up schooling, marriage or health.
- **Urban professionals and rural families:** whether "self-care" talk reaches both.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** family-business sacrifice and filial duty in a separate idiom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Pasyon studies, overseas work and national martyrs; mixed for vows and penance; thin and sensitive for Bangsamoro martyrdom.
