## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila's Quiapo devotees:** the dense urban crowd as a site of self-loss.
- **Aklanon and Cebuano festival towns:** ecstatic festival forms built on drum and dance, and the Ati whose name they carry.
- **Charismatic Catholics and Pentecostal churches:** worship styles that seek the self's surrender, against restrained parishes.
- **Ifugao, Bontoc and other Cordillera peoples:** rituals and burials of their own, as each people describes them.
- **Pala'wan and other southern Palawan peoples:** trance chant in a small-scale society.
- **Bugkalot (Ilongot):** grief and rage described in a classic ethnography.
- **Kapampangan towns and the Aeta of Zambales, Pampanga and Tarlac:** one disaster, two kinds of loss.
- **Muslim Filipinos:** divorce exists in their personal law, so the dissolution of marriage reads differently; also any Sufi practice.
- **Chinese Filipinos and speakers of endangered languages:** assimilation and language loss across generations.
