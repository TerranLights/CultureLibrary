## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila gated villages and informal settlements:** two kinds of lines in the same city.
- **Small lowland towns:** the church-plaza plan of Spanish towns and the barrio beyond it.
- **Cordillera peoples (Bontoc, Kankanaey, Ifugao):** ward systems and sacred precincts with their own rules.
- **Sama, Tausug and other Sulu peoples:** borders that cross the sea and kin.
- **Maranao and Maguindanaon households in mixed towns:** food and purity lines beside Christian neighbors.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** community and marriage lines that are debated inside the community.
- **Lumad peoples with ancestral-domain maps (for example Manobo, Higaonon):** territory drawn by the people themselves.
- **Young and old:** privacy online and at home, and how far parents may enter a grown child's life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban space and Filipino social psychology; mixed for spirit boundaries; thin for sea peoples and for many Lumad groups.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Spanish for colonial sources; Cebuano, Ilocano; Sama and Tausug; Cordillera languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse inside families; Indigenous territory disputes and land conflict; sacred spaces closed to visitors; maritime border tensions.
