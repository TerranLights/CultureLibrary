## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila's urban poor and middle class:** escape priced by class, from street drinking to mall and resort.
- **Cebuano and Hiligaynon speakers:** palm-wine drinking and Visayan entertainment markets of their own.
- **Ilocano north:** a different drink (*basi*) and a reputation for thrift to test, not assume.
- **Muslim Filipinos:** religious bars on drink and gambling shape which escapes are open.
- **Cordillera and Lumad youth:** escape into city life and media, as communities describe their young.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** separate leisure circuits (clan halls, mahjong, travel), to be checked.
- **Young women and young men:** romance reading against gaming and drinking circles.
- **Families of overseas workers:** escape abroad as hope, and the idea of hiding (*TNT*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media, gambling and drug surveys; mixed for drinking customs; thin for minority languages and rural leisure.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino, Taglish and English; Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon; Spanish for older romances.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug-use research after the anti-drug campaign of 2016 onward; street children; undocumented workers abroad (diaspora as comparison only).
- **Held for review:** none.
