# Philippines: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino/Tagalog and English; Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Pangasinan; Maranao (with Arabic and Arabic-script religious terms); Ifugao and Bagobo through ethnographies; Philippine Hokkien. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog and Pangasinan lowlands:** Marian and pilgrimage practice in its densest form.
- **Cebu and the Cebuano Visayas:** the Santo Niño as the center of devotion.
- **Quezon's Banahaw communities:** independent groups with their own sacred geography.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon, Tausug and Yakan Muslims:** prayer, mystical writing and saints' tombs in an Islamic frame.
- **Cordillera peoples:** rites kept, revived or set aside, as each community decides.
- **Bagobo, Manobo, T'boli and other Lumad peoples:** sacred lands and rites under pressure from mining and plantations.
- **Spiritists and charismatic Catholics:** private revelation and spirit gifts, against parish routine.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** temple, home altar and church in one family.
- **Young urban professionals:** whether "spiritual but not religious" talk has taken hold, to be measured.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic devotion and pilgrimages; mixed for spiritists and Chinese-Filipino practice; thin for many Lumad peoples; Islamic manuscript work is specialized.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Spanish for colonial sources; Cebuano, Pangasinan, Ilocano; Maranao and Arabic script; Hokkien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed Indigenous ceremonies (respect, do not research); Marawi after the 2017 siege; contested sects and their leaders.
- **Held for review:** none.
