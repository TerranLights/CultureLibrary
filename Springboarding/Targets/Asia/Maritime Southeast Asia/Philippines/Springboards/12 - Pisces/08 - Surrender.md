# Philippines: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Philippines, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Filipino/Tagalog and English; Cebuano, Hiligaynon; Bikol (Albay); Maguindanaon and Maranao with Arabic religious terms; Teduray through ethnography; Spanish and Italian for the earliest sources. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tagalog speakers:** the home of the *bahala na* debate, which may not travel.
- **Cebuano speakers of Cebu and Mactan:** the region where submission and refusal are first recorded, and remembered both ways.
- **Ifugao and Lubang communities:** two local memories of foreign soldiers giving up.
- **Maguindanaon and Maranao:** surrender of arms in a peace process, and surrender to God in daily faith.
- **Albay's Bikol-speaking farmers:** letting go of land before a volcano.
- **Teduray and other Lumad peoples:** yielding inside a community, apart from war.
- **Former rebels and their families:** surrender as relief, strategy or shame.
- **Families of the dying:** urban and rural choices at the end of life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war history and *bahala na* scholarship; mixed for end-of-life care; thin for Lumad sources.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Bikol; Spanish and Italian for Pigafetta; Maranao and Maguindanaon with Arabic terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rebel-returnee events are political and may be staged; Bangsamoro conflict; end-of-life decisions; adoption records are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
