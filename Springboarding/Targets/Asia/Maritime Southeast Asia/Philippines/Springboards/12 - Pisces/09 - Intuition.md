## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Visayan peoples:** the early sources on omens come mostly from here; compare today's signs with them.
- **Tagalog and other lowland Christians:** dream-telling and number lore in towns and cities.
- **T'boli and other Lake Sebu peoples:** knowledge said to come in dreams, kept by artisans.
- **Ivatan of Batanes:** sea and weather knowledge in a harsh northern climate.
- **Farmers in typhoon-prone regions (Bikol, Eastern Visayas, the Cagayan Valley):** folk signs beside official warnings.
- **Maranao and Tausug:** inspiration and signs inside an Islamic frame.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** geomancy and almanac practice as a trade and a family habit.
- **Healers and their clients:** the healer's felt diagnosis, against the clinic's tests.
- **Women and men:** whether "women's intuition" is a local idea at all, to be checked.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and early sources; mixed for healers and media; thin for measured thinking style and for Islamic divination.
