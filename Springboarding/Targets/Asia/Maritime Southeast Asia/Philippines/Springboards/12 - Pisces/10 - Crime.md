## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Metro Manila (Tondo, Quezon City and others):** the setting of most crime news and outlaw films, not the norm for all.
- **Cavite and other Tagalog provinces:** outlaw legends tied to amulets.
- **Maranao, Maguindanaon and Tausug:** feud, honor and retaliation framed differently from state ideas of crime.
- **Cebuano speakers:** Cebu's own press, prisons and gangs.
- **Families of drug-war dead, mostly urban poor:** who was labeled a wrongdoer, in their own words.
- **Chinese Filipinos:** victims of kidnapping waves, and their community response.
- **Women and children:** domestic and sexual violence, once silenced.
- **Cordillera and Lumad peoples:** their own ways of naming and repairing harm, as they share them; the legal side goes to Capricorn.
- **Business and political elites:** wrongs of the powerful, and their impunity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime news, victim surveys and drug-war documentation; mixed for Mindanao feuds; thin for Lumad and Cordillera views of wrongdoing.
- **Languages needed:** Filipino and English; Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Ilocano; Maranao and Tausug; Spanish for colonial records; Hokkien.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug-war killings and families' safety; avoid stereotyping Tausug or Maranao as violent; clan feuds are live disputes; victims' privacy.
- **Held for review:** none.
