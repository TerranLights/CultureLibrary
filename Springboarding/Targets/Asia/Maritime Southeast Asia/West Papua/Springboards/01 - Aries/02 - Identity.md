# West Papua: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for the 1950s and early 1960s; the self-names of the peoples (Mee, Amungme, Kamoro, Lani, Muyu, Imyan, Ma'ya and others). **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muyu along the southern border:** check how a border people names itself when kin live on both sides.
- **Muslim Papuans of Fakfak, Kaimana and Raja Ampat:** check how faith and Tidore descent enter the answer to "who are you?", against Christian majorities elsewhere.
- **Imyan, Moi and Arfak peoples of the Bird's Head:** compare land-and-clan self-naming where national politics is quieter in the sources.
- **Biak and Serui (Yapen) coast people:** check the Dutch-era educated elite, often named as the early source of a pan-Papuan label.
- **Highlanders living in Jayapura:** check how home-valley and pan-Papuan identities are layered once people move.
- **Asmat and Marind of the south coast:** compare carved and ritual emblems used in festivals with those used at home.
- **Children of mixed Papuan and settler families:** check which labels they are offered and which they take.
- **Migrant settler communities:** present-day neighbors whose own labels shape the Papuan one; not the core.
- **Diaspora in Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu, the Netherlands and Australia:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political identity since the 1950s; mixed for people-level self-names; thin for the Bird's Head and many lowland groups.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for the New Guinea Council era; Papuan Malay for street labels like *amber*; each people's tongue for self-names that differ from outsiders' names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** flag-raising and identity talk have led to treason charges; anonymize interview sources; ancestry claims tied to land disputes.
- **Held for review:** none; no identity pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
