# West Papua: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and standard Indonesian; Dutch for mission-era church records; Hatam (Arfak), Marind, Mee and Lani for local decision terms. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for a person and for a community.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Marind and other Merauke peoples:** check consent processes for land release, where plantation pressure is highest.
- **Lani of the western Highlands against Baliem Hubula:** compare village self-government accounts from Ploeg and Heider.
- **Mee of Paniai and Deiyai:** check church-led self-rule, a separate path from village or state autonomy.
- **Hatam, Meyah and Sougb of the Arfak Mountains:** check how a conservation ethic frames who controls land.
- **Ma'ya and Biak villages in Raja Ampat:** compare *sasi* decisions in Muslim and Christian villages.
- **Women in highland and coastal households:** check who decides on marriage, bride wealth and birth spacing.
- **Young people in Jayapura and Sorong boarding houses:** check how far town life loosens kin control.
- **Transmigration villages near Merauke, Nabire and Sorong:** a present-day contrast in settler household decision-making; not the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; the IDHS gives numbers at province level only; personal autonomy is thin in ethnography; collective autonomy is rich but politicized.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for IDHS and NGO case files; Marind and Hatam for consent and guarding terms that do not translate as "permission".
- **Sensitive or restricted:** self-determination talk carries legal risk; land disputes are live; testimonies of violence need care; statutes go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; no pointer here was dropped for a name.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the IDHS final report and pull the Papua rows of the decision-making tables.
2. Read Ploeg's chapters on leadership and decision, then Giay on Pakage, noting who decides what.
3. Search Pusaka's site and *Jubi* for "Marind" plus "persetujuan" (consent).
4. Search "sasi Raja Ampat" in Garuda for village-level studies.
5. Request GKI founding records via its synod office in Jayapura or Het Utrechts Archief.
