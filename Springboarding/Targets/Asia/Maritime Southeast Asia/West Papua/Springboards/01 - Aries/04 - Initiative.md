# West Papua: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay (market and village talk), Indonesian (programs, statistics), Dutch and French for older craft and mission studies; Biak, Asmat and Mee terms for trade and craft. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Una, Yali and Hubula axe-making and coffee-growing areas:** compare an older and a newer form of starting a trade in the Central Highlands.
- **Asmat carvers and Kamoro carvers:** check how commissions and markets reach each group, given different outside buyers.
- **Wondama and Biak mission villages:** check who founded schools and congregations, Papuan or outsider, in Cenderawasih Bay.
- **Raja Ampat island villages:** check family-run tourism ventures against outside resort owners.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana coastal Muslim communities:** compare trade led by local rulers with village trade elsewhere.
- **Sentani lake villages:** check craft revival as a form of starting anew.
- **Indigenous women sellers against settler kiosk owners:** check access to credit and stalls, a present-day split; settlers are not the core.
- **Moi landholders near Sorong:** check whether any enterprise sources exist at all for the Bird's Head.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; craft and trade studies are old but solid; present-day business data rarely separate Indigenous from settler owners.
- **Languages needed:** French for the Pétrequin axe study; Indonesian for village-fund and cooperative records; Biak and Asmat for trade and carving words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates over whether Papuans are "not enterprising" are loaded; record variation, never one verdict; carved objects may have ritual restrictions.
- **Held for review:** none here; no initiative pointer fell under the standing exclusions.
