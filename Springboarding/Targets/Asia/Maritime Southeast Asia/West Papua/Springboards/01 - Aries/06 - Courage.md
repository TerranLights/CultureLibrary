# West Papua: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay for hero lists, church and press sources; Dutch and German for patrol reports and Highlands studies; Dani, Biak and Eipo terms for war and ordeal. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula of the Baliem against Eipo and Yali of the eastern Highlands:** check whether bravery words and ordeals differ where outsiders arrived later.
- **Asmat against Marind:** compare what the sources on each people say about raids and naming, and who wrote them, reading both critically.
- **Biak and Numfor:** check the *mambri* ideal against wartime and church-era courage in Cenderawasih Bay.
- **Amungme women near Timika:** check women's stands against outside power, rarely recorded as courage.
- **Fakfak's Muslim ruling families:** compare a Bird's Head coastal hero tradition with highland ones.
- **Mee church leaders of Paniai:** check moral courage through dialogue against fighting traditions.
- **Sentani and Jayapura veterans of 1944:** check whose service is remembered locally.
- **Sea people of Raja Ampat:** check tales of storms and voyaging danger, a non-war courage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich but lopsided; Highlands war and Asmat raiding are over-documented by outsiders; women's and moral courage are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for patrol reports; German for the Mek project; Dani for *etai* songs; Biak for *mambri* tales and wartime testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting and warfare imagery feeds stereotypes; the dead are named in songs; rights workers' safety; armed-group material is legally risky.
- **Held for review:** no courage pointer held back.
