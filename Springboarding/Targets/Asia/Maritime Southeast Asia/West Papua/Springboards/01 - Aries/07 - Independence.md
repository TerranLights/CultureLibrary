# West Papua: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay for household and garden talk; Indonesian for aid, food-program and development records; English and Dutch for the political history; Dani, Kamoro, Marind and Moi for staple foods and land. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others: self-reliance and self-sufficiency, for a household and for a people.

English keywords: food sovereignty West Papua; sago livelihoods; subsidized rice Papua; Papuan self-reliance development; West Papua independence movement history; customary forest mapping Sorong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula and Lani gardeners:** check how sweet-potato and pig husbandry is weighed against bought rice in the Central Highlands.
- **Kamoro, Asmat and Marind sago peoples:** compare three lowland and south-coast groups under very different outside pressures (mine, mission, plantation).
- **Moi of the Bird's Head:** check forest self-provisioning and how land mapping is used to defend it.
- **Ma'ya and Biak fishers of Raja Ampat:** check the mix of fishing, tourism and cash.
- **Sentani lake villages:** compare self-provisioning next to the capital with remote villages.
- **Biak and Cenderawasih Bay:** check how the Dutch-era educated class framed self-running government.
- **Muyu along the southern border:** check camp life as a test of self-support.
- **Settler farming villages:** present-day neighbors with rice and market crops, for contrast with Indigenous self-provisioning.
- **Exile communities in Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu and Britain:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; food and nutrition studies are fairly plentiful; self-reliance as a personal ideal is thin; political-independence sources are many but polarized.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for food-program evaluations; Dutch for *Papoeanisering*; Kamoro and Dani staple terms, since outside surveys label all foods in Indonesian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *merdeka* talk can bring arrest; malnutrition coverage can shame communities; keep political independence apart from personal self-reliance in the research.
- **Held for review:** none; self-reliance pointers raised no excluded names.
