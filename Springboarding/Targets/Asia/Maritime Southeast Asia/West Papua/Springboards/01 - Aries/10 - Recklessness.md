# West Papua: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay for street and youth talk; Indonesian for health surveys, police and disaster data; Dutch and German for early expedition accounts; local languages for cautionary tales. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men in Wamena and Highlands towns:** check motorbike, drink and gambling risk against village norms; do not assume one pattern.
- **Mee of Paniai and incoming diggers:** compare risk-taking on the same gold fields by locals and newcomers.
- **Kamoro and Amungme near Timika:** check panning risk and how each group talks of it.
- **Korowai and neighbors in the southern lowlands:** separate their own accounts of danger from tourist media.
- **Biak and Raja Ampat seafarers:** check sea-risk talk and how drownings are explained.
- **Marind of Merauke:** check how they name corporate fire and clearing, recklessness done to them.
- **Street children in Jayapura and Sentani:** check who they are, where they come from, and how they describe risk.
- **Bird's Head towns (Sorong, Manokwari):** check whether road and drink-risk data are split from other regions.
- **Settler miners and traders:** part of the present-day risk picture on gold fields and in towns; not the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; health surveys and incident reports exist; personal accounts of thrill or daring are thin; outsider spectacle (Korowai, Carstensz) is plentiful and misleading.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for STBP and police data; Papuan Malay for youth terms like *anak aibon* and *nekat*; German or Dutch for Harrer and Lorentz originals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children and drug use; HIV status; gold-field violence; avoid the stereotype of reckless highlanders by reporting variation and outside causes.
- **Held for review:** none; no excluded material touched recklessness.
