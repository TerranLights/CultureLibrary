# West Papua: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for church, school and heritage writing; Papuan Malay for sermons, sayings and taboo talk; Dutch for mission archives; Dani, Marind, Yaqai and Biak for sacred and forbidden things. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **GKI congregations of the north coast and Bird's Head:** check how church and adat are ranked in sermons where the church is oldest.
- **Highland churches (GIDI, Kingmi, Baptist) among the Lani, Yali and Dani:** compare rules on drink, dress and feasts set by newer churches.
- **Catholics of the south (Merauke, Asmat, Mappi) and the Mee area:** check where inculturation reshaped feast and carving values.
- **Yaqai, Marind and Muyu of the southern lowlands:** check older sacred orders against mission change.
- **Muslim and Christian Papuans in Fakfak, Kaimana and the Raja Ampat islands:** check how mixed families rank faith, kin and custom.
- **Amungme and Nduga of the mountains:** check which places are held unsellable, in their own statements.
- **Young Papuans in towns and universities:** check generational change in what is ranked first.
- **Migrant settler communities:** present-day neighbors whose values meet Papuan ones in markets and schools, not the core.
- **Papuan congregations abroad (Netherlands):** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** church and heritage writing is plentiful; surveys that sample Papuans by people are almost absent, so values must be read from texts, rites and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for sermons and curricula; each people's language for words of the sacred that translations had to choose between.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sacred knowledge (*dema*, *wusa*, men's-house teachings) may be closed; interfaith clashes are recent and raw; values talk is often tied to independence politics. Rules of law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing in this values sheet was set aside.
