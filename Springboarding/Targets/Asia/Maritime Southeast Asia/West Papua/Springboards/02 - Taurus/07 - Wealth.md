# West Papua: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for poverty, inequality and company data; Papuan Malay for talk of the rich, prices and envy; Dutch and English for early movements of hoped-for abundance; Biak, Mee and Maybrat for feasts and valuables. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Biak and Numfor islanders:** check how wealth from abroad and the hope of Koreri plenty appear in family stories.
- **Maybrat and other Bird's Head peoples:** check whether old cloth still outranks money in bride wealth.
- **Mee of Paniai and Dani, Lani and Yali of the Highlands:** compare pig feasts as wealth shown and given away, valley by valley.
- **Amungme and Kamoro of Timika:** check mine-fund money against older ways of being rich.
- **Ma'ya and other Raja Ampat islanders:** check reef and tourism income as a new kind of wealth.
- **Moi and other Sorong peoples:** check memories of the oil company and timber money.
- **Asmat and Marind of the south:** check what counts as wealth where sago, carving and plantations meet.
- **Papuan civil servants and district elites:** check new wealth from office separately from village wealth.
- **Migrant settler traders:** present-day holders of much town commerce; separate units, not the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good provincial poverty data and a few classic studies of valuables, thin on how most peoples rank wealth today.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for company and movement records; Indonesian for audits and poverty data; each people's terms for valuables and feasts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption claims about named officials; movements of abundance are often dismissed by outsiders, so labels need care; mine-fund records are contested.
- **Held for review:** none; the wealth pointers set nothing aside.
