# West Papua: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay for taste, food and dance talk; Indonesian for health surveys and festival records; German, Dutch and English for early records of feasts, music and ornament; Marind, Lani, Asmat and Hatam terms for drink, greeting and dance. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Marind and other Merauke lowland peoples:** check evening drink, feast and ornament, apart from the better-studied Asmat.
- **Asmat and Kamoro of the south coast:** compare feast foods and dance on two coasts with different mission histories.
- **Biak, Serui and the bay coasts:** check the origins of shared dances and string bands.
- **Dani, Lani and Yali of the Highlands:** check warmth, fire, pork and greeting touch in cold valleys.
- **Hatam, Sougb and Meyah of the Arfak Mountains:** check the stamping dance and mountain foods.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana coasts:** check scent and spice trades, among Muslim and Christian Papuans.
- **Ma'ya and other Raja Ampat islanders:** check how sea pleasures sit beside tourism.
- **Town youth in Jayapura and Sorong:** check new music, cafés and online food.
- **Migrant settler food stalls in the towns:** part of today's tastes, not the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good on dance and festival media, fair on betel, tobacco and drink; thin on touch, smell and comfort as people describe them.
- **Languages needed:** German and Dutch for early feast and music records; Papuan Malay for taste talk; each people's words for dances and greetings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** older works dwell on undress and rites now held private; some dances and songs belong to clans; sexual pleasure itself is left to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing about the senses was set aside.
