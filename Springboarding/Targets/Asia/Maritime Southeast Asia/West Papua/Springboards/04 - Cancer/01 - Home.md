# West Papua: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for older descriptions; Dani/Hubula, Lani, Mee, Biak, Sentani, Moi, Asmat and other local languages. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Papuan Malay | *kitong pu kampung*, *pulang kampung* | our home village, going back home | *pu* is the Papuan Malay possessive |
| Papuan Malay | *tanah adalah mama* | the land is mother | a phrase from land speeches; search it as a phrase |
| Indonesian | *kampung halaman*, *tempat tinggal* | home place, place of residence | in Papua *kampung* is also the official word for a village |
| Indonesian | *pengungsi*, *mengungsi* | displaced person, to flee | home loss through conflict and disaster |
| Indonesian | *rumah layak huni*, *backlog perumahan* | "livable" house, housing shortfall | program and planning words |
| Mee | *owa* | house | *check* |
| Sentani | *yo* | village, the lake settlement | *check* |
| Moi | *Malamoi* | the Moi homeland | *check* |
| Dani/Lani | *honai* (Papuan Malay use) | round house | outsiders use it for any highland house; each valley has its own word |

English keywords: home and homeland West Papua, displacement Papua, return to the village, stilt villages Jayapura.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Moi of Malamoi, Maybrat, Sougb, Hatam):** check how homeland and house are named where oil palm and displacement press on land.
- **Cenderawasih Bay and Biak:** compare island villages with Biak families living in Jayapura, Manokwari and Sorong; what "back home" means between them.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Nduga, Moni, Ngalum):** check how families who fled conflict describe home, valley by valley.
- **South coast and Asmat, Kamoro and Korowai:** compare old river camps and government villages; which of them people call home.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Yei, Muyu):** check where Muyu returnees from Papua New Guinea camps settled and what they call home now.
- **Jayapura and Sentani (Sentani, Tobati-Enggros, Nafri, Skouw):** separate lake and bay stilt villages from new town neighborhoods.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Kawe, Biak-descended villages):** check island and sea homes, and what tourism homestays change.
- **Migrant settlers and transmigration villages:** settlers' own sense of home is part of the present picture, set apart from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for displacement reporting and program data, thin for how people themselves talk about home; most peoples' home words are unrecorded.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for the 1893 survey and mission notes; the local language for each people's words for house and land.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displacement reporting involves security operations; protect the names of the displaced. Origin places can be sacred sites with restricted stories.
- **Held for review:** none; the home sources here (oral histories, press, programs, a novel) fall under no standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. Search "pengungsi Maybrat" and "pengungsi Kiwirok" and log which reports quote the displaced on going home.
2. Find the de Clercq and Schmeltz survey on Delpher or in a library and list the villages it describes.
3. Search "asal usul suku Dani Seinma" and "asal usul suku Sentani" for written versions of origin stories.
4. Pull PUPR housing-backlog figures for each Papuan province and note how a house is defined.
5. Search "Kampung Enggros reklamasi" for reporting on the bay villages.
