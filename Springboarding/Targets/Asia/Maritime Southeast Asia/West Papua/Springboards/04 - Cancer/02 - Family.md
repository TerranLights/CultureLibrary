# West Papua: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch and English for older ethnography; Dani/Hubula, Biak, Sentani, Marind, Korowai, Wambon and other local languages. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit, Hatam):** check how cloth debts and marriage payments define who belongs to whose family.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Waropen and Wandamen:** compare *keret* lines on the islands with clan names carried into mission towns.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, western Dani, Lani, Yali, Mee, Moni):** check moieties against clan-only systems, valley by valley.
- **South coast and Asmat, Kamoro, Korowai, Kombai:** check in-law avoidance, men's-house kin and how the dead are kept in the family.
- **Merauke lowlands and Boven Digoel (Marind, Kanum, Wambon, Muyu):** set the Marind clan-phratry record beside Awyu-Dumut peoples with almost none.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** check ranked clan-village families against mixed town households.
- **Raja Ampat and Fakfak coast (Ma'ya, Mbaham-Matta, Iha):** check ruling families tied to Tidore, with Muslim and Christian lines side by side.
- **Migrant settlers:** their family forms and associations shape town life today; keep them distinct from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; the moiety and marriage record is rich for a few highland groups, but whole regions (the Awyu-Dumut south, the Bird's Head interior, Raja Ampat) have little family study.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for *Bijdragen* and mission files; English for the American ethnographies; local languages for kin terms, which the outside record often garbles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogies can back land claims, so families may guard them; ancestor remains in museums are a repatriation issue. Marriage and adoption law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the kin sources listed are all outside the categories the standing rules set aside.
