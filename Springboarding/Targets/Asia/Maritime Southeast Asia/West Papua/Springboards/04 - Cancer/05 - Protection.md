# West Papua: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for museum records; Asmat, Dani/Hubula, Tehit, Marind, Kanum, Tobati-Enggros, Ma'ya and other local languages. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Tehit of Knasaimos, Moi, Maybrat):** check how forest guarding is organized where logging and plantations come close.
- **Cenderawasih Bay and Biak:** check sea-and-island protection customs, and whether churches served as refuges in past crackdowns.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Nduga, Moni, Damal):** compare the old guarding of gardens with how families protect themselves amid present conflict.
- **South coast and Asmat, Kamoro:** check shields, ancestor poles and today's health programs as different kinds of protection.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Yei):** check how park, plantation and customary claims each say they protect the land.
- **Jayapura and Sentani (Tobati-Enggros, Sentani):** check women's spaces and lake villages against city growth.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Misool villages):** check village reef guarding beside tourism and outside fleets.
- **Migrant settlers:** their own fears and protection needs belong to the present picture, studied apart from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on conflict and rights reporting, thin on everyday protective customs and on how families guard children and elders.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch and English for museum catalogs; local languages for charms, spells and the names of guardians.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conflict protection is dangerous ground; never identify sources; treat statements from armed actors as claims. Spells and charms may be private knowledge. Self-defense and policing law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no guarding or shielding pointer here falls under the standing rules.
