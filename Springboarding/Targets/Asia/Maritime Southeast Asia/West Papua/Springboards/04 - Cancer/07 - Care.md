# West Papua: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for the colonial health service; Mee, Moni, Biak, Moi, Hatam and other local languages for healing words. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Hatam, Maybrat, Sougb):** check plant knowledge and who in the family is the healer.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Serui:** compare long mission-hospital history with healing by *mon* and kin.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Mee, Moni, Hubula, Lani, Yali):** check how far families travel for care, and who goes with the sick.
- **South coast (Asmat, Kamoro, Korowai):** check how sago-camp mobility shapes care of the sick and elders.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check town clinics and village care side by side.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** check hospitals as places where interior families gather to care for a patient.
- **Raja Ampat and Fakfak (Ma'ya, Mbaham-Matta):** check care on scattered islands with boat-borne health services.
- **Migrant settlers:** check how much of the paid health workforce in towns comes from outside; their role is part of the present picture, separate from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; health-system reports are plentiful, but who cares for whom inside families, and with what words, is rarely studied.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for the 1950s health service; local languages for healers' terms and plant names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing knowledge can be owned and secret; plant knowledge raises biopiracy concerns; leprosy and mental illness carry stigma, so protect families' names.
- **Held for review:** none; no carer, healer or care-program pointer here falls under the standing rules.
