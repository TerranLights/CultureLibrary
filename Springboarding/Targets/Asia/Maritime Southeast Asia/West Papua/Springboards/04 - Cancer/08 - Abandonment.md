# West Papua: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for the 1962 departure; Biak, Kamoro and other local languages for songs and tales of being left. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit):** check how widows, orphans and deserted wives are absorbed into clans that run on exchange debts.
- **Cenderawasih Bay and Biak:** check songs and tales of leaving among seafaring families, and how long voyages figure in them.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Nduga, Yali, Mee):** check how clans take in children orphaned by conflict and disease.
- **South coast (Kamoro, Asmat, Sempan):** check tales like Biwar's and how villages treat those left behind after deaths.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check left-behind kin when men go to plantations, towns or across the border.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** check single mothers, elders alone and infants left, the urban face of the concept.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya and island villages):** check whether outer islands empty as families move to Sorong, and who stays.
- **Migrant settlers:** abandoned transmigration sites and settler families who left belong to the present-day picture, not the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin; most material is press coverage and folklore, with almost no study of how each people names and judges leaving.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for memories of 1962; Biak for "Apuse"; local languages for tales and the words for orphan and widow.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conflict orphans and deserted wives need anonymity; 1962 memory is politically charged, so record more than one view. Desertion and child-neglect law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no leaving or being-left pointer here falls under the standing rules.
