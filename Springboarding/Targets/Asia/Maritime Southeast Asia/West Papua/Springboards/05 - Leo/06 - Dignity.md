# West Papua: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; English and Dutch for rights and museum records; Moi, Dani/Hubula, Biak, Asmat and other languages for funeral, initiation and courtesy terms. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Maybrat, Tehit):** check how initiation taught bearing and what counts as shaming a guest or an elder.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Numfor, Yapen:** check how graves and the dead were cared for before and after church burial.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Nduga, Mee):** compare cremation and burial practice, and how the dead of conflict are brought home.
- **South coast, Asmat and Kamoro:** check how people describe their treatment at health posts and in resettled villages.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check how plantation and estate work is spoken of in terms of decent pay and respect.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** look at complaints about town hospitals and offices, and who makes them.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** check how sermons in Muslim Papuan villages teach human dignity.
- **Settler workers:** their claims for decent work are part of today's picture, examined apart from the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Church and rights writing is full of the language of dignity; courtesy, care of the sick and old, and funeral custom people by people are thinly written.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; English and Dutch for rights and museum records; Moi, Dani, Biak and Asmat for funeral and initiation terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human remains, bodies of the slain and images of the dead need family and community consent; initiation knowledge may be closed to outsiders. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the church, rights, wage and funeral sources here needed no setting aside.

## 5. First queries
1. Search "Uskup Saklil martabat" for sermons and interviews.
2. Search the ICP reports for chapters on health and detention, and note the regions covered.
3. Search "kambik Moi" and "upacara kremasi Dani" for ethnography.
4. Search the Ombudsman's Papua office releases for hospital complaints.
5. Search "repatriasi tengkorak Papua museum" for current return cases.
