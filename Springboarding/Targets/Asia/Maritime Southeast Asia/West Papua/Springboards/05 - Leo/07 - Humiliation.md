# West Papua: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; English for rights reports; Biak, Lani, Dani/Hubula, Sentani and other languages for insult, apology and shame terms. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit):** check whether unpaid exchange debts are named in public and whether that is felt as shaming.
- **Biak, Numfor and Raja Ampat:** check where captive or slave descent is still spoken of, and by whom.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Lani, Hubula, Yali, Mee, Nduga):** compare answers to insult (shouting, payment, fighting) and how churches step in.
- **South coast, Asmat and Kamoro:** check whether bans on feasts and men's houses are remembered as loss of face.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Muyu, Kanum):** check reports of mocking in schools and markets, and how families answer.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** look at customary apology and church discipline side by side in lake congregations.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana (Mbaham-Matta, Mairasi):** check how jokes about mixed descent are made and received.
- **Papuan students in Java and Sulawesi:** check ridicule faced far from home and how student associations respond.
- **Settler residents:** check their own accounts of being targeted in unrest, as part of today's picture, apart from the core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** uneven. Degradation by security forces is heavily documented; everyday teasing, shaming within families and ways of restoring a name are thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay for press and online material; English for rights reports; Lani, Dani, Biak and Sentani for insult and apology words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture footage and survivors' testimony need great care; do not reproduce images. Descent from captives may still mark families; never name them. Revenge belongs to Scorpio; law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; humiliation pointers here fall under no held category.
