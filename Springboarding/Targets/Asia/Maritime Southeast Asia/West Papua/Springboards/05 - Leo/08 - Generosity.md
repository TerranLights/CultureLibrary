# West Papua: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for older exchange studies; English for comparative work; Maybrat, Kamoro, Dani/Hubula, Lani, Muyu and other languages for gift and feast terms. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Tehit, Moi, Imeko):** check who gives cloth, who may refuse, and how long a return may wait.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Numfor, Yapen, Waropen:** check how catch and garden food are shared, and how welcome feasts for returning travelers were paid for.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Mee, Moni):** compare how pork is divided and who is left out.
- **South coast, Asmat, Kamoro, Korowai:** check how sago and fish move between households and at feasts.
- **Merauke lowlands (Muyu, Marind, Kanum):** check how Muyu shell-wealth giving changed after the border camps and return.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** look at offerings and auctions in town congregations against lake villages.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** compare *zakat* and *sedekah* in Muslim Papuan villages with Christian neighbors' giving.
- **Settler associations:** their charity drives are part of today's picture, examined apart from Papuan giving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Exchange ethnography for Maybrat cloth, highland pigs and Muyu shell-wealth is rich but old; church giving, *zakat*, kin claims on wages and election gifts are thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for older exchange studies; Indonesian and Papuan Malay for church and press; Maybrat, Kamoro, Lani and Muyu for gift words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** money-politics claims involve living people; do not name them. Quarrels over feast shares can be raw. Reciprocity as obligation belongs to Libra; wealth to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none; no giving pointer here touches a held category.
