# West Papua: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for the colonial and mission record; English for comparative work; Mee, Biak, Sentani, Dani/Hubula, Yali and the island languages for leaders' titles. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit, Hatam):** check how exchange leaders, church elders and regency officials share the work of leading.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Numfor, Yapen:** set clan heads, prophets of Koreri and pastors side by side.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Mee, Hubula, Lani, Yali, Nduga):** check how feast-givers and peace-makers lead, and how regents and churches sit over them.
- **South coast, Asmat and Kamoro:** check who leads now that men's houses have changed, and who speaks to the company and the regency.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Yei, Muyu):** check which clan elders speak for land in estate talks and how they are chosen.
- **Jayapura and Sentani (Sentani, Tobati-Enggros, Nafri):** look at succession to *ondofolo* titles and their role in town politics.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** check what hereditary *raja* lines still do in Muslim and Christian communities.
- **Settler politicians and migrant associations:** part of today's picture, examined apart from Papuan leadership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Typologies of traditional leadership and studies of elite politics are plentiful; women's leadership, youth leaders and leadership in daily work are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Dutch for council and patrol records; Mee, Biak and Sentani for titles and their meanings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living leaders, corruption cases and leaders of independence movements need care; profile roles, not persons, where possible. Authority and hierarchy belong to Capricorn; law to Libra and Capricorn.
