# West Papua: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for mission and patrol records; English for comparative work; Asmat, Marind, Sawi, Dani/Hubula, Lani, Sentani and other languages for oath, name and war terms. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit):** check whether a clan's name is kept through settled exchange debts and public speeches.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Numfor:** check how warrior-voyager honor gave way to honors of church and office.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Nduga, Mee):** compare how killings and insults are answered and who may end a feud.
- **South coast (Asmat, Kamoro, Sawi, Kombai):** check what the men's house and names of the slain meant for honor, and what replaced them.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Yei, Muyu):** look at names, oath rites and the honor of clans in land talks.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** check oath rites and how clans defend their name in the town press.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** check the honor of royal lines and family name in Muslim Papuan households.
- **Papuans in the army and police:** check how the honor of the uniform sits beside clan duties.
- **Settler communities:** check how their own codes of family honor meet Papuan ones in mixed towns, as part of today's picture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Older ethnography on war, peace and men's houses is rich; honor in town life, sport and women's reputation is thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian; Dutch for mission and patrol records; Asmat, Marind and Sawi for name and men's-house terms; Lani and Dani for war and peace words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** headhunting history must be handled without exoticism; women's reputation can carry violence, so use survivors' words with consent; the fallen on both sides of the conflict are contested. Revenge goes to Scorpio, duty to Capricorn, law to Libra and Capricorn.
