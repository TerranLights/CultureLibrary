# West Papua: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian; Dutch for colonial reports; the peoples' own languages for work and healing words (Biak, Waropen, Hubula, Asmat, Kamoro, Nimboran, Moi and others). **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what is good work and a well-kept life (work, service, the daily round, health)?

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Meyah, Sougb, Hatam, Tehit):** check how gardens, cloth exchange and timber or oil jobs divide a household's year.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak, Numfor, Yapen, Waropen:** compare fishing and sailing schedules with sago work, and trace where men's-house skill teaching went.
- **Highlands (Ngalum, Nduga, Hubula, Yali, Lani, Mee):** separate sweet potato and pig work by sex and age, and see how a market or office job reshapes it.
- **South coast, Asmat, Kamoro and Mimika:** set the river and sago round beside mine-town wage work and carving for sale.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Yei, Muyu, Mandobo):** ask how hunting, sago and gardens sit beside estate labor and town jobs.
- **Jayapura, Sentani and Nimboran:** compare lake fishing and gardens with civil service and town trade as ways of earning.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana (Ma'ya, Matbat, Mbaham-Matta, Koiwai):** check how fishing, nutmeg and tourism work line up with Muslim and Christian weekly rhythms.
- **Migrant settler communities:** traders, transmigrant farmers and outside officials shape today's job market; study them apart from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Older ethnography covers subsistence; wage work, women's hours, the daily round and cleanliness sit mostly in local theses. Which lands West Papua holds is not yet decided (its tracker lists Indonesia as the pre-war land); these Virgo sheets cover the Papuan half of New Guinea's west and the islands off it.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay for surveys and theses; Dutch for Held, Kouwenhoven and the handover reports; Biak, Asmat and Hubula for men's-house and task words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** men's-house teaching and some healing knowledge are restricted; ask before recording. Mine and estate labor touch security and land conflicts. The clock and public order are Capricorn's; labor law is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** at the domain level for work and service, nothing was set aside.
