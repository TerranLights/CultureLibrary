# West Papua: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for statistics, audits, school data and rights monitoring; English for demographic, counting-system and biodiversity work; Papuan Malay for everyday reasoning talk; Mee, Biak, Hubula and the Ok languages for number words and ways of reckoning. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ok peoples of the Star Mountains (Ngalum, Ketengban) and Una:** check how body-tally counting is used now that school numbers are taught.
- **Mee of Paniai and Dogiyai:** look at how the *Dou, Gai, Ekowai* sequence is used by Mee writers to talk about reasoning before action.
- **Biak, Numfor and Waropen:** check star and wind lore for sailing, and how it is recorded and tested.
- **Baliem Hubula and Lani:** compare farmers' variety trials with extension-station advice on the same crop.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu, Mandobo):** see how plantation license data are analyzed by clans, churches and investigators.
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Meyah, Hatam):** compare land-boundary reckoning in mapping projects with official surveys.
- **Jayapura, Sentani and the campuses:** set school, university and statistics-office analysis beside church and customary reasoning.
- **Migrant settler communities:** many teachers, auditors and officials come from elsewhere; their analytic habits are part of today's scene, studied as a separate strand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Counting systems and biodiversity surveys are well recorded; everyday reasoning, local record keeping and Papuan self-accounts of thinking are thin; population numbers are disputed.
- **Languages needed:** English for Lean, Elmslie and survey reports; Indonesian for BPK, school data and theses; Ngalum, Mee and Biak for number words and sign lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** population and rights counts are politically charged; cite methods, not totals, at this stage. Some navigation and planting signs are clan knowledge. Learning and information sit in Gemini; truth and education in Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none; the counting, audit and monitoring pointers needed no holding.
