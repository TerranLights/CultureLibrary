# West Papua: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for church, consumer and literacy sources; Papuan Malay for street judgments; Dutch for mission and colonial handbooks; Biak for prophecy; Mbaham-Matta, Hubula and Lani for market and garden judgment. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Biak, Numfor and Supiori:** trace how the Koreri tradition and the GKI each teach people to test a messenger.
- **Highlands (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Nduga, Mee):** compare how pigs, garden land and new preachers are judged, and who is asked.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana (Mbaham-Matta, Iha, Onin, Koiwai):** check how quality is judged in the nutmeg trade, and how Muslim and Christian families advise on partners and trading friends.
- **South coast (Asmat, Kamoro, Marind, Yaqay):** look at how carvers and buyers judge a good piece, and how villages weigh outside traders.
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Tehit, Maybrat, Sougb):** check how bird and dream signs are weighed against church teaching.
- **Jayapura, Sentani and the towns:** look at scams, online claims and digital-literacy training where judgment is tested most often.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Biak Beser, Matbat):** see how villagers judge tourism investors and conservation offers.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settlers are both sellers and targets in town markets and schemes; their part belongs to today's picture, kept apart from Papuan ways of judging.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Prophecy and mission history are recorded; market and garden judgment sit in scattered theses; Papuan accounts of being fooled or of judging strangers are rare in print.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for Klein and the baptism records; Biak for heralds' names and claims; Indonesian for sermons, consumer notices and scam reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sizing up strangers can touch suspicion of informers in conflict zones; do not record names. Some sign-reading belongs to clans or healers. Truth and belief go to Sagittarius; trust to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; prophecy, market and scam pointers needed nothing set aside.
