# West Papua: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay for craft, school and building talk; English for archaeology-of-skill and art-history work; Asmat, Sentani, Una, Biak and Korowai for makers' own words on doing a thing right. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern highlands (Una, Ketengban, Yali, Ngalum):** look at how stone adze and arrow makers judge a finished piece, and how steel tools changed the standard.
- **Asmat and its river districts:** compare festival judging with village judging of the same carvings.
- **Lake Sentani and the Jayapura coast (Sentani, Tobati-Enggros, Ormu):** check who decides which bark-painting motifs are fixed and which are open.
- **Biak, Numfor, Yapen and Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Matbat):** compare canoe making and song cycles as fields where exactness is demanded.
- **Korowai, Kombai and Citak:** look at how house builders guard against error, since a fault can kill.
- **Mamberamo and Sarmi (Kwerba, Isirawa, Sobei):** seldom studied; check what makers there call well done.
- **Bird's Head and Merauke lowlands (Maybrat, Hatam, Marind, Kanum):** see how carving, weaving and garden fences are judged against slapdash work.
- **Migrant settler communities:** contractors and teachers from elsewhere apply national standards to buildings and school work; treat them as part of the present-day setting, separate from Papuan standards.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Craft standards appear in art and skill studies for Asmat, Sentani and Langda; personal perfectionism and its distress are almost unstudied for Papuans.
- **Languages needed:** English for Stout and Kooijman; Indonesian for festival rules, audits and theses; Asmat, Sentani and Una for makers' own words of praise and blame.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some carving forms and song texts are owned by clans or tied to initiation; record only what owners release. Student distress needs ethical care. Discipline and order are Capricorn's; pride and humiliation are Leo's.
- **Held for review:** none; craft, building and school pointers raised nothing to hold.
