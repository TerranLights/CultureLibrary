# West Papua: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for press, church, planning and protest sources; Papuan Malay for teasing, rebuke and satire as spoken; Dutch for mission church-discipline records; Lani, Mee, Biak and Waropen for song duels and kin joking. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highlands (Lani, Mee, Hubula, Yali, Moni):** check how song, oratory and church rebuke carry criticism, and who may speak at a gathering.
- **Biak, Waropen and Yapen:** compare joking kin and mocking songs with church discipline in the oldest congregations.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** look at stand-up, cartoons, campus protest and complaint systems, where public criticism is most visible.
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Arfak peoples, Inanwatan):** see how criticism of exchange partners and village heads is voiced or held back.
- **South coast (Asmat, Kamoro, Marind, Muyu):** check how criticism of companies and officials is passed through churches and customary bodies.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** compare how Muslim and Christian Papuan villages rebuke leaders and members.
- **Ngalum and the Star Mountains:** little written; look for how elders correct the young and how criticism reaches the regency.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settler-run media and settler targets of satire are part of the present picture, studied separately from Papuan forms of criticism.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Political criticism and church statements are plentiful; family rebuke, joking kin and song duels are thin and scattered; self-censorship hides much of the rest.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for press and planning records; Dutch for mission discipline; Lani, Mee and Biak for song and joking terms that lose their edge in translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism of the state can bring arrest; quote public statements only, protect sources, and do not attach names to private complaints. Speech law is for Libra and Capricorn; gossip is Gemini's; humiliation is Leo's.
- **Held for review:** none; the satire, church and protest pointers raised no held category.
