# West Papua: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for development plans, indices and press; Papuan Malay for youth and church talk of "getting ahead"; English for regional development archives; Dutch for 1950s policy; Yali, Asmat, Biak and Mee for initiation and renewal words. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highlands (Yali, Lani, Nduga, Hubula, Mee):** compare road, house and fuel programs with older ideas of making a person whole through initiation.
- **Biak, Numfor and Supiori:** look at how Koreri and the church's story of civilization each define a better state.
- **Asmat and Mimika (Asmat, Kamoro, Amungme):** check how resettlement, mine funds and carving markets were offered as improvement.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** see who counts estate and food-crop schemes as betterment and who as loss.
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Arfak peoples, Tehit):** compare village-index ratings with local judgments of a "good" village.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** look at scholarship returnees, youth seminars and entrepreneur programs, where self-improvement talk is strongest.
- **Raja Ampat and Fakfak (Ma'ya, Mbaham-Matta, Biak Beser):** check how conservation and tourism are framed as improvement, and how Muslim and Christian villages each speak of progress.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settlers are often the face of "development" in trade and government; that role belongs to today's picture and is studied separately from Papuan ideas of betterment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state development plans and their critics; thin for personal self-improvement and for how each people speaks of becoming better in its own language.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for BP3OKP, IDM and critiques; English and Dutch for South Pacific Commission and 1950s files; Biak and Yali for renewal and initiation terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "improvement" is bound up with the political conflict and with claims of neglect; present both sides' words. Initiation knowledge is restricted. Reform and hope sit in Aquarius; ambition and merit in Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the development, renewal and self-help pointers set nothing aside.
