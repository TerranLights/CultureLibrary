# West Papua: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay for everyday complaints of unequal shares; Indonesian for quota rules, wage orders and budgets; Dutch for VOC and colonial trade records; Hubula, Lani, Kamoro, Biak and Ma'ya for the division of pork, catch and harvest. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula, Lani, Yali and Walak:** check how pork and pigs are divided at feasts and compensation payments, to see what counts as a fair cut.
- **Mee and Moni:** compare old loan and shell-money dealings with present quarrels over aid and project money.
- **Amungme and Kamoro:** look at how company community funds are split between the two peoples and among clans.
- **Biak, Numfor and the Cenderawasih Bay coasts:** check catch-sharing among crews and fairness in trade with outside buyers.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Matbat, Biak Beser):** compare closed-season harvest shares and tourism income between villages.
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Maybrat, Tehit):** see how timber and oil-palm payments are shared among land-holding clans.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** examine town arguments over jobs, market stalls and school places, where quotas bite.
- **Merauke (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** compare estate jobs and land payments, and who is counted as Papuan in hiring.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settlers are on the other side of most quota and market disputes; take their complaints of unequal treatment as a present-day voice beside, not inside, Papuan rules of sharing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Quota, wage and budget rules are online; how pork, catch and aid are shared sits in theses and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for regulations and audits; Papuan Malay for complaints; Hubula, Lani and Ma'ya for sharing words still to be found.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** quota debates set Papuans and settlers against each other and can spill into violence; report claims, not blame. Fund-sharing touches corruption cases. Equality as an ideal belongs to Aquarius; merit to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the quota, wage and feast-sharing pointers needed no exclusion.
