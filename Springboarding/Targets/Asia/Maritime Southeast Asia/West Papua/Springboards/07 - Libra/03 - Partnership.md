# West Papua: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for marriage and company law, co-op and plantation rules; Papuan Malay for couple and work-mate talk; Dutch for mission marriage registers; Biak for trading partners; Mek (Eipo), Sentani, Ma'ya and Moi for marriage alliance and shared work. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mee-Pago (Mee, Moni, Wolani):** check how bride-wealth debts tie two families into a lasting working partnership.
- **La-Pago (Hubula, Lani, Yali, Mek, Ngalum):** compare plural marriage, war alliance and garden partnerships, and how the courts treat each.
- **Saereri (Biak, Numfor, Yapen, Waropen):** look at hereditary trading partners and boat crews as bonds between non-kin.
- **Domberai (Moi, Maybrat, Hatam, Sougb, Tehit):** see whether plantation and timber partnerships are entered by clans or by individuals.
- **Bomberai (Mbaham-Matta, Iha, Koiwai):** compare Muslim and Christian marriage, and nutmeg partnerships with outside buyers.
- **Ha-anim (Marind, Kanum, Asmat, Kamoro, Muyu):** check marriage exchange between groups and the terms of estate and mine agreements.
- **Mamta (Sentani, Tobati, Nimboran, Isirawa):** look at how town couples, church weddings and *ondoafi* marriage alliances fit together.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Matbat):** compare village shares in conservation partnerships across islands.
- **Migrant settler communities:** mixed marriages and joint firms with settlers are common in towns; study how bride wealth and business shares are agreed in them, as a present-day layer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Statutes, company deals and conservation reports are online; marriage alliance and bond friendship need ethnography and fieldwork, thin outside a few peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for law and agreements; German for the Eipo volumes; Biak for trading-partner terms; Dutch for mission marriage records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child marriage, plural marriage and violence in couples need care; company agreements are disputed in court. Sexual partnership is Scorpio's; family life is Cancer's.
- **Held for review:** none; marriage, trading-partner and company leads all passed.
