# West Papua: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for election, civil-service and anticorruption law and rulings; Papuan Malay for talk of "our own people" in office; Dutch for colonial administration; Lani, Yali, Mee and Hubula for highland vote-binding and headmen; Mbaham-Matta and Ma'ya for the coastal raja houses. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland regencies using noken voting (Lani, Yali, Nduga, Hubula):** check how a bundled vote is agreed and whether any voter can stand apart from it.
- **Mee and Moni of Paniai and Intan Jaya:** compare election disputes and whether officials from one clan can serve as neutral.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana raja houses (Mbaham-Matta, Iha, Koiwai):** look at how inherited office and the state's offices sit together when a dispute must be judged.
- **Sentani and Tobati:** check how *ondoafi* decide among their own villages and when they defer to outsiders.
- **Biak and Yapen:** compare coastal one-person voting and church office with highland practice.
- **Mimika and Asmat (Amungme, Kamoro, Asmat):** see how company and district funds interlock with local office, through corruption and audit records.
- **Raja Ampat and Sorong (Ma'ya, Moi, Matbat):** check whether village heads and conservation boards hand out permits and jobs evenly, and who is asked to judge when they do not.
- **Merauke (Marind, Kanum):** check land-release and plantation permits for ties between officials and firms.
- **Jayapura and Manokwari civil service:** study promotion and hiring files where kin claims meet merit rules.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settlers hold many posts and contracts, so tender and hiring files must mark who is Papuan and who is not before favoritism can be traced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the letter of the law (rulings, ethics decisions, procurement); thin for how communities themselves pick a neutral judge.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for rulings and audits; Lani, Yali and Hubula to hear noken arrangements first-hand; Dutch for colonial administrative files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and election disputes can turn violent; name no informants. Loyalty and trust go to Scorpio; whether officials are trusted, and obedience, to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; election, procurement and raja-house sources were all kept in the table.
