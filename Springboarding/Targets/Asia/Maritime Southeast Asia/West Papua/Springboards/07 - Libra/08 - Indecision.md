# West Papua: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for legislative programs, election and health records; Papuan Malay for talk of waiting and wavering; Dutch for older council and patrol notes; Moi, Marind, Lani, Mee and Biak for clan deliberation, marriage talks and the right to the last word. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moi, Tehit and Maybrat of the Bird's Head:** check how long land-release talks run and who among the clans can stop them.
- **Marind, Kanum and Yei of Merauke:** compare plantation decisions by clan with the speed companies and offices expect.
- **Lani, Yali, Hubula and Nduga:** look at men's-house councils, church fasting and noken-era elections as three ways to decide.
- **Mee and Moni:** check how deliberation over aid, schools and land is closed, and by whom.
- **Biak and Numfor:** see how marriage talks, elopement and church choices are settled in island families.
- **Asmat and Kamoro:** compare village choices on moving to new settlements or staying on the rivers.
- **Sentani and Jayapura:** study legislative backlogs and the town debates where Papuan bodies sit.
- **Raja Ampat (Ma'ya, Matbat):** look at village votes and meetings on visitor quotas and fishing closures.
- **Migrant settler communities:** their associations sit in town planning meetings; note where their votes speed or stall a choice, as a present-day layer beside Papuan deliberation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Legislative and health delays are recorded; how families and clans weigh a choice must be heard in person.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for legislature and health records; Moi and Marind for land deliberation; Dutch for patrol reports; Lani and Biak for council and marriage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** do not frame slow decisions as a trait of any people; record procedure, not character. Maternal deaths and the 2019 student exodus are painful topics. Rashness belongs to Aries; authority to decide to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the legislative, clinical and clan-deliberation pointers stay in the list.
