# West Papua: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for statutes, NGO and company reports; Papuan Malay for the words that ask someone to let a matter drop; English for international reports; Hubula, Lani, Nduga, Amungme and Kamoro for war-closing rites and settlements; Biak for testimony after 1998. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula, Lani and Walak of the Baliem:** check who pays and who receives when a clan war is closed, and how often public money stands in.
- **Nduga and Intan Jaya:** compare levies paid to armed actors with refusals, and what each costs villages caught between sides.
- **Amungme and Kamoro of Mimika:** study the trade between company money and land claims.
- **Biak, Numfor and Supiori:** trace silence and testimony after 1998 within island families.
- **Mee and Moni:** look at women's reports of violence and how often they end in payment.
- **Sentani, Jayapura and Abepura:** see where the women's service offices and courts sit, and whether townswomen report more than village women.
- **Merauke and the south coast (Marind, Kanum, Asmat):** check how plantation and logging conflicts are calmed by payments or promises.
- **Bird's Head and Raja Ampat (Moi, Arfak peoples, Ma'ya):** compare how disputes with companies and park boards are smoothed over, and who signs.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settler traders also pay levies to stay open; ask them separately what quiet costs them, as a present-day layer and not the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Statutes and NGO reports are available; the private price of keeping quiet must come from testimony, gathered with care.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for law and reports; Papuan Malay for testimony; English for Global Witness and MacLeod; Hubula and Lani for war-closing rites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual violence, security payments and surrender are dangerous to record; protect every survivor and witness. Never present silence as a people's trait. Revenge and secrecy are Scorpio's; courage to speak up is Aries's.
- **Held for review:** none; testimony, payment and statute pointers on keeping quiet were all retained.
