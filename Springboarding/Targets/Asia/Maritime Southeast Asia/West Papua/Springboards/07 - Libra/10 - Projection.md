# West Papua: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for the criminal code, court rulings and state labels; Papuan Malay for blame talk and sorcery accusations; Dutch for mission and patrol reports; English for outsider media and investigations; Korowai, Lani, Dani and Mee for sorcery terms and conversion-era object burnings. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Korowai and Kombai of the south lowlands:** check how a death leads to blame on a hidden insider, and how the state now responds.
- **Lani, Hubula and Yali:** compare revival-era object burnings with present sorcery talk.
- **Mee and Moni:** look at how illness and failed harvests are explained, and when an explanation names a person.
- **Nduga, Intan Jaya and Puncak:** study spy accusations from both sides in armed conflict.
- **Biak, Numfor and Yapen:** check how coastal churches teach confession and how sorcery accusation is handled.
- **Fakfak and Kaimana:** compare Muslim and Christian approaches to blame for harm, and how *suanggi* cases go to court.
- **Highland and coastal Papuans in the towns:** record the labels each uses for the other, as words to study, not traits.
- **Bird's Head, Raja Ampat and Merauke (Moi, Ma'ya, Marind):** check whether land lost to companies is blamed on outsiders, on clan members who signed, or on sorcery.
- **Mamberamo and Sarmi (Kwerba, Isirawa):** seldom studied; check whether blame for illness falls inside or outside the village.
- **Migrant settler communities:** after riots, settlers and Papuans often name each other as the cause; trace each claim to its speaker, keeping the settler side as a present-day layer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Law, court rulings and media criticism are reachable; sorcery and blame talk is sensitive, uneven and often recorded only by outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for law and courts; Papuan Malay for blame talk; Dutch for mission and patrol reports; Korowai and Lani for accusation and conversion terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations and spy killings put lives at risk; never record names. Outsider "primitive" images must not be repeated as description. Revenge and betrayal belong to Scorpio; the self-image of a people to Aries.
- **Held for review:** none; the sorcery, labeling and media leads raised nothing under the standing rules.
