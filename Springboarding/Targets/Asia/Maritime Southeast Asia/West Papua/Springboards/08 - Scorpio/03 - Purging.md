# West Papua: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for audits, environmental reports and press; Papuan Malay for shunning and "putting out" talk; Dutch for mission records of cleansing and disease; the coastal Muslim register of Fakfak and Kaimana; Kamoro, Moi, Hubula and Korowai for polluted rivers, land and the rites of release. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula, Lani and Nduga:** check whether cleansing after war deaths survives and who performs it.
- **Kamoro of the Mimika estuaries:** compare talk of a fouled river with older ideas of what makes water unclean.
- **Korowai and other Boven Digoel peoples:** check how the gold rush is folded into older blame for illness.
- **Moi and Tehit around Sorong:** look at how company exits are framed as cleaning the land.
- **Muslim communities of Fakfak and Kaimana:** test *tolak bala* against Christian deliverance prayer.
- **Biak and Numfor:** check mission-era epidemic files against island memories of disease.
- **Raja Ampat villages:** compare reef-protection enforcement with older penalties for harming the sea.
- **Settler households in Wamena and other towns:** their accounts of leaving belong to the present-day picture and are kept as a separate voice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Pollution, audits and the 2019 departures are well reported; cleansing rites and folk purges are thinly written.
- **Languages needed:** Hubula or Lani to name war-cleansing; Kamoro for the river; Moi for land claims; Dutch for epidemic files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** expulsions and riots involve named victims; deliverance and accusation can stigmatize people; mining pollution is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none: the purging pointers needed no setting aside.
