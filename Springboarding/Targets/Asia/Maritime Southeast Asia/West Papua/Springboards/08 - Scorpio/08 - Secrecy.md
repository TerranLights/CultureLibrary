# West Papua: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for health, press and rights reporting; Papuan Malay for whispered and coded talk; English for declassified foreign files; Hubula, Ngalum, Asmat, Mee and the Raja Ampat languages (Ma'ya, Biak dialects) for restricted objects, names and places. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngalum and other Star Mountains peoples:** check what may be said about cult houses at all before asking more.
- **Hubula, Walak and Lani:** compare who guards sacred objects now that men's houses have changed.
- **Mee and Moni of Paniai:** look at healers' knowledge as family property.
- **Asmat river villages:** test whether clan ownership of designs holds in workshops and festivals.
- **Raja Ampat islands (Ma'ya, Matbat):** compare closed sites with the tourist economy that wants them open.
- **Biak and Sentani coasts:** check whether restricted stories differ from those of the Highlands.
- **Bird's Head initiation peoples (Maybrat, Tehit, Moi):** ask what initiation secrecy still covers, without probing its content.
- **Settler communities:** what they keep private and from whom is part of the present picture, but it is not the Papuan pattern this concept is after.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Secrecy is by nature under-recorded; ethnographies touch it for a few peoples; health and conflict reporting give modern angles.
- **Languages needed:** Ngalum and Hubula for restricted places and objects; Mee for healing; English for declassified files; Papuan Malay for coded talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** this sheet points to restricted knowledge; research should record only what keepers allow and never retell closed stories, names or rites. HIV status and conflict casualties need strict anonymity.
- **Held for review:** none; the secrecy pointers include nothing the standing rules would hold back.
