# West Papua: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for disaster, health and press records; Papuan Malay for hard-times talk; English and Japanese for the 1944 war records; Dutch for postwar files; Biak, Lani, Damal, Kamoro and the Raja Ampat languages for remembered disasters and hungry seasons. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or the country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Biak and Supiori:** check how the 1944 fighting is told by elders, church and the island's war sites.
- **Lani, Damal and Nduga of Puncak:** compare crop failure and frost with conflict as causes named for hunger.
- **Amungme and Kamoro of Timika:** look at malaria and other recurring emergencies as normal or as crisis.
- **Arfak, Moi and Maybrat of the Bird's Head towns:** test how the 2019 unrest is remembered on each side.
- **Raja Ampat islanders:** check who speaks for the reef after a sudden harm.
- **South-coast lowlands (Asmat, Marind, Yei):** compare tides, floods and peat fires as slow crises.
- **Sentani lake villages and Jayapura's Papuan neighborhoods:** look at legends of floods beside present disaster talk.
- **Settler households:** how migrants weather crises and leave or stay belongs to the present-day picture, set apart from Papuan accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Disaster, health and war records are plentiful; personal crisis, breakdown and the native words for hard times are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Lani and Damal for hunger and frost; Biak for war memory; English, Japanese and Dutch for 1944 and postwar records; Indonesian for agency data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine and unrest are politically charged and blame is contested; mental-health cases need privacy; war-grave sites may be sacred to families.
- **Held for review:** none; the crisis pointers carry nothing to set aside.
