# West Papua: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian for integrity surveys, school programs and consumer reports; Papuan Malay for the everyday words of cheating, joking and straight talk; Hubula, Lani, Mee and Biak for exchange debts and promises; Dutch for early traders' complaints, read critically. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hubula, Lani and Yali of the highlands:** check how promised exchange payments are tracked and how a defaulter is spoken of.
- **Mee of Paniai:** look at shell and pig debts in older ethnography against cash dealing now, for what "keeping one's word" covers.
- **Biak and Cenderawasih Bay:** check whether the joke and the polite lie are judged the same way as outright deceit.
- **Mbaham-Matta and Iha of Fakfak and Kaimana:** compare grower and trader accounts of fair weights.
- **Ma'ya and other Raja Ampat islanders:** check how villages handle promises made to tourists.
- **Kamoro and Amungme around Timika:** look at fraud complaints in a fast-growing mining town.
- **Marind and Kanum of the Merauke lowlands, and Sentani:** check how market honesty is judged where Papuan and settler sellers stand side by side.
- **Migrant settler communities:** note their traders' and buyers' own accounts as part of the town economy, without treating either side's charges as fact about a group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: integrity surveys reach the province; honesty in local languages and exchange is thin and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for nineteenth-century trade complaints; Hubula, Lani and Mee for exchange vocabulary; Papuan Malay for *mop* and online talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charges of cheating easily become ethnic blame between Papuans and settlers; report accusations as accusations. Corruption cases in court are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; the honesty pointers include nothing the standing rules hold back.
