# West Papua: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for mission and Tidore-era records; Biak, Mee, Hubula (Dani), Asmat, Maybrat and Moi for what kin, feasts and the dead require. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Maybrat, Moi, Tehit, Hatam):** check how cloth exchange and Catholic or Protestant teaching each set what is owed.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Mee, Yali, Nduga):** compare pig-feast debts and mourning duties valley by valley.
- **Cenderawasih Bay, Biak and Numfor:** look at duties carried by migrants to their home villages and clans.
- **South coast, Asmat and Kamoro:** check what is still owed to the dead and how carving feasts carry it.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana:** separate religious duties of Papuan Muslims from the older debts once owed to sultans and rajas.
- **Jayapura and Sentani:** check how lake and bay villages divide communal tasks across clans.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check what obligations bind clans to land and to each other in land releases.
- **Migrant settler communities:** their hometown associations and remittances are part of today's towns, recorded apart from Papuan duties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Feast and bride-wealth debts are well studied for a few highland and Bird's Head peoples; duties to the state and the obligations of the salaried are mostly in the press.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for ministry and church material; Mee and Hubula for feast terms; Maybrat for the moral talk Courtens records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mourning practices involving the body, and men's-house or initiation obligations, call for care; the Komcad topic touches the conflict, so both Papuan and state voices are needed.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing among these obligation pointers fell under the exclusion rules.
