# West Papua: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Indonesian and Papuan Malay; Dutch for mission reports on blame and misfortune; English for company and NGO material; the languages of the Bintuni Bay, Boven Digoel and Asmat peoples for answerability to land and kin. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bintuni Bay (Sough, Sebyar, Irarutu, Kuri):** check how clans and the gas company each define what the other must answer for.
- **Boven Digoel and Merauke lowlands (Awyu, Wambon, Marind, Muyu):** check how clans speak of answering to descendants for land released or kept.
- **South coast, Asmat and Kamoro:** look at how blame for illness and death is shared among family, healer, church and regency.
- **Central Highlands (Lani, Mee, Hubula, Kimyal, Nduga):** compare which wrongs fall on the clan and which on the person.
- **Jayapura hinterland and Sentani (Kemtuik, Nimboran, Sentani):** check who answers for children and land near the expanding capital.
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Maybrat, Arfak peoples):** look at forest stewardship talk inside clans against conservation pledges from above.
- **Raja Ampat and Biak:** check reef and sea care as a task of named clans or villages.
- **Migrant settler communities:** settler traders and officials are part of the blame talk in towns; record their voices apart from the Papuan core.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Audits, company panels and lawsuits are rich in documents; how people assign fault within families and clans is thinly written down.
- **Languages needed:** Indonesian for audits and court files; English for TIAP and NGO reports; Awyu, Sough and Lani for clan statements on answerability.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can endanger the accused, so never name individuals; shootings and blame in the conflict need every side's account. The letter of liability law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; the blame and accountability pointers raised nothing under the standing rules.
