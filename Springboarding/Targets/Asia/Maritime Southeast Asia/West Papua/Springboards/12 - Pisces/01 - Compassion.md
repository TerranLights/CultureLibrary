# West Papua: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay and Indonesian (church, aid and news usage); Dutch (mission and nursing records); the peoples' own languages, Biak, Hubula, Lani, Mee, Abun and Mbaham-Matta among them, for pity and mercy words. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

English keywords: compassion Papua; mercy and enemies New Guinea highlands; charity images of Papuan children; patient transit houses Papua; animal care West Papua.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Abun, Moi, Meyah, Maybrat):** check how mercy toward animals and toward debtors is weighed against clan claims.
- **Cenderawasih Bay and Biak (Biak, Numfor, Waropen):** check the old island story of captives and foster children against today's church charity.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Walak, Nduga):** check what is owed to refugees of clan fighting, and whether enemies can receive it.
- **South coast and Asmat (Asmat, Kamoro, Sawi):** check who brings the sick to coastal clinics and who stays with them.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check who aids families pushed off forest land and food sources.
- **Jayapura and Sentani (Sentani, Tobati-Enggros, Nafri):** check how town households host sick kin from the interior.
- **Raja Ampat, Fakfak and Kaimana (Ma'ya, Mbaham-Matta, Iha):** check whether alms reach neighbors of the other faith.
- **Migrant settler communities in the towns:** a present-day layer; check how their charities and Papuan ones choose whom to help.
- **Diaspora (the Netherlands, Australia, Vanuatu):** comparison only, for appeals made on behalf of those at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to thin. Aid, church and rights reports are plentiful; no field study of helping strangers or giving is known for Papuan towns, so measures must be built, not found.
- **Languages needed:** Papuan Malay to hear *kasian* used in context; Dutch for mission and nursing letters; Abun, Biak and highland languages for mercy words no dictionary has yet glossed for this scout.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** images of Papuan children raise consent questions; accusation victims and unrest testimony need protection of names.
- **Held for review:** none for compassion; no pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
