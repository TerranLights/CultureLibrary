# West Papua: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** West Papua, region Maritime Southeast Asia. **Language(s) for searching:** Papuan Malay (hunches and bad feelings in daily talk); Indonesian (news, health, popular psychology); Dutch (mission notes on omens); Asmat, Korowai, Kamoro, Numfor-Biak and highland languages for skilled knowing and signs. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bird's Head (Moi, Maybrat, Hatam):** check whether seers and omens are consulted before land deals and journeys.
- **Cenderawasih Bay and Biak (Numfor, Biak, Wandamen):** check the omen lore the first missionaries wrote down against what elders say now.
- **Central Highlands and Baliem (Hubula, Lani, Mee, Yali):** check how clinic staff and families weigh a hunch against a diagnosis.
- **South coast and lowland forests (Asmat, Korowai, Kamoro, Kombai):** check how carvers, hunters and fishers explain knowing without words.
- **Merauke lowlands (Marind, Kanum, Muyu):** check hunting knowledge on lands now crossed by plantations.
- **Jayapura and Sentani (Sentani, Tobati-Enggros, Skou):** check town rumors and *anak indigo* talk against older seers.
- **Raja Ampat and Fakfak (Ma'ya, Mbaham-Matta, Iha):** check whether Muslim and Christian villagers read the same omens.
- **Migrant settler communities in the towns:** a present-day layer; check which dream readings and seers are consulted, and what is borrowed in each direction.
- **Diaspora:** comparison only, for omen lore carried abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin to mixed. Local ecological knowledge has some study; omens, seers and gut judgment are mostly in features and posts, and no measured data on intuitive thinking are known for Papuans.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for mission omen records; Asmat and Kamoro for skilled knowing; Papuan Malay for how bad feelings are voiced.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kidnapper rumors and seers' accusations can lead to violence; carvers' inspiration may draw on restricted ancestral knowledge.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer for intuition relied on a source the standing rules hold back.
