## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria, Franconia, Bavarian Swabia, Upper Palatinate:** check Catholic name days and confirmation names against naming in Lutheran Franconian districts.
- **Vienna against the Länder:** check whether surnames from the whole former monarchy and rural farm names locate the self differently.
- **Innviertel and Rupertiwinkel:** lands that passed between Bavaria and Salzburg or Austria; check whether self-labels follow region or state.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check bilingual house names and how registers spelled their names.
- **Burgenland Hungarians (Oberwart / Felsőőr, Unterwart / Alsóőr):** check name order, and Reformed against Catholic naming.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check names used inside the community against registered names; consent needed.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna, Munich, Franconia and Hohenems:** check imposed surnames and later name changes.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check given names chosen to work in two languages.
- **Sudeten German descendants:** check whether the lost home town appears in self-introduction.
- **Vorarlberg and the Walser valleys:** check Alemannic self-labels against Bavarian-speaking Austria.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for names, dress, portraits and life stories; mixed for self-construal surveys, which rarely split out Bavaria. The pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** dialect reading for house names and life stories; Slovene, Croatian and Hungarian for minority self-naming in registers and press; Kurrent script for older registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era registrations of Roma, Sinti and Jews; Tracht's political uses in the 1930s; personal data in recent registers.
- **Held for review:** none; no naming, dress or body pointer had to be set aside.
