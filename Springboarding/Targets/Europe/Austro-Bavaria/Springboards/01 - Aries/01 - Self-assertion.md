## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria against Franconia:** check *Derblecken* beside the televised Franconian carnival speeches at Veitshöchheim as two licensed stages.
- **Vienna against market towns:** check speaking order in a district council against a rural *Gemeinderat*.
- **Farm households (*Bäuerinnen*, heirs, retired elders):** check who may contradict whom at table and in farm decisions.
- **Apprentices and masters:** check talking back in the trades, where *Lehrling* and *Meister* titles still order the workplace.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check claims for bilingual schooling and signs; separate older and younger activists.
- **Roma and Sinti in Burgenland, Vienna and Bavaria:** check where public self-advocacy began and who leads it.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check code-switching between home, mosque, workplace and school.
- **Sudeten German associations:** check lobbying as a group voice, separate from personal assertion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in media, folk song and political ritual; thin in survey items that measure assertion directly at Land level.
- **Languages needed:** Bavarian and Viennese to read mockery and roast texts in their tone; Slovene and Croatian for minority campaign material; Middle High German for Neidhart.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the town-sign conflict and the 1995 Oberwart bombing; Roma speakers' consent; party rhetoric at Passau.
- **Held for review:** none; the speaking-up and complaint pointers all fall within the standing rules.
