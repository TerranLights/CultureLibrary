*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm heirs and *weichende Erben*:** check who chose a life path, and whether non-inheriting siblings were sent into service, trades or the cloister.
- **Bregenzerwald, Montafon and other Alpine valleys against lowland estates:** check whether old collective self-rule left more room for household choice.
- **Vienna's municipal housing tenants against rural homeowners:** check how much a landlord, a co-op or a village shapes daily choices.
- **Secret Protestants' descendants in Carinthia, Styria and Upper Austria:** check family memory of choosing faith under pressure.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check collective self-organization (schools, co-ops, choirs) against reliance on state bodies.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check, by generation, who takes part in choices of spouse, study and home; avoid assuming one pattern.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check how their own associations set their agenda, separate from programs designed for them.
- **People in care, orders and institutions:** check autonomy for elders in care homes, people with disabilities, and members of religious orders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics and church leaving; mixed for family decision-making, which is seldom surveyed by Land.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent and older German for handover contracts and customals; Slovene for cooperative records; Turkish and Bosnian for family-life accounts in their own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abortion, assisted dying and vaccination debates (law to Libra and Capricorn); shelter data and personal stories; institutional abuse inquiries in children's homes.
- **Held for review:** none set aside; the legal side of handovers, customals and bodily choices is routed to Libra and Capricorn, not held.

## 5. First queries
1. Search Statistik Austria for "Jugendliche im Elternhaushalt nach Bundesland" and the Bavarian office for the same.

5. Search the Frauenhäuser network's annual statistics and the *Arbeitsklima Index* items on autonomy.
