## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vorarlberg's textile towns and Upper Austria's industrial belt against Vienna:** check whether founding stories credit families, firms or the state.
- **Franconia's Nuremberg-Fürth region against Upper Bavaria's Munich cluster:** check older trade and newer tech founding cultures side by side.
- **Expellee towns (Neugablonz, Waldkraiburg, Traunreut):** check how refugees became founders and how descendants tell it.
- **Village associations (fire brigades, music bands, *Landjugend*):** check who calls the first meeting and who waits to be asked.
- **Women founders and farm women's side businesses (farm shops, holiday farms):** check initiative inside a family holding.
- **Carinthian Slovene and Burgenland Croatian associations:** check minority-founded kindergartens, choirs and co-ops.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and other migrant entrepreneurs:** check founding from need against from opportunity by generation.
- **Jewish founders before 1938 and after 1945:** check department stores, coffeehouses and firms, and their seizure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and volunteering statistics; mixed for the everyday side (helping unasked at home, in a workshop).
- **Languages needed:** Turkish and Bosnian for founders' own accounts; Slovene and Croatian for minority associations' minutes; Early New High German for Maximilian's works.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Aryanization" of Jewish businesses; expellee politics; Roma programs run by outsiders.
- **Held for review:** none; the founder, volunteer and petition pointers needed no setting aside.
