## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Salzburg, Old Bavaria, Vorarlberg and Franconia:** check which seasonal custom marks the year's real start in each, and whether it is New Year, Candlemas or spring.
- **Catholic and Protestant families:** check baptism, first communion and confirmation as the first rites against Lutheran confirmation alone.
- **Jewish communities:** check Rosh Hashanah, brit and naming as beginnings kept in Vienna, Munich and Franconia today.
- **Muslim Bosnian- and Turkish-Austrians and Bavarians:** check which calendar's new year, and which first rites, families keep.
- **Refugee cohorts of 1956, 1968, 1981 and the 1990s:** check how each tells its start in Austria, by the generation of the teller.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check *koledovanje* and Croatian New Year songs as minority-language openings of the year.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check family accounts of rebuilding after 1945 for the survivors who returned.
- **Expellee towns in Bavaria and Upper Austria:** check founding-day customs of towns built after 1945.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for calendar customs, origin stories and refugee history; thin for personal fresh starts after divorce, debt or addiction, which sit in counseling and court files.
