## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyroleans and Old Bavarians on 1809:** check how the same war is told as heroism on one side and rebellion on the other.
- **Vienna's February 1934 fighters and their opponents:** check how workers' and *Heimwehr* courage are remembered by party and district.
- **Carinthian Slovene partisans against the plebiscite-defense memory:** check two rival courage stories in one Land.
- **Mountain villages and cities:** check whether rescue volunteering and Alpine danger shape a different ideal from urban civil courage.
- **Women:** check whether endurance in war, farm work and rebuilding is named courage, and by whom.
- **Roma and Sinti survivors and their descendants:** check testimony as courage, and who heard it first.
- **Jewish Austrians and Bavarians:** check resistance, rescue and exile stories in the Jewish museums' oral archives.
- **Bosnian-Austrians:** check memory of the 1992-95 war and of the courage to flee or to stay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in resistance archives, war memory and Alpine literature; thin for everyday moral courage outside dramatic events.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the epic; Slovene for partisan testimony that was never translated; Romani and German for Roma survivor interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Wehrmacht service and veterans' memorials (the Ulrichsberg gathering); Harrer's and other climbers' Nazi ties; trauma in survivor testimony; Turkish-siege commemorations.
- **Held for review:** none; mountaineering pointers were kept to Alpine climbs so that nothing on the courage list needed holding.
