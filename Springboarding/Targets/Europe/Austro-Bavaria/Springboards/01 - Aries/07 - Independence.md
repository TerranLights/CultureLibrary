## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain-farm households (Tyrol, Salzburg, Upper Styria, Bavarian Alps) against lowland grain and wine farms:** check self-provisioning against market and subsidy dependence.
- **Burgenland's weekly commuters to Vienna:** check how dependence on distant work fits ideas of standing on one's own.
- **Vienna's single-person households against multigenerational rural houses:** check living alone as achievement or as loneliness.
- **Women in farm, trade and city households:** check whether a woman's own income or a *Sennerin*'s summer counted as independence.
- **Elders in home care:** check how families and carers describe "staying independent", and who the carers are.
- **Bosnian- and Turkish-Austrians and Bavarians:** check family support and remittances against self-reliance ideals, by generation.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check self-employment and independence from welfare authorities as each group's own associations describe it.
- **Vorarlberg and Bavarian separatist voters:** check regional independence as practical politics or as sentiment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for farm economics, self-employment and welfare; mixed for emotional independence and help-seeking.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for *Ruodlieb*; Slovak and Romanian for carers' own accounts (comparison material); Bosnian and Turkish for family-support studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "scrounger" rhetoric against migrants; welfare data on individuals; separatist politics.
