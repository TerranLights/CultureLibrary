## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Viennese *Grant* against Old Bavarian *Grant*:** check whether the same word names the same mood in the city and the countryside.
- **Upper Palatinate against Franconia and Old Bavaria:** check who joined the Wackersdorf protests from each region, and how each remembers them.
- **Tyrol, Upper Austria and Old Bavaria:** check how each region's peasant-war memory turns past anger into present identity.
- **Men and women:** check who may shout at home, in the inn, on the field, and whose anger is called hysteria.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and German-speaking Carinthians:** check grievance over the plebiscite and the partisan years on each side.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check public anger after 1995 and over Nazi-era compensation.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check anger at discrimination as voiced by the communities' own organizations and media.
- **Jewish communities:** check responses to antisemitic incidents and to the Waldheim affair.
