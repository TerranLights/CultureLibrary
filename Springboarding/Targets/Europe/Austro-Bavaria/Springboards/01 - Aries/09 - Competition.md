## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Alpine Länder against Vienna:** check whether ski racing or football carries more weight in local pride.
- **Rupertiwinkel and Flachgau:** check whether shared whip-cracking contests bind or divide the two sides of the border.
- **Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check sports and school rivalry inside the Free State, as each side tells it.
- **Gailtal Slovenes and German-speaking neighbors:** check who takes part in the ring-tilting today, and in which language its songs are sung.
- **Burgenland Croats and Hungarians:** check village clubs and festival contests as places where language groups meet or stay apart.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check club founding, league access and conflict at matches.
- **Girls and women:** check entry into shooting clubs, Ranggeln, bands and fire-brigade competitions, and when it opened.
- **Roma and Sinti:** sources on sport and contests are thin; ask the associations which clubs and music competitions matter.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, clubs and school data; thin on envy, which is talked about more than measured.
- **Languages needed:** Gailtal Slovene for the ring-tilting songs; Early New High German for the *Freydal* captions; Turkish for club histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** football violence and far-right fan groups; school tracking and migrant children; animal welfare at horse contests.
