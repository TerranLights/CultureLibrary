## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Alpine resort valleys (Arlberg, Ötztal, Werdenfels):** check accident files that separate locals from guests before calling either reckless.
- **Rural districts and Vienna:** check young drivers' crash data against public-transport city life, district by district, not by reputation.
- **Old Bavaria and Upper Austria:** check whether the poacher is remembered as a hero, a criminal or both, and by whom.
- **Salzburg's extreme-sport scene:** check the brand's athletes against local mountain guides' views of acceptable risk.
- **Carinthia and Munich:** check how the Hypo and Wirecard collapses were judged locally, as greed, as daring or as betrayal.
- **Men and women:** check who takes which risks (speed, alcohol, mountains), and how each is talked about.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check whether risk campaigns and addiction services reach these communities in their own languages.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check dialect sayings and folk tales about the rash, collected in their own languages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for accidents, Alpine risk and gambling; mixed for everyday attitudes to risk.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German and Medieval Latin for the two pre-modern pointers; dialect for *Marterl* verses and poacher songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** individual accident and suicide cases; ongoing legal matters around bank collapses (law to Libra and Capricorn); addiction data.
- **Held for review:** none; the risk, gambling and speculation pointers all stand.
