## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol's closed farms against partible-inheritance areas (Vorarlberg, Lower Franconia):** check whether one heir or many shaped what families held and passed on; the legal side goes to Libra.
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Old Bavaria:** check church teaching on thrift, almsgiving and display against each district's spending and giving.
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel against the Weinviertel:** check small-holder linen and potato economies against wine-cellar lanes as stored wealth.
- **Vienna's municipal tenants against rural owners:** check what "one's own" means when the home is rented for generations.
- **Danube Swabians and Sudeten German descendants:** check the objects carried out in 1945-1946 and the stories told about them.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check money sent home and houses built in the old village.
- **Yenish and Roma:** check how itinerant trades and portable goods were reckoned as wealth; sources are thin and community consent matters.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna, Franconia and Hohenems:** check family memory of seized goods against restitution files.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for budgets, probate, cadastres and folk objects; mixed for Bavaria-only splits in German surveys. The pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria; the nation's exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent and Latin for *Urbare* and probate; dialect for folk-object labels; Croatian and Hungarian for Burgenland emigrant letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aryanization and restitution files; personal data in recent probate; Roma and Yenish family economics.
- **Held for review:** none; the probate, budget and folk-object pointers raise nothing under the standing rules.
