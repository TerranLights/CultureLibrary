# Austro-Bavaria: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jewish families of Vienna, Munich and Franconia:** check how seized objects, restituted objects and objects never found figure in family memory.
- **Sudeten German descendants and Danube Swabians:** check which carried objects became family relics, and whether the young keep them.
- **Ukrainian and Polish communities:** check what 2022 arrivals brought in one bag against what earlier Polish migrants shipped later.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish-Bavarians:** check whether the *çeyiz* is still assembled, by whom, and where it is stored.
- **Catholic Old Bavaria against Lutheran Franconia:** check votive giving and relic veneration against Protestant districts with no such treasuries.
- **Mühlviertel and Waldviertel farmhouses against Vienna's flats:** check how storage space (lofts, sheds, cellar compartments) changes what is kept.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check, only with community guidance, how a dead person's belongings are handled; sources are scarce, so start with Romano Centro and the Kulturverein österreichischer Roma.
- **Viennese Czech heritage:** check family objects kept from Bohemia and Moravia across three or four generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court collections, restitution, museums and folk legends; thin for everyday keeping inside homes, which needs interviews or home-inventory studies.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent and early New High German for court inventories; Czech and Ukrainian for heritage-family accounts; dialect for flea-market and junk vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** looted property and named heirs; Roma death customs; hoarding as a mental-health matter; refugees' losses.
- **Held for review:** none; collections, flea markets and lost-property offices sit within the standing rules.
