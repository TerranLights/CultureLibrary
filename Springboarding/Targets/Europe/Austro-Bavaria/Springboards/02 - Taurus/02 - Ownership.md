# Austro-Bavaria: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German; Bavarian, Tyrolean and Styrian dialect terms of tenure; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech, Turkish. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on (including the left-right reading: the share of output the government spends).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrolean *Erbhof* families against Burgenland *Urbarialgemeinde* members:** check single-family ownership across centuries against joint holding of village commons.
- **Old Bavarian village ranks (*Bauer* to *Inwohner*):** check how much standing went with each grade of ownership, and what survived after 1848.
- **Vienna's private-rental, municipal and co-op tenants:** check whether "my flat" means the same in all three.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check returns and non-returns of farms after 1945, and how the losses are spoken of.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna, Munich and Franconia:** check homes and firms taken in 1938 and how many families came back to claim them.
- **Upper Palatinate and the Bavarian Forest:** check what forest use rights in state forests mean to holders and whether the young keep them.
- **East Tyrol and the Salzburg Pinzgau:** check local feeling about second homes and buyers from abroad.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians:** check the order of buying a home here, a home in the old country, or neither.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tenure history, housing statistics and Aryanization files; mixed for the commons, where much sits in local bodies' own papers.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent for *Grundbuch* and tenure records; Slovene for the 1942 seizures; Latin and Middle High German for monastic grants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aryanization and restitution claims with living heirs; the Tyrolean commons dispute (live and partisan); the law of property and of land transfer belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; every tenure, housing and commons pointer stays on the sheet.
