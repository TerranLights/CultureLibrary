# Austro-Bavaria: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German; Bavarian and Viennese varieties; Romani; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Turkish; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is held to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Title holders against *Hackler*:** check whether academic and official titles and hard manual work serve as rival grounds of worth, by Land and generation.
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Old Bavaria:** check sermons and confirmation teaching on worth from grace, works or humility.
- **Upper Styria's steel towns (Eisenerz, Mur-Mürz) and the Upper Palatinate after the Maxhütte closure:** check how men and families spoke of worth when the works shut.
- **Vienna against the rural Länder:** check the city's words for country people and the countryside's answers.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker generation and their children:** check whether *obraz* and pay carry the same weight across generations.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish-Bavarians:** check how *şeref* and *özgüven* are used by young people compared with parents.
- **Roma, Sinti and Yenish:** check self-description after centuries of stigma; Yenish sources are thin, so start with German-language oral history and Austrian Roma and Sinti associations rather than material from neighbors.
- **People with disabilities:** check what self-advocates say restored their standing after the eugenic past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology, honors, literature and suicide statistics; mixed for everyday worth talk, which needs dialect sources and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for executioner and guild records; Romani and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian for community testimonies; Turkish for youth media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data; Nazi euthanasia victims and their families; honor-related violence in any group (report only with care); stigma of Roma and Yenish.
- **Held for review:** none; the Adlerian, honor-code and state-honors pointers fit the rules as written.
