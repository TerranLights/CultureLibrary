## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Those with no religious affiliation against church members:** check value rankings by confession, and how large the non-affiliated share has grown in Vienna and Munich.
- **The Upper Palatinate and the Lower Austrian Danube floodplain:** check how Wackersdorf and Hainburg are remembered locally, a generation on.
- **Vorarlberg against Burgenland:** check referendum and initiative turnout and wording at the two ends of Austria.
- **Muslim Austrians and Bavarians (Bosnian, Turkish, Chechen and Arab-heritage):** check what the IGGÖ and mosque associations teach against what youth say in their own media.
- **Serbian Orthodox and Polish Catholic parishes in Vienna:** check how migrant congregations rank family, faith and work in their teaching.
- **Burgenland Croats and Carinthian Slovenes:** check whether minority-language catechism and choirs carry different moral vocabulary from German-language teaching.
- **Jewish communities (IKG Wien; Munich's community):** check what community education names as core teachings, after rebuilding since 1945.
- **The 1968 generation against their grandchildren:** check survey change by birth cohort, not by labels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, referendums and church documents; mixed for small faith communities, which mostly publish in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German and Latin for Walther and the *Ruodlieb*; Bosnian and Turkish for Muslim community teaching; Polish and Serbian for parish materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *Leitkultur* and integration debates are partisan; avoid reading any one party's list as the culture's own; the legal letter of integration rules goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; survey, church and referendum pointers were all kept.
