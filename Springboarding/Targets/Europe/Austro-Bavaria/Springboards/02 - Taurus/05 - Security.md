# Austro-Bavaria: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and German as used in Bavaria (they differ on pensions and civil service); Bavarian dialect; Hungarian, Burgenland Croatian, Czech; Ukrainian and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian for newer and guest-worker communities. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tenured civil servants against seasonal tourism workers in Tyrol and Salzburg:** check how certainty of income shapes plans for housing and family.
- **Alpine valleys exposed to avalanches (Paznaun, East Tyrol) against Danube and Inn floodplains:** check the local vocabulary and rituals of natural risk.
- **Weinviertel and Burgenland vineyard districts:** check hail and drought insurance uptake and older weather customs side by side.
- **Mühlviertel and Waldviertel along the former Iron Curtain:** check how life on a closed border shaped talk of safety and emigration.
- **Serbian-, Croatian- and Bosnian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check pensions earned here and spent in the old country, and where security is felt to lie.
- **Ukrainian refugees since 2022 and earlier Syrian and Afghan arrivals:** check what basic care covers and what people say it leaves open.
- **Pensioners against young renters in Vienna and Munich:** check which generation names housing, which names pensions, as the main fear.
- **Danube Swabian families:** check how the loss of everything in 1944-1946 shaped saving and insuring in the next generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social insurance, disasters and insurance history; mixed for felt security, where Bavarian figures sit inside German totals.
- **Languages needed:** Austrian and German administrative terms (they differ); Kurrent for 1817 plaques and relief records; Ukrainian and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debt and insolvency records; refugees' situations; disaster deaths; the legal letter of tenancy and social insurance goes to Libra.
