## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm households in Old Bavaria and the Innviertel:** check how a daughter-in-law moving onto the farm describes the family's claims on her husband and on her.
- **Upper Palatinate and Lower Bavarian villages against Vienna:** check whether youth associations still police courtship across village lines.
- **Turkish-, Chechen- and Bosnian-Austrians:** check what community counselors and young women themselves report about control by partners and relatives, against majority patterns in the same surveys.
- **Ukrainian and Polish women in Vienna and Munich:** check access to help in their own languages and what they say they faced.
- **Football districts (Hütteldorf, Favoriten, Giesing):** check fans' sense of a neighborhood as their own.
- **Styria and the Bavarian Forest:** check foraging communities' rules about secret spots and newcomers.
- **Burgenland Hungarians and Carinthian Slovenes:** check courtship and marriage customs recorded in their languages, against German-language records.
- **Men and women across generations:** check whether jealousy is read as love more often by the old than by the young.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for partner-violence surveys, counseling reports, medieval and stage literature; thin for everyday sharing and guarding, which needs ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German; dialect for courtship customs; Turkish, Chechen, Bosnian and Ukrainian for counseling materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** femicide and domestic violence; any reading of a minority as more controlling must come from data, not assumption; stalking law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the epic, courtship-custom and counseling pointers all remain.
