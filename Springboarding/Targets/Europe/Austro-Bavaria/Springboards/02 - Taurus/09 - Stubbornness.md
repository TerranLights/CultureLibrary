## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol against Old Bavaria:** check how 1705 and 1809 are taught and celebrated on each side, now that both lie in one nation.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and German-speaking Carinthians:** check how each side tells the place-sign years, and what each counts as having given way.
- **Upper Austria's Innviertel (Jägerstätter's home):** check how the village's view of its refuser changed from the 1940s to the beatification.
- **Franconia within Bavaria:** check how Franconians speak of being governed from Munich since 1806, and of their own persistence.
- **Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians; Turkish-Austrians:** check how *inat* is used at home and in jokes, and whether the young use it at all.
- **Viennese Czech heritage:** check what kept Czech schools and clubs going across a century of pressure to assimilate.
- **Older and younger people:** check reasons given for keeping cash, old phones and old habits, in surveys split by age.
- **Districts with low and high vaccine uptake in 2021-2022:** check how refusers and their neighbors describe holding firm; avoid labeling regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revolts, saints, political disputes and broadcast archives; thin for private stubbornness, which surfaces in mediation and family interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for 1626 and 1705 sources; Slovene for the place-sign years; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian and Turkish for *inat* in daily speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Waldheim affair and the victim thesis; the place-sign conflict; vaccine refusal; regional self-images of obstinacy are a stereotype risk, so cite them as self-descriptions to test.
- **Held for review:** none; revolt, refusal and mediation pointers needed nothing withheld.
