## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic baroque districts against Lutheran Franconia:** check how worship uses or refuses incense, images and music, parish by parish.
- **Wine country (Wachau, Styria, Lower Franconia) against beer country (Old Bavaria, Upper Austria):** check the seasonal rhythm and setting of drinking in each.
- **Bavarian Swabia and Kneipp spas against Alpine thermal resorts:** check whether cure-taking is described as pleasure, duty or medicine.
- **Jewish Vienna, before and after 1938:** check food memory and café life in memoirs of those who left and those who returned.
- **Bosnian- and Turkish-Austrians:** check *ćeif* and *keyif* as words for leisure, and the hammam and coffee rituals carried over.
- **Burgenland Croats and Burgenland Hungarians:** check feast foods and dance music in their own languages, against German-speaking neighbors.
- **Newcomers and long-time residents:** check how people from elsewhere describe mixed nude saunas and nudist lakes, and how locals explain them.
- **Old and young:** check whether Sunday roast and the *Jause* hold their place against delivery meals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, drink, spas, church art and music; mixed for touch and personal space, which need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Medieval Latin and Middle High German for the *Codex Buranus*; Burgenland Croatian for song and feast; Turkish and Bosnian for leisure words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol harm; nudity norms; Jewish food memory tied to persecution; *Gemütlichkeit* is a national cliché, so cite it only as a self-description to test.
- **Held for review:** none; song, spa, cookbook and tavern pointers are all retained.
