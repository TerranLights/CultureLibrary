## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vorarlberg against the Bavarian-speaking Länder:** check how Alemannic speech west of the Arlberg shapes schooling and everyday talk.
- **Vienna against rural Lower Austria and the Weinviertel:** check when speakers switch between dialect and standard.
- **Franconia and Bavarian Swabia within Bavaria:** check how the Bavarian school standard sits with non-Bavarian dialect speakers.
- **Carinthian Slovenes, Burgenland Croats and Burgenland Hungarians:** check home transmission of the minority language against school registration figures.
- **Deaf signers of ÖGS and of DGS in Bavaria:** check how Deaf schooling, interpreting and broadcasting differ across the border.
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage families:** check the number of generations over which the home language held.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check uptake of heritage-language classes across generations.
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel households:** check long weekly commutes to Vienna and Linz as the shape of "short" journeys there.
