## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jewish communities of Vienna, Fürth and the Upper Palatinate:** check how community printing and bulletins carried information within and between them, before and after 1938.
- **Burgenland Hungarians and Croats:** check which news channels served their villages before and after the 1921 transfer to Austria.
- **East Tyrol and the high valleys:** check reliance on parish bulletins and notice boards against broadband and mobile coverage.
- **Vienna against the Mühlviertel:** check the reach of the national tabloid against regional free weeklies.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish-Bavarians:** check how far satellite and online news from Turkey sits beside local news, and whom each is trusted on.
- **Serbian-Austrians:** check the same question for Belgrade broadcasters and Vienna's community press.
- **Deaf ÖGS signers:** check access to emergency and daily news in sign language.
- **Old Bavarian and Upper Austrian parishes:** check pulpit announcements (*Verlautbarungen*) as a channel for local information.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history, censorship and media surveys; mixed for Bavaria-only figures inside German surveys; thin for Romani-language news.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German and Kurrent for newsletters and broadsides; Hebrew for the Sulzbach and Fürth presses; Turkish and BCS for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state-advertising disputes and ongoing inquiries; personal data in police and census records; Nazi-era propaganda archives.
- **Held for review:** none; the press, archive and statistics pointers here need no setting aside.
