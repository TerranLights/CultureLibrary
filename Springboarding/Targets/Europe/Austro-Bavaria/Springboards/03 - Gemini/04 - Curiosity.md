## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Styria's monastic towns (Admont and its neighbors):** check whether monastic collecting shaped local museums and nature clubs.
- **Munich against rural Lower Bavaria:** check the gap in museum and science-event attendance.
- **Danube Swabian descendants:** check genealogy and trips to ancestral villages as curiosity about origins.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian families:** check which personal questions (marriage, income, children) count as warm interest or as intrusion across generations.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check the record of being objects of Nazi-era "research" and present-day rules on consent before study.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna:** check the museum anthropology of 1938-1945 aimed at them and the responses to it now.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check who collected their folklore, outsiders or their own collectors, and with what aims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collecting, museums and exploration history; mixed for everyday nosiness and its limits; thin for minority attitudes to asking.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Early New High German for Paracelsus and monastic texts; Portuguese for Brazil-side views of the Spix and Martius collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human remains and taken children in collections; Nazi-era racial studies; press intrusion cases involving living victims.
- **Held for review:** none; the exploration, museum and survey pointers stay in under the standing rules.
