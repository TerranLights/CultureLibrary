# Austro-Bavaria: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German; Bavarian, Viennese and Alemannic varieties; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani, Czech, Slovak, Polish; Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna's Polish community:** check whether Polish parishes act as job and housing networks, for older and post-2004 arrivals alike.
- **Burgenland Roma and Lovara families:** check *kirvo* ties and kin networks across borders; sources are thin, so start with community associations.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish-Bavarians:** check how *kirve* and *torpil* sit beside local godparenthood and *Vitamin B*.
- **Bosnian-Austrians:** check hometown associations as channels between Vienna and the home municipality.
- **Weinviertel and Waldviertel villages:** check whether club life and the inn survive out-migration.
- **Upper Palatinate border villages:** check ties reopened with Bohemia after 1989.
- **Vorarlberg:** check how commuting to Switzerland and Liechtenstein shapes everyday networks.
- **Danube Swabian descendants in Upper Austria:** check whether their associations still bind the third generation.
