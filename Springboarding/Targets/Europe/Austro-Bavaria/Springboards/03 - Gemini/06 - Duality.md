*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Augsburg and other bi-confessional towns of Bavarian Swabia:** check how parity offices and Augsburg's own Peace Festival hold two confessions side by side.
- **Carinthian Slovenes of the Gail and Jaun valleys:** check how in-between labels are taken up or refused, generation by generation.
- **Burgenland Hungarians:** check whether younger speakers describe a two-language life as gain or as burden.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check two-country lives: houses built at home, retirements split between Vienna and the village.
- **Turkish-Bavarians:** check *Almancı* as a label from the home side, and replies to it.
- **East Tyrol:** check how a Land half cut off from its other half by a foreign border describes itself.
- **Yenish families:** check how settled and traveling phases of a life are named and valued; sources are scarce, so begin with Yenish associations.

## 4. Flags
