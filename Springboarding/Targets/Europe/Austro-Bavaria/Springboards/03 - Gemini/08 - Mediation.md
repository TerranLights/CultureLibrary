*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lovara and Burgenland Roma:** check whether elders or a *kris* settle disputes, or outside agencies.
- **Turkish-Austrian and Turkish-Bavarian families:** check the *dünürcü* and older relatives as go-betweens in marriage and quarrels.
- **Bosnian-Austrians:** check imams and mosque boards as mediators beside secular counselors.
- **Carinthian Slovene and German-speaking villages:** check who brokered between them before the Consensus Group: priests, teachers, mayors.
- **Vienna's municipal tenants:** check whether quarrels go to Wohnpartner, the caretaker or no one.
- **Polish and Ukrainian parishes:** check priests as go-betweens with Austrian offices for newcomers.
- **Old Bavarian villages:** check what remains of the *Hochzeitlader*'s role at weddings.
- **Deaf ÖGS and DGS signers:** check interpreters as go-betweens in family, school and medical settings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal mediation services and diplomacy; mixed for intercession in devotion; thin for Roma and migrant-family go-betweens.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish and Latin for the Oriental Academy; Slovene for the Consensus Group's critics; Romani for *kris* material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** offender files; domestic violence where mediation is unsafe; minority-politics disputes still live in Carinthia.
- **Held for review:** none; the go-between, peace-center and devotional pointers are clear of the standing rules.
