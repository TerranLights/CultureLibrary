*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German; Bavarian, Viennese and Alemannic varieties; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani, Czech, Slovak; Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Hebrew for Jewish teaching; ÖGS. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Carinthian Slovene villages:** check how informing before the 1942 deportations is remembered, and how village talk treated returnees.
- **Viennese courtyard houses and the Waldviertel:** check how fast talk spreads and whom people name as its carriers.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian communities:** check reputational talk and young people's choices, through community counselors' own accounts.
- **Vienna's Jewish community:** check how teaching on *lashon hara* enters community education.
- **Yenish families:** check rumors about travelers in settled villages and the answers to them; sources are few, so start with Yenish associations.
- **Styrian Erzberg mining towns (Eisenerz, Leoben):** check company-town talk and its effect on standing at work.
- **Expellee newcomers in Upper Austrian villages after 1945:** check rumors about them and how they faded.
- **Deaf clubs of Vienna and Munich:** check how news and rumor move in a small signing community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, press and Nazi-era files; mixed for everyday gossip (few recorded studies); thin for Romani and Yenish material.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent for police and Gestapo files; dialect for Weinheber and folk plays; Slovene for village memory of 1942.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** denunciation files naming families; living people in society columns; minors in cyberbullying data.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing among the press, play and archive pointers calls for setting aside.
