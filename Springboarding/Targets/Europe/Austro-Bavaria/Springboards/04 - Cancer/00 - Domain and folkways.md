## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel against Vienna:** check household size and co-residing generations in farm districts beside the capital's one-person households.
- **Vorarlberg's Rhine valley against Old Bavaria:** check how divided inheritance in the west and single-heir farms in the east shaped who stayed in the parental house.
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Upper Bavaria:** check whether house-father manuals, home devotions and the *Herrgottswinkel* differ by confession.
- **Burgenland Croat and Hungarian villages:** check house forms (the long *Streckhof*) and minority-language kin words in village histories.
- **Danube Swabian and Sudeten German families:** check how a home was rebuilt after expulsion, in their associations' memory books.
- **Filipino care-worker families in Vienna:** check how a household split between two countries is kept up, in the community's own parish and association sources.
- **Slovak and Romanian 24-hour carers:** check how the carers themselves describe living inside an employer's home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household history, vernacular building and family statistics; mixed for everyday home life; thin for Romani, Yenish and Chechen-Austrian family accounts in their own words. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Kurrent for soul registers; Early New High German for Hohberg; Tagalog for care-worker community writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** records of families persecuted in 1938-1945; living persons in parish lists; inheritance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the household, house and family-memory pointers raise no question under the standing rules.
