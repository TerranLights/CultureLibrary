## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Palatinate and Lower Bavarian farm kin:** check how far *Freindschaft* reaches in local speech and at funerals.
- **Burgenland Croat villages:** check whether *kum* and *kuma* still carry duties past baptism and weddings, in Croatian-language parish sources.
- **Carinthian Slovene families:** check kin kept across the 1920 border in family memoirs and Slovene press.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check whether family feasts and sponsorship pass to the second generation, in community associations' own media.
- **Polish community in Vienna:** check parish and association material for how families are linked back to Poland.
- **Foster and SOS-village families:** check how grown children of these families describe "my family".
- **Queer households in Vienna and Munich:** check chosen-family terms in community archives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family history and survey data; mixed for kin terms in daily use; thin for Romani kin structures told by Roma, and for Chechen-Austrian families.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for *Kudrun*; Serbian (Cyrillic) for parish material; dialect for kin words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era descent records; foster-care files; same-sex family histories involving living persons.
- **Held for review:** none; no kin, foster or genealogy pointer had to be held.
