*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German, Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, Alemannic (Vorarlberg), East Franconian; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani and Sinti varieties, Czech, Ukrainian; Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian, Chechen (community sources). **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Commuter-belt villages around Munich and Salzburg:** check how *Zuagroaste* and old families describe each other in parish and club newsletters.
- **Burgenland Croats and Hungarians:** check whether belonging to Burgenland and to their own group is phrased as one or two things.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check plebiscite-day speeches and Slovene press side by side, year by year.
- **Sinti in Bavaria and Roma in Burgenland:** check self-representation through their own associations first.
- **Danube Swabians of Upper Austria:** check how memory books count generations until "local".
- **Ukrainian refugees since 2022:** check early first-person accounts in Ukrainian community channels and NGO reports.
- **Chechen-Austrians:** check community associations' own statements; little exists in print, so look to NGO reports and interviews.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for integration surveys and border-plebiscite history; mixed for felt belonging; thin for Chechen-Austrian and Sinti self-accounts in print.
- **Languages needed:** Slovene for the plebiscite debate; Hungarian for the Sopron vote; Ukrainian and Chechen for refugee voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** plebiscite commemoration is politically charged; refugee testimonies involve living persons; citizenship rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the integration, minority and plebiscite pointers needed no holding.
