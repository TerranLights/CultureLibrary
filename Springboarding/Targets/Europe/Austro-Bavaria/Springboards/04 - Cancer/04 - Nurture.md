# Austro-Bavaria: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German, Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, East Franconian, Alemannic (Vorarlberg); Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani; Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Latin for medieval household writing. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna against rural Bavaria:** check how free city kindergarten places and Bavaria's home-care payments (*Familiengeld*) shape who does early nurture.
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Old Bavaria:** check whether church teaching on raising children differs in parish newsletters and confirmation material.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check whether early care in the minority language is described as nurture or as rescue of a language.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian parents:** check their own associations' parenting courses and community media, not outside commentary.
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel grandparents:** check how much daily child-raising falls to them when parents commute.
- **Roma families:** check Roma associations' own learning-support reports; academic material is thin.
- **Danube Swabian and Sudeten German families of the postwar years:** check memoirs of children raised in camps and emergency settlements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for early-education statistics and childrearing manuals; mixed for everyday practice; thin for minority-language lullaby collections outside Slovene.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Megenberg; Middle High German for Berthold; Slovene for the Urban Jarnik archive; Turkish and BCS for parent courses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Islamic-kindergarten debate is polarized; children's institutional records; Nazi-era manuals need context.
- **Held for review:** none; the manual, curriculum and kindergarten pointers raise no question under the rules.
