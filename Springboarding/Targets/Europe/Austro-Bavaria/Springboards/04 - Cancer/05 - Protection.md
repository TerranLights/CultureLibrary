## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Styrian and southern Burgenland border villages:** check whether *Tabor* and raid memory still appear in local festivals and school chronicles.
- **Upper Palatinate along the former Iron Curtain:** check how border guarding and its end (1989) are told in village histories.
- **Children placed in Viennese and Tyrolean homes (1950s-1980s):** check survivors' own testimony collected by redress commissions.
- **Bosnian-Austrians who came as war refugees (1992-1995):** check how temporary protection was lived, in memoirs and association sources.
- **Chechen-Austrians:** check how community associations describe the protection they sought after flight (asylum, family reunification, fear of return); little is published, so begin with NGO interviews.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check prayer and blessing traditions of protection in Slovene parish books.
- **Hungarian refugees of 1956 and their children:** check memory accounts of being taken in.

## 4. Flags
