# Austro-Bavaria: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German, Bavarian (Boarisch), Tyrolean, Viennese, East Franconian; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani and Sinti varieties, Yenish; Polish, Romanian, Slovak, Dari (refugee sources); Yiddish and Hebrew for postwar camp records. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrolean Lower Inn valley villages:** check whether *Anklöpfeln* singers today ask for lodging or only sing it, from the groups' own records.
- **Franconian convents and Old Bavarian parishes:** check how often and why each gives church sanctuary.
- **Wolfratshausen-Waldram residents:** check how the former DP camp's later expellee families remember the site's first residents.
- **Danube Swabians of Upper Austria:** check memoirs of years spent in barracks camps.
- **Sinti and Yenish traveling families:** check the shortage of transit sites in their associations' statements; sources are few.
- **EU-citizen rough sleepers from Poland, Romania and Slovakia:** check outreach reports for which shelters admit them.
- **Afghan-Austrians who arrived as unaccompanied minors:** check their own accounts of youth shelters, through NGOs and community media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for homeless services and postwar camps; mixed for customs of giving lodging today; thin for Yenish and Sinti transit-site needs.
- **Languages needed:** Yiddish and Polish for DP records; Dari for young refugees' accounts; Latin for hospice charters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter residents' privacy; sanctuary cases with living persons; hidden-survivor testimonies.
- **Held for review:** none; the hospice, sanctuary and camp pointers raised nothing under the standing rules.
