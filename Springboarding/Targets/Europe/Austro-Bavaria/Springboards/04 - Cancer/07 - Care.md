# Austro-Bavaria: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German, Bavarian (Boarisch), Alemannic (Vorarlberg), East Franconian; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani; Tagalog, Polish, Ukrainian, Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Old High and Middle High German for early remedy books. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Upper Austria:** check whether deaconess and convent traditions still shape who runs local care services.
- **Vorarlberg's home-nursing associations:** check membership figures and who volunteers, set against eastern Länder.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian elders of the first guest-worker generation:** check community associations and providers of culturally sensitive care for how families and services share the work.
- **Filipino-Austrian nurses and their families:** check how the carers are themselves cared for in old age.
- **Polish and Ukrainian carers in Bavaria:** check their own online groups for working conditions and care at a distance.
- **Carinthian Slovene and Burgenland Croatian elders:** check whether care in their language is available in homes.
- **Roma elders in Burgenland:** check Roma associations' statements on care; published material is scarce.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for care statistics, nursing history and hospice; mixed for felt duty to care; thin for Roma and Chechen-Austrian family care in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for remedy books; Tagalog and Ukrainian for carers' accounts; Turkish for elder-care associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patients' records; care-home inquiries involving living persons; disability history touches Nazi murders.
- **Held for review:** none; nursing, hospice and land-care pointers stay as written.
