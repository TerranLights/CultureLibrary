## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Upper Bavaria and Lutheran Middle and Upper Franconia:** check whether the festive year (saints' days, church fairs, carnival) differs by confession, since confession set the calendar.
- **Vienna's Prater against Mühlviertel and Waldviertel village fairs:** check who runs amusement, commercial showmen or village associations.
- **Carnival districts (Ausseerland, Werdenfels, Imst) against places with no masked carnival:** check local customs registers before treating masking as general.
- **Burgenland Croats and Burgenland Hungarians:** check their cultural societies' yearbooks for their own festive calendars.
- **Yenish and Sinti showpeople (*Schausteller*):** check how traveling fairground families describe their trade; own-voice sources are thin, so start with showmen's associations and oral history.
- **Danube Swabians in Upper Austria:** check the *Kirchweih* customs their associations carried from the Banat and Batschka.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check wedding halls and association festivals as their own celebration spaces, in community media.
