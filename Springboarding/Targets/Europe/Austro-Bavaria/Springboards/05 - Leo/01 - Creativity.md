## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Franconia's porcelain and basket towns (Selb, Michelau, Lichtenfels):** check whether industrial design and village basketry are told as craft, art or labor, since both draw on the same district's skill.
- **Bavarian Forest glassmaking (Zwiesel, Frauenau):** check whether glass is valued for faithful technique or for the single designer's name.
- **Oberammergau and Hallein carving schools against Tyrolean home carvers:** check what the schools license as correct form and what home makers improvise.
- **Viennese Czech tailors, cabinetmakers and craftsmen of the 1900s:** check their trade associations' records for how a minority craft tradition entered Vienna's applied arts.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check embroidery, church-painting and song collections for who may vary a pattern and who keeps it.
- **Yenish basket-makers and grinders:** check what the Yenish say about traveling trades; their associations' sources are thin, so look to oral history.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian makers in Vienna and Munich:** check community media and migrant-collection catalogs for craft and design kept or reinvented after migration.
