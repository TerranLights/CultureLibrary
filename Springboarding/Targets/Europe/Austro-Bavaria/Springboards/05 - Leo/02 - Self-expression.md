# Austro-Bavaria: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German, the German of Bavaria, Bavarian and Viennese dialect, Alemannic; Carinthian Slovene; Burgenland Croatian and Hungarian; Romani; Turkish; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inner Vienna against the Weinviertel and the Waldviertel:** check value-survey items on self-expression by settlement size, since the city-country gap may outweigh Land differences.
- **Lower Bavaria and the Upper Palatinate against Munich:** check whether Tracht at church and festivals is daily dress, costume or statement in each.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check when each chose to show or hide its language in public, using their own press (*Novice*, *Hrvatske novine*).
- **Turkish-Austrians, including Alevi and Sunni communities, and Bosnian-Austrians:** check community media for how dress and music are discussed inside each, without assuming one pattern.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna and Munich:** check community surveys on wearing visible religious signs in public, and the reasons given.
- **Ukrainian arrivals since 2022 and the older Polish community:** check how newcomers describe showing or masking origin in daily life.
- **Vorarlberg against the eastern Länder:** check Alemannic sources for plain-speech norms that differ from Viennese indirection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dress, music, protest and Pride; mixed for everyday emotional display; thin for Yenish and Roma first-person sources and for Burgenland Hungarian youth.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin and Middle High German for the *Carmina Burana*; Slovene and Burgenland Croatian press; Turkish and Ukrainian community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-minority violence around language signs; faith dress as a political flashpoint; private diaries of living or recently dead writers.
- **Held for review:** none; every voice, dress and protest source above could be named in full.
