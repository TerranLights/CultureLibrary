# Austro-Bavaria: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian and Viennese dialect; Tyrolean; Carinthian Slovene; Burgenland Croatian and Hungarian; Romani; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Turkish. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna against Upper Austria and Lower Bavaria:** check whether school bullying rates and mocking customs differ by urban and rural district in the HBSC tables.
- **Yenish families of Tyrol:** check what slurs such as *Karrner* did to them, in their own oral histories; sources are few, so begin with Tyrolean regional archives and Yenish associations.
- **Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check how slurs at school and work are described in their own memoirs and community media.
- **Roma of Burgenland and Sinti in Bavaria:** check incident reports against their own associations' accounts.
- **Jewish families of Vienna:** check survivors' memoirs of March 1938 for public degradation and its later telling.
- **LGBTQ youth in Styria and Upper Franconia:** check counseling and youth-service data on mockery at school.
- **Recruits from all Länder:** check the armed-forces commission's yearly cases by unit and region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, Nazi-era degradation and school bullying; mixed for workplace humiliation; thin for Yenish experience and for men's humiliation in families.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Viennese German for town punishments; Romani and BCS for community testimony; Hungarian for Burgenland reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' testimony; slurs must be quoted only as search terms; living people in harassment cases; minors in bullying data.
- **Held for review:** none; the shaming, satire and harassment sources were all fit to list.
