*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian and Viennese dialect; Burgenland Hungarian and Croatian; Carinthian Slovene; Romani; Turkish; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian; Ukrainian; Polish. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Old Bavaria and Lutheran Middle Franconia:** check parish collection records and church papers for how giving is taught and asked for under each confession.
- **Burgenland villages on the Hungarian border:** check local memory of 1956 and of 1989 for how help to refugees was organized.
- **Turkish-Austrian and Bosnian mosque associations:** check their own reports on Ramadan meals and alms collections, and who receives them.
- **Serbian- and Bosnian-Austrian hometown associations:** check what they fund in villages of origin, and how donors are named.
- **Jewish community of Vienna:** check how prewar endowments were lost and which were restored, from community and restitution records.
- **Polish and Ukrainian parishes in Vienna and Munich:** check their collections and aid drives since 2022.
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel farms against Vienna:** check whether informal giving (food, labor, lending) shows up in any survey, since formal data may miss it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval charters, foundations and survey-based giving; mixed for tipping and informal help; thin for Roma and Yenish giving customs, where sources are scarce and own-voice accounts should be sought first.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Middle High German for charters and epic; Hungarian for 1956 memories; Turkish and BCS for association reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** donor privacy; Aryanized foundations after 1938; refugees' personal data.
- **Held for review:** none; no gift, endowment or charity source was withheld.

## 5. First queries
1. Search monasterium.net for "Seelgerät" with a house name (Melk, Kremsmünster, Tegernsee) and sort by century.
2. Download the latest *Spendenbericht* and note giving by Land.
3. Search the Austrian National Library catalog for "Kaiser-Jubiläums-Stiftung 1908".
4. Search the Bavarian foundation register for foundations by district and founding decade.
5. Search oral-history collections for "Andau 1956 Flüchtlinge Erinnerung".
