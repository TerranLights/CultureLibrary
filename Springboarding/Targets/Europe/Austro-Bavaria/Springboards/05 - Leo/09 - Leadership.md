# Austro-Bavaria: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian and Tyrolean dialect; Carinthian Slovene; Burgenland Croatian and Hungarian; Romani; Turkish; Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol and the Bavarian highlands:** check how rifle-company captains and mayors overlap, since custom and office may share the same men.
- **Lutheran Franconia against Catholic Old Bavaria:** check church leadership by election (synod, women pastors) beside the Catholic parish-council model.
- **Burgenland and Lower Austria against Tyrol and Vorarlberg:** check women mayors' shares by Land and the reasons given.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check how each minority's associations choose and replace leaders, in their own press.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian associations:** check their bylaws and newsletters for elections and generational change.
- **Roma associations of Burgenland and Vienna:** check how leaders are recognized inside and outside the group; sources are few, so start with the associations themselves.
- **Upper Palatinate and Lower Bavarian family firms:** check succession stories in regional business press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political leaders, the army and associations; mixed for workplace leadership; thin for Roma and Yenish leadership in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for Aventinus and Gaismair; Slovene for the minority bodies; Turkish and Bosnian for community elections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Nazi-era *Führerprinzip* and the word's taboo; party archives with access rules; authority and its legitimacy belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; every leader, office and association named here could be listed openly.
