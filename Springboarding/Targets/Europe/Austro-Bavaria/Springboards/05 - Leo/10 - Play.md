## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol and Old Bavaria against Vienna and Lower Austria:** check where *Watten*, *Schafkopf* and Tarock are played, since card-game borders may mark social regions.
- **Bavarian Swabia and Salzburg:** check puppet traditions and who their audiences are, children or adults.
- **Upper Franconia against Middle Franconia:** check home toymaking against the factory toy town, in museum archives.
- **Burgenland Croat and Carinthian Slovene villages:** check folk-song and rhyme collections for children's games kept in the minority languages.
- **Turkish-Austrian cafés in Vienna and Munich:** check how *okey* and *tavla* evenings are described by players themselves, and who comes.
- **Roma children in Burgenland:** check learning-support and community sources for games in Romani; collections are thin, so look to the Graz Romani material and oral history.
- **Mühlviertel and Waldviertel farm children:** check memoirs and school histories for play squeezed around farm work.

## 4. Flags
