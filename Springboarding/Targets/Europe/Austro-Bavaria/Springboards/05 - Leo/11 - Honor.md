# Austro-Bavaria: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian and Swabian dialect; Early New High German; Carinthian Slovene; Burgenland Croatian and Hungarian; Romani; Turkish; Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Bavarian villages and Bavarian Swabian towns:** check whether village honor (Beck) and guild honor (Augsburg) worked by the same rules, since one is rural and one civic.
- **Vienna's university milieu:** check fraternity records and Jewish students' memoirs for who was granted or refused satisfaction.
- **German-speaking and Slovene Carinthians:** check each side's memorial practice for 1939-1945, in their own press and associations.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians:** check how community writers, counselors and media use their own honor words, since outside reports often flatten them; never assume one pattern.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check Romani-language and association sources for *pativ*, and how speakers relate it to respect from outsiders.
- **Danube Swabian and Sudeten German associations:** check how expellee groups honor their dead and their lost villages.
- **Lutheran Upper Franconia:** check church and civic records for honor and reputation beside Catholic regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court, army, guild and student honor; mixed for village and market honor; thin for Romani and Yenish honor concepts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for *Kudrun*; Early New High German for guild and village records; Romani, Turkish and BCS for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family violence called "honor" must be studied across all groups through counseling and court data, never assigned by origin; veterans' memorials tied to SS units; antisemitic dueling codes.
- **Held for review:** none; the court, guild and fraternity sources needed no setting aside.
