*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian (Boarisch) and Viennese; Alemannic (Vorarlberg), East Franconian, Swabian; Burgenland Croatian, Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Hungarian, Romani (Roman, Lovara, Sinti), Czech, Slovak; Turkish and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: work, service, the daily round, the kept house, craft, rest and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Old Bavaria and Lutheran Middle and Upper Franconia:** check parish calendars for which days were worked, since confession set the holiday count.
- **Alpine farm districts (Tyrol, the Pinzgau, the Upper Bavarian uplands) and the Weinviertel and northern Burgenland:** check farm-history studies for servant systems and harvest labor, which follow the farming type.
- **Salzkammergut salt towns and the Styrian Eisenwurzen:** check miners' association records for inherited trade customs.
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage:** check Czech-language association papers and memoirs on domestic service and brickmaking.
- **Tyrolean and Vorarlberg *Schwabenkinder*:** check their memoirs for how a working life began in childhood.
- **Serbian-, Croatian- and Turkish-Austrians and Bavarians of the guest-worker era:** check oral-history projects for their own accounts of arrival and work.
- **Hungarian and Romanian cross-border commuters and seasonal harvest workers:** compare regional labor data with their own press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for abbey, estate, labor-movement and survey sources; mixed for the rural daily round before 1950; thin for Roma, Sinti and Yenish own voices and for commuters. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria.
- **Languages needed:** Latin, Early New High German and Kurrent for abbey and estate records; Czech for the Viennese workers' press; the Bavarian and Austrian dialect dictionaries for work words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era forced labor (Mauthausen and Dachau quarries; Polish and Ukrainian farm conscripts) and the label *arbeitsscheu* used against Roma, Sinti and others; health microdata.
- **Held for review:** none; no work or health pointer here was set aside.
