## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage:** check their associations' papers for how former maids and cooks described serving, rather than how employers did.
- **Slovak and Romanian live-in carers:** check their own-language forums and the carers' agencies for how they name the job; official data covers only registered workers.
- **Tourism valleys (Zillertal, Pinzgau, Berchtesgaden) against the Waldviertel and the Upper Palatinate:** check whether service work carries different standing where hosting is the main trade.
- **Lutheran Franconia and Catholic Old Bavaria:** compare deaconess and Catholic order records for how vowed service was framed.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check parish and association records for lay service done in their own languages.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check mosque associations' volunteer work and community media.
- **Hungarian and Slovak seasonal workers in ski and spa resorts:** check whether their accounts of guest service appear anywhere outside labor statistics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court records, religious orders and rescue services; mixed for domestic service after 1945; thin for live-in carers' own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Baroque court German and Spanish ceremonial terms for the court records; Czech for the Viennese maids; Slovak and Romanian for carers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names in servants' books; abuse in religious care homes; carers' working conditions.
- **Held for review:** none; no service pointer here was set aside.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Haus-, Hof- und Staatsarchiv finding aids for "Hofstaatsverzeichnis" and "Obersthofmeisteramt".
2. Ask Land museums (Upper Austria, Salzburg, Bavarian district museums) for holdings of "Dienstbotenbuch".
3. Read Glettler, then search the Wienbibliothek for Czech-language association papers on domestic servants.
4. Pull Zivildienstagentur reports for assignments by sector over ten years.
5. Search Slovak-language forums for "opatrovateľka Rakúsko" and note recurring themes.
