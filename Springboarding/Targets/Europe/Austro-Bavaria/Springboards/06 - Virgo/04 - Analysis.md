## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuremberg's reckoning masters (*Rechenmeister*) and Old Bavarian abbey scholars:** check whether commercial reckoning and monastic science trained different kinds of analysts.
- **Jewish Vienna before 1938:** check exile and émigré archives for the analytic schools broken off by persecution; the record lies largely abroad.
- **Women in Viennese science (Lise Meitner, Marietta Blau):** check biographies and university records for who was allowed to do analysis and on what terms.
- **Rural districts (Mühlviertel, East Tyrol, Lower Bavaria) and university towns (Vienna, Graz, Innsbruck, Munich, Erlangen):** check whether *Hausverstand* and expert talk are set against each other in local media.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrian and Bavarian pupils:** check education studies on early school tracking for who reaches the science route.
- **Viennese Czech heritage:** check the Komenský school association's history for science teaching in Czech.
- **Burgenland Hungarians and Croats:** check bilingual school reports for how mathematics and science are taught across languages.
