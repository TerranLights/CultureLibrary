## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna's *Schmäh* and rural *Bauernschläue*:** check dialect dictionaries and local literature for which words mark shrewdness and gullibility in each.
- **Wine districts (Wachau, Weinviertel, northern Burgenland, South Styria) and Franconian wine country:** check wine-school and growers' association materials for how tasting is taught in each.
- **Older people in rural and urban districts:** check police fraud data by age and district for who is targeted by the *Enkeltrick*.
- **Lutheran Franconia and Catholic regions:** check catechisms and confession manuals for how conscience was trained to judge.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check Romano Centro and Sinti associations' own documentation of being judged on sight; sources beyond these are scarce.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check ZARA cases and community media for experiences of prejudgment in housing and hiring.
- **Polish and Ukrainian newcomers:** check their community media for warnings about job-agency and rental scams aimed at new arrivals.
- **Mühlviertel and Bavarian Forest foragers:** check mushroom-advice records for how local knowledge and inspection meet.
