## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna's *raunzen* and Old Bavaria's *granteln*:** check dialect writing and cabaret for whether grumbling is named as a genre in each, and by whom; never assume it describes people.
- **Old Bavarian *Derblecken* and Franconian carnival speeches:** compare who may roast whom, and whether the targets laugh along.
- **Jewish Vienna's interwar cabaret (the Simpl, Fritz Grünbaum, Karl Farkas):** check the Jewish Museum Vienna and cabaret archives for satire ended by 1938.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check their own press for criticism of officials, which may differ from German-language coverage.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check *biber* and community media for criticism of majority society, and for criticism within communities.
- **Upper Bavarian villages with *Haberfeldtreiben* history (Miesbach, Tegernsee valley):** check local memory of shaming against later forms of village criticism.
- **Workplaces in industry against tourism:** check kununu reviews by sector for what employees criticize.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, cabaret, press history and ombudsman records; mixed for family and workplace criticism; thin for Romani and Yenish forms of correction and mockery.
