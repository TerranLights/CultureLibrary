## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria and the Inn and Salzach valleys against Lower Franconia and Bavarian Swabia:** check probate and land records to see how single-heir and divided-inheritance districts treated siblings.
- **Tyrol and East Tyrol:** check court files under the *Höfegesetz* for how payouts to the heirs who leave are fixed.
- **Vienna's tenement districts against the Waldviertel:** check rent-control and housing-allocation rules, where fairness meets scarcity.
- **Lutheran Upper Franconia and the Catholic Upper Palatinate:** check church welfare rules and sermons for different grounds of desert.
- **Danube Swabian resettlers after 1945:** check compensation files under the burden-sharing law (*Lastenausgleich*) in Bavaria and Austrian equivalents, for what counted as a fair share of loss.
- **Polish and Ukrainian workers in Austria and Bavaria today:** check labor-inspectorate and union cases for wage fairness across borders.
- **Same-sex couples:** check inheritance and pension rulings before 2019 for where equal treatment was first written in.
