## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna and Munich against rural Lower Bavaria and the Mühlviertel:** check *Mikrozensus* tables for unmarried couples and late marriage by district.
- **Raiffeisen strongholds in Lower Austria and Styria against urban credit cooperatives:** check how members are bound and how partners exit.
- **Tyrolean and Bavarian Alpine guide families:** check club records for how rope partnerships were formed and passed on.
- **Burgenland Hungarians and Burgenland Croats:** check parish registers for mixed-language couples and the language of the home they chose.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check family-business registers and chamber data for kin-based business partnerships.
- **Same-sex couples:** check registered-partnership data from 2010 and its conversion to marriage after 2019.
- **The Innviertel and the Rupertiwinkel:** check the cross-border bodies, since both sit on the seam the Euregios bridge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, cooperatives and household statistics; mixed for working partnerships outside business law; thin for Yenish and Sinti couple forms in their own words.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** couples' financial and notarial records; the history of criminalized same-sex relations before the reforms of 1969 (West Germany) and 1971 (Austria).
