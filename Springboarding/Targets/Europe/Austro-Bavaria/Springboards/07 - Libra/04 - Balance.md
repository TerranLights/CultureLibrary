## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna as a city-Land against small Länder such as Vorarlberg and Burgenland:** check how far each pushes back on federal power in the *Bundesrat* and in court.
- **Bavarian Swabia and Upper Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check how the old Senate seats and today's district councils balance regions inside Bavaria.
- **Carinthian Slovene and Burgenland Croatian school districts:** check enrollment and curriculum records for how two languages are weighted in practice.
- **Tyrol, East Tyrol and Salzburg's tourism valleys:** check Alpine Convention implementation reports where farming, traffic and visitors pull hardest.
- **Lutheran Middle Franconia against Catholic districts:** check Sunday and holiday customs and shop-hours exemptions for differing calendars of rest.
- **Shift and care workers, many of them Slovak, Romanian and Croatian commuters:** check labor and care-agency data on whose rest pays for others' balance.
- **Orthodox parishes and Muslim communities:** check fasting calendars against the public calendar of rest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional and labor law and survey data; mixed for folk medicine (collected unevenly, often romanticized); thin for minority fasting and rest customs.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for almanacs; Austrian administrative German; Slovak and Romanian for care-worker sources; Slovene for school records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** health records from cure clinics; the 2018 working-time law remains politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none; no source on balance was set aside.
