*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna against the smaller Länder:** check job-posting complaints and audit reports for how patronage differs between a large city administration and small-town offices.
- **Old Bavaria against Lower Franconia:** check district-level quality-of-government scores and local press on how far ties to the governing party matter.
- **Carinthia:** check court and administrative records on bilingual offices, where an official's language can be read as taking a side.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check field-experiment results and equal-treatment complaints for how applicants with their names fare.
- **Women in closed professional bodies (orchestras, the bar, the judiciary):** check admission records over time.
- **Upper Palatinate and the Mühlviertel:** check small-municipality records on recusal where councilors and applicants are often related.
- **Old Catholic and Lutheran minorities in Catholic districts:** check older school and civil-service records for confession as a hidden ground of favor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, rulings, scandals and indices; mixed for everyday favoritism, which surfaces mostly in satire and scandal; thin for minority experience of offices outside the equal-treatment case files.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the *Schwabenspiegel*; Austrian procedural German; Turkish and BCS for community accounts; Romani terms for favor and bias still to be found.
