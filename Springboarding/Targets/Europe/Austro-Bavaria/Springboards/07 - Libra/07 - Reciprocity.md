## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lower Bavaria and the Upper Palatinate against Vienna:** check Maschinenring membership and time-bank records for whether exchange is kin-and-village based or organized among strangers.
- **Salzkammergut and Styrian mining towns:** check *Bruderlade* statutes and accounts for who could claim and on what terms.
- **Burgenland Croats and Burgenland Hungarians:** check thanks formulas and wedding-gift customs in their own collections, since the "God repay it" formula runs through several languages here.
- **Serbian-, Croatian- and Bosnian-Austrians of the guest-worker era:** check remittance data and community surveys for obligations owed to families at home.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Bavarians:** check community associations for whether rotating savings or collective-help customs carried over, and how.
- **Jewish communities in Vienna and Munich:** check burial-society and welfare records for mutual obligation inside the community.
- **Waldviertel and Weinviertel villages:** check harvest and building records for exchange labor before machines.
