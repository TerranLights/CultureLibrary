## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna against Upper Austria and Lower Bavaria:** check permitting times and municipal minutes for how long comparable building decisions take.
- **Munich and the Freising–Erding airport districts:** check local votes and council records, since the runway decision split city and countryside.
- **Carinthia:** check how long the bilingual-sign question stayed undecided in council minutes, as a case of a decision deferred for decades.
- **Families of the Danube Swabian and Sudeten German expellees:** check memoirs for decisions to stay or move on after 1945 and how long the families waited.
- **Polish and Ukrainian workers and refugees:** check community surveys on whether to stay or return, a decision often held open.
- **Same-sex couples:** check how many registered partnerships converted to marriage after 2019 and how quickly, as a measure of choice under new options.
- **Older adults in rural Styria and the Waldviertel:** check advance-directive registrations against Vienna's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for administrative law, election data and infrastructure sagas; mixed for personal indecision, mostly literary; thin for minority decision-making customs in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Early New High German for the imperial registers; Austrian administrative German; Polish and Ukrainian for community surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** advance directives and guardianship files are private; family decisions after expulsion touch trauma.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on indecision was set aside.
