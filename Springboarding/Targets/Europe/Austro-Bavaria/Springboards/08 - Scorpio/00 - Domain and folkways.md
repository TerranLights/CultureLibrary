## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Old Bavaria and Lutheran Middle and Upper Franconia:** check whether All Souls grave visits, memorial cards and requiem masses have Protestant counterparts, and since when.
- **Vienna against the Mühlviertel and the Waldviertel:** check cremation share and the funeral meal in city and farm districts.
- **Bavarian Swabia and Lower Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check handover contracts for whether a farm passed whole or was split among heirs.
- **Burgenland Hungarians of the Reformed and Lutheran parishes in the Oberwart area:** check their own church records for burial and memorial forms.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check grave inscriptions for which language is used at the grave, generation by generation.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check repatriation against local burial over time in their own associations' figures; Orthodox and Muslim burial communities separately.
