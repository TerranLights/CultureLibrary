# Austro-Bavaria: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch) and Viennese idiom; Middle High German for the oldest laments; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani; Turkish and Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how dying, the dead and the meaning of death are understood; grief and mourning included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria and Upper Austria against Lutheran Middle Franconia:** check whether prayer for the poor souls has any Protestant counterpart, since it shapes what the dead are owed.
- **Carinthia, Styria and Vorarlberg:** check suicide figures by district against how local papers report such deaths.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check Slovene-language death notices, grave texts and church songs for grief spoken in the minority language.
- **Muslim families from Turkey and Bosnia:** check hospital and cemetery practice for washing and fast burial, and their own associations' accounts.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check how the dead of the Lackenbach camp and of the camps in Bavaria are mourned, in their own organizations' words.
- **East Tyrol and the Upper Palatinate:** check revenant and omen lore in valley and forest legend collections.
- **Viennese Czech families:** check older Czech-language death notices in Vienna papers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for suicide statistics, hospice and dynastic burial; mixed for folk belief about the dead; thin for grief inside migrant and Romani families.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the *Klage*; Kurrent for legend manuscripts; Slovene, Turkish and Bosnian for minority death notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide (follow media guidelines in any write-up); bereaved parents; Nazi-era deaths of the disabled and of Roma.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on grief or the meaning of death had to be set aside.
