## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol against Vorarlberg and Salzburg:** check how Anabaptist history is remembered locally, since the movement's martyrs and exiles came from these valleys.
- **Viennese Jewish families before 1938 and the community rebuilt after 1945:** check community archives for conversion, return and refounding.
- **Danube Swabians resettled in Upper Austria and Bavaria after 1945:** check their homeland societies' books for the language of a second start.
- **Bosnian-Austrians who arrived as refugees in 1992–95:** check their own associations and memoirs for rebuilding a life.
- **Free churches recognized in Austria and Pentecostal congregations of migrants:** check their testimony meetings for sudden conversion.
- **Salzkammergut and Styrian mining towns:** check how the end of mining and salt work was turned into a new local economy, in local sources.
- **Lutheran Middle Franconia:** check Good Friday and Easter emphasis in Protestant parishes beside Catholic Holy Week.
