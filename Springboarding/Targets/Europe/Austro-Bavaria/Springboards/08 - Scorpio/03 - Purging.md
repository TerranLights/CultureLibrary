# Austro-Bavaria: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch) and East Franconian; Latin and Hebrew for the 1420–21 sources; Early New High German; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani; Turkish and Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check how witch-trial memory is marked locally (plaques, apologies, exhibitions).
- **East Tyrol's Defereggen valley and the Salzburg Pongau:** check family memory of the expelled and of the children kept back.
- **Styria and Carinthia:** check parish and Land records for how the 1599–1600 commissions are recalled in church histories.
- **Sudeten German descendants and Danube Swabians in Upper Austria and Bavaria:** check their own associations' books, read beside Czech and Serbian accounts.
- **Lovara and Burgenland Roma:** check purity terms in each group's own speech; do not carry one group's rules to another.
- **Muslim and Jewish communities of Vienna and Munich:** check ritual washing and the ritual bath in their own community publications.
- **The Weinviertel:** check local press for how Nitsch's work at Prinzendorf was received by neighbors.
