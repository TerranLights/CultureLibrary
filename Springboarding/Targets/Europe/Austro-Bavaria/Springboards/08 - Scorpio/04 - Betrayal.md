## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol against Old Bavaria:** check how the 1809 uprising and its betrayer appear in each side's school books and plays, since Bavaria was the occupier.
- **German-speaking and Slovene-speaking Carinthians:** check plebiscite memory in each language's press for who is called disloyal.
- **Upper Styrian industrial towns and Vienna's working districts:** check union and party papers for the 1950 strike.
- **Viennese Czechs:** check their own newspapers for wartime suspicion and their answers to it.
- **Burgenland Hungarians:** check memoirs and parish chronicles for the 1921 transfer, when loyalties shifted with the border.
- **Catholic parishes of Upper Bavaria and Lutheran ones of Middle Franconia:** check how each church's members spoke of abuse findings in their own parish papers.
- **Serbian-Austrians:** check community memory of 1914 and of the suspicion turned on them then, in their own sources.
