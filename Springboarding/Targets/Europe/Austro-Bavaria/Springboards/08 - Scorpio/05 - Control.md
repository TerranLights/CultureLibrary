## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrolean and Vorarlberg Alpine valleys against the Danube towns of the Wachau and Passau:** check whether avalanche works or flood dams dominate local talk of taming nature.
- **Styria and the Rosenheim district:** check farmers' newspapers on hail defense, where neighbors disagree over seeding.
- **Turkish- and Yugoslav-era workers and their children:** check memoirs and community archives for how recruitment rules shaped their first years.
- **Slovak, Czech and Romanian live-in carers:** check their own groups' reports, since they live inside the households they serve.
- **Vienna's council estates against farm villages of the Waldviertel:** check what rules govern shared space in each, and who enforces them.
- **People with disabilities in institutions in Bavaria and Austria:** check self-advocacy groups' reports on restraint and guardianship.
- **East Franconian towns:** check carnival-session customs for how licensed excess is bounded there.
