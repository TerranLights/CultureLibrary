## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Austria and Salzburg against Vienna and Burgenland:** check vaccination and science-trust figures district by district, and what local sources give as reasons.
- **Jewish communities of Franconia and Bavarian Swabia:** check memoirs and community histories for the trading trust before 1938 and what returned after 1945.
- **Bosnian- and Turkish-Austrians:** check community sermons and associations for the *amanet* idea of a thing held in trust.
- **Burgenland Croats and Hungarians:** check whether parish savings and village cooperatives kept records in their own languages.
- **Roma and Sinti:** check their own organizations' surveys for trust in authorities, read beside the record of persecution and of the 1995 Oberwart attack.
- **Polish and Ukrainian communities, older and recent:** check their own media for trust in Austrian and Bavarian offices.
- **Yenish families:** check whether any source records their dealings with village customers; sources are scarce, so say so rather than borrow Swiss material.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey trust and institutions; mixed for everyday credit and handshake deals; thin for minority and Yenish trust in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the epic; Hebrew and Yiddish for some cattle-dealer family papers; Polish and Ukrainian for newer communities' media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Holocaust-era property loss; vaccination as a political fault line. Trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn, not here.
