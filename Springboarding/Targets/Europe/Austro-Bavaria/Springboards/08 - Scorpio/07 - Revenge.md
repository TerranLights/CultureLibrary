## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Bavarian mining towns against farm districts:** check how 1945 reprisals are commemorated, and who leads the commemoration.
- **The Tyrolean Lechtal and East Tyrol:** check local tellings of the Geier-Wally story beside the real painter's life, to see what the legend adds.
- **German-speaking and Slovene-speaking Carinthians:** check each side's commemorations and school materials, and avoid reading either as the vengeful side.
- **Vienna's council estates and Munich's housing estates:** check mediation records for which disputes turn into long feuds.
- **Jewish survivors and their families in Vienna and Munich:** check their own writing on justice, memory and forgiveness, beyond Wiesenthal.
- **Bosnian-Austrians:** check their own Srebrenica commemorations in Vienna for the language of justice and remembrance.
- **Styrian and Lower Austrian wine villages:** check local chronicles for how festival quarrels were settled between families.
