# Austro-Bavaria: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch) and Viennese idiom; Early New High German and Latin for court and order papers; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech, Yenish; Hebrew and Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden on purpose, and how a people judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Salzkammergut, Upper Carinthia and Upper Styria against Lutheran Franconia:** check how secret worship is remembered where Protestants hid, beside a region where they never needed to.
- **Carinthian Slovene families:** check memoirs and oral-history projects for which wartime events were kept from children, and when.
- **Viennese Czech families:** check whether Czech was kept as a home language and dropped in public in the interwar and Nazi years.
- **Jewish survivors in Vienna and Munich:** check testimony collections for life in hiding and for later silence about it.
- **LGBT people in Tyrol and Vorarlberg against Vienna and Munich:** check archive interviews for differences in what could stay hidden in a village.
- **Yenish families:** check, with Yenish organizations, what of their speech and family history they wish recorded at all.
- **Pullach and the Waldviertel:** check local memory of secret installations and lodges as neighbors saw them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state, court and lodge secrecy; mixed for family secrets; thin for minority and Yenish discretion in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Early New High German for court papers; Kurrent for lodge and Protestant records; Slovene for Haderlap's source milieu; Hebrew and Yiddish for some testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living people in DNA and adoption cases; Yenish speech; Holocaust testimony; foreign-press framings of Austria after the 2006 and 2008 captivity cases are outsiders' claims to examine, not evidence.
