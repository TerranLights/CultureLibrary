## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria against Lutheran Franconia:** check election maps and party membership to see whether the governing party's reach differs across the confessional line.
- **Lower Austria against Vienna:** check how Land and city power are contested between parties, and how each side describes the other's hold.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check their list's council seats and their own press on what local power has won them.
- **Burgenland Croats and Hungarians:** check bilingual municipalities for who holds the mayor's office and in which language councils work.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check council and advisory-body membership, and their own associations' accounts of having or lacking a say.
- **Upper Austrian and Salzburg hydropower valleys:** check local memory of the dams and of those who built them.
- **Women in the Länder and in Bavarian districts:** check the share of female mayors and governors by region, and the explanations offered locally.
