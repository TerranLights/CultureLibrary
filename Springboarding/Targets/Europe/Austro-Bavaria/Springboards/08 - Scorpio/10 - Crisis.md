## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Walser valleys of Vorarlberg against Tyrolean resort valleys:** check how avalanche danger and the tourist economy shape local talk of disaster.
- **The Waldviertel against Passau:** check flood-aid records and local press for how help was shared after each flood.
- **Turkish-Austrians:** check their own media and associations on how 1683 commemorations are received.
- **Bosnian-Austrians who arrived in 1992–95, and Ukrainians since 2022:** check their own associations for how the crisis that brought them is told.
- **Burgenland Croats and Hungarians near the eastern border:** check parish chronicles and local memory for 1945, 1956 and 2015, when crises arrived at their doors.
- **Upper Palatinate and Bavarian Forest districts:** check how Chernobyl fallout on forests and game was talked about where people hunt and gather mushrooms.
- **Vienna's working districts:** check oral histories of 1931–34 for the household side of the banking collapse.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, plague monuments and the banking crisis; mixed for personal crises; thin for how newcomers' own communities narrate the crises that brought them.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Baroque German for vows; Alemannic for Walser memory; Bosnian and Ukrainian for newcomers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and mental-health crisis callers; avalanche and flood deaths with living kin; "crisis" framing of refugees, which needs the newcomers' own voices beside it.
