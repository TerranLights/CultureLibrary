*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian (Boarisch), East Franconian, Swabian and Vorarlberg Alemannic; Latin for the founding texts; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani; Turkish, Bosnian and Serbian; Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** what is true, and where meaning is found: religion, philosophy, higher learning and long journeys, with the region's religious ways and freedom ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria against Lutheran Middle and Upper Franconia:** check denominational maps and church records to see whether the domain question is answered differently across the old confessional line.
- **The Protestant enclaves of Carinthia and Upper Austria:** check their own parish histories for faith kept in secret before 1781.
- **Vienna against Tyrol and Vorarlberg:** check the unaffiliated share by district and cohort before assuming a city and country split.
- **Burgenland's Catholic, Lutheran and Reformed parishes around Oberwart, with Croatian and Hungarian speakers:** check how language and denomination overlap.
- **Serbian and Romanian Orthodox, Alevi and Sunni Muslim communities in Vienna and Munich:** check each body's own publications separately; do not merge them as "migrant religion".
- **The non-religious:** check humanist associations' counts against census categories.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna and Munich, and the rural Jewish past of Franconia and Swabia:** check community archives and village memorial projects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church statistics, art history and Catholic folk piety; mixed for Protestant and Orthodox life; thin for unbelief in the countryside and for Romani religious life. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Severin and the *Conversio*; Kurrent for parish records; Slovene, Croatian and Hungarian for bilingual parishes; Turkish and Bosnian for mosque associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** affiliation is personal data; Nazi persecution of Jews, Roma and Jehovah's Witnesses; church abuse inquiries.
- **Held for review:** religious communities of South Asian origin in Austria and Bavaria, kept to a category here and not pointed to.
