# Austro-Bavaria: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and Bavarian German; Middle High German; Viennese and Bavarian idiom; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Romani, Czech; Turkish and Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Styria's steel valleys and the Upper Palatinate after mine and mill closures:** check whether later closures were studied the way Marienthal was, and what people said was lost besides wages.
- **Heirs of farms in the Mühlviertel and Bavarian Forest:** check whether keeping the farm is spoken of as the point of a life, or as a burden, in farm-succession studies.
- **Members of religious orders against the non-religious in Vienna:** check calling as described in vocation literature and in humanist life-ceremony material.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check memory and meaning across generations in Slovene-language memoirs and the Slovene press.
- **Danube Swabian expellees in Upper Austria and Bavaria:** check homeland-society books for how a lost home was turned into a life's story.
- **First-generation Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrian retirees:** check their own association publications for return, staying and what the work was for.
- **Lovara and Burgenland Roma survivors' families:** check memoirs and their organizations' oral histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology, logotherapy and literature; mixed for survey items on meaning; thin for migrant and Romani voices outside a few memoirs.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for Walther; Slovene for minority memoirs; Turkish and Bosnian for first-generation interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crisis-line data and suicide; Holocaust and Porajmos testimony; unemployment as stigma.
- **Held for review:** contemplative and meditation teachings of South Asian origin offered in the region's retreat centers, noted only as a category.
