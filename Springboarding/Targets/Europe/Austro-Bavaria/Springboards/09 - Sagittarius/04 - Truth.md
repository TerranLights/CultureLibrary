## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Augsburg and Lutheran Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check catechisms and sermons for how each church taught which truths are certain.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and German-speaking Carinthians:** check school histories and local press for two accounts of 1920 and 1945.
- **Sudeten German descendants in Bavaria:** check homeland-society publications on the expulsion against Czech historians' work, as a comparison.
- **Bosnian-Austrians and Serbian-Austrians:** check how each community's media handle July commemorations of Srebrenica and claims of denial.
- **Sinti in Bavaria and Burgenland Roma:** check which archives they trust to tell their history, and their own organizations' statements on it.
- **Vienna's Jewish community:** check how the community's own historians took part in the property-restitution inquiries.
- **Rural Styria and the Innviertel against Graz and Linz:** check pandemic-era trust in health authorities by district, using vaccination uptake as a measure, not a judgment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the reckoning with the Nazi past, the press and fact-checking; mixed for folk ways of telling truth from lies; thin for minority media studies.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German and Early New High German for the chronicle and the confession; Slovene, Turkish and Bosnian for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse survivors' testimony; Holocaust denial (its legal side belongs to Libra); pandemic disputes still divide families.
