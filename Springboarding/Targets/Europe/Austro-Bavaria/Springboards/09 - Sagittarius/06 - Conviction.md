# Austro-Bavaria: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran Franconia against Old Bavaria:** check how each church's histories rate holding firm against compromise in the 1930s.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses and free churches:** check refusal of oaths, arms or blood in their own literature; the medical side belongs elsewhere.
- **Tyrol's Inn and Wipp valleys:** check transit-protest participation by community, and who drops out over the years.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and German-speaking Carinthians:** check partisan and homeland-service memoirs for how each side speaks of standing firm.
- **Burgenland Croats and Burgenland Roma:** check their associations' accounts of activism and its costs inside their own communities.
- **Young climate activists and older farmers in Upper Austria and Lower Bavaria:** check how each group words its own convictions on land and climate, in its own sources.
- **Danube Swabian and Sudeten German homeland societies:** check whether their convictions shifted between the first and later generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Nazi-era resisters, church struggles and protest movements; mixed for survey measures; thin for Romani and Croatian activism outside their own press.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for the *Ausbund*; Slovene for partisan memoirs; Croatian for HAD publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partisan memory in Carinthia; Meiser's antisemitism; current activists face prosecution (the legal side goes to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** principled refusals voiced by South Asian-origin religious groups living in the nation's lands, entered as a category alone.
