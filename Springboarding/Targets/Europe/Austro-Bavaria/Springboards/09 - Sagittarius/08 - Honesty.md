## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vorarlberg, Tyrol and Vienna:** check the Sunday-bag figures and tax-morale studies side by side before reading any regional pattern.
- **Vienna's market stallholders of every background:** check market-office complaint records across all stalls, and what the traders' own associations say about fair weight and pricing.
- **Upper Franconia and Old Bavaria:** check whether public reaction to the Guttenberg case differed in his home region.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check proverb and tale collections in their languages for honest-fool and trickster figures.
- **Burgenland Roma and Sinti in Bavaria:** check the Graz texts and their own associations against police-era files that framed them as dishonest; read the files as a record of how officials saw them.
- **Farmers of the Waldviertel and Mühlviertel:** check honor-system farm stands and *Ab-Hof* sales for how trust is set up and when it fails.
- **University students in Vienna, Graz and Munich:** check plagiarism-software reports and student surveys on cheating.
