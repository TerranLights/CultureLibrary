## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna, Graz, Innsbruck and Munich against the Waldviertel, Lower Bavaria and Upper Franconia:** check degree shares and travel to university by home district.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and Burgenland Croats:** check how many graduates of minority-language schools go on to study, from school annual reports.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrian and Bavarian families:** check the Student Social Survey's migrant-background tables, generation by generation, and the families' own associations' scholarship work.
- **Lutheran Middle Franconia and Catholic Old Bavaria:** check the history of confessional colleges and seminaries, and where each sent its clergy to study.
- **Vorarlberg and Tyrol:** check apprenticeship against university choice, using vocational statistics.
- **Women in science faculties:** check enrollment by field over time, not only totals.
- **Burgenland Roma and Sinti:** check their own organizations for numbers of university students; data are scarce and should not be estimated from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, university history and student surveys; mixed for vocational prestige; thin for Romani and Yenish access to higher learning.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the *Matrikel*; Kurrent for academy records; Slovene and Croatian for minority school reports; Turkish and Bosnian for family associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1938 expulsions; ethnic data on students are limited; religious training is tied to recognition questions that belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; university, vocational, minority-school and seminary pointers are all within the rules as set out.
