# Austro-Bavaria: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and Bavarian German; Latin for the inquisitor's treatise and the oath; Austrian and Bavarian colloquial forms; Carinthian Slovene, Hungarian, Burgenland Croatian; Turkish and Bosnian for press usage. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic traditionalist circles and reform movements such as *Wir sind Kirche*:** check each side's own publications for what "faithful" means; neither side's description of the other is evidence.
- **Lutheran fellowship movements in Franconia and mainline parishes:** check how each words the authority of the Bible in its own statements.
- **Muslim, Orthodox, Catholic and Protestant communities alike:** check each tradition's own theologians on literal and contextual reading; and check how press and state bodies apply the word, since the labeling itself is contested.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses and free churches:** check members' and leavers' accounts side by side.
- **Former Friedrichshof members and their children:** check their published accounts and interviews.
- **Tyrol and Vienna:** check the ISSP item by region before assuming any rural and urban split.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** thin and contested; strong for church history and the word's usage; weak for current strict groups, whose own sources are few.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Passau treatise and the oath; Kurrent for faculty files; Turkish and Bosnian for press usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the label can stigmatize; abuse in closed groups; recognition of religious societies belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** new religious movements with South Asian roots that critics in the region have labeled as sects, entered only as a category.
