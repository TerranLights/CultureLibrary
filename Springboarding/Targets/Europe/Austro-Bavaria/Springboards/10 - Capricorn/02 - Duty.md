*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vorarlberg and Tyrol against Vienna and Lower Austria:** check turnout series from the compulsory-voting era onward to see whether the old duty left a trace.
- **Fire brigades and *Schützen* companies:** check membership rolls and chronicles for who is expected to join, and what is said when a son does not.
- **Daughters and daughters-in-law in farm families of the Waldviertel and Lower Bavaria:** check care surveys and life stories for who took on the old parents.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians:** check remittance studies and community surveys in their own languages for what is owed to parents abroad.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check parish and pilgrimage records for vows kept in Slovene and their continuity.
- **Conscripts and *Zivildiener*:** check service-era accounts for how each describes the duty owed.
- **Lutheran Franconia:** check Protestant teaching on calling (*Beruf*) against Catholic vow custom in Old Bavaria.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corvée history, church endowments and military service; mixed for care duties; thin for Yenish and Sinti accounts of family obligation.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent for land-release files and endowment deeds; Czech for the *robota* background; BCS and Turkish for remittance studies made by the communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care-recipient data; the honoring of wartime service in both armies; personal finances in remittance surveys.
- **Held for review:** none; the corvée, vow and care pointers needed no holding back.
