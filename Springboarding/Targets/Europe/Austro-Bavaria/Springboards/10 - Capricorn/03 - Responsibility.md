## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrol and Salzburg resort valleys:** check local press around Ischgl and Kaprun for how a village assigns blame inside and outside itself.
- **Carinthia:** check Landtag debates and letters to editors on the Hypo guarantees for how a Land's people speak of answering for their leaders.
- **Young men in *Gedenkdienst* and *Zivildienst*:** check their reports for what they say they took on and why.
- **Children of Turkish-, Bosnian- and Chechen-Austrian families:** check studies by researchers from those communities on interpreting and caring roles, before generalizing.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna and Munich:** check their publications on what acknowledgement and responsibility they asked of the state.
- **Upper Franconia and the Upper Palatinate:** check regional memory work on local camps (Flossenbürg) for how villages near camps approach responsibility.
- **Engineers and *Beamte* in building inspection:** check professional bodies' statements after Bad Reichenhall.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for postwar memory politics and disaster inquiries; mixed for household responsibility; thin for Yenish and Sinti self-accounts of blame within families.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for Berthold; Chechen and Turkish for interpreting-children studies; Hungarian for any Burgenland echo of the Hypo debate in local press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** denazification and trial files under archival limits; victims' families after Kaprun and Reichenhall; minors in migration studies.
