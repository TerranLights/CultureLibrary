## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bavarian Swabia and Upper Franconia:** check school statistics and parenting surveys before assuming one Bavarian way of correcting children.
- **Clergy and religious orders running schools:** check order archives and alumni accounts for how internal rules differed from home discipline.
- **Alpine sports families of Tyrol, Salzburg and Styria:** check athletes' memoirs and the 2017 testimonies for how training discipline is described from inside.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Albanian-speaking Muslim families:** check community and mosque publications on Ramadan and Quran-class routines, in their own words.
- **Burgenland Croats and Carinthian Slovenes:** check memoirs of bilingual schooling for discipline linked to which language a child spoke.
- **Conscripts and *Zivildiener*:** check complaint reports and service-year surveys for how each group meets imposed routine.
- **Children placed in homes, including Yenish and Roma children:** check the commissions' survivor testimony; sources are few and need care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school history, abuse inquiries and military training; mixed for home discipline before 1970; thin for Romani and Yenish families' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for Hohberg and the court sermons; Slovak for Viennese families' school memories; Albanian and Turkish for mosque-school material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse survivors' testimony; minors' records; names in children's-home files.
- **Held for review:** none; the school, home and training pointers above fall inside the standing rules.
