*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian and Viennese; Styrian and Carinthian dialects, East Franconian; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech; Turkish and BCS; Polish. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Styria and Upper Austria's industrial towns:** check life stories and union histories for how sons of workers spoke of getting on, through craft or through school.
- **Waldviertel and the Upper Palatinate:** check out-migration data and school-transfer rates in these border regions before reading low transfers as low aspiration.
- **Polish and Ukrainian communities:** check their own parish and association publications for career paths of newcomers and their children.
- **Daughters of farming families in Tyrol and Bavarian Swabia:** check life-story collections for ambitions women named and were steered from.
- **Viennese Czech heritage:** check family histories and Czech-school records for the climb from craft to office in the 1900s.
- **Children of Turkish- and BCS-speaking guest workers:** check first-generation-student studies written with them, in their own terms.
- **Descendants of the nobility:** check how loss of title after 1919 rerouted family ambition into business, church or diplomacy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school-tracking, mobility and youth surveys; mixed for collective and regional ambition; thin for Romani and Yenish voices on getting ahead.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for *Helmbrecht*; Polish and Ukrainian for newcomer accounts; Czech for Viennese Czech schools' records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school records of minors; income data of entrepreneurs; any reading of migrant striving that slips into a group trait.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on climbing, schooling or founding needed to be held.
