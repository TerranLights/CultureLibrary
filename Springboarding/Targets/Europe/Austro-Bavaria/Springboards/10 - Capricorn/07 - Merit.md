*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Middle Franconia (Nuremberg) against Old Bavaria:** check guild and school records for whether a city of crafts and a court region judged merit by different tests.
- **Bosnian-, Serbian- and Croatian-Austrians with foreign degrees:** check recognition statistics and their own associations' reports on credentials left uncounted.
- **Craftsmen and master tradespeople in Upper Austria and Styria:** check chamber data and trade-press interviews for how they rank the *Meister* against academic titles.
- ***Beamte* in Vienna and Munich ministries:** check entry and promotion rules and the audit findings on appointments.
- **Children of the Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage:** check school and scholarship records for routes to the civil service around 1900.
- **Alpine ski villages of Salzburg and Tyrol:** check club and federation selection records for how talent is spotted and who is dropped.
- **Ukrainian and Polish newcomers:** check recognition offices' data, kept apart from older migrant groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, honors and appointment scandals; mixed for craft merit; thin for how Roma and Yenish families judge earned standing.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for the *Tabulatur*; Ukrainian and Arabic for recognition-seekers' own accounts; Hungarian for any Burgenland honors history before 1921.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** individual exam results; hiring-test data; named persons in patronage cases.
- **Held for review:** none; the exam, honor and hiring pointers sit inside the rules without exception.
