# Austro-Bavaria: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Viennese, Bavarian, Styrian; East Franconian; Austrian Sign Language (ÖGS) and German Sign Language (DGS); Hungarian, Polish, Ukrainian, Burgenland Croatian; early New High German for guild records. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" and "them" are drawn, and what members are expected to do together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Franconia and Coburg:** check how Coburg's vote to join Bavaria (1919–20) is remembered locally, as a case of a town choosing its larger group.
- **Styria:** check the Graz football rivalry and Upper Styrian industrial towns for how class and camp shaped which groups people joined.
- **Burgenland Hungarians:** check their cultural association and church records for how a small group keeps members across generations.
- **Polish and Ukrainian communities:** check parish and association records, in their own languages, for how exile, labor and refugee arrivals became one group or stayed apart.
- **The Deaf community:** check Deaf clubs' histories and signed testimony for how membership is decided by language, not hearing status alone.
- **Student fraternities:** check their anniversary books beside critical histories, since members and critics tell the same group's story apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parties, fraternities and football; mixed for trade rites, which survive in a few trades; thin for Deaf club histories in written form.
- **Languages needed:** ÖGS and DGS for Deaf testimony; Ukrainian and Polish for exile parish records; early New High German for the masons' ordinances.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some fraternities' far-right ties; fan violence and police files; 1938 and the Waldheim debate touch living families.
- **Held for review:** none; the camp, fraternity and crowd sources here needed no setting aside.
