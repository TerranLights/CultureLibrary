# Austro-Bavaria: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Bavarian, Viennese, Lower Austrian (Weinviertel) dialect; Burgenland Croatian, Carinthian Slovene, Hungarian, Czech, Slovak, Romani; Turkish and BCS; Latin for monastic letters. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Weinviertel:** check cellar-lane customs and village histories for how men's friendship ties to wine and work.
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage:** check family letters and association memoirs for friendships kept across the language line in one city.
- **Turkish-Austrian and Bosnian-Austrian youth:** check youth-language studies and their own social media for words and practices of friendship that move between languages.
- **LGBTQ communities:** check community archives and oral history for friendship networks that stood in for family.
- **Older people in East Tyrol and the Mühlviertel:** check later-life surveys and local social services for how friendship holds when villages thin out.
- **Sinti and Roma families:** check their own writing and associations for where kin and friendship begin and end; outsiders' accounts are not enough here.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and letter-writing friendships; mixed for survey data on friends; thin for minority-language sources on friendship.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Tegernsee letters; Turkish and BCS for youth slang and social media; Czech for Viennese family letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** letters held by families; minors in school studies; personal data in later-life surveys.
- **Held for review:** none; the letters, surveys and places listed here needed no setting aside.
