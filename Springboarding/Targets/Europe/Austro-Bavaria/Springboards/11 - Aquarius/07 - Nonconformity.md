# Austro-Bavaria: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian Standard German and the German of Bavaria; Viennese (including the literary dialect of the Wiener Gruppe), Bavarian (Munich), Styrian; Latin for medieval heresy records; Carinthian Slovene and Burgenland Croatian for village terms. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Schwabing's bohemia:** check memoirs, cabaret programs and police files side by side for how artists and authorities described the same people.
- **Graz and Vienna avant-gardes:** check journals and scandal coverage for how a provincial and a capital scene drew different lines between provocation and art.
- **Youth of the 1968 generation:** check participants' later interviews about the Vienna "Kunst und Revolution" event and Munich's student actions for how they judge their own rule-breaking.
- **LGBTQ communities:** check community archives for how gender and sexual nonconformity was named by those living it, beside medical and police terms.
- **People with disabilities and psychiatric patients:** check the Gugging artists' own works and patient-advocacy writing, since clinicians' files speak for them otherwise.
- **Small dissenting churches:** check their own histories for refusals of conscience, apart from how neighbors or state described them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for artists, cabaret and avant-gardes; mixed for everyday eccentricity, which lives in local anecdote; thin for medieval dissenters' own voices, known mostly through their accusers.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for inquisition records; Viennese literary dialect for the Wiener Gruppe; Styrian press for Forum Stadtpark scandals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** psychiatric records of the Gugging artists; Nazi-era persecution files on the *Schlurfs*; the pandemic protests are recent and politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none; the cabaret, avant-garde and clinic sources here needed no setting aside.
