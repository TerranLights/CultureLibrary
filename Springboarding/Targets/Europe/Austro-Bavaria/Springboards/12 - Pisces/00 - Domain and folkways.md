## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria and Lutheran Franconia:** check votive, purgatory and saint customs in parish records of both, since the Reformation removed much of this material in one and kept it in the other.
- **Upper Palatinate and the Bavarian Forest:** check border-forest legend books for night spirits and the returning dead, a lore Panzer and his successors gathered heavily there.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check the Urban Jarnik institute's Slovene recordings for charms and legends absent from German collections.
- **Roma of Burgenland:** check Roman-language texts and Roma associations' own publications for how the dead and misfortune are spoken of.
- **Turkish- and Bosnian-Austrians and Bavarians:** check, in Turkish and BCS, whom people consult about the evil eye and distress, through community health studies.
- **The non-religious:** check how people without church ties describe suffering and awe, through survey items and memoir.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for legend collections, votive objects and psychiatric history; mixed for present-day belief in the unseen; thin for Roma and Yenish accounts in their own words. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the pre-war lands carried are Germany and Austria, read here as Bavaria and Austria.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient files and "euthanasia" victim records carry family consent rules; suicide data needs careful handling.
