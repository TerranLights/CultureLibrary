# Austro-Bavaria: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, East Franconian; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech and Slovak; Turkish; Austrian Sign Language (ÖGS) and German Sign Language (DGS) communities; Latin and Middle High German for devotional texts. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage, and Slovak carers:** check family memoirs and carers' testimony for how understanding was built across Czech, Slovak and German, then and now.
- **Carinthian Slovenes:** check bilingual school material and Slovene-language counseling for whether feeling-with is taught differently in two languages.
- **Deaf communities:** check ÖGS and DGS associations' accounts of hearing people's attempts to understand them.
- **Burgenland Hungarians:** check Hungarian-language church and school sources, with the Balint tradition, for imported or local terms.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish Bavarians:** check school mediation projects and parents' accounts in Turkish for how teachers and families read each other.
- **East Franconia and Upper Franconia:** check local school and parish programs for whether listening is taught as a religious or a civic skill.
- **People in recovery:** check self-help groups' own literature on being understood by peers rather than professionals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the history of the term *Einfühlung* and for empathy research in Vienna; mixed for everyday practice; thin for minority-language counseling.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the laments; Slovak and Romanian for carers' forums; sign-language interpretation for Deaf accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** helpline call data is confidential and only published in summary; care workers' testimony may expose employers or agencies.
- **Held for review:** empathy and loving-kindness practices of South Asian origin offered in clinics and courses, held as a category only.
