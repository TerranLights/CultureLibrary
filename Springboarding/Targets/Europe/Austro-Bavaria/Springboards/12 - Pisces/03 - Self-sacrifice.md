## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bavarian Swabia and Old Bavaria:** check Afra and Emmeram cult material for how two regions tell their oldest martyrs.
- **Viennese Czech heritage:** check Czech-language parish and order records for how Restituta and other Czech-born carers are remembered by their own community.
- **Carinthian Slovenes and German Carinthians:** check memoirs in both languages for whose sacrifice in 1918-1920 and 1942-1945 each side commemorates.
- **Danube Swabians and Sudeten German descendants:** check homeland-society yearbooks for what parents gave up during flight, as the families tell it.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Serbian-Austrians:** check family memoirs in Turkish and BCS for how the first generation's years of work are framed by their children.
- **Mühlviertel and Waldviertel farm families:** check rural life-story collections for what children and siblings gave up for the farm.
- **Volunteer firefighters, Red Cross and mountain-rescue volunteers:** check their chronicles for how risk to life is spoken of among volunteers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for saints' lives, war memorials and volunteer brigades; mixed for family sacrifice; thin for caregivers who are children or migrants.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the saints' lives; Middle High German for the epic; Slovene for partisan memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memorials touch Nazi-era commemoration; hunger strikes involve asylum cases and health data.
- **Held for review:** vows and fasts of South Asian-origin religious communities in the nation's cities, kept as a category only.
