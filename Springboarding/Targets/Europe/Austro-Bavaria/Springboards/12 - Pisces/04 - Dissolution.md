*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bavarian Swabia and Franconia:** check convent records of Maria Medingen and Engelthal for how mystical union was recorded and later received.
- **Viennese Czech and Slovak heritage:** check family histories for how quickly Czech and Slovak names and languages faded after 1918.
- **Burgenland Croats and Carinthian Slovenes:** check minority school and parish data for the pace of language shift, in their own organizations' words.
- **Danube Swabians:** check homeland societies for how a dispersed people describes its own end as a settled community.
- **Yenish:** check Yenish associations' own accounts of pressure to give up a traveling life and a language.
- **Festival and club goers:** check fan forums and organizers' archives for how ecstasy in a crowd is described.
- **Families of people with dementia:** check carers' groups for the words used for a person "slipping away".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mysticism, modernist thought and music; mixed for crowd ecstasy; thin for Yenish and Danube Swabian accounts in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Middle High German for the nuns' writings; Slovene and Croatian dialects for language-shift fieldwork.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug-use data and dementia care involve health information; Germanization policies are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** meditation centers and teachers of South Asian origin who teach self-loss, recorded only as a category.
