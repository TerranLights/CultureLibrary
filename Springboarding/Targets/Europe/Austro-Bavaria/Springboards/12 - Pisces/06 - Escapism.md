# Austro-Bavaria: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, East Franconian, Swabian; Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech; Turkish, Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian (BCS) and Polish; Early New High German for the oldest fantasy texts. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Salzkammergut, Semmering and Tegernsee host villages:** check local histories for how hosts recorded the city visitors who came to escape, and what the visitors left behind.
- **Waldviertel and Mühlviertel:** check municipal data on second homes for how weekend retreats change a village's year.
- **Lower Franconia and Upper Franconia:** check parish-fair (*Kärwa*) programs and club records for how a village stages its days off.
- **Turkish-, Bosnian- and Polish-Austrians and Bavarians:** check community media in their languages for which leisure, media and holiday routes they describe as getting away, without assuming any pattern.
- **People in recovery:** check self-help literature and recovery groups' own publications for how they describe escape and its end.
- **Young people in the cities and the countryside:** check the youth internet survey by region for differences in screen use.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for addiction surveys, tourism data and popular culture; mixed for daydreaming and private retreats; thin for minority communities' leisure in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for the *Schlaraffenland* broadsheets; Turkish, BCS and Polish for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction data must not be read against regions or minorities; treatment records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** retreat and wellness offers rooted in South Asian religious practice, listed as a category only.
