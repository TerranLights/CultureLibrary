## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Bavaria and Upper Austria:** check pilgrim registers and parish chronicles for how much spirituality is still tied to shrines and processions.
- **Lutheran Franconia:** check Protestant retreat and prayer movements to see how inwardness is practiced without saints' shrines.
- **The non-religious in Vienna and Munich:** check seekers' forums and fair visitors for spirituality without membership.
- **Waldviertel:** check local tourism and folk-life sources on stones and springs to separate older lore from newer *Kraftort* marketing.
- **Serbian-Austrians and Croatian-Austrians:** check parish and family sources for household feasts and pilgrimages kept abroad.
- **Jewish communities of Vienna and Munich:** check Hasidic and liberal congregations' own publications on prayer and study.
- **Pilgrims and religious orders:** check pilgrim guest books and order chronicles for who walks and who stays to pray.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pilgrimage, monastic life and Catholic popular piety; mixed for New Age practice; thin for Muslim and Orthodox inner life in published form.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for older pilgrimage books; Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin script) for Orthodox parishes; Turkish and BCS for Sufi circles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some neo-pagan and *völkisch* esoteric groups overlap with far-right milieus and need care; closed rituals of any community are not researched.
- **Held for review:** body-practice and meditation schools of South Asian origin in the nation's cities, kept only as a category.
