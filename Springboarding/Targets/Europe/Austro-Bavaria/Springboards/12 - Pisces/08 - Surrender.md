# Austro-Bavaria: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, Tyrolean; Burgenland Croatian, Carinthian Slovene, Hungarian, Czech; Polish and Ukrainian; Turkish and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian (BCS); Latin for Carolingian annals. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vienna and the old court world:** check monarchist associations and press coverage of the 2011 burial for how a dynasty's surrender before death is still staged.
- **Old Bavaria and Upper Bavaria in 1945:** check town chronicles for which places gave up and which fought on, and how each is remembered locally.
- **Lutheran Franconia:** check sermons and hymnals for how Protestant yielding differs from Catholic devotion.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Bosnian-Austrians:** check religious and family sources in Turkish and BCS for how *tevekkül* is taught in illness and grief.
- **Ukrainian and Polish communities:** check parish bulletins for funeral and mourning customs carried into Austria and Bavaria.
- **Hospice and palliative workers:** check their training material and memoirs for how they speak of letting go with patients.
- **People in recovery:** check fellowship literature in German for how surrender of control is worded locally.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court ritual, wartime surrender and art music; mixed for everyday resignation; thin for minority communities' end-of-life practice in published form.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Carolingian annals; Turkish and BCS for religious teaching; Ukrainian for parish material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1938 and 1945 surrender touch questions of complicity; end-of-life records are confidential; assisted dying is a legal matter for Libra.
- **Held for review:** letting-go teachings from South Asian-origin religious traditions offered in clinics and retreats, listed as a category.
