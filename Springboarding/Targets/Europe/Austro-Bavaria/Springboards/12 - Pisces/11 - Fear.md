# Austro-Bavaria: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Austro-Bavaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Austrian and Bavarian German; Bavarian (Boarisch), Viennese, East Franconian, Tyrolean; Carinthian Slovene, Burgenland Croatian, Hungarian, Czech and Slovak; Turkish and Bosnian-Croatian-Serbian (BCS); Early New High German for trial records and broadsheets. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and the ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper Franconia and Old Bavaria:** check witch-trial records and later memorials for how each region remembers its persecutions.
- **Tyrol and East Tyrol:** check alpine-farm and hunting press for how fear of returning predators is argued, valley by valley.
- **Vienna:** check children's books and psychology clinics for how fear in children is handled now beside the Freud tradition.
- **Lower Franconia:** check parish and family accounts around Klingenberg for how the exorcism is remembered locally.
- **Hungarian, Czech and Slovak refugee families:** check their memoirs and associations for fears carried from 1956 and 1968.
- **Turkish-Austrians and Turkish Bavarians:** check Turkish-language sources on *nazar* and protective objects as everyday ways of handling dread.
- **Mountain-rescue volunteers:** check their training and memoirs for how fear is managed in avalanche and rescue work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for witch trials, folklore bogeymen and polling; mixed for everyday anxiety; thin for fears in minority communities in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German and Latin for trial records; Hungarian, Czech and Slovak for refugee memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Michel family's privacy; witch-trial victims' descendants; fear of crime must not be tied to any group.
- **Held for review:** protective rites and amulets of South Asian-heritage households in the nation, listed only as a category.
