## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk IT sector against state enterprises in Mahilioŭ, Viciebsk and Homiel:** check how workplaces reward or sanction ideas from below.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border:** cross-border petty trade and Catholic parish volunteering as separate fields of initiative.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** *talaka* and its later forms in villages; check local names and who called the work.
- **Homiel south and the resettled:** relief and community rebuilding after evacuation; who organized it.
- **Navahrudak, Braslaŭ Lake District and Astraviec:** rural tourism (*ahrasiadziba* farmstays) and small family businesses in border and lake areas *check*.
- **Lipka Tatars, Old Believers, Protestants, Greek Catholics:** check each community's own institutions (schools, charities, mutual aid) in its own sources.
- **Litvak shtetl communities (historical):** self-help and loan societies in Pinsk, Slonim, Babrujsk and elsewhere.
- **Roma:** check occupational records directly; do not rely on outside images of trades.
- **Women and men; younger and older workers:** check survey data by gender and age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and IT-sector press; mixed for civic initiative (many civic groups were closed after 2020); rich but scattered for folk *talaka*.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for business and state data; Belarusian for civic and folklore sources; Ruthenian and Polish for the Nieśviž papers; Yiddish for self-help societies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** civic initiative is politically exposed; volunteers and founders of closed organizations should not be named from recent material. Business regulation goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library catalog for "талака" and read the ethnographic entries by region.
2. Pull Belstat tables on sole proprietors by region for the latest five years.
3. Read Budny's Nieśviž prefaces in a facsimile or modern edition; note how he describes starting the work.
4. Search IPM Research Center for "малый бизнес" survey reports.
5. Search the National Archives guide for "рационализаторские предложения" in BSSR enterprise fonds.
