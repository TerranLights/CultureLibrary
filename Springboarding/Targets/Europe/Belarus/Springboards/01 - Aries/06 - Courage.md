## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk and the war-memorial landscape against village memory:** check whether official and family accounts of wartime courage agree.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina:** partisan zones and the Viciebsk-area burned villages; local museums and testimonies.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** the fortress narrative against Polesian village memory of the war and of postwar years.
- **Navahrudak and the Naliboki forest:** Jewish, Polish and Soviet partisan memories that may conflict; check each group's own sources.
- **Homiel and the Chernobyl-affected south:** liquidators, firefighters and those who stayed; courage measured by health cost.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border:** wartime and postwar Polish underground memory as a separate record.
- **Mahilioŭ and the Dnieper; Braslaŭ Lake District; Astraviec border:** smaller local memories, often in district museums.
- **Lipka Tatars, Old Believers, Greek Catholics, Protestants (check records of conscientious objection), Roma (wartime persecution), the Litvak record:** look for each group's own accounts of courage and martyrdom rather than the majority's accounts of them.
- **Women and men:** check how women's courage is described in award citations against oral history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and Chernobyl; mixed for everyday and civic courage; thin for Roma and for small confessions.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the Soviet archive and award citations; Belarusian for literature and testimony; Polish for underground memory; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish partisan memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime memory is state-guarded and contested (Soviet, Polish underground and nationalist collaborators are remembered differently); Kurapaty and recent protest memory are politically exposed; trauma in testimony needs care.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Ja z vohnennaj vioski* and list the words survivors use for brave and for afraid.
2. Search the National Archives guide for partisan award recommendations ("наградные листы партизан").
3. Find the "Praise" of Astrožski in an edition of the Belarusian-Lithuanian chronicles.
4. Pull the wonder-tale volume and read *Kaciharošak* variants by district.
5. Compare the Brest Fortress museum's texts with Bykaŭ's *Sotnikaŭ* for the qualities each praises.
