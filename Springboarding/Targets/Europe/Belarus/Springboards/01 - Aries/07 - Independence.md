## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk against villages and agro-towns:** check whether self-provisioning (plots, preserves) persists among city families with village kin.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** marsh-edge households, gathering and fishing; compare the West Polesian record with the Turaŭ area.
- **Homiel and the Chernobyl south:** how contamination changed self-provisioning; benefits and dependence debates among the resettled.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border:** interwar *chutary* and the Polish-speaking farm record.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina; Braslaŭ Lake District:** lake fishing and remote villages with aging residents.
- **Mahilioŭ and the Dnieper:** the Barkulabava record and eastern collective-farm villages.
- **Navahrudak, Astraviec and the Lithuanian border:** small farm communities and Lithuanian villages; check their own records.
- **Old Believers, Lipka Tatars, Greek Catholics, Protestants, Roma, Russians and Ukrainians of the south:** check each group's household economy in its own sources rather than by assumption.
- **Litvak communities (historical):** colonies and the livelihood (*parnose*) record.
- **Elders, disabled people, young adults:** separate them in the data on living arrangements and transfers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics on plots and households; mixed for attitudes; thin for Roma and Old Believer household economies.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for state statistics and social services; Belarusian for literature and the BNR papers; Polish for interwar farm records; Old Belarusian for inventories and the Barkulabava text; Yiddish for colony records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national independence touches restricted symbols and the BNR's government in exile (diaspora material is comparison only); disability and welfare data concern vulnerable people. Rules on benefits go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull Belstat tables on household-plot production for the last ten years, by region.
2. Read the Barkulabava chronicle and mark entries on harvests, famine and prices.
3. Search the National Library catalog for "хутарская сістэма" and "хутары Заходняя Беларусь".
4. Download EVS 2018 Belarus data and tabulate the provider-responsibility item by age and settlement size.
5. Search TCSON reports for "одиноко проживающие пожилые" to locate village elder data.
