## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mahilioŭ and the Dnieper:** the Krychaŭ record and eastern peasant memory; compare with western estates.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina:** Nikifaroŭski's district notes on harmful speech and the evil eye.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** curse formulas and charms in the West Polesian speech zone; collect separately.
- **Homiel and the Chernobyl south; the resettled:** grievance over evacuation, compensation and health; check how anger and resignation are both named.
- **Minsk against regional towns:** protest and fan cultures centered in the capital against small-town life.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border; Navahrudak; Braslaŭ lakes; Astraviec:** check whether the Polish- and Lithuanian-language records use different words and remedies.
- **Women and men:** check how display rules for anger are taught to girls and boys in parenting advice.
- **Litvak communities (historical):** wedding and community rituals of quarrel and making up.
- **Old Believers, Greek Catholics, Protestants, Lipka Tatars, Roma, Russians of the eastern cities:** check each group's own teaching or customs on wrath and reconciliation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk speech and charms; mixed for present-day emotion data; thin and risky for protest-era anger.
- **Languages needed:** Belarusian for charms and curses, Russian for police and clinical data, Polish for the Hrodna area, Yiddish for wedding customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** protest participation is prosecuted; survey respondents and interviewees must not be identifiable. Domestic-violence data needs trauma-aware handling. Justice and revenge belong to Libra and Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
