## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk against regional centers:** check whether admission and job contests are framed differently where fewer places exist.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina:** the festival city and its contests; check local press for how winners are treated.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border; Brest region:** football and hockey rivalries between regional towns; Polish-school olympiads and contests.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** village games and gathering contests in the West Polesian speech zone.
- **Mahilioŭ, Homiel and the Chernobyl-affected south:** harvest-competition districts; check whether contamination changed which farms could compete.
- **Navahrudak, Braslaŭ lakes, Astraviec:** small-town and lake-district sport and fishing contests *check*.
- **Litvak communities (historical):** yeshiva rank and the contest for pulpits and pupils, in Yiddish and Hebrew sources.
- **Lipka Tatars, Old Believers, Greek Catholics, Protestants, Roma, Lithuanians and Ukrainians of the border districts:** check each group's own record of contests and envy before assuming the majority pattern.
- **Girls and boys:** check how sport-school selection and olympiad results differ by gender.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, testing and Soviet emulation records; mixed for attitudes; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for testing and sport data; Belarusian for folklore; Old Belarusian, Latin and Polish for guild books; Yiddish and Hebrew for yeshiva history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sport and festivals are tied to state prestige; athletes who spoke out in 2020 faced sanctions, so handle names with care. Market-competition rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
