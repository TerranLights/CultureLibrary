## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men and young women:** check road, water and drinking data by sex and age before reading any pattern into it.
- **Minsk against rural districts:** traffic and drinking-related deaths per capita, and local safety campaigns.
- **Homiel, Mahilioŭ and the Chernobyl south:** people who enter or gather in restricted zones; check what they themselves say about the risk.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** marsh, river and ice dangers; West Polesian sayings on risk.
- **Viciebsk region, the Dvina and the Braslaŭ Lake District:** lake and ice-fishing deaths; check local rescue records.
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border; Astraviec and the Lithuanian border:** check press and court records on cross-border smuggling as one field where risk is taken for profit, without assuming who takes part.
- **Navahrudak and the small towns:** local press on youth mischief at festivals.
- **Litvak communities (historical):** Yiddish sayings and stories on rashness.
- **Lipka Tatars, Old Believers, Greek Catholics, Roma, Protestants (check congregations' teaching on abstinence):** read each group's own teaching or records on drinking and risk before comparing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official injury and traffic statistics; mixed for attitudes; thin for minority groups.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for police, health and tax data; Belarusian for proverbs and tales; Yiddish for proverb collections; Polish for the Hrodna area.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking data can feed stereotypes; report it by region, age and sex only. LTP confinement and exclusion-zone violations involve legal penalties (Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none.
