## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ethnographic zones (Paasiečča, Padniaproŭje, Paniamonne, Central Belarus, Polesia):** check whether household inventories, dowry goods and staple foods differ zone by zone before treating Belarus as one kitchen and one wardrobe.
- **Minsk against small towns and *ahraharadki*:** compare what the capital buys and rents with the village household-plot economy.
- **Hrodna region Poles and Catholics:** test whether gentry-descended households keep distinct heirloom and land records.
- **Brest region and the Polesians (*paliashuki*):** marsh economies of fishing, foraging and livestock need their own reading of "goods".
- **Viciebsk region, Dvina valley and the Braslaŭ lakes; Old Believers there:** compare fasting kitchens and church goods across Orthodox, Catholic and Old Believer households.
- **Mahilioŭ and the Dnieper; Homiel and the resettled of the Chernobyl south:** examine what was carried away from lost villages.
- **Navahrudak, Lipka Tatars, Roma, Ukrainians of the south, Lithuanians near Astraviec, Protestant congregations:** each group's food rules, dress and money-handling should be studied apart.
- **The historical shtetl record:** the Jewish market economy and its dowry customs need Yiddish sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk material culture, food and dress; mixed for household finances; thin for Roma and for Lipka Tatar households outside the main mosque towns.
- **Scope:** no `00 - Scope.md` exists and no change to the pre-war lands is recorded, so these Taurus sheets cover Belarus as it stands; no borders or units are settled here.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian chancery script for the court books, Russian for state baskets and statistics, Polish for interwar estates, Yiddish for the shtetl economy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property seized in the 1939-1950s (deportations, collectivization, the Holocaust) touches restitution claims; household income questions are sensitive under the present government.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on goods, money or food was set aside.
