# Belarus: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian of Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish, Lipka Tatar Arabic-script Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brest-region Polesians (*paliashuki*):** check lending taboos, storage places and house-spirit beliefs in the West Polesian record against the north.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina valley:** compare Jewish, Old Believer and Orthodox inventories of the home; look for objects marked with owners' names.
- **Polish-speaking Catholic families near Hrodna and Lida:** examine whether manor keepsakes and church-linked heirlooms survived 1939 and how families tell it.
- **Homiel region evacuees and their new villages:** separate what the resettled brought from what they rebuilt; compare evacuees with neighbors who never moved.
- **Minsk apartment households:** test the Soviet "good things" display (crystal, carpets, wall units) against post-2000 decluttering.
- **Lipka Tatars of Navahrudak and Iŭje:** look for manuscript *kitabs* and *chamaiły* kept in families and how they were handed on.
- **Old Believers and Greek Catholics:** check how household icons and rosaries pass between generations.
- **Roma and Lithuanians around Astraviec, Ukrainians of the south:** note how little has been gathered for each, and look for local museum holdings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief and museum objects; mixed for present-day household goods; thin for Roma and for Lipka Tatar family holdings.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for statistics and online groups, West Polesian for the Polesian belief record, Yiddish for Jewish household testimony, Polish for Hrodna memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** belongings lost to deportation, the Holocaust and Chernobyl touch grief and restitution; looted objects may sit in private hands. Repatriation claims involve Russia and Poland: report the Belarusian side only.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer about keeping or losing things was withheld.
