## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pensioners against working-age adults:** compare how each group plans for want, and whether pensions or children are counted on first.
- **Dacha and village households against flat-dwellers without land:** check the role of a private plot and home preserves in each.
- **The Homiel and Mahilioŭ contaminated districts:** examine the radiation monitoring record; residents, evacuees and newcomers may describe safety differently.
- **The western border regions (Hrodna, Brest):** look for cross-border work and trade as a hedge against want.
- **State-sector workers against private and IT-sector workers in Minsk:** test job and income security across sectors.
- **Jewish free-loan circles (historical):** separate communal credit in the shtetl from peasant and cooperative credit.
- **Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant congregations, and the Muslim community of the Lipka Tatars:** check each one's charity and mutual-aid funds.
- **Roma households, Old Believers of the Dvina, Lithuanians around Astraviec and the new nuclear plant:** each group's own sources are scarce; local and minority press is where to look.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pensions, deposits and folk protective rites; mixed for household strategies; thin for Roma, Lipka Tatars and minority mutual aid after 1945.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for bank, pension and policy data; Belarusian and West Polesian for rites; Polish for interwar cooperatives; Yiddish and English for the JDC and Jewish loan-fund records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "security" also covers the state security services, which are off-limits here; the Astraviec nuclear plant is a sensitive topic; radiation data is contested.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on safety nets or savings was withheld.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library catalog for "абыдзённы ручнік" and "апорванне вёскі" and list each village recorded.
2. Download National Bank deposit statistics by currency, 1995 to the latest year, and mark the crisis years.
3. Pull the FSZN yearly report on pensioners and average pensions by region.
4. Search the JDC Archives for loan kassas in Pinsk, Brest and Slonim.
5. Read Belrad's published reports for the districts of Brahin, Naroŭlia and Chojniki.
