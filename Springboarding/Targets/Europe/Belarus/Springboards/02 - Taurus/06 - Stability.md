*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polesian villages before and after drainage:** test how residents of the Pinsk, Luniniec and Stolin districts mark the break.
- **Old-Believer settlements of the Braslaŭ lakes and the Dvina valley:** check what kept or changed a minority's ways under several states.
- **Factory towns (Žodzina, Navapolack, Salihorsk) against the capital:** compare job tenure and staying in one town.
- **Hrodna gentry-descended families and Polish-speaking villages:** look for family nests kept across 1939 and 1945.
- **The Chernobyl south: self-settlers, evacuees, and those never moved:** three distinct relations to staying.
- **Jewish families in Motal, Pinsk and Viciebsk (historical):** the "old home" as remembered from abroad needs Yiddish and memoir sources.
- **Lipka Tatar villages with old mosques and cemeteries:** examine continuity of burial ground and prayer house.
- **Settled Roma families:** compare how settlement is told by Roma themselves and by officials.
- **Lithuanians near Astraviec, Ukrainians of the south, Protestant congregations:** check whether a church, a school or a language holds each group's continuity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census and price data and for Polesia's transformation; mixed for family continuity; thin for Roma and Lipka Tatar continuity outside a few villages.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Old Belarusian for visitation records, Russian for factory and state data, Yiddish and Hebrew for memoirs, West Polesian for village speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "stability" is a slogan of the present government; take care to keep the everyday sense apart; the forced settling of Roma and the resettlement of Chernobyl villages are painful subjects.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer about continuity or staying put was dropped.
