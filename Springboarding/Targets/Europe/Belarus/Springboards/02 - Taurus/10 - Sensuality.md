## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Lake District (Braslaŭ, Narač) and the Biełaviežskaja Pušča area:** check how landscape, water and forest enter talk of rest and pleasure.
- **Polesian villages:** compare bath, feast and song customs in the West Polesian record with the north.
- **Catholics near Hrodna, Orthodox believers in the east, Old Believers of the Dvina:** examine the fasting calendars and how strictly each is kept.
- **Minsk restaurant and café culture against district canteens and home kitchens:** separate bought pleasure from home cooking.
- **Jewish Minsk, Viciebsk and Babrujsk (historical):** read Yiddish fiction and memoirs for Sabbath food and courtyard life.
- **Lipka Tatar households:** look at festival foods and dietary rules as their own sources give them.
- **Romani musicians and families:** seek their own recordings and accounts rather than outsiders' descriptions.
- **Baptist and Pentecostal congregations:** check teaching on alcohol, dancing and music.
- **Ukrainians of the south and Lithuanians near Astraviec:** compare festive foods and songs at the borders.
