*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk and the oblast centers against the villages:** check whether news comes from screens, *rajonki*, the shop or neighbors, and how this shifts with distance from a city.
- **The Homiel region and Chernobyl evacuees:** compare what different generations say they were told in 1986 with the official bulletins they recall.
- **Hrodna region Poles:** check which media they follow (Polish, Belarusian state, Russian) and why.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** look at radio and word of mouth where villages are far apart.
- **Viciebsk region, the Dvina valley and the Braslaŭ district:** compare reliance on Russian television near the eastern border with western habits.
- **Russian speakers and Belarusian speakers:** check whether language of media use maps onto trust in state sources.
- **Orthodox, Catholic, Greek Catholic and Protestant congregations:** compare parish bulletins and church radio as channels of information.
- **Lipka Tatars and Roma:** sources are scarce; record where each community keeps its own records (mosque books, family genealogies) and say what is missing.
- **The historical Litvak press:** compare Yiddish, Hebrew and Russian papers read in the same town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the press and official output; mixed for audience behavior; thin for village information flows and minority media.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian and Church Slavonic for the chronicles, Russian, Belarusian, Polish, Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** many independent outlets are banned and reading or sharing them can be prosecuted; never ask respondents in Belarus to share such material. KGB and some party archives are closed.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on news or records was set aside.

1. Request PSRL vol. 35 and list which chronicle texts it contains.

5. Search folklore catalogs for "народны каляндар прыкметы" by region.
