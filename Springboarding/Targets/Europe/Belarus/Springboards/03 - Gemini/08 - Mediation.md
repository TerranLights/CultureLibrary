# Belarus: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic pilgrims at Budslaŭ and Orthodox pilgrims at Žyrovičy:** compare what each asks the Mother of God to mediate.
- **Hrodna region Poles:** check whether parish priests still act as go-betweens in family quarrels.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** compare the healer's and the priest's roles in settling village disputes.
- **Viciebsk region Old Believers:** compare priestless and priested communities: who settles quarrels, elders or clergy.
- **Mahilioŭ and Homiel industrial towns:** check how factory disputes run through union committees.
- **The Chernobyl resettled:** compare who spoke for villages in talks with officials over compensation and moves.
- **Lithuanians near Astraviec:** check who speaks for border villages in disputes over the nuclear plant.
- **Lipka Tatars:** compare the imam's and the elders' roles in family disputes; sources are scarce.
- **Roma:** check whether any internal council exists; record that little is known.
- **Protestant congregations:** look at church discipline and reconciliation between members.
- **Historical Litvak communities:** compare the *shtadlan*, the rabbi and the communal board as go-betweens.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious intercession and Jewish communal history; mixed for labor and family mediation; thin for everyday neighbor peacemaking.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Latin for Catholic shrine records, Church Slavonic for Orthodox ones, Old Belarusian for envoy records, Yiddish and Hebrew for *shtadlanut*, Russian for labor accords.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2020 mediation offers and union work are political; avoid naming living participants inside Belarus. The mediation law and court-annexed mediation belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no go-between pointer was set aside.
