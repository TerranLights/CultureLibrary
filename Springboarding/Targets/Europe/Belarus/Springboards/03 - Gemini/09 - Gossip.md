# Belarus: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns and agro-towns against Minsk:** check whether people describe being watched and talked about differently by place.
- **Hrodna region Poles and Catholics:** compare talk after Mass with talk at the shop, and in which language each happens.
- **Brest region and Polesia:** look at how remote villages pass news of people when the bus comes twice a week.
- **Viciebsk region and the Braslaŭ district:** check summer dacha visitors as new subjects of village talk.
- **Mahilioŭ and Homiel:** compare how Chernobyl evacuees describe being talked about in the villages that received them.
- **Lithuanians near Astraviec and Ukrainians of the south:** check talk across language lines at the border markets.
- **Old Believers and Protestant congregations:** check how each teaches about speaking of others, in its own words.
- **Lipka Tatars:** few sources; check community statements on outsiders' rumors about them.
- **Roma:** look at the rumors told about Roma by others as a separate subject from Roma's own talk; both are thinly documented.
- **The historical Litvak towns:** compare Radun's teaching against evil speech with memoirs of shtetl talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Jewish teaching, Soviet denunciation and crisis rumor; mixed for everyday talk; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Hebrew and Yiddish for the *Chofetz Chaim*, Church Slavonic for service books, Belarusian and Russian for modern sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** doxxing channels name real people; never copy names. Denunciation files can harm living descendants; access is restricted. Slander as an offense belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on rumor or gossip was set aside.
