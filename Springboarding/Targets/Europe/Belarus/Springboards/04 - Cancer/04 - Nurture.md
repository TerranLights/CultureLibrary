# Belarus: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk against district towns and villages:** check kindergarten age, the role of grandparents and the language spoken to small children.
- **The Hrodna region (Polish and Catholic families):** compare catechesis, Polish-language schooling and home language.
- **The Brest region and Polesia:** check cradle, feeding and first-year customs against central Belarus in field records.
- **The Viciebsk region and Braslaŭ lakes (Old Believers):** check how children are taught to read and pray at home.
- **The Homiel south:** compare child-raising in resettled families with families that stayed near the zone.
- **Navahrudak and the Lipka Tatars:** check what is taught at home and what at the community's classes.
- **Astraviec Lithuanians, Roma families, Protestants, the Litvak record:** check who raises children and in which language before grouping these with the majority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and folk ethnography; mixed for parenting values today; thin for Roma and Tatar upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for health and education data; Belarusian for folk pedagogy and the children's press; Polish for catechesis in the west; Hebrew and Yiddish behind Shazar's memoir.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the language of schooling is politicized; child-welfare records are closed; discipline and its law go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on upbringing or feeding was set aside.
