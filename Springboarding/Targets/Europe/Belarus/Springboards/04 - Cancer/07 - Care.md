*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk:** compare reliance on hospices, agencies and paid sitters with village families who keep the old at home.
- **The Hrodna region (Catholic parishes and Poles):** check parish charity, Catholic sisters and care in Polish.
- **The Brest region and Polesia:** compare villages emptied of young people with those where kin still live near the old.
- **The Viciebsk region and the Braslaŭ lakes:** check Old Believer and Orthodox customs of sitting with the sick and the dying.
- **The Homiel south and the Mahilioŭ Dnieper:** check long-term care of Chernobyl cleanup workers and of children sent abroad for health.
- **Navahrudak and the Lipka Tatars:** look for community care of the sick and the old; expect sources to be scarce.
- **Roma, Protestant congregations, Lithuanians of Astraviec and the Litvak record:** check which community bodies, not only families, carry care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state social services and church charity; mixed for unpaid family care; thin for Roma and Tatar care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for social-protection data; Belarusian for folklore; Yiddish and Hebrew for the care societies; English for the host-family charities; Polish for Catholic care.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** institutions for people with mental disabilities are closed to outsiders; the 2020 volunteer initiative is politically marked; Belarusian carers working abroad belong to diaspora comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on tending the sick, old or disabled was set aside.
