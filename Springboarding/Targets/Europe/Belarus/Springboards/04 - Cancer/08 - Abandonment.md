# Belarus: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Homiel south and Mahilioŭ Dnieper:** compare evacuated families, the few who returned, and the old who refused to leave.
- **The Brest region and Polesia:** check emptying villages, Ozaryčy-area memory and the orphan songs of Polesian collections.
- **The Hrodna region (Catholics, Poles):** check foundling entries and church care against Orthodox parishes nearby.
- **The Viciebsk region and the Dvina valley:** compare depopulated villages near the Latvian and Russian borders with district towns.
- **Minsk:** check where children from all regions are placed when taken into care.
- **Navahrudak and the Lipka Tatars:** look for any community record of orphan care; expect little.
- **Roma, Old Believers, Lithuanians near Astraviec, Protestant families and the Litvak record:** check how each names and handles the one who was left.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, Chernobyl and child-protection statistics; mixed for family desertion; thin for Tatar and Roma cases.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Catholic registers; Russian for health and education data; Belarusian for songs and literature; Hebrew and Yiddish for *agune* records; Italian for the 2006 dispute's foreign coverage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-removal and adoption records are closed; Ozaryčy and the deported are painful topics; families split by the post-2020 emigration belong to diaspora comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on leaving, desertion or orphanhood was set aside.
