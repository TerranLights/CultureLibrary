# Belarus: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **City households and village households:** check who sends food parcels from village plots to children in Minsk, and what flows back as money.
- **Catholic families in the Hrodna area:** *opłatek*, the extra place and parish collections; check what Polish-language sermons ask of givers.
- **Orthodox parishes of the east (Mahilioŭ, Viciebsk):** alms at church doors and memorial meals; check who gives to whom.
- **Polesian villages:** sharing labor and food in small, isolated communities; check what is lent and what is given outright.
- **Families of men working abroad:** remittances and gifts carried home; check obligations to parents and siblings.
- **Lipka Tatars:** *kurban*, *sadaka* and support for mosque upkeep; check how the small community funds itself.
- **Protestant congregations:** tithing (*dziesiacina*) and mutual help; check how far it reaches outside the church.
- **Old Believers, Roma and the Astraviec Lithuanians:** check giving inside each community, with interviews where print is thin.
- **The shtetl record:** *tsdoke*, dowry funds for poor brides (*hakhnoses kale*) and free meals for students (*esn teg*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church and folk customs and international giving indexes; mixed for informal gifts and remittances; thin for Tatar, Old Believer and Roma giving.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and old Polish for *fundušy*; Yiddish for *pushke* records; Russian for National Bank tables and tipping talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** after 2020 independent charity funds were blocked and donors prosecuted; gifts to doctors and teachers can shade into bribery, which as law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the gifts-to-doctors pointer stays, with its legal side sent elsewhere.
