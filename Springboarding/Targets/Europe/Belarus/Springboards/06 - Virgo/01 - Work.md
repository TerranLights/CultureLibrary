# Belarus: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-sector and IT workers in Minsk:** compare hours, second jobs and what each calls a good job.
- **Collective-farm villages of the Mahilioŭ and Viciebsk regions:** set workday-unit records against today's farm wages there.
- **The Brest region and Polesia:** check *toloka* against paid farm work, and which tasks stayed communal longest.
- **Hrodna-area Poles and Catholics:** interwar farmhand work and seasonal trips to Latvia; check memoirs in Polish and Belarusian.
- **Old Believers of the Dvina valley and Vietka:** find community trade histories before assuming farm work alone.
- **The Homiel south:** work inside and around the closed zone, and among families resettled to new villages.
- **Astraviec Lithuanians; southern Ukrainians:** ask whether the plant hired locally and what border trade paid.
- **Roma:** check what the post-1956 settlement records say about work assigned to Roma, and what Roma say of it now.
- **The shtetl record:** artisans, traders and the *luftmentsh*, from the 1898 survey and Yiddish memoirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official employment figures and Soviet labor honors; mixed for informal and side work; thin for Tatar, Old Believer and Roma trades.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the labor force survey and farm books; French for the 1898 survey; Yiddish for artisan memoirs; Polish for interwar farmhands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** union activity and the 2020 strikes are risky to study; workers' names stay out. Labor law and the "social dependency" decree belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no employment, craft or migration pointer met a rule.
