# Belarus: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pensioners in Minsk and in Viciebsk-region villages:** compare paid work after retirement with garden and grandchild work.
- **The Homiel and Mahilioŭ lands:** set the land-return program against the views of residents who stayed and those who left.
- **Polesian households:** check which tasks children did and at what age, using village memoirs from the Brest region.
- **Hrodna-area Poles:** the Polish *złota rączka* and parish volunteering; check who is called handy and who is called idle.
- **Disabled people's societies against independent disability groups:** check whether each frames worth by work or apart from it.
- **Lipka Tatars, Old Believers and Roma:** sources on elders' roles are scarce; start with community bodies and ask before generalizing from neighbors.
- **The shtetl and the Soviet Jewish colonies:** what *takhles* meant in each, in the Yiddish record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** statistics on pensioners and disability are available; feelings of being needed or a burden are almost unstudied, so expect thin direct evidence and lean on memoirs and folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian and Church Slavonic for Skaryna; Russian for the tale index and social services; Yiddish for OZET-era press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** talk of being a burden at the end of life touches illness and suicide; handle with care and no names. The worth of disabled people is a rights issue, not a measure to apply.
- **Held for review:** none; the elders, disability and land pointers stood clear of every rule.
