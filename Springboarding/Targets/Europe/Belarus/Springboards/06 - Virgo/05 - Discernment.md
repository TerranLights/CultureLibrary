# Belarus: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Homiel-region villagers and Minsk shoppers:** compare how each checks food: by eye and nose, by market lab, or by label.
- **Polesian and Lake District mushroom gatherers:** check dialect names and the signs each uses to tell kinds apart.
- **Orthodox, Catholic and Protestant faithful:** set the examination of conscience, vocation discernment and evangelical "testing" side by side.
- **Older and younger internet users:** check who reports falling for fraud, using police and bank statistics.
- **Hrodna-area Poles:** Polish-language parish formation and Polish media; check which news sources each age group trusts.
- **Lipka Tatars:** read the *fałnama* tradition through Tatar scholars, not outsiders.
- **The Litvak record:** *seykhl* against *lomdes* in Yiddish memoirs: practical sense and learned reasoning as distinct gifts.
- **Old Believers and Roma:** few sources; ask community bodies how a stranger or a match is judged.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food safety and church teaching; mixed for fraud; thin for folk judgment of people and for Tatar and Roma practice.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Lyščynski; Polish for seminary materials; Arabic script with Belarusian for *fałnama*; Russian for police and health notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** media literacy and "propaganda" are political; projects may be blocked or labeled extremist. Do not treat Tatar sign-books as curiosities.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; heresy trials, fraud warnings and the Tatar sign-books are open to research.
