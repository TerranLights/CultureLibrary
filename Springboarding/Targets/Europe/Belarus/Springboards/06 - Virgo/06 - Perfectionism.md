- **Slutsk and the Navahrudak area:** craft revivals; check whether makers prize exact copying or their own mark.
- **Old Believers of Vietka and the Braslaŭ lakes:** exact ritual against the Orthodox parish norm nearby.
- **Hrodna-area Catholics and Poles:** check the Polish words for "properly done" in parish and school life.
