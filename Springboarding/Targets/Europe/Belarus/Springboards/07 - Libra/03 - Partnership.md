## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna-area Catholics and Poles:** mixed Catholic-Orthodox couples under imperial, interwar and Soviet rules; check what each regime asked of them.
- **Minsk against villages of the Mahilioŭ and Viciebsk regions:** compare unregistered unions by age and settlement in the census.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*) and the Brest region:** check *supraha* and other work partnerships where farms were small and marsh-bound.
- **The Litvak record:** the *zivug* in Yiddish memoir, set beside business partnerships between Jewish and Christian traders in town records.
- **Lipka Tatars:** check Tatar-Christian marriages and their record; very few sources, start with Tatar community scholars.
- **Old Believers around Braslaŭ and Vietka:** check marriage within the community, especially among priestless groups.
- **Roma:** marriage within the group, as Roma organizations describe it; outsiders' accounts are unreliable.
- **Protestant congregations of the Brest region:** check premarital counseling and how churches treat couples who cohabit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaties, codes and census; mixed for business disputes; thin for folk work partnerships and minority marriage.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for codes, treaties and court practice; Polish for interwar Hrodna; Yiddish for Litvak memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex couples have no legal standing and may face risk; refer only to organizations, never to persons. The Union State is a political issue.
- **Held for review:** none; the treaties, codes and wedding honors all remain in scope.
