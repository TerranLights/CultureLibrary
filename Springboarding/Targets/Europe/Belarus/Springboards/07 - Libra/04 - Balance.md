## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian- and Belarusian-speaking families:** check which language each uses at home, school and work, to see how the two-language law plays out by region.
- **Hrodna-area Poles and Lithuanians by Astraviec:** check what the minorities law gives them in schools and signs, against what they report.
- **Polesian farmers (*paliashuki*) and conservationists:** compare how each speaks of the drained and rewetted bogs.
- **Minsk office workers against farm-town staff:** compare hours, overtime and leave taken, using Labor Code complaints and time-use tables.
- **Viciebsk-region Catholics and Orthodox:** check whether Hramnicy sayings differ between the two calendars.
- **Lipka Tatars and the Litvak record:** look for teaching on moderation in kitabs and in *musar* writing; both are thin and need specialists.
- **Old Believers and Protestants:** check rules on rest days and fasting as a weighted round of work and abstinence; little printed.
- **Roma:** nothing known for this concept; begin with community organizations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional texts and statutes; mixed for ecology; thin for folk teaching on moderation and for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the codes and doctrine; Polish and Latin for dietine records; Belarusian for folk-calendar sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1996 and 2022 referendums and the balance of powers are politically charged; whether the checks work in practice is Capricorn's, not this sheet's.
