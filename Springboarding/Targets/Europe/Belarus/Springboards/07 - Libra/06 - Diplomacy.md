# Belarus: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani; Polish and Latin for the Commonwealth's records. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lipka Tatars:** check how the Muslim Religious Association deals with the state and with foreign Muslim bodies that fund mosques; few sources.
- **Hrodna-area Poles:** check how their organizations deal with Minsk and with Warsaw, and which split followed.
- **Lithuanians of the Astraviec district:** read their local statements on the plant beside the two governments'.
- **Ukrainians of the Homiel south:** check cross-border cooperation in Polesia before and after 2022.
- **Orthodox, Catholic, Greek Catholic and Protestant leaders:** compare who speaks for each faith on the interconfessional council.
- **Minsk diplomats against Viciebsk and Mahilioŭ border towns:** set national foreign policy beside town-level ties with Russian regions.
- **The Litvak record:** check how communities negotiated with landlords and officials through their own representatives, as Yiddish memoirs tell it.
- **Roma and Old Believers:** check how each deals with local officials; very little written.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and treaties; mixed for interfaith and interethnic talks; thin for everyday tact and for small groups.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Latin for the Sejm diaries; Russian for Soviet and current documents; Lithuanian for the plant dispute.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the exiled opposition's own diplomacy abroad is a matter for the diaspora, used for comparison only; current foreign relations are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none: no envoy, treaty or welcome-rite pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
