# Belarus: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Homiel-region families in the right-to-resettle zone:** compare who stayed and who left, and how each explains the decision.
- **Minsk against Viciebsk and Mahilioŭ:** check "against all" shares and late-voting patterns by region in CEC tables.
- **Orthodox and Catholic faithful:** compare a confessor's blessing with Catholic discernment as two ways of handing over a choice.
- **The Litvak record:** *kvitlekh* from Hasidic towns against the Misnagdic habit of asking a town rabbi's ruling.
- **Lipka Tatars:** check prayer for guidance in Tatar manuscripts and practice; specialists needed.
- **Polesian villages (*paliashuki*):** look for meadow lots and other chance customs in marsh villages where hay was scarce.
- **Hrodna-area Poles:** check school career guidance in Polish-language classes against Belarusian- and Russian-language ones.
- **Old Believers:** check how priestless communities reach a decision in council; very little in print.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law and election data; mixed for folk chance customs; thin for religious decision aids among minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian for the Statutes; Hebrew and Yiddish for Hasidic sources; Arabic script for Tatar prayer books; Russian for the codes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** election results since 1994 are contested; report the tables without treating them as a measure of real choice. Rashness goes to Aries.
- **Held for review:** none. Ballots, legal deadlines and the rebbes' petitions all remain open to the researcher.
