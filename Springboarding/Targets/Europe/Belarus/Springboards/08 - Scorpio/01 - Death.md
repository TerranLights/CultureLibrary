# Belarus: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian of Belarus, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna borderland, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic letters, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how dying, the dead and the meaning of death are understood, with grief and mourning.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox villages of the Mahilioŭ and Dnieper country:** set the ninth, fortieth and year's-end memorials beside Catholic practice in the west.
- **Catholics of the Hrodna region:** look for the rosary at the coffin and All Souls' candles, and for Polish against Belarusian epitaphs.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** whether keening survives in living memory, and where.
- **Old Believers around Braslaŭ:** burial without priests, and the dress of the dead.
- **The Homiel south:** how the Chernobyl dead are counted and named apart from other deaths.
- **Lipka Tatars:** speed of burial, the shroud, and the Ya-Sin reading; few recorded cases.
- **The Litvak record:** *shive* and *yortsayt* customs from interwar fieldwork, read apart.
- **Roma:** recorded funeral practice is scarce; ask Romani community bodies in Belarus and their own historians.
- **Protestants:** what replaces the lament and the memorial meal in Baptist and Pentecostal funerals.
- **City and village:** check who washes the body in a Minsk flat against a village house.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for rite texts and mortality numbers; mixed for end-of-life care; thin for Roma, Tatar and Old Believer deathways.
- **Languages needed:** Belarusian dialect transcriptions for laments; Polish for magnate funerals; Yiddish for YIVO files; Russian for church guidance and statistics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2020 death toll is politically disputed; suicide and child deaths need care; nothing here names a private family. Grave law and burial rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none. The rite collections, the magnate sermons and the mortality tables all stay in play.
