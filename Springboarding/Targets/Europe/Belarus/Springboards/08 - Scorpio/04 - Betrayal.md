# Belarus: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (both the standard and *Taraškievica*), Belarusian Russian, *trasianka*, the Hrodna region's Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish for the historical record, Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause one owed loyalty to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk region and the Khatyn area:** check how the word *palicaj* is used of neighbors' families, then and now.
- **Hrodna region and its Poles:** 1863 memory and the 1940s; which acts are recalled as betrayals and by whom.
- **Viciebsk and the Dvina valley:** how the 1623 killing is told in Catholic, Greek Catholic and Orthodox sources.
- **Polesia and the Brest region (*paliashuki*):** partisan and village memory where loyalties shifted between powers.
- **Smarhoń district and the Lithuanian border at Astraviec:** how Kreva's story is told on each side of the language line.
- **Lipka Tatars:** 1672 in their own historians' telling; little else in print.
- **The Litvak record:** the *moyser* and the *khaper* as separate charges; Yiddish memoirs first.
- **Old Believers:** apostasy as betrayal; their own books.
- **Roma:** whether a group word for an informer exists here; ask community bodies.
- **Couples in cities and villages:** infidelity norms by age and place; no assumption about either.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** wartime treason and Soviet informing are well documented; infidelity sits in polls and press only; the 1672 episode and any Roma material are sparse.
- **Languages needed:** chronicle Ruthenian for Kreva; Latin and Polish for the 1623 inquest; Russian for the Vasiura trial; Yiddish for *moyser* memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labels of "traitor" after 2020 attach to living people, so cite broadcasts and reports, not names; BYPOL is banned inside Belarus, so contact from there is risky; collaborator families may still bear stigma. Treason law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the defection and collaborator pointers are delicate but within the rules.
