# Belarus: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian in standard and *Taraškievica* spelling, Russian as Belarusians speak it, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna region, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Arabic-script Tatar Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so; self-control goes to Capricorn, freedom from control to Aries.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** canals and later draining were imposed on them; check how Polesian villages tell of losing control of their waters.
- **The Vilejka district and Minsk:** compare those who lost villages to the reservoir with the city that gained water.
- **The Homiel south:** forest bans and zone rules; check how people judge control of land they may no longer use.
- **Collective-farm villages and district towns:** the passport era and leaving; compare generations born before and after 1974.
- **Hrodna-area Catholics and Orthodox of the east:** check the share of "as God grants" talk in each.
- **The Litvak record:** the Pale's limits as lived in Minsk, Pinsk and Viciebsk memoirs.
- **Lipka Tatars:** how decree and effort are taught to children in mosque classes; ask the teachers.
- **Old Believers:** control by the elder over a community's daily rule; sparse.
- **Roma:** luck talk (*baxt*) and planning; ask, do not infer.
- **Workers in state enterprises and private IT firms:** ideology posts exist in one and rarely in the other; compare.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state measures and waterworks; mixed for family control; thin for fate beliefs today.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-c. Polish for Ahinski's and the Crown's canal papers; Russian for MChS notices and the 2014 decree; Yiddish for life under the Pale.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance and workplace ideology posts are touchy subjects for informants; avoid placing them at risk. Political power goes to section 9 and to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing about fate, curfews or canals had to be held back.
