# Belarus: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard, and *Taraškievica* for émigré and pre-1933 print), Russian as heard in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-region Polish, West Polesian, historical Yiddish, Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is researched here for now.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk and the regional capitals:** generalized trust by city size, using the same EVS items.
- **The Hrodna region and its Poles:** whether the Polish-speaking minority trusts the same institutions as its neighbors.
- **The Homiel south and the resettled:** the 1986 silence and later trust in medicine and radiation data.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** neighbor trust in small villages; whether unlocked houses persist.
- **The Viciebsk region and the Dvina:** old trade towns; compare credit and guarantor practice with the south.
- **Lipka Tatars:** trust between community and ruler in their own histories; few authors.
- **The Litvak record:** *tkies-kaf* and fair dealing in Yiddish memoirs, read on its own terms.
- **Old Believers:** trade and credit inside the community; sparse sources.
- **Roma:** trust with outside institutions; ask Roma organizations, assume nothing.
- **Generations:** people formed before and after 1986 and 2020; check the items, not assumptions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** two EVS waves and the election tallies give numbers; fairs and leases give history; what minorities trust is hardly written down.
- **Languages needed:** the estate Polish of lease contracts; Russian for the Credit Register and vaccine data; Yiddish to read *tkies-kaf* in context.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Golos data and polls on the 2020 election are politically charged; envelope wages are illegal, so informants must stay anonymous. Trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the lease records, tallies and surveys were kept.
