*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** standard Belarusian and *Taraškievica*; Russian in Belarusian use; *trasianka*; Hrodna Polish; West Polesian; Yiddish (for the historical communities); Belarusian in Arabic letters (Lipka Tatars); Romani. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; and its alternatives, from payment to forgiveness.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Polatsk land and Zaslaŭje:** how Rahnieda's story is told locally, by guides and in school.
- **Navahrudak and the Hrodna gentry country:** where *zajazdy* clustered, and the memory of feuding petty gentry.
- **The Viciebsk and Lepieĺ forests:** reprisal operations in the war and partisan revenge, set side by side.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** *rusalki* and other avenging dead in Polesian belief, compared with the north.
- **The Homiel south:** whether resettlement broke old village feuds or moved them.
- **The Litvak record:** vengeance after liberation in survivors' own words.
- **Lipka Tatars:** what imams and *kitab* moral sections say on answering a wrong; ask the community.
- **Old Believers and Protestants:** forgiveness teaching among Braslaŭ priestless and Brest-region Baptists, checked against what members say.
- **Roma:** any internal ways of settling scores are undocumented here; ask, do not borrow foreign studies.
- **Men and women, young and old:** whom surveys show as more ready to retaliate, without presuming the answer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** war reprisal is heavily studied; folk revenge sits in ethnography; modern private revenge is mostly in crime press.
- **Languages needed:** chronicle Church Slavonic for Rahnieda; German for Gerlach; Polish for *zajazdy*; Yiddish and Hebrew for *nekome*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivor testimony and revenge-porn cases need care; the 2020 slogans can endanger people quoted. Blood-money and punishment law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing; Gerlach's study and the testimony indexes are ordinary research sources.
