# Belarus: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), the Russian of Belarus, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna area, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden on purpose, and how it is judged what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Greek Catholics of the Viciebsk and Polatsk lands:** hidden practice after 1839 against the open parishes of today.
- **Catholics of the Hrodna region:** the seal of confession and family secrets in Polish-speaking homes.
- **The Mahilioŭ region and the Dnieper:** where wanderers' argots were recorded, and who still remembers words.
- **The Homiel south and the resettled:** what was hidden from them in 1986-1989, in their own words.
- **Families of the Litvak record:** children hidden in the war and roots hidden afterward; testimonies first.
- **Lipka Tatars:** written charms and what may be shown to outsiders; ask the community before publishing.
- **Old Believers:** books and icons hidden in persecution; few studies.
- **Roma:** what outsiders may be told is the community's call; ask, do not search for it.
- **Minsk and small towns:** whether privacy is easier in the capital, as a question.
- **Queer Belarusians:** closet and disclosure by city and age, through community-led interviews only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** state secrecy, archives and argots are traceable in print; family secrets live mostly in memoir and forum talk.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for the Philomaths and lodges; Russian for 1986-1989 orders and adoption advice; Arabic script for the amulets; dialect Belarusian for argots.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tatar amulets and Roma knowledge should be shared only as their keepers allow; death-penalty and KGB-archive topics touch living families; outing anyone is off limits.
- **Held for review:** none at present; the amulet note is a consent caution, not a hold.
