# Belarus: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard; classical *Taraškievica* in exile print), Russian as used across Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (past communities), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how its holders and uses are judged; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk and the regional capitals:** where appointed power sits, and how district towns see it.
- **The Niasviž, Slucak and Navahrudak lands:** magnate memory and the petty gentry's clientage.
- **The Polatsk land and the Dvina:** veča tradition and town self-rule as remembered.
- **Factory towns (Žodzina, Salihorsk, Hrodna's Azot plant):** worker power in 2020, plant by plant.
- **Polesia (*paliashuki*):** the sorcerer's power and the lord's, in village memory.
- **Evacuated districts of the Homiel region:** whether people felt any say in the move, and whom they name as deciding.
- **The Litvak record:** *gvir* and tax farmer against the poor, in Yiddish memoirs.
- **Lipka Tatars:** imam and mufti roles; check community authors.
- **Old Believers:** lay elders' standing; little written.
- **Roma:** how authority is arranged within families and groups is the group's own account; ask.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** gentry and magnate history is rich; the power vertical is documented in notices; hidden power is hard to source from inside.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for magnate memoirs; chronicle Ruthenian for the veča; Russian for appointments and strikes; Yiddish for the *korobka*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** investigative outlets and strike leaders face prosecution; cite reports, not individuals at risk. Legitimacy, hierarchy and law go to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** none. The sanctions lists are labeled as outsiders' claims and kept.
