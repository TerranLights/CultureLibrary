# Belarus: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard, plus *Taraškievica* in diaspora and older print), the Russian spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna borderland, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Tatar Belarusian in Arabic letters, Romani. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Mahilioŭ and Viciebsk lands:** hardest hit in 1654-1667; check whether local memory still names that war.
- **Polesia and the Brest region (*paliashuki*):** floods as yearly trouble; compare with the war and Chernobyl as one-off disasters.
- **The Hrodna region and its Poles and Catholics:** the 2021 border events seen from parishes and villages nearby.
- **The Homiel south and the resettled:** a second disaster layered on 1986; compare the 2020s with the 1990s.
- **Minsk:** the 2011 bombing and shelter lists; city crisis against village crisis.
- **The Litvak record:** town fires and relief in Yiddish and Hebrew sources.
- **Old Believers (Vietka, Braslaŭ):** the end-times frame; check what members say now.
- **Lipka Tatars:** how hardship is explained in sermons and *kitab* consolations; consult community historians.
- **Roma:** crisis support inside families; ask community groups, do not generalize.
- **Pensioners and the young:** stockpiling and hotline use by age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** wars and disasters fill archives; personal crisis is thinly recorded; the 2021 border events and 2011 bombing are contested ground.
- **Languages needed:** Belarusian and Polish for 1654-1667; Hebrew and Yiddish for the fire appeals; Russian for MChS, bank and shelter notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2011 trial and the 2021 border events are politically charged, and migrants' identities must be protected; the war in Ukraine is comparison only. Emergency law and powers go to Libra and Capricorn.
