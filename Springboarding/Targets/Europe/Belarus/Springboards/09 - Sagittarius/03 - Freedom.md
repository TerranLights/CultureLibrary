# Belarus: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard; classical *Taraškievica*), Russian of Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), the Tatars' Arabic-script Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna Catholics and Viciebsk Orthodox:** check whether the 1905 conversions are remembered as freedom regained, and by whom.
- **Greek Catholics:** read their histories for 1839 and 1990 as losses and returns of the freedom to worship.
- **Old Believers (Braslaŭ, the Dvina, the Homiel south):** check their own accounts of why their ancestors settled here.
- **Protestant churches of Brest and Pinsk:** registration refusals and house-church fines; read Forum 18 cases by town.
- **Lipka Tatars:** ask about freedom of worship under each regime; little is published.
- **Litvak Jews:** set Bund records apart from rabbinic and Zionist sources; each meant something different by freedom.
- **Polesians and the Homiel resettled:** access to the closed zone is a freedom question for them alone.
- **Roma:** whether freedom of movement is raised in Roma organizations' own statements.
- **Minsk students and district-town workers:** compare survey answers on speech and movement by setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** archives and folk song are full; current freedom data is mostly monitoring by groups based abroad, which needs care in reading.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Volan; Yiddish for *Di shvue* and the Bund; Polish for Hrodna conversion records; Russian for police reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Radio Svaboda, the Free Theatre and 2020 song material are labeled "extremist" or banned in Belarus; sharing them can endanger people inside. Civil-rights law goes to Libra and Capricorn; autonomy and independence stay in Aries.
- **Held for review:** none; the freedom sources here are European, Jewish and Belarusian only.
