## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox Mahilioŭ and Catholic Hrodna:** check whether parish press uses *iscina* and *praŭda* differently.
- **Greek Catholics and Old Believers:** each defines "the true church"; read their own catechisms, not their rivals' accounts.
- **Litvak Jews:** set the Valožyn and Brisk record of learned truth apart from Hasidic tales from Polesia.
- **Lipka Tatars:** polemical kitab sections are few; ask Tatar scholars which manuscripts hold them.
- **Homiel region and the resettled:** compare memories of the first weeks after the accident with what officials said.
- **Navahrudak and the Hrodna border:** check how Polish- and Belarusian-language accounts of the 1939-1944 years differ.
- **Minsk readers of online media and village TV viewers:** compare trust in sources by medium.
- **Astraviec and the Lithuanian border:** the nuclear plant there is a live dispute over facts; check local voices on both sides.
- **Roma:** check whether Roma organizations have published their own record of wartime persecution.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** church writing, folk tale and memory studies are well stocked; media-trust data is mixed; Roma and Tatar records are scarce.
- **Languages needed:** Budny's Bible is in Polish and the dictionary's citations in Old Belarusian; *Ish ha-halakhah* was first published in Hebrew; Russian for state media; Arabic-script Belarusian for the kitab sections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Chernobyl health data, the Astraviec plant and state-media criticism are politically charged; independent media are labeled "extremist" inside Belarus. Testimony in court goes to Libra; secrecy goes to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on this truth sheet fell under a standing rule.
