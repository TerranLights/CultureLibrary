# Belarus: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard, and the classical *Taraškievica*), Russian in its Belarusian use, *trasianka*, the Hrodna region's Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Arabic-script Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and the warrant people want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polatsk lakes and the Lake District around Braslaŭ:** compare Barščeŭski's 1840s tales with recent recordings from the same lakes.
- **Polesians (*paliashuki*) of Brest and Pinsk:** check whether *bylički* there name the same beings as in the north.
- **Mahilioŭ-Dnieper villages:** test the Bahdanovič-era record against present-day fieldwork.
- **Orthodox, Catholic and Greek Catholic parishes:** compare creeds in use and the language of catechism classes.
- **Old Believers:** priestless communities' teaching on the end times and on non-believers; read their own texts.
- **Brest-region Protestants:** statements of faith of Baptist and Pentecostal unions, which are written and public.
- **Lipka Tatars and the historical Jewish town:** each had a learned and a household layer of belief; keep both.
- **Minsk unbelievers:** check how people who say they hold no religion describe what they do hold.
- **Chernobyl resettlers:** whether beliefs about radiation and the land travel with them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** folk belief is very well collected; afterlife and doctrine items exist in a few surveys; Tatar and Roma belief layers are thin and mostly oral.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Bahdanovič; Polish for Barščeŭski and the trial records; Yiddish and Hebrew for *sgules*; Arabic-script Belarusian for angel and jinn passages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** belief about COVID-19 and vaccines is tied to 2020 politics; fortune-telling ads name private people, so cite none. Beliefs of minorities must not be drawn from their rivals' polemics.
- **Held for review:** none; the folk, Jewish and Muslim belief sources here are open to use.
