# Belarus: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and the classical *Taraškievica*), Russian as Belarusians speak it, *trasianka*, Hrodna-region Polish, West Polesian of the Brest region, Yiddish (historical), the Tatars' kitab Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not a court's finding of guilt).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brest Orthodox and Pinsk Catholics:** the Filipovič and Bobola stories mirror each other; check how each church's press tells the other's martyr.
- **Greek Catholics:** check parish histories for families that refused conversion after 1839, and what records survive.
- **Old Believers of the Braslaŭ lakes:** check whether refusal of change appears in their own family accounts.
- **Brest-region Baptists and Pentecostals:** separate registered and unregistered congregations in court and church records.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses:** read their own published experiences beside state reports.
- **Litvak youth movements:** keep Zionist, Bundist and religious choices apart.
- **Hrodna Catholic clergy and laity:** check diocesan memory of priests imprisoned in the 1940s-1950s.
- **Lipka Tatars:** sources are oral; begin with elders in Iŭje and Navahrudak.
- **Minsk students and district-town workers in 2020:** compare who acted on views and who kept them private, using event data, not assumptions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** martyr and church sources are rich; Soviet dissent is archived but scattered; current material is plentiful but dangerous to handle.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Ruthenian for Filipovič; Polish for Bobola studies; Hebrew and Yiddish for *hakhshara* memoirs; Russian for Baptist bulletins.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of 2020-2023 defendants and protesters can endanger them and their families; cite published statements only. Rival martyr cults are a live interchurch quarrel. Courts, verdicts and alternative-service law go to Libra; courage stays with Aries.
- **Held for review:** none; the martyrs, movements and defenders here pass every standing rule.
