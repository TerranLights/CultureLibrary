# Belarus: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian in both spellings (standard and *Taraškievica*), Belarusian Russian, *trasianka*, Hrodna Polish, the West Polesian of Brest, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar kitab Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Minsk office workers and factory towns (Žodzina, Babrujsk):** check whether workplace pilfering, envelope pay and white lies are judged alike in each.
- **Village and district-town markets:** check shoppers' and sellers' own accounts of fair dealing; never take one side's complaint as a finding.
- **Hrodna Catholics and Viciebsk Orthodox:** compare catechism teaching on lying in each church's material.
- **Brest-region Baptists and Pentecostals:** check whether their churches teach honesty with the state (taxes, declarations) as part of faith.
- **Historical market towns and the Litvak record:** read both peasant tales and Jewish memoirs about fairs, side by side.
- **Lipka Tatars:** few sources; ask how *amanat* is taught.
- **Roma:** check how Roma organizations answer press stereotypes; the press is not evidence about Roma honesty.
- **Students:** compare Minsk universities with regional ones on cheating and plagiarism reports.
- **Hrodna and Brest border districts:** check how small cross-border traders describe customs declarations, in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** corruption indexes, school and academic integrity records are available; folk tales are rich; everyday honesty at work is mostly in press and memoir.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for testing and VAK notices; Polish for Hrodna catechesis; Old Belarusian for town records; Arabic-script Belarusian for *amanat*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2020 election-observation material is politically charged; plagiarism scandals name living scholars; stereotypes of dishonesty attached to any group must be described as stereotypes and never repeated as claims. Fraud and bribery law goes to Libra; trust and secrecy go to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; the corruption, school, market and church pointers needed no setting aside.
