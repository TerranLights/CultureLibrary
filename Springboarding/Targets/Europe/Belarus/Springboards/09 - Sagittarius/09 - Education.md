## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna Catholics and Poles:** set Piarist-era schooling beside Polish-language schooling now; check who studies at seminaries.
- **Greek Catholics:** compare the Basilian school record with what the revived church offers now.
- **Orthodox Mahilioŭ and Viciebsk:** check where seminary students come from today.
- **Litvak Jews:** keep heder and yeshiva, Tarbut, Yiddish secular and Russian-language state schooling apart.
- **Lipka Tatars:** schooling in Arabic script is small and informal; ask the community.
- **Polesian villages and Minsk:** compare census attainment and the distance to the nearest university.
- **Lithuanians at Astraviec and Rymdziuny:** check how pupils move on to higher study across the border.
- **Roma:** check what Roma organizations say about school completion; avoid press shortcuts.
- **Chernobyl-zone schools in Homiel region:** check closures and transfers after resettlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** institutional histories and statistics are plentiful; Tatar, Roma and Old Believer learning is thin and oral.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Polish for Słuck and Piarist sources; Hebrew for Tarbut; Yiddish for the heder trial; Russian for imperial seminaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the closure of the Radaškovičy school, the purge of BSU staff in the 1930s and post-2020 dismissals of students and teachers are political; early and general schooling stay in Gemini; academic freedom is in sheet 03.
- **Held for review:** none; no school, seminary or college on this sheet falls under a standing rule.
