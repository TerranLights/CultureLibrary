# Belarus: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and classical *Taraškievica*), Old Belarusian chancery language, Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), the Lipka Tatars' Belarusian in Arabic script (*kitab* literature), Romani. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed: career, status, reputation and authority, with age, time, rank, order and power ways as the sign's folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna hamlet gentry against Dnieper and Dvina villages:** check whether gentry descent still enters local talk of standing.
- **Minsk professionals against collective-farm villages:** compare which offices and titles confer standing in each.
- **Old Believers (Viciebsk region, Braslaŭ lakes):** check which elders lead a community without clergy, and how they are chosen.
- **Lipka Tatars:** trace service rank from the muster rolls to the present day.
- **The Litvak record:** set the kahal's elders beside rank by learning (*yikhes*) in shtetl memoirs.
- **Polesians (*paliashuki*):** ask what a *hromada* could decide without the district.
- **Hrodna Poles and Astraviec Lithuanians:** check how the interwar *sołtys* is remembered beside the Soviet chair.
- **Families moved out of the Chernobyl zone in the Homiel south:** check who took charge in the new villages built for them.
- **Roma, Greek Catholics, Protestants:** first find who in each is consulted, and by whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** gentry, Soviet and state records abound; surveys are uneven; authority among Roma, Old Believers and Tatars is barely recorded in their own words.
- **Lands (said once, here):** no scope file has been written for Belarus and its tracker notes no change to the pre-war lands, so these Capricorn sheets work from the present republic, settle no border and list no units.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Old Belarusian to read the Tatar revision of 1631 and the 1528 quotas; Russian for the gentry files; Yiddish to follow *yikhes* through memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** assemblies and elections are charged since 2020; name no living official. The letter of the law is Libra's; this sign takes its spirit.
- **Held for review:** nothing; no rank, service or calendar pointer was dropped.
