# Belarus: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Old Belarusian, Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna gentry-descended villages and Viciebsk villages:** check whether the levy and its fines survive in family lore.
- **Brest-region and Polesian farms (*paliashuki*):** compare duties owed under Polish *szarwark* with Soviet collective-farm labor norms.
- **Litvak families:** set rabbinic ethical wills beside secular Bundist memoirs on what children owe.
- **Lipka Tatars:** check how service in the 1930s squadron is told against later Soviet conscription.
- **Old Believers of the Braslaŭ lakes:** check whether religious duties limited state service, past and present.
- **Protestants of the Brest region:** compare duty to God with duty to the army in congregational writing.
- **Hrodna Poles, Astraviec Lithuanians, Ukrainians of the Homiel south:** compare Catholic, Lithuanian and Orthodox godparent duties.
- **Roma:** start from family interviews; duties to kin are not documented in print.
- **Minsk graduates and village graduates:** compare how each treats the work-off.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, schooling and gentry; mixed for surveys; thin for Roma and Old Believer duties in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian and Latin for the levy; Polish for *szarwark*; Hebrew and Yiddish for wills; Russian for draft and graduate press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** draft evasion and army duty involve prosecutions; name no living evader or conscript. Duty set out in statute is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside: levy, oath and will pointers are all kept.
