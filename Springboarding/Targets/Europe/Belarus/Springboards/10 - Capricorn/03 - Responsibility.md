# Belarus: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Russian and Belarusian (both spellings) for state and press sources; Polish for Hrodna parishes; West Polesian and *trasianka* for speech; Yiddish for the kahal; Arabic-script Belarusian for Tatar kitabs; Romani. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages of the old Minsk and Mahilioŭ provinces:** check whether mutual tax liability left a habit of answering as a village.
- **The Litvak towns:** compare *arves* in sermons with the kahal's actual tax liability.
- **Hrodna Catholics against Orthodox parishes of the Dnieper:** compare confession practice and how personal fault is taught.
- **Lipka Tatars:** compare kitab teaching on the reckoning with what elders say today.
- **The Homiel south and families resettled after Chernobyl:** check whom they hold answerable, the BSSR, Moscow or local officials.
- **Astraviec Lithuanians and Poles:** compare local opinion on the plant with Minsk opinion.
- **Polesians (*paliashuki*), Old Believers, Protestants, Roma:** look for each group's own word for owning a fault; most have none in print.
- **Mothers and fathers in juvenile-commission files:** check which parent is called to answer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chernobyl, war and confession texts; mixed for surveys; thin for workplace inquiries, which are rarely published in full.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic-script Belarusian for the kitabs; Russian for village-society and commission records; Polish for the Hrodna booklets; Yiddish for kahal and sermon sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blame for Chernobyl, wartime collaboration and mine deaths touches living families; name no private person. Criminal liability as written is Libra's.
