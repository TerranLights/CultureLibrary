# Belarus: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian in both spellings; Russian as spoken in Belarus; *trasianka*; Polish of the Hrodna parishes; West Polesian; Yiddish (historical); Arabic-script Belarusian of the Tatars; Romani. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, churches, the state) or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Believers of the Dvina against Orthodox parishes of the Dnieper:** compare the length and strictness of the prayer rule.
- **Hrodna Catholics and Poles:** check whether Lenten devotions are kept more in Polish- than Belarusian-language parishes.
- **The Litvak record and Lipka Tatars:** compare correction in kheyder and *mekteb*, using memoirs from each.
- **Polesians (*paliashuki*):** look for the field-spirit and fast customs in Brest-region expedition notes.
- **Brest-region Protestants:** check rules against drink and smoking, and how members hold one another to them.
- **Cadets, athletes and ordinary pupils:** compare daily routines and punishments.
- **Roma, the Homiel south, Astraviec Lithuanians:** little is printed on child-rearing; start with interviews and local teachers.
- **Fathers and mothers:** check who uses the belt and who the word, in MICS and memoirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military schools, sport and the Soviet workplace; mixed for child discipline (one strong survey); thin for minorities' child-rearing.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for *Gorzkie żale*; Yiddish and Hebrew for kheyder memoirs; Old Russian church usage for Old Believer rules; Russian for the 2018 law debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child punishment and coaches' abuse involve minors; discipline in prisons and detention after 2020 is not a safe field topic. Penal law as written is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** not used here; no correction, fasting or training source was withheld.
