# Belarus: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna area, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Arabic-script Belarusian of the Lipka Tatars. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mahilioŭ-region towns and Minsk:** check how the old Škloŭ prestige and today's capital pull shaped local hopes.
- **The Litvak record:** set externs and yeshiva students side by side as two ladders up.
- **Lipka Tatars:** trace officer careers before 1917 and in the interwar Polish army.
- **Hrodna Poles and Catholics:** check which paths (seminary, study abroad, local business) families favor.
- **Polesians (*paliashuki*) and the Homiel south:** compare who leaves for Minsk, Russia or Poland, and who stays on the farm.
- **Old Believers and Protestants of the Brest region:** check whether religious life limits or feeds striving; sources are few.
- **Roma:** check what parents hope schooling will bring their children; ask teachers and community workers, since print says almost nothing.
- **Lithuanians around Astraviec:** check study paths through Lithuanian-language schools.
- **Women and men in the same firms:** compare promotion records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** honors lists, election returns and novels give plenty; aspiration surveys are scattered; the career paths of Roma and Old Believer families are close to unrecorded.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Škloŭ and the personnel reserve; Yiddish and Russian for externs; Polish for Sulkiewicz and the seminary; Belarusian for *zalom* records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** emigration since 2020 is often the ambitious choice now, and it is politically sensitive; emigrants are comparison only. Avoid any claim that one group is more or less driven.
- **Held for review:** nothing; careers, honors and rising-in-the-world sources all stand.
