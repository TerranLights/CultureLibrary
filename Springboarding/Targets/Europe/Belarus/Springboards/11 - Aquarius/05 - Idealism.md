# Belarus: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian in both spellings, Russian as used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-borderland Polish, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Old Belarusian for the chronicles, Lipka Tatar Arabic-script Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Navahrudak and Hrodna readers against Mahilioŭ ones:** check where the Vitaŭt golden age is still told as local history.
- **The Litvak record:** compare Soviet Yiddish poets' hopes with the *luftmentsh* of shtetl fiction.
- **Lipka Tatars:** trace where the Vitaŭt memory is told (mosque, family, print).
- **Esperantists of Minsk and Viciebsk, Jewish and Belarusian:** compare who joined the clubs before 1937 and after 1991.
- **Minsk and Hrodna students against village youth:** see who is called an idealist, and by whom.
- **Hrodna Catholics and Poles:** set Polish "organic work" beside the Belarusian revival as two ideals of patient effort.
- **Afghan-war veterans across regions:** find how they speak of the ideals they were sent with.
- **Polesians, Old Believers and Protestants:** look for each group's own image of the right life in its writings and songs.
- **Roma:** find what Roma sources hold up as a good life, where any exist.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** literary and Soviet sources are rich; youth-value surveys are scattered; Tatar and Roma ideals are almost unrecorded in print.
- **Languages needed:** Old Belarusian for the chronicles; Yiddish for Kharik; Russian for Virgin Lands memoirs; Esperanto for club bulletins.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Afghan-war memory still divides families, and Aleksijevič's work is officially disfavored; quote no living veteran. Ideals of the 2020 protests are left to the Hope and Nonconformity sheets.
- **Held for review:** empty; the chronicle, commune, oath and disillusion rows all stay.
