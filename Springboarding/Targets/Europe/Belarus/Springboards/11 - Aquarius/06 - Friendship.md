# Belarus: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian as spoken in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna Polish, West Polesian, Lithuanian of the border villages, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical), Lipka Tatar *kitab* Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Navahrudak and Hrodna gentry youth against Polesian village youth:** compare written friendship among the educated with sworn brotherhood in villages.
- **The Litvak record:** set *khavruse* pairs beside shtetl friendships outside the study house.
- **Hrodna Catholics and Poles against Orthodox neighbors:** check whether close friendships crossed parish lines, and when.
- **Lipka Tatars:** look for friendship across faith in community memoirs.
- **Braslaŭ lake and Polesian men:** see how far friendship is built around hunting, fishing and the bathhouse.
- **Minsk students against district-town youth:** follow how school friendships survive a move to the capital.
- **Homiel-south families resettled after 1986:** ask which friendships survived resettlement.
- **Roma, Old Believers and Protestants:** check whether friendships reach outside each group, in their own accounts.
- **Lithuanians of the Astraviec border:** read how bilingual friendships form in schools.
