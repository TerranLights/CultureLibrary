# Belarus: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard and *Taraškievica*), Russian of clinics, ministries and Minsk streets, *trasianka*, Polish and Latin of Hrodna and Brest Catholic houses, West Polesian, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical), Lipka Tatar *kitab* Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes. Cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, impartiality, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna and Brest Catholics against Orthodox Polesia:** compare how silence and retreat are practiced in each church.
- **The Litvak record:** set *musar* equanimity beside Hasidic teachings that prize fervor, in the same districts.
- **Homiel and Mahilioŭ villages after 1986:** check who accepted the "radiophobia" label and who refused it.
- **Minsk tower blocks against Polesian villages:** see whether non-involvement in a neighbor's trouble is praised or blamed in each.
- **Old Believers of the Braslaŭ lakes:** note which contacts with outsiders are allowed, and who decides.
- **Doctors and rescuers:** compare what each profession calls proper distance.
- **Lipka Tatars:** look for teaching on patience and restraint in kitab sermons.
- **Protestants of the Brest region:** check how mourning is expected to look in their congregations.
- **Roma and Lithuanians of the border:** find what each group's own sources say about reserve with outsiders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** monastic, Jewish and clinical sources exist; measured reserve and apathy are thin; minority voices on restraint are rare.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Polish for the Carthusian house; Hebrew and Yiddish for *musar*; Church Slavonic and Russian for monastic and deontology texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "apathy" after 2020 is a loaded word; frame it as people's own choice of words. Impartiality as a judge's duty is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** empty; monastic, clinical, official and folk leads stay together.
