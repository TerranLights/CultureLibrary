# Belarus: The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian in its official spelling and in *Taraškievica*, the Russian used in Belarus, *trasianka*, Hrodna-area Polish, West Polesian of the Brest lands, Yiddish (historical), the Arabic-script Belarusian of Tatar *kitabs*, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the hidden and transcendence: suffering, the unconscious, confinement, the unseen and the wish to escape, with magic ways (luck, harm, healing and hidden forces) as the sign's folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Viciebsk and the Dvina valley against the Brest-region marshes:** sort the named spirits in each collection and see which match.
- **Catholics of the Hrodna region and Orthodox of the Mahilioŭ lands:** ask where each sends the sick in mind, priest or doctor, and why.
- **Greek Catholics and Protestants:** check how their small congregations speak of deliverance, healing and the devil, in their own publications.
- **The Litvak record:** keep memorial-book tales of spirits separate from what the towns' rabbis wrote.
- **Lipka Tatars:** find which manuscripts were copied for protection, by whom, and in which villages.
- **Paliashuki (Polesians):** trace whether healers in each district are women, men or both.
- **People moved out of the Homiel south:** ask which dangers they name now that were not named before 1986.
- **Braslaŭ lakes:** collect which lakes are said to hide a church or a town, and who tells it.
- **Roma:** few published sources; start with Romani-led community bodies and with fieldwork on Romani healers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** spirit lore is abundant in print; mental-health numbers are scattered; Tatar and Roma magic ways have little written record.
- **Lands:** with neither a scope file nor any recorded change to its pre-war lands, Belarus is read here as the present republic; this sheet draws no line on any map.
- **Languages needed:** prerevolutionary Russian orthography (two 19th-c. collectors), Mickiewicz's Polish, memorial-book Yiddish, and Arabic letters for Tatar copies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient files are private; name no private patient or healer. The order ways go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no Pisces domain pointer had to be withheld.
