# Belarus: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (standard orthography and *Taraškievica*), Russian in its Belarusian use, *trasianka*, Polish of the Hrodna borderland, the West Polesian dialects around Brest and Pinsk, Yiddish (historical), the Tatars' Arabic-script Belarusian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox east and Catholic west:** compare which martyrs each parish calendar keeps, and whether they overlap.
- **Greek Catholics:** check how their small revived parishes present their own suppressed clergy.
- **The Litvak record:** sort testimony of martyrdom from testimony of rescue and escape.
- **Lipka Tatars:** look for any Tatar records of men who died in the Grand Duchy's or later wars, and how the community remembers them.
- **People resettled after Chernobyl, the Homiel south:** set liquidators' families beside resettled villagers when sacrifice is named.
- **Viciebsk partisan districts and the Brest-region fortress town:** check which war stories each region's schools stress.
- **Polesians:** ask whether mothers' and grandmothers' sacrifice is told differently in marsh villages than in towns.
- **Protestants:** look for teaching on suffering for faith from the Soviet underground years.
- **Old Believers:** search their histories for the flight and endurance of the 17th-18th c.
- **Roma:** check Romani-led memory projects for wartime resistance and loss.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the war and Chernobyl material is huge; religious martyrdom is well printed; caregiving and household sacrifice are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for most Soviet hero and Chernobyl material; Hebrew and Yiddish for testimony; Polish for Catholic martyr lives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hero cults are state-guarded and revisions can be contested; hunger strikes concern current prisoners; name no living prisoner or donor.
- **Held for review:** the standing rules removed nothing from this list.
