# Belarus: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (the official norm and the classical *Taraškievica*), Russian as used in Belarus, the mixed speech *trasianka*, the Polish of the Hrodna lands, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical), Lipka Tatar Belarusian in Arabic script, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (proposed definition).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna and the Polish border:** compare how Polish-speaking and Belarusian-speaking residents tell the partitions.
- **Minsk and the Mahilioŭ-Dnieper towns:** set urban language shift beside the village *trasianka* zone.
- **Homiel-south resettlers:** set the slow dying of villages by depopulation beside the sudden ending of 1986.
- **The Litvak record:** separate the Hasidic teaching of *bitl* from the communal ending recorded after 1941.
- **Lipka Tatars:** check what was kept and what dissolved, script and prayer included.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina valley:** ask how Liozna's Hasidic past is presented locally, if at all.
- **Brest-region Pentecostals against Orthodox and Catholic parishes:** compare which forms of losing oneself in worship each allows.
- **Old Believers of the Braslaŭ lakes:** look at how a small faith marries out or holds on, generation by generation.
- **Roma:** check Romani-run groups' records on whether Romani is still spoken at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** state endings, censuses and depopulation are well documented; mystical union is documented mainly for Hasidism; dementia data are thin.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Latin for the 1795 papers; Hebrew for Chabad discourses; German for some *trasianka* studies; Russian for census tables.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** integration with Russia and the 2019 protests are politically live; small congregations, some unregistered, need care; keep worshippers unnamed. The legal side of the Union State goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** dissolution needed no withholding under any standing rule.
