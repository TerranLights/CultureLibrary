# Belarus: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (present-day norm and *Taraškievica*), Russian of Belarusian speakers, *trasianka*, Polish spoken around Hrodna, West Polesian (Brest region), Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish towns, Tatar Belarusian in Arabic letters, Romani. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hrodna region and the Polish border:** check how "beyond the Bug" and the 1939 line still shape talk of "here" and "there."
- **Brest region and Polesia:** look at border-zone rules alongside marsh villages' older field lines.
- **Dvina valley and Viciebsk:** ask which frontier, Latvian or Russian, residents treat as a real crossing.
- **Lithuanians near Astraviec:** hear the post-1991 line from inside their villages.
- **Catholics and Orthodox:** record which feasts, cemeteries and baptisms each keeps separate.
- **Old Believers:** read their purity rules in their own words where published.
- **Lipka Tatars:** test where marriage, food and burial draw the community's edge.
- **The Litvak record:** set Jewish and Christian memoirs of one town side by side on the same line.
- **Roma:** look for Romani-authored writing on the line between Roma and *gadže*.
- **Minsk:** check how flat-dwellers speak of neighbors and privacy, apart from village norms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** ethnography of rites and memoirs is rich; current border life is mostly press; personal-boundary talk is new and online.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian (split villages), Polish (the Riga line), Yiddish (Sabbath memoirs), pre-1918 Russian (Karski).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** borders are militarized and photography near them can be restricted; the 2021 border crisis is political; do not identify border residents. Border law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** empty.
