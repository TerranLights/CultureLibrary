## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polack and the Viciebsk region:** check whether 1563 is told locally as defeat, betrayal or endurance.
- **Families of former POWs across the country:** compare how veterans' and prisoners' families speak of capture.
- **Hrodna Catholics and Mahilioŭ-region Orthodox:** set *zawierzenie* beside the Orthodox phrase "on everything God's will."
- **Baptists and Pentecostals of the Brest region:** record the altar call as the members describe it.
- **The Litvak record:** compare the Chazon Ish's trust with older Litvak and Hasidic writing from the same districts.
- **Lipka Tatars:** ask imams and teachers whether *tavakkul* figures in today's lessons, and in what tongue.
- **Polesian collective-farm villages and Hrodna farms collectivized later:** set the two collectivization waves side by side.
- **Homiel-south resettlers:** record the words used for the day they left the old village for good.
- **Roma and Old Believers:** look for their own sayings about fate before applying any outside label.
