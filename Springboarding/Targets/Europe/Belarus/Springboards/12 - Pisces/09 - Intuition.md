# Belarus: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Belarus, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Belarusian (today's standard plus the older *Taraškievica*), the Russian of Belarus, *trasianka*, Polish around Hrodna and Lida, West Polesian, Yiddish (historical towns), the *kitab* Belarusian of the Lipka Tatars, Romani. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polesians and the Brest region:** test whether the healer, fisherman and hunter are each credited with a different kind of knowing.
- **Viciebsk region and the Dvina valley:** read An-sky's questions beside local Christian omens from the same towns.
- **Hrodna-region Catholics and Poles:** compare Polish *przeczucie* idioms with Belarusian ones in the same village.
- **The Homiel south:** separate those who trust dosimeters from those who rely on sense, by age.
- **Minsk doctors and village *feldshers*:** check how each speaks of a clinical hunch.
- **The Litvak record:** separate Hasidic trust in a rebbe's sight from study-house reasoning.
- **Lipka Tatars:** look for any divination text and for how the community now regards it.
- **Old Believers:** check their teaching on dreams and signs, in their own words.
- **Roma:** use Romani-authored sources on fortune-telling as a trade, not the outside image of it.
- **Protestants:** ask how "the Lord laid it on my heart" is used in their churches.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** omens and dream lore are well collected; expert intuition in medicine is a narrow, mostly Russian-language debate; survey measures for Belarus are lacking.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for Kolberg; Yiddish for An-sky; Arabic script for Tatar texts; Russian for the medical and church material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** divination texts are records of belief, not guides; avoid stereotyping Roma as fortune-tellers; name no private healer.
- **Held for review:** nothing; omen and divination texts are kept as records of belief.
