# Bulgaria: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (standard, plus Rhodope, Shop, Balkan and Pirin speech), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani (Erli, Kalderash and other varieties), Aromanian, Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz, Karakachan Greek. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here, and how it shows itself: personhood, the body, appearance, naming and the rites that make a new person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bulgarian Orthodox villages by region** (Shopluk, Rhodopes, Upper Thrace, the Danubian Plain, the Northwest, Dobruja, Strandzha): check naming after grandparents, costume and rites region by region, as the record is regional.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie**: check naming rites and how the 1984-89 renaming shows in personal names today.
- **Pomaks of the Rhodopes, the Chepino valley and the Pirin**: check self-designation, which is contested, and name and dress histories after several state renamings.
- **Roma of several groups** (Erli, Kalderash, Millet, Rudari, Turkish-speaking and Orthodox, Muslim and Pentecostal congregations): check group names and naming customs separately.
- **Alevi (Kızılbaş and Babai) communities of the Ludogorie**: check rites of entry into the community, distinct from Sunni practice.
- **Catholic Paulician villages around Plovdiv** (Rakovski) **and on the Danube** (Belene, Oresh): check saints' names and confirmation.
- **Armenians, the Sephardic Jewish community, Aromanians, Karakachans, Gagauz, Lipovan Old Believers**: check each community's naming and dress records; sources may be thin.
- **Descendants of Thracian and Macedonian refugee families**: check whether origin is kept in surnames, societies and costume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Orthodox village rites and costume; mixed for Muslim communities (much written by the state about them); thin for Gagauz, Lipovans and Karakachans.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian; Ottoman and modern Turkish for Muslim naming records; Romani and Judeo-Spanish for community memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1912-13, 1940s and 1970-74 Pomak renamings and the 1984-89 'Revival Process' (*Възродителен процес*) touch living families; treat names as contested. Census ethnicity is voluntary and politically charged. Bulgaria's exact lands and units are still to be settled by the author; this pass is written for Bulgaria as it stands.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on naming, dress or the body was set aside under the standing rules.
