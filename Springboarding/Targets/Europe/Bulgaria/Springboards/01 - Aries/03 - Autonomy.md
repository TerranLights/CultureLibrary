## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhodope households, Christian and Pomak**: check who decides marriage and work migration, and where seasonal labor abroad (*гурбет*) figures in accounts.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain**: check how heavy out-migration and aging change who lives alone and who decides for elders.
- **Sofia and Plovdiv against small towns**: check the age of moving out and living alone, which official data can separate.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie**: check family decisions on work abroad and schooling, in Turkish-language sources.
- **Roma groups** (Kalderash, Erli, Rudari, Pentecostal congregations): check how marriage arrangements and choice of spouse are discussed inside each group's own NGOs and churches, without generalizing across groups.
- **Alevi communities around the Demir Baba *teke***: check how a shrine and its community manage their own affairs.
- **Paulician Catholic villages and Protestant congregations**: check whether a congregation runs itself or is run by a bishop, and what that means locally.
- **Karakachans and Lipovan Old Believers**: check self-run pastoral and religious life; sources thin.
- **Aromanians around Peshtera and Velingrad *check***: check family enterprise and household authority; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good statistics and family history, thin ethnography on present-day personal choice outside Sofia.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian; Ottoman Turkish for household registers; Turkish; Romani; Russian for Lipovan sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced settlement, collectivization and early marriage are charged topics; the legal side of consent, capacity and marriage age goes to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none; consent and marriage-age law goes to Libra and Capricorn, as noted, rather than being held.
