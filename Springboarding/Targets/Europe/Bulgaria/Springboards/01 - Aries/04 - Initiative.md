## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stara Planina craft towns** (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa) **and the Sredna Gora**: check guild and benefactor records, since much Revival-era enterprise is documented here.
- **Sofia's start-up scene against regional towns**: check where new firms and venture funds cluster, using district statistics.
- **Ruse and Varna**: check merchant and port enterprise, including Armenian and Sephardic houses, in the city archives.
- **Turks and Pomaks of the Kardzhali region and the Rhodopes**: check how tobacco, small trade and work abroad are begun and organized, in Turkish- and Bulgarian-language sources.
- **Roma of several groups**: check each group's own trades and how starting a business is discussed in the group's media; never treat Roma as one economy.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv, Varna and Sofia**: check benevolent and church societies' founding records.
- **Aromanians and Karakachans**: check histories of caravan trade and herding enterprise; sources thin.
- **Lipovans of the Danube and Black Sea coast**: check fishing cooperatives and how they were started; sources thin.
- **Thracian refugee settlements**: check how refugee villages were founded and who led the founding.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and Revival enterprise; mixed for present civic initiative; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian (including old Revival orthography), Turkish, Romani, German for Canetti, Armenian for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** post-1989 privatization and wealth are politically charged; separate documented cases from rumor. Socialist "initiatives" were often directed from above; label them that way.
- **Held for review:** none; no enterprise, guild or volunteering pointer was set aside.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library and the Gabrovo regional archive for "еснафска кондика" and list surviving books by town.
2. Download NSI business demography tables by district for the latest five years.
3. Read Neuburger (2013) and list her regional archives for the Rhodopes and Kardzhali.
4. Search the Bulgarian Donors' Forum site for its giving index and volunteering reports.
5. Search library catalogs for memoirs on "бригадирско движение спомени".
