# Bulgaria: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with Shop, Rhodope and Strandzha speech), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani, Karakachan Greek, Russian (Lipovans). **Concept in one line:** how a culture treats starting: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Shopluk and the Pernik region**: check New Year *survakari* rites against spring mumming elsewhere, to see where the ritual year opens.
- **Upper Thrace and Strandzha**: check *kukeri* in spring and the *nestinari* feast of Sts Constantine and Helena as different openings of the ritual year.
- **The Rhodopes, Christian and Pomak villages**: check how *курбан* at a new house or well is held in each faith community.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie**: check Hıdırellez, Kasım and *sünnet* as their own beginnings.
- **Alevi communities**: check the opening feasts of their year at shrines such as Demir Baba.
- **Roma groups**: check Vasilica among Muslim and Orthodox Roma and Ederlezi in May; practice differs by group.
- **Karakachans**: check the start of the move to summer pastures; sources thin.
- **Lipovan Old Believers and Bulgarian old-calendarists**: check how keeping another calendar changes when things begin.
- **Thracian and Macedonian refugee villages**: check founding days and the first years after settlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for calendar rites and birth customs; mixed for modern fresh starts after divorce or failure; thin for Lipovans and Karakachans.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian (with Church Slavonic for the *damaskini*), Turkish, Romani, French and English for League of Nations records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin stories of the state (Thracian, Slavic and Bulgar strands) are argued politically; record each position. The UNESCO dossier is multinational: take only what concerns Bulgaria.
