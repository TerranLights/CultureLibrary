# Bulgaria: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with Northwest, Rhodope and Dobruja speech), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages of the Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain**: check how elders who remain live and get help, using depopulation data and local social services.
- **Sofia and Plovdiv households**: check whether the weekend plot and *зимнина* still matter to self-provisioning in cities.
- **Rhodope households, Pomak and Christian**: check seasonal work away from home as a source of self-reliance and of strain.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie**: check how family links abroad and tobacco or farm work figure in household self-sufficiency (the emigrants themselves are comparison only).
- **Roma groups**: check how Roma organizations discuss work, social assistance and stigma, since public debate about them often stereotypes; separate group by group.
- **Karakachan and Aromanian former herders**: check memory of self-provisioning on the move and after settlement; sources thin.
- **Lipovan fishing households of Dobruja and the coast**: check self-provisioning by fishing; sources thin.
- **Disabled people and their organizations**: check independence as they define it, against the family-care model.
- **Generations**: check how those who grew up before 1989 and after describe "managing on your own".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and Revival texts; mixed for everyday self-provisioning; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian, with Revival spelling, for Karavelov's and Botev's émigré papers; Turkish of Bulgaria for the *gurbet* songs sung on both sides of the language line; Romani wherever Roma organizations or residents discuss work and social assistance in their own language.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** press talk about welfare and Roma communities carries much prejudice: use community and survey sources first. Bulgaria's national independence touches relations with Russia and Turkey; those are separate culture-nations, used only as comparison here.
- **Held for review:** none; welfare law is left to Libra and Capricorn rather than held here.
