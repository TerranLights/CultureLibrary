## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young drivers in Sofia, Plovdiv and Varna against rural districts**: check crash data by district and age before any claim about who takes risks.
- **Black Sea resorts** (Sunny Beach, Golden Sands, Varna, Burgas): check summer injury and police reports for local youth, keeping foreign visitors as a separate case.
- **Kalofer and the Sredna Gora**: check the ice *horo* and how participants describe danger and pride.
- **Mountain towns** (Bansko, Chepelare, Samokov): check ski and avalanche accidents and how local guides talk about warnings.
- **Turks, Pomaks and Alevi communities**: check how drinking and abstinence are discussed in each, and whether that differs by community and generation.
- **Roma groups**: check harm-reduction and health-mediator sources rather than crime reporting, which tends to stereotype.
- **Lipovan fishermen of the Danube and the coast**: check fishing risk and accidents; sources thin.
- **Thracian and Strandzha border villages**: check smuggling lore and how it is told as daring or folly; sources mostly oral.
- **Generations**: check how the 1990s, with pyramids and street violence, are remembered by those who were young then.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road, health and gambling statistics; mixed for meanings; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian, with Revival spelling for Beron's primer and dialect forms for the *луда-млада* ballads; Turkish of Bulgaria to set *pervasızlık* beside *луда глава*; Russian only for the Lipovan fishermen's word *удаль*, itself still to be checked.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use, crash deaths and 1990s organized crime involve living people and allegations; cite official or court-tested sources. Present Botev's *чета* and the April Uprising debate as a scholarly argument, not a verdict.
- **Held for review:** none; no risk, gambling or drug-use source was set aside.
