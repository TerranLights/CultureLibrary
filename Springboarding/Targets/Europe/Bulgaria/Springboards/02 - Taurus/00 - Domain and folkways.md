# Bulgaria: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (standard, with Shop, Rhodope, Balkan and Pirin speech), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani (Erli, Kalderash, others), Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz, Karakachan Greek; Ottoman Turkish and Church Slavonic for older records. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held: possessions, money, resources, food, dress and the ways wealth is gained, shown, given and passed on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and the Shopluk against Upper Thrace (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora):** compare household budgets and food habits in a capital region and a farming plain, to separate urban from agrarian ideas of what is worth having.
- **Pomak villages of the Rhodopes (Smolyan, Madan, Velingrad):** check dowry displays and wedding goods in the communities' own accounts; how people there name themselves is contested, so record each self-name.
- **Turkish villages of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie:** check tobacco income and household goods, because the cash crop shaped what families could buy.
- **Roma groups (Erli in Sofia, Kalderash, Rudari, Turkish-speaking Muslim Roma of Plovdiv):** each group's own record of trades and household goods differs; do not merge them.
- **Karakachan herders (Sliven, Kotel):** check how flocks figure as holdings in their own accounts, since a herd is a different kind of holding from land or cash.
- **Aromanians (Peshtera) and Armenians (Plovdiv, Varna):** merchant and craft histories, separate from the Bulgarian majority's.
- **The Sephardic community (Sofia, Plovdiv) and Bulgarian Catholics (Rakovski):** check community archives for household goods and charity.
- **Lipovans of Dobruja and the Gagauz near Varna:** small communities; check whether any household study exists at all.
- **Thracian refugee families (Haskovo, Burgas):** check what households carried across the border after 1913 and what they lost.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk material culture and official spending data; mixed for the socialist decades; thin for Karakachans, Lipovans and Gagauz. Bulgaria's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign is written for Bulgaria as it stands, with no change to its pre-war lands.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian across its dialects; Turkish for Kardzhali and Ludogorie fieldwork; Romani varieties; Ottoman Turkish and Church Slavonic for probate and monastic records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property sold or lost in the 1989 departure during the "Revival Process"; seizure of Jewish property in 1940-44; demolition of Roma homes; looting of Thracian sites. Inheritance and property law belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** none; no folkways pointer on food, dress or household goods had to be set aside under the standing rules.
