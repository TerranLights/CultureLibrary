# Bulgaria: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani (Kalderash, Erli and others), Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz; Ottoman Turkish for court records. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turkish families of the Kardzhali region and Ludogorie:** check the 1989 departure through family accounts, since what was sold, carried and recovered marks this concept sharply.
- **The Sephardic community of Sofia and Plovdiv:** check community memoirs of goods taken in 1940-44 and left behind in 1948-49.
- **Thracian refugee descendants (Haskovo, Harmanli, Burgas):** check which objects from before 1913 are still kept, and what stories attach to them.
- **Pomak villages of the Pirin (Ribnovo, Satovcha) and the Rhodopes:** compare dowry practice between the two mountain areas, using each community's self-name.
- **Kalderash and Erli Roma:** compare heirloom and wedding-gift accounts from each group's own testimony.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check what happens to belongings in emptying villages, where houses stand closed with their contents.
- **Karakachan families (Sliven, Kotel):** check what could be carried in seasonal moves before settlement, through their associations' memoirs.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv and Varna:** check which objects arrived with refugees after 1915 and how community museums keep them.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check icons and books as household holdings; sources are likely few.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ottoman probate and socialist-era objects; mixed for funeral give-aways (regional variation); thin for Lipovans and Karakachans.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish in Arabic script for *tereke* entries; Turkish for 1989 testimony; Ladino and Hebrew for some Sephardic memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "Revival Process" and the 1989 departure (contested names and memory); seizures from Jews in 1940-44; looting of graves for Thracian objects.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on belongings, keepsakes or the goods of the dead was set aside.
