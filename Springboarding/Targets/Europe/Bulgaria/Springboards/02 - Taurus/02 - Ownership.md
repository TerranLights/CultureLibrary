# Bulgaria: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani, Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz; Ottoman Turkish, Church Slavonic and Byzantine Greek for older charters and registers. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain (Pleven, Ruse):** check collectivization and restitution histories apart from the south's.
- **Dobruja:** check the large grain holdings and the 1940 exchange of population and property with Romania, a separate ownership history (Romania only as comparison).
- **Turkish and Pomak smallholders of the Kardzhali region, Smolyan and Velingrad:** check tobacco plots, houses sold in 1989 and claims on return; present the "Revival Process" as the families themselves name it.
- **Roma neighborhoods (Erli in Sofia's Fakulteta; Turkish-speaking Roma in Plovdiv's Stolipinovo; Sliven's Nadezhda):** check how title is held or lacked in each place.
- **Karakachan herders:** check how winter and summer pasture rights were held, as a claim distinct from freehold.
- **Bulgarian Catholics of the Paulician villages (Rakovski, Plovdiv area) and the Armenian community:** check church-held and community-held property.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai) at Demir Baba Tekke and Akyazılı Baba:** check who holds a shrine (state, museum or community) and how each side speaks of it.
- **Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check holiday-flat sales to foreign buyers, beside village land.
- **Sofia newcomers against old families of the Stara Planina towns (Koprivshtitsa, Tryavna):** check heritage family houses against flats bought on credit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Ottoman tenure, collectivization and restitution; mixed for informal Roma housing; thin for Karakachan grazing history.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for *tahrir* registers; Church Slavonic and Greek for charters; Bulgarian legal-administrative register for cadastre data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property sold or lost in 1989; Roma home demolitions; claims on *vakıf* and church property. The legal rules of title and inheritance go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none set aside; the rules of title and inheritance were routed to Libra and Capricorn rather than held.
