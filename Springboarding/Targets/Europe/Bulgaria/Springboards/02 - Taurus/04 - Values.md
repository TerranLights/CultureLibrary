# Bulgaria: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with Old Church Slavonic and Revival-era registers), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani (Vlax and other varieties), Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox churchgoers and the non-observant:** check whether the same values are named with different sources (Church against civic tradition) in EVS religion breakdowns.
- **Turks and Pomaks served by the Chief Mufti, against Muslims outside its structures:** check whose teaching reaches whom; record the self-name of each group.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai) of the Ludogorie and Deliorman:** check how values pass through the *dede* lineages and the *cem* gathering, from community sources.
- **Bulgarian Catholics of Rakovski and the Plovdiv villages:** check parish teaching and festal customs for a separate religious line.
- **Evangelical and Pentecostal congregations, many with Roma members:** check how conversion testimony reorders priorities, group by group.
- **The Sephardic community (Shalom):** check how the memory of 1943 is used to teach values today.
- **The Armenian schools and churches of Plovdiv, Varna and Sofia:** check what community education stresses.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check community rules on worship and daily life from their own sources; likely thin.
- **Generations:** compare those schooled before 1989 with those after, using survey age groups.
- **Revival towns (Koprivshtitsa, Tryavna, Gabrovo) and new suburbs of Sofia:** check how heritage sites present "the Revival's values" to visitors and pupils.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys, proverbs and Revival writing; mixed for minority religious teaching; thin for Lipovans and Gagauz.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and Revival-era Bulgarian for older texts; Turkish for Muslim teaching; Romani for church testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** culture-war topics (gender, the Istanbul Convention); the Bogomils exist mainly in hostile sources; avoid reading survey averages as one national voice.
- **Held for review:** none; the value surveys and religious teachings named here raised no standing-rule issue.
