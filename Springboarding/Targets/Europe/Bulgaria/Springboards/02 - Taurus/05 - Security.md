# Bulgaria: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani varieties, Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz, Karakachan Greek. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pensioners of the Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain:** check how security in old age is arranged where young people have left.
- **Turkish families of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie:** check accounts of 1989 as a loss of personal and household security, in their own words.
- **Pomak and Turkish labor migrants from the Rhodopes:** check *gurbet* work abroad as a family strategy, from migrants' accounts.
- **Roma neighborhoods (Fakulteta, Stolipinovo, Nadezhda):** check housing and income security group by group (Erli, Kalderash, Rudari, Turkish-speaking Roma), not as one population.
- **The Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas) and Strandzha:** check seasonal tourism work and flood memory.
- **Danubian towns (Svishtov, Ruse):** check memory of the 1977 earthquake damage, to see whether it bears on household precautions.
- **The Sephardic community:** check community memory of 1943, when the safety of persons hung on public action.
- **Armenian and Thracian refugee families:** check, from their memoirs, whether refugee pasts bear on saving and property habits.
- **Karakachans:** check what replaced flocks as security after settlement in the 1950s (check the dates and terms); likely thin.
