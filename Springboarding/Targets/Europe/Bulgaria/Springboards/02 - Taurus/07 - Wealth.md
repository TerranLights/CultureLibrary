## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Revival merchant towns (Koprivshtitsa, Kotel, Zheravna) against cloth towns (Gabrovo, Sliven):** check whether trade and manufacture produced different displays of wealth.
- **Sofia and the Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check where new fortunes of the 1990s and of tourism came from.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check regional income data beside local talk of the rich, without assuming one view.
- **Pirin and Shop builder families:** check *pechalba* earnings and the houses built from them.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie; Rhodope Pomaks:** check tobacco income and migrant earnings, and how families describe being well-off.
- **Roma groups (Kalderash, Erli, Rudari, Turkish-speaking Muslim Roma):** check each group's own accounts of trade and display, and reject press clichés.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv; Aromanians of Peshtera:** check craft, trade and herding histories in each community's own publications.
- **The Sephardic community (Samokov, Sofia):** check banking and trade history before 1940, and what was lost.
- **Karakachans:** check, from their associations' accounts, how a family's standing was reckoned and what part flocks played.
- **Bulgarian Catholics (Rakovski) and Alevi villages of Deliorman:** check how prosperity is spoken of in religious settings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in economic history and income data; mixed on how people talk about the rich; thin for Karakachans and Gagauz.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian; Turkish and Ottoman Turkish for trade records; Ladino for Sephardic business papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1990s fortunes linked to crime (name sources, do not judge); stereotypes of Roma wealth; fortunes made from property taken in 1940-44 or 1989.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on fortunes, display or giving needed holding.
