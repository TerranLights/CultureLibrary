# Bulgaria: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects (Shop speech especially), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Pomak Bulgarian speech, Romani varieties, Aromanian, Western Armenian, Judeo-Spanish (historical), Gagauz, Russian of the Lipovans. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and the Shopluk:** compare the Shop joke genre as told by Shopi with how outsiders tell it, to see who owns the image.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region, the Ludogorie and the Kotel area:** check the 1984-85 protests (for example at Mlechino and Yablanovo) through participants' accounts, as contested history.
- **Pomaks of the Pirin (Kornitsa, Breznitsa, Ribnovo) and the Rhodopes:** check name-change resistance village by village; record self-names.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check how keeping old rites over centuries is told from inside.
- **The Sephardic community and the Plovdiv and Kyustendil networks of 1943:** check who refused, how, and how it is remembered.
- **Roma groups:** check Pentecostal converts against Orthodox and Muslim Roma on keeping or changing practice; group by group.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai):** check how practice was kept through Ottoman and socialist pressure, from their own sources.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv and Varna:** check how language and church were kept across generations.
- **Stara Planina and Sredna Gora towns (Koprivshtitsa, Panagyurishte):** check how holding out is celebrated in local heritage, beside Aries's courage.
- **Karakachans:** check accounts of the end of seasonal movement for resistance or acceptance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1984-89 and protest history; mixed for everyday stubbornness; thin for Lipovans and Karakachans.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian including Shop speech; Turkish for 1984-89 testimony; Russian for Lipovan sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Draginov chronicle is disputed; resistance to renaming is contested ground; regional joke genres carry stereotypes and are sources only as genres; the Serbian *inat* literature is comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none; Serbian scholarship on *inat* is kept out as comparison only, not held.
