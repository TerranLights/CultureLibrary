## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Rhodopes (Smolyan, Shiroka Laka) and the Pirin (Bansko, Melnik):** compare mountain song, bagpipe and food traditions, to see whether and how the two ranges differ.
- **Upper Thrace (Kazanlak, Asenovgrad, Plovdiv):** check roses and wine as both pleasure and labor.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie:** check coffeehouse, bath and festival customs from community sources.
- **Roma musicians and wedding bands (Kardzhali, Sliven, Stara Zagora):** check how players describe the pleasure and the work of playing, group by group.
- **Pomak villages:** check wedding food and music in their own words; record self-names.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai):** check music and shared meals in the *cem* as worship through the senses, from community sources.
- **The Danubian Plain and the Northwest (Pleven, Vidin):** check local wine and food against the south.
- **The Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check the sea and summer holiday as pleasure, among locals and seasonal workers.
- **Sephardic, Armenian and Aromanian households:** check community cookbooks and memoirs for dishes and smells of home.
- **Karakachans and Lipovans:** check dairy, fasting and feast customs from their own sources; likely thin.
