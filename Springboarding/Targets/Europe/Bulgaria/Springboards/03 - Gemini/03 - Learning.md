## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Stara Planina craft towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa) and the plains: check whether apprenticeship ran differently in guild towns than on farms.
- Sofia and the Shopluk: compare the capital's tutoring market with village schools nearby.
- The Northwest (Vidin, Montana): check test-score and enrollment gaps, a region the national figures may average away.
- Kardzhali and the Ludogorie: check Turkish mother-tongue classes and the *mektep* tradition, and how children move between two languages.
- Pomak villages of the Rhodopes and Pirin: check how schooling, religious teaching and family teaching combine; self-names are contested.
- Roma of several groups (Erli, Kalderash, Turkish-speaking Roma, Rudari): separate them before using any school statistic, since group and language shape access.
- Bulgarian Catholics around Plovdiv and Protestants: check their parish and mission schools as distinct traditions.
- The Sephardic community (Sofia, Plovdiv, Ruse): check the shift from community schools after 1944.
- Armenians and Karakachans: sources likely scarce; check community Sunday schools and summer camps through their associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Revival-era schooling and national test results; mixed for learning in families and crafts; thin for Gagauz, Karakachan and Aromanian children's learning.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian and Church Slavonic for the schools before 1878; Ottoman Turkish and modern Turkish for *mektep* records; French and Judeo-Spanish for the Alliance archive.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school segregation of Roma children is a live public issue; the winding-down of Turkish-language schooling under socialism needs its dates checked and is still political; Revival school history is often told as a national myth, so read sources critically.
- **Held for review:** none; the national external assessments remain though Roma school segregation is a live issue, and the statutes on mother-tongue Turkish classes belong with Libra and Capricorn.
