# Bulgaria: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian; Ottoman Turkish for court registers; Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani (Kalderash) for the *kris*; Armenian; French and English for 1943 scholarship. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between (broker, interpreter, intercessor). Law and courts go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Sofia and Shopluk villages: check whether quarrels go to a respected kin member, a priest or the town hall first.
- Kyustendil, Plovdiv and Sofia: check local memory of the 1943 interventions, since each town's role is told differently.
- Roma of several groups: compare the Kalderash *kris* with go-betweens among Erli, Turkish-speaking Roma and Rudari.
- The Rhodopes and the Pirin region: check go-betweens between Pomak, Christian and Turkish villages, using self-chosen names.
- Kardzhali and the Ludogorie: check the imam's and the mayor's role as mediators, and how the Alevi *dede* figures in Kızılbaş and Babai communities.
- Dobruja and the Black Sea coast: check mixed villages (Tatars, Gagauz, Lipovans) for who goes between neighbors of different faiths.
- The Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain: check rural depopulation, which may leave few elders to settle disputes.
- Upper Thrace and Stara Zagora: check matchmaking go-betweens in Catholic and Orthodox villages separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1943 and the 1990 Round Table; mixed for village go-betweens; thin for the *kris* and for Alevi practice, where community-led studies should come first.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for most; Ottoman Turkish for *sulh* entries; Romani (Kalderash) for the *kris*; Turkish of Bulgaria for the Hodja tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1943 is contested (the old lands against the occupied territories); minority consultative bodies are political; the *kris* touches customary law, which goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the Kalderash *kris* pointer remains for how elders settle quarrels, its customary-law side handed to Libra or Capricorn, and the *sulh* entries are read for practice alone.
