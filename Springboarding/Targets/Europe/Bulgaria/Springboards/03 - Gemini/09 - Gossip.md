*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its village speech; Old Bulgarian for early moral collections; Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Judeo-Spanish. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Stara Planina towns (Sopot, Koprivshtitsa, Gabrovo): check café talk in the towns Vazov and others wrote about against present records.
- Sofia's apartment blocks: compare block-entrance talk (*входа*) with village talk; check whether forums replace it.
- The Shopluk and the Northwest (Vidin, Montana): check whether depopulation changes who is talked about and by whom.
- The Rhodopes (Smolyan, Kardzhali): compare *седянка* customs in Christian, Pomak and Turkish villages, by community.
- The Ludogorie and Dobruja: check Turkish, Gagauz, Tatar and Alevi villages for shared and separate places of talk.
- Roma of several groups and faiths: check how reputation and talk work in each, and avoid treating outsiders' rumors about Roma as data on Roma.
- The Sephardic community: check Sofia and Plovdiv memoirs for neighborhood talk before 1944.
- Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas): check how seasonal tourism brings talk about outsiders.
- Strandzha and the Sredna Gora: check small-village teasing songs as local genres.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and literature; mixed for present-day gossip (forums and tabloids, few studies); thin for Gagauz, Karakachan and Lipovan village talk.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian with village speech for *седянка* songs; Old Bulgarian for the 1073 miscellany; Turkish of Bulgaria for *dedikodu*; Judeo-Spanish for proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** socialist-era informing ties gossip to State Security files; rumors about minorities (Roma, Turks, Pomaks) must be studied as rumors, never repeated as findings; defamation goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the Recent Past institute's oral histories on informers stay despite their tie to State Security files, and defamation as a legal wrong is left to Libra.
