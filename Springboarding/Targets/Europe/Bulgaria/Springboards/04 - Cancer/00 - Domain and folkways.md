## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk around Sofia against the Rhodopes:** check whether household size, inheritance and house form differ between the capital's hinterland and mountain herding villages.
- **Upper Thrace (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora) and the Stara Planina craft towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa):** compare Revival merchant houses with farming households in the same period.
- **Dobruja and the Ludogorie (Deliorman):** settlement was reshaped by population exchanges and resettlement; check how that shows in household records.
- **Danubian Plain (Ruse, Pleven) and the Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** the Northwest has heavy outmigration; check what that does to who is left in the house.
- **Pirin (Blagoevgrad), Strandzha and the Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** refugee families of the early twentieth century; check their own accounts of resettling.
- **Bulgarian Orthodox Christians, Turks, Pomaks and Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai), Kardzhali region especially:** separate by faith and language, then check variation inside each.
- **Roma of several groups and faiths (for example Erli and Kalderash):** compare settled, formerly itinerant, Muslim, Orthodox and Protestant groups.
- **Paulician Catholics around Plovdiv, Protestants, Armenians, the Sephardic community, Gagauz, Lipovan Old Believers in Dobruja, Aromanians and Karakachans:** small communities, each with its own records; check what each keeps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Bulgarian Orthodox majority and for census household tables; mixed for Turks and Pomaks; thin and scattered for Karakachans, Gagauz, Lipovans and Aromanians.
- **Lands:** the tracker records no settled units and no change to the pre-war lands; this sign's sheets are written for Bulgaria as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian (modern and Revival-era orthography, before the 1945 spelling reform), Ottoman Turkish for the registers, modern Turkish, Romani; German and Russian for older scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1984-89 "Revival Process" (forced name changes) and the 1989 expulsions split families; records of that period need care and the communities' own accounts first.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on household or kin was set aside; inheritance law is left for Libra rather than held here.
