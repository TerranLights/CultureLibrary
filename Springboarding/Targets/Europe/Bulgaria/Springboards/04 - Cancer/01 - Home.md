# Bulgaria: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with Rhodope and Shop dialect forms), Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani, Armenian, Judeo-Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and other large cities against villages of the Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check whether "home" points to the flat one lives in or to the village house one inherited.
- **Pirin (Blagoevgrad) and Melnik's old houses against the Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** compare old-house heritage with resort-era building.
- **Turks and Pomaks in the Kardzhali region and the Rhodopes:** look at what changed for the home after the "Revival Process" and the 1989 departures, in their own accounts.
- **Roma of several groups:** separate long-settled urban quarters from formerly itinerant Kalderash families; check whether tenure and house form differ between them.
- **Sephardic Jews and Armenians in Sofia, Plovdiv and Varna:** historical quarters and community houses; check what remains and who records it.
- **Thracian refugee families in Strandzha and Burgas:** check how resettled families spoke of the home left behind.
- **Lipovans and Gagauz in Dobruja and around Varna:** check their own house forms and the records kept.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on dwellings and tenure; mixed on what home means to people; thin for Gagauz, Lipovan and Karakachan homes.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian, including Rhodope and Shop dialect words for parts of the house; Ottoman Turkish for the inventories; Romani for residents of Roma quarters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** demolitions in Roma neighborhoods and homes left behind in 1989 are live grievances; private homes are not open archives.
- **Held for review:** none; tenure law and the rules for legalizing houses in Roma quarters go to Libra, so no home pointer was held back.
