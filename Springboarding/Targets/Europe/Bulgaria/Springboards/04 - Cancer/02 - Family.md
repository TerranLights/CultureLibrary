# Bulgaria: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects, Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani, Aromanian, Gagauz, Armenian. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk against the Rhodopes and Pirin:** compare kin terms and the place a bride lived after marriage, region by region, using the dialect atlas.
- **The Stara Planina towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna) against Danubian Plain villages (Pleven, Ruse):** look at craft and trading families beside farming kin groups.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and Ludogorie, Pomaks of the Western and Central Rhodopes:** separate them; ask how each names and gathers kin.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai):** check how *ocak* descent sits beside ordinary family ties.
- **Roma groups (for example Erli, Kalderash, Rudari):** check marriage and kin-group terms group by group; avoid one Roma pattern.
- **Paulician Catholic villages around Plovdiv (Rakovski) and Protestant congregations:** check godparent and marriage choices across confessional lines.
- **Aromanians and Karakachans:** look for records of herding kin groups and their seasonal reunions.
- **Gagauz and Armenians:** see what records each community keeps of family lines and naming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in ethnography, literature and vital statistics; mixed for minority languages' kin vocabularies; thin for Gagauz and Aromanian kin systems.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian dialect vocabulary (kin terms need the dialect dictionaries, not only the standard one), Turkish, Romani by group, and Gagauz and Aromanian for small but distinct kin systems.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced name changes of 1984-89 and earlier Pomak renamings; early-marriage debates about some Roma groups can turn into stereotype, so use Roma-led sources first.
- **Held for review:** none; family-code questions (marriage age, legal kinship) go to Libra, so nothing on kin was set aside.

## 5. First queries
1. ИБЕ-БАН: find the dialect atlas volumes covering kin terms ("термини за родство").
2. Library: "кумство" and "побратимство" in *Български фолклор* and *Българска етнология*.
3. Regional press archive search: "родова среща" with village names in the Rhodopes and the Northwest.
4. NSI: marriages and births tables by district, 1990 to the latest year.
5. Read *Гераците* and *Под игото* with a list of the kin terms and family duties they show.
