# Bulgaria: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Shop, Strandzha and Rhodope forms), Turkish of Bulgaria, Bulgarian speech of the Pomaks, Romani. **Concept in one line:** keeping someone or something from harm: guarding, defending and shielding the vulnerable, one's own and oneself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk and Pernik against Strandzha:** compare which masked and fire customs each keeps for protecting the community.
- **Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas) and the Danubian ports (Ruse, Vidin):** check protector saints and rites tied to fishing and river work.
- **Turks, Pomaks and Muslim Roma (Kardzhali, Smolyan, the Ludogorie):** check the use of written amulets and evil-eye charms, and who prepares them, community by community.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai) in the northeast:** check their own protective rites and holy places.
- **Erli and other Roma groups:** check how Ederlezi is kept and what it is meant to guard, group by group.
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** herding communities; check guard-dog breeding and flock protection.
- **Paulician Catholics and Lipovan Old Believers:** check protector saints and blessings in their own calendars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk custom and festival protection; mixed for state child-protection outcomes; thin for minority communities' own protective practices.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian dialect for charm texts (check for old vocabulary); Turkish and Arabic script for written amulets; Romani for Ederlezi songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charm texts may be private to the women who recite them; domestic-violence protection became a heated public quarrel (2018), so present all sides as sides; law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none withheld; the child-protection and domestic-violence statutes themselves are left to Libra.
