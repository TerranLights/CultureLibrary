## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Thracian and Macedonian refugee families of the early twentieth century (Burgas, Strandzha, Pirin, Sofia's refugee quarters):** check what their associations recorded of being given, or denied, a roof.
- **Kalderash and other once-itinerant Roma groups:** check family accounts of tent life and of the forced settlement decreed in the late 1950s (1958 *check*).
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** check summer and winter herders' shelters and what remains of them in museums.
- **Rhodope highland villages (Smolyan district):** check seasonal huts and sheepfolds as shelter for people and flocks.
- **Sofia against Vidin, Montana and other shrinking towns:** compare homelessness services and their reach.
- **Armenians and the Sephardic community:** check what each community kept of its own refuge history.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check how their ancestors' arrival as religious refugees is told by them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for refugee-settlement history and current asylum figures; mixed for homelessness (definitions and counts vary); thin for herders' and itinerant groups' shelter.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for social-services and refugee files; Ottoman Turkish for inns and pious foundations; Armenian for community records; French and English for League of Nations papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** asylum seekers' treatment and pushbacks at the border are contested; the forced settlement of itinerant Roma and Karakachans touches family memory; avoid identifying individuals.
- **Held for review:** none; asylum law and the legal status of reception centers go to Libra, so no shelter pointer was held.
