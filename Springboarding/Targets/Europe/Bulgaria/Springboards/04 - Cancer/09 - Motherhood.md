*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk villages, Upper Thrace towns (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora) and Sofia:** compare where women give birth now and in grandmothers' memory, and who helped them.
- **Rhodope Pomak villages and Turkish villages of the Kardzhali region:** check birth and postnatal customs and the place of the husband's mother, village by village.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai):** check rites around birth and the naming of a child.
- **Roma of several groups (Erli, Kalderash, Rudari) and faiths:** check whether mothers' age, birth customs and church or mosque roles differ between groups, using Roma-led sources.
- **Paulician Catholics around Plovdiv:** check Marian feasts and parish mothers' groups beside Orthodox practice.
- **Armenians, the Sephardic community, Gagauz:** check what each community's own press or archive kept on mothers and births.
- **Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and Dobruja:** check how emigration and an aging population change who becomes a mother at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for demographic data and folklore; mixed for mothers' own voices (strong online, thin in print before 1990); thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the apocryphon; Bulgarian for songs and forums; Turkish and Romani for mothers in those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the falling birth rate is often framed as a national alarm; the socialist-era limits on abortion (from 1968 *check*) are a charged history; teen motherhood is used to stigmatize Roma; abortion itself belongs with Aries.
- **Held for review:** none; maternity-leave rules go to Libra and abortion as a concept to Aries; no motherhood pointer was held.
