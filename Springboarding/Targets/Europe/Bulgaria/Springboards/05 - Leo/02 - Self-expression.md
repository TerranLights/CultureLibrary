# Bulgaria: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects; the Turkish of Bulgaria; the Bulgarian speech of the Pomaks; Romani; Old Believer Russian for the Lipovans where apt. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia against small towns of the Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check whether open self-display is discussed differently where depopulation is heavy.
- **Pomak women of the central Rhodopes (Smolyan, Madan) and of the Western Rhodopes (Ribnovo, Satovcha):** compare dress and headscarf debates; labels "Pomak" and "Bulgarian Muslim" are contested, so record self-naming.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** check language use in public as a form of expression after the 1984-89 name changes.
- **Roma (Erli of Sofia, Muslim Roma of Plovdiv's Stolipinovo, Kalderash) and Roma evangelical congregations:** check testimony and song traditions in church as a channel.
- **Generations formed before and after 1989:** compare what each says it can say aloud.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja; Catholics of the Rakovski villages; the Sephardic community:** check whether faith-linked rules on dress and speech shape what members show, from each community's own sources; sources thin.
- **Urban LGBTQ communities in Sofia, Plovdiv and Varna:** check expression against family reaction, from their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the socialist period (files, jokes, émigré writing); mixed for the present; thin for minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for COMDOS joke files and *narezhdane* laments; Rhodope dialect for Pomak headscarf and dress words; Turkish for "Bulgaristan Türk edebiyatı"; Romani for Usin Kerim and *Drom Dromendar*; Old Believer Russian for Lipovan community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state-security files name living people; headscarf and Pride disputes are live; speech rights as law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; every self-expression pointer here passed the standing rules, and speech rights as law were routed to Libra rather than held.
