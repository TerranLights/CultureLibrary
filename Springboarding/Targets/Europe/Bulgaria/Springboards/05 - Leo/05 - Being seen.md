# Bulgaria: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects; the Bulgarian speech of the Pomaks; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Judeo-Spanish; Karakachan Greek. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pomak villages of the Western Rhodopes against the central Rhodopes:** check how bridal display and the presence of outside photographers differ.
- **Sofia against small Danubian and Northwest towns:** compare the evening stroll and the square as stages.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region:** check language visibility in public space (signs, broadcasts).
- **Roma of several groups (Erli, Kalderash, Muslim Roma of Stolipinovo and Fakulteta):** check being watched and being ignored as both appear in their own accounts.
- **Karakachans:** check evil-eye beliefs and dress at gatherings, from Karakachan associations; sources thin.
- **Sephardic families (Ruse, Plovdiv, Sofia) and Armenians:** check memoirs for how a minority household handled public view.
- **The elderly in depopulated villages:** check who visits, phones or looks in on them, by region.
- **Gagauz of the Varna area and Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check whether either community has its own rules on being photographed or shown in public; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich folklore on the evil eye and witnesses; modern media data exists; thin on everyday feeling.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for *uroki* and *baene* in Marinov and the "Рибново сватба" literature; Turkish for *nazar* and BNT's Turkish newscasts; Karakachan Greek for *máti*; Judeo-Spanish for *aojado* in Ruse households; Romani for Fakulteta residents' accounts of being watched.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** check how Western Rhodope communities view outside photography of their weddings before reusing any images; news material on Roma may include hate speech; evil-eye healing is private practice.
- **Held for review:** none; no visibility or evil-eye pointer was set aside, and Rhodope wedding images wait on community consent rather than review.
