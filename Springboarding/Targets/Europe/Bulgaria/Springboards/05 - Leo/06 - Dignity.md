# Bulgaria: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Armenian; Karakachan Greek; Ottoman Turkish for court records. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** check how 1984-89 is told as an injury to names and bearing, family by family.
- **Pomaks:** check the earlier name-change campaigns (1912-13, 1940s, 1970s, with Kornitsa in the Western Rhodopes as a known case) in Pomak testimony; labels are contested.
- **Roma of several groups and faiths:** compare accounts from Sofia's Fakulteta, Plovdiv's Stolipinovo and smaller towns.
- **Pensioners in the Northwest and Danubian Plain against Sofia:** check how "a dignified old age" is described where incomes differ.
- **Karakachans:** check how the settling of the herding families around the 1950s is remembered, in their own associations' publications.
- **The Sephardic community:** check memoirs of the 1940-44 restrictions for how bearing was kept.
- **Armenians and Aromanians:** check community accounts; sources thin.
- **Disabled people and their families:** check their own organizations rather than institutional reports alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the communist period and minorities' injuries; mixed for care and old age; thin for Aromanians and Gagauz.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for complaint cases in the Sofia *sicil*; French for Todorov's *Au nom du peuple*; Bulgarian for Ombudsman care-home reports and *другарю*/*господине* address studies; Turkish for *haysiyet*; Romani for *manušipe* in eviction accounts; Karakachan Greek for *axioprépeia*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors' testimony and children in care need care in reuse; rights as written law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the testimony collections on camps and care are kept, with reuse of survivors' words left to the later research's care.
