## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie, and families split by the 1989 departures:** check how the same events are told by those who left, came back, or stayed (emigrants themselves are comparison only).
- **Pomaks of the Lovech and Teteven area against those of the Rhodopes:** check memories of name-change campaigns by region; labels are contested.
- **Roma of several groups:** check school and workplace accounts from Erli, Kalderash, Rudari and Muslim Roma separately.
- **Thracian and Macedonian refugee families:** check family memory of 1913 and 1919 as told in their own societies.
- **Former conscripts:** compare generations before and after 2008.
- **Women in rural Rhodope, Northwest and Dobruja villages:** check how gossip and reputation work, from women's own accounts.
- **LGBTQ youth in Sofia and in smaller towns:** check school-bullying reports.
- **Armenians and the Sephardic community:** check memoirs for humiliations under past restrictions; sources thin outside the 1940s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collective humiliations and satire; mixed for everyday bullying; thin for Karakachans and Gagauz.
- **Languages needed:** Byzantine Greek for Skylitzes on Kleidion; Bulgarian for *Bay Ganyo* criticism, Shrovetide mocking songs and army-hazing press; Turkish for *rezil olmak* in 1989 expulsion testimony; Romani for *ladž* in segregated-school accounts; Armenian for *nvastatsum* in community memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1984-89 and the 1913-1919 losses are politically live; violence against women needs survivor-centered sources; punishment as law belongs to Libra, and revenge to Scorpio.
