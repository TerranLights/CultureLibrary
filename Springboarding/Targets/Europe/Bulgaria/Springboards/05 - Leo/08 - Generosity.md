## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stara Planina towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa):** compare Revival benefactors' giving with the Gabrovo joke tradition about thrift.
- **Rhodope Christian and Pomak villages:** compare the *kurban* and the work bee across faiths in neighboring villages.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** check bayram and *sadaka* customs, and giving to the mosque.
- **Alevi communities (Kızılbaş and Babai):** check shared meals at the Demir Baba and Otman Baba tekkes as separate traditions.
- **Roma of several groups:** check wedding money customs and church giving among evangelical Roma, from their own accounts.
- **Upper Thrace villages and the Paulician Catholic villages near Plovdiv:** compare Catholic and Orthodox name-day and saint's-day treating.
- **Armenians and Sephardic Jews:** check community welfare societies; sources thin.
- **Dobruja (Lipovans, Gagauz):** check community festivals that share food; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Revival philanthropy and folklore of gifts; mixed for modern giving data; thin for smaller communities.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for town *vakfiye* deeds; Bulgarian for *chitalishte* donor lists and IEFSEM work on the *kurban*; Turkish for *sadaka* and bayram giving in Chief Mufti materials; Hebrew and Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic mutual-aid society histories; Romani for wedding-money customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gifts to officials shade into corruption, which belongs elsewhere; the Gabrovo joke tradition should be read as a town's own humor, not a trait.
- **Held for review:** none; the charity, endowment and remittance pointers all stand, and gifts to officials were left to other signs rather than held.
