*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Karakachan Greek; Old Bulgarian and medieval Greek for the khanate and tsardom. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stara Planina and Sredna Gora towns (Koprivshtitsa, Panagyurishte):** check uprising-era leaders and how each town remembers its own.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** compare party, mosque and village leadership.
- **Alevi communities:** separate Kızılbaş and Babai lineage leadership.
- **Pomaks of the Rhodopes and the Pirin region (Blagoevgrad):** check the role of imams, mayors and elders; labels contested.
- **Roma of several groups and faiths:** check Kalderash councils, Muslim Roma neighborhoods and evangelical pastors as separate forms of leading.
- **Karakachans:** check the *tsélingas* model and what remained after settlement; Greek-language scholarship only as comparison.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and Danubian Plain:** check village *kmet* elections in small, aging communities.
- **Armenians, the Sephardic community, Catholics of Rakovski:** check community and parish leadership; sources moderate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national leaders and uprisings; mixed for local and minority leadership; thin for Karakachans and Gagauz.
