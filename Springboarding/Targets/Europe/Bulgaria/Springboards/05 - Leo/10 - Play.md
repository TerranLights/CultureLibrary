## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia against Rhodope and Pirin villages:** check whether street and field play differs where children are few.
- **Turks of the Ludogorie and of the Kardzhali region:** compare wrestling and fair games, and children's game names in Turkish.
- **Pomak villages:** check games tied to bayram and to the Christian calendar of neighbors; labels contested.
- **Roma of several groups:** check children's games and dance-play at weddings, from Roma sources where possible.
- **Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and Dobruja:** compare Todorovden horse races and fair contests.
- **Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check beach and resort play against inland towns.
- **Gagauz, Lipovans, Armenians, Karakachans:** check community games and festivals; sources thin.
- **Older and younger adults:** compare card and board-game clubs with gaming and esports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk games and trickster tales; mixed for modern play; thin for smaller minorities.
