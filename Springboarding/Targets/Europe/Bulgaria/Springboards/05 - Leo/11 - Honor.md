# Bulgaria: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian and its dialects; the Turkish of Bulgaria and the speech of the Pomaks; Romani; Karakachan Greek; Armenian; Judeo-Spanish. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stara Planina and Sredna Gora towns:** check hajduk and uprising songs for local codes of the given word.
- **The Shopluk against the Rhodopes:** compare betrothal customs and what each region records about a bride's good name.
- **Pomak villages of the Western and central Rhodopes:** check *namus* in their own accounts; labels contested.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** compare *namus*, *şeref* and the word given, from Turkish-language sources.
- **Roma of several groups and faiths:** check *pativ* group by group; do not generalize across groups.
- **Karakachans:** check honor terms in their own associations, using Campbell only as comparison.
- **Thracian refugee families of Strandzha and Burgas:** check the Petrova Niva gathering and family memory of the 1903 uprising.
- **The Sephardic community and Armenians:** check how honor appears in community publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for songs and military honor; mixed for family honor; thin for current practice in smaller groups.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for Stoin's hajduk songs and the "честна дума" market studies; Turkish for *namus* and *şeref* among Kardzhali families; Romani for Kalderash *pativ*; Karakachan Greek for *filótimo*; Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic proverb collections; Armenian for *pativ* in Plovdiv's press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-honor customs touch women's control and should be researched in women's own words; feud and revenge belong to Scorpio, duty to Capricorn, honor codes as law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; Campbell's Greek fieldwork is kept as comparison only, and no other honor pointer was set aside.
