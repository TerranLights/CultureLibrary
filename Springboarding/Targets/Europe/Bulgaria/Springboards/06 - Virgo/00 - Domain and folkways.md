# Bulgaria: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Cyrillic) and its dialects; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; the Pomaks' Bulgarian speech; Armenian and Karakachan Greek where apt. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: work, service, the daily round, health, the kept house, craft and rest, and how they are grouped.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and the Shopluk:** check whether the *моба* survives beside a commuter capital.
- **Upper Thrace (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora) and the Kazanlak valley:** check the rose, tobacco and vegetable seasons, and whether the Paulician Catholic villages around Rakovski set rest days by their own parish calendar.
- **The Rhodopes and the Kardzhali region:** Pomak villages (Smolyan, Madan) and Turkish villages; compare mining and tobacco records, noting that the 1970s and 1984-89 name changes break record linkage.
- **Ludogorie and Dobruja:** Turkish and Alevi (Kızılbaş, Babai) villages near Razgrad; Lipovan fishing villages (Tataritsa, Kazashko, *check*); check whether grain and fishing give separate yearly rounds.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Danubian Plain (Ruse, Pleven):** check how farm work shifts to pensioners as villages empty; Chiprovtsi weaving as a craft economy.
- **Stara Planina towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa) and the Sredna Gora:** guild and textile-mill archives.
- **Roma communities:** separate Erli, Kalderash, Rudari and Turkish-speaking Muslim Roma, since trade-based group names change what a work source covers.
- **Karakachan and Aromanian herding families** (Sliven, Kotel; Peshtera, *check*): the transhumant year; few sources.
- **Strandzha and the coast:** descendants of Thracian refugees after 1913; check how resort jobs reshape a village year.
- **Armenian and Sephardic communities** (Plovdiv, Varna, Sofia): urban trades, via their organizations' papers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the folklore era and socialism; mixed after 1989; thin for Karakachans, Aromanians, Gagauz, Lipovans.
- **Lands:** the tracker records no change to the pre-war lands, and the exact territory is still to be settled by the author; this Virgo set is written for Bulgaria as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for СбНУ, NSI and the archives; Ottoman Turkish for the defters; modern Turkish for Kardzhali and Ludogorie interviews; Romani for group-internal trade names; Western Armenian only for that community's papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** socialist labor camps (Belene, Lovech); construction troops that drafted many minority conscripts; the 1984-89 name changes; Roma labor stereotypes in older sources, read as claims.
- **Held for review:** none; every pointer on work ways, routine and the kept house passed the standing rules.
