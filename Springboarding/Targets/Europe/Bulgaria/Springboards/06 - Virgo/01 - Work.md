# Bulgaria: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Cyrillic, with Rhodope, Shop and Balkan speech); the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Karakachan Greek; Aromanian; the Lipovans' Russian. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dobruja grain estates and Ludogorie:** check whether hired harvest labor came from Turkish, Gagauz or Bulgarian villages, and how the harvest records name them.
- **Madan, Rudozem and the western Rhodopes:** compare Pomak mining towns with Smolyan's tobacco villages; mine closures and the name campaigns split the sources in time.
- **Kardzhali region:** check tobacco-family labor across ages and sexes in Turkish villages, using Turkish-language interviews rather than state reports alone.
- **Pernik, Bobov Dol and the Shopluk:** coal and steel towns; check how miners' and steelworkers' own terms differ from the city's office workforce.
- **Lyaskovets, Gorna Oryahovitsa and the Veliko Tarnovo district:** check gardener-migrant families as a separate career pattern.
- **Kazanlak valley and Sredna Gora:** check rose pickers by age and origin, and whether seasonal hands came from Roma neighborhoods nearby.
- **Roma groups:** compare Kalderash, Rudari and Muslim Roma trades separately; check how each group's own term for work is used.
- **Karakachan herding companies:** check how the *tselingas* unit divided tasks before forced settlement; scarce Bulgarian-language sources.
- **Lipovan fishing villages:** check the fishing year; very thin data, so start with the Silistra and Varna regional museums.
- **Danubian Plain (Pleven, Ruse):** check cooperative-farm work memory against the mountain villages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for socialism and the Revival; mixed for the 1990s collapse of work; thin for Lipovans and Karakachans.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for Paisius, Gerov and the novels; Turkish for Kardzhali tobacco interviews; Romani for group trade names in *Studii Romani*; Greek for Karakachan herding terms; Russian for the Lipovan fishing vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor (the Belene and Lovech camps; wartime labor service of Jewish men, 1941-44); the 1920 compulsory labor service and any rules on idleness are law: Libra for their text, Capricorn for their spirit.
- **Held for review:** none; no labor-history, literary or folklore pointer on work had to be set aside.
