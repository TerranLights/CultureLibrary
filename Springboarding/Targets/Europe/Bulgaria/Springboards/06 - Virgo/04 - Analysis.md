## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and its IT and outsourcing sector:** check how analytic work is recruited and named, against the older engineering institutes.
- **Plovdiv, Varna and Ruse:** mathematics high schools and technical universities (Ruse, Gabrovo); check whether regional school results follow the capital's pattern.
- **Kardzhali region and Ludogorie:** Turkish home-language pupils; check assessment results against school resources before drawing any inference.
- **Roma neighborhoods:** check segregated-school data and NGO tutoring programs; separate access from aptitude in every source.
- **Shumen and the Muslim schools:** check the Nüvvab archive and its graduates' later roles.
- **The Sephardic community (Sofia, Plovdiv):** check how its scholarly and professional history is recorded before and after 1948 emigration.
- **Rhodope and Stara Planina herding and farming villages:** check older people's weather and animal lore as a form of inference.
- **Armenian community schools:** check curricula and contests; few sources.
- **Pravets and the former IZOT plants:** check engineers' memoirs of the 1980s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval texts, school data and computing history; mixed for everyday reckoning; thin for Armenian and Muslim school archives.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for Hrabar and the Exarch; Bulgarian for NSI and assessment reports; Turkish (and Ottoman script) for Nüvvab material; Hebrew for the Nikopol scholarship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school results by ethnicity and segregated schooling; the virus era touches crime. Rabbinic and Islamic legal reasoning is law: Libra.
