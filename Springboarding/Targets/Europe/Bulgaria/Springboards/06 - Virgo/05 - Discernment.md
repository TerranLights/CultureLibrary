## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Pirin region (Petrich, Melnik, Blagoevgrad):** pilgrimage to Rupite and the wine trade; check how both kinds of judgment, spiritual and tasting, are described locally.
- **Strandzha:** check whether the hesychast past is part of local memory or only of scholarship.
- **Veliko Tarnovo and the Danubian Plain:** monastic and medieval-capital sources; check monastery guides and sermons on discernment today.
- **Ludogorie and the Haskovo region:** Alevi (Kızılbaş, Babai) communities; check whose counsel is sought and how, through oral accounts.
- **Pomak villages of the Pirin and Rhodopes:** check how matches are judged and by whom; separate the Garmen and Smolyan areas.
- **Sofia's urban audiences:** check media-trust and scam-report data against small towns.
- **Kazanlak valley and Upper Thrace:** check how rose and wine quality judgments are learned on the job.
- **Lipovan Old Believers in Dobruja:** check polemics about the true rite as a form of discernment; very thin.
- **Roma communities:** check outsiders' fortune-telling stereotypes against the communities' own accounts before using any source.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval religious texts and folklore; mixed for consumer judgment; thin for Alevi and Lipovan sources.
- **Languages needed:** Greek for Gregory of Sinai and the Kallistos *Life*; Church Slavonic for the Synodicon; Turkish for Nasreddin variants and Alevi oral accounts; Bulgarian for consumer and media-literacy reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Vanga is a living object of devotion and commercialization; Alevi ritual knowledge may be restricted to members; outsiders' fortune-telling stereotypes about Roma. Anti-discrimination rules (prejudice as false judgment) are law: Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the seer and divination pointers fall under no held category, though the Vanga material needs care.
