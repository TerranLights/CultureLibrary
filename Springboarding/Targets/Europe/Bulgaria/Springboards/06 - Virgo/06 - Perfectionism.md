## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stara Planina craft towns (Tryavna, Gabrovo, Koprivshtitsa):** check guild standards in woodcarving and building against today's heritage restorers.
- **The Northwest (Chiprovtsi, Montana):** Catholic heritage and weaving cooperatives; check how weavers describe a flawed carpet.
- **Sofia and the sports schools:** check gymnastics and music academies' training regimes through former students' own accounts.
- **The Shopluk and Pernik:** compare *survakari* and Bistritsa singing standards; check who decides what is "correct".
- **Muslim communities (Shumen, Razgrad, Kardzhali):** check Qur'an recitation teaching and mosque crafts.
- **Dolen and Satovcha (Pirin region), with Christian and Pomak singers (*check*):** *visoko* multipart singing (UNESCO list, 2021, *check*); check how singers judge an exact performance.
- **Armenian community (Plovdiv):** check church choirs and craft trades; few sources.
- **Roma musicians (wedding bands of the Plovdiv and Stara Zagora areas):** check how musicians themselves rate mastery.
- **Karakachan families:** check whether woven textile standards are recorded; little is published.
