# Bulgaria: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Cyrillic) and Old Bulgarian; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Armenian; Romani where community media exist. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia's press and theater world:** check how critics and satirists describe their own limits before and after 1989.
- **Gabrovo and the Stara Planina towns:** check whether local humor is told as self-criticism or as outsiders' joke, and who tells it.
- **Ruse and the Danubian Plain:** check the 1988 protest memory against later environmental activism in the city.
- **Turkish communities of Kardzhali and Ludogorie:** check Turkish-language press and party media for criticism of the state, especially around 1984-89; the "Revival Process" label itself is contested.
- **Pomak villages of the Rhodopes:** check how the name campaigns were criticized locally, in contemporary records and in later oral history alike.
- **Roma communities:** check Roma-run media and NGOs as critics of public institutions, separately by group.
- **Upper Thrace villages:** check the mother-in-law and daughter-in-law repertoire by region.
- **Armenian community:** check the community press for criticism inside the community.
- **Strandzha and the coast:** check local press critique of resort building, set against Sofia's national press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, literature and socialist records; mixed for family criticism; thin for minority-language press.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for Cosmas; Bulgarian for *Стършел* and the party protocols; Turkish for the Kardzhali press and 1984-89 oral history; Armenian only for that community's papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Markov murder (1978); surveillance files on critics (Committee for Disclosing Documents, *check*); the 1984-89 name changes and the 1989 expulsions; lawsuits against journalists. Censorship and defamation rules are law: Libra for the text, Capricorn for the spirit.
- **Held for review:** none; no satire, press or party-archive pointer was held back.
