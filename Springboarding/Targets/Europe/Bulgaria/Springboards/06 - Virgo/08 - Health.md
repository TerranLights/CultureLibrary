# Bulgaria: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Cyrillic, with Rhodope and western dialect healing terms); the Turkish of Bulgaria and Pomak Bulgarian speech; Romani; the Lipovans' Russian. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Rhodopes (Smolyan, Velingrad) and the Kardzhali region:** check Pomak and Turkish household healing (*muska*, *nazar*, whether a hodja is consulted) alongside clinic use; separate the two communities' sources.
- **The Pirin region (Sandanski, Petrich):** spa and pilgrimage economies; check how visitors and locals describe health seeking there.
- **Strandzha:** fire-dancing (*нестинарство*) feasts tied to saints and healing (*check*); check villagers' own accounts.
- **Roma neighborhoods of Sofia, Plovdiv and Sliven:** check access and vaccination data with mediators' reports; separate groups and faiths.
- **The Northwest and Danubian Plain villages:** check distance to care and ambulance times by village size and age profile.
- **Sofia and the Shopluk:** private clinics and alternative medicine markets; check against public hospital data.
- **White Brotherhood followers (Sofia, Rila):** check health practice in the movement's own publications.
- **Alevi communities near Razgrad:** check healing visits to the Demir Baba tekke, through oral accounts.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check fasting rules and healing customs; very thin.
- **Sephardic and Armenian communities:** check community doctors and charitable clinics in their histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk medicine and official statistics; mixed for mental health; thin for Lipovan and Alevi healing.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for Dimkov, the charm corpus and НЦОЗА data; Turkish for amulet and *nazar* practice in Kardzhali and Ludogorie; Romani for health-mediator fieldwork; Church Slavonic and Russian for Lipovan fasting texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some charm healers keep words secret or pass them only to an heir; mental-health institutions; vaccine debates around Roma neighborhoods. Health-care law and patient rights are law: Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the folk-healing and spa pointers fall under no category the standing rules hold.
