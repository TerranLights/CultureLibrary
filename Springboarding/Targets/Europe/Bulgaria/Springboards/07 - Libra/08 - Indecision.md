*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Voters in Sofia against those in small towns of the Northwest and the Danubian Plain: check turnout and the "I support no one" share across the 2021-2024 elections.
- Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie: check family accounts of the 1989 choice to leave or to stay, and of the return of some.
- Pomaks of the Rhodopes and the Pirin region (a contested label): check how the optional census question on ethnicity is answered or left blank, without treating any answer as the true one.
- Roma groups and the Romanian-speaking Rudari: check self-declaration choices in the census and what drives them.
- Gagauz near Varna: check census self-declaration and language choice; thin sources, so start with local cultural associations.
- Young people in Blagoevgrad, Plovdiv and Varna university towns: check how decisions to emigrate are delayed or reversed.
- Families of Thracian refugees from 1913 and Bulgarians of Southern Dobruja in 1940: check accounts of whether, when and how to move.
- Clients of seers and diviners in the Petrich area and in Sofia: check who consults, about which choices, from published studies only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elections, referendums and big projects; mixed for personal decisions; thin for Gagauz and Rudari self-declaration.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic-flavored eighteenth-century Bulgarian for Paisius; Turkish for 1989 family testimony; Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic phrasing of fate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1915, 1941 and 1944 decisions are argued hotly by historians and parties; seers like Vanga draw both devotion and ridicule; census ethnicity answers among Pomaks, Turks and Roma are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none; Valtchinova's work on Vanga of Rupite is kept, and seers' clients are approached through published studies alone, given the devotion and ridicule the seer draws.
