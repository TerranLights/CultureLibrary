## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Orthodox Bulgarians of the Shopluk, Gabrovo and the Danubian Plain: check how regional joke cycles and nicknames portray neighbors, and what each region says about itself in return.
- Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie: check how they have been portrayed in state media across regimes, and how their own press portrays the majority.
- Pomaks of the Rhodopes and the Pirin region (a contested label): check how rival national narratives claim or blame them, using their own testimony first.
- Roma groups (Erli, Kalderash, Rudari, Turkish-speaking Roma): check moral-panic coverage after crimes, and how each group answers it.
- Sephardic community: check how the 1940 law and its aftermath are explained, and by whom, in community memory.
- Thracian and Macedonian refugee families: check whether family accounts of the losses of 1913 and 1919 point to neighbors, the Great Powers or Bulgaria's own leaders.
- Karakachans and Aromanians: check what outsiders have called them and how they describe the labels; sources are scarce, start with their associations.
- Armenians and Gagauz: check evil-eye customs and how misfortune is explained in each community's own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, Balkanism and the 1940 law; mixed for present-day stereotype surveys; thin for Karakachan, Aromanian and Gagauz material.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the Bogomil-era texts in Ivanov; nineteenth-century Bulgarian for the Revival polemics; Turkish for *nazar* customs and the minority press; Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Batak dispute, "Turkish slavery" against "Ottoman rule", and the "Revival Process" divide public opinion; hate-speech material names real people; regional jokes and Gabrovo humor must be read as texts, never as traits of a region or people.
- **Held for review:** none; the Marginalia hate-speech monitoring stays despite naming real people, and the 1940 Law for the Protection of the Nation anchors the legal strand.
