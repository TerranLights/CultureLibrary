# Bulgaria: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (and dialect forms), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani, Aromanian (Vlach) for the Northwest. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how dying, the dead and the meaning of death are understood; grief and mourning are researched here.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhodope and Pirin Pomak villages against Kardzhali Turkish villages:** check which burial and memorial practices each community records in its own words, and whether language and local history set the records apart.
- **Vlach (Aromanian- and Romanian-speaking) communities of the Northwest (Vidin):** check for memorial feasts recorded separately from Bulgarian *помен*.
- **Strandzha and Thracian refugee families:** check whether graves left behind after 1913 appear in their memorial practice.
- **Erli, Kalderash, Rudari and Muslim Roma:** check each group's own accounts of mourning dress and the period of mourning.
- **Sofia's Sephardic Jews, Armenians and Catholics:** check their sections of the Central Sofia Cemetery and their community records for memorial dates.
- **Karakachans:** check for burial records along transhumant routes and in settled villages after the 1950s.
- **Sofia against villages; generations before and after 1989:** check how socialist-era civil funerals changed who spoke over the dead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for laments and Orthodox custom; good statistics; thin for Karakachan and Gagauz death customs, where community associations and oral history are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the *Требник* and the apocryphon; 18th-century vernacular for the *damaskini*; Turkish for *mevlit* and Kardzhali fieldwork; Romani for the *mulo* lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and families' privacy; human remains in ossuaries and excavations; 1984-89 changes to Muslim graves and names on headstones (contested).
- **Held for review:** nothing held from this sheet; assisted dying and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn, and spirit-contact belongs to Pisces.
