# Bulgaria: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani (Kalderash and other Vlax dialects). **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk (Pernik, Sofia) *survakari* against Sredna Gora (Kalofer) Epiphany rites:** check whether each recorded rite is framed as cleansing, and by whom.
- **Pomaks and Turks:** check fasting and ablution practice in Kardzhali, Smolyan and Shumen separately, and how each community remembers the identity campaigns.
- **Kalderash against Erli and other Roma:** check whether *marime* appears in each group's own speech, so a Vlax term is not applied to all.
- **Alevi Kızılbaş and Babai:** check for the practice of putting a member outside the community (*düşkün*), and whether it is spoken of at all.
- **Families of People's Court defendants and families of camp prisoners:** check which side's memoirs reached print and when.
- **The Sephardic community, 1941-44:** check community memoirs on exclusion from public life; the legal texts belong to Libra.
- **Spa towns of the Rhodopes and Pirin (Velingrad, Sandanski):** check local museum material on bathing customs across faiths.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the People's Court and calendar rites; mixed for folk healing; thin for Alevi and Kalderash practice, where trust must come before any record.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for the Synodikon; Bulgarian for court files and press; Turkish for ablution and fasting accounts; Kalderash Romani for *marime*; English and French for the Carnegie report.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity campaigns against Pomaks and Turks; descendants of the purged and of the purgers; Roma purity rules belong to the community and are easily misused against it.
- **Held for review:** nothing held here; the legal side of lustration and of the 1941-44 exclusions is routed to Libra and Capricorn rather than set aside.
