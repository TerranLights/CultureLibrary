# Bulgaria: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria; Judeo-Spanish for historical Sephardic sources. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lovech and the Stara Planina towns against national commemoration:** check whether local accounts of Levski's arrest name the same betrayer as the textbooks.
- **Sredna Gora towns of the April Uprising (Koprivshtitsa, Panagyurishte, Batak):** check which families are remembered locally as informers, and whether that memory is spoken openly.
- **Dobruja villages under collectivization:** check denunciation in local archives against Petrov's fiction.
- **Turks of Kardzhali and the Ludogorie; Pomaks of Smolyan:** check oral histories of 1984-89 separately for each community and each district.
- **The Sephardic community of Sofia, Plovdiv and Kyustendil:** check its memoirs for who stood by it in 1943, beside the deportations from the occupied territories.
- **Former State Security officers, informers and those they watched:** check whose memoirs exist and how each names the act.
- **Roma communities:** check Roma NGOs' reports (for example Amalipe) for institutional promises felt as broken.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Revival and for State Security files; mixed for 1984-89 (memory is divided); thin for private infidelity outside forums and talk shows.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for COMDOS decisions and Stoin's songs; Ottoman Turkish for any original Levski trial records; Turkish for Kardzhali oral histories; Judeo-Spanish and Hebrew for older Sephardic community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** naming informers harms living families; 1943 is a contested national memory (rescue at home, deportation from occupied lands); Turkish and Pomak oral histories need consent and anonymity.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here; the legal side of treason and of the dossier law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
