## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northwest villages (Vidin, Montana) against the Danubian Plain (Ruse, Pleven):** check whether collectivization records show different patterns of local control.
- **Rhodope Turks and Pomaks displaced by reservoirs:** check resettlement files and oral history by village, kept separate by community.
- **Karakachans:** check community memoirs of settlement and loss of flocks, since state files give only one side.
- **Roma groups affected by the 1950s rules on settling mobile groups:** check which groups' own accounts survive (Kalderash, Rudari and others).
- **Stara Planina towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna) and their guild records:** check how craftsmen's guilds steered members' work.
- **Upper Thrace and Pirin wine villages:** check how *Трифон Зарезан* is kept today and who enforces limits.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check whether their own community rules on conduct have been studied at all; sources may be in Russian.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collectivization and surveillance; mixed for household control (NGO reports only); thin for Karakachans and Lipovans.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for St. Ivan's Testament; Ottoman Turkish for the pass-guard registers; Karakachan Greek for herding terms; Russian for Lipovan community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivors of domestic violence; surveillance files name living people; forced settlement of Karakachans and Roma is painful history; the Kalderash tribunal (*kris*) is a customary legal body, so its rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; St. Ivan's Testament is kept here for obedience in community, while self-discipline belongs to Capricorn.
