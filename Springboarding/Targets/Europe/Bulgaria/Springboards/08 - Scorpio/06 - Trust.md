## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia against small towns and villages:** check whether survey trust items differ by settlement size before reading any pattern into them.
- **Revival-era merchant towns (Plovdiv, Koprivshtitsa, Gabrovo, Tryavna):** check merchant papers for credit networks within and across faiths.
- **Turks of Kardzhali and the Ludogorie; Pomaks of Smolyan:** check survey samples for each, and the community press, for whom each trusts after 1984-89.
- **Roma of several groups:** check health-mediator reports by district and by group, since the program covers very different neighborhoods.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv and Varna:** check community histories for church, school and credit bodies run on trust.
- **Gagauz and Lipovans of Dobruja:** check whether any survey or community study touches trust at all; likely none.
- **Generations before and after 1996-97:** check memoirs and press for how the bank failures shaped saving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys and polling; mixed in ethnology (*кумство* well studied, sworn kinship less); thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for Slaveykov, merchant papers and polls; Ottoman Turkish in some merchant ledgers; Turkish for Kardzhali radio; Vlax Romani to test *pakiv*; Armenian for community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** health-mediator data concern a marginalized group; informal payments touch corruption allegations against named doctors; trust in police and courts is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none held on this sheet; contract trust in writing (the letter of an agreement) is routed to Libra.
