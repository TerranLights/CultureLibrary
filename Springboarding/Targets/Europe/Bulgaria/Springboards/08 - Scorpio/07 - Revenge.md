# Bulgaria: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Rhodope and Balkan dialect forms where recorded), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhodope Christian and Pomak villages (Haitov's ground):** check whether local memory of feuds matches the stories, village by village.
- **Pirin region (Blagoevgrad, Petrich):** check family memories of interwar killings against the published histories.
- **Northwest (Vratsa, Montana, Vidin) after 1923:** check reprisal lists and memorials kept by each side.
- **Stara Planina and Sliven haiduk country:** check which reprisals are honored and which are forgotten.
- **Turks of Kardzhali and Roma groups (Erli, Kalderash, Muslim Roma):** check each community's curse vocabulary in its own language, not through Bulgarian summaries.
- **Sofia in the 1990s:** check press archives on score-settling by district and year.
- **Strandzha villages and Thracian refugee families:** check for revenge or reconciliation motifs in songs of 1903 and 1913.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, film and political history; mixed in ethnology (curses well recorded, feuds less so); crime data good but narrow.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian dialect for Haitov and Hitov; Turkish for *beddua* and Hodja tales; Ottoman Turkish in Arabic script for the *sicil* volumes not yet translated; Romani for *armaja*; southwestern (Pirin) dialect forms in family memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interwar killings still divide families; contract-killing reporting names people never convicted; curses can be tied to a group in a hostile way, so record them as each community describes them.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; private vengeance set against the state's punishment is routed to Capricorn.
