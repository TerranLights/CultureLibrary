# Bulgaria: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with craft argots), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Armenian, Judeo-Spanish for historical community papers. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how what may, must or must not be kept hidden is judged; privacy is one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pomak communities and the claims made about them:** check each community's own statements against outsiders' theories of hidden origins (Verkovich; later writers); the claims are contested.
- **Alevi Kızılbaş and Babai of the Ludogorie and Dobruja:** check what members publish about their gatherings, and respect what they withhold.
- **Stara Planina craft towns (Tryavna, Gabrovo) and Bratsigovo builders:** check whether argot speakers' descendants still know any words.
- **Pirin region (Petrich, Rupite):** check local accounts of Vanga against state-era press.
- **Families of former State Security officers and of the watched:** check how each talks about what was kept from them.
- **Sofia against small towns for privacy:** check whether forums and interviews describe different limits on what neighbors may know.
- **Armenian and Sephardic communities:** check their memoirs for what was kept from outsiders in hard years, and why.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Bogomils (as their adversaries recorded them), the Revival underground and the communist archives; mixed for argots; thin for private secrecy and for closed religious rites.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for Cosmas; Latin for the Secret Book; 19th-century Bulgarian and cipher keys for Levski's papers; Turkish for Alevi and Kardzhali sources; Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** closed Alevi rites are not to be researched beyond what members share; Pomak "hidden origin" claims can be used against the community; LGBT oral histories need anonymity; treasure hunting is often illegal and touches looted sites.
- **Held for review:** nothing held on this sheet; the law on state secrets and data protection is passed to Libra.
