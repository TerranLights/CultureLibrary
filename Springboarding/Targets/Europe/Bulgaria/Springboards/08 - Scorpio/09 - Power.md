# Bulgaria: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria; Ottoman Turkish and Old Bulgarian for older records. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how who holds it, how it is held and how it is used are judged; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Vratsa, Montana):** check Sofronii and Ottoman sources against local histories of strongmen, then against today's depopulated districts.
- **Kardzhali region:** check section-level results and local press on how Turkish-speaking voters describe representation, in Turkish and Bulgarian.
- **Roma neighborhoods (for example in Sliven, Plovdiv's Stolipinovo, Sofia's Fakulteta):** check vote-buying allegations critically against Roma leaders' and residents' own accounts; allegations are not facts about anyone.
- **Alevi Kızılbaş and Babai:** check whether *dede* authority is described differently in the Ludogorie and in Dobruja.
- **Stara Planina Revival towns (Koprivshtitsa, Gabrovo, Tryavna):** check guild records against notables' papers for who decided town affairs.
- **Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check press and court-reported disputes over coastal land for who held power over development.
- **Strandzha border zone:** check memories of socialist-era border controls on villagers' lives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval charters, Ottoman sources and the socialist state; rich in post-1989 think-tank reports; thin for power inside households and small communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for the Rila Charter; Ottoman Turkish for originals behind the *Fontes turcici*; 19th-century Bulgarian for Sofronii; Turkish for Kardzhali and Ludogorie politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** allegations of organized crime and vote-buying name living people and communities; the *чорбаджии* debate is politically loaded; *ocak* lineages are a community matter.
- **Held for review:** none held; legitimate authority and hierarchy are routed to Capricorn, and corruption as written in law to Libra.
