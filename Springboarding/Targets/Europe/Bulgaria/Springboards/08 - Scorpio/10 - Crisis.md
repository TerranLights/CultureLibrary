## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Thracian refugee families (Haskovo, Harmanli, Burgas, Strandzha villages) and Macedonian refugee families (Sofia, Blagoevgrad):** check whether family memory and village histories name the same crisis and the same losses.
- **Southern Dobruja after 1940:** check resettlement after the Bulgarian-Romanian exchange as a crisis for those who arrived and those who left.
- **Upper Thrace (Chirpan, Plovdiv) and the Danube (Svishtov):** compare earthquake records from 1928 and 1977 for state response under different regimes.
- **Turks of Kardzhali and the Ludogorie:** check 1989 accounts of those who stayed and those who returned; emigrants in Turkey are comparison only.
- **Roma neighborhoods (Sliven, Yambol, Sofia):** check 2020 quarantine accounts by residents, not only by officials.
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check depopulation studies for how residents name the slow crisis.
- **Armenians who came as refugees after 1915:** check community records of arrival in Plovdiv, Varna and Burgas.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the national catastrophes, refugees and 1990s economics; mixed for disasters before 1900 (marginal notes and lore); thin for personal crisis outside helpline reports.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and old vernacular for marginal notes; Bulgarian for Miletich; French and English for League of Nations papers; Turkish for 1989 oral histories; Armenian for refugee-era community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1989 and the 2020 Roma quarantines are painful and contested; Batak memory is a national flashpoint; refugee property claims linger in families; helpline data must stay aggregate.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; crisis-era emergency law is routed to Libra, and leaders' authority in emergencies to Capricorn.
