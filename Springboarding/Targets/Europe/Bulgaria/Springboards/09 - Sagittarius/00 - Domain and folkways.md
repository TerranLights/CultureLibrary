## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia and the Shopluk against Upper Thrace (Plovdiv, Stara Zagora):** check census non-response and "no religion" by municipality; the capital may not speak for provincial towns.
- **Rhodope Pomaks (Smolyan, Madan) and Pirin Pomaks (Gotse Delchev, Satovcha):** compare which institutions each turns to (muftiate, church, neither), given separate histories of name changes.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of the Ludogorie:** separate Sunni and Alevi institutions, which one "Muslim" census box hides.
- **Kızılbaş and Babai Alevi communities:** check whether their tekkes and ritual leaders appear in any official register.
- **Roma groups (Erli, Kalderash, Turkish-speaking Muslim Roma, Rudari):** compare Orthodox, Muslim and evangelical affiliation by group and quarter.
- **Catholic Paulician villages (around Plovdiv; Belene and Oresh on the Danube):** check parish and diocesan records of a minority church with its own founding story.
- **Lipovans of Dobruja, Armenians of Plovdiv and Varna, the Sephardic community and its 1943 history, Karakachans, Aromanians, Gagauz:** check which keep separate religious records and which are folded into the majority church's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Orthodoxy, Islam and folk religion; mixed for Protestants, Catholics and Alevis; thin for Gagauz, Karakachan and Aromanian religious life (start with their cultural associations and local museums). The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for Bulgaria as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for the census, Marinov, Georgieva, and Metodiev; Old Church Slavonic for Cosmas and the Rila charter; Turkish for *Müslümanlar* and Alevi terms; German for Kanitz.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the census religion question is voluntary, so non-response is a datum; the 1984-89 "Revival Process" and earlier name changes touch Turks, Pomaks and Roma, and their labels are contested, so record self-designations.
- **Held for review:** nothing held for the domain overview; every source above passed the standing rules.

## 5. First queries
1. NSI census 2021: download the "вероизповедание" tables by municipality and compare with 2011 and 2001.
2. Search COBISS.BG for "народна вяра" and "Маринов" to locate the 1914 text and reprints.
3. Search "Методиев Между вярата и компромиса" and "Миков алевии бекташи" in university catalogs.
4. Search the IEFEM-BAS archive guide for field collections from Strandzha, the Rhodopes and the Ludogorie.
5. Open the Directorate of Religious Affairs' list of denominations and note each small body for later scouting.
