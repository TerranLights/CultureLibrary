# Bulgaria: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani; Church Slavonic for older devotional texts; Lipovan Russian and Western Armenian for two smaller churches. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven: God, gods, spirits, a sacred order; by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhodope Pomaks around Madan and Smolyan against Pirin Pomaks around Ribnovo:** check how religious instruction and mosque life were rebuilt in each after 1989.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of Dobruja:** compare where faith is taught (mosque, home, Quran course) and which bodies fund it.
- **Roma groups in Stolipinovo (Plovdiv), Fakulteta (Sofia) and Nadezhda (Sliven):** compare which churches and mosques each group attends and how affiliation has shifted.
- **Paulician Catholics of Rakovski and Kaloyanovo against the Danubian Catholic villages:** check whether the two dioceses' parish records tell different stories of keeping faith.
- **Protestant congregations of Sofia, Plovdiv and Ruse:** check how their memories of the 1949 trial are passed on.
- **Armenians of Plovdiv and Varna:** check the parish life of the Armenian Apostolic churches as a faith carried by a diaspora community into Bulgaria.
- **The Danubian Plain against the Stara Planina towns:** compare EVS answers by region, since the survey's regional cells may differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Orthodox practice and survey measures; mixed for Islam (good ethnography, thinner Turkish-language print); thin for Lipovans and Armenians outside their own parishes.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and early modern Bulgarian for the *damaskini* and Sofronius; Bulgarian for *Църковен вестник* and trial files; Latin and Italian for Propaganda Fide; Turkish for mosque-life sources in the Kardzhali region; Romani for congregational songs and testimonies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** security-service files on clergy (COMDOS) name living people and their families; pilgrimage vows and healing stories are personal; converts in Roma and Pomak communities may face family pressure, so interview with consent.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside for faith; the Catholic, Protestant and Muslim pointers all passed the standing rules.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the EVS Bulgaria files and pull the "importance of God" and attendance items by region.
2. Search COBISS.BG for "дамаскин" and "Неделник Софроний" for editions and studies.
3. Search for "Славкова ромски евангелски" and "Карамихова поклонничество".
4. Check the COMDOS publications list for the 1949 pastors' trial.
5. Search the *Църковен вестник* digitized runs for "вяра" in issues from the 1950s and the 1990s, to compare two eras.
