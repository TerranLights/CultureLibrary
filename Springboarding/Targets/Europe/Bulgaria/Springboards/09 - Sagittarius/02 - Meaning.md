# Bulgaria: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian; the Turkish of Bulgaria (including Alevi religious vocabulary and Ottoman Turkish for older hagiography); Romani for Roma testimony. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rila and Pirin hikers, White Brotherhood followers and Orthodox pilgrims:** check whether the same mountain sites are given different purposes by each.
- **Alevi communities of Teketo and the Ludogorie against Sunni Turks of the Kardzhali region:** compare how each religious teaching names the aim of life.
- **Industrial towns (Pernik, Dimitrovgrad, Devnya) against farming villages of the Danubian Plain:** check how the end of socialist industry and of cooperative farms differently changed talk of purpose.
- **Pomak villages of the Smolyan region:** check memoirs and interviews for what returning to or leaving Islam meant to individual lives after 1989.
- **Roma evangelical congregations:** compare testimonies of "a new life" with older Roma narratives of trade and family as the point of life.
- **Thracian refugee families of Haskovo and Burgas:** check memoirs for how loss of a homeland was made meaningful across generations.
- **Sofia's young professionals and emigrants who returned:** check surveys and blogs on why people stay, leave or come back.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good survey measures and rich literature, thin direct ethnography of "the meaning of life" as a phrase.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for the Otman Baba hagiography; modern Turkish for *Yeni Işık*; Bulgarian for the *Мисъл* circle, Yovkov, Hadzhiyski and Elenkov; dialect Bulgarian for laments in the sound archive.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data must not be read as a verdict on any region or group; laments and memorial notices are family mourning, so publish with consent; White Brotherhood members may resent outsider labels for their teaching.
- **Held for review:** none for meaning; Deunov's teaching is to be scouted from its own published talks and members' accounts before any outside reading of it.
