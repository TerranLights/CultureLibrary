# Bulgaria: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (including its grammar of reported speech); the Turkish of Bulgaria (which has its own reported-speech forms); Romani; Ottoman Turkish for court registers; German for a folktale index. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and of Shumen and Razgrad:** check which news sources (Bulgarian, Turkish-language local, broadcasts from Turkey) they trust, since the answer may differ by town and generation.
- **Pomak villages around Smolyan and Gotse Delchev:** compare how local historians and elders tell community origins, given the contested national accounts.
- **Roma groups in Sofia, Plovdiv and Sliven:** check oral history projects for who is believed in disputes inside a quarter and with outsiders.
- **Upper Thrace (Batak, Peshtera, Bratsigovo):** compare local memory of 1876 with school accounts, since the event sits in this area.
- **Sofia, Varna and Plovdiv against small Danubian towns:** check whether trust in national media and fact-checkers differs by settlement size.
- **The Sephardic community:** check community-published accounts of 1943, including the deportations from lands under Bulgarian administration, against official commemorations.
- **Older and younger cohorts across all regions:** check how memory of socialist-era news shapes trust now.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memory disputes and media trust; mixed for everyday rumor; thin for minority-language news habits outside polls.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and early modern Bulgarian for Paisius; Ottoman Turkish for the *sicil*; German for the folktale index and parts of Friedman's comparative work; modern Turkish for news-trust interviews; Romani for oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1943 deportations, Batak and the security files are live political subjects; file disclosures name living people; Pomak origin accounts are contested, so present every version as a claim. Testimony in court belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing held for truth; the cross-border history commission is kept as comparison only, under the run's rule on North Macedonia.
