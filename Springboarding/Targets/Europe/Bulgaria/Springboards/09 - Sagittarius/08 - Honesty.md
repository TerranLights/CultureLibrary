## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gabrovo, Tryavna and the Stara Planina towns against Sofia:** check whether local joke and proverb traditions about cleverness differ from the capital's, without reading jokes as fact.
- **Turks and Pomaks of the Kardzhali and Smolyan regions:** compare how religious teaching on honest trade appears in local business talk and sermons.
- **Kalderash and Erli Roma:** check how disputes over broken promises are handled inside each group (customary courts go to Libra), and how outside stereotypes about honesty are answered in Roma media.
- **Black Sea resort towns (Sunny Beach, Golden Sands) against inland villages:** check consumer-complaint patterns, since tourist trade may create different pressures.
- **The Danubian Plain and the Northwest:** check bribery-experience answers by region in CSD data, where samples allow.
- **Armenian and Sephardic merchant families of Plovdiv, Ruse and Sofia:** check community histories for codes of commercial trust.
- **Generations raised before and after 1989:** compare views on "connections" as help or as cheating.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys and folk humor; mixed for everyday honesty (few direct studies); thin for minority codes of trust outside a few histories.
- **Languages needed:** Old Church Slavonic for *Златоструй*; Bulgarian for Slaveykov, Elin Pelin and CSD reports; Turkish for *kul hakkı* booklets and the Hodzha tales' Turkish versions; Romani for Roma media responses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honesty is a common ground for ethnic and regional stereotypes (about Roma, Gabrovo, "the Balkans"); every pointer here is a source on talk, not evidence of a trait; customary dispute courts belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing held for honesty; the Gabrovo joke genre is kept with a warning, since it is a town's own tradition.
