*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk villages and Sofia:** check whether village forms of standing carry into the capital's migrant neighborhoods; rural and metropolitan prestige may need separate units.
- **The Rhodopes (Smolyan, Madan) and the Kardzhali region:** compare Pomak, Turkish and Bulgarian Orthodox villages on who leads a community (mayor, imam, elder); separate units likely.
- **Dobruja and the Ludogorie:** check Alevi (Kızılbaş and Babai) communities, where hereditary *dede* lines may carry religious authority distinct from Sunni practice.
- **Roma of several groups:** compare Kalderash, Erli and other groups on how leaders and elders are recognized; sources differ sharply by group.
- **Stara Planina craft towns (Gabrovo, Tryavna, Koprivshtitsa):** the guild ladder of master, journeyman and apprentice as a source for career and rank ways.
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** pastoral leadership roles; check the scattered literature before treating them as one unit.
- **Black Sea coast (Varna, Burgas):** check tourism and port careers since the 1960s as their own status economy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Bulgarian-language ethnography, proverbs and survey data; thin for Karakachans, Gagauz and Lipovan Old Believers. Bulgaria's tracker records no change to its pre-war lands and its exact units are still to be settled; this run writes for Bulgaria as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian for Hadzhiyski, Slaveykov and the obituary notices; Ottoman Turkish for the defters; German for Kanitz; Greek and Aromanian for the pastoral leadership terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national-character writing (Hadzhiyski, *Бай Ганьо*) can harden into stereotype; read it as a genre. Hereditary *dede* lineages and Roma leadership are community matters; use what communities publish.
- **Held for review:** none; the domain sheet's leads on rank and duty all sit inside the nation's own record.
