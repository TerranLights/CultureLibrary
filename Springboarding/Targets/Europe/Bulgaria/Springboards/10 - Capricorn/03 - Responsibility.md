# Bulgaria: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani, Armenian. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region, Dobruja and the Ludogorie, and Pomaks of the Rhodopes:** check whose responsibility for 1984-89 each community names, in testimony given in its own language; label the campaign's name as the regime's.
- **Roma of several groups:** check how responsibility for children and elders is shared among kin, and how institutions assign it; compare Muslim, Orthodox and evangelical families.
- **Sofia and the Shopluk villages:** compare household responsibility in a commuter belt and in a capital.
- **Ruse and the Danubian Plain towns:** check civic campaigns over pollution and over children's institutions as local traditions of holding power to account.
- **Plovdiv and Stara Zagora (Upper Thrace):** check local media on officials' resignations and audits, to set beside the capital.
- **Bulgarian Catholics and Protestants:** compare teaching on confession and amends with Orthodox practice.
- **Strandzha and the Burgas coast:** check stewardship of forest and sea as local communities and protected-area staff describe it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state accountability and the communist past; mixed for family responsibility (good surveys, few ethnographies); thin for Armenian, Gagauz and Karakachan communities.
- **Languages needed:** Turkish for the IMIR testimony and the press of Turkish communities in the Kardzhali region; Romani for Roma family accounts; English for the BBC film and rights reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the People's Court and the 1984-89 campaign touch living families on all sides; give each side's account and present contested names as contested. Mogilino involved identifiable children; use published reports only.
- **Held for review:** none; the accountability leads here sit wholly in Bulgarian, Turkish and European institutions.
