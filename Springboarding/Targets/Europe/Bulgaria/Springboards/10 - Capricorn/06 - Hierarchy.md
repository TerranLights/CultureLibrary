## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhodope Pomak villages and the Kardzhali region's Turkish villages:** check household and village ranking in each, in its own language, against Brunnbauer's findings.
- **Roma neighborhoods (Fakulteta in Sofia, Stolipinovo in Plovdiv) and Roma groups across the country:** check how groups rank one another and how outsiders rank them; record these as two separate sources, and avoid outsiders' labels.
- **Dobruja:** compare estate-era ranks with the collective-farm ladders that replaced them.
- **Stara Planina towns (Koprivshtitsa, Gabrovo, Tryavna):** check guild and notable families' memory of rank.
- **Lipovan Old Believers (Dobruja and the Danube) and the Armenian community:** check church ranks as community ranks; sources sit mostly in the communities.
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** compare herding-company ranks before and after settlement in the 1950s.
- **Sofia's elite districts against outer estates:** check how address and neighborhood mark rank today.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval, Ottoman-urban and socialist-elite sources; mixed for present-day class (surveys good, ethnography patchy); thin for Lipovan and Gagauz communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Bulgarian for the charters; Greek for *De Ceremoniis*; German for Brunnbauer; Russian for Lipovan statutes; Romani for Roma groups' own names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma group rankings and neighborhood labels can stigmatize; use groups' own names. Nomenklatura files name living families.
- **Held for review:** none; every rank table, charter and church statute cited sits in the open Bulgarian, Byzantine or Ottoman record.
