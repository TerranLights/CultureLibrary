## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia's elite language and mathematics schools against schools in the Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Kardzhali region:** check where exam-based sorting meets regional and language gaps.
- **Turks and Pomaks:** check access to elite schools and to Islamic schooling as separate routes, each in its own language.
- **Roma of several groups:** compare students who rose through scholarships with peers who left school, using the students' own accounts.
- **Ludogorie and Kardzhali wrestling fairs:** check traditional contests of strength as a folk measure of merit among Turks and Bulgarians.
- **Plovdiv and Stara Zagora (Upper Thrace):** check regional universities' hiring and the role of connections reported in local press.
- **Armenian and Sephardic communities:** check community schools and scholarships as their own merit ladders, with sources in community archives.
- **Pirin region (Blagoevgrad):** check the local university's place in regional advancement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, honors and corruption surveys; mixed for socialist admissions (archives open, synthesis thin); thin for Gagauz and Karakachan education routes.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Bulgarian for Tsamblak; Ottoman Turkish for levy registers; Turkish for Islamic-school sources and wrestling fairs; Armenian for community schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the child levy is charged ground in national memory; present Ottoman and Bulgarian historiography side by side. Plagiarism and nepotism cases name living people.
- **Held for review:** none; merit routes in exams, schools and honors here all run through the nation's own institutions.
