## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Roma of several groups:** check which groups keep their own dispute settlement and which turn to state courts or to churches; sources differ by group, so do not generalize from one.
- **Turks of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie, and Pomaks of the Rhodopes:** compare memories of Islamic family law, the 1984-89 period and today's courts, each in the community's own language and naming.
- **Stara Planina and Strandzha villages:** check haiduk memory where the songs and sites cluster.
- **The Sephardic community and its 1941-44 records:** check how members met discriminatory law, through community archives and memoirs.
- **Sofia against Northwest towns (Vidin, Montana):** compare trust in courts and the reported use of connections in legal matters.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check whether community rules still settle disputes; sources likely thin, start with parish elders and Romanian-side scholarship.
- **Armenian community of Plovdiv:** check church and community arbitration in the past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state legal culture and rule-of-law indexes; mixed for customary law (old ethnography, little recent fieldwork); thin for Gagauz and Lipovan legal custom.
- **Languages needed:** Old Bulgarian for the 9th-century code; Latin for the papal answers; Turkish and Ottoman Turkish for Islamic courts; Romani for *kris* and *meshere* accounts; Judeo-Spanish for 1941-44 community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1941 law and the camps touch living families; Roma dispute settlement belongs to the groups that keep it. Current judicial disputes are political: give each side's statement.
- **Held for review:** none; the custom, court and outlaw leads here all stay inside Bulgarian, Ottoman and EU sources.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the *Закон за съдене на хората* and the papal answers side by side, listing what each says law is for.
2. Search COBISS.BG for "Бобчев обичайно право" and "шериатски съдилища".
3. Pull the CVM reports' Bulgarian sections and the WJP index's Bulgaria pages.
4. Search the Central State Archive guide for protests against the 1941 bill.
5. Search folk-song indexes for "хайдушки песни" by region.
