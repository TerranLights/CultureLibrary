## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western mummers' villages (Pernik, Breznik) against the Strandzha and Upper Thrace *kukeri*:** check how the ritual's mock order and its rules differ by region.
- **Roma neighborhoods and villages:** compare residents' accounts of order with police and media accounts; keep each group's name and faith distinct.
- **Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Chiprovtsi Catholic memory:** check how local histories remember lawless periods.
- **Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie:** compare Turkish and Bulgarian accounts of order in mixed towns, in both languages.
- **Black Sea resorts (Sunny Beach, Golden Sands) in summer:** check seasonal disorder and the municipal rules aimed at it.
- **Sofia's prefab housing estates against central districts:** check perceived safety and complaints by district.
- **Gagauz and Lipovan villages of Dobruja:** check community and church rules for order; sources sparse, start with parish and village leaders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, protests and ritual disorder; mixed for household order; thin for Gagauz, Lipovan and Karakachan practice.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Bulgarian for the *Synodikon*; Italian and Latin for the Franciscan reports; Ottoman Turkish for the kırcalı period; Romani for Roma residents' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Katunitsa and policing of Roma are charged; give each side's account and avoid labels. 1990s crime figures still in public life call for named sources only.
- **Held for review:** none; the ritual, protest and crime leads here rest on Bulgarian, EU and Catholic mission sources.
