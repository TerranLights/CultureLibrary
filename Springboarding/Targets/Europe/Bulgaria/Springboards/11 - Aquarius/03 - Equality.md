# Bulgaria: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian; the Turkish of Bulgaria; Romani; Ottoman Turkish for older records. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is the open question.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sofia against the Northwest (Vidin, Montana) and the Kardzhali region:** check whether talk of inequality is framed by region, class or ethnicity in each.
- **Turks of the Ludogorie and Pomaks of the Smolyan region:** check how equal citizenship is described after the 1984-89 name changes, and how the two communities' accounts differ.
- **Roma of several groups (Erli in Sofia, Kalderash, Rudari, Turkish-speaking Muslim Roma):** check whether reported unequal treatment differs by group, faith and town.
- **Women and men across generations:** check memories of socialist-era equality claims against post-1989 experience, by town and village.
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** check how former pastoralists describe their standing after settlement and collectivization.
- **Armenian and Sephardic communities:** check equal-citizenship themes in their own press and memoirs.
- **Thracian and Macedonian refugee families of the 1910s-1920s:** check how resettled families describe their standing beside local villagers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for measured income inequality and socialist-era privilege; mixed for gender and ethnic equality attitudes; thin for Karakachans and Aromanians, where oral history is the route.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for the *defters*; Bulgarian for Konstantinov, *Стършел* and the address studies; Turkish for Kardzhali-region and Ludogorie accounts; English for Ghodsee and the FRA reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma discrimination; the "Revival Process"; socialist privilege with named living people; anti-discrimination law and rulings belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here.
- **Held for review:** none; the equality sources here are Bulgarian, Ottoman and EU-level, and none required setting aside.
