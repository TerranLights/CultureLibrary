# Bulgaria: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (with Northwest and Rhodope dialect forms), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mixed Bulgarian-Turkish villages of the Kardzhali region and the Ludogorie:** check testimony from before and after 1984-89 for how each side describes the other's losses.
- **Pomak villages of the Western Rhodopes (Velingrad, Satovcha in the Pirin district):** check whether the 1912-13, 1942 and 1970s renamings appear in neighbors' accounts at all; the labels are contested, so record each source's own term.
- **Roma groups (Erli, Kalderash, Rudari, Muslim Roma):** check how health mediators and teachers describe misreadings in each direction.
- **Women's lament against men's speech of grief:** check who is permitted to voice another's pain in the Rhodopes and Shopluk.
- **Sofia and Plovdiv against Dobruja villages:** check whether psychology's vocabulary of empathy has reached small towns.
- **Alevi communities of the Ludogorie:** check muhabbet gatherings as a setting for shared feeling.
- **Karakachan, Aromanian and Lipovan communities:** thin; check community newsletters and church records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and literature; mixed in psychology (adapted scales, small samples); thin for intergroup empathy measured directly.
- **Languages needed:** Bulgarian dialect knowledge for Stoin's Northwest songs and the lament texts; Turkish for 1989-era testimony and muhabbet; Romani for mediators' fieldwork; English for scale-adaptation papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Revival Process" testimony is painful and political; the Pomak renamings are contested; never infer a group's capacity for feeling.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the empathy-scale adaptation is a query only, since no named study is known here.
