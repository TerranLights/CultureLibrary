*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Northwest (Vidin, Montana):** check rusalii trance and village depopulation side by side; one region holds two kinds of dissolution.
- **Pomak villages of the Pirin region against those of the central Rhodopes:** check whether the Ribnovo wedding rite has counterparts elsewhere.
- **Alevi villages beside Sunni Turks of the Ludogorie:** semah has no Sunni counterpart; check the boundary each draws around ecstatic practice.
- **Roma groups by faith:** check Pentecostal, Orthodox and Muslim Roma separately, since worship styles differ.
- **Kardzhali district:** check drowned villages and the 1989 departures together as two endings of the same places.
- **Aromanians of Peshtera, Karakachans near Sliven and Kotel, Gagauz of Dobruja, Lipovans of Tataritsa and Kazashko:** check how far each community's language survives and who writes its history.
- **The Sephardic community after the 1948-49 emigration:** check the loss of Judeo-Spanish in Sofia and Plovdiv.
- **Sofia and Black Sea resort crowds against village horo:** check whether club and festival talk of "losing oneself" matches dance-line talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and depopulation statistics; mixed for ecstatic worship; thin for minority language shift and dementia care.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and Greek for the Paroria and Theodosius texts; Turkish for semah ethnography; Romani for Pentecostal worship recordings; Aromanian and Gagauz for community societies' publications; Judeo-Spanish for Sephardic memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Alevi cem is closed to outsiders in many villages; Ribnovo's wedding has drawn intrusive media attention; reburial is private family business.
- **Held for review:** none; divorce and dementia sources (NSI divorce series, Alzheimer associations) still need checking before they earn a row.
