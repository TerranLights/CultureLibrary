# Bulgaria: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani (Kalderash especially), the Russian of the Lipovan Old Believers; Ottoman Turkish for archival pointers. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kalderash, Erli and Muslim Roma:** check whether purity rules apply in each group or only in some; never generalize one group's rules to all.
- **Pomak villages (Smolyan) and Turkish villages (Kardzhali):** check how household space is divided between guests, men and women, and what mahrem covers in each.
- **Lipovan Old Believers of Dobruja:** check how the community explains its separate dishes and church, in its own publications.
- **Strandzha border villages:** check the memory of the closed zone beside today's fence.
- **Southern Dobruja (Dobrich, Balchik):** check family memories of the 1940 exchange on both sides.
- **Stara Planina towns (Koprivshtitsa, Tryavna) against the Shopluk:** check whether house walls and yards differ by region.
- **Karakachans:** check how settlement in the 1950s redrew their lines with village neighbors.
- **Armenian and Jewish quarters of Plovdiv and Ruse:** check old quarter boundaries in town plans and community histories.
- **Sofia panel-block residents against villagers:** check privacy expectations across generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state borders and ethnic distance; mixed on family boundaries; thin on personal space and on Lipovan and Karakachan practice.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for any defter beyond the translated series; Turkish for mahrem in Kardzhali villages; Romani for Kalderash purity terms; Russian for Lipovan church writings; Romanian for the Dobruja exchange's Romanian files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma demolitions and expulsions involve living people and ongoing grievance; State Security files name informers; purity rules are internal community matters. Land-boundary disputes as law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the post-1991 land restitution quarrels are a likely pointer but are left for Libra's sheet.
