## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk and Northwest villages against the Rhodopes:** check whether bean reading and ladouvane differ in form and in who performs them.
- **Turkish and Pomak households of Kardzhali and Smolyan:** check coffee-cup reading and dream interpretation, and who in the village is consulted.
- **Karakachan herders, Black Sea fishers (Sozopol, Nesebar) and Danube fishers:** check weather and animal lore as working knowledge, each in its own trade.
- **Roma fortune-tellers:** check practitioners' own accounts and clients' reasons; this topic is stereotype-laden, so record only what sources show.
- **Alevi villages:** check dream legends against those of Sunni shrines.
- **Stara Planina craft towns (Tryavna, Gabrovo):** check how masters spoke of the trained eye.
- **Sofia and Plovdiv professionals against villagers:** check survey splits on trusting data or one's own sense.
- **Sephardic, Armenian and Gagauz communities:** likely thin; check community memoirs for omen and dream customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk divination and omens; mixed for socialist-era parapsychology; thin for measured intuition in Bulgarian samples.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the omen books; Turkish for tabirname and coffee-cup reading; Romani for fortune-tellers' accounts; Bulgarian dialect help for ladouvane songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is tied to Roma stereotypes; Lozanov's institute and its seer studies are entangled with state interest; divination is studied as practice, never as evidence.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here; Vanga's prophecies are left to the domain sheet so this one does not lean on a single seer.
