# Bulgaria: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian, the Turkish of Bulgaria (Ottoman Turkish for court registers), Romani; Church Slavonic for one text. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Law itself is researched in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); this sheet cites, never repeats, that work.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Strandzha, the Sredna Gora and the Stara Planina:** check where the haidut is a local hero and how each district's songs differ.
- **Turkish and Pomak villages (Kardzhali, Smolyan):** check günah, ayıp and suç talk, and how 1984-89 is named as a wrong done to them.
- **Roma neighborhoods of several groups:** check their own accounts of being victims and suspects; never adopt the news frame.
- **The Northwest (Vidin) and Danube towns:** check embargo-era memories for what neighbors called wrong.
- **Black Sea resorts (Sunny Beach, Varna):** check how residents describe the 1990s racket economy.
- **Ludogorie and Danubian Plain villages:** check livestock-theft memory and village answers to it.
- **Sofia against small towns:** check victimization and reporting rates in CSD surveys.
- **Jewish and Armenian communities:** check their records of wrongs done to them in the 1940s and how they are named.
- **Thracian refugee families:** check memoirs of 1913 violence for how they name the perpetrators' acts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption and organized crime (survey series since the late 1990s); mixed for older rural crime; thin for minority communities' own framing.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish for the sicil; Church Slavonic for the penitentials; Turkish for village terms of shame and sin; Romani for Roma testimony on policing and blame; Bulgarian for CSD and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma and crime is a stereotype minefield; the "Revival Process" case is ongoing political ground; 1990s groups and their successors may still be active. Statutes, courts and punishment go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the 1945 People's Court is left for Capricorn, where the question of the state as judge belongs.
