# Bulgaria: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Bulgaria, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bulgarian (Shop and Northwest dialect forms), the Turkish of Bulgaria, Romani; the Greek of the Karakachans for one community. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shopluk villages (Pernik, Breznik):** check how mask rites name what is being chased away.
- **The Northwest (Vratsa, Montana, Vidin) and Kozloduy:** check flood and nuclear memories side by side.
- **Pirin (Kresna), Svishtov and Pernik:** check whether earthquake memory differs where quakes struck.
- **Kardzhali Turkish and Rhodope Pomak villages:** check jinn and fright cures, and who performs them.
- **Strandzha and Thracian refugee families:** check border fear across generations, from 1913 to 2016.
- **Roma communities:** check their own accounts of fear of eviction and policing, separately by group and town.
- **Karakachans and Aromanians:** check herders' fears (wolves, storms) in their community publications.
- **Sephardic families:** check 1943 testimony for fear in the days before the deportations were halted.
- **Armenian families:** check memoirs for fear carried from 1915.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk fear figures and disasters; mixed for socialist-era fear; thin for measured anxiety by ethnicity.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Bulgarian for the Apocryphal Chronicle; Shop and Northwest dialect for Surva and Radichkov; Turkish for jinn and al karısı material; Romani for testimony on evictions; Karakachan Greek for herders' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant-hunting patrols and Roma evictions involve living people; 1913 testimony is used politically; fright cures belong to healers who may not wish to share.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the "mental health" side needs a Bulgarian clinical survey pointer once one is confirmed.
