# Estonia: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with North and South Estonian dialects, Mulgi, and the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the self, the body, appearance, naming and beginnings: what a self is here and how it shows itself.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers of the North and of South Estonia:** compare naming fashions and dialect self-names, since the census asked about regional speech.
- **Võro and Seto:** check whether naming customs, dress and sauna use are recorded as their own or folded into "Estonian".
- **Islanders of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi and Ruhnu:** check each island's own dress and nickname collections separately.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Tallinn and Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare patronymic use and how the identity card's categories are read.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check baptism and naming by the church calendar and dress norms at worship.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars, the Jewish community:** check each community's records of names kept, changed or translated.
- **Lutherans, the two Orthodox churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** compare rites that make a new self (baptism, confirmation, naming ceremonies).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for names, folk dress and life stories; mixed for body and appearance surveys; thin for Ingrian Finnish, Belarusian and Tatar self-presentation, where community societies are the first stop.
- **Lands:** the tracker records no change to the pre-war lands and no settled unit list; these sheets are written for Estonia as it stands, with no units proposed and no borders settled.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian throughout; Võro and Seto for regional naming; German for Baltic German-era dictionaries and registers; Swedish for the coastal community; Latin for the chronicle.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** census ethnic categories and name changes touch citizenship and russification debates; present each as an account. Recent personal registers are privacy-restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Statistics Estonia: open the first-name statistics and the 2021 census tables on mother tongue and dialect knowledge by county.

3. ERA databases: search *hing* and *vari* with *nimepanek* by parish.
4. DIGAR: search *nimede eestistamine* 1934 to 1940.
5. ERM: request the folk-dress catalogue for Muhu, Kihnu and Setomaa.
