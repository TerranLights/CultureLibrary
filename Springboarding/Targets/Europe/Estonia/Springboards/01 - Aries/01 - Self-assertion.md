# Estonia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects, Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish; Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers of Harjumaa against those of Järvamaa and Mulgimaa farms:** check whether city workplace talk and farm-neighbor disputes leave different records of claim-making.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Narva and Kohtla-Järve, and of Lasnamäe in Tallinn:** compare voice-at-work answers by language of work, so language is not read as temperament.
- **Seto and Võro:** check how lead-singer roles and village speaking orders are described by the communities themselves.
- **Islanders of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu:** compare island legends and island court records with mainland ones before generalizing.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check congregational speaking customs and how members addressed outside authorities in petitions.
- **Ukrainians arrived since 2022, Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, the Jewish community:** check each group's own organizations for how newcomers or small communities press their requests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court records, songs and conversation studies; mixed for surveys that ask about voice directly; thin for small communities, where their own associations are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian for modern sources; German and Russian for complaint letters of the 18th and 19th centuries; Seto for *leelo* texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic workplace comparisons can slide into stereotype; frame every difference as something to check, with structure (sector, language of work) in view. Petitions and court cases name private persons; anonymize in quotation.
- **Held for review:** none.
