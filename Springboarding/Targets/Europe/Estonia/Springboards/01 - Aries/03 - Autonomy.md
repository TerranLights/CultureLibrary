# Estonia: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects, Seto, Võro, the Russian of Estonia (including the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers), Estonian Swedish, German for the Baltic German period. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for persons and for communities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers in Harjumaa against those in rural Järvamaa and Läänemaa:** check whether village and municipal self-rule are spoken of differently where parishes were merged.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Narva, Sillamäe and Kohtla-Järve:** compare accounts of the 1993 referendums with later local-election debates, without assigning a view to the community.
- **Seto:** check how the Seto Kingdom's symbolic offices relate to actual decisions in Setomaa.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check lay self-government records over three centuries.
- **Kihnu, Ruhnu, Vormsi and Hiiumaa:** compare island decision-making on heritage and land use.
- **Estonian Swedes and the historical Jewish and Baltic German communities:** check autonomy records from the Swedish crown era to 1940.
- **Congregations (Lutheran, the two Orthodox churches, the Maausk and Taarausk circles):** check who decides parish matters in each, from their own statutes and minutes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collective self-rule and late-Soviet history; mixed for personal autonomy surveys; thin for Old Believer self-government in published form, so congregational and museum contacts are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian, Russian, German (Baltic German archives), Latin and Danish for the land book, Seto for Kingdom Day material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1993 referendums, the church split and citizenship status are contested; present each account as an account and name each community by its own name. The Pechory side of Setomaa enters only as a comparison for Seto self-rule.
- **Held for review:** none.
