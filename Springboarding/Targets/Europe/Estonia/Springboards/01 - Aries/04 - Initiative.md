# Estonia: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects, Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Ingrian Finnish, Ukrainian for recent arrivals. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers of North Estonia and of South Estonia:** compare work-bee customs and who calls them, parish by parish.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** check enterprise-birth and nonprofit data by county against sector and language of work, so structure is in view.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check family market-garden records and village tourism as their own account of starting things.
- **Seto and Võro:** compare home-host networks and language-revival projects started locally.
- **Islanders of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu and Muhu:** check volunteer rescue and small-firm records on islands with few public services.
- **Ingrian Finns and Ukrainians:** compare community work-bee terms and volunteer networks founded after arrival.
- **Lutheran parishes, both Orthodox churches, the Jewish community and the Tatar congregation:** check who founded their charitable and welfare societies, and when.
- **Women and men:** check founding and volunteering figures by gender in GEM and the nonprofit register.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for startups, civil society and work-bee folklore; mixed for small rural enterprise; thin for Ingrian Finnish and Seto business history in print.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian; Võro and Seto for local projects; Swedish and German for Forselius-era records; Ukrainian for post-2022 networks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** startup lore is marketing as well as history; read founders' myths critically. Soviet-era "voluntary" work is remembered differently by different communities; present each memory as an account.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. ERA: search *talgud* and *talgulaul* by parish; note who called the bee.
