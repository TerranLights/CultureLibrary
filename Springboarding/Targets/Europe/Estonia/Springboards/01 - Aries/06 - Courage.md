# Estonia: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish, Ukrainian, Yiddish (historical); Latin and Middle High German for medieval chronicles. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers of Viljandimaa (old Sakala) and of the islands:** check how local memory of medieval resistance is staged in each place.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians, Red Army veterans' families and Estonian veterans of other uniforms:** compare whose deeds are honored on which day, keeping each community's own name and account.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians:** check how recent arrivals frame courage in their own community media, without reading one war into another.
- **Seto:** check *Peko* and *leelo* against outsiders' collections of Seto material.
- **Miners and families of Ida-Virumaa:** compare workplace-courage stories by language of the teller.
- **The Jewish community:** check how rescue and resistance are remembered in the community's own record.
- **Women:** compare Naiskodukaitse, Kodutütred and women's wartime memoirs with the honors lists, checking whose deeds were counted.
- **Kihnu and Hiiumaa seafarers:** check sea-rescue accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and resistance memory; mixed for civilian and moral courage; thin for Belarusian and Tatar community accounts, where their associations are the start.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian, Russian, Ukrainian; Latin and Middle High German for chronicles; German for Baltic German-era accounts; Seto for *Peko*; Yiddish for pre-war writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Second World War, the Forest Brothers, the deportations and the 2007 events are contested; present each account as an account, name each community by its own name, and never let a pointer settle who was brave. The Holocaust and MS Estonia involve the bereaved; handle with care.
- **Held for review:** none.
