# Estonia: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (including the island speech), the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish, German for the manor period, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** not depending on others: self-reliance and self-sufficiency, for households and for the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers on farms of South Estonia and Järvamaa, and in Tallinn flats:** check self-provisioning figures by settlement type before reading any regional pattern.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** compare *dacha* and garden-plot memory, and each community's account of 1990-91 in its own press.
- **Kihnu, Ruhnu, Hiiumaa and Vormsi islanders:** check winter supply and service dependence island by island.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check fishing and market-garden household economies as forms of provisioning.
- **Seto and Võro households:** compare forest-food and smoke-sauna household economies as recorded in their own archives.
- **Elders, disabled people and caregiving families:** check who is counted as dependent in the care-burden data.
- **Estonian Swedes and the Jewish community (historical):** check pre-1940 community welfare funds as a form of collective self-reliance.
- **The Estonian diaspora:** comparison only (exile government and community funds), not part of the target.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national independence and household statistics; mixed for self-provisioning in practice; thin for island winter isolation in print, where municipal records and local papers are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian; German and Swedish for the Swedish-era and manor registers; Yiddish for community welfare records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the restoration years, citizenship and the "grey passport" are contested ground; present the accounts of the Estonian Congress, the Intermovement and others as accounts. Food-bank and care data concern vulnerable people; use aggregates only.
- **Held for review:** none.
