# Estonia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish (historical); German and Latin for chronicles. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers of Raplamaa, Läänemaa and Järvamaa:** compare local memory of 1858 and 1905 to see how manor-era grievance is kept in each place.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Tallinn and Ida-Virumaa:** check 2007 accounts and oil-shale protests in their own media, with the grievances each names in its own words.
- **Seto:** check lament texts and their commentary from Seto singers themselves, not only collectors.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check how congregational sources speak about quarrels and their settlement.
- **Children and parents:** compare bullying-program materials in Estonian- and Russian-language kindergartens and schools.
- **Women and men:** check shelter and victim-support data alongside men's counseling programs.
- **Estonian Swedes and the historical Baltic Germans:** check how the 1905 events were recorded by coastal Swedes and by manor families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical uprisings and the lexicon; mixed for everyday anger and display; thin for anger in Old Believer and Estonian Swedish sources, where community archives are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian, Russian; Middle Low German, German and Latin for chronicles; Seto for laments; Yiddish for pre-war writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1343 and 1905 events and the Bronze Night are read differently by different communities; keep every account labeled as that community's own, under the name it uses for itself. Domestic-violence data concern victims; use aggregates only.
- **Held for review:** none.
