# Estonia: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia (including the Peipsi Old Believers' speech), Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** possessions, money, resources, food, dress and wealth: what is worth having, and how it is held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Estonian, South Estonian and Mulgi farm regions:** check farm-buying dates and inventories separately; the 19th-century move to owned farms ran at different paces by county.
- **Võro and Seto:** check goods and silver in their own institutes' collections, not only all-Estonian handbooks.
- **Islanders (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** check fishing gear, boat shares and dress as the islands' own holdings.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Tallinn and Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare factory, mine and housing records with farm records.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check market-garden and fishing household economies through their villages' records.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars, the Jewish community, Roma:** check each group's own society and archive for property and trade records.
- **Lutherans, the two Orthodox churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** compare teaching on tithes, alms and sacred places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics, material culture and farm history; mixed for daily money talk; thin for Tatar, Belarusian and Roma household economies (community associations and oral history are the route).
- **Lands:** the tracker header names no settled lands and no change to the pre-war lands, and no scope file exists; these sheets are written for Estonia as it stands, propose no units and settle no borders. Latvia, Lithuania, Finland, Belarus, Poland, Sweden and Russia's culture-nations (including the Pechory district) are separate and for comparison only; the diaspora takes the nation's findings.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian since 1918; German for manor and city records; Swedish and Latin for the land revisions; Yiddish for the pre-war community; Võro and Seto.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property seized in the Soviet deportations, and its return or not after 1991, is contested ground; present each account as an account. Household microdata is access-controlled.
- **Held for review:** none.
