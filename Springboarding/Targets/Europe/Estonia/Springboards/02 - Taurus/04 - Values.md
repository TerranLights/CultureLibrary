*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian, Russian, Võro, Seto, Swedish, Yiddish, with Middle High German for the medieval chronicle. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn, Tartu and rural Järvamaa:** check whether value rankings in the surveys shift with town size and generation.
- **Võro and Seto:** check grove and saint's-day traditions and whether their own institutes name values differently from all-Estonian lists.
- **Russian-speaking residents of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** check value rankings by citizenship and school language, and how Russian-language media frame "Estonian values".
- **Peipsi Old Believers and the two Orthodox churches:** compare fasting, dress and prayer rules as stated values.
- **Lutherans, Maausk and Taarausk adherents, the non-religious:** check how each states what is sacred; people with no religion need their own route.
- **Kihnu, Muhu and Saaremaa islanders:** check what island cultural programs list as worth keeping.
- **Estonian Swedes, the Jewish community, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** check each community's own statements of what it teaches its young.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for values surveys and the values-education program; mixed for lived and revealed values; thin for Tatar and Belarusian value statements, where community schools and newsletters are the source.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian for surveys; Middle High German for the Rhymed Chronicle; German for Baltic German moral writing; Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Yiddish for pre-war community teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Estonian values" in integration and school-language debates is contested; quote each side as its own account. Sacred-site disputes involve adherents' restricted knowledge of some places.
- **Held for review:** none.
