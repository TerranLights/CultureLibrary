# Estonia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa salaried households against farm and forest workers in Võrumaa and Järvamaa:** check savings buffers and pension take-up, which may run apart by income source.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check job and pension security for miners and power workers by citizenship, including holders of the grey passport.
- **Islanders of Hiiumaa, Saaremaa, Kihnu and Ruhnu:** check winter supply habits and fishing-season income.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers and Estonian fishers:** check how lake fishing and market gardening spread risk across the year.
- **Seto and Võro households:** check the role of kin and village in hard years as their institutes' oral histories record it.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community (historically):** check mutual-aid funds and community chests.
- **Ukrainian refugees since 2022, Belarusians, Tatars:** check benefit access and job security from their associations and from refugee-agency data.
- **Lutheran and Orthodox parishes:** compare poor relief and charity funds across the periods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pensions, insurance and household finance; mixed for felt security; thin for 19th-c. self-help societies outside a few counties and for Tatar households, where archives and associations are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian; Swedish and German for the famine-era reports and parish books; Yiddish for community relief records; Ukrainian for refugee accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accounts differ on who lost savings and rights through the 1992 currency reform, the deportations and citizenship status; present each as an account. Benefit microdata is restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
