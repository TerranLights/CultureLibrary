# Estonia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with South Estonian and island dialects), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Ukrainian, Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm villages of Harjumaa, Järvamaa and Mulgimaa:** compare godparent choice in baptism registers to see how far ritual ties reached beyond the village.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** check *praasnik* and cemetery-visit records against northern soul-season customs.
- **Kihnu, Ruhnu and the other small islands:** check how islanders keep ties with those who moved to the mainland, via ferry and phone records and heritage bodies.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** check mobility studies for contact across language lines, and community bodies for ties with kin across the eastern border.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** look at how the long street villages and shared prayer houses link households, through the congregations' own histories.
- **Estonian Swedes, the Jewish community, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Belarusians, Ukrainians:** compare how each small community keeps members in touch across the country and abroad.
- **Workers commuting to Finland:** check family-tie studies on weekly commuting; Finland itself is comparison only.
- **Older people living alone in rural counties:** check loneliness data by county and household type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parish registers, mobility data and the 1989 events; mixed for loneliness (several surveys, differing items); thin for ties inside small communities (Tatar, Belarusian, Ingrian Finnish), where association records and interviews come first.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian; German and Latin in older baptism registers; Seto for *praasnik* songs; Swedish for the Estonian Swedes' links to Sweden; Ukrainian for newcomers' groups; Hebrew and Yiddish for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ties broken by the Soviet deportations and by the 1944 flight West are contested and painful ground; give each family's and community's account as an account. Phone-mobility data is anonymized and access-controlled.
- **Held for review:** none.
