# Estonia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with South Estonian and island speech), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the pre-war community; Latin for the medieval chronicle. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors. (The legal side of conciliation goes to Libra and Capricorn.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North and South Estonian farm parishes:** compare wedding go-between roles; the *kosjamees* and *isamees* records may differ by region.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** check Orthodox intercession and wise-one records separately from Lutheran parishes.
- **Kihnu, Muhu and Saaremaa:** check how island communities settle quarrels over boats, nets and grazing, through heritage bodies.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Narva, Sillamäe and Tallinn:** gather community leaders' and residents' accounts of the 1993 mediation and later roundtables.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check how congregations choose lay leaders to speak to authorities.
- **The Jewish community (pre-war and today), Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Tatars:** check whom each community sent to speak for it, through its own society records.
- **Ukrainian arrivals since 2022:** check interpreters and volunteer go-betweens at offices and schools.
- **Lutherans, the Orthodox of both churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** compare teaching on intercession (saints, clergy, or none) and on making peace.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal mediation services and the 1990s minority talks; mixed for village go-betweens (folklore records); thin for how Belarusian, Tatar and Ingrian Finnish communities settle internal disputes, where interviews are needed.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Henry's chronicle; Estonian and Russian for the 1993 talks and roundtable records; English for OSCE documents; Seto and Church Slavonic for chapel customs; Yiddish for the *shadkhn* and *shtadlan* roles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1993 crisis, citizenship talks and the two Orthodox jurisdictions are contested ground: the government's, the minority representatives' and the mediators' records each tell the talks their own way, and stay separate accounts. Family and labor mediation files are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Library catalog: search "van der Stoel" with "Estonia" for the 1993 letters and recommendations.

4. Henry's chronicle (English or Estonian translation): index *interpres*, envoys and hostages.
5. Seto Institute: ask for studies of *tsässon* feasts and icon prayers.
