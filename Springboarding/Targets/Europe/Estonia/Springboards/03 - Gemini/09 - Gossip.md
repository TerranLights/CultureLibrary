# Estonia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with South Estonian, Mulgi and island speech), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the pre-war community; German for early-modern court records. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small parishes of Mulgimaa, Järvamaa and Läänemaa against Tallinn and Tartu:** check whether rumor studies and forum data cover rural places at all.
- **Võro and Seto villages:** check mocking songs and village nicknames from these parishes in their own forms.
- **Kihnu, Muhu, Vormsi and Ruhnu:** look for island folklore and heritage-body records of how talk travels in very small communities.
- **Russian-speaking Estonians of Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe and Tallinn:** compare which online channels carry local hearsay, separately from national media.
- **Old Believers of the Peipsi shore:** check their teaching on idle talk and slander in congregation writings.
- **The Jewish community, Estonian Swedes, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians:** look in each community's memoirs for how talk inside and about the group was handled.
- **School pupils by teaching language:** compare survey items on rumors and online cruelty.
- **Lutherans, the Orthodox of both churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** compare teaching on the sins or harms of speech.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore of rumor and for youth surveys; mixed for everyday adult gossip (forums and interviews); thin for small minority groups' internal talk, where memoirs and interviews are needed.
- **Languages needed:** Estonian and Russian; German and Latin for the witch-trial records; Russian for KGB files; Swedish for island memoirs; Yiddish and Hebrew for the religious terms; Võro and Seto for mocking songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informer files, the 2007 Bronze Soldier events and wartime denunciations are contested ground: what each community says was said and spread stays its account, not a fact; name no private person from security files. Forum and survey data on named individuals stays private.
- **Held for review:** none.
