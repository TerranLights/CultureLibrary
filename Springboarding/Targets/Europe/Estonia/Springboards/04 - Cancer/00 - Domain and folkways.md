# Estonia: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** what home is and who is family: household forms, parents and fathering, roots, family memory, hospitality, the home's walls, meals, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mainland farm households (Harjumaa, Järvamaa, Mulgimaa, Tartumaa):** check whether household size and headship in the soul revisions differ by region and by manor.
- **Islanders (Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** compare farmstead layouts, and who ran the house while men were at sea.
- **Russian-speaking families of Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Tallinn's estates:** separate postwar arrivals from older lines; read dwelling tables by district.
- **Seto and Võro households:** read each institute's own material on the farmyard and the family before all-Estonian summaries.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believer villages:** set house layout and household size beside nearby Estonian villages.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, the Jewish community, Tatars:** check what each community's own societies keep on family homes before and after 1944.
- **Household rites by faith (Lutheran, the two Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk):** check how house blessing, the family table and the home corner are recorded in each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing, census and benefit figures and farm ethnography; mixed for fathering and home life after 1991; thin for Tatar, Belarusian and Ingrian Finnish households (their societies first).
- **Lands:** no scope file exists and the tracker header records no change to the pre-war lands, so the Cancer sheets cover Estonia as it stands, without a unit list or any border settled here. Neighbors (Finland, Sweden, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Belarus, and Russia's culture-nations, the Pechory district among them) serve as comparisons, and findings made here carry over to the diaspora.
- **Languages needed:** German to read the soul revisions and manor files; Estonian and Russian for benefit data and the parenting survey; Võro for the institute's series; Swedish for Vormsi and Ruhnu farm records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homes lost in the 1941 and 1949 deportations, and the postwar resettlement of Narva, are remembered differently by different communities; keep each account as its own. Household microdata are protected.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Statistics Estonia database: tabulate 2021 households by type (one person, couple, couple with children, several generations) for each county.
2. Saaga: open the 1834 soul revision of one Harjumaa and one Võrumaa parish; count members per farm.
3. Library catalog: search "Palli, Heldur" with *Karuse* for the household reconstitution.
4. Social Insurance Board: download yearly counts of fathers using *isapuhkus* across the 2022 parental-leave changes.
5. Folklore Archives catalog: search *külakost* and *külaline* and sort by parish.
