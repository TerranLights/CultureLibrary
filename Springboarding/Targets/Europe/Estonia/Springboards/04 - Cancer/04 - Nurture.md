# Estonia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with South Estonian and island usage), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish community; German for early printed advice. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mainland farm families of the North and of Mulgimaa:** check ERA infancy records against each other for differences in weaning and the cradle.
- **Islanders of Saaremaa, Kihnu and Muhu:** check who took over the raising of children when fathers were away at sea.
- **Võro and Seto families:** look for each community's own cradle songs and children's *leelo* groups, and who teaches them.
- **Russian-speaking parents in Tallinn and Ida-Virumaa:** compare kindergarten language choices and use of hobby schools by district.
- **Old Believer families of the Peipsi shore:** set church schooling beside public-school schooling.
- **Ukrainian families arriving since 2022, Belarusians, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Estonian Swedes:** check each group's Sunday or heritage schools and what they say they pass on.
- **The pre-war and today's Jewish community:** check the Tallinn Jewish School's own history for the upbringing it aimed at.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox, Catholic and Free Church families; Maausk and Taarausk:** compare confirmation and children's teaching as each faith runs them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, health data and school programs; mixed for home practice (forums, interviews); thin for Tatar and Belarusian upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** German and old Estonian spelling for Masing's paper; Russian for immersion and Old Believer sources; Ukrainian for the newest families; Seto and Võro for song material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** parenting programs and the language of schooling are argued over publicly; record each side's position as its own. Children's health records are restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Folklore Archives catalog: search *hällilaul* and sort hits by county.
2. DIGAR: open *Marahwa Näddala-Leht* and list articles on children.
3. National Institute for Health Development: find the yearly table on breastfeeding at six months by county.
4. ERR archive and press: search *Imelised aastad* for 2014-2016 coverage on both sides.
5. ESTER: search *lapsekasvatus* and *воспитание детей* for Estonian- and Russian-language manuals printed in Estonia.
