# Estonia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with dialects), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish community; German for Baltic German nursing records; Finnish for care work across the gulf. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural households of Võrumaa, Mulgimaa and Järvamaa against Tallinn and Tartu:** set the distance to services beside who does the caring.
- **Islanders of Hiiumaa, Saaremaa, Kihnu and Ruhnu:** check how care is arranged where a doctor or a care home is a ferry ride away.
- **Seto and Võro communities:** read their own records of healers and of tending the old.
- **Russian-speaking families in Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** compare use of care homes and home help, and the Orthodox parishes' part.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check care of the dying in community practice.
- **The Jewish community, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Belarusians, Ukrainians and Estonian Swedes:** find out which welfare or sick-visiting groups each has run, and when.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox, Catholic and Free Church congregations; Maausk and Taarausk:** compare visiting, diaconal and burial-care practice.
- **Men and women as carers:** check SHARE and carers' association data for who carries the work, without presuming it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, health services and church history; mixed for home care in Russian-speaking families; thin for Tatar and Belarusian care practice.
- **Languages needed:** German for deaconess records; Finnish for the care-migration studies; Russian and Church Slavonic for Orthodox and Old Believer practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** institutional care of people with disabilities, past and present, is sensitive; give residents', advocates' and operators' accounts separately. Health data are restricted. Assisted dying goes to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none.
