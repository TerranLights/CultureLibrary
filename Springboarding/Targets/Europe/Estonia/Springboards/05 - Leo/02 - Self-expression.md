# Estonia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with Mulgi and Kihnu speech), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Seto women singers and lamenters:** read their own repertoire and the community's rules on when and where a lament may be sung.
- **Tallinn's Kalamaja and Telliskivi against small towns of Järvamaa and Läänemaa:** check whether dress and public display vary by place, using photo archives and press, not impressions.
- **Russian-speaking young people of Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe and Lasnamäe:** compare channels (radio, Telegram, school media) and which topics each language community airs in public.
- **Võro and Mulgi speakers:** check how dialect use itself works as a statement, and in what settings.
- **Islanders (Kihnu, Muhu, Saaremaa, Hiiumaa):** compare everyday and festive dress codes across islands.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check rules on dress, beards and head coverings, and how younger members handle them.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Tatars, the Jewish community:** check each society's media for how members present themselves in public.
- **Generations:** set Soviet-era subcultures beside post-1991 youth scenes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for life writing, laments and subcultures; mixed for measured expressiveness (linguistic and survey work, few direct measures); thin for Tatar, Belarusian and Ingrian Finnish self-presentation.
- **Languages needed:** Seto and older dialect Estonian for laments; Russian for Raadio 4 and Russian-language life stories; Swedish for coastal letters and diaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Hirvepark meeting, the 2007 Bronze Soldier events and Pride events are contested; present each group's account as an account. Life stories may name living people and carry access rules. Speech law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
