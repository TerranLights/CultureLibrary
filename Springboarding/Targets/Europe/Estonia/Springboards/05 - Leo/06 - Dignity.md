# Estonia: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian, Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Ukrainian, Estonian Swedish; Swedish and German for the petitions of the 17th-18th c. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-speaking pensioners and workers of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** compare equal-treatment items by home language and citizenship status (Estonian, Russian, undetermined).
- **Rural elders of Võrumaa, Setomaa, Järvamaa and the islands:** check care-home access and what local papers report on the old living alone.
- **Seto and Peipsi Old Believer communities:** check each one's own customs for washing, dressing and burying the dead.
- **People with disabilities in institutions and in community homes:** set their own accounts beside the evaluations.
- **Ukrainians since 2022, earlier Ukrainians and Belarusians, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Estonian Swedes:** look for each community's accounts of respect and disrespect received.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox (both churches), Catholic and Maausk teaching:** compare what each says a person is owed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for equal-treatment surveys, repression records and social statistics; mixed for everyday courtesy; thin for the smallest communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish, German and older Estonian for the petitions; Russian for deportation files and Ida-Virumaa; Ukrainian for the newest arrivals' media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation and prison records name victims and perpetrators and carry access rules; the grey passport and language policy are contested ground, so give each account as its community's own. Assisted dying and care law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. EAA catalog (AIS): search *suplik* and *kaebus* in governorate fonds for 1700-1850.
2. EKI: search the language-advice archive for *teietamine*.
3. Ministry of Social Affairs: download the latest *võrdse kohtlemise monitooring* and its tables by language.
4. Social Insurance Board: tabulate *toimetulekutoetus* recipients by county and year.
5. ERR news archive: search *Starship robot* with *lõhkus* or *kiusas*.
