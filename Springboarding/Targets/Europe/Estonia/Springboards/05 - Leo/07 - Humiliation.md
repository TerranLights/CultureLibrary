# Estonia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (dialect forms for old punishments), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish; German for town and manor records. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-speaking communities of Ida-Virumaa and Lasnamäe:** read each community's own press on labels and public insults before setting it beside Estonian-language accounts.
- **Võro and Seto speakers:** compare the two institutes' memory collections on school shaming.
- **Conscripts from Estonian- and Russian-medium schools:** check whether treatment surveys split results by home language.
- **Students in corporations against those outside them:** compare initiation practices across Estonian, Baltic German (historical) and women's corporations.
- **Islanders (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu) and Mulgimaa farmers:** check ridicule songs and jokes told about them, and their replies.
- **Peipsi Old Believers, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians, the Jewish community, Estonian Swedes:** check each community's own records of ridicule or public insult, and how each recovered standing.
- **Gender:** compare what humiliates men and women as recorded in surveys and memoirs, without assuming the answer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school bullying, folklore of mockery and military surveys; mixed for workplace and online shaming; thin for minorities beyond the Russian-speaking one.
- **Languages needed:** German for the pillory and manor records; Russian for army memoirs and the 1990s press; Võro and Seto for the oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1939-40, the deportations and the 2007 Bronze Soldier events are remembered as humiliations by communities on opposing sides; present each account as an account, never as the group's single view. Cases of intimate-image abuse involve minors and are protected.
- **Held for review:** none.
