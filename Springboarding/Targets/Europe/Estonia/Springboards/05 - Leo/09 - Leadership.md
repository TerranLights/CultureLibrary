# Estonia: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian, Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Ukrainian, Estonian Swedish, Finnish for the Ingrian Finns; German for manor and parish records. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Setomaa:** read how the *ülemsootska* is chosen and what is asked of the person, in Seto sources first.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Muhu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** check who leads island bodies and festivals, and how island and mainland leaders deal with each other.
- **Russian-speaking communities of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** compare how leaders are presented in Russian- and Estonian-language media without treating either as one voice.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check how lay leaders are chosen and what standing they hold in the villages.
- **Mulgimaa, Järvamaa and Läänemaa:** compare municipal elders' records after 1866 for farm wealth and literacy.
- **Women and men:** compare survey items on preferring a woman or a man as boss.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars, the Jewish community, Estonian Swedes:** check who speaks for each community and how that person is chosen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, military and festival leadership; mixed for workplace leadership; thin for leadership inside the smallest communities.
- **Languages needed:** German for manor records; Russian for Ida-Virumaa politics and Orthodox church governance; Finnish for Ingrian material; Seto for the Kingdom Day.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Era of Silence, the restoration movements of 1988-91 and the split between the two Orthodox churches are contested; give each account as its holders' own. Formal authority and office law belong to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. EAA (AIS): search *vallavanem* in rural-municipality fonds for one parish each in Harjumaa, Võrumaa and Saaremaa.
2. Library catalog: search *vaikiv ajastu* and *Päts* for histories since 1991.
3. ERR archive: find *Mõtleme veel*, 13 April 1988.
4. Ministry of Social Affairs: download the latest *soolise võrdõiguslikkuse monitooring*.
5. Kooriühing website: list all general conductors of the song celebrations.
