# Estonia: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with older wedding and market vocabulary), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Ukrainian, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish and Hebrew for the Jewish community; Middle Low German and German for guild and student records. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Baltic German (historical) and Estonian student corporations:** compare written honor codes and how they changed after 1918 and in exile.
- **Village Estonians of Harjumaa, Järvamaa, Mulgimaa and the islands:** check wedding and market records parish by parish for what was said to bring a family disgrace.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** read each institute's records of oaths, curses and honoring the dead.
- **Russian-speaking communities of Ida-Virumaa and Tallinn:** check how honor of wartime dead is framed in their own media and memorial practice.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check rules on beards, dress and conduct that keep a family's standing.
- **Veterans of different armies and generations:** keep each veterans' group's account separate.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Estonian Swedes:** check each community's own commemorations and honors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for guild, student and commemorative records; mixed for everyday honor (keeping one's word); thin for honor codes inside small communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for guild statutes; German for student *Comments*; Russian for monument disputes and Orthodox custom; Hebrew and Yiddish for Jewish veterans' records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Estonian Legion, Soviet monuments, the 2007 Bronze Soldier events and wartime memory are contested ground; present each account as an account and name each community by its own name. Records of "dishonored" women name real people. Defamation and insult law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
