# Estonia: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** work, service, the daily round and health: the domain asks what counts as good work and a well-kept life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Järvamaa and Mulgimaa farms:** check whether the day's shape in time-use data splits by city and country more than by language.
- **Võrumaa and Tartu's South Estonian hinterland:** set the Võro farm year in dialect records beside the North Estonian calendar sources.
- **Setomaa:** read the Seto work year through its own feast cycle; check which days are kept free of work.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Tallinn's Russian-speaking districts:** compare shift work and holiday customs in Russian sources with Estonian-language ones.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu), Pärnu and Läänemaa:** check whether and how household tasks were reassigned during seafaring and fishing absences.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check how fasts and church days set the working week on the shore.
- **Smaller communities:** for Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars and Jews, find the trade and guild lists that name members' occupations.
- **Faiths:** ask Lutheran, Orthodox (both churches), Maausk and Taarausk sources which days bar work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the farm year, time use and Soviet labor; mixed for the workplace after 1991; thin for Tatar, Belarusian and Jewish working life (community archives first).
- **Lands:** Virgo is written for Estonia as it stands. With no settled unit list and no change to the pre-war lands in the tracker header, nothing here proposes units or moves a border. Comparison only: Latvia, Lithuania, Finland, Belarus, Poland, Sweden and Russia's culture-nations, including the Pechory district that Seto speakers count as Setomaa. Diaspora communities abroad inherit the nation's findings.
- **Languages needed:** German for Kohl and the manor ledgers behind Kahk's work; Russian for the oil-shale press; Swedish for the coastal dictionary; dialect Estonian for Tampere's song texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor under the manor and in Soviet camps is remembered differently by community; keep each account as an account. Labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside for this domain sheet.
