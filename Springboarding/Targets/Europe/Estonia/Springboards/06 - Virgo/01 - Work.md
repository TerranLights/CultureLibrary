# Estonia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with Kihnu, Hiiumaa and Mulgi work vocabularies), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish; German and Middle Low German for guild and manor records; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare Russian-language factory and mine records with Estonian ones; check how job loss after 1991 is narrated in each.
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** separate office and tech work from port and factory work before comparing with the counties.
- **Kihnu, Muhu, Saaremaa and Hiiumaa:** check seafaring, fishing and island-factory work against inland farming.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare the larger farmsteads' hired labor with smallholdings elsewhere.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check seasonal work away from home and Seto work records in the Seto Institute's own collections.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check fishing and market gardening, and which church days stop work.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Jews:** find occupational lists in each community's society files; sources may be thin.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians:** in the short-term employment data, keep recent arrivals apart from families settled in Soviet decades, and read their unions and job boards in their own languages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor statistics, Soviet labor records and island crafts; mixed for 19th-c. factory life; thin for Tatar, Jewish and Belarusian trades.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for the Canute Guild rolls; Russian for the Kreenholm reports and Soviet model-worker files; Finnish for commuting studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet labor heroism, forced labor in camps and the 1991 job losses carry rival accounts; give each as an account. The Soviet offense of "parasitism" and all labor law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside under the standing rules for work.
