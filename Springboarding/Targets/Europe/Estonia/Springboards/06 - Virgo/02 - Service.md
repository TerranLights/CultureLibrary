*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian, Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia (including church Russian at Pühtitsa and in Old Believer use), Estonian Swedish, Yiddish; Latin and Low German for the medieval land and vassal records. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Tartu and South Estonia:** check who fills public-service and hospitality jobs in the capital and in the university town.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare conscription and civil-service figures with the rest of the country, and read local Russian-language debate on them in its own terms.
- **Setomaa:** check the elected *ülemsootska* (*check*) of Seto Kingdom Day and other rotating Seto roles as service to the community, from Seto sources.
- **Islands and the Pärnu and Läänemaa coast:** compare volunteer sea and fire rescue with mainland crews.
- **Pühtitsa, the Peipsi Old Believers and the Lutheran parishes:** set the convent's obediences, the Old Believers' lay offices and the Lutheran sexton side by side as religious service roles.
- **Võrumaa and Mulgimaa:** check memoirs of young people sent into farm service in larger farms.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Tatars:** look for each congregation's or society's own service offices (the sexton, the burial society, the imam's helpers).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for conscription, civil service and Soviet youth movements; mixed for farm service before 1940; thin for domestic servants in Baltic German town houses (address books and memoirs).
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Danish land book; German for the Knighthood archive and pastors' reports; Russian for the convent and the pioneer press; Yiddish for burial-society records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** service in wartime armies (Soviet, German, Finnish) and the Soviet deportations carry rival accounts; present each as an account and name each community by its own name. Convent life is not open to all visitors; ask first. Conscription law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the scan of service sources turned up nothing that the standing rules exclude.
