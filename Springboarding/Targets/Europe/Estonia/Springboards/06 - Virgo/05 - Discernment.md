## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check port-city savvy (ferry trade, tourist scams) against inland towns' sources.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare scam warnings and media-literacy work aimed at Russian-speaking readers; read local Russian-language accounts first.
- **Saaremaa, Muhu and Hiiumaa:** check island criteria for beer, fish and boats in local museums and contests.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** read Seto and Võro judges' marks in their own words; check where they differ from national contests.
- **Pärnu, Läänemaa and Tori:** look at horse-breeding and fair judging records across the west.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check how congregations weigh outside news and teaching, from their own leaders' statements.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare farm buyers' and sellers' testing of grain, flax and livestock in correspondent replies.
- **Estonian Swedes, Tatars, the Jewish community, Ukrainians:** look for each group's own media and advice channels on scams and news.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scams, consumer complaints and trust surveys; mixed for taste contests; thin for spiritual discernment outside the Lutheran church (Orthodox and Old Believer sources need asking in person).
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for the inspectors' oaths; Russian for Soviet slang and black-market memoirs; Finnish for ferry-trade accounts from the other side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** media-literacy campaigns and disinformation labels are contested ground between Estonian- and Russian-language media; give each account as an account. Hiring-bias research must not become a claim about a people. Fraud law goes to Libra.
