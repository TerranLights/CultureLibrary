# Estonia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (standard and dialect, for the language-norm debates), Võro and Seto (for their own writing standards), the Russian of Estonia, Estonian Swedish; Middle Low German for the goldsmiths' craft. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Tartu:** compare exam pressure and the press on top scores in elite city gymnasiums with rural schools in Järvamaa, Läänemaa and Mulgimaa.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-medium schools in Tallinn:** check how language-correctness checks and exam standards are experienced, from Russian-language sources first.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check debates over Võro and Seto spelling, where the aim may be a separate standard rather than correctness in Estonian.
- **Saaremaa, Muhu and Kihnu:** compare how exact a parish or island costume must be in each place, using the islands' own costume makers.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check exactness in church singing and reading, from the congregations themselves.
- **Lutherans, Methodists and other Free Churches, the two Orthodox churches:** compare teaching on perfection and on scrupulous conscience.
- **Estonian Swedes and Ingrian Finns:** look for their own costume, song and language standards in revival societies; sources may be thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language planning, exams and teacher surveys; mixed for craft standards; thin for clinical perfectionism outside student samples.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German and German for the goldsmiths; Russian for the Quality Mark and for Ida-Virumaa staff accounts; Võro and Seto for spelling debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language checks of Russian-speaking workers and the move to Estonian-medium schooling are contested; present each account as an account and name each community by its own name. Eating-disorder records are protected health data. Language law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** for perfectionism, no pointer was withheld; the standing rules were not triggered.
