# Estonia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with old runo-song language for songs against the manor), the Russian of Estonia, German for 18th- and 19th-c. polemics, Võro and Seto, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Paide and Järvamaa:** compare who speaks at the Opinion Festival with who writes the capital's opinion pages.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** check the university town's literary and student criticism against Tallinn's political press.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-speaking Tallinn:** read local criticism of citizenship rules and school language policy in Russian, as accounts, alongside the officials' replies.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check criticism of the center in Võro and Seto writing and at the Seto Congress.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu):** look at island papers' letters pages for complaints about ferries and the mainland.
- **Peipsi Old Believers and the two Orthodox churches:** check how each answers public criticism, from their own statements.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, the Jewish community, Ukrainians:** check each community's own press for complaint and self-criticism.
- **Generations:** compare Soviet-trained and post-1991 managers on giving workplace feedback.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary criticism, satire and media complaints; mixed for workplace feedback; thin for criticism inside Old Believer and Tatar communities.
- **Languages needed:** German for Eisen; Russian for party minutes, *Советская Эстония* and the Russian opinion pages; old Estonian orthography for *Sakala*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "grey passport" (undetermined citizenship), the Soviet deportations and the 2007 Bronze Soldier events draw sharp, opposed criticism; present each as an account and name each community by its own name. Libel and press law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the criticism sources here met every standing rule; none was moved to review.

1. Regilaul database: search *kubjas* and *mõisnik* and note parishes.
