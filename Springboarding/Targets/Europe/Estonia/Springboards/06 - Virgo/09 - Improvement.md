*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare renovation, adult learning and job retraining; read Russian-language accounts of the language-learning push as accounts.
- **Tartu and South Estonia, Mulgimaa:** check the farmers'-society movement's home ground against later Soviet and EU-funded programs.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check whether local action groups frame improvement as renewal of Võro and Seto culture, from their own project texts.
- **Islands and the west coast (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Pärnu, Läänemaa):** compare drainage, harbors and tourism projects as island improvements.
- **Järvamaa:** look at Soviet-era model kolkhoz settlements and drainage schemes in a farming county.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check which changes to villages and fishing the congregations welcomed or refused.
- **Revival after 1988:** for the Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Jews and Tatars, list which schools, congregations and houses each set out to rebuild, using the minutes of its own society.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land policy, housing programs and adult learning; mixed for Soviet rationalizers (factory files are scattered); thin for self-help reading by Russian speakers (bookshop data first).
- **Languages needed:** German for Hupel and the estate literature; Russian for imperial school policy and the rationalizer files; Võro and Seto for local action-group texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Russification, Soviet resettlement and today's language policy are each told differently by the communities involved; present each as an account and say whose it is. Drained land claims may still be disputed.
- **Held for review:** improvement sources kept within the standing rules; nothing awaits review.
