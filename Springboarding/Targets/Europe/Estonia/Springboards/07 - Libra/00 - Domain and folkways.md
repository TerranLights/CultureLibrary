# Estonia: The domain: partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; German and Latin for older records. **Concept in one line:** partnership and justice: what makes a fair bargain and a good partnership (partners, marriage, contracts, open enemies, balance, justice), with the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa farms against Läänemaa and Pärnu's coast:** check whether bridal gift lists differ with farm wealth and sea trade.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** set Seto wedding song cycles and Võro wedding records beside North Estonian ones; check who leads each rite.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-speaking Tallinn:** compare civil and Orthodox weddings, and marriage and divorce figures by home language.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** check how matches were made across the water, and what treaty-era sources say of island elders.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check how priestless congregations solemnize and register a marriage.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians, the Jewish community:** check each one's marriage registers and what the autonomy law granted it.
- **Faiths:** compare Lutheran and both Orthodox churches' marriage rules with the wedding forms Maausk and Taarausk publish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for laws, marriage statistics and bridal-gift ethnography; mixed for gender attitudes over time; thin for economic games and smaller communities' marriage customs.
- **Lands:** Estonia's exact lands are not yet settled. Every Libra sheet takes the country as it now is, draws up no units and moves no border. Neighbors cited here (Finland, Latvia, Lithuania, Poland, Belarus, Sweden, and the culture-nations of Russia, the Pechory district among them, which the Seto call part of Setomaa) serve as comparison only; emigrant Estonians in Sweden, Canada, Australia and the US take the nation's findings rather than their own.
- **Languages needed:** German for the 1864 code; Latin and Middle High German for the chronicle and treaties; Russian for the 1920 files; Seto and Võro for wedding songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1920 border, cultural autonomy and same-sex marriage are contested; keep each community's and party's account as its own.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Riigi Teataja: open *perekonnaseadus* and *kooseluseadus* in English and list who may marry or register.
2. Statistics Estonia: search *abielud* and *lahutused* by county and ethnic nationality.
3. MUIS: search *veimevakk* and *veimed* and sort objects by parish.
4. Library catalog: "Livländische Reimchronik" in Estonian translation, plus the 1241 treaty text.
5. DIGAR: search *tutvumiskuulutus* in papers of 1925-1938 and 1965-1990.
