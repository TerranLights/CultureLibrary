# Estonia: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the Jewish community before 1941; Middle Low German for Hanseatic papers. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples. (Marriage law and wedding rites are in the domain sheet.)

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Järvamaa and Mulgimaa:** check whether cohabitation before marriage and joint farm ownership differ between capital and farm country.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how couples of mixed language background are counted and how their weddings are held.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** compare crew partnerships on the lake with Estonian fishing villages on the same shore.
- **Lahemaa and the north coast against Saaremaa, Muhu and Hiiumaa:** check how ship and boat shares were held in each.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** compare Seto co-godparent bonds with Lutheran godparents in Võro parishes.
- **Tartu and Pärnu:** check Hanseatic-era merchant partnerships outside Tallinn's well-kept records.
- **Ingrian Finns, Estonian Swedes, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars, the Jewish community:** check each one's records of family firms and partners from the same community.
- **Faiths:** compare marriage-preparation teaching in Lutheran and both Orthodox churches with partnership forms that Maausk and Taarausk publish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cooperatives, treaties and company records; mixed for couple attitudes; thin for crew shares and for the smaller communities' business partnerships.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for Hanseatic papers; Russian for Peipsi crew records and Orthodox godparent customs; Seto for its kinship terms; English for start-up press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** interethnic marriage and the 2004 alliances are argued over in politics; keep each side's account separate. Same-sex partnership is contested; present the debate, not a verdict.
- **Held for review:** none.
