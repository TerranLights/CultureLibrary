## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Järvamaa, Mulgimaa and Läänemaa:** check whether reported overwork and rest patterns split by city and country.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare health-survey stress items, and shift work in the oil-shale industry, with the rest of the country.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** check how the Seto feast and fast cycle and Võro farm calendars pace work and rest.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check fasting rules as a discipline of restraint, from congregation sources.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu:** check hunting and fishing quotas as island communities argue them, and island textile motifs.
- **Pärnu and the west coast:** check summer resort life and second homes as a seasonal swing between work and rest.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars:** check how each describes living between two languages, from its own community press.
- **Faiths:** compare Lutheran and both Orthodox churches' teaching on moderation and the sabbath with Maausk and Taarausk views of balance with nature.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for laws, budgets, health surveys and the forest debate; mixed for folk healing; thin for how the smaller communities talk of balance.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for Tallinn's weights ordinances; Russian for the 1985 campaign and Old Believer fasting rules; dialect Estonian for healing records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1934 emergency rule is judged very differently by historians and parties; the forest and wolf debates divide towns and villages; keep each side's account apart. Health records are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
