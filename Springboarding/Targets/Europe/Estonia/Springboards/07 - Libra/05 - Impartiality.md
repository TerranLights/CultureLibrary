# Estonia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with South Estonian, Mulgi and island dialect forms), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish, Yiddish for the interwar Jewish community; German for manor-era records. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship. Letter of the law only for the rules on judges and officials.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn against small municipalities of Järvamaa, Läänemaa and Võrumaa:** check whether recusal and conflict-of-interest cases look different where councilors and residents are kin.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare corruption-survey replies and press-council complaints in Russian-language media with Estonian-language ones.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and the small islands (Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu, Muhu):** check how one-doctor, one-teacher or one-official communities handle cases where the official knows everyone.
- **Setomaa:** check how the Seto Congress and the *ülemsootska* (elected Seto representative, *check*) are chosen, and how disputes over neutrality are raised.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check congregation elections and lay leaders' roles in village matters.
- **Tatars, the Jewish community, Belarusians, Estonian Swedes, Ukrainians and Ingrian Finns:** look in each group's own newsletters and society minutes for complaints about officials, and for praise of them.
- **Faiths:** compare Lutheran church-court rules and the two Orthodox churches' canons on judging clergy without favor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, surveys and press-council rulings; mixed for local favoritism; thin for how the smaller communities judge officials' neutrality.
- **Languages needed:** German for the 1816-1819 peasant laws; Russian for ETV+ complaints and Ida-Virumaa coverage; Swedish for the *jäv* concept as Estonian Swedes used it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption trials of named politicians, i-voting disputes and language inspections are contested; report each side's account, never a verdict. Declarations of interests are public, but private details in them are not for quotation.
- **Held for review:** none.
