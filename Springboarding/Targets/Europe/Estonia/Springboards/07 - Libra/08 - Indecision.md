# Estonia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with North and South Estonian, Mulgi and island speech), Võro, Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, Estonian Swedish. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others (persons, families, committees, parliaments; chance, signs or God), and its better form, deliberation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** check whether late deciding in referendums and elections differs between city and farm districts.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how residents and town councils describe living with delayed decisions on oil shale and plant closures.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** compare suitor and fortune-telling songs and customs with North Estonian ones; check Seto *leelo* versions.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Muhu:** check how island communities followed long-delayed fixed-link and ferry decisions.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check how congregational decisions are reached and how long they take.
- **Estonian Swedes and Ingrian Finns:** check family accounts of deciding whether to leave for Sweden or Finland, and when.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars and the Jewish community:** check community press for accounts of choosing between staying and moving on.
- **Lutherans, the Orthodox of both churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** compare how each speaks of waiting for guidance or of leaving a decision to God or fate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for election data, public projects and parliamentary records; mixed for literature and folklore of choosing; thin for personal indecision measures by region.
- **Languages needed:** dialect Estonian and Seto for runo songs; Russian for Ida-Virumaa energy debates; Swedish and Finnish for emigration decisions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2023 obstruction and the oil-shale exit are partisan and regional disputes; keep government, opposition and local accounts separate. Health declarations are private.
- **Held for review:** none.
