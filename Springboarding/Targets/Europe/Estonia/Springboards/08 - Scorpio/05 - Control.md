*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mainland farm parishes of Järvamaa and Mulgimaa against Tallinn:** check herding and luck charms by parish, and city sources on drink and its policing.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** look at fire and land customs, and at Seto bodies' own rules on heritage use.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Sillamäe, Kohtla-Järve):** separate the planned arrivals of the 1950s-80s from older Russian-speaking and Estonian residents, and check each one's memory of being sent, housed and watched.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Vormsi and Ruhnu:** compare border-zone memories island by island and the 2020 Saaremaa closure as islanders describe it.
- **Kihnu:** check who on the island decides on heritage, visitors and filming.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check how congregations set rules for members' dress, drink and contact, in their own writings.
- **Jewish community and Tatars:** check what Soviet control of worship meant for each, and what each restored after 1988.
- **Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** check each one's account of planned or forced moves; little is published, so begin with their societies' archives.
- **Faiths:** compare Lutheran household teaching, Orthodox (both churches) teaching on obedience, and Maausk and Taarausk writing on fate and luck.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** Soviet-era restrictions and herding charms are well documented; mixed for partner violence and school rules; thin for measured locus of control.
- **Languages needed:** pre-1870s Estonian orthography for the household tables; Russian for orgnabor files and sobering-up records; Swedish for island border-zone memoirs; Seto for heritage rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner-violence data concern victims, so use aggregates; Soviet-era migration and the border zone are told very differently by communities, so keep each telling as its own; COVID-19 rules remain disputed. Law on these topics goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no control pointer was set aside.
