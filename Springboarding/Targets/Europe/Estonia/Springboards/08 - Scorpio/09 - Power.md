# Estonia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how its holders, its holding and its use are judged. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Tartu and South Estonia:** check where officeholders, party funders and media owners come from, place by place.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** look at *vägi* beliefs in South Estonian records and at how Võro and Seto bodies seek a say in national decisions.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how dependence on one energy employer shapes local politics, in residents' own words, and compare council make-up by language.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu) and Läänemaa:** check island councils' leverage against Tallinn and how the bishops' rule is told on Saaremaa.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** look at who speaks for the shore villages to county and state, and through which bodies.
- **Jewish community and Tatars:** look at each body's own record of representation under the cultural-autonomy arrangements, then and now.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Ingrian Finns:** look for seats each holds on councils and advisory boards; records are few, so their associations' own reports come first.
- **Faiths:** compare the Lutheran church's historical place beside the manor, the standing of both Orthodox churches, and Maausk and Taarausk accounts of power held by land and ancestors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** election records and Soviet-era political history are plentiful; mixed for elite networks; thin for folk *vägi* beliefs in modern synthesis.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Low German for the bishopric's charters; German for manor-era sources; Russian for party files and Intermovement papers; Võro for South Estonian belief records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1919 reform, Soviet-era leaders, Narva's council quarrels and the 2007 events are told very differently by Estonian, Russian-speaking and Baltic German descendants; present each as an account. Party-funding cases involve living people; cite records, not verdicts.
- **Held for review:** none; no power pointer was set aside.
