# Estonia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn tech and service workers against Järvamaa and Mulgimaa farmers:** see whether work, family or land is named first as a source of worth.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** follow miners, power-plant staff and their children through the shale exit, in Russian-language accounts.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check whether language revival and village life appear as reasons to stay; Pechory district belongs to comparison only.
- **The islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** look at returners and newcomers who say island life gave them a point.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** compare students and retired academics on calling and life's work.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** set their sung verses beside younger members' own accounts.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, Jewish community, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** look for each group's memoirs of rebuilding a life after war, deportation or flight; for Ukrainians since 2022, start with their community's own channels.
- **Churchgoers of each faith against the unaffiliated majority:** check whether purpose is described in God-talk, family-talk or nature-talk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey well-being and oral poetry; mixed for philosophy; thin for minority voices on purpose outside Tallinn and Narva.
- **Languages needed:** old runo-song dialects for the lot songs; Church Slavonic and Russian for spiritual verses; Russian for shale-town interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** despair and suicide data are health data, to be used in aggregate; shale-town job loss is politically charged, so labor, state and company accounts stay apart.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the meaning sources above are all nameable.
