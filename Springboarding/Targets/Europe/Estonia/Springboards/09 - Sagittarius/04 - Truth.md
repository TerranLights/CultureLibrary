*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how disputes over facts are settled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tartu and South Estonia against Tallinn and Harjumaa:** weigh how far the old university town and the capital each lean on scientists as arbiters of fact.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-speaking Tallinn:** check which news sources residents name as reliable, and how school history differs by generation; keep each account labeled.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** see whose word on local history counts (archives, elders, revival leaders); Pechory district is comparison only.
- **The islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu):** check whether island museums file elders' memories as record or as story.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** follow Tammsaare's Vargamäe readers and local farm memory against the novel's picture.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** ask who in a prayer house rules on a disputed reading, and on what authority.
- **Jewish community, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Estonian Swedes:** check how each group's own historians challenge or confirm the national account of 1940-1944; few studies exist for Ingrian Finns, so begin with their society's publications.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians:** look at how newcomers since 2022 choose news sources, in their own community channels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history-politics and media studies; mixed for trust in science; thin for folk ideas of truth beyond grammar and jokes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, with care over *правда* and *истина*; Church Slavonic for the old books; Seto for *Peko* criticism.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Soviet deportations, the 2007 Bronze Soldier events and citizenship policy carry rival truths; each account is cited as that community's account, and none is ruled on here.
- **Held for review:** no source on facts, lies or disinformation was withheld here.
