# Estonia: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (a finding of guilt is not this concept).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Tartu and South Estonia:** compare where protests, petitions and pickets start, and who joins them.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-speaking Tallinn:** set the schooling campaign beside other Russian-language voices who back the change; give each its own account.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check language activists' own statements of why they keep Võro and Seto in use; Pechory district only for comparison.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi and Ruhnu:** follow island fights over ferries, offshore wind and harbors, and the words islanders use for holding out.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare farmers' replies to the fur ban and other rural stands with urban activists'.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** see how holding to the old rite is told by elders and by those who left.
- **Jewish community, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** check which causes each community's leaders took up publicly; for Ukrainians, look at volunteer aid since 2022 in their own channels.
- **Lutherans, Catholics, the Orthodox of two churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** set each body's public statements on contested questions side by side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late-Soviet movements and parliamentary records; mixed for religious conviction; thin for quieter, private stands.
- **Languages needed:** 19th-c. Estonian and German for Maltsvet sources; Russian for KGB files and the schooling campaign; Latin and Italian for Catholic records on Profittlich.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the schooling change, citizenship policy and the marriage-law change are contested ground, so every camp's account stays its own; KGB files name living people and carry access rules.
- **Held for review:** the list of stands and those who took them needed no withholding.
