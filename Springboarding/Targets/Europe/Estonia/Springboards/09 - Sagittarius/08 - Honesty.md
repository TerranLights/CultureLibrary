# Estonia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital region and the south:** set Tallinn's free-fare experience and Harjumaa's building-trade cash wages against Tartu's fare checks and South Estonian small-firm reports.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe) and Russian-speaking Tallinn:** check which scam warnings reach Russian-language audiences and what *блат* meant in local memories of Soviet shortages, read beside Estonian-language accounts of the same years.
- **Võrumaa, Setomaa and the border trade:** look at how small cross-border trade is talked about in local memories; Pechory district only as comparison.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa farm families:** set Soviet collective-farm memories of taking and sharing beside each family's own judgment.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu and the west coast:** check island and coastal memories of rum-running and smuggling as told today, honest trade or crime by whose measure.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** look at how market-garden traders describe fair weight and fair price.
- **Jewish community and Tatars; Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians and Belarusians:** search the first two communities' own periodicals for teaching on fair trade; for the other three, write to their associations, since little is printed.
- **Confession and catechism:** check how the Lutheran, both Orthodox, Catholic and Old Believer practices of confession treat lying, and whether Maausk writers address it at all.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and press exposés; mixed for folklore of cheating; thin for minority views of honesty.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-c. Estonian in Stahl's spelling; Russian for scam warnings and *блат* memories; Old Believer Russian for trade terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption and smuggling stories name living people; pilfering memories can shame narrators; no community may be cast as more or less honest than another.
- **Held for review:** the honesty sources above raised no rule and none was withheld.
