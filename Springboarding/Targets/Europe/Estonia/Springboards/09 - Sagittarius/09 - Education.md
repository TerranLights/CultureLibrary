## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tartu and South Estonia against Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare the old university town's academic families with the capital's technical and business graduates.
- **Russian-speaking families of Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe and Tallinn:** trace three generations' paths (Leningrad or Tartu institutes, then private colleges, then Estonian-taught programs) as families tell them.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** look at how many young people go to university and come back, and whether Võro and Seto are studied there; Pechory district is comparison only.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi and Ruhnu:** follow the islands' distance from higher learning through regional campuses and leaving for study.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare the farm families who sent the first Estonian students in the 19th c. with today's rural attainment.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** see how lay learning in the old books (*начётчики*) sits beside university study.
- **Estonian Swedes, Jewish community, Tatars, Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** compare each group's own schools and scholars before 1940 and since 1991; for Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians and Belarusians little is written, so begin with community archives.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox and Catholic schooling bodies:** check what each church-run school or theology program offers and who enrolls.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for university history and attainment statistics; mixed for teachers' standing; thin for minority scholarship outside Tartu.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and German for the old *Album* and the Dorpat years; Latvian for the Valga seminary records; Russian for Soviet and post-1991 Russian-medium higher education.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the move of schools to Estonian-medium teaching is contested ground; give each community's account as its own. Student records after 1940 hold personal data.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage students and staff at Estonian universities, kept as a category only; set aside from the student-mobility data.
