# Estonia: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Latin and German for chronicle and manor records. **Concept in one line:** authority and duty: who commands, who obeys, and what is owed, across career, status and reputation, with age, time, rank, order and power ways as folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn with Harjumaa, set beside Tartu and the south:** check whether capital and university town differ in the address used with superiors.
- **Võrumaa and Mulgimaa:** compare farm-purchase dates, to see how buying land changed a household's standing.
- **Setomaa:** set the Kingdom Day offices beside the municipal council formed in 2017.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check mining and plant ladders, and monitoring answers on having a say.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu), Läänemaa and the Pärnu coast:** compare sea-season with field-season calendars in ERM answers.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** see who chooses the *nastavnik*.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** trace the officers of each group's own societies; records for the last three are scant.
- **Lutherans, the Orthodox of two churches, Maausk and Taarausk:** check how office is handed on in each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and manor-era history; mixed for address and time customs; thin for smaller groups' own offices.
- **Lands:** the post-war lands are unsettled and the tracker carries no pre-war change, so this sign writes for the republic as it now is, drawing no units and deciding no border. Latvia, Lithuania, Finland, Sweden, Poland, Belarus and Russia's culture-nations (Pechory district included, which the Seto count in Setomaa) are comparison only, and the findings made for Estonia carry over to Estonians abroad.
- **Languages needed:** Latin (Henry's chronicle); German (Hupel); Russian for monitoring questionnaires and Soviet-era press; Seto for festival records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** citizenship status and the Päts years are weighed differently from one community to the next; label every verdict with whose it is.
- **Held for review:** none.
