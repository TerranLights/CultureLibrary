# Estonia: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Latin and Low German for medieval chronicles. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Toompea and old Tallinn, and the rest of Harjumaa:** set the seat of government against suburban councils, to see where national and local authority meet.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** check how the university town, home of the Supreme Court, appears in debate over courts' authority.
- **Narva, Sillamäe and Kohtla-Järve:** compare 1993 referendum records and today's council politics, in each community's own words.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** set the Seto Congress beside the municipal council, and check how Võro language advocates address the state.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** check how the *nastavnik*'s word is weighed against state rules.
- **Estonian Swedes and Ingrian Finns:** see what their cultural self-governments (formed 2007 and 2004 *check*) may decide.
- **Islands (Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Ruhnu):** check how small island councils deal with a distant capital.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars and the Jewish community:** find who speaks for each to the state; sources for the last two are newer and few.
- **Faith communities (Lutheran, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk):** see how each names its highest earthly and spiritual authority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, statutes and the 1918 and 1990s founding papers; mixed for workplace authority; thin for minority bodies.
- **Languages needed:** Low German and Latin (the 1343 accounts); German for Stahl; Russian for the Intermovement and Narva records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1990-1991 legitimacy contest, the 1993 referendums and the church dispute are told in rival accounts, each to be labeled; spirit-of-law reading only, since the letter belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
