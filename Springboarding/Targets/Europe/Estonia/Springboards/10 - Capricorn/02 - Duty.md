# Estonia: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Low German and Swedish for manor-era dues. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check whether urban Defence League units recruit differently from the rural ones of Järvamaa and Läänemaa.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** set defense-survey answers on conscription side by side by home language, and see how May 9 is kept.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** check how graveyard feast days keep the duty to the dead, and who leads the laments now.
- **Mulgimaa and Tartu county:** set wackenbook dues of one manor beside another, to see whether burdens differed.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu and Kihnu, with the Pärnu shore:** check how fishing and seafaring households split home duties, in island museums' own records.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** see what prayer and memorial duties their rule lays on each household.
- **Estonian Swedes:** check how churchyards on Vormsi, Ruhnu and Noarootsi are tended since 1944.
- **The Jewish community, Ingrian Finns, Tatars:** look for each community's burial and memorial societies; Ukrainian and Belarusian sources are recent and few.
- **Faiths:** check what Lutheran pastors, priests of each Orthodox church and Maausk keepers ask of the bereaved.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for defense opinion, volunteering and archives of dues; mixed for family care; thin for minority memorial duties.
- **Languages needed:** Low German and Swedish for the wackenbooks; Seto for laments; Russian for May 9 coverage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime service on either side and the May 9 gatherings are contested; each community's account stays its own. Grieving families' care records are private. Duty as a legal command is read for its spirit only.
- **Held for review:** none.
