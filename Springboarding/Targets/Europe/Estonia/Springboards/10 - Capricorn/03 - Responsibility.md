# Estonia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; German for 19th-c. municipal law. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ida-Virumaa's towns (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** look at how Estonian- and Russian-language local papers place the blame for mine closures and polluted sites.
- **Pärnu and the west coast:** check how the 2001 poisonings changed local talk of who answers for drinking deaths.
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check how ministers' resignations are reported, against the audit office's findings.
- **Võrumaa, Setomaa and Järvamaa:** compare village-elder statutes, to see how far each municipality hands responsibility to villages.
- **Kihnu and the other islands (Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Vormsi):** see who is charged with keeping Kihnu's listed heritage alive, as the island's own foundation states it.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers and fishing villages:** check who answers for catch limits on the lake.
- **The Jewish community:** check how its own statements and state bodies speak of responsibility for 1941-1944, each in its own words.
- **Ingrian Finns, Estonian Swedes, Belarusians, Ukrainians and Tatars:** look for community histories naming who stood answerable for the group in hard years; little is in print.
- **Lutherans, Orthodox of both churches, Old Believers:** compare how confession and penance are practiced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for audits, disaster inquiries and attitude surveys; mixed for children's responsibilities; thin for small communities.
- **Languages needed:** German for the 1866 act; Swedish and Finnish beside Estonian for MS *Estonia* inquiries; Russian for Ida-Virumaa press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1995 registration, the MS *Estonia* disputes and 1941-1944 are contested; survivors' and relatives' accounts stay labeled. Youth-case files are closed. Legal liability enters here only as its purpose and spirit.
- **Held for review:** none.
