## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tartu and South Estonia, Mulgimaa:** check Moravian prayer-house density in Livonian parishes against Harjumaa and Läänemaa.
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare school house rules between Estonian- and Russian-language schools.
- **Narva, Kohtla-Järve and Sillamäe in Ida-Virumaa:** set health-behaviour figures and school-discipline reports against national ones, and ask what local experts say drives them.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers and Setomaa:** compare fasting calendars and who enforces them.
- **Võrumaa:** check temperance-society records against those of the north.
- **Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Kihnu:** check island sea schools and crews for their own rules of conduct at sea.
- **Estonian Swedes:** look at Moravian influence in Swedish-speaking Vormsi and Noarootsi, recorded in Swedish.
- **The Jewish community, Tatars:** check religious schooling and fasts (Yom Kippur, Ramadan) in each community's own sources; Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians and Belarusians have few printed sources.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox, Maausk:** compare whom each asks to fast or abstain, and when.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parish and school records and health surveys; mixed for the army; thin for home discipline before 1990.
- **Languages needed:** German for Moravian and pastoral records; Swedish for Vormsi; Russian, with Church Slavonic, for the Peipsi congregations' prayer rule.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** physical punishment and youth-organization memories touch private harm; survivors' accounts first. Old Believer prayer life is shown at the community's discretion.
- **Held for review:** none.
