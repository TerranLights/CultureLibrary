# Estonia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Latin and German for the old university registers. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mulgimaa against Võrumaa:** set farm-purchase dates and house-building side by side, to see when each region's farmers began to buy out the manor.
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check where start-up founders and executives come from, and how many moved in from other counties.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** look at Tartu's student body over time, as the route upward for rural families.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** weigh young people's plans to stay, move to Tallinn or leave, in Russian- and Estonian-language answers.
- **Setomaa and the Peipsi shore:** check what success stories local museums and papers tell, and which are left out.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu and the Pärnu coast:** check how sea captains and shipowners' families are remembered as models of rising.
- **Estonian Swedes:** look at the Swedish-language school and seaman routes from Noarootsi and Vormsi before 1944.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar records of Jewish students at Tartu, including the Chair of Jewish Studies (1934 *check*).
- **Ingrian Finns, Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars:** look at community histories for how members built careers; sources are thin.
- **Lutheran, Orthodox, Maausk communities:** check how sermons and teaching speak of striving and contentment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mobility, youth and election data; mixed for the Soviet honors system; thin for minority careers.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and German for *Album Academicum*; Russian for Soviet labor press and Ida-Virumaa youth surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "rising" under Soviet rule is judged in conflicting ways; keep careerists' and critics' accounts apart. Avoid national-character claims about striving.
- **Held for review:** none.
