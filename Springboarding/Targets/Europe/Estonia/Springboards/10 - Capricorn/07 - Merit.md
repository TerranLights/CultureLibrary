# Estonia: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia and of the Peipsi Old Believers, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Middle Low German for craft statutes. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** see how selective Tallinn gymnasiums are compared with county schools, and who pays for test tutoring.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** compare olympiad winners from Tartu schools with those from Võrumaa and Põlvamaa.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** read language-requirement cases and job-ad demands side by side with local answers on connections.
- **Setomaa:** check how song mastery and village standing relate, in Seto sources.
- **Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** check whether farm inheritance or farm-buying is told as the deserving route.
- **Kihnu, Saaremaa, Hiiumaa:** check how skippers and master boatbuilders earn their name on the islands.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** see how icon painters and singers win standing in the congregation.
- **Ingrian Finns and Estonian Swedes:** find out how scholarships and cultural awards are given by their own bodies.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Tatars and the Jewish community:** look at how qualifications earned from abroad are treated; post-2022 Ukrainian arrivals are a new and thinly studied case.
- **Lutherans, Orthodox, Maausk:** compare teaching on works and grace.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, honors and corruption surveys; mixed for hiring practice; thin for minority awards.
- **Languages needed:** the guilds' Middle Low German (*Schragen*); Russian for Ida-Virumaa job and language cases; Seto for song titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** citizenship by descent and the grey passport, and language requirements, are contested ground; describe no group's character, and attribute each account to whoever gives it.
- **Held for review:** none.
