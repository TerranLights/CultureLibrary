*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North, South, Mulgi, island speech), Võro, Seto; Estonia's Russian, Peipsi Old Believer usage included; Estonian Swedish; Yiddish (prewar Jewish community); German for society records. **Concept in one line:** community and ideals: what binds people into groups, and what they hope for, with settlement, migration, association and affiliation as the social ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check how apartment-district neighboring (Lasnamäe, Mustamäe) differs from old-town and suburban patterns, with home language recorded.
- **Tartu and South Estonia, Mulgimaa:** test the society movement's spread, comparing Livonian parishes with Estland ones.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** set Seto *kirmas* gatherings beside Võro village societies to see which occasions call everyone out.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** compare association counts and their kinds in towns built around one plant.
- **The islands (Muhu, Saaremaa, Ruhnu, Hiiumaa, Vormsi, Kihnu), the Pärnu coast and Läänemaa:** check whether island and shore societies follow the sea season.
- **Old Believer villages on Lake Peipsi:** see whether the prayer congregation also organized fishing-village life, and in what records.
- **Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Estonian Swedes, Ukrainians, the Jewish community, Belarusians:** list the cultural societies each founded before 1940 and after 1988, to date when each rebuilt its group life; some have few sources.
- **Faith bodies (Lutheran; both Orthodox churches; Maausk; Taarausk):** check what each asks of members beyond worship.
