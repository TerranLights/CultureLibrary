# Estonia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian (with Võro, Seto, Mulgi and island speech), Russian as used in Estonia, Estonian Swedish, Ukrainian, Yiddish for the prewar Jewish community; 17th-18th-c. written Estonian for older word senses. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** check whether newcomers (from other counties, Ukraine, abroad) report the capital as easy or hard for friendship, using expatriate forums and newcomer surveys.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** compare student friendships formed in dormitories and student societies with friendships in small towns.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** see whether friendship words and customs differ in Võro and Seto speech, from dictionaries and village memoirs.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** test how many friends across the language line people report, by town and age, without reading low counts as a choice.
- **Ruhnu, Vormsi, Kihnu, Muhu, Hiiumaa and Saaremaa:** ask how friendships survive when school-leavers go to Tallinn, Tartu or Finland, from island papers and alumni groups.
- **Peipsi shore:** look for friendships between Old Believer and Estonian neighbors in village histories.
- **Pärnu, Läänemaa, Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare men's and women's friendship patterns in survey data for rural counties.
- **The Jewish community, Belarusians, Estonian Swedes, Tatars, Ukrainians and Ingrian Finns:** check whether each group's own clubs serve mainly as friendship circles; data for most will be thin.
- **Congregations of every faith, and the non-religious majority:** compare how far church groups supply friends.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and school customs; mixed for cross-language friendship; thin for men's friendship and for smaller groups.
- **Languages needed:** old written Estonian for the corpus; Russian for Dovlatov and Soviet press; Ukrainian for newcomer programs; Võro and Seto for local words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendship across language lines is a charged topic since 2007 and 2022; never assume a pattern for any group. Pen-pal letters and verse albums are private; seek consent before quoting.
- **Held for review:** none.
