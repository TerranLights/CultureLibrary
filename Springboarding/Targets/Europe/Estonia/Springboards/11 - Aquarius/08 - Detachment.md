# Estonia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its regional speech (Võro and Seto among them), Russian in its Estonian and Old Believer forms, Estonian Swedish, Ukrainian, Yiddish; German for pastors' and travelers' descriptions of 1780-1850. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; valued as calm, objectivity or equanimity, condemned as coldness, indifference or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare one-person households and loneliness answers in the capital's districts with rural parishes.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** check whether South Estonian funeral and wedding customs allowed more open feeling than North Estonian ones, using ERM answers.
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** set Seto lament practice, where grief is sung aloud, against Lutheran restraint in neighboring Võro parishes.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** look at turnout and political-interest data by citizenship, so that not voting is not read as indifference where people cannot vote.
- **Kihnu, Ruhnu, Vormsi, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Saaremaa:** check whether island life records privacy and reserve differently from mainland villages.
- **Lake Peipsi villages:** record how Old Believer and Estonian neighbors each say strangers are greeted, spoken to or left alone.
- **Rural west and center (Pärnu, Läänemaa, Järvamaa, Mulgimaa):** check help-seeking by sex where services are far away, so distance from a clinic is not read as reserve.
- **Ukrainian newcomers and older minorities (Tatar, Jewish, Belarusian, Ingrian Finnish, Estonian Swedish):** collect what each says about the distance it met in Estonia, and what its own norms of display are.
- **Each faith body and the unaffiliated majority:** set Lutheran, Orthodox (both churches), Maausk and Taarausk teaching on calm, grief and letting go side by side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household and turnout data; mixed for emotional display; thin for small groups and for men's inner life.
- **Languages needed:** German for Petri; Russian for the Orthodox and Old Believer sources and bystander press; Ukrainian for newcomer forums; Seto for laments.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "cold" and "reserved" are national stereotypes in outsiders' and self-told forms; this sheet treats them only as claims to test, never as traits of any group. Loneliness and mental-health data concern vulnerable people.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation communities in the nation's lands (category only; Buddhism is an open question for the author).
