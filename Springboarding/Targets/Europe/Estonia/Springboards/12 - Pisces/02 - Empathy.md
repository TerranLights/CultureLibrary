# Estonia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Estonian speakers and Russian speakers in Tallinn and Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how each describes understanding the other, across survey rounds; set the 2007 and 2022 rounds beside calmer years.
- **Tartu and South Estonia, Võrumaa, Setomaa:** look at lament and song traditions in which one person voices another's feeling, village by village.
- **The islands (Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu):** test whether small-island life shows up in listening-line or school data at all; sources are likely thin.
- **Läänemaa and the Pärnu coast; Järvamaa; Mulgimaa:** use the school-program rollout lists to see which counties took up social-emotional teaching early.
- **Peipsi Old Believers and Estonian Swedes:** compare how each wants outsiders to grasp their history, from their own museums and memoirs.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Ingrian Finns, Tatars and the Jewish community:** look for each one's accounts of being understood or misread by neighbors; sources are scattered.
- **Faith groups (Lutheran, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk) and the nonreligious:** check whether pastoral or circle training speaks of empathy, and in what words.
- **Men and women, young and old:** split any empathy-scale or helpline data by sex and age before reading it by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for song and lament archives and integration surveys; mixed for school programs and patient surveys; thin for empathy-scale research in Estonian samples and for small-island data.
- **Languages needed:** dialect transcription for orphan songs; Seto for laments; Russian for ETV+ and Ida-Virumaa sources; Ukrainian for refugee testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** perceptions across language communities, especially after the 2007 Bronze Soldier events, are contested; quote each community's own account, never a summary of what one group "feels". Helpline calls are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none.
