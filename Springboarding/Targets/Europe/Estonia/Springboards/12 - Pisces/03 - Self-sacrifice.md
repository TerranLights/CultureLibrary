# Estonia: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; Middle Low German for the chronicles. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Mulgimaa and Järvamaa:** compare Defence League membership and memorial upkeep in the capital and in the farm parishes.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how Sinimäed and the Narva front of 1944 are remembered by Russian-speaking and Estonian-speaking residents.
- **Tartu, Võrumaa and Setomaa:** see where the 1919 battles at Paju and Võnnu sit in local memory, and look at Seto fasting and Seto men's service in each war.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu, Läänemaa:** check volunteer sea-rescue membership and the memory of drowned rescuers.
- **Peipsi shore Old Believers:** compare how strictly Kallaste, Kolkja and Varnja congregations ask fasts to be kept, in their own calendars.
- **Estonian Swedes and Ingrian Finns:** look at men taken by rival armies and at the 1944 flight.
- **The Jewish community and Tatars:** check memorial books for their dead of 1918-1920 and 1941-1945; Tatar sources are thin.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians:** look at how residents since 2022 speak of family members fighting at home; sources are news and community pages.
- **Lutherans, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk:** check what each body's teaching asks its members to give up, and whether it asks differently of women and men.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memorials, war memory and defense surveys; mixed for caregiving; thin for small communities' fasting and for donation by region.
- **Languages needed:** Low German for Russow; dialect transcription for recruit songs; Russian for Chernobyl and May 9 sources; Seto for fasting customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the war memorials of 1941-1945, the Bronze Soldier's move in 2007, the Forest Brothers and the deportations are contested ground: label every account by its teller. Health and donor data are private.
- **Held for review:** none.

1. Defence Ministry site: the latest public-opinion survey on national defence, with the willingness item split by mother tongue.
