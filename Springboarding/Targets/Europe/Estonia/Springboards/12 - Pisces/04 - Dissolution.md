# Estonia: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (proposed definition; the author decides).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Setomaa and Võrumaa:** compare *leelo* choirs with Võro revival work, and see whether either speaks of a language at risk of melting into standard Estonian.
- **Tallinn against Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check mixed-marriage and school-language data, and how each city's Russian-language press frames assimilation.
- **Tartu and Mulgimaa, Järvamaa:** set kolkhoz breakup accounts from farm counties beside town memories of 1991.
- **The islands (Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Kihnu, Ruhnu, Vormsi) and Läänemaa:** look at shrinking villages and the end of the Swedish-speaking coast after 1944; Ruhnu's sources are thin.
- **Pärnu and the west coast:** read resort and mud-bath histories for the language of letting go on holiday.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** see how villages speak of young people leaving and the faith's future.
- **Ingrian Finns and Estonian Swedes:** trace how each community's numbers and Sunday-school or society life thinned or regrew after 1991, from its own societies.
- **The Jewish community, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** look for heritage-language schools and choirs, and what their founders say they were meant to keep from melting away.
- **Lutheran, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk:** check which teaches self-loss in God or nature, and which speaks of parish closures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for song festivals, the Singing Revolution and vital statistics; mixed for mystical and nature writing; thin for trance and psychedelic experience and for small-community language loss.
- **Languages needed:** Seto for *leelo* talk; German for Juniper German records; Russian for school-reform debate; Finnish for Ingrian return files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language policy, the citizenship debate (the undetermined-citizenship "grey passport") and the school reform are contested ground; give each community's account by its own name and none as the truth.
- **Held for review:** none.
