# Estonia: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare apartment-block and detached-house areas for fences, cameras and complaints about neighbors.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check how the river border and the closed-town past shape talk of "our side", in each language.
- **Võrumaa, Setomaa and the Valga border:** set Seto talk of a cut homeland beside Valga's open line; Pechory district is comparison only.
- **Saaremaa, Muhu, Hiiumaa, Vormsi, Ruhnu, Kihnu:** look at stone walls, border-zone permits and island-to-island lines of belonging.
- **Läänemaa, Pärnu coast, Järvamaa, Mulgimaa:** use land-reform files to compare boundary disputes on old manor and farm land.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** check purity rules in their own words, and how outsiders' accounts differ.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** look at where each drew its own community line (marriage, burial ground, school), from each one's records; sources are uneven.
- **Lutheran, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk:** check sacred precincts and who may enter them, such as holy groves (*hiied*) and iconostasis rules.
- **Sauna and touch:** compare mixed and separate sauna customs by region and age, from ERM replies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land maps, border history and privacy reports; mixed for personal space and etiquette; thin for family-boundary norms and small-island data.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and German for old surveys; Russian for Narva and Old Believer sources; Seto for border memory; Latvian for the Valka side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the eastern border, the Tartu treaty line, the citizenship question (the "grey passport") and segregation are contested ground; present each account as its teller's. Violence-survey microdata are restricted.
- **Held for review:** none.
