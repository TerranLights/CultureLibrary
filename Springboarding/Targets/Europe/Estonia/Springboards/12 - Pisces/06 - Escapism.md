## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare nightlife, stag-party tourism and ferry drinking with residents' own habits, as surveys show them.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check overdose and treatment data by home language, and how local Russian-language and Estonian-language papers report them.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** look at home distilling (*hansa*) in memoirs and police reports; Seto feast-day drinking against everyday rules.
- **Tartu and the student world:** check student-society drinking customs and how they changed.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu:** test whether island drinking and gambling data exist at all, and what summer visitors bring.
- **Pärnu and Läänemaa, Haapsalu:** read resort histories for the seaside as an escape from Soviet and present-day work.
- **Järvamaa and Mulgimaa:** compare rural drinking harms with towns, and how farm villages speak of it.
- **Peipsi Old Believers, the Tatars and the Jewish community:** check what each community's own teaching says about drink and gambling, and how members describe practice; few sources.
- **Ukrainians, Belarusians, Ingrian Finns, Estonian Swedes:** look for each one's clubs, choirs and media as escapes, from their own society pages.
- **Lutheran, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk:** set the old Lutheran and Moravian campaigns against drink beside what each body now says about drinking at its own feasts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alcohol and drug statistics and temperance history; mixed for gaming and fantasy reading; thin for smaller communities and for withdrawal into solitude.
- **Languages needed:** German for manor tavern books; Finnish for TV-viewing memory; Russian for Ida-Virumaa harm reduction; dialect transcription for drinking songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug deaths and addiction treatment are private; Ida-Virumaa coverage must not cast one language community as the problem; let each community's own services describe their work.
- **Held for review:** none.
