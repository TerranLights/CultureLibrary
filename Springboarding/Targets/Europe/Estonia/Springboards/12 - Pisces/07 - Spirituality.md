# Estonia: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community; German for Moravian records. **Concept in one line:** a relation to what is taken as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; this sheet takes experience, practice and the sacred outside the church (institutions go to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa against Läänemaa and Hiiumaa:** compare seekers' services in the capital with old Moravian prayer-house villages on the west coast and islands.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** check Orthodox home icons, pilgrimages and Russian-language esoteric offers, and how residents describe their own practice.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** set Seto chapel feasts beside Võro offering-stone memory and present smoke-sauna rites.
- **Saaremaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu:** look at island chapels, sea prayers and lay preachers; check Kihnu's own accounts first.
- **Tartu and South Estonia, Mulgimaa, Järvamaa:** compare sacred-hill disputes and the reach of Maausk and Taarausk circles.
- **Pärnu and the west coast:** see where retreat and wellness businesses cluster.
- **Peipsi Old Believers:** separate congregations by village before reading any general account.
- **Estonian Swedes, Ingrian Finns, the Jewish community, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** gather each community's own account of prayer, revival or renewal; Ingrian Finn Lutheran congregations and Ukrainian Greek Catholic and Orthodox parishes are separate threads.
- **Lutherans, both Orthodox churches, Catholics, Maausk, Taarausk, the nonreligious:** compare where each places the sacred: church, grove, home or self.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Moravian history, sacred-site records and choral heritage; mixed for survey items; thin for present seekers' practice and for smaller faith communities.
- **Languages needed:** German for Moravian diaries; Seto for chapel lore; Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer prayer; Tatar and Arabic for the Islamic congregation; Swedish for coast revivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some sacred sites are kept private by those who tend them; do not publish locations they withhold. The church long under the Moscow Patriarchate is contested ground; quote each body by its own name.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation groups in Tallinn and Tartu, held as a category for the author's open question; none named.
