# Estonia: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Estonia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Estonian and its dialects (North and South Estonian, Mulgi, the island speech of Saaremaa, Hiiumaa and Kihnu), Võro and Seto, the Russian of Estonia, the coastal Swedish of the Estonian Swedes, the Russian of the Peipsi Old Believers, Yiddish for the historical Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tallinn and Harjumaa:** compare business and tech talk of gut decisions with the capital's psychic and coaching trade.
- **Tartu and South Estonia:** set the university's research on reasoning beside farm-calendar and omen records from the surrounding parishes.
- **Võrumaa and Setomaa:** check dream-telling, healers and dowsers in local memory, and who is said to "know" in a village.
- **Ida-Virumaa (Narva, Kohtla-Järve, Sillamäe):** look at Russian-language psychic advertising and miners' accounts of sensing danger underground.
- **The Peipsi shore:** compare Old Believer and Estonian fishermen's ice lore, from each village's own accounts.
- **Saaremaa, Hiiumaa, Muhu, Kihnu, Vormsi, Ruhnu, Läänemaa:** look at sailors' and sealers' weather signs, and which survive among small-boat fishers.
- **Pärnu and the west coast, Järvamaa, Mulgimaa:** compare farm weather sayings by county.
- **Estonian Swedes and Ingrian Finns:** look for sea and weather signs in their own archives (Swedish and Finnish holdings), not in Estonian files alone.
- **The Jewish community, Tatars, Ukrainians, Belarusians:** ask each community body whether dream or omen lore was ever written down; expect little and say so.
- **Lutherans, both Orthodox churches, Maausk, Taarausk:** see which speaks of inner promptings, discernment or signs, and in what words.
- **Women and men, young and old:** check whether surveys and folklore assign "knowing" to one sex or age; record the claim, not adopt it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for omens, weather lore and almanacs; mixed for professional intuition; thin for psychological measures in Estonian samples and for smaller communities.
- **Languages needed:** dialect transcription for weather sayings; Russian for Ida-Virumaa and Peipsi sources; Seto for dream lore; German for early calendars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gut judgments about strangers can be prejudice against one language community; frame any such finding as bias to test. Healers' and psychics' clients are private.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. ERA: *ilmaenne* plus a saint's day, sorted by county.
2. National bibliography: *tähtraamat* holdings by decade, then *unenägude seletaja*.
3. ERR and *Põhjarannik* archives: interviews with Peipsi fishermen explaining how they judge ice, set beside the warnings issued that day.
4. Haigekassa guideline site: search for clinical-judgment wording in primary-care guidelines.
5. Library catalog: "Keres, Paul" annotated games, then Estonian commentary on them.
