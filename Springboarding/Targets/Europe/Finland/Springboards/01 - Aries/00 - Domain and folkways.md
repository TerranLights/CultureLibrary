# Finland: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian for the Russian-speaking community. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it shows itself (personhood, body, appearance), with naming as the folkway and sport as runner-up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region against inland Finland:** check whether naming fashions spread from the capital; name statistics can show it.
- **Finland Proper and Turku against Savo and North Karelia:** check whether the older surname systems differed west and east, because the naming folkway is this domain's core.
- **Ostrobothnia, Tavastia, Kainuu, Lapland:** check regional dress and farm-name use, so no one region stands for all.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check separately; two-language naming and SLS archives give their own record of the self.
- **Sámi (North, Inari, Skolt):** check dress, names and yoik through Sámi institutions, not outsiders' portraits.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check dress and naming with Roma organizations; outsiders' accounts need critical reading.
- **Karelians (evacuees and Orthodox villages) and Ingrian Finns:** check Orthodox name days and Karelian name forms; Finnic Karelians in the pre-war lands may be pointed to as part of the people here.
- **Tatars, Old Believers, Jews, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check naming across scripts and registers; sources are thinner, so start with each community's organization.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for names, folk belief and dress; thin for regional self-construal or "I am..." studies *check*.
- **Scope:** Finland's exact lands are still to be settled (the tracker carries pre-war lands in Russia: Finnic and Karelian people and lands such as Karelia). These sheets are written for the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for the archives; older Finnish spelling for SKVR and Ganander; the three Sámi languages for self-description in their own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as Sámi is a live public question; Sámi objects and remains held by museums; Roma dress and conduct norms belong to the community to explain.
- **Held for review:** the Karelian and Romani name terms in section 1, until a dictionary confirms them.
