# Finland: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Russian. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo, Tavastia and South Ostrobothnia:** collect regional speech-style reputations from dialect humor and dialect research and test them, since such reputations are themselves evidence of how assertion is judged.
- **Uusimaa and Finland Proper:** check workplace and service encounters in the cities, where complaint and negotiation data are densest.
- **Laestadians and the Awakening (*herännäisyys*) in the north and in Savo and Ostrobothnia:** check sermons and hymns on humility against members' own accounts.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check politeness and objection in Swedish-language talk, and Åland's advocacy for its self-government.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check political voice; the Skolt village meeting (*sobbar*) *check* is one forum to look at.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check who speaks for the community and in which settings, through Roma organizations.
- **Karelian evacuees and Ingrian return migrants:** check how claims about lost homes and returning were pressed in public.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check integration-course and school material on "speaking up," and the communities' own reports of it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich on talk and silence as a topic, but much of it repeats one stereotype; regional measures of assertiveness are thin.
- **Languages needed:** spoken Finnish (dialect and colloquial, not only the written standard) for conversation data; Swedish for Finland-Swedish politeness; Sámi for political speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "silent Finn" image is contested; Sámi rights and fishing disputes are current and divisive.
- **Held for review:** the court-record pointer, kept only for village quarrels as speech; any question of rights or procedure goes to Libra or Capricorn.
