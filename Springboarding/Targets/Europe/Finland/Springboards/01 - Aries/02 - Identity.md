# Finland: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia, Savo, Tavastia, Kainuu:** check how strong a regional self-label is against "Finnish," since regional identity may outrank national in some places and not others.
- **Lapland:** check Lapland's regional identity separately from Sámi identity; mixing the two would blur both.
- **Finland-Swedes (Ostrobothnian, capital-region, Turku archipelago):** check whether one Finland-Swedish identity holds or splits by area.
- **Ålanders:** check Åland's identity on its own; it may not follow Finland-Swedish patterns.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check each group's self-description; the Skolt Orthodox heritage and post-war resettlement need their own line.
- **Karelian evacuees, Orthodox Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check generational change; Finnic Karelians in the pre-war lands may be pointed to here as part of the people.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how dress and community membership mark identity, through Roma-led sources.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check long-settled minority histories; each has a small, specific source base.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check hyphenated and two-language identity, using the communities' own media and studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; surveys, biographies and minority histories are plentiful, though Old Believers and some newer groups are thinly covered.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for most material; Sámi for self-description; Russian and Somali for newer communities' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as Sámi (the electoral roll and claims of "Lapp" descent) is disputed; the kin-people idea is bound up with the 1918-22 eastern expeditions and the wartime occupation of East Karelia; handle both with care.
- **Held for review:** pointers to groups claiming "Lapp" descent outside the Sámi Parliament's roll, held until the author decides how to handle the dispute.
