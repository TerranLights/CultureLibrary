*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish (with Åland's Swedish); North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ålanders against mainland Finland-Swedes:** check how collective self-government shapes talk of autonomy; the two may differ.
- **Sámi groups and the herding districts of Lapland:** check where Sámi collective decision-making and mixed Sámi-Finnish herding bodies meet.
- **Ostrobothnian and Savo farm families:** check who decides a child's path at farm handover, since this is where personal and family choice meet.
- **Capital region against Kainuu and rural Lapland:** check leaving-home ages and children's roaming by area.
- **Laestadians:** check family-planning decisions through members' and former members' accounts, keeping both.
- **Orthodox Karelians against Lutheran parishes:** check how far a congregation runs itself under each church's order.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check family decision-making only through Roma-led sources.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check congregational self-governance in small communities.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check how parents and children negotiate choices between two sets of expectations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for youth, work and time use; thinner for small religious communities and family decision-making among newer groups.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for surveys; Åland's own Swedish-language sources; Sámi for herding and self-government in Sámi terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Laestadian family planning; Sámi self-determination, which touches land and herding disputes; anything on its legal side goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the everyman's-rights pointer, kept for roaming as practice; its legal text belongs to Libra.
