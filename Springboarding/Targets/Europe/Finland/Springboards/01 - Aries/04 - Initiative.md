*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia (Finnish- and Swedish-speaking):** check firm-formation and cooperative data rather than the region's reputation for enterprise.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check settlement history and today's start-up rates side by side, since pioneer lore and present data may not match.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check out-migration against local start-ups and work bees in thinly settled areas.
- **Capital region and Turku:** check startup and student-society culture as a separate, recent strand.
- **Ålanders:** check maritime enterprise in Åland's own archives.
- **Sámi:** check *duodji* craft trade and reindeer enterprise through Sámi organizations.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check historical livelihoods and today's Roma entrepreneurs only through Roma-led sources.
- **Tatars and Jews:** check community trade histories (for example Helsinki's old Narinkka market *check*); sources are small and specific.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check immigrant entrepreneurship studies and the communities' own business associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business demography and civic associations; thinner for initiative at home and in schools.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for association records (many movements ran parallel bodies in each language); Russian and Somali for newer founders' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** histories of Roma and Jewish trade can carry old prejudice; read them for the record, not the framing.
- **Held for review:** the citizens' initiative services, kept for who acts first; the rules of initiatives belong to Libra.

5. Read the Nuorisoseurat and Marttaliitto histories for their founding stories.
