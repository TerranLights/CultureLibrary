## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tavastia and Finland Proper:** check medieval founding lore (the Henry legend, Turku) against local memory, to see whether the two tell the start differently.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and the former Karelian lands:** check runo-singing districts' origin songs; Finnic Karelians in the pre-war lands are part of the people here.
- **Karelian evacuees:** check resettlement memory by generation and host parish.
- **Lapland and the Skolt Sámi:** check rebuilding and resettlement as two separate new beginnings, told by those who lived them.
- **North and Inari Sámi:** check where the year and the herding year begin in their own accounts.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language New Year and building rites, which may follow other customs.
- **Orthodox Karelians and Old Believers:** check which calendar sets the start of feasts.
- **Tatars and Jews:** check their own new-year observances in community calendars.
- **Ingrian Finns, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check arrival stories and how long a newcomer is called new.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the folk calendar, origin poetry and post-war resettlement; thinner for personal new starts outside recovery and debt services.
- **Languages needed:** archaic and dialect Finnish for *synnyt*; Swedish for Ostrobothnian and Åland rites; Skolt Sámi for the resettlement accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Henry legend's killing and the conversion story carry rival readings; Skolt Sámi and Karelian loss of homelands is painful memory.
- **Held for review:** New Year tin-casting and other luck-at-the-start customs, kept as custom only; whether they belong here or under a later sign's divination concept is for the author.
