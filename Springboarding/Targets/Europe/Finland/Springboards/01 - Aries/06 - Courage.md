# Finland: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karelian evacuees and the Karelian front parishes:** check how war courage is remembered where the war was fought on home ground.
- **Ostrobothnia and the Kvarken coast:** check sea and ice danger in Finnish- and Swedish-speaking villages separately.
- **Ålanders:** check how courage is framed where military service is not part of the life course (Åland's exemption *check*).
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check memory of the Lapland War and evacuation in Sámi and Lapland voices.
- **Women and men:** check which acts are named brave for each, and whose accounts the war literature holds.
- **Finnish Kale Roma, Jews and Tatars:** check service and recognition in each community's own sources.
- **Ingrian Finns:** check the Ingrian volunteers and their post-war fate as a separate thread.
- **Conscientious objectors and the pacifist tradition:** check moral courage outside the military frame.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check what leaving and arriving asked of people, in their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war courage; thinner for civilian and moral courage, and for minorities' service.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for war literature; Russian for the Continuation War's other side and for Ingrian sources; Romani for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1918 war still divides memory; the wartime alliance with Germany; war-trauma accounts.
- **Held for review:** the *sisu* literature, since popular books sell it as a national trait; keep only studies that treat it as a word to be examined.

## 5. First queries
1. Search WarSampo for Mannerheim Cross citations and list the verbs used for brave acts.
2. Search the SKS catalog for *isoviha* legends by parish.
3. Read Lahti's *sisu* papers for her definitions and questionnaire items.
4. Compare the three *Tuntematon sotilas* films' best-known scenes of fear.
5. Search Finna.fi for Lotta Svärd and Meripelastusseura photographs and memoirs.
