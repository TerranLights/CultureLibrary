## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Savo, Kainuu and North Karelia:** check self-provisioning and cottage use against the cities, to see whether practice and talk of standing alone differ.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check help-seeking and food-aid data where they are densest.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check farm ownership and the crofter question by region, to see how far they differed.
- **Ålanders:** check how island self-government and the independence party shape talk of standing alone.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check self-provisioning and interdependence through Sámi schools and organizations, not outside summaries.
- **Karelian evacuees:** check what losing a self-sufficient farm and rebuilding one meant, by generation.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check help-seeking and mutual support in Roma-led sources.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check remittances and family support across borders, and the communities' own studies.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check community welfare funds and mutual aid inside small congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; parish registers, welfare research and natural-resource surveys are extensive; minority mutual aid is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for the double sense of *itsenäisyys*; old Swedish-language parish entries for pre-1860s registers; Sámi for livelihood terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** welfare stigma; Finlandization remains a charged political label; parish registers name individuals' poverty.
- **Held for review:** the Åland independence party, kept as a living movement to study; its legal standing belongs to Libra.
