# Finland: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish (standard and dialect); Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Ostrobothnia:** check how the knife-fighter era is remembered now, in songs, festivals and local museums, against the records.
- **Savo, Tavastia and North Karelia:** check dialect anger words and sayings region by region.
- **Uusimaa and Turku:** check urban violence and helpline data against rural areas.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language emotion words and whether they map onto Finnish ones.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check anger and its settlement in Sámi accounts, including protest over fishing and land.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check conflict and its settlement only through Roma-led sources and Roma researchers.
- **Karelian evacuees and Ingrian Finns:** check anger over lost homes and deportation in memoir collections.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and Tatars:** check experiences of being the target of anger, and the communities' own responses.
- **Women and men:** check how each is told to show or hide anger, in parenting and health material.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for violence and alcohol statistics; thinner for ordinary anger that never turns violent.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish dialects for emotion vocabulary; Swedish for Finland-Swedish emotion words; Russian for the Russian-speaking community's media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1918 memory; domestic violence; old stereotypes of drinking and knife-fighting, and of Roma feuds, which must not be repeated as findings.
- **Held for review:** the homicide and victim surveys, kept as data on how anger ends; any reading of offenses or courts goes to Libra or Capricorn.
