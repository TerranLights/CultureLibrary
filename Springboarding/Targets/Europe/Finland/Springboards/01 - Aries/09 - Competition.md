## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo:** check church-boat rowing and its village teams as a regional form of contest.
- **South Ostrobothnia and Kainuu:** check pesäpallo and the Tango Market as contests a region claims.
- **Tampere, Turku and Helsinki:** check city rivalries through fan clubs and local media.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check how the Sweden match and Swedish-language clubs are followed, from fans' own accounts.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check herding contests and reindeer racing through Sámi and herders' bodies.
- **Karelian evacuees:** check whether games like *kyykkä* traveled with them and where they live on.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check contests and rivalry only through Roma organizations and Roma researchers.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers and Tatars:** check youth sport and community leagues (football, wrestling, chess) from the communities' own clubs.
- **Laestadians and other revival movements:** check each movement's teaching on striving and on sport in its own publications, beside members' accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and schooling; thin for envy and rivalry in daily life.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for sport media; North Sámi for herding contests; dialect Finnish for folk-game names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** doping and match-fixing cases name individuals; school-ranking lists are disputed.
- **Held for review:** the novelty championships, kept as play and parody; whether they are better studied under Leo's play is for the author.
