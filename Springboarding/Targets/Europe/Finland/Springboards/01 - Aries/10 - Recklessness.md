## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Ostrobothnia:** check whether the old knife-fighter lore still frames young men's daring there, using local media.
- **Lapland and Kainuu:** check snowmobile, hunting and ice risk where help is far away.
- **Lake districts of Savo and Tavastia:** check boating and drowning deaths by lake region and season.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check traffic and nightlife risk where the population is densest.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check sea risk and its lore in the archipelago, in Swedish-language sources.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check how danger in herding and on the ice is spoken of in Sámi accounts.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check risk only through Roma-led health and youth work; avoid outsiders' framings.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and Tatars:** check young people's risk in community and youth-work reports.
- **Young men and young women:** check risk-taking data for each, and which group each safety campaign addresses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deaths, crashes and gambling; thin for how people themselves draw the line between brave and rash.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for accident reports; older printed Finnish for broadside ballads; Swedish for archipelago lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** individual deaths in reports and tabloids; drinking stereotypes must not be repeated as findings.
- **Held for review:** the Nykänen pointer, kept only for how media framed one life; it is a person's private struggle and needs care.
