*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The old southwest (Finland Proper, Turku) and inland Savo:** set manor and merchant households beside inland farms, since what a household held may be recorded differently.
- **Ostrobothnia (both languages):** check tar, shipping and farm wealth in local records before using the region's reputation as evidence.
- **North Karelia and Karelia beyond today's border:** check Orthodox fast foods and pie traditions; Karelians on the Russian side may be counted among this people for food ways.
- **Uusimaa, Tavastia and the capital region:** check whether city spending and rural self-provision (gardens, berries, game) split along an urban line.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check self-provision and seasonal wage work as livelihoods in thinly settled areas.
- **Ålanders:** check island food, dress and goods in Åland sources, not mainland ones.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** ask how each group reckons value in reindeer, fish and *duodji*, and whether the three differ.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check what the dress costs a household and how it is passed down, asking Roma-run bodies first.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check halal, kosher and Old Believer food rules in each community's own calendars.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check money sent abroad and newer food shops as signs of what households keep up.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spending, diet and money history; mixed for the meanings of things; thin for the smallest communities.
- **Scope (said once for all Taurus sheets):** no unit list exists yet, and the row also carries pre-war lands now in Russia, chiefly Karelia with its Finnic and Karelian people; the boundaries wait for the author, so these sheets work from the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** both national languages for spending data and museum labels; old Finnish for SKVR; Sámi and Romani food and dress words with speakers' help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi objects in museums and their return; Roma dress is the community's to explain; food-aid users named in studies.
- **Held for review:** none; property and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
