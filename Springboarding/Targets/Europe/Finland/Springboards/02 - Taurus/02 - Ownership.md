# Finland: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on (custom and meaning here; property law goes to Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finland Proper and Uusimaa:** check manor land, crofts and today's housing companies, where tenancy has deep roots and owning a flat is a share in a company.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check partition, large freehold farms and land kept in one line, comparing Finnish- and Swedish-speaking parishes.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check slash-and-burn rights and the forest-company land purchases around 1900, where much village forest changed hands *check*.
- **Tavastia:** check farm succession and buy-outs of siblings, which drama and memoirs both treat.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check life beside state-managed land, where much of the area is held by Metsähallitus.
- **Ålanders:** check how keeping land in island hands is spoken of; the domicile rules themselves go to Libra.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check *siida* holdings, earmarks and Skolt family areas, each in its own terms.
- **Karelian evacuees:** check the farms left in ceded Karelia and the land given in resettlement, and how former owners still name those farms.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check renting and owning through Roma organizations, with the history of housing barriers in view.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check congregations' own buildings and burial grounds as property held in common.
- **Ingrian Finns, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check the path from renting to owning in each community's own studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tenure, forests and housing; mixed for everyday justifications of owning; thin for Skolt family areas.
- **Languages needed:** early modern Swedish for cadastral and partition records; Finnish and Swedish for housing material; North and Skolt Sámi for holdings described in their own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi land rights in state-held areas are a live dispute; reindeer numbers and marks can be private business information; evacuees' property claims.
- **Held for review:** the Korpijaakko-Labba pointer and anything on Åland domicile, kept for practice; their legal content goes to Libra and Capricorn.
