# Finland: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital region and Turku against Kainuu and North Karelia:** check what people say their worth rests on where jobs are many and where they have left, since unemployment's sting may vary by place.
- **Ostrobothnia, Savo and Tavastia:** check revivalist movements' hymns and sermons on unworthiness against members' life stories, area by area.
- **Lapland:** check boarding-school memory among non-Sámi northerners too, separately from Sámi testimony.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** look for whether a minority language's standing enters talk of personal worth, using Swedish-language memoirs and studies.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check the Truth and Reconciliation hearings by group, since schooling and language loss differed.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check honor, shame and respect for elders through Roma writers and organizations.
- **Karelian evacuees and Ingrian Finns:** check what being newcomers in host parishes, or "returnees," did to a family's standing.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** read each community's own history for slights suffered and for what gave members standing.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check job-market barriers and degree recognition as threats to worth, in the communities' own studies.
- **Men and women; workers and carers:** check whose unpaid care counts, and whether men's accounts tie worth to providing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for work, unemployment and mental health; mixed for worth as people put it in their own words; thin for the smallest minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for Kortteinen, Roos and the novels, Swedish for Finland-Swedish memoirs; Sámi for the commission's testimonies; Romani with community help for honor and shame terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide records; Sámi and war-child testimonies are personal and painful; Roma honor norms are the community's own to explain.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; popular books that sell modesty or low self-esteem as a national trait were left out rather than kept, since they would repeat a stereotype.
