# Finland: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Somali; Russian. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia (Finnish- and Swedish-speaking) and Lapland:** check revivalist and free-church areas against the rest, since stated and lived values may split there by congregation, not region.
- **Capital region and Turku:** check secular, younger and university populations against the national survey average.
- **Savo, Tavastia and North Karelia:** check local value talk in parish and home-district writing rather than in regional jokes.
- **Kainuu:** check how out-migration and an older population shape what is said to matter.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check language-group value differences in ESS data and Swedish-language media separately.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check value teaching in Sámi schools and language nests, each in its own language.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check respect and elder norms through Roma educators.
- **Orthodox Karelians and Old Believers:** check catechesis and fasting as value training outside the Lutheran majority, including Orthodox parishes whose roots lie in Karelia now in Russia.
- **Tatars and Jews:** check congregational schools and their teaching material.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** look at where home teaching and the school's value base meet or clash, in each community's own studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; values surveys, curricula and church records are plentiful, so the risk is over-reading averages rather than lack of data.
- **Languages needed:** old Finnish for Agricola and the catechism; Swedish for Snellman's originals and Moomin first editions; Somali and Sámi for community value teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates on sexuality and religion are live; Roma and Somali families' values must come from their own voices.
- **Held for review:** the prohibition pointer, kept for the value clash; the Prohibition Act itself belongs to Libra.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the value-base chapter of the 2014 core curriculum and list its named values.
2. Download ESS Finnish data and tabulate the Human Values Scale by region and language.
3. Search Digi (National Library newspapers) for *raittius* by decade, 1880-1940.
4. Search the National Archives for one parish's *rippikirja* and note its *kinkerit* marks.
5. Search Finna.fi for Moomin first editions in Swedish and early translations.
