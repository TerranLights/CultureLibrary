## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Savo:** check famine-year death and relief records parish by parish, since the 1860s struck regions unevenly.
- **Capital region:** check housing security, rents and homelessness services, densest in Helsinki.
- **Finland Proper and Tavastia:** check village mutual insurers and their records for how far neighbors carried each other's risk.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Lapland:** check closeness to the eastern border and its weight in surveys of security since 2022.
- **Ålanders:** check how the islands' demilitarized status enters talk of safety, in Åland sources.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** ask Sámi-led sources about herding, fishing and climate risk; for the Skolt Sámi, resettlement after 1944 bears on what a secure home means.
- **Karelian evacuees and Ingrian Finns:** check the loss of a home twice over in family memory, and whether the old Karelian home is still spoken of as the safe place.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** ask Roma-led bodies how families guard against eviction and lost income, since outside records of Roma hardship often carry an outsider's framing.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check whether each congregation kept its own burial or relief fund, and how it was used in the war years.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check remittances, job precarity and the security of residence permits, in the communities' own studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social insurance, defense opinion and housing; mixed for household preparedness; thin for minority welfare funds.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish for early insurance and relief records, Finnish for later ones; Sámi for herding accounts; Somali and Russian for remittance and residence worries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defense and shelter detail is partly classified; famine deaths and homelessness touch named people.
- **Held for review:** benefit and pension rules as written go to Libra; this sheet keeps how they are lived.
