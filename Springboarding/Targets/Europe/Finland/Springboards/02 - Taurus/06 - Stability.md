*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Tavastia:** check the clustering of long-held family farms, which may reflect inheritance practice rather than a regional temperament.
- **Savo with North Karelia:** set the slash-and-burn and Orthodox village pasts beside today's out-migration.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check depopulation, village closures and Päätalo-type memoirs of change.
- **Capital region and Turku:** check rental turnover, short tenures and the newcomer's sense of settling.
- **Finland Proper:** check medieval churches and manors as the oldest continuities in the built record.
- **Ålanders:** check island families' long tenure and seafaring absences, in Åland archives.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check seasonal rounds and the post-war resettlement that broke the Skolt pattern, in Sámi-authored accounts.
- **Karelian evacuees and Ingrian Finns:** check how parish societies and village maps carried a broken continuity forward; the Karelian villages now across the border belong to this people's story here.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check the history of forced and chosen mobility through Roma historians before reading settledness into the data.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check how small congregations kept their calendars and burial grounds unchanged for generations.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check the first years after arrival, frequent moves and the routines that make a place home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration, employment and prices; mixed for routines and attachment to sameness; thin for Skolt and Inari seasonal records outside Sámi scholarship.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for migration tables and local history (Swedish for coastal parishes); Karelian and Finnish dialect for evacuee village books; Russian for newer residents' talk of stability.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma mobility history includes coercion; evacuees' losses are still raw in some families.
- **Held for review:** the farm-certificate pointer, until the awarding body and its rules are confirmed.
