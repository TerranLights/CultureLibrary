## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital region and Uusimaa:** check partner-control survey answers in the cities against the countryside, where social networks are smaller and closer.
- **Savo, Tavastia and Ostrobothnia:** check inherited cottages and farm lots fought over by siblings, using forum posts and mediation reports.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check picking-ground disputes and attitudes to outside pickers in the north.
- **North Karelia and the evacuee parishes:** check how the *Juha* story's Karelian setting is received there; Karelian lands now in Russia are part of this people's frame of reference.
- **Ålanders and Finland-Swedes:** check Swedish-language counseling and survey data on jealousy separately.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** listen for how guarding pastures, fishing waters and cultural symbols is spoken of, in each group's own terms.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how courtship is chaperoned and why, only where Roma writers and organizations have chosen to explain it.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check marriage and family norms in community writing rather than outsiders' summaries.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check reports from migrant women's organizations, since isolation can deepen control.
- **Women and men:** check whose jealousy is excused and whose is named control, in surveys and court-free sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; partner-violence data are rich, but possessiveness short of violence is thinly studied; sharing and territory rest on forums and folklore.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for forums and the national surveys, Swedish for Finland-Swedish counseling; Russian and Somali for migrant women's accounts; Sámi for pasture and symbol disputes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence and stalking cases; Sámi dress disputes are heated; forum posts are personal even when public.
- **Held for review:** the coercive-control and stalking terms, kept as words only; their offenses belong to Libra and Capricorn.
