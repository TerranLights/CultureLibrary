# Finland: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tavastia and Savo:** collect the opposed regional reputations from dialect humor and test them against data, since such reputations are part of how stubbornness is judged.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the memory of the Club War and local revolt in the region's own history writing.
- **Lapland and Kainuu:** check dam, mine and forest disputes where locals and the state dug in.
- **Uusimaa and Turku:** check strikes and urban protest camps against the rural holdout pattern.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** trace the long defense of Swedish-language services and of Åland's self-rule in their own sources.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check which imposed rules each group has refused, from Teno salmon to pasture limits, keeping the three apart.
- **Karelian evacuees and their descendants:** check the split between those who seek Karelia's return and those who have let it go; the lands in question lie in Russia now.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** read Roma writers on keeping dress and customs under pressure to give them up.
- **Laestadians and Old Believers:** check firmness of doctrine and practice through members' and former members' writings.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check keeping a home language and faith against assimilation pressure, in each community's own voice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; disputes, strikes and refusals are well documented; personal stubbornness is mainly found in literature, dialect and the cohort data.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for dam, strike and dialect material; North Sámi for the Teno movement; Russian for sources on the Karelian side of the border.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Karelia's return is a live and divisive issue; total objectors are named people; Sámi fishing rights are contested.
- **Held for review:** the Teno pointer's treaty side and the total objectors' legal status go to Libra and Capricorn; only the refusal itself is kept here.
