*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The west and the east (Savo, North Karelia):** map the *vihta* and *vasta* word line and the sauna customs on each side, since the boundary may mark different bathing habits.
- **Capital region and Tampere:** check public-sauna revival and café culture against the private home sauna.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check folk-dance and fiddle traditions at Kaustinen, and the revivalist restraint nearby, in members' own words.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check the dark season and the midnight sun as felt by residents rather than tourists.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** look at *bastu* customs, coastal food and Åland's harvest pleasures in their own sources.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check taste (*suovas*, fish), sound (yoik, *leu'dd*) and warmth as each group names them.
- **Karelians and Orthodox communities:** check sauna and fast-day food in the Orthodox calendar; the Karelian lands across the border are part of this tradition.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check food, music and celebration as Roma musicians and cooks present them, avoiding outsiders' romanticizing.
- **Laestadians and Old Believers:** check which pleasures are set aside and why, keeping members' and former members' accounts.
- **Tatars, Jews, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check festive foods, bathing and music in each community's own events.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sauna, food and nature; mixed for touch and comfort as felt; thin for some minorities' sensory traditions.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for sauna, coffee and forest research; old Finnish for SKVR and the *Kanteletar*; Sámi for sound and taste terms in their own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** nudity in shared bathing; Laestadian restraint, where members' and critics' accounts both matter; Sámi music has personal yoiks that belong to their owners.
- **Held for review:** sexual pleasure is left to Scorpio; the touch study is kept because it maps touch beyond sex.
