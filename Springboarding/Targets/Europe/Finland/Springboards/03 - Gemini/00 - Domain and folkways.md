*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check slang, multilingual schoolyards and long commutes apart from village patterns.
- **Finland Proper, Turku and its archipelago:** check contact between the two national languages in shared villages and on ferries.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Finnish- and Swedish-speaking parishes separately for neighbor work, since coast and river valleys may differ.
- **Savo, Tavastia, North Karelia, Kainuu:** take dialect recordings and village studies region by region, so no single inland pattern stands for all.
- **Lapland and the Tornio valley:** check distances to school and shops; Meänkieli across the border is comparison only.
- **Ålanders:** check Åland's own speech and schooling records apart from mainland Finland-Swedish ones.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check each language's place at home and in school, and the boarding-school years, through Sámi bodies first.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** ask Roma-run bodies where *romanes* is spoken (home, gatherings, nowhere) and whether that has shifted by generation.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check evacuee speech, Orthodox church language and return migrants' Russian, and villages across the eastern border (scope note below).
- **Deaf signers, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check home-language schooling and clubs, starting from each community's own body.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dialects, grammar, language statistics and travel; mixed for neighbor relations; thin for Old Believers and some newer communities.
- **Scope (this note covers the whole Gemini set):** Finland has no settled unit list, and its row also takes in pre-war lands now inside Russia, Karelia above all, with their Finnic and Karelian people. The borders are the author's to draw; the Gemini sheets cover the core lands.
- **Languages needed:** both national languages and both sign languages; older Finnish spelling for 1700s prints; the Sámi languages, Karelian and Romani with speakers' help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi boarding-school memory goes through the Sámi truth and reconciliation process first.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; statutes on national and minority languages belong to Libra and Capricorn.
