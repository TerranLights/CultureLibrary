# Finland: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the local ideas of what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo, Tavastia, Ostrobothnia, North Karelia:** collect each region's reputation for talk as a folk idea to examine, then set it against recorded conversation.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check multilingual workplaces and schools, where several norms of turn-taking may meet.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check address forms (*nia*, *dua*) and politeness study by study, without assuming Sweden's patterns hold.
- **Turku and Finland Proper; Kainuu and Lapland:** compare city service talk with sparsely settled areas, since setting may change what a pause means.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** study speaking and listening norms in each language's own terms, through Sámi scholars.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check address and respect toward elders through Roma sources; the norms belong to the community to describe.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check speech in Orthodox and evacuee families, and Ingrians' movement between Finnish and Russian.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check children's interpreting, family talk across languages and the communities' own media.
- **Deaf signers:** treat sign-language conversation as its own field of study.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; Finnish conversation analysis and speech-communication research are well developed. Thin for Old Believers and Kale talk.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for recorded data; Swedish for Finland-Swedish pragmatics; Sámi and Romani with speakers' help for norms in those languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "silent" image circulates widely; study it as a claim, never use it as a finding. Recorded conversations carry privacy terms.
- **Held for review:** no pointer withheld. Free-speech rules and the right to an interpreter are statutes, so they wait for Libra (wording) and Capricorn (purpose).
