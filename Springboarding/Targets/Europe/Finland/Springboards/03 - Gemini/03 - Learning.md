## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Finland Proper:** check reading-examination results parish by parish, since the spread of reading may have run unevenly from coast to inland.
- **Savo, Kainuu and North Karelia:** check slash-and-burn and forest households for how children learned work, and the reach of ambulatory schools in scattered settlement.
- **Uusimaa, Tavastia, Turku and Lapland:** set early town schools and manors against rural learning, and long northern school journeys apart.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check SFV's and Åland's own school histories rather than mainland Finnish ones.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check how *árbediehtu* is taught today, and how boarding-school years broke or changed it, through Sámi accounts first.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check learning at home against schooling, with Roma organizations' reports.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check Orthodox religious teaching, Karelian-language classes and Ingrian returnees' language courses; Karelians beyond the border count here as part of the people.
- **Deaf signers:** treat Deaf schooling and sign-language teaching as their own line.
- **Tatars and Jews, then Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and Old Believers:** check Saturday schools, mosque and congregation classes and homework clubs, setting the long-settled communities' schools beside the newer ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literacy history, skills surveys and school records; mixed for learning at home; thin for Old Believer and Tatar teaching.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Finnish for Agricola; old Swedish for parish registers; Sámi for *árbediehtu*; Russian and Somali for weekend schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi boarding-school memories; some Sámi knowledge may be passed only within families; school-performance data on minorities can stigmatize.
- **Held for review:** none withheld here; compulsory-schooling statutes are a Libra and Capricorn matter, and the school system as an institution is Sagittarius's.
