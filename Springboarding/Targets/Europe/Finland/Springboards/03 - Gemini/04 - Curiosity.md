# Finland: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** set science-center and library use here against small municipalities, since access may shape what curiosity looks like.
- **Ostrobothnia, Tavastia and Savo:** check local riddle and charm collections parish by parish; the folk record of asking may vary by area.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelia beyond the border:** check how these lands were themselves objects of collectors' curiosity, and what local people made of the visitors.
- **Lapland:** check amateur naturalists and tourism as two kinds of curiosity about the same land.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language riddle and tale archives at SLS and Åland's own collections.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check how each community answers researchers' questions and what is kept back, starting from Sámi bodies.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check Roma accounts of being studied and stared at, and of what they in turn ask about others.
- **Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check which personal questions each finds welcome or intrusive, through community studies rather than outsiders' reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, science engagement and library use; mixed for everyday nosiness; thin for how smaller communities frame proper asking.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish, Swedish and German for the old folklore scholarship; Russian for the eastern expeditions; Sámi for research-ethics debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi human remains and objects once taken for study; ethnographic photographs of Roma and Sámi taken without consent; true-crime coverage of named victims.
- **Held for review:** nothing held. Where research consent is a legal requirement, that text is for Libra and Capricorn; this sheet keeps to the wish to know.
