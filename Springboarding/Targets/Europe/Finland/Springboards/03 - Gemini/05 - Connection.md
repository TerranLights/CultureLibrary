*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check loneliness and network data for newcomers to the city against those born there.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Lapland:** check how emptying villages and long distances change who keeps in touch with whom.
- **Ostrobothnia and Tavastia:** check associational density and kin-association activity by region, using the register rather than repute.
- **Turku, Finland Proper and Åland:** check island and archipelago ties (ferries, seasonal returns) and Åland's own clubs and networks.
- **Finland-Swedes:** check whether Swedish-language associations act as a separate web of contacts, and for whom.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check *siida*, herding and resettlement ties, and how Sámi living in the south stay linked to the north.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check kin networks that cross into Sweden, with the community's own organizations.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check evacuee and returnee ties, including links to kin in Karelia now in Russia, part of the people for this concept.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers; Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** for the first pair check calls, transfers and visits to kin abroad; for the long-settled three, check congregations as meeting hubs across generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness, associations and survey networks; mixed for favoritism (much is press reporting); thin for Old Believers and for Skolt ties in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian for Karelian kin beyond the border and Russian-speakers; Somali for transfer practices; Sámi for *siida* terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loneliness data on named small places; favoritism claims about living people; Roma and Sámi kin records.
- **Held for review:** no items. Bribery rules and the statute governing associations are law, so Libra and Capricorn take them; here only the ties themselves are scouted.
