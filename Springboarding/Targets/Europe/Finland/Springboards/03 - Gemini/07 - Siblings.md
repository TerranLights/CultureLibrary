# Finland: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check joint households of brothers and how they broke up, set against the farm-succession customs recorded in Ostrobothnia and the southwest.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check large-family histories and emigrant brothers (American Finns as comparison only) through local parish sources.
- **Finland Proper, Tavastia and Uusimaa:** compare manor, croft and town households for older children's care work.
- **Lapland and the North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check sibling and cousin terms and duties in each language, and herding work shared among siblings, through Sámi speakers.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check farm and fishing-household succession among siblings on the coast and islands.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check sibling duties and family gatherings in Roma accounts rather than outsiders' summaries.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check siblings split by evacuation, deportation or return migration, some of them left on the Russian side and counted with the people here.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check older siblings as interpreters and carers, and siblings spread across countries, in each community's own sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family statistics, twins and literature; mixed for joint households; thin for Old Believers and for Kale kin practice in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Finnish for the runos; Sámi and Romani kin terms with speakers; Swedish archives for the war children.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kullervo cycle includes sibling incest; foster-care and war-children records hold named minors; Roma and Sámi family matters belong to those communities to describe.
- **Held for review:** no pointers withheld; how estates must legally be divided among brothers and sisters is a Libra and Capricorn question, while the customs around it stay here.
