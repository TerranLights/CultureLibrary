# Finland: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia, Savo and Tavastia:** check wedding spokesmen and village peacemakers in the folk record, region by region, since the role's name and weight may differ.
- **North Karelia and Karelia beyond the border:** check lamenters and Orthodox clergy as go-betweens; Karelian villages now in Russia are part of the people for this concept.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check professional mediators, HR and housing boards as the urban go-betweens.
- **Turku, Finland Proper and Åland:** check the island settlement's memory and archipelago neighbor quarrels apart from mainland practice.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check who settles disputes over land, fishing waters and herding where offices are far away.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check Sámi ways of settling quarrels within and between *siidas*, and the Skolt village meeting's part.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how elders mediate between families, through Roma organizations only.
- **Ingrian Finns:** check laments and village go-betweens in Ingrian sources, and returnees' use of mediators today.
- **Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Russian-speakers, Old Believers:** check imams, rabbis, priests and elders as go-betweens in family quarrels, in each community's own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, restorative and peace mediation; mixed for folk go-betweens; thin for Old Believers and for Sámi dispute-settling in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Finnish and Ingrian for laments; Russian for Karelian and Ingrian sources; Somali for elders' mediation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma family conflicts and the avoidance custom are the community's own to explain; *noaidi* knowledge is not to be pried into; restorative-mediation cases involve victims.
- **Held for review:** empty; the statutes that set up court-connected and criminal mediation are for Libra and Capricorn, and this sheet scouts only the go-between in practice.
