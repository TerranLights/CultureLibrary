# Finland: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) studied here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo, Tavastia and Ostrobothnia:** check mocking songs, bynames for neighboring parishes and court slander cases by region, since each region's record differs in kind.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** set urban forums, workplaces and tabloids against village talk.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Lapland:** check thinly settled villages, where one store or bus may be the news hub.
- **Revivalist communities (Laestadian, Pietist):** check studies of talk, reputation and exclusion inside these communities, as researchers and members report them.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check SLS and Åland records for island and coastal gossip apart from Finnish-language ones.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check how reputation travels in small Sámi villages, and how rumors about Sámi spread in the majority press.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check rumors told about Roma in the press and the community's own rules on talk and shame, through Roma bodies only.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check evacuee-era and returnee-era rumors and how communities met them, including talk carried between kin on both sides of the eastern border.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check rumors aimed at each community and the talk within it, in their own media and studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court records, folklore, surveys and the online corpus; mixed for everyday village talk; thin for Old Believers and Tatars.
- **Languages needed:** old Swedish for most early court records; old Finnish for songs; Russian and Somali for rumors within and about those communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** court and folklore records name real people and families; witch-trial material touches descendants; rumors about Roma, Sámi and Somali-Finns need careful handling as harmful content.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside. Defamation as an offense, and the old slander statutes behind the court cases above, are for Libra and Capricorn; the talk itself is scouted here.
