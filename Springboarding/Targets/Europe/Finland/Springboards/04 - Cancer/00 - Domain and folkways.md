# Finland: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain, home and family: what home is and who is family, through household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, the home's walls and meals, and the building, family and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Finland Proper against Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check whether two-story farmhouses, closed farmyards and joint households follow a west-east line, from household counts.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check flats and small households against inherited farms elsewhere.
- **Lapland and the North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare the seasonal dwellings and household units each Sámi people kept, with Sámi institutions leading.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check farm and fishing-village household forms and the *gård* as a unit, in SLS and Åland sources.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how kin gatherings, mourning and housing rules shape where families live, from Roma-authored sources.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Karelians in Russia:** check homes lost, rebuilt and remembered, and household forms on both sides of the border.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check how each community's own records describe household headship, kin obligations and hosting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registers, house ethnology and folk poetry; mixed for fatherhood before the 1980s; thin for Old Believer and Tatar household life in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; the three Sámi languages for dwelling and kin words; Karelian and Russian for Russian Karelia; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** household registers hold named people; family matters of the Sámi and the Roma are theirs to describe. Finland's exact lands are still to be settled; the header carries Finnic and Karelian lands now in Russia, pointed to here only as part of the people, with no borders drawn.
- **Held for review:** none; how statute defines a household (marriage, tenancy) waits for Libra and Capricorn.
