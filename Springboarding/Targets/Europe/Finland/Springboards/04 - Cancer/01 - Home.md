# Finland: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check flats in housing companies against owner-built houses, since tenure and home feeling may part ways there.
- **Ostrobothnia and Finland Proper:** check the family farmhouse passed down the line against town homes, through local histories.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check resettled evacuees' farms and the meaning of a home built on given land.
- **Lapland:** check homes rebuilt after 1945 and seasonal moves, apart from the Sámi below.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check home places, seasonal dwellings and the home given after Petsamo, each in its own language.
- **Åland and coastal Finland-Swedes:** check island crofts, fishing homes and summer moves through SLS and Åland archives.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how being refused rentals and moving between towns bears on home, in Roma testimony.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check homes lost, revisited and rebuilt, with Karelians in Russia as part of the same people.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check what each community's own media say about home here and home elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** housing figures, policy history and evacuee lists should be plentiful; what home feels like will need memoirs and interviews; the smaller communities' homes have little in print, so their associations are the way in.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Karelian and Russian for *Oma Mua* and Russian Karelia; Sámi languages for home places; Somali for newcomers' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi place knowledge may be held by families; eviction and fire records concern named households.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; *kotirauha* as written law and tenancy rules are Libra's and Capricorn's.

## 5. First queries
1. Look up *koti* and *kota* in *Suomen sanojen alkuperä*.
2. Pull Statistics Finland's overcrowding table by region and by language.
3. Search the SKS archive catalog for *tulen siirto* and *tupaantuliaiset*.
4. Search Finna for *Lapin jälleenrakennus tyyppitalo* photographs and plans.
5. Search *Oikotie* listings for the frequency of *kodikas* by region.
