# Finland: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Tatar. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia:** check how Conservative Laestadian families describe large families and kin networks in their own papers, apart from neighboring households.
- **Savo, Kainuu and North Karelia:** check how far kin help reaches in thinly settled areas, against the cities.
- **Uusimaa, Turku and Tampere:** check blended, rainbow and single-parent families and how kin terms stretch to fit them.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** check *sohka*-type kin groups, herding families and naming, in Sámi sources first.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check farm and archipelago kin ties and marriage across the language line.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check kin duty, respect between generations and family gatherings, from Roma-run media.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check families split across the border and Orthodox memorial customs.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check what each owes a spouse's parents and the lineage when they live in another country, from the communities' own media.
- **Jews, Tatars and Old Believers:** check congregation and family archives for how small, long-settled communities have kept family memory over generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** association and register sources are many; historical kin words are well documented; Old Believer and Tatar family life has little written on it, so start with the congregations themselves.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old literary Finnish; North Sámi for *sohka*-type terms; Somali, Russian and Tatar for those communities' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi descent records touch the dispute over who is Sámi; adoption and foster files are confidential; Roma family matters are theirs to tell.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; what adoption, marriage and reunification statutes say is Libra's, and their purpose Capricorn's.
