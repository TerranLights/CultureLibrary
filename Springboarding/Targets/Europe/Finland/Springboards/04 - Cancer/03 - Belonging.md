# Finland: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karelian evacuees and their children:** check memoirs and parish histories for how hosts and evacuees spoke of each other over two generations.
- **Ostrobothnia and Åland:** check how long an incomer stays an incomer in close-knit coastal parishes and islands.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether city life eases newcomers' belonging, using the immigrant surveys by municipality.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Lapland:** check how people who moved away still claim the region, and how those who return are received.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check membership, kin recognition and urban Sámi life, each people separately.
- **Finland-Swedes:** check bilingual families and the pull toward Sweden.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check inner belonging and how the majority's suspicion is met, in Roma-authored work.
- **Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers:** check reception in the 1990s against after 2022.
- **Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews:** check how a long-settled minority (Tatars, Jews) and a recent one (Somalis) describe being both local and other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** surveys of the foreign-born and the evacuee literature are strong; felt belonging among Finland-born minorities is patchier; Old Believers barely appear.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; North Sámi for the membership debate; Russian for the Russian-language press; Somali for first-person accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sámi definition dispute is live and painful; discrimination reports name real experiences; *dáčča* and similar words carry weight.
- **Held for review:** all pointers kept; the wording of Åland's domicile rules and of the Sámi roll is for the Libra sheet.
