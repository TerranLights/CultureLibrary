# Finland: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the large cities:** check day-care use and talk of intensive parenting against home-care allowance take-up in other regions.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check how large religious families share the raising of younger children, from their own writings.
- **Savo, Kainuu and North Karelia:** check grandparents' part in raising children where young families have moved away.
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check herding work taught to children and Sámi-language early years, each Sámi people on its own.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language child-health advice and island childhoods.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale parents describe teaching respect and dress customs to children; sources are few, so ask Roma organizations.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers:** check *vospitanie*-style expectations against Finnish clinic advice in bilingual homes.
- **Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check religious teaching of children at home and in congregation schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** clinic, benefit and cohort data are deep; folk infant care is well archived; Roma and Old Believer child-rearing has very little written on it.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Finnish for Lönnrot and the charms; Norwegian for Balto; Karelian and Russian for the language nests; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-welfare and clinic records are confidential; the Sámi boarding-school years belong to later, careful research under Abandonment and Shelter.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the 1984 ban on physical punishment, as statute, goes to Libra.
