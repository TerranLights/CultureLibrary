## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Karelia, Savo and Kainuu:** check how eastern border districts speak of guarding home, and whether western districts speak of it differently.
- **Uusimaa and Tampere:** check fear of crime, locks and cameras in cities against unlocked rural doors, from victim and household surveys.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Civil Guard memory and local defense traditions through parish and museum sources, without assuming one view.
- **Lapland, North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check protection of land, water and herds in each people's own statements.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check fishing-village protective customs and how demilitarized Åland talks of its own safety.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale families protect their own and how far they feel the police and welfare services protect them.
- **Karelians and Orthodox Finns:** check icon-corner blessings and wartime evacuation as protection of families.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Jews, Tatars:** check how safe each community feels, and which bodies its members turn to for protection.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** inquiry reports, defense and welfare sources are abundant; protective folk practice is well archived in Finnish, less so for Karelian and Sámi homes; minority safety data are scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian and Karelian for Orthodox sources; Sámi languages for land statements; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1918 war still divides memory; child-abuse and school-shooting material involves victims; some Sámi protective knowledge may be restricted to families.
- **Held for review:** no pointers set aside; gun-permit rules and the child-welfare act, as texts, belong with Libra and their aims with Capricorn.
