# Finland: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Helsinki and Uusimaa:** check hostels, supported housing and rough sleeping against smaller towns.
- **Lapland and Kainuu:** check wilderness huts and long distances as a shelter question for travelers, workers and herders.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check herding-route shelters and wartime evacuations, each people separately, in Sámi words.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check how island and coastal parishes lodged fishermen, pilots and castaways.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check lodging at farms and later refusals of housing, from Roma memory and writing.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Karelians in Russia:** check refugee and evacuee shelter given and received across the border.
- **Asylum seekers, Ukrainians, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers:** check reception centers and private hosting since 2015 and 2022.
- **Jews and Tatars:** check whether congregations kept hostels or aid for travelers and refugees, from community archives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** homelessness counts and reception statistics are detailed; old lodging customs are scattered across local histories; Kale and Sámi shelter history needs community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Latin and Swedish for Schefferus; Russian and Ukrainian for recent refugees; Somali; Sámi for herding routes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter locations for those fleeing violence are kept secret; asylum cases involve identifiable people; archival searches about the Kale may need old, now offensive terms, used only to find records.
- **Held for review:** nothing is held back; the asylum and housing acts themselves are left to the Libra sheets.
