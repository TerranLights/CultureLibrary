# Finland: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Tagalog. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Lapland:** check distances to care and whether care poverty is reported more where the young have moved away.
- **Uusimaa and the large cities:** check paid home care, private care homes and foreign-born care workers against family care elsewhere.
- **Ostrobothnia and Åland:** check Swedish-language care and island care homes, and the carers who stay.
- **Sámi elders:** check whether North, Inari and Skolt speakers can be nursed in their own tongue, and what is lost when they cannot.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how kin gather at hospital bedsides and how staff meet this, in Roma-reviewed sources.
- **Karelians and the Orthodox:** check church care and wartime-evacuee elders.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Filipino workers:** check care given and received across borders, and work in Finnish care.
- **Men who care:** check how men caring for a wife or parent describe it, since care talk may assume women.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers:** check congregation care for the old, from their own bodies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** statistics, union and regulator sources are rich; folk healing is well archived; minority-language care has thin, mostly report-based coverage.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Sámi languages; Russian, Somali and Tagalog for carers and cared-for of those backgrounds.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care-home cases involve vulnerable named people; patient records are confidential; folk-healing knowledge may be held within families.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; staffing ratios written into the elder-care act, and the family-care act, are matters for Libra.
