# Finland: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia:** check emigrant families and those left behind, and how parishes treated deserted wives.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and eastern Lapland:** check emptied villages and elders left alone after the young moved south.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check solitary deaths and unclaimed burials in cities against the countryside.
- **Sámi families:** check how dormitory schooling parted North, Inari and Skolt children from parents, as each community records it.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check sailors' and emigrants' families and Seili's patients from the Turku archipelago.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check children taken into care and how Kale families tell it.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Karelians in Russia:** check the homes and graves left in 1940 and 1944, and relatives who stayed on the Soviet side.
- **Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check family members left in the country of origin and the guilt or duty migrants describe.
- **Jews, Tatars, Old Believers:** check how small congregations cared for, or lost, members with no kin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** archival and inquiry sources are strong; lament and legend material is rich; Roma and Sámi child-removal history needs community-led work, not desk research.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Karelian and old Finnish for laments; Sámi languages; Russian and Somali for migrant families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child removal, the Sámi boarding schools, Roma children in care and the war children all involve living survivors and their families; treat these as topics for later, careful research, with consent.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the crime of leaving a person helpless and child-support statutes are Libra's.
