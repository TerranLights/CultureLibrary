# Finland: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Tatar. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture (the mother of the nation, the Virgin Mother, Mother Earth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia:** check how mothers of large families in revivalist communities describe motherhood, in their own publications.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check later first births and mothers' paid work against Kainuu, Savo and Lapland.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check grandmothers' and mothers' part in passing on language and handicraft, each people apart.
- **Coastal Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check how island midwives and Swedish-language clinics appear in mothers' own accounts.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale mothers and grandmothers describe their standing, through Roma women's own writing.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Karelians in Russia:** check Orthodox mother devotion and lament words for mothers.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars:** check mothers' expectations brought from elsewhere against clinic advice.
- **Jews and Old Believers:** check religious teaching on mothers in each congregation's own texts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** clinic, statistics and association sources are plentiful; folk birth custom is well archived; Roma and Tatar motherhood is hardly written up, so expect to start with the communities.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish throughout; runo language for the Earth Mother charms; Russian and Church Slavonic for icon feasts; North Sámi for cradle-makers' terms; Somali and Tatar for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** birth and infertility records are private; Sámi and Roma women's knowledge is theirs to share; stories of mothers separated from children (war, care) belong to careful later research.
- **Held for review:** no pointer held back; what the maternity-leave and benefit acts say is a Libra matter.
