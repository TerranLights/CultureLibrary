## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether city festivals and paid hobbies crowd out the village dance and the yard game.
- **South Ostrobothnia, Seinäjoki and the pavilion belt:** check dance-floor courting against revivalist suspicion of dancing in the same parishes.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check feast days, village games and Orthodox feasts against the Lutheran west.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare festival calendars and contests, North, Inari and Skolt each on its own terms.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check summer festivals, sailing and associations against Finnish-speaking neighbors.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check music, weddings and summer gatherings from Kale-authored sources.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, on both sides of the border:** check swings and feast days remembered by evacuees against what survives in Russian Karelia.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check each community's own festival year, wedding customs and children's parties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for leisure surveys and sport statistics; mixed for courtship before the 1950s; thin for Old Believer and Tatar festive life in Finnish print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; the Sámi languages for festival names; Karelian for village feasts; Kale Romani only as Kale organizers allow; Russian, Somali and Tatar for community calendars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling harm touches real families; some Sámi and Kale celebrations are private. Finland's exact lands are still to be settled; the Finnic and Karelian people of the lands now in Russia are pointed to here as part of the people, with no borders drawn, and no unit list is given.
- **Held for review:** none; how the coming gambling license system is written is for the Libra sheet.
