*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Viena Karelia, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how rune-singing villages varied songs, and how outside artists later reworked them.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check the art schools, studios and game firms that gather there, against craft centers elsewhere.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check folk fiddling, woodworking and furniture painting, parish by parish.
- **Finland Proper, Turku and Åland:** check maritime crafts and painters' colonies on the coast and islands.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare *duodji* lines, teaching and ownership rules, each people speaking for itself.
- **Finland-Swedes:** check Swedish-language theaters, publishers and art schools as their own creative world.
- **Karelian evacuees and Karelians in Russia:** check embroidery, weaving and song kept or lost after the moves.
- **Finnish Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check which crafts, songs and writing each community counts as its own creative work, and who teaches them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for art history, design and arts policy; mixed for vernacular making and improvising; thin for Kale, Tatar, Old Believer and Somali creative work in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt for craft terms; Karelian for embroidery words; Russian for Karelian-side archives; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi designs and some *duodji* patterns belong to families and are not for outside copying; who owns the *Kalevala*'s sources is debated.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; copyright and the protection of designs are Libra's to cover.
