# Finland: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check street art, clubs and Pride life against smaller towns.
- **Savo:** check talk and humor as a route of expression, from dialect writing and comedy.
- **Ostrobothnia and Lapland's Laestadian parishes:** check how members themselves write about the limits and freedoms of dress, music and media.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Viena Karelia:** check rune-singing and lament as personal voice, then and now.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare *luohti*, *livđe* and *leuʹdd* and who may sing whom.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check choirs, theater and press as Swedish-language voices.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check song, gospel and dress as ways of speaking, in Kale-authored work.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Somali-Finns:** check where each group speaks freely in its own language and where it keeps quiet in public.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for theater, music and costume archives; mixed for everyday emotional display; thin for Old Believer and Tatar expression in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi to tell the three song forms apart; Karelian for rune and song texts; Somali and Russian for youth and community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** a personal joik belongs to its subject and the singer's people; LGBTI and religious leavers' accounts name real lives.
- **Held for review:** no pointer withheld; what statute lets people say or show is left to Libra.
