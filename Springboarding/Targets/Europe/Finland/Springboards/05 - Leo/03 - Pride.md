# Finland: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Lapland:** check revivalist teaching on pride as sin against hometown boasting in the same parishes.
- **Tampere, Turku and Oulu:** check civic pride through clubs, industry and universities, city by city.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether the capital's pride is read elsewhere as arrogance, from press and jokes.
- **Savo and Kainuu:** check humor that deflates boasting, from dialect comedy and local writing.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare how each people marks its own day, flag and language, without folding them into one.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check language pride and Åland's self-government day as separate prides.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check Roma National Day and Kale-led cultural work as statements of worth.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Karelians in Russia:** check pride in a lost or divided homeland.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check how each group's own institutions speak of its achievements in Finland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national symbols, sport and the press; mixed for everyday bragging norms; thin for Old Believer pride and Somali-Finnish public statements in Finnish.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Latin for Porthan; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt for National Day material; Romani, for Roma National Day texts; Russian and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national pride after 1918 and in wartime is politically charged; Pride events draw counter-protest; envy claims slip easily into stereotype.
- **Held for review:** none; the law on the flag and on flag days waits for Libra.
