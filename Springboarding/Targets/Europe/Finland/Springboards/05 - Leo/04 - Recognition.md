# Finland: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check old academic and church rites of recognition against later ones.
- **Ostrobothnia and Tavastia:** check how farms, parishes and workplaces mark long service, from local papers.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where honors, prizes and juries concentrate, and who outside the capital feels passed over.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare how Sámi institutions honor speakers, makers and elders with state honors, North, Inari and Skolt each separately.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language prizes and the islands' own honors.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check honors within the community against public prizes.
- **Ingrian Finns, Karelians and Russian-speakers:** check returnees' and evacuees' sense of being acknowledged, then and now.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Somali-Finns:** check whether community leaders and veterans appear in honors lists and local histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors, prizes and university ritual; mixed for workplace thanks; thin for Old Believer and Kale-internal recognition.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Latin for older conferment records; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt for language-prize material; Russian for Ingrian returnees; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of war service and of 1918 is divided by side; returnee debates touch identity claims; Sámi recognition overlaps live disputes over membership.
- **Held for review:** none needed; the statutes behind decorations, honorary titles and Sámi status are for Libra.
