# Finland: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; Finnish Sign Language and Finland-Swedish Sign Language; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how street, metro and social-media visibility of minorities differs from small towns.
- **Kainuu, Lapland and North Karelia:** check villages where everyone is seen by everyone, from local papers and memoirs.
- **Lapland's tourist centers and the Sámi home area:** check how North, Inari and Skolt Sámi each meet the tourist gaze.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check visibility of Swedish in signs, media and public service, and the islands' own image.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check dress, public spaces and media from Kale-authored sources.
- **Deaf signers, Finnish and Finland-Swedish:** check visibility of each sign language in schools, TV and services.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers:** check when origin is shown or hidden, before and after 2022.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Somali-Finns:** check how visible each community's places of worship and dress are in city life, and what each group says it wants shown.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media, photography and broadcast archives; mixed for felt invisibility outside surveys; thin for Old Believers and Finland-Swedish Sign Language users in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; both national sign languages for Deaf-led material; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt for Sámi debates; Russian and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** photographs and reality television expose named people; Sámi dress has rules of its own; harassment accounts are personal.
- **Held for review:** none; data-protection and camera rules go to the Libra sheet.
