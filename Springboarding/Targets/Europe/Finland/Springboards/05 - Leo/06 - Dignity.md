# Finland: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tavastia and Uusimaa:** check how 1918's prison camps (Hämeenlinna, Suomenlinna, Tammisaari) are remembered locally, by each side.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check memoirs of crofters, hired hands and evacuees for words of respect and contempt.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare remains, boarding-school memories and care in old age, each people speaking for itself.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language care and the right to be served in one's own language at the end of life.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale writers describe treatment by police, shops, schools and care homes.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and the Karelian lands in Russia:** check memory of deportation, terror and return.
- **Disabled and Deaf people:** check self-advocacy against institutional records.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check burial customs and the care of the old and dying in each community, from community sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1918 victims, veterans and care-home reports; mixed for everyday courtesy; thin for Old Believers and Tatar elder care in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt for repatriation statements; Russian for Sandarmokh lists; Romani for Kale memoir; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human remains and the dead are handled under Sámi and community authority; Sandarmokh's history is politically disputed in Russia; abuse in care names real victims.
- **Held for review:** none; constitutional dignity clauses and end-of-life law go to the Libra and Capricorn sheets.
