# Finland: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and Finland Proper:** check old church punishments and village mocking customs parish by parish.
- **Tavastia and Uusimaa:** check how memory of 1918 shapes who was lowered and who did the lowering.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how incomers, evacuees and Somali arrivals were received in small towns.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare school memories and slurs separately for North, Inari and Skolt, since each had different schools.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check language jibes in both directions, and the islands' place in mainland jokes.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check exclusion from public places, from Kale sources.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers:** check the *ryssä* slur across eighty years, from evacuees to Russian-speakers after 2022.
- **Conscripts and students:** check hazing in units, nations and guilds, across decades.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Somali-Finns:** check slurs and public slights each group reports, and its own ways of answering them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bullying research, the press and 1918 memoirs; mixed for hazing (closed groups); thin for Old Believers and Tatars.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; older Swedish for church records; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi for school memories; Russian; Romani where Kale writers use it; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs must be quoted only as search terms; school and hazing accounts name living people; Sámi school memories belong to the Sámi process of truth-telling.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; defamation and hate-speech provisions are left to Libra.
