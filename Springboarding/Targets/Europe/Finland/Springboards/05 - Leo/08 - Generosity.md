## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia:** check parish poor-boxes, revivalist giving and local foundations in one region.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check large foundations and corporate donors against small-town collections.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check work bees and neighborly help where farms were far apart.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare reindeer gifts, sharing of meat and help between herding families, without assuming the three share one custom.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language foundations, associations and island collections.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale writers describe giving within kin and at gatherings.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check how evacuees were helped and how they later gave back.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check the charities, alms duties and help sent abroad that each congregation or association runs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collections, foundations and household spending; mixed for informal help between kin; thin for Old Believers and Kale-internal giving.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; the herding vocabularies of North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Somali, Tatar and Russian for congregation records; Kale Romani for kin-giving words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** donor and recipient records can name the poor; Sámi herding gifts are family matters.
- **Held for review:** none; the fundraising-permit system belongs on the Libra sheet.
