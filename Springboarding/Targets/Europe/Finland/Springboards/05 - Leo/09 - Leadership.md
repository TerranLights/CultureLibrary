*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Ostrobothnia:** check the remembered peasant leader against revivalist preachers and local politicians in the same parishes.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check corporate and political leadership models against small-town ones.
- **Tavastia and Finland Proper:** check manor, ironworks and parish leadership in older records.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare herding-group heads, the Skolt *sobbar* and elected bodies, keeping the three peoples' institutions apart.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language associations and the islands' politics as separate schools of leadership.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check elders and organization leaders, from Kale accounts.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check village and parish leaders of the lost lands, and evacuee associations.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check how each community chooses imams, rabbis, elders or chairs, and who speaks for it to the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for presidents, military schools and business; mixed for informal leaders in villages and families; thin for Old Believer and Tatar community leadership in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; older Swedish for court records; North Sámi and Skolt Sámi; Meänkieli for the cross-border comparison; Kale Romani for elders' titles; Russian and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mannerheim and Kekkonen divide opinion; Sámi governance is tied to live disputes; Kale family leadership is private.
- **Held for review:** none; the formal right to command is for the Capricorn sheet.
