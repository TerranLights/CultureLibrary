# Finland: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Ostrobothnia:** check the knife-fighting past against today's local pride and its retelling.
- **Finland Proper and Tavastia:** check parish records on brides, insults and settlements in old farming districts.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check horse trading, fairs and the given word, including Kale dealers' own memory.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check workplace and professional honor against older village codes.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** compare how standing is earned among herders and fishers, people by people.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check seafaring and fishing communities' codes of trust.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check respect, family peace and elders' role from Kale sources.
- **1918's two sides:** check honor given to the White and Red dead.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check the words each group uses for honor and good name, and how outsiders' use of "honor" lands with them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and work studies; mixed for older village honor; thin for Sámi, Old Believer and Kale-internal codes in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; the Swedish of 1600s-1700s court clerks; the three Sámi languages for words of standing; Kale Romani for respect terms; Somali and Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "honor-related violence" can stigmatize whole groups; Kale family conflicts are private and painful; 1918 graves are still contested.
- **Held for review:** none; the criminal-code side of honor belongs with Libra, its legal culture with Capricorn.
