# Finland: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Tatar. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how it is named, felt, shown, sought and shared.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lapland and Ostrobothnia:** check revival joy against secular festivals in the same towns.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check whether rock festivals and Orthodox parish days mark gladness differently from western summer fairs.
- **Finland Proper, Satakunta and Tampere:** check how industrial towns built festival joy.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check student and civic celebrations and who joins them.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare gatherings, joik and church feasts, with Sámi organizers as the guides.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check midsummer, song festivals and sailing as each community's own occasions of gladness.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check summer meetings, weddings and music, from Kale sources.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check village dances and feast days remembered and revived.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check which feasts each community holds in public halls and parks and which stay at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and happiness surveys; mixed for everyday displays of joy; thin for Old Believer and Kale festival life in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old literary Finnish for Hemminki; North Sámi with Inari and Skolt; Karelian; Tatar, Somali and Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the happiness ranking is often read as a national trait and must be handled as a measure only; religious joy is described best by members; Sámi views on Lapland tourism vary.
- **Held for review:** none; whether hymns may be sung at school is a legal question for Libra.
