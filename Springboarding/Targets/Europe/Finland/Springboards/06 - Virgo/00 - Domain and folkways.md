*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether office and service jobs give a different daily round from mill towns and farms.
- **Finland Proper, Turku and the archipelago:** check servant and hiring records, the deepest written series.
- **Ostrobothnia, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking:** check how each language community describes its working year.
- **Savo, Tavastia, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check slash-and-burn and forest-work rounds against field farming.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** check the herding, fishing and gathering year, each people from its own sources.
- **Åland and Finnish Kale Roma:** check the island farm-and-sea round, and Kale trades from Kale-led sources.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in Karelia now in Russia:** check the eastern farm year and Orthodox rest days, evacuees against those who stayed.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns:** check each community's own trades, rest days and house rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and for folk material on farm work; the trades and rest days of Old Believers and Tatars are best sought in the communities' own histories, as Finnish surveys hardly separate them.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for the surveys; old Swedish for hiring records; the Sámi languages for herding-year terms; Russian, Somali and Tatar for the communities' own work stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Kale and Sámi livelihoods were long described by outsiders; prefer their own voices. Finland's exact lands are still to be settled; the Finnic and Karelian people of lands now in Russia are pointed to as part of the nation where a concept needs it, with no borders drawn and no unit list given.
- **Held for review:** none; servants' statutes and working-hours rules go to Libra and Capricorn, the clock to Capricorn.
