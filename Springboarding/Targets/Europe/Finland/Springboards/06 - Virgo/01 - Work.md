# Finland: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali; Tatar. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how service, office and gig work are spoken of against older industrial pride.
- **Tavastia and Pirkanmaa's mill towns (Tampere, Valkeakoski, Mänttä):** check the factory-town record of shift work and closures.
- **Ostrobothnia, both language communities:** check the claim of an "entrepreneurial" region as a claim, against farm and workshop records.
- **Kainuu, North Karelia and Lapland:** check seasonal forest work and out-migration for jobs.
- **Sámi (North, Inari, Skolt):** check livelihood as each people defines it, not wage work alone.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check fishing, shipping and archipelago work in Swedish-language sources.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check old trades, present job barriers, and Kale-led training projects.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those across today's border:** check pack trade, collective-farm memories and returnees' work histories.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews:** check trade, shopkeeping and present labor-market entry for each separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and labor history; mixed for unpaid and informal work; Tatar and Jewish trading history sits mostly in community publications.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for the archives and surveys; Karelian for pack-trade memory; North Sámi for livelihood terms; Russian and Somali for newcomers' own work accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "lazy" labels have been aimed at Kale, Sámi and immigrants; treat each as a claim to examine.
- **Held for review:** the vagrancy pointer, kept for idleness as a social label; the vagrancy statutes themselves, and the activation model's rules, go to Libra and Capricorn.
