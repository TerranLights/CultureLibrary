*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check customer-service talk in big chains and hotels against small-town shops where clerk and customer know each other.
- **Ostrobothnia and Finland Proper:** check volunteer fire brigades, village associations and church service in both language communities.
- **Åland:** check how service to the community is framed where islanders are exempt from conscription (*check*).
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check old hired-hand and maid records against the eastern joint-household tradition.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check catechists, herding-cooperative office and Sámi Parliament office as forms of serving one's people, each people on its own terms.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check Kale-run services and mediators, and Kale experience as customers; written sources are scarce, so start with Kale organizations.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in lands now in Russia:** check service in the Orthodox parish and in the Soviet-era workplace, from returnees' memoirs.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check entry into care and service jobs, and service to their own communities through associations.
- **Conscripts, women volunteers and civilian servants:** check how each describes the same duty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and church records and for union surveys; mixed for domestic service, mostly in memoirs; thin for Kale and Tatar service roles in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (parallel fire brigades and parishes); old Swedish for muster rolls; North Sámi for catechists' own words; Russian and Somali for newcomers in care work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** service-work harassment accounts involve identifiable people; maids' memoirs may name employers' families.
- **Held for review:** the conscription pointers, kept for the experience of serving; the duty to serve and the right to refuse arms, as written, belong to Libra and Capricorn.
