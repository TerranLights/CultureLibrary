## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check the weight of universities, the tech sector and ministries against towns where practical sense is the measure.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the old academy town (Royal Academy of Turku, 1640) and Åbo Akademi as a Swedish-language center of learning.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the Chydenius tradition and its present use in regional self-image as a claim to examine.
- **Tampere and Oulu:** check engineering and technology towns against rural neighbors.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check how herding, snow and fishing knowledge is observed and tested in Sámi terms, apart from school science.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language schooling outcomes and scientific bodies such as the Finnish Society of Sciences and Letters (*Societas Scientiarum Fennica*) *check*.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check schooling access before any talk of skills; data are thin and must come with Kale organizations.
- **Karelians, Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers:** check the different schooling traditions behind each group's ways of reasoning.
- **Somali-Finns and other newer communities:** check religious and secular learning paths separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics, audits and school assessments; mixed for everyday reasoning styles; thin for Sámi and Kale perspectives on analysis written in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish for *Tabellverket* and Chydenius; North Sámi for herding knowledge; Russian for the schooling histories of Russian-speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** registers hold personal data under strict rules; outcome gaps between groups must be read with their causes, not as group traits.
- **Held for review:** none; the rules governing registers and audits are Libra's.
