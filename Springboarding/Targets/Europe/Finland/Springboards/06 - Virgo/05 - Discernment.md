# Finland: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check scam exposure, taste cultures and news trust in a dense, connected city against small towns where reputations are known face to face.
- **Savo:** check where the regional sayings about Savonians and their speech came from and who repeats them, as claims to trace, beside Savonians' own accounts of judging a speaker.
- **Ostrobothnia and the revival areas:** check how revival movements judge true faith and worldliness, with care for members' own accounts.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language trickster lore and consumer warnings.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** check how judgment of weather, snow, reindeer and people is taught, in Sámi sources.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how Kale families judge outsiders and are judged by them; the material is mostly oral and should come through Kale organizations.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in Karelia now in Russia:** check how Soviet-era experience shapes trust in official news among returnees.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Tatars:** check news sources and scam exposure in each language.
- **Older people and the young:** check who is targeted by which frauds, from police and NCSC data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scam warnings and media-trust studies; good folk-tale material; thin on Sámi and Kale ways of judging people in their own written words.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; older Finnish for catechism texts; North Sámi for *Stállu* tales; Russian and Somali for community news use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** scam victims are often older and ashamed; revival movements' judgments of members are private matters; Russian-speakers' loyalty is a charged topic since 2022, and no group's judgment should be presented as a trait.
- **Held for review:** one hiring field experiment on judging applicants by name, set aside under the standing rule as work by a scholar of South Asian heritage; look for other discrimination-testing studies instead.
