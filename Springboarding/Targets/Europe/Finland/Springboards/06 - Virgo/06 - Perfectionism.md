# Finland: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Russian. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether pressure to perform runs higher among students and young professionals in Helsinki and Espoo than in smaller towns, as a question for the surveys.
- **Tavastia and Pirkanmaa's industrial towns:** check quality-control and factory standards against craft standards in the same region.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check boatbuilding and woodwork standards in Finnish- and Swedish-speaking villages.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check guild and workshop records, the longest series.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check duodji standards and the debate over correct making, each people's own.
- **North Karelia and the Orthodox:** check icon painting and church ritual, where exact form is part of the rite.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check the Swedish-language boating, choir and sailing worlds for their own ideas of "done right".
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check dress and household standards only as Kale writers present them; little is published.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and other newer communities:** check school pressure as each family describes it, never assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school and student well-being studies; mixed for craft standards (scattered across trade and museum sources); thin for Sámi and Kale views of perfection in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish for guild records; Church Slavonic and Finnish for icon-painting texts; North Sámi for duodji criteria.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders, OCD and self-harm among high achievers; Sámi rules for who may make what are a community matter.
- **Held for review:** none; the legal protection of the duodji mark is for Libra.
