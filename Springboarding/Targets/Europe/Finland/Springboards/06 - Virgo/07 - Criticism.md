# Finland: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether national satire and opinion pages, written mostly in Helsinki, treat the provinces as targets, and how the provinces answer.
- **Kainuu and North Karelia:** check local protest and criticism of distant decision-makers (mines, closures, forests).
- **Ostrobothnia:** check how criticism is phrased in tight village and revival communities, in Finnish and Swedish.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check revue, press and cabaret criticism in Swedish, and Åland's own press on the mainland.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check who may correct whom inside Sámi families and communities, and Sámi critique of the state, each people on its own terms.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check how criticism is handled between families and toward elders, only through Kale-authored sources and organizations.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in Karelia now in Russia:** check memories of what could and could not be said under Soviet rule.
- **Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns:** check who speaks for each community in public and how criticism of them is phrased.
- **Workplaces by sector:** check feedback practice in the state, industry and start-ups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, satire and school assessment; mixed for workplace feedback (mostly practitioner material); thin for Sámi and Kale practices of correction in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; 1700s Swedish for the Turku paper; North Sámi for community humor; Russian and Somali for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anonymous forums include abuse of named people; criticism of the Russian state by Russian-speakers in Finland can carry personal risk.
- **Held for review:** none; defamation and press law, and freedom of expression as written, belong to Libra and Capricorn.
