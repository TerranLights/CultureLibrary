# Finland: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Karelia and Savo:** check how the region's health campaign is remembered against its own food and work traditions.
- **Ostrobothnia, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking:** check the health-difference question between language groups, and local vaccine and revival-movement debates handled with care.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check access, private care and the wellness market against rural waiting times.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check distances to care and ambulance times, and northern healing traditions.
- **Sámi (North, Inari, Skolt):** check health services in Sámi languages and Sámi views of wellness, each people's own.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check health and care experience from Kale-run research and organizations.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in Karelia now in Russia:** check holy springs, Orthodox healing prayer and evacuees' health memories.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Kurdish-origin and other communities:** check each group's use of care and own healers, as the *Maamu* data allow.
- **Åland:** check the island's own health-care organization and its use of mainland and Swedish hospitals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health registers and surveys; mixed for folk healing today (sparse, guarded); thin for Sámi-language health research and for Tatar and Old Believer health beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish for physicians' reports; Meänkieli-flavored northern dialects for healers; North Sámi; Russian, Somali and Kurdish for *Maamu* communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** health registers are restricted; suicide, addiction and HIV data need care; healers' words may be secret, so ask before recording.
- **Held for review:** none; health-care law and patient rights belong to Libra and Capricorn.
