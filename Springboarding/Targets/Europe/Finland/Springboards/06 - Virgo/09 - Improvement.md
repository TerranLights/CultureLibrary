# Finland: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the Economic Society's home ground, where improvement was first organized.
- **Ostrobothnia, both language communities:** check land clearing, drainage and later small-business betterment, in Finnish and Swedish sources.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check self-help, coaching and personal-development markets against older adult-education routes.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check reservoirs, drainage and roads against Sámi revitalization, North, Inari and Skolt each on its own terms.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Savo:** check out-migration and the "class journey" from small farms to cities.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check the mission and state programs aimed at them, and Kale-led self-organization, from Kale-authored sources.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns, including those in Karelia now in Russia:** check the 1941-44 occupation schooling and Soviet-era "progress", each from the people's own record.
- **Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews:** check integration training and each community's own schools and associations.
- **Åland:** check land and harbor improvements under island self-government.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for agricultural, land-use and adult-education history; mixed for self-help culture; thin for Kale and Sámi accounts of imposed improvement in print, though the Sámi truth commission's work may change that.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish for the Economic Society; Karelian and Russian for East Karelian records; Skolt and North Sámi; Somali and Russian for integration accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the East Karelia occupation, the Gypsy Mission's homes and Sámi displacement involve living memory and harm; prefer the affected people's own accounts.
- **Held for review:** none; integration law and land-use statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
