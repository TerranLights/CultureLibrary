## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** set civil-ceremony and cohabitation shares beside those of rural parishes.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the oldest court and church records, from the region where Swedish-era law was first applied.
- **Ostrobothnia, Swedish- and Finnish-speaking:** check revivalist weddings and the knife-fighter (*puukkojunkkari*) court cases, a test of how feuds were judged.
- **Savo, Tavastia and Kainuu:** check inland wedding customs and land quarrels in burn-beating country.
- **North Karelia and Karelians, with Karelia now in Russia:** check Orthodox weddings and laments, evacuee records, and Karelian villages beyond today's border.
- **Lapland and the Sámi (North, Inari, Skolt):** check each people's marriage ways and the Skolt village assembly (*sijdsååbbar*, *check*) as its own forum.
- **Åland:** check island law and island weddings apart from the mainland.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check marriage, avoidance and dispute customs, from Kale-led sources.
- **Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers and Russian-speakers:** compare each religious marriage rite with the civil one, and ask who hears a quarrel first.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, court records, church registers and folk poetry; thin for Old Believers and for Somali-Finn dispute forms, where community histories and interviews are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (old Swedish for codes and court records); the three Sámi languages; Karelian and Russian for the eastern lands; Tatar and Somali for community marriage and quarrel forms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1918 records name families on both sides; Kale feud and avoidance customs need Kale consent. Finland's exact lands are still to be settled; the pre-war lands carried (Finnic and Karelian people in Karelia, now in Russia) are pointed to where a concept needs them, with no borders drawn. Sámi in Norway and Sweden, Estonia, and the Sweden Finn and American Finn diasporas serve as comparison only.
- **Held for review:** none; the domain's letter of the law is kept here, and what law is for belongs to Capricorn.
