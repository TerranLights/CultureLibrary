## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check queuing and service fairness where housing lists and day-care places are scarcest.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check Great Partition complaints in the old, dense villages, where land was most divided.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check inheritance practice on large family farms, where one heir often took over and siblings were paid out.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check partition files in burn-beating country, where village land was held jointly longer.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check reindeer quotas between Sámi and Finnish herders in the same cooperative.
- **The three Sámi peoples:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi views of quota and fishing-permit rules separately.
- **Åland:** check debates on the equalization amount, a fairness question between island and state.
- **Finnish Kale Roma and Somali-Finns:** check tribunal and ombudsman case files for complaints about housing, jobs and service.
- **Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns and Tatars:** check whether discrimination surveys separate them, since each came at a different time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, tribunal decisions and tax publicity; mixed for children's lore; thin for how Tatars and Old Believers divide estates, which family memoirs might show.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish for partition files; North Sámi for herding debates; Somali and Russian for complaints in community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribunal cases and tax lists name private people; quote only the rule, not the person.
- **Held for review:** none; the tax-publicity rule held back in Taurus is taken up here, and what fairness laws are for stays with Capricorn.
