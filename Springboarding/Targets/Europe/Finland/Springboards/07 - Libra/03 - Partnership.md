## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check cohabitation and registered prenuptials where couples marry later and own more apart.
- **Finland Proper, Turku and the archipelago:** check seine-fishing teams and old church registers for how work crews and couples overlapped.
- **Ostrobothnia and Tavastia:** check cooperative dairies and shops, early strongholds of the Pellervo movement.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check farm partnerships between in-laws in joint households.
- **Kainuu:** check log-floating crews as short-lived work partnerships.
- **Lapland, with North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check *siida* ties and the state-Sámi negotiation duty, people by people.
- **Åland:** check cooperatives and shipping partnerships in island firms.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check joint households and Orthodox godparent ties brought from the eastern lands.
- **Tatars, Jews, Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and Finnish Kale Roma:** check how each forms business and marriage partnerships, and which registers record them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for marriage and company law, cooperatives and family surveys; mixed for crews and godparenthood; thin for Somali-Finn and Kale business ties, where interviews and community media are the start.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for statutes and registers; North Sámi for *siida* terms; Tatar and Russian for merchant and family histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prenuptial and divorce files concern private couples; use counts, not names.
- **Held for review:** none; the citizens'-initiative rules flagged in Aries are read here, and the purpose of marriage law stays with Capricorn.
