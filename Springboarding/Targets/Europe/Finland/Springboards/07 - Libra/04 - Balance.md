## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check burnout and long-hours data among office workers against the rest of the country.
- **Finland Proper and the Turku archipelago:** check bilingual municipalities near the threshold, where the language balance is closest.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Swedish-majority towns and the temperance stronghold of the revivalist parishes, as two kinds of balance.
- **Tavastia and Savo:** check the inland folk-medicine records for words of being set right.
- **North Karelia and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check Orthodox fast-feast practice in evacuee parishes.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check seasonal swings in work and rest in forestry and tourism.
- **The Sámi homeland (North, Inari and Skolt Sámi):** check how the language duties land in each municipality.
- **Åland:** check the Delegation's opinions for which side carried each dispute.
- **Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns, Somali-Finns and Finnish Kale Roma:** check acculturation research for each group, and say where none exists.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional texts, working-life surveys and language statistics; mixed for folk medicine; thin for Somali-Finn and Kale views of balance between cultures, where youth and community studies are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish for statutes and the Åland body; the Sámi languages for homeland duties; Russian for acculturation interviews; Church Slavonic for older Orthodox texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burnout and drinking data are health data; use published tables only.
- **Held for review:** none; the Prohibition Act held back in Taurus is read here as text, and the law's aims belong to Capricorn.
