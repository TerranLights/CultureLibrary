# Finland: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship (rules on disqualification and appointment: their letter only).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check procurement and hiring disputes where the largest public bodies buy and hire.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check early court records for gifts to judges and the rules that met them.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check small bilingual municipalities, where officials often know both parties, for disqualification rulings.
- **Tavastia, Savo and Kainuu:** check rural councils' lay-judge choices and local procurement for ties between buyer and seller.
- **North Karelia and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check folktale variants of the clever judge from Karelian singers.
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check earmark disputes and herding-cooperative elections among North, Inari and Skolt Sámi separately.
- **Åland:** check how the island's small administration handles officials' ties, under its own law.
- **Finnish Kale Roma, Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check profiling and service complaints, in each group's own words.
- **Tatars, Jews and Old Believers:** check whether small congregations report unequal treatment, and note where nothing is published.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, oversight decisions and press rulings; mixed for profiling, a recent field; thin for Old Believer and Tatar experience with officials.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Swedish and early modern Finnish for the Judges' Rules; North Sámi for herding records; Russian and Somali for interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** profiling and procurement cases may name persons and firms; quote rules and outcomes, not people.
- **Held for review:** none; whether people trust officials to be fair, and what the rules are for, is Capricorn's matter.
