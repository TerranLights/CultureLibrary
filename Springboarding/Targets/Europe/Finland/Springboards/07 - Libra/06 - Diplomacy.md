# Finland: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Karelian; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life (treaties: the letter of their texts).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how ministries, embassies and firms train staff in protocol, since nearly all foreign contacts start here.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the Swedish-era court and town papers for how Turku merchants dealt with foreign skippers.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Finland-Swedish ties to Sweden across the Gulf of Bothnia as everyday cross-border dealings.
- **Tavastia and Savo:** check the memory of the eastern trade's factories, which hung on each Moscow deal.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians from lands now in Russia:** check peddlers' and borderland villagers' own accounts of crossing talks.
- **Lapland, with North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check consultation on the Teno deal and border herding rules, people by people; Sámi in Norway are a comparison only.
- **Åland:** check how islanders tell the 1921 settlement and the role of the demilitarized status in their own talks with the state.
- **Finnish Kale Roma, Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check who acts as the family's or community's spokesperson with officials, and how that role is learned.
- **Tatars, Jews and Ingrian Finns:** check community leaders' dealings with the state at key moments, such as wartime and return migration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaties and Cold War diplomacy; mixed for everyday tact, where linguistics is the best start; thin for Sámi consultation records, which sit partly in Sámi Parliament files.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish, Swedish and Russian for treaties and trade papers; Latin and old Swedish for early treaties; North Sámi for consultation records; Somali for interpreting studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some foreign-ministry files remain closed; Kekkonen-era sources are much disputed, so read each against others.
- **Held for review:** none; the treaty side of the Teno pointer, held in Taurus, is placed here, while whether "Finlandization" was wise is Capricorn's or Aquarius's.
