## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether informal help between households thins where neighbors move often.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check old account books of gifts at weddings and funerals in manor and farm archives.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revivalist congregations' giving and return of help, and the Swedish-speaking mutual insurers.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check the visiting-gift questionnaires, where the timing of a return visit may be recorded.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians, including Karelia now in Russia:** check bear-feast and memorial-meal sources by village of origin.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** check exchange between herding, fishing and settled families from Sámi sources first; Sámi in Norway and Sweden only as comparison.
- **Åland:** check the island mutual insurer and shipping shares for return obligations among islanders.
- **Finnish Kale Roma and Somali-Finns:** check hospitality and pooled giving, each from its own narrators.
- **Tatars, Jews, Old Believers and Russian-speakers:** check each congregation's charity and loan societies, and note where records are missing.
