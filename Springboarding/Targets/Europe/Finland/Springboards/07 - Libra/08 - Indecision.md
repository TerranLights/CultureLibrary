# Finland: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check long civic decisions (museum, library, metro lines) against faster choices in smaller towns.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the city's long-running plans for its market square and riverbank as a local test of slow deciding.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revivalist parish meetings, where decisions may wait on agreement, against secular councils.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check village lot-drawing records for meadows and fishing waters.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelian evacuees:** check how the Karelian question is discussed within evacuee families across generations.
- **Lapland's Sámi:** check how North, Inari and Skolt Sámi bodies speak of the delayed reforms, each separately.
- **Åland:** check the island's own referendum of 1994 and the slow autonomy revision.
- **Finnish Kale Roma and Somali-Finns:** check who decides marriages, moves and schooling in families, and whether elders take the final word.
- **Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns and Tatars:** check election-study data on late deciding among newer citizens, and say if the samples are too small.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, referendum and election data; mixed for civic decisions, through council minutes; thin for family deciding among Kale, Somali-Finns and Tatars, where interviews are needed.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; North Sámi for Sámi Parliament statements; Russian and Somali for family studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Karelian question touches borders now in Russia; scout the debate, settle nothing. Living wills are private; use register counts only.
- **Held for review:** none; why the courts and the state are held to deadlines is Capricorn's question, and decision by chance as fate is a later sign's.
