## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check reporting of harassment to inspectors against small-town workplaces where staff know one another.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the 1899 address signatures and later compliance-era politics among Turku's elites.
- **Ostrobothnia and Lapland's Laestadian congregations:** check how revival movements handled internal harm, and how members describe it.
- **Tavastia and Savo:** check 1918 memory in towns with large prison camps, where silence was kept longest.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelian evacuees:** check family silence about lost homes in the ceded lands.
- **The three Sámi peoples:** check boarding-school testimony separately for North, Inari and Skolt Sámi, and how each speaks of it.
- **Åland:** check how the demilitarized status is spoken of on the islands, as a price paid or a shield.
- **Finnish Kale Roma, Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers:** check whether each turns to elders, family or police in a quarrel, and why.
- **Jews, Tatars and Ingrian Finns:** check wartime and post-war silences in each community's own histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Russification and Cold War periods and for statutes; mixed for partner-violence mediation, through reports; thin for Somali-Finn mediation and Russian-speaker experience, where fieldwork is needed.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish, Swedish and Russian for the Russification and Cold War material; North Sámi for testimony; Somali for elders' practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse cases, boarding-school testimony and partner violence involve survivors; work from published reports and consent-based collections only.
- **Held for review:** the total objectors' legal status held back in Taurus is read here as statute only; whether yielding to Moscow was wise belongs to Capricorn and Aquarius, not here.
