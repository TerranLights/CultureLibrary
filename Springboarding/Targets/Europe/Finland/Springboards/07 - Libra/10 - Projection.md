# Finland: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other" (offenses of false accusation and agitation: their letter only).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check press framing of newcomers against regional papers.
- **Finland Proper and Åland:** check the island witch trials against mainland trials of the same decades, through each court's records.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the witch-trial cluster and the enemy images of 1918 in a region of White strength.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check the "tribe" sketches told about each region against their own self-accounts.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelian evacuees:** check how hosts and evacuees spoke of one another after 1944, and what lands now in Russia meant in that talk.
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check how North, Inari and Skolt Sámi meet the "sorcerer" image in tourism and media, each in its own sources.
- **Finnish Kale Roma:** check the 1900 report and later policy against Kale writers' answers.
- **Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns and Tatars:** check how each was labeled in 1918, the 1940s, the 1990s and after 2022, period by period.
- **Somali-Finns and Jews:** check press framing at first arrival and in later crises, and each community's replies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for witch trials, wartime propaganda and statutes; mixed for regional images, mostly in school books and jokes; thin for Tatar and Old Believer experience of blame, where community histories are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Latin and old Swedish for trials and Olaus Magnus; North Sámi; Russian and Somali for minority voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for Russians, Kale, Sámi and Somalis appear in the sources; record them as sources' words, not the sheet's. The *noaidi* tradition holds knowledge some Sámi keep restricted. School-shooting reports touch living families.
- **Held for review:** the "Finnish envy" (*kateus*) saying, kept only as a claim to examine where it is used, never as a trait; defamation and agitation as offenses are read here, their aims in Capricorn.
