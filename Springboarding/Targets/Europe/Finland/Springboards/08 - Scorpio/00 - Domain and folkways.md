## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether cremation and secular memorials set the capital apart.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the oldest churchyards and burial registers, to date the move from village grounds.
- **Ostrobothnia, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking:** check revival-movement funerals and large-farm succession.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check memorial trees and funeral feasts in the inland folk record.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check Orthodox memorial days, laments and grounds left beyond the border; Finnic Karelians now in Russia count as part of the people here.
- **Lapland and the North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check each people's own ways with the dead, separately.
- **Åland and coastal Finland-Swedes:** check Swedish-language funeral-feast and estate customs as their own series.
- **Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Old Believers, Ingrian Finns:** set each community's burial, mourning and inheritance practice beside the Lutheran rite, through its own bodies and writers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk poetry, parish registers and survey series; thin for Old Believers and Ingrian Finns, where community histories are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (older forms for parish records); the three Sámi languages; Karelian and Russian for the eastern lands; Tatar, Somali and Kale Romani for community practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi graves and remains in collections, Karelian grounds left behind, the war and 1918 dead, and suicide are later research topics, to be approached with care. Finland's exact lands are still to be settled: there is no scope file, and the pre-war lands carried (Finnic and Karelian people and places now in Russia) are pointed to only where a concept needs them, with no borders drawn. Sámi across the borders, Estonia, and Sweden Finn or American Finn diasporas are comparison only. Legal texts on wills or burial go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on the dead, estates or sex ways was set aside under the standing rules.
