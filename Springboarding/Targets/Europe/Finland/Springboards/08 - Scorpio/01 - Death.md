# Finland: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Tatar; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how dying, the dead and what death means are understood, with grief and mourning; funeral practice sits in the domain sheet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check memorial trees and the lament against the Lutheran funeral, parish by parish.
- **Karelians from the ceded lands and Ingrian Finns:** check lament records and graves left in Russia, and what kin do about graves they cannot visit.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival-movement teaching on a believer's death and on the unconverted dead.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check hospice use and secular funerals as signs of a different way of dying.
- **Lapland and the three Sámi peoples:** check each people's own word and account of the dead, and the reburial question.
- **Åland and coastal Finland-Swedes:** check death notices and grief language in Swedish-language papers.
- **Kale Roma, Jews, Tatars, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers:** check who washes and watches the body in each community, and how long mourning runs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for laments, belief records and statistics; thin for Inari and Skolt Sámi accounts and for Old Believers, where Sámi language bodies and eastern church records are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish; Karelian (lament language is archaic); Swedish; the three Sámi languages; Russian, Tatar and Somali for community grief.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, child death, Sámi remains, war dead and the 1918 dead need care and consent; Kale mourning customs belong to Kale speakers to describe. Euthanasia and burial law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no source on dying or mourning was set aside, and euthanasia and burial statutes are passed to Libra rather than held here.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the SKS archive catalog for *hautausitku* and *karsikko* and list parishes and dates.

5. Ask Siida for its published account of the reburial of Sámi remains.
