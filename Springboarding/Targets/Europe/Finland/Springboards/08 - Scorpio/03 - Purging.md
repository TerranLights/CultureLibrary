# Finland: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia, Lapua and its neighbors:** check memory of the 1930 movement in the region where it arose, beside the memory of those removed.
- **Tavastia and Uusimaa:** check how 1918 camp sites (Hennala, Tammisaari) are commemorated by different local groups.
- **Finland Proper and Satakunta:** check Maundy Thursday customs in the western parishes, where the rite is best recorded.
- **Kainuu and Kymenlaakso:** check local accounts of pollution and cleanup, as two industrial cases.
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check Sámi accounts of the drum campaigns, North Sámi and Inari Sámi apart.
- **Karelians and Ingrian Finns:** check family memory of the terror in Soviet Karelia, against Finnish sources and Russian archives.
- **Kale Roma:** check whether and how Kale writers describe rules of clean and unclean, and what may be told to outsiders.
- **Tatars, Somali-Finns, Jews, Old Believers:** compare fasting calendars and cleansing practices from each community's own sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1918, the Lapua movement and folk-calendar records; thin for Old Believers and for Sámi accounts of the drum campaigns, where Sámi scholars and church records are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian for 1930s Karelian records; North and Inari Sámi; Karelian; Tatar, Somali and Kale Romani for community practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1918 camps, the Soviet terror and Sámi drums are grave topics for later research, not findings; Kale purity rules may be private; the security-police papers may be partly closed. Legal texts on secrecy or banned books go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the withdrawn-books list stays as a record of practice, with its legal basis passed to Libra.
