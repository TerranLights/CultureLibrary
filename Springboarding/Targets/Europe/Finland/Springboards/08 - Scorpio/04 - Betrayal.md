# Finland: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kymenlaakso and the southeast:** check local memory of the Anjala League in the manor district where it formed.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how Sveaborg and the 1939 Terijoki episode are told at the sites themselves.
- **Tavastia and Satakunta:** check strikebreaker and 1918 informer memories in mill and estate towns.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check White-side narratives of 1918, to set beside Red-side ones, without taking either as the frame.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check East Karelian refugee memory and the kinship wars.
- **Ingrian Finns:** check returnee accounts of 1944-45 and of later return migration.
- **Åland:** check islanders' own record of the 1917-21 movement.
- **Jews:** check community histories of the 1942 handovers and of Jewish soldiers in the Finnish army.
- **Lapland and the Sámi:** check Sámi accounts of promises made by church and state, from Sámi sources.
- **Russian-speakers, Tatars, Somali-Finns:** check whether loyalty is questioned in times of tension, and how each community answers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and war history, folk ballads and labor archives; thinner for Sámi and Somali-Finn sources on betrayal, where community media and interviews are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (both for Åland and Sveaborg); Russian for Terijoki, Ingria and East Karelia; Karelian; German for the handovers; Sámi, Tatar and Somali for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** handovers to Germany, Ingrian returns and 1918 informers touch named families; affairs and counseling are private. Treason as law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; treason trials and their verdicts are passed on to Libra and Capricorn, not held.
