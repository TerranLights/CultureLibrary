## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia and the Swedish-speaking coast:** check smuggling memory and temperance campaigns, two sides of drink control in one region.
- **Åland:** check prohibition-era smuggling and the islands' own controls over land and residence as practice.
- **Lapland, Kemijoki and the Sámi:** check flooded-village and reservoir memory, and Sámi boarding-school accounts, North, Inari and Skolt apart.
- **Kainuu:** check local verdicts on the self-government trial.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check church discipline in older parish records.
- **Karelians from the ceded lands and North Karelia:** set Orthodox priests' and brotherhoods' means of keeping order beside Lutheran church discipline.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check workplace monitoring and youth controls in an urban labor market.
- **Kale Roma:** check how authorities' controls are remembered, and what Kale bodies say about them.
- **Laestadian families, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars:** check how rules for teenagers and couples are described from inside each community, in its own media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for prohibition, temperance, church records and child-welfare surveys; thin for Skolt Sámi and Somali-Finn sources, where community bodies and interviews are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (old Swedish for church records); North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali; Kale Romani where Kale bodies use it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in children's homes, boarding schools and partner control are survivor-held topics; religious community rules must be read from many voices. Laws on alcohol, punishment or regulation go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no source on restraint or supervision was set aside, and alcohol and child-welfare statutes go to Libra.
