## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check trust in strangers in mixed urban neighborhoods against smaller towns.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check savings-bank history at its first site, and how depositors' trust is recalled.
- **Ostrobothnia and Satakunta:** check local savings-bank failures of the 1990s and what they did to village trust.
- **North Karelia and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check border-peace traditions and evacuees' trust in new neighbors after resettlement.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check trust in distant central institutions against local ones.
- **The Sámi peoples:** check what each community asks of outsiders before sharing knowledge.
- **Åland:** check confidence in island institutions beside mainland ones.
- **Finland-Swedes and Finnish-speakers:** check trust across the language line, in both directions.
- **Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews:** check experiences of being trusted or doubted, and each community's own trusted bodies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey trust data and banking history; thin for Tatar and Old Believer communities and for trust inside Kale families, which belong to Kale writers to describe.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Sámi languages for research terms; Russian for border history and today's community; Tatar and Somali for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** scam victims and discrimination cases are private; sources on Russian-speakers written since 2022 need careful reading. Police and courts go to Capricorn.
