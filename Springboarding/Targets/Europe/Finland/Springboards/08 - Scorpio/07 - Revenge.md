## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Ostrobothnia:** check knife-fighter songs and the *Pohjalaisia* story against court records, to see how feuds were told and remembered.
- **Tavastia and Pirkanmaa (Tampere):** check 1918 reprisal memory in the war's heartland, on both sides.
- **Uusimaa and Kymenlaakso:** check the Red and White killing sites near the capital and the southeast.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check harm-returning charms and border-raid tales of the eastern runo areas.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** compare how each people's Chud tales end, and who tells them.
- **Finland-Swedes and Åland:** check Swedish-language ballads and island quarrels for their own patterns.
- **Kale Roma:** check how quarrels between families are mediated, from Kale-led sources only.
- **Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars:** check community-led accounts of settling wrongs; customary law goes to Libra and Capricorn.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1918, folk poetry and regional song; thin for Inari and Skolt Sámi and for community dispute practice, where Sámi archives and community bodies are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; old Finnish for charms; Karelian; the Sámi languages; Russian; Somali and Kale Romani for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1918 reprisals name families on both sides; Kale dispute customs belong to Kale speakers; homicide data involve the bereaved. Private revenge against state punishment, and victim-offender mediation, go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no feud, reprisal or forgiveness source was set aside under the standing rules.
