# Finland: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet (proposed home Scorpio, also listed under Pisces).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how privacy and coming out differ between the city and a small municipality.
- **Lapland:** check silence and disclosure around wartime children of German soldiers, town by village.
- **Lapland's Sámi peoples:** check what each community keeps from outsiders, and the terms for sharing it.
- **Ostrobothnia and Tavastia:** check arms-cache memory in the districts where caches were found.
- **Eastern runo-singing areas (Kainuu, North Karelia, Karelia beyond the border):** check who was allowed to learn a healer's words, and when.
- **Åland:** check how privacy is kept in small island communities, against mainland towns.
- **Finland-Swedes:** check lodges, confession and family privacy in Swedish-language sources.
- **Kale Roma:** check whether Kale speakers want the in-group use of their language discussed at all before any research.
- **Russian-speakers, Tatars, Somali-Finns, Jews:** check what each community has kept private under suspicion or surveillance, and why.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Cold War and wartime secrecy, folk belief and the press; thin for Kale and Sámi restricted knowledge, which should not be sought beyond what the communities choose to share.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian and German for intelligence papers; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi sacred sites and Kale in-group knowledge are restricted; Vastaamo victims and wartime children are private persons; some security files remain closed. Secrecy law and freedom-of-information law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; restricted Sámi and Kale knowledge is not held for review but left unsought unless the communities offer it.
