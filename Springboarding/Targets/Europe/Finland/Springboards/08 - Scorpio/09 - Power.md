## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where national elites live, study and meet, against the rest of the country.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check manor and church power in the oldest settled region.
- **Western Uusimaa and the Swedish-speaking coast:** check ironworks and manor records for how owner power was exercised.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check large-farm owners' standing and the pew order in farming parishes.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check master and mistress roles in the farmhouse questionnaires.
- **North Karelia, Kainuu and Karelians from the ceded lands:** check *tietäjä* standing in the eastern runo areas and Orthodox village leadership.
- **Lapland and the Sámi peoples:** check how crown, church and later state power reached each Sámi people, from Sámi sources.
- **Åland:** check island self-rule and who holds local power within it.
- **Tatars, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Kale Roma, Jews:** check who speaks for each community and how that voice is chosen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite studies, estate records, labor history and folk belief; thin for power inside Kale and Somali-Finn communities, which should come from community-led sources.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (old Swedish for estates, ironworks and bailiffs); the Sámi languages; Karelian and Russian; Tatar and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi political disputes (who counts as Sámi) are live and partly legal, so their legal side goes to Libra; Kekkonen-era sources are much disputed; *tietäjä* knowledge may carry limits on sharing. Authority and hierarchy go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no elite, estate or seer-healer source needed setting aside.
