# Finland: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Great Wrath memory in the region's parish records and tales.
- **Savo, Kainuu and North Karelia:** check famine and migration crises in the inland regions, parish by parish.
- **Karelians from the ceded lands:** check evacuation and resettlement from the evacuees' and the receivers' sides.
- **Lapland and its towns:** check the 1944 evacuation and return, beside the reindeer-herding families' accounts.
- **Skolt Sámi, North Sámi and Inari Sámi:** check each people's account of evacuation, separately.
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check the 2020 closure and shelter culture in the city.
- **Åland and Finland Proper:** check maritime disasters and island supply in crisis.
- **Ingrian Finns, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers:** check each community's own crisis of arrival or departure (wartime moves, asylum in 1990, border closure).
- **Finland-Swedes:** check Swedish-language relief and war-child networks as their own record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famines, wars, evacuations and preparedness; thinner for Skolt Sámi and Somali-Finn accounts, where community bodies and interviews are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (old Swedish for 17th- and 18th-century records); Skolt, Inari and North Sámi; Karelian and Russian; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine deaths, wartime evacuations and the *Estonia* dead are matters of grief; evacuees' records name families. Emergency-powers law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; every famine, evacuation and preparedness pointer stays in, and emergency-powers law is passed on.
