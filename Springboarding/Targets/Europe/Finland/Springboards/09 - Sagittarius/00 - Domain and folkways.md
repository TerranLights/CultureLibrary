## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check the most mixed faith map: synagogue, Tatar prayer hall, Orthodox cathedral, many unaffiliated.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the first cathedral and academy, and the Prayer movement's home parishes in the southwest.
- **Ostrobothnia, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking:** check Laestadian, Pietist and free-church presence side by side, with Swedish-speaking free churches as their own series.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check the Awakening's inland home ground, and Tavastia's place in the old offering lore.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check Orthodox village life, rune-singing country and the evacuees' parishes; Finnic Karelians across the border join where a question reaches them.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi separately, the Skolts' Orthodox parish included, and northern Laestadian congregations.
- **Åland:** check the islands' own parish life, seafaring and outward links.
- **Peoples:** set Finland-Swedes, Kale Roma, Ingrian Finns, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns side by side through the bodies each runs for worship; Sámi beyond the borders, Estonians and emigrant Finns are outside comparisons only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church registers, religion statistics and folk-belief archives; mixed for newer faiths; thin for Old Believers.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish, older forms included; Latin for Schefferus; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian and Russian for Orthodox records; Tatar, Hebrew, Yiddish, Somali and Kale Romani for minority faiths.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi sacred sites and drums in collections; Sámi and Karelian knowledge that families may keep private. No scope file exists yet, so the nation's exact lands remain open: the tracker lists Russia among the pre-war lands carried, read here as Finnic and Karelian people and places east of today's border, cited only when a topic calls for them; no border is implied. Church and religious-freedom legislation go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, kept as a category for the later religious-landscape count.
