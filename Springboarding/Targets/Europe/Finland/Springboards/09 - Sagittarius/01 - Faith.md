# Finland: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; Skolt and North Sámi; Karelian; Tatar; Russian; Somali; Kale Romani. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether church resignation and secular confirmation run ahead of the rest, and how immigrant congregations change the city's worship.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the Prayer movement's kneeling devotion in its home parishes beside cathedral-town Lutheranism.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Conservative Laestadian, Awakening and Swedish free-church practice in neighboring parishes, rather than treating the province as one.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check how the Awakening's home ground records faith in letters and gatherings.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check Orthodox village chapels and the evacuees' parishes; Finnic Karelians in today's Russia, where a topic needs them.
- **Lapland:** check Skolt Orthodox, North and Inari Sámi Lutheran and Laestadian practice separately, each in its own voice.
- **Åland:** check island parish life against the mainland.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma, Ingrian returnees, Tatars, Jews, Russian-speaking Orthodox, Old Believers and Somali-Finns each through their own congregations; Sámi across the borders only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for membership counts, revival publications and church surveys; mixed for Muslim and free-church practice; thin for Old Believers, where Karelian and Ingrian community histories are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Church Slavonic and Russian for Orthodox and Moscow-parish life; Skolt Sámi; Tatar, Arabic and Somali for Muslim practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaving a tight-knit movement can split families; wartime faith, including the Jewish soldiers' case, needs careful framing; Sámi conversion history is told first by Sámi. Laws on religious freedom go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands: their devotional life is left as a category for the author to rule on.
