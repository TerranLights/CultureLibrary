# Finland: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Tatar; Somali; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish history; Karelian and Ingrian Finnish for the eastern lands. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal (deciding for oneself and not depending on others stay in Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether free-expression quarrels (art, satire, campus debate) cluster in Helsinki's press and universities.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check Turku's 1800s press and the Swedish-language liberal tradition there.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the Club War and Chydenius, then how the Lapua Movement of 1929–32 used the word freedom in its own papers.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check how Kuopio's Canth circle and Tavastian Red towns put women's and workers' freedom.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check the evacuated Karelians' sense of freedom lost with the home parish; kin in Russian Karelia enter only where the freedom question touches them.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi claims to land and self-rule, each separately, with Sámi parliaments across the borders as an outside yardstick.
- **Åland:** check demilitarization and island self-rule as an islanders' version of freedom.
- **Peoples:** check Finland-Swedish liberal papers; Kale Roma organizations' records on housing and movement; Tatar and Jewish histories of gaining civil rights; Russian-speaking, Old Believer and Somali-Finnish community media on worship and speech, each in its own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history, literature and election studies; mixed for Sámi and Roma sources; thin for Old Believers.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish; Swedish (Chydenius, Runeberg, Snellman wrote in it); Russian for Ingrian and Soviet records; North Sámi; Yiddish for early Jewish papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1918 naming still divides families; the Lapua Movement and wartime censorship need neutral framing; Sámi self-determination is a live political question. Freedom of religion and speech as written law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands: their freedom-of-worship history is held as a category, unnamed.
