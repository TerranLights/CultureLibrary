## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether trust in news and science differs between the capital's districts and the rest of the country, by education and language.
- **Finland Proper and Turku, Tavastia:** check local newspapers and their reach in mid-sized towns as truth-keepers.
- **Ostrobothnia and Lapland revival congregations:** check where scripture is ranked above science on creation, and where it is not, through the movements' own papers.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check the oral-tradition districts, where rune singers were treated as sources of record by collectors.
- **Karelians:** check how evacuee memory of the ceded parishes is stored and disputed; Finnic Karelians in Russia count where the record needs them.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** check whose oral history is entered into the commission's and the archives' records, people by people.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check the Swedish-language press as a separate news world.
- **Peoples:** Russian-speakers, Ingrian returnees, Somali-Finns, Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews and Old Believers: check which outlets, elders or congregations each turns to for news and for the past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for news trust, science trust and philosophy; mixed for oral history; thin for minority-language news use outside Russian.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian for media-contest work; the Sámi languages; Somali; Estonian only to compare a neighbor's fact-checking.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi testimony to the commission belongs to the witnesses; wartime atrocity questions need care; "disinformation" labels can be used against minorities and must not be passed on as findings. Oaths and evidence in court are Libra's.
