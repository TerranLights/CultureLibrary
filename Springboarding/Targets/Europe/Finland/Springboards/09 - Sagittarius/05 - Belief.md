## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether stated atheism and humanist naming ceremonies are more common in the capital than elsewhere, from registers rather than guesswork.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check witch-trial records from the southwest against those from Ostrobothnia, where trials may have run differently.
- **Ostrobothnia and Lapland:** check the doctrinal statements of each Laestadian branch side by side, to see where they part.
- **Savo, Tavastia, Kainuu:** check household-spirit legends against later rural surveys to see what folk belief kept or lost.
- **North Karelia and Karelians:** check Orthodox popular belief and icon customs; Karelian villages now across the border join in where the belief records come from there.
- **North, Inari and Skolt Sámi:** keep the three apart and let each one's scholars and elders set what belief is shared with outsiders.
- **Finland-Swedes and Ålanders:** check Swedish-language legend collections (SLS) as a separate record.
- **Peoples:** for Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Kale Roma, Old Believers, Ingrian Finns and Russian-speakers, read the creeds and lessons each community writes for its own young, not survey shares alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk-belief archives, church surveys and philosophy; mixed for newer Muslim communities; thin for Old Believers and Ingrian Lutheran returnees.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; German and English for the FF Communications indexes; North and Inari Sámi; Russian and Tatar; Somali and Arabic for Muslim teaching texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Sámi belief is not for outsiders; survey shares on "superstition" invite mockery and must be framed as data; the witch trials concern real families' ancestors.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, a category whose creeds and teaching texts this sheet does not name.
