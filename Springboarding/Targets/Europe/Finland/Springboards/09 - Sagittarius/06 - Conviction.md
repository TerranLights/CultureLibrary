# Finland: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Arabic, Somali and Kurdish for recent protest movements; Kale Romani. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not the legal sense of a finding of guilt).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether street protest and blockades concentrate in Helsinki, and who travels in from elsewhere to join them.
- **Finland Proper and Turku, Tavastia:** check Koijärvi's rural setting against city movements.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival dissent from the 1700s on, and the same region's later political movements, using church and court papers.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check rural conscience votes and protest against mines or forest felling in the east.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi land and river activism, each separately; the Norwegian bank of the Deatnu serves only to compare.
- **Åland:** check how islanders' stands take shape in the island's own parliament and press.
- **Finland-Swedes:** check the Swedish-language side of the language struggle and how its press met the name-change waves.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma activists, Tatar and Jewish leaders in interwar and wartime crises, Russian-speakers, Old Believers, Ingrian returnees and Somali-Finns: check each one's own organizations for when and how they took public stands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parliamentary votes, survey data and movement archives; mixed for early dissenters; thin for Kale and Old Believer activism.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish; Swedish, including the 1700s church Swedish of the separatist cases; North Sámi; Russian; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for the 2017 protest material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** objectors and protesters may face court cases; asylum seekers' identities need protection; animal-rights filming raises privacy issues. Service and objection statutes, and church law, are Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category; any public stands they have taken wait for the author.
