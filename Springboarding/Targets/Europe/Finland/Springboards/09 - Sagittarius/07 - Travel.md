# Finland: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Kale Romani; Tatar; Somali; Russian; Italian for Acerbi. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys and commuting stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check package holidays and long-haul travel against rural travel rates in the official survey.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check Turku's port as the gate to Sweden and Åland, and pilgrims on Henry's route.
- **Ostrobothnia, Finnish- and Swedish-speaking:** check emigration parish by parish and returnees' houses and money.
- **Savo, Tavastia and Kainuu:** check log-floating and seasonal labor journeys, and Kainuu as the starting point of Lönnrot's trips.
- **North Karelia:** check homeland trips across the border and the evacuees' settlement in the province.
- **Lapland:** check the 1944 evacuation, tourism in the fells, and North, Inari and Skolt Sámi seasonal movement, each in its own terms; herding routes that cross into Norway and Sweden are compared, not claimed.
- **Åland:** check seafaring families and ferry work.
- **Peoples:** Finland-Swedes with their own emigration records, Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Ingrian returnees, Russian-speakers, Old Believers and Somali-Finns: check each one's own story of arrival and of trips back; Sweden Finns and American Finns only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration records, tourism statistics and wartime evacuation; mixed for Roma history; thin for Old Believers.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (Castrén); Italian and English (Acerbi); Russian for the eastern lands; North and Skolt Sámi; Tatar and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma itinerancy and its regulation are history to study, never a trait to assign; Skolt and Karelian losses are grief, not scenery; Sámi lands in tourism marketing are disputed.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands: arrival stories and trips home are a category awaiting the author.
