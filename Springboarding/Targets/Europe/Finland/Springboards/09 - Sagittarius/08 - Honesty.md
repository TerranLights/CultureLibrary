## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check fraud and scam complaints per resident against smaller towns, using consumer-authority data.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the early town courts' cheating cases as a baseline for later urban trade.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check whether revival teaching on truthfulness shows up in local business histories and memoirs.
- **Savo, Tavastia and Kainuu:** check the folk-tale record of the clever cheat and the cheated master, district by district.
- **North Karelia and the evacuee Karelians:** check the memory of Viena Karelian peddlers (*laukkukauppiaat*) as their descendants tell it, apart from the names outsiders gave them.
- **Lapland:** check reindeer earmark disputes as a matter of trust among North, Inari and Skolt Sámi herders, through herders' own bodies.
- **Åland:** check customs and the tax-free ferry trade as an honesty question in island life.
- **Peoples:** Finland-Swedes, Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Ingrian returnees, Old Believers, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns: check how each is portrayed in honesty debates and what each says of being trusted, without taking the portrayals as fact.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, tax and consumer data and press scandals; mixed for minorities' experience; thin for Sámi and Old Believer sources on this concept.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish; Swedish (old forms for the Turku records); Russian and Somali for community press; North Sámi.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "honest Finn" image is a stereotype to examine, not repeat; minority traders have been slurred as cheats, so pointers must not echo the slurs. Laws on fraud, bribery and tax belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the slur names once used for eastern peddlers, left out of the search terms until the author decides how to handle them; and South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a bare category.
