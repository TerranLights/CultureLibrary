## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how degree attainment in the capital compares with the rest, and the pull of the capital's universities on other regions.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the academy's town and Åbo Akademi as two lines of higher learning.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the Swedish-speaking coast's Vaasa institutions and the Finnish-speaking interior's folk high schools.
- **Savo, Tavastia, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check student-nation records and regional universities (Kuopio, Jyväskylä, Joensuu) as routes up from farm households.
- **Karelians:** check the Sortavala seminary's teachers and their move after 1944; Karelians in today's Russia only where a record needs them.
- **Lapland:** check the University of Lapland and long distances to schooling, and North, Inari and Skolt Sámi language-education gaps, each separately.
- **Åland:** check islanders' study paths to Sweden and to mainland Finland.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Ingrian returnees, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns: check access to upper-secondary and university study through each community's own reports, not national averages alone; Sweden Finns only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registers, exam archives and attainment tables; mixed for adult education; thin for Old Believer and Ingrian schooling.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Latin for the Turku academy; Russian and Church Slavonic for Orthodox training; the Sámi languages; Hebrew, Arabic and Somali for faith schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi boarding-school years and the schooling history of the Kale need care and the communities' own accounts first; attainment gaps can stigmatize. Education statutes and academic-freedom law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, a category; their students' and scholars' associations get no pointer here.
