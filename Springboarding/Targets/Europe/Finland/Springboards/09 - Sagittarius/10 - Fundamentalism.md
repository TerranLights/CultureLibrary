# Finland: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi and Meänkieli for the Tornio valley; Russian; Arabic and Somali for debates on Muslim communities; Kale Romani. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where new and imported strict movements meet the most secular population, and what press labels follow.
- **Finland Proper, Satakunta and Tavastia:** check the *hihhulit* and Prayer-movement districts for how the "zealot" label was attached and resisted.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check each Laestadian branch, free churches and Swedish-speaking Pentecostals separately; data, not reputation, should decide.
- **Savo and Kainuu:** check the Awakening's self-description and outsiders' words for it.
- **North Karelia and the Karelians:** check Orthodox traditionalism debates through Orthodox sources only.
- **Lapland and the Tornio valley:** check Laestadian life among North, Inari and Skolt Sámi and Meänkieli speakers in Sámi scholars' and community terms; Sámi in Sweden and Norway, and Kven in Norway, only as comparison.
- **Åland:** check island free churches.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma congregations, Tatar and newer Muslim communities, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speaking evangelicals and Somali-Finns: check how each describes its own strictness, and how outsiders label it, keeping the two apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Laestadian studies, press debates and church research; mixed for free churches; thin and sensitive for Muslim communities, where security framing dominates.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Meänkieli and North Sámi for the north; Russian; Arabic and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist", "cult" and "sect" are labels, never findings; abuse cases involve real victims; radicalization data can stigmatize whole communities. Church law and anti-terror law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and new movements drawn from them, stay an unnamed category; labeling them is the author's call.
