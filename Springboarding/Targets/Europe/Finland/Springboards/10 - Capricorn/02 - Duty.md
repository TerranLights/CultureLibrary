# Finland: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali; Karelian. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** ask whether civic duties such as voting and volunteering hold equally among the newest residents of Vantaa and eastern Helsinki.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check archipelago parishes, where duties of ferry, road and rescue fell on few households.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival movements' teaching on calling and work in both language groups.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check farm succession and the duties laid on the heir who stays.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check Orthodox duties to the dead and the long-distance caring of emigrant children.
- **Lapland:** check herding-family duties among North, Inari and Skolt Sámi apart, and the Finnish herders' cooperatives.
- **Åland:** check what replaces military service as a duty to the community.
- **Peoples:** check Kale duties to kin at funerals and illness as Kale-led sources describe them; Tatar and Jewish congregations' duties to members; Ingrian Finns and Karelians on duties kept across the border; Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns on what children owe parents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and civic duty; mixed for family duty; thin for Old Believers, where little seems to be in print (start with Orthodox church historians).
- **Languages needed:** Agricola's sixteenth-century Finnish for the prayer book; Swedish for road and parish records; the Sámi languages for theses; Russian and Somali for migrant-elder studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** duties to the war dead and to evacuees' lost parishes stay emotional; caring duties touch private family conflict. Conscription and support duties as written belong to the Libra sheets.
- **Held for review:** none; duty to the dead shades into Scorpio's sheet on death, and the author may decide where it lives.
