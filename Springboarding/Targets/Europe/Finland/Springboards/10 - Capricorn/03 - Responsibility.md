# Finland: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; Skolt and North Sámi; Russian; Hebrew and Yiddish for older Jewish records. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** test whether blame for social problems is placed on persons or on systems differently in wealthy and poorer municipalities.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check archipelago households' answerability for boats, waste and rescue at sea.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check family firms and farms where a name answers for a business across generations; Swedish-speaking coast apart.
- **Tavastia and Savo:** check local memory of who answered for 1918's camps and executions.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check rural responsibility for the elderly where children have moved away.
- **Lapland:** check Skolt, Inari and North Sámi ideas of answering for land and waters, and herders' liability for reindeer on roads.
- **Åland:** check how blame is shared between island and state authorities after a failure.
- **Peoples:** check the Jewish community's reading of the 1942 apology; Kale-led sources on who answers for a kinsman's wrong; Karelian evacuees and Ingrian Finns on whom they hold answerable for their losses; Russian-speakers on collective blame since 2022; Somali-Finns on being made to answer as a group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political and war history; mixed for household responsibility; thin for Sámi stewardship sources in Finnish print, better through Sámi institutions.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for prisoner-of-war files; Skolt and North Sámi for stewardship accounts; the 1549 handbook's Finnish; Hebrew and Swedish for Jewish community records of 1942.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war camps and handovers touch living descendants; collective blame of minorities is charged now.
- **Held for review:** none; liability and the age of criminal responsibility as statutes stay with Libra.
