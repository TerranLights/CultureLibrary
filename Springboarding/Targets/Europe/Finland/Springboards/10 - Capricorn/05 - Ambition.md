# Finland: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** weigh whether startup and finance careers shape talk of ambition there unlike anywhere else.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** compare old merchant and academic families with newcomers who rose.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the emigrant and entrepreneur tradition, and the Swedish-speaking coast's family firms, separately.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check how rural youths' moves to towns were told as rising or as loss.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** trace how the young who left and those who stayed are spoken of.
- **Lapland:** check tourism and mining careers against herding, for Finnish and Sámi families apart.
- **Åland:** check shipping and ferry fortunes as the islands' own ladder.
- **Peoples:** check Kale aspirations in Kale-led writing on schooling and work; Somali-Finnish and Russian-speaking families' hopes for children; Ingrian Finn returnees whose qualifications went unused; Tatar merchant families' trades and their descendants' careers; Jewish families' routes from trade into the professions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mobility, education and election statistics; mixed for attitudes to ambition; thin for Kale and Tatar accounts from within.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Somali for migrant aspiration studies; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi for youth research; Finnish and Swedish for the corpora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ambition talk can slide into national-character claims about envy or modesty; treat these as claims to test.
- **Held for review:** none; whether leveling sayings belong here or under Aries, Competition, is a call for the author.
