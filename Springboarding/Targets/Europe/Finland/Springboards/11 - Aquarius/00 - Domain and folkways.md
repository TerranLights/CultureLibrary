*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** compare housing-estate ties with the old town core: is community made by place or by choice?
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check archipelago villages, where ferries set who meets whom.
- **Ostrobothnia:** count Finnish and Swedish parishes, and revival communities, separately.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** compare dispersed-farm parishes with manor districts for where people gathered.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check Orthodox village life and emptying villages.
- **Lapland:** North, Inari and Skolt Sámi as three communities; herding cooperatives apart from towns.
- **Åland:** check how island institutions draw the line of "us".
- **Peoples:** for Finland-Swedes, Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers, Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns, Karelian evacuees' descendants and Somali-Finns, check which bodies speak for each and what joining means. Finnic people on the Russian side only where needed; Sámi abroad, Estonians and emigrant Finns as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and association records; mixed for Russian-speaking and Somali groups; thin in print for Old Believer and Kale gatherings.
- **Languages needed:** association minutes come in Finnish and Swedish; Skolt village records need Skolt Sámi, with North and Inari Sámi for other *siida* matters; Somali and Russian serve the newer associations; the Sampo runes need archaic Finnish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi village and herding matters belong to Sámi bodies first; hall histories after 1918 carry one side's view. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled: the tracker carries Russia among its pre-war lands, read as Finnic and Karelian people there; no border is drawn. Association law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the author may decide whether the Lutheran confirmation rite is a national or a church unit.
