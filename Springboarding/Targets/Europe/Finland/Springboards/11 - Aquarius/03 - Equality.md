# Finland: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Finnish Kale Romani; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects. Which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check gaps between eastern and western districts of Helsinki and Espoo, where income and origin split.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** look at the archipelago's bilingual parishes as a test of language equality on the ground.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival congregations that oppose women clergy beside secular towns; Swedish Ostrobothnia separately.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how regional-policy promises are judged where they were aimed.
- **Tavastia:** check old manor parishes, where the memory of crofters shapes talk of equal standing.
- **Lapland:** check whether North, Inari and Skolt Sámi report equal access in different ways, and how Sámi and Finnish herders compare.
- **Åland:** check whether Swedish-only island life changes what "equal treatment" by mainland bodies means.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews, Old Believers and Ingrian return migrants: for each, check which forms of equality its own organizations ask for. Finland-Swedes in Ostrobothnia against the capital region. Sámi in Norway and Sweden, and Estonians: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gender, income and language surveys; mixed for minorities, with small samples; thin for Old Believers.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish matters as much as Finnish here, since language equality is itself a topic; each Sámi language for its own speakers' service claims; Russian and Somali for EU-MIDIS context; Kale Romani where Kale writers use it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination testimony names people and businesses; quote only published accounts. Equality and non-discrimination law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** one hiring correspondence-test study by a scholar of South Asian heritage, set aside; the author may add another source on hiring.
