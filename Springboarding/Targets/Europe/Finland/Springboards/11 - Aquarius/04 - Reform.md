*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check how ministries and the capital's press framed reforms that the regions then lived with.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** look at Turku as the seat of the early church and university, and whether its reform history is told apart from Helsinki's.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check temperance and revival parishes, and the Lapua movement's home ground, for who backed and who resisted each reform; Swedish towns separately.
- **Tavastia and the Tampere area:** trace the 1905 strike committees in Tampere and Hämeenlinna and which demands later became reforms.
- **Savo, North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how municipal mergers and hospital reforms were received where distances are long.
- **Lapland:** check the school reform's first years there; and Sámi reform from below (language nests) for each Sámi language on its own.
- **Åland:** check which mainland reforms never reached the islands, and why.
- **Peoples:** check Kale Roma, Sámi and Karelian Orthodox people for reforms done to them and by them; Finland-Swedes for language reforms as gain or loss; Russian-speakers for the Grand Duchy's reforms in memory; Tatars, Jews and Somali-Finns for how integration programs were felt. Estonians: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state reforms, temperance and education; thin for Kale and Old Believer views of reform, which need oral routes.
- **Languages needed:** committee Finnish and Swedish; Russian for Grand Duchy files; Inari and North Sámi for revival work; Kale Romani for mission testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eugenics and children's-home records hold personal data; use published accounts and survivors' testimony first. Laws that reforms produced go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** whether the 1918 war belongs here (reform failed) or under revolution in the domain is for the author.
