# Finland: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian and Karelian for the 1930s Soviet Karelia material; English for emigrant Finns' papers (comparison only). **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check student and NGO circles, where idealistic careers cluster, against suburban views of "world-improvers".
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the Naantali and Turku church past alongside today's Swedish- and Finnish-language student movements.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival piety as an ideal of right living, and the emigrant parishes that sent people to utopian colonies.
- **Tavastia and Tampere:** check the workers' movement's ideals in factory towns.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how the idealized "Karelia" of artists matched or missed the lives of Karelians.
- **Lapland:** check Sámi visions of the future in North, Inari and Skolt Sámi writing, each apart; Finnish settlers' ideals of the north as a separate thread.
- **Åland:** check the peace ideal in schools and public talk.
- **Peoples:** check Ingrian Finns and Karelians in the pre-war lands for the cost of the 1930s ideal; Finland-Swedes for the *ideell* tradition in associations; Jews and Tatars for ideals carried by congregations; Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers for ideals tied to homelands. Emigrant Finns and Estonians: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary groups, student movements and NGOs; mixed for Karelian fever (Russian archives); thin for minority ideals in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Russian and Karelian for Soviet Karelia; North Sámi for Valkeapää's later work; Oshiwambo and Namibian sources for the mission's other side.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the purges of Soviet Karelia hit families still living; mission records describe Namibians in colonial terms.
- **Held for review:** whether Lönnrot's *Kalevala* as a national ideal belongs here or under the domain's golden-age tales is for the author.
