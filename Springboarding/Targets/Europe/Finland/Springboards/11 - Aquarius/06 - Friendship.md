# Finland: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish (with youth slang); Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether students and newcomers in Helsinki describe making friends differently from people who grew up there.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** compare Swedish- and Finnish-medium school friendships in a bilingual city.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check whether revival-movement youth find friends mostly inside the congregation, and what happens to friendships when one leaves.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check hunting clubs and village sports clubs as places adult friendships last.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check friendships kept after young people move south, and the summer return.
- **Lapland:** check *verdde* ties and herding partnerships among North Sámi, and how Inari and Skolt Sámi name their own ties.
- **Åland:** check friendships kept across the sea with Sweden as well as with the mainland.
- **Peoples:** check Russian-speakers, whose language marks degrees of friendship, for how they map Finnish friendship words; Kale Roma for friendship across family lines; Tatars and Jews for friendships within small congregations; Somali-Finns for friendship and clan; Ingrian return migrants for friends left behind. Sámi in Norway and Sweden: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school surveys and volunteer programs; mixed for adult friendship, where Finnish research is spread thin; thin for minority friendship in print.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish youth slang; Swedish for Södergran and coastal sources; North Sámi for *verdde*; Russian for its finer friendship words; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's friendship books and school data are personal; use only archived and anonymized material. The 1944 friendship society touches Cold War loyalties still argued over.
- **Held for review:** none; whether wartime comradeship belongs here or under Group is for the author to settle.
