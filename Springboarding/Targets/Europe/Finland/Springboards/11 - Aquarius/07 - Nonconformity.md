## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check Kallio and the squat scene against the suburbs, to see where difference finds room.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the city's Swedish modernist past, and where in the southwest the 1730s dissenters lived.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival parishes, secular towns and Swedish coastal towns for what leaving or dressing differently costs in each.
- **Tavastia and Tampere:** check Pispala and the factory city's own outsiders.
- **Savo and North Karelia:** check eccentrics in rural lore; set outsider artists such as Rönkkönen (South Karelia) beside them.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check remote villages' tolerance for loners, and the joik's place in North, Inari and Skolt Sámi life as three stories.
- **Åland:** check whether island youth culture follows Sweden or the mainland.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma, Old Believers, Tatars, Jews, Russian-speakers and Somali-Finns: for each, check what counts as nonconformity inside the group, apart from the majority's view. Finland-Swedes in the avant-garde. Sámi in Norway and Sweden: comparison only.
