# Finland: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Russian; Somali; older Finnish and Swedish for sermons. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes. Cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, impartiality, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check living alone and low turnout in particular districts, by language and income.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check archipelago life, where solitude is part of the setting, against the city.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check whether revival meetings permit open display of feeling, and how that sits beside the reserve some writers claim for the region.
- **Savo:** check the talkative self-image often claimed for Savo as a test of any "reserve" claim.
- **North Karelia:** check Orthodox stillness and lament traditions against Lutheran composure at funerals.
- **Kainuu and Lapland:** check wilderness solitude, and North, Inari and Skolt Sámi accounts of reticence, each separately.
- **Åland:** check whether island reserve is described in Swedish terms.
- **Peoples:** Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns and Ingrian return migrants as observers of reserve; Kale Roma, for grieving and gathering customs as Kale writers describe them; Finland-Swedes for whether reserve is claimed or denied in their own self-image; Tatars and Jews for funeral composure. Sámi abroad: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics on living alone, cottages and turnout; mixed for emotional display, where much material repeats a stereotype; thin for Sámi and Kale views.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century Swedish and Finnish for the funeral sermons; modern Finnish and Swedish for statistics and film criticism; Russian and Somali for newcomers' testimony; Sámi-language sources, if any, on reticence.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "the silent Finn" is a common outsider and self-image claim; treat it as a claim to test. War-neurosis patient files are restricted; use published studies.
- **Held for review:** nothing was set aside; one open question for the author is where unwanted loneliness is filed, since this sheet treats chosen solitude.
