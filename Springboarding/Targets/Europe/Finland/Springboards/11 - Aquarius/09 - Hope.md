## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether young adults' hopes in the capital turn on housing and work more than elsewhere.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** look at the futures-studies community and the Swedish-speaking archipelago's hopes for staying put.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival-movement hope for the life to come beside emigrant parishes' hopes for a new land.
- **Tavastia and Savo:** check rural towns' hopes as young people leave.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check hope tied to the border, from trade to closures, as each period brought.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi hopes for herding, language and land separately; mining and tourism hopes in Finnish towns apart.
- **Åland:** check islanders' hopes for self-government and the sea economy.
- **Peoples:** Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns, Tatars and Jews: for each, check what hopes are spoken of for the next generation. Karelians and Finnic people in the pre-war lands where hope of return or contact arises. Emigrant Finns and Sámi abroad: comparison only.
