## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where street outreach, food aid and asylum volunteering concentrate, and how city residents argue about who is owed help.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the medieval charity record against the city's present welfare network, a long series in one place.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Finnish- and Swedish-speaking parishes for compassion toward animals and toward the poor in fur-farming communities.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check how village help for the needy is remembered in local histories and memory collections.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how evacuees from ceded Karelia were received, and what Orthodox parishes did for them.
- **Lapland:** check comfort and care in each Sámi language and how Sámi families experience care given in Finnish.
- **Åland:** check whether island charity runs through parish, municipality or family.
- **Peoples:** check Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Tatars, Jews, Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns and Old Believers each for their own channels of help and whether they are helped as strangers or as members; Sámi beyond the border and Estonia only for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charity history and press debate; mixed for self-compassion; thin for Kale, Old Believer and Sámi-language terms of mercy.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (Wrede's own circle wrote in Swedish); Sámi languages for SámiSoster; Russian, Tatar, Somali and Hebrew for community charity.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prisoners, refugees and the poor are the objects of pity here; their own accounts come first, and names in care records stay private. Pardons and sentencing belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing for this sheet; whether asylum-era online abuse counts as compassion's opposite or as fear is for the author.
