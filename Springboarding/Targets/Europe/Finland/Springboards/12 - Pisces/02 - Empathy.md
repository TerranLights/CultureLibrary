## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether empathy training, human-library events and therapy talk are mainly metropolitan.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the archipelago's small communities for how neighbors read each other with few words.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Swedish-language caring-science teaching in Vaasa against Finnish-language nursing schools nearby.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check the popular claim that Savo speech leaves the listener to work out the meaning, as a claim to examine for how much reading of others it asks, against Tavastian speech.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check whether evacuee families describe being understood by their hosts.
- **Lapland:** check Skolt, Inari and North Sámi viewers' and readers' responses separately, and how majority audiences meet Sámi stories.
- **Åland:** check islanders' accounts of being understood by mainlanders, in Swedish.
- **Peoples:** check Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers (before and after 2022), Ingrian returnees, Tatars and Jews for whom each feels understood by, and who speaks for them; Estonia and Sámi across the border only for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology and care research; mixed for everyday words and sanctions; thin for minority perspectives, where life stories are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (Eriksson's school writes mainly in Swedish); Skolt and North Sámi for film and reception; Russian and Somali for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memories and hate-speech targets are survivors and victims; their testimony comes first and must not be reproduced carelessly. Hate-speech statutes and their cases go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** empty for this sheet; one filing question for the author: is "reading the situation" empathy or intuition?
