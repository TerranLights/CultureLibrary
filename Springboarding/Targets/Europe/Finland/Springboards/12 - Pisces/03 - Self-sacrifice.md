*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether "self-care" talk has displaced sacrifice in younger, urban speech more than elsewhere.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the Turku area's war cemeteries and parish books for how sacrifice was worded.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check Finnish- and Swedish-speaking parishes' hero graves separately, and the region's emigrant families' sacrifices.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check farm wives' memoirs for who gave up what so the farm would go on.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check the Viena passion songs' source villages and the Orthodox view of sacrifice beside Lutheran sermons.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi war memory separately, and Finnish-speaking Lapland's 1944 losses.
- **Åland:** check islanders' accounts of service outside the demilitarized islands.
- **Peoples:** check Tatars, Jews, Kale Roma and Ingrian Finns for how each remembers its war dead, and Somali-Finns and Russian-speakers for the migrant parent's sacrifice; Estonians and emigrant Finns only as outside comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war death and memory; mixed for everyday family sacrifice; thin for minority memory, where community histories carry it.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish; Karelian and archaic Finnic for the passion songs; North Sámi; Tatar and Russian for community accounts; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war deaths, widowhood, organ donation and hunger strikes touch grief and bodily harm; suicide framed as sacrifice must be approached only as a topic for careful later research. Conscription and military-service rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none under the standing rules; hero-grave customs may later move to death if the author prefers.
