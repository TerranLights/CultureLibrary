## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check whether the personal-space claim holds in dense housing, and how newcomers read it.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check archipelago neighbors' customs of landing and passing on another's shore.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the Finnish-Swedish language line village by village, and fenced farmyards on the plain.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check old *pihapiiri* layouts in open-air and village museums for where a visitor stopped.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check border villages on the newly closed line, and their memories of the 1940s border.
- **Lapland:** check North and Inari Sámi sacred-place boundaries and herding lines separately from Finnish-speaking villages.
- **Åland:** check how islanders draw the line between Ålanders and mainlanders, as a question rather than a given.
- **Peoples:** check Kale Roma on respect and avoidance, Somali-Finns and Tatars on family privacy, Russian-speakers and Karelians on the eastern border, and Jews on the community's own lines; the Swedish and Norwegian sides of Sámi lands only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for border history and workplace surveys; mixed for privacy and touch; thin for Kale and Tatar ideas of family boundaries, where community-led research is the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (the language line itself); North and Inari Sámi; Kale Romani; Russian and Karelian for the east; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi sacred places are restricted, and their exact locations and rules come from Sámi sources; Kale purity and avoidance customs are community knowledge; harassment cases involve victims. Trespass, domestic-peace and border statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
