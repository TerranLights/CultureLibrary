# Finland: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish (with older literary Finnish); Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian and Church Slavonic; Somali. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where therapy-based acceptance and decluttering talk spread first, and among whom.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the old Turku printing of sermons on patience and their parish use.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check revival preaching on yielding to God among Finnish- and Swedish-speaking congregations.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check how the Tampere surrender of 1918 is told in Tavastian families on both sides.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check Orthodox humility in chapel life and evacuees' words for the land given up.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi sayings and life stories separately for what is accepted and what is not.
- **Åland:** check Bomarsund's memory and how islanders relate it to their autonomy.
- **Peoples:** check Somali-Finns, Tatars, Russian-speakers, Ingrian Finns, Kale Roma and Jews for how each speaks of accepting loss and illness, recording outsiders' claims as claims; Estonia only for comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war history and therapy and care literature; mixed for everyday resignation; thin for Sámi and Kale sources.
- **Languages needed:** old literary Finnish for Sorolainen; Swedish for Colliander and Åland; North Sámi; Russian and Church Slavonic; Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life decisions, abusive marriage and suicide framed as surrender need careful handling and survivors' and families' voices first. Advance-directive and end-of-life law, and terms of capitulation, go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; whether *sisu* belongs here as surrender's opposite or stays with courage is for the author.
