- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi herders' knowledge separately, with Sámi consent on what can be recorded.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology and professional research; mixed for folk knowledge of land and weather; thin for minority words for intuition.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish in professional and slang registers (for *mutu* and tacit knowledge) and Swedish for the coastal memory collections; North and Inari Sámi for *árbediehtu*; Russian and Somali for community words for a hunch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi traditional knowledge is partly restricted and governed by Sámi bodies; psychic services touch the bereaved; gut judgments of strangers can carry bias, to be studied as such. Rules on police discretion go to Libra and Capricorn.
