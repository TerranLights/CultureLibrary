# Finland: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North Sámi; Russian; Somali. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. Taken here as a moral and social category; statutes, courts and punishment rules belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit) and are only pointed to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check where the gang debate and the migrant-crime framing concentrate, and who speaks back.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check how the press of the old court town treated local scandals against national ones.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check the long life of the region's killer legends and unsolved cases, in Finnish- and Swedish-speaking areas separately.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check rural tolerance of off-the-books work and small wrongs as a question for interviews.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check how wartime and postwar black-market trading is remembered: as wrongdoing or as getting by.
- **Lapland:** check reindeer theft as North, Inari and Skolt Sámi and Finnish herders each discuss it.
- **Åland:** check islanders' view of smuggling and tax-free trade as wrongs or as custom.
- **Peoples:** check Kale Roma, Somali-Finns, Russian-speakers, Tatars, Jews and Ingrian Finns for how each is named or left unnamed in crime talk, and their own words for wrongdoing; Sámi across the border and Estonia only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, press and crime fiction; mixed for folk views of wrongdoers; thin for small communities' internal handling of wrongs.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish, including slang and the 1980s press language of scandal, and Swedish for *Hufvudstadsbladet*; North Sámi for herding-district talk of theft; Russian and Somali for the media-framing studies and community replies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** victims of violence and their families, named suspects and minority groups linked to crime in the press need care; no group trait is to be inferred from coverage. All criminal law, courts, sentencing and prisons go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the line between crime as wrong and crime as legal category is left with the author's ruling.
