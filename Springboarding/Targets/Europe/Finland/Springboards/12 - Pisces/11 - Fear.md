# Finland: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Finland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Finnish; Finland-Swedish; North, Inari and Skolt Sámi; Karelian; Russian; Somali; Finnish Kale Romani. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uusimaa and the capital region:** check fear of the night city against measured risk, and newcomers' fears beside long-time residents'.
- **Finland Proper and Turku:** check the wolf memory of 1880 and the Onkalo debate in nearby Satakunta as two kinds of dread.
- **Ostrobothnia:** check fright charms and hellfire preaching, taking the Swedish coast and the Finnish inland one at a time.
- **Savo and Tavastia:** check lake and forest dangers in local talk and drowning warnings.
- **North Karelia and Kainuu:** check wolf fear and fear of the eastern border, and whether the two are spoken of together.
- **Lapland:** check North, Inari and Skolt Sámi herders' fears separately, predators and Chernobyl's mark on reindeer among them.
- **Åland:** check whether the islands' demilitarized status changes how threat is felt.
- **Peoples:** check Russian-speakers, Somali-Finns, Kale Roma, Tatars, Jews and Ingrian Finns both for what each fears and for how each has been feared, without assigning fear to any group; Sámi across the border and Estonia only as comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for polls and preparedness material; mixed for everyday worry; thin for minority communities' fears, where their own bodies are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Swedish (Holmström writes in Swedish); North, Inari and Skolt Sámi for herders' own accounts; Karelian for eastern fright lore; Russian, Somali and Kale Romani for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anxiety and fear in children, religious trauma, and fear felt by or directed at minorities need care; Sámi herders' fears are to be taken from Sámi accounts first. Civil-defense and emergency-powers law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no pointer was dropped under the standing rules; the author may want fear of the dead filed here or with death.
