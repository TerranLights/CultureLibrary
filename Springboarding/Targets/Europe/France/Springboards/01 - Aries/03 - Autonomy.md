## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Corsica:** check debates on island self-government in Corsican-language and French-language press separately.
- **Brittany:** check the *Emsav* movement's history and how its aims shifted after 1945.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check the 1920s autonomist movement and present calls for regional self-rule.
- **Occitania and the Cévennes:** check religious conscience (Protestant memory) and rural self-reliance movements as two separate threads.
- **Burgundy, the Massif Central and the Nord:** check company towns (Le Creusot, Michelin's Clermont-Ferrand, the mining companies) for the reach of employer control over workers' lives.
- **Wallonia and Luxembourg:** check Walloon self-rule inside federal Belgium, and steel-town paternalism in southern Luxembourg.
- **Maghrebi-French, Portuguese-French and Vietnamese-French families:** check who chooses a spouse, a course of study and a home, using TeO's union and family modules (*check*).
- **The young, the old and disabled people:** check age at leaving home by region, and elders' and disabled people's own groups on keeping control of their care.
- **Antilleans in the metropole:** check how talk of self-rule for the overseas departments enters their community press (pointing to the departments only where needed).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on work, youth and care through national surveys; mixed on family decision-making by origin; thin on Travelers' and smaller communities' views.
- **Languages needed:** French; Corsican and Breton for autonomist sources; Luxembourgish and German for Luxembourg; Kabyle and Arabic for family-choice research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bodily autonomy questions (abortion, end of life, gender) and regional nationalism (including armed Corsican and Breton groups) are charged; the law on each belongs to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** studies of family decision-making among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the TeO comparison.
