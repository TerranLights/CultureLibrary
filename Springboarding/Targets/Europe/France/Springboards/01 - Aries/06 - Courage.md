# France: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with its medieval forms); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Walloon, Alsatian; Arabic, Wolof, Antillean Creole and Armenian for communities. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Nord, Picardy and Wallonia:** check how mining communities remember rescuers and the dead, and whose names appear on memorials.
- **Brittany:** check lifeboat crews, sea widows' memorials and Breton-language songs of the sea.
- **The Alps:** check guides' and rescue teams' accounts and the place of risk in mountain towns.
- **The Massif Central and the Cévennes:** check Protestant villages' sheltering records as moral courage.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check how the *Malgré-nous* and refusers of German conscription are remembered.
- **Luxembourg:** check the 1942 strike memory and families of those deported.
- **Armenian-French and Jewish communities:** check foreign-born resisters and community memorials.
- **West African-French and Maghrebi-French families:** check *tirailleurs* and *Armée d'Afrique* memory and how it is told at home.
- **Harkis:** check how their service is honored or disputed.
- **Women:** check whether medals and memorials record women's endurance and resistance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war, resistance and medals; mixed on civilian everyday courage; thin on smaller communities.
- **Languages needed:** French, including Old and Middle French; Luxembourgish and German; Breton for sea songs; Arabic and Wolof for veterans' families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** colonial wars, collaboration, the Harkis and the *tirailleurs* are contested memory; personal war files may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** war memory and service records of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the colonial-troops pointer.
