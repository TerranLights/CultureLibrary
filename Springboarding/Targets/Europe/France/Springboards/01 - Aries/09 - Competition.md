## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check the concentration of *classes préparatoires* and who reaches them, by origin and region.
- **The Nord, Picardy and Wallonia:** check archery companies, pigeon racing and *balle pelote* as old local contests on both sides of the border.
- **Brittany:** check *gouren* and regattas, and the federations' membership by area.
- **Languedoc and Provence:** check water jousting and pétanque clubs as town against town.
- **Aquitaine and Occitania:** check rugby rivalries between small towns.
- **The Lyon area:** check the Lyon and Saint-Étienne football rivalry and its industrial roots.
- **Luxembourg:** check how school tracking is lived in each language group.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French youth:** check football academies and suburban clubs as routes; avoid assuming talent or aim.
- **Vietnamese-French, the Chinese of Paris and Portuguese-French families:** check school results and aspirations in survey data, not in reputation.
- **Rural farmers and craftsmen:** check agricultural and trade contests as status inside the trade.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on exams, sport and trade contests; mixed on envy and everyday rivalry; thin on small regional games outside their federations.
- **Languages needed:** French, including Old French; Breton; Picard and Walloon for archery and pigeon records; Luxembourgish and German; Chinese and Vietnamese for community school data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** school results by origin are contested; doping and match-fixing records involve personal data.
- **Held for review:** school-path data on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the educational-striving pointer.
