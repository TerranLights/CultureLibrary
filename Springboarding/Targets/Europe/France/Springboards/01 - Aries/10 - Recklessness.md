# France: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (Old French for the epics); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian; Arabic for heritage-community sources. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Alps and the Pyrenees:** check rescue statistics by resort and the place of risk in mountain-town life.
- **Provence and the Camargue; the Landes and Aquitaine:** check bull games as local rites, and who takes part, by age and town.
- **Occitania (Nîmes, Béziers):** check bullfighting's local defenders against its national critics.
- **Brittany and the Atlantic coast:** check offshore racing and fishing deaths as two distinct risk cultures.
- **Paris and Île-de-France suburbs:** check *rodéo urbain* coverage against residents' own accounts; press framing may distort.
- **Wallonia and Luxembourg:** check motor-sport culture around Spa, and road deaths among cross-border commuters.
- **Corsica:** check road-death and rally data by department before any reading.
- **Fairground (*forains*) and circus families, some of them Travelers:** check memoirs of stunt and high-wire work; sources thin.
- **Maghrebi-French, West African-French and other heritage youth:** check whether youth-risk surveys break results down by origin at all; draw nothing where they do not.
- **Young men against young women:** check ESCAPAD and road-safety data by sex and region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in health, road and mountain statistics; mixed in cultural meaning of risk; thin in community and dialect sources.
- **Languages needed:** French, including Old French; Occitan for bull-game lore; Breton for sea lore; Luxembourgish and German.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bullfighting and animal welfare; urban youth and policing; deaths in sport; addiction data. The rules on any of these belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held back from the youth-risk comparison.
