# France: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican, Picard, Walloon; Maghrebi Arabic and Kabyle for heritage communities. **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how it is held: possessions, money, resources, food, dress and wealth.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France against rural France:** check whether goods and food budgets differ more by place or by class.
- **Brittany, Alsace and Provence:** check what each region's folk collectors chose to keep, and why.
- **The Nord, Picardy and Wallonia:** check the mining and textile basins' company stores and street sales across the border.
- **Occitania and the Massif Central:** check the family house (*ostal*) passed whole against equal shares (inheritance rules: Libra).
- **Corsica:** check undivided family land and the chestnut economy in the archives.
- **Luxembourg:** check how finance and the steel past each show in household budgets.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Harkis:** check what was carried and what was lost in 1962.
- **Maghrebi-French, West African-French and Portuguese-French families:** check money sent home and houses built in the home village.
- **Jewish communities:** check the record of goods seized under Vichy, and claims.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the caravan as home and holding; sources are thin.
- **Antilleans:** check the 2009 cost-of-living protests (overseas departments, pointed to where needed).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (surveys, notarial archives, museums); mixed for Wallonia and Luxembourg; thin for Travelers.
- **Scope:** which lands the nation will hold is not yet settled; the Taurus sheets cover metropolitan France with Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg, and point to Flanders, Roussillon and the French Basque Country only for contrast.
- **Languages needed:** French; Middle French for the manuals; Luxembourgish and German; Occitan for the *ostal*; Arabic and Kabyle for remittance studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent wealth records name living families; spoliation files are partly restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose goods, food and dress would otherwise sit in section 3.
