# France: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle, Wolof and Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany and Occitania:** check memories of punishment for speaking Breton or Occitan at school, and whether revival movements speak of restored worth.
- **The Nord and Lorraine mining and steel towns:** check what men and women said they lost when pits and mills closed.
- **Wallonia:** check the Fêtes de Wallonie and Walloon industrial decline for collective worth as people voice it.
- **Luxembourg:** check how cross-border workers and residents describe their standing in a wealthy country.
- **Corsica:** check talk of dignity and respect in island politics, as separate from the island's honor and feud literature.
- **Maghrebi-French and Kabyle-French families:** check the language of *karāma* and *nif* across generations, and how school and work enter it.
- **Harkis:** check the national day of homage (25 September) and how the families respond to it.
- **West African-French and Antilleans:** check how worth is spoken of in community media and in Creole and Wolof.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check their own accounts of stigma; few are published.
- **Older people and the unemployed in every region:** check whether worth after work differs by region and trade.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (sociology of shame, work and unemployment; suicide data; autobiographical literature); mixed for Luxembourg; thin for Travelers and newer West African-French communities.
- **Languages needed:** French; Occitan and Breton for language-shame memories; Arabic, Kabyle and Wolof for heritage-family terms; Creole for Antillean sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and mental-health records; race-based worth is charged, and French statistics rarely record origin.
- **Held for review:** writers and studies on self-worth among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
