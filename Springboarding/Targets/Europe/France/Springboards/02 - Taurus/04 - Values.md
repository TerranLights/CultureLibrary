# France: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish and German; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian; Arabic, Armenian and Vietnamese for faith-community sources. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany and the west against the Center and Limousin:** check whether old maps of religious practice still divide survey answers.
- **The Cévennes and other Protestant areas:** check Protestant teaching and memory as a separate source of values.
- **Alsace and Moselle:** check how their state-funded religion classes shape what children are taught.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check the Catholic, socialist and secular pillars, each with its own schools, unions and health funds.
- **Luxembourg:** check how foreign residents, Portuguese-Luxembourgers among them, enter the survey samples.
- **Corsica:** check family and village loyalty in island writing, beside the republican vocabulary.
- **Paris and the large cities against small towns:** check the urban-rural gap in values surveys.
- **Jewish, Muslim, Armenian Apostolic and Buddhist communities:** check each one's own teaching texts, not outsiders' accounts.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French young people:** check where they place family, faith and work, using their own words in qualitative studies.
- **The Chinese of Paris and Vietnamese-French families:** check values taught through associations and language schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (surveys, school texts, speech archives); mixed for Luxembourg; thin for smaller faith communities' own teaching.
- **Languages needed:** French; German and Luxembourgish for Luxembourg's courses; Arabic and Armenian for faith texts; Vietnamese and Chinese for association materials; Occitan and Breton for proverb collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *laïcité*, Islam and "republican values" are politically charged; studies of origin-based differences must be read with care, since French data rarely record origin or religion.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and their faith groups' value teaching.
