# France: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Normandy:** check the surviving *Charités* and who joins them today.
- **The Nord, Lorraine and Wallonia's old industrial basins:** check miners' and steelworkers' own funds, and what replaced them when the works closed.
- **Paris and its suburbs:** check fear of crime and job insecurity by district in survey breakdowns.
- **The Massif Central and Brittany:** check farmers' mutual insurers and credit as a rural safety net.
- **The Alps and Provence:** check avalanche, flood and fire risk and how households insure against them.
- **Luxembourg:** check cross-border workers' coverage, which links three countries' systems.
- **Corsica:** check how island households speak of property safety and of security in daily life, keeping the two senses apart.
- **Soninke and other West African-French hostel residents:** check village funds and repatriation insurance.
- **Wenzhou-origin traders of Paris and Vietnamese-French households:** check who a tontine or family loan covers when banks refuse.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Harkis:** check how resettlement after 1962 shaped later saving and housing choices.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check access to insurance and banking; little is published.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (social security history, savings data, surveys, protest archives); mixed for informal community insurance; thin for Travelers.
- **Languages needed:** French; German and Luxembourgish for Luxembourg's funds; Soninke, Wolof and Arabic for migrants' own funds; Chinese and Vietnamese for family credit; Norman French terms for the *Charités*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "insécurité" is a loaded political word, and data on crime by origin is contested; benefit and health records are restricted.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and their mutual-aid and savings groups.
