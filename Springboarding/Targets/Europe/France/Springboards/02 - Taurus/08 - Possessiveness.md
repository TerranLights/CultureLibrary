# France: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Occitan, Provençal, Breton, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic and Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Occitania and Provence:** check the troubadour and *charivari* record against present attitudes to jealous partners.
- **Quercy, Périgord and the southern hills:** check secret truffle and mushroom grounds and how pickers speak of them.
- **Brittany and Normandy coasts:** check fishing-ground quarrels between harbors and with outside boats, in the maritime press.
- **Corsica:** check how island courts and press frame jealousy in couples, as a topic separate from vendetta writing.
- **Paris and the large cities against rural communes:** check whether the control items in VIRAGE differ by town size.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Belgian survey wording on control, which may not match the French.
- **Luxembourg:** check partner-control data across its several language groups.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how *ghīra* is discussed by young people themselves, without assuming its meaning.
- **Antilleans:** check Creole songs and radio talk on jealousy (overseas sources only where needed).
- **Portuguese-French and Vietnamese-French families:** check parents' holding on to adult children, in qualitative family studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (literature, surveys, femicide counts, *charivari* studies); mixed for Belgium and Luxembourg; thin for territorial foraging and for most heritage communities.
- **Languages needed:** French; Old Occitan for *Flamenca*; Provençal for *prud'homies*; Arabic for *ghīra*; Creole for song sources; Luxembourgish for local services.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence and femicide data concern victims and families; ethnic framing of jealousy is a stereotype risk and must be avoided; criminal law on control belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose family-control studies would otherwise be compared here.
