## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check Plogoff and later local refusals, and how the phrase *tête de Breton* is used, as a label to study rather than a trait.
- **Occitania and the Cévennes:** check Cathar and Protestant memory of refusal and how heritage tourism tells it.
- **Lorraine and the Nord:** check steel and mining strikes as long refusals, and how former strikers judge them now.
- **The Vendée and the west:** check the memory of the 1793 risings, contested between local and republican tellings.
- **Provence:** check water and land quarrels in villages, in fiction and in local court reporting.
- **Corsica:** check refusals of state projects, separate from the vendetta literature.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check the Fourons and other language disputes as holdouts.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the 1941 refusal is taught in schools today.
- **Jewish communities:** check the Dreyfus Affair's memory and the long wait for state recognition.
- **Harkis:** check the families' decades-long campaigns for recognition.
- **Maghrebi-French young people:** check how they speak of holding firm against discrimination, in their own media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (protest history, literature, comics, apology texts); mixed for Luxembourg; thin for personal stubbornness in heritage families.
- **Languages needed:** French; Occitan for Cathar and Protestant memory; Breton for Plogoff accounts; Dutch for the Flemish side of the Fourons (comparison only); Luxembourgish and German for 1941.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** regional "stubbornness" labels (Breton, Auvergnat and others) are stereotypes; study them as labels only. Religious refusal and wartime memory are painful topics.
