# France: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle, Wolof and Romani. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Burgundy and the Bordeaux country:** check wine festivals and estate tourism as staged pleasure.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check scent, markets and outdoor eating in regional writing and festivals.
- **Brittany:** check *fest-noz* dance and seaside food against the region's religious calendar of feasts and fasts.
- **The Nord and Wallonia:** check carnival excess, beer and *ducasse* fairs across the border.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check winter market foods and *winstub* culture as their own sensory record.
- **The Massif Central:** check *bal musette* migrants to Paris and the spa towns of Auvergne.
- **Luxembourg:** check Moselle wine festivals and Mondorf's spa guests.
- **Corsica:** check *polyphonies* and island food as pleasures named in Corsican.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check the hammam and Ramadan evening meals as sensory gatherings.
- **West African-French and Antilleans:** check dance, music and cooking in community festivals (overseas sources only where needed).
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check music gatherings and pilgrimages (Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer) as their own record.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese Parisian households:** check which festival dishes, smells and textures of home cooking are kept, adapted or dropped across generations in the 13th arrondissement and Belleville.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (gastronomy, spa history, festivals, literature); mixed for touch and smell; thin for some heritage communities' sensory life outside festivals.
- **Languages needed:** French; Middle French for *Le Viandier*; Breton for *fest-noz*; Corsican for polyphony; Arabic and Kabyle for hammam studies; Romani for Manouche music sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the foreign image of "French sensuality" is a stereotype; test it against the data, never repeat it. Sexuality belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose food and music festivals would otherwise be compared here.
