## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lower Brittany against the Gallo-speaking east:** check whether language loss differs across the old Breton-Gallo line.
- **Alsace and German-speaking Moselle:** check home speech after 1945 and the bilingual-school movement since.
- **Corsica:** check Corsican, Italian and French in school, church and family across three generations.
- **Occitania, Provence and Aquitaine:** check Calandreta families against neighbors who never spoke Occitan.
- **Picardy and Wallonia:** check dialect puppet theater (Amiens's Lafleur, Liège's Tchantchès) as a public stage for regional speech.
- **Brussels against Walloon towns:** check mixed French-Dutch street speech; Flanders only as comparison.
- **Luxembourg:** check which language each generation uses at school, in shops and online.
- **Paris's outer commuter belt against the city:** check whether long daily journeys thin out neighboring.
- **Savoy and the Lyon area:** check where Franco-Provençal is still passed on, if anywhere.
- **Maghrebi-French and Kabyle-French families:** check which language each generation uses with the others.
- **Deaf communities:** check LSF and LSFB clubs and schools as a language group of their own.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check Romani varieties at home and the schooling of traveling children; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language history, literacy and travel; mixed for neighboring; thin for Franco-Provençal, Travelers and early Deaf records.
- **Scope:** the author has not yet drawn the nation's borders. This sign covers metropolitan France with Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg; Flanders, the French Basque Country and Northern Catalonia are comparison only; the overseas departments enter only where a concept needs them.
- **Languages needed:** French from the Carolingian oaths onward; each regional language for atlases and vitality surveys; Luxembourgish and German for dictionary work; LSF for Deaf-made material; Darija and Kabyle for home-language studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *patois* label, school punishment for regional speech and the oralist ban on signing are painful topics; language law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose home languages would otherwise join the transmission comparison.
