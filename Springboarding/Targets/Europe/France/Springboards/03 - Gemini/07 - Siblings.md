# France: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with slang); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle; Réunion Creole where the overseas departments are needed. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gascony and Béarn:** check how younger sons who did not inherit are remembered in family histories and songs.
- **Normandy and the Paris basin:** check how co-heirs speak of the family house where equal shares were the old custom.
- **Brittany:** check memoirs of large farm families for the older sister's load.
- **The Ardennes and Wallonia:** check how the four Aymon brothers are staged at Bogny-sur-Meuse, Dinant and in Liège puppet plays.
- **Corsica:** check sisters' place among the mourners in the *voceru* tradition.
- **Luxembourg:** check household surveys for sibling numbers by nationality; sources may be thin.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check the eldest son's and eldest daughter's roles as they describe them, and how they shift in France.
- **Kabyle-French families:** check the brother-sister tie after a sister marries, in family members' own words.
- **West African-French families:** check how children of one father and different mothers name and treat one another.
- **Réunionese of the Creuse** (overseas, where needed): check separated siblings' searches.
- **Jewish families:** check siblings hidden apart in 1942-1944 and later reunions.
- **Blended families:** check step-siblings' terms and living arrangements by region.
- **Suburban young people:** check *frère* and *reuf* as address for friends, and their limits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family demography, inheritance custom and literature; mixed for adult sibling ties outside SHARE; thin for Luxembourg and for half-sibling ties in polygamous households.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for the Aymon cycle; Occitan for Gascon cadet songs; Corsican for *voceri*; Arabic and Kabyle for family terms; Réunion Creole for the Creuse testimonies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Creuse transfers, wartime hiding and polygamy are painful or politically charged; adoption and foster-care files are closed; inheritance law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose sibling roles and terms would otherwise be compared here.
