# France: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish and German; Breton, Corsican, Occitan; Arabic, Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between (broker, interpreter, intercessor).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lower Brittany:** check when the *bazvalan* disappears from collectors' records and what replaced him.
- **Corsica:** check family memory of *paceri*, apart from literary vendetta images.
- **Kabyle-French associations:** check whether a village-assembly style of settling disputes is recreated in France, in members' accounts.
- **Neighborhoods with *femmes-relais*:** check the mediators' own accounts of whom they serve and how.
- **Muslim families:** check whether elders or imams act as go-betweens in marriage quarrels, in qualitative studies only.
- **Provence and Marseille:** check ex-voto requests by period as a record of asking a saint to intercede.
- **Strasbourg and the Ortenau:** check cross-border bodies as standing go-betweens on the Rhine.
- **The Nord and Lorraine industrial basins:** check who brokered strike settlements (prefects, bishops, mayors).
- **Brussels and Walloon cities:** check school-mediator caseloads by commune.
- **Luxembourg:** check how cross-border workers, who cannot vote, view the tripartite.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check departmental mediators for *gens du voyage* against Travelers' own accounts; sources thin.
- **Burgundy and Taizé's visitors:** check who comes to reconciliation gatherings, by age and country.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church history, labor talks and mediation literature; mixed for social mediation (program reports); thin for folk go-betweens after 1900 and for Travelers.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old French for the Peace of God; Breton for *bazvalan* texts; Corsican for feud settlements; Luxembourgish and German for tripartite records; Arabic and Kabyle for community settlement.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** court-annexed and family mediation belong to Libra; mediation in domestic-violence cases is contested; studies of religious go-betweens risk stereotype and must stay with participants' accounts.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose elders and associations as go-betweens would otherwise be compared here.
