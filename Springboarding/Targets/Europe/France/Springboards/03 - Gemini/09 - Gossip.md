# France: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provence:** check how *chambrette* members describe what they talk about, against outsiders' labels.
- **The Mayenne Bocage and the west:** check how villagers speak now of words that once harmed.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check how the evening spinning gatherings gave way to other women's meeting places.
- **Corsica:** check how islanders write of village talk and its pressure, in their own memoirs.
- **Provincial cities:** check later rumor outbreaks of the Orléans type, city by city.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check how Belgian weeklies cover the royal family, against French coverage of public figures.
- **Luxembourg:** check how satire names people in a small society; sources thin.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check reputation talk around young women, as young women themselves describe it.
- **Antilleans** (overseas, where needed): check *milan* and call-in radio.
- **Jewish families of Orléans:** check how the 1969 rumor is remembered by the shopkeepers' families.
- **Office workers:** check *radio-moquette* in workplace studies.
- **Villages against cities:** check whether fear of the *qu'en-dira-t-on* differs by commune size in survey or interview data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for rumor history, denunciation and the celebrity press; mixed for everyday gossip (mostly ethnography); thin for Luxembourg and for heritage families' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the *Évangiles* and the *Caquets*; Provençal for *chambrette* sources; Luxembourgish for satire; Darija for reputation talk; Creole for island radio.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** denunciation files of 1940-1944 name people; antisemitic rumor must be read as evidence of prejudice; studies of gendered reputation risk stereotype; defamation law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose reputation talk would otherwise be compared here.
