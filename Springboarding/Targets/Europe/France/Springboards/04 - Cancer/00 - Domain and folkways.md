# France: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican, Picard, Walloon; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** what home is and who counts as family: households, house forms, fathering and child-rearing, roots, hospitality and the family's memory.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Paris basin against Béarn, Gascony and the Rouergue:** check whether married heirs stayed under the parents' roof.
- **Brittany:** check ATP farm layouts for shared rooms and box beds (*lits clos*).
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check courtyard farmhouses and the half-timbered village as family space.
- **Corsica:** check the village house and the shuttle between village and town, by generation.
- **The Massif Central:** check the farmhouse with byre, and the *buron* as a seasonal home.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check rowhouse districts of the Liège and Charleroi coal towns against Brussels apartments.
- **Luxembourg:** check how households with foreign-born members are counted.
- **Maghrebi-French and Kabyle-French families:** check household size across generations and ties to a house in the home village.
- **West African-French families:** check households in workers' hostels (*foyers*) against family housing after reunification.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese families of Paris:** check grandparents' place in the household in their own accounts.
- **Antilleans and Réunionese** (overseas departments, where needed): check the *case créole* and yard as home.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the caravan and the family plot as home; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics, house types and family history; mixed for fathering before 1970; thin for Travelers and hostel residents.
- **Lands:** the post-war borders are open, so this sign is written for metropolitan France, Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg; Flanders, the French Basque Country and Northern Catalonia appear only as comparison, and the overseas departments only where a concept needs them.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for *livres de raison*; Breton and Alsatian for house terms in the ATP corpus; Luxembourgish for census forms; Darija and Kabyle for home-village ties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** household and housing records near the present name families; the old head-of-household category is a gendered artifact to read as such; family law sits in Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose house layouts and joint households would otherwise join the household comparison.
