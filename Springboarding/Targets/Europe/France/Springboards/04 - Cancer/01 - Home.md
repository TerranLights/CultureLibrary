# France: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Corsican, Occitan, Breton, Alsatian; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle; Portuguese for the Portuguese-French. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris apartment dwellers against owners of periurban houses:** check what each says a home must have, room by room.
- **The Nord's mining cities:** check whether residents of former company housing describe it as home or as the company's.
- **Brittany's coast:** check press and council debates on second homes against locals' housing.
- **Provence and the Côte d'Azur:** check the village house turned holiday rental, and residents' complaints.
- **The Alps and the Lyon area:** check chalet seasonality and empty off-season villages.
- **Corsica:** check the *paese* as the home one returns to from the towns and the mainland.
- **Alsace:** check the stove room in older houses and in memories of childhood.
- **Brussels:** check the *kot* and shared housing among students and young workers.
- **Luxembourg:** check workers who live in Lorraine or Wallonia because of home prices.
- **Portuguese-French families:** check houses built in home villages for retirement or summers.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check summer trips to the *bled* and the house kept there.
- **Armenian-French families of Décines and Marseille:** check the first family house after the camps and hostels of the 1920s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics, architecture and literature; mixed for the feel of home; thin for heritage families' houses abroad.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the 1328 count and du Bellay; Alsatian for house terms; Corsican for village sources; Portuguese and Kabyle for houses in home villages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eviction and poor housing touch living tenants; *nostalgérie* is tied to the Algerian war's memory battles; rules on renting and eviction belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose houses kept in home towns would sit beside the Portuguese and Maghrebi checks.
