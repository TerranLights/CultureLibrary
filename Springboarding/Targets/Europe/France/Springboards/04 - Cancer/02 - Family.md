*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pays bigouden and inland Brittany:** check whether kin help still runs along the lines Segalen mapped.
- **Lille, Roubaix and Tourcoing:** check how textile and retail families keep firms in the family.
- **The Aveyron and the Auvergne's emigrants to Paris:** check kin chains in Paris cafés and coal trades.
- **Alsace:** check Protestant and Catholic villages for family size and marriage across the line.
- **Corsica:** check how local politics uses the word *clan* and whether kin and political networks overlap.
- **Lyon's old industrial families:** check who keeps the family's papers and seats in firms.
- **Wallonia:** check the Ligue des familles' surveys against Walloon municipal family services.
- **Luxembourg:** check PSELL for kin help among Portuguese, Cape Verdean and native-born households.
- **Kabyle-French families:** check *tawacult* in memoirs and what extended kin still expect across the sea.
- **West African-French families:** check children raised by kin other than parents (*confiage*), in family accounts.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check the ancestor altar and who keeps it after a parent dies.
- **Jewish families:** check genealogy as reconstruction after 1942-1944.
- **Same-sex parent families:** check APGL's accounts against census counts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kinship history, family support and genealogy; mixed for Luxembourg and Wallonia's own family studies; thin for foster kin among newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Lambert of Ardres; Alsatian and Breton for kin terms; Luxembourgish, Portuguese and Cape Verdean Creole for the panel's households; Kabyle and Vietnamese for family memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genealogy of deported families and adoption records touch the living; *clan* is a charged word in Corsican politics; family rights and benefits belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose joint-family arrangements would otherwise sit beside the Kabyle and West African checks.
