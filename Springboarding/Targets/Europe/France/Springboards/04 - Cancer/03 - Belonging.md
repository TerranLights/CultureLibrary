# France: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Norman, Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Corsican, Alsatian, Occitan; Luxembourgish; Romani varieties; Darija, Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Normandy's villages:** check how long a *horsain* stays one, in local memoirs and council talk.
- **Burgundy's Châtillonnais:** check Zonabend's Minot against later restudies of the same villages.
- **Corsica:** check *pinzutu* and *Corsu* in press, songs and graffiti, and who uses each.
- **Alsace:** check how incomers from "the interior" are named and received.
- **Brittany's Breton-speaking west:** check whether learners of Breton count as insiders in *fest-noz* and Diwan circles.
- **The Nord's mining towns:** check Lens stadium culture against the mine's closing.
- **Marseille:** check neighborhood ties versus city-wide ties among OM supporters.
- **Brussels:** check the *zinneke* idea among residents of different origins.
- **Luxembourg:** check belonging reported by daily cross-border workers against residents.
- **Harki families:** check camp-born children's accounts against their parents'.
- **Chinese of Paris (Belleville, the 13th arrondissement):** check second-generation associations' language about being French.
- **Antilleans in Île-de-France:** check how those born in the Paris region speak of "home" in the islands.
- **Gitans and Manouches:** check the Saintes-Maries gathering against daily life on *aires d'accueil*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for immigration history and surveys of attachment; mixed for rural newcomers; thin for Travelers' own words and for Luxembourg's newer arrivals.
- **Languages needed:** French with Norman words; Corsican for *Riacquistu* songs; Alsatian; Luxembourgish and Portuguese for Luxembourg sources; Romani for Traveler accounts; Kabyle for Harki-family memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *intégration* and *assimilation* are campaign words, and *pinzutu* and *Hergeloffeni* can wound; quote them as data. Nationality rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose sense of being from here would otherwise join the second-generation checks.
