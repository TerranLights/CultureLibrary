# France: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Darija, Kabyle; Wolof and Soninke for West African-French families. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Morvan (Burgundy):** check nurses' descendants' memories of babies taken in from Paris.
- **The Aveyron and Massif Central:** check how the Victor story is told locally, and what lesson is drawn.
- **Brittany:** check Dastum's lullabies by district, and who still sings them.
- **Corsica:** check *ninna nanne* in recordings and family use.
- **Paris against small towns:** check crèche places against childminders and grandparents.
- **Wallonia:** check ONE consultation advice against French PMI advice.
- **Luxembourg:** check *Maison relais* use by home language.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check Avicenne case literature for grandmothers' place in infant care.
- **West African-French mothers:** check how PMI staff and mothers discuss carrying, feeding and sleep.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check accounts of study help at home, in parents' and children's own words.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how children learn family trades on the road; sources thin.
- **Working-class suburbs:** check *colonies de vacances* use today against the 1950s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for infant care history, child health and the cohort; mixed for fathers' everyday nurture; thin for Travelers and for Luxembourg's newer families.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for Fénelon; Breton and Gallo for Dastum; Corsican; Luxembourgish; Darija, Kabyle, Wolof and Soninke for clinic and family sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clinical case files are confidential; outsider praise or blame of "French parenting" is a stereotype trap; discipline and child-protection rules belong to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose infant-care customs would otherwise sit with the transcultural-clinic comparison.
