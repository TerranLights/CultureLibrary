# France: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Arabic, Portuguese and Tigrinya where migrant shelters are studied. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and its inner suburbs:** check shelter use against street sleeping by district.
- **Calais and the Pas-de-Calais coast:** check residents' and volunteers' accounts of the camps.
- **The Roya valley and Nice:** check how villagers explain sheltering migrants or refusing to.
- **Burgundy and the Velay:** check pilgrim hostels (*gîtes*) at Vézelay and Le Puy and their hosts' rules.
- **The Alps:** check mountain huts (*refuges*) of the Club alpin and the custom of the open winter room.
- **Brussels:** check *hébergement citoyen* hosts by commune.
- **Luxembourg:** check winter-shelter users by nationality and residence status.
- **Algerian families of the old Nanterre shantytown:** check children's memories of the rehousing.
- **Portuguese-French families of Champigny's shantytown:** check how they name and remember it.
- **Retired North African men (*chibanis*) in Adoma hostels:** check how they describe the hostel room as a last home.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check what *aires d'accueil* provide and residents' own accounts.
- **Eritrean and Sudanese arrivals** on the northern coast: check their own testimony collected by aid groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for homeless surveys, asylum figures and camp reporting; mixed for hidden homelessness; thin for shelter among kin.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the *Codex*; Arabic, Tigrinya and Sudanese Arabic for migrant testimony; Portuguese for shantytown memory; Luxembourgish for winter-scheme reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter users and asylum seekers are vulnerable; names and files are protected; help to migrants and eviction rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose arrivals at the coastal camps would otherwise be listed with the Eritrean and Sudanese check.
