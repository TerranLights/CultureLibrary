# France: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish and German; Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian; Arabic; Tagalog and Portuguese for migrant care workers; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Pyrenees and Lourdes:** check who travels as a volunteer carer each year, by diocese and age.
- **Lyon:** check Catholic care networks from Jeanne Garnier to present-day hospices.
- **Alsace:** check the Protestant deaconesses of Strasbourg and their care homes.
- **The Massif Central and rural Brittany:** check whether people still visit *rebouteux* and burn-healers, and for what.
- **Wallonia:** check AVIQ home-care data against French figures for the same kinds of household.
- **Luxembourg:** check how many care workers cross from Lorraine and Wallonia daily.
- **Antillean nurses at the Paris hospitals (AP-HP):** check memories of those recruited in the 1960s and 1970s.
- **Portuguese-French women:** check accounts of cleaning and concierge work that included caring for elderly residents.
- **West African-French home aides:** check their own accounts of work in elders' homes.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how adult children arrange care for parents who retired across the Mediterranean.
- **Jewish community care bodies:** check Casip-Cojasor's homes and home visits.
- **Men caring for a wife or parent:** check carers' surveys by sex.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for care surveys, nursing history and palliative care; mixed for folk healing; thin for carers in Travelers' and newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the Hôtel-Dieu manual; 17th-century French for the Filles de la Charité; Luxembourgish and German for care-insurance reports; Tagalog, Portuguese and Creole for care workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient and care-home files are confidential; dementia and end-of-life care touch families' grief; assisted dying belongs to Scorpio, and care benefits' rules to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose home-care arrangements for elders would otherwise join the cross-Mediterranean check.
