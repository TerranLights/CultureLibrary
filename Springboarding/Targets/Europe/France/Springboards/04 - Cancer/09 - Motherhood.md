# France: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian; Darija, Kabyle; Antillean Creole; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it, including the mother as a figure of the culture (the Virgin, the motherland, the mother tongue).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lyon:** check what women ask for at Fourvière, in written vows and ex-votos, across decades.
- **The Velay and the Quercy:** check pilgrims to Le Puy and Rocamadour for fertility and safe-birth prayers.
- **Wallonia:** check Banneux and Beauraing pilgrims by origin, including Italian-Belgian families.
- **Luxembourg:** check *Mammendag* customs and which languages mothers' courses use.
- **Brittany's ports:** check how sailors' wives raised children with fathers at sea.
- **The Nord's former mining towns:** check lone mothers' own accounts in local studies.
- **Marseille and Provence:** check memoirs by sons and daughters of migrant mothers.
- **Kabyle-French women:** check how the mother-in-law tie is described by daughters-in-law of two generations.
- **West African-French mothers:** check the perinatal survey and midwives' reports for their care needs.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check whether the month of rest after a birth is kept, and how.
- **Antilleans** (overseas and in Île-de-France): check *poto mitan* in songs, novels and interviews.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check women's own accounts of motherhood; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for birth statistics, birth reform and the maternal-instinct debate; mixed for lone mothers' own voices; thin for Travelers and for newer communities' birth customs.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Dhuoda; Walloon for pilgrimage songs; Luxembourgish for *Mammendag*; Kabyle and Darija for in-law sources; Creole for *poto mitan*; Vietnamese for postpartum customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** maternal deaths, miscarriage and regret touch grief; natalist honors carry Vichy's shadow; abortion belongs to Aries and parental-leave and filiation rules to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose birth and postpartum customs would otherwise sit beside the Vietnamese check.
