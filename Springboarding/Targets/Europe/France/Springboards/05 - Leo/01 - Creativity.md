## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris against regional art schools (Lyon, Nancy, Strasbourg):** check where the label *créateur* is granted, and who leaves for Paris to earn it.
- **The Limousin and the Creuse:** check porcelain and tapestry workshops for who signs a piece.
- **Brittany's Pays bigouden and Quimper:** check embroidery and faience for named makers against anonymous motifs.
- **Provence:** check *santonnier* families for when a new figure is accepted.
- **Northern Alsace (Soufflenheim, Betschdorf):** check potters' variation on inherited forms.
- **Wallonia:** check Val-Saint-Lambert crystal design and the Liège workshops.
- **Brussels:** check Art Nouveau and comics against the Paris scene, for where Belgian makers seek credit.
- **Luxembourg:** check the pottery and ceramics of Septfontaines and Nospelt for craft memory.
- **Maghrebi-French writers and rappers:** check what counts as original in rap battles and writing workshops.
- **West African-French designers:** check wax-print fashion for who owns a pattern.
- **Manouche guitarists:** check how improvisation is taught by ear in families.
- **Armenian-French:** check workshop crafts and jewelers' lineages in Marseille and the Lyon area.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for art history, the sociology of artists and craft heritage; mixed for everyday making and DIY; thin for heritage communities' crafts outside music and fashion.
- **Languages needed:** Old French and Latin for Villard; 16th-century French for Palissy; Breton for embroidery motif names; Provençal for *santon* lore; Lingala and Wolof for fashion scenes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** credit disputes between designers and weavers or embroiderers can name living people; art taken from former colonies is a separate restitution debate; authors' rights belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose textile and jewelry crafts would otherwise join the pattern-ownership checks.
