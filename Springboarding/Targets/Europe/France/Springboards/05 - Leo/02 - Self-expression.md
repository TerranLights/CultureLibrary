# France: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan; Darija; Guadeloupean Creole. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Parisian bourgeois families against working-class families of the Nord:** check what may be said at table about feelings, money and politics.
- **Brittany:** check *kan ha diskan* and *fest-noz* singing as a licensed place for improvised lines.
- **Occitania:** check whether *nòva cançon* audiences sang in Occitan or only listened.
- **Marseille:** check *tchatche* as a word the city uses of itself, against how national media use it.
- **Corsica:** check sung laments and political graffiti as two channels for what is held back.
- **Alsace:** check dialect cabaret (for example the *Barabli* in Strasbourg) as a place to say what French could not.
- **Brussels:** check how multilingual youth choose a language to express feeling.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether residents speak their mind more in Luxembourgish, French or Portuguese.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check *ḥshūma* in young people's own words about what is shown before elders.
- **Antilleans:** check carnival as the season when restraint lifts.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check memoirs and films by the second generation on unspoken family feeling.
- **Jewish communities:** check Yiddish and Judeo-Arabic song and theater in Paris as places to voice feeling in a home language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for autobiography, protest art and media history; mixed for everyday emotional display (few surveys); thin for Luxembourg's newer communities' own outlets.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Abelard and Héloïse; Breton for the *Barzaz Breiz* originals; Occitan for song lyrics; Alsatian for cabaret texts; Guadeloupean Creole for *gwoka*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dress and faith in schools stir active quarrels; coming-out stories and teen blogs name living people and minors, so quote sparingly; free-speech rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose youth writing on family restraint would otherwise sit beside the Vietnamese-French check.
