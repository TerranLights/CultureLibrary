## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris against provincial academies (Toulouse, Rouen, Bordeaux):** check who grants literary standing outside the capital.
- **Brittany:** check Collier de l'Hermine lists for which fields and languages are honored.
- **Occitania:** check Jeux floraux prize lists for when Occitan entries returned.
- **Wallonia:** check Mérite wallon citations against Belgian national honors.
- **Luxembourg:** check honors to foreign-born residents and to cross-border workers.
- **The Nord and Lorraine mining towns:** check plaques and medals for miners' long service.
- **Alsace and Moselle:** check how the forcibly conscripted *Malgré-nous* were later recognized.
- **Corsica:** check which island figures appear on street names and statues.
- **West African-French:** check how *tirailleurs*' descendants sought veterans' recognition and pensions.
- **Antilleans:** check the 10 May and 23 May commemorations as two different claims.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check how veterans of the colonial army and their families are honored.
- **Jewish communities:** check the naming of the Righteous and the wording of plaques at schools.
- **Women across the nation:** check the share of women in each honors list by decade.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors files, prizes and commemoration; mixed for praise at work and at home; thin for heritage communities' own honors (association awards, elders' titles).
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Middle French for the royal orders; Occitan for early Jeux floraux poems; Breton for Hermine citations; German and Luxembourgish for Luxembourg honors files; Vietnamese for veterans' groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honors files near the present name the living; removing statues and renaming streets split public opinion; pensions and status rules for veterans belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose veterans of colonial armies would otherwise join the veterans' recognition checks.
