# France: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican, Breton; Darija, Kabyle; Cape Verdean Creole. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris boulevards against small southern towns' *cours* (Aix's Cours Mirabeau):** check the evening promenade as a place to see and be seen.
- **Seine-Saint-Denis:** check how residents describe being seen only through crime news.
- **Corsica:** check Via Stella's audience and what islanders say about their place in national news.
- **Brittany and Alsace:** check regional-language programs' slots and audiences over time.
- **Rural Massif Central:** check what residents say about being forgotten by Paris.
- **Wallonia:** check *Strip-Tease* subjects' later accounts of being filmed.
- **Luxembourg:** check Cape Verdean and Portuguese residents' presence in Luxembourgish-language media.
- **Maghrebi-French and Kabyle-French families:** check the *ʿayn* and the hand-shaped amulet in child-rearing accounts.
- **West African-French workers:** check cleaning and security jobs for who is looked at and who looks through them.
- **Chinese of Paris:** check second-generation creators' videos and podcasts made to be noticed as French.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how camps and caravans are noticed by neighbors and press; sources thin.
- **Disabled people across the nation:** check representation in the Arcom counts against their share in surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media counts, surveillance debates and the philosophy of the gaze; mixed for everyday attention (eye contact, greeting) with few studies; thin for Luxembourg's smaller groups.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for Saint-Simon; Corsican and Breton for broadcast archives; Cape Verdean Creole and Portuguese for Luxembourg sources; Darija for amulet terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *perceived origin* in screen counts is a disputed method; documentary subjects and surveillance footage involve living people's images; data protection rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose presence on French screens would otherwise be counted with the Arcom checks.
