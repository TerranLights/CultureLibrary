# France: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Occitan, Corsican; Arabic and Darija, Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old industrial towns of the Nord and Lorraine:** check union language of *dignité* in strike leaflets since the closures.
- **Paris hotel and cleaning trades:** check West African-French and Maghrebi-French workers' accounts of how clients address them.
- **Brittany's chapels:** check *Danse macabre* and ossuary traditions for how the dead were kept and named.
- **The Massif Central and rural Burgundy:** check elders' accounts of moving into care homes.
- **Provence and Marseille:** check Les Morts de la Rue's southern branches and who attends burials.
- **Corsica:** check village customs for washing and laying out the dead.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check CPAS users' testimony against French food-aid users'.
- **Luxembourg:** check the gap between residents' wealth and the street newspaper sellers' experience.
- **Harki families:** check how elders describe their standing in old age, apart from the camp years.
- **Antilleans in Île-de-France:** check hospital staff's accounts of address and respect on the wards.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check burial and mourning customs as matters of dignity, from their own accounts only.
- **Jewish communities:** check burial societies (*hevra kaddisha*) and their care for the dead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for care, prison and poverty reports; mixed for everyday respect (few surveys on courtesy); thin for Travelers' own words on how they are treated.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for Joinville; Breton for chapel inscriptions; Arabic and Darija for *karāma* in protest songs; Luxembourgish for street-paper issues.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** L'Arche's abuse findings involve victims; care-home and prison inspections describe vulnerable people; end-of-life rules belong to Libra and assisted dying to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose workers in cleaning and delivery jobs would otherwise join the Paris hotel check.
