# France: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish and German; Provençal Occitan; Darija, Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grandes écoles and medical faculties in Paris, Lyon and Toulouse:** check which hazing rites persisted after the 1990s campaigns.
- **Normandy and Brittany towns of 1944:** check local memory of the *tondues* in town histories.
- **Provence and the Nice area:** check whether penitents' processions draw young members.
- **The Nord:** check how Calais tells its 1347 story in schools and festivals.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Belgian accounts of French mockery in schools, workplaces and media.
- **Luxembourg:** check families' silence or speech about sons conscripted by force.
- **Alsace and Moselle:** check press and memoirs on the 1953 Bordeaux trial over Oradour, and the region's sense of being judged by the rest of France.
- **Corsica:** check satirical songs and *chjam'è rispondi* insults for where mockery ends.
- **Maghrebi-French fathers' generation:** check how they recount being addressed as *tu* at work and at counters.
- **Kabyle-French families:** check *lḥif* and silence in elders' accounts of emigration.
- **Harkis:** check how children describe their fathers' standing among other immigrants.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check when the name *pied-noir* was heard as a slight and when it was taken up with pride.
- **Travelers:** check what they report about being turned away from campsites and shops; sources thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hazing, bullying and occupation-era shaming; mixed for everyday ridicule; thin for humiliation inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Froissart and penance records; Provençal for confraternity statutes; German and Luxembourgish for conscription files; Darija and Kabyle for elders' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *tondues*, forced conscription and the Oradour trial touch living families; hazing victims are often minors or students; harassment and defamation rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose elders' accounts of slights at work would otherwise sit with the Benguigui pointer.
