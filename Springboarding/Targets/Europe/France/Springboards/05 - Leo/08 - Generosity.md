# France: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian, Breton; Arabic, Kabyle; Hebrew; Armenian. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Touraine and Picardy (Amiens):** check Saint Martin's feasts and *Saint-Martin* lantern customs for giving to the poor.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check Saint-Martin and Saint-Nicolas gift customs, and the region's charity societies.
- **Brittany:** check *pardon* meals and who pays for the parish feast.
- **The Southwest:** check rugby clubs' *troisième mi-temps* and who buys the rounds.
- **Paris:** check large donors' gifts after the Notre-Dame fire (2019) and the debate over tax relief.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Viva for Life's local events by commune.
- **Luxembourg:** check Télévie gifts against the country's foreign-born share.
- **Maghrebi-French and Kabyle-French families:** check *tiwizi* and money sent to home villages for wells and mosques.
- **West African-French associations:** check hometown projects paid for from workers' wages.
- **Jewish communities:** check the FSJU appeal against synagogue-level giving.
- **Armenian-French:** check the Phonethon's place in Marseille and the Lyon area.
- **Portuguese-French:** check gifts at village feasts in Portugal paid by emigrants, as comparison.
- **Chinese and Vietnamese of Paris:** check red-envelope gifts at the New Year and weddings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal giving (tax data, barometers, telethons); mixed for treating and gifts in daily life; thin for informal giving across borders.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the *Vita Martini*; Arabic for charity appeals; Hebrew for communal texts; Armenian for diaspora press; Soninke for hometown-association minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Islamic charities have faced closure and suspicion, so read official files and the charities' own reports side by side; donor files are private; tax relief on gifts belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose religious and associational giving would otherwise join the faith-charity pointers.
