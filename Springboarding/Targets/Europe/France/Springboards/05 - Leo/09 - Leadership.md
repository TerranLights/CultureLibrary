# France: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican, Breton, Occitan; Arabic, Kabyle; Martinican Creole. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, military and management writing and for trust surveys; mixed for informal leaders; thin for leaders in Travelers' and newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Commynes; Latin for Budé's other works; Dutch for Flemish sources on the Royal Question (comparison only); Kabyle and Soninke for assembly records; Creole for Martinican politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *homme providentiel* idea is used in present campaigns; Corsican political networks have been tied to violence; the formal powers of office belong to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose association and faith-group leaders would otherwise join the community-leaders checks.
