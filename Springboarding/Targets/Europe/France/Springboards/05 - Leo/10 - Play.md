*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check how dense housing limits outdoor play, in families' own accounts.
- **Brittany:** check FALSAB games at *pardons* and summer fêtes against school sports.
- **French Flanders and the Nord:** check estaminet games and who plays them now.
- **The Massif Central:** check *quilles* leagues and the age of their players.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check *pétanque* play outside clubs, on village squares.
- **Alsace:** check Christmas-market toys and German-language toy traditions, as comparison.
- **Wallonia:** check *balle pelote* towns and their rivalries.
- **Luxembourg:** check Easter *Klibberen* and its spread among children of foreign-born families.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check children's games brought from North Africa and kept on estates.
- **West African-French families:** check songs and games in Wolof and Soninke among children in Paris suburbs.
- **Antilleans:** check dominoes and card play at family gatherings in Île-de-France.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check children's play on campsites; sources thin, look to ethnographies.
- **Chinese and Vietnamese of Paris:** check mahjong and Chinese chess clubs as adult play.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for children's games history, toy and video-game figures and school play; mixed for adults' play outside sport; thin for heritage communities' children's games.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for Stella's captions; Breton for FALSAB rules; Flemish of French Flanders and Picard for tavern-game names; Walloon for *balle pelote* lore; Wolof and Soninke for children's songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Gaignebet's rhymes are obscene by design, so quote little; studies of schoolchildren need consent; gaming disorder and gambling harm belong to Aries.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose children's games and board games would otherwise join the heritage-games checks.
