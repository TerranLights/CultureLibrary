# France: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Burgundian, Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican; Kabyle, Darija; Martinican Creole. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Burgundy and Franche-Comté:** check market handshakes and old family firms for the given word.
- **Corsica:** check island historians' and singers' accounts of *onore* against mainland literature.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check honor in bull-game and jousting clubs, from their own bulletins.
- **The Southwest:** check rugby's *esprit* in club histories, and the place of the word given among players.
- **The Nord and Wallonia:** check Golden Fleece memory in civic heraldry and town pageants.
- **Paris and Île-de-France suburbs:** check *respect* and the honor of a neighborhood in youth ethnographies.
- **Luxembourg:** check honor and the given word in Luxembourgish proverbs; sources likely thin.
- **Kabyle-French families:** check *nnif* in songs, as the singers and listeners explain it.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how young women describe *ʿār* and family reputation, in their own words.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Harkis:** check honor and the given word in their accounts of 1962.
- **Antilleans:** check *damyé* revival groups and what members say a fight proves.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the word given in horse and car trading; from their own accounts only.
- **Armenian-French:** check honoring the dead of 1915 in Marseille and the Lyon area.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for chivalry, dueling, military honor and memorials; mixed for honor in daily village and market life; thin for Luxembourg and for Travelers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the Loyal Serviteur and Golden Fleece statutes; Corsican for island songs and memoirs; Kabyle for *izli* poetry; Martinican Creole for *damyé* songs; Dutch for Flemish Golden Fleece sources (comparison only).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence justified by family honor must be researched only in the words of those who lived it, never presumed of a group; literary Corsican vendettas feed outside clichés; defamation and violent crime belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose family-reputation studies would otherwise sit beside the Maghrebi-French check.
