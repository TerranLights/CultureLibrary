## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Île-de-France commuters against small-town workers:** check how long journeys reshape sleep and meal hours.
- **Wine-growing Burgundy, Champagne and the Bordelais:** check who comes for the *vendanges*, the working year's peak.
- **The Breton coast (Douarnenez, Concarneau):** check fishing seasons against women's cannery work.
- **Mining basins of the Nord, Lorraine and Hainaut:** check how shifts set family meal times.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check how the weekly rest day and the feast days kept there frame the working year, by confession.
- **Luxembourg's cross-border workers from Lorraine and Wallonia:** check how the daily crossing stretches the working day.
- **Muslim workers of Maghrebi and West African heritage:** check how the fasting month reshapes work hours.
- **Observant Jewish families:** check how the Sabbath fits a Sunday-based working week.
- **Retired Maghrebi workers (*chibanis*):** check how their oral histories reckon a working life.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the daily round on a halting site (water, power, school runs, work) in their own accounts; sources are thin.
- **Portuguese-French families:** check where the August trip home sits in the working year.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for time use, leisure history and peasant memoirs; mixed for what housework means beyond minutes; thin for Travelers and night workers.
- **Extent:** the author has yet to fix which lands the nation holds. Every Virgo sheet is written for France's European territory, Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg; Flanders, the French Basque Country and Northern Catalonia serve only as comparisons, and overseas departments appear only where named.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the early Cistercian writings; Middle French for the shepherds' calendar; German and Luxembourgish for the *Wort*'s older death notices; Walloon and Picard for work songs; Arabic and Kabyle for the *chibanis*' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** employers' talk of "idle" workers in Poulot and his like is a claim to study, never a description; working-hours rules are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose festival and fasting calendars would otherwise join the weekly-rest checks.
