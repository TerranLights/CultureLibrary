# France: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Walloon, Occitan, Breton, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle, Wolof; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Franche-Comté's car towns:** check how the line generation and their children each speak of factory work.
- **The Liège and Charleroi basins:** check what long unemployment did to the place of work in a life, in oral history.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether residents and cross-border employees rate the same jobs differently.
- **Brittany's food-processing plants:** check how slaughterhouse work is described after closures (Lampaul-Guimiliau, 2013).
- **Corsica:** check seasonal tourist work against public-sector posts in how a "real job" is defined.
- **La Défense and Paris office districts:** check what the *cadre* label adds to the hours and self-image of salaried staff.
- **Algerian and Moroccan workers of the car plants (Billancourt, Poissy, Aulnay):** check their own accounts of line work and the strikes of the early 1980s.
- **West African-French workers:** check how hometown associations rank the jobs members hold in France.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check how descendants of the wartime workers tell that labor.
- **Antilleans recruited in the 1960s and 1970s:** check paths through the post office and customs.
- **Portuguese-French families:** check how building-trade skills passed between generations.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check scrap, basket and seasonal trades in their own words; sources are thin.
- **Women's unpaid work at home:** check whether surveys and women themselves count it as work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for factory ethnography, job surveys and labor archives; mixed for the meaning of unpaid work; thin for informal and undeclared work.
- **Languages needed:** classical French for Vauban; Walloon for Liège workers' songs and memoirs; Luxembourgish for the CSL's survey material; Vietnamese for descendants of the wartime workers; Wolof and Soninke for hometown-association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** workplace suicides (the France Télécom years) touch bereaved families, and the trials belong to Libra; forced colonial labor is painful memory; "lazy" labels attached to the unemployed are political weapons to study, not repeat.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose oral histories of wage work would otherwise join the line-work and night-work checks.
