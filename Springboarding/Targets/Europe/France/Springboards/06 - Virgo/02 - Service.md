# France: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Old and Middle French; Breton, Corsican, Alsatian; Maghrebi Arabic and Berber varieties; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Breton and Auvergnat maids in Paris around 1900:** check their letters and memoirs against employers' accounts.
- **Corsica:** check family histories of careers in the state and colonial service.
- **Luxembourg:** check how a state career and a finance career compare in standing.
- **Wallonia against Brussels:** check how far each region's associations rest on unpaid volunteers.
- **Naval and garrison towns (Brest, Toulon, Metz):** check service families' own accounts of postings.
- **Vendée, Anjou and the Catholic west:** check vocations to serving orders, diocese by diocese.
- **Muslim chaplains in the armed forces and prisons:** check how they describe serving both their faith and the state.
- **West African-French nannies and home aides:** check how they describe the boundary between work and family.
- **Antilleans and the overseas departments** (pointed to here): check how the *Service militaire adapté* is remembered.
- **Muslim associations:** check Ramadan meal service for all comers (for example *Chorba pour tous*, *check*) and who volunteers.
- **Jewish communities:** check volunteer roles in synagogues and youth movements as service.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check service roles inside their churches and families; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic-service history, conscription and public-employment counts; mixed for courtesy at counters and tables; thin for volunteering in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for fealty and Middle French for Christine de Pizan; Luxembourgish for the youth service; Arabic for chaplains' sermons and talks; Creole for the overseas military service.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Papin case is a double murder, so quote little; prison and army chaplaincy touches security matters, so access is limited; servants' records name poor women and their employers.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose community kitchens and their volunteers would otherwise join the meal-service check.
