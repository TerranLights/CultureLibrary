## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm families of Aveyron and the Cantal:** check which tasks elders kept and when they handed them over.
- **Former miners of the Nord and Hainaut:** check how silicosis victims describe being taken off the coal face.
- **Retired fishermen of the Breton coast:** check what shore roles (net mending, rescue crews) they took up.
- **Corsican village elders:** check their role in keeping Corsican spoken at home.
- **Luxembourg:** check who joins elders' clubs by nationality and language.
- **Kabyle-French and Moroccan-French grandparents:** check child-minding across households and across the Mediterranean.
- **West African-French families:** check how children's household tasks are set by age and sex, in the families' own accounts.
- **Chinese and Vietnamese-French shopkeeping families:** check whether and how children help in the shop, as the families tell it.
- **Pieds-Noirs who arrived in mid-career in 1962:** check accounts of lost trades and status.
- **Deaf and disabled self-advocates:** check their rejection of worth measured by output.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check what roles old people keep in family trading and in church life; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for retirement sociology, poor relief and disabled employment; mixed for the feeling of being useful (few direct survey items); thin for children's help at home outside time-use counts.
- **Languages needed:** Corsican for language-keeping at home; 17th- and 18th-century French for Furetière and Sieyès; Kabyle and Darija for grandparents' accounts; Luxembourgish for elders' clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime hospital deaths touch descendants and are argued over by historians; "useless" talk about the disabled and old echoes eugenic language; end-of-life choices belong to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose grandparents' child-minding would otherwise join the cross-household check.
