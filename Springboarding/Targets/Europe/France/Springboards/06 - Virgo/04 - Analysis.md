## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grandes-école graduates, university graduates and vocational workers:** check whether the school method of breaking down a problem shows up in how each group reasons at work.
- **Toulouse's aerospace and Grenoble's research milieu:** check how engineers' cities speak of method and measurement.
- **Wine country (Bordelais, Burgundy):** check sensory analysis against inherited taste in growers' own accounts.
- **French-speaking Belgium:** check school mathematics results against France's in the same surveys.
- **Luxembourg:** check how audit findings are reported in the country's press, and in which languages.
- **Breton and Norman fishermen:** check tide and weather reckoning in their own words.
- **First-generation students of Maghrebi and West African heritage in preparatory classes:** check their accounts of learning the *prépa* method.
- **Jewish communities:** check how yeshivas in Paris and Strasbourg describe their method of text study.
- **Women in engineering and mathematics:** check accounts from the first women at Polytechnique (from 1972).
- **Autistic self-advocates:** check their own accounts of how analytic styles are judged.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check reckoning in trade (horse, scrap, markets) taught outside school; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the history of science, statistics, audits and exams; mixed for everyday reasoning in families and shops; thin for reckoning taught outside schools.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and 17th-century French for Port-Royal; Luxembourgish and German for press coverage of Luxembourg's audits; Breton for fishermen's weather lore; Hebrew and Aramaic for yeshiva study texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the long fight over psychoanalytic against behavioral care for autism divides parents and clinicians; the "Cartesian French" label is a stereotype to study, not a finding.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose own traditions of mathematics and logic would otherwise be pointed to beside Port-Royal and Bourbaki.
