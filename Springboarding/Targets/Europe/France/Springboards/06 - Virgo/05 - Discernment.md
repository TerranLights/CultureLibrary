# France: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Latin for late-medieval theology; Luxembourgish; Breton, Corsican; Arabic; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Savoy and the Annecy area:** check whether Salesian spiritual direction still shapes local religious guidance.
- **Lyon and Marseille against Paris:** check whether verdicts on food and fashion issued in the capital are trusted or resisted.
- **Cattle country of the Massif Central (Salers, Aubrac):** check how dealers learn to judge animals at fairs.
- **French-speaking Belgium:** check scam reports and media-education programs against France's.
- **Luxembourg:** check which language groups report online fraud and through which channels.
- **Muslim families of Maghrebi and West African heritage:** check how prayer for guidance and family counsel are weighed in choosing a spouse.
- **Observant Jewish communities:** check how matchmaking is arranged and who advises.
- **Antilleans (overseas departments as needed):** check when people consult a seer and when a doctor, in their own accounts.
- **Corsican villages:** check how family names and references are used to vouch for a stranger.
- **Older people living alone:** check how scams against them are reported and by whom.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how an outsider's word is weighed before a deal, in their own accounts; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the sociology of taste, consumer complaints and cult monitoring; mixed for how people judge one another (mostly etiquette, dating media and hiring studies); thin for folk ways of reading signs.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Gerson; early 17th-century French for François de Sales; Arabic for prayer-for-guidance sources; Creole for seers' clients; Luxembourgish, German and English for BEE SECURE material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "cult" labels are contested, and some faith groups attack the monitoring bodies; judging character from handwriting or faces can hide prejudice; elder-fraud victims often keep silent out of shame.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose matchmaking customs would otherwise join the spouse-choice check.
