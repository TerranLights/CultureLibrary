# France: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian, Occitan, Corsican; Latin for chant books; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Franche-Comté's watch and clock trades (Besançon, Morteau):** check how precision was taught in workshops and trade schools.
- **Starred kitchens of Burgundy and the Lyon area:** check how chefs describe the strain of ratings, in their own memoirs.
- **Paris couture workrooms:** check how apprentices are trained to a house's finish.
- **Liège gunmakers (the city and Herstal):** check how proof-house rejections are spoken of in the trade.
- **Alsace:** check the Strasbourg cathedral workshop's stonecutters and their standards.
- **Luxembourg craftsmen:** check how the master's diploma affects standing and pay.
- **Students of preparatory classes and their families:** check pressure and its toll, by family background.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check, in their own accounts, how school expectations were set and felt; nothing presumed.
- **Muslims of Maghrebi and West African heritage:** check how sermons and teachers use the idea of *itqān* for work done well.
- **Ballet and gymnastics trainees:** check accounts of weight and body checks.
- **Men and body image:** check gyms and men's magazines as a source.
- **Manouche guitarists:** check how players judge a solo as finished or flawed at festivals and jam sessions; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for craft contests, couture, standards and ballet; mixed for perfectionism as a trait (few French scale studies); thin for clinical counts by region.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for Boileau and Sévigné; Latin for Solesmes's chant books; Luxembourgish for craft-chamber documents; Arabic for sermons on *itqān*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** chefs' suicides and dancers' eating disorders touch living colleagues and families; French slang has an ethnic slur for botched work, to be studied only as evidence of prejudice.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose classical dance and music training would otherwise join the ballet-trainee check.
