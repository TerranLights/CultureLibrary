# France: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **National press against regional dailies:** check which criticism of local notables reaches print in each.
- **Brittany:** check how reporting on farm pollution (the green-algae inquiries) was received locally.
- **French-speaking Belgium:** check satire of the federal state and the monarchy against French satire of the presidency.
- **Luxembourg:** check how criticism of finance and the court is voiced where everyone is near.
- **Occitania:** check the revival of political song in Occitan from the 1970s.
- **Kabyle-French communities:** check song and poetry as criticism of power at home and in exile.
- **Antillean carnival groups (overseas departments as needed):** check songs that mock officials.
- **West African-French hometown associations:** check how members voice criticism of leaders in meetings.
- **Jewish communities:** check internal debate in community magazines.
- **Pupils and teachers:** check how marks and report-card comments are read by families of different schooling.
- **Women and men at work:** check whether the same criticism is described differently by sex.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how community members correct one another, by their own account; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, press history and literary criticism; mixed for feedback at work and in school; thin for criticism inside families.
- **Languages needed:** Old Occitan for Peire Cardenal; Middle French and Latin for *Fauvel*; mid-17th-century French for the pamphlets; Kabyle for Azem's songs; Creole for carnival songs; Luxembourgish for press-council decisions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2015 killings at *Charlie Hebdo* and the quarrel over mocking religion are still raw; whistleblowers' trials are Libra's; insults in satire may target people still living.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose satirical film and song traditions would otherwise join the song-as-criticism checks.
