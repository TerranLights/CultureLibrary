# France: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Corsican, Occitan; Martinican and Guadeloupean Creole; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check murder and judgment ballads in François-Marie Luzel's *gwerz* collections for who punishes, God or the court.
- **Corsica:** check island and national press on the trials after the 1998 killing of the prefect, side by side.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check the local blasphemy article, repealed in 2016,.
- **The Nord and the Pas-de-Calais:** check what Outreau's inquiry records say about local courts and social services.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check the Dutroux commission's hearings for what the inquiry asked of police and judges.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the Bommeleeër hearings were reported in Luxembourgish, German and French.
- **Antilleans:** check the commission on the May 1967 killings in Guadeloupe and the chlordecone case (overseas departments).
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French families:** check death-in-custody campaigns' own statements and the court files that answer them.
- **Harkis:** check the 2022 recognition and reparation law, its text and who qualifies.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check their associations' claims over the rue d'Isly shooting (Algiers, March 1962).
- **Jewish communities:** check survivors' and civil parties' testimony at the Barbie and Papon trials.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the 2016 recognition of the wartime internment camps (Montreuil-Bellay) and descendants' accounts; sources are thin.
- **Armenian-French:** check the 2001 recognition law and the 2012 denial law struck down by the Constitutional Council.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trials, penal history and court statistics; mixed for justice outside courts; thin for Travelers' own legal memory.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old French for the *Olim*; Middle French for remission letters; Luxembourgish for the Bommeleeër coverage; Breton for *gwerz* texts; Guadeloupean Creole for May 1967 testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-abuse cases (Outreau, Dutroux) name victims who are now adults; deaths in custody are under live litigation; purge killings divide local memory.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose dealings with police and courts would otherwise join the custody-campaign check.
