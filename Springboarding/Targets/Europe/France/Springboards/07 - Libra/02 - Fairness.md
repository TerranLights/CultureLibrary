*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Béarn and the Pyrenean valleys:** check farm transfers to one heir under the *attribution préférentielle* rule, and what the other siblings receive.
- **Provence and the lower Rhône:** check irrigation turns kept by water associations along the Canal de Craponne.
- **Boulogne-sur-Mer and the Breton ports:** check union contracts that set the crew share and quarrels over deductions.
- **Lorraine and Franche-Comté villages:** check who counts as a household for *affouage* and newcomers' claims.
- **Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg:** check union and employer statements each time indexation is paused or adjusted.
- **Antilleans:** check the 2009 general strike against *pwofitasyon* in Guadeloupe and Martinique (overseas departments) and its price agreements.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how heirs and notaries handle Islamic inheritance shares beside the Code's equal shares; ask families directly.
- **West African-French men in worker hostels (*foyers*):** check rotation rules for shared rooms and kitchens, from residents' committees.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check what associations called a fair indemnity, against the 1970 compensation law.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how pitches on official halting sites are allotted and what residents say of the allotting; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inequality surveys, admissions and wage rules; mixed for everyday turn-taking and bill-splitting; thin for fishing shares outside union files.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for Boileau and the fabliaux; Guadeloupean and Martinican Creole for the 2009 strike; Arabic for inheritance usage; Dutch for Flemish indexation debates (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *ḥogra* and *piston* are charged claims of exclusion, to report as speakers frame them; inheritance by faith touches private family quarrels.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose shop and family-business shares would otherwise join the inheritance check.
