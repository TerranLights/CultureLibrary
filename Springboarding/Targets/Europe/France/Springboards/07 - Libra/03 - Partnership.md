## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany and Normandy dairy farms:** check how the *conjoint collaborateur* status (1999) changed farm wives' standing in the partnership.
- **Languedoc and Provence:** check notarial *affrèrements* beyond the published sample, and who used them.
- **Savoy, Chamonix and the Oisans:** check guides' and clients' accounts of the rope as a bond of trust.
- **Alsace:** check parish and civil records of marriages across the Catholic-Protestant line and the agreements on children's faith.
- **Corsica:** check what *cumpà* and *cumà* oblige between families, in island ethnography.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check commune registers of *cohabitation légale*, its terms and its ending.
- **Luxembourg:** check couples who live on two sides of a border and how tax and benefit rules treat them.
- **Maghrebi-French couples:** check accounts by spouses who joined a partner in France through family reunification, and the visa rules that shape their first years.
- **Portuguese-French couples:** check retirement plans that send one spouse home and keep the other in France.
- **Chinese of Paris:** check how shop partnerships among Wenzhou-origin families are financed and wound up, in their own accounts.
- **Same-sex couples:** check the first PACS years and the 2013 debate, in the partners' own testimony.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how couples are recognized among families, from their own accounts; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for couple statistics, housework and business law; mixed for partnership outside marriage and firms; thin for ritual kin and Travelers.
- **Languages needed:** Old Picard for *Aucassin*; Occitan and Latin for *affrèrement* deeds; Corsican for co-godparent terms; Portuguese for return-migration couples; Chinese (Wenzhou dialect) for shop partnerships.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2013 marriage debate still divides families and parishes; reunification files hold private details; Herzog's and Lachenal's heirs disputed the expedition story.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose family-firm partnerships would otherwise join the shop-partnership check.
