## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Ariège and Béarn valleys:** check herders' unions and Gascon-language press on the bear releases.
- **The Mercantour and Haute-Provence:** check shepherds' and parks' records on wolf damage and payments.
- **Brittany:** check farmers' unions against shore residents' groups on green algae, in Breton and French.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check the extra public holidays kept under local law (Good Friday, 26 December) and talk of their worth.
- **Corsica:** check debates on the *Assemblée de Corse* and its executive as a counterweight to Paris.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check how French-speaking parties and press describe the *compromis à la belge*, beside Flemish accounts (comparison).
- **Luxembourg:** check how the Council of State's opinions are reported, in Luxembourgish and French.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check hot-and-cold food remedies, from healers and households.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese of Paris:** check herbal and food shops in the 13th arrondissement and Belleville, and practitioners' accounts of balance in the body.
- **Night and shift workers in Île-de-France:** check how their unions bargain over rest against time-use data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional texts, working time and wildlife plans; mixed for balance in the body; thin for folk hot-and-cold systems outside clinical studies.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for Aldebrandin; Dutch for the Belgian state reforms (comparison); Gascon for valley press; Vietnamese and Chinese for shop and clinic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** predator conflicts have brought threats against officials and bear-release supporters; green-algae deaths are in litigation; burnout claims involve medical files.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose traditional medicine of bodily balance would otherwise join the herbal-shop check.
