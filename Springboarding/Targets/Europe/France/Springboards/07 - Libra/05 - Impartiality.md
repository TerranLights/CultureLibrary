# France: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg administrative usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican; Romani and Sinti (Manouche) for Travelers' own accounts. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris's senior civil service:** check HATVP opinions on officials moving to firms they once regulated.
- **Corsica:** check how island scholars and press define *clanisme* and clientelism, and who writes the studies.
- **Each region's audit chamber (*chambres régionales des comptes*):** check reports on municipal hiring, region by region, rather than relying on reputations.
- **The Southwest's rugby clubs:** check complaints about home refereeing in lower divisions, from league disciplinary files.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check how state-paid clergy and religious teaching under local law sit with public neutrality.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check the Publifin and Brussels Samusocial (2017) inquiries side by side.
- **Luxembourg:** check the ministers' code of conduct and how its committee treats family ties.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French job seekers:** check name-based testing results and the firms that answered them.
- **Antilleans:** check how civil-service postings between the overseas departments and the mainland are allotted, from unions' records.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the end of the special travel papers (2017) and the 2012 Constitutional Council ruling, with Travelers' associations' statements.
- **Jewish communities and Muslim families:** check complaints about faith-based treatment filed with the Défenseur des droits; few studies use these files.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scandals, ethics law and testing studies; mixed for everyday favoritism; thin for *clanisme* outside polemics.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French and Latin for royal inquiries and office sales; Luxembourgish for SREL coverage; Corsican for island political writing; Dutch for Flemish comparison on intercommunal cases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption cases name people who may be acquitted on appeal; *clanisme* is a charge thrown at Corsicans, to be examined, not assumed; name-based testing touches faith and origin.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose job seekers would otherwise join the name-based testing check.
