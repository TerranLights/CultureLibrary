# France: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian political usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican; Vietnamese; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Strasbourg and Alsace:** check how the Council of Europe and the European Parliament's sessions shape local talk of Franco-German reconciliation (Germany as comparison).
- **Brussels:** check how residents' groups negotiate with the European institutions over the European quarter.
- **Wallonia:** check negotiators' own memoirs from the Egmont and later state-reform talks.
- **Luxembourg:** check ministers' interviews on acting as broker inside the European Community.
- **Corsica:** check records of the Matignon process (2000) and the Beauvau talks (from 2022) between the state and island elected officials.
- **Lyon and the Alps:** check how Franco-Italian valley councils handled the Lyon-Turin rail line (Magna Lombardia as comparison).
- **West African-French associations:** check how they respond to *Françafrique* debates and to embassy and consulate visits.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check community memory of the 1946 Fontainebleau conference and its failure.
- **Jewish communities:** check the annual CRIF dinner as a ritual of political courtesy and its quarrels.
- **Binational Maghrebi-French families:** check how holidays and visits are shared between two sets of in-laws in two countries, in their accounts.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how organizers of large evangelical gatherings bargain with prefects for land, from both sides' records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and protocol; mixed for everyday tact; thin for family diplomacy outside qualitative studies.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Cambrai and Callières; Dutch for Belgian state-reform files (comparison); Vietnamese for Fontainebleau memory; Arabic for binational family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *Françafrique* files touch living officials and contested deals; Corsican talks follow years of armed struggle; parts of the diplomatic archives stay closed for decades.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose associations' dealings with consulates would otherwise join the West African-French check.
