# France: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian usage); Breton; Antillean Creole; Corsican; Occitan (Auvergne); Vietnamese; Portuguese. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Massif Central (Auvergne, Rouergue):** check threshing and haymaking help (*les battages*) in farm memoirs, and how it was paid back.
- **Burgundy and Champagne:** check what pickers and growers say each owes the other at harvest's end.
- **Lower Brittany:** check gifts and prayers for the *Anaon* at All Saints in Breton-language sources.
- **Corsica:** check chestnut-harvest help in the Castagniccia and how it was repaid.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check exchange-network records of hours traded, beside the French networks.
- **Portuguese Luxembourgers and Portuguese-French families:** check goods and money carried home in summer, and what is expected back.
- **Antilleans:** check *koudmen* for house building and its return, in Martinique and Guadeloupe (overseas departments) and among Antilleans in Île-de-France.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how wedding cash gifts are noted and matched at the next family wedding, in families' accounts.
- **West African-French associations:** check funeral contributions for sending a body home and how contributors are repaid at their own family's deaths.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check offerings to ancestors at Tết and what the living expect in return.
- **Chinese of Paris:** check favors owed between suppliers and shopkeepers, in the shopkeepers' own words.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check help between families on the road (lent vehicles, shared pitches), in their own memoirs; thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gift theory and household-transfer data; mixed for exchange labor (mostly memoirs); thin for reciprocity with the dead outside Brittany.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Fulbert; Breton for Le Braz's originals; Antillean Creole for *koudmen*; Vietnamese for ancestor rites; Portuguese for return visits.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** money sent abroad draws suspicion of fraud or extremist finance, so read official data and senders' accounts together; funeral repatriation touches grief.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose wedding and temple gift exchanges would otherwise join the wedding-gift check.
