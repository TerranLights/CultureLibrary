# France: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Norman and Belgian usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian; Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Normandy:** check where the "p'têt ben" saying first appears in print, and how Norman writers answer it.
- **Paris:** check the decades of hesitation over the Halles redevelopment (from the 1970s to 2016) in council minutes.
- **Brittany:** check petitions and council votes on rejoining the Loire-Atlantique, and the referendum never held.
- **Alsace:** check turnout rules and campaign material in the 2013 vote, and the later European Collectivity.
- **Corsica:** check how the 2003 "no" and the single territorial body set up in 2018 are spoken of.
- **Occitania:** check the 2016 consultation on the new region's name and who took part.
- **Wallonia:** check Walloon deputies' statements when they held up the EU-Canada trade deal (2016).
- **Luxembourg:** check how foreign residents and nationals reacted to the 2015 result.
- **Retired Maghrebi workers (*chibanis*):** check oral histories on putting off the choice to return or stay.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Harkis:** check family accounts of how the 1962 decision to leave was made and by whom.
- **Antilleans:** check decisions to go back to the islands after years in Île-de-France, from return-migration studies.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check memoirs on when and why a family group leaves a site; thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political history, referendums and voter surveys; mixed for private indecision; thin for divination as a decision aid after 1900.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Buridan; classical French for Racine; Luxembourgish for the 2015 coverage; Alsatian and Corsican for regional campaign material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1962 departures are wrapped in grief and blame; clinical doubt and depression cases are confidential; fortune-telling is sometimes tied to fraud cases; the Norman "maybe yes, maybe no" is a regional jibe, to trace, not to confirm.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose families' return-or-stay decisions would otherwise join the *chibani* check.
