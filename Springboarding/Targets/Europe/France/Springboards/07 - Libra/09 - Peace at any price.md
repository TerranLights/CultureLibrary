# France: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican; Portuguese; Vietnamese; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haute-Provence:** check how Manosque and Giono's readers remember his 1939 arrest and pacifism.
- **Corsica:** check how island businesses and press use or reject the *omerta* label when extortion is reported.
- **Brittany and the Vendée:** check diocesan responses after the CIASE report against its figures for western dioceses.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check victims' associations' accounts beside the 2010 inquiries.
- **Luxembourg:** check the press and community response to the 2015 report and apology.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check the long quiet around 17 October 1961 in Paris, in families' accounts.
- **Kabyle-French families:** check what *ḥshūma* keeps unsaid between generations, from second-generation writing.
- **Vietnamese-French families:** check silence about the sea crossings in children's writing and interviews.
- **Portuguese-French families:** check what is said and unsaid about leaving *a salto* under the Estado Novo.
- **Teachers in Île-de-France and other academies:** check #PasDeVague posts by region and the ministry's reply.
- **Jewish communities in Luxembourg:** check survivors' families' statements after the apology.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for appeasement, occupation and church inquiries; mixed for silences inside families; thin for extortion, which victims seldom report.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch for the Belgian church file's Flemish side (comparison); Portuguese for emigration memory; Vietnamese for boat-crossing accounts; Kabyle and Arabic for family silence.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse testimony concerns living victims and named accused; *omerta* is a slur on Corsicans when applied wholesale; 1961 and the Algerian war remain raw.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose families' silences would otherwise join the second-generation writing checks.
