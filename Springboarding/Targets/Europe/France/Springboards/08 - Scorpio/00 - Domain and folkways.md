# France: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican, Walloon; Maghrebi Arabic and Kabyle; Portuguese; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** how the culture faces death, power and what is hidden: death ways, sex ways, inheritance, shared resources, secrets and the ways people are thought to change.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany against Paris:** check grave visiting in Léon parishes beside Parisian cemetery use: the dead kept close or moved out.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check whether cemeteries and death notices still divide by Catholic, Protestant and Jewish community.
- **Corsica:** check family tombs on private land in the countryside against village cemeteries.
- **Savoie and the Alps:** check how families share summer pastures and settle quarrels over them.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check cremation and the scattering of ashes in Belgian funeral data against French figures.
- **Luxembourg:** check how couples of different nationalities hold money, in survey work on mixed households.
- **Portuguese-French families:** check whether the dead are buried in the home parish in Portugal or in France, by generation.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how estates with property on both shores are divided, in notaries' and families' accounts.
- **Antilleans in Île-de-France:** check how the wake (*vèyé*) and the following nights' prayers move into mainland flats and funeral homes.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check what becomes of the belongings of the dead, in Travelers' own writing; sources are few.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the record is deep for the history of death, sex surveys and funeral trade; patchier for household money and commons; scarce for Travelers' own accounts.
- **Map still open:** with no settled unit list, the Scorpio sheets take metropolitan France, Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg as the core; Flanders, the French Basque Country and Roussillon are comparison only; overseas departments appear only where a concept needs them.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Benedicti; Breton for ossuary inscriptions; Luxembourgish and German for funeral notices; Portuguese and Arabic for burial abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wills and notarial files stay closed for decades; sex-survey microdata need an access request; chosen death and inheritance law sit with Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose cremation and mourning practices would otherwise join the Wallonia-Brussels cremation check.
