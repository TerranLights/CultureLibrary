# France: Death (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Breton, Corsican, Occitan, Alsatian, Luxembourgish; Arabic and Kabyle; Hebrew; Vietnamese; Romani. **Concept in one line:** the end of a life, and how a culture understands dying, the dead, grief and mourning, and what death means.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provence and the lower Rhône:** check Vovelle's will series against other provinces' wills for the pace of change in requested masses.
- **Brittany:** check hymns and pardons for the *Anaon* as a living practice or a memory, parish by parish.
- **Corsica:** check who still sings *voceri*, and for which deaths.
- **The Massif Central (Creuse, Cantal):** check home deaths against hospital deaths in rural departments, beside Paris.
- **Lorraine:** check how Verdun's dead are visited by local families, school trips and veterans' groups.
- **Wallonia, Brussels and Luxembourg:** check how memorial-card wording changes from the 1900s to the 2000s.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check burial in Muslim sections of French cemeteries against sending the body home, and the role of repatriation insurance.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check the associations that tend or regroup cemeteries left in Algeria.
- **Jewish communities:** check the *kaddish*, *shiva* and naming ceremonies for the deported.
- **Vietnamese-French and the Chinese of Paris:** check *giỗ* anniversaries and Qingming visits in Île-de-France cemeteries.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how the dead are named or left unnamed in speech, with Williams as the start.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** sources run deep for wills, war dead and end-of-life debate; patchier for grief in daily life; few for Travelers and recent arrivals.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Montaigne; Breton for *Anaon* hymns; Corsican for laments; Hebrew for memorial rites; Vietnamese for ancestor rites; Romani for Manouche terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bereaved parents and Shoah families are living people; suicide counts by place can stigmatize towns; chosen death and its rules belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose death anniversaries would otherwise join the Vietnamese and Chinese check.
