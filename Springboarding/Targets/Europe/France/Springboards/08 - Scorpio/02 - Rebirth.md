# France: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Walloon, Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Luxembourgish; Arabic; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check adult conversions and confirmations by confession, in Catholic and Protestant church data.
- **Brittany:** check how adult new speakers of Breton (*néo-bretonnants*) describe taking up the language.
- **Normandy and the Breton coast:** check how Le Havre's residents speak of a rebuilt-modern city, beside Saint-Malo's walled town rebuilt as before.
- **The Limousin:** check how Oradour's new village and the ruin are lived with by residents.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check how former miners' families speak of Louvre-Lens and the heritage listing.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check the *Grands Feux* and Walloon theater as revivals, town by town.
- **Luxembourg:** check adult baptisms and changes of religion in archdiocesan and STATEC figures.
- **Maghrebi-French young adults:** check accounts of returning to religious practice and of leaving it.
- **West African-French and Antillean families:** check joining evangelical churches in Île-de-France, as members tell it.
- **Vietnamese-French Buddhists:** check teaching on rebirth in Île-de-France pagodas and at family funerals.
- **Former prisoners and people in recovery:** check how a "second chance" is described in their own published accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** church counts, reconstruction history and spiritism are well documented; recovery and reentry rest more on testimony; conversion figures outside the churches are partial.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the altarpiece's inscriptions; Walloon for the society's texts; Breton for new-speaker studies; Arabic for conversion terms; Vietnamese for pagoda teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** converts may face family or community pressure, so interviews need consent and care; recovery fellowships keep members anonymous; prisoners' files are closed.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose teaching on rebirth would otherwise join the Vietnamese Buddhist check.
