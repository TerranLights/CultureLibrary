*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Occitania (Toulouse, the Ariège, the Aude):** check how the Inquisition and Cathar history are presented in local museums and heritage branding.
- **Provence:** check who burns the *Caramentran* and what each town says it burns away.
- **Luxembourg:** check pilgrims' stated reasons at Echternach, residents against visitors.
- **Wallonia:** check how towns near cleaned-up steel and coal sites speak of the pollution.
- **The Nord:** check Metaleurop neighbors' accounts of gardening and children's health on lead-polluted soil.
- **Brittany:** check how the post-1944 purge of the Breton movement's collaborators is remembered inside the movement today.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check the purge in the annexed departments against the rest of France.
- **Maghrebi-French Muslims:** check how purity and ablution are taught in French-language manuals and mosque classes.
- **Jewish communities:** check Yom Kippur observance among families who otherwise practice little, in surveys of Jewish life.
- **Former Communist militants:** check memoirs of expulsion from the party.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** archives of the 1944-1953 purges are vast; the history of confession and medicine is well studied; venting, decluttering and detox rest mostly on press and market data.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for inquisitors' registers; Provençal for carnival texts; Luxembourgish and German for Echternach material; Dutch for the Belgian purge's Flemish files (comparison); Arabic and Hebrew for purity terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** post-war purge files name families still living; the women shaved in 1944 are Leo's and are not repeated here; harassment accusations involve lawsuits; eating disorders are a silence to approach with care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose purity rules and fasts would otherwise join the Muslim and Jewish checks.
