# France: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Old French; Breton, Corsican, Luxembourgish; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check how the 1532 union with France and its promised privileges are told in Breton movement histories.
- **Corsica:** check press and memoirs on how suspected informers were spoken of in the clandestine nationalist years.
- **Lyon and the Rhône:** check how Caluire is presented at the CHRD and in local commemorations.
- **Burgundy (Montceau-les-Mines):** check miners' union memory of the *jaunes*.
- **Wallonia:** check how towns and families with Rexist members speak of the Légion Wallonie today.
- **Luxembourg:** check how former VdB members were treated in their villages after 1944.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check how the 1958 Algiers speech is retold in associations and their press.
- **Harki descendants:** check where *harki* is used as an insult and how descendants answer it, in sociolinguistic and press studies.
- **Young men in housing estates:** check what *balancer* covers, in more than one ethnography and in rap lyrics.
- **Women and men:** check admitted infidelity and its judgment by sex in the Ifop series.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** wartime collaboration and Resistance betrayals fill archives in all three lands; adultery is rich in literature and polls; snitching codes rest on a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for the epics; Luxembourgish and German for VdB files; Dutch for the Flemish side of Belgian collaboration (comparison); Arabic for the insult's use; Corsican for nationalist-era press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *harki* used as a slur wounds living families; collaboration files name descendants; Corsican informer accusations touch unsolved killings; adultery and treason as law are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose words for betrayal of kin would otherwise join the search terms.
