*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Nord and French Flanders:** check how polder residents speak of the pumps and of floods when they failed.
- **Aquitaine (the Landes):** check forest-fire memory and the resin-tappers' accounts beside the older herding life.
- **The Alps and the Pyrenees:** check villages' accounts of RTM planting that closed grazing land.
- **Provence (Vaucluse):** check Wylie's village against the later restudies of it.
- **Corsica:** check how summer fires and their causes are argued over in the press.
- **Brittany and the Massif Central:** check where Rogations are still walked and who walks them.
- **Wallonia and Luxembourg:** check how reservoir villages tell the flooding of their land.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how *inshallah* and *maktūb* are used about plans, in sociolinguistic work, without reading them as fatalism.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check their own accounts of rules on halting sites and of being moved on.
- **Women in couples:** check coercive-control accounts across the three lands' helplines and studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** water, forest and mountain control are well archived; the psychology of control has a distinct French school; coercive control is a new term with a thin French literature so far.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Charron; Flemish dialect for polder records; Occitan for Landes and Pyrenean village records; Luxembourgish for the dam's local history; Arabic for fate terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic-violence files are closed; Travelers' sites raise policing disputes; arson accusations in Corsica can be defamatory; the law on spanking and on surveillance is Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose words for fate would otherwise join the Maghrebi-French check.
