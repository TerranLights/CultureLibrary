# France: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Middle French; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican, Luxembourgish; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here for now.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check local Crédit Mutuel *caisses* as village institutions against the national banks that came later.
- **Aquitaine (Bordeaux):** check how wine deals are sealed and broken between brokers, merchants and estates.
- **Provence (Marseille):** check trust across the city's districts and origins in local surveys, beside the medieval registers.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check *Noir Jaune Blues* results by region, with Flanders as comparison.
- **Luxembourg:** check trust between nationals, foreign residents and cross-border workers in survey items.
- **Corsica:** check trust in public authorities and in vaccines against mainland figures.
- **Guadeloupe and Martinique (overseas departments, named here because the concept needs them):** check trust in health authorities during COVID-19.
- **Maghrebi-French adults:** check trust in public institutions by generation in survey items.
- **Jewish communities:** check confidence in public protection after attacks, in community-run surveys.
- **People harmed by contaminated blood and their families:** check their associations' accounts of trust lost and not restored.
- **Young adults:** check ride-sharing and online-sale trust against older users.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** trust surveys are plentiful and comparable; the history of credit and banking is well kept; trust in everyday dealing (the slate, the handshake) needs fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for *Pathelin*; Latin and Provençal for Marseille's registers; German for early Crédit Mutuel records; Luxembourgish for Spuerkeess history; Arabic for *amāna* and *kelma*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the distrust thesis is politically loaded and should be tested, not assumed; trust in police and courts is Capricorn's; vaccine debates draw abuse online.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose family credit networks would otherwise join the village-credit check.
