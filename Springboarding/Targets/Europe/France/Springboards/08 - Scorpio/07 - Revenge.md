*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Old French; Corsican, Occitan, Breton, Alsatian, Luxembourgish; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; forgiveness and amends are its alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Corsica:** check the peace pacts and the mediators who closed feuds, in Busquet and in island archives.
- **The Nord and Artois:** check Muchembled's village cases against 19th-century assize records from the same area.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check how *revanche* textbooks of 1871-1914 and the 1918 return are remembered locally, apart from Paris's version.
- **Wallonia (Dinant, Tamines, Andenne):** check how the 1914 killings are commemorated and how German visitors are received (Germany as comparison).
- **Provence (Marseille):** check press framing of score-settling killings against residents' own accounts in the districts named.
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check victims' associations formed after 2015 for calls to forgive or to punish.
- **Pieds-Noirs, Harkis and Maghrebi-French families:** check how reprisals of the Algerian war are told in each group's associations, and where accounts cross.
- **Young women and men:** check exposure accounts and revenge images by sex of victim and poster.
- **Luxembourg:** check school mediation reports for how pupils' retaliation is handled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** feud history and literature run deep; national revanchism and wartime reprisals are well studied; everyday payback is mostly online talk and needs careful sampling.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for *Raoul de Cambrai*; Corsican and Italian for feud records; German and Dutch for 1914 sources (comparison); Arabic for *thaʾr* and *ʿafw*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** score-settling killings must not be tied to a district's people; Algerian war reprisals are grieved on many sides; private images may never be reproduced; private vengeance against state punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose teaching on forgiveness would otherwise join the religious-instruction pointer.
