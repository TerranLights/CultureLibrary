# France: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian, Luxembourgish; Arabic; Portuguese and Hebrew for older records. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one of its facets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** state secrecy and wartime clandestinity leave large archives, some still closed; the psychoanalysis of family secrets is a distinct French tradition; everyday privacy rests on Eurobarometer items and the press.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-century French for the *cabinet noir*; Portuguese and Hebrew for Bordeaux's New Christian records; Dutch for the Flemish half of the clandestine press (comparison); Luxembourgish and German for banking history; Arabic for *sitr*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family secrets and DNA findings involve living people; Algerian war testimony is painful and some archives stay classified; Masonic membership is private; privacy and secrecy law belong to Libra.
