# France: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Middle French; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Luxembourgish; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it and how it is used; legitimate authority itself is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Corsica:** check clan politics in towns outside Briquet's cases, and in the regional assembly since 2015.
- **Provence (Marseille):** check favors and patronage across the city's districts and over time.
- **Lorraine (Hayange and the Fensch valley):** check how the Wendel name is remembered by former steelworkers' families.
- **Brittany:** check the weight of regional business networks (for example the Club des Trente, *check*) in Breton politics.
- **The Nord:** check how long-held municipal majorities in the mining basin ended, in local press and voter studies.
- **Wallonia:** check the power of party presidents (*particratie*) over Walloon mayors and lists.
- **Luxembourg:** check the 2008 dispute that reduced the Grand Duke's role, and how residents spoke of it.
- **Maghrebi-French Muslims:** check who speaks for Muslims to the state (mosque federations, national councils) and how members judge them.
- **Women:** check office-holding by level across the three lands.
- **Young people in housing estates:** check who they name as holding power over their lives, in interview studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** elite sociology and political history are very rich; clientelism has a few strong case studies; hidden power invites conspiracy material that needs strict sorting.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for La Boétie; 17th-century French for Richelieu; Corsican for clan terms; Luxembourgish and German for CSV studies; Dutch for Flemish views of the 1988 takeover (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clientelism studies name living politicians; "hidden ruling class" claims slide easily into antisemitic myth and must be handled with care; authority and the spirit of the law are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose community leadership would otherwise join the Muslim-representation check.
