# France: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Occitan (Provençal and Languedocien), Breton, Luxembourgish; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provence (Marseille):** check how 1720 is commemorated, and whether it was recalled in 2020.
- **The Massif Central (Lozère):** check how the Gévaudan story is told in local museums and tourism.
- **Occitania (Aude, Hérault):** check memory of 1907 in winegrowing villages and cooperatives.
- **Brittany (north Finistère):** check how the *Amoco Cadiz* is remembered and whether it shaped later volunteering against spills.
- **Normandy (Rouen):** check how residents organized after Lubrizol.
- **Paris against provincial factories (Sochaux, Nantes):** check how May 1968 was lived in each.
- **Brussels and Wallonia:** check how residents of the Brussels communes named in the press lived the 2015-2016 measures.
- **Luxembourg:** check cross-border workers' experience of border controls in 2020.
- **Seine-Saint-Denis:** check excess deaths and frontline work in 2020 against richer departments.
- **Men and women:** check who calls crisis lines and who is reached by them.
- **Older people living alone:** check isolation and care during lockdowns, in EpiCov and local reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** plague and famine years left parish and city records in quantity; industrial and pandemic crises have rich official and press records; personal crisis is mostly in helpline reports and clinical studies.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-century French for 1720 and the Gévaudan; Occitan for 1907 songs and speeches; Breton for coastal oral memory; Luxembourgish and German for pandemic communications; Dutch for the Belgian inquiry's Flemish testimony (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** terror victims' families still speak in public and must be quoted with care; naming a commune as a "hotbed" stigmatizes its people; Lubrizol and Dexia involve lawsuits; emergency powers as law are Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose pandemic experience would otherwise join the Seine-Saint-Denis check.
