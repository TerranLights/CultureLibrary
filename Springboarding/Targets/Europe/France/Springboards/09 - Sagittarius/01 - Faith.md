# France: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Provençal Occitan, Corsican, Walloon; Antillean Creole; Maghrebi Arabic; Hebrew; Portuguese; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Normandy (Lisieux):** check who visits the basilica now and what they ask in written prayers.
- **Provence:** check whether actors and audiences of the *pastorales* call them devotion or Provençal heritage.
- **Corsica (Sartène):** check how the penitent and the town describe the vow behind the *Catenacciu*.
- **Brittany:** check where Breton hymns are still sung at Mass and where only at festivals.
- **Wallonia (Entre-Sambre-et-Meuse):** check how marchers set the saint against the march itself.
- **Luxembourg:** check the press archive for when faith turns from a public duty to a private choice in its wording.
- **Maghrebi-French Muslims:** check faith across three generations, using Cesari as the 1990s point.
- **Jewish communities of Champagne and Alsace:** check study circles and synagogue life against the heritage use of Rashi.
- **Portuguese-French:** check vows to Our Lady of Fátima paid on summer returns to Portugal.
- **Vietnamese-French Catholics:** check refugees' accounts of faith carried through flight and exile.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check the Catholic chaplaincy for Travelers and its own pilgrimages, which few sources cover.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** surveys measure belonging and attendance far better than trust in God; devotional literature is abundant for Catholics and thin for smaller faiths; Luxembourg depends on the press and church records.
- **Languages needed:** Breton and Provençal for hymns and plays; Corsican for confraternity texts; Medieval Hebrew for Rashi; Creole for carols; Portuguese for vows paid in Portugal.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** talk of losing faith touches family conflict; the clergy-abuse inquiry (the 2021 CIASE report) shaped trust in the Church and needs care; keep studies of Muslim faith apart from security writing.
- **Held for review:** devotional life and vows among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
