## 4. Flags
- **Data:** memory reports, historians' commissions and survey series are plentiful; how families settle disputed facts at home is little studied; Luxembourg relies on one research center.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Hebrew for the 1240 disputation; 17th-century French for Bayle; Bambara or Soninke for griot sources; Lingala for Congolese testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Algeria, Rwanda and the Congo remain open wounds for living families and veterans; access rules for the Rwanda archives need checking; conspiracy research should quote believers' words, not label groups.
- **Held for review:** truth commissions and testimony touching South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
