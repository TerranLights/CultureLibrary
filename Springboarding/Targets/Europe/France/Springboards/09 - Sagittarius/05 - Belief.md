## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Lyon area and the Dombes:** check local memory of the Guinefort cult against Schmitt's record.
- **Aquitaine and the Charentes (La Rochelle):** check how Protestant congregations use their confession today.
- **Corsica:** check who still tells of the *mazzeri*, and whether the belief is lived or retold as heritage.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether Gredt's legends are still taught in schools or used in tourism.
- **Wallonia (Eupen and the Liège area):** check how witnesses of the 1989-1990 wave describe their belief years later.
- **Burgundy (Dijon):** check Christmas customs and the debate about telling children the truth.
- **Brittany:** check beliefs in Sébillot's Breton chapters against today's collectors and storytellers.
- **Jewish communities:** check how belief and belonging separate by generation and by Ashkenazi and Sephardic origin.
- **Maghrebi-French and Turkish-French families:** check belief items (afterlife, the unseen) by generation in survey and interview studies, without assuming one pattern.
- **Antilleans:** check how *kenbwa* is spoken of in the islands and in Île-de-France.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** belief items run long in the EVS and the 1980s parascience surveys; folk-belief collections are old and uneven; current folk belief is mostly in ethnography and press.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Stephen of Bourbon's exemplum; German for Gredt; Corsican for *mazzeri* terms; Creole for *kenbwa*; Hebrew for creed and liturgy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labeling a minority's belief as superstition stigmatizes it; witnesses named in UFO and sorcery cases are private people; folk healing and sorcery shade into Pisces's magic ways and should be shared with that sign.
- **Held for review:** folk-belief and survey studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
