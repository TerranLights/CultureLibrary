# France: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish and German; Occitan (Cévennes); Alsatian; Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic and Kabyle. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not a finding of guilt).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Cévennes and the Gard:** check how the Camisard memory is invoked by Protestants and by later protesters.
- **Aquitaine and Saintonge:** check the Huguenot martyrs' names in local churches and family records, beside Crespin's lists.
- **Alsace:** check whether the 1974 occupation is claimed as an origin by later environmental groups on both banks of the Rhine.
- **Brussels and Wallonia:** check how Catholic, socialist and liberal networks reacted to Baudouin's refusal, in each community's press.
- **Luxembourg:** check how Catholic and secular groups responded to the 2008 episode.
- **Corsica:** check how nationalist militants and their families describe conviction against compromise, in their own memoirs.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Harkis:** check how each group judges the 1960 signers and the Jeanson network.
- **Algerian-French and Kabyle-French families:** check memories of fathers and grandfathers who raised funds or refused, in oral-history projects.
- **Farming regions (Brittany, the Southwest):** check how farmers speak of the crop uprooters and of animal-rights campaigns.
- **Jewish communities:** check how the Dreyfusard tradition is taught as a model of conviction.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** martyrologies, manifestos and the militants' dictionary give a long written record; surveys of political action are regular; the private cost of conviction is mostly in memoirs and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** 16th- and 17th-century French for Crespin and d'Aubigné; German and Alemannic for the Rhine occupation; Luxembourgish for the 2008 debate; Arabic and Kabyle for Algerian-war family memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Algerian-war choices still split families of veterans, Harkis and the FLN generation; Corsican militancy includes violence, which must be described and not endorsed; the content of the abortion and euthanasia statutes is for Libra and Capricorn to take up.
- **Held for review:** activist and dissenter biographies from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
