# France: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Portuguese; Breton, Provençal Occitan, Corsican; Romani (Manouche and Kalderash); Maghrebi Arabic; Soninke and Wolof; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check who walks the Tro Breiz and why, by age and home region.
- **Provence and the Alps:** check how shepherds describe the drove road against the festival version.
- **Corsica:** check how islanders speak of going to *u cuntinente* and of coming back for summer.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check how the *Belgica* voyage is taught and celebrated.
- **Luxembourg:** check how Portuguese and Cape Verdean families describe journeys home, beside Luxembourgers' memories of emigration.
- **Muslim families of Maghrebi and West African origin:** check how the pilgrimage to Mecca is saved for, prepared and celebrated on return.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check their own writing on the road, halts and settling, starting from Maximoff; sources are few.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check later return trips to Algeria, in travel accounts and associations' trip reports.
- **Antilleans:** check the yearly *désann an péyi* and its cost, in family accounts and association surveys.
- **Chinese of Paris (Wenzhou origin):** check migration routes and return trips, in the community's own associations and press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** travel writing, explorers' archives and tourism statistics run deep; seasonal and religious journeys are mostly in ethnography; Travelers' own written record is small.
- **Languages needed:** 16th-century French for Léry; Romani for Maximoff's terms; Portuguese and Cape Verdean Creole in Luxembourg; Wolof and Soninke for pilgrim accounts; Provençal for drove songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *gens du voyage* is an administrative label, and the rules on halting sites belong to Libra and Capricorn; explorers' accounts carry colonial views to be read critically; return trips to Algeria touch grief on all sides.
- **Held for review:** travel, pilgrimage and return-journey studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
