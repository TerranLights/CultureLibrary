## 3. Who to compare inside the nation

- **The Nord and Picardy:** check Picard and Ch'ti sayings on liars and cheats in dialect collections.
- **Antilleans:** check how *Konpè Lapen* tales are told to children in the islands and in Île-de-France.

- **Jewish communities:** check rabbinic teaching on honesty in trade as preached in French synagogues and study circles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** literature and moral writing on lying are abundant; experiments and corruption surveys are recent and national-level; everyday white lies are almost unrecorded.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for the *Provinciales*; Creole for trickster tales; Luxembourgish for proverbs; Picard and Walloon for dialect sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Romand case involves murdered relatives, so quote little and respect the family; youth slang studies can be misread as group portraits; the written tax and fraud codes are Libra's and Capricorn's ground, not this sheet's.
- **Held for review:** trickster tales and youth-speech studies from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
