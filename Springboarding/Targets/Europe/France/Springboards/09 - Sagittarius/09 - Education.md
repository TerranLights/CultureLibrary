# France: Education (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish and German; Latin (medieval university records); Corsican, Breton, Occitan; Arabic; Hebrew; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** formal and higher learning (schools, universities, degrees, scholarship and lifelong study) and the standing of the educated person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Île-de-France:** check how *grande école* and university students describe one another, in student press and surveys.
- **Franche-Comté (Montbéliard):** check what has changed for first-generation students since Beaud's fieldwork.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check how Louvain-la-Neuve, Liège and ULB students and alumni tell the 1968 split and the free-inquiry tradition.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether studying abroad is still a rite of passage now that the national university exists.
- **Corsica:** check who stays to study in Corte and who leaves for the mainland, and why.
- **Brittany and Occitania:** check Breton and Occitan university departments, and what students seek in them.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check the public theology faculties of Strasbourg and the Jewish seminary's Metz roots as distinct paths of religious learning.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French Muslims:** check who enrolls at Islamic institutes and how families weigh them against university.
- **West African-French students:** check present student associations against the FEANF record.
- **Chinese of Paris and the Loiret:** check how Montargis remembers the worker-students and how today's Chinese-French families speak of higher study.
- **Antilleans:** check the choice between the Université des Antilles (an overseas department, named because the concept needs it) and mainland universities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** official statistics on higher education are detailed and yearly; the social history of the educated is deep; religious higher learning outside the state is poorly counted.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for the *Chartularium*; Arabic for Islamic institutes' curricula; Hebrew for rabbinic training; Chinese for worker-student memoirs; Luxembourgish and German for student clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** imam training is a subject of public debate over state policy and funding; the Louvain split still stirs language politics; first-generation students interviewed in ethnographies are identifiable in small towns.
- **Held for review:** university departments and student associations tied to South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
