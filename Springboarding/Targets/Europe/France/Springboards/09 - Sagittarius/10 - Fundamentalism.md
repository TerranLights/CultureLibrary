# France: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Latin (traditionalist liturgy); Arabic; Turkish; Hebrew; Alsatian and German; Occitan (Vivarais). **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Versailles:** check traditionalist parishes, schools and scout groups against diocesan ones.
- **The Vendée and Brittany:** check how Catholic tradition is claimed by traditionalist groups and contested by local parishes.
- **Alsace:** check free churches and revival congregations, and links across the Rhine with German groups.
- **The Massif Central (Vivarais-Lignon):** check Brethren family life and schooling as members and leavers describe them.
- **The Alps (Isère and Savoie):** check how the 1995 deaths changed local attitudes toward small groups.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check how Muslim communities themselves spoke of strict teaching after the mosque's lease ended.
- **Luxembourg:** check for strict currents in any faith through press archives; the sources appear thin.
- **Maghrebi-French Muslims:** check quietist, political and non-practicing positions as separate, using Adraoui's interviews and others, without merging them.
- **Turkish-French communities:** check how mosque federations responded to creationist material and to strictness debates.
- **Antilleans and West African-French:** check Pentecostal and revival churches in Île-de-France for teaching on scripture and separation from the world.
- **Jewish communities:** check *teshuva* (return to strict observance) and Habad-Lubavitch growth in Paris and Marseille, in community sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the topic is heavily written about for Islam and traditionalist Catholicism, much less for evangelicals, Brethren and strict Jewish movements; surveys are few and their methods disputed.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for Salafi preaching; Turkish for creationist and mosque-federation sources; Hebrew for Orthodox teaching; Latin for traditionalist liturgy; German for Alsatian free churches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist" and "*secte*" are labels that harm the labeled; keep strict but peaceful believers apart from violent groups; terror victims and families of the 1995 dead are living people; any statute on cults or separatism is studied under Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** strict religious movements among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
