## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France against the provinces:** check where head offices, ministries and elite schools sit, as a map of where command is located.
- **Brittany:** check Breton-language memoirs for the rector's standing against the mayor's and the schoolteacher's.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check how chambers of trades and office habits from the German period (1871-1918) are remembered.
- **The Nord and the Hainaut:** check mining ranks (engineer, overseer, hewer) in miners' oral histories on both sides of the border.
- **Provence:** check Marseille's elected fishing *prud'homies* as authority held by a trade itself.
- **Occitania:** check how the *capitouls* of Toulouse are recalled as a model of civic office.
- **The Massif Central:** check Auvergnat café owners in Paris as patrons of later arrivals from home.
- **Luxembourg:** check which public posts ask for nationality or the three languages, and who holds them.
- **West African-French:** check how Soninke and other village associations in Île-de-France choose their officers.
- **Harkis:** check what families recall of the camp administrators who ran their lives after 1962.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check family heads and evangelical pastors as authorities, in their own accounts; sources are thin.
