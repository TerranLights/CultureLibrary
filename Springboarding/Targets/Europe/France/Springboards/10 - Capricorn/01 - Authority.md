# France: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Alsatian, Occitan, Corsican, Franco-Provençal; Maghrebi Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check how the 1675 revolt and the 2013 *bonnets rouges* each frame resistance to tax authority.
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check teachers' memoirs from the outer suburbs against those from central Paris on keeping order in class.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check how clergy paid under the local religious regime describe their standing (the rules themselves: Libra).
- **Corsica:** check local press on whose word villagers follow when state and nationalist claims compete.
- **Provence:** check regional polls from 2020 on trust in the Marseille institute against national health bodies.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Comité P (the Belgian police-oversight body) reports for Brussels communes against Walloon towns.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the grand-ducal family's authority is discussed in Luxembourgish and in French-language media.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French parents:** check whom they name, in their own testimony, as authority over children's schooling.
- **Antilleans:** check memoirs and union records of Antillean-born civil servants on commanding and being commanded.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check second-generation memoirs and comics on parents' say over study and career.
- **Jewish communities:** check the Consistoire (1808) as a religious authority shaped by the state.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check what Travelers write of mayors and gendarmes at halting sites; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on political, school and police authority; mixed on the family; thin on authority inside religious minorities, where bodies are recent and contested.
- **Languages needed:** Old French and Occitan for *Girart*; 17th-century French for Bossuet; Breton for 1675 parish songs; Arabic for mosque bodies' own statements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** torture in Algeria and the 1961 putsch still divide veterans' families; police-stop data feeds live political fights.
