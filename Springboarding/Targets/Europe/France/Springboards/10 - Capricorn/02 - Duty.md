# France: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian, Picard; Maghrebi Arabic; Antillean Creole; Portuguese. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany:** check whether vows to a saint are still paid at *pardons* as debts, in parish bulletins.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check Catholic and Lutheran catechisms of the region on duty to parents, side by side.
- **Normandy:** check whether local families and schools take on the care of Allied war graves.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check what miners' and textile workers' mutual funds required of members.
- **The Massif Central:** check what heirs who stayed on the farm say they owed parents, against siblings who left.
- **Corsica:** check the expected return for funerals and the August feast, in diaspora and island accounts.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check JOC alumni memoirs and union histories on duty to one's class.
- **Luxembourg:** check how nationals and foreign residents answer the civic-duty items in each survey wave.
- **Maghrebi-French families:** check how adult children describe caring for parents in France and across the Mediterranean.
- **Harkis' children:** check what they say the state owed their fathers.
- **Portuguese-French:** check vows to Fátima paid during summer returns.
- **Jewish communities:** check how the names wall at the Mémorial de la Shoah is used for family remembrance.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check duties to the dead and to elders in Manouche writers' own books; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for conscription, remembrance and caregiving; mixed for tax attitudes; thin for religious obligations and migrants' village dues.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for Novare; Dutch for the KADOC holdings on Cardijn; Martinican Creole for *dissidence* oral histories; Portuguese for vows.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who is owed remembrance is fought over between groups; caregiving data touch health records.
- **Held for review:** what members of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands owe kin abroad, left out of the village-fund pointer.
