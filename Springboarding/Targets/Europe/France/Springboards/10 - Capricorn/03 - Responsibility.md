## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brittany and the Vendée coast:** check how coastal towns spoke of the oil firm's blame after the *Erika*, in regional papers.
- **Occitania:** check how AZF victims' groups and Toulouse officials each say who was to blame.
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check whether the household mental load splits differently in Paris than in small towns.
- **The Nord:** check whom closing-down towns blame for lost plants (owners, the state, Europe), in local press.
- **The Lyon area:** check how residents of the chemical corridor south of the city speak of industrial risk and who answers for it.
- **Corsica:** check how island and national press assign blame for summer wildfires.
- **Wallonia:** check what Ghislenghien survivors' families say was owed to them.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the Luxair trial was covered in Luxembourgish and in French.
- **Pieds-Noirs and Algerian-French families:** check how each answers state statements on wrongs of the Algerian war.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese of Paris:** check what grown children say they carried in family shops, in second-generation oral histories.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check who answers for a halting site's upkeep, in Travelers' and mayors' accounts; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
