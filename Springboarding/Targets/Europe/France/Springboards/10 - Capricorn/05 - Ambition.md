# France: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian usage); Luxembourgish; Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic; Portuguese; Chinese (Wenzhou and Mandarin). **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check memoirs of provincial arrivals who "went up" to Paris against those who stayed home.
- **Brittany:** check how Breton-speaking families judged children who left for state posts, in memoirs and songs.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check vocational tracks and apprenticeships as aspirations, set beside the general *bac*.
- **Corsica:** check island and diaspora accounts of careers made on the mainland and overseas.
- **Wallonia:** check how Charleroi and Liège press speak of local people who made fortunes elsewhere.
- **Luxembourg:** check cross-border workers' career hopes against residents' in labor-force surveys.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check how the word *beurgeoisie* is used by and about the rising middle class.
- **Portuguese-French:** check second-generation accounts of moving from building trades to owning firms.
- **The Chinese of Paris:** check community press for *chūrén tóudì* and what parents say they want.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check how 1962 arrivals describe rebuilding a career from nothing.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check musicians' paths from family bands to festival stages; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school ambition, mobility and literature; mixed for business and politics; thin for aspirations inside small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century French for Molière; Portuguese and Chinese (Wenzhou dialect) for community testimony; Corsican for island accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tapie's career ran through criminal cases; writing on striving by origin can slide into "model minority" talk, so let each group speak for itself.
- **Held for review:** second-generation hopes and careers within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, outside the community checks above.
