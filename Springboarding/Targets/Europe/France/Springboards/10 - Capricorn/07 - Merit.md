# France: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Alsatian; Maghrebi Arabic; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check the share of elite-school entrants from a handful of central Paris lycées.
- **Brittany:** check memoirs of rural scholarship pupils from the Léon and Cornouaille.
- **Alsace and the Moselle:** check the local master-craftsman diploma as a trade route to standing.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check regional WorldSkills teams and the standing of trades in vocational schools.
- **Occitania and Provence:** check whether Toulouse and Marseille preparatory classes keep pupils in the region or send them to Paris.
- **Corsica:** check how island youth weigh competitive exams for posts on the mainland.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check students' and doctors' statements in the entrance-exam debate.
- **Luxembourg:** check how residents judge the recognition of diplomas earned abroad.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French graduates:** check their own accounts of applying, set beside the hiring-test results.
- **Jewish communities:** check how the 1940 exclusion from public posts and the return after 1944 are told as merit denied and restored.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check colonial-era scholarship students in Paris as an older merit route.
- **Antilleans:** check the civil-service exam as a route to the mainland, in migrants' accounts (overseas departments).
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how diplomas and exams are spoken of in Travelers' own writing; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, selection and the meritocracy debate; mixed for connections in hiring; thin for minorities' own views of merit.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for Jean de Meun; Dutch for the Belgian federal exams' other half; Vietnamese for colonial students' letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hiring tests name employers and are contested by them; the Vichy-era exclusion touches families still living.
- **Held for review:** graduates from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, omitted from the hiring-test and testimony checks.
