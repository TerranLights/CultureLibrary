# France: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian, Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic; Antillean Creole; Chinese (Wenzhou and Mandarin). **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check the regional survey's results for Paris against Seine-Saint-Denis, and residents' own words.
- **Alsace:** check how Strasbourg residents and officials explain the New Year's Eve burnings, year by year.
- **The Lyon area:** check the Minguettes (1981) and Vaulx-en-Velin (1990) unrest, in residents' and police accounts.
- **Provence:** check residents' accounts of order around drug-selling points in Marseille's northern districts, beside police statements.
- **The Atlantic coast and Aquitaine:** check later neighbor disputes over farm noise and smells after the Oléron case.
- **Corsica:** check how island and national press report night bombings as political disorder.
- **Brussels:** check how residents of the canal communes saw the 2016 police plan for their streets.
- **Wallonia:** check town reports on fly-tipping against residents' complaints.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the begging ban was argued in Luxembourgish and in French.
- **Maghrebi-French and West African-French youth:** check how words like *incivilités* and *sauvageons* were heard by those they described, in interviews.
- **Antilleans:** check what *dézòd* names in carnival and in unrest in Martinique and Guadeloupe (overseas departments).
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how mayors and Travelers each describe unauthorized encampments; sources are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policing, urban planning and safety surveys; mixed for noise and household order; thin for how small communities keep their own order.
- **Languages needed:** early 18th-century French for Delamare; Martinican and Guadeloupean Creole for *dézòd*; Wenzhou dialect and Mandarin for the Belleville marches; Luxembourgish for the begging debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** writing on riots and drug-selling districts stigmatizes places and their people; memory of the Commune is still partisan.
- **Held for review:** safety demands voiced by South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, not paired with the Belleville pointer.
