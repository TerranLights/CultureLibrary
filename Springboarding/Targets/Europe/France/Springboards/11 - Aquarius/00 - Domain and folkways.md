# France: The domain: community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican, Walloon; Kabyle and Maghrebi Arabic; Wolof. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for: friends, groups, hopes and ideals toward humanity, with the ways of settlement, association, hospitality and neighboring.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check neighborhood councils in the city against tenants' groups in the outer *grands ensembles*.
- **Brittany:** check who joins *cercles celtiques* and *bagadoù*, by age and by whether members speak Breton.
- **The Nord and Wallonia:** check who belongs to pigeon-racing (*colombophile*) societies, and their cafés and calendars.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check how many village *Vereine* (music, gymnastics, firefighters) a commune keeps, beside an inland commune of like size.
- **The Lyon area:** check the Beaujolais *conscrits* classes as belonging by birth year.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check who sits on the *comité des fêtes*, old families or newcomers.
- **Corsica:** check the Corsican *amicales* of Marseille and Paris and their ties to home villages.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether Portuguese residents join Luxembourgish clubs or found their own, and why.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check the associations founded in the early 1980s wave, and what they were for.
- **West African-French:** check how Senegalese *dahiras* in Paris and Marseille admit members and set dues.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check hometown *amicales* (Oran, Bône) and their reunions and bulletins.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how families meet at large summer halting areas; written sources are few, so start with their associations' publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Which lands:** the Aquarius set treats the Hexagon, Corsica, Wallonia, Brussels and the Grand Duchy as home ground because the nation's final extent awaits the author's ruling; Flanders, the French Basque Country and Roussillon are compared only, and overseas departments are named only where a concept needs them.
- **Data:** association counts and volunteering surveys are plentiful; hospitality and neighboring live mostly in ethnography, memoirs and fiction; small migrant and Traveler groups leave few records.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for confraternity statutes; Breton for circle newsletters; Luxembourgish for club anniversary books; Wolof for *dahira* life; Kabyle for ties to village assemblies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** membership rolls of religious and political groups name living people and may be closed; the law on associations belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose temple committees and cultural associations would otherwise join the association checks above.
