# France: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican; Kabyle and Arabic; Portuguese. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something, and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris:** check which local groups exist at all in Belleville compared with the 16th arrondissement.
- **Brittany:** check Plodémet today against Morin's study, from the town's own bulletins.
- **Normandy:** check how the word *horsain* is used for incomers, in local press and memoirs.
- **Alsace:** check how two congregations share one *simultaneum* church and its calendar.
- **Burgundy and the Nivernais:** check what local memory keeps of the joint-household communities.
- **The Massif Central:** check what holds a Creuse or Cantal village together after its school and café close.
- **Provence:** check Noailles's collectives as a test of inner-city community.
- **Corsica:** check the village *festa* and emigrants' summer return, when a scattered community gathers.
- **Brussels:** check the Marolles' neighborhood committees and who still belongs to them.
- **Luxembourg:** check how Italian, then Portuguese, arrivals entered the parishes and clubs of the southern mining towns.
- **Portuguese-French:** check *ranchos folclóricos* in Île-de-France and who keeps dancing in the second generation.
- **Harkis:** check the forestry hamlets (*hameaux de forestage*) as closed communities, in residents' testimony.
- **Jewish communities:** check how the Sarcelles community built its institutions from the 1960s.
- **Chinese of Paris:** check Teochew associations of the 13th arrondissement and their temple rooms as community centers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** village studies are old and many; neighborhood work is strong for Paris, Marseille and Brussels, slight for small Walloon and Luxembourg towns.
- **Languages needed:** Breton for Plodémet's own voices; Portuguese for *rancho* programs; Kabyle for village-of-origin ties; Luxembourgish for commune bulletins; Teochew and Mandarin for association notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *communautarisme* is a charge as much as a description, so record who uses it and of whom; residents of the forestry hamlets and of Noailles are still living with what happened.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose neighborhood and temple life would otherwise join the Paris comparison.
