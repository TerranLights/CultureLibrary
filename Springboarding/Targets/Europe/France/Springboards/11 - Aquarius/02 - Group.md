# France: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle; Armenian; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit: how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" ends and "them" begins, and what members are expected to do together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and its suburbs:** check rivalries named by housing estate or postcode, from youth workers' reports.
- **Brittany:** check the 1961 Morlaix artichoke-growers' occupation as farmers acting as one body.
- **The Nord:** check how Polish miners' parishes and societies stood toward French unions between the wars.
- **Lorraine:** check how Italian-origin steelworkers of the Fensch valley entered union sections.
- **The Lyon area:** check how Resistance networks (Combat, Franc-Tireur) kept cells apart and bound members.
- **Corsica:** check supporters' groups of Bastia and Ajaccio as carriers of local and island loyalty.
- **Wallonia:** check how socialist and Christian "families" (union, sickness fund, youth movement) divide a village.
- **Luxembourg:** check how cross-border workers are named as a group in residents' talk and comment threads.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check how *beur* was taken up, then refused, as a group name.
- **Harkis:** check the 1975 camp revolts by Harki sons as collective action.
- **Armenian-French:** check how party loyalties divided clubs and churches in Marseille and Lyon.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check how associations split by stance toward the regime in Vietnam.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how families form summer traveling convoys, from their own associations' accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** crowds, unions and factions are thick in historical writing; small youth groups and closed associations are known mainly through a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the bourgeois's diary; Polish for miners' society papers; Armenian for party press; Vietnamese for association bulletins; Corsican for supporters' banners and songs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** research on youth *bandes* touches minors and criminal files; pollsters' group labels by first name can harden the groups they describe; the dissolution of groups by decree is a legal matter for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose regional and religious associations would otherwise join the association-split checks.
