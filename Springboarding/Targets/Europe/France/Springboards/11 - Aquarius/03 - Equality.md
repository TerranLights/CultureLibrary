# France: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Arabic; Antillean Creole; Latin and Middle French (chronicles). **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Seine-Saint-Denis:** check gaps in doctors, post offices and teachers per head across the *périphérique*.
- **Brittany:** check how Breton speakers recall being called *plouc* at school or in the army.
- **Alsace:** check rural Jewish communities' own records of 1789-1791, set beside Grégoire's essay.
- **Normandy:** check Rouen's memory of Montaigne's visitors as a local story of strangers judging the town.
- **Corsica:** check how campaigns for the Corsican language to stand equal with French are argued.
- **Brussels:** check how French and Dutch speakers share posts in Brussels bodies, with Flanders only for comparison.
- **Luxembourg:** check how people who passed the Luxembourgish language test for citizenship describe it.
- **West African-French:** check the hostel rent strikes of 1975-1980 as a claim to equal treatment.
- **Antilleans:** check Antilleans in Île-de-France on equal treatment in public-service careers.
- **Harkis:** check how Harki veterans compare their pensions and honors with other veterans'.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how families describe carrying special travel papers until 2017.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese of Paris:** check how they answer being held up as a "model" group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** inequality numbers are abundant and long-run; how people feel about rank in daily manners is mostly in ethnography, film and memoirs; Luxembourg's gaps by nationality are well counted, Belgium's by origin less so.
- **Languages needed:** Old French and Latin for Jean le Bel; revolutionary French for Maréchal and Gouges; Creole for the Antillean sayings; Luxembourgish for language-test testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class slurs (*plouc*, *beauf*) and race talk must be quoted as objects of study; equality laws, quotas and anti-discrimination rulings are for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose accounts of being ranked by origin and color would otherwise join the Paris comparison.
