# France: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish and German; Breton, Corsican; Arabic; Latin and Middle French (estates and church records). **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole, and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Île-de-France:** check how Meaux and its diocese tell the story of the 1520s reformers today.
- **Brittany:** check who in Brittany sided with or against the CELIB's plans.
- **Champagne:** check Léon Harmel's Val-des-Bois works as a Catholic employer's reform, from workers' memories where they survive.
- **Alsace:** check how Alsatian Catholics fought the 1924 plan to bring French secular schooling to the region.
- **The Lyon area:** check the *Semaines sociales* (from 1904) as a Catholic reform movement, from its proceedings.
- **The Massif Central:** check how farm families recall the young Catholic farmers' (JAC) push to modernize after 1945.
- **Corsica:** check how islanders recall the eastern-plain land scheme (from 1957) and who received the farms.
- **Provence:** check Marseille residents' responses to the Euroméditerranée renewal (from 1995).
- **Wallonia:** check how 1886 is told in Liège and Charleroi labor memory.
- **Luxembourg:** check steelworkers' own accounts of the anti-crisis division.
- **Jewish communities:** check the Union libérale israélite (Paris, Copernic synagogue, from 1907) as religious reform, from its own bulletins.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check mosque associations' replies to state plans to train imams in France.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how families respond to school-attendance reforms for traveling children; sources are scarce.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the word *réforme* fills the press and political history; reforms seen from below (worker-priests, farm modernizers, steelworkers) need oral history and local archives.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French and Latin for Masselin and the Meaux circle; German and Luxembourgish for ARBED records; Corsican for village memory of the land scheme; Arabic for imam-training debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** imam training and the land scheme in Corsica remain charged; reform as statute or court decision belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose religious-reform debates would otherwise join the mosque and synagogue checks.
