# France: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Old French (fabliaux); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Arabic; Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Montreuil:** check who can afford to join shared-housing projects (*habitat participatif*) built on ideals.
- **Brittany:** check what the founding parents of the Diwan schools wrote of their hopes in 1977.
- **The Nord:** check how Lille and Saint-Denis claim Degeyter and the song.
- **Alsace:** check how Albert Schweitzer is taught as a moral model in Alsace, and his house at Gunsbach.
- **The Alps:** check Grenoble's municipal action groups under Hubert Dubedout (from 1965) as civic idealism.
- **Occitania:** check what Motivé-e-s members say of the movement twenty years on.
- **Corsica:** check how Corsican writers answer Rousseau's picture of their people.
- **Burgundy and Franche-Comté:** check Fourierist memory near Besançon, Fourier's birthplace.
- **Wallonia:** check how Mons presents Otlet and La Fontaine to visitors and schools.
- **Luxembourg:** check who volunteers with ASTM and similar NGOs, by nationality and age.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check how young volunteers describe aid trips to Morocco or Algeria with local associations.
- **Jewish communities:** check what young people sent to kibbutzim by youth movements wrote of the experience.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check refugee memoirs for how their authors judged the political ideals they met in France.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** utopian writing and its colonies are well studied; the everyday idealism of volunteers and ethical buyers is mostly in NGO reports and recent ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for *Cocagne*; Middle French for Christine; Hebrew for kibbutz letters; Vietnamese for refugee memoirs; Luxembourgish for NGO newsletters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sneers at idealists (*gauche caviar*, *bobo*) are political weapons; record who uses them. Kibbutz and aid-trip testimony touches politics in the Middle East and North Africa; treat it as personal accounts only.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and French idealists' journeys to South Asia, both set aside from the volunteering checks.
