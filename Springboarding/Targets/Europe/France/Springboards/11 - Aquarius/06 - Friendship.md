# France: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Old French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic, Kabyle; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris:** check newcomers' accounts of making friends in Paris against their home towns, to test the claim of Parisian reserve.
- **Brittany:** check how fishing crews from Concarneau or Le Guilvinec speak of friends on board and ashore.
- **The Nord:** check pit memoirs for friendships among miners of Polish, Italian and Moroccan origin.
- **The Lyon area:** check *mâchons* (early-morning meals) as gatherings of friends.
- **Gascony and the Landes:** check *bandas* (brass bands) at the Dax *féria* as friend groups; the Bayonne fêtes are comparison only.
- **Corsica:** check whether polyphony groups bind singers as friends, from singers' interviews.
- **Wallonia:** check friendships made in *harmonies* and *fanfares*, from society histories.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether schoolchildren's friendships cross the lines of home language.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check friendships across origin in estates, from Charef's novel and later testimony.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check memoirs for friendships with Algerian Muslim neighbors, and reunions after 1962.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check bonds among people who shared a boat or a camp before arrival.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how Manouche guitarists describe friendship with non-Traveler musicians; written sources are few.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the literary and philosophical record on friendship is unusually rich; survey data on friends are thinner and rarely compare men and women in depth.
- **Languages needed:** Old French for *Ami et Amile*; Middle French for Montaigne; Vietnamese for refugee testimony; Kabyle and Arabic for estate memories; Luxembourgish for youth interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** friendship that shades into favors and string-pulling belongs to Capricorn's merit and Libra's fairness; men's closeness should not be read through modern labels without the sources' own terms.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose friendship networks would otherwise join the cross-origin checks.
