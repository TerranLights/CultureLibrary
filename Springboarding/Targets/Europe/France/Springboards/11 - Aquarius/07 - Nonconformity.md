# France: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Middle French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Alsatian, Corsican; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far the departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** bohemias, avant-gardes and youth styles are written up at length; ordinary eccentrics and quiet dissenters in villages are hard to find outside memoirs and local press.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the *sotties*; 17th-century French for the libertines; Luxembourgish and German for Esch venue history; Arabic for testimony about leaving religion.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender nonconformity and leaving religion expose living people to harm; name only what they published. Conscientious objection and censorship as law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and spiritual seekers' turn toward South Asian teachers, both set aside from the counterculture checks.
