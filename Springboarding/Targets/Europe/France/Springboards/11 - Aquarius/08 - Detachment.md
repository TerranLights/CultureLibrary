# France: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Middle French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan; Arabic; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; some forms (calm, objectivity, equanimity) are prized, others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal) condemned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris:** check Métro etiquette campaigns and riders' accounts of silence, eye contact and help to strangers.
- **Brittany:** check how seafaring families describe waiting for news of men at sea and the calm expected of them.
- **Alsace:** check family silence about 1940-1945, in oral-history projects.
- **The Alps:** check how villagers near the Chartreuse describe living beside an enclosed order.
- **The Massif Central:** check farmers living alone in the Cantal and Lozère, from rural social workers' reports.
- **Provence and the Nord:** check field studies that test the outsiders' contrast of a warm South and a reserved North, rather than repeating it.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check whether Akerman's Brussels is recognized by local viewers or read as one director's vision.
- **Luxembourg:** check expatriates' forum accounts of contact with Luxembourgers, as outsiders' claims.
- **Pieds-Noirs:** check how Pied-Noir readers have answered *L'Étranger* as a picture of their Algiers.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check whether and how restraint in mourning is taught in families, from their own accounts.
- **Jewish communities:** check children of survivors on parents' silence and distance (Claudine Vegh, *Je ne lui ai pas dit au revoir*, 1979).
- **Vietnamese-French:** check what family memoirs say, if anything, about the calm asked of mourners at funerals and ancestor rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the spiritual, literary and film record of distance is deep; measured isolation is well counted; reserve between strangers is mostly folklore about regions that needs testing.
- **Languages needed:** Middle and 17th-century French for du Vair, Faret and the Quietists; Vietnamese for family memoirs; Luxembourgish for retreat-house records; Arabic for mourning teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** listening-line and social-work records are confidential and usable only in aggregate; regional "cold" and "warm" labels are stereotypes to test, never findings.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, and meditation and non-attachment teachings of South Asian origin taught in France, both set aside from this sheet.
