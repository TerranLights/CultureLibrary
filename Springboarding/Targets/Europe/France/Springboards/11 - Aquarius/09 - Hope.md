# France: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Middle French; Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican, Walloon; Arabic, Kabyle; Antillean Creole; Chinese. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris:** check who leaves petitions at the Sainte Rita chapel, from its books and its rector's accounts.
- **Brittany:** check Breton migrants' letters home from Paris (1900-1960) for plans and promises.
- **Picardy:** check how families who arrived under reunification in Picardy towns tell their first years.
- **Alsace:** check what young Alsatians expect of cross-border work in Germany and Switzerland.
- **Lorraine and Luxembourg:** check how cross-border commuters speak of the Luxembourg wage as a hope and a strain.
- **Provence:** check petitions at the Nice Sainte Rita church beside the Paris ones.
- **Rural France:** check farm families' views of the future in farm-union and agricultural mutual (MSA) surveys.
- **Corsica:** check young Corsicans' plans to stay or leave, from island surveys.
- **Wallonia:** check Walloon youth surveys on the future, with Flemish figures for comparison only.
- **West African-French:** check how hostel residents speak of the houses they are building in home villages.
- **Antilleans:** check how hope is voiced in Creole songs of migration to Île-de-France.
- **Chinese of Paris:** check New Year wishes and temple petitions in the 13th arrondissement, from community sources.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check petitions carried on pilgrimage, from their own associations' publications; little is written.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** monthly expectation surveys give long, comparable series; religious and folk hope sits in petition books, letters and film; regional differences in outlook are seldom broken down below the region.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Chartier; Arabic for reunification families' letters; Creole for migration songs; Chinese for temple petitions; Luxembourgish for youth surveys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** petition books and children's letters are personal and should be used only in aggregate; farmer suicide and cancer data concern grieving families.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose shrines and vows would otherwise join the petition checks.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Chartier's *Espérance* beside Péguy's *Porche*: hope in a lost war and on the eve of a coming one.
2. Download the CAMME series and the Eurobarometer item; chart them since 1990 for the three lands.
3. Search INA for "10 mai 1981 Bastille" and later anniversary programs.
4. Search Retronews for "catherinettes" by decade to trace the custom's spread and decline.
5. Search Cairn for "suicide des agriculteurs" and follow the studies cited.
