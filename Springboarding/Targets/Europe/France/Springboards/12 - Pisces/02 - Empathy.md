# France: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian and standard German; Occitan, Breton; Maghrebi Arabic; Antillean Creole. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check how hospital interpreters and cultural mediators work between doctors and patients from many backgrounds.
- **The Nord:** check reader letters and local press after mine disasters for how outsiders were asked to feel with miners' families.
- **Alsace:** check whether Alsatian-speaking elders feel understood in French-only care homes.
- **Brittany:** check Breton-language care for the old as a test of being understood in one's own tongue.
- **Occitania and the Midi:** check how Occitan song revivals invite listeners into rural lives that are fading.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Rimé's emotion-sharing studies against everyday talk in Brussels's mixed neighborhoods.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether listening lines and clinics answer in Luxembourgish, French, German and Portuguese, and who goes unheard.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check how second-generation writers ask French readers to see their parents' lives from inside.
- **Antilleans:** check what Antillean nurses in Paris hospitals say about reading patients across lines of origin.
- **Harkis:** check whether their children's testimony asks for understanding, and from whom.
- **Deaf communities:** check how French Sign Language users describe being understood or not by hearing people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** history of sensibility and psychological research are rich; measured empathy in Luxembourg and in minority languages is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for the Passion play; German for the *Einfühlung* literature that French writers answered; French Sign Language for Deaf accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** autistic people's accounts should lead over clinicians' claims about them; listening-line calls are confidential and usable only in aggregate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose patients and interpreters would otherwise join the cultural-mediator checks.
