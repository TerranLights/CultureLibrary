# France: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage and youth slang); Luxembourgish; Walloon; Breton, Occitan; Maghrebi Arabic (and its loans in French slang); Anglo-Norman for the oldest pointer. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check who leaves the city each weekend and for where, by income.
- **Brittany:** check drinking at *festoù-noz* and festivals against public campaigns on young people's binge drinking.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check the *estaminet* and the *ducasse* fair as weekly release in former industrial towns.
- **Normandy:** check what holiday homes on the coast mean to Parisian owners and to local residents.
- **Alsace:** check the Strasbourg Christmas market as an escape sold to visitors, and what residents say of it.
- **The Cévennes and the Ardèche:** check how the 1970s settlers and their children now speak of having left.
- **Provence and the Côte d'Azur:** check the coast as a place of escape for outsiders and a workplace for residents.
- **Corsica:** check the summer flood of visitors against islanders' own ways of getting away.
- **Wallonia:** check student and carnival drinking at Louvain-la-Neuve and Malmedy against health campaigns.
- **Luxembourg:** check where residents of a small country go for weekend getaways, and what they say they leave behind.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check the *chicha* lounge as a place where young people meet outside home and bar.
- **Vietnamese-French and Chinese of Paris:** check which leisure places (karaoke rooms, association halls) older residents of the 13th arrondissement use; sources are few, so start with neighborhood ethnographies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** drink, drugs, leisure time and holidays are well counted; daydreaming, fandom and withdrawal are thinly studied.
- **Languages needed:** Anglo-Norman for Benedeit; Walloon for carnival songs; French youth slang with its Arabic and Romani loans for today's terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** treatment records are confidential; drug users risk exposure; gambling-machine data are held by operators. Drug offenses as law are for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose film and leisure scenes would otherwise join the 13th-arrondissement checks.
