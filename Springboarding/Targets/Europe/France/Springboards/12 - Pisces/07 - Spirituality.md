# France: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Limousin Occitan, Corsican; Arabic (Sufi vocabulary); Hebrew; Latin for medieval texts. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (faith and religion as institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mystical writing and pilgrimage are richly documented; unaffiliated practice shows up mainly in polls and recent ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Bernard; Breton for the *pardons*' hymns; Arabic for Sufi litanies; Portuguese for Fátima devotion abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some seekers' groups are under watch as possible cults, a label they reject; Rabhi's legacy is now disputed; Cathar "spirituality" tourism blurs history.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-origin religions, meditation and body disciplines, and their centers and teachers in the nation's lands, held back from the seekers and retreat comparisons.
