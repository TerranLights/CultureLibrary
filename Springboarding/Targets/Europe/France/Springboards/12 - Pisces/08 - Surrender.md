# France: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic; Vietnamese (for the Indochina war's other side); Latin for the oldest pointer. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check how city hospitals and private clinics speak to families when treatment stops.
- **The Nord and the Pas-de-Calais:** check how the 1940 debacle and exodus are told in towns the armies crossed.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check how annexation after 1871 and 1940 is remembered as submission or survival.
- **Normandy and Brittany:** check how Catholic families of the West speak of a "good death" and the last sacraments.
- **The Lyon area and the Alps:** check mountain-rescue accounts of when searchers decide to stop looking.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check how Pied-Noir families who settled there tell the 1962 departure as a forced letting go.
- **Corsica:** check sayings of resignation in Corsican and when they are used.
- **Wallonia:** check how the May 1940 capitulation is told in Walloon towns, against Brussels memory.
- **Luxembourg:** check how the occupation of 10 May 1940 without fighting is remembered and taught.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check whether young people use *mektoub* as faith, as joke or as comfort.
- **Harkis:** check how families describe being disarmed and left behind in 1962.
- **Vietnamese-French:** check how families who left after 1975 speak of giving up a homeland.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** war memory, devotional writing and end-of-life care are rich; private letting go lives mostly in self-help and online.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Caesar; Vietnamese for the Viet Minh side of 1954; Luxembourgish for local war memory; Maghrebi Arabic for the faith sense of *mektoub*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Lambert family's quarrel involves living relatives; 1940 and 1962 still divide families; calling any group "fatalistic" is a stereotype to avoid. End-of-life law is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose words for fate and acceptance would otherwise join the *mektoub* checks.
