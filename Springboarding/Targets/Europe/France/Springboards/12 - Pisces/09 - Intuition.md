# France: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian and German (almanacs); Corsican; Arabic; Antillean Creole; Middle French for the oldest pointer. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check how trading-floor and start-up staff describe "gut" calls against the engineering schools' ideal of reasoning.
- **Brittany:** check fishermen's accounts of sensing weather and shoals, against the instruments on board.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check whether dowsers are still called to farms to find wells.
- **Alsace:** check almanac weather signs against what older farmers say they trust today.
- **Burgundy and the Massif Central:** check the cattle dealer's eye at fairs against weighing and grading by machine.
- **Provence:** check the Nostradamus trade in Salon-de-Provence as tourism and as belief.
- **Corsica:** check how, when and to whom the *signadora*'s words are passed on, as practitioners tell it.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check Belgian skeptics' tests of seers against the clients of seers in Brussels.
- **Luxembourg:** check whether Luxembourgish weather and harvest sayings are still collected, taught or used.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check *firāsa* as a way of judging people, and where it is talked about.
- **West African-French:** check the role of the *marabout* as adviser on decisions; sources are thin, so start with clients' accounts.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check how horse-dealing and scrap-trading families describe judging a deal; few written sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** philosophy of intuition and expert-judgment studies are rich; belief in second sight is polled now and then; seers' work leaves little in print.
- **Languages needed:** Middle French for Nostradamus; German and Alsatian for the almanacs; Corsican for the *signadora*'s words; Creole for Antillean seers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paid seers can exploit clients in crisis; the *signadora*'s words may be secret and should not be pried out; gut judgments of strangers can be bias and must be studied as such.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose seers and readers of signs would otherwise join the *gadèdzafè* and *marabout* checks.
