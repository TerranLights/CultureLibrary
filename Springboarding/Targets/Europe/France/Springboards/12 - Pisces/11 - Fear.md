# France: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** France, region Europe, with French-speaking Belgium (Wallonia, Brussels) and Luxembourg. **Language(s) for searching:** French (with Belgian and Luxembourg usage); Luxembourgish; Alsatian; Breton, Occitan, Corsican; Maghrebi Arabic; Romani; Latin for the oldest pointer. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it; worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Paris and Île-de-France:** check how fear of attack changed use of terraces, metro and concert halls after 2015, by district.
- **Brittany:** check fishing families' fear of the sea, its prayers and its taboos, as families tell them.
- **The Nord and Picardy:** check fear in the mining basin: firedamp, collapse, and what families did before a shift.
- **Alsace and Lorraine:** check whether fear of war and invasion still shapes how older people talk about the border.
- **The Alps and the Pyrenees:** check fear of wolves and bears among herders, against urban views of the predators' return.
- **Provence and the Midi:** check summer fire fear and the evacuation habits of hill villages.
- **The Massif Central:** check werewolf and *bête* legends in Lozère and Auvergne as told today.
- **Corsica:** check how villagers speak of omens of death and the fear around them.
- **Wallonia and Brussels:** check fear in Brussels's neighborhoods during the 2015-2016 alerts, against how Walloon towns lived the same alert levels.
- **Luxembourg:** check the cross-border nuclear fear against how residents rate other risks.
- **Maghrebi-French:** check how parents explain a child's *khlaʿa* and to whom they take it.
- **Jewish communities:** check how fear after antisemitic attacks has changed school and synagogue routines.
- **Manouche and other Travelers:** check fear of the dead and of certain places; sources are few, so start with Romani studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the history of fear and insecurity surveys are rich; private fears, children's fears and men's fear are thin and need memoirs and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Venette; Luxembourgish and German for the Cattenom campaigns; Maghrebi Arabic for fright illness; Romani for Traveler accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** attack survivors and families of the dead are living people; the *Père Fouettard*'s blackened face is now contested; reporting fear of a group can feed it, so record fears as fears.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose fright illnesses and protective practices would otherwise join the *khlaʿa* checks.
