*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Berlin against other big cities (Hamburg, Cologne, Leipzig):** named local speech styles that may set different thresholds for brusqueness.
- **The former GDR before and after 1989:** from petitions and quiet workarounds to demonstrations, then to workplaces reshaped after reunification.
- **Women and men, girls and boys:** self-assertion courses target girls; check what boys are taught instead.
- **Ruhr and Saar industrial workers against office staff:** collective speaking-up against individual self-promotion.
- **Young people of Turkish, Kurdish and Arab heritage:** assertion against gatekeepers in school, police checks and job searches.
- **Resettlers from the former USSR:** arrival at an age when asking officials for things was new or risky.
- **The Danish minority and North Frisians:** claims made through a party and associations rather than as individuals.
- **Sorbs in Lusatia:** collective claims to land and language against mining and assimilation.
- **Village and city:** who may speak at a council meeting or a parish gathering.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for linguistics, consumer complaints and debate; mixed for regional differences beyond Berlin and the East-West split.
- **Languages needed:** German, including regional speech; Danish, Sorbian, Turkish and Russian for the groups above.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** popular talk of "German directness" is a stereotype to test, not a premise; GDR petitions may name private people.
- **Law:** legal complaint channels, works-council rights and courts belong to Libra; hierarchy and obedience to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
