## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West after 1990:** founding under the Treuhand sell-off and its aftermath against long-running western family firms.
- **Family-firm regions (Württemberg, Ostwestfalen-Lippe, the Sauerland) against the Ruhr's big-firm past:** who starts things where large employers once decided.
- **Hamburg, Bremen and Lübeck:** merchant traditions set against inland craft towns.
- **Berlin and Leipzig startup scenes against rural districts:** initiative with and without capital.
- **Turkish-, Vietnamese-, Arab- and Polish-heritage founders:** need-driven against chosen founding.
- **Rural clubs and volunteer brigades against urban networks:** who starts the work bee.
- **Women founders:** check rates and barriers.
- **Sorbian and Frisian language activists:** founding programs for a small language.
- **Flood-hit valleys (Elbe, Ahr):** spontaneous help in a crisis.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; founding, volunteering and startup surveys are regular and public. Thin for initiative inside households and among children.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Vietnamese and Arabic for migrant founders; Sorbian for language-revival records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** military doctrine sources need historical care, since Wehrmacht-era uses of mission command are contested; disaster accounts may name private victims.
- **Law:** ballot and petition rules belong to Libra; here only who starts them.
- **Held for review:** none.
