## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Sea and Baltic coast and island people:** volunteer lifeboat crews and storm-flood memory.
- **Ruhr and Saar mining towns:** collective courage underground and rescue brigades.
- **Saxony:** a climbing tradition and the 1989 demonstrations, two very different kinds of nerve.
- **East and West veterans and the generations after 1945:** NVA, Bundeswehr and foreign-mission veterans, and families wary of military pride.
- **Conscientious objectors and peace churches against fencing fraternities:** refusal and the duel as rival models.
- **Women:** the Rosenstraße protest (Berlin, 1943), rubble clearance, and later civic courage.
- **Turkish-heritage families after Mölln, Solingen and the NSU murders:** survivors who spoke out.
- **Jewish Germans and their rescuers:** courage seen from both sides.
- **German Sinti and Roma:** the civil-rights campaigns for recognition of persecution.
- **Rural volunteer firefighters against city professionals.**
