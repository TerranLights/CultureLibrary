## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Frisians, North Frisians and Dithmarschen people:** a remembered peasant self-rule, to test against present attitudes to help.
- **Hamburg and Bremen:** civic independence as a city's self-image.
- **Saxony and Thuringia:** "free state" labels, allotment origins, and shortage-economy self-help.
- **East and West after 1990:** mass unemployment, welfare debates and pride in managing alone.
- **Rural households that garden and hunt against urban renters.**
- **Young adults in university towns against those in rural districts:** age of leaving home.
- **Turkish-, Kurdish-, Arab- and Russian-German families:** several generations and earners under one roof; remittances.
- **Disabled adults:** workshop wages against open employment.
- **Elders cared for at home against those in homes.**
- **The "Reichsbürger" milieu:** a fringe claiming independence from the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for living arrangements, care, gardens, food banks; mixed for emotional independence and help-seeking.
- **Languages needed:** German; Low German and Frisian for coastal history; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for heritage households.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "Reichsbürger" scene overlaps with extremism; food-bank users and care recipients need anonymity; welfare debates use loaded words.
- **Law:** welfare entitlements and inheritance belong to Libra; here only the lived side.
- **Held for review:** none.
