## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Westphalia, Münsterland, Lower Saxony and the Rhineland:** church brotherhoods against secular shooting clubs, both crowning a yearly winner.
- **East Frisia and Oldenburg:** road-throwing contests between villages.
- **Schleswig, with its Danish minority:** ring riding shared across languages.
- **Lower Lusatia's Sorbs/Wends:** harvest contests on horseback.
- **East and West:** socialist emulation and state sports schools against club sport and markets.
- **Thuringia:** the singers' contest legend as regional pride.
- **States as rivals:** school results, universities, football.
- **Girls and boys, women and men:** who competes, in what, and who watches.
- **Turkish-, Kurdish-, Polish- and Russian-German clubs:** rivalry inside amateur leagues.
- **Board-game players and esports players:** two modern arenas with different ideals of play.
