## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men against other drivers:** speed and alcohol in crash data.
- **Rural youth and night-time driving after village discos:** a long-running pattern to check in both East and West.
- **East and West after 1990:** check road-death trends when new cars met old roads.
- **Car scenes in the Ruhr, Cologne and Berlin:** tuning, racing and their policing.
- **The Eifel race-track region:** a destination for motorcyclists and drivers from everywhere.
- **Lake and coast regions (Mecklenburg lakes, North Sea and Baltic beaches):** swimming risk.
- **Big-city neighborhoods on New Year's Eve:** fireworks and street disorder.
- **Small investors across generations:** the 1990s share boom and today's crypto traders.
- **Communities that abstain from alcohol (many Muslims, some free-church resettlers):** risk profiles to check, not assume.
- **Urban explorers ("Lost Places") and train surfers:** subcultures that seek danger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, drinking, drowning and gambling; mixed for daring as admired style.
- **Languages needed:** German, including old chapbook German; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for heritage communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths and injuries involve private victims; addiction data need anonymity; avoid tying risk to any ethnic group.
- **Related signs:** courage (this sign), escape (Pisces); traffic law and negligence belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
