# Germany: Domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German (standard, plus Low German); minority languages Upper and Lower Sorbian, Danish, North and Saterland Frisian, Romani; migrant languages Turkish, Russian, Polish, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (money, saving and debt, thrift and display, resources, land, food, dress and wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanseatic cities (Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck):** merchant traditions, a separate history of how wealth was shown or kept quiet.
- **Rhineland and the Ruhr:** Catholic Rhineland against industrial working-class households.
- **Westphalia and Lower Saxony:** farm households, single-heir land customs, Schützen festivals.
- **Württemberg and Baden:** Pietist and partible-inheritance areas; strong saving and building lore to test, not assume.
- **Hesse, Palatinate, Saarland:** wine and apple-wine regions, border trade.
- **Saxony, Thuringia, Saxony-Anhalt, Brandenburg, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania:** forty years of GDR property and shortage, then the 1990s.
- **Berlin:** divided-city consumption.
- **Sorbs (Lusatia), Danes and North Frisians (Schleswig), Saterland Frisians, German Sinti and Roma:** recognized minorities with their own household and dress traditions.
- **Turkish-, Russian-, Polish- and Arab-heritage Germans; Jewish communities; descendants of postwar expellees:** distinct migration paths and losses.
- **Neighbor comparison only:** Bavaria (a separate target nation). **Diaspora comparison only:** Pennsylvania Dutch and Amish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Statistics, consumer history and folklore archives are deep; minority and migrant households are thinner.
- **Lands:** Germany's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this set of sheets is written for the core German lands, with Bavaria outside them.
- **Languages needed:** German, including Fraktur print and Kurrent/Sütterlin script; Low German; Sorbian, Danish, Frisian for minority sources; Turkish, Russian, Polish, Arabic for migrant ones.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era seizure of Jewish property and its restitution; antisemitic money tropes in older sources; GDR expropriations; Sinti and Roma persecution; data-protection limits on household microdata.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Destatis: search "EVS Ausstattung privater Haushalte" and pull the latest tables by state.
2. Bundesbank: search "Private Haushalte und ihre Finanzen" and "Zahlungsverhalten in Deutschland" for the latest reports.
3. Library catalog: "Hausväterliteratur" and "Henriette Davidis Kochbuch" for digitized editions.
4. Search "Dokumentationszentrum Alltagskultur der DDR Sammlung" and "DOMiD Sammlung" for online object records.
5. Search the national intangible heritage register for food and craft entries, then the Sorbian institute's catalog for "Tracht" and "Hochzeit".
