# Germany: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German and Low German; North Frisian, Danish and Sorbian for minority sources; Turkish, Russian, Polish, Arabic for migrant households. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** the GDR job guarantee, then the unemployment of the 1990s; compare the meanings of *Sicherheit*.
- **North Sea and Baltic coasts (North Frisia, East Frisia, Hamburg):** flood risk and dike communities.
- **Ruhr and Saarland mining regions:** mutual funds, union security, and the end of the mines.
- **Civil servants against self-employed and gig workers:** the security of a lifetime post against its absence.
- **Lusatia:** Sorbian and German villages facing mining relocation and the coal phase-out.
- **Turkish-German families of the recruitment era:** savings, return plans, and pensions earned in Germany.
- **Russian-German resettlers and Jewish arrivals from the former Soviet Union:** pensions and security after migration in later life.
- **Refugees since 2015 (Syrian, Iraqi, Ukrainian, among others):** insecurity of status and housing.
- **German Sinti and Roma:** safety after persecution.
- **Neighbor comparison only:** Bavaria, for neighboring saving and insurance habits in the same survey series. **Diaspora comparison only:** Amish refusal of commercial insurance as a German-rooted contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; insurers, statistics offices and the Bundesbank publish heavily.
- **Languages needed:** German; Low German and North Frisian for coastal flood lore; Russian and Turkish for migrant pension and saving stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stasi files have access rules; the national stereotype of "German angst" must be treated as a trope to examine, not a finding; refugee data need care; social-insurance law itself belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
