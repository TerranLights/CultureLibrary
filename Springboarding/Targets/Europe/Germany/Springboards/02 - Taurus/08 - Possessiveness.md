## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ruhr and Rhineland football regions:** territorial loyalty and derby rivalries as ritualized possessiveness.
- **North German coast (East and North Frisia):** fishing grounds and local rights; newcomers and second-home owners.
- **Allotment-garden milieus, East and West:** plot boundaries and the *Datsche* tradition in the East.
- **East and West family models:** two postwar family policies on mothers and work; parental attachment patterns to compare, not assume.
- **Turkish-, Arab- and Kurdish-heritage families:** control of daughters and partners as a contested public topic; voices from inside the communities first.
- **Russian-German resettler families:** gender roles in a second migration.
- **Sorbian and Danish minorities:** guarding language and institutions as a form of collective holding on.
- **Urban polyamory and sharing milieus (Berlin, Leipzig, Hamburg):** the counter-pole.
- **Neighbor comparison only:** Bavaria, for village and club rivalries next door. **Diaspora comparison only:** German-speaking Hutterite colonies, which hold goods in common.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Partner-violence data and literature are rich; everyday jealousy and stinginess are less studied.
- **Languages needed:** German, Middle High German; Turkish and Arabic for counseling materials; Russian for resettler families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "honor"-linked control in migrant families is easily stereotyped; the "towel on the deckchair" image is a national cliché to examine, not a finding; shelter locations are confidential; the law on stalking and violence belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
