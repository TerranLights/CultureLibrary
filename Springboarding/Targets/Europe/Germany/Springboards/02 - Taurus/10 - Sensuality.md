## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhineland:** carnival, wine and Catholic festivity.
- **Palatinate, Moselle, Baden and Saale-Unstrut wine regions:** harvest feasts and wine taverns.
- **East Frisia:** tea ritual and coastal food.
- **Württemberg Pietists:** Pietist teaching on indulgence, set beside the region's own cuisine and feasts.
- **Saxony and Thuringia:** Christmas markets, cakes, sausages and the GDR naturist tradition.
- **Hanseatic north:** coffee trade, fish, and Protestant attitudes to display, to examine.
- **Lower and Upper Sorbs:** Spreewald foods, Easter customs, festive dress.
- **Danish minority:** *hygge* and Nordic food in Schleswig.
- **Turkish-, Arab- and Russian-heritage Germans:** bathhouses, tea gardens, *banya*, home cooking, and fasting months.
- **Neighbor comparison only:** Bavaria (beer gardens and its own festivals belong to that target). **Diaspora comparison only:** Pennsylvania Dutch food customs.
