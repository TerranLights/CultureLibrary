# Germany: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Low German; Upper and Lower Sorbian; North Frisian; Danish; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for the largest immigrant communities; DGS. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the ideas about what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Low German and Frisian north:** compare the short greeting and the long chat (*Klönschnack*), not a single "taciturn" image.
- **Swabians in Württemberg:** the saying "Nix gsagt isch gnug globt" (*check* spelling) as a lead on praise and silence.
- **Rhinelanders (Cologne, Düsseldorf):** carnival speeches (*Büttenrede*) as licensed public mockery.
- **Saxons and Thuringians:** accent stigma inside Germany, and how speakers handle it.
- **East and West:** workplace talk and feedback across the 1990 merger.
- **Turkish-, Kurdish- and Arabic-speaking families:** children as interpreters at offices and clinics.
- **Russian-German Aussiedler:** a German identity with Russian as the home language.
- **The Deaf community:** a signed language with its own address and turn norms.
- **Sorbs:** two-language households and church services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Corpora, pragmatics and media studies are deep; regional and minority conversation data are thinner.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for family talk; DGS for Deaf sources; Low German and Sorbian for minority speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** free speech and its legal limits are law: point to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit). The gender-language debate is polarized; report positions, not verdicts.
- **Held for review:** none.
