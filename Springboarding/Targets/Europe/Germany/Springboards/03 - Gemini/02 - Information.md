# Germany: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** GDR censorship, West TV and the Stasi left different histories of trusting official information.
- **Eastern Saxony:** the area without West TV, against the rest of the GDR.
- **Hamburg:** home of much national press and broadcasting, against regions where local papers have closed.
- **Rural Brandenburg and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania:** broadband gaps and thinning local papers.
- **Danes of South Schleswig and Sorbs of Lusatia:** their own daily or weekly papers and broadcasts.
- **Russian-German households:** exposure to Russian-language state media; handle with care.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-German households:** Turkish national media against Kurdish outlets.
- **Syrian and other Arabic-speaking arrivals after 2015:** information through messaging groups.
- **Older and younger people:** broadcast against platform news.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Media-use and trust surveys are annual; the GDR side is deep in archives and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** German; Danish and Sorbian for minority press; Turkish, Kurdish, Russian and Arabic for diaspora media use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Stasi files hold personal data with restricted access; Nazi propaganda sources need critical framing; data-protection law and press law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none.
