## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** GDR crèches and polytechnic learning against West German family care and the half-day school.
- **Craft regions and trades:** the guild towns and the trade fraternities, where learning by apprenticeship runs deepest.
- **Farm families in the north and east:** children taught by pitching in on farms.
- **Sorbs and Danes:** immersion and minority schools as language learning.
- **Turkish- and Arabic-speaking families:** tutoring, mosque-run classes and children learning German alongside home languages.
- **Russian-German families:** schooling habits brought from the Soviet system.
- **Recent adult arrivals:** integration courses as a second start in learning.
- **Deaf learners:** bilingual DGS and German teaching.
- **Adults with low literacy:** learners who never finished.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Vocational, adult-learning and panel data are deep; learning at home and by watching is mostly in panel items and ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** German; Sorbian and Danish for minority schooling; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for family studies.
