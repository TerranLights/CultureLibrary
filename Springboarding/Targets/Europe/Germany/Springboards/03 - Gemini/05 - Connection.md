# Germany: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Danish and Sorbian for minority networks; Turkish, Kurdish, Russian and Arabic for immigrant ties. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not (belonging is Cancer's; friendship and community are Aquarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhineland:** *Klüngel* as a named local system of favors.
- **The former GDR:** networks of favors under scarcity, and what happened to them after 1990.
- **Families split by the border:** East-West ties kept by parcels, letters and visits.
- **Hanseatic cities:** merchant and family networks centuries deep.
- **Thinning rural regions** (Uckermark, Altmark, Vorpommern): isolation of the old as the young move away.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-German communities:** *hemşehri* ties and cross-border family life.
- **Russian-German Aussiedler:** *blat* habits, and kin still in Kazakhstan and Siberia.
- **Arabic-speaking refugees:** families split by asylum rules; *wasta* in a new setting.
- **Sorbs:** Catholic parishes of Upper Lusatia against Protestant Lower Lusatia as separate webs.
- **University-educated milieus:** fraternity and alumni networks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness and networks in surveys; GDR favors and *Klüngel* are mostly oral history and journalism.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Kurdish, Russian and Arabic for migrant ties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some fraternities are linked to the far right, so read sources critically; corruption cases touch law (Libra); church records hold personal data with access rules.
- **Held for review:** none.
