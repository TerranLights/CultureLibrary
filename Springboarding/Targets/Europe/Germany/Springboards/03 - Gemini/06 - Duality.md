# Germany: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Upper and Lower Sorbian; Danish; Turkish and Russian for the largest two-country communities. **Concept in one line (proposed):** living with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** two states for forty years; the commonest two-ness in German talk.
- **Catholic and Protestant regions:** the Rhineland and Münsterland against the Lutheran north and east.
- **Saarland:** a border region with a French period.
- **South Schleswig Danes and North Frisians:** minorities with two loyalties along an old border.
- **Sorbs:** a Slavic people with two names for everything, on signs and in church.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-Germans:** "Almancı" in Turkey, "foreigners" in Germany; Kurds with a third identity apart from both.
- **Russian-Germans:** Germans abroad, Russians at home.
- **Southwest and Rhineland carnival towns:** the mask as a seasonal second self.
- **People with two passports:** dual citizenship as a lived state (its rules go to Libra).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and East-West surveys; thinner on how ordinary people hold paired worldviews.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Kurdish and Russian for two-country communities; Sorbian and Danish for minorities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Ossi/Wessi" labels carry stereotype, so report them as terms; Nazi twin experiments are a hard topic for twin research history; Stasi files show double lives (informers in families) with privacy limits.
- **Held for review:** none.
