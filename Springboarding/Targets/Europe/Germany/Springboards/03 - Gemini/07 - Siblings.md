# Germany: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Low German; Upper Sorbian; Danish; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for immigrant families. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Single-heir farm regions** (Westphalia, Lower Saxony, Schleswig-Holstein): one sibling keeps the farm, the rest leave.
- **Partible-inheritance regions** (Württemberg, Baden, Hesse, Palatinate): land divided among siblings, by custom to check.
- **Hanseatic merchant families:** siblings bound by the firm.
- **East and West:** GDR family size and work patterns, and siblings split by the Wall.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-German families:** *abla* and *abi* as roles with duties.
- **Russian-German families, including Mennonite and Baptist congregations:** family size to measure, not assume.
- **Arabic-speaking refugee families:** brothers and sisters split across countries.
- **Moravian and other Free Church congregations:** "brother" and "sister" as daily address.
- **Blended families:** step- and half-siblings; measure their share in east and west.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family statistics and panels; thinner on what siblings feel they owe, which needs interviews and letters.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for migrant families; Sorbian for Lusatian family life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence between siblings and estrangement; Nazi-era twin experiments as background to twin research; inheritance and family-reunification rules are law (Libra).
- **Held for review:** none.
