## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages and small towns:** who watches whom, and how long a story lasts, tested against city anonymity (Jeggle's Kiebingen as one model).
- **The Rhineland and Cologne:** local words for chatter (*Verzäll*); compare their use and tone with the north.
- **East and West:** whispered jokes, informers and the *Buschfunk* under the GDR; the tabloid press in the West.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-German families:** *el âlem* and reputation; family honor is a sensitive frame to study with care.
- **Arabic-speaking communities:** *qīl wa-qāl* in new, close-knit networks.
- **Russian-German Free Church congregations:** reputation in tight religious communities.
- **Sorbian Catholic villages:** small speech communities where everyone knows everyone.
- **Teenagers:** gossip moved into group chats and social media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. Historical (witch trials, denunciation) and media sources are deep; everyday gossip needs recorded talk or ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** German, including regional words; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for community reputation studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** denunciation under Nazism and the GDR touches living families; honor-related harm in some migrant families must not be generalized to whole groups; defamation law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
