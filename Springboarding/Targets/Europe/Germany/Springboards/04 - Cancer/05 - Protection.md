## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Sea and Elbe coast communities (Frisian islands, Dithmarschen, Hamburg):** collective defense against water.
- **Ahr and Eifel villages:** protection after the 2021 flood, from below and from the state.
- **Jewish communities:** police guard at synagogues; the Halle attack (2019) and what security means to members.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Arab families:** evil-eye charms for babies beside German safety norms; arson attacks of the 1990s (Mölln, Solingen) as failures of protection.
- **Vietnamese residents of Rostock-Lichtenhagen (1992):** a riot in which police protection collapsed.
- **East Germans:** "protection" as a state word, and trust in police and the state afterward.
- **Rural villages with volunteer brigades against cities with professional services.**
- **Women and girls:** who protects, and protection as control; compare migrant and majority framings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official statistics and disaster records; mixed for household practices (amulets, blessings), which need interviews and folk collections.
- **Languages needed:** German; Low German and Frisian for coast material; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for family practices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-abuse cases (protect victims' identities); racist attacks; antisemitic violence; vigilante "citizen patrols" are a far-right theme to treat as data. Laws on child protection and self-defense go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
