## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hamburg and Berlin against small towns:** visible street homelessness against hidden staying-with-friends.
- **Postwar expellees billeted in private homes:** forced sheltering of strangers, remembered on both sides.
- **North Frisian Halligen and marsh communities:** shelter against the sea.
- **East German towns of the early 1990s (Hoyerswerda, 1991):** hostels for asylum seekers under attack.
- **Syrian, Afghan and Ukrainian refugees:** camps, shared housing and private hosts, compared.
- **EU migrants from Poland, Romania and Bulgaria among rough sleepers:** shelter rules and who qualifies.
- **Young people leaving care:** the step from a children's home to having no roof.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed to rich. National homelessness statistics are new (from 2022 *check*); church charities, archives and street papers fill the gaps.
- **Languages needed:** German; Polish, Romanian, Bulgarian for rough sleepers; Arabic, Dari, Ukrainian for refugees.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter residents' privacy; racist attacks on refugee housing; women's-shelter addresses are secret and must stay so. Asylum law is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
