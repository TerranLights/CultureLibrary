*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Rhineland, Münsterland and Baden against the Protestant north and the secular East:** Caritas, Diakonie and Volkssolidarität as different carriers of care.
- **Rural eastern districts where the young left after 1990:** elders cared for at a distance, or by neighbors.
- **Households with Polish, Romanian or Ukrainian live-in carers:** care as a cross-border arrangement.
- **First-generation Turkish and Kurdish elders:** expectations of children's care against professional care.
- **Post-Soviet Jewish and Russian-German elders:** care in congregations and resettled families.
- **Men as carers of wives and parents:** a minority to seek out.
- **People with disabilities and their self-advocacy groups:** care received and care refused.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: official care statistics, panel surveys, welfare-association archives and a large nursing history.
- **Languages needed:** German; Polish, Romanian, Ukrainian for live-in carers; Turkish, Russian, Arabic for migrant elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi "euthanasia" killings; abuse in care homes and church institutions; undeclared employment of live-in carers; medical privacy. Assisted dying goes to Scorpio; care-insurance law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
