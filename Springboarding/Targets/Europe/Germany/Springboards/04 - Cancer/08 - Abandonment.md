## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Germans:** weekly crèches, state removals, and later the young who left eastern towns.
- **West German *Heimkinder* (for example Freistatt in Lower Saxony):** church homes as places children were left.
- **Turkish-German families:** *Kofferkinder* left with grandparents in Turkey; ask both generations.
- **German Sinti:** children taken from homes during persecution (the Mulfingen children, Württemberg, 1944 *check*); approach through the community.
- **War-child generation and their children:** absence handed on.
- **Fathers after divorce (fathers' groups such as Väteraufbruch für Kinder *check*):** being left as a father, against fathers who leave.
- **Elders in depopulated rural districts:** left behind by migration within the country.
