*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hamburg and Bremen:** declining state honors as a civic tradition, set against other cities' eagerness for them.
- **Rhineland carnival societies:** mock orders and titles awarded by the hundreds each season; recognition as play and parody.
- **East Germans:** the recognition of lives, careers and diplomas from before 1990.
- **The first guest-worker generation and its children:** thanked late, if at all; compare Turkish, Italian, Greek and Yugoslav-origin families.
- **Sinti and Roma:** recognition as victims came decades after 1945; work through their own bodies.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** recognition as peoples, and the forms it takes in schools, signs and media.
- **Academics, the trades and the unpaid:** the doctoral title, the master craftsman's diploma, and care work that earns no title.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors, work surveys and official procedures; thinner for everyday praise at home, where qualitative work is needed.
- **Languages needed:** German (with Middle High German and dialect), Turkish, Russian, Arabic; Sorbian and Danish for minority media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** acknowledgment of the Nazi genocides and colonial crimes; the rules on recognizing foreign diplomas, and on titles, are law and belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
