## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Carnival towns:** the *Büttenrede* lampoons local notables by name; permitted mockery with rules to map.
- **East Germans:** the post-1990 sense of devaluation, by age and by whether one lost work.
- **Immigrant families (Turkish, Arab, Russian-German):** slurs, ethnic jokes and labels used about them in the press.
- **Sinti and Roma:** degrading treatment in schools and offices, as their own bodies report it.
- **Pupils by school track:** the stigma of the lowest secondary track and of repeating a year.
- **Soldiers and fraternity members:** initiation rites that cross into degradation.
- **Young women and LGBTQ youth:** sexual shaming and outing online.
- **Politicians and celebrities:** satire and tabloid shaming; compare outlets, their audiences and who is targeted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bullying surveys, military reports and media scandals; mixed for everyday teasing and family shaming.
- **Languages needed:** German (with older German for chapbooks), Turkish, Arabic, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era shaming photographs show identifiable victims; handle with care. Insult and satire law, and the old shaming punishments, go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
