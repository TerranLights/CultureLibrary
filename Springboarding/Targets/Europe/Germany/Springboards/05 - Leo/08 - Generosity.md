## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hamburg and Bremen merchant families:** foundation-giving as civic tradition.
- **The Catholic Rhineland and the Protestant north:** St. Martin and the carol singers against Protestant diaconal giving.
- **East Germans:** largely outside the churches that run much formal charity; compare recorded donations with Volkssolidarität and informal neighborly help.
- **Family-owned firms in Baden-Württemberg and North Rhine-Westphalia:** large corporate foundations (Robert Bosch Stiftung *check*); test any regional thrift self-image against them.
- **Muslim communities:** *zakāt*, Ramadan meals and mosque building funds.
- **The Jewish community:** its central welfare office (ZWST *check*) and *tzedakah*.
- **Turkish, Balkan and other immigrant families:** remittances and wedding money gifts.
- **Germans from the former Soviet Union:** hospitality customs carried from Kazakhstan and Siberia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal giving, foundations and volunteering; informal giving, remittances and treating are undercounted.
- **Languages needed:** German, Turkish, Arabic, Russian; Hebrew terms for the Jewish community.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** donor privacy; giving within mosques and political suspicion of foreign funding; church tax and charity-status rules are law and go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
