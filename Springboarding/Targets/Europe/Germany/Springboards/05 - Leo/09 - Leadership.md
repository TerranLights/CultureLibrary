## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hamburg, Bremen and Lübeck:** collegial city government and merchant boards; leading as first among equals.
- **Brandenburg and the old Prussian core:** the military tradition and its postwar reform.
- **The Catholic west and the Protestant north:** bishops against synods as models of who leads a church.
- **East Germans:** cadre leadership in the GDR, then their share of top posts after 1990.
- **Family-owned firms:** the founder-patriarch and the handover to the next generation.
- **Women:** the long shift in party, firm and club leadership; Angela Merkel as a case to examine, not an emblem.
- **Turkish- and Arab-German associations:** mosque boards, "big brothers" in youth groups, leaders sent from abroad.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** leadership of a minority within a majority state.
