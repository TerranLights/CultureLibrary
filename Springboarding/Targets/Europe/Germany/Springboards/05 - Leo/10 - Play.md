## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Card-game regions:** Skat across much of the country, Doppelkopf in the northwest; Bavaria's Schafkopf as an outside comparison.
- **East Frisia, Oldenburg and Schleswig-Holstein:** *Boßeln* and *Klootschießen*, road-bowling games as village play.
- **The Rhineland:** carnival as the season when adults are allowed to play.
- **East Germans:** organized childhood play before 1990 and the change after.
- **Sorbs:** Easter egg rolling and other seasonal games.
- **Turkish-German men's cafés and clubs:** *tavla* and *okey* as everyday play and company.
- **Families by class and city size:** free play in courtyards and streets against booked activities.
- **Danish-minority schools:** the Danish split between free play and games, carried into a German setting.
