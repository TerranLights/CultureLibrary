*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hanseatic merchants:** honor as creditworthiness and the kept word.
- **Former officer and student-fraternity milieus:** the duel and the *Mensur* as class honor.
- **Football supporters, especially ultras:** loyalty and standing up for the club.
- **Turkish and Kurdish families:** *namus* and *şeref*, to be separated by region of origin, generation and religiosity; work from their own accounts.
- **Arab-German families and newer refugees:** honor vocabulary and its use in each group, never assumed.
- **Young men in mixed city districts:** "respect" and *Ehre* in street and rap codes, across origins.
- **East and West Germans:** military honor in the GDR's army against the Bundeswehr's tradition debates.
- **Rural communities:** the good name of the family farm and the handshake at the livestock market.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, dueling and military memory; mixed for current everyday honor, where interviews and youth studies will matter most.
- **Languages needed:** German (with Old and Middle High German), Turkish, Kurdish, Arabic, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-based violence must be researched in the words of those affected and must not become a stereotype of any group; the SS motto on honor and loyalty, and the Wehrmacht's honor myth, are politically live. Insult and duel law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
