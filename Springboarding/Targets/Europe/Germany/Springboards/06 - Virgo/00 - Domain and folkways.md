# Germany: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German (standard and regional speech); the recognized regional and minority languages Low German, Upper and Lower Sorbian, Danish, North Frisian, Saterland Frisian and Sinti Romanes; migrant languages, chiefly Turkish, Russian, Polish, Arabic, Kurdish and Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** work, service, the daily round and health; what good work and a well-kept life are taken to be.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former GDR lands and the former West:** women's employment, workplace-based services and post-1990 job loss differ; separate on every strand.
- **Ruhr and Saar:** coal and steel shift work and colliery housing.
- **Württemberg and Baden:** Pietist heritage, small farms with side trades, family firms.
- **Hanseatic north (Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck):** port and merchant work, a different meal rhythm.
- **North-west countryside (Emsland, Münsterland, East Frisia):** seasonal labor abroad, peat colonies, farm households.
- **East Elbian estate country (Mecklenburg, Brandenburg, Western Pomerania):** estate laborers, then collective farms.
- **Sorbs of Upper and Lower Lusatia:** lignite mining and lost villages; Catholic and Protestant Sorbian daily rounds.
- **Danes and North Frisians in Schleswig:** their own schools, associations and health services.
- **Sinti and Yenish:** itinerant trades and their history of persecution.
- **Labor migrants and their children, and Russian Germans:** recruits from Turkey, southern Europe and Yugoslavia; Vietnamese former contract workers in the east; Aussiedler; recent refugees.
- **Class worlds:** workers, salaried employees, civil servants, craft masters, farm families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. The risk is the cliché of one German work ethic and one German tidiness; name variation instead.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled. In this setting Bavaria belongs to Austro-Bavaria, a separate target, so Bavarian material appears in these sheets only as a neighboring comparison.
- **Languages needed:** German, including Fraktur print and Kurrent or Sütterlin handwriting for older sources; Low German, Sorbian, Danish and North Frisian; Turkish, Russian, Polish, Arabic and Vietnamese for migrant communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi misuse of work slogans at camp gates; forced labor (Arolsen Archives, *check*); persecution of people branded "work-shy"; racial hygiene in Nazi health policy; workplace surveillance in the GDR. Labor law, shop hours and sick pay belong to Libra or Capricorn; the clock and public order to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. Destatis: "Zeitverwendungserhebung 2022" tables for paid and unpaid work, sleep and meal times, split East/West and by sex.
2. GESIS: search "ALLBUS Wichtigkeit Arbeit" and the EVS German waves for the work-in-life items.
3. VD17 and the Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek: "Hausvater" and "Oeconomia"; read the household calendars and remedy sections.
4. LWL Alltagskultur archive: questionnaires on "Waschtag" and "Hausarbeit"; repeat with ISGV Dresden for Saxony.
5. DOMiD catalog: "Arbeitsalltag" and "Wohnheim" for labor migrants' daily life.
