# Germany: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Low German, Sorbian and Danish; Turkish, Polish, Russian and Vietnamese for labor-migrant communities. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Württemberg's Pietist towns against the Catholic Rhineland and Münsterland:** the calling against the social-Catholic view of labor, the comparison Weber himself drew.
- **Ruhr and Saar against the engineering belt of Württemberg and Baden:** collective pride of the pit and the mill against the skill pride of family-owned firms.
- **Former GDR lands:** a right to work and the work brigade, then mass job loss after the 1990 closures; very different stories of unemployment.
- **Erzgebirge (Saxony):** mining giving way to cottage woodcraft; work at home as a family economy.
- **Hamburg and Bremen dock labor:** casual hiring and port shift rhythms.
- **Sorbs in Lusatia:** lignite jobs set against villages lost to the pits.
- **Turkish-German families across generations:** *gurbet* factory work, then small businesses and professions.
- **Vietnamese former contract workers:** from GDR factories to market trade after 1990.
- **Yenish and Sinti:** itinerant trades and how they were pushed out.
- **Women in West and East:** the homemaker model against near-universal female employment.
- **Seasonal farm workers from Poland and Romania:** the harvest labor of asparagus and fruit regions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in statistics, labor history and literature. Avoid taking "German diligence" as a premise; it is itself a claim to study.
- **Languages needed:** German (older Fraktur print); Turkish, Polish, Vietnamese and Russian for migrant workers' own sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi forced labor (the "Zwangsarbeit 1939-1945" interview archive at the Free University of Berlin, *check*), the Reich Labor Service and the persecution of the "work-shy"; GDR enterprise surveillance. Labor law, works councils and unemployment rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
