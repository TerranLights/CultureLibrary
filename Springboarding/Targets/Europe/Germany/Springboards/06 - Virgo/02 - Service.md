## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran north and east against the Catholic Rhineland and Münsterland:** diaconal service against Caritas and religious orders.
- **Prussian-rooted Berlin and Brandenburg against Hamburg and Bremen:** service to the state against Hanseatic honorary civic office.
- **Former GDR lands:** Volkssolidarität and expected "social activity" against Western church-based volunteering.
- **Villages and small towns:** fire brigade, rifle club and church choir as the frame of service.
- **Sorbs:** service to the Sorbian people through the Domowina and the Catholic parishes of Upper Lusatia.
- **Jewish communities:** pre-war welfare traditions and post-1990 Russian-speaking members.
- **Muslim communities:** mosque associations' volunteer work, Alevi associations, and refugee aid after 2015.
- **East European live-in care workers:** women serving German elders in private homes, often for weeks at a stretch.
- **Men born roughly 1945-1992:** the choice between army and civilian service.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on volunteering, welfare bodies and the civil service; thinner on customer-service norms.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for community bodies; Polish and Romanian for live-in carers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi-era *Dienst* vocabulary (the Reich Labor Service, the girls' duty year); military service and its return to debate; *hizmet* as the name of the Gülen movement, politically charged in Turkish-German life. Civil-service and conscription law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
