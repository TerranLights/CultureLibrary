## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm families of the north and west:** the retired parents' *Altenteil* on a farm handed to one heir, with its expected tasks for the old.
- **Small-holder country of the southwest:** divided holdings, side trades and the tinkerer's reputation; test the "handy Swabian" image.
- **Ruhr after the last pits closed (2018):** miners and their sons losing the work that made them useful.
- **Former GDR lands after 1990:** a full-employment society turned into one with "superfluous" workers; the "losers of the turn" debate.
- **Disabled people and their organizations:** workshop workers set against inclusion advocates.
- **Retirees:** "active aging" and the volunteer role against care-dependent elders.
- **Refugees and asylum seekers:** work bans during procedures and the idleness they describe.
- **Turkish-German families:** first-generation workers called in for their labor, and their children's ideas of a useful life.
- **Sinti, Roma and Yenish:** the "work-shy" label used against them, then and now.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elders, unemployment and design history; thinner for children's household help.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Arabic, Russian and Vietnamese for migrant families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi "euthanasia" and its slogans ("useless eaters"); handle survivor and victim records with care and respect for privacy. Disability terms change; use the terms disabled people's own groups use. Allotment rules and charitable status belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
