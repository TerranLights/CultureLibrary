## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old university towns (Göttingen, Heidelberg, Jena, Tübingen, Leipzig):** scholarly analysis as local identity.
- **Engineering regions of Württemberg and Baden against Hanseatic trading cities:** the inventor's bench against the merchant's ledger.
- **Former GDR lands:** a state that praised "scientific-technical progress" and math contests, then broke apart; compare later trust in experts.
- **Alternative and anthroposophic milieus:** Waldorf schools and holistic medicine as a counter-current.
- **Religious milieus:** Pietist, Catholic and secular views of reason and doubt.
- **Students of migrant background:** early school tracking and math results, set beside families' own aims.
- **Russian-German and Russian-speaking Jewish families:** Soviet-trained engineers and their schooling ideals.
- **Women in mathematics and engineering (MINT):** shares by Land and East/West.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history, schooling and surveys; avoid using "German thoroughness" as an assumption.
- **Languages needed:** German, including older academic Latin for 18th-century sources; Turkish, Russian and Vietnamese for family sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi race science and the misuse of census data for persecution; the Stasi's analysis of citizens, whose files are restricted by privacy rules. Census law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
