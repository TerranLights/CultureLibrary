## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moravians of Herrnhut and Pietists of Württemberg:** discernment by lot and by self-testing.
- **Catholic Rhineland and Westphalia:** confession and the examination of conscience.
- **Wine regions (Mosel, Rheingau, Palatinate, Baden, Saale-Unstrut, Saxony):** trained palates and their juries.
- **Former GDR lands:** reading official news against Western broadcasts; the Dresden area nicknamed the "valley of the clueless" for lacking Western TV.
- **Rural east and north:** foraging traditions where mushroom knowledge is passed in families.
- **Elders living alone:** the main targets of fraud.
- **Russian-speaking communities and Turkish-German audiences:** how news from home media and German media is weighed.
- **Arab and other Muslim newcomers:** counsel from imams and elders, and *firāsa* as a named way of reading people.
- **Sinti and Roma:** the receiving end of judging strangers on sight.
