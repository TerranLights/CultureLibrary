# Germany: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German, with Kölsch and other regional sayings; Low German and Danish; Turkish, Russian, Polish and Vietnamese for families of migrant background. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Erzgebirge and Glashütte against the Black Forest:** two precision-craft traditions with different histories.
- **Cologne and the Rhineland:** proverbial easygoing sayings set against the precision image; test whether practice differs.
- **Württemberg engineering towns:** car and machine makers' quality culture.
- **Former GDR lands:** official quality seals and improvised repair; the shock of Western standards after 1990.
- **Leipzig and Dresden musicians:** choir-school drilling.
- **Länder with different grading habits:** how Abitur averages and pressure compare.
- **Young women and girls:** appearance standards from casting shows and social media.
- **Families of migrant background:** expectations of school success; a research question, never assumed.
- **Master craftspeople against industrial workers:** whose standard rules, the maker's or the norm's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich, but "German perfectionism" is a stock outsiders' label; treat it as a claim and look for counter-evidence and regional variation.
- **Languages needed:** German, with early 18th-century German for Wolff; Turkish, Russian, Polish and Vietnamese for family sources; Sorbian words for exactness and botching to be found in the Sorbian Institute's dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders and suicide among high achievers; the Nazi cult of the flawless body in sport and film. Master-craft licensing rules belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
