# Germany: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German, with Berlin, Swabian and Rhineland speech; Low German, Danish and Sorbian; Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Vietnamese for migrant communities. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Berlin:** blunt talk as a local brand; test whether criticism is in fact plainer there.
- **Swabia:** withheld praise as a regional saying; how bosses and parents actually give feedback.
- **Rhineland:** criticism through carnival humor and its season.
- **Hamburg and Bremen:** reserve and understatement in disagreement.
- **Former GDR lands:** petitions, "criticism and self-criticism", and the mutual labels of the 1990s (*Jammerossi*, the whining Easterner; *Besserwessi*, the know-it-all Westerner).
- **Sorbian and Danish minority press:** the critical voice of *Serbske Nowiny* and *Flensborg Avis* on their own and majority affairs.
- **Turkish-German writers and comedians:** satire from inside migrant life, and criticism aimed at them in integration debates.
- **Younger workers in start-ups:** imported feedback rituals against older office habits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, the press and literary criticism; thinner for criticism inside families.
- **Languages needed:** German, including 18th- and 19th-century prose; Turkish, Arabic and Vietnamese for community media; Sorbian and Danish for minority papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi persecution of critics (the 1933 book burnings; Ossietzky's imprisonment); Stasi files on dissidents, under privacy rules; integration debates that turned into attacks on Muslims. Insult and press law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
