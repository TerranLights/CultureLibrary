*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Spa towns of Hesse, Baden and Lower Saxony:** the *Kur* as a local economy and a way of life.
- **Former GDR lands:** polyclinics, compulsory childhood vaccines, and later regional splits in vaccination.
- **Anthroposophic milieus (Herdecke, the Filderklinik):** a medical system of their own.
- **Pietist Württemberg:** prayer healing at Bad Boll under the Blumhardts.
- **Catholic Lower Rhine:** healing pilgrimages to Kevelaer.
- **Ruhr mining families:** dust lung and the miners' own insurance fund.
- **Sorbs of Lusatia:** herbal and birth customs.
- **Turkish-German families:** the evil eye, religious healers and German clinics side by side.
- **Russian Germans and Russian-speaking Jews:** steam baths, folk remedies and Soviet clinic habits.
- **Vietnamese families:** remedies brought from home and kept beside German medicine.
- **Sinti and Roma:** access to care and distrust rooted in Nazi medical crimes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys, insurers' data and medical history; folk healing is thinner and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** German (Old High German, early modern German, Fraktur); Sorbian; Turkish, Russian, Arabic and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi medical crimes and forced sterilization; the Hygiene Museum's Nazi-era role; GDR state doping; the thalidomide disaster (1961); pandemic protest movements. Health insurance and healer licensing belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.

## 5. First queries
1. RKI GEDA: self-rated health and doctor visits by Land and migration background.
