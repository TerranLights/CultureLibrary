## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former GDR lands:** improvement planned by their own state, then by the West after 1990; nostalgia as a reply.
- **Ruhr:** structural change and the people it left out.
- **North-west moor and peat colonies:** improvement paid for across three generations, as the saying holds.
- **Württemberg:** house-building and saving as a lifelong project.
- **Sorbs:** schooling and policy that pressed them to become "German", and their own language revival.
- **Lignite villages of Lusatia and the Rhineland:** homes removed for mining in the name of progress.
- **First-generation students (the ArbeiterKind.de initiative):** rising by education, and family responses to it.
- **Turkish-German and other migrant families:** educational climbing and the "striver" charge, inside and outside the family.
- **Refugees:** integration courses presented as betterment, as course takers describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for education, regional policy and self-help; colonial records are scattered and need care.
- **Languages needed:** German, older Prussian administrative German; Low German for moor-colony sources; Sorbian; Turkish, Arabic and Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Herero and Nama genocide in German South West Africa; Nazi "racial hygiene" as "improving" a people; forced Germanization; GDR farm collectivization; the Treuhand. Integration-course obligations and training grants belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
