## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East against West:** cohabitation, births outside marriage and dual-earner couples to compare, against two different family codes before 1990.
- **Catholic Rhineland and Saarland against Protestant Lower Saxony and Schleswig-Holstein:** marriage as sacrament or as worldly estate.
- **Ruhr mining towns:** the *Kumpel* bond underground and the codetermination of the coal and steel industries.
- **Westerwald and other cooperative heartlands:** rural partnership through cooperative banks and dairies.
- **Berlin and Cologne:** large gay and lesbian communities; uptake of registered partnership to set against small towns.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-origin families:** transnational marriages and the role of both families in a match.
- **Russian Germans and Sorbs:** each with its own wedding customs and partnership vocabulary.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on law, demography and labor relations; mixed on courtship customs by region.
