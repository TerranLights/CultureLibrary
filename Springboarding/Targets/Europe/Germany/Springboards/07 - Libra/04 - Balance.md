## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The North Sea and Baltic coasts:** dike duty and storm floods made balance with the sea a matter of shared obligation.
- **Saxony's Ore Mountains:** mining and forestry, where the sustainability idea began.
- **Rhineland Carnival towns against Protestant Lower Saxony:** feast and fast weighed differently through the church year.
- **The poorer Länder (Saxony-Anhalt, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Saarland) against the richer (Hamburg, Hesse, Baden-Württemberg):** givers and takers in fiscal equalization.
- **East against West:** full-time work for mothers and childcare places as two different settings for "reconciling family and job".
- **Shift, care and farm workers:** whose work keeps others' balance and upsets their own.
- **Muslim families:** Ramadan's rhythm set against the working week.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on time use, working hours and fiscal law; mixed on folk medicine and everyday moderation.
- **Languages needed:** German, including Middle High German and Low German; Latin for Hildegard.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Haeckel's writings and the later use of "natural balance" in racial thought; read with care.
- **Comparison:** pre-war equalization figures include Bavaria, now a separate nation, so the flows must be recomputed for the post-war lands.
- **Held for review:** none.
