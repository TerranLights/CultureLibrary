*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cologne and the Rhineland:** a local name for favoritism that can be researched as a tradition in its own right.
- **Hamburg and Bremen:** merchant self-images of probity, to be tested against their own scandals.
- **The eastern Länder:** vetting of officials after 1990 and talk of old *Seilschaften*.
- **Civil servants against private employees:** a separate legal duty of neutrality.
- **Muslim women in public service:** where neutrality law touches their dress.
- **Turkish-, Arab- and Russian-speaking networks:** *torpil*, *wāsṭa* and *blat* as their own words for connections.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** special provisions (the minority party's exemption from the five-percent threshold) as a deliberate exception to equal rules.
- **Small towns against large cities:** recusal and favoritism where everyone knows the judge.
