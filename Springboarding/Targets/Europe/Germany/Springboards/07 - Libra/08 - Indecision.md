## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Herrnhut and other Pietist communities in Lusatia and Württemberg:** decision by lot and by prayerful waiting.
- **Cologne and the Catholic Rhineland:** a joking fatalism ("it comes as it comes") to test against actual practice.
- **Berlin, Brandenburg and Hamburg:** large projects that stalled, and how locals talk about them.
- **East and West:** separate uncertainty-avoidance scores and different memories of planned decisions.
- **Younger and older generations:** words like *merkeln* and the youth reaction to political caution.
- **Farm families in Lower Saxony and Schleswig-Holstein:** succession decisions put off across a generation.
- **Turkish- and Arab-origin families:** who in the family has the final word on study, marriage and moves.
- **Russian Germans:** the decision to emigrate, weighed for years, and its aftermath.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for politics, law and decision science; mixed for everyday indecision, which leaves few records.
- **Languages needed:** German; regional dialects for sayings; immigrant languages for family decisions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "German Angst" and the "Hamlet" image are national labels; treat them as discourse to study, never as a finding. Organ donation and end of life touch religious convictions.
- **Held for review:** none.
