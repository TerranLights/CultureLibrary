# Germany: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German (including Low German for folk texts); Russian, Polish, Turkish and Arabic for immigrant families. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Germans:** the "niche society" of keeping one's head down, and the nonviolent break of 1989.
- **The Cologne archdiocese and other dioceses:** abuse reports that differ in how long silence held.
- **Mennonite and Quaker communities (East Frisia, Krefeld, Bad Pyrmont):** principled peace with a long local history.
- **Mecklenburg-Vorpommern:** a Land directly tied to the Nord Stream debate.
- **The postwar generation against the 1968 generation:** silence about the past against open confrontation.
- **Russian Germans:** divided loyalties since 2022, and family peace kept by avoiding the subject.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-origin families:** conflicts from the homeland kept out of, or brought into, German neighborhoods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history, law and the peace movement; thin for private conflict avoidance, which leaves few records.
- **Languages needed:** German, Low German for the tale; Russian and Polish for proverbs and family sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Noelle-Neumann's Nazi-era journalism; abuse victims' testimony; the Nazi past and "fellow travelers"; Russia since 2022.
- **Held for review:** none.
