## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Württemberg (Swabia):** the Kehrwoche and its neighborly cleaning duties, and how they are enforced.
- **Catholic Rhineland:** carnival's end, Ash Wednesday and confession, against Protestant regions.
- **Saxony:** kept Repentance Day (Buß- und Bettag) as a public holiday (*check*); what the day still means there.
- **The former GDR:** party purges, then the post-1990 screening of officials for Stasi ties.
- **Westphalia-Lippe and the Moselle:** dense witch-trial histories.
- **Rhineland Jewish communities (Speyer, Worms, Mainz):** ritual purity with medieval roots.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Arab Muslims:** ablution, Ramadan fasting and the hamam.
- **Sinti and Roma:** internal purity customs, which are theirs to describe, and their history as targets of "cleansing".
- **Families of the postwar expellees:** being purged from a homeland, as their own memory tells it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for denazification, witch trials and party purges; mixed for folk cleansing; thin for private decluttering and cutting off kin.
- **Languages needed:** German (Fraktur and Kurrent for trial and tribunal files), Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi persecution and genocide as "cleansing"; the expulsions of Germans after 1945, a politically loaded topic; the 1990s attacks on asylum-seekers' hostels (Hoyerswerda, Rostock-Lichtenhagen); outsiders' writing on Romani purity rules is often stereotyped. Loyalty checks for civil servants (the 1972 decree) and the legal side of vetting go to Capricorn. The Kneipp water cure comes from Bavaria, a separate nation here; use it only as a neighboring comparison for fasting and cleansing cures.
- **Held for review:** none.
