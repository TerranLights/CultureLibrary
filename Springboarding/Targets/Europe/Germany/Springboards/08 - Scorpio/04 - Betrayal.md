## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East against West:** test whether eastern memories of betrayal run through informer files, the "Wendehals" and the Treuhand, and western ones through 1918 and Nazi denunciation.
- **Berlin:** the divided city of spies, defectors and border crossings.
- **Sorbs of Lusatia:** villages dug away for coal, and Stasi informing inside the minority.
- **Social Democratic and Communist milieus (Ruhr, Saxony, Berlin):** the old charge of betrayal between the two left parties.
- **Turkish and Kurdish communities:** informer affairs after 2016 (reports of mosque staff informing on Gülen supporters) and Kurdish-Turkish suspicion.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** the 1941 Soviet deportation on a charge of collective treason, as their association records it.
- **Sinti and Roma:** broken promises of recognition and redress after 1945, as their Central Council documents it.
- **Danish minority of South Schleswig:** loyalty between two states, as a question to research.
