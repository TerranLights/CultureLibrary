## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** Gelfand's split samples, Leipzig's survey and the Zersetzung files give a ready contrast on control by the state and by norms.
- **Württemberg and Baden:** Pietist self-examination and the Fasnet's masked license in one Land.
- **Rhineland (Cologne, Mainz):** "et kütt, wie et kütt" as a stance on fate, set against everyday order.
- **The Hanseatic north and East Frisia:** dike work and control of the sea as communal duty.
- **Turkish and Arab families:** jealousy, family honor and who controls daughters' movements, researched through the communities' own advice and women's groups, not outsiders' clichés.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** whether Soviet-era experience shapes talk of luck (*avos'*) and of the state's reach, as a question.
- **Vietnamese Germans in the east:** former contract workers whose lives the GDR state tightly controlled, as their own associations tell it.
- **People with disabilities:** institutional control and the self-advocacy movement.
