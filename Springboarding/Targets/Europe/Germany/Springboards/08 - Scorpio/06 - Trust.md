## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** compare trust in neighbors and trust in institutions on each side, using surveys with split samples.
- **Hamburg, Bremen and Lübeck:** the "Hanseatic" handshake as a living claim, to test.
- **Westerwald, Hesse and Saxony:** cooperative heartlands where credit rested on members' knowledge of each other.
- **Swabia:** small-firm family capitalism and trust kept inside the family business, as a question.
- **Turkish and Kurdish communities:** after the NSU murders and through the Turkish state's reach into German mosques.
- **Syrian and other recent refugees:** whether experience of the states they fled shapes trust in German authorities, as a question.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** trust in German institutions against Russian-language media.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** trust in their own institutions (Domowina; the Danish school association) beside the state's.
- **Jewish communities:** security and trust after the Halle synagogue attack (2019) and others.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; Germany has many trust surveys, with good east-west and migrant splits. Thin on trust inside families and between friends.
- **Languages needed:** German, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Kurdish, Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the NSU case and other far-right violence; antisemitic attacks; surveys of migrants can stigmatize. Trust in police and courts is Capricorn's (the spirit of the law); good-faith rules in contract law are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none.
