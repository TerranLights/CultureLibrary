## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Westphalia:** Vehmic courts and the Masematte of Münster: secrecy in law and in language.
- **The former GDR:** life under the Stasi, then the opening of the files: test how far private discretion and public reticence differ from the West.
- **Hamburg:** the first lodge, and its private banks and merchant houses as a test case for discretion about money.
- **Catholic and Protestant regions:** confession against Protestant private conscience, and both churches' abuse cover-ups.
- **Yenish and other traveling families:** a cant of their own, and long suspicion from settled society.
- **Sinti:** many Sinti keep Romanes for the community and object to outsiders teaching or writing it (*check*); treat the language as restricted.
- **Muslim communities:** *satr*, covering others' faults, and the privacy of the home.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** decades of hiding German identity in the Soviet Union, as their own accounts tell.
- **LGBT people in small towns and in cities:** the closet across regions and generations.
