*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** test elite representation, *Seilschaften* and the memory of Wandlitz in the east against boardroom and family-firm power in the west.
- **Cologne and the Rhineland:** *Klüngel*, carnival societies and party networks as a local power culture to test.
- **The Ruhr:** union and Social Democratic power in the coal-and-steel era, and what replaced it.
- **Hamburg and Bremen:** city-states with senates and old merchant families; who governs now is the question.
- **Baden-Württemberg:** family-owned mid-sized firms (*Mittelstand*) and their local weight.
- **Danes and Frisians of Schleswig; Sorbs (Domowina):** minority power through party and association.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Arab communities:** who represents them, and the reach of foreign governments' associations.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** political voice through their own associations and parties' outreach.
- **Women and men:** in boards, cabinets and local councils.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; elite studies, surveys and lobbying data all exist. Thin on power inside families and informal favors.
- **Languages needed:** German, Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims about foreign governments' influence over migrant associations are politically live; *Klüngel* and *Seilschaft* talk can slide into libel. Legitimate authority, hierarchy and the power ways belong to Capricorn; the legal design of codetermination and lobbying rules goes to Libra.
