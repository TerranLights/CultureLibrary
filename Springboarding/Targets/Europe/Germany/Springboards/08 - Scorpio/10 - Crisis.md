## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Sea and Baltic coasts (North Frisia, East Frisia, Hamburg):** storm floods and the dike associations that answer them.
- **River valleys (Elbe, Oder, Ahr):** recurring floods: compare the 2002 and 2013 Elbe floods with the Ahr in 2021.
- **The former GDR:** 1989-90 and the decade after as crisis and turning point; unemployment in Saxony-Anhalt and Mecklenburg.
- **Lusatia (incl. Sorbs):** the end of coal as a slow regional crisis.
- **The Ruhr:** the long crisis of coal and steel.
- **Older generations against young adults:** which crises each generation names first, as a question.
- **Turkish and Kurdish families:** crises abroad (the 2023 earthquake) felt in German towns.
- **Syrian, Iraqi and Ukrainian refugees:** the crisis they fled and the reception crisis named after them.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** the Soviet collapse as the crisis behind their migration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; disasters, economic crises and fears are well documented, from medieval chronicles to yearly surveys. Thin on how families handle private crises.
- **Languages needed:** German (Early New High German and Fraktur for war and plague chronicles), Low German, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Turkish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "refugee crisis" label is contested; Weimar analogies are politically loaded; flood victims' blame disputes are ongoing. Emergency powers and their legal limits go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
