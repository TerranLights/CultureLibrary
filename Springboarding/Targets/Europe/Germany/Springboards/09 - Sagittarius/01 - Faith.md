# Germany: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German and Low German; Upper and Lower Sorbian; Danish; Sinti Romanes; Turkish, Kurmanji, Arabic, Russian, Polish. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order), and by extension secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East against West:** to test whether faith kept under state pressure differs from faith inherited as a default.
- **Württemberg's Pietist home meetings ("Stunden") against Rhineland and Münsterland processional piety:** two different ways of practicing trust in God.
- **Catholic Sorbs:** faith and language tied together.
- **Russian-German Baptists and Mennonites:** faith formed under Soviet persecution, now in their own congregations.
- **Sunni and Alevi Turkish Germans:** often counted together, so their faith practice needs separating.
- **Yazidis:** a small community whose own bodies decide what is shared about their faith.
- **African and other migrant congregations (Hamburg, Rhine-Ruhr cities):** charismatic forms beside the established churches.
- **Jewish communities:** liberal, Orthodox and Chabad; old families and post-1990 immigrants.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for membership and attendance; mixed for inner faith; thin for small communities.
- **Languages needed:** German, early-modern and Middle High German for the older texts; Sorbian; Turkish, Kurmanji, Arabic, Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in the churches; Stasi files on clergy and believers (access is restricted); Yazidi religious knowledge that is not shared outside the community; state monitoring of some Muslim bodies is contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
