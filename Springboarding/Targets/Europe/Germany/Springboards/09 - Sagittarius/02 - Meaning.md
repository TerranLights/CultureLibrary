## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East German working lives after 1990:** to ask how far meaning was tied to the workplace and the collective, and what replaced it after mass job loss.
- **The Ruhr and Saarland mining towns against Lusatian coal towns:** two endings of the same work under two former systems.
- **Sorbian villages lost to open-pit mining:** meaning tied to a place that no longer exists.
- **First-generation labor migrants (Turkish, Italian, Greek, former Yugoslav) against their children:** return planned and deferred, against settled lives.
- **Farm families in Lower Saxony, Schleswig-Holstein and Mecklenburg:** legacy through farm succession.
- **Hanseatic merchant milieus** (Hamburg's "Ehrbarer Kaufmann" tradition *check*): meaning framed through honorable commerce.
- **The religiously unaffiliated East against churchgoing communities:** where meaning is sought when religion is absent.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for well-being and volunteering; mixed for meaning as such; thin for minorities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** German, Middle High German for Eckhart; Sorbian; Turkish and the other migrant languages for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and media guidelines against copycat effects; post-1990 Eastern grievances are politically charged; life-story archives limit access and quotation.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
