## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Germans who lived through 1989 against Western peers:** to test whether freedoms won within living memory are spoken of differently from freedoms inherited.
- **West Berlin:** an enclave of the free world, a refuge from military service and a squatter scene: a distinct local meaning of freedom.
- **East and North Frisians:** a freedom tradition older than the nation.
- **Hamburg and Bremen ("Freie Hansestadt"):** freedom as civic self-government.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** freedom as the right to stay a people.
- **Turkish and Kurdish Germans, including political exiles:** freedom of expression measured against the country left.
- **Syrian and other post-2015 refugees:** freedom as a reason for coming.
- **The pandemic protest milieus (2020-22):** "Freiheit" claimed against public-health rules; contested and studied.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political history and memory; mixed for everyday senses of freedom; thin for minority-language terms.
