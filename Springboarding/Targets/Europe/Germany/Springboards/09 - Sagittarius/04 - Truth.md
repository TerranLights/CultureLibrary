## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East against West:** trust in the press and in official figures after decades of party-run media in one half.
- **Saxony's protest milieus since 2014:** the "lying press" slogan was revived at Dresden marches *check*; compare with other Eastern states.
- **Russian-speaking Germans:** Russian-language media as rival sources of truth.
- **Turkish Germans:** news via Turkish broadcasters and German media, side by side.
- **Sorbian and Danish readers:** own-language papers as trusted sources.
- **Anthroposophic and alternative-medicine milieus (Stuttgart, Freiburg region and elsewhere) against academic medicine:** different tests of what is true.
- **Survivors' families (Jewish, Sinti):** whose account of the past was believed, and when.
- **"Reichsbürger" and related milieus:** total rejection of official truth, as researchers and security reports map them.
