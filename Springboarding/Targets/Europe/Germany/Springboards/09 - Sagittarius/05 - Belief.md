## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The East's inherited unbelief against the West's leavers:** being "nothing" from birth may differ from having left.
- **Westphalia and the Münsterland:** second-sight lore as a regional folk belief.
- **The Christ Child and Father Christmas zones:** to test whether this childhood belief follows old confessional lines.
- **Sorbs:** field spirits and other beliefs recorded in Sorbian.
- **Turkish and Arab Muslims:** the evil eye and other everyday beliefs beside formal creed; a young generation trained in German Islamic theology.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** Soviet atheist schooling against Baptist and Mennonite family belief.
- **Vietnamese Germans:** ancestor veneration and Buddhism across the old East-West divide.
- **Anthroposophists:** a belief system tied to schools, farming and medicine.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveyed religious belief; mixed for folk belief (old collections, little new data); thin for minority belief in its own words.
- **Languages needed:** German (Fraktur for folklore collections; Latin for the witch literature); Sorbian; Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s folklore projects were tied to Nazi ideology; witch persecution memory and local rehabilitations; labeling groups as "sects" can stigmatize; belief data is protected personal data.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
