# Germany: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German and Low German; North Frisian; Upper Sorbian; Danish; Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Polish. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Expellee families and their descendants:** journeys of loss, and trips back to the former eastern lands.
- **Former East and West holidaymakers:** travel inside the socialist bloc against Western package holidays, and what changed after 1990.
- **North Frisian islanders (Föhr, Amrum, the Halligen):** seafaring and emigration within family memory.
- **Turkish and former Yugoslav families:** the summer journey home, and return migration.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** a migration framed as a return to an ancestral land.
- **Sinti:** travel as trade and as a target of suspicion.
- **Hanseatic port cities:** the sea and the ship as everyday horizons.
- **Black Forest, Eifel, Harz and Palatinate hiking regions:** landscape walking as a regional identity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism and migration counts; mixed for the meaning travelers give their journeys; thin for minority-language travel lore.
- **Languages needed:** German, Fraktur for older guidebooks; Low German and North Frisian; Turkish, Serbo-Croatian, Russian, Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** expellee politics; the Nazi takeover of youth hiking and of "Strength through Joy" tourism; stigmatizing words for itinerant people ("Landfahrer"); Karl May's images of other peoples.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
