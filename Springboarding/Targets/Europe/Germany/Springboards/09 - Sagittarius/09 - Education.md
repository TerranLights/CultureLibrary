## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former East academics and their students:** how the 1990s restructuring changed the standing of learning there.
- **First-generation students against "Bildungsbürgertum" families:** education inherited against education won.
- **Trades and master craftsmen against graduates:** two routes to respect.
- **University towns (Göttingen, Heidelberg, Tübingen, Marburg, Jena) against industrial cities:** town-and-gown relations.
- **Turkish-German and other migrant-background students:** early school tracking and later access to university.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** degrees brought from the USSR, and their recognition.
- **Sorbian, Danish and Frisian learners:** to ask what minority schooling does for language and standing.
- **Fraternity students against left-wing student milieus:** opposite student cultures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and university history; mixed for how the educated are regarded; thin for Frisian and for Sorbian higher learning.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** far-right fraternities; the Nazi purge of Jewish and dissenting scholars; dismissals of East German academics after 1990; school tracking and migrant children is politically charged.
