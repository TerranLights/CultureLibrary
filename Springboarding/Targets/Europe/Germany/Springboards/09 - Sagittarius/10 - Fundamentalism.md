# Germany: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Plautdietsch (Mennonite Low German); Turkish, Arabic, Russian, Polish. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Russian-German resettler congregations in Lippe, Bielefeld and the Paderborn area:** Baptist and Mennonite congregations often called strict by outsiders, to be tested against their own accounts and set beside the Lutheran churches next door.
- **The Siegerland and Wuppertal free-church traditions:** older home-grown free-church milieus, to set beside the resettler congregations.
- **Württemberg's conservative Pietist wings:** strictness inside a regional church that also has liberal currents.
- **Salafi milieus, the large mosque associations and liberal Muslims:** three positions on the letter of the text.
- **Traditionalist Catholics:** strictness against the Council inside the Catholic Church.
- **Völkisch and far-right pagan groups against non-political Germanic heathen associations:** both claim old roots; very different aims.
- **The Greens' "Fundis":** a secular use of the word to compare with the religious one.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; much material is polemical (from churches, the state, opponents or ex-members); balanced studies of insiders are rarer.
- **Languages needed:** German, early New High German for 1534-35; Plautdietsch and Russian for resettler congregations; Turkish and Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Sekte" and "fundamentalist" stigmatize; the security services monitor some Islamist and some far-right religious groups (official, contested framing); violence is rare and must not stand for the whole; leavers' and members' accounts need consent; the law of schooling and bans goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
