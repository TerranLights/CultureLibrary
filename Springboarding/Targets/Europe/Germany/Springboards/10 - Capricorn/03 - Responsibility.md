## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Germans after 1990:** who answers for the Treuhand closures; the sense of being held responsible for the GDR.
- **Ruhr and Saarland:** mining's lasting costs, and towns left to carry them.
- **Ahr valley and other flood districts:** blame between state, district and citizens after 2021.
- **Hanseatic merchant families:** the honorable-merchant model in living firms.
- **Turkish-German families, and victims' relatives after Solingen (1993) and the NSU:** responsibility owed to them by the state.
- **Sinti and Roma:** late recognition of the genocide; who has answered and who has not.
- **Low-income households and the self-employed:** blame for poverty, as each describes it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; the national literature on guilt and memory is very large, so search narrowly.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish and Greek for NSU victims' families; Polish and Russian for forced-labor survivors' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** guilt for the Holocaust and colonial wrongs (the Herero and Nama genocide, 1904-08, and the 2021 declaration with Namibia) is contested ground; read survivors' and descendants' accounts first. Inquiry files may be partly closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
