## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and West:** the GDR school, Pioneers and army against the West German shift after 1968; how each now speaks of strictness.
- **Pietist Württemberg:** self-examination traditions, and the Korntal children's-home inquiry (2018) as its shadow.
- **Upper Lusatia:** Herrnhut's Moravians and the Catholic Sorbs, two religious rhythms of self-denial side by side.
- **Catholic Rhineland:** the yearly turn from carnival into Lent, from license to restraint.
- **Progressive-school milieu:** the Odenwaldschule (Hesse) abuse case, which complicates any strict-against-free story.
- **Muslim families:** Ramadan fasting kept by children and adults in schools and workplaces.
- **Russian-German and Vietnamese families:** school expectations brought from other systems, to research, not assume.
- **Bundeswehr and police trainees:** institutional drill in a state that rewrote it after 1945.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and child-rearing; mixed for self-discipline in everyday adult life, which needs diaries, forums and fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** German (old print for Pietist and Prussian sources); Turkish and Arabic for religious fasting; Russian and Vietnamese for family schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in homes, schools and sport; survivors' accounts first; some inquiry and church files are closed.
- **Held for review:** none.
