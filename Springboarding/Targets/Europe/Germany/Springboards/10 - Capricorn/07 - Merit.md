## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former GDR:** merit set by class and loyalty until 1989, then GDR careers re-examined after 1990 (the *Abwicklung* of university staff).
- **Cologne and the Rhineland:** how people there use their own word for favor networks.
- **Travelling journeymen (*auf der Walz*):** brotherhoods that still vouch for skill and conduct on the road.
- **Turkish-German and Arab-German pupils and applicants:** the track recommendation at about ten, and the callback gap.
- **Russian-speaking Jewish arrivals since 1991; Syrian doctors:** degrees earned abroad, retested by German boards.
- **Jews in Prussia and the Reich:** equal in law, yet kept from chairs and officer ranks unless baptized.
- **Sinti and Roma:** school barriers, as the community's own education study records them.
- **Sorbs and the Danish minority:** their own grammar schools and leaving exams (Bautzen; Flensburg) as parallel routes to credentials.
- **Women in research:** the long *Habilitation* route, and programs meant to correct its outcomes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for education, admissions and elites; thin for how people explain others' success in daily talk (forums, interviews, fiction).
- **Languages needed:** German, older print for guild and Prussian sources; Turkish, Arabic and Russian for applicants' accounts; Danish and Sorbian for minority schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nazi propaganda bound *Leistung* to race; treat it as a period, not a frame. Plagiarism cases concern living people: cite only published decisions.
- **Held for review:** none.
