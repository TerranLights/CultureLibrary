# Germany: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Danish, Upper and Lower Sorbian; Turkish, Arabic, Russian. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it. Capricorn takes the spirit of the law, not its letter.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Germans:** the 1990s argument that the Rechtsstaat delivered procedure instead of the justice people expected.
- **Sorbs of Lower Lusatia:** Horno (*Rogow*) went to court against coal mining and lost; law as a shield that failed.
- **Sinti and Roma:** compensation courts that long refused to call their persecution racial (the 1956 Federal Court of Justice ruling *check*).
- **Large-family networks in Berlin, Essen and Bremen:** members' own accounts against police and media labels.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Arab families:** when elders' mediation is sought, and when a court is.
- **Russian-speaking Germans:** Soviet ways around rules and later trust in German courts, to research, not assume.
- **Church-asylum parishes:** congregations acting on conscience against a decision of the state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for legal culture and trust polls; everyday rule-bending needs ethnography and forums.
- **Languages needed:** German, including older legal print; Arabic, Turkish and Kurdish for mediation; Russian for post-Soviet arrivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "clan" and "parallel justice" talk can stigmatize whole groups. Nazi law and GDR justice are periods, not the frame. Protect prisoners' and victims' identities. Letter-of-the-law questions go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
