# Germany: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German, with Low German and city dialects (Kölsch, Berlinish); Upper and Lower Sorbian, Danish, North Frisian; Turkish, Arabic, Russian and Polish for immigrant neighborhoods. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something, and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages against big-city quarters:** a Lower Saxon or Hunsrück village set beside a Berlin Kiez or a Cologne Veedel.
- **Ruhr colliery estates:** neighborhood bonds built around pits that have since closed.
- **East German prefab estates and shrinking towns** (Halle-Neustadt, Berlin-Marzahn, Lusatian mining towns): community after mass out-migration.
- **Postwar expellees and their towns** (Espelkamp in Westphalia, among others): communities rebuilt from refugees, with home-region associations.
- **Catholic Sorbian villages of Upper Lusatia against the Protestant Lower Sorbs (Wends) of the Spreewald:** faith shaping the village year.
- **North Frisian islands and Halligen:** small, weather-bound communities.
- **Turkish- and Arab-heritage neighborhoods** (Duisburg-Marxloh, Berlin-Neukölln): congregations, shops and family networks.
- **Russian-German resettlers; Jewish congregations; Sinti families:** each with its own picture of who belongs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and village studies; mixed for the inner life of mosque, resettler and Sinti communities.
- **Languages needed:** German; Sorbian, Frisian and Danish for minority sources; Turkish and Arabic for congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Nazi-era *Volksgemeinschaft* colors older writing on community. Studies of Muslim communities are politically charged; read their framing. The 2021 flood is recent grief. Quarrels over quiet hours and neighbor disputes have a legal side for Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
