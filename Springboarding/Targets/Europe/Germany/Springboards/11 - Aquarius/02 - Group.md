# Germany: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Danish and Sorbian for minority groups; Turkish, Kurdish, Arabic and Russian for immigrant groups. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit: how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" and "them" are drawn, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ruhr and Saarland industrial workers against owner-run firms of Württemberg:** the strike and the union against the family firm as group models.
- **Former East against West:** state-organized children's and work groups against voluntary clubs; whether the old habits outlived 1990.
- **Catholic Rhineland against Protestant north:** milieus that once decided party, club and marriage.
- **Football regions:** Ruhr derby towns, Hamburg's two clubs, Berlin's east-west rivalry.
- **Turkish- and Kurdish-heritage associations:** groups shaped partly by homeland politics and its conflicts.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** close networks, often church-based, and their relation to wider society.
- **Danes and Frisians in Schleswig:** organized minority groups with their own party.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for unions, parties and conformity research; mixed for gangs, closed churches and family networks.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Kurdish, Russian and Arabic for immigrant group sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** collective guilt and the Nazi era; GDR files with personal data; the media label "clans" for some extended families is contested and stigmatizing, so read it critically and seek the families' own voices. Far-right groups and their symbols need care. Whether groups may be banned is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
