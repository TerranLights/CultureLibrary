## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Partible-inheritance Southwest against impartible-inheritance North and Westphalia:** whether siblings shared land equally shaped village rank.
- **East against West:** a state-flattened wage scale and women's full-time work, against the West's male-breadwinner pattern; and the later gap in elite posts.
- **Brandenburg and Mecklenburg estate country:** landed nobility, the 1945 expropriation, and what rank means there now.
- **Hanseatic patrician families:** a civic, non-noble elite; how its own stance toward noble titles is told is worth checking.
- **Turkish-, Kurdish- and Arab-heritage Germans by generation:** from guest workers to their grandchildren.
- **Sinti and Roma; Black Germans; Jewish Germans; resettlers:** each with its own record of unequal treatment.
- **Sorbs in Lusatia:** minority standing and the coal regions' structural change.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income, pay and attitudes; thinner for groups the state does not count by ethnicity.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish, Romani and Sorbian for the groups' own voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racism, antigypsyism and antisemitism; colorism and the word choices for Black Germans (use their own terms). Equality before the law, the constitution's equality clause and anti-discrimination law are for Libra; legal culture for Capricorn.
