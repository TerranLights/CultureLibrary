# Germany: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German; Danish and Sorbian for minority sources; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for immigrant communities. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **University towns** (Freiburg, Göttingen, Tübingen, Heidelberg): student and eco-movement milieus; Freiburg's Vauban district as an ideal built anew.
- **Württemberg Pietists** (Korntal, founded 1819, among others): ideals of a godly community.
- **Former GDR generations:** those raised on a state ideal, and their children.
- **Green-voting city districts against rural constituencies:** where ideals take party form.
- **Turkish-heritage Germans:** religious, secular and nationalist ideals in tension.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** an ideal of "Germany" carried for generations, set against arrival.
- **Muslims of many origins:** ideals of right living from faith, in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; German letters and philosophy give unusual depth, and surveys cover youth and values well.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish and Russian for immigrant ideals; older German for Romantic and Pietist sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Nazi era bent idealist language (sacrifice, the new man) to its ends; read older texts with that in mind. *Ülkücü* in Turkish-German contexts names a far-right movement; do not treat it as a plain synonym. Karl May and Native imagery is a live dispute; seek Native voices on it. Philosophy of mind is out of scope except where it shaped wider thought.
- **Held for review:** none.
