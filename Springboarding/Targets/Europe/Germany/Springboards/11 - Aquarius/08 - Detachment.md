# Germany: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Germany, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** German (with Middle High German for the mystics, and Low German); Danish; Turkish, Russian and Arabic for immigrant families' emotional norms. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; some forms are prized (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and others condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North coast and Hanseatic towns against the Rhineland:** test outsiders' pictures of reserve and warmth against data; never take either as given.
- **Protestant and Catholic mourning:** quiet composure against public display at funerals, as each community describes it.
- **Former GDR:** inner distance from the state, and after 1990 from politics; lower turnout as one sign to examine.
- **Berlin and other big cities against villages:** anonymity as relief or as coldness.
- **Professions of distance:** doctors, judges, journalists and police, and how their training sets the line.
- **Turkish-, Arab- and Kurdish-heritage families:** grief and display norms in their own words, and how majority Germans read them.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** composure norms from the Soviet past.
- **Men:** reserve and help-seeking, to be studied, not assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in philosophy, literature and the history of emotions; mixed for measured display rules inside the nation.
- **Languages needed:** German, including older forms for Eckhart; Turkish and Arabic for immigrant norms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "German coldness" is an outside stereotype; record it only as a claim. The Nazi era's cult of hardness and Heidegger's politics need care. Impartiality in courts belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none.
