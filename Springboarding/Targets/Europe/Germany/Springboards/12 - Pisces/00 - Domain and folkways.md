## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Rhineland and Münsterland vs. the Lutheran north:** intercession, relics and pilgrimage against sermon-and-hymn piety change how the unseen is approached.
- **Former GDR lands vs. the old West:** decades of state atheism lie behind belief gaps to measure; check whether the vocabulary shifts from soul to psyche.
- **Catholic Upper Lusatian vs. Protestant Lower Lusatian Sorbs:** one people, two confessions; spirit lore may diverge with them.
- **North Frisians, Saterland Frisians, the Danish minority:** sea and flood lore recorded in three languages.
- **Sinti:** community norms may govern what outsiders are told about the dead; approach through their own organizations.
- **Turkish and Kurdish Germans, Sunni and Alevi apart:** evil eye, jinn and the *hoca*'s amulet in two distinct practices.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** post-Soviet folk healing layered on Lutheran, Mennonite or Baptist piety.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. The classic folklore works (the *Handwörterbuch*, the 1930s *Atlas der deutschen Volkskunde*) were shaped by Nazi-era Volkskunde; read for sources, against framing. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled by the author; this sign's sheets are written for the core German lands. Bavaria lies outside the nation (Austro-Bavaria is a separate target).
- **Languages needed:** German, with Fraktur and Kurrent for archives; Low German; Sorbian; Frisian; Danish; Turkish, Arabic, Russian; Latin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient files of the murdered and confined; former children's-home inmates' testimony; Sinti knowledge of death; neopagan material near far-right use. Commitment and the regulation of healers are law: Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
