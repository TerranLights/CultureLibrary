## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rhineland Catholics vs. Protestant Hamburg and the north:** Caritas, Misereor and Martin's Day against Wichern's Innere Mission; two founding models of organized mercy.
- **Former GDR vs. the old West:** state-run solidarity and the Volkssolidarität against church charity; volunteering and giving rates differ to check.
- **Muslim communities (DITIB, Alevi, Arab):** *zekât* and *sadaka*, Ramadan meals for neighbors; giving through mosques and to home countries.
- **Jewish communities:** ZWST and the communities' own care of elderly Soviet-born members.
- **Danish minority in Schleswig:** its own health and social services; compassion through a minority's institutions.
- **Sinti and Roma:** persecuted under Nazism; how they now speak of being pitied or helped, in their own organizations' words.
- **Villages vs. cities:** *Landfrauen*, church congregations and fire brigades against city food banks and street outreach.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Giving, volunteering and welfare history are well documented; everyday words of pity need ethnography and corpora.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish and Arabic for mosque charity; Russian for resettlers; Hebrew for Jewish welfare.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Nazi *Winterhilfswerk* (compulsory "charity"); refugees and the *Tafel* admission quarrels; the GDR's own welfare claims. Welfare and asylum law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
