## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lusatian Sorbs:** villages dug away and a language thinning at once; dissolution as a community's lived risk, in Sorbian sources.
- **Ruhr and Saar mining towns:** the end of a work world and its words ("Glück auf"); compare with eastern towns after the Treuhand.
- **East Germans born before and after 1975:** a state that dissolved under them; memoirs and surveys split by age.
- **Rhineland carnival towns and Swabian-Alemannic *Fastnacht* towns:** two traditions of masked crowd release, different in form and mood.
- **City club scenes (Berlin, Leipzig, Hamburg) vs. village festivals:** chosen self-loss among the young.
- **Russian-German Pentecostal and Baptist congregations:** ecstatic or restrained worship, and the collapse of the Soviet world in their past.
- **Turkish and Kurdish Sufi circles and Alevi *cem* gatherings:** *vecd* and the *semah* in their own descriptions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutions and closures; mixed for experience, which lives in memoir, oral history and club culture.
