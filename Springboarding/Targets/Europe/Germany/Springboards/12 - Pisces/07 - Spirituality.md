## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern lands:** the nation's highest shares outside any church (check the figures); Jugendweihe and secular rites set against small, intense congregations.
- **Swabian Pietists (Württemberg):** home prayer circles (*Stunden*) and settlements such as Korntal; inwardness organized by lay people.
- **Catholic Rhineland and Münsterland:** pilgrimage, saints and processions as popular religiosity.
- **Upper Lusatian Sorbs:** Catholic village devotion in a minority language.
- **Alevis and Sunni Turks:** *cem* against mosque prayer; two forms of Muslim inner life.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** Orthodox, Mennonite, Baptist and Pentecostal piety, distinct from mainstream churches.
- **Jewish communities:** liberal, Orthodox and Chabad practice since the 1990s immigration.
- **City seekers and neopagans:** esoteric fairs, forest rituals and the contested Germanic revival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Church statistics and spirituality research are strong; seekers outside churches need ethnography and the esoteric book market.
- **Languages needed:** German; Latin; Sorbian; Turkish; Russian; Hebrew.
