*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former GDR and old West:** 1945 remembered under Soviet and Western occupation; 1989 as a regime giving up, told differently by its officials and its citizens.
- **Expellee families from Silesia, East Prussia and the Sudetenland:** loss of home accepted, mourned or contested across generations.
- **Lutheran and Pietist regions:** hymn and prayer language of *Ergebung*; compare with Catholic abandonment to Providence.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Arab Muslims:** *teslimiyet* and *tawakkul* in illness and grief; compare clinic encounters.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** *smirenie* in Orthodox, Baptist and Mennonite piety.
- **Families of the dying:** hospice regions against hospital deaths; who decides to stop.
- **Men and women:** who may "give up" without shame, in sport, work and illness; test any assumption.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1945, 1989 and end-of-life care; thinner for everyday giving-in, which needs interviews and idiom study.
- **Languages needed:** German; Turkish; Arabic; Russian; Polish and Czech for the expellee background.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war memory and the "victim" framing of defeat; expellee politics; assisted dying and baby hatches touch law (Libra, Capricorn): attitudes only here.
