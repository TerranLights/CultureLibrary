## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Münsterland and Westphalian countryside:** a regional lore of second sight (*Spökenkiekerei*) recorded in literature and folklore archives.
- **North Frisian islands and the coast:** weather sense and foreboding among fishers and dike keepers.
- **Farmers vs. city professionals:** *Bauernregeln* and the feel for soil against data-driven farming and office decision tools.
- **Former GDR schooling vs. the West's esoteric market:** scientific-materialist education against a large self-help and psychic trade; check survey splits.
- **Turkish and Kurdish Germans:** *içime doğdu*, dream interpretation and the *hoca*'s guidance.
- **Russian-German resettlers:** premonition and omen lore carried from the Volga and Kazakhstan.
- **Anthroposophic milieus (Waldorf families, biodynamic farms):** intuition taught as a method.
- **Women and men:** "weibliche Intuition" as a stock phrase; test it, do not assume it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for intellectual history and decision research; mixed for folk premonition, which sits in folklore archives and regional literature.
- **Languages needed:** German, including Early New High German for chapbooks; Low German; Danish; Turkish; Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gut judgments as prejudice (profiling, "Bauchgefühl" in policing and hiring); anthroposophy's contested race teachings, as history only.
- **Held for review:** none.
