# Hungary: Self and identity, the domain and its folkways (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, read here as Hungary as it stands plus the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land (a working reading; see Flags). **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (with Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech), Romani (Lovari) and Beash, Danube Swabian German, Slovak, Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac), Serbian, the Romanian of Hungary, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** what a self is here, and how it asserts itself: personhood, body, appearance, naming.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the Great Plain towns (Szeged, Kecskemét, Debrecen):** compare given-name fashions, since name tables are kept by county.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely):** check which label comes first in self-introduction (village, Székely, Hungarian); gate inscriptions as data.
- **Kalotaszeg and Mezőség villages against Kolozsvár:** check costume life-stage marks against an urban minority setting.
- **Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran and Unitarian Hungarians:** check biblical versus saints' names and *kopjafa* versus cross at the grave.
- **Jewish communities (Neolog, Orthodox, Status Quo):** check Hebrew names beside civil names and surname changes across the 19th and 20th centuries.
- **Roma (Romungro, Vlach Roma, Boyash):** check in-group names against registry names and how census self-identification is chosen.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Croats (Bunjevac, Šokac), Serbs, Romanians of Hungary, Rusyns:** check house names, *krsna slava* and name days as markers in each.
- **Armenian-descended Hungarians (Szamosújvár, Erzsébetváros):** check surname forms and baptismal records as lineage markers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for names, costume and portraits; thinner for regional self-concept surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian throughout; Romani and Beash for in-group naming; German and Serbian for house names and *slava*.
- **Lands:** the tracker lists Romania as the pre-war land carried; this sheet reads it as the Hungarians of Transylvania and the Székely Land (Csángó of Moldavia as comparison only), a working reading the author still confirms. The exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surname changes tie into 1944 and the post-1945 expulsions; Transylvanian census counts are contested; recent registers hold living persons' data.
- **Held for review:** name-change and civil-registry law, deferred to Libra.
