# Hungary: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian and its dialects; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against county towns (Miskolc, Győr, Szeged):** check whether negotiation and complaint habits differ by city size; the conciliation boards report by county.
- **Palóc villages of Nógrád and Heves:** check *csúfoló* rhymes and lads' boasts as sanctioned outlets for claims between villages.
- **Hungarians of Kolozsvár and Marosvásárhely against Székely-majority towns:** check when a speaker uses Hungarian in public offices, a minority-specific form of the concept.
- **Reformed congregations (Debrecen, Sárospatak) against Catholic parishes:** check lay voice in presbytery minutes against clergy-led parish councils.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash:** check speaking order at gatherings and whose voice settles a dispute, separately in each.
- **Šokac and Bunjevac Croats (Mohács, Baja):** check teasing couplets as a sung form of public claiming.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish communities:** check the 1868–69 Congress debates and their press for how each side pressed its case.
- **Danube Swabians and Békés Slovaks:** check letters to their weeklies (*Neue Zeitung*, *Ľudové noviny*) for when a returning public voice appears after 1945.
