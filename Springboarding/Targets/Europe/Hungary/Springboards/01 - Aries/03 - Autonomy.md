*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jászság and Kunság against neighboring manorial villages:** check whether the redemption heritage shows in local talk of self-rule, against villages without it.
- **Hajdú towns against Debrecen:** check how privileged soldier towns and a Reformed market town each framed local self-government.
- **Székely villages of Csík and Udvarhely against Kalotaszeg:** check the ordinance tradition beside a region without Székely liberties.
- **Unitarians of the Székely Land against Reformed and Catholic congregations:** check how much a congregation chose for itself.
- **Serbs (Szentendre, Ráckeve) and Romanians of Hungary (Gyula):** check church-run schooling as communal autonomy, each in its own record.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash:** check who decides marriage and moving out, separately for each.
- **Budapest young adults against Ormánság and Szatmár villages:** check age of leaving home and partner choice by settlement type.
- **Danube Swabians of the Swabian Turkey (Tolna, Baranya):** check inheritance customs that shaped who could set up an own household.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for communal self-rule (strong legal-historical and ethnographic records); good survey data for youth; thinner for elderly and disabled people's say.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian and Latin for the early charters; Serbian for church records; German for Swabian inheritance studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** present-day Székely autonomy demands are contested between communities and states; present them as claims, settle no border.
- **Held for review:** minority self-government statutes and present-day autonomy proposals, and any Vlach Roma community court, deferred to Libra and Capricorn.
