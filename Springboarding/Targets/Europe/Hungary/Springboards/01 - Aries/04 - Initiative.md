# Hungary: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Székely and Kalotaszeg villages against Great Plain *tanya* areas:** check whether work bees are called by neighbors, kin or the village, and how scattered farmsteads change that.
- **Békés Slovak Lutherans (Szarvas, Tótkomlós):** check how pastors, schools and cooperatives started local improvements.
- **Danube Swabians against Magyar villages of Tolna:** check founding stories and who is credited for a village's start.
- **Budapest against Miskolc and the industrial north:** check new-firm rates and the post-1990 collapse of heavy industry as a test of who started over.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check who founded associations and businesses after 1989, group by group, without reading economic need as a trait.
- **Reformed Debrecen and Catholic Szeged:** check church-founded schools and charities as different engines of local initiative.
- **Croats and Serbs of the south (Mohács, Ráckeve, Deszk):** check *moba* customs beside Hungarian *kaláka*.
- **Hungarians of Nagyvárad and Kolozsvár:** check EMKE branches and post-1990 civil founding against Székely towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business and civil-society statistics and the reform era; thinner for everyday unasked help at home.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; German for Swabian settlement sources; Slovak for Békés parish records; Serbian and Croatian for *moba*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** socialist-era private trading was policed; files on small traders may sit in state-security archives (ÁBTL) and name living people.
- **Held for review:** company and cooperative law and the legal side of the socialist second economy, deferred to Libra.
