# Hungary: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian and its dialects; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Šokac, Bunjevac); Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eger and the Northern Hungarian fortress towns against Szigetvár and the southern line:** check how each town ritualizes its siege, and which defenders it names.
- **Székely villages (Csík, Háromszék) and Kalotaszeg:** check men's dance and lads'-group trials of nerve against school-taught heroes.
- **Šokac Croats of Mohács against Hungarian neighbors:** check whether the Busójárás legend is told the same way by both.
- **Jewish communities, Neolog and Orthodox:** check survivor testimony for how each tells resistance, endurance and rescue.
- **Roma (Romungro, Vlach Roma, Boyash):** check Roma Holocaust testimony and tales of strong youths in Roma storytelling, separately for each group, and how sources are scarce.
- **Women:** check how women's endurance (1552, 1944–45, 1956) is or is not filed as courage in memorials.
- **Danube Swabians:** check how expulsion and the *malenkij robot* deportations are told in terms of endurance.
- **Catholic, Reformed and Unitarian clergy in Transylvania:** check moral-courage memory under communism, each church in its own record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and literary heroism; mixed for civilian and moral courage; thin for minorities' own hero traditions.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin and Ottoman Turkish for siege sources; Croatian for Busójárás song and talk; Romani for testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1944 (including the role of Hungarian authorities), the 1943 Don bend losses, 1956 and the interwar Order of Vitéz are contested in memory; present each as a claim.
- **Held for review:** military-service duty and conscription law, deferred to Capricorn.
