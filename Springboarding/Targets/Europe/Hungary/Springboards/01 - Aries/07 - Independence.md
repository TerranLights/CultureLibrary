# Hungary: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian and its dialects; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** not depending on others: self-reliance and self-sufficiency, for households, persons and the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Debrecen and the Reformed east against Catholic western Transdanubia (Győr, Sopron):** check whether 1848–49 and 1867 are remembered differently in local ritual.
- **Great Plain *tanya* families against Ormánság villages:** check household self-provisioning and the shame or pride in buying food.
- **Székely Land against the Partium (Nagyvárad, Szatmár):** check how mountain and plain households differ in provisioning and seasonal migration for work.
- **Boyash, Romungro and Vlach Roma:** check each group's historical trades and their end under socialism, using occupation records, never a trait.
- **Danube Swabians of the Swabian Turkey:** check farm inheritance and how a household secured its own land before 1945.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish households:** check *parnose* and the trades left open to them, by period and region.
- **Greek Catholic and Rusyn villages of the north-east:** check seasonal harvest work on the plain as a form of household self-reliance.
- **Elderly people in Budapest against villages:** check living alone, family care and who asks for help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national independence history and household statistics; thinner for emotional independence and help-seeking.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin for the Diet records; Romani and Beash for Roma livelihood testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the kuruc corpus mixes genuine and forged texts; welfare and public-works debates are tied to Roma stigma; keep each claim attributed.
- **Held for review:** the 1867 Compromise's legal text and present-day welfare and public-works rules, deferred to Libra.
