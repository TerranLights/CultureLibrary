## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest elite gymnasiums against county schools (Szeged, Debrecen, Pécs):** check how OKTV and KöMaL results spread by place.
- **Hungarian-language schools in Kolozsvár, Csíkszereda and Sepsiszentgyörgy:** check their own contests and their pupils' entry to Hungary's competitions.
- **Ferencváros and Újpest districts against Debrecen and Győr:** check how club rivalry maps onto neighborhood and city identity.
- **Danube Swabian villages:** check *Kirchweih* lads' contests and dance prizes beside Hungarian Pentecost customs.
- **Šokac and Bunjevac Croats, and Serbs of the south:** check horse races and wrestling at saints'-day fairs.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash:** check music and dance contests and who judges them, separately, noting where sources are thin.
- **Jewish communities:** check Budapest Jewish sport clubs and Orthodox and Neolog attitudes to secular competition.
- **Palóc and Matyó villages:** check embroidery, costume and harvest-festival prizes as women's contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and school contests; thinner for envy and for workplace rivalry.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin for Galeotto; German and Croatian for fair-day contests.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some ultra groups carry extremist symbols; the Kurultáj and "Hun-heritage" sports touch contested origin politics; doping cases name living athletes.
- **Held for review:** competition (antitrust) law and the rules of public tendering, deferred to Libra.
