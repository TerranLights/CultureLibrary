## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Great Plain outlaw country (Szeged, Kiskunság, Bakony as a Transdanubian contrast):** check which local betyárs are remembered as heroes and which as criminals.
- **Nyírség and Szatmár gentry memory against Transdanubian estates:** check gambling and revelry tales in county press and memoirs.
- **Lake Balaton resorts against river towns on the Tisza:** check drownings and rescues by season, and local warning customs.
- **Székely Land mountain villages:** check accidents in logging, haymaking and mountain work, as told in Hungarian-language press there.
- **Palóc and Matyó villages:** check lads' dares at maypole and Pentecost time against what elders punished.
- **Roma (Romungro, Vlach Roma, Boyash):** check whether outside sources label risk in ways the communities reject, and look for each group's own sources; flag where they are thin.
- **Danube Swabians and Slovaks:** check *Kirchweih* and harvest-festival revelry customs and their limits.
- **Young men in Budapest against small towns:** check ESPAD and road data by settlement type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics (roads, drink, gambling, insurance) and literature; thinner for the community's own sense of where daring ends.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin for Brodarics; German and Slovak for festival revelry sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking and risk statistics are easily turned into stereotypes of Roma or of rural men; the foreign-currency loan crisis is politically charged; accident records name private persons.
- **Held for review:** the traffic code, zero-tolerance drink-driving rules and gambling licensing, deferred to Libra.
