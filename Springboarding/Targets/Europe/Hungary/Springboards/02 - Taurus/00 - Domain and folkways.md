# Hungary: Possessions and value, the domain and its folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe: the present state together with Transylvania's Hungarian communities, Székely Land included (working reading; see Flags). **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech), Romani and Beash, Danube Swabian German, Slovak, Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac), Serbian, the Romanian of Hungary, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** what is worth having, and how is it held: possessions, money, resources, food, dress and wealth ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Kiskunság farmsteads (*tanya*):** compare what is bought with what is grown or stored.
- **Wine villages (Badacsony, Sopron, Villány, Tokaj):** check where vineyards and cellars rank among holdings.
- **Matyó, Palóc and Sárköz communities:** check how each source dates the arrival of cash income in dress and interiors.
- **Székely villages (Csík, Udvarhelyszék) and Kalotaszeg:** separate shared forest and pasture from private plots before comparing.
- **Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran and Greek Catholic parishes:** compare records on alms, tithes and fasts.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities (historical):** check kosher provisioning, dowry customs and community charity separately for each.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families:** check each group's own money words and earning trades in community-led sources, not outsiders' reports.
- **Danube Swabians, Békés Slovaks, Šokac and Bunjevac Croats, Serbs, Gyula Romanians, Komlóska Rusyns:** compare trousseau contents and parish feasts village by village.
- **Jász and Kun towns (Jászberény, Karcag) and Armenian-descended Szamosújvár:** check town records of land redemption and of merchant trade.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for material culture, food, dress and price series; thinner for household money talk and smaller minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish and Latin for early tax and estate records; German for Swabian village sources; Romani and Beash for in-group money words; Hungarian throughout.
- **Lands:** there is no scope file yet; the tracker carries Romania, which this run takes to mean Transylvania's Hungarian communities, Székely Land included (Moldavian Csángó for comparison only), pending the author.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property seized in 1944 and in the 1946–48 expulsions; collectivization; contested restitution of church and common property in Transylvania; stereotyped press on Roma poverty.
- **Held for review:** property and inheritance law (the *Tripartitum*, land registry), deferred to Libra; tax morality and legal culture, to Capricorn.
