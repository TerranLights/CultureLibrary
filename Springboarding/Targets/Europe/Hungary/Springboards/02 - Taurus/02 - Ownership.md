# Hungary: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian Transylvanian and Székely communities. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (with Székely and Palóc regional words), German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Romani, Beash, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ormánság and Sárköz villages:** check how each local source links household size to keeping land whole, and who wrote it.
- **Jászság, Kunság and the Hajdú towns:** compare claims of bought or granted freedom as told in local anniversaries.
- **Great Plain farmstead (*tanya*) families against old village cores:** separate land held at a distance from the house-plot.
- **Székely Land and Kalotaszeg:** check common forest and pasture against private plots, and post-1990 restitution claims.
- **Sopron's German-speaking vintners (*poncichter*) and Tokaj growers:** check vineyard tenure and inheritance in each.
- **Danube Swabians, Bukovina Székelys and Hungarians moved from southern Slovakia:** compare how each group's accounts treat the same houses taken over in 1945–48.
- **Jewish communities (Neolog, Orthodox, Status Quo):** check community and family property loss and restitution, from community archives.
- **Roma settlements (*telep*):** check how house titles were held, given or withheld, from local studies and residents' accounts.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Unitarian and Serbian Orthodox churches:** compare church-land loss after 1945 and restitution claims, especially in Transylvania.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical tenure and collectivization; good official land and spending data for Hungary; thin and contested for restitution in Transylvania.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and older Hungarian for urbarial records; German for Swabian property files; Yiddish and Hebrew for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1944 seizures, 1945–48 expulsions and resettlements, collectivization and Transylvanian restitution are each contested ground: present every claim as a claim.
- **Left-right note:** the spending figure exists for Hungary as a state; no separate one exists for the Transylvanian communities, so treat any Romanian figure as comparison only.
- **Held for review:** the *Tripartitum*'s noble land rules, the 1991 compensation statutes and land-registry law, deferred to Libra.
