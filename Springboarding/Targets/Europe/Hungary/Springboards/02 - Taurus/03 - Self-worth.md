# Hungary: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian, Romani, Beash, German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest office workers and Borsod industrial towns (Ózd, Miskolc):** check whether loss of work changes self-ratings differently in each.
- **Debrecen and the Reformed east against Catholic Transdanubia (Győr, Pécs):** compare sermons and school mottos on humility, diligence and grace as grounds of worth.
- **Lesser nobility's descendants and peasant families in the Great Plain:** check how family histories still invoke rank or its absence.
- **Kolozsvár and Székely towns (Sepsiszentgyörgy, Marosvásárhely):** check how minority status enters youth surveys and memoirs, kept separate from Hungary-based samples.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash communities:** check each group's own terms for respect and shame, and how school and work experiences are told by members.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish families (historical) and today's Jewish community:** check how family standing and learning are described in community memoirs.
- **Germans of Baranya and Tolna, Slovaks of the Pilis, Serbs of Szentendre, Romanians of Méhkerék, Rusyns of Múcsony, Bunjevac and Šokac Croats:** check how each group's own press frames recognition of its language and history.
- **Jász and Kun descendants and the Armenian-descended of Erzsébetváros:** check whether a distinct heritage is named as a source of standing in local publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for status history and titles; moderate for measured self-esteem; thin for minority-specific self-worth outside Roma studies.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin for the *Libri Regii*; Romani and Beash for respect vocabulary; Romanian-state data on Transylvanian Hungarians only for comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data invite stereotype: report regions and years, not "a people"; the Order of Vitéz carries interwar political baggage; Roma studies need community voices.
- **Held for review:** title and honors statutes, to Libra; status and reputation as social order, to Capricorn.
