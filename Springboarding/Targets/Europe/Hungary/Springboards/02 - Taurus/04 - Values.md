# Hungary: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian, Romani, Beash, German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformed Debrecen and the Tiszántúl against Catholic Győr, Eger and Szeged:** check child-quality answers and sermon themes separately rather than pooling them.
- **Lutheran Slovaks of Békéscsaba and Lutheran Germans of the Bakony:** check whether shared denomination outweighs language in their church texts.
- **Transylvanian Unitarians (Kolozsvár, Székelykeresztúr):** check which values their own catechisms and sermons put first.
- **Greek Catholics of the Nyírség (Hajdúdorog, Nyíregyháza):** compare liturgy-based moral teaching with Roman Catholic neighbors.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations (historical and present):** separate their schools, sermons and press before generalizing.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families, churchgoing and not:** check whether conversion, not ethnicity, explains differences in surveys.
- **Budapest graduates against Ormánság and Borsod villages:** check whether survey gaps reflect class and age rather than place.
- **Croats of Baranya, Serbs of Szentendre and Romanians of Gyula:** check how each group's Orthodox or Catholic calendar frames teaching in its schools.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: repeated surveys from the 1980s, abundant sermons, catechisms and proverbs; minority-specific survey samples are small.
- **Languages needed:** Hungarian; Latin and Greek for Baranyai Decsi; Hebrew and Yiddish for congregational records; Romani and Beash with community translators.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *népi–urbánus* divide touches antisemitism and current politics; "crisis of values" talk often blames groups: track who says it about whom.
- **Held for review:** the rules on *hálapénz* and on school religious education, to Libra; obedience and legal culture, to Capricorn.
