# Hungary: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian, Romani, Beash, German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest housing estates (Békásmegyer, Újpalota) against old inner districts:** check length of residence and turnover from census tract data.
- **Hollókő, the Őrség and Göcsej:** check how heritage status and depopulation affect who stays.
- **Debrecen *cívis* families and Szeged's Lower Town (*Alsóváros*):** compare family memory of continuity in two Great Plain cities.
- **Csík, Udvarhely and Háromszék villages:** check emigration to Hungary against those who stayed, using Transylvanian Hungarian studies.
- **Germans who were not expelled (for example around Pécs and in Baranya):** check continuity of language and parish life after 1948.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash communities:** check residential history after the clearance program, from family and local accounts.
- **Rusyns of Komlóska, Slovaks of the Pilis, Croats of the Drava, Serbs of Szentendre:** check language continuity across three generations, as Bartha-type studies measure it.
- **Jewish communities:** compare continuity broken in 1944 with the communities rebuilt after, using community archives and oral history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official mobility, marriage and price series and for Kádár-era culture; mixed for Transylvanian residence data held by the Hungarian side.
- **Languages needed:** German for *Heimatbücher*; minority-language skill for language-maintenance studies; Romanian only where Hungarian-side studies cite Romanian censuses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "staying in the native land" carries minority-political weight; Roma rehousing and the German expulsions are contested and painful.
- **Held for review:** the Romanian census of Transylvania (comparison only, a separate culture-nation's source); constitutional and political stability, outside this concept.
