# Hungary: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian, Romani, Beash, German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Székely villages and Kalotaszeg against Budapest:** compare ballad and folk-song treatments of jealousy with present-day forum talk, noting age of sources.
- **Great Plain farmstead families and Transdanubian wine villages:** check mother-in-law and daughter-in-law relations in household ethnographies of each.
- **Lake Balaton's north and south shores:** compare who claims shore access in local press: residents, second-home owners, visitors.
- **Reformed, Catholic and Lutheran congregations:** check how marriage-preparation materials address jealousy and control.
- **Orthodox and Neolog Jewish families (historical):** check matchmaking and courtship customs in memoirs, separately for each.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash families:** check courtship and marriage customs only through community-made sources and Roma women's own accounts.
- **Danube Swabian, Croatian (Šokac, Bunjevac), Serbian, Slovak and Romanian villages:** check wedding songs and dowry-guarding customs in each group's collections.
- **Rusyn Komlóska and the Jász towns:** check whether local monographs record inheritance quarrels between siblings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature, ballads and proverbs; moderate in surveys of control; thin for minority-specific family data.
- **Languages needed:** archaic Hungarian for the *Mária-siralom*; Székely dialect for *Vadrózsák*; German for early Budapest-school psychoanalytic papers; Yiddish for Jewish humor and memoir collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence and femicide data concern victims; Roma courtship customs are a frequent target of stereotype; helpline records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** the statutes on restraining orders and partner violence, to Libra.
