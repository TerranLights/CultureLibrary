## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformed Debrecen and Sárospatak against Catholic Esztergom and Eger:** check how each church's memory of persecution frames holding firm.
- **Transylvanian Unitarians (Kolozsvár, Székelykeresztúr) and Székely Catholics (Csíksomlyó):** compare which refusals each community commemorates, and on which days.
- **Great Plain farmstead families and Transdanubian villages:** check collectivization records for regional differences in delay and refusal.
- **Lutheran Slovaks of Békés and Lutheran Germans:** check whether congregations kept their own language in services against pressure.
- **Danube Swabians:** check how families who stayed and expellees' associations each speak of the 2012 commemoration.
- **Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities:** check community records on keeping separate institutions after the 1868–69 Congress.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families:** check how school and housing files labeled "refusal", and whose word that was, against families' own accounts.
- **Serbs of Szentendre and Ráckeve, Croats of Baranya, Rusyns of Komlóska:** check what each group's histories say about keeping a church or calendar under pressure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for martyr narratives, literature, proverbs and political history; thin for everyday stubbornness outside parenting talk and folktales.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for 16th–17th-century church records; Romanian for Bartók's source text (comparison only); German for Swabian commemorations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mindszenty, 1956 and Transylvanian minority claims are contested: present each as a claim; "stubbornness" labels are often pinned on whole groups, so record who uses them.
- **Held for review:** strike law and civil-disobedience cases, to Libra; obedience and its limits, to Capricorn.
