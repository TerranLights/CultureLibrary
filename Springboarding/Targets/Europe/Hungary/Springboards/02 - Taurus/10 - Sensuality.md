# Hungary: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian, Romani, Beash, German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian, Rusyn, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest bath-goers and the Alföld spa towns (Hajdúszoboszló, Gyula):** check who uses the baths for leisure and who for treatment, from spa and health-insurance data.
- **Szeged and Kalocsa against Transdanubian cooking (Őrség, Göcsej):** compare spice, fat and sweetness in regional cookbooks.
- **Szatmár and the Nyírség against Kecskemét:** check pálinka customs at feasts and wakes in each.
- **Kalotaszeg, Szék (Mezőség) and the Székely Land:** check dance and spinning-room customs from local collections, not only revival sources.
- **Greek Catholics of Hajdúdorog, Roman Catholics and Reformed families:** compare fasting calendars and what is given up.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish households (historical and present):** check kosher rules, Sabbath meals and family recipes separately.
- **Romungro musicians, Vlach Roma singers and Boyash dancers:** check performers' own accounts of performing for others' pleasure and for themselves.
- **Šokac Croats of Mohács (the Busójárás), Danube Swabian wine villages, Serbs of Szentendre:** compare feast foods and dances at each group's main festival.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, baths, music and dance; moderate for touch and greeting norms; thin for private pleasures, as expected.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish (or the Hungarian translation) for Evliya; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish food customs; Romani and Beash for musicians' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "Gypsy music" as entertainment carries a history of stereotype and unequal pay; old sayings that tie pleasure to a national mood are labels to trace, not data; sexual pleasure belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** alcohol and smoking rules, to Libra.
