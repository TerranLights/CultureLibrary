# Hungary: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with Transylvania's Hungarian communities and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian; German (the old Pest press and Swabian papers); Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical); Latin (early press). **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against county seats (Pécs, Győr, Debrecen) and small Nyírség villages:** check where county dailies and local papers survive and what replaced the ones that closed.
- **Székely Land and Kolozsvár readers:** separate news from Hungary, Transylvanian Hungarian outlets and Romanian-language media before comparing.
- **Roma settlements in Borsod and Baranya:** check digital access and the trusted messengers named in community-led studies.
- **German, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian and Romanian nationality papers:** compare what each covers, and what readers turn to instead.
- **Rusyn and Beash readers:** check whether any regular print or online outlet exists; expect scarcity.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities (historical):** compare their papers' audiences and languages.
- **Catholic, Reformed and Lutheran church press:** check parish newsletters as local information channels in small towns.
- **Older rural listeners against urban youth:** check radio, television and phone use by age in the survey tables.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history and digitized newspapers; mixed for current trust (polarized sources); thin for Rusyn, Beash and Romani-language news.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and German for early and Pest press; Romanian for Transylvanian audience studies; Yiddish and German for Jewish papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state-security files name living people and need permits; media-ownership and media-freedom accounts are politically contested, so read both sides.
- **Held for review:** freedom-of-information, press and data-protection statutes to Libra; what openness and secrecy are for, to Capricorn; the state-security files' secret side also bears on Scorpio.
