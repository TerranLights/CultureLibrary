## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest families with private lessons against Kiskunság farmstead (*tanya*) children:** check how far each learns at the kitchen table, in the field or from a tutor.
- **Reformed college towns (Debrecen, Sárospatak, Pápa) against Catholic Eger and Győr:** compare catechism and recitation as learning methods.
- **Kolozsvár Unitarians and Székely Land Catholics (Csíkszereda, Gyergyó):** check what each community's own schools kept up under Romanian rule.
- **Boyash, Vlach Roma and Romungro children:** separate home-language learning for each before comparing school outcomes.
- **Swabian, Slovak and Hungarian villages of Baranya, Tolna and Békés:** compare *cseregyerek* testimony from both sides of each exchange.
- **Palóc and Göcsej craftspeople:** check regional apprenticeship and master-pupil records in craft ethnographies.
- **Serbian Orthodox parishes of Szentendre and Croatian Šokac of Mohács:** compare church-taught literacy in their own scripts.
- **Orthodox and Neolog Jewish families (historical):** compare *héder* with Hungarian state schooling in the same towns.
