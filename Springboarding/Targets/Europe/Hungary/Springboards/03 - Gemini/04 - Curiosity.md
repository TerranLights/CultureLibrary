*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against small Zala and Vas villages (Göcsej, Őrség):** check whether curiosity about neighbors shows up in ethnographies as watchfulness, visiting or prying, and where the line falls.
- **Marosvásárhely and Sepsiszentgyörgy:** compare how Székely Land schools and societies channel inquiry with how Hungary's do.
- **Jász and Kun communities:** check local interest in Cuman and Jász origins, and which sources feed it.
- **Danube Swabian descendants:** check family-history curiosity after the 1946–48 expulsions in village heritage groups.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash communities:** check who asks whom: studies of researchers' questions and the community's own questions about its past.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish traditions:** compare what each treated as proper and improper inquiry.
- **Slovaks of Tótkomlós, Rusyns of Komlóska, Croats of Mohács:** check whether local museums and heritage houses record curiosity about origins in their own language.
- **Armenian-descended Hungarians of Gyergyószentmiklós and Szamosújvár:** check genealogy and heritage-society activity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for scholarship, exploration and popular science; mixed for everyday nosiness; thin for Roma and Beash views of curiosity in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Friar Julian and Bél; German for Reguly's and Vámbéry's circles; Romanian for Transylvanian society records; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish scholarly sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin research overlaps with contested and fringe theories, so treat each claim as a claim; research on Roma communities has a history of intrusive questioning.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on inquiry or exploration was set aside under the standing rules.
