# Hungary: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, and the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and feeling connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest prefab housing estates (*lakótelep*) against Hajdú and Jász market towns:** check which settings the network surveys show yielding the most ties beyond kin.
- **Commuter villages around Budapest and depopulating Ormánság hamlets:** compare who checks on the old and the isolated.
- **Székely and Kalotaszeg villages linked to towns in Hungary:** check partnership records and migrant networks from both ends.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash households:** check each group's own godparent and kin customs in community-led sources.
- **Swabians of Budaörs and the Swabian Turkey (Baranya, Tolna):** compare ties kept with expellee groups in Germany.
- **Šokac and Bunjevac Croats and Szentendre and Battonya Serbs:** compare *kumstvo* with Hungarian *komaság* in their own sources.
- **Jewish communities of the Nyírség and Szatmár (historical) and today's Budapest community:** check town associations and memorial books.
- **Greek Catholic and Reformed parishes in Szabolcs:** compare godparent choice across the confessional line.
- **Armenian-descended families of Erzsébetváros (Dumbrăveni):** check family and church registers for godparent chains.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for network sociology and ritual kinship; mixed for loneliness; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Bunjevac godparent customs.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for medieval charters; German for expellee records; Yiddish, Hebrew and English for memorial books; Romanian for Transylvanian partner-town sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *protekció* and nepotism are live political charges, so keep cases as claims; Roma usury networks are a stigmatized topic; the Holocaust's broken networks.
- **Held for review:** friendship and community to Aquarius; belonging to Cancer; merit against connections to Capricorn.
