# Hungary: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, including the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical); Ottoman Turkish for early sources. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformed Debrecen and the Tiszántúl against Catholic Western Transdanubia (Győr, Sopron):** compare pastors' and priests' place as go-betweens in parish records.
- **Greek Catholic and Rusyn parishes of Szabolcs:** check intercession customs at Máriapócs against Roman Catholic pilgrim records.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash communities:** check whether each has its own settling customs and how they meet Hungarian institutions.
- **Székely villages (Csík, Háromszék):** compare the village's own peacemaking roles with church and council records.
- **Marosvásárhely after March 1990:** check local Hungarian and Romanian accounts of who stepped between the sides, as separate claims.
- **Swabian and Slovak villages:** compare bride-asking and wedding sponsors with Hungarian neighbors' customs.
- **Serbian and Croatian *kum* roles:** check whether the godparent also acts as peacemaker in family quarrels.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** compare the *shadkhn* and the rabbinical court's informal settling with civil institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wedding go-betweens, labor bargaining and the 1989 talks; mixed for family mediation; thin for the *kris*, Beash and Rusyn settling customs.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish and old Hungarian for the pashas' letters; Romanian for the Transylvanian talks; Romani for *kris* studies; Yiddish for matchmaking sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outsiders' writing on the *kris* can exoticize or expose; Marosvásárhely 1990 is contested between communities.
- **Held for review:** court-annexed mediation and its statutes to Libra; diplomacy between states to Libra; authority that imposes decisions to Capricorn.
