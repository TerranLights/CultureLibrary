*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small villages of Zala and Somogy against Budapest districts:** check whether ethnographies and forums show different fears of being talked about.
- **Bukovina Székely settlers of Tolna and Baranya:** compare legend-telling in Kakasd and similar villages with that of their Swabian and Hungarian neighbors.
- **Kalotaszeg and Mezőség villages:** check Kolozsvár folklorists' records of talk about named people.
- **Vlach Roma, Romungro and Boyash families:** check each group's own words for shame and talk, and how outside rumor about them differs from inside talk.
- **Slovak, Swabian and Croatian villages:** compare carnival shaming customs, village by village.
- **Orthodox and Neolog Jewish communities (historical):** check sermons and teaching on *lashn-hore* against the press record of Tiszaeszlár.
- **Reformed and Catholic congregations of the Great Plain:** compare preaching against slander in Debrecen and Kalocsa sermon collections.
- **Teenagers in Budapest and in small towns:** check online gossip and cyberbullying by settlement type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, witch trials and the tabloid press; mixed for today's everyday gossip; thin for Beash and Rusyn terms and settings.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and early Hungarian for trial records; German for Swabian customs; Romani for in-group reputation talk; Yiddish and Hebrew for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the blood libel and anti-Roma rumor must be handled as false or contested accusations; charivari targets were often women and the unmarried; children's online data needs care.
- **Held for review:** defamation and press law to Libra; secrecy and betrayal to Scorpio; reputation and honor to Leo and Capricorn.
