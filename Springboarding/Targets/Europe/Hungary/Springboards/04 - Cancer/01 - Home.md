# Hungary: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarians of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; the Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest renters against owner-occupiers in Great Plain towns (Hódmezővásárhely, Cegléd):** check whether ownership and renting carry different weight in the two settings.
- **Miskolc's "numbered streets" (*Számozott utcák*) and other evicted neighborhoods:** check residents' accounts against city statements.
- **Lake Balaton's holiday houses (*nyaraló*) against the owners' home villages:** check which one people call home.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy) and the Mezőség:** compare accounts of houses threatened in 1988 and houses left behind by workers abroad.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash households:** check settlement and housing records per group, not as one unit.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Croats (Bunjevac, Šokac), Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare what each group's memory says about houses lost or taken over after 1945.
- **Greek Catholics of the northeast against Reformed villages of the Nyírség:** check how house blessing and household icons differ.
- **Budapest Jewish families (Neolog, Orthodox, Status Quo):** compare postwar returns to flats with the yellow-star house records.
- **Ormánság and Göcsej small villages:** check empty houses and how locals speak of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and homelessness counts; mixed for home as feeling; thin for Rusyn and Beash housing.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for the systematization record; German for Swabian house memory; Yiddish for emigrants' "old home".
- **Sensitive or restricted:** evictions of Roma families; the 1951 removals; homes taken in 1944–45; the *vagonlakók* within the contested Trianon memory.
- **Held for review:** tenancy and eviction statutes to Libra; the argument over whether housing is a right to Capricorn.
