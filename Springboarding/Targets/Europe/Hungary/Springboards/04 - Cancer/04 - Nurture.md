## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest crèche users against Great Plain farmstead families:** check how far *bölcsőde* places reach and who cares for infants where they do not.
- **Palóc and Matyó grandmothers against younger mothers:** compare swaddling and feeding advice across generations in oral histories.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely) and Kalotaszeg families with parents working abroad:** check who raises the children left at home.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families in Sure Start areas:** take each group's account of early care from its own parents.
- **Danube Swabian, Slovak, Croat, Serb, Romanian and Rusyn families:** check whether nationality kindergartens or grandparents carry the home language to children.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian Sunday-school traditions:** compare what each church gives children to learn.
- **Budapest's psychoanalytic circle against Orthodox Jewish families of the northeast:** separate a professional theory of childhood from a religious one in pre-1944 sources.
- **Ormánság and Nyírség villages:** check health-visitor reports for small-village differences.
- **Jász towns and Szeged and the South:** check local museums' collections of cradles and toys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health-visitor history, cohort data and children's literature; mixed for home routines; thin for Rusyn and Beash child-rearing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and old Hungarian for *Pax corporis*; German for Ferenczi's originals; Romani and Beash for Sure Start interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma parents in child-protection cases; children placed in state care from poor families; data on infants needs consent.
- **Held for review:** discipline and parental authority go to Capricorn; nothing on nurture itself was set aside.
