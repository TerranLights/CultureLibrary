## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Danube and Tisza flood towns (Szeged, Szolnok, Szentendre):** compare flood-defense memory between towns that were hit and towns that were spared.
- **Budapest against the Great Plain *tanya* world:** check accounts of farmsteads far from police and fire help.
- **Northern Hungary (Miskolc, Eger, the Borsod villages):** check how patrols, police and residents describe safety in mixed Roma and non-Roma settlements.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy):** separate Madéfalva memory from present-day mountain rescue and forest work.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families:** take each community's own account of who protected it in 2008–11.
- **Danube Swabians of Baranya and Tolna, Croats of Mohács, Slovaks and Serbs:** compare patron saints and village guards (fire, hail, plague).
- **Greek Catholics and Catholics of the northeast against Reformed villages:** check whether protective prayers and blessings differ by church.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo survivors of Budapest 1944:** compare which rescuers each community's testimony names.
- **Ormánság, Őrség and Göcsej small villages:** check how ageing villages guard lone older residents against scams.
