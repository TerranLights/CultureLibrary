# Hungary: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarians of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; the Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest mothers against Great Plain and Nyírség village mothers:** check hospital-birth, breastfeeding and return-to-work data by settlement type.
- **Palóc and Matyó grandmothers:** compare oral accounts of village midwives with later hospital births.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Marosvásárhely) and Kalotaszeg:** check Marian prayer collections against parish records of births.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash mothers:** check each group's own birth and naming customs, beside health-visitor reports.
- **Danube Swabian, Slovak, Croat, Serb, Romanian and Rusyn mothers:** compare which mother-centered feast each keeps (Mother's Day, *Materice*, Assumption).
- **Catholic and Greek Catholic Marian devotion against Reformed and Unitarian practice:** check how mothers are honored where Mary is not prayed to.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish women's associations:** check pre-1944 records of maternity aid by community.
- **Ormánság and Sárköz:** check how one-child villages appear in mothers' own words, not only in statistics.
- **Jász and Kun towns; the South around Szeged:** check local museums for birth and christening objects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for birth statistics, Marian devotion and canonical literature; mixed for mothers' own accounts; thin for Rusyn and Beash mothers.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and old Hungarian for early Marian texts; German for Semmelweis; Serbian and Croatian for *Materice*; Romani for Romani women's voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pronatalism and abortion are political in Hungary; Roma women's reproductive health, including past claims of coerced sterilization; the home-birth court cases; medical records are closed.
- **Held for review:** abortion goes to Aries (autonomy); maternity-leave statutes to Libra; nothing else on motherhood was set aside.
