## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest clubs against town balls on the Great Plain (Kecskemét, Hódmezővásárhely):** check which venues carry celebration, and who pays.
- **Szeged against the Hortobágy and Kiskunság:** separate shows staged for visitors from those for locals.
- **Transdanubia (Győr, Sopron, the Őrség, the Balaton wine hills):** compare vintage festivals parish by parish.
- **Palóc lands and Eger's hinterland:** check how costume and the *búcsú* fair mark a feast day.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy), Kalotaszeg and the Mezőség (Szék):** read each village's dance-music collection on its own.
- **Šokac and Bunjevac Croats of Mohács and Baja:** keep carnival masking apart from Hungarian *farsang* records.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check who performs for whom, and in which language.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks of Békéscsaba and the Nyíregyháza *tirpák* farms, Serbs, Romanians of Gyula, Rusyns:** list each nationality's festival calendar; for scarce Rusyn material, start with the local nationality self-governments.
- **Reformed Debrecen, Catholic shrine towns, Neolog and Orthodox Jews:** compare teaching on dance, theater and Purim balls.
- **Jász and Kun towns; Armenian-descended Szamosújvár:** check local histories for their own festivals.
