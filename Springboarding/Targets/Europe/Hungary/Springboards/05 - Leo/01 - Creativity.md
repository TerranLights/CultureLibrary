# Hungary: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarians of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech); Romani and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; Romanian of Hungary; Rusyn; Yiddish where the record is pre-1944. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest design and gallery circuits against Szentendre's painters:** check how each scene recruits, sells and credits work.
- **Hódmezővásárhely and Mezőtúr potters against Korond:** compare who innovates within a pottery tradition and who polices it.
- **Pécs (Zsolnay ceramics, Csontváry, Vasarely) and Győr:** check whether factory, museum and city each claim the makers.
- **Kalotaszeg and the Mezőség against the Székely Land (Csík, Udvarhely):** separate village design rules from individual makers in craft studies.
- **Reformed congregations with painted coffered ceilings (*kazettás mennyezet*) against Catholic and Greek Catholic church art:** check who commissioned what, and how far variation was allowed.
- **Romungro musicians, Vlach Roma singers and Boyash woodworkers:** check where improvisation and inherited form are discussed in each group's own terms.
- **Danube Swabian blue-dyers, Slovak weavers of Békés, Croatian (Šokac) mask carvers of Mohács, Serbian icon painters of Szentendre:** read each nationality's craft studies on their own.
- **Rusyn and Romanian communities of Hungary:** craft literature is scarce; start with local museum catalogs.
- **Neolog Jewish architects and artists in Budapest; Armenian-descended Transylvanian families:** check who commissioned work and how it is labeled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fine art, folk craft and science history; mixed for everyday making; thin for Rusyn craft and Roma visual art outside Budapest.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for Transylvanian museum and craft records; German for Swabian workshop histories; Latin for medieval workshop sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims about "Hungarian inventions" mix fact and national pride, so check each case; labels such as "naive" or "Gypsy art" are contested by the makers.
- **Held for review:** who owns a design goes to Taurus (ownership); patent and copyright law to Libra. No creativity pointer was set aside on other grounds.
