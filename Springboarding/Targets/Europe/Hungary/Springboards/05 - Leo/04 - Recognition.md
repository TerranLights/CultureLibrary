## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest's national honors against town honorary citizenships in Miskolc, Eger and Pécs:** check who nominates and which deeds are named.
- **The Great Plain market towns (Kecskemét, Nagykőrös, Hódmezővásárhely):** compare local hall-of-fame plaques with county press tributes.
- **Transdanubia (Sopron, Győr, the Ormánság):** separate church and civic recognition in parish and town records.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Székelyudvarhely) and Kolozsvár:** compare honors given by Hungarian bodies with those from Romanian institutions.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check which Roma figures are honored, by whom, and whether each group's own leaders appear.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary, Rusyns:** list each nationality self-government's own prizes; Rusyn records will be few.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities:** compare which names each puts on its memorials.
- **Unitarians and Greek Catholics:** check church-given titles and honors for lay service.
- **Women in science and the arts:** count women in MTA and prize lists by decade.
- **Housework, farm and care work:** check where unpaid work enters any recognition record at all.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors lists, graves and memorials; mixed for workplace praise; thin for recognition inside Rusyn and Beash communities.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for armorial grants; Romanian for Kolozsvár and Székely coverage; German for *Stolpersteine* and Swabian honors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Holocaust and Roma remembrance, the removal of names after 1989 and 2012, and Székely autonomy claims are contested; quote each side as a claim.
- **Held for review:** naming law and the legal status of nationalities go to Libra; apologies as legal acts to Libra too; no other recognition pointer was set aside.
