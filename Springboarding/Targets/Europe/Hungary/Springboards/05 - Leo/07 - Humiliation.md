## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Great Plain market towns (Kecskemét, Cegléd, Szeged):** check court and town records for how public shaming was staged.
- **Palóc villages and the Jászság:** compare nickname and mocking-rhyme collections for who is targeted.
- **Transdanubia (Mohács, Pécs, Sopron) and the Ormánság:** check how defeat sites and lost villages are remembered locally.
- **Székely Land (Marosvásárhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy) and Kolozsvár:** compare accounts of minority slights in public offices and schools.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate group experiences of police, school and service encounters; Boyash records are thin.
- **Danube Swabians (expulsion, 1946–48) and Hungarians moved from southern Slovakia:** check memoirs for lists, labels and forced departures.
- **Slovaks, Croats (Bunjevac, Šokac), Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** check whether name-Magyarization pressure appears in family memories.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare forced labor service and ghetto testimonies for degradation recorded.
- **Reformed and Lutheran pastors under socialism; Unitarians and Greek Catholics after 1948:** check church histories for forced public recantations.
- **Conscripts by decade and branch:** separate 1950s, Kádár-era and 1990s accounts.
