# Hungary: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, and the Hungarians of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely dialects); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian incl. Bunjevac and Šokac; Serbian; Romanian (Hungary's community); Rusyn; Yiddish for Hasidic and community records. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest party and business elites against Great Plain market-town notables (Kecskemét, Hódmezővásárhely):** check how local leaders are described in town histories.
- **Jász, Kun and Hajdú towns:** compare captains and town councils in privileged districts with ordinary counties.
- **Northern Hungary (Miskolc, Ózd) and Szatmár:** check how *kiskirály* mayors are reported in local press.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Székelyudvarhely) and Kolozsvár:** compare church, party and civic leaders' roles.
- **Kalotaszeg and the Mezőség:** check where pastors acted as village leaders.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate *vajda*, elders and elected minority self-government leaders; avoid treating one model as Roma-wide.
- **Danube Swabian village judges, Slovak *richtár*, Croatian and Serbian parish leaders, Romanian Orthodox priests in Gyula, Rusyn Greek Catholic clergy:** read each in its own records.
- **Neolog *parnes*, Orthodox rabbis and Hasidic rebbes:** compare how authority rested on election, learning or descent.
- **Catholic bishops, Reformed superintendents, Lutheran and Unitarian bishops:** compare how each church chooses leaders.
- **Women leaders in villages and cities:** check where informal leadership appears in ethnographies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, religious and 1956 leadership; mixed for workplace leadership; thin for Rusyn and Beash leaders.
- **Languages needed:** Yiddish and Hebrew for Hasidic sources; Romanian for RMDSZ and 1989 coverage; Latin for early *vajda* records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current political leadership is polarized; personality-cult studies and Roma "chief" labels can be misused; 1989 Temesvár accounts are contested.
- **Held for review:** formal authority and the right to command go to Capricorn; election law to Libra; nothing else on leadership was put aside.
