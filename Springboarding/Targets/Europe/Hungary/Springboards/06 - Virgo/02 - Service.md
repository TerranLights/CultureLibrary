# Hungary: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (with older courtesy formulas and Székely forms); Beash and Lovari Romani; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian, Bunjevac and Šokac usage; Serbian; Hungary's Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest hotels and cafés against Balaton summer resorts:** check how seasonal service staff describe guests.
- **Great Plain estates and *tanya* farms (Hortobágy, Szeged's hinterland):** compare manor servants' memoirs with farmhands' songs.
- **Palóc and Eger villages, Tokaj vineyards:** look at day laborers and vintners serving landowners.
- **Hajdú towns and the Jászság:** check how privileged service shaped town identity.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and the Székely Land:** compare church, civic and military service records in Hungarian and Romanian.
- **Romungro musicians, Vlach Roma traders, Boyash woodworkers:** check which groups served patrons and on what terms, in their own accounts.
- **Swabian and Slovak servant girls, Croatian and Serbian parish lay workers, Rusyn Greek Catholic cantors:** read each group's service records separately.
- **Neolog and Orthodox communities:** compare the *shames* and community welfare roles; labor-service memoirs need their own reading.
- **Catholic sisters, Reformed and Lutheran deaconesses, Unitarian lay elders:** check how each church frames service.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic service, military and labor-service history; mixed for customer service; thin for Rusyn and Beash service roles and for Armenian-descended community service, where parish records are the first door.
- **Languages needed:** German for Swabian servant studies; Romanian for Transylvanian Caritas and church records; Yiddish and Hebrew for community welfare.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the forced labor service and its survivors; servants' abuse; Roma musicians as entertainers for hire.
- **Held for review:** the rules on servants and on civil service go to Libra; the duty of national service to Capricorn; nothing else on service was set aside.
