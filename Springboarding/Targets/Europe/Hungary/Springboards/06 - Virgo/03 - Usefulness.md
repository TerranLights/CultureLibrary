## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest pensioners against small-village elders in the Ormánság and Zemplén:** check where the old still have tasks and where they are left alone.
- **Great Plain *tanya* families (Kiskunság, Szeged's farms):** compare children's farm tasks before and after collectivization.
- **Transdanubian villages (Őrség, Göcsej):** check reuse and thrift in house inventories.
- **Northern Hungary's former industrial towns (Ózd, Salgótarján):** look at how laid-off men speak of being useful.
- **Székely Land and the Mezőség:** compare elders' roles where the young have emigrated.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check each group's own accounts of children's and elders' tasks.
- **Danube Swabian and Slovak farms:** compare the elders' retained share across nationalities; for Rusyn and Romanian families, start with local museum and self-government holdings.
- **Jewish old-age homes before and after 1944, Neolog and Orthodox:** check how each community cared for the old.
- **Disabled people and their families, with Deaf and blind communities apart:** read each through its own associations.
