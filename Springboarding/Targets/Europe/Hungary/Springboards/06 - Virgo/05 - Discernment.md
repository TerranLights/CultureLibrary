# Hungary: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (with market, wine-hill and Székely fair vocabulary); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian of the Bunjevac and Šokac; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest wine bars against Tokaj, Eger and Villány growers:** check whose taste counts as expert.
- **Great Plain horse and cattle markets (Hortobágy, Kiskunság, Szeged):** compare buyers' tests in market ethnographies.
- **Transdanubia (Sopron, Pécs, the Őrség):** look at how strangers and newcomers are sized up in village studies.
- **Palóc and Szatmár villages:** check matchmaking and suitor-judging customs.
- **Székely Land and Kalotaszeg:** compare fair trading with church counsel on choosing a spouse.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** look for each group's own tales and sayings about trust and deception; avoid outsiders' "character sketches".
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats and Serbs:** check how each community judged mixed marriages; Romanian and Rusyn sources are fewer, so start with their nationality institutes.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jews:** compare rabbinic guidance on choosing a match and a business partner.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian churches:** compare teaching on conscience and spiritual discernment.
