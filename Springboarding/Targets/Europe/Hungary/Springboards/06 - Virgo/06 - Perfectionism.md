# Hungary: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (craft and school vocabulary, with regional idioms); Lovari Romani and Beash; German (Swabian and standard); Slovak; Croatian incl. Bunjevac and Šokac; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew for ritual exactness. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest elite schools and conservatories against small-town schools in Békés and Baranya:** check how grade pressure is reported.
- **Kiskunhalas lace-makers and Kalocsa embroiderers on the Great Plain:** compare craft standards and judging.
- **Győr and Kecskemét car-plant workers:** check how factory quality rules sit beside home habits.
- **Herend, Pécs and the Balaton potteries in Transdanubia:** compare factory and workshop standards.
- **Eger and the Palóc villages:** look at church embroidery and costume care for feast days.
- **Székely Land and Kalotaszeg:** compare carvers' and painters' standards; check makers' own accounts.
- **Romungro musicians and Boyash woodcarvers:** check how each group judges a finished performance or object.
- **Swabian, Slovak, Croatian and Serbian church choirs and costume groups:** compare how each prepares for festival performance; Romanian and Rusyn groups are fewer, so start with their cultural associations.
- **Orthodox, Neolog and Status Quo congregations:** compare ritual exactness.
- **Catholic, Reformed and Unitarian clergy:** check how each treats scrupulosity.
