# Hungary: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (medical, folk-healing and spa vocabularies, with Székely and Palóc words for ailments); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian, including Bunjevac and Šokac; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the northeast (Borsod, Szabolcs-Szatmár-Bereg):** check life-expectancy and access gaps by district.
- **Great Plain spa towns (Hajdúszoboszló, Gyula) and Transdanubian ones (Hévíz, Zalakaros):** compare who uses medicinal water and why.
- **Ormánság and Zala villages:** look at folk-healing records in small, aging communities.
- **Palóc and Eger districts:** check church cures and pilgrimages for healing.
- **Székely Land (Kovászna, Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely) and the Mezőség:** compare spring and gas-bath use with village healers.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** look at each group's own healing practices and its access to clinics, kept apart.
- **Swabian, Slovak, Croatian and Serbian parishes:** compare herb blessings and saints' cures; Romanian and Rusyn customs need their own church sources.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** check communal care for the sick before and after 1944.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Unitarian and Greek Catholic believers:** compare prayers and rites for the sick.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official health statistics and folk medicine; mixed for mental health; thin for Rusyn and Beash healing and for Armenian-descended families.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Weszprémi; Romanian for Transylvanian spa records; Romani and Beash for healers' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma health data and segregation; psychiatric records; suicide and alcohol data at district level.
- **Held for review:** health-care law and compulsory vaccination rules go to Libra; nothing more on health was set aside.
