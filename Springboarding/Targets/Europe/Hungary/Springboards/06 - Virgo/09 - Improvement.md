# Hungary: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Reform-era, socialist and present-day registers; Great Plain floodplain words); Romani and Beash; German as used by the Danube Swabians; Slovak; Croatian with Bunjevac and Šokac speech; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Jewish Enlightenment. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest and the new industrial towns (Dunaújváros, Kazincbarcika) against old market towns of the Great Plain:** check how "progress" is remembered.
- **Szeged and the Tisza villages:** compare flood memories with pride in regulation.
- **Keszthely and the Balaton estates in Transdanubia:** look at estate improvement against peasant practice.
- **Sárköz and the Ormánság:** check floodplain loss beside the *egyke* (one-child) debate in the same villages.
- **Northern Hungary (Miskolc, Tokaj):** compare mining and vineyard improvement schemes.
- **Székely Land, Kolozsvár and Kalotaszeg:** compare Hungarian and Romanian development plans, and villagers' own building.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** read each group's response to programs aimed at them in its own voice.
- **Swabian, Slovak, Serbian and Croatian descendants of the 18th-century settlers:** check each group's settlement memory; Romanian and Rusyn villages need local-archive work.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare positions on reform and modernization.
- **Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran and Unitarian schools:** check how each linked faith and betterment.

## 4. Flags
