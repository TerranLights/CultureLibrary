## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the Great Plain market towns (Kecskemét, Szeged, the Jászság, Kiskunság):** check whether bargains go through notaries or through the fair and the drink.
- **Transdanubia (Sopron, Győr, the Őrség and Göcsej):** compare marriage-settlement deeds of wine-growing burghers and small farmers.
- **Northern Hungary and the Palóc lands, the Nyírség and Szatmár:** look at betrothal gifts and wedding order village by village.
- **Transylvanian Hungarians (Kolozsvár, Kalotaszeg, the Mezőség) and the Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy):** read the Principality's codes and Székely seat records apart from the kingdom's law.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate the three; check each group's own writers before outsiders' reports.
- **Swabian villages of Baranya and Tolna, Békés Slovaks, Bunjevac and Šokac Croats, Serbs of Battonya, Romanians of Méhkerék, Rusyns of Komlóska:** compare wedding order and family contracts from each community's own press.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities:** check marriage contracts and religious courts by community.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Unitarian and Greek Catholic churches:** compare rules on mixed marriage and on ending a marriage.
