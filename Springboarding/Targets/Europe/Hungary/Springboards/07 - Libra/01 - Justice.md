## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest courts against county seats (Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs, Miskolc):** check case loads and how the press reports verdicts in each.
- **Great Plain outlaw country (the Hortobágy, the Kiskunság):** compare court records of the *betyár* with the ballads about them.
- **The Partium, Kolozsvár and the Székely seats:** treat the Várad ordeals, Székely seat judgments and Romanian-era trials of Hungarians as three separate runs.
- **Northern Hungary (Miskolc, the Palóc villages) and Szatmár:** look at village justice and the gendarmerie in interwar files.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate the *kris* (a Vlach Roma body) from what other groups use; check Roma legal-aid files against police data.
- **Danube Swabians and Hungary's Serbs:** check the collective punishments and resettlements of 1945–48 in each group's own records.
- **Jewish communities (Neolog, Orthodox, Status Quo):** compare the 1945–48 tribunal files with survivors' testimony and the rabbinical courts' records.
- **Reformed and Catholic pulpits (Debrecen, Eger):** compare sermons on divine judgment and mercy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for legal history and post-1945 political trials; mixed for everyday justice; thin for Rusyn and Beash practice and for justice inside Armenian-descended families.
- **Languages needed:** Latin to read the ordeal register; Romanian for Securitate-era trial files in Transylvania; German for Swabian expellees' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1945 and 1956 verdicts read differently by each side; the 2008–09 killings of Roma and their trial; the Holocaust; state-security files open only under rules.
- **Held for review:** trust in the courts and obedience to law go to Capricorn; no justice pointer was dropped under the other rules.
