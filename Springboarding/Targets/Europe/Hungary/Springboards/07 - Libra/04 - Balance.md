## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest office workers and the shift towns (Győr's car plants, Kecskemét):** check hours, overtime and rest in labor data for each.
- **Lake Balaton's shore villages and the Kis-Balaton marsh:** compare how locals, anglers and planners talk of the lake being "in balance".
- **Szeged and the South, the Hortobágy:** look at river and grassland management and who wins when farming and conservation clash.
- **Székely Land and Kalotaszeg:** check symmetry in carved gates and textiles, and in church calendars of fasting.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate how each group balances Romani or Beash with Hungarian at home and school.
- **Slovaks, Danube Swabians, Croats, Serbs, Romanians and Rusyns:** compare dual-identity census answers and fasting calendars by faith.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations:** check where each placed itself in the 1868–69 Congress records.
- **Catholics, Reformed, Lutherans, Unitarians and Greek Catholics:** compare teaching on moderation in drink and feast.
