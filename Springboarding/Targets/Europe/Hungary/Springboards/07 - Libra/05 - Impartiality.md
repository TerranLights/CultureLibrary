*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (legal, sporting and office vocabulary); Ottoman Turkish for the occupation era; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest ministries against small Borsod and Szabolcs villages:** check how hiring and benefits complaints differ between the capital and places where everyone is kin.
- **Pécs, Szeged and Debrecen university towns:** compare admission complaints before and after the 2005 exam reform.
- **Transdanubian towns (Sopron, Zalaegerszeg) and the Kiskunság:** look at local procurement cases in K-Monitor by county.
- **Kolozsvár, Marosvásárhely and Csíkszereda:** check discrimination complaints and court-language cases in Romanian records.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate complaints by group and county; check the minority self-government elections for each.
- **Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Germans, Romanians, Rusyns:** compare how often each community uses its language before courts and offices.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** check which disputes each takes to a rabbinical court and which to the state.
- **Reformed, Catholic and Unitarian church courts in Transylvania:** compare clergy discipline and marriage cases.
