# Hungary: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (village, kitchen and office speech); Latin for late medieval sermons; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest housing estates against Great Plain farmsteads (*tanyák*) near Kecskemét:** check how far neighbors still send a taste plate or lend tools.
- **Transdanubian cellar hills (Villány, Badacsony):** compare cellar hospitality and the visits it obliges.
- **Palóc villages and Northern Hungary (Eger, Tokaj):** look at winter singing rounds and who must pay the singers.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy) and Kalotaszeg:** check wedding-gift lists and shared labor debts in village studies.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate exchange customs in each group, and read Roma authors before outsiders.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks of the Nyírség (*tirpák*), Croats and Serbs:** compare thanks formulas and feast-day visits by community.
- **Romanians of Gyula and Rusyns of Komlóska:** look for Orthodox and Greek Catholic alms customs; sources are scarce.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** compare charity and loan funds town by town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk custom and proverbs; mixed for present-day favors; thin for Rusyn and Beash exchange and for Armenian-descended families' gift customs.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Pelbárt; Romani and Beash for Roma accounts; Romanian for Transylvanian village gift records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gratitude money in hospitals is a live dispute; Roma–peasant exchange risks one-sided sources; keep each claim with its author.
- **Held for review:** revenge belongs to Scorpio; charity as generosity was scouted under Leo; nothing else on give-and-take was held back.
