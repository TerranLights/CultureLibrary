# Hungary: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (church, family and political speech); Latin and Ottoman Turkish for the early modern peaces; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest intelligentsia against factory towns (Csepel, Dunaújváros):** check memoirs and oral history for who went along after 1956 and who refused.
- **Hódmezővásárhely and the southern Great Plain:** look for Nazarene communities in parish and court records.
- **Transdanubian villages (Pécs region, the Ormánság):** compare church-recorded quarrels settled out of court.
- **Kolozsvár, Marosvásárhely and Csíkszereda:** check what CNSAS files and memoirs show about bending under Ceaușescu, case by case.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash families:** check who keeps family quarrels inside and who goes to outside help, using Roma-led studies only.
- **Danube Swabian, Slovak and Serbian villages after 1945:** compare silences about expulsion, exchange and the Yugoslav border years.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo memory:** compare how each community speaks of 1944 leadership choices; keep claims attributed.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian churches:** compare their agreements with the socialist state and their critics inside.
