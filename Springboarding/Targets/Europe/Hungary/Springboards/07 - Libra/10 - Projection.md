*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (psychological, church and political vocabulary); German for the Budapest school of psychoanalysis; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest and the provincial towns of the east (Debrecen, Nyíregyháza):** check whether xenophobia and conspiracy scores differ by settlement size.
- **Szeged and the South:** compare the 1728 trials with other Great Plain witch cases and with how the town tells the story now.
- **Northern Hungary (Miskolc, Eger) and the Palóc lands:** look at *kiszehajtás* and other expelling rites village by village.
- **Transylvanian Hungarians (Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad) and the Székely Land:** set Hungarian and Romanian school portrayals side by side.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check how each group is named in crime reporting, and read Roma media's answers.
- **Danube Swabians and Slovaks:** compare how the postwar expulsion and exchange were blamed on each group in the press of 1945–48.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** check how antisemitic blame is recorded in each community's press, and its rabbis' responses.
- **Catholic, Reformed and Unitarian clergy:** compare sermons on judging others and on confession.

## 4. Flags

- **Sensitive or restricted:** antisemitism, anti-Roma talk and migration campaigns are live; quote no slur beyond the search term; present each side as a claim.
