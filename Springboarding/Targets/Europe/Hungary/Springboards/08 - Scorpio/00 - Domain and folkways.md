## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest, Szeged, Pécs and Miskolc against villages:** check where the home wake survives and where funeral firms took over.
- **Reformed Debrecen, the Nyírség and Szatmár against Catholic Transdanubia and the Palóc lands, with Unitarian, Lutheran and Greek Catholic parishes:** compare grave markers, burial orders and days for the dead.
- **The Ormánság and the Sárköz:** check local studies on how heirs shaped family size.
- **Jász and Kun towns (Jászberény, Karcag, Kiskunhalas):** look for traces of the 1745 *redemptio* in how land passed to heirs.
- **Transylvanian Hungarians (Kolozsvár, Kalotaszeg, the Mezőség, the Partium) and the Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Marosvásárhely):** read wake songs, grave posts and common forests separately.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities:** compare burial-society rules, with Holocaust remembrance as its own layer.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate memorial meals and what may be said of the dead.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Šokac and Bunjevac Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary, Rusyns:** check each community's funeral rite in its own press.
- **Armenian-descended Hungarians (Szamosújvár, Erzsébetváros):** check Armenian Catholic burial records; likely thin.

- **Languages needed:** Latin and older Hungarian for early sermons; German for Swabian parish books; Romanian for Transylvanian civil registers after 1920; Hebrew and Yiddish for burial-society minutes.
