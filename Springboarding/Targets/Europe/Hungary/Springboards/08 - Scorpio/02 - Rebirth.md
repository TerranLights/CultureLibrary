## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest's charismatic congregations against Easter rites in the Palóc villages and in Göcsej:** check sudden conversion against the yearly rising of the liturgy.
- **Reformed Debrecen and the Nyírség against Lutheran Sopron and Győr:** compare how each church received its revival movements.
- **Unitarians of Kolozsvár and Székelykeresztúr, Catholics of Csík:** look at confirmation and the Pentecost pilgrimage as entries to a new life.
- **Szeged and the southern Great Plain:** check the rebuilding story against towns that were never rebuilt.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Chabad-linked congregations:** compare who returns to observance after 1989 and how each welcomes them.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate conversion, sobriety and language revival rather than folding them into one story.
- **Rusyns, Swabians, Békés Slovaks and Croats:** check minority revivals after 1990 one by one.
- **Jász and Kun towns:** check the Jász world meetings (*check*) as a heritage revival.
