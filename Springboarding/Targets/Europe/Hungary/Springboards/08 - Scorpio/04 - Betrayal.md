## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The kuruc northeast (Sárospatak, Tokaj, Szatmár) against Sopron and Győr in the west:** check how each region tells the kuruc–labanc split.
- **Budapest literary circles against Pécs and Debrecen:** compare how informer revelations were received in smaller towns.
- **Kolozsvár, Marosvásárhely and Székely villages:** read the Securitate-file debates on their own terms, apart from the Budapest files.
- **Swabian villages of Baranya and the Buda hills:** look at families split between the two loyalty movements.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** check how each community recalls its 1944 leaders' choices.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** ask each group's own writers what counts as betraying the group.
- **Catholics, Reformed, Lutherans and Unitarians:** compare church teaching on adultery and remarriage.
- **Palóc villages and the Great Plain towns:** compare divorce suits and jealousy songs region by region.
