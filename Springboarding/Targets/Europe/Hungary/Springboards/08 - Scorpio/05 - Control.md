## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Csepel and Angyalföld factory districts against Great Plain cooperatives:** compare how norms and inspectors reached workers in each.
- **The Tokaj, Eger and Sopron wine regions:** check hail rites and chapels village by village.
- **Balaton shore towns against Tisza floodplain villages:** check whose land the water managers favor.
- **Kolozsvár, the Mezőség and the Székely Land:** read control under Ceaușescu on its own terms, beside Kádár-era Hungary.
- **Reformed Debrecen and Sárospatak against Catholic Eger and Győr:** compare teaching on providence and human effort.
- **Jewish communities after 1950:** check how the state-supervised national bodies of the 1950s governed Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** look in each group's own writing at police and council control of settlements.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians and Rusyns:** check how the party-run nationality federations steered minority life after 1949.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state control from 1945 to 1989 and for water management; mixed for control inside families; thin for Boyash and Rusyn experience of it.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Sárospatak statutes; Romanian for Transylvanian rationing records; German for Swabian village minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance of living journalists; partner violence; personal files from 1957–89 inspection bodies.
