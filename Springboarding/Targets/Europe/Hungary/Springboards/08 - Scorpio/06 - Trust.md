## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against small towns of the Great Plain and the Ormánság:** compare trust in strangers with trust kept inside kin.
- **Zemplén, Szabolcs and the Tokaj area:** check whether the 1831 rumors are still part of local memory.
- **Kolozsvár against the Székely Land and Nagyvárad:** look at which institutions each community trusts more.
- **Catholics, Reformed, Lutherans, Unitarians and Greek Catholics:** check confidence in one's own church by denomination.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** check trust in the state after 1944 and during restitution.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare trust in schools, doctors and councils, group by group.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians and Rusyns:** check trust in their own self-governments against national bodies.
- **Jász and Kun towns:** look at whether old self-government left a habit of trusting local institutions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional trust (surveys since 2004) and fraud cases; mixed for trust between neighbors; thin for Rusyn and Armenian-descended communities.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Kis-Viczay; German for cooperative books; Romanian for Transylvanian survey reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** village memories of Jewish neighbors; the 2006 riots; fraud victims' names.
