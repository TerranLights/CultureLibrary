# Hungary: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain, Székely usage); Romani (Lovari), Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the villages of the Őrség and Göcsej:** check how much a private life can stay private in each.
- **Székely Sabbatarians, Unitarians and Csík Catholics:** compare a hidden faith with openly kept ones in the same land.
- **Catholics against Reformed and Lutherans:** compare private confession with general confession and what each does with a secret.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo families:** check how silence about origins differed after 1945.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare language as privacy, group by group.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Rusyns and Greek Catholics:** check identities kept quiet in the census and at work, then voiced after 1990.
- **Budapest against Debrecen, Miskolc and Szeged:** compare coming-out accounts by town size.
- **Armenian-descended families of Szamosújvár and Erzsébetváros:** check how descent was recorded, kept or lost.
- **Kolozsvár and Marosvásárhely:** check what Hungarians kept from Romanian authorities, and from each other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy and secret societies; mixed for family secrets; thin for Sabbatarian, Rusyn and Armenian-descended records.
- **Languages needed:** Hebrew and old Hungarian for Sabbatarian texts; German for Swabian memoirs; Romani and Beash for the families' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime rape survivors; people in police registers; adoptees and birth parents; hidden identities of living people.
- **Held for review:** the legal side of state secrets and adoption records goes to Libra; nothing on secrecy itself was set aside.
