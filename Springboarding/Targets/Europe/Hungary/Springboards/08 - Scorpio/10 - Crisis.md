# Hungary: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain, Székely usage); Romani (Lovari), Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish; Latin for medieval laments. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, a family, a community or a country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the northeast (Borsod, Szabolcs, Szatmár):** compare how the 1990s and 2008 crises hit each, and who left.
- **The Kiskunság and the sand ridge against the Tisza valley:** compare slow drought with sudden floods.
- **Szatmár's flood villages and their Transylvanian neighbors:** check how each side rebuilt after 1970.
- **Transylvanian Hungarians and the Székely Land:** read crisis memory (1920, 1940–44, 1989) on its own terms, with Romanian accounts as comparison only.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian clergy:** check sermons that explain disasters as test, punishment or call.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** compare memory of 1944 and of 1956 emigration.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare how each met the 2008–09 killings and the debt crisis.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians and Rusyns:** check which crises each community counts as its own turning points.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national crises and economic data; mixed for household coping; thin for Beash and Rusyn experience of recent crises.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Rogerius and Szerémi; Romanian for the 1970 floods across the border; Romani for community accounts of 2008–09.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1920 treaty and its memory, where claims clash; the Roma killings and their victims' families; evicted families' names.
- **Held for review:** nothing on crisis was set aside; the legal side of eviction moratoria goes to Libra.
