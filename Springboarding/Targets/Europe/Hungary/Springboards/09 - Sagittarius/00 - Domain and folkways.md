## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Great Plain villages and the Ormánság:** compare census non-answers and Sunday attendance before drawing any line on secularity.
- **Reformed Debrecen, the Nyírség and Szatmár against Catholic Szeged, Eger and the Palóc lands, with Lutheran Sopron and Békéscsaba:** set church order (presbyterian, episcopal) beside schools and calendars.
- **Unitarians of Kolozsvár, the Homoród valley and Budapest:** check how sermons and school histories retell the 1568 Torda edict.
- **Greek Catholics of Hajdúdorog and Máriapócs, with Rusyn and Romanian roots:** trace the liturgical-language debates through parish records.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews, with the northeast's Hasidic courts before 1944 (Kálló, Sátoraljaújhely):** separate rite, schooling and remembrance.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare Catholic, Greek Catholic and Pentecostal ties in Roma-written sources.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Bunjevac and Šokac Croats, Serbs of Szentendre and Ráckeve, Romanians of Gyula, Rusyns:** check each church as the keeper of its language.
- **Székelys (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy) and the Jász and Kun heritage:** read origin traditions as each community tells them.
- **Armenian-descended Hungarians of Szamosújvár and Gyergyószentmiklós:** look for Armenian Catholic parish books; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
