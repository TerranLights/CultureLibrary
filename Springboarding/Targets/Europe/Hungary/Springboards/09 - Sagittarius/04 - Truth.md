*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest readers of national dailies against county-paper readers in Békés, Zala or Szabolcs:** check where people say they get trusted news.
- **Szeged and Pécs university towns against small towns:** compare trust in scientists and in the academy's word.
- **Transylvanian Hungarians of Kolozsvár and the Partium against Székelys of Csík and Háromszék:** look at which history books and which broadcasters each treats as reliable.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** separate how each judges state memorial projects.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare which family memories of 1944 entered archives, and which interviewer took them down.
- **Swabians of Tolna, Slovaks of Tótkomlós, Croats of Pécs, Serbs, Romanians of Gyula:** compare minority-language media as sources of truth about their own past.
- **Rusyns of Komlóska and Múcsony:** follow the naming dispute in their own publications.
- **Palóc villages and the Jászság:** collect proverbs on liars and truth-tellers, region by region.
