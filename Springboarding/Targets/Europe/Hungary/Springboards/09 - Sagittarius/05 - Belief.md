# Hungary: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain, Székely); Romani (Lovari), Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Šokac); Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew; Latin for the sixteenth-century disputations. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ormánság and Sárköz Reformed villages against Catholic Göcsej:** check which folk beliefs survived under which church's discipline.
- **Budapest's educated middle class against Great Plain farmsteads:** compare spiritism and modern belief with village wise women and seers.
- **The Partium (Nagyvárad, Szatmár) against Kolozsvár:** trace how Reformed and Unitarian memories of the disputations differ.
- **Székely Catholics of Csík against Reformed Háromszék:** set catechism teaching beside local legend.
- **Šokac Croats and Hungarians of Mohács:** compare explanations of the Busójárás as belief or as play, teller by teller.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations:** compare stated articles of faith with observance surveys.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate church belief from household belief, each in its own language.
- **Swabians of the Buda hills, Slovaks of Békés, Serbs, Romanians of Méhkerék and Rusyns:** look for folk-belief archives in their own institutes.
- **Armenian-descended Hungarians:** check whether parish records keep any distinct devotions; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief (large archives) and confessional history; mixed for present-day private belief; thin for Rusyn and Armenian-descended sources.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the disputations; German for Buda-hills collections; Slovak; Beash and Romani; Hebrew for rabbinic writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** neo-pagan origin claims (contested); clergy collaboration files; ridicule of Roma or rural belief in older ethnography.
- **Held for review:** religious-education rules as law go to Libra; witchcraft trials stay out of this sheet as court material; no other belief pointer was held back.
