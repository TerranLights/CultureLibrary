## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagyvárad and Kolozsvár against the Székely towns:** compare who signed, printed or hid minority samizdat, and where it was safest to refuse.
- **Debrecen and Sárospatak Reformed tradition against Catholic Eger and Esztergom:** check how each church presents its martyrs and confessors.
- **Budapest intellectuals against Győr, Miskolc and Szeged workers in 1956:** compare stands taken by writers and by workers' councils.
- **Danube Swabians of Baranya and Tolna:** separate village by village those who joined the Volksbund from those who refused.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo families, and Zionist youth movements of the 1940s:** look at what each held to when choosing to stay, leave or resist.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash activists:** compare stands taken through parties, churches and foundations.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses, Nazarenes and other small free churches:** check how each frames refusal of arms in its own literature.
- **Slovaks of Békés, Serbs of Szentendre, Croats of the Drava, Romanians of Gyula, Rusyns:** look for local figures who held to their language when it cost them.
