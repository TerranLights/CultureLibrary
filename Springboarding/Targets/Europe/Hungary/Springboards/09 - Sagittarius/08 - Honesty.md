# Hungary: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain, Székely); Romani (Lovari), Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish; sixteenth-century Hungarian for the fables. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Debrecen and Hortobágy fairs against Transdanubian markets of Győr and Pécs:** compare how a bargain was sealed and what counted as cheating.
- **Budapest office workers against small-town craftsmen and builders:** check attitudes to working without invoices in each group's own words.
- **Kolozsvár and Nagyvárad Hungarians against the Székely Land:** set survey answers on bribery beside local press reports.
- **Reformed, Catholic and Lutheran congregations:** look for sermons and church papers that treat lying and tax honesty.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** check their ethical teaching on fair weights and honest trade in their own publications.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare Roma-authored accounts of dealing with shops, banks and offices with outsiders' accounts.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians and Rusyns:** look for proverbs and village rules on keeping one's word, each in its own language.
- **The Jászság and Kunság market towns and the Palóc lands:** test regional tale repertoires for liar heroes against punished liars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys and press scandals; mixed for everyday honesty; thin for Rusyn and Beash lore.
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Hungarian; German for Swabian collections; Romani and Beash; Romanian for comparison with Transylvanian state data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** stereotypes linking any group to cheating (never repeat them as findings); named people in fraud cases; partisan corruption claims.
- **Held for review:** fraud and bribery offenses and receipt rules go to Libra; corruption as legal culture to Capricorn.
