## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against regional university towns (Debrecen, Szeged, Pécs, Miskolc, Eger):** compare who stays, who moves and who goes abroad after graduating.
- **Farmstead children of the Kiskunság and the Hortobágy against city children:** trace distance to school as a factor in reaching university.
- **The Ormánság and Borsod's small villages:** check first-generation graduates in local histories and tracking data.
- **Kolozsvár and Nagyvárad against the Székely Land:** compare Hungarian-language options at each level, as their institutions report them.
- **Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran and Unitarian colleges:** set church-run higher schooling side by side, with their own histories.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** look at yeshiva learning, the rabbinical seminary and the 1920s student exodus abroad as separate paths.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash students:** read their own accounts in special-college publications.
- **Swabian, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian and Rusyn communities:** check minority-school graduates' paths into Hungarian and kin-state universities.
