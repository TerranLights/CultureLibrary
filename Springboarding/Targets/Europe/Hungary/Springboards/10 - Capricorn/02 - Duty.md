# Hungary: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Great Plain, Palóc and Székely usage); Latin for noble levies; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Great Plain farmsteads (the Kiskunság, the Hortobágy) against Transdanubian villages (the Őrség, Göcsej):** check which duties of help fall on neighbors where houses stand far apart.
- **Palóc and Székely villages:** compare which keep vowed holidays and what they owe for them.
- **Budapest against small towns:** test filial duty where children live far from parents.
- **Hungarians of Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad, Kalotaszeg and the Mezőség:** check the weight of church dues and school support where Hungarians are a minority.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Székelyudvarhely):** compare communal work and church dues with the Transylvanian towns.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** separate each group's own words for what is owed to kin and to the dead.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary, Rusyns:** check each church's teaching on duty in its own language.
- **Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran and Unitarian congregations:** compare catechisms on duty to parents and rulers.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare charity and burial duties across community types.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and village-vow history; mixed for filial duty; thin for Roma veterans and Rusyn practice.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for levy records; German for Swabian chronicles; Hebrew and Yiddish for communal minute books; Romanian for Transylvanian tax and parish law (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1942 Újvidék killings and wartime service; Soviet deportations of 1944–45; the Jewish labor service of 1939–45; socialist work-avoidance files contain personal data.
- **Held for review:** jury and assessor rules as written go to Libra; no duty pointer was set aside under the other rules.
