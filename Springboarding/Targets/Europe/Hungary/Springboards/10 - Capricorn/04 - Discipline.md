## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Reformed Debrecen, Sárospatak and the Nyírség against Catholic Eger and the Palóc lands:** compare church-court and school records of punishment.
- **Transdanubian Swabian villages (Baranya, Tolna) against Great Plain Hungarian ones:** check how farm work schedules were set and enforced in each.
- **Budapest against Miskolc and Ózd:** compare factory discipline in old industrial towns with the capital's plants.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and Marosvásárhely colleges:** test how minority schools kept order when state rules changed.
- **Székely Land (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy):** check memories of strict schooling and military service in the Romanian army (comparison).
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash children:** compare school-exclusion data by group, and what each group's advocates report.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish schools before 1944:** compare the timetable and correction in each.
- **Slovaks, Serbs, Croats, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** look at nationality boarding schools and their house rules.
- **Lutheran Sopron and Bonyhád, Unitarian Kolozsvár:** check their schools' discipline codes against the larger churches'.
