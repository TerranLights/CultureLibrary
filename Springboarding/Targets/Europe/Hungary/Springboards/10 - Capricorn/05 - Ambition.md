## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Szabolcs, Borsod and the Ormánság:** compare what young people name as "making it", and how far they expect to move for it.
- **Szeged, Debrecen and Pécs university towns:** check graduate tracking for who stays and who leaves.
- **Győr and Kecskemét car-plant towns:** look at aspirations shaped by large foreign employers.
- **Kolozsvár and Nagyvárad against Csíkszereda and Székelyudvarhely:** compare where Transylvanian Hungarian graduates aim, and whether Hungary or the West draws them.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare how each group's own writers describe rising, and which trades or schools they name.
- **Danube Swabians:** read the land-buying debate beside Swabian village chronicles, which tell it from inside.
- **Neolog Budapest families and Orthodox northeastern ones before 1944:** compare the professions chosen for sons and daughters.
- **Slovaks of Békéscsaba and Tótkomlós, Croats of Baja, Serbs of Szentendre:** check whether rising meant leaving the nationality language.
- **Jász and Kun towns and Hajdú towns:** look for proud local histories of freeholders who bought land and offices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mobility and education data; mixed for envy and attitudes; thin for Beash and Rusyn aspirations.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Bonfini; German for Swabian sources; Romanian for Transylvanian labor-market data (comparison); Yiddish for pre-1944 family letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** name changes and the 1930s land debate touch antisemitic and anti-German framings; Kádár-era files on private traders; talk of Roma success can slide into exception stories.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category (one pointer set aside under the rule); no other ambition pointer was dropped.
