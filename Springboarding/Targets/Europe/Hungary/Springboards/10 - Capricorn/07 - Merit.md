*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest elite schools against county towns (Nyíregyháza, Kaposvár, Salgótarján):** compare who reaches gifted programs and competitions.
- **The Ormánság and the Cserehát against Győr and Székesfehérvár:** check whether hiring through acquaintances is reported more where jobs are scarce.
- **Kolozsvár and Marosvásárhely against Csíkszereda and Sepsiszentgyörgy:** compare exam results and university entry for Hungarian pupils in mixed and in Hungarian-majority towns.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash pupils:** check scholarship and competition data by group, and how Roma educators describe merit being seen or missed.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary:** check whether nationality-school pupils compete on equal terms in national exams.
- **Neolog and Orthodox Jewish families before 1944:** compare how scholarship and learning counted toward standing in each.
- **Reformed colleges (Debrecen, Sárospatak, Pápa) and Catholic orders' schools:** compare scholarship and prize traditions.
- **Székelys and the Jász and Kun towns:** read local histories of earned freedoms against inherited ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school, award and charter sources; mixed for hiring and connections; thin for Beash and Rusyn achievement data.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for charters; Romanian for exam data in Transylvania (comparison); German for Swabian school records; Hebrew and Yiddish for yeshiva honors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class-origin files of the 1950s; political appointments today; Roma pupils' results are easily misread without context.
- **Held for review:** affirmative action and admissions law as written go to Libra; no merit pointer was set aside under the other rules.
