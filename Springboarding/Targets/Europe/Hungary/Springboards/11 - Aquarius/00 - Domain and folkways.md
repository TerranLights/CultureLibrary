# Hungary: Community and ideals, the domain and its folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, read here with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land (a working reading the author still confirms; see Flags). **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain and Székely speech); Romani (Lovari, Romungro usage) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish; Latin for early records. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups and what they hope for, with the social ways of settlement, migration, hospitality and gathering.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest districts against Great Plain market towns and Ormánság villages:** compare association counts by settlement size before any one reading.
- **Transdanubia (Győr, Pécs, the Őrség, Göcsej) against Northern Hungary (Miskolc, Eger, the Palóc villages):** compare church-led and workplace-led gathering.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and the Partium, Kalotaszeg, the Mezőség:** check how Hungarian associations rebuilt after 1990 as a minority's own institutions.
- **The Székely seats (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Marosvásárhely):** check village self-help and how emigration is spoken of.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare their own associations and media, kept apart from outsiders' accounts.
- **Danube Swabians, Slovaks, Bunjevac and Šokac Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary, Rusyns:** compare festival calendars and associations.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jewish communities:** check how each built communal bodies and cultural circles.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Greek Catholic and Unitarian congregations:** compare parish societies and youth groups.
- **The Jász and Kun towns:** check whether the old district identity still shapes local associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-century associations and settlement; mixed for present aid; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended community life.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for confraternity records; German for Swabian club papers; Croatian for southern festivals; Yiddish and Hebrew for communal minutes.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries Romania as the one pre-war land, and no Stage 0 file exists, so the exact lands are still to be settled; this sign reads Hungary with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. The Romanian majority of Transylvania and the Csángó of Moldavia stay comparison only, as does the diaspora.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "us and them" talk touches Roma segregation, Trianon and 1944; aid framing is politically charged since 2015.
- **Held for review:** law on associations goes to Libra; no domain pointer on community, gathering or hope was set aside under the other rules.
