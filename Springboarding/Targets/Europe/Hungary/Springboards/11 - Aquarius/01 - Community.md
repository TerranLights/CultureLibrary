# Hungary: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (standard, Palóc and Székely usage); Latin for Árpád-era charters; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, a cause), know one another as members and owe one another something; and how that bond feels.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest housing estates, inner districts and agglomeration villages:** check where newcomer and old-resident lines run before generalizing.
- **Great Plain farmstead areas (Kiskunság, the Szeged region) against compact villages of the Jászság:** settlement shape decides who meets daily; compare.
- **The Őrség, Göcsej and Lake Balaton villages:** check how holiday-home owners and permanent residents count each other.
- **The Palóc villages and the Tokaj foothills:** compare parish and vineyard as the binding frame.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and the Partium against the Mezőség:** compare a city minority with scattered villages where Hungarians are few.
- **Székelyudvarhely, Csíkszereda and Sepsiszentgyörgy villages:** check how emigration and seasonal work abroad change village membership.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check each community's own meeting occasions and leaders.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare what each minority press reports as "our community".
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations; Reformed, Catholic, Lutheran, Greek Catholic and Unitarian parishes:** separate faith community from village community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for village sociography and minority press; mixed for present-day measures of local ties; thin for Beash and Rusyn community life.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for *hospes* charters; Slovak and Croatian for minority weeklies; Romanian for the Mezőség's state records (comparison).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma settlements as "separate communities" is contested ground; avoid outsiders' labels; Facebook groups hold private persons' posts, so cite only patterns.
- **Held for review:** no community source was set aside; association law belongs to Libra.
