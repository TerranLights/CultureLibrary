# Hungary: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (standard and Székely usage); Latin for 17th-century Transylvanian writing; German for Pest-born writers who wrote in it; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest students and young professionals against older small-town residents:** check whether ideals and disillusion sort by age or by place.
- **Szeged and Debrecen university towns against Miskolc and the North:** compare service-minded callings in different job markets.
- **Transdanubian Catholic regions (Zala, Vas, the Őrség) against the Reformed Tiszántúl:** check how each tradition frames a saintly calling.
- **Kolozsvár and Nagyvárad intellectuals; the Székely towns (Székelyudvarhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy):** compare idealized pasts and service to the community in a minority setting.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** look for each group's own reformers, teachers and activists; record their aims in their words.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs and Romanians of Hungary:** compare each one's ideal figures (teachers, priests, poets).
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare Zionist, assimilationist and religious ideals across the 20th century.
- **Unitarians of the Székely Land, Lutherans, Greek Catholics:** check which ideals each church holds up to its young.
- **Rusyns and the Jász and Kun towns:** thin; check local heroes and revival leaders.
