# Hungary: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (standard, Budapest youth slang); 16th-century Hungarian for Reformation plays; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest's inner districts against county towns:** check whether difference is tolerated more where it is anonymous.
- **Pécs and Szentendre (artists' towns) against Debrecen and Miskolc:** compare art scenes' room to differ.
- **The Great Plain farmsteads and the Ormánság:** check what is said of the village eccentric and the lone farmstead dweller.
- **Kolozsvár and Nagyvárad against the Székely seats (Sepsiszentgyörgy, Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely):** compare a mixed university city's subcultures with small-town Hungarian-majority life.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check how difference within each group is named, and avoid outsiders' labels that treat a whole people as "deviant".
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare community pressure in small minority villages.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** check who counts as leaving the fold, and how each community speaks of it.
- **Reformed, Lutheran, Catholic, Greek Catholic and Unitarian churches, and small free churches:** compare how each treats dissent within.
