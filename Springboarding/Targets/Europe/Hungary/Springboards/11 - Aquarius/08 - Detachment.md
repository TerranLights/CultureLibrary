# Hungary: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (standard and colloquial); Latin for Renaissance verse; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, one's own feelings or outcomes, valued in some forms (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and condemned in others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest commuters and office workers against village life in the Őrség and Göcsej:** check whether public reserve is an urban habit or wider.
- **The Great Plain (Hortobágy herdsmen, Kiskunság farmsteads) against Lake Balaton resort towns:** compare solitude as work and as leisure.
- **Northern Hungary's former industrial towns (Ózd, Miskolc):** check apathy and withdrawal after the factories closed.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and Marosvásárhely; Csíkszereda and Székelyudvarhely:** compare minority reticence in public with private warmth.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check display rules within each community in their own voices, and avoid reading outsiders' impressions as fact.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare silences around expulsion, exchange and assimilation.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare how survivors' composure and its cost are written about.
- **Catholic and Greek Catholic contemplatives; Reformed and Unitarian restraint in worship:** compare church teaching on calm.
- **Doctors, journalists, soldiers and police:** separate professional detachment from general manners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and household data; mixed for emotion research; thin for Beash, Rusyn and Swabian dialect sources.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Janus; German for Balint's and Swabian sources; Yiddish for survivor writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** survivor testimony and labor service; stereotypes of a "gloomy" or "reserved" people must be held as claims, never adopted.
- **Held for review:** contemplative traditions of one faith were left out (Buddhism, held as a category, an open question for the author); no detachment source was set aside under the South Asian rule.
