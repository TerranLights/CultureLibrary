*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (including 16th-century verse and Palóc and Székely usage); Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Danube Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Borsod, Szabolcs-Szatmár and the Ormánság:** check whether expectations follow wealth or local history.
- **The Great Plain market towns (Kecskemét, Szeged) and Transdanubia's western strip (Győr, Sopron):** compare hopes tied to industry and to the border.
- **The Nyírség and Szatmár:** check Greek Catholic, Reformed and Jewish hope traditions in the same landscape.
- **Kolozsvár, Nagyvárad and the Partium; the Székely seats (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Marosvásárhely):** compare hopes for staying against hopes of leaving for Hungary.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check aspirations by group; avoid reading low expectations as a trait.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare emigration, return and revival hopes.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** compare redemption, emigration and staying.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Greek Catholic and Unitarian believers:** check how each preaches hope at Easter and at funerals.
- **Young and old:** compare survey items by age before any national reading.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, letters and poetry; mixed for minority aspirations; thin for Beash and Rusyn.
- **Languages needed:** 16th-century Hungarian for Gergei; German and Slovak for emigrant letters; Hebrew and Yiddish for Hasidic sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1940 and its aftermath are contested ground; Roma aspiration studies risk deficit framing; present claims as claims.
- **Held for review:** nothing about hope or despair was set aside under the standing rules; New Year's customs that forecast the year are kept as folk calendar only.
