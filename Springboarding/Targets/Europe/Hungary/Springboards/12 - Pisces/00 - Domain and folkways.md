# Hungary: The hidden and transcendence, the domain and its folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, read for this sign with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land (a working reading the author still confirms; see Flags). **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (Palóc, Great Plain, Székely usage); Romani and Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian (Bunjevac, Šokac); Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish and Hebrew; Latin. **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, with its magic ways (the line between religion, magic and science; what people do about luck, harm and hidden forces).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against the Great Plain (Kiskunság, Jászság, Szeged and the South):** compare where distress goes, clinic or wise woman, by settlement type.
- **Transdanubia (the Őrség, Göcsej, the Ormánság):** check how small villages named and handled hidden harm.
- **Northern Hungary (Eger, Tokaj, the Palóc lands):** compare Palóc belief vocabulary with the standard forms.
- **Kolozsvár, Kalotaszeg and the Mezőség; the Székely seats (Csíkszereda, Székelyudvarhely, Sepsiszentgyörgy, Marosvásárhely):** check whether belief records were kept in Hungarian or Romanian archives, and by whom.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare dealings with the dead and with misfortune group by group.
- **Night beings by minority:** check what Swabian, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian and Rusyn villages name them, and in which language.
- **Where each church drew the line between prayer and magic:** compare Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran, Greek Catholic and Unitarian visitation records and sermons.
- **Amulets, the evil eye and the dead in Jewish sources:** check Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo writings separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief and psychiatry; mixed for present practice; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended Hungarians.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for trial and visitation records; Romani and Beash for the dead and misfortune; Yiddish and Hebrew for amulet texts.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled (Romania is the one pre-war land carried); this sign reads Hungary plus the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. Transylvania's Romanian majority, the Moldavian Csángó and the diaspora are comparison only.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "superstition" talk can be used against Roma and rural people; fringe origin and pagan-revival claims are contested; camp and asylum memory needs survivors' voices first.
- **Held for review:** order ways (custom keeping order) go to Capricorn, and law to Libra; no pointer on the unseen or on suffering was set aside under the other rules.
