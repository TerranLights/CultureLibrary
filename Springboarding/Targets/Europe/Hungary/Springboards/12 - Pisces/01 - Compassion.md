# Hungary: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (codex Hungarian for the works of mercy; Szeged and Székely usage); Romani and Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest's street outreach against market towns of the Great Plain:** compare who is given to at the church door and who is passed by.
- **Szeged and the South against Szabolcs-Szatmár and the Ormánság:** check whether pity of the poor reads differently where poverty is widespread.
- **Kolozsvár, Kalotaszeg and the Székely seats (Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy):** check church-run care in a minority setting, kept apart from Romanian-majority charity.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare help given within the family and help received from outsiders; avoid treating Roma only as recipients.
- **Orthodox Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Greek Catholic Rusyns:** check alms tied to memorial meals for the dead.
- **Swabian, Slovak and Croatian (Šokac, Bunjevac) parishes:** compare charity societies and harvest-time giving.
- **Catholic orders, Reformed and Lutheran diaconal work, Unitarian congregations:** compare which church does what, and to whom.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo Jews:** check how *tsdoke* societies were set up in each.
- **Hungarians of Armenian descent in Szamosújvár and Erzsébetváros:** sources are scarce; look for almsgiving in Armenian Catholic parish histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church charity and saints' cults; mixed for present giving; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended charity.
- **Languages needed:** codex Hungarian and Latin for the works of mercy; Yiddish for *tsdoke* records; Romani for mourning help.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** refugee help is politically charged since 2015; begging is often framed through Roma; present each claim as a claim.
- **Held for review:** mercy to offenders (pardon, amnesty) is law, so Libra and Capricorn; nothing on pity or alms was set aside under the other rules.
