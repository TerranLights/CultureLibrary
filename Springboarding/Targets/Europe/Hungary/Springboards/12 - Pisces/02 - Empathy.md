*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (with 17th-century Székely Franciscan usage); German for Budapest psychoanalysis; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest professionals (therapists, call-center staff) against Great Plain farm families (the Kiskunság, the Hortobágy):** check where "empathy" is a trained word and where older words stand in.
- **Transdanubian villages (Göcsej, the Őrség) and Pécs:** compare how school sensitization programs land in small and large schools.
- **The Palóc lands and Miskolc:** check whether dialect idioms for feeling-with survive beside the clinical term.
- **Székelyudvarhely, Csíkszereda and the Mezőség:** compare devotional training in co-suffering with present school programs.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** check how each group is asked to explain itself to majority audiences, and who speaks for whom.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare how expulsion and exchange memories ask neighbors to feel with them.
- **Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian preaching on the Passion:** check how each frames suffering with Christ.
- **Survivors' families in Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** check how 1944 testimony is taught and to whom.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychology and psychoanalysis; mixed for school programs; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended Hungarians.
- **Languages needed:** German for Ferenczi; 17th-century Hungarian and Latin for the Passion plays; Romani for Roma participants' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sensitization programs touch Roma segregation and 1944; disability simulations are criticized by disabled people; present claims as claims.
- **Held for review:** nothing on feeling-with was set aside under the standing rules; empathy in the courtroom belongs to Libra.
