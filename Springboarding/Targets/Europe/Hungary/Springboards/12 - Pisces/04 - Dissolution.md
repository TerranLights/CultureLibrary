## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest clubbers and Balaton festival crowds against village dance and pilgrimage crowds:** check which crowds people describe as melting together, and in what words.
- **The Ormánság, Göcsej and Zemplén:** compare villages that emptied with those that held, and how each tells its story.
- **The Jászság and Kunság (Karcag, Kiskunhalas):** check how the Kun and Jász heritage frames its own blending into the Hungarian majority.
- **The Mezőség, Kalotaszeg and the Székely seats:** compare scattered-minority settings with Hungarian-majority Csíkszereda and Székelyudvarhely.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare language shift and its meaning to each group.
- **Minority language shift by generation:** check Swabian, Slovak, Croatian, Serbian, Romanian and Rusyn speakers, and the revival efforts that answer it.
- **Szamosújvár and Erzsébetváros families of Armenian descent:** check how the loss of Armenian is recorded; sources are scarce.
- **Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities:** compare assimilation and the mystic's union as two kinds of losing the self.
- **Restrained and ecstatic worship:** compare styles inside Catholic, Reformed, Lutheran and Unitarian congregations.
