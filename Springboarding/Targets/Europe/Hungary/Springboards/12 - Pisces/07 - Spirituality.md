# Hungary: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (hymnal and folk-prayer Hungarian; Székely usage); medieval Latin; Church Slavonic and Serbian for Orthodox worship; Romani and Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Romanian; Rusyn; Hebrew and Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (faith and religion as institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest seekers and esoteric fairs against Great Plain pilgrim groups (Szeged, the Jászság):** check where private spirituality and group devotion meet.
- **The Palóc lands (Mátraverebély-Szentkút) and the Nyírség:** compare Roman and Greek Catholic shrine customs.
- **Transdanubia (Pécs, the Ormánság, Göcsej):** check Reformed and Catholic villages side by side.
- **The Székely seats and the Mezőség:** compare Csíksomlyó devotion with Unitarian and Reformed congregations nearby.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare Catholic, Pentecostal and other paths, each in its own words.
- **Serbian Orthodox, Romanian Orthodox and Rusyn Greek Catholic communities:** check monastery, icon and feast-day practice.
- **Swabians, Slovaks and Croats (Bunjevac, Šokac):** compare pilgrimages kept in the mother tongue.
- **Inward prayer against decorum in synagogue worship:** compare Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo congregations.
- **The Jász and Kun towns:** check the place of their patron saints and shrines in local identity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pilgrimage, hymnody and surveys; mixed for esotericism; thin for Beash, Rusyn and Armenian Catholic devotion.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Gerard; Church Slavonic and Serbian for Grábóc; Hebrew for prayer texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pagan-revival and sacred-landscape claims are contested; closed rites of any community are respected, not researched.
