# Hungary: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (herders' and vintners' trade vocabulary; Mezőség and Székely usage); Latin for the chronicles; German for Budapest psychoanalysis; Romani (Lovari) and Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest professionals against Hortobágy and Kiskunság herders:** compare whose hunches are trusted at work and why.
- **Tokaj and the Palóc lands around Eger:** check how vintners and village healers describe trained senses.
- **The Ormánság and the Őrség:** compare weather and harvest forecasting in small villages.
- **The Mezőség, Kalotaszeg and the Székely seats (Csíkszereda, Marosvásárhely):** check seers and forecasting kept in Hungarian, with Romanian neighbors as comparison only.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare each group's own tales and terms for knowing; keep outsiders' fortune-teller images apart.
- **Swabians, Slovaks, Croats and Serbs:** compare saints'-day forecasting in each language.
- **Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** check Orthodox and Greek Catholic dream and omen customs.
- **Hasidic, Neolog and Status Quo Jews:** compare holy men's foresight with rational religious teaching.
- **Catholic and Reformed preaching:** check how each church treated omens and inner promptings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk forecasting and science writing; mixed for psychology; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended Hungarians.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Anonymus; German for Ferenczi; Yiddish for Hasidic tales.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fortune-telling is often pinned on Roma women; "peasant sense" can carry anti-expert politics; seers' claims are recorded as belief.
- **Held for review:** intuition in court (judges, juries) belongs to Libra; no hunch or foresight pointer was set aside under the other rules.
