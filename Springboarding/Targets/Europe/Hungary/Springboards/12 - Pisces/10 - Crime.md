# Hungary: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hungary, region Europe, with the Hungarian communities of Transylvania and the Székely Land. **Language(s) for searching:** Hungarian (outlaw-ballad and underworld slang; Székely usage); Latin for the chronicles; Romani and Beash; Swabian German; Slovak; Croatian; Serbian; Romanian; Rusyn; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it (law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Budapest against Szabolcs-Szatmár and Borsod:** compare which wrongs local papers report and how they name the wrongdoer.
- **Szeged and the South against the Bakony and Somogy:** check how each outlaw tradition is celebrated or condemned today.
- **The Jászság and Kunság:** compare herdsmen's and farmers' tales of livestock theft.
- **Csíkszereda, Sepsiszentgyörgy and Marosvásárhely:** check how a Székely outlaw's fame is told at home and in Hungary.
- **Romungro, Vlach Roma and Boyash:** compare how each community speaks of wrongdoing within, against how outsiders label them.
- **Swabian and Croatian border villages:** check memories of smuggling and who was condemned for it.
- **Slovaks, Serbs, Romanians of Hungary and Rusyns:** compare the outlaw figures of each tradition (*zbojník*, *hajduk*, *haiduc*).
- **Memory of false accusations against Jews:** check how Neolog, Orthodox and Status Quo communities each keep it.
- **Catholic and Reformed preaching:** compare how sermons tied sin and crime.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, legends and famous cases; mixed for everyday tolerated wrongs; thin for Rusyn, Beash and Armenian-descended Hungarians.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the chronicle; Romani for community views; Slovak, Serbian and Romanian for outlaw comparisons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *cigánybűnözés*, 1946 and the 2008–09 killings of Roma are contested and painful; victims' families come first.
- **Held for review:** the law of crime (codes, courts, sentencing) goes to Libra and Capricorn; no pointer on wrongdoing or wrongdoers was set aside under the other rules.
