# Latvia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Yiddish for smaller and historical communities. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against rural Vidzeme, Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether public speaking norms in the capital's offices differ from farm and small-town meetings.
- **Generations socialized before and after 1991:** check how complaint and refusal are narrated by each, since institutions changed around them.
- **Women and men:** check who negotiates pay and who speaks at meetings, using union and NGO records.
- **Latgalians:** check how Latgalian speakers raise language and regional claims in public forums.
- **Russian speakers, including the Old Believers:** check community councils and the Russian-language press for the forms a claim takes, apart from the policy dispute itself.
- **The Suiti:** check the lead singer's role in drone singing, as one formal setting where a single voice leads a group.
- **Daugavpils Poles and Belarusians:** look in school and parish minutes for who is allowed to speak for the community.
- **Ukrainian newcomers since 2022 against older Ukrainian residents:** check how each asks for services and support.
- **Jewish Riga, past and present:** set interwar communal minutes beside today's community bodies to see who represents members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for folklore and parliamentary records, thinner for everyday workplace assertion, where surveys may be few.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Russian for half the public debate; Latgalian for eastern regional claims.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language-policy protests and the status of non-citizens are contested ground; present each community's account as an account.
- **Held for review:** none; the formal routes for consumer and civic complaints are a Libra matter and were left out rather than held.
