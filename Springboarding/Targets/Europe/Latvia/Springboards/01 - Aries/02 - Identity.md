# Latvia: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia, Belarusian, Polish, Latvian Romani, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latvians by confession (Lutheran Kurzeme and Vidzeme, Catholic Latgale, Orthodox parishes):** check whether faith is named before or after nationality in self-descriptions.
- **Latgalians:** check whether "Latgalian" is used as a regional, linguistic or ethnic label, since sources use all three.
- **Livonians:** check self-identification in the register against community membership; the numbers may differ.
- **The Suiti:** check how a Catholic enclave keeps a separate label inside Kurzeme.
- **Russian speakers:** check self-labels ("Russian", "Russian-speaking", "Latvian Russian") separately, and the Old Believers apart from later Soviet-era arrivals.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check whether school and parish shape identity more than home language.
- **Latvian Roma:** check whether Roma and outside sources use the same group names.
- **Jews of Latvia:** separate Yiddish-, German- and Russian-speaking self-descriptions before 1941 from those of the present community.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how a revived faith ties identity to the folk signs and the *dainas*.
- **Riga against Latgale and Kurzeme:** check whether urban and regional self-labels pull apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national identity and integration surveys; mixed for the Latgalians; thin for the Livonians and Roma, where the community bodies are the first stop.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian and Russian above all; Latin and German for the chronicle and Baltic German sources; Latgalian and Livonian for the regional bodies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 9 May commemorations, the removal of the Victory Monument in Riga (2022), non-citizen status and the 16 March Legion commemoration are contested; set out each community's version under its own name.
- **Held for review:** none; citizenship and naturalization rules as written belong to Libra and were not scouted here.

## 5. First queries
1. Read the identity chapters in Muižnieks (2010) and pull every survey they cite.

4. Contact *Līvõd Īt* and the Rēzekne museum for their own publications on self-naming.
5. Read Kaprāns on 9 May and the deportation days as markers of group memory.
