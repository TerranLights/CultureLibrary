## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme's coast (Ventspils, Liepāja) against inland Zemgale:** check founding records of fishing and farming cooperatives, since trades differ.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check how schools and parishes credit their founders.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils):** check the press-ban era and present business registers, to see whether historical and present initiative are told differently.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check startup and company density against the regions.
- **Russian-speaking entrepreneurs, including Old Believer merchant families of the past:** check the Russian-language business press and community histories separately.
- **Belarusians and Poles of Daugavpils:** check who founded their societies and schools after 1991.
- **Ukrainian newcomers since 2022:** check their self-help networks against older Ukrainian associations.
- **Jewish founders before 1941:** trace firms and charities started in Riga, Daugavpils and Jēkabpils through the business registers and the Jews in Latvia museum.
- **The Livonians:** check who started the Livonian Union and the Mazirbe People's House.
- **Women and men:** check GEM and register data by sex.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for company data, cooperatives and founders' biographies; mixed for everyday initiative at work and home; thin for Roma enterprise, where sources may not exist in print.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian, Russian, Latgalian for the press-ban sources, German for duchy records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet-era figures (such as Lācis) are read with both their work and their political role in view; the 2022 refugee response touches a war still under way.
- **Held for review:** none; every founder, cooperative and volunteer pointer found could be named.
