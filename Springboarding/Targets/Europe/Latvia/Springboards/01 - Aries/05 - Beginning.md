## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran Vidzeme and Kurzeme against Catholic Latgale:** check when the year's start and a child's first rites fall, since church calendars differ.
- **Latgalians:** check Christmas Eve and new-year customs in Latgalian collections against national ones.
- **Livonians:** check how revival writing frames a new start against loss.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their own calendar customs for local beginnings.
- **Riga:** check founding jubilees against rural parish founding stories.
- **Russian speakers:** check whether the Orthodox and Old Believer calendars (Christmas on 7 January, the Old New Year) set a second beginning of the year.
- **Old Believer settlers in Latgale:** read their arrival narratives apart from the stories of later Russian-speaking migration.
- **The Jewish community:** look at how Rosh Hashanah is kept and how the community describes its post-1991 restart.
- **Deportees and their families:** check return-from-Siberia memoirs by region.
- **Returning emigrants:** check restart stories inside Latvia; their time abroad is comparison only.
- **Dievturība followers:** check which solstice they treat as the year's opening.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the folk calendar, origin chronicles and state founding dates; mixed for personal fresh starts; thin for Roma and Livonian calendar practice.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Middle High German and Latin for the chronicles; Russian for the Orthodox calendar; Latgalian for eastern customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** founding narratives of the crusade era and of 1918 and 1990 are told differently by different communities; the deportations touch living families. Present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** none; the origin, calendar and fresh-start sources all fell inside the rules.
