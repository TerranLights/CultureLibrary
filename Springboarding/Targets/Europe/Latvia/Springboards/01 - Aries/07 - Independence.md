## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farmstead regions (Vidzeme, Kurzeme, Zemgale) against Latgale's village settlement:** check household production and kin help, since settlement forms differ.
- **Sēlija:** check whether this small region's own sources differ from Zemgale's and Latgale's on farm self-reliance.
- **Riga and Pierīga against rural municipalities:** check benefit use and household food production side by side.
- **Russian speakers in Riga and Daugavpils:** check *dacha* and garden-plot provisioning separately from rural Latvians.
- **Old Believer villages of Latgale:** check community histories for how they provided for themselves.
- **Livonians of the coast:** check fishing and household self-reliance before and after the border zone.
- **Belarusians, Poles and Lithuanians in Latgale and Zemgale:** check family help networks across the borders.
- **Jewish mutual aid:** compare interwar loan and welfare societies with the services the community offers its members now.
- **Latvian Roma:** check how families combine state help and kin support; sources likely thin, start with Roma NGOs and social services.
- **Disabled people and elders:** check deinstitutionalization reports and home-care records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and national independence history; mixed for personal self-reliance; thin for Roma and Old Believer household economies.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Russian for *dacha* and Soviet-era sources; German for manor records; Latgalian for eastern community histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national independence touches contested memories of 1940, 1944 and 1991; welfare use carries stigma, so personal data needs care.
- **Held for review:** none; welfare entitlements as written are a Libra question and were left out, not held.

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the Household Budget Survey tables on self-produced food by region.

5. Read the *72 stundas* guide and its press coverage for how self-reliance is asked for.
