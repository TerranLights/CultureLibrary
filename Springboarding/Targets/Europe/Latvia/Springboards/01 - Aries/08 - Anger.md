*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme, Kurzeme and Zemgale against Latgale:** check charm and curse collections region by region, since collecting was uneven.
- **Riga against small towns:** check protest records and police reports for where collective anger gathers.
- **Russian speakers:** check their press and broadcasters for grievance framing, as an account beside the Latvian-language press.
- **Old Believer communities:** read their writing on persecution for how their teaching tells members to handle anger at it.
- **Polish and Belarusian parishes in Latgale:** look for how disputes among members were settled in parish and school records.
- **Yiddish-speaking Latvia:** keep pre-war Yiddish quarrel and curse material apart from post-Holocaust testimony.
- **Latvian Roma:** check whether any recorded sources exist; likely thin, so start with Roma NGOs and the folklore archive's Roma holdings.
- **Livonian and Suiti archives:** search these small holdings for quarrel and reconciliation customs.
- **Women and men:** check hotline, probation and survey data for who is counted as angry and who as harmed.
- **Teenagers and adults:** check school-based aggression studies against adult data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for revolts, charms and protests; thin for everyday display rules, where research may need new fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Russian; German for 16th-19th c. sources; Yiddish for the pre-war Jewish material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic violence data involves victims; 1905 and 2009 are politically framed in different ways; present each account as an account. Hate-speech questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no charm, protest or violence source needed holding.
