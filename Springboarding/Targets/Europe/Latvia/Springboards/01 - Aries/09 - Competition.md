## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and regional towns (Liepāja, Ventspils, Daugavpils, Valmiera):** check club rivalries and school rankings for how towns measure themselves against the capital.
- **Regional folk collections:** see whether swinging, egg-knocking and riddle contests were collected evenly in Kurzeme, Vidzeme, Zemgale and Latgale.
- **Latvian-language and minority-language schools:** check exam and olympiad results by language of instruction across the school reforms.
- **Russian speakers:** check sport clubs, chess and school contests organized in Russian.
- **Latgalians:** check Latgalian-language prizes and festivals as a separate ranking system.
- **Polish and Belarusian schools in Daugavpils:** look at their olympiad entries and folk-ensemble contests.
- **Jewish sport and chess:** trace pre-war sport clubs (for example Maccabi clubs) and Riga's chess circles.
- **Latvian Roma:** check music and dance contests involving Roma ensembles; sources likely few.
- **Women and men:** check federation and Olympic records by sex.
- **The Suiti and Livonians:** check whether their ensembles enter national contests or stay apart.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, school rankings and choirs; mixed for envy and everyday rivalry; thin for Roma and Livonian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Russian for sport and chess history; Latgalian for regional prizes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sport against Russia and Belarus carries political charge after 2022; school results by language of instruction feed a contested debate; report each side's reading of the results.
- **Held for review:** none; the sport, school and festival sources raised no standing-rule issue.
