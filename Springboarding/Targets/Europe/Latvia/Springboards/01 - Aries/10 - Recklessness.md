## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Kurzeme, the Livonian Coast and the Gulf of Riga fishing villages:** check sea and ice accident records against inland lakes, since exposure differs.
- **Latgale's lake district (Lubāns, Rāzna):** check ice-fishing rescues and drownings by municipality.
- **Riga and Jūrmala against rural roads:** check crash and drink-driving data by place.
- **Russian speakers:** check the Russian-language press and harm-reduction reports for how risk is discussed, beside Latvian-language campaigns.
- **Lutheran and Catholic regions:** check temperance history by confession, since church bodies led different campaigns.
- **Old Believer congregations:** compare their rules on drink and tobacco with the regional survey data.
- **Young men and young women:** check survey and crash data by sex and age.
- **Latvian Roma:** check whether health surveys reach Roma at all; if not, say so and start with Roma NGOs.
- **The pre-war Jewish community:** handle Yiddish sources on drink and gambling as their own set.
- **Ukrainian newcomers:** check whether their data appear in the surveys yet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, drowning, alcohol and gambling statistics; mixed for how recklessness is judged; thin for minorities in the health surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian; Russian; German for Stender's context; Latgalian for eastern local sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and drowning data invite a drinking stereotype; report data by group and region and never as a trait of a people. Drug and gambling data may touch identifiable people in small towns.
- **Held for review:** none; traffic and gambling rules as written belong to Libra and were left out rather than held.
