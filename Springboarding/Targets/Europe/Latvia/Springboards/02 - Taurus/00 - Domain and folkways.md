# Latvia: The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (with the Middle dialect, the Tamian speech of northern Kurzeme and Vidzeme, and High Latvian), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian, Polish, Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having in these lands, and how it is held, spent, shown, eaten, worn and handed on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme, Vidzeme, Zemgale, Sēlija and Latgale farms:** check dowry, food and dress sources region by region before calling any national.
- **Riga and Pierīga against farmsteads:** compare survey data, since urban and rural holdings may diverge.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their costume and wedding records as a separate Catholic case in western Kurzeme.
- **Livonians of the Livonian Coast:** check fishing-village goods; records are few, so start with the Livonian Institute.
- **Latgalians and Latgale's Old Believers:** separate Catholic and Old Believer household and food rules.
- **Russian speakers in Riga, Daugavpils and Liepāja:** read Russian-language consumer sources on their own.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community bodies for festive food.
- **Latvian Roma:** check community sources on livelihoods; likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** compare interwar commerce with property lost in 1940-41 and the Holocaust.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the revived faith frames offerings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song, dictionaries, archaeology and official statistics; mixed for minority households; thin for Roma and Livonian everyday goods.
- **Languages needed:** German for the 1286-1649 sources on goods and debt; Russian for Soviet consumer statistics; Yiddish for interwar shop notices; Livonian for coastal goods.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** property seized in 1940-41, during the Holocaust and in the 1949 deportations, and restored after 1991, is contested ground; present each account as an account. Latvia has no settled lands in its tracker and no scope file yet: its exact lands in the post-war order are still to be settled, so this sign is written for Latvia as it stands.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on goods, food or dress had to be set aside.
