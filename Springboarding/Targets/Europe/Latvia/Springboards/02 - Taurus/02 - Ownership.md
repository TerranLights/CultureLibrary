# Latvia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; German for manor-era records; Polish for Latgale's estates. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme farm families against Latgale's:** check when and under which lords buy-out and the 1920 reform reached each.
- **Heirs of prewar owners against people living in restored properties:** read each side's account of denationalization separately.
- **Russian-speaking residents who arrived in the Soviet era:** check how citizenship status shaped their voucher and flat ownership, as they and the authorities describe it.
- **Livonian coastal families:** check land and fishing access before the border zone and after 1991.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check manor-era tenure as a case apart.
- **Polish and Belarusian families in Latgale:** check estate and village holding under the Polish-Lithuanian and Russian periods.
- **The Jewish community:** check property lost in 1940-41 and the later restitution debate, through community bodies.
- **Old Believer villages of Latgale:** check village landholding before and under Soviet rule; sources may be Russian only.
- **Baltic German heirs:** check how their families tell the 1920 expropriation.
- **Roma:** check housing tenure in Roma community reports; thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land records, censuses, government-spending series and privatization history; mixed for attitudes; thin for Livonian and Roma tenure.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Swedish-period and manor files; Polish for the Latgale estates; Russian for kolkhoz records and the 1990s Russian-language press; Swedish for some reduction files held in Stockholm.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restitution to prewar owners, the Jewish community's property claims, land sales to foreign buyers and the voucher rules for non-citizens are contested; present each as an account. The law of property, title and restitution belongs under Libra (the letter of the law), not here.
- **Held for review:** none; tenure, privatization and government-spending sources needed no setting aside, and title law is left to Libra rather than held.

## 5. First queries
1. Download Eurostat general government total expenditure for Latvia, 1995 to the latest year; then the State Treasury's central and local budget totals for the same years.
2. Pull State Land Service land-ownership tables by owner type and region.

4. Query Latvian news archives for "denacionalizēto namu īrnieki" from 1995 to 2010.
5. Ask the State Historical Archives for the 1680s Swedish map of one Vidzeme parish.
