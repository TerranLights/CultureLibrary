# Latvia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, the Russian of Latvia; Latvian Romani, Polish and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm masters' families and former farmhands' families:** check whether worth tied to farm rank lasts in rural Vidzeme and Zemgale memories.
- **Latgalian speakers in Rēzekne and the countryside:** check attitudes to their own speech in school memories and surveys.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** read each group's account of language exams and naturalization as they describe it.
- **Old Believers of Latgale and Riga:** check community sources on standing and faith through Soviet and later times.
- **The Jewish community:** compare prewar sources, survivor testimony and today's community voices on worth and memory.
- **Latvian Roma:** check what community leaders and Roma-led groups report about school and work.
- **Livonians:** check how the small community writes about the standing of its language.
- **Men in former industrial towns (Liepāja, Daugavpils, Ventspils):** check job-loss and suicide studies by region.
- **Disabled people's organizations:** check members' own testimony.
- **Lutheran, Catholic and Dievturība congregations:** check teaching on worth before God or the gods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song, school surveys and literature; mixed for adult self-esteem measures; thin for Roma and Livonian sources (look to community groups, not neighbors' data).
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the Volkov studies and Russian-language accounts of exams; Latgalian for congress papers; German for Moravian Brethren records of the 18th century; Yiddish and English for survivor testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the status of non-citizens, language exams, Latgalian's status and Holocaust testimony are contested or painful ground; present each as an account. Suicide data need care in presentation.
- **Held for review:** none; the honor, shame and dignity sources here are Latvian, minority and testimony archives, and none fell under a standing rule.
