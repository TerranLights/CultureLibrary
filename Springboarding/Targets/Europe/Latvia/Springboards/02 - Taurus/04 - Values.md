# Latvia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran Kurzeme and Vidzeme, Catholic Latgale, Orthodox and Old Believer communities:** check how each church's teaching ranks values, rather than reading one confession as the nation.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their Catholic tradition apart from neighboring Lutheran parishes.
- **Dievturība followers:** compare the 1930s doctrine with today's congregations.
- **Russian speakers:** check values surveys split by home language, and Russian-language church and school sources, before drawing any line.
- **Riga and the countryside:** compare survey answers; urban and rural samples may differ in what they put first.
- **Generations formed before 1940, under Soviet rule and after 1991:** check cohort splits in values surveys.
- **The Jewish community:** check community teaching and memory sources.
- **Livonians:** check church and community sources on the coast.
- **Poles and Lithuanians of Latgale and Zemgale:** check their parish and school sources.
- **Roma:** check community sources on what elders teach; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church texts, folk song and surveys; mixed for minority faiths; thin for Livonian and Roma sources.
- **Languages needed:** German and Latin for the 16th-century catechisms' context; Polish and Latin for Latgale's Catholic texts; Russian for Orthodox and Old Believer teaching; Livonian for the 1942 scripture.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates over women's ordination, same-sex partnership and "virtue" lessons in schools are contested; present each side as it presents itself. The law behind the school debate belongs under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the catechisms, canon list and values surveys raised nothing under the standing rules for this concept.
