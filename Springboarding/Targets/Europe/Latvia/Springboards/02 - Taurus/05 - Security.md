# Latvia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian, Polish and Yiddish for particular communities; Livonian for coastal sources. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pensioners in Latgale and in Riga:** compare pension levels and the role of gardens and family support in each.
- **Russian speakers in Riga, Daugavpils and Rēzekne:** check how they report national and personal security after 2014 and 2022, in their own media and in defense polls.
- **Livonian Coast and Kurzeme fishing families:** check risk at sea and seasonal want in local sources.
- **Farm families of Zemgale's grain belt against forest smallholders of Vidzeme and Sēlija:** check how harvest risk and savings differ.
- **Old Believers of Latgale:** check community and kin support in Russian-language sources.
- **Belarusians, Ukrainians and Poles:** check community bodies, and for Ukrainians the newer arrivals since 2022 apart from long-settled families.
- **Roma households:** check social-service and community reports on housing and income; sources thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check welfare work for elderly members, including survivors.
- **Young workers in Riga's service jobs:** check contract and job-tenure data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pensions, unemployment, banking and defense polling; mixed for household food storage; thin for Roma and Livonian sources.
- **Languages needed:** German and Swedish for the 1690s famine records; Russian for Soviet-era savings and for Russian-language security coverage; Belarusian, Polish and Yiddish for community welfare sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national-security topics after 2022, and public debates that question Russian speakers' loyalty, are contested and charged; present each account as an account and never let a source speak for a whole group. Some folk signs were misused in the 20th century; read their history carefully. Deportation memory belongs under Possession and Stability.
- **Held for review:** none; famine, pension, defense and energy sources raised nothing under the standing rules.
