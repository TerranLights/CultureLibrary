# Latvia: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale and Sēlija villages against Pierīga suburbs:** check population-change data, since emptying and growth may sit side by side.
- **Families with deportation or exile in their past:** check how breaks in place are told against families who stayed.
- **Russian speakers who settled in Riga, Daugavpils and Liepāja in the Soviet decades:** check how they describe putting down roots, in their own words.
- **Old Believers:** check community records for continuity of rite and village.
- **Livonians:** check continuity of language and place on the coast.
- **The Suiti:** check how song and costume are handed on.
- **Jewish families:** check the few continuous families and the community re-formed after 1945 and 1991.
- **Poles and Belarusians in Daugavpils and Krāslava:** check long-settled family records.
- **Lutheran and Catholic parishes:** check parish histories for families in one parish for generations.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the revived faith frames continuity with an older past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, elections, economic history and festival history; mixed for family continuity; thin for Livonian and Roma continuity (ask community bodies).
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and German for 17th-century sources behind the "Swedish times"; Russian for Old Believer and Soviet-era settler sources; Livonian for revitalization materials; Latgalian for Klīdzējs's world.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet-era settlement, the 2008-10 austerity and the end of Jewish town life are contested or painful; present each account as an account. Who counts as "rooted" can be a charged question in public debate.
- **Held for review:** none; the migration, currency-peg and continuity sources here needed no setting aside.
