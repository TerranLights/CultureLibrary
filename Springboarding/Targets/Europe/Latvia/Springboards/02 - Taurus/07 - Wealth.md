# Latvia: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish, Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale:** check income and wealth data side by side before any talk of "Latvian" prosperity.
- **Ventspils and Liepāja port towns:** compare how transit and industry wealth is described by residents and by outsiders.
- **Zemgale grain farmers and Vidzeme forest owners:** check land and timber as sources of rural wealth.
- **Latvian- and Russian-speaking business owners:** check how each language's press covers fortunes and connections.
- **Old Believer merchant families of Riga and Latgale:** check community histories for prerevolutionary trade, without reading trade as a trait.
- **Livonian fishing families:** check catch and sea-luck beliefs as wealth in their own terms.
- **The Jewish community:** check property records and how the community tells its loss in 1940-41, not prewar occupations read as traits.
- **Roma households:** check how Roma organizations themselves describe wealth and poverty; few sources.
- **Polish and Belarusian families of Daugavpils:** check community sources on prewar estates and later poverty.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the revived faith frames abundance and the household spirits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, declarations and investigative reporting; mixed for attitudes; thin for minority-held wealth and for Roma.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Blackheads' and Courland records; Estonian for Loorits on the Livonians; Russian for Soviet kolkhoz sources and the Russian-language business press; Yiddish for prewar community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "oligarch" debates name living people, some in court cases; cite each claim as an account and give the subject's reply. Nostalgia for the Ulmanis era is politically charged. Never link wealth or poverty to an ethnic group.
- **Held for review:** none; the rich lists, declarations and folk-belief sources on riches raised nothing under the standing rules.
