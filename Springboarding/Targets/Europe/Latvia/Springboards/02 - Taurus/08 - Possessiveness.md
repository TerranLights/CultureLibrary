# Latvia: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, the Russian of Latvia; Yiddish and Polish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's younger couples against rural Latgale and Sēlija households:** check control and jealousy items by age and place in the survey data.
- **Latvian- and Russian-speaking respondents:** compare survey answers and advice columns in each language before drawing any contrast.
- **The Suiti and Latgalian Catholic villages:** check wedding claims against Lutheran Vidzeme and Kurzeme records.
- **Old Believer communities:** check marriage and guarding customs as the communities themselves describe them.
- **Livonian fishing villages:** check claims over nets, landing places and catch as coastal records show them.
- **Charmers and their heirs:** check who may receive charms and why, as practitioners tell it.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how vows in the revived rite are framed.
- **Roma families:** check what Roma organizations publish on marriage and family; thin, and never infer from outsiders.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar marriage customs through community and Yiddish sources.
- **Polish and Belarusian families of Daugavpils:** check parish and community records on marriage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for violence and control surveys; mixed for folk belief and wedding customs; thin for Roma, Livonian and Old Believer sources on the topic.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Russian-language advice columns and Old Believer sources; Latgalian for Latgale's wedding and charm terms; Yiddish for prewar Jewish marriage sources; Polish for Daugavpils parishes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence data concern living people; use published aggregates only. Charmers' words may be restricted knowledge that practitioners do not share; respect their limits. The debate over ratifying the Istanbul Convention is contested; its law belongs under Libra. Envy sayings must never be used as proof of a national trait.
- **Held for review:** none; the control surveys, envy beliefs and wedding sources raised nothing under the standing rules, and charmers' restricted words are flagged above, not held.
