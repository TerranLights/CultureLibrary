## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latvian *pirts* and the Russian or Old Believer *banya* of Latgale:** check whether bath customs differ in build, timing and talk.
- **Livonian and Kurzeme coastal households:** check fish, smoke and sea in their own food records.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their festive foods and song as a separate case.
- **Lutheran, Catholic, Orthodox and Old Believer households:** compare fasts and feasts across the year.
- **Riga and Jūrmala against rural Latgale and Sēlija:** compare leisure time and spending on pleasures.
- **Russian speakers who remember Jūrmala's Soviet resort era:** read their own memoirs apart from Latvian ones.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the revived faith frames midsummer herbs, beer and sauna.
- **The Jewish community:** check Sabbath and festival foods through community sources.
- **Polish and Belarusian families of Latgale:** check Christmas Eve and Easter food customs.
- **Roma:** check music and festive customs through Roma-led groups; sources thin.
