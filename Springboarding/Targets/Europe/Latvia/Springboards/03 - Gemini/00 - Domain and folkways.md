## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against regional towns:** check language choice in shops and offices by setting before treating Riga's speech as the country's.
- **Kurzeme's Tamian coast and the Suiti of Alsunga:** check dialect material for speech forms apart from the standard.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza):** separate Latgalian, Latvian, Russian, Belarusian and Polish use town by town.
- **Livonians of the Livonian Coast:** check revival classes as learning a language with no home speakers.
- **Russian speakers in Riga, Daugavpils and Liepāja:** compare home-language data with community sources on the school reform.
- **Old Believers of Latgale and Riga:** check Church Slavonic reading as a separate learning line.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community language classes.
- **Latvian Roma:** check Romani teaching assistants in schools; likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** compare the interwar *kheyder* and Hebrew and Yiddish schools with the Riga community school today.
- **The Deaf community:** check Latvian Sign Language users as a language community.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the revived faith teaches song and ritual to the young.
