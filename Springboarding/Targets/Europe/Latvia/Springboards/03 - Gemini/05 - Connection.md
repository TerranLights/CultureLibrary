# Latvia: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Polish, Belarusian, Ukrainian, Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale and Kurzeme villages:** compare godparent choice in Catholic and Lutheran registers before reading either as typical.
- **Riga newcomers from the regions:** check district associations and reunions as ways of keeping home ties.
- **Thinly settled rural parishes of Sēlija and Latgale:** check isolation data for elders left behind.
- **Livonians:** check the festival and the online community as a scattered network.
- **Russian speakers:** check several organizations, not one, for how networks form across Riga, Daugavpils and Jūrmala.
- **Old Believers:** check kin and parish ties across Latgale's villages.
- **Poles, Belarusians and Ukrainians:** check each community society's own events.
- **Latvian Roma:** check kin-network sources through Roma organizations; likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check how survivors and their descendants rebuilt ties after 1945, as they tell it.
- **Former deportees:** check the associations of the politically repressed as networks born of exile.
- **The Suiti:** check whether their community ties run through the parish, the singing groups or family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parish records, folklore and EU surveys; mixed for networks of favors (memoir-based); thin for Roma and Old Believer kin networks.
- **Languages needed:** German and Latin for the oldest registers; Polish for the Union of Poles; Russian for *blat* memoirs; Livonian for the festival's own materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Russian-speaking organizations and the 'non-citizen' question are contested; give each body's account as its own. Parish registers hold personal data on living descendants; use published studies first.
- **Held for review:** none; no ties, reunion or loneliness source here fell under a standing rule.
