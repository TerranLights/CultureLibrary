# Latvia: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (including the poetic diminutives of the folk songs), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Polish, Belarusian, Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme Lutheran farms against Catholic Latgale:** check heir and dowry customs region by region before naming any one pattern.
- **Riga apartment families:** compare birth-order data with rural municipalities.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check wedding and inheritance records for brothers' and sisters' roles.
- **Livonian fishing families:** check how boats and nets passed among brothers; likely thin.
- **Russian-speaking families in Daugavpils and Riga:** check kin-term use and cousin closeness in their own memoirs.
- **Old Believers of Latgale:** check family-size and kin-duty sources in community records.
- **Poles and Belarusians:** check parish registers for godparent and sibling links.
- **Latvian Roma:** check how Roma organizations describe the duties of brothers and sisters; sources likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check testimony of siblings separated or lost in 1941, as survivors tell it.
- **Deported families:** check how siblings sent to Siberia in 1941 and 1949 found each other again.
- **Blended and single-parent households:** check half- and step-sibling data from household surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk songs, tales and household records; mixed for present-day sibling ties; thin for Roma and Livonian sibling customs.
- **Languages needed:** German and Russian for the revision lists; Russian for Russian-speaking families' memoirs; Yiddish for interwar family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children in care and siblings separated by deportation and the Holocaust are painful ground; use published testimony and protect living persons.
- **Held for review:** none; inheritance law as written is for Libra, and no sibling source here needed setting aside.
