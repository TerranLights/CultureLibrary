# Latvia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Latin and German for medieval and Baltic German sources; Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lutheran Kurzeme and Vidzeme against Catholic Latgale:** check whether people turned to a pastor, a priest or a saint, in parish sources from each.
- **Rural parishes against Riga:** compare kin and neighbor go-betweens with paid professionals in recent surveys and press.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their wedding go-between roles as a separate Catholic case in the west.
- **Livonians:** check whether fishing villages had their own elders for disputes over nets and shores.
- **Russian speakers:** check community organizations and the OSCE record as two different kinds of go-between.
- **Old Believers:** check the *nastavnik*'s role from community sources.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Latgale:** check parish priests as go-betweens in mixed parishes.
- **Latvian Roma:** check how community leaders describe their role toward schools and offices.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar arbitration and today's community leaders.
- **Ukrainians arriving since 2022:** check volunteers and interpreters who stand between newcomers and offices.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether the revived faith names any go-between figure between people and the gods.
