# Latvia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; German for the witch-trial records; Polish, Latvian Romani and Yiddish for particular communities. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns of Vidzeme and Kurzeme against Riga:** check whether people describe the reach of talk differently by town size, in memoir and surveys.
- **Latgale villages:** check Latgalian anecdotes and nicknames as a separate store of village talk.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether close-knit parish life leaves its own records of talk and reputation.
- **Livonian Coast villages:** check memoirs of fishing villages for how news of neighbors moved; likely thin.
- **Russian speakers in Riga and Daugavpils:** compare Russian-language celebrity and neighbor talk with the Latvian-language press.
- **Old Believers:** check how community sources treat talk about members.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Latgale:** check parish and community sources.
- **Latvian Roma:** check how outsiders' rumors about Roma have been recorded, and how Roma organizations answer them; avoid repeating the rumors.
- **The Jewish community:** check how the 1941 propaganda used rumor against Jewish neighbors, as survivors and historians tell it.
- **Teenagers online:** check helpline and school data on rumor and shaming apart from adult forms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk songs, tabloids and online-safety reports; mixed for workplace talk; thin for Roma, Livonian and Old Believer sources.
- **Languages needed:** German for 17th-century trial records; Russian for communal-flat memoirs and society pages; Yiddish and Hebrew for rabbinic teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** occupation-era propaganda and the Holocaust must be handled as history of harm, never repeated as content; present each account as an account. Gossip about living people and children's cyberbullying cases involve private data.
- **Held for review:** none; slander and defamation as written law go to Libra, and no rumor or tabloid source needed setting aside.
