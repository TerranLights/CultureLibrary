# Latvia: Family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (and the in-law vocabulary of the folk songs), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale, Latvian Romani, Yiddish for prewar Jewish family records. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Vidzeme against Riga:** check how far kin ties stretch for urban flat-dwellers against farm families.
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether in-law terms and lineage gatherings differ between the two.
- **The Suiti:** check Catholic godparent and kin ties in Alsunga's own records.
- **Latgale's Catholic, Orthodox and Old Believer families:** check family size and kin reach in each congregation's records.
- **Russian-speaking families in Riga and Daugavpils:** check kin networks that cross into Russia and Belarus, from their own accounts.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check family ties across borders.
- **Mixed-language families:** check which kin terms are used at home.
- **Livonians:** check descent claims within families.
- **Roma:** check kin reckoning with community contact.
- **The Jewish community:** check how survivors' families were rebuilt after 1945.
- **Dievturība followers:** check lineage rites as practiced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for historical kinship and folk-song kin terms; mixed for present kin networks; thin for Roma and Old Believer family records outside church histories.
- **Languages needed:** German for catechisms and manor-era household lists; Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer records; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish family records; Latgalian for parish-feast sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as family is tied to current debates on partnership and marriage; these are Libra matters and are left out. Families split by deportation or the Holocaust: present each account as given.
- **Held for review:** none; the kinship, survey and community sources here raised nothing under the standing rules.
