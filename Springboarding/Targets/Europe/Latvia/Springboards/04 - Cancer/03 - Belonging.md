# Latvia: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (including Kurzeme and Latgale regional speech), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian, Polish and Ukrainian for community bodies; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish community sources. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latvians of Vidzeme, Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether district loyalty and national belonging pull together or apart.
- **Latgalians:** check self-naming as Latgalian, Latvian or both, by town and generation.
- **The Suiti:** check whether belonging is tied to Alsunga or to the faith.
- **Livonians:** check who counts as Livonian and by what sign (descent, language, register entry).
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check survey answers by citizenship status, not by language alone.
- **Old Believers:** check community membership as separate from Russian-speaker belonging.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community bodies' own statements.
- **Roma:** check belonging in their own organizations' words.
- **The Jewish community:** check membership and its rebuilding after 1945 and after 1990.
- **Riga newcomers from the regions:** check how internal migrants describe settling in.
- **Dievturība followers:** check the faith's account of belonging to a people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national-identity surveys and minority debates; mixed for regional belonging; thin for Roma and Livonians.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Russian speakers' own accounts and the 2022 monument debate; Latgalian for regional identity sources; German and Latin for the *Undeutsche* records; Ukrainian for the newest arrivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 'non-citizen' status, Latgalian's standing, 9 May and the 2022 monument removal are contested ground; present each as an account and let no source stand for a whole group. Citizenship law as written is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; formal-membership and identity sources here needed no setting aside.
