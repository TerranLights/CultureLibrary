# Latvia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (with the cradle-song language of the folk songs), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Latvian Romani (terms to be taken from a Baltic Romani dictionary); Yiddish for the prewar Jewish home. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga parents:** check reliance on paid care and kindergartens against regional towns.
- **Rural Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check whether children's farm chores still shape upbringing.
- **Latgale's Catholic families:** check catechism and first communion as steps in raising a child.
- **The Suiti:** check whether girls learn the drone singing from the older women, and how.
- **Livonian Coast families:** check language nests and summer schools as nurture outside the home.
- **Russian-speaking families:** check the grandmother's role and Russian-language kindergartens, in their own sources.
- **Old Believers:** check how prayer and reading are taught to children at home.
- **Belarusian, Polish and Ukrainian families:** check weekend schools as cultural nurture.
- **Roma families:** check the gap between school and home teaching; community contact needed.
- **The Jewish community:** check the community school and Sabbath customs as upbringing.
- **Dievturība followers:** check naming rites and children's festivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song and child-care statistics; mixed for parenting practice; thin for Roma and Old Believer homes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Soviet kindergarten memoirs and Russian-speaking grandparents; Latgalian and Livonian for regional cradle songs; Yiddish for the lullaby collections.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Pioneers and the Youth Guard are remembered and judged differently by different communities; present each account. Child discipline at its legal edges is a Capricorn or Libra matter.
- **Held for review:** none; the upbringing and youth-movement sources raised nothing under the standing rules.
