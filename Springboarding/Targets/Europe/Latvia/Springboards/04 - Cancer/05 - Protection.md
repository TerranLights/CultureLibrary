## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state agencies and folk belief; mixed for household practice; thin for Roma and Livonian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic for *Pokrov* and Old Believer sources; Latgalian for village-cross studies; German for 19th-century fire-society records; Romani for community memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the fire cross, the interwar *Aizsargi* and the Holocaust, including the murder of Roma, are contested ground; present each account as an account. Child-protection procedure and self-defense law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
