# Latvia: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Ukrainian for arrivals since 2022; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish rescue testimony; German for medieval Riga's almshouses. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against regional towns (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Liepāja, Ventspils):** check shelter capacity and who runs it in each.
- **Rural Vidzeme and Sēlija:** check whether kin take in the homeless where no shelter exists.
- **Kurzeme coast villages and Livonians:** check 1944 boat departures and who stayed to shelter others.
- **Latgale:** check Catholic parish charity and Old Believer communities, the latter settled as refugees from persecution centuries ago.
- **Russian speakers:** check representation among shelter users in Russian-language reporting.
- **Ukrainians since 2022:** check housing arrangements in their own accounts.
- **Belarusians and Poles:** check community aid to new arrivals.
- **Roma:** check shelter access; likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check rescue testimony against perpetrator records.
- **Asylum seekers from beyond Europe:** check NGO accounts alongside official ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for refugee history and rescue records; mixed for homelessness outside Riga; thin for Roma and for shelter by kin.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Siberian-deportee and Soviet records; Swedish for 1944 Gotland reception; German for Ecke's Convent; Ukrainian for the 2022 arrivals; Yiddish and Hebrew for rescue testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the forest brothers, the Holocaust, the deportations and the 2021 Belarus border crisis are contested; present each account as an account. Asylum law as written is Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; the refuge, rescue and relief sources raised no standing-rule issue.
