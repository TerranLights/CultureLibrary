# Latvia: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Polish for Catholic charity in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew for prewar Jewish care societies. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against small towns and farms:** check access to paid home care against care by kin.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza):** check Catholic, Orthodox and Old Believer care networks separately.
- **Kurzeme, including Liepāja:** check Lutheran diakonia and municipal care in port towns.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check rural feldsher coverage.
- **Families with members working abroad:** check how care for parents is arranged from a distance.
- **Russian-speaking pensioners:** check their own accounts of care and isolation, in Russian-language media.
- **The Suiti and Livonian Coast villages:** check whether small, aging communities rely on neighbors for care.
- **Belarusians and Poles:** check parish and community care.
- **Roma:** check access to health and care services; likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check care for elderly survivors today.
- **Disabled people and their families:** check care as described by the disabled themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health surveys and institutional history; mixed for unpaid care at home; thin for Roma and Old Believer care.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Old Believer and Russian-speaking pensioners' sources; German for 19th-century hospital records; Yiddish and Hebrew for prewar Jewish care societies; Latgalian for parish material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care-home deaths in the pandemic and Soviet-era psychiatric institutions are painful subjects; present each account as given. Entitlements as written belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the nursing, charity and folk-healing sources here fell inside the rules.
