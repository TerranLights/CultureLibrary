# Latvia: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (and the mother words of the folk songs), Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Polish for Latgale's Marian devotion; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar Jewish home. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture (the mother of the nation, the Virgin Mother, Mother Earth).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latvian Lutherans of Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check Mother's Day and church customs for mothers.
- **Catholic Latgale and the Suiti:** check Marian devotion as a frame for motherhood in each.
- **Orthodox and Old Believer families:** check icons of the Mother of God in the home and the place of the mother in community records.
- **Russian-speaking mothers in Riga and Daugavpils:** check their own forums and press, not Latvian-language summaries.
- **Rural Zemgale and Sēlija:** check distance to maternity wards and its effect on birth.
- **Livonian women:** check their part in keeping the language at home.
- **Belarusian, Polish, Ukrainian and Lithuanian mothers:** check community schools and churches.
- **Roma mothers:** check health access through community contact.
- **The Jewish community:** check mothers in the community's rebuilding after 1945.
- **Dievturība followers:** check rites for mother and child.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief, benefits and birth statistics; mixed for mothers' own accounts; thin for Roma and Livonian mothers.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Soviet honors and Russian-language forums; Polish and Latgalian for Marian devotion in Latgale; Yiddish and German for prewar Jewish women's groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abortion, surrogacy and leave rules are Aries, Libra or Capricorn matters and are left out; Soviet-era honors and the deportation of mothers with children are remembered differently by different communities; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** none; the mother-figure, maternity and benefit sources needed no setting aside.
