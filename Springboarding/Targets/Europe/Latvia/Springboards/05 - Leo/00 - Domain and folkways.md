## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check whether capital stages and nightlife differ in kind from village halls.
- **Liepāja and Ventspils:** check port-city music scenes against inland Kurzeme's collectives.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check drone singing and festival dress as a separate Catholic line within Kurzeme.
- **Livonians of the coast:** check what the festival and song groups carry for so few speakers.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check Catholic feast days, pottery and Latgalian-language music.
- **Sēlija:** check who is building a regional festive identity.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Russian-language theater, contests and Old Believer feast customs on their own sources.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community ensembles and church feasts.
- **Roma:** check community music and dance ensembles, and how they view mummers' "Gypsy" masks.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar Riga and Daugavpils Yiddish stages and today's community events.
