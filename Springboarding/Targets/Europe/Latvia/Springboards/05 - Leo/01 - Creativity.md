## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check weaving and woodcraft circles and how they teach newcomers.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils):** check pottery families against art-school ceramists for who counts as original.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check how far a lead singer may change a drone song before it is "not ours".
- **Livonians of the coast:** check boat-building and needlework records, and who keeps the patterns.
- **Kuldīga, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check town craft houses against farm crafts.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check Rundāle and Jelgava as courts of hired foreign masters against local makers.
- **Russian speakers in Riga:** check theater, literature and design schools teaching in Russian.
- **Old Believers:** check the line between copying a holy model and adding one's own hand.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community craft circles and church art.
- **Roma:** check musical improvisation and who learns it from whom; sources likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar artisans, painters and architects, and the gap the Holocaust left.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how ornament signs are made and taught inside the faith.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song, craft heritage and art history; mixed for invention and the creative economy; thin for Roma and Livonian making today.
- **Languages needed:** German for guild statutes and Baltic German art history; Russian for Soviet art-school records and Russian-language writers; Latgalian for pottery lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whether folk ornament is common property or a mark to be protected is argued over; treat each side as an account. Soviet-era artists' unions and censorship are remembered in different ways.
- **Held for review:** none; ownership of works as property goes to Taurus, and design and copyright law to Libra.
