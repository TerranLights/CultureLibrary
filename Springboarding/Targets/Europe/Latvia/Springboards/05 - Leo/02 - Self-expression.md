## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's art and club scenes against small towns in Vidzeme:** check where unusual dress and public feeling draw comment.
- **Latgale:** check Latgalian-language songs and press as a choice of voice, not just a dialect.
- **The Suiti:** check what drone songs let women say in public.
- **Livonians:** check poems written in Livonian and the reasons poets give.
- **Liepāja:** check the port city's rock history as a separate line of expression.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check how farm families describe speaking of feelings, through local life stories.
- **Russian speakers in Riga, Daugavpils and Jūrmala:** check their own media and stages on what they feel free to say.
- **Old Believers:** check rules on dress and speech in community life.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community press and choirs as voices of their own.
- **Roma:** check song and poetry in Romani; likely needs community contact.
- **The Jewish community:** check the prewar Yiddish and Hebrew press and today's community media.
- **Dievturība followers:** check folk dress and song as statements of faith.
- **LGBTIQ people across regions:** check the gap between Riga and the rest.
