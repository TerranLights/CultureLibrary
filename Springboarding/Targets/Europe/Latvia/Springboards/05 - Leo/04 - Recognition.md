*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check whether national honors cluster in the capital.
- **Latgale:** check how Latgalian writers, musicians and potters are credited at home and in Riga.
- **Kurzeme, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check local honorary-citizen titles against national lists.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check farm and co-op awards before and after 1991.
- **The Suiti and the Livonians:** check what formal listing has changed for each, as they themselves describe it.
- **Russian speakers:** check Russian-language awards, Soviet-era honors still worn, and the standing of 'non-citizens' as an argued account.
- **Old Believers:** check whether their communities appear on heritage lists and in honors.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check minority-association awards and state honors for community leaders.
- **Roma:** check whether Roma persecution in the war is publicly named; sources likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check rescuers' recognition and the naming of Holocaust victims.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the faith's public standing is described by its followers and by others.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors, prizes and deportation lists; mixed for workplace credit; thin for Roma and Livonian recognition.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Soviet labor honors and Russian-language community awards; Latgalian for regional prizes; Yiddish and Hebrew for Yad Vashem files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** non-citizen status, Latgalian's standing, Soviet versus Legion veterans and the Holocaust are contested; present each account as an account. Deportation lists hold personal data on living families.
- **Held for review:** none; the award lists, honor registers and deportee lists named here fell inside the rules.
