*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga:** check which districts are covered in the news and which are not.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how far Latgalian speakers see themselves on screens and signs.
- **Rural Sēlija and Zemgale:** check whether people speak of their region as overlooked, and where they say it.
- **Kurzeme coast and the Livonians:** check visibility on signs, maps and in schools.
- **The Suiti:** check what heritage listing brought in attention, wanted and unwanted.
- **Russian speakers:** check how they appear in Latvian-language media and how they appear in their own.
- **Old Believers:** check whether their villages and churches appear on tourist maps.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community media and the census names they choose.
- **Roma:** check how they are named in surveys and the press; likely needs community contact.
- **The Jewish community:** check the marking of former Jewish quarters and synagogue sites in Latgale towns.
- **Elderly people in villages:** check visiting programs and loneliness data.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether they choose to be visible at public festivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census, media regulation and KGB files; mixed for everyday attention norms; thin for Roma and Livonian visibility.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for KGB files, the resort's past and Russian-language media; Latgalian for regional media; Yiddish and Hebrew for memorial sites.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the KGB card index names people still living; Holocaust sites and the place of Russian speakers in public media are contested; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** none; the evil-eye beliefs, census tables and social-network sources raised no standing-rule issue.
