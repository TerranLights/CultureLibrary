*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Latgale:** check deprivation, distance to care and how old villagers speak of being looked after.
- **Riga's microdistricts:** check care for the old living alone in Soviet-built flats.
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme farmsteads:** check what families say decency requires when money is short, in their own accounts.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check the legacy of collective-farm work in how labor is spoken of.
- **The Livonians:** check how the coast's people recall the Soviet border zone that cut off their villages.
- **The Suiti:** check Catholic charity and the parish's role in care.
- **Russian speakers:** check 'non-citizen' pensioners' accounts against official accounts, naming each by its own name.
- **Old Believers:** check community care for the old and the dead.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check parish and community aid.
- **Roma:** check access to housing and services; reports likely thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check burial, remembrance and community welfare after the Holocaust.
- **People with disabilities:** check Soviet-era institutions against present ones.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the faith frames care for the old and dying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deportation memoirs, poverty data and care-home inspections; mixed for everyday courtesy; thin for Roma and Livonian accounts.
- **Languages needed:** German for Merkel and Baltic German manor records; Russian for Russian-speaking pensioners' and NGOs' accounts; Yiddish and Hebrew for Holocaust naming projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportations, the Holocaust, non-citizen status and the end-of-life debate are contested or painful; present each account as an account. Care-home reports may hold personal details.
- **Held for review:** none; the testimony, poverty and ombudsman sources here needed no setting aside, and rights as written are left to Libra.
