*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale:** check memories of language shaming, and how Latgalian activists frame them.
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check manor-era memory and the corvée songs collected there.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check collective-farm discipline and public "criticism meetings".
- **Riga:** check school bullying data and online shaming against the regions.
- **Liepāja and Karosta:** check how a closed military town's past is told by its residents.
- **The Livonians and the Suiti:** check whether outsiders' mockery of their speech or customs appears in their own accounts.
- **Russian speakers:** check their accounts of language inspections and citizenship tests, set beside Latvian accounts of Soviet-era Russification.
- **Old Believers:** check their records of persecution and how they recount it.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community memories of forced assimilation in different eras.
- **Roma:** check community-held accounts first.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivor testimony and how humiliation is taught at the Holocaust museums.
- **Former conscripts:** check hazing memories across generations.
- **Dievturība followers:** check accounts of persecution under Soviet rule.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deportation and Holocaust testimony and school bullying; mixed for workplace and online shaming; thin for Roma and Livonian accounts.
- **Languages needed:** German for manor and town-court records; Russian for army memoirs and Russian-language accounts of inspections; Yiddish, Hebrew and English for survivor testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Holocaust, the deportations, language inspections and the citizenship question are contested or painful; present each account as an account. Testimony archives hold personal data; follow their terms of use. Revenge goes to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; the hazing, pillory and hate-speech sources were kept, and hate-speech law as written goes to Libra.
