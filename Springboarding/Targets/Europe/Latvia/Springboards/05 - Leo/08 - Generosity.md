## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga:** check big-city charities and online giving against neighborly help in small towns.
- **Vidzeme and Zemgale farms:** check work bees, harvest sharing and the treating of helpers.
- **Kurzeme and the Suiti:** check Catholic and Lutheran parish giving side by side.
- **Latgale:** check church festivals, feast-day hospitality and mummers' rounds.
- **Sēlija:** check whether village work bees survived collectivization.
- **The Livonian coast:** check how fishing households shared a catch, from their records.
- **Russian speakers:** check Russian-language charities and the treating of guests in their own sources.
- **Old Believers:** check alms customs tied to feast days and funerals.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Lithuanians and Ukrainians:** check community aid, and Ukrainians as both givers and receivers since 2022.
- **Roma:** check gift customs at weddings and funerals through community contact; sources thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check welfare before 1941 and today's community aid.
- **Dievturība followers:** check gift-giving at seasonal rites.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, charity platforms and EU surveys; mixed for informal help; thin for Roma and Livonian giving.
- **Languages needed:** German and Latin for Riga's medieval foundations; Russian for Old Believer and Russian-language charities; Ukrainian for refugee-aid sources; Yiddish for prewar welfare.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** aid to Ukrainians has become a political subject; present each account as an account. Gift exchange as reciprocity goes to Libra, wealth to Taurus.
- **Held for review:** none; the dowry, charity-platform and refugee-aid sources raised nothing under the standing rules.
