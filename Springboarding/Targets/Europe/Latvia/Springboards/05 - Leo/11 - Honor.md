## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme and the Suiti:** check whether Catholic and Lutheran parishes honor the dead the same way at *kapu svētki*.
- **Latgale:** check honor of family and parish around roadside crosses and feast days.
- **Vidzeme:** check the War of Independence battle sites (Cēsis, 1919) and local memory.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check farm families' good name in land disputes, through local histories.
- **Riga:** check the student corporations and city commemorations.
- **Livonians:** check how the coast honors its fishermen and its dead.
- **Russian speakers:** check how Soviet war dead are honored and how that is described by participants and critics.
- **Old Believers:** check honor owed to elders and the dead in community rules.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community war memorials and honor customs.
- **Roma:** check family honor customs only through community voices; sources thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check honor of the dead after the Holocaust and of 1919 veterans.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how the faith frames honor and the ancestors.
- **Women across regions:** check how a woman's good name is spoken of, then and now.
