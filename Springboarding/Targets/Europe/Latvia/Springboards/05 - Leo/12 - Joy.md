*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*), Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*), the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian and Polish in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check summer festivals and outdoor gladness against winter gatherings.
- **Kurzeme and the Suiti:** check drone singing at feasts as joy, not only as heritage.
- **The Livonian coast:** check what the summer festival and fishing seasons bring in song.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils):** check church feasts, village bands and Latgalian song.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check harvest gatherings and their songs.
- **Riga and Jūrmala:** check city nightlife and resort summers.
- **Russian speakers:** check *Maslenitsa*, New Year and family feasts in their own media.
- **Old Believers:** check Easter as the high point of joy in the community year.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check community festivals and songs.
- **Roma:** check feasts and music through community voices.
- **The Jewish community:** check Purim, Simchat Torah and community festivals today.
- **Dievturība followers:** check seasonal rites as occasions of joy.
- **Older people living alone:** check joy and its absence in later life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song, festivals and well-being surveys; mixed for everyday delight; thin for Roma and Livonian joy.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for *Maslenitsa* and Russian-language media; Belarusian for Kupala Night; Yiddish for klezmer history; Latgalian for wedding music.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outsiders' remarks on how cheerful or dour people seem are claims to test, not findings. Mental-health data must be handled carefully. Grief goes to Scorpio, hope to Aquarius.
- **Held for review:** none; the song, dance, happiness-survey and holiday sources raised no standing-rule issue.
