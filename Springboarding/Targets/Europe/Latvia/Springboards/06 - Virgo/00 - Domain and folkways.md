## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against rural Vidzeme:** check whether the factory, office and commute day keeps a different rhythm from the farmstead year.
- **Zemgale:** check how the manor-and-farm past and today's large farms shape the working year.
- **Kurzeme coast, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check port, fishing and transit work against inland farming.
- **Livonian Coast:** check the fishing round and the Soviet border-zone limits on it from Livonian sources.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check smallholding, factory and border-trade work, and the Catholic holy-day rest, in Latgalian sources.
- **Sēlija and the Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether their records keep a separate work and feast calendar.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Soviet-era industrial work and Old Believer rules on rest days, on their own sources.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check seasonal and settled labor histories, and Ukrainian arrivals in the labor market since 2022.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar workshops in Riga and the Latgale towns, and Sabbath rest.
- **Roma:** check community-led accounts of livelihoods rather than outsiders' labels.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether their calendar marks days of work or rest of its own.
