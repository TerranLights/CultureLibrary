# Latvia: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar Jewish trades. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's service and office workers against Zemgale farm workers:** check whether praise of hard work names different tasks and bodies.
- **Jelgava and Daugavpils plant workers:** check how Soviet-era factory memories differ by home language and generation.
- **Kurzeme fishers and the Livonian Coast:** check what the kolkhoz years did to a household-based fishing trade.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils, villages):** check the weight of seasonal and cross-border work, and of Latgalians leaving for Riga or abroad.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley (Cēsis):** check tourism and forestry work against farm work.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga and Sēlija:** check whether their local histories record trades or work customs apart from neighbors.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check work histories in their own press, including the language requirements for jobs as each side tells it.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Lithuanians and Ukrainians:** check border-zone and migrant work, including Ukrainians hired since 2022.
- **The Jewish community:** check the prewar trades and the Bund against the Riga merchant world.
- **Roma:** check how Roma organizations describe employment barriers, without taking outsiders' labels as facts.
- **Platform workers:** check who they are by home language, citizenship and district.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for injury, labor-force and enterprise data; mixed for attitudes to effort; thin for the porters' brotherhoods outside German-language archives.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German and German for the Riga brotherhoods; Russian for Soviet plant papers; Yiddish for Bund materials; Latgalian for Latgale's own work memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet-era migration into factories feeds the contested citizenship debate; workers' memoirs of the plan years can carry nostalgia or grievance. Keep each as the teller's account.
- **Held for review:** none; employment and strike law lies with Libra, and no pointer for the work concept needed setting aside.
