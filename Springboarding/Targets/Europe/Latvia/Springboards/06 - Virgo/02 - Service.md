*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against small towns in Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check whether the served-customer culture of the capital differs from service as neighborly help in towns.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils):** check how far Latgale sent maids and farmhands to other regions, as Latgalian sources tell it, and Catholic church service roles.
- **Kurzeme, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check port and naval service traditions, including Liepāja's Karosta navy town.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti:** check their own records of serving manors and the church before 1940.
- **Sēlija and the Gauja valley (Cēsis):** check local memory of service in the 1919 battles near Cēsis.
- **Russian speakers:** check how Soviet-era military and service-sector work is remembered in Russian-language sources, and how the service language rules are told.
- **Old Believers:** check unpaid service roles in priestless congregations through their own councils.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians and Ukrainians:** check parish and community service roles, and Ukrainian refugees as both served and serving.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar welfare and burial service bodies and the community's revived ones.
- **Roma:** check service jobs and mediators from Roma organizations' own reports.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether any role in their congregations is framed as service.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military service and Soviet memoir; mixed for volunteering; thin for domestic service after 1945 and for Roma and Livonian service histories.
- **Languages needed:** German for the servant regulations and the deaconess house; Russian for Intourist, *sadale* and veterans' material; Yiddish and Hebrew for burial-society records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Riflemen and the *Aizsargi* carry rival memories; Soviet-army service, the 16 March Legion commemoration and service under occupying powers are contested ground. Present each as the account of the group that gives it.
- **Held for review:** none; service law and military-duty law belong to Libra and Capricorn, so the pointers stay on practice and memory.
