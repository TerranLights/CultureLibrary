## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Latgale against Riga:** check how older people's roles on smallholdings compare with the city pensioner's, using Latgalian and Riga sources apart.
- **Vidzeme and Zemgale farm households:** check which jobs fall to children and to the old in farm families today.
- **Kurzeme fishing villages and the Livonian Coast:** check what tasks shore families kept for the old and the young, from Livonian sources where they exist.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether community revival gives the old a named role as keepers of song and dress.
- **Sēlija and the Gauja valley:** check depopulated parishes, where who counts as needed may shift.
- **Russian speakers in Daugavpils and Riga:** check retirees' and disabled people's own accounts, in Russian-language media.
- **Old Believers:** check the household roles of the old in their congregations, from their own writings.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check how their community bodies keep elders active.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar vocational schooling against today's welfare work for the aged.
- **Roma:** check how Roma organizations speak of young people's training and work prospects.
- **Disabled people's own organizations:** check self-advocacy accounts against official categories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disability and pension statistics; mixed for attitudes to being needed; thin for how Livonian, Roma and Old Believer households parcel out tasks by age.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Literary Society; Russian for Soviet disability and vocational records; Yiddish and Hebrew for ORT materials; Latgalian for rural memoir.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disability, old age and the language of job ads are tender ground; give disabled people's and each language community's own accounts, and do not read official categories as their view.
- **Held for review:** none; end-of-life choice goes to Scorpio and disability law to Libra, so the sheet kept to roles and worth.
