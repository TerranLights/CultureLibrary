## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's research and university world against small towns:** check where analytic careers cluster and how the provinces speak of "clever" people who leave.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check its universities' own research and the region's exam results, read with its language mix in mind.
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether regional schools and math clubs produce olympiad winners in proportion.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check rural schools' math results and their teachers' accounts.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check whether Livonian sources keep their own counts and measures for fishing and weather.
- **The Suiti and Sēlija:** check whether local measures or tallies survive in their archives.
- **Russian speakers:** check Soviet-trained scientists' and engineers' careers after 1991, in their own accounts, and Russian-medium schools' results before the switch to Latvian.
- **Old Believers:** check how their communities regard secular schooling and their own book learning, from their own writers.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar yeshiva learning in Latgale against Riga's secular Jewish scientists.
- **Poles, Belarusians and Lithuanians:** check school-language data for their pupils.
- **Roma:** check schooling data and Roma groups' own views on how mathematics is taught to their children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for assessments, exams and science institutions; mixed for everyday reckoning; thin for pre-metric measures outside archives and for minority communities' own reasoning traditions.
- **Languages needed:** German for the assessment books and the Polytechnic; Russian for Soviet institutes; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Rogatchover's work; Latgalian for local measures.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** comparisons of school results by language feed the language-policy dispute; report gaps as data with the method stated, never as a trait of a group.
- **Held for review:** none; the scholars, archives and assessments chosen for reasoning and measurement raised no standing-rule issue.
