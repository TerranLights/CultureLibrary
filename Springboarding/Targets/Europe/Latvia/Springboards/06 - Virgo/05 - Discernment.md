*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's Russian and Latvian audiences:** check how each weighs news and which outlets each trusts, from surveys that split by home language.
- **Older people across regions:** check scam-victim data by age and municipality, and how seniors' groups train members.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check Catholic vocation discernment and the Latgalian press's own warnings on scams.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley, Zemgale:** check farm buyers' judgment of stock and land, from farm press.
- **Kurzeme coast and the Livonian Coast:** check sea-lore for reading weather and fish, from Livonian and fishing sources.
- **The Suiti and Sēlija:** check whether their folk song corpora carry their own advice on choosing partners.
- **Old Believers:** check how communities judge correct books and rites, from their own councils and writers.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check which media each relies on, and their own press on misinformation.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar Yiddish press advice columns against today's community media.
- **Roma:** check how Roma groups describe being judged on sight by others, as a matter of prejudice to document, never as a trait.
- **Dievturība followers:** check their teaching on judging right living by the folk songs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fraud and disinformation reports; mixed for taste; thin for how Livonian, Roma and Old Believer communities themselves frame good judgment.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Jesuit letters; Russian for slang and Russian-language media; German for prewar art-market records; Yiddish for the interwar press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disinformation research and bans on Russian channels are politically contested; present each as one side's account and keep prejudice studies apart from judgments of any group.
- **Held for review:** none; consumer and media law belong to Libra, so nothing in this sheet on telling true from false needed setting aside.
