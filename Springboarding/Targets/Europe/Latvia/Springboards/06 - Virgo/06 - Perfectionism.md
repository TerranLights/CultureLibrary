## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga's professional and academic families against rural Latgale:** check whether school-grade pressure is reported alike, using surveys that split by region.
- **Kurzeme (Kuldīga, Liepāja) and Zemgale (Jelgava):** check craft standards in historic town trades through local museums.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check folk-dance and choir groups' accounts of rehearsal standards outside Riga.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check how exact copying of drone song and dress is taught and judged.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check how revival groups handle correctness in Livonian speech and song.
- **Sēlija:** check whether regional dress reconstruction faces its own correctness debates.
- **Russian speakers:** check ballet, music and mathematics schools in Russian-language memory, and how Soviet-trained teachers describe standards.
- **Old Believers:** check the weight of exact ritual against change, from their own councils.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check community choirs' and schools' own standards.
- **The Jewish community:** check today's community schooling and prewar yeshiva standards from Jewish sources.
- **Roma:** check whether Roma groups report school-grade pressure or the opposite problem of low expectations set by others.
