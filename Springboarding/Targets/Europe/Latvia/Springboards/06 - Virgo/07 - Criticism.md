# Latvia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; German for the church visitations. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga newsrooms and regional towns:** check whether local papers in Kurzeme, Vidzeme and Zemgale criticize local leaders as freely as Riga's outlets criticize national ones.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how Latgalian voices frame criticism of the center, in Latgalian and Latvian.
- **Liepāja and Ventspils:** check press criticism of long-serving city bosses, as each side tells it.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti:** check whether their own media criticize state heritage policy.
- **Sēlija and Cēsis:** check local councils' public-meeting minutes for how residents criticize officials.
- **Russian speakers:** check criticism of the state in Russian-language outlets, and the state's criticism of those outlets, as two separate accounts.
- **Old Believers:** check how their councils handle internal criticism and dissent, from their own records.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check their community press for complaints about minority schooling and recognition.
- **The Jewish community:** check the community's own statements in public disputes over Holocaust memory and the 16 March commemoration.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations' criticism of schools and officials.
- **Workplaces:** check whether criticizing the boss differs between firms with Nordic, Russian or local owners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the press, television and Soviet records; mixed for workplace and family criticism; thin for Livonian, Roma and Old Believer forms of correction in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** German for the visitations; Russian for Party records and Russian-language press; Latgalian for regional complaint.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** media bans, the 16 March commemoration and the Holocaust are contested topics in which criticism itself is the dispute; report each side's criticism as its own account.
- **Held for review:** none; defamation and media law go to Libra, so no criticism pointer needed setting aside here.
