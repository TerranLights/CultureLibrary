## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale:** check life span, waiting times and doctor numbers, and how residents explain the gaps.
- **Kurzeme (Liepāja, Ventspils) and Zemgale:** check regional hospital networks and the closure of small hospitals as local people describe it.
- **Vidzeme and the Gauja valley:** check rural access to family doctors and the role of pharmacies.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti lands:** check remote coastal and rural access, and any local healing traditions in their own sources.
- **Sēlija:** check whether its thinly settled parishes face distinct care problems.
- **Men and women:** check health-seeking and drinking data by sex, region and age, without reading a trait into either.
- **Russian speakers:** check care in Russian, trust in doctors and vaccination uptake from Russian-language sources and surveys.
- **Old Believers:** check fasting rules and attitudes to medicine from their own communities.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check language access in clinics, including Ukrainian refugees' access since 2022.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar health societies and the community's current care for the aged.
- **Roma:** check Roma groups' reports on clinic access and vaccination outreach.
- **Dievturība followers:** check their teaching on the body, bathing and healing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for vital statistics and health-system data; mixed for folk illness belief; thin for Livonian and Roma health views in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** German and Latin for the St George's Hospital records; Russian for Soviet TB care; Yiddish for OSE papers; Latgalian for regional memoir.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, alcohol and the vaccination quarrel need careful framing; present the vaccination dispute as accounts from each side, and handle the suicide data under safe-reporting practice.
- **Held for review:** none; patient-rights law goes to Libra and death and dying to Scorpio, so the health pointers stayed on keeping well and getting care.
