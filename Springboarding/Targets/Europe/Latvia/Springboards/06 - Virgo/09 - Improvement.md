*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; German for the Baltic German reform societies; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Jewish Enlightenment in Courland. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale:** check who gains from EU-funded "improvement" and how each region describes progress or neglect.
- **Zemgale and Vidzeme farm districts:** check drainage, consolidation of fields and farmers' own accounts of what was gained or lost.
- **Kurzeme coast and the Livonian Coast:** check how Livonian sources view state and EU projects for their villages and language.
- **The Daugava valley (Pļaviņas, Koknese) and Sēlija:** check memory of the flooded valley among those moved out.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether revival is framed as renewal or as keeping things unchanged.
- **Cēsis and the Gauja valley:** check heritage restoration against new building.
- **Russian speakers in microdistricts:** check housing renovation and self-development culture in Russian-language sources.
- **Old Believers:** check how their communities weigh change against keeping the old ways, from their own writers.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check community schooling and adult learning in their own organizations.
- **The Jewish community:** check the Haskalah's legacy against Orthodox currents, through Jewish historians.
- **Roma:** check how Roma organizations describe programs aimed at them, and whether they asked for them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for EU funds, housing and drainage records; mixed for self-improvement culture; thin for Livonian and Roma views on improvement aimed at them.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Economic Society; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Haskalah; Russian for Soviet drainage and dam records; Latgalian for regional views of development.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "improving" others carries the history of Baltic German tutelage and Soviet planning; Staburags and the drainage campaigns are contested memory. Present each as the account of those who give it.
- **Held for review:** none; planning and land law go to Libra, and the improvement pointers stay on aims, practice and memory.
