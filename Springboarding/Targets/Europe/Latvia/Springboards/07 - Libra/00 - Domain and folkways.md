## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against the rural regions:** check whether marriage and divorce figures split by town and country more than by region.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check Catholic and Orthodox marriage rites in Latgalian and Russian sources; Latgale lived under Russian imperial law until 1918.
- **Kurzeme, Zemgale and Vidzeme:** check the Courland statutes against Livonia's Swedish and imperial layers as sources of local legal habit.
- **Suiti of Alsunga and Sēlija:** check for a wedding order and in-law customs of their own.
- **Livonians of the coast:** check marriage and dispute material in Livonian sources, not only in neighbors' accounts.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check priestless marriage rules and Soviet civil weddings in their own publications.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check mixed marriages in the Daugavpils and border-town registries.
- **The Jewish community and Roma:** check each one's own ways of marrying and settling quarrels, in its own sources.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether they publish a wedding rite of their own.
