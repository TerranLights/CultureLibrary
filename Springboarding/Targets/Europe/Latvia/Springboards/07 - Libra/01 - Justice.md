*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how court language and the imperial-law past bear on justice as people there describe it, in Latgalian and Russian sources.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check the capital's caseload and its high-profile trials against regional courts.
- **Kurzeme (Liepāja, Ventspils), Vidzeme (Cēsis, the Gauja valley), Zemgale and Sēlija:** check the township-court series region by region for differences in what was judged.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti:** check whether their own records keep accounts of local wrongs and their settling.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check their press for how trials touching 1940-91 are reported, since readings divide.
- **Families of the deported:** check their associations' accounts of redress and of what remains unanswered.
- **The Jewish community:** check its own record of trials, compensation and memorials, Rumbula above all.
- **Roma:** check community sources for any internal tribunal; outside accounts are few, so mark gaps.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check how they figure in court and rights complaints.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for codes, court statistics and the trials of the occupation years; mixed for the township courts (much unedited); thin for Roma and Livonian ways of answering wrongs.
- **Languages needed:** German for the township-court protocols; Russian for the Soviet trials and Latgale's court users; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Dvinsk rabbis.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportations, the Holocaust, the partisans and the Kononov case are bitterly contested between communities and states; present each account as an account. Rehabilitation and prison files hold personal data with access limits.
- **Held for review:** none; justice draws on law, which this sign covers, so nothing here needed setting aside.
