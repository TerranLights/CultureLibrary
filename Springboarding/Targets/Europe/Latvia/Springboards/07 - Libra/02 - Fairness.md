# Latvia: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian, Livonian and the coastal Tamian speech, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish; Low German for Riga's market records. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale against Riga and Pierīga:** check whether talk of a fair regional share runs differently in Daugavpils and Rēzekne papers than in the capital's.
- **Kurzeme, Zemgale and Vidzeme farm districts:** check whether one heir or several take the family farm.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti lands:** check crew shares and common fishing and grazing rules in their own records.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check how each describes the pension and privatization rules, in Russian-language and Latvian-language press side by side.
- **Old Believers of Latgale:** check community rules on dividing goods and on inheritance in their own publications.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check how fair treatment in work and services is discussed in their community media.
- **The repressed and their families:** check how the extra privatization share for them was argued and received.
- **Roma:** check community-led reports on equal treatment in housing and schooling; such sources are few.
- **The Jewish community:** check how restitution and compensation were weighed as fair by the community itself.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tax, consumer and pension sources and folk tales; mixed for everyday splitting customs, which need oral and online material; thin for Livonian and Roma practice in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Low German and Latin for the Riga market records; Russian for the non-citizen pension debate; Latgalian for the regional press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the privatization and pension rules are sharp grievances for many Russian speakers and are defended by others; give each side as an account and keep 'non-citizen' as the legal term.
- **Held for review:** none; fair-treatment sources here are civil records and folklore with no standing-rule problem.
