# Latvia: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and High Latvian, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia (and Church Slavonic for Old Believer rites); Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the interwar press; German for the Livonian estates. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check whether registered partnership and cohabitation cluster in the capital, and why respondents say they do or do not marry.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check Catholic, Orthodox and Old Believer rules on marriage and godparenthood side by side.
- **Vidzeme and Cēsis:** check the Moravian legacy against Lutheran parish practice.
- **Kurzeme, Zemgale and Sēlija:** check farm cooperatives and family-farm partnerships between spouses and siblings.
- **Suiti of Alsunga:** check godparent and in-law ties in a Catholic enclave within Lutheran Kurzeme.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check intermarriage with Latvians and how Livonian families record it; sources are few, so start with family histories.
- **Russian speakers:** check how *гражданский брак* is used and how mixed couples describe language at home.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check godparent (*kum*) ties and cross-community marriage in Daugavpils.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar matchmaking against today's small community's practice.
- **Roma:** check community-led accounts of how marriages are arranged; outside accounts are few and often biased.
- **Same-sex couples:** check the testimony gathered by their organizations before and after 2024.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, statistics and folk song; mixed for business partnerships; thin for Roma and Livonian partnership customs.
- **Languages needed:** German for the diet records; Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer rules; Yiddish for *Frimorgn*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex partnership and the 2005 marriage clause are fiercely argued; give the campaigns for and against as accounts. Mixed-marriage data can be read as a political measure; keep it a demographic question.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; partnership law here is the letter of the statutes, which belongs to this sign.

## 5. First queries
1. On *likumi.lv*, read the Notariate Law chapter on partnership registration.
2. Pull CSP tables on births outside marriage by region since 1990.

4. Find a library copy of *Akten und Recesse der livländischen Ständetage* and list the treaty-like recesses.
5. Search *periodika.lv* for "Segodnya" marriage notices in one year, for example 1930.
