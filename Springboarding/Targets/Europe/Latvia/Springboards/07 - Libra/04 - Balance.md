## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against the regions:** check whether talk of work-life balance carries to farm and small-town households.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how a three-language life is described from inside, in Latgalian, Russian and Latvian sources.
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether the Courland constitutional past shows in local memory.
- **Vidzeme, Cēsis and the Gauja valley:** check conservation against farming and forestry around the national park.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti:** check how fishing limits and nature protection are weighed by coastal families.
- **Sēlija:** check whether it claims a place of its own in symbols that leave it out.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check how integration policy reads from their side, and the Old Believer fast-and-feast year as a rhythm of its own.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians, Lithuanians:** check how each community's organizations place themselves between Latvian and Russian.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how they reconstruct a year of balanced halves, and the critics of that reconstruction.
- **The Jewish community and Roma:** check each one's own place in integration policy as it describes it.
