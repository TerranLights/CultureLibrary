*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the interwar Jewish deputies' world; Latin for the thirteenth-century chronicle. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ventspils, Liepāja and Kurzeme:** check whether port-city politics produced its own debates on favor and office, from local sources on both sides.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check city-hall procurement and hiring cases, and how the capital's press reports them.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how town governments and courts are judged for fairness in Latgalian and Russian media.
- **Small towns of Vidzeme, Zemgale and Sēlija:** check how recusal works where officials know everyone.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti lands:** check whether these small communities report particular strains between kin ties and office.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check their experience of language rules in hiring and of public media's balance, in their own outlets.
- **Old Believers and the Jewish community:** check each one's history of seeking an intercessor, and its own account of equal treatment today.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check complaints of unequal treatment raised by their organizations.
- **Roma:** check community-led reports of bias in schools, police and hiring; such sources are few.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for anticorruption law, KNAB and GRECO material; mixed for everyday favoritism, which needs surveys and the press; thin for Livonian, Suiti and Roma sources.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Henry's chronicle; Russian for the minority press on language rules and public media; Yiddish and Hebrew for Dubin's community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Lembergs case, the language rules for office and public media's language choices are contested; give each side as an account. KNAB case files hold personal data.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; the officials, trials and bodies here are all Latvian public matters within the rules.
