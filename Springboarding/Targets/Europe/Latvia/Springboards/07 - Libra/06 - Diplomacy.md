# Latvia: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish; Latin, Low German and Old Russian for the medieval treaties; English and French for the interwar and exile files. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne) and the former Abrene district's people:** check how the border treaty and cross-border ties are discussed in Latgalian and Russian sources.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check where foreign envoys, ministries and forums sit, and how capital opinion on dealing with Russia compares with the regions'.
- **Kurzeme (Skrunda, Liepāja, Ventspils):** check local accounts of the radar's removal and of the Soviet naval base's departure.
- **Vidzeme, Zemgale and Sēlija:** check local memory of dealing with neighbors across the border.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check Livonian contacts with Estonian and Finnish kin bodies as diplomacy of a small people.
- **The Suiti:** check how their community deals with the state over heritage status.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check their accounts of language switching and of Latvian-Russian relations, in their own media.
- **Old Believers, Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check how each community's leaders deal with the state and with its kin state.
- **The Jewish community:** check how it has dealt with the state on memory and compensation.
- **Roma:** check community mediators as go-betweens with schools and offices; sources are few.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and its archives; mixed for everyday tact, which needs language surveys and conduct books; thin for Livonian, Suiti and Roma dealings in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin, Low German and Old Russian for the 1229 and 1230 treaties; Russian for the 1920 and 1994 negotiations; English and French for the exile legations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the border treaty, the 2005 Moscow visit and Latvian-Russian relations are read in opposed ways; give each as an account. Some Foreign Ministry files are still closed.
- **Held for review:** none; diplomacy here runs through state archives and public bodies that raised no standing-rule question.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the National Library catalog for "Meierovics de iure atzīšana" and list the editions of his papers.

4. Read the Constitutional Court's 2007 border-treaty judgment and its summary of the parties' arguments.
5. Search *periodika.lv* for "Rīgas miera līgums" in the 1920 press.
