# Latvia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish; Latin for the thirteenth-century chronicle. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check how city-council deadlocks are reported against regional towns' decisions.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check the Catholic and Orthodox practice of seeking a priest's counsel before a choice, and family decisions about leaving for work abroad.
- **Kurzeme and the Livonian Coast:** check how coastal families told the choice to stay or leave under the Soviet border zone.
- **Vidzeme, Zemgale and Sēlija:** check farm-succession decisions put off between generations.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether community choices on heritage status were reached by consensus or vote.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check how people explain putting off or declining naturalization, in their own words and media.
- **Old Believers:** check the role of the community's elder (*наставник*, *nastavnik*) in personal decisions.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check how Ukrainian arrivals since 2022 describe the choice to stay or return.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivors' and emigrants' accounts of the choice to leave in 1940-41 or the 1970s.
- **Roma:** check whose voice decides family matters, from community sources only; written material is scarce.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public decisions, statutes and folk custom; mixed for personal indecision, which needs oral history and forums; thin for Livonian, Suiti and Roma practice.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Henry's chronicle; Russian for the Old Believer and Russian-speaking accounts; German for exile-era memoirs written in DP camps.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 flight, emigration and naturalization choices carry grief and political charge; give each account as its teller's. Oral-history interviews may have access terms.
- **Held for review:** none; deciding, delaying and default rules came from law, public record and folklore, with nothing that called for holding.
