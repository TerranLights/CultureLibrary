# Latvia: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar and wartime record; German for the seventeenth-century trial records. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself, and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check how nicknames and jokes about Latgalians circulate, and how Latgalian writers and media turn them around.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check whether national tensions are blamed on the capital or the capital blames the regions, in each one's press.
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale, with Liepāja and Ventspils:** check images of the Baltic German manor in local tales and museum displays.
- **Vidzeme, Cēsis and Sēlija:** check whether the Thiess case and other trial records show regional patterns of accusation.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti:** check how each small community has been pictured by neighbors, and its own replies.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check how they are cast in Latvian-language media and how they cast Latvians, in their own outlets.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Lithuanians, Ukrainians:** check whether any has been made a scapegoat in a crisis, from their own organizations.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivor testimony and historians' work on 1941 propaganda and its aftermath.
- **Roma:** check press images and community responses; outside sources dominate, so mark gaps.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how they have been labeled by churches and the press, and how they respond.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for propaganda, media studies and folklore; mixed for everyday blame in couples and workplaces; thin for Roma, Suiti and Livonian sources in their own voice.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Thiess trial; Russian for the Russian-language media and Old Believer sources; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish community records of 1941.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1941 propaganda, the Holocaust, regional slurs and Russian-Latvian media wars are painful and contested; quote no slur beyond its search form, and give each account as an account. Hate-speech case files may name private people.
- **Held for review:** none; every scapegoating and propaganda source above is Latvian, Jewish-community or Baltic material that the rules leave open.
