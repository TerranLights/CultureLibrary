## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme and Vidzeme parishes beside Latgale's:** check which calendar of the dead each follows (*Svecīšu vakars*, *Dvēseļu dīna*) and where roadside crosses stand.
- **Riga and Pierīga against farm districts of Zemgale and Sēlija:** check where funerals moved from home to funeral hall, and where cremation gained ground.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their Catholic burial and mourning order inside Lutheran Kurzeme.
- **Livonians of the coast:** check coastal grave sites and drowned-sailor memory in Livonian, not neighbors', sources.
- **Russian-speaking Orthodox and Old Believer congregations:** check *Radonitsa* and memorial meals in their own parish publications.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check each church's All Souls customs in Daugavpils and the border parishes.
- **The Jewish community:** check memorial practice for Rumbula, Šķēde and the Latgale towns through its own bodies.
- **Roma:** check funeral and memorial customs from Roma-led sources; outside descriptions dominate and need care.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether they publish their own rite of seeing off the dead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk song, parish records and mortality figures; mixed for sex ways and money inside couples; thin for Livonian and Roma death customs in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** German for Einhorn and the older parish books; Russian for Soviet files and Old Believer writing; Yiddish and Hebrew for memorial books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportations, the Holocaust and the 16 March Legion day are contested; give each as an account. Opened KGB files name living people.
