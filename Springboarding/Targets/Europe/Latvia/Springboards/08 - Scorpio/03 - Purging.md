*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale's Catholic and Orthodox parishes against Lutheran Kurzeme:** check whether Lent, confession and fasting rules are kept and how strictly, in each church's own guidance.
- **The Livonian Coast and Kurzeme fishing villages:** check cleansing customs after a death at sea or a bad catch.
- **The Suiti:** check whether their calendar holds distinctive rites of cleansing before feasts.
- **Riga and Pierīga against Vidzeme farm districts:** check the bathhouse as a working household space against the paid sauna business.
- **Russian speakers:** check views of the monument removals and channel bans in their own press, as accounts beside the government's.
- **Old Believers:** check rules on ritual purity of food and dishes, from their own publications.
- **The Jewish community:** check the mikveh's history in Riga and Daugavpils, and how 1915 and 1941 are remembered as removals.
- **Roma:** check purity rules (*marime*) only from Roma-led sources; sources are scarce and outside descriptions often distort.
- **Dievturība followers:** check whether fire or water rites of cleansing appear in their published rituals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for purges, charms and bathhouse lore; mixed for corruption and lustration; thin for personal fasting and confession practice.
- **Languages needed:** German for the werewolf and witch trials, Russian for NKVD and party-purge records, Yiddish for the 1915 expulsion accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lustration, monument removals and channel blocks are contested ground; present each side's account. The 1937-38 shootings and 1941 killings involve named victims and perpetrators. Roma purity rules are community matters, not to be reported from outsiders. Political purging links to power (Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none; the purge and cleansing sources used are Latvian, Soviet, church and civic, with nothing to hold back.
