*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Livonians and Vidzeme's Turaida area:** check how Caupo is told in Livonian community writing against Latvian school texts.
- **Latgale:** check whether 1920s-30s charges of disloyalty against Latgalian activists appear, and how Latgalian writers answer them.
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check memory of the 1919 Landeswehr and the Niedra cabinet, centered on Liepāja and Jelgava.
- **Russian speakers:** check how "traitor" is applied to and by Russian-speaking residents after 2022, in their own outlets.
- **Old Believers:** check whether their own histories frame the 17th-century schism as faithfulness kept rather than broken.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivor testimony on neighbors who betrayed or hid them, as testimony.
- **Roma:** check wartime denunciations and rescues in Roma testimony; sources are few, start with community archives.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians:** check how exiles in Riga treat those who stayed or served the other side.
- **Former Legion soldiers' and Soviet veterans' families:** check how each side's "traitor to the homeland" labels are remembered.

## 4. Flags
