# Latvia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Yiddish where prewar Jewish sources speak of fate; German for the manor and serfdom records. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-discipline is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale's small towns:** check whether control of household money and time differs by town size more than by language.
- **Zemgale and Vidzeme farm districts:** check how memories of manor and kolkhoz control are told by families who farm the same land.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check the Soviet border-zone controls on fishing and movement, recalled by Livonian families.
- **Latgale's Old Believers:** check how far a priestless congregation's spiritual guide (*наставник*) directs fasting, marriage and daily rules, from their own writing.
- **Russian speakers:** check accounts of language inspections and citizenship rules as control, beside the state's own account.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Daugavpils:** check how Polish- and Belarusian-language schools and associations dealt with Soviet and later rules.
- **The Suiti:** check how parish and family elders steered marriage and land in their Catholic enclave.
- **The Jewish community:** check how *bashert* and providence appear in prewar Riga and Daugavpils writing.
- **Roma:** check internal community authority and outside policing from Roma-led accounts; scarce, so start with Roma NGOs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for serfdom, Soviet controls and the Daugava story; mixed for partner control (surveys exist); thin for everyday control in families outside forums.
- **Languages needed:** German for Merkel and the manor records; Russian for Glavlit and kolkhoz files and for the inspected workers' side; Polish for Daugavpils associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language inspections and the Istanbul Convention fight are contested; present each side as its own account. Partner-violence data concern survivors. Power as a concept is section 9; self-discipline is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none; the fate songs, archives, surveys and regulators here gave no item to set aside.
