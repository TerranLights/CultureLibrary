# Latvia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Ukrainian for the newest residents; Polish and Lithuanian along the southern border; Yiddish for prewar trading and credit. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed (loyalty is researched here for now).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against Latgale's municipalities (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza, Krāslava):** check gaps in vaccine uptake and trust in parliament by place, beside language.
- **Russian speakers, citizens and 'non-citizens':** check each group's trust in state bodies separately, in their own surveys and press, given contested citizenship rules.
- **Old Believers of Latgale:** check how far business and marriage stay within the congregation, from their own accounts.
- **Livonian descendants:** check trust in state heritage bodies after decades of border-zone restrictions on the coast.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether community heritage work runs through local trust networks or state programs.
- **Ukrainians since 2022:** check trust in Latvian institutions as newcomers report it.
- **Lithuanians and Poles of the southern border:** check cross-border trading and credit, past and present.
- **The Jewish community:** check how trust in neighbors after 1941 is spoken of in testimony and community statements.
- **Roma:** check trust in police and schools from Roma-led surveys; data are scarce, so start with Roma NGOs and ombudsman reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional trust (polls, Eurobarometer); mixed for trust between groups; thin for handshake dealings and godparent bonds today.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Russian-language trust surveys and scam warnings; German for Stender's context; Ukrainian for newcomers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** non-citizen status and trust across language lines are contested ground; treat each finding later as an account, not a verdict on a group. Trust in police and courts is researched under Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; polls, banks, proverbs and fairs supplied every pointer here, with no case for holding.
