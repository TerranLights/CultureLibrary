# Latvia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects, Latgalian, Livonian, the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian of Daugavpils; Ukrainian; Yiddish and Hebrew for postwar Jewish testimony; German for the 1905-06 reprisals and the student corporations. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; with forgiveness, amends and letting go as the alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme and Vidzeme manor districts:** check which parishes saw 1905 burnings and reprisals, and how local memory of them differs.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Ludza, Daugavpils):** check whether 1905 and the postwar partisan years are told differently under a Catholic and Polish-landlord past.
- **Riga against small towns:** check where 1990s gang revenge was concentrated and how it entered local talk.
- **Russian speakers:** check Russian-language memory of the 1919 reprisals and the 1944-56 years, as their own account.
- **The Jewish community:** check how the 1965 killing of Herberts Cukurs in Montevideo is spoken of, as justice or revenge, in Jewish and Latvian sources.
- **Livonian fishing families:** check coastal feud lore, if any, in Livonian sources; expect little.
- **The Suiti and the Old Believers:** check whether each keeps community ways of settling wrongs before they turn to payback.
- **Roma:** check how quarrels between families are settled, using only what Roma organizations and authors publish; little exists, so begin with Roma NGOs in Ventspils, Talsi and Jelgava.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians since 2020-22:** check how anger at the war's perpetrators is voiced from Latvia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1905, 1919 and 1941 as history; mixed for everyday grudges; thin for feuds between families today.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Baltic German accounts of 1905-06 and 1919; Russian for punitive and partisan files; Hebrew and Yiddish for postwar Jewish testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1941 retaliation framing, the Forest Brothers and Cukurs are contested ground; give each as an account and never adopt a propaganda frame. Intimate-image abuse cases must not be reproduced. The state's punishment against private vengeance is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none; curses, reprisals and online harms here come from open folk, historical and civic sources, so none had to be held.
