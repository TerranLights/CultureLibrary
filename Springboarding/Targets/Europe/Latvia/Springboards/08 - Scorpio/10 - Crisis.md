*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme's coast and the Livonian villages:** check memory of 1915 and 1944 flights from the coast and of storms at sea.
- **Sēlija and the Daugava towns (Jēkabpils, Pļaviņas, Aizkraukle):** check how flood seasons enter local calendars and talk.
- **Latgale's border districts (Krāslava, Ludza, Daugavpils):** check how the 2021 emergency and the 2022 war are lived near Belarus and Russia.
- **Riga's Zolitūde and Imanta neighborhoods:** check how a single disaster is remembered by those who live beside its site.
- **Russian-speaking factory towns and districts:** check accounts of the 1990s job losses and of 2009 austerity, which may differ from Latvian-language ones.
- **Old Believers:** check whether the congregations kept their own relief for members in the 1990s.
- **Ukrainians since 2022:** check how the war's arrivals describe crisis and help received.
- **The Jewish community:** check accounts of 1915 expulsion and 1941 as crises, and the relief networks behind each.
- **Roma:** check how hardship in 2009 and in the pandemic reached Roma households; data are scarce, so begin with ombudsman and NGO reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 2009 crisis, wartime flights and disasters; mixed for floods and fires; thin for personal crises outside counseling data.
- **Languages needed:** Early New High German for Russow; Russian for 1990s factory memories and border-area voices; Ukrainian for war arrivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2009 austerity, the border fence and pushbacks, and Zolitūde's court cases are argued over; present each as an account. Crisis-line data are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none; disasters, surveys and crisis services here are public and domestic, so nothing called for holding.
