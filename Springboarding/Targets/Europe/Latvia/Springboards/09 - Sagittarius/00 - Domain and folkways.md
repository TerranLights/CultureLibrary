## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme Lutheran parishes beside Latgale's Catholic ones:** check whether the denominational map still follows the 1561-1772 line between Swedish-Lutheran and Polish-Catholic rule.
- **Riga and Pierīga against rural Sēlija and Zemgale:** check where congregations closed or grew after 1991.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check their Catholic parish life as a separate unit in Lutheran Kurzeme.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check church and chapel life in Livonian sources, not only in Lutheran parish records.
- **Russian speakers:** check Orthodox, Old Believer and unaffiliated Russian speakers separately; so that one language label is not read as one religious way.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Lithuanians and Ukrainians:** check each group's parish, worship language and jurisdiction.
- **The Jewish community:** check religious life before 1941 against today's congregations in Riga and Daugavpils.
- **Roma:** check church membership as Roma congregations report it.
- **Dievturi and other native-faith groups:** check numbers, rites and publications from the groups themselves.
- **Liepāja and Ventspils:** check the port towns' Baptist and free-church congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church history and folk religion; mixed for present practice, since returns are self-reported; thin for Roma religious life and Livonian-language Christianity.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Courland and Livonia church orders; Russian for Soviet atheist records and Old Believer writing; Polish for Latgale's Catholic past; Hebrew and Yiddish for rabbinic writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Soviet years, the Holocaust and the Orthodox Church's jurisdiction are contested ground; present each as an account. The words *iebraucēji* and *okupanti* for Soviet-era settlers are charged; quote them, never use them as labels. Law sits in Libra and Capricorn.
