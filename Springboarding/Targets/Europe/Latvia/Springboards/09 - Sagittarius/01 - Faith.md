# Latvia: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (Middle, Tamian and High Latvian speech); Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia, including Old Believer Church Slavonic usage; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Jewish community. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme's and Kurzeme's Lutherans against Latgale's Catholics:** check whether prayer, attendance and confirmation rates differ by confession or by town size.
- **Riga's congregations against those of Sēlija and the Zemgale plain:** check where faith life was rebuilt after 1991 and where buildings stayed empty.
- **Orthodox and Old Believer Russian speakers in Daugavpils and Rēzekne:** check practice in each body's own publications, as they are separate church bodies.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Latgale:** check language of the Mass and of private prayer, parish by parish.
- **The Suiti of Alsunga:** check whether their Catholic devotions follow Latgale's pattern or a Kurzeme one of their own.
- **Livonians:** check faith life through Livonian-language texts and the Mazirbe congregation's records.
- **The Jewish community:** check observance before 1941 and today separately, each through its own archives.
- **Baptists and Pentecostals in Liepāja, Ventspils and Riga:** check free-church faith language against the old Lutheran pattern.
- **The unaffiliated:** check what secular faith (in the nation, in science) looks like among people who list no religion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church history and hymnody; mixed for personal faith, which surveys measure only roughly; thin for Roma and Livonian believers.
- **Languages needed:** German for Fürecker's world and the old hymnals; Church Slavonic and Russian for Old Believer texts; Hebrew and Yiddish for Chabad and the town rabbis; Polish for Latgale's older Catholic print.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet persecution of clergy and believers is documented by churches and by the state archives, and accounts differ in emphasis; present each as told. Sloskāns's cause is a church process, not a settled verdict.
- **Held for review:** none; the hymnals, church press, saints' causes and surveys used here all fell clear of the holding rules.
