# Latvia: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in the Daugavpils area; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar Jewish towns. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Latgale against Riga and Pierīga:** check whether well-being and suicide measures diverge, and what local sources give as reasons, without assuming one cause.
- **Vidzeme's Gauja valley and Cēsis against the Zemgale grain plain:** check how place-based culture projects (festivals, restored manors) are presented as giving a town its point.
- **Kurzeme's port towns, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check accounts of meaning lost and rebuilt after the closure of Soviet-era industry and the naval base.
- **Russian-speaking workers of Daugavpils and Riga's former factory districts:** check life stories from their own interviews, not from outside commentary.
- **Livonians and the Suiti:** check how heritage revival is described as purpose by the communities themselves.
- **Latgalians:** check Latgalian-language writing on purpose separately from Latvian-language accounts of Latgale.
- **The Jewish community:** check prewar Yiddish writing apart from today's community voices.
- **Roma:** check for life stories in Roma-led projects; outside sources are few, so note gaps.
- **Older and younger generations:** check whether meaning-of-work items differ between those formed in the Soviet economy and those formed after 2004.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and essay; mixed for survey measures of meaning, which come mostly from European instruments; thin for Roma and Livonian voices.
- **Languages needed:** Latvian literary register for Ziedonis and Mauriņa; Russian for post-1991 minority writing; Yiddish for interwar literary circles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data must be handled without blaming a region or group; Rothko's center sits in debates about how a city presents its Jewish past. Meaning in death belongs mainly to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; the essayists, films and surveys chosen for meaning raised no case under the holding rules.
