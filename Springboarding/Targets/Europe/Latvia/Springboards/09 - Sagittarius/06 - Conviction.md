*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme's Gauja valley and Turaida:** check how the Rose legend is told locally and in school texts, and whether the telling has changed since Soviet times.
- **Riga against the regional towns:** check where petition signers and demonstrators come from, by municipality.
- **Latgale's Catholics, Poles and Belarusians:** check how clergy and lay people who held firm under Soviet rule are commemorated parish by parish.
- **Russian speakers:** check the 2012 vote and later civic action through the community's own organizations, not only through outside commentary.
- **Old Believers of Latgale:** check their own histories of refusing church reforms as a model of holding firm, in their terms.
- **Free churches in Liepāja, Ventspils and Riga:** check Baptist, Adventist and Jehovah's Witness records of refusal.
- **The Jewish community:** check movement memoirs of interwar Riga and Daugavpils against post-war Soviet-era activism.
- **Livonians:** check whether keeping the language alive is described as a conviction by its own speakers.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations' own campaigns; sources are few, so record the search.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet-era dissent and church persecution; mixed for present-day civic action; thin for Roma and Livonian activism.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for trial files and the 2012 campaigns; Hebrew and Yiddish for Zionist movement archives; English for émigré dissident publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2012 referendum, the deportations and the Legion day are contested; present each account as an account. Living dissidents and informers appear in files. Legal guilt is not this concept.
- **Held for review:** none; the dissident, church and youth-movement sources here sat clear of any holding rule.
