## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale against Riga:** check emigration rates and return plans by municipality, and what local studies give as reasons.
- **The Livonian Coast and Kurzeme's ports (Liepāja, Ventspils):** check seafaring families' records against inland farm parishes.
- **Vidzeme's Gauja valley:** check the history of domestic tourism around Cēsis and Sigulda against summer resort travel on the coast.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check the 19th-century movement of landless families toward Riga and Russia's interior.
- **Russian speakers:** check travel and emigration by citizenship status, with the non-citizen travel document as one factor; present each account as an account.
- **The Jewish community:** check emigrant-era letters and later Soviet-era exit stories through the community's own archives.
- **Roma:** check travel and settlement histories from Roma-led sources, without assuming any fixed way of life.
- **Belarusians, Poles and Ukrainians:** check cross-border kin visits and recent arrivals separately.
- **Old Believers:** check their memory of arrival and of pilgrimages to holy places across the eastern border.
- **The diaspora (US, Canada, Australia, UK, Ireland):** comparison only; check returnees' accounts inside Latvia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration statistics and port history; mixed for leisure travel before 1991; thin for Roma and Livonian journeys in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Soviet fleet and tourism records; Yiddish and German for emigrant-port papers; Portuguese for the Brazil settlement's sources; English for diaspora comparison.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1956 decree and the deportations are forced movement, not travel by choice, and need the communities' own framing; non-citizens' travel documents are contested ground. Short trips and commuting stay in Gemini.
- **Held for review:** none; ports, fleets, trails and migration studies here raised no question under the standing rules.
