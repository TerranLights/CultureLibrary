# Latvia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian in Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish for the prewar trading towns; German and Low German for Riga's port records. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against Kurzeme and Vidzeme towns:** check whether bribery-experience and scam reports differ between the capital and smaller places.
- **Latgale's market towns (Rēzekne, Daugavpils, Ludza):** check bribery and trust items by municipality, and local press coverage of honest and dishonest dealing.
- **Zemgale's former collective-farm districts:** check memories of collective property against those of Vidzeme's forest districts.
- **Russian-speaking and Latvian-speaking pupils:** check school-copying surveys by language of instruction before and after the move to Latvian-language schooling.
- **Older people in each language group:** check scam targeting and reporting by age.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar trade memoirs from Riga and Daugavpils for their own account of business ethics.
- **Livonians and the coast fishermen:** check catch-sharing and sale records for how honesty was secured among crews.
- **The Suiti and Latgalian Catholics:** check confession and church teaching on lying in their own parish materials.
- **Roma:** check Roma-authored accounts of being suspected and accused; outsiders' claims are not evidence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys and press exposés; mixed for everyday white lies; thin for Livonian and Roma sources.
- **Languages needed:** German and Low German for the port inspectors' records; Russian for Soviet memoirs and today's scam warnings; Yiddish for interwar trading-town memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** scandals name living people and some cases were contested in court; present each account as an account. Theft as crime belongs to Pisces, law to Libra and Capricorn. No group is to be described as honest or dishonest.
- **Held for review:** none; honesty pointers came from port, folklore, school, police and survey sources the rules do not restrict.
