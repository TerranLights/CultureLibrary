## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against Latgale and Sēlija:** check degree attainment and access to higher learning by municipality, and what local studies give as causes.
- **Zemgale (Jelgava):** check the Courland academy's legacy beside today's agricultural university in the same town.
- **Kurzeme's ports, Liepāja and Ventspils:** check the regional universities there and what they train people for.
- **Vidzeme's Cēsis and Valmiera:** check the teacher-training tradition and regional college today.
- **Russian speakers:** check accounts of the move to Latvian-language schooling from both school communities and parents; present each as an account.
- **Poles and Belarusians:** check the history of their own schools in Daugavpils, Krāslava and Riga, through their associations.
- **The Jewish community:** check the interwar Yiddish and Hebrew school networks against today's community school.
- **Livonians:** check how Livonian is taught today, and by whom, in their own reports.
- **Roma:** check access to secondary and higher learning in studies written with Roma organizations; data are scarce, so record the gap.
- **Old Believers:** check community schooling and literacy traditions in their own histories.
