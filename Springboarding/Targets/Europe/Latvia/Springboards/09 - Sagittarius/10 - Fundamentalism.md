# Latvia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian and its dialects; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia and Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Polish and Belarusian; Latvian Romani; Hebrew and Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale's Old Believer villages and towns (Rēzekne, Daugavpils, Krāslava):** check how communities describe holding to the old rite, in their own terms, and whether the second generation keeps the rules.
- **Riga's Russian-speaking charismatic congregations:** check their own teaching on scripture against Latvian-speaking free churches.
- **Kurzeme's Baptist parishes and Liepāja's free churches:** check the history of literal Bible reading against the Lutheran parish norm.
- **Lutheran Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check debates over confessional strictness inside the Lutheran church, with each side's statements.
- **Latgale's Catholics, Poles and Belarusians:** check traditionalist currents in their parishes, if any, from parish sources.
- **Dievturi:** check how they speak of purity of the native faith against folklorists' readings.
- **The Jewish community:** check how Orthodox and liberal bodies describe themselves; use self-descriptions only.
- **Muslims (Tatar and newer communities):** check the communities' own statements beside security reports.
- **Livonians and the Suiti:** check whether strictness or revival in their church life is described by members; data likely thin.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led congregations' own descriptions; outside labels are not evidence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Old Believer history and 1990s press debates; mixed for scholarly measures; thin for Muslim, Roma and Livonian voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer texts; Hebrew and Yiddish for Orthodox Jewish life; Tatar and Arabic for Muslim community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist", "sect" and "fanatic" are labels; quote them, never apply them. The Orthodox jurisdiction question, the morality amendments and *Pērkonkrusts* are contested ground; give each account. Law to Libra and Capricorn.
