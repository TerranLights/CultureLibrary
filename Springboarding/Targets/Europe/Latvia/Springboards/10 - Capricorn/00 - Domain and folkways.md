## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kurzeme and Zemgale (the old duchy) against Vidzeme:** check whether peasant obligation and rank differ along the 1561-1795 line.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza):** check Polish-Lithuanian and Catholic layers of authority, in Latgalian sources too.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check guild, council and corporate ladders apart from farm hierarchies.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti of Alsunga:** check each community's own heads, so neither is folded into a Kurzeme average.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Soviet-era migrants, older Russian families and Old Believer parishes as separate sources on legitimacy.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check which offices each community's associations and clergy recognize.
- **The Jewish community:** check rabbinic and lay authority before 1941 apart from today's congregations.
- **Roma:** check kin roles only through Roma-led sources.
- **Baltic Germans (to 1939):** check manor and church authority in their own records as well as in Latvian accounts.

## 4. Flags
