*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (with Kurzeme and High Latvian dialect forms in folk sources); Latgalian; Livonian; Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian of Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew; Swedish and German for the 17th-century church records. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme against Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether Swedish-era parish duties in Vidzeme left records unlike those of the ducal Lutheran church in the west.
- **Latgale:** check Catholic duties (fasts, pilgrimages, prayer books) in Latgalian, and how the press-ban years are remembered as a duty kept.
- **Families who served in different armies:** check Legion, Red Army and post-1991 service accounts as separate testimonies, without merging them.
- **Russian speakers:** check how 9 May and other duties to the dead are kept in Russian-language families, apart from official calendars.
- **Old Believers:** check fasting and burial duties in Pomor parish sources.
- **Poles and Belarusians:** check grave-tending and parish obligations in Daugavpils, Krāslava and Ludza.
- **Livonians and the Suiti:** check song and church sources for duties named in their own words.
- **The Jewish community:** check obligations of mourning and remembrance after Rumbula alongside prewar religious duties.
- **Roma:** check family obligations through Roma organizations; outside material is sparse.
- **Dievturi:** check duties to ancestors and festivals in the group's own publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church, military and civic duty; mixed for duties inside families; thin for Livonian and Roma sources.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and German for the 1686 church law and visitations; Russian for Red Army memoirs and Timurite material; Polish for the 1920 graves.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Legion, the Red Army corps and the naturalization pledge sit on contested ground; present each side's account as an account, and name each veterans' body by its own name.
- **Held for review:** nothing; this concept's pointers are church, army, profession and community sources with no item needing to be set aside.
