*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; Livonian; Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian of the Daugavpils area; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew; Latin and German for the chronicle and manor layers. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against rural Latgale and Sēlija:** check whether people place responsibility for hardship on the self or on the state differently where jobs are scarce.
- **Latgalians:** check Catholic teaching on confession and penance in Latgalian prayer books against secular accounts.
- **Russian speakers and non-citizens:** check how responsibility for the 1991 citizenship settlement is assigned in Russian-language and Latvian-language accounts, as accounts.
- **Old Believers:** check how a lay-led community shares upkeep of prayer houses and graves.
- **Poles, Belarusians and Lithuanians in the borderlands:** check local parish and association sources on caring for their own.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check who is said to carry the language now that no native speakers remain.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivor and community testimony on answerability for 1941 beside state statements.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations' accounts of the wartime murders and of later responsibility.
- **Men and women:** check who is reported as answering for household finances and care, as a measured question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political and corporate accountability; mixed for families; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the chronicle; Russian for Chernobyl and Soviet sources; Latgalian for Catholic penitential texts; Yiddish and Hebrew for survivor testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** local participation in the Holocaust, responsibility for the deportations and the non-citizen question are contested: present each account as an account. Child-protection records are confidential; use published statistics only.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the answerability sources found are public bodies, surveys and press, none affected by a standing rule.
