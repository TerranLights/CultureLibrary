# Latvia: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; Livonian; Russian of Latvia (much of the 1990s business world wrote in it); Polish and Belarusian of Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish and Hebrew; German for the early modern church and town. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga against Latgale, Sēlija and rural Vidzeme:** check how plans differ where jobs, schools and wages differ, using regional surveys.
- **Latvian speakers and Russian speakers:** check the 1990s business press and later career data in each language, and how state-language requirements for public posts enter those accounts.
- **Latgalians:** check whether moving to Riga or abroad is told as success or loss in Latgalian writing.
- **The Jewish community:** check professional and commercial careers before 1941 against the community's paths after 1991.
- **Poles, Belarusians, Ukrainians and Lithuanians:** check each community's own success stories in its associations' media.
- **Old Believers:** check merchant families of the past against today's parishioners, in their own publications.
- **Livonians and the Suiti:** check how leaving the coast or Alsunga for study is told in their own sources.
- **Roma:** check the first Roma graduates and professionals as they speak of their routes, through Roma organizations.
- **Men and women:** check career and provider expectations by gender in interviews and statistics, as a question.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for careers, business and sport; mixed for private aspiration; thin for Livonians and Roma.
- **Languages needed:** German for Reiters's era; Russian for Vācietis, the Red Army and the 1990s business press; Latgalian for regional writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Vācietis and other Red Army Latvians are contested figures: present Soviet and Latvian accounts as accounts. Avoid ranking communities by success.
- **Held for review:** nothing; ambition's sources here are biographies, schools, programs and press, none reached by a standing rule.
