## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme against Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check whether 19th-c. societies spread at the same pace on both sides of the old duchy line.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza):** check the village-to-farm change and Catholic parish life as separate roots of community.
- **Riga, Pierīga and Jūrmala:** check urban club, guild and apartment-block life apart from rural neighbors.
- **Liepāja and Ventspils:** check port-town clubs and crews.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti lands (Alsunga):** check each one's own gatherings and halls, kept apart from a Kurzeme average.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Soviet-era arrivals and old-settled congregations as two distinct sources.
- **Belarusians, Poles, Lithuanians and Ukrainians:** check older associations against the groups formed by Ukrainian arrivals after 2022.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar institutions apart from today's congregations and societies.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led associations as the source on their own ties.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how a small revived faith organizes its congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for associations, the national movement and survey data; mixed for neighborly custom; thin for Roma and Livonian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Low German for the guild statutes; Russian for Old Believer and Soviet-era records; Latgalian for village history; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "tolerance", integration and the non-citizen question are contested ground: give each account as an account and name each community by its own name. With no post-war lands entered in the tracker and no scope file drafted, the Aquarius sheets take today's Latvia as their ground; fixing its lands is left to the author.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the domain's sources are archives, registries and local press, and none met a standing rule.
