# Latvia: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; the Russian of Latvia; Yiddish and Russian for the imperial Jewish layer; Latvian Romani; Polish; Livonian. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is the open question.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale and Sēlija:** check whether the same income measures read differently where costs and jobs differ.
- **Kurzeme's port towns (Liepāja, Ventspils):** check the gap between port and hinterland incomes.
- **Women and men:** check pay, pensions and care time as separate questions, not one "gender gap".
- **Russian speakers, citizens and non-citizens:** check each status group's own surveys and associations, and keep them apart.
- **Latgalians:** check whether Latgalian speakers report unequal treatment of their language in schools and media.
- **Roma (Kurzeme and Latgale):** check Roma-led accounts of schooling and work beside outside reports.
- **The Jewish community:** check the imperial residence rules, the interwar years and the present as three different settings.
- **Old Believers, Poles and Belarusians:** check how each recalls its standing under the empire and under Latvian rule.
- **Livonians:** check how descendants discuss their standing as an autochthonous people.
- **Disabled people and older pensioners:** check their own organizations' accounts of access and income.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income, gender and regional statistics; mixed for attitudes; thin for Roma and for Livonian descendants.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Yiddish for the Pale and for Jewish emancipation; Russian for non-citizen sources; Latgalian for language-status debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** non-citizen status, the standing of Latgalian and minority schooling are contested: give each side's account as an account and never as fact about a group. What the anti-discrimination statutes say belongs to Libra and is left out here.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the equality sources are statistics, reports and histories, none touched by a standing rule.
