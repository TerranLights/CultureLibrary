# Latvia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (dainas and the Bible's older usage too); Latgalian; the Russian of Latvia; Livonian; Polish and Belarusian of Latgale; Yiddish; Latvian Romani. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Jūrmala against small towns in Kurzeme and Vidzeme:** check whether friendships form mainly at school, work or in clubs in each.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check friendship across Latgalian, Russian, Polish and Belarusian speakers in mixed towns.
- **Russian speakers and Latvian speakers:** check friendship networks in mixed and in separate schools.
- **Men and women:** check how each describes a close friend, without assuming a gap.
- **Soviet-era adults and those born after 1991:** check whether "friendship of peoples" memories color older people's accounts.
- **The Suiti and the Livonian Coast:** check whether friendships reach beyond a small community, and how.
- **The Jewish community:** check pre-1941 friendship memoirs apart from present-day accounts.
- **Old Believers:** check friendships within and outside the congregation.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led accounts of friendship with non-Roma; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and Soviet press; mixed for surveys; thin for minority friendship networks.
- **Languages needed:** older Latvian for Glück's Bible and the dainas; Russian for the Soviet friendship ideology and for mixed-school sources; Yiddish for interwar memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "friendship of peoples" and friendships broken by the deportations and the Holocaust are contested memory: present accounts as accounts. Children's albums are private; use museum or published examples.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the friendship sources are folk, film, veterans' and survey material, none under a standing rule.
