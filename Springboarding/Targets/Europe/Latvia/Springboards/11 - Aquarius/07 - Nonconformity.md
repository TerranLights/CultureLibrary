# Latvia: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; the Russian of Latvia (Old Believer and Soviet sources too); Latin and German for 17th-c. church writing; Polish; Livonian; Latvian Romani. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Liepāja:** check whether late-Soviet youth scenes were stronger in the capital or the port, and why.
- **Latgale's Old Believers:** check how congregations tell their own history of refusing reform.
- **Latgalians:** check writers and singers who kept to Latgalian against those who moved to standard Latvian.
- **Russian-speaking youth:** check Russian-language rock, punk and informal groups in Riga and Daugavpils apart from Latvian-language ones.
- **Dievturība followers:** check the Soviet-era private practice against the post-1990 public revival.
- **Gay, lesbian and trans people:** check their own organizations' and memoirs' accounts across Latvian and Russian speakers.
- **The Suiti and Livonians:** check whether holding to a small community's ways was seen as stubborn difference or ordinary life, in their own words.
- **Rural Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check how eccentrics and loners are spoken of in parish memory.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led accounts of pressure to settle or assimilate; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet repression, film and art history; mixed for youth scenes; thin for hippies, Roma and queer history before 1990.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and German for Einhorn; Russian for Old Believer history, psychiatric files and Russian-language scenes; Latgalian for literary sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Soviet psychiatric and repression files name living people; use published testimony. Queer oral histories need the teller's consent. The standing of sexual minorities is contested: present each side's account as an account.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the dissent, art and survey sources for this concept met no standing rule.
