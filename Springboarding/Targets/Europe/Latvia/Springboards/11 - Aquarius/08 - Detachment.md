# Latvia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian (for laments and village memory); the Russian of Latvia (for Old Believer laments and Russian-language poetry); Yiddish and Russian for survivors' testimony; Livonian; Polish. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms (calm, objectivity, equanimity) are prized and others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal) condemned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale and Sēlija against Kurzeme and Vidzeme:** check whether lament and open grief or quiet composure is the funeral custom in each, parish by parish.
- **Old Believers:** check their laments and funeral conduct in their own recordings.
- **Riga's Latvian and Russian speakers:** check whether each describes the other as distant, and record such claims only as claims.
- **Russian-speaking writers and artists:** check how Riga's Russian-language literature treats distance from both communities.
- **Soviet-era adults and post-1991 generations:** check "inner emigration" against later disengagement from politics.
- **Men and women:** check talk of feelings and help-seeking as questions, without presuming a pattern.
- **Rural hermits and newcomers in the Gauja valley:** check chosen solitude against isolation from depopulation.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivors' silence and its end, in testimony.
- **Livonians and the Suiti:** check whether outsiders' images of reserve match these communities' own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, film and media codes; mixed for political alienation; thin for hermits and for emotional norms measured by survey.
- **Languages needed:** Latgalian and Russian for laments; Russian for *Orbita* and for Russian-speaking views of distance; Yiddish and Russian for survivors' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** this concept invites national stereotypes ("cold", "reserved"); every such image here is an outsiders' or a meme's claim and must stay one. Survivors' testimony and burnout studies concern private grief; use published material.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation and retreat practice in Latvia's lands, held as a category only, since Buddhism is an open question for the author; none is named.
