# Latvia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; the Russian of Latvia, with Church Slavonic terms for Old Believer sources; Ukrainian for recent arrivals; Yiddish and Hebrew; Polish and Belarusian of Latgale; Livonian. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga against Latgale and Sēlija:** check whether people's expectations for the next five years differ where jobs and people are leaving.
- **Kurzeme's coast (Liepāja, Ventspils, the Livonian villages):** check hopes tied to the sea, ports and fishing.
- **Latgalians:** check Catholic hope and secular regional hopes as separate strands.
- **Old Believers:** check religious hope against everyday hopes for children and work.
- **Russian speakers and non-citizens:** check post-1991 hopes and disappointments in their own surveys and press, as accounts.
- **Families of the deported:** check how the hope of return was kept and how it ended.
- **The Jewish community:** check hopes of renewal after 1988 against interwar hopes.
- **Ukrainians:** check new arrivals' intentions separately from long-settled Ukrainians.
- **The unemployed and pensioners:** check hope and waiting in each group's own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for songs, surveys and the Singing Revolution; mixed for religious hope; thin for Livonian and Roma sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer teaching; Ukrainian for refugees' intentions; Latgalian for the press-ban generation; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish revival writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the deportations, the Holocaust and the hopes of 1991 for different communities are contested memory: present each account as an account. Children's medical appeals name minors; cite the appeals, not the families.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the hope pointers here are folk, survey, charity and community sources, and none fell under a standing rule.
