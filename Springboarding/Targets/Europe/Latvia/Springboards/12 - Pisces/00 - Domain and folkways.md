# Latvia: The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (Middle dialect, Tamian, High Latvian); Latgalian (*latgalīšu volūda*); Livonian (*līvõ kēļ*); the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian and Polish of Latgale and Daugavpils; Latvian Romani; Yiddish; German and Latin for older records. **Concept in one line:** the hidden and transcendence: how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (with magic ways as the folkway).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vidzeme and Kurzeme against Latgale:** check whether Lutheran and Catholic regions keep different public places for the unseen.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check how clinics and psychics change where city people take distress.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check its sea and fishing lore separately from inland belief.
- **The Suiti lands (Alsunga):** check this Catholic enclave in Lutheran Kurzeme on its own customs for sickness and death.
- **Sēlija and Zemgale:** check whether this borderland of faiths mixes charms and saints.
- **Russian speakers and Latgale's Old Believers:** check Soviet-era urban arrivals and old priestless congregations as two sources.
- **Belarusians and Poles in Daugavpils and Krāslava:** check their own healing and blessing customs.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led accounts; outside writing on Roma fortune-telling is no source on their beliefs.
- **The Jewish community:** check pre-1941 religious life in Daugavpils and Riga against today's congregations.
- **Dievturība followers:** check how a revived faith reads the soul from the *dainas*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** plentiful for legend and belief archives; patchy for distress measures; scant from Roma, Belarusian or Livonian speakers themselves.
- **Languages needed:** German and Latin for trial records; Russian for Soviet hospital files and the esoteric market; Latgalian for parish devotions; Yiddish for the Jewish record.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Holocaust, the 1941-42 killing of patients, the deportations and Soviet psychiatry are contested ground: set out each version as one community's account, using that community's own name for itself. On scope: the tracker lists no settled post-war lands and there is no scope file, so the Pisces sheets treat Latvia within its present borders; its lands are for the author to decide.
- **Held for review:** nothing; legends, court and hospital records and statistics gave no reason to set anything aside.
