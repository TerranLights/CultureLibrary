# Latvia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian (all three dialects); Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia; Polish and Belarusian of Latgale; Latvian Romani; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Ludza, Preiļi):** check Catholic giving at feasts and funerals against parish help in Lutheran regions.
- **Vidzeme, Kurzeme and Zemgale:** check how 19th-c. parish poorhouses shaped what villages still expect from the municipality.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check street begging, night shelters and charities, where most help is organized.
- **Liepāja and Daugavpils:** check two industrial cities hit by job loss after 1991, and who was pitied or blamed there.
- **The Suiti lands:** check how a small Catholic enclave helps its own members.
- **Russian speakers:** check Orthodox and Old Believer congregations as separate channels of help, and Russian-language appeals.
- **Ukrainian arrivals after 2022:** check how help offered to them was described, and by whom.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Daugavpils and Krāslava:** check their Catholic and Orthodox charities as their own.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar welfare against today's aid to elderly members.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led accounts of being helped or refused; outside reports alone are not enough.
- **Livonians:** check coastal mutual help in storms and at sea, if records exist.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** deep for charity, giving surveys and literature; uneven for burnout and animal care; sparse for Livonian, Roma and Belarusian help networks.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Orthodox charity; Yiddish and Hebrew for interwar Jewish welfare; Latgalian for Catholic feast customs; German for Stender's context.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pity can be felt as insult by those receiving it; record each group's own words for being helped. Mercy toward offenders touches law: leave sentencing and pardon to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing; charities, parishes and the press all sat within the rules.
