# Latvia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia; Polish; Latvian Sign Language; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latvian and Russian speakers:** check whether each side's press asks readers to feel the other's history (deportations, the 1990s) or only its own.
- **Mixed Latvian-Russian families:** check family memoirs for how a household holds both views.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne):** check whether Latgalians describe being understood by Riga, and in which language they say it.
- **Riga against small towns in Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check whether city services and rural neighbors read distress differently.
- **The Deaf community:** check Deaf-led sources on hearing people's guesses about them.
- **Older pensioners and the young:** check how the generations of Soviet and post-1991 schooling describe not being understood.
- **The Livonian Coast and the Suiti lands:** check whether tiny communities report being heard by outsiders, as they tell it.
- **Ukrainians after 2022 and Belarusians:** check how hosts and newcomers describe reading each other.
- **Roma:** check Roma mediators' and Roma-led reports on school staff reading Roma children.
- **The Jewish community:** check survivor testimony and how Latvian audiences were asked to hear it after 1991.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; little measurement in Latvian, much in theater, film and anthropology; thin for Roma, Livonian and Deaf sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the Russian-language press and comment threads; Latvian Sign Language through Deaf interpreters; Yiddish for older Jewish writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** empathy across the language line touches contested history (non-citizens, 1940-41, 1944-91): let each side speak in its own account, and use each community's own name for itself.
- **Held for review:** nothing; anthropology, stage, screen and helplines raised no rule.
