*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check festival, club and charismatic-church settings for losing oneself in a crowd.
- **Rural Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check what the end of the collective farms changed in village life, parish by parish.
- **Latgale (Daugavpils, Rēzekne, Ludza):** check where Latvian, Latgalian, Russian, Belarusian and Polish speech have merged in families, and what speakers call the mixture.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check the Livonian record of a language's end in its own revival's words.
- **The Suiti lands:** check whether Suiti identity is described as fading, holding or reviving, and by whom.
- **Russian speakers:** check separately the Soviet-era arrivals, their children schooled in Latvian, and the Old Believers.
- **Belarusians, Poles and Ukrainians:** check each for language shift toward Russian or Latvian.
- **The Jewish community:** check how Yiddish life ended and what of it is kept, in the community's own words.
- **Dievturība and Christian congregations:** check how each describes union with the divine or with nature.
- **The old and their carers:** check dementia talk in Latvian and Russian, by region.
