*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale against Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check whether the old Inflanty line still marks how people speak of "us" and "them".
- **Riga's old center and its suburbs:** check where historical walls and Soviet districts left lines of language and class.
- **Microdistrict residents:** check privacy and noise talk in Purvciems, Imanta and Daugavpils' blocks against single farmsteads.
- **Russian speakers:** check how the school change and the citizenship question are told as lines drawn around them, each account as an account.
- **Old Believers:** check purity and outsider rules in their own words, apart from Orthodox neighbors.
- **Livonians:** check how a people entered as Latvians in records drew their own line.
- **The Suiti lands:** check how a Catholic enclave kept its edge within Lutheran Kurzeme.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija border towns (Bauska, Jēkabpils):** check Lithuanian-Latvian daily crossings.
- **The Jewish community:** check the meaning of the Moscow Suburb and the ghetto line in community memory.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led sources on lines they keep and lines drawn around them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and language lines; mixed for household privacy; thin for personal-space measures and for Roma and Livonian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Old Believer rules and school memories; Latin (or Brundage's English) for the 1207 division; Lithuanian for the southern border; Latgalian for the Inflanty past.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** citizenship, the school language change and the ghetto are contested or painful ground: lay out each community's version as its account, under its own name. Border law and treaties belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing; folk, municipal and press leads fell inside every rule.
