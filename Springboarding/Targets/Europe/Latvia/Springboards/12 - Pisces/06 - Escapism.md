*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Latgale (Ludza, Krāslava, Balvi):** check home distilling and border contraband apart from Riga's bars.
- **Riga against small towns:** check where gambling halls cluster and who protests them.
- **Coastal Kurzeme and the Livonian Coast:** check ice fishing and sea trips as men's withdrawal.
- **Russian speakers:** check Russian-language entertainment and treatment services as separate channels.
- **The Soviet-era young of the 1970s-80s:** check rock, hippie and book-collecting memories against today's young.
- **Women and men:** check who is said to drink, gamble or game, and who is said to read or pray, and whether data bear it out.
- **Catholic Latgale and Lutheran Vidzeme:** check whether retreats and pilgrimages are offered as the blessed form of leaving.
- **Belarusians and Poles of Daugavpils:** check their own clubs and churches as places of respite.
- **Roma:** check Roma-led sources only on leisure and drink; outside reporting runs to stereotype.
- **The Jewish community:** check interwar Riga's Yiddish theater and press as places of escape.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alcohol and gambling figures; mixed for reading and gaming; little on Livonian, Roma or Belarusian leisure.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for treatment services and Russian-language entertainment; German for manor tavern records; Latgalian for local distilling lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drink and addiction invite group stereotypes: report variation, never a group's habit. Licensing law belongs to Libra.
