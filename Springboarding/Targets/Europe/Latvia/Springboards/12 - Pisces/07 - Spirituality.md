## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic Latgale (Aglona, Rēzekne, Preiļi):** check shrine, spring and roadside devotion apart from parish worship.
- **Lutheran Vidzeme and Kurzeme:** check where sacred feeling is placed outside church: trees, hills, songs.
- **Zemgale and Sēlija:** check Skaistkalne and other shrines in a mixed-faith border region.
- **Orthodox and Old Believer Russian speakers:** check the monastery, the icon corner and pilgrimage as separate traditions.
- **The Livonian Coast:** check the Livonians' own marking of sea and church.
- **The Suiti lands:** check Alsunga's Catholic devotions as a local form.
- **Dievturība followers and the folklore movement:** check how each describes the sacred in nature and song.
- **The Jewish community:** check prayer and study life in Riga and Daugavpils as members describe it.
- **Poles and Belarusians of Daugavpils and Krāslava:** check their own pilgrimages and parish feasts.
- **Riga's unaffiliated young:** check what they call sacred without a church, as a question to measure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church sites and folklore; mixed for survey measures; thin for Livonian practice and for the unaffiliated.
- **Languages needed:** Russian and Church Slavonic for Orthodox and Old Believer devotion; Latgalian for shrine lore; Latin for the *Terra Mariana* passages; Yiddish and Hebrew for Jewish prayer life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some sacred-site keepers and congregations limit access or photography; ask first. Soviet suppression of religion is contested in memory: present accounts as accounts.
