## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Kurzeme and Livonian coasts:** check sea sense among fishing families against inland farm lore.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Ludza, Krāslava):** check Catholic and Old Believer ways of testing a premonition.
- **Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check dowsers and hunters by district.
- **Riga:** check how professionals (nurses, traders, police) speak of the hunch against the checklist.
- **Russian speakers:** check psychic television audiences and the *чуйка* idiom as a separate record.
- **Women and men:** check who is credited with intuition in songs, magazines and clinics, and whether it differs by sex.
- **Older rural people and the young in Riga:** check whether premonition stories are told across generations.
- **The Suiti lands:** check local seers and healers in Alsunga's own records.
- **Roma:** check whom Roma families themselves turn to for advice, through Roma-led organizations; outsiders' reports are not data here.
- **The Jewish community:** check rabbinic and Hasidic accounts of inner knowing in pre-1941 Latgale, as the community records them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and occupational lore; sparse for measurement and clinical practice; scarce for Livonian and Roma knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for psychic media and Old Believer counsel; Latgalian for local seers; Livonian and Estonian for coastal lore; Yiddish for pre-1941 religious writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paid clairvoyance and healing claims are commercial claims: record them as beliefs and trades, never as evidence.
- **Held for review:** nothing; folklore, trades and nurse training stay within the rules.
