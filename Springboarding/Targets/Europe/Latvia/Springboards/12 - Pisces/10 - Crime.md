# Latvia: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia (with its criminal slang); Polish; Finnish and Estonian for Baltic smuggling records; Yiddish; German for old town records. **Concept in one line** (named by the author): what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it. The law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn; this sheet takes the idea of wrongdoing and the wrongdoer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check how a city district comes to be called dangerous, and who spreads the name.
- **Latgale's eastern border (Ludza, Zilupe, Krāslava):** check smuggling as livelihood against smuggling as crime, in local voices.
- **The Kurzeme and Livonian coasts:** check sea smuggling lore across the interwar and Soviet years.
- **Rural Vidzeme and Zemgale:** check poaching and timber theft as tolerated or condemned, by district.
- **Liepāja and Daugavpils:** check post-1991 job loss and how residents link it to wrongdoing, each account as an account.
- **Russian speakers:** check the Russian-language crime press and prison slang as separate sources, never as a trait.
- **Families of Legion veterans and of Red Army soldiers:** check how each names wartime wrongs in its own account, presenting both commemorations as accounts.
- **The Jewish community:** check how it records crimes done to it in 1941, in its own institutions.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations' answers to crime framing in the press.
- **Women and families:** check what was kept silent at home, through victims' own groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, folklore and victim surveys; mixed for public moral judgment; thin for Livonian, Polish and Roma voices.
- **Languages needed:** Russian, including prison and street slang; Finnish and Estonian for smuggling; German for old Riga punishment records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime coverage invites ethnic stereotyping: record variation and who frames whom, never a group trait. The Holocaust and Soviet deportations are contested ground: name each community by its own name. Criminal law, courts and sentencing belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing; folk, press and survey sources passed every rule.
