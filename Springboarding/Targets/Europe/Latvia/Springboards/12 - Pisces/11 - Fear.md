# Latvia: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Latvia, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Latvian; Latgalian; Livonian; the Russian of Latvia; Belarusian and Polish of Latgale; Yiddish; German for the early catechisms. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Kurzeme and Livonian coasts (Liepāja, Ventspils, Kolka):** check sea and storm fears in fishing families against inland villages.
- **Latgale (Rēzekne, Daugavpils, Ludza):** check fright cures and saints' protection in Catholic and Orthodox homes.
- **Riga and Pierīga:** check how the city's dangers (traffic, crime, war) are ranked in surveys and talk.
- **Vidzeme and Zemgale farms:** check thunder, wolf and fire fears in rural belief records.
- **Russian speakers:** check fears since 2022 in their own press, and the *бабайка* tradition in their childhood memoirs.
- **Old Believers:** check end-times teaching as the community states it, not as neighbors describe it.
- **Families of the deported and of the Holocaust's survivors:** check how fear passes down, in each family's own words.
- **The young and the old:** check anxiety measures by age against older generations' "we were not afraid" stories.
- **The Suiti lands:** check local charms against fright in Alsunga's records.
- **Roma and Belarusians:** check their own childhood fear figures, if recorded.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief and press; mixed for anxiety measures; little recorded from Livonian, Roma or Belarusian speakers on what frightens them.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the modern Russian-language record and Old Believer writing; German and early Latvian orthography for the 1586 catechism; Latgalian for fright cures.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** fears tied to war, deportation and the 2022 border with Russia are contested ground: present each community's fears as its own account, name each community by its own name, and never let a bullet say what a group is.
- **Held for review:** nothing; catechism, charm, press and drug-use leads were all clear of the rules.
