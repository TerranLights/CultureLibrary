*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitians:** check whether Samogitian is named as a language or a dialect in self-description; the Samogitian cultural movement treats this as an identity question.
- **Dzūkians and Suvalkians:** check regional self-names in local museum and festival materials against national ones.
- **Lithuania Minor (*lietuvininkai*) and Klaipėda:** check how a Lutheran, once-Prussian identity is remembered after the population changes of the 20th century.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check the range of self-labels (Polish, *tutejszy*, Lithuanian citizen of Polish identity); the Vilnius question is contested, so take each side's account.
- **Russians and Russian speakers:** separate Old Believers, interwar residents and Soviet-era arrivals (Visaginas); their sources differ.
- **Belarusians:** check whether census ethnicity, home language and church agree.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check how each community describes itself in its own publications, apart from outsiders' labels.
- **The Jewish community:** check the present community's sources beside the Litvak past; the Holocaust breaks the line, and the memory of it is contested.
- **Roma and Ukrainians:** check whether self-names in community sources differ from official ones.
- **Romuva:** check identity claims tied to Baltic faith; present as the movement's own account.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the national story; mixed for regional identity; thin for the Karaim, Tatars and Roma (community publications and a few specialists).
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, Polish, Russian, Belarusian, Yiddish; Ruthenian chancery language and Latin for chronicles; Karaim for the community's own writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Vilnius question, Karaim origin debates, and wartime memory are contested; name each community by its own name and present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** no identity source was set aside; the census and community self-labels are kept, with contested ones presented as accounts.
