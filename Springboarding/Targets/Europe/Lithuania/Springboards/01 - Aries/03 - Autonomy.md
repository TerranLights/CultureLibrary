## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm households across the ethnographic regions:** check who chose a spouse and an heir in Aukštaitija, Samogitia, Dzūkija and Suvalkija; inheritance customs differed by area.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** check the memory of interwar self-rule against Prussian and Lutheran parish records.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check collective autonomy claims separately from personal choice; the 1990-1991 episode is contested.
- **Old Believers:** check lay self-government in congregations around Zarasai, Utena and Vilnius.
- **The Jewish community:** check the old *kehile* records against the present community's own bodies.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim of Trakai:** check what records survive of their communal privileges and of how far each community set its own rules.
- **Catholic believers in the Soviet years:** separate open dissent, quiet practice and accommodation; each left different sources.
- **Women and men, and generations:** check who decides about marriage, study and moving abroad, by age cohort.
- **Romuva members:** check how a faith without a hierarchy decides its rites.
- **Roma:** check whether family decision-making is recorded by community members themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collective autonomy (archives, histories); mixed for personal choice (surveys, literature); thin for Karaim and Tatar communal records in accessible form.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, Polish, Russian; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Council of Lithuania; German for the Klaipėda Region.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** KGB files name informers and victims; access rules apply and use needs care. Abortion and family debates are live; present each voice. The Polish autonomy episode is contested. Legal texts go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the Klaipėda statute's legal text is routed to Libra rather than held.
