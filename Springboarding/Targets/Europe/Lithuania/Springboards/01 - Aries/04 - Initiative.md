## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Lithuania Minor:** check who carried books across the Prussian border; the smuggling routes ran through these areas, so their local museums hold the records.
- **Aukštaitija and Dzūkija:** check *talka* customs region by region; who could call one and what was owed back.
- **Suvalkija:** check the founders of the national press and the region's schools for where their projects started.
- **Kaunas and Vilnius:** compare start-up and cooperative records in the interwar capital with today's capital.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check community-run schools, choirs and parish projects as initiative inside a minority.
- **Russian speakers of Visaginas:** check what local projects followed the nuclear plant's closing, and who began them.
- **Old Believers and Belarusians:** check whether congregations and cultural societies record their own founders.
- **The Jewish community:** check charitable and free-loan societies of the prewar Litvak towns, and today's community projects.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check who started the restoration of mosques and the kenesa after 1990.
- **Women and men:** check founders' lists by sex across cooperatives, firms and volunteer groups.
