## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aukštaitija, Samogitia, Dzūkija, Suvalkija:** check which day opens the farm year in each region's records; the folklore archives sort customs by region.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** check prewar Lutheran customs against the postwar settlers' new ones; most old residents left, so two beginnings overlap.
- **Vilnius and the Vilnius region:** check how Poles and Lithuanians each tell the city's founding and its 20th-century changes of rule; contested accounts.
- **Visaginas and other Soviet-era towns:** check founding stories told by residents who came from across the USSR.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check each community's own arrival narrative against chronicle sources.
- **Old Believers:** check community histories of their first settlements in Lithuania after the church schism.
- **The Jewish community:** check rebuilding after the Holocaust and after 1990 in survivors' and newcomers' accounts.
- **Returning migrants and deportees:** separate those who came back from Siberia, from the West and from recent work abroad; each faced a different start.
- **Romuva and Catholic parishes:** compare the year's beginning in each calendar.
