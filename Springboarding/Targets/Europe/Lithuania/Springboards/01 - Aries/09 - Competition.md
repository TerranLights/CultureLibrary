## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kaunas and Vilnius:** check fan and media sources from both cities; the city rivalry is the obvious frame, and each side tells it differently.
- **Samogitia, Aukštaitija, Dzūkija, Suvalkija:** check folk games and evil-eye beliefs region by region in the folklore records.
- **Klaipėda:** check the port city's own clubs and sailing regattas against inland sport.
- **Polish- and Russian-language schools:** check exam and olympiad results by language of instruction; school politics makes these numbers contested.
- **Russian speakers of Visaginas:** check sport and school contests in a town shaped by one employer.
- **The Jewish community:** check yeshiva rank and learning contests in the prewar record, and present-day community sport.
- **Lithuanian Tatars, Karaim, Belarusians, Old Believers:** check whether community festivals include contests; sources are thin, so start with each community's press.
- **Roma:** check Roma participation in sport and school contests as reported by Roma organizations.
- **Women and men:** check women's basketball and other sport beside the men's game.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** check how diaspora clubs and games are reported back home.
