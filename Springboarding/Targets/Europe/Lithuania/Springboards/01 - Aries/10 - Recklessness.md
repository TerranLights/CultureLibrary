## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** check the temperance movement's reach parish by parish; its records are richest there because it started there.
- **Klaipėda and the Curonian Spit:** check fishers' memoirs and rescue logs, to see where people who work the sea and the lagoon draw the line between daring and folly.
- **Aukštaitija and Dzūkija lake and forest districts:** check summer drowning and mushroom-picking accidents in regional data.
- **Kaunas, Vilnius and smaller towns:** compare road and alcohol data by municipality rather than by region.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region and Russian speakers:** check minority-language media coverage of road safety and drinking, so one community's press does not stand for all.
- **The Jewish community:** check Yiddish literature and memoir for the *luftmentsh* and the gambler as Litvak figures.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check religious teaching on drink and gambling in each community's own sources.
- **Young men and young women:** check survey and injury data by sex and age before any claim about either.
- **Generations:** check late-Soviet and post-1990 youth for how risk was framed in each period.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public-health and road data; mixed for folklore of rashness; thin for minority religious teaching on risk.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, German (Rhesa's edition), Polish, Russian, Yiddish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** alcohol and suicide data invite national stereotypes; report variation by place, age and sex only. Gambling self-exclusion data are protected.
- **Held for review:** no risk, drink or gambling pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
