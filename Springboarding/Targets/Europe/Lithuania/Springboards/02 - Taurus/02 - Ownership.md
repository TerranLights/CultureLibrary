# Lithuania: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian, Polish of the Vilnius lands, Russian, Belarusian, Yiddish (prewar), and the Latin, Ruthenian and Polish of the old Grand Duchy's registers. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suvalkija (Sūduva):** compare farm inheritance with Žemaitija and Aukštaitija; its 19th-century history differs from the lands east of the Nemunas.
- **Klaipėda region:** check Prussian-era deeds and the post-1945 resettlement of farms whose owners had fled or been expelled.
- **Vilnius district and Šalčininkai:** check who returned land to whom after 1991, from Polish and Lithuanian sources side by side.
- **Visaginas and Russian speakers:** check flat privatization in a town built for a power plant.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check the old service-land villages near Vilnius and Alytus against current holdings.
- **Karaim:** check how the Trakai kenesa and community houses are held.
- **The Jewish community:** check how communal property is claimed and managed today, apart from prewar private property.
- **Old Believers:** check community ownership of prayer houses and cemeteries.
- **Roma:** check the Kirtimai settlement in Vilnius, where housing tenure and demolition were contested; give each side's account.
- **Garden-plot holders:** check the Soviet-era garden associations (*sodų bendrijos*) whose plots became private.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medieval and manor records, and for post-1990 data; strong but contested for Soviet confiscation; thin for minority holdings.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Polish; Russian; Latin and Ruthenian (Old Belarusian) for the Metrica; Yiddish and Hebrew for communal records; German for Klaipėda deeds.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restitution in the Vilnius region, Jewish communal property and the Šnipiškės cemetery are live disputes; present every side as an account. Property law itself (statutes, restitution laws, codes) belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no ownership pointer here needed setting aside.
