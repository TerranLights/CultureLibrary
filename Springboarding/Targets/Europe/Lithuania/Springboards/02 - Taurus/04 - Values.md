*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Catholic parishes by region:** compare Samogitian, Aukštaitian and Dzūkian parish life on fasts, pilgrimages (Šiluva, Žemaičių Kalvarija) and what sermons rank first.
- **Lutherans of Lithuania Minor:** check catechism and hymn-book teaching in Klaipėda and Šilutė congregations against the Catholic inland.
- **Vilnius-region Polish Catholics:** check whether Polish-language parishes stress different devotions from Lithuanian ones in the same city.
- **Orthodox Russians and Old Believers:** compare church teaching and community rules (*ustav*) on work, drink and dress.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check how the *kitabs* and the Tafsir render Islamic duties.
- **Karaim:** check the community's own statements of its religious duties; sources are few, so record the gap.
- **The Jewish community today:** separate present statements from the prewar Litvak ranking of learning.
- **Romuva:** check how the Baltic-faith movement states its own principles.
- **Secular city dwellers:** compare Vilnius and Kaunas residents who choose ethics class over religion class.
- **Generations:** check pre-1990 and post-1990 cohorts separately in every survey above.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys and church writing; moderate for minority religions; little written by Roma and Karaim on their own ranking of values.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Polish; Russian and Church Slavonic; Yiddish and Hebrew; Arabic-script Polish and Belarusian; German for Prussian Lutheran material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "traditional values" against equal rights is a live political fight; give each side's account in its own words. Moral law in the strict sense is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld from the values pointers.
