## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm households:** compare Dzūkija's sandy-soil villages with Suvalkija's farms on stores kept, crop insurance and fallback income.
- **Seaside and lagoon workers:** check how fishers of the Curonian Lagoon and Klaipėda port workers spread seasonal risk.
- **Visaginas:** check how a town of Russian speakers spoke of job and housing security before and after the plant closed.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check threat perception and trust in state pensions in Polish-language surveys and press.
- **Old Believers:** check community self-help in the northeast (Zarasai, Rokiškis).
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check how two very small communities keep mutual aid going; sources may be thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check today's welfare services for elderly members against prewar free-loan societies.
- **Roma:** check housing and income security after the Kirtimai demolitions, in community and NGO reports.
- **Pensioners and the young:** compare the generation that lost savings in 1992-1993 with those who joined the pension funds later.
- **Ukrainian and Belarusian newcomers:** compare people who fled war or repression with longer-settled communities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for financial surveys and defense polling; mixed for Soviet-era household coping; limited for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, Russian and Polish for the main surveys; Ukrainian and Belarusian for newcomers; Yiddish and English for the JDC files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation memory and the threat from the east are live public issues; give witnesses' and communities' accounts as accounts. Pension and social-insurance law as written belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** security pointers needed no setting aside.
