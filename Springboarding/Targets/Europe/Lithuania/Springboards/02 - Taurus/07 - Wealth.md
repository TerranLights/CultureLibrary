*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitian coast and Plungė:** compare memories of manor patronage with those of manor labor in the same parishes.
- **Šiauliai and Panevėžys:** check industrial fortunes and their loss in the 20th century.
- **Vilnius and the regions:** compare the capital's new rich with poverty-risk figures in rural municipalities.
- **Suvalkija and Dzūkija:** compare farm wealth across soils, without leaning on regional money jokes.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check what happened to gentry and farm wealth after 1939 and 1944.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check the old Tatar noble families and their present standing as the community records it.
- **Karaim:** check traditional trades of the Trakai community and their records.
- **Litvak history:** compare wealthy and poor households in Yiddish memoirs and yizkor books.
- **Old Believers and Russians:** compare merchant families of the 19th century with Soviet-era and post-Soviet fortunes.
- **Roma:** check community-made sources on livelihood and outsiders' claims; flag the gap if few exist.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for manors, museums and post-1990 statistics; mixed for interwar fortunes; Soviet-era wealth hard to trace.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Polish (manor archives); Ruthenian and Latin (the 1528 muster); Russian; Yiddish (Lestschinsky's sources); German (the amber firm).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the wealth of murdered Jews and of deported farmers is contested ground; present each account as an account. Envy and money jokes about regions are folklore to collect, never traits.
- **Held for review:** no wealth pointer was set aside.
