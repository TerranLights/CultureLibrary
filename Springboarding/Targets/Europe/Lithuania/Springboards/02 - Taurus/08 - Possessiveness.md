*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** compare jealousy and mother-in-law songs by region in the song archives.
- **Dzūkija:** check how foragers keep or share mushroom and berry spots, and what local papers report on it.
- **Klaipėda and Šilutė Lutherans:** check Prussian-era courtship and marriage-guarding customs in church and folklore records.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** compare Polish-language advice columns and church courses with Lithuanian ones.
- **Russian speakers:** check whether the separate words for jealousy and envy shape how forums frame the problem.
- **Old Believers:** check community rules on courtship and marriage outside the community, from their own sources.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check each community's own writing on marrying within a small faith group; no assumption either way.
- **Litvak history:** check Yiddish fiction and rabbinic responsa for claims over spouses and children.
- **Roma:** check marriage and family customs only in community-made or community-checked sources.
- **Romuva:** check how the movement's wedding rites speak of union and freedom.
