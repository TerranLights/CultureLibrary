# Lithuania: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian (Samogitian plant names in older texts), Polish for Vilnius fairs and kitchens, Russian for bathhouse and resort culture, Belarusian, Yiddish for Litvak food ways; German for the Nida colony of painters and writers. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Aukštaitija:** check home brewing in Biržai and Pasvalys families against licensed breweries.
- **Samogitia:** compare festival foods (*kastinys*, *šiupinys* at Užgavėnės) and herb lore with the inland east.
- **Dzūkija and the spa towns:** compare forest and mushroom tastes with the resort culture of Druskininkai.
- **Curonian Spit and Klaipėda:** check smoked fish, dunes and sea bathing as Lutheran fishing villages and summer visitors each experienced them.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check Kaziuko fair sellers and Palm Sunday crafts in Polish-language sources.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** compare bathhouse customs and fasting tables.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check festival dishes and their meaning in the community's own cookbooks or interviews.
- **Karaim:** check *kibinai* and other Trakai dishes as the community presents them, apart from restaurant marketing.
- **Litvak history:** compare Sabbath foods and pleasures in memoirs with Lithuanian village tables.
- **Romuva:** check the movement's use of fire, herbs and song at midsummer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, music, festivals and spas; thin for touch and greeting norms; erotic folklore exists but is scattered and little published.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian with older Samogitian plant names; Polish; Russian; Yiddish; German for Nida's colony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking customs invite stereotypes; record them as practices of named places and groups, never as national traits. Sexuality itself is Scorpio's.
