## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia against Aukštaitija:** check how far Samogitian speakers switch to the standard, and with whom, since these are the two main dialect groups the atlases divide.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** compare Dzūkian village speech with the Suvalkija speech that Jablonskis drew on (*check*), to see how far each stood from the school norm.
- **Vilnius and the Vilnius region:** check Šalčininkai and the Vilnius district for how Polish, Lithuanian, Russian and the local speech divide by setting.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare the old Lutheran printing and schooling of the Klaipėda region and the Curonian Spit with the Catholic inland.
- **Kaunas, Šiauliai, Panevėžys against villages:** check urban and rural differences in neighbor contact and schooling.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Visaginas and the Old Believer villages near Zarasai for their schools and parish teaching.
- **Southeastern Belarusian speakers:** check what the home speech is called and how it is recorded.
- **Karaim of Trakai; Lithuanian Tatars:** check religious schooling and language teaching as each community records it.
- **The Litvak community:** separate prewar *kheyder*, yeshiva and Yiddish secular schools from today's small community.
- **Roma, Ukrainians, Romuva:** check each group's own schooling and gathering sources; note where they are scarce.
