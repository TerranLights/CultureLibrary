## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lithuanian-, Russian- and Polish-language audiences:** check where each gets news, to see whether trust in sources splits by language.
- **The Vilnius region:** check how much the Polish-speaking districts follow media from Poland against Lithuanian outlets.
- **Visaginas:** check local news outlets and their languages in the town built for the Ignalina plant.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare the German, Lithuanian and later Soviet press of the port with the inland record.
- **Village and city:** check broadband, local papers and parish bulletins in Dzūkija and Aukštaitija villages against Vilnius and Kaunas.
- **Older people:** check the Eurostat-harmonized age splits on internet use and on exposure to scams.
- **Old Believers:** check what news circulates through their parishes and press.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check each community's newsletters and websites.
- **Litvak history:** compare the Yiddish, Hebrew and Polish-language press of Jewish Vilna and Kaunas, and how wartime records survived.
- **Roma:** check NGO and community reports on access to official information; likely scarce.
- **Ukrainians and Belarusians who arrived after 2020:** check the exile media based in Vilnius as a channel of their own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history, samizdat and disinformation work; mixed for news habits by language group; thin for Roma and the smallest minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian for the *Kronika* and village calendars; Russian for the Special Archives and Soviet radio; Polish and Belarusian for Vilnius-region outlets and the Bychowiec text; Yiddish and Hebrew for the ghetto's Paper Brigade; German for the Klaipėda daily; Latin and Middle High German for the Order's route reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** KGB files name informers and victims and are restricted in part; broadcast bans and "disinformation" labels are contested between communities and the state; the Holocaust record of 1941-1944 is contested ground. Present each account as an account, and send the letter of the press and data laws to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no press, archive or data pointer needed holding.
