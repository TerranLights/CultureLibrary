*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** check where parish schools spread earliest and how much reading was taught at home, using diocese and parish records region by region.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** compare village crafts and herding childhoods with the farm households of Suvalkija in the ethnographic record.
- **Polish- and Russian-language schools:** check the Vilnius region, Visaginas and Klaipėda for how children in minority-language schools learn Lithuanian and what results show.
- **Lithuania Minor:** check Lutheran catechism schooling and Prussian school records for the Klaipėda region and the Curonian Spit.
- **Litvak history:** separate *kheyder*, the great yeshivas and the Yiddish and Hebrew secular schools of interwar Kaunas and Vilnius.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check religious instruction in mosque communities such as Nemėžis and Keturiasdešimt Totorių.
- **The Karaim of Trakai:** check how the language is now taught in a very small community.
- **Old Believers:** check community schooling against state schooling.
- **Roma:** check NGO and municipal reports on Roma children's schooling; avoid general claims and say where data stop.
- **Deaf learners:** check Deaf-led accounts of schooling.
- **Romuva:** check whether the movement runs its own teaching of songs and rites, as it describes it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for test results, school history and Litvak learning; mixed for home learning and crafts; thin for Roma and Karaim learning today.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Polish; Russian; Latin for the Jesuit records; Yiddish and Hebrew for the yeshivas; Karaim; Arabic-script Slavic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minority-language schooling in the Vilnius region has been a point of public dispute; present each community's account by name, and leave the letter of education law to Libra. Some songs and crafts may carry local rules on who teaches them; ask the groups.
- **Held for review:** none; no school, craft or yeshiva pointer needed setting aside.
