# Lithuania: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish; Russian (including Old Believer usage); Belarusian; Yiddish and Hebrew; Karaim; Latin and German for medieval go-betweens. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; and the go-between in general (brokers, interpreters, intercessors). Mediation as a court procedure, and its legal letter, belongs to Libra.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia, Dzūkija and Aukštaitija:** check the matchmaker's role and the mock-hanging rite in each region's wedding records.
- **Catholic inland and Lutheran Lithuania Minor:** compare intercession of saints and the Virgin with Lutheran practice in the Klaipėda region.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check whom Polish-speaking villages send to municipalities and ministries, and how disputes over schools are taken up.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check the *nastavnik*'s role and the community's other go-betweens.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check how the Muftiate and imams stand between members and the state.
- **The Karaim of Trakai:** check the role of the community's spiritual leader in settling internal matters, as the community describes it.
- **Litvak history:** separate the *shtadlan*, the *shadkhn* and rabbinic go-betweens across eras.
- **Roma:** check go-betweens with schools and police in Vilnius and Panevėžys, in Roma voices first.
- **City and village:** check *seniūnaičiai* and neighbor mediation in villages against apartment blocks in Šiauliai and Kaunas.
- **Romuva:** check how its gatherings handle disputes among members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wedding customs and official go-betweens; mixed for informal neighbor and family mediation; thin for Roma and Karaim practice.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian for elders' and ombudsmen's records; Polish for the youth-exchange and minority bodies; Russian for the Old Believers' *nastavniki*; Belarusian for southeastern village go-betweens; Yiddish and Hebrew for *shtadlanut*; Karaim for that community's intercessors; Latin and Middle High German for the Order's interpreters and the 1420s arbitration.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the historians' commission and Polish-Lithuanian bridge programs sit on contested ground; present each side's view of them as an account. Court-annexed mediation rules go to Libra.
