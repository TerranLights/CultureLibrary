## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Village and city:** check talk about neighbors in small Dzūkija and Samogitia villages against Vilnius apartment blocks, using fieldwork, not assumption.
- **Samogitia and Suvalkija:** check the mocking songs and wedding teasing in each region's archives.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check gossip and reputation in Polish-language memoirs and village studies of Šalčininkai.
- **Visaginas:** check how rumor and reputation work in a planned town, from residents' own accounts.
- **Klaipėda and Lithuania Minor:** compare Lutheran teaching on speech with Catholic confession practice.
- **Litvak history:** check how the teaching on *loshn-hore* was taught and how it shows in memoirs of small towns.
- **Tatar and Karaim communities:** check how very small communities speak of members to outsiders, in their own publications.
- **Old Believers:** check community writings on talk and reputation.
- **Roma:** check Roma-run sources on being the subject of rumor.
- **Ukrainian newcomers:** check rumors about arrivals since 2022 in local press and their own media.
- **Gender and age:** test, do not assume, claims about who gossips, using survey and fieldwork data.
