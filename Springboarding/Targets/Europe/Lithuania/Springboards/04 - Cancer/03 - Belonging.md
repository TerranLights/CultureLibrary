## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitians against Aukštaitians:** check whether regional pride shows in language choice, flags and festivals in each, since the Samogitian-language press gives a ready source on one side.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check how people from these regions describe "our" villages and parishes against the next region, using local press and folklore.
- **Kaunas against Vilnius:** check the long-running rivalry over which city stands for the nation, in press and humor.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check belonging to Lithuania, to Poland and to the locality separately; the three are not the same question.
- **Russian speakers:** separate Visaginas, Klaipėda and Vilnius, and the old Old Believer villages, whose roots are centuries deep.
- **Belarusians:** separate southeastern villagers from recent exiles in Vilnius.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check how each frames six centuries in the Grand Duchy as grounds for belonging.
- **The Litvak community:** check how today's small community, descendants abroad and returning visitors each claim a place.
- **Roma:** check community voices first, then press and school data, on being counted as locals.
- **Ukrainians and other recent arrivals:** check school and municipal data on how newcomers since 2022 are taken in.
- **Lutherans of Lithuania Minor:** check parish life as a place of belonging around Klaipėda and Šilutė.

## 4. Flags
