*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian (with the diminutive-rich speech of child-rearing and the regional words for it); Polish for Vilnius-district schools and scouting; Russian for Russian-medium schools; Belarusian; Yiddish for Litvak upbringing; Karaim; Tatar community Polish and Lithuanian. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia, Aukštaitija, Dzūkija, Suvalkija:** check lullaby and nursery-rhyme variants by region, since the song edition gives the place of each recording.
- **Lithuania Minor:** compare Lutheran confirmation and home catechism teaching around Klaipėda with Catholic first-communion preparation inland.
- **City and village:** check day-care use and grandparents' part in raising children in Vilnius and Kaunas against rural districts.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check how scouting, parish and Polish-medium school share the work of raising children in Šalčininkai and the Vilnius district.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** check Old Believer home teaching of prayer and reading apart from Russian-medium state schools.
- **Belarusians:** check whether Belarusian lullabies and upbringing words survive in southeastern families, and where they are recorded.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check how each community passes on faith and language in a very small population.
- **The Litvak community:** separate prewar *kheyder* and youth-movement upbringing from today's community programs.
- **Roma:** check Roma mediators' and NGOs' reports on early childhood, noting gaps.
- **Romuva:** check whether it runs children's camps or family festivals, and what it teaches there.
- **Ukrainian families since 2022:** check how they combine Lithuanian schools with Ukrainian online schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk songs, children's literature and health data; mixed for everyday parenting practice; thin for Tatar, Karaim and Roma upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian with dialect words; Polish; Russian; Belarusian; Yiddish; Karaim; German for Lithuania Minor.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** helpline and child-welfare data are confidential: aggregates only. The legal ban on physical punishment goes to Libra; its spirit to Capricorn. Ghetto sources need survivor context.
- **Held for review:** none; no song, schooling or health pointer was set aside.
