## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia against Aukštaitija:** check whether written Samogitian projects and regional festivals give a separate outlet for expression.
- **Dzūkija:** check whether lament collections from Dzūkian villages differ in content from other regions'.
- **Suvalkija and Lithuania Minor:** check Lutheran hymn singing and Prussian-era diaries for a different line on public feeling.
- **Vilnius, Kaunas and the smaller cities:** compare subculture and LGBT visibility in Vilnius and Kaunas with Šiauliai, Panevėžys and small towns.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check Polish-language stages, choirs and media for the themes they choose.
- **Russians, Old Believers and Visaginas:** separate Old Believer communities' own rules on dress and worship from secular Russian-language culture.
- **Belarusians of the southeast:** check which language they choose in public, and why.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check what each community's periodicals print about language, dress and faith.
- **The Litvak community:** read prewar Yiddish diaries and press apart from survivors' later memoirs.
- **Roma, Ukrainians and Romuva:** check each group's own voice in media; for Roma, note where only outsiders' writing exists.
