## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** check how each region's newspapers and festivals present regional achievement against the national story.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check local museums' "famous people of the district" exhibits, which show what each place chooses to boast of.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare pride in a Prussian Lithuanian past with Klaipėda's port-city and German-era heritage claims.
- **Vilnius against Kaunas:** check the old capital and interwar capital rivalry in press and sport, since each claims different glories.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check their own surveys and press rather than Lithuanian descriptions of them.
- **Russians, Old Believers and Visaginas:** separate Old Believer pride in long settlement from Soviet-era arrivals' memory of the nuclear town.
- **Belarusians:** check whether Grand Duchy heritage appears in Belarusian-language material as shared or contested.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check jubilee books and periodicals for how each tells its own history.
- **The Litvak community:** read prewar Yiddish sources and postwar memorial writing as separate layers.
- **Roma, Ukrainians and Romuva:** check community organizations' own statements; Roma sources may be thin.
