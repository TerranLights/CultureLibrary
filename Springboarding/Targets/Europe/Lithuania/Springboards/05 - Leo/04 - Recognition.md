## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia, Aukštaitija, Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check district honorary-citizen lists and local award nights for who each region credits.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** check whether Prussian Lithuanian figures appear in state award lists and Klaipėda honors.
- **Kaunas, Šiauliai and Panevėžys against Vilnius:** check whether city honors repeat national honors or name local figures the state ignores.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** compare Polish state honors given to Poles in Lithuania with Lithuanian honors given to the same community.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** check what happened to Soviet titles held by Russian-speaking workers in Visaginas and Vilnius after 1990.
- **Belarusians:** check whether Belarusian community leaders appear in municipal or state honors at all.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check the community press for honors given inside the community and from outside.
- **The Litvak community and rescuers:** read rescuers' recognition files together with survivor testimony; they do not always agree.
- **Roma, Ukrainians and Romuva:** check each group's own lists of honored members; thin data likely for Roma.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state awards and arts prizes; mixed for workplace and everyday credit; thin for minority communities' internal honors.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Polish; Russian; Belarusian; Yiddish and Hebrew; Latin and Chancery Slavonic for Grand Duchy acts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honors to postwar partisans and recognition of rescuers touch contested accounts of the 1940s: give each as whose account it is. Name-spelling and religious-recognition law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no award, credit or honor pointer was set aside.
