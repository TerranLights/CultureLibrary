# Lithuania: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Dzūkija:** check whether dialect speakers report being heard or mocked in national media, and where.
- **Aukštaitija and Suvalkija:** check local photography and press archives for whom they put on the page.
- **Lithuania Minor and the Curonian Spit:** check whether Prussian Lithuanian and German-era residents appear in museum displays or are left out.
- **Vilnius against smaller cities:** compare street life and public visibility in Vilnius and Kaunas with Šiauliai and Panevėžys.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check Polish-language media on how Šalčininkai and Vilnius district residents are shown nationally.
- **Russians, Old Believers and Visaginas:** check whether Visaginas appears in national news beyond the nuclear plant's closure.
- **Belarusians:** check Belarusian-language community events in Vilnius, both the older community and newer arrivals.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check each community's own periodicals on how outsiders see them.
- **The Litvak community:** check memorial-site histories for how visibility of the murdered changed.
- **Roma, Ukrainians and Romuva:** check whether each group is shown in its own words; note gaps.
