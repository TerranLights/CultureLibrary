## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia (Plateliai) against Aukštaitija and Dzūkija:** check how Shrovetide mockery and its targets differ in each region's records.
- **Suvalkija and Lithuania Minor:** check regional joke cycles (query "anekdotai apie suvalkiečius") as material on who teases whom; record them as jokes, never as traits.
- **Vilnius and Kaunas against smaller towns:** compare workplace and school humiliation reports by place.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** read Polish-language accounts of the interwar and Soviet decades alongside Lithuanian ones, especially on 1938-1940.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** separate Old Believer memories of earlier persecution from Russian speakers' post-1990 accounts.
- **Belarusians:** check how southeastern villagers' speech has been mocked or not, and in which sources.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check community writing for slights they record, and from whom.
- **The Litvak community:** read testimony from 1941 in Yiddish and Hebrew first; Lithuanian-language accounts after.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations' records of being mocked in media and carnival; data are likely few.
- **Ukrainians and Romuva:** check how each group reports ridicule, and where.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime and Soviet-era testimony; mixed for folk shaming customs; thin for minority accounts of everyday ridicule.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian and Samogitian; Polish; Russian (army slang); Belarusian; Yiddish and Hebrew; German.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1941 events, the partisan war and the Vilnius question are contested ground: present each account as whose it is, and never as a verdict on a people. Ethnic carnival masks may wound living communities; describe the sources, not the caricatures. Hate-speech and language law belong to Libra.
