## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia against Dzūkija:** check whether calendar giving rounds and hospitality rules recorded in each differ in what is owed.
- **Aukštaitija and Suvalkija:** check farm memoirs from Suvalkija's larger holdings against Aukštaitian smallholders' memoirs for how help to neighbors, hired hands and beggars was recorded.
- **Lithuania Minor:** compare Lutheran poor relief and diaconal records with Catholic parish charity.
- **Vilnius and Kaunas against small towns:** compare crowdfunding and tax-share giving by municipality.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check parish charity in Polish-speaking parishes and aid arriving from Poland, kept apart in the records.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** check whether Old Believer congregations keep their own charity funds, and how Russian-speaking Orthodox parishes give.
- **Belarusians:** check southeastern village hospitality records and Belarusian community aid after 2020.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check each community's own publications on giving at feasts and funerals.
- **The Litvak community:** separate prewar communal charity from today's small community's welfare work.
- **Roma, Ukrainians and Romuva:** check giving inside each group and aid given to them, and avoid mixing the two.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, crowdfunding and calendar customs; mixed for everyday hospitality; thin for Karaim, Roma and Old Believer giving.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian with regional terms; Polish; Russian; Belarusian; Yiddish and Hebrew; Latin and Polish for church foundations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** donations to the army and to Ukraine are politically charged; record giving without judging causes. Inheritance and tax rules on giving go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no giving or hospitality pointer had to be withheld.
