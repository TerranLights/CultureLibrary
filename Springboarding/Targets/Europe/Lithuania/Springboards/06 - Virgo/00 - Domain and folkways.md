# Lithuania: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian (standard, plus Aukštaitian, Samogitian and Dzūkian speech); the Polish of the Vilnius region; the Russian of Lithuania; southeastern Belarusian; Yiddish for the Litvak past; Trakai Karaim; Tatar *kitab* writing; German for Lithuania Minor. **Concept in one line:** what good work and a well-kept life are: where work sits in a life, the daily and yearly round, the kept house and body, chores, craft, rest, and the work ways of a people.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia against Aukštaitija:** check farmstead and village records for how each organized the work year, since settlement patterns differed.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** set soil, forest and farm-size data beside work songs and memoirs, since the land shaped a household's work.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare Prussian estate labor and Lutheran sources with Grand Duchy material, because the manorial frames differed.
- **Vilnius, Kaunas and the industrial towns:** separate factory, office and port daily rounds from village ones.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region (Šalčininkai, Vilnius district):** check farm and commuting data by municipality.
- **Russians, Old Believers and Visaginas:** separate Old Believer village records from Soviet-era industrial migration to the power-plant town.
- **Belarusians of the southeast:** check whether their work customs are recorded apart from Polish and Lithuanian material; sources may be few.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Trakai Karaim:** start from community publications and the Trakai Karaim exhibition for trades and households.
- **The Litvak community:** use YIVO for prewar trades; treat the Holocaust's destruction of that working world as its own subject.
- **Roma and Ukrainians:** look for community-led and NGO sources on livelihood; little may exist.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, collective-farm memory and folklore of work; mixed for cleanliness and the household; thin for Karaim, Belarusian and Roma work customs.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian with dialect skill for work-song texts; Polish and Russian for minority and Soviet sources; Belarusian; Yiddish for Bund papers; Karaim; Arabic-script Slavic; German for Prussian estate records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Lithuania's exact lands are still to be settled, so the Virgo sheets cover the present country. Soviet labor records and memoirs carry deportation and forced-labor history; present each account as an account. Labor law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no work, routine or household pointer had to be set aside.
