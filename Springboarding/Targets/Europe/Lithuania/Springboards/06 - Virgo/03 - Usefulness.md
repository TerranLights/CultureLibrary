## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Aukštaitija and Dzūkija against Vilnius and Kaunas:** check SHARE and census data for where grandparents carry child care and farm work, since emigration hit districts unequally.
- **Samogitia:** read Valančius's children's tales against later Samogitian folklore records to see whether ideals of the helpful child shift.
- **Suvalkija:** compare farm-school graduates and agricultural advice uptake with other regions, since practical education spread unevenly.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** check Lutheran school primers for their own ideas of useful learning.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** compare disability, pension and grandparent care data for Šalčininkai and Vilnius district.
- **Russian speakers and Visaginas:** check how early pensioners and laid-off specialists describe their usefulness after the plant closed.
- **Old Believers, Karaim and Lithuanian Tatars:** start from each community's own accounts of elders as keepers of chant, language or *kitab* reading.
- **The Litvak community:** read productivization debates in Yiddish and Hebrew as well as Russian, since official and community framings differ.
- **Belarusians, Roma and Ukrainians:** look for social-service reports on disability and elder care; sources may be thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disability administration, ageing surveys and emigration debates; mixed for folk tales; thin for Belarusian and Roma material.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, including 16th-century orthography for Daukša; Russian for imperial productivization records; Yiddish and Hebrew; Polish; Karaim; Church Slavonic for Old Believer chant books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "being a burden" touches elder suicide and disability stigma; handle with care and do not read numbers as character. Euro-orphan coverage can stigmatize emigrant families; present it as a debate. Pension and disability law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no usefulness pointer had to be set aside.
