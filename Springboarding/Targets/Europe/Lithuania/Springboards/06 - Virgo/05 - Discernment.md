## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lithuanian Catholics by region:** check whether retreat attendance, confession practice and prayer-book use differ between Samogitia (with its Telšiai diocese) and Dzūkija.
- **Lutherans of Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare Mažvydas and later Lutheran catechisms with Catholic devotional print for what each teaches believers to reject.
- **The Litvak community:** separate Musar self-scrutiny from other Litvak approaches, and read each school's own literature.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** start from *kitab* specialists and community scholars before using outside readings of their sign and dream texts.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region and Russian speakers:** compare which outlets each audience trusts, using surveys broken down by language, since disinformation work targets them separately.
- **Old Believers:** check community rules on contact with outsiders and on reading worldly books, from their own sources.
- **Romuva:** check how the movement itself treats lots and signs in its rites.
- **Older and younger people:** set scam statistics by age against media-literacy programs in schools.
- **Belarusians, Karaim and Roma:** sources on judgment and taste may be thin; check community publications first.
