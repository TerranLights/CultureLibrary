## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vilnius against Kaunas, Šiauliai and Panevėžys:** check school-level exam data for where top scores and tutoring cluster, since pressure may track city size.
- **Dzūkija, Aukštaitija and Samogitia:** compare craft-contest records (eggs, sashes, cakes) for how each region's makers define a flawless piece.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check the palm-makers' villages and Polish-language school results for their own standards and pressures.
- **Old Believers:** treat ritual exactness through their own books and clergy, and separate Pomorian practice from other Orthodox Russian speakers.
- **Lithuanian Catholics:** compare devotional writing on perfection with counsel on scrupulosity in confessors' manuals.
- **The Litvak community:** look at printers, scribes and proofreaders, and keep the yeshivas' standards of study for the Analysis sheet.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check manuscript traditions for each community's own copying standards; sources are few.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** compare Lutheran hymnal printing standards with Catholic print.
- **Russian speakers of Visaginas:** check the nuclear plant's safety and quality culture, a precision trade with its own workforce.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, school surveys and folk-craft contests; mixed for guild records; thin for Karaim and Belarusian material.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Latin, Polish and Old Ruthenian for guild statutes; Hebrew and Aramaic for the Romm edition; Russian and Church Slavonic; Arabic-script Slavic for *kitabs*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** youth anxiety and suicide sit close to exam pressure; use public data with care and no individual cases. The Romm press and its workers' fate belong to Holocaust-era history. Exam rules go to Libra.
