## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aukštaitija against Samogitia and Dzūkija:** check folk-song collections for where mocking songs against landlords and priests were recorded, since the manorial past differed.
- **Kaunas and Vilnius:** compare the interwar Kaunas press with Polish-era Vilnius papers for what each allowed to be criticized under its own censor.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** read the Polish-language press and its party politicians on minority-rights complaints, giving each side's account.
- **Russian speakers, Visaginas and Old Believers:** compare criticism in Russian-language media with Old Believer community norms on airing disputes outside the congregation.
- **The Litvak community:** separate prewar Yiddish satire from today's community statements on Holocaust memory, which are criticism of a different kind.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and the Karaim:** check how each community voices complaints to the state, through its own organizations; sources are few.
- **Belarusians:** check whether there is a Belarusian-language press in Lithuania that carries criticism; it may be small.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** look at German- and Lithuanian-language papers of the 1923-1939 Klaipėda Region for criticism across the national line.
- **Generations:** compare school-diary debates and online comment culture with Soviet-era habits of private complaint.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet satire, dissident papers and press debates; mixed for workplace and family criticism; thin for Karaim, Tatar and Belarusian voices.
- **Languages needed:** Latin; Lithuanian; Russian for party records; Polish; Yiddish; German for Klaipėda papers; Belarusian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informing (*skundimas*) to the KGB is a raw subject, and archive files name people; handle with care. The Vilnius question, the 1941 events and the Holocaust, and the partisans are contested; give each criticism as one side's account. Defamation and hate-speech law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no criticism pointer had to be set aside.
