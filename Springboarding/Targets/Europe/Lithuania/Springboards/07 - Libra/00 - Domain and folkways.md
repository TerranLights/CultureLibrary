## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The four ethnographic regions and Lithuania Minor:** compare wedding agreements and dowry customs region by region, as the folklore archives are filed.
- **Vilnius-region Poles (the Šalčininkai and Vilnius district municipalities):** check Polish-language parish and civil records for marriage across language lines.
- **Russian speakers, Old Believers and Visaginas:** separate priestless Old Believer marriage practice from Orthodox and Soviet civil-registry practice.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check *nikah* and mosque records against civil registration; sources are few, start with community publications.
- **Trakai Karaim:** look for the marriage rules and judges' records of a very small community.
- **The Litvak community:** use rabbinic and communal records for the prewar legal world; treat the Holocaust as its own subject.
- **Romuva, Roma and Ukrainians:** look for each group's own accounts of weddings and quarrels; Roma sources are scarce, so start with NGO reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, courts and wedding folklore; mixed for disputes outside court; thin for Karaim, Tatar, Belarusian and Roma practice.
- **Languages needed:** dialect Lithuanian for wedding songs; Latin, Polish and chancery Ruthenian for the Statutes; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Council; German for Klaipėda.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Lithuania's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the country as it stands. The partnership debate, the Vilnius question and domestic-violence law are live; present each account as an account. Private court files are restricted.
