## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vilnius against the smaller towns (Šiauliai, Panevėžys, Visaginas):** compare survey answers on fair pay and chances, since wage gaps between them are wide.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check minority-school and land-restitution debates separately from pay and tax debates; the first set raises group fairness, the second individual.
- **Russian speakers and Visaginas:** check how the closing of the power plant is told as fair or unfair treatment of a town.
- **Farm families of Dzūkija, Suvalkija and Samogitia:** compare estate-division court cases by region, since farm size shaped what could be split.
- **Litvak communities:** use communal tax records and memoirs for the prewar period.
- **Tatars and Karaim:** look for how communal funds and shares were divided; little may be published, so begin with community chronicles.
- **Younger and older cohorts:** separate those who received vouchers from those born after.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, pay data and policy debate; mixed for folklore of dividing; thin for minority communal fairness.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** Vilnius-region land restitution is contested between Polish and Lithuanian accounts; present each as an account. Estate cases name families: use summaries.
