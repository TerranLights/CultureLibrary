## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** compare Užgavėnės dramas and the recorded range of *sutartinės* (mainly northeastern, *check*) to see which balance rites belong to which region.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** read Vydūnas and Lutheran sources apart from Catholic ones, since teaching on measure may differ.
- **Curonian Spit communities:** check fishers' and foresters' accounts of the dunes against park plans.
- **Vilnius-region Poles, Russians and Belarusians:** compare how each describes being between languages, using studies that ask each group.
- **Visaginas:** check work-hours and burnout data for a town built around one plant.
- **Romuva and Catholic parishes:** compare equinox and Lent customs, each in its own words.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check how fast and feast are balanced in their calendar; sources are scarce, so start with community publications.
- **The Litvak community:** check prewar Musar and rabbinic ethics for teaching on the measured middle in character, read in Hebrew and Yiddish.
