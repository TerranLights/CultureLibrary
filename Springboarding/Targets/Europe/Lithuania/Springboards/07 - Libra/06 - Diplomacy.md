## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vilnius elites and regional towns:** compare how local leaders in Kaunas, Šiauliai and Panevėžys present negotiations with the capital.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check how the minority's representatives bargain with ministries over schools and names, through their own press and the Lithuanian press.
- **Russian speakers and Visaginas:** check how the town's leaders negotiated the plant's closure and what followed.
- **Belarusians, long-settled and newly exiled:** keep these apart; the exile community's sources are new.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** read their own accounts of how Vytautas's pact is remembered and taught.
- **Trakai Karaim:** check how a very small community has dealt with every state since the Grand Duchy; sources are scarce, so begin with community histories.
- **The Litvak community:** use interwar autonomy records; treat the Holocaust as the end of that negotiated order.
