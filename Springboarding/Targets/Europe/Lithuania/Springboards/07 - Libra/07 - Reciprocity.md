## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia, Aukštaitija, Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** compare wedding gift lists and funeral duties by region, since the folklore archives record both by place.
- **Polish- and Belarusian-speaking villages of the southeast:** check forefathers'-eve and funeral customs in their own languages, apart from Lithuanian ones.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** check memorial meals and the help owed among Old Believer families; sources are few, so start with the Old Believers' own publications.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check how sacrifice-feast meat is shared among households, through community sources; little may be published.
- **Trakai Karaim:** look for hosting and gift customs in community writings.
- **The Litvak community:** use memoirs for hosting and free-loan customs; treat the Holocaust as the end of those networks.
- **Apartment-block residents of Vilnius, Kaunas, Klaipėda and Visaginas:** compare renovation disputes, since cost-sharing differs by block and town.
