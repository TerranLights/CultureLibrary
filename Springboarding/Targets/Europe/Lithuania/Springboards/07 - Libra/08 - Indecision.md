## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Visaginas against Ignalina and other districts:** compare the 2012 vote by municipality, since the plant's future mattered differently in each.
- **Vilnius-region Poles and Russian speakers:** check school-choice studies by language and municipality before reading any one family's account.
- **Vilnius against Kaunas:** compare how each city's projects stalled or moved, using the cities' own records.
- **Samogitia, Aukštaitija, Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check whether Christmas Eve divinations differ by region in the folklore archives.
- **The Litvak community:** use the interwar press for emigration debates; the decisions of 1940-1941 were often not freely made, so treat them apart.
- **Tatars and Karaim:** check how small communities decide collectively; sources are scarce, so start with their own boards' minutes or publications.
- **Emigrants and returners:** the diaspora abroad is comparison only; returners living in Lithuania are part of its people.
