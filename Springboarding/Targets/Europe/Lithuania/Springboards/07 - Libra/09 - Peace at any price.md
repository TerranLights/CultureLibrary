# Lithuania: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish, Russian and Belarusian as used in Lithuania; Russian for Old Believer histories; Yiddish and Lithuanian for Litvak survivor writing; Latin and German for the Order's treaties. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia against Aukštaitija:** compare how each region's histories tell the Salynas cession and the risings, since one region paid the price.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** check Polish-language accounts of the Soviet years and of 1939-1940 separately; their frame of what was given up may differ.
- **Russian speakers, Old Believers and Visaginas:** look at how each describes going along with or standing apart from Soviet and post-1990 politics.
- **Families of partisans, deportees and Soviet officials:** compare memoir collections on silence and surrender; present each as an account.
- **The Litvak community:** read survivor writing on bargaining for life apart from the postwar debate on bystanders.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Trakai Karaim:** check their own histories of keeping a low profile under successive states; sources are scarce, so start with community writings.
- **Roma:** look for NGO reports on unreported violence and harassment; few sources may exist.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1940, the partisans and domestic-violence data; mixed for everyday conflict avoidance; thin for minority accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Russian for Soviet and Old Believer sources; Polish; Yiddish; Latin and German for Salynas.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1940, the partisans' surrender, the Holocaust and clergy abuse are contested or painful; present each account as an account and name no private victims. Revenge and family secrets belong to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on giving way or silence was set aside.
