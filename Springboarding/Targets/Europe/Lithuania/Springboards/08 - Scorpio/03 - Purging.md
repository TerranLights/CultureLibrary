*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** the court books and the temperance archive are richest here; check whether witch accusations and pledges cluster by parish.
- **Dzūkija and Aukštaitija:** compare bathhouse and calendar cleansing customs across the forest villages and the northeast lake country.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** look at the Lutheran confessional books and at the postwar emptying of the region as two kinds of clearing.
- **Poles of the Vilnius district and Šalčininkai:** read how *repatriacja* and the purges of Polish elites after 1939 and 1944 are told in Polish-language memoirs.
- **Russian speakers in Visaginas and Vilnius:** check press coverage of monument removals and screening for how this community frames being "cleared away".
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** look for ablution and fasting customs in mosque and *kitab* writing; very little is published.
- **The Litvak community:** use communal records of the *mikve* and burial-society washing; the Holocaust stands apart as destruction, not "purging".
- **Old Believers:** check the rules on what is ritually clean in priestless communities' own writing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** strong on folk cleansing, temperance and post-Soviet debates; uneven on witch trials; slight on Tatar and Old Believer purity rules.
- **Languages needed:** Polish, Latin and chancery Ruthenian for court books; Russian for Soviet files; Yiddish for communal records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the expulsions, the Holocaust and the screening of collaborators are contested; give each side's telling as a telling and never call a people's destruction a "cleansing" in the sheet's own voice. Eating disorders and detox clinics involve health records.
- **Held for review:** none; no purging pointer had to be held back.
