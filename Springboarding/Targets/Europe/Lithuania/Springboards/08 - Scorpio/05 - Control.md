*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suvalkija and Aukštaitija's former manor lands:** check whether serfdom songs and memories of the manor steward differ between richer and poorer farming districts.
- **Dzūkija's forest villages:** look at how the farmstead clearing and drainage campaigns were received where single farms were fewer and forests larger.
- **Samogitia:** examine its strong charm tradition beside Catholic prayer against storms (the Candlemas candle); how each claims to steer weather.
- **Polish-speaking villages around Vilnius:** check memoirs of the collective farm and of school-language disputes for how outside control is described.
- **Visaginas and its Russian-speaking workforce:** compare how plant discipline, closure and language checks are recalled in Russian-language local media.
- **Old Believer parishes of the northeast:** check their own histories for state control of worship under the tsars and the Soviets.
- **The Litvak community:** look at prewar communal self-government (the *kahal*) as control within, and at ghetto councils as control imposed; keep these apart.
- **Lithuanian Tatars, Karaim and Roma:** check community writing for Soviet registration and settlement policies; for Roma, reports on the Kirtimai settlement's demolition are the likeliest first source.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** solid on Soviet campaigns, censorship and partner violence; uneven on household decision-making; thin on minority experience of control.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the censorship and collective-farm files; German for the Order's route reports; Yiddish for the *kahal*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** case files on domestic violence and child protection are closed; the child-removal scare involved disinformation, so separate documented cases from rumor. The ghetto councils are contested in memory.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on control needed holding back.
