## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old manor districts of Aukštaitija beside Samogitia's petty-gentry villages:** check whether tales and memoirs of the lord differ where great estates or small noble holdings prevailed.
- **Polish-speaking municipalities (Šalčininkai, Vilnius district):** compare how local power is described in Polish-language and Lithuanian-language press, without taking either as the neutral view.
- **Visaginas:** check how a town built around one employer described power before and after the plant closed, in its Russian-language media.
- **Klaipėda and Lithuania Minor:** look at prewar German-Lithuanian struggles over the port's administration in each side's records.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** use community scholarship on the *mirza* families and their later loss of rank; check what the community stresses.
- **Trakai Karaim:** read the records the community keeps of its spiritual head (*hazzan*, *ullu hazzan*) and of relations with rulers.
- **The Litvak community:** look at *shtadlanut* (intercession) and the communal elite in the Grand Duchy; separate prewar communal politics from the Holocaust.
- **Old Believers and Roma:** check their histories for dealings with local power under each regime; both are thinly documented.
