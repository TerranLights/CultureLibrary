*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Suvalkija against Vilnius:** compare where Soviet-era Catholic petitions found most signers, using the committee's own lists.
- **Biržai and Kėdainiai Reformed families; Klaipėda Lutherans:** check how a Protestant minority kept its confession through Counter-Reformation and Soviet years, in church histories.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** separate the voices of teachers, parents and party leaders in the school protests; they did not always agree.
- **Old Believers and other Russian speakers:** set Old Believer steadfastness in the old rite with the convictions of Soviet-era arrivals, which surveys lump together.
- **The Litvak community:** keep apart religious, Zionist, Bundist and refusenik convictions; each left its own records.
- **The Karaim and the Lithuanian Tatars:** trace how each community kept its faith under Russian and Soviet rule; little is written, so the communities' own histories come first.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses, Pentecostals and other small faiths:** read their own accounts of refusal, then official records.
- **Romuva members:** look at how they speak of persisting with Baltic faith through Soviet years.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Soviet-era dissent and partisan sources; good on parliamentary votes; thin on Tatar and Karaim persistence and on present-day objectors.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Kulvietis; Russian for imperial and Soviet records; Polish for the school protests; Yiddish and Hebrew for Litvak movements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the partisan war and 1941 are contested; partisan sources and other communities' accounts must stand side by side, each named. Objectors' files may name living people. Rules on conscription and objection are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** none; no conviction pointer needed holding.
