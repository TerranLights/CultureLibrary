# Lithuania: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish and Russian of Lithuania's towns and southeast; Belarusian; Yiddish and Hebrew for the ghettos and rabbinic teaching. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** check whether parish confession and penance customs, as clergy recorded them, differ by diocese.
- **Dzūkija:** compare local memory books from partisan districts, to see how villages assign blame for killings and betrayals on all sides.
- **Klaipėda and Lithuania Minor:** look at Lutheran church discipline records for how wrongdoers answered before the congregation.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** compare Polish- and Lithuanian-language accounts of the 1944 killings in the region, keeping each community's own account.
- **Visaginas and other Russian speakers:** check how plant workers and liquidators describe the state's answerability to them.
- **The Litvak community:** treat prewar communal responsibility and the debate on Lithuanian participation in the Holocaust as two lines of inquiry.
- **Lithuanian Tatars, the Karaim, Belarusians, Ukrainians, Roma:** look in community bodies' records for who answers for members before officials; sources are thin.
- **Romuva and the unaffiliated:** see whether movement writing frames care for nature as a responsibility, in its own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on the Holocaust and lustration debates, official surveys and public crises; thin on minority groups' internal answerability.
- **Languages needed:** Hebrew and Yiddish for kahal and ghetto records; Russian for Soviet and liquidator sources; Polish for the southeast's accounts of 1944.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1941, the ghettos, collaboration and the partisan war are disputed; present every version as an account and name its teller. A child's death is private grief; use only public reports.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on blame or answerability was set aside.
