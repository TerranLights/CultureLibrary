# Lithuania: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish (the "rise in the world" idiom is shared); Russian; Belarusian; Yiddish for the interwar youth of Vilna and Kaunas. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Suvalkija and Samogitia:** compare memoirs of farm sons sent to seminary, gymnasium or the city, to see which routes up each district favored.
- **Aukštaitija and Dzūkija:** check emigration and return statistics by municipality, since leaving and coming back are a main path of rising.
- **Klaipėda and Lithuania Minor:** look at port and shipping careers, and at how Prussian schooling once shaped the climb, in local histories.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** check whether young people's plans point to Vilnius, Poland or elsewhere, in Polish-language youth surveys and the local press.
- **Russian speakers of Visaginas:** compare young people's plans before and after the nuclear plant closed.
- **The Litvak community:** read prewar strivers' own writing apart from later emigrant success stories, which belong to diaspora comparison.
- **Lithuanian Tatars, the Karaim, Belarusians, Ukrainians:** look for community newsletters on members who studied or rose; sources are scarce.
- **Roma:** check NGO and school reports on young Roma's education plans, in the community's own voice where possible.
- **Romuva:** see whether its writing treats striving as a value or a danger.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business, migration and Soviet careers; mixed for youth surveys; thin for small minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Latin for the Radvila sources; Yiddish for the youth autobiographies; Russian for Komsomol files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "envy" circulates as a self-description; treat it as a claim to test, never as a finding. Soviet careers and 1990s fortunes touch living people; use public records only.
- **Held for review:** none; no source on striving was set aside.
