## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** check petty-gentry hamlets against peasant villages in the same parish, and whether each kept its own genealogies and marriage circles.
- **Aukštaitija and Dzūkija:** compare manor inventories and land registers to see how far the serf ladder differed between large estates and small ones.
- **Suvalkija:** look at farm-owner and farmhand records, and test what the earlier, Napoleonic-era end of personal serfdom there *check* changed in rank.
- **Klaipėda and the Curonian Spit:** separate Prussian estate and fishing-village records from the inland pattern.
- **Poles of Šalčininkai and the Vilnius district:** check gentry descent claims and their place in Polish-community life, using community sources.
- **Russian speakers and Old Believers:** compare Soviet-era managerial families with the priestless villages, whose ranking rests on other ground.
- **Belarusians, Ukrainians, Roma:** check how each appears in census occupation tables, and whether any community source describes internal rank.
- **The Litvak community:** read rank words such as *sheyne* and *proste* in prewar memoirs, not in later nostalgia.
- **The Karaim and Romuva:** check how each lists its officeholders; sources are thin.
