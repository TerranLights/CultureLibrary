# Lithuania: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish and Latin for monastic sources; Russian for Soviet-era writing and Visaginas; Belarusian; Hebrew and Yiddish for Musar texts. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms are counted as calm, objectivity or equanimity, others as coldness, indifference or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dzūkija and Aukštaitija:** compare funeral recordings for how much open grief is expected and from whom.
- **Samogitia:** check parish and folklore records for customs of silence at wakes, without assuming any regional temper.
- **Vilnius and Kaunas against small towns:** compare survey data on living alone and meeting neighbors.
- **Klaipėda region Lutherans:** check pietist and Lutheran devotional writing for teaching on restraint.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** look in Polish-language media for how civic disengagement is discussed locally.
- **Russian speakers in Visaginas and Klaipėda:** compare their own accounts of distance from the majority's public life.
- **Old Believers:** check priestless devotional manuals for rules on withdrawing from worldly life.
- **Litvak Musar and Hasidic memoirs:** separate the training of composure from rival teachings on joy.
- **Tatars and Karaim:** look for community writings on mourning restraint; few are likely.
- **Ukrainians arriving since 2022:** check aid groups' reports on isolation among newcomers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for monastic and Musar texts, election surveys and Soviet-era memoir; mixed for loneliness data; thin for minority mourning and greeting manners.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Polish for Pažaislis and Jesuit sources; Hebrew for Musar; Russian for Visaginas; Belarusian dialect for the southeast.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and trauma research needs care with survivors' privacy; "inner emigration" is a judgment some former Soviet-era officials reject, so give it as one account.
- **Held for review:** one category: Buddhist meditation groups in the cities (an open question for the author; no community named).
