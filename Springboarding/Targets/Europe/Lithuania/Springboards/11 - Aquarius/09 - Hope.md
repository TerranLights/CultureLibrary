# Lithuania: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian; Polish (the Vilnius shrines and region); Russian; Belarusian; the Tatars' *kitab* writing; Hebrew and Yiddish for Litvak messianic and migration hopes. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and the Šiluva area:** compare pilgrimage petitions with those at Žemaičių Kalvarija for whether hopes differ by shrine.
- **Vilnius:** separate Polish- and Lithuanian-language devotions at the Gate of Dawn.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check midsummer and Christmas Eve customs for local variants of wishing.
- **Klaipėda and Lithuania Minor:** look at Lutheran hymnals and burial inscriptions for hope of the life to come.
- **Visaginas and other Russian-speaking towns:** read residents' own accounts of what they hoped for after the plant closed.
- **Old Believers:** check their writings on the end of days for a distinct religious hope.
- **Belarusians, both long settled and recent exiles:** keep the two groups apart.
- **Ukrainians since 2022:** check aid-group and community media for hopes of return or settlement.
- **Tatars and Karaim:** each community's prayer books first.
- **Litvak survivors and descendants:** read their own writings on hope after the Holocaust.
- **Roma:** check Roma organizations for young people's stated hopes; little is likely.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for shrines, the press ban, deportation memoirs and migration data; mixed for folk wishing customs; thin for Roma and Karaim sources on hope.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for the Gate of Dawn's records; Hebrew for the Gaon's circle; Russian for Visaginas and Old Believer texts; Arabic script for *chamaiły*; Belarusian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deportation memoirs belong to contested ground; suicide data needs care with small municipalities; give each account as its author's.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing about hope was held back.
