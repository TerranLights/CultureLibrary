*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian and its dialects; Vilnius-region Polish; Lithuania's Russian; southeastern Belarusian; Trakai Karaim; Tatar *kitab* texts; Yiddish for the Litvak past. **Concept in one line:** how suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape are met: the hidden self, confinement and institutions, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence, and the magic ways that handle luck, harm and hidden forces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aukštaitija, Samogitia, Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** test whether spirit names and charm types cluster by land, using the archive's parish tags.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** keep Lutheran parish records and the Spit's legend trail apart from the Catholic interior.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** read *dziady* and *zaduszki* in Polish-language sources first.
- **Belarusians of the southeast:** look for *shaptukha* healers in Belarusian ethnography of the Šalčininkai and Varėna districts.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** compare ancestors' Saturdays across Zarasai, Utena and Visaginas congregations.
- **Lithuanian Tatars; Trakai Karaim:** each people's manuscripts and community histories, read separately.
- **The Litvak community:** Yiddish collections and survivors' testimony first for prewar lore and its destruction.
- **Roma:** sources in their own voice are likely few; start with Roma organizations in Vilnius.
- **Romuva:** set the movement's teaching on the dead beside what the folklore archive holds.
