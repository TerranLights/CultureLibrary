## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Aukštaitija:** check *sutartinės* singers' own talk of singing together, since the form belongs to the northeast.
- **Samogitia:** compare Samogitian-speakers' accounts of their tongue's decline with Ministry of Culture language records.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** test whether farmstead destruction and village emptying are remembered differently in each.
- **Poles and Belarusians of the Vilnius region:** look at intermarriage and the *tutejsi* layer, and at how each community names assimilation.
- **Russians of Visaginas and Old Believers of the northeast:** compare a Soviet-built town's ending with older congregations' long continuity.
- **Karaim and Tatars:** check each community's histories for how they tell language loss or retention.
- **Litvak community:** destruction is not dissolution; keep the Holocaust apart and let survivors' terms lead.
- **Lutherans of Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda:** look for how the 1945 population change is told by those who stayed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for village undoing, census and music; thin for mystical experience and new religious movements.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian and Samogitian; Russian for Visaginas; Polish; Karaim; German for prewar Klaipėda.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** assimilation words (*nutautėti*, *wynarodowienie*) carry the Vilnius dispute; present each community's account under its own name. Calling a group a "sect" is a charge; cite each side.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on merging or coming apart was withheld.
