*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** check parish temperance pledge rates against later drinking data, since Valančius's movement was strongest there.
- **Dzūkija:** compare forest-going and mushroom-picking customs with Aukštaitija's lake country.
- **Coastal Klaipėda and Palanga:** separate resort economies from the inland farm world.
- **Poles of the Vilnius region:** look for Polish-language studies of drink and leisure in the Šalčininkai and Vilnius districts.
- **Russians of Visaginas and Old Believer villages:** compare dacha life in a planned town with congregations' own teaching on drink.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check community writings on drink and leisure, which follow their own religious rules.
- **Roma:** check outsiders' claims against Roma organizations' own accounts; avoid sources that blame.
- **Generations:** Soviet youth subcultures against today's screens and betting apps; ESPAD helps here.
- **Women and men:** NTAKD and mortality data split by sex; check who escapes, and how, without assuming.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for alcohol (surveys, temperance history, press) and folk drinking songs; good for gambling statistics; thin for fantasy reading, gaming and daydreaming.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian; Russian for Soviet-era press and Visaginas; Polish; Belarusian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drink is often used to stereotype rural people and some minorities; cite measurements and named voices only. Alcohol rules are law: Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the escape sources above raised no rule.
