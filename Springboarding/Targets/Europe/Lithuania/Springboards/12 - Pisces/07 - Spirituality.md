## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia and Aukštaitija:** check sacred-place density and pilgrimage customs, since Vaitkevičius surveyed each separately.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** look for folk prayer and *atlaidai* differences in the archive by district.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** set Polish-language devotion at Trakai and the Vilnius Calvary beside Lithuanian practice there.
- **Lithuania Minor and Klaipėda Lutherans:** Pietist gatherings, read on their own terms.
- **Russians, Orthodox and Old Believers:** separate Orthodox parishes from priestless congregations; their practices differ.
- **Belarusians of the southeast:** check for Catholic and Orthodox mixed devotion in Belarusian-language sources.
- **Karaim and Lithuanian Tatars:** each community's own liturgy and calendar.
- **The Litvak community:** Musar, the Gaon's learning and Hasidic currents were rivals; keep them apart.
- **Romuva and the unaffiliated:** compare seekers' words for the sacred with folklore sources they cite.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic pilgrimage, sacred places and Litvak learning; mixed for Romuva and the unaffiliated; thin for Belarusian and Roma devotion.
