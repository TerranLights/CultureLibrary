## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** compare Łasicki's record with later Samogitian archive material on omens, to see what survived and what is the writer's invention.
- **Dzūkija and Suvalkija:** check Kūčios divination variants by district.
- **Aukštaitija's lake country and the coastal Curonian Spit:** compare weather reading among farmers, forest people and lagoon fishers.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** gather Polish-language folk omens in Šalčininkai and Vilnius districts, and for local readings of Mickiewicz.
- **Russians and Old Believers:** check congregations' teaching on dreams and signs, and Russian-language psychic media.
- **Belarusians of the southeast:** check for village seers or healers in Belarusian ethnography.
- **Lithuanian Tatars:** check whether *fałnama*-type texts appear in *kitab* manuscripts, and how the community describes them.
- **Karaim of Trakai:** look for community writings on omens; likely thin.
- **The Litvak community:** use YIVO folklore and the Hasidic quarrel's own documents, keeping the two currents distinct.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk omens and calendar lore; mixed for professional intuition; thin for measured intuition and minority practice.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian with dialect help for Kūčios omens and weather sayings; Latin for Łasicki; Polish for Mickiewicz's "Romantyczność"; Russian for the psychic-contest shows' forums; Belarusian for southeastern divination customs; Hebrew and Yiddish for the 1772 ban; Arabic script for Tatar texts on signs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** psychics and healers may be private practitioners; use what they publish. Łasicki is a hostile and partly unreliable outsider: read critically.
- **Held for review:** none; nothing on hunches and signs needed holding.
