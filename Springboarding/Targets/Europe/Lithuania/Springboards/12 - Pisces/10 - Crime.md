# Lithuania: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Lithuania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Lithuanian (Samogitian forms for outlaw lore); Vilnius-region Polish; Lithuania's Russian, including Soviet prison slang; Belarusian; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it (the culture's idea of wrongdoing; the law itself is researched under Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Samogitia:** compare Blinda lore with other districts' robber legends, since outlaw heroes cluster by land.
- **Suvalkija and Lithuania Minor borderlands:** check book-smuggling memory where the routes crossed from Prussia.
- **Dzūkija and the Belarus border:** look for how residents talk about smuggling and those who do it, in local media.
- **Vilnius-region Poles:** read *Kurier Wileński*-type local Polish press for which local wrongs make news and which pass in silence.
- **Russians of Visaginas and Klaipėda:** check Russian-language media framing of crime and of Soviet prison culture.
- **Roma of Kirtimai:** compare press framing of drug arrests with Roma organizations' own statements; avoid sources that make an ethnic group the crime.
- **The Litvak community:** prewar Yiddish press on crime and the postwar record of crimes against Jews need separate handling.
- **Lithuanian Tatars and Karaim:** check community writing on wrongdoing; likely thin.
- **Partisans and Soviet records:** one side's "bandits" are another's fighters; keep each label with its source.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys, official counts and 1990s press; mixed for folk ideas of wrongdoing; thin for minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** Lithuanian, with 16th-century spelling for Daukša; Russian for prison slang and Soviet case files; Polish; Belarusian; Yiddish for the prewar press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who did wrong in 1941, in the deportations and in the partisan war is fought over in memory; keep every charge with the community or archive that makes it. Do not link any people to crime by stereotype. What the law says belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; each source on wrongs and wrongdoers here could be named.
