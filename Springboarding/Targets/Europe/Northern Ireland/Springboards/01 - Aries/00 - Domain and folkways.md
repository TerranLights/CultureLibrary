## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast by district:** check how east (the shipyard streets), west (the Falls and the Shankill) and north (interface districts) use address and school inside a name.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how people from the Bogside, the Fountain and the Waterside say where they are from in a city with two names.
- **Mid and north Antrim, the Ards Peninsula:** check Ulster-Scots surname clusters and whether by-names tell people apart.
- **Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check Gaelic and Hebridean name stocks shared across the sea, and hurling clubs in local self-description.
- **South Armagh, Tyrone, the Sperrins, the Fermanagh lakelands:** check townland and parish-club identity as units between household and province.
- **Communities:** Catholic and nationalist; Protestant and unionist, split by Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Elim congregations; "others", whose census write-ins show who declines the two-way frame.
- **Irish Travellers, Roma, the Belfast Jewish community, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese (Dungannon) and Filipino families, Syrian refugees, Muslim communities:** check name adaptation at school, work and registration, group by group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census and survey identity; mixed for folk naming; thin for Roma, Syrian and East Timorese naming, where community groups are the first route.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; this Aries set is written for the six counties as they stand, the rest of the island as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** Irish and Ulster Scots for naming; Old Irish for the sagas; Cant for Traveller sources; Cantonese, Tetum and Polish for newer families' names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** identity labels are contested; "telling" studies touch on how people were targeted, so read them as accounts.
- **Held for review:** one domain-level pointer, category only: South Asian-heritage communities in Northern Ireland's lands.
