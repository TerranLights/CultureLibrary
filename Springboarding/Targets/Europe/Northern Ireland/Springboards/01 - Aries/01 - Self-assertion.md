*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West Belfast and the Gaeltacht Quarter:** check what happens when people ask for public services in Irish, a way of claiming space through language choice.
- **East Belfast and the Shankill:** check how residents' groups and community workers describe "not being heard" in forums on interfaces and regeneration.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check speaking-up episodes on the Cityside and in the Waterside from the 1960s onward, and how each side remembers them.
- **Mid-Ulster farms, the Sperrins, south Down:** check whether disputes go to neighbors, the church, or bodies like the Ulster Farmers' Union.
- **Women in each community:** check what girls and boys are told about answering back at home, in church, at school and in the mill.
- **"Others" and cross-community groups:** check whether refusing the two labels is itself treated as a claim, and by whom.
- **Irish Travellers, Roma families in south Belfast, Polish and Lithuanian meat-plant workers, Filipino nurses, Hong Kong Chinese restaurant families, Syrian families:** check each group's routes for complaint (unions, agencies, community groups, church) separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political and public speech; thin for everyday conversation, which needs recordings (Ulster Folk Museum dialect tapes, radio archives).
- **Languages needed:** Irish for language-rights claims; Ulster Scots for dialect verse and talk; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and Tetum for workplace testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** public assertion around parades, flags and marches is contested ground; present each account as an account. Legal complaint routes belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here; the workplace-voice route was chosen through Dungannon's migrant support group and needed no withheld item.
