## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast neighborhoods:** check whether naming a district (the Shankill, the Falls, the Short Strand, Ballymacarrett) answers "who are you?" before any faith or nation is named.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check how Fountain and Bogside residents name and describe the city, and whether the name used is heard as a statement.
- **South Armagh, Fermanagh, south Tyrone:** check how cross-border kin, parishes and markets shape answers to the national-identity question.
- **Ballymena, north Antrim, the Ards:** check where Ulster-Scots heritage claims and Scottish ancestry claims are made, and by whom.
- **Glens of Antrim:** check where Scottish Gaelic lineage (the MacDonnells) and Irish identity meet in local self-description.
- **Denominations:** check whether Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian and Brethren members answer with a church, "Protestant", "British" or "Ulster" first.
- **Children of mixed marriages and "others":** check how they report identity on surveys where the two-way frame is offered.
- **Irish Travellers:** check which name (Mincéirí, Pavee, Traveller) is used in their own publications; sources thin, start with Traveller-led groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese (older settlers and recent arrivals), Polish, Lithuanian, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian, Jewish and Muslim residents:** check whether people answer first with a country, a Northern Irish town or a faith.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich and heavily studied for the two main blocs; thinner for "others", newer communities and Travellers, where self-published and community sources lead.
- **Languages needed:** Irish and Middle Irish for origin texts and Gaeltacht sources; Ulster Scots for heritage writing; Cantonese, Lithuanian and Tetum for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** origin theories (the Cruthin, Plantation narratives) are contested and politically used; give each as one group's account.
- **Held for review:** one identity pointer set aside, under the category South Asian-heritage communities in Northern Ireland's lands.
