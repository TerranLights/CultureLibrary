*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Congregations by government:** check who decides in Presbyterian sessions, Church of Ireland vestries, Catholic parishes and Brethren assemblies with no clergy, and how far each decision reaches into members' lives.
- **Mid Antrim, Ballymena and Lisburn:** check local disputes over Sunday rules in public spaces and who sets them.
- **Lough Neagh shore (Toome, Ardboe):** check the fishery co-operative's own records for how members decide.
- **The Bogside and Creggan; loyalist districts in Belfast and Larne:** check each episode of claimed self-rule (1969, 1974) as each side tells it.
- **Farm families in Tyrone, Fermanagh and south Down:** check who chose the heir, the spouse and the trade, and how that changed after the 1960s.
- **Young adults across the counties:** check living at home against moving out by class and district.
- **Older and disabled people:** check self-directed support uptake by health trust.
- **Irish Travellers:** check accommodation preferences in Traveller-led sources against official assessments.
- **Roma in south Belfast, Syrian families placed by resettlement, Filipino nurses on recruited contracts, Polish and Portuguese agency workers:** check how much say each reports over housing, hours and work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; church records and political history are deep, while household decision-making in Northern Ireland is studied less than in Britain.
- **Languages needed:** Irish and Old Irish terms for status and self-rule; Ulster Scots for congregational records written in dialect; Polish, Portuguese and Arabic for newer residents' accounts of choice at work and at home.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** bodily autonomy (abortion, sexuality) and claimed self-rule (Free Derry, the 1974 strike) are contested; give each as an account. Laws on capacity, consent and abortion belong to Libra; what law is for, to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the care and housing routes here run through public bodies, and no community-specific item had to be withheld.
