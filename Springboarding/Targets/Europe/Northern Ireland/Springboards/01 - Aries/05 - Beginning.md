## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check how landscape origin tales (the Causeway giants, the first Norse raid) are told by islanders and residents, compared with tourist versions.
- **Downpatrick, Saul and Strangford Lough:** check how Church of Ireland, Catholic and civic groups each use Patrick's arrival as their own start.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check which founding date local groups use (Colm Cille's monastery or the 1613 charter) and why.
- **Belfast:** check whether city histories begin with the Gaelic past, the charter or the industrial boom.
- **Ulster-Scots districts of Antrim and Down:** check New Year customs against Scottish ones across the North Channel.
- **Evangelical congregations (Free Presbyterian, Elim, Brethren, Methodist):** check conversion testimony as the start of a life story.
- **Ex-prisoners of both sides:** check how each network frames release.
- **Jewish, Hong Kong Chinese, Syrian, Roma, East Timorese, Polish and Lithuanian residents:** check arrival stories and how each dates its start in the province.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for early annals, saints' lives and calendar custom; mixed for newcomers, whose arrival stories are recent and mostly unpublished.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish and Latin for annals and Patrician texts; Irish for season customs; Arabic, Polish and Cantonese for arrival testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Plantation and founding stories are contested; prisoner release after 1998 is contested ground; give each as an account.
- **Held for review:** no item withheld; the arrival-story pointers came from resettlement bodies and community histories that raised none.
