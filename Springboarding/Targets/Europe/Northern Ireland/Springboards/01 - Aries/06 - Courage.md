*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kilkeel, Portavogie and Ardglass:** check how fishing families memorialize sea losses and rescues, and who speaks at those memorials.
- **The Mournes and south Down:** check how rescue volunteers recruit, and whether local farmers and climbers describe risk differently.
- **Enniskillen and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check how civilian moral courage after 1987 is commemorated in each community.
- **Unionist and loyalist districts:** check which kinds of courage the Somme commemorations name, and how that changed after the 1990s.
- **Nationalist and republican districts:** check how 1798, 1916 and the 16th Division are remembered and how bravery is worded in each.
- **Victims' groups, ex-combatant groups and security-force veterans:** check how each frames bravery and fear; the frames clash.
- **Women in every community:** check whether keeping a household going through the Troubles is described as courage, and by whom.
- **Boys' and Girls' Brigade, Scouts, GAA youth sections:** check which youth bodies give bravery awards and for what.
- **Syrian refugees, East Timorese and Portuguese workers, Roma families:** check how leaving home and arriving here are told, as the courage-to-leave facet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, commemoration and the Troubles; thinner for everyday courage (whistleblowing, standing up to harassment), which needs newspaper and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for saga valor terms; Ulster Scots for the 1798 dialect writing; Arabic and Tetum for newcomers' leaving stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as brave in the Troubles is contested (paramilitary claims, security-force honors, victims); quote no side as fact. Prison testimony carries consent conditions.
- **Held for review:** none; the military and rescue pointers needed nothing set aside, and the courage-to-leave sources for newer arrivals are still to be found.
