## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rathlin and the islands of Strangford Lough:** check provisioning, ferry dependence and pride in managing alone.
- **Small farms in Fermanagh, Tyrone and the Sperrins:** check what share of income comes from the farm, and how families talk about support payments.
- **School-leavers from every county:** check who leaves for Britain to study, who returns, and whether it differs by community background.
- **Unionist, loyalist, nationalist, republican and "others":** check each strand's own documents on national independence, unity or the Union, without merging them.
- **Older people living alone in rural Mid-Ulster and the Glens:** check how they describe accepting help.
- **Disabled people:** check independent-living use by health trust.
- **Irish Travellers:** check self-employment and dependence on public sites in Traveller-led sources.
- **Lithuanian and Polish workers, Filipino nurses, East Timorese families in Dungannon, Syrian refugees:** check remittances home and dependence on employers or resettlement support.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics and 1790s letters; mixed for attitudes to help-seeking, which need qualitative work.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for neighbors' mutual help; Ulster Scots for rural self-reliance talk; Lithuanian, Polish, Tagalog and Tetum for remittance and household sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national independence and unity are contested; give each proposal as its authors' account. Welfare entitlement is law (Libra).
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; remittance and household-earner questions were left to the community bodies above.
