## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Causeway Coast towns (Portstewart, Portrush, Ballymoney):** check how road-racing deaths are remembered beside the safety debates that follow each one.
- **Rural roads of Tyrone, Fermanagh and south Derry:** check young drivers' crash figures against Belfast's, and how local campaigns speak to them.
- **West and north Belfast:** check accounts of the joyriding years from residents, youth workers and former joyriders themselves.
- **Bonfire builders in loyalist districts (July) and nationalist districts (August):** check how each describes risk to homes and how councils respond; both are contested.
- **Farm families in every county:** check how danger on the farm is spoken of across generations.
- **Young men and young women:** check the behavior survey's gender splits rather than assuming them.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and East Timorese workers in meat plants and construction:** check injury data by nationality where HSENI or unions keep it.
- **Irish Travellers:** check Traveller-led health sources on injury, since outsider reports are prone to bias.
- **Temperance traditions:** check Presbyterian, Methodist and Free Presbyterian abstinence teaching beside the Catholic Pioneer pledge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official risk statistics (roads, drink, gambling, deaths); thin for how people themselves draw the line between bold and reckless, which needs interviews and local press.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for saga terms of frenzy; Ulster Scots for folk words for the rash; Polish, Lithuanian and Portuguese for migrant workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** joyriding and its punishments, and bonfires, are contested and touch on paramilitary violence; give each as an account. Drink-driving and licensing law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the workplace-risk pointers drew on a regulator's data that raised nothing to withhold.
