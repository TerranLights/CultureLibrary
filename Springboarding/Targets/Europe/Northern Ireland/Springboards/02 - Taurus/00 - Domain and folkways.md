## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast, east, west and north, the Shankill and the Falls:** check how oral histories describe household goods, credit and "keeping a good house".
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how shirt-factory wages, credit and pawning appear in women's accounts from both banks.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin, the Causeway Coast:** check fair-day trading, seaweed goods and Scottish market links.
- **Mid-Ulster, the Lough Neagh shore, south Armagh and the orchards:** check eel, apple and small-farm incomes and cross-border pricing.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Mournes:** check boat shares, stone-walled fields and granite work as property.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, the Fermanagh lakelands:** check hill stock and land kept in one family name.
- **Communities and congregations:** check Catholic, Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Elim teaching on money, and "others" in wealth surveys.
- **Irish Travellers, Roma, Jewish Belfast, Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian and Muslim families:** check what each keeps, sends home or passes on; community contacts first.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income, farm and probate records; mixed for food and dress lore; thin for Travellers, Roma and newer communities, where community groups lead.
- **Scope:** the tracker carries "UK" as pre-war lands and the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled by the author; the Taurus set is written for the six counties, with the rest of the island as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the grade and tribute texts; Ulster Scots for harvest words; Cant for dealing terms; Polish, Lithuanian, Tetum and Cantonese for newer families' shopping.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land touches the Plantation and contested accounts of confiscation; give each as an account. Poverty data is no trait of a district.
- **Held for review:** no pointer was set aside for this domain sheet; the newer-community route above is given as a query, not a named study.
