*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **County Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how land held by London companies, and later sold, is remembered on each side of the river.
- **North Down and the Ards:** check private settlement outside the official Plantation, and whether owners there tell their land's origin differently.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check the Antrim estate and island holdings, and the commonage on hill grazing.
- **The Lough Neagh shore:** check fishers' and sand-dredgers' claims beside the estate's ownership of the bed.
- **The Mournes and south Down:** check turbary and grazing rights on mountain land and what the water board's wall changed.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and Tyrone:** check large estates beside small owner-occupied farms and the use of conacre.
- **Belfast and Derry housing estates:** check Housing Executive tenants who bought and those who did not, across Catholic, Protestant and mixed estates.
- **Irish Travellers:** check how families describe halting sites they do not own and private sites they do; sources thin, start with Traveller-led groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian and Syrian families:** check business premises, private renting and first purchases, group by group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for estate and housing records; mixed for attitudes; thin for newer communities and Travellers.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for saga and Gaelic land terms; early-modern English for grants and leases; Ulster Scots for tenure words; Polish and Cantonese for owners' associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Plantation, land confiscation and housing allocation are contested; give each account as an account, in both communities' framings, and the city as Derry / Londonderry.
- **Held for review:** Plantation and tenure pointers were checked against the law-only rule; their legal sides are noted for Libra, and nothing was set aside.
