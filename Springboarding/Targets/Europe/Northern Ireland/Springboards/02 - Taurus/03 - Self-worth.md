## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West and north Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** check how ex-prisoners and their families speak of worth after release, on both sides.
- **East Belfast:** check what men from the shipyard and engineering trades said about standing after redundancies.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check civil-rights memoirs against Fountain residents' accounts of being outnumbered.
- **Mid-Ulster and the Sperrins:** check farm families on the "good name" and what a failed farm or sale does to it.
- **Fermanagh, south Armagh, the border:** check families who left during the conflict and how they tell their standing.
- **The Ards, north Antrim (Ulster Scots speakers):** check whether heritage revival is described as restoring worth to a dialect once mocked.
- **Irish speakers in west Belfast:** check the same question for Gaeltacht Quarter families.
- **Congregations:** check worthiness for communion in Presbyterian, Free Presbyterian and Brethren practice against Catholic confession and Church of Ireland confirmation.
- **Travellers, Roma, Hong Kong Chinese (older settlers and recent arrivals), East Timorese, Filipino and Syrian families, Muslim and Jewish residents:** check each group's own accounts of being looked down on or respected at work and in school.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental-health statistics and political grievance writing; mixed for everyday pride and shame; thin for newer communities, where oral history is the route.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for *enech* and the saga; Irish and Ulster Scots for revival writing; Cantonese, Tetum and Arabic for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data needs care; grievance writing from each community is an account, not a finding; prisoners' worth is contested by victims' groups.
- **Held for review:** honor-price texts were weighed for this sheet; their legal content is left to Libra, and no pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
