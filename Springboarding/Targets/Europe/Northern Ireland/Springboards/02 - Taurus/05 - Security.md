## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Interface districts of north and west Belfast:** check whether residents on either side of a wall rank safety from attack above other kinds of security.
- **East Belfast and the shipyard streets:** check talk of "a job for life" before and after the yard's decline.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check shirt-factory women's wages as household security, and the credit-union story in the city.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check eel and sand incomes against farm and factory work.
- **The Mournes, Kilkeel and Annalong:** check fishing families on losses at sea and how they insure boat and crew.
- **South Armagh and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check how border farmers speak of animal disease, floods and the border itself as risks.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check island and glen families on winter isolation and food stores.
- **Communities and congregations:** check saving and insurance through Catholic and Protestant bodies, and church benevolence funds in Presbyterian, Methodist and Elim congregations.
- **Travellers, Roma, Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese and Filipino workers, Syrian refugees:** check housing and work security group by group; Roma sources are thin, start with community organizations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and housing statistics; mixed for mutual-aid history; thin for newer communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for proverb and Famine memory; Arabic, Polish and Tetum for refugees' and workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** peace walls, intimidation and paramilitary "protection" are contested and dangerous topics; give accounts as accounts and protect informants.
- **Held for review:** policing and "national security" were considered here and left aside as Capricorn and Libra matters; nothing was set aside under the naming rules.
