## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Shankill, Divis and the Markets:** check how redevelopment and the motorway cut through settled streets, and how residents on each side tell it.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check the Fountain's population loss against the Bogside's rebuilding, each in its own residents' words.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and the Sperrins:** check families on the same townland for generations, and the townland-name campaign.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check how island families measure staying when the young leave.
- **The Ards and north Down:** check commuter growth and old fishing villages side by side.
- **Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check towns where meat and food plants drew new workers, and whether those workers settle.
- **Congregations:** check families "in the same pew" in Presbyterian and Church of Ireland parishes against Catholic parish loyalty and newer Elim and Free Presbyterian churches.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check how each group describes moving and settling in its own terms, not by settled people's measures; sources thin.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino and Hong Kong Chinese families:** check how long they plan to stay and what makes them settle.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census-linked and vital statistics; mixed for ritual and place continuity; thin for Traveller and Roma accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for place-name and "old stock" words; Ulster Scots for staying words; eighteenth-century English and Scots for emigrant letters; Cant for Traveller mobility terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** parades, routes and commemorations are contested; both "tradition" claims are accounts. NILS needs approved, secure access.
- **Held for review:** parade-route disputes touch on rulings by the Parades Commission; those rulings are left to Libra, and no other pointer was set aside.
