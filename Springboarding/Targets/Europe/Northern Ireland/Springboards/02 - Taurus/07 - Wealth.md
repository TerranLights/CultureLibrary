*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Belfast (Malone) and north Down (Holywood, Cultra):** check old merchant wealth against newer professional wealth and how each displays it.
- **East and west Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** check how working-class districts talk about "the money" up the road.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** check shirt-factory owners' wealth and the city's later fortunes in memory on both banks.
- **Mid-Ulster (Cookstown, Dungannon) and the Lough Neagh shore:** check family firms in quarrying, food and engineering and how local people talk of them.
- **South Armagh and the Armagh orchards:** check border trade and farm wealth, and talk of hidden money.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and the Sperrins:** check land as wealth against cash, and the "strong farmer".
- **The Causeway Coast, the Glens and Rathlin:** check tourism money and second homes.
- **Congregations:** check Presbyterian, Methodist and Catholic teaching on riches and giving, and Free Presbyterian and Elim views on prosperity.
- **Irish Travellers:** check what Travellers themselves name as wealth, from Traveller-authored sources only.
- **Belfast Jewish families, Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, East Timorese, Filipino and Syrian families, Muslim communities:** check remittances, family firms and giving customs, group by group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deprivation, business and historical records; thin for measured household wealth; thin for minorities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for cattle wealth; Irish for proverbs and folklore; Cantonese, Polish and Tetum for remittance and gift customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims about wealth gaps between communities are contested and politically charged; present each as an account with its data. Hidden money and border trade touch on crime claims; do not name people.
- **Held for review:** donor and business-family pointers were limited to published, public figures; none was set aside under the standing rules.
