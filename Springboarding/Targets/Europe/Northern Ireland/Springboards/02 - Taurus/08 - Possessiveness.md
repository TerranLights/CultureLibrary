# Northern Ireland: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect); Old and Middle Irish for the Ulster Cycle; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic and Cantonese for newer communities; Romanes for Roma families where sources exist. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Interface areas of north and west Belfast, east Belfast's loyalist estates:** check how each side marks and polices "its" streets, and how residents describe those markings.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the Fountain and the Bogside on shared city space, and fishing claims on the river.
- **South Armagh and south Down:** check the border and its roads described as "ours" by different groups.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check islanders and coastal families on visitors and second-home owners.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and the Sperrins:** check farm inheritance quarrels and who keeps the "home place".
- **Couples and families across communities:** check mixed-marriage families, and congregations from Catholic to Free Presbyterian, on who claims the children's religion.
- **Men and women separately:** check how each describes controlling partners, using service reports from both MAP and women's services.
- **Roma, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and East Timorese families, Syrian refugees, Irish Travellers:** check accounts of being kept out of districts or jobs; sources are mostly press and advocacy reports.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Filipino and Muslim families, the Jewish community:** check jealousy and marriage customs only from each community's own writing, not outsiders' claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for abuse statistics and territorial studies; mixed for courtship lore; thin for newer communities' own accounts of jealousy.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for the two tales; Ulster Scots for grasping words; Arabic, Polish and Romanes for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic abuse records need anonymity and care; territorial marking and flag disputes are contested; attacks on Roma families must be reported as events, never as a trait of any district.
- **Held for review:** the stalking and coercive-control offenses came up here and were left for Libra; no pointer was set aside under the naming rules.
