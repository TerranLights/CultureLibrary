## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Belfast and the Cyprus Avenue streets:** check how musicians and residents describe the district's sounds and smells.
- **West Belfast and the Gaeltacht Quarter:** check festival food and music against Irish-language accounts of pleasure.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check dance halls and the city's music scene across the cityside and the Waterside.
- **The Causeway Coast, Rathlin and the Glens of Antrim:** check sea bathing, seaweed and island food.
- **The Mournes, south Down and Strangford Lough:** check oysters, walking and hill pleasure in local writing.
- **The Armagh orchards and the Lough Neagh shore:** check apple-blossom time and eel eating as seasonal pleasures.
- **North Antrim and the Ards:** check Free Presbyterian, Reformed Presbyterian, Brethren and Methodist views on dancing, drink and Sunday leisure, and how younger members describe them.
- **Catholic parishes and Church of Ireland congregations:** check worship through the senses (incense, music, flowers) and fasting customs.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian and Hong Kong Chinese families, Muslim and Jewish communities, Irish Travellers, Roma:** check food, music and bathing customs in each group's own sources; most are thin and start with community organizations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music, verse and places; thin for measured pleasure, since few surveys ask about it here.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the quatrain and *Mesca Ulad*; Ulster Scots for comfort words; Cantonese, Polish and Arabic for festival and food sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the showband story includes the 1975 killings of the Miami Showband; treat it with care and as an event. Dance and drink rules in some congregations are matters of faith; report them in members' own words.
