## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east, west, north; the Shankill and the Falls):** check street visiting and speech networks either side of the peace walls.
- **The Gaeltacht Quarter and Irish-medium families:** check how new speakers are raised and schooled.
- **The Ards and mid and north Antrim:** check where Ulster Scots is heard at home and where it is taught.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the cross-river and cross-border daily round.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check the last native Irish and the Scottish sea link.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes, the Sperrins, Tyrone, the Lough Neagh shore and Fermanagh:** check rural school runs, evening visiting and dialect reach.
- **Congregations (Catholic, Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim) and "others":** check Sunday schools and study groups as places of early learning.
- **Deaf signers:** check which of BSL or ISL each school and club used.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí), Roma, Hong Kong Chinese, Belfast Jews, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian and Muslim families:** check home languages, Saturday schools and who interprets; each group's own organizations first.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language tables, dialect study and school history; mixed for neighborliness; thin for Cant, Romani and the Tetum speakers of Dungannon.
- **Scope:** "UK" is the tracker's only pre-war entry and no lands are settled yet; this sign's sheets write for the six counties and use the Republic, Donegal included, only to compare.
- **Languages needed:** Ulster Scots and Ulster Irish for dialect work; Old Irish and Latin for Bangor and the *Auraicept*; Cant with Traveller guidance; Cantonese and Tetum for newer families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** language is contested (Irish-language act debates, Ulster-Scots funding, Derry / Londonderry); give each account as an account. Cant word lists need Travellers' consent.
- **Held for review:** the census and school pointers for this overview were weighed against the standing rules and none needed setting aside.
