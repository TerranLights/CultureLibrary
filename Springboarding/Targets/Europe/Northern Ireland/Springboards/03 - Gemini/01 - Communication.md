## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East and west Belfast; the Shankill and the Falls:** check whether "telling" and guarded talk are described alike on both sides of the peace walls.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the city's talk radio and humor as a separate speech style from Belfast's.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough:** check where Ulster-Scots forms of greeting survive in conversation.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check Scottish and Irish layers in local talk.
- **South Armagh and south Down, the Sperrins and Tyrone:** check rural greeting on the road and talk with strangers near the border.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and the Lough Neagh shore:** check how isolated townlands kept in touch before phones.
- **Unionist and nationalist communities, and "others":** check whether surveys and oral histories show different norms for raising the conflict in mixed company.
- **Deaf BSL and ISL users:** check who interprets for them and in which language.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check when Cant is spoken and with whom; consent first.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian, Roma and Muslim families:** check children as language brokers and use of the interpreting service.
- **The Jewish community of Belfast:** check its own histories for languages kept at home over generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for accent studies and media reports; mixed for everyday norms of talk; thin for Cant and for Tetum and Romani speakers.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for *Audacht Morainn*; Ulster Scots and Ulster Irish for spoken material; Polish, Cantonese and Arabic for interpreting data.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "telling" and guarded speech touch the Troubles; present each account as an account, never as a trait of a community. Traveller language is held by Travellers.
- **Held for review:** interpreting-service pointers were weighed for sources that would name communities under the standing rules; the service is given as a public body only, and nothing had to be set aside.
