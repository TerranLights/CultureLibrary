## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check readership of the *Journal* and the *Sentinel* by area.
- **West and east Belfast, the Falls and the Shankill:** check community papers and murals as news media, and trust in police information.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands, the Sperrins and Tyrone:** check broadband gaps and local weekly papers.
- **South Armagh and south Down:** check cross-border media (RTÉ and Republic papers) against UK outlets.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check ferry, weather and sea information for islanders.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough:** check *The Ulster-Scot* readership and local radio.
- **Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check how news on the lough's water quality reached fishers and residents.
- **Unionist, nationalist and "other" readers:** check which outlets each uses and trusts, from survey breakdowns.
- **Irish speakers in the Gaeltacht Quarter:** check Irish-language news use since *Lá Nua* closed.
- **Irish Travellers and Roma:** check how official notices reach families on sites; sources thin, ask their groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian and Muslim families:** check which home-country outlets and group chats carry their news.
- **The Jewish community of Belfast:** check its newsletters and archive.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the press, the conflict archive and surveys; mixed for word-of-mouth news; thin for newer communities' media.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for Clandeboye verse; Irish for *Lá*; Polish, Lithuanian, Cantonese and Arabic for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informers, army information policy, the Boston College tapes and the broadcasting ban are contested; give each as an account and protect named individuals.
- **Held for review:** the conflict-data pointers were checked for sources the standing rules would exclude; none came up, so the list stands whole.
