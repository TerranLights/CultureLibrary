## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Working-class east and west Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** check the underachievement data by ward and community, not as a trait.
- **Grammar-school towns and Derry / Londonderry:** check transfer-test tutoring and the pull of St Columb's and Foyle schools.
- **The Gaeltacht Quarter and Irish-medium units in Tyrone, Armagh and Down:** check how children learn Irish when parents do not speak it.
- **The Ards, mid-Antrim and the Ulster-Scots heartland:** check where Ulster Scots enters school programs.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check small schools, island schooling and boarding on the mainland.
- **The Sperrins, Fermanagh lakelands and south Armagh:** check learning on the farm, small rural schools and long school runs.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check fishing and farm skills passed down outside school.
- **Integrated schools and "others":** check what parents who chose neither sector say they wanted taught.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check attendance, home learning and schools' support; few published sources, start with their groups.
- **Weekend schools of the newer communities (Cantonese classes of the Hong Kong Chinese; Polish and Lithuanian Saturday schools; Portuguese and Tetum-speaking East Timorese families in Dungannon; Filipino, Syrian and other Muslim families):** check what each teaches beyond the state curriculum and who runs it.
- **The Jewish community of Belfast:** check its classes for children and its history of teaching Hebrew.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school statistics and policy; mixed for learning at home and at work; thin for Traveller, Roma and East Timorese children.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Adomnán; Old Irish for fosterage terms; Irish for Irish-medium records; Polish, Lithuanian and Arabic for weekend schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** academic selection and school sectors are fiercely argued; give each side's case as its own. Data on children needs care.
- **Held for review:** the newcomer-pupil data was reviewed for any language groups that would fall under the standing rules; the figures are cited as a whole, and nothing was set aside.
