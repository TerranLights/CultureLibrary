## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check how locals and visitors talk about the stones and the island.
- **West Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** check residents' views of tour groups looking at murals and walls.
- **Armagh and the orchards:** check the observatory and planetarium's reach into the city and county.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the walls tours and the Museum of Free Derry's visitors, as places of looking.
- **The Ards and Strangford Lough:** check birding and shore recording.
- **The Mournes, south Down and south Armagh:** check stories about places not to dig or disturb.
- **Fermanagh lakelands, the Sperrins and Tyrone:** check archaeology and caves (Marble Arch) and how locals use them.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check fishers' and farmers' knowledge-seeking about the lough.
- **Catholics, Protestants and "others":** check which questions about a stranger are asked and which are avoided.
- **Irish speakers and Ulster-Scots speakers:** check which local words for "nosy" and "wonder" each uses.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check how they feel about questions from settled people and officials; their groups first.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Belfast Jews, and Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian and Muslim newcomers:** check which questions from neighbors they find welcome or intrusive.
