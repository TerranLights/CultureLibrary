## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how residents of the Cityside and the Waterside use the two names in daily talk.
- **East and west Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** check reactions to bilingual signs in each district.
- **The Ards, mid-Antrim and the Ulster-Scots heartland:** check whether Ulster-Scots identity is held as a third strand or as part of British.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check mixed Scottish and Irish family lines and how people describe them.
- **South Armagh and the Fermanagh border:** check Irish passport take-up and cross-border identity.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check mixed parishes and the labels used there.
- **The Mournes and south Down:** check how a mixed coast area answers the identity questions.
- **Mixed-marriage families and "others":** check which labels children of mixed homes choose.
- **Irish speakers and Ulster-Scots speakers:** check whether speakers feel tied to one bloc or to neither.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check how they answer British, Irish or Traveller identity questions; ask Traveller groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, East Timorese, Filipino, Roma, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish residents:** check dual citizenship and how they place themselves outside the two-bloc frame.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for identity surveys and census tables; mixed for daily code-switching; thin for newcomers' views on the two blocs.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for Suibne; Irish for bilingual-sign debates; Cantonese and Polish for dual-status families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names of the place, flags, passports and anthems are contested ground; give every name in both forms and each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** identity datasets were checked for categories the standing rules would require holding; none was used by name, so this line records nothing held.
