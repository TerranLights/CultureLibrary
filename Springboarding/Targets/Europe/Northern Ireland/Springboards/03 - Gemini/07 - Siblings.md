*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Fermanagh:** check farm inheritance among brothers and the unmarried siblings who stayed on.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check large families, eel fishing and which sibling took the boat.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough:** check fishing and farming families in Presbyterian and Brethren homes.
- **South Armagh and the orchards:** check whether orchard land was split or kept whole among heirs.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check siblings who emigrated to Scotland and those who stayed.
- **Belfast (east, west, north; the Shankill and the Falls):** check siblings housed near each other and shared childcare.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check elder sisters in the shirt factories and their younger brothers' schooling.
- **Catholic, Protestant and "other" households:** check family size over time from census data rather than assuming.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check sibling roles at weddings and funerals; sources few, start with Traveller groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** check siblings in the family takeaway or restaurant.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Roma, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish families:** check siblings who migrated in sequence and who cares for parents left at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census households and care statistics; mixed for sibling feelings and rank; thin for newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for Lir; Irish for kin terms; Tetum and Cantonese for age-ranked sibling terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** siblings in care and bereaved siblings are vulnerable groups; sibling deaths in the conflict must not be used as color.
- **Held for review:** sibling and care sources were looked over for anything the standing rules would set aside; none came up.
