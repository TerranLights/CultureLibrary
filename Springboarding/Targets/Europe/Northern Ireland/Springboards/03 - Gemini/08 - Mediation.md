## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the back channel, local clergy and business go-betweens on both banks.
- **North Belfast and the Ardoyne and Glenbryn interfaces:** check interface workers and phone networks as mediators.
- **The Shankill, the Falls and east Belfast:** check how restorative schemes are described by residents and by critics.
- **South Belfast's Ormeau Road and Portadown's Garvaghy Road:** check local talks around parades.
- **South Armagh and the Fermanagh border:** check who brokered local disputes when police were little used.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Ards:** check clergy and elders as settlers of quarrels in small places.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check land and fishing disputes and who stepped in.
- **Presbyterian, Catholic, Church of Ireland, Methodist and other churches:** check each tradition's teaching on intercession and peacemaking.
- **East Timorese families in Dungannon:** check whether customary dialogue is used in disputes here.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Filipino, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish communities:** check who acts as broker with officials.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check mediation with councils over sites and with settled neighbors; ask their groups.
