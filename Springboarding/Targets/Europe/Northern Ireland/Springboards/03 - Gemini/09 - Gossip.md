*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns of the Sperrins, Tyrone and Fermanagh:** check how people describe being known by everyone.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check factory-floor and street talk in oral histories from both sides of the river.
- **West and north Belfast, the Falls and the Shankill:** check rumor at interfaces, and how residents check what they hear.
- **East Belfast and the Ards:** check church-gate talk and kirk-session records of "scandal".
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check island and glen talk where few families live close.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and south Down:** check silence and talk about who does what near the border.
- **Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check how news of newcomers in Dungannon and Portadown moved through talk.
- **Catholics, Protestants and "others":** check each church's teaching on the tongue.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check how rumor about them spreads in settled communities; check how their own sources describe it.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish residents:** check group-chat news within each community and talk from outside about them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries and media; mixed for everyday talk; thin for gossip inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for Bricriu; Old Irish for satire terms; Polish, Cantonese and Arabic for community chats.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rumor during the conflict cost lives; tabloid crime reporting and trial talk touch living people; name no private individuals.
- **Held for review:** pointers on rumor about minorities were weighed under the standing rules; nothing from them was set aside here.
