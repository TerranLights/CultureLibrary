## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast's interface streets (north Belfast, the Short Strand, the Shankill and the Falls):** check where children say their "area" ends and how they learned it.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** set Protestant residents of the Cityside beside Catholic residents of the Waterside for who reports feeling at home where.
- **The Causeway Coast, the Glens and Rathlin:** compare islanders' and coast-dwellers' views of summer visitors and second-home owners.
- **Mid-Ulster, Tyrone and the Sperrins; the Fermanagh lakes:** check how border-county families describe belonging to a county against a state.
- **The Ards Peninsula and south Down:** check Ulster-Scots and Irish heritage events and who attends both.
- **People who name themselves neither unionist nor nationalist ("others"):** check how they find belonging outside the two blocs.
- **Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Elim congregations; Catholic parishes; Church of Ireland and Methodist circuits:** compare how each receives a new member who moved in.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check accounts of being refused service and where Travellers feel accepted; Traveller groups first.
- **Hong Kong Chinese (old and recent BN(O) arrivals), Belfast Jews, Muslims:** compare older settled families with newer arrivals in one community.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Roma and Syrian residents:** check what "local" means after ten years in Dungannon, Craigavon or Belfast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on community division and identity; mixed on newcomers' belonging; thin on Roma and Cant-speaking Travellers.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for Ó Cianáin; Cantonese and Polish for community media; Tetum for Dungannon interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** parades, flags and the names of the place are contested ground; present each as one account. Racist attacks on newcomers are documented but victims' identities must be protected.
- **Held for review:** surveys of minority-ethnic belonging were looked at for anything the standing rules would set aside; none was.
