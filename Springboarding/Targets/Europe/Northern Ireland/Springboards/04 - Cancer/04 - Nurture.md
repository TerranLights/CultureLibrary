## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West Belfast and the Gaeltacht Quarter:** check Irish-medium nursery families against English-speaking neighbors.
- **East Belfast and the Shankill:** check Sure Start reach, Sunday schools and band practice as places children are formed.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare grandmothers' share of childcare where mothers worked in the shirt factories and after.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check children learning eel fishing, farm work or trades beside parents.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and the Sperrins:** compare small rural schools, distance to services and who minds children.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check whether island and glen children board or travel far to school.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and the Ards Peninsula:** compare farm and fishing families' teaching of skills to the young.
- **Congregations (Catholic, Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim) and families of no faith:** check catechesis, children's ministries and Sabbath teaching.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check who teaches trades and household skills; school records are outsiders' views, so pair them with Traveller accounts.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Roma, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish families:** check language at home, weekend schooling and grandparents brought over to help.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health-visitor and early-years statistics; mixed for home practice; thin for Roma and Tetum-speaking families.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for fosterage texts; Ulster Irish and Ulster Scots for rearing words and lullabies; Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for weekend-school material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-protection records are closed; physical punishment of children is a live public question whose legal side goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** early-years research and weekend schools were screened under the standing rules, and nothing had to be set aside.
