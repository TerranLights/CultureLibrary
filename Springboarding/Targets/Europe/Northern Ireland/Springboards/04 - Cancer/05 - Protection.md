## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Interface areas of north and west Belfast; the Short Strand and east Belfast:** check who residents say keeps them safe, and set police and community accounts side by side.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** look at the Foyle Search and Rescue volunteers and river-safety work.
- **South Armagh and the border:** check how farm families guarded livestock and homes during and after the conflict, in their own oral histories.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and the Fermanagh lakes:** check water-safety teaching and volunteer rescue on the loughs.
- **The Mournes, the Sperrins and the Glens of Antrim:** compare mountain and hill rescue, and how farmers look out for lone neighbors.
- **The Causeway Coast, Rathlin and the Ards:** check coastguard and lifeboat crews drawn from fishing families.
- **Catholic homes with holy-water fonts and scapulars; Protestant homes with Scripture texts; households of other faiths and none:** compare objects and prayers kept for protection.
- **Women, children and older people across communities:** check who is named as needing protection in services' reports, and who is left out.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check experiences of attacks and of policing; scant written record, so seek their own groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Syrian, Muslim and Jewish residents:** check who they turn to first after harassment, through each community's organizations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state statistics and conflict-era records; mixed for household protective customs; thin for Roma and Cant-speaking families.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the Law of Adomnán and the Breastplate; Ulster Irish for folklore items; Polish, Portuguese and Cantonese for community safety leaflets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paramilitary "protection", vigilantism and policing are contested; never let one side's account stand as the record. Abuse records and survivors' identities are restricted.
- **Held for review:** hate-crime and abuse sources were checked against the standing rules; none needed holding back.
