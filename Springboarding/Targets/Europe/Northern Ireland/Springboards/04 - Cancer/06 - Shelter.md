## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast city center, the Markets and Sailortown:** check how rough sleepers and hostel users describe safe and unsafe places.
- **Ardoyne, Clonard and the Shankill:** set families burned out in 1969 beside those who took them in, from each side's own records.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare hostels and the 1969 to 1972 flights across the border to Donegal.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and Newry and south Armagh:** check flood shelters and rest centers opened in recent floods.
- **The Mournes, the Sperrins and the Glens of Antrim:** look at mountain huts, byres and neighbors' houses as weather shelter for walkers and farmers.
- **Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check where islanders and visitors shelter when ferries stop.
- **Church networks (Catholic Society of St Vincent de Paul, Methodist missions, Presbyterian and Church of Ireland congregations, Brethren and Elim halls):** compare who each opens its doors to.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check roadside camps, transit sites and evictions in Travellers' own words; written sources are scarce.
- **Syrian, East Timorese, Roma, Filipino and other refugees and migrant workers:** check hostels, tied housing and overcrowded lets, with each community's own groups.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Belfast Jewish and Muslim communities:** check whether, and how, each has housed its own newcomers or refugees.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for homelessness statistics and Poor Law records; mixed for informal taking-in of kin; thin for Traveller and Roma shelter.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Muirchú; Irish for place-name and folklore items; European Portuguese, Arabic and Tetum for migrant-worker and refugee accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** refuge locations are confidential; intimidation-related homelessness is contested, so give each account as an account; asylum seekers' cases are private.
- **Held for review:** refugee and hostel sources were weighed against the standing rules, with no shelter pointer set aside.
