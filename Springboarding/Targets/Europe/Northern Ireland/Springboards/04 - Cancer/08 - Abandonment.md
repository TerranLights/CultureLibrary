## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (Shankill, Falls, east and north):** check children's homes in the city and how former residents describe being placed there.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** look at Termonbacca and Nazareth House survivors' accounts beside those of Protestant-run homes.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Fermanagh lakelands:** check empty farmhouses, emigrant children and elders left alone on farms.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check island and glen depopulation and how those who stayed speak of it.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and the Lough Neagh shore:** compare emigration, the "American wake" and returnees.
- **The Ards Peninsula, Strangford Lough and Mid-Ulster:** check towns that lost mills or factories and residents' words for it.
- **Churches that ran homes (Catholic orders, Church of Ireland, Presbyterian, Methodist, Brethren) and people of no faith:** compare how each has spoken of children in its care.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check children taken into care and Traveller views of it; records are mostly official.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** check children sent back to grandparents and elders left in Hong Kong.
- **Portuguese, East Timorese, Filipino, Roma, Syrian, Muslim and Belfast Jewish families:** check relatives left behind and children reunited later.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiry testimony and migration history; mixed for desertion and family estrangement; thin for Roma and Traveller accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the Conla tale; Polish and Lithuanian for migrant press; Tetum and Cantonese for families split across continents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse survivors' testimony and adoption records are restricted; give church and state accounts as accounts beside survivors'.
- **Held for review:** inquiry and adoption sources were weighed against the standing rules; nothing about being left was set aside.
