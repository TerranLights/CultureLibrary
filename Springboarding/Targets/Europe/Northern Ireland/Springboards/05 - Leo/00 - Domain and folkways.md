## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east, west, north; the Shankill and the Falls):** compare band parades, féile events and club nights for who gets public space for fun.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how the Halloween carnival and the Maiden City festival each frame the city.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** compare hurling, feiseanna and seaside holidays as summer pleasures.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Mournes and south Down:** compare Ulster-Scots and Irish music in neighboring parishes.
- **South Armagh, the orchards, the Lough Neagh shore, the Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster:** trace road bowling, fiddling and harvest fairs by district.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands:** mumming and boating as seasonal play.
- **Catholic, Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Elim congregations, and "others":** compare teaching on Sunday pleasure, dancing, gambling.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** fairs, music and family celebrations as Travellers describe them; few written sources.
- **Hong Kong Chinese and Filipino families, the Belfast Jewish community, and Syrian and other Muslim households:** check which celebrations stay in homes and halls and which go public.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for arts funding, sport and festivals; mixed for courtship and private pleasure; thin for Cant, Tetum and Roma sources.
- **Lands:** the tracker carries only "UK" and no settled lands, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this Leo set writes for the six counties, the Republic (Donegal, Cavan, Monaghan included) as comparison only.
