*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east, west and north; the Shankill and the Falls):** compare murals, band uniforms and club fashion as statements.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how the city's comedy and music give room to speak out.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Mid-Ulster:** compare dialect verse and song for what is said in the open.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and south Down:** check Irish-language song and reserve in border parishes.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast, Rathlin, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** compare speech across the sea-facing districts.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and the Lough Neagh shore:** look at rural reticence and speaking out at fairs and meetings.
- **Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim and Catholic congregations; "others":** compare testimony, confession and plain speech.
- **Deaf people from Catholic and from state schools:** check how two sign forms grew apart and meet.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask how Cant is used to speak freely among Travellers; few published sources.
- **Muslim families (Syrian and others) and the Belfast Jewish community:** check head coverings, dress and language at school and work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for songs, theater and flag display; mixed for private feeling; thin for Deaf, Traveller and Roma voices.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for Oriel song; Ulster Scots for dialect verse; BSL and ISL for Deaf sources; Polish and Arabic for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** flags, murals, drumming and rap lyrics are contested; give each account as an account. Mental-health and coming-out stories involve living people.
- **Held for review:** the school-dress pointer was kept to the published guidance rather than any one pupil's case, and no song, drama or rights source here needed setting aside.
