## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east, west and north; the Shankill and the Falls):** check whose plaques, murals and street names appear in each area.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare civic honors voted by the council over time.
- **The Causeway Coast, the Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** look at how road-racing and hurling heroes are marked.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins and Mid-Ulster:** check the harpers, poets and footballers each district credits.
- **South Armagh, the Armagh orchards, south Down and the Mournes:** compare county football honors and parish memorials.
- **The Ards and Strangford Lough; the Fermanagh lakelands; the Lough Neagh shore:** look at how fishing, farming and seafaring work is credited.
- **Catholic, Protestant (Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim) and "other" communities:** set each community's honored dead and living side by side.
- **Women across the communities:** check who restores credit for care, mill and campaign work.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask what recognition means in Traveller groups' own words.
- **Filipino and Hong Kong Chinese community associations:** check credentials, community awards and who speaks for them in public.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for honors, plaques and sport; mixed for workplace credit; thin for Traveller, Roma and Tetum-speaking views.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for harping and bardic patronage; Ulster Scots for local naming; Tagalog and Portuguese for nursing staff.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of the dead and of victims is contested; present each account as its own. Legal recognition of peoples and languages goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** credential-recognition pointers were kept to nursing routes generally; within them, no single group's study was set aside for this sheet.
