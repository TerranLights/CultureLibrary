# Northern Ireland: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (the Shankill and the Falls; east and north):** compare how residents near interfaces describe being watched.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how the city is filmed and photographed, and by whom.
- **South Armagh and the border parishes:** look at being watched from the hills as lived over decades.
- **The Causeway Coast, the Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check how tourism makes residents into scenery.
- **The Mournes and south Down; the Ards and Strangford Lough:** compare fishing ports for whose labor is seen.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster, the Lough Neagh shore and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check rural isolation, and older farmers living alone.
- **Armagh orchards and meat-plant towns (Dungannon, Portadown):** see how newer workers are noticed or not.
- **Catholic, Protestant (Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim) and "other" people:** set accounts of being "told" apart side by side.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí) and Roma:** check census counts against community estimates; few independent sources.
- **Roma families and Syrian and other Muslim households of south and north Belfast:** ask where each feels watched and where unseen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveillance history, language campaigns and census counts; mixed for everyday staring and greeting; thin for Roma and Traveller accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for the tale; Irish and Ulster Scots for signage; Tagalog for fishing crews; Arabic for women's reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** watchtowers, "telling" and surveillance are contested; give each account as its own. Folk belief about the evil eye should be recorded as people tell it.
- **Held for review:** the census and workforce pointers were weighed for whether a group under the standing rules would be named; they name none, and no pointer on visibility was set aside.
