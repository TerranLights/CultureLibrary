## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast, north and east, with the Shankill and the Falls:** set relief marches, housing campaigns and mill memories side by side.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare shirt-factory work and housing campaigns as dignity claims.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check how small farmers spoke of thrift and respectability.
- **South Armagh, south Down and the Mournes:** look at border families' accounts of searches and checkpoints.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast, Rathlin, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** check how fishing and farming families describe old age and care.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and the Armagh orchards:** check eel fishers and seasonal pickers for work conditions.
- **Presbyterian, Catholic, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Elim members, and "others":** compare church teaching on the worth of the person and on charity.
- **People with learning disabilities and their families:** check what dignity in care means to them.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** look at site conditions and health services from Travellers' groups; written sources are few.
- **Migrant workers from Poland, Lithuania, the Portuguese-speaking countries and the Philippines:** check treatment at work, in housing and at the end of life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor history and inquiries; mixed for end-of-life care; thin for Traveller, Roma and asylum seekers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Patrick; Irish for proverbs; Arabic, Tetum and Portuguese for some migrants' testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Disappeared, institutional care and checkpoints involve living families and contested accounts; present each as its own. Assisted dying is debated as law in Libra.
- **Held for review:** for end-of-life care the strategy document was chosen over any individual clinician or hospice founder, which left this line empty.
