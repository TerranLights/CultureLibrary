## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (the Shankill and the Falls; north, south and east):** set church, club and community-group giving side by side in the same streets.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check credit-union and parish giving against city charity appeals.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster and the Fermanagh lakelands:** look at neighbors' labor, wake hospitality and the open door on farms.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check giving between island and mainland families.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Mournes and south Down:** compare fishing-village collections after losses at sea.
- **South Armagh, the Armagh orchards and the Lough Neagh shore:** check how border parishes raise money for their own.
- **Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim and Catholic congregations, and those with no church:** compare tithes, Lenten giving and freewill offerings.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask how giving at weddings and funerals is described by Travellers; little in print.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Filipino and East Timorese households:** check money sent home, feast hospitality and support for newcomers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church and charity giving; mixed for informal and kin giving; thin for remittances by nation and for Traveller and Roma practice.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for the annals; Irish for proverbs on stinginess; Tagalog, Tetum and Polish for remittance and community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lodge, club and church fundraising can be read along community lines; present each as its own. Giving records may name living donors.
- **Held for review:** blank for generosity; the remittance row is limited to the four destination countries tied to this sheet's own community list, and the appeals chosen are open to all givers.
