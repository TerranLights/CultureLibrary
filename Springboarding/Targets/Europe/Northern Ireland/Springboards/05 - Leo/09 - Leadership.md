# Northern Ireland: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect; Latin and Early Modern Irish for older sources); Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Tetum, Cantonese, Arabic, Tagalog. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (the Falls and the Shankill; east and north):** compare community workers, clergy and party figures as leaders in each district.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how civil-rights, church and business figures led the city at different times.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins and Mid-Ulster:** look at the O'Neill legacy, county football and farm cooperatives.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check how lordship and later parish leadership worked on both sides of the water.
- **South Armagh, south Down, the Mournes, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** compare who leads in border and coastal parishes.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands, the Lough Neagh shore and the Armagh orchards:** check leaders of fishing, farming and growers' bodies.
- **Unionist, nationalist and "other" parties; Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim and Catholic churches:** set elected, ordained and lay leaders side by side.
- **Women across communities:** check informal leadership against formal posts.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask Traveller groups how families choose who speaks for them; little in print.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Muslim and Jewish community bodies of Belfast:** check who leads associations and how they are chosen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political memoirs and church structures; mixed for informal leaders; thin for Traveller, Roma and Tetum-speaking leaders.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Columbanus; Irish for inauguration sources; Tetum and Tagalog for community bodies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaders tied to armed groups are contested; give each account as its own, and keep law and formal authority for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no entry. The choice here lay between leaders' own memoirs and outside studies of them; memoirs were preferred so that each movement speaks for itself, and outside studies follow in research.
