## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (the Falls and the Shankill; north and east):** compare memorial gardens, murals and plaques as honoring practices in each area.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how the siege and the civil-rights dead are each honored, and by whom.
- **South Down and the Mournes; south Armagh:** look at Dolly's Brae and border memorials as local memory.
- **Armagh orchards and Mid-Ulster (Scarva, Portadown):** check Black and Orange displays against Catholic parish memorials.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, the Lough Neagh shore and the Fermanagh lakelands:** compare war-memorial names and GAA club memorials.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast, Rathlin, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** check sea-loss memorials and the honor of lifeboat crews.
- **Women in each tradition:** check how women describe family name, reputation and loyalty, in their own words.
- **Presbyterian, Reformed Presbyterian (Covenanter), Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim and Catholic congregations; "others":** compare oath, vow and confession as forms of honor.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask Traveller groups how family name and respect are spoken of; avoid outsiders' framings.
- **The Belfast Jewish community, Muslim families and East Timorese households:** check how each honors its dead and its elders here.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memorials and commemoration; mixed for everyday word-keeping; thin for Traveller and Roma accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the sagas; Scots for Covenant-era papers; Tagalog for *utang na loob* research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** commemoration of the conflict dead, emblems and police honors are contested; present each account as its own. Honor-price and oaths as law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing here. The memorial rows stay with bodies rooted in the six counties, and the wider war-grave registers are left for the research stage.
