# Northern Ireland: The domain, work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English (Belfast speech, Mid-Ulster English); Ulster Scots (Ullans); Irish (Ulster dialect; the Gaeltacht Quarter of west Belfast); the Cant (Gammon) of the Irish Travellers; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Tetum, Cantonese, Tagalog and Arabic for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** work, service, the daily round and health: what counts as good work and a well-kept life; work ways are the folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east, west and north; the Shankill and the Falls):** compare shipyard, mill and dock work for who was hired and the hours kept.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check whether shirt-factory wages changed who kept house.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check fishing, kelp and hotel seasons against the farm round.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough; the Mournes and south Down:** compare Portavogie and Kilkeel boats and granite work for the yearly round.
- **South Armagh and the Armagh orchards; the Lough Neagh shore:** check apple picking and eel fishing as seasonal work with their own calendars.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster and the Fermanagh lakelands:** compare small farms, food plants and memories of the hiring fair.
- **Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim and Catholic congregations, and "others":** check teaching on Sunday work and rest.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask Traveller organizations how a working day and good health are named; read outsiders' accounts as claims.
- **Portuguese and East Timorese workers in Dungannon; Lithuanian and Polish workers in Mid-Ulster; Filipino care staff:** keep apart; employers, shifts and languages differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for linen and farm history; mixed for the household round; thin for Cant, Tetum and Roma sources.
- **Lands:** with no lands yet settled beyond the "UK" carried in the tracker, the Virgo sheets take the six counties as the core and the Republic (Donegal, Cavan, Monaghan included) as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Columbanus; Ulster Scots for Orr; Irish for folklore replies; Portuguese, Tetum and Lithuanian for workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sectarian hiring is contested ground; give each account as an account, and leave fair-employment law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** hospital-staffing history was the likeliest source of a rule-covered name here; the domain sheet cites none, and withholds no one.
