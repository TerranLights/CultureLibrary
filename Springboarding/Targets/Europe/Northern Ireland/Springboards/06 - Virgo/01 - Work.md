## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Belfast and the Shankill; the Falls and west Belfast:** compare who reached apprenticeships in the Yard and the engineering shops.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check accounts of the "women's town" label and men's unemployment, each as told.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check how crofting, fishing and tourism jobs were mixed in one year.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Mournes and south Down:** compare Portavogie, Ardglass and Kilkeel crews and Mourne stone workers.
- **South Armagh and the orchards; the Lough Neagh shore:** check picking seasons and family farm labor.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Mid-Ulster:** compare Coalisland's pits with the newer engineering plants.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands:** check farm, forestry and tourism work against outward migration.
- **Ulster-Scots speakers in north Antrim and the Ards:** check the farm-service vocabulary still in use.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers; Portuguese and East Timorese workers in Dungannon:** keep apart, since their jobs, agencies and forums differ.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** check the shift from catering toward the professions across generations.
