## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (the Falls, the Shankill, east and north):** compare the voluntary bodies each district relied on for first aid and relief.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check service to the city through credit union, church and council, and who led each.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check guesthouse work and island ferry service.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough and south Down:** compare big-house service at Mount Stewart and Castle Ward with fishing-village help.
- **South Armagh and the Lough Neagh shore:** check what neighbors did for one another where state services were distrusted, as residents describe it.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone, Mid-Ulster and the Fermanagh lakelands:** compare farm girls' service in town with part-time military service from farms.
- **Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist and Catholic lay bodies, Brethren and Elim congregations:** check which service each teaches: visiting, preaching or relief.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check Traveller peer health workers as Travellers' own service to their community.
- **Filipino nurses and carers, Polish and Lithuanian care staff:** keep apart; check which home-care and hospital roles each fills.
- **Muslim communities in Belfast and Craigavon:** check soup runs and iftar meals offered to neighbors, as organizers describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military, church and civil-service history; mixed for domestic service; thin for the care workforce's own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for Patrick's life; Polish and Tagalog for care workers' accounts; Arabic for mosque outreach.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the UDR, the RUC and the Patten reforms are contested; give each account as an account. Police law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nursing and home-care writing was where a rule-covered name might have come up; this sheet points to unions, hospitals and lay bodies, and kept nothing back.
