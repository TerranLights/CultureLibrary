## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (Queen's and the south of the city; east Belfast; west Belfast):** compare who reached the sciences, and from which schools.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check Magee's place in the long argument over a university for the north-west.
- **Armagh city and its orchards:** compare the observatory and planetarium town with the countryside's weather and crop reckoning.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check sea and weather reckoning by fishers and ferrymen.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Mournes and south Down:** check how boat and tide knowledge was taught.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check engineering firms' design offices against farm record keeping.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands:** check water-level and flood reckoning on the Erne.
- **Grammar, secondary, integrated and Irish-medium schools:** compare mathematics and science teaching, without assuming a ranking.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check oral counting and dealing skills, as Travellers describe them, against school measures.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Polish and Lithuanian families:** keep apart; check what each expects of children's mathematics and how weekend schools teach it.
