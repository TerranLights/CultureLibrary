## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast (east and north; the Shankill and the Falls):** check how residents describe judging strangers' safety, as each district tells it.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare the city's two names as cues, and how people decide which to use with whom.
- **South Armagh and the Armagh orchards:** check growers' and buyers' quality judgments, and cross-border dealing as traders describe it.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check weather and sea reading by boatmen.
- **Islandmagee, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** check how the 1711 trial is remembered locally now.
- **Mid-Ulster, Tyrone, the Sperrins and the Lough Neagh shore:** check the dealer's and the mart's lore of the good beast.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and the Mournes:** compare hill-farm judging of sheep with lowland cattle.
- **Presbyterian, Free Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist and Catholic clergy:** compare how each tests a calling or a claimed vision.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check the horse-dealer's eye as Travellers present it.
- **Muslim households and Hong Kong Chinese families:** keep apart; check food certification and scam warnings separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church disputes and farm judging; mixed for everyday judgment of people; thin for newer communities' own warning networks.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for Cormac's teachings; Arabic for halal guidance; Cantonese and Polish for warnings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reading people by name, school or street is contested and painful ground; give each account as its own and never as a method.
- **Held for review:** halal certification is kept as a search, not a named certifier, until checking shows which bodies operate; nothing else was weighed for holding.
