## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lisburn and the Lagan valley:** compare damask and linen finishing standards with the coarser mill trade.
- **East Belfast and the Shankill; west Belfast and the Falls:** check band, banner and dance schools as places where precision is taught.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check the shirt factories' piece-rate inspection, as machinists recall it.
- **The Ards and Strangford Lough; the Mournes and south Down:** check boatbuilding and stone dressing standards.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin; the Fermanagh lakelands:** check whether visitor-trade standards changed how houses and gardens are kept.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** compare engineering plant inspection with farm work done "near enough".
- **South Armagh and the orchards:** check fruit grading and what a "perfect" apple means to a grower.
- **Holiness, Reformed and Catholic traditions:** compare teaching on perfection, scruples and grace.
- **Grammar, non-selective, integrated and Irish-medium schools:** check pressure as pupils describe it, without assuming a ranking.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check craft and horse-turnout standards as Travellers present them.
- **Polish and Filipino communities:** keep apart; family ritual and professional testing are different sources.
