## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare radical journalism and street comedy as forms of criticism of all parties.
- **Belfast (the Shankill and the Falls; east and north):** check which local critics each district's papers and clubs gave space to.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough:** check Porter's satire and how the 1798 memory carries criticism of authority.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** check how small communities voice criticism without open quarrel, as residents describe it.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and south Down:** check criticism of the security forces and of paramilitaries, each as residents chose to voice it.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster and the Lough Neagh shore:** check council and farm-paper letters for criticism of planning and water.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands:** check the *Impartial Reporter*'s editorial voice across the century.
- **Kirk sessions, presbyteries, Church of Ireland bishops and Catholic dioceses:** compare how each body disciplines or rebukes its own clergy and members.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check how Travellers' groups answer criticism in the press, and in their own media.
- **Muslim communities and the Hong Kong Chinese community:** keep apart; check how each responds to public criticism and who speaks for it.
- **Polish and Lithuanian readers:** check their own-language Facebook groups for complaints about services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, church and official complaints; mixed for family criticism; thin for newer communities' own-language debate.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for satire; Polish, Lithuanian and Arabic for community responses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ombudsman reports, sermons and cartoons touch contested ground; present each response as an account. Complaint law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the 2014 sermon dispute is kept as a public event without naming private people caught up in it; no pointer was withheld.
