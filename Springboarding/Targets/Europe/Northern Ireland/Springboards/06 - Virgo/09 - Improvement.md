## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare the 1960s growth-plan disputes with later regeneration in the city's own telling.
- **Belfast (the Shankill, the Falls, the docks and east Belfast):** check redevelopment, as residents of each district recall it.
- **Craigavon, Lurgan and Portadown:** check the new city as planners, incomers and older residents each describe it.
- **The Lough Neagh shore:** check the drainage schemes' cost to fishers, eel men and boat families.
- **The Ards, Strangford Lough, the Glens of Antrim and the Causeway Coast:** compare estate improvement on coast farms with tourism plans.
- **South Armagh, the orchards, the Mournes and south Down:** check what border regeneration funds paid for, and who sat on the boards.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins, Mid-Ulster and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check farm improvement, college training and land reclamation on hill and lake ground.
- **Irish learners and Ulster-Scots enthusiasts:** compare how each frames learning the language as self-improvement or as heritage.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check how Travellers answer schemes meant to "settle" or "improve" them, in their own words.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Syrian and Hong Kong Chinese learners of English:** keep apart; classes, needs and aims differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for plantation, estate and planning records; mixed for personal self-improvement; thin for newer communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern English and Irish for plantation sources; Irish for the language campaign; Polish and Arabic for ESOL learners.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** plantation, planning and regeneration are contested ground; present each "improvement" first through those it fell upon, and each other account as its own.
- **Held for review:** the plantation, estate and planning authors named are the full set weighed for improvement; none was dropped.
