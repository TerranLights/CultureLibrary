## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North and west Belfast (Ardoyne, the Shankill, the Falls):** compare community truth projects with inquest records for the same deaths.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** set the Saville findings beside the city's earlier Widgery record and both sides' memorials.
- **South Armagh, east Tyrone and the Fermanagh border:** check border-area victims' groups for which cases they press and which courts they use.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and the Ards:** check rural petty sessions for the ordinary docket away from conflict cases.
- **The Mournes and south Down; the Sperrins; the Lough Neagh shore:** check fishing and land cases for justice over resources, not conflict.
- **Ex-prisoners' groups, loyalist and republican:** compare their writings on justice with victims' groups' writings on the same cases.
- **Irish speakers:** check court and tribunal records for requests to plead in Irish before and after 2022.
- **Roma families in south Belfast:** check what reached court after the 2009 attacks; sources thin, start with NGO reports.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Portuguese defendants and witnesses:** check court-interpreting records separately; languages and caseloads differ.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check Traveller-led groups on contact with courts and prisons; official data may not separate them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** the Troubles left a deep paper trail of inquiries and judgments; pre-1922 local justice is patchier, and newer communities have written little of their own.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish, with German for Thurneysen; Irish; Polish, Lithuanian and Portuguese for interpreting records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** legacy files, closed material and victims' testimony; Bloody Sunday, internment and punishment attacks are contested, so each account stands as an account.
- **Held for review:** prison-memoir pointers were weighed for this sheet; none fell under the standing rules, so nothing is held.
