# Northern Ireland: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster usage; Old Irish for the law tracts); Tagalog, Cantonese, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and Arabic for newer communities' terms of fair dealing. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare housing and franchise records with both nationalist and unionist accounts of the 1960s.
- **Belfast, east and west (the shipyard and the Falls mills):** check hiring records against Fair Employment Tribunal cases from the same firms.
- **The Ards Peninsula, Strangford Lough and the Mournes:** check crew-share rules at Portavogie, Ardglass and Kilkeel for differences between ports.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Fermanagh lakelands:** check bog and grazing shares and how siblings split a small farm.
- **South Armagh and the Armagh orchards:** check picking rates and who set them.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check shares in commonage and island grazing.
- **Portuguese and East Timorese workers in Dungannon and Craigavon:** check wage and agency-fee complaints separately; employers and contracts differ.
- **Filipino nurses and care staff:** check pay-band and visa-fee disputes through union records.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check site allocation and service complaints through Traveller-led groups' submissions.
- **Women in work:** check equal-pay claims (the Northern Ireland civil-service equal-pay settlement, *check*) for whose work was ranked as equal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** tribunal decisions and labor statistics are plentiful; turbary and crew-share rules sit in scattered local papers and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for *Mellbretha* and *Bechbretha*; Ulster Scots dictionaries for children's claim words; Tagalog and Portuguese for workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination in housing and jobs before 1972 is fought over; each figure has rival readings, so cite the reading with the source.
- **Held for review:** union and health-service pay sources were weighed for the nurses' bullet; none named a person or body under the standing rules, so nothing is held.
