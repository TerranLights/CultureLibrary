*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast's interfaces (north Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls):** check flag-flying and peace-wall decisions for how each side's demands are weighed.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare council naming and shared spaces on both banks.
- **North Down and the Ards Peninsula; Strangford Lough:** check where "other" and cross-bloc transfers are highest; check fishers' and conservationists' cases on the reef.
- **Ballymena, Mid-Ulster and the Sperrins:** check local debates on Sunday opening against the 1997 Order's limits.
- **Fermanagh and south Armagh:** check border-district votes and how the Windsor Framework is argued there.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and the Causeway Coast:** check water quality and visitor-pressure reports as pictures of balance in the land.
- **Irish speakers and Ulster Scots speakers:** compare the two commissioners' first plans and the funding set beside each.
- **Voters who designate as neither unionist nor nationalist:** check how they describe the center and whether they call it balance or a third bloc.
- **Hong Kong Chinese and Muslim communities:** check health leaflets for ideas of moderation in food and rest (heating and cooling foods; Ramadan alongside shift work), each in its own language.
- **Polish and Lithuanian shift workers:** check work-hours complaints and rest-day patterns separately.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** statutes, election results and flag disputes are well recorded; ideas of bodily balance in newer communities are scarcely written down.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for the commissioners' material; Ulster Scots; Cantonese and Arabic for health leaflets.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** flags, designations and the post-Brexit arrangements are contested; each reading is an account.
- **Held for review:** the theory pointers were weighed both for the standing rules and for a spread between defenders and critics of the blocs model; none was set aside.
