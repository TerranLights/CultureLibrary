# Northern Ireland: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Irish (Ulster usage; Old Irish for judges' tracts); Ulster Scots; Arabic, Portuguese, Tetum, Tagalog, Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for words for "connections" among newer communities. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns of the Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check how officials handle cases among neighbors where *kenspeckle* faces make recusal hard.
- **Belfast's courts and the Shankill and Falls jury pools:** compare juror-intimidation claims with the record of non-jury trials.
- **Derry / Londonderry:** compare the city's inquiries and their reception on each bank of the Foyle.
- **South Armagh and east Tyrone:** check what local people said about the neutrality of police, army and courts; every account stands as an account.
- **The Ards, Mid-Ulster and the Sperrins:** check council hiring and grant decisions for how conflicts of interest were declared.
- **Lough Neagh shore and the Mournes:** check fishery and grazing licensing for whether incomers and locals faced the same rules.
- **Civil servants and special advisers:** compare their written codes with what the inquiry evidence records.
- **Syrian refugee families:** check how favoritism and intercession are discussed in resettlement advice; few sources, start with charities' reports.
- **East Timorese and Portuguese workers in Dungannon:** check whether jobs pass through agencies or through *cunha*.
- **Filipino health staff:** check union and employer material on promotion and *palakasan*.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check complaints of bias by service providers through Traveller-led groups; records are uneven.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** official codes and court studies are easy to reach; how people use connections is seldom written down and needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the judges' tracts; Arabic, Portuguese, Tetum and Tagalog for "connections" vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** juror-intimidation and security files are restricted; claims of bias by police and courts are contested on all sides.
- **Held for review:** for this concept the likeliest rule-covered items were studies of hiring by migrant background; none named here falls under the rules, so nothing is held.
