*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and Mid-Ulster:** check how exchange labor at threshing survived the coming of contractors.
- **The Fermanagh lakelands and south Armagh:** check wake food and funeral attendance as debts to be returned across the border.
- **North Belfast and the Shankill and Falls:** check how the press and each community's memorials frame killings said to answer earlier ones; every framing is an account.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check funeral thanks notices on both banks for differences in who is named.
- **The Glens of Antrim, Rathlin and the Causeway Coast:** check gifts of fish and turf between households and island-mainland exchange.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough; the Mournes:** check how boats help each other at sea and what is owed after.
- **The Lough Neagh shore:** check eel-fishing crews' shares of gear and help.
- **Polish and Lithuanian families:** compare *opłatek* and *Kūčios* customs and who is invited from outside the family.
- **Filipino health staff:** check pooled giving through associations and churches.
- **Muslim communities in Belfast and Craigavon:** check *zakat* and Eid giving through each mosque.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check help and loans within extended families through Traveller-led groups; outside accounts are thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funeral notices and folklife; mixed for parish dues; thin for newer communities' exchange customs.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish and German for Thurneysen; Polish, Lithuanian and Tagalog for community pages; Arabic for mosque notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "tit-for-tat" killings are a raw subject; families' accounts and armed groups' claims differ, and none stands as fact.
- **Held for review:** one pointer, set aside and kept as a category only: South Asian-heritage communities in Northern Ireland's lands.
