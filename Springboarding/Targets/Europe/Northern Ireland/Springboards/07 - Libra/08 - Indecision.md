# Northern Ireland: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster usage; Old Irish wisdom texts); Tagalog, Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and Cantonese for phrases that defer or leave a choice open. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry, Strabane and the Sperrins:** check what farmers and road campaigners on the A5 route say about the years of undecided plans.
- **Belfast and Mallusk (south Antrim):** check how residents and waste bodies spoke of decisions stalled without ministers.
- **Newry, the Mournes and south Down:** check the Narrow Water bridge plans for how a cross-border project was approved and paused.
- **Fermanagh and south Armagh:** check how border voters describe a constitutional choice left open.
- **Rathlin, the Glens of Antrim and the Causeway Coast:** test how long ferry contracts and coastal-erosion schemes stayed undecided, and who was blamed.
- **The Ards Peninsula and Strangford Lough; the Lough Neagh shore:** check how fishing and water decisions wait on several bodies.
- **Voters who answer "don't know" or "other":** check by age and district in poll tables before reading them as one group.
- **Quakers and other consensus bodies:** compare minute styles for how a decision is left pending without a vote.
- **Filipino and Muslim families:** check whether phrases that defer to providence appear in health and care advice, read in their own languages.
- **Polish and Portuguese workers:** check leaflets on settled status for how choices about staying are framed.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** ask Traveller-led groups which council site proposals stayed pending longest, rather than relying on council minutes alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public deadlock (Assembly suspensions, court rulings, polls); thin for personal indecision, which needs interviews or advice columns.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the wisdom texts; Tagalog and Arabic for faith-tinged deferral phrases; Ulster Scots dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** a border poll and the Assembly's collapses are contested; party claims of blame are accounts, not facts.
- **Held for review:** the capacity-law and care pointers were weighed for any rule-covered clinical or community study; none was found among those named, so nothing is held.
