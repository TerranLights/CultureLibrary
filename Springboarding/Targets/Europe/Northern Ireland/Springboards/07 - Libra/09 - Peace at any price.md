# Northern Ireland: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Irish (Ulster usage; Early Modern Irish for the Tudor submissions); Ulster Scots; Polish, Lithuanian, Cantonese, Tagalog and Romanian where newer communities' silence or reporting is concerned. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare the Lundy rite with nationalist accounts of the city's compromises over parades.
- **West Belfast (Divis and the Falls) and the Shankill:** check how silence about disappearances and extortion was kept and later broken, side by side.
- **South Armagh and east Tyrone:** check border families' statements on staying quiet about killings; read each as an account.
- **Mid-Ulster, the Sperrins and the Lough Neagh shore:** check rural parish memories of clerical abuse and who was told.
- **The Ards Peninsula, north Down and the Mournes:** check where shops and building firms reported extortion and where they did not.
- **The Glens of Antrim, the Causeway Coast and the Fermanagh lakelands:** check small-community reluctance to report domestic abuse against helpline data by district.
- **Prisoners' families and victims' families:** compare their submissions on the 1998 release terms.
- **Roma families from Romania:** check what was said, and to whom, before and after the 2009 departures.
- **Hong Kong Chinese restaurant owners:** check reports of racist attacks against what was reported to police.
- **Filipino and Polish workers:** check union material on raising complaints while on work visas.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check Traveller-led groups' reports on harassment left unreported.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** heavy for prisoner releases, the on-the-runs scheme and abuse inquiries; thin for everyday conflict avoidance, which needs advice columns and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern English for Tudor papers; Romanian for Roma sources; Polish and Tagalog for workers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disappearances, clerical abuse, extortion and early release touch living victims; each claim is an account, and some files stay closed.
- **Held for review:** the pointer most likely to be held was research on minority-business owners and extortion; the sources found name no community under the standing rules, so nothing is set aside.
