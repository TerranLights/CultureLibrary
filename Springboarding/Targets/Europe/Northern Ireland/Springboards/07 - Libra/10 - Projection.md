# Northern Ireland: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster usage); seventeenth-century English for pamphlets; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Romanian, Arabic, Cantonese and Tagalog for words for blame and the scapegoat. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Belfast, the Shankill and the Falls:** compare murals and bonfires on each side for the image of the other they carry.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check how each bank's papers describe the other bank's commemorations.
- **South Armagh:** check outsider labels (from British press and army sources) against residents' own descriptions.
- **Mid-Ulster and east Tyrone:** check press nicknames for the area against local accounts.
- **North Down, the Ards and Strangford Lough:** test whether suburban "neither" voters describe the blocs as the source of trouble.
- **Fermanagh, the Sperrins and the Lough Neagh shore:** check farmers' blame for disease and water quality: badgers, incomers, agencies.
- **Rathlin and the Glens of Antrim:** test whether folk tales name kin, neighbors or strangers as bearers of the evil eye.
- **Muslim communities in Belfast and Portadown:** check their own statements after the 2014 sermon and other incidents.
- **The Jewish community of Belfast:** check its statements when Middle East flags appear in local streets.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** check community reports of pandemic-era abuse against police records.
- **Roma families from Romania; Polish and Lithuanian workers:** separate press blame for jobs, housing and crime by group and year.
- **Irish Travellers (Mincéirí):** check Traveller-led media monitoring for who is blamed when crimes are reported.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** much on sectarian images and enemy framing; thin on projection inside couples and families, which needs clinical or advice sources.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century English for Temple; Romanian, Arabic and Cantonese for minority statements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1641 accounts, bonfire effigies and the 2014 sermon are contested; give each account as an account and quote no slur.
- **Held for review:** the pointer most likely to touch the rules was work on hostility toward minority faiths in the six counties; the items named here do not, and nothing is held.
