## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **West Belfast (the Falls, the Gaeltacht Quarter) and east Belfast:** set wake-house custom beside funeral-home use.
- **Derry / Londonderry, the Foyle and the Sperrins:** test whether keening memories in Tyrone and Derry schools differ from Antrim's.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** look for banshee and death-omen lore carried across the Moyle.
- **Ards and the Strangford shore:** seek Ulster Scots words for coffin, bell and dowry in Down sources.
- **South Armagh, the Mournes and the Fermanagh lakelands:** compare farm succession on family land that straddles the border (Monaghan, Cavan and Donegal only as comparison).
- **Churches (Catholic; Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist; Free Presbyterian, Brethren, Elim):** compare funeral orders and cremation rules, one by one.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** seek Traveller-led accounts of funerals and grave-tending before press reports.
- **Polish and Lithuanian households:** compare November grave-visiting with repatriation of the dead.
- **Hong Kong Chinese and East Timorese households:** treat grave-sweeping and the lifting of mourning as separate questions.
- **Muslim communities and the Jewish community of Belfast:** check where each buries, how fast, and in which cemetery sections.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for saga texts, cemeteries and statistics; mixed for savings custom; thin for Cant, Tetum and Roma death ways (community workers are the likelier route).
- **Lands:** the tracker gives only "UK" as the carried land and names no units; the Scorpio sheets treat the six counties as the core and the rest of the island as a mirror, leaving the lines to the author.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for the *aided* tales; Ulster Irish for lament terms; Ulster Scots; Cantonese and Tetum for mourning terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Troubles deaths, abortion and sexual policing touch living people; each account stands as its own. Lodge rites are known only as far as members publish them.
- **Held for review:** courtship and burial studies of the neighboring Irish counties were weighed and kept as comparison only; no pointer fell under the standing rules.
