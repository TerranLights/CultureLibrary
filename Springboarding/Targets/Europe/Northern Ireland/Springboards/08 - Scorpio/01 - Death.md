## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North Belfast (Ardoyne, Tiger's Bay, the New Lodge):** compare street memorials and anniversary Masses or services across the three districts.
- **Derry / Londonderry, the Foyle and Limavady:** trace the hospice movement and the Roe valley's funeral tune.
- **South Armagh and the Newry border:** check how Kingsmill and other rural killings are kept present by each set of families.
- **Antrim's Ulster Scots country (Ballycarry, Islandmagee, the Braid):** look for older words for dying in weaver verse and session books.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and the Lough Neagh shore:** compare death omens and lament memories in rural school returns.
- **Fermanagh lakelands:** check island graveyards and boat funerals in local histories.
- **Downpatrick and Lecale:** compare how Catholic and Church of Ireland pilgrims visit the saint's grave.
- **Catholic, Presbyterian, Church of Ireland and Free Presbyterian clergy:** compare teaching on purgatory, prayer for the dead and assurance.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller organizations about grieving customs and early deaths, before using outside reports.
- **Filipino nurses' families:** check whether *lamay* and *pasiyam* are kept here for deaths back home.
- **Muslim communities and the Belfast Jewish community:** record where each has burial ground and how quickly a burial can be arranged.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for headstones, war memory and Troubles memory; mixed for grief practice; thin for Roma and East Timorese mourning (ask community workers).
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the relic accounts; Ulster Scots for Orr; Tagalog and Arabic for mourning phrases.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Troubles deaths, suicide and infant burial touch living families; each telling is its own account. Assisted dying and inquests are law (Libra).
- **Held for review:** grief-counseling programs aimed at the whole UK were weighed and passed over for lacking a Northern Ireland breakdown; nothing sits under the standing rules.
