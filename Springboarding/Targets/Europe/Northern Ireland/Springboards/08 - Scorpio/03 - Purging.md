# Northern Ireland: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English and Ulster Scots (disgust and tidying words); Irish (Ulster dialect); medieval Latin for the pilgrimage texts; Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian, Cantonese and Romani for fasting, confession and purity words. **Concept in one line** (proposed): removing what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt from a body, mind, group, institution or place, so that the whole is clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **East Belfast, the shipyard and the Short Strand:** set 1920 workplace expulsions against later accounts from both sides of the river.
- **West Belfast and the H-Block families:** compare how the protest's dirt is remembered by former prisoners, their families and staff.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and south Tyrone border parishes:** test displacement claims through family, church and press records, account by account.
- **The Foyle and Faughan valley:** set Mobuoy residents, farmers and anglers apart from official statements.
- **Lough Neagh shore (Toome, Ardboe, Antrim town):** compare eel fishers', farmers' and water users' accounts of the pollution.
- **Clogher diocese parishes in Fermanagh and Tyrone:** count which parishes still organize Lough Derg buses.
- **Presbyterian, Free Presbyterian and Methodist bodies:** keep each one's temperance and separation rules apart.
- **Roma households in Belfast:** check purity terms only with Roma-led organizations; outsider ethnographies of other countries' Roma do not stand in.
- **Muslim and Polish households:** compare fasting seasons with shift work in food plants (Dungannon, Portadown, Ballymena).
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** check whether herbal "toxin" remedies are mentioned to Western clinics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1920, the prison protests and pollution cases; mixed for fasting and folk cleansing; thin for Roma purity rules, which may be private to the group.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for Saltrey; Ulster Scots and Irish disgust and penance words; Romani with care; Arabic and Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "ethnic cleansing", "pogrom" and the no-wash protest are contested labels, so each is given as an account; Roma purity rules may be restricted knowledge.
- **Held for review:** eating-disorder services were weighed, but the Northern Ireland sources found were clinical leaflets with nothing on meaning; left for the later research; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
