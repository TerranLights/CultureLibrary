## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lower Falls and Divis:** set the 1970 curfew's accounts against later army and civilian records.
- **West Belfast's estates and north Belfast interfaces:** compare how residents describe control by armed groups and by police.
- **Bangor and north Down:** use the Comgall rule to frame monastic control, then compare the town's later churches' discipline.
- **Holylands and south Belfast:** separate students', long-term residents' and landlords' views of who should control the streets.
- **Fermanagh lakelands:** compare flood control seen from Enniskillen, the islands and the Donegal end (comparison only).
- **The Mournes and south Down:** look at how valley families and Belfast water bodies speak of the catchment.
- **Dungannon, Portadown and Ballymena:** compare Filipino, Portuguese, East Timorese and Polish workers' accounts of agency control, kept apart by group.
- **Disabled people, patients and their families:** compare their accounts of restraint with staff and inspectors' accounts.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller organizations about moves along by police and councils, and how control of stopping places is described.
- **Free Presbyterian, Brethren and Catholic families:** compare teaching on headship and obedience in the home, as each publishes it.
