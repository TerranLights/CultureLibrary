## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ballymena, the Braid and Mid-Antrim:** compare unionist "conditional loyalty" in local rhetoric with trust in Westminster at key moments (1912, 1985, 2019).
- **West Belfast and Derry's Bogside:** check accounts of trust and distrust toward state bodies other than the police, from residents' own media.
- **Armagh orchards, the Ards and the Glens:** survey honesty-box survival as a local trust test.
- **Fermanagh and Tyrone farming districts:** compare mart dealing and verbal contracts, sale by sale.
- **North Belfast and the Shankill:** set doorstep-lending reports against credit-union use.
- **Families of children treated in Belfast hospitals:** read inquiry evidence for how trust was lost and partly rebuilt.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller groups about trust in health and housing services, keeping their accounts first.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** keep their health-trust surveys apart; their organizations and sources differ.
- **Hong Kong Chinese elders and newer Hong Kong arrivals:** compare which advisers each group relies on.
- **East Timorese in Dungannon:** check for any community survey; likely none, so ask community workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional trust (inquiries, surveys, elections); mixed for everyday trust (honesty boxes, marts); thin for newer communities and the Tetum-speaking group.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for the Airgíalla poem; Ulster Scots; Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for community reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hyponatraemia deaths and illegal lending involve living families and possible criminal matters; trust in police and courts is Capricorn's, not this sheet's.
- **Held for review:** large UK trust barometers were weighed, but most give no separate Northern Ireland figure; left for the research stage; nothing was set aside on standing-rule grounds.
