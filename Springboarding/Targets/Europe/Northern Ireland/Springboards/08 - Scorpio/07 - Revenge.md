# Northern Ireland: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect; Middle Irish for the saga); Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese and Cantonese for revenge and grudge words. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing, or deter the next wrong; forgiveness, payment and letting go are its alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Armagh (Loughgall, the Diamond, Portadown):** compare 1790s raid-and-reprisal records with later Orange and nationalist memory.
- **Antrim and north Down farm districts:** set the Hearts of Steel record against landlord and tenant accounts of the same burnings.
- **The Shankill and the Lower Shankill estates:** check the 2000 feud through residents' accounts as well as press.
- **West Belfast and Divis:** compare the 1975 and 1987 republican feuds in families' own words.
- **Derry / Londonderry, Bogside and Creggan:** follow Moore's forgiveness story in local press and parish life.
- **North Belfast interfaces (Ardoyne, Glenbryn, the New Lodge):** read youth-work reports on retaliation among teenagers.
- **South Armagh and the Newry border:** check how rural killings and answering killings are described by each set of families.
- **Victims' groups of each background:** compare what each says it seeks (truth, punishment, recognition); keep them apart.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller-led organizations how disputes are settled; treat press "feud" headlines as outsiders' framing.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Portuguese residents:** check whether their own press covers disputes and how they are named.
- **Hong Kong Chinese residents:** check Cantonese-language community press for how quarrels are mediated; likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for paramilitary retaliation and 1790s disturbances; mixed for youth and neighbor revenge; thin for newer communities' grudges.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish for *Brislech*; Ulster Irish; Polish; Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reprisal killings and feuds involve living families and contested claims; never repeat unconfirmed names of perpetrators; each account stays its own.
- **Held for review:** "honor culture" studies built on other regions were considered and left out, since they would frame the culture from outside; nothing was held under the standing rules.
