*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect); Cantonese for privacy terms used by Hong Kong families; Polish, Lithuanian and Arabic for secret and privacy words. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyrone and Fermanagh uplands (the Sperrins, Cuilcagh foothills):** compare Mass-rock and poitín memory, two kinds of hidden practice in one landscape.
- **Antrim and Down Presbyterian districts (Ballynahinch, Saintfield, Ballymena):** check 1798 secrecy through family papers and church memory.
- **Portadown, Armagh and the Orange heartland:** set lodge histories against outsiders' accounts of degrees.
- **Catholic parishes in Down and Connor:** compare Hibernian and sodality records with Orange ones for what each keeps private.
- **Police and prison officers' families in mixed towns:** check memoirs for how children learned not to tell.
- **Belfast and Derry LGBT communities:** separate men's and women's histories; their sources and risks differed.
- **Women who traveled for abortions, and families who campaigned against abortion:** keep each group's testimony in its own frame.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, Muslim and Jewish communities:** compare reasons for keeping private (health, safety, faith); do not merge them.
- **Mincéirí:** note that the Cant is held by the community; ask Traveller-led groups what, if anything, they choose to share about it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fraternal orders, 1798 and penal-era sites; mixed for family secrets and the closet; thin for Cant (rightly) and for newer communities' privacy norms.
- **Languages needed:** Ulster Scots and Irish for secrecy idioms; Cantonese; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lodge rites are known only as members publish them; the Cant is the Mincéirí's own knowledge and is not to be collected without consent; abortion and police-family secrecy touch living people.
- **Held for review:** published exposés of Orange and Masonic rituals were weighed and left out as outsider claims of uncertain accuracy; the standing rules removed nothing from this sheet.
