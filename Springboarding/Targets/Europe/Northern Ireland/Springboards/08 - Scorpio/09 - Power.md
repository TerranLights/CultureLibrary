# Northern Ireland: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect; Early Modern Irish for lordship texts); Tetum, Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for words about pull, rule and connections. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used (legitimate authority sits in Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** set ward-boundary disputes of the Stormont years against each side's later account of the city's government.
- **Tyrone, Tullaghoge and the Sperrins:** read Gaelic lordship texts alongside planter estate records for the same ground.
- **East Belfast shipyard districts and the Lagan valley:** compare industrial employers' and unions' power, decade by decade, using Gibbon and labor records.
- **West and north Belfast:** look at former prisoners' standing and residents' views of it, in each bloc separately.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and the Colebrooke estate country:** set Brookeborough's local power base against Fermanagh nationalists' accounts of the same years.
- **Rural Orange and GAA parishes (Armagh, Fermanagh, south Derry):** compare how hall, club and lodge shape local influence, each on its own sources.
- **Women in politics across parties:** check memoirs and interviews for how power was gained or blocked.
- **Polish and Filipino residents:** keep their electoral participation apart; registration and party routes differ.
- **East Timorese in Dungannon:** check for community leadership structures carried from home (*ukun* terms).
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller-led groups about representation on councils and boards; record their own account first.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Stormont-era power, unionist and nationalist politics, and church bodies; mixed for paramilitary influence; thin for newer communities' power, still emerging.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for *Ceart Uí Néill*; Tetum and Arabic for community leadership terms; Polish; Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination under Stormont, paramilitary sway and the 2024 change are contested; present each claim as its holder's account; legitimacy belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** elite-network studies of boards and quangos were considered; none confined to the six counties came to mind, so they wait for the research stage; the standing rules caused no omission here.
