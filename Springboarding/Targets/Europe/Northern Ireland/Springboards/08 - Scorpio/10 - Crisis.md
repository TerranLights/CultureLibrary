## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Lagan valley and north Antrim farms:** compare accounts of the 1740-41 frost in estate and church records.
- **Tyrone and the Sperrins:** test whether Big Wind dating survives in families' telling.
- **North and west Belfast (Bombay Street, Ardoyne, Farringdon Gardens):** keep each side's August 1969 accounts as separate testimonies.
- **East Belfast and the shipyard streets:** read Blitz memory beside wartime industry records.
- **Rural Fermanagh, Tyrone and the Glens of Antrim:** compare how isolated households met Storm Éowyn.
- **Oil-heated rural homes and Belfast's gas-heated terraces:** compare fuel-crisis reports by heating type.
- **Health workers (nurses, junior doctors, care staff):** separate each group's crisis account; their unions and sources differ.
- **Ukrainian arrivals and their hosts:** set arrivals' accounts against host families' experience.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers in 2008-10:** check whether the crash prompted return home, through community press.
- **Hong Kong Chinese elders:** check how the pandemic and isolation were met through Cantonese community services.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller-led groups how pandemic rules on stopping and gathering affected families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, wartime and Troubles crises; mixed for household hardship; thin for newer communities' crisis coping.
- **Languages needed:** Irish for storm and famine lore; Ukrainian and Polish; Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** August 1969 and the Troubles' naming are contested; the pandemic inquiry involves bereaved families; give each account as its own.
- **Held for review:** end-times preaching was considered as a crisis frame, but no sermon series specific to the six counties came to mind; left for the research stage, and the standing rules took nothing out.
