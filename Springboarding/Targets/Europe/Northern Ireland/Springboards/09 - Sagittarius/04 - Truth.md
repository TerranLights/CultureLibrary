## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** compare Bogside, Fountain and Waterside accounts of 1972, each as told.
- **West and north Belfast:** check whether families' doubts about the HET differ by area and by the force involved.
- **East Belfast and the Shankill:** check loyalist community-history projects as a record set beside official accounts.
- **Fermanagh lakelands:** study the annalist families of Lough Erne as the oldest local keepers of record.
- **Mid-Ulster, Tyrone and south Armagh:** compare rural families' accounts of unresolved killings against police files.
- **Presbyterian and Free Presbyterian communities:** keep their evolution debates apart; their colleges and periodicals differ.
- **'Others' and mixed families:** look for accounts that refuse either side's frame, in integrated-school and cross-community projects.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** separate their news channels; they rely on different media at home.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, old and new:** compare messaging use and trust across arrival waves.
- **Syrian refugee families and other Arabic speakers:** check satellite and phone news habits in resettlement records.
- **Mincéirí:** ask Traveller groups how press reports about Travellers are checked inside the community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, press and the science-religion clash; mixed for school teaching; thin for newcomers' news habits, which need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Irish and Latin for the annals; Polish, Lithuanian, Cantonese and Arabic for community channels.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** inquiries, the legacy body and Bloody Sunday are contested; each account stands as its source's; families are living. Law on legacy goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Republic-based inquiries into cross-border killings were weighed as comparison only; no truth-telling source here fell under the standing rules.
