## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** set Colmcille exile tradition beside the city's emigrant-port history.
- **The Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** compare seasonal crossings to Scotland with permanent emigration from the same families.
- **The Ards and Strangford Lough:** check eighteenth-century emigrant ship lists against congregational histories.
- **The Sperrins and Fermanagh:** follow rambling-house visiting and its decline.
- **Belfast's Shankill and Falls:** compare holiday destinations in the Twelfth fortnight by district.
- **Students from grammar and non-selective schools:** check who goes to Britain for university, using UCAS data.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** compare travel home with settling, using flight and school data.
- **Filipino and East Timorese newcomers:** check each community's own accounts of the journey here and of visits home, kept apart because their migration is recorded in different sources.
- **Hong Kong arrivals and Syrian refugee families:** separate chosen and forced journeys; their sources differ.
- **Mincéirí:** start from Traveller groups' own accounts of the road, the fairs and stopping places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration history and tourism statistics; mixed for holiday habits; thin for Timorese and Filipino journeys.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for *Immram Brain*; Early Modern Irish for O'Donnell; Cant; Tagalog, Tetum and Arabic for migrant journeys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Troubles-era departures and refugee routes involve trauma; "Londonderry" and "Derry" both stand in port records; Traveller stopping places are a live dispute (statutes: Libra).
- **Held for review:** diaspora sources from Appalachia, Canada and Australia were weighed and kept as comparison only; nothing set aside under the rules.
