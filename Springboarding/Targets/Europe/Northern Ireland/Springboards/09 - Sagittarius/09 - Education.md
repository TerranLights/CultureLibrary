## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** set the 1965 university decision beside today's Magee expansion debate.
- **Coleraine and the Causeway Coast:** check how the town that won the university tells the story.
- **West Belfast's Gaeltacht Quarter:** follow Irish-medium pupils from primary to university.
- **Ards and north Down:** look at the Presbyterian academy tradition and Ulster-Scots teaching in the same districts.
- **Armagh city and the orchards:** compare the medieval school tradition with the present college and observatory.
- **Fermanagh and the Sperrins:** check rural school-leavers' routes to Britain or to further education.
- **Ex-prisoners of each background:** keep republican and loyalist education accounts apart.
- **Polish and Lithuanian families:** check supplementary-school records separately.
- **Hong Kong arrivals, Chinese international students and earlier Hong Kong Chinese families:** compare three distinct routes into higher education.
- **Syrian and other Arabic-speaking families:** check weekend schools and university access through resettlement reports.
- **Mincéirí:** use school-leaver data and Traveller groups' own accounts of further study.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for university history and official statistics; mixed for prison education and Ulster-Scots teaching; thin for Timorese and Traveller higher study.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Middle Irish for Armagh's schools; Ulster Scots; Polish, Cantonese and Arabic for community schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** university siting and denominational colleges are contested; school leavers' data on small groups risks identifying pupils.
