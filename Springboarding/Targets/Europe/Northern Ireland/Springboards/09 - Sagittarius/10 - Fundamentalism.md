# Northern Ireland: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English; Ulster Scots; Irish (Ulster dialect); Polish, Lithuanian, Cantonese and Arabic for how strict movements are named in each community. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular or political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ballymena, the Braid and north Antrim:** check Free Presbyterian, Reformed and Brethren congregation counts against the census.
- **East Belfast (Ravenhill Road) and Crossgar:** compare the movement's founding sites and their present congregations.
- **The Causeway Coast:** look at local responses to the 2012 exhibit, by church and visitor group.
- **Mid-Ulster and Tyrone:** follow gospel halls and Seceder heritage in rural congregations.
- **Fermanagh and south Armagh:** set Catholic traditionalist groups beside parish life.
- **Mediating evangelicals and 'others':** compare ECONI-style and secular critics, whose sources differ.
- **Leavers and second-generation members:** check accounts by sex and age.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Catholics:** check whether traditionalist groups draw from these communities, through their own notices.
- **Hong Kong Chinese Christians:** check how congregations describe their doctrinal positions.
- **Muslim communities:** check how imams and members name currents in their own faith, rather than press labels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Free Presbyterian history and creationism disputes; mixed for Brethren (few written records); thin for Catholic traditionalist and Muslim self-descriptions.
- **Languages needed:** Ulster Scots for Seceder sources; Polish and Lithuanian for parish notices; Cantonese and Arabic for congregation self-descriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist" is a charge many reject; record each group's own term; avoid linking any faith community to violence. Religious-liberty statutes belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** scholarship that sorts faiths by a cross-religious "fundamentalism" model was weighed and kept as background, since it frames groups from outside; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
