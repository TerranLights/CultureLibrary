# Northern Ireland: The domain, authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Mid-Ulster and Belfast English; Ullans; Ulster Irish, with Old Irish and Latin for early texts; Traveller Cant; for newer residents' papers, Arabic, Cantonese, Tetum, Portuguese, Lithuanian and Polish. **Concept in one line:** authority and duty: who gives orders, who follows them and what each owes; career, status, reputation, and the ways of age, time, rank, order and power are its folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Belfast's four quarters, with the Shankill and Falls apart:** compare how school and job are used to place a stranger.
- **Derry / Londonderry and the Foyle:** check civic office-holding since the 1970s against earlier council control; both names in use.
- **Estate villages and harbors of the Ards Peninsula:** set estate villages beside fishing harbors for traces of the landlord order.
- **South Armagh; the orchard country round Loughgall:** compare whose authority people cite (parish, party, family) in local oral histories.
- **Tyrone, the Sperrins and Cookstown-Magherafelt:** check farm succession and hiring memories for how age and rank were settled.
- **Fermanagh's lakes; the Glens and Rathlin:** check where estate papers and Irish-language memory overlap.
- **Protestant denominations, from Church of Ireland to gospel hall and Elim:** compare how each chooses and removes its leaders.
- **Gaeltacht Quarter Irish speakers and mid-Antrim Ulster Scots speakers:** check which words for "boss" and "elder" each keeps.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** start with Traveller-run groups on family authority before outside studies.
- **Hong Kong Chinese, long-settled and post-2021:** compare the two waves; their records sit in different places.
- **East Timorese residents in Dungannon:** look for associations that record customary leadership; Tetum sources likely scarce.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for party politics, estates and status data; thinner for age and time ways; power-distance indexes score only the UK as a whole.
- **Lands:** "UK" is the only pre-war holding the row records, and no territory has been fixed for Northern Ireland yet; Capricorn's sheets therefore treat the six counties as home ground and use the Republic, Ulster's three southern counties included, purely as a foil.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old Irish to read the Armagh claim; Ulster Scots for village sketches; Tetum and Cantonese for association papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the legitimacy of the state is itself contested; each party's account of authority stays its own. Law belongs to the Law sheet (spirit) and to Libra (letter).
- **Held for review:** for a domain this broad, class surveys and landowner returns were the likeliest to touch a restricted name or community; neither does, so nothing is held.
