*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Maiden City (Derry / Londonderry) and its Foyle hinterland:** set the city's long campaigns for a university and for jobs beside personal success stories; both names in use.
- **Craigavon, Lurgan and Portadown:** check how residents and planners measure the new city's promises against outcomes.
- **East Belfast's shipyard and aircraft trades:** check apprenticeship records for what a "good trade" opened, and to whom.
- **Grammar and non-grammar schools in Tyrone and the Sperrins:** compare stated aims of pupils from each, within one town.
- **Gaeltacht Quarter families:** check how Irish-medium schooling is spoken of as ambition for a child and for a language.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families, first and second generation:** compare parents' and children's stated aims in Cantonese and English sources.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** check how far recognition of homeland qualifications shapes stated plans.
- **Filipino residents:** check community newsletters for plans to stay, move on or return.
- **East Timorese in Dungannon:** check how Portuguese passports and onward moves figure in plans; Tetum sources scarce.
- **Mincéirí:** check Traveller-run groups' own accounts of schooling and enterprise before outside reports.
- **Fermanagh and the Glens:** compare young people who leave with those who stay on farms, in local oral histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schooling, emigration and memoir; mixed for present attitudes; thin for newer communities' own writing on ambition.
- **Languages needed:** Ulster Irish for Mac Gabhann; Cantonese and Tetum for community material; Portuguese for Timorese residents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** selection at eleven and fair-employment history are contested; success stories in community press are each a community's own account.
- **Held for review:** ambition-and-mobility studies of minority pupils were weighed; the ones listed cover all communities without naming any restricted group, so nothing is held.
