## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fermanagh estates (Castle Coole, Crom, Florence Court) and their tenant townlands:** compare memories of the big house from inside and outside its walls.
- **The Londonderry plantation's livery-company villages (Moneymore, Draperstown, Eglinton):** check whether the planned layout still sorts residents.
- **The Ards and north Down's farm country:** check farmer, servant and cottier ranks in Lyttle's district against census returns.
- **Armagh city:** compare the two cathedrals' public precedence on civic occasions.
- **Belfast's Malone, the Shankill and the Falls:** check address as rank in property listings and in residents' talk.
- **The Lough Neagh shore and the Sperrins:** check how standing within farming and fishing families is reckoned (eldest son, the one who stays).
- **Hong Kong Chinese family associations:** check how elder and younger are ranked at gatherings, in their own Cantonese sources.
- **East Timorese residents in Dungannon:** check whether *dato* and *liurai* standing is kept up abroad; few sources.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** compare place on the job ladder with homeland qualifications, using census tables and union surveys.
- **Mincéirí and settled neighbors:** check how Traveller organizations describe being ranked by settled society, in their own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for estates, plantation and official grades; mixed for present class talk; thin for rank inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for *Uraicecht Becc*; medieval Latin for the Armagh registers; Ulster Scots dialogue in Lyttle.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** community background and rank are intertwined in fair-employment history; titles such as "deputy" and the names of places stay each side's own usage.
- **Held for review:** the census tables by country of birth could have pulled in a restricted community by name; they are used here as a whole table, with no group singled out, so nothing is set aside.
