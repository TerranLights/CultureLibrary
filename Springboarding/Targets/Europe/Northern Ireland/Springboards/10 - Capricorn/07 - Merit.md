*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grammar and non-grammar pupils in north Belfast, Derry and Newry:** compare the stated reasons each gives for where they ended up.
- **Catholic and Protestant civil servants of the 1950s and 1960s:** check memoirs and oral histories on promotion and the ceiling each met.
- **Shipyard and engineering apprentices of east Belfast and Larne:** check whether apprenticeships were won by exam or by being spoken for.
- **Irish-medium families in the Gaeltacht Quarter and in Derry:** compare their admissions ideas with selective grammar schools'.
- **Rural Tyrone and Fermanagh families:** check how the transfer test is spoken of where the nearest grammar is far away.
- **Hong Kong Chinese families:** compare *有料* (ability) with *走後門* (back door) in Cantonese community talk.
- **Portuguese and East Timorese residents:** check how *cunha* and recognition of Portuguese qualifications shape their stories of getting work.
- **Arabic-speaking residents, Syrian families among them:** check how *wāsṭa* is contrasted with local hiring in their own accounts.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** check stories of qualifications not recognized and how they are told.
- **Mincéirí:** check Traveller groups' own accounts of schooling and testing, before attainment tables.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for academic selection and fair employment; mixed for survey attitudes; thin where qualifications earned abroad meet local hiring, which few have written up.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the poets' grades; Portuguese, Cantonese and Arabic for connection terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** selection at eleven, fair employment and the teachers' exception are contested; each position is an account. Equality law itself belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the social-mobility and attainment pointers were the ones that might name a restricted community; the listed ones report all groups together, so none is held.
