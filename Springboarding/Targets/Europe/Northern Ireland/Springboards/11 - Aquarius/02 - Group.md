# Northern Ireland: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English (street, workplace and terrace usage); Ulster Scots; Ulster Irish, with Old Irish for the Ulster tales; Tetum, Portuguese, Lithuanian, Cantonese and Arabic for newer residents' group words. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" ends and "them" begins, and what acting together is expected.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mid-Ulster flute-band villages and Belfast's lodge districts:** check whether band and lodge draw on the same families, from band histories.
- **GAA parishes of south Armagh and the Sperrins:** compare club rules on transfers between parishes.
- **East Belfast's former shipyard streets:** check workplace and union records for when trade loyalty and creed loyalty pulled apart.
- **Derry / Londonderry's Bogside and Fountain:** both city names in use; check each estate's own groups and how they mark their edges.
- **Alliance, Green and other "others":** check survey items for people who refuse both blocs and the groups they form instead.
- **Irish speakers and Ulster Scots enthusiasts:** compare who joins language bodies and from which background.
- **Mincéirí extended families:** check Traveller-written sources on how a family group decides and who speaks for it.
- **Tetum-speaking East Timorese in Dungannon:** check how the *ita*/*ami* distinction appears in their own group talk; material likely thin.
- **Lithuanian and Polish associations:** compare their rules of membership and who runs them.
- **Muslim congregations in Belfast, Craigavon and Derry:** check how each mosque committee is chosen.
- **Hong Kong Chinese church and association members:** check overlap between the two kinds of group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bloc voting and workforce composition; mixed for how groups decide inside; thin for newer residents' group structures.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for the Ulster tales; Tetum for the inclusive and exclusive "we"; Cantonese for association papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loyal orders, bands, GAA, the 1974 strike and the 1920 shipyard expulsions are contested; each account stays an account. Paramilitary groups belong to other signs' sheets, and their membership lists are protected.
- **Held for review:** for group, the likely trouble lay in congregation and association lists of newer residents; those named here needed no setting aside.
