# Northern Ireland: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English, including the vocabulary of the 1998 settlement; Ulster Scots of Antrim and Down; Ulster Irish; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Tetum, Cantonese and Arabic for minority and newcomer sources. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures part ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry and Dungannon:** both city names in use; compare housing and franchise records of the 1960s with local accounts from each side.
- **West Belfast and east Belfast:** check deprivation scores beside each district's own talk of being left behind.
- **Fermanagh and south Tyrone farms against the Ards and north Down:** check how land, wages and schooling differ, using census tables, not reputation.
- **Women in the Belfast linen mills and today's workforce:** compare mill histories with current pay data.
- **Free Presbyterian and Brethren congregations against the Church of Ireland:** check what each teaches on equal standing in church office, and whether women may hold it.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** check Traveller-led groups on accommodation and schooling, against official figures.
- **Roma families in south Belfast:** check what advocacy and Roma-led groups record on access to housing and work.
- **Portuguese and East Timorese workers around Dungannon and Portadown:** check how equal pay and conditions appear in their own groups' and unions' records.
- **Filipino residents:** check community associations for complaints and campaigns on equal treatment; sources likely sparse.
- **Hong Kong Chinese and Muslim households:** check racist-incident reporting separately for each, since the reporting routes differ.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for discrimination debates, deprivation and pay; mixed for leveling customs and manners; thin for newer groups' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Antrim and Down Scots to read the weavers on laird and laborer; Portuguese and Tetum where workers' groups record pay disputes; Cantonese and Arabic for incident and community reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discrimination before 1968, "parity of esteem" and selection at eleven are contested; each source is an account. Anti-discrimination statutes and the equality duty of 1998 belong to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** attitude surveys and incident reports were weighed for equality, since they sort people by origin; none named here was set aside.
