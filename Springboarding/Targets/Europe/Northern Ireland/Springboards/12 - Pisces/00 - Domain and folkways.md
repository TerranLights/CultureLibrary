# Northern Ireland: The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English (Belfast and Mid-Ulster speech); Ulster Scots (Ullans); Ulster Irish, with Old Irish for saga and annal terms; the Travellers' Cant; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Tetum, Cantonese and Arabic for newer residents. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain: how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape (the hidden self, confinement and institutions, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence); magic ways are its folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Glens of Antrim and Rathlin:** check how Glens storytellers sort fairy from ghost material.
- **South Armagh and the Mournes:** compare Slieve Gullion parishes with the Kingdom of Mourne for cures and the good people.
- **Shankill and Falls, east and north Belfast:** check how memorial gardens and murals handle the Troubles dead; each is an account.
- **Derry / Londonderry (both names in use):** look for Gransha hospital histories beside city ghost lore.
- **Presbyterian, Church of Ireland, Methodist, Brethren and Elim congregations; Catholic parishes:** separate sermons on providence and the unseen by tradition.
- **"Others" of neither tradition:** check humanist funerals as their own record.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** begin with Traveller-led groups' publications on wakes and grave visits.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** follow All Souls' candles in parish notices.
- **East Timorese in Dungannon:** sources likely scarce; ask the community's associations.
- **Hong Kong Chinese; Muslim communities, Syrian families among them:** compare funeral and spirit vocabulary through each group's own centers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for asylums and suicide statistics; mixed for fairy and cure lore, held in folklore archives; thin for newer residents.
- **Extent of the target:** no post-war boundary is on record (the tracker names only "UK"). The Pisces sheets read the present six counties as the core; Donegal, Cavan, Monaghan and the rest of the Republic serve only as outside reference.
- **Languages needed:** Old Irish for saga and annals; Ulster Irish and Ulster Scots for lore; Tetum and Cantonese for newcomers' words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient files and mother and baby home records concern living families; the Troubles dead and 1641 are contested ground.
- **Held for review:** the folk-healing and belief pointers were the likeliest to touch knowledge a community keeps to itself; all fourteen point to published or archived material, so none was held.
