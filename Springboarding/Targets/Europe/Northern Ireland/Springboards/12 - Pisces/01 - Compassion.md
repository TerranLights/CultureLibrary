# Northern Ireland: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of Belfast and the counties; Ullans; Gaeilge as spoken in Ulster, and Old Irish for saints' lives; the tongues in which Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, East Timorese, Hong Kong and Arabic-speaking residents give and ask for help. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lurgan, Armagh and the Lough Neagh shore:** check Famine relief records union by union beside later memorial projects.
- **Fermanagh lakelands and Enniskillen:** compare how the 1987 bombing's commemorations speak of mercy over decades.
- **Derry / Londonderry (both names in use):** check hospice and city-mission records for who gave and who received.
- **Glens of Antrim and the Causeway Coast:** look for parish poor-box and wreck-relief traditions on the coast.
- **Society of St Vincent de Paul conferences; Presbyterian, Methodist and Church of Ireland relief; Quakers:** separate each tradition's visiting and relief records rather than reading them as one.
- **Jewish community of Belfast:** read Millisle and refugee committee papers in the community's own histories.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** check what Traveller-led groups record about receiving and refusing charity at the door.
- **Syrian families resettled after 2015:** compare welcome-group reports with the families' own accounts where published.
- **Polish and Filipino communities:** follow parish and association collections sent home after disasters; separate the two.
- **Roma families in Belfast:** check press and church accounts of the 2009 attacks and the shelter offered; sources scarce, so start from community advocates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Poor Law, Famine and charity records; mixed for everyday giving; thin for newer communities' giving.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Arabic for parish and mosque appeals; Irish for hagiography.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Enniskillen and other Troubles losses touch living families; refugee files name people; workhouse registers name children.
- **Held for review:** zakat and Ramadan collections were weighed here as minority-faith giving; the mosques' own reports raise no rule, so nothing is held.
