# Northern Ireland: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English in its Belfast and rural registers; Ulster Scots; modern Irish and the Old Irish of the *Táin*; British and Irish Sign Languages for the Deaf community; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Cantonese and Arabic for interpreted encounters. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Loyalist and republican ex-prisoners' groups:** check how each group's projects describe meeting victims and former enemies.
- **Families of RUC and PSNI officers; families of people killed by state forces:** compare whose testimony is collected in which archive.
- **South Armagh and the Fermanagh border:** look at which families on each side of the frontier gave testimony to story-gathering projects, and which declined.
- **Derry / Londonderry, Cityside and Waterside:** look at Theatre of Witness and city story projects for each bank.
- **Lough Neagh shore and Mid-Ulster:** check local fiction and parish memory work after rural killings.
- **Deaf community (BSL and ISL users):** check Deaf-led groups on interpreting and being misread in health care.
- **Autistic people and their families:** start with autistic-led writing before clinical accounts.
- **Polish and Lithuanian residents:** compare interpreting complaints in health services, by language.
- **Portuguese speakers and East Timorese in Dungannon:** separate the two; check community-interpreter reports.
- **Hong Kong Chinese elders:** check Cantonese-language health outreach for what staff were told to understand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for conflict storytelling and contact research; mixed for clinical empathy; thin for newer communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** BSL and ISL; Cantonese and Portuguese for interpreted care.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** perpetrator-victim encounters and prison testimony concern living people; consent terms of oral archives limit reuse.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the Autism NI and Deaf-community pointers needed the most care, and both lead to the groups' own published work rather than to case files.
