## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Six Mile Water, Ballymena and Mid-Antrim:** compare ministers', converts' and critics' records of each revival.
- **Elim, Free Presbyterian and Pentecostal congregations; Catholic charismatic prayer groups:** check each group's own descriptions of worship before outsiders'.
- **Rathlin, the Glens of Antrim and the Sperrins:** look for the last native Irish speakers and how locals recall the change.
- **East Belfast and Dunmurry:** compare shipyard and factory closures for what the workers' own memoirs say ended with them.
- **Couples in mixed marriages, and their children:** check what NICMA and school records say about naming, schooling and church.
- **People living with dementia:** start with Dementia NI members' own writing.
- **Belfast's Jewish community:** check congregation histories as a record of a community shrinking.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Hong Kong Chinese second generations:** compare weekend-school records for heritage-language loss, community by community.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** check what Traveller-led groups say about the Cant passing, or not passing, to children.
- **Muslim communities:** check whether any Sufi circle exists before applying Sufi terms; if none is recorded, drop the term.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revivals, church records and divorce; mixed for raves and ecstatic experience, mostly memory and press; thin for newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Rathlin and Glens Irish; Ulster Scots for Antrim speech; Arabic for faith terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "turned" and mixed marriage carry threat histories; dementia records concern living patients; drumming is contested ground.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation groups were the obvious candidates for a self-loss sheet; as an open question for the author, they are held as a category (Buddhist practice in Northern Ireland's lands), with no group named.
