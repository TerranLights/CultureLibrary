## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shankill, Falls and east Belfast:** compare social clubs and bars as refuges in the Troubles; club histories and licensing files differ by district.
- **Derry / Londonderry (both names in use):** check Donegal weekends and Bundoran trips in local memory.
- **Causeway Coast and Rathlin:** compare the resort towns' own accounts with incomers' views of the summer crowds.
- **The Sperrins, Tyrone and south Armagh:** check poteen lore and seizures by parish.
- **Fermanagh lakelands:** look at boating holidays and dance halls as a lakeland escape.
- **The Mournes and south Down:** check walking clubs' histories.
- **Catholic and Protestant abstainers:** separate Pioneer and Presbyterian temperance records.
- **Mincéirí (Irish Travellers):** start from Traveller-led accounts of horse fairs such as Ballyclare's.
- **Lithuanian and Polish residents:** compare sports clubs and weekend gatherings through each community's own media.
- **Muslim communities:** check mosque and youth-group guidance on drink and betting, and what young members say of it.
- **Hong Kong Chinese elders:** check Cantonese media and center programs for leisure in older age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drink, temperance and death statistics; mixed for leisure and holidays; thin for gaming and newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** reading *Mesca Ulad* calls for Old Irish; slang for drunkenness and truancy needs Ulster Scots and Belfast dictionaries; club and sports news is in Polish and Lithuanian media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** paramilitary-run clubs and drug supply are criminal matters (see Crime); the Miami Showband killings and the marching season are contested ground.
- **Held for review:** this sheet weighed the province's treatment and harm-reduction groups, several of them church-run; none fell under the standing rules.
