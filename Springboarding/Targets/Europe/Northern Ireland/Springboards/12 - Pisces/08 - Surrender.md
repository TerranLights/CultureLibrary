# Northern Ireland: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Northern Ireland, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ulster English, including wall slogans and gospel-mission speech; Ulster Scots; Irish, with Early Modern Irish for the Flight of the Earls; Polish, Lithuanian, Portuguese, Filipino (Tagalog), Cantonese and Arabic for sayings of resignation and trust in God. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Derry / Londonderry, the Fountain and the Bogside:** compare the siege commemoration with nationalist accounts of the same streets.
- **Tyrone and the Sperrins:** check how O'Neill country remembers 1603 and 1607 at Dungannon and Tullyhogue.
- **Shankill and east Belfast; west and north Belfast:** compare murals and accounts of decommissioning district by district.
- **South Down and the Ards:** look at gospel-mission records in coastal and farming parishes.
- **Limavady and the Roe Valley:** check local histories of the Londonderry Air.
- **Catholic parishes and Presbyterian congregations:** keep "offer it up" apart from evangelical surrender.
- **Families bereaved in the Troubles:** start with victims' and survivors' groups of different backgrounds.
- **Filipino Catholics:** check parish groups for *bahala na* and Marian devotion in their own accounts.
- **Polish residents:** look at Polish-language parish notices for words of resignation and trust.
- **Hong Kong Chinese elders:** check Cantonese-language care services on end-of-life choices.
- **Muslim communities:** check imams' and families' own accounts of illness and *tawakkul*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** sieges, ceasefires and decommissioning fill archives and press files; recovery and dying sit mostly in fellowship and hospice literature; newer communities' sayings of resignation are barely written down.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Irish for the Flight narratives; Tagalog and Cantonese for faith and care sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "surrender" is a charged word in unionist and republican politics alike; decommissioning, the siege and ceasefires are contested; assisted dying is a legal matter for Libra.
- **Held for review:** the ceasefire and decommissioning pointers came closest to the rule on contested ground; all three are kept with both sides' accounts named, so none is held.
