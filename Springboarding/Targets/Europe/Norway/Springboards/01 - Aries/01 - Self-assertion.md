# Norway: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with the dialects; North and South Sámi; Kven; Romani (*tatere*); Polish, Arabic and Somali for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim and Tromsø:** check meeting minutes and call-in radio for differences in turn length and interruption, rather than leaning on regional reputations.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing crews:** check how a crew member raised a claim to the skipper (*høvedsmann*) in fishery memoirs, since boat hierarchy shaped who could speak.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi (*siida*) and coastal Sámi:** check how claims are raised inside a herding group against claims raised to the state.
- **Kvens and the Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check organization records (Norske Kveners Forbund, Finnskogen's museum) for language claims; sources for Finnskogen are scarce, so begin with the local museum.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each community's own organizations for how claims to the state were voiced.
- **The Jewish community:** check the Mosaic congregations' public statements as a record of a small group's voice.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check how children and parents describe speaking up at school, since home and classroom may ask different things.
- **Haugean and *bedehus* congregations, Laestadians:** check sermons and hymns on humility against the record of Hauge's lay preaching and the opposition it met.
- **Women and men, young and old:** check workplace surveys for who negotiates pay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Jante debate, bullying research and workplace studies; thin for how Forest Finns or Kvens voiced everyday claims.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North Sámi for the Alta and Fosen material; Kven; Polish and Arabic for the newer communities' own press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Alta, Fosen and the Romani policy history are contested ground; present each account as an account and name communities by their own names. Workplace and consumer rules belong to Libra or Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** English-language "doing business in Norway" guides were weighed and left out as outsider generalizations about directness; no South Asian pointer came up.
