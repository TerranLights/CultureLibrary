# Norway: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with the regional dialects; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Romani (*tatere*); Somali, Kurdish, Arabic and Polish for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trøndelag, Northern Norway, Vestlandet, Sørlandet and Østlandet:** check which label people give first (region, town, or Norwegian), since the order shows where identity sits.
- **Nynorsk and Bokmål users:** check whether the written standard a person chose is named as part of who they are.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule and South Sámi areas, and those reclaiming Sámi identity after Norwegianization:** check enrollment trends and the commission's testimony; let Sámi institutions frame the result.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check which of the two names each group prefers and why, since the naming itself is debated.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** sources are few; check the museum and local *bygdebøker* first, never Finnish-side material in their place.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each community's self-names in its own organizations' publications.
- **The Jewish community:** check how the 1942 deportation enters family identity, through the community's own accounts.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian, Vietnamese, Polish and Lithuanian Norwegians, first and second generation:** check how each answers "where are you from?" and whether the answer shifts by setting.
- **Church of Norway members, Catholics, Muslims, Laestadians:** check whether faith is named before nation in self-descriptions.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check whether people name Svalbard or a mainland town as where they are from.
- **Norwegian-Americans (comparison only):** check what the diaspora kept from the homeland, as a contrast, not as Norway.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national-romantic sources, surveys and Sámi research; thin for Forest Finn and Rom self-description.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and older Dano-Norwegian for 19th-century texts; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Kurdish and Arabic for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization and the 2023 commission, the treatment of the Romani people and the 1942 deportation are contested ground; give each account as an account. Ethnic identity is sensitive personal data in Norway's registers.
- **Held for review:** while gathering second-generation voices for this concept, one pointer was set aside under the South Asian rule; category: South Asian-heritage communities in Norway's lands.
