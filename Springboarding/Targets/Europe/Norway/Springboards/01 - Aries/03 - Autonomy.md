# Norway: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Lithuanian and Tigrinya for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and the large towns against the inland valleys of Gudbrandsdalen and Hedmark:** check the age of leaving home and who chooses a child's school path, and whether distance to upper-secondary school brings early moves out of some valleys.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi in Kautokeino and Karasjok against South Sámi herders in Trøndelag:** check how decisions are split between the family, the *siida* and the state.
- **Coastal Sámi and Kvens of Finnmark and Troms:** check local fishing and land use as fields of self-rule, through their own organizations.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check the old slash-and-burn settlement as a record of living beyond the authorities' reach; sources are scarce, so begin with the Norsk Skogfinsk Museum.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the past removal of children and forced measures through the community's own testimony.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* families:** check, from members' and former members' memoirs, which choices are personal and which are shaped by the congregation.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Iraqi, Syrian, Polish and Lithuanian Norwegians:** check how parents and grown children describe choosing a spouse or a career, from each community's own media.
- **Disabled people using user-controlled assistance:** check municipal differences in access.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check how self-rule works where employers provide housing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, Sámi self-government and disability policy; mixed for family decision-making in newer communities, where outsider reporting outweighs community voices.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North Sámi for Sámi Parliament debates; Somali, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Arabic for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "negative social control" is politically charged; never let it stand for a whole group. The removal of Romani children and Norwegianization are contested ground; give each account as an account. Self-government's legal frame belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** press case stories that make one community the face of family control were weighed and left out; nothing here fell under the South Asian rule.
