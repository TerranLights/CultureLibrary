*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Vestlandet and Sørlandet against the inland valleys and Østlandet:** check SSB start-up counts per head and local histories of shipping and boat-building firms, to see where initiative was family-borne.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark fishing communities:** check how the decision to go out is made and by whom, in fishery memoirs.
- **Coastal Sámi of Kåfjord and the Lyngen area, and reindeer-herding Sámi:** check who starts cultural and business ventures, through Sámi festival archives and the Sámi Parliament's business grants.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger:** check local association records for Kven-led cultural projects; material is scarce and sits mostly with local museums.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check the clearing of new farms in the forest as a record of starting things; begin with the Norsk Skogfinsk Museum.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the community's own histories of trades and crafts (tinwork, horse trading) for who started a trade and how it was passed on.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Vietnamese, Somali, Eritrean and Kurdish Norwegians:** check self-employment and the founding of associations and mosques or churches by each community.
- **Haugean and *bedehus* communities:** check prayer houses built by local initiative, using parish and mission histories.
- **Svalbard:** check who started Longyearbyen's associations and businesses after the mining era wound down.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for volunteering, statistics and work-life research; thin for Kven and Forest Finn initiative outside museum files.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old English and Old Norse for Ohthere and the sagas; North Sámi for festival and business records; Polish and Vietnamese for community business media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 22 July material touches grief; use the official report and the survivors' own published accounts only. Labor-relations rules belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** motivational and "hustle" content aimed at Norwegian readers was weighed and left out as marketing, not evidence; no South Asian pointer arose.
