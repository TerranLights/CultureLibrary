*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farming valleys of Gudbrandsdalen and Telemark against the Lofoten and Vesterålen coast:** check whether the working year begins with the land or with the fishery, using *primstav* and fishery records.
- **Vestlandet and Rogaland:** check how Hafrsfjord and Harald's unification figure in local pride against the rest of the country.
- **Trøndelag:** check Olsok's place beside Syttende mai and Sámi National Day, the first Sámi meeting having been held in Trondheim.
- **Reindeer-herding, coastal and South Sámi:** check which point in the herding or fishing year counts as the start, from Sámi sources.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns of Finnmark:** check how the 1945 rebuilding is remembered in Kven families, through Kven museums.
- **Forest Finns:** check the founding stories of Finnskogen's farms; the record is local and thin.
- **The Jewish community:** check the founding of the Oslo and Trondheim congregations and the rebuilding after 1945.
- **Vietnamese, Kurdish, Eritrean, Somali, Syrian and Polish Norwegians:** check new-year customs kept in Norway, and how arrival is told across generations.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* communities:** check conversion and awakening accounts as personal new beginnings.
- **Church of Norway, Catholic and humanist families:** check which confirmation each family picks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for calendar customs, founding monuments and emigration; mixed for Sámi and Kven accounts of the rebuilding, which sit in local museums.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and older Dano-Norwegian; Old Norse for sagas and the ballad's sources; North Sámi; Kurdish and Vietnamese for community festivals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the post-war rebuilding overlaps with Norwegianization; give Sámi and Kven accounts as their own. The Christian founding story is not everyone's; give Sámi and other accounts beside it. Prison policy as law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** "discovery" accounts by outside explorers of the far north were weighed and left out as one-sided; no South Asian pointer came into play.
