*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Vestlandet, Lofoten and Finnmark against the inland valleys:** check whether courage stories are mostly sea stories or mountain stories, using local rescue logs and memorials to men lost at sea.
- **Telemark and Rjukan:** check how the sabotage story is told by the town itself against national film and television.
- **Northern Norway and Svalbard:** check trappers', sealers' and miners' endurance accounts, a different register from the polar heroes.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi, coastal Sámi and South Sámi:** check herding work in winter storms as a field of endurance, from Sámi accounts rather than outside admiration.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check wartime evacuation memories from 1944-45 in Kven family accounts.
- **The Jewish community:** check rescue and escape to Sweden in 1942, from survivors' and helpers' accounts, and the community's own view of who helped and who did not.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check whose wartime fates are remembered and whose courage is left out of the national story.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian, Iraqi and Kurdish Norwegians:** check flight stories as told by the families themselves, where leaving was itself a risk.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check dangerous-trade records (construction, fishing plants) for how injury and risk are discussed.
- **Women and men in rescue services and the armed forces:** check who receives awards.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for WWII, polar history and rescue services; thin for Sámi, Kven and Romani accounts of courage outside the national frame.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse for the saga; North Sámi; Somali, Tigrinya and Arabic for refugees' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the occupation, Quisling, the 1942 deportation of Norway's Jews and the war sailors' treatment are contested ground; give each account as an account. Veterans' and rescuers' trauma files are confidential. Military law belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** recent war-film blockbusters were weighed as courage sources and kept out as dramatized; no South Asian pointer was needed.
