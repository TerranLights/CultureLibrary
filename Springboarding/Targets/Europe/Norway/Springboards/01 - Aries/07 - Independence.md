## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inland farm valleys (Gudbrandsdalen, Østerdalen) against Oslo and Bergen:** check *matauk* and home food stores by place, since self-provisioning may follow access to outfields.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark coastal households:** check how *kombinasjonsbruk* households describe needing others in hard seasons.
- **Cotter (*husmann*) and farmer families of the 1800s:** check cotter memoirs for how dependence on the farm was lived and resented.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi, coastal and Markasámi:** check how each speaks of managing alone and relying on kin and the *siida*.
- **Kvens of the Varanger fjord and Porsanger:** check Kven household economies through Kven museum material; sources are thin.
- **Forest Finns:** check burn-beat farming as self-provisioning, using Finnskogen's local histories only.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check how the community's own accounts describe trading independence against the state's later control.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check remittances and multi-earner households, and how needing welfare is spoken of in each community's own media.
- **Svalbard:** check how residents speak of living where almost everything is shipped in.
- **Elders across the country:** check aging in place against moving near grown children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, national-independence history and welfare research; thin for Kven and Forest Finn household economies.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Middle Low German for Hanseatic records; North Sámi; Somali, Polish and Tigrinya for remittance studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the oil fund and the occupation are contested ground; give each account as an account. Hamsun's wartime sympathies make his work contested reading. Welfare rules as law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** survivalist and "prepper" web forums were weighed and left out as unrepresentative; nothing for this concept fell under the South Asian rule.
