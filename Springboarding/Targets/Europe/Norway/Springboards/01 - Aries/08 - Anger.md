# Norway: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with dialect swearing; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Lithuanian and Vietnamese for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo, Bergen, Stavanger and Trondheim against rural districts:** check protest records and toll and hospital fights, to see whether grievance gathers along center and periphery lines.
- **Northern Norway and Finnmark:** check dialect swearing and humor about temper in local radio and comedy, against outsider caricature.
- **Reindeer-herding and coastal Sámi:** check how anger at land loss and the state is voiced in Sámi media, and how anger inside a community is handled by Sámi custom.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check anger in testimony to the 2023 Truth and Reconciliation Commission, as each group states it.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the community's own published reactions to past state measures, beside the official reports.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check teaching on anger as sin and on confession and forgiveness between members.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check how parents and children describe raising a voice at home and at school, from their own media.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check conflict at worksites through union and Fafo reports.
- **Women and men:** check who is referred to Alternativ til Vold, and how women's anger is written about in the press.
- **Svalbard:** check how conflict is cooled in a small, isolated town, through local paper archives (*Svalbardposten*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for violence research, mediation and media debate; thin for everyday anger in Kven, Sámi and Romani homes.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse for skaldic verse; North Sámi; Somali, Arabic and Tigrinya for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic-violence and treatment records are confidential; Norwegianization and Romani history are contested ground, so give each account as an account. Hate-speech law and mediation law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** of the expressions of anger considered here, one pointer was set aside under the South Asian rule; category: South Asian-heritage communities in Norway's lands.
