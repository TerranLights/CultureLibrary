## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Bergen against the smaller towns:** check football derbies and local press for town rivalry (Brann against Rosenborg, for one).
- **Østlandet inland valleys and Trøndelag:** check ski clubs' records for how early children are entered in ranked races.
- **Telemark, Setesdal, Hardanger and Valdres fiddlers and dancers:** check contest results by valley, since folk competitions also rank traditions.
- **Reindeer-herding and coastal Sámi, and South Sámi:** check who enters reindeer racing and lasso contests, and how Sámi organizers set the rules.
- **Kvens:** check local sports and contest records in Vadsø and Porsanger; sources are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check games and contests named in each community's own histories and festivals, not in outsiders' descriptions.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check club founding, entry to youth leagues and school-result pressure as each community describes it.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check teaching on sport, Sunday competition and pride.
- **Girls and boys:** check NIH data on who stops competing and when.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, school results and broadcast contests; thin for Kven and Romani contest traditions.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse for the sagas; North Sámi for contest rules; Polish and Somali for community sports clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the tax lists are a privacy debate; doping cases involve medical data. The rules of sport and tax publication as law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Sweden's ski rivalry was weighed as a pointer and kept as comparison only, since Sweden is a separate culture-nation; no South Asian pointer was involved.
