## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet fjords (Lysefjorden, Romsdalen, Hardanger) against the Oslo area:** check accident and rescue records, since tourist risk and local risk may be judged differently.
- **Rural Østlandet, Trøndelag and the inland valleys:** check the *råner* scene and drink-driving data against cities.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark fishers:** check how sea deaths are remembered in memorials and local histories.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi of Finnmark and the South Sámi:** check snowmobile and ice accidents as herders' working risk, from Sámi sources, against outside framing.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check river and fjord accident lore in Kven local histories; little is published.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check how official records and each community's own accounts describe danger and caution, side by side.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check teaching on drink, cards and dancing, and how members describe leaving or keeping those rules.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi and Syrian Norwegians:** check drowning prevention, worksite safety and youth driving campaigns aimed at each, alongside the communities' own views.
- **Young men and women in the russ weeks:** check injury and drinking data by sex.
- **Svalbard residents against visitors:** check rescue and polar-bear records for who takes risks.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for accidents, rescue, drink and gambling statistics; thin for how Kven and Sámi communities talk about risk in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Polish, Lithuanian and Arabic for safety campaigns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accident and rescue records name the dead; addiction data is health data. The black-metal crimes are a painful history for the affected parishes. Traffic and drug law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** "daredevil" stunt videos on social media were weighed and left out as self-promotion with no local frame; no South Asian pointer surfaced for this concept.
