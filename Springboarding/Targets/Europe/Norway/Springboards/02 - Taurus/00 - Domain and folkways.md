# Norway: Possessions and value, the domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Norwegian (Bokmål, Nynorsk and the dialects); Old Norwegian for medieval records; North and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Tigrinya, Vietnamese, Polish and Lithuanian where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, and how it is held (money, resources, food ways, dress ways and wealth ways).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet's border districts (Østfold, Hedmark) against Vestlandet and the north:** check *harrytur* frequency and spending, since distance from Sweden may shape price habits.
- **Oslo's west and east ends:** check spending and secondhand use by city district.
- **Fishing households of Lofoten, Vesterålen and the Finnmark coast:** check how boats, quotas and gear are counted as household wealth since quotas became tradable.
- **Farm families of Gudbrandsdalen, Telemark and Setesdal:** check heirloom silver, painted chests (*rosemaling*) and the farm as goods handed down, through local museums.
- **Reindeer-herding, coastal and South Sámi families:** check what each counts as wealth (herd, fishing place, *duodji*), as Sámi institutions present it.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns of Troms and Finnmark:** check household goods and trade in Kven sources (scarce; start at the Vadsø Kven museum).
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check trade, peddling and craft histories through Taternes Landsforening and Romano Kher, not police or welfare files.
- **Laestadians in the north; *bedehus* communities in Sørlandet and Vestlandet:** check tracts and sermons on worldly goods.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Vietnamese, Polish and Lithuanian communities:** check savings circles and money sent home through each community's own associations.
- **Longyearbyen:** check what residents ship in and leave behind in a town of short stays.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for spending, prices and food intake; mixed for heirlooms and gift customs, which sit in museum questionnaires and local histories; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani household economies.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for the laws and medieval receipts; North and South Sámi for herd and craft words; Somali, Tigrinya and Vietnamese for savings circles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi objects held in museums are a returns question (the Bååstede project, *check*); oil wealth is contested ground, so give each account as an account; inheritance and odal rules as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** consumer-goods brand websites were considered as a source of "what is worth having" and left out as advertising, not household practice; no pointer for this overview fell under the South Asian rule.
