# Norway: Possession (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with dialect words; Dano-Norwegian for 1700s records; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Arabic and Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** having and holding things: the bond between people and the things they keep, use, carry, store, lend, lose and give away.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finnmark and Nord-Troms families evacuated in 1944:** check survivor accounts of buried and lost belongings, so the burned region's memory of things is read in its own sources.
- **The Jewish community of Oslo and Trondheim:** check confiscation records and the 1997 report's two accounts side by side.
- **South Sámi and Lule Sámi communities:** check how receiving museums describe returned drums and *duodji*, and who decides access.
- **Reindeer-herding families of Kautokeino and Karasjok:** check what is carried on the seasonal migration and what is stored, through Sámi museum publications.
- **Valley farms of Gudbrandsdalen and Setesdal:** check the *stabbur* (storehouse) and the loft as places where goods were kept, through open-air museum records.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the communities' own accounts of horses, wagons and boats as carried belongings, and their loss under settlement policy.
- **Syrian, Eritrean and Somali Norwegians:** check refugee memory projects on objects carried on the journey.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers keeping homes in two countries:** check what is bought in Norway and shipped home.
- **Longyearbyen:** check what residents must carry outside the settlement, and what is left at the door indoors.
- **Elders moving to care housing (*omsorgsbolig*):** check what is kept and what is cleared, through *eldreomsorg* studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lost property, clothing and theft statistics; mixed for heirlooms and the dead's goods; thin for Kven and Forest Finn possessions, where sources are scarce and local museums are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Dano-Norwegian for the mission records; South and North Sámi for the drum returns; Arabic and Tigrinya for refugee object projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi drums are sacred objects, and how they may be shown is decided by Sámi museums; the confiscation of Jewish property and restitution are contested ground, so present each account as an account; theft and finds as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** tabloid stories of hoarders' homes were considered and set aside as exposure of private persons; no pointer on keeping and losing things fell under the South Asian rule.
