# Norway: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norwegian; North and South Sámi; Kven; Russian for Barentsburg; Polish, Somali and Vietnamese for tenure by background. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sámi, Kven and Norwegian residents of Finnmark:** check how each group's claimed rights appear in FeFo and Commission reports, and where accounts differ.
- **South Sámi herding communities of Trøndelag and Nordland:** check land-use quarrels with landowners and wind-power firms (Fosen) as each party presents them.
- **Coastal Sámi and small-boat fishers of the northern fjords:** check how they argue rights from long use against quota holders.
- **Odal families of the inland valleys against buyers from outside:** check the debate over the residence duty (*boplikt*) as a social argument, not as law.
- **Hardanger, Telemark and Sogn power towns (Odda, Rjukan, Årdal):** check local memory of who owned the falls and what reversion meant to them.
- **Sørlandet and Oslofjord shoreline:** check quarrels between cabin owners and the public over beaches (*strandsonen*).
- **Oslo's *borettslag* members, owner-occupiers and renters:** check SSB tenure by district and how renters describe their standing.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the community's own accounts of houses bought for them under settlement programs, and of owning on those terms.
- **Polish, Somali, Iraqi and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check SSB tenure by country background, and community media on buying a first home.
- **Longyearbyen and Barentsburg:** check who may own a home on Svalbard, and how the Russian-run mining town holds its land.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tenure, state holdings and government accounts; mixed for Sámi and Kven customary rights; thin for Forest Finn use of the Finnskogen forests.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for the Land Law; North and South Sámi for *siida* and *sijte* rights; Kven for Finnmark hearings; Russian for Barentsburg.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Fosen, the Finnmark rights process and the Alta case are contested ground; present each party's account as its own. Property law itself goes to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** estate agents' market commentary was considered for attitudes to buying and set aside as sales talk; nothing on tenure was set aside under the South Asian rule.
