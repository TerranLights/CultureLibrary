# Norway: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse for the old poems and laws; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish for community media. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Workers in closed mining and fishing towns of Finnmark and Nordland:** check what local studies record about worth once the job ended, so men's and women's accounts are both heard.
- **Trøndelag and Northern Norway cohorts:** check the HUNT and Tromsø items for self-esteem by region and sex before reading any national figure.
- **Sámi youth in Kautokeino and Karasjok against South Sámi youth in mostly Norwegian schools:** check how SANKS and Sámi youth projects describe worth and shame in each setting.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and the Varanger fjord:** check whether Kven language revival projects write of restored dignity; sources are scarce, start with the Kven language institute's reports.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check life stories written by community members, not only official inquiries.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi and Kurdish Norwegians:** check how their own media and youth groups answer stigma in public debate.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check sermons on worthiness before God against worldly success.
- **Oslo's east and west schools:** check youth research after *Skam* on status, clothes and comparison.
- **Elders in care homes across the country:** check how the dignity debate in elder care (*verdighet i eldreomsorgen*) is voiced by residents and relatives.
- **Disabled people's self-advocacy groups (Unge funksjonshemmede):** check how they speak of worth against charity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental-health cohorts and suicide statistics; mixed for worth after job loss; thin for Kven and Forest Finn accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the poem and the compensation scales; North Sámi for clinical and youth sources; Somali and Arabic for community media on stigma.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data must be handled with the research centers' own care rules; Norwegianization and the 2023 Truth and Reconciliation report are contested ground, so give each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** commercial confidence coaching and self-help seminars were considered and set aside as selling worth rather than recording it; no South Asian pointer was set aside for this concept.
