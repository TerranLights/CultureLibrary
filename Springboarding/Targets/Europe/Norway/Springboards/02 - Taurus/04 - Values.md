## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sørlandet and Vestlandet against Oslo:** check whether ESS value items split by county along the band of lay-Christian strength the press calls the *bibelbeltet*.
- **Coastal Nordland and Troms:** check what Petter Dass's long use in church and school left in local value talk.
- **Sámi congregations and Sámi without church ties:** check how each names values in Sámi terms, through the Sámi church council and the Sámi journal.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check how Kvenlandsforbundet and Kven newspapers frame values; sources are thin.
- **Haugean-heritage farm districts of Østfold and Telemark:** check whether lay-movement heritage shows in thrift and temperance attitudes by municipality.
- **The Jewish community of Oslo and Trondheim:** check DMT statements in debates over circumcision and slaughter, as sacred values meeting state rules.
- **Muslims (Islamsk Råd Norge) and Catholics (with many Polish members):** check how each congregation states its values in public hearings.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Vietnamese and Syrian Norwegians:** check what parents in each community's associations say they hope children keep.
- **Generations:** check WVS waves by birth cohort before reading any "national" trend.
- **Longyearbyen's many-nationality population (with large Filipino and Thai groups):** check the local church's and community council's reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for value surveys and official value statements; mixed for minority congregations; thin for Kven and Forest Finn value vocabulary, where the route is local museums and Kven media.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for the homilies; North Sámi for the Sámi journal; Arabic and Polish for congregational sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the slaughter and circumcision debates touch a community's sacred practice and should be read from its own statements; "Norwegian values" is a contested political phrase, so present each side's use as an account.
- **Held for review:** party programs were considered as statements of values and set aside, since the election study handles them better; no faith-community pointer on values had to be set aside for this concept.
