# Norway: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norwegian for guild statutes; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark coast fishers:** check how the income guarantee and seasonal pay shaped household saving.
- **North Sámi herders in Finnmark and Lule Sámi families in Tysfjord:** check accounts of *goavvi* winters and emergency feeding, in Sámi sources first.
- **Quick-clay districts of Østlandet and Trøndelag (Gjerdrum; Verdal, 1893):** check how slide memory is passed on locally.
- **Rockslide-watched fjords of Sunnmøre and Sogn (Åkneset):** check how residents speak of living under a monitored mountain.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers in building and fish processing:** check contract security through union and Arbeidstilsynet reports.
- **Refugee families settled by municipalities (Syrian, Eritrean, Somali):** check housing and job insecurity in the first years, through IMDi follow-up studies.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check community accounts of child removal as the loss of a family's security; follow their own organizations' guidance on access.
- **Kvens of Nord-Troms and Finnmark:** check household security in local museum material; sources are sparse.
- **Retirees:** check how Pensjonistforbundet speaks of pension security after 2011.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check how housing tied to jobs shapes security.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social insurance, crime-fear surveys and disaster reports; mixed for household saving habits; thin for Kven and Forest Finn coping in hard years.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian and Latin for the guild statutes; North and Lule Sámi for herding crises; Polish and Lithuanian for workers' forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-removal records concerning Romani families are personal and restricted; benefit rules as law belong to Libra or Capricorn; the occupation is contested ground, so present rationing memories as accounts.
- **Held for review:** private security firms' marketing about burglary was considered and set aside, since it sells fear; no South Asian item was considered for this concept.
