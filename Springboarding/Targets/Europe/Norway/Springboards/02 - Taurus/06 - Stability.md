# Norway: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with dialect words; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Lithuanian for settling patterns. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm heirs of Gudbrandsdalen and Hedmark against Finnmark fishing villages:** check SSB migration series and local studies to see where staying and leaving each became the norm.
- **Residents of Røros and of Sørlandet's white wooden towns (Lillesand, Grimstad):** check how they speak of living inside a protected townscape.
- **Coastal Sámi of Porsanger and Lyngen:** check continuity of settlement in their own centers' publications.
- **Reindeer-herding families:** check how migration routes held for generations are kept against cabin fields and wind farms, as herders record it.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check which farms have stayed in one family since the 1600s, through the forest Finn museum's records.
- **Towns of Finnmark and Nord-Troms rebuilt after 1944:** check what "old" and "continuous" mean where almost nothing standing predates 1945.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check who settles with family and who rotates, using SSB's emigration figures for immigrants.
- **Long-time residents of Oslo's inner east (Grünerløkka, Tøyen):** check accounts of displacement as rents rose.
- **Church of Norway and Laestadian families who keep the same seats and meetings for generations:** check parish and prayer-house histories.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how a town of short stays keeps yearly customs such as the sun festival (*Solfestuka*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration, prices and heritage; mixed for routine and habit, which sits in media and ethnology; thin for Kven and Forest Finn family continuity.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and dialects for *heimføding* and farm terms; North Sámi for coastal Sámi records; Kven for Nord-Troms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wind-power conflicts on grazing land are contested ground, so present each party's account; the *kår* and odal as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nostalgia groups on social media ("du vet du er fra...") were considered and set aside as unrepresentative samples; this concept raised nothing under the South Asian rule.
