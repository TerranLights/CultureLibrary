# Norway: Wealth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with dialect words; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Vietnamese and Lithuanian. **Concept in one line:** an abundance of what a culture counts as valuable, and the standing that comes with it. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rogaland and Hordaland oil towns against Nordland and Finnmark:** check how new wealth is spoken of where oil money arrived and where it did not.
- **Old shipping and whaling families of Vestfold and Bergen:** check family histories and museum material on how old fortunes present themselves.
- **Nordland fishers and the families of old trading posts:** check the memory of debt to the merchant, in both families' accounts.
- **Large farms of Østlandet's plains against small holdings in the western fjords:** check farm size and wealth in the agricultural census and local histories.
- **Reindeer-herding families:** check how herd counts are reported and whether herders discuss them; Sámi institutions advise on what may be asked.
- **Kvens of Nord-Troms and Finnmark:** check Kven sources on trade and smallholding; little is published, so start with local museums.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check the community's own accounts of horse trading and craft as livelihoods.
- **Vietnamese, Somali and Lithuanian Norwegians:** check self-employment, low-income rates and money sent home, using SSB by background and each community's own media.
- **Oslo's west and east:** check wealth by city district, since some of the widest gaps sit inside one municipality.
- **Svalbard:** check what the coal company's history (Store Norske) says of wages and wealth in a company town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registers, wealth statistics and rich lists; mixed for attitudes to the rich; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani economic history.
- **Languages needed:** North Sámi for herd wealth; Vietnamese and Somali for community business and remittance media; older Dano-Norwegian for merchant letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the wealth tax and the oil fund are contested ground, so give each side's account; household wealth data are register-based and access-controlled; tax rules as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** gossip and celebrity coverage of named rich families was considered and set aside as unreliable for the concept; nothing on wealth was set aside under the South Asian rule.
