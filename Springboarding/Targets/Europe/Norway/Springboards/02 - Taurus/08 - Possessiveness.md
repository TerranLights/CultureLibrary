## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Women and men in survey data:** check control and jealousy items by sex before reading any total.
- **Small municipalities of Finnmark and the inland valleys against Oslo:** check whether crisis-center use differs with distance and with how hard it is to stay anonymous in a small place.
- **Sámi women and men:** check Sámi-language help services in Finnmark and Sámi researchers' studies of violence in close relationships.
- **Reindeer-herding *siidas*:** check grazing disputes as each side reports them, not only through state agencies.
- **Kurdish, Iraqi, Somali and Syrian Norwegians:** check how the communities' own women's and youth organizations frame jealousy and control.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Norwegians:** check help-seeking through native-language services.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check local song and story collections for jealousy themes; sources are few, and local museums are the place to begin.
- **Separated parents across the country:** check custody-conflict studies (*høykonflikt*) by region.
- **Berry pickers and anglers of Nordland, Troms and the inland:** check local talk of secret spots and of outsiders who take them.
- **Svalbard:** check how Longyearbyen and the Russian settlement of Barentsburg speak of their claims on the same archipelago.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for violence statistics and help services; mixed for everyday jealousy, which sits in youth sites and psychology studies; thin for Kven and Forest Finn material.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse and medieval Norwegian for ballads; North Sámi for help services; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for community framings of honor and jealousy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence and homicide involve identifiable victims; use only published, anonymized material. Any link between a community and control must come from that community's own voices, never be assumed.
- **Held for review:** tabloid coverage of jealousy murders was considered and set aside as sensational; nothing for this concept came under the South Asian rule.
