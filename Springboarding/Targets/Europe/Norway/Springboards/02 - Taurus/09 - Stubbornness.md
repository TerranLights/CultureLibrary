## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet's Nynorsk districts against Oslo and Østlandet:** check where the refusal of merged-language forms was strongest, using the 1950s campaign's signature lists.
- **Farmers around Bergen and in Agder in the 1700s:** check their own petitions against officials' reports, since the record of refusal was mostly written by the other side.
- **Teachers of 1942 and their families:** check memoirs against the occupation authorities' records; the occupation is contested ground.
- **Reindeer-herding families in Finnmark:** check how herders describe refusing culling orders and wind farms, beside the state's account.
- **Kvens of the Varanger fjord and Porsanger:** check memoirs of keeping Kven at home under Norwegianization; few are published, so ask the Kven institute.
- **Romani people (*tatere*):** check how families describe refusing settlement and the mission's terms, in their own organizations' publications.
- **Industrial workers of Telemark and Hardanger:** check strike memory in union and museum archives.
- **Young children across the country:** check defiance measures by sex in MoBa before reading anything as a general trait.
- **First-generation and Norway-raised members of the Somali, Polish and Vietnamese communities:** check youth projects on how disagreement and giving way are handled across generations.
- **Svalbard miners at Svea:** check how the mine's closure was resisted and accepted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, the language struggle and wartime refusal; mixed for everyday stubbornness, which sits in child cohorts and media; thin for Kven and Forest Finn material.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the saga; Dano-Norwegian for 1700s petitions; Nynorsk for the language struggle; North Sámi for herders' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the culling case and wind power on grazing land are contested, as is the occupation; present every party's account as an account. Court rulings as law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** online anti-vaccine forums were considered and set aside, since they record conviction rather than its social reading; no South Asian pointer was considered here.
