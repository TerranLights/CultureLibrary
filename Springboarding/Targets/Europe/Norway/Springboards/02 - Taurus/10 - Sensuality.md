## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm brewers of Voss, Hardanger and Sogn against Trøndelag's *karsk* tradition:** check how each district's drink lore is told, since home brewing and home spirits sit in different laws and customs.
- **Coastal families of Lofoten and Finnmark in the *skrei* season:** check the *mølje* meal and its local etiquette.
- **Valdres and inland Østlandet against Sørlandet:** check *rakfisk* and *lutefisk* as regional tastes, through local food festivals.
- **Pietist and *bedehus* districts of Sørlandet and Vestlandet:** check histories of dance and fiddle bans and how congregations speak of pleasure today.
- **Laestadian congregations of Nord-Troms and Finnmark:** check their own published teaching on alcohol and leisure, and members' accounts.
- **Sámi families, North and South:** check food, joik and the feel of *gákti* as Sámi institutions present them.
- **Forest Finns and Kvens:** check sauna customs in local collections; little is written, so ask local museums first.
- **Eritrean, Somali, Vietnamese and Polish Norwegians:** check community food festivals, cafés and the coffee ceremony as their own members describe them.
- **Tromsø and Longyearbyen residents in polar night:** check light, warmth and festival life (Longyearbyen's jazz festival in the dark season, *check*).
- **Elders in care homes:** check studies of food, music and touch in care.
