*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo against small districts in Hedmark, Oppland and Nordland:** check local-paper coverage and where news comes from when a paper closes.
- **Fishing villages in Lofoten and Vesterålen and along Finnmark's coast:** check weather and catch information networks, from radio to apps.
- **Sámi communities:** check which data the Sámi themselves collect and control, and how they ask outsiders to handle it.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check Kven-language news and what was recorded about them in censuses (scarce; Kven associations).
- **The Jewish community:** check how the 1942 registration lists figure in its own account of the deportation, as a history of information used against people.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how registration by missions and the state is told in each group's own histories.
- **Laestadian congregations:** check what rules on radio, television and the internet each branch states for itself.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian, Iraqi and Kurdish communities:** check reliance on WhatsApp groups, satellite TV and community media, as studies record it.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check which language media they follow while in Norway.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for news use, press history and trust surveys; mixed for privacy and misinformation (new field); thin for Sámi and Kven data; check first what is collected at all.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for press before 1900; North Sámi for Sámi media; Somali, Arabic and Polish for community channels.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** registration of Jews (1942), the *tatere* and the Sámi is contested and painful ground; present each account as an account in the community's own name. Sámi data has its own governance claims; ask before use.
- **Held for review:** commercial data-broker reports were considered for the data-economy facet and left aside as unreachable and unvetted; no information pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
