# Norway: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Latin and Old Norwegian for medieval texts; Danish-Norwegian for 18th-century science; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trondheim and Bergen against Oslo:** check how each city's old learned bodies and science centers shaped local science culture.
- **Northern Norway (Tromsø, Alta, Lofoten):** check aurora tourism and research beside local and Sámi accounts of the night sky.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi:** check which questions about herds, land and family are counted as intrusive, as Sámi sources say.
- **Coastal and South Sámi:** check how each meets researchers and tourists asking about them.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check how outsiders' folklore collecting is remembered (scarce; local museums).
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check accounts of being studied, photographed and recorded by outsiders, in each group's own histories.
- **The Jewish community:** check how questions about origins and faith are met, through community publications.
- **Norwegians of Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Syrian or Vietnamese background:** check their own accounts of the "where are you really from?" question.
- **Svalbard:** check what draws researchers and visitors, and how residents describe being looked at.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for museums, science festivals and attitudes to research; mixed for nosiness and morbid curiosity (media and fiction more than surveys); thin for Kven and Forest Finn experiences of being studied.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for *Historia Norwegie*; Danish-Norwegian for the DKNVS papers; North Sámi for accounts of remains and the sky.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi human remains and collections are a returns question and painful ground; treat each account as an account. Race-biology research on Sámi and *tatere* is contested; cite it only as history, through the peoples' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** online true-crime forums were considered for morbid curiosity and left out for naming real victims; among the curiosity pointers, none touched the South Asian rule.
