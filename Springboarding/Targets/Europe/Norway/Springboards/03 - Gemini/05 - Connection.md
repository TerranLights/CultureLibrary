# Norway: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; runic Old Norse for inscriptions; Dano-Norwegian and emigrant Norwegian for letters; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Tigrinya and Vietnamese for ties abroad. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Island and fjord communities in Nordland, Vestlandet and Finnmark:** check how bridges, tunnels or lost ferry routes changed contact with kin and services.
- **Oslo's districts against small inland municipalities in Innlandet:** check loneliness figures and club membership side by side.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi and their settled partners:** check *verdde* ties as both sides describe them.
- **South Sámi in Trøndelag and Nordland:** check how a widely scattered people keeps contact across distance.
- **Kvens:** check gatherings and associations that keep ties (scarce; Kven associations).
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check cross-border ties with Värmland's Finnish settlements as a comparison.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check family networks and gatherings through each group's own organizations.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check ties kept with home through parishes, buses and messaging, by length of stay.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese families:** check family chats, associations and transfers as each community describes them.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how people in a town of short stays build and drop ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness and social-support surveys; mixed for networks used for jobs and favors; thin for *verdde* ties today and for Kven and Romani networks.
- **Languages needed:** runic Old Norse for inscriptions; 19th-century Norwegian and emigrant English for letters; North and South Sámi; Polish, Somali and Tigrinya for community ties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** loneliness and suicide-line data are sensitive; Romani family networks were once tracked by state and missions, so ask the communities first.
- **Held for review:** the Upper Midwest's Norwegian-American lodges were considered and kept as diaspora comparison only, not as part of the nation; this sheet set no South Asian pointer aside.
