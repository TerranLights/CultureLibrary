## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small places in Helgeland, Lofoten and the Finnmark coast against Oslo's districts:** check whether people describe gossip as pressure, care or both.
- **Telemark and Setesdal:** check *stev* customs and what verses were allowed to say about whom.
- **Prayer-house (*bedehus*) circles in Sørlandet and Vestlandet, and Laestadian congregations in Troms and Finnmark:** check sermons and tracts on speech about others.
- **Reindeer-herding, coastal and South Sámi communities:** check talk about families and herds as each community describes it; sources scarce, start with Sámi media.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check local histories for village talk about language and origin.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how rumors about them were spread and answered, from their own organizations.
- **The Jewish community:** check rumors in the 1930s press and community accounts of them.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish and Syrian communities:** check community talk and reputation as their own members and researchers describe them, without assuming a pattern.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check workplace talk across languages.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how a small town with high turnover handles talk.
