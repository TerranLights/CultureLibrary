# Norway: Home and family, the domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with dialect house and kin words; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what home is and who family is (household forms, house types, kin, child-rearing, roots, hospitality and family memory).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Valley farms of Gudbrandsdalen against fjord farms of Sogn and Hardanger:** check where the *kårstue* stood and how the yard was laid out.
- **Oslo against Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing villages:** check who ran the household during the winter fishery, through local histories.
- **Reindeer-herding families of Kautokeino and Karasjok, coastal, Lule and South Sámi:** check how each describes its own dwellings, since the herding year sets another home rhythm.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Varanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check house plans and inheritance customs through their museums, since sources are few.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check winter quarters and settled housing in the groups' own organizations, set against mission records.
- **Laestadian families of Nord-Troms and the *bedehus* congregations of Sørlandet:** check family-size and home-devotion customs as the congregations describe them.
- **Jewish, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Polish and Vietnamese households:** check multigenerational living in SSB's immigrant tables and the communities' own reports.
- **Longyearbyen:** check what a fixed-term home does to family life, through *Svalbardposten*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics, census history and building customs; mixed for child-rearing; thin for Forest Finn, Kven and Romani homes before 1950.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Sundt and the 1801 census; Old Norwegian for the *King's Mirror*; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding schools (*internat*) under Norwegianization (*fornorskning*), the mission's taking of *tatere* children and the family breakups in the Truth and Reconciliation Commission's report (2023) are contested: present each as an account, in each people's own name. Law questions go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** house-builders' sales catalogs were weighed as house-type sources and left out as advertising; no domain pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
