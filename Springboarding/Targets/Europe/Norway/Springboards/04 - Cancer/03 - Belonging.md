# Norway: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali and Arabic where apt. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old families against *tilflyttere* in a Sogn or Telemark valley:** check how many years local histories say an incomer needs.
- **Oslo's east-end youth and west-end youth:** check where each says they belong when asked city, nation or parents' country.
- **Coastal Sámi of Kåfjord and Porsanger against herding Sámi of Kautokeino:** check whether revival and continuity are spoken of differently.
- **Kvens who say *kven* and those who say *norskfinne*:** check what each name claims, through their own associations.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check how a people with few speakers left marks descent.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how each is received in the places where they winter or settle.
- **Jewish community of Oslo and Trondheim:** check the 1851 change and the post-war return in community histories.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian, Kurdish, Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check belonging by generation in IMDi and SSB figures.
- **Trøndelag and Northern Norway against Østlandet:** check regional pride as a layer of belonging.
- **Longyearbyen's Norwegian, Thai and Filipino residents:** check who takes part in local life on a fixed stay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national identity debates and immigrant surveys; mixed for rural incomers; thin for Forest Finn and Romani belonging.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål, Nynorsk and Danish-Norwegian; North Sámi; Kven and Finnish; Somali, Arabic and Polish for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as Sámi or Kven is argued within the peoples themselves; the 1942 deportation and the Romani people's treatment shape Jewish and Romani accounts; present each telling as an account and never state what a group is. Citizenship law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** opinion-poll questions on "who is a real Norwegian" were weighed and kept only as debate material, not as data; no belonging pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
