# Norway: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic, Vietnamese and Somali where apt. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo families against farm families of Gudbrandsdalen and Hedmarken:** check children's chores and who teaches them, through NEG answers and oral histories.
- **Fishing families of Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark's coast:** check how upbringing was shared when parents were at sea or at the fish plants.
- **North Sámi families of Karasjok and Kautokeino; South Sámi families of Snåsa:** check how each describes teaching children by doing.
- **Kven and Forest Finn families:** check grandparents' part in raising children, in the few oral histories there are.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check each community's own telling of child-rearing, set apart from the mission and welfare files.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* families of Sørlandet and Nord-Troms:** check Sunday school and home devotion as nurture.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Syrian, Iraqi and Vietnamese parents:** check how they speak of Norwegian kindergartens, through parents' groups and ICDP reports.
- **Longyearbyen families:** check child-rearing through the polar night, in school and kindergarten plans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for kindergartens, child health and cohort data; mixed for home practice; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and dialect for lullabies; Old Norse for the sagas; North Sámi for Sámi plans and Balto's sources; Kven; Polish, Arabic, Vietnamese and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child-welfare (*barnevern*) disputes with newcomer families, the boarding schools under Norwegianization (*fornorskning*) and the removal of *tatere* children all bear on who was allowed to raise whom; give each as an account, in each people's own name. Discipline and punishment in law go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** commercial parenting-influencer accounts were weighed as a source of current nurture talk and kept for the research stage, since they are hard to cite stably; no nurture pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
