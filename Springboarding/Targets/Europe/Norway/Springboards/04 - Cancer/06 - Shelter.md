# Norway: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic and Ukrainian where apt. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain districts of Dovre, Jotunheimen and Hardangervidda:** check the custom of the open cabin and what users owe the next traveler.
- **Finnmark and Nord-Troms:** check shelter in the 1944-1945 evacuation as remembered by those who hid and those who were moved.
- **Reindeer-herding families on the migration routes:** check how the tent and the *gamme* are spoken of as shelter on the move.
- **Kvens of the Varanger coast:** check lodging customs on the old travel routes; sources are few.
- **The *tatere*:** check winter quarters, the labor colony and what came after, in the people's own accounts.
- **Jewish families of Oslo and Trondheim:** check who hid them in 1942 and how that memory is kept.
- **Asylum seekers and refugees (Syrian, Eritrean, Somali, Iraqi, Ukrainian):** check reception centers and host homes from the residents' side.
- **Women and children in crisis centers:** check by county and by country background.
- **Lofoten crews and Longyearbyen's field travelers:** check work shelters shared between strangers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for asylum, crisis-center and civil-defense figures; mixed for open-cabin custom; thin for Kven lodging and for evacuation-era hiding beyond a few towns.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the sagas; Danish-Norwegian for older poor relief; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Tigrinya and Ukrainian for refugee voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Svanviken colony, the 1942 deportation, church sanctuary and asylum reception are contested; present each as an account, in each community's own name, and keep crisis-center locations out of any published note. Asylum law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** refugee-camp photo essays were weighed as shelter sources and held for a later rights check; no shelter pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
