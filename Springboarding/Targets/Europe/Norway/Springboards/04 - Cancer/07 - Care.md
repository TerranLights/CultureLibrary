# Norway: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *omsorg*, *ta vare på* | care; to take care of | *omsorg* names both the feeling and the public service |
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *pleie*, *stell* | nursing; tending (also of animals) | *stell* is the everyday word for washing and dressing the old and infants |
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *pårørende* / *pårørande* | next of kin who help | the policy word for family carers |
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *hjemmesykepleie* / *heimesjukepleie*, *sykehjem* / *sjukeheim* | home nursing; nursing home | municipal services, the core of elder care |
| Bokmål | *omsorgsstønad* | care allowance paid to family carers | formerly *omsorgslønn* |
| Bokmål | *legd*, *legdslem* | rotation of paupers among farms; a person so kept | 18th- and 19th-century poor relief |
| Old Norse | *ómagi* | a dependent who must be kept | *check* in Fritzner and the law glossaries |
| North Sámi | *dikšut*, *dikšu* | to tend, look after; tending | in Norway, search Karasjok and Kautokeino nursing-home plans with Sámi-speaking staff, where the word marks care given in Sámi |
| North Sámi | *guvhllár* | healer | *check*; search with *læsing* (Norwegian: healing by reading prayers), whose healers in Troms and Finnmark serve Sámi, Kven and Norwegian clients alike |
| Kven | *hoito*, *huolehtia* | care; to look after | in Norway, search Kven elder-care material from Nordreisa and Vadsø, where few services are given in Kven |
| Polish / Lithuanian | *opieka*; *globa* | care; guardianship | Polish and Lithuanian care workers in Norwegian municipalities |
| Arabic / Vietnamese / Somali | *ʿināya* (عناية); *chăm sóc*; *daryeel* | care | *check* forms; Arabic and Somali elders' groups in Oslo, Vietnamese associations in Bergen |

English keywords: Norwegian elder care, family carers, home nursing, deaconesses, folk healing, care ethics, care workers, poor relief.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural municipalities of Innlandet and Nordland against Oslo:** check distance to care and who fills the gap.
- **Sámi elders of Karasjok and Kautokeino; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check care given in one's own language, through the care-research centers.
- **Kven and Norwegian Finn elders of Nordreisa and Vadsø:** check whether any care is offered in Kven; sources are few.
- **Laestadian families of Nord-Troms and Alta:** check care inside the congregation and family before services.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how elders are cared for, through the groups' own organizations.
- **Jewish community:** check community care for elders, through Det Mosaiske Trossamfund.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese families:** check expectations of family care for elders against municipal services.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Filipino care workers:** check the carers' side of paid care.
- **Longyearbyen:** check what the absence of long-term care does to staying in old age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public care statistics and nursing history; mixed for family care; thin for Kven, Romani and Forest Finn care practices.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for poor-relief records; Old Norse for the laws; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** folk healing (*læsing*) may be private to healers and their clients; ask before naming anyone. Care of *tatere* and Sámi elders touches Norwegianization (*fornorskning*); give each telling as an account. Law on care rights goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** care-home inspection reports naming individual residents were weighed and left out for privacy; no care pointer fell under the South Asian rule.
