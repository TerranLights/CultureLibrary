# Norway: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål, Nynorsk and Northern Norwegian speech for frankness idioms; Danish-Norwegian for 17th- to 19th-century texts; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northern Norway and Trøndelag against Østlandet and Sørlandet:** check how idioms of frankness and reserve are used in local press and dialect corpora.
- **Bergen against Oslo:** check how civic self-display (the *buekorps* boys' brigades, the city songs) differs from the capital's.
- **North Sámi youth of Kautokeino and Karasjok against coastal and South Sámi:** check when wearing the *gákti* or speaking Sámi in public is described as a statement.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Nordreisa:** check how the revival of the language is spoken of, since sources are few.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how each speaks of hiding or showing who they are, through their own associations.
- **Muslim, Jewish and Catholic Norwegians:** check how religious dress and symbols are discussed by each community itself.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* families:** check the congregations' own rules and the memoirs of those who left.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Polish and Vietnamese youth:** check second-generation media for how expression differs at home and outside.
- **Longyearbyen:** check the *Svalbardposten* letters page as a small-town outlet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** surveys on free speech and the press give plenty; diaries and albums are spread across museums; Kven and Romani voices are few in print.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Engelbretsdatter and Jæger; North Sámi for Valkeapää's own wording; Kven; Arabic and Polish for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hijab, Pride and religious-dress debates are contested: give each side's account; Norwegianization (*fornorskning*) bears on who could show Sámi or Kven identity. Speech law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** anonymous gossip and confession apps were weighed as expression channels and left out as unverifiable.
