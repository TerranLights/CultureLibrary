*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergen and Vestlandet against Oslo:** check city songs, football chants and press for civic rivalry.
- **Trøndelag and Northern Norway:** check regional anthems and dialect pride in music and local radio.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, coastal Sámi of Porsanger and Kåfjord, South Sámi of Snåsa:** check when each began to show Sámi emblems openly.
- **Kvens of Varanger and Nord-Troms; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check revival-era sources for pride in descent.
- **The *tatere*, the Rom and the Jewish community:** check how each speaks of pride after persecution, in its own publications.
- **Laestadians and Haugean *bedehus* circles:** check what their sermons and songbooks count as sinful pride.
- **Fishing families of Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark:** check pride in boats and catches in local press.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Polish and Lithuanian communities:** check pride in origin against pride in Norway, by generation.
- **Longyearbyen:** check pride in living farthest north, through local events.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** speeches, sports broadcasts and survey items are easy to reach; regional pride is spread through music and local press; Kven and Forest Finn pride is barely documented.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Holberg and Brun; Old Norse for ONP citations; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Tigrinya and Polish for community programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** national pride touches the occupation and Quisling's use of national symbols; Eritrean national-day events are disputed within the community; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** nationalist and anti-immigrant websites were weighed as material on group pride and left out as campaign writing.
