# Norway: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse for skaldic praise; North Sámi; Kven; Romani (*tater*) and Romanes for apology debates; Somali and Lithuanian where apt. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo against Finnmark and Nordland:** check honors lists for how often each region's people are named.
- **Sámi of Kautokeino, Karasjok and the coast; Lule Sámi of Tysfjord; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check how each answered the 1997 apology and later truth-commission work.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check what national-minority status changed for each, in their associations' own papers.
- **Romani (*tater*) families and the Rom of Oslo:** compare Taternes Landsforening's statements after the state's apology with Rom leaders' words after 2015.
- **The Jewish community of Oslo and Trondheim:** check responses to the 2012 apology in community publications.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Eritrean, Syrian and Somali professionals:** check credential approval and job-market credit in NOKUT and IMDi figures.
- **Women in fishing and farming families of Lofoten, Vesterålen and the inland valleys:** check whose work local histories credit.
- **Church of Norway and *bedehus* congregations:** check how lay service is thanked in each.
- **Longyearbyen and Svalbard's miners:** check which mining-era names *Svalbardposten* and the museum single out for tribute.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** honors lists, prize archives and apologies are public and easy to reach; workplace recognition is mixed; Kven and Forest Finn recognition rests mostly on association papers.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for skaldic verse; North Sámi for Sámi press; Kven; Romani (*tater*) and Romanes for community responses; Somali and Lithuanian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state apologies and minority status are contested ground: give each community's account in its own name. Minority law goes to Libra; the Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2023) is a social record here.
- **Held for review:** paid "award" schemes and business rankings were weighed as recognition sources and left out as sold honors.
