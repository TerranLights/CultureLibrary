# Norway: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Latin and Old Norwegian for medieval charters; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's east and west ends:** check whose neighborhoods appear in news counts and in Retriever searches.
- **Small towns of Sørlandet and Telemark against Bergen:** check greeting habits on the street through local press and dialect corpora.
- **Sámi of Finnmark, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord and South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros:** check which group gets screen time and which says it is left out.
- **Kvens of Nord-Troms and Varanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check when each began to describe itself as overlooked, and in what venue.
- **The Jewish community:** check how visible signs of faith in Oslo are discussed by the community itself.
- **Polish and Lithuanian building and fish-plant workers:** check how they say they are seen at work, through union and Fafo material.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check casting and news presence in media studies.
- **Older people living alone in Gudbrandsdalen and the inland valleys:** check visiting services and municipal reports.
- **Longyearbyen's dark season:** check what the polar night does to social contact, through local reporting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** media archives and kindergarten research give a lot; ethnic visibility in statistics is mixed, since Norway registers no ethnicity; Kven, Forest Finn and Romani visibility is thin in print.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old Norwegian for the charters; Danish-Norwegian for older church records; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Lund Commission's findings are still argued over; Sámi, Kven and Romani accounts of hiding one's background under Norwegianization (*fornorskning*) are contested ground: give each as its people tell it. Surveillance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** paparazzi and celebrity-gossip sites were weighed for the attention facet and left out, since *Se og Hør*-type material is already pointed to elsewhere.
