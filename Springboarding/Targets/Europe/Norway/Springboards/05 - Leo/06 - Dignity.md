# Norway: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Care homes in Oslo against small municipalities in Gudbrandsdalen and Sogn:** check *Verdighetsgarantien* complaints by municipality.
- **Sámi elders in Kautokeino and Karasjok; Lule Sámi in Tysfjord; South Sámi in Snåsa:** check whether care in Sámi is offered and how patients describe it.
- **Kven elders in Nord-Troms and Varanger:** check access to care in Kven or Finnish, through local projects.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how dealings with health and welfare services are described in each group's own reports.
- **Laestadian families in Nord-Troms and Finnmark:** check how congregations speak of dying and end-of-life care.
- **Muslim, Jewish and Catholic communities:** check burial and washing customs and how hospitals meet them.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers in fish plants and building sites:** check housing and work-condition cases in labor-inspection material.
- **Somali, Eritrean and Syrian families:** check NAV and housing encounters in their own associations' reports.
- **Fishing villages of Lofoten and Finnmark:** check local histories of the old under the poor-relief system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** care regulation, user surveys and the assisted-dying debate are well documented; workers' and poor people's own words are mixed; Kven and Romani sources are few.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *mannhelgi*; North Sámi for the anthem and care material; Kven; Arabic, Somali and Polish for client material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the assisted-dying debate is contested; the treatment of the *tatere* (including sterilizations) and Sámi boarding schools are contested ground: present each account in each people's own name. Care and burial law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** care-home sales brochures were weighed as dignity material and left out as marketing.
