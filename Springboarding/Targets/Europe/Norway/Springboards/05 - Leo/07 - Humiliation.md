# Norway: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for church books and Sundt; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural parishes of Gudbrandsdalen and Telemark against Bergen and Trondheim:** check church books for how often public confession was imposed.
- **Sámi pupils of Finnmark boarding schools, coastal Sámi of Kåfjord, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi of Snåsa:** check school memoirs and commission testimony for each group.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Nord-Troms; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check mockery for speaking Kven or Finnish, in the commission's material and local histories.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check what slurs and treatment each describes, through Taternes Landsforening and Rom spokespeople.
- **The Jewish community:** check how the 1942 deportation and later harassment are described in community histories.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish and Polish Norwegians:** check racist incidents in Antirasistisk Senter and police hate-crime figures.
- **Laestadian congregations:** check how public confession of sins is described from inside.
- **Oslo's school youth against Northern Norway's:** check *russelåt* lyrics and local press debates by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** bullying, inspection and harassment reports are rich; church-book confessions need parish-by-parish work; Kven and Forest Finn testimony is thin outside the 2023 commission.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for church books and Sundt; Old Norse; North Sámi for Larsen; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization (*fornorskning*), the treatment of the *tatere*, the 1942 deportation and the post-war treason settlement are contested; present each account in each people's own name. Church-book entries name private persons: cite by parish, not by person.
- **Held for review:** online "exposé" pages that name private individuals were weighed and left out, since citing them would repeat the shaming.
