# Norway: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with fishing-crew and work-gang titles; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali and Arabic where apt. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fishing communities of Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark against farming valleys of Gudbrandsdalen:** check how skippers and farm heads were followed, in local histories.
- **Trøndelag against Vestlandet:** check sports clubs and cooperatives for different leader styles as described by members.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, coastal Sámi and South Sámi of Snåsa:** check how leaders are chosen in *siida*, associations and the Sámediggi.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check who speaks for each people and how, since sources are few.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check how each organization chooses spokespeople, in their own accounts.
- **Laestadian congregations and *bedehus* circles against the Church of Norway:** check lay leadership against ordained leadership.
- **Muslim congregations and the Catholic diocese:** check how imams, boards and priests share leadership.
- **Polish, Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish and Vietnamese associations:** check how leaders are chosen and by whom.
- **Longyearbyen:** check leadership under the governor, the company and the local council.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** elite studies, gender statistics and expedition writing are rich; workplace style is mixed and often described by outsiders; Kven, Forest Finn and Romani leadership is thin.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *Hirðskrá*; North Sámi for *siida* material; Kven; Polish, Somali and Arabic for associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the occupation, Quisling's regime and the Alta and Fosen disputes involve contested leadership: present each account as an account. Command authority and the board-quota law belong to Capricorn or Libra.
- **Held for review:** popular business-culture books that rate Norwegian managers by national type were weighed for the outsiders' view and left out as generalizations.
