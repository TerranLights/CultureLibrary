# Norway: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rjukan and other dark valleys of Telemark against sunny Sørlandet:** check how seasonal joy is described in local press.
- **Tromsø and Lofoten against Oslo:** check what *mørketid* and midnight-sun seasons do to festival calendars.
- **Márka Sámi of Ofoten, North Sámi of Kautokeino, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord and South Sámi of Snåsa:** check each festival's stated purpose.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check choir and dance gatherings in their associations, since written sources are few.
- **The *tatere* and the Rom:** check music and feasts as each describes them, through their own organizations.
- **Pentecostal, Laestadian, *bedehus* and Church of Norway congregations:** check how each sings and shows joy in worship.
- **The Jewish, Muslim and Catholic communities:** check holiday joy (Purim, Eid, Christmas) as each community presents it.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Polish, Kurdish and Vietnamese families:** check joyful occasions in SSB's survey breakdowns and community media.
- **Longyearbyen:** check the sun-return week and other shared festivities in a town without old families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** quality-of-life surveys and happiness research are rich; festival and dance records are mixed; Kven, Forest Finn and Romani joy is thin and needs association contacts.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse to read the feast and Christmas-ale words in context; Danish-Norwegian for Landstad's hymn texts; North, Lule and South Sámi for festival programs; Kven for choir material; Somali and Vietnamese for community event notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Laestadian and *bedehus* limits on dance and drink are described differently from inside and outside; give each congregation's own account. Alcohol law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** Buddhism, as a category only: one festive-occasion pointer was set aside under the author's open question.
