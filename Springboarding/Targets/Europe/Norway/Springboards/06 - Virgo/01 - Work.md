# Norway: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with mining, fishing and navvy vocabulary; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven and Forest Finnish; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Røros and Kongsberg mining towns against Rjukan:** check how each town's histories name skill and toil, since state mining and company industry ran on different terms.
- **Stavanger and Bergen offshore families:** check how rotation shifts fit family life, in local press and union papers.
- **Coastal Finnmark and Lofoten fishing crews against Østlandet farm families:** check where "work" means the season and where it means the year.
- **North Sámi herders of Kautokeino and Karasjok, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi of Røros:** check how herding, fishing and wage work are named in each language.
- **Kven coastal workers in Vadsø and Nordreisa; Forest Finns of Grue and Åsnes:** check memoir projects, since little else is printed.
- **Romani (*tater*) and Rom families:** check the trades each people names as its own in its own associations' material, and the later loss of those trades.
- **Haugean and *bedehus* communities of Vestlandet and Sørlandet:** check how sermons and biographies speak of a calling.
- **Polish and Lithuanian building workers, Eritrean, Syrian and Somali newcomers, Vietnamese families who arrived from 1975:** check each group's own press for its words about work.
- **Longyearbyen's miners and service staff:** check how the work changed as mining gave way to tourism and research.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** labor statistics and labor history are abundant; Sámi herding has its own records; Kven, Forest Finn and Romani work history is scattered and thin.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Friis; Old Norse for *Rígsþula*; Nynorsk for Uppdal; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Swedish and Finnish for Gottlund's diaries; Polish and Lithuanian for workers' portals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** herders' disputes with the state over land (Alta, Fosen) are contested: present each side as its own account. Labor law and contracts go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** staffing-agency recruitment pages were weighed as a view on labor migration and left out as advertising.
