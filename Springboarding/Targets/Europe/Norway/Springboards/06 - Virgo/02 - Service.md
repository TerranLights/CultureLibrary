# Norway: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet manor and farm districts against Vestlandet freeholders:** check cottars' and servants' records by district, since the weight of the *husmann* system differed.
- **Oslo and Bergen hotel and restaurant workers against Lofoten tourist seasonal staff:** check union and press material on tips, hours and how customers address them.
- **Finnmark and Troms Sámi communities:** check Sámediggi reports on how Sámi speakers are served in their own language by care homes, schools and clinics.
- **Kven families of Nord-Troms and Porsanger:** check memoirs of service on Norwegian farms and in fishing households.
- **Romani (*tater*) and Rom communities:** check their own accounts of public services and of the welfare and mission bodies they dealt with, each in its people's own name.
- **Church of Norway, Catholic, Muslim and Jewish congregations:** check each one's own volunteer and diaconal service, through parish, mosque and synagogue bulletins.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Eritrean, Somali, Syrian and Vietnamese workers:** check how each community's media describe work in cleaning, care and food service.
- **Longyearbyen's Filipino and Thai residents:** check how service workers in the town speak of their place in it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** public-service and customer surveys are good; old service ranks are well recorded in local histories; thin for Kven and Romani experience of service.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for catechisms and Thrane's paper; Old Norse for the levy; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Somali for workers' media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** services were used in Norwegianization (*fornorskning*) and in the treatment of the *tatere*: present each account in each people's own name. Service law, the old servants' acts and conscription law go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** hotel chains' service-training manuals were weighed for the customer facet and left out as in-house material that is hard to reach.
