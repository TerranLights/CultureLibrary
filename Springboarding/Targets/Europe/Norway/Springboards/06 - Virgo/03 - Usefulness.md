# Norway: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with western dialect words; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Arabic where apt. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose: for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet and Trøndelag farm families against Oslo's service economy:** check how children's help is described in local histories against city parenting forums.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark fishing villages:** check which tasks children and elders take in the season, through local museum interviews.
- **Herding Sámi of Kautokeino, coastal Sámi of Porsanger, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi of Røros:** check each one's own words for a person who contributes.
- **Kvens of Børselv and Vadsø; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check elders' roles in language and craft revival.
- **Romani (*tater*) families and the Rom:** check their own accounts of trades and roles, kept apart from what officials and missions wrote about them.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* households:** check how congregation papers speak of the old and the sick within the fellowship.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Kurdish, Syrian and Eritrean families:** check each community's own associations for how elders' and young people's roles are spoken of.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how residents talk about leaving Svalbard when they can no longer work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** good for older workers, disability benefits and recycling; mixed for children's help; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani views.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Jenssøn and Strøm; Nynorsk and dialect for *gagn* and *duga*; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Somali and Arabic for parents' groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the sterilizations under the 1934 law and the *tatere*'s treatment bear on who was called useless: present each account in its people's own name. Benefit rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** productivity apps and self-help titles were considered for "feeling useful" and set aside as imported material with no Norwegian specificity.
