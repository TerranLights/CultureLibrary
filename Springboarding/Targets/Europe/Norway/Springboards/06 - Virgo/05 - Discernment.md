# Norway: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norwegian; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lofoten stockfish sorters against Ålesund klippfisk producers:** check how each trade grades its fish, since grading is discernment made into a job.
- **Vestlandet horse breeders and Gudbrandsdalen farmers:** check show and *kåring* records for who is trusted as a judge.
- **Karasjok reindeer owners, sea Sámi fishers of the Hammerfest fjords, Lule Sámi of Divtasvuodna and South Sámi of Snåsa:** check whose eye is trusted to name and grade animals, snow and fishing water.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and the Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check place-name and craft records for their own distinctions, since outsiders' records lump them together.
- **Romani (*tater*) people and the Rom:** check their own accounts of being judged by officials on sight, set beside the officials' records.
- **Pentecostal, Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations, and the Church of Norway:** check how each congregation decides whether a message or a preacher is to be trusted.
- **Jewish and Muslim congregations:** check their own teaching material on judging food, conduct and truth.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese newcomers:** check community media for how they are taught to judge Norwegian institutions, contracts and news.
- **Oslo's media-saturated youth against rural Innlandet and Finnmark:** check Medietilsynet's figures by county and age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media literacy, fraud and food quality; mixed for craft and grading knowledge; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani terms.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for *Barlaams saga*; Danish-Norwegian for Aasen; North Sámi for herding vocabulary; Kven for place names; Arabic and Somali for religious teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hiring and housing discrimination touch named communities: present results as findings to check, never as traits of a group. Some herding knowledge is held by families.
- **Held for review:** commercial wine and beer rating apps were considered for the taste facet and set aside as international platforms with little that is Norwegian.
