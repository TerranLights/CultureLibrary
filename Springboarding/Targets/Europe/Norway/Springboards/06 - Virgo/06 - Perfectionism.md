# Norway: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jæren and Sørlandet pietist districts against Oslo:** check revival-era sermons and memoirs for how moral perfection was demanded and doubted.
- **Hardanger, Setesdal and Telemark bunad makers:** check each district's rules for correct dress and who enforces them.
- ***Gákti* sewers and *duodji* judges of Inner Finnmark against Lule Sámi makers at Árran and South Sámi makers around Snåsa:** check who sets the standard for a correct piece and how a flawed one is spoken of.
- **Kven and Forest Finn craft revivals:** check whether revived crafts set new exact standards, since old models are few.
- **Romani (*tater*) craft traditions and the Rom:** check standards of finish in their own associations' material, where any survives.
- **Laestadian, *bedehus* and Pentecostal congregations:** check how each speaks of sin, grace and the fear of not being good enough.
- **Trondheim, Oslo and Bergen high schools:** check grade data and pupils' own media for pressure in different cities.
- **Polish and Lithuanian building tradesmen, Vietnamese and Eritrean families, Syrian newcomers:** check each community's own press for words on standards at work and school, without assuming a pattern.
- **Longyearbyen guides and scientists:** check how safety routines on Svalbard ask for exact procedure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for youth pressure, sport and standards; mixed for religious scrupulosity; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani craft standards.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for verse forms; Danish-Norwegian for Holberg; *landsmål* for Garborg; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic and Vietnamese for community sermons and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders and suicide among high achievers need care: use services' and patients' own accounts, not case details. Imitation *gákti* touches Sámi cultural ownership.
- **Held for review:** coaching and self-help courses on perfectionism were looked at and left out as sold material with little Norwegian specificity.
