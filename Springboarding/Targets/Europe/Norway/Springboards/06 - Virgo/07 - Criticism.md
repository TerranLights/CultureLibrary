# Norway: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norwegian; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali, Arabic, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergen, Stavanger and Oslo press milieus:** check letters pages and editorials for how bluntly local power is criticized, since each city has its own press history.
- **Trøndelag and Northern Norway against Østlandet:** check local revues and dialect comedy for who is mocked and how sharply.
- **North Sámi writers and editors of Karasjok and Kautokeino, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi in Trøndelag:** check how each criticizes the state and each other, in Sámi and in Norwegian.
- **Kven associations and the Forest Finns:** check their criticism of older naming and recognition policy, in their own papers.
- **Romani (*tater*) organizations and the Rom:** check their critiques of state policy as they have voiced them in hearings and press.
- **Laestadian, *bedehus* and Pentecostal congregations:** check how each handles correction of members, in congregation papers and memoirs.
- **Jewish and Muslim communities:** check their own statements in public debates on criticism of religion.
- **Polish, Kurdish, Iraqi, Syrian, Eritrean and Vietnamese writers and journalists in Norway:** check exile and community press for criticism of Norway and of the homeland.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how residents criticize decisions made in Oslo about Svalbard.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and press criticism; mixed for workplace feedback; thin for Kven and Romani voices in print.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for the bishops' speech; Danish-Norwegian for Wessel; North Sámi for *Ávvir*; Kven; Kurdish, Arabic and Vietnamese for exile writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blasphemy and hate-speech law go to Libra or Capricorn. The cartoon controversies, the ČSV period and the Alta controversy are contested ground: present each side as an account.
- **Held for review:** anonymous gossip apps were weighed for the complaint facet and left aside, since the Gemini gossip sheet already points there.
