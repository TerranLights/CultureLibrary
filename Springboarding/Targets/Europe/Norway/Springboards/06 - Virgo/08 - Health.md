# Norway: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with folk-medicine dialect words; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven and Forest Finnish; Polish, Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet coastal districts against Østlandet inland farms:** check folk-medicine archives and life-expectancy tables for whether diet and work leave a mark.
- **Bergen and Hordaland:** check leprosy-era patient records and family memoirs against the rest of the coast.
- **North Sámi of Karasjok and Kautokeino, coastal Sámi of Porsanger and Tana, Lule Sámi of Divtasvuodna, South Sámi of Snåsa:** check whose healing knowledge is shared openly and in which language patients are seen.
- **Kvens of Lyngen and Nord-Troms; Forest Finns of Grue:** check memoir collections on cupping, sauna and charms.
- **Laestadian congregations of Nord-Troms:** check how members speak of prayer and medicine together.
- **Romani (*tater*) people and the Rom:** check their own testimonies about health care, and the sterilization history, in their own words.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Polish, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check each community's own health associations and media for their words on illness and care.
- **Jewish and Muslim communities:** check their own guidance on fasting, food and care at the end of life.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check how they speak of polar night, care and leaving for the mainland when ill.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for public-health statistics and medical history; mixed for folk medicine; thin and scattered for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani health knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *heill* and *sótt*; Danish-Norwegian for early asylum and leprosy records; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Tigrinya for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi healers' knowledge and joik may be restricted; the forced sterilizations of Romani people (*tatere*) and the race-biology era are contested ground: present each account in its people's own name. Patient-rights law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** wellness influencers and supplement sellers were weighed for the "healthism" facet and left out as commercial content.
