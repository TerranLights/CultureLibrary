# Norway: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norwegian; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet grain districts against Vestlandet fjord farms:** check Norges Vel and Ny Jord records for which districts were treated as needing improvement.
- **Industrial towns (Odda, Sauda, Rjukan) against valleys flooded for power:** check how each tells the story of progress and loss.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, sea Sámi of Kåfjord, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi of Snåsa:** check how each describes revival in its own words, apart from state programs.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Nordreisa; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check revival projects and their own measures of success.
- **Romani (*tater*) people and the Rom:** check their own organizations' accounts of the settlement policies and of what they now ask for.
- **Haugean, *bedehus* and Laestadian communities:** check how sermons and memoirs speak of bettering one's life and soul.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Kurdish, Eritrean and Vietnamese communities:** check courses, youth groups and media in each language for their own goals of getting on.
- **Oslo's east and west ends:** check the area-improvement programs and residents' own views of them.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how short-stay and long-stay residents differ on the town's future.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for adult education, agriculture and self-help; mixed for renovation figures; Kven, Forest Finn and Romani perspectives on improvement are thinly recorded.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norwegian for the merchant's lessons; Danish-Norwegian for the *Topographisk Journal*; North Sámi for revival material; Kven; Polish and Somali for community courses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization (*fornorskning*), the Truth and Reconciliation Commission (2023), the treatment of the *tatere* and the Alta controversy are contested ground: present each account as an account, naming each people in its own name. School and assimilation law goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** motivational-speaker and coaching businesses were weighed for the self-help facet and set aside in favor of Selvhjelp Norge and the study associations.
