# Norway: Partnership and justice, the domain and folkways (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål, Nynorsk and older Danish-Norwegian; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** the domain of partnership and justice: what a fair bargain and a good partnership are (marriage, contracts, open enemies, disputes, gender ways), with the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet (Hardanger, Sogn) against Østlandet and Gudbrandsdalen:** check wedding rites and bridal crowns in county museums.
- **Trøndelag:** check *Frostatingslova*'s terms against Gulating ground.
- **Sørlandet *bedehus* congregations and Haugean circles:** check their own papers on marriage, divorce and quarrels.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, coastal Sámi, Lule Sámi of Tysfjord, South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros:** check settlement and wedding rites as each records them.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Varanger; Forest Finns of Grue and Åsnes:** check church books for marriages across groups; print is scarce.
- **Laestadians in Nord-Troms:** check the movement's own preaching on marriage and reconciliation.
- **Jewish community in Oslo and Trondheim; Catholics, Polish and Lithuanian among them:** check each body's marriage rules against civil law.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check associations' own wedding and registration guidance.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of marriage and quarrels before outside records.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how workers from treaty nations use the Svalbard rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, marriage figures and old law; mixed for district wedding custom; thin for Sámi, Kven, Romani and Forest Finn settlement outside commissions.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the provincial laws; Danish-Norwegian for *Norske Lov*; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Tigrinya, Somali and Arabic for wedding guidance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization (*fornorskning*) bears on Sámi and Kven marriage records; the treatment of the *tatere* reaches into marriage and family records: give each account as an account, in that people's own name. The spirit of law goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** commercial wedding-planner sites were weighed for the wedding facet and left out as advertising, pending a better industry source.
