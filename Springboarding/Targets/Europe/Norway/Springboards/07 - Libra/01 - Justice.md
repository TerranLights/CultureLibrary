# Norway: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North and South Sámi (South Sámi terms to be gathered from Saemien Sijte); Kven; Polish, Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society (the law-related facets by their letter only).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo courts against Finnmark's:** check how Indre og Østre Finnmark tingrett hears North Sámi testimony.
- **North Sámi herders, coastal Sámi and South Sámi:** check which justice claims each brings (grazing, fishing, land) and in which forum.
- **Kvens/Norwegian Finns and Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check each group's own submissions to the 2023 commission.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own organizations' responses to state redress, kept apart.
- **Jewish community:** check Det Mosaiske Trossamfund's own record of the 1998 settlement.
- **Laestadians in Nord-Troms; *bedehus* congregations of Sørlandet and Vestlandet:** check sermons and tracts on divine judgment and forgiveness.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Polish communities:** check each community's own media on due process as written.
- **Trøndelag:** check Fosen's local press from herders' and landowners' sides.
- **Svalbard:** check which court hears Longyearbyen's cases.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, judgments and commission material; mixed for victims' experience; thin for Kven and Forest Finn justice claims before 2018.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for outlawry; Danish-Norwegian for the 1850s trial; North and South Sámi (South Sámi terms to be gathered from Saemien Sijte); Kven; Polish and Arabic for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Fosen, the 2023 report, the Jewish property inquiry and the 22 July case are contested or painful ground: give each account as an account, in each community's own name. What law is for and obedience to it go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** survivors' private testimony in the commission's archive was weighed for the historical facet and left out, since access may be restricted.
