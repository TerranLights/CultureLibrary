# Norway: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet farm districts against Vestlandet:** check odal and inheritance disputes in *jordskifte* (land consolidation) court records by county.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark coast fishing families:** check whether crew-share rules differ by gear and harbor.
- **North Sámi herding *siida* of Kautokeino and Karasjok; South Sámi herders of Trøndelag:** check how each frames shares of pasture and herd cuts.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Varanger:** check local histories for shared fishing and farm work, since sources are thin.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers in construction and fish processing:** check their own unions' and associations' words on fair wages.
- **Somali, Syrian and Vietnamese parents:** check school-parent forums for views on grading and admission by marks, in their own languages.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own organizations on fair access to housing and schooling.
- **Oslo east and west:** check how school admission by grades is debated in each half of the city.
- **Svalbard:** check debate on the archipelago's own tax rate as fair or not.

## 4. Flags
