# Norway: Partnership (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** a committed bond between two or more parties who share aims, risks and returns as (more or less) equals: in love and marriage, in work and business, and between peoples.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Agder's shipping towns against Bergen's merchant houses:** check whether partnership in a ship or a firm was spread among neighbors or kept in one family.
- **Vestlandet herring crews and Lofoten cod crews:** check how each binds its partners for a season.
- **Røros and Gudbrandsdalen dance traditions against Hardanger's:** check which couple dances each kept.
- **North Sámi *siida* in Finnmark and South Sámi *sïjte* in Trøndelag:** check how each sets out who shares a herd and pasture.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check farm and fishing partnerships across families, from local histories.
- **Laestadian families in Nord-Troms; Catholic Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese families:** check each congregation's own teaching on the married couple.
- **Somali, Syrian and Eritrean couples:** check community associations' own family-life courses.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of the couple and the traveling family as a working unit.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how couples on short contracts register partnership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for couple statistics and partnership law; mixed for shipowning shares; thin for Kven and Romani partnership forms.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *félag*; North and South Sámi for herding plans; Danish-Norwegian for nineteenth-century ship registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *siida* disputes can reach court; give each family's account as its own. Sexual partnership belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** dating-app data was weighed for courtship and left out, since no public Norwegian release is known.
