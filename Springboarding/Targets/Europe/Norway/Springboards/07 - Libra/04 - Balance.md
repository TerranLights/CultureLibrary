# Norway: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hedmark's wolf zone and Finnskogen against western sheep districts:** check how each local press weighs predators and grazing.
- **Finnmark and Nordland against Oslo:** check the area-factor debate in each region's papers.
- **Vestlandet and Trøndelag fish-farming coasts:** check how municipalities talk of growth and wild salmon.
- **North Sámi herders on the Finnmark plateau; South Sámi herders in Nordland and Trøndelag:** check how each weighs herd size against pasture in its own papers.
- **Kvens of Nordreisa and Porsanger:** check accounts of the combined farm-and-fishing year; print is scarce.
- **Nynorsk users in Vestlandet and Telemark against Bokmål users:** check views of the Nynorsk quota.
- **Laestadians, *bedehus* congregations and Muslim congregations in Tromsø and Oslo:** check each body's own teaching on rest, fasting and moderation.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese workers:** check their associations' words on hours and rest.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check how the protection plans are read by guides, scientists and miners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, budgets and hours worked; mixed for folk design; thin for Kven and Romani notions of measure.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse; Danish-Norwegian for 1884; North Sámi; Arabic for fasting guides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wolves, the oil fund and fish farming are contested: give each side its own account. The spirit of the constitutional balance belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** health claims about cabin life were weighed for the rest facet and left out as untested lifestyle writing.
