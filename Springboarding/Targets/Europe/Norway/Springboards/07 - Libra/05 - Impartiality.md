# Norway: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small municipalities in Sogn, Nordland and Finnmark against Oslo and Bergen:** check how often disqualification cases arise where everyone knows everyone.
- **North Sámi of Karasjok and Kautokeino; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check how each views the neutrality of courts and commissions that hear land and herding cases.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Vadsø; Forest Finns of Grue:** check local histories for village patronage, since few sources exist.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Arabic-speaking and Polish users of interpreters:** check each community's own accounts of interpreted hearings.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own organizations' accounts of equal treatment by offices.
- **Rosenborg, Brann and Tromsø IL supporters:** check how each club's fans argue about referees.
- **Svalbard:** check how the Sysselmester's double role as governor and police chief is described.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and appointment rules; mixed for everyday favoritism; thin for patronage in Kven and Forest Finn villages.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the Land Law; Danish-Norwegian for Holberg; North Sámi; Somali and Arabic for interpreter material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2023 share cases are recent and contested: give each account as an account. Trust in courts and the spirit of the rule of law belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** country corruption indices were weighed and left out here, since they rank nations rather than open Norway's own ways of judging bias.
