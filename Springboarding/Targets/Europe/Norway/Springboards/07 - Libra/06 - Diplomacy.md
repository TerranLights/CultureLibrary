# Norway: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Russian and Russenorsk; Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finnmark border towns (Kirkenes, Vardø) against Oslo:** check local diplomacy with Russian neighbors against the capital's.
- **Bergen's merchant tradition against inland Gudbrandsdalen:** check advice on bargaining and plain speech in each region's press.
- **North Sámi, Lule Sámi and South Sámi:** check how each takes part in consultations, and whose voice speaks for whom.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger:** check accounts of villages that dealt with Norwegian, Sámi and Finnish neighbors at once.
- **Russian-speaking residents of Kirkenes; Barentsburg's residents:** check their own press for the border's everyday dealings.
- **Somali, Syrian, Iraqi and Vietnamese associations:** check each one's guides to raising concerns with schools and offices.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check preaching on peacemaking among members.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check how each people's spokespersons describe the meetings that led to the state's apologies (1998 and 2015, *check*), and who was invited to speak.
