# Norway: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven and Forest Finnish; Polish, Tigrinya and Somali where apt. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal Vestlandet and Trøndelag fishing villages against inland valleys:** check work exchange at haying and at the landing of a catch.
- **North Sámi herders and coastal Sámi:** check how *verdde* exchange with settled families is described in each area's own accounts.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen and Kvens of Porsanger:** check whether work parties survived and how they were repaid.
- **Christmas customs in Setesdal, Hardanger and Finnmark:** check the *julebukk* visit as a gift for a treat, by district.
- **Polish, Lithuanian and Filipino workers:** check what each community's own press says of help owed between coworkers.
- **Eritrean, Somali and Vietnamese associations:** check savings clubs and gift customs at weddings and funerals, in their own words.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations; Pentecostal churches:** check teaching on offering, tithe and giving without return.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of hospitality and its return.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how departing residents pass on gear and furniture (search *Svalbardposten*, "gis bort").

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, insurance and fisheries; mixed for gift customs; thin for Forest Finn and Kven work parties.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *Hávamál*; North Sámi for offering-site reports; Polish and Tigrinya for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *sieidi* locations and practices may be restricted by Sámi communities and by heritage law: ask before use. Revenge belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** national giving indices were weighed and left out, since they count charity, not exchange.
