# Norway: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Trøndersk and northern dialect forms; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Somali where apt. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet (Stad, Sogn) against Oslo:** check how each region's press frames long-delayed projects.
- **Trøndelag and Northern Norway:** check dialect hedges in recorded speech against Oslo speech, using the dialect archives.
- **North Sámi and South Sámi communities:** check how *siida* and Sámediggi decisions are timed and who has the last word.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check local histories for decisions to emigrate or stay, since few other records exist.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check teaching on waiting for God's leading before choosing.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers; Somali and Syrian families:** check their own forums on choosing to settle or return.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of choices about settling or traveling.
- **Longyearbyen:** check the years-long choice of what replaced the coal power plant, in *Svalbardposten*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for election and consumer data; mixed for literature and planning history; thin for minority accounts of choosing.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Garborg and Ibsen; dialect transcription skills; North Sámi; Polish and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the government quarter, the EU question and end-of-life choices are contested or painful: give each account as an account. Leadership and responsibility belong to Capricorn.
