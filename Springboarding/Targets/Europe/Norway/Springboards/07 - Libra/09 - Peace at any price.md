# Norway: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; German for occupation records; Arabic and Somali where apt. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rogaland's Quaker and *bedehus* milieus against Oslo's labor movement:** check how each argued pacifism between the wars.
- **Finnmark and Nord-Troms, burned in 1944:** check whether postwar silence about the war and the evacuation is described in local memoirs.
- **Coastal Sámi of Porsanger and Kåfjord; North Sámi of the inland:** check accounts of keeping quiet about Sámi identity, in each group's own words.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check the same question, with Kven elders' interviews and Finnskogen memoirs.
- **Jewish community:** check its own record of the 1940s and of silence afterward, through its own institutions.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Syrian and Eritrean communities:** check how each community's own sources describe raising complaints with landlords, schools and employers.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of when they spoke up and when they stayed quiet with authorities.
- **Workplaces in the oil sector against municipal care services:** check whistleblowing cases by sector.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for occupation history and labor law; mixed for family silence; thin for Kven and Forest Finn accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Ibsen; German and Norwegian for 1940 records; Old Norse for the truce formulas; North Sámi; Arabic and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the occupation, Quisling and the 1940 negotiations are contested ground: present each account as an account. Abuse cases need care for privacy. Fear and surrender belong to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** religious bodies' internal abuse inquiries were weighed and left out until each is public and can be cited without naming a community as a problem.
