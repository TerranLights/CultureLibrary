# Norway: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian of the trial records; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Arabic where apt. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finnmark (Vardø, Vadsø) against southern Norway:** check how each region tells the story of the witch trials and who was accused.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Varanger:** check their own organizations' accounts of the "Finnish danger" years.
- **North Sámi, coastal Sámi and South Sámi:** check how each group's media describe outside images of them.
- **Jewish and Muslim congregations in Oslo, Bergen and Trondheim:** check their own replies to attitude surveys.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own museum work (Glomdalsmuseet) against the legends recorded about them.
- **Polish, Somali, Syrian and Iraqi communities:** check their own press for how they answer public blame, in their own words.
- **Trøndelag:** check local memory of the Bjugn case against national coverage.
- **Bergen, Trondheim and Oslo:** check the jokes each city tells about the others.
- **Longyearbyen and Barentsburg:** check how each settlement's press pictures the other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trial records and attitude surveys; mixed for moral-panic history; thin for Kven sources on the "Finnish danger".
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian to read the Vardø court protocols; North Sámi for the Sámi accused's names and words; Kven for the border-policy years; Polish and Arabic for replies to public blame.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the trials, the "Finnish danger", the occupation and the Bjugn case are contested or painful ground: name each community by its own name and give each account as an account. Revenge and betrayal belong to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** anonymous online forums were weighed for the blame-game facet and left out, since their posts cannot be traced to a region or a speaker.
