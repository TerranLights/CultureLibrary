*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rogaland's oil communities against Trøndelag's fishing coast:** check how a workplace disaster and a storm at sea are mourned and memorialized.
- **Nordland, Lofoten and Vesterålen:** check sea-ghost lore against modern rescue and drowning records.
- **Oslo and Hole after 22 July:** check whose voices shaped the memorials, and who opposed them.
- **Finnmark and Nord-Troms, burned in 1944; Trøndelag's camp at Falstad:** check war graves, executions and lost churchyards.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Kåfjord; Lule Sámi of Tysfjord; South Sámi:** check mourning words and the return of remains, through Sámi institutions.
- **Laestadian congregations:** check how their funerals and sermons speak of a death.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Børselv; Forest Finns of Grue:** check local museums' holdings on mourning.
- **Jewish community:** check how its own institutions name the 1942 dead.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Syrian, Kurdish and Eritrean families; Polish and Lithuanian Catholics:** check condolence gatherings and sending the dead abroad, in each community's own sources.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own mourning customs as they choose to share them.
