# Norway: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for revival-era print; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending (conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, the dead who return).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rogaland, Agder and Vestlandet *bedehus* congregations against Oslo's secular milieu:** check how often testimonies of conversion appear in local papers, and in what words.
- **Skien and Telemark against Østfold's Haugean districts:** check how a revival that left the state church and one that stayed inside it are remembered.
- **Troms Laestadians, in Sámi, Kven and Norwegian:** check the movement's own records for how awakening is described in each language.
- **Markasámi of Ofoten and coastal Sámi of Porsanger against reindeer-herding families of Kautokeino:** check whether revival words fit both, in Sámi-run media.
- **South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros:** check language-revival work at Saemien Sijte and in schools.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check revitalization as each community's own bodies describe it.
- **Kristiansund and Molde against Finnmark:** check rebuilt-town narratives from bombed towns and from a burned county.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Catholics; Eritrean Orthodox and Pentecostal congregations:** check adult baptism and confirmation customs in their parish material.
- **Vietnamese community:** check its own memoir collections on a new life after the sea crossing.
- **Former prisoners and people in recovery:** check peer organizations' own publications, not only staff accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revivals, rebuilding and language movements; mixed for recovery; thin for Forest Finn revival and for leaving closed congregations.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Lammers and Ibsen; Old Norse for saga usage; North and South Sámi; Kven and Finnish for Laestadian sources; Polish and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi and Kven revival follows Norwegianization (*fornorskning*): give each account as an account. Exit programs and prison stories involve living people: no names. *Firehundreårsnatten* is a partisan phrase.
- **Held for review:** former members' accounts of closed congregations were weighed for the leaving-a-faith facet and left out until published memoirs are found, so no community is named only through critics.
