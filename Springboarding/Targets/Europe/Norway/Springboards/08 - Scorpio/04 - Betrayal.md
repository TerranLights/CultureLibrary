# Norway: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Trøndersk for Trondheim's wartime record; Old Norse; German for occupation files; North and South Sámi; Kven. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trondheim and Trøndelag against Oslo:** check how the Rinnan informers and the capital's collaborators are each remembered.
- **Families of the convicted against families of the resistance:** check memoirs from both sides of the settlement, in each family's words.
- **War children and their mothers:** check their own association's accounts of being let down by the state.
- **Jewish community:** check its institutions' account of the deportation against official histories.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check how each recalls loyalty questions in the interwar years.
- **North Sámi of Finnmark, South Sámi of Trøndelag:** check truth-commission testimony on broken trust, by area.
- **Laestadian congregations and *bedehus* communities:** check sermon and hymn texts on Judas and on faithfulness in marriage.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check their associations' and unions' accounts of promises broken by agencies and employers.
- **Somali, Eritrean and Iraqi communities:** check how each community's own media and poetry word a friend's or partner's broken faith.
- **Longyearbyen and Barentsburg:** check how each town's own records describe the other during the Cold War.
