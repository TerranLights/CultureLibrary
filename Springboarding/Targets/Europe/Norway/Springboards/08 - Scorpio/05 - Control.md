# Norway: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for 18th-century decrees; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Lithuanian where apt. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sørlandet and Rogaland mission-house districts against Oslo:** check the rules each sets on conduct, in their own papers and in local press.
- **Counties that voted dry against wet in 1926:** check temperance lodges and local debates.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing communities:** check how fishermen describe supervisors and quotas.
- **Gudbrandsdalen and Østerdalen:** check flood memory and the dikes built after.
- **Reindeer-herding families of Kautokeino and Karasjok against coastal Sámi:** check who decides over land and herds, in Sámi-run media.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Varanger:** check who has had a say over fjord fishing, in Kven accounts.
- **Longyearbyen residents against visitors:** check how rules for the wild are taught and kept.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check their unions' and associations' accounts of who controls hours and pay.
- **Somali, Syrian and Eritrean families:** check parents' own associations on raising children in Norway, in their words.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of control by officials over travel and schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public control (decrees, referendums, commissions); mixed for control in couples; thin for Kven and Rom accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for 1741 and 1789 sources; Old Norse; North Sámi for herders' media; Kven; Polish and Lithuanian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the government term *negativ sosial kontroll* is applied mostly to minority families in policy: record it as a policy term, never as a description of a community. Herd reductions are an open dispute. Self-control as discipline belongs to Capricorn; autonomy to Aries.
- **Held for review:** commercial tracking apps for children and partners were weighed for the technology facet and left out as marketing, pending a neutral usage survey.
