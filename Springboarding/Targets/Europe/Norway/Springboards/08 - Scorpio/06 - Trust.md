# Norway: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Northern Norwegian for the old fish trade; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing families against Bergen's merchant houses:** check old credit ties in both sets of records.
- **Rural Østlandet and Gudbrandsdalen against central Oslo:** check honesty-box sales and locked doors in local press.
- **Oslo and Hole after 22 July:** check trust measures before and after, by district.
- **North Sámi of Karasjok and Kautokeino, coastal Sámi, South Sámi:** check trust in health and school services as SAMINOR and Sámi media report it.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check trust toward the state in each community's own testimony; print is thin.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check trust in schools and health services in each community's own media and surveys by background.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check trust in employers and unions in their own associations.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check whether each people's own organizations describe dealings with schools and welfare offices in terms of trust, and in what words.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check how trust in God and in neighbors is preached in their papers.
- **Svalbard:** check trust between short-term residents and long-term ones in Longyearbyen's local paper, *Svalbardposten*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys of trust; mixed for everyday trust in deals and credit; thin for Kven, Rom and Forest Finn accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Ibsen; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust figures by background are easy to misread as traits; give them as answers to a survey. Trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn. The 2009 vaccine case involves injured people.
- **Held for review:** commercial brand-trust rankings were weighed for the business facet and left out as marketing research, pending an independent source.
