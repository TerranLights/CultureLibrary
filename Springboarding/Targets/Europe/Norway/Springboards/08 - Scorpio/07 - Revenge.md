# Norway: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; German for occupation reprisals; North Sámi; Kven; Kurdish, Somali and Arabic where apt. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Telemark, Setesdal and Gudbrandsdalen against the coast:** check feud and knife-fight lore in each folklore collection.
- **Telavåg and Sotra against Oslo:** check how a reprisal village and the capital remember 1942-45.
- **Families of NS members and families of the resistance:** check memoirs on 1945's street reprisals, from both sides.
- **North Sámi of the Finnmark coast and inland:** check which raider legends each area tells, and their endings.
- **Kvens of Varanger and Porsanger:** check whether tale collections record revenge as a theme; sources are scarce.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check sermons on forgiveness and grudges in their own papers.
- **Oslo after 22 July, and AUF members:** check how survivors spoke of revenge and forgiveness in their own books.
- **Kurdish, Somali and Arabic-speaking communities:** check each community's own poetry, sermons and media on forgiving and on letting a wrong go.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own stories for how wrongs between families are settled.
- **Schoolchildren and teenagers:** check Ungdata and school surveys for retaliation in bullying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the occupation and 22 July; mixed for folk feud custom; thin for Sámi and Kven words for revenge.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse; German for reprisal orders; Danish-Norwegian for 19th-century folk records; North Sámi; Kven; Kurdish, Somali and Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reprisals of 1945, the mine-clearing prisoners and the settlement are contested ground: present each account as an account. 22 July has living survivors. Revenge as crime belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** news coverage of revenge killings was weighed for the violence facet and left out where it would tie a named community to crime; use homicide counts by circumstance instead.
