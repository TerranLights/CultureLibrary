# Norway: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for trial records; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Russian for Barentsburg; Somali, Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and the central east against Finnmark, Troms and Nordland:** check center-periphery language in election coverage and local papers.
- **Bergen and Vestlandet:** check how the old western capital's memory is used in today's regional politics.
- **Trøndelag:** check the merged county's own account of power shared between Trondheim and the districts.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok against coastal Sámi:** check who holds voice in the Sámi Parliament and in herding districts.
- **South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros; Lule Sámi of Tysfjord:** check their place in Sámi politics, in their own media.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check representation in councils of Porsanger, Vadsø, Grue and Åsnes.
- **Fishing communities of Lofoten and Finnmark against fish buyers and quota holders:** check who decides over catch and sales.
- **Somali, Polish, Iraqi, Eritrean and Vietnamese communities:** check their councillors' and associations' own accounts of local influence.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* communities:** check their weight in local councils and church elections.
- **Barentsburg and Longyearbyen:** check how each town's residents describe who decides their lives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for power studies, elections and the oil fund; mixed for local networks; thin for Kven and Forest Finn representation.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for the 1692 trial; Old Norse; North, South and Lule Sámi; Kven; Russian; Somali, Polish and Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Poulsen drum and its symbols: follow Sámi museum guidance on what is shown. The oil fund and county mergers are disputed: give each side's account. Authority and legitimate power belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** rich lists were weighed for economic power and left out here, as they rank wealth rather than show how power works.
