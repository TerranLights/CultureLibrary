# Norway: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sørlandet's coast against the inland valleys:** check the blockade hunger in local histories of Grimstad and of Gudbrandsdalen farms.
- **Sunnmøre and Sogn fjord villages:** check how slide-threatened communities record their choices.
- **Agder, Hedmark and Telemark farm districts:** check where forced auctions were blocked in the 1930s, in local papers.
- **Rogaland's oil towns against Oslo:** check downturn accounts in each.
- **Reindeer-herding families of Finnmark and Troms against coastal Sámi:** check crisis words and help received, in Sámi media.
- **South Sámi herders of Trøndelag:** check winter crises in their own reports, apart from northern herding.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check whether hungry years are kept in family memory; sources are scarce.
- **Barentsburg's Russian and Ukrainian residents and Longyearbyen:** check how each town remembers 1996.
- **Syrian, Eritrean, Somali and Ukrainian arrivals:** check how their own associations tell their first year in Norway.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* congregations:** check sermons that read crises as a warning or a test.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, weather and economic downturns; mixed for personal crisis; thin for Kven and Forest Finn memory.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian to read clergy reports of the 1742 dying and blockade-era letters; Old Norse for famine annals; North and South Sámi for herders' own accounts of bad winters; Kven for hunger memories; Russian and Ukrainian for the 1996 crash; Arabic and Somali for arrival stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s farm movement's ties to NS are contested: give each account. "Crisis" applied to arrivals is a frame: record who uses it. The 1996 crash and slide disasters have living kin. Fear belongs to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** preparedness-shop catalogs were weighed for the crisis-goods facet and left out as advertising.
