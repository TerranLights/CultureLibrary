# Norway: Belief and philosophy, the domain and folkways (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Romani and Romanes; Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of belief and philosophy: what is held true and where meaning is sought, with religion, philosophy, higher learning and long journeys, and the religious and freedom ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Østlandet against Agder and Rogaland:** check affiliation and attendance by municipality before using the "Bible belt" label.
- **Vestlandet fjord parishes (Hardanger, Sogn, Sunnmøre):** check *bedehus* minute books against parish records.
- **Trøndelag:** check how the Olav cult and the pilgrim revival appear in Trondheim's civic calendar.
- **North, Lule and South Sámi; coastal Sámi:** check each group's own church and pre-Christian accounts.
- **Laestadians of Nord-Troms and Finnmark; Kvens:** check where the movement's meetings and Kven speech overlap.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check local museum holdings for old belief and charms; print is thin.
- **Jewish community; Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish and Syrian Muslims; Eritrean Orthodox; Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese Catholics:** check each community's own yearbooks and websites.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check what each people has published on the revival preachers and mission societies that sought them out.
- **Human-Etisk Forbund members; Svalbard residents:** check how a secular stance, and a short-stay town, organize belief.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church statistics and mission archives; mixed for philosophy outside the universities; thin for Forest Finn, Kven and Rom belief.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse and Danish-Norwegian for the old print; Telemark dialect for the ballad; the three Sámi languages for church texts; Kven; Tigrinya and Somali for community pages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi sacred sites and drums carry restrictions set by Sámi institutions; mission records on Sámi religion are hostile witnesses. Church-state law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** popular "Viking faith" revival sites were weighed as a pointer to old belief and left out until a scholarly account of the modern Norse-faith groups is found.
