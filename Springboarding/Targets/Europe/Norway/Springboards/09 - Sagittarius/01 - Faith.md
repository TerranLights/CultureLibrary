# Norway: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya, Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Agder, Rogaland and the Vestlandet coast against Oslo:** check revival language and prayer-house life in surveys, by municipality.
- **Trøndelag and Helgeland:** check where the *leser* healing tradition is reported and who seeks it.
- **North Sámi of inner Finnmark and Sámi Laestadians:** check how faith is spoken of at summer meetings and in Sámi church services.
- **Skolt Sámi of Neiden:** check the Orthodox calendar and the community's own museum material.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Nordreisa:** check hymn and prayer language in Kven-speaking homes.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check local museums for old prayers and charms, sources being scarce.
- **Catholics (Polish, Lithuanian, Vietnamese, Filipino) and Eritrean Orthodox:** check each parish's own records on worship and religious instruction.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish and Syrian Muslims:** check each mosque's teaching material on trust in God.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own testimony of faith in Pentecostal and Catholic settings.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how the church serves a town where few stay long.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Lutheran, revival and literary sources; mixed for newer congregations; thin for Skolt Sámi, Kven and Forest Finn faith.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for the catechism and hymn print; North Sámi Bible language; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Tigrinya for sermons; Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese for parish material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing by prayer touches health claims; let practitioners speak for themselves. Grief after 22 July 2011 is personal; use published accounts only.
- **Held for review:** televised faith-healing campaigns were weighed for the healing facet and left out until a study of their audience is found.
