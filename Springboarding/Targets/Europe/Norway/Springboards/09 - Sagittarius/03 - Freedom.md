# Norway: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Romani; Tigrinya, Kurdish and Somali where apt. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet towns and Oslo against the Vestlandet fjords:** check how the 1814 and 8 May celebrations are run, and who speaks.
- **Telemark and Setesdal:** check whether the valley farmer's freedom is a theme in local history and song.
- **Agder and Rogaland *bedehus* circles:** check how religious freedom is invoked in their own press.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok; Lule Sámi; South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros:** check how each frames collective freedom in its own institutions.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger:** check the right to one's language as a freedom, in Kven publications.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check memory of the burn-clearing life as a free life, in local museum writing.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own records of travel restrictions and their aftermath.
- **Jewish community:** check how freedom is marked in its own calendar and memory work.
- **Eritrean, Kurdish, Syrian and Somali Norwegians:** check what freedom of speech and worship means in each community's own media.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how its residents of many nationalities speak of coming there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1814, occupation and press-freedom material; mixed for religious freedom's social history; thin for Kven and Forest Finn sources.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the thrall words; Danish-Norwegian for Wergeland; North and South Sámi; Kven; Tigrinya, Kurmanji and Sorani Kurdish, Somali for exile press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the occupation, Quisling and the deportation of 1942 are contested memory; present each account as an account. Constitutional and access law go to Libra and Capricorn.
