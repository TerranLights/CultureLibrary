# Norway: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Latin and Old Norse for the medieval histories; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo readers against Vestlandet and Northern Norway:** check which outlets each region trusts, from local-paper and NRK district figures.
- **Agder and Rogaland lay movements:** check how scripture's truth is set against science in their own schools and magazines.
- **North Sámi reindeer herders, coastal Sámi of the fjords, South Sámi of Trøndelag:** check how each group's knowledge is recorded and credited.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns of Troms and Finnmark; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check their testimony to the 2023 commission as a source on being believed.
- **Laestadians:** check preaching on what may be taught as true, in the movement's own papers.
- **Jewish community:** check its own accounts in the 1942 historiography debate.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Syrian, Polish and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check which language media each relied on in 2020-2021, from their own outlets.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check how each people's account of its past is now recorded and received.
- **Longyearbyen residents and researchers:** check the island's science community and its local paper as rival sources on Arctic change.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the commission, press ethics and trust surveys; mixed for rumor and legend; thin for Kven-language and Forest Finn records.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old Norse for the medieval histories; North Sámi for the commission's Sámi materials and the journal; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese for health information.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2023 commission, the 1942 deportation and Norwegianization are contested ground; give each account as an account. Some testimony was given in confidence. Witness oaths and courts go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** popular conspiracy-theory websites were weighed for the margins facet and left out until a peer-reviewed Norwegian study of them is identified.
