# Norway: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with coastal dialect forms; Danish-Norwegian; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Polish and Tigrinya where apt. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Telemark, Setesdal and Gudbrandsdalen:** check how long belief in the hidden people and land spirits was recorded in each valley's folklore volumes.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Sunnmøre fishing families:** check sea taboos and omens across generations, in dialect studies.
- **Oslo against Agder:** check belief in God and the afterlife, not only membership, by county in survey data.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi of the Lyngen and Porsanger fjords; South Sámi:** check how each speaks of the old beliefs in its own media and museums.
- **Kvens of Varanger and Porsanger:** check whether Kven folk belief was ever recorded apart from Sámi and Norwegian; start with the Kven institute and local museums.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check charms and healing words in local collections, read apart from neighbors' tales about them.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check confessions of faith and what each requires its preachers to hold.
- **Muslims (Somali, Iraqi, Syrian, Kurdish), Catholics (Polish, Lithuanian, Vietnamese) and Eritrean Orthodox:** check what each community teaches its young as creed.
- **Humanists and the unaffiliated:** check how belief is described by those who list none.
- **Svalbard:** check trappers' and miners' luck customs in Longyearbyen's local history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore archives and survey belief items; mixed for newer communities; thin for Kven and Forest Finn belief.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for mission and folklore print; coastal dialect for sea words; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Polish and Tigrinya for teaching material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi pre-Christian belief was recorded by its suppressors; give Sámi institutions' accounts first. The angel-school debate concerns a living person; use published press only.
- **Held for review:** commercial clairvoyant and psychic services were weighed for the margins facet and left to Pisces's magic ways, where they fit better.
