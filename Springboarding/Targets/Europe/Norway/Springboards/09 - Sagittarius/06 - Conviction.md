*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet and Agder Christian voters against Oslo's secular left:** check how each speaks of principle and compromise in party and church press.
- **Trøndelag:** check how the archbishop's stand of 1537 is retold locally.
- **Telemark and Østlandet industrial towns:** check union memory of strikes as stands on principle.
- **North Sámi activists of Karasjok and Kautokeino; South Sámi reindeer herders of Fosen; coastal Sámi:** check each group's own statements on refusing land use they oppose.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger:** check Kven organizations' writing on holding to a language under pressure.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check local history for refusals of Norwegianization; sources are scarce.
- **Quakers, Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check each one's rules on oaths, arms and worldly compromise.
- **Muslims (Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian, Somali), Catholics (Polish, Lithuanian) and the Jewish community:** check each community's own debate pages on conscience in public life.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check organizations' accounts of standing up for their people.
- **Svalbard:** check how conviction plays in a town of temporary residents, in *Svalbardposten* debates.
