# Norway: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old English and Old Norse for the earliest voyage account; North Sámi; Kven; Russian for the Pomor trade; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Agder and Rogaland emigrant parishes against inland Østlandet:** check the emigrant registers for who left and who came back.
- **Fishing families of Nordland, Lofoten and Vesterålen:** check the winter fishery journey across generations in museum interviews.
- **Gudbrandsdalen, Telemark and the mountain valleys:** check the Easter mountain trip and summer-farm moves as seasonal travel.
- **North Sámi herding families of Kautokeino and Karasjok; South Sámi of Trøndelag and Nordland:** check how each describes its routes and the obstacles on them.
- **Coastal Sámi and Kvens of the Varanger and Porsanger fjords:** check their accounts of the Pomor trade and border crossings.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check memory of the 1600s move from Savo, read in local sources only.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of the travel routes and their closing.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers; Somali, Eritrean, Vietnamese and Syrian families:** check trips home and the journey to Norway in each community's own memoirs and media.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check how they speak of tourism's growth in *Svalbardposten* debates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emigration, shipping and tourism statistics; mixed for seasonal labor; thin for Kven and Forest Finn migration records.
- **Languages needed:** Old English and Old Norse for Ottar; Nynorsk for Vinje; North and South Sámi; Kven; Russian for Pomor sources; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Vietnamese for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reindeer routes are disputed land; give herders' own accounts. Asylum journeys are recent and personal; use published accounts. Border and residence rules go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** commercial travel-review sites were weighed for the tourism facet and left out as unrepresentative samples.
