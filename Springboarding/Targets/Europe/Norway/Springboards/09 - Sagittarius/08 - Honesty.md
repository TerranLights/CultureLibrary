## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergen and Trondheim's old trading towns against inland farming valleys:** check how fair dealing is described in merchant and farm histories.
- **Northern Norway and Trøndelag storytellers:** check where tall tales are told as a craft, and how they are told apart from lying.
- **Oslo against Agder and Rogaland:** check whether attitudes to tax and benefit honesty differ in survey data.
- **Fishing families of Lofoten and the Finnmark coast:** check catch-sale and weighing customs in local histories, as fishers themselves describe them.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi; South Sámi:** check proverbs and teaching on keeping one's word, in each group's own publications.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check whether any sayings on honesty were recorded; sources are scarce, start with the Kven institute and Finnskogen museums.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check confession and restitution practice in their own writings.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Arabic-speaking and Vietnamese parents:** check how honesty is taught at home, in each community's own parenting material.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own writing on being trusted and distrusted by officials.
- **Svalbard:** check the honor rules of emergency huts, in residents' and trappers' accounts.
