## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo, Bergen and Trondheim against Finnmark and inland Hedmark:** check attainment and distance to a campus as reasons people study or stay.
- **Vestlandet folk-high-school and Nynorsk milieus:** check the *danning* ideal against exam-driven study in their own histories.
- **Agder and Rogaland lay-movement families:** check the choice between faith-based colleges and state universities, in school yearbooks and magazines.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok; Lule Sámi of Tysfjord; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check each group's access to schooling in its own language.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns:** check the history of Kven teaching and the recent university courses.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check schooling's role in language loss, in local museum material.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and Rom:** check each people's own accounts of schooling and of recent education initiatives.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Polish, Vietnamese, Iraqi and Syrian families:** check student associations and parents' groups for what each hopes higher education brings.
- **Fishing and farming families of the coast and valleys:** check the trades against university study in local papers.
- **Svalbard:** check how students and staff at UNIS describe study there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, university histories and policy; mixed for the standing of the educated in everyday talk; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Rom education history.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Danish-Norwegian for early school records; Nynorsk; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya and Polish for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** schooling was a main tool of Norwegianization; boarding schools for Sámi and Kven children are part of the 2023 commission's record, so give former pupils' accounts first. Education law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** university league tables were weighed for the prestige facet and left out; their methods measure research output more than standing.
