# Norway: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for the trial records; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Agder, Rogaland and Sunnmøre prayer-house districts against Oslo congregations:** check doctrinal statements and attendance, not reputations, before calling any district strict.
- **Vestlandet and Østlandet mission societies:** check how each phrases its view of Scripture, from their own constitutions.
- **Laestadians in Nord-Troms, Alta and Finnmark:** check the movement's branches and their own accounts of rules and leaving.
- **North Sámi and Kven Laestadians:** check Sámi- and Kven-language preaching records, separately from Norwegian-language ones.
- **Pentecostal congregations and independent charismatic churches:** check their own teaching on Scripture and prophecy.
- **Catholics (Polish, Lithuanian, Vietnamese, Filipino):** check how traditional and modern liturgy is debated in parish media.
- **Muslim congregations (Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian):** check each congregation's own teaching on reading scripture, and the scholarly debate as an account.
- **Jewish community:** check the place of orthodox practice in a small congregation, in its own publications.
- **Humanists:** check how Human-Etisk Forbund uses "fundamentalism" as a charge, as part of the debate.
- **Svalbard:** check whether any strict fellowship has met there, in the local church's history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church-press debates and lay-movement history; mixed for strict fellowships' internal life; thin for Kven-language preaching and newer communities' own writing on literalism.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for trial records; Nynorsk for western church press; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic and Polish for community debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the word is an insult in most mouths; never apply it to a group, only report who uses it. Strict fellowships and leavers dispute each other's accounts; read both as accounts. The witchcraft trials involved Sámi victims; give Sámi accounts first. Registration and grants go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** anti-cult websites were weighed for the leavers' facet and left out; they are advocacy, not evidence.
