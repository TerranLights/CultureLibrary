# Norway: Authority and duty, the domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål, Nynorsk and dialects; Old Norse and Danish-Norwegian; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of authority and duty: who commands, who obeys and what is owed, with career, status and reputation, and the age, time, rank, order and power ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet's old official towns (Christiania, Kongsberg) against the freeholding valleys of Gudbrandsdalen and Vestlandet:** check whether office and farm standing ran on separate ladders.
- **Fisher-farmers of Lofoten and Vesterålen:** check who set the rules at sea and how skippers and crews split command.
- **North Sámi herding *siida* of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi; South Sámi of Snåsa and Røros:** check each one's own account of decision making, apart from state reindeer rules.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns; Laestadians:** check sermons and Kven press on duty to God and state.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check local museum and farm records; print on their leaders is scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** two peoples, two record sets: check each one's memoirs on which officials they had to answer to.
- **Jewish community; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check each one's own media on duties to elders and employers.
- **Svalbard:** check how authority runs where few stay long.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the state, elites and labor; mixed for farm and fishing customs (deep archives, little synthesis); thin for Forest Finn, Kven and Rom rank.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the levy; Danish-Norwegian for the 1821 debates; Nynorsk for valley deeds; North and South Sámi; Kven; Somali and Tigrinya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization and the state's treatment of the Romani people and the Rom are contested: give each account as an account. Herding disputes may be live in court; cite rulings only. Statutes as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the Lofoten fishery rules were weighed for time and order ways and left to the Order sheet.
