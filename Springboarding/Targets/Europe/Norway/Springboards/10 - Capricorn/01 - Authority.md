# Norway: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Sorani Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right (law-related facets by their spirit: what authority is for and when obedience ends).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Østlandet against Agder and Rogaland's prayer-house parishes:** check whether church and lay leaders' authority is weighed apart in each.
- **Vestlandet (Hardanger, Sunnmøre):** check the lay preachers' tradition of standing up to clergy, in parish histories.
- **Finnmark (Kautokeino, Karasjok, the coast):** check Sámi and Norwegian accounts of FeFo and the state side by side.
- **South Sámi (Snåsa, Røros) and Fosen herders:** check their own statements on obeying a ruling the state was slow to carry out.
- **Kvens and Laestadians of Nord-Troms; Forest Finns:** check each group's own record of officials; Forest Finn sources are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each people's own accounts of mission and welfare officials, kept apart.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own record of 1942, beside the church's.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check each community's own media on whom they were told to answer to on arrival.
- **Svalbard:** check how Longyearbyen residents speak of the Governor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church, labor and state; mixed for the home and the workplace; thin for Forest Finn and Kven views of officials.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for the catechism; Old Norse for the Land Law; North Sámi for Alta and FeFo voices; Kven for sermons; Polish and Lithuanian for worker forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Alta, Fosen, the occupation and child welfare are contested: give accounts as accounts and keep families' names out. The letter of the statutes and judgments goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the permission share and correctional-control figures were weighed here and left for Stage 2, where they are measured from the law in force.
