# Norway: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Vietnamese and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to (law-related facets by their spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gudbrandsdalen and Østerdalen against Vestlandet:** check whether *legd* and cotter duties left different records.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing families:** check how the war sailors' duty is remembered in coastal towns that lost many men.
- **North Sámi families of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check each group's own account of who cares for the old and who takes up the herd.
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns of Vadsø and Porsanger:** check Kven family histories on duties to parents.
- **Forest Finns:** check Finnskogen local histories for duties to kin; sources are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each people's own writing on family obligations.
- **Laestadians of Nord-Troms; *bedehus* members of Agder:** check what preachers say is owed to God first and to the state second, or the reverse.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Polish, Lithuanian, Iraqi and Vietnamese families:** check each community's own media on caring for parents in the new country.
- **Svalbard:** check what short-stay residents are asked to owe Longyearbyen.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military service and survey items; mixed for family duty in the newer communities; thin for Forest Finn and Kven family duty.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the dependents' chapter; Danish-Norwegian for cotter contracts; North Sámi for care studies; Kven; Arabic and Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the war sailors' recognition and the conscription of women are contested; give each account as an account. The letter of the duty-to-rescue rule goes to Libra.
