## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo against Trøndelag and Northern Norway:** check whether *egenansvar* talk in health and welfare debates splits by place.
- **Vestlandet and Sørlandet fishing and farming families:** check who answers for the boat or the farm when the elder steps back.
- **North Sámi herding districts of Finnmark; South Sámi districts of Trøndelag:** check each district's own use rules and minutes on shared grazing.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Porsanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check who each group holds answerable for language loss; Forest Finn sources are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each people's own statements on who should answer for past state policy.
- **Laestadians:** check how confession in the meeting places responsibility on the one who confesses.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian, Iraqi, Polish and Lithuanian families:** check each community's own discussion of children who interpret for parents (*barn som tolk*) and the 2022 rule against it.
- **Svalbard:** check who answers for safety in the field outside Longyearbyen, in the Governor's rules and residents' own accounts.
