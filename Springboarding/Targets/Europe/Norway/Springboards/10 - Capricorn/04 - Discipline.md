# Norway: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others (parents, teachers, armies, employers, churches, the state) or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo against Sørlandet and Rogaland:** check whether parenting talk in prayer-house parishes keeps older discipline words longer.
- **Vestlandet and Trøndelag fishing and farm households:** check memoirs of work discipline set by weather and season.
- **North Sámi families of Karasjok and Kautokeino; coastal Sámi; Lule Sámi of Tysfjord:** check Sámi accounts of child-rearing, and of boarding schools, from their own writers.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check family memoirs and the 2023 commission's chapters on schooling; Forest Finn sources are few.
- **Romani people (*tatere*) and the Rom:** check each people's own testimony on children's homes and schools.
- **Laestadians:** check members' and former members' own accounts of the rules they live by.
- **Muslim, Eritrean Orthodox, Polish Catholic and Vietnamese families:** check each community's own parent programs on fasting, prayer and study habits.
- **Svalbard:** check how the school in Longyearbyen keeps routine through months of polar night.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schools, prisons and sport; mixed for the home; thin for Kven and Forest Finn upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the fasting rules; Danish-Norwegian for nineteenth-century school print; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Arabic and Somali for parent programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's homes, Bastøy and boarding schools under Norwegianization involve survivors; their accounts come first and names stay out. Physical punishment as written in the law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** online self-discipline influencers aimed at young men were weighed and left out, since no Norwegian-language study of that audience is known to this scout.
