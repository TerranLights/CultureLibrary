# Norway: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Stavanger against Telemark and Gudbrandsdalen:** check whether talk of careers and firms differs between the oil and finance cities and the inland valleys.
- **Bergen's merchant families and Sunnmøre's fishing and furniture firms:** check firm histories for how founders describe their own drive.
- **Northern Norway (Lofoten, Tromsø) and Finnmark:** check youth surveys on whether getting ahead means leaving home.
- **North Sámi herding families; coastal Sámi; South Sámi:** check who takes over the herd or the boat, and how Sámi youth describe their choices.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Vadsø; Forest Finns:** check family histories for moves away for schooling; sources are thin.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** two peoples to keep apart: check what each one's authors and spokespeople say about schooling and trades.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese families:** check each community's own student and parent associations on hopes for children.
- **Svalbard:** check why people come for a few years, from their own accounts in *Svalbardposten*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for register mobility, admissions and youth surveys; mixed for founders' own words; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Rom aspiration.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *frami*; Danish-Norwegian for Ibsen's original; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic and Vietnamese for family sources; Polish and Lithuanian for forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** studies by country background can harden into stereotype; report variation within each group and the group's own words. The oil fund's wealth is contested ground; keep it to accounts.
- **Held for review:** scholarship by South Asian-heritage scholars abroad, as a category only. Writing on the Jante idea was weighed and left to the sheets that already open it.
