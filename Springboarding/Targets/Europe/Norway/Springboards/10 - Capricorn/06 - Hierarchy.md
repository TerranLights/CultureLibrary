# Norway: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's west and east districts:** check how residents of each name the other, in local papers and district plans.
- **Bergen's old merchant families and Vestlandet farm valleys (Sogn, Hardanger):** check whether rank was reckoned by trade or by land.
- **Gudbrandsdalen and Trøndelag farm parishes:** check the ladder from *storbonde* to cotter in bygdebøker.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen crews; Finnmark's coast:** check fishermen's own accounts of the station owners.
- **North Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi; South Sámi:** check each group's own description of rank within families and *siida*.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check how each was placed in the national order, from their own associations; Forest Finn sources are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** check how each people's spokespeople describe where parish and city records placed them.
- **Newer and older communities (Jewish, Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian, Vietnamese):** check each one's own press on titles and forms of address carried over from the homeland.
- **Svalbard:** check former miners' memoirs on the old company town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for class schemes, pay and Oslo districts; mixed for workplace rank; thin for Kven and Forest Finn placement.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the compensation tables; German for Schjelderup-Ebbe; Danish-Norwegian for *kondisjonerte*; North Sámi; Kven; Polish and Lithuanian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the ranking of minorities under Norwegianization is contested; give each account as an account and do not rank peoples. Herders' internal ranking can be private family matter; use published testimony only.
- **Held for review:** royal precedence was weighed and left out here, since the domain sheet opens rank ways at court and nothing more specific was found for this concept.
