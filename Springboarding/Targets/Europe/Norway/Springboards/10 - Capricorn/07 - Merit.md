# Norway: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Telemark dialect for the ballad; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Bergen professionals against Trøndelag and Hedmark farm families:** check whether success is credited to skill, family or luck in each.
- **Coastal crews of Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark:** check how fishers who lack quota speak of catch-history rules.
- **Telemark and Setesdal:** check how ballad and fiddle traditions judge a singer or player's worth.
- **North Sámi herding families of Kautokeino and Karasjok; South Sámi of Røros:** check whose skill counts, the school's or the elders'.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check Kven and Finnskogen school histories for how pupils were graded under Norwegianization; Forest Finn records are scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** check each people's own accounts of trades and skills that schools did not count.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese newcomers:** check each community's own forums on getting foreign degrees and trade papers counted.
- **Laestadian meetings in Nord-Troms; prayer-house parishes of Agder:** check whether sermons ever praise earthly achievement, given the grace teaching.
- **Svalbard:** check how jobs on the islands are filled, by advertisement or by network.
