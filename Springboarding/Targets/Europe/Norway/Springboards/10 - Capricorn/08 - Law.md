# Norway: Law (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali, Arabic and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community, and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey it, use it, bend it or avoid it (its spirit here; the letter of statutes and judgments is in Libra).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Trøndelag (Frosta, Fosen) against Oslo:** check press from 2021-2024 on a ruling the state was slow to carry out, herders' and the state's accounts apart.
- **Vestlandet fjord parishes and Gudbrandsdalen:** check local histories for disputes settled by neighbors and *forliksråd* rather than courts.
- **North Sámi herders of Finnmark; South Sámi of Fosen and Snåsa; coastal Sámi:** check each group's own account of custom that works as law.
- **Kvens of Nord-Troms and Varanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check their submissions on laws that bore on language and land; Forest Finn records are thin.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** check each people's own account of laws used against them, in their own organizations' words.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own record of law under occupation.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Vietnamese communities:** check each community's own guides on Norwegian rules, and what they say was new to them.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check preaching on God's law against the state's.
- **Svalbard:** check which mainland rules residents say do not reach the islands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for penal thought, courts and survey items; mixed for everyday bending; thin for Kven and Forest Finn legal custom.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the Frostating text; North and South Sámi for custom; Kven; Polish and Somali for newcomer guides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Vagrancy Act, Fosen and the occupation courts are contested; give each account as an account. Some Sámi customary knowledge may be shared only by agreement; ask before citing. The letter of all statutes and rulings goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the postwar treason trials were weighed for law's purpose and left out, since other sheets open them from the purge side and their legal letter is Libra's.
