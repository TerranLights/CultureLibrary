# Norway: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with the dialects; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self (public order's legal side by its spirit).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's inner east against Bærum and Asker:** check co-op rules and noise complaints side by side.
- **Bergen and Trondheim's old wooden quarters:** check fire-safety rules and their part in town order.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishing villages:** check fishers' own memories of the start signal and the overseers.
- **North Sámi herders of Kautokeino and Karasjok; coastal Sámi; South Sámi of Snåsa:** check each group's own terms for a well-ordered year.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check village histories on order kept by neighbors; Forest Finn material is scarce.
- **Romani people (*tatere*); the Rom:** check how each people describes encounters with public-order rules, in their own media.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Vietnamese residents:** check each community's own advice on co-op rules and rubbish sorting.
- **Laestadians and Agder's prayer-house parishes:** check views of *russefeiring* in their own papers.
- **Svalbard:** check residents' views of the Governor's rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for town plans, by-laws and survey items; mixed for home order; thin for Kven and Forest Finn customs of order.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the peace section; Danish-Norwegian for the fishery laws; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic and Somali for newcomer guides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sámi drums are sacred objects; follow Saemien Sijte's terms. Local begging bans and encampment rules are contested; give each side's account. Police law as written goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** riot and protest records (Blitz, the rose marches) were weighed for disorder and left to the sheets that already open them; reuse only for a question about how order was restored.
