# Norway: Community and ideals, the domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål, Nynorsk and the dialects; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups and what they hope for, from associations, causes and humanitarian ideals to the social ways of settlement, hospitality and neighboring.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's east side and the Østlandet towns against Vestlandet's fjord parishes:** check which kinds of club lead in each.
- **Sørlandet's *bedehus* districts and Church of Norway parishes:** check how each receives a newcomer.
- **Trøndelag and Gudbrandsdalen farm valleys:** check *bygdebøker* for work parties and neighbor circles before and after consolidation.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Tromsø:** check seasonal crews and fish-village life against town associations.
- **Coastal Sámi, reindeer-herding Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, South Sámi:** check each one's organizations and gatherings (Riddu Riđđu in Kåfjord).
- **Kvens and Norwegian Finns; Forest Finns of Finnskogen; Laestadians:** check each group's clubs and prayer meetings; Forest Finn sources start at the Finnskogen museums.
- **Jewish congregations of Oslo and Trondheim:** check each congregation's own history.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check the two peoples' organizations separately.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Iraqi, Syrian, Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese communities; Catholics; Muslims:** check each community's associations and own media.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how clubs and the local council deal with members who come and go.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registered associations, volunteering and aid; mixed for neighbor customs (deep in *bygdebøker*, seldom compared); thin for Forest Finn, Kven and Rom group life.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk for valley histories; Old Norse for guilds; Danish-Norwegian for early movement papers; North and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali and Tigrinya for association pages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Norwegianization (*fornorskning*), the 2023 Truth and Reconciliation report and the policies toward the tatere are disputed ground; present each version as its tellers give it. Statutes on associations go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the World Values Survey's world-citizen items were weighed and left to the Idealism and Equality sheets, where they bear more directly.
