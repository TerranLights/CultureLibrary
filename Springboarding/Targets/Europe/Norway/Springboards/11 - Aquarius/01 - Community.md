# Norway: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Trøndersk and northern dialect sources; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Polish, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *lokalsamfunn*; *bygd*, *bygdesamfunn* | local community; rural parish-settlement | *bygd* carries a rural sense the English word lacks; pair with a valley name |
| Bokmål / Nynorsk | *naboskap*, *nabolag* / *grannskap* | neighborliness; neighborhood | the Nynorsk form surfaces in Sunnmøre and Sogn local histories |
| Bokmål | *velforening*, *vel* | residents' improvement society (for example "X Vel") | a suburban and cabin-area form; search by place name plus "vel" |
| Bokmål | *borettslag*, *sameie* | housing cooperative; jointly owned building | annual meetings and house rules show who counts as a member |
| Nynorsk | *ungdomshus*, *grendehus* | youth-society hall; hamlet hall | the buildings where rural community life met; search the halls' jubilee booklets |
| Old Norse | *herað*, *heraðsmenn* | district; the district's people | *check*; read in sagas and charters as records of who gathered and stood together |
| North Sámi | *siida*, *gilli* | herding work group and its land; village | *check*; Finnmark reindeer-district papers use the first, coastal Sámi village histories the second, so the pair separates two kinds of community |
| Lule Sámi | *sijdda* | the Lule Sámi form of siida | *check*; search Tysfjord and Hamarøy (Divtasvuodna) material, where Lule Sámi and coastal settlement meet |
| Kven | *kylä* | village | *check* the Kven spelling; search Vadsø and Børselv histories, where a Kven village sat inside a Norwegian municipality |
| Polish / Somali / Vietnamese | *Polonia*; *jaaliyad*; *hội người Việt* | the Polish community abroad; community (from Arabic); Vietnamese association | *check*; how each community in Norway names itself |

English keywords: local community, residents' association, housing cooperative, village hall, depopulation, community after disaster.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's west-side villa streets and the Groruddalen high-rises:** check *vel* records against *borettslag* minutes.
- **Vestlandet fjord *bygder* (Sogn, Hardanger) and Østlandet's commuter belt:** check what local papers report as community events in each.
- **Røros and other Trøndelag mining towns:** check company and parish records for the work community.
- **Finnmark coastal towns rebuilt after the 1944 burning:** check rebuilding memoirs on whether old neighbor ties survived the move.
- **Karasjok herding siida; Sámi of the Ofoten and Lyngen fjords; Tysfjord's Lule Sámi; South Sámi:** check what each counts as its community, in its own institutions' words.
- **Vadsø's and Porsanger's Kvens; Grue and Åsnes Forest Finns:** check town and farm records; Forest Finn writing is sparse, so the heritage sites come first.
- **Laestadian prayer-house gatherings in Lyngen and Kåfjord:** check the members' own forms of address and meeting.
- **Jewish community in Trondheim:** check the congregation's own history of a small community's survival.
- **Tatere and the Rom:** check, in each people's organizations, how a scattered membership keeps in touch.
- **Polish workers in Vestlandet and Vietnamese families in Bergen and Oslo:** check parish and association pages for the community each describes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing cooperatives, rural halls and local history; mixed for newer communities (mostly association pages); thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Rom records.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk for hall histories; Old Norse for saga and charter terms; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Polish and Vietnamese for association media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1944 burning of Finnmark and the Gjerdrum and Longyearbyen deaths touch living families: cite reports and published memoirs, not private names. Removals of tatere children enter only through the people's own published testimony.
- **Held for review:** Utøya and 22 July as a community trauma were considered; they are covered in the Trust sheet and left there to avoid a repeat.
