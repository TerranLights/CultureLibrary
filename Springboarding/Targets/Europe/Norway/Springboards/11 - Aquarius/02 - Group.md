# Norway: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Østlandet and Trøndersk usage; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Kurdish and Tigrinya where apt. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's east side and Bergen's working districts against the Hedmark and Oppland farm *bygder*:** check union records against farmers' co-op records for how each acted together.
- **Trondheim, Bergen and Oslo football towns:** check supporters' songs and fanzines for the rivalries each keeps up.
- **Herding siida in Kautokeino; coastal Sámi of Porsanger; South Sámi in Røyrvik:** check how each decides as a group, in its own scholarship.
- **Kvens and Laestadians in Lyngen and Storfjord:** check prayer-house and Kven association records on joining and leaving.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check emigration and farm records; the group's own written voice is scant, so start with the local museums.
- **Tatere; Rom:** two peoples: check how each one's memoirs describe the traveling family band and its loyalties.
- **Jewish families in Oslo after 1945:** check the community's own histories of regrouping after the deportation.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish and Syrian youth and women's associations:** check their own reports on what the group offers members.
- **Polish and Lithuanian crews in construction and fish processing:** check union material in their languages.
- **Barentsburg residents and Longyearbyen residents:** check how each settlement's press describes the other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for unions, parties, sport and the war years; mixed for Sámi group decisions (well studied, sensitive in practice); thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Rom group life.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for the civil-war sagas; Nynorsk for valley sources; North and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Kurdish and Tigrinya for association pages; Russian for Barentsburg.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the treason settlement, the treatment of women and children tied to German soldiers, and Laestadian splits are raw for families: present each side's account as its own. Siida disputes may be in court: cite published rulings only; reindeer law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** youth gang reporting was weighed and set aside: its framing tends to pin groups on whole communities, and the community's own sources belong first.
