# Norway: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Danish-Norwegian for the nineteenth century; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's student and activist milieu against rural Vestlandet *ildsjeler*:** check what each praises as working for a cause.
- **Haugean and *bedehus* communities of Sørlandet and Rogaland:** check missionary and aid ideals in their own papers.
- **Hardanger and Sogn, where hydropower and nature fights met:** check local writing on Mardøla-style protests.
- **Sámi youth in Karasjok and Kautokeino; South Sámi youth in Snåsa:** check Noereh and school texts for the ideals they name.
- **Kvens of Vadsø and Nord-Troms:** check the revival movement's own statements of aims.
- **Forest Finns:** check Finnskogen associations' writing; scant, so treat it as an open question.
- **Tatere; Rom:** check, organization by organization, the hopes for recognition each people states, never merging the two.
- **Jewish community:** check how it remembers Wergeland, in its own publications.
- **Vietnamese, Polish, Somali and Eritrean associations:** check their aid and development work for home regions, in their own words.
- **Longyearbyen's researchers and seasonal residents:** check how they speak of Svalbard as a place for science and the world.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for aid, peace diplomacy and environmental idealism; mixed for local *ildsjel* culture (local press, little synthesis); thin for Kven and Forest Finn ideals in writing.
- **Languages needed:** Danish-Norwegian for Wergeland; Old Norse for *Völuspá*; North and South Sámi for youth texts; Kven; Arabic and Vietnamese for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Oslo process and peace diplomacy are politically disputed: give each account as its author's. The 22 July 2011 attack targeted a youth camp of idealists; cite published accounts only and respect survivors.
- **Held for review:** South Asian works, as a category only; one such pointer came up while weighing this concept and was kept off the sheet.
