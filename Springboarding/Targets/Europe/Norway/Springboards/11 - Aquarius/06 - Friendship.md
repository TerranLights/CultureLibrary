# Norway: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Oslo, Bergen and Northern Norway dialect usage; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Russenorsk (historical); Polish, Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's school districts and the villages of Telemark and Sogn:** check how friend groups form when the same class stays together for ten years.
- **Bergen, Stavanger and Trondheim student towns:** check student-society records for friendships made away from home.
- **Northern Norway's fishing villages (Lofoten, Vesterålen):** check crew memoirs for friendship at sea.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi and coastal partners in Finnmark:** check *verdde* ties as each family describes them; South Sámi in Røros: check youth sources.
- **Kvens of Porsanger; Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check oral histories; little is written down.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check memoirs for friendships kept inside and across each people's boundary.
- **Jewish families during the occupation:** check accounts of neighbors and friends who helped.
- **Vietnamese families from the boat-refugee years; Kurdish, Eritrean and Somali arrivals; Lithuanian and Polish workers:** check each community's own writing on friends inside and outside the group.
- **Laestadian and *bedehus* youth:** check how fellowship and friendship are spoken of.
- **Longyearbyen's short-stay residents:** check what the local paper reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and fiction; mixed for men's friendship (much comment, few studies); thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani friendship.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for *Egils saga*; Nynorsk and dialect dictionaries; North and South Sámi; Kven; Russenorsk for the Pomor trade; Polish, Somali and Tigrinya for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** newcomers' complaints about how slowly friendships form here are outsiders' claims: record each with its source, never as a finding. Wartime help between friends touches families still living; cite published accounts.
- **Held for review:** dating and friend-finding apps were weighed and left out: they belong with partnership in Libra.
