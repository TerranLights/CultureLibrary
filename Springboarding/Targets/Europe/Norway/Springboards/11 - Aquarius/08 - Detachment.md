# Norway: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Trøndersk and coastal speech; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes. Cultures value some forms of it (calm, objectivity, impartiality, equanimity) and condemn others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's one-person households and Vestlandet's farm households:** check living-alone rates against how local papers write about loneliness.
- **Trøndelag and Gudbrandsdalen speakers and Bergen speakers:** check whether dialect self-descriptions (calm, talkative) differ, as speakers themselves describe them.
- **Lofoten and Finnmark fishing crews:** check rescue and accident memoirs for composure at sea.
- **Sámi patients and pupils in inner Finnmark, and South Sámi families in Røros:** check Sámi-language health and school sources on silence in conversation.
- **Laestadians and Church of Norway congregations of Nord-Troms:** check worship descriptions for open emotion against restraint.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check oral histories; written sources on emotional style are few.
- **Tatere; Rom:** check, people by people, memoirs on keeping officials at arm's length.
- **Somali, Arab, Polish and Vietnamese Norwegians:** check what their newspapers and podcasts say about warmth and distance at work and next door.
- **Contemplative communities (Tautra, Munkeby) and secular retreat seekers:** check how each describes withdrawal.
- **Longyearbyen's wintering residents:** check accounts of the dark season.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics and codes of professional ethics; mixed for emotional style (much opinion, little fieldwork); Kven, Forest Finn and Romani emotional norms are barely written down.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse for saga composure; Nynorsk and dialect dictionaries for *likesæl*; North Sámi for clinical sources; Kven; Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "the reserved Norwegian" is an outsiders' stereotype: note it as a claim, with its source. The Kielland disaster's families still press their case; cite published testimony.
- **Held for review:** for detachment, a single pointer was set aside and is kept here as a category only (Buddhism), pending the author's ruling.
