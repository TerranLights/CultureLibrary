# Norway: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk, with Northern Norway and Vestlandet speech; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Tigrinya, Kurdish, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet's prayer-house districts and secular Oslo:** check how hope is spoken of at funerals and in hymns against civil ceremonies.
- **Finnmark and Nordland coastal towns:** check *framtidstro* debates when fish plants close or open.
- **Hardanger and Ryfylke pilgrims and hikers on the revived pilgrim paths:** check what walkers say they hope for.
- **North Sámi in Alta, Kautokeino and Masi; South Sámi:** check Sámi-language sources on hope after Alta.
- **Kvens of Porsanger and Nord-Troms:** check revival writing on hope for the language.
- **Forest Finns:** check Finnskogen museums for any hope tied to the old ways; written sources are scarce.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check, each people for itself, what recognition is hoped for, in the organizations' statements.
- **Laestadians:** check hope of grace in sermons, as members record them.
- **Eritrean, Somali, Kurdish and Syrian families; Polish and Vietnamese families:** check each community's media on hopes for children and for return.
- **Longyearbyen residents:** check the town's own debate on its future.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys of well-being, emigration and the war years; mixed for everyday hope customs (scattered); thin for Kven and Forest Finn sources.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk for *von* and the western press; Old Norse for saga words; North Sámi for Alta songs; Kven; Tigrinya, Somali and Arabic for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide research touches grieving families; cite reports, never cases. The Alta controversy is disputed ground: give each side's account as told.
- **Held for review:** the *Draumkvedet* vision was weighed for religious hope and left out, as earlier sheets lean on it.
