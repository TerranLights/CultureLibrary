# Norway: The hidden and transcendence, the domain and folkways (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with the dialects (Setesdal, Trøndersk, the northern coast); Old Norse; North, Lule and South Sámi; Kven; Forest Finnish for Finnskogen; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the domain of the hidden and of transcendence: how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape, from madness and confinement to spirits, dreams and folk magic.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Telemark and Setesdal against Østlandet:** check whether dense legend files reflect belief or only collecting.
- **Vestlandet's *bedehus* coast against Oslo:** check how suffering is preached in each.
- **Lofoten and Vesterålen fishers:** check sea-death legends against inland lore.
- **Coastal, reindeer-herding and South Sámi:** check each one's institutions for what may be shared.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check whether healers' records survive apart; expect sparse files.
- **Tatere, Rom and the Jewish community:** check each one's own accounts of confinement.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish, Syrian and Eritrean communities:** check their associations' own words for distress.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Catholics; Vietnamese families:** check each one's own accounts of remembering the dead.
- **Longyearbyen:** check whether short-stay workers and long-time residents speak of the dark season differently.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and institutions; mixed for current paranormal belief; thin for Kven and Forest Finn healing and for newer communities' spirit beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and dialect for legend files; Dano-Norwegian for asylum and trial records; North and South Sámi; Kven; Somali and Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi drums, *noaidi* knowledge and *sieidi* sites are held by Sámi communities; start with Saemien Sijte, RiddoDuottarMuseat and the Sámediggi, and expect parts to be closed. The *tatere* confinement history is contested; read each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** surveys of migrants' psychiatric care that sort patients by origin were weighed and left to the Fear and Compassion sheets, where the communities' own sources can stand beside them.
