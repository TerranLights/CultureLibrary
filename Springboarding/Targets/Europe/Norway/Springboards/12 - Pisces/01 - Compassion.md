# Norway: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian, Tigrinya and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo's east-side parishes and the city missions:** check whether help is spoken of as charity or as a right.
- **Vestlandet and Sørlandet *bedehus* congregations:** check mission bazaars and the words used for giving.
- **Trøndelag and Gudbrandsdalen farm parishes:** check *legd* records for which poor were kept and which were sent off.
- **Finnmark and Nord-Troms Laestadians:** check sermons for mercy toward sinners and to whom it is extended.
- **North and Lule Sámi families:** check Sámi-language sources for the words used at a sickbed.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's account of being the object of charity.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own reading of Wergeland's campaign.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Kurdish associations:** check their own fundraising for kin abroad and for neighbors here.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check Catholic parishes' charity work in their languages.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how help reaches people with no family on Svalbard.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charities and appeals; mixed for everyday pity; thin for Kven and Romani words of mercy.
- **Languages needed:** Dano-Norwegian for poor-law era records; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Somali, Arabic, Polish and Tigrinya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mercy toward the offender after 22 July and the 2015 arrivals are contested; present each side as its speakers did. Mercy in sentencing goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** donor surveys that rank causes by giver background were considered; set aside until community sources on giving are found.
