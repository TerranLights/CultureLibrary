# Norway: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic, Kurdish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo, Bergen and Tromsø listeners:** check pragmatics studies for how the same blunt reply is read in each.
- **Sørlandet and Vestlandet prayer-house youth:** check youth-camp material for teaching on "loving the neighbor" as feeling or as act.
- **Northern fishing families and urban animal-welfare groups:** check both sides' words in the seal and whale debates.
- **North Sámi, Lule Sámi and South Sámi:** check whether Sámi teachers' materials frame empathy in their own terms.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check commission testimony for what each asked the majority to grasp.
- **Rom and tatere:** check each people's media and memoirs for being misunderstood or seen.
- **Somali, Syrian, Iraqi, Eritrean and Vietnamese parents:** check their own associations' guides on children and feelings.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check workplace and union surveys for what they say about being understood.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how newcomers describe learning to read a small, rotating community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school programs and media; mixed for adult everyday practice; thin for Kven and Romani language terms.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Polish, Arabic and Kurdish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** empathy toward the 22 July perpetrator is painful ground; read victims' families first. Commission testimony must be quoted only within its published terms.
- **Held for review:** personality-scale studies that compare empathy across Norway's origin groups were weighed and left aside; they would pit communities against each other without their own voices.
