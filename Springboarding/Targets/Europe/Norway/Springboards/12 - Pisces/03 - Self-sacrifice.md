# Norway: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Tigrinya where apt. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

English keywords: martyr saints Norway, wartime sacrifice memory, Alta hunger strike, family caregivers Norway, cash-for-care debate, mining accidents Svalbard, missionary children.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vestlandet coast and Trøndelag:** check whether medieval martyr cults left feast customs in one region and not the other.
- **Lofoten and Finnmark fishing villages:** check memorial wording for drowned men against farm-valley gravestones.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, and coastal Sámi:** check Sámi-run sources for which sacrifices for land and language are honored.
- **Kvens of Nord-Troms and Porsanger:** check whether war-era evacuation and burning of Finnmark (1944) are told as sacrifice or as loss.
- **Oslo and Sørlandet mission families:** check mission archives for what a calling cost a household.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's own memoirs for what was given up under assimilation pressure.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own memorial books for those who hid others and those who were taken.
- **Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Eritrean families:** check each one's own media for split households.
- **Svalbard miners and their families:** check Store Norske's and the miners' unions' records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, mission and care; mixed for mothers' self-denial (mostly in fiction and debate); thin for Kven and Romani accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse and Latin for saints and poems; Dano-Norwegian for 1800s fiction; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Somali and Tigrinya.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Alta conflict, the burning of Finnmark and Eritrean diaspora politics are contested; present each account as an account. Sámi offering places are for Sámi institutions to describe.
- **Held for review:** surveys that rate parents' sacrifice by origin group were considered and set aside, since they would put a ranking where each community's own account belongs.
