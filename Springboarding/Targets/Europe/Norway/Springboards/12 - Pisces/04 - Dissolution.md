# Norway: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse and medieval Latin; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Forest Finnish (historical); Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the coming apart of a fixed form: the loss of the self's edges in something larger (another person, a crowd, nature, the divine), and the ending of bonds, groups and identities (proposed definition).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Setesdal, Telemark and Gudbrandsdalen:** check where *bergtatt* legends cluster and whether they were still told in the 1900s.
- **Oslo's and Bergen's Pentecostal congregations and Sørlandet's *bedehus*:** check which accept ecstatic worship and which keep it at arm's length.
- **Laestadians of Finnmark and Nord-Troms:** check Sámi-language and Kven-language revival histories for how *lihkadus* is described from inside.
- **North, Lule and South Sámi:** check each area's own revival reports for its account of shift and return.
- **Kvens and Forest Finns:** check for the dates and places where each language stopped being passed on.
- **Inland villages of Hedmark and Nordland, and the fishing villages of Finnmark:** check how depopulation is told by those who stayed.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's own media for language kept or let go.
- **Arabic-, Polish- and Vietnamese-speaking families:** check heritage-language schools for what parents fear losing.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how a town of short stays keeps any memory once people leave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for language shift, revival movements and depopulation; mixed for ecstatic experience; thin for Forest Finnish and Romani language loss.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for *Historia Norwegie*; Old Norse; Nynorsk and dialect for legends; North and Lule Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi trance practice and its knowledge belong to Sámi communities; read outsider texts only beside Sámi institutions. Norwegianization is contested ground; present each account as an account. Psychedelic use is a drug-law matter for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** cult-recovery cases from small closed congregations were weighed and left out, because the few named cases would identify private families.
