# Norway: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Romanes; Arabic, Polish, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo apartment blocks and Vestlandet farm clusters:** check how noise, shared walls and shared yards are handled in each.
- **Sørlandet and Vestlandet prayer-house communities:** check how the line to "the world" is drawn and redrawn by generation.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, and South Sámi of Trøndelag:** check Sámi accounts of pasture lines against cabins, roads and wind power.
- **Kvens and Skolt-area families of Sør-Varanger:** check how the Russian and Finnish lines shaped kin visits.
- **Forest Finns of Finnskogen:** check family histories along the Swedish line.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's own writing on the line between them and settled society.
- **Jewish community of Oslo and Trondheim:** check the community's own sources on marriage, conversion and synagogue security.
- **Somali, Arabic-speaking, Polish and Vietnamese families:** check their associations' own parent guides on privacy and children's phones.
- **Longyearbyen and Barentsburg:** check how residents of the two settlements visit across.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for working life, children's media and borders; mixed for personal limits; thin for Kven, Romani and Forest Finn boundary words.
- **Languages needed:** Old Norse; Dano-Norwegian for parish records; North and South Sámi; Kven; Romanes; Somali, Arabic and Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in congregations needs survivors' own accounts first. Grazing lines and the Fosen case are contested; present each account as an account. Property, border and roaming statutes go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** residential-segregation indexes for Oslo by origin were weighed and left out; they map where people live, not where they draw their own lines.
