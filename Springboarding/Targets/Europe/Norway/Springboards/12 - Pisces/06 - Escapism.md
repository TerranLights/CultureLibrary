## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oslo and Østlandet against Vestlandet's *bedehus* districts:** check whether drinking, dancing and charter trips are spoken of differently.
- **Northern Norway, Tromsø and Lofoten:** check how the dark season is filled, from local papers and festival programs.
- **Gudbrandsdalen, Telemark and Setesdal cabin valleys:** check how hosts speak of city visitors who come to escape.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi and coastal Sámi:** check Sámi-run festival and youth sources for what release means in each.
- **Laestadians, Kvens and Forest Finns:** check revival and village sources for which pastimes were refused.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's own media for travel and summer gatherings.
- **Polish and Lithuanian workers:** check their forums for how they spend free time far from family.
- **Somali, Syrian, Eritrean and Vietnamese youth:** check community youth clubs' own programs.
- **Longyearbyen:** check what residents and seasonal workers do in the polar night.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cabins, charter travel, alcohol and youth surveys; mixed for daydreaming and fandom; thin for Kven and Romani leisure.
- **Languages needed:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese; German for Ritter.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use and drink in Sámi and newer communities must not be read from outside sources alone; drug policy and its statutes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** treatment-admission figures split by country of birth were weighed and left out, since without each community's own sources they read as blame.
