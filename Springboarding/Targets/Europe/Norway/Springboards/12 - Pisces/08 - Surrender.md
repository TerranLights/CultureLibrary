# Norway: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Polish, Lithuanian, Arabic, Somali and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death), with release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Østlandet and Trøndelag war memory against Northern Norway's:** check whether 1940 is told as defeat, refusal or betrayal in each region.
- **Finnmark coastal and inland families:** check how Sámi, Kven and Norwegian families tell staying or leaving in 1944.
- **Laestadians and Church of Norway parishes in the north:** check how each speaks of yielding to God at a deathbed.
- **Vestlandet *bedehus* and Oslo's secular milieus:** check the assisted-dying debate by speaker and region.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi families:** check SANKS and Sámi media for letting go of herding when it ends in a family.
- **Forest Finns and tatere:** check whether any accounts tell of giving up language or travel, in their own words.
- **Rom and the Jewish community:** check memoirs for what was surrendered or refused under persecution.
- **Muslims of Somali, Iraqi and Syrian background; Polish and Vietnamese Catholics:** check their own congregations' teaching on illness and acceptance.
- **Longyearbyen:** check how residents speak of leaving Svalbard when work ends.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war memory and end-of-life care; mixed for everyday letting go; thin for Kven and Romani words.
- **Languages needed:** Dano-Norwegian for 1814; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian and Somali.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the occupation, Quisling and the Finnmark evacuation are contested ground; give each account as an account. Assisted dying is a law question for Libra and Capricorn; here only as public debate.
