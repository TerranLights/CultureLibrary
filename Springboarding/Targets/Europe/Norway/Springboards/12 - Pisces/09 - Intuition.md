# Norway: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with coastal and valley dialects; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese where apt. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jæren and Vestlandet against Østlandet:** check whether *vardøger* and second-sight stories cluster in some parishes or only in some collectors' files.
- **Lofoten, Vesterålen and Finnmark fishers:** check how sea-sense is described against valley farmers' weather sayings.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi of Kautokeino and Karasjok, and South Sámi herders:** check Sámi-led sources for how herders describe knowing without reasons.
- **Forest Finns and Kvens:** check majority legends that credited them with second sight, read as neighbors' claims, beside the communities' own accounts.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check how each separates a prompting from God from a hunch.
- **Tatere and Rom:** check each people's own writing for how outsiders' claims about them were received.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Catholics; Vietnamese families:** check community sources for premonition and dream talk.
- **Svalbard guides and residents:** check how they speak of sensing polar-bear danger and weather beyond town.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore and fiction; mixed for expert judgment; thin for Kven, Forest Finn and Romani terms.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and dialect; Old Norse; North Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Polish, Lithuanian and Vietnamese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** old legends that tie Sámi, Kvens or Forest Finns to sorcery are outsiders' claims; never take them as fact. Sámi herders' knowledge belongs to its holders; start with Sámi institutions.
- **Held for review:** child-welfare cases where a professional's "concern" decided an outcome were weighed and left out; they are contested and belong to law and authority work.
