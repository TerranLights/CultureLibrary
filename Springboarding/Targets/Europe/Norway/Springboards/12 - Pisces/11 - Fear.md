# Norway: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Norway, with Svalbard, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Bokmål and Nynorsk with the valley dialects; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Polish where apt. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Telemark and Setesdal against coastal Vestlandet:** check which supernatural fears each collected, and whether the sea or the dead dominates.
- **Sunnmøre and Sogn fjord villages:** check local papers for living under rockslide risk.
- **Hedmark and Østerdalen sheep farmers and Oslo readers:** check the wolf debate by speaker and place.
- **South Sámi herders of Trøndelag and Nordland, and North Sámi of Finnmark:** check Sámi media for how each speaks of threats to the herd.
- **Kvens and Finnmark families:** check evacuation-era memories for fear of the war and of the eastern border.
- **Laestadians and *bedehus* congregations:** check preaching on damnation before and after 1953.
- **Jewish community and Muslim congregations:** check each one's own statements on living with security after attacks.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Kurdish, Syrian and Polish families:** check their associations' own material on children's worries in a new country.
- **Longyearbyen:** check residents' accounts of avalanche, polar bears and the dark.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, legends and health surveys; mixed for fear of God; thin for Kven and Romani fear vocabularies.
- **Languages needed:** Nynorsk and dialect; Old Norse; North and South Sámi; Kven; Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Polish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 22 July and the attacks on the synagogue and the Bærum mosque are painful ground; start with those affected. The wolf and Chernobyl debates are contested; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** surveys of fear toward named minorities were weighed and left aside; they measure fear of a group, which belongs beside that group's own sources, not alone.
