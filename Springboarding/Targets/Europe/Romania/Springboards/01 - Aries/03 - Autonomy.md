## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Cluj against rural Oltenia and Moldavia:** whether leaving home and choosing a spouse happen at different ages; Eurostat regional figures and village studies are the test.
- **Free peasant villages (*răzeși* in Moldavia, *moșneni* in Muntenia and Oltenia) against former serf villages:** whether a long history of self-run land left traces in later village decision-making.
- **The Moți of the Apuseni:** check how the 1784 revolt and later mountain life figure in local talk of being one's own master.
- **Roma women and men of different groups:** whose decisions are recorded, by whom; the *robie* past and its afterlife must be separated from present choices.
- **Transylvanian Saxons and Banat Swabians:** how far communal self-government shaped personal choice, and what emigration did to it.
- **Lipovans (priestly and priestless), Baptists, Pentecostals and Adventists:** how congregations that govern themselves set limits for their members.
- **Turks and Tatars of Dobruja:** who decides within families and through the Muftiate's councils.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians and Székely autonomy claims (Hungary's row); Moldova; the diaspora.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Decree 770 and serfdom; mixed for family decision-making; thin for minority family choice outside Roma studies (look in ISPMN papers, not neighboring countries).
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for Stahl, Marian and the *rumânie* documents; Greek and Romanian for Phanariot-era acts; German and Latin for Saxon University records; Romani for E-Romnja's community material; Russian for Old Believer church texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Decree 770, *robie* and Roma family life are sensitive; present claims as claims and favor Roma voices. Saxon and Székely self-rule touch law and borders: point law to Libra or Capricorn and settle no borders.
- **Held for review:** Hungarian-language writing on Székely self-rule was weighed for this concept and placed on the comparison line rather than held; no autonomy pointer was set aside.
