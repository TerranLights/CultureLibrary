*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Brașov, Sibiu and Sighișoara (Saxon towns) against Wallachian market towns:** whether guild rules and later firm founding follow the same timeline.
- **The Jiu Valley and the Țara Făgărașului:** worth separating as places where single industries closed; check who started things after.
- **Moldavian market towns (Iași, Botoșani, Suceava):** compare town and guild records on who founded firms, to test inherited claims about Jewish and Armenian merchants against the record.
- **The Apuseni Moți:** check the Roșia Montană campaign for how local initiative and outside activists divided roles.
- **Roma groups by trade (Căldărari, Lingurari, Rudari woodworkers):** whose crafts were self-employment and how that shifted after 1990; draw on Roma authors first.
- **Lipovan fishing villages (Jurilovca, Sfântu Gheorghe):** check how fishing work is started and shared in the Delta.
- **Banat Serbs and Slovaks (Nădlac):** check local histories for cooperatives and founding societies.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose firms and founders are counted in Hungary's row; Moldova's start-up and registry data; Romanian founders abroad, as a receiving-country record.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics and the socialist-era informal economy; mixed for civic initiative; thin for minority enterprise outside Roma programs.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for ONRC, Ispirescu and Haret; German and Latin for the Saxon guild books; Romani for Roma-authored trade histories; Russian for Lipovan community material; Serbian and Slovak for Banat local histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the merchant-minority question carries old antisemitic and anti-Armenian claims; test them against records, never repeat them. Roma trades are a stereotype risk: name groups, never traits. Business law and the volunteering law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** some pointers on founders came from diaspora business press; they belong to the diaspora comparison, so they were dropped rather than held.

## 5. First queries

5. Search GEM's site for the Romania national reports and note the years covered.
