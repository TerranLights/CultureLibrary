## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moldavia and Bukovina against Muntenia and Oltenia:** check which founding story (Dragoș or Negru Vodă) each region's schools and festivals stage.
- **Moldavian bear and goat mask processions (Comănești and the Trotuș Valley) against Transylvanian carolling groups:** whether the New Year is opened by the same acts.
- **Old Calendarists, Lipovans and Ukrainians of Maramureș and Bukovina:** check how keeping a second calendar changes when things begin.
- **Turks, Tatars and Roma of Dobruja:** whether *Hıdırellez* and Nevruz mark a start in the same way across groups.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** compare their settlement stories with the Romanian *descălecat* stories as two founding traditions in one country.
- **The Banat (Timișoara):** check how 1989 is told as a beginning locally against in Bucharest.
- **Returned migrants in Romanian towns:** how they describe coming home and starting over; their lives abroad belong to the diaspora comparison.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians and their settlement accounts; Moldova's *Mărțișor*; the Aromanians.
