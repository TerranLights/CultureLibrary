*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia and Maramureș:** check whether their haiduc songs praise the same deeds, using field recordings, not edited anthologies.
- **Moldavia (Iași, Suceava) and Muntenia:** compare which medieval rulers are held up as brave in local schools and monuments.
- **The Făgăraș and Muscel highlands:** separate survivor memory of the mountain resistance from the political uses made of it.
- **Greek Catholic, Orthodox, Baptist and Pentecostal congregations:** check how each tells its martyrs and prison years.
- **Roma communities whose families were deported:** how survivors and descendants tell endurance, in Romani and Romanian, against outside framings.
- **Saxons of the fortified-church villages:** check how defense stories are kept after emigration emptied the villages.
- **Hutsuls, Ukrainians and Banat Serbs:** whether their hero traditions are shared across the border or kept apart.
- **The Jewish community of Iași and Bucharest:** check how memory of rescuers and of the 1941 Iași pogrom is held.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians and their 1989 role in Timișoara; Moldova; the diaspora.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, ballads and communist-era resistance; mixed for civilian courage today; thin for Tatar and Lipovan courage lore (look in community unions' publications and say so if nothing turns up).
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for the anthologies, Rudeanu and the memorials; Romani for deportation testimony; German for Saxon defense lore; Ukrainian for Dovbush songs; Yiddish and Hebrew for some rescuer files.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the mountain resistance, the "prison saints" cult and Antonescu-era honors are contested and touch the far right; the Holocaust and Roma deportation are grave; present every claim as a claim and never let a bullet say what a group is.
- **Held for review:** for courage, a Tatar epic pointer was considered but left out because no edition in Romania could be named with confidence; nothing was held.
