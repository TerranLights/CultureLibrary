*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maramureș, Oaș and Bihor against Muntenia:** check whether cursing songs survive as a living outlet or only in archives, by region.
- **Bukovina and Moldavia:** compare Niculiță-Voronca's beliefs with field recordings from Iași and Suceava counties.
- **Rural Muntenia and Moldavia (1907) against the Jiu Valley (1990, 1999):** separate peasant and miners' collective anger, and how each is remembered.
- **Roma communities:** check how Romani curse formulas and ideas of a quarrel's settlement differ by group, and record violence against Roma in their own words.
- **The Jewish community of Iași:** check how 1941 is commemorated locally and by whom.
- **Lipovans and Ukrainians:** whether Old Believer and Ukrainian Orthodox teaching on wrath differ from Romanian Orthodox parish teaching.
- **Saxons, Swabians and Banat Serbs:** check pastors' sermons and community papers for how anger was handled in tight villages.
- **Football supporters in Bucharest, Cluj and Craiova:** compare club cultures rather than city reputations.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians (and interethnic clashes such as Târgu Mureș 1990, which touch Hungary's row); Moldova; the diaspora.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for collective anger and church teaching; mixed for everyday temper; thin for Tatar and Turkish anger idioms in Dobruja (look for community-language dictionaries, never pad from Turkey).
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for the folk collections, Rebreanu and Slavici; Church Slavonic for some mural inscriptions; Romani for curse formulas; Yiddish for Iași testimony; Russian and Ukrainian for Old Believer and Ukrainian church teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Iași pogrom, Hădăreni and the mineriads involve living victims and contested blame; present claims as claims. Domestic violence data are partial. Court cases and the protection-order law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** Târgu Mureș 1990 was considered and set on the comparison line because it touches Hungary's row; no anger pointer went to the held list.
