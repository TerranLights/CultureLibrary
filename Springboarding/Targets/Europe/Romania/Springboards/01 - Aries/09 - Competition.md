## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia's Căluș villages and Oaș's *Sâmbra*:** check how each ritual contest picks a winner and whether the role is honorary or material.
- **Cluj (two clubs) and Craiova:** compare local football rivalries for how the city takes sides; Bucharest's derbies are a separate case.
- **Exam-heavy cities (Cluj, Iași, Bucharest) against small towns:** check county exam results and tutoring spending for differences in how much rides on rank.
- **Roma *lăutari* families and *manele* performers:** how musicians themselves describe rivalry and patronage; keep outsiders' moral panic about *manele* separate.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** check which village contests ended with emigration and which were revived.
- **Lipovans of the Delta and Tatars of Dobruja:** check sport clubs and festivals for contests tied to fishing and wrestling.
- **Banat Serbs, Bulgarians and Slovaks:** check whether their church fairs carry contests of their own.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose Hungarian-line school results and sports clubs sit in Hungary's row; Moldova's exam system; diaspora athletes who compete for other countries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, exams and communist-era contests; mixed for folk contests (some revived for tourists); thin for minority contests in Dobruja and the Banat.
- **Languages needed:** Italian or the Romanian translation for del Chiaro; Romanian for the exam data and Căluș sources; German for Saxon feast records; Russian for Lipovan club histories; Turkish for Dobruja festival programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the gymnastics system involves abuse claims; *manele* debates carry anti-Roma prejudice; the Steaua name dispute is a court matter (Libra). Competition law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** pointers on the economics of the private-tutoring market were weighed but no named Romanian study could be cited with confidence, so the market is left to the first queries instead of being held.
