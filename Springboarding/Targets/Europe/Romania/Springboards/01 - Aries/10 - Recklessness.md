## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maramureș, Bihor and Oltenia:** check how home distilling (*horincă*, *pălincă*, *țuică*) and drinking at feasts differ, using health surveys rather than reputation.
- **Moldavian and Bukovina villages:** compare festive noise customs and their injury records with other regions.
- **Cluj against other counties:** separate the Caritas years, rooted in Cluj, from the FNI losses; check where FNI savers lived before comparing.
- **Young drivers in big cities and on rural national roads:** check crash data by road type before drawing any regional contrast.
- **Roma communities:** check prevention programs led by Roma mediators against outside accounts; avoid any trait claims.
- **Lipovan and Ukrainian fishers of the Delta:** check sea and river accident reports for how risk at work is handled.
- **Saxons, Swabians and Banat Slovaks:** check community papers for how youth risk is talked about in small, ageing communities.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose road-risk and accident data belong to Hungary's row; Moldova; Romanian workers abroad, whose workplace accidents are recorded by the receiving countries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road, youth and drug statistics and for the 1990s schemes; mixed for festive risks; thin for minority-language talk of risk (look in community newspapers, not neighboring countries).
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for Ureche, Costin and the police data; French for Istrati's originals; German for Saxon community press; Russian for Delta fishing accounts; Romani for mediators' materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug use, drinking and gambling touch health and stigma; Roma health is a stereotype risk. The FNI and Caritas cases and gambling law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** an older ethnography of Roma bear-leaders (*ursari*) and their animals was weighed for daring with animals but left out as off-concept and a stereotype risk; it was dropped, not held.
