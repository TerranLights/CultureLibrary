# Romania: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; Romani; German; Ukrainian and Russian; Turkish; with Church Slavonic and French for older sources. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Vrancea and the Mărginimea against the Bărăgan:** check whether mountain commons survived collectivization while the old lowland estates did not, and how heirs speak of each.
- **Moldavian *răzeși* villages, Wallachian *moșneni* villages and former serf villages:** check which tell founding stories of free holding.
- **Transylvanian villages where Saxon land went to new holders in 1945:** gather both sides' accounts of the transfer as claims.
- **Roma neighborhoods without title (Cluj, Baia Mare, Bucharest's edges):** compare long use with paper ownership in each.
- **Lipovans and Romanian fishers of the Delta:** check how fishing rights are held and inherited.
- **Turks and Tatars of Dobruja:** check land and *vakıf* holding after 1878 and after collectivization.
- **Bucharest owners and tenants of nationalized houses:** check which side each family's memoirs take.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose Székely common forests are argued in Hungarian sources for Hungary's row; Moldova, whose post-Soviet land privatization follows another timeline.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land reform and decollectivization; mixed for commons; thin for *vakıf* land and Lipovan fishing tenure.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and Cyrillic-script Romanian for the charters; French for Iancu; Turkish and Ottoman Turkish for *vakıf* deeds; German for *Hattert* records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restitution quarrels, the 1945 expropriations and Roma evictions are live disputes: present each account as a claim. Property and restitution law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); the spending figures are collected at Stage 2.
- **Held for review:** a general theory of how commons are governed was weighed for the *obști* and left out as bringing no Romanian record; nothing was held.
