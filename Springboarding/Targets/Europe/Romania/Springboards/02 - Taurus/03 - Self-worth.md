# Romania: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; Romani; German; Ukrainian and Russian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jiu Valley and Hunedoara against Cluj's and Bucharest's office workers:** check what each says earns standing, with the closures as the hinge.
- **Moldavian and Oltenian villages with many workers abroad:** check how work abroad raises or lowers standing at home, for those who go and those left behind.
- **Pensioners in small industrial towns:** check whether worth is told through the old workplace or through children and grandchildren.
- **Roma activists and community elders:** check whose words for respect (*pakiv*) and shame reach the record, and in which Romani dialect.
- **Greek Catholic parishes (Blaj, Cluj, Maramureș):** check how a church banned in 1948 speaks of restored standing after 1990.
- **Lipovans:** check Old Believer teaching on humility and pride in their own books.
- **Dobruja Tatars and Turks:** check the use of *ayıp* and honor words across generations.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose minority self-esteem studies in Hungarian go to Hungary's row; Moldova, whose own "small country" essays answer another history.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, film and national-character essays; mixed for survey data on self-esteem; thin for minority self-description outside Roma activism.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for the *Divanul* original; Romani for Giuvlipen's texts; Turkish for Dobruja honor words; English for PISA and Eurofound.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cioran's essay and other interwar texts carry fascist and antisemitic strands: study them as objects. Suicide data need care. Never let a pointer on national inferiority become a claim about Romanians.
- **Held for review:** psychology scales built abroad were weighed for self-esteem and left to the first queries until a Romanian adaptation is found; nothing was held.
