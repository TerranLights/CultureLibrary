## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox, Greek Catholic, Roman Catholic, Baptist, Pentecostal and Adventist believers:** check how each church settles a collision of values, such as a Saturday sabbath against a work shift.
- **Rural Moldavia and Oltenia against Bucharest, Cluj and Timișoara:** check the EVS splits by settlement size and age before any regional claim.
- **Roma Pentecostal converts and Orthodox Roma:** check what each says changed or stayed when faith changed.
- **Saxons and Swabians who stayed:** check whether village association rules survived the 1990s emigration.
- **Lipovans of Tulcea and Brăila:** check which customs the community itself names as non-negotiable.
- **Turks and Tatars of Dobruja:** compare sermons in Turkish with those in Romanian.
- **The Jewish community today and before 1940:** check how remembrance is taught by the Federation's communities.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose Reformed and Unitarian church teaching belongs to Hungary's row; Moldova, whose EVS sample is separate and must not be pooled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey data (EVS since 1993) and church teaching; mixed for regional and minority values; thin for Lipovan and Tatar sources in Romanian libraries.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for Varlaam; German for the *Bruderschaft* articles; Russian and Church Slavonic for Old Believer rules; Turkish for Dobruja sermons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2018 referendum and church funding are live political disputes; quote each side as a side. Noica's past ties to the far right need noting. *Marime* is internal Roma knowledge: rely on Roma authors. Any law on family goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** global value-map scores for Romania were weighed and left in favor of the Romanian team's own EVS work; nothing was held.
