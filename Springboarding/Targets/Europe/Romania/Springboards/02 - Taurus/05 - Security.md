# Romania: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; Romani; German; Ukrainian and Lipovan Russian; Turkish; Banat Serbian. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest residents of red-dot blocks against Iași, Cluj and Timișoara:** check how known quake risk enters choices of home.
- **Public-sector workers against private and day-wage workers:** check whether a state post is still sought as security after 2010.
- **Rural pensioners in Moldavia and Oltenia against urban pensioners:** check what stands behind the pension (land, garden, children abroad).
- **Roma households (Kalderash, Rudari, settled Roma):** check FRA results against Roma-run surveys, and the day-labor (*zilieri*) round.
- **Saxons and Swabians who stayed:** check what security meant to those who did not take the emigration road.
- **The Jewish community of Iași and Bucharest:** check how memories of the 1940s shape talk of safety today.
- **Dobruja farmers, Turkish, Tatar and Romanian, and Lipovan fishers:** check reliance on irrigation and fish against drought and flood.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose church welfare funds in Hungarian go to Hungary's row; Moldova, whose 2014 bank fraud is another country's shock.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pensions, deprivation and earthquake risk; mixed for food stores and informal saving; thin for Lipovan and Tatar household security.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for Pamfile, Stancu and the IICCMER interviews; German for deportation testimony; Turkish for Dobruja farmers' accounts; English for Ioanid and FRA.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Securitate past colors the word itself; the 1945 deportations and the paid emigrations are painful and disputed in detail; present each as an account. Social-insurance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** for security, prepper and survival media were weighed and left out for lack of a named Romanian source; nothing was held.
