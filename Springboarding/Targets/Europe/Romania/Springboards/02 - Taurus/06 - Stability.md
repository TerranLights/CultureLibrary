# Romania: Stability (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; Romani; German; Ukrainian and Lipovan Russian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar. **Concept in one line:** continuity: things, places, relationships and routines staying as they are over time.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maramureș and Bukovina villages against Bucharest's new suburbs:** check how long families have lived in one house in each, from census tables.
- **Old boyar and merchant lines against families that rose after 1990:** check which tell their story through continuity.
- **Saxon villages after the emigration (Mălâncrav, Criț, Saschiz):** compare the accounts of remaining Saxons, Romanian neighbors and Roma newcomers.
- **Mărginimea shepherds against settled farmers of the plains:** check whether moving herds count as a stable life in each community's words.
- **Kalderash and other once-traveling Roma against long-settled Roma:** check what settlement changed, by their own telling.
- **Lipovans and Dobruja Tatars:** check how each community kept continuity through emigration waves.
- **Moldavia's villages of migrant workers:** check what stays the same when most working-age people are abroad.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose village-continuity studies in Hungarian go to Hungary's row; the migrants' own lives abroad, counted here only through the villages they return to.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, inflation and the *sistematizare* episode; mixed for family continuity; thin for Tatar and Lipovan generational records.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian for Bernea, Sadoveanu and the census; French for the 1989 campaign; German for Saxon-village accounts; Russian for Lipovan sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the forced settling of traveling Roma and the systematization plan are painful histories; present accounts as accounts. Blaga's timeless village is a claim, not a description.
- **Held for review:** for stability, a general measure of cultural tightness was weighed and left out as a cross-country score without Romanian depth; nothing was held.
