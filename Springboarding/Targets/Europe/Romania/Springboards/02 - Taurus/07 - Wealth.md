## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest-Ilfov and Cluj against Vaslui, Botoșani and Teleorman:** check the IDUL scores before any claim about rich and poor regions.
- **Saxon patrician families of Sibiu and Brașov:** check what remains of their wealth and collections after 1945 and emigration.
- **Armenian and Jewish merchant families (Bucharest, Iași, Suceava, Dumbrăveni):** check community records for what survived nationalization.
- **Roma communities:** check how wealth is described by Roma authors and Roma-run media, as against tabloid coverage.
- **Lipovan fishing households of the Delta:** check income from fish and tourism.
- **Turks and Tatars of Constanța and Mangalia:** check merchant histories in Turkish-language community papers.
- **Migrant-sending villages against villages with few migrants:** check how remittances change who counts as well-off.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose aristocratic estate histories in Hungarian go to Hungary's row; Moldova, whose oligarch fortunes belong to another state's record.
