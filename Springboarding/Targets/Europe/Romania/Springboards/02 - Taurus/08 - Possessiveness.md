# Romania: Possessiveness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; German; Ukrainian and Lipovan Russian; Turkish; Banat Serbian; Romani through Roma speakers. **Concept in one line:** holding on to what one sees as one's own, including people, beyond what the bond or the thing warrants, and the urge to keep others away from it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Maramureș, Oaș and Oltenia village weddings against city weddings:** check whether bride-stealing survives as play and who pays the ransom.
- **Younger and older respondents:** check the Eurobarometer and dating-violence studies by age before reading jealousy as love or control.
- **Roma women:** check what Roma women's groups report about control in couples, in their own words and dialects.
- **Orthodox and neo-Protestant couples:** compare what marriage preparation teaches about jealousy and trust.
- **Lipovans of Tulcea and Turks and Tatars of Constanța:** check how each community explains marrying within it.
- **Bucharest and Cluj block neighborhoods (Drumul Taberei, Mănăștur):** check how street-spot claims are made and settled.
- **Banat Serbs and Swabians:** check in-law and household-claim customs in each community's own folklore.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose Hungarian-language relationship surveys go to Hungary's row; Moldova, whose bride-stealing customs need their own sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, song and women's-rights reporting; mixed for survey data; thin for Lipovan, Tatar and Banat Serbian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Cantemir; Romanian for Pamfile, Petrescu and the forums; Turkish for Dobruja marriage accounts; Russian for Lipovan community writing; Romani only through Roma interviewers, since no sure Romani word for jealousy is given here.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** control and partner violence; bride abduction must never be presented as a group's custom, only as a practice to check through that group's own accounts. Protection orders and custody law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** jealousy studies across many countries were weighed and left out until a Romanian sample is found; nothing was held.
