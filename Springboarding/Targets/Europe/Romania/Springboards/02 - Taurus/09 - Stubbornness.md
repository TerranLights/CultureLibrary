*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox and Greek Catholic parishes in the same villages (Maramureș, Bihor, Cluj):** compare each side's account of who would not yield and why.
- **Lipovans of the Delta:** check how far the refusal of change reaches past rite into dress and food.
- **Jiu Valley miners against other unions:** check whether long strikes are told as pride or as loss.
- **City protesters and rural voters:** check how each side names the other's refusal.
- **Roma families facing eviction and city officials:** gather both accounts of a standoff.
- **Saxons and Swabians who stayed:** check what words they use for their choice.
- **Dobruja Muslims who stayed against those whose kin left for Turkey:** check how staying is told.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose own church and school disputes are argued in Hungarian sources for Hungary's row; Moldova, whose quarrel over the language's name is another state's standoff.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protests, strikes and literature; mixed for psychology data; thin for Tatar and Lipovan accounts in Romanian libraries.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian and Latin for the Sofronie records, with German for the Habsburg side; Russian for Lipovan histories; Turkish for the Dobruja emigration; German for the stayers' memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** church disputes, the 1977 strike, evictions and vaccine refusal are live and contested: give each side's account as a claim, and never describe a people as stubborn. Expropriation law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** a world study of apology customs was weighed for this concept and left out as having no Romanian case; nothing was held.
