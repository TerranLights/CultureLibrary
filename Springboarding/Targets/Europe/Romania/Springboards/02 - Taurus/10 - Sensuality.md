*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Households that keep the Orthodox fasts and secular urban households:** check how the yearly table differs in cities against villages.
- **Baptists, Pentecostals and Adventists:** check what their teaching says about alcohol, dance and adornment, in their own texts.
- **Roma musicians of Clejani and Zece Prăjini:** check how they speak of their own pleasure in playing against playing for pay.
- **Banat Swabians and Transylvanian Saxons:** compare fair, dance and food customs in each community's own records.
- **Lipovans of the Delta:** check how steam baths and fasting sit together in the week.
- **Turks and Tatars of Dobruja (Constanța, Medgidia):** check the hamam, Ramadan evenings and Bayram foods.
- **Oltenia, Moldavia and Transylvania:** compare regional cookbooks and festivals before any claim about regional tastes.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose Sovata and Székely spa traditions are documented in Hungarian for Hungary's row; Moldova, whose wine heritage must not be pooled with Cotnari's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, wine, song and spas; mixed for touch and personal space; thin for Lipovan bathing and Tatar food customs in print.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for the Văcărescu originals; German for *Kirchweih* records; Russian for Lipovan bathing terms; Turkish for Dobruja hamam and feast words; Romani for the musicians' own interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual pleasure belongs to Scorpio; keep this sheet on the senses. Romanticized images of Roma music and "passion" are a stereotype risk: use musicians' own words.
