## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muntenia and Bucharest against Moldavia (Iași) and Transylvania's Romanian towns:** check which papers each region read first and when, since the printed press began separately in each.
- **Older villagers in Oltenia and Maramureș against young people in Cluj:** check which channel (TV, radio, social media, church) each group names as its first source.
- **Roma groups:** check how news reaches Kalderash, Rudari and settled Roma neighborhoods, and who in the community passes it on.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** check how the German-language press of Sibiu and Timișoara now informs a small community and many Romanian readers.
- **Lipovans and Ukrainians (Tulcea, Suceava, Maramureș):** check whether Russian- and Ukrainian-language channels from abroad sit beside Romanian ones in the home.
- **Turks and Tatars of Constanța:** check Turkish-language television from abroad against local papers.
- **The Jewish community:** check how its papers informed readers in the Antonescu years and after 1944.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose media world is mapped from Hungary's row; Moldova, whose Russian-language channels pose different questions of trust; diaspora news sites, kept apart because they inform readers abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history and trust surveys; mixed for village information flows; thin for Romani-language news and Tatar media.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for Cantacuzino and the 1829 papers; Turkish for *Hakses*; Yiddish for the Iași press; Russian for Lipovan sources on fishing knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2024 election annulment is a live political dispute; give each side's account as a claim. The events of December 1989 are contested and caused deaths. Rules on access to information and data protection go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** global press-freedom rankings were considered but kept out, because they rank a state from outside rather than open how people inside get news.
