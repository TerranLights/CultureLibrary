# Romania: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian (including Oltenian and Delta words for huts); Banat Serbian and Swabian German for the 1951 deportations; Polish for the 1939 refugees; Romani; Lipovan Russian. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Banat Serbs, Swabians and Romanians deported to the Bărăgan:** compare each group's memoirs of building shelter and of coming back.
- **Bucharest's street homeless and those in Cluj and Iași:** check what each city offers in winter and who goes without.
- **Moldavia and Transylvania after the 1970 floods:** check who took in the displaced, kin or the state.
- **Dobruja and Delta fishers (Lipovans, Romanians, Ukrainians of the Delta):** check seasonal hut practice by village.
- **Roma in Bihor and Crișana:** check housing and shelter reports from Roma-led groups before municipal ones.
- **Moți of the Apuseni and shepherds of the Mărginimea Sibiului:** compare mountain shelters for people and flocks.
- **Orthodox and Catholic monasteries; Baptist and Pentecostal congregations:** check which run shelters, soup kitchens or guest houses.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvanian Hungarian church shelters (Hungary's row), for the Catholic and Reformed models working in the same cities; Moldova, which received refugees from Ukraine alongside Romania; Romanians sleeping rough abroad, reported by diaspora charities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for deportation memoir and monastery history; mixed for current homelessness (whether a regular national count exists is to be checked); thin for Delta huts and Roma shelter work outside Bihor.
- **Languages needed:** old Cyrillic-script Romanian for Dosoftei; Serbian and German for Bărăgan memoirs; Polish for the 1939 refugee accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homeless people and asylum seekers are vulnerable; avoid naming them. The 2013 stray-dog debate is still bitter; keep each side's words. Housing law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the Posadas and sanctuary-movement models in the concept block were weighed; carolers asking to be let in looked like the nearest parallel and went to the Home sheet instead.
