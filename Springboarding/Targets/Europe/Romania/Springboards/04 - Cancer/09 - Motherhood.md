*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina against Transylvania (Cluj, Sibiu):** compare the mother in ballads and pilgrimages across Orthodox, Greek Catholic and Roman Catholic regions.
- **Oltenia and Muntenia against Bucharest:** check teenage and late first births by county against city figures.
- **Women who received the *Mama eroină* title and their daughters:** check how each generation speaks of family size.
- **Kalderash, Ursari and Rudari mothers:** check health and school reports from Roma women's groups, group by group.
- **Banat Bulgarians, Serbs and Slovaks:** check midwives'-day and birth customs village by village.
- **Ukrainians and Hutsuls; Lipovans; Turks and Tatars:** compare the forty-day customs after birth by faith and language.
- **Baptist and Pentecostal families:** check how church writing speaks of mothers of many children.
- **Csángó mothers of Bacău county:** contested ground; compare the claims made in Romanian and Hungarian sources about their language at home.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvanian Hungarian Marian pilgrimage at Șumuleu Ciuc (Hungary's row), as a contrast to Nicula and Cacica; Moldova's Mother's Day and family policy; Romanian mothers working in Italy and Spain, whose mothering from afar meets the Abandonment sheet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ballads, Marian devotion and birth statistics; mixed for mothers' own voices; thin for Tatar, Lipovan and Banat Bulgarian birth customs.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian ballad variants in regional speech for *Miorița*; Banat Bulgarian for *Babin den*; Romani for Roma women's writing; Ukrainian for Hutsul birth customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Decree 770 is contested ground; any use of it here stays a sourced claim. Home birth and teen motherhood are live arguments; give each side as a claim. Leave and family law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** Mother's Day verses that circulate online under Eminescu's or Arghezi's name stayed off the table, since their authorship needs a checked edition before anyone cites them.
