# Romania: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian and its regional speech; Romani; Banat Swabian and Saxon German; Ukrainian; Lipovan Russian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Serbian; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia against Moldavia (Iași):** check Pride and street-art records for where open statements are made and where they are removed.
- **Oltenia and Maramureș:** compare what village songs let a singer say about a neighbor or lover, set against what is said plainly.
- **The Banat (Timișoara):** check memoirs of the Phoenix era and of the Aktionsgruppe for how German and Romanian writers in one city spoke out.
- **Crișana (Oradea) and Transylvania's Romanian towns (Cluj, Sibiu, Brașov):** check student press and youth theater for how far the young speak on stage.
- **The Jiu Valley and the Apuseni (the Moți):** check union and village memoirs for when grievance was voiced aloud and when kept back.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari, Romanianized Roma):** check how dress and speech rules differ by group, in Roma authors' words.
- **Lipovans:** check how Old Believer rules on beards and dress are kept or loosened by younger men.
- **Saxons and Swabians, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Serbs, Slovaks, Armenians, the Jewish community:** check whose language and dress are shown in public, and where.
- **Orthodox, Greek Catholic, Roman Catholic and neo-Protestant congregations:** set each church's teaching on dress, speech and tattoos side by side.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose language rights on signs sit in Hungary's row and are read only beside Romanian public speech; Moldova's protest songs; Romanian migrants' blogs abroad, for what people say once away.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for music, satire and post-1989 media; mixed for dress and therapy; thin for Tatar, Lipovan and Banat Bulgarian expression.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and old Romanian Cyrillic for the marginal notes; German for the Aktionsgruppe texts; Turkish and Tatar for the Dobruja dress pointer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sexual identity and church dress codes involve living people and active disputes; give each claim as a claim. Speech law and hate-speech rulings go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** jokes that fix a temperament on a region were weighed as a pointer and kept off the table; only their spread as jokes, not what they claim, could be studied.
