# Romania: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian and its regional speech; Romani; German; Ukrainian; Lipovan Russian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Serbian; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia against Moldavia (Iași, Vaslui):** check how national television shows each region, and how often.
- **Oltenia and Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** compare evil-eye charms in Orthodox and Muslim homes, without treating either as quaint.
- **The Banat and Crișana:** check *corso* memoirs from Timișoara and Oradea for who could walk where, by class and faith.
- **Bukovina (Suceava) and Maramureș:** check how painted-monastery and wooden-church tourism changes who is looked at in villages.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns (Brașov, Sibiu) and the Țara Făgărașului:** compare Dracula-tourism attitudes in Bran with nearby villages.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari, Boyash):** check which groups appear in media monitoring, and which almost never do.
- **Lipovans, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Serbs, Banat Bulgarians, Slovaks and Czechs:** check minority broadcast schedules for each group's share.
- **Armenians and the Jewish community:** check which museums, plaques and tours show each community's history.
- **Disabled and older people:** compare official counts with NGO estimates.
- **Csángó of Moldavia:** contested ground; check how the census and each camp's writers count or name them, each as a claim.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-language broadcasting in Transylvania, which sits in Hungary's row, set beside other minority air time; Moldova's media portrait of Romania; diaspora workers as Romanian media show them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media monitoring and official statistics; mixed for promenades and gaze customs; thin for Czech, Slovak and Banat Bulgarian visibility.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish (or the Romanian translation) for Evliya; German for Banat *corso* memoirs; Turkish and Tatar for *nazar* customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** press monitoring touches hate speech, whose legal side goes to Libra; reports of the dead found alone name families.
- **Held for review:** the wider vampire-film and novel canon stays at the single Bran pointer; beyond it, that material shows how outsiders imagine the country and says nothing of how people inside want to be seen.

## 5. First queries
1. Download the 2021 census tables on ethnicity and religion and list the counties with the highest shares of "not declared".
2. Search the ActiveWatch site for reports on Roma in the press and note their years.
3. Ask the Romanian Academy Library for the Romanian translation of Evliya's chapters on Moldavia and Wallachia.
4. Search Timișoara and Sibiu library catalogs for "corso" memoirs and photo albums.
