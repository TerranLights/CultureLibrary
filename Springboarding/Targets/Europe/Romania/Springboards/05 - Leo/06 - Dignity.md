# Romania: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia and the Banat:** compare who sings the dawn song and over whom, and whether the custom survives in towns.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Muntenia:** check aid queues and social-canteen records for how the poor are received in each region.
- **Brașov and the Jiu Valley:** set the 1987 Brașov testimonies beside miners' accounts for the words each uses for being degraded.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns (Cluj, Sibiu) and the Apuseni (the Moți):** check *Memorandum* memory and village funeral practice side by side.
- **Bukovina (Suceava), Maramureș and Dobruja (Tulcea):** check how far a hospital or care home is from remote villages, and how families care for the old there.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari, Boyash):** check ID-paper campaigns and health studies for which communities are named, and in whose voice.
- **Lipovans, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Turks and Tatars, Serbs, Banat Bulgarians:** check each faith's rules for washing and burying the dead.
- **The Jewish community and Armenians:** check burial societies and elder care for what each still runs.
- **Orthodox, Greek Catholic, Roman Catholic and neo-Protestant charities:** compare how each hands out aid, and how much choice it leaves.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-language care homes in Transylvania, which belong to Hungary's row, read only for contrast with the state system; Moldova's pensioners, for a near neighbor's case; Romanian carers working in Italy, whose working conditions belong to the diaspora comparison.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for prison memoirs and health studies; mixed for aid and disability; thin for burial of the unclaimed and for Lipovan and Tatar elder care.
- **Languages needed:** old Romanian Cyrillic for Dosoftei; German for how Vienna received the *Memorandum*; Romani for ID campaign material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prison and revolt testimony names victims and guards; care-home deaths name families. Patients' rights and euthanasia law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** reports from countries that allow assisted dying were looked at for contrast and left out, since Romania has no such practice to measure; the sheet stays with the Romanian debate itself.
