*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia against Iași and Moldavia:** check school inspectorate cases for how complaints about teachers' insults are handled in each.
- **Oltenia and Maramureș:** compare mocking customs at village feasts and how far a target may answer back.
- **The Jiu Valley and Hunedoara:** check former miners' memories of being called names in national media after 1990.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns (Cluj, Sibiu) and the Banat (Timișoara):** check student and workplace surveys for whether belittling is reported differently.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari, Boyash):** check Roma organizations' own complaints and campaigns about slurs, group by group.
- **The Jewish community:** check how 1941 testimony is collected and taught, and in which towns.
- **Lipovans, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Turks and Tatars, Serbs:** check community press for slurs aimed at each and how they answer.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** check emigration memoirs for the humiliations of leaving (searches, bribes, lost property).
- **Men and women:** compare recruits' hazing memories with women's accounts of online exposure, without assuming either.
- **Orthodox, Greek Catholic and neo-Protestant churches:** check how each tradition teaches chosen humility against unjust humiliation.
- **Comparison only:** the 1940 Vienna Award as Hungarian sources tell it, which belongs to Hungary's row, read only to see two memories of one summer; Moldova's memory of the same losses; migrant workers' own accounts abroad, set beside the home press label.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for prison, hazing and Holocaust testimony; mixed for school and workplace; thin for slurs aimed at Lipovans, Tatars and Banat Bulgarians.
- **Languages needed:** Romanian Cyrillic and Greek for pre-1800 punishment records; Yiddish for some 1941 testimony; Romani for Roma campaigns against the slur.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Pitești, the 1941 pogrom and Roma slurs are painful and contested in public memory; give each claim as a claim, survivors first. Insult and defamation law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the televised 1989 trial of the Ceaușescus came up as a case of a ruler's public degradation; it stays off this sheet because the footage is a stock image for many sheets and any use needs the full trial record first.
