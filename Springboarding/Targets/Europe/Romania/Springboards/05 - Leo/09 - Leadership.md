## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muntenia and Oltenia against Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** compare which prince each region's legends hold up as the model leader.
- **The Mărginimea Sibiului and Vrancea:** check how sheepfold crews choose and drop a *baci* in each.
- **Banat and Crișana towns (Lugoj, Reșița, Oradea):** check how long mayors stay and how they campaign.
- **Transylvania's Romanian villages (Țara Făgărașului, the Apuseni):** check lads' bands and church councils for leadership by age or by merit.
- **Hunedoara and the Jiu Valley:** set union leaders' memoirs beside miners' accounts of following them.
- **Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** compare how the mufti, Lipovan elders and Orthodox clergy are chosen.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** compare leadership terms and choosing in each group's own accounts.
- **Saxons and Swabians, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Serbs, Banat Bulgarians, Slovaks:** check how each community body picks its head.
- **The Jewish community and Hasidic memory of Maramureș:** check memoirs for how rebbes and community heads were followed.
- **Women and men:** check where women lead in village, church and party, and under what title.
- **Comparison only:** the Hungarian party of Transylvania and its leaders, which belong to Hungary's row, read only to see minority politics from another side; Moldova's presidents; diaspora parish leaders.
