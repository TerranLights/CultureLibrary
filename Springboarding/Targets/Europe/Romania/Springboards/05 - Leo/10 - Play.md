## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia against rural Oltenia:** compare playground counts and how far village children roam unsupervised.
- **Transylvania's Romanian villages (Hunedoara, the Țara Făgărașului) and Muntenia:** compare horned-mask games, which differ in name and staging.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** check whether park chess and backgammon draw the same people as in the capital.
- **The Banat (Timișoara) and Crișana (Arad, Oradea):** check toy-factory and industry records for what was made locally.
- **Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** compare *tavla* in Turkish and Tatar coffeehouses with Lipovan pre-Lent games.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** check settlement ethnographies for children's play in Roma writers' and researchers' accounts, not outsiders' impressions.
- **Saxons and Swabians, Ukrainians and Hutsuls, Serbs, Slovaks and Czechs of the Banat:** check community memoirs for games each kept.
- **The Jewish community:** check memoirs of childhood in Iași, Botoșani and Sighet for games of Purim and Hanukkah.
- **Neo-Protestant households:** check what games and cards each church allows, and on which days.
- **Girls and boys:** compare play memories by sex, without assuming the split.
- **Comparison only:** Székely and Hungarian village games in Transylvania, which belong to Hungary's row, read only to tell shared games from Romanian ones; Moldova's children's games; diaspora children in Italy and Spain, for which home games survive abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for memories, jokes and masks; mixed for gaming and gambling; thin for Tatar, Slovak and Czech games.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic (or the Romanian translation) for Paul of Aleppo; Russian for Lipovan *Maslenitsa* coverage; Turkish and Tatar for *tavla* culture.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gambling harm involves vulnerable players; aggregate only. Gambling licensing law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** figures on gaming disorder were sought for the excess side; no Romanian clinical count could be named, so that edge rests on the *păcănele* pointer for now.
