# Romania: Domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian and its regional speech; Romani (Kalderash); Saxon and Zipser German; Ukrainian and Hutsul; Lipovan Russian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Serbian and Slovak; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: good work and a well-kept life, taking in the daily round, cleanliness, craft and rest, with work ways as the sign's folkway.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The capital, Muntenia and Oltenia:** set Bucharest commutes beside Oltenian village days built round the garden plot.
- **Iași's Moldavia and Suceava's Bukovina:** check which saints' days still halt field work, and whether towns keep them.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** line up Lipovan fishing seasons with Turkish and Tatar farm calendars.
- **Banat (Timișoara) and Crișana (Oradea):** compare Swabian farmsteads, Serb *moba* and Slovak forest villages in Bihor for how shared work was repaid.
- **Maramureș:** read Romanian, Ukrainian and Zipser accounts of the wood trade together.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** keep Mărginimea Sibiului herders, Moți woodworkers, Făgăraș farmers and Jiu Valley miners apart.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari and others):** ask which trades each group names itself by, in its own words first.
- **Saxons, Banat Bulgarians, Czechs, Armenians, the Jewish community:** find each one's guild or workshop records.
- **Churches:** check how Adventist Saturdays, Orthodox and Greek Catholic fasts and Pentecostal Sundays fit shift work.
- **Moldavia's Csángó:** their farm year appears in both Romanian and Hungarian studies; contested, so each is a claim.
- **Comparison only:** Székely and other Hungarian villages of Transylvania, whose crafts go to Hungary's row; Moldova and the Aromanians, for shepherding outside this row; diaspora workers in Italy, Spain and Germany, for how work abroad reshapes the household left behind.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** plentiful in INS tables, monographs and folk calendars; mixed for socialist labor memory; thin for Zipser, Czech and Banat Bulgarian work.
- **Languages needed:** Greek for Mavrocordat's chancery; Church Slavonic and Romanian Cyrillic for Paisie's rule; German for the Zipser histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** owed labor (*clacă*), the enslavement of Roma (*robie*) and socialist labor camps are contested; read claim by claim. Statutes on labor belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Lands:** which lands Romania will hold is not yet settled; the Virgo sheets keep to present-day Romania, with its Hungarian minority left to Hungary.
- **Held for review:** Fischer's four founding work ethics (*Albion's Seed*) were weighed as a frame and left aside, being drawn for British America; whether any fits the Principalities is the author's call.
