## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Măgurele against rural Muntenia and Oltenia:** set the research institutes beside county exam results for Teleorman, Giurgiu and Olt.
- **Iași and Suceava:** compare the university's informatics intake with olympiad lists from Moldavian and Bukovinian high schools.
- **Cluj and Timișoara:** check how software firms and the two technical universities recruit, and from which counties.
- **Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** look at what Turkish and Tatar language classes and Lipovan Russian classes teach in math and science.
- **The Banat:** check exam results in Serbian- and German-language sections, and the Banat Bulgarian village schools of Dudeștii Vechi and Vinga.
- **Maramureș and Bukovina:** check the Ukrainian-language high school in Sighetu Marmației *check* and how its pupils fare.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns (Sibiu, Brașov, Alba Iulia):** separate pupils in German-language lycées from those in Romanian ones.
- **Roma pupils:** check the university places set aside for Roma students and which subjects they choose, beside school segregation data.
- **Baptist, Pentecostal and Adventist families:** look at how churches answer evolution in school biology, through their own publications.
- **The Jewish community:** check how Jewish students and scientists were shut out by the 1920s *numerus clausus* campaigns and the 1938–40 laws.
- **Csángó children:** accounts disagree over which language their lessons should be in; contested, so every count is a claim.
- **Comparison only:** the Hungarian line of Babeș-Bolyai University (Hungary's row), to set a parallel teaching language beside the Romanian; Moldova's olympiad teams, for a neighbor trained in the same tradition; Romanian programmers abroad, for brain drain seen from home.

## 4. Flags

- **Sensitive or restricted:** school results by ethnicity touch segregation; the exclusion of Jewish students needs their own accounts. Statistics law and audit statutes go to Libra.
