## 4. Flags
- **Data:** good on scams, polls and church writing; patchy on taste and market skill; thin for Armenian, Slovak and Banat Bulgarian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the *Dobrotolubie*; Greek for the Philokalia's terms; Turkish for Dobruja Nastratin variants; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Hasidic courts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the standing of Arsenie Boca is disputed; scam reporting risks profiling. Fraud law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** booklets of prophecies credited to monks, sold at church stalls and online, were weighed and left out, since their authorship is uncertain; whether to trace them as a discernment problem is for the author.
