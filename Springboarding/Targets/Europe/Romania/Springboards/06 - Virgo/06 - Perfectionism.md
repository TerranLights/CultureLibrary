*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest's top lycées against rural Teleorman and Giurgiu schools:** check how the hunt for straight tens differs between them.
- **Oltenia (Craiova, Vâlcea):** set Hurezi's carved stone beside today's Horezu workshops for how a standard is passed on.
- **Moldavia and Bukovina:** follow Neamț's copying tradition and Marginea's potters; check Ukrainian and Hutsul egg painters' rules for a finished pattern.
- **Dobruja:** look at Lipovan icon painters and cantors in Tulcea and Brăila for how errors in rite are corrected.
- **The Banat and Crișana:** check Swabian and Serb guild books in Timișoara and Lugoj for rejected work and fines.
- **Maramureș:** ask Romanian and Ukrainian carvers how a gate or cross is judged finished.
- **Transylvania's Romanians (Sibiu, Brașov, Alba Iulia, the Apuseni):** set Mărginimea Sibiului icon-on-glass painters beside Moți woodworkers.
- **Roma coppersmiths (Kalderash):** record what makes a cauldron or still well made, asking the smiths.
- **Pentecostal and Baptist churches:** check how holiness teaching treats sinless living, and how members describe the strain.
- **The Jewish community:** check how Torah scrolls were kept fit for use and where they were sent for repair.
- **The Csángó:** their embroidery and chant are judged "authentic" by rival Romanian and Hungarian standards; contested, every such verdict a claim.
- **Comparison only:** Corund potters and Székely carvers (Hungary's row), to set a second makers' standard beside Marginea; Moldova's ten-point school marks, for grade pressure under a shared scale; Romanian children in Italian and Spanish schools, for how parents' standards travel.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** good on crafts, music and school marks; thin on clinical perfectionism and on Armenian and Slovak craft; the People's House tales are hearsay until checked.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for Uric's manuscript and the Lipovan service books; German for Saxon and Swabian guild books; Yiddish and Hebrew for scroll records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pupils' stress and any self-harm need care and adult consent; Ceaușescu-era building stories mix memory and legend.
- **Held for review:** press talk of olympiad pupils burning out was weighed as a frame and kept back, since no Romanian study behind it can be named here; the scale adaptations come first.
