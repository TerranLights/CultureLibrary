## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** set block renovations in the capital beside new houses in Teleorman and Argeș villages.
- **Oltenia (Dolj, Gorj):** check which villages gained water and roads under PNDL and which waited, using county lists.
- **Iași and Bukovina:** follow the college tradition from Trei Ierarhi on, and Bukovina houses built with earnings from abroad.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare Lipovan fishing villages' memory of the polders with what irrigation meant to Tatar and Turkish farm villages.
- **The Banat and Crișana:** check how new owners rebuilt Swabian houses left after 1990, and how Slovak villages in Bihor and Nădlac modernized.
- **Maramureș and Oaș:** compare new Romanian and Ukrainian village houses with the wooden ones they replace.
- **Transylvania's Romanians (Cluj, Sibiu, the Apuseni):** read Moldovan's program beside Moți villages' accounts of schooling and roads.
- **Roma:** keep Roma-led assessments of the Decade apart from government reports, noting which changes were asked for.
- **Saxon villages:** check how heritage foundations restore them, and what the remaining Saxons say.
- **Baptist and Pentecostal churches:** read conversion testimonies told as lives made better.
- **The Jewish community:** trace the *haskole* and Romanian Jewish schools of the 19th century in Iași and Bucharest.
- **The Csángó:** Romanian and Hungarian sponsors each frame their Csángó schooling projects as uplift; contested, each a claim.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-run village renewal in Harghita and Covasna, on Hungary's row, as a contrast for PNDL; Moldova's EU-funded projects, for a neighbor's path; migrants' building at home seen from Italy and Spain, for the diaspora's own account.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** solid for programs, surveys and press; mixed for self-help and DIY; scarce for Tatar, Slovak and Armenian sources.
- **Languages needed:** old Romanian and Greek for Trei Ierarhi printings; German for Saxon heritage projects; Romani for Roma assessments; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Moldavian *haskole*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eugenics and the socialist "new man" were imposed improvement; Roma inclusion programs must be read with Roma voices first. Planning and building statutes are Libra's.
- **Held for review:** interwar far-right writings on the "spiritual renewal" of the nation were weighed as an improvement source and kept off this sheet until the author rules how such texts are handled.
