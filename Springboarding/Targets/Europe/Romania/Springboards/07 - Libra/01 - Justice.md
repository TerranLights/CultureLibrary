## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moldavia's and Wallachia's old courts:** read Lupu's 1646 penalties against the Wallachian 1652 code to see where the two lands wrote punishment differently.
- **Transylvania's Saxon seats and Romanian villages under them:** check which court a Romanian serf faced in Saxon land before 1848, and in what language.
- **Bukovina (Suceava) and the Banat (Timișoara):** set Habsburg court records beside the Principalities' for the same decades.
- **Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** trace the switch from *kadı* to Romanian courts after 1878 in Turkish and Tatar property cases.
- **The Jiu Valley and Hunedoara:** look up how miners charged after the 1990s marches were tried.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** keep each group's own dispute hearings apart from their cases before state courts, and let members describe the former.
- **The Jewish community:** follow survivors' testimony from the Iași and Transnistria cases into the 1940s court records.
- **Greek Catholic and Roman Catholic Romanians:** read the regime's charge sheets against their clergy after 1948 beside the churches' own martyrologies.
- **Neo-Protestants and Jehovah's Witnesses:** look for prosecutions under the communist codes for worship or refusing arms.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian defendants in Transylvanian trials after 1956 and 1990, whose files belong to Hungary's row; Moldova's courts, which read some of the same old codes in another state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for codes and transitional justice; mixed for prewar provincial courts; thin for Ottoman Dobruja and Lipovan cases.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for the 1646 code and boyar charters; Ottoman Turkish for the *kadı* registers; German for Saxon seat records; Romani for Roma complainants' statements.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *robie*, the Holocaust under Antonescu, the Iași pogrom and Transnistria, and communist-era repression are contested; give each claim its source and name each community by its own name. Victims' files at CNSAS have access rules. Trust in the courts and what law is for go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** reparations debates for Roma enslavement were noted, but no bill or ruling on them could be named with confidence, so they await a sourced lead.
