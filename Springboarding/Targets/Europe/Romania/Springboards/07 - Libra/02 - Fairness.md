## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest against Oltenian and Muntenian villages:** check whether suits over flats cluster in cities and land suits in villages.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** look in Ipsilanti-era and Moldavian codes for how each land split estates between sons and daughters.
- **Dobruja:** compare Islamic inheritance shares that Turkish and Tatar families follow with the Civil Code's shares, from the families' own accounts.
- **Banat and Crișana (Timișoara, Oradea):** look for Swabian and Slovak single-heir farm customs against equal division among siblings.
- **Maramureș and Bukovina's Ukrainians:** look for school-admission complaints filed in Ukrainian-language schools.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Cluj, Brașov, the Moți, the Jiu Valley):** find how Moți wood rights and Jiu Valley mine pay were argued as fair or not.
- **Roma pupils and families (Kalderash, Rudari, Ursari, settled Roma):** gather each group's own view of reserved places and desegregation orders, not only officials' reports.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** check how church and community property was returned after 1989 compared with private claims.
- **The Jewish community:** follow claims under the restitution laws for communal buildings in Iași and Bucharest.
- **Csángó families in Moldavia:** treat requests for schooling in their own speech as a contested claim, with both sides' sources.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians, whose church-property claims are read from Hungary's row; Moldova, which split collective-farm land under another law; diaspora workers' pay in Italy and Spain, as the yardstick some apply to wages at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and court records; mixed for school measures; thin for Banat Slovak and Lipovan inheritance custom.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian and Greek for Ipsilanti's 1780 code; German for Swabian farm inheritance; Turkish for Dobruja families' inheritance accounts; Romani for families' views on desegregation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restitution pits families against each other and still divides towns; reserved places for Roma are argued over in public. What fairness the law aims at in spirit is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** folk sayings that tie cheating to a particular region or people turned up while choosing search terms; they were left out and should stay out.
