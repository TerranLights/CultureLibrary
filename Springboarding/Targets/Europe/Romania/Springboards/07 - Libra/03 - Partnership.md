## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia against Oltenia:** check whether census cohabitation shares differ between capital districts and Oltenian villages.
- **Iași and Suceava in Moldavia and Bukovina:** look for church-marriage and civil-marriage gaps, county by county.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare Turkish, Tatar and Lipovan rules on marrying outside one's faith, each in its own sources.
- **The Banat and Crișana:** find mixed Serb–Romanian and Swabian–Romanian marriages in parish registers, and which church held the wedding.
- **Maramureș:** look up the tie between Romanian *nășie* and Ukrainian *kumivstvo* where villages sit side by side.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Sibiu's Mărginime, the Moți, Făgăraș):** set the shepherd's seasonal absence beside how couples run a household alone, using local monographs.
- **The Jiu Valley:** see whether miners' work pairs and their families appear in union or mine records.
- **Roma groups:** set the partnership forms Kalderash, Ursari and Rudari describe beside each other, each in its own words.
- **The Saxons:** check in Saxon community papers how the 1990 emigration split or rejoined couples and business partners.
- **Armenians and the Jewish community:** look for marriage-alliance and business-partner patterns in their community archives.
- **Csángó villages:** read the rival Romanian and Hungarian accounts of their wedding partners as contested sources.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian couples in Transylvania, kept for Hungary's row; Moldova, where the same church rite sits under different family law; couples with one partner working in Italy or Spain, compared only for how distance reshapes the bond.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, census and church rites; mixed for business partnerships; thin for Kalderash alliances and Lipovan mixed marriage.
- **Languages needed:** Greek and Latin for the merchant company records; Church Slavonic for older Romanian and Lipovan rites; Romani for the *kumpania*; Turkish for Dobruja marriage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** same-sex partnership is argued over in Parliament and the churches; give each side in its own sources. Violence inside couples belongs with Justice and the law's spirit with Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the closest call was the Greek merchant companies, whose members came from across the Balkans; they stay in because their charters and ledgers sit in Sibiu and Brașov.
