## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** read ministry files against the capital's press for the gap between talks and public account.
- **Oltenia (Craiova):** look at local memory of the 1940 treaty signed there, and who came north from Southern Dobruja.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** check how the 1940 loss of northern Bukovina is taught in Suceava against Iași.
- **Dobruja and the Delta (Constanța, Tulcea):** compare the Turkish, Tatar, Bulgarian-origin and Lipovan bodies' dealings with foreign states.
- **The Banat (Timișoara):** follow Serbs and Banat Bulgarians as cross-border communities in Romanian-Serbian and Romanian-Bulgarian talks.
- **Maramureș and Bukovina's Ukrainians:** see how the 1997 treaty's minority clauses are cited by Ukrainian organizations.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Alba Iulia, Sibiu, Brașov):** trace how 1595 and 1918 are staged in Alba Iulia's commemorations.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** read the emigration deals from the families' side, in their community papers.
- **The Jewish community:** look at the communist-era emigration bargains through community and state records, with each claim sourced.
- **Roma organizations:** find which Roma bodies speak to foreign governments or EU institutions, and on whose behalf.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian parties in Transylvania, whose role in the 1996 treaty is Hungary's row; Moldova, the most sensitive bilateral tie, read here only for Bucharest's side of the talks; diaspora consulates in Italy and Spain, as a measure of how the state treats citizens abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 20th-century treaties and archives; mixed for Porte-era envoys; thin for Lipovan and Tatar community diplomacy.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish and Greek for the *capuchehaie* reports; Latin for the 1595 treaty; French for Titulescu's Geneva speeches; German for the emigration negotiator's account; Bulgarian for the Craiova exchange.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the loss of territory in 1940, payments for emigration, and Moldova are touchy; give each claim its source. What treaties mean to citizens, beyond their text, is for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** in choosing the everyday side, foreign business guides that rank peoples on directness were passed over, since they would speak for Romanians instead of letting them speak.
