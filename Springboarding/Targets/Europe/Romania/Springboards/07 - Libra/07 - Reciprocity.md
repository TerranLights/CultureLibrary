*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest against Muntenian and Oltenian villages:** check whether property-for-care contracts cluster where children moved away.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** look at the godparent gift calendar, comparing Orthodox and Roman Catholic parishes.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** set the Muslim *Kurban* sharing among neighbors beside Lipovan work parties, each in community sources.
- **The Banat and Crișana:** look for Serb and Swabian rules of return help at harvest, in parish and association records.
- **Maramureș:** set Romanian *clacă* records beside Ukrainian villages' in the same valleys: is returned labor counted alike?
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Sibiu's Mărginime, the Moți, Făgăraș, the Jiu Valley):** check whether Romanian villages beside Saxon ones wrote down help rules of their own.
- **Roma groups (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** gather each group's own account of lending and returning between families, rather than outside summaries.
- **Neo-Protestant congregations:** check church rules on mutual help among members, and whether help goes beyond the congregation.
- **The Jewish community and Armenians:** compare their communal lending and aid funds from the 19th century on.
- **The Csángó:** treat claims about their village exchange customs as contested between Romanian and Hungarian sources.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian *kaláka* work in Székely villages, for Hungary's row; Moldova's laws on gifts to officials, for a neighboring text; the diaspora, which enters only where money sent home is answered by care given at home.
