## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** count how many local permits in the capital are granted by tacit approval against other counties, from court and press records.
- **Oltenia:** check where bean diviners are still consulted, using ethnographers' field notes from Gorj and Mehedinți.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** look for omen-book manuscripts copied in monasteries, and who owned them.
- **Dobruja (Constanța, Tulcea):** ask Turkish, Tatar and Lipovan families who has the last word on a marriage or a move, from their own community writing.
- **The Banat (Timișoara) and Crișana (Oradea):** compare Swabian, Serb and Romanian village councils' minutes for items left pending.
- **Maramureș:** set Romanian and Ukrainian customs for answering a marriage proposal side by side.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Cluj, Alba Iulia, the Apuseni, the Jiu Valley):** follow the long-stalled Roșia Montană mining decision through the Moți villages' own accounts.
- **Roma groups (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** look for Roma writers' accounts of who settles a family choice and how long it may wait, group by group.
- **Pentecostal and Baptist congregations:** check how church members describe seeking guidance before a choice, from their own publications.
- **The Jewish community:** follow the waiting before emigration in memoirs, when families weighed leaving over years.
- **Comparison only:** Székely Land's councils belong to Hungary's row; Moldova's tacit-approval law, as a sister text; diaspora families deciding whether to return, which bears on Romania only from the receiving side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and political satire; mixed for exit polls; thin for minority-language terms and for diviners' practice today.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for the omen-book manuscripts; Romanian dialect notes for Oltenian bean divination; Turkish and Russian for Dobruja families' writing; Yiddish for emigration memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roșia Montană divides villages; give each side its source. Divination is practiced by living people, so describe it as they do. Why officials stall, beyond what the law says, is for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** choice-overload psychology was left out after a look, as it would bring a lab finding, not a Romanian source.
