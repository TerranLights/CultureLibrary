*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muntenia and Oltenia:** set Bucharest's funeral homes and cremation against Oltenian village burials.
- **Moldavia and Bukovina:** read Cantemir against later fieldwork from Iași and Suceava counties.
- **Dobruja and the Danube Delta:** in Babadag and Tulcea, compare who washes and buries the dead among Muslims, Lipovans and Orthodox Romanians.
- **Banat and Crișana:** check Serbian *zadušnice*, Swabian All Souls' customs and Romanian *Moși* in the same villages.
- **Maramureș:** compare Romanian and Ukrainian burial and memorial meals in neighboring valleys.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities, from the Moți to the Jiu Valley:** check whether funerals after mine accidents in Petroșani follow a different order from those in Mărginime shepherd villages.
- **Roma groups:** ask each group (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari and others) for its own funeral and memorial order.
- **Greek Catholic, Roman Catholic and neo-Protestant churches:** check how converts handle the Orthodox memorial meals kept by their kin.
- **Jews and Armenians:** look for burial societies and cemeteries in Iași, Botoșani and Gherla.
- **Csángó:** treat funeral studies from Romanian and Hungarian authors as rival claims about the same villages.
- **Comparison only:** Transylvania's Hungarians and Székely Land, whose carved grave posts go to Hungary's row; Moldova, which Cantemir's chapters also cover; diaspora families bringing their dead home from Italy or Spain.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for funeral lore and death statistics; mixed for sex ways and couples' money; thin for Slovak, Czech and Banat Bulgarian death ways.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Cantemir's original; Ottoman Turkish for Babadag's inscriptions; Hebrew and Yiddish for the FCER cemeteries; German for the Sighișoara stones.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Decree 770, the Holocaust dead and Roma burial customs need care; source every claim. Wills and inheritance law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Lands:** Romania's exact lands are still to be settled, so the Scorpio sheets follow the present state, leaving the Székely Land and Transylvania's Hungarians to Hungary's row.
- **Held for review:** experiments on how reminders of death shift attitudes were weighed, but no run on a Romanian sample could be named with confidence.
