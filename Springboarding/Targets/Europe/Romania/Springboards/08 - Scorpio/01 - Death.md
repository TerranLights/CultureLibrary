## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia against Muntenia:** test whether the Gorj songs Brăiloiu printed are still sung, and what Bucharest funerals keep instead.
- **Moldavia and Bukovina:** look in Iași and Suceava diocesan papers for how long mourning dress is kept.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare the *mevlit* among Turks and Tatars, Lipovan remembrance days and Orthodox forty-day services in the same towns.
- **Banat and Crișana:** check Serbian and Banat Bulgarian graveyard days against Romanian ones in Timiș and Bihor villages.
- **Maramureș:** follow Kligman's villages and set Ukrainian laments from the Vișeu valley beside them.
- **Transylvanian Romanians (Sibiu's Mărginime, Făgăraș, the Moți, Hunedoara):** compare returning-dead lore recorded in shepherd and mining places.
- **Roma groups:** let Kalderash, Ursari and Rudari speakers describe mourning separately; Boyash terms may differ.
- **Neo-Protestant converts (Baptists, Pentecostals, Adventists):** ask how they mourn without memorial alms.
- **Jews:** find how the Iași and Transnistria dead are named in yearly services and memorial books.
- **Csángó:** read death-lore studies from both Romanian and Hungarian authors, as claims each makes.
- **Comparison only:** Székely Land's mourning goes to Hungary's row, Moldova's laments are a close parallel only, and the diaspora's dead are counted abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for laments, war graves and statistics; mixed for palliative care; thin for Slovak, Czech and Armenian grief.
- **Languages needed:** old Romanian for Costin's poem; German and Latin for the Saxon sermons; Turkish for *mevlit* texts; Yiddish and Hebrew for Iași memorial lists; Romani for mourning terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Holocaust dead, the Marotinu case and suicide need sourced, sober handling. Assisted dying and burial law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** interwar séance circles in Bucharest came up for contact with the dead; they are left to Pisces' magic ways rather than counted here.
