*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** set backers of Bucharest's anticorruption cases against those calling them persecution.
- **Moldavia (Iași, Vaslui) and Bukovina:** map where monasteries read exorcism prayers and how often families travel there.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare ablution, Lipovan table rules and Orthodox fasting as three purity codes in one county.
- **Banat (Timișoara) and Crișana:** read Swabian memoirs of 1945 beside Serbian and Bulgarian accounts of postwar removals in the same villages.
- **Maramureș:** follow the Baia Mare spill down the Săsar and Lăpuș into Romanian and Ukrainian villages.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Sibiu, Copșa Mică, Alba Iulia, the Apuseni and Moți, Hunedoara):** compare cleanup stories in Copșa Mică and the Apuseni mining areas.
- **Roma groups:** keep Kalderash, Ursari and Rudari purity rules apart; never generalize from one group.
- **Greek Catholics and Roman Catholics:** set their confession and penance practice beside Orthodox *molitfe*.
- **Jewish and Armenian communities:** check how purity rules were kept as numbers fell.
- **Csángó:** Romanian and Hungarian authors both describe Catholic purity and penance customs; read each as a claim.
- **Comparison only:** early-modern witch trials and postwar purges among Transylvania's Hungarians go to Hungary's row; Moldova's Soviet-era purges are a separate case; émigrés pushed out by a purge are diaspora context.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political purges and spills; mixed for church discipline; thin for Tatar, Turkish and Ukrainian purity customs.
- **Languages needed:** old Romanian in Cyrillic for the *cărți de blestem*; German for deportation memoirs; Romani for purity rules; Hebrew for mikveh records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Tanacu, Roma purity rules and the anticorruption fight are contested; present claims as claims. Lustration and anticorruption law belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the Danube quarantine stations of the 1830s were weighed as public-health cleansing and left for the research stage, since whether border sanitary rules count as purging is still open.
