## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia and Muntenia:** compare which *Miorița* variants keep or drop the plot, and whether the plotters' origins are named.
- **Moldavia (Iași):** read Neculce's legends against later Moldavian school readers to see which betrayals were taught.
- **Bukovina (Suceava):** look for Ukrainian and Romanian memories of neighbors who informed after 1944.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** set Müller's novel beside Serbian and Bulgarian Banat memoirs of informers in their own villages.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare how Lipovan priestly and priestless groups speak of the schism, and how Dobrujan Tatar writers treat the 1944 Soviet charge against Crimea's Tatars.
- **Maramureș and Crișana (Oradea):** look for local "traitor" lists drawn up at each wartime change of rule.
- **Transylvanian Romanians (Alba Iulia, Sibiu, Cluj, the Moți, the Jiu Valley):** ask how Michael the Brave's 1601 death is retold at Turda and Alba Iulia.
- **Roma groups:** sources on informing and loyalty inside Roma communities are scarce; ask Roma researchers which Kalderash, Ursari or Rudari accounts exist before citing any.
- **Saxons:** look at how stayers and leavers of 1990 speak of each other.
- **Jewish community:** find testimony on neighbors who denounced or sheltered Jews in Iași and Bukovina.
- **Csángó:** treat claims of disloyalty made about them from either side as contested.
- **Comparison only:** Hungary's row holds the Transylvanian Hungarian view of 1918 and 1940; Moldova reads August 23 through its own Soviet history; Cold War exiles are diaspora context.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for informers, wartime turns and 1989; mixed for infidelity; thin for Ukrainian, Tatar and Roma accounts.
- **Languages needed:** German for Müller and Saxon accounts; Russian and Church Slavonic for the Lipovan schism texts; Turkish and Tatar for the Crimean charge; Romanian chronicle language for Neculce.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** CNSAS files name living people; the 1989 case and August 23 are fought over. Treason and whistleblower law go to Libra and Capricorn.
