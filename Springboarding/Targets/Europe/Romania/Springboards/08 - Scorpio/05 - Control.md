## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oltenia and Muntenia:** compare *Paparuda* records with who performs it now, and whether priests' drought prayers replaced it.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** study forest disputes in Bukovina's Romanian, Ukrainian and Hutsul villages, and who calls the logging out of control.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** set Lipovan fishers' accounts of fishing bans beside the reserve's reports; trace Ada Kaleh families now in Constanța and Turkey.
- **Banat and Crișana:** check how Serbian and Romanian villages along the Danube's Clisura remember the dam's flooding.
- **Maramureș:** follow forest clashes and *solomonari* stories in the same valleys.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Cluj, Brașov, Sibiu's Mărginime, the Apuseni, the Jiu Valley):** compare factory towns' memory of quotas with shepherds' accounts of grazing rules.
- **Roma groups:** let Kalderash families describe the 1980s seizures, and ask whether Ursari and Rudari met other controls.
- **Neo-Protestant and Orthodox families:** check how each speaks of a parent's say over children.
- **Jewish community:** find accounts of passport and emigration control in the 1970s–80s.
- **Csángó:** read claims of church control over language in Moldavian Catholic parishes as contested from both sides.
- **Comparison only:** Székely Land's quarrels over forest ownership belong to Hungary's row; Moldova's Soviet-era controls are a separate history; live-in carers in Italy, whose days employers set, are diaspora context.
