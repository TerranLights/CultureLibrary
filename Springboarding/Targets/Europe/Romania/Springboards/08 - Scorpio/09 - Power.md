## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** compare how outlets whose owners are tied to the energy contracts covered them with untied outlets.
- **Oltenia:** look at party switching among Dolj and Gorj mayors in Expert Forum data.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina:** compare chronicle accounts of Moldavian boyar factions with those from Wallachia.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** see how the Turkish, Tatar and Lipovan seats are won within each community, and who keeps them.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** check Serbian, Bulgarian and Slovak seat-holders and their links to local councils.
- **Crișana (Oradea) and Maramureș:** compare Ukrainian representation in Maramureș with how power is shared in local councils.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (Sibiu, Brașov, Alba Iulia, the Jiu Valley):** ask Sibiu's Romanian voters why they chose German Forum mayors.
- **Roma groups:** do not let one group's leaders stand for all; compare Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari and urban Roma views of the reserved seat.
- **Orthodox and other churches:** use press archives on how church leaders are courted at election time.
- **Jewish and Armenian communities:** check who has held their seats and how small electorates choose.
- **Csángó:** treat claims about who speaks for them, church, Romanian state or Hungarian groups, as contested.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian party power in Transylvania belongs to Hungary's row; Moldova's oligarch politics are a separate case; diaspora voting power, though counted in Romanian elections, is context here.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political history and post-1989 privatization; mixed for local power networks; thin for power inside small communities like the Lipovans and Serbs.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century Romanian for the rival chronicles; German for the Forum's records; Romani for Partida Romilor debates; Turkish, Tatar and Russian for minority-seat politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** energy contracts, media owners and Roma politics involve living people and ongoing cases; present claims as claims. Legitimate authority, hierarchy and power ways belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** claims of a "parallel state" holding hidden power were left out: the sources are mostly partisan, so they wait until they can be tested against court and inquiry records.
