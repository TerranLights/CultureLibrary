## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** compare memories of 1940 and 1977 with responses to RO-ALERT warnings today.
- **Oltenia:** look at relic processions in drought years in Dolj and Olt, and who organizes them.
- **Moldavia (Iași, Vrancea) and Bukovina:** check how villages near the Vrancea epicenter speak of earthquakes beside Bucharest.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** see how Lipovan, Turkish and Tatar villages met the 1990s inflation and fishing crises; sources are scarce, so ask community unions.
- **Banat:** trace the embargo years through Serbian, Romanian and Czech villages along the Danube gorge.
- **Crișana and Maramureș:** compare how the 2009 crisis hit border towns that trade with neighbors.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities (the Jiu Valley, Hunedoara, the Apuseni, Cluj):** set mine-disaster towns against towns where a factory closed.
- **Roma groups:** let Roma residents of quarantined neighborhoods describe 2020 in their own words; never infer from press framing.
- **Ukrainians and Hutsuls:** ask what the 1986 cloud meant for Bukovina and Maramureș pastures, from their own testimony.
- **Neo-Protestant churches:** look at how congregations organize help in a local crisis.
- **Csángó:** treat accounts of how their villages weathered the 1990s as contested between authors.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-language accounts of the 1940 quake in Transylvania belong to Hungary's row; Moldova's 1990s crisis is a parallel; remittances sent home from Spain and Italy after 2008 are diaspora context.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for earthquakes, economic crises and mine disasters; mixed for personal crisis; thin for Turkish, Tatar and Lipovan experience of hard times.
- **Languages needed:** chronicle Romanian for the plague and locust entries; Serbian for embargo accounts; Romani for 2020 testimony; Ukrainian for Bukovina memories of 1986.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2020 quarantines and embargo smuggling can be told in ways that blame communities; give each claim its source. How the state may act in an emergency is Libra's and Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** nothing outside the rules was set aside; a possible pointer on private crisis lines was dropped instead, because no line for adults in crisis could be named with confidence.
