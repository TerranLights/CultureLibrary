# Romania: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian (with old and regional forms); Romani; German; Ukrainian; Lipovan Russian; Serbian; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state, and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muntenia:** read the Islaz program against what Wallachian peasants in Teleorman and Olt gained in 1864 and how 1907 is told there.
- **Oltenia:** check whether Tudor Vladimirescu's 1821 rising is recalled locally as a freedom story or a peasant one.
- **Moldavia (Iași):** set Kogălniceanu's emancipation speeches beside Moldavian Roma families' own memory.
- **Bukovina:** check how families recall Habsburg-era dues and rights against Moldavia's.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** separate the Lipovans' freedom of worship story from Turkish and Tatar accounts of emigration and staying.
- **Banat:** collect border-escape memories from Romanian, Serbian and Swabian villages along the frontier.
- **Crișana:** Oradea's neo-Protestant congregations are a place to ask about religious freedom before and after 1989.
- **Maramureș:** look at how the 1940s partisan groups and Ukrainian villages recall the postwar years.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** Țara Făgărașului for the resistance memoirs; the Apuseni for the Horea rising of 1784; Brașov for the 1987 workers' revolt.
- **Roma groups:** ask each group whether 20 February is marked, and how, rather than assuming one memory of *robie*.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** compare those who left under *Freikauf* with those who stayed.
- **Jews:** look for community memoirs of the years spent waiting for permission to leave for Israel.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian accounts of 1848 in Transylvania (Hungary's row); Moldova's Soviet-era stories of release; exiles in Paris and Munich, used above only for what they broadcast home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1848, 1989 and dissent; mixed for Roma memory of emancipation; thin for Slovak, Czech and Banat Bulgarian freedom stories.
- **Languages needed:** German for the *Freikauf* files; Russian for *Zorile* and Lipovan histories; Romani for 20 February events; Serbian for Banat border memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *robie*, the mountain resistance's Legionary links and the disputed deaths of December 1989 are contested; give each claim with its source. Laws on speech, worship and assembly go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Romani songs about bondage were sought as an oral pointer from before 1856; none could be confirmed as recorded that early, so the search stays open.
