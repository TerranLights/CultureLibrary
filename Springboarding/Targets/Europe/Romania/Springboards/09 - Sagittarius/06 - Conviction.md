## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** separate the 1990 University Square protesters' accounts from those who opposed them.
- **Oltenia and Muntenia:** look for parish priests who refused to sign or inform under communism, from diocesan memory.
- **Moldavia:** test whether Iași's student movements tell their stands in the same terms as Bucharest's.
- **Bukovina and Maramureș:** compare Orthodox and Greek Catholic families' accounts of who held out in 1948.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** record what Lipovans say about keeping old-rite rules, beside the rules Turkish and Tatar mosque communities set for themselves.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** compare the 1956 student files with December 1989 memories.
- **Crișana:** Oradea's Adventist and Baptist members can speak to keeping rules at school.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** the Apuseni for Roșia Montană; Blaj for Micu-Klein; the Jiu Valley for strikes.
- **Roma groups:** ask convert pastors, Orthodox Roma families and secular activists separately what holding firm means, and keep the answers apart.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** ask both leavers and stayers how they weigh the choice to stay.
- **Jews:** the Rosen debate is held inside the community; find both sides there.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian Reformed pastor László Tőkés's 1989 stand in Timișoara, which Hungary's row covers, matters here only for how Romanian neighbors joined it; Moldova's 1989 protests; diaspora exiles who refused to return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dissent and protest; mixed for religious rule-keeping; thin for Ukrainian, Turkish and Tatar accounts of holding firm.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and German for Visarion's and Micu-Klein's Habsburg files; German for the pastors' memoirs; Russian for Lipovan rules; Romani for the interwar Roma press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1744–61 church quarrels, abortion refusals and the Rosen debate are contested; quote no verdict. CNSAS access rules apply. Conscience law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** prison "saints" venerated by some circles after 1990 came up; their movement ties are disputed, and a fair pointer would need named sources from both sides, which could not be given with confidence.
