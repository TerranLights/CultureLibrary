# Romania: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian; Romani; German; Ukrainian; Polish (Bukovina); Lipovan Russian; Turkish; Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the road, the foreign and the traveler are regarded (short journeys stay with Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** look at who takes foreign holidays and who goes only to the seaside or the mountains.
- **Oltenia:** follow the 1990s seasonal labor routes from Dolj and Olt to Spain through county migration data.
- **Moldavia:** ask returnees in Iași county how they describe the journey to Italy: sacrifice, escape, or in other words of their own.
- **Bukovina:** compare Cacica pilgrims (Polish and Romanian) with Orthodox pilgrims at the painted monasteries.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** set Turkish and Tatar emigration stories beside Lipovan guiding and the Constanța port.
- **Banat:** read Leigh Fermor's hosts against Swabian and Serbian village histories of the same years.
- **Crișana and Maramureș:** compare Oaș and Maramureș house-building with what the builders' children say.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** trace the routes of Mărginimea shepherds, Apuseni migrants and Jiu Valley workers one at a time.
- **Roma groups:** let each group tell its own journeys; avoid outsiders' framing of Roma as wanderers.
- **Jews:** emigration through Constanța and by later bargained exit; seek community memoirs.
- **Ukrainians:** map the destinations named in Ukrainian villages of Maramureș and Suceava apart from their Romanian neighbors'.
- **Comparison only:** the Romanian diaspora in Italy, Spain, Germany and the UK, seen here only through those who return; Moldova's labor migrants, who use some of the same routes; Székely Land seasonal migration, for Hungary's row.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor migration and tourism; mixed for pilgrimage; thin for Polish, Ukrainian and Roma travel accounts in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian and Greek for the *proschinitare*; German for the Karpatenverein records; Turkish for the *muhacir* histories; Hebrew and Yiddish for *Struma* memorial material; Polish for Cacica.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *Struma*, Roma mobility and migrants' work abroad are open to stereotype; quote families, not press labels. Border and residence law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** a pre-1800 Romani song of the road was looked for and could not be placed with a date or collector, so it is not listed.
