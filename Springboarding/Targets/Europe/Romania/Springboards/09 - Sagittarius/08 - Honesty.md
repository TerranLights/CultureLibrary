## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** compare bribery reports for city hospitals and town halls with those in small towns.
- **Muntenia and Oltenia:** look at market trade in Craiova and Pitești for how sellers and buyers check weight and price.
- **Moldavia:** check what Phanariot-era Iași records say about office and payment, beside Wallachian ones.
- **Bukovina:** Habsburg-era officials left their own reports; set them beside Moldavian ones on the same problems.
- **Dobruja:** ask Turkish and Tatar traders in Constanța how a deal is sealed by word, in their own terms.
- **Banat and Crișana:** compare tax-compliance and receipt-lottery uptake in Timiș and Bihor with poorer counties.
- **Maramureș:** look for village customs on returning lost animals or goods.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** Cluj and Sibiu universities each publish integrity codes; check how students there describe cheating.
- **Roma groups:** ask Roma traders how they build trust with buyers, in their own words, and set aside outsiders' accusations.
- **Saxons and Swabians:** look for village rules that fined lying or false weight, as the communities kept them.
- **Jews:** Iași and Bucharest merchant memoirs may speak of credit kept on one's word; check before 1940.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-speaking Transylvanian towns (Hungary's row) share some surveys; Moldova's bribery figures are a separate state's; returning migrants' views of honesty abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption and tax measures; mixed for everyday honesty; thin for Ukrainian, Serbian and Banat Bulgarian sayings on lying.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for *Floarea darurilor*; Ottoman Turkish and Greek for the *peșcheș* records; Romani for terms on lying and trust; Turkish for Dobruja traders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** corruption talk breeds stereotypes of whole groups, above all Roma; quote no group trait. Bribery and tax law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a well-known saying that ties cheating to getting ahead was weighed for the search terms and left out, since quoting it bare risks reading as a trait of a people.
