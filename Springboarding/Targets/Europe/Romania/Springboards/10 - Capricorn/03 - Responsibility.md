*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** check whether blame after city disasters falls on the mayor, the ministry or the firm in local press.
- **Muntenia and Oltenia:** look for old *dușegubina* cases and see whether villages kept any custom of answering jointly.
- **Moldavia and Bukovina:** compare Orthodox confession manuals with Old Believer ones on owning a sin.
- **Dobruja:** set Turkish, Tatar and Romanian school materials side by side for lessons on looking after shared things.
- **Banat:** check Swabian and Serb village histories for who answered for wartime and postwar wrongs.
- **Maramureș:** see how Ukrainian and Romanian parishes keep accounts of church funds and who audits them.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns and the Saxon villages near them:** set visitation reports beside Romanian parish records to see how each side held its clergy to account.
- **The Jiu Valley:** trace who was blamed for mine accidents in miners' and officials' accounts.
- **Roma groups:** check whether a family or the person is asked to answer for a member's wrong, in each group's own words.
- **Neo-Protestant churches:** compare how a member's fault is confessed before the congregation.
- **Csángó:** treat accounts of who answers for their schooling in their language as rival claims.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian Lutheran and Reformed visitations in Transylvania, kept for Hungary's row as its own churches' practice; Moldova's own reckoning with deportations; diaspora parents answering to foreign child-welfare offices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, surveys and church records; mixed for workplace blame; thin for Lipovan and Banat Bulgarian sources.
- **Languages needed:** Slavonic and old Cyrillic Romanian to read the *dușegubina* charters; early modern German and Latin for the visitations; Yiddish for community records in the Antonescu-era debates.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Holocaust responsibility is contested; name the source for each claim. Malpractice files involve patients; use decisions, not case details.
- **Held for review:** a pointer on the age of criminal responsibility was dropped, since the letter of the law belongs to Libra, and no source on how that age is argued about could be named apart from the code.
