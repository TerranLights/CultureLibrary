# Romania: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian (with the chancery formulas of the old charters); Romani; German; Ukrainian; Lipovan Russian; Turkish; Serbian. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** check whether top-school placement data favor the capital's districts over Ilfov villages.
- **Oltenia and Muntenia:** look for political families in county councils and how local press treats them.
- **Moldavia (Iași):** compare medical-school and residency results with graduates' later moves abroad.
- **Bukovina:** read Habsburg-era school records for how rank was earned by exam before 1918.
- **Dobruja:** set the *hafız* title, Lipovan chanting skill and Romanian school prizes side by side as kinds of earned standing.
- **Banat and Crișana:** compare German-, Serbian- and Slovak-language schools' entry rules.
- **Maramureș:** check whether Ukrainian-language pupils sit the same national exams on equal terms.
- **Transylvania's Romanian towns and villages:** follow teacher-exam results by county and the gap between city and Apuseni schools.
- **The Jiu Valley:** look at how mining once rewarded seniority and danger, and what replaced it.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Ursari, Rudari):** ask how each group recognizes skill in a trade or in music, in members' own terms.
- **Neo-Protestant congregations:** check how they choose preachers: by gift, by study or by election.
- **Csángó:** treat school-access claims tied to language as disputed.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian-language university tracks in Cluj and Târgu Mureș, kept for Hungary's row; Moldova's students sitting Romanian exams for scholarships; doctors' credentials weighed in the diaspora's host countries.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams and their results; mixed for hiring; thin for Lipovan, Serbian and Slovak schools' entry rules.
- **Languages needed:** old Cyrillic Romanian and Slavonic for the charter formula; Turkish for *hafız* ceremonies; German for the DSD materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** leaked exams and favoritism cases name real people; cite rulings and official reports first.
- **Held for review:** the hiring experiments with Roma and non-Roma names were weighed here, but they test discrimination more than merit, and situation testing of hiring is already a pointer on Libra's Impartiality sheet.

5. Find the Special Eurobarometer on corruption's Romanian factsheet and copy the connections items.
