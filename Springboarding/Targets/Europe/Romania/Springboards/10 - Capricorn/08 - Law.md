## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest:** compare trust in courts among people who have been to one and people who never have.
- **Muntenia and Oltenia:** look for land quarrels that kin kept out of court.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** set Tatar *adet*, Lipovan church rule and fishermen's unwritten codes beside state rules on the water.
- **Banat, Crișana and Bukovina:** test whether Habsburg-era order is invoked when people judge today's officials, against Iași and the old Moldavian lands.
- **Maramureș:** compare logging and border-smuggling memories in Ukrainian and Romanian villages.
- **The Hațeg country and Hunedoara:** trace the old *ius valachicum* districts against local memory.
- **The Apuseni Moți and the Jiu Valley:** check how miners and woodcutters speak of forest and mine rules.
- **Roma groups:** where a group keeps its own hearings, ask members how they weigh them against state courts.
- **Neo-Protestant churches:** look at teaching on obeying the state and where conscience comes first.
- **The Jewish community:** follow how memory of the 1940s laws shapes its stance toward the state's law.
- **Csángó:** treat claims about their legal treatment as rival accounts.
- **Comparison only:** the Székely Land's arguments over self-rule as a legal claim, a matter for Hungary's row; Moldova, whose share of the old Moldavian custom met Soviet law; diaspora Romanians judging Italian or Spanish courts against home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and political argument; mixed for discretion in practice; thin for Tatar custom and Lipovan rules.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the *ius valachicum* charters; Crimean Tatar for *adet*; Romani for any hearing members choose to describe.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the anticorruption drive, the Securitate's legacy and Roma hearings are contested; name sources, not verdicts. The *ius valachicum* sits inside the Transylvanian continuity debates; present each claim as a claim.
