# Romania: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian (with Banat and Maramureș usage); Kalderash Romani; Banat Swabian German; Ukrainian; Lipovan Russian; Turkish; Yiddish; Old Church Slavonic for the early charters. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** what took over the parish's role when the *mahala* gave way to the block estate.
- **Oltenia:** who votes in the *moșneni* forest commons of Gorj and Vâlcea, and who is left out.
- **Moldavia (Iași, Vrancea):** the *răzeși* commons beside the Jewish *târg* towns whose kehile life ended in the 1940s.
- **Bukovina (Suceava):** work parties in Ukrainian and Hutsul villages near Câmpulung Moldovenesc.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** keep apart old Constanța's Turkish *mahalle*, Tatar villages and Lipovan lagoon settlements.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** the Romanian *ruga* against Serb *slava* gatherings, Swabian Kirchweih and Banat Bulgarian feasts in Dudeștii Vechi.
- **Crișana (Oradea):** test how far a Baptist or Pentecostal congregation stands in for the village in Bihor.
- **Maramureș:** read *toloka* and *clacă* side by side across a Ukrainian and a Romanian valley.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** check the Jiu Valley colonies, Mărginimea Sibiului, the Moți, Făgăraș and Brașov's Șchei one by one before grouping them.
- **Roma groups:** what binds families in a Kalderash *kumpania*, set beside settled Ursari or Rudari neighborhoods.
- **Csángó of Moldavia:** note how rival scholars draw the community's edge, by faith or by language, as claims.
- **Comparison only:** the common forests of Székely villages, a Hungarian-row parallel to the *obști*; Moldova's *răzeși* villages, across the Prut; diaspora parishes in Italy and Spain, where village communities partly re-form.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the historic village commune; mixed for urban quarters; thin for the Lipovan *mir* and for Roma-authored writing on the *kumpania*.
- **Languages needed:** Old Church Slavonic and Cyrillic-script Romanian for the DRH charters; German for Kirchweih and Habaner records; Ukrainian for Ruscova valley sources; Yiddish and Hebrew for the yizkor books not yet translated.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the church disputes are live; give both sides' sources. The planned razing of villages is told in rival ways; claims as claims. The law of associations belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** interwar movement "work camps" that billed themselves as village community-building came up; they belong to the contested Legionary movement, so how they are sourced is left for the author.
