## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** measure the income gap between the capital and Teleorman or Giurgiu, and how each side speaks of it.
- **Oltenia:** look at Rudari and Romanian households in Dolj for who gets land, schooling and village posts.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina:** follow the citizenship debate in Iași's Jewish press, and Ukrainian schooling in Bukovina after 1918.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** compare how Turks, Tatars, Lipovans and Romanian settlers entered the state after 1878 and 1913.
- **Banat and Crișana (Timișoara, Oradea):** see whether the Fărșang inversion and Swabian, Serb and Slovak village life kept rank lines softer or sharper.
- **Maramureș:** the 1918 shift for Ukrainian villages from one state's citizenship to another.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** the *Supplex* claim in Blaj and Alba Iulia; the Moți's grievances; Saxon privilege as the comparison inside the same towns; miners' wages in the Jiu Valley.
- **Roma groups:** compare former monastery-owned, boyar-owned and state-owned Roma families, where records allow.
- **Women and men:** pay, housework and political office, checked for Roma, Romanian and minority women separately.
- **Churches:** Orthodox, Greek Catholic, Roman Catholic and neo-Protestant standing before the state, sourced from each church.
- **Csángó:** weigh the Council of Europe text against Romanian and Csángó voices as claims.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian demands for equal language rights in Transylvania, which are Hungary's row; Moldova's own minority debates; Romanians abroad who report unequal treatment in Italy and Spain.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emancipation history and income data; mixed for gender and leveling customs; thin for Lipovan and Tatar views of equality in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Andreanum; French for Iancu; Yiddish for Jewish press on citizenship; Turkish and Crimean Tatar for Dobruja community sources; Romani for E-Romnja interviews kept in the original.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Roma slavery and its legacy, the Holocaust under Antonescu and Csángó identity are contested; name each community by its own name and give claims as claims. Anti-discrimination law and the council's rulings belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the 1907 peasants' revolt was weighed for equality of outcome; its land demands sit between this concept and Reform, so it is held until the author places it.
