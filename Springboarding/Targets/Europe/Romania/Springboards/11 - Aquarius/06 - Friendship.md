# Romania: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Romania, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Romanian (Moldavian *cumătru*, Transylvanian *bade*); Vlax Romani; Saxon German dialect; Banat Serbian; Ukrainian of Maramureș; Turkish and Crimean Tatar; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** test whether newcomers to the capital make friends through work, faith or hometown circles.
- **Oltenia:** look at whether *nași* in Dolj and Gorj villages come from friends or from richer patrons.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina:** compare the Moldavian *cumetrie* with the Ukrainian *kum* in Bukovina's villages.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** record friendships across Turkish, Tatar, Lipovan and Romanian households in mixed towns, and what each side calls them.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** Serb *kumstvo*, Romanian *nășie* and Banat Bulgarian Catholic sponsorship in shared villages.
- **Crișana (Oradea):** whether Baptist and Pentecostal "brother" and "sister" ties replace or join older friendships.
- **Maramureș:** sworn brotherhood customs in Romanian and Ukrainian valleys, as each records them.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** the shepherds of Mărginimea Sibiului, the miners of the Jiu Valley, the Moți, Cluj's students and Brașov's Șchei; check where friendships are made in each.
- **Roma groups:** ask whether Kalderash, Ursari and Rudari families choose co-parents inside or outside their own group.
- **Jewish community and its neighbors:** friendships named in Righteous files, set beside survivors' own accounts.
- **Csángó:** godparent choices across language lines, presented as each side reports them.
- **Comparison only:** Hungarian and Székely godparent customs, part of Hungary's row; Moldova's *cumătrism*, a debate across the Prut; diaspora friend networks in Italy and Spain, which show what friendships migrate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for godparenthood and ritual brotherhood; mixed for survey data; thin for Lipovan and Tatar friendship customs in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Cyrillic-script Romanian for the *înfrățire* charters; German dialect for the Saxon dictionary; Serbian for *kumstvo* sources; Romani for *kirvipe* interviews; Hebrew for Yad Vashem files kept untranslated.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Holocaust under Antonescu: rescuers' files are not the whole story, so survivors' testimony comes first. Favors between friends can shade into corruption; describe practices, not people.
- **Held for review:** friendships among children raised in the communist-era state homes would be told best by the survivors, but their accounts are bound up with abuse; how far this sheet should reach into them is the author's decision.
