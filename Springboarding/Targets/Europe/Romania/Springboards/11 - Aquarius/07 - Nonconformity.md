## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bucharest and Muntenia:** Club A and the Pride march give two generations' sites of standing out in one city.
- **Oltenia:** look at how villages around Caracal and Dolj speak of the Căluș's *Mutul* off the dance ground.
- **Moldavia (Iași) and Bukovina (Suceava):** Moinești's Dada sons and the Slătioara Old Calendarists, two kinds of break from the same region.
- **Dobruja and the Delta:** the Lipovan accords trace their origin to a refusal; check how they handle dissent of their own.
- **Banat (Timișoara):** the Aktionsgruppe and the Phoenix rock band's city; check how Swabian and Romanian circles each treated their rebels.
- **Crișana (Oradea):** neo-Protestant converts under communism, called *sectanți*, in Bihor's own records.
- **Maramureș:** the Ukrainian and Romanian valleys' ways of handling a villager who will not marry or attend church.
- **Transylvania's Romanian communities:** Cornea's Cluj, the 1744 refusers around Sibiu and Alba Iulia, the miners of the Jiu Valley who struck in 1977 and the Moți; check each break and its price.
- **Roma groups:** how each sanctions a member who breaks its rules, studied only through Roma-led or Roma-consenting work.
- **Jewish community:** the freethinker, the Zionist and the observant as each other's nonconformists.
- **Csángó:** whose rules the Csángó are said to break, presented as rival claims.
- **Comparison only:** the Hungarian Unitarians and Sabbatarians of Transylvania, a dissent tradition in Hungary's row; Moldova's own Old Calendarists; diaspora punk and metal scenes abroad, which formed elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for communist-era dissidents and religious dissent; mixed for subcultures; thin for Lipovan and Tatar views of the nonconformist.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-century Romanian and Latin for the 1744 and 1759 reports; German for the Aktionsgruppe's texts; Russian for Lipovan church writing; French for Tzara's early Romanian reception abroad.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** psychiatric confinement of dissidents, the Old Calendarists' persecution and LGBT history touch living people and families; claims as claims. Criminal statutes on sexuality belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** a pointer to dissenters jailed in the 1950s was dropped from the body: the prison memoirs mix in movement loyalties that need sources from several sides first.
