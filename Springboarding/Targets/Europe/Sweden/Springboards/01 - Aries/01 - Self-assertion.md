# Sweden: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish; Finnish and Meänkieli; the Sámi languages; Bosnian, Persian, Arabic and Yiddish where a community's own word is needed. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Malmö and Skåne against Stockholm and the Mälardalen:** check press and forum use of *ta för sig* and *framfusig* by city, since the same act may be named differently.
- **Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** check union-meeting and dockside records, where collective speaking up has its own archive.
- **Norrbotten, Västerbotten and Jämtland:** check rural meeting minutes (*byastämma*, village meetings), an older forum with its own order of speaking.
- **Sámi, by language area and by herding or non-herding:** check how claims are put forward in Sámi organizations against how they are put before Swedish authorities.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check generational accounts of being told to keep quiet in school, which bear on when voice was claimed later.
- **Bosniaks, Iranians, Iraqis and Syrians:** check whether the community's own words for brashness and pride are used in Swedish-language settings, or kept apart.
- **Jewish community and Yiddish speakers:** check how *khutspe* is used inside the community and in Swedish media.
- **Chilean exiles of 1973:** check their associations' petitions and open letters as a record of political claiming in a new country.
- **Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations against Church of Sweden parishes:** check who may testify or speak in services.
- **Women and men at work:** check salary-negotiation surveys by gender from the unions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in corpora, press and consumer records; thin for how speaking up works inside Sámi, Roma and Traveller gatherings, which have little written record.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish for nearly all of it; Bosnian, Persian and Arabic to read community media on brashness and pride; Finnish and Meänkieli for school memories in the Torne Valley.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *jantelagen* is often used as a ready-made national self-description; trace who uses it and when, and do not take it as a finding.
- **Held for review:** Sandemose's 1933 novel that coined the Jante rules was left to the Norway and Denmark rows, so only its Swedish reception is pointed to; no South Asian-rule pointer arose here.
