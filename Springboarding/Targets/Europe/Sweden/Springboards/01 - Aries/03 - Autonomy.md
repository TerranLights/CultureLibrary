# Sweden: Autonomy (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish; North Sámi and the other Sámi languages for collective self-rule; Finnish; Swedish Sign Language; Arabic, Persian and Somali for family-decision material. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gotland:** check how island self-rule is narrated from *Guta saga* to the present Region Gotland.
- **Sámi *samebyar* in Norrbotten, Västerbotten and Jämtland, and non-herding Sámi:** check who decides over land use in each account, since herding and non-herding Sámi are placed differently.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check memories of school language bans as a record of choices taken away, and the later language rights work.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the Travellers (*resande*):** check community organizations' accounts of custody, housing and registration.
- **Disabled people and the Deaf community:** check personal assistance users' groups and the Deaf movement's own history of the 1981 recognition of sign language.
- **Older people in inland Norrland against Stockholm:** check care-user surveys by municipality, since distance changes what choice is possible.
- **Young adults in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö:** check housing queues and their effect on moving out (the move itself is the Independence sheet).
- **Iraqi, Syrian, Somali, Kurdish and Iranian families:** check community-run family-counseling and youth projects for how spouse and career choices are discussed within families.
- **Laestadians and the free churches:** check their own writings on conscience and lay leadership.
- **Workers by sector:** check union reports on monitoring and scheduling apps.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for work, care and children's surveys; the history of sterilization, Roma policy and Sámi land is documented but heavily disputed in how it is told.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; North and South Sámi for *sameby* material; Finnish and Meänkieli for school memories; Arabic, Persian and Somali to read community family-counseling material directly.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *hedersrelaterat våld och förtryck* (honor-related violence and oppression) is a policy category in Sweden; check how it is defined, who uses it and how the named communities answer it, and do not attach it to any group as a trait.
- **Held for review:** two legal pointers on this concept (a 2020 court ruling on Sámi hunting and fishing rights, and the personal-assistance statute) were set aside for Libra; nothing was set aside under the South Asian rule.
