## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Östergötland and Uppland:** check runestone praise formulas region by region, since stone-raisers chose different words.
- **Skåne, Blekinge and Halland:** check how *snapphanar* appear in local museums and school texts against national histories.
- **Gotland, Bohuslän and the coasts:** check sea-rescue logs and fishing-village memorials, where danger at sea frames bravery.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen and the mountain north:** check mountain-rescue volunteer records and avalanche accounts.
- **Sámi of each language area:** check tale collections and herders' own accounts of storms and predators, as shared.
- **Sweden Finns and Tornedalians:** check how *sisu* is used of oneself in community media, and whether Meänkieli has its own word.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the Travellers (*resande*):** check activists' memoirs and community radio for who is held up as brave.
- **Jewish community:** check memoirs of survivors who came in 1945 and their accounts of rescuers.
- **Eritreans, Somalis, Kurds, Iranians, Bosniaks:** check exile and arrival memoirs for courage in flight and in political opposition.
- **Chilean exiles:** check exile associations' commemorations of resistance after 1973.
- **Free churches and the labor movement:** check conscientious-objection records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military memory, rescues and literature; thin for courage as named inside the smaller minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; runic and Old Swedish for inscriptions; Finnish for Sisuradio; Tigrinya, Somali and Kurdish for flight and exile memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Second World War rescue and neutrality are contested ground; present the White Buses and Wallenberg stories as accounts with their critics.
- **Held for review:** the statutes of state orders and medals for valor were kept out because their award rules are law and belong to Capricorn; no South Asian-rule item came up on this sheet.
