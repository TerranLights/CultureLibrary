# Sweden: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish and late medieval Swedish; Finnish and Meänkieli; North and South Sámi; Kurdish, Tigrinya and Somali where independence carries a homeland meaning. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna, Jämtland and Hälsingland summer-pasture areas:** check *fäbod* records for which tasks households did alone and which in common.
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten inland against Stockholm:** check distances to services against living-alone rates, since remoteness changes what independence demands.
- **Jämtland:** check how the mock republic is described by its organizers and by critics.
- **Värmland's Forest Finn districts:** check the Finnskogen material for how self-provisioning is remembered.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check food-network and *sameby* sources for collective against household self-sufficiency, as Sámi bodies state it.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check labor-migration memoirs from the 1960s and 1970s for how earning one's own way is told.
- **Kurds, Eritreans and Somalis:** check community associations for how homeland independence movements sit beside personal independence in Sweden; present each as the community's own account.
- **Estonian Swedes and Baltic refugees:** check memoirs on the 1945-46 extradition, which bear on how far refugees could count on Swedish protection.
- **Young adults in Uppsala, Lund and Gothenburg:** check student-housing queues against moving-out ages.
- **Older people living alone:** check PRO and municipal care data by county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for living arrangements, student finance and welfare use; mixed for the neutrality record, which is archived but disputed in how it is read.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (including fifteenth- and sixteenth-century texts); Finnish and Meänkieli for labor-migration memoirs; North Sámi for food-sovereignty material; Kurdish and Tigrinya for homeland-independence publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Second World War neutrality (transit of German troops, iron-ore exports) is contested ground; present it as competing accounts. *Bidragsberoende* is a political charge; record who uses it.
- **Held for review:** small groups campaigning for Skåne's separation were weighed and left out as unreliable starting points (Skåne's regional identity is in the Identity sheet); nothing on this sheet fell under the South Asian rule.
