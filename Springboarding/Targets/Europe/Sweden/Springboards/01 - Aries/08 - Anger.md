# Sweden: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish and Old Swedish; North Sámi; Finnish; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Bosnian, Somali and Chilean Spanish for anger words used inside newer communities. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland and Dalarna:** check how each province commemorates its uprisings (statues, plays, school units), since the memory may be cherished or played down.
- **Västernorrland (Ådalen):** check labor-movement commemorations against local family memory.
- **Stockholm's Million Programme suburbs (Husby, Rinkeby, Rågsved) against the inner city:** check press framing of collective anger by district.
- **Gothenburg:** check accounts of 2001 by protesters, police and residents side by side.
- **Sámi herding families:** check Sámi media and organizations for anger over predator policy and land use, as Sámi state it.
- **Tornedalians:** check the truth commission's hearings for anger over school assimilation.
- **Roma:** check community responses to the police register revealed in 2013, as their organizations present them.
- **Chilean exiles, Kurds and Iranians:** check exile associations' protest archives, where anger at home regimes was organized in Sweden.
- **Football supporters (AIK, Djurgården, Hammarby, Malmö FF):** check fanzines and club codes of conduct for how rivalry anger is channeled.
- **Women and men:** check surveys and helpline data for gendered patterns of anger reported at home and at work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history, film and survey data; thin for everyday display rules by region and for anger words in the smaller minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with Old Swedish); North Sámi for Sámi media; Persian, Kurdish and Spanish for exile protest archives; Somali and Arabic for community youth projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** riots in suburbs are integration-debate ground; give residents' accounts beside official ones, and never let a riot stand for a group. Domestic-violence data hold personal cases.
- **Held for review:** crime-council violence statistics were set aside so that criminal law stays with Libra and Capricorn; no South Asian-rule item was set aside for this concept.
