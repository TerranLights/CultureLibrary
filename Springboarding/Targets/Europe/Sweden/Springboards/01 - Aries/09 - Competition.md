## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gotland and Dalarna:** check how island games and church-boat races are justified (heritage, sport, tourism), since revived contests may serve different ends.
- **Skellefteå, Luleå and Örnsköldsvik hockey towns against Stockholm clubs:** check local press for how a town's standing is tied to its team.
- **Stockholm and Gothenburg football derbies:** check supporter writing for rivalry codes.
- **Småland (Jönköping):** check whether esports draws a different social mix from club sport.
- **Sámi:** check Sámi sports associations for which contests they hold and how herding skills are ranked.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Finnish-language club histories and cross-border Torne Valley contests.
- **Bosniaks, Kurds, Iranians, Somalis, Eritreans:** check SCB or RF data on club founding and youth participation by background, as numbers, never as traits.
- **Free churches and Laestadians:** check their own publications on sport, betting and contests on Sundays.
- **Girls and boys:** check RF's participation and dropout figures by sex and age.
- **Pupils in independent and municipal schools:** check grade distributions by school type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in sport, broadcasting and school data; thinner on envy and status contests outside sport, which need ethnographic and press sources.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; Latin for Olaus Magnus; North Sámi for Sámi sports associations; Finnish for Torne Valley clubs; Bosnian, Kurdish and Persian for club histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1997 reality-show death concerns a named refugee and his family; handle it through the public debate, not personal details. School-competition data touch the segregation debate.
