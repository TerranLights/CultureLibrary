## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inland Norrbotten and Västerbotten against Stockholm and the Mälardalen:** check road deaths and snowmobile accidents per capita, since distance and terrain shape exposure as much as choice.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen and Dalarna mountain districts:** check avalanche and mountain-rescue reports for residents against visitors.
- **Bohuslän, the Stockholm archipelago, Gotland and Öland:** check summer boating and drowning data by place.
- **Skåne and the southern plains:** check *raggare* and moped-car (*A-traktor*) culture against northern towns, through local press.
- **Sámi herding families:** check occupational-injury studies and herders' organizations for how danger in the work is described from inside.
- **Tornedalians:** check river and ice accident memory in the Torne Valley press and local museums.
- **Newly arrived families (Syrians, Iraqis, Somalis, Eritreans):** check swimming-skill and drowning-prevention projects as a question of access to lessons, never as a trait.
- **Young men and young women:** check CAN and Trafikverket splits by sex and age.
- **Temperance families, free churches and the Church of Sweden:** check IOGT-NTO and church youth material on drinking and risk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official safety, health and accident statistics; thinner on how risk-takers themselves speak of it, which needs forums, memoirs and subculture press.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and Old Swedish; Finnish for Torne Valley sources; North Sámi for herders' accounts; Arabic, Bosnian and Persian for drowning-prevention and youth projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** individual accident and drowning cases involve named dead; use aggregate data. Crime fiction set in suburbs feeds the integration debate; read reception critically.
- **Held for review:** drink-driving and narcotics statutes, with their court data, were routed to Libra and Capricorn rather than used here; no pointer met the South Asian rule.
