# Sweden: Possessions and value, the domain and folkways (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish and its provincial speech, the Sámi languages, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, and newer communities' languages (Arabic, Syriac, Somali and others). **Concept in one line:** the domain of possessions and value: what is worth having, how it is held, and the food, dress and wealth ways around it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against inland Norrbotten and Västerbotten:** check spending and cash use by county, since distance to banks and shops may shape how money is held.
- **Bergslagen ironworks (*bruk*) communities and the Kiruna and Malmberget ore towns:** check company housing and company stores, where a firm stood between people and their goods.
- **Skåne, Småland and Gotland farms:** compare estate inventories by province for land, stock and silver, instead of repeating provincial sayings about thrift.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, across the language areas:** check how herd, earmark and *duodji* figure in household worth; ask Sámi institutions what they share.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check farm and forest holdings in the valley and the goods of 1960s labor migrants.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check through their own organizations which goods are kept or inherited, not through official files.
- **Jewish community, Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans:** check gold as savings, remittances and wedding gifts, each in its own sources.
- **Church of Sweden, free-church and Laestadian households:** check each body's periodicals for teaching on giving and display.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household records and spending statistics; thinner in minority languages. No settled lands are recorded and no change to the pre-war lands is noted, so this sign is written for Sweden as it stands; its exact lands are still to be settled.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish, including 1700s print for manuals and inventories; North and South Sámi for herd records; Meänkieli for valley holdings; Somali, Syriac and Arabic for remittance and gold customs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi grazing and land rights are contested and before a Truth Commission; give each account as an account. Herd numbers touch family livelihoods; use only what Sametinget publishes.
- **Held for review:** the inheritance rules in the provincial laws and the 1734 Code were weighed and routed to Libra and Capricorn, leaving only the inventories here; no South Asian-rule pointer arose.
