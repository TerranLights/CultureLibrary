# Sweden: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with older runic and hymnal Swedish), Meänkieli, Finnish, North Sámi, Romani, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mill and mine towns of Norrbotten, Västerbotten and Bergslagen after closures:** check what laid-off workers said they lost, since worth through work may weigh differently where one employer dominated.
- **Stockholm professionals against small-town Småland and Halland:** check self-presentation in job ads and local press, to separate grandiosity complaints from city to town.
- **Sámi, reindeer-herding and non-herding, North to South Sámi:** check mental-health surveys and Sámi-led writing for what restores standing after the race-biology era.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check school-shame testimony from the Tornedalian truth commission and Sweden Finnish memoirs, since language loss and worth are told together.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check their own magazines and associations for respect terms, not official reports.
- **Iranian, Iraqi, Syrian and Chilean professionals in exile:** check deskilling accounts by origin and arrival year.
- **Somali and Eritrean young people:** check youth projects and community radio for how school results and "being somebody" are discussed.
- **Pentecostal and Laestadian congregations against the Church of Sweden:** check testimonies and hymns for worth from God against worth from deeds.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for youth surveys, health registries and labor studies; mixed for minority voices, which sit in memoirs, magazines and commission testimony.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (runic and 1800s hymnal Swedish too); Meänkieli and Finnish for school-shame accounts; North Sámi for Sámi health material; Arabic and Persian for professionals' deskilling stories; Romani for Roma press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the State Institute for Racial Biology (Uppsala, 1922) and the sterilizations of 1934-76 attacked people's worth; give survivors' and communities' accounts, not the institute's own claims. Suicide data on small groups can identify people; use only published aggregates.
- **Held for review:** popular "national inferiority" or "national pride" essays by outsiders were considered and left aside as unhelpful starting points; no item here fell under the South Asian rule.
