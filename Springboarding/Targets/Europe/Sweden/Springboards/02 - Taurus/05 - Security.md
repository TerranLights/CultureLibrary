# Sweden: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (and 1800s parish Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Finnish, Arabic, Kurdish, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten inland against Mälardalen suburbs:** check unemployment-fund membership and savings buffers by county, since the sources of security may differ by labor market.
- **Blekinge, Småland and Värmland poor-relief parishes:** compare how *undantag*, granaries and the boarding-out of paupers worked, using parish records.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi across the North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi areas:** check grazing-crisis years and feeding costs as herders report them; set non-herding Sámi security (jobs, housing) apart.
- **Tornedalians:** check how cross-border work with Finland and mine jobs shaped household security.
- **Gotland and Öland islanders:** check reliance on ferries and seasonal work in local press during crises.
- **Somali, Eritrean, Syrian and Iraqi households:** check remittance and kin-insurance practices in community sources, and the gap between felt and recorded security.
- **Bosniaks and Kurds who came in the 1990s:** check oral histories for how a secure life was rebuilt.
- **Gig workers and fixed-term staff in Stockholm and Gothenburg:** check union and platform-worker surveys, since they may sit outside the *a-kassa* system.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: pensions, insurance, labor and safety surveys are thorough, and older parish records cover famine and relief. Thin on informal security in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (including 1800s parish hands); North Sámi for herding terms; Somali and Arabic for remittance practice; Bosnian and Kurdish for war-to-arrival memories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sweden's Second World War neutrality is contested ground; present each historian's account as an account. Fear-of-crime material is easily turned against named suburbs or groups; report districts, not peoples. The poor-relief and race-biology era overlap; use survivors' and historians' accounts.
- **Held for review:** the employment-protection statute and the social-insurance acts were considered for job and income security and routed to Libra and Capricorn; no pointer met the South Asian rule.
