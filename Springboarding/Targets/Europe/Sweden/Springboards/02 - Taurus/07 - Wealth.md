## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Djursholm, Östermalm and Danderyd against Norrland's inland municipalities:** check wealth and income by municipality, and how each place is described in national press.
- **Skåne estate-owning families against Västra Götaland's merchant and industrial families:** check the source of old fortunes (land, trade, industry) through local histories.
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten:** check regional writing on where mine, forest and hydropower wealth went, giving each account as an account.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi and non-herding Sámi:** check how herd size, silver and land use figure in Sámi accounts of wealth, and how outsiders' reports (Schefferus onward) differ.
- **Tornedalians:** check border trade and mining incomes in the valley's own histories.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs in Södertälje, Iranians and Kurds in Stockholm:** check business and family-firm histories in community media.
- **Somali and Eritrean households:** check how remittances shape what counts as having enough, via community and NGO reports.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own economic histories (peddlers, shopkeepers, professionals and refugees alike), and keep studies of antisemitic money myths a separate subject.
- **Free-church and Laestadian congregations:** check sermons and periodicals on riches, giving and display.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: long historical wealth series exist, but official household wealth statistics stopped after 2007, so recent wealth relies on estimates and lists.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and Latin (for Schefferus); North Sámi for herd-and-silver accounts; Arabic, Persian and Kurdish for business media; Somali for remittance practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** antisemitic and anti-Roma myths about money must not be repeated as data; point only to community histories and studies of the myths. Norrland's "colony" framing and Sámi resource claims are contested ground; present them as accounts.
- **Held for review:** the wealth-tax and inheritance-tax statutes and their repeal were weighed for this sheet and routed to Libra, keeping only the public debate here; the South Asian rule did not apply to any pointer.
