*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and Malmö rental blocks with shared laundry rooms against detached houses in Halland and Småland:** check where *arga lappar* gather, since shared space may sharpen guarding.
- **Värmland and Dalarna hunting teams (*jaktlag*):** check lease disputes and newcomer access in local press, a case of turf over game.
- **Berry and mushroom pickers in Norrland forests:** check how local pickers, seasonal commercial pickers and landowners describe disputes over places.
- **Reindeer-herding *samebyar*:** check disputes over grazing between neighboring *samebyar* and with landowners, as each side tells it.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Finnish- and Meänkieli-language counseling and song for how jealousy is named.
- **Young people of every background, Swedish-born and newer communities alike:** check youth-led and community associations' own accounts of family control, and keep the public "honor" category under scrutiny rather than applying it to any group.
- **Free-church and Laestadian congregations:** check marriage-preparation material on jealousy and trust.
- **Children in preschool across city and country:** check how sharing is taught in preschools of different size.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich on partner violence and shared-housing life; thin on possessiveness over objects and knowledge; folk-belief material is rich but scattered.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with ballad language); South, Ume and North Sámi for guarding and sharing words; Meänkieli and Finnish for counseling; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for community organizations' own material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "honor-related" violence is contested ground in integration debates; give the state's category and community and youth organizations' accounts side by side, and never let it describe a people. Shelter and counseling material may involve identifiable cases; use published reports only.
- **Held for review:** stalking and partner-violence statutes were weighed here and routed to Libra and Capricorn, keeping only surveys and services; nothing on this sheet was set aside under the South Asian rule.
