## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna and Gotland local protest traditions against Stockholm city planning fights:** check how holdouts are reported in local and national press, since praise and blame may depend on the paper.
- **Norrbotten miners and Västerbotten revival parishes:** check whether the same people appear as "principled" in their own records and "obstinate" in company or church records.
- **Sámi of Jokkmokk and the Lule and North Sámi areas:** check how land protests are framed by Sámi organizations, residents who wanted jobs, and the company, as three accounts.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check whether *sisu* is used as praise in Sweden Finnish media and how younger writers treat it.
- **Laestadian congregations and the free churches:** check their own writing on steadfast faith against changing church practice.
- **Roma and the *resande*:** check their organizations' records of long campaigns for housing and schooling.
- **Kurds, Iranians and the Chilean exiles:** check exile organizations' papers on holding to a political cause over decades.
- **Bosniaks and Somalis:** check community media and mosque or association guidance on making peace after a quarrel (who yields first, and how), since reconciliation customs vary.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: protest, strike and mediation records are rich; personality-type data on stubbornness is thin and should not stand in for a culture.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; Lule and North Sámi for Gállok material; Finnish for *sisu* debates; Kurdish, Persian and Spanish for exile movements; Bosnian and Somali for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land and mine protests on Sámi land are contested ground between herders, residents, firms and the state; present every side as an account. The Church's apology and the Sámi responses belong together; quote neither out of context.
- **Held for review:** the 1726 ban on lay gatherings and other religious statutes behind the dissenter cases were weighed and routed to Libra and Capricorn; no pointer met the South Asian rule.
