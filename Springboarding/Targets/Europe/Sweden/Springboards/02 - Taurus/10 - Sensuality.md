*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne and Gotland food calendars against Norrland's:** check seasonal treats (goose, saffron, cloudberries, fermented herring) by province, since sensory pleasures may follow local harvests.
- **Bohuslän and Halland summer coast against inland Dalarna and Jämtland:** check bathing, sauna and outdoor-dance customs, and how each region is advertised.
- **Forest Finns of Värmland and Sweden Finns in Mälardalen towns:** check whether sauna customs were kept, lost or revived.
- **Sámi across the North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi areas:** check food, coffee and yoik customs in Sámi media; ask which may be described.
- **Tornedalians:** check coffee-cheese and berry customs in valley sources.
- **Laestadian, Pentecostal and temperance households:** check their writing on restraint, so the counter-tradition is heard in its own words.
- **Jewish community and Muslim communities:** check Sabbath and Ramadan meals, and how each describes pleasure within religious rules.
- **Assyrian and Syriac bakeries of Södertälje, Bosnian coffee customs, Chilean *once*:** check community sources for which home tastes became shared with neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, drink and bathing history; thin for touch and smell in daily life, where interviews will be needed.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and Latin (Linnaeus); Lule and North Sámi for food and yoik; Finnish and Meänkieli for sauna and valley foods; Yiddish, Arabic and Bosnian for religious and coffee customs; Spanish for Chilean exile meals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** personal yoiks belong to the people they describe; ask before using recordings. The Vipeholm trials used institutionalized people without consent; give critical histories, not the trials' own framing.
- **Held for review:** for sensual pleasure, a Buddhist-derived meditation and retreat strand was set aside as a category for the author's open question, naming no group or center; no pointer here came under the South Asian rule.
