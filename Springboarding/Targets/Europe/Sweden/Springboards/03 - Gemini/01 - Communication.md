# Sweden: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (standard and Norrland and Scanian speech), Swedish Sign Language, North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Syriac, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the ideas held about what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten speakers against Skåne and Stockholm:** check recorded conversation for pause length and listener signals, since regional pace may change what counts as rude or warm.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule and South areas:** check how talk moves between Sámi and Swedish in families, through Sámi media and language centers.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Meänkieli and Finnish switching at home and on Sisuradio to see when each language is chosen.
- **Deaf signers and the hard of hearing:** check Deaf clubs and interpreting services for a separate set of conversational rules.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs of Södertälje, Iranians, Kurds and the Chilean exiles of 1973:** check exile media and children's roles as interpreters in each.
- **Somalis, Eritreans and Bosniaks:** check how community radio and messaging groups carry news between Sweden and kin abroad.
- **Pentecostal, Mission Covenant and Laestadian congregations:** check testimony, lay preaching and confession as church-specific forms of speech.
- **Young people in Stockholm, Göteborg and Malmö suburbs:** check how they name and use their own speech varieties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public speech, letters and telecom figures; good conversation-analysis material; thin for recorded talk in minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (older print for letter manuals, Old Swedish and Latin around Vadstena); Swedish Sign Language for Deaf sources; Meänkieli and Finnish for switching studies; Persian and Spanish for exile radio.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** labels for suburban speech feed the integration debates; give speakers' names for their speech beside media and political ones. Daun's thesis is contested; present it as one account.
- **Held for review:** the 1766 press-freedom ordinance and the free-speech statutes were left to Libra, so only spoken attitudes to free speech stay here; the South Asian rule touched none of this sheet's pointers.
