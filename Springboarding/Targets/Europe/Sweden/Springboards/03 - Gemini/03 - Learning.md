# Sweden: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Syriac, Somali, Persian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life (schooling as an institution is Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergslagen ironworks towns and Norrland sawmill and mine towns:** check memoirs and company archives for how sons and daughters learned work beside parents, since the workplace may have taught more than the school.
- **Farm families in Gotland, Öland, Småland and Halland:** check folk-memory answers on children's tasks by age.
- **Reindeer-herding and non-herding Sámi:** check how each set of families describes children learning in the herd and on the land, and the nomad-school memories beside them.
- **Tornedalians:** check memoirs of learning Swedish at school and of Meänkieli relearned later.
- **Sweden Finns:** look at the Sweden Finnish independent schools and ask what children learn there at home that other pupils learn at school.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check church classes in Classical Syriac in Södertälje and Norrköping.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check the *brobyggare* (bridge-builder) program in schools as the communities and schools each report it.
- **Somali and Eritrean families, and the Chilean exiles' children:** check homework clubs, Koran classes and exile Saturday schools.
- **Jewish community:** check religious classes and the Jewish school in Stockholm through the community's own records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** adult, preschool and second-language learning are well counted; learning by watching and pitching in is not, and must be dug out of folk-memory answers and work memoirs.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with 1700s primers and guild hands); Latin for Linnaeus; North and Lule Sámi for traditional-knowledge work; Meänkieli for valley courses; Syriac, Yiddish and Somali for community classes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the nomad schools and the Swedish-only valley schools are contested ground and before truth commissions; let former pupils' and the state's accounts stand as accounts. Some inherited Sámi knowledge is held by families and not for publication.
- **Held for review:** the school system itself (curricula, the Education Act, compulsory schooling's history) was sent on to Sagittarius and Libra, so this sheet keeps the home, the trade and adult learning; no source was set aside on South Asian grounds.
