# Sweden: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Latin for early science), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Syriac, Swedish Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far it is encouraged, channeled, limited or punished.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm against small towns in Småland and Värmland:** check memoirs and local studies for how being watched by neighbors is described in each, rather than assuming the countryside pries more.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check, from Sámi sources, which questions about herds, family and land are held to be improper for outsiders to ask, and the Sámi Parliament's research-ethics work.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadian families:** check Laestadian publications on television, novels and idle questions, read as each congregation states it.
- **Newer Swedes (Iranians, Kurds, Somalis, Eritreans, Syrians):** check memoirs and surveys for which questions about origin they are asked, and how they answer.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check village associations tracing roots back to Tur Abdin, as a community form of curiosity about origins.
- **The *resande* and the Kale and Kalderash Roma:** collect what their associations and the 2014 White Paper hearings record about researchers who measured and photographed them, as their accounts.
- **Deaf signers:** check norms on looking and staring, which may run differently where speech is seen rather than heard.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check how local history and archaeology groups draw amateurs into the island's past.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science popularization, genealogy and museum life; thin for everyday norms on prying and permitted questions, which sit in memoirs and etiquette columns.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with 1600s Swedish and Rudbeck's Latin); North Sámi for research-ethics material; Arabic and Persian for newcomers' memoirs on origin questions; Syriac for village-association histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** research on Sámi, Roma and *resande* bodies and families (the State Institute for Racial Biology, 1922) is contested ground where curiosity did harm; give the communities' accounts first. Sámi knowledge that families keep restricted is not for outsiders' asking.
- **Held for review:** the antiquities and metal-detector rules on Gotland and Öland were weighed for amateur exploring and passed to Libra; the sheet met no South Asian-rule case.
