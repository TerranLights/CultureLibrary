# Sweden: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North and Lule Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Kurdish, Syriac, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Elderly people living alone in Norrland inland and in Stockholm:** check survey and helpline data for loneliness, since distance and city anonymity may isolate in different ways.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check how *sameby*, kin and *verdde* ties are described by Sámi sources, and how distance across Sápmi is bridged.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare how kin are visited and phoned across the river border with how labor migrants of the 1960s stayed linked to home parishes in Finland.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Bosniaks and Iranians:** check how their federations and slang words for pull describe the use of contacts, each in its own sources.
- **Somalis and Eritreans:** check WhatsApp family groups and clan or village associations as ways ties are kept across several countries.
- **The Chilean exiles of 1973:** check solidarity committees and the ties kept or broken after return migration.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma and the *resande*:** ask their organizations how weddings, funerals and summer travel bring scattered relatives together.
- **Laestadians and the free churches:** check how revival meetings, prayer chains and congregational visits keep members in touch.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness, living alone and network surveys; mixed for diaspora ties; thin for *verdde* and other older cross-community ties on the Swedish side.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (runic and 1600s registers); North Sámi for *verdde* and kin terms; Arabic, Persian and Bosnian for words of pull and favor; Somali for family-chat practices; Finnish and Meänkieli for border kinship.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims about who "uses contacts" are easily turned against named groups in the integration debates; report what each community's own sources say. Loneliness and dying alone touch named people; use aggregates.
- **Held for review:** the conflict-of-interest (*jäv*) and procurement rules behind favoritism cases were given to Libra and Capricorn, leaving the slang and the public argument here; no South Asian-rule question came up.
