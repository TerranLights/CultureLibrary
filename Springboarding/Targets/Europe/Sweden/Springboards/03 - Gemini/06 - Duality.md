# Sweden: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Scanian and Gutnish material), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Syriac, Arabic, Spanish, Estonian Swedish dialects, Swedish Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** how two-ness is lived with: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne, Halland and Blekinge:** check whether the Danish past and today's Øresund commuting appear in local identity writing, kept separate from separatist groups.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** see in memoirs which rooms of life (kitchen, classroom, prayer house) were given to which language.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, and those reclaiming Sámi identity as adults:** check how the twentieth-century split between herding and non-herding Sámi is described by each.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** follow the two names through each federation's and each club's own publications, and keep the two accounts apart.
- **Estonian Swedes (*estlandssvenskar*):** check their association's memoirs of two homelands since 1944.
- **Children of the Chilean exiles, of Iranians and of Kurds:** check exile and second-generation writing on return dreams and double belonging.
- **Bosniaks and Somalis:** check how dual citizenship and summers "back home" appear in community media.
- **Deaf signers:** check bilingual Deaf schooling memories against oral-method schooling.
- **Laestadians:** check how belonging to both the revival and the Church of Sweden is described in their own press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bilingualism, literature and border statistics; mixed for folk belief; thin for the everyday "two faces" theme, which sits in fiction and press debate.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with Old Gutnish for *Guta saga*); Danish for the Skåne accounts; North Sámi for Turi; Meänkieli and Finnish for valley writing; Syriac and Arabic for community name debates; Spanish for exile memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dual citizenship and "loyalty" are argued over in the integration debates; present each account as an account. The Assyrian and Syriac naming question and Sámi identity disputes involve living communities; name each by its own name.
- **Held for review:** the citizenship statute that allowed dual citizenship and any loyalty provisions were handed to Libra so this sheet keeps the lived side; no item on this concept came under the South Asian rule.
