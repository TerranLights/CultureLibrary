# Sweden: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish and Latin for Birgitta), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Somali, Arabic, Persian, Syriac, Romani. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and more broadly the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergslagen and Västernorrland mill and mine towns:** check union and company archives for how disputes were brought to a third party, since the strike memory of each town differs.
- **Reindeer-herding Sámi and their *samebyar*:** check how talks with forestry, mining and wind-power firms are run and who speaks for whom, from Sámi sources first.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadian congregations:** check how quarrels inside a congregation are handled, from the movement's own writings.
- **Somalis and Eritreans:** check elders' peacemaking in Sweden and how it meets the social services, in community reports.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check church leaders as go-betweens within families and between the two federations.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma and the *resande*:** record how Roma organizations themselves describe bridge-builders hired to stand between families and schools or social services.
- **Muslim communities:** check imams' family counseling and its debate, through mosque associations' and critics' own words.
- **Jewish community:** check how disputes are brought to a rabbi or the congregation board, from community sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor and international mediation; mixed for family and school programs; thin for informal go-betweens in minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with Latin and Old Swedish for Birgitta's letters); North and South Sámi for *samråd* reports; Somali, Arabic and Persian for community mediation; Syriac for church sources; Romani for Roma organizations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** religious go-betweens in family matters are part of the integration debates; give community accounts and critics' accounts side by side. Sámi consultation over land is contested ground; present herders', companies' and the state's views as accounts. Ådalen and Bernadotte's record are remembered in rival versions.
- **Held for review:** victim-offender mediation and court-linked settlement were set aside for Libra, and the Romani *kris* and other community tribunals that hand down judgments went to Capricorn as imposed decisions; no South Asian-rule material turned up for mediation.
