# Sweden: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with 1600s court and parish language), Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, North Sámi, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna, Hälsingland and Ångermanland parishes of the witch panic:** check how rumor moved between villages there against parishes that stayed quiet.
- **Small towns in Värmland and Småland against Stockholm:** check novels, memoirs and local papers for how reputation travels in each setting.
- **Gotland and Öland island communities:** check local history for gossip about newcomers and summer residents.
- **Sámi and Tornedalians:** check Sámi and valley fiction and theater, since academic sources on village talk are thin here.
- **Laestadian congregations:** check what their own sermons and writings say about speaking of others and about confession.
- **Muslim communities and the Jewish community:** check teaching on *ghība* and *loshn-hore* through mosques' and congregations' own material.
- **Somali, Bosnian and Syrian households:** check how rumors moved in community chats during 2020 and how community organizations answered them.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma and the *resande*:** see whether and how their federations document rumors spread about them, and what they ask the press to do.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for legends, tabloids, the witch panic and workplace bullying; mixed for gossip inside minority communities; thin for Sámi material outside fiction.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (1600s court and parish hands for the panic); Meänkieli for valley fiction; Arabic, Persian and Somali for sermons and chats; Yiddish and Hebrew terms for Jewish teaching; Bosnian for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anonymous forums and the 2012 Göteborg events concern named minors; use only aggregate or published accounts. Rumors about Roma, *resande* and newer communities must never be repeated as data; point to studies of the rumors. Court rumors of the 1950s involve named people; use the published inquiries.
- **Held for review:** the insult and slander rules from the provincial laws to today's defamation statute were routed to Libra; the many conspiracy theories about the 1986 Palme murder were weighed as rumor material and kept out as unreliable starting points. The South Asian rule had nothing to remove here.
