# Sweden: Home and family, the domain and folkways (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Dalecarlian, Gutnish and Norrland farm words), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Kurdish, Arabic, Persian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the domain of home and family: what home is and who counts as family, through household forms, roots, family memory, hospitality, house-building, family order and child-rearing.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna and Hälsingland farm families:** check whether farm names and the retired parents' cottage still tie kin to the land, since farm households lasted longest there.
- **Skåne's estate country:** check estate laborers' households against freeholders', because who lived under one roof differed by class.
- **Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö, and their Million Programme suburbs (Rinkeby, Rosengård, Hammarkullen):** check one-person households in the inner cities against how flat sizes fit large or several-generation families in the suburbs.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding; Tornedalians; Sweden Finns:** check family words and household memories in their own languages, since the registers record them only in Swedish.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Kurds, Iraqis, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks:** check how kin living in nearby flats are counted when they do not share one dwelling.
- **Jewish community, Roma (Kale, Kalderash), the *resande*, Chilean exiles, Estonian Swedes:** check how each rebuilt a household after flight or exclusion, through its own memory projects.
- **Laestadians, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check each one's family teaching in its own publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for household statistics, family policy and vernacular houses; thinner for kin networks spread over several dwellings. Sweden's tracker records no settled lands and no change to the pre-war lands, so this sign is written for Sweden as it stands; which lands the post-war nation holds is still open.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for Linnaeus and the catechism; North Sámi for the Karesuando families' own words behind Labba's book; Meänkieli and Finnish for valley households; Yiddish, Kurdish, Arabic, Persian and Somali for each community's home media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sámi relocations and nomad schools broke up households and are contested ground; give Sámi and state accounts as accounts. Roma and *resande* housing exclusion needs the same care.
- **Held for review:** the 1979 ban on corporal punishment and the marriage and parental codes were weighed for child-rearing and family order and sent to Libra and Capricorn; Murdock's outsider codings for Scandinavia were left aside until Swedish accounts are in hand.
