# Sweden: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Gutnish and Scanian self-names), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Arabic, Persian, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gotland and Öland islanders against mainland arrivals:** check local writing on *fastlänningar*, since island belonging has its own markers.
- **Jämtland-Härjedalen and Skåne, once Norwegian and Danish:** compare who is called a "real" *jämte* or *skåning* in local media, since a provincial "we" can sit beside or against the national one.
- **Herding and non-herding Sámi:** see whether youth groups report being doubted as "real" Sámi from either side, through Sáminuorra and Sámi research centers.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns, Kvens' kin across the border:** check how each group names itself and where those names overlap.
- **Kale, Kalderash and Lovara Roma, and the *resande*:** look at whether each group's associations accept the others under one minority name.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje):** check how the two names divide or join one community, using both sides' own bodies.
- **Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks, Chilean exiles:** check memoirs and surveys for the moment a person first felt Swedish, or did not.
- **Jewish community and Estonian Swedes:** check how two old minorities with long Swedish histories describe belonging today.
- **Church of Sweden, free churches, Laestadians, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check membership rites and welcome work for newcomers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** plenty on minority rights and pupils' surveys, less on felt belonging, little in Roma and Eritrean voices.
- **Languages needed:** Old Gutnish for *Guta saga*; North and South Sámi for youth material; Meänkieli and Finnish for valley and Sweden Finn archives; Romani for association writing; Arabic, Persian and Spanish for diaspora debate.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as Sámi is contested within Sámi society and between Sámi and the state; give each body's account as an account. The integration debates around *utanförskap* are contested ground; never let a source's label stand for a group.
- **Held for review:** the Citizenship Act's terms and the Sámi Parliament's electoral-roll rules were weighed for formal membership and sent to Libra; Aquarius takes the group side of community.
