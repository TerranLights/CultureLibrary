# Sweden: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali, Bosnian, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural Norrland and Dalarna against Stockholm and Malmö:** check preschool start ages and grandparent help in each, since distance to kin shapes who raises toddlers.
- **Sámi families, herding and non-herding:** check how children learn herding and *duodji* by joining in, in Sámi educators' own writing.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check language-nest reports and parents' memories of being raised in Swedish-only schools.
- **Somali, Syrian, Iraqi, Kurdish and Eritrean parents:** check parent courses held in their languages and what the parents themselves report from them.
- **Assyrian and Syriac families (Södertälje):** check church youth work and grandparents' share in raising children.
- **Laestadian families, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations:** check Sunday school and summer-meeting programs for children as settings of formation.
- **Jewish families and Bosniak families:** check how survivors of war raised their children, in second-generation memoirs.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma and the *resande*:** check how their associations describe raising children after decades of children taken into care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for child-health services, preschool and advice books; mixed for minority families' own practices; thin for Roma and Eritrean parenting sources.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for Rosén von Rosenstein; Latin or the 1950s Swedish translation of Schefferus; North Sámi for *duodji* and herding learning; Meänkieli and Finnish for language nests; Somali, Arabic, Kurdish and Persian for parent courses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children taken into care from Roma and *resande* families, and the nomad-school history, are contested ground; give families' and agencies' accounts as accounts. Sámi cradles in museums are part of the repatriation debate.
- **Held for review:** the Education Act's right to a preschool place, weighed for who nurtures toddlers, went to Libra; parents' discipline goes to Capricorn, and schooling proper stays with Gemini and Sagittarius.
