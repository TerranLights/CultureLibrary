## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrland and the inland north against the Stockholm and Göteborg regions:** check winter deaths and night-shelter capacity, since cold and distance change what lacking a roof means.
- **Sámi herding families:** check how tents, huts and cabins along the migration routes are kept and shared, in Sámi accounts.
- **Gotland and the east coast:** check 1944 reception memories against the Estonian Swedes' own archive.
- **Chilean exiles, Bosniaks, Kurds, Iranians, Syrians, Somalis, Eritreans:** check how each group remembers its first shelter, through associations and oral-history projects.
- **Unaccompanied young people of 2015:** check their own writing and youth groups, apart from municipal reports.
- **Sweden Finns in Stockholm's homeless services:** check Finnish-speaking outreach, since aging labor migrants appear in the counts.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash), the *resande*, and EU migrants from Romania and Bulgaria:** check camp clearances and emergency shelters, keeping citizens and EU migrants apart.
- **Parishes that hid people or ran night shelters (Church of Sweden, Catholic, free churches):** compare their own minutes and statements, since each gave its own reason.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for homelessness counts and asylum housing; mixed for inn history; thin for Sámi shelter outside heritage registers.
- **Languages needed:** older Swedish for inn records; North Sámi for migration-route dwellings; Estonian and Estonian-Swedish dialect for the 1944 crossings; Spanish for the embassy accounts; Arabic, Kurdish, Somali and Bosnian for arrivals' stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homelessness and asylum files concern identifiable people; use aggregates. Camp clearances of EU migrants and Roma are contested ground; give each account as an account. Sámi dwelling sites can be sacred or family places; ask before naming them.
- **Held for review:** the Social Services Act's duty to give emergency housing and the Planning and Building Act's housing rules were weighed and sent to Libra; the market for dwellings stays in Taurus.
