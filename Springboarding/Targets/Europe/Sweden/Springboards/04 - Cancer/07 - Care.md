# Sweden: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inland Norrland municipalities against Stockholm and Skåne:** check home-help distances and the share of old people living far from children, because geography sets who can care.
- **Sámi elders, herding and non-herding:** check Sámi organizations' reports on care in Sámi languages and on family care in herding seasons.
- **Sweden Finns and Tornedalians:** check Finnish- and Meänkieli-speaking care units and the aging labor migrants of the 1960s-70s.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Iraqis, Syrians, Iranians, Somalis, Bosniaks:** check how many families use home help against kin care, and what the associations report about their elders' needs.
- **Care workers born abroad:** check Kommunal and research on the staff, since many assistant nurses came to Sweden as adults.
- **Women and men as family carers:** compare VAB days and carers' surveys, without assuming one division.
- **Jewish community and Holocaust survivors:** check the community's own elder-care home and survivors' care needs.
- **Deacons of the Church of Sweden, free-church and Catholic visiting groups, mosque volunteers:** set each one's sick-visiting records side by side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public elder care and sick-child leave; mixed for unpaid family care; thin for folk healers and for Eritrean and Somali carers.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and church Latin for the *Helgeandshus*; 1800s parish hands for *rotegång*; North Sámi and Finnish for minority-language care; Arabic, Persian, Somali and Bosnian for kin-care accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care-home deaths in 2020 are contested ground; give the commission's, the regions' and families' accounts as accounts. Sámi healing knowledge can be restricted to its holders; do not chase it beyond what Sámi sources share.
- **Held for review:** the Social Services Act and the 1994 act on support for disabled people (LSS) were weighed for who must give care and sent to Libra and Capricorn; the 1992 *Ädel* reform's legal side goes the same way.
