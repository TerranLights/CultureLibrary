# Sweden: Creativity and self-expression, the domain and folkways (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Gutnish game names and Dalecarlian craft words), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Swedish Sign Language, Kurdish, Syriac, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the domain of creativity and self-expression: how people shine, play and take pleasure, through sport, festivity, romance, children as joy, fame, display and games of chance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Norrbotten and Västerbotten:** check whether *Kulturvanor* separates city stages from village halls.
- **Dalarna, Hälsingland and Värmland:** compare fiddlers' gatherings, dance-band weeks and costume days, where much of the folk calendar sits.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check the island games and their own competitions against mainland sport bodies.
- **Skåne, Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** compare student-town and port-city festival calendars.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding:** check yoik, *duodji* and reindeer racing through Sámi bodies' programs, not tourist listings.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** see which dance halls, tango evenings and choirs run in Finnish or Meänkieli, and who attends.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash), the *resande*, the Jewish community:** check music and festive life through each group's own associations and media.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** check New Year, wedding and music events in their associations' calendars.
- **Laestadian, Pentecostal, Mission Covenant and Muslim congregations:** read what each says about dancing, card games and television in its own guidance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, participation and gambling; mixed for festivity and romance; thin in minority languages. Sweden's tracker sets no lands and logs no change to its pre-war ones, so this sign treats Sweden as it is now; its post-war lands are yet to be settled.
