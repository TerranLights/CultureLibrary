# Sweden: Creativity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Dalecarlian craft words and Norrland garage slang), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** making something new that others find valuable or fitting, in the arts, in crafts, in solving problems and in everyday life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna, Hälsingland and Halland:** compare painted-hanging traditions for where faithful copying gave way to a painter's own hand.
- **Gotland:** check medieval workshop finds against today's island crafts sold to summer visitors.
- **Stockholm against Norrbotten and Västerbotten:** compare grant lists to see whether funding for making follows population or institutions.
- **Småland and Bergslagen:** check glassworks, sawmills and small-firm districts for the inventor's and toolmaker's tradition.
- **Sámi of the several language areas:** check who may make and sell *duodji* with which marks, through Sámi bodies' own rules.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check how new works in Meänkieli and Finnish are funded and staged.
- **Roma, the *resande*, the Jewish community:** check archives of crafts (tinwork, horse-trading gear) and music for who is credited as maker.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Chilean exiles:** check their arts associations' exhibitions for work made in exile.
- **Church of Sweden, free churches, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check hymn writing, church textiles and mosque decoration for whose name goes on the work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts, patents and the music and game industries; thinner for everyday improvising and for exile artists.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and Latin for font inscriptions; Dalecarlian for painters' signatures; North and South Sámi for makers' accounts; Meänkieli for scripts; Arabic, Persian, Bosnian and Somali for exiles' arts papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** copying of Sámi patterns and *gákti* by outside designers is contested; give makers' and companies' accounts as accounts.
- **Held for review:** copyright and design-protection law for ownership of what is made was passed to Libra; owning designs as property goes to Taurus.
