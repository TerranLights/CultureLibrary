# Sweden: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Swedish Sign Language, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Kurdish (Kurmanji), Persian, Arabic, Romani, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm against the Norrland towns:** check local papers' graffiti, Pride and street-style coverage for different responses to the same display.
- **Västergötland, Värmland and Dalarna:** compare raggare meets and dance halls with city clubs for where young people show off and how.
- **Torne Valley Laestadians against Church of Sweden parishes:** check how each describes emotion in worship, from their own writing.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, and Sámi in cities:** check how *gákti* is worn on which days and how people describe choosing it.
- **Deaf signers:** check Deaf theater and poetry against hearing stages for how expression is judged.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Chilean exiles:** compare exile writing in each language for what could not be said before arrival.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans:** check youth music and media in each community against the parents' media.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check whose recordings exist and who made them.
- **Pentecostal and Mission Covenant churches, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check teaching on dress, speech and display in their own publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for subcultures and Pride; mixed for everyday reserve, where much is claim; thin for Romani and Somali voices.
- **Languages needed:** 1740s Swedish for Nordenflycht; Meänkieli and Finnish for the revival's testimony; Swedish Sign Language for Deaf poetry, which print cannot carry; Kurmanji and Persian for exile presses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dress and head-covering debates in schools are contested ground, as are Laestadian practices; give each account as an account and keep members' words apart from outsiders'.
- **Held for review:** the press-freedom and free-speech statutes and court cases on dress codes were weighed for political expression and sent to Libra; Aquarius takes nonconformity.
