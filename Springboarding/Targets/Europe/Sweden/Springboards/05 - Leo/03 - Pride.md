# Sweden: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Scanian and Jämtland regional words), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Bosnian, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne, Jämtland and Gotland:** compare regional-flag use and local slogans, since each was once outside or apart from the Swedish crown.
- **Norrbotten and Bergslagen mining and mill towns:** check museum and union material for pride in a trade and how it is spoken of after closures.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check the Sámi flag's use at Sámi and municipal events through Sámi bodies' reports.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check *Finnkampen* coverage in Sisuradio and valley papers for loyalties on the day.
- **Afro-Swedes, Somalis, Eritreans:** compare associations' own events and statements on group pride with press coverage of them.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Bosniaks:** check how each community's flag days and namings are marked, using each name as its users give it.
- **The Jewish community, Kale and Kalderash Roma, the *resande*:** look for pride in having endured, in Judiska museet's exhibitions and Roma and Traveller associations' publications.
- **Church of Sweden, Laestadians, free churches, Muslim communities:** check sermons and teaching material on pride and humility in each tradition.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for flag days, sport and the great-power past; mixed for everyday pride; thin for minority pride outside the Sámi.
- **Languages needed:** 1600s Swedish for Grubb and Dahlbergh's captions; North Sámi for the flag's naming; Finnish and Meänkieli for *Finnkampen* coverage; Somali and Tigrinya for independence-day programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Karl XII commemorations and national-pride debates are contested ground; give each group's account as an account and never let a source's label stand for a people.
- **Held for review:** the flag regulations and the 2005 holiday statute were weighed and sent to Libra; outsiders' rankings of national pride were left aside in favor of Swedish sources.
