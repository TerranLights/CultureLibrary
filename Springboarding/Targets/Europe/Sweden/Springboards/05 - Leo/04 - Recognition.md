# Sweden: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North and Lule Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Syriac, Kurdish, Arabic, Persian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppsala and Lund against newer universities (Umeå, Luleå, Södertörn):** check whether newer universities copy the old conferment rituals or make new ones.
- **Dalarna, Uppland and Hälsingland fiddlers:** check *Zornmärket* juries' records for which districts' styles are recognized.
- **Sámi of each language area:** check how the 1998 apology and later Church of Sweden steps are discussed in North, Lule and South Sámi media.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare reactions to minority status in 2000 in each group's papers and associations.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash, Lovara) and the *resande*:** check whether each group sees itself named in the recognition and in later reports.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check how the 2010 vote is marked in each community's own media and church calendars.
- **Kurds, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks, Chilean exiles:** check associations' statements on recognition of qualifications and of each community's history.
- **Jewish community and Estonian Swedes:** compare how each old minority's past in Sweden is acknowledged in museums and school material.
- **Church of Sweden, free churches, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check honors for lay service (church medals, community awards) in each.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for prizes, orders and state acknowledgments; thinner for everyday praise at work and at home.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for the academies' founding papers; German for Meitner's letters; North and Lule Sámi for responses to apologies; Syriac and Kurdish for recognition campaigns; Somali for associations' honors.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** state apologies, the minorities' status and genocide recognition are contested ground; present each account as an account, and use each group's own name (Assyrian, Syriac, Chaldean) as its users choose.
