# Sweden: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Swedish Sign Language, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm against small towns of Norrland and Småland:** compare Lucia elections and greeting customs in local papers.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding:** compare how Sámi media cover moments of national attention against everyday coverage.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check each group's own accounts of being unseen in schools and statistics.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash, Lovara) and the *resande*:** check whose accounts of the 2013 register and later inquiries are on record.
- **Jewish community in Malmö, Stockholm and Gothenburg:** compare accounts of public visibility city by city.
- **Afro-Swedes, Somalis, Eritreans:** check representation counts and associations' statements on being seen or watched in public space.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Assyrians and Syriacs:** check evil-eye customs through each community's own media, apart from outsiders' summaries.
- **Deaf signers and disabled people:** check sign-language media and disability groups for being noticed as oneself.
- **Church of Sweden, free churches, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check how each witnesses births, marriages and deaths.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveillance history and minority inquiries; mixed for representation; thin for evil-eye lore in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Latin or the Swedish translation for Schefferus; Swedish Sign Language for Deaf viewers' own words; Meänkieli for valley testimony; Romani for association statements; Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish and Bosnian for family lore.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Roma register, police registration and Sámi images are contested and touch living people; present each account as an account and keep personal data out.
- **Held for review:** the data-protection, camera-surveillance and signals-intelligence statutes were weighed for being watched and sent to Libra; the census-category law went the same way.
