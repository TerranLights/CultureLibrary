# Sweden: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Swedish Sign Language, North and Ume Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Bosnian, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrland's sparsely settled municipalities against Stockholm and Skåne:** compare elder-care and complaint reports, where distance and staffing shape care.
- **Bergslagen and Norrbotten industrial towns:** check union and museum material for how work and its loss are tied to self-respect.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, across the language areas:** check Sámi bodies' own statements on remains, place names and treatment in care.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Finnish- and Meänkieli-speaking care units for being cared for in one's own language.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check associations' accounts of treatment by social services and housing offices.
- **Bosniaks, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Eritreans:** check memorials, welfare groups and memoirs for each community's wording on decent treatment after flight.
- **Deaf signers and people with intellectual disabilities:** check self-advocacy groups' own publications against agency reports.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, the Jewish community, Muslim communities:** compare burial customs and the dignity of the dead in each, through their own funeral societies.
- **Church of Sweden, Laestadians, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations:** check each one's diaconal and visiting work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health care, elder care and disability; mixed for work; thin for newer communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for the peasant estate's minutes; Swedish Sign Language for Deaf testimony; North and Ume Sámi for reburial coverage; Bosnian, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish and Somali for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the remains of the Sámi dead and the people in former institutions are sensitive; follow Sámi and self-advocacy bodies' wishes. The begging debate is contested ground; keep every account as an account and never let a source's label stand for a group.
- **Held for review:** the 1335 Skara ordinance ending thralldom, the Social Services Act's dignity clause and the begging bylaws were weighed for this sheet and sent to Libra; assisted dying as a legal question goes the same way.
