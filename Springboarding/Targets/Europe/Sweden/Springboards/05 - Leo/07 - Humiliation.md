# Sweden: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Bosnian, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppsala, Lund and the technical schools:** compare *nollning* rules and complaints to see where each campus draws the line.
- **Parishes of Västergötland, Uppland and Norrland:** check *kyrkoplikt* cases in parish minutes for which offenses drew public penance, region by region.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check nomad-school and boarding memories by language area and by decade.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check memories of being punished for speaking Finnish or Meänkieli at school, and how families spoke of it afterwards.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check each group's own accounts of slurs and exclusion, apart from officials' files.
- **Jewish community and Afro-Swedes:** check how each community's associations and hotlines document public insults and online abuse.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans:** check memoirs and youth projects for experiences of lowering at work and in offices, in each language.
- **Laestadians, free churches, Church of Sweden:** compare confession and penance customs, keeping members' accounts apart from outsiders'.
- **Military units and sports teams:** check hazing reports and the inquiries that followed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for bullying research, press ethics and church penance; mixed for hazing; thin for newer communities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s-1800s parish hands for *kyrkoplikt*; North Sámi and Meänkieli for school testimony; Finnish for Laestadian texts; Romani, Yiddish, Bosnian, Kurdish, Somali, Arabic and Persian for community accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** nomad-school memories, wartime neutrality and the treatment of the *resande* and Roma are contested ground; present each account as an account and never let a source's label stand for a people. Laestadian confession is a religious practice; use members' published accounts first.
- **Held for review:** the defamation and harassment statutes and the rules on shared intimate images were weighed for public exposure and sent to Libra; revenge for humiliation goes to Scorpio.
