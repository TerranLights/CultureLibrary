# Sweden: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Småland and Dalarna custom words), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland and Västergötland against Stockholm:** compare giving surveys and church collections to test whether free-church presence tracks giving.
- **Dalarna and Hälsingland:** check folk-custom records for door-to-door giving at Christmas and Easter, district by district.
- **Sámi *samebyar*, herding and non-herding Sámi:** check how sharing after slaughter and help between families are described by Sámi themselves.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** check revival writing on alms and help to neighbors in hard years.
- **Jewish community:** check the community's own welfare and *tsdoke* history in Stockholm and Gothenburg.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks, Iraqis, Syrians:** check remittances and home-town projects through each community's own associations, not only bank data.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians:** check church, association and New Year appeals for who gives to whom.
- **Muslim communities, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations, Catholics, Church of Sweden:** compare teaching on obligatory and voluntary giving in each one's literature.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check whether associations record gift customs at weddings and funerals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organized charity, aid and fundraising control; mixed for kin giving; thin for informal giving in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and Latin for charters; 1800s Swedish for folk-custom records; North Sámi for sharing customs; Yiddish for community welfare papers; Arabic, Somali, Persian and Bosnian for appeals and remittance studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** official scrutiny of remittance offices is contested ground; give each account as an account. Questions on Sámi sharing can touch herding economics; follow Sámi bodies' guidance.
- **Held for review:** the fundraising and foundation statutes and the tax rules on gifts were weighed for this sheet and sent to Libra; exchange and return go to Libra, and stinginess to Taurus.
