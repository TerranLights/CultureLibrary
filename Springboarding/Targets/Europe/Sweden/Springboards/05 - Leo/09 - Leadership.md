# Sweden: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Kurdish, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland and the Mälardalen against Dalarna and Småland:** compare the founding stories of leaders acclaimed or resisted by the provinces, read in local histories.
- **Bergslagen mills and Norrbotten mines:** check how foremen, union stewards and company heads are remembered in work-life archives.
- **Gothenburg and Stockholm firms:** compare management writing from the two business centers for differing leader ideals.
- **Sámi of the several language areas, herding and non-herding:** check *sameby* boards and Sámi Parliament parties for how leaders are chosen.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** check how lay preachers come to lead and how they step down, from members' accounts.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans:** check association boards and congregations for who leads and how elections are held.
- **Muslim communities, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations, Church of Sweden, Catholics:** compare how pastors, imams, priests and elders are selected.
- **Jewish community, Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check each group's own representative bodies and how they speak for members.
- **Women and men across sectors:** compare AllBright and municipal figures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for management research, politics and associations; mixed for informal leaders; thin for leadership in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and Latin for the medieval sources; 1500s Swedish for Gustav Vasa's letters; North and South Sámi for Sámi Parliament debates; Arabic for imam debates; Kurdish, Bosnian and Somali for association life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi governance and the relation of the Sámi Parliament to the state are contested ground; present each body's account as an account. The imam-training debate is the same.
- **Held for review:** the codetermination act and the Sámi Parliament act were weighed for how leaders consult and are chosen and sent to Libra; command and authority go to Capricorn.
