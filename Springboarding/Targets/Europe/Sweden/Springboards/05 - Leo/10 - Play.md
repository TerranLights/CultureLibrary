# Sweden: Play (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö against Norrland villages:** compare who supervises outdoor play and where children may roam, through municipal and memoir sources.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check whether *kubb* and *varpa* keep their island rules once played on mainland lawns and in schoolyards.
- **Skåne and Uppsala student towns:** compare *spex* traditions and their audience customs.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding:** check children's games and contests in Sámi preschools and at winter events.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check childhood memoirs for games played in Meänkieli or Finnish and the words used.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check whether music, dance and play are named apart in each group's own accounts.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Kurds, Syrians, Iraqis, Bosniaks:** check parents' groups and youth centers for the games children bring and the games they take up.
- **Deaf children:** check Deaf schools and sport clubs for playground games adapted to sign.
- **Laestadians and Pentecostal congregations:** check each one's own guidance on games, television and children's leisure.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for children's lore, toys and amusement parks; mixed for adult play; thin for games in newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** 1730s Swedish and Latin for Linnaeus; North Sámi for *suohpan* and preschool material; Swedish Sign Language for Deaf play; Romani, Somali, Tigrinya, Kurdish and Bosnian for parents' groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Sámi games may tie to herding knowledge families keep for themselves; follow Sámi bodies' guidance. Gaming among children is a contested health topic; give each side as an account.
- **Held for review:** the video-violence and game-classification rules of the 1980s and the gambling statutes were weighed for this sheet and sent to Libra; gambling as such sits with the domain.
