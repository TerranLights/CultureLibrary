# Sweden: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Hälsingland and Ångermanland dialect words), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani, Yiddish, Estonian, Bosnian, Arabic, Kurdish, Somali. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hälsingland and Ångermanland against Skåne and Uppland:** compare court records on fights over insults, and when such fights ended.
- **Nobility, freeholders and the landless:** check Riddarhuset sources against peasant records for how honor depended on estate.
- **Military and veterans' families, including the Hemvärnet:** check memoirs and associations for honor in service at home and abroad.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check Sámi sources on honoring elders and the dead, without opening restricted yoik or burial knowledge.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Finnish- and Meänkieli-language obituaries, including notices for Finnish war veterans in Sweden.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check each group's own writing on respect and good name, keeping community judgment for Capricorn.
- **Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis and other newer communities:** check each community's own associations, women's groups and youth projects, never letting one case stand for a people.
- **Estonian Swedes and the Jewish community:** compare how each honors its dead and its rescuers.
- **Lutheran parishes, Laestadian meetings, free-church and Muslim congregations:** compare teaching on oaths, a good name and duty to parents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for runestones, nobility, early modern court records and the 2000s debate; mixed for everyday honor; thin for honor in work and trade.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for *Erikskrönikan*; 1600s-1700s court hands for Liliequist's sources; North Sámi for honoring the dead; Estonian for the *Estonia* families; Kurdish, Arabic, Bosnian and Somali for community debate, read in the speakers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related violence and the "honor culture" framing are contested ground; present every account as an account, name each community by its own name, and never let a bullet state what a group is. Grave and wreck debates touch the bereaved.
- **Held for review:** the dueling edicts, the defamation statutes and the honor-related crime provisions were weighed for this concept and sent to Libra and Capricorn; feud and revenge go to Scorpio.
