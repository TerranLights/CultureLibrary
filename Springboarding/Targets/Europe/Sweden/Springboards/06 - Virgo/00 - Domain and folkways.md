# Sweden: Work and service, the domain and folkways (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Dalecarlian and Norrland work vocabulary), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Bosnian, Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the domain of work and service: what counts as good work and a well-kept life, across work, service, the daily round, the kept house and health.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Bergslagen's mill towns:** set office memories beside *bruk* (ironworks) memories of the working day.
- **Dalarna, Härjedalen and Hälsingland:** compare *fäbod* records on who did the herding work.
- **Skåne and Halland farms against Småland's Gnosjö firms:** set farm diaries beside firm histories for the yearly round.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check limestone, fishing and farm calendars in island museum records.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi areas, herding and non-herding:** compare Sámi writers on the herding year with those on fishing, crafts and wage work.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check *talkoot* records and Finnish factory workers' memories.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** look for horse-dealing, tinning, copper and peddling work in records kept by the families' own associations, set beside police and parish files.
- **Bosniaks, Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Kurds, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** check associations' records of first jobs and of work parties held here.
- **Church of Sweden parishes, Laestadian, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations, Catholics, Muslim communities and the Jewish community:** read each one's guidance on a day of rest and on which chores it allows.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for working life, sick leave and vacations; mixed for daily routine and housework; thin for minority work parties in their own languages. No lands are fixed yet in Sweden's tracker, which records its pre-war lands unchanged, so these Virgo sheets are written for today's Sweden and its exact post-war extent is still open.
- **Languages needed:** 1500s Swedish for Brahe's household book; dialect skills for the herding calls; several Sámi varieties for the herding year; Meänkieli and Finnish for valley records; Bosnian and Arabic for work-party and mosque notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** older Sámi ethnographies were written by outsiders while the state controlled Sámi work and schooling; read them as accounts, beside Sámi writers.
- **Held for review:** the vacation and working-hours statutes and the old servants' statutes came up under rest and service and were passed to Libra; no other pointer was set aside here.
