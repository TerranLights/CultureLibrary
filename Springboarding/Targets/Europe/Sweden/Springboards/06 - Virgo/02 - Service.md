# Sweden: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Syriac, Arabic, Persian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm households against Dalarna's seasonal women workers (*dalkullor*):** check how town service and seasonal labor in the capital were recorded and remembered.
- **Värmland, Småland and Halland farms:** compare maids' and farmhands' service years in parish moving records.
- **Boden, Karlskrona and other garrison towns:** check local histories for how conscription shaped the town's ties to service.
- **Sámi of the Lule, Pite and North Sámi areas:** check how catechists and Sámi church workers served, and which Sámi accounts are critical of that service.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** check how unpaid lay preachers and helpers at summer meetings describe their role.
- **Sweden Finns and Estonian Swedes:** check postwar records of women in hotel, hospital and domestic work.
- **Jewish community, Muslim communities, Assyrians and Syriacs:** compare how each congregation names and rewards volunteers.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Kurds and Iranians:** check association reports on volunteering inside and outside the community.
- **Pentecostal, Mission Covenant and Salvation Army congregations:** compare their volunteer and soup-kitchen records with the Church of Sweden's *diakoni*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic service history, conscription and volunteering; mixed for customer service; thin for service roles inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and Latin for Vadstena; runic Old Norse for Ramsund; North and Lule Sámi for catechist records; Syriac for church service books; Arabic, Persian and Somali for association life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sweden's wartime neutrality is contested ground, so readiness memories should be read as accounts; Sámi church history is part of the Truth Commission's work, so present the catechists' role through more than one account.
- **Held for review:** the 1944 domestic-workers' hours act and the conscription statute were weighed for terms of service and passed to Libra; obedience in the ranks goes to Capricorn.
