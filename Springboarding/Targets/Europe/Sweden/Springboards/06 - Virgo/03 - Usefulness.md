# Sweden: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish and Meänkieli, Arabic, Kurdish, Bosnian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrbotten and Bergslagen towns after closures:** check local studies for how laid-off miners and mill workers described losing a role.
- **Stockholm and Gothenburg against Gotland and Öland:** compare what older people report doing for grandchildren and neighbors in urban and island surveys.
- **Småland and Västra Götaland:** check thrift and reuse customs in household-economy records and local museums.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, North, Lule and South Sámi:** check how makers and teachers speak of objects made for use versus for sale.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare elders' accounts of usefulness in Finnish-speaking care units with those in Swedish ones.
- **Disabled people and Deaf Sweden:** check self-advocacy groups' own statements on work and worth.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Bosniaks:** check how families describe children's help and elders' roles in interviews and association talks.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check where officials once called these families idle, and set those files beside the families' own accounts of their work.
- **Laestadians and free-church congregations:** compare what each says about time well used.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for benefits, programs and design history; mixed for elders and children; thin for minority-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for Kalm and the utility debates; North and South Sámi for *duodji* teaching; Finnish and Meänkieli for elder care; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for family studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** judging who was "useful" fed the racial-biology research and the sterilizations of 1934-76, which hit Travellers, Roma and disabled people among others; treat it as contested ground and use survivors' and descendants' accounts. Disability and benefit debates are live political quarrels.
- **Held for review:** the disability-support act (LSS) and the rules on sickness compensation came up for daily activity and early retirement and were passed to Libra; nothing else was set aside.
