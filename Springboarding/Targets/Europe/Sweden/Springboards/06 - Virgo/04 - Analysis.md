# Sweden: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Latin (for early science), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppsala and Lund against Stockholm's Kista and Gothenburg's Chalmers:** compare how university towns and tech districts present analytical work in their own histories.
- **Linköping and Östergötland:** check aircraft and defense-industry engineering records for how testing and measurement are taught.
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten herding areas, and Sámi of the Lule, North and South Sámi areas:** check how herders' observation is described by herders and by researchers working with them.
- **Tornedalians:** compare valley schoolbooks in Meänkieli and Swedish on arithmetic and nature study.
- **The Jewish community:** check study-circle and synagogue programs for text study as a form of close reasoning.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** look at who did the counting in each inventory and how the families' organizations now answer those records.
- **Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians and Bosniaks:** check SCB education data by country of birth and associations' science and homework clubs.
- **Church of Sweden, Laestadians and free churches:** compare sermons and study material on faith and reason.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history, statistics and state inquiries; mixed for everyday reasoning; thin for minority-language sources on thinking styles.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and 1700s Swedish for Linnaeus and the Academy; Lule and North Sámi for herding knowledge; Yiddish for study circles; Arabic and Persian for community education material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the inventories of Roma and Travellers and the racial-biology measurements are contested ground; present officials' and families' accounts as accounts. Sámi herding knowledge may be shared on the holders' terms; ask before quoting.
- **Held for review:** the register and personal-data statutes behind Project Metropolitan came up and were passed to Libra; no other kind of pointer was set aside.
