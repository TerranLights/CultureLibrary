# Sweden: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Latin (Vadstena), North and Lule Sámi, Sweden Finnish and Meänkieli, Yiddish, Arabic, Spanish (Chilean). **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and Gothenburg against small towns in Västerbotten and Jämtland:** check fraud reports and consumer complaints by county for differences in who gets caught out.
- **Skåne's Österlen and Gotland:** compare local food fairs and tasting events for how taste is judged and taught.
- **Norrland and Småland mushroom and berry pickers:** check whether people learn species from family, from courses or from guidebooks.
- **Sámi herders of the North, Lule and South Sámi areas:** check how herders describe learning earmarks and reading herds, against non-herding Sámi.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** check how congregations decide whether a newcomer's faith is sincere, in members' accounts.
- **The Jewish community and Muslim communities:** compare kosher and halal supervision as ways of judging food, through each community's own bodies.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Kurds:** check name-based hiring tests and their own job-search accounts.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check how housing and job refusals are reported by their own organizations and the equality ombudsman.
- **Older people in all regions:** compare fraud-victim reports by age and county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for consumer testing, hiring discrimination and fraud; mixed for taste; thin for spiritual discernment outside the Church of Sweden.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the Birgittine sources; North and Lule Sámi for herding skills; Yiddish and Arabic for religious judgment; Finnish and Meänkieli for valley congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sámi drums are sacred and some are subject to return claims; describe them only through Sámi sources and do not reproduce drum figures. Hiring-discrimination findings feed the integration debates; present each study as one account.
- **Held for review:** the ban on unstunned slaughter and the discrimination statute were weighed for food supervision and hiring and passed to Libra; no other kind of pointer was set aside.
