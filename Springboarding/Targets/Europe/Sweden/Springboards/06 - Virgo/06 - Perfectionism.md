# Sweden: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish and Meänkieli, Arabic, Bosnian, Spanish (Chilean). **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eskilstuna and Sörmland's metalworking towns:** check company and museum histories for how precision is described and rewarded.
- **Småland's glassworks and Dalarna's knife and Dala-horse makers:** compare how each craft talks about seconds, flaws and the mark of the hand.
- **Gothenburg (SKF, Volvo) against Stockholm's design and media firms:** check quality programs and design juries for different words for "finished".
- **Sámi *duodji* makers of the North, Lule and South Sámi areas:** check how masters train apprentices and when work is called ready.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** compare what sermons and memoirs say about vanity in dress and home against care in work.
- **Pentecostal, Mission Covenant and holiness congregations:** check teaching on sanctification and on scrupulous conscience.
- **The Jewish community, Muslim communities and Assyrian and Syriac churches:** compare rules for exact prayer and liturgy, from each body's own material.
- **Bosniaks, Kurds, Iranians, Somalis and Eritreans:** check school and youth surveys by background for grade pressure, without assuming any pattern.
- **Young women and young men:** compare the *duktig* debates with data on stress and grades by sex.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for craft standards, contests and the "good girl" debate; mixed for clinical perfectionism; thin for minority craft standards outside Sámi *duodji*.
- **Languages needed:** 1600s-1700s Swedish for guild records; North Sámi for knife and silver work; Finnish and Meänkieli for valley sermons; Arabic for *itqān* in mosque talks; Bosnian and Spanish for family and youth sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders and self-harm among high achievers need care and no individual stories without consent; Sámi makers' marks and patterns can belong to families, so ask before reproducing them.
- **Held for review:** the guild ordinances' rules on who might trade were set aside for Libra, keeping only their masterpiece and judging rules here; nothing else was held.
