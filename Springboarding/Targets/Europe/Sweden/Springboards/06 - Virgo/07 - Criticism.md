# Sweden: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli and Sweden Finnish, Kurdish, Persian, Arabic, Spanish (Chilean). **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm's national press against local papers in Norrbotten, Jämtland and Gotland:** check how openly letters to the editor name local officials.
- **Skåne (Malmö, Lund) and Gothenburg:** compare satirical traditions and revues for whom they mock.
- **Uppsala and Lund academic culture:** check doctoral defenses and seminar customs for how scholars criticize each other's work.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule and South Sámi areas, herding and non-herding:** compare criticism in Sámi magazines aimed at the state and debate inside Sámi politics.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check *Haparandabladet* and Finnish-language papers for valley criticism of language policy.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Eritreans and Chilean exiles:** check exile publications' criticism of home governments and the pressures their writers report.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check how each community's press handles the dispute over its name, without taking a side.
- **Workplaces with strong unions against small private firms:** compare appraisal practice and who may criticize a manager.
- **Laestadians, free churches and the Church of Sweden:** compare congregational discipline and how members correct one another.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, satire and literary criticism; mixed for workplace and family criticism; thin for criticism inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for Dalin, Kellgren and Thorild; North Sámi for Sámi debate; Finnish and Meänkieli for valley papers; Kurdish, Persian, Tigrinya and Spanish for exile publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** exile writers may face pressure from home governments; check before naming living critics. The Assyrian-Syriac name dispute and Sámi internal debates are contested ground: present each side as an account.
- **Held for review:** the Freedom of the Press Act and the parliamentary ombudsman's remit came up for public criticism and were passed to Libra; whether criticizing rulers is a civic duty goes to Capricorn.
