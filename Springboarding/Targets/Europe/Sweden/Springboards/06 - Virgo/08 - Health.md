# Sweden: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (Old Swedish for the leechbooks), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Somali, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten inland against Stockholm and the Mälardalen:** check distance to care, cottage-hospital history and life expectancy by municipality.
- **Malmö and Skåne:** check the Malmö Commission's (2013) work on health gaps between neighborhoods.
- **Gotland, Öland and Halland:** compare folk-remedy records with the district doctors' reports from the same parishes.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi areas, herding and non-herding:** compare health studies of herders and non-herders, as Sámi researchers design them.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadians:** check congregations' own material on medicine and vaccination, and valley healers in Meänkieli records.
- **Sweden Finns:** check sauna and Finnish-language health-information use.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check health surveys and bridge-builder reports, set beside the sterilization history.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Syrians, Iraqis, Assyrians and Syriacs:** check vaccination coverage by district and community health workers' reports, without assuming causes.
- **Muslim and Jewish communities:** check guidance on fasting and health from each community's own bodies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registers, surveys and medical history; mixed for folk medicine; thin for health ideas in newer communities' own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the leechbooks; 1700s Swedish for district reports; North Sámi for Sámi health; Meänkieli and Finnish for valley healers; Romani, Arabic, Somali and Tigrinya for community health material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the racial-biology institute measured Sámi, Tornedalians and others, and sterilizations (1934-76) fell on Roma, Travellers and disabled people among others; treat these as contested ground and lead with those affected. Folk-healing charms were held by named healers, so ask families before quoting.
- **Held for review:** the patient-safety act and the old smallpox-vaccination ordinance came up for prevention and care and were passed to Libra; no further pointer was set aside.
