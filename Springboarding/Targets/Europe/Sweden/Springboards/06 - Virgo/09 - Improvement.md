# Sweden: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali, Spanish (Chilean). **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne against Dalarna:** compare land-reform histories in early-enclosure plains and in villages that kept split holdings longest.
- **Norrland settler districts and Sámi areas (North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi):** check settler and Sámi accounts of the same valleys, side by side.
- **Stockholm's Klara district and Gothenburg's Haga:** compare renewal plans with former residents' memories.
- **Million Programme suburbs (Rinkeby, Rosengård, Hammarkullen):** check residents' associations' own renewal proposals against municipal plans.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Swedish-only schooling as "improvement" against the 2020s language-renewal work, in Meänkieli and Finnish sources.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check the 1950s-60s schooling and housing programs aimed at them, through their own organizations' accounts.
- **Laestadians and free churches:** compare revival as inner renewal with the welfare state's outward improvement.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Somalis, Assyrians and Syriacs, Chilean exiles:** check *klassresa* stories and associations' homework and language programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land reform, housing, rationalization and quality registers; mixed for self-help; thin for improvement ideas inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** 1700s Swedish for Alströmer and the land reforms; North and South Sámi for revival and settler history; Meänkieli and Finnish for language renewal; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish and Somali for community programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eugenics, the racial-biology institute and the sterilizations of 1934-76 are contested ground; so are Sámi land and herding rights in settler areas and the treatment of Roma and Travellers. Present each account as an account and name each people by its own name.
- **Held for review:** the enclosure ordinances and the sterilization acts were weighed for the land and eugenics strands and passed to Libra, keeping only their effects here; nothing else was set aside.
