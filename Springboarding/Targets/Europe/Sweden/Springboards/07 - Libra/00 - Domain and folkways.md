# Sweden: Partnership and justice, the domain and folkways (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Kalderash Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Somali, Swedish Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the domain of partnership and justice: what a fair bargain and a good partnership are, across bonds between equals, promises, open enemies, the settling of quarrels, marriage and gender ways, and the letter of the law.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne, Halland and Blekinge:** check court books for Danish-era law (*Skånelagen*) and custom kept after 1658.
- **Gotland:** compare *Gutalagen*'s marriage and compensation rules with the mainland laws.
- **Jämtland and Härjedalen:** look for Norwegian-era law, held until 1645, in local court books.
- **Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö against Småland and Norrland counties:** set cohabitation and divorce figures side by side.
- **Sámi of each language area, with and without reindeer:** keep grazing disputes inside *samebyar* apart from non-herders' claims, through Sámi Parliament papers.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check marriage and inheritance practice in Meänkieli and Finnish-language parish and family papers.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Kale) and the *resande*:** ask how their own organizations describe matches and the settling of quarrels, apart from police and welfare files.
- **Congregations:** set the Jewish, Muslim and Assyrian or Syriac marriage contracts, and the Church of Sweden, Catholic, Laestadian, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant wedding services, beside civil registration.
- **Estonian Swedes and the Chilean exiles of 1973:** look in association archives for how leadership was re-formed in exile.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland (with the Finland Swedes and Åland), Denmark, Estonia, Iceland; the Swedish-American Midwest, where the nation's findings apply.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, court books and family statistics; mixed for rivalry and civility; thin for minority dispute settling in the communities' own words. No territory has been fixed for Sweden and no change to its lands is recorded, so the Libra sheets describe the country as it is today; its post-war extent remains open.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Swedish for the provincial laws and seventeenth-century Swedish for court books; North Sámi; Meänkieli, Finnish, Romani and Yiddish; Arabic and Somali for religious marriage contracts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi land rights, the treatment of Roma and Travellers, and religious marriage are contested; give each side's account by name. Court books name private families: cite by case.
- **Held for review:** policing and public order came up beside dispute settling and went to Capricorn; no named pointer was set aside.
