# Sweden: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Persian, Kurdish, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten herding areas against Stockholm:** compare reactions to the Girjas ruling in northern and national press.
- **Sámi, reindeer-owning and not:** check whether the truth commission's hearings record different wrongs for each group.
- **Tornedalians:** read the commission's testimony on schooling beside parish records of the same years.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Kale) and the *resande*:** set each group's response to the white paper side by side, from their own organizations.
- **Iranian and Kurdish exiles:** check community media on the Nouri trial and the exchange.
- **Bosniaks:** look at how Srebrenica remembrance events in Sweden speak of justice still owed.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje):** check what the Riksdag's 2010 vote on the 1915 genocide meant in each federation's press.
- **Eritreans:** follow the campaign for the imprisoned journalist Dawit Isaak as a justice cause.
- **The Jewish community:** check its responses to the 1990s commission on Nazi assets in Sweden.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime statistics, victim surveys and court records; mixed for historical redress; thin for justice as Roma, *resande* and newer communities describe it in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Swedish for the land law; North, Lule and South Sámi for commission testimony; Meänkieli; Romani; Persian and Kurdish for exile press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** truth-commission testimony is personal and may be restricted; the Nouri exchange, the white paper and the Girjas ruling are contested; give each party's account. Police and court records name private people.
- **Held for review:** trust in the police and the courts' legitimacy came up and went to Capricorn; the sources weighed for punishment and redress raised no naming-rule question.
