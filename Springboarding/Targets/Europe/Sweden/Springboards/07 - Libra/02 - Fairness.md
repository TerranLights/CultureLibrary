# Sweden: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Somali, Chilean Spanish. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna:** check *storskifte* records for how splitting land among all heirs played out over generations.
- **Skåne:** compare estate tenants' records from the *enskifte* with freeholders' in Småland.
- **Stockholm against Norrbotten and Jämtland towns:** set housing-queue waits beside municipal vacancy data to see where the queue rule bites.
- **Gotland and Öland:** look at shares in fishing teams and grazing commons in island museum records.
- **Herding Sámi in each *sameby*:** check member and county records on herd caps, and Sámi writers on who loses out.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check union and factory records for pay grading of Finnish-speaking workers.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma, and *resande* families:** find DO cases on refused service and housing, beside their organizations' accounts.
- **Somalis, Syrians, Iraqis and Eritreans:** check DO filings and associations' reports on job applications and housing queues.
- **Chilean exiles and Bosniaks:** compare exile associations' views on how equal treatment met them after 1973 and the 1990s.
- **Parish and free-church preaching:** check Church of Sweden, Pentecostal and Mission Covenant sermons on the vineyard workers' equal wage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pay, tax, inheritance and land-reform records; mixed for playground and everyday fairness; thin for minority communities' own terms for fair shares.
- **Languages needed:** eighteenth- and nineteenth-century Swedish for land-reform protocols; North Sámi for *sameby* minutes; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Somali and Spanish for association material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** income data are public but personal; housing-queue fraud and discrimination cases name individuals; herd caps are a live quarrel between *samebyar* and the state.
- **Held for review:** equality as an ideal leans toward Aquarius and merit toward Capricorn; only the everyday-shares side was kept, and no sources raised a naming-rule question.
