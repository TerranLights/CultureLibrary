## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna, Hälsingland and Värmland:** set *nattfrieri* records side by side to see how the custom's rules varied by parish.
- **Uppland and Östergötland:** compare the provincial laws' terms for the spouses' estate.
- **Stockholm and Skåne against Norrbotten:** compare parental-leave sharing and cohabitation rates by municipality.
- **Sámi herding groups from Karesuando to Jämtland:** trace how working partnerships form and split, from *sameby* records and Sámi memoirs.
- **Tornedalians and Laestadian congregations:** look at what congregation writings say about choosing a spouse.
- **Kurds and Bosniaks:** check the ritual kin made at circumcisions and weddings, in each community's own media.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** look in church and federation magazines for how matches are proposed and blessed.
- **Chilean exiles:** check memoirs on how exile reshaped couples and political comradeship.
- **Roma and *resande* families:** ask the families' own organizations how a marriage is agreed between households.
- **Estonian Swedes:** check how island communities arranged marriages before 1944 and after resettlement.
