# Sweden: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Värmland and Dalarna:** compare hunters', farmers' and conservationists' statements on wolf numbers in county consultations.
- **Norrbotten Sámi in Laponia:** check how *samebyar* and the state each report the joint body's working.
- **Öland and Gotland:** look at grazing-commons rules and current county plans for the alvar.
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Bergslagen mill towns:** set working-hour records of office and shift work side by side.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look for writing on living between two languages, in Meänkieli and Finnish as well as Swedish.
- **Somalis, Iranians and Syrians:** ask parents' groups and associations how they describe holding two ways of life together.
- **Temperance and free-church congregations (IOGT-NTO, Pentecostals, Mission Covenant) against the Church of Sweden:** check teaching on abstinence against moderation.
- **Mosque associations and Jewish congregations:** compare guidance on fitting Ramadan and the Sabbath around Swedish working hours.
- **Laestadian congregations:** check their writings on Sunday rest and on keeping apart from some modern media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for working hours, fiscal rules and environmental policy; mixed for moderation and the political center; thin for the idea of balance in minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Linnaeus's academic Latin and eighteenth-century Swedish; North and Lule Sámi to read Laponia's papers; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Persian and Somali for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** predator policy and grazing rights are a live quarrel between herders, hunters and conservationists; give each side's account. *Lagom* is easily made a cliché: study it as a word in use, never as a national trait.
- **Held for review:** the clinical side of burnout was already scouted under Virgo's health and is not repeated; balance in broadcast news is left to impartiality in this sign. The ecology and design sources weighed here raised nothing under the naming rules.
