# Sweden: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish and Meänkieli, Romani, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gothenburg:** set the scandal coverage beside the city's follow-up audits.
- **Small Norrland and Småland municipalities against Stockholm:** check municipal audits for how often *jäv* is raised where council members know everyone.
- **Skåne:** read the police-register case through Roma organizations, the police and JK side by side.
- **Sámi Parliament and the *samebyar*:** check the electoral roll and membership rules for how the bodies guard against favoring kin.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look for complaints about Finnish-speaking clients' treatment by agencies, in Finnish and Meänkieli media.
- **Arabic, Persian and Bosnian speakers:** ask associations and memoirists how judges and officials in Sweden compare, in their eyes, with those they knew before.
- **Kalderash, Kale and *resande* families:** check DO and JK cases as each group's organizations describe them.
- **Church of Sweden members and free-church congregations:** compare the party-list system with the free churches' own ways of choosing leaders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, governance research and hiring experiments; mixed for everyday favoritism; thin for impartiality as minority communities name it.
- **Languages needed:** Reformation-era Swedish for Olaus Petri; North Sámi for Sámi Parliament rules; Finnish and Meänkieli; Romani for Roma groups' statements on the register; Arabic, Persian and Bosnian for newcomers' comparisons of officials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Roma register and the neutrality commission are contested; give the state's and the affected parties' accounts by name. Talk of kin networks in crime is part of the integration debates: never let it stand for any community.
- **Held for review:** wartime neutrality as a policy toward belligerents is left to diplomacy and peace at any price in this sign; the hiring-bias experiments were already scouted under Virgo's discernment and were left out here; nothing was set aside under the naming rules.
