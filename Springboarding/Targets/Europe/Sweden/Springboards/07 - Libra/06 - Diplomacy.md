# Sweden: Diplomacy (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with medieval Latin), North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Persian, Arabic, Kurdish, Tigrinya, Chilean Spanish (Tigrinya terms still to be found with Eritrean Swedes' help). **Concept in one line:** managing relations and disagreements between parties by talk, tact and negotiation rather than force: between nations and peoples, and in everyday social life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Haparanda and the Torne Valley:** check twin-town council minutes for how two municipalities across a border settle shared matters.
- **Skåne:** check regional papers on the Øresund border controls (2015-16) and the talks with Denmark that followed.
- **Värmland and Jämtland:** compare local memory of 1905 along the Norwegian border with Stockholm's record.
- **Gotland:** check how islanders describe the yearly influx of Almedalen.
- **Sámi:** compare Sámi Parliament and Saami Council papers with the Swedish government's own.
- **Kurds:** read Kurdish associations' statements on the NATO memorandum beside government replies.
- **Eritreans:** check how the Isaak campaign is discussed in the community's own media.
- **Chilean exiles:** compare memoirs of the embassy refuge with Swedish foreign ministry files.
- **Iranians and Assyrians or Syriacs:** look at how each community reacted when Swedish votes or trials strained ties with a home state.
- **Sweden Finns:** check whether Finnish-speaking families used children as interpreters in the 1960s and 1970s, in oral-history projects.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state diplomacy and archives; mixed for everyday tact; thin for diplomacy as minority communities practice it among themselves.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin and Swedish for charters; seventeenth-century Swedish and Latin for Oxenstierna; North Sámi; Finnish; Persian, Arabic, Kurdish and Tigrinya; Spanish for exile memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent foreign-ministry files are closed under secrecy rules; the NATO memorandum and the Isaak case are contested, so give each party's account by name.
- **Held for review:** the labor and international mediation sources were already scouted under Gemini and are not repeated; diplomacy as appeasement goes to peace at any price. No pointer met the naming rules' hold.
