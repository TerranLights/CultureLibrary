# Sweden: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish and Meänkieli, Somali, Arabic, Persian, Yiddish, Chilean Spanish. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrland and Dalarna villages against Stockholm suburbs:** compare *byordningar* and work-party records with present neighbor surveys.
- **Skåne estate villages and Småland freeholders:** check how help was owed to a lord against how it was swapped between equals.
- **Gotland:** look at island fishing and farm cooperation in Gotland museum records.
- **Sámi, herding and fishing families in different language areas:** ask how exchange between them is described in Sámi accounts.
- **Sweden Finns:** check oral histories on how the free-movement treaties shaped what workers felt owed and owing.
- **Somalis and Eritreans:** look at savings groups and funeral collections through the associations that run them.
- **The Jewish community:** check burial-society and congregation records for duties owed to members.
- **Chilean exiles:** compare the Chile committees' papers with exiles' memoirs of solidarity repaid.
- **Iranians and Kurds:** check how New Year visiting is returned, in community media.
- **Roma families (Kale, Kalderash) and Traveller (*resande*) families:** learn from their own associations how hospitality is given and returned.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk customs and welfare debates; mixed for everyday gifts and invitations; thin for exchange inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** the land law's fourteenth-century Swedish and the bylaws' early modern Swedish; North Sámi; Finnish and Meänkieli; Somali, Arabic and Yiddish; Spanish for exile papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi offering sites and the practices tied to them can be restricted knowledge; follow the Sámi Parliament's and local *samebyar*'s guidance before publishing locations. Welfare reciprocity is a party-political quarrel: give each account.
