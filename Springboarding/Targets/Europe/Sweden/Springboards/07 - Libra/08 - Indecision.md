# Sweden: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Bosnian, Persian. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and Gothenburg:** compare how each city's voters and politicians handled the congestion-tax choice.
- **Norrbotten, Jokkmokk and Gällivare:** check how long mine and wind-power permits took, and how the waiting is described by *samebyar*, firms and towns.
- **Dalarna, Hälsingland and Småland:** set *årsgång* and other divination records side by side to see which choices were left to omens.
- **Sámi *samebyar*:** compare meeting minutes on contested grazing choices with members' own accounts.
- **Sweden Finns and Tornedalians:** check Finnish-language and Meänkieli press on families weighing a return to Finland or a move south.
- **Muslim communities and Bosniaks:** look at how the guidance prayer is taught for marriage and moves.
- **Laestadian and Pentecostal congregations:** check sermons on leaving decisions to God's will.
- **Iranians and Chilean exiles:** check memoirs on staying in Sweden or going back, a long-deferred choice for many.
- **Gotland and Skåne:** check how county and municipal votes on wind farms and bridges were postponed or reopened.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for referendums, elections and government records; mixed for management and decision research; thin for indecision as minority languages name it.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century Swedish for council minutes; dialect skills for folk records; North Sámi; Finnish and Meänkieli; Arabic, Bosnian and Persian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clinical indecision (depression, obsessive doubt) belongs to health research; keep personal forum posts anonymous. Permit delays in Sápmi are contested: give herders', firms' and the state's accounts.
- **Held for review:** the prohibition vote already appears under balance and the Kallak mine under Taurus, so neither is repeated here; folk divination was kept only as a decision aid. Nothing was held under the naming rules.
