# Sweden: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with dialect nicknames), North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Russian. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ångermanland and Dalarna:** compare witch-trial records to see how accusers and accused differed by parish.
- **Stockholm against Skåne, Gothenburg and Norrland:** collect mocking regional names and jokes to map who pictures whom.
- **The Stockholm archipelago:** check how islanders and the navy each recall the submarine hunts.
- **Uppsala:** look at how the university has handled both Bollhusmötet and race biology in its own histories.
- **Sámi of each area:** check Sámi art, memoirs and the Sámi Parliament's statements on race-biology images and returned remains.
- **The *resande* and Roma (Kale, Kalderash):** look at how their organizations answer the old state inventories and the police register.
- **Sweden Finns:** check oral histories of slurs at work in the 1960s and 1970s, in Finnish and Swedish.
- **The Jewish community:** set community accounts of 1939 beside university records.
- **Muslim communities, Iraqis, Syrians and Somalis:** check each community's own media on being blamed in crime debates, beside Brune's press data.
- **Russian speakers and Baltic Swedes:** look at how changing enemy images since 2014 and 2022 are described in their own associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trials, press archives and race-biology records; mixed for regional mirror labels; thin for projection as minority languages name it.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century Swedish for trial records; dialect skills for parish nicknames; North Sámi; Finnish; Arabic, Persian and Russian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** race-biology photographs show identifiable people, and Sámi bodies ask for care in their use; follow their guidance. The submarine affair, Expo's role and crime-and-immigration debates are contested: give each account by name, and never let a bullet describe a group.
- **Held for review:** rumor-led witch accusations and Finnish-Swedish identity writing were already scouted under Gemini and Aries, so this sheet takes the trials only from the commissions' side; the naming rules kept nothing back from these sources.
