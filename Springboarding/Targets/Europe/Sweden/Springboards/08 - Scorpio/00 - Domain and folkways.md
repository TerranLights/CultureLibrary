# Sweden: Death and transformation, the domain and folkways (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with runic and Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Bosnian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the domain of death and transformation: how death, the dead and change of state are faced; what is hidden and shown; shared resources and inheritance; sex ways and death ways; power as runner-up.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland against Gotland and Öland:** check whether stone-raisers and grave goods differ between runestones and island picture stones.
- **Dalarna and Bergslagen:** compare inventories with enclosure maps for how farms passed among heirs.
- **Skåne, Halland and Blekinge:** read burial registers across the 1658 change of church order.
- **Stockholm and Gothenburg against inland Norrland:** set cremation and ash-grove shares side by side.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding:** look at grave sites and returned remains only through Sámi institutions' accounts.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Laestadian and Finnish-language funerals in congregation records.
- **Jewish, Bosniak, Somali, Kurdish and Syrian Muslim, and Assyrian or Syriac (Södertälje) communities:** compare who washes the body, how soon burial follows, and whether the dead are sent abroad.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** ask their organizations how a dead person's belongings are handled.
- **Chilean exiles of 1973 and Estonian Swedes:** look for memorials kept here for graves in another country.
- **Outside the target, compared only:** Norway; Finland with its Swedish speakers and Åland; Denmark; Estonia; Iceland. Swedish-American Midwest graves fall under the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parish books, inventories and runestones; mixed for sex ways and money talk; thin for minority death customs in their own words. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; with no change to its pre-war lands recorded, these Scorpio sheets cover Sweden as it now stands.
- **Languages needed:** runic Norse for the stones; older Swedish for registers; North Sámi; Finnish and Meänkieli; Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Bosnian and Somali for congregations' burial rules.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi remains gathered in the racial-biology era, and their return, are contested: give the Sámi Parliament's and the holders' accounts by name. Sámi drum and *noaidi* knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** power ways went to Capricorn and inheritance statutes to Libra; among the folk-custom pointers, none was set aside.
