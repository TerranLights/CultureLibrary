# Sweden: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Latin for the mission sources), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Elfdalian, Gutnish, Kurdish, Persian, Tigrinya, Yiddish, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery and redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and beliefs that the dead return.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna (Nås) and Hälsingland:** check parish books for the Jerusalem emigrants and the Erik-Janssonists who left for Bishop Hill.
- **Norrbotten's Laestadians against Västerbotten's EFS congregations:** compare how a conversion is marked in each.
- **Jönköping county against Stockholm:** set free-church membership over time side by side.
- **Sámi of the several areas:** read Christianization, seized drums and today's language work through Sámi-written histories.
- **Tornedalians:** look at Meänkieli revival since its recognition in 2000.
- **Älvdalen and Gotland, including Fårö:** check how each speech association counts new learners.
- **Kiruna's samebyar:** look at how herders' submissions weigh the town move.
- **Kurds, Iranians and Eritreans:** compare spring-renewal and baptism feasts as each community holds them here.
- **Jewish congregations:** check teaching on *tshuve* at the High Holy Days.
- **Chilean exiles:** trace who went back after 1990 and who returned to Sweden.
- **Converts to Christianity among asylum seekers, and their congregations:** compare church records with agency decisions.
- **Comparison only, as separate culture-nations:** Norway, Finland (its Swedish speakers and Åland included), Estonia, Denmark, Iceland; the Midwest's Swedish Americans carry the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revival history, emigration and recovery bodies; mixed for asylum conversions; thin for older Sámi renewal ceremonies, which may not be shared.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Rimbert; nineteenth-century Swedish for revival records; Finnish, Meänkieli and North Sámi for Laestadian sermons; Elfdalian and Gutnish for revival bodies; Kurdish, Persian and Tigrinya for festival material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi Christianization, asylum conversions and exit from extremist groups are contested: give each account by name. Ex-members of churches deserve care.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist teaching on rebirth, named among the concept's avenues, is held as a category for the author; no community or text is named.
