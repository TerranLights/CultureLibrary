# Sweden: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with seventeenth-century court Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Kalderash Romani, Yiddish and Hebrew, Arabic, Persian. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna and Ångermanland:** compare the Mora and Torsåker trial records for who was accused and who testified.
- **Blekinge, Bergslagen and Västerbotten (Rönnskär):** set residents' complaints about poisoned water and soil beside the agencies' reports.
- **North Sámi families relocated south, and the herders already there:** read both sides' accounts in Sámi publications.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check sauna customs and Laestadian church discipline in their own associations' records.
- **Roma (Kalderash, Kale) and the *resande*:** look for how pollution rules and state removals are described, keeping each group apart.
- **Jewish congregations:** check mikveh use and Yom Kippur practice as congregations describe them.
- **Bosniak, Somali, Syrian and Iraqi Muslims:** compare mosque guidance on purity and fasting.
- **Iranians and Kurds:** look at fire-jumping and spring cleaning before the new year in their associations.
- **Laestadians, Jehovah's Witnesses and other closed congregations:** read former members' and the communities' own accounts of exclusion.
- **Stockholm's cultural institutions against provincial ones:** check whether scandal-driven resignations cluster in the capital.
- **Comparison only, never merged in:** Norway; Finland with Åland and its Swedish speakers; Denmark; Estonia; Iceland. Findings here hold for Swedish emigrants in the Midwest.

## 4. Flags

- **Sensitive or restricted:** sterilization, the Roma white paper, Sámi relocations and the State Institute for Racial Biology are contested: give each account by name. Named people in #MeToo cases appear only as public reports name them. Eating disorders need care.
