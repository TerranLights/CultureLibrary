# Sweden: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish of the rhymed chronicles), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Arabic, Kurdish, Persian, Bosnian, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for chronicles, labor and spy history and state inquiries; mixed for infidelity; thin for informing inside minority communities, where sources are mainly security reports.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish verse and sixteenth-century prose; North Sámi; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Bosnian and Chilean Spanish for community writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Baltic extradition, wartime transit for German troops, the church and the Sámi, and diaspora informing are contested: give each account by name. Informer claims can endanger people; cite only published sources.
