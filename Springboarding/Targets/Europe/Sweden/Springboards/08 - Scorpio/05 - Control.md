# Sweden: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with eighteenth-century church Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Arabic, Somali, Kurdish. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so; self-control is Capricorn's, freedom from control is Aries's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lule River samebyar and the dam towns (Porjus, Vietas):** set herders' and hydropower workers' accounts of the regulated river side by side.
- **Sámi counted and not counted as Sámi under *lapp ska vara lapp*:** check who was sent to nomad schools and who was not, in Sámi histories.
- **Tornedalians:** look at testimony on school control of speech in Finnish and Meänkieli.
- **Västmanland and Bergslagen:** compare forest owners', firefighters' and residents' accounts of the 2014 fire.
- **Kalmar, Uddevalla and Gothenburg:** check works-council records for how far line workers steered their own pace.
- **Skåne:** look at cross-border buying from Denmark as a way around the liquor monopoly.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma, and the *resande*:** compare how each group's organizations answered the 2013 register.
- **Kurdish, Iranian, Iraqi, Somali, Assyrian and Syriac families:** compare what youth and women's associations publish on who decides a young person's marriage and outings, beside agency reports.
- **Laestadian and Pentecostal congregations:** read members' and former members' accounts of congregational oversight.
- **Temperance lodges against wine and beer clubs:** set their papers on the monopoly side by side.
- **Kept as comparison, not as part of Sweden:** Norway, Finland with Åland and the Finland Swedes, Denmark, Estonia, Iceland, each with its own alcohol policy history; Swedish Americans of the Midwest carry the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church registers, alcohol policy, workplace studies and state inquiries; mixed for couples' control; thin for control inside minority families in the families' own words.
- **Languages needed:** older church Swedish for registers; North Sámi for Lule River papers; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Somali and Kurdish for associations' writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** honor-related control is a contested framing: give agencies', associations' and critics' accounts by name, and never let a bullet stand for a people. The Roma register, Vipeholm and the dams involve harmed people; cite by report.
- **Held for review:** self-control as a virtue went to Capricorn, freedom from control to Aries, liquor statutes to Libra; no workplace or surveillance source had to be dropped under the naming rules.
