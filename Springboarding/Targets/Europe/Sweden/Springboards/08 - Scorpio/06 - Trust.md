## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm suburbs, small Norrland towns and rural Gotland and Öland:** set neighbor-trust survey answers by municipality side by side.
- **Sámi of each language area:** compare *verdde* ties with farmers and fishers, where remembered, against trust in agencies today.
- **Tornedalians:** check commission testimony for trust in schools and church across generations.
- **Somalis:** look at *ayuuto* and *xawaala* as their associations describe them.
- **Syrians, Iraqis and Eritreans who arrived after 2014:** compare trust in agencies at arrival and years later in MIM-type panels.
- **Jewish community:** check trust in police protection as the congregations' security bodies report it.
- **Roma and the *resande*:** look at trust in social services after the 2013 register, through their organizations.
- **Bosniaks and Chilean exiles:** compare memoirs of first trust in the Swedish state against the states they left.
- **Church of Sweden, Mission Covenant and Pentecostal members:** check confidence in their own church against confidence in other institutions.
- **Neighbors held apart:** Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Estonia and Finland (with Åland and the Finland Swedes) are separate nations whose trust surveys serve only as context; the Midwest's Swedish Americans come under this nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trust surveys, institutional confidence and banking history; mixed for trust inside minority communities; thin for older Sámi *verdde* ties on the Swedish side.
- **Languages needed:** runic Norse; North Sámi and Meänkieli for commission testimony; Somali for savings groups; Arabic, Persian, Bosnian and Spanish for association research.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sámi truth commission, the Roma register and pandemic policy are contested: give each account by name. "High-trust Sweden" is a phrase to test, not to repeat.
- **Held for review:** trust in police and courts is Capricorn's; among the survey and finance pointers, no scholar or dataset was set aside.
