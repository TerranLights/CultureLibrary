## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bohuslän, Västergötland and Östergötland:** check local legends and church lore around the Vänge, Marstrand and Rök sites.
- **Malmö, Stockholm's Järva, Uppsala and Gothenburg:** compare residents' associations' accounts of retaliation with Brå's district figures.
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten herding areas:** read herders' and farmers' accounts of harm to reindeer and the replies.
- **Torne Valley Laestadians:** look at how absolution is used after quarrels in the congregation.
- **Iraqi, Syrian, Kurdish and Somali associations:** check whether they run *ṣulḥa* or elder mediation here, and how members use it.
- **Kalderash and Kale Roma, and the *resande*:** look in their organizations' material for go-betweens who keep a quarrel from growing.
- **Bosniaks:** compare how associations speak of wartime crimes: courts, memory or reprisal.
- **Chilean exiles:** search their writing after 1990 for justice set against revenge.
- **Stockholm football supporters:** set the three clubs' fan-group statements side by side.
- **Not part of the target, for contrast only:** the feud literature of Iceland, Norway, Denmark, Estonia and Finland (including the Finland Swedes and Åland); emigrant Swedes in the Midwest share this nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime statistics, fiction and film; mixed for forgiveness practices; thin for minority mediation customs as practiced in Sweden.
- **Languages needed:** runic Norse for Rök; medieval ballad Swedish; North Sámi and Meänkieli; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for mediators; Bosnian and Spanish for exile writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gang violence, "honor" readings and harm to reindeer are contested: give each account by name and never tie revenge to a people. Victims of shootings and of revenge porn are not to be named beyond public reports.
- **Held for review:** state punishment belongs to Capricorn; the Icelandic family sagas were set aside as Iceland's sources, for comparison only.
