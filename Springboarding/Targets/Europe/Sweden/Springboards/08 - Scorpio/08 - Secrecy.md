# Sweden: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with eighteenth-century Swedish and the Traveller and peddler cants), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Västergötland's Sjuhärad and the *resande*:** compare how the two cants were used, and who still knows them.
- **Jämtland and Småland villages against Stockholm:** read ethnologists' village studies for what neighbors may know of each other.
- **Sámi of the several language areas:** check which knowledge each community keeps restricted, only through Sámi bodies' rules.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look for families who spoke Finnish so children would not follow, and Finnish war children who stayed.
- **Jewish families:** check survivor memoirs for pasts kept from children.
- **Roma and the *resande*:** look at accounts of hiding one's background at school and work.
- **Iranians, Kurds and Eritreans:** compare anonymous writing in diaspora media where home-state surveillance is feared.
- **Adoptees from Chile, South Korea and Colombia:** set their associations' accounts beside the inquiry's findings.
- **LGBT Swedes in free-church congregations and in the Church of Sweden:** compare coming-out memoirs from each.
- **Somali and Bosniak associations:** check what they say may be discussed outside the family.
- **Records-access traditions next door, for contrast:** Finland (whose Swedish speakers and Åland stay Finland's), Norway, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland are their own nations; Midwest Swedish Americans fall under this nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy, the press and societies' own histories; mixed for privacy attitudes; thin for family secrets in minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** eighteenth-century Swedish; the cants; North Sámi; Meänkieli and Finnish; Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Somali and Bosnian for memoirs and media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi restricted knowledge is pointed to, never retold. Cold War disclosures, adoption origins and diaspora surveillance are contested: give each account by name.
