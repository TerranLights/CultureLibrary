# Sweden: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with sixteenth-century chancery Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how it is judged who holds it, how it is held and how it is used; legitimate authority is Capricorn's.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland and Dalarna with Bergslagen:** compare how the Dacke and Engelbrekt risings are remembered locally.
- **Bergslagen mill towns against Stockholm's finance houses:** set *brukspatron* records beside the holding companies' histories.
- **Gotland:** check who gets stage time at Almedalen week in Visby.
- **Norrbotten:** compare state firms' power (LKAB, Vattenfall) with municipal and Sámi voices.
- **Sámi bodies:** set the Sámi Parliament, the *samebyar* and non-herding Sámi associations side by side on who speaks for whom.
- **Tornedalians:** look at STR-T and the councils of Haparanda, Pajala and Övertorneå.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs in Södertälje:** check how churches, football clubs and parties link up locally.
- **Somali, Iraqi, Kurdish, Bosniak and Iranian Swedes:** compare representation in councils with each group's associations' own accounts.
- **Jewish community:** look at how the Central Council represents congregations to the state.
- **Women and men in the party organizations:** compare candidate lists before and after 1991.
- **Comparison only, outside this target:** Norway's and Denmark's corporatist histories, Finland (with Åland and the Finland Swedes), Estonia and Iceland are read beside Sweden, never folded in; Swedish-American power in the Midwest falls under this nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official inquiries, elites and labor; mixed for informal power; thin for power inside minority communities, beyond election figures.
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Swedish for Vasa's letters; nineteenth-century Riksdag Swedish; North Sámi; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali and Tigrinya for association material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi self-government, family wealth and the Knutby case are contested or painful: give each account by name and name victims only as published.
- **Held for review:** legitimate authority, hierarchy and power ways are Capricorn's; the Girjas ruling and other land-rights cases are Libra's.
