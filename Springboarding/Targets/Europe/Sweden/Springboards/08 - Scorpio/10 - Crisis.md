# Sweden: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with early-modern proclamation Swedish), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, family, community or country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Västerbotten, Norrbotten and Värmland:** set famine-year parish deaths and relief records side by side.
- **Gothenburg, Malmö, Uddevalla and Karlskrona:** compare how shipyard towns told the closures in local papers.
- **Bergslagen:** look at steel-town accounts of the 1970s through union and municipal archives.
- **Småland and Halland:** check forest owners' and municipalities' reports after Gudrun.
- **South Sámi herders in Jämtland and Västerbotten, and North Sámi herders farther north:** compare accounts of fallout years and of hard grazing winters.
- **Torne Valley towns:** check how spring ice-break flooding is handled and remembered in Haparanda and Övertorneå.
- **Skåne:** compare the 1943 crossings with the 2015 arrivals at Malmö's station.
- **Syrians, Eritreans, Somalis, Bosniaks, Kurds, Iranians and the Chilean exiles:** look in each group's associations for how the crisis that brought them is remembered here.
- **Estonian Swedes:** check accounts of the 1944 boat crossings.
- **Church of Sweden, Pentecostal and mosque congregations:** compare what each opened as shelter in 2015.
- **Comparison only, not in the target:** Finland (Åland and the Finland Swedes included), Norway, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland, each with its own preparedness tradition; findings here carry to Swedish emigrants in the American Midwest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for famines, preparedness and disaster reviews; mixed for personal crisis; thin for newcomers' own crisis memories in their languages.
- **Languages needed:** the Swedish of early-modern proclamations; North and South Sámi for herders' own words for bad years; Meänkieli and Finnish for famine memory; Arabic, Persian, Somali and Chilean Spanish for arrival stories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2015 reception, the Chernobyl compensation question and wartime neutrality are contested: give each account by name. Helpline data must stay aggregate.
- **Held for review:** emergency powers belong to Capricorn and the clinical side of breakdown was scouted under Virgo; among the preparedness, famine and disaster pointers, nothing needed setting aside.
