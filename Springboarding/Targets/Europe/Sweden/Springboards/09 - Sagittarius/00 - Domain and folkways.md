## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church records and learned history; mixed for faith outside the Lutheran church; thin for the older Sámi religion in Sámi voices. The nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; with no change to its pre-war lands recorded, these Sagittarius sheets cover Sweden as it now stands.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old Norse for Adam and the conversion era; three Sámi languages for drum and church files; Meänkieli and Finnish; Syriac, Arabic and Yiddish for congregations' papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi drums and offering sites (*sieidi*) may hold restricted knowledge; the church's past toward the Sámi is contested: give the Sámi Parliament's and the church's accounts by name.
- **Held for review:** for the religious landscape, Buddhist communities were kept as a category for the author's open question, and one count of South Asian-heritage communities in Sweden's lands was set aside under the standing rule.
