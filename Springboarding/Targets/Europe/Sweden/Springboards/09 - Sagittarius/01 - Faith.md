# Sweden: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Reformation-era Swedish and Latin), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Syriac, Bosnian, Kurdish, Tigrinya, Spanish, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland around Jönköping and Fröderyd against Stockholm:** check how often revival hymns are chosen at funerals, to see where revival faith outlived its movements.
- **Bohuslän, Gotland and the Blekinge coast:** compare votive-ship dates with shipwreck years.
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten coasts:** check *bönhus* (prayer houses) against parish churches for where lay worship gathered.
- **Sámi of the North, Lule, Pite, Ume and South Sámi areas, herding and non-herding:** check Sámi-language worship, hymn and Bible use per language, through Sámi-run sources.
- **Torne Valley villages:** locate every Laestadian prayer house and note which branch meets there and since when.
- **Sweden Finns:** check Finnish-language congregations' records for revival and Lutheran streams.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** compare Syriac Orthodox, Chaldean and Church of the East fasting calendars as each church publishes them.
- **Muslim communities in Norrland and the south:** check the fasting times that northern mosques publish for a summer Ramadan under the midnight sun.
- **Iranians and Kurds:** keep converts, practicing Muslims and secular voices apart in any sample.
- **Eritreans:** check Orthodox Tewahedo and Pentecostal congregations separately.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check Romani-led Pentecostal congregations and Traveller church ties.
- **Jewish community; Catholics, Chilean exiles among them:** check Shabbat attendance by city; check Spanish-language Mass.
- **Beyond the borders, comparison only:** the four Nordic neighbors' churches, Finland's Swedish speakers and Åland among them, and Estonia's; Swedish-American churches follow the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hymnals, revival papers and surveys; mixed for minority congregations; thin for Romani faith life in its own words (ask Romani organizations).
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Swedish for the Vasa Bible; the older written Sámi of the 1755 and 1811 scriptures; Syriac and Tigrinya for liturgy; Kurdish dialects for convert and Muslim voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** conversions claimed in asylum cases are disputed by the agency and by churches: give each account by name. Sámi faith before and under mission needs Sámi institutions' framing.
- **Held for review:** spiritualist circles and Swedenborgian séance traditions were weighed for the faith-healing strand and passed to Pisces; no faith pointer here met the South Asian rule.
