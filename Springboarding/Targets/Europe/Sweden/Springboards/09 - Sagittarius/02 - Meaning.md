# Sweden: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Spanish, Kurdish, Persian, Somali, Greek. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bergslagen mill and mine towns against central Stockholm:** check whether people name work, place or family as the point of life, before and after closures.
- **Skåne's farm parishes against Malmö:** set life stories from rural and city districts of one county side by side.
- **Torne Valley and Norrbotten inland:** check how out-migration of the young shows in local writing about staying or leaving.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, of several language areas:** compare what reindeer work and its loss mean, through Sámi-led studies.
- **Sweden Finns:** check retired factory workers' life stories at Södertälje and Västerås for work as a source of meaning.
- **Chilean exiles of 1973:** look at exile life-history collections for how a political cause carried or lost its meaning.
- **Kurds and Iranians:** check exile associations' memoirs for the place of the struggle at home.
- **Somalis and Eritreans:** compare youth surveys in Rinkeby, Tensta and Gothenburg's suburbs for what young people name as purpose.
- **Laestadians and free-church members against the unaffiliated:** check whether surveys show different answers to the meaning item by faith.
- **Estonian Swedes and the Jewish community:** check postwar memoirs for meaning rebuilt after flight.
- **Beyond the borders, comparison only:** Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Estonia and Finland, the Finland Swedes and Åland included; Swedish Midwest families take the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, radio and well-being surveys; mixed for life-history archives; thin for meaning in minority languages, where community associations are the starting point.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the Vadstena devotional text; North Sámi for Utsi; Meänkieli and Finnish; Spanish, Kurdish, Persian and Somali for exile and youth voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** despair, burnout and suicide need care; closures and the "lost meaning" of a town are contested between residents, owners and the state: give each account by name.
- **Held for review:** suicide counts were left to Scorpio's death sheet, and meditation retreats with Buddhist roots, sold as meaning-seeking, were kept only as a category for the author's open question.
