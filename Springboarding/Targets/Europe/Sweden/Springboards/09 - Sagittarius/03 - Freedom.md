# Sweden: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani, Estonian Swedish dialect, Spanish, Kurdish, Persian, Arabic, Tigrinya, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna and Bergslagen:** check how Engelbrekt and Gustav Vasa's Mora speech are staged locally, against Stockholm's official histories.
- **Skåne and Blekinge:** compare memory of the *snapphanar* after 1658, as resistance or banditry, in local and national accounts.
- **Östergötland and Sörmland estates:** check *statare* records for which parishes kept the system longest.
- **Västernorrland (Ådalen):** compare unions', employers' and soldiers' versions of 1931.
- **North Sámi herding families and the southern herding areas that received them:** check both communities' accounts of the relocations.
- **Tornedalians:** check how the valley remembers the ban on Finnish in school as freedom withheld.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** compare records of exclusion from towns and campgrounds by group, through their own organizations.
- **Kurds:** check exile associations' statements against government statements during the 2022-24 NATO accession talks, presenting each account.
- **Eritreans and Iranians:** check reports on pressure from home governments on exiles, from both community and authority sources.
- **Estonian Swedes, the Jewish community and Bosniaks:** compare arrival stories (1944; the 1943 crossing from Denmark; the 1990s).
- **Muslim communities and free churches:** check what each says about religious freedom in schools.
- **Comparison only, beyond the borders:** Norway, Denmark and Iceland, Estonia, and Finland with Åland and its Swedish-speakers; Swedish-American freedom stories fall under the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, wartime and press history; mixed for minorities' own freedom narratives; thin for Traveller voices, where *resande* associations are the first contact.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the chronicle; North Sámi for relocation testimony; Romani; Kurdish, Persian and Tigrinya for exile politics; Chilean Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** neutrality in the Second World War, the relocations and Kurdish activism are contested: give each party's account by name, and never sum up a group's stance.
- **Held for review:** two legal texts weighed for freedom of movement and belief, the 1751 border codicil on herders' crossings and the 1951 religious-freedom act, were passed to Libra; no freedom pointer met the South Asian rule.
