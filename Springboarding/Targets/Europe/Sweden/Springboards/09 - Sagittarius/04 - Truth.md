# Sweden: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Latin for the 1500s), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Syriac, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience), and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, rulings and surveys; mixed for oral truth-telling; thin for written Romani accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Olaus Magnus; Meänkieli and Finnish for commission testimony; North and South Sámi for knowledge records; Arabic, Syriac, Persian and Somali for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *Estonia* sinking, the Palme case and the submarine question are open disputes: give each party's account by name. Sámi traditional knowledge may be shared only on its holders' terms.
- **Held for review:** courtroom truth (oaths, evidence rules, the Thomas Quick retrials) was weighed and sent to Libra, so only the public and media side stays; no truth pointer came under the South Asian rule.
