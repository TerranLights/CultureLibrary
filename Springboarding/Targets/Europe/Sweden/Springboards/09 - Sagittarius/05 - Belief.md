# Sweden: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with medieval Latin and 1600s Swedish), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani, Arabic, Syriac, Bosnian, Kurdish, Persian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland and Gotland:** check which saints' cults (St Erik; Gotland's local saints) left the most miracle records, and where.
- **Dalarna and Värmland:** compare folk-belief records of the forest spirit with those of Småland, to see whether the belief was told the same way.
- **Jämtland and Härjedalen:** check visitors' and locals' accounts of haunted sites separately.
- **Sámi of the South, Ume, Pite, Lule and North areas:** check through Sámi scholars how older beliefs are described in each language area.
- **Tornedalians:** compare Laestadian teaching on belief with folk-belief records from the same villages.
- **Sweden Finns:** check Finnish-language congregations' confirmation teaching against the Swedish-language one.
- **Muslim communities by origin (Bosniak, Somali, Iraqi, Syrian, Kurdish):** compare what mosque schools teach in *ʿaqīda* classes, through their own materials.
- **Iranians:** keep secular, Muslim, Christian-convert and Bahá'í respondents apart in any survey.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs; Eritreans:** compare children's teaching in the Syriac Orthodox and Eritrean Orthodox churches.
- **Jewish community:** check Orthodox, Conservative and Reform teaching for the three city congregations.
- **Roma and the *resande*:** check Romani organizations for material on beliefs they choose to share.
- **Neighbors held apart for comparison:** Finland (its Swedish-speaking Finns and Åland too), Norway, Estonia, Denmark, Iceland; belief among Swedish Americans takes the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for catechisms, surveys and folk-belief scholarship; mixed for newer beliefs; thin for Romani belief in writing, by the communities' own choice.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for the St Erik legend; 1600s Swedish for Svebilius; South and North Sámi for belief vocabulary; Arabic and Syriac for teaching materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi beliefs recorded by pastors and collectors may hold restricted knowledge; ask Sámi institutions how to use them. Surveys of Muslim belief are often used in political argument: give the authors' aims by name.
- **Held for review:** within the new-movements strand, groups of South Asian origin were set aside as a category under the standing rule, and those with Buddhist roots were kept as a category for the author's open question.
