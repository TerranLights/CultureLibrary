# Sweden: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with 1500s church Swedish and Latin), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Spanish, Bosnian, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (not the legal sense of a guilty verdict).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hälsingland:** compare parishes that lost members to the Erik-Janssonists with those that did not, to see how neighbors judged them.
- **Västerbotten and Värmland against Stockholm and Gothenburg:** check the 1922 vote by county for where temperance convictions held.
- **Bergslagen and Norrbotten:** check union records for refusal and strike-breaking, and how each side named the other.
- **Sámi of the South and North areas, herding and non-herding:** compare stands on mining and wind power in Sámi organizations' statements, and check where they diverge.
- **Tornedalians:** check Laestadian branches' published positions on matters of conscience, each by its own name.
- **Free-church Småland and the Jönköping area:** check how pastors and members took the midwives' case.
- **Muslim communities (Bosniak, Somali, Syrian, Iraqi):** check workplace disputes over conscience as reported by both parties.
- **Kurds and Iranians:** compare exile associations' bulletins for how long-held political stands are passed to the young.
- **Chilean exiles of 1973:** check whether solidarity committees' members stayed, returned or changed causes.
- **Eritreans:** check accounts from both critics and supporters of the home government on pressure in the community.
- **Kale, Kalderash and *resande* campaigners:** ask each group's own organizations for the life stories of its civil-rights activists.
- **Comparison only, abroad:** Finland and its Swedish-speaking minority and Åland, Estonia, Denmark, Norway, Iceland; Bishop Hill and the Swedish-American churches follow the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for popular movements and parliamentary records; mixed for recent activism, where accounts are partisan; thin for minorities' movements in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** 1500s church Swedish and Latin for 1593; Sámi languages for activists' statements; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish and Tigrinya for exile politics; Chilean Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the climate strike, the midwives' case and diaspora politics are contested: present each account by name, and keep any bullet from saying what a group thinks.
- **Held for review:** the European court ruling in the midwives' case and the conscription statutes behind total refusal were weighed and passed to Libra; no conviction pointer met the South Asian rule.
