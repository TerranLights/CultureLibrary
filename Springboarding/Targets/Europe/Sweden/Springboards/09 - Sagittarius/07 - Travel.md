# Sweden: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with runic Old Norse and 1700s Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Romani (the *resande* variety), Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian, Spanish, Estonian Swedish. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys and commuting stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland and Södermanland against Gotland:** check where stones for eastern journeys cluster, and how Gotland's trade finds differ.
- **Småland and Värmland:** compare parish emigration counts with the inquiry's letters, to see who left from where.
- **Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö suburbs:** check long-haul travel figures by income and district.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check how summer tourism figures sit beside year-round residents' accounts.
- **Sámi of each herding district, and non-herding Sámi:** compare route maps and accounts of roads, railways and wind farms that cut the moves.
- **Tornedalians:** check cross-border daily life in Haparanda-Tornio as both towns record it.
- **Kale, Kalderash and the *resande*:** set each group's own account of the years on the road beside parish moving-in and moving-out records.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** check pilgrimage and family travel to Tur Abdin through church and association accounts.
- **Muslim communities:** check Hajj and Umrah travel through Swedish agencies and mosque notices.
- **Somalis, Eritreans and Iraqis:** compare return visits and summer travel, with community and agency sources kept apart.
- **Estonian Swedes and Chilean exiles:** check return journeys after 1991 and 1990, and who stayed.
- **Neighbors, compared only:** Norway, where the Olav trail ends; Finland, with Åland and the Finland Swedes; Denmark; Estonia; Iceland. Swedish-American "roots trips" take the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for explorers, emigration and tourism statistics; mixed for pilgrimage; thin for Traveller and Romani journeys told in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** runic Old Norse; Arabic for Ibn Fadlan; 1700s Swedish for Sparrman; North and South Sámi for herding routes; Somali and Tigrinya for migration journeys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Rus question, Hedin's politics, the 2015 arrivals and children sent abroad are disputed: give each account by name. Herding routes may be shared only on the *samebyar*'s terms.
- **Held for review:** among the eighteenth-century voyage accounts, one was set aside as a category under the South Asian rule, and another account takes its place.
