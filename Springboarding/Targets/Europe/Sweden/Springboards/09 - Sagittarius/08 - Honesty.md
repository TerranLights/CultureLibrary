# Sweden: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gothenburg against Stockholm and Malmö:** compare municipal corruption cases by city, to test whether one city's scandal shows a wider pattern.
- **Skåne and Halland farm roads:** check unmanned farm stands' own notices and press reports for how the honor system fares.
- **Norrbotten and Jämtland:** check how reindeer-theft and earmark disputes are reported in Sámi and local press, keeping theft records apart from herders' accounts.
- **Sámi craftspeople of several areas:** compare who uses the *Sámi Duodji* label and who sells without it.
- **Haparanda and the Torne Valley:** read Meänkieli and Finnish local reporting on smuggling and customs at the open border, from both sides of it.
- **Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis and Bosniaks:** check how small businesses in each community describe inspections and tax rules, from their associations and from the agencies.
- **Jewish community and free-church members:** compare teaching on truthfulness in sermons and youth material.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** ask their organizations which records of fraud accusations against them they reject, and why.
- **Students by background:** check UKÄ figures by subject and by campus, not by origin, to avoid profiling.
- **Other Nordic and Baltic rows, for contrast only:** set corruption cases in Denmark, Norway, Iceland, Estonia and Finland (Åland and the Finland Swedes included) beside Sweden's; Swedish-American firms take the nation's findings.
