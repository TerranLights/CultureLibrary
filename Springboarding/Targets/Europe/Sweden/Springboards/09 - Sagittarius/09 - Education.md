# Sweden: Education (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with academic Latin), Swedish Sign Language, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** formal and higher learning (schools, universities, degrees, scholarship and lifelong study) and the standing of the educated person; learning, curiosity and early schooling stay in Gemini.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppsala and Lund against Umeå and Luleå:** check how far the northern universities (1965, 1971) changed who in Norrland went on to study.
- **Skåne:** compare Lund's early student rolls for Skåne-born against those from the old Swedish provinces.
- **Stockholm's inner city against Botkyrka and Södertälje:** set attainment by district beside each district's age and birthplace mix.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding, of the several language areas:** compare nomad-school memories with Sámi-school curricula today.
- **Tornedalians:** check boarding-home and language-ban memories by village, as the commission recorded them.
- **Sweden Finns and the Deaf community:** compare how each fought for schooling in its own language.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check the 1960s special classes against what their organizations ask of schools now.
- **Jewish community:** check day-school and supplementary-school enrollment over time.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Somalis, Iraqis, Syrians and Bosniaks:** compare weekend schools run by congregations and associations, as each describes its aims.
- **Iranians and Chilean exiles:** check exile graduates' accounts of having degrees recognized, from their associations.
- **Free churches and Muslim communities:** compare seminaries and imam-training proposals.
- **Outside the target, compared only:** the Norwegian, Danish, Estonian, Icelandic and Finnish systems, the Åland and Finland Swedish schools among them; Augustana and other Swedish-American colleges follow the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for university histories and attainment data; mixed for religious education; thin for Romani experience of schooling in its own voice.
- **Languages needed:** academic Latin for early theses; Swedish Sign Language for Deaf education; North and Lule Sámi; Meänkieli; Arabic, Somali and Persian for supplementary schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** nomad schools, boarding homes and the Roma special classes are contested: give Sámi, Tornedalian and Romani accounts beside the state's. Imam training and religious independent schools are disputed: present each side by name.
- **Held for review:** the school statutes (the 1842 elementary-school act and the Education Act) were sent to Libra and Capricorn; among the supplementary schools, those of South Asian-heritage communities in Sweden's lands were set aside as a category.
