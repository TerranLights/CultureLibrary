# Sweden: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with 1600s church Swedish), Meänkieli and Finnish, North Sámi, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali, Kurdish. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Swedish | *sekt*, *slutet sällskap*, *avhoppare* | sect; closed group; defector, leaver | *sekt* is used as a charge: note who uses it |
| Swedish | *kreationism*, *intelligent design* | creationism | school-debate vocabulary |
| Swedish | *radikalisering*, *våldsbejakande extremism* | radicalization; violence-endorsing extremism | the second is the state's term of art |
| Meänkieli / Finnish | *vanhoillislestadiolaisuus*; *esikoiset* | conservative Laestadianism; the Firstborn branch | Swedish forms (*gammallaestadianer*, *förstfödda*) *check* |
| Arabic | أصولية *uṣūliyya*; سلفية *salafiyya* | fundamentalism; Salafism | both are disputed labels; record who uses them |
| Persian | بنیادگرایی *bonyādgerāyi* | fundamentalism | exile press, often about the Iranian state |
| Bosnian | *vehabije* | "Wahhabis" | an outsiders' label among Bosniaks, *check* |
| Somali; Kurdish | *xagjir*; *tundrew* | extremist | both *check* |

English keywords: Word of Faith, Knutby, Salafism report, Muslim Brotherhood report, creationism, leaving high-control groups.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppsala:** compare Livets Ord's own histories with the 1980s press record and with university studies.
- **Jönköping, northern Småland and Västergötland:** check where *Svenska Folkbibeln* is used in place of *Bibel 2000*, congregation by congregation.
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten coasts:** compare EFS and Pentecostal congregations' teaching on creation in their own materials.
- **Torne Valley and Lapland:** set each Laestadian branch's statements beside the accounts of those who left, without merging the branches.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check Sámi accounts of strict mission teaching and of Laestadian life, through Sámi institutions.
- **Gothenburg's Angered and Bergsjön, Stockholm's Järva and Örebro's Vivalla:** compare the 2018 study's claims with residents' and mosques' replies.
- **Bosniaks:** check how Bosnian imams' association and younger currents describe their differences.
- **Somalis, Iraqis, Syrians and Kurds:** keep secular, practicing and strictly observant voices apart, and record who applies "fundamentalist" to whom.
- **Iranians:** check how exiles apply the word to the state they left.
- **Jewish community:** compare Orthodox and Reform congregations' publications on observance.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Eritreans:** check how Orthodox churches' teaching is described by members and by younger evangelical converts.
- **Across the border, for contrast:** the Laestadian heartlands in Finland and Norway, the Finland Swedes and Åland, and Denmark, Estonia and Iceland stay comparison rows; strict Swedish-American churches take the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state reports and the Christian press; mixed for scholarship on Salafi and Laestadian currents; thin for members' own accounts of closed groups.
- **Languages needed:** 1600s church Swedish for the *hustavla*; Finnish and Meänkieli for Laestadian branches; Arabic, Somali and Bosnian for mosque teaching; Persian for exile writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2017 and 2018 reports, Knutby and the Laestadian branches are contested: present each account as an account, by each community's own name. "Fundamentalist" and "sect" are charges, not descriptions.
- **Held for review:** the 2010 ban on home education and the rules on confessional schools were sent to Libra; among strict new movements, those of South Asian origin were set aside as a category under the standing rule.
