# Sweden: Authority and duty, the domain and folkways (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish and Old Gutnish), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Bosnian, Arabic, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the domain of authority and duty: who commands, who obeys and what is owed; career, status and reputation; age, time, rank, order and power ways as folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm, Uppland and the Mälardalen against Bergslagen:** compare state officials' records with the ironworks masters' (*brukspatroner*) on who gave orders.
- **Dalarna and Småland:** set the Dalecarlian risings and the Dacke feud (1542) beside crown records, for local memory of resistance.
- **Gotland:** read *Gutalagen* and the *Guta saga* on the island's assembly and its compact with the king.
- **Skåne, Halland and Blekinge:** check how local histories tell the change from Danish to Swedish rule (1658).
- **Västerbotten, Norrbotten and the Torne Valley:** compare Laestadian lay preachers' standing with the clergy's, from both sides' records.
- **Sámi of each language area, herding and non-herding:** compare *sameby* rolls with the Sámi Parliament's electoral roll as two sources of standing.
- **Sweden Finns, with Värmland's Forest Finns:** look for Finnish-language parish and union records.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check which associations speak for each group, and how the state chose partners for consultation.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Bosniaks, Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** compare association boards, church hierarchies and exile parties, as each describes itself.
- **Jewish community, Estonian Swedes, Church of Sweden, free churches, Catholics, Muslim communities:** check how each picks leaders.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland (with its Swedish speakers and Åland), Estonia, Denmark, Iceland; the Swedish Midwest takes Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state records, parish registers and surveys; mixed for workplaces; thin for Roma, *resande* and newer communities in their own words. Sweden's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; no change to its pre-war lands is recorded, so these sheets cover Sweden as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish, Old Gutnish and Old Icelandic for the laws and Snorri; Sámi languages for Sámi Parliament papers; Finnish and Meänkieli; Romani, Bosnian, Arabic, Somali and Spanish for community bodies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi self-government, the Sámi Truth Commission, Roma dispute-settling and the racial-biology era are contested; give each body's account by name. Parish registers name private people.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the domain-wide sources weighed (state inquiries, estate debates, foreigners' guides) raised no naming-rule question.
