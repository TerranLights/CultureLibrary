# Sweden: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Romani, Persian, Kurdish, Spanish, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and Mälardalen counties against Norrbotten and Västerbotten:** compare trust in police and agencies by county, where the polls allow.
- **Bergslagen mill towns:** set the ironworks patron's authority beside the union's in local histories and oral-history collections.
- **Skåne's estate parishes against Småland's smallholdings:** check manor records for how far a landlord's command reached.
- **Älvdalen and the Dalarna villages:** look for village-meeting (*byalag*) records on who could call people to common work.
- **Sámi herding groups, *samebyar*, and the Sámi Parliament:** separate three layers of authority, and Sámi outside the *samebyar*, as Sámi bodies describe them.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare Laestadian elders' standing with schoolteachers' and foremen's, in Meänkieli and Finnish sources.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check their organizations' and the police's separate accounts of the Skåne police register revealed in 2013.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje):** read church and lay federations' own papers on where clerical authority ends.
- **Chilean exiles, Iranians, Kurds, Eritreans:** compare exile associations on authority in the states left behind; for Eritreans, give the associations' and the Swedish press's accounts of the homeland government's reach.
- **Somalis, Bosniaks, Iraqis, Syrians:** check associations' programs on elders' and parents' standing.
- **Church of Sweden, free churches, Catholics, the Jewish community, Muslim communities:** compare episcopal, congregational and lay authority, as each describes itself.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trust polls, agency records and labor history; mixed inside families; thin inside Roma, *resande* and newer communities, in their own languages.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Skåne Roma register, state authority over the Sámi and Eritrean community politics are contested; give each party's account by name. Pandemic policy remains disputed.
