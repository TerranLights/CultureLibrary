# Sweden: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Arabic, Bosnian, Somali, Yiddish, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland and Västergötland soldier-croft parishes:** compare muster rolls *rote* by *rote* to see how the soldier duty fell on farms.
- **Jämtland and Härjedalen:** check how levy and tax duties changed after the move from Norwegian to Swedish rule in 1645.
- **Gotland:** look into how service in the island's own 1800s militia was organized.
- **Torne Valley and Norrbotten:** compare work-bee (*talkoo*) customs in Meänkieli memoirs with parish records.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check what Sámi organizations say a family owes the herd and the land, and how it differs by area.
- **Sweden Finns:** look at Finnish war children's (*krigsbarn*) memoirs for duties to two families.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** look in memoirs and association papers for what is said to be owed to parents and kin.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks:** compare money sent home and hometown associations' collections, from bilateral remittance figures and associations' reports.
- **Chilean exiles:** follow the 1990s debates on the *retorno*, and who felt bound to go back.
- **Church of Sweden members against Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations:** compare the church fee with tithing as giving owed.
- **Jewish community and Muslim communities:** check how each congregation's own bodies set out members' duties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for conscription, levy and survey records; mixed for family duties; thin for duty as smaller communities state it in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the levy; early modern Swedish for muster rolls; North Sámi; Finnish and Meänkieli; Arabic, Bosnian, Somali and Yiddish for congregation and association papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** muster rolls and grave registers name private people; conscription of Sámi and Roma men, and refusals on grounds of conscience, call for each side's account.
- **Held for review:** none; the religious-duty pointers weighed for Muslim, Jewish and free-church communities stayed as comparison rows, and none raised a naming-rule question.
