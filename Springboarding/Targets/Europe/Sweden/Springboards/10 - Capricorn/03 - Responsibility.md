# Sweden: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), Swedish Sign Language, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jämtland and Härjedalen:** set Fitinghoff's story beside the local famine records of the 1860s.
- **Stockholm against Norrland inland municipalities:** compare how often auditors refuse discharge to committees.
- **Gothenburg (Hisingen):** read the 1998 Backa disco-fire inquiry beside the bereaved families' accounts of who answered.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check how Sámi organizations received the church's apology, area by area.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** look at their organizations' claims under the sterilization act and against child removals.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** check Finnish- and Meänkieli-speaking workers' memoirs on safety at mines and mills.
- **Somalis, Syrians, Iraqis, Kurds, Iranians:** compare community reports on children brokering language for parents.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje):** check family-firm histories for who takes charge when a founder steps down.
- **Laestadian congregations and the Church of Sweden:** compare confession and forgiveness among believers with church confession, as each describes it.
- **Jewish community:** check its responses to the state's Forum for Living History on Swedish responsibility in the 1940s.
- **Chilean exiles and Bosniaks:** see how exile associations spoke of who answers for the coup and the war they fled.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, committee reports and surveys; mixed for responsibility in households; thin for blame and amends as Roma, *resande* and newer communities describe them.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the provincial law; Swedish Sign Language for signed sources; North Sámi and Meänkieli; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali and Bosnian for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sterilization, racial biology, the church and the Sámi, and the Backa fire are contested; present each account as an account. Age-of-responsibility proposals are live politics.
- **Held for review:** none; large firms' sustainability reports were weighed and dropped as marketing, not set aside under any rule.
