## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Västerbotten's sawmill towns against Bergslagen's mill towns:** compare temperance-lodge membership and bell rules for work.
- **Gothenburg's Torslanda line against Kalmar and Uddevalla:** check workers' oral histories for how each pace of work felt.
- **Dalarna and the Vasaloppet villages against Stockholm:** compare who enters endurance races, by home county and job.
- **Sámi of each language area:** separate herding families' training of children in the herd from boarding-school rules, as Sámi describe both.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** collect testimony on punishment for speaking Finnish or Meänkieli at school.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check their own organizations on schooling rules imposed on their children.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, the Jewish community, Muslim communities, Laestadians, Pentecostals:** compare fasting, abstinence and Sabbath rules as each community teaches them.
- **Arabic-speaking parents:** check community media and the social services' own statements on the 2022 dispute over child-care orders.
- **Somalis, Kurds, Bosniaks, Iranians:** look for community press on the striking ban as new parents met it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school, temperance and labor records; mixed for home discipline (survey-based); thin for self-discipline in smaller communities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** early modern Swedish for the Church Law; North Sámi; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Syriac, Persian, Bosnian and Somali for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** nomad-school and Finnish-ban testimony; the 2022 child-care dispute is contested and was the subject of a disinformation campaign by some accounts; present each party's account.
- **Held for review:** for spiritual disciplines, Buddhist meditation practice in Sweden was kept as a category for the author's open question; no community named.
