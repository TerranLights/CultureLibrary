# Sweden: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali, Syriac, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Småland (Gnosjö, Älmhult) against Stockholm:** compare small-firm enterprise lore with the capital's tech and finance careers.
- **Norrland inland municipalities against Luleå and Boden:** check young people's moving patterns beside the new steel and battery projects.
- **Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** look at engineering career ladders in the big manufacturers' company histories.
- **Malmö (Rosengård) against Lund:** set football academies and the university side by side as two routes up.
- **Sámi:** check Sámi Parliament business-support records for what Sámi entrepreneurs aim at, in their own terms.
- **Tornedalians:** follow out-migration to the south in Meänkieli memoirs and municipal data.
- **Sweden Finns:** compare the 1960s-70s labor migrants' hopes with their children's schooling, from Finnish-language press.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje):** read Assyriska FF's and Syrianska FC's histories for how a rise in sport was framed by each club.
- **Iranians, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks:** compare SCB education registers by birth country with how community media speak of degrees.
- **Somalis and Eritreans:** look at business registrations and associations' programs on youth careers.
- **Chilean exiles and their children:** check exile and second-generation writing on what was given up and what was built.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** take aspirations for schooling from Roma organizations' own reports.
- **Outside Sweden:** Norwegian and Danish uses of Jante for contrast only; Swedish-American success stories in the Midwest take the nation's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mobility, education and sick-leave statistics; mixed for how ambition is spoken of (press and memoir); thin for smaller communities' own views.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the chronicle; Finnish and Meänkieli; Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali and Spanish for community sources; Syriac for church-linked clubs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** attainment by birth country is easily misread as group traits; report measured gaps only, with causes left open. Burnout data come from medical records.
- **Held for review:** none; founders' and celebrities' memoirs were weighed and cut to two, and none touched the naming rules.
