# Sweden: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with early modern Swedish), North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gothenburg against Stockholm and Malmö:** compare procurement and hiring scandals, and whether *vänskapskorruption* is a local or national charge.
- **Uppsala and Lund against Umeå and Luleå:** check how old and new universities honor degrees and doctorates.
- **Västergötland and Småland furniture towns:** look for journeyman tests in furniture making and who still takes them.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** see which crafts and skills Sámi bodies certify, and how duodji masters are recognized.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare grades in Sweden Finnish independent schools with parents' stated reasons for choosing them.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** take accounts of barriers to grades and tests from Roma bridge-builders' and organizations' reports.
- **Applicants with Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali or Tigrinya names:** compare field-experiment results by name group and occupation.
- **Chilean exiles and Bosniaks:** check how exile writing contrasts *pituto* and *štela* with Swedish hiring.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** look at scholarships awarded by churches and associations, and on what grounds.
- **Church of Sweden against Pentecostal and Mission Covenant congregations:** compare sermons on grace and works as teachings on deserving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for grades, tests and hiring experiments; mixed for honors; thin for merit as smaller communities define it.
- **Languages needed:** early modern Swedish for ennoblement letters; North Sámi for duodji; Finnish and Meänkieli; Arabic, Persian, Bosnian and Chilean Spanish for community and exile sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** name-based hiring results invite group generalizations; report callback gaps as measured, with causes left open. The 2018 Academy crisis involved criminal proceedings; keep to the public record.
- **Held for review:** none; matched-application studies were weighed and the earliest well-known one kept, with no naming-rule issue.
