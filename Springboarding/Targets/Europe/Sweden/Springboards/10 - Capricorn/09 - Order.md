# Sweden: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm (Rinkeby, Husby), Gothenburg (Angered, Biskopsgården), Malmö (Rosengård):** compare police reports with residents' and associations' accounts of order in the same places.
- **Uppsala and Lund against other university towns:** check how Walpurgis Night is policed and how students describe it.
- **Småland:** set the Gudrun reports beside forest owners' oral accounts.
- **Dalarna and Härjedalen:** look at summer-pasture (*fäbod*) rules as an old order of land use.
- **Norrbotten (Kiruna, Malmberget):** collect residents' and Sámi villages' accounts of the town moves.
- **Sámi, herding and non-herding:** compare how Sámi bodies describe a well-ordered landscape with the forestry model's own description.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and the *resande*:** check their organizations' statements on begging bans, kept apart from the situation of EU migrants.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Syrians, Iraqis, Kurds, Assyrians and Syriacs:** look at residents' associations in Million Programme areas and what they ask of police and landlords.
- **Jewish community and Muslim communities:** compare how each describes the security around its buildings.
- **Comparison only:** Danish and Norwegian housing-area policies, for contrast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for police, housing and planning records; mixed for order in the home (older ethnology is strong, newer is thin); thin for order as newer communities define it.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the peace oaths; Meänkieli and Finnish; Arabic, Persian, Somali and Bosnian for residents' media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the police area list, the Husby events, begging bans and gang violence are contested; present each as an account and never let a place stand for its people.
- **Held for review:** none; cosmic order was weighed and left thin, since its Swedish sources sit in religious and scientific works scouted under other signs.
