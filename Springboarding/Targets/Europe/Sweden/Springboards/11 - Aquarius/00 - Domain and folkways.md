# Sweden: Community and ideals, the domain and folkways (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Kurdish, Persian, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the domain of community and ideals: what binds people into groups and what they hope for; associations, humanitarian ideals, the future; settlement, migration, hospitality and joining as social ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen beside Bergslagen's mill towns:** check whether the employer or a web of associations ran leisure, from company and union papers.
- **Uppland, Östergötland and Skåne's plains:** set enclosure maps against parish books to date each village's breakup.
- **Småland, Värmland and Halland:** compare parishes' emigration losses with the associations that stayed.
- **Dalarna (with Elfdalian Älvdalen) and Gotland (with Fårö):** look for *byalag* customs written down in dialect.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten, Norrbotten and the Torne Valley:** compare prayer houses of the Laestadians with Folkets hus halls as places to gather.
- **Sámi, split by language area and by herding status:** *sameby* rolls and Sámi associations enroll different people; read both.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns, Estonian Swedes:** find each federation's founding papers.
- **Jews, Kale and Kalderash Roma, *resande*:** list each group's own associations, and mark where only outsiders' files exist.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Syrians, Iraqis, Iranians, Kurds, Bosniaks, Södertälje's Assyrians and Syriacs, Chileans of the 1973 exile:** compare churches, mosques, clubs and exile parties in their own publications.
- **Congregations (Church of Sweden, Pentecostal, Mission Covenant, Catholic, Muslim):** check which keep membership rolls.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland (Finland Swedes and Åland included), Estonia, Denmark, Iceland; Swedish America takes Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in movement archives, surveys and inquiries; mixed on home hospitality; thin for Roma, *resande* and the newest communities in their own words. No post-war lands have been settled and none changed, so the sheets for this sign cover present-day Sweden.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for bylaws; dialect for Isof replies; Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish for minority federations; Arabic, Kurdish, Persian, Somali, Tigrinya and Spanish for newer associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial biology, Sámi land and herding rights, the Travellers' and Roma's treatment and the 1945 rescue accounts are contested; cite each account by its source.
- **Held for review:** none; the movement, aid and settlement sources weighed for this domain raised no naming-rule question.
