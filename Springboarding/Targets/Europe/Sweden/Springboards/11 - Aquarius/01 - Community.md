# Sweden: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, North Sámi, Syriac (Turoyo), Arabic, Bosnian, Persian, Somali, Spanish, Swedish Sign Language. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norrbotten church-town parishes and the Torne Valley:** compare seasonal gatherings with year-round village life, from parish and Laestadian records.
- **Lule Sámi of Jokkmokk with South Sámi of Jämtland and Härjedalen:** check how each area's meeting places and markets differ in the sources.
- **Kiruna and Malmberget:** follow residents' own surveys and LKAB's, side by side, through the move.
- **Järva, Rosengård, Angered:** compare residents' associations with the planners' descriptions of the same estates.
- **Gotland and Öland parishes; Småland and Värmland forest villages:** look for village halls and local yearbooks that record who belonged.
- **Skåne's and Halland's villages under commuter growth:** check whether newcomers join old associations, from membership rolls.
- **Uppland and Östergötland:** use parish statistics to compare Church of Sweden life in towns and countryside.
- **Södertälje's Assyrians and Syriacs:** keep each self-name's churches, clubs and media apart.
- **Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Iranians, Chileans:** check where each gathers (mosque, church, association, sports field) by their own notices.
- **Kale and Kalderash Roma, *resande*, Jews, Sweden Finns, Deaf Swedes:** compare community bodies and where their records are kept.
- **Comparison only:** neighborhoods and villages in Norway, Finland, Estonia, Denmark and Iceland; Swedish-American settlements fall under Sweden's own results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing, health surveys and parish counts; mixed for village halls; thin for Roma and *resande* communities' own accounts and for the newest arrivals.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for the Land Law; Meänkieli and Lule Sámi for Norrbotten gatherings; Turoyo and Arabic for Södertälje media; Bosnian, Somali, Persian and Spanish for association notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Roma white paper, the suburbs' public image and Kiruna's losses are contested; quote each side under its own name. The disaster's victims' families deserve care.
- **Held for review:** none; the faith communities weighed here were Christian, Jewish and Muslim, and no pointer needed setting aside.
