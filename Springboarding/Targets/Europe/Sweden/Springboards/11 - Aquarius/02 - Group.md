# Sweden: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Norse runic forms), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Arabic, Syriac, Kurdish, Bosnian, Somali, Persian. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland and the Mälardalen runic districts against Gotland's picture stones:** check whether group memorials name partners, kin or crews.
- **Ådalen, Norrbotten's mining towns and Bergslagen:** compare union records with company records on strikes, and who crossed lines.
- **Stockholm's three football clubs, and Gothenburg's IFK and GAIS:** check supporters' own fanzines for how rival crowds draw borders.
- **Student nations by home province (Norrland, Småland, Värmland, Skåne, Gotland):** compare their rolls to see whether region of birth still sorts students.
- **Torne Valley Laestadian branches:** separate each branch's own histories before comparing them.
- **Sámi herding groups by *sameby*, and Sámi outside herding:** check who decides inside a *sameby*, from meeting minutes and testimony.
- **Travellers (*resande*), Kale and Kalderash Roma:** compare how each was counted by the state and how each counts itself.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs:** keep both self-names; quote each side's own bodies.
- **Kurds, Iranians, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks:** look for exile parties, clan or regional associations and mosque boards in each group's own notices.
- **Men conscripted to the wartime labor companies:** compare their memoirs with the army's files.
- **Comparison only:** unions, sects and fan crowds of Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland stay outside; the American Midwest's Swedish lodges inherit Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for unions, elections and student nations; mixed for supporters; thin and hostile-sourced for the *tattare* inventory and wartime camps.
- **Languages needed:** runic Old Norse for the stones; Latin and Middle Low German for guild statutes; Meänkieli and Finnish for Laestadian minutes; Turoyo, Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for diaspora disputes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Travellers' inventory, the naming dispute and clan terms touch living people; give every account its own name and never a group's verdict on another.
- **Held for review:** none; group sources weighed here (exile parties, sects, supporters) included nothing under the naming rule.
