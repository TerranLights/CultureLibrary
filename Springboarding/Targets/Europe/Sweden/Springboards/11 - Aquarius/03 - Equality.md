# Sweden: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani (Kalderash), Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Skåne's manor country against Dalarna's and Norrland's freeholding parishes:** check land records for how far estate tenancy shaped later views of rank.
- **Stockholm's inner city against Järva, Rosengård and Angered:** set SCB income and school results by district beside residents' own groups' accounts.
- **Norrbotten and Västerbotten against the Mälardalen:** compare regional equalization debates in local press and county reports.
- **Sámi, herders and non-herders:** check how the nomad-school rule split families by occupation.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** compare school-language testimony across the two, before and after minority status (2000).
- **Roma (Kalderash, Kale) and Travellers:** look for each group's housing and school campaigns in its own associations' papers.
- **Women, by class and origin:** compare KvinnSam collections with migrant women's associations' records.
- **Somalis, Iraqis, Syrians, Iranians, Eritreans:** use audit studies and DO reports to check where unequal treatment is reported, group by group.
- **Deaf and disabled Swedes:** compare Sveriges Dövas Riksförbund's equality claims with the Independent Living movement's.
- **Congregations (Lutheran, Pentecostal, Covenant, Catholic, Jewish, Muslim):** check who may preach, lead or sit on boards, from each body's own rules.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia, Iceland; Swedish Midwest communities follow Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for income, gender and benefit statistics; mixed for manners; thin and largely outsider-written for Roma and *resande* before the 1960s.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish and runic Norse for early status words; Meänkieli and Finnish for commission testimony; Romani for Taikon's circle; Arabic, Somali and Persian for migrants' own reports.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial-biology studies (Uppsala, 1922), the sterilization laws (1934-76), Sámi and Tornedalian schooling and integration debates are disputed ground; give each account under its author's name. Truth Commission testimony may carry restrictions.
- **Held for review:** none; the equality sources considered (audit studies, commissions, movement archives) needed no naming-rule set-aside.
