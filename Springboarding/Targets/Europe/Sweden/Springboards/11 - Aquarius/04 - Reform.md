## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dalarna and Småland:** check how the Reformation and the Dacke feud are told locally against royal chronicles.
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten's revival parishes:** compare readers' (*läsare*) and Laestadian records on change sought from below.
- **Bergslagen and Värmland's temperance lodges:** look at lodge minutes for which towns went dry and who opposed it.
- **Stockholm's academics and Gothenburg's shipping families:** compare reform societies' membership rolls by class.
- **Sámi and Tornedalians:** separate reforms imposed by the state from reforms each people pursued; testimony from both truth commissions marks the line.
- **Travellers (*resande*) and Roma:** check the sterilization inquiry's case files against both groups' associations' own accounts.
- **Iranians, Kurds, Chileans, Eritreans in exile:** compare each exile press's debates over reform or revolution at home.
- **Catholic, Jewish and Muslim congregations:** check internal reform debates in each body's own journals.
- **Revival congregations against the state church:** set revival sermons beside church-assembly (*kyrkomötet*) papers on renewal.
- **Comparison only:** temperance and reform movements in Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland are reference points, not targets; Swedish America's reform churches are covered by Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for inquiries, movements and the Reformation; mixed for personal reform; thin for minority and exile reform debates outside their own presses.
- **Languages needed:** Birgitta's Latin and its Old Swedish translations; Early Modern Swedish for Olaus Petri; Meänkieli and Sámi for revival and revitalization; Kurdish, Persian, Arabic and Spanish for exile presses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sterilizations and the Uppsala racial-biology institute, imposed schooling of Sámi and Tornedalians, and policies toward Travellers and Roma are contested; give each account under its source. Sterilization files name living people.
- **Held for review:** none; the religious-reform pointers stayed within Christian and Muslim sources, and no item needed setting aside.
