# Sweden: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Old Swedish), North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Romani (Kalderash), Bosnian, Kurdish, Arabic, Persian, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm's tavern and student traditions against Lund's and Uppsala's nations:** check which friendship rites (toasts, songs) belong to which setting.
- **Small towns of Småland and Värmland against suburban Järva and Rosengård:** use ULF tables by municipality type to place friendlessness.
- **Gotland, Öland and the Skåne coast in summer:** compare seasonal friendships around summer houses with year-round ones in local memoirs.
- **Herding Sámi and settled neighbors in Jämtland and Norrbotten:** look for *verdde* traces on the Swedish side before assuming them.
- **Haparanda and the Torne Valley:** compare friendship across the border with friendship across language inside Sweden.
- **Men and women, by generation:** check the ULF close-friend item by sex and age before taking any claim about men's loneliness.
- **Kalderash Roma and Travellers:** compare ritual co-parenthood with Church of Sweden godparenthood, each from its own sources.
- **Bosniaks, Kurds, Somalis, Iranians, Syrians:** look at each group's youth associations for where friendships across origin form.
- **Chilean exiles of the 1970s:** check exile memoirs for *compañero* friendships carried into Swedish life.
- **Jews, Estonian Swedes and Sweden Finns:** compare friendship networks kept through their own associations.
- **Comparison only:** how friends are made in Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland is reference only; Swedish-American friendship clubs count under Sweden.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and literature; mixed for rituals; thin for *verdde* on the Swedish side and for Roma ritual friendship in Roma voices.
- **Languages needed:** Old Swedish for *Konungastyrelsen*; eighteenth-century Swedish for Bellman; North Sámi for *verdde*; Romani for *kirvo* accounts; Kurdish, Bosnian, Somali and Spanish for youth and exile groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *heval* carries political weight; helpline material is anonymous and must stay so; newcomers' remarks on how hard friends are to make here are claims to test, not findings.
- **Held for review:** none; the friendship sources weighed (helplines, expat forums, ritual co-parenthood) needed no naming-rule set-aside.
