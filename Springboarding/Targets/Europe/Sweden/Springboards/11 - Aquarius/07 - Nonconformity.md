# Sweden: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with early-1800s Swedish and youth and suburban registers), North and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Persian, Arabic, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm's Södermalm and suburbs (Rågsved, Rinkeby, Botkyrka):** compare punk, squat and hip-hop scenes for who joined and who was policed.
- **Västmanland, Dalarna and Norrland car towns:** check *raggare* club records against local newspapers.
- **Västerbotten and Småland revival districts:** compare readers' and free-church records on who was disciplined for dissent.
- **Dalsland and Värmland small towns:** use local press and school records for how misfit youth were treated.
- **Sámi, North to South Sámi areas:** separate the church's records of drums from Sámi testimony and return claims.
- **Tornedalians:** check Laestadian discipline records for how breaches were handled.
- **Travellers (*resande*) and Roma:** compare how each was made an outsider by the state with each group's own accounts.
- **Iranian and Chilean exiles, Kurds:** look at how dissidents abroad are described in each exile press.
- **Youth of Syrian, Somali, Iraqi and Eritrean families:** check each group's own youth and women's associations for how they describe breaking with family expectations.
- **LGBTQ people in Malmö, Gothenburg and Stockholm:** compare RFSL branch histories by city.
- **Comparison only:** Norwegian, Finnish, Danish, Estonian and Icelandic subcultures and dissenters are set beside, not studied; Swedish-American dissenting sects fall under Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for subcultures, LGBTQ history and the press; mixed for religious dissent; thin and outsider-written for the drums' original owners.
- **Languages needed:** early modern Swedish for consistory records and Lasse-Maja; Sámi languages for drum claims; Meänkieli for Laestadian discipline; Persian, Arabic and Spanish for exile dissidents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi drums may carry restricted knowledge and repatriation claims; family-honor debates and integration debates are contested and stigmatizing, so present only named accounts; sexual-minority records name people.
- **Held for review:** none; the dissent and subculture pointers considered here raised no naming-rule issue, and no pointer was set aside.
