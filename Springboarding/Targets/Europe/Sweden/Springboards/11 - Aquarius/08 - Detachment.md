# Sweden: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with Latin and Old Swedish), Norrland dialects, Meänkieli, North and Lule Sámi, Arabic, Spanish, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms (calm, objectivity, impartiality, equanimity) are praised and others (coldness, indifference, withdrawal) condemned, and which is which varies.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten dialect areas against Skåne:** check greeting and small-talk customs in dialect recordings rather than repeating regional jokes.
- **Torne Valley Laestadians:** compare meeting records of *liikutukset* with Church of Sweden services in the same parishes.
- **Stockholm inner city against rural Värmland and Jämtland:** use SCB one-person-household data to see where living alone clusters.
- **Sámi of the Lule and South Sámi areas:** look for Sámi-written accounts of mourning and composure, and note how thin they are.
- **Gotland and Öland islanders:** check local memoirs for winter isolation and summer crowds.
- **Doctors, nurses, journalists and police:** compare training materials for how each profession teaches distance.
- **Chilean, Bosnian and Somali arrivals:** look in each group's memoirs and press for how distance on arrival was described, as their accounts.
- **Older people in Norrland inland municipalities:** compare loneliness data with home-help records.
- **Men and women:** check NASP and ULF tables by sex before any claim about men's reserve.
- **Catholic, Jewish, Muslim and Orthodox mourners:** compare funeral customs from each community's own guides.
- **Comparison only:** neighboring Nordic and Baltic reserve (Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia, Iceland) is a yardstick, not a target; Swedish-American communities take Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for households, press ethics and the pandemic; mixed for emotion; thin for Sámi composure and mourning in Sámi voices.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Vadstena; eighteenth-century Swedish and Latin for Linnaeus; Meänkieli and Finnish for Laestadian accounts; Arabic, Bosnian and Spanish for arrivals' memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and lonely deaths involve real people; the pandemic strategy and the "lonely Swede" image are contested, so cite each account to its author.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation in Sweden, weighed as a source on equanimity and kept back as a category until the author settles how Buddhism is handled; nothing else was set aside.
