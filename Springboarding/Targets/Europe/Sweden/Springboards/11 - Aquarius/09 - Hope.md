## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Uppland and Södermanland runic parishes:** check whether soul-prayer formulas cluster by district.
- **Småland and Öland:** compare offering-spring records and emigration letters as two ways of acting on hope.
- **Värmland:** set Lagerlöf's novel beside local emigration and waiting stories in the parish books.
- **Västerbotten and the Torne Valley:** compare revival hymnody with Laestadian sermons on hope of salvation.
- **Sámi youth and elders, herding and not:** check Sáminuorra statements against Sámi Parliament programs.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look for hope language in Meänkieli and Finnish revival campaigns.
- **Chilean exiles:** compare exile periodicals of the 1970s with return memoirs after 1990.
- **Syrian, Iraqi, Eritrean and Somali arrivals of the 2010s:** use each group's associations and press for hopes stated on arrival.
- **Asylum-seeking families of the resignation-syndrome cases:** quote only published, consented accounts and the clinicians on both sides.
- **Bosniaks of the 1990s arrival:** check memoirs and associations for hope of return against settling.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and Travellers:** look for hope in each group's own songs and writing, and note thin sources.
- **Comparison only:** the light and New Year rites of Norway, Finland, Denmark, Estonia and Iceland serve as contrast; Swedish-American Lucia festivals inherit Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hymns, rituals, surveys and lotteries; mixed for offering springs; thin for Roma, Travellers and newest arrivals' hopes in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** runic Norse and seventeenth-century Swedish; North Sámi and Meänkieli for youth and revival texts; Arabic, Persian, Kurdish, Somali, Bosnian and Spanish for arrivals' and exiles' sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** resignation syndrome concerns sick children and a disputed diagnosis; suicide-line material is anonymous; refugee reception and integration debates are contested; give each claim its source.
