*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** compare when each big hospital closed and where its patients went.
- **Uppland, Östergötland, Skåne:** set Isof beside Folklivsarkivet (Lund) to see whether spirits and remedies shift south to north.
- **Dalarna (Elfdalian Älvdalen), Värmland's Forest Finns, Bergslagen:** check whether *vittra*, mine spirits and Finnish charms sit in one collection or apart.
- **Gotland, Öland, Halland, Blekinge:** look for legends of drowned places and the sea's dead.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten, Norrbotten, the Torne Valley:** find where Laestadian prayer houses and older healers both appear, and how each names the other.
- **The Sámi, by language area and herding or non-herding life:** keep Sámi institutions' publications apart from clergy and collectors' notes.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns:** compare their associations' words for distress.
- **Jews, Roma (Kale, Kalderash), *resande*:** find each community's own words for the dead and illness; mark where only official files exist.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** check, in their own media, whether distress goes to clergy, healers or clinics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for asylums, surveys and folk belief archives; mixed for living healers; thin for the newest communities' own accounts. Sweden's tracker records no settled lands and no change to its pre-war lands, so this sign is written for Sweden as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern Swedish and dialect for charm books and Isof answers; the Sámi languages and Meänkieli for healing terms; Arabic, Kurdish and Somali for healers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Sámi ritual knowledge is not for outsiders; patient files are sealed by secrecy rules; the racial-biology era touches confinement, read through survivors' accounts. Order ways and law go to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** meditation retreats with Buddhist roots, weighed for the facet on transcendence outside religion, stay a category for the author; no pointer named.
