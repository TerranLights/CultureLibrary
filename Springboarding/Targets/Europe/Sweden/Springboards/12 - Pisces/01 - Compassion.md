# Sweden: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Arabic, Syriac, Persian, Somali, Tigrinya. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** check whether city missions or the welfare office lead street help, from each mission's yearbooks.
- **Småland's free-church belt (Jönköping) and Östergötland:** compare congregation-run help with parish and municipal help.
- **Skåne and Halland:** read council minutes on begging permits beside the press, town by town.
- **Dalarna, Värmland, Bergslagen, Gotland, Öland:** compare *fattigstuga* records in mining and island parishes before 1900.
- **The north (Jämtland to the Torne Valley):** set the Laestadian sense of *armo* beside free-church and municipal help.
- **The Sámi, herding and non-herding, by language area:** find Sámi words for mercy in hymnals and Sámi organizations' own aid work.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** see who cares for old people who speak no Swedish, and in which language.
- **Jews, Kale and Kalderash Roma, *resande*:** note who gave and who received in each record, and where Roma and Travellers appear only as recipients.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs (Södertälje), Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chileans:** trace money sent home and hometown appeals, from their own associations.
- **Church of Sweden, Pentecostal, Mission Covenant, Catholic and Muslim congregations:** find which run food and clothing help of their own, outside municipal care.
- **Comparison only:** neighboring welfare states with their own charity histories (Norway; Finland with Åland and the Finland Swedes; Estonia; Denmark; Iceland); Swedish-American congregations take Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charities, broadcasts and survey rankings; mixed on everyday pity; thin on how recipients themselves tell it.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish for parish and press sources; Meänkieli and Finnish for Laestadian and Sweden Finnish material; Arabic, Somali and Tigrinya for congregations' own giving.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the begging debate and the 2015 refugee arrivals are contested; present each side as an account. Mercy toward offenders goes to Crime here and to Libra for the law.
- **Held for review:** two animal-rights groups were weighed and left out as campaign sources rather than compassion sources; nothing set aside under the naming rules.
