# Sweden: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, Swedish Sign Language, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Romani, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Skåne and Gothenburg:** compare school bullying reports and the Friends figures, where reading peers well is measured in each region.
- **Uppland, Östergötland, Småland:** check whether local readers of *Nils Holgersson* in each province learned the same lessons, from schoolbook editions and memoirs.
- **Värmland, Dalarna, Bergslagen:** look at worker memoirs for how mill and mine hands spoke of a hurt workmate.
- **Gotland, Öland, Halland, Blekinge:** test whether fishing and farming parishes record sharing sorrow at sea losses differently, from parish and press sources.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten, Norrbotten, the Torne Valley:** compare Laestadian meeting accounts of weeping together with secular sources.
- **The Sámi (South, Lule, North; herding and not):** check how *Sameblod* and the truth-commission hearings were received by each language area.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** find accounts of children punished for their language and whether teachers' memoirs show any feeling for them.
- **The Deaf community (Swedish Sign Language) and autistic people:** both lead their own debates on being misread; take their associations' writing first.
- **Jews, Roma (Kale, Kalderash), *resande*:** check what each community asks the majority to understand, in its own press.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chileans:** look for exile literature written in Swedish to explain an inner life to a new public.
- **Comparison only:** the schools of Norway, Denmark, Iceland, Estonia and Finland (Åland and the Swedish-speaking Finns counted with Finland), for how each teaches seeing from another's side; Swedish emigrant communities take Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in school, patient and psychology sources; mixed in literature and broadcasting; thin on minority-language words for feeling-with.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and Swedish Sign Language first; Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish for minority terms (no Kale term is known here; ask Kale speakers' associations); Arabic, Persian and Bosnian for exile writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the police-check (REVA) debate and racial biology are contested; present each side as an account. Diagnostic labels (autism, "psychopath") stay with the people's own associations, not outsiders' claims.
- **Held for review:** cross-cultural emotion-reading studies from abroad were considered and left out as not Sweden-specific; no naming-rule cases arose for this concept.
