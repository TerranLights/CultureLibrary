## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Gothenburg:** compare who serves as volunteer rescuers or blood donors in cities and in coastal parishes, from the societies' own figures.
- **Södermanland and Uppland:** trace local saints' cults (Eskil, Botvid) in church art and parish names.
- **Jämtland and Härjedalen:** check how the Carolean dead are commemorated on both sides of the mountains.
- **Bergslagen, Norrbotten's ore field:** compare strike memory and mine-accident memorials as two kinds of giving.
- **Småland, Värmland and Halland:** look in emigrant letters for parents who stayed so children could leave.
- **Gotland, Öland, Blekinge:** find sea-rescue station histories and the families of drowned rescuers.
- **The Sámi, by language area and herding life:** check Sámi accounts of what was given up under the forced moves, and keep ritual offering apart from personal sacrifice.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look at the war-child and labor-migration memory (Finnish children sent to Sweden belong to Finland's row; their Swedish lives belong here).
- **Jews:** check community records on Holocaust survivors who came in 1945 and those who helped them.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Bosniaks, Eritreans, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Chileans:** compare how each names its martyrs and what its associations ask of the living.
- **Church of Sweden, Pentecostal, Mission Covenant, Catholic, Muslim:** compare fasting calendars and the missionary vocations each sent abroad.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland (with Åland and the Finland Swedes), Estonia, Denmark and Iceland, for their own sea-rescue and war-memorial customs; Swedish America takes Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for registers, surveys and rescue societies; mixed for caregivers; thin for martyr language in newer communities outside their own media.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and Old Swedish for saints' lives; Swedish; Syriac, Arabic, Kurdish and Bosnian for martyrs' days.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "martyr" is political in several communities; give each account as the community's own. Wallenberg's fate and Swedish wartime choices are contested.
- **Held for review:** for protest as self-sacrifice, one pointer was set aside as a category under the standing rule (South Asian-heritage communities in Sweden's lands).
