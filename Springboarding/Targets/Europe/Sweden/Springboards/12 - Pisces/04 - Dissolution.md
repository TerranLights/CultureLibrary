# Sweden: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Yiddish, Arabic, Persian, Spanish. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger (a person, a crowd, nature, the divine), and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Gothenburg:** compare club and festival crowds with church crowds.
- **Småland's free churches against Uppland's and Östergötland's state-church parishes:** check how tongues and trance are received in each.
- **Dalarna (Elfdalian Älvdalen), Bergslagen:** look at mining legends of the mountain taking men, and fiddlers' accounts of playing until dawn.
- **Värmland:** check local memory of 1905 along the border.
- **Skåne, Halland, Blekinge:** look at the dissolution of Danish ways after 1658 as Scanians tell it (*försvenskning* in the south).
- **Gotland and Öland:** check depopulation and the closing of island parishes.
- **Jämtland, Västerbotten, Norrbotten and the Torne Valley:** compare Laestadian meetings with Pentecostal ones, and *försvenskning* in schools with Sámi and Tornedalian memory of it.
- **The Sámi, by language area; herding and non-herding:** policy treated these two groups apart; check how each lost or kept its language.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns, Estonian Swedes:** ask whether grandchildren still speak the language, in each group's own surveys of its members.
- **Jews; Roma (Kale, Kalderash); *resande*:** look at how Yiddish and Romani faded or held, and at fears of absorption in each press.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** check what the second generation keeps, through mother-tongue teaching uptake.
- **Comparison only:** Norway (the other half of 1905), Finland (Åland and the Finland Swedes with it), Estonia, Denmark (for Skåne before 1658) and Iceland; Swedish America is where Swedish itself faded, but takes Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy, language and dementia records; mixed on ecstatic worship; thin on mystical experience as people report it.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish for language-shift data; Yiddish, Arabic and Persian for mystical vocabularies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *försvenskning* and the Sámi and Tornedalian schools are contested; take survivors' and the commissions' accounts first. The integration debate is live; give each position as an account.
- **Held for review:** psychedelic-research pointers were weighed and left out as too uncertain in Sweden to name; meditation traditions with Buddhist roots, for ego loss, stay a category for the author.
