## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Gothenburg and Västra Götaland:** compare suburbs on the police lists with residents' own neighborhood media.
- **Skåne, Halland, Blekinge:** check Öresund commuting and the older line of 1658 in Scanian memory.
- **Uppland, Östergötland, Småland, Värmland:** compare boundary-inspection quarrels on the plains with Forest Finn claims in the *finnmarker*.
- **Dalarna (Elfdalian Älvdalen):** see whether shared summer-farm grazing set lines among villages, from local records.
- **Gotland and Öland:** compare stone walls and island land lines with the mainland.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten:** check the cultivation limit against South Sámi grazing claims.
- **Norrbotten and the Torne Valley:** compare Tornedalian life across a river that is also a state line.
- **The Sámi, herding and non-herding:** check who was inside and who outside the herding rules, and how each group describes the line.
- **Sweden Finns, Estonian Swedes:** ask where each locates the edge of its community, from its own associations.
- **Jews and Muslim communities:** look at each one's purity rules as it states them in the slaughter debate.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and *resande*:** find out at which town edges their camps were tolerated, and by whose decision.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chileans:** check each community's own talk on family privacy and on outsiders in the home.
- **Comparison only:** Finland (across the Torne; Åland and the Finland Swedes too), Norway (the mountain line), Denmark (across the Sound), Estonia and Iceland; the diaspora takes Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in maps, border statistics and housing data; mixed on personal space and touch; thin on how families draw lines in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with old survey terms); Sámi and Meänkieli for land lines; Hebrew, Yiddish and Arabic for purity terms; Kurdish, Persian and Somali for home privacy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi land rights, the police area lists and the slaughter debate are contested; present each account by its source. The consent law and the access rules are law; send them to Libra.
- **Held for review:** gated communities were weighed and dropped for want of clear Swedish cases; nothing set aside under the naming rules for boundaries.
