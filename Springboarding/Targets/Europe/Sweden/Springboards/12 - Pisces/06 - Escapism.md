# Sweden: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Sweden Finnish, Meänkieli, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Gothenburg, Malmö:** compare allotment queues and club nights with suburb youth centers as places to get away.
- **Uppland:** check student festival records against town police reports.
- **Skåne:** compare Lund's harm-reduction history with other cities' refusal.
- **Småland, Östergötland:** see whether free-church temperance still shapes local drinking and gaming talk.
- **Värmland, Dalarna, Bergslagen:** look at mill-town leisure (dance halls, hunting, fishing) in local histories.
- **Gotland and Öland:** check the summer-visitor economy against islanders' own winter.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten, Norrbotten, the Torne Valley:** compare mountain-cabin and snowmobile escapes with Laestadian views on leisure.
- **The Sámi (by language area; herding and non-herding):** take Sámi youth-health studies first; do not import outsiders' claims.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look for dance halls, saunas and tango nights in their own papers, and for their associations' work on drink.
- **Jews, Roma (Kale, Kalderash), *resande*:** list the clubs, camps and music each community ran for its young, from its own press.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iranians, Iraqis, Syrians, Bosniaks, Somalis, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** compare music nights, cafés and satellite TV from home as retreats, in each group's own media.
- **Comparison only:** Denmark's and Norway's different drug and gambling regimes, and Finland's (Åland and the Finland Swedes with it), Estonia's and Iceland's; Swedish Americans take Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gambling, drugs and screens; mixed for fantasy and play; thin for minority communities in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish; Finnish and Meänkieli for regional temperance and dance-hall sources; Bosnian, Somali, Arabic and Persian for retreats in newer communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug policy and khat use are contested and the second is criminalized; present accounts by source, and never tie a substance to a people.
- **Held for review:** for switching off, calm-down apps built on Buddhist-derived practice were weighed; they are a category for the author, not a pointer.
