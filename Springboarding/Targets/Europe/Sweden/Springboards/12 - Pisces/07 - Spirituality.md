# Sweden: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with medieval Latin), North, Lule and South Sámi, Meänkieli, Yiddish, Arabic, Syriac, Persian, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; here the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (institutions are Sagittarius's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Uppland:** compare mosque, synagogue, retreat and choir attendance in the capital with Sigtuna's quiet days.
- **Östergötland and Vadstena:** check how pilgrims and retreat guests describe the town's sacred past today.
- **Skåne, Halland, Blekinge:** look for local holy springs and their present visitors.
- **Gothenburg and Västra Götaland, Småland:** compare free-church prayer meetings with spiritual fairs in the same towns.
- **Dalarna, Värmland, Bergslagen:** check summer services at chapels and summer farms (*fäbodar*) as nature worship.
- **Gotland and Öland:** look at medieval church and saint traditions alongside Forn Sed gatherings.
- **The north, from Jämtland and Härjedalen to the Torne Valley:** see how outdoor mountain services, Sámi church days and Laestadian meetings each place the holy.
- **The Sámi (South, Ume, Pite, Lule, North; herding and non-herding):** check which sites and practices each area shares in public, and keep closed knowledge closed.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns:** find revival songs and prayers kept in Meänkieli and Finnish.
- **Jews:** compare congregations in Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö on prayer and Shabbat.
- **Roma (Kale, Kalderash), *resande*:** check Pentecostal and Orthodox ties in their own accounts.
- **Christian Assyrians, Syriacs and Eritreans; Muslim Bosniaks, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians and Somalis; Iranians and Chilean exiles of many faiths or none:** look for home icons, saints' days, Sufi circles and private prayer in each group's own media.
- **Comparison only:** the spiritual landscapes of Norway, Finland (including Åland and the Finland Swedes), Estonia, Denmark and Iceland, often grouped with Sweden in secularization studies; Sweden's diaspora takes its findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and church sources; mixed for retreats and choirs; thin for seekers' own words outside fairs and forums.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for Petrus; Swedish; Sámi and Meänkieli for revival and holy-place terms; Arabic, Syriac, Persian and Bosnian for newer devotion.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi offering sites and ritual knowledge are partly closed; take only what Sámi bodies publish. Mental illness and religious experience must not be equated.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation centers in Sweden, an obvious pointer for contemplative practice, stay a category for the author.
