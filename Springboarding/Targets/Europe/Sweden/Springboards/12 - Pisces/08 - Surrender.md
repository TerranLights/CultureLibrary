# Sweden: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish, North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish, Arabic, Persian, Bosnian, Somali, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen against Skåne:** compare the take-up of palliative home care and hospices, and how each region frames the end of treatment.
- **Gotland:** check how Visby retells 1361 in its own festivals and guides.
- **Uppland and Östergötland, Västra Götaland:** see where Lutheran and Catholic retreat houses draw guests seeking to let go.
- **Småland's free-church belt:** compare altar-call testimonies with Church of Sweden confirmation material.
- **Värmland, Dalarna, Bergslagen:** look at strike defeats remembered in mill towns and how locals speak of them.
- **Västerbotten and Norrbotten, with the Torne Valley, Jämtland and Härjedalen:** read Laestadian penitence beside the region's novels of submission and endurance.
- **The Sámi (by language area; herding and non-herding):** check how Sámi accounts of land loss speak of yielding or holding on.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look at what the Finnish war of 1808-09 means in Torne Valley memory.
- **Jews; Roma (Kale, Kalderash); *resande*:** collect each group's sayings about fate and yielding, taken from its own publications and recordings.
- **Bosniaks; Chilean exiles; Kurds; Iraqis and Syrians:** compare how each remembers a defeat or a fall (1995; 1973; the wars after 2003 and 2011) in its Swedish commemorations.
- **Somalis, Eritreans, Assyrians and Syriacs, Iranians:** check sermons and community radio for *tawakkul* and its Christian counterparts.
- **Comparison only:** neighbors whose wartime yieldings differ from Sweden's: Denmark's capitulation and Norway's occupation in 1940; Finland's memory of 1809 (Åland and the Finland Swedes with it); Estonia and Iceland; the Swedish diaspora takes Sweden's findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for care, debt and inquiry data; mixed on religious surrender; thin for the newest communities' own vocabulary of fate.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish (with 1800s prose); Finnish and Meänkieli for 1808-09 memory; Arabic, Persian, Bosnian and Somali for words of reliance on God.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime concessions and resignation syndrome are contested; give each account as an account. Do not call any people fatalistic; record such claims only as claims. Assisted dying is law; send it to Libra.
- **Held for review:** acceptance taught through Buddhist-derived mindfulness in Swedish clinics was weighed and is held as a category for the author.
