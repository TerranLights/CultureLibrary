# Sweden: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Sweden, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Swedish (with dialects), Lule and North Sámi, Meänkieli, Sweden Finnish (and the Forest Finns' older Finnish), Arabic, Persian, Somali, Bosnian. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm and the Mälardalen, Gothenburg:** compare emergency departments that use RETTS on how nurses describe overriding a score.
- **Skåne, Halland, Blekinge:** check southern omen and dream traditions in Folklivsarkivet (Lund) against Isof's northern answers.
- **Småland, Östergötland, Uppland:** see whether dowsers were sought in farming parishes, and by whom.
- **Värmland and Dalarna's Forest Finns, Bergslagen:** compare Finnish *tietäjä* lore with Swedish wise-woman lore in the same parishes.
- **Gotland and Öland:** look for sailors' and fishers' weather sense in island collections.
- **Norrland's inland, from Härjedalen and Jämtland up through Västerbotten and Norrbotten:** set hunters', loggers' and herders' memoirs side by side for how each reads land and weather.
- **The Sámi (Lule, North, South, Ume, Pite; herding and non-herding):** keep herders' published knowledge apart from what remains restricted; check which language each source uses.
- **Tornedalians, Sweden Finns:** note premonitions (*aavistus*) and dreams told as warnings in Torne Valley and Sweden Finnish life stories.
- **Jews; Roma (Kale, Kalderash); *resande*:** check each community's own accounts of premonition and luck, avoiding outsiders' fortune-teller clichés entirely.
- **Iranians, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Bosniaks:** compare *istikhāra*, *fāl* and *faal* as each community practices them, from its own media.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Eritreans, Chilean exiles:** check dream-telling and saints' signs in church and family settings.
- **Comparison only:** Norway, Finland (where the *tietäjä* tradition centers; Åland and the Finland Swedes too), Estonia, Denmark, Iceland; Sweden's diaspora takes its results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk belief archives and decision research; mixed for clinical intuition; thin for newer communities' omen practices outside their own media.
- **Languages needed:** Swedish and dialect; older Finnish for Gottlund; Lule Sámi for Ryd's terms; Arabic, Persian and Somali for guidance-seeking practices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sámi knowledge of land and weather is partly held within families; take what is published. Never tie fortune-telling to the Roma or *resande*. Folk omen readings are records of belief, not evidence.
- **Held for review:** personality-type tests sold to Swedish firms with "intuitive" types were weighed and left out as foreign products, not Swedish sources.
