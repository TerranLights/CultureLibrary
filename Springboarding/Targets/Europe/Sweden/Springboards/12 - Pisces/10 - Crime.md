## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Stockholm, Gothenburg and Malmö suburbs against small towns:** compare fear and blame in residents' own media with police lists, without taking either as the residents' voice.
- **Uppland and Östergötland:** check runic curse formulas and medieval killings in local museums.
- **Skåne and Halland:** compare the biker war's memory with older smuggling and bog-body lore.
- **Värmland, Dalarna, Bergslagen:** look at the Kevin case and other rural cases where blame fell on a family.
- **Småland and Gotland:** test whether murder ballads sold at fairs here tell different morals.
- **Jämtland, Härjedalen, Västerbotten, Norrbotten, the Torne Valley:** check smuggling across the Torne and logging-camp violence in memoirs.
- **The Sámi (herding and non-herding; by language area):** find Sámi accounts of crimes against them that went unpunished, as their organizations publish them.
- **Tornedalians and Sweden Finns:** look at how Finnish-speaking workers were cast as troublemakers in the press of the 1960s and 70s.
- **Jews, Muslims, Roma (Kale, Kalderash) and *resande*:** compare each community's own documentation of crimes against it with official counts.
- **Assyrians and Syriacs, Kurds, Iraqis, Syrians, Somalis, Eritreans, Bosniaks, Iranians, Chileans:** check how each community's press answers the public link drawn between immigration and crime.
- **Comparison only:** the biker war spanned the Nordic countries, so Denmark, Norway and Finland (with Åland and the Finland Swedes) are checked for their sides of it, Estonia and Iceland for context; Swedish America takes Sweden's results.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys, reports and media; mixed on older popular attitudes; thin for minority communities' own documentation.
- **Languages needed:** runic and Early Modern Swedish for curses and ballads; Swedish; Sámi, Meänkieli and Finnish for northern sources; Arabic, Kurdish, Somali and Tigrinya for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** crime and birthplace, gang violence, and the Roma register are contested; present each account as an account and never state what a group is. Victims and the wrongly accused are named only as published. Courts, statutes and sentencing go to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** for the redemption story, memoirs by living former offenders were weighed and left unnamed until checked, so that no private person is singled out; no naming-rule case arose.
