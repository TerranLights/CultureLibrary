# Ukraine: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hutsul villages against Dnipro lowland villages:** check feud and reconciliation customs in each, using ethnography rather than literary types.
- **Galicia, the Left Bank and the Donbas:** compare protest issues and forms in the event database.
- **Russian-speaking and Ukrainian-speaking youth:** check swearing and online-anger registers, including whether Russian *мат* lost ground after 2022.
- **Crimean Tatars:** compare commemorative grief with protest anger, in exile and today.
- **Polish and Jewish memory of the *haidamaky*:** set each community's account of 1768 beside the Ukrainian literary one.
- **Women and men:** check how violence surveys and hotline data split by gender and region.
- **Hungarians and Romanians of the west:** check how minority media express grievance in language disputes; the legal side goes to Libra.
- **Veterans and their families:** check post-service anger as framed by veterans' organizations.
- **Orthodox (both jurisdictions), Greek Catholics, Baptists:** compare sermons and catechesis on wrath and forgiveness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, protest data and wartime emotion polls; mixed for everyday display rules; thin for folk beliefs tying anger to illness (start with charm collections, *замовляння*).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and *surzhyk*; Russian for older invective and Babel-era sources; Crimean Tatar; Polish and Yiddish for the 1768 memory; Hungarian and Romanian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime hatred is raw and politicized; the Koliivshchyna and the Volhynia massacres are contested ground; obscenity sources need care in a public repo (short titles only).
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside. Clinical anger studies from abroad were considered and dropped as off the nation's own sources; none fell under a standing rule.
