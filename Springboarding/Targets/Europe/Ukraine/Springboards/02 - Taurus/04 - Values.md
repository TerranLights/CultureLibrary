# Ukraine: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia against the east and south:** check EVS and national survey items region by region before any talk of "two Ukraines".
- **Greek Catholics, both Orthodox jurisdictions and Roman Catholics:** compare catechisms and pastoral letters on the same questions.
- **Baptists and Pentecostals:** check Soviet-era testimony and today's church statements on conscience and public life.
- **Jewish communities:** compare Hasidic tales with Odesa's enlightenment (*Haskalah*) writers on what a life should aim at.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check nonresistance and the work ethic in their records and emigrant memoirs.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check what the community's schools and media teach as *adet* and as faith.
- **Hungarians of Transcarpathia:** compare Reformed and Catholic parishes' teaching on family and faith.
- **Generations:** compare those schooled before and after 1991, and those who came of age after 2014.
- **Russian-speaking cities of the south and east:** check values items by language of interview, not by assumption.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and religious texts; mixed for what is lived against what is said; thin for Gagauz, Karaim and Urum communities (look to their cultural societies).
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the 1076 miscellany; Ukrainian; Hebrew and Yiddish for Hasidic texts; German for Mennonite records; Russian for Baptist samizdat; Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "European" against "Russian" value talk is a political frame; surveys in wartime and occupied areas are partial; disputes over gender and family are live and need each side's own sources.
- **Held for review:** none. Values surveys sometimes come with outside commentary, but this sheet points only to the data and to Ukrainian, Jewish and church sources.
