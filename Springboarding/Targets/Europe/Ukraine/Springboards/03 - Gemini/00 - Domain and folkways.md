## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia (Lviv, Ternopil, Ivano-Frankivsk) against Slobozhanshchyna (Kharkiv, Sumy):** check reported home and public language, and traces of Habsburg and Russian imperial schooling in classroom speech.
- **Polissia and Volhynia:** check northern dialect words for talk and neighbors that atlases record and schooling may have leveled.
- **Hutsuls, Boykos and Lemkos (including resettled Lemkos):** compare how each group's speech is recorded and taught after the 1940s resettlements.
- **Transcarpathian Rusyns and Hungarians:** check school, home and church language separately; names here are contested.
- **Bukovinian and Budjak Romanians, Bulgarians and Gagauz:** check a child's home, school and church language, village by village.
- **Crimean Tatars, at home and displaced since 2014:** compare teaching of the language before and after the move to mainland cities.
- **Donbas, Odesa and the Azov coast (Mariupol Greeks):** check how Russian, Ukrainian, *surzhyk*, Rumeic and Urum share daily talk, and wartime shifts.
- **Deaf communities:** check Ukrainian Sign Language use apart from spoken-language data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dialectology and language debate; mixed for neighbors and commuting; thin for Karaim, Krymchak, Urum and Romani speech (go through community bodies and specialists).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with western and Polissian dialect reading, Church Slavonic for the early grammars and lexicons, Russian for Soviet-era statistics, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian and Yiddish for minority schooling.
- **Lands:** which territory the nation will hold is still to be settled; this sign follows the sources' own Ukraine, treats Crimea and the east as homelands of their own peoples, and settles no border.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Russification, Ukrainization and the language laws are contested; present each as an account, laws to Libra and Capricorn. Occupied areas cannot be surveyed openly.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside. The grammars, dialect maps and school leagues chosen here are the community's own or regional scholarship, and no candidate for talk or schooling raised a rule.
