## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Poltava and Chernihiv villages and the Hutsul Carpathians:** compare the matchmakers' formulas and refusal signs in wedding ethnographies.
- **Greek Catholic, Orthodox (both jurisdictions) and Protestant clergy:** check how each has stepped between parishioners, between churches, and between protesters and police.
- **Crimean Tatars:** compare community go-betweens before 1944, in exile and since the return, in their own histories.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check how *shtadlanim*, rabbis and matchmakers went between families and authorities in Podillia and Volhynia.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** compare colony elders and outside relief bodies as intermediaries with Soviet officials.
- **Industrial Donbas and Kryvbas:** check how strikes were settled and who brokered them, in conciliation records and union papers.
- **Hungarian and Romanian communities of the west:** check which bodies speak for them to Kyiv and to Budapest or Bucharest.
- **Roma groups:** check whether disputes go to community elders or outside bodies, using Roma organizations' own descriptions; the legal side goes to Libra.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wedding go-betweens, diplomacy and the 2014-to-2022 monitoring; mixed for everyday quarrel-settling; thin for Krymchak, Karaim and Urum practice.
- **Languages needed:** Ottoman Turkish and Crimean Tatar for Khanate envoys, Latin and Polish for the Hadiach talks, Hebrew and Yiddish for *shtadlanut*, German for colony relief, Ukrainian and Russian for modern conciliation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Minsk talks, prisoner exchanges, the Mejlis and the church jurisdictions are contested ground; present each account as an account. Mediation law and community courts go to Libra and Capricorn; exchange details may be withheld for safety.
- **Held for review:** none. Western mediation textbooks were weighed and left out as saying nothing about local go-betweens; no standing rule was touched.
