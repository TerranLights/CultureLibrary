# Ukraine: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian and its dialects, the Russian of Ukraine, *surzhyk*, Crimean Tatar, Romanian, Hungarian, Polish, Hebrew and Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor, unconfirmed reports passed from person to person, is included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pokuttia, Hutsul and Boyko villages:** compare *коломийки* and other mocking songs as public commentary, region by region.
- **Small towns of Polissia and big-city apartment blocks in Kyiv and Kharkiv:** check where talk about neighbors happens and how it is described in ethnography and urban studies.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check religious and family teaching on backbiting, and how exile and return changed community talk.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** compare Hasidic teaching on speech with shtetl satire and the Yiddish press.
- **Romanian and Hungarian villages of the west:** compare evening gatherings and village festivals as places of talk.
- **Front-line towns and the displaced since 2014 and 2022:** check rumors about collaboration and loyalty through careful reporting and oral history; labels are contested and can endanger people.
- **Roma communities:** check how rumor about Roma spreads in neighboring towns and how Roma organizations answer it; sources are scarce.
- **Schools and online youth spaces:** compare girls' and boys' reports of rumor and bullying in youth surveys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folklore, satire and literary portraits; mixed for media studies of rumor; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Urum communities.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Ukrainian for Zinoviiv, Hebrew for the Hasidic text, Polish for Kolberg, Romanian for *șezătoare*, Russian for Soviet archives and the mass press, Ukrainian for the rest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** witchcraft accusations, Soviet denunciations and wartime collaboration rumors are contested ground and can harm living people; present each as an account, name no private individuals, and send defamation law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; celebrity gossip sites were the class weighed here, and they were dropped as unreliable rather than set aside under a standing rule.
