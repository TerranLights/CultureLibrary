# Ukraine: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian and its dialects, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, German (colonies), Yiddish (historical), Polish. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dnipro and Left Bank villages:** check what painted walls, the *покуть* and the stove meant in the rooms of a house.
- **Hutsul and Transcarpathian villages:** compare how houses were dressed and blessed, and how emigrant earnings rebuilt them.
- **Kharkiv, Kyiv and Dnipro flat-dwellers:** compare the urban flat and the dacha as two homes, through surveys and memoirs.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check what "home" names after 1944 and since the return, in Crimean Tatar writing first.
- **Donbas families:** check accounts of homes kept, left and lost since 2014, from people on both sides of the front.
- **Jewish families (historical):** compare the shtetl home of Yiddish fiction with memoirs and memorial books.
- **Mennonite and German colonists (historical):** compare the colony home in memoirs with emigrant memory of it.
- **Roma of Transcarpathia:** check housing in settlements through Roma-led sources; outside reports alone are not enough.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk interiors, song and literature; mixed for measured housing since 2022, as losses are still being counted; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Gagauz homes.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Maksymovych's edition, German for Dyck, Yiddish for the shtetl texts, Crimean Tatar for return-era writing, Ukrainian (with Transcarpathian dialect) for the rest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war damage and occupied homes are contested and still unfolding; repression in the Slovo building needs care with victims' families. Property and compensation law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none. Home-decor influencer feeds were looked at as a pointer class and left out for want of a stable archive; no standing rule decided it.
