*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia, the center and Slobozhanshchyna:** compare how locals and newcomers are named in *Підслухано* pages and surveys, region by region.
- **Displaced people from Donbas and Crimea in Lviv, Kyiv and Dnipro:** compare reported belonging over time across the survey rounds.
- **Crimean Tatars, Karaim and Krymchaks:** check each people's own markers and rites of membership, in their own bodies' words.
- **Rusyns, Hutsuls and Boykos:** check how self-names and group membership are argued, and who argues them.
- **Hungarians of Transcarpathia and Romanians of Bukovina:** compare the reasons people give for a second citizenship.
- **Azov Greeks and Budjak Bulgarians and Gagauz:** compare festivals and associations as ways to stay a group.
- **Armenians (historical and today):** compare old town communities with later ones.
- **Orthodox (both jurisdictions) and Greek Catholic parishioners:** compare membership and the movement between parishes; Protestant congregations too.
- **Roma groups:** check belonging through papers and through community, in Roma-led sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys of displaced people and identity; mixed for local belonging between regions; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Azov Greek groups since 2014 and 2022.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Ukrainian for the register, Armenian and Polish for the Lviv community records, Hungarian and Romanian for citizenship debates, Crimean Tatar, Rusyn, Russian for many local groups, Ukrainian for the rest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dual citizenship, language choice, Rusyn status and occupied Crimea and Mariupol are contested; present each account as an account. Citizenship law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing. Global loneliness indexes were weighed as a source class and dropped as too coarse for regions; no rule applied.
