# Ukraine: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian and its dialects, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Yiddish (historical), Polish, Romani. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polissian, Podillian and Hutsul villages:** compare the age at which children were given herding and household tasks.
- **Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa families:** check reliance on kindergartens against grandparents, and changes since 2022.
- **Galician Greek Catholic and Orthodox families:** compare church teaching on raising children and the first-communion age.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check lullabies and upbringing words in Crimean Tatar collections, and teaching in the language since return.
- **Hungarian and Romanian families:** compare preschool in their own languages with Ukrainian-language preschool.
- **Jewish families (historical):** compare kheyder-age learning with home nurture in memoirs.
- **Roma families:** check school and home roles through mediators' and parents' own accounts.
- **Families of labor migrants:** compare children raised by grandparents with those raised by parents.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pedagogy, children's literature and programs; mixed for measured parenting practice; thin for Gagauz, Karaim and Krymchak upbringing.
- **Languages needed:** the bookish Slavonic-based literary language for Skovoroda, Yiddish for the song and kheyder memory, Crimean Tatar for *ninni*, Hungarian and Romanian for minority preschools, Ukrainian for the rest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** children's data in program reports is protected; Soviet pedagogy and wartime child care are contested ground. Child-protection and family law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none set aside. Imported parenting-guru books were the class weighed here, and they were dropped as saying nothing of Ukrainian practice.
