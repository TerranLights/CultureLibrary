# Ukraine: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Transcarpathia, Lviv and Ivano-Frankivsk as host regions:** compare hosting by families, churches and dormitories since 2022.
- **Kharkiv, Mariupol and Kherson:** check sheltering in cellars and metro stations through residents' accounts.
- **Crimean Tatars:** compare the return-era camps with sheltering after 2014, in Crimean Tatar sources first.
- **Karaim and Krymchaks:** check community memory of refuge sites and wartime fate; sources are scarce.
- **Jewish survivors and their rescuers:** compare hiding stories across Galicia, Volhynia and Podillia; Polish rescuers and Greek Catholic clergy too.
- **Greek Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant congregations:** compare church shelters for displaced people.
- **Roma families displaced since 2022:** check reports of being refused or given shelter, through Roma-led groups.
- **Street homeless in Odesa, Kyiv and Kharkiv:** compare charities' accounts with city counts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 2022 hosting and wartime sheltering; mixed for homelessness counts; thin for Karaim refuge history and for Roma shelter access.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Yiddish for hiding stories, Crimean Tatar and Russian for return-era camps, Hungarian for Transcarpathian church relief, Middle Ukrainian for the *шпиталі*, Ukrainian for the rest.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Mariupol strike, the Holocaust and the Crimean Tatar return are contested ground; present each account as an account. Survivors' and shelter residents' identities need care. Housing and refugee law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none. Rental-platform data on hosting was weighed and dropped as proprietary; no standing rule was at play.
