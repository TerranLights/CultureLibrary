## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hutsul and Pokuttian highlands against Polissia and the Left Bank:** check which games, couplet duels and dances each records, and who led them.
- **Galicia and Bukovina against Slobozhanshchyna and the Donbas:** compare feast days and Soviet-made holidays by the crowds each still draws.
- **Kyiv, Lviv, Kharkiv and Odesa:** compare club, theater and festival audiences through listings and ticket data.
- **Crimean Tatars:** look at weddings, dances and wrestling in Crimea and on the mainland since 2014, in their own media.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns, Bukovinian and Budjak Romanians, Budjak Bulgarians and Gagauz:** compare village balls, round dances and youth gatherings.
- **Jewish communities (historical and today):** compare the Purim play, the wedding jester (*badkhn*) and Odesa's stage with later forms.
- **Roma groups:** check musicians' and dancers' roles at others' celebrations against Roma-led sources on their own feasts.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check choir festivals and Sunday rules in colony histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for song and game collections, festivals and TV; mixed for leisure time and spending; thin for Karaim, Krymchak, Gagauz and Azov Greek pastimes (try community bodies and regional museums).
- **Lands:** the post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled; the Leo sheets follow the lands the sources treat as Ukraine, with Crimea and the east as lands with their own peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with dialect skill for couplets and spring games; Russian for Soviet-era and gambling press; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian and Romanian; German; Yiddish for the Purim play.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** festivals in occupied Crimea and the east since 2014 and 2022 run under other authorities; present each account as an account. Gambling and festival law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** this line stays empty. Leisure-trend marketing reports were the doubtful class here, and they fall away on reliability alone.
