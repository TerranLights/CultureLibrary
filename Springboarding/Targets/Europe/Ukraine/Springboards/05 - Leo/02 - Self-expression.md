# Ukraine: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Romani, Yiddish (historical), with Church Slavonic and Middle Ukrainian for school drama. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lviv and Ivano-Frankivsk against Kharkiv and Dnipro:** check how youth subcultures dress and gather, and how town press reacts to them.
- **Men in uniform and veterans against civilians:** compare what each lets themselves say about feeling, in poetry, posts and interviews.
- **LGBTQ Ukrainians in Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa:** compare Pride turnout and counter-protest by city, through organizers' own records.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check what is voiced openly in Crimea since 2014 and what in mainland or exile media.
- **Hasidic and other Jewish communities:** compare song, prayer and humor as outlets, in their own sources.
- **Roma groups:** check stage and festival self-presentation against community-led accounts.
- **Greek Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant congregations:** look at testimony, confession and preaching styles as different channels for speaking the inner life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literary and music scenes and for 2004, 2013 and 2022 protest culture; mixed for everyday display rules; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Gagauz voices (route: community publications).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and Russian for scenes and press; Middle Ukrainian and Latin for school drama; Crimean Tatar for verse; Yiddish and Hebrew for the Hasidic song literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Pride and counter-protest, speech in occupied areas, detention at Izolyatsia, and the arrests of the 1960s and 1970s are contested or painful; present each account as an account, and protect named activists. Speech law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing sits on this line. Anonymous confession pages were the source type doubted, on privacy grounds, and they are left unlisted for that reason.
