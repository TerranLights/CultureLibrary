# Ukraine: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine (including prison and army slang), Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Polish, Yiddish (historical). **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Villages of Podillia, Polissia and the Left Bank:** compare gate-tarring, the pumpkin and festival mockery, and who carried out each.
- **Conscripts of the Soviet years and soldiers since 2014:** check how hazing and its reform are told in memoirs and surveys.
- **Crimean Tatars:** compare memories of the special settlements with accounts of detention since 2014.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check how the 1944 roundup is marked in villages and schools.
- **Jewish communities:** compare pogrom testimony in Yiddish, Russian and Ukrainian for how degradation is named.
- **Lemkos and deportees from Poland:** compare the reception in eastern and southern villages with later memory.
- **Former prisoners and their families:** check how prison hierarchies follow people outside.
- **Pupils in large cities and small towns:** compare bullying reports and campaigns, by school type.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, deportation history and the war; mixed for village shaming customs (scattered ethnography); thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Gagauz experiences (look to community memoirs).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and Russian (with slang) for army and prison sources; Crimean Tatar for exile memory; Hungarian for the 1944 roundup; Polish for the deportations; Yiddish for pogrom testimony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prisoners of war, sexual shaming, prison hierarchy and the pogroms involve living victims; the Mazepa rite and the deportations are contested in memory; present each account as an account, protect names and avoid graphic detail. Punishment and military law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld under a standing rule. Leaked videos of the abuse of captives were deliberately not listed, out of care for the people in them, and testimony stands in their place.
