## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zaporizhzhia and the Left Bank against Galicia and Transcarpathia:** compare remembered forms of choosing leaders (Cossack councils, village heads, priest-politicians).
- **Hutsul pastoral crews:** check how a head shepherd was picked and obeyed, in summer-pasture ethnography.
- **Crimean Tatars:** compare informal exile-era leaders with elected community bodies, in their own histories.
- **Jewish communities:** compare Hasidic, lay and political leaders, in Yiddish and Hebrew sources.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check how elders, district heads and reformers shared power.
- **Rusyns and Transcarpathian Ukrainians:** compare readings of the 1938 to 1939 leaders.
- **Soldiers and commanders since 2014:** compare accounts of who is followed in units, through memoirs and interviews.
- **Women in parish, civic and political life:** check who led before 1939 and after 1991.
