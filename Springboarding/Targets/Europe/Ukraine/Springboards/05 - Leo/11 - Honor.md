## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zaporizhzhia and the Left Bank against Galicia:** compare Cossack and Riflemen traditions of honor as each region keeps them.
- **Hutsul and Bukovinian villages against the steppe south:** check wedding and courtship customs tied to family honor, and when they lapsed.
- **Polish communities of Lviv and Volhynia:** compare their honor mottoes and commemorations with Ukrainian ones, in both languages.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how decorated veterans and the deported are honored in their own media.
- **Jewish communities:** compare honors in *pinkasim* with later Soviet and present communal life.
- **Port and mining cities' criminal milieus:** check codes of word and debt in court records and memoirs.
- **Soldiers and their families since 2014:** compare military and civilian ways of honoring the fallen, town by town.
- **Hungarians of Transcarpathia and Romanians of Bukovina:** check community war memorials and the honors they pay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military memory, Cossack sources and literature; mixed for village honor customs; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Gagauz codes (look to community histories).
- **Languages needed:** Middle Ukrainian for chronicles; Russian for imperial and criminal sources; Polish; Crimean Tatar; Hebrew and Yiddish for *pinkasim*; Romanian and Hungarian for western memorials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Riflemen, Kruty, the UPA and Soviet war heroes are claimed and disputed by different groups; customs tied to women's conduct need care; present each account as an account. Military law and criminal law go to Libra and Capricorn.
