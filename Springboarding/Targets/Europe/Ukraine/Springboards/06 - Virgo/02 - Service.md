## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia and Volhynia against the Left Bank and Slobozhanshchyna:** check where domestic service in towns drew its maids, and how memoirs from each side describe the work.
- **Orthodox of both jurisdictions and Greek Catholics:** check how each church's press frames *служіння* by clergy and lay people.
- **Baptists, Pentecostals and other Protestants:** check whether their aid networks run separately from state and Orthodox channels.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check hospitality duties at home against public service under occupation and in exile on the mainland.
- **Jewish communities:** check the beadle's, the cantor's and the rebbe's service in Hasidic and non-Hasidic sources.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians, Rusyns and Roma:** check who serves whom in relief programs run from abroad.
- **Mennonite colonies (historical):** check their alternative to army service (forestry and medical units) against Ukrainian peasant service.
- **Railway, hospital and municipal workers in front-line cities (Kharkiv, Zaporizhzhia, Mykolaiv):** check how their service is reported locally and nationally.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime volunteering and civil-service reports; mixed for domestic service; thin for Karaim and Krymchak service roles and for Roma volunteers (try community bodies and Roma NGOs).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian; Polish and German for Galician maids; Hebrew for Hasidic texts; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobilization and draft evasion are disputed in the press since 2022; give each side as an account. Civil-service and military-service law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; accounts of Ukrainian domestic workers serving families abroad were the one class weighed, and they belong to diaspora comparison rather than to this line.
