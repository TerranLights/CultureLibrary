## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kharkiv and Kyiv against Lviv:** check which school of science, statistics or engineering each city's universities claim as their tradition.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check the study house against the reformers who wanted secular sciences, in Hebrew and Yiddish sources.
- **Poles of Lviv (historical):** check the Lwów school's own memoirs against Ukrainian accounts of the same university.
- **Karaim of Crimea and Halych:** check their way of reading scripture by reason, through their own community scholars (sources thin).
- **Crimean Tatars:** check secular and religious learning in Bakhchysarai and Simferopol schools before 1944 and after the return.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check schooling in Hungarian against Ukrainian-language math and science results.
- **Village and city:** check how farmers reckon (yields, weather, prices) against the exam-based reckoning of town schools.
- **Russian-speaking engineering towns of the east and south:** check how Soviet technical schooling is remembered locally.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history and statistics; mixed for everyday reckoning; thin for Karaim and Crimean Tatar learning traditions (start with diaspora scholars and community libraries).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian; Polish for the Lwów school; Latin for Mohyla courses; Hebrew and Yiddish; Italian for Slutsky's paper.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** OSINT on troop movements may be restricted in wartime; study the method, never live data. Audit and statistics law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** for analysis, nothing went on hold; cross-national "thinking style" studies with a Ukrainian sample were the questionable kind, and they wait on a check that such a sample exists rather than on any rule.
