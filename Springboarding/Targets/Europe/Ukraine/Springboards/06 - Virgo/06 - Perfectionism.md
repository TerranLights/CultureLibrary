# Ukraine: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Romanian, Hungarian, Crimean Tatar, Hebrew and Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia and the Hutsul Carpathians against Poltava:** check whose embroidery or carving standards are judged by evenness, and who judges.
- **Bukovinian Romanians and Ukrainians of Bukovina:** check how each community's egg-painters describe a "right" egg.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check exactness in ritual work (scribes, slaughterers) against a looser everyday religious practice described in memoirs.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check craft standards set by masters in exile and after the return, in their own community media.
- **Kharkiv and Kyiv science schools:** check the stories each tells of its exacting founders.
- **Athletes and their families:** check how gymnasts and coaches from Kyiv, Kharkiv and Donetsk describe pressure and injury.
- **Mennonite colonies (historical):** check how travelers and the colonists themselves describe their orderly farms; treat outsiders' praise as a claim.
- **Village and city homes:** check how renovation standards differ and what neighbors say of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crafts, contests and Soviet quality campaigns; mixed for perfectionism as a psychological trait; thin for scribes, Crimean Tatar stonework and Karaim crafts.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian for Soviet standards and Landau sources; Romanian; Hebrew and Yiddish; Crimean Tatar; Church Slavonic for the Gospel.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** coaching abuse in elite sport has been alleged; give each account as told and keep athletes' privacy. Standards law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none, and none was close: the doubtful sources for this concept were glossy design magazines, which carry advertising rather than evidence.
