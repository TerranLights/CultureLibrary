*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia against Kyiv and the Left Bank:** check how church polemics, the Greek Catholic press and the Kyiv press criticized clergy in each period.
- **Hasidic and Haskalah writers (historical):** check satire from each side against community records of the same disputes.
- **Odesa, Kharkiv and Dnipro:** check comedy and satire traditions in Russian and Ukrainian, and who is mocked in each city.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how the national movement's petitions and the community press criticized the authorities, before and after 2014.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check minority media's criticism of language and school policy, as their own accounts.
- **Protestant churches (Baptists, Pentecostals):** check how church discipline handles a member's faults, in their manuals.
- **Front-line soldiers and civilians since 2022:** check where open criticism of commanders and officials appears (blogs, letters, press) and how it is answered.
- **Teachers and parents:** check grading disputes in parents' forums by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, dissent and press criticism; mixed for workplace feedback; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Gagauz forms of criticism (start with community bodies).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian for Soviet and Odesa sources; Polish and Ruthenian for polemics; Hebrew and Yiddish; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wartime criticism of the army and state is contested; record who says what. Under occupation, criticism can bring prosecution; protect sources. Speech and press law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** reviews by anonymous Telegram channels were the uncertain class; they are dropped because their authors cannot be checked, which needs no rule, so the line holds nothing.

## 5. First queries
1. Read a modern edition of *Апокрисис* and list each charge laid against the bishops.
2. Search press archives for «книга скарг» in Kyiv and Kharkiv papers of the 1970s and note what kind of complaint was printed.
3. Read the English translation of Perl's satire (*Revealer of Secrets*) and mark which Hasidic practices it parodies.
4. Compare five *ЛітАкцент* reviews and five *Читомо* reviews of the same novels and note tone and faults named.
