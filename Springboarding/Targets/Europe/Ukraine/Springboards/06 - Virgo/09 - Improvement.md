## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia and Bukovina against the Left Bank:** check how Austrian and Russian imperial "improvement" is remembered in local histories of each.
- **The Zaporizhzhia and Kherson steppe:** check how reservoirs, irrigation and breeding stations are told by locals and by planners.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check the Jadid tradition's place in community memory and schooling after the return.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check Haskalah reformers against Hasidic and traditionalist answers in Hebrew and Yiddish.
- **Roma groups:** check settled and formerly traveling families' own accounts of state programs.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check how colony histories present farm improvement and how Ukrainian neighbors' accounts treat it.
- **Villages and city suburbs:** check renovation and *благоустрій* spending patterns by oblast.
- **People who rebuilt homes after 2022 (Irpin, Bucha, Kharkiv region):** check whether "build back better" is their phrase or officials'.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science and breeding history and for city programs; mixed for self-help culture; thin for Roma and Karaim experience of imposed betterment (start with Roma-led NGOs and community bodies).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian; German for Franzos and Austrian sources; Hebrew and Yiddish; Crimean Tatar and Ottoman Turkish for Gasprinski; Romani where testimony exists.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Kakhovka breach and occupied Askania-Nova are contested, with rival accounts; record each as an account. Planning and land law go to Libra and Capricorn.
