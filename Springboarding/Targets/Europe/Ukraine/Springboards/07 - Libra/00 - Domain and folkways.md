# Ukraine: The domain, partnership and justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian and its dialects, *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Rusyn, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Gagauz, Polish, German (colony records), Yiddish and Hebrew (historical). **Concept in one line:** what counts as a fair bargain and a good partnership: marriage, contracts, open enemies, the settling of quarrels and the letter of the law, with marriage ways and gender ways as the folkways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hutsul, Boyko and Lemko highlands against the Poltava and Chernihiv Left Bank:** check which wedding stages and payments each region's song collections record.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how *nikâh* and civil registration have sat side by side since the return from exile.
- **Budjak Bulgarians and Gagauz, Bukovinian Romanians, Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check wedding sponsorship and dowry lists in their own-language collections.
- **Jewish communities (historical), Karaim and Krymchaks:** check which bodies ruled on marriage and disputes, and whose courts the parties used.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check colony rules on marital property against those of nearby villages.
- **Orthodox of both jurisdictions, Greek Catholics, Roman Catholics, Baptists and Pentecostals:** check each body's rules on church marriage and divorce.
- **Kharkiv, Odesa and the Donbas against Lviv and Ternopil:** check divorce caseloads and marriage registration by oblast.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wedding ethnography, family law and court decisions; mixed for contract practice in daily life; thin for Krymchak, Karaim and Azov Greek marriage ways (start with community bodies and museum collections).
- **Lands:** the survey has not yet drawn Ukraine's lines for partnership and justice, so these pointers follow the sources' own Ukraine, with Crimea and the east reached through their peoples' records.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with Church Slavonic for the old law; French for Vovk; German for the colony files; Yiddish and Hebrew for the kahal records; Crimean Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family-violence and same-sex-union law are live debates, and archives in the occupied areas are hard to reach; give each account as an account. Law's spirit goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing was set aside under a rule here. Wedding-industry price surveys did come up for the marriage facet, but they record spending rather than bargains and are left for a later stage.

## 5. First queries

4. Search the Vernadsky library catalog for «рукобиття» and «сватання» and sort the hits by region.
