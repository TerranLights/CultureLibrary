# Ukraine: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian (with Boyko and Hutsul herding terms), *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish (historical), Hungarian, Romanian. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Boyko and Hutsul herders against Poltava and Kherson grain villages:** check whether shares followed contribution, need or equal heads in each economy.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check land and housing allotment after the return, beside the shares given to other Crimean residents.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check how kahal levies were spread and who protested them, in Yiddish sources.
- **Mennonite and German colonies (historical):** check how indivisible farms and the landless were weighed in colony records.
- **Kharkiv and Dnipro industrial cities against Lviv and Uzhhorod:** check housing-list records and informal-payment studies city by city.
- **Internally displaced people since 2014 and 2022 against host communities:** check how aid and housing shares are argued over in local press.
- **Roma groups and Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check equal-access complaints in schools and housing, using their own organizations' files.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong for experiments, law and regulation, thinner for everyday turn-taking and bill-splitting (start with forums and lifestyle press). Thin for Azov Greek and Karaim sharing customs.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with Carpathian herding vocabulary; Russian for Skalkovsky and Soviet housing records; Yiddish for Mendele; Crimean Tatar.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Crimean land claims sit inside an occupation and a contested history; give each account as an account. Tax fairness and its spirit sit with Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** tourist tipping guides were the only candidates on the bench; they speak to visitors, not to fair shares among residents, so they stayed out with no rule involved.
