# Ukraine: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian (with Polissian usage), the Russian of Ukraine, German and Romanian (Bukovina), Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Latin (the 1710 text). **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Bukovina's Ukrainians, Romanians and Germans:** check how the 1910 settlement was received in each people's own press.
- **Crimean Tatars, Russians of Crimea and Ukrainians of Crimea:** check how representation quotas were argued for and against in each community's papers.
- **Polissian villages against the drained collective farms' new settlers:** check how each speaks of the land before and after drainage.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check how they read the minority law's balance in their own press and church bodies.
- **Greek Catholics, Orthodox of both jurisdictions and Baptists:** check how each body's calendar sets fast against feast across the year.
- **Frontline cities (Kharkiv, Zaporizhzhia) against the far west:** check how burnout and rest are covered in local media.
- **Hutsul and Boyko ornament against Podillian and Poltava ornament:** check symmetry rules in museum catalogs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for constitutional and labor texts; mixed for folk ideas of balance in the body; thin for Karaim and Krymchak sources here (start with community museums).
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Orlyk's original; German for the Bukovina Diet; Romanian; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian for the minority-law response.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** constitutional reversals, the Crimean quota and the minority law are contested; give each account as an account. The spirit of checks and balances goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no rule-based hold. The one pointer left out, travel copy selling Carpathian "wellness" retreats, reads as advertising rather than as a source on balance in the body.
