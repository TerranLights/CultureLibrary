# Ukraine: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Polish and Latin (old court books). **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volhynia and Kyiv castle-court parties of different estates and faiths:** check who challenged judges and on what grounds in the act books.
- **Donetsk, Dnipro and Lviv elites:** check how studies trace patron networks in each and which offices they reached.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check trial reports from occupied Crimea alongside the occupying side's case files, where published.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Bukovinian Romanians:** check how testing in the state language has been argued over as fair or unfair to their pupils.
- **Rural *hromady* against Kyiv:** check how small-town officials handle kin in hiring, in local press and audit findings.
- **Roma groups:** check ombudsperson complaints on police and school bias, and how Roma organizations log them.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check rabbinical rules barring relatives from judging, as applied in Ukrainian towns' community records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for anticorruption law, judicial reform and election observation; mixed for everyday favoritism; thin for Karaim and Azov Greek community arbitration.
- **Languages needed:** Polish, Latin and Middle Ukrainian for the act books; Ukrainian and Russian legal language; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2020 Constitutional Court crisis and occupied-Crimea trials are contested; present each side's reading without settling it. Judges' family details in investigative work concern living people. The spirit of an independent judiciary belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** popular "corruption index" league tables were considered and left out in favor of experts' administrative-impartiality measures, a fit judgment rather than a rule.
