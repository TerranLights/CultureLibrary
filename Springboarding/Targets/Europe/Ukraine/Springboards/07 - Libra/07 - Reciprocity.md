# Ukraine: Reciprocity (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian (with Volhynian and Polissian ritual terms), *surzhyk* and the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical), Romanian. **Concept in one line:** returning what one receives, in kind: gifts for gifts, help for help, harm for harm; the give-and-take that binds people and keeps accounts between them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volhynia and Polissia against the Left Bank:** check whether the post-Easter days for the dead keep grave meals and gifts, and in what form, in each.
- **Hutsul and Boyko villages against Kyiv suburbs:** check whether godchildren still carry the Christmas supper and what they receive.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how hospitality and its return are described in exile memoirs and after 1989.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check work-help customs in both communities' ethnographies, village by village.
- **Jewish communities (historical):** check how Hasidic and non-Hasidic sources describe gifts to rebbes and to the poor.
- **Displaced people since 2022 and host households:** check what each side reports was owed or given back.
- **Greek Catholics, Orthodox of both jurisdictions and Pentecostals:** check whether each keeps commemorative meals and what their clergy say of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for carols and calendar customs; mixed for everyday favors; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Azov Greek gift customs (start with community museums and the Mariupol-area collections, many of them now displaced).
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with Volhynian and Polissian ritual vocabulary; Hebrew and Yiddish for Hasidic material; Hungarian; Crimean Tatar; Russian for Soviet patronage records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grave customs in occupied areas and the war's displacement are painful ground; use published accounts. The letter of gift law is here; what the law is for is Capricorn's. Revenge as returned harm belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** marketing studies of Ukrainian holiday spending came up and were set aside as retail data that says little about return obligations; a judgment of fit, with no rule invoked.
