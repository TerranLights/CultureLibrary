*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia, the Dnipro lands and the Donbas:** check how "against all" and "hard to say" shares differed by oblast before 2014.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how a return or departure decision is described as a family's or a community's, in oral histories.
- **Jewish families (historical):** check who in the family held the final word in emigration memoirs.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Bukovinian Romanians:** check how families describe choosing between two states' documents and schools.
- **Hutsul and Polissian villages against Poltava villages:** check which divination customs each region recorded for choosing a spouse.
- **Displaced families since 2022:** check how decisions to return or stay away are discussed in local press and surveys.
- **Baptists and Pentecostals against Orthodox and Greek Catholic parishes:** check how each community's guidance hands a decision to prayer, elders or family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foreign-policy law, elections and polling; mixed for divination customs (old ethnographies dominate); thin for everyday indecision talk, and for Karaim and Azov Greek decision customs.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian with dialect skill for calendar customs; Russian for 1990s policy debates and many memoirs; Yiddish; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2013, the non-bloc status and wartime departures are contested and personal; present each account as an account. No claim that any people is indecisive: the saying-level material is to be traced, not adopted. Rashness belongs to Aries.
- **Held for review:** expatriate business blogs on "slow decisions in Ukrainian offices" were weighed and dropped as anecdote that edges toward stereotype; the call was one of fit, not of a standing rule.
