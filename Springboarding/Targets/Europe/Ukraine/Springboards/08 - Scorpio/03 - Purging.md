# Ukraine: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Church Slavonic, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Hebrew, Romanian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Orthodox (both jurisdictions) and Greek Catholics against Baptists and Pentecostals:** check whether fasting and confession are kept and how the fast is counted.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how fasting and ablution are taught in Crimea and among those who moved to the mainland after 2014.
- **Jewish communities of Uman and Kyiv:** check who uses the mikveh and the *tahare* rooms now, and how they are kept.
- **Bukovinian Romanians and Hutsuls:** check cleansing cures with egg, wax and water in mixed villages.
- **Vlax Roma, the *servy* (*check*) and Transcarpathian Roma:** check whether purity rules are recorded at all, from Roma authors first; thin.
- **Donbas and Kharkiv towns against Lviv and Ternopil:** check how monument removals were met in each.
- **Party families and the families of the purged:** check memoirs on both sides of the 1930s cleansings for the word *чистка*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church fasting, monument removals and the 1930s terror; mixed for folk cures; thin for Roma purity and for Karaim rules.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the *Номоканон*; Russian for party files and Ivanov; Crimean Tatar; Yiddish and Hebrew; Romanian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mob lustration, decommunization, church anathemas and the expulsions of whole peoples (1944, 1947) are contested; give each community's own account. The lustration law itself belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** for the detox facet, imported wellness books were considered and left as too general; nothing was held under a rule here.
