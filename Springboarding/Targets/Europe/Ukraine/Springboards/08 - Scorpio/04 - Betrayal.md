# Ukraine: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Hebrew, Polish, Hungarian, Romanian. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which loyalty was owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia and Volhynia against central and eastern oblasts:** check which wartime figures each region's school textbooks and local museums call traitors or heroes.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how families recount the 1944 charge and its lifting, against the official texts.
- **Jewish communities of the former Pale towns:** check memoirs of the cantonist years and of informing (*mesires*) for how communities judged their own.
- **Poles of Lviv and Volhynia:** check the Polish volumes beside the Ukrainian ones for which events each names first.
- **Russian-speaking families in Kharkiv, Odesa and the Donbas:** check how they describe divided loyalties within one household since 2014, in their own interviews.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check how the 1939 and 1944 changes of rule are told locally, including who is said to have switched sides.
- **Veterans and their partners:** check where men's and women's accounts of wartime infidelity diverge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political treason narratives and memoirs; mixed for infidelity; thin for Krymchak, Karaim and Gagauz sources on informing.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and Old East Slavic; Russian for MGB files and Kostomarov; Polish; Crimean Tatar; Yiddish for cantonist memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the OUN and UPA, the Volhynia massacres, wartime collaboration in the Holocaust and since 2022, and the Crimean Tatar charge are contested; give each community's account and accuse no group. Collaboration law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** for wartime collaboration in the Holocaust a single historian's monograph was considered and left out, since one voice would stand in for a debate this sheet cannot hold fairly; no standing rule was involved.
