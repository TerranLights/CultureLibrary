## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former collective-farm villages of the center and east against Galicia, collectivized later:** check memory of passport and movement controls by generation.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check how carnival and fasting seasons set and lift restraint in each community.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check what *taqdir* and *qader* carry in sermons and daily speech, against Ukrainian talk of *доля*.
- **Jewish communities:** check *bashert* in matchmaking and in talk of fate.
- **Bukovinian Romanians:** check beliefs about the *ursitoare* at birth beside Ukrainian fate figures.
- **Winegrowing districts of Odesa, the Budjak (Bulgarians, Gagauz) and Transcarpathia:** check how the 1985 campaign is remembered.
- **Women and men in shelter and survey data:** check which kinds of control each names, without assuming direction.
- **Gig workers in Kyiv and Lviv:** check whether app control is described differently by city.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet controls and fate folklore; mixed for coercive control in couples; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Roma views of fate in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian; Russian for Potebnia and Soviet documents; Hungarian; Crimean Tatar; Yiddish; Romanian; English for Orwell's preface.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** shelter data must protect survivors; Soviet controls are remembered differently by region. Law on violence goes to Libra; discipline to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** wartime mobilization rules came up for control over men's movement, but they are law and belong to Libra and Capricorn; nothing was held on any other ground.
