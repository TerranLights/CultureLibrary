# Ukraine: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Polish, Yiddish and Hebrew, Crimean Tatar, Romanian, Hungarian. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how what may, must or must not be kept hidden is judged; privacy is one facet.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Greek Catholics of Galicia and Transcarpathia (1946 to 1989):** check how hidden liturgies and ordinations are described in memoirs, apart from church histories.
- **Jewish communities of Podillia and Lviv:** check what was kept hidden in the 18th century and during the war, and how survivors and descendants speak of it now.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how religious and community life was kept up quietly in exile, from their own memoirs.
- **Protestant congregations that refused registration in Soviet times:** check how they hid meetings, printing and children's teaching.
- **Hutsuls and Boykos:** check whether village healers' and singers' knowledge was passed on in secret, and on what terms it is now shared.
- **Bukovinian Romanians and Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check how second passports and family ties abroad are spoken of, or not.
- **Workers in the informal economy of all regions:** check how envelope wages are discussed openly or kept quiet, city against village.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state secrecy and secret-police archives; mixed for family secrets; thin for Karaim and Krymchak communal secrecy, where community leaders and museum keepers are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and Russian; Polish and Hebrew for the Frankist sources; Yiddish; Crimean Tatar; Romanian and Hungarian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informers' names, HIV status and sexual identity are personal data; report patterns, not persons. The OUN and UPA underground and wartime secrecy are contested; give each account. Medical secrecy as law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** keepers of restricted healing and ritual knowledge (Hutsul *мольфари* and others) are a lead only as they choose to speak; nothing more was set aside, and no standing rule was triggered.
