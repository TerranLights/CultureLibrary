## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia and Volhynia against the Donbas and Kharkiv:** check how voters describe their influence on government in the same survey waves.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how the Mejlis and Qurultay describe their own authority, and how the khanate's past is used in that account.
- **Bessarabian Bulgarians and Gagauz of the Budjak:** check how district reform changed their seats and offices.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Rusyns:** check minority parties' and associations' own statements on local power.
- **Roma of Transcarpathia and the south:** check how leadership is named by Roma themselves, not by press labels; thin.
- **Jewish communities, past and present:** check who spoke for communities to the state, from the kahal to today's federations.
- **Students and workers in Kyiv, Lviv and the Donbas:** check protest memoirs (1989, 1990, 2004, 2013 to 2014) for who held power and who took it.
