# Ukraine: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern steppe (Mykolaiv, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson) against Poltava and Kyiv regions:** check which famine years each region's family memory holds.
- **Donbas mining towns against Kryvbas and Lviv-Volhynia coal towns:** check how mine disasters are mourned and explained locally.
- **Odesa's many communities (Ukrainian, Russian-speaking, Jewish, Bulgarian):** check epidemic memories of 1812 and 1970 in each community's writing.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians, Rusyns and Roma:** check who received flood aid after 1998 and 2001, by village.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how 2014 is told in Crimean Tatar media against Ukrainian-language national media.
- **Families with foreign-currency loans:** check whether city and town borrowers describe the crisis differently.
- **Internally displaced people since 2014 and 2022:** check how those who stayed near the front and those who left describe the turning point.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for wartime and economic crises; mixed for Soviet-era disasters, which were hushed at the time; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Azov Greek community accounts of crisis.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian and Russian; Latin or translations for Carpini; English for relief archives; Crimean Tatar; Hungarian; French for Richelieu's papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** famine numbers, the 2014 events in Crimea and the war since 2022 are contested; give each count as its source's. Emergency law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** COVID-era vaccine rumors were weighed for the panic facet but belong with trust (sheet 6); nothing here met a standing rule.
