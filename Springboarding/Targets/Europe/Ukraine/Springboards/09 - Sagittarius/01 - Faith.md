# Ukraine: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish and Hebrew, Polish, Romanian, Hungarian, Romani. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Faithful of the Orthodox Church of Ukraine and of the Ukrainian Orthodox Church:** check which saints, icons and shrines each promotes, and how people who moved between them explain the move.
- **Greek Catholics of Galicia against those of the Mukachevo eparchy:** check whether underground-era memories and devotions differ between Lviv and Uzhhorod.
- **Roman Catholics of Zhytomyr, Podillia and Lviv:** check Marian shrines and Polish-language devotion against Ukrainian-language parishes.
- **Jewish communities of Berdychiv, Dnipro and Odesa:** check heritage pilgrimage against the practice of resident congregations.
- **Crimean Tatars in exile and after return:** check how faith was kept and rebuilt, in their own oral histories.
- **Evangelicals of the Kherson and Mykolaiv steppe; Roma congregations:** check conversion accounts by date and by whose press printed them.
- **Hutsul and Boyko villages:** check which devotions sit beside the church rite and who leads them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church writing, sermons and survey items; mixed for secular faith (in the army, science, the nation), which needs survey items read for that purpose; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Roma faith.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic and old Ukrainian for Tuptalo and Haliatovsky, Hebrew for *Kedushat Levi*, Crimean Tatar and Russian for the exile interviews, Romani for any Roma sermon recordings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hrushiv and the underground church are told differently by the Greek Catholic church, Soviet files and Orthodox authors; the two Orthodox churches dispute each other's standing. Give every account with its teller's name. Oaths in court and office go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the faith-healing and charismatic megachurch pointers considered here were dropped as outside this concept's center rather than set aside under a rule; Fundamentalism takes them up.
