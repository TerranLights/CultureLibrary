# Ukraine: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Students and teachers in Kharkiv, Lviv and Odesa universities:** check plagiarism cases and how each campus handles them.
- **Small traders of the Uzhhorod and Chernivtsi border areas:** check what they count as fair dealing, in their own words.
- **Officials of hromadas created in 2020 against oblast agencies:** check bribe reports by service level.
- **Roma communities of Transcarpathia and Odesa:** check how press labels are answered by community bodies.
- **Jewish merchants of 19th c. Berdychiv and Odesa:** check business correspondence and communal records on honest dealing, read beside non-Jewish sources.
- **Crimean Tatar shopkeepers and craftsmen:** check community writing on trade ethics; sources are few.
- **Villages of Polissia and the Steppe:** check which tall tales and liar tales are told where.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption surveys and watchdog cases; mixed for everyday honesty and white lies; thin for honesty norms among Karaim, Krymchak and Azov Greek traders.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for Soviet files and 1990s slang; Hungarian, Romanian and Slovak for the border studies; Romani for community responses; Yiddish for 19th c. merchant letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** smuggling and corruption talk slides easily into stereotypes about borderlanders, Roma and others; every pointer here asks what people say and do, never what a group is. Court cases and anti-corruption law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** lost-wallet field experiments were weighed for this concept, but no Ukrainian city could be confirmed in them from memory, so nothing is named; no rule-bound item came up.
