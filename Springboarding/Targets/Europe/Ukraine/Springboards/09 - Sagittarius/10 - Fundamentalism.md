# Ukraine: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, Russian, Romanian (for the Balta movement), Hebrew and Yiddish, Crimean Tatar and Arabic, Hungarian. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself disputed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for Soviet anti-sect material and church polemic; thin and partisan for small groups since 1991; scarce for Krymchak and Azov Greek strict currents, where nothing should be filled in from neighbors.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for *Перестрога*; Hebrew and Yiddish for the Belz and Munkács writings; Romanian and Russian for Inochentism; Crimean Tatar, Russian and Arabic for Crimean Muslim sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "sect", "extremist" and "Wahhabi" are labels used as weapons; give each with its user. Prosecutions in occupied Crimea are reported by opposed parties; attribute every claim. Violence is the rare edge and must not color whole communities.
- **Held for review:** one pointer on a religious movement was set aside under the standing rule, kept here as a category only: South Asian religious movements in the nation's lands.
