# Ukraine: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical), German (colonist sources), Polish and Old Ruthenian (Lithuanian-era registers). **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former serf villages of the Right Bank against Cossack-descended villages of the Left Bank:** check which labor and service duties each kind of community remembers carrying.
- **Families with men at the front, families abroad and families in occupied areas:** check how each group's own testimony describes what is owed and to whom.
- **Bukovinian Romanians and Transcarpathian Hungarians:** check which state each group served in and which war duties its villages record.
- **Shtetl communities of Podillia and Volhynia:** check communal duties (charity, burial, education) in society ledgers.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check religious duties and duties to kin in exile and after return, in the community's own press.
- **Mennonite colonies and Baptist and Pentecostal congregations:** check how each body ranks duty to God against the state's call to arms.
- **Older rural women in Polissia and Podillia:** check care for grandchildren of migrant parents as a duty, in local studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for serf and Cossack obligations and for wartime service; mixed for family duty; thin for Krymchak, Karaim and Roma obligations.
- **Languages needed:** Old Ruthenian and Polish for the 1528 register; Russian for the inventory rules; Hungarian for *levente* sources; Crimean Tatar and Arabic terms for alms duties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobilization, evasion and the conduct of call-up officers are contested and partisan; present each account as its source's. Whether the law may compel service is Libra's question; what service is for stays here.
- **Held for review:** a pointer on men's duties in the diaspora was considered and kept back, since the diaspora is comparison only and the sheet stays inside the nation.
