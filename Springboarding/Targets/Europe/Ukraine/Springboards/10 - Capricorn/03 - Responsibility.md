# Ukraine: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Polish, Yiddish (historical), Old Rus' (early legal texts). **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Polissia and Volhynia villages against Kherson and Tavria farm towns:** check whether the commune's joint answerability left different memories in old and newer settlements.
- **Chornobyl evacuees and Slavutych residents:** check how those moved from the zone speak of who was answerable, in their own oral histories.
- **Crimean Tatars, Black Sea Germans, Azov Greeks:** check how each community's writing answers the collective charges of the 1940s.
- **Kahal-era and later Jewish communities:** check whether surety for members appears in community ledgers.
- **Lviv and Galician municipal politics against Kharkiv and Dnipro:** check how mayors and governors are held to account in each city's press.
- **Transcarpathian Hungarians and Bukovinian Romanians:** check local-council accountability in the minority-language press.
- **Military families and veterans' groups:** check how units and families speak of accountability for losses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and inquiries; mixed for everyday responsibility; thin for Karaim and Roma views of collective answerability.
- **Languages needed:** Old Rus' for the *Правда*, Russian for the Chornobyl memoirs and commune records, German and Rumeic Greek for deportee histories, Hungarian and Romanian for local press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ilovaisk, Chornobyl and the 1940s deportations are contested; name each account's author and side. Where a court assigned guilt, the verdicts belong to Libra; here only what being answerable means.
- **Held for review:** a world database on corporate responsibility rankings came up and was dropped, since its Ukrainian coverage could not be confirmed from memory.
