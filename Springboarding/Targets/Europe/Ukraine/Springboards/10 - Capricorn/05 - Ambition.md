*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galician and Bukovinian villages with many migrant workers:** check what families say a migrant's earnings are for, and how success is shown.
- **Kyiv and Kharkiv newcomers from small towns:** check how quota workers and students recall the climb into city life.
- **Hetmanate officer families and their descendants:** check family histories for careers made in Petersburg against those kept at home.
- **Jewish families of Odesa, Kyiv and the shtetls:** check what routes up (trade, schooling, emigration) each generation chose.
- **Crimean Tatars after 1989:** check how rebuilding and rising are told in the community's own press.
- **Hungarian and Romanian youth of Transcarpathia and Bukovina:** check whether study and work plans point to Kyiv or across the border.
- **Women in Soviet and post-Soviet careers:** check memoirs and interviews for how ambition was spoken of by women and by men.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for biographies and literature; mixed for surveys of aspiration; thin for Roma, Karaim and Urum ambitions (start with community histories).
- **Languages needed:** Old Rus' for the chronicle, Russian for imperial biographies and Soviet migration, Polish for Galician women's sources, Yiddish and Russian for Jewish education.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** oligarch wealth and wartime profiteering are partisan topics; migrants' houses show private property, so avoid naming owners.
- **Held for review:** national rich lists came up again and were left off, since striving, not fortunes, is this sheet's subject and Taurus already points to them.
