## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galicia under Austria and the Dnipro lands under the empire:** check whether exams or estate decided entry to gymnasia and offices.
- **Kharkiv and Kyiv first-generation students of the 1920s:** check memoirs of workers' faculty graduates for how they saw their own rise.
- **Shtetl and Odesa Jewish families:** check learning against wealth as a route up, generation by generation.
- **Crimean Tatar returnees:** check how degrees earned in Central Asia were recognized or not after return.
- **Hungarian and Romanian school graduates of Transcarpathia and Bukovina:** check how language of schooling affects test results and later posts.
- **Front-line units and civilian employers:** check how veterans' wartime records are counted in hiring.
- **Roma pupils in Transcarpathian schools:** check who reaches secondary and higher schooling, and through which programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for testing, admissions and honors; mixed for opinion on merit and connections; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Urum educational paths.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** the police re-attestation, admission bribery and wartime reward schemes involve named people; cite reports, not individuals. Court reversals belong to Libra.
