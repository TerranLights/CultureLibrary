# Ukraine: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine, Polish, Crimean Tatar, Hungarian, Romanian, Yiddish and Hebrew (historical). **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lviv and Zhovkva's planned old towns against Odesa's grid and Soviet microdistricts in Kharkiv:** check what each town plan was meant to keep in place.
- **Hutsul and Polissian villages:** check which seasonal rites mark the return of order after license.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check how community writing describes order kept in exile and after return.
- **Hasidic and other Jewish communities, past and present:** check how ritual order shaped shared space.
- **Budjak Bulgarians, Gagauz and Bukovinian Romanians:** check the set order of village rites in each language.
- **Kyiv, Kharkiv and Odesa residents under martial law:** check how curfew and alarm rules are kept in each city.
- **Protesters, police and residents in 2013 to 2014:** check each group's account of who kept order and who broke it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for town plans, festivals and wartime rules; mixed for crime data under martial law; thin for Karaim, Krymchak and Roma ideas of order.
- **Languages needed:** Ukrainian for *koliadky* and city complaints, Russian for Odesa plans and Kazantip press, Romanian for Bukovina, Hebrew and Yiddish for the *eruv*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Odesa events, Maidan and the occupied areas are contested; name each account's source. Policing powers as written belong to Libra; martial-law data are partly withheld.
- **Held for review:** rankings of "safe cities" built from visitors' ratings were weighed and dropped, as crowd-sourced scores of unknown quality.
