# Ukraine: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian (and Church Slavonic for monastic tales), the Russian of Ukraine and its youth slang, Yiddish, Hungarian, Polish, Crimean Tatar. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lviv and Kyiv late-Soviet scenes against Kharkiv and Odesa ones:** check how police and party papers treated each city's *неформали*.
- **Galician provincial writers of the "Stanislav phenomenon" against Kyiv's literary establishment:** compare who published where.
- **Russian-speaking Odesa artists and comics:** check how their nonconformity is now read after 2014 and 2022.
- **Pentecostal, Adventist and Jehovah's Witness objectors:** compare each church's stance and the cases the press covered.
- **Trans and queer people in big cities and small towns:** use Insight's breakdowns by place.
- **Polissian and Hutsul villages:** compare the roles given to the village fool or blessed one.
- **Young Hungarians of Berehove and young Romanians of Chernivtsi region:** ask which capital's music and fashion their scenes followed, and why.
- **Crimean Tatar artists and musicians since 1989:** check how they mark difference within their own community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for late-Soviet art and youth scenes; mixed for objectors; thin for subcultures among Gagauz, Rusyns and Roma, so search their own media first.
- **Languages needed:** Church Slavonic for the Patericon, Russian slang for hippie and informal sources, Yiddish for *apikoyres* writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** objectors' cases are argued fiercely in wartime; gender nonconformity draws church and political attacks; some hippie and scene archives are private and need permission.
- **Held for review:** psychiatric case files of Soviet dissidents were left out of this sheet because they hold private health data; they belong to a later, permission-based step, and no standing rule was involved.
