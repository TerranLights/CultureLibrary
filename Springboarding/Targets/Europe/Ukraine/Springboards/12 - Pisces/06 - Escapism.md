# Ukraine: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, the Russian of Ukraine (much escapist fiction and film was written in it), *surzhyk*, Crimean Tatar, Yiddish, German (Mennonite), Hungarian (Transcarpathia). **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Slobozhanshchyna and the Right Bank:** check how each region's histories tell flight from war as part of who they are.
- **Black Sea resort towns (Odesa, Zatoka) and the Carpathian valleys:** check which escapes each sells, and to whom.
- **Russian-language and Ukrainian-language readers of fantasy:** check whether fan communities divide by language or overlap.
- **Mennonite colonists (historical):** check the emigration debate in colony letters.
- **Crimean Tatars:** check escape stories in family memory beside the forced departures, keeping the two kinds apart.
- **Transcarpathian border villages (Rusyn, Hungarian, Romanian):** check how mountain and river crossings are spoken of locally.
- **Village households of Polissia and Podillia:** check where home distilling sits in custom and in health data.
- **Young people in Kyiv and Kharkiv:** check fandom, gaming and drinking against the ESPAD figures.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Soviet holidays and youth surveys; mixed for fandom; thin for older village forms of escape beyond drink.
- **Languages needed:** Russian for the Koenker sources and the Kyiv fantasy writers, German for the 1874 letters, Hungarian and Romanian for border-village sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mobilization evasion is a live dispute, and the men and families involved may face penalties, so record accounts without names; substance-use data on minors is protected.
- **Held for review:** the shelf is empty for this sheet; Western "digital escapism" studies came up as candidates but were judged too generic, and Ukraine's own gaming material sits in Leo.
