# Ukraine: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Ukraine, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** Ukrainian, Church Slavonic, the Russian of Ukraine, Crimean Tatar, Hebrew and Yiddish, Hungarian, Arabic (one 17th-century outsider). **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion; the experience, the practice and the sacred outside the church (faith and religion as institutions belong to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Galician Greek Catholics and the Orthodox faithful of the center and east:** check which private devotions (springs, home prayer, pilgrimages) each keeps outside the liturgy.
- **Those who answer "just a Christian":** check age, region and language to see who is spiritual without a church.
- **Hasidic pilgrims and the towns that host them (Hadiach, Uman, Medzhybizh):** check how locals and pilgrims each describe the graves.
- **Crimean Tatar Muslims:** check home prayer and shrine visits since return, in their own media.
- **Hungarian and Rusyn Catholics of Transcarpathia:** check whether pilgrimages cross language lines.
- **Steppe and Tavria villages:** check new pagan and eco-spiritual use of old sites against local Orthodox practice.
- **Donbas monastic pilgrims:** check how the war changed who visits Sviatohirsk and why.
- **Karaim, Krymchaks and the Azov Greeks:** check what survives of home ritual; printed sources are few, so ask community elders and museum staff.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for monasteries, music and survey data; mixed for springs and home devotion; thin for small peoples' practice outside their houses of worship.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic (or a translation) for Paul of Aleppo, Church Slavonic for Galiatovsky, Hebrew for Chabad sources, Hungarian for the *búcsú*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sviatohirsk and the 2016 procession sit inside the church-jurisdiction dispute; Kamiana Mohyla and Crimea are under occupation; closed rites stay unresearched.
- **Held for review:** two pointers were put aside under the standing rules, one in the category of South Asian-derived meditation and body practices and one in the category of Buddhism, an open question for the author.
