## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland crofting townships and Western Isles:** community control (Sabbath observance on Lewis and Harris) sits beside fierce land self-rule; worth separating.
- **Orkney and Shetland:** island governance claims differ from mainland Scotland's.
- **Cornwall:** self-government claims as a minority, not a region.
- **North East England:** a region that voted on its own assembly in 2004; compare with Wales and Scotland.
- **Wales:** Welsh-speaking rural communities and Valleys towns may answer "who decides" differently.
- **Disabled people:** independent living as a movement.
- **Young people:** leaving home and street freedom vary by class and region.
- **Gypsies and Travellers:** mobility and stopping places as the core of self-rule.
- **Haredi Jewish communities:** strong communal direction; leavers' accounts.
- **British Caribbean and British Chinese families:** family say over careers and marriage against individual choice.
- **Irish in Britain:** Catholic parish life as a frame on personal choice.
- **Comparison only:** Northern Ireland, whose own devolved settlement differs; British communities abroad.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on disability, work discretion and land; mixed on family decision-making by group.
- **Languages needed for autonomy:** Gaelic and Welsh for land and self-rule records; Scots for kirk session records; Yiddish for Haredi sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abortion, assisted dying, capacity and gender (their legal side belongs in Libra or Capricorn); forced settlement of Travellers; island independence politics.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
