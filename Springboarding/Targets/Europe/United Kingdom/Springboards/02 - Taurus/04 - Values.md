## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lewis and Harris Presbyterians:** a sacred day held against trade and leisure.
- **Welsh chapel communities, north and south:** temperance and Sabbath as taught values, and their decline.
- **Quakers and other dissenting traditions:** a written order of values.
- **Jewish communities, Orthodox and progressive:** different rankings within one minority.
- **Yemeni and other Arab communities (South Shields, Sheffield, Cardiff):** faith-based limits on interest and trade.
- **Polish Catholic families:** values passed on through parish Saturday schools.
- **British Chinese families:** family duty and schooling as stated priorities.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities:** the Romani way set against settled norms.
- **Generations and the capital:** London against smaller towns; the young against the old.
- **Northern Ireland (comparison only):** values surveys split by religious community, a check on how Britain's surveys are cut.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; values surveys and moral writing are plentiful, but ethnic and regional subsamples are small.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for gnomic verse; Welsh for chapel archives; Gaelic for island church life; Arabic and Polish for community material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "British values" is tied to counter-extremism policy and contested; Sabbath disputes divide island communities; avoid treating any group's values as uniform.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
