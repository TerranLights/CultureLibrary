## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yorkshire against Nottinghamshire coalfields:** strikers and working miners in the same strike.
- **Highland crofting townships:** rent strikes and land raids.
- **Lowland and south-west Scotland:** Covenanter memory.
- **Cornwall:** rebellions and a motto of unity.
- **Welsh-speaking Wales:** language campaigns won by refusing to yield.
- **The Fens:** a holdout legend.
- **Liverpool:** decades of campaigning by families.
- **The Black Country:** women's labor firmness.
- **Yemeni and other Arab seamen's families in South Shields and Cardiff:** resistance to unequal treatment.
- **British Caribbean communities in Bristol and London:** boycotts.
- **Polish families from the postwar wave:** a refusal to return.
- **Jewish East End families:** street resistance in memory.
- **Northern Ireland (comparison only):** slogans of refusal on both community sides, set beside British holdout memories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, protests and campaigns; thin for everyday stubbornness (family feuds, habits), which needs oral history and advice columns.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for *Maldon*; Gaelic for Braes testimony; Welsh for language-campaign archives; Scots for *thrawn* in literature; Polish and Arabic for community memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Miners' Strike still divides families and towns; Hillsborough and the Post Office cases involve living victims; Brexit identities are polarizing. Strike law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
