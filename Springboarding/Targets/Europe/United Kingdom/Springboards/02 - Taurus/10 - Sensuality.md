## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Welsh valleys and chapel towns:** choral pleasure and chapel restraint.
- **Lowland Presbyterian parishes against Highland and island ceilidh culture:** policed pleasure and shared song.
- **Islay and Speyside:** smell and taste as local identity.
- **Cumbria and the Lake District:** landscape as sensory delight.
- **Seaside towns (Blackpool, Brighton, Scarborough):** pleasure resorts and their decline.
- **British Caribbean communities:** carnival, sound systems and food.
- **Polish families:** fasting and feasting at Wigilia.
- **Somali and Yemeni communities:** coffee houses and guest rooms as sensory and social spaces, compared across Cardiff, Sheffield and South Shields.
- **Jewish families:** the Sabbath table.
- **British Chinese communities in Liverpool and Manchester:** Chinatown food and New Year.
- **Cornwall:** croust, pasties and the Lowender Peran festival.
- **Northern Ireland (comparison only):** the Ulster fry and the showband dance halls as a near neighbor's sensory culture.
