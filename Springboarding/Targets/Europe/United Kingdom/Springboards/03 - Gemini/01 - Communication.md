# United Kingdom: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its dialects, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish, BSL; Middle English for letters. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the local idea of what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Merseyside against the rest of the North West:** a strongly marked urban accent; compare corpus speakers from Liverpool with those from Greater Manchester before generalizing about the region.
- **Birmingham and the Black Country:** accents that score differently in attitude studies; separate the listener's region from the speaker's.
- **Aberdeen and the North-East Doric area:** check how Doric and English are switched in school, at home and at work.
- **Norfolk and East Anglia:** the Norwich study and the Paston Letters give two time points for one area.
- **New Welsh speakers in Cardiff against first-language speakers in Gwynedd:** check whether *ti* and *chi* choices differ by how Welsh was learned.
- **British Chinese families:** separate Cantonese, Hakka and Mandarin speakers and the generation acting as interpreters for parents.
- **Kurdish communities:** Kurmanji and Sorani speakers use different interpreters; check NHS interpreting records for each.
- **Deaf BSL users and deafened adults:** different communication routes; never pool.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: corpora, accent studies and archives of letters and telephones; thin for interpreting in minority languages.
- **Languages needed:** Middle English for the Pastons; Welsh and Gaelic for the formal and familiar "you"; BSL for Deaf sources; Cantonese and Kurdish for language-brokering work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** accent bias touches hiring and class; Samaritans call content is confidential, so use only its published material.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
