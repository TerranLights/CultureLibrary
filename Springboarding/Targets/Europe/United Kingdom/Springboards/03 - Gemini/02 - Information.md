## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Caithness and Sutherland, and the Western Isles:** distance and broadband gaps; check local-press and connectivity figures by council area before comparing with cities.
- **Galloway and Ayrshire:** check how far local titles have closed or merged, and what replaced them.
- **The East Midlands:** a region often missing from regional news maps; check its share of local outlets against its population.
- **Cornwall and the Isles of Scilly:** separate island and mainland access to news and services.
- **Jewish communities:** separate communal papers and the Haredi press (Yiddish and Hebrew) from mainstream use.
- **Irish in Britain:** compare readership of the Irish community press by decade of arrival.
- **Hong Kong arrivals since 2021:** check which Cantonese-language channels and group chats they use; period of arrival is the variable.
- **Romanian and Bulgarian communities:** information through own-language social-media groups; check group size and moderators.
- **Showpeople and Boat Dwellers:** no fixed address affects post, voter and census contact; check outreach records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history, broadcasting and official statistics; mixed for community and migrant channels.
- **Languages needed:** Old English and Latin for the Domesday and Chronicle layer; Welsh for the *papurau bro*; Gaelic for island news; Cantonese, Romanian, Bulgarian, Yiddish and Hebrew for community channels.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the law on information (freedom of information, official secrets, data protection) belongs in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit), not here; wartime morale files describe named places and must be read in context.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
