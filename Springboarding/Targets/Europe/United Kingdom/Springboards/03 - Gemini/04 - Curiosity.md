*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Birmingham and the West Midlands:** compare the Lunar Society record with visitor data from the region's science center today.
- **Cornwall:** mining-region inquiry through the polytechnic and the Camborne mining school; check membership by occupation.
- **The Dorset coast and the Isle of Wight:** fossil collecting by residents and visitors; separate local collectors from tourists, and note women collectors such as Mary Anning (check).
- **Orkney:** community archaeology and open days at excavations; check volunteer and visitor counts.
- **Edinburgh's learned societies against the Gaelic Highlands:** compare society proceedings with Gaelic oral lore (for example *Carmina Gadelica*, check) for what was asked about nature and the sky.
- **Hobby communities (birders, rail enthusiasts, detectorists):** check membership by age, gender and region rather than the labels.
- **Hong Kong arrivals and Latin American communities:** check each group's own etiquette and orientation materials for which personal questions count as intrusive; separate by place of origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: learned societies, museums, citizen science and science-attitude surveys; thin for everyday nosiness, which needs fiction, letters and Mass Observation-style writing.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for early Royal Society material; Old English for *fyrwit*; Gaelic for Highland oral lore; Welsh for *chwilfrydedd* in the press; Cantonese and Spanish for the community etiquette sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human remains and sacred objects in British museums are under repatriation claims; patient records at Bethlem are closed; privacy law belongs in Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
