# United Kingdom: Connection (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish; Old and Middle English for the early record. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected or not.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Welsh Marches:** a border zone where ties cross English and Welsh lines; compare Frankenberg's village with later restudies.
- **North East pit villages and the South Wales Valleys:** check club and welfare-hall records before and after the 1980s closures.
- **Argyll and the Western Isles:** ferry timetables shape contact; separate island and mainland survey responses.
- **Irish in Britain:** separate the 1950s wave from later arrivals; county associations differ by period.
- **British Caribbean families:** church and *pardner* records; separate the Windrush generation from British-born children.
- **Ghanaian, Nigerian and Zimbabwean communities:** hometown and church associations; separate by country, ethnic group and decade.
- **Romanian and Bulgarian communities:** recent transnational ties; check remittance and travel data.
- **Kurdish communities:** community centers as connectors; separate by home country.
- **Older people living alone:** compare ONS loneliness rates by region and urban or rural setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness statistics and community studies; mixed for migrant networks.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for the exile poems; Welsh for *cynefin* and *hiraeth*; Gaelic for *dùthchas*; Twi, Yoruba, Igbo and Shona for African associations; Romanian, Bulgarian, Kurdish and Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Windrush documents scandal touches family records; Hong Kong arrivals' identities need protecting; rules on nepotism belong in Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
