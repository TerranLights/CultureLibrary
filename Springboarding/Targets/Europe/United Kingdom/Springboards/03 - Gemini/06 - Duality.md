# United Kingdom: Duality (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish; Old and Middle English for the early record. **Concept in one line** (proposed): how people live with two-ness: holding two identities, languages, loyalties or views at once; dividing the world into pairs; and how people who show two faces are treated.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Gàidhealtachd and Galldachd:** check who drew the Highland-Lowland line, when, and in which language.
- **Welsh speakers and non-Welsh speakers within Wales:** compare survey answers on national identity by home language and by north and south.
- **The Anglo-Scottish border (Berwick, Carlisle, the Borders):** identity surveys near the line against those further away.
- **The Marches and Monmouthshire:** a county long described in records as of uncertain country; check how residents answer identity questions.
- **Italian Scots and Welsh Italians:** separate the interned generation from their grandchildren.
- **Greek Cypriot and Turkish Cypriot Londoners:** a homeland divided in two; keep each community's institutions apart and check for joint bodies.
- **British Chinese families:** separate Hong Kong, mainland and Malaysian origins when studying home and school languages.
- **Mixed-heritage people:** compare census self-identification across 2001, 2011 and 2021.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for identity surveys, bilingualism and literature; thin for double lives, which leave few records.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for Siarad; Gaelic for the Highland pair; Scots; Jamaican Creole; Greek, Turkish and Italian; Polari; Old and Middle English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the guarded lives of gay men before 1967 and wartime internment are painful histories; the Cyprus division is politically charged; the law in those histories belongs in Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
