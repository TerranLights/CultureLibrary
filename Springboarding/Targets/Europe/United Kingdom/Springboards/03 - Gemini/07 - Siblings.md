# United Kingdom: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its dialects, Welsh (Middle Welsh for the medieval tales), Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish, Angloromani; Old and Middle English. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Greater Manchester and Lancashire:** "our kid" as a sibling term; check its use by age and gender in dialect recordings.
- **Welsh upland farming families:** which sibling takes the farm; compare county records and oral history.
- **Highland and island crofting families:** which sibling stays on the croft; check Crofting Commission tenancy records over time.
- **Landed families:** eldest and younger sons; compare peerage records with family papers.
- **Haredi Jewish communities in Salford and Stamford Hill:** separate household-size data by stream before comparing.
- **Gypsy and Traveller families:** separate Romany Gypsies and Irish Travellers; check family-size data against community reports.
- **Vietnamese families:** many arrived as refugees from 1979; check records of siblings separated and later reunited.
- **Nigerian and Ghanaian families:** naming customs for twins and birth order differ by ethnic group (Yoruba, Igbo, Akan); separate them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for family history, twin research and care statistics; thin for adult sibling ties outside surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Middle Welsh for the Mabinogi; Old English for *Beowulf*; Gaelic; Angloromani; Yoruba, Igbo and Twi for naming customs; Vietnamese; Hebrew and Yiddish for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care records and sibling violence touch children's privacy; inheritance rules (primogeniture, Kentish gavelkind, Welsh partible inheritance) belong in Libra, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
