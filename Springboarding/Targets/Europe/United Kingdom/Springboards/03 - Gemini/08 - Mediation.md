# United Kingdom: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish; Old and Middle English and Latin for medieval settlement. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach an agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between who stands between parties (brokers, interpreters, intercessors).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The Western Isles and Skye:** check recorded courtship customs for who acted as go-between at a *rèiteach*, by island and period.
- **Welsh chapel communities:** check deacons' minute books for who settled members' quarrels, north and south.
- **The 1984-85 coalfields:** compare Yorkshire, Nottinghamshire and South Wales records of conciliation talks and of local clergy or councilors as intermediaries.
- **Orthodox Jewish communities:** matchmakers and rabbinic go-betweens; keep religious courts for Capricorn.
- **Roma from Romania and Slovakia:** some groups have their own elders' assemblies; check community-led sources only, and leave any judging role to Capricorn.
- **Turkish, Kurdish and Turkish Cypriot Londoners:** separate the community centers each group runs and the languages their advice workers use.
- **Latin American communities:** separate country of origin and Portuguese from Spanish speakers in interpreting and advocacy records.
- **Vietnamese communities:** check community associations as intermediaries with housing and health services since the 1980s.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: workplace conciliation and interpreting are well recorded; family and neighbor go-betweens leave few records except in oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Middle English and Latin for lovedays and intercession; Gaelic for the *rèiteach*; Welsh for chapel records; Turkish, Kurdish, Spanish, Portuguese, Romani and Vietnamese for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family mediation tied to courts, restorative justice and religious courts belong in Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); the Kyle inquiry involves a death.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
