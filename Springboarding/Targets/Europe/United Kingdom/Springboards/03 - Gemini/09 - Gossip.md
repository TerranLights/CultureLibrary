# United Kingdom: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its dialects, Scots, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Cornish; Old and Middle English and Latin for the early record. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Merseyside:** check *jangling* and its users in local dialect recordings, by age and gender.
- **Salford and Greater Manchester:** compare Tebbutt's oral histories with the soap's setting, as two kinds of source for one area.
- **Ceredigion and Carmarthenshire coast:** the towns behind *Under Milk Wood*; check Welsh-language local papers' "news of the village" columns.
- **The West Country (Dorset, Somerset, Wiltshire):** skimmington rides; compare dates and places in Thompson's sources.
- **Shetland and Orkney:** small island communities; check oral-history holdings at the Shetland Museum and Archives and the Orkney Library and Archive.
- **The Isle of Wight:** compare the island press's community pages with mainland Hampshire papers.
- **Jewish communities:** check community teaching materials on speech about others, by stream.
- **Showpeople:** a traveling community with its own trade paper (*World's Fair*, check); separate show families from the public they meet.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: court and kirk records, social history, press and soaps; thin for gossip among men, which may go under other names.
- **Languages needed:** Old and Middle English and Latin for church records; Scots for kirk sessions; Welsh for village columns; Gaelic and Cornish sources; Hebrew for community teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** defamation, church courts, privacy and phone hacking are law: Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit); modern forum posts name private people, so cite threads, not individuals.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
