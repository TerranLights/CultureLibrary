# United Kingdom: The domain: home and family (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and their islands; Northern Ireland is scouted as its own culture-nation), region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English (with dialect forms), Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Old and Middle English for the older record; Angloromani and Cant for Traveller communities. **Concept in one line:** the domain question, what is home and who is family: household forms, roots, family memory, hosting, the home's walls, building ways, family ways and child-rearing ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gaelic-speaking Lewis and Harris against the Lowland central belt: separate house forms, kin naming and the language used at home.
- Welsh-speaking Gwynedd and Anglesey against the south-east valleys: the Welsh terms for home and kin may diverge with language use.
- North East pit villages (Durham, Northumberland): company-built housing and its after-life once the pits closed.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities; Travelling Showpeople: mobile homes, yards and sites as a household base.
- Jewish communities (north and north-west London, Manchester, Glasgow): the home as a site of observance; check by congregation.
- Households hosting Ukrainian arrivals since 2022: what hosting meant on both sides, from the hosts' and guests' accounts.
- Northern Ireland, as comparison only: its household and kin forms are scouted as a separate culture-nation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: long parish and census series, large panel surveys, life-writing archives.
- **Languages needed:** English and Scots for most sources; Gaelic for the Hebridean recordings; Welsh for the Welsh-medium press and broadcast archive; Old English for the earliest household vocabulary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Traveller site disputes; census returns stay closed for a century.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for England, Scotland, Wales and their islands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Cross-sign: food ways to Taurus, heritage to Capricorn, inheritance to Scorpio, family law to Libra or Capricorn.
