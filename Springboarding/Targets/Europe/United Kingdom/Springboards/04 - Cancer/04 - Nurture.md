# United Kingdom: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and the islands), region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English (with Midland and Northern dialect), Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Old and Middle English for courtesy and rearing texts; Polish and Cantonese for community schools. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Nottingham and the East Midlands, the Newsons' city: separate social classes as the study did, then trace change since.
- Bristol and the Severn (ALSPAC area) against the north-west: regional differences in feeding and early routines.
- Welsh-medium and Gaelic-medium families: nurture in the home language against nurture in English.
- Polish Saturday schools and British Chinese weekend schools: what parents add to state schooling; check by generation.
- Gypsy and Traveller families: children learning by working alongside adults; check Traveller education services' records.
- Eritrean and Sudanese refugee families: separate practices brought with them from the advice given by health visitors.
- Fife and the Clyde towns: Growing Up in Scotland sweeps by area.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; Britain has several long birth cohorts and a deep advice literature.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh and Gaelic for language-nest materials; Polish and Cantonese for supplementary-school sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** smacking is a live question with legal change in Scotland and Wales (legal side to Libra); cohort data need approved access.
- **Held for review:** parenting and child-rearing studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
