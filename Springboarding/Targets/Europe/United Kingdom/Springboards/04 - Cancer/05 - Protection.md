# United Kingdom: Protection (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Fishing towns (Hull, Moray coast, Shetland, Newlyn): lifeboats, sea charms and wives' campaigns; check by port.
- South Wales valleys and other coalfields: disaster memory and union protection; separate Aberfan from pit disasters.
- Essex and Lincolnshire coasts: flood memory, 1953 onward.
- Jewish communities: community-run security and refugee-children testimony; check by city.
- Residents of North Kensington's estates, including Eritrean, Sudanese and Moroccan families: the Grenfell testimony by community.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities: reports on harassment and on who protects sites; check the Traveller Movement's material.
- Highland and Hebridean Gaelic speakers: charm and blessing traditions against Lowland folk practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state and charity protection; mixed for protective folk practice, which sits in folklore journals and building surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Gaelic for the charm tradition; Welsh for *gwarchod* in childminding sources; Old English for the charm texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aberfan, Grenfell and child-abuse inquiries involve bereaved families; community security reports; child-protection case records are closed.
- **Held for review:** community-protection and safety studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Child-protection law and statutory guidance go to Libra or Capricorn.
