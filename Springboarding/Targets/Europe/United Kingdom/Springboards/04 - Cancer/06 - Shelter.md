## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- London's rough sleepers against Glasgow and Edinburgh: separate the nations' differing homelessness duties and counts.
- Irish men in Britain's old lodging houses: check the Camden and Kilburn records and later outreach reports.
- Eritrean and Sudanese asylum seekers in Kent and on the south coast: barracks, hotels and charity hosting.
- Ukrainian arrivals since 2022 and their host families: separate both sides' accounts.
- Scottish Travellers and Romany Gypsies: tents, wagons and sites as shelter; check the Travellers' own memoirs.
- Lewis and Harris, and Powys and the Welsh uplands: seasonal shelters (*àirigh*, *hafod*) and their memory.
- Military veterans: housing charities and veterans' hostels; check by service.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; homelessness is counted in detail, and shelter has a long institutional record from almshouse to refuge.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh and Gaelic for the seasonal-shelter vocabulary; Ukrainian for hosting accounts; English hostel slang varies by city.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** refuge addresses are confidential; asylum accommodation is contested; workhouse records name the destitute.
- **Held for review:** homelessness and housing-need studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
