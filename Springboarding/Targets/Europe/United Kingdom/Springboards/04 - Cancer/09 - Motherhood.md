# United Kingdom: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Wales and the islands), region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its regional forms, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Old and Middle English; Yoruba for one of the larger African communities. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- North and south Wales: *nain* against *mam-gu*, a word split worth following into grandmothers' roles.
- The West Midlands ("mom") against the North ("mam"): regional speech as a first sorting key.
- Glasgow and the Clyde towns: maternity and child-welfare records from the tenement era to now.
- Nigerian and Ghanaian families: separate first-generation from British-born mothers; check community surveys on who helps after a birth.
- Maltese and Cypriot families: Marian devotion and mothers' feast days; check by parish.
- Catholic Irish in Britain: mother-and-baby home testimony (the homes sat in Ireland and Britain); separate the two.
- Mothers in the armed forces and military families: separate service mothers from service wives.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; strong birth and maternal-health data, and a deep literature of mothers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for Kate Roberts and for grandmother terms; Gaelic for Hebridean birth customs; Yoruba for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** maternal deaths and mother-and-baby homes involve living families; maternity records are closed.
- **Held for review:** maternity and mothering studies of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
- Abortion belongs to Aries; marriage and gender roles to Libra; birth itself to Scorpio.
