## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **England's regions:** the North-East and North-West against the South-East, to separate class from region; East Anglia and the South-West for Fischer's frame.
- **Scotland, Highland and Lowland:** Hebridean cèilidh against Lowland Glasgow and Edinburgh; check Sabbath observance by parish.
- **Wales, north and south:** Gwynedd and Anglesey against the valleys and Cardiff; chapel against rugby club as rival centers.
- **Orkney and Shetland:** their festivals (Up Helly Aa, the Kirkwall Ba') as separate units.
- **British Caribbean communities:** carnival, sound systems and church, each with its own record.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities:** Romanichal Gypsies, Irish Travellers and Roma from Central Europe kept apart; separate fairs and gatherings from outsiders' writing about them.
- **Irish in Britain:** dance halls and county associations, by arrival generation.
- **Northern Ireland (comparison only):** a separate culture-nation; useful for contrasting Sunday observance and parading traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for England; good for Scotland and Wales through their own surveys; thinner for the islands and minority communities, where festival records and oral history stand in for surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for eisteddfod records; Scottish Gaelic for Mòd and cèilidh material; Scots for Lowland song; Old and Middle English for the early words of pleasure.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** carnival policing, Traveller fairs and festival membership disputes are live public quarrels; present each side's record, take no side. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for Great Britain and its islands.
- **Held for review:** domain-wide pointers on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
