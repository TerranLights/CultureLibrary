## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cornwall:** St Ives artists against Cornish-language revival makers (Gorsedh Kernow); check who counts as a Cornish artist.
- **Glasgow against Edinburgh:** two art schools and two art economies; compare student origins and public commissions.
- **Sheffield and the West Riding mill districts (Leeds, Bradford):** trade skill in metal and textile design; separate craft pride from factory work.
- **Welsh-language poets and English-language Welsh writers:** two literary systems with different ideas of originality.
- **Outer Hebrides:** home weaving, Gaelic song-making and their link to the croft economy.
- **British Chinese makers:** separate Hong Kong-origin families from recent arrivals and from mainland-born students.
- **Turkish and Kurdish Londoners:** music and textile making in community centers; check what records exist.
- **Deaf BSL users:** sign poetry and visual vernacular as their own art form.
