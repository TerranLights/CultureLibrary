# United Kingdom: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic; Old and Middle English; Polish and Twi for communities below. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tyneside and Northumberland:** Jarrow and the shipyards beside rural Northumberland's farm laborers; separate unemployment from poverty in work.
- **Dundee:** the jute-mill city; check women's share of mill work and compare women's and men's oral histories.
- **Hull's fishing families:** trawlermen's wives and widows as a group with its own records.
- **The South Wales valleys:** before and after pit closures; check Welsh- and English-speaking villages separately.
- **Nottingham and other estates:** residents' accounts against media and policy accounts of the same places.
- **Polish workers in the Lincolnshire Fens:** farm and food-processing work; separate seasonal from settled workers and check Polish-language community media.
- **Ghanaian care workers and nurses:** dignity at work in the care sector; separate NHS staff from agency and home-care workers.
- **Irish Travellers on council sites and roadside camps:** site conditions as a dignity question; check their own organizations' reports.
- **Older people in care homes and disabled people living independently:** separate by setting and by who makes the decisions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on care quality, poverty and disability (regulators, charities, social history); mixed on everyday courtesy; good oral history for mining, fishing and mill towns.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for valley and chapel sources; Scots for older north-east and Dundee writing; Polish and Twi for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** care-home and hospital records involve identifiable people; use published reports only. Assisted dying is a contested public debate; cite both campaigns in their own words.
- **Held for review:** respect and dignity sources from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; held aside under the standing rule.
