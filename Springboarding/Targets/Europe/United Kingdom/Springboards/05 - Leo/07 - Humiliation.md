## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Somerset and Dorset:** skimmington country; check parish and court records for ridings by period.
- **Sussex:** Lewes against other Sussex bonfire towns; who is burned in effigy and why.
- **Wrexham and the north-east Welsh border:** schooling, language shift and the Welsh Not where English and Welsh meet; compare with Gwynedd.
- **Highland and island schools:** check whether a Gaelic counterpart to the Welsh Not is documented, and where.
- **Boarding-school pupils against state-school pupils:** separate institution from class.
- **Military recruits:** training establishments by service and decade.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities:** "No Travellers" signs and refusals of service; separate Romanichal, Irish Traveller and Roma experience.
- **Polish communities after 2016:** reported hate incidents; check Home Office statistics against Polish-language community reports.
- **Irish in Britain in the 1970s and 1980s:** suspicion during the bombing campaigns and "Irish jokes", checked by generation and city.

## 4. Flags
