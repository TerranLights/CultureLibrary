## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lancashire mill towns:** Wakes Weeks and seaside excursions; check the end of the custom town by town.
- **Somerset and the Mendips:** Glastonbury, cider wassails and village fêtes; separate locals from festival visitors.
- **Edinburgh against Glasgow:** Hogmanay street parties and cup-final celebrations; compare how each city stages public joy.
- **Catholic South Uist and Barra against Presbyterian Lewis and Harris:** Sabbath observance and dance as variables, checked by parish and decade.
- **Revival districts in Wales:** chapels touched by the 1904 to 1905 revival against those that were not.
- **Ghanaian and Caribbean churches:** worship styles and celebrations, by founding body and generation.
- **Jewish communities:** Purim and Simchat Torah, with Hasidic Stamford Hill, other Ashkenazi and Sephardi congregations separated.
- **Vietnamese families:** Tết gatherings; check community associations' records.
- **Congolese communities:** music and dance nights; check church and secular events separately, in French and Lingala media.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for measured well-being and for festive history; mixed for religious joy; thin for private, everyday delight, which needs diaries and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for revival reporting; Scottish Gaelic for island song; Hebrew and Yiddish terms for Jewish festive sources; Twi, Vietnamese, French and Lingala for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drug-linked euphoria and mental-health data need care; use aggregates and published accounts.
- **Held for review:** festivals, worship and celebration sources among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; kept back by the standing rule.
