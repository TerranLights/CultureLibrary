*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lancashire mill towns** (Wakes Weeks) and the **Durham coalfield against the South-East commuter belt:** separate a one-industry town calendar from the commuter day; test by ONS region.
- **Highland and Island crofting** (Lewis, Skye) **against the Lowland Central Belt:** croft work as one of several jobs; check Crofting Commission records.
- **Gwynedd's slate villages against the south Wales Valleys:** Welsh-language and English-language working lives, quarry and pit.
- **Orkney and Shetland:** fishing, knitting and, since the 1970s, oil (Sullom Voe) as three rounds to separate.
- **East Anglia, the Wessex counties, the North Midlands and the Borders:** Fischer's *Albion's Seed* (1989) traces four American work ethics to these regions; check it from the British side (*check*).
- **Romany Gypsy, Irish Traveller and Scottish Traveller families; Showpeople:** self-employed, seasonal and traveling work; the census category "Gypsy or Irish Traveller" (2011) and "Roma" (2021) (*check*).
- **British Caribbean (Windrush generation):** separate the 1950s recruitment routes (NHS, London Transport) by period and island.
- **Haredi Jewish communities (Stamford Hill, Broughton Park in Salford):** the Sabbath as a fixed limit on the working week; compare with Reform congregations.
- **Northern Ireland,** a separate culture-nation, only as comparison: shared institutions, separate statistics (NISRA).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for English-language and labor-movement sources; mixed for Scots and Welsh; thin and mostly oral for Cornish, Angloromani and Traveller Cant.
- **Lands:** the United Kingdom's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for Great Britain and its islands.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh and Scottish Gaelic for the sound archives; Old English for the estate texts; dialect glossaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mass Observation writers' privacy terms; Gypsy and Traveller records (consult community bodies first); health records. Employment law belongs to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
