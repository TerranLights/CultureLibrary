*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cotswold and other country-house estates:** separate estate staff (gamekeepers, gardeners, house staff) from tenants; check estate papers in county record offices.
- **Wiltshire and Salisbury Plain garrison towns** (Tidworth, Bulford): military service as a family's way of life; compare with civilian neighbors.
- **Northumberland and Borders hinds and bondagers:** a tied-service system to date and map.
- **Highland gillies and stalkers on sporting estates** (Inverness-shire, the Great Glen): Gaelic-speaking service to absent owners.
- **Coastal lifeboat towns** (Cornwall, the Isles of Scilly, the Hebrides, Shetland): separate crewing by family line and decade.
- **Filipino nurses and care workers; Nigerian and Ghanaian health workers:** separate recruitment wave, grade and region of posting.
- **British Caribbean women in the early NHS:** check recruitment records and RCN oral histories by island of origin.
- **Haredi Jewish community services** (Hatzola volunteer ambulance, Stamford Hill) **against Reform congregations' volunteering:** service within a community against service outward.
- **Irish in Britain:** domestic service and nursing as routes; compare by county of origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic service, the civil service and volunteering; thinner for informal service inside minority communities.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh (Urdd and chapel records; *gwasanaeth* has a double sense to watch); Gaelic for estate and croft oral history; Old English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** child migrants and children placed in service; abuse in institutional service; honors as a political subject. The law of service contracts and conscription belongs to Libra (the letter) or Capricorn (the spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. *The Gazette*: search honours citations "for services to" with a county name (Angus, Carmarthenshire, Cumbria) and compare the categories.
2. I-CeM: domestic servants by county, 1881 and 1911.
3. Imperial War Museums: "National Service" oral histories, filtered by region of birth.
4. Read Kussmaul, then Delap, then Powell, in that order, to cover four centuries of household service.
5. Community Life Survey tables: volunteering by region and ethnic group.
