# United Kingdom: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Merthyr and the Heads of the Valleys:** men out of work after the pits; separate long-term sickness from unemployment in the statistics by decade.
- **Stoke-on-Trent and the Potteries:** craft skills made obsolete by closures; check potters' oral histories.
- **Orkney farms and the Llŷn peninsula:** practical all-round skills on small holdings; Welsh-speaking elders on Llŷn as keepers of language.
- **British Chinese families in catering:** children's help in the business (Song); compare generations and Hong Kong arrivals since 2021, whose professional skills may go unused while they requalify.
- **Iranian, Kurdish and Eritrean refugees:** separate asylum seekers barred from work from those granted status; time spent waiting as the variable.
- **Romany Gypsy and Scottish Traveller elders:** separate the elder's role as keeper of family history and Romani or Cant words, as community bodies describe it, from outsiders' accounts.
- **Disabled people:** separate by impairment and by era (Remploy factories; supported employment; the closures of 2008-13).
- **Northern Ireland,** only as comparison: its own disability and economic-inactivity statistics (NISRA) beside Great Britain's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for utility as an idea and for later-life surveys; mixed for the felt sense of being useless, which sits in oral history and memoir.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh and Gaelic proverbs and dictionaries; Old English for the Exeter Book; Cantonese for older British Chinese oral histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disabled people's worth; "burden" talk in end-of-life debates; asylum seekers' status. The work-capability assessment as written goes to Libra; the purpose behind it, to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
