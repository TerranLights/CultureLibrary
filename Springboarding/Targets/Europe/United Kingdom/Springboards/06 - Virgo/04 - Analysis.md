## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; Britain kept long statistical series and a large history-of-science literature. Mixed on everyday attitudes to analysis, which sit in surveys and media more than in archives.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for the medieval reckoning texts; Old English for Byrhtferth; Welsh for Welsh-medium school data; Scots for dictionary work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** census confidentiality (personal returns closed for 100 years); medical case records.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
