## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yorkshire Dales and Lake District wallers against Cotswold stone and thatch trades:** separate regional standards of the same craft family.
- **Gwynedd and Ceredigion strict-meter poets against free-verse writers:** check the Chair and Crown competitions over decades.
- **Inverness, Oban and South Uist pipers:** separate competition piping from dance and pub piping.
- **Unst and Fair Isle knitters:** separate making for sale from making for home.
- **Showpeople's fairground painters and gilders:** a decorative standard kept in family workshops; check their own accounts.
- **Hong Kong and mainland Chinese students:** separate domicile, subject and support use in university counseling data before any claim about pressure.
- **Haredi soferim against Reform congregations' scroll practice:** what each requires of a ritual object.
- **Boys and men:** body-image standards; separate by age group in the NHS surveys, which are often read only for girls.
