# United Kingdom: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom (post-war), region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English (parliamentary, press and playground registers); Scots (the flyting tradition); Welsh and Scottish Gaelic (bardic satire); Old English; Caribbean English Creoles for community media. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Westminster, Holyrood and the Senedd:** separate question formats and the tone the standing orders allow.
- **Shetland and Orkney local papers** (*The Shetland Times*, *The Orcadian*) **against London dailies:** criticism in print where writer and target know each other; check the share of named letters.
- **Welsh-language and English-language press in Wales:** compare what each criticizes and how sharply.
- **Black British Caribbean and African-heritage communities:** separate grime and sound-system clash traditions from church and community-press criticism.
- **Nigerian, Ghanaian and Filipino health workers:** use NHS staff-survey breakdowns on raising concerns by grade and trust.
- **Kurdish, Eritrean and Iranian refugees and Hong Kong arrivals:** separate criticism of the home regime from criticism of Britain; check fears of reprisal.
- **Teesside and Lancashire working men's clubs:** the heckle and the club comic as local critique; check club histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, press and parliament; mixed for criticism in families and at work, which sits in surveys and forums.
- **Languages needed:** Scots for flyting; Middle Welsh and Classical Gaelic for bardic satire; Caribbean Creoles for community recordings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whistleblowers' identities; defamation (the law of libel is Libra's); diaspora critics facing reprisal.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
