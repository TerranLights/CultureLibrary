*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Glasgow and West Central Scotland against Edinburgh and the Lothians:** separate the excess-mortality pattern by cause, deprivation and decade.
- **Blackpool and other seaside towns:** check health indicators against inland neighbors in Fingertips.
- **The Western Isles, Orkney and Shetland:** island care and travel to hospital; read the Highlands and Islands scheme's records alongside current data.
- **Carmarthenshire and the Welsh-speaking west:** Myddfai lore and present Welsh-language care; check language of service.
- **Romany Gypsy, Irish Traveller and Showpeople families:** separate registration with a GP, site type and travel pattern.
- **British Caribbean communities:** separate the Windrush-era health workforce from patients' experience; sickle cell as a case.
- **British Chinese patients and Chinese-medicine practitioners:** separate by language and generation when reading surveys of remedy use.
- **Haredi Jewish families in Stamford Hill:** check vaccination and maternity data by ward against the community's own health bodies' guidance.
- **Eritrean, Kurdish and Iranian refugees:** separate asylum status, GP access and trauma services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich; long series of official health data, a large history-of-medicine literature, and the NHS's own archive record. Thinner for folk healing today and for Traveller health.
- **Languages needed:** Old English and Latin for the leechbooks; Middle Welsh for Myddfai; Classical Gaelic for the medical manuscripts; Cantonese for older British Chinese patients.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** patient records; mental-hospital archives (closed periods); vaccine debates within communities. The NHS statutes as written are for Libra; what health law is for, and how far it is obeyed, for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
