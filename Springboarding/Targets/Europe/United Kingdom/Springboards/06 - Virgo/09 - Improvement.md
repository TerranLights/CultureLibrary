## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sutherland and the Isles against the Lothians and Angus:** the same improving age seen from cleared townships and from model farms; compare estate papers with Gaelic song.
- **Welsh-speaking Wales after 1847:** Llŷn and Ceredigion chapels and schools; check the response in the Welsh-language press.
- **Glasgow's overspill towns and Milton Keynes:** planned new towns; separate the first settlers' oral histories from later arrivals.
- **Sunderland and Wearside:** workplace improvement imported with a new employer; compare with older shipyard memories.
- **Scottish Travellers:** postwar settlement schemes presented as improvement (the Pitlochry huts, *check*); use Traveller-led accounts first.
- **East End Jewish immigrants of the 1880s-1910s:** the Jews' Free School and anglicization; keep separate from today's Orthodox and Haredi schooling.
- **Lithuanian and Romanian workers:** ESOL enrollment and requalification by region as the variable.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; "improvement" is a key term of British agrarian, urban and educational history, and the critique of it is well documented. Thinner for private self-improvement today.
- **Languages needed:** Scottish Gaelic for the land-agitation songs; Welsh for the 1847 response; early Middle English for *Ancrene Wisse*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Clearances and the 1847 report are live grievances; eugenics archives; Traveller settlement policy. Enclosure acts and planning statutes go to Libra for their wording and to Capricorn for their purpose.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
