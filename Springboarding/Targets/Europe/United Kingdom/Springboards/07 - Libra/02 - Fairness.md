## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North against South of England:** dialect words for claiming and truce differ by town; check Opie's maps against later surveys before drawing a line.
- **Scotland:** fixed inheritance shares, Glasgow's equal-pay fight and crofting rules; separate law, period and place.
- **Highland and island crofting townships (Lewis, Skye, Shetland):** soumings and grazings committees; compare Gaelic-speaking and Norse-heritage townships.
- **Wales:** the Fair Trade Nation campaign, and Welsh-medium schools' own rule words; check bilingual sources.
- **Lancashire and the Fens (Boston, Lincolnshire):** seasonal and gang labor; separate Polish, Romanian and British Chinese workers' records by year.
- **Quakers:** fixed prices and fair dealing in early Quaker trade; check business archives (Cadbury, Rowntree, Clarks).
- **Greek and Turkish Cypriot Londoners:** shares among siblings, dowry houses and family shops; separate island of origin and generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on pay, rationing and children's lore; mixed on everyday turn-taking, where diaries and forums must stand in for surveys.
- **Languages needed:** dialect dictionaries (the English Dialect Dictionary; the Dictionary of the Scots Language) for the claim and truce words; Gaelic for crofting minutes; Polish and Romanian for migrant-worker sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Morecambe Bay deaths and their families; live equal-pay claims; tribunal names.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
