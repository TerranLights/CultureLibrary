## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Durham, Northumberland and Cumbria coalfields:** marras and the Bond; separate pre- and post-1947 nationalization records.
- **Cornwall and the North-East Scottish and Shetland fishing towns:** share systems on boats; compare seine companies with Shetland and Moray Firth crew shares by period.
- **Scotland against England and Wales:** firms as legal persons and rights for cohabitants; compare statute before practice.
- **Welsh-speaking Wales:** chapel and civil weddings in Welsh; check registers and *Y Cymro*-type Welsh newspapers for partnership notices.
- **Jewish communities:** marriage contract, religious divorce and the 2002 Act; separate Orthodox, Masorti and Reform bodies.
- **Greek and Turkish Cypriot Londoners:** *koumbaros* and other sponsors; separate church and mosque records and generations.
- **Nigerian and Ghanaian families:** customary marriages contracted abroad and their civil recognition; check immigration and register sources.
- **British Chinese family firms and Hong Kong arrivals since 2021:** partnership in restaurants and trade; separate older Hong Kong migration from the newest.
- **Same-sex couples:** before and after 2004, 2013 and 2014; compare Scotland and England.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, household surveys and letters; thin for working partnerships outside unions and companies (crews, takeaways), where oral history is needed.
- **Languages needed:** Middle English for Chaucer and the Pastons; Welsh for chapel and press sources; Greek and Cantonese for community records; coalfield dialect glossaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent marriage and divorce records are closed; couples' counseling files are confidential; earlier same-sex relationships were criminalized, so names in old records need care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
