## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lancashire and West Yorkshire mill towns:** Wakes Weeks and Saint Monday; separate textile from mining towns and date each change.
- **Bath and Edinburgh:** planned symmetry against older crooked towns; check who paid and who was housed elsewhere.
- **Lewis, Harris and the Free Church Highlands:** Sabbath observance and Sunday ferries and flights; separate denominations and islands.
- **Wales:** chapel temperance, the 1881 Act and the later local polls on Sunday opening; compare north-west and south-east.
- **Orkney:** solstice monuments and the farming year; check Norse-heritage calendars against mainland Scots term days.
- **Peak District and Perthshire uplands:** estates, tenants and conservation bodies; separate owners' and residents' voices.
- **British Chinese and British Caribbean households:** "heatiness" and "cooling" remedies; separate generation, origin (Hong Kong, Jamaica, Trinidad) and who teaches the remedies.
- **Jewish communities:** the Sabbath and the eruv (Barnet, 2003); separate Orthodox and other bodies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for medicine, temperance, architecture and well-being statistics; thin for household folk remedies, which need oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Old English and Middle Welsh for the medical texts; Latin for medieval medicine; Gaelic for island Sabbath sources; Cantonese for home remedies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grouse-moor and raptor-persecution cases are live and legal; folk remedies may touch on health claims; Sabbath disputes are local and personal.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
