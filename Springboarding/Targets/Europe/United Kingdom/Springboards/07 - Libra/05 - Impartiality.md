## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Scotland:** its own appointing board and court system, and a refereeing dispute (the 2010 referees' strike); separate the law from sport.
- **Wales:** Welsh-language courts and S4C; check whether impartiality rules are tested in Welsh-language cases.
- **Gaelic-speaking Highlands and islands:** BBC Alba and local appointments in small communities where everyone is known; compare recusal practice.
- **Romany Gypsies and Irish Travellers:** the 1989 and 2000 rulings, and later council and policing reports; use each community's own organizations.
- **Polish, Romanian and other EU nationals:** status decisions after 2020; separate by year and region of settlement.
- **Kurdish and Turkish communities:** court and police interpreting; separate dialect (Kurmanji, Sorani, Turkish) and the community associations that mediate.
- **The City of London:** its business vote in Corporation elections and its livery companies; compare written electoral rules with the rest of the country.
- **Comparison only:** Northern Ireland, a separate culture-nation, where fair-employment monitoring laws aim at even hiring between communities.
