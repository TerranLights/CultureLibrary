*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Cornwall and South Wales:** "dreckly" and "now in a minute"; test whether these mark delay, politeness or neither, with dialect recordings.
- **Lowland Scotland and Ayrshire:** *swither* and Halloween divination; separate the poem's period from later school customs.
- **Wales and Scotland as voting nations:** referendum turnouts and margins; separate 1979 from 1997.
- **Shetland:** Up Helly Aa's debate; check the community's own minutes against national coverage.
- **Quakers:** waiting for unity; compare local and yearly meetings.
- **British Chinese families:** consulting elders and the almanac before weddings and moves; separate generation and Hong Kong or mainland origin.
- **Somali and other Muslim communities:** guidance prayer before a choice; check imams' and families' own accounts.
- **Westminster and Holyrood:** deadlock and delay in two parliaments; compare procedures for breaking ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed. Rich for votes, referendums, inquiries and default schemes; thin for private wavering, which needs diaries, Mass Observation-type replies and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for counsel words; Middle English for the debate poem; Scots for Burns; Cantonese and Arabic terms for guidance-seeking.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life and organ-donation choices; Brexit and independence remain divisive, so sources must be balanced by side.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
