## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Essex, East Anglia and Lancashire:** witch trials of 1612 and the 1640s; separate accuser, victim and judge by parish.
- **Scotland:** a larger trial record and an official apology; compare Lowland and Highland accusations.
- **Cornwall, Devon and Somerset:** ill-wishing and cunning folk; check museum holdings against court cases.
- **Lincoln, Norwich and York:** blood libel sites and their later commemorations; separate medieval texts from Victorian retellings.
- **Cardiff (Butetown) and Liverpool:** 1919 riots; use the seamen's descendants' memory projects.
- **Irish in Britain:** policing in the 1970s-1990s; separate first-generation and British-born.
- **Romany Gypsies and Irish Travellers:** press images (*My Big Fat Gypsy Wedding*, 2010); compare media monitoring by community bodies with broadcasters' replies.
- **Greek and Turkish Cypriot, and British Chinese communities:** evil-eye practice in the first two; hostility recorded during COVID-19 for the third; separate generation and source.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for witch trials, moral panics and riots; mixed for living folk practice and everyday blame, which need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for medieval chronicles; Scots and Gaelic for trial records and charms; Cornish dialect glossaries; Greek and Turkish for evil-eye practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** antisemitic and racist material must be quoted sparingly; descendants of the accused and of riot victims; hate-crime figures depend on reporting.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
