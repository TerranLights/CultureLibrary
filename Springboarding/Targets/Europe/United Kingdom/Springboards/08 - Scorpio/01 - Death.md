- **Jewish burial societies, Ashkenazi and Sephardi apart:** United Synagogue and Adath societies against the Spanish and Portuguese congregation; compare mourning timetables and cemetery records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (death studies, long statistical series, folklore collections); thinner for minority-community grief in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh, Gaelic and Scots for laments and omens; Greek, Turkish, Twi, Yoruba, Cantonese and Hebrew/Yiddish for community records of mourning.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and child deaths need care; recent bereavement interviews require ethics approval; human remains held by museums are under debate. Assisted dying as law goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
