# United Kingdom: Rebirth (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh (much revival writing is Welsh-language), Scottish Gaelic, Cornish, Scots; Old English for conversion-era texts; Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for newer congregations. **Concept in one line:** a new life that comes through an ending: conversion, recovery, redemption, renewal after disaster, cultural revival, and the dead returning (resurrection, reincarnation).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Isle of Lewis against Catholic Barra and South Uist:** sudden revival conversion against sacramental renewal in Gaelic-speaking islands; check each source's denomination.
- **South Wales against north Wales chapels:** where the 1904-05 revival ran, who led it, and in which language; check mining valleys against slate districts.
- **Cornwall's revivalists and new speakers:** language revival against Methodist revival history; separate the two meanings of "revival".
- **Pagan and Druid revival groups:** Stonehenge and Glastonbury gatherings against Welsh Gorsedd ceremony; check which group publishes what.
- **British Caribbean and West African Pentecostal congregations, separately:** baptism and testimony practice; check founding decade and parent church.
- **Polish and Lithuanian Catholic parishes:** Easter food blessings and parish life after 2004 arrivals; separate the two nationalities and the postwar exiles from later migrants.
- **Hong Kong arrivals since 2021:** starting over as the arrivals themselves describe it; separate them from earlier Hong Kong migration.
- **Coventry, Clydebank and Plymouth:** check council rebuilding records against residents' memories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for revivals and conversion writing; mixed for recovery (fellowships publish little by design); thin for recent arrivals.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh, Gaelic and Cornish for revival literature; Old English; Polish, Lithuanian and Cantonese for parish and community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recovery fellowships are anonymous; former members of high-control groups need care; reoffending and second chances as law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
