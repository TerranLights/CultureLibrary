## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Spa towns (Harrogate, Bath, Strathpeffer, Llandrindod Wells):** compare who came to be cleansed, by class and period; check visitor books.
- **Shetland and the Moray coast:** fire-festival custom against Lowland practice; check when each festival took its present form.
- **Lancashire and Yorkshire mill towns:** doorstep scouring and temperance pledges; check street and chapel sources against later memory.
- **East Anglia and the Fens:** Dowsing's parishes against neighbors; check churchwardens' accounts for what was removed and restored.
- **Sutherland and the north-west Highlands:** Clearances named from the Gaelic side (*fuadach*) against estate papers; keep the two record sets apart.
- **Liverpool:** the Militant expulsions against other cities' local parties; check which records came from whom.
- **Greek Cypriot Orthodox households and Jewish households:** Lent and Passover cleansing calendars; within Jewish practice, check Ashkenazi against Sephardi household custom.
- **Cornwall's coastal towns:** sewage campaigning; check surfing, fishing and tourism groups separately.

## 4. Flags
