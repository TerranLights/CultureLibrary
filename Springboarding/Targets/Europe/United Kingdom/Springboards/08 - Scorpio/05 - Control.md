## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lowland kirk-session parishes against Highland Free Church congregations:** discipline records before and after 1843; check denomination and period.
- **Company towns (New Lanark, Saltaire, Port Sunlight, Bournville, Barrow-in-Furness):** employer control of housing and leisure; set residents' memories against founders' writings.
- **Welsh chapel districts (Gwynedd, Ceredigion around Aberystwyth) against the industrial south:** Sunday-closing votes and chapel discipline; check which districts voted to stay closed, and when.
- **Cornish Methodist circuits:** class meetings and temperance; compare mining with fishing parishes.
- **Haredi communities of Stamford Hill and Gateshead against Reform and Liberal congregations:** community governance through each body's own publications; check who speaks for whom.
- **Former residents of long-stay hospitals and asylums:** institutional control as survivors recount it; check oral histories against hospital records.
- **Romany Gypsy and Irish Traveller residents of council sites:** site rules and wardens as a form of control; check residents' own accounts against councils' site regulations, by decade.
- **Couples across income groups:** who controls household money, from survey items rather than assumption; check by region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutional and religious control (kirk sessions, workhouses, company towns); mixed for felt control (a few panel items); thin for control inside families outside abuse surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for chapel records; Scots and older Scottish hands for kirk minutes; Gaelic for island congregations; Middle English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse-survey microdata are restricted; hospital and asylum records are closed for long periods; research in close religious communities needs their consent. Coercive control as an offense goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
