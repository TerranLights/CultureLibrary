## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hill-farming districts at livestock marts (Lairg in Sutherland, mid-Wales, the Cumbrian fells):** handshake sales and luck money; check auctioneers' records against farmers' accounts.
- **Lancashire co-operative towns (Rochdale, Oldham):** membership and dividend; check by decade.
- **Hatton Garden traders:** separate Jewish trading families from traders of other backgrounds; check bourse rules against stated practice.
- **British Caribbean savers:** who is chosen to hold a pardner pool; check by generation and church network.
- **British Chinese, by origin:** New Territories families, Hong Kong urban migrants and newer Fujianese arrivals; check each association's own records.
- **Somali households (Bristol, Cardiff's Butetown, east London):** rotating savings and money-transfer operators; check community organizations' accounts.
- **Polish and Lithuanian arrivals since 2004:** whom newcomers relied on first (parish, employer, agency); check parish newsletters.
- **The Isles of Scilly and other small island communities:** honesty-box and unlocked-door claims as testable questions, not assumptions; check by period.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (many survey series and a long co-operative and mutual-society record); mixed for informal credit; thin for trust inside newer communities in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Somali, Cantonese, Polish and Lithuanian for community savings and parish sources; Welsh and Gaelic for mart and township talk; Middle English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law (Capricorn); trusts and contracts as written law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
