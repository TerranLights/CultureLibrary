*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **North-east Scotland farm servants (Buchan and Aberdeenshire):** the Horseman's Word; check oral accounts against printed exposés and their dates.
- **Fishing ports (the Moray Firth, Fife's East Neuk, Whitby and Scarborough):** taboo words and luck at sea; compare port with port, not a single "fisher" custom.
- **Cornish coves and the Kent and Sussex coast:** smuggling lore against customs records.
- **Highland and island glens:** illicit distilling, excise records against local memory.
- **West Cumbria (Seascale, Whitehaven):** nuclear secrecy in workers' and families' oral histories.
- **Gay men's networks in London, Brighton and Blackpool:** Polari and the closet; check by decade.
- **Romany Gypsy and Irish Traveller communities:** Anglo-Romani and Gammon (Shelta) as each community chooses to publish them; never extract unpublished vocabulary.
- **Vietnamese arrivals with insecure immigration status (kept apart from Hong Kong arrivals on the BN(O) route):** keeping status hidden from employers and services; check advice charities' casework reports, by year, rather than community labels.
- **Mothers of forced adoption:** Catholic, Church of England and Salvation Army homes kept apart; check each agency's surviving records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for state secrecy, Freemasonry's public face and inquiries; thin by nature for private and family secrets, best reached through testimony and released files.
- **Languages needed:** Scots (north-east Doric), Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish, Old and Middle English; Anglo-Romani and Gammon only through community publications.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** in-group languages and lodge rites stay with their owners; adoption records are closed to outsiders; living people in cover-up inquiries need care. Official secrets and privacy law go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
