## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Highland estates against community-owned land (Assynt, Gigha, Eigg):** landlord power and buyouts; check estate papers against trust minutes.
- **Durham and Northumberland coalfield:** union power before and after 1984; check lodge banners and records.
- **Welsh-speaking professional circles against the valleys:** who uses "crachach", of whom, and when; check press and speech.
- **The City and Westminster against Manchester, Leeds and Newcastle:** regional power and its programs; check by government period.
- **Cornwall and Dartmoor Duchy tenants:** landlord power seen by tenants; check tenancy records.
- **Shetland and Orkney:** island councils and oil-era funds; compare the two island groups.
- **Plymouth and Devonport:** a navy and dockyard town's dependence on one employer; check by decade.
- **Nigerian British hometown associations:** chieftaincy titles held by UK residents; check how associations record conferment, by ethnic group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (elite studies, land records, union archives, class survey); mixed for hidden power, which shows mainly in journalism; thin for power inside newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Gaelic and Welsh (estate and chapel records), Old and Middle English, Yoruba for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living persons and privacy; Royal Archives access is limited. Authority, legitimacy and the constitution go to Capricorn (spirit) or Libra (letter).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
