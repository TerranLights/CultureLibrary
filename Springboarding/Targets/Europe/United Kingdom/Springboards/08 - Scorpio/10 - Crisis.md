## 3. Who to compare inside the nation

- **Isle of Lewis:** the *Iolaire* in Gaelic song and local press; check when and how commemoration began.
- **East coast flood communities (Canvey Island, Lincolnshire, Norfolk Broads):** 1953 memory community by community; check warnings and evacuation records.
- **Exmoor and Lynmouth:** the 1952 flood; record later rumors as claims and check them.
- **Hull and Grimsby:** trawler losses and the Cod Wars; keep the two ports apart.
- **North Kensington:** survivors' and bereaved groups' own accounts after Grenfell; check across the many communities affected.
- **Vietnamese refugees resettled from 1979:** reception, dispersal and regrouping; check by arrival cohort.
- **British Chinese and the Morecambe Bay cockling deaths (2004):** check community organizations' accounts against inquest and press.
- **The Yemeni community of South Shields:** seafaring hardship and the 1930 disturbances; check Tyne and Wear Archives.
