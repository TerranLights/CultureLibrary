## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- North East England (Lindisfarne, Durham) against the South West (Glastonbury, Cornish Methodism): compare saints' cults and chapel density by period.
- Hebrides: Free Church and Free Presbyterian Lewis and Harris against Catholic Barra and South Uist; split by island, never "the Highlands" as one unit.
- Wales north and south: Welsh-speaking chapel districts (Gwynedd, Ceredigion) against the industrial valleys, by language of worship.
- Lowland Scotland: parishes, with Glasgow's Catholic and Orange records apart.
- Orkney and Shetland: Norse-era Christianity (St Magnus Cathedral) and later Presbyterian and Methodist records.
- British Caribbean communities: Black-majority Pentecostal churches, Seventh-day Adventists and Rastafari, split by island of origin and generation.
- Jewish communities: Orthodox, Masorti, Reform, Liberal and Haredi (Stamford Hill, Gateshead), via the Institute for Jewish Policy Research's breakdowns *check*.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities: Pentecostal missions (Light and Life *check*) and Catholic Irish Travellers, separated since the census merges them.
- Northern Ireland, comparison only: its own culture-nation; useful as a contrast on attendance and public religion.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Great Britain (censuses, long-run surveys); thin for the islands and small communities, where samples cannot be split.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for chapel and revival papers; Scottish Gaelic for Hebridean worship; Scots for kirk session minutes; Latin for medieval church records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** west-central Scotland's sectarian history; safeguarding inquiries; minority sacred practice, sourced from each community's own publications. The established churches' legal position is law: Libra (letter), Capricorn (spirit).
- **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; these sheets are written for the core lands of Great Britain.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only, set aside for the domain survey).
