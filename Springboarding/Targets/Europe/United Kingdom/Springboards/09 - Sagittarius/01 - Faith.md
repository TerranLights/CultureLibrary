*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Scots, Cornish; Old and Middle English; Polish, Arabic and Cantonese for newer congregations. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order); by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gaelic-language congregations on Skye and Lewis against English-language Highland parishes: separate faith by language of worship, using psalm-precenting recordings.
- Lancashire recusant Catholic families against Irish-origin Catholics on Merseyside and the Clyde: inherited against migrant Catholicism, by parish records.
- Cornish and Durham mining Methodism: chapel membership before and after mine and pit closures.
- South Wales Pentecostals against North Wales Welsh-language chapels: compare healing ministries with hymn-centered worship.
- Black-majority churches: Caribbean-founded (1950s-1960s) against Nigerian and Ghanaian-founded (from the 1980s), by founding decade.
- Polish Catholic missions after 2004 against English parishes they share buildings with.
- Turkish, Kurdish (including Alevi cemevi), Bosnian and Moroccan communities: separate mosque and cemevi networks by origin and language.
- British Chinese: Hong Kong arrivals since 2021 against older Cantonese-speaking communities, by church, temple or none.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the historic churches; mixed for newer independent churches, many outside any central count; thin for healing and prayer practice at home.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh to read hymnody and chapel papers; Gaelic for psalmody; Polish and Arabic for newer congregations' bulletins.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** healing ministries and safeguarding; ex-members' accounts; vandalism of places of worship.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category for faith practice.
