# United Kingdom: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Old and Middle English; Vietnamese and Turkish for two refugee and migrant communities. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Former coalfields (South Wales valleys, Durham, South Yorkshire, Nottinghamshire, Fife, Ayrshire): compare "worthwhile" scores by local authority, sorted by the date each pit closed.
- Hebridean and Raasay communities: land and clearance memory as possible sources of meaning, to check township by township; separate Gaelic-language sources from English ones.
- Orkney and Shetland: compare island authorities with the mainland, checking sample sizes first.
- Windrush-generation elders: life review in Caribbean oral histories, separated by island and decade of arrival.
- Vietnamese refugees of 1979-1980s dispersal: purpose after exile, by arrival cohort and place of settlement.
- Turkish Cypriot and mainland Turkish communities in north London: separate by origin and generation.
- Humanist celebrants and the families they serve: how a non-religious life is summed up at a funeral, against church funerals in the same area.
- Rural parishes against new towns (Milton Keynes, Stevenage): place-based meaning, by settlement age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for measured well-being and for literature; thinner for everyday talk of meaning, which needs oral archives and diaries.
- **Languages needed:** Gaelic and Welsh for the poetry above; Vietnamese and Turkish for community voices; Old English for the elegies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and drug-death statistics (ONS; National Records of Scotland) are needed but must be handled with care; refugee testimony has access conditions.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, held as a category for later work on purpose and life review.

## 5. First queries
1. Download ONS "worthwhile" scores by local authority, 2011 to the latest year; flag the former coalfield authorities.
2. Search British Library Sounds for "National Life Stories" collections on steel, coal and fishing.
3. Read the Exeter Book elegies in a parallel-text edition; list the key words for transience.
4. Search the Desert Island Discs archive for guests from Wales, Scotland's islands and the North East.
5. Ask the UK Men's Sheds Association for member surveys on purpose and isolation.
