*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Anglo-Scottish borders (Northumberland, Cumbria, Scottish Borders, Dumfries and Galloway): test Fischer's "natural liberty" claim against reiver history and present surveys, never assume it.
- East Anglia: the Puritan source region in Fischer's account; check against parish and chapel records.
- Crofting counties (Highland, Western Isles, Orkney, Shetland): freedom bound to land tenure; Orkney and Shetland's older udal land is a legal matter (Libra).
- Peak District, Manchester and Sheffield: access and rambling movements, by club and decade.
- British Caribbean communities: emancipation memory, Rastafari talk of "Babylon" and liberation (British reggae such as Steel Pulse's *Handsworth Revolution*, 1978 *check*), and the Windrush status scandal (2018) as a loss of freedom to stay.
- Kurdish refugees and Hong Kong arrivals: freedom as a reason for coming; separate by route and status.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities: freedom to travel against settled-site policy, in the communities' own publications.
- Rebecca-country south-west Wales against industrial Merthyr: rural and industrial freedom claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in texts and archives; mixed for measurement (the European Values Study freedom-equality item *check*; free-speech polls, often commissioned by interested parties).
- **Languages needed:** Middle Scots and Latin for the medieval texts; Gaelic for Napier evidence; Welsh for the radical press; Kurmanji and Cantonese for community voices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Scottish independence and slavery-memory disputes (statues) are partisan; Kurdish and Hong Kong politics carry risk for informants. Civil liberties in law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only; held from the freedom and emancipation pointers).
