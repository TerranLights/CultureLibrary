## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lancashire and northern recusant families against Covenanter south-west Scotland: martyr memory, separated by denomination and century.
- Gwynedd and Llŷn against the anglicized south-east of Wales: language activism, by local records of protests and court appearances.
- Conscientious objectors: Quakers, Christadelphians and socialist objectors, split by motive in the Pearce Register.
- Yorkshire and Nottinghamshire coalfields in 1984-1985: striking and working areas, through oral histories, without ranking either.
- Highland Free Church congregations: Disruption memory, by presbytery.
- Bristol's and Brixton's British Caribbean communities: campaigns by decade and organization.
- Stroud and Totnes environmental groups against neighboring farming parishes: what each side's own publications say.
