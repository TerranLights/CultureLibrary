## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Cornwall and Devon: hosts and visitors, second homes, by parish; the "emmet" and "grockle" words as data on relations.
- Lancashire and Yorkshire mill towns against southern seaside towns: wakes weeks against resort economies.
- Highlands and Islands: emigration by estate and decade; Orkney's company recruits as a separate stream.
- West Wales droving districts (Ceredigion, Carmarthenshire): routes, fairs and the drovers' banks (Llandovery *check*).
- British Caribbean families: the Windrush generation against grandchildren's journeys back.
- Hong Kong and New Territories Chinese: lineage migration against recent BN(O) arrivals.
- Romany Gypsies, Irish Travellers, Scottish Travellers and Showmen: four traveling communities, never merged.
- Irish in Britain: seasonal harvest workers in Scotland and navvies, by period.
- Polish arrivals after 2004: circular migration and return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, emigration records and travel writing; thinner for Travellers' own records outside community publications.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for drovers' and pilgrims' sources; Gaelic for emigration songs; Cantonese for lineage records; Latin for Gerald.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Traveller site disputes (law: Libra); asylum crossings; family-history material in Traveller communities, used with consent. The Patagonian Welsh and the Cornish mining diaspora are comparisons only.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category; outside the migration pointers here).
