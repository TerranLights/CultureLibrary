## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Rural Highland, Cumbrian and Cornish honesty-box districts against cities: test whether honor-system selling is a rural pattern, by locality.
- Scottish parishes against English church courts: two discipline systems for lying and slander, compared over the same centuries.
- Quaker firm towns (York, Birmingham's Bournville, Bristol): company records on pricing and trading rules.
- British Caribbean pardner groups: how trust is kept and broken, from members' own accounts.
- Somali households using xawaalad: transfer rules and agents' reputations, through community organizations.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities: separate press stereotypes from the communities' own accounts of trade and dealing.
- Welsh-speaking communities: uses of "chwarae teg" in sport and public argument.
- Public servants against the private sector: Veracity Index and Standards Committee data by occupation.

## 4. Flags
