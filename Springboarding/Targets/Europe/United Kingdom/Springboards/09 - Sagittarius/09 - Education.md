## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- England, Scotland and Wales: three school and university systems, with their own curricula and fees.
- Gwynedd and Ceredigion against Monmouthshire and Flintshire: Welsh-medium and English-medium schooling by county.
- The Western Isles against Glasgow and Edinburgh: Gaelic-medium enrollment by setting.
- North East England and coastal towns against London and the South East: participation by POLAR area.
- Cornwall: access to degrees since the Penryn campus (2004 *check*).
- British Caribbean families: supplementary schools by decade and borough.
- British Chinese and Polish Saturday schools: what they teach and who runs them.
- Gypsy, Roma and Traveller pupils: the school census categories and Traveller Education Services' records.
- Haredi Jewish schooling against mainstream Jewish schools: separate by institution, handled with care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (censuses, HESA, PISA, inquiries, institutional histories); thinner for supplementary schools and community-run learning.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for the 1847 reaction and circulating-school records; Gaelic for Gaelic-medium sources; Latin and Old English for early sources; Cantonese and Polish for Saturday schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pupils' records; unregistered schools; the "Welsh Not" and colonial-era schooling as living memory. Education Acts and fee rules are law: Libra (letter), Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category; outside the supplementary-school list).
