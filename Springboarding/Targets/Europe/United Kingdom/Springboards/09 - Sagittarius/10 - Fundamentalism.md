# United Kingdom: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh; Middle English; Hebrew and Yiddish, Arabic and Turkish for the self-descriptions of strict communities. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular and political certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lewis and Harris congregations: Free Church, Free Church (Continuing) and Free Presbyterian, by congregation and Gaelic or English worship.
- Lanarkshire and the Clyde: Orange and Catholic sectarianism treated as a separate variable from scriptural strictness.
- Devon and Plymouth: Brethren history and today's Plymouth Brethren Christian Church schools.
- Wales: the Evangelical Movement of Wales against the Union of Welsh Independents, on scripture and creation.
- Haredi communities of Stamford Hill, Gateshead and Salford: described in their own terms; the outside label is contested.
- Somali, Arab, Turkish, Kurdish and Bosnian Muslim congregations: separate scriptural strictness from political extremism, and never apply either label wholesale.
- Jehovah's Witnesses and Latter-day Saints: discipline and leaving, from members' and leavers' sources side by side.
- Black-majority Pentecostal churches: views on scripture from survey data, not reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich history and sociology; thin current measurement outside evangelical surveys; small groups resist outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Gaelic for Hebridean synod and sermon material; Welsh for EMW publications; Hebrew, Yiddish, Arabic and Turkish for strict communities' self-descriptions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the word itself is a charge; extremism policy and leavers' safety; abuse inquiries. For blasphemy and extremism statutes see Libra; for what such law is for, Capricorn.
