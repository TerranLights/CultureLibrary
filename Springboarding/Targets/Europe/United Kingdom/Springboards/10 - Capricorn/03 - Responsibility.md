## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **South Wales valleys:** Aberfan and pit closures; check local oral histories against official reports for how answerability was assigned.
- **North-East Scotland:** offshore oil communities; compare Piper Alpha memory in Aberdeen with Shetland's local histories of the Sullom Voe terminal.
- **North Kensington and West London:** separate Grenfell survivors' groups, tenants' associations and council records.
- **British Caribbean:** separate Windrush-era arrivals from their children; check the Review's case studies and community organizations' own testimony.
- **Irish in Britain:** check by decade (the 1970s against now) using Irish community centers' archives and Hillyard's sources.
- **British Chinese and Vietnamese families:** separate Hong Kong-origin, mainland-origin and Vietnamese refugee-origin households before comparing children's roles.
- **Scotland against England and Wales:** the age of criminal responsibility and access rights; check public-attitude surveys before and after each change.
- **Former child migrants (sent abroad until the 1970s):** the 2010 prime-ministerial apology; check survivor-group statements.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on public accountability (inquiries, Hansard, resignations); mixed on household responsibility; thin on minority families as they describe themselves.
- **Languages needed:** English for inquiry and parliamentary records; Welsh for Aberfan-era community sources; Old English and Anglo-Norman Latin for frankpledge records; Scots for *wyte* in older Scottish writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster inquiries involve bereaved families; Grenfell and Windrush are ongoing grievances; terrorism-era material on the Irish in Britain is politically raw. Children's interviews need consent.
- **Held for review:** who answers for whom in South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
