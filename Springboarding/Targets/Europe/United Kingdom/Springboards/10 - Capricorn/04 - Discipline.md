## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Public schools against comprehensives:** separate boarding, day independent and state schools; check inspection reports and school histories by decade.
- **Lowland kirk sessions against Highland parishes:** check whether session records survive equally, and when discipline lapsed in each.
- **Wales north and south:** chapel temperance and the *seiat* in rural Gwynedd against the industrial south; compare denominational records.
- **Cornwall and the North:** Methodist strongholds; check class-meeting records by circuit.
- **Garrison towns (Colchester, Catterick):** check military-prison and training records, kept apart from civilian norms.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller pupils:** check exclusion data alongside community organizations' accounts of schooling.
- **Haredi communities in Stamford Hill and Gateshead:** check the communities' own educational bodies and inspection disputes, without merging the two.
- **Polish Saturday schools and Chinese supplementary schools:** check each school network's own records of study discipline, by generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for institutions (schools, prisons, church courts); mixed for home discipline; thin for self-discipline outside campaigns and fitness.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Adomnán and early monastic rules; Scots for kirk session minutes; Welsh for chapel and temperance records; Hebrew and Yiddish terms for Haredi schooling material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in schools, borstals and care homes (inquiry testimony; survivors first); school data on named children is closed; deaths in military training are legally contested.
- **Held for review:** school and home discipline in South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
