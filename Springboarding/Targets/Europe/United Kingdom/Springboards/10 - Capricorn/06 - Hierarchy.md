## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Landed estates against industrial towns:** separate county gentry from manufacturing elites; check estate papers against town directories by period.
- **Highland clan system and its end:** compare chief, tacksman and cottar before and after the Clearances, using parish accounts from both Statistical Accounts.
- **North-East Scotland farm hierarchy:** check feeing-market and bothy records by county (Aberdeenshire, Angus).
- **Cornwall:** mine ranks against landed rank; check whether Methodism changed standing within mining villages.
- **Shetland and Orkney:** merchant-laird and tenant relations; compare the two island groups rather than merging them.
- **The armed forces:** Highland regiments and the Guards against other units; check officer-recruitment records by school and region.
- **Jewish communities:** compare the United Synagogue's structure with Haredi communities' own authorities; check by congregation.
- **British Caribbean:** check community studies of how island of origin and shade were discussed, from the community's own writing, by generation.
- **Migrant domestic workers:** check support organizations' casework reports for the employer-worker relation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich on class (official schemes, cohort studies, literature); mixed on rank inside minority communities; thin for Gaelic and Shetland material outside archives.
- **Languages needed:** medieval Latin for Domesday; Middle Welsh for the bardic grades; Gaelic and Scots for parish, estate and song sources; Cornish mining English for underground ranks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class slurs and colorism must be described, never used as labels; heraldic and peerage claims can be disputed; domestic workers' casework is confidential.
- **Held for review:** material on rank within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
