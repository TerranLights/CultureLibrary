## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guild towns (London, York, Chester, Coventry):** check admission routes to the Freedom across centuries, town by town.
- **Welsh-speaking eisteddfod culture, north and south:** check local *eisteddfodau* and chapel competitions as well as the National.
- **Gaelic communities:** compare Mòd entrants from Lewis, Skye and Glasgow's Gaelic population; check the medal records.
- **Cornwall:** Gorsedh bards against Cornwall's wider honors; check the stated grounds for each admission.
- **The armed forces and the civil service:** compare entry routes before and after the 1850s-1870s reforms; check Highland and Welsh regiments' officer lists.
- **Dissenting, Catholic and Jewish communities:** check alumni and admissions records after 1871 for each, separately.
- **Irish in Britain:** check nursing and construction workforce histories for routes of recognition and advancement.
- **Overseas-trained professionals:** separate Hong Kong, West African and Central European arrivals; check recognition and licensing outcomes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on institutional reforms and admissions; rich on hiring experiments; mixed on everyday talk of connections; thin on Cornish and Gaelic measures outside festival records.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for adjudications and Eisteddfod reporting; Gaelic for Mòd records; Cornish for Gorsedh citations; Old English for *Geþyncðo*; the guild records' Latin and early English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hiring-discrimination findings are politically contested; honors scandals involve living people; licensing data on individuals is confidential.
- **Held for review:** credential and hiring studies centered on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
