# United Kingdom: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Shetland and Orkney:** festival committees and island councils; check how each manages its own street customs.
- **Glasgow and the West of Scotland:** football-related disorder; check by period and by club, using the parliament's debates on the 2012 football Act and its repeal.
- **Rural West Wales:** *ceffyl pren* and Rebecca traditions; check where community shaming survived into the 20th century.
- **Sussex and the South-East:** bonfire societies as organized disorder; compare society rules across towns.
- **British Caribbean London:** Brixton, Notting Hill and Tottenham; separate carnival organizers, residents' groups and inquiry evidence by decade.
- **Jewish and Irish East End:** Cable Street memory; check each community's own archives.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities and Showmen:** check fairground and encampment records separately; Showmen's yards and fairs (Nottingham Goose Fair, Hull Fair) are a distinct case.
- **New Towns against old cities:** Cumbernauld, East Kilbride and Milton Keynes residents' surveys against older city neighborhoods.
- **Vietnamese refugee households:** check accounts of the late-1970s dispersal across Britain and later regrouping.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on riots, inquiries, planning and festivals; mixed on household order; thin on Gaelic and Shetland material outside festival records.
- **Languages needed:** Early Modern English for the homilies; Welsh for *ceffyl pren* and Rebecca sources; Scots for everyday terms of mess and tidying; Gaelic for island order; Vietnamese for refugee community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** riots involve living participants and ongoing grievances; Glasgow football disorder overlaps with sectarian tension; eviction casework is confidential.
- **Held for review:** public-order and street-festival material on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
