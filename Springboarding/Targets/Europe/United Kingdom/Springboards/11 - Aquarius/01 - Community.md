# United Kingdom: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its dialects, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Middle English for parish records. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, an interest, a cause), recognize one another as members and owe one another something; and the felt quality of that bond.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Former coalfield villages of Durham, South Yorkshire and the South Wales valleys:** separate generations before and after the 1984-85 strike and the closures; use ward-level data.
- **Highland and island townships in community ownership against those on private estates:** ownership form as the variable.
- **Isles of Scilly and Cornish harbor villages:** second-home share and seasonal residents as variables, read with the local outsider words.
- **Butetown, Cardiff:** separate seafaring families by origin and arrival period instead of treating the docks as one community.
- **Jewish communities of north Manchester, north London, Gateshead and Glasgow's southside:** separate congregation types using the community's own surveys.
- **Romany Gypsy and Irish Traveller families:** site-based and housed families as separate cases.
- **Tower-block estates in London and Glasgow:** tenants' associations, with the estate's build date and tenure mix as variables.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for English parishes, mining towns and faith communities; good for Scottish community ownership; thin for Cornish-language sources on community.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for the *papurau bro* and the Welsh serial; Gaelic for township and place-bond terms; dialect glossaries for the incomer vocabulary in Cumbria, the Dales and Cornwall.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aberfan and other disaster testimony involves bereaved families; Traveller site counts sit close to enforcement; the statutes behind land buyouts go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their place and faith communities, as a category).
