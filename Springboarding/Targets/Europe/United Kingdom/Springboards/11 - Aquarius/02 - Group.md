# United Kingdom: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its slang and dialects, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Old English for early group vocabulary. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ba' and Shrovetide sides (Kirkwall, Ashbourne, Alnwick):** separate the written rules of who plays for which side from how players describe it.
- **Border surname families of Cumbria, Northumberland and the Scottish Borders:** separate reiver-era records from the modern Common Riding associations that invoke them.
- **Showmen and fairground families:** guild membership against descent as the entry rule.
- **Kurdish, Eritrean and Somali community associations:** separate welfare bodies from homeland political factions, using charity-register records.
- **Football supporters in Glasgow, Liverpool and Manchester:** separate supporters' trusts from informal firms, and the 1970s-80s from the all-seater era.
- **Leave and Remain voters in England, Scotland and Wales:** test whether referendum identities hold the same way in each nation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for guilds, friendly societies, unions and fandom; mixed for clans and surname groups, where romantic retellings crowd out records; thin and police-framed for street gangs.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for the *seiat* records and the 1847 controversy; Gaelic for kin-group words in older song and poetry; Old English for war-band vocabulary; slang dictionaries for informer and strikebreaker words by region.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sectarian football and marching-band groups in west-central Scotland; police gang databases have drawn complaints of racial bias; lodge membership lists are private. The right of association as law goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their own associations and census categories, as a category).
