# United Kingdom: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English (with class and regional usage), Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Middle English for the 1381 material. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coastal and post-industrial towns against London and the South East:** separate area-level mobility from household class, using the Commission's local data.
- **Lowland Scotland and the Gaelic Highlands:** separate the Burns and "lad o' pairts" tradition from the crofters' land-equality songs; date each.
- **Welsh-speaking and English-speaking Wales:** check whether *gwerin* and *crachach* talk tracks language, region or period.
- **British Caribbean campaigners in Bristol, Brixton and Handsworth:** separate campaigns by decade and by target (transport, housing, schools, policing).
- **British Chinese families of Liverpool's seafaring community:** the 1940s deportations of Chinese seamen and descendants' accounts as one case.
- **Roma from central and eastern Europe, Romany Gypsies and Irish Travellers:** three groups with different histories; keep data separate.
- **Disabled people and Deaf BSL users:** separate social-model claims from Deaf claims as a language minority.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for class, gender and race in England; good for disability; mixed for Scotland and Wales outside the official reports; thin for Cornish.
- **Languages needed:** Middle English for the 1381 chronicles; Gaelic for land-agitation song; Welsh for the 19th-century press; Scots for Burns and proverbs of equal worth.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** class slurs and racial terms appear in sources and stay described, never repeated; equality statutes and court cases go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their equality campaigns and labor disputes, as a category).
