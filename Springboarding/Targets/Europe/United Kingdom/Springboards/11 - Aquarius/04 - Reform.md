# United Kingdom: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Middle English for Lollard texts. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lancashire mill towns (Preston, Rochdale, Oldham):** separate employer-led reform from workers' own temperance, co-operative and factory campaigns.
- **The Welsh valleys and Newport:** compare how towns along the 1839 march route mark the Chartists today.
- **Crofting districts of Skye, Lewis and Sutherland:** separate Gaelic testimony from landlords' evidence in the Napier volumes.
- **British Caribbean parents in London, Birmingham and Bristol:** compare supplementary-school projects by city and decade.
- **Salvation Army corps and hostel residents:** keep the reformers' records apart from the reformed people's testimony.
- **Cornish Methodist circuits:** separate mining districts from fishing ports in the records of personal reform.
- **Peak District access campaigners:** the 1932 Kinder Scout trespass, through the Working Class Movement Library (Salford), as reform by direct action.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for 19th-century English reform movements; good for Scottish church discipline and Highland land reform; thinner for Cornish and for the voices of those who were reformed.
- **Languages needed:** Middle English for Lollard tracts; Gaelic for crofters' evidence and Land League song; Welsh for the circulating schools and the *diwygiad* sense of revival; Scots for session minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent kirk-session and hostel records name individuals and may be closed; Reform Acts and other statutes go to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their reform campaigns, as a category).
