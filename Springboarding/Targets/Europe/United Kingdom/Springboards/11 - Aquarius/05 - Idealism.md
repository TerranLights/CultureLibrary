# United Kingdom: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish; Middle English and early modern Latin for the oldest ideal-society texts. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality; it includes visions of a better society (utopias).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Findhorn and the Forres area on the Moray Firth:** separate members' accounts from neighboring townspeople's; date by decade.
- **Glastonbury and Totnes:** separate spiritual-seeker idealism from Transition-town practicality; check overlap in the same households.
- **One Planet Development smallholders in Pembrokeshire:** idealism tested by a planning policy; use application and monitoring records.
- **Garden-city residents (Letchworth, Welwyn) against New Town residents (Stevenage, Cumbernauld, Cwmbran):** planned ideal against lived town, by arrival cohort.
- **Whiteway Colony (Gloucestershire) and the Bruderhof (Darvell, East Sussex):** secular Tolstoyan and religious community of goods as two kinds of idealism.
- **Hong Kong arrivals since 2021:** separate democracy activists from families who moved for other reasons.
- **Woodcraft Folk groups against Scout and Guide groups:** compare the promises and pledges each asks of children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for utopian writing, communities and movements; mixed for counting idealists (no single survey); Gaelic and Welsh rich in poetry and youth-movement texts, thin in data.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and early modern English for More; Middle English for Langland; Gaelic for MacLean (originals, not only translations); Welsh for the Urdd messages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** living members of communes and religious communities; Kibbo Kift and some youth movements drew later political suspicion that sources may overstate or understate.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their own idealistic movements and service traditions, as a category).
