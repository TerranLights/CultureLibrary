# United Kingdom: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English and its regional address words, Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh, Cornish, Angloromani; Old English, Middle English and medieval Latin for early sources; British Sign Language for the Deaf community. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pit and shipyard towns of the North East and Cumbria:** the *marra* bond at work; separate generations before and after closures.
- **South Wales valleys:** friendship through rugby clubs and male voice choirs; check how far it reaches women and newcomers.
- **Deaf BSL users:** compare cities whose Deaf clubs closed with those whose clubs survive.
- **Polish communities:** separate postwar ex-servicemen's circles (POSK, Hammersmith) from post-2004 arrivals.
- **Gay and lesbian networks in Brighton and Manchester's Canal Street:** compare generations before and after the 1980s epidemic.
- **Older men in rural Wales and the Highlands against city men:** isolation and shed membership as variables.
- **Romany and Traveller families:** test whether "friend" outside kin is the right frame before counting friendships.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for history and loneliness research; good cohort data; thin for friendship in Cornish and Gaelic sources beyond the kin-and-friend words.
- **Languages needed:** Latin for Aelred; Middle English for the romance; Gaelic and Scots, where "friend" can mean kinsman, a sense a researcher must not miss; BSL for Deaf club oral histories; Angloromani for the community's own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** oral histories of gay men from before 1967 and of the 1980s epidemic; Gypsy Lore Society material carries the period's outsider prejudice.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their friendship and visiting customs, as a category).
