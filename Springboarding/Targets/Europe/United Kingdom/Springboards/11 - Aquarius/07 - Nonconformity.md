# United Kingdom: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English (including religious-history vocabulary and subcultural slang), Scots, Scottish Gaelic, Welsh; Polari for one community's coded speech. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Welsh chapel congregations against the Church in Wales:** chapel closures per county as the variable over the 20th century.
- **Lancashire Catholics:** separate old recusant families from 19th-century Irish Catholic arrivals.
- **Free Church and Free Presbyterian congregations of Lewis and Harris:** dissent from the national church that became the local norm; trace disputes over Sunday observance as dated events.
- **Huddersfield objectors against Clydeside objectors:** compare religious and socialist grounds for refusal by town.
- **New Travellers and Romany Gypsies:** a chosen mobile life against an inherited one; never merge the data.
- **Rastafari in Handsworth and Brixton:** separate the religious houses from young people who took up the style.
- **Whitby residents and visiting goths:** the town's records of welcome and complaint across three decades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for religious dissent and for youth subcultures; mixed for objectors outside England; thin for Cornish-language sources on dissent (Cornish Methodism is mostly recorded in English).
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for chapel records and the chapel-church divide; Gaelic for Free Church preaching and psalm-singing communities; Scots for Covenanting and later dissent; Polari for its own texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gay men's records from before 1967 and objectors' tribunal files name individuals; Travellers and ravers were targets of the 1994 public-order legislation, which goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (youth styles and dissenting movements among them, as a category).

## 5. First queries
1. Pull the 1851 religious census summary tables and rank registration districts by Nonconformist share.
2. Search Coflein for chapels in two contrasting counties (say Ceredigion and Monmouthshire) and record founding and closure dates.
3. Query Pearce's register for objectors by home town and stated grounds.
4. Read the CCCS archive's catalog for working papers on skinheads, Rastafari and punk.
5. Search local Whitby and Yorkshire press for coverage of the goth weekend by year.
