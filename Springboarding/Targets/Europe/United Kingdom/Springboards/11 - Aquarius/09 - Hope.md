*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ukrainian guests and their hosts:** separate guests' plans from hosts' hopes; date each source by year since 2022.
- **Hong Kong arrivals since 2021:** compare stated hopes on arrival with later community surveys.
- **Eritrean and Sudanese asylum seekers awaiting decisions:** hope as long waiting; use the Home Office's processing-time statistics beside the claimants' own accounts.
- **Welsh speakers and Gaelic speakers:** compare language-revival hopes set by government with families' choices of Welsh-medium and Gaelic-medium schooling, by council area.
- **Young people in former coalfield towns against university cities:** the Youth Index by region and background.
- **Pilgrims at Walsingham (Norfolk) and St Winefride's Well (Holywell, Flintshire):** healing petitions in England and north Wales; separate Catholic and Anglican pilgrims.
- **Orkney and Shetland:** hopes tied to oil (Flotta, Sullom Voe) and to renewables; date each wave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for well-being and youth surveys; rich for religious and millennial hope; mixed for asylum seekers, whose waiting is recorded more by officials than in their own voices.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for the cross and poem; Welsh for language-revival debate and song; Gaelic for island and Highland speech about the future; Ukrainian for guests' own media and associations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Samaritans' and crisis data on suicide; asylum case details; healing petitions at wells are private acts, to be described and never collected.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their pilgrimages and hopes for the next generation, as a category).
