# United Kingdom: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic; Old English and Early Modern English for martyr literature; Polish for the wartime and newer Polish community; German for Kindertransport family papers. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coalfields of south Wales, Yorkshire and the North East:** compare strike-year sacrifice by pit and union lodge, not by region alone.
- **Fishing ports (Hull, Grimsby, Aberdeen, Lerwick):** losses at sea by decade; check port memorials against registers of lost vessels.
- **Cornish and Northumbrian lifeboat stations:** compare crew-loss records and how each village keeps its memorials.
- **South-west Scotland:** Covenanter memory; separate the Presbyterian denominations that tend it.
- **Polish community:** separate wartime veterans and their families from arrivals after 2004.
- **Jewish refugees of 1938-39:** testimony from children and later generations, read separately.
- **British Caribbean wartime volunteers:** RAF and munitions-worker records and family testimony; check how memorials name them.
- **Young and older carers:** Census age bands; compare carers under 25 with carers over 65.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; war and memorial records are deep, caregiving data are current and official. Thin for sacrifice inside families outside surveys.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh and Gaelic for chapel and memorial wording; Polish; German for refugee family letters; Early Modern English.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** grief of named families; strike memory is still divisive in mining towns; force-feeding records are distressing. Organ-donation consent law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** wartime service records and memorials of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the War Memorials Register for "self-sacrifice" and "gave his life" and sort by county.
2. Read the Postman's Park tablets and the Royal Humane Society's award lists for the same decades.
3. Pull unpaid-care counts by local authority from the 2021 and 2022 censuses.
4. Search the Swansea coalfield catalog and the Hull History Centre for women's accounts of 1984-85 and 1968.
5. Search The Acts and Monuments Online for a few local martyrs, then match them to their towns' memorials.
