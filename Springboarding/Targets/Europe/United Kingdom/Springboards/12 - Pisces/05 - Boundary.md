# United Kingdom: Boundary (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** United Kingdom, region Europe. **Language(s) for searching:** English, Scots, Welsh, Scottish Gaelic, Orkney and Shetland dialect; Old English for charter bounds; Hebrew for eruv terms; Anglo-Romani for Traveller usage; Cantonese for Chinatown community material. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Scottish Borders and Cumbria:** Common Ridings and border towns; compare the two sides of the line by town.
- **The Welsh March (Powys, Monmouthshire, Shropshire, Herefordshire):** language and identity by parish along Offa's Dyke.
- **Orkney and Shetland:** incomer and native categories; check island surveys and local press, not mainland impressions.
- **Orthodox and Haredi Jewish districts (Golders Green, Stamford Hill, Salford's Broughton Park):** compare eruv debates council by council.
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities:** separate English Romany Gypsies, Irish Travellers, Scottish Travellers and Roma from Central Europe; each has its own rules and history.
- **British Chinese:** Manchester, Liverpool (Europe's oldest Chinatown *check*) and London; compare by migration period.
- **Peak District and Pennine towns:** the access movement against landowners' records.
- **Suburban England:** hedge and fence disputes by local authority complaint data, not reputation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for physical and historical boundaries; mixed for personal boundaries, where British-specific surveys are few.
- **Languages needed:** Old English for charters; Scots and Welsh; Hebrew and Yiddish for eruv rulings; Anglo-Romani with community consent; Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Romani and Traveller purity rules are community knowledge; use what communities publish. Eruv disputes have drawn antisemitic comment; read with care. Trespass, access and hedge law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** neighborhood-boundary studies centered on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, set aside under the standing rule.
