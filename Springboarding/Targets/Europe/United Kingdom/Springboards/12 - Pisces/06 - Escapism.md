## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Glasgow, Dundee and Edinburgh:** compare measured drug and alcohol death rates by council area and year, not reputation.
- **North East and North West England against the South-East:** alcohol-specific deaths by local authority; check deprivation as the separating variable.
- **Lancashire and Yorkshire mill towns:** Wakes Weeks by town; compare the timing and the resorts each town chose.
- **Seaside towns (Blackpool, Skegness, Margate, Rhyl):** separate the town as escape for visitors from the town as home for residents.
- **Welsh chapel districts and valleys clubs:** temperance strongholds against working men's clubs; compare by parish and decade.
- **British Caribbean:** blues parties, sound systems and carnival; separate first-generation and British-born accounts.
- **Irish in Britain:** dance halls and pubs by city; compare London with Birmingham and Coventry, and the 1950s with the 1980s.
- **Shetland:** Up Helly Aa (Lerwick) as a winter festival of release; check organizers' own histories.

## 4. Flags
