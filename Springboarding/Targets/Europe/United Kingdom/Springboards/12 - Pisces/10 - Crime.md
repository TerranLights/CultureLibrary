## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Glasgow and west-central Scotland:** compare the gang novels and press panics of the 1930s with the violence-reduction era, using measured homicide data.
- **Liverpool:** the Hillsborough Independent Panel (2012) and the long fight over blame; compare press and families' accounts.
- **The Borders and Cumbria:** reiver memory in festivals and museums (Tullie House, Carlisle *check*) against the records.
- **Cornwall and Sussex coasts:** smuggling and wrecking legends against customs and court records.
- **Irish in Britain:** 1970s-90s policing; compare community testimony with official records by city.
- **British Caribbean:** "mugging" panics and stop-and-search disputes; read Hall and Macpherson beside community archives (Black Cultural Archives).
- **Gypsy, Roma and Traveller communities:** media framing of Travellers as wrongdoers; check the Traveller Movement's reports against press samples.
- **Spiritualist mediums:** Helen Duncan's 1944 case (Portsmouth) as a window on where the hidden meets wrongdoing.
