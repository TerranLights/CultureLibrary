# Araucanía-Patagonia: The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; working frame: Wallmapu and Patagonia on both sides of the Andes. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun with its regional speech and the Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh (Chubut); German (Llanquihue, Valdivia); Croatian (Magallanes); Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Gününa küne (revitalization); Afrikaans (Chubut). **Concept in one line:** the self, the body, appearance, beginnings and initiative; the domain question is what a self is here and how it asserts itself. Folkway: naming ways (sport ways runner-up).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche and Williche**: compare naming ceremonies and lineage names, since each territory may keep or have lost them differently.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro (Puelmapu) against the Araucanía**: check how two states' registries shaped names.
- **Aónikenk families of Santa Cruz and Chubut**: check which person words and names survive through revitalization.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Puerto Natales, Yagán of Villa Ukika, Selk'nam descendants of Tolhuin**: check how self-names are rebuilt where outsiders wrote the record.
- **Chilote families**: compare island nicknames and godparent naming with the mainland.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower Chubut valley and the Andes (Trevelin, Esquel)**: check bardic names and bilingual given names.
- **German-descended (Llanquihue, Valdivia), Croatian-descended (Magallanes) and Boer-descended (Sarmiento) families**: check how long first names stayed in the old tongue.
- **Chilean migrants in Argentine Patagonia; Italian, Spanish, Basque and Lebanese settler families**: check self-presentation in a second country.
- **Oil workers of Comodoro Rivadavia and sheep-station hands**: check the working body in self-description.
