# Araucanía-Patagonia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia, both slopes of the Andes). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of southern Chile, Chiloé and Argentine Patagonia; Mapudungun (Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche speech; Williche *tsesungun*); Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche and Wenteche communities**: check who may speak at a *trawün* and in what order, since rules may differ by territory and by age.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: compare their council petitions with those of the Araucanía, for a separate history of claiming.
- **Mapuche women and young people**: check who speaks for a household when elders and *werken* are present.
- **Oil-town residents of Neuquén and Comodoro Rivadavia**: compare blockade-era public speaking with quieter channels.
- **Sheep-station and meatpacking workers of Santa Cruz and Magallanes**: check how complaints moved from bunkhouse to union.
- **Welsh-descended chapel members**: check whether chapel meetings trained public speaking and how that carried into civic life.
- **German-descended and Croatian-descended families**: compare club minutes for how members raised disagreements.
- **Chilean migrants in Argentine Patagonia**: check how newcomers spoke up at work across a border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, protests and Mapuche claims; mixed for everyday complaining and haggling; thin for the Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam, where search should begin with community organizations' own statements.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for oratory terms; Welsh for chapel minutes and petitions in *Y Drafod*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land recoveries and police operations in the Araucanía are disputed; the 1920-22 strikes and their repression are told in rival accounts. Present each as an account.
- **Held for review:** Chile's and Argentina's statutes on public order and the right to protest, set aside for Libra and Capricorn.
