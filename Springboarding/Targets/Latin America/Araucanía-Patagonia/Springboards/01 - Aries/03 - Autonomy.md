*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia across the Andes). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of southern Chile and Argentine Patagonia (with Chiloé); Mapudungun and the Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** deciding for oneself: freedom from being controlled, for persons and for collectives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche coastal communities against Pewenche highland ones**: compare who decides over sea and over forest.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check how their 1793 treaty tradition shapes claims unlike those further north.
- **Mapuche communities of Neuquén and Chubut**: check how co-management and provincial politics give a different route from Chile's.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán communities**: check how coastal-use requests express collective say over waters.
- **Young people in Temuco, Osorno, Neuquén and Comodoro Rivadavia**: compare age of leaving home and family say over career and partner.
- **Welsh-descended chapel members and German-descended Lutherans**: compare congregational self-rule with Catholic parishes of Chiloé.
- **Residents of company towns** (oil camps of Comodoro Rivadavia; estancias of Santa Cruz and Magallanes): check how far an employer ruled daily life.
- **Croatian-descended and Boer-descended families**: check how clubs and churches ran their own affairs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective autonomy for the Mapuche and the Welsh colony; mixed on personal autonomy (youth surveys by region exist in Chile, fewer in Argentina); thin for Boer-descended families, where church records in Sarmiento are the likeliest start.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for political vocabulary; Welsh for the colony's council papers; German for congregational minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** autonomy claims, land recoveries and state responses are live disputes; give each side's account as an account, with no group cast as one voice.
- **Held for review:** ILO Convention 169 and the texts of the coastal-space law and provincial constitutions, as law on self-rule, set aside for Libra and Capricorn.
