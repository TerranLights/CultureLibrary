# Araucanía-Patagonia: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia on both Andean slopes). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun with Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche and Williche smallholders**: compare who starts a venture and who is expected to wait for kin consent.
- **Chilote island households**: check how the *minga* host takes the lead and what that obliges.
- **Aysén *pobladores* and Magallanes estancia workers**: compare settler-founded holdings with company-run stations.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower Chubut valley and of Trevelin**: check whether cooperative or private ventures came first.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue and Valdivia**: check founding records of mills, breweries and fire companies.
- **Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas and Porvenir; Lebanese and Spanish trading families**: compare shopkeeping starts across groups.
- **Italian fruit growers of the Alto Valle and Chilean migrant workers there**: check who moves from laborer to owner, and how.
- **Oil workers and Boer-descended families of Comodoro Rivadavia and Sarmiento**: check how company towns left room for private starts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on settler ventures and state-backed micro-enterprise; mixed on everyday initiative at work and at home; thin for Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam enterprises, where community associations in Puerto Natales, Puerto Williams and Tolhuin are the place to ask.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for cooperative ledgers; German for colonist letters; Italian for Alto Valle founders' papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "pioneer" narratives of Llanquihue, Aysén and Santa Cruz overlay displacement of Native peoples; present settler initiative and Indigenous loss as separate accounts.
- **Held for review:** regional jokes and sayings that rank groups as hard-working or idle, set aside under the no-stereotypes rule; cooperative law in both countries, set aside for Libra.
