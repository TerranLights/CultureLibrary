# Araucanía-Patagonia: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia either side of the cordillera). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and the Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche, Wenteche and Lafkenche communities**: check how anger is handled between neighbors and when it becomes a sorcery accusation.
- **Williche and Chilote families**: compare the island idiom of *brujos* and *mal tirado* with the mainland's.
- **Mapuche young people in Temuco and Neuquén**: compare the anger of hip-hop and protest with elders' *ül* and *nütram*.
- **Selk'nam descendants and estancia-founder families of Magallanes**: check how each reacts to films, monuments and street names.
- **Sheep-station and oil workers**: check how anger in shearing sheds and oil camps is handled and who cools it.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check chapel discipline and letters for how temper was judged.
- **German-descended and Croatian-descended families**: check memoirs for anger at wartime blacklists and suspicion.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check whether family accounts remember the South African war with grievance, and how that is spoken of.
- **Women in southern cities**: compare violence-survey and hotline data with men's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on collective anger and protest; mixed on everyday temper; thin on anger words in Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien, where the dictionaries are the only start.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-century Spanish for *Cautiverio feliz*; Mapudungun for *ül*; Welsh for colonists' letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can still harm living people; the Selk'nam killings, the 1920-22 strikes and the 2019 unrest are told in rival accounts; domestic-violence data needs care.
- **Held for review:** Chile's antiterrorism statute as applied in Mapuche cases, and both countries' domestic-violence statutes, set aside for Libra and Capricorn.
