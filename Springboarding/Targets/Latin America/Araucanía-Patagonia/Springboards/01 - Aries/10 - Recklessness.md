## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche, Lafkenche and Wenteche communities**: check how the *ngen* and place rules frame rash acts in mountains, lakes and sea.
- **Chilote fishers, divers and *loberos'* descendants**: compare sea lore with accident records.
- **Young men in Temuco, Neuquén and Comodoro Rivadavia**: compare drink-driving and road deaths across the border.
- **Oil and gas workers of Vaca Muerta and the San Jorge Gulf**: check how boom work frames risk and money.
- **Climbers, guides and rescuers of El Chaltén, Bariloche and Torres del Paine**: check where locals draw the line against visitors' daring.
- **Croatian-descended gold-rush families of Porvenir**: check family stories of the island gamble.
- **Welsh-descended families of Chubut**: check chapel teaching on drink and rashness against local outlaw lore.
- **Selk'nam descendants**: check how Selk'nam families themselves remember the gold-rush years.
- **Aysén and Magallanes ranch families**: compare weather and river-crossing risk with townspeople's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road, sea and drug statistics; mixed for folklore; thin for Kawésqar and Yagán views on risk at sea, where community associations in Puerto Edén and Puerto Williams are the place to ask.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for *ngen* terms; Welsh for temperance writing; German for Popper's papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the gold rush and its adventurers are tied to the Selk'nam killings; tell admiration and harm as separate accounts; *animitas* mark real deaths.
- **Held for review:** zero-tolerance drink-driving statutes in Chile and Argentine provinces, set aside for Libra.
