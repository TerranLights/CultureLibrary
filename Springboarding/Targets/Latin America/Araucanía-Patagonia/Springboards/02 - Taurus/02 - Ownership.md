# Araucanía-Patagonia: Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu, with Chilean and Argentine Patagonia). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Selk'nam, Kawésqar, Yagán, Aonekko ʼaʼien and Gününa küne in revitalization. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, keep others from, and give away or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche communities**: check how claims to beach, sea and shellfish grounds are made and named.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Neuquén**: check claims to *pewen* forest, where gathering rights and title may differ.
- **Wenteche and Nagche families on *hijuelas***: compare a divided plot's owner with the old community's claim.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check how early titles are used in today's claims.
- **Mapuche *fiscaleros* and reserve families of Neuquén, Río Negro and Chubut**: check life on land held by revocable permit.
- **Aónikenk of Camusu Aike and steppe families of Chubut**: check land returned or held as reserve; records are thin.
- **Selk'nam descendants, Kawésqar and Yagán**: check claims to coast and sea against estancias and parks.
- **Welsh-, German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families (Chubut valley, Llanquihue, Porvenir, Sarmiento)**: compare how settler lots were granted, kept, sold or lost.
- **Estancia companies, *puesteros*, foreign and conservation owners; Zanón's workers and owners**: compare who holds title with who works or lives there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in land records, registries and press; mixed for Indigenous accounts of tenure, which live in oral history and claims files; thin for Aónikenk and Gününa küne tenure.
- **Languages needed:** notarial Spanish; Mapudungun for oral accounts of *lof* land; Welsh for colony land minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** live land conflicts and court cases; the 'Pacification of the Araucanía' and the 'Conquest of the Desert' as competing accounts of how title began; ancestral remains.
- **Held for review:** the letter of the land statutes (Chile's Indigenous Law and its land fund, the 1979 division decree, the Water Code; Argentina's Indigenous-territory emergency law and rural-land law) goes to Libra, and what those laws are for to Capricorn; this sheet keeps only the registers and records they produced.
