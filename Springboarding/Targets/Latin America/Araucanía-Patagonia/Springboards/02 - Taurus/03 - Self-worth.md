# Araucanía-Patagonia: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and both Patagonias). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche of Temuco against rural *lof* families**: check whether worth is spoken of through the lineage or through school and work.
- **Lafkenche and Pewenche communities**: compare honor tied to ceremony roles with honor tied to land defense.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check worth regained through language and poetry where speech was lost.
- **Mapuche and Aónikenk families of Comodoro, Neuquén and Río Gallegos**: check self-description after generations of city life.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: check how people speak of exhibition, captivity and today's recognition.
- **Gününa küne descendants of Río Negro's Línea Sur**: check the little in print; ask community associations.
- **Chilote families at home and as migrants east**: compare standing on the islands with standing in Argentine oil towns.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check honors, chapel office and the bardic chair as grants of worth.
- **German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: check whether old-country origin was carried as standing or hidden.
- **Oil workers, shearers and *puesteros***: check worth tied to work, and what happens after layoffs or old age.
- **Evangelical congregations in the south**: check testimony as a road back to worth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: survey data are good at national and regional level; literary and testimonial sources are rich for Mapuche and Welsh voices, thin for Boer-descended families and Gününa küne.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for poetry and testimony; Italian for Molina's first edition; Welsh for Gorsedd records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human-zoo history and the Selk'nam killings (contested accounts, descendants alive); mental-health and suicide data; insults used as search terms only.
- **Held for review:** the anti-discrimination statutes behind the survey rows (Chile's 2012 law; Argentina's 1988 law) are matters for Libra.
