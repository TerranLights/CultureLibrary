# Araucanía-Patagonia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia across the Andes). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche and Williche**: check whether the four ideals of the person are named alike in each territory.
- **Mapuche of Puelmapu**: compare values taught in Argentine intercultural schools with those in Chile.
- **Mapuche Catholic, Evangelical and traditional families**: check which feasts, drinks and gifts each keeps or drops.
- **Aónikenk, Gününa küne, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: check values stated by the communities; most print is outsiders', so ask first.
- **Chilote families**: check vows, chapel duties and *minga* duties ranked against wage work.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare chapel, language and eisteddfod as the values each generation names.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue and Valdivia**: check school creeds against what families say at home.
- **Croatian-descended families of Magallanes; Boer-descended families of Chubut**: check parish and club papers for the values they put in writing.
- **Aysén *colonos* and newcomers**: compare how each side weighed dams, jobs and landscape.
- **Oil and mine towns (Comodoro, Río Turbio)**: check values named in union and company papers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: national values surveys exist, but southern samples are small; Mapuche and Welsh writing on values is rich; thin for Boer-descended families and the steppe peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for *ngülam* and school texts; Welsh for chapel and Coleg Camwy papers; German for Anwandter-era sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *machi* and *ngillatun* teaching may not be for outsiders; Ceferino and mission history are read differently by Church and communities.
- **Held for review:** *az mapu* as a code of right conduct shades into customary law: what the code says goes to Libra, what it is for to Capricorn; it stays here as a search term only.
