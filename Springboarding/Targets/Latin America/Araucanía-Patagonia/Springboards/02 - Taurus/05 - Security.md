# Araucanía-Patagonia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia west and east of the cordillera). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche, Lafkenche and Wenteche families**: compare food stores (pine nuts, shellfish, grain) as each one's safety margin.
- **Mapuche *crianceros* of Neuquén and the Línea Sur**: check how snow and ash losses are met by kin, state or none.
- **Williche fishers and Chilote shellfish gatherers**: check security tied to the sea and to salmon jobs.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Yagán of Puerto Williams**: check how isolated places secure supplies; sources are few, so ask the communities.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families of the steppe**: check what is known of food and shelter security; little is in print.
- **Welsh-descended families of the valley**: check how floods and drought were met through chapel and kin.
- **German-descended farmers of Llanquihue and Osorno**: check cooperatives and credit as their safety net.
- **Croatian-, Spanish- and Italian-descended families of Magallanes and Chubut**: compare mutual-aid membership across groups.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check how the first, hard years are remembered; records are thin.
- **Miners of Río Turbio, oil workers of Comodoro and Cerro Sombrero, salmon workers of Los Lagos**: compare security given by employer, union and state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for crime, pension and disaster data; mixed for food security (national samples); thin for the canoe and steppe peoples.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for INTA and press; Croatian and Italian for mutual-aid papers; Mapudungun for gathering and storage words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land-conflict violence, arson and policing in the Araucanía (contested accounts on all sides); disaster deaths.
- **Held for review:** the emergency decrees and anti-terror prosecutions in the Araucanía came up as part of the security record and are left for Libra (their text) and Capricorn (their purpose), with no pointer here.
