# Araucanía-Patagonia: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu and Patagonia on both sides of the range). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Purén and Lumaco**: check how the old resistance is told as pride, as warning, or both.
- **Lafkenche of Mehuín and Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: compare a fishers' holdout with land-claim holdouts inland.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Neuquén**: check holding to forest and summer grounds against outside plans.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check holding to old titles and councils.
- **Aónikenk, Gününa küne and Selk'nam descendants**: check persistence against claims that they no longer exist.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén**: check holding to a place and to sea routes; ask the community.
- **Chilote families**: check how the royalist past and later refusals (to move, to sell) are told.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check holding to chapel, Sabbath and language.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue**: check which customs and words were kept against change, and which let go.
- **Croatian-descended families of Magallanes; Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check holding to faith and language across generations.
- **Magallanes townspeople against the central state**: compare protest refusals over decades.
- **Estancia owners and public walkers**: check holding to closed gates and to rights of way.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich press and studies for land and environmental conflicts; folklore holds sayings; thin for steppe and canoe peoples outside revitalization projects.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and Mapudungun for conflict sources; Welsh for the Sabbath dispute; Aonekko ʼaʼien and Selk'nam through teachers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land conflicts, hunger strikes and live cases; whether a refusal is called resistance or obstruction depends on the source, so give both.
- **Held for review:** Mapuche prisoners' hunger strikes and the trials behind them were weighed and kept off this sheet; they turn on court records, which Libra and Capricorn take up.
