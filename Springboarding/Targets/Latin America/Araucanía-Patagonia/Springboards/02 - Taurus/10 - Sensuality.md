# Araucanía-Patagonia: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (Wallmapu, the lakes and islands, the steppe and Tierra del Fuego). **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Wenteche and Nagche**: compare feast foods (sea, *pewen*, grain) and the music at each.
- **Williche of Chiloé and Osorno**: check where Chilote and Williche pleasures of table and dance meet.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: check hot springs, *chivito* and summer-ground feasts as Puelmapu pleasures.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne descendants**: check song and dance remembered or revived; sources are few.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: check which songs, foods and fires are shared openly, and which not.
- **Chilote families**: compare island waltzes and *curanto* with what migrants brought to Argentine towns.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check singing, tea and chapel as pleasures, and what chapel teaching said of drink and dance.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue and Valdivia; Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas**: compare festivals, baking and music clubs.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check food and song kept from South Africa; little is written.
- **Sheep-station and oil-camp workers**: check pleasures of men far from home: the *asado*, mate, music.
- **Evangelical congregations**: check which sensory pleasures are dropped and which (song) are raised up.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, music and festival sources; mixed for touch and comfort, which surveys seldom ask about; thin for canoe and steppe peoples outside outsiders' recordings.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for song texts; Welsh for hymn and chapel records; Selk'nam through Chapman's notes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Selk'nam and Mapuche ceremonial song may be restricted; *ngillatun* food and music are not for outside recording.
- **Held for review:** erotic island lore and the touch of *machi* healing were weighed and passed over, the first to Scorpio's sexuality, the second to the communities' own consent; nothing fell under the standing rules.
