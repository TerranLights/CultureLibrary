# Araucanía-Patagonia: Communication (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America (the Mapuche lands and Patagonia on both Andean slopes). **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish (Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun with Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh, German, Croatian, Afrikaans; the Fuegian and steppe languages in revitalization; two sign languages. **Concept in one line:** the exchange of meaning between people, by speech, writing, gesture, silence and media, and the ideas held about what good communication is and what it is for.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche and Williche speakers**: check whether *pentukun* runs the same length and order in each territory.
- **Mapuche of Puelmapu (Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut)**: compare the place of Mapudungun in meetings where Spanish dominates.
- **Aónikenk, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam families**: check what is spoken at home during revitalization, and which phrases are taught first.
- **Chilote islanders**: check how radio notices and boat days shape who hears news and when.
- **Welsh-descended speakers of the valley and the Andes**: compare Welsh, Spanish and switching between them at chapel and at the tea table.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue and Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas and Porvenir**: check which settings kept the old language longest (church, club, kitchen).
- **Boer-descended families**: check whether Afrikaans survives past the grandparents; the record may be thin.
- **Deaf people in provincial towns**: compare access to sign-language interpreting in the Araucanía and in Tierra del Fuego.
- **Oil-field crews and sheep-station hands**: check radio and shift-change talk as registers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Mapuche verbal art and Chilean and Argentine Spanish pragmatics at national level; mixed for southern regional speech; thin for Gününa küne, Selk'nam and Boer-descended talk.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun genre terms in Golluscio's and Díaz Meza's texts; 16th-c. Spanish for Valdivia's letters; Chakavian Croatian for family letters in Magallanes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *ül* and parliament speech may carry lineage claims not meant for outside publication; recordings of elders need community consent.
- **Held for review:** the laws recognizing LSCh and LSA, and rules on interpreters in public offices, came up while scouting the Deaf pointers; Libra takes the wording, Capricorn its purpose.
