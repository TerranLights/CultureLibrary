# Araucanía-Patagonia: Information (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the frame is Wallmapu with Chilean and Argentine Patagonia. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (south Chilean, Chilote, Argentine Patagonian); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán, Kawésqar, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** what is known or reported about the world, and how it is gathered, passed on, trusted, withheld and used: news, facts, data, records.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche communities in the Araucanía and in Neuquén**: check who in a *lof* is asked first for knowledge, and how that sits beside radio and phone news.
- **Williche communities of the Osorno coast and Chiloé**: check which councils publish their own records.
- **Yagán, Kawésqar and Selk'nam descendants**: check which archives, museums and images each community asks to control.
- **Aónikenk families of Santa Cruz**: check where their history is held (museums, family papers) and whether they hold copies.
- **Chilote fishing families**: compare official sea and weather bulletins with what is learned from other boats.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare the colony's own press with Argentine papers on the same events.
- **German-descended and Croatian-descended families**: check which community papers survive and who read them.
- **Oil and mining towns (Comodoro Rivadavia, Añelo, Río Turbio)**: check company bulletins against independent news.
- **Chilean migrants in Argentina**: check which country's news they follow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys and digitized press; mixed for local radio archives; thin for Fuegian and steppe peoples' own records, which sit mostly in outsiders' archives.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for *Y Brut*; German and Croatian for community papers; Latin and Spanish for the *cartas anuas*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Selk'nam and Yagán ceremonial photographs; contested accounts of the Maldonado and Huracán cases; *lof* knowledge that is not for outside publication.
- **Held for review:** set aside here are the transparency and access-to-information statutes of both states and the court files on the Huracán case: the written law of who may see records goes to Libra, and its purpose to Capricorn.
