# Araucanía-Patagonia: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; working frame Wallmapu plus Patagonia on the Chilean and Argentine sides. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean south, Chiloé and Argentine Patagonia; Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** how people come to know and to do: in the family, by watching and pitching in, through play, in early school, at work and across a life (schooling as an institution sits under Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche and Lafkenche families**: check how teaching follows the year's work (*piñoneo*, shore gathering) and when children join it.
- **Nagche, Wenteche and Williche families**: compare what elders teach through *ngülam* with what the same child hears at school.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: compare the provincial program with Chile's *educadores tradicionales*.
- **Selk'nam, Yagán and Kawésqar descendants**: check how mission-school memories shape present teaching of language and crafts.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check where learning happens in revitalization (workshops, schools, family).
- **Chilote families**: check the boat, the *fogón* and the chapel as places children learn.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare learners (*dysgwyr*) with home speakers in the valley and the Andes.
- **German- and Croatian-descended families**: check the trades and school associations that carried learning between generations.
- **Sheep-station and oil-field families**: compare learning a trade on the job with boarding-school schooling.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for intercultural education policy and Mapuche educational research; mixed for rural boarding schools; thin for Fuegian children's own accounts, which come mainly from later descendants.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun teaching terms; Welsh for adult-class materials; Italian and Spanish for Salesian sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mission-school histories are painful and contested; what elders teach in *ngülam* may be family knowledge.
- **Held for review:** the certification rules deciding who may teach as an *educador tradicional* or *kimeltuchefe* are written law: Libra for the text, Capricorn for its intent.
