# Araucanía-Patagonia: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the frame is Wallmapu and Patagonia across both Andean slopes. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish (Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun, *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Gününa küne, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; and, more broadly, the go-between: brokers, interpreters, intercessors.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche and Williche communities**: check who is sent as *werken* and whether the *lonko* speaks directly in talks.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: compare go-betweens accepted in provincial talks with those accepted in Chile.
- **Aónikenk, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam families**: check which outsiders (missionaries, state officials, researchers) acted as go-betweens, and how descendants remember it.
- **Gününa küne families**: check whether any go-between role survives in memory; sources may be very few.
- **Chilote communities**: compare the *fiscal* with the parish priest and the municipality.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare chapel elders' role with Argentine courts.
- **German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: check club, church and consular figures who stood between the community and the state.
- **Oil, salmon and sheep-station workers**: check who brokers between crews and companies (union delegates, priests, officials).
- **Catholic and Evangelical communities**: compare saints and pastors as intercessors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for colonial go-betweens and church mediation; moderate for intercultural health; thin for Fuegian and steppe peoples' own mediators.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for parliament records; Mapudungun ritual speech; Welsh for minute books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Villa Mascardi, hunger strikes and land conflicts are contested and ongoing; *machi* ritual roles may be closed to outsiders.
- **Held for review:** the family-mediation statutes of both states and the courts' rules for settlements under Indigenous custom were found but not listed; their wording goes to Libra and their aims to Capricorn.
