# Araucanía-Patagonia: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; working frame: Wallmapu and the small towns, islands and estancias of Patagonia on both sides of the Andes. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean south, Chiloé and Argentine Patagonia; Mapudungun and Williche speech; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien, Gününa küne. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche and Williche communities**: check where talk about absent people happens (*kawiñ*, *trawün*, sports days) and when it turns to accusation.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Bariloche**: compare how online rumor about Mapuche claims circulates in Argentine local media.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam families**: check how outsiders' hearsay shaped what was written about them, and how descendants answer it.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check whether any record shows talk inside the community; sources are likely scarce.
- **Chilote islanders**: compare island talk with the talk of Chilote migrants in Argentine towns.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check where teasing stays within bounds and where it becomes a quarrel.
- **German- and Croatian-descended families**: check how club and church circles handle a member's scandal.
- **Boer-descended families**: check whether letters record reputation within the small settlement.
- **Oil, salmon and sheep-station workers**: compare camp and bunkhouse talk with the talk of a town.
- **Students and young people in Valdivia, Temuco and Comodoro Rivadavia**: check group chats as places of rumor and warning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: moderate for literature, press and school surveys; thin for everyday gossip itself, which is best reached by fieldwork in small towns; thin for Fuegian and steppe peoples' own talk.
- **Languages needed:** Chilote Spanish for Cavada; Welsh for festival satire; Mapudungun terms in Bacigalupo and Guevara.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can harm living people; the 2020 clashes and harassment cases name real persons; small-town groups are semi-private.
- **Held for review:** defamation and slander provisions in both penal codes, and rules on online harassment, lie behind several pointers; a sheet for Libra (the wording) and Capricorn (its purpose), not this one.
