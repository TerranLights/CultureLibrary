# Araucanía-Patagonia: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche country of both slopes, the southern lakes and archipelagos, the steppe and the Fuegian islands. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Chilean, Chilote and Argentine Patagonian Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien as communities share them. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche coastal families and Pewenche highland families**: check whether "home" names a house, a *lof* or a stretch of coast or forest in each.
- **Mapuche families on the Argentine side**: check how families who came from the west name their *tuwün* now.
- **Williche of the Osorno coast and Chiloé**: compare home as island or coast with home as a recovered land title.
- **Yagán, Kawésqar and Selk'nam families**: check which places each community calls home after removals, and who speaks for that.
- **Chilote families on the islands and in Argentine towns**: check where a family that works away says home is.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare *hiraeth* for Wales with attachment to the valley itself.
- **German- and Croatian-descended families**: check when *Heimat* or *zavičaj* shifted from Europe to the south.
- **Newcomers in *campamentos* and *barrios populares***: check how first-generation households describe a home without title.
- **Sheep-station and oil workers**: compare company housing with the house each calls home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics and disaster coverage; mixed for felt home in settler groups; thin for Selk'nam and Aónikenk home places in written form.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for *tuwün* writing; *tsesungun* and Welsh for poetry; Dalmatian Croatian for club records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** home as territory runs straight into the land conflicts and Mapuche claims; any map of home places drawn by a community is theirs to share or not.
- **Held for review:** what was weighed here was eviction and land-title law behind the *toma* and *recuperación* rows; that part of the record of losing a home is Libra's text and Capricorn's purpose.
