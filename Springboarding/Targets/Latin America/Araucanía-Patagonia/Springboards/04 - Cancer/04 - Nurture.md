# Araucanía-Patagonia: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu on both sides of the cordillera, the Chilean south from the Araucanía to Magallanes, and Argentine Patagonia to Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both countries; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán, Kawésqar, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien and Gününa küne materials for children. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche and Lafkenche households**: check which relatives give *ngülam* and when, highland against coast.
- **Mapuche families in Neuquén schools and in Araucanía schools**: compare two states' programs as they reach the same kind of child.
- **Williche families of Osorno and Chiloé**: check whether grandparents still raise grandchildren while parents work away.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check what upbringing appears in recorded accounts; material is thin, so start with family memory.
- **Yagán and Kawésqar families**: compare language-nest teaching with what families pass on at home.
- **Selk'nam descendants**: check how upbringing is spoken of in families who now take up the Selk'nam name.
- **Chilote families**: compare children's work in *minga* and on boats with schooling.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check which generation stopped singing Welsh to babies.
- **German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: compare upbringing in home language and church with upbringing through Spanish school.
- **Oil-town and sheep-station families**: check who minds children when fathers work rotations or live on the station.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for Chilean early-childhood programs; moderate for Mapuche education writing; thin for Fuegian and steppe child-rearing.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for *ngülam* and *ül*; Welsh for chapel verse; Croatian and German for settler papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recordings of children and of family songs need consent; boarding schools and mission upbringing touch removals.
- **Held for review:** here the record of nurture meets child-welfare and schooling statutes; none was listed, since their text goes to Libra and their aim to Capricorn.
