# Araucanía-Patagonia: Self-expression (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche country of both Andean slopes, the lakes and Chiloé, and the Patagonian provinces and regions to Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Kawésqar and Yagán in community media. **Concept in one line:** showing one's inner self (thoughts, feelings, tastes, beliefs, identity) outwardly, in speech, appearance, art and action.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche in Temuco and in rural *lof***: compare what can be said in Mapudungun at home with what is said in Spanish in town.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Chubut**: check whether flags, dress and language are displayed differently under the Argentine provinces.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check how writers and *lonko* speak for the community.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne descendants**: check where, if anywhere, public self-naming happens.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: compare community media with how outsiders still describe them.
- **Chilote families**: compare speech at a *minga* or chapel feast with speech on the mainland.
- **Welsh-, German- and Croatian-descended families**: check which language carries feeling, and which is for public use.
- **LGBTQ people in oil towns and in the lake districts**: compare Pride visibility in Comodoro Rivadavia with Puerto Varas.
- **Evangelical congregations**: check testimony and *culto* as a place where feeling is spoken aloud.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for Mapuche literature, flags and political speech; thin for everyday reserve and for steppe peoples' voices.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun oratory terms; Croatian, including older Dalmatian forms, for immigrant press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** displaying the *Wenufoye* or Mapuche dress can bring reprisal at school or work; Pride participants in small towns may not want to be named.
- **Held for review:** kept off the sheet: newspaper columns that call southerners "closed" or "reserved" as a people; they profile a group and could be used later only as evidence of the label itself.
