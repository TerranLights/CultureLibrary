# Araucanía-Patagonia: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, the lake country and Chiloé, Aysén and Magallanes, and Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche communities near Purén and Lumaco**: check how memories of 1598 are told locally.
- **Pewenche and Lafkenche communities**: compare whether pride is phrased through land, sea or *pewen*.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Chubut**: check whether pride slogans match the Chilean side's.
- **Aónikenk families of Santa Cruz**: check how they answer settler monuments.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: compare public statements about statues and anniversaries.
- **Chilote families**: compare island-born with second-generation Chilotes in Comodoro and Río Gallegos.
- **Welsh-, German- and Croatian-descended families**: check how each marks its founders' arrival.
- **Oil and sheep-station workers**: compare pride in the job before and after layoffs.
- **Boer-, Italian-, Basque- and Lebanese-descended families; Chilean migrants in Argentina**: check which pride each keeps in public.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for monuments, anniversaries and the Mapuche movement; mixed for trade pride; thin for Boer and Aónikenk families.
- **Languages needed:** colonial verse Spanish for *Purén indómito*; Welsh for the 2015 programs; Afrikaans for Boer papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** settler monuments and anniversaries sit on contested ground; present each side's account as an account.
- **Held for review:** statutes on the use of national and provincial flags and anthems were the one legal layer met here; their wording goes to Libra, their aims to Capricorn.
