# Araucanía-Patagonia: Recognition (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu across the Andes, the southern Chilean regions from the Araucanía to Magallanes, and the five Argentine Patagonian provinces. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Selk'nam, Yagán and Kawésqar in community statements. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche communities of the Araucanía**: compare how they answered the 2017 apology with how they answered the 2020 prize.
- **Mapuche of Río Negro and Neuquén**: check how they frame Roca's statue and the honors given to the generals of 1879.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check which of their elders the state or the province has honored.
- **Aónikenk families**: compare their place in Welsh-colony memory with their own accounts.
- **Selk'nam, Yagán and Kawésqar descendants**: check what kind of recognition each asks for, and from whom.
- **Chilote families**: check whether craft awards reach island makers or mainland intermediaries.
- **Welsh-, German- and Croatian-descended families**: compare who is listed as a "pioneer" in each town's anniversary books.
- **Malvinas veterans in Patagonian towns**: check how recognition differs by province.
- **Women in sheep-station and oil households**: check whose unpaid work enters any honors list.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for state honors, apologies and monuments; mixed for municipal honors; thin for how recognition is granted inside Indigenous communities.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for Crown titles; Mapudungun for terms of esteem; Welsh for chapel thanks.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** monuments of the 'Conquest of the Desert' and the 'Pacification of the Araucanía' are live disputes; Selk'nam descent claims are debated, and each account stays an account.
- **Held for review:** the formal acts by which a state lists a people or registers a community were weighed and left out; they are Libra's letter and Capricorn's spirit, and this sheet keeps only the campaigns and their reception.
