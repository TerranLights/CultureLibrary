# Araucanía-Patagonia: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu on both slopes, the Chilean south from the Araucanía to Cape Horn, and Argentine Patagonia from Neuquén to Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Aonekko ʼaʼien and Gününa küne in community use. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche of Temuco and of rural *lof***: compare who is greeted, named and counted in each setting.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: check how national news frames their claims and how their own media does.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check whether they are seen as Mapuche, as Chilote, or as neither.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check where they are seen at all, outside museums.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: compare old exhibition photographs with images they now make.
- **Chilote salmon and plant workers**: check who in the islands sees their work.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check how the attention of visitors from Wales shapes their public image.
- **German- and Croatian-descended families**: check when they kept a low profile and when they stepped forward.
- **Elders on remote Aysén and Santa Cruz holdings**: compare visiting networks of church, school and neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for media studies and census debates; thin for greeting and gaze customs outside Mapuche ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for greeting forms; Italian for De Agostini's captions and books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** early photographs of Fuegian peoples may be ones descendants do not want reproduced; ask before reusing.
- **Held for review:** the pointer type weighed here was police and intelligence files on Mapuche activists, the state's watching eye; surveillance is Scorpio's and its rules are law, so none is listed.
