# Araucanía-Patagonia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche lands of both slopes, the Chilean south to Magallanes, and Patagonian Argentina to the Beagle Channel. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Selk'nam and Kawésqar through community organizations. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche of the Araucanía**: compare Galvarino as told in schools with how families tell him.
- **Pewenche and *campesino* families of Lonquimay**: check how Ránquil is remembered in the valley.
- **Mapuche of Río Negro and Chubut**: check memory of captivity and forced moves after 1879.
- **Williche families**: compare school-language memories with those of Mapuche to the north.
- **Selk'nam, Kawésqar and Yagán descendants**: check how exhibition and press images are spoken of, and by whom.
- **Chilote migrants in Argentine Patagonia and their children**: check how labels were felt and answered.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare school memories under Argentine schooling with family memory of Wales.
- **German- and Croatian-descended families**: check wartime suspicion and name changes as remembered humbling.
- **University students in Valdivia, Temuco and Punta Arenas**: compare hazing on campuses that banned it and those that did not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for contested history and school data; thin for everyday teasing and for shaming within small communities.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for shame terms; Welsh for school memories; French for 1889 exhibition sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 'Conquest of the Desert', the 'Pacification of the Araucanía' and the exhibitions are contested: present each account as an account; never describe a people through its humiliation.
- **Held for review:** archive photographs of captives from the 1879-85 campaigns and of Fuegian people exhibited in Europe were deliberately not listed; reaching them first through descendants' organizations avoids repeating the harm.
