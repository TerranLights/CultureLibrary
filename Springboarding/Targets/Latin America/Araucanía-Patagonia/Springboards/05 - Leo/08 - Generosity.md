# Araucanía-Patagonia: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, Chiloé and the lakes, Aysén, Magallanes, and the steppe and Andean provinces of Argentine Patagonia. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán and Aonekko ʼaʼien where communities share sharing customs. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche and Nagche communities**: compare *ngillatun* hosting costs on the coast and on the plains.
- **Pewenche families**: check how *piñón* harvests are shared with kin and guests.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: check feast-giving at *kamarikun* and who supplies it.
- **Aónikenk families**: check what is remembered of gift exchange with travelers and settlers.
- **Yagán families of Puerto Williams**: check accounts of sharing a large catch or stranded whale, as they tell them.
- **Chilote families**: compare *medán* and *minga* on the islands and among Chilotes in Argentina.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare chapel giving in the lower valley and the Andes.
- **German-, Croatian- and Lebanese-descended families**: check whether charity runs through clubs, churches or kin.
- **Oil and sheep-station workers**: check collections for injured mates and widows.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for telethons and service clubs; thin for everyday giving and for Fuegian and steppe sharing customs.
- **Languages needed:** 19th-c. Spanish for de la Cruz; Mapudungun for feast terms; Welsh for chapel thanksgiving.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** feast sponsorship can map family standing; ask before naming givers.
- **Held for review:** set aside for Libra and Capricorn: the donation-tax statutes of both states, which shape who gives through a foundation and who gives by hand.
