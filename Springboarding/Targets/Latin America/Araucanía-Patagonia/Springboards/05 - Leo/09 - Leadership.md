# Araucanía-Patagonia: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, Los Ríos, Los Lagos and Chiloé, Aysén and Magallanes, with Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Arabic and Spanish for Lebanese-descended families' papers. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche, Wenteche and Lafkenche communities**: compare whether *lonko* succession runs by lineage, election or both.
- **Pewenche communities**: check how *lonko* and *werken* share the work of speaking out.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: compare community leaders with those of provincial Mapuche confederations.
- **Williche of Osorno and Chiloé**: check how *caciques* are chosen in the councils.
- **Aónikenk families**: check what is remembered of 19th-c. leaders and who keeps it.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam organizations**: check how small communities choose spokespeople.
- **Chilote crews and parishes**: compare the skipper's lead at sea with the lay leader's in chapel.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare chapel *blaenoriaid* with town leaders in Trelew and Trevelin.
- **German-, Croatian- and Lebanese-descended families**: check which families led clubs, firms or provinces, and how followers were won.
- **Oil and sheep-station workers**: compare union leaders with *capataces*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 19th-c. Mapuche and Tehuelche leaders and provincial politics; mixed for women's leadership; thin for Fuegian peoples' leadership after the missions.
- **Languages needed:** 16th-c. verse Spanish; Mapudungun for role names; Welsh for chapel and council records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current *lonko* disputes and political figures are live; give each side's account.
- **Held for review:** near the line here was authority: the legal powers of *lonko* under state registration and of provincial governors; command and its rules go to Capricorn and the text of law to Libra, so this sheet keeps the leadership people follow freely.
