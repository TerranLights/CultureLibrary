# Araucanía-Patagonia: Play (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche lands of both slopes, the Chilean lakes, islands and fjords, and the Argentine Patagonian provinces. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Basque for card-game clubs. **Concept in one line:** activity done for its own sake, set apart from ordinary life by rules or make-believe: games, sport as play, pretending, joking, toying.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche communities**: compare *palin* as sport, school subject and ceremony.
- **Lafkenche and Pewenche families**: check which games belong to the coast and which to the *piñón* season.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: check whether games survive at *kamarikun* and in schools.
- **Aónikenk families**: check which games and contests are remembered, and through whom.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán families**: check children's games at sea and shore, in their own memory.
- **Selk'nam descendants**: check which pastimes, if any, families revive.
- **Chilote families**: compare island games with those of Chilote neighborhoods in Comodoro Rivadavia.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check chapel limits on Sunday play and how they eased.
- **German-, Croatian-, Basque- and Italian-descended families**: compare club card and bowling nights.
- **Oil and sheep-station workers**: check pastimes in camps and bunkhouses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for *palin* and festival games; thin for Fuegian and steppe play and for adults' card games outside clubs.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-c. Spanish for Rosales; Mapudungun for riddles and tales; Croatian and Basque for club games.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *palin* can be ceremonial; ask before filming a match.
- **Held for review:** considered and left aside: outsiders' photographs and films of the Selk'nam *Hain*, sometimes catalogued as games or theater; the ceremony is not play and may hold restricted knowledge.
