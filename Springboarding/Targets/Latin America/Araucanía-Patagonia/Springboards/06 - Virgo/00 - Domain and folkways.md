# Araucanía-Patagonia: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Virgo's frame runs from the Araucanía coast and *pewen* country through Los Ríos, Los Lagos with Chiloé, Aysén and Magallanes, and Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz and Argentine Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish in its southern Chilean, Chilote and Patagonian Argentine forms; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar and Yagán as their programs share them. **Concept in one line:** work and service: what makes good work and a well-kept life? Folkways: work ways (no runner-up folkway sits in Virgo).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche, Nagche and Pewenche communities**: how tasks split among shore gathering, plains fields and Lonquimay summer pastures.
- **Williche communities from the Osorno coast to southern Chiloé**: whether chapel duties and the *minga* are called work or service.
- **Mapuche *crianceros* of Neuquén**: set the herding calendar beside oil-field shift rosters in the same families.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: any record of their work as guides and station hands; thin, start with provincial archives.
- **Channel and Fuegian peoples (Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam)**: how their own organizations describe canoe, shellfish and station work.
- **Chilote families**: compare which child kept the smallholding and which went to Argentine stations, as families tell it.
- **Welsh-, German-, Croatian-, Boer- and Lebanese-descended families**: compare lower-valley farm diaries (Welsh), dairy, shop and freight papers; Boer sources scarce.
- **Station hands and chapel, Catholic and Evangelical congregations**: when Sunday rest was waived for lambing or harvest.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for labor statistics, station and oil history and Welsh farm papers; thin for Aónikenk, Gününa küne and Boer families' own work records. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled, so Virgo's sheets read work and service across the frame above; the Biobío, La Pampa, southern Buenos Aires, southern Mendoza and the Falklands / Malvinas serve for contrast only.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for task and season names; Victorian English for the missionary magazine; Welsh for farm diaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** forced labor in missions and on stations touches living families; *lawen* knowledge belongs to its healers.
- **Held for review:** the colonial *tasas* on Native personal service and the two labor codes behind the Jara, Urbina and injury rows: text to Libra, purpose to Capricorn.
