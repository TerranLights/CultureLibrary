# Araucanía-Patagonia: Service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, the Valdivian and Llanquihue lake country, Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes, both halves of Tierra del Fuego, and Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the southern regions of both countries, Chiloé included; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** work or effort done for another (a person, a household, a customer, the public, a nation, a cause or God), as a role or as an act, paid or unpaid.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche *lof* of the Araucanía and of Neuquén**: check how *werken* and *lonko* offices are filled and left on each side.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa**: check who keeps chapel keys and for how long.
- **Chilote families**: compare the *fiscal*'s term with daughters' years in Punta Arenas service.
- **Selk'nam, Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check whether memories of children placed as servants are shared; only with consent.
- **Yagán and Kawésqar communities**: look at how mission-era work for missionaries is described in their own publications.
- **Welsh-descended families**: read deacons' lists and preaching rotas.
- **German-descended families**: compare fire-company rolls with school and church committees.
- **Croatian-descended families**: check who held unpaid office in the Punta Arenas societies.
- **Families of Boer and Lebanese descent**: compare church offices with shop service on credit in steppe towns.
- **Conscripts, hotel staff and rural doctors**: compare service chosen, assigned and forced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for the Carrasco case, tourism work and chapel offices; thin for forced service of Native children and for Boer congregations.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish and Latin for Jesuit letters; Welsh for chapel rotas; Afrikaans or Dutch for church books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the Carrasco case and of children placed as servants; road-building under the dictatorship is disputed memory.
- **Held for review:** set aside: the conscription statutes of both states and the domestic-worker rules behind the Carrasco, CMT and domestic-service rows; their wording to Libra, their aims to Capricorn.
