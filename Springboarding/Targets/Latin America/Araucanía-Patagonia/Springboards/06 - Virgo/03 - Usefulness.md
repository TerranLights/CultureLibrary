# Araucanía-Patagonia: Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu west and east of the Andes, Los Ríos, Los Lagos and Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes, Fuegia, and the Patagonian provinces of Argentina. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish as spoken in the Chilean south, on Chiloé and in Argentine Patagonia; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar and Yagán where elders' programs share terms. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose; for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche elders on both sides of the Andes**: check which ceremonial and counsel tasks are kept for them at a *ngillatun* and at a *kamarikun*.
- **Williche of the Osorno coast**: check how elders' land memory is sought for claims and what they ask in return.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán communities**: check how elder speakers are asked for knowledge by programs, and the limits they set.
- **Selk'nam families**: check how they answer outsiders who wrote them off, in recognition campaigns and genealogy work.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: check what usefulness elders hold as keepers of words; thin.
- **Chilote families**: compare the *maestro chasquilla* with master builders on the islands.
- **Families of Welsh, German and Croatian descent**: compare care in homes with care by kin.
- **Oil, rail and station retirees**: compare *centros de jubilados* with retirees who keep a trade.
- **Disabled people in remote Aysén and on the steppe**: check access to work far from services.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for aging surveys and invasive-species policy; thin for Native elders' own words on being needed.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-c. Spanish for Olivares; Mapudungun for elders' terms; Welsh for chapel obituaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and disability data need careful handling; elders' knowledge may be withheld from outsiders.
- **Held for review:** individual case files from old people's homes and rehabilitation programs were weighed and left out as private records; none of the rows needs them.
