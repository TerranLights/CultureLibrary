# Araucanía-Patagonia: Analysis (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Araucanía and the *pewen* highlands, Valdivia, Osorno, Llanquihue and Chiloé, the Aysén fjords, Magallanes and the Fuegian channels, and the Argentine provinces from Neuquén to Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; French (for one expedition); Croatian; Kawésqar and Yagán where communities share sea and weather terms. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche of the Araucanía and of Neuquén**: check how a *machi*'s diagnosis and a hospital test are weighed in the same illness.
- **Lafkenche fishers of the coast**: compare their reading of tides and catches with fisheries surveys.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán communities**: check what channel and weather knowledge they share with or keep from survey and research teams.
- **Aónikenk families**: look for track and weather reading in family testimony; thin.
- **Chilote boatmen**: compare *cabañuelas* and wind signs with marine forecasts.
- **Welsh-descended families**: read surveyors' and irrigation notebooks for how measurement was taught.
- **German-descended families**: check naturalists, pharmacists and dairy chemists among the colonists.
- **Croatian-descended families**: check engineers and accountants in Punta Arenas firms.
- **Oil-town residents and engineers in Neuquén**: compare what each side counts as proof.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, science institutions and audits; thin for Native reckoning systems and Aónikenk sources.
- **Languages needed:** French for the 1882-83 volumes; German for Steffen; 18th-c. Spanish for Febrés.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *pewütun* belongs to *machi*; the 1882-83 measurements of Yagán bodies and the 1966-68 count under a military government need careful handling.
- **Held for review:** genetic-ancestry studies of Mapuche and Fuegian descendants were weighed: they analyze people rather than show how people analyze, and some sampling is disputed by communities, so none is listed.
