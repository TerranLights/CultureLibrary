# Araucanía-Patagonia: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, the lake and island south of Chile, Aysén, Magallanes and Fuegia, and the Argentine provinces from Neuquén to Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both Andean slopes and of Chiloé; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche and Wenteche families**: check how *pewma* and an elder's *ngülam* weigh in a decision, as families choose to tell it.
- **Mapuche communities of Neuquén**: compare how assemblies vet oil and tourism projects brought to them.
- **Aónikenk families**: set Viedma's trade judgments beside what families remember of frontier dealing; thin.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán communities**: check how they decide which visitors, filmmakers and researchers to work with.
- **Selk'nam organizations**: check how claims of descent are weighed, only as they publish it.
- **Chilote families**: check how islanders tell a healer from a *brujo*.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare *seiat* records across denominations.
- **Club members of German and Croatian descent**: check who vouched for newcomers in clubs.
- **Lebanese-descended shopkeepers**: check who was judged fit for credit.
- **Station breeders and elders living alone**: compare the skilled eye with the easy mark.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for wine, livestock shows and fraud news; thin for Aónikenk sources and for dream-guidance, which families may not share.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-c. Spanish for Viedma; French for Tounens's own writings; Welsh for *seiat* records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *pewma* and *perimontun* are personal and spiritual; media-framing studies sit inside a live conflict; scam victims' identities.
- **Held for review:** a pointer kind weighed here was hiring and housing audit studies that test prejudice; they belong more to Libra's fairness concepts, so none is listed.
