# Araucanía-Patagonia: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, Valdivia to Chiloé, the Aysén and Magallanes south, Fuegia, and Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (Chile's south and Chiloé; Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán and Kawésqar where craft revivals name techniques. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *perfeccionista*, *exigente*, *bien hecho* | perfectionist; demanding; well made | pair with crafts and building: "bien hecho tejido Dalcahue", "artesana exigente Gaiman" |
| Spanish (Chile) | *terminaciones*; *a la rápida*; *mañoso* | finishing work in building; done in a rush; fussy | *terminaciones* is the word of complaint over new houses in Temuco and Puerto Montt |
| Spanish (Argentina) | *prolijo*, *prolijidad*; *desprolijo* | neat and careful, neatness; sloppy | a school and workplace grade of care; try Neuquén and Trelew school reports |
| Spanish (Chiloé) | *tejuela* | hand-split wooden shingle | the patterned shingle as a standard of island carpentry |
| Mapudungun | *ñimin*; *trarikan* | woven design work; tied-resist dyeing | the exacting techniques of Mapuche weaving; *check* both |
| Welsh | *cynghanedd*; *trylwyr*; *perffaith* | the strict sound-patterning of Welsh verse; thorough; perfect | the chair poem at the eisteddfod must keep *cynghanedd* |
| German | *Gründlichkeit*; *pingelig* | thoroughness; fussy, nitpicking | praise and teasing in Llanquihue and Valdivia |
| Croatian | *temeljit*; *pedantan* | thorough; pedantic | Punta Arenas family and firm papers |
| Afrikaans | *noukeurig* | meticulous | Chubut Boer memoirs, *check* |

English keywords: Chiloé shingles; Mapuche weaving technique; Welsh strict-metre poetry Patagonia; housing defects southern Chile; perfectionism students Chile Argentina.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche weavers of the Araucanía and of the Río Negro steppe**: check what each says about undoing a flawed piece.
- **Lafkenche and Pewenche ceremonial hosts**: compare who keeps the order of a ceremony and how a slip is put right.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: look for any memory of robe-painting standards; very thin.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán makers in revival workshops**: check who sets the standard for a basket or a model canoe.
- **Selk'nam organizations**: check how closely revival body painting follows old photographs.
- **Chilote families**: compare shingle makers with knitters at Dalcahue.
- **Welsh-descended families**: read adjudications of *cynghanedd* entries over decades.
- **Families of German and Croatian descent**: check precision as praised in firm histories and obituaries.
- **Southern students and young athletes**: compare test pressure in Temuco with Neuquén.
- **Social-housing residents**: check who they hold to account for poor finishing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for Welsh poetry, Chilote architecture and housing complaints; thin for Fuegian and steppe craft standards.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh (strict-metre terms) for adjudications; Mapudungun for weaving terms; Victorian English for Musters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** errors in ceremony may not be discussed outside a community; eating-disorder data need care.
- **Held for review:** building-code and housing-warranty rules lie behind the housing row; what they require is Libra's, what they intend Capricorn's.
