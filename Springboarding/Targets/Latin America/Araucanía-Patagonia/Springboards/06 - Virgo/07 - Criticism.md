# Araucanía-Patagonia: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Araucanía and Malleco, Los Ríos, Osorno, Llanquihue and Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes, Fuegia, and Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chilean, Chilote and Patagonian Argentine); Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche of Malleco and of Neuquén**: check who may voice faults at a *trawün*: elders, women, young people.
- **Williche of Osorno**: check petitions sent to intendants and how sharply they are worded.
- **Selk'nam, Kawésqar and Yagán organizations**: look at their statements on museum displays of ancestors' photographs.
- **Aónikenk families**: check what complaints reached Río Gallegos authorities; thin.
- **Chilote families**: compare complaint toward Santiago with criticism kept inside the village.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare adjudications in Trevelin and Trelew.
- **Members of German and Croatian clubs**: check quarrels recorded in club and society minutes.
- **Esquel and oil-town residents**: compare how criticism of a company is organized.
- **Pastors and priests of the south**: check when rebuke is given from the pulpit and when in private.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press letters, assemblies and ombudsman reports; thin for Fuegian and steppe peoples' own voice.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-c. Spanish for Tesillo; Welsh for adjudications; Mapudungun for *koyagtun*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** escraches and the 2019 assemblies are disputed; whistleblowers in small towns are easy to identify.
- **Held for review:** defamation and press statutes surfaced behind the letters and escrache rows; what they say belongs to Libra; why they exist, to Capricorn.
