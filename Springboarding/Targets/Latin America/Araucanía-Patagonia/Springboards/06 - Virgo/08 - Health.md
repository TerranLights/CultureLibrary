# Araucanía-Patagonia: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu from Temuco to the Neuquén valleys, Los Ríos, Los Lagos and Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes, Fuegia, and Río Negro, Chubut and Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (both countries' southern speech and Chiloé's); Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán and Kawésqar as health and language programs share them. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche patients west and east of the Andes**: compare how referrals run between *machi* and hospital on each side.
- **Lafkenche and inland communities**: check whether gallbladder-cancer studies sample coast and interior separately.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: check winter access to care when roads close.
- **Yagán and Kawésqar communities**: check how the epidemics of contact are told by families, apart from mission records.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne families**: look for station-era health memories; thin.
- **Chilote families**: compare the *posta rural* with herb knowledge in the same household.
- **Settler-descended families (Welsh, German, Boer, Croatian)**: set remedy notebooks and herbalists side by side.
- **Oil and salmon workers**: check health care in camps and on rotation.
- **Coyhaique and Temuco households**: compare wood heating and lung health across incomes.
- **Pentecostal and Catholic congregations**: compare prayer for healing with hospital chaplaincy.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for epidemiology, air quality and outbreaks; mixed for intercultural care; thin for Aónikenk and Gününa küne health memory.
- **Languages needed:** 18th-c. Italian for Molina; Mapudungun for illness terms; Afrikaans and Welsh for remedy books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *machitun* and *lawen* knowledge belong to healers; cancer and mental-health data need care; the Epuyén outbreak left blame in a small town.
- **Held for review:** weighed and kept out: Río Negro's mental-health statute and the air-plan decrees behind the reform and smoke rows, whose wording is Libra's work and whose intent is Capricorn's.
