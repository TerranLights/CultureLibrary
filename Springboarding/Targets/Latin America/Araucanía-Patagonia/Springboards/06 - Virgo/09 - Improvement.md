# Araucanía-Patagonia: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu, the Valdivia and Osorno lowlands, Llanquihue and Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes and Fuegia, and the Argentine south from the Neuquén dams to Santa Cruz. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean lakes, Chiloé and the Argentine steppe; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

English keywords: Pacification of the Araucanía; Conquest of the Desert scientific commission; Aysén forest fires pasture; Capuchin missions Araucanía; El Chocón dam displacement; thermal retrofit subsidy Chile.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche communities of Malleco and Cautín**: check whether restituted land is spoken of as restored, improved or still owed.
- **Mapuche of the Limay valley**: check what families displaced by the dams say about the "progress" that moved them.
- **Williche of Osorno and San Juan de la Costa**: check how mission schooling is remembered in the families.
- **Selk'nam, Kawésqar and Yagán families**: compare their own revival work with how missions described "civilizing" them.
- **Aónikenk of Camusu Aike**: check whether reserve land is framed as kept or bettered; thin.
- **Aysén *pobladores***: compare pride in clearing with regret over burned forests.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check literary societies as self-betterment.
- **Llanquihue families of German descent**: compare praise of clearing in chronicles with forest histories.
- **Croatian- and Lebanese-descended families**: check rise-from-nothing stories in firm and club histories.
- **First-generation students and adult learners**: compare Temuco with Neuquén.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonization, missions and housing programs; thin for Aónikenk and Gününa küne voices on imposed change.
- **Languages needed:** German for Capuchin mission papers; 18th-c. Spanish for Franciscan records; Welsh for literary societies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "Pacification" and the "Conquest of the Desert", land restitution and mission schooling are contested ground; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** left for Libra and Capricorn: the colonization and immigration laws of both states, and the rules on *mejoras* as grounds for title, which stand behind the colonization and burning rows.
