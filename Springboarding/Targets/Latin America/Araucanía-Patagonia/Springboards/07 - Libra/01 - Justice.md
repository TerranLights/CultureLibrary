# Araucanía-Patagonia: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the courts of five Chilean regions and five Argentine provinces, Temuco to Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish and its court Spanish in both states; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *justicia*, *hacer justicia*, *impunidad* | justice; to see justice done; impunity | pair with a southern court or case: "justicia Temuco tribunal oral", "impunidad Neltume 1973" |
| Spanish | *justicia por mano propia* | taking justice into one's own hands | used on both sides of the Andes; search Neuquén and Araucanía press |
| Spanish (court reporting, Chile) | *comunero*; *querella*; *formalización* | community member (as press and charges name Mapuche defendants); criminal complaint; formal charging | *comunero*: note how files and press label parties; do not adopt the label |
| Mapudungun | *nor*; *norche* | straight, right; an upright person | *check*; the "straight" root as one possible justice word |
| Mapudungun | *kullin* | livestock; old sense of payment or fine for a wrong | *check* in Febrés and Augusta: the compensation track of justice |
| Mapudungun | *illkun* | anger at a wrong suffered | the word a land-loss study puts in its title; ties grievance to claim |
| Welsh | *barn*; *llys* | judgment; court | the colony's early court in Rawson-era minutes |
| German; Afrikaans; Croatian | *Recht*; *reg*; *sud* | law and a right (as in *derecho*); the same; court | test whether colonist, Boer and Punta Arenas papers keep the double sense |

English keywords: Mapuche antiterrorism trials; Indigenous land suits Patagonia; Selk'nam killings inquiry.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche communities around Ercilla, Collipulli and Victoria**: check which court files name their *lof*, and how each party tells the same case.
- **Lafkenche of Puerto Saavedra**: see whether coastal disputes reach courts or stay in assemblies.
- **Mapuche of Panguipulli and Neltume**: set the 1973 killings' trials beside the forestry-workers' and families' own memory.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén's oil country**: compare which wrongs go to *amparo* courts and which to provincial agencies.
- **Mapuche-Tehuelche of Cushamen and Leleque**: compare Chubut and Buenos Aires coverage of the Esquel suit.
- **Selk'nam descendants (Rafaela Ishton community; Chilean Selk'nam groups)**: compare official records of the killings with family memory.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Yagán of Puerto Williams**: check whether any wrong has reached a court; likely thin.
- **Welsh settlers of the lower valley**: read the colony's first jury verdicts before Argentine courts arrived.
- **Catholic and Evangelical prison chaplaincies in Temuco and Neuquén**: check what they record of whom they visit.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes and published judgments; mixed for colonial and frontier-era cases; thin for Kawésqar, Yagán and Aónikenk records of their own justice.
- **Languages needed:** court Spanish of two systems; Mapudungun for testimony given through interpreters; English for UN and Inter-American documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** several cases are recent and grieved on more than one side; treat every judgment as a record and every party's reading as an account. Juvenile files are sealed.
- **Held for review:** the debate on whether the antiterrorism law is a legitimate tool was found beside the Ley 18.314 row; that is the spirit of the law and belongs in Capricorn.
