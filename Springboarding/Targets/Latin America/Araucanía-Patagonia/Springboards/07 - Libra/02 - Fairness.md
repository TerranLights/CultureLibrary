# Araucanía-Patagonia: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; from the Cautín plains and Chiloé's fishing coves to the Chubut valley farms and the Santa Cruz sheep steppe. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile and Argentina; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: check how summer pasture turns (*veranada*) are shared among families each year.
- **Nagche communities around Traiguén and Lumaco**: check who received which plot under the 1979 division, and how families judge the split now.
- **Lafkenche fishing communities**: check how a boat crew divides the catch, beside the quota rules.
- **Chilote families and salmon-plant workers**: compare piece rates, shifts and who gets the better line.
- **Shearing crews (*comparsas*) in Chubut and Santa Cruz**: check who keeps the tally and how pay is split within the crew.
- **Mapuche *crianceros* of Neuquén**: read grazing permits on public land for how they are allotted.
- **Welsh-descended farmers of Gaiman and Dolavon**: compare canal turns in drought years.
- **Aónikenk families of Camusu Aike**: check how reserve land is divided among households; sources scarce.
- **Croatian-descended families of Porvenir**: check how stations and shops passed among children.
- **Oil workers of Comodoro Rivadavia and Las Heras**: compare zone pay with neighbors in other trades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for statutes, subsidies and fishing law; one experimental sample; thin for Fuegian and Aónikenk sharing rules.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish of the codes and of station work; colonial Spanish for the *tasas*; Mapudungun for game and sharing words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1920-21 strikes and the 1979 land division are told in conflicting ways; fishing-quota passage drew bribery prosecutions, to read as court records.
- **Held for review:** travel writing that grades peoples' honesty in trade was considered and left out: it profiles groups instead of pointing to sources.
