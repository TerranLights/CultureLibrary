# Araucanía-Patagonia: Balance (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; balances of body, household, land and power from the Budi lake and Chiloé to the Aysén rivers, the Neuquén oil basin and the Fuegian forests. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile and Argentina; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán through its community programs. **Concept in one line:** the even weighting of two or more forces, parties or demands so that none overwhelms the others; and the steadiness that results, in a person, a household, a society or the land.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: check how the limits of the *pewen* harvest are set, and who teaches them.
- **Lafkenche of the Budi lake**: check rules on gathering shellfish and seaweed by tide and season.
- **Williche of Chiloé**: compare island tellings of Trentren and Kaikai with mainland Mapuche ones.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén's oil country**: set the provincial royalty debates beside community statements on the same fields.
- **Aysén settler families**: read both sides' material in the HidroAysén fight by town (Cochrane, Coyhaique).
- **Yagán of Navarino Island**: check how the community's statements speak of beavers, fish and shellfish beds.
- **Welsh chapel members**: compare temperance pledges with tea-house and festival customs.
- **Sheep-station families of Santa Cruz**: check flock-reduction choices after degradation studies.
- **Oil and salmon workers on rotating rosters**: look for union and family surveys of time at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for statutes and environmental review; mixed for oral narrative; thin for Fuegian peoples' published teachings on balance.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for cosmological terms; Chilote Spanish for folklore; Welsh for chapel minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *ngillatun* dances and *machi* healing are ritual knowledge; use what communities publish.
- **Held for review:** named *machi* and their treatments came up for the health row; they stay with their communities, so the row points to public programs instead.
