# Araucanía-Patagonia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; judges, officials, arbiters and referees from Temuco and Osorno to Río Gallegos, Esquel and Porvenir, where many towns are small enough that everyone knows the judge. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile and Argentina; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche parties in Temuco and Angol courts**: check in hearing records when an interpreter was asked for and granted.
- **Williche councils of Osorno's coast**: read their own statutes for how *caciques* are chosen and whether kin may judge kin.
- **Kawésqar groups of Puerto Edén and Puerto Natales**: check how CONADI decided between overlapping applications for the same waters.
- **Mapuche communities of Río Negro**: read the provincial ombudsman's files on their complaints, and the replies.
- **Residents of Río Gallegos and Caleta Olivia**: set local press on the *Vialidad* case against Buenos Aires coverage.
- **Small towns (Porvenir, Puerto Natales, Esquel, Trevelin)**: count recusal motions where everyone knows the judge.
- **Welsh-descended families of Trevelin**: compare how the 1902 vote is told at the Trevelin museum and in boundary histories.
- **Chilean migrants in Neuquén and Santa Cruz**: check public job notices for nationality rules.
- **Evangelical congregations of the Araucanía**: check who settles quarrels between members and how a pastor steps back when kin are involved.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, transparency data and judgments; mixed for colonial court records; thin for any written Mapuche, Aónikenk or Fuegian rule on judging without favor.
- **Languages needed:** court Spanish of both states; colonial Spanish; English for the 1902 award papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *Vialidad* and *Norín* cases are politically charged; quote neither side as the verdict on a people or a party.
- **Held for review:** investigation files in provincial corruption cases still open were left out; only judgments already public are listed.
