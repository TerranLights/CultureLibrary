# Araucanía-Patagonia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; decisions put off, reversed or left pending, from colonial decrees and Mapuche assemblies to the Chacao channel, the Patagonian territories and the land laws still waiting on both sides of the Andes. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile and Argentina; colonial Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Spanish (legal) | *prórroga*; *emergencia*; *suspensión de desalojos* | extension; emergency; stay of evictions | the words by which a law keeps a matter undecided |
| Mapudungun | *epu rakizuam* | two thoughts, being of two minds | *check* whether speakers use it so; *rakizuam* is thought |
| Mapudungun | *pewma* | a dream, here as one sign consulted before a choice | how a decision is handed to a sign rather than made alone |

English keywords: Chacao bridge cancellation; Argentine Indigenous land emergency law extensions; Chile constitutional plebiscites results by commune.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche communities of Malleco on the land-fund list**: check how years of waiting appear in their own minutes beside CONADI's files.
- **Lafkenche applicants for coastal spaces**: compare filing dates with decision dates.
- **Williche and other Chiloé residents**: see how island councils argued the bridge before and after cancellation.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro under Ley 26.160**: check which communities' surveys were finished and which remain open.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: read published assembly records for the number of meetings before a decision.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower valley**: compare chapel lists before and after 1902.
- **Chilote families with kin in Argentina**: check in oral histories who decided to cross the Andes and how long it took.
- **Aónikenk of Camusu Aike**: check the status of pending land matters; sources few.
- **Selk'nam groups in Chile and Tierra del Fuego**: follow the recognition bills through committee.
- **Catholic and Evangelical pastors**: check how they counsel members facing a move or a marriage.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, election returns and project records; mixed for assembly records; thin for Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Boer families' own accounts of deciding.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for royal decrees; Mapudungun for assembly speech; Welsh for emigration letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** land deferrals touch active claims and evictions; present the state's and the communities' readings side by side.
- **Held for review:** commentary on whether the delays are a deliberate state strategy was considered; that reads the law's purpose and belongs in Capricorn.
