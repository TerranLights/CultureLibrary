# Araucanía-Patagonia: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; quiet kept or bought, and silences broken, from Malleco and Osorno to Cutral Có, Coyhaique, Chiloé's ports, Punta Arenas and the Santa Cruz stations. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile and Argentina; colonial Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche communities of Ercilla and Collipulli**: check how their statements and local press describe daily life under emergency rule.
- **Residents of Cutral Có and Plaza Huincul**: compare memories of 1996 among oil workers, merchants and the unemployed.
- **Townspeople of Coyhaique and Puerto Aysén**: check which 2012 promises are said to have been kept.
- **Chiloé fishers and divers**: see who accepted the 2016 payments and who refused.
- **Catholic laity of Osorno**: trace who spoke up, when, and what it cost them in the parish.
- **Families of Santa Cruz station hands and ranchers**: set descendants' accounts of 1921 side by side.
- **Selk'nam descendants and station families of Tierra del Fuego**: check where the killings are spoken of and where they are passed over.
- **Punta Arenas families**: compare silence and protest under the dictatorship, including the 1984 *Puntarenazo*.
- **Welsh-descended chapel members**: check how disputes with officials were settled or endured.
- **Evangelical pastors in the Araucanía**: check counsel to women in violent marriages: forgive, or leave.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for statutes, emergency decrees and protest coverage; mixed for strike and occupation records; thin for Kawésqar and Aónikenk accounts of giving way or resisting.
- **Languages needed:** official and colonial Spanish; Mapudungun for counsel and rising words; Welsh for chapel records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** emergency rule, the 'Pacification', the 1921 shootings and the Selk'nam killings are contested and grieved; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** survivors' testimony with identifying details from church cases was considered; it is private, so only public inquiry and court records are pointed to.
