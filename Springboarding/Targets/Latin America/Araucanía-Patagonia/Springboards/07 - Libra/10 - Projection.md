# Araucanía-Patagonia: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the images outsiders and neighbors have pinned on the south's peoples, and the blame for misfortune placed on others, from Magellan's "giants" and the Fuegian exhibitions to Chiloé's witch trial, cross-Andean labels and today's press. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of Chile, Chiloé and Argentina; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar and Selk'nam through their organizations. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Selk'nam organizations in Chile and Argentine Tierra del Fuego**: set exhibition photographs against images the groups now publish of themselves.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Punta Arenas**: read the repatriation record and their statements on how they were shown abroad.
- **Aónikenk families of Santa Cruz**: compare the "giant" image in tourism and street names with community accounts.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: check how the claim that Mapuche are foreign to Argentina appears in press and textbooks, and how Mapuche organizations answer it.
- **Mapuche of the Araucanía**: compare national and regional headlines on the same events with Mapuche-run media.
- **Chilote families**: compare the 1880 trial record with island tellings of the *brujos*; then follow the *chilote* label across the Andes.
- **Welsh-descended families of Chubut**: check responses to travel writing and documentaries about "Welsh Patagonia".
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue**: check whether Chilean schoolbooks carry a stock colonist image, and how descendants receive it.
- **Croatian-descended families of Magallanes**: compare how Punta Arenas and Santiago papers have pictured them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for outsiders' images and modern media; mixed for court records of blame; thin for Fuegian peoples' own published answers, though growing.
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Italian and Spanish; Victorian English; French for the exhibitions and Tounens; Mapudungun for harm and sorcery terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** human exhibitions and the Selk'nam killings are contested and grieved; outsiders once wrote the Fuegian peoples off: study that as an outsiders' image, never repeat it. *Kalku* accusations can harm living people.
- **Held for review:** body measurements and remains files of Fuegian and Aónikenk people in museum archives were considered; they are under repatriation claims, so none is named.
