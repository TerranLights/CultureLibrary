# Araucanía-Patagonia: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Wallmapu and Patagonia on both slopes of the Andes, Araucanía and Neuquén to Porvenir and Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Chilean, Chilote and Patagonian Spanish; Mapudungun and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Gününa küne via their programs. **Concept in one line:** how death, power and what is hidden are faced: death and rebirth, sex, shared resources, inheritance, secrets. Folkways: death ways and sex ways; power ways as runner-up (home sign Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison) and Puerto Saavedra**: check whether coastal *eltun* (burial grounds) are recorded apart from inland ones.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: ask how a winter death is handled when the passes close.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa**: set the Pucatrihue pilgrimage beside Osorno burial books.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: look for burial grounds disturbed by roads or oil works, and what followed.
- **Aónikenk descendants (Camusu Aike)**: read cairn digs against families' wishes for their dead.
- **Selk'nam, Yagán and Kawésqar descendants**: compare what each now shares of initiation and mourning; outsiders long wrote them off, so weigh the record that followed.
- **Chilote families across the Andes**: compare island wakes with those held in Comodoro Rivadavia.
- **Welsh-, German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: compare who inherits the farm, shop or estancia, and the grave's language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for vital statistics and old ethnography of Mapuche funerals; mixed for sex ways (few southern cuts); thin for Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Gününa küne voices. The author has yet to fix the nation's lands, so Scorpio follows the Andes-spanning frame above; the Biobío, La Pampa, southern Buenos Aires and Mendoza, and the Falklands / Malvinas are comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** colonial notarial Spanish for estate inventories; German Kurrent in Llanquihue burial books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** graves, remains and the *Hain* belong to their peoples; *machi* knowledge is guarded; sorcery accusations harm the accused. The 'Pacification of the Araucanía' and the 'Conquest of the Desert' frame many death records: read each as an account.
- **Held for review:** power ways (rank, who rules) surfaced beside the estancia and strike pointers and wait for Capricorn; the law of wills goes to Libra.
