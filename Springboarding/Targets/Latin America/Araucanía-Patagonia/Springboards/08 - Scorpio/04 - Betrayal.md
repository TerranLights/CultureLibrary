# Araucanía-Patagonia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; old frontier forts and *lof* of the Araucanía, the Beagle Channel, the strike country of Santa Cruz, the oil fields of Comodoro Rivadavia and the Welsh valley. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states, with each side's slang; Mapudungun; Welsh; Yagán through the Puerto Williams community; English for mission records. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche (*abajinos*) and Wenteche (*arribanos*)**: compare which alliances with the Chilean state each side's descendants remember as kept or betrayed in the 1860s-80s.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: look at the 1934 Ránquil killings for accounts of who led the police to the camps.
- **Yagán of Puerto Williams**: set the community's own telling of Wulaia beside the mission's.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Trevelin**: check family letters for blame aimed at the 1865 promoters or at the Argentine government.
- **Shearers and peons of Santa Cruz, Chilote workers among them**: compare surviving strikers' accounts of the surrenders with army reports.
- **Oil families of Comodoro Rivadavia**: check union memory of strikebreakers against company histories.
- **Croatian- and German-descended families of Magallanes and Llanquihue**: check whether 1940s wartime loyalties split clubs and families, and how members recall the split.
- **Chilean migrants in Neuquén and Río Gallegos**: see how 1978 and 1982 changed what neighbors suspected of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial wars, the strikes and the dictatorship; mixed for infidelity (no southern survey); thin for Kawésqar and Aónikenk accounts of broken agreements.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for parliament and war records; Victorian English for mission papers; nineteenth-century Welsh; prison and street slang on both sides.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Valech testimony is sealed by law for decades; *yanakona* and *sapo* are charges against real people, so name none; the strikes, the Wulaia killings and 1982 are contested.
- **Held for review:** named informers in Valech testimony and in Mapuche case files were considered; they are left out because naming them would expose living people, and the treason statutes are Libra's.
