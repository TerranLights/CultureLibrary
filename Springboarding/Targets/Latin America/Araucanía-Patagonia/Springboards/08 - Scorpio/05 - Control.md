# Araucanía-Patagonia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the dammed Limay and Chubut, Andean forests, Malleco's forestry estates, Chiloé's old *encomiendas*. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche communities along the Limay and Collón Curá**: check which *lof* lost land to the reservoirs, and how compensation was set.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: compare fire brigades' rules with families' own burning and gathering calendar.
- **Nagche families near forestry estates in Ercilla and Collipulli**: compare their accounts of gates and patrols with the companies'.
- **Williche of Chiloé and Osorno**: read the 1712 rising and later *cacique* councils for memory of control by *encomenderos*.
- **Welsh-descended farmers of the lower valley**: check irrigation-company minutes for who sets the water turns.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue**: look at household manuals and school rules for the place of order and restraint.
- **Croatian- and Chilote-descended families of Punta Arenas**: compare who joins the winter carnival and who stays away.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check church and farm records for who decides over children and land.
- **Comodoro Rivadavia and Vaca Muerta oil crews**: compare shift systems that set workers' hours with home life on rest days.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dams, fire agencies and colonial labor; mixed for partner control (helpline and service data exist, surveys thin by region); thin for Mapuche writing on control in the home.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for *encomienda* files; Mapudungun derivations of *ngünen*; Welsh for irrigation minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** helpline and service data are anonymized but small southern communes risk identifying people; forestry security and the land conflict are contested and partly before the courts.
- **Held for review:** surveillance of Mapuche organizations by state agencies came up beside the forestry pointer; as policing and its rules it is left to Libra (the statutes) and Capricorn (what policing is for).
