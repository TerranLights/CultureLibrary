# Araucanía-Patagonia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Río Negro's old river route, the San Julián coast, the wool towns of the steppe, the Carretera Austral, and the *lof* and towns between Temuco and Osorno. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states; Mapudungun; Welsh; Croatian; German. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is researched here until it has a home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche of Puerto Saavedra and Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: compare whom fishers trust with catch and money (the union, the buyer, kin) with inland practice.
- **Pewenche of Icalma**: check how a *werken* is chosen and how often replaced.
- **Williche of Osorno's coast**: compare trust in *cacique* councils with trust in municipal offices.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén near Vaca Muerta**: check how communities judge company promises against agency ones.
- **Chilote families on the Argentine steppe**: compare trust in *paisanos* with trust in Argentine employers in Río Gallegos.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Trevelin**: check chapel and cooperative records for who stood surety for whom.
- **Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas and Porvenir**: compare society loans with bank loans across generations.
- **German-descended farmers of Llanquihue and Osorno**: look at dairy cooperative papers for trust kept inside the group or opened to others.
- **Evangelical and Catholic congregations**: compare whom members turn to first for a loan or a job.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national trust surveys and colonial diaries; mixed for southern breakdowns; thin for Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Yagán accounts of whom they trusted among newcomers.
- **Languages needed:** eighteenth-century Spanish for the diaries; Mapudungun for *feyentun* and *konchotun*; Welsh; Croatian for society minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** distrust between Mapuche communities and the state is a contested matter; read every source as one side's account. Trust in police and courts is the spirit of the law, for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** private family loan books and surety papers were weighed as a pointer; they are kept only as a type, since names in them would point at living families.
