# Araucanía-Patagonia: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the old war frontier and Malleco's burned estates, Puerto Natales and Punta Arenas in the strike years, Trelew's naval base, and the Fuegian stations. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states; Mapudungun; English for station and mission papers; Croatian and Welsh for community memory. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong; forgiveness and payment for wrongs are its alternatives.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *venganza*, *represalia*, *ajuste de cuentas* | revenge; reprisal; settling of scores | *represalia* is the word of strike and war records: "represalia Natales 1919", "represalia Trelew 1972" |
| Spanish | *desquitarse*, *cobrárselas*, *me la vas a pagar* | to get even; to make someone pay; "you'll pay for this" | rural southern speech; try in testimony collections and call-in radio |
| Spanish | *desquite*; *tenerle bronca*, *guardar rencor* | a getting-even; to bear a grudge | *bronca* is common on both sides of the Andes; grudge talk in Temuco, Osorno and Neuquén forums |
| Mapudungun | *malon*; *malokan* | a raid; to raid | here as reprisal: which raid answered which wrong, in *nütram* and chronicles |
| Mapudungun | *weychan* | war, fighting | *check* spelling; the larger frame in which a raid paid back a loss |
| Mapudungun | *kullin* | payment in animals for a wrong | the other road: compensation in place of revenge, as communities describe it |
| Croatian | *osveta* | revenge | Punta Arenas worker and society memory of 1920 |
| Welsh | *dial*; *maddeuant* | revenge; forgiveness | chapel writing after the 1884 killings in the Chubut interior |

English keywords: Wilckens Varela 1923; Trelew massacre 1972; Puerto Natales 1919 uprising; Federación Obrera Magallanes 1920 fire; negative reciprocity Chile Argentina.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche families of Malleco**: compare how burnings and reprisals are told in community *nütram* and in farm families' testimony.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: check whether disputes between neighbors are settled by *trawün* and payment before they become feuds.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: read memory of the 1934 killings for talk of reprisal or of forgiving.
- **Selk'nam descendants in Tierra del Fuego**: compare their families' account of the station killings with station records.
- **Croatian- and Spanish-descended workers' families of Punta Arenas**: compare memory of 1920 across union and patriotic-league families.
- **Families of Trelew and Rawson**: check how the 1972 killings are told by townspeople, sailors' families and prisoners' kin.
- **Welsh-descended families of the Chubut interior**: look for chapel teaching on revenge or forgiveness after the 1884 killings.
- **Criollo and Chilote ranch hands of Neuquén and Chubut**: compare range quarrels settled by reprisal with those taken to a *juez de paz*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the strikes, Trelew and frontier warfare; mixed for present-day revenge in rural feuds; thin for Aónikenk and Kawésqar accounts of reprisal.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for frontier histories; English for station and mission papers; Croatian for 1920 memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the land conflict's burnings and killings are recent, grieved on several sides and before the courts; station killings and the strikes are contested; name no living person as an avenger.
- **Held for review:** the court rulings on Trelew and on the Malleco burnings appeared beside their rows; what the law says and how punishment is meant to stand in for revenge are left to Libra and Capricorn.
