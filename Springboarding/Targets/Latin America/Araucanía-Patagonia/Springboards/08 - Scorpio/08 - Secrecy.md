# Araucanía-Patagonia: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Bariloche and Isla Huemul, the Andean smuggling passes, Chiloé, Neuquén and Chubut's former detention sites, Fuegian families. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states and their argots; Mapudungun; German; Welsh; Selk'nam as revived. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy is researched here as one of its facets.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Icalma**: check family accounts of the smuggling passes against customs records.
- **Wenteche and Nagche communities**: compare what *machi* allow to be recorded with what they keep; record only what is shared.
- **Williche of Chiloé**: read the 1880-81 trial record against families' silence since, without naming any.
- **German-descended families of Bariloche and Llanquihue**: compare what was said in public and at home after 1994.
- **Selk'nam descendants in Río Grande, Tolhuin and Porvenir**: compare when each family began to speak of its ancestry.
- **Neuquén and Trelew families of the disappeared**: compare how secrets about the dead came out, by court, by archive or by a neighbor.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower valley**: check chapel and family letters for what was kept out of *Y Drafod*.
- **Chilean migrants in Argentine Patagonia**: look at how undocumented status was hidden and later regularized.
- **LGBT people in oil towns and island towns**: compare who comes out where, Comodoro Rivadavia against Castro.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Priebke, Huemul and the dictatorship registries; mixed for smuggling; thin for privacy surveys with southern cuts.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for the *derroteros*; German for community papers; prison argot; Mapudungun for hiding words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *machi* and *Hain* knowledge stays with its keepers; sorcery accusations and the *Recta Provincia* harmed the accused; adoption and DNA cases involve living people; LGBT people in small towns must not be identifiable.
- **Held for review:** surveillance law and the secrecy rules of courts and archives came up beside the registry and Vicaría rows; their text goes to Libra and their purpose to Capricorn, and no restricted ceremonial material was pointed to.
