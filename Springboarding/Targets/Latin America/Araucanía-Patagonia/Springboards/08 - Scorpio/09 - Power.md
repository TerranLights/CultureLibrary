# Araucanía-Patagonia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Temuco's farm and forestry lobbies, the Mapuche municipalities, Neuquén's teachers' marches, the Chubut colony under its first governor, and Comodoro Rivadavia's years under military rule. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Spanish of both states; Mapudungun; Welsh; German. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it and how it is used (legitimate authority is Capricorn's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche *lof* of Malleco and Cautín**: compare *lonko* power within a *lof* with the power of Mapuche mayors in Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison) or Galvarino.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa and Puerto Saavedra**: check how coastal leaders gained a say over the sea, and through which offices.
- **Pewenche of Icalma and Lonquimay**: compare community leaders' standing with that of forestry and tourism firms nearby.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén (Confederación Mapuche de Neuquén)**: set the *Confederación*'s weight beside the oil companies' and the province's.
- **Aónikenk descendants of Santa Cruz**: look for any record of leadership after Orkeke's removal; scarce, start with provincial archives in Río Gallegos.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower valley**: compare the colony's elected council with the governor's office in the 1880s-90s.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue and Osorno**: check who sat on dairy, club and school boards, and for how long.
- **Croatian- and Spanish-descended merchant families of Punta Arenas**: compare their hold on trade and city office before and after 1950.
- **Oil and teachers' unions in Neuquén and Comodoro Rivadavia**: compare their reach in provincial politics over decades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial war, state arrival and unions; mixed for corporate power (self-description heavy); thin for Aónikenk and Kawésqar leadership in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish; Mapudungun for titles and *newen*; Welsh for council minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Fuentealba case and the land conflict are recent and contested; *newen* belongs to *machi* to explain; elite families are private people below the public offices.
- **Held for review:** the legitimacy of office and of the courts' power (authority, rank, power ways) sits beside every pointer here and is left to Capricorn; anti-corruption statutes are Libra's.
