# Araucanía-Patagonia: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the frontier forts of Malleco, the prisons of Temuco and Angol, the fenced steppe of Santa Cruz, the gold beaches of northern Tierra del Fuego, the factory floor of Neuquén and the channels of Puerto Edén. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both slopes; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; Afrikaans; German; Croatian; Kawésqar and Aonekko ʼaʼien through their programs. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; freedom as a belief and a political ideal (autonomy and independence stay in Aries).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Malleco**: look at how prisoners' families describe a jailed relative's freedom, and what the farm families nearby ask for.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Icalma**: check whether crossing to Neuquén for summer grazing is talked of as a freedom.
- **Mapuche of Chubut (El Maitén, Cushamen)**: follow community radio and land statements for the words they use for freedom.
- **Aónikenk descendants of Camusu Aike**: trace how the reserve's fences enter family memory.
- **Selk'nam and Kawésqar descendants**: compare how each community tells of captives sent to Europe.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower Chubut and Trevelin**: set the founders' religious freedom beside later families' choice of school and language.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: look at why some returned to South Africa and some stayed.
- **Croatian-descended families of Magallanes**: read letters for any mention of leaving Austro-Hungarian service.
- **Factory and oil workers of Neuquén and Comodoro**: compare union and cooperative accounts of a free worker.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial and labor history; mixed for Mapuche freedom in Mapuche voices (strong in movements' writing, thin in surveys); thin for Kawésqar and Aónikenk accounts of confinement.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century Spanish and notarial hand; Welsh press prose; Afrikaans and Dutch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** prisoners' cases are open or recent, with victims on several sides; the "Pacification of the Araucanía" and the "Conquest of the Desert" are named so in the sources and are contested; human-zoo images need the communities' consent to reuse.
- **Held for review:** what the constitutions and statutes say about expression, worship and movement belongs to Libra; whether "public order" should limit those freedoms is Capricorn's question.
