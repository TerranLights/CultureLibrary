# Araucanía-Patagonia: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the Nagche and Lafkenche communities and their *nütram*, the salmon coves of Chiloé, Bariloche's lakeshore legends, the old Trelew airport and the sheep country of Magallanes. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Chilean, Chilote and Patagonian Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán and Selk'nam where their communities publish. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it (scripture, elders, scientists, the press, one's own experience) and how disputes over facts are settled.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Ercilla and Collipulli**: compare how the same event is told in *nütram*, police statements and local radio.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: see which sources people cite when asked how they know of a land claim's history.
- **Williche fishers of Chiloé (Quellón, Ancud)**: set their account of the 2016 red tide beside the expert report's.
- **Yagán of Villa Ukika and Selk'nam descendants**: weigh their accounts of the nineteenth century against Harambour's and the missions' records.
- **Welsh-descended families of the lower Chubut**: contrast what the handbook promised with diaries of the first winter.
- **German-descended and Jewish families of Bariloche**: find how each responds when the Nazi-refuge stories are retold to tourists.
- **Croatian- and Chilote-descended families of Porvenir**: compare family memory of the gold rush with written histories.
- **Readers in Aysén and Santa Cruz towns without a local paper**: check where news comes from instead.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial and travel accounts; mixed for trust surveys (national samples); thin for Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Gününa küne ways of vouching for a claim.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun to read *nütram* in full; eighteenth-century English for Byron; Welsh for the handbook.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some *nütram* are kept within a *lof*; conspiracy material about Jewish communities must be studied, not repeated; the "Conquest of the Desert", the "Pacification of the Araucanía", the Selk'nam killings and the 1972 killings are told in rival accounts, each to be presented as an account.
- **Held for review:** the rules of evidence in court and the oath were considered and moved: the letter of those rules to Libra, the trust placed in courts to Capricorn.
