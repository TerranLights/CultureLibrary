# Araucanía-Patagonia: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; Villarrica's and Temuco's mission parishes, the mythology country of Chiloé, the Lutheran confirmation classes of Frutillar and Puerto Varas, the steppe of Río Negro and Chubut, the skies over Bariloche and the western channels of the Kawésqar. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Chilean and Chilote Spanish; Patagonian Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar and Aonekko ʼaʼien through their programs. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenteche of Temuco's countryside**: check which beliefs families say they share with the Catholic or evangelical church next door, and which they keep apart.
- **Lafkenche of Puerto Saavedra and Lago Budi**: ask what is believed about the *ngen* of the lake and coast, as communities choose to share it.
- **Williche of Chiloé and the Osorno coast**: compare who still holds the island beliefs and who tells them as tales.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: set the 1921 teachers' reports beside today's community accounts.
- **Aónikenk descendants of Santa Cruz**: trace what the Lehmann-Nitsche recordings hold of their grandparents' beliefs, with the families' leave.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Puerto Natales; Yagán of Villa Ukika**: note how few sources exist and look first to the communities' own books.
- **German Lutherans of Llanquihue**: compare confirmation rolls across three generations.
- **Welsh chapel families of the Chubut valley**: map which denomination each family kept.
- **Croatian Catholics of Punta Arenas and Lebanese Orthodox of Neuquén**: check what the parish taught and what grandmothers added.
- **Bariloche residents and visitors**: separate what locals hold of the UFO and lake lore from what is sold to tourists.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Chilote folklore and colonial accounts; mixed for surveys; thin for Kawésqar, Aónikenk and Gününa küne belief except through outsiders.
- **Languages needed:** Italian or Spanish translation for Molina; German for parish and cylinder catalogs; Welsh for chapel minutes; Afrikaans for catechism papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *machi* knowledge and the *ngen* of named places may be restricted; sorcery beliefs harm the accused, so read them as accounts; the Selk'nam *Hain* spirits belong to their people.
- **Held for review:** the cylinder recordings were weighed as restricted ceremonial sound and kept, on condition that descendants are asked before any use; no pointer was set aside under the standing rules.
