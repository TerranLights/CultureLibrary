# Araucanía-Patagonia: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the old Purén frontier, Malleco's burned chapels, Viedma's cathedral, the oil pads of Añelo, the Croatian halls of Punta Arenas and the fjords of the Kawésqar reserve. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both slopes (with Chilean and Argentine political slang); Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar through community statements. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content (a finding of guilt is not this concept).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Purén and Lumaco**: look for how the old frontier stands (Elicura, Curalaba) are cited in today's speeches, and by whom.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: check which actions local leaders defend as principle, and which neighbors oppose on principle.
- **Mapuche of Añelo and the Neuquén oil basin**: weigh Campo Maripe's statements against those of Mapuche families who took oil jobs.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa**: see how conviction is expressed when a sacred site faces a project.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Natales and Puerto Edén**: trace who speaks for the campaign and who stays out.
- **Catholics of Viedma and Neuquén**: compare memories of Hesayne with memories of other bishops of the period.
- **Evangelical congregations of Ercilla and Collipulli**: record how pastors describe staying after an attack.
- **Croatian families of Punta Arenas**: find which families gave to the wartime committees and which held back.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento and Comodoro**: test whether "bitter-ender" ancestry is claimed, and when.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman**: check where chapel principle (Sabbath, temperance) still guides a household choice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church and protest records; mixed for surveys of conviction; thin for Kawésqar and Aónikenk voices.
- **Languages needed:** Latin and colonial Spanish for Jesuit reports; Croatian for émigré papers; Afrikaans for family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** arson and armed groups' statements involve trials and victims on several sides; the "Pacification of the Araucanía" and the "Conquest of the Desert" frame many speeches and are contested; objector lists name individuals and must be cited by hospital only.
- **Held for review:** a pointer to a jailed leader's manifestos was weighed and dropped, since it would profile a movement through one man and his case is in the courts; the objection statute goes to Libra.
