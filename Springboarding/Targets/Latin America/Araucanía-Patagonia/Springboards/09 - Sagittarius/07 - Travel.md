# Araucanía-Patagonia: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the Andean cattle passes, the Santa Cruz shearing circuit, the Puerto Montt-Punta Arenas sea lane, Isla Pavón and the Antarctic docks of Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both slopes and Chilote Spanish; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Arabic family terms where settler records use them; Yagán and Kawésqar canoe words through community teachers. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Icalma**: find out whether summer trips over the passes are told as travel or as staying within one land.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén's Andean valleys**: compare stories of relatives on the Chilean side and of journeys to see them.
- **Chilote families of Quinchao and Quellón**: trace which family members went to Argentina, for how long, and who stayed home.
- **Aónikenk descendants**: set their family accounts of the old routes beside Musters's and the archaeologists'.
- **Yagán of Villa Ukika and Kawésqar of Puerto Edén**: compare which canoe routes each community names and wants marked.
- **Welsh-descended families of the Chubut**: compare the 1885 trek west with today's tourism to Trevelin.
- **German-descended families of Frutillar and Puerto Varas**: look at who goes back to Germany, for study or for roots.
- **Croatian families of Punta Arenas and Porvenir**: see which generation first visited Brač and what they reported.
- **Lebanese-descended families of Neuquén and Comodoro**: compare peddling routes with later shop towns.
- **Oil and Antarctic workers of Comodoro and Ushuaia**: record how long rotations away shape family life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for explorers' and settlers' journeys; mixed for tourism data (two states' surveys); thin for Kawésqar, Yagán and Aónikenk travel in their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Spanish; Welsh diaries; Croatian letters; Afrikaans; some Arabic for family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** explorers' and missionaries' journeys are honored by some and contested by others ("Conquest of the Desert" campaigns were also "expeditions"); private family letters need leave.
- **Held for review:** tourism and immigration statutes, and the treaties that set the passes, came up here; Libra takes what they say, Capricorn why the passes are policed, so only the journeys stay here.
