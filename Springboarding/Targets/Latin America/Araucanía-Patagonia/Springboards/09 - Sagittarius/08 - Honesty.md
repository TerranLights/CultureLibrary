# Araucanía-Patagonia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the salmon pens of Magallanes and Aysén, the free-trade zone of Punta Arenas and the Fuegian border posts, the hake grounds off Los Lagos, Bariloche's and El Calafate's tourist trade, and the shearing sheds where work was hired on a man's word. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both slopes, with Chilean and Argentine slang; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán and Kawésqar through their communities. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenteche families near Temuco**: compare the *gülam* on lying with what children hear at an intercultural school.
- **Lafkenche fishers of Puerto Saavedra**: check how they speak of catch declarations against how the industrial fleet does.
- **Williche and Chilote fishers of Quellón and Calbuco**: set inspectors' reports beside fishers' own explanations of under-reporting.
- **Salmon-plant workers in Puerto Natales and Puerto Aysén**: listen for whether falsified data is spoken of openly, and the risk in saying so.
- **Chilote shearers in Santa Cruz**: find what a broken word cost a shearer or a foreman in their own telling.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Trelew**: see how the cooperative's end is told by different families.
- **German-descended dairy farmers of Osorno**: test whether a handshake sale still binds, and when lawyers step in.
- **Croatian merchant families of Punta Arenas**: look for ledgers and credit notes showing how far a customer's word was trusted.
- **Yagán of Villa Ukika and Kawésqar of Puerto Natales**: record how they tell the old canoe trade, beside the sealers' logs.
- **Hosts and guides of Bariloche and El Calafate**: compare their account of fair pricing with visitors'.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press and enforcement records; mixed for surveys; thin for honesty in Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Gününa küne terms.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for *gülam*; Welsh for cooperative papers; nineteenth-century English for sealers' logs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** company cases may be in court or settled; naming fishers or workers who admit to false reports can harm them; "*precio gringo*" complaints can carry national prejudice and must be read as claims.
- **Held for review:** a pointer on alleged corruption in one Patagonian province's government was considered and set aside because the cases are partisan and in court; tax and customs law go to Libra.
