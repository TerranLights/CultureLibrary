# Araucanía-Patagonia: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; the colonial diocese of Concepción's southern parishes, the Kingdom Halls and neo-Pentecostal temples of Temuco, Osorno and Neuquén, the Boer congregation of Comodoro Rivadavia, and the salmon and dam disputes of Aysén and Los Lagos. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of both states, with church slang; Mapudungun; Welsh; German; Afrikaans; Croatian where parish papers need it. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenteche of Padre Las Casas and Cholchol**: compare how a Witness or Pentecostal family and a *ngillatun*-hosting family each describe the line between their faith and the other's.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: check whether calls to restore "pure" rites come from elders, young leaders or both.
- **Williche of Chiloé**: see whether chapel *fiscales* resist or welcome stricter Catholic or evangelical rules.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: weigh how the *kamaruko* revival talks of Christian elements, against Chilean-side debates.
- **Welsh-descended chapel families of Gaiman and Trevelin**: look for who still keeps a strict Sunday, and how others speak of them.
- **German-descended Lutherans of Osorno and Puerto Varas**: compare sermons or parish letters on Colonia Dignidad across decades.
- **Boer-descended families of Sarmiento and Comodoro**: test what church discipline meant when no minister lived nearby.
- **Neo-Pentecostal converts in Temuco's and Neuquén's outer neighborhoods**: record what members say they left behind.
- **Salmon workers and environmental activists of Aysén**: find who uses "fundamentalist" of whom in the same dispute.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press and church records; mixed for surveys (no strictness item with southern cuts); thin for small groups' own writings.
- **Languages needed:** colonial church Spanish and Latin; Afrikaans and Dutch; German for Lutheran and colony papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "sect" and "fundamentalist" are labels with harm attached; Colonia Dignidad involves crimes and victims; Mapuche restoration debates are internal and must be cited in participants' words; leavers may face shunning if named.
- **Held for review:** a pointer to a list of "dangerous sects" from an anti-cult group was set aside, since it profiles small faiths through their opponents; the evangelical caucus's bills go to Libra, and the place of religion in public law to Capricorn.
