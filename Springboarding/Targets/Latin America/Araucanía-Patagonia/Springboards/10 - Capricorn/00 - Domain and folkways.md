# Araucanía-Patagonia: The domain: authority and duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Araucanía, Los Ríos, Los Lagos with Chiloé, Aysén and Magallanes; Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz and both halves of Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** southern Chilean, Chilote and Patagonian Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Gününa küne through revitalization programs. **Concept in one line:** who commands, who obeys, and what is owed: career, status and reputation, with the folkways of age, time, rank, order and power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison) and Nagche of Lumaco**: check whether coastal and lowland *lof* name the same offices in the same order.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Wenteche of Padre Las Casas**: compare how elders' standing is spoken of among summer-pasture families and farm families.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa and Chiloé**: the councils of *caciques* keep their own records of office.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Río Negro**: set *lof* statements beside provincial lists of community officers.
- **Aónikenk of Santa Cruz and Chubut**: travelers' and army papers plus family memory; sources are thin.
- **Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: outsiders wrote them off; check who each community now names to speak for it.
- **Chilote families**: compare what is owed to kin and chapel with what is owed to employers in Aysén and Argentina.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Trevelin; German-, Croatian-, Boer-, Lebanese-, Italian-, Basque- and Spanish-descended families**: note which clubs and firms gave rank.
- **Chilean migrants, oil and sheep-station workers**: compare union seniority with company grades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for frontier records and labor surveys; mixed for attitude surveys, which are national; thin for Aónikenk, Kawésqar and Gününa küne ideas of authority. For this sign of authority and duty, the author has yet to settle the nation's lands; the frame above is a working one, with Alto Biobío, La Pampa, southern Buenos Aires and Mendoza, and the Falklands / Malvinas as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun across alphabets for titles of office; Welsh for chapel posts; Victorian English for Musters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disputes over who is a rightful *lonko* are live; give each side's account.
- **Held for review:** the founding statute of the national territories was weighed; its wording goes to Libra, while the experience of distant rule stays here.
