# Araucanía-Patagonia: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; from the *lof* of Malleco and Cautín to the naval posts of the Beagle Channel. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar and Yagán through their programs. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche *lof* of Malleco and Cautín**: check where an elected community president now outranks a *lonko* in dealings with the state, and where not.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay, with Alto Biobío as comparison**: ask how the Ralco dispute is remembered as a test of whose word counted.
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison) and Puerto Saavedra**: trace who signed coastal claims for each *lof*.
- **Williche councils of Osorno and Chiloé**: compare the Junta's offices with Chiloé's councils of *caciques*.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Chubut**: compare co-management boards with federal forces' presence in land disputes.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Yagán of Puerto Williams and Ushuaia**: check which permits and harbor rules families name when they speak of the state.
- **Welsh chapels of Gaiman and Trevelin; Lutheran and Catholic parishes of Llanquihue**: compare a deacon's say with a visiting pastor's, and confirmation teaching across generations.
- **Croatian clubs of Porvenir; Boer families of Sarmiento**: note who spoke for the group to the state.
- **Oil-camp families of Comodoro and Plaza Huincul**: compare managers' say before and after YPF's 1990s sale.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Mapuche political history; mixed for opinion data (more southern samples in Chile than Argentina); thin for Kawésqar and Yagán dealings with the Navy in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for titles of office and *machi* speech; colonial Spanish for *parlamento* records; German for catechisms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Villa Mascardi and Cushamen events, and disputes over a *lonko*'s right to speak, are live and grieved; *machi* knowledge has limits that outsiders must respect.
- **Held for review:** the permission-share and correctional-control figures rest on the law in force; those statutes were weighed and passed to Libra, keeping here only what they mean for who obeys whom.
