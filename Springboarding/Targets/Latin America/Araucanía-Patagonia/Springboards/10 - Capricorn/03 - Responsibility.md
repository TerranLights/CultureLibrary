# Araucanía-Patagonia: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; from the lower Chubut valley's canals and Añelo's oilfield pits to Villa Santa Lucía and the forestry estates of Malleco. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *responsabilidad*; *hacerse cargo*; *rendir cuentas* | being answerable; to take charge of someone or something; to give account | in the south try "hacerse cargo de los nietos Chiloé", "rendición de cuentas municipio Temuco", "responsabilidad empresa salmonera" |
| Spanish | *dar la cara*; *lavarse las manos* | to face up; to wash one's hands of it | press phrases after disasters and spills: which one officials are accused of |
| Spanish (Argentina) | *pasivo ambiental* | environmental liability left behind | the term for old oil pits and mine tailings in Neuquén and Santa Cruz |
| Mapudungun | *itrofill mongen* | all of life together, the whole web of living things | used in Mapuche writing on care for the land; *check* spelling and age |
| Mapudungun | *ngen* | owner or keeper of a place (water, forest, hill) | here: to whom a person answers when taking from a place |
| Welsh | *cyfrifoldeb*; *atebol* | responsibility; answerable | canal and chapel minutes in the lower valley |
| German | *Verantwortung*; *Haftung* | responsibility; liability | colony and company papers of Llanquihue |
| Croatian; Afrikaans | *odgovornost*; *verantwoordelikheid* | responsibility, in each language | mutual-aid and congregation minutes, *check* |

English keywords: accountability Patagonia provinces; disaster blame southern Chile; oil waste Neuquén; forestry certification Mapuche; maritime accident inquiry Chile.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Ercilla and Collipulli beside forestry estates**: check what audit files and community statements each say the firm owes.
- **Lafkenche fishers of Queule and Mehuín**: compare who is held answerable for harm to the sea.
- **Pewenche of Icalma and Lonquimay**: look at herders' accounts of who answers for animals in the high pastures.
- **Mapuche *lof* of Añelo and Rincón de los Sauces**: compare community and company statements on waste pits.
- **Chilote families of Quellón and Castro**: check how a skipper's family speaks after a loss at sea.
- **Aysén settler families of Villa Santa Lucía and Chaitén**: compare blame in two disasters a decade apart.
- **Welsh-descended farmers of Dolavon and Gaiman**: read canal minutes for fines on those who failed to clear their stretch.
- **German-descended families of Frutillar and Puerto Octay**: compare the agent's memoir with settler letters.
- **Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: check who took charge of newcomers and orphans.
- **Bariloche guides and Chaltén climbers**: compare rules about who pays for a rescue.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for audits and disaster inquiries; mixed for corporate files (some open, some not); thin for Aónikenk and Kawésqar ideas of answerability in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for *residencia* files; Welsh for canal minutes; German for colony letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** families of the dead in disasters and at sea; living officials named in inquiries; the land commission is contested by every side.
- **Held for review:** press pieces that laid the Comarca Andina fires (2021) on a Mapuche group were weighed and left out, since they cast a people as culprits without findings.
