# Araucanía-Patagonia: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; from the colonial frontier garrisons and Salesian classrooms in Viedma and Punta Arenas to mountain-troop barracks at Bariloche and horse corrals on the Santa Cruz steppe. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself.

## 1. Search terms
| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spanish | *disciplina*; *portarse bien*; *mano dura* | training to a standard; to behave; a firm, harsh hand | in the south try "disciplina escolar Temuco", "mano dura Araucanía" (crime talk) and "disciplina militar Bariloche" to separate home, school and state |
| Spanish (Argentina) | *penitencia*; *amonestación* | a child's grounding; a formal school reprimand | *penitencia* is the household word; *amonestaciones* were replaced by school agreements in many provinces, *check* Patagonia |
| Spanish (Chile) | *correazo*; *malcriado* | a blow with a belt; badly brought up | how parents and grandparents in Chiloé and Temuco speak of correction then and now |
| Mapudungun | *ngülamtun* | to give counsel, the formal advice of elders to the young | discipline by spoken counsel rather than blows; *check* form |
| Mapudungun | *palin* | the stick-and-ball game played between *lof* | training, rules and restraint on the field |
| Welsh | *hunanddisgyblaeth*; *dirwest* | self-discipline; temperance | Band of Hope (*Gobeithlu*) pledges for children, *check* in Chubut |
| German | *Zucht*; *Turnen* | strict upbringing; gymnastics as training | club and school records in Valdivia and Osorno |
| Croatian; Afrikaans | *stega*; *tug*, *selfbeheersing* | discipline; church discipline, self-control | Punta Arenas schools; Boer congregation books, *check* |

English keywords: Salesian preventive system Patagonia; school discipline Chile regions; horse gentling Patagonia; frontier army Chile colonial; Antuco tragedy.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenteche families in Temuco schools and Nagche families in Purén**: compare counsel at home with school sanctions.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: check how *palin* matches are trained for and refereed between *lof*.
- **Williche of Chiloé and San Juan de la Costa**: look at chapel and school records for the punishments older people remember.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: compare provincial school codes with community counsel.
- **Yagán families of Ukika and Ushuaia**: ask what training of the young is shared now, and what stays with the community.
- **Former Salesian pupils of Viedma, Punta Arenas and Río Grande**: set their memories beside the order's own account.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman; German-descended families of Osorno**: compare chapel pledges with gymnastics clubs as schools of habit.
- **Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas; Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: check church and school rules kept in the home.
- **Shearers and horse breakers of Santa Cruz and Chubut**: compare how a new hand is trained.
- **Conscripts from Araucanía and Patagonian towns**: compare service memories across decades.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military and school records; mixed for household correction (surveys exist, southern cuts uncertain); thin for Aónikenk and Kawésqar training of the young in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for garrison papers; Welsh for chapel youth records; Italian for Salesian house chronicles; Afrikaans for Boer church books.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in boarding schools and in military service is told by survivors first; Yagán and Selk'nam initiation knowledge is not to be pointed at beyond what the communities share.
- **Held for review:** a child-rearing guide that ranks whole peoples by how strictly they raise children was weighed and dropped, since it profiles groups rather than pointing to evidence.
