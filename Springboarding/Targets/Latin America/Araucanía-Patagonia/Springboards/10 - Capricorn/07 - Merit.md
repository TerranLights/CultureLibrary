# Araucanía-Patagonia: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; from the bardic chair at Trelew and the shearing boards of Río Mayo to the entrance lists of Temuco's universities and the boatyards of Chiloé. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (southern Chile, Chiloé, Argentine Patagonia); Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

English keywords: meritocracy Chile survey; civil service merit Chile; provincial public employment Patagonia; Indigenous admission universities Chile; eisteddfod chair adjudication.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mapuche students from Cautín and Malleco entering Temuco universities**: compare those admitted by test score with those admitted on Indigenous places, and how classmates speak of each.
- **Lafkenche families of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison)**: check whether local posts in health and schooling go by contest or by community proposal.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa**: look at how council titles pass, by inheritance or by proving oneself.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: compare provincial teacher boards with community choices for intercultural teachers.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán**: few formal records; ask how a canoe-builder or weaver earns the community's recognition now.
- **Chilote shipwrights' families**: compare sons who learned in the yard with outsiders who trained elsewhere.
- **Welsh-descended poets of Trelew and Trevelin**: count chairs won by valley poets and by visitors from Wales.
- **German-descended families of the Llanquihue colleges**: check prize rolls for family names over generations.
- **Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas**: check whether club honors follow service or wealth.
- **Shearers and oil workers of Chubut and Santa Cruz**: compare contest winners and union rank lists.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for test scores and civil-service contests in Chile; mixed for Argentine provinces (payrolls disputed); thin for merit among Aónikenk and Fuegian peoples in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** sixteenth-century Spanish for *probanzas*; Welsh for adjudications; Mapudungun for school terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous admission places draw hostile comment; describe the arguments, never repeat slurs.
- **Held for review:** press exposés that name individual provincial employees as *ñoquis* were weighed; they accuse living people one by one, so only payroll studies in aggregate are pointed to.
