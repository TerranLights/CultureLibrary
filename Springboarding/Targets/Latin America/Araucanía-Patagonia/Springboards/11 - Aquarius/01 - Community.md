# Araucanía-Patagonia: Community (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche country of both slopes, the lake and island south, the fjords, the steppe and the Fuegian islands. **Language(s) for searching:** rural and town Spanish of the Araucanía, the lakes, the islands and the Argentine valleys and steppe; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien and Gününa küne through their communities. **Concept in one line:** the bond among people who share a life (a place, a faith, a trade, an origin, a cause), know one another as members and owe one another something, and how that bond feels.

English keywords: Mapuche community life; reduction communities Chile; Chiloé mutual help; Welsh Patagonia community; company towns Patagonia.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Lumaco and Purén**: check what a reduction community owes a member who leaves to work in Santiago and comes back.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and Alto Biobío (comparison)**: compare summer and winter settlements as two settings of one community.
- **Williche of San Juan de la Costa**: check which coastal communities still hold work parties, and how payment is reckoned.
- **Mapuche of Bariloche's Alto and Neuquén's west side**: compare urban neighborhood bodies with *lof* membership.
- **Aónikenk of Camusu Aike; Kawésqar of Puerto Edén; Yagán of Villa Ukika**: small communities; look for what each has published itself before using outsiders.
- **Chilote towns and islands**: compare islands with a chapel and a school against islands that lost both.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Dolavon**: check which halls and chapels still gather people weekly.
- **German-descended families of Frutillar and Nueva Braunau; Croatian-descended families of Porvenir; Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: compare when each stopped marrying only within the community.
- **Oil and gas workers of Añelo and Rincón de los Sauces**: newcomer camps with little shared life; check what forms there.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for settler villages and disasters; mixed for urban neighborhood data; thin for Fuegian and steppe communities' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for unity and work-party words; 16th-c. Spanish for Vivar; German for Treutler's original, *check*; Welsh for hall and chapel records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** community registration ties into land claims; some *lof* choose not to be counted.
- **Held for review:** for the registration dates, the founding statute of 1993 and Argentina's community-registration law were considered; their texts belong to Libra, and only the counts by year stay here.
