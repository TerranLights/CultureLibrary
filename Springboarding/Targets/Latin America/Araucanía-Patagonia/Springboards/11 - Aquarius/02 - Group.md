# Araucanía-Patagonia: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu across the cordillera, the Chilote archipelago, the fjords and the steppe to the Beagle Channel. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the frontier towns, the islands, the oil basins and the sheep country; Mapudungun (Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche speech) and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien as their communities now teach them. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen, as a unit: how the group is weighed against the person, where "us" ends, and how members are expected to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche of Tirúa (Biobío edge, comparison) and Nagche of Ercilla**: check whether coastal and lowland communities answer an organization's call the same way, using recent *trawün* records.
- **Pewenche of Quinquén and Lonquimay**: compare decisions taken together on pine-nut gathering with decisions on outside projects.
- **Williche councils of Osorno and Chiloé**: check how the two councils split or act together, in their minutes.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Chubut**: compare provincial confederations with *lof* that act alone.
- **Aónikenk, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: few members and scattered; check how each forms a group voice, and from which documents.
- **Chilote fishers and salmon workers**: check union and island-assembly records for who joins blockades.
- **Welsh-, German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: check which clubs split over war, politics or church, and why.
- **Oil and health workers of Neuquén; sheep-station crews of Santa Cruz**: compare union-led and self-called actions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, protests and organizations; mixed for experiments, which are few and old; thin for small Fuegian and steppe groups.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun person-marking; colonial Spanish for Góngora; Welsh for Gorsedd papers; Afrikaans only for family papers, if any.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the CAM, RAM and police cases are live and contested; never let an organization stand for a people.
- **Held for review:** the anti-terrorism prosecutions were weighed as a source on collective blame; the statute's text and the rulings go to Libra and their wider legal culture to Capricorn, while press and community accounts of blame stay here.
