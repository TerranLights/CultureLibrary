# Araucanía-Patagonia: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the old frontier of the Araucanía, Valdivia and the lakes, Chiloé, Aysén and Magallanes; Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean south and of Patagonia, Chilote Spanish; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam through revitalization. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche of Malleco and Wenteche of Cautín**: compare communities that joined fence-moving in 1970-73 with those that waited for the state's title, in oral history.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay; Alto Biobío (comparison)**: check how consultations on roads and dams were received.
- **Williche of Chiloé and Osorno**: check the coastal-space claims and fishers' protests as one record or two.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén**: compare provincial health and planning reforms as remembered in *lof* and in provincial histories.
- **Aónikenk and Gününa küne descendants**: look for who leads revival classes and what they say about earlier "civilizing" programs.
- **Kawésqar and Yagán**: compare language programs run by communities with those run by schools.
- **Chilote fishers and Magallanes station workers**: compare who gained a lot or a quota under each reform.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check what the valley built for itself before the state arrived.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue; Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas**: compare how each met the agrarian reform on its own farms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for agrarian reform and Neuquén's planning; mixed for consultation records; thin for revival programs' own reports.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-c. Spanish for the *guerra defensiva*; Mapudungun for return words; Welsh for the school's records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1970-73 recoveries and their undoing involved violence and are still argued; give each account.
- **Held for review:** what the 2012 fisheries law and the consultation rules say was weighed and left to Libra, and what they are meant to do to Capricorn; only the campaigns and answers stay here.
