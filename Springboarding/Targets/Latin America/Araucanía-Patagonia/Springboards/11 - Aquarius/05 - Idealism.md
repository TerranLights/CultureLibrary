# Araucanía-Patagonia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu on both slopes, the Chilean lakes and Chiloé, the Aysén fjords, the Magallanes and Fuegian south, and the Argentine provinces from Neuquén to Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean lakes and islands and of the Andean and coastal towns of Argentine Patagonia; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Aonekko ʼaʼien, Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Gününa küne through revitalization. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche of the Budi coast, with Tirúa (Biobío edge) as comparison**: check how coastal organizations state their ideal of the good life.
- **Wenteche of Temuco**: compare urban autonomist writing with rural *lof* statements on the same ideal.
- **Mapuche of Nahuel Huapi and Villa La Angostura**: check how families living in the park tell the story of its founding gift.
- **Aónikenk, Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam descendants**: outsiders wrote them off and then idealized them; check how each community answers romantic images in tourism and film.
- **Chilote islanders**: compare visiting students' and volunteers' ideals with what islanders ask of them.
- **Welsh-descended families**: check whether the founders' ideal is told as achieved, betrayed or unfinished.
- **German-descended families of Valdivia and Llanquihue; Croatian-descended families of Punta Arenas; Boer-descended families of Sarmiento**: compare the reasons each gives for having come south.
- **Alternative settlers of El Bolsón and Lago Puelo**: compare founding settlers with later arrivals over what the town was meant to be.
- **Anarchist and socialist workers of Santa Cruz and Magallanes**: their own papers first, then later histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Welsh colony and the 1920s labor press; mixed for alternative communities; thin for idealism in Fuegian and steppe peoples' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Welsh for Eluned Morgan; French for Saint-Exupéry; Mapudungun for autonomist texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** romantic images of Native peoples are outsiders' stereotypes and are named as such; autonomist programs are debated among Mapuche themselves.
- **Held for review:** a commercial tourism text that sells Patagonia as an empty "end of the world" was weighed and dropped as a pointer, since it would make an outsiders' idealization the frame.
