# Araucanía-Patagonia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: the Mapuche lands of both slopes, Los Ríos, Los Lagos and Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes, and the Argentine south from Neuquén and Río Negro to Chubut, Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego. **Language(s) for searching:** everyday Spanish of southern Chile and of Argentine Patagonia, with Chilote speech; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán, Kawésqar, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien through their communities. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lafkenche fishers of Puerto Saavedra**: check whether friendships between Mapuche and non-Mapuche boat crews show in shared catches or in *compadrazgo*.
- **Pewenche of Icalma**: compare friendships with Argentine Pewenche across the pass, through family accounts.
- **Williche of Chiloé**: check whether "friend" and "*compadre*" are separate roles or one, in baptism books.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén's cities**: compare friendship networks at school with those at the *lof*, using ELRI's Argentine counterparts if any.
- **Selk'nam descendants of Tierra del Fuego; Yagán of Puerto Williams; Kawésqar of Puerto Natales**: check how each community remembers outsiders who were called friends, against what the outsiders wrote.
- **Aónikenk descendants**: look for travelers' named companions in their families' own tellings.
- **Welsh-descended families of Trevelin and Gaiman**: compare stories of Tehuelche friends with stories of Welsh friends.
- **Croatian-descended families of Porvenir**: check whether *kum* sponsorship still joins families who are not kin.
- **Oil workers and Chilean migrants of Neuquén**: compare friend groups made at work with those from home towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich in memoirs and folklore, thin in measured data outside the Araucanía panel.
- **Languages needed:** 17th-c. Spanish for Pineda; Mapudungun for ritual-friend words; Welsh for letters; Croatian for sponsor ties.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** settlers' accounts of Native "friends" can hide unequal ties; read them beside descendants' words.
- **Held for review:** private letters between friends in Welsh and German families were weighed; they stay unnamed as pointers until families agree to their use.
