# Araucanía-Patagonia: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America; frame: Wallmapu across the Andes, the Chilean south from the Araucanía to Magallanes with Chiloé, and the Argentine Patagonian provinces down to Ushuaia. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the Chilean south, Chiloé and Patagonia; Mapudungun and *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán, Kawésqar, Selk'nam, Aonekko ʼaʼien and Gününa küne through community programs. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wenteche and Nagche communities**: compare *ngillatun* petitions recorded in 1910 with those communities now publish, if they choose to.
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay**: check what hopes for the pine-nut harvest and for roads appear in local radio and press.
- **Williche of Carelmapu and Maullín**: compare vows at the Candelaria with Williche councils' own statements of hope.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén's oil country**: compare hopes tied to royalties and jobs with hopes tied to land, in *lof* statements.
- **Aónikenk and Mapuche families of Chubut**: check how the restitution of remains is told by families, and by the museums.
- **Yagán, Kawésqar and Selk'nam communities**: check what each names as its hope in language and land programs, from its own publications.
- **Chilote migrants and their children**: compare letters home with what they told their children about the south.
- **Welsh-descended families**: compare the promise in early letters with later family memories.
- **German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: check each group's founding hopes in anniversary books and how later generations retell them.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Chilean migrants; oil and assembly-plant workers**: compare hopes at arrival with hopes ten years on.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for migration and boom towns; thin for Indigenous hopes in their own published words outside Mapudungun texts.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for Augusta's prayers; Welsh for letters; Croatian for gold-rush family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restitution and the search for the disappeared involve living families' grief; some prayers belong to the community and may not be for wider use.
- **Held for review:** for the lottery data, studies that link gambling to particular ethnic groups were weighed and left out, as they would turn a market into a profile of a people.
