# Araucanía-Patagonia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish as heard in Temuco, Valdivia, Castro, Coyhaique, Neuquén, Trelew and Río Gallegos; Mapudungun with its Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche and Wenteche forms; Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Kawésqar, Yagán, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien in revival. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

| Spanish (Chile) | *achunchar*; *pasar vergüenza ajena* | to make someone feel awkward; to feel shame on another's behalf | *vergüenza ajena* is felt empathy at its sharpest, *check* southern use against Argentine *me da cosa* |

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nagche and Wenteche communities of Malleco and Cautín**: compare CEP's Mapuche sample with community statements from the same communes.
- **Lafkenche of Puerto Saavedra and Toltén**: check how converts' *testimonio* is received where Pentecostal and Mapuche life overlap.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén capital and the Ruka Choroy and Aluminé valleys**: test Comahue's samples against rural and urban Mapuche settings.
- **Williche of Chiloé and Osorno**: set wake customs beside Williche accounts of mourning together.
- **Kawésqar of Puerto Edén and Puerto Natales**: find whether any Kawésqar response to ferry visitors is published.
- **Yagán of Puerto Williams and Selk'nam descendants in Tierra del Fuego**: compare school projects on the two sides of the Beagle Channel.
- **Welsh-descended families of Gaiman and Trevelin**: ask how Welsh visitors are received in the valley against the Andes.
- **German-descended families of Llanquihue**: look for German-school teaching of *Einfühlung* against the Chilean curriculum.
- **Croatian-descended families of Magallanes**: check club bulletins for condolence customs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; health-education and psychology studies give measured empathy, while cross-group empathy lives mostly in film, testimony and survey items on how each group sees the other.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for Lenz's texts; colonial Spanish for frontier records; Welsh for condolence notices; Kawésqar and Yagán through living programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Selk'nam killings and Mapuche land conflicts are contested ground; present each film or survey as one account. Converts' testimonies are personal; describe the practice, not the people.
- **Held for review:** a pop-psychology book on "empaths" sold in Bariloche's wellness shops was considered as a sign of the trade and left out, since it says nothing about this nation's own understanding.
