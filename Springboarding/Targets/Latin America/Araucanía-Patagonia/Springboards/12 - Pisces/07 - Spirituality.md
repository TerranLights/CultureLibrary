# Araucanía-Patagonia: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Araucanía-Patagonia, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of the south, and the devotional Spanish of Chiloé's chapels; Mapudungun (Lafkenche, Pewenche, Nagche, Wenteche) and Williche *tsesungun*; Welsh; German; Croatian; Afrikaans; Yagán, Kawésqar, Selk'nam and Aonekko ʼaʼien, as their communities choose to share. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (the experience, the practice, the sacred outside the church; institutions belong to Sagittarius).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pewenche of Lonquimay and the Neuquén *pewen* country**: compare what each says of the tree in protection debates.
- **Lafkenche of the coast**: check whether sea and shore sites appear in their sacred accounts.
- **Wenteche and Nagche communities**: set *We Tripantu* in a *lof* beside its staging in Temuco.
- **Williche of Chiloé and San Juan de la Costa**: compare chapel devotion with Williche ceremonial life.
- **Mapuche of Neuquén and Chubut**: look at *We Tripantu* on the eastern slope, and who leads it.
- **Yagán of Puerto Williams and Ushuaia**: ask how descendants regard the published *Kina*.
- **Chilote families**: compare *fiscales*' devotions on small islands with town parishes.
- **Welsh-descended families of the valley and the Andes**: trace home worship across generations.
- **German-, Croatian- and Boer-descended families**: compare household devotion in three settler streams.
- **Incomers to El Bolsón and Lago Puelo**: check how they describe the land's sacredness against local people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for lay Catholic devotion and Chiloé; mixed for Mapuche practice (much in Mapuche-authored sources, some restricted); thin for Kawésqar, Yagán and Selk'nam spirituality in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Mapudungun for ceremonial terms; Welsh for memoirs; German and Croatian for family papers; Yagán with descendants.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *machi* practice, *Kina* and *Hain*, and some *ngillatun* parts are not for outsiders; New Age borrowing of Mapuche symbols is a live grievance, so report it as a dispute, not a finding.
- **Held for review:** two categories of pointer were considered for spirituality outside the churches and set aside: South Asian-derived meditation and body-practice centers in the nation's lands, under the standing rule; and Buddhist communities, held for the author's open question.
