# Argentina (without Patagonia): The domain: self and identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America; the core is Argentina north of Patagonia. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish (with *lunfardo*) and the Spanish of the interior; Guaraní; Santiago del Estero Quichua; Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá; Quechua and Aymara; Yiddish; Arabic; Armenian; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** what a self is here and how it shows itself: personhood, the body, appearance, naming ways, and sport ways as a statement of the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city against the Conurbano:** check whether naming, dress and self-labels split by district and class; the essays speak mostly from the capital.
- **Córdoba, Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy, Cuyo, La Pampa:** check provincial self-labels (*cordobés*, *provinciano*) against the porteño one.
- **The Litoral, Misiones and Santiago del Estero:** check Guaraní and Quichua names, nicknames and address forms in daily use.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá and Mbyá:** compare naming rites and body ideas people by people; ethnographies treat them apart.
- **Kolla, Diaguita and Huarpe; Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdeans:** check census self-identification against community records, and against the contested 'disappearance' account.
- **Italian, Spanish, Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian, Volga German, Ukrainian, Polish and Mennonite descent:** check double labels and ethnic nicknames (*tano*, *gallego*, *ruso*, *turco*) as used and as received.
- **Okinawan, Korean, Chinese, Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian families:** check double given names and registry practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buenos Aires and the essays; mixed for the provinces; thin outside ethnography for Pilagá, Moqoit and Huarpe.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Qom for the Chaco and Litoral work; German for Paucke's original; Yiddish for the early colony press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mbyá name-giving is restricted knowledge. Census race categories and the 'disappearance' account are contested; present each as an account. Naming and gender-identity law go to Libra. No lands are settled yet: the sheets cover Argentina north of Patagonia, with Patagonia and the Mapuche lands as comparison only.
- **Held for review:** a personhood pointer from the temple life of some East Asian-heritage families was set aside: Buddhism is held as a category, an open question for the author.
