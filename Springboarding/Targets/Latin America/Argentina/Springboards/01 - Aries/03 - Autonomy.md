## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Provinces against the federal capital:** check how Córdoba, Santa Fe, Tucumán and Cuyo tell their own past self-rule, since provincial histories differ from Buenos Aires ones.
- **Córdoba's university and students elsewhere:** check whether the 1918 tradition shapes student self-rule beyond Córdoba.
- **Workers in recovered firms and cooperatives:** compare decision-making with ordinary workplaces in the Conurbano, Rosario and Santa Fe dairy towns.
- **Rural workers of the Pampa, past and present:** check how the old controls on movement are remembered.
- **Wichí, Qom, Pilagá and Moqoit communities:** check who decides for a community and how assemblies work.
- **Kolla communities of the Puna and the Quebrada de Humahuaca:** check community councils and their records.
- **Mbyá of Misiones:** check what the *tekoa* decides for itself, and what may be shared.
- **Jewish colonies of Entre Ríos and their cooperatives; Mennonites of La Pampa:** check self-governing community bodies and their minutes.
- **Women, young adults and the elderly:** check who decides on marriage, moving out and care, by class and region.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1918, labor and cooperatives; mixed for family decisions outside Buenos Aires; thin for Pilagá and Moqoit assemblies.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for mission letters; Wichí for community statements; Yiddish and Italian for the early labor press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abortion, end-of-life choices and Indigenous land claims are contested; give accounts, and send any law to Libra. Some Mbyá community decisions are not for outsiders. Comparisons with Patagonian self-rule claims stay comparison only.
