## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Salta and Jujuy against Buenos Aires:** check whether independence-era courage is remembered through gauchos and exodus in the North and through generals in the capital.
- **Cuyo:** check how Mendoza and San Juan tell the Andes crossing as their own.
- **Rural Pampa and Córdoba riders:** check what the *jineteada* praises and what it mocks.
- **Football fans of Buenos Aires, Rosario and Córdoba:** check how *aguante* is defined from club to club.
- **Qom, Moqoit, Pilagá and Wichí:** check each people's own words for bravery and endurance, and how testimony is given.
- **Kolla communities:** check the Éxodo story as told in the Quebrada and the Puna.
- **Afro-Argentines:** check how community historians tell soldiers' service.
- **Corrientes devotees and Litoral families:** check Guaraní terms for bravery in songs and *chamamé* lyrics.
- **Veterans and conscripts' families:** check by province, since many came from the North and the Litoral.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Ukrainian communities:** check whose bravery their community histories honor.
