## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires renters against provincial homeowners:** check how *casa propia* is pursued and talked about in each.
- **Santiago del Estero and Chaco peasant families:** check self-provisioning and seasonal migration as two routes to getting by.
- **Qom and Wichí of the Pilcomayo and Bermejo:** check foraging, fishing and wage work in each people's own account.
- **Mbyá of Misiones:** check reciprocity and what is shared outside the community.
- **Kolla herders of the Puna:** check household self-sufficiency in herding and barter.
- **Jewish colonists, Volga Germans and Mennonites of La Pampa:** compare colony self-sufficiency with cooperative dependence.
- **Italian and Spanish mutual-aid societies (Centro Gallego, *mutuo soccorso*):** check whether help from one's own community was felt as independence.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan households:** check how several earners and remittances fit ideas of standing alone.
- **Elders and disabled people:** check who decides between family care, institutions and independent living.
- **Recipients of social programs and their critics:** compare the words each side uses.
