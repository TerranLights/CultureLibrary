## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires clubs against provincial derbies:** check whether city rivalries in Rosario, Córdoba and Tucumán follow class, neighborhood or founding lines.
- **Pampa and Litoral towns:** check rural contests (*pato*, *cuadreras*, *bochas*, *truco*) and their clubs.
- **Cuyo:** check how departments compete through the Vendimia queens.
- **Rugby and polo clubs against neighborhood football clubs:** check class and school ties in each.
- **Women's sport:** check coverage and rewards for women athletes against men's.
- **Students of selective schools and of open-access universities:** compare how they talk about earning a place.
- **Qom, Wichí and Moqoit communities:** check traditional and present games and how a win is treated.
- **Kolla carnival groups of the Quebrada de Humahuaca:** check whether *comparsas* compete, and how.
- **Jewish, Armenian and other community sports clubs:** check how they balance competing with others and keeping members.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan football leagues in Buenos Aires:** check rivalry between home regions inside the migrant leagues.
- **Korean, Chinese and Argentine shopkeepers:** check how each describes business rivalry, from their own accounts.
