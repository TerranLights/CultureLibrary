## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires against the Northwest and the Litoral:** check helmet, seat-belt and drinking figures province by province rather than as one national rate.
- **Young men and young women:** check how risk is split by gender in the youth surveys.
- **Córdoba's student and nightlife scene against small towns:** check drinking customs and their policing.
- **Turf bettors, lottery players and casino patrons:** compare who gambles where, and how each is judged.
- **Middle-class savers and speculators of the 1970s and 2001:** check how each generation tells its losses.
- **Cuyo climbers and guides on Aconcagua:** check what guides call reckless.
- **Qom, Wichí and Mbyá communities:** check their own terms for heedless acts, since data on them come mostly from outside health agencies.
- **Truckers and long-distance drivers:** check devotions and road customs along the national routes.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Korean communities:** check community press on risk at work and on the road.
- **Evangelical and Catholic youth groups:** compare their teaching on drink and risk.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for road, drug and drinking surveys; mixed for gambling; thin for Indigenous communities and for informal street racing.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and *lunfardo* turf and drinking slang; Guaraní for Litoral health work; Korean for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cromañón and other mass-casualty events remain painful and contested; give accounts. School graduation trips go to Bariloche, a Patagonian destination: comparison only. Traffic and gaming law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; harm-reduction and recovery groups were considered for heedless drinking and drug use, and all that came to mind are Argentine or international secular groups.
