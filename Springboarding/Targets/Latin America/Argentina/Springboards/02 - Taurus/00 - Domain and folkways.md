# Argentina (without Patagonia): The domain: possessions and value (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America; written for the provinces north of Patagonia. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish (*voseo*, *lunfardo*) and the Spanish of Córdoba, Cuyo and the Northwest; Guaraní (Corrientes, Misiones); Santiago del Estero Quichua; Quechua and Aymara; Qom and Wichí; Yiddish; Arabic; Ukrainian and Polish (Misiones). **Concept in one line:** what is worth having and how it is held: possessions, money, resources, values, self-worth; food, dress and wealth ways.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city, the Conurbano and the Pampa towns:** check the survey's regional split against ethnographies of poorer districts.
- **Córdoba, Santa Fe and Rosario:** check farm-family budgets and land as wealth against city households.
- **The Northwest (Salta, Jujuy, Tucumán, Catamarca, La Rioja) and Santiago del Estero:** check herd and land as wealth, and Quichua and Quechua words for goods.
- **Cuyo:** check water and vineyard holdings as the core resource.
- **The Litoral, Misiones, the Chaco and Formosa:** check forest extraction, yerba and cotton economies, and Guaraní terms for things and money.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá, Mbyá, Kolla, Diaguita, Huarpe:** compare each people's own account of what is held in common and what is personal; never pool them.
- **Italian, Spanish, Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian, Ukrainian, Polish, Volga German and Mennonite families:** check account books and community savings bodies, group by group.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan, Peruvian, Korean, Chinese and Okinawan communities; Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdeans:** check where each keeps its own records of goods and remittances.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for national surveys and Buenos Aires consumption; mixed for provincial food and dress; thin for the Chaco peoples and the Huarpe.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish throughout; Guaraní and Quechua for words for goods and money; Yiddish and Arabic for household papers of the immigrant era.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** price and poverty statistics of the 2007-2015 period are contested; present each series as one account. Inheritance and property law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing was set aside; the candidates weighed for possessions and value (spending surveys, mission records, cookbooks, company and museum holdings) fell under none of the standing rules.
