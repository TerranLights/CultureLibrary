# Argentina (without Patagonia): Ownership (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (Rioplatense, Cuyo, Córdoba, the Northwest); Guaraní; Quechua; Qom and Wichí community documents; Yiddish; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** the recognized claim to have, use, exclude others from, give or sell a thing, and what that claim rests on; here also the left-right reading by public spending.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pampa owners, renters and *contratistas*:** check how land is held through rental and contracting, not only title.
- **Córdoba, La Rioja and San Luis co-heirs:** check undivided family lands against surveyed private lots.
- **Cuyo's irrigated oases:** check whether water or land is the owned thing that matters.
- **Diaguita, Kolla and Huarpe communities:** check colonial titles and community lands, valley by valley.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá and Mbyá:** check each people's own words for territory against the state's survey categories.
- **Conurbano settlements and *villas* of Buenos Aires, Rosario and Córdoba:** check what counts as one's house when no deed exists.
- **Swiss, Italian and Volga German farm colonists of Santa Fe and Entre Ríos; Jewish Colonization Association settlers; Ukrainian and Polish settlers of Misiones; La Pampa's Mennonites:** check how each acquired land and how colony holdings are shared or split.
- **Bolivian growers who rent plots on city edges:** check renting against buying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for censuses, Pampa history and urban tenure; mixed for colonial grants; uneven for Indigenous lands, where community documents and state surveys disagree.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for Corrientes and Misiones; Qom and Wichí for community statements; Yiddish for colony records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Indigenous land conflicts, occupations and evictions are live disputes; present each side's account. Property law and the statutes on community lands go to Libra; what law is for, to Capricorn. The figures on public spending are collected at Stage 2.
- **Held for review:** none set aside; the left-right data pointers were checked against the no-law rule and kept, since they measure spending, not statute.
