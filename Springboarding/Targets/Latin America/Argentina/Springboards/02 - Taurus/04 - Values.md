# Argentina (without Patagonia): Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish (Rioplatense and provincial); Guaraní (including the Jesuit-era printed Guaraní); Quechua; Qom; Yiddish; Arabic; Ukrainian and Polish. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, how those things are ranked, and how worth is assigned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires against Córdoba, Rosario and Mendoza:** check whether the same value words carry the same weight in each city's press.
- **Northwest towns (Salta, Jujuy, Tucumán, Catamarca):** check Catholic feast life and Andean reciprocity side by side.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní moral vocabulary from the missions to present-day speech.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua sayings in Vidal de Battini and in later collections.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá, Mbyá; Kolla, Diaguita and Huarpe:** check each people's published principles; do not merge them under "Indigenous values".
- **Catholic, Evangelical and Pentecostal, Jewish and Muslim congregations:** check sermons and school programs for how each ranks family, work and charity.
- **Italian-, Spanish- and Syrian-Lebanese-descended families; Ukrainian, Polish and Mennonite settlers:** check parish and community school curricula.
- **Generations:** check the young against their grandparents in survey data, not in essays.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for essays, schoolbooks and sermons; mixed for surveys broken down by province; thin for Indigenous values outside organizations' own statements.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial and modern Guaraní; Quechua; Qom; Yiddish and Arabic for community schools.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Peronist texts, the "viveza criolla" and "mediocrity" essays and the "gente de bien" label are contested; treat each as a claim. Moral debates tied to statutes send the legal text to Libra and the spirit of the law to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the religious pointers (Catholic, Evangelical, Jewish, Muslim) were weighed and none fell under the held categories.
