# Argentina (without Patagonia): Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires middle-class savers against Conurbano informal workers:** check whether security means dollars and deposits or a registered job and a union health fund.
- **Gated suburbs (Pilar, Nordelta, the Córdoba *countries*) against city neighborhoods:** check how walls, guards and cameras are justified on each side.
- **San Juan and Mendoza:** check earthquake memory and household preparation.
- **Northwest and Santiago del Estero:** check seasonal migrants' and small herders' ways of storing food and money.
- **Wichí, Qom, Pilagá and Moqoit of Salta, Chaco and Formosa:** check food and water insecurity as each community reports it, not as an aggregate.
- **Kolla and Bolivian households:** check *pasanaku* and kin loans as insurance.
- **Italian, Spanish and Jewish community institutions; Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian clubs:** check mutual aid and burial societies, one by one.
- **Korean and Chinese shopkeepers:** check rotating clubs and the community's own account of crime against shops.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for savings crises, pensions and fear of crime; mixed for mutual-aid history outside Buenos Aires; thin and contested for Chaco food security.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Italian for mutual-society records; Yiddish for early AMIA papers; Korean and Aymara or Quechua for community savings groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the AMIA bombing, the *corralito* and the Wichí child deaths are painful, contested ground; present each account as an account. Pension and criminal law go to Libra; *seguridad* as policing and order goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the community savings-group pointers were reviewed for temple-based groups held as an open question for the author, and none was tied to one.
