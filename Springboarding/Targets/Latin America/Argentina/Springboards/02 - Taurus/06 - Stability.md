## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires barrios against new Conurbano and gated districts:** check "lifelong neighborhood" talk against fast-growing suburbs.
- **Pampa small towns and Córdoba's sierras:** check towns losing their young against retirees arriving.
- **Salta and Jujuy old families; Kolla and Diaguita communities:** check yearly vows, feasts and community land as anchors of continuity.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check families that move each harvest season against those who stay.
- **Mbyá of Misiones:** check *tekoha* and village relocations in the Mbyá's own terms.
- **Qom and Wichí villages:** check relocation histories community by community.
- **Volga German, Swiss and Italian farm villages of Entre Ríos and Santa Fe; Jewish colonies; La Pampa Mennonites:** check how each kept or let go of language and calendar.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan migrants:** check intentions to stay against plans to return.
- **Afro-Argentine families of long standing:** check family memory of continuity in the city against the 'disappearance' account.

## 4. Flags

- **Languages needed:** Spanish; German for Volga German community papers; Guaraní for Mbyá *tekoha* materials; Quechua for Northwest feast records; Yiddish for colony memory.
