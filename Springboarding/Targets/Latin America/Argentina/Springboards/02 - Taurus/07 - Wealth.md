## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Pampa landed families against new agribusiness and finance fortunes:** check how each displays wealth and how the press labels it.
- **Tucumán and Jujuy sugar-mill owners against mill workers:** check both sides' accounts, and the folklore of the mills.
- **Mendoza and San Juan wine families:** check vineyard ownership as the measure of wealth in Cuyo.
- **Córdoba and Rosario grain and industrial families:** check provincial elites against the porteño one.
- **Kolla, Diaguita and Huarpe communities; Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá:** check each people's own reckoning of what is wealth.
- **Syrian-Lebanese, Jewish, Armenian, Italian and Spanish family firms:** check business histories and community records firm by firm; never generalize from one family.
- **Korean, Chinese and Bolivian traders:** check textile and retail fortunes, and what their communities say about them.
- **Afro-Argentine and Cape Verdean families:** check property records and community histories for wealth that the 'disappearance' account omits.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the landed elite, inequality data and literature; mixed for provincial fortunes; thin for Indigenous wealth reckoning outside Puna herding studies.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; French for period travel writing; Quechua for the Puna; Yiddish and Arabic for community business records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "oligarchy", *garca* and the Peronist-era philanthropy are contested; present each account as an account. Wealth and inheritance law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the rich-list and family-firm pointers were checked so that no group's members are named as a group, and none fell under the standing rules.
