*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Porteño and Conurbano couples against provincial towns of Corrientes, Santiago del Estero and Salta:** check case data and song repertoires for how jealousy is framed in each.
- **Litoral Guaraní speakers:** check chamamé lyrics and envy remedies against Buenos Aires usage.
- **Pilagá, Qom, Wichí and Moqoit families:** check sibling and sharing customs in each people's own accounts; the Henry study is one outsider's record.
- **Kolla and Diaguita communities:** check how herd and land are kept from outsiders, and how shared work is organized.
- **Italian- and Spanish-descended families:** check family-firm and inheritance quarrels described in memoirs, not assumed.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Ukrainian families:** check the community press for advice on marriage, jealousy and family property.
- **Football supporters' groups across Buenos Aires, Rosario and Córdoba:** check territorial claims club by club.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan migrant women:** check helpline and shelter data for migrant-specific patterns of control.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for case records, literature and tango; mixed for provincial song and folk practice; thin for Indigenous sharing customs outside older ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for chamamé; Yiddish and Arabic for community press; English for the Henry study.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** femicide and partner violence are painful ground; describe how each source frames them, never a group's nature. The Malvinas / Falklands claim is a contested territorial question and is comparison only. Family and violence law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the folk envy-remedy pointers were checked against restricted healing knowledge and kept to publicly shared practice.
