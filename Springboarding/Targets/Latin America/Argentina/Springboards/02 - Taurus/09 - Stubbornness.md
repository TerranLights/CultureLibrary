## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pampa and Litoral farm towns against Buenos Aires:** check how the 2008 conflict is remembered on each side.
- **Catamarca, La Rioja and Jujuy mining towns:** check which protests last and how townspeople explain staying the course.
- **Salta's oil towns and Tucumán's sugar towns:** check roadblock and strike memories against the press of the time.
- **Entre Ríos (Gualeguaychú):** check the assembly's records against later local views.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Kolla and Diaguita communities:** check land claims held over decades in each community's own documents.
- **Mennonite colonies of La Pampa and Volga German villages:** check how each colony records its rules for keeping or changing customs; never describe a colony from outside only.
- **Jewish, Armenian and Ukrainian institutions:** check how each kept language and memory across a century.
- **Unions and recovered-firm workers:** check how long holdouts are justified in their own bulletins.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protest, strikes and political standoffs; mixed for folklore; thin for stubbornness as a personal trait outside psychology articles and folktales.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Quechua for sayings; German and Plautdietsch for colony records; Yiddish for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1976-83 dictatorship, the ESMA apology, Peronist and anti-Peronist resistance, and the 2008 conflict are contested; present each account as an account. Court cases go to Libra; obedience and defiance of authority go to Capricorn.
