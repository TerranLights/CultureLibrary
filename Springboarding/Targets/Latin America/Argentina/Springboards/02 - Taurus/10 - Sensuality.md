*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish and *lunfardo*; the Spanish of the Northwest and Cuyo; Guaraní; Quechua; Wichí and Qom (through ethnobotany); Yiddish; Arabic; Korean. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound, sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires milongas against Córdoba cuarteto dances and Corrientes chamamé dances:** check how closeness and touch differ by dance and setting.
- **The Northwest (Jujuy, Salta, Tucumán):** check carnival with basil, flour and *chicha*, and coca chewing, against Pampa food and drink customs.
- **Cuyo and Cafayate:** check wine as daily table drink against wine as tourism.
- **The Litoral and Misiones:** check *tereré*, *chipa* and Guaraní words for joy.
- **Wichí, Qom, Pilagá and Moqoit:** check wild foods and fermented drinks in each people's own descriptions, not only in outsiders' botanies.
- **Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdean Argentines:** check candombe and community dances as present practice, not only history.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian, Korean, Chinese, Bolivian and Peruvian communities:** check food and music as each community's own sensory life, through its clubs, restaurants and fairs.
- **Catholic, Evangelical and Jewish congregations:** check fasts, abstinence and worship through the senses.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mate, beef, wine, tango and folk dance; mixed for provincial bathing and carnival; thin for Indigenous sensory life outside ethnobotany.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for Litoral food words; Quechua for carnival and coca vocabulary; Yiddish, Arabic and Korean for community cookery and music.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the censorship era and the dictatorship's moral rules are contested; coca chewing touches drug law (to Libra). Sexuality as such belongs to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** none; the bathing and wellness pointers were checked for practices drawn from held religious traditions, and none was included.
