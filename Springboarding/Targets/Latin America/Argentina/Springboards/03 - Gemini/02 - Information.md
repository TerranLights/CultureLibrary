## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital readers and provincial readers:** check whether trust follows the Buenos Aires national papers or the provincial dailies (*La Voz del Interior*, *La Gaceta* of Tucumán, *El Tribuno* of Salta *check*).
- **Rural Pampa and Córdoba farm families:** compare reliance on official weather and market reports with sayings, neighbors and cooperatives' bulletins.
- **The Northwest and Santiago del Estero:** check Quechua- and Quichua-language radio and how news reaches the high Puna.
- **Wichí, Qom and Pilagá communities:** check their own radios and assemblies as news channels; ask what knowledge they restrict.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** compare their newspapers' coverage of the same national events.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check community radio in the Conurbano and messaging groups as news sources.
- **Generations:** compare television-first and phone-first users in the news-use survey, by region where the sample allows.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Buenos Aires press and news-use surveys; mixed for provincial media; thin for Indigenous-language news outside a few radios.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Yiddish for the Jewish dailies; English for the *Herald*; Wichí and Quechua for community media.
