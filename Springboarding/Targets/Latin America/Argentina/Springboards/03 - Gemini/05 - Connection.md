## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires merchant and professional families:** check whether godparent and marriage ties still steer careers, from colonial records to club memberships.
- **Galician, Italian and Spanish village networks:** compare how long each kept hometown societies alive after the arrival generation.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check how burial, mutual-aid and school bodies doubled as ways of connecting members.
- **Korean, Chinese and Okinawan communities:** check which associations link families, and how new arrivals are introduced.
- **Kolla households of the Puna and Quebrada:** check godparent choice and exchanged labor as the ties that bind across valleys.
- **Qom, Wichí and Moqoit families in cities:** check how ties to home communities are kept up: visits, churches, phones.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan workers:** check remittance and phone links, and the role of feasts in keeping ties.
- **Cape Verdean and Afro-Argentine families:** check halls and societies as the places ties were kept.
- **Conurbano barrios:** check brokers' networks against neighbors' own organizations as rival channels of help.
