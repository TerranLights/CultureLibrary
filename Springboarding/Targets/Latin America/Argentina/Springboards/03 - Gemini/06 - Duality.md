## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **La Rioja and Catamarca:** check how Diaguita communities and townspeople describe the *Tinkunaco* pair, and whether accounts agree.
- **Salta, Jujuy and the Quebrada:** check paired ideas in Kolla rites against archaeologists' readings of the Aguada imagery.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check where speakers name their speech mixed, and where they insist on two separate languages.
- **Buenos Aires and the provinces:** compare how each side tells the capital-against-interior pair.
- **Italian- and Spanish-descended families:** check who seeks a second citizenship, and what reasons they give.
- **German-descended families and Volga Germans of Entre Ríos and La Pampa:** check how school and church splits of the 1930s-40s are remembered.
- **Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check memoirs for two-homeland themes, and how each changed over generations.
- **Supporters of both political camps:** read each camp's own account of "la grieta"; never let one side describe the other.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan families in the Conurbano:** check how children describe holding two national belongings.
