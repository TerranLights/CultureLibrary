## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check colonial protectors' files against Native towns' own petitions.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check how Qom, Wichí, Pilagá and Moqoit communities choose the person who speaks to officials.
- **Salta's highlands and Chaco lowlands:** compare how health agents work with Kolla households and with Wichí villages.
- **Catholic, Evangelical and Jewish congregations:** compare which clergy families call on to settle quarrels.
- **Rural Catamarca, Luján and the Litoral:** check saints and shrines as intercessors, shrine by shrine.
- **Union and employers' bodies in Córdoba and Buenos Aires:** check how each describes the state's role as broker.
- **Volga German and Mennonite colonies:** check customary go-betweens in marriage and church disputes.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Korean communities:** check community councils and matchmakers as internal mediators.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan workers:** check who brokers between workshop owners and workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor conciliation and national crises; mixed for community and school mediation; thin for Moqoit and Huarpe peacemaking.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Quechua and Wichí for health-agent and community work; German dialects for the colonies; Yiddish for matchmaking records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1976-83 period, the Malvinas / Falklands war and Peronist labor politics are contested ground; present each account as an account. Mediation statutes and court-annexed mediation go to Libra.
