*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires tenements and *villas*:** check the *conventillo* image of gossip against present-day ethnographies of crowded housing.
- **Small towns of the Pampa, Córdoba and Santa Fe:** check what residents say *pueblo chico* means in practice.
- **Tucumán and Salta's provincial capitals:** check how family name and church networks carry talk in elite circles.
- **The Quebrada and the Puna:** check carnival verse as licensed mockery against everyday restraint.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá villages:** check accusations and rumor as each people describes them, never pooled.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Korean communities:** check whether community papers and congregations name gossip as a fault, and how.
- **Italian- and Spanish-descended neighborhoods:** check old sayings on loose tongues brought from home.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check how rumor travels through workshops, feasts and radio.
- **Schoolchildren by province:** compare the school survey's rumor and bullying items.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for celebrity media and recent movements; mixed for school surveys and crisis rumors; thin for small-town and Indigenous reputation work outside a few ethnographies.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Yiddish for community press; Quechua for Puna carnival verse; Korean for community talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sorcery accusations can put people at risk; never name individuals. *Escraches* and 2018 testimonies are contested; present each as an account. Slander and defamation as law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the magazines, programs, surveys and colonial files weighed for gossip touched no standing rule.
