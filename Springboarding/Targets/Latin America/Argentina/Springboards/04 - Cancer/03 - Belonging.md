*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Porteños and *provincianos* in the Conurbano:** check whether people of provincial origin still name a province as theirs two generations on.
- **Santiago del Estero and Corrientes migrants:** compare the reach of their *centros de residentes* and *peñas*.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check *originario* and outsider talk in Quebrada towns today against the colonial lists.
- **Huarpe of Cuyo, Diaguita of the Calchaquí valleys and Kolla of the Puna:** check how each people's recognition process defines who is a member.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá in Rosario, Resistencia and the Conurbano:** check urban members' standing in rural home communities.
- **Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdean Argentines:** check self-identification against how neighbors class them.
- **Armenian, Syrian-Lebanese, Jewish, Ukrainian and Polish communities:** check what membership in a community school or church means for a third generation.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian migrants and their Argentine-born children:** check what *ni de acá ni de allá* means to each generation.
- **Korean and Chinese communities:** check school belonging of the Argentine-born.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for immigration history; mixed for belonging measures; thin for small groups such as Moqoit and Pilagá in cities.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Quechua for the inclusive and exclusive "we"; Armenian, Arabic and Yiddish for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs for migrants and the "disappearance" of Afro-Argentines are contested ground; present each account as an account. Community membership is decided by the peoples themselves; cite their statements. Citizenship law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the membership and exclusion pointers weighed (registers, festivals, residents' clubs, label studies) met every standing rule.
