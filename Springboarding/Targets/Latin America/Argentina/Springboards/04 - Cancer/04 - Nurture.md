# Argentina (without Patagonia): Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America; written for the provinces north of Patagonia. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Corrientes Guaraní; Quechua; Wichí and Pilagá through ethnography; Yiddish for community-school records; English for the papers of the American teachers who founded kindergarten training. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Porteño families with psychoanalytic vocabulary:** check how much parents in Buenos Aires city draw on therapy talk compared with families inland.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check the siesta tales as current teaching or as memory.
- **Kolla and Quechua-speaking households of Jujuy and Salta:** check whether *uyway* links raising a child to raising animals in practice.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check grandmothers raising children while parents work away.
- **Wichí and Pilagá communities:** check upbringing against school routines set by outsiders.
- **Mbyá of Misiones:** check community-run schooling against state kindergartens.
- **Jewish, Armenian and Ukrainian families:** check community schools as a second household of nurture.
- **Italian- and Spanish-descended families of the Pampa gringa:** check the grandmother's place in raising children in farm towns.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan families in the Conurbano:** check how parents raise children between two sets of norms.
- **Evangelical and Catholic congregations:** check Sunday schools and catechism as nurture programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medical advice and kindergarten history; mixed for regional practice; thin for Wichí and Pilagá upbringing in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Quechua for the raising vocabulary; Guaraní for siesta tales; Yiddish for community-school papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** studies of Indigenous children need community consent; use published work. Child protection and its rules go to the Protection sheet and to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing put aside; the advice manuals, folklore, screening norms and community-school pointers weighed for nurture met the standing rules.
