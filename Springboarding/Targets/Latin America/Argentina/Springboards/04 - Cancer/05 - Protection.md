## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check private guards and window bars against neighborhood alarm groups, by income.
- **Córdoba and the Pampa farm towns:** check whether parents name spraying or crime as the main danger to children.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check *payé*, Itatí vows and forest dangers as family protection.
- **Santiago del Estero and the Chaco:** check how peasant families guard land and animals against eviction and theft.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check offerings for safe passage among herders and traders today.
- **Wichí, Qom, Moqoit and Pilagá:** check each people's own protectors (elders, healers, community guards) as the communities describe them.
- **Cuyo:** check earthquake drills and household readiness in Mendoza and San Juan.
- **Jewish community:** check community security after the 1994 bombing; contested ground, present each account as an account.
- **Families whose shop and home share one building, native-born and migrant (Korean, Chinese, Bolivian and others) alike:** check how both are guarded together, without assuming any group's trade.
- **Women and girls across regions:** check who is expected to escort them, by city and town.
