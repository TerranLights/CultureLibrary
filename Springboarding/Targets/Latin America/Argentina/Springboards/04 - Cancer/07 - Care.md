## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check hired carers and *geriátricos* against care by daughters at home, by income.
- **The Conurbano:** check community kitchens and neighbor care networks as care beyond the family.
- **Córdoba's sierras and Traslasierra:** check parish and neighbor care in thinly served valleys.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check care of elders left while younger kin migrate.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check healers and *promesas* alongside clinics.
- **Kolla and Bolivian families:** check Andean healers' care against clinic care in Jujuy and the capital.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá:** check how each community describes caring for its sick and old, in its own health programs.
- **Italian, Spanish, Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and German communities:** check what remains of community hospitals' care for their own.
- **Paraguayan and Peruvian families:** check how many women work in paid care here, and how those who do arrange care for their own kin abroad.
- **Men who care:** check fathers and sons as carers across regions, where surveys allow.
