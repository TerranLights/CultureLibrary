## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check the institute and gallery circuit against neighborhood workshops.
- **The Conurbano:** check improvised trades and repairs (*rebusque*) as making outside the arts.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** compare mission-music heritage and later art scenes with the capital's.
- **Catamarca, La Rioja, Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán:** check weavers' and potters' accounts of who may change a pattern.
- **Cuyo:** check winemakers' and irrigators' talk of invention.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** compare Guaraní and Mbyá craft with the Ukrainian, Polish and German colony crafts.
- **Wichí, Qom and Pilagá:** check chaguar, pottery and palm crafts in the makers' words, apart from dealers' catalogs.
- **Jewish, Armenian, Syrian-Lebanese, Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check craft trades and festival costume workshops as making traditions.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Buenos Aires fine art, literature and science policy; mixed for provincial crafts; thin for Indigenous makers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Quechua for craft vocabularies; Ukrainian and Polish for colony craft papers; Yiddish for the art and stage press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Mbyá and Andean designs belong to a community and may not be copied; ask first. Copyright law is Libra's. Artists exiled under the 1976-83 dictatorship are contested ground; present each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the mission-music, invention-list, avant-garde and craft pointers weighed for making something new raised no standing-rule issue.
