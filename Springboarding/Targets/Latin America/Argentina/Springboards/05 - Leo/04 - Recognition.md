*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Guaraní; Quechua and Aymara; Wichí; Yiddish; Armenian; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** others' acknowledgment of a person's or group's worth, achievements or standing, from a word of credit to a formal honor or title.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check which awards and street names are local and which national.
- **The Conurbano:** check neighborhood plaques, club murals and the naming of schools.
- **Córdoba, Rosario and Mendoza:** compare provincial honors and their own idols with the capital's.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check the colonial recognition of Native lords against present community demands.
- **Santiago del Estero and Corrientes:** check recognition of Quichua and Guaraní singers and writers.
- **Wichí, Qom, Pilagá and Moqoit:** check how each people asks to be recognized as a people and as owners, in its own terms.
- **Kolla, Diaguita and Huarpe:** check recognition claims by peoples once said to have vanished; contested ground.
- **Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdean Argentines:** check census self-identification against public honors.
- **Armenian, Jewish and Syrian-Lebanese communities:** check recognition sought from the state and given inside the community.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan, Peruvian and Venezuelan migrants:** check credential validation and workplace credit.
- **Women in care and household work:** check recognition through pensions, days and awards.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for awards, press and naming; mixed for workplace and household recognition; thin for Indigenous peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Quechua for thanks and titles; Wichí for land-claim testimony; Armenian and Yiddish for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recognition of Indigenous peoples and of Afro-Argentines touches contested accounts of "disappearance"; present each account as an account. Legal recognition of communities and degrees belongs to Libra; point there.
- **Held for review:** nothing put aside; the awards, banknote, naming, death-notice and land-claim pointers weighed for acknowledgment raised no standing-rule question.
