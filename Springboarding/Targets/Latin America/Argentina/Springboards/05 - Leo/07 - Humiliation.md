# Argentina (without Patagonia): Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish and its football and school slang; interior Spanish; Guaraní; Quechua; Yiddish; Arabic; Plautdietsch. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check televised humiliation as a national product against viewers' responses by region.
- **The Conurbano:** check crowd and police humiliations in residents' own accounts.
- **Rosario and Córdoba:** compare rivalry chants with the capital's.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Corrientes:** check effigy burnings and carnival mockery as town customs.
- **Santiago del Estero and Tucumán:** check how migrant workers describe humiliations at work away from home.
- **Cuyo and La Pampa:** check small-town shaming and gossip as sanctions.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá:** check how each people names shame and insult in its own language, and the humiliations recorded in testimony.
- **Mennonites of La Pampa:** check congregation discipline as a sanction distinct from public mockery.
- **Jewish community:** check community discipline and the shame of the trafficking era as recorded by the community.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check slurs and school bullying in children's own accounts.
- **Malvinas veterans:** check how veterans describe their treatment on return; contested ground.

## 4. Flags
