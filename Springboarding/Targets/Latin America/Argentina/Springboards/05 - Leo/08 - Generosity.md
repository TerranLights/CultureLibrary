## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check donors to foundations against small givers to parishes and schools, before averaging the city.
- **The Conurbano:** compare street pots, parish kitchens and Pentecostal churches as giving networks.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe:** check farm-town giving to volunteer fire brigades, hospitals and clubs.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán:** check feast sponsorship and the *yapa* as forms of giving with return.
- **Santiago del Estero and Catamarca:** check help sent home by seasonal migrants.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní terms of open giving against Mbyá ones.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá:** check how each people names sharing and gift, apart from outsiders' studies of sharing.
- **Kolla communities:** check *ayni* and *minga* as reciprocity, not charity.
- **Jewish, Armenian and Syrian-Lebanese communities:** check welfare societies, loan funds and church giving in each community's own records.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check feast sponsorship and money sent home.
- **Catholic and Evangelical congregations:** check tithes, collections and the giving each asks of members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church and foundation records; mixed for informal giving; thin for Indigenous terms and for money sent home within the country.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for *jopói*; Quechua and Aymara for Andean exchange; Yiddish and Hebrew for loan societies; Arabic and Armenian for community charities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recipients of aid named in charity files should not be named. Giving tied to political networks is contested ground; present each account as an account. Tax rules on donations are Libra's; point there.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the bequest, foundation, telethon, blood-plan and feast-sponsor pointers weighed for giving freely touched no standing rule.
