# Argentina (without Patagonia): Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Guaraní and Mbyá; Quechua; Wichí and Qom; Arabic; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check business, church and union leaders as separate leadership worlds before reading them as one.
- **The Conurbano:** compare party brokers, union delegates, priests and Pentecostal pastors as neighborhood leaders.
- **Córdoba:** check union leadership traditions apart from the capital's.
- **La Rioja and Catamarca:** check how caudillo memory is told locally against national histories.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** compare Kolla community assemblies with the old Calchaquí and Andean lords in colonial records.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check peasant movement leaders and provincial family dynasties as distinct kinds of leadership.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Mbyá leadership against Guaraní-speaking rural leaders.
- **Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá:** check how each people names and chooses leaders, and the role of churches.
- **Afro-Argentines:** check society kings and later association leaders.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check how community institutions choose presidents and rabbis or priests.
- **Women:** check women as leaders of soup kitchens, movements and parishes against their share of office.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for caudillos, Peronism and unions; mixed for church and community leaders; thin for Indigenous leadership in the peoples' own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Mbyá for leadership titles; Wichí and Qom for community records; Quechua for colonial lords; Yiddish and Arabic for community politics.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Peronism, the 1976-83 repression of shop-floor leaders and Third World priests are contested ground; present each account as an account. Formal authority and command belong to Capricorn; office law to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the caudillo, broker, union, assembly and church-leader pointers weighed for willing following raised no standing-rule issue.
