## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check plaza play, *calesitas* and paid activities by class and neighborhood.
- **The Conurbano:** check street football against the loss of open lots.
- **Pampa towns and La Pampa:** check bowls, *sapo* and cards at clubs and bars as adult play.
- **Córdoba and the sierras:** check summer play and tourism play in the hill towns.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-language games and the Ukrainian and Polish parish games.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Catamarca:** check games tied to the carnival season and to herding children.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check games in Quichua-speaking homes.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá:** check children's games in the community against school recess, in each people's own language.
- **Mbyá Guaraní:** check play in the communities apart from what schools record.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check holiday games and club table games.
- **Korean, Chinese and Japanese communities:** check New Year games and seniors' clubs.
