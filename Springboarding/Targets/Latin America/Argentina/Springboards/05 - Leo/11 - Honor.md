# Argentina (without Patagonia): Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with tango and football slang; interior and colonial Spanish; Guaraní; Quechua; Yiddish; Arabic; Armenian; Italian. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check gentlemen's clubs, tango suburbs and football stands as separate settings of honor.
- **The Conurbano:** check supporters' codes and street disputes in residents' accounts.
- **Córdoba:** check colonial insult suits and later university honor rules.
- **Pampa towns and La Pampa:** check the given word in cattle trading.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check landholding families' honor against Kolla and peasant accounts of the same towns.
- **Santiago del Estero and Catamarca:** check family name in small towns and among migrants.
- **Corrientes and Entre Ríos:** check Guaraní rural terms and the Jewish colonies' terms side by side.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá:** check whether each language names good name and insult; look to community dictionaries.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Italian communities:** check each community's own records of honor and marriage.
- **Malvinas veterans and the armed forces:** check the military's account against conscripts' accounts.
- **Women:** check honor claimed by women for themselves in suits and memoirs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dueling, clubs, the military and football; mixed for colonial insult records; thin for Indigenous terms for honor.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, colonial Spanish for court records; Guaraní and Quechua for good-name terms; Yiddish, Arabic, Armenian and Italian for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence over family honor must be researched in the words of those who lived it, never assigned to a group. The Malvinas war and the armed forces' conduct in 1976-83 are contested ground; present each account as an account. Defamation and dueling law belong to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none set aside; the dueling, insult-suit, club-panel, regiment and colony pointers weighed for honor kept and lost met every standing rule.
