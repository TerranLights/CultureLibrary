# Argentina (without Patagonia): Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Corrientes and Mbyá Guaraní; Quechua and Aymara; Yiddish; Arabic; Armenian; Ukrainian. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check murga, rock concerts and football joy as separate crowds before calling any of them the city's.
- **The Conurbano:** compare dance-hall nights with Pentecostal meetings as two settings of collective joy in the same districts.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check *cuarteto* dances and student festivals against the capital's crowds.
- **The Pampa and San Antonio de Areco:** check rural festivals as celebrations of tradition.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** compare the students' festival with Andean carnival and saints' feasts.
- **Santiago del Estero, Catamarca and La Rioja:** check rural wakes, saints' feasts and *chacarera* nights.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check *chamamé* nights and Mbyá prayer-house dance as different kinds of joy.
- **Cuyo:** check harvest-season joy in wine towns.
- **Qom, Wichí and Pilagá:** check church singing and community dances in each people's own terms.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check wedding and feast music as each community's own channel.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check patron-saint dances and fiestas in Buenos Aires.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for football, music and religious crowds; mixed for provincial feasts; thin for Indigenous joy words outside church contexts.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Mbyá; Quechua and Aymara for Andean feasts; Yiddish and Arabic for wedding music and dance.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mbyá prayer-house dance and some Andean rites are not for outsiders to film. Joy around the 1978 World Cup is contested ground; present each account as an account. Crowd disasters at concerts need care with victims' names.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the festival, crusade, wake, concert and wedding-music pointers weighed for gladness raised no standing-rule question.
