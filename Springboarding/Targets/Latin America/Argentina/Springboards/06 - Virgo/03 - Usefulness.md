# Argentina (without Patagonia): Usefulness (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish and *lunfardo*; rural Spanish of Santa Fe, Corrientes and the western Chaco; Guaraní; Qom and Wichí; Argentine Sign Language for disability sources. **Concept in one line:** being of practical value to others or to a purpose: for a person, contributing and being needed; for a thing or a piece of knowledge, practical value.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check how the retired, the jobless and the disabled describe their days, by district.
- **Pampa farm towns:** check what is asked of children and elders in family farms against town jobs.
- **Santa Fe and the Northeast:** check what families expect the farm schools to make of their children.
- **Santiago del Estero and Catamarca:** check elders left in charge of houses and grandchildren while adults migrate.
- **The western Chaco:** check criollo and Indigenous plant knowledge as two separate catalogs of the useful.
- **Mbyá, Qom, Wichí and Kolla:** check each people's own words for a person's role and contribution, not outsiders' labels.
- **Jewish, Volga German and Mennonite colonies:** check how each farm colony assigned tasks to the old and the young.
- **Deaf and disabled people's associations:** check their own terms against official ones.
- **Korean, Chinese, Bolivian and Syrian-Lebanese communities:** where a family runs a shop or stall, check how the family itself describes children's help in it.
