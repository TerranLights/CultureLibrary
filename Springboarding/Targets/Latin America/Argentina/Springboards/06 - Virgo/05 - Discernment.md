## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check con tricks, fact-checking and taste markers by neighborhood, from the old police catalogs to today.
- **The Conurbano:** check who is stopped by police on looks, and what young people say about it.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check the same profiling and scam patterns outside the capital, as a test of whether they hold.
- **Santiago del Estero and Tucumán:** check how healers are told from sorcerers in living memory, against colonial trials.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** check coca reading and Andean signs beside clinic and church counsel.
- **Cuyo:** check wine judging as a local trade and its prestige.
- **The Pampa:** check horse, cattle and land judging at rural shows and fairs.
- **Jewish (Ashkenazi and Sephardic) and Muslim communities:** check whose food seals each trusts and why.
- **Catholic charismatic and Evangelical congregations:** check how each teaches discernment of spirits.
- **Qom, Wichí and Mbyá:** check how each people judges a healer's or leader's worth, in its own accounts.
