*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check perfectionism as talked about in therapy, school and body culture, by class.
- **The Conurbano:** check how industrial workers describe quality demands on the line.
- **Córdoba:** check the malambo's drill culture and the province's car plants as two kinds of exactness.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check rawhide and silver craft contests at rural shows.
- **Mendoza and San Juan:** check wine and kitchen standards set by outside guides against local ones.
- **Misiones and Corrientes:** check mission carving traditions in today's carvers' own accounts.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Catamarca:** check the Andean weaving standards of the Puna and Belén through the weavers' words.
- **Qom, Wichí and Mbyá makers:** check each people's craft standards apart from buyers' tastes.
- **Jewish, Volga German and Mennonite colonists:** check what colony memoirs say about order and exact work.
- **Japanese-Argentine community:** check, in community histories, how standards in family trades were taught.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dance contests, cosmetic surgery data and psychology studies; mixed for industrial quality; thin for Indigenous craft standards in makers' own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for mission legacies; Qom for potters' terms; Yiddish and German for colony memoirs; Japanese for community histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders and anxiety data are clinical; use published aggregates only. Some craft knowledge may be held by families; use what makers share.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the mission-carving, standards, contest and clinical pointers weighed for flawlessness raised no standing-rule issue.
