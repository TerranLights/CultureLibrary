## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check satire, the opinion press and talk radio as a criticism industry, and who is mocked.
- **The Conurbano:** check criticism of mayors and *punteros* in local radio and neighbors' groups.
- **Córdoba:** check its own satirical tradition and the student criticism of the 1918 reform's heirs.
- **Rosario and Santa Fe:** check local press criticism of provincial power.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check criticism of governors and of family dynasties in provincial media, and Andean carnival verse as a vent.
- **Formosa and Chaco:** check how Indigenous communities voice criticism of provincial officials, and through which outlets.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-language radio and chamamé verse as places for complaint.
- **Jewish community:** check criticism inside community institutions after the AMIA bombing (1994), a contested ground.
- **Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check how each community press handled criticism of its leaders.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check their own radio and papers as forums for criticizing employers and officials.

## 4. Flags
