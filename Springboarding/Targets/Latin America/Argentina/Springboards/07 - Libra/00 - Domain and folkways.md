## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check mediation and court use against neighborhood go-betweens.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check spoken deals in cattle and land rent, and when paper takes over.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe:** check provincial mediation laws against the national ones.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check what Kolla communities of the Quebrada and Puna settle themselves.
- **Santiago del Estero, Catamarca and La Rioja:** check the rural justice of the peace.
- **Cuyo:** check water turns and vineyard partnerships as bargains with their own rules.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní dispute talk and Mbyá dealings with provincial courts.
- **Wichí, Qom, Moqoit and Pilagá:** check each people's settling of wrongs, in its own published words.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check religious marriage and divorce beside the civil forms.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check marriages made abroad and work deals handled here.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for codes and legal history; mixed for regional marriage custom; thin for Indigenous settling of wrongs.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, with its notarial register; Guaraní and Quichua for rural justice; Wichí and Qom for community statements; Yiddish and Arabic for religious marriage and reconciliation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the dictatorship trials, Peronism's quarrels and the AMIA case are contested ground; present each account as an account. Some community ways of settling wrongs may not be for outsiders.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the religious-court pointers came nearest a rule, but they point to public procedure, not restricted rites.
