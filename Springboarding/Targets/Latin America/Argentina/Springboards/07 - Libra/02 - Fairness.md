## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check queue manners at banks and buses, and the talk of *colados*.
- **The Pampa and Santa Fe:** check how farm families split land among children, and when one heir buys out the rest.
- **Córdoba:** check open university entry as the province's students debate it.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check harvest piece rates and how crews settle disputed counts.
- **Cuyo:** check vineyard sharecroppers' shares (*contratistas de viña*) as contracts state them.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check how yerba harvest pay is weighed and contested.
- **Wichí and Qom of the Pilcomayo and the Bermejo:** check fishing and foraging shares in each people's own words.
- **Jewish colonists of Entre Ríos; Volga German and Mennonite farmers:** check cooperative and church rules for dividing land and gains.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian workers:** check piece-rate horticulture and garment work through their own associations' complaints.
- **Party politics:** check the list orders each party fielded under the quota and parity laws.
