## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check work hours and talk of being *quemado* against smaller cities.
- **The Conurbano:** check double jobs and how households balance earning and care.
- **The Pampa and Córdoba:** check rest days in farm work and the weight of harvest season.
- **Santa Fe and Rosario:** check port and industrial shifts against office work.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check warm-and-cool healing and the August rites for the earth as each town keeps them.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua-speaking healers and weavers on what "set right" means.
- **Cuyo:** check wine work and drinking rules side by side.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check rewilding's reception, and Mbyá voices on the forest.
- **The Chaco and Formosa (Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá):** check each people's healing and land-keeping terms, in its own words.
- **Chinese and Korean communities:** check work hours in family shops, and remedies used at home.
