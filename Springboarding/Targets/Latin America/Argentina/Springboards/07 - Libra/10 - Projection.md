## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check each camp's images of the other in the press of 1945-55 and since.
- **The Conurbano:** check how its residents answer the crime images placed on them, in their own media.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check the regional jokes and images aimed at porteños, and the reverse.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check devil figures and blame in sugar-mill and mining towns, from residents' tellings.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check frontier-era enemy images and how Ranquel descendants answer them.
- **The Chaco and Formosa (Qom, Wichí, Moqoit, Pilagá):** check how outsiders' images of each people were made, and each people's own reply.
- **Jewish community:** check how 1919, 1994 and later accusations are told inside the community.
- **Syrian-Lebanese, Korean and Chinese communities:** check rumors aimed at their shops, from each community's records.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check their associations' answers to blame for crime and for hospital use.
