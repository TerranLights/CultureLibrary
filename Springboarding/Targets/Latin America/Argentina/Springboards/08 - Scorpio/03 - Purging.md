## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check Orthodox Jewish, Maronite and Catholic fasting calendars side by side.
- **The Conurbano:** check police purges as residents and dismissed officers each tell them.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check stadium bans and *barras* in second cities, not only Boca and River.
- **Jujuy and the Puna:** check Abra Pampa and Kolla cleansing of homes as separate trails.
- **Salta and Tucumán:** check sugar-town memories of who was fired or expelled in the 1970s.
- **Santiago del Estero and Catamarca:** check smoke and herb cleansing among Quichua-speaking families.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Mbyá healers' smoke against *criollo* home cures.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check how Qom and Wichí congregations describe driving out harm through prayer.
- **Cuyo and La Pampa:** check rural accounts of fasting and Holy Week penance.
- **Peronist and anti-Peronist families:** compare memories of 1955 and of the erased symbols.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political purges and the dictatorship; mixed for police and stadium bans; thin for Indigenous cleansing rites outside ethnography.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including the dictatorship's euphemisms; Quechua for Puna practice; Mbyá Guaraní for smoke healing; Hebrew for ritual-bath rules; Arabic for fasting terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the disappearances, 1955 and Peronism are contested ground: give each account as an account. Mbyá healing may be restricted knowledge. Eating disorders belong here as a silence; treat any clinic material with care.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the dictatorship lexicon came nearest a rule, as its words could read as one side's voice, so it is used only to study that voice.
