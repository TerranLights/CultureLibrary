## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires and the Conurbano:** check the informer's name in barrios, prisons and unions, each separately.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check frontier memory of broken treaties against Rankülche descendants' telling.
- **Córdoba:** check accounts of the 1969 uprising and its splits for who was called a traitor.
- **Santa Fe and Rosario:** check strikebreaking in port and rail disputes.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check sugar-mill strike memory and charges of informing in mill towns after 1976.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-language song and *chamamé* lyrics of false lovers.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua sayings on deceit.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Pilagá accounts of promises made by the state and its agents.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check how each community press names an informer or a defector.
- **Malvinas veterans:** compare officers' and conscripts' accounts of who failed whom; the name is contested.
