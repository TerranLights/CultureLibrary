## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check middle-class talk of control through therapy, money and planning.
- **The Conurbano:** check how households control spending under inflation, and who holds the purse.
- **The Pampa:** check locust and flood memory in farming families.
- **Córdoba:** check fire and dam talk in the sierras, by long-term residents and newcomers.
- **Corrientes and Entre Ríos:** check carnival organizers' rules and the crowd's view.
- **Salta's Wichí communities:** check memory of mission rule, in Wichí words.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Pilagá accounts of state tutelage, separately from Wichí ones.
- **Tucumán and Jujuy:** check sugar-mill company towns and their rules.
- **Cuyo:** check irrigation turns and frost defenses in vineyards as control of nature.
- **Korean, Chinese and Bolivian communities:** check community press on currency controls and sending money home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for economic controls and state surveillance; mixed for partner control; thin for Indigenous accounts of mission rule.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and money slang; Wichí and Qom for mission memory; English for mission archives; Quichua and Guaraní for terms of command.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** price and currency controls are tied to Peronism and its opponents: give each account as an account. Oesterheld's family were disappeared; handle with care.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the Wichí mission pointer was the closest call, since mission papers speak over Wichí voices, so it carries the instruction to pair them.
