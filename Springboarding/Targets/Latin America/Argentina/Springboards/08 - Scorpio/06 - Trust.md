## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check trust in banks against savings kept in dollars and changers.
- **The Conurbano:** check who residents rely on in an emergency: kin, church, a party organizer or a club.
- **The Pampa:** check how a farmer vouches for a hired hand or a buyer.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe-Rosario:** check trust in provincial governments against the national index.
- **Misiones:** check cooperative trust among settler groups, each separately.
- **Corrientes:** check godparent ties and Guaraní-speaking households' vouching.
- **Jujuy, Salta and Tucumán:** check Andean ritual kin and Kolla community trust in outsiders.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí and Moqoit relations with criollo neighbors and with the state.
- **Cuyo:** check trust in the water inspector and the bodega buyer.
- **Korean, Syrian-Lebanese and Jewish communities:** check how each community's credit and business circles vouch for members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trust in government and institutions; mixed for neighborly and ritual-kin trust; thin for Indigenous views of outsiders outside land disputes.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and its fraud slang; Quechua and Quichua for ritual kin; Guaraní for vouching terms; Wichí for intergroup accounts; Korean and Arabic for community business records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the INDEC dispute and election-fraud history are tied to Peronism and its opponents: give accounts. Migrant networks touch exploitation cases; name no group as exploiters. Trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn.
