*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish and *lunfardo*; the political vocabulary of the 1970s and of the trials; Northwest Spanish; Moqoit and Qom for the Chaco risings; Guaraní. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; forgiveness and payment are its alternatives.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires and the Conurbano:** check how neighbors answer theft: report, mediation or retaliation.
- **Rosario and Santa Fe:** check the city's gang feuds, and separately Moqoit memory of 1904 in the north.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check small-town political feuds and family quarrels over land.
- **Córdoba:** check how football rivalries frame payback.
- **Tucumán and Salta:** check civil-war memory of reprisals, and how each province tells it.
- **Jujuy:** check carnival song duels as a licensed way to answer an insult.
- **Santiago del Estero and Catamarca:** check rural feud memory in local oral-history collections.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-language song and story of the wronged who return.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Moqoit and Pilagá accounts of risings and reprisals, each in its own telling.
- **Mennonites and Evangelical congregations:** check teaching on forgiveness against members' accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political violence and its literature; mixed for everyday payback; thin for Indigenous accounts of reprisals and for Guaraní terms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, with political and police jargon; Moqoit and Qom for community history; Guaraní; Quechua-inflected Spanish for the *coplas*; Plautdietsch and German for Mennonite teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aramburu's killing, the trials, Peronist feuds and the 1904 rising are contested ground: give each account as an account. Revenge-porn sources must not be reproduced. Private vengeance against the state's punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** none withheld; the Montoneros texts weighed most, since quoting them could amplify a claim, so they are pointed to only as primary sources to read critically.
