## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check how the capital's elites describe their own power: business, church, press, courts.
- **The Conurbano:** check neighborhood power through party networks, churches and clubs.
- **The Pampa:** check landowners and cooperatives as rival centers of power.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe:** check industrial and union power in second cities.
- **Santiago del Estero and San Luis:** check long-ruling provincial families, from each side.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check sugar companies, Kolla communities and the Diaguita union as rival sources of power.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check local *caudillo* families and yerba and timber firms.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí and Pilagá dealings with provincial machines, in community words.
- **Catamarca and La Rioja:** check whether mining firms and old families share or contest power.
- **Evangelical, Catholic and Jewish congregations:** check each one's political weight, as its own leaders and critics describe it.
