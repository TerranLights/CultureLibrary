## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check middle-class accounts of 1989 and 2001 against the 1890 and 1930 busts.
- **The Conurbano:** check neighborhood responses in each crisis, in residents' words.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check drought and flood years in farm-family memory.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe-Rosario:** check how industrial towns lived the factory closures.
- **Cuyo:** check earthquake memory in San Juan and Mendoza across generations.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check epidemic and mudslide memory among Wichí, Kolla and *criollo* families, separately.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check fire and crop-price crises among Guaraní-speaking, Ukrainian, Polish and German growers.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Pilagá accounts of floods and hunger times.
- **Santiago del Estero and Tucumán:** check the sugar crisis of 1966 and the Santiago revolt (*santiagueñazo*) of 1993.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Korean communities:** check how each community's institutions acted in 2001.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for economic crises and earthquakes; mixed for epidemics before 1950; thin for Indigenous accounts of floods and hunger.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and its crisis slang; Wichí and Qom for community accounts; Guaraní; Yiddish for community press; Ukrainian and Polish for settler records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Malvinas / Falklands war and the dictatorship's disaster response are contested ground, as are blame for the cholera and the Tartagal slide: give accounts. Suicide-crisis material needs care.
- **Held for review:** nothing withheld; the cholera row came nearest the stereotype rule, since coverage could cast the sick as the cause, so it studies who was blamed and by whom.
