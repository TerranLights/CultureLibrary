## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check how press, protest and Pride freedoms are spoken of by neighborhood and by generation.
- **The Conurbano:** check whether freedom from want or freedom from the state is named first in working-class districts.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check rural memory of the *matrero* and the pass laws against farm families' talk of open land.
- **Rosario and Córdoba:** compare the 1969 risings' memory with the libertarian think tanks of the same cities.
- **The Northwest:** check Kolla and Diaguita communities on land and self-rule, in their own statements.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí and Pilagá accounts of movement, land and policing.
- **Afro-Argentines and Cape Verdean Argentines:** check community-led histories of manumission and of the 'disappearance' account, which is contested.
- **Jewish, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check how flight from persecution frames freedom.
- **Jehovah's Witnesses and other small faiths:** check members' memory of the 1976-83 ban.
- **Mennonites of La Pampa:** check how a colony asks for freedom to keep its own schools and ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political rhetoric, press freedom and anarchism; mixed for religious liberty; thin for Native peoples' own words on freedom.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Yiddish and Italian for anarchist papers; Guaraní and Qom for Litoral and Chaco voices; Arabic and Armenian for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Peronism, the dictatorship, the 2020 lockdown and current libertarian politics are contested; present each account as an account. Freedom-of-worship and press law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the closest call was the libertarian and Peronist rows, which could read as party advocacy; both are pointed to as primary sources only. No item was withheld.
