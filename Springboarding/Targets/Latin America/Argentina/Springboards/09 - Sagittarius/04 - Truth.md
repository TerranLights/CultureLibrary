## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check which outlets and influencers each district trusts for news, measured, not assumed.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check university scientists' standing beside talk radio's.
- **Tucumán:** check how the 1975 military operation is remembered in families and in the official record, and where the two part ways.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua speakers' testimony and the evidential markers in how they report.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check Kolla and Wichí oral histories of land against land titles and maps.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Moqoit memory of Napalpí against archive and court records, each named as an account.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-language rumor and radio, and how a claim gets checked there.
- **The Jewish community:** check how AMIA families and community bodies weigh the rival accounts of the bombing.
- **Malvinas veterans:** check soldiers' memory against the wartime press's reports of 1982.
- **Afro-Argentines:** check community historians' accounts against the textbook 'disappearance' story, which is contested.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the dictatorship's record, media fights and science policy; mixed for public trust surveys; thin for Wichí and Pilagá oral history in print.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; early modern Spanish for the 1602 poem; Quichua for evidentials; Qom and Moqoit for Napalpí memory; Yiddish and Arabic for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the dictatorship, Peronism, the Malvinas war, Napalpí, the AMIA bombing and the Nisman case are contested; give each account as an account. Truth trials and courts go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** set aside nothing; the Nisman row came nearest a rule, since polls on it can be read as partisan verdicts, so it is framed as a study of split opinion only.
