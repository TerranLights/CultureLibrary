## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires and the Conurbano:** check believers without a church against parish and Pentecostal members, by district.
- **Córdoba and its sierras:** check UFO and new-belief seekers in Capilla del Monte against older Catholic townsfolk.
- **The Northwest (Jujuy, Salta, Catamarca):** check Kolla and Diaguita narrators' beliefs about land beings, told on their own terms.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua tales of the uncanny against parish teaching in the same villages.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** compare Guaraní belief tales with Mbyá, Ukrainian and German-descended families' beliefs.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí and Moqoit believers in their churches and older specialists, each in their own words.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check rural luck and weather beliefs among Volga German, Italian and *criollo* families, and the Mennonite colony.
- **The Jewish community:** separate observant, traditional and secular households before reading belief figures.
- **Syrian-Lebanese families:** separate Maronite, Orthodox, Sunni and Alawite households.
- **Bolivian and Peruvian communities:** check beliefs brought from home and how they change in Buenos Aires.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey belief items and folk-belief collections; mixed for spiritism and new beliefs; thin for Pilagá and Huarpe belief in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní for Litoral tales; Quechua for Puna tales; Yiddish and Arabic for community belief; Plautdietsch for the Mennonite colony.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Native and Kolla beliefs are held privately; ask before recording. Folk belief must not be written up as backwardness; use narrators' own framing. The pandemic's vaccine quarrels remain contested.
- **Held for review:** a few candidate pointers were set aside under the standing rules and kept as two bare categories: South Asian-heritage communities and traditions in the nation's lands, and Buddhist belief (an open question for the author).
