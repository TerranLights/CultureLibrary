## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check which causes draw marchers to the Plaza de Mayo, and how often the same people return.
- **The Conurbano:** check *piquetero* and parish-based movements, and how members explain staying.
- **Córdoba:** check union principle in the auto plants of 1969 against later generations' politics.
- **Rosario:** check the city's memory of Guevara alongside other local exemplars of conviction.
- **La Rioja and Catamarca:** check Angelelli's diocese and the anti-mining towns as two rural kinds of holding out.
- **Salta's Tartagal and Mosconi area:** check unemployed workers' blockades and Wichí communities' stands as separate cases.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check how the Caaró story is told in Guaraní and Catholic memory today.
- **The Chaco:** check Qom and Moqoit organizations' long campaigns for Napalpí's recognition, in their own words.
- **Jewish, Armenian and Ukrainian communities:** check activism tied to the homeland's past (recognition of genocide, the Holodomor, Israel), each separately.
- **Evangelical congregations:** check how converts describe conviction of sin and later public stands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political, union and church activism; mixed for the abortion debate's two camps; thin for Moqoit and Pilagá activism in print.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Latin and Guaraní for 1628 sources; Italian for anarchist papers; Yiddish and Arabic for community memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Guevara, Peronism, the dictatorship and the abortion debate are contested; describe conviction that turned to violence without endorsing it, and give each camp's account as its own.
