## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires porteños:** check who travels abroad, who to the coast, and who never takes a holiday, by income.
- **The Conurbano:** check whether union and social-tourism trips still shape working families' holidays.
- **Córdoba and its sierras:** compare residents of the hills with the summer visitors they host.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check pilgrims, border porters and Kolla families who cross to Bolivia as three separate kinds of travel.
- **Santiago del Estero and Tucumán:** check seasonal migrants' long trips for harvest work and their returns.
- **Misiones:** check Mbyá movement across borders, in Mbyá terms, apart from tourist traffic to the falls.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Wichí journeys to cities and back, as they tell them.
- **Italian- and Spanish-descended families:** check whether ancestral passports shaped emigration in 2001.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check trips home, buses and remittances as their own accounts give them.
- **Jewish and Syrian-Lebanese families:** compare arrival stories, return visits and later emigration.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism statistics, travel accounts and migration; mixed for exile; thin for Mbyá, Wichí and Qom travel in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; English for 19th-century travelers; Italian, Yiddish and Arabic for emigrant letters; Mbyá and Quechua for Native and Andean journeys.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** questions about Mbyá journeys may touch sacred matters not for outsiders, so ask first; the dictatorship's exiles and Peronist tourism are contested ground; migrant status can be sensitive for informants.
- **Held for review:** none set aside; the nearest call was the Guevara diary, a pointer that could slide into hero-worship, so it is offered only as one traveler's own text.
