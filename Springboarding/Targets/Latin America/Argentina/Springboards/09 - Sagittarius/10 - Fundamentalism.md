## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare traditionalist Catholic parishes, ultra-Orthodox congregations and secular neighbors on how each draws its boundaries.
- **The Conurbano:** check strict Pentecostal churches' rules on dress, music and media, as members state them.
- **Córdoba and Mendoza:** check Catholic-nationalist circles in the universities and officer corps over time.
- **The Litoral and Misiones:** check how the mission-era separation is remembered by Guaraní and Mbyá communities.
- **La Pampa:** check the Old Colony Mennonites beside their *criollo* and Volga German neighbors.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check whether Qom and Wichí churches are called "fundamentalist" by outsiders, and what members say.
- **The Jewish community:** separate Chabad, Syrian Jewish, Conservative and secular congregations before reading any strictness measure.
- **Muslim communities:** check how each mosque describes its own teaching and its ties to the wider public, in its own words.
- **Evangelicals:** separate historical Protestant, Pentecostal and neo-Pentecostal churches.
- **Leavers:** check ex-members' accounts across these groups.
