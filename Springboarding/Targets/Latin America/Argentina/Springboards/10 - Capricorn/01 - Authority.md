# Argentina (without Patagonia): Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with *lunfardo*; Northwest Spanish; Correntino Guaraní; Wichí; Quechua of the Puna; Arabic; Ukrainian; Korean. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check police stops of young people before and after the edicts were repealed, district by district.
- **Córdoba:** check its provincial misdemeanor code and how police detentions are reported there, against the capital.
- **Santa Fe and Rosario:** check trust in police where the drug trade is a daily story.
- **Tucumán:** check accounts of the 1975 army operation in the hills and how each side tells it.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** compare landlord, Kolla community and provincial authority over Puna pasture.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní terms of address for men of standing, and the authority of Mbyá leaders beside village officials.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check who speaks for Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá communities to the provincial state, people by people.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese, Ukrainian and Korean communities:** check who holds authority inside each: board, clergy, elders.
- **Catholic, Evangelical and Mennonite congregations:** compare a bishop's, a pastor's and an elder's authority over their members.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on coups, presidents and police; mixed on teachers and families; thin on Moqoit and Pilagá leadership.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and *lunfardo* for police and rock sources; colonial Spanish and Guaraní for mission papers; Wichí for leaders' own accounts; Ukrainian and Korean for community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the coups, the dictatorship of 1976-83 and Peronism are contested: every reading is one account. Some Wichí and Qom leaders' disputes are internal community business; ask first.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the rock song was the close call, since lyrics stay under copyright: cite the title and give no lines.
