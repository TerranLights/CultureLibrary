## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check the non-voter register by district, and who gets out of polling-station service.
- **The Pampa (Buenos Aires province and La Pampa):** check Volga German and Mennonite households for duties to parents and congregation.
- **Córdoba and Santa Fe-Rosario:** check mutual-aid societies' rules on what members owed one another.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check promises in Guaraní speech, and Polish and Ukrainian church duties in the colonies.
- **Salta, Jujuy, Tucumán and Catamarca:** check Kolla and Diaguita community labor owed to the community, and fiesta duties.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check seasonal migrants' earnings sent back to rural households, in Quichua-speaking families.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check how Qom, Wichí and Moqoit households describe duties among kin, each in their own terms.
- **Jewish community:** compare burial, charity and school duties in Once and in the Entre Ríos colonies.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check remittance duties and who in the family is expected to send.
- **Malvinas veterans:** separate conscripts' and officers' accounts of service owed and not repaid.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on conscription, the vote and school rites; mixed on kin care; thin on Indigenous duty terms outside the Andes.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Quechua for Inca and colonial labor terms; Guaraní for promises; Yiddish for burial-society minutes; Polish, Ukrainian, Armenian and Plautdietsch for community archives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Malvinas / Falklands war (the name is contested) and conscription under the dictatorship are contested: give each account as an account. Burial-society records hold private family data.
- **Held for review:** nothing; family-law rulings came nearest, since they name minors; keep to published summaries.
