## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check how commuters, unions and families assigned blame after the Once crash, line by line.
- **Córdoba:** check how its long Jesuit past is used in present arguments about who answers for the colonial estates.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán:** compare sugar-company workers' and owners' accounts of company blame under the dictatorship.
- **Salta, the Chaco and Formosa:** check Wichí, Qom, Pilagá and criollo families' accounts of who must answer for forest loss and land delays.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Mbyá and Guaraní-speaking farm families' terms for taking charge of land and kin.
- **Okinawan, Korean and Chinese communities:** check associations' rules on who answers for a member's debt or failure.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check which children carry adult dealings for their families.
- **The army and the Catholic Church:** compare each institution's public statements of fault and the replies of relatives' groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on disasters, trials and institutional statements; mixed on the household load; thin on Qom and Wichí terms for answering for a wrong.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial paleography; Qom and Wichí; Japanese, Korean and Chinese for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** blame for the dictatorship of 1976-83, firms' complicity and the "two demons" account are contested; give each as an account. Rail-crash families are living people; use what they made public.
