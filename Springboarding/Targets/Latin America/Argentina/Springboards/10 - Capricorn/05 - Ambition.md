## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check aspirations by district against Dalle's mobility findings.
- **The Pampa:** check how Italian- and Spanish-descended farm families steered sons to land, trade or degrees.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check the university and industry as two ladders, and who chose which.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** compare old landed families, Kolla migrants to the cities and the sugar towns' young people.
- **Santiago del Estero, Catamarca and La Rioja:** check out-migration as the main route to rising, and what is sent home.
- **Cuyo:** check wine and mining jobs as local ladders against moving to Buenos Aires.
- **Misiones and Corrientes:** check Ukrainian, Polish and Guaraní-speaking farm families' hopes for children.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Wichí accounts of what rising means for a family and a community.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check paths from shop to profession across generations, family by family.
- **Korean, Chinese and Bolivian communities:** check parents' hopes for children born in Argentina, each in its own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on mobility and immigrant history; mixed on youth hopes; thin on Native and Paraguayan accounts in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; 16th-century Spanish for the *capitulación*; Italian; Guaraní; Korean, Chinese and Arabic for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** income and family stories identify living people; use published work. Peronism's promise of upward mobility is contested ground: give each reading as an account.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the closest call was the magazine lists, a practitioner and boosters' source to read with care, not a rule issue.
