*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish; colonial church Spanish; Qom and Wichí; Yiddish; Italian; Arabic; Korean. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** check where teacher points lists and civil-service contests are used, and where contracts are.
- **The Pampa:** check whether farm families credit work or land for a household's success, in their own accounts.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check the universities' research ranks against private-sector hiring by contacts.
- **Tucumán, Salta and Jujuy:** check provincial public jobs and how candidates describe getting them.
- **Santiago del Estero, Catamarca and La Rioja:** check hiring in provinces where the state is the largest employer, in workers' own words.
- **Cuyo:** check the wine industry's tasting and quality contests as merit for firms and growers.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní-speaking and Mbyá teachers' routes to a post.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá teachers against the provincial points rules.
- **Afro-Argentines:** check how service records of Black officers and soldiers were kept and later read, in community histories.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Korean communities:** check scholarships and prizes each gives, and on what grounds.
- **Italian-descended families:** check immigrant-era accounts of getting placed through a *paesano*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on teaching, the civil service and sport; mixed on attitudes; thin on Qom and Wichí accounts of earned standing.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial church Latin and Spanish for parish contests; Qom and Wichí; Yiddish, Italian, Arabic and Korean for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the meritocracy quarrel and public-sector hiring are partisan ground: give each side as an account. Teacher and civil-servant lists name people; use aggregates.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the polo lists were the nearest, being a ranking of living players; use the public lists only.
