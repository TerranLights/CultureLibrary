## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check tower-block solitude against old-barrio life, and how residents and newcomers each describe the city's warmth or coldness.
- **The Conurbano:** check whether neighbors in *villas* and gated estates describe more distance or less.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check rural reserve with strangers in farm towns and the Mennonite colony's separateness.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check how students from small towns describe arriving in a big city.
- **Catamarca, La Rioja and Tucumán:** check Esquiú's place in local devotion and ideals of renunciation.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check composure at Kolla funerals and feasts against city mourning.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Mbyá ideals of discipline and the Ukrainian and Polish settlers' church teaching on detachment.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check how Qom, Wichí and Pilagá speakers describe showing or holding back feeling, avoiding outsiders' labels.
- **Japanese-Argentine families:** check how *gaman* is taught and how younger generations judge it.
- **Jewish, Italian and Syrian-Lebanese families:** check open and reserved emotional styles across immigrant generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in essays, literature and film on urban solitude; mixed in psychology; thin on Native and rural emotional styles in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; early 19th-century English; Mbyá Guaraní; Italian; Japanese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** claims that a people is "cold" or "warm" are outsiders' stereotypes: record them only as claims. Bystander silence under the dictatorship is contested ground; give each account as an account. Mbyá teachings may be restricted; use what communities publish.
