# Argentina (without Patagonia): Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish; Mbyá and Corrientes Guaraní; Quechua of the Northwest and Bolivian barrios; Italian (migrant letters); Yiddish; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come; for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check hopes for the next generation across old immigrant families and new arrivals.
- **The Conurbano:** check where residents place hope: church, union, school, saint or lottery.
- **The Pampa and La Pampa:** check farm families' hopes tied to rain, prices and the children who leave.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check students' hopes against the local labor market.
- **Salta, Jujuy and Tucumán:** check Kolla communities' hopes for land and the young, in their own words.
- **Cuyo:** check *rogativas* and wine-harvest hopes beside Huarpe accounts.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** compare Mbyá teaching on the land without evil with Guaraní-speaking Catholics' saints.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom, Wichí, Moqoit and Pilagá church and community hopes after past violence.
- **Jewish, Syrian-Lebanese and Armenian communities:** check hopes tied to two homelands.
- **Bolivian, Paraguayan and Peruvian communities:** check the hopes that brought them and those sent home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in religion, song and migrant letters; mixed in expectations surveys; thin on Native hopes in published first-person form.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Guaraní and Mbyá; Quechua; Italian for migrant letters; Yiddish and Arabic for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mbyá prophetic teaching may be restricted; use what communities publish. The Centenary and the children's city sit in contested political memory: give each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the closest call was the Clastres synthesis, an outsider's reading of Guaraní prophecy, kept on condition that Mbyá sources come first.
