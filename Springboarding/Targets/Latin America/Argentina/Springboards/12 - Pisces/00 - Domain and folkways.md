# Argentina (without Patagonia): The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with *lunfardo*; the Spanish of Santiago del Estero and the Northwest; Santiago Quichua; Correntino Guaraní; Qom; Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** how a people meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden and the unconscious, confinement, transcendence, and the magic ways that handle luck, harm and hidden forces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check how analytic talk, parish and folk healing sit in one household.
- **The Conurbano:** compare Pentecostal deliverance prayer with *curanderos* for the same complaint.
- **The Pampa:** check how small towns keep the memory of healers.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua vows, the Telesita and wakes as ways of meeting suffering and the unseen.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní ghost words, *payé* and the folk-saint shrines along the roads.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Wichí healers and dream accounts, each in its own terms and as each people shares them.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** check Kolla families' handling of illness caused by the earth or by fright.
- **Córdoba and Cuyo:** check detention memory sites and the asylum reform as two remembered confinements.
- **Jewish families of Once and the Entre Ríos colonies:** check what tales of spirits and possession came with Yiddish speakers.
