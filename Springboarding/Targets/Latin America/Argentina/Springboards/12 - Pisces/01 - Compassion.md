*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare parish, Jewish and secular charities on who they name as deserving.
- **The Conurbano:** check how *villa* residents describe being pitied by outside volunteers.
- **Salta:** check Wichí organizations' own words for help and for intrusion, beside the province's.
- **Misiones:** compare Mbyá communities with the Ukrainian sisters' and parishes' works of mercy.
- **Corrientes and Entre Ríos:** check Guaraní mercy words in prayer and in household speech.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check care for the poor and sick in Quichua-speaking villages.
- **Córdoba and Rosario:** check the animal-protection groups and street-dog debates.
- **Tucumán:** check how press over the decades pitied or blamed sugar-harvest poverty.
- **Syrian-Lebanese families:** check whether Muslim and Christian households use *raḥma* in the same contexts.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan communities:** check whom migrants turn to first: compatriots, church or state.
- **La Pampa's Mennonite colony:** check mutual aid inside the colony and toward outsiders.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church charity and welfare debates; mixed for animal welfare; thin for Wichí, Qom and Pilagá terms of compassion in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial Spanish for the brotherhood's books; Guaraní; Wichí for community statements; Yiddish, Arabic and Ukrainian for community welfare papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the pardons are contested ground; the Wichí crisis needs the communities' own accounts first; "*planero*" is a slur.
- **Held for review:** older relief reports and press on the Northeast's Native peoples use outside names; those reports remain pointers, and the outside names appear only inside quotations.
