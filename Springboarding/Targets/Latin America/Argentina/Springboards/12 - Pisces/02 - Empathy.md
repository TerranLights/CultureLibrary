*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with *lunfardo*; Spanish of Córdoba and the Northwest; Guaraní; Qom; Quechua of Bolivian communities; Korean. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** check whether analytic training shapes how middle-class speakers describe understanding others.
- **The Conurbano:** compare how church youth groups and school programs teach "putting yourself in another's place".
- **Córdoba:** check call-center workers' accounts against those of the city's medical students.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check how emotional-education programs reach Guaraní-speaking pupils and Mbyá schools.
- **The Chaco:** check how Qom teachers in bilingual schools describe reading a child's feelings.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** check Kolla patients' accounts of being understood by health staff.
- **Mendoza and San Juan:** check how vineyard migrant workers report being heard by employers.
- **Bolivian communities of the Buenos Aires garment district:** check how they describe being understood, or not, by neighbors.
- **Korean-Argentine families of Flores:** check how parents and children describe understanding across two languages.
- **Children and grandchildren of the disappeared, and relatives of the convicted:** read each group's own account.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for psychometric and medical-education work and for memory theater; mixed for migrant health; thin for Native-language terms of feeling with another.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial Spanish for the 1555 text; Guaraní; Qom; Quechua for Bolivian patients; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the trials and the perpetrators' families are contested ground; present each account as an account. Patient studies must protect identities.
- **Held for review:** nothing was dropped; press on Córdoba's call centers tends to sketch one city's workers as a type, so that row is limited to the workers' own testimony.
