# Argentina (without Patagonia): Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Guaraní for Corrientes conscripts' families; Ukrainian; Quechua and Guaraní of Bolivian and Paraguayan workers; Italian. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city and the Conurbano:** compare how Peronist and anti-Peronist families tell the same "sacrifices" of 1951-55.
- **Santa Fe and Rosario:** check how San Lorenzo's sergeant is taught in local schools against Buenos Aires readers.
- **Salta and Jujuy:** compare the Church's martyr story of the Zenta with Wichí and other Chaco peoples' memory of mission frontiers.
- **Corrientes:** check the province's Malvinas conscripts and how their towns remember them.
- **Misiones:** compare Ukrainian and Polish pioneer memoirs with Mbyá accounts of the same land.
- **Tucumán and Santiago del Estero:** check how migrant harvest workers' families speak of the years away.
- **Bolivian and Paraguayan workers:** check what sending money home means in each community's own words.
- **Afro-Argentine and Cape Verdean Argentine families:** check their own accounts of ancestors' service in the wars.
- **Catholic, Evangelical and Jewish congregations:** check how each speaks of martyrs and of giving one's life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war and martyr memory; mixed for caregiving and remittances; thin for Native peoples' own accounts of mission-era deaths.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial Spanish and Latin for the 1683 sources; Guaraní; Ukrainian; Quechua for Bolivian families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Peronism, the dictatorship, the Malvinas / Falklands war and the Afro-Argentine "disappearance" account are contested ground; give each as an account. Veterans' suicides are sensitive; use veterans' own groups.
- **Held for review:** the 1683 mission sources call the Chaco peoples by colonial names, so the Church's martyr story is kept only beside Native accounts, with those names left inside quoted titles.
