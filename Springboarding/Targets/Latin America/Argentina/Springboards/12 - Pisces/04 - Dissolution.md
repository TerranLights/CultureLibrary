# Argentina (without Patagonia): Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish; Córdoba Spanish; Northwest Spanish with Quechua loans; Vilela and Qom; Volga German dialect; Yiddish. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self losing its edges in something larger (a person, a crowd, nature, the divine), and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare tango dancers on the embrace with Pentecostals on falling in the Spirit.
- **Córdoba:** compare cuarteto crowds with Comechingón communities rebuilding a dissolved collective identity.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** check how Quebrada towns speak of carnival's close, the *cacharpaya*.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Vilela, Qom and Moqoit speakers on language loss, each in their own terms.
- **Entre Ríos:** compare old Federación's townspeople with Volga German and Jewish colony descendants on what dissolved.
