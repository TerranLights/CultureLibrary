*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with *lunfardo*; rural Pampa Spanish; Quechua of the Northwest; Guaraní; Hebrew and Arabic religious terms; Plautdietsch. **Concept in one line:** the lines a person or group draws between self and others, and between groups, places and categories, and how firmly they are held.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare apartment-block boundaries with *conventillo* memory, when families shared one courtyard.
- **The Conurbano:** check where gated developments and settlements touch, from residents on both sides.
- **The Pampa of Buenos Aires province:** check how farm families speak of fences, roads and neighbors' cattle.
- **Catamarca and Tucumán:** compare the Inca frontier sites with Diaguita communities' own accounts of their lands.
- **Santa Fe and the Paraná islands:** check how islanders and city people draw the line between river and town.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Mbyá prayer houses closed to outsiders and how visitors are told so.
- **Formosa and the Chaco:** check Qom, Pilagá and Wichí accounts of fenced land and closed roads.
- **Jujuy:** check Kolla herders' boundary markers and the border trade's daily crossing.
- **La Pampa's Mennonite colony:** check how it marks itself off from town and state.
- **Orthodox Jewish and Muslim congregations:** check each in its own terms; do not merge them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for urban walls, frontier history and family therapy; mixed for greeting and touch; thin for Native territorial terms in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and *lunfardo*; Quechua; Guaraní; Qom, Pilagá and Wichí; Hebrew; Plautdietsch.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the frontier campaigns, the 2018 curriculum dispute and the Formosa closure are contested ground; give each as an account. Native territorial claims are live; settle no borders.
- **Held for review:** Native territorial maps were the pointer nearest a rule, because outside maps draw community lines for them; only maps the communities publish themselves are to be used, and none is reproduced here.
