# Argentina (without Patagonia): Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense Spanish with *lunfardo*; Córdoba Spanish; Northwest Spanish and Quechua; Guaraní; colonial Spanish. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare streaming, theater and *boliche* nights as escapes by class.
- **The Conurbano:** check the place of bingo halls and slot rooms in neighborhood life.
- **Córdoba:** check student nightlife and the sierras' weekend houses as two separate escapes.
- **Rosario and Santa Fe:** check what young readers and gamers say they seek in fantasy.
- **Mendoza and San Juan:** check wine tourism as visitors' escape and as workers' labor.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** compare Kolla residents of the Quebrada with the visitors they host.
- **Santiago del Estero and Tucumán:** check drinking at feasts in Quichua-speaking villages, in community terms.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check Guaraní terms for drink and play, and how families treat a drinker.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Wichí community programs on alcohol, in their own words.
- **Paraguayan and Bolivian migrant workers:** check Sunday football leagues and feasts as relief from the work week.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drug and gambling surveys and for leisure literature; mixed for regional drinking; thin for Native communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and *lunfardo*; colonial Spanish for the synods; Quechua and Guaraní; Qom and Wichí.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** drinking in Native communities is a stereotype trap: use only community-led sources; addiction records are confidential; *Fútbol para Todos* is tied to Peronist and anti-Peronist debate.
- **Held for review:** the fernet row risked reducing a province to a drink; it survives as a claim to be tested and nothing more.
