# Argentina (without Patagonia): Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Argentina (without Patagonia), region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Rioplatense and interior Spanish; Guaraní; Quechua of the Northwest; Plautdietsch and German; Arabic; Hebrew. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher (God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death); release and letting go are covered too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare recovery groups and analysts on "letting go".
- **The Conurbano:** check what Evangelical converts say they surrendered, by age and sex.
- **Corrientes:** check Malvinas conscripts' families and Guaraní sayings of God's will.
- **Córdoba:** check how the city's nuns, past and present, describe giving up the will.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** check the *despacho* in Kolla towns and the same farewell in city families.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check Quichua prayers at wakes for words of acceptance.
- **La Pampa:** check the Mennonite colony's yieldedness against neighbors' views of it.
- **The Chaco:** check how Qom and Moqoit communities tell the end of armed resistance, in their own words.
- **Syrian-Lebanese families and Argentine-born converts:** compare inherited and chosen Islam.
- **Peronist and anti-Peronist families:** read each side's account of 1955 on its own terms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political surrenders and recovery groups; mixed for end-of-life choices; thin for Native peoples' memories of defeat told in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; colonial Spanish for convent books; Guaraní; Quechua; German and Plautdietsch; Arabic.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1955, 1982 and 1989 are contested ground; give each as an account. Recovery meetings are anonymous: use published literature only.
- **Held for review:** some outside health studies call patients "fatalistic"; the label is listed as those authors' claim to be tested, never as a trait of anyone.
