## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Buenos Aires city:** compare racetrack bettors' hunches with traders and doctors on trusting the gut.
- **The Conurbano:** check how youth coaches in neighborhood clubs talk about seeing talent.
- **The Pampa:** check what trackers' and herders' skills survive among farmhands now.
- **Santiago del Estero:** check rain signs and dream warnings in Quichua-speaking villages.
- **Jujuy and Salta:** check Kolla coca readers and how townspeople regard them.
- **Bolivian communities of Buenos Aires:** check whether *yatiris* are consulted in the city, and by whom.
- **Mendoza and San Juan:** compare tasters, growers and harvest workers on judging a vintage.
- **Corrientes and Misiones:** check healers' "gift" against Mbyá accounts of knowing through dreams, as shared.
- **The Chaco and Formosa:** check Qom and Wichí knowledge of land and water, each in its own words.
- **Rosario and Córdoba:** check the cities' football academies' scouting methods.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for neuroscience and medicine; mixed for scouting and tasting; thin for Native and rural knowledge in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and *lunfardo*; Santiago Quichua; Quechua and Aymara; Guaraní; Qom and Wichí.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some diviners' and healers' knowledge is restricted; Sarmiento wrote as a partisan.
- **Held for review:** the coca-reading pointer came closest to the rule that divination is never a source; it stays strictly as a record of practice and belief, with no reading taken as knowledge.
