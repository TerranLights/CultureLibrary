## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Huaso families of O'Higgins, Maule and Ñuble: check rodeo competition across generations of riders and *criadores* (breeders).
- Santiago's private, subsidized and public schools: check how admissions-test rank is talked about at home.
- Valparaíso and Viña del Mar supporters: check port-city rivalry against rivalry with Santiago.
- Copper towns of Antofagasta and Calama and the port of Iquique: check whether a club carries a city's standing against the capital.
- Aymara, Quechua and Afro-Chilean troupes of Arica: check competition between dance groups at carnival and festivals.
- Rapa Nui families: check today's Tapati contests against the remembered birdman competition.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check *palin* as a contest and as a gathering.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check Club Deportivo Palestino's rivalries as a community matter.
- Haitian, Peruvian and Bolivian newcomers: check community leagues against entry into Chilean clubs.
- Evangelical congregations: check views on football, betting and contests.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for football, rodeo and admissions; mixed for business and workplace rivalry; thin for Juan Fernández and Chango coastal contests (fishing, rowing), where local press is the start.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including huaso and sports registers; Rapa Nui for contest names; Mapudungun for *palin* rules and calls.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rodeo is contested on animal-welfare grounds; stadium violence and the Chilean-Peruvian pisco and border rivalries need both sides.
- **Held for review:** none set aside; gambling regulation was considered and left to Libra, keeping only racing and betting as practice.
