# Chile: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America (north and center, Rapa Nui, Juan Fernández). **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish and street speech; Mapudungun; Aymara; Quechua; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Coal families of Lota and Coronel against the nitrate-era families of the Norte Grande after the *oficinas* closed: check how two mining closures are remembered as losses of standing.
- *Población* youth and university youth in Santiago: check whether *chaqueteo* and *ningunear* name different experiences in each setting.
- Pentecostal and Catholic households of the *poblaciones*: check conversion talk about being "someone" and the label *canuto*.
- Men and women after job loss or retirement: check, in health and survey data, how worth is spoken of when providing stops.
- Haitian, Venezuelan, Peruvian and Bolivian residents: check reported treatment at work and in offices, and how each community answers it.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check school and workplace accounts of surnames and appearance, and the role of urban associations.
- Aymara families who moved to Arica and Iquique: check accounts of language shame and of revival.
- Afro-Chileans of the Azapa Valley: check how recognition efforts frame the worth of a people.
- Rapa Nui elders and young people: check how 1964 is told on the island and in the mainland press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health surveys, discrimination and literature; mixed for the measures of self-esteem (mostly student samples); thin for Colla, Chango and Diaguita voices, where community organizations are the start.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and its slang for class talk; Haitian Creole for Haitian residents' own accounts; Rapa Nui for island memory of 1964; Aymara for revival speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data; the 2019 protests and their naming, which sides read differently; discrimination accounts; the *roto* and *canuto* labels.
- **Held for review:** the self-help and life-coaching market was considered and dropped as too general; nothing fell under the standing rules.
