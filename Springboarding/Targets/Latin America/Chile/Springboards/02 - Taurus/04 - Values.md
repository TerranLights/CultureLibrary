# Chile: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America (north and center, Rapa Nui, Juan Fernández). **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish; Mapudungun; Aymara; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic, Evangelical and no-religion households: check how the Bicentenario and WVS rankings of family, faith and work split by these groups.
- Generations formed before 1973, under the dictatorship, and after 1990: check whether value rankings move by cohort or by event.
- *Huaso* families of O'Higgins and Maule against Santiago's professionals: check what "keeping one's word" covers in each setting.
- Norte Grande mining families: check how the union past and the shift-work present shape stated priorities.
- Aymara communities of Arica y Parinacota and Lickanantay of the Salar: check how community norms are stated in their own terms, and by whom.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check how *az mapu* is taught in the city, and by whom.
- Rapa Nui families: check which island obligations are named first when they meet mainland ones.
- Palestinian-Chilean and Jewish community institutions: check the values their schools and clubs state publicly.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check which values orientation programs present to them as "Chilean".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys (WVS, Latinobarómetro, Bicentenario); mixed for Indigenous norms; thin for Colla and Diaguita statements, which communities would have to give themselves.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for surveys and press; Mapudungun for *az mapu*; Aymara and Rapa Nui for community terms; Arabic for the language of Orthodox parish and family teaching.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** church abuse cases; values after the dictatorship and the 2019 protests, read differently by each side; Indigenous teachings shared only on a community's terms.
- **Held for review:** scholarship on moral education by foreign authors was weighed; none was set aside here.
