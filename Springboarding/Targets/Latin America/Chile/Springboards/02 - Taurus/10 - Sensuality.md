# Chile: Sensuality (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America (north and center, Rapa Nui, Juan Fernández). **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish and colloquial speech; Mapudungun; Aymara; Kunza; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** enjoyment of the senses (taste, touch, smell, sound and sight) and of the body's comfort and pleasure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Central Valley wine and *huaso* households against Norte Chico pisco families: check which drinks mark a celebration and who drinks them.
- Santiago's *carrete* crowds and Pentecostal congregations: check how pleasure, drinking and dancing are spoken of on each side.
- Norte Grande mining towns: check how shift workers use their days off, and which pleasures are on offer.
- Lickanantay and Aymara families: check herbs, teas, carnival and drink in their own ceremonies, and what is not shared with outsiders.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check *mudai* and other foods at urban gatherings.
- Rapa Nui families: check feasting, dance and bathing in the sea, as islanders describe them.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check how Levantine food and music are kept at home and at club events.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check which foods and music they bring, and how Chilean neighbors receive them.
- Older and younger generations: check greeting kisses, touch and dance across age.
