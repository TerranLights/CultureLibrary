*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Upper-middle and working-class Santiaguinos: check how *pitutos* work at each level and how each speaks of them.
- Central Valley rural families: check ritual kinship and neighbor help against city ties.
- Norte Grande mining towns: check workmates and unions as the main web of contacts.
- Aymara families split between villages and Arica: check fiestas and associations as the threads between them.
- Lickanantay villages: check water rites and work days as connection.
- Rapa Nui: check family obligations and links to Tahiti and the wider Pacific.
- Palestinian-, Croatian- and Italian-descended families: check clubs, marriages and family firms as ties.
- Haitian, Venezuelan, Peruvian and Bolivian newcomers: check ties home (calls, remittances) and ties made in Chile.
- Older Chileans living alone: check who checks on them, by region.
