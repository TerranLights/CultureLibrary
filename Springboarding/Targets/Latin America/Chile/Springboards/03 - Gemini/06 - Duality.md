## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aymara communities of Tarapacá and Arica: check whether paired halves still organize fiestas and work.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check what is kept and what is set aside at work, school and gatherings.
- Rapa Nui families: check Polynesian and Chilean belonging, and Rapa Nui, Tahitian and Spanish in one household.
- Palestinian-, Croatian- and Italian-descended families: check two belongings at elections, wars and football.
- Arica families with Peruvian kin: check two-country lives across the line.
- Afro-Chileans of Arica: check how mixed descent is stated on forms and in daily life.
- Haitian and Venezuelan families: check the children's two languages.
- Catholic and Evangelical households: check whether sacred and worldly life are held apart or together.
- Supporters of opposite plebiscite options: check how each tells the other's story, without taking either as the norm.
