# Chile: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish; Mapudungun; Aymara; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision, and more broadly the go-between: brokers, interpreters and intercessors. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Families in Santiago's *poblaciones* against high-income *comunas*: check who is called first in a quarrel (kin, pastor, *junta*, police, mediator).
- Mining unions of Antofagasta and Calama: check how disputes reach a third party, and who is trusted in that role.
- Central Valley farm communities: check the role of the priest, the *patrón* in the past, and neighbors today.
- Catholic and Evangelical households: check intercession by saints against direct prayer.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check whether an elder or *werken* speaks between families and outsiders.
- Aymara and Lickanantay communities: check community authorities against state offices as go-betweens.
- Rapa Nui: check the elders' council and clan heads as go-betweens with mainland bodies.
- Haitian families: check children and church leaders as go-betweens with schools and employers.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check whether community figures settle family and business quarrels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church mediation, Beagle and labor figures; mixed for family and school mediation; thin for Colla, Diaguita and Chango dispute ways and for Juan Fernández.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for most records; Mapudungun and Aymara for community roles; Rapa Nui for the council's own statements; Kreyòl for migrant brokers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Church's role under the dictatorship is read differently across political accounts; Rapa Nui representation is contested; family disputes are confidential.
- **Held for review:** this sheet's go-between sources were all usable; no name or work fell under a standing rule, and law-letter material was left for Libra.
