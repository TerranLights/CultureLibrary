# Chile: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (popular and *coa* registers); Mapudungun (loanwords); Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, together with rumor: unconfirmed reports passed from person to person.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Small Central Valley towns against Santiago: check how far *el qué dirán* reaches and who says they feel it.
- Norte Grande mining camps and ports: check talk in shift work and company housing.
- Elite families of Santiago's east: check society pages and private circles as gossip channels.
- *Poblaciones*: check the *almacén*, *feria* and *pasaje* as talk places, and the line against *sapear*.
- Rapa Nui and Juan Fernández: check gossip in small island populations where everyone is related.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check whether *cahuín* is still heard with its older sense of a gathering.
- Palestinian-Chilean and Croatian-Chilean circles: check reputation and marriage talk in close communities.
- Haitian, Venezuelan and Peruvian newcomers: check rumor about papers and work.
- Catholic and Evangelical congregations: check teaching on gossip, and gossip inside congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for celebrity media and *funas*; mixed for neighborhood gossip (mostly ethnography); thin for gossip norms among the Colla, Diaguita and Chango.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and its slang; Mapudungun for loanword history; Kreyòl for migrant rumor channels; Arabic for religious teaching in Palestinian-Chilean homes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *funas* name living people and are disputed; school cases involve minors; rumors about migrants can feed hostility.
- **Held for review:** for gossip, the scan covered media, ethnography and church sources; nothing here needed to be set apart.
