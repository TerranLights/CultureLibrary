# Chile: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish; Mapudungun; Aymara; Quechua; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Owners, renters and *allegados* in the same *comuna*: check whether *la casa propia* marks adulthood differently for each.
- Antofagasta and Calama *campamentos*: check how settlers name and furnish a house they may lose.
- Santiago's eastern *comunas* and gated condominiums against the *poblaciones*: check walls, bars and the front garden as statements.
- *Huaso* families of Maule and Ñuble: check the country house and its patio, and the move to town.
- Aymara and Lickanantay families: check the village house kept for fiestas and returns.
- Rapa Nui families: check house and family land as one claim.
- Juan Fernández islanders: check the rebuilt village and who returned after 2010.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check room rental, shared kitchens and *lakay* as a word for Haiti.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check *tuwün* where a family has lived two or three generations in the city (Wallmapu, in Araucanía-Patagonia, as comparison).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for housing statistics, policy and disasters; mixed for the home as feeling (literary and ethnographic); thin for Diaguita and Colla homes and for Juan Fernández (start with municipal and community records).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for nearly all housing data; Aymara and Quechua for village house rituals; Rapa Nui for land-and-home terms; Kreyòl for newcomer renters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** evictions from *tomas*; the Rapa Nui land dispute is contested ground, to be read as competing accounts; homes lost in disasters.
- **Held for review:** architecture-press features on Chilean houses were considered for the home as design; their authorship was unclear, so they were dropped instead of held, and no rule applied.
