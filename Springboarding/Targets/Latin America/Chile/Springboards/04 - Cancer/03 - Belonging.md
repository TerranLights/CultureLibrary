# Chile: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Old Santiago families and new arrivals from the regions: check how long it takes to be *santiaguino*.
- *Nortinos* of Iquique, Antofagasta and Calama: check what makes one a northerner after migration from the south and abroad.
- *Porteños* of Valparaíso and Viña del Mar: check the *cerro* as the unit one belongs to.
- *Huaso* communities of O'Higgins, Maule and Ñuble: check admission to clubs and teams.
- Aymara, Lickanantay, Diaguita and Colla peoples: check who a community counts as its own, and how descent and residence weigh.
- Afro-Chileans of Arica and the Azapa valley: check belonging to the nation and to the north.
- Rapa Nui and mainland residents of the island: check the *popa'a* line and mixed families.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check what *warriache* and *champurria* mean to those who use them.
- Palestinian, Jewish, Croatian and Italian communities: check clubs and schools as gates.
- Haitian, Venezuelan, Peruvian and Bolivian newcomers: check where and when they are called *chilenos*.
- Evangelical and Catholic congregations: check membership and felt belonging in the *poblaciones*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration and identity studies; mixed for local belonging (mostly ethnographies); thin for the Chango and for Juan Fernández (start with municipal and island sources).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Mapudungun and Aymara for insider and outsider terms; Rapa Nui for island usage; Kreyòl and Arabic for newcomer and Palestinian-Chilean sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** anti-migrant events are contested ground, to be read as competing accounts; slurs and labels (*turco*, *champurria*) are research objects only; Rapa Nui belonging is tied to the island's contested status.
- **Held for review:** a Buddhist community was weighed as a further belonging group and is held, as a category only, for the author's decision on Buddhism.
