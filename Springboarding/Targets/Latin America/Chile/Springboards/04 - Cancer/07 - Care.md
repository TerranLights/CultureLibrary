# Chile: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish; Mapudungun; Aymara; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Families who hire help and families who cannot: check who bathes, feeds and sits up with an elder.
- *Huaso* and rural families of Maule and Ñuble: check folk healers, bonesetters and the daughter who stays.
- Norte Grande mining families: check who takes on home care after a work injury or lung disease.
- Aymara and Quechua families: check *yatiri*, clinic and kin in caring for the sick.
- Lickanantay and Colla communities: check herbal knowledge and distance to clinics.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check *lawen* and urban healers beside the public clinic.
- Rapa Nui: check care on an island far from specialist hospitals, and evacuation to the mainland.
- Peruvian and Haitian women: check care work in Chilean homes and care sent home.
- Catholic and Evangelical congregations: check visits to the sick and prayer for healing.
- Men who care for a wife or parent: check how they describe the work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for policy, surveys and the care economy; mixed for folk and Indigenous healing; thin for Juan Fernández and for Diaguita and Chango care (start with island and municipal health posts).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Aymara for healers in the north; Mapudungun for urban healers; Rapa Nui for island practice; Kreyòl for Haitian carers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some healing knowledge is held within families and communities; health records are closed; care-home abuse cases.
- **Held for review:** the 1980s *ollas comunes* literature was weighed and left to its earlier sign, so the 2020 kitchens stand in for neighborly care; no item here was caught by a standing rule.
