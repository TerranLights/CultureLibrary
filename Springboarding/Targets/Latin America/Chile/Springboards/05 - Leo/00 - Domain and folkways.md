## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Santiago's eastern *comunas* and its *poblaciones*: check which displays each calls *siútico* or *picante*, since those labels sort the domain.
- Norte Grande ports and Andean villages: check how dance societies and Carnival divide festive from devotional time.
- *Huaso* families of O'Higgins, Maule and Ñuble: check the *medialuna*, *ramada* and *trilla* as occasions to shine.
- Valparaíso and the coal towns of Lota and Coronel: check murals and fireworks in the port, bands and clubs in the mining towns.
- Aymara, Quechua and Lickanantay communities: check who takes the fiesta sponsor's role (*alférez* *check*) and its cost.
- Diaguita, Colla and Rapa Nui: check revival pottery and ceremony in the Norte Chico, and which island families field Tapati candidates.
- *Warriache* of Santiago: check song and *palin* as Mapuche display in the city (Wallmapu, in Araucanía-Patagonia, as comparison).
- Afro-Chileans of Arica: check the *tumbe carnaval* (*check*) as revival and claim.
- Palestinian-, Croatian- and Basque-descended families; Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check club galas, and where newcomers may hold parties.
- Evangelical and Pentecostal churches: check what members are taught about *cueca* and the *fondas* in September.

## 4. Flags
