*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- University students in Santiago, Valparaíso and Concepción: check how *mechoneo* differs by faculty, and who refuses it.
- Secondary students in municipal and private schools: check what insults mark a target, and where teachers step in.
- Workers in mining camps and in retail: check what counts as a humiliating boss, by trade.
- *Warriache* families in Santiago: check memories of surname shame and of renaming.
- Rapa Nui families: check how the sheep-company years are remembered as degradation.
- Arica's fishers and Aymara residents: check how the 2014 ruling and older border wounds are told.
- Peruvian and Bolivian newcomers: check what they report hearing at matches and at work.
- Haitian newcomers: check mockery of accent and color, as they recount it.
- Former political prisoners and their families: check how they choose to speak of degradation.
- Pilgrims of the Central Valley: check where chosen lowering is admired and where it is doubted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school, sport and memory sites; mixed for workplace and family humiliation; thin for Diaguita, Colla and Chango experience and for Juan Fernández (start with community leaders and local schools).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including vulgar and youth registers; colonial paleography; Aymara and Quechua shame words; Mapudungun for urban testimony; Kreyòl for newcomers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** detention-center testimony is survivors' to give, and the dictatorship is contested ground; mockery of neighbors' nations touches the War of the Pacific; minors in bullying cases must not be named.
- **Held for review:** the Rettig and Valech reports were weighed for degradation in custody; they already stand on the Gemini sheet on information, so here the Villa Grimaldi site and its own testimony carry that question.
