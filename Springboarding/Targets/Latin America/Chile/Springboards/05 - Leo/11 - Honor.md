# Chile: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (including *coa*); Aymara; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- *Huaso* families and cattle traders of the Central Valley: check how far a spoken deal binds, and how a breach is punished.
- Navy and army families of Valparaíso and Talcahuano: check how service honor passes from parents to children.
- Volunteer fire companies of each immigrant community: check the honor of the company against that of its founding community.
- Inmates and their families: check prison codes and the *coa* words for honor and shame.
- Rural women of Maule and Ñuble: check whether a woman's good name rests on conduct, and how that has changed (a question, not an assumption).
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check *sharaf* in marriage choices and in business.
- Aymara households: check the fiesta sponsor's renown and the cost of refusing.
- Rapa Nui families: check honor tied to lineage names and *ahu*.
- Football supporters of Colo-Colo, Universidad de Chile and Cobreloa: check the honor of the colors, and fights in their name.
- Haitian newcomers: check what *bon non* means in a new country.
- Masons and Catholic lay brotherhoods: check oaths and the weight of the given word.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** colonial honor and political duels have a good paper trail; everyday honor among men and women rests on ethnographies and press; Colla, Chango and Diaguita codes need fieldwork with community leaders.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including colonial hands and *coa*; Aymara for sponsorship; Rapa Nui for lineage names; Arabic for Palestinian-Chilean families; Kreyòl for newcomers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence justified by honor, inside families or between men, is to be researched in the words of those who lived it; the 1891 civil war and the armed forces are contested ground; military oaths and discipline as duty go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the Mapuche *admapu* and warrior ideals were weighed and left for the Araucanía-Patagonia sheets; colonial *injurias* suits already stand on the Taurus sheet on self-worth.
