## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Older people in Santiago, Valparaíso and Concepción: check which con scripts reach them and how families step in.
- *Huaso* breeders and rodeo judges of O'Higgins, Maule and Ñuble: check how the eye for an animal is passed on.
- Wine families of Colchagua and the Maule and small growers of the Itata: check whose palate counts as expert.
- Santiago's eastern *comunas* and its *poblaciones*: check the labels each uses to size up strangers.
- Aymara and Quechua families of the far north: check when a specialist is consulted and when a doctor, lawyer or priest is.
- Mapuche households of Santiago's *comunas*: check how dreams and elders' counsel are weighed against school and work advice; Mapuche life in Wallmapu belongs to Araucanía-Patagonia and enters only as comparison.
- Rapa Nui: check how islanders judge outsiders' offers on land and business.
- Catholic, Pentecostal and Jewish communities: check how a calling, a marriage or a prophecy is tested.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check how trust in business partners and suitors is weighed.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check which advisers they trust on papers and jobs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for consumer fraud, wine criticism and hiring discrimination; mixed for spiritual and dream discernment; thin for Diaguita, Colla and Chango practice (start with community leaders, with consent).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for press, police and regulators; Aymara and Quechua for ritual readings; Mapudungun for dream accounts; Rapa Nui for island counsel; Kreyòl for scam warnings in Haitian groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Aymara and Mapuche guidance practices are private and should be recorded only with leave; the constitutional campaigns are contested ground; fraud victims may not wish to be named.
- **Held for review:** reality-TV matchmaking formats were weighed for the "judging a partner" facet and dropped as imported formats with little local shaping; no item was caught by a standing rule.
