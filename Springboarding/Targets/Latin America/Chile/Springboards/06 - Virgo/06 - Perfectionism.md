# Chile: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (including school and youth slang); Rapa Nui; Aymara; Mapudungun; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Pupils of selective public schools, subsidized schools and private schools: check how much pressure each describes and from whom.
- Families in Santiago's eastern *comunas* and in the *poblaciones*: check what a "perfect" house or party has to show to guests.
- Military and naval families of Valparaíso and Santiago: check drill and inspection as standards carried into home life.
- Central Valley *huaso* and *cueca* families: check judged standards in dance, dress and horsemanship.
- Builders and engineers of Concepción and the Biobío coast: check how the 2010 earthquake changed talk of tolerances.
- Aymara and Lickanantay weavers of the north: check what counts as fine work and whether an irregularity is meant.
- Rapa Nui carvers: check the island's own standards for a carving against what tourists buy.
- Pentecostal and Catholic congregations: check holiness and scrupulosity in counsel and testimonies.
- Palestinian- and Croatian-descended family firms: check how quality is checked and taught to heirs.
- Haitian and Venezuelan pupils and workers: check how unfamiliar grading and quality rules are met.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for academic pressure, standards and the body; mixed for craft standards; thin for perfection in Diaguita, Colla and Chango craft and ritual (start with community craft groups).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for schools, norms and the press; Rapa Nui for carving terms; Aymara for weaving terms; Kreyòl for school experiences of Haitian pupils.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders and youth suicide must be approached with care; the Parada Militar's meaning after 1973 is contested ground; Rapa Nui quarry sites are protected.
- **Held for review:** the Japanese ideas of beauty in imperfection that the concept list offers as outside examples were weighed and kept off the sheet, since they open no Chilean source; nothing here was caught by a standing rule.
