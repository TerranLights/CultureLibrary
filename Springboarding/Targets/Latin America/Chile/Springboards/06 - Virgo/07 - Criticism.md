# Chile: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Office workers in Santiago and the regional capitals: check whether criticism of a boss goes up the line or only sideways.
- Families in the eastern *comunas*, the middle-class *comunas* and the *poblaciones*: check how children are scolded and how teachers' notes are taken.
- Valparaíso and Concepción: check student and port-union traditions of open critique.
- Mining communities of the Norte Grande: check criticism of companies through unions, consultations and the press.
- Aymara and Quechua communities: check elders' admonition inside the village against formal complaints to the state.
- Lickanantay and Colla communities: check how criticism of water and lithium projects is voiced and recorded.
- Rapa Nui: check criticism of the state and of tourism inside the island's own bodies.
- Mapuche families and organizations in Santiago: check elders' counsel and public statements as two kinds of critique.
- Catholic, Pentecostal and Jewish communities: check fraternal correction, church discipline and criticism of leaders.
- Palestinian-Chilean community: check public criticism of events in Palestine and its reception in Chile.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check what criticism of Chile they feel free to voice, and where.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, press history and political criticism; mixed for workplace and family criticism; thin for correction practices among the Diaguita, Chango and on Juan Fernández (start with community leaders and municipal councils).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for satire, press and school records; Aymara, Quechua and Kunza revival terms for consultation records; Mapudungun for elders' counsel; Kreyòl for newcomers' accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** criticism under the dictatorship, the Rettig and Valech accounts, and the 2019 *estallido* are contested ground; school records concern minors; consultation files may be disputed in court.
- **Held for review:** the *funa* (public shaming of an offender) was considered and left out, since Chile's Anger (Aries) and Gossip (Gemini) sheets already carry it; no pointer here fell under a standing rule.
