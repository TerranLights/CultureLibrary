# Chile: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish; Aymara; Quechua; Kunza; Rapa Nui; Mapudungun; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- High-income and low-income *comunas* of Santiago: check how admission by lottery and by test is argued in each.
- Families of the Central Valley: check how farmland and houses are split among siblings, and the place of the child who stayed.
- Norte Grande mining towns: check pay bands between contractors' and direct employees and what each calls unfair.
- Artisanal fishers of the *caletas*, including Chango-descended families: check how the quota system is judged on the quay.
- Aymara and Lickanantay irrigators: check who calls the water turns and how drought shares are argued.
- Rapa Nui and mainland residents on the island: check the residence law as each tells it.
- Mapuche workers in Santiago: check complaints of unequal treatment under the 2012 law.
- Haitian, Venezuelan and Peruvian workers: check pay complaints and the sense of a "price for foreigners".
- Palestinian-Chilean and Croatian-descended family firms: check how heirs and partners divide the business.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for competition cases, admission policy and survey items; mixed for inheritance practice; thin for Colla and Diaguita water-sharing and for Juan Fernández lobster shares.
- **Languages needed:** legal and economic Spanish; Aymara and Kunza for irrigation turns; Rapa Nui for the residence debate; Kreyòl for migrant pay complaints.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the fishing-quota law and admission reform are contested in politics; competition files hold confidential evidence.
- **Held for review:** one cross-national experiment on children's sharing was weighed, but it sampled no Chilean site, so it went out on relevance; no standing rule was involved.
