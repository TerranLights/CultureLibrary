*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- *Huaso* families and former *medieros* of O'Higgins and Maule: check how half-share bonds are recalled and whether any survive.
- Norte Chico small miners and pisco growers: check partnership by contract against partnership by kinship.
- Norte Grande mining couples with long rotating shifts: check how partners divide decisions across weeks apart.
- Santiago's eastern *comunas* and *poblaciones*: check marriage against living together, and pooled against separate money.
- Aymara and Quechua households: check how herds and town wages are shared within the couple.
- Lickanantay and Rapa Nui bodies: check how they describe their co-management bonds with the state, in their own terms.
- Mapuche couples in Santiago: check marriage within and outside the people, and how kin on both sides weigh in.
- Palestinian-Chilean and Croatian-descended families: check how business partners are chosen among kin.
- Haitian, Venezuelan and Bolivian couples: check partners separated by migration and family reunification.
- Catholic and Pentecostal couples: check what each church teaches about headship and equal partnership.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for coalitions, cooperatives and marital statistics; mixed for sharecropping and small-mine partnerships (often oral); thin for Colla, Diaguita and Chango couples and for Juan Fernández fishing crews.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for registry and archive; Aymara for highland households; Mapudungun for urban kin terms; Rapa Nui for the park agreement as the island reads it; Arabic and Croatian for family-firm papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Concertación's record and the island's co-management are argued over by rival camps; domestic money arrangements are private.
- **Held for review:** a foreign couples-therapy model sold in Santiago clinics was weighed for the couple row and left out as imported practice, not a Chilean source; no item met a standing rule.
