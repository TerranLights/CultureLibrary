# Chile: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (legal and popular); Aymara; Rapa Nui; Mapudungun; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Families of the eastern *comunas* and graduates of the *liceos emblemáticos*: check how each speaks of entry by merit and by name.
- Small *comunas* of Ñuble, Maule and the Elqui Valley: check how hiring rules on relatives work where kin fill the town.
- Norte Grande mining towns: check contractor selection and local-hire promises, and who is said to get the posts.
- Aymara and Quechua communities: check how community officers are chosen and how disputes over favoritism are aired.
- Lickanantay organizations of the Salar: check how benefit funds from mining are allotted among communities.
- Rapa Nui: check clan ties in island posts and in the Ma'u Henua hiring.
- Mapuche organizations in Santiago: check how municipal funds for Indigenous associations are assigned.
- Palestinian-Chilean and Croatian-descended business families: check what *wāsṭa* or its Spanish equivalents mean in firms and clubs.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check treatment at migration counters and whether fixers are paid to move a file.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for probity, lobbying and electoral law; mixed for municipal nepotism (scattered rulings); thin for Colla, Diaguita and Juan Fernández offices.
- **Languages needed:** legal Spanish for the codes and rulings; Aymara for rotating community offices; Rapa Nui for clan and hiring talk; Arabic for diaspora usage; Kreyòl for migrants' dealings with offices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 2024 judicial scandal is open in the courts; disclosure data concern named persons; island hiring disputes involve named families.
- **Held for review:** Chilean hiring-audit studies by surname and address were considered for the bias row but already carry the Virgo discernment sheet; the elite-school studies sit on the Taurus wealth sheet; neither was copied here, and no standing rule applied.
