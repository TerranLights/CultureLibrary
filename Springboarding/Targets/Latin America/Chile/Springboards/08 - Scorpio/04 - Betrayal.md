*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Coal-town families of Lota and Coronel and nitrate-era families of the north: check how strikebreakers were treated and remembered across generations.
- Santiago's *poblaciones* and prisons: check the cost of being called *sapo*, and who enforces silence.
- *Huaso* families of the Central Valley: check the old estate's loyalty to the *patrón* and what leaving it meant.
- Rapa Nui families: check the 1888 agreement's two texts and how each generation tells the broken promise.
- Aymara communities of Arica y Parinacota: check accusations of siding with the state or with firms in water disputes.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check how broken frontier parliaments are recalled in the city; the events belong to Araucanía-Patagonia and serve as comparison.
- Survivors and families of the 1973 period, and military families: check what each calls the betrayal of that year.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check how loyalty to Chile and to Palestine is weighed, and the word *khiyāna* in community debate.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check accounts of being betrayed by recruiters or landlords.
- Pentecostal and Catholic congregations: check teaching on adultery and the shame of leaving the church.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political betrayal and labor history; mixed for infidelity; thin for betrayal in Colla, Diaguita and Chango communities, where none of the usual sources exist.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for Valdivia; Rapa Nui for the 1888 text; *coa* for prison codes; Kreyòl for migrants' accounts of fraud.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1973, collaborators' memoirs and the Rapa Nui agreement are contested; read each text as an account. Domestic violence files are confidential.
- **Held for review:** experimental trust games were weighed for the betrayal-aversion facet; no Chilean study is known well enough here to name, so the facet waits for the research stage.
