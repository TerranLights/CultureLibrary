# Chile: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (standard, popular, *coa*, press); Aymara; Quechua; Mapudungun; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Nitrate-era families of Iquique and the pampa: check whether 1907 is remembered as a debt to be paid or a loss to be mourned.
- Peruvian- and Bolivian-descended families of Arica, Iquique and Antofagasta: check memory of the leagues and of the expulsions.
- Santiago's *poblaciones* (La Legua, Bajos de Mena and others): check retaliation between crews and who tries to stop it.
- *Huaso* families of the Central Valley: check feuds over water, fences and honor at rodeos and fairs.
- Concepción and the Biobío coast: check how the frontier wars are taught near where they were fought.
- Rapa Nui: check how island tellers frame the Poike war today.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check how frontier raids are told by families who came north (the frontier itself falls under Araucanía-Patagonia).
- Families of the disappeared and of the executed, and former members of the forces: compare how their associations draw the line between justice and vengeance in public statements.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check reprisals against migrants after reported crimes.
- Pentecostal and Catholic congregations: check teaching on forgiving the killer of a relative.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for political reprisal and frontier chronicles; mixed for gang retaliation; thin for feud customs among Aymara, Lickanantay, Diaguita and Colla communities (ask the communities themselves).
- **Languages needed:** the Rosales chronicle needs seventeenth-century Spanish; the Poike story needs Rapa Nui; crews' payback talk is in *coa*; attacks on migrants are reported in Kreyòl and Venezuelan Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reprisals of 1986 to 1987, the *funas* and the massacre's aftermath are contested ground with living families; gang violence endangers informants. Private vengeance against the state's punishment is the spirit of the law, left for Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside under a rule; revenge telenovelas fit the expression facet, but no title is certain enough to name.
