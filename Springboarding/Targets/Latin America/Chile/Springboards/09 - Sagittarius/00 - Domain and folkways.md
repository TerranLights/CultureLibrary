# Chile: The domain, belief and philosophy (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America (core: the Norte Grande to the Biobío, with Rapa Nui and the Juan Fernández Islands). **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (standard, *huaso* and northern rural speech, Santiago *coa*); Mapudungun; Aymara; Quechua; Kunza; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** what is held true and where meaning is sought: religion and its absence, philosophy, higher learning, the foreign and far away, and the religious and freedom ways that carry them.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic parishes against Pentecostal congregations in one *población*: check which rites, calendars and kinds of leadership each keeps.
- *Huaso* households from Rancagua to Chillán: check where the *canto a lo divino* survives and who learns it.
- Coastal and Norte Chico *chino* brotherhoods: check their ties to the parish and to fishing coves.
- Aymara and Quechua villages above Arica and Pozo Almonte: check which rites move down to the coast with migrants.
- Lickanantay communities around San Pedro de Atacama: check the *talatur* and Kunza revival as religious ways.
- Rapa Nui: check how parish life and pre-mission knowledge are kept side by side.
- *Warriache* in Pudahuel or Cerro Navia: check urban Mapuche rites; rites in Wallmapu are Araucanía-Patagonia's, a comparison only.
- Palestinian-Chilean, Jewish and Muslim communities: check their own institutions, schools and calendars.
- Haitian believers: check whether they found Creole-language churches or join Chilean ones.
- Secular and Masonic circles: check where unbelief and laic thought are organized.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic history, Pentecostal sociology and survey series; mixed for philosophy (one strong history, scattered monographs); scarce for Chango and Colla religious life and for the Juan Fernández Islands. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled (where the southern line runs, Rapa Nui's status); Sagittarius is scouted for the north, the center and both island groups.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish and Mapudungun for Valdivia's grammar; Aymara and Quechua for highland rites; Rapa Nui for hymns; Kreyòl for newer congregations; Arabic for Orthodox and Muslim community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aymara, Lickanantay and Rapa Nui ritual knowledge may be withheld from outsiders; clergy abuse cases sit in other sheets; any religious-freedom statute is Libra's or Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** for the map of smaller faiths, Buddhist practice in Chile is held as a category only, its place being an open question for the author; no group, temple or text is named.
