## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Practicing Catholics and those who call themselves *creyente sin religión*: check which doctrines each accepts or drops.
- Pentecostal congregations of the Biobío coal towns: check written doctrine against the beliefs members voice in testimony.
- Miners and *pirquineros* of Atacama and Coquimbo: check mining beliefs held alongside company safety rules.
- Aymara villagers of Putre and Socoroma and their relatives in Arica: check what the city keeps of village beliefs.
- Lickanantay *ayllus*: check belief statements made in Kunza revival settings.
- Rapa Nui islanders: check how Catholic teaching and older beliefs are named and kept apart or together.
- Mapuche *warriache*: check belief in *machi* healing in Santiago; the *machi* of Wallmapu (Araucanía-Patagonia) only as a comparison.
- Haitian families: check whether a belief from home is spoken of openly or kept private.
- Palestinian-Chilean Orthodox and Muslim Chileans: check what each community teaches its children as creed.
- Unbelievers and positivist heirs: check freethought statements in Masonic and secular publications.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church doctrine and colonial synods; mixed for folk beliefs (collections are old and need fresh fieldwork); thin for Haitian belief life and for Chango and Colla beliefs.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish; Aymara for village rites; Kunza word lists for Lickanantay terms; Rapa Nui for creator stories; Kreyòl for Haitian families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Haitian Vodou is stigmatized and should be approached only through community voices; Rapa Nui sites are protected; the 1974 declaration is contested ground; freedom of belief as a statute belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the Inquisition history by José Toribio Medina was considered for beliefs once punished as heresy and left out, since the Gemini gossip and Scorpio purging sheets already carry it.
