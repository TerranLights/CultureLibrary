## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Party militants of the left and right in Santiago: check how each tradition praises *ser consecuente* and punishes switching.
- Catholic social activists and Pentecostal pastors in the same *población*: check what each means by acting on conviction.
- Former officers and conscripts: check accounts of orders refused or obeyed in 1973, as each tells it.
- Student leaders of 2006 and 2011 now in office: check how they explain changing or keeping positions.
- Lickanantay and Aymara communities facing mining: check long refusal against negotiated agreements.
- Rapa Nui factions: check rival convictions about the island's status.
- Mapuche *warriache* in Santiago: check support for causes in Wallmapu, which is Araucanía-Patagonia's (compare only).
- Santiago's Palestinian-descended and Jewish families: check how each holds a stance on events abroad while sharing one city.
- Haitian churches and associations: check which convictions they voice in public.
- Fishing coves of Coquimbo and Valparaíso: check campaigns over quotas held by small fishers.

## 4. Flags

- **Languages needed:** Spanish for most; Rapa Nui for island debate; Kreyòl and Arabic for community media; Kunza only for the Lickanantay's own naming of the cause.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the coup, the regime that followed and the 2019 unrest are contested ground: give each actor's account as an account; conscientious objection in health care and the military is a statute question for Libra and Capricorn.
