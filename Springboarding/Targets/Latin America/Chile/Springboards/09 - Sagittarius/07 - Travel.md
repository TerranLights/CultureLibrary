# Chile: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (including northern mining speech); Aymara; Quechua; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** going far from home: long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage and migration, and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler (short journeys and commuting stay in Gemini).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Families of Las Condes and of La Pintana: check who has ever left the country, and how they describe it.
- Long-distance mine workers on rotating shifts in Antofagasta: check travel as a work rhythm, and the families left behind.
- Seasonal fruit pickers moving through O'Higgins and Maule: check the travel of work against travel for pleasure.
- Aymara families of Arica and the altiplano: check journeys home for fiestas and across borders.
- Rapa Nui families: check the cost and frequency of the trip to the mainland and to Tahiti.
- Juan Fernández islanders: check what the long boat or small-plane trip means for study, health and work.
- Newcomers from Haiti, Venezuela, Peru and Bolivia: check their routes, and their trips home or the lack of them.
- Families of Croatian, Italian or Basque descent: check visits to ancestral towns.
- Returned exiles and their children: check how the return trip is told (exiles abroad as comparison only).
- Pentecostal and Catholic pilgrims: check pilgrimage as the long journey of families who rarely travel otherwise.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tourism and migration statistics; mixed for travel customs (press and theses); thin for the mobility of Chango fishing families and Colla herders, and for Juan Fernández travel beyond municipal reports.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish throughout; Kreyòl for Haitian accounts of arrival and return; Rapa Nui for voyaging; Aymara for border mobility; Croatian for some family records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrant crossings in the far north and the 2018 return flights involve people at risk and contested accounts; immigration rules are a statute matter for Libra.
- **Held for review:** the Colchane crossings and the 1990s exile-return office were weighed; both are carried already, on the Cancer shelter and Aries beginning sheets, so they were not repeated.
