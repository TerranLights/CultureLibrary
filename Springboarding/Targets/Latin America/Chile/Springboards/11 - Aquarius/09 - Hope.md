# Chile: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (devotional, political and everyday); Aymara; Kunza; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aymara villages above Arica: check how a good year is asked for in planting rites and in church.
- Lickanantay villages of Peine, Socaire and Toconao: check what hope for water means beside mining's demand.
- Families in Calama and Copiapó: check how waiting, searching and rescue shaped local talk of hope.
- Central Valley farmers in the long drought: check what rains, prayers and water rights each are hoped for.
- Santiago's *poblaciones*: check lottery play, vows and saving for children's study as rival paths of hope.
- Pentecostal and Catholic congregations: check how each speaks of hope in this life and the next.
- Valparaíso and Viña del Mar fire survivors: check how rebuilding is hoped for and doubted.
- Rapa Nui families: check which returns they hope for, of objects, land and authority.
- Palestinian-Chilean families: check how hopes for a distant homeland are passed to Chilean-born children.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check what they wrote home in the first year and later.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for devotion, campaigns and surveys; mixed for migrants' and searching families' accounts; thin for Kunza song texts and for Colla and island hopes.
- **Languages needed:** colonial Spanish for council records; Aymara for Pachallampe; Kunza for *talatur* songs; Rapa Nui; Kreyòl.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** searching families and the 2019 movement are grieving or contested ground: give their testimony first and each side's account as an account. Water rites may hold restricted songs; record only what communities share.
- **Held for review:** for false hope sold to the desperate, a Chilean prosperity-gospel preacher would fit, but none comes to mind with confidence; the research should start from a query ("teología de la prosperidad Chile iglesias") instead of a name.
