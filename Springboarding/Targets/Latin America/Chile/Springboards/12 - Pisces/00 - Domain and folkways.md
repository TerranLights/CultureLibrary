# Chile: The domain, the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America: Arica and Parinacota down to the Biobío coast, plus Rapa Nui and the Juan Fernández Islands. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of Chile, including street *coa*; Aymara; Quechua; Kunza; Rapa Nui; Mapudungun; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** how people meet suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden mind, confinement, the dead and spirits, healing, luck and the magic ways of daily life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Aymara of Parinacota and their urban kin in Arica: check where the *yatiri* still works and where clinic or pastor took the role.
- Lickanantay of the Loa basin: check which beings of hill and water are spoken of openly and which are kept.
- Diaguita of the Huasco and Elqui: check what healing knowledge survived the years before recognition.
- Ñuble and Maule farm families: check whether the healers in Laval and Vicuña Cifuentes still practice, and under what name.
- Working-class and wealthy Santiago: check who takes the same trouble to a *meica*, a psychiatrist, a pastor or a tarot reader.
- Lota and Coronel: check pit-disaster ghosts against those told in the nitrate north.
- Rapa Nui: check which family knowledge of the dead is shared with outsiders at all.
- Haitian, Peruvian and Bolivian newcomers: check whether their healers find clients beyond their own community.
- Pentecostal and Catholic neighbors: check how each names one unseen harm, as spirit, sin or illness.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in folklore and colonial records; mixed for asylum history; scant on Kunza, Chango lore and Robinson Crusoe Island. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled, both the southern line and Rapa Nui's status; for now Pisces covers north, center and the islands, reading the Araucanía, Los Ríos, Los Lagos with Chiloé, Aysén, Magallanes and Wallmapu as Araucanía-Patagonia's, comparison only (Chiloé's lore and the *machi* among them).
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century Spanish for Inquisition files; Aymara and Rapa Nui for names of beings; Kreyòl for Haitian healers' terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Rapa Nui, Aymara, Lickanantay and Mapuche ceremonial knowledge is the communities' to share; Heyerdahl's book is itself a case of exposure. Asylum files hold living families' names.
- **Held for review:** foreign ghost-hunting shows filmed at Chilean sites were dropped as entertainment without sources; South Asian-origin items are held, as a category only, on the Dissolution and Spirituality sheets.
