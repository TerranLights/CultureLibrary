# Chile: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Spanish of Chile (devotional, military and colloquial); Aymara; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Conscripts and career officers: check whether the oath is felt as a real offer of one's life or as a ceremony.
- Families who refused organ donation and those who gave: check what each says of the dead body as a gift.
- Women caring for disabled or old relatives in the Maule and Ñuble countryside: check who is expected to stay, and what is said of a daughter who leaves.
- First-generation graduates from northern mining towns: check the debt felt toward parents, in money and in guilt.
- *Chinos* dancers of the coves and *bailes* of La Tirana: check the pain each describes, and whether women dance in the same way.
- Catholic and Pentecostal households: check whether self-denial is aimed at saints, the church or the family.
- Haitian and Peruvian workers sending money home: check which hardships they report taking on for whom.
- Rapa Nui families: check which sacrifices for kin or clan are told publicly and which are not.
- Mapuche families in Santiago: check what is given up to keep children in touch with kin in the south, which is compared only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on saints, military rites and caregiving research; mixed on donation; scarce on Indigenous offering, which communities share on their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including religious biography; Rapa Nui for the birdman era; Arabic for diaspora usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Acevedo's death and the War of the Pacific are contested ground, told differently by each side. Aymara and Rapa Nui offerings are the communities' to describe.
- **Held for review:** Mapuche hunger strikes belong with Araucanía-Patagonia, where the strikers' cause lies in Wallmapu; they wait for that nation's sheets and serve here only as comparison.
