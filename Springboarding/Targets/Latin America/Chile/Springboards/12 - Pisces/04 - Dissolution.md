## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Pentecostal and Catholic charismatic congregations: check which welcome trance and which hold it in check.
- Football supporters of Santiago and Concepción: check what members say of losing themselves in the stand.
- Lickanantay and Chango communities: check how each tells the gap between loss and recovery.
- Rapa Nui elders and youth: check what is said to have been lost and what never was.
- *Warriache* families: check whether a Spanish surname or a Mapuche one is passed on now, and why.
- Families of Croatian, Italian or Basque descent in Antofagasta and Valparaíso: check at which generation the old language stopped at table.
- Married couples before and after 2004: check how separations were named when divorce did not exist.
- Families of people with dementia in Valparaíso and Santiago: check the words used for a self that fades.
- City seekers at plant ceremonies and Andean communities: check how each frames these rites, and who objects.
