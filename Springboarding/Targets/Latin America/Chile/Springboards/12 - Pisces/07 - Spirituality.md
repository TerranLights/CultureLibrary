*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Valley families of the Limarí and Elqui: check whether a local image draws more devotion than the parish, and who keeps it.
- Lickanantay communities: check what they share about sacred peaks and what they decline to share.
- Aymara herders and their city-born children: check whether mountain lords are still addressed in Arica.
- Afro-Chileans of Azapa and Lluta: check which devotions came with ancestors and which were adopted later.
- Pentecostal congregations: check how experience of the Spirit is told by men and by women.
- Young people without religion in Santiago and Valparaíso: check what they call sacred, and where.
- Haitian Catholics, Protestants and those who serve the *lwa*: check how each side speaks of the others.
- Rapa Nui families: check how Catholic faith and ancestral *mana* are held together, in their words.
- *Huaso* families: check sung devotions and household altars against Sunday Mass.
- Jewish and Muslim Chileans: check private prayer and home practice in a small community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on popular Catholicism and survey items; mixed on Evangelical experience; very scarce on Vodou, Afro-Chilean and Diaguita practice.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; Aymara and Quechua for mountain beings; Rapa Nui for ancestral terms; Kreyòl for Haitian practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Lickanantay, Aymara, Rapa Nui and Mapuche ceremonial knowledge is the communities' to share, never to be scouted as public; Vodou is stigmatized; ancestral remains are sacred to Rapa Nui.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist communities in Chile, an open question for the author, are held as a category only; South Asian-origin items are held under the standing rule, as a category only.
