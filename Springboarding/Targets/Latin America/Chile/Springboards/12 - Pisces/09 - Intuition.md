# Chile: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Chile, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Chilean Spanish (street, workplace and devotional usage); Aymara; Quechua; Rapa Nui; Haitian Creole; Arabic. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Nurses and doctors in Santiago's public hospitals and in small northern clinics: check whose hunches are heeded, and by whom.
- Copper workers of Calama and *pirquineros* of the Norte Chico: check whether experience or instruments decide when to leave a tunnel.
- Aymara herders: check what signs in animals and sky they trust over radio forecasts, as they choose to share.
- *Huaso* families at cattle fairs: check how a buyer's eye for an animal is spoken of and tested.
- Police and the young men they stop in the *poblaciones*: check how each describes the moment of suspicion.
- Sisters, wives, brothers and husbands: check whether "*intuición femenina*" is claimed, mocked or both.
- Pentecostal and Catholic believers: check whether a hunch is called a gift, a prompting or a temptation.
- Haitian and Venezuelan newcomers: check how they read strangers in a city whose signals they are still learning.
- Rapa Nui and Juan Fernández fishing families: check how sea sense passes between generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in health and decision research; mixed in folk belief about quakes and seers; scarce on island and Indigenous practice.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish slang and trade talk; Aymara and Quechua sense verbs; Kreyòl for dream lore; Rapa Nui for sea knowledge.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** profiling touches discrimination claims; Andean and Rapa Nui knowledge of signs is the communities' to share; paid seers are read as a trade, not as sources.
- **Held for review:** Aymara coca-leaf reading stays on the Virgo discernment sheet, which already treats it as a way of deciding; repeating it here would add nothing.
