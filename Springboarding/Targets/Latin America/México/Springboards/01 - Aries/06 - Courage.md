# México: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (with northern ranch and revolutionary-era speech); classical Nahuatl; Ñuu Savi; Rarámuri; Tsotsil; the Spanish of Afro-Mexican Veracruz and the Costa Chica. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chihuahua, Durango and the Sierra Madre Occidental:** check frontier and revolutionary memory (Villa, Tomóchic) against what Rarámuri and O'dam communities recall of the same wars.
- **Jalisco, Los Altos, Michoacán and Colima:** check Cristero memory in parishes and families, including shrines to the canonized martyrs.
- **Morelos and Guerrero:** check Zapatista-era veterans' testimony (INAH oral-history projects) against the national hero cult.
- **Ñuu Savi and Tlaxcalan sources:** check how pre-conquest and colonial Native records present valor, beyond the Mexica.
- **Afro-Mexican communities of Veracruz and the Costa Chica:** check the commemoration of Yanga and of Vicente Guerrero in local festivals and schoolbooks.
- **Sinaloa, Veracruz, Tamaulipas and Guerrero:** check searching mothers' and local reporters' accounts state by state.
- **Women and men:** check whose endurance is called courage, using Salas and Gutmann as a start.
- **Mennonite and Evangelical communities:** check how their pacifist teaching and refusals of arms are discussed by neighbors and in their own records.
- **Spanish Republican exiles and Jewish refugees of the 1930s-40s:** check memoirs for the courage of flight.
- **Central American migrants in Chiapas and along the routes north:** check shelters' testimonies (Hermanos en el Camino, La 72 *check*).
- **Comparison only:** Yucatán (memory of the Caste War), Sonora (the Yoeme wars), Cascadia's Baja California and the CSA's Tejano border ballads of defiance; heroes shared across these lines need care, and the lines are the author's to draw.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military, revolutionary and Cristero history and for ballads; mixed for women's courage; thin for most Indigenous peoples' own accounts of valor in their languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl and Ñuu Savi for the pictorial glosses; Rarámuri and Tsotsil for community memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence and disappearance are ongoing; searchers and reporters face threats; ballads praising traffickers are contested; Cristero memory divides families.
- **Held for review:** none on courage.
