## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guadalajara, Monterrey and Mexico City:** check football rivalry and fan groups as city identity, using club and press records.
- **Sinaloa:** check where *ulama* is still played and who keeps it; sources are few, so look to the state's cultural institute.
- **Ñuu Savi and Zapotec towns, and their migrant teams:** check *pelota mixteca* leagues as town-to-town rivalry.
- **Purépecha towns:** check where *uárhukua* is played today and at which festivals.
- **Rarámuri and O'dam communities:** check races and wagers in the people's own accounts, apart from the ultramarathon tourism.
- **Tsotsil and Tseltal towns:** check cargo competition, and the disputes when converts decline festival spending.
- **Jalisco, the Bajío and the north:** check *charrería*, cockfights and horse races (*carreras parejeras*) by region, with their betting.
- **Women:** check the *escaramuza charra*, women's football and boxing, and the *ariwete*.
- **Middle-class and working-class families in the big cities:** check exam pressure and private tutoring by class.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check local dance and verse contests (*chilena*, *artesa*) *check*.
- **Comparison only:** Sonora (its horse-race ballads), Yucatán, Cascadia's Baja California and the CSA's Tejano communities, whose clubs and rivalries cross into the core's leagues; the author decides where México ends.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for football, boxing, *charrería* and exams; mixed for envy and prestige studies; thin for *ulama*, *uárhukua* and Afro-Mexican contests.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl; Rarámuri, Purépecha, Ñuu Savi and Tsotsil for community games.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** cockfighting and bull events raise animal-welfare disputes; Foster's thesis is contested; some betting circuits may touch organized crime, so field safety matters.
