*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City and Monterrey against small towns of Jalisco and Michoacán:** check whether risk takes urban forms (street races, nightlife) or fair forms (*jaripeo*, horse races) in the data.
- **Tlaxcala and Puebla:** check bull runs and gunpowder carnivals town by town, with injury data.
- **Totonac and Nahua towns of Veracruz and Puebla:** check how flyers and dancers are trained and how risk is spoken of.
- **Guerrero's coast:** check the cliff divers of La Quebrada *check* as a staged daring trade.
- **Sinaloa and Durango:** check how young people discuss trafficker imagery, using local journalism, not outside assumptions.
- **Rarámuri and Wixárika:** check how drinking at ceremonies is regulated in their own accounts; sources are few, so start with community radio and INPI reports.
- **Women and men:** check sex differences in crash, drinking and injury data, and changes since 2000.
- **Evangelical, Mormon and Mennonite communities:** check their rules on alcohol and gambling as a contrast in the same towns.
- **Central American migrants in the south:** check the risks of the freight-train route as forced, not chosen, danger.
- **Comparison only:** Cascadia's Baja California (Tijuana's border nightlife), Sonora, Yucatán and the CSA's Tejano communities, as contrasts for drinking and road data; borders are for the author.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for traffic, alcohol and drug statistics and for festival press; mixed for the meaning of risk in festivals; thin for Indigenous peoples' own accounts of daring.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl; Totonac for the flyers; Rarámuri and Wixárika where ceremonial drink comes up.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trafficking and violence; addiction; bull and animal events; Day of the Dead imagery is easily turned into a stereotype; drink and driving laws belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none for recklessness.
