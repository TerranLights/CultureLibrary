## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **States at both ends of the ENDIREH control items:** check by state and municipality before naming any region, so no place is typed by a headline figure.
- **Tsotsil, Tseltal and Sierra Norte Nahua towns:** check bride-taking and bride service in women's own accounts and in community debates over the custom.
- **Mazahua families of the State of Mexico:** check Paradise's findings on sharing against present-day classrooms.
- **Purépecha fishing towns of Lake Pátzcuaro:** check how fishing grounds are guarded against outsiders and who settles quarrels.
- **Mexico City and Guadalajara streets:** check *franeleros*, vendors' corners and saved parking places as claims to shared space.
- **Catholic and Evangelical couples:** check marriage-preparation teaching on jealousy and on who holds the household money.
- **Young urban adults against their parents:** check whether jealousy is still read as love in youth surveys and dating studies.
- **Same-sex couples in the capital and the Isthmus:** check whether surveys and NGO reports capture control in their relationships.
- **Mennonite colonies:** check courtship and household authority; sources are scarce outside the colonies, so look for former members' accounts.
- **Comparison only:** Sonora, Yucatán, Cascadia's Baja California (border dating and control studies) and the CSA's Tejano communities; studies of Mexican-American couples are diaspora data. Where México ends against them is for the author.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for partner control (ENDIREH is large and repeated) and for colonial marriage records; mixed for jealousy as a felt emotion; thin for Indigenous words for jealousy and their senses.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for colonial terms; Tsotsil, Tseltal and Nahuatl of Puebla for marriage customs; Mazahua.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against women and feminicide; bride-taking customs; survivors' testimony needs care; family-law questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the possessiveness pointers raised no rule.
