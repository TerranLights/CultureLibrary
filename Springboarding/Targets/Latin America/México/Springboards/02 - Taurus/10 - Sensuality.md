*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Veracruz port and Mexico City's dance halls:** check who dances *danzón*, at what age, and how the halls describe propriety.
- **Southern Veracruz *jaraneros* against the revival collectives:** check how the *fandango* is described by older players and by urban newcomers.
- **Oaxaca's Central Valleys, Isthmus and Sierra:** check taste words and dishes town by town, without ranking cuisines.
- **Puebla and its convent kitchens:** check the *mole* and *chiles en nogada* stories as told in convent and family recipe books.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check community accounts of the *artesa* and of foods and songs.
- **Nahua, Zapotec and Hñähñu midwives:** check the *temazcal* as birth and healing care in their own accounts.
- **Evangelical, Pentecostal, Mennonite and Latter-day Saint communities:** check rules on alcohol, dance and adornment as each states them.
- **Monterrey and the northern states:** check the Sunday *carne asada* as a gathering, by class and neighborhood.
- **Lebanese-Mexican families of Puebla and the capital:** check family and restaurant histories of dishes that crossed into everyday food.
- **Older adults and disabled people:** check whether pleasure and touch in old age or disability appear at all in Mexican studies.
- **Comparison only:** Yucatán (its own cuisine and *jarana*), Cascadia's Valle de Guadalupe wine country, Sonora's ranch foods and the CSA's Tex-Mex. Where México ends against them is for the author.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food history, cookbooks and music; mixed for touch, comfort and rest; thin for Indigenous sensory vocabularies outside classical Nahuatl.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for songs and the herbal; Zapotec and Huasteca Nahuatl for living taste and scent words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *temazcal* rites offered commercially without community consent; alcohol; sexuality itself belongs to Scorpio; ceremonial foods and plants may carry restrictions.
