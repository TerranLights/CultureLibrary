## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tamaulipas, Sinaloa and Guerrero:** check where local papers stopped covering crime and which social-media pages took their place, so silence is mapped rather than guessed.
- **The Huasteca (Veracruz, Hidalgo, San Luis Potosí):** check community-radio audiences and the messages families send through them.
- **Guerrero's Montaña (Me'phaa, Ñuu Savi, Nahua towns):** check how XEZV and town loudspeakers carry notices compared with mobile phones.
- **Zapatista and non-Zapatista towns in Chiapas:** check each one's radio, assemblies and printed notices as separate channels.
- **Wixárika and Rarámuri communities:** check which knowledge each community keeps closed, by its own statements, before any data gathering.
- **Mennonite colonies:** check each colony's own rules on radios, phones and internet, since these differ by group.
- **Jewish community bodies (Tribuna Israelita) and Lebanese-Mexican associations:** check their own bulletins and archives as community information channels.
- **Comparison only, for news markets that straddle the lines:** Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's weekly *Zeta* and its slain editors), Sonora's border papers, Yucatán's long-running regional press, the CSA's Spanish-language Texas television, and diaspora networks (Univision, Telemundo) also watched south of the border. Where each belongs is for the author to rule.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press archives, colonial reports, survey data and press-freedom monitoring; mixed for Indigenous-language media; thin for Kikapú, Afro-Mexican and Chinese-Mexican news channels.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for the annals; Huasteca Nahuatl, Hñähñu and Tepehua for Veracruz radio; Me'phaa and Ñuu Savi for Montaña stations; Tsotsil for reportative speech.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** violence against journalists and sources (do not expose them); 1968 and the Dirty War archives; restricted ritual knowledge; transparency and privacy law go to Libra and Capricorn; INAI's 2025 dissolution is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none; the press, statistics and radio pointers met no standing rule.
