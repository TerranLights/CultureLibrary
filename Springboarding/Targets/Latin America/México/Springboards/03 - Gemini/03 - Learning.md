## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zinacantán and Chamula (Tsotsil):** check weaving and farming apprenticeship against school attendance, using the long-run Greenfield and de León data.
- **Nahua towns of the Sierra Norte de Puebla:** check studies of children pitching in to household work, so "learning by observing" is tested, not assumed.
- **Purépecha craft towns (Paracho guitars, Santa Clara del Cobre copper):** check how workshop skills pass from master to apprentice and into family firms.
- **Small hamlets of the Sierra Madre Occidental (Rarámuri, Ódami, Wixárika):** check CONAFE and boarding-school (*albergue*) records against what families teach at home.
- **Agricultural day-labor camps in Sinaloa and the Bajío:** check schooling programs for migrant children who move with the harvest.
- **Rural normal-school regions (Guerrero, Michoacán, Oaxaca):** check teacher training and the CNTE's own pedagogy projects.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check school data since the 2015 intercensal survey first counted Afro-descendants.
- **Mennonite colonies:** check each colony's own school program in German against state requirements.
- **Comparison only, for schooling that crosses the edges:** Cascadia's San Quintín farmworker children, Sonora's Yoeme and O'odham schools, Yucatán's Maya bilingual schools, the CSA's Texas border schools with daily crossers, and diaspora heritage-language classes. How far México reaches here is for the author.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school history, national surveys and developmental research with Mexican samples; mixed for Indigenous home learning outside Chiapas and Puebla; thin for Kikapú, Afro-Mexican and Chinese-Mexican learning practices.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for Book 3; Tsotsil for Zinacantán; Purépecha for craft towns; German and Plautdietsch for Mennonite schools; LSM for Deaf schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ayotzinapa (2014) and the normal schools are politically charged; boarding schools and forced Spanish carry painful memories; teacher-union disputes are partisan; school law goes to Libra and Capricorn; higher education is Sagittarius.
- **Held for review:** none; the apprenticeship, schooling and literacy pointers met no standing rule.
