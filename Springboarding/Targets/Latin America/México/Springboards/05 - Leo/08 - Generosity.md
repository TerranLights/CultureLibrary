## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Juchitán and Tehuantepec:** check the ledgers kept by *vela* sponsors and who records each gift.
- **Ñuu Savi towns of the Mixteca Alta:** check whether Monaghan's account of Nuyoo holds in neighboring towns.
- **Los Altos de Jalisco along the pilgrim roads:** check free food for pilgrims against paid stalls, year by year.
- **Culiacán and the rest of Sinaloa:** check giving at the Malverde chapel in devotees' own words, kept apart from press framing.
- **The capital and Monterrey:** check food-bank giving against informal *cooperachas* in offices and neighborhoods.
- **Evangelical congregations of Chiapas and Tabasco:** check tithes against the fiesta spending they replaced.
- **Jewish and Lebanese communities of the capital:** check community welfare societies' yearbooks for who gives to whom.
- **Mennonite colonies of Chihuahua:** check colony mutual-aid funds in German records.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check wedding and fiesta gifts; written records may be few.
- **Older *cerillos* in city supermarkets:** check how packers describe tips: wage, gift or charity.
- **Comparison only, because some of the best-recorded giving customs sit across these edges:** Yucatán (the *gremios* that sponsor Mérida's fiestas), Sonora (Yoeme fiesta hosts), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's donation-fed shelters), the CSA's Tejano *mutualistas*, and the diaspora's hometown associations. Where México ends is for the author.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fiesta sponsorship in Oaxaca and Chiapas; mixed for formal philanthropy and giving surveys; thin for tipping studies and for giving words in most Indigenous languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for Book 9; Ñuu Savi for Nuyoo; Isthmus Zapotec for *vela* records; German for colony funds.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Malverde devotion is tied in the press to drug traffickers' giving; debts from fiesta sponsorship; pressure to tithe; tax treatment of donations is law (Libra, Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** none; the feasting, tipping and charity sources prompted no hold.
