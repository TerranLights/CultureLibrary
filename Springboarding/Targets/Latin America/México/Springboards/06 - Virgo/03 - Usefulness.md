## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guadalajara's working- and middle-class families:** check how parents speak of an *acomedido* child against one who waits to be told.
- **Nahua families of the Huasteca and the Sierra Norte de Puebla:** check children's tasks by age and how help is praised.
- **Hñähñu towns of the Valle del Mezquital:** check elders' roles when the young leave for the cities.
- **Rarámuri of the Sierra Tarahumara:** check how *kórima* is described and asked for.
- **Triqui and Ñuu Savi families in the capital:** check children who interpret for parents in offices and clinics.
- **Graduates of the IPN and the Tec de Monterrey:** check how each school's alumni speak of practical skill.
- **Disabled people's self-advocacy groups in the capital and Monterrey:** check the words they reject (*inválido*) and those they choose.
- **Mennonite colonies of Chihuahua:** check machine repair, reuse and children's farm tasks.
- **Chinese-Mexican families of Tapachula:** check children's work in family shops.
- **Afro-Mexican households of the Costa Chica:** check elders' tasks in fishing and farming families.
- **Comparison only, as other culture-nations (the edge is the author's to draw):** Yucatán (Yucatec Maya children's work studied in village homes), Sonora (Comcaac elders as keepers of plant and sea knowledge), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's trade in secondhand goods from across the border), the CSA's Tejano family businesses and children's help in them, and the diaspora's child interpreters in the United States.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for children's help and for aging surveys; mixed for repair and reuse; thin for Chinantec and Mazatec elders' roles, where village studies are few.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including *arrimado* and *acomedido* in regional use; classical Nahuatl for the Codex Mendoza glosses; Tsotsil for children's talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** reports of coerced sterilization of Indigenous women; slurs for disabled people; elders left alone. End-of-life law (advance directives) is for Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; the usefulness pointers raised nothing that had to be set aside.
