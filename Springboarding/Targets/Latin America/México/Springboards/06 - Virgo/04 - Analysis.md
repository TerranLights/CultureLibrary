## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monterrey's engineering schools against the capital's UNAM and El Colegio de México:** check which kinds of reasoning carry prestige in each.
- **Guadalajara's electronics and software corridor:** check how data work is described by those who do it.
- **El Colegio de México's founding as La Casa de España (1938) by Spanish Republican exiles:** check how the exiles shaped analytic disciplines.
- **Ayuuk towns around Zempoaltépetl:** check when people turn to calendar specialists and when to agronomists or clinics.
- **Nahua and Hñähñu market sellers in Puebla, Hidalgo and the Valley of Toluca:** check mental reckoning and old measures at the stall.
- **Ñuu Savi bilingual schools:** check how base-twenty counting is taught beside the national curriculum.
- **Mennonite colony schools in Chihuahua:** check what arithmetic and record-keeping are taught.
- **Women scientists:** check the Academia Mexicana de Ciencias's programs and memoirs for who is allowed to analyze.
- **Catholic and Evangelical congregations:** check catechesis and sermons on science and evolution.
- **Comparison only, the line being the author's:** Yucatán (Maya mathematics and the codex tables kept in that tradition), Sonora (Comcaac knowledge of sea and desert set out with ethnobiologists), Cascadia's Bajas (Ensenada's CICESE research center and its seismology), the CSA's Tejano scholar Américo Paredes's analyses of border ballads, and Mario Molina's career in California as diaspora science.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the history of science and statistics; mixed for everyday reasoning; thin for northern Indigenous number systems (Rarámuri, Wixárika, O'dam), where the start is grammars and INALI materials.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, both scientific and colloquial; classical Nahuatl for land-register glyphs; Ayuuk for calendar terms; Latin for 17th-century treatises.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** calendar specialists' knowledge may be closed to outsiders; early "intelligence" testing of Indigenous children, to be read critically; ethnic categories in census data.
- **Held for review:** none; the history-of-science and reckoning pointers needed no setting aside.
