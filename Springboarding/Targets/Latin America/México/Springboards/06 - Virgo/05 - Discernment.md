# México: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (capital, Puebla, Jalisco, Oaxaca); classical Nahuatl; Valley Zapotec; Tseltal; Wixárika; Mazatec. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Zapotec mezcal towns of the Central Valleys:** check how *maestros mezcaleros* judge a batch and who is taught to.
- **Puebla's Talavera workshops:** check how makers and buyers tell certified pieces from copies.
- **Los Altos de Jalisco and the tequila valley:** check how agave growers judge ripeness and price.
- **Nahua families of Tlaxcala and Puebla:** check how compadres and marriage partners are weighed and by whom.
- **Mazatec of Huautla de Jiménez:** check, through Mazatec voices, how true and false *sabios* have been told apart since the visitor rush of the 1960s.
- **Wixárika (Huichol):** check, only through what Wixárika authors publish, how a *mara'akame* is recognized.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check accounts of being judged by skin color, in their own words.
- **Mennonite Old Colony elders in Chihuahua:** check how they weigh outside technology (rubber tires, phones) before allowing it.
- **Evangelical congregations:** check preaching on discernment and "false prophets".
- **Elders in the capital:** check family advice on scams and phone extortion.
- **Comparison only (their judges belong to other culture-nations; the boundary is the author's):** Yucatán (its own quarrels over "authentic" regional cooking), Sonora (*bacanora* and its rival denomination of origin), Cascadia's Bajas (wine tasting in the Valle de Guadalupe), the CSA's Tejano Tex-Mex against "real Mexican" quarrels, and the diaspora's *notario* fraud cases in the United States.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for consumer protection, certification and taste economies; mixed for spiritual discernment (church archives); thin for how Indigenous communities recognize healers and officers, much of which is restricted.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including *colmillo* and *chafa*; classical Nahuatl for the Nahua witnesses of 1666 and the opossum tales; Zapotec for mezcal-making terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Mazatec and Wixárika ritual knowledge; colorism data; extortion victims' safety. Fraud law is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the taste, trust and inquiry pointers needed nothing set aside.
