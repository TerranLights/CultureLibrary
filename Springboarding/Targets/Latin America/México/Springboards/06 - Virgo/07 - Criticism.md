## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Monterrey and the capital:** check how teasing and blunt correction among coworkers differ, from workplace studies rather than reputation.
- **Journalists of Culiacán (*Ríodoce*) and Chihuahua:** check what can be printed, and at what cost, after the 2017 killings of Javier Valdez and Miroslava Breach.
- **Zapotec, Ayuuk and Chatino towns of Oaxaca:** check how assemblies criticize officials and when they remove them.
- **Tseltal, Tsotsil, Ch'ol and Tojolabal Zapatista communities:** check how criticism works inside autonomous councils as well as toward the state.
- **Carnivals of Mazatlán and Veracruz:** check whom the *quema del mal humor* effigies mock each year.
- **Three-generation households in Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check who may correct whom, and whether a daughter-in-law may answer back.
- **Evangelical congregations:** check church discipline and public correction of members.
- **Mennonite Old Colony churches:** check the *Bann* (shunning) as the sharpest sanction.
- **Spanish Republican exiles:** check the exile press's criticism of Franco from Mexican soil.
- **Comparison only (other culture-nations' presses and satires; where México ends is the author's to decide):** Yucatán (Mérida's opposition newspaper tradition), Sonora (the Cananea strikers' manifestos and the press that backed them), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's weekly *Zeta* and its reporters' losses), the CSA's Tejano *corridos* rebuking Texas Rangers, and Chicano satire in the diaspora, such as Lalo Alcaraz's comic strip.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the press, satire and cartoons; mixed for criticism in families and workplaces; thin for Triqui and Chatino assembly records in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including *balconear* and *chayote*; Tseltal and Tsotsil for Zapatista community documents; Zapotec and Ayuuk for assembly minutes; Plautdietsch for Mennonite church discipline.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** journalists' safety and their sources; religious expulsions in highland Chiapas; defamation law is for Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; the satire, press and assembly pointers needed no setting aside.
