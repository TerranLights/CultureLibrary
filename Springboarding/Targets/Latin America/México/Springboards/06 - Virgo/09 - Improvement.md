## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Residents of self-built peripheries (Nezahualcóyotl, Ecatepec, Iztapalapa's hillsides):** check how a house in *obra negra* is spoken of across decades.
- **Migrant-sending towns of Zacatecas and Michoacán:** check how remittance houses and town works are judged by those who stayed.
- **Mazatec and Chinantec communities moved by the Papaloapan dams:** check how relocation is remembered, as progress or as loss.
- **Hñähñu of the Valle del Mezquital:** check irrigation with the capital's wastewater and the development programs that came with it.
- **Ñuu Savi and Isthmus Zapotec writers' academies and workshops:** check language renewal set on their own terms.
- **Wixárika (Huichol) of Wirikuta's defense:** check how mining "development" is answered.
- **The Bajío's and Monterrey's private-university families:** check *superación* reading and ladders of schooling.
- **Evangelical converts:** check testimonies that tie conversion to saving, sobriety and rising.
- **Afro-Mexican Yanga (Veracruz):** check how the town tells its founding as a story of rising from slavery.
- **Spanish Republican exiles:** check memoirs of rebuilding a life after 1939.
- **Lebanese-Mexican families:** check community histories of first-generation peddlers and later generations, as the families tell them.
- **Comparison only (each belongs to another culture-nation; where México ends the author decides):** Yucatán (tourism-built "development" around Cancún), Sonora (Porfirian surveys of Yoeme land and the Green Revolution in the Yaqui Valley), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's colonias built from recycled border materials), the CSA's Tejano memory of Americanization campaigns, and the diaspora, where those who rose abroad may be called *pochos*.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for development history, social mobility and self-help publishing; mixed for home improvement; thin for relocated Chinantec and Mazatec voices, best sought in INAH oral-history holdings.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, including the *superación* register; Mazatec and Chinantec for relocation memory; Tu'un Savi and Zapotec for language-renewal projects.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *mejorar la raza* and colorism; abuses in coercive *anexos*; forced relocation. Land and water law are for Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
