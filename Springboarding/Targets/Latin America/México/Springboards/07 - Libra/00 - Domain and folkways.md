## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Capital and Puebla-Tlaxcala:** check whether *compadrazgo* occasions multiply alike in a megacity and in Tlaxcalan villages.
- **Monterrey and Saltillo:** check marriages between business houses as alliance.
- **Los Altos de Jalisco and the Bajío:** check how parish books count church against civil weddings.
- **Purépecha towns of the Meseta:** check elders' councils hearing marriage and land quarrels.
- **Zapotec of the Isthmus and of the Central Valleys:** check wedding gift ledgers in two branches of one language family.
- **Me'phaa, Ñomndaa and Ñuu Savi of Guerrero:** check the *Policía Comunitaria*'s hearings as a court.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check godparent ties and quarrels settled far from state courts.
- **Mennonite colonies of Chihuahua; Jewish communities of the capital:** check church discipline and the rabbinical court beside civil law.
- **Evangelical congregations in Chiapas:** check how membership changes who may sponsor or marry whom.
- **Comparison only, as their marriage records and courts sit under other culture-nations (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (its 1841 constitution first wrote the amparo), Sonora (Yoeme treaties and wars; Comcaac, O'odham), Cascadia's Bajas (Kumiai, Cucapá, Paipai, Kiliwa), CSA Tejanos under the 1848 treaty, and the US diaspora (binational marriages; "notario" fraud).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, registry data and colonial court records; mixed for customary dispute settlement, written up town by town; thin for the Kikapú and the O'dam. México's lands are not yet settled by the author; the Libra sheets are written for the core of pre-war Mexico.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish legal register for codes and deeds; classical Nahuatl for the speeches; Ñuu Savi for codex glosses; Tsotsil for Zinacantán hearings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** partner violence (ENDIREH; killings of women in Ciudad Juárez); marriage payments in Guerrero's Montaña, reported from outside and contested; community courts' records belong to the communities.
- **Held for review:** none; the marriage, notarial, codex and survey pointers met no standing rule.
