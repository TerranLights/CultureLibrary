# México: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (bureaucratic and procedural register; the capital's slang; sports talk); classical Nahuatl; Zapotec; the Indigenous languages into which INALI has rendered the Constitution. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Teachers' sections of Oaxaca, Chiapas, Guerrero and Michoacán:** check how inherited and sold posts were defended and how the reform was applied in each state.
- **The Huasteca potosina:** check municipal and court records under a boss's rule against his memoir.
- **Small judicial districts of Los Altos de Jalisco and Zacatecas:** check how often judges file *excusas* where everyone is kin or *compadre*.
- **Zapotec municipalities under *sistemas normativos* in Oaxaca:** check the 2007 case of a woman barred from standing in Santa María Quiegolani and the rulings that followed.
- **Japanese-Mexican families moved to the capital and Guadalajara in 1942:** check how rules for enemy nationals were written and applied to them.
- **Central American migrants at southern checkpoints:** check complaints to the CNDH of unequal treatment by agents.
- **Afro-Mexican people of the Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check complaints of being detained as presumed foreigners.
- **Mazahua and Triqui vendors in the capital:** check how street-trade permits are allotted and enforced.
- **Job seekers in the capital, Guadalajara and Monterrey:** check which cities audit studies sampled and what traits they varied.
- **Comparison only, since each famous case of rule by connection here falls in another culture-nation (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the henequen planters' *casta divina*), Sonora (the Sonoran presidents' circle of the 1920s), Cascadia's Bajas (Baja California's first opposition governorship and its electoral body; Kumiai, Cucapá, Paipai, Kiliwa), the CSA's South Texas county machines and Tejano voters, and US audit studies of hiring for the diaspora.
