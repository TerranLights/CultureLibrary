## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ayuuk and Zapotec towns of Oaxaca's Sierra Norte:** check how long assemblies run before agreement and when a matter is held over to the next.
- **Ayuuk and Ñuu Savi families who consult diviners:** check which decisions go to a maize reading and which do not.
- **Nahua speakers of Milpa Alta and the Sierra Norte de Puebla:** check living Nahuatl expressions for doubt against Molina's.
- **Tseltal and Tsotsil speakers:** check "two hearts" expressions in conversation and church writing.
- **Texcoco and Atenco:** check how residents recall the airport decided, withdrawn and decided again.
- **Voters of the capital and the states with high null votes in 2009:** check who campaigned to spoil ballots and why.
- **Students in Monterrey, Guadalajara and the capital:** check which cities career-indecision studies sampled.
- **Families caring for a dying parent:** check advance-directive use in the capital against states with no such law.
- **Mennonite colonies of Chihuahua:** check how decisions to move whole colonies were deliberated and voted.
- **Central American migrants in Tapachula:** check how long they wait before choosing to seek asylum or go north.
- **Comparison only, because each decision custom named here belongs to another culture-nation (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (Maya diviners' crystal stones), Sonora (the Yoeme eight towns' councils), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's migrants deciding to cross or stay; Kumiai, Cucapá, Paipai, Kiliwa), the CSA's Tejano late deciders in Texas polls, and return-or-stay choices in the diaspora.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for election, consultation and legislative records, and for the philosophy; mixed for psychology, mostly student samples; thin for divination practice, which is rarely written down.
- **Languages needed:** classical Nahuatl for Book 4 and *nepantla*; Ayuuk for diviners; Tseltal and Tsotsil for "two hearts" idioms; Plautdietsch for colony records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** diviners' knowledge on their own terms; end-of-life choices; migrants' plans.
- **Held for review:** none; the calendar, divination and philosophy pointers met no standing rule.
