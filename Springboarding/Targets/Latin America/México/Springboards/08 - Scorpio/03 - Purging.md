*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **The capital's market herbalists and their clients:** check what kinds of trouble (envy, bad luck, illness) bring people to a *limpia*.
- **Taxco and Guerrero's Holy Week towns:** check how penitents describe their vows.
- **Tsotsil communities of Chamula and Zinacantán:** check healers' cleansing beside expulsions of converts.
- **Chinantec and Zapotec villages of Oaxaca:** check whether *susto* is still diagnosed and treated as Rubel described.
- **Nahua of the Sierra Norte de Puebla:** check *temazcal* use after childbirth.
- **Chinese-Mexican families of Sinaloa and the capital:** check family memory of the 1930s expulsions.
- **The capital's Jewish community:** check how the 1649 *auto de fe* is remembered.
- **El Salto and Juanacatlán:** check residents' framing of a poisoned river.
- **Old Colony Mennonites:** check the church ban and shunning in their own records.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (Maya healers' cleansings), Sonora (center of the 1931 Chinese expulsions), Cascadia's Bajas (the Cucapá and the dried Colorado delta), CSA Tejano *curanderismo*, and the diaspora (the 1930s Repatriation): each sets a purge or cleansing beside México's for contrast only.
