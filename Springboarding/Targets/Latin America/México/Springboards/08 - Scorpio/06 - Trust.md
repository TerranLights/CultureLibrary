# México: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (everyday, commercial and survey registers); classical Nahuatl; Isthmus and Sierra Norte Zapotec; Ayuuk; Purépecha. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tzintzuntzan and the Lake Pátzcuaro towns:** check restudies of Foster's pair-by-pair ties.
- **Zapotec and Ayuuk towns of the Sierra Norte:** check *guelaguetza* and *tequio* ledgers as written records of trust.
- **Market women of Oaxaca City and the capital:** check how *tandas* are run among stallholders.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos de Jalisco:** check membership of *cajas populares* against banks.
- **Monterrey's business families:** check how far non-kin hold posts of trust.
- **Lebanese-Mexican and Jewish merchant families:** check early-century installment selling (the *aboneros*) and how credit was extended.
- **Afro-Mexican Costa Chica:** check trust in state programs since constitutional recognition (2019).
- **Mennonite colonies:** check credit and sales between colonies and their neighbors.
- **Internal migrants in the capital's *colonias*:** check how far neighbors are trusted with keys and children.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (Maya labor exchange in the milpa), Sonora (Yoeme dealings over federal water promises), Cascadia's Bajas (fishing cooperatives' shares), CSA Tejanos (the *cundinas* in Vélez-Ibáñez) and diaspora hometown clubs and remittance agents: each tests a radius of trust beside México's.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for trust surveys; mixed for informal credit (scattered theses); thin for Kikapú, Triqui and Chinantec credit ties.
- **Languages needed:** Zapotec and Ayuuk for exchange ledgers; classical Nahuatl for Book 10; Purépecha for the Pátzcuaro towns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** extortion victims; savings-club frauds and their members; party-split trust in elections.
- **Held for review:** trust in police and courts, and the police *control de confianza* exams, set aside and pointed to Capricorn.
