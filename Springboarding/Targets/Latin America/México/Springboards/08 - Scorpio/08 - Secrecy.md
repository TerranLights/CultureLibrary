# México: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mazatec of Huautla:** check how ceremony knowledge was guarded after 1957, as Mazatec speakers tell it.
- **Wixárika communities:** check only what they choose to say about knowledge kept from outsiders.
- **Tsotsil and Tseltal curers:** check whether prayers pass only to apprentices.
- **The capital's gay community:** check memory of 1901 and of the closet across generations.
- **Monterrey and Nuevo León:** check family claims of hidden Jewish descent, a contested field.
- **Jewish communities of the capital:** check how community archives decide what to open.
- **Masonic lodges of Veracruz and the capital:** check their own published histories.
- **Reporters in Tamaulipas, Veracruz and Guerrero:** check what goes unreported, from reporters' own accounts.
- **Central American migrants crossing the south:** check hiding one's nationality and accent on the route.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the guarded shrine of the Talking Cross), Sonora (Yoeme ceremonies closed to cameras), Cascadia's Bajas (smuggling tunnels under the border), CSA Tejanos (crypto-Jewish descent claims in South Texas) and the diaspora (undocumented status kept quiet): each places what is hidden beside México's.
