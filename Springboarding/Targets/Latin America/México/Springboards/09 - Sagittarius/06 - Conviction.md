# México: Conviction (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (political, evangelical and *corrido* registers); classical Nahuatl for Inquisition trials; Ayuuk (Mixe) and Zapotec for *comunalidad* writing; Rarámuri; Plautdietsch. **Concept in one line:** a firm, settled belief that a person holds to and acts on: the strength of a belief, not its content.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Jalisco, Michoacán and Guanajuato:** check how Cristero martyr memory is kept in parishes and families against secular towns nearby.
- **Tabasco:** check what the 1930s persecution left in local Catholic and secular memory, beyond Greene.
- **Morelos:** check how agrarian and Protestant stands overlapped in Jaramillo's followers.
- **Nuevo León and Coahuila:** check mothers' and bishops' human-rights stands against the region's business elites.
- **Oaxaca (Sierra Norte, Isthmus):** check Ayuuk and Zapotec *comunalidad* writers, and the 2006 teachers' and APPO protests, as distinct kinds of conviction.
- **The Sierra Tarahumara:** check Rarámuri and O'dam land defenders in their own statements.
- **Guerrero's Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check Afro-Mexican and Me'phaa organizers' campaigns and their continuity.
- **Mennonite colonies and Jehovah's Witness congregations:** check what each will not do, and how they explain it.
- **Evangelical converts in Chiapas and Hidalgo:** check conversion testimonies where converts held to new faith despite expulsion.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the Cruzob's holdout after the Caste War), Sonora (Yoeme resistance under Porfirian deportation), Cascadia's Bajas (San Quintín farmworkers' 2015 strike), the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (Chicano movement and farmworker boycotts): compare how long stands are held and against whom, without merging them.
