## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City (Tepito, La Merced) and Puebla:** check street-market norms of fair weight and price against chain stores.
- **Nuevo León and Chihuahua:** check *autos chocolate* and cross-border goods as honesty toward the state in border states.
- **Jalisco and the Bajío:** check family-business norms of keeping one's word in trade.
- **Oaxaca (Isthmus Zapotec market women; Ñuu Savi and Mazatec towns):** check bargaining customs and trickster tales in each language.
- **Highland Chiapas:** check how lying and keeping one's word are spoken of in Tsotsil and Tseltal sources; community justice itself is for Libra and Capricorn.
- **The Sierra Tarahumara:** check Rarámuri teaching on honesty as given in leaders' speeches.
- **Afro-Mexican Costa Chica:** check oral tales of tricksters and honest poor men, where recorded.
- **Mennonite colonies, Lebanese-Mexican and Jewish merchants:** check each community's trade ethics in its own records.
- **University students:** check plagiarism and cheating surveys at UNAM, the Tec de Monterrey and state universities.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (Maya trickster tales), Sonora (cross-border trade at Nogales), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's *autos chocolate* trade and the *línea*), the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (scams aimed at migrants sending money): compare where honest dealing is expected, without merging them.
