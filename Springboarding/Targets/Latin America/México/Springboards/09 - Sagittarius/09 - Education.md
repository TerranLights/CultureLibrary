## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check UNAM, IPN and the private universities as distinct paths to standing, without letting the capital stand for the nation.
- **Jalisco and Los Altos:** check Catholic and public universities' rival histories in Guadalajara.
- **Nuevo León:** check the Tec and the state university (UANL) on what an engineer or *licenciado* is worth.
- **Michoacán and Guerrero:** check rural *normal* schools and their graduates' standing in villages.
- **Chiapas, Oaxaca and Puebla:** check intercultural universities and UPN graduates among Tsotsil, Tseltal, Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Nahua and Totonac students.
- **Purépecha highlands:** check teachers' and Purépecha-language programs.
- **The Sierra Tarahumara:** check boarding schools and Rarámuri students' paths into higher learning.
- **Afro-Mexican Costa Chica:** check who reaches university and which programs serve them.
- **Mennonite colonies:** check Old Colony church schools against SEP schooling and the families who leave for further study.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the Universidad Autónoma de Yucatán and Maya-language teacher training), Sonora (the Universidad de Sonora and Yoeme students), Cascadia's Bajas (UABC and CICESE), the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (Mexican students crossing for degrees): compare who gets a degree and where, without merging them.
