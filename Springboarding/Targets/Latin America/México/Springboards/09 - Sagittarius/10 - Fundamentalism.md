# México: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (Catholic, evangelical, anticlerical and press registers); Tsotsil for highland Chiapas; English and Spanish for the Mormon colonies; Plautdietsch; Nahuatl as revived by *mexicanidad* groups. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Guanajuato, Querétaro and Los Altos de Jalisco:** check *sinarquista* and Cristero memory, and traditionalist Catholic chapels today.
- **Guadalajara:** check La Luz del Mundo's Hermosa Provincia as a unit of its own, and how neighbors speak of it.
- **Puebla:** check El Yunque's origins and university networks as reported, separating proven from alleged.
- **Tabasco:** check how anticlerical memory and present-day evangelical growth sit together.
- **Highland Chiapas:** check traditionalist municipalities, expelled converts, Pentecostal churches and the small Muslim community as four separate cases.
- **Chihuahua and Durango:** check Old Colony Mennonite separation, mainstream Latter-day Saint colonies and the LeBarón offshoot, never as one group.
- **Mexico City:** check *mexicanidad* dance groups (*concheros*) and the capital's traditionalist parishes.
- **Hidalgo and the Huasteca:** check conflicts between Catholic majorities and evangelical minorities in Hñähñu and Nahua villages.
- **Monterrey and the north:** check large evangelical congregations and their schools.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (Pentecostal growth in Maya towns), Sonora (the 2019 killings of LeBarón family members near Bavispe), Cascadia's Bajas (Molokan and Pentecostal communities in the Guadalupe Valley), the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (La Luz del Mundo's congregations in California and Texas): compare strictness and separation, without merging them.

## 4. Flags
