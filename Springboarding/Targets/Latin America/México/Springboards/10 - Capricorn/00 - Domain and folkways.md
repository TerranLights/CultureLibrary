## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City and the Valley of Mexico:** check ministries and unions against the street trades of Tepito, where titles and bosses work differently.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check the Native council records against later municipal and church offices in the same towns.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos de Jalisco:** check parish and ranchero lines of standing against factory careers in León, Querétaro and Guadalajara.
- **Oaxaca (Central Valleys, Isthmus, Mixteca, Sierra Norte):** check assembly offices among Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk (Mixe), Chinantec and Triqui towns, which differ town by town.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil, Tseltal and Ch'ol cargo ladders against parties and Zapatista autonomous offices.
- **The Sierra Madre Occidental:** check Rarámuri, Wixárika (Huichol), O'dam and Náayeri (Cora) governors and elders.
- **Northern states (Nuevo León, Chihuahua, Coahuila, Durango, Tamaulipas):** check Monterrey's company careers, Old Colony Mennonite elders and Kikapú leadership as separate cases.
- **Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check Afro-Mexican town offices and brotherhoods in their own records.
- **Lebanese-Mexican, Jewish, Chinese-Mexican and Japanese-Mexican families; Republican exiles' descendants; internal migrants:** check how each rebuilt standing after arrival.
- **Comparison only (where México ends is the author's call):** Cascadia's Baja California (on California's clock; first opposition governor, 1989), Sonora (Yoeme pueblo governors; no summer time), Yucatán (its own time zone and Maya town offices), the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (hometown clubs that elect officers).
