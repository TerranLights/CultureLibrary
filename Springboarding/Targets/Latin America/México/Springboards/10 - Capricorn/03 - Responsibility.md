# México: Responsibility (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (press, courts, family speech); classical Nahuatl; Ñuu Savi; Tsotsil; Hñähñu; Spanish of Central American migrants in the south. **Concept in one line:** being answerable: taking charge of a task or a person, and answering for the results, good or bad.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check how Tláhuac and Iztapalapa residents (Line 12) and Coapa families (Rébsamen) name those responsible.
- **Hidalgo's Mezquital:** check Hñähñu and mestizo towns near Tlahuelilpan on blame after the fire.
- **The Mixteca Alta:** check Ñuu Savi collective answerability in the colonial court records against today's town assemblies.
- **Chiapas and Tabasco:** check shelters and parishes on the southern migrant route, and Central American migrants' own accounts of who helped.
- **Los Altos and the Bajío:** check how families assign a son's or daughter's responsibility to parents left behind by migration.
- **Agricultural valleys of Sinaloa and the Huasteca:** check day-laborer children's work and who answers for their schooling.
- **The Costa Chica:** check Afro-Mexican and Me'phaa families on who carries the household when fathers migrate.
- **Monterrey and the northern industrial cities:** check plant-safety cultures and how firms answer for accidents.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Sonora (the 2009 Hermosillo daycare fire and the long fight over blame), Yucatán (the 2021 apology to Maya peoples), Cascadia's Bajas (who takes charge of people waiting in Tijuana's shelters), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (children who interpret for their parents): check them to see where answerability is handled differently.
