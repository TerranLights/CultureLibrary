# México: Discipline (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (home, school, barracks, church and gym); classical Nahuatl; Ayuuk; Rarámuri; Spanish of the border factory towns. **Concept in one line:** training and keeping behavior to a standard, whether imposed by others or by oneself (self-discipline, self-control, willpower).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check military, Catholic and public schools against each other for codes of conduct and punishments.
- **The Bajío:** check penitential retreats and Catholic boarding schools beside new factory regimes in Guanajuato and Querétaro.
- **Tlaxcala and Puebla:** check classroom discipline in Nahua towns against mestizo towns, using Rockwell's cases.
- **Oaxaca's Sierra Mixe and Sierra Norte:** check Ayuuk and Zapotec band schools and how practice is tied to town service.
- **The Sierra Tarahumara:** check how Rarámuri families describe correcting children, in their own accounts.
- **Chihuahua's border plants and Monterrey's industry:** check shift rules, quotas and penalties.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal Evangelical converts' conduct rules against Catholic and traditionalist neighbors.
- **Chihuahua's Old Colony Mennonites:** check how church discipline is described by the colonies and by former members.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's export plants, set beside Juárez), Sonora (Yoeme deer and *pascola* dancers' training), Yucatán (Catholic and Presbyterian school codes), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (school-discipline data on Mexican-American pupils): check them to see where rules and punishments change.
