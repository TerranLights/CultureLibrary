# México: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (business, political, migrant and street registers; Sinaloa and northern speech for the ballads); Guerrero Nahuatl; Mazahua; Arabic for some Lebanese-Mexican firm records. **Concept in one line:** the wish and drive to rise, achieve or become more than one is: in work, wealth, standing, skill or service.

English keywords: social mobility Mexico; political careers Mexico; self-made Mexican businessmen; migrant aspirations; narco ambition; aspiracionismo debate.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check how the poor east of the city and the business west speak of *salir adelante*, using interviews, not one city-wide voice.
- **Monterrey and Nuevo León:** check company towns, Tec graduates and small traders separately.
- **Sinaloa and Durango:** check how young men and women discuss lawful and unlawful routes up, with care for safety.
- **Guerrero's Alto Balsas:** check Nahua painter-traders against neighbors who migrated north.
- **Estado de México and Michoacán:** check Mazahua and Purépecha migrants to the capital and to the US.
- **Oaxaca:** check Zapotec, Ñuu Savi and Triqui migrants' stated goals in home-town assemblies and abroad.
- **Los Altos de Jalisco and Zacatecas:** check how a son's or daughter's ambition is weighed against inheritance and the family land.
- **Lebanese-Mexican, Jewish, Chinese-Mexican and Japanese-Mexican communities; Spanish Republican exiles' descendants:** check each community's own histories of rising, not an outsider's summary.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's plants as a goal for workers from the south), Yucatán (Mérida's Lebanese-Yucatecan firms), Sonora (Hermosillo's car-plant careers), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (the *sueño americano* lived and told from the other side): check them to see where the ladder runs elsewhere.
