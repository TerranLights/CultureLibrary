# México: Hierarchy (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (with its class-marked accents and slang); classical Nahuatl; Ñuu Savi; Hñähñu; Teenek; Hebrew and Judeo-Spanish for some community records. **Concept in one line:** the ordering of people, groups, places or beings into higher and lower ranks, with differences of power, honor and access.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check how the Lomas and Polanco set apart from Iztapalapa and Neza in accent, space and service, using residents' own words.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check old creole families against Nahua towns and how each ranks the other.
- **Oaxaca's Mixteca and Central Valleys:** check whether Ñuu Savi and Zapotec descendants of colonial *caciques* still claim standing.
- **The Huasteca (Veracruz, San Luis Potosí, Hidalgo):** check Teenek, Nahua and Hñähñu places in the regional order against ranchers and towns.
- **Chiapas:** check *ladino* and Tsotsil, Tseltal and Ch'ol relations in San Cristóbal and the coffee fincas.
- **The Costa Chica:** check Afro-Mexican, Ñomndaa and mestizo towns on who ranks whom and by what words.
- **Monterrey and Guadalajara:** check old industrial and landed families against new money.
- **Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican and Spanish-exile families:** check marriage and school networks as rank-keepers.
- **Domestic workers and employers in every city:** check how workers from Oaxaca, Puebla, Hidalgo and Guerrero describe household rank.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the henequen planters long called the *casta divina*), Sonora (Yoeme towns and Porfirian deportations), Cascadia's Bajas (Kumiai and Paipai communities beside the tourist corridor), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (colorism research north of the border): check them to see where rank orders differ.

## 4. Flags

- **Sensitive or restricted:** color and class slurs (describe, never adopt); domestic workers' privacy; elite families' consent.
