# México: Merit (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (bureaucratic, academic and union registers); classical Nahuatl; Ayuuk, Zapotec and other languages of Indigenous scholarship holders. **Concept in one line:** deserving reward, rank or praise because of one's own effort, ability and results, rather than birth, connections or luck.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check UNAM, IPN and private-university students on how they entered and what they think opened doors.
- **Oaxaca, Chiapas, Guerrero and Michoacán:** check CNTE teachers' and parents' arguments over exams, seniority and service to the community.
- **Oaxaca's Ayuuk and Zapotec towns:** check how assembly service counts toward standing against diplomas.
- **Monterrey:** check private-university and corporate hiring that promises merit, against family firms.
- **Guadalajara and the Bajío:** check engineering and factory careers built by certification or by referral.
- **Federal bureaucrats in the capital:** check career civil servants against appointees in the same ministries.
- **Indigenous graduates (Nahua, Ñuu Savi, Tsotsil, Purépecha, Hñähñu):** check how fellows and their home towns speak of their success.
- **Spanish Republican exiles' descendants and Jewish and Lebanese-Mexican families:** check the schools and networks each used to rise, in their own histories.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Sonora (Yoeme offices given by ceremony, not test), Yucatán (Maya speakers in the state university system), Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana plants hiring by referral), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (whether Mexican degrees count north of the border): check them to see where merit is judged by other rules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for exams, researcher rankings and civil-service studies; mixed for hiring by connections (mostly surveys and anecdotes); thin for merit in Indigenous languages' own terms.
