## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check the Centro's vendor removals and Tepito's own codes against planned districts such as Santa Fe.
- **Ciudad Juárez and Monterrey:** check how residents describe the return of order after the violent years, and who they credit.
- **Veracruz and the Gulf:** check carnival, *son jarocho* fandangos and their unwritten rules beside port policing.
- **Highland Chiapas:** check Tsotsil town order (plaza, cargo processions) against the city of San Cristóbal's regulations.
- **Nayarit's sierra:** check Náayeri and Wixárika Holy Week and *mitote* ceremonies as ordered ritual time.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check carnival *huehues* and *camadas* as licensed disorder and the town committees that run them.
- **The Bajío's colonial grids (Guanajuato, Querétaro):** check how historic-center rules order space and who is moved out.
- **Afro-Mexican towns of the Costa Chica:** check how fandangos and the *danza de los diablos* are scheduled and ended.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Cascadia's Bajas (Tijuana's violent years and its policing; La Paz's carnival), Sonora (Yoeme Easter, where masked *chapayekas* stage disorder), Yucatán (Mérida's safe-city image, a claim to test), and the CSA's Tejanos and the US diaspora (El Paso, across from Juárez): check them to see where order is kept and broken differently.
