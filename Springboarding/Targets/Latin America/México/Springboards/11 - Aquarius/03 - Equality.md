## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check Polanco, Santa Fe and Iztapalapa side by side, and the *cuarto de servicio* on the rooftop, for how inequality is built into homes.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check *casta*-era parish records of the city against today's Nahua and Totonac towns of the state.
- **The Bajío and Jalisco:** check light-skin ideals in Los Altos and Guadalajara against factory workers' pay.
- **Oaxaca:** check whether fiesta spending levels Zapotec, Ñuu Savi and Ayuuk towns, and the Triqui and Mixteca migrants who leave to earn.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal women's claims against *kaxlan* landowners' and coffee planters' records.
- **Guerrero's Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check Afro-Mexican and Ñomndaa towns for recognition, color and schooling.
- **The north (Nuevo León, Chihuahua, Coahuila):** check Monterrey's wealth beside its *colonias*, Rarámuri day laborers in Chihuahua City and the Kikapú's standing.
- **Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican, Chinese-Mexican and Japanese-Mexican families:** check exclusion and the 1930s anti-Chinese campaigns of the north.
- **Central American migrants and internal migrants:** check how newcomers are ranked by origin and accent.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Yucatán (the 1916 feminist congresses and the Maya-*dzul* line), Sonora (the anti-Chinese expulsions of the 1930s), Cascadia's maquila cities and the CSA's Tejanos (school segregation and LULAC's fight): check where equality was argued first outside México's lands.
