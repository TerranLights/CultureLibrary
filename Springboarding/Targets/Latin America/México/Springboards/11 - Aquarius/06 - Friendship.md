# México: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (Mexico City slang, northern and Jalisco speech, and the *compadrazgo* vocabulary of the countryside); classical Nahuatl (song and poetry); Tsotsil; Ñuu Savi; Zapotec. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check *cuates* and *carnales* in old barrios like Tepito against office friendships among *godínez* workers.
- **Tlaxcala and Puebla:** check how many *padrinos* a family takes, and whether friends or kin fill the roles.
- **Jalisco and Los Altos:** check *compadrazgo* in ranchero families, and whether men's friendships form around the *charreada*, the cantina, the parish or none of these.
- **Monterrey and the north:** check where adult friendships form (work, church, weekend gatherings) and whether they cross between Rarámuri and mestizo neighbors in Chihuahua.
- **Oaxaca:** check Zapotec and Ñuu Savi friendships formed in *guelaguetza* exchange and in migration.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal co-parenthood with mestizo patrons as friendship across rank.
- **Veracruz and the Costa Chica:** check Afro-Mexican and Nahua friends at *fandangos* and *son jarocho* gatherings.
- **Chihuahua's Mennonites and Coahuila's Kikapú:** check whether friendships cross the colony's or the town's edge.
- **Lebanese-Mexican, Jewish and Japanese-Mexican families:** check friends inside and outside the community, and school ties.
- **Central American migrants and internal migrants:** check friendships formed on the road and in shelters.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** Friendship Park at the Tijuana border (Cascadia), where friends and families meet through a fence; Sonora and Yucatán, for northern and Maya friendship words; Tejano *compadres* in the CSA and diaspora friends: check how far *compadrazgo* stretches across México's edges.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for *compadrazgo* and urban networks; mixed for women's friendships; thin for Kikapú, Triqui and Chinantec friendship terms.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** *cuatismo* shading into corruption (its law goes to Libra); men's drinking and its harms.
