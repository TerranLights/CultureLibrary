# México: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (youth slang by decade; the Inquisition's colonial Spanish); Isthmus Zapotec; classical Nahuatl; Tsotsil; Plautdietsch. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far that departure is tolerated, punished or prized.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check El Chopo, Coyoacán's bohemians and Nezahualcóyotl's punk scene, and whose difference is called art and whose is called crime.
- **Querétaro and the Bajío:** check the 2008 attacks, and how residents of different faiths and ages describe pressure to conform.
- **Guadalajara:** check its rock and gay scenes against Los Altos towns nearby.
- **Oaxaca's Isthmus:** check muxe life in Juchitán, and whether Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk and Triqui towns have a comparable place, in their own words.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil converts and Zapatista women who refuse custom, and how each town answers.
- **Veracruz, the Huasteca and the Costa Chica:** check carnival inversion in Veracruz and Afro-Mexican and Nahua dancers who cross-dress at fiestas.
- **Monterrey and Ciudad Juárez:** check Monterrey's *kolombia* youth scene and Juárez's cholo styles.
- **Chihuahua's Mennonites and the Rarámuri:** check who leaves the colony, and Rarámuri who refuse both mission and mestizo ways.
- **Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican, exile and Evangelical communities:** check members who leave or marry out, and how families respond.
- **Comparison only (the line is the author's):** Cascadia's Tijuana punk and cholo scenes, the CSA's El Paso (where the *pachuco* name began), the Los Angeles zoot suiters of the diaspora, and Sonora's and Yucatán's own carnivals: check where México's subcultures were borrowed from or carried to.
