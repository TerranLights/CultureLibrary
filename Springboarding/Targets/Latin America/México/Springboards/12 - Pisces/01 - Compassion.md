# México: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (church, welfare-office and street registers); classical Nahuatl; Tsotsil and Tseltal; Rarámuri; Isthmus Zapotec. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check alms at church doors and to children at traffic lights against the city's welfare offices and street-outreach groups.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check confraternity and parish charity against relief run by textile employers.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos:** check how parish committees choose whom to help after a death or a crop failure.
- **Jalisco and Guadalajara:** check migrant shelters on the western rail route and the parish networks behind them.
- **Michoacán:** check Purépecha town hospitals descended from Quiroga's foundations against today's clinics.
- **Oaxaca:** check *tequio* help for a family in trouble in Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk and Triqui towns, and whom it excludes.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal care for widows and orphans; aid to migrants at the Guatemalan line.
- **Guerrero, the Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check Afro-Mexican and Nahua towns' collections for the bereaved.
- **The Sierra Madre and the north:** check Rarámuri sharing in famine years; Monterrey's company welfare; Mennonite mutual aid; Kikapú care for elders.
- **Smaller communities:** check Jewish and Lebanese-Mexican welfare societies; Chinese-Mexican and Japanese-Mexican aid after the expulsions and internments; Evangelical rehab ministries; Central American migrants as givers and receivers.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** shelters in Yucatán, Sonora's Nogales, Cascadia's Tijuana, the CSA's Tejano border towns, and the diaspora's remittances: check them separately because the busiest migrant aid sits on those borders and its records would swamp México's own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial hospitals, Catholic charity and migrant aid; mixed for measured helping; thin for Kikapú, Chinantec and Triqui norms of pity.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl for the prayers and Molina; Tsotsil and Tseltal for terms of loving pity; Rarámuri for sharing in want.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** migrants' identities; children in welfare files; mercy toward offenders under law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist relief and meditation groups teaching compassion (a category; Buddhism is an open question for the author).
