*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check the self-denying mother of the city's cinema and press against working mothers' own accounts.
- **Estado de México and Morelos:** check Chalma walkers by town of origin, and whether Mazahua and Hñähñu groups describe the vow differently from city groups.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos:** check family memories of Cristero dead against the teachers' union's memory of its maimed teachers.
- **Michoacán:** check the cost of Purépecha fiesta offices and who may refuse one.
- **Oaxaca:** check unpaid town service in Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk and Chinantec communities, and how migrants pay it from far away.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal office-holding against converts who will not serve.
- **Guerrero and the Costa Chica:** check Afro-Mexican and Me'phaa towns for vows made for emigrant sons.
- **Coahuila, Nuevo León and the north:** check miners' families in Coahuila; how Monterrey's large firms ask workers for loyalty and what workers answer; Mennonite mutual aid; Rarámuri work parties.
- **Smaller communities:** check Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican and Chinese-Mexican founders' stories of hardship; exiles' descendants; Evangelical tithing; Central American migrants' sacrifices on the route.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** Yucatán, Sonora, Cascadia's Bajas, the CSA's Tejano border and the diaspora: check them apart because migrants' deaths in the desert and on the river crossings are mourned on the far side, and those rites must not be counted as México's.
