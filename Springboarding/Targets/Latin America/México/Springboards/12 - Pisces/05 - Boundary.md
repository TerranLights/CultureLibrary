## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check *vecindad* courtyards against high-rise and gated housing on knocking, visiting and noise.
- **Puebla and Tlaxcala:** check Nahua towns' boundary markers and walks against municipal maps.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos:** check how emigrant households keep news from town gossip, as residents tell it.
- **Jalisco:** check Guadalajara's *cotos* against the old barrios around them.
- **Michoacán:** check Purépecha communities' forest boundaries and the guards some posted, as told by the communities.
- **Oaxaca:** check Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk, Chinantec and Triqui towns' boundary disputes with neighbors, each in its own telling; the Zoque side of the Chimalapas.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal rules on who may enter a house or ritual space; the Suchiate as daily crossing for Guatemalan workers.
- **Guerrero and the Costa Chica:** check Afro-Mexican and Ñomndaa towns' land lines against cattle ranchers'.
- **The north:** check Juárez, Nuevo Laredo and Matamoros residents on crossing; Chihuahua's Mennonite colony fences; the Kikapú on their seasonal crossings, as they describe them.
- **Smaller communities:** check Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican and Japanese-Mexican families on in-marriage and the edges of the community; Protestant converts set apart in Catholic towns; Central American migrants held at the southern line.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** Yucatán's frontier with Belize and Guatemala, Sonora's Nogales, Cascadia's Tijuana and Mexicali, the CSA's Texas side of each river crossing, and the diaspora: check them separately because those are where México's own outer edges will run, and the author settles them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land disputes, border towns and housing; mixed for touch and privacy norms; thin for Kikapú and Triqui accounts of their own bounds.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish; classical Nahuatl and Ñuu Savi for painted maps; Zoque for the Chimalapas; Tsotsil for household thresholds.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clergy abuse survivors; migrants at the southern line; sacred precincts closed to outsiders. Land law and boundary rulings go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the map, border, housing and therapy pointers raised no standing rule.
