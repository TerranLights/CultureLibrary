# México: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** México, region Latin America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mexican Spanish (street slang of the capital, northern colloquial speech, clinic and pulpit registers); classical Nahuatl; Rarámuri; Tsotsil. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check *pulquerías*, cantinas and *sonidero* nights against *anexos* in the same boroughs.
- **Puebla, Tlaxcala and Hidalgo:** check pulque-producing towns, where people may class the drink as food, against city talk of it as vice.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos:** check emigrant towns where leaving for the north is itself the way out, and how those left behind cope.
- **Jalisco:** check tequila towns and their tourist trains against how locals there drink.
- **Michoacán and Morelos:** check Purépecha fiesta drinking rules; weekend towns around Cuernavaca and Tepoztlán.
- **Oaxaca:** check mezcal in Zapotec and Ñuu Savi ritual against the mezcal tourism trade.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal *posh* (cane liquor) in ritual against Evangelical converts' abstinence, as each tells it.
- **Guerrero and the Costa Chica:** check Acapulco's visitors against Afro-Mexican and Me'phaa towns nearby.
- **Veracruz and the Huasteca:** check carnival in the port and Teenek fiesta drinking.
- **The north:** check Juárez and Monterrey drug use; Rarámuri maize beer as ceremony, as they frame it; Mennonite colonies' ban on drink and media; Kikapú customs.
- **Smaller communities:** check Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican and Chinese-Mexican families' leisure clubs; Protestant temperance; Central American migrants' coping in shelters; rural migrants in city slums.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** Yucatán's Cancún, Sonora's border towns, Cascadia's Tijuana and Los Cabos, the CSA's Texas side and the diaspora: check them apart because the best-known resort and border-nightlife escapes lie there; check how many of their visitors come from México's own lands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for drug surveys, tourism figures and popular media; mixed for leisure time; thin for Kikapú and Triqui norms of drinking and withdrawal.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish (slang dictionaries are essential); classical Nahuatl for pulque terms; Tsotsil for *posh* in ritual; Rarámuri for maize beer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in *anexos*; users' identities; ceremonial drink and plants, which must not be filed as escape unless the people say so. Drug trafficking belongs to the Crime sheet; drug law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the drink, media, tourism and recovery pointers met no standing rule.
