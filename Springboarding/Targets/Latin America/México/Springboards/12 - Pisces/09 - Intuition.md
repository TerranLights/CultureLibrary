# México: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mexico City:** check *me late* and *colmillo* in office and street speech, and whether the young use them alike.
- **Puebla, Tlaxcala and the Sierra Norte:** check Nahua and Totonac healers' corn readings against clinic diagnosis in the same towns.
- **The Bajío and Los Altos:** check traders' and horse dealers' talk of a good eye.
- **Jalisco:** check *charrería* judges and cattle buyers on judging animals by feel.
- **Michoacán:** check Purépecha accounts of dreams before events.
- **Oaxaca:** check Mazatec, Zapotec, Ñuu Savi, Ayuuk and Chinantec diviners, only as each community shares them.
- **Chiapas:** check Tsotsil and Tseltal pulse-readers against nurses in the same clinics.
- **Guerrero, the Costa Chica and Veracruz:** check Afro-Mexican and Nahua fishers and farmers on reading sea and weather.
- **The Sierra Madre and the north:** check Rarámuri and Wixárika dream guidance only as shared; Monterrey's scouts and engineers on the gut against data; Mennonite farmers' weather judgment; Kikapú hunting knowledge.
- **Smaller communities:** check Jewish, Lebanese-Mexican and Chinese-Mexican merchants on judging partners; Evangelical "discernment" of spirits; Central American migrants' sense for danger on the route.
- **Comparison only (the boundary is the author's):** Yucatán's Maya day-keepers, Sonora's Yoeme, Cascadia's Bajas, the CSA's Tejanos and the diaspora: check them separately because famous outsiders' books on "Mexican" seers draw on those areas, and the hunches of crossing the northern line belong to them.
