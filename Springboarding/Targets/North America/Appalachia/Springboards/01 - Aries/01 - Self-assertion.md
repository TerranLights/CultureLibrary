# Appalachia: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English (with Pittsburgh speech in the north); Cherokee of the Eastern Band; Pennsylvania German; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coalfield union towns against farm and craft counties of the Blue Ridge:** check whether speaking up was learned in the union hall or the church house.
- **Pittsburgh and the mill towns against east Tennessee and western North Carolina:** check whether the same act (correcting a boss, sending food back) reads as normal or rude.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check council and family speaking order on the Band's terms, not through the outside "harmony" frame.
- **Old Regular Baptists against Holiness congregations:** compare who may stand and speak; testimony rules differ by church.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities:** check whether a refusal is voiced by the church district as a body or by individual members.
- **Women across the region:** check when daughters are told to speak or to keep still, by class and by church.
- **Latino newcomers in poultry and orchard towns:** check *igualado* and *aguantar* against shop-floor reality.
- **Out-migrants in Midwestern cities:** comparison only, for how speech was judged after the move.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in song, sermon, oral history and dialect studies; thin in measured assertiveness data for the region.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for council speech; Pennsylvania German for church framing; Spanish and possibly K'iche' for Morganton's workers (*check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** church discipline records name members; strike and union files involve living people; much humor collected about the region is outside-made, so separate it.
- **Held for review:** none; every speech, song and church source for speaking up passed the standing rules.
