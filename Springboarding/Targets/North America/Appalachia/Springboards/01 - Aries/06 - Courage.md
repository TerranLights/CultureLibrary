# Appalachia: Courage (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee of the Eastern Band; Pennsylvania German; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** acting rightly in the face of fear, danger, pain or social cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coalfield families against Blue Ridge farm families:** check whether endurance underground and endurance on the land are praised in the same words.
- **Steel and mill towns of Pittsburgh and the Ohio Valley:** check how Slovak, Polish, Hungarian and Italian families remember danger at work.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check honors and veterans' standing as the Band describes them.
- **Black Appalachians:** compare courage stories from coal-camp unions with those from the Clinton and Knoxville campaigns.
- **Amish, Mennonites and Brethren of the Shenandoah and Ohio:** check how nonresistance teaching and wartime service are each remembered in their congregations.
- **Women:** check whether miners' wives, widows and anti-strip-mining activists are named brave or only enduring.
- **Unionist and Confederate families of east Tennessee and western North Carolina:** check how each side's descendants tell the war.
- **Latino newcomers:** check *aguantar* against speaking up at work.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in war, labor and disaster records; mixed for women's and minorities' courage, which may sit in oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for war titles; Pennsylvania German for nonresistance; Italian for Monongah families' letters; Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** serpent-handling congregations have been filmed for spectacle; living disaster families; Cherokee war traditions are the Band's to share.
- **Held for review:** none; the war, rescue and labor pointers for courage needed no hold.
