# Appalachia: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee of the Eastern Band; Pennsylvania German; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency, from the household to the region.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coalfield counties against farm counties of the Blue Ridge and the Shenandoah Valley:** check whether wage work in mines changed what self-sufficiency meant.
- **Northern mill towns:** check lodge and union mutual aid against the southern household's own stores.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check collective self-provisioning and tribal programs on the Band's terms.
- **Black Appalachians:** check how segregated coal camps and towns built their own institutions when public ones were closed to them.
- **Melungeon families of the Tennessee-Virginia line:** check how mission schools and churches figure in local memory of help and independence.
- **Amish and Mennonites:** check church aid against neighbors' use of public programs.
- **Latino newcomers:** check remittances and family pooling against individual independence.
- **Elders:** check who stays on the homeplace alone and who moves in with children.
- **Back-to-the-land migrants and university-town newcomers:** check differences in what self-sufficiency is for.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public records, extension archives and literature; data on transfer income are easy to misuse as a stereotype, so read them as data only.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania German; Cherokee; Spanish; Slovak, Hungarian and Rusyn for lodge records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** poverty and welfare framing from outside the region; personal benefits records; law on taxes and benefits goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; no self-provisioning or relief source was held back.
