*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian basketball towns against Western Pennsylvania football towns:** check whether a school's record is spoken of as a town's standing.
- **Old-time and bluegrass players:** check contest players against those who play only at home or church.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check stickball's place between communities on the Band's terms; its preparation may be restricted.
- **Black Appalachians:** compare coal-camp leagues with Pittsburgh's Black clubs.
- **Scots-Irish heritage groups:** check how heritage games recast ancestry, and who takes part.
- **Polish, Slovak, Hungarian and Rusyn lodges:** check whether gymnastic and choir contests kept old-country ties.
- **Amish and Mennonites:** check whether school, play and fairs include ranked contests, district by district.
- **Latino newcomers:** check leagues against school sport for their children.
- **Women:** check contests open to women (fairs, pageants, fiddle categories) and when they changed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, fairs and music contests; envy and attitudes to winning are thin and may sit in oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee; Slovak and Polish for lodge records; Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** stickball's spiritual preparation is the Band's to share; racing's moonshine lore is an outside selling point to read critically; betting and election contests go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no sport, fair or contest source was set aside.
