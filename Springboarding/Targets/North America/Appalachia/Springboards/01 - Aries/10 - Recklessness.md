# Appalachia: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Pennsylvania German (Amish youth); Spanish; Cherokee only through Eastern Band program staff. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Coalfield counties against Blue Ridge tourism counties:** check whether crash, overdose and injury figures follow industry, roads or income.
- **Pittsburgh and Ohio Valley mill towns:** check how steel and glass work framed acceptable risk for young men.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check youth-risk programs as the Band reports them, without importing regional figures.
- **Black Appalachians:** check whether youth-risk data are reported separately for Black coal-town and city youth, or merged.
- **Holiness, Old Regular Baptist and Catholic congregations:** compare teaching on drink and gambling.
- **Amish and Mennonites:** check what the running-around years permit, district by district, and what closes them.
- **Italian, Polish and Slovak families:** check whether feast days and the fireworks trade carry their own safety lore.
- **Latino newcomers:** check road and workplace risk data by language.
- **Families who moved to Akron, Dayton and Detroit factories:** comparison only, to see whether risk on the road and at work changed after the move.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in public-health and crash data, and in outside media; data can be misread as a regional trait, so tie each figure to its cause.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Cherokee through program staff.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction, overdose deaths and suicide involve living families; reality TV and "hillbilly" humor are outside images; law on liquor, gambling and helmets goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; no risk, health or gaming source was withheld under the standing rules.
