# Appalachia: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; the Scots and Ulster church vocabulary; Spanish; Italian for mutual-aid societies. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Appalachia's independent Baptist counties** against **Northern Appalachia's Catholic and Orthodox mill towns**: check whether church discipline and parish life rank conduct differently.
- **The Shenandoah Valley's Mennonite and Brethren families**: check Valley church records against Scots-Irish Presbyterian session books.
- **Amish settlements of Appalachian Ohio and Pennsylvania**: check what each settlement's rules forbid and how members explain the ranking.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: separate values as the Band publishes them from those outsiders wrote down.
- **Black Appalachians**: check Black church and school records in coal camps and Knoxville.
- **Lodges founded by Rusyn, Italian, Hungarian and Slovak miners**: check mutual-aid bylaws for what members owed one another.
- **Latino newcomers**: check Spanish-language parish life in Dalton, Gainesville and Morganton.
- **Melungeon congregations** such as the Vardy church: check surviving records.
- **Out-migrant churches** in Ohio cities: comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in church records and outside accounts; outside "values" lists are a known trap here, so inside records come first.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for the Band's own value terms; Pennsylvania German and old German print for the *Ausbund*; Italian and Slovak lodge papers; Spanish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deficit framing of the region; Cherokee teaching the Band may not share outside; church discipline names real families.
- **Held for review:** none; no values survey or regional values text was held back.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Billings (1974) first, then Jones (1994), and note the method each uses.
2. Search the Southern Baptist Historical Library's catalog for minutes of one coalfield and one Blue Ridge association, and tally the charges brought.
3. Ask the Presbyterian Historical Society for session minutes of an early Shenandoah Valley congregation.
4. Read the Cherokee Preservation Foundation's annual reports for the values its grants name.
5. Search Berea's archives for *Mountain Life & Work* indexes on "values" and "character".
