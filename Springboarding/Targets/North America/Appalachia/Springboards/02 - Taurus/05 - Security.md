# Appalachia: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Slovak and Polish for parish and lodge papers. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coalfields**: check how union welfare funds and black-lung benefits figure in household planning.
- **Pittsburgh and the Ohio Valley mill towns**: compare steel pensions with coal benefits, for how a closure changed old-age plans.
- **Great Smoky Mountains and Blue Ridge farm counties**: check gardens, canning and livestock as stores against want.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: ask how Band members plan around per-capita payments and the minors' trust fund, in tribal sources first.
- **Black Appalachians**: check lodge benefits and church relief in coal camps such as Lynch and Kimball.
- **Rusyn, Slovak, Polish, Hungarian and Italian families**: compare parish credit unions and saving for burial across groups.
- **Plain churches of Holmes County and Big Valley**: check church aid against county insurance coverage figures.
- **Latino newcomers**: check how enforcement fears change banking, savings and school attendance.
- **Melungeon and other Newman's Ridge families**: sources likely scarce; start with Hancock County records and oral history.
- **Out-migrant households** of the northern factory cities: comparison only, for how leaving was used as a hedge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, unions and benefits; mixed for present savings, which national surveys report only by state.
- **Languages needed:** Slovak and Polish parish and lodge papers; Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Cherokee for the Band's own terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recent flood grief; immigration status; black-lung claims involve health records; tribal payment data belongs to the Band.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on safety nets, savings or food security was set aside.
