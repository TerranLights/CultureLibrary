## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Appalachian ballad counties** (western North Carolina, east Tennessee): check how jealousy is voiced in songs sung by women and by men.
- **Central Appalachian coalfields**: check quarrels over land and timber lines in court records against the outside "feud" image.
- **Pittsburgh's row-house neighborhoods**: compare street and parish claims to space with rural claims to land.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: ask the Band whether, and through whom, courtship and jealousy may be studied at all; sources likely restricted.
- **Black Appalachians**: check church and family sources on courtship and jealousy, sources likely scarce; start with oral history.
- **Melungeon families**: check whether families guarded lineage papers against outside claims.
- **Amish and Mennonite families**: look for church teaching against coveting and sibling equality in farm transfers.
- **Italian, Polish, Slovak and Hungarian families**: check courtship customs and chaperoning in parish memoirs.
- **Latino newcomers**: check Spanish-language shelter and church counseling services.
- **Out-migrant families**: comparison only.
