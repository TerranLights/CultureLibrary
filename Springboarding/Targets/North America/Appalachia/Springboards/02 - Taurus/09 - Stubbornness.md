# Appalachia: Stubbornness (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; the Scots and Ulster layer; Welsh; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** holding to a position, habit or course against persuasion, evidence or pressure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coalfields**: compare refusals by landowners against companies with refusals by miners against operators.
- **Families displaced for the Smokies park and TVA reservoirs**: check how holdouts are remembered by descendants and by the agencies.
- **Southwest Virginia and the New River Valley**: check pipeline holdouts against earlier fights over dams and mines in the same counties.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: compare the Snowbird community with the Boundary townships, and the Band's own accounts of Tellico's town sites.
- **Black Appalachians**: check Black miners' place in long strikes and in land fights, in union and family records.
- **Italian, Polish, Hungarian, Slovak and Welsh miners and steelworkers**: compare strike roles by group in the same towns.
- **Amish and Old Order Mennonite affiliations**: compare what each refuses and how each decides when to change.
- **Latino newcomers**: compare Guatemalan and Mexican workers' roles in plant campaigns.
- **Primitive and Old Regular Baptist congregations**: check splits and what each side refused.
- **Out-migrant union locals** in Akron, Dayton and Detroit: comparison only, for strike habits carried north.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on strikes, holdouts and church refusals; the outside image of the "stubborn mountaineer" must be kept apart from the record.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania German; Welsh and Slavic union and lodge papers; Spanish; Cherokee.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** union violence and its victims; Cherokee town sites under Tellico Lake; living families and protesters tied to land fights.
- **Held for review:** none; no pointer on holding firm or refusing change was set aside.
