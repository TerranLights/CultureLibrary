# Appalachia: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Appalachian), Pennsylvania German, Spanish, Cherokee, Hungarian. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Central Appalachian coal counties: check how residents speak about national television coverage of their towns, through local papers and oral histories.
- Pittsburgh's Black, Italian, Polish, Slovak and Hungarian neighborhoods: check which slurs and workplace slights each met, and how each group's press answered.
- Black Appalachians and descendants of expelled communities: check how expulsions are remembered and commemorated by families, not only by historians.
- Old Order Amish and conservative Mennonite districts: check how shunning is practiced and described by members and former members alike.
- Old Regular, Primitive and Holiness congregations: check whether discipline in the minutes aimed to restore members, and how often.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check mockery as performed by Cherokee dancers against the mockery Cherokee people faced in tourism and schooling.
- Latino newcomers: check reported harassment and insults through worker centers and churches, in Spanish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on stereotypes and media images (a large literature); mixed on church discipline (scattered minute books); thin on Latino and Arab communities' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania German for discipline terms; Hungarian, Polish and Slovak for ethnic-press replies; Spanish for newer towns.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slurs are to be searched and quoted only as evidence of use; expelled families' descendants; minors in bullying data; church minutes may name living members' kin.
- **Held for review:** nothing on humiliation was set aside; outside poverty specials are kept only for the replies they drew.
