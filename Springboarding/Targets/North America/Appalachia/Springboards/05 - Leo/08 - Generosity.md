# Appalachia: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Appalachian), Cherokee, Spanish, Arabic, Polish, Pennsylvania German. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Coal-camp and union households: check how strike relief and union funds sat beside church and family giving.
- Mill-town ethnic parishes and fraternal societies: check whether giving ran mainly within each group (death benefits, parish building) or across groups.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check how gadugi and shared revenue are described by the Band, and what members may keep private.
- Old Order Amish and Mennonite districts: check mutual aid within the church against relief work for outsiders.
- Black Appalachian churches and lodges in coal and steel towns: check benevolent funds and burial societies through congregation and lodge records.
- Lebanese and Syrian families: check church festivals and hospitality as described in Maronite and Orthodox parish histories.
- Latino newcomers: check remittances, *padrino* sponsorship and church collections, which tax data will miss.
- Households receiving outside charity: check how they speak of being *beholden*, set beside how givers describe the gift.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: formal giving is measured; informal giving (food, labor, lending to kin) is large and mostly unrecorded except in oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for gadugi and community terms; Spanish for remittance and sponsorship; Polish, Slovak and Hungarian for society minutes; Arabic for older church records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** per capita payments and members' finances (use only published aggregates); disaster victims; recipients of charity named in appeals.
- **Held for review:** no giving pointer was set aside; the Samaritan's Purse entry concerns giving outward and should not be mistaken for giving inside the region.
