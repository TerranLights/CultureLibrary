# Appalachia: Leadership (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Appalachian), Cherokee, Pennsylvania German, Spanish, Slovak. **Concept in one line:** guiding a group so that others follow willingly, by example, persuasion, vision or standing; distinct from the formal right to command (authority, Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check how the Band's own accounts describe leaders who persuade, against the elected offices of the Tribal Council and the Principal Chief.
- Coal counties' union locals and rank-and-file movements: check how local leaders emerged in strikes, and how they were removed.
- County political machines in Mingo, Harlan and similar counties: check informal leadership by families and officeholders, through memoirs and local papers.
- Old Order Amish districts in Ohio and Pennsylvania: check leadership chosen by lot against leaders chosen by vote, as members describe it.
- Pittsburgh's ethnic parishes and Black churches: check how pastors, lodge officers and newspaper editors led their communities.
- Women's leadership in community organizations, churches and strikes: check through the organizations' own records.
- Latino newcomers: check emerging leaders through churches, soccer leagues and civic groups, in Spanish.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor and community organizing (well archived); mixed for church and Amish leadership (mostly qualitative); thin for Latino leadership before about 2010.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee syllabary to read nineteenth-century council papers; Pennsylvania German for the titles of Amish ministers; Spanish for newer civic groups; Slovak and Polish for lodge minutes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current tribal politics and elections; family-run county politics (living people); the formal right to command and legal office belong to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing on leadership was set aside; the Cherokee council records' location and access terms need confirming with the Band.
