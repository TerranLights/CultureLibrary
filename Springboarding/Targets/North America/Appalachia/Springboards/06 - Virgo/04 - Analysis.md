*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee, Spanish, Pennsylvania German. **Concept in one line:** taking a whole apart to understand it: reasoning from parts, evidence, measurement and detail.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Oak Ridge, Knoxville and the surrounding farm counties:** check how lab workers and their neighbors speak of secret science, to separate an expert enclave from its hinterland.
- **Pittsburgh's universities against the mill towns:** check oral histories for how the move to computing is told on each side.
- **Coalfield miners:** check what miners' own reading of roof, gas and water looks like beside engineers' reports.
- **Communities after spills and floods** (Charleston, 2014; Martin County, Kentucky, slurry spill, 2000): check whose tests residents accepted.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check how the Band tells the syllabary's making, before outside histories.
- **Black Appalachians:** check alumni records of West Virginia State and Bluefield State for paths into science.
- **Shape-note singing communities:** check whether rudiments are still taught at singing schools and conventions.
- **Amish settlements:** check how Old Order schooling limits and directs reckoning.
- **Melungeon families:** check reactions to the DNA study in the Melungeon Heritage Association's publications.
- **Latino newcomers:** sources thin; check school districts' reports on English learners in mathematics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science institutions and disaster inquiries; mixed for household reckoning; thin for vernacular reasoning outside schools.
- **Languages needed:** Appalachian English for schoolroom and reckoning words; Cherokee to follow how the syllabary divides the spoken language; Pennsylvania German for Old Order school materials; Spanish for English learners' math records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** genetic studies of Melungeon and Native ancestry touch identity and belonging; let the families' own organizations frame them. Outside measures of the region (distress indexes) carry stigma; present them as labels to examine. Inquiry findings and liability go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing about measurement, testing or schooling was withheld for this concept.
