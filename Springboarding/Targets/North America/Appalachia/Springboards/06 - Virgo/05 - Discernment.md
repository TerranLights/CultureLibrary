# Appalachia: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee, Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Primitive and Old Regular Baptists against Holiness and Pentecostal churches:** check how each tests a call, a vision or a gift, and whether a spoken account or an outward sign is weighed.
- **Serpent-handling congregations** (Jolo, West Virginia, and others, *check*): check believers' own accounts of being "anointed" against outside news coverage.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check which stories tellers share publicly and which they keep for their own people before quoting any of them.
- **Black Baptist and Methodist congregations in coal towns:** check how members tested claims of a call to preach.
- **Amish settlements in Ohio and Pennsylvania:** check how choosing ministers by lot is described by members themselves.
- **Seed savers and growers on the Cumberland Plateau and in the Blue Ridge:** check how bean and apple varieties are named and told apart.
- **Pittsburgh and the Ohio Valley towns:** check older ethnic markets (the Strip District) for how shoppers judge goods.
- **Latino newcomers:** check community groups' and churches' warnings about immigration fraud, a judgment forced on new arrivals.
- **Rusyn and Hungarian parishes:** check whether old-country sayings about tricksters survive; likely thin.
- **Out-migrants:** comparison only; check whether returnees describe city dwellers' judgment of them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious discernment and food and seed knowledge; mixed for trading and weather signs; thin for fraud data below the state level.
- **Languages needed:** Appalachian English for the vocabulary of savvy and of being fooled; Cherokee for trickster stories in their own telling; Pennsylvania German for choosing ministers by lot; Spanish for fraud warnings aimed at newcomers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Cherokee stories and medicine knowledge are not for outsiders; follow the Band's guidance on what may be used. Serpent handling has drawn sensational outside coverage; begin with the believers' own words. Distilling and fraud law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** no pointer on judging people, goods or spirits was set aside here.
