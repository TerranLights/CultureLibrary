# Appalachia: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coal counties:** check checkweighman disputes and scrip cases for how a miner's fair pay was measured.
- **Northern Appalachia** (gas counties of Pennsylvania, West Virginia's northern panhandle, Appalachian Ohio): compare royalty lawsuits across state lines under different statutes.
- **Shenandoah Valley and Wachovia's German congregations; burley counties:** compare church price rules with county rates, and quota shares between landowners and tenants.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check the per capita ordinance's history of amendments.
- **Black Appalachians:** check heirs' property and partition cases for who brings them and how land is split.
- **Lebanese, Syrian and Jewish peddler families:** check license fees and local ordinances; sources scattered.
- **Italian, Polish, Slovak and Hungarian miners:** check whether job-bidding and seniority rules were issued in their languages.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements:** check probate files for how estates are divided among children.
- **Latino farm and poultry workers:** compare H-2A contracts with plant workers' wage records.
- **Out-migrants** (comparison only): seniority disputes at Ohio and Michigan auto plants involving mountain-born workers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, royalty and tobacco law; mixed for daily fairness customs; thin for peddlers and for Melungeon heirs' property (search Hancock County, Tennessee, deeds).
- **Languages needed:** German script for the Salem board's minutes; Spanish for H-2A job orders and complaints; the Band's own Cherokee for any per capita terms it uses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** per capita and minors' trust records are private to members; royalty suits name families. Labor-movement and company accounts each argue a side: read both.
- **Held for review:** the wage, royalty and allotment pointers raised nothing to hold back.
