*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coal counties:** check broad form deed and valley-fill cases for which side courts favored before and after each reform.
- **Northern Appalachia** (Pennsylvania's gas counties, the Monongahela Valley, Pittsburgh): compare support-estate and shale cases as two weighings of land against industry.
- **Allegheny Plateau and West Virginia:** read the 1851 apportionment quarrel from the western counties' petitions.
- **Southern Appalachia and the Blue Ridge:** check wet and dry votes by county and decade.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** compare the Band's wildlife rules with what its speakers choose to share about *tohi*.
- **Black Appalachians:** check folk-health practice in coal camps and city neighborhoods separately; sources thin outside oral history.
- **Melungeon families:** no balance-specific source known; start with Hancock County oral histories.
- **Italian, Slovak, Polish and Rusyn** mill-town families: check parish calendars of fast and feast against the mill's shifts.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements:** compare technology rulings across districts.
- **Latino newcomers:** check hot-and-cold practice and bilingual schooling in poultry and carpet towns.
- **Out-migrants** (comparison only): families who split the week between a city job and the home place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mineral and environmental law; mixed for moderation and work-rest balance; thin for folk-health balance outside Anglo and Cherokee sources.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for any teaching of *tohi*; Pennsylvania German for church teaching on yielding; Spanish for folk-health interviews.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cherokee teaching on balance and healing is partly restricted; ask the Band what may be used. Mountaintop removal is a live dispute: read coal-company and community sources side by side.
- **Held for review:** nothing on mineral, civic or bodily balance needed holding back.
