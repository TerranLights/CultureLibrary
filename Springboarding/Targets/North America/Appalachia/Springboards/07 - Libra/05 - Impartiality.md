# Appalachia: Impartiality (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** judging or treating people without favoring anyone for who they are or how they are tied to the judge: no favorites, no prejudice, no special treatment for rank, wealth, kin or friendship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coal counties:** check who paid deputies and who sat on juries in company-dominated counties against independent towns.
- **Northern Appalachia** (the anthracite counties, Pittsburgh, the Monongahela Valley): compare the Coal and Iron Police with city police under reform charters.
- **Tennessee Valley and Southern Appalachia:** check TVA personnel records against county patronage jobs.
- **Shenandoah Valley and southwest Virginia:** trace benches of justices of the peace for kin ties before and after 1851.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check how the Band's court and election rules handle kin, in its own documents.
- **Black Appalachians:** check jury lists, grand juries and the hiring of Black teachers and officers by county.
- **Irish and Welsh anthracite families:** check trial and press records for how each group was named in court.
- **Italian, Hungarian and Slovak** miners: check whether foreign-language newspapers covered trials as partial.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements:** check court records on Amish jurors and witnesses.
- **Latino newcomers:** check interpreter use and complaints in mountain county courts.
- **Melungeon families:** check whether court or election officials recorded their race in ways that limited their rights; sources thin.
- **Out-migrants** (comparison only): hiring and housing complaints in Midwestern cities that name mountain origin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for federal cases and hearings; mixed for county-level patronage (often in oral history and newspapers); thin for Rusyn and Lebanese communities' dealings with courts.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and Maya languages (Q'anjob'al, *check*) for interpreter records; German script for church-court records in the Valley; Cherokee for any Band documents issued only in the syllabary.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** vote-buying and corruption cases name living people; reversed convictions must be reported as reversed. Some outside writing presents mountain counties as run by kin alone: label it as outside and test it against records.
- **Held for review:** the jury, recusal and patronage pointers raised no concern under the standing rules.
