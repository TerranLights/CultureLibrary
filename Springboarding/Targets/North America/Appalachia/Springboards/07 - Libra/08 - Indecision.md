# Appalachia: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), German and Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coal counties:** check how long permit, reclamation and lawsuit decisions stay open, county by county.
- **Northern Appalachia** (the mill towns after the closures, the Southern Tier): check families' decisions to wait for a mill to reopen or to move.
- **Southern Appalachia, the Blue Ridge and the Smokies:** compare almanac-timed farm choices with extension-service advice.
- **Allegheny Plateau and West Virginia:** use Corridor H's record as the case of a choice deferred across generations.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** read the Band's own account of Tellico and of how its council decides.
- **Black Appalachians:** compare out-migrants' reunion clubs with those who stayed in Lynch and McDowell County.
- **Melungeon families:** no indecision-specific source known; start with Hancock County oral histories on leaving or staying.
- **Moravian, Amish and Mennonite** communities: compare the lot in each and when each stopped or kept it.
- **Italian, Polish, Slovak and Rusyn** families: check return-migration records for those who went back to Europe.
- **Latino newcomers:** check how families decide between settling and returning; mostly oral.
- **Out-migrants** (comparison only): returns after plant closures, as Berry's interviews allow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public decisions (permits, dams, highways); mixed for church decision-making; thin for personal wavering outside fiction and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** German script for Moravian lot records; Pennsylvania German for Amish accounts; Spanish for newcomers' decisions to stay; Cherokee for council terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life registries are confidential; church conference records may name members disciplined. Outside writing has long cast mountain people as slow to decide: label any such claim as outside and test it.
- **Held for review:** no pointer on the lot, on delay or on default rules was withheld.

## 5. First queries
1. Moravian Archives catalog: search "lot" and "marriage" for the Wachovia congregations; list years covered.
2. Read *TVA v. Hill* (1978); then find the 1979 appropriations rider that exempted Tellico.
3. Search West Virginia's highway department and court records for "Corridor H" lawsuits; list dates.
4. Library catalog: Dorgan (1989); note every passage on business meetings and unanimity.
5. Read Kentucky's intestacy chapter; list the order of heirs when there is no will.
