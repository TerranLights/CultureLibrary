# Appalachia: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English (with Pittsburgh's Scots-derived usage); Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, a mind, a group, an institution or a place, so the whole can be clean or healed again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Church of God and other Holiness churches against Old Regular Baptists: check how each names cleansing from sin, and how each puts out a member.
- Amish of Holmes and Tuscarawas counties, Ohio: check how shunning is applied and how former members describe it.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check what the Band shares about purification and what stays with its practitioners.
- Black descendants of families driven from Forsyth County and Corbin: check their accounts against town and county histories.
- Melungeon families of Hancock County: check how they remember records rewritten under Virginia's 1924 racial registration (the statute itself belongs to Libra).
- Donora and Mon Valley mill families: check whether cleaner air is remembered as rescue or as loss of work.
- Coalfield watershed volunteers: check who leads cleanup and who is reluctant to name a polluter.
- Mexican and Guatemalan newcomers in north Georgia: check cleansing remedies and healers across generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for folk medicine and church discipline; mixed for environmental cleanup; thin and painful for racial expulsions, where silence was part of the act.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee syllabary only through the Band; Pennsylvania German for Ohio church letters; Spanish and K'iche' or other Maya languages for newcomer healers (*check* which).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Cherokee medicine formulas are restricted knowledge; expulsions name living descendants on both sides; eating disorders sit under purging and need clinical sources.
- **Held for review:** folk-medicine, church-discipline and expulsion sources all cleared the rules; the list is complete as written.
