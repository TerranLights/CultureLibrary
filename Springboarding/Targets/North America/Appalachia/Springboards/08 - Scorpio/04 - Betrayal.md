# Appalachia: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Italian, Polish and Slovak for union and lodge papers of the coal and steel towns. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause to which one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Unionist and Confederate descendants in East Tennessee and western North Carolina: check how each family's telling and each county history name the other side.
- Union families and families of men who crossed picket lines in Harlan and Mingo counties: check whether the word "scab" still follows a surname.
- Black mining families of Lynch, Kentucky, and McDowell County: check Black church and family accounts of strikebreaking charges against union records.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee (Cherokee Nation in Oklahoma for comparison only): check how the Band remembers the New Echota signers.
- Descendants of families removed for Shenandoah, the Smokies and Norris Dam: check reunion groups' and churches' words for the promises made.
- Catholic parishes in the Pittsburgh, Altoona-Johnstown and Wheeling-Charleston dioceses: check survivors' and parishioners' own accounts.
- Italian, Polish and Slovak mine families: check heritage-language union and lodge papers for strikebreaking disputes.
- Latino workers after the 2018 raid at Bean Station, Tennessee: check how informing by employers or neighbors is discussed in Spanish-language accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Civil War loyalty and labor conflict; mixed for removals; thin for infidelity at the county level beyond songs and divorce counts.
- **Languages needed:** Italian and Polish for the union press; Spanish for newcomer accounts; Cherokee only as the Band shares it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse survivors; informing on undocumented neighbors; adultery in named living families; the New Echota killings remain charged inside Cherokee communities.
- **Held for review:** of the informer, strike and infidelity leads considered, none had to be withheld.
