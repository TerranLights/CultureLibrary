# Appalachia: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish; German for Salem's Moravian board minutes. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Primitive Baptists of the Blue Ridge against Holiness and Pentecostal churches: check how each weighs God's control against a person's own effort.
- Residents of former company towns (Benham, Lynch, Jenkins) against independent farm families nearby: check who each says decided housing, buying and schooling.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check the Band's own account of federal oversight and of self-rule since gaming revenue began.
- Amish of Holmes County, Ohio: check how *Gelassenheit* and the *Ordnung* are explained from inside.
- Mon Valley steel families: check memory of shop-floor control struggles after Homestead.
- Women in coalfield and mill-town households: check survivors' accounts of controlling partners through the regional coalitions.
- Grandparents raising grandchildren in eastern Kentucky: check how control over children shifts when parents are in addiction.
- Latino poultry workers in north Alabama and north Georgia: check line and break controls as workers themselves report them.
- Moravian descendants around Winston-Salem: check how the overseers' rule over Salem's trades is remembered and taught.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for company towns and land; mixed for household and partner control; the "fatalism" literature is largely outside writing and must be read as such.
- **Languages needed:** Kurrent for Salem's board minutes in the original; Pennsylvania German for Amish community papers; Spanish for plant workers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic-violence survivors and their locations; custody of children in kinship care; land and mineral control spills into property law, which belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing on company, household or land control was kept back for review.
