# Appalachia: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Arabic for Lebanese and Syrian family papers. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed (loyalty is researched here for now).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Coalfield counties, mill towns and the cities of Knoxville and Asheville: check how wide the circle of trust is in each, using the same survey items county by county.
- Snowbird Cherokees and the Qualla Boundary: check how trust in neighbors and in the tribal government is described in each community.
- Black families of Lynch and Harlan: check trust in doctors, schools and the union, against white neighbors' accounts.
- Lebanese and Syrian families of West Virginia: check family memories of credit given and repaid.
- Amish and Mennonite communities of Appalachian Ohio: check church mutual aid used in place of commercial insurance.
- Latino newcomers of Dalton and Morristown: check whom they rely on (church, consulate, employer) and how a raid changes it.
- Families served by free clinics and the Frontier Nursing Service: check how trust in outside caregivers was earned or refused.
- Out-migrants (comparison only): check whether trust in home-county kin held after the move north.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mutual aid and health outreach; mixed for survey trust (most items are national with small regional samples); thin for newcomers' trust.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for newcomer interviews; Arabic for older Lebanese family papers; Pennsylvania German for Ohio church letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** immigration status; vaccine views tied to living people; trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn; outside "distrust of strangers" imagery is not to be repeated as a finding.
- **Held for review:** a lead on trust was kept out of this sheet under the standing rule; it waits for the author only as a category: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
