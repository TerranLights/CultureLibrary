# Appalachia: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee (Eastern Band, syllabary); Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Italian and Hungarian for immigrant labor papers. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it, how it is held and how it is used (legitimate authority is Capricorn's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Clear Fork Valley and Pittsburgh's corporate offices: check how power over coal was described where it was mined and where it was owned.
- Mingo, Logan and Clay county voters: check how residents speak of courthouse rings, beside reformers' and outside journalists' accounts.
- Black voters and officeholders of McDowell County: check standing over the twentieth century, from Black sources first.
- The Eastern Band of Cherokee: check tribal-politics coverage in its own paper before outside reports.
- Women organizers (Mother Jones; black lung widows; Brookside strike wives in Harlan County, 1973): check what power they claimed and how others judged it.
- Italian and Hungarian immigrants under labor brokers: check padrone arrangements in heritage-language papers.
- Mexican families in Dalton's schools: check how parents describe gaining a say.
- Farm counties served by the TVA: check how residents weighed cheap power against lost land and voice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; power is among the most studied themes in Appalachian Studies. The "internal colony" frame is contested inside the field and should be read as a debate.
- **Languages needed:** Italian and Hungarian for padrone and strike-era papers; Spanish for Dalton; Cherokee only as the Band shares it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** current tribal disputes; named county officials; legitimate authority and the spirit of law belong to Capricorn, corruption as statute to Libra.
- **Held for review:** every elite, machine and organizing study considered could be named, so no hold applies.
