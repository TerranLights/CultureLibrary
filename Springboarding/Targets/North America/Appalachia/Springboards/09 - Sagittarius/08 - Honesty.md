# Appalachia: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English (trading, promise and church-discipline words); Cherokee scripture usage; Pennsylvania German; Spanish, for newcomers' dealings with institutions; Hungarian and Slovak for lodge and mutual-aid records. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Farm counties of the Blue Ridge and the Cumberland Plateau against Pittsburgh and the mill towns:** check how a person's word was relied on in each setting, where neighbors traded face to face or strangers met in markets.
- **Coal-camp families:** check company store records and union grievances for how honest weight and fair prices were argued about.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check how the Band and the Qualla Arts and Crafts Mutual guard against fake "Cherokee" goods sold to tourists.
- **Black Appalachians:** check church and lodge records for how members' honesty was vouched for when outside credit and courts were closed to them.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities:** check their own periodicals for honesty teaching in business with outsiders.
- **Hungarian and Slovak mutual-aid societies:** check bylaws and minutes for how members' claims were checked and fraud handled.
- **Latino newcomers:** check community groups' warnings for the schemes aimed at them and who sounds the alarm.
- **Out-migrants in Ohio and Michigan cities (diaspora, comparison only):** check memoirs for whether they say city dealings tested the habits of home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in church records, readers and press; mixed for present-day practice; thin for honesty measures cut to the region, and for Cherokee- and Spanish-language sources.
- **Languages needed:** Appalachian English for trading and promise words; Cherokee for truthful speech in scripture; Pennsylvania German for Amish honesty lessons; Spanish for fraud warnings to newcomers; Hungarian and Slovak for mutual-aid claim records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** discipline records name real families; the outside image of "mountain cunning" must not turn a trading tale into a trait. Fraud prosecution and contracts are law questions for Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** class books, school readers and trade tales passed the standing rules; nothing on candor or cheating was kept back.
