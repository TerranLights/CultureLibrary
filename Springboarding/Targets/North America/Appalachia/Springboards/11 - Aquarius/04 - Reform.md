# Appalachia: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families served by settlement schools** in Knott and Harlan counties: check letters and oral histories for whether reform was asked for or imposed.
- **Union rank and file against officers** in the coalfields: check Miners for Democracy and black lung records for reform from inside.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: check how the Eastern Band's own histories separate reforms it chose from those forced on it.
- **Black teachers and churches** in coal counties: check school and church records for reform led by Black Appalachians themselves.
- **Mon Valley steel families**: check how immigrant parishes answered Progressive reformers' surveys.
- **Dry and wet counties** across Kentucky and Tennessee: check local-option election records against church and moonshiner sources.
- **Amish and Mennonite churches** in Ohio and Pennsylvania: check how splits over the Ordnung are told by each side.
- **Latino newcomers**: check parish and advocacy groups for the reforms they pursue.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** very rich for the War on Poverty, settlement schools and coal reform; thin for Latino and Lebanese reform efforts.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for Eastern Band accounts of school and language reform; Pennsylvania German for church-split writings; Spanish for advocacy materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school memory is painful; outside reformers' records often describe locals in demeaning terms, so read them as outside accounts. Statutes and court cases on mining, schooling or alcohol belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no reform pointer was set aside; Caudill's influential framing is kept but flagged for the critiques it drew.
