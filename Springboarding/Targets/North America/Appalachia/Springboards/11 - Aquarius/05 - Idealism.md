# Appalachia: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Back-to-the-land newcomers** of the 1970s (Floyd County, Virginia; Madison County, North Carolina) and long-settled neighbors: check how each side's letters and newspapers describe the other.
- **Black Appalachians** in the Kanawha Valley and the coalfields: check school and church records for their own ideals of uplift and equality.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: check how the Eastern Band teaches right living in its own language programs, apart from outsiders' romantic images.
- **Amish and Mennonite families** of Holmes County: check whether *Martyrs Mirror* ideals are named in present-day church writings.
- **Pittsburgh's steel towns**: check what library and union records say about rival ideals of uplift.
- **Southern Tier and Allegheny Plateau** towns of New York and Pennsylvania: check for 19th-century reform and religious colonies.
- **Latino young people** in Dalton and Gainesville: check school and church youth programs for how they phrase their hopes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonies, schools and missions run by outsiders; thinner for idealism voiced by working people themselves, which lies in songs, letters and oral history.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for teaching on *tohi*; German for Moravian mission originals; Pennsylvania German for Amish writings; Spanish for youth and church materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** outsiders' idealism often cast locals as needing rescue; label each source's vantage. Cherokee teaching on right living is shared at the Eastern Band's discretion.
- **Held for review:** no idealism pointer was set aside; Carnegie's essay is kept as an outside industrialist's view, not the towns' own.
