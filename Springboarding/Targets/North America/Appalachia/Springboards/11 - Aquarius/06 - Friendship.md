# Appalachia: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Cherokee (Eastern Band), Pennsylvania German, Spanish. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Men in hunting and fishing circles** across the Allegheny Plateau and Central Appalachia: check camp logbooks and club rosters for who befriends whom.
- **Women in quilting and church circles** in the Blue Ridge: check circle minutes and letters for friendship apart from kin.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: check what the Eastern Band shares about friendship rites before using Payne's notes.
- **Amish young people** in Holmes County: check youth-group sources and Amish writers for friendship before baptism, and what changes after it.
- **Black and immigrant miners** in southern West Virginia camps: check oral histories for friendships across the color line and where they stopped.
- **Latino families** in north Georgia: check whether *compadrazgo* ties reach non-Latino neighbors.
- **Musicians at Galax and Clifftop** (West Virginia): check whether music friendships cross class and age lines.
- **Out-migrants** in Dayton and Detroit: comparison only; check letters home for friendships kept across distance.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in fiction, song and memoir; thin in measured data (no regional friendship survey known); Amish and Latino sources exist but need permission.
- **Languages needed:** Cherokee for friendship terms; Pennsylvania German, where *Freind* means a relative; Spanish for parish and family sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** baptism registers and prison messages are personal; ask first. Cherokee ceremonial detail stays with the Eastern Band.
- **Held for review:** one friendship pointer was set aside under the standing rule and is held here only as a category: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
