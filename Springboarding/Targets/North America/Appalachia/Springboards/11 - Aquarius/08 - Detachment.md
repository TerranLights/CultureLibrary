# Appalachia: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English, Pennsylvania German, Spanish; Cherokee (Eastern Band) terms through the Eastern Band's language program. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms are prized (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and others condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |

English keywords: reserve, stoicism, composure, harmony ethic, yieldedness, solitude, withdrawal, mine disaster waiting, grief customs, deadpan ballad singing, radio quiet zone, despair.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Regular Baptist and Primitive Baptist** congregations against **Holiness and Pentecostal** ones: check which feelings each lets show in worship and at funerals.
- **Mining families** in the southern West Virginia and Virginia coalfields: check disaster testimony for who is expected to stay calm and who may break down.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee**: check whether the Eastern Band accepts or rejects the outside "harmony ethic" label.
- **Amish and Old Order Mennonite** districts: check how *Gelassenheit* is taught to children in their own schools and writings.
- **Moravian descendants** in the Wachovia area: check whether lot customs survive in memory.
- **Mon Valley steel families**: check oral histories for men's withdrawal after job loss.
- **Black Appalachian** families in Knoxville and the coalfields: check church and family sources for their own customs of grief and restraint.
- **Latino newcomers** in Dalton and Gainesville: check whether reserve with outsiders is tied to fear of immigration enforcement, from their own accounts.
- **Green Bank newcomers** against long-time Pocahontas County residents: check how each describes the other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: strong in religion, ballads and fiction; thin in measured emotion data for the region (no regional display-rule studies known).
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania German for *Gelassenheit* writings; German script for Moravian lot records; Spanish for newcomer interviews; Cherokee through the Eastern Band.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, overdose and mine deaths involve living families; reserve can be mistaken for coldness by outside writers, so name Kephart and Gulick as outside sources. Immigration status is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; Buddhist meditation groups in the region's cities are left as a category for the author's decision, with no group named.
