# Appalachia: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee; Pennsylvania German; Spanish; Polish, Slovak, Italian and Welsh for the mill and mine towns. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Central Appalachian coalfields:** check whether company and union relief left records unlike church benevolence in the same hollows.
- **Pittsburgh and the mill towns:** check lodge and parish relief among Slovak, Rusyn, Polish and Hungarian families during strikes and layoffs.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check communal help in the Big Cove and Snowbird communities against tribal social programs.
- **Black coal-camp communities (Lynch, Benham, McDowell County):** check reunion associations and churches as channels of help after out-migration.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities of Appalachian Ohio and Pennsylvania:** check how church-wide aid works where commercial insurance is refused.
- **Latino newcomers in Gainesville (Georgia), Dalton and Morganton:** check parish ministries and hometown associations for help that never reaches public records.
- **Lebanese and Syrian families:** check Maronite and Antiochian parish charities in Charleston and Wheeling.
- **Out-migrants in Cincinnati (comparison only):** check urban Appalachian service agencies for help away from home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organized charity and missions (much of it written by outside givers); thin for help given hand to hand, which leaves few records.
- **Languages needed:** Slovak and Polish for lodge minute books; Spanish for parish bulletins; Cherokee for communal-work terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** free-clinic and food-line imagery is the stuff of poverty tourism; ask who took the pictures and for whom. Mercy toward offenders goes to Crime here; the courts' side to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a source bearing on mercy was left out under the standing rule and stands here only as a category: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Library of Virginia's catalog for Wise, Lee and Scott County order books, then for "poor" and "overseers".
2. In the Nunn Center's online index, filter the Leslie County interviews to those given by former patients and their children.
3. Find the Fogelson and Kutsche article in the Bureau of American Ethnology bulletins.
4. Download *Map the Meal Gap* county data for West Virginia and eastern Kentucky.
5. Search Huntington and Charleston council minutes, 2015 to 2019, for "syringe".
