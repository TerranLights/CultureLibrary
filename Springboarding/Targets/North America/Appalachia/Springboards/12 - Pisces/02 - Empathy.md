# Appalachia: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Appalachia, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Appalachian English; Cherokee; Spanish; Slovak, Polish and Hungarian for the mill towns; Pennsylvania German. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Asheville and Knoxville:** check whether long-time residents and recent arrivals describe each other's lives in the same terms during housing and tourism disputes.
- **Central Appalachian coalfields:** check how residents answer the national media that came after the 2016 election, in letters to local papers.
- **Black Appalachians and the Affrilachian writers:** check whom white regional writing imagines as "us", and what Black writers set against it.
- **Eastern Band of Cherokee:** check storytellers' framing of animal and human viewpoints before any ethnographer's commentary.
- **Slovak, Rusyn and Hungarian mill-town families in the Monongahela Valley:** check novels, parish histories and union records for portraits of one group by another.
- **Guatemalan Maya and Mexican newcomers:** check interpreters' and promotoras' accounts of what clinicians miss.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities:** check how their own papers describe outsiders' attempts to understand them.
- **Out-migrants in Detroit and Chicago (comparison only):** check how city schools and social workers described Appalachian children.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in literature and in writing about representation; thin in direct measures of empathy below state level.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and the Maya languages of western North Carolina's newcomers (*check* which) for clinic material; Slovak and Hungarian for parish and lodge papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** photographs of poor families are a live dispute; check consent and the sitters' later views. Outside images of the region come back here as "explanations" of its people; treat them as sources on the outsiders.
