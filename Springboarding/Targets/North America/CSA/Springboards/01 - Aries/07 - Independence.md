# CSA: Independence (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English, African American English, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Louisiana French, Vietnamese, Texas German, Mikasuki and Muscogee, Chickasaw. **Concept in one line:** not depending on others; self-reliance and self-sufficiency.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Texas freedmen's settlements and Black farm owners:** check how families framed owning land as independence from planters.
- **Agrarian small farmers and Piedmont mill families:** compare self-provision on the farm with wage dependence in the mill village.
- **Anglo, Tejano and Black Texans:** check whose independence each Texas holiday (March 2, San Jacinto Day, Juneteenth) celebrates.
- **Florida Seminole, Miccosukee and the Seminole Nation of Oklahoma:** check each nation's own word for its self-supporting history.
- **Cuban exiles and Haitian refugees:** compare the federal help each received and how each community talks about it.
- **Vietnamese gardeners and fishers of New Orleans East and the Mississippi coast:** check food self-provision after Katrina (the Viet Village farm project *check*).
- **Rural Black Belt elders and Florida retirees:** compare aging alone, with kin, or in care.
- **Hill Country German Freethinkers and their neighbors:** check how Unionist dissent in 1861-62 is remembered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for emancipation, secession and exile history; mixed for household self-reliance; nation-run food programs are best read in the nations' own reports.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, Vietnamese, Louisiana French, German for Hill Country papers, French for Le Page du Pratz, the nations' languages.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** secession and its tie to slavery; welfare rhetoric that has been racialized; present-day secession talk as contested. The legal side of secession and of tribal sovereignty belongs to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their household self-sufficiency and remittance studies are set aside under the standing rule.
