*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black high schools of the Prairie View Interscholastic League and white UIL schools:** check segregated-era records and what was lost or kept at merger.
- **HBCU and SEC rivalries:** compare whether bands, tailgates or games carry the rivalry.
- **Mississippi Choctaw, Muscogee and Cherokee Nation ball players:** check what each nation shares about its ball game.
- **Anglo rodeo, Black rodeo and trail riders, and Tejano charros:** compare who competes, who judges, and who watches.
- **Pageants by community (Miss Black America, Miss Lumbee, Mardi Gras courts):** check what each pageant rewards.
- **Cuban, Spanish and Italian Tampa (Ybor City) and Cuban Miami:** compare baseball and domino traditions.
- **German and Czech Texans:** check shooting and singing contests in club records.
- **Gulf Coast fishing rodeos (Dauphin Island and others):** compare entrants across Cajun, Vietnamese and Anglo boats.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport and pageantry; mixed for everyday envy and rivalry talk; thin for Vietnamese and Isleño contests.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish, Vietnamese, Louisiana French, Choctaw, German for club records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** segregated leagues and pageants; school mascots that use Native imagery are contested, with the nations' own views to be sought; hazing in bands and Greek-letter groups.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; pointers on their academic contests are set aside under the standing rule.
