# CSA: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English, African American English, Louisiana French, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese, Muscogee. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men of rural white, Black and Tejano communities:** check how each community explains risky deaths: choice, fate or circumstance.
- **Gulf Coast stayers and evacuees:** compare the reasons each gives, across Vietnamese, Black, Cajun and white families.
- **Rural *courir* riders in Mamou and Church Point and urban New Orleans revelers:** check the rules that contain risk in each.
- **Holiness snake-handling congregations of northeast Alabama:** check how faith frames danger; their core area belongs to the Appalachia target, so mark the line.
- **Mississippi coast casino towns and Choctaw-run and Seminole-run casinos:** compare how gaming risk and its revenue are spoken of.
- **Spring-break visitors and residents of Panama City Beach and South Padre:** check conflict over rowdiness and who sets the limits.
- **Rio Grande Valley listeners of corridos:** compare admiration and rejection of reckless heroes across generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for injury and crash data; mixed for meanings; the humor sources need care, because jokes about rural poor whites slide into stereotype.
- **Languages needed:** Tejano and Cuban Spanish for daring-to-a-fault slang, Vietnamese for *liều* in the community press, Louisiana French for the rural Mardi Gras run, Muscogee for the *Hadjo* war name.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** young men's deaths; drug violence; addiction as illness or choice, with both views named. Drunk-driving, gambling and fireworks law belong to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their risk-behavior survey breakdowns are set aside under the standing rule.
