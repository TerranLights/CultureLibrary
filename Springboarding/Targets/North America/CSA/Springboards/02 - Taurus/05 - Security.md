# CSA: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English, African American English, Gullah Geechee, Louisiana French (including Houma and Isle de Jean Charles speakers), Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** protection against loss and want: keeping one's livelihood, holdings and person safe now and in the future.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

English keywords: food insecurity, mutual aid, burial society, benevolent society, payday loans, hurricane insurance, flood, safety net, savings, preparedness, levee.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta and Black Belt households and Atlanta and Houston suburban households:** compare who is expected to carry a family through a hard month: kin, church, employer or state.
- **Free Black and later Black fraternal orders, and Tejano *mutualistas*:** check how each pooled risk when public aid was closed to them.
- **Houma and Biloxi-Chitimacha-Choctaw communities of the bayous:** check how each speaks of land loss and of where security now lies.
- **Vietnamese and Cajun shrimping families:** compare savings, boat insurance and rotating groups in the off-season.
- **Cuban arrivals of 1960-74 and of 1980:** check how differently public aid reached the two waves.
- **Coastal Florida and inland Florida homeowners:** compare insurance, storm shutters and plans to leave or stay.
- **Ozark and Wiregrass farm families:** check canning, freezers and hunting as household security, and how that is changing.
- **Elders on fixed incomes in rural counties:** check the role of Social Security checks in a whole family's month.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food insecurity, insurance and disaster records; mixed for household saving habits; thin for Houma and Biloxi-Chitimacha-Choctaw sources beyond news and the communities' own publications.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *mutualista* records and Cuban aid files, Vietnamese for parish recovery material, Louisiana French for bayou communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the exclusion of Black workers from early safety-net programs and the treatment of Black refugees in 1927 and 2005 must be stated plainly and each community's account given; poverty data can feed stereotypes of the rural South; guns kept for protection are a sensitive topic, with their law in Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; studies of their savings and mutual-aid circles are set aside under the standing rule.
