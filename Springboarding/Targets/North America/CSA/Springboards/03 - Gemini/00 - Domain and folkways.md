# CSA: Domain and folkways, communication and learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English in its regional forms, African American English, Gullah Geechee, Louisiana French and Creole, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese, Muscogee, Choctaw, Chickasaw, Mikasuki, Lumbee English, ASL. **Concept in one line:** the domain of communication and learning: how people talk, learn and connect close to home (neighbors, siblings, short journeys, early learning).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sea Island Gullah Geechee households and inland Carolina neighbors:** compare which tongue a child hears at home and which at school, and when a speaker switches.
- **Acadiana parishes against New Orleans:** check whether the *veillée*, French radio and the Creole tale survive in the same places.
- **Valley, San Antonio and Houston Tejanos:** check how home Spanish and school English sort by city and generation.
- **Black Belt and Delta communities against Piedmont mill towns:** check how school histories shaped what a family expects to teach at home.
- **Seminole and Miccosukee, Mississippi Choctaw, Poarch Creek, Lumbee:** see what each nation publishes on its language teaching, and who may share what.
- **Vietnamese families of New Orleans East, Biloxi and Houston:** check which generation carries talk between home and town.
- **Deaf Southerners, Black and white:** check how separate state schools shaped signing communities.
- **Latino Miami against Anglo North Florida:** compare the language a stranger or a shop is first addressed in.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for dialect study, Black school history and folklore tapes; mixed for neighboring and short trips; thin for Native-language teaching beyond each nation's own publications.
- **Lands:** which lands the CSA will hold is not yet decided (its pre-war row carries the USA); the Gemini sheets write for the core South, the Carolinas and Florida to Texas, Ozarks included.
- **Languages needed:** Cajun French and Kouri-Vini to use the Lafayette tapes, Spanish for the Texas corpus, Vietnamese, the Native nations' languages for their own teaching, ASL for Deaf school histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** laws barring enslaved people from reading, and segregated schools, are history to state plainly, with contested memory; dialect has long been mocked, so treat "proper" and "country" as labels to study; some Native language materials are for members only. Language and school law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their heritage-language classes stay out of this sheet.
