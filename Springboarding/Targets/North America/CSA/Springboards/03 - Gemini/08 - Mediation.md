# CSA: Mediation (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (Southern and Black church usage), Louisiana French, Sicilian Italian, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese, Muscogee and Mikasuki for the nations' own accounts. **Concept in one line:** a third party helping two or more sides settle a conflict or reach agreement without imposing a decision; more broadly, the go-between (broker, interpreter, intercessor).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Creek, Seminole and Choctaw:** check each nation's own account of its peacemakers and interpreters, before colonial records.
- **Black Baptist and white Baptist congregations of the same county:** compare how church minutes record quarrels settled inside the church.
- **Gullah Geechee praise-house communities:** check how dispute settling changed as praise houses closed.
- **Sicilian New Orleans and Tejano Valley Catholics:** compare petitions to saints and to the Virgin, and who carries the vow.
- **Cuban Miami across exile waves:** check how talk with the island's government has been judged, and by whom.
- **Vietnamese Gulf Coast parishes:** check whether the priest, an elder or a youth advocate goes between families and agencies.
- **Piedmont mill towns and Delta towns:** compare outside conciliators sent in strikes and in racial conflicts, and local trust in each.
- **Tejano and Vietnamese immigrant children:** check what tasks they take on as brokers, and at what age.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church discipline, colonial interpreters and civil-rights negotiation; mixed for family and neighbor go-betweens today; thin for Native peacemaking practice, which each nation describes for itself.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Valley shrine petitions and exile press on the *Diálogo*; Vietnamese for parish newsletters; Italian for St. Joseph altar traditions; French for parish records; Muscogee and Mikasuki for the nations' own words on peacemakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** go-betweens under segregation were judged harshly by some on both sides, so present more than one view; the *Diálogo* remains bitter in Miami; children as brokers is a welfare question. Court-annexed mediation and legal settlement go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their community go-betweens are not covered.

## 5. First queries
