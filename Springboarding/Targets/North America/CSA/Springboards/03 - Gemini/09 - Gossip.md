# CSA: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern and African American English, Cajun French, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese, Lebanese Arabic. **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct, with rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) studied here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eatonville and other Black towns against mixed towns:** check how porch talk worked where the whole town was Black.
- **Black and white beauty shops and barbershops of one city:** compare what is said, who listens, and what is kept out.
- **Small towns of the Wiregrass and Ozarks against Atlanta and Houston:** compare how far and how fast talk travels, and who it reaches.
- **Tejano *barrios* of San Antonio and Valley *colonias*:** check how *el qué dirán* is spoken of across generations.
- **Cuban and Venezuelan Miami:** check what Spanish gossip media covers, and which community figures it names.
- **Vietnamese of Houston and New Orleans East:** check when accusation and rumor in community media have led to fear or harm.
- **Lebanese, Greek and Jewish small-town families:** check how standing in a congregation was guarded against talk.
- **Lumbee, Poarch Creek, Choctaw, and Florida's Seminole and Miccosukee:** ask what each teaches about speaking of absent people, in its own words.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for rumor and racial violence, fiction and salon studies; mixed for everyday gossip (oral, mostly unrecorded); thin for Tejano *chisme* and for Native teaching on talk, which needs community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for gossip television and *barrio* talk; Vietnamese for community media; French for parish talk; Arabic for Lebanese family talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** insurrection scares and Rosewood are history of racial terror to state plainly, with contested memory; "Terror in Little Saigon" concerns unsolved killings and living people; the claim that women gossip more is a stereotype to test, never assume. Slander and defamation law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; talk and reputation inside them are not scouted here.
