# CSA: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern English (coastal and inland), Gullah Geechee, Cajun and Houma French, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Haitian Creole, Vietnamese, Muscogee. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us" (proposed home Cancer; Aquarius takes the group side).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Muscogee, Seminole and Miccosukee:** check how citizenship, clan and town each figure in counting who belongs, and keep the two Florida nations separate.
- **Houma, Coushatta and Lumbee:** compare how state or federal standing and community recognition each work.
- **Black Southerners of long-settled towns and recent Black arrivals from the North or the Caribbean:** check whether the second group is called local, and when.
- **White Southerners of old families, retirees on the coast and Sun Belt arrivals in Atlanta, Charlotte and Austin:** compare who calls whom a newcomer.
- **Tejanos of old San Antonio and Valley families, and arrivals from Mexico, Honduras or Guatemala:** check where the *de aquí* line falls.
- **Cuban arrivals of the 1960s, of Mariel (1980) and of the rafter years:** compare how earlier exiles received each.
- **Haitian Miami:** check what membership in the city means where both language and status were contested.
- **Jewish, Greek and Lebanese Southerners:** compare paths to acceptance in small towns.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Southern-identity polling, exclusion history and immigrant reception; mixed for everyday welcome rites; thin for Houma and Coushatta belonging beyond their own publications.
- **Languages needed:** Haitian Creole for Little Haiti voices, Vietnamese for Versailles community papers, Spanish for exile and Tejano archives, French for Houma elders' accounts, Muscogee for the nation's own terms of membership.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** sundown towns, the flag fights and slavery's legacy are contested memory to state without siding; tribal membership is each nation's decision; rules on citizenship sit with Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; questions of their welcome are not scouted on this sheet.
