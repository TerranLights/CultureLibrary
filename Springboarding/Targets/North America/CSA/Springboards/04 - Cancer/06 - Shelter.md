# CSA: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern English (including Texas and Louisiana usage), Cajun French, Tejano and border Spanish, Cuban and Haitian Miami usage, Vietnamese, Mikasuki and Creek (Seminole). **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black New Orleanians evacuated to Houston and Atlanta:** compare who was taken in by kin, by churches and by public shelters.
- **Lumbee and Robeson County neighbors:** check how the swamp-refuge story is told by Lumbee and by others.
- **Seminole and Miccosukee families:** keep the two nations' accounts of Everglades refuge separate.
- **Tejano parishes in the Valley:** check how congregations explain sheltering migrants, and who in the parish disagrees.
- **Vietnamese families of the 1975 camps and of later boat arrivals:** compare first shelters and sponsors.
- **Cuban and Haitian arrivals in Miami:** compare how each was received and housed on arrival.
- **Cajun families on the coast:** check how storm refuge and the fishing camp figure in leaving and returning.
- **Rural white families in Tornado Alley's southern reach:** check storm cellars and who shares them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for homelessness counts, disaster shelter and public housing; mixed for taking in kin; thin for Native refuge traditions outside each nation's own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** border Spanish for respite-center and parish records, Vietnamese for camp memoirs, Haitian Creole for Miami arrival accounts, French for coastal storm memories, the Seminole languages for building terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** flight from slavery and the Lowry war carry contested memory; homeless people and migrants in shelters are living people whose privacy must be kept; asylum and housing law go to the Libra and Capricorn sheets.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; any shelter pointer for them waits for review.
