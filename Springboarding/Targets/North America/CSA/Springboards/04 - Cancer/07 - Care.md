# CSA: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern English, Cajun French, Tejano Spanish, Miami Spanish, Vietnamese, Greek, Choctaw. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Southern congregations, Baptist and Methodist:** check what the nurses' guild and the mothers' board each do at services and in homes.
- **White Protestant households, Delta planters' kin and Piedmont mill families:** compare care given at home against care hired.
- **Acadiana Catholics:** check who still seeks a *traiteur*, and how the faith healer and the doctor are used together.
- **Valley Tejano households:** check who in the family becomes the paid home aide, and how that sits with kin duty.
- **Cuban and Nicaraguan Miami:** compare elder care at home and in adult day centers.
- **Vietnamese elders of Alief and Versailles:** check how children and parishes split their care.
- **Greek and Lebanese Orthodox parishes:** compare women's societies and their sick visits.
- **Choctaw, Coushatta and Seminole:** see what each nation's health programs say about family and tribal care.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for nursing history, domestic work and caregiving surveys; mixed for funeral and sick-visiting practice; thin for Native healing, much of which each nation keeps to itself.
- **Languages needed:** French for *traiteur* prayers and their keepers, Spanish for healers' accounts and aides' interviews, Vietnamese for elder-program staff, Greek for parish society records, Choctaw for healing terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** domestic work under Jim Crow and the isolation of leprosy patients need the workers' and patients' own voices first; Native healing knowledge may be restricted; health-care law and benefits fall under Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their caregiving is not scouted on this sheet.
