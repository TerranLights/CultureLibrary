# CSA: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English, African American English, Louisiana French, Tejano and Cuban Spanish, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Southerners across segregation:** check how daily rituals of deference (stepping aside, back doors) are remembered by those made to perform them.
- **Poor white Southerners of the hill country, the Wiregrass and the mill towns:** check how contempt for "trash" was felt and answered in memoirs.
- **Tejano schoolchildren of the 1920s-60s:** compare accounts of punishment for Spanish across South and West Texas.
- **Cajun and Creole schoolchildren:** check whether shame for French is told the same way in prairie and bayou parishes.
- **Choctaw, Seminole and Miccosukee families:** check how boarding-school memories are kept or withheld.
- **Cuban arrivals by decade:** compare memories of *repudio* rallies and of the welcome or scorn met in Miami.
- **Vietnamese Gulf Coast families:** check how losing face is talked about in parish and business life; sources scarce, start with community papers.
- **Cadets, band members and Greek-letter members:** compare hazing traditions and how members justify them.
- **LGBTQ students in rural and urban districts:** compare harassment reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for slavery, segregation and civil-rights records; mixed for hazing and bullying; thin for everyday ridicule in Native and Vietnamese communities.
- **Languages needed:** French for *charivari* recordings; Spanish for school-punishment testimony and exile memory; Vietnamese for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slave sales, segregation's rituals and boarding schools are degradation to state plainly in the survivors' and descendants' words first; hazing deaths and bullying involve living families; class slurs are quoted only as history. Punishment law and hazing statutes go to Libra and Capricorn; revenge goes to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; school bullying they report is not scouted here.
