# CSA: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** revival and market English of the South, Black vernacular, Cajun French, Mexican-Texan and Cuban Spanish, Muscogee and Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Revival churches on both sides of the color line:** compare how each tested a conversion or a call, and who did the testing.
- **Pentecostal and mainline Protestant churches:** check how each speaks of spiritual gifts, and which it doubts.
- **Cotton and tobacco trading towns (Memphis, Greenville, Wilson):** check how buyers' and growers' judgments of quality met at the sale.
- **Panhandle and Hill Country ranch families:** compare how horse and cattle judgment is taught to the young.
- **Apalachicola and Gulf oyster families (white, Black, Vietnamese):** check how knowledge of the water is passed on and trusted.
- **Muscogee and Poarch Creek speakers:** ask how sound counsel is recognized, in the nations' own terms.
- **Cuban exile generations in Miami:** compare who was suspected and who was trusted after each wave of arrivals.
- **Mexican and Central American arrivals in Texas towns:** check who they turn to when sizing up a stranger's offer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church records and commodity grading; mixed for everyday "sizing up" of people, which lives in oral history; thin for Native terms of judgment outside dictionaries and each nation's teaching.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *notario* warnings and exile press; Vietnamese for community warnings; Muscogee for the nations' own words; French for Acadiana's sayings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** where judging people slid into judging by race, say so plainly, since telling apart and prejudging share a history here; the Wasp Network case is contested (spies or defenders, by side); conversion testing under slavery took place under masters' watch. Fraud law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their trust networks are not scouted here.
