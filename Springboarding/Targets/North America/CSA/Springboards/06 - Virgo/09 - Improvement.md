# CSA: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** farm-bulletin, sales-floor and pulpit English of the South, Black uplift and street usage, Cajun French, Westside San Antonio and Valley Spanish, Vietnamese of New Orleans East, Lumbee English. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

| Language | Term | Gloss | Notes |

English keywords: self-improvement, uplift, New South, progress, farm demonstration, home demonstration, extension, mechanization, makeover, self-help, motivation, community organizing.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planter, yeoman and freed families after 1865:** compare what each meant by improving land, and who owned the improved land.
- **Black uplift leaders and the poor they addressed:** check how ordinary families answered calls to change their habits.
- **Delta families displaced by machines and those who stayed:** compare memories of the same "progress".
- **Dallas and Houston salespeople and self-employed women:** check how motivational teaching is taken up, and doubted.
- **Westside San Antonio and Valley colonia residents:** compare improvement won by organizing with improvement bought.
- **Lumbee, Poarch Creek and Mississippi Choctaw development efforts:** ask each nation how it tells its own betterment, apart from outsiders' plans.
- **Vietnamese youth and elders around Mary Queen of Vietnam parish:** compare how each generation names the goals of rebuilding.
- **Waco and other renovated towns' longtime renters:** compare who gained and who left.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for farm extension, the New South and self-help; mixed for household betterment; thin for Native-directed development outside each nation's reports, and for Vietnamese youth groups beyond their own materials.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for COPS parish records and Valley press; Vietnamese for New Orleans East programs; Cajun French for Acadiana's renewal talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "improvement" also named Removal, assimilation schooling and the clearing of Black neighborhoods, to be told first by those it struck; Ruffin was a leading secessionist; extension services ran segregated for decades; *mejorar la raza* is a colorist phrase. Land and housing law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their self-improvement and business networks are not scouted here.
