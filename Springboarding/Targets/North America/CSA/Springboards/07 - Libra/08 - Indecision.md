# CSA: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** everyday Southern English with its hedging verbs; revival and praise-house speech; Cajun French; Tejano Spanish; Vietnamese of the temples; election and court English. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upper South and Deep South states in 1860-61:** compare how long each convention took and who argued to wait.
- **Black and white congregations:** check how long "seeking" or the altar call lasts and who judges that a decision is real.
- **Louisiana, Texas and Georgia voters:** compare the jungle primary, the runoff and the plurality rule, and turnout in each second round.
- **Seminole and Miccosukee families of the 1950s:** see who voted to organize and who refused to choose either government.
- **Families facing end-of-life decisions in Catholic, Baptist and Vietnamese households:** check who speaks for the patient.
- **Coastal and inland Texans before a hurricane:** compare who decides to leave, and when.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for election, jury and legislative rules and for the 2000 recount; mixed for revival and praise-house practice; thin for family decision-making among Vietnamese and Tejano households (interviews needed).
- **Languages needed:** Gullah Geechee for seekers' accounts; French for *jongler* and family talk; Vietnamese for temple practice; Spanish for Tejano families' end-of-life choices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Schiavo case concerns a named family and remains painful; secession and massive resistance are contested memory, stated plainly; temple guidance is a religious practice, not a curiosity.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; their family decision customs fall outside this sheet.
