# CSA: Purging (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of Baptist minute books and patent-medicine ads; African American English (rootwork usage); Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Vietnamese; Greek. **Concept in one line:** getting rid of what is felt to be polluted, poisonous or corrupt, in a body, mind, group, institution or place, so the whole can be clean again.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Primitive Baptist, Missionary Baptist and Methodist congregations:** compare how often minute books record expulsions, and for which faults.
- **Black congregations in the Black Belt and the Lowcountry:** check discipline records against white churches in the same associations.
- **Descendants of expelled Black communities (Forsyth County and others):** check how each family tells the leaving and what it seeks now.
- **Tejano South Texas and Cuban and Puerto Rican Florida:** compare cleansing rites, folk purgatives and who performs them.
- **Rootworkers of the Delta and the Carolina coast:** separate spiritual cleansing from harming; take what practitioners publish.
- **Muscogee, Seminole and Miccosukee:** purification at their ceremonies only as each nation describes it.
- **Greek Orthodox, Jewish and Vietnamese Southerners:** compare fasting, ritual bathing and repentance calendars.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church discipline, red-baiting and contamination; mixed for racial expulsions (local records were often destroyed); thin for private cleansing rites.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for botánica and *empacho* materials; Vietnamese for temple rites; Greek for parish fasting guides; French for Carnival-to-Lent writing in Acadiana.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** racial expulsions and removal are history to state plainly; memorials and land claims over them are contested; folk-remedy dangers need care, not ridicule; Native purification is ceremonial and partly closed.
- **Held for review:** cleansing and fasting practice in South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
