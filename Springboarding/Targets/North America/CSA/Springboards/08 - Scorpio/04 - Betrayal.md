# CSA: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of war memory, church and jukebox; African American English; Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Texas German; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause one owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Piney Woods, Hill Country and upcountry Unionists against plantation districts:** check where "traitor" pointed during and after the war.
- **Sea Island and Lowcountry freedpeople's descendants:** check how land taken back in 1865 is remembered by family.
- **Muscogee, Seminole, Choctaw and Miccosukee:** read each nation's own account of treaties, truces and leaders who signed.
- **Tejanos in the Valley and in San Antonio:** compare memories of the 1930s removals by town and by citizenship.
- **Cuban Miami across exile waves:** check which acts (talks with Havana, visits, remittances) have drawn the charge of treason, and from whom.
- **Vietnamese Houston and New Orleans East:** check how charges of disloyalty arise in exile politics and who brings them.
- **Black congregations and civil-rights veterans:** separate the informer inside the movement from the informer inside the Klan, as each side named them.
- **German, Czech and Wendish Texans:** compare Civil War loyalties and later memory by settlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Civil War, Reconstruction and civil-rights informing; mixed for marital betrayal (songs and surveys, few diaries); thin for betrayal inside small immigrant communities, where the press is the main record.
- **Languages needed:** German for the Comfort and Hill Country papers; Spanish for repatriation testimony and exile radio; Vietnamese for exile-press charges; French for Cajun songs of cuckoldry.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who betrayed whom in the war and Reconstruction is contested memory, to be shown from every side; treaty betrayals are the nations' to tell; informer files name living families.
- **Held for review:** how betrayal is told in South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
