# CSA: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta and Black Belt planter families against Piedmont mill owners and Gulf oil and port interests:** check how each built and passed on local power.
- **Black officeholders since 1965:** compare Black Belt counties with Atlanta, Birmingham and New Orleans in what power was won and what remained out of reach.
- **Tejano South Texas:** set boss rule and Raza Unida side by side; check how power shifted county by county.
- **Cuban, Haitian and Black Miami:** check who gained power in the city and who felt shut out.
- **Seminole, Mississippi Choctaw, Poarch Creek and Lumbee:** compare the routes each took to influence (enterprise, federal recognition, the ballot), as each nation tells it.
- **Gullah Geechee coast:** compare Reconstruction-era power with present influence over land and development.
- **Women in politics, Black and white:** check how widows, wives and daughters reached office and what they were allowed to do.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for machine politics, civil-rights power and elites; mixed for hidden power (memoirs and press, little data); thin for spiritual power beyond legend, and for power inside small immigrant communities.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Raza Unida and Miami exile politics; Vietnamese for officeholders' campaigns; French for Creole and Acadian parish politics; Muscogee and Mikasuki only as the nations publish them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Reconstruction's overthrow and disfranchisement are history to state plainly; boss rule is contested memory among descendants; spiritual power is described only as believers describe it. How power is legitimated and checked belongs to Capricorn; rules of office to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the rise of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands to local office.
