# CSA: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of storm bulletins, blues and relief reports; African American English; Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Haitian Creole; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** a turning point of acute danger or breakdown, when things cannot go on as they were and must change, for a person, family, community or country.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mid-South river counties and the Ozarks' eastern edge:** check how earthquake and flood memory are told and whether preparation follows.
- **Cajun coastal parishes and Gulf ports:** compare storm-season customs, who stays and who leaves.
- **Delta and Black Belt families:** check how hunger, floods and farm crises are remembered against official reports.
- **Tejano Valley towns:** check which crises (storms, freezes, peso devaluations) are named and dated in family talk.
- **Cuban, Haitian and Vietnamese communities:** compare crises lived at a distance through kin abroad with those at home.
- **Lumbee, Seminole and Miccosukee:** look at tribal storm and flood relief beside county and federal aid, as each nation reports it.
- **Church-based relief in white and Black congregations:** compare who is helped first and who leads.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hurricanes, floods and the Depression; mixed for droughts and freezes; thin for crises inside immigrant households, which reach the record mainly through press and radio.
- **Languages needed:** French for Audrey survivors' tellings; Spanish for Beulah and Mariel coverage; Haitian Creole for diaspora radio after 2010; Vietnamese for Harvey follow-up in Alief.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** disaster deaths involve living kin; blame for failed responses is contested; the label "crisis" for groups of people (boatlift arrivals, migrants) is itself a question to study, not adopt.
- **Held for review:** disaster response within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
