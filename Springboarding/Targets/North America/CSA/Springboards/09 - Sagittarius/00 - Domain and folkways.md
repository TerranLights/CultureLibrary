## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Belt and Delta congregations, Black and white:** check whether one county's denominational mix splits by race, and since when.
- **Lowcountry and Sea Islands:** compare praise-house ways with inland Baptist practice.
- **Acadiana, New Orleans and Catholic Gulf Coast:** test where a Catholic majority shifts the public calendar.
- **South Texas Tejanos and the Hill Country Germans:** compare parish feasts with Lutheran, Catholic and freethinking settlements.
- **Cuban and Haitian Miami; Vietnamese Houston, New Orleans East, Biloxi:** check which faiths came with each exile and how surveys count them.
- **Seminole, Miccosukee, Mississippi Choctaw, Poarch Creek, Coushatta, Lumbee:** each nation's own account of its churches and ceremonies first.
- **Jewish Southerners; Greek and Lebanese Texans:** compare how visible small congregations are.
- **Ozarks, Piedmont, Florida's retiree belt:** test whether these fit Bible Belt patterns; Melungeon and highland comparisons belong to Appalachia.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for denominational counts and Protestant history; mixed for Catholic and Jewish life by county; thin for Vodou, Vietnamese temples and freethought.
- **Lands:** the CSA's borders await the author's decision (its row carries pre-war USA lands); these Sagittarius sheets aim at the core South, Virginia Tidewater and the Carolinas through Florida and Texas.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for mission letters and feast records; French for parish registers; German for freethinkers' papers; Haitian Creole and Vietnamese for living congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** proslavery theology and Lost Cause religion are history and contested memory; Seminole and Miccosukee ceremonies are closed; Vodou carries stigma.
- **Held for review:** temples and congregations of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
