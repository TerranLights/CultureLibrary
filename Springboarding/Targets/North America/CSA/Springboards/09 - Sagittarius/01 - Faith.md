# CSA: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** testimony and prayer-meeting English, white and Black; Louisiana French; the Spanish of Texas shrines and of Cuban Miami; Choctaw; Vietnamese; Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what cannot be seen or proven (God, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta and Black Belt Baptists against Lowcountry praise-house families:** check how answered prayer and conversion are described in each.
- **Pentecostal and Holiness congregations of the Piedmont and Gulf Coast:** compare where healing is a test of faith and where medicine is welcomed.
- **Acadiana and New Orleans Catholics:** check how vows to the Virgin and the saints work beside parish life.
- **Tejano South Texas and Cuban Miami:** compare *mandas* and shrine devotion with the faith of exile.
- **Vietnamese parishes and temples of the Gulf:** check how two faiths shaped one community's rebuilding.
- **Choctaw, Poarch Creek, Coushatta and Lumbee church communities:** check whether Christianity came by mission, choice or pressure, as each tells it.
- **Hill Country Lutherans, Jewish Southerners and the unchurched of Florida's suburbs:** test the edges of a "faith expected" setting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for evangelical faith and Black church history; mixed for Catholic devotion and healing revivals; thin for Native Christian practice and for leaving faith.
- **Languages needed:** Choctaw for hymn texts and their meanings; Vietnamese for La Vang devotion; Spanish for vows at shrines; Haitian Creole for faith talk outside the parish.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** missions among Native nations came with coercion and removal, to be stated as history; faith-healing deaths need care; private vows belong to the people who make them.
- **Held for review:** devotional life of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
