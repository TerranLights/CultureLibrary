# CSA: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of the porch, the sickroom and the plant gate, in white and Black usage; Cajun French; South Texas Spanish; Vietnamese; the German of the Moravian settlements. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Piedmont mill towns and Delta farm counties:** check how the loss of a main employer changed what people say their lives are for.
- **Black church members and the unchurched young in Atlanta, Memphis and Houston:** compare where purpose is named (church, family, career, cause).
- **Acadiana and New Orleans:** check whether music, food and family carry the weight that church carries elsewhere.
- **Tejano families of San Antonio and the Valley:** compare *ganas* and family duty as stated sources of meaning across generations.
- **Vietnamese and Cuban exiles and their children:** check whether the exile story still gives the second generation its point.
- **Seminole, Miccosukee, Lumbee and Houma communities:** each one's own words on land and kin as purpose; no outside reading.
- **Veterans near Fort Liberty (Bragg), Fort Cavazos (Hood) and the Gulf bases:** test what service gives and what leaving it takes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and surveys; mixed for oral histories of work and its loss; thin for Native, Vietnamese and Cajun sources on purpose itself.
- **Languages needed:** German script for the Moravian memoirs; Vietnamese for refugee memoirs; Spanish for Tejano *dichos* and oral history; Cajun French for older speakers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** despair, suicide and overdose touch living families; Native statements on land and purpose belong to the nations; veterans' accounts need consent.
- **Held for review:** purpose and life-story writing from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Ask the Moravian Archives (Southern Province) for an index of *Lebensläufe*, 1760-1860, and request a sample by sex and status.
2. Pull GSS LIFE by region and race for each decade since 1973.
3. Read Pew's 2018 report and request any regional tables.
4. Search Charlotte and Kannapolis papers for "Pillowtex" with "purpose" or "what's left", 2003-2008.
