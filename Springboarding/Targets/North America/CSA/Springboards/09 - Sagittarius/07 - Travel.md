# CSA: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English of the circuit rider, the cattle trail, the bus station and the tourist court; Black migration usage; Cajun French; Valley and Miami Spanish; Québécois French in Florida; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta and Black Belt families with kin in Chicago, Detroit and Los Angeles:** check how visits home and return moves are spoken of.
- **Valley Tejano migrant families and San Antonio's settled middle class:** compare the meaning of going north for work.
- **Cajun prairie and bayou parishes:** check how the expulsion story and later oil-field travel shape attitudes to leaving.
- **Sea Island families and newcomers to resort islands:** compare how arrival is judged on each side.
- **Cuban, Haitian and Venezuelan arrivals to South Florida:** check whether the route (airlift, raft, plane) changes how arrival is told.
- **Gulf Coast Vietnamese parish and temple members:** compare pilgrimage, return trips and refugee memory.
- **Miccosukee, Seminole and Mississippi Choctaw:** each nation's own account of roads, removal and staying.
- **Florida retirees from the Midwest and Québec:** test the snowbird welcome by county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for migration data and Great Migration history; mixed for tourism and pilgrimage; thin for Native travel accounts in their own words and for Québécois Florida.
- **Languages needed:** French (Cajun and Québécois); Spanish for Tejano migrant oral histories and Cuban arrival records; Vietnamese for pilgrimage and return-trip talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** removal and the domestic slave trade were forced journeys, to be stated plainly as history; raft crossings involve the dead and missing; heritage-trail selection is contested memory.
- **Held for review:** migration and travel of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
