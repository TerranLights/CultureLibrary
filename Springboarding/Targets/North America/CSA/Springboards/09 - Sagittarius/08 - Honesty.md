# CSA: Honesty (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of the trading post, the courthouse ring and the campus honor court; Black and Sea Island tale-telling; Cajun French; Texas border Spanish; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** truthfulness in word and deed: not lying, cheating or stealing; candor; keeping one's word.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mill villages and tenant counties:** check how honesty in the books and in the pay envelope was judged from below and from above.
- **Lowcountry and Georgia coast Gullah tellers:** compare when the trickster's lies are praised and when punished.
- **Louisiana parishes and Texas counties:** check how voters speak of politicians' honesty in each.
- **Valley and San Antonio Tejano families:** compare trickster tales across generations and churches.
- **Coushatta, Mississippi Choctaw, Seminole and Miccosukee:** check what each nation's own councils and papers say about honesty in office.
- **Vietnamese, Cuban, Greek and Lebanese merchant families:** compare how credit, reputation and the handshake work in each.
- **Students at the Citadel, UVA, HBCUs and large state universities:** test how honor codes are kept or ignored.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for corruption cases, honor codes and folklore; mixed for business honesty; thin for Native and Vietnamese teaching on honesty in their own terms.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Urdemalas tales and border radio; Cajun French for tale archives; Vietnamese for advice columns; Gullah for Jones's texts in their original spelling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** trickster tales must never become a finding about any people's honesty; Black dialect in planter-collected tales is a collector's rendering; fraud cases name living people.
- **Held for review:** honest dealing among South Asian-heritage merchants and families in the nation's lands.
