# CSA: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of seminary quarrels, prophecy charts and Bible-college rulebooks; Holiness and Black church usage; Spanish of Texas Pentecostal congregations and of Cuban Catholic writing; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Upstate South Carolina and East Texas Baptists, independent and convention:** check how separatism is drawn in each.
- **Black Holiness and Pentecostal churches of the Delta and Memphis:** compare their rules for separation from the world with white separatist churches.
- **Churches of Christ in Texas and the Mid-South:** check how restoration is argued and where congregations split.
- **Tejano and newer Central American Pentecostals in Houston and the Valley:** compare rules for dress, drink and dancing across generations.
- **Cuban Catholics and Cuban evangelicals in Miami:** check how each uses "fanatic" of the other.
- **Seminole and Mississippi Choctaw Baptists and traditionalists:** each nation's own account, no outsider labels.
- **Dallas, Atlanta and Houston megachurch members against small rural fundamentalist congregations:** test where literal reading softens or hardens.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Protestant fundamentalist history and survey items; mixed for Pentecostal and restorationist groups; thin for Catholic, Vietnamese and Native views of strict faith in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Varela and for Pentecostal congregations; Vietnamese for temple and parish disputes; Seminole and Miccosukee terms as the Tribes publish them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist" and "cult" are often insults; the Waco siege is contested memory with living survivors; anti-evolution statutes and court cases belong to Libra; abuse inside groups involves living people.
- **Held for review:** strict movements within South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
