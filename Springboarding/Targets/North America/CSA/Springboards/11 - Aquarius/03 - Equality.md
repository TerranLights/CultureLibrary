# CSA: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English across the region's color lines; Louisiana French; Tejano Spanish; Vietnamese; Haitian Creole; Cantonese; Moravian German for colonial records. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects; which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta and Black Belt counties against Atlanta, Houston and Charlotte:** check whether equality talk centers on land and wages or on schools and jobs.
- **Creoles of color and Anglo-American Black New Orleanians:** compare how each framed equal standing after 1868 and after 1896.
- **Tejanos of the Valley and of San Antonio:** test whether claims centered on schools, juries or wages in each.
- **Cuban, Haitian and Black American Miami:** check how each group judges the others' treatment by the city.
- **Delta Chinese, Lebanese and Jewish storekeeping families:** check where each was seated, schooled and buried in a two-race order.
- **Poarch Creek, Lumbee and Mississippi Choctaw:** compare campaigns for equal schooling under segregation, each in its own sources.
- **Women of the region's churches:** check where equal worth before God is taught and where equal roles are refused.
- **Moravian Salem and Lowcountry Anglican parishes:** compare where church membership crossed the color line, and when it stopped.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the civil-rights era and survey data; mixed for Tejano and Creole sources (much in Spanish and French); thin for Native, Chinese and Vietnamese equality campaigns outside local papers.
- **Languages needed:** French for *The Crusader*; German for unpublished Moravian diaries; Spanish for Tejano papers; Cantonese for Delta Chinese family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** slavery, segregation and the civil-rights struggle are stated plainly as history; white suffragists' racism is contested and needs all sides' records; no single group's memory speaks for the region.
- **Held for review:** equality campaigns of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
