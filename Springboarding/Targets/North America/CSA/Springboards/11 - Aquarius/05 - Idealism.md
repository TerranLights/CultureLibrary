# CSA: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern English, white and Black; Texas German; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Vietnamese; Louisiana French. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hill Country German freethinkers and Gulf Coast socialists:** compare why each colony was founded and what ended it.
- **Gulf Coast single-taxers and Ozark labor-college radicals:** check how neighbors and the press treated each.
- **Civil-rights students and their elders:** compare how SNCC and older church leaders state the same ideals.
- **South Texas ranch families and their Anglo neighbors:** test who idealizes which past in the same county.
- **Cuban, Vietnamese and Haitian exiles:** check how each holds an ideal homeland, and how the second generation answers it.
- **Seminole, Poarch Creek and Mississippi Choctaw:** start from what each nation teaches of right living; outsiders' romantic pictures of Native life get labeled as outsiders' images.
- **Communards at Twin Oaks and The Farm and the rural counties around them:** compare what each side says of the other.
- **Marshallese families of the Ozarks:** check whether church and island ideals are carried into Springdale; sources are scarce (start with community groups and church bulletins).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonies (newsletters, archives) and the movement; mixed for golden-age myths (contested memory); thin for Marshallese and small Native communities in published form.
- **Languages needed:** German for Bettina letters; Spanish for *Caballero*'s setting and Cuban exile memory; Vietnamese for the cause's language; Marshallese for Ozark church life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** plantation nostalgia and the "Lost Cause" are contested memory, to be stated plainly and set beside the enslaved and their descendants; Nashoba's enslaved residents left few records.
- **Held for review:** service and faith movements of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
