# CSA: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern and African American English; Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Vietnamese; Haitian Creole; Texas German; French for colonial journals. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planter-class sons and Black Southern men of the same decades:** check what sources survive for each group's friendships, and why the gap exists.
- **Rural Alabama and Atlanta women:** compare where friendship forms (church circle, café, office).
- **Black barbershop regulars and white hunting-camp members:** compare men's friendship spaces and who is let in.
- **Vietnamese alumni groups and Cuban café circles:** check how exile friendships keep a lost school or town alive.
- **Creole trail riders and Hill Country German tavern regulars:** compare rural friendship clubs built around a shared pastime.
- **Quapaw, Chickasaw and Choctaw accounts of alliance:** check how each nation tells "friend" as a word for alliance, beside French and English records.
- **Gay Southerners and chosen family:** test how friendship stands in for or beside kin in small towns and cities.
- **Tejano compadres and Anglo best men:** compare formal and informal friendship ties at weddings and baptisms.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elite letters and fiction; mixed for working-class and Black men's friendships (oral history needed); thin for Vietnamese, Haitian and Native friendship customs in print.
- **Languages needed:** French for Marquette and Cajun usage; Vietnamese for alumni programs; Spanish for Tejano and Cuban friendship talk; German for tavern-club records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** films of cross-race friendship under employment are contested; the softening of same-sex bonds on screen is a known issue to state plainly; Native alliance terms come first from the nations.
- **Held for review:** friendship networks among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
