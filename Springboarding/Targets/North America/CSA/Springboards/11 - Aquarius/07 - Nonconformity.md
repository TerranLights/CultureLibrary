# CSA: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern and African American English; Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Vietnamese; Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature, and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Piedmont Quakers and Lowcountry planters:** check how each community recorded those who refused the common practice.
- **Austin and the rest of Texas:** test whether the "weird" brand matches surveys outside the city.
- **Houston's rap scene and Atlanta's:** compare how each city's own nonconforming sound was received at home.
- **Ozark haven towns and Delta county seats:** check where the different find room and where they leave.
- **Cuban exiles of 1959-62 and Mariel writers:** compare whose difference each group would tolerate.
- **Black Southerners called "acting white" and white Southerners called "race traitors":** check how each label was used to hold people in line, using period sources.
- **Vietnamese and Haitian second generations:** check what elders and youth media say about children who leave the expected path; little is in print, so begin with call-in radio and youth-group pages.
- **Mississippi Choctaw and Seminole:** let each nation's own media say how it treats members who live apart from its ways.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and music; mixed for queer and dissenter histories (oral history matters); thin for Vietnamese and Haitian nonconformity in print.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *Mariel*; Vietnamese and Creole for radio and youth pages; French for Cajun usage.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "acting white" is a disputed claim; queer Southerners in small towns may not wish to be named; outsider artists' mental health should not be guessed at.
- **Held for review:** dissenting and minority-faith currents among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Coffin's chapters on New Garden, North Carolina, and note how neighbors treated antislavery Quakers.
2. Read O'Connor's essay, then McCullers's novel, and list the "outsider" figures each names.
3. Search the Briscoe Center finding aid for Maverick papers and the word's earliest uses.
4. Pull Pew's unaffiliated share for every Southern state, 2014 and the latest wave.
5. Search Eureka Springs newspaper archives for coverage of newcomers, 1970-2000.
