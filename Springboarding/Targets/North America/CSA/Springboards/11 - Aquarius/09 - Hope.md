# CSA: Hope (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern and African American English; Gullah Geechee; Louisiana French; Tejano and Cuban Spanish; Vietnamese; Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** wanting a good that is uncertain, and believing or acting as though it can come, for oneself, one's family, one's people or humanity.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Delta migrants of 1879 and of the Great Migration:** compare where each placed its hope and what the letters home say.
- **Lowcountry singers and inland congregations:** check how a song of hope moved between them.
- **Houston megachurch members and rural Baptist congregations:** compare preaching on hope and on increase.
- **Lottery buyers by county income:** test who spends most in each Southern state.
- **First-wave Cuban exiles and their grandchildren:** check whether the hope of return is kept, reshaped or let go, generation by generation.
- **Haitian and Vietnamese refugee families:** check how each speaks of hope for children against hope of return.
- **West Side San Antonio groups and Valley colonia groups:** compare the hopes each names in its mission and programs.
- **Mississippi Choctaw, Seminole, Poarch Creek and the Houma:** read each nation's own words on its future; hopes about land and recognition are theirs to state.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for songs, sermons and the movement; mixed for exile hopes (much in Spanish and Vietnamese); thin for Native statements beyond each nation's own media.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for exile oral histories; Haitian Creole for family accounts; Vietnamese for songs of return; Gullah Geechee for the song's early forms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nat Turner's rising killed many and brought reprisals; state it plainly and flag the contested text; deaths of despair touch living families; detention deaths need care.
- **Held for review:** hopes and prayers of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
