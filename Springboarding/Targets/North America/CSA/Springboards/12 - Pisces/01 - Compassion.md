# CSA: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English; Black Southern church speech; Tejano Spanish; Haitian Creole; Vietnamese; Cajun French; Choctaw. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black congregations and white congregations of the same towns:** set their benevolence funds and pantries side by side for whom each reaches.
- **Rio Grande Valley Tejanos:** check parish mercy work against county services, given how much rests on churches there.
- **Miami's Cubans and Haitians:** compare how each was received and how each now receives newer arrivals.
- **Gulf Coast Vietnamese, Buddhist and Catholic:** check whether temple and parish relief follow different words for compassion.
- **Jewish Southerners in Galveston, Atlanta, Memphis:** trace benevolent societies from their minute books.
- **Choctaw in Mississippi and the other Native nations:** ask how each nation tells its own acts of giving.
- **Lumbee and Black Belt rural counties:** compare where church aid stands in for scarce public aid.
- **Germans and Czechs of Central Texas:** check lodge sick-benefits as mercy organized by dues.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charity, health policy and giving data; mixed for immigrant relief; thin for Native giving beyond what each nation publishes.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Valley shelter records; Haitian Creole for Miami parish work; Vietnamese for temple drives; Choctaw for the 1847 memory as the nation tells it.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** charity under slavery and segregation (paternalism, segregated wards) needs plain statement and the recipients' voices; asylum seekers' identities must stay protected; mercy to offenders belongs to the crime sheet, and law to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** disaster relief and charity run by South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
