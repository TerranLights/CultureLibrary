# CSA: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** CSA, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Southern American English; African American English; Tejano Spanish; Cuban Spanish with Lucumí terms; Vietnamese; Cajun French; Gullah Geechee. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black and white Protestants:** compare "something told me" testimony across Baptist, Methodist and Pentecostal congregations, by race.
- **Tejano families of the Valley:** check who is said to have *el don*, and how families test it against clinics.
- **Miami Cubans in Lucumí houses and Catholic parishes:** compare readings, dreams and prayer as ways of knowing what to do.
- **Gulf Coast Vietnamese:** check premonition and fate-reading separately in Buddhist, Catholic and secular households.
- **Cajun and Creole Acadiana:** compare treaters' sense of an illness with Creole practice and parish clinics.
- **Gullah Geechee:** start from older Gullah speakers on dream travel and elders' reading of seekers.
- **Spiritualist camp residents and their visitors:** separate mediums' self-description from clients' accounts.
- **Native nations:** knowledge of land and weather may be shared or closed; begin from each nation's published environmental and cultural pages.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Spiritualism, psychic trade and sport; mixed for church discernment; thin for small communities' inner knowing, which needs interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Lucumí vocabulary to follow what a Miami reading says; Vietnamese for fortune-teller listings; Valley Spanish for the healers' *don*; Gullah for dream travel.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gut "reads" of strangers can be racial bias, so flag studies of profiling and send any law angle to Libra and Capricorn; seers and healers have been ridiculed, so start from their own words; closed Native knowledge stays closed.
- **Held for review:** intuitive and meditative teachings popularized in the South by U.S. teachers from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
