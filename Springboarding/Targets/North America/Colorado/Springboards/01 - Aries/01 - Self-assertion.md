## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range cities against Western Slope and Eastern Plains counties:** check whether public comment at county commissions follows the same turn order and tone as Denver's council.
- **Ski and mining towns (Aspen, Telluride, Leadville):** check how locals and second-home owners claim a voice at town meetings.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check the speaking order at acequia and parish meetings and who may challenge a *mayordomo*.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo; newer Mexican and Central American arrivals:** check how protest generations differ from those wary of public visibility.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check how tribal council and general meetings order speakers, from the Tribes' own records.
- **Japanese Americans of the Amache generation:** check the community's own accounts of the split between forbearance and open protest.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points and Colorado Springs:** check church, club and press records for who spoke for the community and how.
- **Evangelical and military Colorado Springs:** check how chain-of-command and congregational norms frame speaking up to a superior.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese communities:** check parents' and children's accounts of switching between home and school norms.
- **Ranching families:** check grazing and water hearings for how ranchers press claims against agencies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public meetings, press and protest archives; mixed for everyday talk (no conversation-analysis corpus known for Colorado); thin for Native languages and the Aurora communities.
- **Languages needed:** Mexican Spanish for *igualado*-type sanctions; *manito* Spanish for valley meetings; Japanese and English for the 1940s Denver press; Korean and Vietnamese for parent-child talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Alan Berg's murder; the camp-era draft dispute; the 1879 White River conflict behind any Ute negotiation record. Treat each as an account. Legal claims (water, schools) go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** assertiveness-training books by authors outside Colorado were considered and left out as not local; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
