## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range metro against the Eastern Plains, Western Slope and South and North Park:** check which regional label people give first and whether "Colorado" comes before or after it.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check how the choice between *Hispano*, *Spanish American* and *mexicano* has moved by generation and village.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo; newer Mexican and Central American communities:** check how *Chicano* is taken up or set aside by recent arrivals.
- **Nuche:** check Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute self-description separately, and the Northern Ute's Colorado history as told from Utah.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsétsėhéstȧhese:** check how Plains nations removed from Colorado frame their tie to Colorado places today.
- **Japanese Americans; the Chinese history of Denver:** check generation by generation, since camp and riot shaped each differently.
- **Black Coloradans (Five Points, Dearfield, Colorado Springs):** check how Western and Black identities are joined in community museums.
- **Volga German families:** check colony of origin against church as the first answer to "who are you?".
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** check what parishes, mosques and associations say about the second generation.
- **Latter-day Saint towns of Conejos County; evangelical and military Colorado Springs; ranching families:** check pioneer heritage, faith and service as identity claims.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Chicano movement, Amache and Hispano history; mixed for newer communities; thin for Volga German identity since the 1980s.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for village self-names; Japanese for Issei papers; Amharic, Tigrinya and Ge'ez for parish life; Korean and Vietnamese for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Native identity and enrollment belong to each nation; do not judge membership. Removal, Amache and the 1880 riot are contested: present each as an account. Census race categories touch law; take only the self-identification side here.
- **Held for review:** general Asian American identity surveys were considered; none was held, but they should be read only community by community.
