## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range suburbs against mountain counties:** check whether HOA rule and off-grid living cluster in different places and why.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check each Tribe's own account of self-government; the two differ in land base and enterprise.
- **Hispanos of Costilla and Conejos counties:** check collective rights on the commons against individual title.
- **Mexican and Central American families, Denver and the Arkansas Valley:** check who decides on marriage, work and care in surveys that separate them.
- **Volga German beet families:** check how family labor contracts bound children, from photographs and reports.
- **Japanese Americans at Amache:** check the gap between camp "self-government" and real control, as former inmates describe it.
- **Latter-day Saint towns of Conejos County:** check how "agency" is used in ward and family records.
- **Evangelical and military Colorado Springs:** check homeschooling and the limits a service member's family lives under.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora; Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check how parents and grown children negotiate choice of spouse and career.
- **Ranching families of North Park and the Western Slope; ski-town workers:** check grazing permits and seasonal jobs as limits on one's own time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for HOAs, schooling, drug and end-of-life reports; mixed for family decision-making by community; thin for Ute household practice outside what the Tribes publish.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for grant and commons records; Japanese for camp papers; Amharic, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese for parent-child decision-making.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal sovereignty is the Tribes' own to describe. The Sangre de Cristo commons dispute and Amache are contested: present each as an account. Any statute (home rule, end-of-life, cannabis, water) goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** rural retreat settlements of a religion the author has not yet ruled on were considered as an off-grid autonomy pointer; held as a category only (Buddhist retreat communities in the nation's lands).
