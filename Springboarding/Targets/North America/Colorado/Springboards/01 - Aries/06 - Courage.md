## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pueblo and the Arkansas Valley against the Front Range metro:** check how military honors enter civic identity in a steel town and in suburbs.
- **Mountain towns (Leadville, Silverton, Glenwood Springs):** check mine-rescue, avalanche and wildfire records for whose courage is told.
- **Colorado Springs military and evangelical families:** check unit histories and church memorials for the language used of the fallen.
- **Tsétsėhéstȧhese and Hinóno'éí:** check their own accounts of Sand Creek and of warrior societies, kept apart from soldiers' records.
- **Nuche:** check the Tribes' veterans' honors and what they share of earlier warrior tradition.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check penance, endurance and wartime service in parish and *morada* records the members open.
- **Japanese Americans:** check service against draft resistance as two kinds of courage the community still debates.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Buffalo Soldier and civil-rights records in Five Points.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese communities:** check refugee-flight narratives as courage of leaving.
- **Ranching families and rodeo circuits:** check how injury and fear are discussed among riders and stock handlers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war, firefighting and labor history; mixed for civilian rescue by region; thin for refugee communities' own accounts and for Ute warrior tradition.
- **Languages needed:** Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éitíít terms only from the nations; southern Colorado Spanish for penance and service records; Japanese for family letters from Amache; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for flight narratives.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ludlow and Amache are contested; present each account as an account. *Hermandad* rites are partly private. The Storm King deaths and suicide prevention need care with families.
- **Held for review:** military-history scholars of the 10th Mountain Division were reviewed for the standing rules; nothing was set aside.
