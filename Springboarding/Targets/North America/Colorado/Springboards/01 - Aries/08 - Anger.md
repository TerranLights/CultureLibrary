*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range cities against the Eastern Plains and Western Slope:** check where road-rage, bar-fight and domestic-violence records cluster and how local papers frame them.
- **Mining towns and the southern coalfields (Trinidad, Walsenburg):** check labor anger in union records against company and militia papers.
- **Nuche:** check the Tribes' own accounts of 1879 and removal, apart from the settler press.
- **Tsétsėhéstȧhese and Hinóno'éí:** check how grief and grievance are carried in the healing run and in tribal statements.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley; Chicanos of Denver, Pueblo and Boulder; newer Mexican and Central American arrivals:** check folk-illness terms and protest memory by generation.
- **Black Coloradans in Denver and Aurora:** check protest records and community statements after police deaths.
- **Japanese Americans and the Chinese history of Denver:** check how riot, camp and redress are remembered.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check community health sources for anger named as illness.
- **Evangelical and military Colorado Springs:** check church and base programs on anger after deployment.
- **Italian American Denver:** check its own account of the Columbus Day conflict.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for riots, labor war, protest and school violence; mixed for everyday display rules; thin for anger concepts in the Aurora communities' own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish in both registers for folk-illness terms; Korean for *hwabyeong*; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for community health; Japanese for camp letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, 1879, the 1880 riot, Ludlow, Los Seis, Columbine, Elijah McClain and Amache all involve deaths or ongoing grief; present each account as an account and contact families' and nations' organizations with care. Legal findings go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** psychological studies of anger with only national samples were weighed and left out for lacking a Colorado breakdown; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
