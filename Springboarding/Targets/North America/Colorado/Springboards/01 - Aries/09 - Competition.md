## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sport, fairs and the Olympics; mixed for school competition by community; thin for diaspora leagues and Ute games outside what the Tribes publish.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *charrería* and lowrider clubs; Korean for tutoring academies; Amharic and Somali for diaspora sport; Japanese for Nisei-era leagues.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Native games carry ceremony; cite only what the nations share. Envy and the evil eye touch folk belief; record names and practices, not judgments. School-rating disputes touch law: to Libra.
- **Held for review:** sport-science researchers at Colorado Springs and Boulder were reviewed against the standing rules; nothing was set aside, and none are needed for a first pass.
