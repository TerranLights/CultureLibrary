*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain and ski towns (Aspen, Telluride, Crested Butte, Silverton):** check how residents and visitors are judged differently for the same backcountry risk.
- **Front Range metro drivers against Eastern Plains and San Luis Valley roads:** check crash causes by road type before any claim about drivers.
- **Gambling towns (Black Hawk, Central City, Cripple Creek):** check how mining-era risk is retold to sell gaming.
- **Leadville and the San Juans:** check boom-and-bust memory in town museums.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley and southern Colorado:** check *descansos* as a record of road deaths, without inferring cause.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** check how in-language road-safety and harm-reduction materials describe risk.
- **Nuche:** check what the Tribes publish on youth safety and substance programs, in their own framing.
- **Colorado Springs military families:** check base safety campaigns on off-duty risk after deployment.
- **Korean, Vietnamese and Ethiopian Aurora:** check community health outreach on gambling and driving, through providers' materials.
- **Ranching families:** check farm and ranch injury records against the language of "getting the job done".

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for avalanches, crashes, gaming and fires; mixed for youth risk by community; thin for how smaller communities name rashness in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** ski and climbing slang of the mountain towns; Spanish for *descansos* and in-language outreach; Korean, Vietnamese and Amharic for community health materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** avalanche and climbing deaths and *descansos* involve grieving families; the Hayman Fire involved a criminal case (legal side to Libra). Avoid tying risk to any community without data.
- **Held for review:** ultrarunning and free-solo media were weighed for this sheet and kept out because Competition and Courage already cover them; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
