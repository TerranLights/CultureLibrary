## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche (Ute): Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute, and the Northern Ute's Colorado past:** check each tribe's own account of goods, gifts and tribal enterprise, as their land histories differ.
- **Hinóno'éí (Arapaho) and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse (Cheyenne):** set the trading-post ledgers beside what the nations publish about horse and robe wealth.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** separate grant villages, where commons and water shares define holdings, from Front Range towns.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo; newer Mexican and Central American arrivals:** compare steel-wage and movement-era records with remittance and *tanda* sources.
- **Black Coloradans (Five Points, Dearfield):** compare city business ownership with homestead land.
- **Japanese Americans; Volga German beet families:** separate Amache material from later farm records; read beet contracts and church records for household goals.
- **Latter-day Saint towns (Manassa, Sanford):** check tithing and storehouse practice.
- **East African, Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** look for *equb*, *ayuuto*, *gye* and *hụi* circles.
- **Ranching families (Eastern Plains, North and South Park) and the mining and ski towns:** set cattle and land holdings beside boom fortunes and second-home wealth.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mining, ranching and Denver; mixed for Hispano and Nuche material; thin for Somali, Eritrean and Central American households (look to community newsletters and oral histories, not other states).
- **Languages needed:** both Spanish registers for village records and current media; Núuchi-u and Hinóno'éitíít only through the tribes' own programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Nuche regalia and ceremonial items, and Mesa Verde burial goods (the Pueblo peoples' heritage, comparison only), are not for display or quotation; Sand Creek, the Ute removal, Amache and the land-grant disputes are contested accounts. Colorado's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for the state as it stands.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside for this overview; property and water-court law went to Libra and Capricorn instead.
