## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** read each nation's narratives separately, and set them beside Sand Creek testimony only as accounts.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** compare each tribe's own statements of its priorities in its publications.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** set proverbs and Hermandad teaching beside current parish and acequia materials.
- **Chicanos and newer Mexican families in Denver and Pueblo:** separate movement writing from parish and school materials in Spanish.
- **Central American families:** check parish and consular outreach; sources may be few.
- **Boulder and Fort Collins:** compare open-space and growth debates with college-town politics.
- **Colorado Springs (evangelical ministries, bases):** check how campaign language and military codes rank duties.
- **Western Slope energy and orchard towns (Grand Junction, Delta, the North Fork):** compare energy jobs against land and water in editorials.
- **Eastern Plains farm towns:** check county fair, 4-H and church records.
- **Mountain resort towns (Aspen, Telluride):** growth and wealth against workforce housing in council minutes.
- **Black churches of Five Points:** sermons and civil-rights records.
- **Volga German congregations of Weld and Larimer:** church histories and confirmation records.
- **Latter-day Saint wards of Manassa and Sanford:** ward histories.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese congregations in Aurora and Denver:** check bulletins and youth programs, in each community's language.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys and public argument; mixed for Native nations (written narratives exist, but much teaching is oral and shared only on the nations' terms); thin for Somali and Eritrean communities.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for proverbs and parish materials; Arapaho for the bilingual narrative edition; Korean and Amharic for congregation bulletins.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Arapaho and Cheyenne teaching is ceremonial and not for outside use; Amendment 2 and Sand Creek are contested; law aspects go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the teaching of Buddhist temples and contemplative schools in Colorado, as a category only (an open question for the author).
