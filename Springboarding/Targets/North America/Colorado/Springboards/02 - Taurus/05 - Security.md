## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** read each tribe's own description of its water, food and elder programs, and keep the two apart.
- **Hispano villages (Antonito, San Luis, Conejos):** check *Sociedad* records and parish aid for pooling before the New Deal.
- **Chicano and Mexican families of Pueblo:** check steel-mill layoffs of the early 1980s through union records.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American workers:** check day-labor and remittance sources; little may be written.
- **Plains wheat and cattle families:** Dust Bowl memory and crop-insurance practice.
- **Western Slope orchard and energy towns:** frost and price risk for Palisade growers against gas-field busts around Rifle and Parachute.
- **Mountain towns (Leadville, Silverton, Aspen):** compare mining busts with seasonal resort work.
- **Front Range suburbs:** fire, flood and insurance after 2012-2021.
- **Military families of Colorado Springs:** check deployment-era housing and spouse-employment studies.
- **Japanese Americans:** compare Issei *tanomoshi* practice with what was lost in 1942 and rebuilt after.
- **Volga German families:** check beet-contract records for advances and debts.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese households:** check whether each community's savings circle operates locally.
- **Black Coloradans:** Dearfield's collapse in the 1930s as a case of lost security.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, food help and pensions; mixed for mutual aid; thin for East African and Central American households.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *Sociedad* minute books; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for any savings-circle records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homelessness and food-aid data concern vulnerable people; tribal water settlements and Amache losses are contested; gun ownership for protection touches law, which goes to Libra and Capricorn.
