## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** continuity framed by the tribes themselves; compare with the Northern Ute's removal history.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check how the nations, now based outside the state, speak of Colorado places; sources lie with them.
- **Hispano families of San Luis, Chama and Antonito:** compare parish and ditch continuity across villages.
- **Chicano families of Auraria, the Northside and Pueblo's Bessemer:** check displacement and staying power in oral histories.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points:** check rising costs and church continuity in recent coverage.
- **Japanese Americans:** set the Amache uprooting beside the later Sakura Square community.
- **Volga German and Mexican beet-worker families of Fort Collins, Greeley and Windsor:** neighborhood surveys and church records.
- **Latter-day Saint towns of Manassa and Sanford:** check ward and family histories for generations on one farm.
- **Ranching families of North Park, South Park and the Plains:** check how long ranches have stayed in one family.
- **Western Slope energy towns:** cycles of arrival and departure.
- **Mining and ski towns (Silverton, Telluride, Aspen):** compare mining families who stayed with the resort era.
- **Military families of Colorado Springs:** check reassignment records and school-transfer studies for how often households move.
- **Korean, Vietnamese and East African Aurora and Denver:** check how long businesses along Havana Street and Federal Boulevard have lasted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mobility statistics, preservation and boom-bust history; mixed for Hispano continuity; thin for East African communities.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for parish and village records; German dialect for Volga German letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Bear Dance is shared publicly only in part; the Ute removal and Auraria displacement are told differently by different sides; the newcomer argument can slide into stereotype, so record each side's claims as claims.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist congregations in Denver, as a category only, pending the author's ruling on Buddhism.
