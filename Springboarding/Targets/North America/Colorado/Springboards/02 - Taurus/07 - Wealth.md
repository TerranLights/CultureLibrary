## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** read pre-1859 wealth in horses and robes through the nations' accounts first; outsider ledgers second.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** compare each tribe's reports on energy and gaming income; do not merge them.
- **Hispano landholders and herders of the San Luis Valley:** separate *patrón* families from smallholders in county records.
- **Chicanos of Pueblo and Denver:** check union wage records and small-business histories.
- **Central American and Mexican newcomers:** check remittance flows; written sources may be scarce.
- **Black Coloradans:** Five Points business owners and Dearfield's founders.
- **Chinese Denver of the 1870s-80s:** check the press before and after the 1880 riot for how Chinese merchants' wealth was portrayed.
- **Japanese American farmers of the Arkansas Valley and Brighton:** produce and nursery businesses before and after the war.
- **Volga German beet families:** check probate inventories and farm-sale bills for what counted as wealth once land was owned (acres, machinery, livestock, church gifts).
- **Ranching families:** land, herds and royalties against cash.
- **Resort towns (Aspen, Vail, Telluride):** second-home owners and local workers in the same valley.
- **Evangelical congregations of Colorado Springs:** sermons on prosperity and on giving.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora and Denver:** small-business wealth along Havana Street and Federal Boulevard; check community chambers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fortunes, foundations and county inequality; mixed for Hispano village wealth; thin for East African and Central American households.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *partido* and parish records; Cantonese only if Chinese merchant papers turn up.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal per-capita payments are internal matters; the 1880 riot, Sand Creek and the land-grant disputes are contested; tax law and royalty law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** no item set aside; personal wealth of named living families was left out as gossip rather than held.
