## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ute Mountain Ute and Southern Ute:** compare how each tells the 1895 split, since the bands took different paths that year.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check accounts of treaty refusal and acceptance among different bands, in their own histories.
- **Hispano Hermanos and parishes:** compare brotherhood records with church records on the same disputes.
- **Grant heirs of San Luis:** fence-cutting and court persistence as heirs tell it.
- **Chicano students and activists of Denver and Pueblo:** walkout and movement records.
- **Coal-mining families of Weld, Huerfano and Las Animas counties:** compare strikers' and companies' records.
- **Japanese Americans:** set those who resisted orders beside those who complied, without ranking either.
- **Mountain towns (Crested Butte, Telluride, Aspen):** anti-development fights.
- **Northeastern Plains counties:** secession talk and its local critics.
- **West Slope and Front Range water interests:** negotiating positions.
- **Volga German congregations:** check how long German-language services lasted.
- **Evangelical and secular Colorado Springs:** school-board and city-council deadlocks.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali families in Aurora:** check community press for generational quarrels over keeping old ways; sources likely thin.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, water and town fights; mixed for Native refusals (much recorded by outsiders); thin for newer immigrant communities.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Hermandad rules and local press; Japanese for Issei-era community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hermandad matters are private to the brotherhood; Dog Soldier and Summit Springs accounts are contested; the Granby case involves violence and should not be framed as heroism or as a group's trait; law questions go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the treaty-era refusals were kept as accounts from both sides rather than held.

## 5. First queries
1. Read *Penitente Self-Government* for council rulings on outside pressure.

3. Search Colorado newspapers for "Crested Butte" with "AMAX", 1977-1985.
4. Search 1927 Colorado newspapers for "Columbine mine" and "strike".
5. Pull the 2013 county ballot results and local editorials on the 51st State.
