## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check each tribe's research-review rules and publications to see who controls data about their members.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check how their oral accounts of Sand Creek are recorded against Army and press documents.
- **San Luis Valley and Arkansas Valley Hispanos:** check the Spanish-language press against the English papers of the same towns for differences in what was reported.
- **Amache families:** check what records were kept on them and which ones descendants have sought out.
- **Eastern Plains and North Park ranch counties:** check local-news loss and broadband gaps county by county.
- **Mountain and ski towns:** check avalanche and wildfire information use among seasonal workers against year-round residents.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American residents:** check census self-response by tract for reluctance to give information to government.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese residents:** check which home-country and diaspora media each group follows.
- **Colorado Springs military and defense workers:** check how classified work shapes what families may say and know.
- **Western Slope (Grand Junction, the North Fork):** check where news came from before and after statewide broadcasting.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press history and broadband data; mixed for tribal information (published by the tribes on their terms); thin for East African and Central American news habits.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for the southern Colorado press; Japanese for the WRA-era camp sections; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for diaspora media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute and Arapaho knowledge may be restricted; Sand Creek and Ute removal are contested and need each account named as an account; the open-records statute goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; national trust-in-media polls were considered and left as background because they give no Colorado figures.
