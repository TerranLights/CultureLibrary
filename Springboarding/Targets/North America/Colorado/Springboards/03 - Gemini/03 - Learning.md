## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check how each tribe's school and language program places elders in teaching.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check boarding-school records for Plains children sent from or through Colorado, beside the nations' own accounts.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check mission-school and public-school records against oral histories of learning in the family.
- **Denver and Pueblo Chicanos:** check community-school curricula of the 1970s against district curricula of the same years.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American families:** check whether children's household help is described as learning, by the families themselves.
- **Volga German beet families:** check field-work and school-attendance records by season.
- **Eastern Plains and South Park ranch families:** check 4-H project records for which skills each generation was taught.
- **Amache generation:** check camp schooling against pre-war and post-war records of the same families.
- **Aurora's Somali, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Korean residents:** check weekend Quran, church and language-school schedules for what is taught outside public school.
- **Colorado Springs homeschoolers and military families:** check how frequent moves and faith shape where children learn.
- **Latter-day Saint towns (Manassa, Sanford):** check seminary and family teaching records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for schooling statistics and boarding-school records; mixed for family learning, which needs interviews; thin for East African weekend schools.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for village and mission-school records; Japanese for camp-school materials; Somali and Amharic for weekend schools; Ute and Arapaho only through the nations' programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school records name children and must be read with the nations' consent and accounts first; beet-field child labor touches living families; school-desegregation law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside here; Boulder's private alternative schools were considered and left for the education concept under Sagittarius.
