## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check which gatherings each tribe holds open and how relatives travel between Ignacio, Towaoc and the Uintah-Ouray lands.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check their ties to Colorado sites today, such as Sand Creek visits by the nations.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check baptism records for who is chosen as *padrino*, and from how far away.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American residents:** check consular and hometown-club records for ties kept across the border.
- **Five Points and Dearfield:** check church and lodge rolls for ties kept after families left.
- **Volga German beet families:** check godparent and wedding-witness entries for which ties crossed village-of-origin and church lines once families met in the beet towns.
- **Amache generation:** check reunion and pilgrimage records for how ties were rebuilt after the camps.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese residents:** check association rolls for whether ties follow home region, faith or language.
- **Ski and mountain towns:** check housing and commute data for whether workers can live where they work.
- **Eastern Plains and North Park:** check isolation measures and distances to the nearest town center.
- **Colorado Springs military families:** check how long families stay and where they find ties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social-capital and survey data; mixed for church and association networks; thin for Eritrean and Somali networks, which may be held in community memory only.
- **Languages needed:** Amharic and Tigrinya for association records; Japanese for *kenjinkai* records; Spanish for baptism registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bear Dance and other Ute gatherings have rules on recording; rural isolation touches on suicide data, handle with care; Amache families are living.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; dating apps as a way of making ties were weighed, but no Colorado-specific source came to mind, so they wait for the research stage.
