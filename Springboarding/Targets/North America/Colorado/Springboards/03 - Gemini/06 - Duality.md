## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check how each tribe tells its different path through the allotment years, since one took allotments and one largely did not; legal detail to Libra.
- **Northern Ute history in Colorado:** check how the White River and Uncompahgre bands' removal to Utah is remembered on both sides of the state line.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check which self-name families use with outsiders and which at home.
- **Denver and Pueblo Chicanos:** check movement writing for how the hyphen and the border are framed.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American residents:** check school and church records for children raised between two countries' customs.
- **Amache generation:** check interviews for answers to the 1943 questions and what followed in each family.
- **Volga German families:** check church records for when German was dropped from services.
- **Front Range against Eastern Plains and Western Slope:** check ballot results and water fights for where the split shows and where it fades.
- **Ski towns:** check housing data for resident workers against part-year owners as two populations in one place.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian and Eritrean residents:** check generational surveys of language use for where second generations stand.
- **Colorado Springs and Boulder:** check press portrayals of the two cities as paired symbols, for variation behind the label.
