## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check the nations' own accounts of the 1840 peace and the 1864 council against the written record.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check whether tribal peacemaking or elders' councils are described publicly, and in what terms.
- **Northern Ute history in Colorado:** check accounts of 1879 from both Ute and settler sides.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check acequia minutes for how often the *mayordomo* settled a quarrel inside the ditch.
- **Southern coalfield families (Italian, Greek, Slavic, Mexican):** check union and company records for who spoke for each language group in talks.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American residents:** check *promotora* program reports for the questions families bring.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali residents:** check congregation and mosque practices for elders' roles in disputes.
- **Korean and Vietnamese residents:** check church and association records for whom families ask to settle a quarrel.
- **Amache generation:** check how Yasui's later city work is remembered in the Japanese American community.
- **Western Slope ranchers and towns:** check land-use collaboratives for who facilitates and how trust is built.
- **Front Range professionals:** check mediator directories for which languages are offered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor and professional mediation; mixed for acequia and village peacemaking (minutes exist but are scattered); thin for East African elders' mediation, mostly unwritten.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for acequia records; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for elders' practice; Italian and Slavic languages for coalfield papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, the 1879 events and Ludlow are contested; Native peacemaking may be restricted; court-connected mediation and water courts go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; national mediation caseload figures were weighed and left as background, since Colorado's own court program sits with Libra.
