# Colorado: Home (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; Valley and southern Colorado Spanish; Mexican Spanish; Núuchi-u; Volga German; Japanese; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali, Korean, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the place a person or group lives from and returns to, and what it means to them: a dwelling, a household's base, and the wider place called home.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check oral histories for who did the yearly mud replastering of adobe houses, and how the task was organized.
- **Mexican American Westside families (Auraria, La Alma-Lincoln Park):** check where displaced households went after the 1970s clearance, and whether they speak of a return.
- **Nuche [Ute] bands:** check how tribal publications name mountains and rivers as home, and how the post-1881 reservations are spoken of.
- **Japanese Americans:** compare Amache barrack homemaking with the Denver houses families bought or rented after release.
- **Pueblo steel and southern coalfield families (Slovenian, Italian, Mexican, Hispano):** check company-house tenancy against later home-buying in Bessemer.
- **Volga German beet families:** check house histories in Fort Collins, Greeley and Sterling for the move from beet shacks to owned houses.
- **Eastern Plains and North and South Park ranch families:** check homestead files for dugouts and sod houses, and the farmhouses left empty since.
- **Mountain-town residents (Aspen, Telluride, Silverton, Leadville):** check who holds deed-restricted units against second-home owners.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese households:** check tract-level tenure (owning or renting) before interviews.
- **Fire-affected households of Superior and Louisville:** check rebuild records for who returned.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for buildings and the Front Range; mixed for the Valley (much in Spanish oral sources); thin for East African households' own writing on home.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for adobe and hearth terms; Japanese for Issei letters on lost farms; Somali and Tigrinya for any community media on housing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Ute homeland and the 1881 removal are contested; place names belong to the tribes. Eviction, tenancy and property law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; the Montrose museum is named by description because its official title uses an older outside term.

## 5. First queries
1. History Colorado: "architectural field guide" and its house-type entries; then the state's historic-sites inventory for Costilla County adobe houses.
2. Fort Collins History Connection: "Buckingham" and "Andersonville" house histories.
3. Library: Clark, *Finding Solace in Soil*; Matthews, *Droppers*.
4. Maria Rogers Oral History Program: search "home", "fire" and "flood".
5. Steelworks Center of the West: ask for company-house rent records and *Camp and Plant* issues.
