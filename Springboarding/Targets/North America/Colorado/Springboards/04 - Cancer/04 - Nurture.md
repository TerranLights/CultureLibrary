# Colorado: Nurture (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; Valley Spanish; Mexican and Central American Spanish; Núuchi-u; German (Volga dialects); Japanese; Amharic, Somali, Korean, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** raising another toward growth, above all a child: feeding, warming, teaching and shaping them toward who they should become.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the Valley:** check Rael's tales and Valley oral histories for which counsels (*consejos*) are aimed at children, and who gives them.
- **Chicanos (Denver, Pueblo):** check Escuela Tlatelolco's surviving lesson materials against public-school ones of the same years.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American families:** check home-visiting enrollment in Spanish, and who in the family attends.
- **Nuche [Ute]:** check the Montessori Academy's language goals, and cradleboard-making classes as the tribe announces them.
- **Hinóno'éí [Arapaho] and Tsétsėhéstȧhese [Cheyenne]:** check which Colorado-held cradleboards and children's items the nations have commented on.
- **Volga German families:** check family memoirs against the 1916 report on children's beet work.
- **Black Coloradans:** check youth programs of Denver's oldest Black congregations, such as Zion Baptist (1865) *check*.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache camp school records and Denver Japanese-language school notices.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check heritage-language weekend schools and their stated aims.
- **Ranching families (Eastern Plains, North Park, Western Slope):** check 4-H project records by age.
- **Evangelical and military families of Colorado Springs:** check homeschool rates and child-development-center records on post.
- **Mountain-town families (Eagle County, Telluride):** check child-care waitlists against resort-work schedules.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for program data and Front Range surveys; mixed for Hispano oral material, rich in Spanish but scattered; thin for East African parents' own writing.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Rael and Valley counsel; German for beet-town memoirs; Japanese for language-school notices; Amharic, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese for heritage-school material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school history touches Nuche, Arapaho and Cheyenne nurture; teachings on raising children belong to the nations; cradleboards in museums may be subject to repatriation questions.
- **Held for review:** none; the records of Denver's early juvenile court, a natural source on upbringing gone wrong, were left to Libra and Capricorn.
