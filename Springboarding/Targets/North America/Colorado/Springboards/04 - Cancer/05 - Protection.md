## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check plaza-era defense accounts and Hermandad mutual-aid records against Nuche accounts of the same years.
- **Chicanos of Denver:** check movement-press accounts of community self-defense against police records of the same events.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American families:** check advocacy groups' family-preparedness guides for immigration raids (the legal side goes to Libra).
- **Nuche [Ute]:** check the tribes' own accounts of the 1879 White River events and of guarding the homeland.
- **Tsétsėhéstȧhese [Cheyenne] and Hinóno'éí [Arapaho]:** check Sand Creek testimony on the flags raised over the camp, as descendants retell it.
- **Volga German families:** check whether families dropped German in 1917-18 to shield themselves from suspicion.
- **Black Coloradans (Five Points):** check the *Denver Star* *check* in the Klan years for steps taken to protect homes and churches.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache guard-tower and "protection" wording in War Relocation Authority papers against memoirs.
- **Southern coalfield and steel families (Italian, Slovenian, Greek, Mexican):** check Ludlow survivors' accounts of the cellar beneath the tents.
- **Ranching families:** check stock-protection practice (blizzard feeding; predator control) in county histories.
- **Mountain-town residents:** check avalanche and rescue volunteers by town.
- **Military and evangelical households of Colorado Springs:** check church security teams and post family-readiness material.
- **Aurora's East African and Asian newcomers:** check community liaison programs with police and fire.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, rescue and child protection (Colorado-born field); mixed for household self-protection; thin for newcomers' own protective practice.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Hermandad and plaza records; Cheyenne terms through the nation's own dictionary; Japanese for camp-era papers; Somali and Amharic for any community safety material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ludlow, White River (1879) and Amache are contested; Columbine and other school shootings involve living families; domestic-violence records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** none; Colorado's home-defense statute is a pointer only for Libra's sheet, so it is not listed here.
