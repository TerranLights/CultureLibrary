## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range cities:** compare arts funding inside and outside the SCFD's Denver counties (Colorado Springs, Fort Collins, Pueblo).
- **Eastern Plains towns:** check the county fair, 4-H ring and Friday game as public stages.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check patron-saint feasts in San Luis and Antonito against the old *baile*.
- **Latter-day Saint towns (Manassa, Sanford):** check how July's Pioneer Days are staged.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check what each tribe makes public about its dances and fairs.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** governments now in Oklahoma, Wyoming and Montana; check powwow histories tied to Colorado land.
- **Mining against resort towns (Leadville, Silverton; Aspen, Telluride):** compare heritage days with luxury events.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese communities:** check which holidays are held in public halls.
- **Colorado Springs's evangelical and military communities:** check church and base event calendars.
- **Volga German beet families (Windsor, Sterling):** check wedding and church-fair records.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for outdoor recreation, Denver arts and the stock show; thin for Volga German, Somali and Eritrean leisure, where oral history is the route. The post-war nation's exact lands are still to be settled (the pre-war lands carried are listed only as the USA); the Leo sheets are written for the state as it stands, neighbors as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** both registers of Spanish to read festival records; Núuchi-u and Hinóno'éitíít for dance and game vocabularies; Amharic and Korean for community flyers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute ceremonies set their own rules on recording; *Hermandad* rites are private. Cannabis and psychedelics appear in leisure surveys; their legal side goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist institutions in the nation's lands (category only).
