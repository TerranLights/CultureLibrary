*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check *santero* and *colcha* oral histories for how makers describe copying a master against adding their own.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check tribal cultural-department guidance on who may make, sell or show particular designs.
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check mural registers for who commissioned, protected or painted over each wall.
- **Boulder and Golden's lab and startup workforce:** check whether local press calls inventors "creative" in the same words as artists.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points:** check jazz musicians' oral histories for how improvising was learned and credited.
- **Amache survivors and descendants:** check who holds camp-made objects now and how families tell their making.
- **Ranching families of the Plains and North Park:** check fair entries and saddle-shop ledgers for craft passed down in families.
- **Volga German families:** check embroidered and quilted pieces held by heritage societies.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check church icon painting and the tailoring of festival dress in local shops.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the arts economy and museum holdings; mixed for Hispano devotional craft; thin for Volga German and Eritrean making, where heritage societies and churches are the route.
- **Languages needed:** the Valley's Spanish for *santero* records and labels; Hinóno'éitíít and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse craft vocabularies, checked with speakers; Japanese for camp craft notes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Ute, Arapaho and Cheyenne designs and objects are not for public display or copying; museum returns have a legal side that goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the Boulder and Golden lab pointers were kept at the level of institutions, so no individual researchers had to be screened or set aside.
