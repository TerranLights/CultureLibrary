*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pueblo steelworking families:** check union newsletters and plant-closing coverage for how pride in the trade was voiced.
- **San Luis townspeople:** check how the town's claim to be the state's oldest appears on signs and in anniversaries.
- **Valley Hispanos and newer Mexican immigrants:** check which descent labels each uses in school heritage programs.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check royalty pageants and fair parades.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse descendants:** check accounts of the annual Sand Creek Spiritual Healing Run, where grief and pride meet.
- **Five Points:** check Juneteenth festival programs for which figures are honored.
- **Amache families:** check the camp memorial's inscriptions and family photo collections for service.
- **Colorado Springs military families:** check unit and base heritage displays.
- **Arkansas Valley growers:** check how the "Rocky Ford" melon name is defended as a place's product.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check national-day events and which flags each community flies.
- **Korean Aurora:** check Liberation Day (광복절) gatherings.
- **Volga German families:** check heritage-society newsletters for how field labor is framed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for civic, sports and movement pride; mixed for Hispano descent debates; thin for Eritrean and Volga German pride, where newsletters and oral history lead.
- **Languages needed:** *manito* Spanish to catch how descent labels shift by speaker; Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse for coup and bravery words; Tigrinya beside Amharic, since the two communities hold separate events.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pairing Amache with military service is contested among survivors; Ethiopian and Eritrean national pride can clash; "Spanish" descent claims are disputed in scholarship.
- **Held for review:** none; every group-pride source here comes from tribal, movement, veterans' or local archives, and none required screening under the standing rules.
