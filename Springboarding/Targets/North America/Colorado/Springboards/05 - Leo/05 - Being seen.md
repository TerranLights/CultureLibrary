*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern Plains counties:** check the 2013 ballot coverage for complaints of being overlooked.
- **Western Slope (Grand Junction, the North Fork):** check local reactions to how novelists, tourism ads and the state press portray them.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check tribal rules on photographing dances, and the state's missing-persons cases.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check plaza strolls and *mal de ojo* remedies in oral histories.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American arrivals:** check census-outreach records in Spanish-speaking neighborhoods.
- **Latter-day Saints of Manassa:** check how Jack Dempsey's fame put the town on the map and how the town keeps it there.
- **Five Points residents:** check how coverage of the neighborhood's change names, or omits, longtime residents.
- **Amache survivors:** check private photographs against official ones.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** check whom the 2019 curriculum review listed.
- **Ethiopian families:** check how the envious look is described in family interviews, with consent.
- **Somali families:** check whether school and census data count them apart from broader "Black" or "African" categories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, photography and census sources; mixed for rural grievance (opinion-heavy); thin for evil-eye beliefs, which are private and oral.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for *ojo* and plaza vocabularies; Amharic for the *buda* belief; Somali for outreach materials; Japanese for camp photo captions.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute removal and Amache are contested; missing-person cases involve living families; Ute dances may forbid cameras.
- **Held for review:** none; the outsiders' photographs (Jackson, the War Relocation Authority) were kept as pointers with a "read critically" note rather than set aside.
