*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Disabled Coloradans:** check activists' oral histories for how they framed the 1978 protest.
- **Coalfield families of Trinidad and Walsenburg (Hispano, Italian, Greek and Slavic miners):** check strike demands about company towns, scrip and guards.
- **Beet-field families (Volga German, Hispano, Mexican):** check Hine's captions against later family histories.
- **Unhoused Denverites:** compare street-paper writing with city statements.
- **Nuche bands:** check tribal histories on rations and removal.
- **Cheyenne and Arapaho descendants:** check how reburial and the dead of Sand Creek are addressed.
- **Valley Hispanos:** check wake and burial customs in the *Hermandad*'s records.
- **Amache survivors:** check hearing testimony for what they named as the deepest indignity.
- **Detained immigrants in Aurora:** check monitors' reports against detainees' accounts.
- **East African refugees:** check agency language against clients' own words.
- **Rural Plains elders:** check county health reports for how distance to care shapes late-life choices.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, disability and Sand Creek records; mixed for detention (access restricted); thin for East African experiences of resettlement, where interviews are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for wake customs; Japanese for redress-era memoirs; Tigrinya and Somali for refugees' own accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ludlow, Ute removal and Amache are contested; *Hermandad* records are private; detention and end-of-life sources involve living people.
- **Held for review:** none; the end-of-life and detention pointers were kept to public reports, with their legal sides routed to Libra or Capricorn rather than set aside.
