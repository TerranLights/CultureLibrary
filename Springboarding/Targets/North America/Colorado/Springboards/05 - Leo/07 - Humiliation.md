## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chinese Coloradans' descendants:** check family and Cantonese-language accounts of 1880 against the Denver dailies.
- **Hispanos and Mexican Americans:** check oral histories on the 1936 blockade and on school punishment for Spanish.
- **Nuche bands:** check Ute accounts of the White River agency's rules.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check how the display of the Sand Creek dead is told in tribal histories.
- **Catholic, Jewish and Black Coloradans of the 1920s:** check *The Denver Catholic Register* and *Intermountain Jewish News* for Klan-era targeting.
- **Volga German and Anglo Plains towns:** check shivaree accounts for who was singled out and why.
- **Japanese American families:** check how the questionnaire is told in families who answered differently.
- **Campus and military communities:** check hazing and assault inquiries for how complaints were received.
- **Jefferson County suburbs:** check anti-bullying programs adopted after 1999.
- **Somali, Ethiopian and Eritrean youth in Aurora schools:** check whether district bullying reports break out by language group.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press-recorded collective humiliations and youth-survey bullying; mixed for teasing customs; thin for East African youth experiences.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese-era Chinese sources for 1880; Japanese for questionnaire-era letters; Valley Spanish for proverbs and nicknames.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ute removal, Amache, the Klan era and Columbine involve living families and contested accounts; never let any account stand as the whole.
- **Held for review:** none; the Columbine and hazing pointers name only institutions and published accounts, and no victims are singled out.
