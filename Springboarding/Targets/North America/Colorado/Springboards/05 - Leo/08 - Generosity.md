## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ranching families of the Plains and North Park:** check how help at branding and haying is repaid, in kind or in time.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check *limpia* rolls and *matanza* accounts for who must come and who comes freely.
- **Latter-day Saint towns:** check storehouse records for who received help and on what terms.
- **Nuche, Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse families:** check giveaway announcements at Ignacio, Towaoc and Denver powwows.
- **Colorado Springs evangelicals:** check sponsorship and tithing language in church bulletins.
- **Mountain resort workers:** check local papers on tip dependence and housing.
- **Mexican and Central American households:** check hometown associations and remittance agents along Federal Boulevard.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check *iddir* and mosque aid for who is covered.
- **Vietnamese Coloradans:** check Tết gift customs and parish collections.
- **Five Points churches:** check benevolence funds and church histories.
- **Boulder County fire survivors:** check how donations reached renters and owners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foundations, giving days and disaster funds; mixed for church giving; thin for informal giving (remittances, *iddir*, *matanza*), which tax data miss.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for mutual-aid minutes; Amharic and Somali for association records; Hinóno'éitíít and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse for giveaway terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** giveaways belong to families and may not be recorded; church welfare records are restricted; aid recipients are private people.
- **Held for review:** one pointer set aside, category only: Buddhist congregations in Colorado.
