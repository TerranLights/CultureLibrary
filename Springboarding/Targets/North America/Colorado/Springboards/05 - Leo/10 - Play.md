## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mining-heritage towns (Leadville, Fairplay):** check race entry lists for locals against visitors.
- **Ski towns:** check employee handbooks and local papers on time off for fresh snow.
- **Eastern Plains rodeo families:** check age limits and helmet rules for children's events.
- **Valley Hispanos:** check WPA files for *chueco*, *cañute* and other games still played or lost.
- **Nuche:** check hand games and horse races at tribal fairs, as the tribes share them.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check Culin's entries against present tribal accounts.
- **Amache survivors:** check camp leagues and who played.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical youth:** check camp schedules for the place of games.
- **Front Range suburbs against Denver's core:** compare ParkScore access by neighborhood income.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian and Somali Aurora:** check New Year and Eid events for which games appear.
- **Pueblo's Italian families:** check bocce clubs and church picnics.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals, parks and time use; mixed for Native games (outsider catalogs, tribal sources to add); thin for East African games and Volga German pastimes.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for game names in WPA notes; Núuchi-u and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse for game terms Culin spelled his own way; Somali and Amharic for game names at events.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Native games carry ceremony and may not be recorded; children's rodeo events draw animal-welfare disputes.
- **Held for review:** none; every play festival and camp named here passed the standing rules without needing to be set aside.
