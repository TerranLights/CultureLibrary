# Colorado: Joy (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** the English of the mountains, the Plains and the cities; Chicano, *manito* and Mexican Spanish; Núuchi-u; Hinóno'éitíít; Volga German; Japanese; Korean; Vietnamese; Amharic and Tigrinya; Somali. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of gladness and delight, and the larger sense of being glad to be alive; how a culture names, feels, shows, seeks and shares it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check how each tribe describes the Bear Dance's place in its year.
- **Arkansas Valley and Western Slope growers:** check who began each harvest festival and why produce is given free.
- **Mexican and Chicano Denver:** check which of the two September and May holidays draws which families.
- **Valley Hispanos:** check *posada* routes and *luminaria* customs by parish.
- **Five Points:** check Juneteenth's history and the debates over moving or shrinking it.
- **Colorado Springs charismatic congregations:** check worship recordings for words of joy.
- **Ski and summit communities:** check how "bluebird" days appear in posts and local papers.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check each community's separate church calendar.
- **Korean, Vietnamese and Somali Aurora:** check festival programs over several years.
- **Volga German beet towns:** check wedding accounts for music and length.
- **Amache survivors:** check camp newspaper notices of weddings, births and New Year gatherings, and how later reunions recall moments of delight inside the fence.
- **Eastern Plains towns:** check homecoming and county-fair weeks as the year's high point.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals and well-being surveys; mixed for church joy; thin for Volga German and Somali celebration in Colorado. Suicide and depression data stand at the edge of this concept; handle with care.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for parish and festival programs; Núuchi-u for the Bear Dance; Amharic and Tigrinya for two separate church calendars; Somali for Eid notices.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Bear Dance and *Hermandad* rites carry rules on recording; Ethiopian and Eritrean festivals may be politically charged.
- **Held for review:** Buddhism, as a category (one pointer).
