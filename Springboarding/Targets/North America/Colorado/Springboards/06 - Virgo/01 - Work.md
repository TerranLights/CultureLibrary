*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hardrock towns (Leadville, Cripple Creek, Telluride, Silverton) against the southern coalfields (Trinidad, Walsenburg):** check each strike's demands for what a fair shift meant underground.
- **Pueblo's Slovene, Italian and Mexican steel families:** check CF&I personnel files for job ladders by origin.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos and potato growers:** check how village family farming and commercial potato labor coexist in local histories.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check how the tribes' own enterprise and employment reports describe tribal work today.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse descendants (now outside the state):** check their oral histories of the buffalo economy in Colorado, held by their present nations.
- **Volga German and Mexican beet families of the South Platte:** check contracts for who signed for the family's labor.
- **Somali, Ethiopian, Eritrean and newer Central American workers:** check ACS occupation by birthplace and meatpacking-town news, each group separately.
- **Ranch families of North Park, South Park and the Plains:** check day-work and neighbor branding crews in ranch memoirs.
- **Ski and mountain towns:** check employee-housing studies for who does the seasonal work.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check family-business labor in small-business data.
- **Wyoming and Utah coal and ranch country:** comparison only, for strike and ranch-labor parallels across the line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mining, steel and beets; mixed for ranch and ski labor; thin for Native work as the nations frame it, and for East African and Central American workers.
- **Languages needed:** *manito* Spanish for folktales and village records; Japanese for prewar papers; Volga German for church and family records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Meeker affair, Ludlow and the 2006 Greeley raid are contested ground; workers' immigration status is sensitive in any oral history.
- **Held for review:** none; the pointers considered were all from labor, folklore and government sources, and none fell under a standing rule.
