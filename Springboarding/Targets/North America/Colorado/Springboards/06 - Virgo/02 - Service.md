## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Colorado Springs military households and evangelical ministries:** check whether base newspapers and ministry newsletters use "service" for the same acts.
- **Hermandad villages of the San Luis Valley and Huerfano and Las Animas counties:** check chapter rules for who keeps watch at a wake and who cooks.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check the published order of honoring veterans and elders at tribal gatherings.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Nisei veterans' memoirs against draft-resister accounts from the camps.
- **Black Coloradans (Five Points, Central City):** check women's club and lodge minutes for whom they served and why.
- **Mountain and ski towns:** check volunteer fire, patrol and rescue rosters for full-time residents against seasonal ones.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean communities:** check whether *idir* and parish associations share their bylaws with outsiders.
- **Somali Coloradans (Aurora, Greeley, Fort Morgan):** check mosque and community-center volunteer programs.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check church and community-association volunteer rosters.
- **Latter-day Saints of the San Luis Valley:** check mission farewells and returns in local papers.
- **Plains farm towns:** check grange and volunteer fire department records.
- **Chicano Denver:** check Escuela Tlatelolco and Crusade for Justice service programs.
- **Northern New Mexico's brotherhood chapters and Utah's church welfare system:** comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military, ministry and outdoor volunteering; mixed for Hispano mutual aid; thin for East African associations and Native service roles, which communities may keep internal.
- **Languages needed:** *manito* Spanish for brotherhood and mutualista records; Amharic and Tigrinya for association bylaws; Japanese for Issei views of Nisei service.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Hermandad rites are private; Sand Creek and Amache are contested ground; domestic workers' status is sensitive.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist communities, as a category.

## 5. First queries
1. Search the Colorado Historic Newspapers Collection for "Protección Mutua" and "Antonito" for lodge notices.
2. Find the Soule and Cramer letters and the 1865 testimony; list who testified and from which side.
3. Search AmeriCorps' volunteering data for Colorado state and Denver metro tables.
4. Ask the National Ski Patrol and Alpine Rescue Team for their published histories.
5. Search Weigle's bibliography for chapter rule books held in Colorado archives.
