## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Plains, North Park and South Park ranch families:** check ranch memoirs for which tasks went to each age, from small child to grandparent.
- **Western Slope orchard families (Palisade, the North Fork):** check succession and land-sale records for where the older grower's role goes.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check acequia minutes for elders who advise after they stop doing ditch work.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check elder and language programs for the roles the tribes give elders.
- **Hinóno'éí descendants:** check Kroeber's age-grade account against their own present accounts.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Densho interviews for how fathers and sons spoke of providing in camp.
- **Refugee families from Ethiopia, Eritrea, Somalia and Vietnam:** check school and clinic interpreter programs for children who interpret for parents.
- **Korean Aurora:** check family-business households for grandparents' child-care roles.
- **Disabled Coloradans:** check Atlantis and ADAPT writing for their own terms against "productive".
- **Leadville and other mining towns:** check post-closure newspapers for retraining and departure.
- **Colorado Springs military retirees:** check second-career programs at Fort Carson and Peterson.
- **Neighboring ranch states:** comparison only; their ranch-family accounts may show where help ended and child labor began elsewhere.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water talk, disability rights and 4-H; mixed for elders' roles; thin for how Native nations and East African families frame usefulness in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** *manito* Spanish for *dichos*; Japanese for Issei writing; Amharic and Somali through community partners.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** end-of-life reports and any suicide data need great care; Ute removal is contested ground; Native plant knowledge may be restricted.
- **Held for review:** nothing; the only borderline pointer, a national work-values dataset, was dropped for having no Colorado slice rather than under a rule.
