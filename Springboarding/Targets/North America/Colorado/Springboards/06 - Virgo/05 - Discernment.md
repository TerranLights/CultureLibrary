*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Backcountry skiers and mountain-town residents against visiting skiers:** check CAIC accident write-ups for how decisions are described by party.
- **Plains, North Park and South Park ranch families:** check brand-book and stock-show records for who is trusted to judge animals.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check oral histories for *consejos* on judging a stranger or a suitor.
- **Latter-day Saints of Manassa and Sanford:** check published ward histories for how members describe seeking guidance before choices.
- **Colorado Springs evangelicals and Front Range Catholics:** check how seminary and retreat programs each teach discerning a call.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute, Northern Ute history):** check Duke interviews on how leaders weighed treaty promises.
- **Japanese Americans:** check picture-bride accounts for who vetted matches.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean communities:** check whether *shimagile* still vet marriages in Colorado.
- **Mexican, Central American and Somali newcomers:** check consumer-fraud warnings issued in their languages.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check Aurora police and community-group scam alerts in Korean and Vietnamese.
- **Hunters across the state:** check CPW misidentification cases by region.
- **Wyoming's official "Code of the West" (2010, *check*):** comparison only, for how a neighbor wrote ranch judgment into a code.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for avalanche, livestock and taste institutions; mixed for spiritual discernment; thin for how Native, East African and newer immigrant communities judge people in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for picture-bride letters; Amharic and Tigrinya for elder mediation; *manito* Spanish for *consejos*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** treaty-era accounts are contested ground; religious guidance practices are private; scam victims underreport.
- **Held for review:** two categories: Buddhist communities; South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

1. Read a season of CAIC accident reports and note the words used for decisions.

4. Search the Colorado Historic Newspapers Collection for "tenderfoot" and "humbug", 1859-1900.
5. Pull the latest Great American Beer Festival judging guidelines and National Western judging rules.
