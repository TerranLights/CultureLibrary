## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range newsrooms and their owners:** check staff statements and ownership changes since 2009 for how criticism flowed upward.
- **Aspen, Telluride and other ski towns:** check council public-comment records on growth and housing.
- **Western Slope and North Park ranchers:** check comment letters to the BLM and Forest Service for tone and target.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check Land Rights Council statements against landowners' and courts' accounts.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check tribal statements on place names and the Capitol's monuments.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse nations:** check their written submissions on Mount Blue Sky and on Sand Creek sites.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Omura's and Carr's records against camp leaders' who counseled compliance.
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check 1969 walkout statements and later school-board testimony.
- **Colorado Springs military:** check how after-action reviews handle criticism of senior officers.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean communities:** check diaspora media for criticism of home governments, a sensitive subject.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check community-paper opinion pages for criticism of city services.
- **Somali Coloradans:** check how community leaders answered workplace and press criticism after the 2008 meatpacking prayer dispute.
- **Utah and Wyoming press:** comparison only, for satire and public-land criticism across the state line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, satire and political dissent; mixed for family and workplace criticism; thin for criticism inside Native and East African communities as they would describe it.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for *Rocky Shimpo*'s Japanese pages; Amharic and Tigrinya for diaspora media; *manito* Spanish for village satire and *mitote*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ludlow, Amache and La Sierra are contested ground; diaspora dissent can endanger relatives abroad.
- **Held for review:** none; campus-controversy pointers were weighed and dropped as off-concept, not under any rule.
