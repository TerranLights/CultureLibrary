## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Front Range cities against the Eastern Plains and San Luis Valley:** check access-survey regions for distance and cost barriers.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check oral histories for *médicas* and *parteras* and when clinics replaced them.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check tribal health reports for priorities the tribes name themselves.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí histories:** check Bent's letters on epidemics against each nation's own memory.
- **Jewish Denver (West Colfax):** check JCRS patient letters for how illness and charity were spoken of.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Five Points clinic histories after Justina Ford.
- **Western Slope uranium towns (Uravan, Naturita, Grand Junction):** check oral histories of mill workers' families.
- **Rocky Flats neighbors (Arvada, Westminster):** check community surveys against agency studies.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox families:** check how parishes share or keep private holy-water healing.
- **Somali Coloradans:** check community health worker reports in Aurora and Greeley.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check APDC program reports for how families approach mental health care.
- **Latter-day Saints of the San Luis Valley:** check ward histories for Word of Wisdom teaching.
- **Mountain resort counties and Colorado Springs military families:** check behavioral-health service reports for each.
- **Neighboring Mountain West and Diné uranium history:** comparison only; check mill-town health studies from Uravan, Naturita and Grand Junction against the Diné record across the line, to see which illness accounts are Colorado's own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the TB era, survey data and environmental-health disputes; mixed for Hispano folk healing; thin and restricted for Native healing, which belongs to the nations.
- **Languages needed:** Yiddish for JCRS letters; *manito* Spanish for remedy names; Amharic and Tigrinya for parish practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Native ceremonies and healing are not for outside research unless shared; uranium, Rocky Flats and mental health data need care.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist institutions; South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (both as categories).
