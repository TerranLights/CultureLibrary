# Colorado: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Eastern Plains homesteading families (Baca, Kiowa, Kit Carson counties):** check final-proof files for what each swore as improvement, and Dust Bowl departures.
- **Western Slope irrigators (Uncompahgre Valley, Grand Valley):** check irrigation-district histories for who benefited from Reclamation water.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check grant-era settlement terms against later landowners' "improvements" on La Sierra.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute; Northern Ute removal):** check language-program materials for their own words for renewal.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse nations:** check their testimony in the boarding-school reports.
- **Denver Chicanos of Auraria and the Westside:** check displacement oral histories against city plans.
- **Five Points Black residents:** check how renewal in the 1970s and later gentrification are told by long-time families.
- **Southern coalfield immigrant families (Italian, Greek, Slovene, Mexican):** check Rockefeller Plan records against miners' memoirs.
- **Japanese Americans:** check WRA class records against residents' diaries.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali refugees:** check Community College of Aurora credential programs for who enrolls.
- **Korean and Vietnamese merchants:** check business-improvement district minutes for their voice.
- **Latter-day Saint towns of the San Luis Valley:** check local church histories for how town-building is framed.
- **Utah's irrigation colonies and New Mexico's grant disputes:** comparison only, as parallels, not units.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water projects, homesteading and urban renewal; mixed for self-improvement movements; thin for how East African and Asian communities frame betterment in their own words.
- **Languages needed:** *manito* Spanish for grant records; Japanese for camp diaries; the Native languages through tribal programs only.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding schools, the Meeker affair, Ludlow, La Sierra and Auraria are contested ground; boarding-school testimony is held by the nations.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; self-help authors were weighed and left out as national rather than Colorado sources.
