## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Acequia *parciantes* and Front Range ditch shareholders:** check how each divides a dry year, under its own rule.
- **Eastern Plains and mountain school districts:** read their *Lobato* testimony for what they called uneven.
- **Mexican and Volga German beet families:** compare their contract terms in the sugar-company files.
- **Chicano and Black families in Denver:** check the *Keyes* trial record for each group's complaints.
- **Evangelical Colorado Springs and LGBTQ Coloradans:** read the briefs on both sides of the two Supreme Court cases for each side's account of fair treatment.
- **Nuche:** read how the Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute codes set per-capita shares of tribal revenue.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora businesses:** check whether wage-theft and consumer complaints are filed in those languages.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali savings circles:** ask how members fix the order of payout.
- **Resident and nonresident hunters; ranch families:** check the license quotas and landowner vouchers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court and agency records; thin for Native and newer-immigrant rules of sharing.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for acequia bylaws; German for beet-family records; Korean, Vietnamese, Amharic and Somali for complaint and circle practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the two antidiscrimination cases are live political ground; present both sides' filings. Tribal revenue rules may be internal.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; experimental fairness-game studies were considered, none known to be run in Colorado, so the national survey item stands in.
