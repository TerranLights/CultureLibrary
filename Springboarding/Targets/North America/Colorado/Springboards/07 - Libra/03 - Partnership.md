## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche:** check what the Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute cultural departments share about the Bear Dance's pairing, and what they keep closed.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** read Cheyenne family histories that answer George Bent's letters on trade marriages.
- **Hispanos of the Valley:** check church books for *padrinos de casamiento* and who sang the *entriega*.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** listen to Spanish-language call-in shows for how *pareja* and *unión libre* are discussed.
- **Japanese Americans:** check camp wedding notices for named go-betweens.
- **Volga German families:** read family files for farms run jointly by brothers or in-laws.
- **Latter-day Saint Valley towns:** compare sealing records with county marriage records.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Five Points business directories for partnerships between families.
- **Ranch families:** look at family-ranch LLC filings and easements with land trusts.
- **Military Colorado Springs:** read family-readiness materials on spouses' roles during deployment.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check how family business partnerships are registered.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law and census; mixed for church and camp records; thin for Native partnership customs.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for *entriega* texts; Japanese for Issei marriage papers; German for colony records; Somali and Korean for business-partnership practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Bear Dance details may be closed; the 1975 licenses touch living people; the CF&I plan sits inside the Ludlow aftermath, to be read as contested accounts.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; Aurora mosque and church marriage registries were weighed and left as a source type, since no single registry is known to the scout.
