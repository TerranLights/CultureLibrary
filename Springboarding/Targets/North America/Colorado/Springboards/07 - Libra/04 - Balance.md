## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Western Slope (Grand Junction, the North Fork) and the Front Range:** check how each side words compensation when water crosses the Divide.
- **San Luis Valley farmers and acequia users:** look at subdistrict fees and well-curtailment rules for who carries the cost of recovery.
- **Eastern Plains counties:** read Legislative Council analyses of how Gallagher shifted property-tax shares between homes and farms.
- **Ranch families and Front Range voters:** compare precinct returns on wolves with the livestock-compensation rules.
- **Nuche:** read the seasonal-round material each tribe chooses to publish, Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute separately.
- **Hispanos and Mexican families:** compare Valley healers' hot-and-cold remedies with Denver *yerberías*.
- **Ski-town workers:** check housing lotteries and seasonal-worker surveys for the split of work and rest.
- **Evangelical Colorado Springs:** read Focus on the Family's published guidance on work and family time.
- **Military families:** check deployment-cycle programs at Fort Carson on home and duty.
- **Korean, Vietnamese and Ethiopian business owners:** look for community surveys of owners' hours (likely thin).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water, budget and election records; mixed for health; thin for Native and immigrant teachings on balance.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for *remedios*; Ute only through the tribes; Korean, Vietnamese and Amharic for owners' time-use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute teachings on land and season may be restricted; wolf reintroduction is a live rural-urban fight, to be shown from both sides.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist and Buddhist-inspired contemplative institutions in the nation's lands (category).
