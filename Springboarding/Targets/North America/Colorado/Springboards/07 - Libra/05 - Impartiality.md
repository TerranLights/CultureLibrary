## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small mountain and Plains counties:** check recusal motions and performance reviews where a single county judge knows most parties.
- **Hispano acequias:** read how bylaws bar the *mayordomo* from favoring kin in water turns.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** compare Stands In Timber's account of chiefs' conduct with the outsiders' casebook.
- **Nuche:** check how the Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute constitutions separate the tribal court from the council.
- **Japanese Americans of Amache:** read internees' letters on how block managers handled requests.
- **Military Colorado Springs:** check honor-board outcomes reported in the Academy's own reviews.
- **Rodeo and ranch families:** read stock-show rules on judges' ties to exhibitors.
- **Western Slope election offices:** follow the Mesa County clerk prosecution (2022-24) as contested accounts of a breach of election-office neutrality.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Somali and Amharic speakers:** check how court interpreters from small communities are screened for conflicts (Judicial Branch language-access office).
- **Latter-day Saint Valley towns:** check how bishops' councils handle members related to the bishop.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for judges, ethics and elections; mixed for camp and acequia records; thin for Native and newer-immigrant practice.
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for bylaws; Cheyenne terms only through Cheyenne programs; Japanese for internee letters; Korean, Vietnamese, Amharic and Somali for interpreter practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Mesa County case and judicial-discipline scandals involve living people; give each side's account. Honor-board files are partly private.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; general studies of judges' bias were weighed, but none set in Colorado is known to the scout, so the state's own evaluations stand in.
