*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche:** set Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute tellings of the 1873 talks beside Brunot's report, and ask how each tribe framed the 2008 agreement.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check their own histories of the 1840 peace and of Camp Weld.
- **Hispano heirs of the Sangre de Cristo grant:** follow the access arrangements negotiated after the 2002 ruling.
- **Mexican and Central American families:** check school and clinic studies of children who interpret for parents.
- **Japanese Americans:** read Mile-Hi JACL records on wartime dealings with officials.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check the diaspora's part in the Denver-Axum sister-city tie.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** look at the city's immigrant-affairs liaison with business associations.
- **Colorado River negotiators:** read the Upper Colorado River Commission's records for the state's stance in the latest round.
- **Military Colorado Springs:** check NORAD's history office on how a joint U.S.-Canadian command settles differences.
- **Organized labor and brewers:** read accounts of the Coors boycott (1977-87) and its settlement from both sides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaties, compacts and state offices; mixed for consular and sister-city records; thin for everyday tact in immigrant communities.
- **Languages needed:** Ute, Cheyenne and Arapaho only through the tribes; Spanish for consular material; Japanese for JACL-era letters; Amharic and Somali for association records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Camp Weld and the Ute cessions are contested; give each account. Tribal consultation files may be confidential.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; Korbel School faculty publications were weighed and kept only as an institutional pointer, without naming faculty.
