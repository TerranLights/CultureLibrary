## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the Valley:** compare what the mutual-aid lodges wrote down with the unwritten ditch duties.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** check how *tanda* members handle a member who stops paying.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** read *idir* bylaws for who counts as a member's family when a death occurs.
- **Somali Aurora:** ask how *ayuuto* groups square their practice with religious rules on interest.
- **Korean Aurora:** check how church networks and *gye* overlap.
- **Vietnamese Denver (Federal Boulevard):** look for court cases filed when a *hụi* collapsed.
- **Japanese Americans:** check family papers for *kōden* registers and the return gifts sent.
- **Hinóno'éí, Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Nuche at urban powwows:** ask how host families record and later repay a giveaway, as they choose to share.
- **Ranch families of the parks and Plains:** gather oral histories of branding days and who owes labor to whom.
- **Latter-day Saint Valley towns:** check ward histories for fast offerings and welfare given and received.
- **Mountain towns:** compare search-and-rescue volunteering with the fees outdoor users pay.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law and co-op records; mixed for mutual-aid lodges; thin for rotating savings circles and Native giveaways (likely oral).
- **Languages needed:** Valley Spanish for lodge papers; Japanese for kōden lists; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese for circle practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** giveaways and honor songs may be closed to recording; savings circles involve private money and immigration status.
- **Held for review:** on the facet of gifts to religious bodies: Buddhist congregations in the nation's lands (category only).
