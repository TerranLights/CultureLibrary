## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Backcountry skiers and climbers (Silverton, Aspen, the Front Range peaks):** check accident reports for who in a party held the final say.
- **Japanese Americans of Amache:** read registration files for those who left questions blank or answered with conditions.
- **Hispano grant heirs of the Valley:** gather petitions and letters written while claims sat pending.
- **Nuche:** set agents' complaints of delay beside Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute accounts of how councils reach agreement.
- **Eastern Plains farm families:** compare crop-insurance deadlines with the timing advice they follow.
- **Unaffiliated voters:** check ballot-return timing and roll-off by county.
- **Foothill households in the wildland-urban edge:** read fire reviews for how evacuation calls were made.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese families:** ask community health programs who is named to decide for an elder (likely thin).
- **Evangelical Colorado Springs:** look at sermon archives on "discerning God's will" before a major choice.
- **Denver voters of 1972:** compare precinct returns on the Olympics with the campaign pamphlets.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mountain accident reports, elections and water courts; mixed for camp files; thin for family decision-making among newer arrivals.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for Issei letters on the questionnaire; Valley Spanish for heirs' petitions; Amharic, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese for family practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the loyalty questionnaire divided families and remains painful; avalanche reports concern deaths; handle both with care.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; divination as a way to settle a choice was weighed, and no Colorado-specific pointer was found worth holding.
