*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** compare oral accounts of peace-seeking chiefs with accounts of the Dog Soldier bands who refused the same terms.
- **Nuche:** set the Uncompahgre leaders' agreements beside White River accounts of 1879, from both sides.
- **Japanese Americans of Amache:** compare cooperation with the camp authorities against the resisters' trials and families.
- **Hispano and Mexican families:** ask Spanish-language advocates how "dirty laundry" sayings affect reporting of harm at home (sensitive).
- **Coal-camp families of the southern fields and Weld County:** read union and company records from 1915 to 1927 for acceptance and refusal.
- **Catholic parishes:** compare dioceses' statements and survivors' groups on the years of silence.
- **Suburban HOA residents:** check which complaints reach the state office and which are dropped.
- **Military and evangelical Colorado Springs:** set the objectors' camp beside the bases, and check local press of the 1940s.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean associations:** check written rules on keeping homeland politics out of community events, and their enforcement.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora businesses:** ask police outreach programs whether crimes against small shops go unreported.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for law, abuse inquiries and Amache; mixed for labor; thin for proverbs in Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali, and for unreported harm.
- **Languages needed:** Cheyenne for oral-history terms (through the tribes); Japanese for *Rocky Shimpo*; Valley Spanish for proverbs; Korean and Vietnamese for business outreach.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Sand Creek oral histories, the 1879 crisis, the Amache split and the clergy-abuse years are contested and painful; give each account. Domestic-violence data involve survivors' safety.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the Hermandad's teaching on penance and endurance was weighed and left out, as it belongs to another concept.
