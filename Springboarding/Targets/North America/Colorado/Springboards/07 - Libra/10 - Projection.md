*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche:** check how Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute histories answer the 1879 press images, each in its own words.
- **Hinóno'éí:** read how the Arapaho language project explains the spider-word for outsiders: projection running the other way.
- **Chinese Denver:** compare the riot press with Chinese merchants' damage claims sent through diplomatic channels.
- **Catholic, Jewish, Black and Mexican Coloradans of the 1920s:** check Klan literature for how each group was cast, and each group's own press in reply.
- **Japanese Americans:** compare the 1943 coverage with the 1944 county returns on the land amendment.
- **Hispano brotherhoods:** set outsiders' Holy Week stories against the brotherhood's published rules.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** ask clergy and community workers how *buda* accusations are handled (sensitive).
- **Newcomers and longtime residents:** check "Native" bumper-sticker and anti-California talk against state migration data.
- **Boulder and Colorado Springs:** trace the nicknames each city gives the other in local press.
- **Littleton families after 1999:** compare early blame with later findings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for newspapers and the 1920s Klan; mixed for Amache press; thin for evil-eye beliefs among newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** Arapaho and Ute through the tribes; Japanese for *Rocky Shimpo* replies; Chinese for merchants' claims; Amharic for *buda*.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Klan rolls name families still living in Colorado; Columbine involves victims' families; the Ute removal, Amache and Amendment 2 are contested: give each account.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; psychoanalytic training institutes in Denver were weighed for the clinical sense of projection, but none is known to the scout well enough to name.
