## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check how Holy Week penance and parish confession sit side by side in each village.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** compare who uses *limpias* across first and later generations.
- **Nuche (Ute):** check which cleansing practices, such as the sweat lodge, the tribes describe in their own media.
- **Chinese Coloradans' descendants:** check how they frame the 1880 riot and any recent city acknowledgment.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox Aurora:** check how long fasting seasons shape restaurant menus and family meals.
- **Somali Aurora:** check how Ramadan is kept in workplaces such as meatpacking plants.
- **Latter-day Saints of the San Luis Valley:** check what church discipline records exist and who may see them.
- **Uranium families of the Western Slope:** check how former Uravan residents speak of the town's razing.
- **Leadville and Silverton:** compare pride in mining with the label of a cleanup site.
- **Unhoused Denver:** check the sweeps from their own testimony.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cleanups, prohibition and renamings; mixed for folk cleansing; thin for Somali and Eritrean practice.
- **Languages needed:** Mexican Spanish for *limpia* and folk-illness terms; Amharic and Tigrinya for fasting seasons; Somali for Ramadan in workplaces.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute cleansing ceremonies belong to the tribes; Penitente penance is kept within the brotherhood; eating disorders and detox are health matters.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside: the cleansing-rite pointers were all Hispano, Mexican, Orthodox or tribal, and none fell under the standing rules; school-discipline statutes go to Libra.
