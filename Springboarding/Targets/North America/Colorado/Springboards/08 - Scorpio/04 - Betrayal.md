# Colorado: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; Chicano, Mexican and *manito* Spanish; Japanese and the English of the camp generation; Greek and Italian for coalfield records; Korean; Vietnamese; Amharic; Tigrinya; Somali. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check how tribal statements today describe the flag flown over the camp and the promise from Fort Lyon.
- **Nuche (Ute):** compare White River, Uncompahgre and Southern Ute accounts of who agreed to what in the treaty years.
- **Hispanos of Costilla County:** compare heirs who stayed in the fight for La Sierra with families who sold or left.
- **Japanese Americans:** check how "no-no" families and veterans' families spoke of each other after the war.
- **Greek, Italian and Mexican coal families of Las Animas and Huerfano counties:** check union records for who was named a strikebreaker.
- **Evangelical Colorado Springs:** check how congregations wrote about restoring a fallen pastor.
- **Military families:** check how discharge disputes are told in veterans' groups.
- **Arkansas Valley farm towns:** check local papers for the words used about neighbors who sold water rights.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check community papers for how business-partner disputes are reported.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sand Creek, Ludlow and the scandals; mixed for land-grant memory; thin for infidelity outside court and survey data, and for Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali accounts.
- **Languages needed:** regional Spanish for grant heirs' testimony; Greek and Italian for coalfield union papers; Japanese for some camp letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek and Ute removal are living grief; assault survivors' identities in the Academy records must stay protected; Eritrean diaspora politics can make loyalty questions dangerous to ask.
- **Held for review:** no pointer held; the national infidelity surveys were weighed and left out because they carry no Colorado breakdown, not under any rule; treaty, grant and court texts are left to Libra.
