## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Acequia communities of the San Luis Valley:** check how disputes among *parciantes* are settled and recorded.
- **Front Range suburbs:** compare HOA complaint counts and covenant fights across counties.
- **Ranching families of North Park, Grand County and the Western Slope:** check livestock-loss and compensation claims for how ranchers speak of losing control of their herds.
- **Nuche (Ute Mountain Ute and Southern Ute):** check how each tribe sets access rules for its sites, and who explains them.
- **Japanese Americans:** compare *shikata ga nai* with petitions and protests written inside Amache.
- **Evangelical Colorado Springs:** check which parenting materials congregations actually use, and whether they changed.
- **Pueblo steel families:** check oral histories of life under the company's welfare programs.
- **Fremont County:** check how prison workers and their families describe the work.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check what community bodies govern money and disputes, and who sits on them.
- **Korean and Vietnamese families:** check which studies on parenting exist for Colorado at all before relying on national ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water, wildlife, prisons and land; mixed for family control; thin for immigrant households.
- **Languages needed:** acequia Spanish for ditch minutes; Amharic and Somali for community bodies; Japanese for camp-era documents.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** coercive-control accounts involve victims' safety; Ute site access is the tribes' to set; HOA complaint files can expose private addresses.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; a national parenting-style dataset was considered and dropped for its lack of Colorado detail, not under a rule. Prison and water law go to Libra and Capricorn.
