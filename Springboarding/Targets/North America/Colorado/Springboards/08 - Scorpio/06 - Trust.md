## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ranching families:** check whether brand inspection and the auction ring still carry trust as land passes to outside owners.
- **Hispanos of Conejos and Costilla counties:** check how godparents were chosen across villages and generations.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American arrivals:** check which banks, credit unions and senders they use, and whether that varies by status.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check how *equb* rules handle a member who stops paying.
- **Somali Aurora:** check whether savings circles also meet a wish to avoid interest.
- **Korean Aurora:** check whether *gye* run inside or outside church networks.
- **Vietnamese Denver:** check community papers for *hụi* that collapsed, and how members spoke of it.
- **Black Coloradans:** compare medical trust in Five Points with what health-system surveys show.
- **Nuche (Ute):** check how the tribes themselves report on agreements with the state.
- **Mesa County and the Western Slope:** compare trust in local election officials before and after 2021.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elections, credit unions and health data; mixed for ranch dealing; thin for savings circles, which leave few papers.
- **Languages needed:** Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for savings circles; Korean and Vietnamese for community papers; regional Spanish for parish registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** immigration status shapes whom people trust and what they will say; election-trust questions are politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none of the savings-circle, ranch or election pointers called for holding; trust in police and courts is passed on to Capricorn rather than held here.
