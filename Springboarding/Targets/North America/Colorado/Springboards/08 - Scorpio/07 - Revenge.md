# Colorado: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of Conejos and Costilla counties:** check whether descendants' tellings of the Espinosas differ from the printed histories.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse (Cheyenne):** check Cheyenne-authored histories for how the 1865 raids are framed.
- **Nuche, White River descendants now in Utah:** compare their account of 1879 with the one kept in Meeker.
- **Western Slope ranch families:** check family papers for range "deadlines" and who crossed them.
- **Grand County residents:** check how the town that was damaged speaks of the online praise of its attacker.
- **Greek, Italian, Mexican and Slavic coal families:** check union and church memory of the armed response after Ludlow.
- **Chicano, union and LGBTQ activists of the Coors boycott:** check what each group said it was answering.
- **Somali elders in Aurora:** check, only from community voices, whether customary compensation is used and when.
- **Denver neighborhoods served by gang intervention:** check the workers' own records of retaliations stopped or not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the frontier and labor eras; mixed for range feuds; thin for present-day private revenge, which leaves few records.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for folktales and Hispano memory; Somali and Tigrinya for customary compensation; Greek and Italian for coalfield community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek and 1879 are contested between peoples; the Granby attacker's online cult is live; gang data can expose individuals. Private vengeance against state punishment goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a revenge-motive account of a school attack was weighed and left for the Crisis sheet, where the grievance narrative can be handled with its own care; nothing held under a rule.
