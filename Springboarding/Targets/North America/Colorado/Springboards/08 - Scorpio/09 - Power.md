## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of Las Animas, Costilla and Conejos counties:** check *partido* sheep contracts and county offices for patron power in the early 1900s.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo:** compare movement accounts with what surveillance files later showed.
- **Black Denver:** check how Five Points organizations built the 1991 coalition.
- **Nuche:** compare Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute economic reports for how each tribe frames its own reach.
- **Western Slope water users:** check the River District's own histories of the diversion fights.
- **Eastern Plains counties:** check which grievances the 2013 campaigns put first.
- **Aspen and the ski towns:** compare the 1970 race with later contests over second-home wealth and local control.
- **Greek, Italian and Slavic mine locals:** check membership rolls for who led, and in which language.
- **Colorado Springs:** check city records for how military bases and national ministries enter local decisions.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check Aurora City Council and school-board races for newer communities' candidates.
- **Korean and Vietnamese business owners:** check business-association minutes for who speaks for each community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Denver politics, labor and water; mixed for Hispano patronage; thin for newer communities' power, which is recent.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Barela-era and movement papers; Amharic and Somali for local campaign media; Korean and Vietnamese for business groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** surveillance files name living people; tribal finances are the tribes' to disclose. Legitimate authority and hierarchy are Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; Barela's biography was kept despite its age, since its Spanish-language view is not found elsewhere.
