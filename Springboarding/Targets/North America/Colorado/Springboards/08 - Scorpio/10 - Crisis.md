*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain and ski towns:** compare how fire evacuation and the housing shortage are each described as a crisis in council minutes.
- **Eastern Plains families of Baca and Prowers counties:** check whether Dust Bowl memory appears in talk about present drought.
- **Pueblo steel families:** check oral histories for who left, who stayed and what replaced the mill.
- **Western Slope towns:** compare the 1982 oil-shale bust with the closing of coal mines and power plants in the 2020s.
- **Southern Ute Tribe:** check its own statements on the Gold King spill; the Diné side is comparison only.
- **Japanese Americans:** check how *gaman* is used in Amache memorial materials and by whom.
- **Front Range suburbs:** check how the Columbine generation speaks of school drills as parents now.
- **LGBTQ Colorado Springs:** check community records of the response after 2022.
- **Hispano acequia villages:** check minutes from dry years for how shortage was shared.
- **Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali refugees:** check whether crisis is told as rupture or as the opening of a new chapter.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, epidemics and economic busts; mixed for personal crisis; thin for refugee and Somali accounts in Colorado, which need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese, Amharic and Somali for refugee testimony; Spanish for acequia minutes; Japanese for Amache-era writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mass-shooting survivors and families; suicide and overdose data by small area can identify people.
- **Held for review:** the school-attack grievance narrative passed on from the Revenge sheet is handled here only through the official review and one narrative history; no attacker writings are pointed to.
