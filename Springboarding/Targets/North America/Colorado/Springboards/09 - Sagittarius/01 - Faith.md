# Colorado: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; *manito* Spanish; Mexican and Central American Spanish; Núuchi-u, Hinóno'éitíít, Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse; Volga German; Japanese; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali, Korean, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute beside Ute Mountain Ute:** check which ceremonies each tribe announces publicly and which it keeps closed, as a measure of how faith is shared or withheld.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check *promesa* practice: what is vowed, to which saint, and how it is paid (a walk, a candle, a feast).
- **Newer Mexican and Central American Catholics (Denver, Aurora, Greeley):** check whether they join the old Hispano parishes or form separate devotional groups (Guadalupe, Salvadoran patron feasts).
- **Colorado Springs evangelicals and military families:** check how prayer is organized around deployment (base chapels, prayer chains).
- **Japanese Americans of the Amache generation:** check the Christian side of faith in the camp; the other side is held below.
- **Volga German families:** check whether the Russian-era parish split (Lutheran, Reformed, Catholic) survived in the beet towns.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** check how many congregations hold services in the home language and when the second generation moves to English.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points and Dearfield:** check church minutes for faith in hardship, the Dust Bowl failure of Dearfield included.
- **Comparison only:** northern New Mexico's shrine devotions, for walks that start in Colorado and end across the state line.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic, evangelical and Black church history; thin for Somali and Eritrean faith life, where mosque and parish leaders are the first source.
- **Languages needed:** Ge'ez as the liturgical language behind Amharic and Tigrinya worship; *manito* Spanish for the *alabados*; Ute for any ceremony vocabulary, and only as the tribes allow.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** peyote and Sun Dance practice is the tribes' to share; Columbine accounts touch grieving families; Amache is contested ground on what the camp did to faith.
- **Held for review:** one category set aside on faith under wartime incarceration: Buddhist communities and institutions in Colorado's lands (an open question for the author).
