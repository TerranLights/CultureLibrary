# Colorado: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; southern Colorado and Mexican Spanish; Núuchi-u; Volga German; Japanese; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali, Korean, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Fort Carson and Air Force families, and veterans along the Front Range:** check which groups (chapels, outfitters, service nonprofits) say they rebuild purpose, and how they measure it.
- **Leadville, Climax and the coal towns of Trinidad and Walsenburg:** check what local papers wrote when the mines closed, for how a town named its loss.
- **Ski-town workers of Aspen, Telluride, Vail and Summit County:** check housing and mental-health reports for the gap between the chosen life and its cost.
- **Eastern Plains farm and ranch families:** check county extension records on farm crisis counseling (the 1980s; drought years).
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check how the movement's survivors tell their lives now, in oral histories of the Crusade for Justice.
- **Amache survivors and descendants:** check pilgrimage speeches at the camp site for the meanings offered.
- **Nuche youth programs:** check tribal education and culture departments for how the tribes frame purpose for the young.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali and Vietnamese families:** check parent-and-child gaps in what migration was for.
- **Volga German beet families:** check memoirs for meaning bound to land after the stoop labor years.
- **Comparison only:** Wyoming's and Utah's suicide-prevention campaigns, to check whether they frame purpose as Colorado's do, separating the regional from the state's own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for despair statistics and for Anglo and Chicano writing; thin for meaning in Somali and Eritrean life, and for Arapaho and Cheyenne voices in the state.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for Issei testimony; Spanish for movement and *manito* oral history; Vietnamese for refugee memoirs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data and veterans' accounts touch the grieving; Ute purpose stories may sit inside ceremony and stay with the tribes.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; the psilocybin row is kept to the state program and public client accounts, so no contemplative lineage behind any facilitator is named.
