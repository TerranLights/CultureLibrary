## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check how each tribe's government and newspapers speak of self-rule and hunting rights today.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos of Costilla and Conejos counties:** check oral histories of fence-cutting and grazing for how access was lived, apart from the courts.
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check Pueblo's own movement papers against Denver's for differences in what liberation meant.
- **Eastern Plains and northern ranch counties:** check 2013 campaign leaflets for the freedoms named (water, energy, guns, schooling).
- **Western Slope and North Fork:** check how coal, oil and gas families and newer organic growers each speak of land freedom.
- **Mountain resort towns:** check local papers for slope freedom set against housing limits.
- **Black Coloradans of Dearfield and Five Points:** check Juneteenth celebrations in Five Points since the 1950s for how freedom is staged.
- **Amache generation and their children:** check redress-era testimony (1981 hearings) for how loss of liberty was told.
- **Eritrean, Ethiopian, Somali and Vietnamese Aurora:** check national-day events for whether freedom means the old country's independence, escape from it, or both.
- **Colorado Springs military and evangelical families:** check how sermons and base speeches pair service with freedom.
- **Comparison only:** Utah's and Wyoming's public-lands transfer campaigns, to check which arguments Colorado's rural counties share and which are their own.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, Chicano and public-lands debates; thin for Arapaho and Cheyenne voices in the state today and for Somali and Eritrean commemorations, which live mostly on community social media.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for land-grant oral history; Tigrinya for Eritrean independence material; Vietnamese for refugee commemorations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute removal, Sand Creek's aftermath, Ludlow and the land-grant disputes are contested; gun violence touches recent victims; legal questions on any of these go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; freedom of worship was the facet likeliest to reach a held faith, and its rows (the Volga emigrants, Amache) are scoped to conscience, conscription and civil liberty.
