## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos, Latter-day Saints and newer residents:** check how each group tells the valley's UFO and apparition stories, whether as faith, folklore or joke.
- **Nuche:** check what the Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute cultural departments allow to be published from old tale collections, and what they keep back.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check whether tribal colleges (Wind River, Chief Dull Knife) reissue or reject the early collections.
- **Colorado Springs evangelicals:** check how worldview curricula define the warrant for belief (scripture, evidence, experience).
- **Front Range nones and skeptics:** check paranormal-tour customers and skeptics' meet-ups for how they hold belief lightly.
- **Volga German families:** check church-confirmation records for which catechism was taught and how long it was kept in German.
- **Mexican and Central American newcomers:** check parish youth groups for *doctrina* classes and the folk beliefs the classes push against.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali families:** check how children's religious schooling sits beside public school schedules.
- **Korean and Vietnamese congregations:** check generational gaps in creed, using bilingual youth ministries.
- **Comparison only:** Roswell and northern New Mexico's sighting tourism, to separate the San Luis Valley's own UFO lore from what crossed the border.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Hispano folklore and for evangelical teaching materials; mixed for Native tales, much of it from early outsider collections; thin for Somali and Eritrean belief transmission.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish (tales were recorded in the speakers' Spanish); Arapaho and Cheyenne to check what early translators did; Amharic for church schooling.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Ute, Arapaho and Cheyenne stories are seasonal or restricted, and may be told only at set times; mutilation and haunting lore touches ranch families' losses. Beliefs about law, oaths and courts go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** one category set aside on the household-altar facet of belief: Buddhist communities and institutions in Colorado's lands (an open question for the author).
