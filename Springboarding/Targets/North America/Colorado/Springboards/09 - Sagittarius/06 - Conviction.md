## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ute Mountain Ute beside Southern Ute:** check each tribe's account of the allotment split for how they now tell holding out versus accepting.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check who in the Sand Creek peace chiefs' lineages (Black Kettle's band) is honored for keeping to peace, as descendants recount it.
- **Coalfield Italian, Greek, Slavic and Hispano miners of Trinidad and Walsenburg:** check strike-era union rolls for who held out longest.
- **Chicano Denver and Boulder:** check how the Crusade for Justice and the Boulder student groups differed on methods.
- **Amache families:** check the split between those who volunteered for the army and those who resisted, using family oral histories.
- **Five Points:** check church-led civil-rights campaigns (fair housing, schools) for which pastors stood out.
- **Colorado Springs and Lakewood evangelicals:** check how "standing firm" is preached in campaigns on marriage and conscience.
- **Rocky Flats workers and Arvada neighbors:** check memoirs and union records for loyalty to the plant against the protesters' case.
- **Eastern Plains farm towns near missile fields:** check local responses to the Plowshares action and to silo life.
- **Eritrean, Ethiopian and Somali Aurora:** check how rival homeland factions organize in Colorado and how they speak of compromise.
- **Comparison only:** the Heart Mountain resisters in Wyoming, to check whether Amache's resisters linked up with them or stood alone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, peace and civil-rights conviction; mixed for evangelical campaigns, which are documented mainly by supporters and opponents; thin for diaspora politics.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for Issei family views; Spanish for coalfield and movement oral history; Tigrinya and Amharic for homeland-cause material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Los Seis, Sand Creek, Amache resisters, Amendment 2 and homeland conflicts are contested; present each as accounts and say whose. Court outcomes go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** nothing held; the peace-witness rows rest on the Colorado actors' own records (the Dominican sisters, Boulder activists, coalfield organizers), so no outside teaching on nonviolence had to be set aside.
