## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ranch families of the Eastern Plains, North Park and the Western Slope:** check sale-barn customs for when a handshake binds and when a check or brand paper is asked for.
- **Hispano acequia communities of the San Luis Valley:** check ditch minutes for how a water thief is named and shamed.
- **Mining-town descendants (Leadville, Cripple Creek, Silverton):** check local museum displays for whether swindlers are shown as villains or as color.
- **Nuche:** check tribal accounts of the Brunot Agreement and later land cessions for how each side's good faith is told.
- **Air Force Academy cadets and Colorado Springs families:** check how honor-code language passes into family and church life.
- **Volga German beet growers:** check grower-association records for disputes with the sugar company over weights and payment.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check how each rotating-savings tradition vets members and handles a default.
- **Mexican and Central American workers in meatpacking towns (Greeley, Fort Morgan):** check worker-center records for wage theft complaints, as workers frame dishonesty by employers.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points:** check business histories for how lenders and landlords dealt with Black customers, in the community's own record.
- **Comparison only:** Wyoming's brand-inspection system and sale barns, to check which handshake customs are regional and which are Colorado's own.

- **Sensitive or restricted:** treaty dealings are contested ground; wage-theft and fraud cases involve living people; anything on courts, contracts or crimes goes to Libra and Capricorn.
