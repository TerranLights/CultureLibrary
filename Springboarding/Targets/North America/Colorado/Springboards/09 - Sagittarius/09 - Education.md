## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check tribal education departments for language-teaching goals and how boarding-school memory shapes trust in schools.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse descendants:** check Fort Lewis enrollment by tribe for how many study in Colorado now.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check Adams State oral histories for the village teacher and the first-generation student.
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check how Pueblo's Chicano studies efforts differed from Denver's school.
- **Eastern Plains and South Park towns:** check consolidation votes for how communities weighed losing their school.
- **Volga German beet families:** check school-attendance records against harvest calendars in Weld and Larimer counties.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical families and Air Force Academy cadets:** check homeschool and academy admission paths as rival routes to standing.
- **Five Points and Dearfield:** check Black teachers' careers in Denver schools and the colony's school.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean, Somali, Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check Saturday schools for what parents add to public schooling.
- **Japanese Americans:** check postwar college enrollment of Amache's young people in Denver and beyond.
- **Comparison only:** Utah's and New Mexico's land-grant and tribal-college histories, to check which Colorado arrangements (the Fort Lewis waiver above all) have no neighbor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for universities, normal schools and attainment statistics; mixed for boarding schools, which are being documented now; thin for Somali and Eritrean schooling hopes and for Saturday schools.
- **Languages needed:** Arapaho and Ute for language-program materials, with the tribes' consent; Spanish for valley oral histories; Korean, Vietnamese and Amharic for Saturday-school materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school records involve survivors and burials; Amache schooling and the 1969 walkout are contested ground; school law and desegregation cases (busing among them) go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** two categories set aside from the higher-learning facet: Buddhist communities and institutions in Colorado's lands (an open question for the author); South Asian-heritage scholars at Colorado universities.
