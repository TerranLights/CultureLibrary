# Colorado: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; Mexican, Central American and *manito* Spanish; Volga German; Japanese; Amharic, Korean, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Colorado Springs and Lakewood evangelical congregations:** check statements of faith on inerrancy, and whether members accept or refuse the word "fundamentalist."
- **Front Range Catholics:** check traditional Latin Mass parishes and diocesan papers for how "orthodox" and "traditionalist" are used of themselves and others.
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check how the *Hermandad* and the parishes describe strict penitential practice, so that outsiders' labels are not mistaken for theirs.
- **Latter-day Saint wards of the valley:** check whether local members answer press mix-ups with Utah-based splinter groups, and in what words.
- **Mexican and Central American Pentecostal churches in Pueblo, Greeley and Denver:** check how Catholic neighbors' words (*secta*, *aleluya*) land, and what the churches call themselves.
- **Volga German Lutheran and Brethren families:** check whether pietist prayer hours survived in the beet towns.
- **Amish of Westcliffe and Monte Vista:** check how county neighbors and the settlements themselves describe separation.
- **Aurora's mosques and Somali families:** check how they answer publicly when the press revisits Qutb's Greeley year.
- **Korean and Vietnamese congregations:** check denominational affiliations and creeds instead of assuming theology from ethnicity.
- **Nuche, Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check how Native Christians and traditional practitioners each describe pressures to choose one path.
- **Comparison only:** the Utah-Arizona border's polygamist communities, to check which press stories about "Colorado" polygamy are misplaced.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the 1920s Klan, Colorado Springs ministries and extremist crimes; mixed for the 1920s church controversy; thin for strictness debates inside immigrant churches, which need interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Amharic and Tigrinya for Orthodox-evangelical labels; Spanish for Pentecostal congregations; Arabic sources on Qutb come through translation.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist" is usually an outsider's word; never apply it to a group, only report who used it; extremist violence is the rare edge; Klan history and the Berg murder involve living families. Any legal question (religious-liberty cases, faith-healing exemptions) goes to Libra and Capricorn.
