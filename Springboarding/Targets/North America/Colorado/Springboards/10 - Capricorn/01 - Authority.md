# Colorado: Authority (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; southern Colorado Spanish; Mexican and Central American Spanish; Núuchi-u, Hinóno'éitíít, Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse; Volga German; Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali. **Concept in one line:** the recognized right to direct others, decide for them or speak for them, and others' acceptance of that right.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check how elected councils and elders' voices are weighed in each tribe's own minutes and papers.
- **Northern Ute history in Colorado:** check accounts of the White River agency (1879) as a clash over an agent's authority, each side's account first.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley and Trinidad:** check whether patrón politics left traces in county offices after 1900.
- **Mexican Americans and Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo:** check police-community records against movement papers, for who was seen as legitimate.
- **Newer Mexican and Central American communities:** check what consulates and churches say on dealing with immigration enforcement.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical families:** check how parenting media from local ministries is used at home, through congregation newsletters.
- **Military households (Fort Carson, the Air Force Academy, Peterson and Schriever):** check spouses' and children's memoirs on rank carried into the home.
- **Eastern Plains counties:** check sheriffs' statements on which state laws they would enforce, as stated by them.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Orthodox parishes in Aurora:** check how priests' authority is exercised in parish disputes, from bulletins.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check how elders' associations and pastors are addressed in community papers.
- **Ski towns (Aspen, Telluride):** check town councils' disputes with resort companies and second-home owners.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public disputes over authority (news, oversight reports); thin for authority inside Aurora's East African households.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Barela-era papers; Amharic and Tigrinya for parish records; Korean and Vietnamese for church papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clergy-abuse survivors' testimony comes first; police encounters (for example in Aurora, 2019) are contested and in litigation; tribal council business may be closed. The permission-share number is scored from the law in force under Libra.
- **Held for review:** survey and oversight pointers on police authority were weighed; nothing here was set aside under the standing rules.
