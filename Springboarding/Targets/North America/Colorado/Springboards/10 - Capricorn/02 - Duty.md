## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **San Luis Valley Hispanos:** check *morada* charity rolls and acequia work rosters, for duties owed to neighbors rather than to the state.
- **Mexican and Central American newcomers in Denver and the Arkansas Valley:** check remittance agencies' calendars and hometown associations, for duties carried across the border.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check what each tribe publishes on duties to elders, in council and newspaper.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check how repatriation and memorial work are framed as obligations, in their own statements.
- **Japanese Americans (Amache families):** check oral histories for how a Nisei son's duty was weighed against parents' wishes in 1943.
- **Volga German families of the beet towns (Greeley, Windsor, Loveland, Fort Collins):** check church records and family books for inheritance and children's work.
- **Military families of Colorado Springs and Aurora:** check what spouses' groups say about duties left behind when one parent deploys.
- **Eastern Plains and North Park ranch families:** check oral histories for duties owed to neighbors at brandings and in blizzards.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check burial-society bylaws and accounts of diaspora levies, each side's account.
- **Somali Coloradans:** check mosque and community-group records on collective contributions for family emergencies; Colorado-specific sources likely sparse.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check elder-care arrangements described in church bulletins and senior associations, for who carries filial duties.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: civic duty is well measured; family duties among newer communities rest on oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for *alabados* and *morada* rules; Japanese for Issei letters; Amharic and Tigrinya for society bylaws.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *morada* membership and rites are private; Sand Creek memorial practice belongs to the tribes; the Eritrean diaspora levy is politically contested. Legal duties (duty of care, jury law) go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the pointers on duties owed to the dead (cemeteries, wake hymns, the memorial run) were checked against the standing rules; none needed setting aside.
