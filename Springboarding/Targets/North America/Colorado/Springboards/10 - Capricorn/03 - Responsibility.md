## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check what they have asked of institutions answerable for Sand Creek, in their own statements.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check tribal responses to the Gold King spill and to energy leases for how they frame stewardship of their lands.
- **Mountain mining towns (Silverton, Leadville):** check local papers on who should pay for old mine pollution.
- **Ski and hiking communities (Aspen, Telluride, Boulder):** check rescue-team logs and editorials on accidents.
- **Front Range suburbs (Superior, Louisville, Arvada):** check fire and Rocky Flats debates for neighbor against utility or government.
- **Eastern Plains and South Park ranch families:** check county pamphlets and grazing records for duties of fencing and weed control.
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check acequia minutes for how a member who fails at ditch upkeep is held to account.
- **Japanese Americans and the people of Granada:** check redress testimony and preservation records for who took on remembering.
- **Children of Mexican, Vietnamese, Ethiopian and Somali immigrants:** check school and clinic sources on children who interpret for parents and carry adult tasks.
- **Pueblo's steel families:** check CF&I accident files on how blame for injuries was assigned.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public blame after disasters; thin for household responsibility in Aurora's newer communities.
- **Languages needed:** English carries most inquiries; Spanish for acequia minutes; Amharic, Somali and Vietnamese for parent-child brokering.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek and Ludlow are contested; Gold King and the Marshall Fire involve live claims. Legal liability goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** for the blame-after-disaster pointers, the scan looked for named outside experts; none had to be set aside under the standing rules.
