## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check acequia bylaws and minutes for how a broken rule is answered without the state.
- **Southern Ute and Ute Mountain Ute:** check each tribe's court materials for sentencing aims and the role of elders.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check descendants' statements on the 1860s for their legal claims as they frame them.
- **Western Slope and Arkansas Valley irrigators:** check ditch-company minutes for when neighbors share water outside priority.
- **Eastern Plains counties:** check sheriffs' and commissioners' statements on discretion in enforcement.
- **Denver's Mexican and Central American residents:** check consulate and church guidance on dealing with police and immigration agents.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check community accounts of elders' reconciliation as an alternative to court.
- **Somali Coloradans:** check mosque or community mediation of family disputes; sources likely sparse in Colorado.
- **Vietnamese and Korean Aurora:** check community papers on reporting crime and using small-claims court.
- **Cannabis growers and dispensaries (Pueblo, Denver):** check industry and county records for how operators handle the state-federal gap.
- **Mining towns past and present:** check district records and press for miners' own rules beside territorial law.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for water, juvenile justice and cannabis; mixed for tribal courts (some opinions private); thin for diaspora mediation.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for acequia rules; Amharic, Tigrinya and Somali for elders' mediation; Vietnamese for village-custom sayings in use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek claims, the Taylor Ranch dispute and immigration sanctuary are contested; tribal court records may be closed. Statute texts, treaty wording and decrees as written go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** in looking for scholars of tribal and customary law, academic names were weighed one by one; none had to be set aside under the standing rules.
