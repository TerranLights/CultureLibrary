# Colorado: The domain, community and ideals (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; southern Colorado (*manito*) Spanish; Mexican and Central American Spanish; Núuchi-u (Ute); Hinóno'éitíít (Arapaho); Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse (Cheyenne); Volga German; Japanese; Amharic; Tigrinya; Somali; Korean; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** what binds people into groups, and what they hope for: friends, groups, hopes and ideals toward humanity, with the social ways of settlement, migration, association and hospitality.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley and southern Colorado:** compare the plaza-and-ditch settlement form with later towns, since it is the oldest non-Native settlement shape still in use.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute; the Northern Ute's Colorado history):** separate the two reservations' own accounts of gathering, because band histories differ.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** read the descendant nations' accounts (outside Colorado) of the Plains camp years, to keep their Colorado history from being folded into the Ute story.
- **Front Range newcomers:** check relocation forums and newcomer meetup groups, to test how people who arrived as adults find a circle.
- **Eastern Plains and North Park ranch and farm towns:** check fair books and fire-department histories, since dispersed settlement changes who gathers where.
- **Colorado Springs's military and evangelical communities:** check base orientation material and church small-group programs, as two separate joining routes.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali communities:** check *iddir*, *equb* and mosque associations as the groups' own structures.
- **Volga German, Japanese American, Black and Latter-day Saint communities:** check each one's founding colony or congregation records (Fort Collins, Amache, Dearfield, Manassa).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Denver and the Front Range; mixed for the Western Slope and the San Luis Valley; thin for North Park and for Somali and Eritrean associations, where the route is community organizations and oral history. Colorado's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the state as it stands.
- **Languages needed:** Cobos-era southern Colorado Spanish for the plaza and commons words; Amharic and Korean for the association names that will not appear in English directories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute ceremonial gatherings are not for outside documentation; Sand Creek and Ute removal are contested and descendant-led accounts come first.
- **Held for review:** a second Plains-town novel was weighed for the fiction row and dropped so the media layer is not one author's; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
