## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Volga German and Hispano beet-working families:** check Great Western Sugar contracts and wage books for pay and housing by origin.
- **San Luis Valley Hispano villages:** check *patrón*-era account books for how land and water shares were apportioned among households.
- **Nuche:** check Ouray's and Chipeta's recorded statements against the "Utes Must Go!" press, as two accounts of the same years.
- **Black Coloradans of Five Points and Park Hill:** check HOLC grades and the school-board debates of 1968-74.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache testimony to the 1981 Commission hearings for how loss and redress are framed.
- **Resort-town service workers:** check ACS origin and commute data for who works in a town but cannot live in it.
- **Colorado Springs military families:** check how on-post housing is assigned by rank.
- **Aurora's Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali residents:** check the city's immigrant-affairs office reports and community statements on housing and hiring complaints.
- **Women's clubs of the 1890s:** check minute books for which women (by class and race) were admitted.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for suffrage, redlining and busing; mixed for beet labor (company records partial); thin for Somali and Eritrean experience in print.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for village account books; Somali and Amharic for complaint and community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ute removal, Amache and the San Luis commons are contested; all court cases (busing, the commons, Amendment 2) belong to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a feminist press of 1890s Denver was considered for the suffrage row and dropped until its title is confirmed; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
