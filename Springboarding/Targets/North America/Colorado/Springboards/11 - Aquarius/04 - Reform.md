*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Nuche:** check White River agency reports and boarding-school enrollment against Ute-told accounts, to separate reform claimed from reform experienced.
- **Hispanos of the *Hermandad*:** check how *moradas* answered diocesan suppression, a case of a reformed institution that resisted.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo:** check Tlatelolco's curriculum and the Pueblo movement schools for what change meant to the founders.
- **Coal and steel workers (Pueblo, Huerfano, Las Animas):** check employee-representation minutes for what workers raised under the company plan.
- **Women's reform associations, 1880s-1920s:** check WCTU and suffrage minutes for which women led and which causes were chosen.
- **Boulder and Front Range environmental groups:** check Rocky Flats protest records for how a movement chose its demands.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical ministries:** check 1992 Colorado for Family Values campaign materials as moral reform, set against its opponents' materials.
- **Western Slope coal and Plains ranch towns:** check local papers for how reforms that cost them (plant closures, wolf reintroduction) are told.
- **Vietnamese refugee families:** check community papers for whether reform words carry homeland memory.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the Progressive era, Ludlow's aftermath and ballot measures; mixed for boarding schools (records scattered, some restricted); thin for reform movements within the Somali and Vietnamese communities in print.
- **Languages needed:** Vietnamese for the refugee press; Volga German for pietist renewal records in beet-town congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school records involve living families; Meeker and Ludlow are contested; the juvenile court, cannabis and Amendment 2 have legal sides that go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** a 1890s Denver Christian-socialist pastor was weighed for a Social Gospel row and dropped because his name could not be confirmed; nothing set aside under the standing rules.
