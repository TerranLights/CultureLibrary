# Colorado: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Planned-colony towns (Longmont, Colorado Springs, Nucla):** check founders' prospectuses against later town histories for which ideals outlasted the founders.
- **Boulder and Aspen:** check Chautauqua and Aspen Institute program lists for whose ideals each stage gave voice to.
- **Chicanos of Denver:** check memoirs written decades after 1969 for how the Aztlán ideal was later judged.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check descendant-nation commemorations at Sand Creek for which leaders are honored and why.
- **Undocumented youth activists (Denver, Aurora):** check their groups' statements for the ideals they invoke.
- **Latter-day Saints of Conejos County:** check colonists' diaries for the ideal of gathering to build Zion.
- **Ranch families and rural newcomers:** check county "Code of the West" booklets for the rural ideal handed to people moving out from the cities.
- **Eritrean and Ethiopian associations in Aurora:** check event programs for homeland ideals and how generations differ on them.
- **Colorado Springs ministries and military families:** check calls to service in sermons and base chapels.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonies, Aspen and the Chicano movement; mixed for Plains peace leaders (outsider sources dominate; descendant accounts must lead); thin for Korean and Eritrean idealism in print.
- **Languages needed:** Hinóno'éitíít and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse for the peace leaders' names and teachings; Korean for congregational writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek remains contested and grieved; Mesa Verde's past belongs to Pueblo nations; homeland politics in Eritrean and Ethiopian circles is divisive.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist institutions in Colorado, as a category only, for the facet of settlements built around an ideal; nothing else held.
