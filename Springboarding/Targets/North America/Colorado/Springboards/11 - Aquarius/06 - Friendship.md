# Colorado: Friendship (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; southern Colorado Spanish; Mexican Spanish; Volga German; Japanese; Korean; Vietnamese; Somali; Amharic. **Concept in one line:** a voluntary, personal bond of liking, loyalty and help between people who are not, or not only, kin or partners.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of Conejos and Costilla counties:** check *padrino* choices in baptismal registers for whether ritual friends cross family and plaza lines.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse:** check the Bent letters against descendant-nation accounts for how trade friendships were understood on each side.
- **Nuche:** check Ute-told histories for which neighboring peoples are named as friends or allies, and in which periods.
- **Volga German families:** check family letters to see when *Freindschaft* means kin and when it means friends.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Densho narrators for friendships with townspeople and with other camps' inmates.
- **Ski-town and 10th Mountain circles:** check reunion newsletters for wartime friendships turned business partnerships.
- **Ranch families of North Park, South Park and the Plains:** check branding-day helper lists for whose friendship is counted in labor.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora:** check whether church age-cohort groups are where adult friendships start.
- **Ethiopian and Eritrean households:** check who is invited to the coffee ceremony and how often, as a friendship practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for ritual friendship (parish registers) and for veterans; mixed for men's rural friendship (mostly letters and memoir); thin for Somali and Eritrean friendship customs in print.
- **Languages needed:** Volga German for family letters; Korean for church bulletins on fellowship; parish-register Spanish and Latin for *padrino* entries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Amache narrators' accounts are personal and must be quoted with permission; Plains descendant accounts lead over traders' letters.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as a category, for the facet of friendship between peoples.

## 5. First queries
