## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mountain towns (Aspen, Telluride, Nederland):** check 1970s town elections and festival permits for how oddness was folded into civic identity.
- **Boulder and Manitou Springs:** check council minutes on street performers and camping for where tolerance stopped.
- **Chicanos of Denver and Pueblo:** check cruising ordinances against lowrider club records for whose style was policed.
- **LGBTQ Coloradans:** compare Denver and Colorado Springs organizations' records from 1992-96 for how each answered Amendment 2.
- **Northeastern Plains counties:** check county commissioners' statements in 2013 for how dissent from the state was framed.
- **Japanese Americans:** check letters to Governor Carr in 1942 for how his stand was received.
- **Nuche:** check Ute-told and settler accounts of leaders who refused agency rules (Colorow), labeled very differently by each.
- **Evangelical youth ministries in Colorado Springs:** check curricula on standing apart from the wider culture.
- **Off-grid settlers of the San Luis Valley flats:** check county code-enforcement records against residents' own blogs and forums.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for the counterculture, Aspen and LGBTQ history; mixed for lowrider culture (much in club memory and photographs); thin for Korean and Japanese American attitudes to standing out.
- **Languages needed:** Chicano Spanish slang for club and cruising records; Japanese for Issei-era community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Amendment 2 and the 2013 secession votes are polarizing; off-grid residents may not want attention; Two-Spirit teachings only as the nations share them.
- **Held for review:** Buddhism in Colorado, held as a category for the facet of religious dissent in the counterculture years.
