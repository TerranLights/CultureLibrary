# Colorado: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Mountain West English; southern Colorado Spanish; Mexican Spanish; Japanese; Korean; Somali; Amharic. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings or from outcomes; some forms count as calm or objectivity, others as coldness or withdrawal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ranch families of North Park, South Park and the Plains:** check cowboy poems and ranch memoirs for how men voice loss after drought or a lost herd.
- **Hispano villages:** check wake customs and *alabado* singing against English-language funeral notices of the same towns, to compare public grief.
- **Japanese Americans:** check when Amache survivors began speaking at pilgrimages and hearings, and what families say of the years before.
- **Military families of Fort Carson and the Air Force bases:** check post-deployment behavioral-health reports for how trained composure follows soldiers home.
- **Nuche:** check visitor rules at the tribes' public events as statements of the distance asked of outsiders, not of the tribes' own manners.
- **Somali and Ethiopian Aurora:** check mental-health outreach materials in Somali and Amharic for the words chosen for distress and calm.
- **Korean and Vietnamese elders:** check senior-center programs for elders living alone after their children moved away.
- **Working-age men statewide:** check how Man Therapy segments its audience by trade and region.
- **Mountain-town seasonal workers:** check turnover and housing data for transience as a form of distance from place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for suicide, living alone and turnout; mixed for emotional styles (mostly memoir, poetry and recordings); thin for Somali and Amharic sources on composure.
- **Languages needed:** southern Colorado Spanish for *alabado* texts; Japanese for *gaman* and *enryo* in Issei writing; Somali for outreach materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and veterans' violence need care and aggregate data only; Ute mourning customs are not for outside study; Amache memory is personal.
- **Held for review:** Buddhism, as a category only, under the facet of non-attachment and contemplative calm.
