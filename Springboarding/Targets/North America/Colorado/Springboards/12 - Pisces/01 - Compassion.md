# Colorado: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English as spoken on the Front Range and the Plains; Hispano and Mexican Spanish; Hinóno'éitíít and Núuchi-u through tribal lexicons; Amharic, Tigrinya, Somali; Korean, Vietnamese, Japanese; Volga German. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of Conejos and Costilla counties:** check *mutualista* minute books for who qualified for aid and who was turned away.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** check parish *Posadas* and hometown-association drives for whom shelter is offered.
- **Nuche (Southern Ute, Ute Mountain Ute):** check tribal elder-care and social-services reports, as each tribe publishes them.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse histories:** check treaty-era council records for appeals to pity and how they were answered.
- **Black Coloradans (Five Points):** check church benevolence committees in Blair-Caldwell holdings.
- **Japanese Americans:** check postwar resettlement aid in Denver's Japanese press.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check *iddir* and *qaraan* collections against agency aid.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** check church relief committees for who is helped first.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical and military families:** check deployment-support ministries for mercy shown to strangers versus to the unit.
- **Ski-town and ranching communities:** check benefit dinners and crowdfunding for injured workers and hands.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Denver's charities and the sanatorium era; mixed for Hispano *mutualistas*; thin for East African and Southeast Asian giving in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *mutualista* records; Amharic and Somali for association bylaws; German script for Volga relief letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homelessness debates are live and partisan; the Meeker account is a contested captivity narrative; child-sponsorship appeals use children's images.
- **Held for review:** one category, Buddhist bodies in Colorado, set aside where a pointer touched compassion taught as a trained practice.
