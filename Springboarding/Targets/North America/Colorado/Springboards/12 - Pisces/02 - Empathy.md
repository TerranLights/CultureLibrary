# Colorado: Empathy (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** American English (Denver metro, Western Slope, Plains); Spanish, both Chicano Denver usage and the older San Luis Valley speech; Korean; Vietnamese; Japanese; Amharic; Somali; German; Ute and Cheyenne via tribal programs. **Concept in one line:** feeling or grasping what another person feels or thinks, from their side.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check *velorio* and *pésame* customs for who is expected to share a family's grief, and for how long.
- **Chicano Denver and Pueblo:** check movement newspapers (*El Gallo*, *check*) for appeals to see through a barrio resident's eyes.
- **Mexican and Central American newcomers:** check clinic and school interpreter guides for where staff misread *simpatía*.
- **Nuche:** check tribal statements on outside "empathy" exercises that borrow Ute dress or ceremony.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse descendants:** check Sand Creek commemorations for which outside gestures descendants accept.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Denver's Black press on whether white reformers' sympathy was asked for or refused.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Sansei memoirs for children grasping parents' unspoken camp years.
- **Korean, Vietnamese, Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check community health-worker training on reading distress across languages.
- **Ranching families:** check Grandin's reception in stock-grower magazines.
- **Military Colorado Springs:** check spouse-support groups on civilians failing to grasp deployment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for school programs and medical training; mixed for minority-language terms; thin for Ute and Cheyenne words, which belong to the tribes' own programs.
- **Languages needed:** Korean for *nunchi* discourse; Spanish across two registers; Amharic and Somali through community interpreters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Columbine materials touch living families; Sand Creek letters are contested testimony; autism framing should follow autistic authors' own accounts.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the medical-school and neuroscience pointers were weighed for named researchers under the standing rules, and the sheet names labs and institutions instead.
