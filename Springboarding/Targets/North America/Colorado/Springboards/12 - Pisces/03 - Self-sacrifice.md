# Colorado: Self-sacrifice (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (military, mining and firefighting registers included); Spanish of the *Hermandad* and of Mexican Denver; Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Núuchi-u via tribal sources; Japanese; Tigrinya and Amharic; Somali; Korean; Vietnamese; German. **Concept in one line:** giving up one's own interests, comfort, health or life for another person, a group, a cause or God.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check *mandas* recorded at Conejos and San Luis parishes against Hermandad penance.
- **Mexican and Central American workers:** check remittance-agency advertising on Denver's Federal Boulevard for how sending money is pictured.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí:** check whose accounts of Summit Springs and Sand Creek honor defenders, and who keeps them.
- **Nuche:** check how Ouray's land cessions are judged in Ute publications today.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache family records for the cost carried by parents of soldiers.
- **Volga German beet families:** check church jubilee books for children kept from school for field work.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Buffalo Soldier and Dearfield records for service unhonored in its time.
- **Eritrean and Ethiopian Aurora:** check Martyrs' Day programs and fasting-season bulletins.
- **Colorado Springs military and evangelical families:** check Gold Star family groups for how loss is framed.
- **Mining towns (Leadville, Silverton, Trinidad):** check disaster memorials for rescuers named.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military, firefighting and labor history; mixed for caregiving; thin for Somali and Vietnamese sources in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for Hermandad documents; Japanese for Issei letters; Tigrinya for Martyrs' Day speeches.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Penitente rites are the brothers' own; Ludlow and Sand Creek accounts conflict; school-shooting rescuers' families are living.
- **Held for review:** no pointer held back here: the fasting and vow pointers came from Catholic, Orthodox, Latter-day Saint and Muslim practice, and none needed holding.
