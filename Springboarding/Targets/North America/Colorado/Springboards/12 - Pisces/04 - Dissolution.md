# Colorado: Dissolution (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English; the Spanish of southern Colorado and of Chicano Denver; Núuchi-u and Hinóno'éitíít (revival programs); German as the beet towns kept it; Japanese; Korean; Vietnamese. **Concept in one line** (proposed; the author decides): the coming apart of a fixed form: the self's edges lost in something larger, and the ending of bonds, groups and identities.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on ghost towns and psilocybin policy; mixed on boarding schools; thin on Somali and Eritrean language loss in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish across generations; German for church records; Ute and Arapaho through the tribes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school records touch living families; high-control-group coverage names private people; psychedelic accounts can be medical records.
