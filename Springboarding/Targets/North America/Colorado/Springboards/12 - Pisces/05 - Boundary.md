## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of Costilla and Conejos counties:** check whether *mojoneras* and grazing rights are still named in family land talk.
- **Mexican and Central American newcomers:** check tenant-union records for crowding and privacy complaints.
- **Nuche:** check how each reservation's jurisdiction lines are explained to members.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check how removal maps appear in tribal schools.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Five Points oral histories for which streets were not crossed.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache memoirs for the first walk outside the wire.
- **Chinese Denver's history:** check descendants' statements at Hop Alley markers.
- **Volga German beet families:** check farm leases with Great Western Sugar for the field limits of tenant families.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check clinic patient-guide materials on touch and gender of caregivers.
- **Mountain resort towns (Aspen, Telluride):** check housing debates for lines between locals and second-home owners.
