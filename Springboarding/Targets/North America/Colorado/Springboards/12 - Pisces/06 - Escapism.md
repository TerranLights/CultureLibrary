## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check village dance-hall memories against parish temperance appeals.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** check which leisure spaces (soccer leagues, *bailes*) appear in Spanish-language radio listings.
- **Nuche:** check tribal wellness departments' reports on alcohol and gaming.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check Denver urban-Native accounts of the relocation era for what city life offered and cost.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Five Points jazz-club histories as places of release.
- **Japanese Americans:** check Amache baseball and dance accounts for recreation behind the wire.
- **Volga German beet families:** check church records on dances and drinking at harvest's end.
- **Korean and Vietnamese Aurora and Denver:** check *noraebang* and *nhậu* spaces in community press.
- **Ski and mountain towns:** check seasonal-worker accounts of the "ski bum" years.
- **Off-grid settlers of Costilla County:** check county and press records on why people come.
- **Colorado Springs military households:** check base programs on drinking after deployment.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cannabis, gaming, lottery and overdose; mixed for ski-town life; thin for Korean and Vietnamese leisure in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for radio and *baile* sources; Korean and Vietnamese community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** recovery groups are anonymous by design; overdose data touch identifiable small towns; off-grid residents may not wish to be studied.
- **Held for review:** the sheet's escapes are drink, cannabis, gaming, resorts and the road; none touched the standing rules, and ceremonial plant use is left to the Spirituality sheet.
