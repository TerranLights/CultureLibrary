## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of the San Luis Valley:** check feast-day records (Santiago, Santa Ana, San Isidro) for field and *acequia* blessings.
- **Mexican and Central American Catholics and Pentecostals:** check December 12 processions in Denver against storefront *iglesias*.
- **Nuche:** check what each tribe publishes about Bear Dance and Sun Dance seasons, and what it asks outsiders not to record.
- **Hinóno'éí and Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse descendants:** check consultation on sacred sites in Colorado (Sand Creek, Mount Blue Sky).
- **Black Coloradans:** check Zion Baptist and Shorter AME histories for revival and testimony practice.
- **Volga German families:** check pietist brotherhood meetings against parish worship.
- **Latter-day Saints of Manassa and Sanford:** check ward histories for personal-revelation accounts.
- **Somali, Ethiopian and Eritrean Aurora:** check mosque and parish calendars for home-based practice.
- **Vietnamese Denver:** check La Vang devotions alongside ancestor altars.
- **Boulder and mountain-town seekers:** check how "the mountains" stand in for church in trail and club writing.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic, evangelical and landscape devotion; mixed for New Thought; thin for Somali and Volga German practice in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for parish records; Amharic and Ge'ez for liturgy; Korean for church bulletins; German for pietist hymnals.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sun Dance, the Sacred Arrows and the Flat Pipe are closed; Penitente rites are private; the Native American Church's practice is protected and shared only by members.
- **Held for review:** two categories from the sacred-outside-the-church facet: Buddhist temples and centers in Colorado, and South Asian-heritage religious communities in the nation's lands.
