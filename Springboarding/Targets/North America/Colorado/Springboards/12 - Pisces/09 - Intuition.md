## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for avalanche, wildfire and aviation; mixed for nursing; thin for minority-language premonition vocabulary in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for weather lore; Japanese for Issei farm records; Korean and Amharic through community workers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** vision and dream knowledge of the Ute and Cheyenne is often closed; accident reports involve the dead and their families; prophetic-ministry claims should be recorded as claims.
- **Held for review:** no pointer held; the premonition and dream sources here are Christian, Cornish, Hispano and Cheyenne, and the clinical sources are named by institution rather than by person.
