# Colorado: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Colorado, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English (press, true-crime and street registers); Spanish of the old Hispano villages and of Mexican and Central American Denver; Japanese; Amharic and Tigrinya; Somali; Korean; Vietnamese; German; Ute and Cheyenne terms only as the nations publish them. **Concept in one line:** what the culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it (moral and social side only; law is researched in Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hispanos of southern Colorado:** check *corridos* and family memory for outlaws told as avengers.
- **Mexican and Central American Denver:** check Spanish-language radio call-ins on who is blamed for neighborhood crime.
- **Nuche:** check tribal statements on missing and murdered Indigenous relatives in the Four Corners.
- **Tsėhésenėstsestȯtse and Hinóno'éí descendants:** check whether the word "massacre" or "battle" is used of Sand Creek, and by whom.
- **Black Coloradans:** check Five Points press on policing and on which wrongs drew no charges.
- **Japanese Americans:** check community views of the Amache resisters, then and after 2000.
- **Chinese Denver's history:** check how the 1880 riot's wrongdoers were later named.
- **Italian, Greek and Slavic coal families of Las Animas and Huerfano counties:** check union lodge records on strikebreakers.
- **Ethiopian, Eritrean and Somali Aurora:** check elders' mediation for wrongs handled without police.
- **Colorado Springs evangelical households:** check prison-ministry testimonies of redemption.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Sand Creek, Ludlow, Columbine and Packer; mixed for Chicano movement cases; thin for East African and Southeast Asian community views in Colorado.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *corridos* and radio; Japanese for camp-era debates; Somali and Amharic through community mediators.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sand Creek, Ludlow, Ute removal and Amache are contested; school-shooting families are living; missing-relatives cases are active. Statutes, courts and sentencing go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the crimes and reckonings here are Colorado events named by place and institution; none raised the standing rules, so this sheet holds nothing back.
