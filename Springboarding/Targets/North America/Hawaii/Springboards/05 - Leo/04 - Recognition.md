## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli: compare Kingdom-era honors, lineage titles and today's acknowledgments; check who is asked to speak first at gatherings.
- People of Niʻihau: titles and family standing are internal matters; ask only through the community.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: compare honors from Washington with honors from Tokyo, and how families display each.
- Chinese and Korean families: look at foreign-government honors and surname-society awards.
- Filipino residents: separate veterans' fight for recognition from professional credential struggles, by Ilocano and Visayan towns of origin.
- Portuguese and Puerto Rican descendants: check what clubs honor and whether outside bodies honored them.
- Samoan and Tongan residents: compare title bestowal with church and sport honors.
- Marshallese and Chuukese residents: look at church and school events; formal records are scarce.
- Haole residents and military families: medals and unit awards against civilian honors; check whether base families appear on island honor lists.
- Paniolo families of Waimea and Upcountry Maui: check ranching halls of fame and rodeo titles.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for formal honors and war recognition; mixed for everyday credit; thin for Micronesian communities.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for Kingdom registers and notices; Japanese and Korean for foreign-honor coverage; Samoan for *saofaʻi* programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Apology Resolution and the overthrow are contested; give each reading as an account, and send legal analysis to Libra and Capricorn. Titles of *aliʻi* descent are claimed by several families; record claims as claims.
- **Held for review:** one recognition pointer, an annual honors program run by a Buddhist body, is held as a category (Buddhism).

## 5. First queries
1. Hawaii State Archives finding aids: "royal order" registers and decorations correspondence, 1865–1893.
2. congress.gov: Public Law 103-150 text, then community responses in *Ka Wai Ola* and the daily papers, November 1993.
3. Search "Hawaii Board on Geographic Names" decisions list; note restored names by island.
4. Search "Congressional Gold Medal" 2010 and 2017 with "Honolulu" for local ceremony coverage.
5. Hawaii Sports Hall of Fame site: inductee list by sport and island.
