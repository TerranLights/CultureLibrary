*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli on Oʻahu's Waiʻanae Coast and in Hilo and Puna: check arrest-to-sentence paths by district.
- Kānaka Maoli land and water activists: compare how protesters charged at Mauna Kea, Kahoʻolawe or Kalama Valley describe their trials with court records.
- Samoan and Tongan families (Kalihi, Lāʻie, Waiʻanae): check whether an *ifoga* or a church apology runs alongside a criminal case.
- Chuukese and Marshallese defendants: check interpreter access and plea rates; data will be thin, so ask community groups and public defenders.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: separate the memory of provost courts and internment from the later redress.
- Filipino residents (Ilocano, Visayan, Tagalog speakers): check the 1924 Hanapēpē strikers' trials and later records for how the courts treated them.
- Haole residents, from old families to recent arrivals: check how the Massie case is retold within the group and in schools.
- Military families: check how military and state courts divide offenses by service members.
- Lānaʻi, Molokaʻi and Kauaʻi residents: check distance to courts and jails; rural justice is a matter of travel.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for incarceration and court data; mixed for historical trials; thin for Micronesian, Niʻihau and Tongan sources.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for 1840s trial reports and royalist laments; Ilocano for strike-trial testimony; Chuukese for present-day court use.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the overthrow and the 1895 trials, Massie, Mauna Kea arrests and prison transfers are contested; present each account as an account. Victims' and defendants' families are living; do not reopen cases.
- **Held for review:** a prison-ministry pointer was set aside as a category (Buddhism); the church ministries were kept.
