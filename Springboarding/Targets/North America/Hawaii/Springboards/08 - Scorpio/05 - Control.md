# Hawaii: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli fishing families of Miloliʻi and Hāʻena: compare community fishing rules with state control of the same waters.
- Kānaka Maoli of Puna and Kaʻū: check how lava diversion talk is heard by families with ties to Pele.
- Niʻihau residents: check, through the community, how decisions on access are made and felt.
- Lānaʻi residents of Filipino, Japanese and Kānaka Maoli descent: compare company-town memories with the present owner era.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: check martial law and school control in family memory, by island.
- Filipino families (Ilocano, Visayan): check who holds the household purse, and how "under de saya" is used and by whom.
- Chinese and Korean descendants: compare control of family businesses across generations.
- Portuguese families: check parents' control over courtship in plantation-era memory.
- Tongan, Samoan, Marshallese and Chuukese families: check how authority at home and in church reaches young people born in Hawaii.
- Military families: compare base rules with off-base life in Wahiawā and Kāneʻohe.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** plentiful on martial law, island ownership and tourism plans; patchy on control inside households; scarce for Micronesian and Niʻihau practice.
- **Languages needed:** Japanese for the Hochi's school campaign; Hawaiian for fishing *kapu* in the nūpepa; Marshallese for land-title talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lava diversion touches Pele practice; present Kānaka Maoli objections in their own words. Martial law and the school campaign are contested history. Partner-control data concern victims; use published tables only.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; the military, company, press and survey sources weighed for control raised no question under the standing rules.
