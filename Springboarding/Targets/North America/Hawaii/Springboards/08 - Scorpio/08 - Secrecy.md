*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli families of Kawaihae and Kohala: check, through them, how hidden burials are spoken of and by whom.
- People of Niʻihau: access to the island has long been restricted; approach only through community members and on their terms.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: compare what parents kept from children in camp days with what grandchildren now ask.
- Chinese descendants: check what lodge members' families were told.
- Ilocano and Visayan families: check lodge membership and its private rites across generations.
- Korean descendants: check what was hidden from the Japanese consulate's watch in the 1910s–40s, as families recall it.
- Portuguese families: check secret recipes and family matters kept from the parish.
- Samoan and Tongan families: check what is kept within the *ʻāiga* or *kāinga* and not told in church.
- Marshallese residents: check what stays within the clan; English sources are scant.
- Military families: compare what service members may not tell with what families guess.
- Fishers and surfers on Kauaʻi and the North Shore: check how "secret spots" are kept and policed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** good on lodges, codebreaking and the cave dispute; patchy on what families hide; very little for Micronesian and Niʻihau practice.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for burial and *koʻa* terms; Cantonese for lodge records; Tagalog and Spanish for Filipino lodge papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** burial places, *iwi kūpuna*, family genealogies, *koʻa* and some chants are restricted; go through burial councils, families and hālau, and never publish locations. Forbes Cave is contested among Kānaka Maoli groups; give each account. HIV records are confidential.
- **Held for review:** closed rites of island temple congregations were weighed for this sheet and kept back as a category (Buddhism); the Masonic, Chinese and Filipino societies stand.
