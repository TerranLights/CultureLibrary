## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli in Hawaiian-language congregations (Molokaʻi, Hāna, Waiʻanae) and in hālau: compare church and *hula* as two keepers of the sacred.
- The people of Niʻihau: ask through Kekaha contacts which church life the island keeps and who may speak of it.
- Okinawan and Japanese families (Hilo, Waipahu, Wahiawā): separate Okinawan hearth practice from mainland-Japanese shrine visits.
- Chinese and Korean families: set Chinatown society altars and the Korean churches beside each group's other congregations.
- Ilocano and Visayan families (Waipahu, Kahului, Kauaʻi): compare parish fiestas with *Iglesia ni Cristo* and Protestant chapels.
- Azorean, Madeiran and Puerto Rican descendants (Kula, Hilo): ask who still makes a *promessa* and to which saint.
- Samoan and Tongan communities (Lāʻie, Kalihi): separate Latter-day Saint from Congregational and Wesleyan congregations.
- Paniolo families (Waimea, Upcountry Maui) and military families: set ranch-country churches beside base chaplaincies.
- Haole newcomers (Windward Oʻahu, Puna): check the unaffiliated and seekers against long-settled haole church families.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mission history and Kānaka Maoli religion in print; mixed for today's affiliation (small samples); thin for Micronesian churches and unbelief.
- **Lands:** the tracker settles no lands (the row carries only the USA) and no scope file exists, so the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the Hawaiian Islands with Papahānaumokuākea.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for scripture and nūpepa church columns; Japanese and Cantonese for society records; Samoan and Tongan for congregation histories.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some chant, hālau teaching and heiau protocol are practitioner-held; go through them. The 1819 break and the missions' role come in rival accounts; give each as one.
- **Held for review:** the temples' share of the islands' religious landscape is held as a category (Buddhism); one further pointer is held only as a category: South Asian-heritage communities and scholars in the Hawaiian Islands.
