# Hawaii: Travel (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hawaii (the Hawaiian Islands), region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi, Pidgin, island English, Japanese, Uchinaaguchi, Cantonese, Korean, Tagalog, Ilocano, Portuguese, Samoan, Tongan, Marshallese, Chuukese. **Concept in one line:** going far from home (long journeys, exploration, pilgrimage, migration) and how the culture regards the road, the foreign and the traveler.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli voyaging families (Kawaihae, Waiʻanae, Molokaʻi): separate the canoe as teaching from the canoe as travel.
- Niʻihau families: check how travel to and from Kauaʻi is arranged and spoken of; follow the community's lead.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: compare return trips to Japan with the Uchinanchu festival trips.
- Chinese descendants: check village-visit trips to Guangdong across generations.
- Korean families: compare first-wave descendants' travel with post-1965 arrivals' ties.
- Ilocano and Visayan families: set the long Manila journeys beside island-born children's first visits.
- Portuguese and Puerto Rican descendants: check whether anyone still travels to the Azores, Madeira or Puerto Rico.
- Samoan, Tongan, Marshallese and Chuukese families: compare obligation travel and the costs it brings.
- Military families and haole transplants: check how newcomers describe arriving and whether they stay.
- Paniolo families (Waimea, Upcountry Maui): check the 1908 Cheyenne trip as a travel memory, not a sport one.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** visitor and migration counts are plentiful; residents' own travel and Micronesian journeys are under-recorded.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for the voyaging moʻolelo; Japanese and Ilocano for migrant records; Samoan for *malaga* accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some navigation teaching is held by its practitioners and by Satawal's lineage; tourism limits are argued locally; the Lahaina reopening after 2023 is contested.
- **Held for review:** summer homeland trips timed to temple observances were set aside as a category (Buddhism); festival and family return trips stay in the table.
