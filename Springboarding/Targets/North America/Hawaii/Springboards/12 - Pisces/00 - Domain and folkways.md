# Hawaii: The domain: the hidden and transcendence (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Hawaii (the Hawaiian Islands: the eight main islands with Papahānaumokuākea), region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi with Niʻihau speech; Pidgin; the English of Hawaii; Japanese and Uchinaaguchi; Ilocano and Tagalog; Cantonese; Korean; Portuguese; Samoan; Tongan; Marshallese and Chuukese. **Concept in one line:** how the culture meets suffering, the unseen and the wish to escape: the hidden self, confinement and institutions, sacrifice, compassion, transcendence, and the magic ways that handle luck, harm and hidden forces.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kānaka Maoli of Molokaʻi (Hoʻolehua, Kalaupapa's descendants), Waiʻanae, Kaʻū and Hāna: test how exile, distress and the unseen are named, rural against urban.
- Niʻihau families and their Kekaha kin: check whether any of their own accounts are in print at all before using outsiders'.
- Japanese and Okinawan descendants: set wartime detention memory beside the plantation-era endurance vocabulary.
- Ilocano and Visayan families of Waipahu, Kalihi and Lānaʻi City: separate healers' practice by home province.
- Portuguese and Puerto Rican descendants of Upcountry Maui and Hilo: check evil-eye and healing vocabularies against Catholic devotion.
- Samoan, Tongan, Marshallese and Chuukese families: compare clinic use with church and kin routes for distress.
- Haole residents and military families: check which route for distress (therapist, chaplain, none) their memoirs name.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Kalaupapa, detention and measured distress; mixed for folk healing; thin for Niʻihau, Chuukese and Puerto Rican accounts.
- **Lands:** where Hawaii's borders fall is open: there is no `00 - Scope.md`, and the USA is the tracker's only pre-war land. The Pisces sheets work from the eight main islands and Papahānaumokuākea and add no units.
- **Languages needed:** Hawaiian for the *leina* sources and kingdom health reports; Japanese for detention letters; Ilocano for healers' terms; interpreters for Marshallese and Chuukese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** *kahuna* and *ʻanāʻanā* knowledge, burial sites and family genealogies are restricted; go through the communities' own institutions. Kalaupapa residents set their own limits. The Lahaina fire and Kahoʻolawe are contested and raw; give accounts as accounts.
- **Held for review:** one unseen-world pointer, on rites for visiting spirits of the dead, came from Buddhist practice: held as a category.
