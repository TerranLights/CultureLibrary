# Midwest Republic: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch and German; Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish; Polish, Czech, Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Order Amish and Mennonites (Elkhart-LaGrange; Holmes County as a border case):** check how yieldedness is taught and where speaking up is allowed, such as church votes.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check whether *igualado* is used of the same acts in Pilsen and in Humboldt Park; the two communities have separate histories with city hall.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** compare pulpit, press and union hall as the places where public claims were made, generation by generation.
- **Sauk, Meskwaki and Potawatomi:** check recorded speeches against each nation's own accounts, since most were written down by interpreters.
- **Scandinavian- and Finnish-descended Upper Midwesterners:** test whether a rule against standing out is named locally or only by outsiders.
- **Hmong women in the Twin Cities:** check women's organizations for training in speaking at clan and family meetings.
- **Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi Dearborn:** separate the three before comparing verse dueling, mosque and city meetings.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for organizing, speeches and folk humor; thin for recorded everyday conversation by region (no corpus known to the agent; search the linguistics departments at Michigan, Ohio State and Minnesota).
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for *igualado* and *dejado*; Arabic for *zajal*; Somali for verse; Sauk and Meskwaki for checking what interpreters wrote.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Young Lords and Black organizing files include police surveillance records, a contested account in themselves. The legal side of complaints and claims goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; boasting traditions in Dearborn and Minneapolis were checked against the standing rules and needed no hold.
