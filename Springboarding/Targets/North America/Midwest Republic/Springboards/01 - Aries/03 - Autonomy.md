## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Order Amish settlements in Indiana, Wisconsin and Iowa:** check how the choice of baptism is framed by each church district.
- **Amana and Bishop Hill descendants:** check family papers for how members spoke of the loss or gain of choice when communal life ended.
- **Menominee, Meskwaki and the Red Lake Ojibwe:** check each nation's own account of collective self-rule; their histories differ sharply.
- **Hmong families in St. Paul, Milwaukee and Wausau:** compare generations and genders on who decides marriage and schooling.
- **Rural Iowa and Minnesota farm families:** compare with city families of the same states on teenagers' say.
- **Polish and Italian Catholic daughters of the Hull-House era:** check settlement records for clashes over wages and marriage.
- **Autoworkers in Flint, Detroit and Toledo:** check by plant and decade how control of the line was won or lost.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for communal societies and labor; mixed for Native collective self-rule in print; thin for Somali and Arab family decision-making from inside the communities.
- **Languages needed:** Amana German and Swedish for colony records; Meskwaki for checking the Fox Project's claims; Hmong for clan-council sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal governance and enrollment are each nation's own business; Meskwaki views of the Fox Project researchers; boarding-school records hold children's names. Court rulings on Amish schooling go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none; the communal and religious sources on choice here are Christian and Native, and nothing fell under the open questions.
