# Midwest Republic: Initiative (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; German, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Czech; Spanish; Arabic and Neo-Aramaic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** starting things and acting without being told; taking the lead unasked.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chaldean, Lebanese and Yemeni Detroiters:** separate them; check which trades each entered first and through whose credit.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** compare savings clubs, street vending and storefronts by neighborhood.
- **Black Chicago, Detroit and Indianapolis:** check business founding across the Great Migration and after urban renewal, decade by decade.
- **Amish and Mennonite workers around Elkhart-LaGrange:** check the shift between factory work and home businesses (search Goshen College's Mennonite Historical Library).
- **Hmong and Somali Minnesotans:** compare farming, stalls and malls as first ventures, by arrival cohort.
- **Rural Iowa, Wisconsin and Minnesota towns:** check volunteer fire departments and work bees as routines that start without orders.
- **Ho-Chunk, Oneida and Potawatomi nations:** check each nation's enterprise histories in its own publications; gaming's legal frame goes to Libra.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for business statistics, service clubs and Black business history; mixed for immigrant ventures (news more than studies); thin for Native-told histories of enterprise.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for savings clubs and vendors; Arabic and Neo-Aramaic for Dearborn and Chaldean business press; Somali for mall traders; Norwegian and Finnish for checking communal-work terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** informal credit and remittance networks can draw legal scrutiny; handle by community consent. The Heidelberg demolitions are a contested account.
- **Held for review:** one pointer for this concept was set aside under the rule on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands; category only.
