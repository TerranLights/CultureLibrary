# Midwest Republic: Beginning (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; French (colonial records); German, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, Polish; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin, Dakota. **Concept in one line:** how starting is treated: origins, firsts, new starts, and starting over after failure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dakota and Anishinaabeg:** check how each nation's origin and migration accounts are placed against settler founding dates in state museums and schools.
- **Descendants of French Detroit and the Illinois Country:** check which founding they keep (1701 Detroit, 1703 Kaskaskia) and how.
- **Norwegian- and Swedish-descended rural families:** compare the arrival story families retell with the letters in the archives.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** check how the migration north is placed as a family's beginning, by generation.
- **Hmong and Somali arrival cohorts:** compare first-wave and later-wave stories of the first winter.
- **Detroit, Flint and Gary residents:** check who tells the "comeback" and who rejects it.
- **Mexican Chicagoans:** check *quinceañera* and New Year customs for how a young person's or household's start is marked.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for settler and immigrant letters and for city revival coverage; mixed for refugee arrival records; thin in print for Menominee and Ho-Chunk origin accounts.
- **Languages needed:** French for colonial registers; Norwegian and Swedish for America letters; Hmong for New Year texts; Dakota for place names.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Anishinaabe sacred stories may be told only in certain seasons; check each community's practice before quoting. Founding-date plaques are contested ground.
- **Held for review:** none; the calendars on this sheet (Hmong, Mexican, Christian, Anishinaabe) raised no item under the open questions.
