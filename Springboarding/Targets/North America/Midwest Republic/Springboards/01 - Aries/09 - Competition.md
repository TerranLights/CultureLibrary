## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small-town Indiana and Indianapolis:** check how the end of single-class basketball (1997) changed town rivalries.
- **Iron Range towns and the Twin Cities:** compare hockey's place in town standing.
- **Black Kansas City, Chicago and Detroit:** check Negro Leagues, Golden Gloves and Joe Louis-era sources for who organized contests and why.
- **Arab Americans of Dearborn:** check school sports as a public stage, and how families weigh sport against study.
- **Hmong Minnesotans and Wisconsinites:** check which New Year contests continue and who enters them.
- **Mexican Chicagoans:** compare leagues and *charreadas* organized by home state.
- **Amish youth groups:** check, by settlement, which games are allowed and whether scores are kept.
- **Anishinaabeg and Ho-Chunk:** check lacrosse and moccasin-game revival as each community shares it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for sports, fairs and market rivalry; mixed for immigrant leagues (news more than archives); thin for Somali youth tournaments.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish for league records; Hmong for New Year programs; German and Czech for Turner and Sokol papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lacrosse has a spiritual standing in some Native communities; mascots taken from Native peoples are contested.
- **Held for review:** none; the games and festivals named here raised no item under the held categories.
