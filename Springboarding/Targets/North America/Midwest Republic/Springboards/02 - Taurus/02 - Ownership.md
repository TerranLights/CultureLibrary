## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dakota, Anishinaabeg (Ojibwe, Odawa, Potawatomi), Meskwaki, Menominee and the Oneida of Wisconsin:** check each nation's tenure history on its own record, since cession, purchase, removal and termination produced different holdings.
- **Owner-operators against tenants in Iowa and central Illinois:** check tenure surveys for absentee and inherited ownership, which shape who works land they do not own.
- **Bronzeville and the West Side against Chicago's white-ethnic parishes:** check deeds, covenants and contract sales neighborhood by neighborhood rather than by group.
- **Milwaukee and Cleveland against the Twin Cities and Indianapolis:** check how far public and cooperative ownership of utilities went in each.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements:** check how church districts buy and pass on farmland, from their own publications.
- **Mexican, Puerto Rican, Arab and Hmong homebuyers:** compare lending and title records across Pilsen, Humboldt Park, Dearborn and St. Paul's East Side.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land records, tenure surveys and city housing history; mixed for collective Native holdings, where each nation's own publications are needed; contested for redlining and covenants.
- **Languages needed:** Dakota, Ojibwe and Meskwaki for land terms; Finnish for co-op records; German, Polish and Czech for farm and parish deeds; Spanish and Arabic for present buyers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** treaty and removal history (the 1830 act under its published title, the Dakota expulsion after 1862); the law of property itself belongs to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** none; legal pointers (deed law, covenant cases) were moved to Libra rather than held.
