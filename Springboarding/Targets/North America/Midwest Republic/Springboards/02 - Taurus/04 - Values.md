*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **ELCA, Missouri Synod and Wisconsin Synod Lutherans:** check their teaching documents side by side, since the synods divide on the questions this concept asks.
- **Christian Reformed West Michigan and Pella against German Catholic Stearns County and Dubuque:** check Sabbath, drink and schooling records in each.
- **Amish affiliations of Indiana and Iowa:** check how each settlement's *Ordnung* ranks technology, schooling and work.
- **Black churches of Bronzeville and Detroit:** check sermons and church histories for how values were taught across the Great Migration.
- **Lebanese and Iraqi Shi'a of Dearborn, Yemeni Southend families and Somali Minneapolis:** separate mosque and school materials by community.
- **Anishinaabeg and Dakota:** check each nation's own published teachings; do not merge them.
- **Chicago and the Twin Cities against downstate Illinois and rural Iowa:** check PRRI and GSS tables for the urban and rural split.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for religious, school and survey sources; mixed for Mexican, Hmong and Somali families, where community organizations hold the records.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania Dutch and German for Amish and Lutheran teaching; Dutch for early Reformed records; Polish for parish banners and societies; Arabic and Somali for mosque materials; Ojibwe and Dakota for teachings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** culture-war material and debates around Dearborn are contested; cite as accounts. Some teachings are shared only in person; use what the nations publish.
- **Held for review:** of the civic sources weighed for values, one was set aside under the South Asian rule and is carried only as the category of South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
