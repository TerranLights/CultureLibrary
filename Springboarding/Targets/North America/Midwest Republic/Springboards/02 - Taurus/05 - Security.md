## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Norwegian, Polish, Czech and German lodge members:** check the benefit tables and minutes of each society, since immigrant groups built separate insurance.
- **Amish and Mennonite districts against Lutheran farm families:** check how each covers loss, through church aid or through insurers.
- **UAW families of Detroit and Flint against Iowa farm households:** compare job security and land security records in the two crises of the 1980s.
- **Milwaukee's North Side and Chicago's South and West Sides:** check eviction and lending records street by street.
- **Somali and Mexican households:** compare *ayuuto* and *tandas* with the formal accounts each community uses.
- **Ojibwe nations of Minnesota and Wisconsin:** check each band's account of annuity years and present food programs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for social-insurance history, fraternal records and food and eviction data; thinner for informal savings, which community members hold.
- **Languages needed:** Norwegian, Polish and Czech for lodge minutes; Somali and Spanish for savings circles; Arabic for views on insurance and interest; Ojibwe for food-storage terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Sandy Lake and annuity history; remittance closures and Dearborn surveillance debates are contested; eviction records name living people.
- **Held for review:** none; the lodge, church-aid and public-insurance sources weighed here touch no held category.
