*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Lac du Flambeau and other Ojibwe spearers against northern Wisconsin anglers and resort owners:** check each side's own records of the 1980s, since both claimed the same lakes.
- **Hmong and non-Hmong hunters of northern Wisconsin:** check Wisconsin DNR outreach and community records after 2004, rather than press summaries alone.
- **Chicago wards and suburbs:** check where "dibs" is practiced, tolerated or banned, block by block.
- **Arab, Somali and Hmong families in counseling settings:** compare how each community's agencies describe jealousy and control.
- **Iowa and Minnesota farm heirs:** check extension casework on keepsakes and land.
- **Tool-library members against suburban households:** check what each will lend and borrow.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for domestic-violence programs and for resource conflicts; thin for everyday jealousy norms in Somali and Arab households, where agencies are the main door.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic, Somali and Hmong for counseling and advocacy materials; Spanish for song and radio; Ojibwe for words on sharing and stinginess; Polish and German for older family letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** intimate-partner violence, marriage by capture, the walleye conflict and the 2004 shootings are all contested or painful; cite each as an account; never infer a group's character from a case.
- **Held for review:** none; the advocacy agencies and the fishing and hunting disputes were weighed for restricted community knowledge, and none needed holding.
