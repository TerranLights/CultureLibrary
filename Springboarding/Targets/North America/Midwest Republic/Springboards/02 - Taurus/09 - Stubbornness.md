## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Finnish, Norwegian, Swedish and German Midwesterners:** check who tells the stubbornness jokes, to whom, and whether insiders and outsiders mean the same.
- **Polish residents of Poletown against the city and GM:** check both records before calling any party obstinate.
- **Shawnee, Myaamia and Dakota histories:** check each nation's own account of resisting cession, since outsiders' records often frame it as hostility.
- **Mexican parents of Little Village and the school board:** check both sides' statements during the hunger strike.
- **Anishinaabe water protectors and Iowa farm holdouts:** compare motives and alliances from each group's own statements.
- **Dutch Reformed seceders and those who stayed:** check minutes of both sides of the 1857 split.
- **Flint and Kohler workers against management:** check union and company archives together.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, splits and protests; thinner for everyday family obstinacy, which needs oral history and humor collections.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for *sisu* in use; Dutch for secession records; Polish for Poletown parish sources; Bosnian for *inat*; Ojibwe and Shawnee for resistance histories in the nations' terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pipeline protests, the Council Wars and the Dakota expulsion are contested; ethnic jokes are data, never a portrait of a people.
- **Held for review:** none; holdout, strike and church-split sources were weighed, and the ethnic jokes needed only the caution above, not a hold.
