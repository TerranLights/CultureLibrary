## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Seventh-day Adventists of Battle Creek and Methodist temperance families against Milwaukee's German beer halls:** check how each record frames pleasure in drink and food.
- **Finnish sauna households against Scandinavian Lutheran congregations:** compare where heat, bathing and coffee sit in each community's life.
- **Polish, Czech and Slovene dance-hall communities:** check polka records by town, since dance customs differ.
- **Black and Latino club dancers of Chicago and Detroit:** check oral histories from the dancers themselves.
- **Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi Dearborn and Somali Minneapolis:** check how fasting months shape the evening's pleasures in each.
- **Hmong vendors and shoppers of St. Paul:** check market talk about taste and freshness.
- **Anishinaabe bands:** check what each shares about sugaring and ricing, and what stays with the community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for food, temperance and dance history; good festival coverage; thin for touch and comfort customs in Somali and Hmong homes.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for sauna words; German for beer-hall culture; Polish and Czech for dance-hall records; Arabic and Somali for fast-breaking and scent; Hmong for market talk; Ojibwe for harvest seasons.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ojibwe ceremonies tied to sugar and rice, and sweat lodges, are not for outside use; drinking data can feed stereotypes and must be read as rates, never as a people's character.
- **Held for review:** none; festival, temperance and dance-hall pointers were weighed for faith-based restrictions on pleasure, and none applied.
