# Midwest Republic: Learning (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish and Old Order Mennonite settlements (LaGrange, Holmes, Kalona, Cashton):** check how home work and the eighth-grade school fit together, by affiliation.
- **Anishinaabe communities with and without immersion programs (Lac Courte Oreilles, Leech Lake, Bay Mills):** check each program's own reports.
- **Dakota communities of Minnesota (Lower Sioux, Prairie Island, Upper Sioux, Shakopee Mdewakanton):** check language teaching against the long absence after 1862.
- **Meskwaki Settlement School, College of Menominee Nation, Ho-Chunk language classes:** check what each nation's schools say they teach.
- **ELCA, Missouri Synod and Wisconsin Synod Lutherans; Catholic parish families:** check confirmation and catechism as distinct teaching methods.
- **Danish and Finnish heritage communities:** check folk schools and co-op study circles as adult learning.
- **Black Chicago and Detroit:** check the freedom schools of the 1963 Chicago school boycott and church teaching.
- **Little Village and Pilsen families:** check the 2001 hunger strike for a neighborhood high school, in organizers' own records.
- **Hmong and Somali families:** check *dugsi* and clan teaching beside public school, by generation.
- **Auto and steel towns (Detroit, Toledo, Gary):** check shop-floor and union apprenticeship.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for primers, Dewey and settlement teaching; mixed for trades; thin for *dugsi*, Hmong home teaching and Ho-Chunk programs, where community organizations and tribal education departments are the route.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania Dutch and German for Plain and Lutheran sources; Somali and Arabic for Qur'an schools; Hmong; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for immersion materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school files name children and living families; seek the nations' guidance. Plain communities limit outside study of their schools.
- **Held for review:** the 1972 Amish schooling case from Wisconsin goes to Capricorn and Libra, and institutional schooling to Sagittarius; nothing else was held.
