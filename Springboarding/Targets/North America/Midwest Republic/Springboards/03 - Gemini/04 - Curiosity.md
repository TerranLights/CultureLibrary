# Midwest Republic: Curiosity (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** English; Pennsylvania Dutch; German, Norwegian, Swedish, Polish, Czech and West Michigan Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** the wish to know, to find out and to explore, and how far a culture encourages, channels, limits or punishes it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **State-fair farm families and Chicago or Detroit museum audiences:** check which places of wonder each frequents and how often.
- **Anishinaabe knowledge keepers and university partners:** check what sky and plant knowledge each community opens and what it keeps closed.
- **Dakota, Ho-Chunk and Menominee nations:** check their repatriation claims to Midwestern museums and universities, in their own words.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements, and tourists in Shipshewana and Holmes County:** check curiosity in both directions, the visitors' gaze and the hosts' answer.
- **Hmong, Somali and Dearborn Arab residents:** check community reports on the personal questions strangers ask them.
- **Dayton and Detroit engineering families:** check tinkering lineages from NCR, Delco and the auto shops.
- **Central Sands of Wisconsin:** check the local memory of 1957 against the outside press.
- **Upper Peninsula and Northwoods rockhounds, foragers and deer-camp storytellers:** check how lore of the woods is passed on and to whom.
- **Lutheran, Catholic and Reformed congregations:** check sermons and catechism on inquiry against prying.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for fairs, museums and aviation; mixed for hobby clubs; thin for refugee and Native community views on questioning, where community newspapers and tribal cultural departments come first.
- **Languages needed:** English for most; Anishinaabemowin for sky and plant terms; Spanish, Arabic and Somali for "nosy" and "curious" in community talk.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some star and plant knowledge is not for every asker; use only what communities publish. The 1957 case and later Milwaukee and Chicago murders have victims' families still living.
- **Held for review:** a recent streaming drama on a Milwaukee murderer was weighed as a morbid-curiosity case and left out out of regard for victims' families; nothing fell under the standing rules.
