# Midwest Republic: Shelter (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Spanish; German; Polish; Swedish; Dutch; Bosnian; Arabic; Somali; Hmong; Dakota; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** cover and refuge from danger and the elements: a roof, a safe place for those who have none, and the duty to give it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Black Chicagoans of the public-housing era and their children**: check former residents' own oral histories against planners' records.
- **Somali Minneapolitans and Hmong St. Paulites**: check the first apartments after resettlement, as each community tells it.
- **Bosnians of St. Louis**: check shelter given by kin already settled against agency housing.
- **Urban Native people in Minneapolis and Chicago**: check how Native-run shelters frame need, compared with city counts.
- **Seasonal and migrant farmworkers in Michigan and Ohio** (Mexican and Mexican American): check camp housing conditions as workers describe them.
- **Ice anglers of Mille Lacs and Lake of the Woods; deer-camp hunters of the Northwoods**: check temporary shelters as folk building.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities**: check taking in kin after fire or loss, and barn and house rebuilding.
- **Rural Iowa and Indiana**: check hidden homelessness (doubling up, cars) against city counts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for counts, missions and Chicago housing history; thinner for rural hidden homelessness and for farmworker camps.
- **Languages needed:** German and Swedish for nineteenth-century charity reports; Spanish for Posadas and farmworker accounts; Somali, Hmong and Bosnian for resettlement memories; Dakota for lodge terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** names in eviction and shelter records; encampment disputes are live; tenancy and asylum law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** one pointer was set aside under the South Asian rule and stands here only as the category of South Asian-heritage scholars.
