# Midwest Republic: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Spanish; German; Polish; Norwegian; Czech; Finnish; Arabic; Somali; Hmong; Potawatomi and Myaamia (through the nations' own programs). **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Potawatomi (Pokagon, Forest County, Hannahville), Myaamia and Shawnee descendants**: check how each frames removal and return, in its own publications.
- **Anishinaabe families with boarding-school survivors**: check survivors' testimony against school records.
- **Menominee**: check termination as told by the people who lived it.
- **Black families of the Great Migration**: check children left with grandparents in the South and later reunions.
- **Mexican Chicagoans with kin in Mexico**: check *los que se quedan* from both ends of the migration.
- **Somali Minnesotans**: check *dhaqan celis* as parents and young people each describe it.
- **Hmong veterans' families**: check views on being left in 1975, by generation.
- **Detroit, Flint and Gary residents**: check how long-time residents name what happened to their cities, beside outsiders' terms.
- **Iowa and Upper Peninsula small towns**: check emptied main streets and farms as residents describe them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foster care, orphanages and Detroit property; mixed for migration's left-behind kin; boarding-school records are incomplete and scattered.
- **Languages needed:** German and Polish for orphan-home archives; Spanish for families split by migration; Somali and Hmong for diaspora accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school testimony and removal histories belong to the nations; foster and adoption files are sealed; the Rust Belt account and *dhaqan celis* are contested.
- **Held for review:** none; the attachment-research pointers for this concept were left general, so no named study had to be set aside.
