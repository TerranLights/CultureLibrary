# Midwest Republic: Motherhood (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Spanish; Polish; German; Pennsylvania Dutch; Swedish; Finnish; Czech; Dutch; Arabic; Somali; Hmong; Anishinaabemowin; Dakota. **Concept in one line:** being a mother, and the role, standing and ideals attached to it: bearing, raising and standing for a child; and the mother as a figure of the culture.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Anishinaabe and Dakota mothers and grandmothers**: check women as life-givers and keepers of language in each nation's teachings, without carrying one nation's teaching to another.
- **Black mothers in Bronzeville, Englewood and Detroit**: check church mothers, othermothers and activist mothers as separate threads.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican mothers in Chicago**: check Guadalupe devotion and *abnegada* ideals by origin and generation.
- **Polish and Czech Catholic mothers**: check Marian devotion and parish women's sodalities.
- **German, Scandinavian and Dutch Protestant farm mothers**: check farm magazines and church women's groups by synod or denomination.
- **Arab American mothers of Dearborn; Chaldean mothers in the Detroit suburbs**: check which Mother's Day is kept, and how motherhood is honored in each.
- **Hmong and Somali mothers**: check postpartum and birth customs as mothers describe them, by generation.
- **Amish mothers**: check large-family mothering and home birth by settlement.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, magazines and health surveys; thinner for Hmong, Somali and Meskwaki mothers' own accounts, which need community channels.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for parish devotion; Spanish for Guadalupe devotion and mothers' groups; Arabic, Hmong and Somali for mothers' interviews; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for teachings on mothers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** women's ceremonies of Native nations only as shared; Emmett Till and Margaret Garner are histories of violence against families; maternity law and benefits go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** none; the maternal-health and devotional pointers weighed here all fell inside the standing rules.

1. Read Schoolcraft's poems on her son, then the editor's notes on Ojibwe terms.

4. Search Chicago Spanish-language press for "Des Plaines" and "peregrinación".
5. Search nursing databases for "Hmong postpartum" and "Somali doula Minnesota".
