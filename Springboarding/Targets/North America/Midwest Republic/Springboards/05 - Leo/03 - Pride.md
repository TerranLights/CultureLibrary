# Midwest Republic: Pride (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; Norwegian, Swedish; Polish; Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** feeling and showing that oneself, one's work or one's own people have worth, from healthy self-respect to arrogance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Old Order Amish and Mennonite congregations:** check how *Hochmut* is preached against in each and where pride in a farm or craft is allowed.
- **Scandinavian Lutherans of Minnesota and Wisconsin and Dutch Reformed West Michigan:** compare sermon vocabulary on pride as sin.
- **Detroit's and Flint's autoworkers, the Iron Range's miners and Gary's steelworkers:** check pride in the trade and its loss, in each union's records.
- **Black Chicagoans, Detroiters and St. Louisans:** check how museums, churches and the Black press framed pride in each city.
- **Polish, Irish, Italian, Czech and Mexican Chicagoans:** compare parades, with each group's own committee records.
- **Puerto Ricans of Humboldt Park:** check the Paseo Boricua flags and the Puerto Rican People's Parade apart from Mexican sources.
- **Arab Americans of Dearborn and Chaldeans of Detroit's suburbs:** check whether pride in origin is voiced through church, mosque, club or business.
- **Hmong and Somali Minnesotans and Bosnian St. Louisans:** check how newer communities stage heritage pride, and who speaks for it.
- **Anishinaabeg, Dakota, Meskwaki and Ho-Chunk:** check pride in sovereignty and in veterans, nation by nation.
- **College towns against farm counties:** Big Ten rivalries against small-town booster clubs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for parades, archives and fiction; mixed for private boasting and humility, which needs sermons and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** Norwegian, Swedish, Dutch and Pennsylvania Dutch for church teaching on pride; Polish and Spanish for parade committees' papers; Hmong and Somali for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** settler pride in county histories sits on dispossession; the Rust Belt story is contested; supremacist groups' "pride" needs care.
- **Held for review:** laws on which flags may fly over state and city buildings are left to Libra.
