# Midwest Republic: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; Polish; Spanish; Arabic; Bosnian; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Potawatomi, Myaamia and Shawnee histories:** check how removed peoples are shown, or missing, in Indiana, Ohio and Illinois museums and textbooks.
- **Dakota and Ojibwe families of Minneapolis and the reservations:** check MMIW accounts on and off reservation separately.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** compare the Black press's self-portrait with mainstream coverage, decade by decade.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of metro Detroit:** check census, press and surveillance as three different kinds of being seen.
- **Somali Minnesotans:** check accounts of the 2014-15 federal pilot on extremism as accounts, with community voices first.
- **Hmong Minnesotans, Wisconsinites and Michiganders:** check portrayals in film and news against community media.
- **Amish of Holmes County, Elkhart-LaGrange and Kalona:** check tourism's pressure in each.
- **Bosnian St. Louisans:** check whether oral-history projects reach those who arrived as adults and as children.
- **Rural Iowa, the Upper Peninsula and the Iron Range:** check news deserts and "flyover" grievance as local accounts.
- **Disabled and elderly Midwesterners:** check isolation data by city and county.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for media, census and Black press; mixed for rural isolation; thin and sensitive for surveillance experiences.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Bosnian for evil-eye and visibility terms; Somali for community media; Anishinaabemowin for witnessing words; Pennsylvania Dutch for Amish views of outsiders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** MMIW cases involve grieving families; the Till images and funeral are a family's legacy; surveillance and extremism accounts are contested; Amish photographs need consent.
- **Held for review:** the legal basis of surveillance programs goes to Libra.
