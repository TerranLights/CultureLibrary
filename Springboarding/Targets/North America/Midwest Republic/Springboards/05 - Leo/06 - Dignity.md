# Midwest Republic: Dignity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; German; Polish; Czech; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** the worth a person has as a person, and the bearing and treatment that honor it; usually held to be inherent rather than earned.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish and Mennonite families:** check care of the old at home against nursing-home care nearby.
- **Flint, Detroit, Toledo and Gary industrial workers:** check union, company and family accounts of respect at work.
- **Black Chicagoans of the South Side and West Side:** check housing and policing accounts across generations.
- **Menominee, Shawnee and Dakota histories:** check how each nation's own historians tell termination, removal and 1862.
- **Mexican, Guatemalan and Somali meatpacking workers (Iowa, southern Minnesota):** check accounts of breaks, prayer and pace from the workers.
- **Hmong elders of St. Paul:** check dignity in hospitals and funerals from Hmong-led sources.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of metro Detroit:** check accounts of airport and street searches as accounts, not verdicts.
- **Disabled Minnesotans and Chicagoans:** check self-advocates' records, not only agencies'.
- **Rural Iowa and the Upper Peninsula's old:** check end-of-life care where hospitals are far apart.
- **Catholic and Lutheran charities:** check how each describes the people it serves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor history, housing and disability; mixed for the newest immigrant workers; thin for private elder care.
- **Languages needed:** Spanish and Somali for worker accounts; Hmong for elder care; German and Polish for church labor teaching; Anishinaabemowin for respect teachings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** institution and raid records hold personal data; 1862 and removal are contested ground; end-of-life accounts involve the dying.
- **Held for review:** assisted-dying statutes and labor law go to Libra (letter) and Capricorn (spirit).

## 5. First queries
1. Read Harrison's account of the August 1810 Vincennes council, then search for Shawnee-told versions.
2. Search the Studs Terkel Radio Archive for "dignity" and "respect".
3. Search the Genesee Historical Collections Center catalog for sit-down strike interviews.
4. Read Respecting Choices' published history and its La Crosse studies.
5. Search Iowa newspapers for "Postville" with "workers" in May-December 2008.
