# Midwest Republic: Humiliation (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; Norwegian; Polish; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Dakota. **Concept in one line:** being lowered in standing or worth against one's will, usually before others; also the act of lowering someone.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Dakota descendants in Minnesota and settler descendants of the Minnesota River valley:** check how each community's own histories narrate 1862.
- **Potawatomi (Pokagon, Forest County, and removed bands) and the Myaamia:** check removal memory, band by band.
- **Black Detroiters and Chicagoans:** check 1919, 1943 and 1967 accounts from residents first.
- **Old Order Amish and those who left:** check discipline as members and as former members describe it.
- **Kenosha, Flint, Toledo and Gary:** check plant-closing accounts by men and by their families.
- **Polish, Scandinavian, Finnish and Cornish Midwesterners:** check where ethnic jokes were felt as play and where as insult.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of Dearborn and the northern suburbs:** check post-2001 experiences in their own surveys; the debates around Dearborn are accounts, not verdicts.
- **Mexican Detroiters and Chicagoans:** check repatriation memory in family histories.
- **Hmong and Somali youth:** check school bullying and harassment reports by group.
- **LGBTQ youth in small towns:** check the student survey against urban data.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for 1862, 1967 and plant closings; mixed for everyday teasing; thin for shunning and family shaming, which are private.
- **Languages needed:** Dakota for community narratives; Pennsylvania Dutch for church discipline; Arabic, Hmong and Somali for shame vocabulary; Polish for the old press's response to slurs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1862, removal, boarding schools and 1967 are contested and grieving histories; ex-Amish accounts are personal; youth surveys protect identities.
- **Held for review:** the trials of 1862 and the criminal side of hazing go to Libra.

## 5. First queries

4. Read Dudley's chapters on workers' accounts of the Kenosha closing.
5. Pull Minnesota Student Survey bullying items for 2013-2022 by county.
