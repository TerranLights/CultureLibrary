# Midwest Republic: Generosity (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch; Norwegian; German; Polish; Spanish; Arabic; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** giving freely, beyond what is owed: money, goods, time, food, attention or praise.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements:** check work bees and church aid plans against outside insurance and charity.
- **Lutheran (several synods) and Catholic parishes:** compare Ladies' Aid, altar societies and parish festivals as giving systems.
- **Polish, Czech, Norwegian and Slovene fraternal members:** check lodge aid against church aid.
- **Dakota, Ho-Chunk, Ojibwe and Menominee communities:** check giveaways and nation-to-nation grants as each shares them, apart from casino-revenue debates.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** compare hometown associations and *padrino* sponsorship by origin.
- **Somali Minnesotans:** check remittance and mosque giving from community sources.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of metro Detroit:** check zakat, church giving and business-funded charity separately.
- **Hmong clans of St. Paul and Wisconsin:** check funeral and wedding contributions as clan obligations, from clan leaders' accounts.
- **Old-money Chicago, Cleveland and Minneapolis donors:** check named buildings and foundation boards against small-town giving.
- **Black churches of Chicago and Detroit:** check church-based aid during the Great Migration and after.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for foundations, fraternal societies and surveys; mixed for church giving; thin for informal and clan giving.
- **Languages needed:** Polish, Czech and Norwegian for lodge minutes; Arabic and Somali for religious giving; Spanish for hometown clubs; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for giveaway words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** giveaway customs belong to the nations; remittance records touch on security debates; donor files are private.
- **Held for review:** charitable-tax and gaming-revenue rules go to Libra.
