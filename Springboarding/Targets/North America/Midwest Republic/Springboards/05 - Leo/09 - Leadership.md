## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chicago against Milwaukee and Minneapolis:** machine politics against Progressive and Farmer-Labor traditions; check each city's own records.
- **Amish, Old Order Mennonite and assimilated Mennonite congregations:** compare lot, election and appointment.
- **Missouri Synod, Wisconsin Synod and ELCA Lutherans:** check pastor and lay leadership, and women's roles, synod by synod.
- **Polish, Irish, German and Mexican Catholic parishes:** check lay leadership and conflict with bishops.
- **Red Lake, Mille Lacs, Ho-Chunk, Menominee and Oneida governments:** check hereditary, clan and elected leadership, nation by nation.
- **Dakota communities of Minnesota:** check how leadership is described after 1862, from Dakota sources.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** compare church, union and political leadership.
- **Hmong clans and Somali elders and younger elected officials:** check generational change from each community's own accounts.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of metro Detroit:** check mosque, church and business leaders and who speaks for whom.
- **Auto, steel and meatpacking unions:** check local leadership against the internationals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for city politics, unions and churches; mixed for tribal and clan governance (shared selectively); thin for Somali elders' roles in print.
- **Languages needed:** Polish for parish disputes; Pennsylvania Dutch for church leadership terms; Hmong and Somali for clan and elder roles; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for leadership titles.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tribal leadership disputes are internal; machine-politics accounts are partisan; clan matters are private.
