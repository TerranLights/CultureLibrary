*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Illinois Country French descendants and Ste. Genevieve's neighbors:** check which colonial customs survive and who keeps them.
- **Amish and Mennonite communities:** compare singing, weddings and frolics as occasions of gladness.
- **Norwegian, Swedish and Finnish settlements:** check Christmas, Midsummer and sauna gatherings as joy, settlement by settlement.
- **Black churches of Chicago and Detroit:** check praise, gospel and homegoing services, where joy sits beside grief.
- **Puerto Rican and Mexican Chicagoans:** compare fiestas, parades and Día de Muertos.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans of metro Detroit:** check weddings, Eid and church feasts as separate sources.
- **Bosnian St. Louisans, Somali Minnesotans and Hmong families:** check what each community names as its happiest occasions.
- **Anishinaabeg, Dakota, Ho-Chunk and Menominee communities:** check harvest, powwow and homecoming as each shares them.
- **The Twin Cities, the Iron Range and the Upper Peninsula in winter:** check winter festivals against seasonal-gloom data.
- **Sports cities (Chicago, Detroit, Green Bay, Cleveland):** compare victory celebrations and their aftermath.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for festivals, music and surveys; mixed for private gladness; thin for Bosnian and Somali celebration in print.
- **Languages needed:** Illinois Country French for colonial customs; Norwegian and Pennsylvania Dutch for Christmas and hymn traditions; Bosnian, Somali, Hmong and Arabic for community celebration terms; Anishinaabemowin for words of gladness.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some harvest and ceremonial gatherings are not for outsiders; festival disputes in Dearborn and Humboldt Park are live.
- **Held for review:** fireworks and liquor-licensing rules that shape celebrations go to Libra.
