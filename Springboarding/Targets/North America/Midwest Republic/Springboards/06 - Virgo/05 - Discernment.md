# Midwest Republic: Discernment (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch, German, Dutch, Norwegian, Polish, Spanish, Arabic and Hmong community usage; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** the ability to tell things apart and judge well: true from false, good from bad, fine from coarse, the trustworthy from the untrustworthy, what matters from what doesn't.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Grain, livestock and dairy country (Iowa, southern Minnesota, Wisconsin):** check how grading by eye and grading by standard sit side by side.
- **Amish and Conservative Mennonite districts:** check how each district decides what to accept, and who speaks in that decision.
- **Reformed, Lutheran (Missouri Synod, WELS, ELCA), Catholic and Quaker congregations:** check how each judges belief and conduct, and how splits were explained.
- **Anishinaabeg, Dakota and Ho-Chunk:** check what each nation publishes about elders' counsel and teaching, and what it keeps private.
- **Hmong clans:** check how marriage and dispute choices are weighed by negotiators and elders.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican families in Chicago:** check how *consejos* from elders are given and heard.
- **Older adults in rural and small-city areas:** check who is targeted by scams and how communities warn each other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for grading, judging and church records; mixed for consumer fraud by group; thin for Native and Hmong frames of judgment outside community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Dutch and German for church records; Polish and Spanish for Catholic vocation material; Hmong for marriage negotiation; Anishinaabemowin for teachings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** dreams, visions and seeking guidance through ceremony are restricted in Anishinaabe and Dakota practice; do not seek them. Church discipline records name individuals; use with care. The Missouri Synod split is still contested.
- **Held for review:** the test-market literature was weighed for how it frames "average" Midwesterners; kept as a claim to test, never as a description.
