# Midwest Republic: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; German, Pennsylvania Dutch, Norwegian, Polish, Spanish, Arabic, Hmong and Somali community usage. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chicago, Detroit and Milwaukee newspaper traditions:** check how columnists and editorial pages criticized city machines and police, and who answered.
- **Small towns of Illinois, Ohio and Minnesota:** check how towns responded to novels and humor that criticized them.
- **Black Chicago and Detroit:** check how the Black press directed criticism outward and inward, and how Detroit 1967 is framed by competing accounts.
- **Potawatomi, Shawnee and Anishinaabe voices:** check each nation's own record of its leaders' criticisms, beside settler transcripts.
- **Norwegian- and Swedish-descended communities:** check whether *Janteloven* is cited at all, and by whom.
- **Arab, Chaldean and Hmong communities:** check how criticism inside the community differs from criticism aimed at outsiders.
- **Workplaces (auto plants, insurance offices, hospitals):** check how feedback is given across rank.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for press, criticism and satire; mixed for everyday family criticism; thin for Native and Somali forms outside community media.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic for Dearborn debate; Hmong and Somali for community criticism; Norwegian and German for older humor and press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Dearborn debates, Detroit 1967 and book challenges are contested ground: present each as an account. Teasing as correction in Native communities is each nation's to describe.
- **Held for review:** "Minnesota nice" humor was weighed as stereotype material; kept only as a claim to test.
