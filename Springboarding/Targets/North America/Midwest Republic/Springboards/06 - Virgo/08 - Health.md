# Midwest Republic: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch, German, Polish, Spanish, Arabic, Hmong and Somali community usage; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Seventh-day Adventists, Mennonites and Amish:** check how each group's teaching on diet and medicine shapes care choices.
- **Anishinaabeg, Menominee, Meskwaki, Potawatomi and Dakota:** check what each nation's health programs publish about their own medicine, and what they keep restricted.
- **Hmong families in St. Paul, Wausau and Sheboygan:** check how shamans and clinicians share care.
- **Somali families in Minneapolis and Columbus:** check how vaccine choices are discussed inside the community.
- **Arab and Chaldean Detroit:** check how faith, family and clinics divide health tasks.
- **Black Chicago, Detroit and Flint:** check how public-health crises are remembered and how trust is rebuilt.
- **Rural Iowa, the Upper Peninsula and southern Illinois:** check how hospital closures and distance shape care.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicago:** check where *curanderos*, *botánicas* and clinics overlap.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for clinics, public-health data and health reform; mixed for folk medicine in immigrant communities; thin and restricted for Native healing knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Hmong and Somali for current care beliefs; Arabic for Dearborn; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for medicine terms; Pennsylvania Dutch for folk healing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Midewiwin and other Native healing knowledge, Hmong ceremonies, and medical records are restricted; use only what is shared. The Flint crisis and the measles outbreak are contested ground.
- **Held for review:** Huron Smith's bulletins were weighed for consent problems in how plant knowledge was collected; kept, with the nations' views to come first.
