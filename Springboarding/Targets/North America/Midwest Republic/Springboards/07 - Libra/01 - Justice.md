*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chicago against downstate Illinois:** check how prosecutors' elections and the 2011 repeal vote split, since reform was argued along that line.
- **Detroit and its suburbs; Milwaukee's north and south sides:** check courts and policing records by neighborhood, with redlining and segregation read as contested accounts.
- **Black Midwesterners from the Great Migration onward:** check the Black press and church records as their own record of the courts, not only official data.
- **Dakota communities in Minnesota:** check how each community tells 1862 before using military-commission records.
- **Ojibwe treaty bands of Wisconsin, Minnesota and Michigan:** check court rulings beside each band's own resource codes.
- **Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Oneida and Meskwaki:** check each nation's court and code separately; the Menominee's termination and restoration is a case of its own.
- **Somali elders in Minneapolis; Hmong clan councils in St. Paul:** check whether community hearings run alongside the courts, and for which matters.
- **Rural Iowa and the Driftless Area:** check county-court and sheriff records against the metro picture.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, court data and exonerations; mixed for community justice in refugee communities; Native nations' own processes are theirs to share.
- **Languages needed:** Somali and Hmong to reach community hearings; Dakota and Anishinaabemowin for each nation's terms of law; the German and Finnish labor press for strike trials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1862 and the Mankato executions, Detroit 1967, Minneapolis 2020 and police torture in Chicago are contested; present every one as an account. Some tribal proceedings are closed.
- **Held for review:** third-party punishment games were weighed and left out, since the agent knows of none sampled in these states.
