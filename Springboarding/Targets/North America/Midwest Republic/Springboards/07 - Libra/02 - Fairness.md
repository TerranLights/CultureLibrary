*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **German- and Yankee-descended farm families in Illinois, Iowa and Wisconsin:** check inheritance and land-transfer records family type by family type, as Salamon framed them.
- **Amish and Mennonite families around Elkhart-LaGrange and Holmes County:** check how farms pass between children and how church districts handle quarrels over shares.
- **Dearborn's Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi families; Somali families of Minneapolis:** check how fixed Islamic inheritance shares meet state probate law when estates are divided.
- **Ojibwe and Menominee communities:** check rules for dividing the wild-rice and fish harvests as each community states them.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** check the fairness claims in housing and hiring through community archives and court filings.
- **Autoworkers in Flint, Detroit and Toledo; steelworkers in Gary and the Calumet:** check seniority and grievance files by plant.
- **Chicago neighborhoods, Bridgeport to Logan Square:** check whether the parking "dibs" custom is defended or opposed, and by whom.
- **Small-town and county-seat Indiana:** check the class-basketball debate by school size.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for labor, housing and survey records; thin for written rules of sharing among refugee communities; Native harvest rules belong to the communities.
- **Languages needed:** Arabic and Somali for inheritance practice; Polish and Dutch for church minutes on shares; Spanish for Chicago's fairness words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** redlining and contract buying are contested history; present them as accounts with sources, never as a group's character.
- **Held for review:** cross-society ultimatum-game studies were considered and left out, since their samples sit outside these states.
