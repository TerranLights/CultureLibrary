*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Driftless Area and central Wisconsin farm families:** check conservation-district records on how yield and soil were weighed over generations.
- **Menominee, Ojibwe of White Earth and Leech Lake, and Potawatomi of Forest County:** check each nation's own teaching on balance with land, as it publishes it.
- **Shift workers in Battle Creek, Flint, Toledo and the Calumet:** check how plant schedules shaped home time, plant by plant.
- **Amish of Elkhart-LaGrange and Ohio:** check how church rules on rest days and machines shape work hours.
- **Hmong families in St. Paul and Wisconsin:** check how healers and hospitals have negotiated care together.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check hot-cold and *espiritismo* practices separately for each.
- **Dearborn's Muslim and Chaldean Catholic families; Somali families in Minneapolis:** check fasting and feast calendars as balance of the year, community by community.
- **Swedish- and Finnish-descended Upper Midwesterners:** check whether terms such as *lagom* survive in heritage speech or only in marketing.
- **Swing counties of Ohio and Wisconsin against safe-seat metros:** check ticket-splitting rates.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for land, water, labor and election records; thin for folk health practices outside Chicago; Hmong and Ojibwe healing knowledge is partly restricted.
- **Languages needed:** Hmong for healers' terms; Spanish for *curanderismo*; Anishinaabemowin and Menominee for each nation's land teachings; German for the almanac layer.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** wolf hunts and manoomin rights are live disputes; ceremonial knowledge stays with the nations.
- **Held for review:** commercial wellness surveys from outside the region were looked at and dropped in favor of the federal time-use data.
