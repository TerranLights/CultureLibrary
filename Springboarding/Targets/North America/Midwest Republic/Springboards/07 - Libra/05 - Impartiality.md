*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chicago and Cook County against Minnesota and Wisconsin:** check ethics enforcement records side by side.
- **Small county seats in Iowa, Indiana and downstate Illinois:** check recusal motions and how often judges and parties share kin or church.
- **Amish church districts:** check how the lot is carried out and what happens when it is disputed.
- **Onʌyoteˀa·ká (Oneida), Hoocąk (Ho-Chunk), Omāēqnomenēwak (Menominee) and Anishinaabe (Ojibwe) courts *check* forms:** check each nation's written rules on kin, recusal and removing judges.
- **Somali families in Minneapolis:** check how mediators are chosen across clan lines.
- **Hmong families of St. Paul:** check whether disputants seek clan leaders from outside their own *xeem*.
- **Dearborn's Arab Americans:** check how *wāsṭa* is discussed against city hiring rules.
- **Black and Mexican job seekers in Milwaukee and Chicago:** check audit-study results by city and decade.
- **Polish and Irish Chicago wards:** check patronage rolls by ward in the machine era.

## 4. Flags
