*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Anishinaabeg and Dakota:** check each people's own accounts of council protocol and the pipe, kept apart from French and American records of the same councils.
- **The Myaamia and the Shawnee:** check treaty-council records of the Ohio and Wabash country against the Myaamia Center's own histories.
- **Twin Cities, rural Minnesota and the Red River Valley:** check whether the "nice" label is claimed, mocked or rejected in each.
- **Detroit's auto plants and Chicago's stockyards:** check union-management talks by industry and decade.
- **Dearborn's Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi residents; Detroit's Chaldeans:** check liaison with police and federal agencies for each group, as contested accounts.
- **Somali elders in Minneapolis; Hmong clan leaders in St. Paul:** check who speaks for the community to city government and how that role is chosen.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check children's interpreting and consular ties separately, since Puerto Ricans are U.S. citizens with no consulate.
- **German- and Scandinavian-descended Lutherans of several synods:** check etiquette in congregational disputes by synod.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaty, labor and foreign-policy records; mixed for everyday politeness (popular writing outruns research); thin for Somali and Hmong go-betweens outside community sources.
- **Languages needed:** Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for protocol terms; French for the colonial councils; Somali and Hmong for elders' roles; Arabic for Dearborn's civic press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pipe ceremonies and making-relatives rites are not for outside description in detail; the 1851 treaties and post-2001 surveillance are contested.
- **Held for review:** commercial "culture map" guides to directness were weighed and left out; they rate nations, not these communities.
