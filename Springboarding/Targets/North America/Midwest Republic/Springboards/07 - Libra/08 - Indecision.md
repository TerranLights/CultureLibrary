# Midwest Republic: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch; Polish; Norwegian, Finnish; Czech; Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Somali; Hmong. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish settlements of Elkhart-LaGrange, Holmes County and Kalona:** check how long the baptism choice may wait in each, and how districts differ.
- **Quaker meetings of Richmond and central Indiana:** check minutes for items held over across sessions.
- **Oneida and Ho-Chunk General Councils:** check how each handles quorum and decisions sent back for more talk.
- **Swing counties (Macomb, Kent, the Ohio suburbs) against safe districts:** check late-decider shares by county.
- **Hmong and Somali families:** check who decides in medical, marriage and school choices, and how long consultation runs.
- **Dearborn's Muslim and Chaldean families:** check how leaving an outcome to God appears in counseling and in family decisions.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check whether a family or a single member makes the large choices, generation by generation.
- **Flint, Detroit and Benton Harbor:** check emergency-manager decisions as contested accounts.
- **La Crosse and the Driftless Area against Chicago:** check advance-directive completion where the program began and where it did not.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for elections, legislatures and planning; mixed for families' decision customs; refugee communities' patterns are mostly in clinical literature.
- **Languages needed:** Hmong and Somali for family consultation; Pennsylvania Dutch for the baptism years; Arabic for religious framing; Spanish for Chicago families.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** emergency management and Flint are contested; Amish youth and Native councils are private communities, so rely on what they publish.
- **Held for review:** choice-overload lab studies were looked at and left out; none ties a finding to these states.
