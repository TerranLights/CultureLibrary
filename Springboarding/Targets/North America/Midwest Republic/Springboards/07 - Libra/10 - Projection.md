*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **German-, Czech- and Danish-descended Iowans and Wisconsinites:** check family and church records of 1917-1918 for how the suspicion was felt and remembered.
- **Polish and Italian Midwesterners:** check how ethnic jokes and slurs aimed at them were answered by fraternal and civic groups.
- **Black Midwesterners in Chicago, Detroit and Milwaukee:** check how the Black press answered blame after 1919, 1943 and 1967, reading each as an account.
- **Asian Americans of Detroit:** check the Vincent Chin case's place in community memory.
- **Hmong families of northern Wisconsin:** check press and community responses after the 2004 hunting shootings, as contested accounts.
- **Somali Minneapolitans and Dearborn's Arab Americans:** check community records on surveillance and suspicion.
- **Mexican families of Gary, East Chicago and Detroit:** check repatriation-era records and descendants' oral histories.
- **Myaamia, Shawnee, Lenape and Illinois-descended nations:** check how each tells the Prophet's era and the mascot debates.
- **Rural Wisconsin and Madison or Milwaukee:** check how each side's talk about the other is recorded in Cramer's and later studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in history, journalism and survey data; thinner on folk beliefs such as the evil eye, which live in oral practice and *botánicas*.
- **Languages needed:** German and Czech for the 1918 layer; Italian and Polish for folk medicine; Arabic, Somali and Hmong for living communities' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** riots, lynchings, the Klan, post-2001 surveillance and the Rust Belt account are contested; present each as an account and never as a group's character.
- **Held for review:** attribution studies comparing national newspapers were weighed and left out; their samples are not Midwestern.
