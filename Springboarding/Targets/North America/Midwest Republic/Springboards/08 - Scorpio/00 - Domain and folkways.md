## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Yankee-, German-, Scandinavian-, Czech- and Dutch-descended farm counties:** check estate and farm-transfer records, to extend Salamon's two-group comparison.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements** (Elkhart-LaGrange, Arthur, Kalona, Cashton): compare funeral and ban practice by affiliation, since each district sets its own *Ordnung*.
- **Black Midwesterners** (Bronzeville, Detroit, Milwaukee, St. Louis): check whether Great Migration families send their dead South.
- **Mexican Americans and Puerto Ricans:** compare sending bodies home with local burial.
- **Dearborn's Arab Americans and Detroit's Chaldeans:** separate by faith as well as origin.
- **Hmong, Somali and Bosnian communities:** check burial timing and cemetery access, and how war dead overseas are remembered.
- **Anishinaabeg (Ojibwe, Odawa, Potawatomi), Dakota, Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Meskwaki, Oneida of Wisconsin:** check each nation's own published accounts of mourning, power and renewal, kept apart rather than merged.
- **Polish, Czech, Irish and Italian Catholic parishes against Lutheran synods:** compare All Souls customs and burial societies.
- **Appalachian out-migrants** in Cincinnati, Dayton and Chicago's Uptown: check burial "back home," with Appalachia as comparison only.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for cities and settler-descended groups; mixed for Hmong, Somali and Bosnian communities; thin and community-controlled for Native nations. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet takes the Great Lakes states and the upper Mississippi Valley as the core.
- **Languages needed:** Polish, Norwegian and German for lodge and parish death records; Hmong and Somali for living practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ancestral remains; ceremonies closed to outsiders; Mankato 1862; Amish privacy; sex-research confidentiality. Inheritance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** Anishinaabe and Dakota funeral songs were weighed and left unnamed, since which recordings the nations allow in circulation is not known here.
