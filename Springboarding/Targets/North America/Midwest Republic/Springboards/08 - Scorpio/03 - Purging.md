## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish and Mennonite affiliations of Elkhart-LaGrange and Nappanee:** compare who is banned and how a shunned member returns, since the rules are local.
- **Dutch Reformed West Michigan and Missouri Synod Lutherans:** check church-discipline records for which offenses brought censure, decade by decade.
- **German-descended towns in Iowa, Wisconsin and Cincinnati:** check what was renamed or dropped in 1917-1918 and what returned later.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check where *limpia* and *despojo* are offered side by side and by whom, since one city holds both.
- **Finnish Upper Peninsula and Iron Range families:** check how the sauna is described across generations.
- **Black Detroit and Chicago neighborhoods cleared for highways and projects:** collect residents' accounts alongside planners' reports.
- **Dearborn's Muslims, Somali Minnesotans and Bosnian St. Louis:** compare fasting practice and how employers and schools respond.
- **Hmong households:** check how shamanic cleansing and hospital care are combined.
- **Anishinaabeg, Dakota and Meskwaki:** each nation's own published statements on sweat lodges and smudging, noting what each keeps closed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for temperance, Red Scare, Klan and environmental records; mixed for folk healing; restricted in places for Native ceremony.
- **Languages needed:** German for the WWI press; Dutch for consistory minutes; Spanish for healing practice; Arabic and Somali for fasting.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Klan; anti-German violence; repatriation and deportation; urban clearance; shunning; eating disorders. Expulsion and deportation law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** tribal enrollment disputes in the region were weighed; any such case should be read only through the nation's own published statements, so none is named.
