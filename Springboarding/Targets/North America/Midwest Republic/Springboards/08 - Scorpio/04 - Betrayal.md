# Midwest Republic: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; German; Polish; Swedish; Finnish; Spanish; Arabic; Bosnian; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin, Dakota, Sauk and Meskwaki, Potawatomi. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause one owed loyalty to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Sauk, Meskwaki, Potawatomi, Dakota and Ojibwe:** keep each nation's account of its treaties separate, since the documents and the grievances differ.
- **Finnish, Croatian and Italian Copper Country families:** check strike-era names for strikebreakers in each language.
- **Polish and Black packinghouse workers in Chicago:** check whom the Polish-language papers and the *Chicago Defender* each named as strikebreakers, 1904-1922.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters:** check how informing to police is talked about in oral histories, by generation.
- **Catholic parishes and evangelical megachurches:** compare how each handled abuse and leadership scandals in public.
- **Arab Americans and Chaldeans in metro Detroit:** check how loyalty questions after 2001 were met in community papers.
- **Hmong veterans' families:** compare the generation that fought with their grandchildren.
- **Bosnians of St. Louis:** check whether betrayal is named as personal or political.
- **Amish who leave and those who stay:** check former members' memoirs and the church's own view of leaving.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for treaties, strikes and scandals; mixed for infidelity (private by nature); thin in English for Hmong and Bosnian experience.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and Polish for strike-era papers; Hmong and Bosnian for oral history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** clergy abuse; the Hampton raid; the 1862 war; informant cases; affairs of living people. Treason and fraud law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** Dearborn's post-2001 informant cases were considered; reports vary too much to name one here, so they wait for the research stage.
