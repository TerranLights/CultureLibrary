*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; German; Polish; Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish; Czech; Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin; the languages of the Greenville and Prairie du Chien signatory nations. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Flint residents and Michigan state agencies:** compare who residents believed during the crisis and how that changed later.
- **Dearborn's Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi families and Detroit's Chaldeans:** check the Detroit Arab American Study by origin and faith, since trust may split along both.
- **Somali Minnesotans:** check how health workers and imams worked together, from both sides' accounts.
- **Black Chicagoans of the South and West Sides:** check medical-trust studies against the city's long hospital history.
- **Polish, Czech, Swedish and Norwegian fraternal members:** compare how each society screened members and paid claims.
- **Amish districts in Indiana and Iowa:** check aid plans against commercial insurance use.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check *tanda* and corner-store credit against bank use.
- **Myaamia, Shawnee, Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Meskwaki:** compare each nation's account of treaty councils, since each signed different papers.
- **Hmong families:** check trust in doctors and interpreters across generations.
- **Farm towns of Iowa and Wisconsin:** compare handshake dealing at auctions with written contracts now.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for surveys, treaties and the Flint record; mixed for everyday trust in towns; thin for Hmong and Somali in English.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and Scandinavian languages for fraternal minutes; Arabic and Somali for community media; Hmong for clinic studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Flint crisis and its litigation; vaccine outbreaks; post-2001 surveillance. Contracts go to Libra; trust in police and courts to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** Native-run lenders and community development funds in the region were weighed as trust pointers; none is known well enough here to name.
