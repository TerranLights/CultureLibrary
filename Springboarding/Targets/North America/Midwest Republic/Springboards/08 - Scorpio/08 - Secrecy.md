# Midwest Republic: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch; German; Norwegian; Finnish; Polish; Czech; Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin, Ho-Chunk. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden from others on purpose, and how what may, must or must not be hidden is judged; privacy included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Small towns of Ohio and Minnesota:** compare literary portraits with local oral histories, since novels are a biased witness.
- **Quaker, Free Black and Wesleyan networks of Indiana and Ohio:** check which ones kept written records of the Underground Railroad and which kept none.
- **Finnish, French-Canadian and Anishinaabe families of the Upper Peninsula:** check who passed healing charms and under what rule of silence.
- **Masons, Odd Fellows and Catholic and Reformed opponents of lodges:** compare what each side said about oaths of secrecy.
- **Gay Chicagoans of the 1920s through 1970s:** check bar raids, court records and memoirs for how double lives were kept.
- **Amish of Elkhart-LaGrange and Kalona:** check what members share with *Englischer* neighbors, writers and photographers.
- **Hmong veterans' families:** compare what the war generation tells children with what the children learn from books.
- **Dearborn's families:** check what counselors and imams say about covering faults versus reporting harm.
- **Chicago Outfit families and federal informants:** compare trial testimony with neighborhood silence.
- **Ho-Chunk, Menominee and Anishinaabe ceremonial societies:** record only what each nation says may be known.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature, lodges and federal cases; mixed for family secrets; closed by design for ceremonial knowledge.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish and French for UP charms; Hmong for war memory; Pennsylvania Dutch for Amish privacy.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Midewiwin and the Ho-Chunk Medicine Rite (older books published parts; do not retell); bearwalk accusations; outing; informants' families. Privacy and confidentiality law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the 2001 closures of Somali money-transfer offices in Minneapolis were weighed; kept back until community accounts can be set beside the official ones.
