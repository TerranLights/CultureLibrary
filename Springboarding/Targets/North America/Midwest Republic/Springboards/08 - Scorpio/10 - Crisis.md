## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iowa, Minnesota and Wisconsin farm families:** compare the 1930s and 1980s crises, since both left oral histories.
- **Flint, Youngstown and Gary workers:** check how plant closings are named by those who stayed and those who left.
- **Detroit retirees and newcomers:** compare accounts of the bankruptcy.
- **Lake towns of the Upper Peninsula and Wisconsin's Northwoods:** check memorials of fire and shipwreck as local calendars of crisis.
- **Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** check *ataque de nervios* in clinic studies against how families describe it.
- **Hmong, Somali and Bosnian refugees:** compare first-year accounts of arrival in St. Paul, Minneapolis and St. Louis.
- **Dearborn families:** check how wars in Lebanon, Yemen and Iraq reach households here.
- **Amish and Mennonite districts:** check how fire and storm losses are met by church aid and work bees.
- **Minneapolis neighborhoods around the 2020 memorial:** collect residents', business owners' and officials' accounts side by side.
- **Anishinaabeg:** record the Seven Fires only as each community chooses to tell it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters, epidemics and economic crises; mixed for private crises; sensitive for 2020 and the farm-crisis suicides.
- **Languages needed:** German and Norwegian for farm-crisis letters; Spanish for *ataque de nervios* studies; Hmong, Somali and Bosnian for refugee accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide; the 2020 events and policing (policing goes to Capricorn); the Rust Belt account; Anishinaabe prophecy.
- **Held for review:** Dearborn families' crises during wars abroad were weighed; no source is known here that documents them from the Dearborn side, so none is named.
