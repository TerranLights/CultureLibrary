## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inland North Yankee counties versus upland-Southern southern Illinois, Indiana and Ohio:** whether church order still splits along the settlement line.
- **Lutheran synods (Missouri, Wisconsin, ELCA, Laestadians of the Upper Peninsula and Iron Range):** synod lines track different ideas of doctrinal authority.
- **National Catholic parishes of Chicago, Milwaukee, Detroit, Cincinnati and St. Louis (German, Irish, Polish, Czech, Italian, Mexican, Puerto Rican):** how founding nationality shaped parish life.
- **Dutch Reformed West Michigan:** its confessional publishing and schools as a separate subculture.
- **Amish and Mennonite settlements (Holmes County, Elkhart-LaGrange, Kalona):** compare affiliations, not "Plain" as one group.
- **Black Midwesterners of Bronzeville, Detroit, Gary and St. Louis:** from historic Baptist churches to the Nation of Islam and the Moorish Science Temple, both founded in the region (*check*).
- **Dearborn's Lebanese, Yemeni and Iraqi Muslims; Detroit's Chaldeans; Hmong, Somali and St. Louis Bosnians:** separate by rite and arrival period.
- **Anishinaabeg (Ojibwe, Odawa, Potawatomi), Dakota, Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Oneida, Meskwaki; removed Myaamia and Shawnee:** each nation's own account of ceremonial and Christian life.
- **Appalachian out-migrants in Cincinnati, Dayton and Chicago's Uptown:** their churches as a distinct urban layer.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for denominations and immigrant churches; mixed for Muslim, Hmong and Somali life; thin and properly gated for Native ceremony.
- **Languages needed:** German and Pennsylvania Dutch for colony and synod papers; Nordic languages, Polish, Czech and Dutch for church press; Arabic and Hmong for recent communities; Native languages through each nation's programs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Midewiwin knowledge and birchbark scrolls belong to their keepers. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this pass writes for the Great Lakes and upper Mississippi core, with Appalachian Ohio, the Plains, the CSA and New England as comparison only.
- **Held for review:** this domain's survey of the region's faiths leaves two groups of pointers out, carried as categories alone: Buddhist traditions, pending the author's ruling, and South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
