*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Plant towns (Kenosha, Flint, Youngstown, Gary, Toledo) against white-collar Chicago, Columbus and Minneapolis:** check how losing or keeping industrial work is narrated as losing or keeping a point.
- **Iowa and southern Minnesota farm families after the 1980s farm crisis:** what interviews and rural ministries recorded about land, inheritance and purpose.
- **Iron Range and Upper Peninsula mining towns of Finnish, Slovene, Italian and Cornish descent:** compare accounts after mine closures.
- **Great Migration families in Bronzeville and Detroit:** check how meaning attaches to the move north and to church and union life.
- **Dutch Reformed and Lutheran college towns (Holland, Grand Rapids, Northfield, Decorah):** compare doctrines of calling in their own teaching materials.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicago:** separate first-generation work narratives from second-generation ones.
- **Hmong elders and their American-born children:** where each locates a worthwhile life.
- **Ojibwe and Ho-Chunk communities:** each nation's own teachings on living well, against outsider ethnography.
- **Dearborn and Somali Minneapolis:** check sermons and community writing for purpose framed through faith and family.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for work and deindustrialization (oral histories, ethnographies, labor archives); mixed for recent refugee groups; thin and community-controlled for Native teachings.
- **Languages needed:** Hmong for sung poetry and elder interviews; Finnish and Slovene-heritage sources for the Range; Spanish for first-generation testimony; Anishinaabemowin for teaching terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide and overdose data concern grieving families; the Rust Belt account and the "despair" framing are contested, and some residents reject them.
- **Held for review:** the popular "purpose" bestsellers were weighed and left out, since none is tied to the region; only Midwestern-made media were kept.
