# Midwest Republic: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; German; Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish; Polish, Czech, Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Meskwaki, Kickapoo (historical). **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Adams County and similar Mississippi River counties with German, Yankee and Black settlers:** check whether folk-belief records separate by community or blend.
- **Lutheran and Reformed confirmation classes in German, Norwegian, Dutch and English:** compare what changed in the taught creed when the language changed.
- **Czech freethinkers of Chicago and Cedar Rapids against Czech Catholic parishes:** a split inside one immigrant group over belief itself.
- **Detroit's Catholics, Protestants and Jews of the 1950s against their grandchildren:** rerun the Detroit Area Study questions.
- **Lebanese and Iraqi Shia, Yemeni Sunni and Chaldean Catholic Detroiters:** separate belief items by sect and arrival.
- **Hmong households keeping spirit rites, Christian Hmong and mixed households:** check how each describes the spirits' reality.
- **Kickapoo and Meskwaki:** each nation's own position on which old texts may be used.
- **Black Chicagoans in the Great Migration's new movements versus historic congregations:** compare stated creeds.
- **Spiritualist and other alternative-belief communities of Indiana and Michigan:** check how they recruit and describe themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for creeds, folklore archives and Detroit surveys; mixed for Hmong and Somali belief (community and ethnographic sources); outsider-collected Native texts need the nations' review.
- **Languages needed:** German, Dutch and Norwegian for confirmation materials; Czech for freethought papers; Hmong and Arabic for recent communities; Meskwaki for the texts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Meskwaki and Kickapoo religious texts may carry restrictions; Hmong spirit practice has been misreported in the press; the Moorish Science and Spiritualist sources need their members' own accounts beside critics'.
- **Held for review:** paranormal and legend pointers (dogman and lake-monster lore) were weighed and kept out of the table as more suited to Pisces's magic ways.
