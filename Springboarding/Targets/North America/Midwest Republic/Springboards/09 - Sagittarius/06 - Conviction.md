## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish, Mennonite and Brethren communities in Ohio, Indiana and Iowa:** compare how each recorded wartime refusal in 1917-18 and 1941-45.
- **Quakers of Richmond, Indiana, and Oberlin's Congregationalists:** check how antislavery conscience was framed in each body's own minutes.
- **Methodist and Baptist temperance towns versus German and Irish Catholic and German Lutheran wards:** read both sides' papers on prohibition.
- **Flint and Detroit autoworkers, by generation and by origin (Black, Polish, Appalachian):** whose stand is remembered, and how.
- **Keweenaw Finns, Croats and Italians and the mine company's supporters:** check the divided memory of 1913.
- **Milwaukee's North and South Sides, 1967-68:** compare marchers' and residents' accounts of the same marches.
- **Dakota descendants in Minnesota and the Dakotas, and settler descendants in Mankato and New Ulm:** how the 1862 memory is kept.
- **Shawnee and Myaamia nations:** each nation's own framing of the 1800s resistance.
- **Lebanese, Iraqi and Yemeni voters in Dearborn and Hamtramck:** separate the precinct results by community; do not merge them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for church, temperance, labor and civil-rights archives; mixed for recent political conviction; Shawnee sources sit with nations now in Oklahoma.
- **Languages needed:** German and Pennsylvania Dutch for peace-church records; Finnish and Italian for Keweenaw strike sources; Arabic for Dearborn civic media; Dakota for ride and memorial materials.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 1862, the Milwaukee marches and the 2011 and 2024 disputes are contested and involve living people; Italian Hall's deaths (mostly children) are a grief site.
- **Held for review:** draft-era court trials were noted but kept out, the law side being Libra's and Capricorn's; one further conscience pointer was set aside and is held only as a category: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
