## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Flagship and land-grant towns (Madison, Ann Arbor, Champaign-Urbana, Ames, Columbus) against small farm counties:** check how far "going away to school" means leaving for good.
- **Amish affiliations in Ohio, Indiana and Iowa:** check how each regards schooling beyond eighth grade (the court side goes to Libra).
- **Lutheran, Catholic, Reformed and Jewish college and seminary networks:** compare what each said higher learning was for.
- **Finnish and other labor-movement schools of the Iron Range and Upper Peninsula:** education outside the church and the state.
- **Black students of Wilberforce, Central State and Chicago's city colleges:** compare access across eras.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicago:** first-generation students and the campus struggles of the 1970s.
- **Hmong, Somali and Arab American families of St. Paul, Minneapolis and Dearborn:** check how community centers track college-going.
- **Menominee, Ojibwe, Ho-Chunk and the removed Myaamia:** each nation's own aims for tribal colleges and language programs.
- **Appalachian out-migrant families in Cincinnati, Dayton and Columbus:** college-going in the second generation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for universities and their archives; rich attainment data; thinner for Hmong and Somali higher-education studies; Native data in tribal-college and nation sources.
- **Languages needed:** Finnish for Work People's College; Polish and Dutch for seminary and school records; Myaamia, Anishinaabemowin and Menominee for language-program materials; Hmong and Somali for family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the land-grant land account is contested by universities and nations alike; tribal-college enrollment data may be the nations' to release.
- **Held for review:** schooling below college (immersion schools, school readers) was weighed and left to Gemini.
