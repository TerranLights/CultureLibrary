## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Inland North against Midland settlement streams** (Michigan, northern Ohio, Wisconsin beside central Indiana, Illinois, Missouri): check township records and church polity for different sources of accepted authority.
- **Chicago against central and southern Illinois towns:** compare ward records with county courthouse records for how power is reached.
- **Amish and Mennonite districts** (Elkhart-LaGrange, Holmes County, Ohio, Kalona, Iowa): separate affiliations, since each district's *Ordnung* is set locally.
- **Anishinaabeg (Ojibwe, Odawa, Potawatomi), Dakota, Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Oneida of Wisconsin, Meskwaki:** each nation's own governance documents, read nation by nation; the removed Myaamia and Shawnee through their present nations' archives.
- **Black Midwesterners of Bronzeville, Detroit, Milwaukee, St. Louis:** compare church, union and civic leadership records across cities.
- **Hmong of St. Paul and Wisconsin, Somali of Minneapolis, Bosnians of St. Louis:** check how clan, elder and mosque authority sits beside city and school authority.
- **Polish, Czech, German and Dutch congregations:** compare Catholic and Lutheran parish governance with Reformed consistories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for big-city labor, church and survey records; thinner and community-held for the smaller Native nations. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign is written for the Great Lakes states and the upper Mississippi Valley.
- **Languages needed:** English for most sources; German, Norwegian, Polish and Czech for the 19th-century immigrant press; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for each nation's governance terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Midewiwin birchbark scrolls and some teachings are restricted; ask, do not seek out. The U.S.-Dakota War of 1862 and the 1830s removals are contested ground; record each account as an account.
- **Held for review:** business-culture guides and refugee orientation handbooks stay unnamed source types; no Midwest-specific title is certain.
