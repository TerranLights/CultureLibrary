## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Missouri Synod, Wisconsin Synod and ELCA Lutherans:** compare catechism editions and polity for how far pastors and voters' assemblies decide.
- **Christian Reformed and Reformed Church in America congregations of West Michigan:** check consistory records for how church authority reaches into family and school.
- **Amish affiliations of Elkhart-LaGrange and Holmes County:** separate districts, since a bishop's reach is set locally.
- **Ho-Chunk, Menominee, Oneida of Wisconsin, Meskwaki, Anishinaabe bands:** read each constitution and council record separately; check where hereditary or clan roles sit beside elected ones.
- **Chicago, Detroit, Milwaukee, Minneapolis, St. Louis (with Ferguson):** compare police-oversight records city by city; each crisis is a contested account.
- **Hmong clans, Somali elders, Dearborn mosque boards, Chaldean parishes:** check how community authority meets city, school and court authority.
- **Iron Range and Copper Country miners:** compare company-town and union records for who directed daily life.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for police, church and Detroit survey records; thin and partly closed for clan and elder authority in Hmong and Somali communities.
- **Languages needed:** German and Dutch for synod and consistory records; Hmong and Somali for community leadership; Anishinaabemowin for women's leadership terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** police-killing cases, Detroit 1967 and Menominee termination are contested; treat each as an account. Hmong clan disputes may be private.
- **Held for review:** obedience experiments are left for the research stage, as no Midwestern replication is known for certain.
