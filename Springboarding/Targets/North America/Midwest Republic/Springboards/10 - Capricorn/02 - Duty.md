# Midwest Republic: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch; German; Norwegian, Finnish; Polish; Chicago Spanish (Mexican and Puerto Rican); Dearborn Arabic; Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin; Dakota. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Amish and Mennonite districts (LaGrange, Holmes, Kalona):** compare who houses aging parents and who pays medical bills when church aid replaces insurance.
- **German Catholic and Lutheran counties of Wisconsin and Ohio:** check Civil War and World War I records for divided loyalties at the parish level.
- **Black families of the Great Migration in Chicago and Detroit:** look at letters and church records for duties owed to kin still in the South.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans:** separate hometown-association duties from island-bound ones; the two run through different networks.
- **Hmong families in St. Paul, Wausau and Sheboygan:** check funeral and clan obligations against workplace leave policies.
- **Dakota, Ho-Chunk, Ojibwe bands:** read each nation's own statements on duties to land, water and ancestors, separately.
- **Iron Range and Copper Country Finns:** compare cooperative and church duties with company paternalism.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Civil War, World War I and caregiving statistics; thin for Somali and Yemeni remittance practice in public sources.
- **Languages needed:** Norwegian for letters home; German for wartime parish records; Somali, Hmong and Arabic for present-day obligation talk; Anishinaabemowin for water teachings.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1862 executions and Dakota memorial observances; some Ojibwe water and harvest teachings belong to ceremonies; Somali money-transfer cases touch federal prosecutions.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside; Chicago's hometown clubs carry the communal-work strand the block draws from Mexican towns.
