## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Flint, Detroit and Benton Harbor residents:** check how blame for water and city finance is assigned in each city's own press and hearings.
- **Youngstown, Toledo and Gary steelworkers' families:** separate union, company and city records on who answered for the plant closings.
- **Rural Wisconsin and Iowa against Milwaukee and Des Moines:** compare talk of personal against shared responsibility in local papers and town meetings.
- **Irish Chicagoans and the fire's memory:** see how parish and Irish-American societies handled the blame placed on them.
- **Arab Americans of Dearborn and Chaldeans of Detroit:** check community statements after 2001 for how collective responsibility was answered.
- **Menominee, Potawatomi and Ojibwe stewardship programs:** read each nation's forestry, water and wild-rice plans on its own terms.
- **Immigrant children in Mexican, Hmong and Somali households:** look for studies of children's adult tasks, community by community.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for disasters and inquiries (fire, Eastland, Cuyahoga, Flint); thin for Somali and Hmong family responsibility in published sources.
- **Languages needed:** French for the Kaskaskia records; Czech and Polish for Eastland victims' communities; Menominee and Potawatomi stewardship terms from the nations themselves.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Flint litigation is ongoing; post-2001 surveillance of Dearborn is contested; deaths in 1915 touch living families.
- **Held for review:** nothing set aside. Apologies from outside these lands did not carry over; the 1997 Chicago fire resolution is the local act of redress to start from.
