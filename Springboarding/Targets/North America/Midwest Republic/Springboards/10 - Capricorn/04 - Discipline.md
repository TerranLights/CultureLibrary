## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **German Turners and Czech Sokols:** compare drill manuals and festival programs to see how each society framed bodily discipline.
- **Amish, Old Order Mennonite and Christian Reformed congregations:** check the records of church discipline (the ban, *tucht*) for who is corrected and how.
- **Ojibwe, Odawa, Menominee and Ho-Chunk families with boarding-school histories:** read each nation's survivor projects separately; schools included Mount Pleasant, Tomah, Pipestone and Harbor Springs.
- **Black Chicagoans and Detroiters in church, gym and school:** look for community-run discipline next to school-imposed discipline.
- **Mexican, Puerto Rican, Hmong and Somali parents:** check how each community discusses spanking and child-welfare law, without assuming one view.
- **Factory towns (Dearborn, Pullman, Kohler, Gary) against farm counties:** compare clock-based workplace discipline with seasonal farm routines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for company towns, prisons and school data; mixed for church discipline (consistory minutes are often closed for decades).
- **Languages needed:** German and Czech for gymnastics society records; Dutch for older consistory minutes; Hmong and Somali for parenting talk; Anishinaabemowin terms only as communities share them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** boarding-school abuse (survivors first, no names without consent); fasting rites; Amish shunning cases; prison isolation.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation training in the nation's cities, held as a category for the self-discipline facet until the author decides on Buddhism; nothing named.
