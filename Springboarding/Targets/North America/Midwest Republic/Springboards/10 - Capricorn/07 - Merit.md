## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Chicago patronage wards against Wisconsin and Minnesota civil-service traditions:** check hiring rules and scandal records for where connections and exams each decide.
- **Black professionals in Detroit, Chicago's Bronzeville and St. Louis's Ville:** compare earned credentials with the housing and hiring barriers recorded against them.
- **Amish and Old Order Mennonite communities:** look at what counts as earned skill (craft, farming) when formal credentials are refused.
- **Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicagoans in city hiring:** check the patronage-organization records and the community press's own debates.
- **Iraqi, Lebanese, Yemeni and Chaldean professionals in Detroit; Bosnians in St. Louis:** compare how licensing and credential recognition worked for each group.
- **Potawatomi, Ho-Chunk and Ojibwe powwow circuits:** separate each host nation's rules for honoring dancers, drums and veterans.
- **Farm counties of Iowa and Indiana:** compare 4-H and FFA judging records over time.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for court records, testing and school policy; thin for how immigrants' credentials were weighed before the 1990s.
- **Languages needed:** Polish, Czech and Spanish for talk of connections; Arabic and Bosnian for credential stories; Potawatomi and Anishinaabemowin terms for honors only as the nations share them.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** affirmative action, patronage trials and the Sweet case are contested; eagle feathers and some honors are sacred, so describe the judging, not the ceremony.
- **Held for review:** an open question rather than a held item: legacy admissions at Michigan and Illinois state universities, with no record in hand.
