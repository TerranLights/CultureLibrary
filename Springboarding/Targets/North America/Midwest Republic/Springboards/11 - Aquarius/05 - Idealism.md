# Midwest Republic: Idealism (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Amana German and Pennsylvania Dutch; Swedish; French (Icarian records); Dutch; Spanish; Arabic; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** living and judging by ideals (principles, and visions of how things ought to be) held above present interest or practicality, including visions of a better society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **German, Swedish and French utopian colonies (Amana, Harmony, Bishop Hill, the Icarians):** check each in its own language's records, not only in English summaries.
- **Shakers at Union Village (Ohio) and the Zoar Separatists at the eastern edge:** check as comparison cases for celibate and Separatist ideals.
- **University towns (Madison, Ann Arbor, Iowa City, Oberlin) and nearby farm counties:** check service and third-party records as traces of different ideals.
- **Puerto Rican Humboldt Park and its independence ideal:** contested; check the Puerto Rican Cultural Center's *check* own publications beside outside accounts.
- **Christian Reformed West Michigan and Benedictine central Minnesota:** check how each church's institutions put ideals into schools and work.
- **Ojibwe and Menominee educators:** check how each nation publishes its teachings; do not merge them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for nineteenth-century colonies (their records survive well); mixed for present-day ideals outside churches and universities; thin for Somali and Arab American writing on idealism in English.
- **Languages needed:** German (including Amana's own dialect), Swedish and French for colony records; Dutch for Kuyperian sources; Anishinaabemowin terms checked with Ojibwe speakers' published dictionaries.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some Anishinaabe teachings are shared only in ceremony; colony records include private confessions and discipline cases; ideals of the Heartland have been used in contested political arguments, to be read as claims.
- **Held for review:** famous utopias just outside the core (the Oneida Community of New York, the Hutterite colonies of the Plains) were left to the sheets of the nations whose lands hold them.
