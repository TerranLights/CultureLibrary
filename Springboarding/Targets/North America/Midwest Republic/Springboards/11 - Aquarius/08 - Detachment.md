# Midwest Republic: Detachment (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern English; Pennsylvania Dutch and German; Norwegian, Finnish; Polish; Spanish; Arabic (including Yemeni usage); Hmong; Somali; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** keeping distance (emotional, personal or social) from people, from one's own feelings, or from outcomes; some forms are prized (calm, objectivity, equanimity) and others condemned (coldness, indifference, withdrawal).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Northwoods and Upper Peninsula hunters and anglers:** check whether deer shacks and ice shanties serve as solitude or as group sociability, from users' own accounts.
- **Norwegian and Finnish Lutheran towns and Polish and Italian Catholic parishes:** check claims of reserve or warmth as outsiders' claims; never adopt them.
- **Amish and Old Order Mennonite communities:** check how separation from the world and closeness inside the church district are taught together.
- **Hmong elders in St. Paul and Wausau:** check community health sources on isolation after resettlement, from the elders' own accounts.
- **Yemeni and Lebanese men in Dearborn's Southend:** check songs and letters on separation from home and family.
- **Rural farm counties and Detroit or Chicago neighborhoods:** check one-person households and loneliness items side by side; the Rust Belt account of withdrawal is contested.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for literature and nature writing on solitude; mixed for measured loneliness (recent state surveys only); thin for Somali and Hmong experiences in published form.
- **Languages needed:** Norwegian and Finnish for immigrant letters; Pennsylvania Dutch and German for Anabaptist terms; Arabic, Hmong and Somali for present-day community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** isolation research touches mental health and suicide; Anishinaabe fasting and solitary vision practice is ceremonial and not public; shunning cases involve living families.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation teaching in the region's cities came up for contemplative detachment; it is an open question for the author, so it is held as a category, with no center or text named.
