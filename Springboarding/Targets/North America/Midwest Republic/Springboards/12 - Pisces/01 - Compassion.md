# Midwest Republic: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Spanish, Polish, German, Norwegian, Dutch, Arabic, Somali, Hmong; Anishinaabemowin. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Amish and Mennonite settlements against Lutheran and Catholic towns: check how far help reaches beyond one's own church.
- Black churches and mutual-aid societies of Bronzeville and Detroit: check self-organized care against charity received from outside.
- Mexican and Puerto Rican Chicago: check hometown associations, Las Posadas and remittances as mercy across a border.
- Arab Dearborn (Lebanese, Yemeni, Iraqi) and Detroit's Chaldeans: check zakat committees against church charity.
- Somali Minneapolis and Hmong St. Paul: check what clan and kin are expected to give, and what goes to strangers.
- Anishinaabeg, Dakota, Ho-Chunk and Menominee: check giveaway and feeding customs only as each nation describes them.
- Norwegian and Swedish Lutherans: check the deaconess movement and its hospitals.
- Appalachian out-migrants in Uptown Chicago: check how they describe the aid offered them.
- Farm towns against the big cities: check benefit suppers against citywide agencies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for giving and food-bank statistics; mixed for church and parish records (scattered); thin for Somali and Hmong records in their own languages.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and German for parish and synod papers; Arabic and Somali for mosque materials; Hmong for funeral and clan records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Till photographs and family wishes; Native giving ceremonies; names of recipients in charity case files.
- **Held for review:** 1990s field experiments on helping strangers in U.S. cities were weighed; which Midwestern cities they sampled is uncertain, so they wait for a check rather than taking a row.
