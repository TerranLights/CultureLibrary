# Midwest Republic: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Pennsylvania Dutch, German, Norwegian, Polish, Spanish, Arabic, Somali, Hmong. **Concept in one line:** seeking relief from a reality felt as harsh, dull or confining, through fantasy, intoxication, entertainment, travel, withdrawal or disappearance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Amish and Mennonite affiliations: check how much running around each allows and what Amish writers say of it.
- Wisconsin and Minnesota tavern towns beside dry or nearly dry Reformed and Mennonite communities: check how escape by drink is spoken of.
- Black Chicago and Detroit: check blues clubs, Bronzeville's Stroll and the policy wheel as residents remember them.
- Polish and German neighborhoods: check taverns, halls and picnics as places of release.
- Cabin owners and those without access: check who goes "Up North" and who works the resorts.
- Ho-Chunk, Potawatomi and Ojibwe nations: check how each speaks of its casinos and of its own sobriety and wellness work.
- Arab Dearborn, Somali Minneapolis: check hookah lounges and khat as debated inside each community.
- Hmong youth in St. Paul: check their own accounts of leisure and games.
- Appalachian out-migrants in Uptown Chicago and Detroit: check taverns and country-music bars as home-places.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for substance-use surveys, gaming revenue and temperance archives; mixed for leisure ethnography; thin for Somali and Hmong sources on leisure.
- **Languages needed:** Pennsylvania Dutch for Amish youth sources; Arabic and Somali for café and khat debates; Polish for tavern history.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** minors in survey data; khat's legal status (Libra); Native nations' gaming finances (their own disclosures only).
- **Held for review:** a much-shared ranking of "drunkest" Midwestern cities from a commercial site was weighed and left out; its method is unclear, and federal surveys stand in its place.
