# Midwest Republic: Spirituality (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Midwest Republic, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Midwestern American English; Polish, German, Czech, Finnish, Spanish, Arabic, Somali, Hmong; Anishinaabemowin, Dakota, Ho-Chunk. **Concept in one line:** a person's or group's relation to what they take as sacred, transcendent or ultimate, inside or outside organized religion (institutions belong to Sagittarius; this sheet takes experience, practice and the sacred outside the church).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Anishinaabeg, Ho-Chunk, Menominee and Dakota: check only what each nation publishes; Midewiwin and other lodge knowledge stays closed.
- Shawnee and Myaamia: check how renewal movements are remembered in each nation's own histories.
- Black Chicago and Detroit: check Spiritual churches, gospel worship and Muslim movements as members describe them.
- Mexican, Puerto Rican and Polish Catholics: check pilgrimages and patronal feasts, parish by parish.
- Belgian Walloons of the Green Bay area: check the Champion shrine against other farm-country devotions.
- Lutheran synods (ELCA, Missouri, Wisconsin) and Finnish Laestadians of the Upper Peninsula: check private devotion and revivals.
- Czech freethinkers and the unaffiliated in Twin Cities and Madison: check what they hold sacred, if anything, in their own words.
- Arab Dearborn (Shia and Sunni), Chaldeans, Somali Minneapolis, Bosnians of St. Louis: check personal piety apart from mosque or church institutions.
- Hmong of St. Paul: check traditional household practice against Hmong Christian congregations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for survey data and Catholic shrines; mixed for Black and Muslim movements (good histories, few datasets); thin for Somali and Hmong practice in print.
- **Languages needed:** Polish and German for parish devotion; Czech for freethinker papers; Arabic for Dearborn; Anishinaabemowin and Dakota for teachings shared publicly.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Midewiwin and other closed ceremonies (respected, not researched); peyote ceremonies of the Native American Church; apparition claims under church review.
- **Held for review:** Buddhist meditation centers and temples in these lands are held as a category, pending the author's ruling; one further pointer, from South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, was set aside under the standing rule.
