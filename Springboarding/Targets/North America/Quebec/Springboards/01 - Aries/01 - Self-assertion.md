# Quebec: Self-assertion (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French, Quebec English, Innu-aimun, East Cree, Mi'gmaq, Inuktitut, Haitian Creole, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** declaring and acting on one's own existence, wishes and claims in the presence of others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Working-class francophones of east-end Montreal and of the Quebec City region: compare recorded speech and call-in radio audiences before assuming one style.
- English-speaking Quebecers of the West Island, the Townships and the Gaspé coast: check how advocacy groups and individual memoirs differ.
- Italian families of Saint-Léonard: the 1968-69 school-language dispute is a social event to read for how parents pressed their claims.
- Haitian youth of Montréal-Nord and Saint-Michel: compare community-organization records with media accounts of them.
- Innu of Uashat mak Mani-utenam and Mi'gmaq of Listuguj: check each nation's own texts and films against hearing transcripts.
- Inuit of Nunavik and Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: compare consultations they designed with ones run by outside bodies.
- Women and men at union meetings and in the *Cercles de fermières*: who held the floor.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for francophone speech corpora and public hearings; mixed for immigrant communities; thin in print for Indigenous terms of assertion, which sit with each nation's language bodies.
- **Languages needed:** Quebec French and its colloquial registers; Innu-aimun and Mi'gmaq for community sources; Yiddish and Haitian Creole for community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the language laws and the 1980 and 1995 referendums are contested ground; treat them as social events only and send the law to Libra and Capricorn. The *radio-poubelle* label is itself a contested judgment.
- **Held for review:** in looking for speech corpora and assertiveness literature, no pointer was set aside; the self-help shelf was left as a query because Quebec titles could not be named with confidence.
