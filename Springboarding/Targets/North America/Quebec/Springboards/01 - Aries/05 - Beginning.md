## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Quebec City, Montreal and the Gaspé: each claims a founding moment; check how local plaques and festivals word it.
- Mi'gmaq of Gespeg, Wendat of Wendake, Kanien'kehá:ka and Innu: compare each people's own origin accounts with the settler founding stories set on their lands.
- Inuit of Inukjuak and other Nunavik communities: compare relocation accounts with the move to permanent villages.
- Irish Quebecers and the descendants of Loyalist settlers in the Townships: compare arrival stories.
- Haitian, Vietnamese, Chilean and Lebanese Montrealers: compare first-year accounts by decade of arrival.
- Moroccan Sephardic and Ashkenazi Jewish households: check which new-year and spring rites each keeps.
- Abitibi and Laurentides pioneer families: check local oral histories of the first winters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for New France founding texts and demography; mixed for newcomers; Indigenous origin stories are held by each people and shared as they choose.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French; Inuktitut for relocation testimony; Haitian Creole and Yiddish for community calendars.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "founding" stories cross Indigenous homelands; the High Arctic relocation and residential schools are painful and contested; some creation tellings are seasonal or restricted.
- **Held for review:** the arrival memoirs and migration data were checked against the standing rules, and none of them was set aside.
