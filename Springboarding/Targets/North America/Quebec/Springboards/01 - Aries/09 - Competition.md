## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal and Quebec City: compare press coverage of the same games before treating the rivalry as one story.
- Saguenay, Abitibi and Côte-Nord towns at the Jeux du Québec: check how regional teams are named and cheered.
- Lacrosse players and families in Kahnawà:ke, Kanehsatà:ke and the Anishinaabe communities of the Outaouais: compare lacrosse's meanings within each community.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare *katajjaq* and the dog-sled race as contests in different registers.
- Francophone and English-speaking farm families: compare fair records in the Townships and the Montérégie.
- Montreal's Italian, Portuguese, Greek and Latin American communities: check club records for rivalry with and within each.
- Parents of public and private school pupils: compare views on rankings by neighborhood.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for hockey, school rankings and fairs; mixed for amateur community sport; Indigenous games are partly documented, with meanings held by communities.
- **Languages needed:** French and English for sport history; Kanien'kéha and Inuktitut for game terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** lacrosse's appropriation is a live issue; some *katajjaq* recordings may carry community conditions; school rankings are disputed.
- **Held for review:** in surveying sport histories and contest broadcasts, no pointer met the grounds for setting aside.
