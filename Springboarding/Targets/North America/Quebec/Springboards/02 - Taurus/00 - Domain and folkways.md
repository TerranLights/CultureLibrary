*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Habitant households of the Côte-de-Beaupré against later colonists of Abitibi-Témiscamingue: check whether inventories and settlers' letters list the same kinds of goods.
- The Golden Square Mile's Anglophone and Scottish merchant families against Gaspé and Townships farm families: compare estate records, not reputations.
- Eeyou, Innu and Atikamekw: check how each nation's own documents describe holding land and sharing harvest, separately.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare country-food sharing records with store-bought food spending.
- Madelinots and Gaspé fishing families: check how a household's boat, gear and catch rights were valued and passed on.
- Montreal's Haitian, Italian, Portuguese and Vietnamese families: compare food spending and money sent abroad, each from its own sources.
- Montreal's Ashkenazi, Moroccan Sephardic and Hasidic communities: compare relief and loan-society records rather than generalize.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for probate inventories, spending surveys and food heritage; mixed for immigrant communities; Indigenous holdings and sharing practices are documented mostly in each nation's own and contested sources. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sheet is written for Quebec as the province stands, with Labrador, the Maritimes and Franco-Ontario as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** old notarial French for the inventories; Inuktitut and East Cree for country-food and land documents; Yiddish for early relief-society records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the James Bay agreements and the *Paix des Braves* are contested ground; Indigenous sharing customs are shared only as the nations choose; property and inheritance law itself belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** probate, spending and museum pointers were weighed for this domain; none had to be set aside under the standing rules.
