## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Workers of single-industry towns (Murdochville, Thetford Mines, Val-des-Sources) against Montreal service workers: check whether worth is tied to the trade, the town or the paycheck.
- Atikamekw of Manawan, Wemotaci and Opitciwan: compare each community's own statements on dignity in care.
- Innu poets and writers of Mashteuiatsh and Uashat mak Mani-utenam: compare literary voices with health and social-service records.
- Inuit women of Nunavik's Hudson and Ungava coasts: separate the two coasts' programs before comparing.
- Haitian Montrealers who arrived in the 1970s against their children born in Quebec: check how each generation names respect and slights.
- Anglophone Quebecers of the Gaspé coast and the West Island: compare minority-vitality studies, not reputations.
- Rural men and farmers in the Bas-Saint-Laurent: check prevention programs aimed at them and how they are received.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for essays, satire and literature; mixed for measured self-esteem by region; Indigenous dignity statements are recent and community-held.
- **Languages needed:** Quebec French, including stage *joual* for monologues; Innu-aimun and Atikamekw for the peoples' own words; Haitian Creole for community oral accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Joyce Echaquan's death and residential-school testimony are grave and personal; suicide data need care; essays on collective inferiority belong to the contested account of the *Grande Noirceur* and should be read as one account.
- **Held for review:** the self-esteem and literary pointers were reviewed under the standing rules, and none was held back.
