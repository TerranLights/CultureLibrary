# Quebec: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French, Quebec English, Kanien'kéha, Innu-aimun, East Cree (Eeyou), Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole, Arabic (Maghrebi communities), Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Francophones of Quebec City's Lower Town and of rural Bas-Saint-Laurent parishes: check whether the move away from church practice ran at the same pace.
- Anglophone Protestants of the Eastern Townships and Irish Catholics of Montreal: compare their schools' moral teaching before the school boards became linguistic.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Akwesasne: compare how each school teaches the Thanksgiving Address.
- Eeyou and Inuit school boards: compare which teachings each sets in the curriculum and in which language.
- Muslim Quebecers of Maghrebi and Lebanese origin, and Hasidic and Orthodox Jews of Outremont: compare each community's own briefs to the 2013 consultation.
- Haitian Montrealers of Catholic and evangelical congregations: check how church teaching and family teaching are described in each.
- Younger and older respondents in values surveys: compare generations, not slogans.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for values surveys, curricula and public consultations; mixed for immigrant communities' own statements; Indigenous teachings are shared on each nation's terms.
- **Languages needed:** French for church and state texts; Kanien'kéha and Inuktitut for school materials; Arabic and Yiddish for some communities' own writing.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Charter of Quebec Values, the secularism law (Bill 21) and the values test are contested; present each as an account. Ceremonial teachings may be restricted to members.
- **Held for review:** survey, curriculum and consultation pointers on values were weighed under the standing rules; nothing in them called for holding back.

5. Ask Kahnawà:ke's language center what may be cited from the Thanksgiving Address.
