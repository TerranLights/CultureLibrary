*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Catholic and liberal Montrealers in the Guibord era: check each camp's press for words of praise and blame for firmness.
- Inuit of Puvirnituq and Ivujivik against those of Kuujjuaq: compare how dissidents and signatories each describe the 1975 choice.
- Eeyou of Whapmagoostui and Chisasibi: compare their roles and memories in the Great Whale campaign.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kanehsatà:ke and residents of Oka: present each community's account of 1990 separately.
- Italian families of Saint-Léonard and the francophone parents opposing them: check each side's own records.
- Union members and managers in the 1972 strike: compare words for firmness and obstruction.
- Farm families in Chaudière-Appalaches: check how fence and boundary quarrels are told in local histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for strikes, language disputes and Indigenous campaigns; thin for everyday stubbornness and personality data by province.
- **Languages needed:** French for most sources; Inuktitut and East Cree for the dissident and Great Whale records; Italian for Saint-Léonard family sources; Kanien'kéha for Kanehsatà:ke's own records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Kanehsatà:ke 1990, the James Bay agreements and the language disputes are contested; each account is one among several. Their law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the dispute, apology and folktale pointers for stubbornness were weighed under the standing rules; none needed setting aside.
