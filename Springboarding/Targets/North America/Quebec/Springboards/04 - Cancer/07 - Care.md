# Quebec: Care (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with the speech of the Beauce and the Gaspé), Quebec English, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Kanien'kéha, Haitian Creole, Cantonese, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** attending to the needs and well-being of another, above all one who can't meet them alone: the attitude, the work and the relationship.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Abitibi, the Côte-Nord and the Gaspé: compare outpost-nursing memories with today's distance to care.
- Beauce and Bas-Saint-Laurent families: check whether folk healers and family carers are still named in local oral history.
- Montreal's CHSLDs and the Capitale-Nationale's hospices: compare care at the end of life in each.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check what patients and escorts say about care in Montreal against care at home.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke: check how the community hospital frames care, from its own reports.
- Haitian, Filipino and African care workers: compare who does paid care work, from their own associations.
- Chinese and Black Montrealers: check the community hospitals and clubs that cared for their own.
- Jewish Montrealers: compare sanatorium and later community care.
- Anglophone Montrealers and Townships seniors: check access to care in English.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health-system history and the 2020 inquiries; mixed for family caregiving; thin for folk healers and for some communities' own care.
- **Languages needed:** French for health-system and inquiry records; English for Anglophone institutions; Inuktitut for patients' accounts; Kanien'kéha for community health sources; Haitian Creole and Tagalog for care workers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in care homes and the treatment of Indigenous patients are painful and in dispute; patient records are closed. Medically assisted dying goes to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** caregiving surveys, hospital archives and pandemic reports were all reviewed under the standing rules; nothing among them was set aside.
