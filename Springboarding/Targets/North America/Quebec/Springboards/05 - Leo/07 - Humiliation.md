*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gaspé and the Bas-Saint-Laurent: check how small-town schools and families speak of bullying after the 2011 case.
- Parishes of the Beauce, the Mauricie and Charlevoix: compare the *blasons populaires* each gave and received.
- Montreal Anglophones and Italian Montrealers: check how they describe language enforcement, against the language office's own account.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and residents of Châteauguay and LaSalle: compare accounts of August 1990, each in its own words.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check elders' memories of numbering, relocation and dog killings as humiliation.
- Haitian Montrealers: compare accounts of the 1983 ban and later episodes of public suspicion.
- Jewish Montrealers: check how the 1934 strike and the boycotts of the 1930s are remembered in the community.
- University and cégep students in Montreal, Quebec City and Sherbrooke: compare hazing traditions and their reform.
- Sovereigntist and federalist Quebecers: check how each tells 1981 and 1990.

## 4. Flags
