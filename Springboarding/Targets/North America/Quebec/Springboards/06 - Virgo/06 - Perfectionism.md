# Quebec: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French, Quebec English, Wendat (in revitalization), Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole, Vietnamese. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Saint-Hyacinthe and the Montérégie: check how organ-building and other precision trades train apprentices.
- Montreal's dance, music and circus schools: compare performers' accounts of perfect form with their teachers'.
- Quebec City and the regions' universities: check whether student distress surveys differ by field and campus.
- Laval and Montreal suburbs: check how the overpass collapse is remembered by those living nearby.
- Wendat of Wendake: check how craft standards are described by makers, beside language revitalization.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check how seamstresses and carvers describe a well-made piece, and who judges it.
- Innu and Atikamekw: compare beadwork and birchbark standards as makers explain them.
- Francophone, Haitian and Vietnamese Montreal families: check what each group's own studies record about grades and pressure, without assuming any pattern in advance.
- Anglophone Montrealers in engineering and medicine: check McGill sources on the Iron Ring and the culture of error.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for craft firms, contests and student surveys; mixed for perfectionism as such; thin for Indigenous craft standards outside makers' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** French for church, school and clinic sources; English for engineering and McGill material; Wendat and Inuktitut for craft words; Haitian Creole and Vietnamese for family studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** eating disorders, burnout and suicide among high achievers need care; some Indigenous makers' methods are not for outsiders. Discipline goes to Capricorn, pride and humiliation to Leo.
