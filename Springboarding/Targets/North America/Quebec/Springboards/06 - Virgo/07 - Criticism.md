# Quebec: Criticism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with *joual*), Quebec English, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Innu-aimun, Haitian Creole, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** pointing out faults or judging the merits of people, work, institutions or oneself, and the forms this takes: feedback, critique, complaint, reproach, satire, dissent and self-criticism.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal and Quebec City: check whether satire and opinion journalism target different figures in each city.
- Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean and Abitibi: compare regional papers' tone toward Quebec City and Montreal.
- Townships and West Island Anglophones: check how English-language letters pages answered Richler and the language debates.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check how Nunavimmiut writers and radio hosts criticize agreements and agencies.
- Innu of the Côte-Nord: compare public protest with criticism voiced inside community assemblies.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Akwesasne: check how criticism of band councils and of outside governments is voiced in community media.
- Haitian Montrealers: check how indirect criticism in song differs from what is said in community radio debate.
- Jewish Montrealers: check how Yiddish and English writers criticized their own community and Quebec.
- Hockey fans in Quebec's regions: compare call-in criticism of the Canadiens with criticism of the junior teams at home.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for satire, censorship and press criticism; mixed for criticism inside families and workplaces; thin for First Nations' ways of correcting members, which nations may not wish to share.
- **Languages needed:** French, including satirical slang and church vocabulary; English for Richler and Anglophone replies; Inuktitut for Nunavik commentary; Haitian Creole for song; Yiddish for older community press.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** debates over language laws, the 1980 and 1995 referendums and the 1990 Kanehsatà:ke resistance carry rival accounts; present each as an account. Gossip goes to Gemini, honesty to Sagittarius, authority to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** the satire, press-council and whistleblower pointers weighed for criticism turned up nothing that a standing rule holds back.
