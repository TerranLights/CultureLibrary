# Quebec: Health (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with Saguenay and Beauce speech), Quebec English, Eeyou (East Cree), Innu-aimun, Atikamekw, Kanien'kéha, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** the state of body and mind that a culture counts as well, and how it is kept, lost and restored, by whom, and at whose cost.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean: check how families and clinics speak of the region's founder-effect diseases.
- The Gaspé, the Côte-Nord and the Magdalen Islands: compare access to doctors and trips to distant hospitals in residents' accounts.
- The Beauce and the Bois-Francs: check memories of *ramancheurs* and folk healers beside the clinic.
- Montreal's poor districts: compare health histories of Saint-Henri or Hochelaga with the West Island.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: check how *miyupimaatisiiun* is used in Eeyou health planning.
- Atikamekw of Manawan and Wemotaci: check how the communities describe care in regional hospitals.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke: compare community-led prevention with provincial programs.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check how patients and families describe care far from home.
- Haitian, Jewish and Maghrebi Montrealers: check each community's own clinics, remedies and health associations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for medicare, epidemics and health surveys; mixed for folk healing; thin for Indigenous medicine, which is shared only as healers and nations allow.
- **Languages needed:** French, including old medical and folk vocabulary; English for McGill and Anglophone hospital records; Eeyou, Innu-aimun, Atikamekw and Inuktitut for health planning; Haitian Creole and Yiddish for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths of Indigenous patients, residential-school legacies and psychiatric histories need great care; Indigenous healing ceremonies are not for outsiders. Death and end-of-life go to Scorpio, care to Cancer, addiction to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** the folk-healing, hospital and public-health pointers weighed for this concept held nothing back under the standing rules.
