# Quebec: Improvement (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French, Quebec English, Kanien'kéha, Innu-aimun, Eeyou (East Cree), Haitian Creole, Portuguese. **Concept in one line:** making something better than it was (oneself, one's work, a home, land, a community, a people), and the belief that betterment is possible, desirable and owed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Bas-Saint-Laurent and Kamouraska farm parishes: check how model-farm honors and agronomists' visits are remembered.
- Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean: compare the TEVEC experiment's memory with later adult-education programs.
- The Côte-Nord: check how Innu of Pessamit and Uashat describe the dams, beside the province's account of progress.
- Quebec City's lower town and Montreal's old districts: compare owners' and tenants' accounts of renewal.
- Innu, Eeyou, Atikamekw, Anishinaabeg and Inuit: check survivors' and communities' own accounts of schooling that called itself improvement.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Akwesasne: check how language renewal is described as the community's own project.
- Haitian and Portuguese Montrealers: check how community sources tell the move to Quebec as a search for a better life.
- Anglophones of the Townships and the Gaspé: compare English-language farm-improvement societies with francophone ones.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for farm improvement, renewal and reforms; mixed for self-help; rich but painful for residential schools, where survivors' accounts come first.
- **Languages needed:** French, including old land-grant vocabulary; English for Townships farm societies; Kanien'kéha, Innu-aimun and Eeyou for renewal and survivors' sources; Haitian Creole and Portuguese for migration accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** residential schools, the hydro projects and the *Grande Noirceur* account carry contested and painful histories; present each as an account. Reform goes to Aquarius, ambition to Capricorn, rebirth to Scorpio.
- **Held for review:** the farm, renewal, self-help and schooling pointers weighed for improvement left nothing to set aside under the standing rules.
