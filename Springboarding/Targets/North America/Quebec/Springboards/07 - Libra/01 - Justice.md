# Quebec: Justice (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (court French and everyday speech), Quebec English, Eeyou (James Bay Cree), Inuktitut of Nunavik, Kanien'kéha, Haitian Creole, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** giving each person and group their due: how wrongs are judged and answered, and how rights, goods and burdens are rightly ordered in a society.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- The Gaspé and the Bas-Saint-Laurent: check how the Coffin case is retold locally against the Montreal press's version.
- Montreal (Bordeaux, Pied-du-Courant): compare how the city marks the places where its condemned died.
- Quebec City and the Capitale-Nationale: check French-regime court records for differences from Montreal's.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: check how each community's justice committee works under the Cree Nation Government.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare the itinerant court's sittings with community justice in the Hudson Strait and Ungava Bay villages.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Akwesasne: check how two communities in one nation divide justice across a provincial and an international border.
- Innu and Atikamekw of the Côte-Nord and the Haute-Mauricie: check which Gladue services serve each, and in which language.
- Haitian Montrealers: check how community radio and associations talk about trials involving their members.
- Anglophone Quebecers of the West Island and the Townships: check English-language coverage of high-profile trials beside the French.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for French-regime court records, statutes and court statistics; mixed for restorative work, which is often confidential; thin for Kanien'kehá:ka and Innu justice bodies beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** old court French for the *Prévôté*; legal French and English for statutes and rulings; Eeyou and Inuktitut for community justice; Haitian Creole for community media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Patriote hangings, the Coffin case and the trials after the 1990 Kanehsatà:ke resistance carry rival accounts; present each as an account. Victims' identities in court files must be protected. Policing and trust in the courts go to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** in weighing court, tribunal and restorative pointers for justice, nothing came up that a standing rule holds back.
