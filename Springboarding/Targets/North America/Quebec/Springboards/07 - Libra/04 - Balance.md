*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal's Chinatown and Côte-des-Neiges: compare herbal shops and household remedies used to restore balance in the body.
- Haitian Montrealers of Saint-Michel and Montréal-Nord: check how hot-and-cold ideas appear in their own health sources.
- Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean: check how shoreline residents and the smelter's workers each describe the lake's balance.
- Nunavimmiut of Hudson Bay and Ungava Bay: compare the co-management board's decisions as each coast reports them.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: check how "being alive well" is used in Cree health and land programs.
- Atikamekw of Manawan, Wemotaci and Opitciwan: check how the seasonal round is described in their own publications.
- Abitibi-Témiscamingue and Côte-Nord mining towns: compare fly-in rotations and family life in local press.
- Anglophones of Montreal and the Townships: check English-language commentary on balance between the two language groups.
- Voters of the Quebec City region and of Montreal's island: compare support for third-position parties by region.
