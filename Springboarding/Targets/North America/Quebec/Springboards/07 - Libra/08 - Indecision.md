# Quebec: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with *joual* and rural speech), Quebec English, Kanien'kéha, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Quebec City and Lévis: compare how residents on each shore describe the delay over the third link.
- Montreal: check whether the OCPM's consultations are judged as care or as delay in different boroughs.
- Rural parishes of the Beauce and the Bas-Saint-Laurent: check church-site quarrels in parish histories.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Kanehsatà:ke: compare how each community reaches major decisions.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check how consultation and decision are described in Inuktitut and in English or French reports.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: check how community assemblies decide on development proposals.
- Older and younger francophones: check how advance directives and the protection mandate are used by age.
- Haitian and Maghrebi Montrealers: check how family members share end-of-life choices.
- Anglophones of the West Island: check how they took part in Montreal's consultations and referendums, in their own press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statutes, consultation reports and press on public projects; mixed for private hesitation, which needs interviews; thin for First Nations' and Inuit decision processes beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** old French for parish petitions; legal French for statutes; English for federal law and Anglophone press; Kanien'kéha and Inuktitut for community processes; Haitian Creole and Yiddish for family accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the referendums and the Clarity Act carry rival accounts; present each as an account, and never read a vote as a trait of a people. Rashness goes to Aries, authority to Capricorn, surrender to Pisces.
- **Held for review:** among the consultation, guardianship and drama pointers for indecision, nothing fell under the standing rules.
