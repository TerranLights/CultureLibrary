## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Francophones and Anglophones of Montreal: compare each group's press for images of the other in the same week of a language debate.
- Abitibi-Témiscamingue: check how Amos and the region tell the internment camp's story.
- Quebec City and the Capitale-Nationale: check local press on the mosque attack against Montreal's coverage.
- Wendat of Wendake: check how the community tells the 1630s epidemics and the blame that followed.
- Inuit of Nunavik: check how Inuit writers and broadcasters describe *qallunaat*.
- Innu of the Côte-Nord: compare images of white neighbors in Innu-authored writing and community radio.
- Jewish Montrealers: check how community archives recorded the 1930s boycotts.
- Ukrainian Quebecers: check how descendants of internees tell the story.
- Haitian and Maghrebi Montrealers: check how each community's media answer being cast as a problem in public debate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for polemics, film, hate-crime data and the colonial record; mixed for everyday blame in families and towns; thin for Innu and Atikamekw accounts of how outsiders are imagined, beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century court French; modern French and English for the polemics; Inuktitut, Innu-aimun and Wendat for the nations' own accounts; Haitian Creole, Yiddish and Ukrainian for community records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1930s writings, the language debates and the 2017 attack carry rival accounts and real grief; present each as an account and record slurs only as claims. Revenge goes to Scorpio, fear to Pisces, identity to Aries.
