# Quebec: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French, Quebec English, Innu-aimun, Kanien'kéha, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal, Quebec City and the regions under the 2021 curfew: compare compliance and protest in police and press records.
- Laurentides villages around Morin-Heights and the Portneuf region around Saint-Casimir: check how local people tell the Solar Temple deaths.
- Côte-Nord Innu of Ekuanitshit and Unamen Shipu: compare their positions on river protection and hydro projects.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Akwesasne and Kahnawà:ke: check how each community's council and Longhouse speak of policing and borders.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare the regional police's reports with community statements on policing.
- Rural parishes and Montreal's working-class wards: check how far temperance pledges and liquor-store hours reached each.
- Anglophone Protestants: check their own temperance societies and their votes in 1919, beside francophone ones.
- Haitian, Maghrebi and Latin American families: check how parents describe control of teenagers in their own community press and studies.
- Couples across regions: compare shelters' reports of coercive control in Abitibi, the Outaouais and Montreal.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for liquor control, curfews, inquiries and Church history; mixed for control in couples and families; thin for Atikamekw and Naskapi views on policing and land control beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for the police rules; Innu-aimun for river and land statements; Kanien'kéha for border statements; Haitian Creole for family studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Solar Temple deaths touch living families; coercive control and youth-protection cases involve victims. Rules as written go to Libra; discipline and order to Capricorn; freedom from control to Aries.
- **Held for review:** the liquor, curfew, policing and river pointers weighed for control gave no reason to hold anything back.
