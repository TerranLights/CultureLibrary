# Quebec: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with *joual*), Quebec English, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Eeyou (James Bay Cree), Kanien'kéha, Haitian Creole, Italian, Yiddish. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or an institution, accepting the risk of being let down, in the expectation of not being harmed; loyalty is studied here for now.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal, Quebec City and Gaspé readers: check whether trust in the same outlets differs by region in press-council complaints.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: compare coastal and inland communities' reactions to mercury and fish advice.
- Inuit of Ungava Bay and Hudson Bay: check how country-food advice is received in each, by age.
- Pekuakamiulnuatsh of Mashteuiatsh and their Lac-Saint-Jean neighbors: compare each side's own statements on the treaty talks.
- Innu of Essipit and Nutashkuan: check their own accounts of the same treaty process.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke: check how lending and guarantees work where land is held in common.
- Italian, Portuguese and Haitian Montrealers: compare community credit unions and loan funds, from their own histories.
- Anglophone voters of the West Island and francophone voters of the east end: check accounts of the 1995 count in each press.
- Parents in the Estrie, Montreal and Nunavik: check the reasons each group gives for vaccinating or not, in regional studies.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for polling, health research and cooperative history; mixed for everyday credit and handshakes; thin for Atikamekw and Naskapi trust in outside institutions beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for card-money papers; Inuktitut and Eeyou for country-food and mercury advice; Italian, Haitian Creole and Yiddish for community credit.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1995 referendum is contested ground; Inuit and Eeyou health findings are governed by their own research rules. Trust in police and courts belongs to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** for trust, the credit, health-advice and polling pointers examined turned up nothing the standing rules would hold.
