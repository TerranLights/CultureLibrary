# Quebec: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with *joual*), Quebec English, Kanien'kéha, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore honor or standing, or deter the next wrong.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke and Kanehsatà:ke: check how their own historians describe raids of the 1680s and 1690s.
- Montreal's West Island and old Lachine: compare how the 1689 raid is marked at local sites.
- Lower Laurentides and Montérégie villages: check whether family and parish histories still tell of houses burned in 1837-1838.
- Quebec City and Montreal hockey publics: compare each press's account of the 1984 brawl.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare versions of the Lumaaq tale between Hudson Bay and Ungava Bay communities.
- Eeyou and Innu: check their own collections of tales for stories of retaliation and of making peace.
- Haitian Montrealers: check how sayings of payback and of leaving it to God are used in community radio and writing.
- Rural parishes of the Beauce and the Mauricie: check court and parish records for charivaris and spell accusations after quarrels.
- Teenagers in Montreal and in the regions: compare school reports of image-based revenge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial wars, the 1837-1838 reprisals, gang conflict and press; mixed for folk revenge; thin for Atikamekw, Wendat and Naskapi narratives of feud beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for raid accounts; Kanien'kéha for community histories; Inuktitut for Lumaaq tellings; Haitian Creole for proverbs.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "mourning war" model is an outsider's frame and contested; the biker war involves living victims' families; image-based abuse must be studied without reproducing images. The state's punishment against private vengeance goes to Capricorn; anger to Aries.
- **Held for review:** for revenge, the raid, feud, folk-curse and crime pointers weighed raised no item for the review line.
