# Quebec: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with *joual*), Quebec English, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Innu-aimun, Naskapi, Mi'gmaq, Kanien'kéha, Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how a culture judges who holds it and how it is used.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal's francophone east end and Anglophone west: compare who held city hall, the boards and the press in each period.
- Rural counties of the Beauce, the Mauricie and the Gaspé: check patronage records county by county under the Union nationale.
- Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean and the Côte-Nord: compare company towns' power relations in union and town histories.
- Innu of Matimekush–Lac John and Naskapi of Kawawachikamach: check each nation's own account of the mining era, separately.
- Inuit of Nunavik: compare views of Makivvik and of the regional government across villages.
- Mi'gmaq of Listuguj and Gesgapegiag: check how each runs its fishery and speaks of it.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke, Kanehsatà:ke and Akwesasne: check how elected councils and the Longhouses each describe their authority.
- Eeyou of Eeyou Istchee: compare coastal and inland communities' views of the Cree Nation Government's power.
- Haitian, Lebanese, Italian and Greek Montrealers: check how each community's entry into elected office is described in its own press.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for party history, biographies and media; mixed for power inside families and workplaces; thin for Atikamekw and W8banaki political histories beyond what they publish.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for Frontenac's papers; Innu-aimun and Naskapi for Schefferville; Inuktitut for Nunavik politics; Mi'gmaq for Listuguj.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the *Grande Noirceur* account, Bill 21 and the referendums carry rival accounts, presented as accounts; Haudenosaunee chiefs' titles and their raising belong to the Longhouses. Authority, rank and power ways are Capricorn's; leadership is Leo's.
- **Held for review:** the party, Church, company and Indigenous-government pointers weighed for power left nothing for the review line.
