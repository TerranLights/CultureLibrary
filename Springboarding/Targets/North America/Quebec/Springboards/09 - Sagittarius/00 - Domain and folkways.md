## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal and the West Island beside the Capitale-Nationale: check where "no religion" shares and church closures diverge.
- The Saguenay–Lac-Saint-Jean, the Beauce and the Bas-Saint-Laurent: compare parish life, shrine-going and how the cours classique was reached from each.
- Anglophone Protestants of the Eastern Townships and the Gaspé: check their churches' records beside the francophone parishes in the same counties.
- Innu, Atikamekw and Anishinaabeg of the Côte-Nord, the Mauricie and Abitibi-Témiscamingue: compare what each nation publishes on its own ceremonies and on the church.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kahnawà:ke, Kanehsatà:ke and Akwesasne: check Catholic, Protestant and Longhouse accounts as each group gives them.
- Mi'gmaq of Listuguj and Gesgapegiag and Inuit of Nunavik: set missionary records beside their own oral histories.
- Ashkenazi, Moroccan Sephardic and Hasidic Montrealers: check each community's own institutions for religious authority.
- Haitian, Lebanese, Greek, Italian, Maghrebi and Latin American Montrealers: check which congregations keep their own histories.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic records, census religion and the history of ideas; mixed for secular and humanist life; thin for Naskapi, Wolastoqiyik and W8banaki belief beyond their own publications. The nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign takes the province as it stands as the core, with Labrador, the Maritimes and Franco-Ontario as comparison only.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for Récollet and Jesuit texts; Innu-aimun, Atikamekw and Kanien'kéha for ceremony terms; Haitian Creole for spirit service; Yiddish and Hebrew for rabbinic records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** First Nations and Inuit ceremonies belong to their keepers; residential schools, the *Grande Noirceur* account and the secularism debates are contested ground, to present as accounts. Law goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in Quebec's lands (category only); Buddhist communities, held as a category (an open question for the author).
