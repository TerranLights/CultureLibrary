*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal and the Capitale-Nationale: check whether consumer-fraud reports and honor-system practices differ, adjusting for population.
- Rural Eastern Townships, Île d'Orléans and Montérégie farm country: compare roadside stands and what owners say about trust.
- The Beauce and Chaudière-Appalaches: check how small-business owners talk about a handshake deal against a written contract.
- Innu and Atikamekw: compare their own teachings on keeping one's word with the record of fur-trade dealings.
- Kanien'kehá:ka and Wendat: check how wampum and spoken promises are explained today by their own keepers.
- W8banaki of Odanak and Wôlinak: compare craft sellers' accounts of fair and unfair buyers over time.
- Haitian, Lebanese, Greek and Vietnamese Montrealers: check how small merchants in each community describe trust with suppliers and customers.
- Hasidic and secular Jewish Montrealers: check what each community's own teaching and press say about fair dealing in trade.
- Anglophone and francophone readers: check whether *J.E.*-style exposés and English-language consumer columns choose different targets.
