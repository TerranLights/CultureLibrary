# Quebec: Order (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (including *joual*), Quebec English, Atikamekw, Eeyou (James Bay Cree), Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole. **Concept in one line:** things and people being where and as they should be: the arrangement that holds off chaos, in the cosmos, society, the home and the self.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Montreal's Plateau, Hochelaga-Maisonneuve and the West Island: compare 311 complaint types by borough.
- Quebec City and Montreal: check how each city tells its fires, riots and parades.
- Farm country of the Montérégie and the Beauce against suburban rings: compare CPTAQ disputes and who brings them.
- Eeyou of the coast and of the inland communities, and Inuit of Nunavik: compare their own accounts of the land categories.
- Atikamekw of Manawan, Wemotaci and Opitciwan: check whether the season names and dates differ by community.
- Anglophone Montrealers: compare English-language memory of the 1955 riot with French accounts.
- Haitian and Latin American Montrealers in Montréal-Nord and Saint-Michel: check community groups' own words on public order and policing.
- Unhoused Montrealers, including Inuit and First Nations people in the city: check advocacy groups' records of encampment removals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for colonial ordinances, city data and press; mixed for household order, scattered across magazines; thin for Naskapi and Wolastoqiyik ideas of order beyond their own publications.
- **Languages needed:** eighteenth-century French for ordinances; Atikamekw and Inuktitut for season and weather terms; Haitian Creole for Montréal-Nord sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the James Bay agreements and the 1968 parade carry rival accounts; give each as an account. Encampment records involve vulnerable people; use published sources only. Elders' knowledge is shared on the nations' terms.
- **Held for review:** in looking at Montreal's community order and policing sources, no pointer had to be held back.
