*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

- Wendat of Wendake: read the Jesuit dream reports against Wendat historians.
- Atikamekw Nehirowisiwok of Manawan, Wemotaci and Opitciwan: ask whether school culture programs teach dream-telling.
- Haitian Montrealers of Saint-Michel and Montréal-Nord: compare Catholic, Protestant and Vodou accounts of misfortune.
- Italian, Maghrebi and Vietnamese Montrealers: check which protective practices each community's press records.
- Hasidic and older Ashkenazi Montrealers: compare Yiddish sources on the soul.
