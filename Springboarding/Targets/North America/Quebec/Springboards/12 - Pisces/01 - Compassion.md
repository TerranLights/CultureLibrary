# Quebec: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Hochelaga-Maisonneuve and Westmount: check how neighborhood papers write about the poor nearby, and in what words.
- Abitibi and the Côte-Nord: compare how towns far from Montreal keep a doctor or a nurse, and what residents say they owe them.
- Inuit in Montreal and in Nunavik: look at how Inuit organizations describe help for relatives who went south.
- Innu of Uashat mak Mani-utenam: check what their own social services publish on caring for elders and the sick.
- Haitian Montrealers of Saint-Michel and Vietnamese Montrealers of Côte-des-Neiges: look at who first took in each wave of arrivals, parish or compatriots.
- Armenian and Lebanese Montrealers: set church-led refugee sponsorship beside each other.
- Hasidic communities of Outremont and Boisbriand: check their own aid institutions (meals, loans, medical help).
- Anglophone Montrealers of the West Island: compare charities founded in English with francophone ones in the same suburbs.
- Rural parishes of the Bas-Saint-Laurent: check what remains of parish charity after the church withdrew.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charity institutions, shelters and homelessness counts; mixed for pity in speech, which needs sociolinguistic work; thin for Atikamekw, W8banaki and Naskapi practices, where only what each nation publishes should be used.
- **Languages needed:** seventeenth-century French for the *Relations*; Inuktitut and Innu-aimun for terms of care; Armenian and Arabic for sponsorship groups' own records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** homeless and Indigenous people's names appear in press stories; the Vanier revelations involve victims; harm reduction is disputed in some neighborhoods; give each side as an account. Mercy as a legal matter (pardons, sentencing) goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** the care, shelter and refugee-aid leads gathered here met every standing rule, so nothing on mercy had to be set aside.
