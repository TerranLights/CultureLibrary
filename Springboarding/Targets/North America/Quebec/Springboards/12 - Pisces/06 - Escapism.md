# Quebec: Escapism (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lumber regions (Mauricie, Haute-Côte-Nord) and city tellers: compare *chasse-galerie* versions for who escapes and from what.
- Montreal Jews of the Laurentian resort era and their children: check memoirs for how the hills were remembered.
- Kanien'kehá:ka of Kanehsatà:ke and Atikamekw of Wemotaci, Manawan and Opitciwan: read treatment centers' own reports, never outside summaries.
- Inuit of Nunavik: look at how the regional health board frames substance use and its causes.
- Saguenay and Abitibi hunting families: compare the bush camp as escape with the bush as workplace.
- Montreal suburbs and the Gaspé: check who goes south in winter by income and age before naming any regional habit.
- Haitian Montrealers and Colombian or Chilean Montrealers: look in each community's radio and papers for which getaways (a *kompa* night, a trip home) are praised and which are worried over.
- Teen runaways in Laval and Montreal: check youth-protection data and survivors' accounts on where they went.
- Anglophone Montrealers of the West Island: compare cottage country in the Laurentians and the Eastern Townships.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for gambling, tourism, drug surveillance and popular culture; mixed for daydream and fantasy; thin for Naskapi and W8banaki sources on escape and recovery.
- **Languages needed:** rural Quebec French of the folklore recordings; Yiddish and English for resort memoirs; Kanien'kéha and Atikamekw for treatment centers' own words.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** substance use in First Nations and Inuit communities must be read in their own organizations' framing, never generalized; runaway and exploitation cases involve minors; the prison escape involves living people. Recklessness goes to Aries.
