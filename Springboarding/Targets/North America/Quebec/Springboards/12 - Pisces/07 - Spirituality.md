*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Lower Saint-Lawrence and Beauce parishes: compare who tends the wayside crosses and when they are blessed.
- Wendat of Wendake: check how singers and elders present the carol and other sacred songs.
- Innu and Atikamekw: compare how Catholic devotion and older teaching sit together, in each nation's own publications only.
- Anishinaabeg of Kitigan Zibi and Lac-Simon: look at what each community says publicly of its gatherings.
- Mi'gmaq of Listuguj and Gesgapegiag: compare Saint Anne's feast in each place.
- Inuit of the Hudson and Ungava coasts: check differences in Anglican, Catholic and Pentecostal worship.
- Azorean Portuguese and Haitian Montrealers: compare vows to saints across the two communities.
- Montreal Plateau seekers and Quebec City's older Catholics: check survey and interview data for how each speaks of "something greater".
- English-speaking Quebecers of the Townships: compare United, Anglican and evangelical practice with francophone neighbors.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Catholic heritage and census religion; mixed for lay and New Age practice; thin for Naskapi and W8banaki spirituality, which is theirs to share.
- **Languages needed:** Wendat for the carol's text; Innu-aimun, Atikamekw and Inuktitut for sacred words; Portuguese and Vietnamese for home devotion.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** ceremonies of the First Nations and Inuit are closed unless the nation shares them; the ayahuasca church involves a controlled substance; Bill 21 touches religious symbols and is contested ground (law to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** two pointers were set aside under the standing rules and are kept as categories only: Buddhist communities in Quebec's lands; South Asian-heritage communities in Quebec's lands.
