# Quebec: Intuition (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** Quebec, region North America. **Language(s) for searching:** Quebec French (with rural speech of the Gaspé and the Beauce), Quebec English, Eeyou (James Bay Cree), Innu-aimun and Naskapi, Inuktitut of Nunavik, Haitian Creole, Italian, Vietnamese, Arabic, Portuguese. **Concept in one line:** knowing or judging without conscious reasoning: the gut feeling, the hunch, the sense for people and situations.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- Gaspé and Beauce farm families: compare weather sayings collected in each, and which are still repeated.
- Innu hunters of Pessamit and Unamen Shipu, and Naskapi hunters: ask whether reading a burnt shoulder blade is still spoken of, and set any public answer against Moore's thesis.
- Eeyou of Mistissini and Waswanipi: compare how hunters' foresight is taught in land-based school programs.
- Inuit of the Hudson and Ungava coasts: check how ice knowledge is shared between elders and young hunters, in Inuit sources.
- Haitian and Italian Montrealers: set *tchala* and *smorfia* side by side as two dream-number practices.
- Vietnamese and Maghrebi Montrealers: look for premonition and character-reading words in community writing.
- Quebec City and Montreal nurses: compare how each hospital network trains clinical judgment.
- Readers of skeptics' and psychic publications: check who reads which, by age and region, if data exist.
- Anglophone Townships readers and francophone readers of crime fiction: compare which detectives each buys.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for health-care judgment, folklore and skeptics' records; mixed for dream-number practices, known mostly from community writing; thin for Atikamekw and Anishinaabeg sources on foresight.
- **Languages needed:** Eeyou, Innu-aimun and Naskapi for hunting knowledge; Haitian Creole and Italian for dream books; Inuktitut for ice terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hunters' divination and dreams may be private or restricted; use only what each nation publishes. Suspicion that becomes bias (profiling) belongs with crime and justice sheets. Divination is studied as practice, never as a source.
- **Held for review:** the hunch, folk-forecast and nursing-judgment leads here cleared the standing rules; nothing on intuition was held back.
