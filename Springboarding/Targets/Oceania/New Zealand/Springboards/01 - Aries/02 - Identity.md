# New Zealand: Identity (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori; Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean for those communities. **Concept in one line:** what a person or group takes itself to be: the answer to "who are you?"

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāi Tahu since its settlement:** a tribal identity rebuilt in the south, with its own dialect revival.
- **Ngāi Tūhoe and Te Urewera:** a strong tribal self-definition tied to one forest homeland.
- **Ngāpuhi in Auckland:** the largest iwi, many members far from home marae; identity at a distance.
- **Mainlanders against Aucklanders:** the South Island's self-label as a regional identity.
- **West Coasters:** a mining and timber region's sense of being apart.
- **Pacific peoples by island of origin, and by generation:** Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Niuean, Tokelauan, Fijian; "Pasifika" as a pan-label some reject.
- **People of mixed descent** (Māori-Pākehā, Māori-Chinese, Pacific-Māori): who chooses which identity, and when.
- **Older European minorities:** Dalmatians of Northland, the Bohemian settlers of Puhoi, Scandinavians of Dannevirke and Norsewood, postwar Dutch.
- **Korean and Filipino New Zealanders:** first generation still forming.
- **New Zealanders in Australia:** comparison only, to see what identity looks like from outside.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. Identity is one of the most-studied topics in New Zealand social science.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, including iwi dialects; Samoan and Tongan; Chinese for older community papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** who counts as Māori or as a member of an iwi is decided by iwi and is sensitive; avoid treating DNA ancestry as a test of identity; Moriori identity questions go through Moriori organizations.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
