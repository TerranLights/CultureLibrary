*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi by maramataka and new-year marker:** Whanganui and Taranaki iwi (Puanga) against iwi who look to Matariki; Tūhoe as the source of much revived star knowledge.
- **Moriori:** their own origin traditions on Rēkohu, long replaced in schoolbooks by a false "pre-Māori people" story.
- **Ngāti Porou and Tairāwhiti:** a region that claims the day's first light.
- **Settler provinces:** Otago's Free Church founding and Canterbury's Anglican founding as distinct origin stories.
- **Canterbury and Hawke's Bay:** beginnings after earthquakes.
- **Pacific peoples:** watch-night church services and arrival stories of the 1950s to 1970s.
- **Chinese and Korean New Zealanders:** Lunar New Year at home and in city lantern festivals.
- **Refugee communities:** Polish, Cambodian, Somali and others whose start was forced.
- **Young Pākehā on the OE:** leaving to start over abroad, and the return.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Māori origin traditions, migration records and new-year revival; thinner for personal "starting over" after failure.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori with iwi dialects; Moriori; Samoan and Tongan; Mandarin and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some creation and star knowledge is taught only in wānanga; the "Moriori myth" is a hurt to Moriori and should be cited only as error; the Treaty's legal side belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Read Matamua's *Matariki* and list the iwi sources it names for new-year practice.
2. Search Te Ara for "Puanga" and "maramataka" and note which iwi are named.
3. Compare the creation sequences in White's volumes for two iwi, for example Ngāi Tahu and Tainui.
4. Search Papers Past for "Anniversary Day" in Otago and Wellington papers of the 1850s to 1870s.
5. Search NZ On Screen for *Tagata Pasifika* episodes on arrival and migration.
