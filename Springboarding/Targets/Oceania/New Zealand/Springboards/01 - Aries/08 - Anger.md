# New Zealand: Anger (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan; Korean and Mandarin for those communities. **Concept in one line:** the emotion of grievance and the urge to strike back or set things right, and how it is expressed.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi practice of redress:** compare how utu and muru are explained by northern iwi and by Tūhoe or Ngāti Kahungunu, so that "revenge" is not read as one custom.
- **South Auckland Māori and Pacific families:** the setting of *Once Were Warriors*, and the counter-stories its critics told.
- **Samoan and Tongan churches:** teaching on anger and forgiveness in congregations.
- **Rural Pākehā men:** how their anger is shown in film and handled in men's programs, against what they report themselves.
- **Korean New Zealanders:** hwabyeong as a named way of holding anger.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** whether and how grievance over the poll-tax era was voiced, before and after the 2002 apology.
- **Women:** whose anger is treated as protest and whose as hysteria.
- **Young people online:** anger shaped by platforms.
- **Wellington against provincial protesters in 2022:** collective anger split by place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on family violence and cohort data; mixed on everyday anger and display rules.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for church and family; Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** family violence affects survivors; the Christchurch mosque attacks of 2019 and other mass killings are grievous and should not be used as illustrations; avoid stereotyping Māori or Pacific men through crime data; the legal side of violence belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
