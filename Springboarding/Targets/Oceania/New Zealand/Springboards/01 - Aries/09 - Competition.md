## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kapa haka regions at Te Matatini:** Tainui, Te Arawa, Mātaatua, Tairāwhiti, Ngāti Kahungunu and Waitaha teams each bring their own style and their own record of winning.
- **Rugby provinces:** Canterbury, Auckland, Waikato and smaller unions such as Taranaki or Southland, where holding the Shield can mark a town's years.
- **Elite boys' schools and their first fifteens:** rivalry as a school tradition, and its critics.
- **Rural A&P towns:** contests over stock, crops and baking that urban New Zealand rarely sees.
- **Samoan and Tongan communities:** national-team rivalry carried into Auckland suburbs.
- **Chinese and Korean families:** school striving and tutoring, read from data, not stereotype.
- **Women in netball against men in rugby:** whether their contests are reported and rewarded alike.
- **Wellington against Auckland:** the city rivalry in jokes, sport and pride.
- **Cooperative traditions:** church, marae and community groups that avoid ranking people.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on sport and kapa haka; mixed on workplace and school competition; thin on envy.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (for judging criteria and commentary); Samoan and Tongan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** kapa haka compositions are owned by their groups; avoid reading Pacific or Asian achievement through stereotypes; competition law belongs to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
