# New Zealand: Recklessness (Aries): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan; Korean and Mandarin for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** courage or initiative pushed past its useful range; acting without regard for danger or consequence.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Young men by region:** rural Southland and Waikato against city youth; road deaths, drink-driving and hunting incidents may differ.
- **Rangatahi Māori in small towns:** licensing barriers, unlicensed driving, and the "Ghost Chips" audience.
- **Pacific men and kaimoana gathering:** drowning risk while fishing and diving.
- **Queenstown and Wānaka:** a resort economy built on sold risk, with locals and visitors to separate.
- **Back-country hunters and trampers:** the Mountain Safety Council's core concern.
- **Overseas visitors and new migrants at beaches and on roads:** unfamiliar surf and roads, a risk distinct from bravado.
- **Investors of the 1980s and 2000s:** speculation by middle-class savers.
- **Rēkohu and remote coasts:** dangerous fishing work far from rescue.
- **Women and girls:** whether risk-taking is measured and judged in them at all.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich. ACC, drowning, road and alcohol data are unusually complete; attitudes toward risk need media and qualitative sources.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for water-safety work.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** deaths in the Whakaari/White Island eruption (2019), adventure-tourism accidents and road crashes involve families still grieving; avoid using one ethnic group's injury rates as a stereotype; drink-driving law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
