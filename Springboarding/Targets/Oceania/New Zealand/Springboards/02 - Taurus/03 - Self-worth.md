# New Zealand: Self-worth (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (New Zealand usage), te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the value a person holds themselves to have, and what that value is believed to rest on.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Māori who were punished at school for speaking te reo vs. kōhanga reo graduates:** two generations with opposite school lessons about their language and selves.
- **Rural Pākehā men (farming, shearing, bush work):** test how far worth is tied to physical competence; compare with urban professional men.
- **Pacific young people in South Auckland and Porirua:** check church, family obligation and sport as sources of standing; ask Samoan and Tongan youth separately.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** "face" talk among old Cantonese families vs. recent Mandarin-speaking arrivals.
- **Survivors of state and faith-based care:** a group whose own accounts of worth are on record.
- **Former freezing-works and mill towns (Pātea, Kawerau, Tokoroa):** worth after the loss of a main employer.
- **Rainbow and takatāpui people:** standing within whānau and church set against public acceptance.
- **Deaf New Zealanders (NZSL users):** pride in a language long discouraged at school.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys and health data; mixed for Pacific and Chinese self-accounts; Māori concepts need te reo sources, not only English glosses.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese; NZ Sign Language for Deaf accounts.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide, abuse in care and racism are raw; use survivors' published words only. Mana has iwi-specific senses; do not flatten it into one meaning.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. NZAVS publications list: search "self-esteem" and note the waves and group splits used.
2. Youth2000 site: open the latest summary reports for Māori and Pacific students.
3. Library catalog: request Metge's *In and Out of Touch* and Durie's *Whaiora*.
4. NZETC or library: find pātere in *Nga Moteatea* and list who composed them and against what.
5. Royal Commission site: read the survivor-voice chapters of *Whanaketia* on identity and worth.
