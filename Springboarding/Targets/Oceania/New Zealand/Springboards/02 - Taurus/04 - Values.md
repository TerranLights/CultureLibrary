# New Zealand: Values (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (New Zealand usage), te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** what a person or a culture holds worth having, doing or being, and how those things are ranked; and valuation, how worth is assigned to things.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Otago and Southland (Presbyterian founding) vs. Canterbury (Anglican founding):** two church-planned settlements; test whether founding creeds still mark local talk.
- **Māori by church:** Rātana, Ringatū, Catholic and Latter-day Saint Māori (the last with centers around Hastings and Temple View, Hamilton); compare what each church's members put first.
- **Samoan and Tongan congregations:** ask how far the church is where values are taught and enforced; compare denominations.
- **Wellington's public-service and university suburbs vs. rural Southland and Waikato:** the poles of social-issue votes.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** Christian, Buddhist and secular families differ; old and new arrivals differ again.
- **Muslim communities (Christchurch, Auckland):** how values are taught at mosque and home, and voiced in public since 2019.
- **Exclusive Brethren and other closed churches:** a separate moral world; few public sources; handle with care.
- **Generations:** baby boomers who saw the 1980s reforms vs. people born after 1990.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in surveys and public debate; deep Māori and Pacific value terms need fluent readers, since official glosses can flatten them.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (for tikanga texts and church writings), Samoan and Tongan (for fa'aSamoa and anga fakatonga), Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** te reo value words are widely borrowed by agencies and firms; note who is using a word and for what. Moral referendums and their laws go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. WVS site: download the NZ waves and tabulate the child-qualities and life-domain items over time.
2. NZES site: list the moral-issue and postmaterialism items by year.
3. Library: request Mead's *Tikanga Māori* and King's *Being Pakeha*.
4. Ministry of Education site: compare the values text of the two national curricula side by side.
5. Papers Past and library newspaper indexes: search "Values Party" 1972-1975 for coverage and letters.
