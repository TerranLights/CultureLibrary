# New Zealand: Security (Taurus): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Canterbury households:** a decade of quake claims, rebuilds and insurer fights sets their sense of protection apart.
- **Hawke's Bay and Tairāwhiti (Ngāti Kahungunu, Ngāti Porou):** flood and cyclone recovery on iwi and orchard land.
- **Chatham Islanders (Moriori and Māori):** food and fuel supply at the end of a long sea and air link.
- **Pacific families:** security spread across kin in two countries, through church and remittance.
- **Seasonal workers from Pacific nations (the Recognised Seasonal Employer scheme):** temporary workers with little local safety net; compare as a category.
- **Older homeowners on Super vs. young private renters:** opposite exposure to housing costs.
- **Drought-exposed farmers (Canterbury, North Otago, Hawke's Bay):** income security tied to rain and markets.
- **Wellington residents:** the earthquake-prone building question as a daily worry.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** saving and property as household protection; compare older and newer arrivals.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in public statistics and social policy history; mixed on felt security; Māori and Pacific coping networks are documented mostly in recent disaster research.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (iwi disaster accounts), Samoan and Tongan (remittance and church networks), Chinese (household saving).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hardship and housing loss are stigmatized; anonymize. Benefit and ACC entitlements in law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Library: request McClure's *A Civilised Community* and Simpson's *The Sugarbag Years*.
2. MSD site: open the latest *Household Incomes Report* hardship chapter and note the measures.
3. Stats NZ: pull GSS safety and money-worry items by region and ethnicity.
4. Journals: search "marae" with "earthquake" or "Gabrielle" for iwi-led response studies.
5. NZETC: read the storage and food-supply chapters of Best's *Maori Agriculture*.
