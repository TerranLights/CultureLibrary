*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui:** the Kīngitanga's heartland, where an institution's continuity is lived up close.
- **Moriori (Rēkohu):** continuity broken and rebuilt; their own trust now leads its telling.
- **Old farming families of Otago, Southland and the Wairarapa:** century farms and long-held stations.
- **Auckland's private renters:** frequent moves and short leases at the other pole.
- **Dalmatian winemaking families of West Auckland:** firms held in one family for generations.
- **Pacific churchgoers:** the congregation as the steady point through migration; ask each denomination.
- **Christchurch and Kaikōura residents:** homes and routines broken by earthquakes, and rebuilt or not.
- **Mill and freezing-works towns after 1984:** steady work lost in a few years.
- **Recent Chinese and Korean arrivals vs. old Cantonese families:** first-generation unsettledness against century-old roots.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on mobility, jobs and prices; mixed on personal routines; Māori continuity needs iwi-held histories.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, Croatian for older family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** placenta-burial and other birth customs are personal and sometimes tapu; seek consent. The Kīngitanga's internal matters are for its own people to share.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Stats NZ: pull years at usual residence by territorial authority and tenure from the latest census.
2. Search "Century Farm and Station Awards" and list winners' family stories in local papers.
3. Library: request Easton's *Not in Narrow Seas* and King's *Moriori*.
4. Kīngitanga site and Māori Television archive: search "Koroneihana".
5. Growing Up in New Zealand: read reports on residential moves and school changes.
