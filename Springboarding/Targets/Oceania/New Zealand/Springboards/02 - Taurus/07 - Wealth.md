## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Canterbury runholding families vs. Auckland property investors:** old landed wealth against fortunes made in houses since the 1990s.
- **Iwi with large settlement assets vs. iwi still in negotiation:** collective wealth arrived on different timetables.
- **Remuera, Herne Bay and Fendalton vs. South Auckland and Porirua:** wealth and want a short drive apart.
- **Queenstown and Wānaka:** second homes, absentee owners and the workers who serve them.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands families:** ask how far wealth is counted in mats, barkcloth, quilts, church giving and remittances as well as money.
- **Old Cantonese merchant families vs. recent investor migrants:** two routes to Chinese New Zealand prosperity.
- **Dalmatian families of Northland:** from poor kauri-gum diggers to vineyard owners.
- **Early Jewish merchant families (Auckland, Wellington, Dunedin):** trading houses of the colonial economy.
- **Tairāwhiti and Northland rural towns:** places with long histories of low income, read through their own accounts.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on money wealth and elites; mixed on ceremonial and collective wealth; Pacific wealth is best found in theses and community sources.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (trust and incorporation reports often bilingual), Samoan, Tongan, Cantonese and Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** church giving in Pacific congregations is a touchy public topic; handle without blame. Iwi accounts of settlement assets belong to their members. Taxation in law goes to Libra; what tax is for goes to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
