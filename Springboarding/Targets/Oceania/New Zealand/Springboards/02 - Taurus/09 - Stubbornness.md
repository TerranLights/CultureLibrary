## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taranaki iwi (Parihaka):** a community that keeps a living memory of non-violent refusal; compare with iwi remembered for armed resistance.
- **Tūhoe:** Te Urewera's long distance from state authority; check how members distinguish principle from obstinacy.
- **Moriori:** ask how Nunuku's teaching figures in identity today; compare their framing with Māori ones.
- **West Coast mining and union towns (Blackball, Runanga, Waihi in the north):** check whether holding out is told as local pride.
- **Southland and Otago:** test the "dour" settler label against local sources instead of assuming it.
- **Samoan New Zealanders:** ask how the Mau's memory and the 2002 apology enter talk of holding firm.
- **Pacific activists of the 1970s and their children:** ask whether refusal is passed down as a family story.
- **Farmers in Southland and Waikato:** resistance to environmental rules, and those farmers who did not join it.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** the long campaign for poll-tax redress, as its leaders tell it.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protest, strikes and resistance history; thin for everyday obstinacy in families, which needs interviews and fiction.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Moriori (limited sources), Samoan, Tongan, Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** armed resistance, raids and occupations are painful and still argued; give each side's own sources. The 2022 occupation is polarizing; avoid labels. Treaty claims, nuclear legislation and protest law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Library: request Scott's *Ask That Mountain* and Binney's *Mihaia*.
2. NZETC: open Cowan's *New Zealand Wars* chapters on Ōrākau and Parihaka.
3. Papers Past: search "Blackball" 1908 and "Waihi strike" 1912 for strikers' and owners' statements.
4. Te Aka and whakataukī lists: search "manawanui", "turi" and "pakeke" with example sentences.
5. News archives: compare RNZ and Samoan-language coverage of the 2002 apology.
