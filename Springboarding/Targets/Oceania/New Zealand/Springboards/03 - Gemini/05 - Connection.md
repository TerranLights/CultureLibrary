*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Niuean, Cantonese and Mandarin, Croatian. **Concept in one line:** the ties that link people to one another, and the work of making, using and keeping them: keeping in touch, introducing, networking, and the felt sense of being connected (or not).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei:** an iwi on its own land in central Auckland, hosting many Māori from elsewhere; host and guest ties.
- **Taura here groups:** members of distant iwi living in cities, keeping a line back to their marae.
- **Urban Māori without known iwi links:** connection rebuilt through urban authorities, schools and kapa haka.
- **Niuean and Tokelauan communities:** most of their people live in the nation; ties to small home islands.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** church and family obligations as the channels of tie-keeping; remittances home.
- **Cantonese district-association families and recent Mandarin-speaking arrivals:** older and newer networks that may not meet.
- **Dalmatian-Māori families of Northland:** ties across two peoples, through marriage and work.
- **Farming districts and older people living alone:** where isolation is measured and where help reaches.
- **Comparison only:** New Zealanders in Australia, whose ties back across the Tasman (visits, family chats) sit outside the nation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for loneliness statistics, urban Māori studies and Pacific migration; mixed for Chinese and Dalmatian networks; thin for weak ties and job networking.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Niuean, Cantonese, Croatian.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whakapapa is held by whānau and iwi, and some lines are not for publication; ask before use. Nepotism in law and public office goes to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
