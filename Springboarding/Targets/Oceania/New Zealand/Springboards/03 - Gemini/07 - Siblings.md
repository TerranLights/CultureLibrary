# New Zealand: Siblings (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** brothers and sisters, and how the sibling bond is understood: who counts as a sibling, what siblings owe one another, and how they rank, compete and care.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Hapū with senior and junior lines (e.g., among Ngāti Kahungunu or Ngāpuhi):** whether tuakana descent still weighs in leadership; check case by case.
- **Whāngai families across iwi:** siblings by custom, not birth; how they are counted.
- **Samoan families:** brother-sister covenant and its strength in families born in the nation.
- **Tongan families:** the sister's ritual precedence (fahu) at weddings and funerals in Auckland.
- **Cook Islands Māori families:** close kin terms with Māori; compare tuakana-teina use.
- **Pākehā farming families:** which sibling takes over the farm; sisters and brothers at succession.
- **Chinese migrants from the one-child generation:** many only children, raising sibling sets in the nation.
- **Blended and rainbow families:** half-, step- and donor siblings.
- **Survivors of state care:** siblings lost and found again.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Māori kin terms, family statistics and fiction; mixed for Pacific sibling duties in the nation; thin for adult siblings' care of parents.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whakapapa and senior-line claims can be contested within iwi; abuse-in-care testimony is painful, cite with care. Inheritance and Māori land succession in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. NZETC: open Grey's *Polynesian Mythology*, the chapter on the children of Heaven and Earth, then find tribal versions.
2. Te Aka Māori Dictionary: look up tuakana, teina, tuahine and tungāne, and follow the example sentences.
3. Library: Morton's *Becoming Tongan*, the chapters on siblings.
4. Royal Commission site: search *Whanaketia* for "siblings".
5. Stats NZ: download family type by ethnicity for the latest census.
