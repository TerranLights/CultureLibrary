*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori (incl. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moriori:** Nunuku's covenant against killing, as Moriori sources tell it; compare how quarrels were ended with Māori practice.
- **Iwi with recorded tatau pounamu agreements:** whose peace was sealed by marriage or gifts, and whether those ties are still named.
- **Rātana and Ringatū followers:** prophets and ministers as go-betweens with God and the state.
- **Samoan families:** ifoga and the role of matai and ministers in settling quarrels.
- **Tongan congregations:** church ministers and elders as mediators inside the community.
- **Urban Māori in West and South Auckland:** marae-based and urban-authority programs, not iwi structures.
- **Chinese community associations:** older associations that settled disputes among members.
- **Farming districts:** neighbors' boundary and water quarrels and who steps in.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for restorative practice and family group conferences; mixed for customary peacemaking and Pacific practice in the nation; thin for Chinese and rural informal mediation.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan; Moriori sources mostly come through English and te reo.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Moriori history is painful and was long misrepresented; follow Moriori-led sources. Courts, Treaty settlements, tribunals and the statutes behind family group conferences go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
