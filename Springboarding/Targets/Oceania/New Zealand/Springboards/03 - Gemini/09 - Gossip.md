# New Zealand: Gossip (Gemini): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, German (for wartime records). **Concept in one line:** talk about absent people, especially their private lives and conduct; rumor (unconfirmed reports passed from person to person) is researched here too.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Marae communities and women composers of pātere:** a sung, public answer to gossip; compare with today's replies on social media.
- **Small towns in Taranaki, the West Coast and Southland:** compare how reputation is made and lost with city neighborhoods.
- **Samoan and Tongan congregations:** test how news moves through church networks, and whether talk attaches to a family or a person.
- **Rural farming families on old party lines:** a shared line on which neighbors could listen in.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** past targets of hostile rumor; compare with talk inside their own community.
- **German-born families in World War I:** rumor turned into suspicion and internment.
- **Secondary-school students:** online gossip by gender and ethnicity, as surveys report it.
- **Christchurch residents after 2010-2011:** rumor in a long disaster.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for tabloid history, youth surveys and wartime records; mixed for Pacific networks; thin for gossip inside Chinese and other migrant groups.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (chant texts), Samoan, Tongan, Cantonese and Mandarin; German for wartime letters.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** chant texts may name ancestors and old quarrels; ask iwi before use. Workplace and abuse allegations involve living people; use only published sources. Defamation and harmful digital communications in law go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
