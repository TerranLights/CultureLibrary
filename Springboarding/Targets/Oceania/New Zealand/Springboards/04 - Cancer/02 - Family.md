*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the people a person counts as their own, by blood, marriage, adoption, ritual or choice, and what they owe one another.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Porou and Tūhoe:** deep recited genealogies held on home ground; family as lineage.
- **Kāi Tahu:** a formal registry decides who is in the tribal family; compare with iwi that rely on marae knowledge.
- **Urban Māori without iwi ties:** families who rebuilt kin links through urban authorities or never did.
- **Samoans:** titles and family duties kept up across the sea; **Tongans:** the fahu line ranks some kin above others; **Cook Islands Māori and Niueans:** families larger in New Zealand than on the home islands.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** surname and county associations as family beyond the household.
- **Waipu Scots and other chain-migration Pākehā communities:** one family network moving as a body.
- **Takatāpui and Rainbow families:** chosen or reshaped family.
- **Closed religious communities** (Gloriavale; also Exclusive Brethren families): family and church fused.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for Māori and Pacific kin; mixed for Pākehā extended kin; thin for smaller minorities.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Niuean, Cantonese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whakapapa belongs to whānau and iwi and is often closed; Gloriavale matters involve abuse allegations. Family law (adoption, whāngai standing, care of children) goes to Libra (letter) or Capricorn (spirit).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (named only as a category).
