# New Zealand: Belonging (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, NZSL. **Concept in one line:** being accepted as part of a group or a place, and feeling so: being "one of us".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui:** belonging rebuilt after confiscation, centered on one marae and the Kīngitanga.
- **Urban Māori, including those unsure of their iwi:** belonging by city and service rather than descent.
- **Pākehā of long settler lines against recent British and South African arrivals:** who counts as a "real" local, and how quickly.
- **NZ-born against island-born Samoans and Tongans:** belonging pulled between two homes, and teasing inside the group.
- **Chinese, Korean and Filipino New Zealanders:** the "where are you from" question for families here for generations or for a few years.
- **Moriori of Rēkohu:** recognition as a people, not just as individuals.
- **The Deaf community:** belonging through NZSL.
- **Auckland against the provinces; West Coasters and Southlanders:** regional insider labels.
- **Rainbow youth in schools:** belonging at school, a question for the Youth2000 survey series (University of Auckland *check*).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich (NZAVS, school surveys, community histories); thin for smaller Pacific and Asian groups outside Auckland.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese (Cantonese and Mandarin); NZSL.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** pepeha and whakapapa are personal; racism and the Dawn Raids are painful; citizenship rules go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (a category, nothing named).
