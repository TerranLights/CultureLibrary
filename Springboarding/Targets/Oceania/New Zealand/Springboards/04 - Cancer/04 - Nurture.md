*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kōhanga reo whānau against mainstream early childhood families:** who does the raising, and in which language.
- **Tūhoe and Ngāti Kahungunu households:** Rose Pere's own upbringing as a starting case; compare other iwi accounts.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands families:** church, language preschool and older siblings in raising the young.
- **Pākehā parents in Playcentre or in paid care:** a parent-led model against a bought one; class differences to test.
- **Grandparent-headed households:** who steps in when parents cannot.
- **Chinese and Korean families with visiting grandparents:** three-generation child-raising.
- **Farm families:** children reared among animals and seasons.
- **Deaf children of hearing parents:** nurture across a language gap, and NZSL's place in it.
- **Fathers raising children alone or at home:** a less visible pattern.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich: cohort studies, Plunket records, early childhood data; older Māori and Pacific child-rearing mainly in memoirs, songs and elders' accounts.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Chinese, NZSL.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the smacking debate shades into law (the 2007 change), which belongs to Libra or Capricorn; oriori are owned by their lines.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category; held under the standing rule).
