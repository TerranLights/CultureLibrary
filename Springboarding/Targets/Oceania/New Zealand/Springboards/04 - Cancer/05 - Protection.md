## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taranaki iwi at Parihaka:** protection by nonviolence; the memory of it today.
- **Tūhoe:** the 2007 police raids at Rūātoki, where the state was felt as the threat, not the shield.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu whānau in Hawke's Bay:** at the center of the child-removal dispute.
- **Samoan families:** the brother's guardianship of his sisters; how it holds or changes in New Zealand.
- **Muslim communities in Christchurch and elsewhere:** protection after an attack aimed at them.
- **Coastal families against inland ones:** water safety as a family duty.
- **Rural and farming households against cities:** firearms kept for farm work, help far away; a contrast to test, not assume.
- **Whānau with gang ties:** protection inside and from the gang.
- **Canterbury and East Coast households:** protection learned through earthquake and flood.

## 4. Flags
