# New Zealand: Abandonment (Cancer): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, NZSL. **Concept in one line:** leaving someone or something one was bound to care for or stay with; and the fear, and the experience, of being left.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Māori children placed in state care from the 1950s to the 1980s:** removal from whānau and then neglect; their share among survivors is to be checked against the inquiry.
- **Pākehā and Māori birth mothers of the closed-adoption years:** forced leaving, not chosen leaving.
- **Tongan and Samoan families:** grandparents and children left in the islands, or elders left alone in Auckland.
- **Chinese families of the poll-tax era:** wives and children left in Guangdong.
- **Deaf children sent to residential schools** (Kelston, van Asch): separation from hearing families, as Deaf people tell it.
- **West Coast and Taranaki towns left by mining and meat works:** places abandoned, and the families who stayed.
- **Rural elders whose children moved to cities or to Australia:** the measured side of being left.
- **Rainbow youth cut off by family:** a question to test, not assume.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich for state care, adoption and migration; mixed for desertion inside marriages; thin for older Pacific and Moriori accounts.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, NZSL.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse-in-care testimony is traumatic and some is suppressed; adoption records may be closed; child-support and adoption law go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only; nothing named).
