## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Porou:** Ngata's leadership and the Ngarimu VC; recognition earned through war service.
- **Waikato-Tainui and Ngāi Tahu:** early large settlements; how each iwi received a Crown apology.
- **Taranaki iwi and Parihaka:** a later Crown apology (2017 *check*) after long non-recognition.
- **Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei:** Bastion Point; recognition as Auckland's people of the land.
- **Samoans, Tongans and others affected by the Dawn Raids:** apologies decades on; compare generations' responses.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** goldfields-era families who sought the poll tax apology, against recent arrivals with other concerns.
- **Skilled migrants (Korean, Filipino and others):** credentials recognized or not.
- **Women:** suffrage commemorations and who is honored; the share of honors over time.
- **Rural workers and volunteers:** shearing, A&P and fire-brigade awards.
- **Deaf community:** NZSL made an official language (2006 *check*); recognition through status.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich: public honors lists, apology texts, naming decisions and awards records are open.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (mihi, whaikōrero acknowledgments); Pacific languages for community responses to apologies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** apologies touch grief and unfinished claims; settlement law goes to Libra; some iwi reject Crown honors or titles, which needs careful handling.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. DPMC: download honors lists for 1990-2025; tag by sector, gender and region.
2. Settlement documents: collect the Crown apology sections of five iwi settlements from different decades.
3. Geographic Board: list every dual or restored name and its decision date.
4. Papers Past: search "dux" and "prize-giving" for 1880-1950 school reports.
5. Library: Manying Ip on the poll tax and its apology; then Samoan and Tongan responses to the 2002 and 2021 apologies in Pacific media.
