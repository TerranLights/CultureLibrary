# New Zealand: Being seen (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori, New Zealand Sign Language; Samoan and Tongan. **Concept in one line:** being noticed and perceived as one really is; the need to be witnessed, and the rules for who may be visible, when and to whom.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mataawaka and urban Māori:** being seen by the home marae when living in Auckland or Wellington; kanohi kitea at a distance.
- **Tūhoe:** visibility as risk after surveillance and raids.
- **Moriori and Chatham Islanders:** long absent from national pictures of the country.
- **Pacific youth born in New Zealand:** seen as "islanders" at school and as "palagi" or "plastic" on visits to the islands (*check* terms).
- **Asian New Zealanders:** long-settled Chinese families taken for newcomers; Korean and Filipino communities seen mainly through migration debates.
- **Muslim New Zealanders:** a small community made visible by an attack.
- **Disabled people and the Deaf community:** counted or not; NZSL interpreters on national broadcasts.
- **Older people living alone, especially rural:** who notices them.
- **Night and service workers:** cleaners, drivers and care aides.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for official counting and representation debates; thinner for everyday gaze norms (eye contact, greeting), which need fieldwork.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Samoan and Tongan for relational terms; NZSL.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse-in-care testimony, the 2019 attacks and the 2007 raids are traumatic and some proceedings are restricted; tangihanga are private family events. Surveillance law goes to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
1. Stats NZ: find the 2018 Census quality panel reports and the coverage rates by ethnicity.

4. NZ On Screen: watch the first season of *bro'Town* and gather its review coverage.
