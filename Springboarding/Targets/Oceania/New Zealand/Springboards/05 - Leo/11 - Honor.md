# New Zealand: Honor (Leo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English (published titles keep the spelling "honour"), te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan; Mandarin and Cantonese. **Concept in one line:** worth held in others' eyes and one's own, won and kept by conduct (keeping one's word, courage, loyalty, sexual propriety) and lost by insult or failure; it can be defended.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Porou and the East Coast:** the Māori Battalion's C Company; service as a source of iwi honor.
- **Waikato-Tainui:** opposition to conscription in the First World War (*check*); honor in refusal.
- **Taranaki iwi:** Parihaka's code of peaceful resistance; honor without arms.
- **Tūhoe:** honor and loyalty within the iwi against the state's claims.
- **Black Power and Mongrel Mob members and families:** patch codes, often Māori and Pacific, studied apart from crime.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** service, titles and family name; daughters' and sons' conduct.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** face in business and family; long-settled against recent.
- **Pākehā rural men:** the handshake deal and the "good keen man".
- **Military families and veterans:** RSA life and moral injury.
- **Pacifists and conscientious objectors:** honor in refusal (Baxter's family; Quakers).

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for military honor, gang history and Māori warfare; mixed for family honor in Pacific and Asian communities, which needs fieldwork and care.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (mana, utu; the Ballara sources), Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war dead and battalion memory are family taonga; gang research needs ethics; family-honor topics can touch violence against women; revenge goes to Scorpio, duty and the honour of the Crown to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
