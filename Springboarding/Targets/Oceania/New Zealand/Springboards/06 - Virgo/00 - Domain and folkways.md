# New Zealand: The domain: work and service (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English and te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Niuean and Chinese for community sources. **Concept in one line:** the sign's domain (work, service, daily routine, health): what good work and a well-kept life are, and how these things are grouped.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tūhoe in Te Urewera:** whether marae duties give the week a different shape from wage towns.
- **Rakiura Māori and Kāi Tahu:** a harvest season held by descent, balanced against paid jobs.
- **Ngāti Porou in Tairāwhiti:** lost forestry and farm jobs, and what filled the day after.
- **Pākehā of Otago and Southland, Canterbury, and Auckland:** a Presbyterian founding, a pastoral-run economy and a commuter city; does talk about work differ?
- **West Coast towns:** mining, timber and whitebait seasons, and dangerous work remembered.
- **Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands, Niuean and Tokelauan families:** church and village-association calendars in the week, each group separate.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** goldfields and market-garden descendants against recent arrivals; family-business hours to study, not assume.
- **Croatian (Dalmatian) families of Northland and West Auckland:** gumdigging, then vineyards.
- **Chatham Islanders, Moriori among them:** fishing and farming far from mainland routines.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, Papers Past and Te Ara; mixed for Māori categories (strong surveys, uneven by iwi); thin for Pacific and Asian communities' own words for work and rest.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori with iwi dialect terms; Samoan and Tongan; Cantonese and Mandarin for older Chinese records.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Tītī Islands belong to rights-holders; marae kitchen and food rules follow tapu and noa and vary by iwi; some maramataka knowledge is taught only in wānanga. Employment and holiday law go to Libra; the clock and public time to Capricorn.
- **Scope:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core lands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
