# New Zealand: Work (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands Māori for Pacific workers; Cantonese and Croatian for two older migrant work histories. **Concept in one line:** purposeful effort that produces, keeps up or provides something of value, paid or unpaid, and the place that effort holds in a life.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Kahungunu in Hawke's Bay:** freezing-works jobs that closed (Whakatū, 1986, *check*) and orchard work after.
- **Ngāti Tūwharetoa and the Kaingaroa forest towns:** forestry and pulp-mill work, and job loss after state forests were sold.
- **Ngāpuhi and Te Rarawa in Te Tai Tokerau:** gumfields, then forestry, then seasonal and urban work.
- **Kāi Tahu:** mahinga kai beside wage work; whether food-gathering counts as work in family talk.
- **Māori who moved to Auckland and Porirua after 1945:** factory and railway work in the city, and what it changed.
- **Cook Islanders in Tokoroa:** a migrant community formed around one mill.
- **Samoan and Tongan workers of 1960s-70s Auckland factories:** shift work, then the 1970s overstayer raids.
- **Shearing gangs:** often Māori-led, with their own ranks (shearer, rousie, presser).
- **Pākehā sharemilkers in Waikato and Taranaki:** a rung-by-rung path to owning a dairy farm.
- **Croatian-Māori (Tarara) and Chinese grower families:** family labor across generations.
- **Disabled workers:** sheltered workshops (IHC and others) closed in the 2000s; worth and pay at issue.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for statistics, labor history and strike records; mixed for Māori and Pacific work histories (strong in Tribunal reports and community histories, thin in surveys before the 1980s); thin for informal and household work.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori; Croatian and Cantonese for family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the Dawn Raids and the 2021 government apology; Pike River families and the drift recovery; RSE workers' conditions are disputed. Employment law (the 1991 and 2000 Acts) goes to Libra; work discipline as a moral norm to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
