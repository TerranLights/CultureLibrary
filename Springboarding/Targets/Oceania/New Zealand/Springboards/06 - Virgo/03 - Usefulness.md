## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kaumātua and kuia on their own marae:** roles on the paepae and at the karanga; whether usefulness grows with age there in a way it does not in city life.
- **Urban Māori elders away from their marae:** whether loss of a role follows the move.
- **Samoan and Tongan grandparents:** minding children so parents can work; elders brought over for that purpose.
- **Chinese grandparents on parent visas in Auckland:** a similar pattern, with language isolation added.
- **Retired Pākehā farmers in small towns:** off the land but still "useful" through boards, brigades and sheds.
- **Disabled New Zealanders and their families:** where worth is argued in terms other than output.
- **Teenage paper-run and farm-help workers:** rural against urban children's practical tasks.
- **Chatham Islanders and Stewart Island residents:** small places where every skill is needed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good on ageing, disability and policy; thin on everyday talk of being useful or a burden, which needs interviews, letters and forums.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan; Mandarin and Cantonese for migrant elders.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** abuse in state and church care; eugenic ideas in early 20th-century New Zealand (a topic to check); assisted dying; disabled people's own voices should lead.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
