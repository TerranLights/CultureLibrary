## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Kahungunu ki Wairarapa:** home of the whare wānanga teachers Smith recorded; how the iwi today reads that record.
- **Ngāti Kahu and Te Tai Tokerau navigators:** wayfinding as evidence-based knowledge taught by practice.
- **Ngāi Tahu:** an iwi with its own research center and whakapapa database; iwi as analysts of their own people.
- **Tūhoe:** long observation of Te Urewera forest and seasons, now under co-governance; local knowledge against agency science.
- **Waikato and Taranaki dairy farmers:** benchmarking data and farm consultants against inherited judgment.
- **University cities (Dunedin, Christchurch, Palmerston North):** older science institutions; town and gown.
- **Auckland's tech and finance sector:** analysis as paid work.
- **Pacific researchers and communities:** talanoa and kakala methods against standard surveys.
- **Chinese and Korean families:** subject choices in science and mathematics, studied rather than assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for science history, statistics, inquiries and the 2021 dispute; mixed for Māori and Pacific ways of reasoning (strong recent scholarship, older sources filtered through Pākehā recorders).
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (technical vocabulary is partly new coinage; *check* dates); Tongan and Samoan; Mandarin and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the mātauranga-science dispute is heated; whare wānanga knowledge was tapu and some is still restricted; linked data raises privacy and sovereignty issues; Erebus remains painful.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
