*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tūhoe at Maungapōhatu and Ruatāhuna:** a community that weighed a prophet's claims; families remember the choice differently.
- **Kāi Tahu Poutini on the West Coast:** keepers of pounamu; telling the stone's kind and source.
- **Ngāti Porou and Te Tai Tokerau honey-producing trusts:** Māori land owners whose product's genuineness is contested abroad.
- **Marlborough, Central Otago and Hawke's Bay:** three wine districts; whether local taste and judging standards differ.
- **High-country runs of Canterbury and Otago:** stud breeders and classers judging stock and wool.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** the weight of elders' and ministers' counsel in choosing partners and paths.
- **Chinese-speaking New Zealanders:** targeted by impersonation scams in their own language (*check* reporting).
- **Older New Zealanders in small towns:** fraud warnings aimed at them; trust in strangers.
- **Wellington and Auckland media audiences:** news trust split by age, politics and ethnicity.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for authenticity disputes, scams and news trust; mixed for traditional modes of judging (matakite, signs) where much is oral; thin for everyday "reading people" except in fiction and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan; Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** matakite and prophetic movements are living faiths; star and season knowledge may be taught only in wānanga; misinformation research is politically charged. Legal sides of trademarks and discrimination go to Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
