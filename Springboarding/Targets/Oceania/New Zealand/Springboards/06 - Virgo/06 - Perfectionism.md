# New Zealand: Perfectionism (Virgo): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** New Zealand English, te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for performance and church standards; Mandarin for school-pressure sources. **Concept in one line:** holding oneself, others or one's work to a standard of flawlessness, and the striving, checking and distress that come with it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Kapa haka groups of Te Arawa, Tainui and Tūhoe:** different stage traditions, each judged by one national standard.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu and Wairarapa shearing families:** a home ground of competitive shearing; pride in clean work.
- **Bay of Plenty packhouse workers:** RSE, local Māori and backpacker staff applying fruit standards.
- **Samoan and Tongan students at Auckland secondary schools:** Polyfest rehearsal alongside NCEA pressure.
- **Chinese and Korean families:** school-pressure patterns to be measured, not presumed.
- **Christchurch and Wellington builders:** the trade's own account of shortcuts and the rebuild after the earthquakes.
- **Elite athletes, especially in cycling and rowing:** pressure, inquiries and change.
- **Rural women in baking and craft sections of local shows:** quiet standards kept over generations.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; rich for judged contests and sport reviews; thin for everyday household perfectionism and for Pacific and Asian families' own accounts.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (ritual terms); Samoan and Tongan; Mandarin and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** karakia knowledge may be tapu; athlete deaths and youth distress need care; students' mental health. Building-code law goes to Libra; discipline as an ideal to Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
