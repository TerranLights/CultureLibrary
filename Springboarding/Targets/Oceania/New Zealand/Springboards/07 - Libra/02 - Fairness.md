# New Zealand: Fairness (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, Tagalog. **Concept in one line:** even-handed treatment in everyday dealings: getting one's due share, taking turns, and being held to the same rules as everyone else.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Owners of Māori land in many-share blocks:** fragmented titles make fairness among kin a daily legal matter; compare Ngāti Porou incorporations with small Northland blocks.
- **Care-sector workers, many of them Pacific women:** the pay-equity cases were brought largely on their work.
- **Filipino and other migrant farm workers in Canterbury and Southland:** exploitation cases test whether the same rules reach everyone.
- **Pākehā farmers against urban wage earners:** different stakes in tax fairness debates (land, capital gains, rates).
- **Renters against owners across generations:** housing costs as the newest fairness complaint.
- **Chinese and Korean small-business owners:** queues, prices and treatment by inspectors seen from the counter side.
- **Sports communities (rugby, cricket, netball):** fair-play norms with their own enforcers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in consumer, pay and tax records and television archives; thinner on everyday turn-taking, which needs fieldwork or forum material.
- **Languages needed:** English for most sources; te reo Māori for proverbs and land records; Tagalog and Chinese for migrant-worker and community forums.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the "bludger" label has targeted beneficiaries and particular groups; handle quotations with care. Māori land ownership lists name living people.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
