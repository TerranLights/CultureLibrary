# New Zealand: Indecision (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tagalog, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** difficulty or failure in reaching a decision: weighing too long, wavering, putting a choice off, or leaving it to others.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi:** long disputes over who holds the mandate to settle make delay itself part of the history; use the iwi's own accounts.
- **Tūhoe:** a long negotiation that did end in settlement, for contrast.
- **Aucklanders against regional voters:** big transport decisions made, reversed and remade in one city while the rest watch.
- **Canterbury residents after the earthquakes:** a rebuild where official choices were slow and changed.
- **Samoan and Tongan congregations:** decisions by council and elders, where slowness may signal respect.
- **Filipino community:** *bahala na* as a phrase to test, not a trait.
- **School leavers and young adults:** delayed choices of study, work and home, by region.
- **Whānau making end-of-life choices together:** collective decisions set against the individual consent the law assumes.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for public decisions (referendums, coalition talks, infrastructure); thin for private indecision, which needs survey or interview work.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for hui and divination sources; Samoan, Tongan and Tagalog for community decision-making.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Ngāpuhi mandate disputes are live; *niu* and seers belong to tohunga knowledge that some iwi restrict; end-of-life material involves grief.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.

## 5. First queries
