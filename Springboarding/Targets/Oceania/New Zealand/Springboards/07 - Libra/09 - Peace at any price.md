# New Zealand: Peace at any price (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Moriori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese, Korean. **Concept in one line:** keeping peace or harmony by giving way, staying silent or paying whatever it costs, even when the cost is one's own (or someone else's) rights, truth or justice.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Moriori of Rēkohu:** a pacifist covenant and its terrible cost; tell it with the Moriori trust's framing, and with care toward the descendants of the 1835 invaders.
- **Taranaki iwi at Parihaka:** principled nonviolence that refused both war and surrender.
- **Pākehā peace churches and objectors:** conscience against conformity in two world wars.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** relational care and respect for elders that may keep disputes private; test, do not assume.
- **Chinese and Korean New Zealanders:** endurance and mood-reading as everyday norms, by generation.
- **Members of closed religious communities (West Coast):** harmony as a rule of membership.
- **Waikato and Hamilton in 1981:** the cancelled match as the point where a quarrel broke into the open.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for pacifist history, family-violence surveys and the 1981 tour; thinner for everyday conflict avoidance, which needs workplace and family studies.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Moriori terms from Moriori sources; Samoan and Tongan; Chinese and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the 1835 events on Rēkohu are painful to more than one people; family-violence and closed-community material involves living survivors; never cast a group as conflict-avoidant.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
