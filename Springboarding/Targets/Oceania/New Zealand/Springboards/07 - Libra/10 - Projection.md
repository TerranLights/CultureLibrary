# New Zealand: Projection (Libra): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** seeing in others (partners, rivals, enemies, outsiders) what one disowns in oneself: one's own faults, impulses, force or fears; and how a culture places its own unwanted traits and troubles on an outside "other".

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Pacific communities in Auckland and Wellington:** the raids made them the problem of a downturn; their memory of it is documented.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** early 1900s hostility, and 1990s anti-immigration rhetoric aimed at newer Asian arrivals.
- **Muslim communities, above all in Christchurch:** targets of imported hatred; their own accounts first.
- **Māori in crime and welfare news:** a long pattern to test by content analysis, not assume.
- **Young people:** from 1950s "delinquents" to "boy racers"; folk devils of each decade.
- **Aucklanders and the regions:** jokes and slurs each side uses about the other, as mirror images.
- **Gang communities (Mongrel Mob, Black Power):** frequent folk devils; get their own and whānau voices.
- **Diaspora (comparison only):** New Zealanders in Australia, whose images of the two nations mirror each other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for moral panics, inquiries, apologies and press archives; thin for private projection in couples and families, which needs therapy literature and interviews.
- **Languages needed:** English; te reo Māori (mākutu and healing sources); Samoan and Tongan (raids testimonies); Chinese (community press).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mākutu beliefs and related court cases involve grief and restricted knowledge; abuse prosecutions name living people; the 2019 attack is recent trauma.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
