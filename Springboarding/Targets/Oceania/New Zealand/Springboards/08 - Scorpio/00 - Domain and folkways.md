# New Zealand: The domain: death and transformation (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (New Zealand usage), te reo Māori, New Zealand Sign Language; Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Niuean, Chinese (Cantonese and Mandarin) for the larger communities. **Concept in one line:** death and transformation: how death, power and the hidden are faced, with the folkways of death, sex, shared resources and inheritance.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi and hapū, separately:** Ngāpuhi, Ngāti Porou, Waikato-Tainui, Tūhoe, Te Arawa, Ngāi Tahu; tangihanga order and speaking rights differ by marae kawa.
- **Moriori of Rēkohu (Chatham Islands):** their own traditions of the dead; never folded into Māori.
- **Pākehā by settler stream:** Otago Scots, Canterbury Anglicans, West Coast Irish Catholics, Northland Dalmatians; burial and wills may sort along these lines.
- **Pacific peoples:** Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands, Niuean and Tokelauan families; funeral gift exchange and sending bodies to the islands.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** goldfields-era Cantonese families against recent migrants, for grave visiting.
- **Faith communities:** Rātana, Ringatū, Latter-day Saints, Jewish and Muslim New Zealanders, each with its own burial order.
- **Rural and urban:** farming and mining towns against Auckland, for cremation and funeral-home use.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Māori death ways and war remembrance; mixed on Pākehā mourning and couples' money; thin on adult sex ways.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori with dialect awareness (Kāi Tahu forms in the south), Samoan, Tongan, Cantonese; NZ Sign Language for Deaf community practice.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some karakia, whakapapa and burial places are tapu and held by iwi; approach through iwi authorities. Kōiwi tangata are never specimens.
- **Lands:** the nation's exact post-war lands are still to be settled; the Scorpio sheets are written for the core islands.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, whose death and inheritance ways would otherwise be a unit here.
