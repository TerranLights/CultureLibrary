*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Te Arawa:** geothermal waters of Rotorua and Ōhinemutu, where bathing and cleansing have their own history.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu and Ngāti Porou:** lifting tapu after a death in rural marae settings, which may differ by kawa.
- **Waikato-Tainui:** the Waikato River cleanup under iwi co-governance, told in the iwi's own terms.
- **Tauranga Moana iwi:** the *Rena* and reef pollution.
- **Samoan, Tongan and other Pacific families:** traditional healers and church fasting alongside the memory of the Dawn Raids.
- **Germans and other WWI-era migrants:** internment and name changes.
- **Christchurch and the Hutt Valley:** two moral panics forty years apart.
- **Temperance districts and "wet" districts:** Presbyterian and Methodist strongholds against West Coast pubs.
- **Closed religious communities:** Gloriavale (West Coast) and the Exclusive Brethren, for shunning.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on temperance, moral panics and contamination; mixed on rongoā (restricted parts); thin on everyday detox and decluttering.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori karakia registers (handled with leave), Samoan and Tongan healing terms.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** karakia for lifting tapu belong to tohunga and iwi; the Crèche case and police inquiry involve living people; eating disorders need care.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for cleansing customs.
