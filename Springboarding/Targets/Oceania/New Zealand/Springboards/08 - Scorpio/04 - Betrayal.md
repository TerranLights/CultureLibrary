# New Zealand: Betrayal (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** breaking the trust of those who relied on one: turning against, deserting, deceiving or exposing people or a cause owed loyalty.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Taranaki iwi:** Parihaka's memory of betrayal by the Crown, kept through monthly gatherings.
- **Ngāi Tahu:** a claim held across seven generations; betrayal handed down as a family duty.
- **Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei:** Takaparawhau (Bastion Point) and land promised but taken.
- **Ngāti Porou and Te Arawa:** alliances with the Crown, and how "kūpapa" is weighed inside these iwi.
- **Samoan New Zealanders:** Black Saturday and the Dawn Raids as betrayals by the country they live in.
- **Waterfront and mining unions:** strikebreaking and loyalty in Lyttelton, Wellington and the West Coast.
- **Farming districts:** 1973 and the loss of the British market.
- **Women patients and survivors of care:** betrayal by doctors, the state and churches.
- **Gang members:** codes against informing, and the cost of breaking them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Crown–iwi grievance and on institutional inquiries; mixed on labor history; thin on private infidelity.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (Tribunal evidence is often given in te reo), Samoan for Mau memory.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "kūpapa" is a charged word for living descendants; survivors' testimony is personal; family betrayals inside whānau are private.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for loyalty and betrayal.
