# New Zealand: Control (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin. **Concept in one line:** directing or restraining people, events and one's own life, and the felt need to do so (self-control as such is Capricorn's; freedom from control is Aries's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tūhoe:** Rūātoki raids and the iwi's own governance of Te Urewera.
- **Ngāti Porou and Te Tai Tokerau iwi:** lockdown checkpoints run by iwi.
- **Ngāti Tūwharetoa and Whanganui iwi:** waters taken under state hydro control.
- **Southland and the West Coast:** Manapōuri; hunters and farmers against 1080.
- **Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands and Niuean families:** discipline across church and home, with generational change.
- **Chinese and Korean families:** parental direction of schooling, as a question.
- **Auckland and the rest:** the longer 2021 Auckland lockdown against other regions.
- **Disabled people and their families:** institutional control and its end.
- **Students and young people:** Dunedin's licensed disorder; curfews.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on lockdown, family violence and environmental control; mixed on parenting; thin on everyday fate talk.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (rāhui notices are bilingual), Samoan and Tongan for Pacific parenting literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** rāhui are laid by those with mana to lay them; family-violence data needs care; Operation Eight involves living people.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for family control and lockdown experience.
