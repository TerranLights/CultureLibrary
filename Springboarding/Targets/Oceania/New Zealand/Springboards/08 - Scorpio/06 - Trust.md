# New Zealand: Trust (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin, Korean. **Concept in one line:** willingness to rely on a person or institution, accepting the risk of being let down; loyalty is researched here. ## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi:** internal trust and mandate, set against Crown trust.
- **Ngāi Tahu and Waikato-Tainui:** iwi that settled early, for trust after settlement.
- **Tūhoe:** a people that has refused or limited reliance on the state, for its own reasons.
- **Pākehā small towns against Auckland:** honesty boxes and unlocked doors as a question, not a given.
- **Dairy and sheep farmers:** trust in cooperatives and in handshake dealing.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands families:** the church and the minister as trusted figures.
- **Chinese and Korean New Zealanders:** guanxi, church networks and Chinese-language social media.
- **Refugee communities (Somali, Syrian, Afghan):** trust in authorities after flight.
- **Older people:** scam victims and finance-company investors.
- **Deaf New Zealanders:** reliance on interpreters.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on survey trust (several series); mixed on everyday trust; thin on loyalty as such.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for iwi records; for the church and social-media sides of trust, Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin and Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** mandate disputes are live and personal; vaccine trust became politically charged.
- **Cross-reference:** trust in police and courts is Capricorn's; do not research it here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for trust networks.
