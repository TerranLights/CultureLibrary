# New Zealand: Revenge (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin. **Concept in one line:** harming someone in return for a harm they did, to get even, restore standing or deter the next wrong; with its alternatives (payment, apology, forgiveness).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi:** Hongi Hika's campaigns and how Northland iwi remember them.
- **Ngāti Toa Rangatira:** the southern raids and Wairau, as the iwi tells it.
- **Ngāi Tahu:** the Kai Huanga feud inside the iwi, and memory of Te Rauparaha's raids *check*.
- **Moriori and Taranaki iwi on Rēkohu:** a pact against killing and the 1835 invasion, with descendants on both sides.
- **Rongowhakaata and Ngāti Porou:** Matawhero and its reprisals.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** ifoga and church mediation as ways to end payback.
- **Gang whānau:** feud codes and truces.
- **Pākehā rural districts:** feuds between neighbors over boundaries and water, as a question.
- **Muslim communities after 15 March 2019:** public responses to an attack, including refusals of revenge.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on 19th-century reprisal; mixed on gangs; thin on private grudges.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (utu is easily mistranslated as revenge only), Samoan for ifoga.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Musket Wars killings are still live in iwi relations; the 1835 invasion of Rēkohu concerns living descendants on both sides; gang retaliation involves current cases. Private vengeance set against the state's punishment is Capricorn's.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for feud and forgiveness.
