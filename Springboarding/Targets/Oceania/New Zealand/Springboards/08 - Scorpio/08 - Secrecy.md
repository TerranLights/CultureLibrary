# New Zealand: Secrecy (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin. **Concept in one line:** keeping knowledge hidden on purpose, and how a culture judges what may, must or must not be kept hidden; privacy included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Kahungunu ki Wairarapa:** the whare wānanga teachings and who may speak of them now.
- **Iwi with silent files (e.g., Ngāti Whātua in Tāmaki, Waikato hapū):** different choices on disclosing sacred sites.
- **Adoptees and birth mothers of the closed-adoption era:** sealed records and later reunions; Māori whāngai families as a contrast.
- **Rainbow New Zealanders before and after 1986:** the closet across generations and between town and city.
- **Exclusive Brethren:** a church that separates itself and keeps its affairs private.
- **People of the intelligence stations (Waihopai, Tangimoana):** secrecy as a job, in a small community.
- **Pacific families:** illness, debt and family trouble handled inside the aiga or kāinga.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** family shame and privacy across generations.
- **Pākehā small towns against Auckland and Wellington:** how much stays private where everyone knows everyone.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on state secrecy and adoption; mixed on restricted Māori knowledge (by design); thin on private secrets.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for whare wānanga and Tribunal evidence; Samoan and Tongan; Mandarin.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** restricted knowledge is recorded only as iwi explain it and is never retold; silent-file locations must not be sought out; adoption files are personal. Official-information law belongs to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for family privacy.
