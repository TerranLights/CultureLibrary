# New Zealand: Power (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Mandarin. **Concept in one line:** the capacity to make things happen and to affect others, with or against their will, and how its holders, holding and use are judged (legitimate authority is Capricorn's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui and the Kīngitanga:** a monarchy beside the state.
- **Ngāpuhi:** He Whakaputanga and northern claims of sovereignty.
- **Tūhoe:** mana motuhake in Te Urewera; power held apart from the state.
- **Ngāti Porou:** Apirana Ngata's parliamentary path to power.
- **Ngāi Tahu:** a corporate iwi model of economic power.
- **Rātana followers:** faith turned into votes.
- **Canterbury and Hawke's Bay landholding families:** old rural Pākehā power.
- **Wellington's public servants and Auckland's business elite:** two centers of Pākehā power.
- **Unions (West Coast miners, Auckland waterfront):** collective power.
- **Samoan and Tongan communities:** matai titles, nobles and church office.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** business associations and the first MPs.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on Crown–iwi power and on electoral reform; mixed on business elites; thin on power inside families.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for mana and the Treaty texts; Samoan and Tongan chiefly registers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Kīngitanga succession and internal iwi politics are live; rank among Pacific nobles carries protocol.
- **Cross-reference:** legitimacy, authority and the Treaty as law go to Capricorn and Libra.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for business and political power.
