# New Zealand: Crisis (Scorpio): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāi Tahu and Christchurch's east side:** the red zone and unequal recovery.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu, Rongowhakaata and Ngāti Porou:** marae relief after Cyclone Gabrielle.
- **Ngāti Kurī (Kaikōura):** the 2016 earthquake, with the town cut off.
- **Ngāti Awa:** Whakaari/White Island (2019) and responsibility for a sacred volcano's tourists.
- **Ngāti Rangi:** Ruapehu and the Tangiwai lahar.
- **West Coast mining towns:** Pike River beside the older mine disasters of the region.
- **The Muslim community of Christchurch:** 15 March and its aftermath.
- **Pacific communities of South Auckland:** church networks in lockdown and in island disasters abroad.
- **Deaf New Zealanders:** NZ Sign Language interpreting at emergency briefings.
- **Farmers and growers:** drought, silt and flood, with rural suicide as a silence.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on earthquakes and recent inquiries; mixed on economic crises; thin on private family crisis.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for iwi relief reports; NZ Sign Language; Samoan and Tongan for church responses.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 15 March and Pike River concern bereaved families; the attacker is not named or quoted; suicide follows media guidelines.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, for disaster and crisis response.
