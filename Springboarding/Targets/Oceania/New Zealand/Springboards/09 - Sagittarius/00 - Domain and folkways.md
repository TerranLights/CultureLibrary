## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi with their own prophetic churches:** Rongowhakaata and Tūhoe (Ringatū; Te Kooti and Rua Kēnana), Ngāti Apa and Whanganui iwi near Rātana Pā, Taranaki iwi around Parihaka; each church's history is told differently by its own people.
- **Ngāpuhi and the Bay of Islands:** first mission ground, Papahurihia's movement; the oldest layer of contact.
- **Ngāi Tahu and its papatipu rūnanga:** a different missionary and settler history from the north.
- **Moriori of Rēkohu (Chatham Islands):** a separate people, tradition and revival; not a sub-group of Māori.
- **Otago and Southland Presbyterian Scots versus Canterbury Anglican settlement:** two founding church orders to separate.
- **Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands, Niuean and Fijian congregations in South Auckland, Porirua and Christchurch:** church as village; denominations differ by island.
- **Chinese New Zealanders (Otago goldfield families, recent migrants), Korean and Filipino congregations:** distinct faith mixes and migration stories.
- **Muslim communities, Christchurch above all after 2019:** handle with care.
- **Secular urban Wellington and Auckland professionals versus Waikato and Northland towns:** the "none" share is uneven.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Christian history and census counts; mixed for living Māori and Pacific religious practice, where much is held by the communities themselves.
- **Territory:** the nation's exact lands are still to be settled; this sign's sheets are written for the core islands.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (including the *niupepa*), Samoan and Tongan for Pacific churches, Chinese and Korean for migrant congregations.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** tapu knowledge, whare wānanga lore and karakia may be restricted by iwi; the Io tradition is contested; the 2019 Christchurch mosque attacks; Gloriavale and other closed communities; the 1907 suppression of tohunga (its text belongs to Libra and Capricorn).
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
