# New Zealand: Faith (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Korean and Chinese for congregations. **Concept in one line:** trust in and commitment to what can't be seen or proven (God, gods, spirits, a sacred order) and, by extension, secular faith in people, progress, science or the nation.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ringatū communities (Rongowhakaata, Tūhoe, Ngāti Awa, Whakatōhea):** faith founded in war and exile; scripture-based, with its own sabbath.
- **Rātana adherents (Rangitīkei-Whanganui, Northland, Waikato):** faith founded in healing; a political arm.
- **Hokianga and Northland Catholic Māori:** a faith tradition from Pompallier's mission rather than the CMS.
- **Māori Latter-day Saints (Hawke's Bay, Waikato):** faith and genealogy bound together.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands congregations:** faith expressed through obligation, giving and choir; differences between Methodist, Congregational and Catholic islanders.
- **Korean and Chinese evangelical churches in Auckland:** migrant faith, often with English-language youth services.
- **Muslim communities of Christchurch and Auckland:** faith after 2019, from their own accounts.
- **Brethren and closed groups (Gloriavale on the West Coast):** faith as total commitment, and what former members say.
- **Secular Wellington and Dunedin professionals:** faith in science and the state, measured rather than assumed.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for Pākehā churches and survey counts; mixed for Māori church faith, where oral and church-held sources matter most; thin in English for Pacific and Korean congregations.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (scriptural and nineteenth-century register), Samoan, Tongan, Korean.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** karakia that iwi hold as tapu; Ringatū and Rātana internal teaching; grief after the mosque attacks; abuse in faith-based care (the Abuse in Care Royal Commission) as a cause of broken faith; ex-members of closed groups.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (their congregations set aside here).
