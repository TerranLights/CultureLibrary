# New Zealand: Meaning (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for Pacific health and youth work. **Concept in one line:** what makes life, work and suffering worth it: the sense that life has a point, and where that point is found.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Māori respondents by iwi affiliation and by marae connection (Te Kupenga allows this):** whether meaning tracks ties to tūrangawaewae.
- **Tūhoe and Te Urewera communities:** meaning bound to a named homeland returned to the iwi's governance.
- **Pākehā rural men in Southland, Waikato and the King Country:** farm work, isolation and the Farmstrong data.
- **Pacific youth in South Auckland and Porirua:** meaning shaped by church, family duty and sport, with possible tension between them.
- **Retirees and Men's Shed members:** purpose after work ends.
- **Canterbury after the earthquakes:** purpose found or lost in rebuilding.
- **Chinese and Korean migrant families:** education and sacrifice for children as purpose, to be tested.
- **Moriori of Rēkohu:** meaning tied to Nunuku's covenant of peace and its revival, from Moriori sources.
- **Urban Wellington and Auckland graduates:** career, travel and "impact" as sources.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich in official well-being statistics, which are extensive; thinner on meaning in its own right, which has to be teased out of well-being and spirituality items.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for Pacific health literature.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** suicide data (follow media guidance on reporting); youth suicide in particular communities; restricted whakapapa recitations.
- **Held for review:** none.
