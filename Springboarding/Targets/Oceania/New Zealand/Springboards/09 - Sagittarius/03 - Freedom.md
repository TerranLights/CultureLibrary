# New Zealand: Freedom (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for Pacific histories of independence and migration. **Concept in one line:** being able to act, speak, believe and move without constraint by others or by the state; and freedom as a belief and a political ideal.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi and the northern hapū:** freedom read through He Whakaputanga; the Bay of Islands and Hokianga.
- **Waikato-Tainui and Ngāti Maniapoto:** the Kīngitanga, war and confiscation; the King Country as a once-closed district.
- **Taranaki iwi and Parihaka:** freedom through nonviolence, and the memory of imprisonment in the south.
- **Tūhoe:** mana motuhake and Te Urewera; the 2007 police raids as a recent wound.
- **Moriori:** captivity and release; their own revival.
- **Samoan, Tongan and other Pacific communities:** Mau memory, the Dawn Raids, and freedom to stay.
- **Pākehā farming districts:** freedom as private property and the right to exclude, set against walking access.
- **Rainbow communities, Wellington and Auckland:** freedom as living openly, with the 1986 reform as a turning point.
- **Conservative Christian groups:** freedom of conscience and religion as they frame it.
- **2022 occupation participants versus Wellington residents:** two uses of "freedom" in one place.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for protest history and Māori sovereignty movements; mixed for everyday freedom as felt, which needs oral history and survey work.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (nineteenth-century political letters and petitions), Samoan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Moriori enslavement history; the 2007 Urewera raids; the 2022 occupation (live divisions); Treaty interpretation is contested. Legal texts (the Bill of Rights Act, Treaty law) go to Libra and Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** none.
