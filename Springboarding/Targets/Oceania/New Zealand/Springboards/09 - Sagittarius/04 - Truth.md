# New Zealand: Truth (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan and Chinese for community media. **Concept in one line:** what is so, and how a culture decides what is so: whom it trusts to say it, and how it settles disputes over facts.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tūhoe and Ngāti Kahungunu tradition-holders:** whare wānanga lineages and the contested Io teachings; where the line between authentic and later lore is argued.
- **Ngāi Tahu:** extensive published tribal history and claim evidence; written and oral records side by side.
- **Te Arawa:** Rotorua's long contact with tourists and ethnographers, which shaped what was told to outsiders.
- **Moriori:** a people whose story was falsified in schoolbooks and is now told by them.
- **Pacific churchgoers:** pastors as trusted sources, and COVID-19 vaccine messaging through churches.
- **Chinese-language readers on WeChat and similar apps:** a separate news and rumor environment.
- **Scientists, Royal Society fellows and universities:** the science side of the 2021 dispute, which was itself divided.
- **Rural and small-town districts with low vaccine uptake (Northland, the East Coast, parts of the West Coast):** distrust of official sources, to be measured not assumed.
- **Survivors of state and faith-based care:** testimony and belief.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on media trust and public inquiries; mixed on how iwi weigh truth in their own forums, which may not be public.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Chinese for community media; Samoan and Tongan for church media.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whare wānanga knowledge and Io lore (contested and in places restricted); the mātauranga debate is still heated; abuse-in-care testimony; COVID-19 era divisions.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (one misinformation-research pointer set aside on this ground).
