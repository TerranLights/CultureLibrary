# New Zealand: Belief (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan and Chinese for folk and community belief. **Concept in one line:** holding something to be true: what people take to be so about God, the world, people, and right and wrong, religious or secular, and what warrant they want for it.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui and Ngāti Naho (*check*) around the Meremere roading dispute:** taniwha belief in a modern planning fight.
- **Ngāti Kurī and Te Aupōuri:** belief about the dead centered on the far north.
- **Tūhoe:** Best's chief informants; how Tūhoe now read his record of their belief.
- **Liberal and conservative Presbyterians:** the Geering split as a fault line still visible.
- **Christchurch:** a city where a belief-driven panic played out, and later grief and rebuilding.
- **Tongan and Samoan families in Auckland:** spirit belief alongside church, by generation.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** Qingming and grave-tending at old goldfield cemeteries; "superstition" as a label applied from inside and out.
- **Secular Wellingtonians and the humanist societies:** organized disbelief.
- **Former members of Centrepoint and Gloriavale:** belief imposed and then shed.
- **Anti-mandate movement participants, especially in Northland and the Bay of Plenty:** new belief communities, to be studied as they describe themselves.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on church controversies and survey items; mixed on folk belief (scattered across local history and news); thin in English on Pacific spirit belief.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan; Chinese.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** some taniwha and wāhi tapu knowledge is held by iwi; the crèche case remains disputed; ex-member accounts of closed groups involve abuse; anti-mandate research is politically charged.
- **Held for review:** none.
