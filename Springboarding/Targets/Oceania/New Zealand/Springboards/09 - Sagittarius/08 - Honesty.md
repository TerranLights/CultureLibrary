## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rural districts with honesty-box stalls (Canterbury plains, Hawke's Bay and Central Otago orchards) against Auckland:** whether the honor system survives scale and anonymity.
- **Timaru and South Canterbury:** a community hit hard by a finance collapse; trust and blame among neighbors.
- **Ngāi Tahu, Waikato-Tainui and other settled iwi:** the Crown's word as a long historical question.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** where respect for elders and the matai may shape what can be said bluntly; a question to research.
- **Chinese and Korean small-business owners:** honest dealing and trust inside and outside the community, without assuming a single norm.
- **Tradespeople and the building industry:** reputations after the leaky-homes era.
- **University students and secondary-school candidates:** cheating norms by institution.
- **Public servants versus private-sector staff:** written codes against workplace practice.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; strong institutional and consumer records, thin direct study of everyday honesty norms.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan; Chinese and Korean for business communities.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** avoid tying dishonesty to any ethnic group; fraud cases name living people; settlement apologies are politically live.
- **Held for review:** none.
