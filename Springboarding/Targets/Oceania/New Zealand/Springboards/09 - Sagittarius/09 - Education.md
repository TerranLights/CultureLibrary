## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Raukawa, Te Āti Awa and Ngāti Toa:** the tribal plan (1975 onward) that built Te Wānanga o Raukawa, a planned path into higher learning.
- **Ngāti Porou and Te Aute's old boys:** the East Coast's early graduates and their legacy.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu ki Wairarapa:** the lineage named as the source of the 1913 publication (*check*), and its descendants' views on it.
- **Tūhoe:** iwi-run schooling and learning initiatives in Te Urewera (*check*), as the iwi describes them.
- **Pacific first-in-family students (Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands, Niuean) in South Auckland and Porirua:** graduation as a family event and an obligation.
- **Chinese and Korean migrant families:** credential expectations and tutoring, studied from data, not assumption.
- **Dunedin, a university town, against Auckland's commuter campuses.**
- **Southland and West Coast trades pathways:** apprenticeship and the farm against university.
- **Kura kaupapa Māori graduates entering tertiary study.**
- **Mature and second-chance learners:** Te Wānanga o Aotearoa's student base.
