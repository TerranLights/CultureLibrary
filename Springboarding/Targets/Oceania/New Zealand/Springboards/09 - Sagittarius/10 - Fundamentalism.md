# New Zealand: Fundamentalism (Sagittarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for strict-Sabbath and Pentecostal Pacific churches. **Concept in one line:** the insistence that one truth, usually a sacred text or founding doctrine read literally, is complete and binding, and the movement to restore it and defend it against modern change; whether the word fits secular certainties is itself contested.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Tūhoe at Maungapōhatu and Ruatāhuna:** Rua Kēnana's restoration community and its memory.
- **Taranaki and Waikato descendants of Pai Mārire followers:** a label imposed on their ancestors.
- **Ringatū congregations:** scripture-centered worship without the separatist label; useful contrast with Pai Mārire.
- **Exclusive Brethren communities (Christchurch, Palmerston North, Auckland, to confirm):** separation in suburbs, businesses and schools.
- **Gloriavale members and leavers:** a total community on the West Coast, and those who left it.
- **Tongan and Samoan strict-Sabbath congregations:** observance that outsiders may misread; ask what members call it.
- **Pentecostal megachurches versus Destiny Church:** two styles of literal faith with different public faces.
- **Homeschooling families in Canterbury, Waikato and Northland:** to be measured, not assumed.
- **Muslim communities after 2019:** targets of extremism, often wrongly labeled with it.
- **Critics of the 1980s reforms:** the word used against economists rather than believers.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; good histories of nineteenth-century Māori movements and the Brethren, thin and contested data on closed communities today.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori (Pai Mārire and Ringatū texts), Samoan and Tongan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** "fundamentalist" is a slur for many; abuse cases at Gloriavale and in some churches; the 2019 Christchurch attack was ethnonationalist, so handle terrorism and religion with care; avoid labeling Māori prophetic churches with an outsider's term.
- **Held for review:** none.
