# New Zealand: Duty (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English and te reo Māori first; Samoan and Tongan for family and church obligations; Chinese and Korean for duties to parents. **Concept in one line:** what a person owes and must do because of a role, a tie or a promise, whether or not they wish to.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui:** resistance to First World War conscription after the land confiscations; duty to the Crown questioned by grievance.
- **Ngāti Porou and other East Coast iwi:** heavy enlistment in the Māori Battalion; the contrast with Waikato is the point.
- **Otago Christian pacifists and Quakers:** duty to conscience as a rival duty.
- **Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands and Niuean families:** fa'alavelave and church giving; the weight on New Zealand-born young earners.
- **Chinese, Korean and Filipino families:** obligations to parents abroad and parents brought over.
- **Rural districts with volunteer brigades and land search crews:** duty where help is far off.
- **Women and men within each group:** who carries unpaid care, to research, never assume.
- **Iwi trust members:** duties set down in tribal deeds and charters, as each iwi publishes them.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich on war service and volunteering; mixed on family care and Pacific giving; thin on Asian households.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for tribal sources; Samoan and Tongan for church and family giving; some Chinese and Korean for parent-care studies.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** war dead and their whānau; conscientious objectors' treatment; fa'alavelave debt and hardship.
- **Law:** the letter of conscription, jury and care law goes to Libra; this sheet keeps what the duty is felt to be for.
- **Held for review:** one category under the standing rule: South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
