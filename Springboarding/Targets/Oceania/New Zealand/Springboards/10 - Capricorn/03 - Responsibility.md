## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Greymouth and the West Coast coalfields:** a century of mine deaths behind Pike River; who answers, argued by miners' families.
- **Canterbury after the earthquakes:** blame for buildings, insurance and rebuilding.
- **Iwi with settled claims (Waikato-Tainui, Ngāi Tahu, Tūhoe):** how each received the Crown's apology, and the duties it took on after settlement.
- **Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei and Taranaki iwi:** stewardship of returned land and of Parihaka.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** ifoga and similar rites as answerability between families.
- **Pacific communities of the Dawn Raids era:** collective memory of being blamed for overstaying.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** descendants of poll-tax payers and their view of the 2002 apology.
- **Dairy and sheep farmers against urban environmental groups:** responsibility for waterways, argued from both sides.
- **Children who interpret and broker for parents (Pacific, Chinese, Korean, Filipino families):** early answerability at home.
