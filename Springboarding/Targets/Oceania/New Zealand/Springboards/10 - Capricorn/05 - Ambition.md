# New Zealand: Ambition (Capricorn): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Porou and the East Coast:** home of Ngata's program for Māori advancement; ambition for the iwi.
- **Ngāi Tahu and Waikato-Tainui investment arms:** post-settlement wealth and what it is meant to achieve.
- **Rural towns of Northland and the East Cape:** leaving to rise, and the weight on those who stay.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands families:** schooling, church and sport as rival paths for children.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** descendants of goldfields and market-garden families set beside recent professional migrants; two routes upward.
- **Korean and Filipino New Zealanders:** professional migrants and their children's tracks.
- **Pākehā farming families:** ambition tied to land, succession and the farm's growth.
- **Auckland against the provinces:** moving to the city, or abroad, to get on.
- **Women leaders:** the group most studied under the tall poppy label; check against men's reports.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed; the tall poppy idea is talked about far more than it is measured.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for pēpeha; Samoan and Tongan for family hopes; Chinese and Korean for parental ambition.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** stereotypes about Pacific boys and sport, and about Asian students and study; report as claims to test.
- **Held for review:** the standing-rule category, South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
