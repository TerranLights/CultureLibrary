## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Konohi and Ngāti Porou (East Coast):** firstborn and gender in succession, the questions *The Whale Rider* raised.
- **Te Arawa and other iwi with different marae kawa:** speaking rights differ by iwi; compare as each describes its own kawa.
- **Moriori (Rēkohu):** enslaved after the 1835 invasion; rank imposed by conquest, told by Moriori first.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands communities:** island ranks inside Auckland congregations and families.
- **Canterbury's landed families against the West Coast's miners:** gentry and labor in one island.
- **Remuera and Fendalton against South Auckland and Porirua:** ranked suburbs.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** standing within the old Cantonese village-origin societies set beside the rank that new property wealth buys.
- **The Wellington public service:** graded ranks inside a society that prides itself on informality.
- **Gang and prison hierarchies:** ranked orders apart from the state, to keep separate.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; class, iwi rank and inequality are well studied, though the egalitarian story is argued over.
- **Languages needed:** chiefly-rank vocabularies in Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands Māori; te reo for kawa material.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** whakapapa establishing rank is held by iwi; Moriori enslavement history; marae protocol on gender is debated inside iwi.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category only, per the standing rule).
