*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Porou:** an iwi that turned one soldier's Victoria Cross into a collective honor (1943).
- **Māori and Pacific students in health sciences:** targeted entry, and how graduates speak of it.
- **Pākehā families in elite state-school zones:** merit bought through house prices.
- **Chinese and Korean students in NCEA and Scholarship:** exam success as family project.
- **Filipino, Chinese and other skilled migrants:** credentials earned abroad, judged again here.
- **Samoan and Tongan families:** titles and church posts by service, rank or skill.
- **Māori rugby players from the exclusion era:** merit denied by selection policy.
- **Rural and small-town schools against city schools:** who reaches Merit and Excellence.
- **Women on honors and board lists:** a separate line on who is judged deserving.

## 4. Flags
