*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand (Aotearoa), region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English and te reo Māori above all; Samoan and Tongan for village and church codes; Chinese for business dealings. **Concept in one line:** the binding rules of a community and how people relate to them: what law is for, whose it is, whether it is just, and whether to obey, use, bend or avoid it (the spirit; the letter is Libra's).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi:** the flagstaff war and long claims that Crown law was never accepted in the north.
- **Tūhoe:** the 2007 police raids and dropped terrorism charges, read as law against mana motuhake.
- **Whanganui iwi:** the river's legal personhood (2017), where state law took on an iwi idea of the river.
- **Moriori (Rēkohu):** a law of peace, kept and broken.
- **Pacific communities:** overstayer raids of the 1970s and later trust in police.
- **Chinese and Korean business owners:** formal rules against personal obligation in small firms.
- **Rural districts:** self-help where police are far off.
- **Patched gangs:** codes outside state law and the politics of banning patches.
- **Christchurch Muslim communities:** the 2019 gun-law change seen by those it was meant to protect.
- **Māori lawyers (Te Hunga Rōia Māori o Aotearoa) against the wider profession:** two views of what law is for.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich; the meeting of tikanga and state law is one of New Zealand's most written-about legal subjects.
- **Languages needed:** te reo for judgments and wānanga on tikanga; Samoan and Tongan where village rules shape church life.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** hapū decide what of their tikanga is shared; the 2007 raids and gang matters are raw.
- **Law:** statutes and court rules as written belong to Libra; this sheet keeps purpose, obedience and legal culture.
