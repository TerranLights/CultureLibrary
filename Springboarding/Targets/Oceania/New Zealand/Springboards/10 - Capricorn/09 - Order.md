## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi of Tai Tokerau and Te Urewera:** keeping order on their own roads in 2020, with or against police.
- **Ngāpuhi and Waitangi:** the national day as a yearly test of whose order holds.
- **Christchurch red-zone residents:** order imposed after disaster, and what they lost.
- **Pacific communities of 1970s Auckland:** the Polynesian Panthers and police "order" campaigns, as they recall them.
- **Dunedin students against long-term residents:** the town-and-gown quarrel over noise and fire.
- **Provincial towns with boy-racer cultures:** youth car scenes, by their members and their neighbors.
- **Wellington residents, protesters and police (2022):** three accounts of one occupation.
- **Chinese and Korean shopkeepers in Auckland:** robbery, safety and calls for policing.
- **Rēkohu and other small islands:** order kept in a small, isolated community.

## 4. Flags
