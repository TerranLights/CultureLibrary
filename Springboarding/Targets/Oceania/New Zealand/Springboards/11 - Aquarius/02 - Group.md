# New Zealand: Group (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan; gang and sporting slang in NZ English. **Concept in one line:** a set of people who see themselves, or are seen by others, as a unit; here, how a culture weighs the group against the person, draws the line between "us" and "them", and expects members to act together.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi grouped by waka:** Tainui, Te Arawa, Mātaatua and other canoe confederations; alliances and old rivalries run along these lines.
- **Descendants of kūpapa and of "rebel" hapū:** one war, two group memories.
- **Gang whānau:** families of Mongrel Mob and Black Power members, often Māori and Pacific; members speak of the gang as kin.
- **Samoan and Tongan congregations:** matai, aumaga and youth groups give a ranked group life inside the church.
- **Waterfront, freezing-works and mining unions:** the strongest traditions of acting together.
- **Rugby towns against city protest groups,** as split in 1981.
- **University student cultures** (Dunedin's student quarter): flat groups, initiations and mass gatherings.
- **Korean and Chinese churches and student groups:** different lines between insider and outsider.
- **Moriori and Ngāti Mutunga on Rēkohu:** a painful shared history between two groups.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich: strong election and labor data, a large literature on rugby and on the 1981 tour, and gang research. Pacific village-group life in NZ is thin in print.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for tribal and kapa haka material; Samoan and Tongan for congregation groups.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gangs (members, families and victims; restrictions on gang insignia may bear on images); kūpapa and the Moriori-Ngāti Mutunga history; Treaty settlement matters belong to Libra and Capricorn, not here.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category; their group life is not scouted here).
