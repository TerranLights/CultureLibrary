# New Zealand: Equality (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Chinese. **Concept in one line:** the idea that people are of equal worth or standing and should be treated alike in some respects. Which respects, and among whom, is where cultures differ.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Iwi with large settlements against iwi still in negotiation:** unequal tribal resources shape how equality is argued.
- **Rangatira lines and commoner whānau within iwi:** rank still matters on the marae.
- **Tongan New Zealanders:** a homeland with nobles and a monarch; how rank is kept or dropped in Auckland.
- **Samoan matai and untitled members:** titled authority inside an egalitarian-talking society.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** the poll tax made them the clearest case of a group taxed at the border; compare the families who paid it with arrivals after 1987.
- **Runholder districts against freezing-works and mill towns:** where talk of a fair go met the sharpest class gaps of the colony.
- **Northland and Tairāwhiti against central Auckland and Wellington:** the steepest regional gaps on the deprivation map.
- **Women and men:** suffrage pride and pay gaps side by side.
- **Takatāpui and rainbow communities; disabled and Deaf people:** each argues equality from a different starting point.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich: income, deprivation and ethnic-disparity data are strong; manners and leveling customs are less measured and need media and oral sources.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for Article 3 debates and marae rank; Tongan for noble and commoner terms; Chinese for poll-tax certificates and family papers.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Treaty rights and equal-versus-distinct status are political flashpoints; anything legal goes to Libra or Capricorn. Ethnic disparity statistics can feed stereotypes; read them with community sources.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category; their place in pay and discrimination data is not scouted here).

## 5. First queries
1. Download the latest MSD household incomes report and the NZDep maps; note the ethnic and regional breakdowns.
2. Read the Hunn Report beside *Puao-te-Ata-tu*.
3. Search Papers Past for "classless" and "tall poppy" by decade, to date when the leveling vocabulary took hold.
4. Search the NZAVS publication list for "social dominance".
5. Pull Ballara's *Iwi* for the older ranks, then search Mead and Grove for proverbs on boasting and on chiefs who serve.
