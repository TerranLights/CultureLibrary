# New Zealand: Reform (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan and Chinese for community responses to reforms. **Concept in one line:** deliberate change meant to improve a society's institutions, practices or people, short of overturning the whole; and the figure of the reformer.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Wellington's policy circles against provincial towns:** reform designed at the center, felt in places like Pātea and Ōtorohanga.
- **Ngāti Porou:** home of Ngata's land-development schemes; reform from inside an iwi.
- **Kīngitanga and the King Country:** the old agreement keeping liquor out of the district, a Māori-led restriction to set beside settler temperance.
- **Tūhoe:** long suspicious of reforms imposed from Wellington; compare its own renewal programs.
- **Former no-licence towns** (Invercargill and others): temperance as a local identity.
- **Farmers after 1984:** subsidies removed almost overnight; how rural districts tell that story.
- **State-housing tenants and public servants after the restructuring:** the reform from below the line.
- **Pacific communities:** reforms to immigration and work permits in the 1970s, as Pacific families remember them.
- **Rātana followers:** a church that became a political reform force through alliance with Labour.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** likely rich: the 1890s and 1980s reforms are among the most written-about subjects in NZ history; Māori-led renewal has strong biographies and archives; personal-reform programs are thin and private.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori for the prophets' and language movement's sources; Samoan and Tongan for oral memories of 1970s changes.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the reforms done to Māori land and people are tied to Treaty claims; their legal side is for Libra and Capricorn, and the prophets' teachings belong to their faithful (Rātana, Ringatū, Pai Mārire followers today). Recovery programs protect members' anonymity.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (category; reforms of immigration policy as they affected them are not scouted here).
