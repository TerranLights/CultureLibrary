# New Zealand: Nonconformity (Aquarius): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (NZ slang), te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori. **Concept in one line:** departing from a group's norms of belief, dress, conduct or way of life, by choice or by nature; and how far the culture tolerates, punishes or prizes the departure.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Karangahape Road and Ponsonby (Auckland):** long a home for drag, queer and bohemian life; gentrification since.
- **Cuba Street (Wellington):** the capital's street of buskers, cafés and alternative dress.
- **Dunedin students and musicians:** a small city with a strong indie scene.
- **West Auckland "westies":** a working-class subculture with its own style.
- **Takatāpui and Pacific gender roles (fa'afafine, fakaleitī, akava'ine):** difference that a home culture places differently from Pākehā norms.
- **Separatist faiths** (Exclusive Brethren, Gloriavale): nonconformity to the nation, strict conformity inside.
- **Tūhoe:** political dissent from the state, including the 2007 Te Urewera raids and their aftermath.
- **Families of objectors** (Otago's Baxters and others): dissent passed down.
- **Small rural towns against city neighborhoods:** how a "hard case" is tolerated in one and pushed out in the other.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** mixed: rich for objectors, music scenes, gender diversity and the census; thin for everyday tolerance of oddness, which needs oral history and local news.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands Māori for gender roles in their own terms; NZ slang dictionaries (e.g., Harry Orsman's *Dictionary of New Zealand English*, 1997 *check*).
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gender and sexual diversity (use people's own terms and sources); psychiatric-hospital histories and abuse in state care (the Royal Commission of Inquiry into Abuse in Care, 2018-2024); the 2007 raids are still contested.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands (listed as a category; their dissenters and subcultures get no pointers here).
