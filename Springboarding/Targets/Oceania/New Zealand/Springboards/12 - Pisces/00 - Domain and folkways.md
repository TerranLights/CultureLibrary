## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāpuhi and the Far North iwi:** the spirits' road to Te Rerenga Wairua crosses their lands, so death-lore may be local there.
- **Tūhoe of Te Urewera:** Rua Kēnana's Maungapōhatu; prophecy and healing lines of their own.
- **Te Arawa:** the 1886 Tarawera eruption and its reported ghost canoe; geothermal sites under tapu.
- **Ngāti Porou and Tairāwhiti:** Ringatū country, and recent flood loss.
- **Ngāi Tahu and Canterbury:** southern spirit-lore, and a city remade by the 2010-2011 earthquakes.
- **Moriori of Rēkohu:** their dead, their sacred trees and their history of loss are their own.
- **Rātana followers:** a church that began in faith healing.
- **Pacific communities of South Auckland and Porirua,** separated by island group: each has its own spirits and healers.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** goldfield-descended families against recent Mandarin speakers, in care of the dead.
- **Otago-Southland Presbyterians and West Coast Catholics:** two settler churches of consolation.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for mental-health statistics, asylum history and Māori concepts; mixed for paranormal belief (few national polls *check*); thin in English for Pacific healing.
- **Languages needed:** iwi dialects of te reo Māori, Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori, Cantonese; NZSL for Deaf sources.
- **Scope:** which lands the post-war nation holds (the Realm states, the outlying islands) is not yet settled; every Pisces sheet is written for the main islands.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** karakia, whakapapa and tapu knowledge belong to iwi and hapū; mākutu accusations hurt living families; Abuse in Care testimony and suicide reporting need care. The legal side of healing suppression goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** any pointer on South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
