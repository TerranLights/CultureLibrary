# New Zealand: Compassion (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English and te reo Māori; Samoan, Tongan, Cook Islands Māori and Niuean for church-centered giving; Tagalog, Korean and Mandarin for newer communities. **Concept in one line:** being moved by another's suffering and wanting to relieve it; mercy toward those in one's power.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Waikato-Tainui:** the Poukai tradition centers giving to the needy on the Kīngitanga marae.
- **Whanganui iwi:** the river communities where Catholic and Māori charity first met at Hiruharama.
- **Ngāti Kahungunu and Tairāwhiti iwi:** marae that opened as relief centers in Cyclone Gabrielle (2023), set beside official aid.
- **Samoan and Tongan congregations:** collections and family obligations (Samoan fa'alavelave) that move money to the needy, and debate over their weight.
- **Christchurch Muslim communities:** people of many origins on the receiving end of national sympathy after 2019, and how it felt.
- **Filipino New Zealanders** (Christchurch rebuild, dairy farms): bayanihan as their own form of mutual help.
- **Rural farming districts:** Rural Support Trusts and neighbor help in drought and flood.
- **Inner-city rough sleepers** (Auckland, Wellington): the people compassion is aimed at, in their own accounts.
- **Secular and church givers:** whether giving now flows through charities, the state or one's own hand.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for charities and volunteering (registers, reports); mixed for Māori and Pacific giving, which often runs through marae and churches unrecorded; thin for mercy toward enemies.
- **Languages needed:** Tagalog, Samoan, Tongan and te reo Māori for community sources.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** 2019 victims' families and refugee testimony; crowdfunding pages name the sick. Mercy toward offenders as a matter of sentencing goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands, as givers and receivers.
