*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Families served by urban Māori authorities** (Waipareira in West Auckland, the Manukau body in the south): urban families cut off from their iwi and rebuilding ties; a contrast to rural marae communities.
- **Ngāti Ruanui and Pātea:** a works town that emptied.
- **Tarara families of the Far North:** a fused Māori-Croatian identity.
- **Dutch New Zealanders:** a large postwar group whose language faded fast, as a question to measure.
- **Christchurch red-zone households:** neighborhoods ended by policy after disaster.
- **Gloriavale and its leavers:** total merging into a group, and exit.
- **Golden Bay and Coromandel alternative communities:** chosen dissolving into commune and land.
- **Pacific Pentecostal and mainline congregations:** different worship styles of losing oneself.
- **Whānau caring for kaumātua with mate wareware.**
