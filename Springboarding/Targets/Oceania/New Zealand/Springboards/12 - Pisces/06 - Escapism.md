## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Otago and Southland against the North Island:** crib and bach, and possibly different holiday customs behind the words.
- **West Coast mining and timber towns:** the pub as the center of off-shift life.
- **Tongan and Fijian kava clubs in Auckland:** a sober-headed gathering that some families criticize for long nights away; the line between rest and flight.
- **Hunters and trampers** (Central North Island, the Southern Alps): escape into the bush as respected male practice.
- **Young gamers** in Auckland and Wellington, including Korean-run PC rooms.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** mahjong evenings and casino visits.
- **Northland whānau** in the Te Ara Oranga program.
- **New Zealanders on their OE:** comparison only; the leaving as escape.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (health survey, gaming machine data, wastewater testing, tourism counts); thin for kava, mahjong and pre-contact leisure beyond colonial accounts.
- **Languages needed:** te reo Māori, Tongan, Samoan; Korean for gaming venues.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** addiction testimony; kava's ceremonial side belongs to its communities. Liquor licensing, pokies and cannabis regulation go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** escape and leisure among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
