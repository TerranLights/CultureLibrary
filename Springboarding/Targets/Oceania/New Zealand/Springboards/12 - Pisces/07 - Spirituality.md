## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Rātana followers** (Whanganui-Rangitīkei and beyond): faith healing and prophecy as the heart of practice.
- **Ringatū congregations** of Tairāwhiti and the eastern Bay of Plenty: their own prayer calendar.
- **Whanganui iwi:** the river as a living ancestor.
- **Ngāti Tūwharetoa:** sacred mountains and their gift.
- **Māori Latter-day Saints** of Hawke's Bay and Waikato: Māori belief and Mormon practice joined.
- **Samoan, Tongan and Cook Islands parishes:** devotion inside large, demanding congregations, kept separate by island group.
- **Filipino Catholics:** dawn Masses and home altars, as newer arrivals.
- **Taiwanese and Chinese Buddhists in Auckland.**
- **Pākehā seekers** (Golden Bay, Waiheke, Nelson): meditation and nature practice outside churches.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for census and denominational history; mixed for experience measures (ISSP waves *check*); Māori and Pacific practice well written in te reo and Pacific languages, thinner in English.
- **Languages needed:** Tagalog and Cook Islands Māori as well as te reo Māori, Samoan and Tongan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** many karakia and star knowledge are tapu to their iwi; Ringatū and Rātana rites are for adherents to share. The legal personhood of rivers and Te Urewera goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** pointers on devotion among South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
