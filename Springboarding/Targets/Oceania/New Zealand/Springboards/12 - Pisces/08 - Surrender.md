# New Zealand: Surrender (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English; te reo Māori (war, peacemaking, dying); Samoan and Tongan (apology and submission rites); Tagalog and Korean for migrant sayings of acceptance. **Concept in one line:** ceasing to resist; giving oneself, one's will or one's control over to something stronger or higher: God, fate, a lover, an enemy, illness, death; release and letting go included.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ngāti Maniapoto and Waikato iwi:** Ōrākau's refusal and the later confiscations; how defeat and non-surrender are remembered side by side.
- **Ngāpuhi and Ngāti Whātua:** peace-making after the musket wars, where yielding was negotiated, not imposed.
- **Samoan families:** ifoga as a way to end a dispute by humbling oneself.
- **Filipino New Zealanders:** bahala na as everyday acceptance, to test, not assume.
- **Esk Valley and other flood-hit households:** leaving the land.
- **Kaumātua and their whānau at the end of life:** tangihanga and dying at home as frames for letting go.
- **Men in rugby and farming communities:** where giving up is shamed.
- **Members of twelve-step fellowships:** surrender as the first step.
- **Gloriavale members and leavers.**

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich for war history and end-of-life reports; mixed for recovery; thin and unrecorded for ifoga outside court and press reports.
- **Languages needed:** Samoan (ifoga sources), te reo Māori, Tongan, Tagalog.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** the New Zealand Wars remain painful, and iwi tell them differently; dying and assisted death are private; ifoga belongs to Samoan families. The legal side of assisted dying and of land buyouts goes to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** on acceptance and letting go, South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
