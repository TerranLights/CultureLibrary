# New Zealand: Crime (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English (with local slang), te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for how congregations and kin respond to a wrongdoer; Cantonese for early Chinese community records. **Concept in one line:** what a culture counts as a serious wrong against the community, deserving public condemnation and punishment, and how it sees those who commit it (law itself is researched in Libra and Capricorn).

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Mongrel Mob and Black Power chapters** (Hawke's Bay, Waikato, Wellington): their own codes of wrong, set beside the public image.
- **Māori whānau and hapū:** hara and whakamā fall on kin as well as the offender; repair may run through the marae.
- **Samoan and Tongan youth in South Auckland:** the "youth gang" label against how churches and families see their young.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** a minority once on the receiving end of unpunished hostility.
- **Christchurch:** the city of two famous panics (1954 killing, 1990s crèche case).
- **Hutt Valley:** the 1950s delinquency scare.
- **West Coast and rural Southland:** local memory of Graham and Dean.
- **Pukekawa and northern Waikato farming districts:** the Crewe case's long shadow.
- **Online true-crime audiences:** a new public jury.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (victim survey, press archives, gang histories, family-violence data); thin for Pacific framings of wrongdoing in English.
- **Languages needed:** Cantonese for early records; te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** gang research needs care for informants; family violence and abuse testimony; Māori over-representation is a charged topic, to be framed by Māori researchers' work first. Courts, sentencing and statutes go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** wrongdoing as seen in South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
