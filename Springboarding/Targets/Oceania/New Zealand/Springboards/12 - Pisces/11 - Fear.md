# New Zealand: Fear (Pisces): springboard

*Scouting, not research. From the agent's own knowledge; nothing here is verified. Every name is marked *check*.*
*Use: a starting point for research, not its limit. Know why each pointer is here, check it, then follow every relevant lead beyond it (RUNBOOK, "Research from the springboard").*

**Target:** New Zealand, region Oceania. **Language(s) for searching:** English, te reo Māori; Samoan and Tongan for spirit fears and family warnings; Mandarin and Cantonese for anti-Chinese history and recent experience. **Concept in one line:** the emotion felt at danger, real or imagined, and a culture's ways of naming, showing, managing and using it, with worry and anxiety as its diffuse forms.

## 3. Who to compare inside the nation
- **Ōtautahi Christchurch residents:** years of aftershocks and what that left behind.
- **West Coast and Fiordland communities:** living on the Alpine Fault.
- **Tairāwhiti and Hawke's Bay coasts:** tsunami and flood warnings in recent memory.
- **Rotorua and Taupō communities:** volcanic risk and the ancestral tales tied to it.
- **Chinese New Zealanders:** from the leagues of the 1900s to slurs during COVID-19.
- **Muslim New Zealanders after 2019:** the fear of being targeted.
- **Pacific churchgoers in South Auckland:** blamed for a 2020 cluster, and how that felt.
- **Hunters and farmers against conservation groups:** opposite fears in the 1080 dispute.
- **Teenagers:** rising worry reported in school surveys.
- **The Otago Peninsula, Aramoana included:** a village shaped by one day.

## 4. Flags
- **Data:** rich (quake science, preparedness surveys, youth surveys, press archives); thin for Pacific spirit fears in English.
- **Languages needed:** Chinese for the league era and after; Samoan and Tongan for spirit fears; te reo Māori throughout.
- **Sensitive or restricted:** Aramoana and 2019 survivors; some ancestral stories belong to Te Arawa to tell; the 2022 occupation is still divisive. Policing and protest rules go to Libra or Capricorn.
- **Held for review:** fears reported by South Asian-heritage communities in the nation's lands.
