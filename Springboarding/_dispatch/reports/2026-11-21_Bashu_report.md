## Do the sheets exploit the nation's depth?
**Largely yes.**
- **All five provinces:** every sheet names Sichuan, Chongqing and Hubei, and about 125 name Yunnan and Guizhou.
- **Southwestern Mandarin** (Sichuanese, Chongqing 言子, Wuhan speech) is in the search terms of 125 sheets.
- **Non-Han peoples** are spread widely: the Yi, Miao/Hmong/Hmu, Dong, Hui, Dai, Naxi/Mosuo, Tujia, Bai, Qiang, Lisu and Hani.
- **Pre-modern pointers draw on the region's own past:**
  - Ba-Shu and Chu archaeology: Sanxingdui and Jinsha, and the Shuihudi, Guodian and Jiudian slips.
  - Chinese classics: *Huayang Guozhi*, *Jing-Chu suishi ji* and *Chuci*.
  - Minority texts: the Nuosu epic *Hnewo Teyy*, Dongba manuscripts, Shui script books and Miao ancient songs.
- **Modern Bashu-specific sources** run through the sheets: Third Front, Wenchuan and Three Gorges resettlement, Wuhan 2020, and Chengdu teahouse scholarship.

The gaps are the repeated "who to compare" units and the long tail of smaller peoples noted above. A future prompt could ask each sign's agent to rotate its comparison units and to include at least one Yunnan or Guizhou small minority per sheet.
